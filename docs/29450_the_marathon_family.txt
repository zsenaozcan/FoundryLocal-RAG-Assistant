Title: The Marathon Family (1982)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Slobodan Šijan
Main Cast: Bogdan Diklić, Danilo 'Bata' Stojković, Pavle Vuisić, Mija Aleksić, Radislav Lazarević
Keywords: small town, funeral, serbia, mafia, grave digger, graveyard, 1930s

Overview: The Topalovic family has been in the burial business for generations. When the old (150 yrs old) Pantelija dies, five generations of his heirs start to fight for the inheritance.
