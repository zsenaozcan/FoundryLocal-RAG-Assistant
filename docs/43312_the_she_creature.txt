Title: The She-Creature (1956)
Genres: Romance, Fantasy, Horror
Director: Edward L. Cahn
Main Cast: Chester Morris, Marla English, Tom Conway, Cathy Downs, Lance Fuller
Keywords: monster, reincarnation, supernatural, murder, creature, psychic, alcoholic, sea monster, amusement park, hypnotist

Overview: A mysterious hypnotist (Chester Morris) reverts his beautiful assistant (Marla English) back into the form of a prehistoric sea monster that she was in a past life.
