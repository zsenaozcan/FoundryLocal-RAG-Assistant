Title: The Return of a Man Called Horse (1976)
Genres: Action, Drama, Western
Director: Irvin Kershner
Main Cast: Richard Harris, Gale Sondergaard, Geoffrey Lewis, Jorge Luke, Jorge Russek
Keywords: based on novel, conflict, wild west, sioux tribe, rites of passage, flintlock pistol

Overview: Lord John Morgan has returned to civilized life in England, but finds he has nothing but disdain for that life. Yearning to embrace the simplicity of the American West-and the Yellow Hands Sioux tribe he left behind, Morgan returns to the tribe's land only to discover that they've been decimated by ruthless, government-backed fur traders. They must regain their land, led by Horse.
