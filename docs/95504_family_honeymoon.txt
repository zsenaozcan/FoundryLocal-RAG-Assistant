Title: Family Honeymoon (1948)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Claude Binyon
Main Cast: Claudette Colbert, Fred MacMurray, Rita Johnson, Gigi Perreau, Jimmy Hunt

Overview: Grant Jordan, bachelor botany professor, marries Katie, a widow with three kids, despite the machinations of Grant's former girlfriend Minna. But on the wedding day, Aunt Jo, who was to babysit, breaks a leg; so the kids come along on the honeymoon.
