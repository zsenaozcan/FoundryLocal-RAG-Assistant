Title: I Confess (1953)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Alfred Hitchcock
Main Cast: Montgomery Clift, Anne Baxter, Karl Malden, O.E. Hasse, Brian Aherne
Keywords: confession, secret, blackmail, suspicion of murder, quebec, police, murder, priest, caretaker, lawyer
