Title: Mean Guns (1997)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Crime, Thriller
Director: Albert Pyun
Main Cast: Christopher Lambert, Ice-T, Michael Halsey, Deborah Van Valkenburgh, Tina Cote
Keywords: mexican standoff, will to survive, organized crime, shootout, witness elimination

Overview: One hundred mid- and low-level gangsters who are on their boss' bad side are locked inside a newly-built high-security prison, and given plenty of guns, ammo, and baseball bats, then told that the last survivor will get a suitcase with 10 million dollars.
