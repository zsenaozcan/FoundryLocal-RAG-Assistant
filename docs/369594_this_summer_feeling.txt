Title: This Summer Feeling (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mikhaël Hers
Main Cast: Anders Danielsen Lie, Judith Chemla, Marie Rivière, Féodor Atkine, Dounia Sichov
Keywords: paris, berlin, psychology, friendship, new york city, psychological drama

Overview: Sasha, a French woman of thirty, dies suddenly in Berlin. Her Fiancé, Lawrence, and her sister Zoé, must come to terms as the consequences of this shock set in.
