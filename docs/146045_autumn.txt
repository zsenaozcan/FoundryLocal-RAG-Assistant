Title: Autumn (1990)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Arvo Kruusement
Main Cast: Margus Lepa, Liina Tennosaar, Anne Reemann, Kaljo Kiisk, Ita Ever
Keywords: competition, brother brother relationship, jealousy, piglet, twins, wedding ceremony, merchant, graveyard, courting, rumor

Overview: In the follow-up of Estonian classics "Kevade" (1969) and "Suvi" (1976) the same characters have reached their middle-age. Everybody are living quiet family-life but Kiir the tailor who one more time decides to become a farmer and get married.
