Title: Days and Clouds (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Silvio Soldini
Main Cast: Margherita Buy, Antonio Albanese, Giuseppe Battiston, Alba Rohrwacher, Carla Signoris
Keywords: married couple, job interview, shortage of money, household, workplace, call center, unemployment, marriage problems, rich vs poor

Overview: Set in Genoa, the film concerns the financial struggles and emotional strain that occur after Michele loses his job. He and his wife Elsa are forced to give up their affluent lifestyle and cope with the tensions of moving into a smaller home, finding new work, and making sacrifices.
