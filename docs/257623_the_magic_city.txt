Title: The Magic City (1955)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nikos Koundouros
Main Cast: Thanasis Vengos, Manos Katrakis, Margarita Papageorgiou, Stephanos Stratigos, Eva Brika
Keywords: greek, city

Overview: A look at poverty in post-war Athens. Kosmas, a young man living in the slums, constantly tries to make ends meet, hoping for a better day. Because of debts, he gets tangled up in smuggling. His morals are tested and he tries to find a way out.
