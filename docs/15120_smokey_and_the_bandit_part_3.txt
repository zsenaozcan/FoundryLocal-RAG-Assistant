Title: Smokey and the Bandit Part 3 (1983)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Dick Lowry
Main Cast: Jackie Gleason, Jerry Reed, Paul Williams, Pat McCormick, Colleen Camp

Overview: The Enos duo convince Cletus, aka The Bandit, to come out of hiding and help them promote their new restaurant. With a little coaxing, he agrees, producing an almost-creaky Trigger as his mode of transport. But his nemesis, Sheriff Buford T. Justice, is on the hunt, forcing Cletus and Trigger to hit the road. Can they steer clear of the vengeful sheriff?
