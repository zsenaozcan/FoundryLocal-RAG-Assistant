Title: A Gun For Jennifer (1997)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime
Director: Todd Morris
Main Cast: Deborah Twiss, Benja Kay, Rene Alberta, Tracy Dillon, Veronica Cruz
Keywords: rape, vigilante, revenge

Overview: A woman comes to New York City from Ohio to escape an abusive husband and gets entangled with murderous female vigilantes who prey on abusive men.
