Title: Lila Says (2004)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Ziad Doueiri
Main Cast: Vahina Giocante, Moa Khouas, Karim Ben Haddou, Edmonde Franchi, Lotfi Chakri
Keywords: paris, sex, obsession, ghetto, party, love, independent film, teacher, gang, writer

Overview: Based on a controversial French novel, Lila Says tells the story of a quiet young poet named Chimo who develops a crush on the pretty, blond Lila, a girl who recently moved into his Arab ghetto with her aunt. When the leader of a rival gang also falls for Lila, the ensuing love triangle initiates a journey of sexual discovery -- and sets off a chain of devastating events.
