Title: The Blackbird (1926)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Tod Browning
Main Cast: Lon Chaney, Owen Moore, Renée Adorée, Doris Lloyd, Andy MacLennan
Keywords: silent film

Overview: Two thieves, the Blackbird and West End Bertie, fall in love with the same girl, a French nightclub performer named Fifi. Each man tries to outdo the other to win her heart.
