Title: Celal ile Ceren (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Togan Gökbakar
Main Cast: Şahan Gökbakar, Gökcen Gökçebağ, Dilşah Demir, Ezgi Mola

Overview: Celal, who dreams of achieving happiness as a ladies' man and charmer, soon gets into difficulties and suffers misfortune. Back when Celal was in a relationship, he was jealous of the life of a bachelor.
