Title: Personal Shopper (2016)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Olivier Assayas
Main Cast: Kristen Stewart, Lars Eidinger, Anders Danielsen Lie, Sigrid Bouaziz, Nora von Waldstätten
Keywords: paris, female nudity, nudity, medium, poltergeist, apparition, spirit, text message, shopping, dress shop
