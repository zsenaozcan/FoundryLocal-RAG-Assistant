Title: Landscape Suicide (1987)
Genres: Unknown
Director: James Benning
Main Cast: Rhonda Bell, Elion Sucher

Overview: In Landscape Suicide Benning continues his examination of Americana through the stories of two murderers. Ed Gein was a Wisconsin farmer and multiple murderer who taxidermied his victims in the 1950s. Bernadette Protti was a California teenager who stabbed a friend to death over an insult in 1984.
