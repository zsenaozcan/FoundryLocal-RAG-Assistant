Title: Rubber Johnny (2005)
Genres: Animation, Horror
Director: Chris Cunningham
Main Cast: Chris Cunningham, Percy Rutterford, Elvis
Keywords: wheelchair, cellar, horror, basement, drug, chihuahua, short, techno music

Overview: Rubber Johnny is a six-minute experimental short film and music video directed by Chris Cunningham in 2005, using music composed by Aphex Twin. The name Rubber Johnny is drawn from a British slang for "condom" as well as a description of the main character, which explains the title sequence.
