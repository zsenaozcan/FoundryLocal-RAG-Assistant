Title: The Barrens (2012)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Darren Lynn Bousman
Main Cast: Stephen Moyer, Mia Kirshner, Allie MacDonald, Erik Knudsen, Max Topplin
Keywords: new jersey devil

Overview: A man takes his family on a camping trip and becomes convinced they are being stalked by the legendary monster of the New Jersey Pine Barrens: the Jersey Devil.
