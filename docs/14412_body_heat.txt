Title: Body Heat (1981)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime, Romance
Director: Lawrence Kasdan
Main Cast: William Hurt, Kathleen Turner, Richard Crenna, Ted Danson, J. A. Preston
Keywords: prison, smoking, infidelity, femme fatale, no air conditioning, neo-noir

Overview: In the midst of a searing Florida heat wave, a woman convinces her lover, a small-town lawyer, to murder her rich husband.
