Title: Scanner Cop (1994)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror
Director: Pierre David
Main Cast: Daniel Quinn, Darlanne Fluegel, Richard Grove, Mark Rolston, Richard Lynch
Keywords: telekenesis

Overview: Sam Staziak, a rookie cop with the Los Angeles Police Department, is also a 'scanner' (psionic). When a string of murders begins to decimate the police department, Sam faces sensory overload and possible insanity as he uses his powers to hunt the man responsible for the killings.
