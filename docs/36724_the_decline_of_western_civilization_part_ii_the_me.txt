Title: The Decline of Western Civilization Part II: The Metal Years (1988)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Penelope Spheeris
Main Cast: Dave Mustaine, Ozzy Osbourne, Chris Holmes, Ian Fraser Kilmister, Lemmy
Keywords: heavy metal, anarchist, anti social, thrash metal, metal, anthropology, punk rock, social commentary, woman director

Overview: An exploration of the heavy metal scene in Los Angeles, with particular emphasis on glam metal. It features concert footage and interviews of legendary heavy metal and hard rock bands and artists such as Aerosmith, Alice Cooper, Kiss, Megadeth, Motörhead, Ozzy Osbourne and W.A.S.P..
