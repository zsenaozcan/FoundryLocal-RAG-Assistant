Title: Lola (1970)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Comedy
Director: Richard Donner
Main Cast: Charles Bronson, Susan George, Honor Blackman, Michael Craig, Trevor Howard
Keywords: age difference, older man young girl relationship, underage girlfriend

Overview: A middle aged writer of pornographic novels meets and falls in love with a sixteen year old school girl. This alone is cause for concern but when the couple get married and move to America, the trouble (and fun) really begins.
