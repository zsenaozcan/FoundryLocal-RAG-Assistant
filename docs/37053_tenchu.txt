Title: Tenchu! (1969)
Genres: History, Action, Drama
Director: Hideo Gosha
Main Cast: Shintarô Katsu, Tatsuya Nakadai, Yukio Mishima, Yûjirô Ishihara, Takumi Shinjo

Overview: A ronin desperately seeks a way out of financial straits; he allies with the Tosa clan under the ruthless leader Takechi, who quickly takes advantage.
