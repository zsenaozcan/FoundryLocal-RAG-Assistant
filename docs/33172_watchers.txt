Title: Watchers (1988)
Genres: Action, Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Jon Hess
Main Cast: Corey Haim, Michael Ironside, Lala Sloatman, Barbara Williams, Duncan Fraser

Overview: A boy takes in a stray dog, later finding out that its an ultra-intelligent runaway from a genetic research lab. Unknow to him, the dog is being stalked by another escaped creature thats not quite so friendly.
