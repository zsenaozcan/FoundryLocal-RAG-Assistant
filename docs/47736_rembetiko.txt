Title: Rembetiko (1983)
Genres: Drama
Director: Costas Ferris
Main Cast: Sotiria Leonardou, Michalis Maniatis, Themis Bazaka, Giorgos Zorbas, Vicky Vanita

Overview: The story of a group of Rembetes, singers and musicians of the Greek equivalent to the blues, in the early decades of the 20th century, seen through the eyes of a young female singer.
