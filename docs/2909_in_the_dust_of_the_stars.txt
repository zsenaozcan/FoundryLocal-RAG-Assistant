Title: In the Dust of the Stars (1976)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Gottfried Kolditz
Main Cast: Zephi Alșec, Violeta Andrei, Milan Beli, Ștefan Mihăilescu-Brăila, Jana Brejchová
Keywords: slavery, spaceship, outer space

Overview: A retro '70s vision of the future, IN THE DUST OF THE STARS was made by the DEFA film studio. A radio signal attracts a team of astronauts to a mysterious planet. Once there, they are warmly welcomed by an alien race, but are soon suspicious of what is really occurring on this strange land.
