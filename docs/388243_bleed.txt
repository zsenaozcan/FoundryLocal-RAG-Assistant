Title: Bleed (2016)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Tripp Rhame
Main Cast: Chelsey Crisp, Riley Smith, Michael Steger, Lyndon Smith, Brittany Ishibashi
Keywords: new home

Overview: It seemed perfect - a new house, a new marriage, a child soon to be born. But when Sarah and Matt invite their friends to celebrate, the situation turns deadly as they enter a burned-out prison on a ghost hunt.
