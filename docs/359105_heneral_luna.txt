Title: Heneral Luna (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Action, Drama, History, War
Director: Jerrold Tarog
Main Cast: John Arcilla, Mon Confiado, Mylene Dizon, Archie Alemania, Jeffrey Quizon
Keywords: philippines, period drama, philippine history, pinoy
