Title: The Assailant (2009)
Genres: Drama, Action, Adventure
Director: João Daniel Tikhomiroff
Main Cast: Aílton Carmo, Jéssica Barbosa, Sérgio Laurentino, Flávio Rocha, Irandhir Santos

Overview: Based on the life of a legendary capoeira fighter from Bahia, "Besouro" spins a fantastic tale of a young Brazilian man of African descent in search of his mission.
