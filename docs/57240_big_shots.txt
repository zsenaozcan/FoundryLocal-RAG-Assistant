Title: Big Shots (1987)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Robert Mandel
Main Cast: Ricky Busker, Darius McCrary, Robert Joy, Robert Prosky, Jerzy Skolimowski
Keywords: kids, interracial friendship

Overview: Following the death of his father, suburbanite Obie runs away from home and winds up on Chicago's South Side. After being mugged, he befriends con artist Scam. The two then embark on a Down South adventure involving gangsters, the police, and a search for Scam's father.
