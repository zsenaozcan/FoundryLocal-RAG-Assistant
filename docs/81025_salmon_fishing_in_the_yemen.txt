Title: Salmon Fishing in the Yemen (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lasse Hallström
Main Cast: Emily Blunt, Ewan McGregor, Kristin Scott Thomas, Rachael Stirling, Amr Waked
Keywords: bureaucracy, wife husband relationship, desert, salmon, arab, yemen, missing in action, flood, dreamer, public relations

Overview: A fisheries expert is approached by a consultant to help realize a sheik's vision of bringing the sport of fly-fishing to the desert and embarks on an upstream journey of faith and fish to prove the impossible possible.
