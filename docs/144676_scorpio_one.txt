Title: Scorpio One (1998)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Worth Keeter
Main Cast: Robert Carradine, Jeff Speakman, Robin Curtis, Steve Kanaly, George Murdock
Keywords: cia, space station

Overview: Using ultra-advanced technology, scientists aboard Scorpio One have made a monumental discovery -- and paid for it with their lives. Now, with time and oxygen running out, Scorpio One is about to become ground zero for an explosive showdown high above the Earth, where the stakes are nothing less than the future of the world.
