Title: Little Man (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Esben Tønnesen
Main Cast: Nikolaj Rasmussen, Malene Rasmussen, Philip Gjedde, Lotte Bergstrøm

Overview: Young Matthias was in school assignment to write an essay on any subject. The boy decides to go with the responsibility to implement it - he wants to make a guide to communicating with girls. But where to get the information he needs? Can the attitude of boys and girls be defined by rules? So Matthias decides to conduct his own research.
