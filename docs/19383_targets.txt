Title: Targets (1968)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Peter Bogdanovich
Main Cast: Tim O'Kelly, Boris Karloff, Arthur Peterson, Jr., Monte Landis, Nancy Hsueh
Keywords: vietnam veteran, sniper, suspense, drive-in theatre, movie star

Overview: An elderly horror-film star who, while making a personal appearance at a drive-in theatre, confronts a psychotic Vietnam veteran who has turned into a mass-murdering sniper.
