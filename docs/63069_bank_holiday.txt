Title: Bank Holiday (1938)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Carol Reed
Main Cast: John Lodge, Margaret Lockwood, Hugh Williams, René Ray, Merle Tottenham
Keywords: holiday, nurse, train station, hotel, beach, romance, brighton, hospital, pier, seaside

Overview: A 1930's British summer Bank Holiday starts at midday on Saturday with a rush for the trains to the seaside. Doreen and Milly are off to a beauty contest, Geoffrey and Catherine are having an illicit weekend in the Grand Hotel and May and the kids are set for a more straightforward holiday of sea, sand, and pub. Meanwhile, the manager and performers on the pier are praying for rain.
