Title: Our Nixon (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Penny Lane
Main Cast: Richard Nixon, John Ehrlichman, Harry Robbins Haldeman, Dwight L. Chapin, Lawrence Higby
Keywords: watergate scandal, woman director

Overview: Never before seen Super 8 home movies filmed by Richard Nixon's closest aides - and convicted Watergate conspirators - offer a surprising and intimate new look into his Presidency.
