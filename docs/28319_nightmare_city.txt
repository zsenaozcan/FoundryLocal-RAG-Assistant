Title: Nightmare City (1980)
Genres: Horror
Director: Umberto Lenzi
Main Cast: Hugo Stiglitz, Laura Trotter, Francisco Rabal, Mel Ferrer, Maria Rosaria Omaggio
Keywords: zombie, virus

Overview: An airplane exposed to radiation lands, and blood drinking zombies emerge armed with knives, guns and teeth! They go on a rampage slicing, dicing, and biting their way across the Italian countryside.
