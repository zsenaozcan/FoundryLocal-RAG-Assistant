Title: Delgo (2008)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Animation, Comedy, Science Fiction, Family
Director: Marc F. Adler
Main Cast: Freddie Prinze Jr., Jennifer Love Hewitt, Val Kilmer, Anne Bancroft, Chris Kattan

Overview: In a divided land, it takes a rebellious boy and his clandestine love for a Princess of an opposing race to stop a war orchestrated by a power hungry villain.
