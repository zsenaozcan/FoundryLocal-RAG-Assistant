Title: Forever Amber (1947)
Genres: Drama
Director: Otto Preminger
Main Cast: Linda Darnell, Cornel Wilde, Richard Greene, George Sanders, Glenn Langan
Keywords: prison, england, suppressed past, duel, king

Overview: Amber St Clair, orphaned during the English Civil War and raised by a family of farmers, aspires to be a lady of high society; when a group of cavaliers ride into town, she sneaks away with them to London to achieve her dreams.
