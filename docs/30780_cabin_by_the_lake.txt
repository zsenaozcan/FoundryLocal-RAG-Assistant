Title: Cabin By The Lake (2000)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Po-Chih Leong
Main Cast: Judd Nelson, Hedy Burress, Susan Gibney, Bernie Coulson, Colleen Wheeler
Keywords: kidnapping, fear of water, made for cable tv, serial killer, underwater, dungeon, post modernism

Overview: A screenwriter does research for his new script by actually kidnapping and drowning young girls. He then places them in his "garden" of other dead girls coming back daily to check on them. One girl narrowly escapes and the other bodies are found leading to an ingenious plot to try and capture the killer.
