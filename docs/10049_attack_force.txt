Title: Attack Force (2006)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Michael Keusch
Main Cast: Steven Seagal, Danny Webb, Andrew Bicknell, Mark Dymond, David Kennedy
Keywords: army, slaughter, drug

Overview: Marshall Lawson is the commander of an elite military unit. Marshall Lawson loses his strike-team in a cold-blooded and seemingly random attack, and he takes it upon himself to investigate the attack, with the help of his girlfriend Tia and his friend Dwayne.
