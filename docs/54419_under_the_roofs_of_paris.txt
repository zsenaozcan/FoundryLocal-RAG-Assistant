Title: Under the Roofs of Paris (1930)
Genres: Music, Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: René Clair
Main Cast: Albert Préjean, Pola Illéry, Edmond T. Gréville, Bill Bocket, Gaston Modot
Keywords: musical

Overview: Albert is smitten for Pola but ends up wrongly committed in jail, in the meantime her affections are sought after by his friend, and on his release both love and friendship must be tested.
