Title: H.M.S. Defiant (1962)
Genres: History, Drama, Action, Adventure
Director: Lewis Gilbert
Main Cast: Alec Guinness, Dirk Bogarde, Anthony Quayle, Maurice Denham, Nigel Stock
Keywords: tall ship, high seas

Overview: Defiant's crew is part of a fleet-wide movement to present a petition of grievances to the Admiralty. Violence must be no part of it. The continual sadism of Defiant's first officer makes this difficult, and when the captain is disabled, the chance for violence increases.
