Title: The Devil's Honey (1986)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Brett Halsey, Corinne Cléry, Blanca Marsillach, Stefano Madia, Paula Molina
Keywords: perversion

Overview: A young woman abducts a doctor whom she holds responsible for the death of her boyfriend. and subjects him to various sexual torture acts.
