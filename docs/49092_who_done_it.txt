Title: Who Done It? (1942)
Genres: Comedy, Mystery
Director: Erle C. Kenton
Main Cast: Bud Abbott, Lou Costello, Patric Knowles, William Gargan, Louise Allbritton
Keywords: murder, radio program

Overview: Two dumb soda jerks dream of writing radio mysteries. When they try to pitch an idea at a radio station, they end up in the middle of a real murder when the station owner is killed during a broadcast.
