Title: Dirty Lies (2016)
Genres: Action, Drama, Crime, Thriller
Director: Jamie Marshall
Main Cast: Tania Raymonde, AJ Michalka, Keith David, Scout Taylor-Compton, Francia Raisa

Overview: An under-appreciated intern entrusted with a million dollar necklace races to find out which of his money hungry room-mates betrayed him as he battles a desperate criminal duo bent on stealing.
