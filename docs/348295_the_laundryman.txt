Title: The Laundryman (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Chung Lee
Main Cast: Joseph Chang Hsiao-Chuan, Sonia Sui, Regina Wan, Yann Yann Yeo

Overview: A-Gu enlists a group of contract killers in the disguise of laundry service. One of them, code-named "No.1 Qingtian Street", is haunted by the ghosts of his victims. He seeks help from Lin Hsiang, a psychic.
