Title: The Rooftop (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Action, Drama
Director: Jay Chou
Main Cast: Jay Chou, Xu Fan, Li Xin Ai, Alan Kuo, Darren Chiu

Overview: Taiwanese pop sensation Jay Chou, drawing on his years as an actor, director, and recording star, creates a fantasy playground of music, moves, and magic as a backdrop to an action comedy which pays homage to the wondrous musicals of the past, while exploding to life with today’s sound and spirit.
