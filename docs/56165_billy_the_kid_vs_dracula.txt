Title: Billy the Kid vs. Dracula (1966)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Horror
Director: William Beaudine
Main Cast: John Carradine, Melinda Plowman, Chuck Courtney, Virginia Christine, Olive Carey
Keywords: vampire, dracula, billy the kid, b movie
