Title: Axe Giant - The Wrath of Paul Bunyan (2013)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror
Director: Gary Jones
Main Cast: Dan Haggerty, Joe Estevez, Amber Connor, Kristina Kopf
Keywords: boot camp, paul bunyan, lumberjack, giant

Overview: Axe Giant - The Wrath of Paul Bunyon is a dark, contemporary take on the tall tale. Kids at a first-time offenders' boot camp discover the legend of lumberjack Paul Bunyan is real, but is much more horrifying than they could have imagined. They incur the wrath of the 15-foot monstrous giant, who was banished from town 100 years ago and thought dead.
