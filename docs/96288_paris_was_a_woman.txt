Title: Paris Was a Woman (1996)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Greta Schiller
Main Cast: Gisèle Freund, Sylvia Beach, Janet Flanner
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Women (many of them lesbian) artists, writers, photographers, designers, and adventurers settled in Paris between the wars. They embraced France, some developed an ex-pat culture, and most cherished a way of life quite different than the one left behind.
