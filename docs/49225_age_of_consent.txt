Title: Age of Consent (1969)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Michael Powell
Main Cast: James Mason, Helen Mirren, Jack MacGowran, Neva Carr-Glynn, Andonia Katsaros
Keywords: painter, exotic island, lolita, australian, lovers, biography, vacation, old man

Overview: An elderly artist thinks he has become too stale and is past his prime. His friend (and agent) persuades him to go to an offshore island to try once more. On the island he re-discovers his muse in the form of a young girl.
