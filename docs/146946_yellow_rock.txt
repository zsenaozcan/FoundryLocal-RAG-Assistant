Title: Yellow Rock (2011)
Genres: Western
Director: Nick Vallelonga
Main Cast: Michael Biehn, James Russo, Lenore Andriel, Michael Spears, Eddie Spears
Keywords: missing child

Overview: Five men ride into the eerie town of Yellow Rock, hoping to rescue a family member and his lost boy. The leader, Max Dietrich, hires Mountain Man, Tom Hanner, to guide them into the Black Paw Tribe territory for the search.
