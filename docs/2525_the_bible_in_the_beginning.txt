Title: The Bible: In the Beginning... (1966)
Genres: Drama
Director: John Huston
Main Cast: Michael Parks, Ulla Bergryd, Richard Harris, Franco Nero, George C. Scott
Keywords: bible, christian, flood, noah, noah's ark, adam and eve, abraham

Overview: Extravagant production of the first part of the book of Genesis. Covers Adam and Eve, Noah and the Flood and Abraham and Isaac.
