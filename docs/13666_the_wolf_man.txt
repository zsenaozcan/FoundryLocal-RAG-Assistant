Title: The Wolf Man (1941)
Genres: Horror, Drama
Director: George Waggner
Main Cast: Lon Chaney Jr., Claude Rains, Evelyn Ankers, Maria Ouspenskaya, Patric Knowles
Keywords: gypsy, fortune teller, shotgun, wolf, amulet, full moon, beautiful woman, good vs evil, trap, wolfman

Overview: After his brother's death, Larry Talbot returns home to his father and the family estate. Events soon take a turn for the worse when Larry is bitten by a werewolf.
