Title: Impulse (2008)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Charles T. Kanganis
Main Cast: Angus Macfadyen, Willa Ford, Robert Moloney, Ron Selmour, Pablo Silveira
Keywords: false identity, psychopath, sexual fantasy

Overview: Acting on impulse, Claire Dennison (Willa Ford), enjoys a brief fling with a sexy stranger (a dead ringer for her husband), only to realize that the object of her desire is a crazed, manipulative, and tenacious psychopath.
