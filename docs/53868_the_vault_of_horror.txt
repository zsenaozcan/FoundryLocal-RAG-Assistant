Title: The Vault of Horror (1973)
Genres: Horror
Director: Roy Ward Baker
Main Cast: Anna Massey, Terry-Thomas, Glynis Johns, John Forbes-Robertson, Curd Jürgens
Keywords: vault, vampire, painting, supernatural, anthology, scam, brutal death, india, dismemberment, crypt keeper

Overview: The sequel to Tales from the Crypt.  Five strangers trapped in a basement vault converse about their recurring nightmares. Their stories include vampires, bodily dismemberment, east Indian mysticism, an insurance scam, and an artist who kills by painting his victims' deaths.
