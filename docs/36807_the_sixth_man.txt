Title: The Sixth Man (1997)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Randall Miller
Main Cast: Marlon Wayans, Kadeem Hardison, David Paymer, Saundra McClain, Harold Sylvester
Keywords: college, basketball, dying man

Overview: Antoine and Kenny Tyler are NCAA college basketball players, and Antoine is the star. Suddenly Antoine dies of heart attack and Kenny has to fill his shoes as leader of team. Some time later, Antoine returns as a ghost and helps Kenny in game and in life, but Kenny changes in the process and doesn't quite like it.
