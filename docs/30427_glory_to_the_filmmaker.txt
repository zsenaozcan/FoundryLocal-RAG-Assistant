Title: Glory to the Filmmaker! (2007)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Takeshi Kitano
Main Cast: Takeshi Kitano, Toru Emori, Kayoko Kishimoto, Anne Suzuki, Keiko Matsuzaka

Overview: Takeshi Kitano plays a version of himself in which he's a struggling director cycling through a number of different genres in an effort to complete his latest project.
