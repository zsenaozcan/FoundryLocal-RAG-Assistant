Title: The One Percent (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jamie Johnson
Main Cast: Nicole Buffet, Chuck Collins, Steve Forbes, Cody Franchetti, Milton Friedman
Keywords: upper class, usa, greed, playboy, money, economics, rich, inequality, politcs, wealth differences

Overview: Jamie Johnson takes the exploration of wealth that he began in Born Rich one step further. The One Percent, refers to the tiny percentage of Americans who control nearly half the wealth of the U.S. Johnson's thesis is that this wealth in the hands of so few people is a danger to our very way of life.
