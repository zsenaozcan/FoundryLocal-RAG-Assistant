Title: Cross My Heart (1987)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Armyan Bernstein
Main Cast: Annette O'Toole, Martin Short, Paul Reiser

Overview: A man and a woman go out on a "big" third date. He's ashamed to admit he just lost his job, and she's afraid he'll run away if he finds out that she has a kid. Small lies lead to bigger ones and the night gets crazy very soon.
