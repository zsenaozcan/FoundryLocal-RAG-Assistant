Title: Henry: Portrait of a Serial Killer (1986)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: John McNaughton
Main Cast: Michael Rooker, Tracy Arnold, Tom Towles, David Katz, Mary Demas
Keywords: chicago, prostitute, rape, sadistic, nudity, sister, psychopath, murder, video camera, serial killer

Overview: Henry likes to kill people, in different ways each time. Henry shares an apartment with Otis. When Otis' sister comes to stay, we see both sides of Henry: "the guy next door" and the serial killer.
