Title: Attack of the 50 Foot Cheerleader (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Kevin O'Neill
Main Cast: Jena Sims, Sean Young, Treat Williams, Sasha Jackson, Olivia Alexander
Keywords: cheerleader, college, role reversal, fraternity, giant man, gender disguise, science experiment

Overview: Aspiring college cheerleader, Cassie Stratford consumes an experimental drug that grants her beauty and enough athletic ability to make the cheer squad. The drug has an unforeseen side effect — Cassie starts to grow and grow and grow!
