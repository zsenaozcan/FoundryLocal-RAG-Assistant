Title: Just Jim (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Craig Roberts
Main Cast: Craig Roberts, Emile Hirsch, Richard Harrington, Mark Lewis Jones, Sai Bennett
Keywords: wales, teenager

Overview: In a small Welsh town where people talk to themselves we meet Jim, a lonely  teenager who is given the chance to increase his popularity when a cool American kid moves in next door.  Written and directed by Craig Roberts, who also plays the lead role.
