Title: Pride and Vengeance (1967)
Genres: Action, Drama, Romance, Western
Director: Luigi Bazzoni
Main Cast: Franco Nero, Tina Aumont, Klaus Kinski, Guido Lollobrigida, Franco Ressel
Keywords: spaghetti western

Overview: A Spanish army officer, Don Jose (Nero), stationed in Seville, meets and begins a relationship with a mysterious gypsy, Carmen (Aumont). After he discovers she has cheated on him with his Lieutenant, he kills the officer and flees the city with Carmen. He recovers from his wounds and is forced to begin the life of a bandit.
