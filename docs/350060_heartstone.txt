Title: Heartstone (2016)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Guðmundur Arnar Guðmundsson
Main Cast: Baldur Einarsson, Blær Hinriksson, Diljá Valsdóttir, Katla Njálsdóttir, Søren Malling
Keywords: friendship, coming of age, first love

Overview: A remote fishing village in Iceland. Teenage boys Thor and Christian experience a turbulent summer as one tries to win the heart of a girl while the other discovers new feelings toward his best friend. When summer ends and the harsh nature of Iceland takes back its rights, it's time to leave the playground and face adulthood.
