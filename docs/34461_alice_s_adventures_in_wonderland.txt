Title: Alice's Adventures in Wonderland (1972)
Genres: Family, Fantasy, Music
Director: William Sterling
Main Cast: Fiona Fullerton, Michael Crawford, Robert Helpmann, Peter Sellers, Spike Milligan
Keywords: queen, musical, alice in wonderland, based on children's book
