Title: The Leech Woman (1960)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Edward Dein
Main Cast: Coleen Gray, Grant Williams, Phillip Terry, Gloria Talbott, John van Dreelen
Keywords: suicide, africa, dynamite, expedition, alcoholism, murder, dysfunctional marriage, tribe, jungle, doctor
