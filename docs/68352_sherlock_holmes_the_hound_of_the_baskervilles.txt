Title: Sherlock Holmes: The Hound of the Baskervilles (1988)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Brian Mills
Main Cast: Jeremy Brett, Edward Hardwicke, Raymond Adamson, Alastair Duncan
Keywords: sherlock holmes

Overview: Sir Charles Baskerville dies on the moor under mysterious circumstances and rumors abound about a demonic hound. When the American heir arrives to take charge, a family friend calls in Holmes and Watson to get to the heart of the mystery.
