Title: Gabriel (2007)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Action, Science Fiction
Director: Shane Abbess
Main Cast: Dwaine Stevenson, Jack Campbell, Erika Heynatz, Andy Whitfield, Samantha Noble
Keywords: archangel gabriel, heaven, matter of life and death, hell, good and bad

Overview: Gabriel tells the story of an archangel who fights to bring light back to purgatory - a place where darkness rules - and save the souls of the city's inhabitants.
