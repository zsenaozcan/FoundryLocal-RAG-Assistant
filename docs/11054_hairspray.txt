Title: Hairspray (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Music
Director: John Waters
Main Cast: Sonny Bono, Ruth Brown, Divine, Deborah Harry, Ricki Lake
Keywords: baltimore, integration, racial segregation, detention, woman played by man, cross cultural relations, interracial love, cult classic, interracial friendship, fashion model

Overview: Ample teen Tracy Turnblad wants nothing more than to be on the hip local TV dance program, "The Corny Collins Show" -- and when her dream comes true, her lively moves and bubbly personality meet with unexpected popularity. But after witnessing firsthand the terrible state of race relations in 1960s Baltimore, Turnblad becomes an outspoken advocate for desegregation.
