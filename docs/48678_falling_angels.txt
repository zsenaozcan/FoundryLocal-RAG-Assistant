Title: Falling Angels (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Scott Smith
Main Cast: Miranda Richardson, Callum Keith Rennie, Katharine Isabelle, Kristin Adams

Overview: The wickedly funny story of three sisters' coming of age in a wildly dysfunctional family, set against the backdrop of the 60's, free love, the Cold War, LSD and the dawn of feminism. Affectionately dubbed "Little Women on acid", this is a story about the destructive effects of secrecy and the bonds of duty between parents and their children.
