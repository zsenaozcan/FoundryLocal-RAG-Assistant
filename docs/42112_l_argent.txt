Title: L’argent (1983)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Robert Bresson
Main Cast: Christian Patey, Vincent Risterucci, Sylvie Van den Elsen, Michel Briguet, Caroline Lang
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A forged 500-franc note is cynically passed from person to person and shop to shop, until it falls into the hands of a genuine innocent who doesn't see it for what it is - which will have devastating consequences on his life, causing him to turn to crime and murder.
