Title: Last Stand at Saber River (1997)
Genres: Western, Action, Drama
Director: Dick Lowry
Main Cast: Tom Selleck, Suzy Amis, Rachel Duncan, Haley Joel Osment, Keith Carradine

Overview: As America recovers from the Civil War, one man tries to put the pieces of his life back together but finds himself fighting a new battle on the frontier. Cable is an embittered Confederate soldier who returns from the war to reclaim his Arizona homestead from rebel pioneers who sympathize with the Union war effort.
