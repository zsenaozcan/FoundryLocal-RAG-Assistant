Title: Jack Frost (1997)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Comedy
Director: Michael Cooney
Main Cast: Scott MacDonald, Christopher Allport, Stephen Mendel, F. William Parker, Eileen Seeley
Keywords: snowman, serial killer

Overview: Serial killer is genetically mutated in car wreck on the way to his execution. After which, he becomes a murdering snowman hell-bent on revenge for the sheriff who caught him.
