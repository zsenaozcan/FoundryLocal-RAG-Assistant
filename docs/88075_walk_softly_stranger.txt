Title: Walk Softly, Stranger (1950)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Robert Stevenson
Main Cast: Joseph Cotten, Alida Valli, Spring Byington, Paul Stewart, Jack Paar
Keywords: gambling, wheelchair, handicap, film noir
