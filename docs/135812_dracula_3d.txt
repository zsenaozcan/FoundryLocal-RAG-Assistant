Title: Dracula 3D (2012)
Genres: Horror, Romance, Thriller
Director: Dario Argento
Main Cast: Thomas Kretschmann, Asia Argento, Rutger Hauer, Marta Gastini, Unax Ugalde
Keywords: dracula, gothic horror, evil, bite mark, 3d, vlad

Overview: When Englishman Jonathan Harker visits the exotic castle of Count Dracula, he is entranced by the mysterious aristocrat. But upon learning that the count has sinister designs on his wife, Mina, Harker seeks help from vampire slayer Van Helsing.
