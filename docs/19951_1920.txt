Title: 1920 (2008)
Genres: Horror
Director: Vikram Bhatt
Main Cast: Rajneesh Duggal, Adah Sharma, Anjori Alagh, Rajendranath Zutshi, Vipin Sharma
Keywords: horror, bollywood

Overview: The year is 1920 and the house isolated in the wilderness has a secret. It is waiting for the curse to come true. For years, everyone who has bought the house and tried to pull it down has died of mysterious circumstances. It is like the house has a will and a life of its own.
