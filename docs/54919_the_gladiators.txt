Title: The Gladiators (1969)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction, War
Director: Peter Watkins
Main Cast: Arthur Pentelow, Frederick Danner, Hans Bendrik, Daniel Harle
Keywords: television, fictional game show, soldier, war game

Overview: Some time in the future, East and West have stopped maintaining standing armies and nuclear weapons. Instead, to settle their differences they pit different teams of crack combat specialists against each other.
