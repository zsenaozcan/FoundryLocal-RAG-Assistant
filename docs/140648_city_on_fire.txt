Title: City on Fire (1979)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Alvin Rakoff
Main Cast: Barry Newman, Susan Clark, Shelley Winters, Leslie Nielsen, James Franciscus
Keywords: fire, firemen, fire engine, revenge, rescue, disaster, arson, hospital, explosion, trapped

Overview: A pyromaniac, ex-employee of a city oil refinery creates an explosion at the facility which starts a chain-reaction of fires that engulf the entire city.
