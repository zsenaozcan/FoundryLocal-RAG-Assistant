Title: The Concorde... Airport '79 (1979)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: David Lowell Rich
Main Cast: Alain Delon, Susan Blakely, Robert Wagner, Sylvia Kristel, George Kennedy
Keywords: stewardess, missile, pilot, hot air balloon, panic, arms dealer, fighter jet, airplane accident, drone, sabotage

Overview: The last of the 'Airport' series again stars George Kennedy as aviation disaster-prone Joe Patroni, this time having to contend with nuclear missiles, the French Air Force and the threat of the plane splitting in two over the Alps!
