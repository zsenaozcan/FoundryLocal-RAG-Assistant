Title: Twice Upon a Yesterday (1998)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: María Ripoll
Main Cast: Lena Headey, Douglas Henshall, Penélope Cruz, Charlotte Coleman, Gustavo Salmerón
Keywords: ex-girlfriend, love, friends, destiny, spanish, woman director, actor

Overview: Victor Bukowski is an out-of-work actor with problems. He's got a lousy agent, he has a habit of falling out with directors and he's still in love with his ex-girlfriend. However, Victor is about to embark on an unexpected emotional journey which will make him confront his future and his past mistakes.
