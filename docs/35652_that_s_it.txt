Title: That's It (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Matheus Souza
Main Cast: Gregório Duvivier, Érika Mader, Nathalia Dill, Marcelo Adnet, Álamo Facó
Keywords: romantic comedy

Overview: Girl is decided to leave her boyfriend and to runaway to an unknown place. Before she leaves, she decides to meet up with him, but they have only one hour to make a very funny balance of their lives.
