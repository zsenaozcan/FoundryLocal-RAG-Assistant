Title: Clonehunter (2009)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Andrew Bellware
Main Cast: Jef Betz, Greg Oliver Bodine, H. R. Britton, Nadia Dassouki, Angela Funk

Overview: 2525 A.D. Man has colonized the stars. The wealthy and powerful implant their brains in cloned versions of themselves to gain immortality. As a side-effect, occasionally a clone develops mutant abilities. A clonehunter and his new partner have to track down a clone who threatens to destroy the planet unless the rich man he was cloned for gives him all his wealth.
