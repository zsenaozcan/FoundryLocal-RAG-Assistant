Title: Summer Stock (1950)
Genres: Music, Romance
Director: Charles Walters
Main Cast: Judy Garland, Gene Kelly, Eddie Bracken, Gloria DeHaven, Marjorie Main
Keywords: barn, musical, farm, play, new england, actress, summer stock

Overview: To Jane Falbury's New England farm comes a troup of actors to put up a show, invited by Jane's sister. At first reluctant she has them do farm chores in exchange for food. Her reluctance becomes attraction when she falls in love with the director, Joe, who happens to be her sister's fiance.
