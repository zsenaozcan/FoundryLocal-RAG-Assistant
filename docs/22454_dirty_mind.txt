Title: Dirty Mind (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Foreign
Director: Pieter Van Hees
Main Cast: Wim Helsen, Robbie Cleiren, Kristine Van Pellicom, Peter van den Begin
Keywords: stuntman, brainwashing

Overview: Shy loser Diego wakes up after an accident and has been transformed into Tony T. a smooth-talking no-holds-barred stuntman. Suffering from a case of Frontal Syndrome he's the perfect case-study for young neurologist Jaana. Everyone hopes that Diego will soon recover, except for Diego himself.
