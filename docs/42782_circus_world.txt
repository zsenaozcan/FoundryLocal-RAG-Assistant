Title: Circus World (1964)
Genres: Western, Drama
Director: Henry Hathaway
Main Cast: John Wayne, Claudia Cardinale, Rita Hayworth, Lloyd Nolan, Richard Conte
Keywords: wild west show, trapeze

Overview: Circus owner Matt Masters is beset by disasters as he attempts a European tour of his circus. At the same time, he is caught in an emotional bind between his adopted daughter and her mother.
