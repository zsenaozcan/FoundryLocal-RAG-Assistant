Title: Santitos (1999)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Romance
Director: Alejandro Springall
Main Cast: Dolores Heredia, Demián Bichir, Alberto Estrella, Pedro Altamirano, Roberto Cobo

Overview: After St. Jude appears in Esperanza's oven, she sets out to find her daughter, who died under mysterious circumstances. The journey forces Esperanza to challenge her own beliefs and face her fears in order to be reunited with her beloved daughter.
