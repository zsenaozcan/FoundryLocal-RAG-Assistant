Title: Desperate Search (1952)
Genres: Drama, Adventure
Director: Joseph H. Lewis
Main Cast: Howard Keel, Jane Greer, Patricia Medina, Keenan Wynn, Robert Burton
Keywords: airplane crash, cougar

Overview: A man (Howard Keel), his wife (Jane Greer) and his famous-aviator ex-wife (Patricia Medina) search for their two children lost with a cougar.
