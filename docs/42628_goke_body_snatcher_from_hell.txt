Title: Goke, Body Snatcher from Hell (1968)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Hajime Sato
Main Cast: Teruo Yoshida, Tomomi Satô, Eizo Kitamura, Hideo Kô, Kathy Horan
Keywords: vampire, blob, airplane crash, alien invasion

Overview: The survivors of a plane crash in a remote area are attacked by blob-like alien creatures that turn their victims into blood-thirsty vampires.
