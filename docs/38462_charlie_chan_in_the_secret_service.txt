Title: Charlie Chan in the Secret Service (1944)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Mystery
Director: Phil Rosen
Main Cast: Sidney Toler, Mantan Moreland, Arthur Loft, Gwen Kenyon, Sarah Edwards
Keywords: charlie chan

Overview: Charlie Chan is an agent of the U.S. government working in Washington DC and he is assigned to investigate the murder of the inventor of a highly advanced torpedo. Aiding Chan is his overeager but dull-witted Number Three son Tommy (Benson Fong) and his Number Two Daughter Iris Chan (Marianne Quon).
