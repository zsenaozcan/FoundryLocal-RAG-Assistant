Title: Sube y baja (1959)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Miguel M. Delgado
Main Cast: Cantinflas, Tere Velázquez, Joaquín García Vargas, Carlos Agostí, Domingo Soler
Keywords: sport, mistaken identity, elevator, acapulco mexico

Overview: It is the story of a sportsman who is a little bit dumber than most people but nevertheless he succeeds in getting a job at a sports article saler's. Having this job is the beginning of a lot of funny problems he causes due to his dumbness.
