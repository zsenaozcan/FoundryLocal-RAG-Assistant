Title: The Way You Wanted Me (1944)
Genres: Drama
Director: Teuvo Tulio
Main Cast: Marie-Louise Fock, Ture Ara, Kunto Karapää, Lauri Korpela, Annie Sundman
Keywords: older man younger woman relationship, city as place of sin

Overview: The plot involves an innocent island girl who moves to the city after her fiancé lacks the courage to face his father who is against their relationship. In the city the girl winds up being abused by men, giving birth to a child out of wedlock, and supporting herself on prostitution.
