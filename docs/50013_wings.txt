Title: Wings (1966)
Genres: Drama
Director: Larisa Shepitko
Main Cast: Maya Bulgakova, Sergey Nikonenko, Zhanna Bolotova, Pantelejmon Krymov, Leonid Dyachkov
Keywords: biography, nostalgia, drama, aviation, independent film, school principal, woman director

Overview: Maya Bulgakova gives a marvelous performance as a once heroic Russian fighter pilot now living a quiet, disappointingly ordinary life as a school principal.
