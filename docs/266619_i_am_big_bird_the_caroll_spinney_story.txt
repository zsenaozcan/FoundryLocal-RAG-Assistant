Title: I Am Big Bird: The Caroll Spinney Story (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Dave LaMattina
Main Cast: Caroll Spinney, Jim Henson, Joan Ganz Cooney, Frank Oz, Emilio Delgado

Overview: A documentary about Caroll Spinney who has been Sesame Street's Big Bird and Oscar the Grouch since 1969. At 78-years-old, he has no intention of stopping.
