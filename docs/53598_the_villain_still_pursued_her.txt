Title: The Villain Still Pursued Her (1940)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Edward F. Cline
Main Cast: Billy Gilbert, Anita Louise, Margaret Hamilton, Alan Mowbray, Richard Cromwell
Keywords: melodrama, spoof, disinheritance

Overview: Victorian melodrama gets a big send-up in this spoof production of the old play "The Drunkard; or, The Fallen Saved." The play within the movie is the old one where evil villain Cribbs schemes to get his lusty clutches on the heroine by driving her naive husband to alcoholic ruin. Luckily, a temperance lecturer is on hand to set things straight, as is the great Buster Keaton as the drunkard's brother.
