Title: A Madea Christmas (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Tyler Perry
Main Cast: Tyler Perry, Shannon Williams, Chandra Currelley-Young, Alexis Jones, Cassi Davis

Overview: When a family meets for Christmas at their posh Cape Cod estate, family arguments and secrets cause a stir. It takes a real down-to-earth family - like Aunt Bam and the almighty Madea - to save this holiday.
