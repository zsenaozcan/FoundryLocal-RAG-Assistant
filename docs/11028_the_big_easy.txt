Title: The Big Easy (1986)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime, Mystery
Director: Jim McBride
Main Cast: Dennis Quaid, Ellen Barkin, Ned Beatty, John Goodman, Lisa Jane Persky
Keywords: corruption, investigation, police, murder, gang

Overview: Remy McSwain is a New Orleans police lieutenant who investigates the murder of a local mobster. His investigation leads him to suspect that fellow members of the police force may be involved.
