Title: Nearlyweds (2013)
Genres: Romance, TV Movie
Director: Mark Griffiths
Main Cast: Danielle Panabaker, Jessica Parker Kennedy, Brittney Irvin, Ryan Kennedy, Travis Milne
Keywords: based on novel, wedding

Overview: Revolves around three friends who have dream weddings only to find out that the pastor who married them failed to complete the key paperwork required to make them official.
