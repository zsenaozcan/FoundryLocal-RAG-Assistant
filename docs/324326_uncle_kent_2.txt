Title: Uncle Kent 2 (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Todd Rohal
Main Cast: Sophia Takal, Tom Fitzpatrick, Jennifer Prediger, Robert Longstreet, Kent Osborne

Overview: In a desperate search to create a follow-up to Joe Swanberg's 2011 film Uncle Kent, Kent Osborne travels to a comic book convention in San Diego where he loses his mind and confronts the end of the world.
