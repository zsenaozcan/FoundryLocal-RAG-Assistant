Title: Red: Werewolf Hunter (2010)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Action, Thriller
Director: Sheldon Wilson
Main Cast: Felicia Day, Kavan Smith, Stephen McHattie, Greg Bryk, Rosemary Dunsmore
Keywords: red hair, fairy tale, warrior woman, little red riding hood, red, werewolf

Overview: The modern-day descendant of Little Red Riding Hood brings her fiancé home to meet her family and reveal their occupation as werewolf hunters, but after he is bitten by a werewolf, she must protect him from her own family.
