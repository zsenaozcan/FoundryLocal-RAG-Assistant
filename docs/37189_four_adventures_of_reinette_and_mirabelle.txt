Title: Four Adventures of Reinette and Mirabelle (1987)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Éric Rohmer
Main Cast: Joëlle Miquel, Jessica Forde, Philippe Laudenbach, Fabrice Luchini, Marie Rivière

Overview: Two young girls meet, Reinette from the countryside and Mirabelle from Paris, and decide to take a flat together in Paris where they attend University. Four successive stories about their daily lives illustrate the very different views, characters and relation to the world of these two friends.
