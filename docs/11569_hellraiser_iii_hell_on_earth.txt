Title: Hellraiser III: Hell on Earth (1992)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, War
Director: Anthony Hickox
Main Cast: Kevin Bernhardt, Terry Farrell, Ken Carpenter, Doug Bradley, Paula Marshall
Keywords: nightclub, game of dice, scuplture, pinhead, cenobyten, reporter, puzzle box, evil, cenobites

Overview: Pinhead is trapped in a sculpture and fortunately for him the sculpture is bought by a young playboy who owns his own night club. Pinhead busies himself escaping by getting the playboy to lure victims to his presence so he can use their blood. Once free, he seeks to destroy the puzzle cube so he need never return to Hell, but a female reporter is investigating the grisly murders and stands in his way.
