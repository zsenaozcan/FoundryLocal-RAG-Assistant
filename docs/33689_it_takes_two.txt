Title: It Takes Two (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Romance
Director: Andy Tennant
Main Cast: Kirstie Alley, Steve Guttenberg, Ashley Olsen, Mary-Kate Olsen, Jane Sibbett
Keywords: orphan, identical twin

Overview: Identical 9-year-olds from very different backgrounds: orphaned Amanda and wealthy Alyssa meet at summer camp and decide to switch places -- and play matchmaker between Alyssa's dad, Roger, and the kind social worker who cares for Amanda.
