Title: Burning Secret (1988)
Genres: Drama
Director: Andrew Birkin
Main Cast: David Eberts, Faye Dunaway, Klaus Maria Brandauer, Ian Richardson, John Nettleton
Keywords: austria, american diplomat

Overview: Burning Secret is a 1988 drama film, based on the short story Brennendes Geheimnis by Stefan Zweig, about an American diplomat's son who befriends a mysterious baron while staying at an Austrian spa during the 1920s. The film was written and directed by Andrew Birkin.
