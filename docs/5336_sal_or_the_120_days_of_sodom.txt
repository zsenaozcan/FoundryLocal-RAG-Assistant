Genres: History, War, Drama, Horror
Director: Pier Paolo Pasolini
Main Cast: Paolo Bonacelli, Giorgio Cataldi, Umberto Paolo Quintavalle, Aldo Valletti, Hélène Surgère
Keywords: italy, rape, fascism, nudity, bishop, fascist, sadism, torture, sexual torture, libertine
