Title: Bullet in the Head (1990)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: John Woo
Main Cast: Tony Leung Chiu-Wai, Jacky Cheung, Waise Lee, Simon Yam, Fennie Yuen
Keywords: killing, vietnam, vietnam war

Overview: When three close friends escape from Hong Kong to war-time Saigon to start a criminal's life, they all go through a harrowing experience which totally shatters their lives and their friendship forever.
