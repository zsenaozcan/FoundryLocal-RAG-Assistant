Title: For Here or to Go? (2017)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Rucha Humnabadkar
Main Cast: Ali Fazal, Melanie Chandra, Omi Vaidya, Rajit Kapoor, Samrat Chakrabarti
Keywords: immigration, silicon valley, entrepreneurship, woman director

Overview: An aspiring Indian tech entrepreneur in the Silicon Valley finds himself unexpectedly battling the bizarre American immigration system to keep his dream alive or prepare to return home forever.
