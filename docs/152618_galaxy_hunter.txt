Title: Galaxy Hunter (2004)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Mark Borchetta
Main Cast: Shelley Michelle, Stacy Keach, Alain Benatar, Tiffany Hendra, Michelle Betts
Keywords: bounty hunter, secret agent, spaceship

Overview: Well into the future, a senior interplanetary investigator, 3V3, sends a distress signal. Ginger, a tough and ravishing stellar investigator takes the assignment and follows the signal to a planet where a nasty villain, Zidian, is brewing and exporting an addictive drug called "toad." It's also he who has captured 3V3. Ginger recruits four tough female bounty hunters. We learn more about 3V3's identity and Ginger's skills at entrapment. Plus, there's Ray, a bartender who has a history with Ginger. Can the five women find and rescue 3V3, foil Zidian's plans, and escape with their lives?
