Title: Operation Crossbow (1965)
Genres: Action, Thriller, War
Director: Michael Anderson
Main Cast: Sophia Loren, George Peppard, Trevor Howard, John Mills, Richard Johnson
Keywords: german, nazis, rocket

Overview: Allied agents infiltrate the Nazi rocket complex at Peenemunde in order to obtain their secrets and sabotage the plant.The film alternates between German developments of the V-1 missile and V-2 rocket (with a German cast speaking their own language) and discovery by British Intelligence of the weapon.
