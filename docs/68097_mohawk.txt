Title: Mohawk (1956)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Kurt Neumann
Main Cast: Scott Brady, Rita Gam, Neville Brand, Lori Nelson, Allison Hayes
Keywords: artist, fort, native american

Overview: An artist working in a remote army post is juggling the storekeeper's daughter, his fiancée newly arrived from the east, and the Indian Chief's daughter. But when a vengeful settler manages to get the army and the braves at each other's throats his troubles really begin.
