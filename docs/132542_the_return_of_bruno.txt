Title: The Return of Bruno (1987)
Genres: Unknown
Director: James Yukich
Main Cast: Bruce Willis, Michael J. Fox, Elton John, Bill Graham, Dick Clark

Overview: A "documentary" about the major influence that a '60s rock singer named Bruno has had on rock music, as attested to by such rock legends as Ringo Starr, Brian Wilson and the Bee Gees, among others.
