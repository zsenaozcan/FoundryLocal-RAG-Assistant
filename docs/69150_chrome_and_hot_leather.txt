Title: Chrome and Hot Leather (1971)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Lee Frost
Main Cast: William Smith, Tony Young, Michael Haynes, Peter Brown, Larry Bishop
Keywords: biker, revenge, green beret

Overview: A Green Beret returns home from the Vietnam war to find that a gang of murderous bikers has killed his fiancee. He calls on several of his Green Beret buddies to come and help him take revenge on the gang.
