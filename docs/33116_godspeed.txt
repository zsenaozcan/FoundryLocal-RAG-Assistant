Title: Godspeed (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Robert Saitzyk
Main Cast: Cory Knauf, Ed Lauter, Courtney Halverson, Jessie Ward, Joseph McKelheer
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Joseph McKelheer and Cory Knauf (The Hamiltons, The Violent Kind) star in this intense thriller set in the lingering light of the Alaskan midnight sun. Charlie Shepard, a modern day faith healer with a small flock of believers, is stunned when his family is brutally murdered.  Six months later a young, mysterious girl (Courtney Halverson) appears and becomes the link to his family's killing and the murderers themselves, ultimately leading to violent consequences for all.
