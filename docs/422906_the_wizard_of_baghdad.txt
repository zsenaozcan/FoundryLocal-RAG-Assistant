Title: The Wizard of Baghdad (1961)
Genres: Comedy
Director: George Sherman
Main Cast: Diane Baker, Dick Shawn, Barry Coe, Robert F. Simon, Vaughn Taylor
Keywords: magic, archery, genie, crystal ball, wine shop

Overview: A genie turned mortal after his many failures is sent to Baghdad. As his last chance to prove himself he must help a prince and princess fulfill a prophecy.
