Title: What Is to Be Done? (1970)
Genres: Drama
Director: Raúl Ruiz
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Mordant, self-aware, freighted with sensitivity toward Chile’s problem, wary of caricature, disposed toward consciousness of human fallibility, it is a deft blend of fiction and documentary set in the tumultuous days leading up to the election of Dr. Allende in 1970.
