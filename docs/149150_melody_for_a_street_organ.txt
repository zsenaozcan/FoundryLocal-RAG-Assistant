Title: Melody for a Street Organ (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kira Muratova
Main Cast: Lena Kostyuk, Roma Burlaka, Natalya Buzko, Zhan Daniel, Georgiy Deliev
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Two young orphan siblings travel to Moscow in search of their missing father. Scared of being separated and sent to orphanages, they hope to reunite with the last link of their shattered family.
