Title: The Sun (2005)
Genres: Drama, History, Foreign
Director: Alexander Sokurov
Main Cast: Issei Ogata, Robert Dawson, Kaori Momoi, Shirô Sano

Overview: The Sun (Russian: Сóлнце, Solntse) is a 2005 Russian biographical film depicting Japanese Emperor Shōwa (Hirohito) during the final days of World War II. The film is the third drama in director Aleksandr Sokurov's trilogy, which included Taurus about the Soviet Union's Vladimir Lenin and Moloch about Nazi Germany's Adolf Hitler.
