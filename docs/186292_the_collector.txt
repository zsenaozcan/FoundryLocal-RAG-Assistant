Title: The Collector (1997)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Auli Mantila
Main Cast: Leea Klemola, Elina Hurme, Rea Mauranen, Robin Svartström, Henriikka Salo
Keywords: arsonist, mental instability, woman director, butterfly collector

Overview: Eevi's life isn't going the way she would want it to, so she runs away, leaving destruction in her wake. No one will ever say no to her again.
