Title: War (2002)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Aleksey Balabanov
Main Cast: Aleksey Chadov, Ian Kelly, Ingeborga Dapkunaite, Sergei Bodrov Jr., Yuri Stepanov

Overview: During the bloody war in Chechnya, a British couple and two Russian soldiers are taken hostage by Chechen rebels. Two of the hostages are then released to bring the money for the British woman who is forced to wait for the ransom.
