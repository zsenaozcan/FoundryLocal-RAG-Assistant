Title: Bluebeard (1963)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Claude Chabrol
Main Cast: Charles Denner, Stéphane Audran, Michèle Morgan, Juliette Mayniel, Catherine Rouvel
Keywords: court case, biography

Overview: France, WWI. Landru, the father of four Children, contacts Parisian women through newspapers, seduces and eventually kills them in order to feed his little family.
