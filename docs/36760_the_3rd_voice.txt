Title: The 3rd Voice (1960)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime
Director: Hubert Cornfield
Main Cast: Edmond O'Brien, Julie London, Laraine Day, Olga San Juan, George Eldredge
Keywords: mexico, bar, double crossed

Overview: Marian Forbes has been having an affair with her boss and when he drops her for another woman. In an act of jealousy and greed she convinces an acquaintance to murder her former lover and then impersonate him just long enough to get their hands on a large sum of money.
