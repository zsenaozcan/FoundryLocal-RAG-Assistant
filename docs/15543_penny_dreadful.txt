Title: Penny Dreadful (2006)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Richard Brandes
Main Cast: Rachel Miner, Mimi Rogers, Chad Todhunter, Mickey Jones, Liz Davies
Keywords: phobia, psychologist

Overview: Young Penny goes on a retreat with her psychologist; the intention is to help her overcome her phobia, an intense fear of cars. Unexpected events find her in a nightmarish situation where her worst fears come true.
