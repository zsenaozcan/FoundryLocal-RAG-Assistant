Title: How the Grinch Stole Christmas! (1966)
Genres: Animation, Family, Comedy
Director: Chuck Jones
Main Cast: Boris Karloff, June Foray, Thurl Ravenscroft, Dal McKennon
Keywords: holiday, based on novel, monster, obsession, santa claus, heart, materialism, sleigh, christmas tree, affection

Overview: Bitter and hateful, the Grinch is irritated at the thought of the nearby village having a happy time celebrating Christmas. So disguised as Santa Claus, with his dog made to look like a reindeer, he raids the village to steal all the Christmas things. The village is sure to have a sad Christmas this year.
