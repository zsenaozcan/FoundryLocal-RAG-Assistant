Title: The Good Boy (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Oksana Karas
Main Cast: Semyon Treskunov, Anastasiya Bogatyreva, Ieva Andrejevaite, Konstantin Khabenskiy, Irina Denisova

Overview: Contemporary Moscow. Ninth-grader Kolya falls in love with a beautiful young teacher at his high school. The most beautiful girl in the senior class falls in love with him. How will he make his way through this triangle?
