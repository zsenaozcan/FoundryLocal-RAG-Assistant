Title: Up Periscope (1959)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Thriller, War
Director: Gordon Douglas
Main Cast: James Garner, Edmond O'Brien, Andra Martin, Alan Hale Jr., Edd Byrnes
Keywords: world war ii, u.s. navy, pacific theater, frogmen

Overview: Lieutenant Braden discovers that Sally, the woman he's been falling in love with, has actually been checking out his qualifications to be a U.S. Navy frogman. He must put his personal life behind him after being assigned to be smuggled into a Japanese-held island via submarine to photograph radio codes.
