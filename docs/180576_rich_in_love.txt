Title: Rich in Love (1992)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bruce Beresford
Main Cast: Albert Finney, Jill Clayburgh, Kathryn Erbe, Kyle MacLachlan, Piper Laurie
Keywords: south carolina, family secrets

Overview: The legendary Albert Finney heads an all-star cast (Jill Clayburgh, Kyle MacLachlan and Ethan Hawke) in this heartwarming drama of a retired man whose family blossoms after his wife leaves them.
