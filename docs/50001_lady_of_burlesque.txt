Title: Lady of Burlesque (1943)
Genres: Comedy, Thriller, Mystery, Romance
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: Barbara Stanwyck, Michael O'Shea, Iris Adrian, Charles Dingle, J. Edward Bromberg
Keywords: burlesque

Overview: After one member of their group is murdered, the performers at a burlesque house must work together to find out who the killer is before they strike again.
