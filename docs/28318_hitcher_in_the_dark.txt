Title: Hitcher in the Dark (1989)
Genres: Crime, Horror, Thriller
Director: Umberto Lenzi
Main Cast: Joe Balogh, Josie Bissett, Jason Saucier, Robin Fox, Gary Wade Morton
Keywords: kidnapping, caravan, murder, giallo, surprise ending, captive, hitchhiking, female serial killer

Overview: Sick young man drives around in his daddy's camper, looking for lone stray females to kidnap, torture, rape, and murder.
