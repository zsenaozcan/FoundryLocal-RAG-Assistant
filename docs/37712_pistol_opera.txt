Title: Pistol Opera (2001)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime, Foreign
Director: Seijun Suzuki
Main Cast: Makiko Esumi, Sayoko Yamaguchi, Hanae Kan, Masatoshi Nagase, Mikijiro Hira
Keywords: assassin, sequel, surrealism, avant-garde, psychedelic

Overview: As one of Suzuki's last fims, it is related to his 1967 Branded to Kill, either as a remake or sequel. The plots of both films involve a third-ranked hit man deposing the top-ranked hit man to claim the top rank.
