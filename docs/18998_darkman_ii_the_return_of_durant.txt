Title: Darkman II: The Return of Durant (1995)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Bradford May
Main Cast: Arnold Vosloo, Larry Drake, Kim Delaney, Renee O'Connor, Lawrence Dane
Keywords: experiment, sequel, superhero, darkman

Overview: Darkman and Durant return and they hate each other as much as ever. This time, Durant has plans to take over the city's drug trade using high-tech weaponry. Darkman must step in and try to stop Durant once and for all.
