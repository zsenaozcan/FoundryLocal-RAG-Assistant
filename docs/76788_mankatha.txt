Title: Mankatha (2011)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Venkat Prabhu
Main Cast: Ajith Kumar, Arjun Sarja, Trisha Krishnan, Vaibhav Reddy, Mahat Raghavendra
Keywords: gambling, money laundering, cricket, heist, bad cop, cops and robbers , bookie

Overview: Vinayak and his 4 team members rob a booty of 500 crores INR. Trouble starts when two of the team betrays them.
