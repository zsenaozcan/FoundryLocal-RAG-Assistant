Title: Demon Hunter (2012)
Genres: Action, Horror
Director: Rene Perez
Main Cast: Robert Amstler, Jenny Allford, Raven Lexy

Overview: Cosette is cursed. Demons Ghouls and Ghosts from the darkest corners of Hell are summoned to bring her to the other side. Now Cosette and Klaus her protector must face an unspeakable evil in an attempt to free her from this demonic curse.
