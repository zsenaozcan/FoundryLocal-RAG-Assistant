Title: Duffy (1968)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Robert Parrish
Main Cast: James Coburn, James Mason, James Fox, Susannah York, John Alderton
Keywords: boat, heist, hijack

Overview: Duffy is a cunning aristocrat of criminals who is hired by Stefane, a young playboy, to hijack a boat carrying several million dollars of his father's fortune. The plot succeeds, with a little help from Segolene, Stefane's girlfriend - but also with an unexpected, sudden turn of events.
