Title: K2: Siren of the Himalayas (2012)
Genres: Adventure, Documentary
Director: Dave Ohlson
Main Cast: Gerlinde Kaltenbrunner, Simone Leorin, Jake Meyer, Chris Szymiec, Fabrizio Zangrilli
Keywords: pakistan, climbing, mountain climbing, mountaineering, karakoram

Overview: Following a group of climbers attempting to climb K2 in 2009, on the 100-year anniversary of its landmark 1909 expedition. Experience the adventure, peril and serenity of a group's attempt to climb the most challenging peak on earth.
