Title: The Oath (2010)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Laura Poitras
Main Cast: Osama Bin Laden
Keywords: terrorism, yemen, woman director, al qaeda, post 9/11

Overview: Tells the story of two men, Abu Jandal and Salim Ahmed Hamdan, whose fateful encounter in 1996 set them on a course of events that led them to Afghanistan, Osama bin Laden, 9/11, Guantanamo, and the U.S. Supreme Court.
