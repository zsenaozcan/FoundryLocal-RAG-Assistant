Title: Dragon Ball Z: Broly - Second Coming (1994)
Genres: Action, Animation
Director: Shigeyasu Yamauchi
Main Cast: Bin Shimada, Masako Nozawa, Takeshi Kusao, Kara Edwards
Keywords: anime, dragon ball,

Overview: The film opens with a Saiyan Space pod flying through space and crash-landing on Earth out of which a wounded Saiyan crawls out: Broly, the Legendary Super Saiyan. The wounded Broly shouts out in frustation and turns into normal form. The place soon freezes, trapping him in it and he falls into a coma.
