Title: Curry and Pepper (1990)
Genres: Foreign, Comedy, Action
Director: Blacky Ko Sau-Leung
Main Cast: Jacky Cheung, Stephen Chow, Ann Bridgewater, Blacky Ko Sau-Leung, Eric Tsang

Overview: Two cops--both best friends and partners--both become the subject of a beautiful news reporter's documentary. The two soon find themselves competing for screen time as well as the love of the reporter while after a murderous arms dealer.
