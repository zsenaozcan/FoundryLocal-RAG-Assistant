Title: Colour Me Kubrick (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Brian W. Cook
Main Cast: John Malkovich, Sam Redford, Tom Allen, Lynda Baron, Linda Bassett

Overview: The true story of a man who posed as director Stanley Kubrick during the production of Kubrick's last film, Eyes Wide Shut, despite knowing very little about his work and looking nothing like him.
