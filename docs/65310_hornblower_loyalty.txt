Title: Hornblower: Loyalty (2003)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, War
Director: Andrew Grieve
Main Cast: Ioan Gruffudd, Robert Lindsay, Paul McGann, Lorcan Cranitch, Tony Haygarth

Overview: Hornblower is given a dangerous mission to deliver an emigre French nobleman to a secret rendezvous near Brest while coping with enemy agents in his own ranks.
