Title: 2 Become 1 (2006)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Law Wing-cheong
Main Cast: Miriam Yeung, Richie Ren, Jo Kuk, Guo Tao, Victoria Wu Yu-Chun

Overview: Bingo leads a carefree city life that revolves around work, friends and dating. Her yuppie life is turned upside down one passionate night when her hot date Dr. V discovers a lump on her left breast.
