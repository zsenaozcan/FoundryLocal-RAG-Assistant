Title: Drifters (2015)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Peter Grönlund
Main Cast: Malin Levanon, Lo Kauppi, Kalled Mustonen, Jan Mattson, Niklas Björklund
Keywords: drug addiction, friendship, prostitution, drug, social drama

Overview: Minna, a drug addict who makes a living dealing drugs, can't pay rent and after cheating some delinquents she runs with the money. She meets Katja and they move together to an illegal residence outside the city, where a group of people have decided to join and live by their own rules.
