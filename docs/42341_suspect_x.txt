Title: Suspect X (2008)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Hiroshi Nishitani
Main Cast: Masaharu Fukuyama, Kazuki Kitamura, Yasuko Matsuyuki, Shinichi Tsutsumi, Dankan

Overview: A male corpse is discovered with a smashed face and burned hands. Strangely, the cause of death is determined to be strangulation. When Detective Kaoru Utsumi attempts to corroborate the victim’s ex-wife’s alibi she discovers the mysterious neighbor and only a few small clues to help her disprove a seemingly "airtight" alibi...
