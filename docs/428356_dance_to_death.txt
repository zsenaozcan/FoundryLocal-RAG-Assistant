Title: Dance to Death (2017)
Genres: Action, Fantasy
Director: Andrey Volgin
Main Cast: Agniya Ditkovskite, Denis Shvedov, Anvar Khalilulaev, Lukerya Ilyashenko, Evgeniy Mikheev
Keywords: dancing, moscow, russian film

Overview: Deadly dance tournament is carried out in the city survived after nuclear apocalypse.  Energy is extracted from the loser for the city to survive. A young guy from the street is forced to take part in the tournament and falls in love with girl who volunteered to participate in the battle. Will they survive or the arena will take their lives?
