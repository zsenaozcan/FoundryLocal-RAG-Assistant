Title: The Police Serve the Citizens? (1973)
Genres: Foreign, Action, Crime
Director: Romolo Guerrieri
Main Cast: Enrico Maria Salerno, Giuseppe Pambieri, John Steiner, Venantino Venantini, Daniel Gélin
Keywords: corruption, chase, investigation, police, mafia, shootout, crime, violence, port
