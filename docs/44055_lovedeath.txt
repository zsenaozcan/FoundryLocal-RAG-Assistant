Title: LoveDeath (2006)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Ryûhei Kitamura
Main Cast: Shinji Takeda, Anouk Nora, Kohei Otomo, Kanako Fukaura, Masakatsu Funaki

Overview: A man and a woman are together on the 'Chrysalis Day', the fatal day everything in your life will be decided depends on how you spend the day. A violent man holding pure love, Sai and a stunningly beautiful playgirl, Sheila. While Sheila flees with a large sum of money, she is chased by a cool but violent gang, crazy killers and an out of control cop. No one can stop Sai and Sheila's love runaway.
