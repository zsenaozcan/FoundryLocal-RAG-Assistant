Title: Head Over Heels 2 (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Roberto Santucci
Main Cast: Ingrid Guimarães, Maria Paula, Luis Miranda, Bruno Garcia, Cristina Pereira
Keywords: brazilian, sequel, romance, comedy team

Overview: Alice owns a network of sex shops and workaholic who, in trying to reconcile the harsh routine of work and family life, suffers nervous breakdown, she is forced by her husband to go to spa. Precisely at this time, appears unique opportunity to expand its business in New York. Using fun gimmicks, risks his health, leaves spa party there with family to ride, but actually in order to facilitate their professional interests. In trying to reconcile the agendas, engage in hilarious situations and mistakes that culminate in the possible separation of the couple.
