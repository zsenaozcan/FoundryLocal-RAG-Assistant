Title: Shark in the Head (2004)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Maria Procházková
Main Cast: Oldřich Kaiser, Jana Krausová, Kristýna Leichtová, Jiří Lábus, Vlasta Žehrová
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Shark in the Head unfolds into a stunning cinematic powerhouse that combines comedy and drama for a touching story about how we influence the people we meet while being profoundly affected ourselves by the colors they bring to our own lives.
