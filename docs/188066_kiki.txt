Title: Kiki (1926)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Clarence Brown
Main Cast: Norma Talmadge, Ronald Colman, Gertrude Astor, Marc McDermott, George K. Arthur

Overview: Kiki, a poor young woman who sells newspapers on the street corners of Paris, is able to land a job singing and dancing at a nearby theater. While she is there, she invites herself into the life of the revue's manager, with whom she has fallen in love.
