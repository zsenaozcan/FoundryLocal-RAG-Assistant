Title: Romanovin kivet (1993)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Aleksi Mäkelä
Main Cast: Samuli Edelmann, Santeri Kinnunen, Stig Fransman, Kari-Pekka Toivonen, Katariina Kaitue

Overview: This action packed Finnish thriller tells the tale of two friends who get their revenge against the millionaire who double-crossed them. Patrick and Tony are hired by the wealthy gambler to steal the priceless Romanov stones, Russian jewels. They do it, but almost lose their lives when he double-crosses them. They turn around and get revenge, his money, his wife, and his daughter.
