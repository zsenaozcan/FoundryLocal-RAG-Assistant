Title: The Whoopee Boys (1986)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: John Byrum
Main Cast: Michael O'Keefe, Paul Rodríguez, Lucinda Jenney, Denholm Elliott, Carole Shelley
Keywords: orphanage, inheritance, high society, peddler

Overview: Two New York hustlers (Michael O'Keefe, Paul Rodriguez) go to Palm Beach, Fla., and enroll in a charm school to please an heiress.
