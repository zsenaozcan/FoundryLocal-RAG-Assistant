Title: Alice Through the Looking Glass (1998)
Genres: Family, Fantasy
Director: John Henderson
Main Cast: Kate Beckinsale, Geoffrey Palmer, Ian Holm, Steve Coogan, Penelope Wilton
Keywords: chess, queen, beautiful woman, alternate dimension, alice in wonderland, tea party

Overview: A modern adaptation of the classic children's story 'Alice through the Looking Glass', which continued on from the popular 'Alice in Wonderland' story. This time Alice is played by the mother, who falls asleep while reading the the bedtime story to her daughter. Walking through the Looking Glass, Alice finds herself in Chessland, a magical and fun world. There she meets the Red and White Queens, as well as many other amusing friends on her journey across the chessboard countryside onto become a crowned queen.
