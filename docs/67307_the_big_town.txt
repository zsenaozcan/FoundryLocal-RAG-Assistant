Title: The Big Town (1987)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Ben Bolt
Main Cast: Matt Dillon, Diane Lane, Tommy Lee Jones, Bruce Dern, Lee Grant
Keywords: gambler, craps

Overview: It is 1957. J.C. Cullen is a young man from a small town, with a talent for winning at craps, who leaves for the big city to work as a professional gambler. While there, he breaks the bank at a private craps game at the Gem Club, owned by George Cole, and falls in love with two women, one of them Cole's wife.
