Title: The Prowler (1981)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Mystery
Director: Joseph Zito
Main Cast: Vicky Dawson, Christopher Goutman, Lawrence Tierney, Farley Granger, Cindy Weintraub
Keywords: nudity, mask, murder, psychopathic killer, gore, serial killer, slasher, maniac, pitchfork

Overview: A masked killer, wearing World War II U.S. Army fatigues, stalks a small New Jersey town bent on reliving a 35-year-old double murder by focusing on a group of college kids holding an annual Spring Dance.
