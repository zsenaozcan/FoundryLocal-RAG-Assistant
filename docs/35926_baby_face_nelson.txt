Title: Baby Face Nelson (1957)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Don Siegel
Main Cast: Mickey Rooney, Carolyn Jones, Cedric Hardwicke, Leo Gordon, Anthony Caruso
Keywords: outlaw, gangster, film noir

Overview: Famed Depression-era gangster “Baby Face Nelson” (Mickey Rooney) robs and kills while accompanied by his beautiful moll (Carolyn Jones).
