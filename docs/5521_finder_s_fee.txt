Title: Finder's Fee (2001)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Jeff Probst
Main Cast: Erik Palladino, Dash Mihok, Carly Pope, Frances Bay, James Earl Jones
Keywords: poker, man, lottery, gain, suspense, independent film

Overview: After finding a wallet in the street tepper calls the owner in order to return it. After making the call he discovers that the lottery ticket inside is a $6 million winner. To add to things his friends are on their way over for their weekly poker night & the groups tradition is to bet their lottery ticket.
