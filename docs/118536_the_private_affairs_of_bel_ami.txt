Title: The Private Affairs of Bel Ami (1947)
Genres: Drama
Director: Albert Lewin
Main Cast: George Sanders, Angela Lansbury, Ann Dvorak, John Carradine, Susan Douglas Rubes
Keywords: journalist, manipulation, ambition, deception, duel, high society, rogue, scoundrel, war buddies, jilted woman

Overview: A self-serving journalist (George Sanders) uses influential women in late-1800s Paris and denies the one (Angela Lansbury) who truly loves him.
