Title: 38 Parrots (1976)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Ivan Ufimtsev
Main Cast: Vasiliy Livanov, Nadezhda Rumyantseva, Vsevolod Larionov, Mikhail Kozakov
Keywords: jungle

Overview: Parrot, Monkey and Elephant are excited to understand how long their friend Boa is. They finally arrive to the result, though it is measured in quite a strange fashion.
