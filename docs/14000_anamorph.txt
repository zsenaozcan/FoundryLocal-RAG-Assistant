Title: Anamorph (2007)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Henry Miller
Main Cast: Willem Dafoe, Scott Speedman, Peter Stormare, Clea DuVall, James Rebhorn
Keywords: detective inspector, artwork, suspense

Overview: A psychological thriller based on the concept of anamorphosis, a painting technique that manipulates the laws of perspective to create two competing images on a single canvas.
