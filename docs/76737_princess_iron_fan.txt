Title: Princess Iron Fan (1941)
Genres: Fantasy, Animation, Drama
Director: Wan Laiming
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: based on novel, mountain, travel, magical object

Overview: The story was liberally adapted from a short sequence in the popular Chinese folk tale Journey to the West. Princess Iron Fan is a main character. Specifically, the film focused on the duel between the Monkey King and a vengeful princess, whose fan is desperately needed to quench the flames that surround a peasant village.
