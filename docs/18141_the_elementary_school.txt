Title: The Elementary School (1991)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, War
Director: Jan Svěrák
Main Cast: Jan Tříska, Zdeněk Svěrák, Libuše Šafránková, Rudolf Hrušínský, Václav Jakoubek
Keywords: teacher, school, 1940s

Overview: The time is 1945-46. 10 year old Eda and his friend Tonda live in a small village outside Prague. In school, their class is so wild and indisciplined that their teacher quits and is replaced by the militant Igor Hnidzo. He is very strict - but also very just. His weakness is his interest in young women. Plot by Mattias Thuresson.
