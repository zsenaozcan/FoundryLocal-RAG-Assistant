Title: Janie Jones (2010)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: David M. Rosenthal
Main Cast: Abigail Breslin, Alessandro Nivola, Elisabeth Shue, Brittany Snow, Peter Stormare
Keywords: rock and roll, drug addiction, self-destruction, rock star, daughter
