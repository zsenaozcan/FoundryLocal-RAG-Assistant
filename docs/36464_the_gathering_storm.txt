Title: The Gathering Storm (2002)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Richard Loncraine
Main Cast: Albert Finney, Vanessa Redgrave, Jim Broadbent, Linus Roache, Lena Headey
Keywords: world war ii, history

Overview: A love story offering an intimate look inside the marriage of Winston and Clementine Churchill during a particularly troubled, though little-known, moment in their lives.
