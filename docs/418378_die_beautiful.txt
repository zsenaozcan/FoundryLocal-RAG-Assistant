Title: Die Beautiful (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jun Lana
Main Cast: Paolo Ballesteros, Joel Torre, Gladys Reyes, Luis Alandy, Albie Casiño
Keywords: gay, drag queen, funeral, beauty contest, friendship, philippines, family relationships, gay man, death, lgbt

Overview: Trisha, a Filipino transgender woman, suddenly dies while being crowned in a beauty pageant. Her last wish was to be presented as a different celebrity on each night of her wake.
