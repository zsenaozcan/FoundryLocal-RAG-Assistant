Title: Interceptor (1992)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Michael Cohn
Main Cast: Jürgen Prochnow, Andrew Divoff, Elizabeth Morehead, J. Kenneth Campbell

Overview: A U.S. Air Force officer tries to stop a murderous group of terrorists whom are trying to steal two top secret F-117A Stealth Fighters from the cargo hold of a gigantic C-5 Galaxy Transport flying at 30,000 feet over the Atlantic Ocean.
