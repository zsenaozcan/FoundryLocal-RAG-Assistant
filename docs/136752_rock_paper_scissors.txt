Title: Rock, Paper, Scissors (2012)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime
Director: Hernán Jabes
Main Cast: Leandro Arvelo, Ernesto Campos, Alberto Alifa, Jesús Careca, Julio César Castro
Keywords: venezuela, insecurity, destiny, random, day by day

Overview: The chance of an innocent child's game will unveil a betrayal that will forever change the lives of two families when their paths cross, endangering what they love the most, in a city that does not give them a truce.
