Title: Return to Montauk (2017)
Genres: Drama
Director: Volker Schlöndorff
Main Cast: Stellan Skarsgård, Nina Hoss, Bronagh Gallagher, Niels Arestrup, Susanne Wolff
Keywords: regret, writer

Overview: During a book tour in the United States, Max meets and falls in love with a young woman. Many years later, Max returns to the United States, hoping to reunite with his young lover.
