Title: The Matrix (1999)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Lana Wachowski
Main Cast: Keanu Reeves, Laurence Fishburne, Carrie-Anne Moss, Hugo Weaving, Gloria Foster
Keywords: saving the world, artificial intelligence, man vs machine, philosophy, prophecy, martial arts, self sacrifice, fight, insurgence, virtual reality

Overview: Set in the 22nd century, The Matrix tells the story of a computer hacker who joins a group of underground insurgents fighting the vast and powerful computers who now rule the earth.
