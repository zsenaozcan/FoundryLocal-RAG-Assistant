Title: Going Straight (1916)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Chester M. Franklin
Main Cast: Norma Talmadge, Ralph Lewis, Ninon Fovieri, Francis Carpenter, Fern Collier

Overview: A man and his wife both have criminal pasts, but have quit crime and are now respectable citizens. One day a member of their old gang shows up and threatens to expose them if they don't help him pull a heist.
