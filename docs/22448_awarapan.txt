Title: Awarapan (2007)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Mohit Suri
Main Cast: Emraan Hashmi, Mrinalini Sharma, Salil Acharya, Purab Kohli, Shriya Saran

Overview: Awarapan is the story of man called Shivam (Emraan Hashmi) who searched for joy but found nothing but pain and loneliness. It is the journey of a godless heartbroken lover who in order to escape from the ghosts of his tragic past dedicates himself to serve his gangster boss Malik who runs a chain of hotels in Hong Kong.
