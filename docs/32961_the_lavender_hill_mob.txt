Title: The Lavender Hill Mob (1951)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Charles Crichton
Main Cast: Alec Guinness, Stanley Holloway, Sid James, Alfie Bass, Marjorie Fielding
Keywords: france, bank, gold, souvenir, ealing

Overview: A meek bank clerk who oversees the shipment of bullion joins with an eccentric neighbor to steal gold bars and smuggle them out of the country as miniature Eiffel Towers.
