Title: Gurren Lagann The Movie: The Lights in the Sky Are Stars (2009)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Animation, Comedy
Director: Hiroyuki Imaishi
Main Cast: Tetsuya Kakihara, Katsuyuki Konishi, Yukari Fukui, Narushi Ikeda, Takaya Kamikawa

Overview: Seven years after the defeat of the spiral king, Simon and the Dai-Gurren brigade must set out to the vastness of space to defeat a new threat and save the universe.
