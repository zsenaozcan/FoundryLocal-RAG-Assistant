Title: Double Tide (2009)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Sharon Lockhart
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Artist and filmmaker Sharon Lockhart is known for creating beautiful, meditative films that incorporate subtle movement and a static, photographic gaze to examine her subjects. Her new film, Double Tide, documents the work of a female clam digger in the mudflats of coastal Maine and is filmed on the rare occasion in which low tide occurs twice within daylight hours—once at dawn and once at dusk.
