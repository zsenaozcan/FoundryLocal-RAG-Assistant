Title: Cyanide (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Séverine Cornamusaz
Main Cast: Alexandre Etzlinger, Roy Dupuis, Sabine Timoteo, Christophe Sermet, Ludivine Geschworner
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Thirteen years old Achille is waiting for his father to return from prison. Will everything go as planned and could the family survive?
