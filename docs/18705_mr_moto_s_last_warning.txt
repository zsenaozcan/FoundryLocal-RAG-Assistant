Title: Mr. Moto's Last Warning (1939)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Norman Foster
Main Cast: Peter Lorre, Ricardo Cortez, Virginia Field, John Carradine, George Sanders
Keywords: radio series, mr. moto

Overview: A Japanese man claiming to be Mr. Moto, of the International Police, is abducted and murdered soon after disembarking from a ship at Port Said in Egypt. The real Mr. Moto is already in Port Said, investigating a conspiracy against the British and French governments.
