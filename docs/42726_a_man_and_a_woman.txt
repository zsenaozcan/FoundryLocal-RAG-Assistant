Title: A Man and a Woman (1966)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Claude Lelouch
Main Cast: Jean-Louis Trintignant, Anouk Aimée, Pierre Barouh, Valérie Lagrange, Simone Paris
Keywords: widow, widower

Overview: A man and a woman meet by accident on a Sunday evening at their childrens' boarding school. Slowly, they reveal themselves to each other, finding that each is a widow.
