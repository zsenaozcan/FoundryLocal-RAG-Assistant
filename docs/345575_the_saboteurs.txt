Title: The Saboteurs (2015)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Per-Olav Sørensen
Main Cast: Espen Klouman-Høiner, Christoph Bach, Dennis Storhøi, Anna Friel, Pip Torrens
Keywords: miniseries

Overview: The story about the heavy water war unfolds during the prolouge, eruption and the end of the second world war. We follow the story of the the key personnel in the race of who develops the atomic bomb first.
