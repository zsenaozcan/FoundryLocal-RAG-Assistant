Title: Muthu (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Action
Director: K. S. Ravikumar
Main Cast: Rajinikanth, Meena, Sarath Babu, Raghuvaran, Radha Ravi

Overview: Muthu starring by rajinikanth and meena. A lavish Maharaja (Rajinikanth) lived with his sister-in-law and stepbrother Rajasekhar (Raghuvaran), helping people incessantly. His sister-in-law has a son, to whom the Maharaja bequeaths a major portion of his property. At this juncture, a new baby is born to the Maharaja.His wife dies soon after. Brother Rajasekhar cheats the Maharaja as he fears that his son's property might be taken back and given to the Maharaja's own son. When the cheating comes to light, the Maharaja hands over all his property and his baby to his sister-in-law...
