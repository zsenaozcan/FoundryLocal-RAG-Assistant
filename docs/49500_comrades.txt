Title: Comrades (1987)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bill Douglas
Main Cast: Keith Allen, Phil Davis, Joanna David, Vanessa Redgrave, Imelda Staunton
Keywords: deportation, labor union

Overview: The story of "The Tolpuddle Martyrs". A group of 19th century English farm labourers who formed one of the first trade unions and started a campaign to receive fair wages.
