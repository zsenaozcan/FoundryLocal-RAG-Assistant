Title: Three on a Match (1932)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Mervyn LeRoy
Main Cast: Joan Blondell, Ann Dvorak, Bette Davis, Warren William, Lyle Talbot
Keywords: infidelity, drug addiction, kidnapping, pre-code, schoolmates, child neglect, desertion

Overview: Although Vivian Revere is seemingly the most successful of a trio of reunited schoolmates, she throws it away by descending into a life of debauchery and drugs.
