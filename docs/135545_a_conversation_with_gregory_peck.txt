Title: A Conversation with Gregory Peck (1999)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Barbara Kopple
Main Cast: Gregory Peck, Cecilia Peck, Veronique Peck, Colleen Sheehy, Don Peck
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Not your usual film biography, A Conversation With Gregory Peck (2000) goes on-the-road and behind-the-scenes with Gregory Peck and his one man show. The actor's traveling program features question and answer sessions with the American icon and allows the actor to reminisce about his career.
