Title: Since You Went Away (1944)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: David O. Selznick
Main Cast: Claudette Colbert, Jennifer Jones, Joseph Cotten, Shirley Temple, Monty Woolley
Keywords: lodger, love, teenage girl, war effort, homefront, rationing

Overview: While husband Tim is away during World War II, Anne Hilton copes with problems on the homefront. Taking in a lodger, Colonel Smollett, to help make ends meet and dealing with shortages and rationing are minor inconveniences compared to the love affair daughter Jane and the Colonel's grandson conduct.
