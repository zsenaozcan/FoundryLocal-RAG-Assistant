Title: In Orange (2004)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Family
Director: Joram Lürsen
Main Cast: Thomas Acda, Wendy van Dijk, Yannick van de Velde, Erik van Muiswinkel, Peter Blok

Overview: An 11 year old talented soccer player, Remco, has one big dream: to be selected for the national team under 12 years. His father Erik coaches him and everything seems to work out fine, until Erik suddenly dies. Remco and mother Sylvia are shocked. Remco keeps working to fulfil his dream, being helped by his friends Anne and Winston.
