Title: Kept Woman (2015)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Michel Poulette
Main Cast: Courtney Ford, Shaun Benson, Andrew W. Walker, Rachel Wilson

Overview: Jessica and her fiancé Evan just moved from the city into their dream home on a quiet suburban street. Soon after, Jessica catches her seemingly friendly new neighbor, Simon, in a strange lie and can't let her suspicions rest. The danger escalates when Simon lures her inside his home and imprisons Jessica in his secret bunker, meticulously decorated in the idealized style of the 1950's.
