Title: Anna (1988)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Music, Family
Director: Frank Strecker
Main Cast: Silvia Seidel, Patrick Bach, Eberhard Feik, Ilse Neubauer, Anton Diffring

Overview: This film is the theatrical follow-up to a very popular German TV-series (also called "Anna"), about a young girl who wants to make it as a prima ballerina.
