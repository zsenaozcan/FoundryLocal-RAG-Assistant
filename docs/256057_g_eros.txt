Title: Güeros (2015)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Alonso Ruiz Palacios
Main Cast: Tenoch Huerta, Leonardo Ortizgris, Sebastián Aguirre, Ilse Salas, Sophie Alexander-Katz
Keywords: road movie, dramedy

Overview: Set amidst the 1999 student strikes in Mexico City, this coming-of-age tale finds two brothers venturing through the city in a sentimental search for an aging legendary musician. Shot in black-and-white, Güeros brims with youthful exuberance.
