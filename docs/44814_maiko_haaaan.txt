Title: Maiko Haaaan!!! (2007)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Nobuo Mizuta
Main Cast: Sadao Abe, Kou Shibasaki, Shinichi Tsutsumi, Saori Koide, Kotomi Kyôno

Overview: Kimihiko Onizuka is a salaryman infatuated with maiko (apprentice geisha) and whose greatest goal in life is to play a party game called "yakyuken" with one.
