Title: Captain Webb (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Justin Hardy
Main Cast: Warren Brown, Georgia Maguire, Steve Oram, Terry Mynott, Hannah Tointon
Keywords: biography, romance

Overview: Captain Webb aka The Greatest Englishman tells the story of Captain Matthew Webb, the first-ever person to successfully swim the English Channel in 1875 equipped only with his moustache, doses of Brandy and a wire wool swimsuit.
