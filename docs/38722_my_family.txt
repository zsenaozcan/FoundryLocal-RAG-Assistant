Title: My Family (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Gregory Nava
Main Cast: Edward James Olmos, Jimmy Smits, Esai Morales, Jennifer Lopez, Jacob Vargas
Keywords: immigrant, marriage, independent film, family relationships, los angeles, family, death of wife

Overview: Traces over three generations an immigrant family's trials, tribulations, tragedies, and triumphs. Maria and Jose, the first generation, come to Los Angeles, meet, marry, face deportation all in the 1930's. They establish their family in East L.A., and their children Chucho, Paco, Memo, Irene, Toni, and Jimmy deal with youth culture and the L.A. police in the 50's. As the second generation become adults in the 60's, the focus shifts to Jimmy, his marriage to Isabel (a Salvadorian refugee), their son, and Jimmy's journey to becoming a responsible parent.
