Title: The Girl from the Marsh Croft (1935)
Genres: Drama
Director: Douglas Sirk
Main Cast: Hansi Knoteck, Ellen Frank, Friedrich Kayssler, Theodor Loos, Kurt Fischer-Fehling
Keywords: german, remake

Overview: In this Swedish romance, a strapping farm boy is betrothed to a pretty socialite. Unfortunately, he really loves a simple girl from the marsh. His engagement to the wealthy lass is broken after he gets into a barroom brawl. He is then able to marry the woman he really loves.
