Title: Kissin' Cousins (1964)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Gene Nelson
Main Cast: Elvis Presley, Arthur O'Connell, Glenda Farrell, Jack Albertson, Pamela Austin
Keywords: army, smoky mountains

Overview: An Army officer returns to the Smoky Mountains tries to convince his kinfolk to allow the Army to build a missile site on their land. Once he gets there, he discovers he has a lookalike cousin.
