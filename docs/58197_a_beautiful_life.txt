Title: A Beautiful Life (2008)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Alejandro Chomski
Main Cast: Dana Delany, Bai Ling, Debi Mazar, Angela Sarafyan

Overview: A love story about the encounter of two drifting souls. Escaping her abusive past, a woman stays the night with an illegal immigrant friend of a friend. The two get closer and fall in love, but face turmoil.
