Title: Monkeyshines, No. 3 (1890)
Genres: Unknown
Director: William K.L. Dickson 
Main Cast: G. Sacco Albanese

Overview: Experimental film that follows up on the results of "Monkeyshines, No. 1" and "Monkeyshines, No. 2". Once again, an Edison company worker moves around in front of the motion picture camera.
