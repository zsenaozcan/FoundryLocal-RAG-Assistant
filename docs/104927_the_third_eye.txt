Title: The Third Eye (1966)
Genres: Horror
Director: Mino Guerrini
Main Cast: Franco Nero, Gioia Pascal, Erika Blanc, Olga Solbelli, Marina Morgan
Keywords: prostitute, obsession, nightmare, hallucination, sadism, stripper, taxidermy, murder, gothic horror, mother son relationship

Overview: The young Count Mino live in a big, old castle together with his dominant mother and a maid. Mother does not want him to marry his fiancee Laura, which turns out to have dire consequences.
