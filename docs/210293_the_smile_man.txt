Title: The Smile Man (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Anton Lanshakov
Main Cast: Willem Dafoe, Camille Guaty, Damien D. Smith, Alexana Thomas
Keywords: drama, dark comedy, medical

Overview: THE SMILE MAN focuses on a gentleman dealing with the consequences of a car accident, which has left him with a spinal injury that means he has a permanent smile on his face.
