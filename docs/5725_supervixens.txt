Title: Supervixens (1975)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Russ Meyer
Main Cast: Charles Pitts, Charles Napier, Shari Eubank, Uschi Digard, Henry Rowland
Keywords: female nudity, impotence, underwear, dark humor, erotic movie

Overview: Clint Ramsey has to leave his job working at Martin Bormann's gas station and flee after his wife is murdered by psycho cop Harry Sledge, who tries to pin the murder on Clint. Crossing America, Clint gets sexually harassed on all sides by various voluptuous nymphomaniacs, and it all ends in a literally explosive climax.
