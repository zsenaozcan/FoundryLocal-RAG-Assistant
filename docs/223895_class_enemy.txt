Title: Class Enemy (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rok Biček
Main Cast: Igor Samobor, Nataša Barbara Gačner, Tjaša Železnik, Maša Derganc, Robert Prebil

Overview: Relations between the students and the new teacher of German are extremely tense. When one female student commits suicide, her schoolmates blame the teacher for her death. An awareness that things are not quite so black and white comes too late.
