Title: The Angels' Share (2012)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Ken Loach
Main Cast: Paul Brannigan, Siobhan Reilly, John Henshaw, Gary Maitland, William Ruane
Keywords: scotland, whiskey, community service

Overview: Narrowly avoiding jail, new dad Robbie vows to turn over a new leaf. A visit to a whisky distillery inspires him and his mates to seek a way out of their hopeless lives.
