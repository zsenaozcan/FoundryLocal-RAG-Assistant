Title: The Purchase Price (1932)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: Barbara Stanwyck, George Brent, Lyle Talbot, Hardie Albright, David Landau
Keywords: north dakota, singer, mail order bride

Overview: Barbara Stanwyck plays Joan Gordon, a torch singer who runs away from her gangster boyfriend (Lyle Talbot) to become a mail-order bride to a struggling North Dakota farmer (George Brent). Their relationship has a rocky start, but just as Joan realizes that she is developing feelings for her husband, her old boyfriend arrives to win her back.
