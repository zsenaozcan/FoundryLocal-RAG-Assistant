Title: Kidco (1984)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ronald F. Maxwell
Main Cast: Scott Schwartz, Clifton James, Cinnamon Idles, Elizabeth Gorcey, Tristine Skyler
Keywords: court, kids, manure

Overview: A boy who lives at a country club where his father works decides to make some extra money by selling composted horse manure as fertilizer, and has his three sisters (two of which are older) join him in the enterprise. As their sales increase, they draw increased scrutiny from the IRS and state tax board, as well as the large scale competitor who seeks to put them out of business at any cost.
