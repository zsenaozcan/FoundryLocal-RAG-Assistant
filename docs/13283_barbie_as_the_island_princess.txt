Title: Barbie as the Island Princess (2007)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Greg Richardson
Main Cast: Mar Bordallo, Paloma Blanco, Tony Cruz, Miguel Ángel Valera, Ana Alborg

Overview: Barbie plays Rosella in this new musical film. Shipwrecked as a child, Rosella grows up on the island under the watchful eyes of her loving animal friends. The arrival of Prince Antonio leads Rosella and her furry pals to explore civilization and ultimately save the kingdom by uncovering a secret plot.
