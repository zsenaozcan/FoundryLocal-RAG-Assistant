Title: Dr. Kildare's Strange Case (1940)
Genres: Drama
Director: Harold S. Bucquet
Main Cast: Lew Ayres, Lionel Barrymore, Laraine Day, Shepperd Strudwick, Samuel S. Hinds
Keywords: dr. kildare

Overview: Kildare (Lew Ayres) tries brain surgery, advised by Dr. Gillespie (Lionel Barrymore), and faces a rival for nurse Lamont (Laraine Day).
