Title: Hard Target (1993)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Crime, Thriller
Director: John Woo
Main Cast: Jean-Claude Van Damme, Arnold Vosloo, Lance Henriksen, Yancy Butler, Sven-Ole Thorsen
Keywords: new orleans, mercenary, hunting human beings, money, drifter, union, sailor, human prey

Overview: When a woman's father goes missing, she enlist a local to aid in her search.  The pair soon discover that her father has died at the hands of a wealthy sportsman who hunts homeless men as a form of recreation.
