Title: Frogs for Snakes (1998)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy, Crime
Director: Amos Poe
Main Cast: Lisa Marie, Barbara Hershey, Debi Mazar, Mike Starr, Robbie Coltrane
Keywords: collections, loan shark, actor

Overview: A group of unemployed theater actors survive by working as illegal money collectors. The loan shark they are working for owns an Off-Broadway theater. As he decided to play "American Buffalo" there, a bloody battle for the favorite roles begin.
