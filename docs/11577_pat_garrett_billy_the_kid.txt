Title: Pat Garrett & Billy the Kid (1973)
Genres: Western
Director: Sam Peckinpah
Main Cast: James Coburn, Kris Kristofferson, Richard Jaeckel, Katy Jurado, Chill Wills
Keywords: sheriff, aging, billy the kid, lawlessness, gun battle, gore, breast, old friends, flogging, man slaps a woman
