Title: Bird (1988)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Clint Eastwood
Main Cast: Forest Whitaker, Diane Venora, Keith David, Damon Whitaker, Michael Zelniker
Keywords: psychiatric hospital, heroin addict, electroconvulsive therapy, death of daughter, bebop, jazz music, eiffel tower paris

Overview: Saxophone player Charlie Parker comes to New York in 1940. He is quickly noticed for his remarkable way of playing. He becomes a drug addict but his loving wife Chan tries to help him.
