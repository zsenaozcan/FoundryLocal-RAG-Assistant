Title: Radio Unnameable (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Paul Lovelace
Main Cast: David Amram, Judy Collins, Robert Downey Sr., Arlo Guthrie, Judith Malina
Keywords: disc jockey, radio, pioneer, late night, woman director

Overview: The story of legendary New York City disc jockey Bob Fass who pioneered free expression on the airwaves with his long running FM program 'Radio Unnameable'.
