Title: The Shopworn Angel (1938)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: H. C. Potter
Main Cast: Margaret Sullavan, James Stewart, Walter Pidgeon, Hattie McDaniel, Nat Pendleton
Keywords: patriotism, coney island, soldier, brooklyn new york city, showgirl

Overview: During WWI Bill Pettigrew, a naive young Texan soldier is sent to New York for basic training. He meets worldly wise actress Daisy Heath when her car nearly runs him over.
