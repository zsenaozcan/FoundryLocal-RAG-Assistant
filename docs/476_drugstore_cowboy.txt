Title: Drugstore Cowboy (1989)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Gus Van Sant
Main Cast: Matt Dillon, Kelly Lynch, James Le Gros, Heather Graham, Grace Zabriskie
Keywords: individual, robbery, drug abuse, sheriff, support group, 1970s, portland, drug dealer, junkie, overdose

Overview: Bob and his friends Dianne, Rick and Nadine have been drug addicts for years and live from one high to the next. Gus Van Sant attempts to show an intimate look into the lives of heroin addicts with his film Drugstore Cowboy.
