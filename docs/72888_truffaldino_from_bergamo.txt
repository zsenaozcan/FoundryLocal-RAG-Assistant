Title: Truffaldino from Bergamo (1977)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Vladimir Vorobyov
Main Cast: Konstantin Raykin, Viktor Kostetsky, Valentina Kosobutskaya, Natalya Gundareva, Lev Petropavlovski
Keywords: musical

Overview: A funny musical comedy based on the famous theater play "Servant of Two Masters" by Carlo Goldoni.
