Title: In the Land of Women (2007)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Jon Kasdan
Main Cast: Meg Ryan, Kristen Stewart, Adam Brody, JoBeth Williams, Olympia Dukakis
Keywords: infidelity, party, love, cancer, neighbor, writer, teenager, illness, flashback, actress
