Title: Memories of My Melancholy Whores (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Henning Carlsen
Main Cast: Geraldine Chaplin, Ángela Molina, Dominika Paleta, Alejandra Barros, Emilio Echevarría

Overview: Chronicles the romantic enchantment of a 90 year old reporter with a girl 70 years younger than himself.
