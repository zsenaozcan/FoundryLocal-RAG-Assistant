Title: The Exception (2017)
Genres: Drama
Director: David Leveaux
Main Cast: Lily James, Jai Courtney, Eddie Marsan, Christopher Plummer, Janet McTeer
Keywords: spy, based on novel, resistance, dutch, german, world war i, investigation, love, holland, jewish
