Title: Murder of a Cat (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy
Director: Gillian Greene
Main Cast: Fran Kranz, Nikki Reed, J.K. Simmons, Blythe Danner, Greg Kinnear
Keywords: woman director

Overview: When someone murders his beloved cat, Clinton, an adult child, demands justice. Taking it upon himself to solve the case, he teams up with an unlikely ally, Greta, and the two set out to find the culprit lurking in their small suburban town. But as Clinton searches for the truth, he begins to uncover a conspiracy that goes far deeper than he anticipated.
