Title: Salting the Battlefield (2014)
Genres: Action, Drama, Crime
Director: David Hare
Main Cast: Bill Nighy, Helena Bonham Carter, Ralph Fiennes, Ewen Bremner, Judy Davis
Keywords: mi5, johnny worricker

Overview: David Hare concludes his trilogy of films about MI5 renegade Johnny Worricker with another fugue on power, secrets and the British establishment. Johnny Worricker goes on the run with Margot Tyrell across Europe, and with the net closing in, the former MI5 man knows his only chance of resolving his problems is to return home and confront prime minister Alec Beasley.
