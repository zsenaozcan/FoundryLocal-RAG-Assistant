Title: Kandahar (2010)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Andrei Kavun
Main Cast: Bogdan Benyuk, Alexandr Golubev, Aleksandr Baluev, Vladimir Mashkov, Andrei Panin
Keywords: afghanistan, taliban, escape

Overview: A docudrama about the escape of Russian pilots after being taken hostage by the Taliban in 1996.
