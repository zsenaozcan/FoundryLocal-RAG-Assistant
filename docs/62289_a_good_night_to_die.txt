Title: A Good Night to Die (2003)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Thriller
Director: Craig Singer
Main Cast: Michael Rapaport, Gary Stretch, Seymour Cassel, Robin Givens, Deborah Harry

Overview: One day in the life of a hit man, Ronnie, who spends it trying to save the life of fellow hit man, August, who he had brought into the business three years ago. August has become a real problem for some of the bosses around town, and Ronnie will soon find out just what his Frankenstein's monster will cost him.
