Title: Single Santa Seeks Mrs. Claus (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Family
Director: Harvey Frost
Main Cast: Steve Guttenberg, Crystal Bernard, Dominic Scott Kay, Thomas Calabro, Armin Shimerman
Keywords: holiday

Overview: Nick's plan to let fate bring his wife to him must be altered because he must replace his father as Santa Claus on December 26. Ernst, the right-hand man to several generations of Santas, generates a list of potential mates. Nick dutifully sets off to meet them, but fate may have a candidate after all.
