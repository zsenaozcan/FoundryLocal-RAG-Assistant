Title: The Remains (2016)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Thomas Della Bella
Main Cast: Todd Lowe, Lisa Brenner, Brooke Butler, Ashley Crow, Maria Olsen
Keywords: haunted house, house, ghost

Overview: After a family moves into an old Victorian home, they discover a chest in the attic containing antiques tainted by a malevolent spirit. As the antiques slowly possess each family member, the spirit grows stronger, hellbent on kidnapping the children.
