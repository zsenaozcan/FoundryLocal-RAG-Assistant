Title: Repast (1951)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mikio Naruse
Main Cast: Ken Uehara, Setsuko Hara, Yukiko Shimazaki, Yôko Sugi, Akiko Kazami
Keywords: based on novel, marriage, meal, unhappiness

Overview: Michiyo lives in the small place Osaka and is not happy with her marriage, all she does is cook and clean for her husband.
