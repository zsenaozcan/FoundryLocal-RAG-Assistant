Title: Attention! Bandits! (1951)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Carlo Lizzani
Main Cast: Andrea Checchi, Gina Lollobrigida, Vittorio Duse, Lamberto Maggiorani, Maria Laura Rocca

Overview: During the winter of 1944, the partisans stationed in the Ligurian Apennines must go to a factory in Genoa, in order to pick up a delivery of weapons. Meanwhile, there's a strike in the city and the Nazis are trying to suppress it violently.  The factory becomes the scene of fighting between the Germans and the partisans but the latter, aided by the workers, will be able to get the better.
