Title: Alice in Wonderland (1933)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Family, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Norman Z. McLeod
Main Cast: Charlotte Henry, Richard Arlen, Roscoe Ates, William Austin, Gary Cooper
Keywords: alice in wonderland

Overview: On a boring winter afternoon, Alice dreams, that she's visiting the land behind the mirror. This turns out to be a surrealistic nightmare, with all sorts of strange things happening to her, like changing her size or playing croquet with flamingos.
