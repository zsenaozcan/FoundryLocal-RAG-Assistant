Title: Come Back to Me (2014)
Genres: Horror
Director: Paul Leyden
Main Cast: Matt Passmore, Katie Walder, Nathan Keyes, Laura Gordon, Caroline Clements
Keywords: hidden camera, resurrection, horror, thriller, neighbor, pregnancy, mysterious

Overview: Sarah &amp; Josh are married suburbanites. After a car accident, Sarah suffers blackouts &amp; ends up pregnant though Josh is sterile. As her life spirals she hides a camera in her home &amp; unlocks a horrific secret with far-reaching consequences.
