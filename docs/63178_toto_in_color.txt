Title: Toto in Color (1953)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Steno
Main Cast: Totò, Mario Castellani, Luigi Pavese, Isa Barzizza, Franca Valeri

Overview: 'Totò a colori' was the first Italian movie to be shot with a colour film, but the sensitivity was very low (6 asa!) so the actors had to act under a very strong lighting and high temperature.
