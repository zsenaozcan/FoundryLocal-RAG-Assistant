Title: Burke & Hare (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Thriller
Director: John Landis
Main Cast: Simon Pegg, Andy Serkis, Isla Fisher, Tom Wilkinson, Tim Curry
Keywords: heart attack, black humor, autopsy, murder, cruelty, anti villain, grave digger

Overview: Two 19th-century opportunists (Simon Pegg, Andy Serkis) become serial killers so that they can maintain their profitable business supplying cadavers to an anatomist (Tom Wilkinson).
