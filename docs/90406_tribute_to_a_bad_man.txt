Title: Tribute to a Bad Man (1956)
Genres: Western
Director: Robert Wise
Main Cast: James Cagney, Don Dubbins, Stephen McNally, Irene Papas, Vic Morrow
Keywords: based on novel, rancher

Overview: Jeremy Rodock is a tough horse rancher who strings up rustlers soon as look at them. Fresh out of Pennsylvania, Steve Miller finds it hard to get used to Rodock's ways, although he takes an immediate shine to his Greek girl Jocasta.
