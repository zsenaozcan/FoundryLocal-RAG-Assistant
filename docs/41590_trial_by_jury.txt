Title: Trial by Jury (1994)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller
Director: Heywood Gould
Main Cast: William Hurt, Joanne Whalley, Gabriel Byrne, Armand Assante, Kathleen Quinlan
Keywords: organized crime, mob boss

Overview: Valerie is a juror in the trail of a mob boss. When her young son's life is threatened, she has no option other than to see that justice isn't done.
