Title: An Awfully Big Adventure (1995)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Comedy
Director: Mike Newell
Main Cast: Alan Rickman, Hugh Grant, Georgina Cates, Rita Tushingham, Alun Armstrong
Keywords: england, based on novel, liverpool, theatre company, 1940s

Overview: An Awfully Big Adventure is a 1995 drama film about a theatre company in Liverpool, set in 1947 and based on the 1989 novel of the same name by Beryl Bainbridge.
