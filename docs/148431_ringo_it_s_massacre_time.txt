Title: Ringo, It's Massacre Time (1970)
Genres: Western
Director: Mario Pinzauti
Main Cast: Jean Louis, Omero Gargano, Lucia Bomez, Giovanni Ivan Scratuglia, Mickey Hargitay
Keywords: sheriff, brother, spaghetti western

Overview: Ringo comes to a Mexican village, searching for his brother from whom he has heard no news. He comes with a sheriff who immediately has work on his hands, as those who get killed are certainly too many.
