Title: Ambush (1999)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Olli Saarela
Main Cast: Peter Franzén, Irina Björklund, Kari Heiskanen, Kari Väänänen, Tommi Eronen
Keywords: world war ii, continuation war

Overview: It is the summer of 1941 and the Finnish army has been mobilized along the border with Russia. A platoon led by Lt. Eero Perkola is waiting for orders to go on the offensive. The platoon receives orders for a recon mission through the wilderness around the Lieksa lake to search for possible Russian defensive positions.
