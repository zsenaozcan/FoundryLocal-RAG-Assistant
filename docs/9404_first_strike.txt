Title: First Strike (1996)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Stanley Tong
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Bill Tung, Jackson Liu, Annie Wu, Ailen Sit
Keywords: helicopter, martial arts, australia

Overview: Jackie Chan reprises his role as Chan Ka-Kui (also known in some versions as Jackie) yet again as a Hong Kong cop who works with Interpol to track down and arrest an illegal weapons dealer. Later Jackie realizes that things are not as simple as they appear and soon find himself a pawn of an organisation posing as Russian intelligence.
