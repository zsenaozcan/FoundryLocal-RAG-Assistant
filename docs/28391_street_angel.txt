Title: Street Angel (1928)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Frank Borzage
Main Cast: Janet Gaynor, Charles Farrell, Natalie Kingston, Henry Armetta, guido trento
Keywords: italy, circus, based on novel, naples, jail, melodrama

Overview: A spirited young woman finds herself destitute and on the streets before joining a traveling carnival, where she meets a vagabond painter.
