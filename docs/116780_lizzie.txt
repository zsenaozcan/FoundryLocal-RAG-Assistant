Title: Lizzie (1957)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hugo Haas
Main Cast: Eleanor Parker, Richard Boone, Joan Blondell, Hugo Haas, Ric Roman
Keywords: hypnosis, mother daughter relationship, attempted suicide, multiple personality, spinster

Overview: A psychiatrist (Richard Boone) treats a woman (Eleanor Parker) of three faces: neurotic Elizabeth, wanton Lizzie, charming Beth.
