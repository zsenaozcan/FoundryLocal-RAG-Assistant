Title: Higglety Pigglety Pop! or There Must Be More to Life (2010)
Genres: Family, Fantasy
Director: Chris Lavis
Main Cast: Meryl Streep, Spike Jonze, Alain Goulem, Al Tuck, Audrée Juteau

Overview: Jennie the terrier has everything a dog could ever wish for, but still feels that something is missing. She leaves home to discover what that is.
