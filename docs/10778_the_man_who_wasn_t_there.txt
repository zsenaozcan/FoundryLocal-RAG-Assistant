Title: The Man Who Wasn't There (2001)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Joel Coen
Main Cast: Billy Bob Thornton, Frances McDormand, Michael Badalucco, James Gandolfini, Scarlett Johansson
Keywords: hairdresser, small town, con man, insurance fraud, encounter

Overview: A tale of passion, crime and punishment set in the summer of 1949. Ed Crane, a barber in a small California town, is dissatisfied with his life, but his wife Doris' infidelity presents him with a chance to change it.
