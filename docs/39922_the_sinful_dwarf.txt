Title: The Sinful Dwarf (1973)
Genres: Crime, Horror
Director: Vidal Raski
Main Cast: Anne Sparrow, Gerda Madsen
Keywords: rape, dwarves, bondage, brothel, nudity, slavery, sadism, psychopath, alcoholism, sexploitation

Overview: Olaf and his mother run a boarding house and a white slavery ring. They also smuggle heroin to keep the addict girls happy so they do not try and escape. A young couple move into the house and the evil landlords take a liking to the female.
