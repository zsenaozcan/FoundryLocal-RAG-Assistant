Title: Bell, Book and Candle (1958)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Romance
Director: Richard Quine
Main Cast: James Stewart, Kim Novak, Jack Lemmon, Ernie Kovacs, Hermione Gingold
Keywords: witch, cat, love of one's life
