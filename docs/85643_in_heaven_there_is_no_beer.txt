Title: In Heaven There Is No Beer? (1984)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Les Blank
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: polka, food

Overview: A joyous romp through the dance, food, music, friendship, and even religion of the Polka. The explosive energy and high spirits of the polka subculture are rendered with warmth and dedication to scholarship in this journey through Polish-American celebrations. Polka stars like Jimmy Sturr, Eddie Blazonzyck and Walt Solek are featured.
