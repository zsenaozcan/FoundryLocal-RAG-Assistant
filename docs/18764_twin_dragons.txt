Title: Twin Dragons (1992)
Genres: Comedy, Action
Director: Tsui Hark
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Maggie Cheung, Nina Li Chi, Teddy Robin Kwan, James Wong Jim
Keywords: mistaken identity, martial arts master, pianist, twins, grand piano, concert pianist

Overview: Twins, separated at birth, end up as a Hong Kong gangster and a New York concert pianist. When the pianist travels to Hong Kong for a concert, the two inevitably get mistaken for each other.
