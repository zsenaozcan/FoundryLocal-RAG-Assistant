Title: The Invisible Boy (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Adventure, Science Fiction, Fantasy
Director: Gabriele Salvatores
Main Cast: Ludovico Girardello, Valeria Golino, Fabrizio Bentivoglio, Noa Zatta, Christo Jivkov

Overview: Michele is thirteen year old, shy, unpopular at school, and in love with Stella. After wearing a costume for a Halloween party, he finds out that he's invisible.
