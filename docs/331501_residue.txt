Title: Residue (2015)
Genres: Science Fiction, Mystery, Horror, Thriller
Director: Alex Garcia Lopez
Main Cast: Natalia Tena, Iwan Rheon, Jamie Draven, Danny Webb, Franz Drameh

Overview: The government cover-up of the causes behind a massive explosion in a futuristic UK metropolis spur photo journalist Jennifer Preston on to search for the truth and in the process blow open a paranormal phenomenon haunting the city.
