Title: Pingpong (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Matthias Luthardt
Main Cast: Sebastian Urzendowsky, Marion Mitterhammer, Clemens Berger, Falk Rockstroh
Keywords: drama

Overview: The film shows the apparently intact world of a middle class family, whose harmonious facade crumbles due to the unexpected visit of their relative Paul, a young man of 16 years. Paul arrives looking for love and support after the suicide of his father.
