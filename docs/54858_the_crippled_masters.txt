Title: The Crippled Masters (1979)
Genres: Action, Foreign
Director: Law Chi
Main Cast: Chan Muk-Chuen, Li Chung-Chien, Ma Cheung, Thomas Hong Chiu-Ming, Sam Chung-Chuen
Keywords: martial arts, cripple

Overview: Two men skilled in the arts of Kung-Fu are betrayed by their master and crippled for life, one left with no arms and the other with no legs. Despite their obvious disadvantages, they learn to combine their martial arts skills and seek revenge against the evil master.
