Title: Roustabout (1964)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance
Director: John Rich
Main Cast: Elvis Presley, Barbara Stanwyck, Leif Erickson, Joan Freeman, Sue Ane Langdon
Keywords: rock and roll, fair, lovers, elvis

Overview: Elvis plays a bad-boy singer roaming the highways on his Japanese motorcycle; laid up after an accident, he joins a carnival owned by the feisty Barbara Stanwyck.
