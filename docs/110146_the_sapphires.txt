Title: The Sapphires (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Wayne Blair
Main Cast: Chris O'Dowd, Deborah Mailman, Jessica Mauboy, Shari Sebbens, Miranda Tapsell
Keywords: vietnam war, musical, biography, based on true story, girl group, indigenous australians, australian aborigine, mother child separation, stolen generation
