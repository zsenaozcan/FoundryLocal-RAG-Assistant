Title: Ex-Girlfriends (2012)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Alexander Poe
Main Cast: Jennifer Carpenter, Kristen Connolly, Alexander Poe, Liz Holtan, Ian Unterman
Keywords: new york, relationship, romantic entanglements

Overview: Jennifer Carpenter, Kristen Connolly and Alexander Poe star in a comedy about three New Yorkers struggling to uncover the truth behind their romantic entanglements. Disheartened by his latest relationship catastrophe, Graham (Poe) attempts to rekindle the spark with his ex-girlfriend, Laura (Connolly). In the attempt to win her back Graham discovers he has a unique problem: Laura and another one his ex-girlfriends (Carpenter) are dating the same guy.
