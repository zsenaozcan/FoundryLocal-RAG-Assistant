Title: San Giovanni decollato (1940)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Amleto Palermi
Main Cast: Totò, Titina De Filippo, Silvana Jachino, Augusto Di Giovanni, Eduardo Passarelli

Overview: Mastr'Antonio Miciaccio caretaker and shoemaker is devoted to Saint John Beheaded and is trying to find out who has been stealing the oil that keeps the candle to the saint burning. He also does not approve of his daughter's boy friend and he does not want him to marry her.
