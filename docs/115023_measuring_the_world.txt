Title: Measuring the World (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Detlev Buck
Main Cast: Florian David Fitz, Albrecht Schuch, Vicky Krieps, Katharina Thalbach, David Kross

Overview: Germany in the early 19th century. "Die Vermessung der Welt" follows the two brilliant and eccentric scientists Alexander von Humboldt and Carl Friedrich Gauss on their life paths.
