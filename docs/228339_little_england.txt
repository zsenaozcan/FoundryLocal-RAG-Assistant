Title: Little England (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Pantelis Voulgaris
Main Cast: Pinelopi Tsilika, Sofia Kokkali, Maximos Mammouris, Aggeliki Papathemeli, Anneza Papadopoulou

Overview: Τwo sisters from the island of Andros, dubbed Little England because of its affluence, are both in love with the same man. Set in the Greek Civil War period and ending in the 1950s, the movie is based on the homonymous novel by Ioanna Karystiani.
