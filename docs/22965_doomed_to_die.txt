Title: Doomed to Die (1940)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: William Nigh
Main Cast: Boris Karloff, Marjorie Reynolds, Grant Withers, William Stelling, Catherine Craig
Keywords: detective, daughter, reporter, shipping, magnate

Overview: Shipping magnate Cyrus Wentworth, downcast over a disaster to his ocean liner 'Wentworth Castle' (carrying, oddly enough, an illicit shipment of Chinese bonds) is shot in his office...at the very moment of kicking out his daughter's fiance Dick Fleming. Of course, Captain Street arrests Dick, but reporter Bobbie Logan, the attractive thorn in Street's side, is so convinced he's wrong that she enlists the help of detective James Lee Wong to find the real killer.
