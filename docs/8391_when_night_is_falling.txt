Title: When Night Is Falling (1995)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Patricia Rozema
Main Cast: Pascale Bussières, Rachael Crawford, Henry Czerny, Don McKellar
Keywords: professor, lgbt, woman director

Overview: A prudish woman, working on tenure as a literacy professor at a large urban university, finds herself strangely attracted to a free-spirited, liberal woman whom works at a local carnival which comes to town.
