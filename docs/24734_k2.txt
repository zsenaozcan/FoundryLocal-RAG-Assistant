Title: K2 (1991)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: Franc Roddam
Main Cast: Michael Biehn, Matt Craven, Annie Grindlay, Blu Mankuma
Keywords: climbing, big mountain

Overview: They call it the savage mountain. 27 people have perished trying to reach the summit on K2, the world's second highest peak. A U.S. climbing team, funded by millionaire Clairborne is determined to conquer K2.
