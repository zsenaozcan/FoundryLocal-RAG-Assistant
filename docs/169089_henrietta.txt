Title: Henrietta (1983)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lars Lennart Forsberg
Main Cast: Janne 'Loffe' Carlsson, Svante Grundberg, Per Oscarsson, Gunnar 'Knas' Lindkvist, Claire Wikholm
Keywords: countryside, based on novel, character name in title

Overview: Pettersson is a hard working director in the porn industry with two strippers and a naked gay in his shows. One day, after a little bridge accident, he meets Henrietta and falls in love. The film is based on a novel named "Henrietta skall du också glömma" by Stig Claesson.
