Title: Dead of Winter (1987)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Arthur Penn
Main Cast: Mary Steenburgen, Roddy McDowall, Jan Rubes, William Russ, Ken Pogue
Keywords: suspense

Overview: A fledgling actress is lured to a remote mansion for a screen-test, soon discovering she is actually a prisoner in the middle of a blackmail plot.
