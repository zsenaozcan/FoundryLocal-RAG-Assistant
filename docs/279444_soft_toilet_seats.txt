Title: Soft Toilet Seats (1999)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Tina Valinsky
Main Cast: David Rosen, Alexa Jago, Jonathan Aube, Sammi Davis, Michael Greene
Keywords: suicide, female nudity, house, murder

Overview: Arne buys a house in suburban Los Angeles, only to discover that the previous owner died in a grisly manner. Then he gets a visit from the dead woman's best friend, who is convinced that she didn't commit suicide. Together, they investigate what Tilly is sure must be a murder, and come up against the toughest moral dilemma of their lives.
