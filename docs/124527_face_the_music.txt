Title: Face the Music (1954)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Terence Fisher
Main Cast: Alex Nicol, Eleanor Summerfield, John Salew, Paul Carpenter, Geoffrey Keen
Keywords: london england, trumpet player

Overview: A famed trumpet player is suspected of murdering a blues singer. Using only two minor clues, he narrows the suspects to four people, but only after surviving poison placed on the mouthpiece of his trumpet!
