Title: Walking Too Fast (2010)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Radim Špaček
Main Cast: Ondřej Malý, Kristína Farkašová, Martin Finger, Luboš Veselý, Lukáš Latinák
Keywords: communist party, secret police, státní bezpečnost, stb, state security

Overview: The psyche of a ruthless secret agent in Cold War Czechoslovakia begins to unravel when he obsesses over the girlfriend of a suspected subversive he is tracking. This taut political thriller is a bleak and potent rendering of the emotional destruction wreaked by totalitarianism.
