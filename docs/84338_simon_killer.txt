Title: Simon Killer (2012)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Antonio Campos
Main Cast: Brady Corbet, Mati Diop, Michaël Abiteboul, Constance Rousseau, Lila Salet

Overview: A recent college graduate goes to Paris after breaking up with his girlfriend of five years. His life should be open-ended and full of promise, but he can’t shake his feelings of loss. Being a stranger in a strange land only aggravates his situation. When he falls in love with a young mysterious prostitute, a fateful journey begins, though we soon learn that Simon is the one with deeper secrets.
