Title: Carnosaur (1993)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Adam Simon
Main Cast: Diane Ladd, Raphael Sbarge, Jennifer Runyon, Harrison Page, Ned Bellamy
Keywords: vegetarian, gore, dinosaur, credit rolling down

Overview: After being driven to extinction, great bloodthirsty dinosaurs come back to life with the assistance of a demented genetic scientist. She plans to replace the human race with a super-race of dinosaurs who will not pollute the planet.
