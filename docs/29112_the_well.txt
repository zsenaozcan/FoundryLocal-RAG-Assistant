Title: The Well (1951)
Genres: Drama
Director: Russell Rouse
Main Cast: Richard Rober, Gwendolyn Laster, Maidie Norman, George Hamilton, Ernest Anderson

Overview: In a racially mixed American town, a 5-year-old black girl falls unnoticed into a hidden, forgotten well on her way to school. Having nothing better to go on, the police follow up a report that the child was seen with a white stranger, and rumors run wild. Before hapless, innocent Claude Packard is even found, popular hysteria has him tried and convicted. But is he guilty?
