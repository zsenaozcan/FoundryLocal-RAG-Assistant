Title: The Walk (2015)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Thriller
Director: Robert Zemeckis
Main Cast: Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Ben Kingsley, Charlotte Le Bon, Ben Schwartz, James Badge Dale
Keywords: 1970s, skyscraper, based on true story, tightrope, planning, world trade center
