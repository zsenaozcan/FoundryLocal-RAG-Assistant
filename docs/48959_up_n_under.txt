Title: Up 'n' Under (1998)
Genres: Comedy
Director: John Godber
Main Cast: Gary Olsen, Samantha Womack, Neil Morrissey, Ralph Brown, Griff Rhys Jones
Keywords: sport

Overview: The Cobblers Arms have been the best and most feared Amateur Rugby League team for the past ten years. Ex-pro Arthur bets their boss that he could train a bunch of deadbeats to defeat them in a local rugby sevens tournament. But to do so he must first get them into shape with the help of the very attractive Hazel Scott.
