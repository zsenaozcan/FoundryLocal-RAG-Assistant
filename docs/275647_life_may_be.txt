Title: Life May Be (2014)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Mark Cousins
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: An epistolary feature film: a cinematic discourse between a British director Mark Cousins, and an Iranian actress and director Mania Akbari which extends the concept of "essay film" with startling confrontations in the arenas of cultural issues, gender politics and differing artistic sensibilities. A unique journey into the minds of two exceptional filmmakers which becomes a love affair on film.
