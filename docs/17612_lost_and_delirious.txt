Title: Lost and Delirious (2001)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Léa Pool
Main Cast: Piper Perabo, Jessica Paré, Mischa Barton, Jackie Burroughs, Mimi Kuzyk
Keywords: sex, based on novel, roommate, identity, secret, nudity, passion, party, love, lesbian

Overview: Lost and Delirious is the story of three adolescent girls' first love, their discovery of sexual passion, and their search for identities. Set in a posh, private boarding school surrounded by luxuriant, green forest, Lost and Delirious moves swiftly from academic routine, homesickness, and girlish silliness to the darker region of lover's intrigue.
