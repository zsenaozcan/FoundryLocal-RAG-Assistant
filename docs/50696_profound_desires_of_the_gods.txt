Title: Profound Desires of the Gods (1968)
Genres: Drama
Director: Shôhei Imamura
Main Cast: Rentarô Mikuni, Kazuo Kitamura, Hideko Okiyama, Yoshi Katô, Yasuko Matsui

Overview: Tokyo engineer Kariya arrives on a primitive tropical island, where he interacts with the Futori clan, to drill a well to power a sugar mill.
