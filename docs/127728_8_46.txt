Title: 8:46 (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jennifer Azano
Main Cast: Shelly Shenoy, Mike DiGiacinto, Elizabeth Eggers, Jzaneen Damji, Laurie Dawn

Overview: A glimpse into the lives of an ensemble of characters leading up to the moment the world changed forever - 8:46 a.m. Tuesday September 11, 2001.
