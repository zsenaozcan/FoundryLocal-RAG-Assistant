Title: The Revenant (2015)
Genres: Western, Drama, Adventure, Thriller
Director: Alejandro González Iñárritu
Main Cast: Leonardo DiCaprio, Tom Hardy, Will Poulter, Domhnall Gleeson, Paul Anderson
Keywords: father son relationship, rape, based on novel, mountain, winter, grizzly bear, wilderness, frontier, revenge, murder

Overview: In the 1820s, a frontiersman, Hugh Glass, sets out on a path of vengeance against those who left him for dead after a bear mauling.
