Title: Zhongkui: Snow Girl and the Dark Crystal (2015)
Genres: Action, Fantasy, Romance
Director: Zhao Tianyu
Main Cast: Chen Kun, Li Bingbing, Bao Bei-Er, Summer Jike, Winston Chao
Keywords: elves, black magic, love, battle, evil plot

Overview: The story of Chinese legendary anti-hero Zhong Kui, a young man endowed with mysterious powers who is forced into a battle among the realms of Heaven, Earth and Hell in the course of his attempt to save his countrymen and the woman he loves.
