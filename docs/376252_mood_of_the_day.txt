Title: Mood of the Day (2016)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Jo Kyu-Jang
Main Cast: Moon Chae-won, Yoo Yeon-seok, Jo Jae-yoon, Kim Seul-Gi, Park Min-woo
Keywords: one-night stand, romantic comedy

Overview: By chance, a man and a woman meet on the KTX train and spend 24 hours in the unfamiliar city of Busan, South Korea.
