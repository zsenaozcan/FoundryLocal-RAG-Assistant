Title: Evidence (2013)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Olatunde Osunsanmi
Main Cast: Radha Mitchell, Torrey DeVitto, Nolan Gerard Funk, Stephen Moyer, Dale Dickey
Keywords: fire, police, multiple murders, misleading information, collision, shaky camera

Overview: Detectives use clues from various recording devices to piece together events surrounding a massacre involving bus passengers at an abandoned gas station.
