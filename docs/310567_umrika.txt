Title: Umrika (2015)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Prashant Nair
Main Cast: Suraj Sharma, Tony Revolori, Prateik Babbar, Smita Tambe, Pramod Pathak

Overview: When a young village boy discovers that his brother, long believed to be in America, has actually gone missing, he begins to invent letters on his behalf to save their mother from heartbreak, all the while searching for him.
