Title: Lucky Partners (1940)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Lewis Milestone
Main Cast: Ginger Rogers, Ronald Colman, Jack Carson, Spring Byington, Cecilia Loftus
Keywords: luck, sweepstakes

Overview: Ginger Rogers and Ronald Colman star in this whimsical romantic comedy. Two strangers split a sweepstake prize to go on a fake honeymoon with predictable results.
