Title: Hearts and Minds (1995)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Documentary, Romance, Foreign
Director: Ralph Ziman
Main Cast: Danny Keogh, Patrick Shai, Septula Sebogodi, Ernest Ndhlovu
Keywords: cop, thriller

Overview: In the final days of the pro-apartheid government, bigoted policeman and assassin Andries Fourie is assigned an undercover job. His task is to pose as a political progressive to infiltrate the African National Congress and assassinate one of the party’s leaders.
