Title: Appleseed (2004)
Genres: Animation, Action, Science Fiction
Director: Shinji Aramaki
Main Cast: Ai Kobayashi, Jūrōta Kosugi, Yuki Matsuoka, Asumi Miwa, Mami Koyama
Keywords: man vs machine, cyborg, terrorist, metropolis, future, post-apocalyptic, manga, mecha, cyberpunk, police officer

Overview: In a utopian society created at the end of the third world war, a female warrior who has been plucked from the badlands begins to see cracks in this new facade. And what does this community have planned for the rest of humankind?
