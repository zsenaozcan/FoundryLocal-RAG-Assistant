Title: Bonobo (2014)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Matthew Hammett Knott
Main Cast: James Norton, Will Tudor, Tessa Peake-Jones, Eleanor Wyld, Orlando Seale
Keywords: commune, gay interest

Overview: Judith, an uptight divorcee, is appalled when her daughter Lily quits law school to move into a commune of hippie-misfits who live according to the behavioural principles of the bonobo monkey, a species famous for its 'make love not war' philosophy.
