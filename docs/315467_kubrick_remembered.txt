Title: Kubrick Remembered (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Gary Khammar
Main Cast: Christiane Kubrick, Keir Dullea, Malcolm McDowell, Ryan O'Neal, Stanley Kubrick

Overview: An 83-minute candid look into the life of Kubrick, including interviews with his widow, family, coworkers and actors, and featuring a tour of the Archive in London and an inside look into Kubrick's home.
