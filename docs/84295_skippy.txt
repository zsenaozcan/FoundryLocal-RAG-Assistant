Title: Skippy (1931)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Norman Taurog
Main Cast: Jackie Cooper, Robert Coogan, Mitzi Green, Jackie Searl, Willard Robertson
Keywords: shantytown

Overview: Skippy, the mischievous son of a wealthy doctor, meets Sooky in poverty-ridden Shantytown, and together they try to save Sooky's pet from a cruel dogcatcher.
