Title: Courted (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Christian Vincent
Main Cast: Fabrice Luchini, Sidse Babett Knudsen, Michaël Abiteboul, Claire Assali, Miss Ming
Keywords: court, jury, courtroom, legal drama, jury selection, criminal trial

Overview: When a feared judge of the French court, Xavier Racine, encounters a French-Danish juror, Ditte Lorensen-Coteret, at a murder trial, their shared past is slowly uncovered.
