Title: Bus Stop (1956)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Comedy
Director: Joshua Logan
Main Cast: Marilyn Monroe, Don Murray, Arthur O'Connell, Betty Field, Eileen Heckart
Keywords: montana, bus, diner, cowboy

Overview: Cowboys Beauregard Decker and Virgil Blessing attend a rodeo in Phoenix, where Decker falls in love with beautiful cafe singer Cherie. He wants to take Cherie back to his native Montana and marry her, but she dreams of traveling to Hollywood and becoming famous. When she resists his advances, Decker forces Cherie onto the bus back to Montana with him, but, when the bus makes an unscheduled stop due to bad weather, the tables are turned.
