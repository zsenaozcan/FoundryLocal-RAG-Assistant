Title: Girl Walks Into a Bar (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sebastian Gutierrez
Main Cast: Carla Gugino, Zachary Quinto, Danny DeVito, Emmanuelle Chriqui, Rosario Dawson
Keywords: bar, night life, independent film, los angeles, ensemble cast

Overview: A sharp-witted comedy that follows a group of apparent strangers in interlocking stories taking place in ten different bars during the course of one evening throughout Los Angeles.
