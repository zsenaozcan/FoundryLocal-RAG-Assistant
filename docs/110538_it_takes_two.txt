Title: It Takes Two (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: David Beaird
Main Cast: George Newbern, Leslie Hope, Kimberly Foster, Barry Corbin, Anthony Geary

Overview: Travis is due to marry Stephanie in a few days when he is convinced by beautiful saleswoman Jonni to buy a fancy sports car. The car looks good, but it turns out to be a piece of junk. Travis is determined to get satisfaction and he and Jonni hit the road to confront the crooked car dealer.
