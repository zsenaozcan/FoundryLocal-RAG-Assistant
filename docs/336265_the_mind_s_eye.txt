Title: The Mind's Eye (2015)
Genres: Action, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Joe Begos
Main Cast: Graham Skipper, Lauren Ashley Carter, John Speredakos, Larry Fessenden, Noah Segan
Keywords: psychokinesis, powers

Overview: The Mind’s Eye follows a drifter with telekinetic abilities who targets a doctor who is creating a synthetic telekinetic power serum.
