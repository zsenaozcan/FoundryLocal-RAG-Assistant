Title: Crawl (2011)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Paul China
Main Cast: Lauren Dillon, George Shevtsov, Paul Holmes, Georgina Haig, Bob Newman
Keywords: waitress, murder

Overview: A seedy bar owner hires a mysterious Croatian to commit murder, but a planned double-crossing backfires when a young waitress is taken hostage. A suspenseful, yet darkly humorous chain of events builds to a bloodcurdling climax.
