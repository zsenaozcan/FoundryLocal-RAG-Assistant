Title: The Seamstress (2009)
Genres: Horror, Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Jesse James Miller
Main Cast: Lance Henriksen, Kailin See, David Kopp, James Kirk, Lara Gilchrist
Keywords: attempted murder, island, murder, blood, needle, seamstress

Overview: The Seamstress was brought into being by the desperate curse of an innocent woman being tortured to death by a vigilante mob. Voracious for blood, the hideously-mutilated specter hunts a small group of friends who become trapped on the island where she died.
