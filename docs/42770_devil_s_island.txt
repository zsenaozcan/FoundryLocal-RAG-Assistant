Title: Devil's Island (1996)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Comedy
Director: Friðrik Þór Friðriksson
Main Cast: Gísli Halldórsson, Baltasar Kormákur, Pálína Jónsdóttir, Ingvar Eggert Sigurðsson

Overview: The story of families living in barracks, left by the US Army in Reykjavik at the conclusion of World War II.
