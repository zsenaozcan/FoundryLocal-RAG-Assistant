Title: Love Camp (1977)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Thriller
Director: Jesús Franco
Main Cast: Muriel Montossé, Ada Tauler, Monica Swinn, Esther Studer, Brigitte Meyer
Keywords: prison, women, prostitute, brothel, nudity, murder, jungle, erotic 70s

Overview: A group of women are kidnapped by guerrillas and brought to a jungle brothel where they are forced to work as prostitutes for the soldiers. Those who refuse to cooperate are decapitated by the cruel, sadistic wardress of the camp, who is privately interested in some of the women herself.
