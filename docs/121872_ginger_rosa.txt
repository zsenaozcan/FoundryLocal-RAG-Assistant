Title: Ginger & Rosa (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sally Potter
Main Cast: Elle Fanning, Alice Englert, Christina Hendricks, Alessandro Nivola, Timothy Spall
Keywords: female friendship, protest march, political protest, woman director

Overview: A look at the lives of two teenage girls - inseparable friends Ginger and Rosa -- growing up in 1960s London as the Cuban Missile Crisis looms, and the pivotal event the comes to redefine their relationship.
