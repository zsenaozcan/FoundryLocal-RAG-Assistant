Title: A Woman Is a Woman (1961)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Jean-Luc Godard
Main Cast: Jean-Claude Brialy, Anna Karina, Jean-Paul Belmondo, Marie Dubois, Jeanne Moreau
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Exotic dancer Angéla attempts to have a child with her unwilling lover Émile. In the process, she finds herself torn between him and his best friend Alfred.
