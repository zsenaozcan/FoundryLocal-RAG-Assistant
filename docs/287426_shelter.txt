Title: Shelter (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Paul Bettany
Main Cast: Jennifer Connelly, Anthony Mackie, Amy Hargreaves, Bruce Altman, Andrew Polk
Keywords: homeless person, immigrant, heroin, homelessness, sex scene, manhattan, new york city, nigerian

Overview: Hannah and Tahir fall in love while homeless on the streets of New York. Shelter explores how they got there, and as we learn about their pasts we realize they need each other to build a future.
