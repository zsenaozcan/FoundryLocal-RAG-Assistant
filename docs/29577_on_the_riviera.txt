Title: On the Riviera (1951)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Walter Lang
Main Cast: Danny Kaye, Gene Tierney, Corinne Calvet, Marcel Dalio, Jean Murat
Keywords: entertainer, riviera

Overview: In this fast-paced remake of the Muarice Chavlier vehicle Folies Bergere, talented Danny Kaye plays both a performer and a heroic French military pilot.
