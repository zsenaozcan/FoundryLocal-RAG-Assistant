Title: The Case for Christ (2017)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jon Gunn
Main Cast: Mike Vogel, Erika Christensen, Faye Dunaway, Robert Forster, Frankie Faison
Keywords: christianity, based on novel, court case, atheist, based on true story, religion, trial

Overview: Based on the true story of an award-winning investigative journalist -- and avowed atheist -- who applies his well-honed journalistic and legal skills to disprove the newfound Christian faith of his wife... with unexpected, life-altering results.
