Title: The Covenant (2006)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy, Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Renny Harlin
Main Cast: Steven Strait, Laura Ramsey, Jessica Lucas, Taylor Kitsch, Chace Crawford
Keywords: magic, male friendship, college, supernatural powers, teenager

Overview: Four young men who belong to a supernatural legacy are forced to battle a fifth power long thought to have died out. Another great force they must contend with is the jealousy and suspicion that threatens to tear them apart.
