Title: Agony: The Life and Death of Rasputin (1975)
Genres: Drama
Director: Elem Klimov
Main Cast: Aleksey Petrenko, Anatoli Romashin, Leonid Bronevoy, Alisa Freyndlikh, Yuri Katin-Yartsev

Overview: Details the life of the Russian monk Rasputin. The film shows his rise to power and how it corrupted him. His sexual perversions and madness ultimatly leads to his gruesome assasination.
