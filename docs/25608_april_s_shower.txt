Title: April's Shower (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Trish Doolan
Main Cast: Maria Cina, Trish Doolan, Randall Batinkoff, Denise Miller, Joe Tabbanella
Keywords: homosexuality, lesbian interest, woman director

Overview: April's Shower begins with a group of people gathered for a wedding shower for April. At first, it seems to be a normal gathering for such an occasion but, as time goes on, secrets and stories begin to be revealed. Alex  is a chef and a maid of honor at April's wedding. She ultimately reveals her true feelings which ends up taking an effect on everybody at the shower.
