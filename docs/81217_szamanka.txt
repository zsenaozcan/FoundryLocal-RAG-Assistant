Title: Szamanka (1996)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery
Director: Andrzej Żuławski
Main Cast: Bogusław Linda, Iwona Petry, Piotr Machalica, Pawel Burczyk, Stanislaw Jaskulka

Overview: The story of an anthropology professor Michal's who as the movie progresses develops two overwhelming obsessions. The first one is a mummified, 3,000 year-old, perfectly preserved body of a Shaman he and his colleagues have recently dragged out of a swamp, and the second – an enigmatic student he meets by chance at Krakow Railway Station.
