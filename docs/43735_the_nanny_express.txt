Title: The Nanny Express (2009)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Family
Director: Bradford May
Main Cast: Vanessa Marcil, Brennan Elliott, Dean Stockwell, Stacy Keach, Natalie Dreyfuss

Overview: With their mother dead, Emily and Ben torment their nannies to quit as fast as their father hires them. Till a nanny named Kate shows up and slowly wins Ben over. That's bad enough, but when her father and Kate start to fall in love, Emily sets out to break them up and match him with her ballet teacher.
