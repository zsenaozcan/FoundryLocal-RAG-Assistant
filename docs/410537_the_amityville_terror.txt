Title: The Amityville Terror (2016)
Genres: Horror
Director: Michael Angelo
Main Cast: Nicole Thompson, Kaiwi Lyman, Kim Neilsen, Amanda Barton, Trevor Stines

Overview: When a new family moves to an old house in Amityville, they are tormented and tortured by an evil spirit living in the home while trapped by the malicious townspeople who want to keep them there.
