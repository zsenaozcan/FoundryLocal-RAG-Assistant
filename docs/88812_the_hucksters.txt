Title: The Hucksters (1947)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Jack Conway
Main Cast: Clark Gable, Deborah Kerr, Sydney Greenstreet, Adolphe Menjou, Ava Gardner
Keywords: widow, advertising, pratfall, integrity

Overview: A World War II veteran wants to return to advertising on his own terms, but finds it difficult to be successful and maintain his integrity.
