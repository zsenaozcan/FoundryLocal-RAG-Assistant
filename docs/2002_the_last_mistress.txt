Title: The Last Mistress (2007)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Catherine Breillat
Main Cast: Asia Argento, Fu'ad Aït Aattou, Roxane Mesquida, Claude Sarraute, Yolande Moreau
Keywords: love triangle, loss of lover, lover (female), ancien régime, aristocrat, woman director

Overview: Secrets, rumors and betrayals surround the upcoming marriage between a young dissolute man and virtuous woman of the French aristocracy.
