Title: The Handcuff King (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Arto Koskinen
Main Cast: Miikka Enbuske, Emil Lundberg, Heikki Hela, Maija Junno

Overview: It is winter in the mid-1970s in Tornio, a village near the northern border between Finland and Sweden. Esko is a boy with a lot of problems. He has succeeded in getting on the wrong side of his gang.
