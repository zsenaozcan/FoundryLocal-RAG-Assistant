Title: Dr. Wai in the Scriptures with No Words (1996)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Ching Siu-Tung
Main Cast: Jet Li, Rosamund Kwan, Takeshi Kaneshiro, Charlie Yeung, Law Kar-Ying

Overview: A serial adventure writer with problems in his personal life lives out the adventures of his literary hero, King of Adventurers.
