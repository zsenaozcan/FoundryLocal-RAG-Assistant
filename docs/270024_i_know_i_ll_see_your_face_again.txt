Title: I Know I'll See Your Face Again (2001)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alex Stockman
Main Cast: Stefan Perceval, Stefanie Bodien, Senne Rouffaer, Jacqueline Bir, Josse De Pauw

Overview: Writer Joris (the author's alter-ego) has packed in his job and left his routine. He wanders through Brussels, pretending to be in other places and as far as we can verify lying to everyone. He stays in a dead-beat motel and meets other absurd people, never to any actual end. Will it be different when he meets Luzie and falls in love?
