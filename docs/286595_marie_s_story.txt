Title: Marie's Story (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jean-Pierre Améris
Main Cast: Isabelle Carré, Ariana Rivoire, Brigitte Catillon, Laure Duthilleul, Martine Gautier
Keywords: religion, disabled

Overview: Marie Heurtin is born both blind and deaf. Sister Marguerette wins her trust and teaches her how to express herself.
