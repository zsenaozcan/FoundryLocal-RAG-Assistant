Title: Dead Ringer (1964)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Paul Henreid
Main Cast: Bette Davis, Karl Malden, Peter Lawford, Philip Carey, Jean Hagen
Keywords: twin sister, twins

Overview: The working class twin sister of a callous wealthy woman impulsively murders her out of revenge and assumes the identity of the dead woman. But impersonating her dead twin is more complicated and risky than she anticipated.
