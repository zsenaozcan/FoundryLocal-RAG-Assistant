Title: Ace Ventura Jr: Pet Detective (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: David M. Evans
Main Cast: Josh Flitter, Emma Lockhart, Art LaFleur, Reed Alexander, William Haze

Overview: "I will try to be normal" 12-year-old Ace Ventura Jr. promises. Thats cool, except whats normal for him is finding missing mutts, kidnapped kitties or gone gators and creating hilarious chaos every step of the way.
