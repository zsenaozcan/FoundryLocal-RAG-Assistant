Title: A Town Like Alice (1956)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Jack Lee
Main Cast: Virginia McKenna, Peter Finch, Kenji Takagi, Tran Van Khe, Jean Anderson

Overview: In 1941, The advancing Japanese army captures a lot of British territory very quickly. The men are sent off to labor camps, but they have no plan on what to do with the women and children of the British.
