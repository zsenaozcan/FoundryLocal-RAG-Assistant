Title: The Gamma People (1956)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror, Comedy
Director: John Gilling
Main Cast: Paul Douglas, Eva Bartok, Leslie Phillips, Walter Rilla, Philip Leaver
Keywords: mutant, gamma rays

Overview: An American reporter smells a story when he is stranded in an Iron Curtain country where the local dictator is using gamma rays to transform children into mutated henchmen.
