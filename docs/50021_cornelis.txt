Title: Cornelis (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Amir Chamdin
Main Cast: Helena af Sandeberg, David Dencik, Hans-Erik Dyvik Husby, Malin Crépin, Johan Glans
Keywords: sweden, musician, biography, escape from prison

Overview: ”Cornelis” is the unique chronicle about one of Sweden’s greatest music legends - Cornelis Vreeswijk. An honest and gripping account of an exceptionally talented, but self-destructive artist’s intense life both on and behind the stage. A man who during his whole life was searching for love and confirmation.
