Title: Enthiran (2010)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: S. Shankar
Main Cast: Rajinikanth, Aishwarya Rai Bachchan, Danny Denzongpa, Santhanam, Karunas
Keywords: love triangle, artificial intelligence, android, killer robot, robot, scientist, dual role, robot human relationship, humanoid robot

Overview: Dr. Vasi invents a super-powered robot, Chitti, in his own image. The scientific body, AIRD, that must approve the robot, declines it based on its not having emotions and the ability to make rational judgment. A sudden flash of lightning evokes emotions in the robot, and Chitti is seemingly ready for integration into the human world. Then, Chitti falls in love with Dr. Vasi's fiancée Sana and turns on his creator.
