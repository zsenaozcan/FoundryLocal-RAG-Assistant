Title: The Capture of the Green River Killer (2008)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller, Crime
Director: Norma Bailey
Main Cast: Tom Cavanagh, Currie Graham, Ingrid Rogers, Jessica Harmon, Michelle Harrison
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A pair of girls seeking adventure beyond the their Western Washington trailer park encounter the area's most ruthless serial killer. Based on Sheriff David Reichert's book, "Chasing the Devil: My Twenty-Year Quest to Capture the Green River Killer".
