Title: The Mambo Kings (1992)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Arne Glimcher
Main Cast: Antonio Banderas, Armand Assante, Cathy Moriarty, Maruschka Detmers, Pablo Calogero
Keywords: mambo

Overview: The Mambo Kings tells the story of César and Nestor Castillo, brothers and aspiring musicians who flee from Cuba to America in search of the American Dream.
