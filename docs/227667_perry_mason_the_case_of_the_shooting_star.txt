Title: Perry Mason: The Case of the Shooting Star (1986)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Ron Satlof
Main Cast: Raymond Burr, Barbara Hale, William Katt, Joe Penny, Ron Glass
Keywords: detective, murder, courtroom

Overview: An actor rigs a fake shooting on TV with the connivance of his friend, the show's host, but the practical joke goes wrong when the gun turns out to contain a live round.
