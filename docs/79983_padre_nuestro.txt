Title: Padre nuestro (2007)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Christopher Zalla
Main Cast: Jesús Ochoa, Armando Hernández, Jorge Adrián Espíndola, Paola Mendoza, Leonardo Anzures

Overview: A Mexican boy smuggles himself to Brooklyn to meet his long-lost father - only to have his identity stolen upon arrival by an impostor who seeks to steal the fathers' fortune.
