Title: The Concorde Affair (1979)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Ruggero Deodato
Main Cast: James Franciscus, Mimsy Farmer, Venantino Venantini, Fiamma Maglione, Edmund Purdom
Keywords: airplane, airplane crash

Overview: A reporter tries to stop the crash of an aircraft after uncovering an airline's plot to save their business by sabotaging Concorde flights and have them decommissioned.
