Title: Wrong Turn at Tahoe (2009)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Franck Khalfoun
Main Cast: Cuba Gooding Jr., Harvey Keitel, Alex Meneses, Miguel Ferrer, Louis Mandylor
Keywords: bodyguard, revenge, gunfight, drug lord, gun shot

Overview: A debt collector for the mob (Academy Award(R)-winner Cuba Gooding, Jr.) finds his fate taking a series of treacherous turns after his powerful boss and mentor (Miguel Ferrer) is caught in a dangerous double cross with the most dangerous drug dealer around (Academy Award(R)-nominee Harvey Keitel) in this crime thriller from writer/director Franck Khalfoun.
