Title: The Brussels Business (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Matthieu Lietaert
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: europe, brussels, money, european

Overview: The Brussels Business is a docu-thriller that dives into the grey zone underneath European democracy. An expedition into the world of the 15,000 lobbyists in the EU-capital, of the PR-conglomerates, think tanks and their all embracing networks of power and their close ties to the political elites.
