Title: Floyd Norman: An Animated Life (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Michael Fiore
Main Cast: Cosmo Anzilotti, Jane Baer, David Bossert, Suzanne Bothke, Adrienne Brown-Norman
Keywords: animation, film production

Overview: Animator. Storyman. Troublemaker. At 80 years old, see how Disney Legend Floyd Norman, the first African-American animator at Disney, continues to impact animation and stir up "trouble" after the company forced him to retire at age 65.
