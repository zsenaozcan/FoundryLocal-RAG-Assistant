Title: Throw Away Your Books, Rally in the Streets (1971)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Shūji Terayama
Main Cast: Yukiko Kobayashi, Fudeko Tanaka, Sei Hiraizumi, Keiko Niitaka, Akihiro Miwa

Overview: Conditions have been better for the nameless protagonist: his grandmother is a shoplifter and his war criminal father and sister have an unhealthy, intimate relationship with the family rabbit.
