Title: Road of No Return (2008)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Parviz Saghizadeh
Main Cast: Michael Madsen, David Carradine, Michael Blain-Rozgay, Ernest Anthony, Shane Woodson
Keywords: drug traffic, hitman, covert operation

Overview: Road of no Return follows the final nine days in the lives of four atypical hit men who are secretly brought together in a covert operation to fight the drug trafficking epidemic in the country.
