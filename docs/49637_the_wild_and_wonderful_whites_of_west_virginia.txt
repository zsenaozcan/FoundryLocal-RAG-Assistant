Title: The Wild and Wonderful Whites of West Virginia (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Julien Nitzberg
Main Cast: Jesco White
Keywords: dancing, west virginia, independent film

Overview: A documentary about the White family of Boone County, West Virginia. The 1991 documentary The Dancing Outlaw focused on Jesco White, a famous mountain dancer. This film focuses on the other members of his family.
