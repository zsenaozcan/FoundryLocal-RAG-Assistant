Title: Bridal Wave (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Michael Scott
Main Cast: Arielle Kebbel, Andrew W. Walker, Jaclyn Smith, David Haydn-Jones, Colleen Wheeler
Keywords: nurse

Overview: With her wedding day rapidly approaching, an anxious bride-to-be has doubts about her pending marriage. At a romantic island resort, she encounters a handsome kindred spirit and must now decide if her "perfect" fiancé is really her true love.
