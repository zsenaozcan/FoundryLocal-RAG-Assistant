Title: This Could Be the Night (1957)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Robert Wise
Main Cast: Jean Simmons, Paul Douglas, Anthony Franciosa, Joan Blondell, J. Carrol Naish
Keywords: nightclub, secretary

Overview: To earn extra money, a prim schoolteacher takes a second job as secretary to the uncouth owner of a boisterous nightclub. Released in 1957, starring Jean Simmons, Paul Douglas, Anthony Franciosa, Joan Blondell, Julie Wilson, Rafael Campos, Neile Adams, ZaSu Pitts and J. Carrol Naish.
