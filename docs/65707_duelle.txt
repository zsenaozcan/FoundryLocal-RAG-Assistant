Title: Duelle (1976)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Mystery, Romance
Director: Jacques Rivette
Main Cast: Juliet Berto, Bulle Ogier, Nicole Garcia, Claire Nadeau, Hermine Karagheuz
Keywords: love triangle, moon, surreal, goddess, mysticism

Overview: The Daughter of the Moon battles the Daughter of the Sun over a magical diamond that will allow the winner to remain on Earth, specifically in modern day Paris.
