Title: Elephant White (2011)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Fantasy, Crime
Director: Prachya Pinkaew
Main Cast: Kevin Bacon, Djimon Hounsou, Jirantanin Pitakporntrakul, Weeraprawat Wongpuapan, Suteerush Channukool

Overview: An assassin is hired by a businessman to avenge the murder of his daughter by white slave traders in Thailand.
