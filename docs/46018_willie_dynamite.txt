Title: Willie Dynamite (1974)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Gilbert Moses
Main Cast: Roscoe Orman, Diana Sands, Thalmus Rasulala, Joyce Walker, Roger Robinson
Keywords: new york, prostitute, pimp, cops, blaxploitation

Overview: Willie Dynamite is a pimp who operates in New York City. Willie was a big success as a pimp, but now, just as fast as he rose to the top, he has hit bottom. A former prostitute who has become a social worker tries to get Willie to clean up his life while it is still possible.
