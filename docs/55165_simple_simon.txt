Title: Simple Simon (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Andreas Öhman
Main Cast: Bill Skarsgård, Martin Wallström, Cecilia Forss, Sofie Hamilton, Susanne Thorson
Keywords: brother brother relationship, small town, girlfriend, asperger's syndrome

Overview: Simon has Asperger's syndrome and doesn't like changes in his life. He lives with his big brother Sam and Sam's girlfriend Frida. When Frida can't stand having to adapt to Simon's peculiarities anymore she takes off, leaving Simon with having to find a new, perfect, girlfriend for his brother.
