Title: Little Big Soldier (2010)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Adventure
Director: Ding Sheng
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Leehom Wang, Steve Yoo, Lin Peng, Do Yuk-Ming
Keywords: martial arts, showdown, general, slavery, musician, blood splatter, escape, duel, soldier, dark hero

Overview: The story of a farmer forced into conscription, who has been looking to get out of the army ever since. His great chance arrives when he stumbles upon a wounded general from an enemy state, and he kidnaps him, intending to claim credit for the capture, which includes five "mu" of land, and most importantly, honorable discharge from the army.
