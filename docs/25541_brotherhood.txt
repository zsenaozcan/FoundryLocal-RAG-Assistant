Title: Brotherhood (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nicolo Donato
Main Cast: Nicolas Bro, David Dencik, Claus Flygare, Michael Grønnemose, Hanne Hedelund
Keywords: gay, male nudity, homosexuality, xenophobia, nazism, neo-nazis

Overview: Former Danish servicemen Lars and Jimmy are thrown together while training in a neo-Nazi group. Moving from hostility through grudging admiration to friendship and finally passion, events take a darker turn when their illicit relationship is uncovered.
