Title: Played (2006)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Foreign
Director: Sean Stanek
Main Cast: Imran Ahktar, George Berg, Patrick Bergin, Sile Bermingham, Gabriel Byrne
Keywords: prison, sentence, murder, criminal, criminal underground

Overview: An examination of the malevolent London underworld with it's despicable criminal underground. Ray (Mick Rossi) just finished an eight year prison sentence after getting set up. Now he is back on the streets to settle the score.
