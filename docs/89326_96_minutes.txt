Title: 96 Minutes (2011)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Aimee Lagos
Main Cast: Brittany Snow, Christian Serratos, David Oyelowo, Evan Ross, Anna Enger
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The story of 4 lives slammed together in a shocking moment. Intercutting between a carjacking and the separate stories of the 4 kids, we watch as they hurtle toward a life-changing end.
