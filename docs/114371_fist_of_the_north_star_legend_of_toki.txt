Title: Fist of the North Star: Legend of Toki (2008)
Genres: Animation, Action
Director: Kobun Shizuno
Main Cast: Hiroshi Abe, Takashi Ukaji, Yuriko Ishida, Ken'yuu Horiuchi
Keywords: martial arts, post-apocalyptic, fighter, nuclear holocaust, blood

Overview: An OVA which centers around Toki and his relationship with his brother Raoh, leading up to Toki's imprisonment in Cassandra and eventually his duel with Raoh. The OVA introduces another new character named Sara, a female doctor who watches over Toki and becomes his love interest.
