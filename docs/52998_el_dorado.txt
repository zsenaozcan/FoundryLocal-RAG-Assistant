Title: El Dorado (1988)
Genres: Adventure, History
Director: Carlos Saura
Main Cast: Omero Antonutti, Lambert Wilson, Eusebio Poncela, Inés Sastre
Keywords: spain, river, central and south america, gold, exploration, amazon, discovery of america, based on true story, jungle, conquest

Overview: The story of an expedition down the Orinoco and Amazon rivers in 1560 by Spanish soldiers searching for the fabled city of gold, El Dorado.
