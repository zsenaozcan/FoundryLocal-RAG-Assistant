Title: Drama of Jealousy (1970)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Ettore Scola
Main Cast: Marcello Mastroianni, Monica Vitti, Giancarlo Giannini, Marisa Merlini

Overview: A three-way love affair in the Rome of the early seventies. Construction worker Oreste and young fiancee Adelaide meet Nello, cook in a pizzeria. This love triangle often go to communist rallies, and enjoy the filthy beach of Ostia. Will the hostile environment leave a way to jealousy?
