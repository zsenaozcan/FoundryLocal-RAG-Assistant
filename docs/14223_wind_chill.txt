Title: Wind Chill (2007)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Gregory Jacobs
Main Cast: Emily Blunt, Ashton Holmes, Martin Donovan, Ned Bellamy
Keywords: terror, winter, paranoia, cold, supernatural, snow, student, crash

Overview: Two college students share a ride home for the holidays. When they break down on a deserted stretch of road, they're preyed upon by the ghosts of people who have died there.
