Title: Casual Sex? (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Geneviève Robert
Main Cast: Lea Thompson, Victoria Jackson, Andrew Dice Clay, Mary Gross, Stephen Shellen
Keywords: holiday, sex, dream, nudity, friends, resort, dating, attitude

Overview: Two girls go away to a holiday resort looking for a change of pace, hoping to meet some nice men for a change. They discover that they can't find the perfect man, and this forces them to reconsider their attitudes to men in general.
