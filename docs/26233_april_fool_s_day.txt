Title: April Fool's Day (2008)
Genres: Horror
Director: Phil Flores
Main Cast: Taylor Cole, Josh Henderson, Scout Taylor-Compton, Joseph McKelheer

Overview: The new "April Fool's" revolves around a party given by two rich siblings for their friend. Tragedy strikes when a friend falls over a balcony and dies. One year later, the teens responsible for the accident become the targets of a twisted killer who begins hunting them down one by one.
