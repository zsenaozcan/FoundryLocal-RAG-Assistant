Title: The Black Room (1935)
Genres: Crime, Horror
Director: Roy William Neill
Main Cast: Boris Karloff, Marian Marsh, Robert 'Tex' Allen, Thurston Hall, Katherine DeMille
Keywords: prophecy, castle, twin brother, baron, gothic

Overview: Karloff is once again chilling and brilliant in this highly-regarded dual role which showcases two sides of the actor. He plays twin brothers Anton and Gregor. It has been prophesied that Anton, the younger brother, will eventually murder Gregor in their castle's "Black Room." Gregor is an evil Baron who is loathed by everyone, while Anton is highly regarded and revered.
