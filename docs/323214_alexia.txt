Title: Alexia (2013)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Andrés Borghi
Main Cast: Sergio Berón, Paula Carruega, Pilar Boyle
Keywords: heartbreak, ghost, social media

Overview: Although Alexia, Franco's ex-girlfriend, has been deceased for some time, Franco still has her as a contact on his social network. When he decides to delete her and move on, something strange starts to manifest through his computer.
