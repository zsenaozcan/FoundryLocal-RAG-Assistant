Title: The Yes Men Fix the World (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Mike Bonanno
Main Cast: Andy Bichlbaum, Mike Bonanno, Reggie Watts

Overview: THE YES MEN FIX THE WORLD is a screwball true story about two gonzo political activists who, posing as top executives of giant corporations, lie their way into big business conferences and pull off the world's most outrageous pranks.
