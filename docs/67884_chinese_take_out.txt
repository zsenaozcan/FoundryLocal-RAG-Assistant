Title: Chinese Take-Out (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sebastián Borensztein
Main Cast: Ricardo Darín, Muriel Santa Ana, Javier Pinto, Ignacio Huang, Enric Cambray

Overview: A comedy that chronicles a chance encounter between Robert and a Chinese named Jun who wanders lost through the city of Buenos Aires in search of his uncle after being assaulted by a taxi driver and his henchmen.
