Title: Five Days One Summer (1982)
Genres: Drama
Director: Fred Zinnemann
Main Cast: Sean Connery, Betsy Brantley, Lambert Wilson, Jennifer Hilary, Isabel Dean
Keywords: alps, swiss alps, mountaineering

Overview: A 1930s Scottish doctor (Sean Connery) goes climbing in the Alps with an infatuated niece (Betsy Brantley) he passes off as his wife.
