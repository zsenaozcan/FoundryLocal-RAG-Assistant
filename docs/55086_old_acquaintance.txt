Title: Old Acquaintance (1943)
Genres: Drama
Director: Vincent Sherman
Main Cast: Bette Davis, Miriam  Hopkins, Gig Young, John Loder, Dolores Moran
Keywords: playwright, old friend

Overview: Old friends Kit Marlowe and Millie Drake adopt contrasting lifestyles: Kit is a single, critically acclaimed author while married Millie writes popular pulp novels.
