Title: The Unholy Three (1925)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Tod Browning
Main Cast: Lon Chaney, Mae Busch, Matt Moore, Victor McLaglen, Harry Earles
Keywords: robbery, silent film, sideshow

Overview: Three sideshow performers leave their lives of captivity and form a conspiracy known as "The Unholy Three" - a sideshow ventriloquist, midget, and strongman work together to commit a series of robberies.
