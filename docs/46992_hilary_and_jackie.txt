Title: Hilary and Jackie (1998)
Genres: Music, Drama
Director: Anand Tucker
Main Cast: Emily Watson, Rachel Griffiths, James Frain, David Morrissey, Charles Dance
Keywords: sister sister relationship, cellist, career, musician, concert, controversial, flautist

Overview: The tragic story of world-renowned cellist Jacqueline du Pré, as told from the point of view of her sister, flautist Hilary du Pré-Finzi.
