Title: Joshua Tree, 1951: A Portrait of James Dean (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Matthew Mishory
Main Cast: James Preston, Dan Glenn, Clare Grant, Erin Daniels, Rafael Morais

Overview: Part period melodrama, part film noir, part 50s road movie "JOSHUA TREE, 1951" is a portrait of screen legend and outsider icon James Dean as you have never seen him before. The film is written &amp; directed by Matthew Mishory and starring James Preston (ABC’s The Gates), Dan Glenn, Dalilah Rain, and Edward Singletary, Jr.
