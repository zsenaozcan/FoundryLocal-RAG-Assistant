Title: Not Another Teen Movie (2001)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Joel Gallen
Main Cast: Chyler Leigh, Chris Evans, Jaime Pressly, Eric Christian Olsen, Mia Kirshner
Keywords: underdog, ball, kiss, high school, school party, parody, crush, teenage crush, prom queen, sitting on a toilet
