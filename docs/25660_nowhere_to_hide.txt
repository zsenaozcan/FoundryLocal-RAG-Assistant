Title: Nowhere to Hide (1999)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Lee Myung-Se
Main Cast: Park Joong-hoon, Ahn Sung-Ki, Jang Dong-gun, Choi Ji-woo, Ahn Jae-mo

Overview: Over the next 72 days, Detective Woo will push his skills to the limits and his team of cops to the edge as they conduct a manhunt for an elusive criminal. Woo takes the law into his own hands as the hunt becomes personal, and as his target may get away.
