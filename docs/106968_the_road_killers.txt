Title: The Road Killers (1994)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: Deran Sarafian
Main Cast: Christopher Lambert, Craig Sheffer, David Arquette, Josh Brolin, Michelle Forbes

Overview: After a young boy is almost runover by a maniac on a highway, a re-encounter and confrontation by the boy's father with the driver sets off conflicts with a carful of maniacs.
