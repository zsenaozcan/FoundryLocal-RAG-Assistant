Title: Questioning Darwin (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Antony Thomas
Main Cast: Brian Protheroe

Overview: Literal and creationist interpretation of the Bible is the fastest-growing branch of Christianity in the U.S. This film takes an in-depth look at the views of these Christians who reject Charles Darwin's theory of evolution--while also examining how Darwin handled the question of God himself as he developed his theory of natural selection in the mid-1800s.
