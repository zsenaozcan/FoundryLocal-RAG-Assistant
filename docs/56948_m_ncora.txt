Title: Máncora (2008)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Ricardo de Montreuil
Main Cast: Jason Day, Elsa Pataky, Enrique Murciano, Phellipe Haagensen, Liz Gallardo
Keywords: beach, peru

Overview: Following the suicide of his father, haunted by inner demons and his hatred for the world, Santiago decides to escape from Lima and take refuge in Máncora, a beach to the north of Peru where it is always summer.
