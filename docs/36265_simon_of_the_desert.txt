Title: Simon of the Desert (1965)
Genres: Comedy, History, Drama
Director: Luis Buñuel
Main Cast: Claudio Brook, Silvia Pinal, Hortensia Santoveña, Enrique Álvarez Félix, Francisco Reiguera
Keywords: female nudity, nightclub, miracle, prophet, devil, rock band, desert, saint, hypocrisy, religious fanatic

Overview: Simon, a deeply religious man living in the 4th century, wants to be nearer to God so he climbs a column. The Devil wants him come down to Earth and is trying to seduce him.
