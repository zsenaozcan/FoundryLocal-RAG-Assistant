Title: By Hook or by Crook (2001)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Harriet Dodge
Main Cast: Silas Howard, Harriet Dodge, Stanya Kahn, Carina Gia, Joan Jett
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Shy, a small-town butch with a nagging messiah complex, heads to the big city to immerse herself in a life of crime. On her journey, she meets her match in Valentine, a wise-acre adoptee who is searching for her birth-mother. The two lonely grifters join forces and embark on a journey through the skid-row streets of San Francisco and learn the true meaning of poise under pressure and the importance of friendship.
