Title: Cries and Whispers (1972)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ingmar Bergman
Main Cast: Harriet Andersson, Ingrid Thulin, Kari Sylwan, Liv Ullmann, Erland Josephson
Keywords: sister sister relationship, sweden, dying and death

Overview: When a woman dying of cancer in turn-of-the century Sweden is visited by her two sisters, long repressed feelings between the siblings rise to the surface.
