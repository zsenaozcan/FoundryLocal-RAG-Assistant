Title: Witchboard 2: The Devil's Doorway (1993)
Genres: Mystery, Horror
Director: Kevin Tenney
Main Cast: Ami Dolenz, Timothy Gibbs, Laraine Newman, John Gatins, Julie Michaels
Keywords: supernatural being, haunted apartment

Overview: A beautiful young woman starts receiving messages through a ouija board, claiming to be from the former occupant of her apartment. The former tenant claims she's been murdered, but there's no record of a murder or even her death.
