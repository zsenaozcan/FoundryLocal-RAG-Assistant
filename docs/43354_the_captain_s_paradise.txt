Title: The Captain's Paradise (1953)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Anthony Kimmins
Main Cast: Alec Guinness, Yvonne De Carlo, Celia Johnson, Charles Goldner, Miles Malleson
Keywords: independent film, ship captain

Overview: Mediterranean ferryboat captain Henry St James has things well organized - a loving and very English wife Maud in Gibraltar, and the loving if rather more hot-blooded Mistress, Nita in Tangiers. A perfect life. As long as neither woman decides to follow him to the other port.
