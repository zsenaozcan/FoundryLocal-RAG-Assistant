Title: Skirt Day (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jean-Paul Lilienfeld
Main Cast: Isabelle Adjani, Denis Podalydès, Jackie Berroyer, Yann Collette, Karim Zakraoui
Keywords: adolescence, hostage, teachers and students
