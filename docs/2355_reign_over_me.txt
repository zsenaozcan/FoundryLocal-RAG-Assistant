Title: Reign Over Me (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mike Binder
Main Cast: Adam Sandler, Don Cheadle, Jada Pinkett Smith, Liv Tyler, Saffron Burrows
Keywords: confidence, trauma, leaving one's family, childhood memory, airplane, war on terror, music, loss of daughter, alone, cowardliness

Overview: A man who lost his family in the September 11 attack on New York City runs into his old college roommate. Rekindling the friendship is the one thing that appears able to help the man recover from his grief.
