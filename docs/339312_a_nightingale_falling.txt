Title: A Nightingale Falling (2014)
Genres: War, Drama, History
Director: Garret Daly
Main Cast: Tara Breathnach, Muireann Bird, Gerard McCarthy, Brian Fortune, Elliot Moriarty
Keywords: irish republican army

Overview: Set against a backdrop of a turbulent, war-torn Ireland in the early 1920’s, this is a story of three people and the unfolding events from a crucial time in their extradionary and tragic lives.
