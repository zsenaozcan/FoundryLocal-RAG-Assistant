Title: Babycakes (1989)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Ricki Lake, Craig Sheffer

Overview: Grace is an overweight mortuary cosmetician who falls in love with Rob, a handsome subway train conductor who doesn't even know she exists. When she finally devises a way to meet him, they discover that although they are physically different, they have a lot more in common than they think.
