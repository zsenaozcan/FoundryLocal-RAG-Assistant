Title: Kansas Raiders (1950)
Genres: Action, Adventure, War, Western
Director: Ray Enright
Main Cast: Audie Murphy, Brian Donlevy, Marguerite Chapman, Scott Brady, Tony Curtis
Keywords: jesse james
