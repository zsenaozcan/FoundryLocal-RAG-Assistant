Title: Shakes the Clown (1991)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Bobcat Goldthwait
Main Cast: Bobcat Goldthwait, Julie Brown, Robin Williams, Kathy Griffin, Florence Henderson
Keywords: clown, liquor, birthday party, alcoholism, dark comedy, cult classic, mimes, barflies

Overview: Shakes plods about his duties as party clown, and uses all of his free time getting seriously drunk. Binky, another clown, wins the spot on a local kiddie show, which depresses Shakes even more, and his boss threatens him with unemployment if he can't get his act under control.
