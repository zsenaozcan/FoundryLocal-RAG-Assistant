Title: Day of the Animals (1977)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror, Thriller
Director: William Girdler
Main Cast: Christopher George, Leslie Nielsen, Lynda Day George, Richard Jaeckel, Michael Ansara
Keywords: snake, camping, wolf, bird, wilderness, ozone layer, bear, dog, vulture, hawk

Overview: The depletion of the earth's ozone layer causes animals above the altitude of 5000 feet to run amok, which is very unfortunate for a group of hikers who get dropped off up there by helicopter just before the quarantine is announced.
