Title: House Of 9 (2005)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Horror, Mystery
Director: Steven R. Monroe
Main Cast: Kelly Brook, Dennis Hopper, Hippolyte Girardot, Peter Capaldi, Susie Amy
Keywords: suspense

Overview: Nine strangers wake up in a house with no recollection how they got there and no way out. The voice on the PA introduces them to a grisly game they must play. The prize is $5 million and their life.
