Title: The Devil's Rock (2011)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Thriller, War
Director: Paul Campion
Main Cast: Craig Hall, Matthew Sunderland, Gina Varela, Karlos Drinkwater, Jessica Grace Smith
Keywords: german, nazis, new zealand, black magic, occult, succubus

Overview: Set in the Channel Islands on the eve of D Day, two Kiwi commandos, sent to destroy German gun emplacements to distract Hitler's forces away from Normandy, discover a Nazi occult plot to unleash demonic forces to win the war.
