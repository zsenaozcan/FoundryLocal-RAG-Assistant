Title: I Spit on Your Grave (1978)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Meir Zarchi
Main Cast: Camille Keaton, Eron Tabor, Richard Pace, Anthony Nichols, Gunter Kleemann
Keywords: rape, castration, revenge, suspense, blood, gang rape, cruelty, hanging, cult film, rape and revenge

Overview: An aspiring writer is repeatedly gang-raped, humiliated, and left for dead by four men whom she systematically hunts down to seek revenge.
