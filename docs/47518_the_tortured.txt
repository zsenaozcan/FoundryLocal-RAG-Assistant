Title: The Tortured (2010)
Genres: Horror
Director: Robert Lieberman
Main Cast: Erika Christensen, Jesse Metcalfe, Bill Moseley, Chelah Horsdal, Fulvio Cecere
Keywords: wife husband relationship, death of a child, death of son, child murder

Overview: An upper-middle-class couple's life is destroyed when their only child is kidnapped and killed. Obsessed with revenge, the couple seizes an opportunity to kidnap the killer.
