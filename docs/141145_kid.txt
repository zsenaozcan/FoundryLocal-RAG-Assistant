Title: Kid (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Fien Troch
Main Cast: Gabriela Carrizo, Bent Simons, Maarten Meeusen, Rit Ghoos, René Jacobs
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A seven-year-old child, his brother Billy and their depressed mother live on a farm. Then something painful happens to the family and the kids are left to live by themselves.
