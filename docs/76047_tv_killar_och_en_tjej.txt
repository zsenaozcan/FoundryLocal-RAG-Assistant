Title: Två killar och en tjej (1983)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Lasse Hallström
Main Cast: Brasse Brännström, Magnus Härenstam, Pia Green, Lars Amble, Ewa Fröling

Overview: Three friends who haven't seen each other for seventeen years reunite and have a lot to tell about what has happened in the meantime. The film follows the friends for some time afterwards and then moves another twenty years in the future...
