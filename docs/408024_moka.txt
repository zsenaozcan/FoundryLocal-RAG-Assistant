Title: Moka (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Frédéric Mermoud
Main Cast: Emmanuelle Devos, Nathalie Baye, Diane Rouxel, Olivier Chantreau, Jean-Philippe Écoffey

Overview: Diane Kramer is led by one obsession: to find the driver of the mocha color Mercedes which hit her son and devastated her life. With a few belongings, some money and a gun, she goes to Evian, where she's learned the driver lives.
