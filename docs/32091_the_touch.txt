Title: The Touch (2002)
Genres: Fantasy, Action, Foreign
Director: Peter Pau
Main Cast: Michelle Yeoh, Ben Chaplin, Richard Roxburgh, Gabriel Harrison, Winston Chao
Keywords: buddhism, china, collector, tibet, thief, artifact, acrobats

Overview: A sister and brother, the last heirs of a family of acrobats, are called upon by a Buddhist monk sect to retrieve an artifact that their ancestors have protected throughout the ages.
