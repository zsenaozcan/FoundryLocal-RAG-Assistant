Title: Bloody Psycho (1989)
Genres: Horror
Director: Leandro Lucchetti
Main Cast: Peter Hintz, Louise Kamsteeg, Brigitte Christensen, Sacha Darwin, Nubia Martini
Keywords: murder, slasher, ghost, extrasensory perception

Overview: This is one of the five films legendary director Lucio Fulci supervised in 1989 to re-use some of the gory bits for his 1990 gorefest "A Cat in the Brain". "Bloody Psycho" features a haunted castle plus wimpy doctor Vogler,who is performing some sort of a psychic therapy on lesbian owner of the place.
