Title: Backstage (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Emmanuelle Bercot
Main Cast: Emmanuelle Seigner, Isild Le Besco, Samuel Benchetrit, Noémie Lvovsky
Keywords: woman director

Overview: An adolescent groupie zeroes in on her Blondie-like idol after the singer chances to cross her orbit on a publicity tour. Gradually their lives intertwine as, with near-operatic intensity, the film delves into the emotional dependency on both sides of celebrity culture.
