Title: The Boondock Saints II: All Saints Day (2009)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: Troy Duffy
Main Cast: Sean Patrick Flanery, Norman Reedus, Billy Connolly, Clifton Collins Jr, Julie Benz
Keywords: boston, sheep, friendship, sequel, revenge, ireland

Overview: Skillfully framed by an unknown enemy for the murder of a priest, wanted vigilante MacManus brothers Murphy and Connor must come out of hiding on a sheep farm in Ireland to fight for justice in Boston.
