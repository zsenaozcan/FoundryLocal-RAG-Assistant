Title: Jeff Ross Roasts America (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jay Karas
Main Cast: Jeffrey Ross
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Jeff Ross visits several cities across the country, roasting the towns and the residents in volunteer-only speed roasts. Roasting his way through cities including Seattle, Toronto, Las Vegas, Miami and Madison, Ross roasts a statue of Abe Lincoln in Washington D.C., gets roasted by John Rich in Nashville, and in Minneapolis, brings an old friend onstage to tell a very intimate story the way only Jeff Ross can.
