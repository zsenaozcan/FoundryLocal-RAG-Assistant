Title: Autoerotic (2011)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Adam Wingard
Main Cast: Amy Seimetz, Lane Hughes, Megan Mercier, Kate Lyn Sheil, Joe Swanberg

Overview: Joe Swanberg and Adam Wingard’s AUTOEROTIC follows four interconnected Chicago couples as they explore the boundaries of self-pleasure and sexual exploration. Through a unique blend of outrageous comedy and in-your-face sex, AUTOEROTIC insightfully illuminates the private sexual lives of America’s urbanites.
