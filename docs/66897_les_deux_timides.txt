Title: Les deux timides (1928)
Genres: Comedy
Director: René Clair
Main Cast: Pierre Batcheff, Jim Gérald, Véra Flory, Maurice de Féraudy, Françoise Rosay

Overview: Garadoux has beaten his wife. His lawyer Fremissin is young and very shy, and therefore, not very efficient... Two years after, Garadoux is trying to seduce Cecile, but she prefers Fremissin...
