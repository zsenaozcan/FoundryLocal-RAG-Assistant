Title: Mad Monster Party? (1967)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Horror
Director: Jules Bass
Main Cast: Boris Karloff, Allen Swift, Gale Garnett, Phyllis Diller, Ethel Ennis
Keywords: frankenstein, spoof

Overview: When Dr Frankenstein decides to retire from the monster-making business, he calls an international roster of monsters to a creepy convention to elect his successor. Everyone is there including Dracula, The Werewolf, The Creature, Dr Jekyll &amp; Mr Hyde and many more. But Frankenstein's title is not all that is at stake. The famous doctor has also discovered the secret of total destruction that must not fall into the wrong hands!
