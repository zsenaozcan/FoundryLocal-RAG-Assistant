Title: The Middle of the World (2003)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Vicente Amorim
Main Cast: Cláudia Abreu, Wagner Moura, Ravi Ramos Lacerda, Carol Castro, Sidney Magal
Keywords: romance, family drama, road movie

Overview: Romão, illiterate and unemployed, feels destiny drawing him on an odyssey to Rio de Janeiro in pursuit of a job and a decent life. A family of seven journeys 2,000 miles across the hinterlands of Brazil on bicycles. Along the way, the story explores the inner dynamics of a family facing a great challenge with the courage to pursue dreams.
