Title: Gambling City (1975)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: Sergio Martino
Main Cast: Luc Merenda, Dayle Haddon, Enrico Maria Salerno, Corrado Pani, Lino Troisi

Overview: Luca Altieri is a gambler. He likes cards and he is a master in playing poker. He is a cardsharper too. He begins working for "The President", who has many gambling houses and everything seems to go well until Luca falls for Maria Luisa. Unfortunately for them, she is the girl of Corrado, the son of "The President"...
