Title: The Fearless Young Boxer (1979)
Genres: Action
Director: Jimmy Shaw
Main Cast: Peter Chang Chi-Long, Hwa Ling, Casanova Wong, Ga Hoi, Lung Fei
Keywords: circus, martial arts, kung fu

Overview: While on a fishing trip, Shao Lung's father is killed by Wu Pa Feng in front of the young man's eyes. Lung takes shelter with his uncle's traveling group of Kung Fu acrobats and begins honing his skills so he can one day have his revenge.
