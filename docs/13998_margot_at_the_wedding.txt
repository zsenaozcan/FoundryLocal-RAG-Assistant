Title: Margot at the Wedding (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Noah Baumbach
Main Cast: Nicole Kidman, Jack Black, Jennifer Jason Leigh, Zain Pais, John Turturro
Keywords: jealousy, secret, independent film, neighbor, family

Overview: Margot and her son Claude decide to visit her sister Pauline after she announces that she is getting married to less-than-impressive Malcolm. In short order, the storm the sisters create leaves behind a mess of thrashed relationships and exposed family secrets.
