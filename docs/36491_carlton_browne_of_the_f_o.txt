Title: Carlton-Browne of the F.O. (1959)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Roy Boulting
Main Cast: Terry-Thomas, Peter Sellers, Luciana Paluzzi, Ian Bannen, Thorley Walters

Overview: Great Britain has had an international agreement for the last 50 years with a small pacific island. It has been ignored until the death of their king brings it to the attention of the Foreign Office in Whitehall. They decide to send Cadogan de Vere Carlton-Browne to re-establish friendly relations.
