Title: Castle Keep (1969)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Romance, War
Director: Sydney Pollack
Main Cast: Burt Lancaster, Jean-Pierre Aumont, Peter Falk, Bruce Dern, Патрик О’Нил
Keywords: castle, battle of the bulge

Overview: During the Battle of the Bulge, an anachronistic count shelters a ragtag squad of Americans in his isolated castle hoping they will defend it against the advancing Germans.
