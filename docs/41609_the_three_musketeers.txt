Title: The Three Musketeers (1948)
Genres: Romance, Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: George Sidney
Main Cast: Lana Turner, Gene Kelly, June Allyson, Van Heflin, Angela Lansbury
Keywords: musketeer, gascon

Overview: The hectic adventures of D'Artagnan, a young provincial noble who just comes to Paris to enter the musketeers. He will meet action, love, hate, the king and the queen as his impetuousness gets him involved in political plots... and of course virile and indestructible friendship with the three musketeers Athos, Porthos and Aramis. Written by Yepok
