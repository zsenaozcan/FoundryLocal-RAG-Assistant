Title: 9 Star Hotel (2007)
Genres: Documentary, Foreign
Director: Ido Haar
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: jerusalem

Overview: Slipping through the predawn darkness over highways, through traffic and across the border, Palestinian construction workers go to work clandestinely in Israel every day. Haar's raw, handheld photography follows workers who build their own border shanty community to enter Israel more easily, with no choice but to risk their lives simply to earn a living.
