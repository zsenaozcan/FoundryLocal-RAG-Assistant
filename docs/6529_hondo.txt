Title: Hondo (1953)
Genres: Western
Director: John Farrow
Main Cast: John Wayne, Geraldine Page, Ward Bond, Michael Pate, Rodolfo Acosta
Keywords: apache, native american, military officer, cavalry, 19th century

Overview: Army despatch rider Hondo Lane discovers a woman and her son living in the midst of warring Apaches, and he becomes their protector.
