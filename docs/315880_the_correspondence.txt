Title: The Correspondence (2016)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Giuseppe Tornatore
Main Cast: Jeremy Irons, Olga Kurylenko, Simon Johns, Shauna Macdonald, Simon Meacock

Overview: The relationship between Ed, a married astronomer and Amy, his lover, who spend their years apart, is based only on phone calls and texts. One day Amy begins noticing something strange in Ed's messages.
