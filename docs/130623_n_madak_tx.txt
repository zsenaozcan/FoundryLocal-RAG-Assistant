Title: Nömadak Tx (2006)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Raúl de la Fuente
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Nömadak Tx is a road movie documentary of Igor Otxoa and Harkaitz Mtnez. de San Vicente of the Basque Country in Spain who take their ancient Txalaparta percussion instrument to native peoples in India, the Arctic Circle, Mongolia, Algeria, and the Saharan Desert.
