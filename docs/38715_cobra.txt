Title: Cobra (1925)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Joseph Henabery
Main Cast: Rudolph Valentino, Nita Naldi, Casson Ferguson, Gertrude Olmstead, Hector Sarno

Overview: An impoverished Italian nobleman (Rudolph Valentino) takes a job with a New York antique dealer and falls in love with the secretary. Complications arise when he makes a date with the antique dealer's wife who mysteriously is murdered at the appointed meeting place.
