Title: Chicago Massacre: Richard Speck (2007)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Michael Feifer
Main Cast: Corin Nemec, Andrew Divoff, Tony Todd, Debbie Rochon, Joanne Chew

Overview: A horror film based on a real-life mass murder that took place in 1966. Richard Franklin Speck was a mass murderer who systematically tortured, raped and murdered eight student nurses from South Chicago Community Hospital in 1966.
