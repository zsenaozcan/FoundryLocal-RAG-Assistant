Title: Bye Bye Braverman (1968)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sidney Lumet
Main Cast: George Segal, Sorrell Booke, Jessica Walter, Godfrey Cambridge, Phyllis Newman
Keywords: funeral, journey

Overview: Friends gather for the funeral of a friend. Sub-plots unfold as the group attempts to link-up to attend the funeral of their friend, Braverman.
