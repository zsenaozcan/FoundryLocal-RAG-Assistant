Title: Homage (1995)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Ross Kagan Marks
Main Cast: Blythe Danner, Frank Whaley, Sheryl Lee, Danny Nucci, Bruce Davison
Keywords: obsession, widow, tragic event, fatal attraction

Overview: The young caretaker at the estate of a reclusive woman develops a dangerous obsession with her daughter, a sexy television star. Unexpected twists and turns.
