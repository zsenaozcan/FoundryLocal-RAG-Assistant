Title: Omagh (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Pete Travis
Main Cast: Gerard McSorley, Michele Forbes, Brenda Fricker, Stuart Graham, Peter Ballance
Keywords: bomb, terror, loss of son, loss of brother, loss of family, bomber, time bomb, death of a friend, northern ireland, car bomb

Overview: The movie starts at the 1998 bomb attack by the Real IRA at Omagh, Northern Ireland. The attack killed 31 people. Michael Gallagher one of the relatives of the victims starts an examination to bring the people responsible to court.
