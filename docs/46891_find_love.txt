Title: Find Love (2006)
Genres: Romance
Director: Erica Dunton
Main Cast: Christian Camargo, Alexie Gilmore, Traci Dinwiddie, David P. Emrich, Kristin Foster
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Find Love is the story of two strangers who aren't looking for love but find it anyway. It is a Twenty-Four hour romance ride that truthfully captures that first feeling of falling for someone.
