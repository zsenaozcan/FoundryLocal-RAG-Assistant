Title: Handsome Harry (2009)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Bette Gordon
Main Cast: Jamey Sheridan, Steve Buscemi, Aidan Quinn, Campbell Scott, John Savage
Keywords: independent film, woman director

Overview: An ex-Navy man carrying out the last wish of a dying shipmate renews contact with old friends to break the code of silence around a mysterious, long-buried crime.
