Title: The Groundstar Conspiracy (1972)
Genres: Action, Crime, Mystery
Director: Lamont Johnson
Main Cast: George Peppard, Michael Sarrazin, Christine Belford, Cliff Potts, James Olson
Keywords: secret, research, government, conspiracy, torture, explosion, sabotage

Overview: A break-in and sabotage attempt occurs at a top secret research institute and the culprit is cornered and captured. The problem is that he's been badly injured and claims to have lost his memory entirely. A cat-and-mouse game ensues between investigator Tuxan, the mystery intruder Welles and the people who sent him on the mission.
