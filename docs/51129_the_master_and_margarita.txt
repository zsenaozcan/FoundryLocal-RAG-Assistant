Title: The Master and Margarita (2005)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Romance
Director: Vladimir Bortko
Main Cast: Anna Kovalchuk, Aleksandr Galibin, Oleg Basilashvili, Aleksandr Abdulov, Aleksandr Bashirov

Overview: The Master and Margarita is a Russian TV mini-series, based on the novel written by Soviet writer Mikhail Bulgakov between 1928 and 1940.
