Title: Tintin and the Lake of Sharks (1972)
Genres: Adventure, Mystery, Family, Animation
Director: Raymond Leblanc
Main Cast: Jacques Careuil, Claude Bertrand, Micheline Dax, Serge Nadaud

Overview: Tintin is sent to guard Professor Calculus, who has invented a machine that can duplicate anything, and is staying in a village near the border of Syldavia and Bodouria. Rastapopoulous, an infamous and ruthless international criminal, tries to lure Calculus and Tintin away by kidnapping two children, who live nearby, in order to get his clutches on the machine.
