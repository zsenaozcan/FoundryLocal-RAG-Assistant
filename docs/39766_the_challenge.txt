Title: The Challenge (1970)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: George McCowan
Main Cast: Darren McGavin, Broderick Crawford, Mako, James Whitmore, Paul Lukas
Keywords: fight to the death

Overview: All-out war between the United States and an Asian country is averted when the two sides agree to settle their differences by each choosing a single soldier as champion and having the two men fight to the death on an isolated island.
