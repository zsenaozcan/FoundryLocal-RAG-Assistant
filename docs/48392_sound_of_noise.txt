Title: Sound of Noise (2010)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Johannes Stjärne Nilsson
Main Cast: Bengt Nilsson, Sanna Persson, Magnus Börjeson, Marcus Haraldsson Boij, Johannes Björk
Keywords: conductor, drums, musical, drummer, metronome, music store, operating room

Overview: A tone-deaf cop works to track down a group of guerilla percussionists whose anarchic public performances are terrorizing the city.
