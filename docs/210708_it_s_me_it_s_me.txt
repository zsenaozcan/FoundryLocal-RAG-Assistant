Title: It's Me It's Me (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Satoshi Miki
Main Cast: Kazuya Kamenashi, Yuki Uchida, Ryo Kase, Midoriko Kimura, Ryo Iwamatsu

Overview: Hitoshi Nagano (Kazuya Kamenashi), who works at an electronics store, picks up a cellphone left behind by a customer and goes about a scam. He calls the person's mother and pretends to be her son. He then gets the mother to transfer money to his bank account. Soon, Hitoshi gets a lot more than he bargained for. (Source: Asianwiki)
