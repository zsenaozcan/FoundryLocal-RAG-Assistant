Title: The Big Empty (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Mystery, Science Fiction
Director: Steve Anderson
Main Cast: Jon Favreau, Joey Lauren Adams, Rachael Leigh Cook, Daryl Hannah, Kelsey Grammer
Keywords: suitcase, bizarre, alien, independent film, neighbor, conspiracy, desert, fbi agent, cowboy

Overview: Struggling actor John Person agrees to drive a blue suitcase from Los Angeles to the small town of Baker, Calif., and hand it over to a mysterious cowboy in return for having his credit card debt of $27,000 paid off. Upon his arrival, John can't find the cowboy but receives an ominously head-shaped package he's supposed to hang onto. While waiting, John gets close to Ruthie, whose psychotic boyfriend, Randy, keeps threatening to kill him.
