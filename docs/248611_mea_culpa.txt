Title: Mea Culpa (2014)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Fred Cavayé
Main Cast: Vincent Lindon, Gilles Lellouche, Nadine Labaki, Gilles Cohen, Medi Sadoun
Keywords: police

Overview: Franck and Simon are both good cops. They work as partners. But their lives take a tailspin when Simon, driving drunk, causes a tragic car wreck. A few years later, out of the police, he is forced to take matters into his own hands when his family is in danger.
