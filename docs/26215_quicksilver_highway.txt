Title: Quicksilver Highway (1997)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Mick Garris
Main Cast: Christopher Lloyd, Matt Frewer, Raphael Sbarge, Missy Crider, Silas Weir Mitchell
Keywords: salesman

Overview: This film is actually two one hour stories, the first based on a Stephen King story called the "Chattering Teeth" about a man who picks up a hitchhiker and the second is based on a Clive Barker story called "The Body Politic" about hands that rebel against the body.
