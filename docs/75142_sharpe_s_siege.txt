Title: Sharpe's Siege (1996)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Tom Clegg
Main Cast: Sean Bean, Daragh O'Malley, Abigail Cruttenden, James Laurenson, Féodor Atkine

Overview: Sharpe, with his new commanding officer, is sent to capture a castle when news comes of locals who will rise against Bonaparte. However, he is somewhat distracted by thoughts of his wife whom he was forced to leave while stricken with fever.
