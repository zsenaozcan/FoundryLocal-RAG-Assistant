Title: East Wind (1993)
Genres: Drama
Director: Robert Enrico
Main Cast: Malcolm McDowell, Jean-François Balmer, Pierre Vaneck, Ludmila Mikaël, Wojciech Pszoniak
Keywords: historical figure

Overview: The true story of General Smyslovsky and the anti-Communist 1st Russian National Army receiving shelter in Liechtenstein in 1945 and successfully resisting Soviet pressure to be returned for execution in the USSR.
