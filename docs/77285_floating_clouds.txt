Title: Floating Clouds (1955)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Mikio Naruse
Main Cast: Hideko Takamine, Masayuki Mori, Mariko Okada, Isao Yamagata, Chieko Nakakita
Keywords: based on novel, bathing, forbidden love

Overview: A married Japanese forester during WWII is sent to Indochina to manage forests. He meets a young Japanese typist and promises to leave his wife. He doesn't and after the war, she turns up and the affair resumes.
