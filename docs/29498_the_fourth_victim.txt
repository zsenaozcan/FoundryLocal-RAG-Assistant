Title: The Fourth Victim (1971)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Mystery
Director: Eugenio Martín
Main Cast: Carroll Baker, Michael Craig, Miranda Campa, José Luis López Vázquez, Enzo Garinei
Keywords: drowning, giallo

Overview: A man's wife is found suspiciously drowned in their swimming pool and because he two previous wives also died strange deaths he is charged with murder and only gets off because his motherly housekeeper lies to clear his name. When a pretty blonde woman shows up and starts flirting with him it's clear from the start that she may not be who she seems to be.
