Title: Broken Blossoms (1919)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: D.W. Griffith
Main Cast: Lillian Gish, Richard Barthelmess, Donald Crisp, Arthur Howard, Edward Peil Sr.
Keywords: london england, suicide, china, boxer, buddhist, murder, racism, alcoholic, silent film

Overview: Broken Blossoms is an American silent film from director D. W. Griffith. This melodrama tells the love story of an abused English woman and a Chinese Buddhist in a time when London was a brutal and harsh place to live.
