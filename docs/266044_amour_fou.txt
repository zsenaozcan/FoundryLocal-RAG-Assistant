Title: Amour fou (2014)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Jessica Hausner
Main Cast: Christian Friedel, Birte Schnoeink, Stephan Grossmann, Katharina Schüttler, Hana Sofia Lopes
Keywords: suicide, biography, poet, love, woman director, 19th century

Overview: A "romantic comedy" based loosely on the suicide of the poet Henrich von Kleist in 1811.
