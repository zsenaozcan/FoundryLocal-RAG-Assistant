Title: Fanboys (2009)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Kyle Newman
Main Cast: Sam Huntington, Chris Marquette, Dan Fogler, Jay Baruchel, Kristen Bell
Keywords: fan, road trip, fan culture, rivalry, science fiction, urination, buddy, cameo, film fan, space opera

Overview: In 1999, Star Wars fanatics take a cross-country trip to George Lucas' Skywalker Ranch so their dying friend can see a screening of The Phantom Menace before its release.
