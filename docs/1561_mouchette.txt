Title: Mouchette (1967)
Genres: Drama
Director: Robert Bresson
Main Cast: Nadine Nortier, Jean-Claude Guilbert, Marie Cardinal, Paul Hébert, Jean Vimenet
Keywords: suicide, alibi, discontentedness and displeasedness, rage and hate, poacher, policeman, humiliation

Overview: Robert Bresson plumbs great reservoirs of feelings with one of the most searing portraits of human desperation ever put on film.
