Title: The Sixth (1981)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Samvel Gasparov
Main Cast: Sergey Nikonenko, Mikhail Kozakov, Vladimir Grammatikov, Mikhail Pugovkin, Larisa Belogurova

Overview: The Civil War in Russia has just finished. The sixth chief of militia came to a small town in the south. Five Glodov’s predecessors had been killed by a gang of elusive Vakhromeyev. Now Glodov and his crew are going to have a mortal combat with the smart gangsters…
