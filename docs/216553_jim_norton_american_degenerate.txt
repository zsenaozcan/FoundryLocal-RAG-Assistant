Title: Jim Norton: American Degenerate (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Shannon Hartman
Main Cast: Jim Norton, Michael Kenneth Williams, Robert Kelly
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: In the follow-up to his first EPIX comedy special Please Be Offended, caustic comedian Jim Norton continues to push every hot button he can find... all in the name of a good laugh, of course. With his trademark self-deprecating brand of humor, Norton outrageously covers a wide range of topics that somehow all lead back to sex.
