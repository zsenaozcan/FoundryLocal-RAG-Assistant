Title: Nude For Satan (1974)
Genres: Horror, Foreign
Director: Paolo Solvay
Main Cast: Rita Calderoni, Renato Lupi, Iolanda Mascitti, Luigi Antonio Guerra, James Harris
Keywords: mansion, car accident, gothic

Overview: Italian Gothic horror that tells the tale of a man who stops at a remote castle hoping to get medical help for an injured woman, only to find the inhabitants mirror the darker sides of the woman and himself.
