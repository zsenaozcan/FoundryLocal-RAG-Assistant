Title: Love in the Rough (1930)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Charles Reisner
Main Cast: Robert Montgomery, Dorothy Jordan, Benny Rubin, J.C. Nugent, Penny Singleton
Keywords: golf

Overview: When a shipping clerk is recruited by his employer to help his golf game, his boss insists he conceal his humble identity at the country club.
 Robert Montgomery plays Jack Kelly, a shipping clerk who gives the boss tips on the links while romancing his daughter (Dorothy Jordan) in this fluffy, addition to what must be one of the most obscure of all movie subgenres: The Golf Musical!
