Title: Dirty Rotten Scoundrels (1988)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Frank Oz
Main Cast: Steve Martin, Michael Caine, Glenne Headly, Anton Rodgers, Barbara Harris
Keywords: competition, luxury, seduction, con man, money

Overview: Two con men try to settle their rivalry by betting on who can swindle a young American heiress out of $50,000 first.
