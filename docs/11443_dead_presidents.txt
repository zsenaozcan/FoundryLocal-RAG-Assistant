Title: Dead Presidents (1995)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, History
Director: Albert Hughes
Main Cast: Larenz Tate, Keith David, Chris Tucker, Freddy Rodríguez, Rose Jackson
Keywords: money, loss of virginity, hiding in a dumpster, mercy killing, armored truck, crushed by a car

Overview: Depicts a heist of old bills, retired from circulation and destined by the government to be "money to burn". However, more broadly, it addresses the issues of Black Americans' involvement in the Vietnam War and their subsequent disillusionment with progress in social issues and civil rights back home in the United States, during the 1960s.
