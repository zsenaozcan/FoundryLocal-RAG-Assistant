Title: The Beast (1975)
Genres: Horror
Director: Walerian Borowczyk
Main Cast: Sirpa Lane, Lisbeth Hummel, Elisabeth Kaza, Pierre Benedetti, Guy Tréjan
Keywords: sex, forest, beautiful woman, stalking, werewolf, erotic movie, erotic 70s

Overview: The head of a failing French family thinks that fate has smiled down on him when the daughter of a wealthy man agrees to be married to his son.
