Title: Ice Cream Man (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Paul Norman
Main Cast: Clint Howard, Olivia Hussey, David Warner, Jan-Michael Vincent, Sandahl Bergman
Keywords: independent film, spoof, ice cream, slasher spoof

Overview: Poor Gregory, after being released from the Wishing Well Sanatorium, all he wants to do is make the children happy. So Gregory reopens the old ice cream factory, and all the unappreciative brats are reprocessed into the flavor of the day.
