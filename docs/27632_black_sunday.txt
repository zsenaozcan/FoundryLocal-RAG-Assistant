Title: Black Sunday (1960)
Genres: Horror
Director: Mario Bava
Main Cast: Barbara Steele, John Richardson, Ivo Garrani, Arturo Dominici, Enrico Olivieri
Keywords: witch, vampire, crypt, gothic horror, iron mask

Overview: A vengeful witch and her fiendish servant return from the grave and begin a bloody campaign to possess the body of the witch's beautiful look-alike descendant. Only the girl's brother and a handsome doctor stand in her way.
