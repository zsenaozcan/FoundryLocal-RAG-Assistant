Title: Presumed Innocent (1990)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Alan J. Pakula
Main Cast: Harrison Ford, Brian Dennehy, Raúl Juliá, Bonnie Bedelia, Paul Winfield
Keywords: loss of lover, lover (female), investigation, lawyer, courtroom

Overview: Life takes a nasty twist for D.A. Rusty Sabich when the woman he has been romantically involved with is murdered. He is assigned to investigate the case, but is soon charged with the murder.
