Title: White Rage (2015)
Genres: Drama, Documentary
Director: Arto Halonen
Main Cast: Miro Mäkelä, Jouko Hokkanen, Linus Tuomenvirta, Samu Loijas, Zaida Hämäläinen
Keywords: biography, docudrama, psychological drama

Overview: Documentary about a bullied man, who kept his school mass shooting rage inside himself.
