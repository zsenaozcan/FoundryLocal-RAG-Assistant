Title: Love Liza (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Todd Louiso
Main Cast: Philip Seymour Hoffman, Jack Kehler, Sarah Koskoff, Kathy Bates, Stephen Tobolowsky
Keywords: suicide, lovesickness, drug abuse, sadness, mother-in-law, independent film, model airplane
