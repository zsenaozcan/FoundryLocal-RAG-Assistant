Title: The Match King (1932)
Genres: Drama
Director: William Keighley
Main Cast: Warren William, Lili Damita, Hardie Albright, Glenda Farrell, Juliette Compton
Keywords: chicago, sweden, monopoly, tycoon, rise and fall, womanizer, counterfeit, downfall, counterfeiter, deceit

Overview: Unscrupulous Chicago janitor Paul Kroll uses deceit to fund a return trip to his homeland of Sweden. There, via ongoing continuing deceit and manipulation, he gradually attains a monopoly on the matchstick market in several countries and becomes an influential international figure. Based on the true story of Ivar Kreuger.
