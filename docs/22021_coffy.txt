Title: Coffy (1973)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Adventure, Drama, Crime
Director: Jack Hill 
Main Cast: Pam Grier, Robert DoQui, Sid Haig, Booker Bradshaw, William Elliott
Keywords: orderly, right and justice, vigilante, blaxploitation, afro, illegal drugs

Overview: After her younger sister gets involved in drugs and is severely injured by contaminated heroin, a nurse sets out on a mission of vengeance and vigilante justice, killing drug dealers, pimps, and mobsters who cross her path.
