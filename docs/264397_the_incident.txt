Title: The Incident (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Isaac Ezban
Main Cast: Raúl Méndez, Humberto Busto, Hernán Mendoza, Fernando Álvarez Rebeil, Gabriel Santoyo
Keywords: twilight zone, illogical spaces

Overview: Two parallel stories about people trapped in illogical endless spaces: two brothers and a detective locked on an infinite staircase, and a family locked on an infinite road...for over 35 years.
