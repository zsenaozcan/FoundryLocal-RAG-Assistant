Title: Nick the Sting (1976)
Genres: Crime
Director: Fernando Di Leo
Main Cast: Luc Merenda, Lee J. Cobb, Gabriele Ferzetti, Lee J. Cobb, Luciana Paluzzi

Overview: Nick Hezard, a young con man, wants to avenge the death of a friend of his and organizes a swindle trying to cheat Robert Turner, an American businessman he thinks responsible for his friend's death. He succeeds in getting a hundred thousand swiss francs and uses them to carry out the second part of his plan.
