Title: Private Detective 62 (1933)
Genres: Crime
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: William Powell, Margaret Lindsay, Arthur Hohl, Ruth Donnelly, Gordon Westcott
Keywords: murder, drug addict, private detective, gambler, detective agency

Overview: A former government agent in France, who has failed at an assignment and been disavowed, is deported back to the USA, where can only find work at a low-rent detective agency. He quickly gets involved with a woman with ties to a crooked gambler, who is a client of his agency.
