Title: Warm Bodies (2013)
Genres: Horror, Comedy, Romance
Director: Jonathan Levine
Main Cast: Nicholas Hoult, Teresa Palmer, Analeigh Tipton, Rob Corddry, Dave Franco
Keywords: post-apocalyptic, dystopia, zombie, zombie apocalypse, interspecies romance, based on young adult novel
