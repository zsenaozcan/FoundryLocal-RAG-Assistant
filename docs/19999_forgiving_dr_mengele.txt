Title: Forgiving Dr. Mengele (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Bob Hercules
Main Cast: Eva Mozes Kor
Keywords: holocaust, auschwitz, mengele

Overview: Eva Mozes Kor, who survived Josef Mengele's cruel twin experiments in the Auschwitz concentration camp, shocks other Holocaust survivors when she decides to forgive the perpetrators as a way of self-healing.
