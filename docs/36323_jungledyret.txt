Title: Jungledyret (1993)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Stefan Fjeldmark
Main Cast: Jesper Klein, Kaya Brüel, Jytte Abildstrøm, Anne Marie Ottersen, Mark Camacho
Keywords: musical

Overview: This film introduces us to Hugo, an one-of-a-kind animal who lives in a jungle. Youthful and carefree, Hugo is prone to playing practical jokes on his friends, Zig and Zag the monkeys. His idyllic lifestyle is interrupted when he is captured by CEO of a famed movie company, Conrad Cupmann, to be co-star in a Hollywood-style film. In order to return from Copenhagen to his jungle home, he must escape with the help of a newly found friend, Rita the fox.
