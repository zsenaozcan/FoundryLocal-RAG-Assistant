Title: My Chauffeur (1986)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: David Beaird
Main Cast: Deborah Foreman, Sam J. Jones, Howard Hesseman, E.G. Marshall, Penn Jillette

Overview: A feisty young woman accepts a mysterious offer to become a chauffeur for a Beverly Hills limousine business, much to the chagrin of her older male coworkers and the disbelief of her customers.
