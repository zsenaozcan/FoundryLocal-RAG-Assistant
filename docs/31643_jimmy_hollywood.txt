Title: Jimmy Hollywood (1994)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Barry Levinson
Main Cast: Joe Pesci, Christian Slater, Victoria Abril, Harrison Ford, Barry Levinson

Overview: Jimmy Alto is an actor wannabe who stumbles into the role of a lifetime. He becomes a vigilante crime-fighter, aided by his sidekick William, who has suffered a head wound and has problems with short-term memory. Jimmy's vigilante alter ego soon becomes a media wonder--but Jimmy remains a total unknown and his long-suffering girl friend Lorraine is getting fed up with the whole situation.
