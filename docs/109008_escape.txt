Title: Escape (1940)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Mervyn LeRoy
Main Cast: Norma Shearer, Robert Taylor, Conrad Veidt, Alla Nazimova, Felix Bressart
Keywords: germany, world war ii, nazis, concentration camp prisoner, countess, escape, nazi officer, dying mother, expatriate, rescue operation

Overview: An American goes to Germany to find his mother and discovers her in a concentration camp. With the help of an American-born widowed countess he seeks to engineer her escape.
