Title: The Gardener (1912)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Victor Sjöström
Main Cast: Victor Sjöström, Gösta Ekman, Lili Beck, Gunnar Bohman, John Ekman

Overview: The Gardener is a 1912 Swedish silent drama film directed by Victor Sjöström. It is mostly known for being the first film to ever be banned by the Swedish censor system.
