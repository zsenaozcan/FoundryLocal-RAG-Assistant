Title: Outland (1981)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Peter Hyams
Main Cast: Sean Connery, Peter Boyle, Frances Sternhagen, James Sikking, Kika Markham
Keywords: jupiter, wife husband relationship, marshal, wife, dystopia, earth, space centre

Overview: Thriller about an honest marshal in a corrupt mining colony on Io, Jupiter's sunless third moon, who is determined to confront a violent drug ring even though it may cost him his life. After his wife angrily deserts him, he waits alone for the arrival of killers hired by the company to eliminate him. Futuristic remake of "High Noon".
