Title: L.A. Confidential (1997)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Curtis Hanson
Main Cast: Kevin Spacey, Guy Pearce, Danny DeVito, James Cromwell, Kim Basinger
Keywords: corruption, detective, shotgun, morgue, f word, domestic violence, corpse, crime, district attorney, bandage

Overview: Three detectives in the corrupt and brutal L.A. police force of the 1950s use differing methods to uncover a conspiracy behind the shotgun slayings of the patrons at an all-night diner.
