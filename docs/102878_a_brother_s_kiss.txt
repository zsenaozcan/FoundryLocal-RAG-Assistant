Title: A Brother's Kiss (1997)
Genres: Drama
Director: Seth Zvi Rosenfeld
Main Cast: Nick Chinlund, Michael Raynor, Justin Pierce, Joshua Danowsky, Cathy Moriarty

Overview: Two brothers, Lex and younger Mick, are living in Harlem. Mick is a policeman, and Lex, who spent youth years in reformatory because of injustice after he confronted the cop who tried to sodomize Mick on the street, is living with his wife Debbie trying to make ends meet and failing. One day Lex calls Mick - he has a problem...
