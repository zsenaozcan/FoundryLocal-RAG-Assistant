Title: Planet Hulk (2010)
Genres: Animation, Action, Science Fiction
Director: Sam Liu
Main Cast: Rick D. Wasserman, Marc Worden, Mark Hildreth, Kevin Michael Richardson, Sam Vincent
Keywords: illuminati, marvel comic, superhero, based on comic, space opera, hulk, superhuman strength, sword and planet, beta ray bill

Overview: When the Hulk becomes too dangerous for the Earth, the Illuminati trick Hulk into a shuttle and launch him into space to a planet where the Hulk can live in peace. Unfortunately, the Hulk's struggle to escape makes a malfunction in the shuttle causing Hulk to land on the planet Sakaar where he is sold into slavery and trained as a gladiator.
