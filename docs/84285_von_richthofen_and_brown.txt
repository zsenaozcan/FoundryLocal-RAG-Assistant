Title: Von Richthofen and Brown (1971)
Genres: War, Drama, Action, Foreign
Director: Roger Corman
Main Cast: John Phillip Law, Don Stroud, Barry Primus, Corin Redgrave
Keywords: world war i, aerial combat, fighter airplane

Overview: The story of Manfred von Richtofen, the german air ace during the World War I and his struggle with the enemy aces and some jealous german officers.
