Title: Black Magic M-66 (1987)
Genres: Action, Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Hiroyuki Kitakubo
Main Cast: Ichirō Nagai, Shinji Ogawa, Yoshiko Sakakibara, Kōzō Shioya, Kazuya Sokabe
Keywords: android

Overview: A freelance reporter stumbles on a desperate military operation. An army chopper has crashed and its cargo has been activated. Now there are two killer androids running loose in the wilderness and have the daughter of their inventor as their target. Can the army stop them or can the reporter reach the little girl before the droids do?
