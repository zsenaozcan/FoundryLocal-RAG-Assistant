Title: Dead on Course (1952)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Terence Fisher
Main Cast: Zachary Scott, Robert Beatty, Naomi Chance, Kay Kendall, Colin Tapley
Keywords: blackout, pilot

Overview: A former pilot suffering from blackouts discovers that a fellow flyer is suspected of being mixed up with a web of smugglers.  While searching for his missing buddy, he unwittingly becomes entangled in a morass of suspicion.
