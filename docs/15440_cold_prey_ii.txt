Title: Cold Prey II (2008)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Mats Stenberg
Main Cast: Ingrid Bolsø Berdal, Marthe Snorresdotter Rovik, Viktoria Winge, Andreas Cappelen, Fridtjof Såheim
Keywords: nightmare, snow, hospital

Overview: Jannicke wakes up in the hospital. All of her friends are dead. As she walks through the dark corridors, she thinks she is left alone. But the nightmare isn't over yet.
