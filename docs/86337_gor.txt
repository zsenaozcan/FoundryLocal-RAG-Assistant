Title: Gor (1987)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Science Fiction, Fantasy
Director: Fritz Kiersch
Main Cast: Oliver Reed, Urbano Barberini, Rebecca Ferratti, Jack Palance, Paul L. Smith
Keywords: magic, planet, barbarian, ring

Overview: American professor Tarl Cabot is transported via a magical ring to planet Gor, where he must help an oppressed country overthrow its evil king and his barbarian henchmen.
