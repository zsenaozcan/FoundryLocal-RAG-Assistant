Title: Excuse Me for Living (2012)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Ric Klass
Main Cast: Tom Pelphrey, Christopher Lloyd, Robert Vaughn, Melissa Archer, Ewa Da Cruz

Overview: A charming, suicidal druggie must obey his rehab-clinic's demand to lead a seniors men's group or face incarceration and lose the love of his psychiatrist's daughter.
