Title: Eat Sleep Die (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Gabriela Pichler
Main Cast: Nermina Lukač, Milan Dragisic, Jonathan Lampinen, Peter Fält, Ruzica Pichler
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A young Eastern European immigrant working in Sweden is faced with a painful choice when she's laid off from her factory in the name of "efficiencies."
