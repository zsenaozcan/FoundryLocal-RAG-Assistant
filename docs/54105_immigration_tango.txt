Title: Immigration Tango (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: David Burton Morris
Main Cast: McCaleb Burnett, Elika Portnoy, Ashley Wolfe, Carlos Leon
Keywords: green card

Overview: When Elena, a Russian immigrant studying in Miami and Carlos her Columbian boyfriend run out of legal options for staying in the U.S. the couple switch partners with Betty and Mike, an American couple who are also their best friends. All is well until the stress of keeping up appearances to their family members and a savvy immigration enforcement agent eventually begins to take its toll.
