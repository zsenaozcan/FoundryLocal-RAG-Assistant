Title: All About My Mother (1999)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Pedro Almodóvar
Main Cast: Cecilia Roth, Marisa Paredes, Penélope Cruz, Candela Peña, Antonia San Juan
Keywords: barcelona spain, transvestism, transsexuality, drug abuse, transplantation, madrid, birthday, autograph, actress, friend

Overview: A single mother in Madrid sees her only son die on his birthday as he runs to seek an actress' autograph. Beside herself with grief, she returns to Barcelona to tell the boy's father about the death of the son he never knew he had.
