Title: Fiesta (1947)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance
Director: Richard Thorpe
Main Cast: Esther Williams, Akim Tamiroff, Ricardo Montalban, John Carroll, Mary Astor
Keywords: musical, matador

Overview: A non-aquatic change of pace: Esther Williams disguises herself in matador gear and secretly fills in for her twin matador brother (Ricardo Montalban) after he abandons the ring.
