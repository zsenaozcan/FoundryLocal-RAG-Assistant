Title: Cyber Seduction: His Secret Life (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Tom McLoughlin
Main Cast: Jeremy Sumpter, Lyndsy Fonseca, John Robinson, Kelly Lynch, Briony Glassco
Keywords: sex addiction, friendship, internet, swimmer

Overview: A high school kid (Sumpter) develops an addiction to Internet porn so intense that it begins to destroy his life and tear his family apart.
