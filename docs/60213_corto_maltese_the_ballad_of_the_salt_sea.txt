Title: Corto Maltese: The Ballad of the Salt Sea (2003)
Genres: Animation
Director: Richard Danto
Main Cast: Richard Berry, Patrick Bouchitey, Barbara Schulz

Overview: In 1913, laconic sea captain Corto Maltese, adrift in the Pacific, gets rescued by his bandit friend Rasputin who's taking two rich shipwrecked teens to an island where his boss the Monk will hold them for ransom. WWI complicates things.
