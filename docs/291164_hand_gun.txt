Title: Hand Gun (1994)
Genres: Crime, Action, Thriller
Director: Whitney Ransick
Main Cast: Treat Williams, Seymour Cassel, Anna Levine, Paul Schulze, Zoë Lund

Overview: Jack is in the midst of a major robbery, which leaves him injured and his accomplices dead. Jack manages to hide the $500,000 from the robbery before he makes his way to his death bed.
