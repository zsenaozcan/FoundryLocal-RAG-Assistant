Title: Play Misty for Me (1971)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Clint Eastwood
Main Cast: Clint Eastwood, Jessica Walter, John Larch, Donna Mills, Jack Ging
Keywords: disc jockey, stalker, obsessed fan, jaguar car, cinnamon

Overview: A brief fling between a male disc jockey and an obsessed female fan takes a frightening, and perhaps even deadly turn when another woman enters the picture.
