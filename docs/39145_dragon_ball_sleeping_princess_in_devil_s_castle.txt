Title: Dragon Ball: Sleeping Princess in Devil's Castle (1987)
Genres: Action, Animation
Director: Daisuke Nishio
Main Cast: Mayumi Tanaka, Nachi Nozawa, Hiromi Tsuru, Masako Nozawa, Tōru Furuya
Keywords: anime, dragon ball,

Overview: Goku and Kuririn are given an assignment by Kame-Sen'nin: "Retrieve the sleeping princess from Lucifer and I will take you as my students." But the mission proves to be more perilous than originally thought.
