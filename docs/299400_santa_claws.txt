Title: Santa Claws (2014)
Genres: Family
Director: Glenn Miller
Main Cast: Max Baroudi, Damoni Burkhardt, Ezra James Colbert, Peyton Shayler
Keywords: cat, christmas

Overview: Santa is allergic to cats, so he has a policy against delivering them as gifts, but little Tommy has been SO good, and all he wants is one small kitty. Santa says OK, but instead of one, the whole litter climbs into the sack. When Santa has a major allergic reaction, the kittens have to take over and deliver the presents on time.
