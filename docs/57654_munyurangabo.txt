Title: Munyurangabo (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lee Isaac Chung
Main Cast: Jeff Rutagengwa, Eric Ndorunkundiye, Jean Marie Vianney Nkurikiyinka, Jean Pierre Harerimana, Narcicia Nyirabucyeye
Keywords: rwanda

Overview: An orphan of the Rwandan genocide travels from Kigali to the countryside on a quest for justice.
