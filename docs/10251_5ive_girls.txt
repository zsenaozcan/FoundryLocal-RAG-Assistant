Title: 5ive Girls (2006)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Warren P. Sonoda
Main Cast: Ron Perlman, Amy Lalonde, Terra Vnesa, Barbara Mamabolo, Krysta Carter
Keywords: supernatural powers, approved school , girls' boarding school, little girl, demon
