Title: Men in Hope (2011)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Jiří Vejdělek
Main Cast: Bolek Polívka, Vica Kerekes, Simona Stašová, Petra Hřebíčková, Berenika Kohoutová

Overview: Charming bon vivant Rudolf is convinced that infidelity is the basis for a happy marriage, since, after all, a woman does not want to be bored with her partner.
