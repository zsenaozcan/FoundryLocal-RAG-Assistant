Title: Shark Week (2012)
Genres: Horror
Director: Christopher Ray
Main Cast: Patrick Bergin, Yancy Butler, Josh Allen, Erin Coker, Bart Baggett
Keywords: shark attack, shark

Overview: A group of complete strangers find themselves isolated by a wealthy madman on his island compound. They are forced into a horrifying gauntlet where they must survive a barrage of ever deadlier species of shark.
