Title: Nate Bargatze: Full Time Magic (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ryan Polito
Main Cast: Nate Bargatze
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Filmed at the Gramercy Theatre in New York, the hilarious and charming “Full Time Magic” is Nate Bargatze’s first one-hour special. It’s a good thing after wanting to quit comedy early on, he stayed with it simply because he didn’t know who to quit to.
