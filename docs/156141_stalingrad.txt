Title: Stalingrad (1989)
Genres: War, Drama, History
Director: Yuri Ozerov
Main Cast: Mikhail Ulyanov, Archil Gomiashvili, Sergey Nikonenko, Boris Nevzorov, Bruno Frejndlikh

Overview: The WWII pivotal battle of Stalingrad is shown through the eyes of the soldiers and officers on both sides of the war.
