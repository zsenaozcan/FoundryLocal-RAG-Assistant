Title: Sabata (1969)
Genres: Action, Foreign, Western
Director: Gianfranco Parolini
Main Cast: Lee Van Cleef, William Berger, Ignazio Spalla, Aldo Canti, Franco Ressel
Keywords: sawed off shotgun, spaghetti western

Overview: Several pillars of society have robbed an Army safe containing $100,000 so they can buy the land upon which the coming railroad will be built. But they haven't reckoned on the presence of the master gunslinger, Sabata.
