Title: 7 Kilos in 7 Days (1986)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Luca Verdone
Main Cast: Renato Pozzetto, Carlo Verdone, Silvia Annichiarico, Tiziana Pini, Elena Fabrizi

Overview: Two not very clever young doctors open a fitness center and promise to let people lose seven kilos in seven days. When the enterprise fails they open a gourmet restaurant.
