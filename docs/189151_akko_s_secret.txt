Title: Akko's Secret (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Fantasy
Director: Taisuke  Kawamura
Main Cast: Haruka Ayase, Masaki Okada, Takeshi Kaga, Masako Motai, Muga Tsukaji
Keywords: based on manga

Overview: Young Atsuko Kagami comes into possession of a magical mirror that lets her transform into anything she wishes. Atsuko Kagami then attempts to save a company which is about to be sold by using her transformation abilities. She also falls in love as a 22-year-old college student.
