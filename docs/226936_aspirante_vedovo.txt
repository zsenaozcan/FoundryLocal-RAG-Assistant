Title: Aspirante vedovo (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Massimo Venier
Main Cast: Fabio De Luigi, Luciana Littizzetto, Alessandro Besentini, Roberto Citran, Tatti Sanguineti

Overview: A ne'er-do-well who's married to a millionnaire realizes his financial trouble might be solved if his wife was dead - and sets out a plot to achieve just that. A remake of Dino Risi's "il vedovo".
