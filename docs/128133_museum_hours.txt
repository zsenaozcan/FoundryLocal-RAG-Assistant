Title: Museum Hours (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jem Cohen
Main Cast: Mary Margaret O'Hara, Bobby Sommer, Ela Piplits

Overview: At the Kunsthistorisches Art Museum in Vienna, a museum guard and a visiting out-of-towner find refuge in life, art, and each other, in Jem Cohen’s painterly rumination on how art influences and echoes contemporary society. (TIFF)
