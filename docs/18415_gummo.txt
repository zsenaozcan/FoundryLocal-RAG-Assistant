Title: Gummo (1997)
Genres: Drama
Director: Harmony Korine
Main Cast: Jacob Sewell, Darby Dougherty, Chloë Sevigny, James Lawhorn, Linda Manz
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Solomon and Tummler are two teenagers killing time in Xenia, Ohio, a small town that has never recovered from the tornado that ravaged the community in the 1970s.
