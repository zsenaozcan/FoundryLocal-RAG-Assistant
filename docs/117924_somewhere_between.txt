Title: Somewhere Between (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Linda Goldstein Knowlton
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: california, pennsylvania, massachusetts, adoption, chinese, nashville tennessee, newburyport massachusetts, berkeley california, interracial adoption, heritage

Overview: Questions of race, identity and heritage are explored through the lives of young American women growing up as adoptees from China. These four distinct individuals reflect on their experiences as members of transracial families.
