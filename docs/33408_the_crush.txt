Title: The Crush (1993)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Alan Shapiro
Main Cast: Cary Elwes, Alicia Silverstone, Jennifer Rubin, Kurtwood Smith, Amber Benson
Keywords: journalist, obsession, revenge, suspense, erotic thriller

Overview: A precocious and obsessive teenager develops a crush on a naive writer with harrowing consequences.
