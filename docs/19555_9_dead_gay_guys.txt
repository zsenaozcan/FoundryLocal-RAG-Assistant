Title: 9 Dead Gay Guys (2003)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Lab Ky Mo
Main Cast: Glen Mulhern, Brendan Mackey, Steven Berkoff, Michael Praed
Keywords: male nudity, dark comedy, gay interest, criminal

Overview: The story of two lads from Belfast as they stumble their way through the London gay underworld in search of 'gainful employment'. This being the offering of sexual favors to older gay men in order to subsidize their respective giros.
