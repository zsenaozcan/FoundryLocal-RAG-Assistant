Title: Chain Camera (2001)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Kirby Dick
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Award-winning director Kirby Dick gave video cameras to 10 students to record their lives at Los Angeles's John Marshall High School with no limitations on what they could shoot. After one week, the cameras were given to 10 new students, and so on, forming a virtual chain letter and a portrait of young America at the turn of the 21st century.
