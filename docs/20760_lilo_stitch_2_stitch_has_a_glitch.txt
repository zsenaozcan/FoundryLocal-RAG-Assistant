Title: Lilo & Stitch 2: Stitch has a Glitch (2005)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Family
Director: Michael LaBash
Main Cast: Tia Carrere, Matt Corboy, Dakota Fanning, Jillian Henry, Jason Scott Lee

Overview: Now, we find the rowdy extraterrestrial getting used to life with his new ohana. However, a malfunction in the ultimate creation of Dr. Jumba soon emerges, which reinstates his destructive programming and threatens to both ruin his friendship with Lilo and to short him out for good!
