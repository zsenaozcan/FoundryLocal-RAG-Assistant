Title: Kinyarwanda (2011)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Alrick Brown
Main Cast: Cleophas Kabasita, Edouard Bamporiki, Cassandra Freeman, Mazimpaka Kennedy
Keywords: rwanda, hutu, tutsi, genocide, tolerance, genocide in rwanda

Overview: A young Tutsi woman and a young Hutu man fall in love amid chaos; a soldier struggles to foster a greater good while absent from her family; and a priest grapples with his faith in the face of unspeakable horror.
