Title: You and I (2006)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Comedy, Romance, Foreign
Director: Julie Lopes-Curval
Main Cast: Marion Cotillard, Julie Depardieu, Tomer Sisley, Jonathan Zaccaï, Chantal Lauby
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In Paris, Ariane and Lena are sisters. Ariane writes photo novellas for the magazine "Toi et Moi." She's emotional and her long-time boyfriend, Farid, has her in a state because he won't propose, won't ask her to live with him, and often is unavailable. Lena is a cellist, quiet and circumspect in life and in her playing. She lives without passion with her boyfriend François; they're talking about having a baby.
