Title: The Nutcracker Prince (1990)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Paul Schibli
Main Cast: Kiefer Sutherland, Megan Follows, Mike MacDonald, Peter O'Toole, Phyllis Diller
Keywords: holiday, musical

Overview: After young Clara receives a wooden nutcracker as a Christmas gift, she dreams about a fantastical battle between her Nutcracker Prince and the evil Mouse King. At stake is the Nutcracker's freedom - and Clara's future happiness.
