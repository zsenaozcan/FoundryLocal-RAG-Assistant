Title: The 39 Steps (1959)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action, Comedy, Thriller, Crime
Director: Ralph Thomas
Main Cast: Kenneth More, Taina Elg, James Hayter, Barry Jones, Barbara Steele
Keywords: based on novel, remake, chase scene

Overview: Remake of the classic hitchcock movie. Whilst generally not as good, it is a rather fun movie, and more lighthearted than the original. This is largely due to Kenneth More's acting.
