Title: Breakthrough (1979)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Andrew V. McLaglen
Main Cast: Richard Burton, Rod Steiger, Helmut Griem, Klaus Löwitsch, Michael Parks
Keywords: assassination, world war ii, adolf hitler, normandy, troops

Overview: Starting in late May 1944, during the German retreat on the Eastern Front, Captain Stransky (Helmut Griem) orders Sergeant Steiner (Richard Burton) to blow up a railway tunnel to prevent Russian forces from using it. Steiner's platoon fails in its mission by coming up against a Russian tank. Steiner then takes a furlough to Paris just as the Allies launch their invasion of Normandy.
