Title: St Trinian's 2: The Legend of Fritton's Gold (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Adventure
Director: Barnaby Thompson
Main Cast: Talulah Riley, Rupert Everett, Gemma Arterton, David Tennant, Colin Firth
Keywords: treasure hunt, girls' boarding school, pirate

Overview: The girls of St. Trinians are on the hunt for buried treasure after discovering headmistress Miss Fritton is related to a famous pirate.
