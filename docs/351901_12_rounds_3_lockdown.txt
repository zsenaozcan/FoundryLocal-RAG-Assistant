Title: 12 Rounds 3: Lockdown (2015)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Stephen Reynolds
Main Cast: Jonathan Good, Roger Cross, Daniel Cudmore, Lochlyn Munro, Ty Olsson
Keywords: cat and mouse, family, revenge drama

Overview: Lockdown Follows a police officer who returns to duty after recovering from a gun shot wound to discover incriminating evidence of illegal activities against those closest to him. He quickly finds himself trapped inside his own precinct, hunted and in search of the truth, as the crooked cops stop at nothing to recover the evidence.
