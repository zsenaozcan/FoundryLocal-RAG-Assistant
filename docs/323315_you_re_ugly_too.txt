Title: You're Ugly Too (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mark Noonan
Main Cast: Lauren Kinsella, Aidan Gillen, Erika Sainte, George Pistereanu, Patrick Murray

Overview: After her mother’s death, Stacey moves with her uncle Will to a remote region in the Irish midlands. As the two cautiously get to know each other, they have to deal with the dark shadows of the past.
