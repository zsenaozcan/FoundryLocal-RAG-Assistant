Title: Emperor (2012)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Peter Webber
Main Cast: Matthew Fox, Tommy Lee Jones, Eriko Hatsune, Masayoshi Haneda, Kaori Momoi
Keywords: atomic bomb, emperor, japanese surrender, u.s. military

Overview: As the Japanese surrender at the end of WWII, Gen. Fellers is tasked with deciding if Emperor Hirohito will be hanged as a war criminal. Influencing his ruling is his quest to find Aya, an exchange student he met years earlier in the U.S.
