Title: Iberia (2005)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Carlos Saura
Main Cast: Sara Baras, Antonio Canales, Marta Carrasco

Overview: Adaptation of the suite "Iberia" by Isaac Albeniz. Documentary about the world of flamenco. The story arises from the music itself and those who interpret it: the musicians and dancers. The film recreates and reinvents musical pieces and merges classical ballet, contemporary and Spanish dance and flamenco
