Title: Palm Trees in the Snow (2015)
Genres: Romance
Director: Fernando González Molina
Main Cast: Mario Casas, Adriana Ugarte, Macarena García, Laia Costa, Berta Vázquez

Overview: Kilian is a young man who has never left the mountains of Huesca which saw him grow up. In 1953 he will travel to the exotic island of Fernando Poo to work in a cacao field alongside his father and his brother. During 20 years in this island he will undertake a journey towards maturity and knowledge, but will also have to deal with pain and loss.
