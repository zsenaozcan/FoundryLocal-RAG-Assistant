Title: A Southern Yankee (1948)
Genres: Comedy, History, War
Director: Edward Sedgwick
Main Cast: Red Skelton, Brian Donlevy, Arlene Dahl, George Coulouris, Lloyd Gough
Keywords: confederate spy

Overview: Red Skelton plays Aubrey Filmore, a feather-brained but lovable bellboy who dreams of becoming an agent for the Union's secret service during the Civil War.
