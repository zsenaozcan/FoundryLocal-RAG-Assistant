Title: Bless Me, Ultima (2013)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Carl Franklin
Main Cast: Luke Ganalon, Miriam Colón, Benito Martinez, Dolores Heredia, Castulo Guerra

Overview: In a village in New Mexico, the life of young farm boy Antonio is dramatically changed when an old medicine woman joins his household. This affecting coming-of-age tale recounts Antonio's experiences to reveal the spiritual conflict in his community.
