Title: Midnight's Children (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Deepa Mehta
Main Cast: Satya Bhabha, Shahana Goswami, Shabana Azmi, Rajat Kapoor, Siddharth
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A Canadian-Brith film adaptation of Salman Rushdie's novel of the same name. The film tells the story of a pair of children born within moments of India gaining independence from England grow up in the country that is nothing like their parent's generation.
