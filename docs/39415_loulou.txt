Title: Loulou (1980)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Maurice Pialat
Main Cast: Isabelle Huppert, Gérard Depardieu, Guy Marchand, Humbert Balsan, Christian Boucher

Overview: This 1980 French film features Isabelle Huppert as a restless young woman who ditches her boring, tempramental boyfriend for a wild, leather-clad bad boy (played by an incandescent, youthful Gerard Depardieu).
