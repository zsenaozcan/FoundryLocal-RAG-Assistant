Title: The Big Store (1973)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Claude Zidi
Main Cast: Gérard Rinaldi, Jean Sarrus, Gérard Filipelli, Jean-Guy Fechner, Michel Galabru

Overview: Four guys working for a small grocer in trouble, declare war on a new giant neighborhood supermarket by attempting several coups. A film about the big store taking over the business of smaller stores.
