Title: The Last Horror Movie (2004)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Julian Richards
Main Cast: Kevin Howarth, Mark Stevenson, Antonia Beamish, Christabel Muir, Jonathan Coote
Keywords: found footage

Overview: A serial killer uses a horror video rental to lure his next victim. What begins as a teen slasher transforms into a disturbing journey through the mind of Max Parry, a mild mannered wedding photographer with a taste for human flesh.
