Title: Lackawanna Blues (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: George C. Wolfe
Main Cast: S. Epatha Merkerson, Marcus Carl Franklin, Yasiin Bey, Louis Gossett, Jr., Macy Gray

Overview: In a story fueled by rhythm and blues, a young boy's life is shaped by love and the stories of a cast of characters in the boarding house where he lives in 1960s Lackawanna, New York.
