Title: Kapoor & Sons (Since 1921) (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Shakun Batra
Main Cast: Sidharth Malhotra, Alia Bhatt, Fawad Afzal Khan, Rishi Kapoor, Rajat Kapoor

Overview: A story revolving around a dysfunctional family of two brothers who visit their parents and grandfather in Coonoor, and end up falling for the same woman.
