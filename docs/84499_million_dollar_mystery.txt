Title: Million Dollar Mystery (1987)
Genres: Comedy, Thriller
Director: Richard Fleischer
Main Cast: Tom Bosley, Rick Overton, Eddie Deezen, Kevin Pollak, Penny Baker
Keywords: comedy

Overview: A dying man in a roadside diner tells the other patrons about money he has hidden in four places.  But instead of directions, he leaves them only with clues they must solve in order to claim the loot.
