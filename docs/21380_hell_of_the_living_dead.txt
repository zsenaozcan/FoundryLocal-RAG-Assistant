Title: Hell of the Living Dead (1980)
Genres: Horror
Director: Bruno Mattei
Main Cast: Margit Evelyn Newton, Franco Garofalo, Selan Karay, José Gras, Gabriel Renom
Keywords: nuclear power plant, zombie, video nasty, chemical disaster

Overview: A tough female reporter and her cameraman boyfriend team up with a four-man commando unit in the New Guinea jungle whom are fighting flesh-eating zombies.
