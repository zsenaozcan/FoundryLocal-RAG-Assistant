Title: Les papillons japonais (1908)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Segundo de Chomón
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Two Japanese paint a silk worm on a screen. The insect comes alive and begins to spin its cocoon. He is soon trapped in his prison silk. The cocoon is now well in sight. He suddenly opens and a beautiful butterfly takes flight. After fluttered to the right and left, the beautiful insect lands and then begin a series of transformations.
