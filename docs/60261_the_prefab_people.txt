Title: The Prefab People (1982)
Genres: Drama
Director: Béla Tarr
Main Cast: Judit Pogány, Koltai Róbert, Kyri Ambrus, Jánosné Bráda, János Fábián
Keywords: wife husband relationship, frustration, family relationships, black and white, hungarian, realism, social realism, council estate

Overview: Using verite conventions, a young couple with a baby and a child are worn away by the monotony of their lives.
