Title: Maniac (1934)
Genres: Horror
Director: Dwain Esper
Main Cast: Horace B. Carpenter, Ted Edwards, Phyllis Diller, Theo Ramsey, Jenny Dark
Keywords: rape, cat, nudity, exploitation, mad scientist, independent film, gore, mad doctor, violence, eye gouging

Overview: An ex-vaudeville actor is working as the assistant to a doctor who has Frankenstein aspirations. The ex-vaudeville actor kills the doctor and decides to assume the identity of the dead physician.
