Title: What's a Human Anyway? (2006)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Reha Erdem
Main Cast: Turgay Aydin, Arzu Bazman, Ali Düşenkalkar

Overview: In an apartment building where neighbors, friends, and family are living in close quarters, three male protagonists encounter three phases of manhood in Turkish society. Directors Reha Erdem's light touch and slyly amusing style do not miss the opportunity to illuminate some serious points in a strictly patriarchal society.
