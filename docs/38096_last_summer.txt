Title: Last Summer (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Frank Perry
Main Cast: Barbara Hershey, Richard Thomas, Catherine Burns, Bruce Davison, Ernesto Gonzalez
Keywords: sex, beach, love, friends, summer, teenager, violence, drug

Overview: During summer vacation on Fire Island, three young people--a girl and two guys--become so close that they form a sort-of threesome. When an uncool girl tries to infiltrate the trio's newly found relationship, they construct an elaborate plot that has violent results.
