Title: Lightning Bolt: The Power Of Salad (2002)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Nick Noe
Main Cast: Brian Gibson, John Dwyer, Brian Chippendale

Overview: The Power of Salad, also known as The Power of Salad &amp; Milkshakes, is a film by Peter Glantz and Nick Noe, featuring and documenting the Providence, Rhode Island noise rock band Lightning Bolt. The film follows the avant-garde duo, Brian Gibson and Brian Chippendale, through a tour of the states, along with interviews of the band and their friends and family.
