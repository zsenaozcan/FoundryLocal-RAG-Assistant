Title: Homeless to Harvard: The Liz Murray Story (2003)
Genres: Drama, TV Movie
Director: Peter Levin
Main Cast: Thora Birch, Michael Riley, Kelly Lynch, Robert Bockstael, Makyla Smith
Keywords: homeless teenager

Overview: Based on a true story. Liz Murray is a young girl who is taken care of by her loving, but drug-addicted parents. Liz becomes homeless at 15 and after a tragedy comes upon her, she begins her work to finish high school.
