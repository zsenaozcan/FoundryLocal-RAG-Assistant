Title: Solomon Kane (2009)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Action, Science Fiction
Director: Michael J. Bassett
Main Cast: James Purefoy, Pete Postlethwaite, Alice Krige, Mackenzie Crook, Max von Sydow
Keywords: mercenary, murder, puritan, sorcerer, trap, bag over head, violence, gothic horror, covered wagon, monastery

Overview: A nomadic 16th century warrior, condemned to hell for his brutal past, seeks redemption by renouncing violence, but finds some things are worth burning for as he fights to free a young Puritan woman from the grip of evil.
