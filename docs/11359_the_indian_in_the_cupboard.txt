Title: The Indian in the Cupboard (1995)
Genres: Adventure, Family, Fantasy
Director: Frank Oz
Main Cast: Hal Scardino, Litefoot, Lindsay Crouse, Richard Jenkins, Rishi Bhat
Keywords: cupboard, games, puppet, parallel world, toy comes to life

Overview: A nine-year-old boy gets a plastic Indian and a cupboard for his birthday and finds himself involved in adventure when the Indian comes to life and befriends him.
