Title: Absurdistan (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Veit Helmer
Main Cast: Max Mauff, Kristyna Malérová, Assun Planas, Ilko Stefanovski, Nino Chkheidze
Keywords: sex, first time, fantasy, water, village, source, rescue, teenage crush, falling in love, rescue team

Overview: An allegorical comedy centered on two childhood sweethearts who seem destined for one another until the women of their isolated village, angered by male indifference toward the water shortage, go on a sex strike that threatens the young couple's first night of love.
