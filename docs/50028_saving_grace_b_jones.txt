Title: Saving Grace B. Jones (2009)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Connie Stevens
Main Cast: Rylee Fansler, Evie Thompson, Tatum O'Neal, Michael Biehn, Penelope Ann Miller
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In a 1950s-era Missouri town the life of a couple is thrown into chaos when the husband's sister is released from the local asylum and comes to live with the family.
