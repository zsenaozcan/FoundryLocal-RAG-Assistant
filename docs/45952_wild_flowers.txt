Title: Wild Flowers (2000)
Genres: Drama, Action, Comedy
Director: F. A. Brabec
Main Cast: Linda Rybová, Dan Bárta, Jana Švandová, Bolek Polívka, Karel Roden

Overview: Seven seemingly unconnected fairy tales - glued together only by folklore, mood, color and light - make up this Czech collection of visual poetry. The original piece of literature, written by Karel Jaromír Erben in 1853, contained twelve tales.
