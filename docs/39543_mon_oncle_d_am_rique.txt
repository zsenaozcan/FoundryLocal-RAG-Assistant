Title: Mon Oncle d'Amérique (1980)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Alain Resnais
Main Cast: Gérard Depardieu, Nicole Garcia, Roger Pierre, Nelly Borgeaud, Pierre Arditi
Keywords: psychology, punishment, reward

Overview: The didactic film is built around the ideas of French physician, writer and philosopher Henri Laborit, who plays himself in the film. It uses the stories of three people to illustrate Laborit's theories on evolutionary psychology regarding the relationship of self and society.
