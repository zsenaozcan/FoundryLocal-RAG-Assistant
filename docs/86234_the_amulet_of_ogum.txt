Title: The Amulet of Ogum (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nelson Pereira dos Santos
Main Cast: Ney Santanna, Anecy Rocha, Joffre Soares, Maria Ribeiro, Jards Macalé
Keywords: brazil, criminal, guitar player

Overview: This story is narrated by an ubiquitous folk singer and tells of a young boy whose mother arranges for him to have an amulet bearing Ogum's blessings which would make him immune to gunfire. The amulet apparently works, for the boy becomes a member of a mobster's hit-team and then joins with a group of people who resist his original employers.
