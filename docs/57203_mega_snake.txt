Title: Mega Snake (2007)
Genres: Horror
Director: Tibor Takács
Main Cast: Michael Shanks, Siri Baruc, Michal Yannai, John T. Woods

Overview: Duff Daniels (John T. Woods), the younger, stupider brother of Les (Michael Shanks), can't help himself from stealing a rare snake that's been kept in a jar by a Native American snake dealer (Ben Cardinal), and which has to follow some rules: don't let it out of the jar, then don't let it eat anything anything living, and never fear the heart of the snake.
