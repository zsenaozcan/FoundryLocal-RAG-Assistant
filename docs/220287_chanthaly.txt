Title: Chanthaly (2013)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: Mattie Do
Main Cast: Amphaiphun Phimmapunya, Douangmany Soliphanh, Soukchinda Duangkhamchan, Khouan Souliyabapha, Soulasath Souvanavong
Keywords: suicide, isolation, laos, suicide by hanging, ghost, woman director

Overview: A sickly young woman experiences visions of her dead mother. She struggles to determine if the apparition is simply a side effect of her daily medication, or her mother actually reaching out to her from beyond the grave.
