Title: Aries Spears: Comedy Blueprint (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mark Altman
Main Cast: Aries Spears
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Armed with his ferociously aggressive style of comedy MADTV alum, Aries Spears, takes the stage in Philadelphia for "Comedy Blueprint". No one, not black, not white, not gay, not straight is safe from his jokes and impressions.
