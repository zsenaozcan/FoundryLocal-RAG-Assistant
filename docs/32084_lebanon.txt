Title: Lebanon (2009)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Samuel Maoz
Main Cast: Oshri Cohen, Michael Moshonov, Yoav Donat, Itay Tiran, Zohar Strauss

Overview: June, 1982 - The First Lebanon War. A lone tank and a paratroopers platoon are dispatched to search a hostile town - a simple mission that turns into a nightmare. The four members of a tank crew find themselves in a violent situation that they cannot contain. Motivated by fear and the basic instinct of survival, they desperately try not to lose themselves in the chaos of war.
