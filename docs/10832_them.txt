Title: Them (2006)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: David Moreau
Main Cast: Olivia Bonamy, Michaël Cohen, Adriana Mocca, Maria Roman, Camelia Maxim
Keywords: night, forest, suspense, romania, underground tunnel

Overview: Lucas and Clementine live peacefully in their isolated country house, but one night they wake up to strange noise. They're not alone... and a group of hooded assailants begin to terrorize them throughout the night.
