Title: The Last Run (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Jonathan Segal
Main Cast: Fred Savage, Amy Adams, Erinn Bartlett, Steven Pasquale, Andrea Bogart
Keywords: infidelity, one-night stand, independent film

Overview: When a young accountant is devastated after discovering his inspiringly beautiful girlfriend is cheating on him, his best friend, who's engaged to a girl he doesn't love, convinces him to go on a "run" and sleep with as many women as he can to get over his heart break.
