Title: Cinemanovels (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Comedy
Director: Terry Miles
Main Cast: Lauren Lee Smith, Jennifer Beals, Ben Cotton, Kett Turton, Katharine Isabelle
Keywords: father, retrospective, life

Overview: The estranged daughter (Lauren Lee Smith) of a recently deceased filmmaker gains a new understanding of her father when she offers to create a retrospective of his work.
