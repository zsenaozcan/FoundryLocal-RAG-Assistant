Title: To Catch a Killer (1992)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Eric Till
Main Cast: Brian Dennehy, Michael Riley, Margot Kidder, Meg Foster, Martin Julien
Keywords: handcuffs, biography, sadist, serial teen killer

Overview: "To Catch a Killer" tells the true gruesome story of John Wayne Gacy - a good friend and helpful neighbour, a great child entertainer, a respectful businessman, and a violent serial killer who raped and murdered over 30 young boys.
