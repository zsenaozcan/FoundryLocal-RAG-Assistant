Title: Teodora, Slave Empress (1954)
Genres: History, Adventure, Romance, Drama
Director: Riccardo Freda
Main Cast: Georges Marchal, Gianna Maria Canale, Renato Baldini, Irene Papas, Carlo Sposito

Overview: Teodora, a Roman courtesan and former slave girl, marries the Roman emperor Justinian and assumes the throne as Empress of Rome. But the divide between nobility and slave is too great. Teodora seeks justice for her people, and revolution and armed conflict erupt in both Byzantium and Rome.
