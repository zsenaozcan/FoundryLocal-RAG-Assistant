Title: Midnight Lace (1960)
Genres: Thriller
Director: David Miller
Main Cast: Doris Day, Rex Harrison, John Gavin, Myrna Loy, Roddy McDowall
Keywords: london england, suspicion, threat to death, cowardliness

Overview: This 1960 thriller, set in London, stars Doris Day as a wife who begins to unravel when she receives threatening telephone calls informing her she's soon to be murdered.
