Title: Unicorn City (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Bryan Lefler
Main Cast: Devin McGinn, Jaclyn Hales, Jon Gries, Kevin Weisman, Matt Mattson
Keywords: role playing, larp

Overview: Voss entices local gamers to create a utopia to impress a potential employer, but paradise is disrupted when his nemesis lays claim to the city and Voss's true love.
