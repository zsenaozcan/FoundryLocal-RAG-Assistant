Title: Amy (1997)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Nadia Tass
Main Cast: Alana De Roma, Ben Mendelsohn, Rachel Griffiths, Nick Barker, Kerry Armstrong
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Amy's father, Franco, was a popular rock musician accidentally electrocuted while performing on stage. The psychological trauma leaves Amy mute and deaf. So the 8-year-old is brought by her mother, Tanya, to Melbourne to diagnose the reasons for her continued silence.
