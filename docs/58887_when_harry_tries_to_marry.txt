Title: When Harry Tries to Marry (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Nayan Padrai
Main Cast: Rahul Rai, Stefanie Estes, Freishia Bomambehran, Osvaldo Hernandez, Kelly Washington

Overview: An Indian-American surprises his family when he announces his desire for an arranged marriage with an Indian woman, though his affection for a longtime American friend complicate his plan.
