Title: Hell Ride (2008)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Larry Bishop
Main Cast: Larry Bishop, Michael Madsen, Eric Balfour, Dennis Hopper, Leonor Varela
Keywords: exploitation, biker, independent film, grindhouse

Overview: Hell Ride is a 2008 feature film from Larry Bishop being released under the “Quentin Tarantino Presents” banner. The film promises to be a blood and sex-soaked tale of motorcycle revenge and retribution.
