Title: Bad Man's River (1971)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Eugenio Martín
Main Cast: Lee Van Cleef, James Mason, Gina Lollobrigida, Simón Andreu, Diana Lorys
Keywords: spaghetti western, mexican

Overview: Robber Roy King loses his wife, Alicia, to revolutionary Montero. Despite their rivalry they collaborate in an attempt to rob the Mexican government of one million dollars.
