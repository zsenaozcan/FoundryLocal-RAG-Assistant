Title: Summertime (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Inari Niemi
Main Cast: Anna Paavilainen, Iina Kuustonen, Minka Kuustonen, Lauri Tilkanen, Pihla Viitala
Keywords: holiday, beach, drama, romance, summer, finland, woman director

Overview: 25-year-old Iiris and Karoliina have been best friends since childhood. Karoliina has been travelling and living abroad for some years now, but is coming back home for the summer as Karoliina has gotten them a job as waitresses in Hanko, the best summer city in Finland!
