Title: Marie-Octobre (1959)
Genres: Mystery, Drama
Director: Julien Duvivier
Main Cast: Danielle Darrieux, Paul Meurisse, Bernard Blier, Noël Roquevert, Lino Ventura
Keywords: traitor, french resistance, suspense, betrayal

Overview: A group of ex-resistance fighters are brought together by Marie-Octobre, the code name of Marie-Helene Dumoulin. The former members of the network have carried on with their lives after the war, but this evening they are going to have to live again a fateful night – the night their leader was killed. He had been betrayed, his name given to the Germans. The search for the traitor puts each personality in the spotlight – and also that of the killed leader, Castille.
