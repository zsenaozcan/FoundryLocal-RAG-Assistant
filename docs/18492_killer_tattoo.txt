Title: Killer Tattoo (2001)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Yuthlert Sippapak
Main Cast: Suthep Po-ngam, Somchai Kemglad

Overview: Set in a future Bangkok, the story picks-up after a hit-gone-wrong on "Iron Cop", the area police chief. The would-be killer "Pae", together with his equally inept collection of thugs, now become wanted men themselves as the Thai underworld bristles with the shame &amp; dishonor of such an ignoble bungling.
