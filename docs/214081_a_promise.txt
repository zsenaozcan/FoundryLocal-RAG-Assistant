Title: A Promise (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Patrice Leconte
Main Cast: Rebecca Hall, Alan Rickman, Richard Madden, Toby Murray, Maggie Steed
Keywords: germany, wartime, married woman

Overview: A romantic drama set in Germany just before WWI and centered on a married woman who falls in love with her husband's teacher. Separated by the war, they pledge their devotion to one another.
