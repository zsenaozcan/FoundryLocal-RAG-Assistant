Title: Carnosaur 3: Primal Species (1996)
Genres: Action, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Jonathan Winfrey
Main Cast: Scott Valentine, Janet Gunn, Rick Dean, Anthony Peck, Rodger Halston
Keywords: dinosaur

Overview: International terrorists get a surprise when their cargo turns out to contain living dinosaurs. The army commando team now have to think fast, if they want to prevent the extinction of the human species, instead of the reptiles.
