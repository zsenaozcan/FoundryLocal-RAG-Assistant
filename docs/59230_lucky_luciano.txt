Title: Lucky Luciano (1973)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Francesco Rosi
Main Cast: Gian Maria Volonté, Edmond O'Brien, Rod Steiger, Vincent Gardenia, Charles Cioffi
Keywords: gangster, lucky luciano

Overview: Lucky Luciano is one of the bosses of the Mafia. He orders the slaughter of 40 other responsibles, therefore becoming the only boss. But a few years later he is put into jail. In 1946, he got a pardon and is sent back to Sicilia. There, he begins becoming one of the chief of the Mafia. The US Army seems to refrain from interfering.
