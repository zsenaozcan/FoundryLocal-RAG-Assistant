Title: Devoured (2012)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Drama
Director: Greg Olliver
Main Cast: Marta Milans, Kara Jackson, Bruno Gunn, Tyler Hollinger, Luis Harris

Overview: An Intense, troubling film boasting a spectacular lead performance from Marta Milans. Greg Olliver's psychological thriller records with cold, clinical precision a young woman's gradual descent into madness.
