Title: Year Zero: The Silent Death of Cambodia (1979)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: John Pilger

Overview: John Pilger vividly reveals the brutality and murderous political ambitions of the Pol Pot/Khmer Rouge totalitarian regime which bought genocide and despair to the people of Cambodia while neighboring countries, including Australia, shamefully ignored the immense human suffering and unspeakable crimes that bloodied this once beautiful country.
