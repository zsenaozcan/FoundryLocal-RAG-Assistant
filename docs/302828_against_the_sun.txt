Title: Against the Sun (2014)
Genres: War, Adventure
Director: Brian Falk
Main Cast: Tom Felton, Garret Dillahunt, Jake Abel, Nadia Parra
Keywords: survival, lifeboat, bombardier

Overview: A WWII pilot, bombardier, and radioman find themselves adrift on a lifeboat without food or water after being forced to ditch their plane during a scouting mission.
