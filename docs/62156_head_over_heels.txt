Title: Head Over Heels (2010)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Roberto Santucci
Main Cast: Ingrid Guimarães, Bruno Garcia, Maria Paula, Denise Weinberg, Flávia Alessandra
Keywords: brazilian, comedy

Overview: After being left by her husband and fired from her work, a workaholic businesswoman is forced to go through a big change in her life and becomes a partner on a decadent sex shop.
