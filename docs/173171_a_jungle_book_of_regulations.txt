Title: A Jungle Book of Regulations (1974)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Stanisław Bareja
Main Cast: Jacek Fedorowicz, Halina Kowalska, Jerzy Dobrowolski

Overview: Nie ma róży bez ognia title in Polish: There is no rose without fire, in reference to two mixed up proverbs – There is no rose without thorns, and There is no smoke without the fire – is a Polish comedy film from 1974 directed by Stanisław Bareja.
