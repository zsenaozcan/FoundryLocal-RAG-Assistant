Title: I Am Jane Doe (2017)
Genres: Crime, Documentary
Director: Mary Mazzio
Main Cast: Jessica Chastain
Keywords: child sex trade, legal system, child sex trafficking, section 230

Overview: Chronicles the epic battle that several American mothers are waging on behalf of their middle-school daughters, victims of sex-trafficking on Backpage.com, the adult classifieds section that for years was part of the Village Voice.
