Title: Androids Dream (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Ion De Sosa
Main Cast: Manolo Marín, Moisés Richart, Marta Bassols, Coque Sánchez, Margot Sánchez
Keywords: android

Overview: It is the year 2052, on Earth, in Spain, and the final days of almost everything. The city is a string of abandoned construction sites. Everyday there are fewer people and not all of them are human. A sheep costs four and a half million pesetas and you have to spend a lot of bullets to pay for one.
