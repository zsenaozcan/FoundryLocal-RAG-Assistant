Title: A Report on the Party and the Guests (1966)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jan Němec
Main Cast: Ivan Vyskočil, Jan Klusák, Jiri Nemec, Pavel Bosek, Karel Mareš
Keywords: symbolism, picnic, freedom, satire, surrealism, bully, conformity, non conformity, hunting dog, tyranny

Overview: In Jan Němec’s surreal fable, a picnic is rudely transformed into a lesson in political hierarchy when a handful of mysterious authority figures show up. This allegory about oppression and conformity was banned in its home country but became an international success after it premiered at the New York Film Festival.
