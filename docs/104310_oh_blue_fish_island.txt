Title: Oh! Blue Fish Island (1977)
Genres: Mystery
Director: Kim Ki-young
Main Cast: Lee Hwa-si, Kim Jeong-cheol, Choi Yun-seok, Mi-hye Kwon
Keywords: island

Overview: A company is studying the possibility to open a spa hotel named after a mythical island, Ieodo. The island is inhabited by the souls of drowned sailors. During a study trip to the location of the hotel lost a journalist under mysterious circumstances. One of the contractors go to Ieodo's neighboring island, populated by widows of the dead sailors, to unravel the disappearance.
