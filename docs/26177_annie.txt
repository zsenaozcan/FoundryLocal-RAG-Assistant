Title: Annie (1999)
Genres: Music, Family, Comedy
Director: Rob Marshall
Main Cast: Kathy Bates, Victor Garber, Alan Cumming, Audra McDonald, Kristin Chenoweth
Keywords: holiday, musical, franklin delano roosevelt, based on play or musical, based on short story
