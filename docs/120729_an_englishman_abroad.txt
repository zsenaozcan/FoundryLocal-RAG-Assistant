Title: An Englishman Abroad (1983)
Genres: Drama, TV Movie, Comedy
Director: John Schlesinger
Main Cast: Alan Bates, Coral Browne, Charles Gray, Harold Innocent, Douglas Reith

Overview: Actress Coral Browne travels to Moscow, and meets a mysterious Englishman. Turns out he's the notorious spy, Guy Burgess. Based on a true story, with Ms. Browne playing herself.
