Title: Fantasia 2000 (1999)
Genres: Animation, Family, Music
Director: James Algar
Main Cast: Steve Martin, Itzhak Perlman, Quincy Jones, Bette Midler, James Earl Jones
Keywords: orchestra, musical segments

Overview: Blending lively music and brilliant animation, this sequel to the original 'Fantasia' restores 'The Sorcerer's Apprentice' and adds seven new shorts.
