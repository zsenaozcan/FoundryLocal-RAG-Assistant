Title: The Black Pimpernel (2007)
Genres: Drama, History, Romance, Thriller
Director: Åsa Faringer
Main Cast: Michael Nyqvist, Lumi Cavazos, Kate del Castillo, Lisa Werlinder, Carsten Norgaard
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The story about Swedish ambassador in Chile - Harald Edelstam - and his heroic actions to protect the innocent people from the execution during and after the military coup on September 11th 1973.
