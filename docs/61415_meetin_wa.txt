Title: Meetin' WA (1986)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jean-Luc Godard
Main Cast: Woody Allen, Jean-Luc Godard

Overview: Revolutionary French New Wave director Jean-Luc Godard conducts a twenty-five minute interview with influential and acclaimed American director Woody Allen on the cultural radiation, the ubiquity and significance of Television, and how Television compares with cinema as a medium and form of expression.
