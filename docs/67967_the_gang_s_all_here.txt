Title: The Gang's All Here (1943)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Busby Berkeley
Main Cast: Alice Faye, Carmen Miranda, Phil Baker, Benny Goodman, Eugene Pallette
Keywords: dance, world war ii, musical, singer, soldier, chorus girl

Overview: Playboy Andy Mason, on leave from the army, romances showgirl Edie Allen overnight to such effect that she's starry-eyed when he leaves next morning for active duty in the Pacific. Only trouble is, he gave her the assumed name of Casey. Andy's eventual return with a medal is celebrated by his rich father with a benefit show featuring Eadie's show troupe, at which she's sure to learn his true identity...and meet Vivian, his 'family-arrangement' fiancée. Mostly song and dance.
