Title: The Invisible Woman (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Ralph Fiennes
Main Cast: Ralph Fiennes, Felicity Jones, Joanna Scanlan, Kristin Scott Thomas, Tom Hollander
Keywords: biography, charles dickens

Overview: In 1857, at the height of his fame and fortune, novelist and social critic Charles Dickens meets and falls in love with teenage stage actress Nelly Ternan. As she becomes the focus of his heart and mind, as well as his muse, painful secrecy is the price both must pay.
