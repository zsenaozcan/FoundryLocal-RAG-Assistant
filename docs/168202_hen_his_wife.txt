Title: Hen, His Wife (1990)
Genres: Animation
Director: Igor Kovalyov
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: chicken, surreal

Overview: Extraordinarily detailed and beautifully drawn animation of a bizarre and surreal world; the domestic life of a fat man, his wife, a sort of oversized obese chicken, and their child/pet, a slug-like creature with a human head. This expressionistic and interior vision of Soviet animator Kovalyov is like an animated Eraserhead.
