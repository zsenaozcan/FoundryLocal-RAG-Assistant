Title: A Sister's Nightmare (2013)
Genres: TV Movie, Thriller
Director: Vic Sarin
Main Cast: Kelly Rutherford, Natasha Henstridge, Peyton List, Matthew Settle, Eric Breker

Overview: Police officer Jane Rydert's life goes into a tailspin the day her older sister, Cassidy, shows up at her door after sixteen years of confinement in a psychiatric hospital.
