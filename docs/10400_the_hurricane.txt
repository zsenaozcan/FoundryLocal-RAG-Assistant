Title: The Hurricane (1999)
Genres: Drama
Director: Norman Jewison
Main Cast: Denzel Washington, Vicellous Reon Shannon, Deborah Kara Unger, Liev Schreiber, John Hannah
Keywords: prison, boxer, boxing match, boxing school, biography
