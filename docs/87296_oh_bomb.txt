Title: Oh, Bomb! (1964)
Genres: Music, Comedy, Action, Crime
Director: Kihachi Okamoto
Main Cast: Yûnosuke Itô, Fubuki Koshiji, Ichirô Nakatani, Hideo Sunazuka, Noriko Shigeyama

Overview: During the mayoral election, two ex-prisoners decide to replace the lucky pen of an annoying candidate with a mini-bomb
