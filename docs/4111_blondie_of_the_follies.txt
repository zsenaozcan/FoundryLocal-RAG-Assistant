Title: Blondie of the Follies (1932)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Edmund Goulding
Main Cast: Marion Davies, Billie Dove, Robert Montgomery, James Gleason, Jimmy Durante
Keywords: yacht, great depression, scuffle, showgirls, follies, sugar daddy, best friends, romantic rival, pretensiousness, loving father

Overview: New York City tenement dwelling neighbors Blondie and Lottie are best friends. When Lottie makes the cast of a big Broadway show, she arranges for Blondie to join the cast as well. But the friendship goes awry when Lottie's sweetheart, wealthy Larry Belmont, falls for Blondie.
