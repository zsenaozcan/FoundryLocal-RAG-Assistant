Title: And So It Is (1966)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jorge Sanjinés
Main Cast: Benedicta Mendoza, Néstor Peredo, Vicente Verneros Salinas, Elsa Antequera, Néstor Cárdenas
Keywords: rape, revenge

Overview: While her husband is on his way to the market, a peasant woman is raped and murdered. Her widower carefully plans his revenge.
