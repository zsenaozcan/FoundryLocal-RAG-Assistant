Title: Chicago Joe and the Showgirl (1990)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Crime
Director: Bernard Rose
Main Cast: Kiefer Sutherland, Emily Lloyd, Keith Allen, Patsy Kensit, Harry Fowler
Keywords: war, gangster, violence, showgirl

Overview: During World War II, an American serviceman in London decides to impress his English girlfriend by acting as an American gangster, which soon turns deadly.
