Title: Illumination (1973)
Genres: Drama
Director: Krzysztof Zanussi
Main Cast: Stanislaw Latallo, Małgorzata Pritulak, Monika Dzienisiewicz-Olbrychska, Jan Skotnicki, Edward Zebrowski
Keywords: psychology, drama, meaning of life

Overview: A man looks for the meaning of life through science, work, love, marriage, family, death, and spirituality.
