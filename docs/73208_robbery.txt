Title: Robbery (1967)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Peter Yates
Main Cast: Stanley Baker, Joanna Pettet, James Booth, Frank Finlay, Barry Foster
Keywords: brit noir, train robbery

Overview: A dramatization of the Great Train Robbery. While not a 'how to', it is very detail dependent, showing the care and planning that took place to pull it off.
