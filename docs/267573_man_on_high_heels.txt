Title: Man on High Heels (2014)
Genres: Action
Director: Jang Jin
Main Cast: Cha Seung-won, Esom, Oh Jung-se, Park Sung-woong, Ahn Gil-kang
Keywords: detective, cop, transgender

Overview: Ji-wook is a cold-blooded detective who would brutally assault and stop at nothing to catch criminals. However, despite her perfectly masculine appearance, Ji-wook struggles with a secret desire to be a woman. At last, Ji-wook decides to transition. However, a gang Ji-wook brutally arrested by force plots revenge against him. While Ji-wook resigns and secretly dreams of transitioning into a woman, people close to her get caught up in a conspiracy and killed. When the one person he must protect, Jang-mi, also falls in danger, Ji-wook faces a crossroads in making his choice.
