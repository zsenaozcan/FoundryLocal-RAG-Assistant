Title: Zatôichi's Pilgrimage (1966)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kazuo Ikehiro
Main Cast: Shintarô Katsu, Michiyo Ohkusu, Kunie Tanaka, Hisashi Igawa, Masao Mishima
Keywords: samurai, sequel, zatoichi

Overview: Our hero Zatoichi sails to one of the distant islands to begin a pilgrimage to a total of 88 Shinto shrines.
