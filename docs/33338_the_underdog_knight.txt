Title: The Underdog Knight (2008)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Ding Sheng
Main Cast: Liu Ye, Anthony Wong, You Yong, Liu Yang, Sun Honglei
Keywords: kung fu

Overview: Lao San is a young veteran high in Kungfu power but low in intelligence. After landing on a job as a body guard for a wealthy antique collector, Lao San finds out his boss's plot to rob the National Art Museum.
