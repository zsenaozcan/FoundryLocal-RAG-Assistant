Title: Some Body (2001)
Genres: Drama
Director: Henry Barrial
Main Cast: Stephanie Bennett, Billy Ray Gallion, Laura Katz, Sean Michael Allen, James Franco

Overview: Culled from the real life experiences of its star/co-writer/producer Stephanie Bennett, and starring some of her real life ex-lovers, this is the story of Samantha (Bennett), a twenty-something Los Angeles schoolteacher, searching for excitement and an elusive happiness.
