Title: Om Shanti Om (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Farah Khan
Main Cast: Shah Rukh Khan, Deepika Padukone, Arjun Rampal, Shreyas Talpade, Kirron Kher
Keywords: 1970s, dance, bombay india, musical, woman director

Overview: In this modern remake of Karz, Om - an aspiring actor from the 1970s - is murdered, but is immediately reincarnated into the present day. He attempts to discover the mystery of his demise and find Shanti, the love of his previous life.
