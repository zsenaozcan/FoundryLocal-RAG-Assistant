Title: Superstar: The Karen Carpenter Story (1988)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Todd Haynes
Main Cast: Merrill Gruver, Michael Edwards, Melissa Brown, Rob LaBelle, Cynthia Schneider
Keywords: bulimia, barbie, the carpenters, anorexia

Overview: The final 17 years of American singer and musician Karen Carpenter, performed almost entirely by modified Barbie dolls.
