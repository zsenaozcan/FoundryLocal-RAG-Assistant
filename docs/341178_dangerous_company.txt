Title: Dangerous Company (2015)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Sheldon Larry
Main Cast: Alicia Leigh Willis, Rick Ravanello, Melissa Marty, Susan Slome, David Alan Graf
Keywords: thriller

Overview: Pauline begins to lose track of time, misplace things and find herself disoriented. She fears falling victim to a disease, but all is not as it seems. She must find the truth and prove it to the police and herself.
