Title: Flodder Does Manhattan! (1992)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Dick Maas
Main Cast: Huub Stapel, Nelly Frijda, Tatjana Simić, René van 't Hof, Jon Polito
Keywords: luxury, exchange, chaos, family

Overview: The mayor of Sunny Dale see a chance to get rid of the Flodder family: They send the asocial bunch for an international exchange to New York. There they get confused with a Russian delegation of medical doctors while the street worker Werner who accompanies them becomes imprisoned.
