Title: Time Bomb (1953)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Ted Tetzlaff
Main Cast: Glenn Ford, Anne Vernon, Maurice Denham, Harcourt Williams, Victor Maddern
Keywords: bomb, train, detonate, uxb

Overview: When a saboteur places an explosive device on a train full of sea mines, the authorities call for bomb expert Peter Lyncort to diffuse the situation, unaware that he has explosive problems of his own.
