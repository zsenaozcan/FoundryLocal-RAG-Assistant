Title: Stag (1997)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Gavin Wilding
Main Cast: Mario Van Peebles, Jerry Stiller, Kevin Dillon, William McNamara
Keywords: stripper, stag night, death

Overview: Victor Mallick returned to his home and found out that all of his friends have arranged a surprise bachelor/stag party for him. Two strippers were hired to cheer up the party. However, when some of the men accidentally killed one of the strippers, they are left to face the consequences of their action.
