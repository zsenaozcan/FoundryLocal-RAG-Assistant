Title: Madly Madagascar (2013)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Family
Director: David Soren
Main Cast: Ben Stiller, Chris Rock, David Schwimmer, Jada Pinkett Smith, Danny Jacobs
Keywords: africa, lion, hippopotamus, madagascar, savannah, zebra, monkey, spin off, valentine's day, love potion

Overview: Your favorite Madagascar pals are back in an all-new adventure! Alex's favorite holiday, Valentine's Day, brings hilarious surprises and excitement for the entire gang. Melman plans a big surprise for Gloria, Marty tries to impress a new friend and everyone wants to get their hands on King Julien's love potion. You'll fall in LOVE with Madly Madagascar!
