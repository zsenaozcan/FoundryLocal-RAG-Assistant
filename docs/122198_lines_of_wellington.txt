Title: Lines of Wellington (2012)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Valeria Sarmiento
Main Cast: Nuno Lopes, Soraia Chaves, Marisa Paredes, John Malkovich, Carloto Cotta
Keywords: troops, cult director, woman director, wellington, 19th century
