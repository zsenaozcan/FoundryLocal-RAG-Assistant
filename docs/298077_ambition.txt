Title: Ambition (2014)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Tomek Baginski
Main Cast: Aidan Gillen, Aisling Franciosi
Keywords: future, apprentice, telekinesis, mind control, space, planet, destruction of planet, destruction, master, building

Overview: Tomek Bagiński’s latest project - tells of a young apprentice (Aisling Franciosi) struggling to master nanotechnology on an alien world and prove herself to her enigmatic master (Aidan Gillen).  Will her herculean goals remain tantalizingly out of reach, or will she fulfill them and in the process, change life as we know it?
