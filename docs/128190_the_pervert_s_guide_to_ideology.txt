Title: The Pervert's Guide to Ideology (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Sophie Fiennes
Main Cast: Slavoj Žižek
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Philosopher Slavoj Žižek and filmmaker Sophie Fiennes reunites for this follow-up to their hit The Pervert's Guide to Cinema, using their interpretation of moving pictures to present a compelling cinematic journey into the heart of ideology – the dreams that shape our collective beliefs and practices.
