Title: Love Affair (1939)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Leo McCarey
Main Cast: Irene Dunne, Charles Boyer, Maria Ouspenskaya, Lee Bowman, Astrid Allwyn
Keywords: artist, madeira, cruise, orphanage, playboy, love, singer, chapel, grandmother, empire state building

Overview: French playboy Michel Marnet and American Terry McKay fall in love during the transatlantic passage of a ship. They arrange to reunite six months later, if neither has changed their mind.
