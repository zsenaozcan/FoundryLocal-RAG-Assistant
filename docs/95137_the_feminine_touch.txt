Title: The Feminine Touch (1941)
Genres: Comedy
Director: W.S. Van Dyke
Main Cast: Rosalind Russell, Don Ameche, Kay Francis, Van Heflin, Donald Meek
Keywords: jealousy, professor, publisher

Overview: A professor (Don Ameche) and his wife (Rosalind Russell) move to New York and confuse a publisher's romance with his assistant (Kay Francis).
