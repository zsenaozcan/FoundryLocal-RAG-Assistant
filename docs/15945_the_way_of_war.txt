Title: The Way of War (2009)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: John Carter
Main Cast: Cuba Gooding Jr., J.K. Simmons, John Terry, Jaclyn DeSantis, Lance Reddick

Overview: Paramilitary operative David Wolfe stumbles upon an international conspiracy connecting presidential cabinet members to a Middle Eastern terrorist plot. Wanting to expose the truth, Wolfe defies orders and returns to the US as an army of one fighting for American security and integrity
