Title: For the Love of Spock (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Adam Nimoy
Main Cast: Leonard Nimoy, William Shatner, George Takei, J.J. Abrams, Simon Pegg

Overview: A documentary film about the life of Star Trek's Mr. Spock as well as that of Leonard Nimoy, the actor who played Mr. Spock for almost fifty years, written and directed by his son, Adam Nimoy.
