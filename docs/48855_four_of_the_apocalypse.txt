Title: Four of the Apocalypse (1975)
Genres: Western, Action, Foreign
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Fabio Testi, Lynne Frederick, Michael J. Pollard, Harry Baird, Tomás Milián
Keywords: revenge, mining town, spaghetti western

Overview: Four petty criminals, three men and a woman, wander through the trackless terrain of the Wild West Utah and are hounded by a sadistic bandit.
