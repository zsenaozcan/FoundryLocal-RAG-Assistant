Title: Zombies: The Beginning (2007)
Genres: Action, Horror
Director: Bruno Mattei
Main Cast: Yvette Yzon, Alvin Anson, Paul Holmes, James Gregory Paolleli, B.B. Johnson
Keywords: mutant, island, sequel, undead, zombie, soldier, medical experiment, scientific experiment

Overview: A woman floating on a raft in the middle of the Pacific. She was found by a rescue team and taken ashore. Once there, she tells a story of how she escaped from an island full of zombies. She was persuaded to lead a commando unit to the island to explore.
