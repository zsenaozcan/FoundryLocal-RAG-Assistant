Title: My 5 Wives (2000)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sidney J. Furie
Main Cast: Rodney Dangerfield, Andrew Dice Clay, John Byner, Molly Shannon, Jerry Stiller

Overview: A three times divorced real estate tycoon, who thought he was through with love. Gets more then he bargained for when he invests in some property for a ski resort in Utah.
