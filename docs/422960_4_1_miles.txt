Title: 4.1 Miles (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Daphne Matziaraki
Main Cast: Kyriakos Papdopoulos, Tasos Batis, Iraklis Miliopoulous, Giannis Mavrogiannis, Christos Sapounas
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A coast guard captain on a small Greek island is suddenly charged with saving thousands of refugees from drowning at sea.
