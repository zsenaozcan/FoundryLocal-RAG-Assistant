Title: Beyond Sleep (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Boudewijn Koole
Main Cast: Reinout Scholten van Aschat, Pål Sverre Hagen, Jakop Ahlbom, Maria Annette Tanderød Berglyd, Anders Baasmo Christiansen
Keywords: norway, geologist

Overview: A young man's mythical quest for a stone that fell from the sky. He searches, stumbles and suffers as he traverses the sadistic universe of Northern Europe. He only finds redemption on the very edge of madness. Based on the iconic Dutch novel 'Nooit meer slapen' by Willem Frederik Hermans.
