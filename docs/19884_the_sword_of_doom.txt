Title: The Sword of Doom (1966)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Kihachi Okamoto
Main Cast: Tatsuya Nakadai, Yûzô Kayama, Michiyo Aratama, Yôko Naitô, Toshirō Mifune
Keywords: swordplay, samurai, independent film
