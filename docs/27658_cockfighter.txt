Title: Cockfighter (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: Monte Hellman
Main Cast: Warren Oates, Richard B. Shull, Harry Dean Stanton, Ed Begley Jr., Laurie Bird
Keywords: gambling, chicken, gambling debts, independent film, charles willeford, cock fighting

Overview: Warren Oates plays a world-weary gambler trying to make big money cockfighting. Always on the road, he works inside and outside the rules to try to work up to the biggest fight of the year.
