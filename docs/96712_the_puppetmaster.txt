Title: The Puppetmaster (1993)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, War
Director: Hou Hsiao-hsien
Main Cast: Li Tian-Lu, Lim Giong, Ming Hwa Bai, Fue Choung Cheng, Tsai Chen-Nan
Keywords: puppeteer, biography, puppet

Overview: In the first half of this century, young Li Tienlu joins a travelling puppet theatre and subsequently makes a career as one of Taiwan's leading puppeteers.
