Title: Bill Bailey: Part Troll (2004)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Nick Morris
Main Cast: Bill Bailey, Kevin Eldon, John Moloney, Martin Trenaman

Overview: Bill Bailey's Part Troll tour has been a huge success playing over 60 dates throughout the country. This DVD, filmed at the Hammersmith Apollo, captures one of the country's best and most original stand-ups at the top of his game.
