Title: Beyond (2003)
Genres: Science Fiction, Animation
Director: Kouji Morimoto
Main Cast: Hedy Burress, Matt McKenzie, Pamela Adlon, Tara Strong, Tress MacNeille

Overview: While looking for her cat, a young woman and some kids find an abandoned building where strange things happen and the rules of physics don't always apply. Part of the Animatrix collection of animated shorts set in the Matrix universe.
