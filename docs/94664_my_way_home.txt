Title: My Way Home (1965)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Miklós Jancsó
Main Cast: András Kozák, Sergey Nikonenko, Béla Barsi, László Csurka, Zoltán Gera
Keywords: villa, world war ii, friendship, train, wounded soldier, young soldier

Overview: In the final days of World War II, a young Hungarian is making his way home, through countryside full of the debris of war, when he is captures and imprisoned by Russians. Left in the custody of a young Russian soldier, the two youths form a friendship in spite of not speaking each other's language.
