Title: Heroes Shed No Tears (1986)
Genres: Action, Foreign
Director: John Woo
Main Cast: Chin Yuet-Sang, Doo Hee Jang, Ho Kon Kim, Lam Ching-Ying, Eddy Ko
Keywords: mexican standoff, mercenary, bullet time

Overview: Thai government hires group of Chinese mercenaries to capture a powerful druglord from Golden Triangle. The mercenaries manage to capture the druglord, but his men are trying to set him free.
