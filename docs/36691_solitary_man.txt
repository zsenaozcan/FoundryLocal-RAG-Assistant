Title: Solitary Man (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Brian Koppelman
Main Cast: Michael Douglas, Danny DeVito, Jesse Eisenberg, Susan Sarandon, Mary-Louise Parker
Keywords: infidelity, bankrupt, loneliness, comedy, family drama
