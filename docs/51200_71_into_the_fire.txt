Title: 71: Into the Fire (2010)
Genres: War
Director: John H. Lee
Main Cast: Kim Seung-woo, David Lee McInnis, Christina Cha, Choi Seung-Hyun, Jeong Seung-won
Keywords: war, drama, based on true story, number in title, korea, student, historical, fight to the death, korean war, north korea

Overview: A civil war broke out in the 1950s between North and South Korea which changed the country forever. 71: Into the Fire centers on the struggles of 71 student soldiers who fought through the Korean War. Using real people and events based on the opening moves of the Battle of P'ohang-dong, the film exposes the personal and physical conflicts that these students faced when finding themselves on the last line of defense at P'ohang-dong Girl's Middle School against the NKPA's advancement, needing to hold out until back up from other Korean troops and the Allies arrived.
