Title: The Unfaithful (1947)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Crime
Director: Vincent Sherman
Main Cast: Ann Sheridan, Lew Ayres, Zachary Scott, Eve Arden, Jerome Cowan
Keywords: killer, film noir

Overview: Chris Hunter kills an intruder and tells her husband and lawyer it was an act of self-defense. It's later revealed that he was actually her lover and she had posed for an incriminating statue he created.
