Title: Worlds Apart (2015)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Christoforos Papakaliatis
Main Cast: Christoforos Papakaliatis, J.K. Simmons, Andrea Osvárt, Maria Kavoyianni, Minas Hatzisavvas

Overview: Three separate narratives each following a love story between a foreigner and a Greek. Each story represents a different generation falling in love during a time of socioeconomic turmoil that dominates Southern Europe as a whole, only to connect as a single story in the end.
