Title: Divorce Italian Style (1961)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Pietro Germi
Main Cast: Marcello Mastroianni, Daniela Rocca, Stefania Sandrelli, Lando Buzzanca, Leopoldo Trieste
Keywords: italy, sicily, divorce

Overview: A married Sicilian baron falls in love with his cousin and vows to wed her, but with divorce illegal he must concoct a crime of passion to do away with his wife.
