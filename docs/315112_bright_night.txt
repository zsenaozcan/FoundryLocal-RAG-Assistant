Title: Bright Night (2015)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: Florian Gottschick
Main Cast: Benno Fürmann, Anna Grisebach, Michael Gwisdek, Vladimir Burlakov, Kai Ivo Baulitz
Keywords: gay, secret, psychology, past, homosexuality, couple

Overview: A drama with a supernatural edge, this genre-crossing gem finds two couples visiting a home from their past, and sexual tension that brings out the worst in each other. Long-buried grudges resurface and it seems unlikely the couples will return intact. Faced with an outcome that will upset their delicate balance of happiness, the world offers them a bizarre opportunity to correct it.
