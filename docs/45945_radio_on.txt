Title: Radio On (1979)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery, Music, Foreign
Director: Chris Petit
Main Cast: David Beames, Lisa Kreuzer, Sandy Ratcliff, Andrew Byatt, Sue Jones-Davies

Overview: Set in 1970s Britain, a man drives from London to Bristol to investigate his brother's death. The purpose of his trip is offset by his encounters with a series of odd people.
