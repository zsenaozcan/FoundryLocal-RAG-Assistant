Title: A Walk Into the Sea: Danny Williams and the Warhol Factory (2007)
Genres: Documentary, History
Director: Esther Robinson
Main Cast: Callie Angell, Brigid Berlin, John Cale, Danny Fields, Nat Finkelstein
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Esther Robinson's portrait of her uncle Danny Williams, Warhol's onetime lover, collaborator and filmmaker in his own right, offers a exploration of the Factory era, an homage to Williams's talent, a journey of family discovery and a compelling inquiry into Williams's mysterious disappearance at age 27.
