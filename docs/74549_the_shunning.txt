Title: The Shunning (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Landon Jr.
Main Cast: Danielle Panabaker, Sherry Stringfield, Sarah Maine, Willie Stratford, Jason Loughlin

Overview: Beautiful Katie Lapp has always felt something missing in her simple Amish existence -- until a mysterious "Englisher" comes to Lancaster County looking for the baby girl she gave up for adoption 19 years ago.
