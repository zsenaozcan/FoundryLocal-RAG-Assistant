Title: The Scapegoat (1959)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Robert Hamer
Main Cast: Alec Guinness, Bette Davis, Nicole Maurey, Irene Worth, Pamela Brown
Keywords: upper class, france, chauffeur, marriage contract, murder, countess, look-alike, mistress, assumed identity, dowager

Overview: An Englishman (Alec Guinness) in France unwittingly is placed into the identity, and steps into the vacated life, of a look-alike French nobleman.
