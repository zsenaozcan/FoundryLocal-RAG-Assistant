Title: La sociedad del semáforo (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rubén Mendoza
Main Cast: Alexis Zúñiga, Abelardo Jaimes, Gala Bernal, Romelia Cajiao

Overview: By applying all his knowledge in electricity and electronics, Raúl Tréllez, a bum who lives in the downtown streets of Bogotá, struggles to control the time of a red light so that jugglers, cripples and other homeless people can achieve longer shows in the red light corner.
