Title: What's New About Love? (2012)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Hugo Alves
Main Cast: Joana Santos, Miguel Raposo, David Cabecinha, João Pedro Sousa, Nuno Casanovas
Keywords: portugal, lisbon, love, unmarried couple

Overview: Six friends gather every evening in a basement to make music. But during the day, regarding the things of life, there are no tests. Every experience leaves its mark.
