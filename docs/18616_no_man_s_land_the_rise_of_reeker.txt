Title: No Man's Land: The Rise of Reeker (2008)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Dave Payne
Main Cast: Stephen Martines, Valerie Cruz, Mircea Monroe, Desmond Askew, Michael Robert Brandon
Keywords: sheriff, robber, burned alive, gore, serial killer, grim reaper, desert, held at gunpoint, death, taser

Overview: A sheriff and his son who are tracking down a group of bank robbers on their way to Mexico, only to discover that they are being stalked by a far more deadly enemy — The Reeker.
