Title: My Son (2007)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Jang Jin
Main Cast: Cha Seung-won, Ryu Deok-hwan

Overview: Lee Gang-sik sits in jail with a life sentence because of murder. His good behavior the last 15 years earns him the opportunity to be released for one day &amp; visit his family. But how will his son, whom he let down for 15 years, react?
