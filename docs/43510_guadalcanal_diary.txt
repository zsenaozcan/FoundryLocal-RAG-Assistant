Title: Guadalcanal Diary (1943)
Genres: Action, War, Drama
Director: Lewis Seiler
Main Cast: Preston Foster, Lloyd Nolan, William Bendix, Richard Conte, Anthony Quinn
Keywords: war correspondent, jungle warfare, guadalcanal

Overview: Concentrating on the personal lives of those involved, a war correspondent takes us through the preparations, landing and initial campaign on Guadalcanal during WWII.
