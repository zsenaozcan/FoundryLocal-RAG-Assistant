Title: Night Of The Living Dead 3D (2007)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jeff Broadstreet
Main Cast: Brianna Brown, Joshua Desroches, Sid Haig, Greg Travis, Johanna Black

Overview: Both an homage to and a re-imagining of the original 1968 film, this update follows a group of survivors trapped in a farmhouse battling a siege of undead zombies....in 3D!
