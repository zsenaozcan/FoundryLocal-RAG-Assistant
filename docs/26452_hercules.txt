Title: Hercules (1983)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Luigi Cozzi
Main Cast: Lou Ferrigno, Sybil Danning, Brad Harris, Bobby Rhodes, Ingrid Anderson
Keywords: greek mythology, sword fight, hercules, roman mythology

Overview: The film is an odd retelling of the story of Hercules battling the wizard Minos, who uses "science" in an attempt to take over the world. Hercules must stop him and rescue his princess love in the process. Part of the adventure finds Hercules battling giant robots brought to life by stop motion animation.
