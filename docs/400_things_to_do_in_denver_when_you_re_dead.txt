Title: Things to Do in Denver When You're Dead (1995)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Gary Fleder
Main Cast: Andy García, Christopher Lloyd, William Forsythe, Bill Nunn, Treat Williams
Keywords: father son relationship, bounty hunter, boat, way of life, coffin, denver, godmother, paranoia, hitman, friendship

Overview: A mafia film in Tarantino style with a star-studded cast. Jimmy’s “The Saint” gangster career has finally ended. Yet now he finds him self doing favors for a wise godfather known as “The Man with the Plan.”
