Title: The Millionairess (1960)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Anthony Asquith
Main Cast: Sophia Loren, Peter Sellers, Vittorio De Sica, Alastair Sim, Dennis Price
Keywords: exam, doctor, wedding, millionaire

Overview: When her father dies, Epifania Parerga, an Italian in London, becomes the world's richest woman. She feels incomplete without a husband and falls in love with a humble, Indian physician, Ahmed el Kabir, much loved by his indigent English patients.
