Title: Halloweed (2016)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: LazRael Lison
Main Cast: Simon Rex, Shannon Brown, Jim O'Heir, Michelle Mueller, Jayson Bernard

Overview: A comedic blend of murder, mystery and marijuana. Trying to escape the infamy of a serial killer father, Trent Modine and his goof-ball stepbrother Joey, both seeking a new start, find refuge in the small town of Mooseheart.
