Title: The Day He Arrives (2011)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Hong Sang-soo
Main Cast: Yu Jun-sang, Kim Sang-Joong, Song Seon-mi, Kim Bo-Kyeong, Kim Eui-sung

Overview: Sang-Joon is a professor in the film department at a provincial university. He goes to Seoul to meet his senior, Young-Ho, who works as a film critic. Sang-Joon stays in a northern village in Seoul for 3 days.
