Title: Tomboy (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Céline Sciamma
Main Cast: Zoé Héran, Malonn Lévana, Jeanne Disson, Sophie Cattani, Mathieu Demy
Keywords: sexual identity, gay interest, transgender, gender roles, lgbt child, first crush, sexual confusion, expectant mother, gender dysphoria, puppy love

Overview: A French family with two daughters, 10-year-old Laure and 6-year-old Jeanne, moves to a new neighborhood during the summer holidays. With her tomboy ways, Laure is immediately mistaken for a boy by the local kids and passes herself off as Mickaël.
