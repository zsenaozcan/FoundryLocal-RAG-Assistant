Title: Dead Space: Downfall (2008)
Genres: Animation, Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Chuck Patton
Main Cast: Bruce Boxleitner, Kelly Hu, Jeff Bennett, Jim Cummings, Grey Griffin
Keywords: vacuum, fanatic, colony, infection, extreme violence, violence, splatter, adult animation, severed leg, head chopped off

Overview: On a deep space mining mission to a remote planet, an ancient religious relic - thought to be proof of the existence of God - is unearthed and brought aboard. When the unholy artifact unleashes a long-dormant alien race, its glimpse of Heaven transforms the ship into a living Hell. A prequel to the events of the 2008 video game Dead Space.
