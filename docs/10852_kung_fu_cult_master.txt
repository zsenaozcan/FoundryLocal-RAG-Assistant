Title: Kung Fu Cult Master (1993)
Genres: Fantasy, Action, History, Science Fiction
Director: Wong Jing
Main Cast: Jet Li, Sammo Hung, Francis Ng, Richard Ng, Chingmy Yau
Keywords: martial arts, power takeover, battle for power, hostility, revenge, family feud

Overview: A wild and rollicking martial arts fantasy extravaganza that features prized swords and swordsmen, a crazy monk attached to a rolling boulder, serious clan and cult rivalries, and lots of magic and flying.
