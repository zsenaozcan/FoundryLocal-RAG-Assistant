Title: Metastases (2009)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Branko Schmidt
Main Cast: Franjo Dijak, Robert Ugrina, Rakan Rushaidat, Ivo Gregurević, Ljiljana Bogojevic
Keywords: neo-nazi, robber, drug traffic, junkie, neglect of one's spouse, alcoholism, hooligan

Overview: Eliciting images of cancer, this drama explores the illnesses that plague modern Croatia. Four young junkies in Zagreb maturing in the wake of war reflect the petty hatreds, violence, prejudices and mood hanging over the country like a disease that spreads with no cure in sight.
