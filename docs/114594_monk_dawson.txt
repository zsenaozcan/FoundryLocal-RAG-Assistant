Title: Monk Dawson (1998)
Genres: Drama
Director: Tom Waller
Main Cast: Benedict Taylor, Michael Cashman, Rhona Mitra, Oliver Stone, Toby Hadoke

Overview: Monk Dawson is a film that was released in 1998, directed and produced by Tom Waller and starring John Michie, Benedict Taylor, Rhona Mitra and Paula Hamilton. It was based on the novel of the same name written by Piers Paul Read.
 The film is about Eddie Dawson, a monk who has led a sheltered existence at a Benedictine monastery, but when he is expelled from his order he has to learn to deal with the harsh realities of everyday life, including falling in love, and the betrayal of a friend.
