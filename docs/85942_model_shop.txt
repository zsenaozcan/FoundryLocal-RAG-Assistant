Title: Model Shop (1969)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Jacques Demy
Main Cast: Gary Lockwood, Anouk Aimée, Alexandra Hay, Carol Cole, Tom Holland
Keywords: infidelity, architect, model

Overview: While trying to raise money to prevent his car from being repossessed, George is attracted to Lola, a Frenchwoman who works in a "model shop" (an establishment which rents out beautiful pin-up models to photographers). George spends his last twelve dollars to photograph her, and discovers that she is as unhappy as he.
