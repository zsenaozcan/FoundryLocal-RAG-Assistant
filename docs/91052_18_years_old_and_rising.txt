Title: 18 Years Old and Rising (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Frédéric Louf
Main Cast: Pierre Niney, Audrey Bastien, Lou de Laâge, Ali Marhyar, Victor Bessière

Overview: Primo is an ordinary middle-class youngster studying for his baccalaureate, who gets side-tracked when he falls in love with a rich girl. They soon learn they have to deal with her condescending friends who look down upon this upstart.
