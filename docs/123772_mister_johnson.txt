Title: Mister Johnson (1990)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bruce Beresford
Main Cast: Maynard Eziashi, Pierce Brosnan, Edward Woodward, Beatie Edney, Denis Quilley
Keywords: worker, nigeria, trade, tribal law, wife beater

Overview: In 1923 British Colonial Nigeria, Mister Johnson is an oddity -- an educated black man who doesn't really fit in with the natives or the British. He works for the local British magistrate, and considers himself English, though he has never been to England. He is always scheming, trying to get ahead, which lands him in a lot of hot water.
