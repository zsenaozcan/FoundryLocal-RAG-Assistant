Title: Earthquake (1974)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Mark Robson
Main Cast: Charlton Heston, Ava Gardner, George Kennedy, Lorne Greene, Geneviève Bujold
Keywords: fire, supermarket, architect, earthquake, parking garage, natural disaster, national guard, disaster, child in peril, pool hall

Overview: Earthquake is a 1974 American disaster film that achieved huge box-office success, continuing the disaster film genre of the 1970s where recognizable all-star casts attempt to survive life or death situations. The plot concerns the struggle for survival after a catastrophic earthquake destroys most of the city of Los Angeles, California.
