Title: The Origin of Stitch (2005)
Genres: Animation
Director: Mike Disa
Main Cast: Chris Sanders

Overview: Stitch discovers Jumba’s secret computer that reveals what creatures Jumba had used to create Stitch, and also hints at his other 625 experiments. Stitch is scared to find out what a monster he is, only for Jumba to come and explain how he found love when he met Lilo.
