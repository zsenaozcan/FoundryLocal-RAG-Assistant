Title: Rescue From Gilligan's Island (1978)
Genres: Comedy, TV Movie
Director: Leslie H. Martinson
Main Cast: Bob Denver, Alan Hale Jr., Jim Backus, Natalie Schafer, Judith Baldwin
Keywords: island, marooned

Overview: When a decaying Russian satellite crashes on the island, the Professor uses a key component for a barometer. With that device, he learns that a massive wave is going to swamp the island. In desperation, the castaways lash their huts together into one structure in order to have any chance to ride the disaster out.
