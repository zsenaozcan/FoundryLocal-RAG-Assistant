Title: Up from the Depths (1979)
Genres: Horror
Director: Charles B. Griffith
Main Cast: Sam Bottoms, Susanne Reed, Virgil Frye, Kedric Wolfe, Charles Howerton
Keywords: tourist, shark

Overview: A menacing shark-like predator attacks a Hawaiian tourist area in this low-budget creature feature. An uncredited Roger Corman served as executive producer.
