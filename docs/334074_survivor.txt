Title: Survivor (2015)
Genres: Thriller
Director: James McTeigue
Main Cast: Pierce Brosnan, Milla Jovovich, Dylan McDermott, Angela Bassett, Robert Forster
Keywords: new year's eve, fire, showdown, terrorist, embassy, hand grenade, truck, laboratory, on the run, orphan

Overview: A Foreign Service Officer in London tries to prevent a terrorist attack set to hit New York, but is forced to go on the run when she is framed for crimes she did not commit.
