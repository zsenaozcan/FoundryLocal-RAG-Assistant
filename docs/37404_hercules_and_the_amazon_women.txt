Title: Hercules and the Amazon Women (1994)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy, TV Movie
Director: Bill L. Norton
Main Cast: Kevin Sorbo, Anthony Quinn, Roma Downey, Michael Hurst, Lucy Lawless
Keywords: hero, mythology, greek mythology, warrior woman, gods, hercules, warrior

Overview: Hercules and Iolaus take time out from Iolaus' wedding preparations, to help a distant village under attack from "monsters". When they reach their destination, they find the monsters are in fact Amazonian women who are controlled by Hera. "Hercules and the Amazon Women" is the first movie-length pilot episode of the television series "Hercules: The Legendary Journeys".
