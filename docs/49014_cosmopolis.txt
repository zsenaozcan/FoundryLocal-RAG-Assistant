Title: Cosmopolis (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: David Cronenberg
Main Cast: Robert Pattinson, Juliette Binoche, Sarah Gadon, Paul Giamatti, Samantha Morton
Keywords: sex, bookshop, homeless person, nudity, paparazzi, limousine, anarchist, murder, billionaire, limousine driver

Overview: Riding across Manhattan in a stretch limo in order to get a haircut, a 28-year-old billionaire asset manager's day devolves into an odyssey with a cast of characters that start to tear his world apart.
