Title: Nightlife (2015)
Genres: Mystery, Crime, Drama
Director: Damjan Kozole
Main Cast: Pia Zemljič, Jernej Šugman, Marko Mandić, Peter Musevski, Petre Arsovski
Keywords: night life, night, attorney

Overview: The lives of a wealthy married couple radically change in an instant. While the husband is in critical condition after an accident that occurred under strange circumstances, the wife tries to understand the situation and to salvage what she can… In this constricted, small-scale drama by a renowned Slovenian filmmaker, Pia Zemljič excels in the role of the resourceful wife.
