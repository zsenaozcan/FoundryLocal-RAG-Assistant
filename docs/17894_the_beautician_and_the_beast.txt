Title: The Beautician and the Beast (1997)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Ken Kwapis
Main Cast: Timothy Dalton, Fran Drescher, Michael Lerner, Ian McNeice, Patrick Malahide
Keywords: dictator, female protagonist, beautician

Overview: The story follows the misadventures of a New York City beautician who is mistakenly hired as the school teacher for the children of the president of a small Eastern European country.
