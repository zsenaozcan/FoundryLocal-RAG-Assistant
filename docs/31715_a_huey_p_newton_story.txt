Title: A Huey P. Newton Story (2001)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Spike Lee
Main Cast: Roger Guenveur Smith
Keywords: black panthers, based on play or musical

Overview: A Huey P. Newton Story is a 2001 film directed by Spike Lee. It is a filmed performance of Roger Guenveur Smith's one-man show of the same name. Smith sits in a chair on a stage and tells about the past, mostly dealing with Huey P. Newton's life and times.
