Title: Racing with the Moon (1984)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Richard Benjamin
Main Cast: Sean Penn, Nicolas Cage, Elizabeth McGovern, John Karlen, Rutanya Alda

Overview: Henry and Nicky are small town pals from blue collar families with only a short time before they ship off to World War II. Henry begins romancing new-to-town Caddie Winger, believing her to be wealthy. Mischievious and irresponsible, Nicky gets into trouble which forces the other two to become involved, testing their relationship, as well as the friendship between the boys.
