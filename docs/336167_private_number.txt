Title: Private Number (2015)
Genres: Mystery, Horror, Thriller
Director: LazRael Lison
Main Cast: Judd Nelson, Tom Sizemore, Hal Ozsan, Nicholle Tom

Overview: A series of cryptic phone messages and visions haunt a writer while he struggles to finish a novel. As they increase in intensity, he loses his grip on reality, eventually obsessing over an old mystery that will lead to horrific revelations about both him and his loyal wife.
