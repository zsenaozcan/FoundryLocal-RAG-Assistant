Title: Into the Grizzly Maze (2015)
Genres: Action, Horror, Thriller
Director: David Hackl
Main Cast: James Marsden, Thomas Jane, Piper Perabo, Scott Glenn, Michaela McManus
Keywords: grizzly bear, wilderness, forest, alaska, survival, bear

Overview: Two estranged brothers reunite at their childhood home in the Alaskan wild. They set out on a two-day hike and are stalked by an unrelenting grizzly bear.
