Title: Random Harvest (1942)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Mervyn LeRoy
Main Cast: Ronald Colman, Greer Garson, Philip Dorn, Susan Peters, Henry Travers
Keywords: world war i

Overview: An amnesiac World War I vet falls in love with a music hall star, only to suffer an accident which restores his original memories but erases his post-War life.
