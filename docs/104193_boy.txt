Title: Boy (1969)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Nagisa Ōshima
Main Cast: Fumio Watanabe, Akiko Koyama, Do-yun Yu, Tsuyoshi Kinoshita
Keywords: child abuse, faked accidents, family travels

Overview: A family of four lives off of scams in which they pretend to be injured by automobiles. After suffering an injury during the war, the father believes he is an invalid. He and his wife have a 10-year-old boy and a 3-year-old girl. The adults pretend to be injured by autos in crowded traffic, blackmailing the fearful motorists with threats to call in the police.
