Title: Brothers at War (2009)
Genres: Documentary, History
Director: Jake Rademacher
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: independent film, iraq war

Overview: BROTHERS AT WAR is an intimate portrait of an American family during a turbulent time.  Jake Rademacher sets out to understand the experience, sacrifice, and motivation of his two brothers serving in Iraq. The film follows Jake’s exploits as he risks everything—including his life—to tell his brothers’ story.
