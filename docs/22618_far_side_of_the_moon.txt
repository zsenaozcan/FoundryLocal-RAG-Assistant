Title: Far Side of the Moon (2003)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Robert Lepage
Main Cast: Robert Lepage, Céline Bonnier, Anne-Marie Cadieux, Marco Poulin, Érika Gagnon
Keywords: newspaper, video, outer space, lecture, cosmonaut

Overview: After the death of his mother, a man tries to discover a meaning to his life, to the universe and to rebuild a relationship with the only family he has left: his brother.
