Title: Twentieth Century (1934)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Howard Hawks
Main Cast: John Barrymore, Carole Lombard, Walter Connolly, Roscoe Karns, Ralph Forbes
Keywords: stage, theatre play, train ride, disguise, rehearsal, theater, protégé, broadway director, actress, ham

Overview: Oscar Jaffe is a successful Broadway director, Lily Garland his biggest star. When she leaves his direction, his success goes with her. When he recognizes her aboard the Twentieth Century Limited, the train that both of them are riding, he tries to get her back for a new show. But accomplishing that feat isn't as simple as he had thought.
