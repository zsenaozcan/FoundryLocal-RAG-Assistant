Title: Castle of Purity (1973)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Arturo Ripstein
Main Cast: Claudio Brook, Rita Macedo, Arturo Beristáin, Diana Bracho, Gladys Bermejo
Keywords: female nudity, poison, adultery, wife husband relationship, domestic abuse, salesman, prostitution, hypocrisy, misogynist, jealous husband

Overview: The story of a disciplined and sexually driven man who keeps his family isolated in his home for years to protect them from the “evil nature” of human beings while inventing (with his wife) rat poison.
