Title: Sweet Hearts Dance (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Robert Greenwald
Main Cast: Don Johnson, Susan Sarandon, Jeff Daniels, Elizabeth Perkins, Holly Marie Combs

Overview: Wiley and Sandra have been happily married for years and are now in the process of breaking up. Sam, his childhood friend, is just beginning to fall in love with a new teacher at the high school. As they try to adjust to these conflicting emotions they find themselves having to evaluate their own relationship as well.
