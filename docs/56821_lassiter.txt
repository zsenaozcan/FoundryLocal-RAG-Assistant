Title: Lassiter (1984)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Roger Young
Main Cast: Tom Selleck, Jane Seymour, Lauren Hutton, Bob Hoskins, Joe Regalbuto
Keywords: london england, nudity, embassy, police, thief, jewel

Overview: Lassiter is a handsome jewel thief operating in London in the late 1930s. One day he is arrested and told that if he wishes to avoid prison, he must break into the heavily guarded German Embassy in London and steal millions in Gems. Written by John Vogel
