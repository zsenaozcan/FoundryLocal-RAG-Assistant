Title: The Parent Trap (1961)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: David Swift
Main Cast: Hayley Mills, Maureen O'Hara, Brian Keith, Charles Ruggles, Una Merkel
Keywords: deception, twins

Overview: Hayley Mills plays twins who, unknown to their divorced parents, meet at a summer camp. Products of single parent households, they switch places (surprise!) so as to meet the parent they never knew, and then contrive to reunite them.
