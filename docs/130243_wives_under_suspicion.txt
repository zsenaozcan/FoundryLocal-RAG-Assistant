Title: Wives Under Suspicion (1938)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: James Whale
Main Cast: Warren William, Gail Patrick, Constance Moore, William Lundigan, Ralph Morgan
Keywords: district attorney

Overview: A merciless district attorney prosecutes a case that mirrors his own life. Director James Whale's 1938 drama stars Warren William, Gail Patrick, William Lundigan, Constance Moore, Cecil Cunningham, Milburn Stone, Ralph Morgan, Lillian Yarbo, Jonathan Hale, James Flavin and Samuel S. Hinds.
