Title: Oblivion (1994)
Genres: Adventure, Science Fiction, Western, Comedy
Director: Sam Irvin
Main Cast: Richard Joseph Paul, Jackie Swanson, Andrew Divoff, Meg Foster, Isaac Hayes
Keywords: cyborg, low-budget, revenge, alien, undertaker, cowboy

Overview: The alien outlaw, Redeye, murders the town sheriff and then he and his gang terrorize the town. It's up to the sheriff's son, an empath, and his native american sidekick, to rescue the town and exact revenge for his father's murder.
