Title: The Twelve Months (1956)
Genres: Fantasy, Animation, Family
Director: Ivan Ivanov-Vano
Main Cast: Tatyana Barysheva, Yuliya Yulskaya, Lyudmila Kasatkina, Erast Garin, Grigoriy Shpigel

Overview: A poor girl was given an impossible task by her stepmother: to gather snowdrops in a winter forest. Suddenly she stumbled across twelve brothers who happened to be the twelve months.
