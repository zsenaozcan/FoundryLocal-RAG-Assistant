Title: Pool of London (1951)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Basil Dearden
Main Cast: Bonar Colleano, Earl Cameron, Susan Shaw, Renée Asherson, Moira Lister
Keywords: london england, melodrama, ealing

Overview: Jewel thieves, murder and a manhunt swirl around a sailor (Bonar Colleano) off a cargo ship in London.
