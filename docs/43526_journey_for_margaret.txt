Title: Journey for Margaret (1942)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: W.S. Van Dyke
Main Cast: Robert Young, Laraine Day, Fay Bainter, Nigel Bruce, Margaret O'Brien
Keywords: london england, newspaper man

Overview: An American newspaperman and his wife, caught in the London blitz, lose their unborn child in an air raid. Outraged, they visit a shelter for homeless children where they fall in love with orphans Margaret and her brother Peter. They eventaully adopt the children and bring them to America.
