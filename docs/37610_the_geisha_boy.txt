Title: The Geisha Boy (1958)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Frank Tashlin
Main Cast: Jerry Lewis, Marie McDonald, Sessue Hayakawa, Barton MacLane, Robert Hirano
Keywords: geisha

Overview: Gilbert Wooley is a second-rate magician who is sent to entertain the troops in the pacific. During his time in Japan he becomes attached to a little orphan boy.
