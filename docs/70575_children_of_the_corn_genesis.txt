Title: Children of the Corn: Genesis (2011)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Joel Soisson
Main Cast: J.J. Banicki, Dusty Burwell, Kai Caster, Kelen Coleman, Billy Drago

Overview: Tim and Allie seek shelter in a remote desert compound after becoming lost and stranded. A strange Manson-like character, Preacher, reluctantly allows them inside with strict orders to be gone by morning and not wander "where you are not invited."
