Title: Whirlygirl (2006)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Jim Wilson
Main Cast: Monet Mazur, Julian Morris, J.A.Q., Johnny Pruitt, Fran Kranz
Keywords: robbery, new love, party

Overview: After a run in with a hot exotic dancer one wintry night straight-laced prep school student James will find himself on the ride of his life. Risking his future he follows this mysterious woman to New York City - ditching school dodging cops and partying at hot nightclubs along the way.
