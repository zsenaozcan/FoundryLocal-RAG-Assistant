Title: Seven Old Men and One Girl (1968)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Yevgeni Karelov
Main Cast: Svetlana Savyolova, Valentin Smirnitskiy, Boris Novikov, Aleksei Smirnov, Nikolai Parfyonov

Overview: Lara Velichko is a young coach who's dreaming about coaching world champions but suddenly she's forced to train a group of a very grumpy old men.
