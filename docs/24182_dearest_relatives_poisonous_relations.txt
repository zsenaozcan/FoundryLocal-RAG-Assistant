Title: Dearest Relatives, Poisonous Relations (1992)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mario Monicelli
Main Cast: Alessandro Haber, Paolo Panelli, Cinzia Leone, Tommaso Bianco, Eugenio Masciari
Keywords: drama, family, natale, capodanno, sulmona, avós, natal, ceia, velhice

Overview: Like every year, everyone is going back to the ancestral family's home for the Christmas holidays. But this time, the old folks have a surprise, a good surprise for them. They have decided to go living with one of their children and make him or her inherit the family house.
