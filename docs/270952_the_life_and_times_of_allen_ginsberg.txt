Title: The Life and Times of Allen Ginsberg (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jerry Aronson
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: independent, documentary - For 25 years, Academy Award®-nominated director Jerry Aronson accumulated more than 120 hours of film on Allen Ginsberg, resulting in this comprehensive portrait of one of Americas greatest poets, author of Howl and other ground-breaking poems.  - Jerry Aronson
