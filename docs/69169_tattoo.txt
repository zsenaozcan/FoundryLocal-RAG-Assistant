Title: Tattoo (1981)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Horror
Director: Bob Brooks
Main Cast: Bruce Dern, Maud Adams, Leonard Frey, Frederikke Borge, John Getz
Keywords: tattoo

Overview: Karl Kinsky, an unbalanced tattoo artist, becomes obsessed with Maddy, a model he meets when he is hired to body-paint several women for a photo shoot, making the women look like they have large tattoos. As Kinsky grows more obsessed with Maddy, he becomes increasingly determined that Maddy should bear his "mark" -- forever.
