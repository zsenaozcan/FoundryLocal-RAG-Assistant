Title: Storm Warning (2007)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jamie Blanks
Main Cast: Nadia Farès, Robert Taylor, John Brumpton, David Lyons, Mathew Wilkinson
Keywords: island, sadism, castration, psychopath, revenge, gore, survival, torture, brutality, violence

Overview: A yuppie couple lost in a swamp seek refuge at an isolated farmhouse only to discover they've jumped out of the frying pan into the fire.
