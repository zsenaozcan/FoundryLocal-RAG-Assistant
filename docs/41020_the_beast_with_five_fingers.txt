Title: The Beast with Five Fingers (1946)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Robert Florey
Main Cast: Robert Alda, Andrea King, Peter Lorre, Victor Francen, J. Carrol Naish
Keywords: murder, old house, hand, severed hand, piano player

Overview: Locals in an Italian village believe evil has taken over the estate of a recently deceased pianist where several murders have taken place. The alleged killer: the pianist's severed hand.
