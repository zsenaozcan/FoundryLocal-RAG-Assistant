Title: Phenomena (1985)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Dario Argento
Main Cast: Jennifer Connelly, Donald Pleasence, Daria Nicolodi, Patrick Bauchau, Fiore Argento
Keywords: detective, daughter, psychopath, revenge, murder, school, doctor, maniac, chimpanzee, sleepwalking

Overview: A young girl, with an amazing ability to communicate with insects, is transferred to an exclusive Swiss boarding school, where her unusual capability might help solve a string of murders.
