Title: Auggie Rose (2000)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Matthew Tabak
Main Cast: Jeff Goldblum, Anne Heche, Nancy Travis, Timothy Olyphant, Joe Santos

Overview: An insurance salesman's humdrum existence takes a turn when a stranger, ex-con Auggie Rose, unexpectedly dies in his arms. Assuming the identity of the dead man, the salesman embarks on a double life, keeping it secret from his live-in girlfriend.
