Title: It Felt Like Love (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Eliza Hittman
Main Cast: Gina Piersanti, Ronen Rubinstein, Jesse Cordasco, Giovanna Salimeni, Andrew McCord
Keywords: independent film, woman director

Overview: Fourteen-year-old Lila is experiencing an ennui-filled Brooklyn summer. She awkwardly wears a Kabuki-esque mask of sunscreen at the beach and plays third wheel to Chiara, her more experienced friend, and Chiara’s boyfriend, Patrick. Determined to have a love interest of her own, a bravado-filled Lila pursues Sammy, a tough but handsome older boy....
