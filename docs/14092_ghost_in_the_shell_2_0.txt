Title: Ghost in the Shell 2.0 (2008)
Genres: Action, Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Mamoru Oshii
Main Cast: Atsuko Tanaka, Akio Ohtsuka, Kouichi Yamadera, Tamio Ohki, Tesshou Genda
Keywords: cyborg, android, future, dystopia, cyberpunk

Overview: In the year 2029, Section 9, a group of cybernetically enhanced cops, are called in to investigate and stop a highly-wanted hacker known as 'The Puppetmaster'.  Ghost in the Shell 2.0 is a reproduced version of its original 1995 counterpart. Among a numerous enhancements, for the film's 2.0 release, were a number of scenes were overhauled with 3D animation, visual improvements, and soundtrack rerecorded in 6.1 surround sound.
