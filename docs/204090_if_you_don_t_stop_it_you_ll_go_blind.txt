Title: If You Don't Stop It...You'll Go Blind!!! (1975)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Keefe Brasselle
Main Cast: George Spencer, Jane Kellem, Uschi Digard, Patrick Wright, Garth Pillsbury
Keywords: nudity, adult humor

Overview: A series of vignettes, usually of a crude sexual nature, from various not so well known comedians combining to make a hilarious spoof.
