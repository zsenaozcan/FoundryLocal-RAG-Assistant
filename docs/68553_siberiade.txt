Title: Siberiade (1979)
Genres: War, Drama, History
Director: Andrei Konchalovsky
Main Cast: Nikita Mikhalkov, Vitali Solomin, Sergey Shakurov, Natalya Andreychenko, Lyudmila Gurchenko

Overview: The evolution of a nation during six decades of turbulent history is dramatized via two families from opposing classes.
