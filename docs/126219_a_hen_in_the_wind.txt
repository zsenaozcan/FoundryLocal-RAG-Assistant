Title: A Hen in the Wind (1948)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Main Cast: Kinuyo Tanaka, Shûji Sano, Chieko Murata, Chishu Ryu, Hohi Aoki
Keywords: prostitute, guilt, post world war ii

Overview: Tokiko is a mother patiently waiting for her husband's return from the war when her 4-year old son becomes ill. She takes him to the doctor for treatment but has no way of paying. She resorts to prostitution. One month later her husband returns from WWII to find his desperate wife, who tells him the truth. Together they must deal with the consequences.
