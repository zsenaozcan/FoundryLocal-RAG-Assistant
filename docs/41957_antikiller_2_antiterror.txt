Title: Antikiller 2: Antiterror (2003)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Yegor Konchalovsky
Main Cast: Yuriy Kutsenko, Lyubov Tolkalina, Aleksey Serebryakov, Sergey Veksler, Sergey Shakurov
