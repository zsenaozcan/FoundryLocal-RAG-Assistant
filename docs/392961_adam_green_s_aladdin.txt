Title: Adam Green's Aladdin (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Adam Green
Main Cast: Adam Green, Alia Shawkat, Macaulay Culkin, Natasha Lyonne, Jack Dishel
Keywords: aladdin

Overview: In this retelling of the classic tale, Aladdin is an out of work indie-rock singer living in a video-game-world ruled by a perverted technology-obsessed Sultan.
