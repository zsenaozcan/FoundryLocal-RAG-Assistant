Title: My Summer of Love (2004)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Romance
Director: Paweł Pawlikowski
Main Cast: Natalie Press, Emily Blunt, Paddy Considine, Dean Andrews
Keywords: england, brother sister relationship, becoming an adult, northern england, female friendship, moped, lesbian relationship, homosexuality, summer, tomboy

Overview: In the Yorkshire countryside, working-class tomboy Mona meets the exotic, pampered Tasmin. Over the summer season, the two young women discover they have much to teach one another, and much to explore together.
