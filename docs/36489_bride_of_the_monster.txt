Title: Bride of the Monster (1955)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Edward D. Wood Jr.
Main Cast: Bela Lugosi, Tony McCoy, Tor Johnson, Loretta King, Harvey B. Dunn
Keywords: experiment, octopus, b movie, woman reporter, cult director

Overview: Dr. Varnoff captures twelve men for his experiment: to turn them into supermen using atomic energy. Newspaperwoman Lawton gets too snoopy for her own good.
