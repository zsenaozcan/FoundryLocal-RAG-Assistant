Title: Arrambam (2013)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Drama
Director: Vishnuvardhan
Main Cast: Ajith Kumar, Arya, Nayanthara, Taapsee Pannu, Mahesh Manjrekar

Overview: The film's plot is set in the backdrop of 26/11 serial blasts in Mumbai, and revolves around Ashok Kumar (Ajith), who is on a mission to expose a scam regarding faulty bullet proof jackets that are provided to security forces, which resulted in many bloody deaths including his best friend and colleague, Assistant Commissioner Sanjay (Rana).
