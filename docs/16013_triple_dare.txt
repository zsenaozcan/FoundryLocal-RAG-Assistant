Title: Triple Dare (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Christina Rosendahl
Main Cast: Emma Leth, Cathrine Bjørn, Amalie Lindegård, Sebastian Jessen, Charlotte Sieling

Overview: Rebekka, Claudia and Sofie are freshmen in high school and will no longer accept being treated as juniors. Taking matters into their own hands, they devise a rite of passage, the 'fortune teller', symbolizing their entry into adulthood. As they take turns challenging each other's sexual boundaries, they eventually have to ask themselves whether performing weird rituals is the easiest way to get to know themselves and feel grown up.
