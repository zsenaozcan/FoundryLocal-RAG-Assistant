Title: Sadie Thompson (1928)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: Gloria Swanson, Lionel Barrymore, Blanche Friderici, Charles Willis Lane, Florence Midgley
Keywords: suicide, south seas, religion, silent film, u.s. marine corps

Overview: Sadie Thompson arrives in Pago-Pago to start a new life, but when extremist missionary Davidson lashes out against her lifestyle and tries to force her back to San Francisco, she may lose her second chance.
