Title: Indiscretion of an American Wife (1953)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Vittorio De Sica
Main Cast: Jennifer Jones, Montgomery Clift, Gino Cervi, Richard Beymer
Keywords: train, extramarital affair

Overview: Prior to leaving by train for Paris, a married American woman tries to break off her affair with a young Italian in Rome's Stazione Termini.
