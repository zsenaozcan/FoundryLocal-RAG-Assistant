Title: S&Man (2006)
Genres: Horror
Director: J.T. Petty
Main Cast: Elizabeth Cartier, Carol J. Clover, Carol J. Clover, Debbie D, Michelle Glick

Overview: S&amp;Man (also known as Sandman) is a 2006 pseudo-documentary film that examines the underground subculture of horror films. It combines real interviews with indie horror film makers and a scripted plot that does not immediately come into focus until the second half of the film.
