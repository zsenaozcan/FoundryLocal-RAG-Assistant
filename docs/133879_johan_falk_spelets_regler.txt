Title: Johan Falk: Spelets regler (2012)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Charlotte Brandström
Main Cast: Jakob Eklund, Joel Kinnaman, Mikael Tornving, Meliz Karlge, Zeljko Santrac
Keywords: criminal investigation, woman director

Overview: It's been two years since John Falk and Frank Wagner broke their cooperation and Frank started working undercover with the police. Frank's search for Kevin crosses Johan's investigation and opens up a new collaboration between them - with the big difference being that this time it's personal for Frank.
