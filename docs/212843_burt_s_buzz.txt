Title: Burt's Buzz (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jody Shapiro
Main Cast: Burt Shavitz

Overview: Burt's Buzz is an in-depth and personal look at the life of Burt Shavitz, known to millions around the world as the 'Burt' of the Burt’s Bees natural product brand. The documentary explores what it means to be marketed as an icon, and how that life differs from the one of the man behind the logo.
