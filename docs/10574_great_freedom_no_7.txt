Title: Great Freedom No. 7 (1944)
Genres: Drama
Director: Helmut Käutner
Main Cast: Hans Albers, Ilse Werner, Hans Söhnker, Hilde Hildebrand, Gustav Knuth
Keywords: brother brother relationship, loss of brother, ex-girlfriend, dying and death, musical

Overview: Singing sailor Hannes, who entertains the crowd in St. Pauli's Hippodrom, promises his dying brother to take care of his ex-girlfriend Gisa. Taking Gisa to Hamburg to live with him, Hannes slowly falls in love with her, but soon has to face Gisa's affection for Willem.
