Title: A Quiet Outpost (2011)
Genres: Drama, Action, War
Director: Sergei Makhovikov
Main Cast: Andrey Chadov, Sergei Selin, Igor Savochkin, Iya Ninidze

Overview: On the edge of Dushanbe, in the Republic of Tajikistan, a quiet border checkpoint under Russian control is attacked by Afghans and Tajiks who want it under their control.
