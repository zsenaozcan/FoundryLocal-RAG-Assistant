Title: Crash (1996)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Romance
Director: David Cronenberg
Main Cast: James Spader, Deborah Kara Unger, Holly Hunter, Elias Koteas, Rosanna Arquette
Keywords: gay, sexual obsession, adultery, sadistic, sexuality, wife husband relationship, eroticism, car journey, simulation, dead wish

Overview: After getting into a serious car accident, a TV director discovers an underground sub-culture of scarred, omnisexual car-crash victims who use car accidents and the raw sexual energy they produce to try to rejuvenate his sex life with his wife.
