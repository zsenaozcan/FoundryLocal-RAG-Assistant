Title: Feet First (1930)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Clyde Bruckman
Main Cast: Harold Lloyd, Barbara Kent, Robert McWade, Lillian Leighton, Henry Hall

Overview: Ambitious shoe salesman, Harold, unknowingly meets the boss' daughter and tells her he is a leather tycoon. The rest of the film he spends hiding his true circumstances, in the store and later on a ship. Trying to deliver a letter, he later finds himself dangling high above the street on a building's scaffolding.
