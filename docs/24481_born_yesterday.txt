Title: Born Yesterday (1950)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Drama
Director: George Cukor
Main Cast: Judy Holliday, Broderick Crawford, William Holden, Howard St. John, Frank Otto
Keywords: washington d.c., congress, tycoon

Overview: Uncouth, loud-mouth junkyard tycoon Harry Brock descends upon Washington D.C. to buy himself a congressman or two, bringing with him his mistress, ex-showgirl Billie Dawn.
