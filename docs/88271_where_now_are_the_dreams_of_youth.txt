Title: Where Now Are the Dreams of Youth? (1932)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Main Cast: Ureo Egawa, Kinuyo Tanaka, Tatsuo Saitô, Haruo Takeda, Ryôtarô Mizushima

Overview: When a young man inherits his father's lucrative business, he cheats the system to set up three of his college friends with jobs.
