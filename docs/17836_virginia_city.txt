Title: Virginia City (1940)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: Errol Flynn, Miriam  Hopkins, Randolph Scott, Humphrey Bogart, Frank McHugh
Keywords: prison, escape, stagecoach

Overview: Union officer Kerry Bradford escapes from a Confederate prison and races to intercept $5 million in gold destined for Confederate coffers. A Confederate sympathizer and a Mexican bandit, each with their own stake in the loot, stand in his way.
