Title: Reluctant Witness (2015)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Monika Mitchell
Main Cast: Mia Kirshner, Paul McGillion, Madeleine Arthur, James Kirk, Bruce Ramsay

Overview: After giving up her mafia husband to the police, and starting a new life in witness protection, Erin thinks she's rid of Jimmy forever. However, a mysterious stranger has her worrying Jimmy is back.
