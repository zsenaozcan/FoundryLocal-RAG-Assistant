Title: One Man's Justice (1996)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Kurt Wimmer
Main Cast: Brian Bosworth, MC Hammer, Neal McDonough, Bruce Payne, Jeff Kober
Keywords: fbi, investigation, drug, military
