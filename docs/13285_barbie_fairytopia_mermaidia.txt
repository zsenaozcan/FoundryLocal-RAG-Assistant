Title: Barbie Fairytopia: Mermaidia (2006)
Genres: Animation, Family, Adventure, Fantasy
Director: William Lau
Main Cast: Kelly Sheridan, Lee Tockar, Christopher Gaze, Kathleen Barr, Chiara Zanni

Overview: In this animated follow-up to Fairytopia, Elina (played by Barbie) enlists the help of a mermaid, Nori, to save her friend Nalu, a merman prince who has been captured by the wicked Laverna.
