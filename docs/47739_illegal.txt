Title: Illegal (1955)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Lewis Allen
Main Cast: Edward G. Robinson, Nina Foch, Hugh Marlowe, Jayne Mansfield, Albert Dekker
Keywords: court, criminal defence lawyer

Overview: A hugely successful DA goes into private practice after sending a man to the chair -- only to find out later he was innocent. Now the drunken attorney only seems to represent criminals and low lifes.
