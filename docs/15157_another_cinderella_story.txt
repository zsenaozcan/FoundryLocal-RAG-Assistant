Title: Another Cinderella Story (2008)
Genres: Music
Director: Damon Santostefano
Main Cast: Selena Gomez, Jane Lynch, Katharine Isabelle, Emily Perkins, Jessica Parker Kennedy
Keywords: dancer, cinderella, dance, masked ball, fairy tale, musical, high school, celebrity, orphan, teenager

Overview: A guy who danced with what could be the girl of his dreams at a costume ball only has one hint at her identity: the Zune she left behind as she rushed home in order to make her curfew. And with a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity in front of him, he sets out to find his masked beauty.
