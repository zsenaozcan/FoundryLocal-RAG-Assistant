Title: Battle for Terra (2007)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Aristomenis Tsirbas
Main Cast: Evan Rachel Wood, Brian Cox, James Garner, Chris Evans, Danny Glover
Keywords: mutiny, cave, chase, invasion, surrealism, robot, air battle, pacifist, council

Overview: A peaceful alien planet faces annihilation, as the homeless remainder of the human race sets its eyes on Terra. Mala, a rebellious Terrian teenager, will do everything she can to stop it.
