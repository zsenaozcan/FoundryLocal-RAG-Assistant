Title: The Mistletoe Promise (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, TV Movie
Director: David Winning
Main Cast: Jaime King, Luke MacFarlane, Christie Laing, Lochlyn Munro, Sarah Smyth
Keywords: holiday, christmas, pretend relationship

Overview: A chance meeting between two strangers who share a disdain for Christmas results in The Mistletoe Promise, a pact to help them navigate their holiday complications - together. But as they spend more time with each other and experience the magic of Christmas the phony couple discovers there may be more to their contract than business.
