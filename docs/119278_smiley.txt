Title: Smiley (2012)
Genres: Horror
Director: Michael J. Gallagher
Main Cast: Caitlin Gerard, Melanie Papalia, Shane Dawson, Roger Bart, Keith David

Overview: After learning of an urban legend in which a demented serial killer named SMILEY can be summoned through the Internet, mentally fragile Ashley must decide whether she is losing her mind or becoming Smiley's next victim.
