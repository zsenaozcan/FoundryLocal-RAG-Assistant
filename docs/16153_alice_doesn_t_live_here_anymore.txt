Title: Alice Doesn't Live Here Anymore (1974)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Martin Scorsese
Main Cast: Ellen Burstyn, Kris Kristofferson, Alfred Lutter, Harvey Keitel, Diane Ladd
Keywords: waitress, widow
