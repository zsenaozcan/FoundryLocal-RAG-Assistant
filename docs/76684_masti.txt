Title: Masti (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Indra Kumar
Main Cast: Vivek Oberoi, Aftab Shivdasani, Ritesh Deshmukh, Amrita Rao, Genelia D'Souza
Keywords: blackmail, extramarital affair

Overview: Three henpecked friends try to escape their unhappy marriages by seeking out some extramarital fun, only to end up being blackmailed.
