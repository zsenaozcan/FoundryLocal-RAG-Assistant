Title: Slightly Scarlet (1956)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Crime, Documentary
Director: Allan Dwan
Main Cast: John Payne, Rhonda Fleming, Arlene Dahl, Kent Taylor, Ted de Corsia
Keywords: red hair, kleptomania
