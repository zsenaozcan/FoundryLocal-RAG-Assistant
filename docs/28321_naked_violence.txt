Title: Naked Violence (1969)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Fernando Di Leo
Main Cast: Pier Paolo Capponi, Nieves Navarro, Marzio Margine, Renato Lupi, Enzo Liberti
Keywords: rape, murder, teacher, interrogation, poliziotteschi

Overview: Some youngsters rape and kill their teacher; but they won't tell their motivation. The police detective on the case feels some sinister influence behind the young murderers.
