Title: A Dirty Carnival (2006)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Yoo Ha
Main Cast: Jo In-sung, Cheon Ho-jin, Lee Bo-young, Namgoong Min, Lee Hyun-woo
Keywords: fistfight, kick boxing, gangster

Overview: Byung-du is a 29-year-old career criminal, working for the middle-rank enforcer Sang-chul. Burdened with a terminally ill mother and taking care of younger siblings, Byung-du is feeling financial pressure as a substitute patriarch. When the big boss President Hwang is cornered by a corrupt prosecutor, Byung-du volunteers for a whack job and wins the big man's trust.
