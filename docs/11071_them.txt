Title: Them! (1954)
Genres: Science Fiction, Mystery, Horror
Director: Gordon Douglas
Main Cast: James Whitmore, James Arness, Joan Weldon, Edmund Gwenn, Onslow Stevens
Keywords: monster, new mexico, radiation, fbi, atomic bomb, mutation, ant, army, giant monster, desert

Overview: Nuclear tests in the desert result in the growth of gigantic mutant ants who menace cities in the American south-west as a team of investigators and the army search for a way to control their spread in this Cold War-era monster film.
