Title: Barnacle Bill (1941)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Richard Thorpe
Main Cast: Wallace Beery, Marjorie Main, Virginia Weidler, Leo Carrillo, Donald Meek
Keywords: fisherman, father daughter relationship, sailor

Overview: Director Richard Thorpe's 1941 film stars Wallace Beery as a crusty old San Pedro fisherman, Virginia Weidler as his precocious and motherless daughter, Marjorie Main as a waterfront spinster hoping to reel in a husband, and an ever-present pelican with immaculate comic timing.
