Title: To Kill with Intrigue (1977)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Lo Wei
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Hsu Feng, Sin Il-Ryong, Yu Ling-Lung, George Wang
Keywords: martial arts, kung fu, melodrama

Overview: Young master Cao Le chases his pregnant girlfriend away from the family castle. He does it in order to save her from vicious bandits who are going to murder his family.
