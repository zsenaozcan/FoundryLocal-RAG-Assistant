Title: Boy Eats Girl (2005)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Horror
Director: Stephen Bradley
Main Cast: Samantha Mumba, David Leon, Tadhg Murphy, Laurence Kinlan, Mark Huberman
Keywords: zombie

Overview: A boy declares his love for his girlfriend, only to die the same night. He is brought back to life by his mother as a flesh-craving zombie, who sires more teen undead while trying to control his, er, appetite for his beloved.
