Title: Colegas (1982)
Genres: Drama
Director: Eloy de la Iglesia
Main Cast: Antonio Flores, Rosario Flores, José Luis Manzano, José Manuel Cervino, Queta Ariel
Keywords: friendship, juvenile delinquent, drug, social issues, cine quinqui

Overview: The plot follows the misadventures of two young friends who are forced into street hustling and ever-expanding life of crime when one impregnates the sister of the other and they need to get the money to pay for her to have an abortion.
