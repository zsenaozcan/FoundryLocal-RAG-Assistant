Title: Death Warrant (1990)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Deran Sarafian
Main Cast: Jean-Claude Van Damme, Robert Guillaume, Cynthia Gibb, Patrick Kilpatrick, Art LaFleur
Keywords: prison, prisoner, murder

Overview: The Canadian policeman Louis Burke is assigned in a jail to investigate in some murders of prisoners and jailors. When in jail, Lois, using his outstandings martial arts is able to save his life and make himself respected in that violent world. At least, helped by two another prisoners, he succeded in finding the truth about the dreadful crimes.
