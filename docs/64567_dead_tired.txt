Title: Dead Tired (1994)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michel Blanc
Main Cast: Michel Blanc, Philippe Noiret, Marie-Anne Chazel, Charlotte Gainsbourg, Carole Bouquet

Overview: Stressed and overworked, famous French movie star Michel Blanc (Michel Blanc) is beginning to wear down, physically and mentally, from the pressure and demands of fame. Already in a fragile state of mind, strange events start to transpire all around him, and he gradually loses his grip. Taking the advice of a psychiatrist, Blanc retreats to the countryside with his friend, Carole Bouquet (Carole Bouquet), but Blanc still has not managed to escape all of his problems.
