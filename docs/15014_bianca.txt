Title: Bianca (1984)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Nanni Moretti
Main Cast: Nanni Moretti, Laura Morante, Roberto Vezzosi, Remo Remotti

Overview: Michele is a mathematics professor who just started a new job in a school with some peculiar teaching methods. After a woman in his neighborhood is murdered, Michele meets beautiful colleague Bianca, and a relationship begins between the two. Where is this relationship heading, and will Michele be able to help the police catch the murderer?
