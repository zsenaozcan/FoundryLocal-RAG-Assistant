Title: Rakht Charitra (2010)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Ram Gopal Varma
Main Cast: Vivek Oberoi, Shatrughan Sinha, Abhimanyu Singh, Sushant Singh, Radhika Apte
Keywords: revenge, gangster, political killing, political thriller

Overview: A killer bandit decides to become a politician in order to avenge deaths in his family.
