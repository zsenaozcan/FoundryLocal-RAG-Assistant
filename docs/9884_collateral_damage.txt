Title: Collateral Damage (2002)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Drama
Director: Andrew Davis
Main Cast: Arnold Schwarzenegger, Francesca Neri, Elias Koteas, Cliff Curtis, John Turturro
Keywords: terrorist, fbi, colombia, firemen, revenge, explosion, car explosion, bomb attack
