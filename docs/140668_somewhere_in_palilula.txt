Title: Somewhere in Palilula (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Silviu Purcărete
Main Cast: Áron Dimény, George Mihaiță, Răzvan Vasilescu, Constantin Chiriac, Sorin Leoveanu

Overview: The 60's, Romania. Serafim, a young doctor, is sent to the Hospital in Palilula, a small town lost somewhere on the country map, after the death of the previous pediatrician, Mr. Pantelică.
