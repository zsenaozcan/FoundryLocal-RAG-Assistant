Title: Empire of Silver (2009)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, History, Family, Romance
Director: Christina Yao
Main Cast: Aaron Kwok, Lantian Chang, Jennifer Tilly, Hao Lei, Zhang Tie-Lin
Keywords: women, feminism, christian, banking, independent film, parent, asian, religious, period drama, docudrama

Overview: In 1899, Lord Kang must decide which of his three sons will take over his family's Chinese banking empire. When circumstances dictate that he appoint his unreliable youngest son, family bonds are pushed to the limit as father and son clash in a climate of political turmoil. Winner of the Special Jury Award at the 2009 Shanghai International Film Festival.
