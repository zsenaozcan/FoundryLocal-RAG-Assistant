Title: The Auction (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sébastien Pilote
Main Cast: Gabriel Arcand, Pierre-Luc Brillant, Normand Carrière, Claude Desjardins, Sophie Desmarais
Keywords: quebec, farm, rural setting, father daughter relationship, elderly
