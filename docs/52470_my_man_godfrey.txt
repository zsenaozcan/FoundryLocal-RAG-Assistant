Title: My Man Godfrey (1957)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Henry Koster
Main Cast: June Allyson, David Niven, Jessie Royce Landis, Robert Keith, Eva Gabor
Keywords: butler

Overview: The eccentric Bullock household again need a new butler. Daughter Irene encounters bedraggled Godfrey Godfrey at the docks and, fancying him and noticing his obviously good manners, gets him the job. He proves a great success, but keeps his past to himself. When an old flame turns up Irene's sister Cordelia starts making waves.
