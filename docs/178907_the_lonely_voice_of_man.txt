Title: The Lonely Voice of Man (1987)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alexander Sokurov
Main Cast: Tatyana Goryacheva, Andrey Gradov, Vladimir Degtyarev
Keywords: man-woman relation

Overview: The film is Sokurov's feature film debut and is based on the novel The River Potudan by Andrei Platonov. It's a story about the lives of people in the Russian countryside in the beginning of the 20th century. The film was completed in 1979 as a graduation film, but was banned by the Soviet state and didn't premiere until 1987.
