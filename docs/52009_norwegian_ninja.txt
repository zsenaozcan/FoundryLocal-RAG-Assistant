Title: Norwegian Ninja (2010)
Genres: Drama, Action, Comedy, Thriller
Director: Thomas Cappelen Malling
Main Cast: Mads Ousdal, Jon Øigarden, Trond-Viggo Torgersen, Linn Stokke, Amund Maarud
Keywords: usa, submarine, norway, cold war, soviet union, wreck a movie, island camp, vtol, ninja training

Overview: Norwegian Ninja is the true story of how Commander Arne Treholt and his Ninja Force saved Norway during the Cold War. In 1983 the Ninja Force discovers that the sinister NATO force Stay Behind, who take charge in times of war and emergency, are planning a coup-d'état in peacetime. Treholt and the Ninjas see only one solution: a full can of whoop-ass.
