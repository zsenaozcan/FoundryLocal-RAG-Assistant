Title: An Inspector Calls (1954)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Romance
Director: Guy Hamilton
Main Cast: Alastair Sim, Jane Wenham, Brian Worth, Eileen Moore, Olga Lindo
Keywords: suicide, corruption, prostitute, secret, department store, fiancé, scandal, engagement, alcoholism, suspense

Overview: Based on a famous stage play and set in the year 1912, an upper crust English family dinner is interrupted by a police inspector who brings news that a girl known to everyone present has died in suspicious circumstances. It seems that any or all of them could have had a hand in her death. But who is the mysterious Inspector and what can he want of them?
