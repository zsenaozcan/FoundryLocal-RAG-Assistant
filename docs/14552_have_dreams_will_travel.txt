Title: Have Dreams, Will Travel (2007)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Brad Isaacs
Main Cast: Cayden Boyd, AnnaSophia Robb, Val Kilmer, Heather Graham, Lara Flynn Boyle
Keywords: lovers, innocence, romance, psychological thriller

Overview: West Texas, in the 1960's. A tale of two 12-year-olds who embark on an adventure to find new parents in order to escape their unhappy and emotionally unsatisfying family life.
