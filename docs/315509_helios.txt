Title: Helios (2015)
Genres: Crime, Action
Director: Sunny Luk Kim-Ching
Main Cast: Jacky Cheung, Nick Cheung, Shawn Yue, Chang Chen, Wang Xue-qi
Keywords: weapon of mass destruction, nuclear weapons, terrorist plot

Overview: Wanted criminal "Helios" and his assistant stole a quantity of uranium and plan to produce weapons of mass destruction. They are planning to trade with a terrorist organization in Hong Kong. Chief Inspector Lee Yinming and Inspector Fan Kaming lead the Counter Terrorism Response Unit of the Hong Kong Police Force. Along with a Chinese senior official, a physics professor, and two South Korean weapon experts, they hope to defuse a crisis sufficient to destroy the world.
