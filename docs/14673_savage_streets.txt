Title: Savage Streets (1984)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Danny Steinmann
Main Cast: Linda Blair, John Vernon, Robert Dryer, Johnny Venocur, Sal Landi
Keywords: street gang, rape, fire, nudity, exploitation film, punk band
