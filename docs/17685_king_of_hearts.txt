Title: King of Hearts (1966)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, War
Director: Philippe de Broca
Main Cast: Alan Bates, Geneviève Bujold, Pierre Brasseur, Michel Serrault, Jean-Claude Brialy
Keywords: asylum, time bomb, who are the crazy ones?

Overview: An ornithologist mistaken for an explosives expert is sent alone into a small French town during WWI to investigate a garbled report from the resistance about a bomb which the departing Germans have set to blow up a weapons cache.
