Title: Daughter of the Nile (1987)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hou Hsiao-hsien
Main Cast: Yang Lin, Jack Kao Kuo-Hsin, Li Tian-Lu, Tsui Fu-Sheng, Hsin Shu-Fen

Overview: This movie presents the changing face of an industrialized Taiwan, as seen through the eyes of a young girl left to care for her family after her mother's death.
