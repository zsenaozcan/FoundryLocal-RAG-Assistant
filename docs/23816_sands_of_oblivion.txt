Title: Sands Of Oblivion (2007)
Genres: TV Movie, Action, Adventure, Fantasy
Director: David Flores
Main Cast: Adam Baldwin, Victor Webster, Morena Baccarin, George Kennedy, Azie Tesfai
Keywords: beautiful woman, ancient egypt, brunette woman, egyptian mythology, egyptology, egyptologist

Overview: The film tells the story of a prop from the 1923 movie The Ten Commandments that was actually an authentic artifact from antiquity with cursed powers. In the modern day these resurface leading to murder and mayhem.
