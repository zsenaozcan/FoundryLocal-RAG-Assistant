Title: The Last Deadly Mission (2007)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Olivier Marchal
Main Cast: Daniel Auteuil, Olivia Bonamy, Catherine Marchal, Francis Renaud, Gérald Laroche

Overview: A washed-up Marseilles cop (Auteuil) earns a chance at redemption by protecting a woman from the man who killed her parents as he is about to be released from prison.
