Title: Pride and Prejudice (1940)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Robert Z. Leonard
Main Cast: Laurence Olivier, Greer Garson, Edward Ashley, Marten Lamont, E.E. Clive
Keywords: jane austen

Overview: Mr. and Mrs. Bennet have five unmarried daughters, and Mrs. Bennet is especially eager to find suitable husbands for them. When the rich single gentlemen Mr. Bingley and Mr. Darcy come to live nearby, the Bennets have high hopes. But pride, prejudice, and misunderstandings all combine to complicate their relationships and to make happiness difficult.
