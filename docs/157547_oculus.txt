Title: Oculus (2013)
Genres: Horror
Director: Mike Flanagan
Main Cast: Karen Gillan, Brenton Thwaites, Katee Sackhoff, James Lafferty, Rory Cochrane
Keywords: hallucination, supernatural, mirror, skepticism, ghost

Overview: A woman tries to exonerate her brother's murder conviction by proving that the crime was committed by a supernatural phenomenon.
