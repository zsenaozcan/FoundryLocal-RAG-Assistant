Title: Baden Baden (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rachel Lang
Main Cast: Salomé Richard, Claude Gensac, Lazare Gousseau, Swann Arlaud, Olivier Chantreau
Keywords: realism, twenty something, strasbourg

Overview: Charts the trials and tribulations of Ana, a free-spirited 26 year-old returning home to Strasbourg for the summer after living abroad for long enough to feel out of place everywhere.
