Title: The Flame and the Arrow (1950)
Genres: Adventure, Romance
Director: Jacques Tourneur
Main Cast: Burt Lancaster, Virginia Mayo, Robert Douglas, Aline MacMahon, Frank Allenby
Keywords: rebel, rescue, niece, battle, lombardy

Overview: Dardo, a Robin Hood-like figure, and his loyal followers use a Roman ruin in Medieval Lombardy as their headquarters as they conduct an insurgency against their Hessian conquerors.
