Title: His Wife's Diary (2000)
Genres: Drama, History, Romance
Director: Aleksey Uchitel
Main Cast: Andrey Smirnov, Olga Budina, Evgeny Mironov, Galina Tyunina, Elena Morozova

Overview: It is a story about the last love affair of Ivan Bunin (played by Andrei Smirnov). It is set in the French Riviera in the 1940s.
