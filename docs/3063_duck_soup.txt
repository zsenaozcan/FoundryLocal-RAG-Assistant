Title: Duck Soup (1933)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Leo McCarey
Main Cast: Groucho Marx, Harpo Marx, Chico Marx, Zeppo Marx, Margaret Dumont
Keywords: dictator, spy, ambassador, siege, widow, slapstick, cigar smoking, lemonade, mirror, musical number

Overview: Rufus T. Firefly is named president/dictator of bankrupt Freedonia and declares war on neighboring Sylvania over the love of wealthy Mrs. Teasdale.
