Title: Spring (1969)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Arvo Kruusement
Main Cast: Arno Liiver, Riina Hein, Aare Laanemets, Margus Lepa, Ain Lutsepp
Keywords: based on novel, lifestyle, friendship, love, school, 19th century

Overview: Semi-autobiographical story by Oskar Luts about friendship, love and life in a small Estonian country boarding school in the late 1800s. Followed by Suvi (1976) and Sügis (1990).
