Title: I Will Follow You Into the Dark (2012)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Romance, Thriller
Director: Mark Edwin Robinson
Main Cast: Mischa Barton, Ryan Eggold, Leah Pipes, Frank Ashmore, Melinda Browne

Overview: A woman reeling from the death of her parents becomes attached to an alluring man whose sudden disappearance sends her and her friends into a haunted high-rise to find him.
