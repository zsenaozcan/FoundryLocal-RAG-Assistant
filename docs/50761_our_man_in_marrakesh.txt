Title: Our Man In Marrakesh (1966)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Mystery, Crime
Director: Don Sharp
Main Cast: Tony Randall, Senta Berger, Terry-Thomas, Herbert Lom, Wilfrid Hyde-White
Keywords: spy, bus, casablanca, marrakesh

Overview: One of six travelers who catch the bus from Casablanca airport to Marrakesh is carrying $2 million to pay a local operator to fix United Nations votes. But which one?
