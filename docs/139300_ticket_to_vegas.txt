Title: Ticket to Vegas (2013)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Gor Kirakosian
Main Cast: Ivan Stebunov, Ingrid Olerinskaya, Vladimir Yaglych, Nataliya Nozdrina, Mikhail Galustyan
Keywords: gambling, russian, friends, money, las vegas, moscow

Overview: Four Russian friends come from Moscow to Las Vegas to get 5 million dollars they've won in lottery. But everything goes wrong, when they discover that someone stole their winning ticket.
