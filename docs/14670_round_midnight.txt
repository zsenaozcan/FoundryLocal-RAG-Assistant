Title: ’Round Midnight (1986)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bertrand Tavernier
Main Cast: Dexter Gordon, François Cluzet, Gabrielle Haker, Christine Pascal, Pierre Trabaud
Keywords: jazz, musical, biography

Overview: Inside the Blue Note nightclub one night in 1959 Paris, an aged, ailing jazzman coaxes an eloquent wail from his tenor sax. Outside, a young Parisian too broke to buy a glass of wine strains to hear those notes. Soon they will form a friendship that sparks a final burst of genius in director Bertrand Tavernier
