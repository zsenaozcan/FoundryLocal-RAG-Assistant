Title: Snow Bride (2013)
Genres: Family, TV Movie
Director: Bert Kish
Main Cast: Katrina Law, Patricia Richardson, Susie Abromeit, Jordan Belfi, Robert Curtis Brown
Keywords: winter, lying, christmas, pretend relationship, tabloid reporter, mountain resort

Overview: When a reporter encounters the eldest son of a famous political family at a mountain retreat, she winds up pretending to be his girlfriend over Christmas so he can save face with his family. Should she secretly expose newsworthy scoops about the famous family in order to save her job, or trust that she's falling in love for real? Stars Patricia Richardson and Katrina Law.
