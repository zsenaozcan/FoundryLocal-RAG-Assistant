Title: Realive (2016)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Mateo Gil
Main Cast: Tom Hughes, Charlotte Le Bon, Oona Chaplin, Barry Ward, Julio Perillán
Keywords: suicide, depression, future, cryogenics, cancer, fear, medical experiment, death, pretty woman, hunks

Overview: Marc, a successful, ambitious man, is diagnosed with terminal cancer, and is given a few months to live.  Unable to accept death, he decides to cryogenically freeze himself. The love of his life is devastated. Seventy years later, Marc becomes the first cryogenic resuscitated person in history. But this doesn't happen in the idealized way he dreamt of.
