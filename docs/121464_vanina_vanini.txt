Title: Vanina Vanini (1961)
Genres: Drama
Director: Roberto Rossellini
Main Cast: Sandra Milo, Laurent Terzieff, Martine Carol, Paolo Stoppa, Isabelle Corey

Overview: Vanina Vanini, a bored, spoiled Roman countess, falls in love with a dedicated young patriot who is in Rome to assassinate a traitor to the brotherhood of the Free Masons.
