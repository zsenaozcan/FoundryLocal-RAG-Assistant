Title: Dead Daughters (2007)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: Pavel Ruminov
Main Cast: Yekaterina Shcheglova, Mikhail Dementyev, Nikita Emshanov, Darya Charusha, Ravshana Kurkova

Overview: In present-day Moscow ghosts of three little girls killed by their insane mother randomly pick a person to watch over for a three days. If during this "probation period" person's moral standards appear not to be high enough The Daughters then kill him using telekinesis.
