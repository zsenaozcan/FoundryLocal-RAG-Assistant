Title: The Wildest Dream (2010)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Anthony Geffen
Main Cast: Liam Neeson, Natasha Richardson, Conrad Anker, Ralph Fiennes, Leo Houlding
Keywords: nepal, himalaya, climbing, mountain climbing, mountaineering, mount everest

Overview: Uses astonishing visuals to tell the intersecting stories of George Mallory, the first man to attempt a summit of Mount Everest, and Conrad Anker, the mountaineer who finds Mallory's frozen remains 75 years later.
