Title: S.I.S. (2008)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: John Herzfeld
Main Cast: Keith David, Christina Cox, Peter Stebbings, Matthew Nable
Keywords: brother brother relationship, fall, drug trade

Overview: S.I.S., or Special Investigation Squad, is an elite branch of the LAPD. A new psychopath terrorizing Los Angeles proves to be their hardest case yet.
