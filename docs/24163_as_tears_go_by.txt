Title: As Tears Go By (1988)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime, Romance
Director: Wong Kar-wai
Main Cast: Andy Lau, Maggie Cheung, Jacky Cheung, Alex Man Chi-Leung, Ronald Wong Ban

Overview: Two parallel stories of Wah caught in the mist of a love affair with his beloved cousin, Ngor and his relationship with his triad brother, Fly, who seems to never fall out of trouble.
