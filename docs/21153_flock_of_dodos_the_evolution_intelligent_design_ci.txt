Title: Flock of Dodos: The Evolution-Intelligent Design Circus (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Randy Olson
Main Cast: Michael Behe, John Calvert, Jack Cashill, Tom Givnish
Keywords: independent film, religion, evolution

Overview: Filmmaker and evolutionary biologist Randy Olson tries to figure out if it is the Darwinists or Intelligent Design supporters who will become a flock of dodos.
