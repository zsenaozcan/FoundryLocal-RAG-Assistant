Title: Mister Lonely (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Harmony Korine
Main Cast: Diego Luna, Samantha Morton, Denis Lavant, James Fox, Werner Herzog
Keywords: paris, commune, michael jackson

Overview: In Paris, a young American who works as a Michael Jackson lookalike meets Marilyn Monroe, who invites him to her commune in Scotland, where she lives with Charlie Chaplin and her daughter, Shirley Temple.
