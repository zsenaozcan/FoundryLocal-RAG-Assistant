Title: A Further Gesture (1997)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Romance
Director: Robert Dornhelm
Main Cast: Stephen Rea, Brendan Gleeson, Alfred Molina, Rosana Pastor, Pruitt Taylor Vince

Overview: Dowd, an IRA prisoner in the H-blocks, is gloomily facing his sentence, until he joins a comrade in a risky escape. Dowd begins a new life in New York, but he might as well be in prison again - until he strikes up a friendship with co-worker Tulio and gets to know his close group of Guatemalan exiles.
