Title: Four Shades of Brown (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Tomas Alfredson
Main Cast: Robert Gustafsson, Maria Kulle, Jonas Inde, Henrik Schyffert, Johan Rheborg
Keywords: suicide attempt, crematorium, group therapy, funeral, unemployment

Overview: Four stories, all related to fatherhood, are presented: A father tries to stimulate his son by bringing him to his workplace with disastrous consequences. Richard Brunn and his wife, who run a design hotel, suffer a strained relationship as his parents visit. An extended family is gathered to find out who will inherit the wealth of a famous jockey. A cooking class develops into a therapy session.
