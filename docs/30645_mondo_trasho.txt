Title: Mondo Trasho (1969)
Genres: Comedy
Director: John Waters
Main Cast: Mary Vivian Pearce, Divine, David Lochary, Mink Stole, Bob Skidmore
Keywords: drag, mondo, foot fetish

Overview: A day in the lives of a hit-and-run driver and her victim, and the bizarre things that happen to them before and after they collide (sexual assault by a crazed foot-fetishist, visions of the Virgin Mary, strange chicken-foot grafting operations).
