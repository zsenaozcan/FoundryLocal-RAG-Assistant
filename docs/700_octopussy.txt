Title: Octopussy (1983)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Thriller
Director: John Glen
Main Cast: Roger Moore, Maud Adams, Louis Jourdan, Kristina Wayborn, Kabir Bedi
Keywords: atomic bomb, eastern block, crocodile, secret intelligence service, kgb, snake charmer, hot air balloon, east berlin, british secret service

Overview: James Bond is sent to investigate after a fellow “00” agent is found dead with a priceless Farberge egg. James bond follows the mystery and uncovers a smuggling scandal and a Russian General who wants to provoke a new World War.
