Title: Teenage Dirtbag (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Regina Crosby
Main Cast: Scott Michael Foster, Noa Hegesh, Chris Ellis, Bethany D. Hart, Regina Crosby
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A popular high school girl is harassed by a delinquent boy until they are placed in creative writing class together. Through written words, they create a bond, but tragically a bond that cannot withstand her social pressures or his brutal home life.
