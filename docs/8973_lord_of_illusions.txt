Title: Lord of Illusions (1995)
Genres: Mystery, Horror, Thriller
Director: Clive Barker
Main Cast: Scott Bakula, Kevin J. O'Connor, Famke Janssen, Joel Swetow, Daniel von Bargen
Keywords: fanatic, supernatural, wizardry, resurrection, sect, occultism

Overview: During a routine case in L.A., NY private investigator Harry D'Amour stumbles over members of a fanatic cult who are preparing for the resurrection of their leader Nix, a powerful magician who was killed 13 years earlier.
