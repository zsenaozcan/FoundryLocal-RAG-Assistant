Title: Virtual Nightmare (2000)
Genres: Science Fiction, TV Movie
Director: Michael Pattinson
Main Cast: Michael Muhney, Tasma Walton, John Noble, Mitchell Butel, Paul Gleeson

Overview: Advertising executive Dale Hunter (Michael Muheny) works at the agency Arora. But after a car accident, his life changed abruptly. He has nightmares at night and by day he suffers from hallucinations. The world looks like from a computer. Numbers and names adhere to the objects. A doctor can not help him and the hallucinations are getting worse. Librarian Wendy seems to be the only person he can trust. Together they try to find out the truth.
