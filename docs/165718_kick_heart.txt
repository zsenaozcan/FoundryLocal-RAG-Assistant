Title: Kick-heart (2013)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Romance
Director: Masaaki Yuasa
Main Cast: Kokoro Kikuchi, Kyouko Chikiri, Makiko Nitta, Mitsuaki Kanuka, Miyuki Satou

Overview: A love story between two people that each have a secret to hide. One a pro-wrestler, the other a Nun. Losing never felt so good.
