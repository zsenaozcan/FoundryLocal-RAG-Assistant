Title: Run (1991)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Drama
Director: Geoff Burrowes
Main Cast: Patrick Dempsey, Kelly Preston, Ken Pogue, Alan C. Peterson, James Kidnie
Keywords: man on the run

Overview: While conveying a Porsche from L.A. to Atlantic City, student Charlie has to make a stop due to engine trouble. Mistaken as rich, he's invited to a big poker game -- and wins big time. However Denny Halloran, son of a mighty mobster, is a bad loser and starts a fight. But he's also a bad fighter: he falls on his head and is dead. Now Charlie's in trouble; after he flees Halloran's father puts a reward on his head. Even the police won't help him against the mighty man... so Charlie has to run for his life.
