Title: Erotikon (1929)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Gustav Machatý
Main Cast: Karel Schleichert, Ita Rina, Olaf Fjord, Theodor Pištěk, Charlotte Susa
Keywords: seduction, stormy night

Overview: Andrea, a beautiful daughter of a railway gateman, is seduced by a rich passenger, who missed his train. Left pregnant, Andrea marries another man and moves to Prague, only to encounter her former lover and recover her former lust.
