Title: Cemetery Man (1994)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Horror, Comedy
Director: Michele Soavi
Main Cast: Rupert Everett, François Hadji-Lazaro, Anna Falchi, Fabiana Formica, Mickey Knox
Keywords: insanity, undead, grim reaper, zombie, macabre, grotesque

Overview: A cemetery man has the unusual problem of the dead rising from the grave. Himself and his assistant must end these creatures' lives again after they are reborn. Everything is going well until "She" comes along and stirs things up a bit.
