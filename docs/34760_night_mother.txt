Title: 'night, Mother (1986)
Genres: Drama
Director: Tom Moore
Main Cast: Sissy Spacek, Anne Bancroft, Ed Berke, Carol Robbins, Jennifer Roosendahl
Keywords: suicide, epilepsy, pulitzer prize, based on play or musical, mother daughter relationship, suicide by gunshot

Overview: It happened in 1983. It was a rare and remarkable theatrical experience. Controversial. Provocative. And shocking. Now, two Academy Award-winning actresses make the Pulitzer Prize-winning play the motion picture event of the year. What would you do if someone you loved sat down with you one night and calmly told you that they were going to end their life before morning?
