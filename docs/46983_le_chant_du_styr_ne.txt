Title: Le chant du Styrène (1959)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Alain Resnais
Main Cast: Pierre Dux, Sacha Vierny
Keywords: short

Overview: Le chant du Styrène is a 1958 French documentary film directed by Alain Resnais. The film was an order by French industrial group Pechiney to highlight the merits of plastics.
