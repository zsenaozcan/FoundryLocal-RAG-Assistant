Title: The Banquet (2006)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Action, History
Director: Feng Xiaogang
Main Cast: Zhang Ziyi, Ge You, Daniel Wu, Zhou Xun, Ma Jing-Wu

Overview: 907 AD, the Tang Dynasty in China. The movie is set in an empire in chaos. The Emperor, the Empress, the Crown Prince, the Minister and the General all have their own enemies.
