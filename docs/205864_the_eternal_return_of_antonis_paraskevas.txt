Title: The Eternal Return of Antonis Paraskevas (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Elina Psikou
Main Cast: Christos Stergioglou, Maria Kallimani, Yorgos Souxes, Theodora Tzimou, Syllas Tzoumerkas
Keywords: hotel, kidnapping, tv reporter, financial crisis, woman director

Overview: Antonis arrives at a hotel resort by the sea. It is wintertime, the hotel is closed and Antonis drifts around alone. He has a lot of time to kill. Until television announces the disappearance of the famous TV host Antonis Paraskevas…
