Title: Welcome to Me (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Shira Piven
Main Cast: Kristen Wiig, James Marsden, Linda Cardellini, Wes Bentley, Jennifer Jason Leigh
Keywords: talk show, dark comedy, woman director, mental disorders, winning lottery

Overview: A year in the life of Alice Klieg, a woman with Borderline Personality Disorder who wins Mega-millions, quits her meds and buys her own talk show.
