Title: Politexnitis and Erimospitis (1963)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Alekos Sakellarios
Main Cast: Thanasis Vengos, Marika Krevata, Giorgos Gavriilidis, Niki Linardou, Giannis Vogiatzis
Keywords: photographer, pharmacist, police officer, job hunting, referee

Overview: Thanasis Vengos plays a poor young man that comes to Athens looking for a job. We watch as he tries many different jobs; druggist, photographer, even wrestling referee. The result is always hilarious and explosive!
