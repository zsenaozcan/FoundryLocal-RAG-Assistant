Title: The Chosen One (2010)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Rob Schneider
Main Cast: Rob Schneider, Steve Buscemi, Holland Taylor, Peter Riegert, Brandon Molale
Keywords: chosen one

Overview: Paul is an ordinary man who is at the end of his rope. He hates his job, his beautiful wife has left him, and his mother and gay, Buddhist-monk brother constantly remind him of his shortcomings. Although Paul doesn't know it yet, his life is about to change in a big way.
