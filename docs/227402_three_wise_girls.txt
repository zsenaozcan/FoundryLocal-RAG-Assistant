Title: Three Wise Girls (1932)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Drama
Director: William Beaudine
Main Cast: Jean Harlow, Mae Clarke, Marie Prevost, Walter Byron, Andy Devine
Keywords: infidelity, wealth, model

Overview: Romantic comedy drama about three friends in New York. Cassie has come to New York and goes to work as a model where her friend Gladys works. She falls in love with wealthy young Jerry who is already married. Gladys has the same probelm with her man Phelps.
