Title: What a Way to Go! (1964)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: J. Lee Thompson
Main Cast: Shirley MacLaine, Paul Newman, Robert Mitchum, Dean Martin, Gene Kelly
Keywords: dark comedy

Overview: This black comedy opens with Louisa Foster donating a multimillion dollar check to the IRS. The tax department thinks she's crazy and sends her to a psychiatrist. She then discusses her four marriages, in which all of her husbands became incredibly rich and died prematurely because of their drive to be rich.
