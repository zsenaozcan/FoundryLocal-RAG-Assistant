Title: Black Angel (1946)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Roy William Neill
Main Cast: Dan Duryea, June Vincent, Peter Lorre, Broderick Crawford, Constance Dowling
Keywords: falsely accused, film noir

Overview: A falsely convicted man's wife, Catherine (Vincent), and an alcoholic composer and pianist, Martin (Duryea) team up in an attempt to clear her husband of the murder of a blonde singer, who is Martin's wife. Their investigation leads them to confrontations with a determined policeman (Crawford) and a shifty nightclub owner (Lorre), whom Catherine and Martin suspect may be the real killer.
