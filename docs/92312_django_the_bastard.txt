Title: Django the Bastard (1969)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Western
Director: Sergio Garrone
Main Cast: Anthony Steffen, Paolo Gozlino, Luciano Rossi, Teodoro Corrà, Jean Louis
Keywords: revenge, spaghetti western, django, ghost

Overview: A Union soldier returns from the dead to take revenge on three officers who betrayed his unit in battle.
