Title: Backwoods (2008)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, TV Movie
Director: Marty Weiss
Main Cast: Ryan Merriman, Haylie Duff, Mark Rolston, Danny Nucci, Troy Winbush
Keywords: suicide, fbi, camping, redneck, torture, paintball , bound and gagged, attempted rape, bow and arrow, religious cult

Overview: While camping in Jasper Park up North California, the couple Tom Alan Smithee and Gwen Marie Holden are attacked by a group of strangers; Tom is immediately killed and Gwen is abducted by the men to an underground facility to breed.
