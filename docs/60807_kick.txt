Title: Kick (2009)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Thriller, Romance, Foreign
Director: Surender Reddy
Main Cast: Ravi Teja, Ileana D'Cruz, Brahmanandam, Shaam, Sayaji Shinde

Overview: Kalyan (Ravi Teja) is a kind of guy who prefers a life full of thrills than leading a regular life style. He seeks kick in whatever he does. He finds liking in Naina (Ileana). They separate due to differences. Krishna (Shyam) is a young cop who is in lookout for a big ticket thief who happens to be Kalyan. Meanwhile, Naina agrees for an engagement with Krishna. The rest of the story is all about how Kalyan wins back his lady love and the real intent behind the robberies of Kalyan.
