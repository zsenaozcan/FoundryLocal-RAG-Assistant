Title: Suzhou River (2000)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Romance
Director: Lou Ye
Main Cast: Zhou Xun, Jia Hongsheng, Zhongkai Hua, An Nai, Anlian Yao
Keywords: city portrait, river, shanghai, courier, film noir

Overview: A tragic love story set in contemporary Shanghai. The film stars Zhou Xun in a dual role as two different women and Jia Hongsheng as a man obsessed with finding a woman from his past.
