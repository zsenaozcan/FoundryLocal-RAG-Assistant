Title: Why Worry? (1923)
Genres: Comedy, Adventure
Director: Fred C. Newmeyer
Main Cast: Harold Lloyd, Jobyna Ralston, John Aasen, Wallace Howe, Jim Mason

Overview: Harold Van Pelham (Lloyd) is a hypochondriac, rich businessman who sails to the tropics for his 'health.' Instead of the peace and seclusion he is seeking, he finds himself in the middle of a revolution. He is imprisoned where he befriends the friendly giant, Colosso (Aasen), and they engineer an escape. Together, they quell the revolution.
