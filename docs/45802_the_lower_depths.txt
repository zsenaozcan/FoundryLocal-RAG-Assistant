Title: The Lower Depths (1936)
Genres: Romance, Crime, Drama
Director: Jean Renoir
Main Cast: Jean Gabin, Suzy Prim, Louis Jouvet, Jany Holt, Junie Astor
Keywords: independent film

Overview: The film treats the imprisoning hold of poverty; the disheartening odds of people rising from such social despair, and the ease in which those in the upper spheres of Society may descend.
