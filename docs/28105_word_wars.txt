Title: Word Wars (2004)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Eric Chaikin
Main Cast: Mike Birbiglia, Joe Edley, Matt Graham, Marlon Hill, Joel Sherman
Keywords: competition, scrabble, documentary, board game

Overview: The classic board game, Scrabble, has been popular for decades. In addition, there are fanatics who devote heart and soul to this game to the expense of everything else. This film profiles a group of these enthusiasts as they converge for a Scrabble convention where the word game is almost a bloodsport.
