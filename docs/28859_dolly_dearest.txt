Title: Dolly Dearest (1991)
Genres: Horror
Director: Maria Lease
Main Cast: Denise Crosby, Sam Bottoms, Rip Torn, Lupe Ontiveros, Chris Demetral
Keywords: killer doll

Overview: An American family moves to Mexico to fabricate dolls, but their toy factory happens to be next to a Sanzian grave and the toys come into possession of an old, malicious spirit.
