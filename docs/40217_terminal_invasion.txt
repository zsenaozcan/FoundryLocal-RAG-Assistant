Title: Terminal Invasion (2002)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Sean S. Cunningham
Main Cast: Bruce Campbell, Chase Masterson, Jason Jones, C. David Johnson, Kedar Brown
Keywords: airport, prisoner, stranded, alien, alien invasion, criminal, evil child, terminal

Overview: Aliens in human disguise commandeer a rural airport during a snowstorm. To survive, the people trapped inside must determine which of their own is not of this Earth.
