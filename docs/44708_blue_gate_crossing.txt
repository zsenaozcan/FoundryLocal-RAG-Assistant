Title: Blue Gate Crossing (2002)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Yee Chin-Yen
Main Cast: Chen Bolin, Gwei Lun-mei, Yolin Liang, Chau Ching, Ming Gin-Cheng
Keywords: love triangle, high school, unrequited love, gay lead character

Overview: An is-she-or-isn't-she gay comedy focused on a Taiwanese teen, the boy she might like, and the girl she may love.
