Title: Land of Death (2004)
Genres: Horror, Action, Thriller, Foreign
Director: Bruno Mattei
Main Cast: Claudio Morales, Lou Randall, Cindy Jelic Matic, Ydalia Suarez, Silvio Jimenez

Overview: Commandos head deep into the Amazon jungle to rescue a general's daughter who has been kidnapped by a cannibal tribe.
