Title: Lazer Team (2016)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Matt Hullum
Main Cast: Alan Ritchson, Gavin Free, Michael Jones, Burnie Burns, Colton Dunn
Keywords: rooster teeth

Overview: In the late 1970's, the SETI project received a one time signal from outer space. It looked exactly as theorists thought a communication from an alien civilization would -- unfortunately it has never been decoded. Or so we were told. Unbeknownst to the general public the signal was translated and told us two things:  1) We are not alone.  2) The galaxy is a dangerous place.
