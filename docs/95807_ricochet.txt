Title: Ricochet (2011)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Drama, Crime
Director: Nick Gomez
Main Cast: John Corbett, Julie Benz, Kelly Overton, Haaz Sleiman, Kadeem Hardison
Keywords: based on novel

Overview: Two homicide detectives find their careers - and lives - on the line when they get caught up in a case of murder and betrayal in high-society Savannah.
