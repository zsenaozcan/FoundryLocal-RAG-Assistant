Title: The Secret Life of Zoey (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: Robert Mandel
Main Cast: Mia Farrow, Julia Whelan, Cliff DeYoung, Andrew McCarthy, Katharine Isabelle

Overview: Marcia always thought her overachieving daughter Zoey was the dream child. But then she discovers that her perfect teen has a not-so-perfect drug addiction. Marcia and her ex-husband go through plenty of denial as their daughter spirals out of control. It's a true-to-life tale that parents and their kids should watch together.
