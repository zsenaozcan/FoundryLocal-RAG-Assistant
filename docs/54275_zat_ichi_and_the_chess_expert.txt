Title: Zatôichi and the Chess Expert (1965)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: Kenji Misumi
Main Cast: Shintarô Katsu, Mikio Narita, Chizu Hayashi, Kaneko Iwasaki, Gaku Yamamoto

Overview: Zatoichi makes friends with a dangerous chess player, while fending off angry yakuza and bloodthirsty relatives out for revenge, and trying to save a sick child. Meanwhile, his luck with dice is turning.
