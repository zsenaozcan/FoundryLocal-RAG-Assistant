Title: Mister Universo (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Tizza Covi
Main Cast: Tairo Caroli, Wendy Weber, Arthur Robin, Lilly Robin
Keywords: circus, woman director

Overview: Tairo, a young lion-tamer, is unhappy. Losing his lucky charm gives him a reason to set off for a journey across Italy in search of Arthur Robin, a former Mister Universe, who gave him the charm a long time ago.
