Title: And So It Goes (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Rob Reiner
Main Cast: Michael Douglas, Diane Keaton, Sterling Jerins, Paloma Guzmán, Frances Sternhagen
Keywords: grandfather granddaughter relationship, neighbor, wealthy, elderly, estrangement

Overview: A self-centered realtor enlists the help of his neighbor when he's suddenly left in charge of the granddaughter he never knew existed until his estranged son drops her off at his home.
