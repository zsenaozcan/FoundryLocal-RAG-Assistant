Title: The Philly Kid (2012)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Jason Connery
Main Cast: Devon Sawa, Wes Chatham, Sarah Butler, Neal McDonough, Lucky Johnson
Keywords: fight, sport, mixed martial arts, kickboxing

Overview: A former NCAA champion wrestler is paroled after 10 years in prison. Now, to save a friend's life, in a series of cage fights he must agree to do the impossible - lose.
