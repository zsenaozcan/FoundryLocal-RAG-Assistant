Title: Rabbit Without Ears (2007)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Til Schweiger
Main Cast: Til Schweiger, Jürgen Vogel, Nora Tschirner, Matthias Schweighöfer, Armin Rohde
Keywords: love of one's life, ladykiller, one-night stand, kindergarten

Overview: Rainbow press reporter Ludo is sentenced to 8 months, but is released on probation. But he has to work 300 hours for a local daycare center and meets Anna who has unfinished business with him.
