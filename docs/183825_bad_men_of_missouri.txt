Title: Bad Men of Missouri (1941)
Genres: Western
Director: Ray Enright
Main Cast: Dennis Morgan, Jane Wyman, Wayne Morris, Arthur Kennedy, Victor Jory
Keywords: missouri, jesse james, cole younger

Overview: The Younger brothers return to Missouri after the Civil War with intent to avenge the misdeeds of William Merrick, a crooked banker who has been buying up warrants on back-taxes and dispossessing the farmers.
