Title: Flyboys (2006)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, History, Romance, War
Director: Tony Bill
Main Cast: James Franco, David Ellison, Jean Reno, Philip Winchester, Abdul Salis
Keywords: world war i, biplane

Overview: The adventures of the Lafayette Escadrille, young Americans who volunteered for the French military before the U.S. entered World War I, and became the country's first fighter pilots.
