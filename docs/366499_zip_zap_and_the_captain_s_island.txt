Title: Zip & Zap and the Captain's Island (2016)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Oskar Santos
Main Cast: Toni Gómez, Teo Planell, Elena Anaya, Goizalde Núñez, Fermí Reixach

Overview: Naughty brothers Zip and Zap finds themselves in a home for lost children that might just be haunted. Will they ever escape?
