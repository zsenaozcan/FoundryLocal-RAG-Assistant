Title: Three Comrades (1938)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Frank Borzage
Main Cast: Robert Taylor, Margaret Sullavan, Franchot Tone, Robert Young, Guy Kibbee
Keywords: german, world war i, war

Overview: A love story centered on the lives of three young German soldiers in the years following World War I. Their close friendship is strengthened by their shared love for the same woman who is dying of tuberculosis.
