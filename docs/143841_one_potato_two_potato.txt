Title: One Potato, Two Potato (1964)
Genres: Drama
Director: Larry Peerce
Main Cast: Barbara Barrie, Bernie Hamilton, Richard Mulligan, Harry Bellaver, Marti Mericka
Keywords: marriage, prejudice, racism, miscegenation, child custody, mixed race marriage

Overview: Study of interracial marriage in the 1960's. A white divorcée falls in love with and marries an African-American man. When her ex-husband sues for custody of her child, arguing that a mixed household is an improper place to raise the girl, the new husband fights for his parental rights in court, fighting against a judge who represents the prejudices of the era.
