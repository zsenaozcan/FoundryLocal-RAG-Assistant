Title: The House at the End of Time (2013)
Genres: Horror
Director: Alejandro Hidalgo
Main Cast: Ruddy Rodriguez, Gonzalo Cubero, Guillermo García, Adriana Calzadilla, Rosmel Bustamante
Keywords: suspense

Overview: The House at the End of Time is the first Venezuelan thriller suspense and terror. It tells the story of Dulce (Ruddy Rodriguez) a mother who has encounters with apparitions inside her old house, where she must decipher a mystery that could trigger a prophecy: the death of her family.
