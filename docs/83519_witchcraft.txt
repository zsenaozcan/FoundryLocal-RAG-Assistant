Title: Witchcraft (1964)
Genres: Horror
Director: Don Sharp
Main Cast: Lon Chaney Jr., Jack Hedley, Jill Dixon, Viola Keats, Marie Ney
Keywords: witch, cult, curse, witchcraft, graveyard

Overview: When her grave is disturbed by modern-day land developers, a 300-year-old witch is accidentally resurrected and terrorizes an English village.
