Title: Rage (1972)
Genres: Drama
Director: George C. Scott
Main Cast: George C. Scott, Richard Basehart, Martin Sheen, Barnard Hughes, Nicolas Beauvy
Keywords: revenge, government cover-up, government conspiracy, nerve gas

Overview: An accidental nerve gas leak by the military kills not only a rancher's livestock, but also his son. When he tries to hold the military accountable for their actions, he runs up against a wall of silence.
