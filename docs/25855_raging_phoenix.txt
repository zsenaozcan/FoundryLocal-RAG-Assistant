Title: Raging Phoenix (2009)
Genres: Thriller, Action
Director: Rashane Limtrakul
Main Cast: JeeJa Yanin, Marc Hoang, David Bueno, Kazu Patrick Tang, Nui Saendaeng
Keywords: martial arts

Overview: A violent gang is abducting and killing women around Thailand. Sanim and his friends, having had loved ones abducted, have joined together to break the gang of kidnappers. In a botched kidnap attempt, Deu is saved by Sanim's crew. After learning their unique martial arts style, Deu helps lure the gang into an epic battle to save the women across Thailand.
