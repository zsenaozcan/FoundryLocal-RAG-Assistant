Title: No Highway in the Sky (1951)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Henry Koster
Main Cast: James Stewart, Glynis Johns, Marlene Dietrich, Janette Scott, Jack Hawkins
Keywords: stewardess, suspense, movie actress, aeronautical engineer, aerospace

Overview: James Stewart plays aeronautical engineer Theodore Honey, the quintessential absent-minded professor: eccentric, forgetful, but brilliant. His studies show that the aircraft being manufactured by his employer has a subtle but deadly design flaw that manifests itself only after the aircraft has flown a certain number of hours. En route to a crash site to prove his theory, Honey discovers that he is aboard a plane rapidly approaching his predicted deadline.
