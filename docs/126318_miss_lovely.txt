Title: Miss Lovely (2012)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Ashim Ahluwalia
Main Cast: Anil George, Nawazuddin Siddiqui, Niharika Singh, Zeena Bhatia, Menka Lalwani

Overview: A meeting with a struggling actress (Niharika Singh) gives a mild-mannered filmmaker (Nawazuddin Siddiqui) the idea to break away from his controlling brother (Anil George) and direct a feature of his own.
