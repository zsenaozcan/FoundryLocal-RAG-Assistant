Title: Dressed to Kill (1946)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Roy William Neill
Main Cast: Basil Rathbone, Nigel Bruce, Patricia Morison, Edmund Breon, Frederick Worlock
Keywords: sherlock holmes

Overview: A convicted thief in Dartmoor prison hides the location of the stolen Bank of England printing plates inside three music boxes. When the innocent purchasers of the boxes start to be murdered, Holmes and Watson investigate.
