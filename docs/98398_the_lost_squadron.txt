Title: The Lost Squadron (1932)
Genres: War, Drama, Action, History
Director: George Archainbaud
Main Cast: Richard Dix, Mary Astor, Robert Armstrong, Dorothy Jordan, Joel McCrea
Keywords: pilot, hollywood, stunt pilot

Overview: When WW1 comes to an end, three pilots find themselves on hard times but they wind up in Hollywood where they work as stunt fliers for a sadistic director.
