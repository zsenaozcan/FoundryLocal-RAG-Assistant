Title: Killer Toon (2013)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Kim Yong-gyun
Main Cast: Lee Si-young, Uhm Ki-Joon, Hyun Woo, Kwon Hae-hyo, Kim Do-Young

Overview: A series of murders resembling Ji-yoon’s horror webcomic drawings occur and detective Ki-chul unravels the terrifying truth of the comics himself.
