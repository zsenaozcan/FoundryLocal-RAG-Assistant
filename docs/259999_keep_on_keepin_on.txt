Title: Keep On Keepin’ On (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Alan Hicks
Main Cast: Clark Terry, Justin Kauflin

Overview: Eighty-nine year old trumpeting legend Clark Terry has mentored jazz wonders like Miles Davis and Quincy Jones, but Terry’s most unlikely friendship is with Justin Kauflin, a 23-year-old blind piano player with uncanny talent, but debilitating nerves. As Justin prepares for the most pivotal moment in his budding career, Terry’s ailing health threatens to end his own.
