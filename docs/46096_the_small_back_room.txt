Title: The Small Back Room (1949)
Genres: War, Drama, Thriller
Director: Emeric Pressburger
Main Cast: David Farrar, Kathleen Byron, Jack Hawkins, Leslie Banks, Michael Gough
Keywords: bomb, england, world war ii, suspense

Overview: At the height of World War II, the Germans begin dropping a new type of booby-trapped bomb on England. Sammy Rice, a highly-skilled but haunted bomb-disposal officer, must overcome his personal demons to defeat this new threat.
