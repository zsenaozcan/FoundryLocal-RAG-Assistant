Title: Crayon Shin-chan: Pursuit of the Balls of Darkness (1997)
Genres: Animation, Comedy
Director: Keiichi Hara
Main Cast: Miki Narahashi, Keiji Fujiwara, Akiko Yajima

Overview: The Nohara family gets involved in a dispute between two clans who are fighting to possess a sphere claimed to be able to bring back to life a powerful evil spirit.
