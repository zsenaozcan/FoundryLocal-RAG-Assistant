Title: The Karate Kid, Part III (1989)
Genres: Family, Adventure
Director: John G. Avildsen
Main Cast: Ralph Macchio, Pat Morita, Martin Kove, Thomas Ian Griffith, Robyn Lively
Keywords: japan, japanese, confidence, karate, business man, adversary, fighter, duel, good and bad
