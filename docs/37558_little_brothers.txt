Title: Little Brothers (1999)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Jacques Doillon
Main Cast: Stéphanie Touly, Iliès Sefraoui, Mustapha Goumane, Nassim Izem, Rachid Mansouri

Overview: Talia runs away from home taking her beloved dog with her. When her dog is stolen Talia enlists the help of a gang of boys to help her find her pet.
