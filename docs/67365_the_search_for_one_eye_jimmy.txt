Title: The Search for One-eye Jimmy (1994)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sam Henry Kass
Main Cast: Holt McCallany, Michael Badalucco, Nicholas Turturro, Ray Mancini, Steve Buscemi

Overview: While working on a documentary on his old neighborhood, a young film school graduate shifts the focus of his production onto the disappearance of a local resident and the strange characters who are conducting the search to find him.
