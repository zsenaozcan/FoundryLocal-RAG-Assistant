Title: Were The World Mine (2008)
Genres: Music, Romance
Director: Tom Gustafson
Main Cast: Tanner Cohen, Judy McLane, Zelda Williams, Nathan Geist, Wendy Robie
Keywords: gay, shakespeare, homophobia, musical, high school, independent film, gay relationship, lgbt child, lgbt

Overview: If you had a love-potion, who would you make fall madly in love with you? Timothy, prone to escaping his dismal high school reality through dazzling musical daydreams, gets to answer that question in a very real way. After his eccentric teacher casts him as Puck in A Midsummer Night's Dream, he stumbles upon a recipe hidden within the script to create the play's magical, purple love-pansy.
