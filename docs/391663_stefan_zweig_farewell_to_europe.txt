Title: Stefan Zweig: Farewell to Europe (2016)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Maria Schrader
Main Cast: Josef Hader, Barbara Sukowa, Aenne Schwarz, Matthias Brandt, Charly Hübner
Keywords: literature, biography, writer, third reich, woman director

Overview: Before Dawn charts the years of exile in the life of famous Jewish Austrian writer Stefan Zweig, his inner struggle for the "right attitude" towards the events in war torn Europe and his search for a new home.
