Title: Outlaw: Heartless (1968)
Genres: Foreign, Crime, Action, Thriller
Director: Mio Ezaki
Main Cast: Tetsuya Watari, Chieko Matsubara, Kôji Wada, Hiroshi Nawa, Isao Tamagawa

Overview: Goro Fujikawa (Tetsuya Watari) was indebted to Mitsugimoto. Sawada, a low rank yakuza with a gambling problem, owed Mitsugimoto three million yen. This equation can only lead to one answer. Mitsugimoto needs to pay and Goro's coming to collect.
