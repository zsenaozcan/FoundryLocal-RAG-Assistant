Title: The Sea (2002)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Baltasar Kormákur
Main Cast: Gunnar Eyjólfsson, Hilmir Snær Guðnason, Hélène de Fougerolles, Kristbjörg Kjeld, Sven Nordin
Keywords: sexual abuse, fisherman, dysfunctional family

Overview: Wealthy, aging patriarch Thordur assembles his scattered heirs in his remote Icelandic fishing village to discuss the future of the family fishery. But bringing everyone together unleashes a storm of long-repressed dark family secrets.
