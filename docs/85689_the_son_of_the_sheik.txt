Title: The Son of the Sheik (1926)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action, Romance
Director: George Fitzmaurice
Main Cast: Rudolph Valentino, Vilma Banky, George Fawcett, Montagu Love, Agnes Ayres
Keywords: ransom, sheik, love, revenge

Overview: Ahmed, son of Diana and Sheik Ahmed Ben Hassan, falls in love with Yasmin, a dancing girl who fronts her father's gang of mountebanks. She and Ahmed meet secretly until one night when her father and the gang capture the son of the sheik, torture him, and hold him for ransom.
