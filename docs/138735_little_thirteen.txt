Title: Little Thirteen (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Christian Klandt
Main Cast: Muriel Wimmer, Antonia Putiloff, Isabell Gerschke, Joseph Bundschuh, Philipp Kubitza
Keywords: sexuality, love, teenager, pregnancy

Overview: The everyday lives of teenagers, coming from various social backgrounds. For them, sexuality has become a substitute for love, resulting from emotional neglect.
