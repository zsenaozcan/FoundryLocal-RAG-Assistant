Title: The Stalking Moon (1968)
Genres: Western, Action
Director: Robert Mulligan
Main Cast: Gregory Peck, Eva Marie Saint, Robert Forster, Charles Tyner, Richard Bull
Keywords: based on novel, new mexico

Overview: When an army scout retires to a farm in New Mexico he takes pity on a white woman and her half-breed son recently rescued from indians, and invites them to join him. He does this even knowing the child's father is a feared and murderous Apache and that sooner or later a showdown is almost inevitable.`
