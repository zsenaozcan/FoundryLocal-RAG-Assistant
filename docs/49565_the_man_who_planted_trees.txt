Title: The Man Who Planted Trees (1987)
Genres: Animation, Drama
Director: Frédéric Back
Main Cast: Philippe Noiret, Christopher Plummer
Keywords: france, shepherd, world war ii, world war i, village, forest, widower, deforestation, reforestation, 1940s

Overview: This animated short film tells the story of a shepherd who repairs the ruined ecosystem of a secluded valley by singlehandedly cultivating a forest over a thirty year period.
