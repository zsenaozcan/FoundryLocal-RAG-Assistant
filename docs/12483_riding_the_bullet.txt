Title: Riding the Bullet (2004)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Mick Garris
Main Cast: Jonathan Jackson, David Arquette, Cliff Robertson, Barbara Hershey, Erika Christensen
Keywords: suicide, ex-girlfriend, halloween, apoplectic stroke

Overview: Set in 1969, Alan Parker (Jackson) is a young artist, studying at the University of Maine. He becomes obsessed with death, and believing he is losing his girlfriend, Jessica (Christensen), he tries to commit suicide on his birthday but his friends manage to stop him, and he recovers. He receives news that his mother is dying and decides to hitchhike, in an attempt to reach his dying mom.
