Title: The Deadly Companions (1961)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Western
Director: Sam Peckinpah
Main Cast: Maureen O'Hara, Brian Keith, Steve Cochran, Chill Wills, Strother Martin
Keywords: funeral, native american, escort

Overview: Ex-army officer accidentally kills a woman's son, tries to make up for it by escorting the funeral procession through dangerous Indian territory.
