Title: Don't Let Me Drown (2009)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Cruz Angeles
Main Cast: E.J. Bonilla, Gleendilys Inoa, Damián Alcázar, Raúl Castillo
Keywords: independent film

Overview: In a post-September 11th world overflowing with fear and hate, two Latino teens discover that sometimes the only thing that can keep them from drowning is love.
