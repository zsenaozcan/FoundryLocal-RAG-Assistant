Title: The Lair of the White Worm (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Ken Russell
Main Cast: Amanda Donohoe, Hugh Grant, Catherine Oxenberg, Peter Capaldi, Sammi Davis
Keywords: temple, vampire, dangerous, worm, derbyshire, archaeologist, monastery

Overview: In a remote corner of England's Peak District, a mysterious skull is unearthed. But even weirder is that Lady Sylvia steals the skull for use in worshiping - very erotically - her pagan god, The White Worm, who hungers for the taste of virginal flesh.
