Title: On the Twelfth Day of Christmas (2015)
Genres: Romance, TV Movie
Director: Harvey Crossland
Main Cast: Brooke Nevin, Robin Dunne, Dani Kind, Geri Hall, Alan C. Peterson
Keywords: journalist, small town, lying, radio host, christmas spirit, christmas, secret santa

Overview: Maggie Chalke is torn between her journalistic integrity and helping her long time crush Mitch O'Grady when her boss orders her to write a story for the paper about the Secret Santa gifts she's been sending Mitch to help him relearn his love for Christmas.
