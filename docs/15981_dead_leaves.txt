Title: Dead Leaves (2004)
Genres: Science Fiction, Animation, Comedy, Crime
Director: Hiroyuki Imaishi
Main Cast: Kappei Yamaguchi, Takako Honda
Keywords: moon, prison, clone, escape from prison, sexual humor, cloning, crazy, robot, criminal, space prison

Overview: Pandy and Retro, two unlikely renegades, awaken naked on Earth with no recollection of their past. After embarking on a devastating crime spree for food, clothing and transportation in downtown Tokyo, they are captured by authorities and sent to the infamous lunar prison called Dead Leaves.
