Title: Kaïrat (1992)
Genres: Drama
Director: Darezhan Omirbayev
Main Cast: Samat Beysenbin, Baljan Bisembekova, Indira Jeksembaeva, Kariat Mahmetov
Keywords: kazakhstan, bus conductor

Overview: Living in Almaty, Kazakhstan, a young man is preparing to become a bus conductor. One day, in between wandering the city streets, and going to the movies, he makes the acquaintance of a female student.
