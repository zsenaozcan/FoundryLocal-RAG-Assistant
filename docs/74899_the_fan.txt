Title: The Fan (1981)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Ed Bianchi
Main Cast: Lauren Bacall, James Garner, Maureen Stapleton, Héctor Elizondo, Michael Biehn
Keywords: obsession, stalker, obsessed fan

Overview: A record store clerk is an obsessed fan of an actress of stage and screen (Lauren Bacall). However when his letters are rejected, the fan strikes out at the actresses friends, then at her.
