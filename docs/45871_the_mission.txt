Title: The Mission (1999)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Johnnie To
Main Cast: Anthony Wong, Francis Ng, Jackie Lui Chung-Yin, Roy Cheung, Lam Suet

Overview: Triad boss Lung (Eddy Ko), who has just escaped being killed in an assassination attempt hires the killers Curtis (Anthony Wong), James (Lam Suet), Mike (Roy Cheung), Roy (Francis Ng) and Shin (Jackie Lui) for his protection.
