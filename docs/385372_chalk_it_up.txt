Title: Chalk It Up (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Hisonni Johnson
Main Cast: Maddy Curley, John DeLuca, Nikki SooHoo, Drew Seeley, Julie Warner
Keywords: gymnastics

Overview: When a super girly-girl is dumped by her boyfriend; she decides to do everything she can to get him back by building a college gymnastics team, quickly learning that she is capable of a lot more than just getting an MRS degree.
