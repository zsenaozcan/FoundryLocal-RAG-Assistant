Title: Three Men in a Boat (1979)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Naum Birman
Main Cast: Andrey Mironov, Aleksandr Shirvindt, Mikhail Derzhavin, Larisa Golubkina, Alina Pokrovskaya

Overview: Three London gentlemen take vacation rowing down the Thames, encountering various mishaps and misadventures along the way.
