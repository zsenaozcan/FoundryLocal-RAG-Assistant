Title: Monster Dog (1984)
Genres: Horror, Foreign
Director: Claudio Fragasso
Main Cast: Alice Cooper, Victoria Vera, Carlos Santurio, Pepa Sarsa, Pepita Full James
Keywords: star, pop star, dog

Overview: A (dubbed) Alice Cooper stars as Victor Raven, a famous rock star, who returns to his childhood home to shoot a music video. Believing his presence is responsible for the return of a monstrous hound that killed folks when he was kid, the locals decide to do something violent about it.
