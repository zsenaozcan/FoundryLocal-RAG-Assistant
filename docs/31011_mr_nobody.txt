Title: Mr. Nobody (2009)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama, Romance, Fantasy
Director: Jaco Van Dormael
Main Cast: Jared Leto, Diane Kruger, Linh Đan Phạm, Sarah Polley, Rhys Ifans
Keywords: surrealism

Overview: Nemo Nobody leads an ordinary existence with his wife and 3 children; one day, he wakes up as a mortal centenarian in the year 2092.
