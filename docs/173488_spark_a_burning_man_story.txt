Title: Spark: A Burning Man Story (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jessie Deeter
Main Cast: Katy Boynton, Marco Cochrane, Harley K. Dubois, Marian Goodell, Larry Harvey
Keywords: festival, woman director

Overview: Each year, 60,000 people from around the globe gather in a dusty windswept Nevada desert to build a temporary city, collaborating on large-scale art and partying for a week before burning a giant effigy in a ritual frenzy. Spark takes a peek behind the curtain with Burning Man organizers and participants, revealing a year of unprecedented challenges and growth.
