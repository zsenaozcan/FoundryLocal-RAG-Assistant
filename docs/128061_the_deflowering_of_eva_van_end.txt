Title: The Deflowering of Eva van End (2012)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Michiel ten Horn
Main Cast: Vivian Dierickx, Rafael Gareisen, Jacqueline Blom, Freerk Bos, Frans de Wit

Overview: The ugly duckling of a fraught middle-class household has her life turned upside-down by the arrival of a handsome German exchange student, in this caustic, absurdist satire of petit-bourgeois family life from Dutch director Michiel ten Horn. (TIFF)
