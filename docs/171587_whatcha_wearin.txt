Title: Whatcha Wearin'? (2012)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Comedy
Director: Byun Sung-hyun
Main Cast: Kim Ah-joong, Ji Sung, Shin So-yul, Kang Kyung-joon, Kim Seong-oh
Keywords: south korea

Overview: Yoon-Jung accidentally calls a stranger, instead of her boyfriend, and has phone sex with the unknown man. When Yoon-Jung's relationship with her boyfriend turns sour, Yoon-Jung meets Hyun-Seung, the stranger she had phone sex with.
