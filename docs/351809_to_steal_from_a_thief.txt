Title: To Steal from a Thief (2016)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Daniel Calparsoro
Main Cast: Luis Tosar, Raúl Arévalo, Marian Álvarez, Nani Jiménez, Rodrigo de la Serna
Keywords: coma, government, tunnel, steal, accident

Overview: On a rainy morning six armed men in disguise rob a bank in Valencia .But what seemed like an easy heist quickly goes wrong with nothing unfolding as planned, and mistrust quickly builds between the two leaders of the gang.
