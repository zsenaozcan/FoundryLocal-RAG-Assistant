Title: Loving (2016)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Jeff Nichols
Main Cast: Joel Edgerton, Ruth Negga, Marton Csokas, Nick Kroll, Michael Shannon
Keywords: biography, marriage, court, supreme court, interracial marriage, virginia, interracial couple, civils rights

Overview: The story of Richard and Mildred Loving, an interracial couple, whose challenge of their anti-miscegenation arrest for their marriage in Virginia led to a legal battle that would end at the US Supreme Court.
