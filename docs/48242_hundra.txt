Title: Hundra (1983)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Action, Science Fiction, Foreign
Director: Matt Cimber
Main Cast: Laurene Landon, Cihangir Gaffari, María Casal, Luis Lorenzo, Tamara

Overview: Born in a tribe of fierce warrior women, Hundra has been raised to despise the influence of men. An archer, fighter and sword fighter, Hundra is superior to any male. Hundra finds her family slain and takes a vow of revenge until one day she meets her match.
