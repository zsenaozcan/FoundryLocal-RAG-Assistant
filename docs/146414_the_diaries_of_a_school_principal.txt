Title: The Diaries of a School Principal (1975)
Genres: Family, Drama
Director: Boris Frumin
Main Cast: Oleg Borisov, Iya Savvina, Alla Pokrovskaya, Lyudmila Gurchenko, Yelena Solovey

Overview: The everyday life of a Soviet high school principal, a WWII veteran and an unfulfilled writer, whose vocation is teaching kids.
