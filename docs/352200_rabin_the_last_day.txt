Title: Rabin, the Last Day (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Documentary, Drama
Director: Amos Gitai
Main Cast: Einat Weizman, Ischac Hiskiya, Pini Mitelman, Shimon Peres, Yogev Yefet
Keywords: assassination, israel, political

Overview: Itzhak Rabin's murder ended all efforts of peace, and with him the whole left wing of Israel died. The movie shows the last of his days as prime minister, and what led to his murder.
