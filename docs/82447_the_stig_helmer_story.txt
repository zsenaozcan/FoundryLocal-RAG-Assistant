Title: The Stig-Helmer Story (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lasse Åberg
Main Cast: Lasse Åberg, Filip Arsic-Johnsson, Jonas Bane, Elisabet Carlsson, Tove Edfeldt

Overview: The Stig-Helmer Story is a 2011 Swedish comedy film directed by Lasse Åberg. The film is the sixth in the series about Stig-Helmer Olsson.
