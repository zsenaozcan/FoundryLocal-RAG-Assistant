Title: The Eternal (1998)
Genres: Horror
Director: Michael Almereyda
Main Cast: Alison Elliott, Jared Harris, Christopher Walken, Jason Miller, Lois Smith
Keywords: witch, soul, horror, gothic

Overview: An alcoholic American couple travel to the UK with their son so he can meet his grandmother but they walk in on their crazed uncle who is in the midst of reviving a centuries-old Druid witch.
