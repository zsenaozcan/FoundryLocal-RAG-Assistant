Title: Smosh: The Movie (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Action, Adventure
Director: Alex Winter
Main Cast: Anthony Padilla, Ian Hecox, Shane Dawson, Mark Edward Fischbach, Jillian Nelson
Keywords: defy media

Overview: An embarrassing video of Anthony surfaces online right before his fifth high school reunion. The Smosh dudes then race to pull down the clip before it blows Anthony's chances of reconnecting with his teen crush Anna.
