Title: W.E. (2011)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Madonna
Main Cast: Abbie Cornish, Andrea Riseborough, James D'Arcy, Richard Coyle, David Harbour
Keywords: auction, beating, bathtub, clothes, woman director

Overview: In 1998, an auction of the estate of the Duke and Duchess of Windsor causes great excitement. For one woman, Wally Winthrop, it has much more meaning. Wally becomes obsessed by their historic love story. As she learns more about the sacrifices involved, Wally gains her own courage to find happiness.
