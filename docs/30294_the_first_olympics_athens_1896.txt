Title: The First Olympics: Athens 1896 (1984)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alvin Rakoff
Main Cast: David Ogden Stiers, Hunt Block, Alex Hyde-White, Benedict Taylor, David Caruso
Keywords: olympic games, track and field, sports arena

Overview: This movie describes the founding of the modern Olympics and concentrates on the creation of the American team and their trials in getting to the Olympics in Athens.
