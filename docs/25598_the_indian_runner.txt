Title: The Indian Runner (1991)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sean Penn
Main Cast: David Morse, Viggo Mortensen, Valeria Golino, Patricia Arquette, Charles Bronson
Keywords: brother brother relationship

Overview: An intensely sad film about two brothers who cannot overcome their opposite perceptions of life. One brother sees and feels bad in everyone and everything, subsequently he is violent, antisocial and unable to appreciate or enjoy the good things which his brother desperately tries to point out to him.
