Title: One Man's War (1973)
Genres: Drama
Director: Risto Jarva
Main Cast: Eero Rinne, Tuula Nyman, Tauno Hautaniemi, Martti Pennanen, Aimo Heino
Keywords: marriage, business ethics, poverty, construction site, construction, unemployment, entrepreneur, kitchen sink realism

Overview: Man leaves his factory job and sells everything to buy an earth mover and start his own business, travelling from place to place with his wife and child. There is not enough work and he is eventually forced to emigrate after many hardships.
