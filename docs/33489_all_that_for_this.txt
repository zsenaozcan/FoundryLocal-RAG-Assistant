Title: All That... for This?! (1993)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Claude Lelouch
Main Cast: Fabrice Luchini, Vincent Lindon, Jacques Gamblin, Francis Huster

Overview: The plot is about a trial against three men who tried to earn loads of money by illegal methods to get to Canada and about the lawyers and the judge who get on with the trial and who are being unfaithful to their couples.
