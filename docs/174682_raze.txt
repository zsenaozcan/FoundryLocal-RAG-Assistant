Title: Raze (2013)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Josh C. Waller
Main Cast: Zoë Bell, Rachel Nichols, Tracie Thoms, Sherilyn Fenn, Doug Jones
Keywords: extortion, death match, elimination derby, women fighters

Overview: After Sabrina is abducted, she finds herself in an underground lair, forced to do battle with other innocent women for the amusement of unseen spectators. Each of these reluctant warriors has something to lose, but only one will remain when the game is done.
