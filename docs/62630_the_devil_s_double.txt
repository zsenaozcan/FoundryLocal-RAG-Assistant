Title: The Devil's Double (2011)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime, War
Director: Lee Tamahori
Main Cast: Dominic Cooper, Ludivine Sagnier, Raad Rawi, Mehmet Ferda, Philip Quast
Keywords: palace, impostor, pervertion

Overview: A chilling vision of the House of Saddam Hussein comes to life through the eyes of the man who was forced to become the double of Hussein's sadistic son.
