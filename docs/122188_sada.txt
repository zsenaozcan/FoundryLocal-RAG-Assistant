Title: Sada (1998)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Nobuhiko Ôbayashi
Main Cast: Hitomi Kuroki, Tsurutaro Kataoka, Norihei Miki, Kippei Shiina, Ayumi Ito

Overview: Nobuhiko Obayashi, director of the cult horror/comedy "House," gives his unique spin on the true story of Sada Abe, who strangled her lover and cut off his genitals as a keepsake.
