Title: The Flower of Evil (2003)
Genres: Drama
Director: Claude Chabrol
Main Cast: Benoît Magimel, Mélanie Doutey, Suzanne Flon, Bernard Le Coq, Nathalie Baye
Keywords: return, suppressed past, bourgeoisie, mayor elections, suspense, wealth, family conflict

Overview: Three generations of a wealthy Bordeaux family are caught in the crossfire when Anne decides to run for mayor, thanks to a political pamphlet that revives an old murder scandal.
