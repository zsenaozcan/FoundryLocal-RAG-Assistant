Title: Santa Claus. Battle of Mages (2016)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Aleksandr Voytinskiy
Main Cast: Fyodor Bondarchuk, Taisiya Vilkova, Egor Beroev, Kseniya Alfyorova, Nikita Volkov

Overview: Young girl Masha accidentally discovers the organization of snow and ice mages fighting the global devil under the leadership of Santa Claus.
