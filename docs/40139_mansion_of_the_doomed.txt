Title: Mansion of the Doomed (1976)
Genres: Horror
Director: Michael Pataki
Main Cast: Richard Basehart, Gloria Grahame, Lance Henriksen, JoJo D'Amore, Trish Stewart
Keywords: independent film, gore, car accident, transplant, eyeball

Overview: An insane surgeon finds himself up to his armpits in eyeballs after guilt prompts him to begin removing the eyes of abducted people in hopes of performing transplants on his daughter who lost her own in a car-accident he caused.
