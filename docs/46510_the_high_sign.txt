Title: The High Sign (1921)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Edward F. Cline
Main Cast: Buster Keaton, Bartine Burkett, Charles Dorety, Joe Roberts, Al St. John
Keywords: bodyguard, oath, trapdoor, slapstick, train, drifter, dog, silent film, amusement park, death threat

Overview: Buster is thrown off a train near an amusement park. There he gets a job in a shooting gallery run by the Blinking Buzzards mob. Ordered to kill a businessman, he winds up protecting the man and his daughter by outfitting their home with trick devices.
