Title: For a Lost Soldier (1992)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: Roeland Kerbosch
Main Cast: Maarten Smit, Jeroen Krabbé, Andrew Kelley, Freark Smink, Elsje de Wijn
Keywords: sexual identity, dance performance, world war ii, first time, biography, soldier, lgbt

Overview: The story of a romantic relationship between a Canadian soldier and a child set in the Netherlands near the end of WWII. Told in flashback from the present day, the child, now a man, is still seeking his lost soldier.
