Title: Godfrey: Black By Accident (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Godfrey

Overview: With his signature wide-eyed humor and treasure trove of voices Godfrey asks the questions that plague the depths of our being. Why do people duck in the rain and protect just one ear? What if guido bouncers ran the TSA? Stand-up live at New York City’s Gramercy Theatre.
