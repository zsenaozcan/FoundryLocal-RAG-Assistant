Title: The Woman in the Rumor (1954)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Kenji Mizoguchi
Main Cast: Kinuyo Tanaka, Tomoemon Otani, Yoshiko Kuga, Eitarô Shindô, Bontarô Miake
Keywords: suicide attempt, kyoto japan

Overview: Yukiko's fiance learns her mother runs a geisha house and ends their engagement. She despises what her mother does until one of her clients shows interest and starts to woo her.
