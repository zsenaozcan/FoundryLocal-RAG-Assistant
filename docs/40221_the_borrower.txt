Title: The Borrower (1991)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy, Horror
Director: John McNaughton
Main Cast: Rae Dawn Chong, Don Gordon, Tom Towles, Antonio Fargas, Neil Giuntoli
Keywords: magic, beautiful woman, decapitation, severed head

Overview: An alien criminal exiled to Earth in human form is ripping the heads off human beings after his own head explodes.
