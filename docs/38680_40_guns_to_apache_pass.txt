Title: 40 Guns to Apache Pass (1967)
Genres: Western
Director: William Witney
Main Cast: Audie Murphy, Michael Burns, Kenneth Tobey, Laraine Stephens, Robert Brubaker
Keywords: apache, rifle

Overview: The Apaches are on the warpath and the Army must defend them. Murphy's mission is to get a shipment of rifles, but it's stolen by greedy white traders with the help of mutinous soldiers
