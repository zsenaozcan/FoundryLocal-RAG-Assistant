Title: Adios amigos (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Albert Jan van Rees
Main Cast: Martijn Lakemeier, Yannick van de Velde, Margot Ros, Bas Hoeflaak

Overview: Three young guys with disabilities want to go on a party holiday in Salou, just as their friends do. Only their parents block this idea. But then a caretaker, found on the internet, makes the dream possible. Adios Amigos is a remake of the Belgium movie Hasta La Vista.
