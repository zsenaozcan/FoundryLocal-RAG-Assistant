Title: The Escape (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Action
Director: Neill Blomkamp
Main Cast: Clive Owen, Dakota Fanning, Jon Bernthal, Vera Farmiga, Daniel Kash
Keywords: driver, bmw, short, advertisement

Overview: After the controversial disappearance of their Chief Medical Officer, a shadowy bio genetics company is under siege by the FBI for secretly cloning humans. Amidst the chaos, Molgen’s head mercenary Holt (Jon Bernthal) is sneaking out the last living clone, a young woman named Lily (Dakota Fanning) to a mysterious buyer. Holt has hired The Driver (Clive Owen), accompanied by a heavily armed squadron of fellow Molgen mercenaries, to evade the authorities and deliver Lily and himself to the buyer. The journey takes an unexpected turn and Holt is forced to take matters into his own hands, as the driver once again demonstrates his mettle and extraordinary driving skills.
