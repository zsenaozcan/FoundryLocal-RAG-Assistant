Title: Straight Shooting (1917)
Genres: Western
Director: John Ford
Main Cast: Harry Carey, Duke R. Lee, George Berrell, Molly Malone, Ted Brooks
Keywords: murder, cattle, change of heart

Overview: Cattleman Flint cuts off farmer Sims' water supply. When Sims' son Ted goes for water, one of Flint's men kills him. Cheyenne is sent to finish off Sims, but finding the family at the newly dug grave, he changes sides.
