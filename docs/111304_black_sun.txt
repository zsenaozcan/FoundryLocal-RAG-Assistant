Title: Black Sun (1964)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Koreyoshi Kurahara
Main Cast: Yuko Chishiro, Chico Roland, Tamio Kawaji, Tatsuya Fuji

Overview: You’ve probably never seen anything quite like this manic, oddball, anti–buddy picture about a young, jazz-obsessed Japanese drifter and a black American GI on the lam in Tokyo. The two outsiders become outlaws, and Koreyoshi Kurahara depicts their growing bond as an alternately absurd and tragic culture clash.
