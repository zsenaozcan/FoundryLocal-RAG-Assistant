Title: The Adventures of Buratino (1975)
Genres: Family, Fantasy, Adventure
Director: Leonid Nechayev
Main Cast: Dmitri Iosifov, Tatyana Protsenko, Nikolay Grinko, Vladimir Etush, Vladimir Basov

Overview: A wooden boy Buratino tries to find his place in life. He befriends toys from a toy theater owned by evil Karabas-Barabas, gets tricked by Alice the Fox and Basilio the Cat and finally discovers the mystery of a golden key given to him by kind Tortila the Tortoise.
