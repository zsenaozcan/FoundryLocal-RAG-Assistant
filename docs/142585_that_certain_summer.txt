Title: That Certain Summer (1972)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Lamont Johnson
Main Cast: Hal Holbrook, Martin Sheen, Joe Don Baker, Marlyn Mason, Scott Jacoby
Keywords: coming out, homosexuality, family relationships, lgbt

Overview: The first US teleplay to deal sympathetically with homosexuality.  Divorced San Francisco contractor Doug Salter is looking forward to a summer visit from his fourteen-year-old son Nick, who lives in Los Angeles with his mother Janet. The boy does not know that his father is gay and committed to Gary McClain, his life partner of several years.
