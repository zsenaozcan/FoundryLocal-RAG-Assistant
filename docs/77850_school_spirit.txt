Title: School Spirit (1985)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Alan Holleb
Main Cast: Tom Nolan, Elizabeth Foxx, Larry Linville, Roberta Collins, Brian Frishman
Keywords: condom, school, ghost, invisibility

Overview: The only thing keeping Billy Batson from the girl of his dreams is one little condom -- or rather the lack of one. Lucky Billy finds one at an all-night roadhouse -- but speeding back to his girl, he is killed in a head-on collision with a truck. Now invisible, Billy must find his sweetheart and rekindle her fire -- but when he comes back to school as a ghost, his first stop is the girl's locker room, and he's beginning to "see" all the possibilities!
