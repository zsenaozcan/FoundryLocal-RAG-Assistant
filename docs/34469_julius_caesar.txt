Title: Julius Caesar (2002)
Genres: War, Drama, History
Director: Uli Edel
Main Cast: Jeremy Sisto, Richard Harris, Christopher Walken, Chris Noth, Valeria Golino
Keywords: roman empire, ancient rome, biography, history

Overview: Twenty year-old Julius Caesar flees Rome for his life during the reign of Sulla but through skill and ambition rises four decades later to become Rome's supreme dictator.
