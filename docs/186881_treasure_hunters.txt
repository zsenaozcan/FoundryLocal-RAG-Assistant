Title: Treasure Hunters (1981)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Foreign
Director: Lau Kar-Wing
Main Cast: Alexander Fu Sheng, Gordon Liu Chia-Hui, Johnny Wang Lung-Wei, Wilson Tong, Chan-Peng Chang

Overview: Fu Sheng and his real-life brother star as friends who are searching for a treasure that a Shaolin priest (Gordon Liu), a villainous traitor (Wang Lung Wei) and his sister (Yang Ching Ching) are also trying to find.
