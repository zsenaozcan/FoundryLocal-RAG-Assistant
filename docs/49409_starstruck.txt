Title: Starstruck (1982)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Gillian Armstrong
Main Cast: Jo Kennedy, Ross O'Donovan, Margo Lee, Max Cullen, Melissa Jaffer
Keywords: satire, singer, dance club, woman director

Overview: A new-wave extravaganza in which a young woman wants to become the next big singer with the help of her friends and despite the wishes of her working class family.
