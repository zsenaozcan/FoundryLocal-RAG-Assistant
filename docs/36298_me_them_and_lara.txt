Title: Me, Them And Lara (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Carlo Verdone
Main Cast: Carlo Verdone, Laura Chiatti, Angela Finocchiaro, Anna Bonaiuto, Marco Giallini

Overview: A priest, after spending years in humanitary missions in Africa, reunites with his family, which is in worse conditions than when he left, and above all meets a young lady who will make him doubt both himself and his faith.
