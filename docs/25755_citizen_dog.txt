Title: Citizen Dog (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Fantasy, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Wisit Sasanatieng
Main Cast: Mahasamut Boonyaruk, Saengthong Gate-Uthong, Sawatwong Palakawong Na Autthaya, Nattha Wattanapaiboon, Raenkum Saninn
Keywords: based on novel, bus, mountain, factory, bangkok, bottle, surrealism, train, dog, bright color

Overview: Follow the life of Pod as he moves to Bangkok from the country. Pod's new life starts with getting a job, loosing a finger and dreaming about a girl. A movie were nothing is impossible, and just because you get killed by raining red helmets doesnt mean you have to stop driving motorcycle taxi.
