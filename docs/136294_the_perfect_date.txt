Title: The Perfect Date (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Dominique Farrugia
Main Cast: Clovis Cornillac, Virginie Efira, Manu Payet, Laurence Arné, Shirley Bousquet
Keywords: paris

Overview: Michel dreams of a perfect meeting with the woman of his dreams, entirely due to chance, as his parents and grandparents experienced. Vincent, Michel's contrast, is completely different. He is more the man of the direct approach and he follows his...! The two childhood friends Michel and Vincent, with completely opposite views when it comes to women, soon discover the limits of their respective techniques when they meet Angèle and Nathalie.
