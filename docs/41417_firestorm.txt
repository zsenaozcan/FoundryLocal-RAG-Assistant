Title: Firestorm (1998)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Thriller
Director: Dean Semler
Main Cast: Howie Long, Scott Glenn, Suzy Amis, William Forsythe, Christianne Hirt
Keywords: prison, fire, firemen, criminal, jail break, forest fire

Overview: Firefighter Jesse Graves has to save ornithologist Jennifer and other people caught in a forest fire, which was set up by the lawyer of convicted killer Earl Shaye, who escaped from the prison with several of his inmates posing as firefighters to recover $37,000,000 in stashed loot.
