Title: Wheel of Time (2003)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Werner Herzog
Main Cast: The Dalai Lama, Lama Lhundup Woeser, Takna Jigme Sangpo, Matthieu Ricard, Madhureeta Anand
Keywords: buddhism, tibet, dalai lama, ritual, india

Overview: Wheel of Time is Werner Herzog's photographed look at the largest Buddhist ritual in Bodh Gaya, India.
