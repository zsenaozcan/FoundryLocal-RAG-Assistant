Title: Remembrance (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Anna Justice
Main Cast: Alice Dwyer, Dagmar Manzel, Mateusz Damiecki, Susanne Lothar, David Rasche
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Hannah, a young Jewish girl, is rescued from a concentration camp by her Polish boyfriend, and believes he died after their perilous escape. More than 30 years later, the married Hannah faces an emotional crisis when she learns he's alive.
