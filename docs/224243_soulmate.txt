Title: Soulmate (2013)
Genres: Horror, Mystery
Director: Axelle Carolyn
Main Cast: Anna Walton, Tom Wisdom, Nick Brimble, Tanya Myers, Emma Cleasby
Keywords: suicide, gothic, ghost, woman director

Overview: A widow retreats to a remote cottage to recover following a suicide attempt, only to discover the place is haunted. A ghost story in the tradition of The Others and The Orphanage.
