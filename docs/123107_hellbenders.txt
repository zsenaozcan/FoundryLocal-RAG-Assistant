Title: Hellbenders (2013)
Genres: Horror, Comedy, Thriller
Director: J.T. Petty
Main Cast: Clancy Brown, Clifton Collins Jr, Andre Royo, Robyn Rikoon, Macon Blair

Overview: An exorcism comedy which follows the Order of Hellbound Saints (Brooklyn Parish), a highly secretive and profoundly blasphemous men of God, as they battle demonic forces too terrible to be cast out by traditional Vatican-approved methods.
