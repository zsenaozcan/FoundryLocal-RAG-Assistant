Title: H.G. Wells' War of the Worlds (2005)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: David Michael Latt
Main Cast: C. Thomas Howell, Rhett Giles, Jake Busey, Peter Greene, Tinarie Van Wyk-Loots
Keywords: nudity, dystopia, space invasion, alien, priest, astronomer, soldier, alien invasion

Overview: In this modern retelling of H.G. Wells' classic sci-fi horror, civilization is laid to ruin when a super race of aliens invades Earth. In a blink of an eye, massive "walkers" cover the planet, annihilating all in their path. As cities crumble and human flesh is ripped from the bone, one man struggles to find the one weapon that will turn the tide for mankind.
