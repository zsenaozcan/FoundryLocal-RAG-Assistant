Title: Americano (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mathieu Demy
Main Cast: Salma Hayek, Mathieu Demy, Geraldine Chaplin, Chiara Mastroianni, Carlos Bardem
Keywords: mexico, pole dancer, inheritance, estate

Overview: A real estate agent from Paris arrives in Los Angeles to settle his late mother's estate, but a found photograph sends him on an impromptu journey to Mexico to find a woman named Lola.
