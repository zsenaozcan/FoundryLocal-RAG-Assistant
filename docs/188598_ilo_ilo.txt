Title: Ilo Ilo (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Anthony Chen
Main Cast: Yann Yann Yeo, Tian Wen Chen, Angeli Bayani, Koh Jia Ler, Jo Kukathas
Keywords: migrant worker, asian financial crisis

Overview: Set in the mid 1990s in Singapore, ILOILO chronicles the relationship between a family and their maid from Ilo Ilo, a province in the Philippines.
