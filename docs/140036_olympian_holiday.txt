Title: Olympian Holiday (1976)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Risto Jarva
Main Cast: Antti Litja
Keywords: vacation

Overview: After a mixup at the check-in, Aimo Niemi boards a charter plane bound for the sunny island of Rhodes, Greece, instead of the Innsbruck Winter Olympics he's packed for. It's the early days of package tourism, and he is too shy and clueless to really sort out any of his problems — a suitcase filled with women's clothes, a flirtatious female roommate, and no money to buy food since he was prepared for full room &amp; board in Austria.
