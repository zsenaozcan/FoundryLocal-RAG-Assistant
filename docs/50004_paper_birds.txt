Title: Paper Birds (2010)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Emilio Aragón
Main Cast: Imanol Arias, Lluís Homar, Roger Príncep, Carmen Machi, Fernando Cayo

Overview: Jorge and Enrique are two artist in the post Spanish Civil War times that adopt an orphan child called Miguel. With a varieté company that travel around the country, Jorge and Enrique see the horrors left by the war meanwhile the Republic Army watch Jorge and the rest of the company suspecting that some of them collaborate with the rebels opposite to the Francoist Regime.
