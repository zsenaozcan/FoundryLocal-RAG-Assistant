Title: SOS (1988)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lasse Åberg
Main Cast: Lasse Åberg, Jon Skolmen, Birgitte Söndergaard, Tor Isedal, Anders Ahlbom

Overview: The landlubber Stig-Helmer and his Norwegian friend Ole get on a barge by mistake. They arrive at an archipelago and have to spend Midsummer among the fancy rich boat people, their yachts and summer houses.
