Title: Yonkers Joe (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Robert Celestino
Main Cast: Chazz Palminteri, Christine Lahti, Tom Guiry, Michael Lerner, Nate Bynum
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Yonkers Joe tells the story of a dice hustler whose determination to make one last grab for a big score in Vegas is complicated by the reappearance of his estranged, mentally challenged son into his life.
