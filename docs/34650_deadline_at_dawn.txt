Title: Deadline at Dawn (1946)
Genres: Thriller, Mystery, Romance
Director: Harold Clurman
Main Cast: Susan Hayward, Paul Lukas, Bill Williams, Joseph Calleia, Osa Massen
Keywords: blackout, new york city, sailor, film noir

Overview: A young Navy sailor has one night to find out why a woman was killed and he ended up with a bag of money after a drinking blackout.
