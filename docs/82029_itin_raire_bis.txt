Title: Itinéraire bis (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Jean-Luc Perreard
Main Cast: Fred Testot, Leïla Bekhti, Jean-François Stévenin, Catherine Gandois, Clément Aubert

Overview: 35-year-old Jean still lives with his mother in a small village in Corsica and works as a cook in the family's restaurant. His future, to his great despair, is all mapped out: he'll take over the restaurant. But one day Nora, a young and forceful woman, is thrown into the sea from a racing yacht. She washes up on the beach at Jean's feet - adventure has at last knocked at his door.
