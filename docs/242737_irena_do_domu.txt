Title: Irena do domu! (1955)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Jan Fethke
Main Cast: Lidia Wysocka, Adolf Dymsza, Ludwik Sempoliński, Hanka Bielicka, Helena Buczyńska
Keywords: taxi driver, housewife, driving school

Overview: A factory foreman discovers (to his discontent) that his housewife secretly takes driving lessons in order to get a job and send their son to kindergarten.
