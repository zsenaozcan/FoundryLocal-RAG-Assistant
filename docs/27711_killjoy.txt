Title: Killjoy (2000)
Genres: Horror
Director: Craig Ross Jr.
Main Cast: Ángel Vargas, Vera Yell, Lee Marks, Carl Washington, Jamal Grimes

Overview: Deep in an inner city hell, a ghastly figure is killing off the bad guys. A vigilante, or a demon? For the beautiful high school student, Jada, that's the question that will bring her face to face with the killer clown Killjoy.
