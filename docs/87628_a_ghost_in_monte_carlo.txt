Title: A Ghost in Monte Carlo (1990)
Genres: Romance, History
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Sarah Miles, Oliver Reed, Christopher Plummer, Lysette Anthony, Samantha Eggar

Overview: A chance meeting between British nobleman and lovely young Mistral foils a plot by her manipulating Aunt Emilie to avenge the death of her sister, Mistral's mother, who died in childbirth. But when an unscrupulous blackmailer and a rapacious Rajah enter the plot, the growing attraction between Lord and convent girl becomes yet more fraught with Danger.
