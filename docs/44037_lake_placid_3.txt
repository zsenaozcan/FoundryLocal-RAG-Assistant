Title: Lake Placid 3 (2010)
Genres: Horror, Action, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Griff Furst
Main Cast: Kirsty Mitchell, Colin Ferguson, Yancy Butler, Kacey Clarke, Roxanne Pallett
Keywords: crocodile, crocodile attack, animal horror

Overview: A game warden moves his family to Lake Placid, once the site of deadly crocodile attacks. Locals assure him the crocs are gone, but his mischievous young son finds a few baby crocs and begins feeding them. They quickly grow into very big adults and start attacking the game warden's family and nearby town.
