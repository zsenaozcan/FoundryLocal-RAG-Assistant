Title: The Nine Lives of Tomas Katz (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Fantasy, Mystery
Director: Ben Hopkins
Main Cast: Tom Fisher, Ian McNeice, Tony Maudsley, William Keen, Andrew Melville
Keywords: satire, futuristic, surrealism, doomsday

Overview: The last day of creation. A stranger arrives in London. No one knows who he is or where he has come from. By the time he leaves, the entire universe will have been erased.
