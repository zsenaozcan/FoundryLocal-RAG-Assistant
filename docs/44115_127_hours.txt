Title: 127 Hours (2010)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Thriller
Director: Danny Boyle
Main Cast: James Franco, Kate Mara, Amber Tamblyn, Clémence Poésy, Lizzy Caplan
Keywords: mountain, despair, adventure, utah, alone, canyon, climbing, based on true story, rescue, survival

Overview: The true story of mountain climber Aron Ralston's remarkable adventure to save himself after a fallen boulder crashes on his arm and traps him in an isolated canyon in Utah.
