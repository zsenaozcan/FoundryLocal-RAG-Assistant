Title: Shadow People (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Keith Coulouris
Main Cast: Lesli Kay, Keith Coulouris, Kaela Dobkin, Lee Spencer, Robert Sampson

Overview: Dan and Gretchen, a couple with a secretive past and a failing relationship, rent a guest house from another couple in California's San Fernando Valley. But jealousy and betrayal turn a casual friendship into a game of manipulation and deception.
