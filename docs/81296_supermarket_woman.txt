Title: Supermarket Woman (1996)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Juzo Itami
Main Cast: Nobuko Miyamoto, Masahiko Tsugawa, Sen Yano, Shirô Itô, Yûji Miyake

Overview: Goro's supermarket is not doing well; the rival "Bargains Galore" threatens his business. A chance encounter with Hanako, an energetic woman he knew in grade school, results in big retail and life changes.
