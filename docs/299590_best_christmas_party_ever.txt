Title: Best Christmas Party Ever (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: John Bradshaw
Main Cast: Torrey DeVitto, Steve Lund, Emily Coutts

Overview: With the holidays approaching, a young party planner arranges a special Christmas party for a New York toy store. When a powerful corporation threatens to shut her down, she decides to follow her heart, moving forward with the party plans and finding true love in time for Christmas.
