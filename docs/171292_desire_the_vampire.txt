Title: Desire: The Vampire (1982)
Genres: TV Movie, Crime, Horror, Thriller
Director: John Llewellyn Moxey
Main Cast: David Naughton, Dorian Harewood, Marilyn Jones, Barbara Stock, Arthur Rosenberg
Keywords: female vampire

Overview: A Coroner's assistant (who is also a law student) gets involved in a strange case involving his girlfriend's place of employment (a hospital) and Vampires after Milton King is drained of his blood. Strange things happen as a defrocked priest tries to thwart all efforts to solve this case.
