Title: Treasure Island (1972)
Genres: Adventure, Family
Director: John Hough
Main Cast: Orson Welles, Kim Burfield, Walter Slezak, Ángel del Pozo, Rik Battaglia

Overview: Young Jim Hawkins finds himself serving with pirate captain Long John Silver in search of a buccaneer's treasure, in this short adaptation of Robert Louis Stevenson's classic tale.
