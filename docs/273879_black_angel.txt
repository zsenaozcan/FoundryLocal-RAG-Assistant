Title: Black Angel (1980)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy
Director: Roger Christian
Main Cast: Tony Vogel, Tony Vogel, John Young, Patricia Christian
Keywords: space opera, tie-in

Overview: A knight returns home from the Crusades only to find his village devastated by disease and his family gone. He roams the forest searching for them, until he finds a mysterious maiden in a lake who asks him to kill the evil black knight.
