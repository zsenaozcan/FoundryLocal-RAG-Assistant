Title: The Jolson Story (1946)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Alfred E. Green
Main Cast: Larry Parks, Evelyn Keyes, William Demarest, Bill Goodwin, Ludwig Donath
Keywords: musical, biography, classic

Overview: The Jolson Story is a 1946 musical biography which purports to tell the life story of singer Al Jolson. It stars Larry Parks as Jolson, Evelyn Keyes as "Julie Benson" (approximating Jolson's wife, Ruby Keeler), William Demarest as his manager, Ludwig Donath and Tamara Shayne as his parents, and Scotty Beckett as the young Jolson.
