Title: Kyaa Kool Hai Hum (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sangeeth Sivan
Main Cast: Tusshar Kapoor, Ritesh Deshmukh, Isha Koppikar, Neha Dhupia, Anupam Kher
Keywords: bollywood

Overview: When flatmates Karan and Rahul are unable to pay the rent, they are dramatically evicted. However, they sneak their way back in within hours as the building is being renovated.
