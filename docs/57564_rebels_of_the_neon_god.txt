Title: Rebels of the Neon God (1993)
Genres: Drama
Director: Tsai Ming-liang
Main Cast: Chen Chao-jung, Lee Kang-Sheng, Lu Yi-Ching, Tien Miao, Jen Chang-bin

Overview: Defying his parents, disaffected youth Hsiao Kang drops out of the local cram school to head for the bright lights of downtown Taipei. He falls in with Ah Tze, a young hoodlum, and their relationship is a confused mixture of hero-worship and rivalry that soon leads to trouble.
