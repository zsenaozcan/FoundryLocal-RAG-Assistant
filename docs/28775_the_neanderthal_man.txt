Title: The Neanderthal Man (1953)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: E.A. Dupont
Main Cast: Robert Shayne, Richard Crane, Joyce Terry, Doris Merrick, Tandra Quinn
Keywords: sheriff, waitress, bar, experiment, transformation, tiger, injection, game warden, laboratory, mad scientist

Overview: A scientist develops a formula which will cause animals to regress to the form of their primitive ancestors, and tries it on himself with disastrous results.
