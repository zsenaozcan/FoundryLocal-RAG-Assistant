Title: Planet B-Boy (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Benson Lee
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: breakdance, b-boying

Overview: Think breakdancing died in the eighties? Think again. PLANET B-BOY is a feature-length, theatrical documentary that re-discovers one of the most incredible dance phenomena the world has ever seen. Originally known as "B-boying", breakdancing was an urban dance form that originated from the streets of New York City during the seventies.
