Title: Henry Poole Is Here (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Mark Pellington
Main Cast: Luke Wilson, George Lopez, Radha Mitchell, Morgan Lily, Adriana Barraza
Keywords: depression, miracle, graffiti, house, faith, terminal illness, dying man, loneliness, independent film, neighbor

Overview: Henry Poole abandons his fiancée and family business to spend what he believes are his remaining days alone. The discovery of a 'miracle' by a nosy neighbor ruptures his solitude and restores his faith in life.
