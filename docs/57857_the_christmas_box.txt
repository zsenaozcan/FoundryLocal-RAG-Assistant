Title: The Christmas Box (1995)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy
Director: Marcus Cole
Main Cast: Annette O'Toole, Maureen O'Hara, Kelsey Mulrooney, Richard Thomas
Keywords: dream, christmas, ski shop

Overview: A ski-shop owner reluctantly moves himself, his wife, and his daughter in to an estate as live-in help for an elderly widow. While struggling to balance his career and family life, he has recurring dreams about an angel.
