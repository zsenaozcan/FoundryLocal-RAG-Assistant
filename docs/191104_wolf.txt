Title: Wolf (2013)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime
Director: Jim Taihuttu
Main Cast: Raymond Thiry, Bo Maerten, Slimane Dazi, Nasrdin Dchar, Chemseddine Amar

Overview: A young boxer is making a name for himself inside the ring. Outside the ring, he is sucked into a world of crime. He becomes increasingly entangled in his own ambition.
