Title: The Year That Trembled (2002)
Genres: War, Drama, Romance
Director: Jay Craven
Main Cast: Jonathan Brandis, Charlie Finn, Jay R. Ferguson, Meredith Monroe, Marin Hinkle
Keywords: 1970s, vietnam, lottery

Overview: The Year That Trembled is a 1970 coming-of-age story set in the shadow of Kent State that focuses on a group of young characters facing the Vietnam Draft Lottery.
