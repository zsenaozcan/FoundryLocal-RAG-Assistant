Title: Treasure Buddies (2012)
Genres: Adventure, Family
Director: Robert Vince
Main Cast: Richard Riehle, Edward Herrmann, Mason Cook, Adam Alexi-Malle, Mo Gallini
Keywords: cat, airplane, treasure hunt, monkey

Overview: Disney's irresistible talking puppies are back in an all-new movie that takes them halfway across the world to the ruins of ancient Egypt. With the help of some exotic new friends, this epic adventure is a treasure trove of pure Buddy fun. In a race against a devious cat, the Buddies and their new friends, Cammy and Babi, must avoid booby traps, solve puzzles and explore a mysterious tomb - all in search of the greatest treasure known to animalkind!
