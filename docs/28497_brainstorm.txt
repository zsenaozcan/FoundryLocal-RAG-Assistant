Title: Brainstorm (1965)
Genres: Thriller
Director: William Conrad
Main Cast: Jeffrey Hunter, Anne Francis, Dana Andrews, Viveca Lindfors, Stacy Harris
Keywords: suicide attempt, driven insane

Overview: Brainstorm, released in 1965, is a late film noir whose male protagonist at first prevents the suicide of his employer's wife, falls in love with her, and is later driven to crime and insanity.
