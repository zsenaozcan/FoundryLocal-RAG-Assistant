Title: The Seaside Motel (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Kentaro Moriya
Main Cast: Toma Ikuta, Kumiko Asou, Takayuki Yamada, Tetsuji Tamayama, Riko Narumi

Overview: The Seaside Motel is trapped in the mountains, without even enough water to serve their guests 24 hours a day, much less having a view of a body of water of any kind.
