Title: Gardener of Eden (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Kevin Connolly
Main Cast: Lukas Haas, Erika Christensen, Giovanni Ribisi, Jim Parsons, Jon Abrahams
Keywords: rape, new jersey, hero, drop-out, independent film

Overview: When a young deli-counter clerk and general slacker in New Jersey accidentally saves a young woman from a serial rapist, he becomes convinced that his new role in life is to become a vigilante.
