Title: The Apple (1998)
Genres: Drama
Director: Samira Makhmalbaf
Main Cast: Massoumeh Naderi, Zahra Naderi, Ghorban Ali Naderi, Azizeh Mohamadi, Zahra Saghrisaz
Keywords: apple, woman director

Overview: After twelve years of imprisonment by their own parents, two sisters are finally released by social workers to face the outside world for the first time.
