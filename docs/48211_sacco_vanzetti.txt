Title: Sacco & Vanzetti (1971)
Genres: Crime, Drama, History
Director: Giuliano Montaldo
Main Cast: Gian Maria Volonté, Riccardo Cucciolla, Cyril Cusack, Rosanna Fratello, Geoffrey Keen
Keywords: immigrant, biography, anarchist

Overview: The story of two anarchists who were charged and unfairly tried for murder when it was really for their political convictions.
