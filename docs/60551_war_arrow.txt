Title: War Arrow (1954)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Romance, War, Western
Director: George Sherman
Main Cast: Maureen O'Hara, Jeff Chandler, John McIntire, Suzan Ball, Dennis Weaver
Keywords: adventure, war, romance

Overview: A thrilling Cavalry-versus-Indians adventure starring Jeff Chandler as an Army official recruiting Seminole allies, against his superior's wishes, to stop a planned Kiowa attack.
