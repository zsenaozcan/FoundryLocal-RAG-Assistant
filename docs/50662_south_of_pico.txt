Title: South Of Pico (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Gina Torres, Kip Pardue, Henry Simmons
Keywords: independent film

Overview: "South of Pico" is an emotionally charged drama in which four strangers witness an unimaginable tragedy and are catapulted into the defining moment of their lives. Set in present day Los Angeles, a chauffeur, a waitress, a doctor and a young boy each deal with life's daily challenges, only to find themselves at the scene of an accident the moment it happens.
