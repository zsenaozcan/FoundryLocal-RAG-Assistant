Title: Winter of Frozen Dreams (2009)
Genres: Crime, Drama, History, Thriller
Director: Eric Mandelbaum
Main Cast: Thora Birch, Keith Carradine, Brendan Sexton III, Leo Fitzpatrick, Dan Moran

Overview: Barbara Hoffman is a University of Wisconsin science student. She is a part-time employee at a local massage parlor. She is also a killer. Now all Detective Lulling has to do is prove it.
