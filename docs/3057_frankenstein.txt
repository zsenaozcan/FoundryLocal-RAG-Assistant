Title: Frankenstein (2004)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Science Fiction, Mystery
Director: Kevin Connor
Main Cast: Luke Goss, Alec Newman, Julie Delpy, William Hurt, Donald Sutherland
Keywords: monster, frankenstein

Overview: Frankenstein is a 2004 U.S. television miniseries (edited into a film) based on the book Frankenstein by Mary Shelley. It follows the original book more closely than other adaptions. The story is of a scientist who brings life to a creature fashioned from corpses and various body parts.
