Title: The Stranger's Return (1933)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: King Vidor
Main Cast: Lionel Barrymore, Miriam  Hopkins, Franchot Tone, Stuart Erwin, Irene Hervey
Keywords: based on novel, farm, rural setting

Overview: "Miriam Hopkins plays Louise Starr, who gets divorced from her husband and returns to the home she left on a farm where she reunites with her grandfather. He introduces her to Guy Crane with whom she falls in love; however, he is married."
