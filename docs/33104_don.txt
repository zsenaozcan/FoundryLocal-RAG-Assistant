Title: Don (1978)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Crime, Foreign, Thriller
Director: Chandra Barot
Main Cast: Amitabh Bachchan, Zeenat Aman, Pran, Iftekhar, Om Shivpuri
Keywords: undercover cop, look-alike

Overview: A ruthless gangster named Don is killed during a scuffle with the police. Since only DSP DeSilva knows of this incident, he recruits another person named Vijay who is a spitting image of Don. Vijay is planted in Don's place in Don's gang and DeSilva has a plan to nab the entire gang in this way. But when DeSilva dies during a raid, the secret that Vijay is not Don is also buried with him.
