Title: Seven Minutes in Heaven (1985)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Linda Feferman
Main Cast: Jennifer Connelly, Byron Thames, Maddie Corman, Alan Boyce, Michael Zaslow
Keywords: teenager, woman director, teenage romance

Overview: Natalie allows her classmate Jeff, who ran away from home after a fight with his stepfather, to stay at her place while her father is away on a business trip. Natalie soon starts dating Jeff's friend James Casey, who isn't as faithful as she thinks, while her best friend Polly falls in love with baseball player Zoo Knudsen.
