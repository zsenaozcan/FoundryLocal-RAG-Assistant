Title: King of the Gypsies (1978)
Genres: Drama
Director: Frank Pierson
Main Cast: Sterling Hayden, Shelley Winters, Susan Sarandon, Judd Hirsch, Eric Roberts
Keywords: gypsy, fate, inheritance

Overview: Zharko, leader of the Romanis in NYC,  passes his position of leadership on to his unwilling grandson, Dave, leading to infighting between Dave and his father.
