Title: The Shrine (2010)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jon Knautz
Main Cast: Cindy Sampson, Aaron Ashmore, Meghan Heffern, Ben Lewis
Keywords: journalist, coffin, diary, village, supernatural, cult, escape, trapped

Overview: A group of young journalists investigate a cult said to practice human sacrifice, but their ambitious ways may lead them to becoming the cult's next victims.
