Title: The Corridor (1994)
Genres: Drama
Director: Šarūnas Bartas
Main Cast: Daiva Ksivickiene, Mantvydas Janeliunas, Yekaterina Golubeva, Šarūnas Bartas
Keywords: lithuania

Overview: The atmosphere of a corridor between yesterday and tomorrow, where many doors open into the unknown ... A series of faces, gestures and images both real and imagined time.  (A fragmentary narrative without dialogue and depicts several people in Vilnius)
