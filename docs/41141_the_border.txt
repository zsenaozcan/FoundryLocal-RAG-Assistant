Title: The Border (2007)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Lauri Törhönen
Main Cast: Martin Bahne, Tommi Korpela, Minna Haapkylä, Hannu-Pekka Björkman, Leonid Mozgovoy

Overview: The young man must set up a clear border between Finland and Russia, white and red, enemy and friend, us and them. While the task seems clear he finds out the execution of his command in concrete situations is very difficult. Right choices turn out to be wrong ones and correcting them make things worse.
