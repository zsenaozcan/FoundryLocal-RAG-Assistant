Title: New Kids Nitro (2011)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Steffen Haars
Main Cast: Tim Haars, Huub Smit, Wesley van Gaalen, Steffen Haars, Flip van der Kuil
Keywords: zombie

Overview: Gerrie, Richard, Rikkert, Robbie and Barry from Maaskantje are in a big fight with the village of Schijndel. When a zombie kills someone from Brabant, things get out of hand.
