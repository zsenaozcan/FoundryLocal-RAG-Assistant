Title: Women Aren't Funny (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary
Director: Bonnie McFarlane
Main Cast: Adam Carolla, Anthony Cumia, Todd Glass, Gregg Hughes, Artie Lange
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Comedian Bonnie McFarlane dons her investigative journalist's hat to find out once and for all if women are funny and report her unbiased findings in what some are calling the most important documentary of our generation.
