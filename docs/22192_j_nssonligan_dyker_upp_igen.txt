Title: Jönssonligan dyker upp igen (1986)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Mikael Ekman
Main Cast: Gösta Ekman, Ulf Brunnberg, Björn Gustafson, Birgitta Andersson, Per Grundén
Keywords: computer chip, stockholm, jönsson gang, ikea
