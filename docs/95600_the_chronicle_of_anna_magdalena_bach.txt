Title: The Chronicle of Anna Magdalena Bach (1968)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jean-Marie Straub
Main Cast: Gustav Leonhardt, Christiane Lang, Paolo Carlini, Ernst Castelli, Hans-Peter Boye
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A chronicle of Johann Sebastian Bach's life, eschewing drama to focus almost entirely on his music. Narrated by his wife Anna in voiceover, it consists largely of static scenes of Bach conducting and/or playing his brilliant compositions.
