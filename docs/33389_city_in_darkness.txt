Title: City in Darkness (1939)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Herbert I. Leeds
Main Cast: Sidney Toler, Lynn Bari, Richard Clarke, Harold Huber, Pedro de Cordoba
Keywords: charlie chan

Overview: Chan goes to Paris for a reunion with friends from World War I. There he investigates the murder of a munitions manufacturer who was supplying arms to the enemy. At the end Charlie preaches to us about the dangers of peace conferences.
