Title: Swept Away (1974)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance, Foreign
Director: Lina Wertmüller
Main Cast: Mariangela Melato, Giancarlo Giannini, Riccardo Salvino, Isa Danieli, Aldo Puglisi
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Stranded and alone on a desert island during a cruise, a spoiled rich woman and a deckhand fall in love and make a date to reunite after their rescue. He is stood up.
