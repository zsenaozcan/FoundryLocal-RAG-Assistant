Title: To Joy (1950)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ingmar Bergman
Main Cast: Maj-Britt Nilsson, Stig Olin, Birger Malmsten, John Ekman, Margit Carlqvist
Keywords: orchestra, violinist, mistress, orchestra conductor

Overview: To Joy (Swedish: Till glädje) is a 1950 Swedish film directed by Ingmar Bergman about a young married couple who play together in a Swedish orchestra.
