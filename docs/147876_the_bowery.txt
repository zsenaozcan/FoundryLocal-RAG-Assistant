Title: The Bowery (1933)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: Wallace Beery, George Raft, Jackie Cooper, Fay Wray, Pert Kelton
Keywords: saloon, knockout, bookmaker, fall from grace

Overview: "In the Gay Nineties New York had grown up into bustles and balloon Sleeves ... but The Bowery had grown younger, louder and more rowdy until it was known as the 'Livest Mile on the face of the globe' ... the cradle of men who were later to be famous.
