Title: Zanjeer (1973)
Genres: Action
Director: Prakash Mehra
Main Cast: Amitabh Bachchan, Jaya Bachchan, Pran, Om Prakash, Ajit Khan
Keywords: bollywood

Overview: The world erodes around Vijay, an honest police officer. He is jailed for 6 months on false charges, trapped by gang leader Teja. When Vijay is released from jail, he plans to take revenge.
