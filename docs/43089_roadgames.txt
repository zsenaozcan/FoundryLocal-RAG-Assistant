Title: Roadgames (1981)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Mystery
Director: Richard Franklin
Main Cast: Stacy Keach, Jamie Lee Curtis, Marion Edward, Grant Page, Thaddeus Smith
Keywords: australia, truck, suspense, serial killer, hitchhiker, truck driver, ozploitation
