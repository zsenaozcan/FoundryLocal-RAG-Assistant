Title: Little Fauss and Big Halsy (1970)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sidney J. Furie
Main Cast: Robert Redford, Michael J. Pollard, Lauren Hutton, Noah Beery, Jr., Lucille Benson
Keywords: motor sport, motorcycle

Overview: A comedy/drama about an unlikely pair who share a passion for motocross. Robert Redford is the womanising but shallow star while in the end the unlikely Little Faus (Micheal J.Pollard) shows that depth can count.
