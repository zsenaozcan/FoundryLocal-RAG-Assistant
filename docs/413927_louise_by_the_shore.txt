Title: Louise by the Shore (2016)
Genres: Animation
Director: Jean-François Laguionie
Main Cast: Dominique Frot, Jean-François Laguionie, Diane Dassigny, Tom Anthony

Overview: On the last day of summer, Louise, an old woman realizes that the last train has departed without her. She finds herself alone in a small seaside resort town, abandoned by everyone. The weather quickly turns for the worse followed by the seasonal tides. Fragile and coquettish, not nearly as well-prepared as a would-be Robinson Crusoe, Louise isn’t likely to make it through the winter. Yet, Louise takes her abandonment as a challenge. She’s going to survive, confronting the elements as well as her memories, which have found the perfect occasion to join in the adventure…
