Title: Rikki-Tikki-Tavi (1975)
Genres: Animation
Director: Chuck Jones
Main Cast: Orson Welles, Les Tremayne, June Foray, Michael LeClair, Shepard Menken

Overview: Rikki is a young mongoose who is adopted by a human family after nearly drowning in the river. He returns the favour by protecting them from two murderous cobra.
