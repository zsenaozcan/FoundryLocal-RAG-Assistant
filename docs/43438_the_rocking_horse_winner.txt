Title: The Rocking Horse Winner (1949)
Genres: Drama
Director: Anthony Pelissier
Main Cast: Valerie Hobson, John Howard Davies, Ronald Squire, John Mills, Hugh Sinclair
Keywords: gambling, horse race, horse, rocking horse

Overview: A strange and ultimately tragic tale of a young boy who learns how to pick winners at the racetrack by riding his rocking horse to aid his parents out of their endless round of debts.
