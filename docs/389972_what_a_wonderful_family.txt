Title: What a Wonderful Family! (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Yôji Yamada
Main Cast: Isao Hashizume, Yu Aoi, Satoshi Tsumabuki, Tsurube Shôfukutei

Overview: A husband (Isao Hashizume) and wife (Kazuko Yoshiyuki) have been married for 50 years. For her birthday, the husband asks the wife what she wants for her birthday present. She replies that she wants a divorce. The wife's divorce announcement sends the entire family into chaos.
