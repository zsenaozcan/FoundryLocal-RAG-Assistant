Title: Skinwalkers (2002)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Chris Eyre
Main Cast: Adam Beach, Wes Studi, Harrison Lowe, James Dalgam, Saginaw Grant

Overview: Faced with the murder of three medicine men, Navajo police must find the culprit. That the murders appear to be the work of a Skinwalker, or bad medicine man, complicate and illuminate the detective's work.
