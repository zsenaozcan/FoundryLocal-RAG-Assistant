Title: A Chinese Ghost Story (1987)
Genres: Fantasy, Action
Director: Ching Siu-Tung
Main Cast: Leslie Cheung, Joey Wong, Wu Ma, Lau Siu-Ming, Lam Wai
Keywords: horror, rescue, elope, monsters of the wild, hell storming, spirit

Overview: Ning Tsai-Shen, a humble tax collector, arrives in a small town to carry out his work. No one is willing to give him shelter for the night, so he ends up in the haunted Lan Ro temple. There, he meets Taoist Swordsman Yen Che-Hsia, and the beautiful Nieh Hsiao-Tsing, with whom he falls in love.
