Title: Rollerball (1975)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Science Fiction
Director: Norman Jewison
Main Cast: James Caan, John Houseman, Maud Adams, John Beck, Moses Gunn
Keywords: sports team, future, dystopia, games, rollerskating, future sport

Overview: In a corporate-controlled future, an ultra-violent sport known as Rollerball represents the world, and one of its powerful athletes is out to defy those who want him out of the game.
