Title: Night People (1954)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Nunnally Johnson
Main Cast: Gregory Peck, Broderick Crawford, Anita Björk, Rita Gam, Walter Abel
Keywords: germany, russian, intelligence officer

Overview: A US intelligence officer, stationed in Germany, is caught in a political dilemma when the Russians kidnap a young Army private, the son of prominent American businessman. In exchange for the soldier's return, the Russians attempt to barter a trade for an elderly German couple who they want for treason.
