Title: The Barchester Chronicles (1982)
Genres: Unknown
Director: David Giles
Main Cast: Donald Pleasence, Janet Maw, Nigel Hawthorne, Angela Pleasence, Barbara Flynn

Overview: When a crusade against the Church of England's practice of self-enrichment misfires, scandal taints the cosy community of Barchester when their local church becomes the object of a scathing, investigative report.
