Title: The Traveling Executioner (1970)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Western
Director: Jack Smight
Main Cast: Stacy Keach, Bud Cort, Marianna Hill, Graham Jarvis, James Sloyan

Overview: Jonas Candide performs his job as state executioner in early 20th century Mississippi like a combination preacher and carnival barker, persuading condemned men to accept their deaths before electrocuting them on his electric chair. After he's assigned his first woman to execute, however, Jonas' sense of purpose is shaken.
