Title: Evil Angel (2009)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Richard Dutcher
Main Cast: Ving Rhames, Bart Johnson, Ava Gaudet, Kristopher Shepard, Richard Dutcher
Keywords: lilith, fallen angel

Overview: Set in present day Chicago, the ancient avenging demon Lilith, attempts to destroy a young paramedic's life while destroying anyone who interferes with her plan.
