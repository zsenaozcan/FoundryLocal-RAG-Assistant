Title: Man on the Train (2002)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Patrice Leconte
Main Cast: Jean Rochefort, Johnny Hallyday, Pascal Parmentier, Isabelle Petit-Jacques, Edith Scob
Keywords: poet, suspense, gangster

Overview: A man, Milan (played by Johnny Hallyday) steps off a train, into a small French village. As he waits for the day when he will rob the town bank, he runs into an old retired poetry teacher named M. Manesquier (Jean Rochefort). The two men strike up a strange friendship and explore the road not taken, each wanting to live the other's life.
