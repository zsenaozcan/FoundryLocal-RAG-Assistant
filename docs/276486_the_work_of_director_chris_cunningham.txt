Title: The Work of Director Chris Cunningham (2003)
Genres: Music
Director: Chris Cunningham
Main Cast: Fiona Maclaine, Carl Antolin, Richard D. James, Sigtryggur Baldursson, Robert Ball

Overview: British director Chris Cunningham has drawn acclaim for groundbreaking music videos for the likes of Autechre, Aphex Twin, Squarepusher, Bjork, and Portishead, his award-winning commercials, and his risky short films. The Work of Director Chris Cunningham is the second release in Palm's Directors Label Series.
