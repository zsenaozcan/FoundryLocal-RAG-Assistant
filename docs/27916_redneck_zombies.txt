Title: Redneck Zombies (1989)
Genres: Horror
Director: Pericles Lewnes
Main Cast: Lisa M. DeHaven, William E. Benson, William Decker, Tyrone Taylor, James H. Housely

Overview: A barrel of radioactive waste is lost out in the woods. Some demented rednecks find it and use it as part of their still. Everybody who drinks from the liquor they produced turns into a zombie.
