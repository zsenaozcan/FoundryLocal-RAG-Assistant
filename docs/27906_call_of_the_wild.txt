Title: Call of the Wild (2009)
Genres: Adventure
Director: Richard Gabai
Main Cast: Christopher Lloyd, Ariel Gade, Kameron Knox, Timothy Bottoms, Devon Graye
Keywords: husky, snow, family holiday

Overview: Self-centered and status-conscious, ten year old Ryann Hale journeys to Montana to visit her down-to-earth grandpa, Bill Hale. Ryann bonds with a wolf-dog, Buck, and schemes to save him from a cruel dogsledder.
