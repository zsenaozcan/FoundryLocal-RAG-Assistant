Title: The Jane Austen Book Club (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Robin Swicord
Main Cast: Kathy Baker, Maria Bello, Emily Blunt, Amy Brenneman, Hugh Dancy
Keywords: hippie, infidelity, literature, book, older woman seduces younger guy, jane austen, book club, single, teacher, mother daughter relationship

Overview: Six Californians start a club to discuss the works of Jane Austen. As they delve into Austen's literature, the club members find themselves dealing with life experiences that parallel the themes of the books they are reading.
