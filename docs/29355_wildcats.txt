Title: Wildcats (1986)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Michael Ritchie
Main Cast: Goldie Hawn, Swoosie Kurtz, Woody Harrelson, Wesley Snipes, Robyn Lively
Keywords: american football, sport, coach, the big game

Overview: Molly is a high school track coach who knows just as much about football as anyone else on the planet. When a football coach's position becomes vacant, she applies for the job, despite snickers from fellow staff members and her former husband.
