Title: La Haine (1995)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mathieu Kassovitz
Main Cast: Vincent Cassel, Hubert Koundé, Saïd Taghmaoui, Abdel Ahmed Ghili, Souleymane Dicko
Keywords: paris, neo-nazi, skinhead, robbery, police brutality, hip-hop, gun, socially deprived family, breakdance, arabian

Overview: Aimlessly whiling away their days in the concrete environs of their dead-end suburbia, Vinz, Hubert, and Said -- a Jew, African, and an Arab -- give human faces to France's immigrant populations, their bristling resentment at their social marginalization slowly simmering until it reaches a climactic boiling point. La Haine means Hate.
