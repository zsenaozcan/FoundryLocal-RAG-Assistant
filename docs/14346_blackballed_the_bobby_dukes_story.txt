Title: Blackballed: The Bobby Dukes Story (2004)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Brant Sersen
Main Cast: Rob Corddry, Paul Scheer, Rob Riggle, Rob Huebel, Dannah Feinglass Phirman

Overview: Caught cheating and banned for ten years, Bobby Dukes, paintball's first superstar, returns to reclaim his title and erase the memory of his tainted past.
