Title: How Sweet It Is! (1968)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jerry Paris
Main Cast: James Garner, Debbie Reynolds, Maurice Ronet, Terry-Thomas, Paul Lynde
Keywords: hippie, photographer, lawyer

Overview: All-American couple who try to bridge the generation gap with their free-spirited son on a trip, frisky business and misunderstandings galore ensue, all funny, vibrant and charming.
