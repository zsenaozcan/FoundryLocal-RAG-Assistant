Title: An Eye for an Eye (1981)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Crime
Director: Steve Carver
Main Cast: Chuck Norris, Christopher Lee, Richard Roundtree, Matt Clark, Mako
Keywords: undercover, fight, police, drug, death

Overview: Sean Kane is forced to resign from the San Francisco Police Department's Narcotics Division when he goes berserk after his partner is murdered. He decides to fight alone and follows a trail of drug traffickers into unexpected high places.
