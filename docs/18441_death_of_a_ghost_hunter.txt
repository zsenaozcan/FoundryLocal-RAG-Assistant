Title: Death of a Ghost Hunter (2007)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Sean Tretta
Main Cast: Patti Tindall, Mike Marsh, Davina Joy, Lindsay Page
Keywords: knife, ghost, blood on wall, supernatural investigation, evp, mysterious photo, menstrual blood

Overview: Renowned "ghost hunter", Carter Simms is paid to conduct a paranormal investigation of a supposedly haunted house. Along with a cameraman, a reporter, and a spiritual advocate, she embarks on a three night journey into terror.
