Title: Gamera vs. Jiger (1970)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Science Fiction
Director: Noriaki Yuasa
Main Cast: Tsutomu Takakuwa, Kelly Varis, Katherine Murphy, Kon Ohmura, Ryô Hayami
Keywords: japan, submarine, monster, turtle, sequel, giant monster, outer space, kaiju, gamera, statue

Overview: A giant creature attacks Japan during the World Fair and its up to Gamera to stop it. But the monster injects Gamera with its offspring and a research team must take a craft into Gamera's body to eliminate the parasite.
