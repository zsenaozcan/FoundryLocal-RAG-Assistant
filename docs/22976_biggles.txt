Title: Biggles (1986)
Genres: Action, Family, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: John Hough
Main Cast: Neil Dickson, Alex Hyde-White, Fiona Hutchison, Peter Cushing, Marcus Gilbert
Keywords: world war i, time travel, twin brother, fighter ace

Overview: Unassuming catering salesmen Jim Ferguson falls through a time hole to 1917 where he saves the life of dashing Royal Flying Corps pilot James "Biggles" Bigglesworth after his photo recon mission is shot down. Before he can work out what has happened, Jim is zapped back to the 1980s......
