Title: Kate Plays Christine (2016)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Robert Greene
Main Cast: Kate Lyn Sheil, Dr. Steven C. Bovio, Stephanie Coatney, Michael Ray Davis, Zachary Gossett
Keywords: suicide, live television

Overview: Follow actress Kate Lyn Sheil as she prepares for her next role: playing Christine Chubbuck, a Florida newscaster who committed suicide live on-air in 1974. As Kate investigates Chubbuck’s story, uncovering new clues and information, she becomes increasingly obsessed with her subject.
