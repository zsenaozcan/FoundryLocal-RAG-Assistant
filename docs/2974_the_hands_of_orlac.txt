Title: The Hands of Orlac (1924)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Horror
Director: Robert Wiene
Main Cast: Conrad Veidt, Alexandra Sorina, Fritz Kortner, Carmen Cartellieri, Fritz Strassny
Keywords: murder, pianist, transplant, silent film, torment, train wreck, psychological terror

Overview: A world-famous pianist loses both hands in an accident. When new hands are grafted on, he is horrified to learn they once belonged to a murderer.
