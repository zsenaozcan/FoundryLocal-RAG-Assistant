Title: Naked Under the Moon (1999)
Genres: Foreign, Romance, Drama
Director: Lav Diaz
Main Cast: Klaudia Koronel, Elizabeth Oropesa, Julio Diaz, Isabel Granada, Mike Magat
Keywords: philippines, pinoy, sex party

Overview: A young woman recalls how her father (a fallen priest), her mother (a woman with a secret past) and her teenage sister returned with her to live in their ancestral home after the family business failed. She was plagued with mysterious problems of sleepwalking and began a romance with a young man who tried to cure her.
