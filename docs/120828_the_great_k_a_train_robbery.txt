Title: The Great K&A Train Robbery (1926)
Genres: Western
Director: Lewis Seiler
Main Cast: Tom Mix, Dorothy Dwan, Will Walling, Harry Gripp, Carl Miller
Keywords: silent film, train robbery

Overview: Cullen has hired Tom to try and stop the robberies on his railroad. Knowing Cullen's secretary Holt is tipping off the gang, Tom works undercover by posing as a highwayman. To help him bring in the gang he enlists the help of the hobo DeLuxe Harry.
