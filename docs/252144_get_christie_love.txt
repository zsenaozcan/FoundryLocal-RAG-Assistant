Title: Get Christie Love! (1974)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Drama, TV Movie
Director: William A. Graham
Main Cast: Teresa Graves, Harry Guardino, Louise Sorel

Overview: Get Christie Love! is a 1974 made-for-television film starring Teresa Graves as an undercover female police detective who is determined to overthrow a drug ring. This film is based on Dorothy Uhnak's crime-thriller novel, The Ledger.
