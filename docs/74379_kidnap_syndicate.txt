Title: Kidnap Syndicate (1975)
Genres: Crime
Director: Fernando Di Leo
Main Cast: Luc Merenda, James Mason, Marino Masé, Irina Maleeva, Daniele Dublino
Keywords: kidnapping

Overview: After his son is kidnapped, a millionaire industrialist (James Mason) seeks revenge, in spite of the potential danger that his rash actions will bring about for his child and another kidnapped boy, the son of a poor mechanic (Luc Merenda).
