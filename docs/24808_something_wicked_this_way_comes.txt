Title: Something Wicked This Way Comes (1983)
Genres: Fantasy, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Jack Clayton
Main Cast: Jason Robards, Jonathan Pryce, Diane Ladd, Royal Dano, Vidal Peterson
Keywords: circus, witch, based on novel, lightning, dream, maze, elephant, clown, carnival, spider

Overview: In a small American town, a diabolical circus arrives, granting wishes for the townsfolk, but twisted as only the esteemed Mr. Dark can make them. Can two young boys overcome the worst the devil himself can deal out?
