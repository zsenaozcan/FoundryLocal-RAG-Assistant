Title: The Tale of Zatôichi Continues (1962)
Genres: Adventure, Action
Director: Kazuo Mori
Main Cast: Shintarô Katsu, Yaeko Mizutani, Masayo Banri, Tomisaburō Wakayama, Yutaka Nakamura
Keywords: japan, japanese, samurai, japanese mafia, sequel, sword fight, zatoichi, criminal

Overview: Returning to the village where a year before he had killed Hirate, a much-admired opponent, Zatoichi encounters another swordsman and former rival in love: his own brother. He must face in combat not only the pursuing retainers but his own flesh-and-blood.
