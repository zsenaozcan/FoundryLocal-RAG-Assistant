Title: Inuyasha the Movie 2: The Castle Beyond the Looking Glass (2002)
Genres: Action, Animation, Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Toshiya Shinohara
Main Cast: Francesco Pezzulli, Maria Letizia Scifoni, Fabio Boccanera
Keywords: anime, inuyasha

Overview: With their most formidable foe vanquished, Inuyasha and his comrades begin returning to their everyday lives. But their peace is fleeting as another adversary emerges: Kaguya, the self-proclaimed princess from the Moon of Legend, hatches a plot to plunge the world into an eternal night of the full moon. Inuyasha, Kagome, Miroku, Sango and Shippou must reunite to confront the new menace.
