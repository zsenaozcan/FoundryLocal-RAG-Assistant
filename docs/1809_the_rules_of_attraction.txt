Title: The Rules of Attraction (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Roger Avary
Main Cast: James Van Der Beek, Shannyn Sossamon, Ian Somerhalder, Jessica Biel, Kate Bosworth
Keywords: love triangle, independent film, multiple storylines

Overview: The incredibly spoiled and overprivileged students of Camden College are a backdrop for an unusual love triangle between a drug dealer, a virgin and a bisexual classmate.
