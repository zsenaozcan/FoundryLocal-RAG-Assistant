Title: Lonesome (1928)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Pál Fejős
Main Cast: Barbara Kent, Glenn Tryon, Fay Holderness, Gusztáv Pártos, Eddie Phillips
Keywords: loneliness, amusement park

Overview: Two lonely people in the big city meet and enjoy the thrills of an amusement park, only to lose each other in the crowd after spending a great day together. Will they ever see each other again?
