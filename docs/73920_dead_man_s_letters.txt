Title: Dead Man's Letters (1986)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Konstantin Lopushanskiy
Main Cast: Rolan Bykov, Iosif Ryklin, Aleksandr Sabinin, Nora Gryakalova
Keywords: post-apocalyptic, strugatsky, nuclear holocaust

Overview: In a world after the nuclear apocalypse a scholar helps a small group of children and adults survive, staying with them in the basement of the former museum of history. In his mind he writes letters to his son — though it is obvious that they will never be read.
