Title: A Haunting at Silver Falls (2013)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Brett Donowho
Main Cast: Steve Bacic, Tara Westwood, Tadhg Kelly, James Cavlo, Alix Elizabeth Gitter
Keywords: twins, justice, ghost, false conviction

Overview: A small town is haunted by the twin daughters of a wrongfully convicted man. Young Jordan is sent down the path to who the real killer is only to find the killer is very close to her.
