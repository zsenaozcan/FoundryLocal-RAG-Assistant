Title: Pokémon the Movie: Kyurem vs. the Sword of Justice (2012)
Genres: Animation
Director: Kunihiko Yuyama
Main Cast: Jason Griffith, Rica Matsumoto, Vic Mignogna, Mamoru Miyano, Sarah Natochenny

Overview: The movie features Ash and his two friends Iris and Cilan, as they help a Keldeo battle against a powerful Dragon-type Pokémon named Kyurem as his test to become a Sacred Swordsman.
