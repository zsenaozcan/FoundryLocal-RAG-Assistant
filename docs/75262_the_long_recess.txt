Title: The Long Recess (1972)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Aleksei Korenev
Main Cast: Mikhail Kononov, Evgeni Leonov, Aleksandr Zbruev, Rolan Bykov, Svetlana Kryuchkova

Overview: A young teacher goes to a school for adults. He is younger than many of his students and some of them are starting to miss school.
