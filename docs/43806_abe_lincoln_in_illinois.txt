Title: Abe Lincoln in Illinois (1940)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: John Cromwell
Main Cast: Raymond Massey, Gene Lockhart, Ruth Gordon, Mary Howard, Minor Watson
Keywords: biography, historical figure, abraham lincoln

Overview: Abe Lincoln in Illinois is a 1940 biographical film which tells the story of the life of Abraham Lincoln from his departure from Kentucky until his election as President of the United States.
