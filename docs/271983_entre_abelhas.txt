Title: Entre Abelhas (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Ian SBF
Main Cast: Fábio Porchat, Irene Ravache, Marcos Veras, Marcelo Valle, Leticia Lima
Keywords: blindness and impaired vision, failing marriage

Overview: Bruno is a young film editor who has just broke up his marriage with Regina, and returned living in his mother's house. Drowned in deep sorrow, something very weird happens in his life: people around him are gradually disappearing. But only for him. Some kind of blindness.
