Title: Low Fidelity (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Devon Gummersall
Main Cast: Ed Quinn, Meredith Monroe, Gale Harold, Lauren Stamile, Elaine Hendrix

Overview: A dramedy about relationships and fidelity, or the lack thereof. A group of old high school friends, now in their late thirties, get together for the weekend to celebrate the 4th of July, for better or for worse.
