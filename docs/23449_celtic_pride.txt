Title: Celtic Pride (1996)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Tom DeCerchio
Main Cast: Damon Wayans, Daniel Stern, Christopher McDonald, Gail O'Grady, Paul Guilfoyle
Keywords: boston, irish-american, kidnapping, sport, basketball, boston celtics

Overview: The National Basketball Association play-offs are taking place, and the Boston Celtics are facing the Utah Jazz in a crucial series. This is also the last year that any games will be played in the Boston Garden, which is going to be torn down after the end of the season. Mike and Jimmy, two die-hard sports fans who will risk anything for the Celtics to win, are really getting into the games...
