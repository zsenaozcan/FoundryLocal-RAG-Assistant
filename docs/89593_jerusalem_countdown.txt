Title: Jerusalem Countdown (2011)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Harold Cronk
Main Cast: David A.R. White, Randy Travis, Lee Majors, Stacy Keach, Jaci Velasquez
Keywords: christian

Overview: When nuclear weapons are smuggled into America, FBI Agent Shane Daughtry (David A.R White) is faced with an impossible task -- find them before they are detonated. The clock is ticking and the only people who can help are a washed up arms dealer (Lee Majors), a converted Israeli Mossad Agent (Stacy Keach) and a by- the-book CIA Deputy Director (Randy Travis).
