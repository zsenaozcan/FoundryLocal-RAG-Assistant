Title: Saving Private Ryan (1998)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Steven Spielberg
Main Cast: Tom Hanks, Matt Damon, Vin Diesel, Tom Sizemore, Barry Pepper
Keywords: war crimes, self sacrifice, war veteran, world war ii, war ship, airplane, bravery, normandy, parachute, troops
