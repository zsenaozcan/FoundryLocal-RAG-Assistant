Title: The Coven (2015)
Genres: Horror, Fantasy
Director: John Mackie
Main Cast: Dexter Fletcher, Cloe Mackie, Holly Mackie, Tony O'Callaghan, Howard Lee

Overview: In Highgate London, five young teenagers hoping to witness Pagan activity, camp in historic Queen's Wood on Halloween but soon discover they are part of the ritual when a mysterious Biker hunts them down.
