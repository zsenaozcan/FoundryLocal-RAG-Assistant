Title: Tenderness (2009)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: John Polson
Main Cast: Russell Crowe, Jon Foster, Sophie Traub, Laura Dern, Alicia Harding

Overview: Russell Crowe stars as Detective Cristofuoro, a semi-retired police officer who is trying to unravel the past to discover whether a violent teenager, Eric Poole (Jon Foster), was responsible for the murder of his family. Sophie Traub co-stars as Lori, the teenage runaway who falls under Eric's spell. Based on the novel by Robert Cormier.
