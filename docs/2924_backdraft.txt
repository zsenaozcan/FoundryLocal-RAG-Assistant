Title: Backdraft (1991)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Ron Howard
Main Cast: Kurt Russell, William Baldwin, Robert De Niro, Donald Sutherland, Jennifer Jason Leigh
Keywords: brother brother relationship, corruption, chicago, fire, office, firemen, danger of life, investigation, psychopath, rescue

Overview: They say a blast of flames can take a life ... and hide a secret. But now firemen brothers Brian and Stephen McCaffrey are battling each other over past slights while trying to stop an arsonist with a diabolical agenda from torching Chicago.
