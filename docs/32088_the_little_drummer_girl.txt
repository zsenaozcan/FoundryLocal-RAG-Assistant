Title: The Little Drummer Girl (1984)
Genres: Thriller
Director: George Roy Hill
Main Cast: Diane Keaton, Klaus Kinski, Sami Frey, Eli Danker, Ben Levine
Keywords: palestinian, american

Overview: An American Actress with a penchant for lying is forceably recruited by Mosad, the Israeli intelligence agency to trap a Palestinian bomber, by pretending to be the girlfriend of his dead brother.
