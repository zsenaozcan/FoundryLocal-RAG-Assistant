Title: My Old Classmate (2014)
Genres: Romance
Director: Frant Gwo
Main Cast: Zhou Dongyu, Lin Gengxin, Mike Sui, Zhang Zi-Feng

Overview: In the summer of 1993, timid and shy Zhou Xiao Zhi meets her table partner. Sitting in the classroom’s infamous back row is troublemaker Lin Yi. They go through junior high, high school, and college years together. Lin Yi said to Zhou Xiaozhi: "One day, I’ll take you to a place that only belongs to us." Fast forward ten years...
