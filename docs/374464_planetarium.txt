Title: Planetarium (2016)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Mystery
Director: Rebecca Zlotowski
Main Cast: Natalie Portman, Lily-Rose Depp, Emmanuel Salinger, Amira Casar, Pierre Salvadori
Keywords: sister sister relationship, ghost, woman director

Overview: Follows the journey of sisters who are believed to possess the supernatural ability to connect with ghosts. They cross paths with a visionary French producer while performing in Paris.
