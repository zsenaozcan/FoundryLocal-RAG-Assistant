Title: Lassie (2005)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Family
Director: Charles Sturridge
Main Cast: Peter O'Toole, Samantha Morton, John Lynch, Peter Dinklage, Jonathan Mason
Keywords: ritual, train ride, rescue, little girl, collie dog, lassie

Overview: A family in financial crisis is forced to sell Lassie, their beloved dog. Hundreds of miles away from her true family, Lassie escapes and sets out on a journey home.
