Title: Gremlins (1984)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Comedy
Director: Joe Dante
Main Cast: Zach Galligan, Phoebe Cates, Hoyt Axton, Polly Holliday, Dick Miller
Keywords: holiday, monster, small town, department store, bars and restaurants, human animal relationship, pet, sunlight, chain saw, salesperson
