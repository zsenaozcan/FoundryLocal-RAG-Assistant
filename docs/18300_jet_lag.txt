Title: Jet Lag (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Danièle Thompson
Main Cast: Jean Reno, Juliette Binoche, Sergi López, Scali Delpeyrat, Karine Belly
Keywords: romantic comedy, woman director

Overview: At Charles de Gaulle Airport in Paris, a beautician on her way to a new job in Mexico accidentally meets a cook who is on his way back from America. Labor strikes, bad weather, and pure luck cause the two of them to share a room overnight at the airport Hilton hotel. Will their initial mutual indifference and downright hostility turn into a one night stand or perhaps something more?
