Title: Black Scorpion (1995)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Jonathan Winfrey
Main Cast: Joan Severance, Bruce Abbott, Rick Rossovich, Stephen Lee, Garrett Morris
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Darcy is a cop who is also a supehero named Black Scorpion at night who kicks and beats evildoers to a pulp. She soon catches wind of an asthmatic mad scientist who plans on tainting the city's air supply with a toxin. Only Darcy in her superhero garb can stop him with the assistance of a petty thief named Argyle.
