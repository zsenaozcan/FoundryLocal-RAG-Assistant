Title: Ed Hardy: Tattoo the World (2010)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Emiko Omori
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Ed Hardy is emblazoned on clothing worn by Madonna, Bruce Springsteen and Mick Jagger, on wine and dozens of other products. Now you'll meet the real Ed Hardy, the godfather of modern tattooing and artist extraordinaire who gave up a promising career in the fine arts to pursue his childhood obsession: tattoos.
