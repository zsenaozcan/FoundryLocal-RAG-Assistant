Title: Mongol: The Rise of Genghis Khan (2007)
Genres: History
Director: Sergei Bodrov
Main Cast: Tadanobu Asano, Sun Honglei, Khulan Chuluun, Aliya, Ba Sen
Keywords: mongolia, genghis khan

Overview: The story recounts the early life of Genghis Khan, a slave who went on to conquer half the world in the 11th century.
