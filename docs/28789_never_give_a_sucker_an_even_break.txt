Title: Never Give a Sucker an Even Break (1941)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Edward F. Cline
Main Cast: W.C. Fields, Gloria Jean, Leon Errol, Billy Lenhart, Kenneth Brown
Keywords: musical, niece, car chase, uncle niece relationship, maternity ward

Overview: Never Give a Sucker an Even Break is a 1941 film about a man who wants to sell a film story to Esoteric Studios. On the way he gets insulted by little boys, beat up for ogling a woman, and abused by a waitress. W. C. Fields' last starring role in a feature-length film.
