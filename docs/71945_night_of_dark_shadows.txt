Title: Night of Dark Shadows (1971)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller, Mystery, Romance, Foreign
Director: Dan Curtis
Main Cast: David Selby, Grayson Hall, Kate Jackson, Lara Parker, John Karlen
