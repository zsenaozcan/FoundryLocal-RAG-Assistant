Title: Halloweentown High (2004)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Family, Fantasy, TV Movie
Director: Mark A.Z. Dippé
Main Cast: Debbie Reynolds, Joey Zimmerman, Emily Roeske, Judith Hoag, Kimberly J. Brown
Keywords: holiday, witch, magic, halloween, sorcery, kids and family, disney channel

Overview: Marnie Piper prepares to begin a new school year, she asks the Halloweentown Hot Witches' Council to work toward openness between Halloweentown and the mortal world. She proposes to bring a group of Halloweentown students to her own high school in the mortal world.
