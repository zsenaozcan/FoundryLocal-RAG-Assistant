Title: Night of the Demons (1988)
Genres: Horror
Director: Kevin Tenney
Main Cast: Cathy Podewell, Alvin Alexis, Amelia Kinkade, Linnea Quigley, Hal Havins
Keywords: halloween, possession, demon, celebration

Overview: While conducting a seance during a Halloween party, high-school seniors unlock the demon that remains locked in the crematorium.
