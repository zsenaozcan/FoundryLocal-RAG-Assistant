Title: Babysitters Beware (2009)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Family
Director: Douglas Horn
Main Cast: Trenton Rogers, Rico Rodriguez, Brittany Finamore, Kate Orsini, Danny Trejo
Keywords: babysitter, family relationships, young boy

Overview: Seven-year-old Danny Parker is a good kid who loves his parents. But they have to go out on business dinners all the time, leaving him stuck with a sitter. It's not so bad when it's his favorite babysitter, Janelle but he also gets stuck with sitters like Ms. Greene who won't let him have any fun. Danny just wants to spend more time with his Mom and Dad.
