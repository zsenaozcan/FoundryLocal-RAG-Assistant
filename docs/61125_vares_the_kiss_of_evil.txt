Title: Vares - The Kiss of Evil (2011)
Genres: Action, Crime
Director: Anders Engström
Main Cast: Antti Reini, Eppu Salminen, Matti Onnismaa, Jasper Pääkkönen, Mikko Leppilampi

Overview: A young college student is found stabbed to death,and private detective Vares life takes an interesting turn when the girls family asks him to investigate the case.
