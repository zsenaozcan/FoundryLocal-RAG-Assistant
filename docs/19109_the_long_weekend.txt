Title: The Long Weekend (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Pat Holden
Main Cast: Chris Klein, Brendan Fehr, Chandra West, Cobie Smulders, Paul Campbell

Overview: Cooper is an actor who sees life as one big party, while Ed is in advertising and takes life too seriously. When Ed gets stressed over a deadline he has to meet, his brother Cooper works hard in trying to get his brother hooked up with a girl, thus a long weekend of stress and beautiful women, culminating in Ed meeting, and making love to, the woman of his dreams.
