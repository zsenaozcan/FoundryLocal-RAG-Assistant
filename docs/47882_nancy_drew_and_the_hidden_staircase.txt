Title: Nancy Drew and the Hidden Staircase (1939)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Mystery
Director: William Clemens
Main Cast: Bonita Granville, Frankie Thomas, John Litel, Frank Orth, Renie Riano
Keywords: chauffeur, evidence, murder, estate, nancy drew

Overview: Nancy Drew and Ted Nickerson solve a murder and save two elderly ladies from losing their family home. The old mansion is complete with a moving wall and a hidden staircase.
