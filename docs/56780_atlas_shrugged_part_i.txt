Title: Atlas Shrugged Part I (2011)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Science Fiction, Mystery
Director: Paul Johansson
Main Cast: Taylor Schilling, Paul Johansson, Michael O'Keefe, Grant Bowler, Edi Gathegi
Keywords: recession, strike, coercion, business, motorcycle, trilogy, force, ayn rand

Overview: A powerful railroad executive, Dagny Taggart, struggles to keep her business alive while society is crumbling around her. Based on the 1957 novel by Ayn Rand.
