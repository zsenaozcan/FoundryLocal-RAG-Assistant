Title: Advantageous (2015)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama, Family
Director: Jennifer Phang
Main Cast: Jacqueline Kim, James Urbaniak, Freya Adams, Ken Jeong, Jennifer Ehle
Keywords: identity, unemployment, dystopic future, woman director, corporation, mind transfer

Overview: In a near-future city where soaring opulence overshadows economic hardship, Gwen and her daughter, Jules, do all they can to hold on to their joy, despite the instability surfacing in their world.
