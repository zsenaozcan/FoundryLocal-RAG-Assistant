Title: Ventoux (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nicole van Kilsdonk
Main Cast: Abbey Hoes, Maruschka Detmers, Jonas Smulders, Wim Opbrouck, Kasper van Kooten
Keywords: reunion, bike riding, woman director

Overview: A group of friends reunite after 30 years to climb the Mont Ventoux again. Memories to a happy youth but also new discoveries from the past. One person is not present at the reunion. Why is that? What happened?
