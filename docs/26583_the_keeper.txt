Title: The Keeper (2009)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Thriller
Director: Keoni Waxman
Main Cast: Steven Seagal, Luce Rains, Liezl Carstens, Kisha Sierra, Arron Shiver
Keywords: texas, boxer, bodyguard, ex-cop, night club

Overview: Roland Sallinger is an LA cop who after nearly being killed by his greedy partner, and eventually being forced to retire for medical reasons, flees to San Antonio, Texas, after being asked to work as a bodyguard for the daughter of a wealthy businessman.
