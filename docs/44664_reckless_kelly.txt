Title: Reckless Kelly (1994)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Yahoo Serious
Main Cast: Yahoo Serious, Melora Hardin, Alexei Sayle, Hugo Weaving, John Pinette
Keywords: bank robber, australia, motorcycle

Overview: A satirical take on a modern day Ned Kelly who is forced to Hollywood in order to make enough money to save his family's land. As it goes against his belief, he cannot simply rob banks for his own benefit (all money goes to the poor). Ned is forced to find another way to come up with the $1 million required to save his family island.
