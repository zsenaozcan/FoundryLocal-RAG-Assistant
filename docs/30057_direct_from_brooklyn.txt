Title: Direct from Brooklyn (1999)
Genres: Music
Director: Adam Bernstein
Main Cast: John Flansburgh, John Linnell
Keywords: music video

Overview: Direct from Brooklyn is a compilation of music videos by American alternative rock group They Might Be Giants. It was released on VHS in 1999 and DVD in 2003. The title refers to the home of John Linnell and John Flansburgh, founding members of the band. Many of the music videos were filmed in Brooklyn and other parts of New York City.
