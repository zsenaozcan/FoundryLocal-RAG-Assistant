Title: Under the Stars (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Félix Viscarret
Main Cast: Alberto San Juan, Emma Suárez, Julián Villagrán, Luz Valdenebro, Violeta Rodríguez

Overview: Selfish, opportunistic Benito returns to his home in Navarre when he learns that his father is dying. There, he reconnects with his younger brother Lalo, a metal sculptor who is emotionally needy. Benito also develops a surprising bond with Ainara, the young daughter of his brother's girlfriend, Nines.
