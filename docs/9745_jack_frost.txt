Title: Jack Frost (1998)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Troy Miller
Main Cast: Michael Keaton, Kelly Preston, Mark Addy, Joseph Cross, Henry Rollins
Keywords: holiday, loss of lover, christmas party, loss of father, loss, snowman

Overview: A father, who can't keep his promises, dies in a car accident. One year later, he returns as a snowman, who has the final chance to put things right with his son before he is gone forever.
