Title: Ten Seconds to Hell (1959)
Genres: Romance, Drama, War, Action
Director: Robert Aldrich
Main Cast: Jack Palance, Jeff Chandler, Martine Carol, Robert Cornthwaite, Dave Willock
Keywords: prisoners of war, ex soldier

Overview: Two rivals (Jeff Chandler, Jack Palance) from a German bomb squad are left to deactivate duds in postwar Berlin.
