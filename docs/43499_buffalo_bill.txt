Title: Buffalo Bill (1943)
Genres: Drama, Action, Western
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: Joel McCrea, Maureen O'Hara, Linda Darnell, Thomas Mitchell, Edgar Buchanan
Keywords: historical fiction, buffalo bill

Overview: Scout William F. Cody (Joel McCrea) marries a U.S. senator's daughter (Maureen O'Hara), fights the Cheyenne and leads a Wild West show.
