Title: Watch Your Left (1936)
Genres: Comedy
Director: René Clément
Main Cast: Jacques Tati, Max Martell, Louis Robur, Cliville, Jean Aurel

Overview: Roger, son of a farmer, wants to be a boxer, and gets his chance by filling in for a boxer's sparring partner. However, Roger does not know how to box and reads a rule book while in the ring.
