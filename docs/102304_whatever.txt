Title: Whatever (1998)
Genres: Drama
Director: Susan Skoog
Main Cast: Liza Weil, Chad Morgan, Frederic Forrest, Kathryn Rossetter, Marc Riffon
Keywords: sex, new jersey, teenager, drug, woman director

Overview: A teen faces her impending adulthood in the carefree sex and drug revolution years of the early 80's prior to fears of AIDS. She lives with her bitter, divorced mother and her sadistic kid brother. Looking forward to a life based on her passion for art, she nevertheless gets in with the partying crowd which causes her to miss deadlines and poor grades which jeopardizes her future. Ultimately she ends up losing her virginity to an older artist wanna-be who she has had a longstanding crush on.
