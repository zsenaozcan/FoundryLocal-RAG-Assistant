Title: Fort Saganne (1984)
Genres: War, Drama, History
Director: Alain Corneau
Main Cast: Gérard Depardieu, Philippe Noiret, Catherine Deneuve, Sophie Marceau, Michel Duchaussoy
Keywords: africa, nurse, world war ii, feminism, world war i, bigamy, sahara, love, revenge, slaughter
