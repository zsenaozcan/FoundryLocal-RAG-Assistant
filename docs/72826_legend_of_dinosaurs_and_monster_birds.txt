Title: Legend of Dinosaurs and Monster Birds (1977)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Junji Kurata
Main Cast: Nobiko Sawa, Shôtarô Hayashi, Tomoko Kiyoshima, Fuyukichi Maki, Tsunehiko Watase
Keywords: dinosaur

Overview: A Japanese geologist discovers that dinosaurs still exist in caves beneath the surface of a volcanic lake.
