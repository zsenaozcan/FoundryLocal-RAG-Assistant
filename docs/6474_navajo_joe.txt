Title: Navajo Joe (1966)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Sergio Corbucci
Main Cast: Burt Reynolds, Aldo Sambrell, Nicoletta Machiavelli, Fernando Rey, Tanya Lopert
Keywords: revenge, native american, spaghetti western
