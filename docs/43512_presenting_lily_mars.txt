Title: Presenting Lily Mars (1943)
Genres: Music, Romance
Director: Norman Taurog
Main Cast: Judy Garland, Van Heflin, Fay Bainter, Richard Carlson, Spring Byington
Keywords: musical, times square, small town girl

Overview: Starstruck Indiana small-town girl Lily is pestering theatrical producer John Thornway for a role but he is reluctant.
