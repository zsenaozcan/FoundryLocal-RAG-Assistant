Title: Hitler: The Last Ten Days (1973)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, War, History
Director: Ennio De Concini
Main Cast: Alec Guinness, Simon Ward, Adolfo Celi, Diane Cilento, Gabriele Ferzetti
Keywords: suicide, bunker, nazis, biography, nazism

Overview: Hitler: The Last Ten Days takes us into the depths of der Furher’s Berlin bunker during his final days. Based on the book by Gerhard Boldt, it provides a bleak look at the goings-on within, and without.
