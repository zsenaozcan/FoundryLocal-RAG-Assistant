Title: Haunting of Cellblock 11 (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Andrew P. Jones
Main Cast: Jeffrey Johnson, Linara Washington, Charley Koontz, John Zderko, Bill Lithgow

Overview: A group of ghost hunters must up their game to compete in the competitive world of paranormal TV shows, leading them to a truly haunted prison with a grisly past that proves to be more than they bargained for.
