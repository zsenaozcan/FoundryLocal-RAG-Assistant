Title: One Rogue Reporter (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary
Director: Tom Jenkinson
Main Cast: John Bishop, Steve Coogan, Hugh Grant, Owen Jones, Max Mosley

Overview: Rich Peppiatt delivers a satirical dissection of the newspaper trade by turning the tables on unscrupulous editors. Through a series of mischievous stunts and interviews with heavyweights of journalism, comedy &amp; politics, Peppiatt hilariously exposes the hypocrisy at the heart of modern journalism.
