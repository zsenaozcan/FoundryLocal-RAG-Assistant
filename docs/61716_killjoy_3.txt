Title: Killjoy 3 (2010)
Genres: Horror
Director: John Lechago
Main Cast: Trent Haaga, Victoria De Mare, Al Burke

Overview: The demon clown Killjoy is resurrected once again, but this time he is not given the name of his victim and is trapped in his realm. Using a magic mirror he lures four unsuspecting college students into his realm where he can have his macabre fun! A mysterious man returns and we finally discover who Killjoy's true target is!
