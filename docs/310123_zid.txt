Title: Zid (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Romance
Director: Vivek Agnihotri
Main Cast: Mannara Chopra, Karanvir Sharma, Shraddha Das, Seerat Kapoor, Mohan Kapoor
Keywords: love triangle, obsession, vertigo, goa, hit and run, crime reporter, erotic thriller

Overview: A girl falls in a love with a journalist, who is still involved with his ex girlfriend. The pair get trapped in a hit and run case. Will they be able to prove their innocence?
