Title: Paranoia (2013)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Robert Luketic
Main Cast: Liam Hemsworth, Harrison Ford, Gary Oldman, Amber Heard, Josh Holloway
Keywords: spy, based on novel, paranoia, bodyguard, deception, escape

Overview: An entry-level employee at a powerful corporation finds himself occupying a corner office, but at a dangerous price: he must spy on his boss's old mentor to secure for him a multi-billion dollar advantage.
