Title: The L-Shaped Room (1962)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bryan Forbes
Main Cast: Leslie Caron, Tom Bell, Brock Peters, Bernard Lee, Avis Bunnage
Keywords: french, pregnant, writer

Overview: Leslie Caron plays a young, pregnant, unmarried Frenchwoman who takes a room in a seedy boarding house in London. She soon makes friends with Toby, a struggling writer who lives downstairs, and eventually gets to accept her room and the strange characters in the house. But what to do about her baby? And what to do about Toby?
