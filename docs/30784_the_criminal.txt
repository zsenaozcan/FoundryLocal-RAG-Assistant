Title: The Criminal (1960)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Joseph Losey
Main Cast: Stanley Baker, Sam Wanamaker, Grégoire Aslan, Margit Saad, Jill Bennett
Keywords: robbery, b movie, racetrack, brit noir

Overview: When a robbery at a racetrack goes wrong ex-con Johnny Bannion is caught and sent back to prison. He won't tell the rest of the gang where he has stashed the loot leading to violent consequences.
