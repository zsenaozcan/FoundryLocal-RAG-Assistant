Title: The Rumble in the Air: Conditioned Auditorium (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Chuck O'Neil
Main Cast: Bill O'Reilly, Jon Stewart, E.D. Hill
Keywords: political activism, talk show, social commentary

Overview: Bill O'Reilly, Jon Stewart, 2 podiums,1 air-conditioned auditorium... In "O'Reilly vs. Stewart 2012: The Rumble in the Air-Conditioned Auditorium," O'Reilly &amp; Stewart take an entertaining and comedic approach to today's pressing political issues, in an attempt to find the best direction for America. The debate, which was streamed live online, was recorded on Saturday, October 6th, 2012, at the Lisner Auditorium at The George Washington University, in Washington, D.C.  The proceeds from the sale of the debate go to multiple charities picked by both O'Reilly &amp; Stewart, including the Fisher House Foundation, Doctors Without Borders, and the USO.
