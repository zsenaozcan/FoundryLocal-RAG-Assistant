Title: King Uncle (1993)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Rakesh Roshan
Main Cast: Jackie Shroff, Pooja Ruparel, Paresh Rawal, Shah Rukh Khan

Overview: Ashok Bansal has a traumatic childhood which teaches him that money matters most to people, even more than their kids. Ashok starts hating poor people &amp; starts working hard to get out of poverty, at the expense of his own family. However, Ashok’s life changes forever when Munna enters his life.
