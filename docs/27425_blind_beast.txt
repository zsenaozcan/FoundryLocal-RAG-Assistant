Title: Blind Beast (1969)
Genres: Horror, Drama
Director: Yasuzô Masumura
Main Cast: Eiji Funakoshi, Mako Midori, Noriko Sengoku

Overview: A blind sculptor and his mother kidnap a young woman and use her as a model. In the darkness of an abandoned warehouse, she suffers at the hands of the artist, eventually losing both her sight and her mind as whips give way to knives and knives give way to meat cleavers.
