Title: The Giant Claw (1957)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Fred F. Sears
Main Cast: Jeff Morrow, Mara Corday, Morris Ankrum, Louis Merrill, Edgar Barrier
Keywords: monster, giant bird, ufo, b movie, united nations, alien monster

Overview: Global panic ensues when it is revealed that a mysterious UFO is actually a giant turkey-like bird that flies at supersonic speed and has no regard for life or architecture.
