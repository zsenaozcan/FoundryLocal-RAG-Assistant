Title: New Year (2011)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Phil Borg
Main Cast: Julian DeZotti, Joanna Douglas, Nicholas Rose, John Bregar, Morgan Kelly
Keywords: party, pregnancy, near-death experience

Overview: A group of 20-somethings reunite at a New Year's Eve bash in middle-class suburbia. But when a near-death experience, a pregnancy, and the appearance of past rivals disrupts the night, how will each see their place in the world come new year's day?
