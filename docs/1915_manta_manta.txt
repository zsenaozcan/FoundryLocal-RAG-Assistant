Title: Manta, Manta (1991)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Wolfgang Büld
Main Cast: Til Schweiger, Tina Ruland, Michael Kessler, Stefan Gebelhoff, Ömer Simsek
Keywords: car race, hairdresser, ruhr, mercedes benz, idiot, macho, turk, opel manta, tussi

Overview: A not so clever guy called Berti with a Opel Manta (a low budget European sports-coupe from the '80s) gets competition in a street race from a guy with a fast Mercedes, and another slick guy with a Ferrari tries to get his hands on Bertis girlfriend.
