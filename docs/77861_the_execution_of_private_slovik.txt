Title: The Execution of Private Slovik (1974)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Lamont Johnson
Main Cast: Martin Sheen, Charlie Sheen, Mariclare Costello, Ned Beatty, Gary Busey
Keywords: biography, firing squad, desertion, 1940s

Overview: The story of Eddie Slovik, who was executed by the Army in 1945, the only American soldier to be executed for desertion since the Civil War.
