Title: The Bride Walks Out (1936)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Leigh Jason
Main Cast: Barbara Stanwyck, Gene Raymond, Robert Young, Ned Sparks, Helen Broderick
Keywords: bride, job, fashion model

Overview: Barbara Stanwyck takes top-billing as Carolyn Martin, a fashion model who hastily marries her boyfriend, engineer Michael Martin (Gene Raymond). But part of the marriage arrangement requires that Carolyn quit her $50-per-week modeling job to be a full-time housewife; the couple will instead live on Michael’s $35-per-week job.
