Title: If I Were You (2013)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Joan Carr-Wiggin
Main Cast: Marcia Gay Harden, Leonor Watling, Aidan Quinn, Joseph Kell, Valerie Mahaffey
Keywords: woman director

Overview: When a woman tries to outwit her husband's sexy young mistress, the unexpected consequences include starring as King Lear in a very amateur production - with the mistress, an aspiring actress, playing The Fool.
