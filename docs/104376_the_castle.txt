Title: The Castle (1994)
Genres: Drama
Director: Aleksey Balabanov
Main Cast: Nikolay Stotskiy, Svetlana Pismichenko, Anvar Libabov, Viktor Sukhorukov, Igor Shibanov
Keywords: based on novel

Overview: Closely based on Franz Kafka's book "Das Schloß", the movie shares the same action on a land surveyor who is called to a village to do a job that no one seems to have ordered. Once there, he takes up the struggle against bureaucracy emanating from the castle.
