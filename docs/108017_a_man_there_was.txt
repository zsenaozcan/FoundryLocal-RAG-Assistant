Title: A Man There Was (1917)
Genres: Drama
Director: Victor Sjöström
Main Cast: Victor Sjöström, Bergliot Husberg, August Falck, Edith Erastoff, Eric Abrahamsson
Keywords: famine, tragedy, silent film, family man, seafarer

Overview: Terje Vigen, a sailor, suffers the loss of his family through the cruelty of another man. Years later, when his enemy's family finds itself dependent on Terje's benevolence, Terje must decide whether to avenge himself.
