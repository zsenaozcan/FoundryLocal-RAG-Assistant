Title: The Evil Within (2017)
Genres: Horror
Director: Andrew Getty
Main Cast: Fred Koehler, Sean Patrick Flanery, Brianna Brown, Dina Meyer, Michael Berryman
Keywords: demon, animatronic

Overview: The sadistic tale of a lonely, mentally handicapped boy who befriends his reflection in an antique mirror. This demonic creature orders him to go on a murderous rampage to kill the people he loves most.
