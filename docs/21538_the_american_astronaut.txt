Title: The American Astronaut (2001)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Science Fiction, Music
Director: Cory McAbee
Main Cast: Joshua Taylor, Greg Russell Cook, Rocco Sisto, Cory McAbee
Keywords: breast, astronaut

Overview: Samual Curtis's first mission in this bizarre science fiction musical comedy requires him to take a cat to a saloon on an asteroid. There, he meets his former dance partner (the Blueberry Pirate) and collects his payment: a device capable of producing a Real Live Girl. Including music by alternative rock group The Billy Nayer Show, this film began life as a live show with a loyal following.
