Title: Mutant Hunt (1987)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Science Fiction, Horror
Director: Tim Kincaid
Main Cast: Rick Gianasi, Mary Fahey, Ron Reynaldi, Taunie Vrenon, Bill Peterson
Keywords: female nudity, cyborg, android, mad scientist, campy

Overview: In New York City sometime in the near future, Z, the evil chairman of the Inteltrax Corporation, has taken a small army of cyborgs designed to perform hazardous tasks and altered them to kill humans for pleasure. The inventor of the cyborgs, Dr. Paul Haynes, is held captive, and his sister Darla seeks the aid of Matt Riker, an expert in high-tech weaponry and martial arts.
