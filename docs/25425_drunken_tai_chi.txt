Title: Drunken Tai Chi (1984)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Yuen Woo-ping
Main Cast: Donnie Yen, Yuen Cheung-Yan, Lydia Shum, Yuen Yat-Choh, Don Wong Tao
Keywords: hong kong

Overview: Most notable as being Donnie Yen's first film and starring role, Drunken Tai Chi is one of Yuen Woo Ping's funniest films and possesses all of the elements of a great comedic kung fu epic.
