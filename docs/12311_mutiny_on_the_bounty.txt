Title: Mutiny on the Bounty (1935)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, History, Romance
Director: Frank Lloyd
Main Cast: Charles Laughton, Clark Gable, Franchot Tone, Herbert Mundin, Eddie Quillan
Keywords: bounty, captain, boat, mutiny, island, sailing, ship, tahiti, tyrant, flogging

Overview: Fletcher Christian successfully leads a revolt against the ruthless Captain Bligh on the HMS Bounty. However, Bligh returns one year later, hell bent on avenging his captors.
