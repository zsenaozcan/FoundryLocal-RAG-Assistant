Title: Secrets & Lies (1996)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mike Leigh
Main Cast: Timothy Spall, Brenda Blethyn, Marianne Jean-Baptiste, Phyllis Logan, Claire Rushbrook
Keywords: london england, secret, socially deprived family, reunion, mother daughter relationship, illegitimate son

Overview: A middle-aged London factory worker is shocked when the mixed-race daughter she gave up at birth decides to track her down. At first she denies she is her mother. All family members become emotional, as everyone's secrets are exposed.
