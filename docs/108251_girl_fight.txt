Title: Girl Fight (2011)
Genres: Drama, TV Movie
Director: Stephen Gyllenhaal
Main Cast: Anne Heche, Jodelle Ferland, Tess Atkins, Keely Purvis, Genevieve Buechner

Overview: Inspired by a true story, Girl Fight recounts the harrowing story of a 16-year-old, stellar high school student whose life spirals downward when her former friends conspire to upload onto the Internet a shocking video of them beating her up.
