Title: Possessed (1947)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Curtis Bernhardt
Main Cast: Joan Crawford, Van Heflin, Raymond Massey, Geraldine Brooks, Stanley Ridges
Keywords: los angeles, psychiatric ward, film noir

Overview: A dazed woman walks the streets of Los Angeles looking for a man named David. After collapsing in a diner, she's taken to the psychiatric ward of a nearby hospital. Flashbacks reveal her obsession for David as a result of borderline personality disorder which ultimately leads to murder.
