Title: Richard Pryor: Live and Smokin' (1971)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michael Blum
Main Cast: Richard Pryor

Overview: Richard Pryor: Live &amp; Smokin' is the first stand-up act of Richard Pryor to be filmed out of the four that were released in total. This film was filmed in 1971 but not released until 1985, on VHS. This was the first stand-up act that Pryor did before he hit the mainstream audience. With only 48 minutes of footage, it is the shortest of Pryor's stand-up routines.
