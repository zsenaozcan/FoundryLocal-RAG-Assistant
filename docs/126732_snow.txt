Title: Snow (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Aida Begić
Main Cast: Zana Marjanović, Jasna Beri, Sadžida Šetić, Vesna Mašić, Emir Hadžihafizbegović
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The daily hardships of a war-scarred Bosnian village, where all that remains are widows and orphans, are painstakingly documented in this first feature from director Aida Begic. Snow offers insight about the psychological aftereffects of the 1992-95 civil war from a distinctively female point of view without showing any of the brutality or carnage.
