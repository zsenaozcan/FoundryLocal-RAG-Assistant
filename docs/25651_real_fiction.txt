Title: Real Fiction (2000)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Kim Ki-duk
Main Cast: Joo Jin-Mo, Jin-ah Kim, Son Min-seok, Je-rak Lee, Myeong Sun-mi

Overview: A repressed artists receives ritual abuse from those who pass him as he paints portraits in the park. Gradually becoming more frustrated, anger builds up within the artist, leading him to embark upon a quest to gain revenge on all those who have humiliated him.
