Title: Bad Company (1972)
Genres: Western, Drama
Director: Robert Benton
Main Cast: Jeff Bridges, Barry Brown, David Huddleston, Jerry Houser, Geoffrey Lewis
Keywords: runaway, marshal, loyalty, betrayal, draft dodger

Overview: Jeff Bridges stars as young con man named Jake Rumsey in this highly original Western. After Drew Dixon (Barry Brown), an upright young man, is sent west by his religious family to avoid being drafted into the Civil War, he drifts across the land with a loose confederation of young vagrants.
