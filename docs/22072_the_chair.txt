Title: The Chair (2007)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Brett Sullivan
Main Cast: Alanna Chisholm, Lauren Roy, Nick Abraham, Paul Soren, Nickolas Tortolano

Overview: Psychology student Danielle inadvertently wakes a evil spirit while renting a century old Victorian house. In setting out to prove his existence, Danielle inadvertently frees and becomes a puppet of the spirit of serial killer Edgar Crowe. Danielle's sister Anna now must find a way to stop Crowe without killing her sister.
