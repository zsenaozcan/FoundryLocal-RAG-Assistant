Title: Old Shatterhand (1964)
Genres: Adventure, Western
Director: Hugo Fregonese
Main Cast: Lex Barker, Guy Madison, Pierre Brice, Daliah Lavi, Ralf Wolter
Keywords: ranch, apache, old friend

Overview: Renegades trying to get the army to abandon their fort get the Indians addicted to whiskey, then convince them to attack and drive out the soldiers.
