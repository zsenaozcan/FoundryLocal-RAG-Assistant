Title: Flesh + Blood (1985)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Paul Verhoeven
Main Cast: Rutger Hauer, Jennifer Jason Leigh, Tom Burlinson, Jack Thompson, Susan Tyrrell
Keywords: rape, sex, kidnapping, nudity, mercenary, prince, castle, sword fight, betrayal, priest

Overview: A band of medieval mercenaries take revenge on a noble lord who decides not to pay them by kidnapping the betrothed of the noble's son. As the plague and warfare cut a swathe of destruction throughout the land, the mercenaries hole up in a castle and await their fate.
