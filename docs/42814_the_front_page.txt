Title: The Front Page (1931)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lewis Milestone
Main Cast: Pat O’Brien, Adolphe Menjou, Mary Brian, Edward Everett Horton, Walter Catlett
Keywords: reporter, newsroom, editor, press room

Overview: Newspaper reporter, Hildy Johnson is engaged and planning to move to New York for a higher-paying job. While various newsmen wait to cover the hanging of Earl Williams,  a dramatic event takes place that throws the news reporters into chaos and causes Hildy to second guess his departure.
