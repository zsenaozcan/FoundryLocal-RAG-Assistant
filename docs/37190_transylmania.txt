Title: Transylmania (2009)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: David Hillenbrand
Main Cast: Oren Skoog, Patrick Casey, Jennifer Lyons, Tony Denman, Patrick Cavanaugh
Keywords: female nudity, witch, vampire, strip club, sequel, vampire slayer, blood bank

Overview: Spoof horror in which a group of college kids do a semester abroad in Romania and realise that if the partying doesn't kill them, the vampires just might!
