Title: One on One (2014)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Kim Ki-duk
Main Cast: Ma Dong-seok, Kim Young-min, Lee Yi-Kyung, Cho Dong-in, Ji-hye Ahn

Overview: On May 9th, a female high school student was brutally murdered. There are 7 suspects and 7 shadows that terrorize them. Who are they and which one of them is you?
