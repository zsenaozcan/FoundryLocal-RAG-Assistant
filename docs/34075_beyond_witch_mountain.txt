Title: Beyond Witch Mountain (1982)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Robert Day
Main Cast: Eddie Albert, Tracey Gold, Andy Freeman, Efrem Zimbalist, Jr., J. D. Cannon

Overview: Made for TV movie and third Witch Mountain installment of the continuing exploits of the alien kids Tony and Tia. After news of the boy's amazing abilities surfaces Tony and Tia are sent to go find him knowing that the boy must be from their world. They are joined by an old friend Jason O'Day and a black cat named Winky. Together they race to find the boy before their old nemesis Aristotle Bolt does. Written by ElvenSprite
