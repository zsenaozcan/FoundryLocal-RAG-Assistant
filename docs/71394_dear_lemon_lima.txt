Title: Dear Lemon Lima (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Suzi Yoonessi
Main Cast: Savanah Wiltfong, Shayne Topp, Eleanor Hutchins, Kari Nissena, Beth Grant
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Vanessa gets a dose of reality when Philip, her one true love, ends their relationship, again. The quirky teen enrolls in his school to win him back, but ends up making matters worse. Downgraded to social outcast, Vanessa struggles to reclaim Phillip's affection. Luckily, when Vanessa is declared a captain for the school's Snowstorm Survivor competition, she assembles a team of like-minded misfits to prove they deserve to compete and hopefully win her love's heart again.
