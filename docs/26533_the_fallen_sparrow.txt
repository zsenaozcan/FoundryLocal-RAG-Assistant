Title: The Fallen Sparrow (1943)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Richard Wallace
Main Cast: John Garfield, Maureen O'Hara, Walter Slezak, Patricia Morison, Martha O'Driscoll
Keywords: post traumatic stress  disorder, prisoners of war, trauma, spanish civil war, footsteps, film noir

Overview: A former Spanish Civil War prisoner, John McKittrick arrives in New York to find the truth behind the death of his friend Louie Lepetino. Based on the popular Dorothy B. Hughes best seller.
