Title: Standoff (2016)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Adam Alleca
Main Cast: Thomas Jane, Laurence Fishburne, Jim Watson, Ella Ballentine, Joanna Douglas
Keywords: camera, mask, death of a child, murder, rifle, farmhouse, death of son, graveyard, hide, eyewitness

Overview: A troubled veteran gets a chance at redemption by protecting a girl from an assassin after she witnesses a murder. Holding a shotgun with a single shell, he engages in physical and psychological warfare in a desperate fight for the girl's life.
