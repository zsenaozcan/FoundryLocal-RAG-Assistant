Title: May 18 (2007)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Foreign
Director: Jihun Kim
Main Cast: Kim Sang-kyung, Jeong In-gi, Lee Joon-gi, Na Moon-hee

Overview: The citizens of Gwangju lead a relatively peaceful life, until one day the military takes over the city, accusing the residents of conspiracy and claiming that they are communist sympathisers preparing a revolution against the current government. Seeing as the soldiers beat defenceless people, mainly students, to death, the citizens are in for retaliation and form a militia.
