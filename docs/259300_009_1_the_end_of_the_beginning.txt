Title: 009-1: The End of the Beginning (2013)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Koichi Sakamoto
Main Cast: Mayuko Iwasa, Minehiro Kinomoto, Nao Nagasawa, Mao Ichimichi, Aya Sugimoto
Keywords: cyborg, human trafficking, zombie, dystopic future, bio-engineered cyborg

Overview: Marking the 75th anniversary of his birth, Shotaro Ishinomori's classic spy heroine manga "009-1" has been adapted into a live-action motion picture for the first time ever.
