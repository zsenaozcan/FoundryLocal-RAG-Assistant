Title: People in Places (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Juan Cavestany
Main Cast: Jorge Cabrera, Tusti de las Heras, Raquel Guerrero, David Luque, Diego Martín

Overview: This kaleidoscopic film weaves together approximately 20 fragmented scenarios that offer a view of contemporary Spain, drawing conclusions about the persistence of the human condition, strangeness, and the chaos within relationships. Starring Raul Arevalo, Eduard Fernandez and Santiago Segura.
