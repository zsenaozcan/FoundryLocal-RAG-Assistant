Title: Dark Asylum (2001)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Gregory Gieras
Main Cast: Paulina Porizkova, Judd Nelson, Larry Drake, Jürgen Prochnow, Jake Eberle

Overview: Asylum is a taut psychological thriller about a deranged fearsome killer running lose in an abandoned high security Asylum. Pitting the county psychiatrist Dr. Maggie Belham against the psychotic maniac, Dr Belham is trapped in a maze of corridors, and her only help is the janitor, Quitz who may in fact be a forgotten asylum inmate.
