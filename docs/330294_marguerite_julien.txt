Title: Marguerite & Julien (2015)
Genres: Drama, History, Romance
Director: Valérie Donzelli
Main Cast: Anaïs Demoustier, Jérémie Elkaïm, Aurélia Petit, Frédéric Pierrot, Geraldine Chaplin
Keywords: brother sister relationship, escape, incest, voice over, brother sister incest, woman director, anachronism, unwanted marriage

Overview: Julien and Marguerite de Ravalet, son and daughter of the Lord of Tourlaville, have loved each other tenderly since childhood. But as they grow up, their affection veers toward voracious passion.
