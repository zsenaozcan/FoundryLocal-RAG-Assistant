Title: Carnage Park (2016)
Genres: Action, Crime, Horror, Thriller
Director: Mickey Keating
Main Cast: Ashley Bell, Pat Healy, Alan Ruck, Darby Stanchfield, James Landry Hébert
Keywords: robbery, hostage, kidnapping, bank robbery

Overview: Part crime caper gone awry, part survival horror film, this 1970s set thriller depicts a harrowing fight for survival after a pair of wannabe crooks botch a bank heist and flee into the desert, where they inexplicably stumble upon Carnage Park, a remote stretch of wilderness occupied by a psychotic ex-military sniper.
