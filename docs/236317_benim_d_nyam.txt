Title: Benim Dünyam (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Beren Saat, Ugur Yücel, Ayça Bingöl, Turgay Kantürk, Hazar Ergüçlü

Overview: At 2 Ela lost her ability of seeing and hearing because of a severe illness. And then she became a total disappointment of her family due to her untamed behaviors. But everything changed after an unorthodox teacher stepped into their lives to educate Ela while healing himself.
