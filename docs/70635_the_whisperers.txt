Title: The Whisperers (1967)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Bryan Forbes
Main Cast: Edith Evans, Nanette Newman, Harry Baird, Jack Austin, Gerald Sim

Overview: The Whisperers tells the story of an impoverished old woman living alone in a seedy apartment who enjoys a rich fantasy life as an heiress. When she discovers stolen money hidden by her son, she believes her fantasy has come true.
