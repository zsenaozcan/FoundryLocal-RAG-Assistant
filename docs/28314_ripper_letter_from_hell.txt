Title: Ripper: Letter from Hell (2001)
Genres: Horror
Director: John Eyres
Main Cast: A.J. Cook, Bruce Payne, Ryan Northcott, Emmanuelle Vaugier, Claire Keim

Overview: A massacre survivor (A.J. Cook) studies serial killers under a famous expert (Bruce Payne), but her classmates soon start dying at the hands of a Jack the Ripper copycat.
