Title: Liza Minnelli: Liza With a Z (1972)
Genres: Music
Director: Bob Fosse
Main Cast: Liza Minnelli, Ann Reinking
Keywords: live concert, tv special

Overview: Liza Minnelli stars in a television concert directed and choreographed by Bob Fosse. She performs such songs as the title number, "Liza with a 'Z'" and "Son of a Preacher Man". The concert concludes with a medley of songs from the film Cabaret (1972).
