Title: The Gold of Naples (1954)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Vittorio De Sica
Main Cast: Sophia Loren, Silvana Mangano, Eduardo De Filippo, Totò, Paolo Stoppa
Keywords: napoli

Overview: Tribute to Naples, where director De Sica spent his first years, this is a collection of 6 Napolitean episodes : a clown exploited by a gangster ; an inconstant pizza seller (Sofia) loosing her husband's ring ; the funeral of a dead child ; the gambler Count Prospero B. defeated by a kid ; the unexpected and unusual wedding of Teresa, a prostitute ; the "professor" Ersilio Micci, a "wisdom seller".
