Title: Min and Bill (1930)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: George W. Hill
Main Cast: Marie Dressler, Wallace Beery, Dorothy Jordan, Marjorie Rambeau, Don Dillaway
Keywords: boarding house, waterfront, truant officer

Overview: Min, the owner of a dockside hotel, is forced to make difficult decisions about the future of Nancy, the young woman she took in as an infant
