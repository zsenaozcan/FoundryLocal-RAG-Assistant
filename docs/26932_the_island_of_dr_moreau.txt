Title: The Island of Dr. Moreau (1977)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Don Taylor
Main Cast: Burt Lancaster, Michael York, Nigel Davenport, Barbara Carrera, Richard Basehart
Keywords: experiment, mutant, island, genetics, hybrid, remake, medical experiment

Overview: A ship-wrecked man floats ashore on an island in the Pacific Ocean. The island is inhabited by a scientist, Dr. Moreau, who in an experiment has turned beasts into human beings.
