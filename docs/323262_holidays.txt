Title: Holidays (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Kevin Smith
Main Cast: Harley Quinn Smith, Lorenza Izzo, Seth Green, Clare Grant, Ruth Bradley
Keywords: holiday, new year's eve, halloween, darkness, midnight, anthology, easter, blood, dark, mother's day
