Title: Cry, Onion! (1975)
Genres: Western
Director: Enzo G. Castellari
Main Cast: Franco Nero, Sterling Hayden, Martin Balsam, Leo Anchóriz, Romano Puppo
Keywords: spaghetti western

Overview: Onion Jack (Franco Nero) has bought a piece of land on which to settle, but the property is still in possession of the orphans of the original owner and is coveted by the local oil baron.
