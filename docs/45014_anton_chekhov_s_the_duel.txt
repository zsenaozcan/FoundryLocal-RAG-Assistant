Title: Anton Chekhov's The Duel (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Dover Koshashvili
Main Cast: Andrew Scott, Fiona Glascott, Tobias Menzies, Niall Buggy

Overview: Set in a seaside resort in the Caucasus, the story centers on n'er do well, Laevsky (Andrew Scott) and his illicit relationship with his mistress Nadya (Fiona Glascott). Laevsky has convinced Nadya to leave her husband for him, but now wants to abandon her.
