Title: Grand Central (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Rebecca Zlotowski
Main Cast: Léa Seydoux, Tahar Rahim, Olivier Gourmet, Denis Ménochet, Johan Libéreau
Keywords: infidelity, nuclear power plant, woman director, radioactivity

Overview: A young unemployed man finds work at a nuclear power plant and begins an illicit affair with the fiancée of one of his senior workmates.
