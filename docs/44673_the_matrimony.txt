Title: The Matrimony (2007)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller, Foreign
Director: Hua-Tao Teng
Main Cast: Fan Bingbing, Leon Lai, René Liu, Songzi Xu

Overview: A woman finds the key to a room in the attic that her husband forbids her from entering. When she opens the door, she is confronted with the haunting existence of the woman her husband refuses to forget.
