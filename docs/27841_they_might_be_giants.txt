Title: They Might Be Giants (1971)
Genres: Comedy, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Anthony Harvey
Main Cast: George C. Scott, Joanne Woodward, Jack Gilford, Lester Rawlins, Al Lewis
Keywords: loss of lover, mental disease, suppressed past, fantasy, sherlock holmes, psychiatry

Overview: They Might be Giants chronicles the adventures of Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson in modern-day New York City. The fact that Sherlock Holmes is a psychotic paranoid and Dr. Watson is a female psychiatrist fascinated by his case is almost beside the point.
