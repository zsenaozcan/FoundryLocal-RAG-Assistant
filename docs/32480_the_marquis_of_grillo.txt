Title: The Marquis of Grillo (1981)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mario Monicelli
Main Cast: Alberto Sordi, Paolo Stoppa, Flavio Bucci, Marc Porel, Giorgio Gobbi

Overview: The marquess of Grillo is a good-natured, clever and women-loving man who is always inventing stories and cracking all types of jokes. The film tells the story of his life.
