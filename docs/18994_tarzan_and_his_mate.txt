Title: Tarzan and His Mate (1934)
Genres: Drama, Adventure, Action
Director: Cedric Gibbons
Main Cast: Johnny Weissmuller, Maureen O'Sullivan, Neil Hamilton, Paul Cavanagh, Forrester Harvey
Keywords: africa, lion, elephant, tarzan, wildlife, pre-code, chimpanzee, ape man

Overview: Harry Holt returns to Africa to head up a large ivory expedition. This time he brings his womanizing friend Marlin Arlington. Holt also harbors ideas about convincing Jane to return to London. When Holt and Arlington show Jane some of the modern clothes and perfumes they brought from civilization, she is impressed but not enough to return.
