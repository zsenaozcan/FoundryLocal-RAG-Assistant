Title: Love Steaks (2013)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Jakob Lass
Main Cast: Lana Cooper, Franz Rogowski, Kerstin Abendroth, Daniela Adenauer, Eric Popp

Overview: A luxury hotel. Steaks frizzle, muffintops getting massaged. Clemens (rare) joins the wellness area as a rookie.  Lara (well-done) needs to assert herself in the kitchen pack.  The elevator brings the two of them together.  They encounter each other, until they clash.
