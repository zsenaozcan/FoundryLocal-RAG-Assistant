Title: Normal (2009)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Thriller
Director: Julius Ševčík
Main Cast: Milan Kňažko, Pavel Gajdos, Dagmar Veškrnová-Havlová, Zuzana Kajnarová, Miroslav Táborský
Keywords: serial killer, true crime

Overview: Dreaded serial killer Peter Kurten voluntarily turns himself over to the authorities. A young lawyer, eager to prove Kurten's mental derangement, agrees to defend a man who terrorized Germany between 1929 and 1931. In studying the case and meeting with the intelligent, cold-blooded manipulator, the young idealist's values become warped.
