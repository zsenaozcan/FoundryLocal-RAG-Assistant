Title: Bad Biology (2008)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror
Director: Frank Henenlotter
Main Cast: Charlee Danielson, Anthony Sneed, Ginger Starr, John A. Thorburn, Tom Kohut
Keywords: female nudity, orgasm

Overview: Driven by biological excess, a young man and woman search for sexual fulfillment, unaware of each other's existence. Unfortunately, they eventually meet, and the bonding of these two very unusual human beings ends in an explosive and ultimately over-the-top sexual experience, resulting in a truly god awful love story....
