Title: Tom Dick and Harry (1941)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Romance
Director: Garson Kanin
Main Cast: Ginger Rogers, George Murphy, Burgess Meredith, Alan Marshal, Lenore Lonergan
Keywords: mechanic, salesman, car salesman

Overview: Ginger Rogers, a telephone operator, is pursued by George Murphy (a down-to earth fellow), Alan Marshal (a rich man) and Burgess Meredith (a noncomformist). She dreams what life would be life married to each of them.
