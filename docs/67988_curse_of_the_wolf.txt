Title: Curse of the Wolf (2006)
Genres: Horror, Action, Comedy
Director: Len Kabasinski
Main Cast: Lanny Poffo, Darian Caine, Brian Daniel Heffron, Pamela Sutch

Overview: Dakota, a young werewolf, has finally learned to control her nighttime transformations. She desperately wants to live a normal life, and to break free from her curse, she flees to hide in the city. When the pack aggressively hunts her down, the bouncers offer her protection and band together to battle the werewolves who want her dead... and who won't give up their wolf without a vicious fight.
