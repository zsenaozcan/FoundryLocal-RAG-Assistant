Title: Royal Palace (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Valérie Lemercier
Main Cast: Valérie Lemercier, Lambert Wilson, Catherine Deneuve, Denis Podalydès, Mathilde Seigner
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Eugenia is the queen of an imaginary European country. When her husband dies, quite unexpectedly, the country is left without a king. According to the law, the new king needs to be married so that leaves out the eldest son. Her youngest son, Prince Arnaud is married to the lovely Armelle and they have two young children. They become the future rulers of the kingdom.
