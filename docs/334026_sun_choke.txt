Title: Sun Choke (2015)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Thriller
Director: Ben Cresciman
Main Cast: Sarah Hagan, Barbara Crampton, Sara Malakul Lane, Evan Jones, William Nicol
Keywords: female nudity, held captive, therapy, recovery, psychotic break

Overview: Janie’s just trying to get well. As she recovers from a violent psychotic break, she’s subjected each day to a bizarre holistic health and wellness regimen designed, and enforced, by her lifelong nanny and caretaker. But when she develops an obsession with a stranger, Janie's buried demons begin to surface.
