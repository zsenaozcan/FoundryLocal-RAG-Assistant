Title: Cadet Kelly (2002)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Larry Shaw
Main Cast: Hilary Duff, Christy Carlson Romano, Gary Cole, Shawn Ashmore, Aimee Garcia
Keywords: military school

Overview: Hyperactive teenager Kelly is enrolled into a military school when her new stepfather becomes the Commandant. At first she has problems fitting in and taking orders until she tries out for the drill team.
