Title: Stavisky (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alain Resnais
Main Cast: Jean-Paul Belmondo, François Périer, Michael Lonsdale, Roberto Bisacco, Claude Rich
Keywords: biography

Overview: Irrestisible charm and talent helps Serge Alexandre alias Stavisky, small-time swindler, to make friends with even most influential members of French industrial and political elite during the early 30s.
