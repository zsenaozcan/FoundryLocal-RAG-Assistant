Title: Hurricane of Fun: The Making of Wet Hot (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Amy Rice
Main Cast: Amy Poehler, David Wain, Molly Shannon, Ken Marino, David Hyde Pierce
Keywords: cult, satire, comedy, witty, making of, goofy
