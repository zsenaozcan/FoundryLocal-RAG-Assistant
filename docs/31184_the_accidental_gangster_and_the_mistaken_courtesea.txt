Title: The Accidental Gangster and the Mistaken Courtesean (2008)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Yeo Kyun-dong 
Main Cast: Lee Jung-jae, Kim Ok-bin, Yeo Kyun-dong , Kim Suk-hoon

Overview: Korea, 1724. Once upon a time in the Choson Dynasty, Chun-doong falls in love at first sight with a beautiful woman whilst in the middle of a fight.When she is delivered by accident to Chun-doong's tavern to work as a maid, he is the happiest man in the land until she is snatched away by her rightful owner.He vows to get her back, but alone he is no match for Man-deuk.
