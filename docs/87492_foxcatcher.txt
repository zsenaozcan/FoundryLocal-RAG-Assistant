Title: Foxcatcher (2014)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Bennett Miller
Main Cast: Steve Carell, Channing Tatum, Mark Ruffalo, Sienna Miller, Vanessa Redgrave
Keywords: biography, wrestling, sport, based on true story

Overview: The greatest Olympic Wrestling Champion brother team joins Team Foxcatcher led by multimillionaire sponsor John E. du Pont as they train for the 1988 games in Seoul - a union that leads to unlikely circumstances.
