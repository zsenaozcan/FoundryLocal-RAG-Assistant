Title: The Ditch (2010)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Wang Bing
Main Cast: Zhengwu Cheng, Niansong Jing, Xiangnian Li, Renjun Lian, Ye Lu

Overview: It recounts the harrowing story of life at one of Mao's camps, at the end of the fifties, where 'rightists' were sent to be 're-educated through labor'.
