Title: Legend of the Galactic Heroes: Golden Wings (1992)
Genres: Animation
Director: Keizou Shimizu
Main Cast: Hikaru Midorikawa, Hiromi Murata, Etsuo Yokobori, Kouji Ishii, Hiromi Tsuru

Overview: Reinhard von Müsel, a poor nobleman's son, one day discovers that his sister, Annerose, has been sold to the royal family. To get her back he sets out to rise quickly through the military along with his friend Siegfried Kircheis.
