Title: Promised Land (1987)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Michael Hoffman
Main Cast: Jason Gedrick, Tracy Pollan, Kiefer Sutherland, Meg Ryan
Keywords: tattoo, nudity, basketball, wedding, skinny dipping

Overview: Three recent high school grads -- star athlete Hancock (Jason Gedrick), his cheerleader girlfriend, Mary (Tracy Pollan), and the awkward Senator (Kiefer Sutherland) -- aim to leave their small town behind and realize big dreams, but their idealistic plans collide with life's harsh realities. One of the first-ever entries at the Sundance Film Festival, this drama written and directed by Michael Hoffman illustrates the promise of youth gone awry.
