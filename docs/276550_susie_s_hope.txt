Title: Susie's Hope (2013)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Jerry Rees
Main Cast: Emmanuelle Vaugier, Burgess Jenkins, Andrea Powell, Willette Thompson, Gregory Waller

Overview: Family - In this family tale based on a true story, a woman who suffered a violent pit bull attack comes to terms with the harrowing experience by adopting a puppy that was severely beaten, burned and left to die. -  Emmanuelle Vaugier, Burgess Jenkins, Andrea Powell
