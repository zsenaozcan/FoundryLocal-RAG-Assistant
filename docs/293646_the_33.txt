Title: The 33 (2015)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Patricia Riggen
Main Cast: Antonio Banderas, Rodrigo Santoro, Juliette Binoche, James Brolin, Lou Diamond Phillips
Keywords: mining, chile, based on true story, survival, woman director, mining accident

Overview: Based on a true story about the collapse at the mine in San Jose, Chile that  left 33 miners isolated underground for 69 days.
