Title: Dutch in Seven Lessons (1948)
Genres: Documentary, Comedy
Director: Charles Huguenot van der Linden
Main Cast: Audrey Hepburn, Wam Heskes, Han Bentz van den Berg, Magda Janssens, Els Boon
Keywords: travel

Overview: A film-within-the-film scenario involving a cameraman who's given a week to photograph the aerial highlights of Holland for a travelogue.
