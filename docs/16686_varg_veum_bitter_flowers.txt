Title: Varg Veum - Bitter Flowers (2007)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Ulrik Imtiaz Rolfsen
Main Cast: Trond Espen Seim, Bjørn Floberg, Endre Hellestveit, Kathrine Fagerland, Anders Dale

Overview: Karsten Aslaksen, chief engineer of a large chemical company, disappears without trace. His married lover, a successful politician named Vibeke Farang, approaches private investigator Varg Veum to track him down discreetly.
