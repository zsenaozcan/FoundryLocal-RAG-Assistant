Title: Rosencrantz & Guildenstern Are Dead (1991)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Tom Stoppard
Main Cast: Gary Oldman, Tim Roth, Richard Dreyfuss, Iain Glen, Donald Sumpter
Keywords: shakespeare, fate, existentialism

Overview: Two minor characters from the play, "Hamlet" stumble around unaware of their scripted lives and unable to deviate from them.
