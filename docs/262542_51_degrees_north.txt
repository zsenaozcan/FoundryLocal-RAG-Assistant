Title: 51 Degrees North (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Science Fiction, Drama, Mystery
Director: Grigorij Richters
Main Cast: Moritz von Zeddelmann, Dolly-Ann Osterloh, Steven Cree, Steve Nallon

Overview: When Damon Miller, a talented, young London filmmaker becomes involved in the disturbing research surrounding Near-Earth Objects he stumbles onto the discovery that the Earth stands on the brink of an extraterrestrial disaster.
