Title: Once Upon A Time in Phuket (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Staffan Lindberg
Main Cast: Susanne Thorson, David Hellenius, Jenny Skavlan, Frida Hallgren, Grynet Molvig

Overview: Life coach Sven travels to Thailand to fulfil his dreams as a writer. He meets Gitte a yoga-practising free spirit from Norway. Then along comes Anja, an editor who works for a Swedish publisher. What does he really want to do with his life, and who does he want to do it with?
