Title: Yarn (2016)
Genres: Animation, Documentary
Director: Heather Millard
Main Cast: Tinna Thorudottir Thorvaldar, Olek, Toshiko Horiuchi MacAdam, Tilde Björfors of Cirkus Cikör, Barbara Kingsolver
Keywords: creativity, woman director

Overview: The traditional crafts of crochet and knitting have become one of the hottest movements in modern art. We follow a few International artists and knitters as they bring yarn to the streets and into our lives in new ways. Starting in Iceland, this quirky and thought-provoking film takes us on a colourful and global journey as we discover how yarn connects us all.
