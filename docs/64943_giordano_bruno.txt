Title: Giordano Bruno (1973)
Genres: Drama
Director: Giuliano Montaldo
Main Cast: Gian Maria Volonté, Hans Christian Blech, Mathieu Carrière, Renato Scarpa, Giuseppe Maffioli

Overview: Flying from his enemies in the Catholic Church, the free thinking philosopher, poet and scientist Giordano Bruno (1548-1600) has found some protection in Venice. But the Roman Inquisition, fearing his influence in Europe, wants to bring him on trial for 'heresy'.
