Title: Tales from the Golden Age (2009)
Genres: Comedy, History, Foreign
Director: Cristian Mungiu
Main Cast: Tania Popa, Diana Cavallioti, Radu Iacoban, Vlad Ivanov, Liliana Mocanu
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Tales from the Golden Age is composed of 5 unconventional short stories, each one dealing with the late communist period in Romania, told through its urban myths from the perspective of ordinary people. The title of the film refers to the alluded "Golden Age" of the last 15 years of Ceauşescu's regime.
