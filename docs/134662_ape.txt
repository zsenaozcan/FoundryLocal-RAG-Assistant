Title: Ape (2012)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Joel Potrykus
Main Cast: Joshua Burge
Keywords: rebel, dark comedy, stand-up comedian

Overview: Trevor Newandyke is a struggling comedian. Not only does he bomb on stage, but he bombs in everyday life. He’s fed up with all the jerks who push him around. All he wants is a break, and for someone to get him. Instead of taking a breath and getting himself together or taking his anger to the stage, he turns to the loud din of his headphones and the crackling glow of fire to ease his mind. He’s not only a lousy comic, but a pyromaniac, as well.
