Title: Sweet Charity (1969)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Music, Romance
Director: Bob Fosse
Main Cast: Shirley MacLaine, John McMartin, Ricardo Montalban, Sammy Davis, Jr., Chita Rivera
Keywords: broken engagement, charity, celebrity, based on play or musical, tragic love, dancehall girl, based on movie
