Title: White Mane (1953)
Genres: Drama
Director: Albert Lamorisse
Main Cast: Alain Emery, Laurent Roche, Clan-Clan, Pascal Lamorisse, François Périer
Keywords: france, horse

Overview: In the south of France, in a vast plain region called the Camargue, lives White Mane, a magnificent stallion and the leader of a herd of wild horses too proud to let themselves be broken by humans. Only Folco, a young fisherman, manages to tame him. A strong friendship grows between the boy and the horse, as the two go looking for the freedom that the world of men won’t allow them.
