Title: Kyle Kinane: Loose in Chicago (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Marcus Raboy
Main Cast: Kyle Kinane
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: In his third one-hour special, Kyle Kinane talks about why his girlfriend doesn’t need to worry about him cheating, reveals the whitest thing he’s ever said, and explains why you have to keep fashion in mind if you insist on carrying a gun.
