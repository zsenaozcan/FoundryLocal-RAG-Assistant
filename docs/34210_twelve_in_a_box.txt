Title: Twelve in a Box (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Thriller
Director: John Mckenzie
Main Cast: Ed Bennett, David Burrows-Sutcliffe, Robert Cargill, Lucy Chalkley, Gareth Clarke

Overview: At a school reunion dinner in a remote country mansion, a dozen people are offered the chance to collect a million pounds each if they can stay on the estate, cut off from the outside world, for 96 hours.
