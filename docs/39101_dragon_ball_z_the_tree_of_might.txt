Title: Dragon Ball Z: The Tree of Might (1990)
Genres: Action, Animation
Director: Daisuke Nishio
Main Cast: Toshio Furukawa, Mayumi Tanaka, Masako Nozawa, Masako Nozawa, Masako Nozawa
Keywords: anime, dragon ball,

Overview: The Saiyajin named Turlus has come to Earth in order to plant a tree that will both destroy the planet and give him infinite strength. Son Goku and the Z Warriors cannot let this happen and duke it out with the invaders for the sake of the planet.
