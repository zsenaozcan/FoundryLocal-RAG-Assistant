Title: New Trial (2017)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kim Tae-yoon
Main Cast: Jung Woo, Kang Ha-Neul, Kim Hae-sook, Lee Dong-Hwi, Lee Kyoung-young
Keywords: drama, based on true story, trial, crime, court trial

Overview: A taxi driver is found dead, and Hyun-woo, the only witness, charged with a murder and serves 10 years in prison. While offering pro bono services, a lawyer Junyoung meets Hyun-woo, and they begin their journey to prove his innocence.
