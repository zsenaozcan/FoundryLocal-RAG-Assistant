Title: Cahill U.S. Marshal (1973)
Genres: Drama, Western
Director: Andrew V. McLaglen
Main Cast: John Wayne, George Kennedy, Gary Grimes, Neville Brand, Clay O'Brien
Keywords: u.s. marshal, cowboy and outlaw

Overview: J.D. Cahill is the toughest U.S. Marshal they've got, just the sound of his name makes bad guys stop in their tracks, so when his two young boy's want to get his attention they decide to rob a bank. They end up getting more than they bargained for.
