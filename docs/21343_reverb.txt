Title: Reverb (2007)
Genres: Horror
Director: Eitan Arrusi
Main Cast: Leo Gregory, Eva Birthistle, Pamela Banks, Margo Stilley, Stephen Lord

Overview: A rock musician, in need for a hit album, locks himself and his band up overnight inside a recording studio. While sampling an old recording with a spooky voice inside, a life and death struggle against the ultimate evil ensues.
