Title: The Final Member (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jonah Bekhor
Main Cast: Sigurdur Hjartarson, Páll Arason, Tom Mitchell
Keywords: animal penis

Overview: Thirty miles from the Arctic Circle, in the northern Icelandic town of Husavik, stands the Icelandic Phallological Museum - the world's only Penis museum. Over 40 years, the founder and curator has collected every specimen from every mammal except for one elusive penis needed to complete his collection: The Human Specimen.
