Title: Martian Child (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Menno Meyjes
Main Cast: John Cusack, Bobby Coleman, Amanda Peet, Joan Cusack, Oliver Platt
Keywords: underdog, adoption, education, adoptive father, childhood trauma, alienation, alien

Overview: A recently-widowed, science fiction writer considers whether to adopt a hyper-imaginative 6-year-old abandoned and socially-rejected boy who says he's really from Mars.
