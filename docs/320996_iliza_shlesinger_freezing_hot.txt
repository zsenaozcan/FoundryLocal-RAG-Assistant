Title: Iliza Shlesinger: Freezing Hot (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Iliza Shlesinger
Keywords: stand-up comedy, aftercreditsstinger, duringcreditsstinger

Overview: Smart and brazen comedian Iliza Shlesinger shares her unflinchingly honest observations on the differences between men and women. Filled with hashtag-able catch phrases, this is a laugh-out-loud revelation exposing some of women’s best kept - and ugliest - secrets, including truths about first date attire, fantasy break-ups and the tireless pursuit of not being cold while still looking hot.
