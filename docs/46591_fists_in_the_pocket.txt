Title: Fists in the Pocket (1965)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Foreign
Director: Marco Bellocchio
Main Cast: Lou Castel, Paola Pitagora, Marino Masé, Liliana Gerace, Stefania Troglio
Keywords: epilepsy, alienation, epileptic, blind mother, low iq

Overview: Ale, a deeply disturbed young man subject to seizures, benignly decides to murder members of his dysfunctional family for altruistic reasons.
