Title: Sweet Movie (1974)
Genres: Mystery, Comedy, Drama
Director: Dusan Makavejev
Main Cast: Carole Laure, Pierre Clémenti, Anna Prucnal, Sami Frey, John Vernon
Keywords: bizarre, beauty queen contest, independent film

Overview: The winner (Carole Laure) of the Miss World Virginity contest marries, escapes from her masochistic husband and ends up involved in a world of debauchery.
