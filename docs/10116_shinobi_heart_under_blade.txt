Title: Shinobi: Heart Under Blade (2005)
Genres: Action
Director: Ten Shimoyama
Main Cast: Yukie Nakama, Erika Sawajiri, Tomoka Kurotani, Joe Odagiri, Tak Sakaguchi
Keywords: japan, ninja fighter, forbidden love

Overview: Even though Gennosuke and Oboro are from rival ninja villages, they are secretly in love. At an annual conference with the Lord, it is dictated that a competition--a fight to the death--will take place between the five best shinobi from each village. Gennosuke and Oboro's love is made even more impossible when they each got picked as the leader of the five to represent their respective villages.
