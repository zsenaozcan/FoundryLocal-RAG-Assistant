Title: Into the Universe with Stephen Hawking (2010)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Iain Riddick
Main Cast: Stephen Hawking, Benedict Cumberbatch, Melissa Ricci

Overview: Into the Universe with Stephen Hawking is a science documentary mini-series written by British physicist Stephen Hawking. The series was created for Discovery Channel and features computer generated imagery of the universe. In the United Kingdom the series has a modified title, Stephen Hawking's Universe.
