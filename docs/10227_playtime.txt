Title: PlayTime (1967)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jacques Tati
Main Cast: Jacques Tati, Barbara Dennek, Rita Maiden, France Rumilly
Keywords: paris, modernity, modern society, glass, steel

Overview: Jacques Tati’s gloriously choreographed, nearly wordless comedies about confusion in the age of technology reached their creative apex with Playtime, a lasting testament to a modern age tiptoeing on the edge of oblivion.
