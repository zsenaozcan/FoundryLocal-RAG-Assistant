Title: Mad Love (1935)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Karl Freund
Main Cast: Peter Lorre, Frances Drake, Colin Clive, Ted Healy, Sara Haden
Keywords: obsession, surgeon, mad doctor, pianist, knife throwing, actress, wax figure

Overview: An insane surgeon's obsession with an actress leads him to replace her wounded pianist's hands with the hands of a knife murderer which still have the urge to throw knives.
