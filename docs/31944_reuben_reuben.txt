Title: Reuben, Reuben (1983)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Robert Ellis Miller
Main Cast: Tom Conti, Kelly McGillis, Roberts Blossom, Joel Fabiani, Cynthia Harris
Keywords: sex, obsession, poet, alcoholism

Overview: Based on a novel by Peter De Vries. Story of an alcoholic, lecherous Scottish poet on a tour of New England Universities, where he goes from one bed and bottle to another.
