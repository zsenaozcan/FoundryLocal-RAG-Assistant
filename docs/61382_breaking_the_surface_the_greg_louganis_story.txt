Title: Breaking The Surface: The Greg Louganis Story (1997)
Genres: Drama
Director: Steven Hilliard Stern
Main Cast: Mario López, Michael Murphy, Jeffrey Meek, Rosemary Dunsmore, Bruce Weitz
Keywords: diving, coming out, biography, sport, gay interest

Overview: Breaking the Surface is about the tough times Greg Louganis had on his way to becoming one of the world's top Olympic divers. Some topics discussed were Greg's childhood problems, his homosexuality, and him contracting the HIV virus.
