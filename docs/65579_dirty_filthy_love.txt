Title: Dirty Filthy Love (2005)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Adrian Shergold
Main Cast: Michael Sheen, Shirley Henderson, Adrian Bower, Claudie Blakley, Anastasia Griffith
Keywords: obsessive compulsive disorder, tourette's syndrome

Overview: A man's life falls apart as a result of his affliction with Obsessive Compulsive Disorder and Tourette's Syndrome in this touching and funny tale.
