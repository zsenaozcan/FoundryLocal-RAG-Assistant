Title: White People (2015)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Lisa Aschan
Main Cast: Pernilla August, Vera Vitali, Isaka Sawadogo, Emil Almén, Aliette Opheim
Keywords: prison, deportation, sacrifice, freedom, escape, woman director

Overview: The story follows a group of people who are kidnapped from the street and held hostage in an underground prison to await deportation. Will they escape? What will be the cost of freedom?
