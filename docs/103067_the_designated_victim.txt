Title: The Designated Victim (1971)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Maurizio Lucidi
Main Cast: Tomás Milián, Pierre Clémenti, Katia Christine, Luigi Casellato, Marisa Bartoli
Keywords: giallo, mistress, psychological

Overview: A wealthy, decadent count convinces a disillusioned playboy that they shall murder one another's relatives, to get away with the perfect crime. The count murders the playboy's unpleasant wife. Now the playboy finds he cannot keep his end of the bargain, while the police finger him for the murder.
