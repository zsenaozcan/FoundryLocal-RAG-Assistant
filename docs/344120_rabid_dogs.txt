Title: Rabid Dogs (2015)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Éric Hannezo
Main Cast: Lambert Wilson, Guillaume Gouix, Franck Gastambide, François Arnaud, Virginie Ledoyen

Overview: After a bank job goes badly wrong, three desperate criminals take a young woman and a father and child hostage - it's the beginning of a frantic and violent road trip that not all of them will survive.
