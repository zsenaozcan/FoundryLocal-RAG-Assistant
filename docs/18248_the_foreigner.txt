Title: The Foreigner (2003)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Michael Oblowitz
Main Cast: Steven Seagal, Jeffrey Pierce, Max Ryan, Harry Van Gorkum, Anna-Louise Plowman
Keywords: assassin, van, package

Overview: This story is about a freelance agent (Seagal) who is the courier of a package from France to Germany. He soon finds that many people want to get their hands on it.
