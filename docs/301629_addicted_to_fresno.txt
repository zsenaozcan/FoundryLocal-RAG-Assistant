Title: Addicted to Fresno (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jamie Babbit
Main Cast: Judy Greer, Aubrey Plaza, Natasha Lyonne, Clea DuVall, Ron Livingston
Keywords: sister sister relationship, cover-up, woman director, fresno california, accidental crime

Overview: Two co-dependent sisters, a recovering sex addict and a lonely lesbian who work as hotel maids in Fresno, go to ludicrous lengths to cover up an accidental crime.
