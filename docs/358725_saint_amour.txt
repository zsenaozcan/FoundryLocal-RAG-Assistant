Title: Saint Amour (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Benoît Delépine
Main Cast: Benoît Poelvoorde, Gérard Depardieu, Vincent Lacoste, Chiara Mastroianni, Solène Rigot

Overview: Every year, Bruno makes a tour of all the wine stands, without setting foot outside the Show’s premises and without ever finishing his wine trail. This year, his father suggests they finish it together, but a real wine trail, across the French countryside. Accompanied by Mike, a young, quirky taxi driver, they set off in the direction of France’s major wine regions. Together, they are going to discover not only the wine trails, but also the road that leads back to Love.
