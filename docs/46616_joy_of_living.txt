Title: Joy of Living (1938)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Tay Garnett
Main Cast: Irene Dunne, Douglas Fairbanks Jr., Alice Brady, Guy Kibbee, Jean Dixon
Keywords: island, ship, musical, marriage, courtroom, theater

Overview: Broadway star Margaret Garrett has spent her whole life working to support her sponging relatives. When she meets carefree Dan Webster, she learns how to have fun for the first time.
