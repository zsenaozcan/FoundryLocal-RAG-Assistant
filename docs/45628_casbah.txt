Title: Casbah (1948)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Music, Romance
Director: John Berry
Main Cast: Yvonne De Carlo, Tony Martin, Peter Lorre, Märta Torén, Hugo Haas
Keywords: algiers

Overview: Pepe Le Moko (Tony Martin) leads a gang of jewel thieves in the Casbah of Algiers, where he has exiled himself to escape imprisonment in his native France.
