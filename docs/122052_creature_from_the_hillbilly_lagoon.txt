Title: Creature from the Hillbilly Lagoon (2005)
Genres: Horror
Director: Richard Griffin
Main Cast: Andrew Vellenoweth, Tanith Fiedler, William DeCoff, V. Orion Delwaterman, Adam Brown

Overview: A group of college students run afoul of not only a passel of insane, inbred rednecks, but also a humanoid fish monster bent on making chum of them all.
