Title: Love on the Air (2015)
Genres: TV Movie, Romance
Director: Kristoffer Tabori
Main Cast: Alison Sweeney, Jonathan Scarfe, John Kapelos, David Lewis, Emily Maddison

Overview: When a radio host gets dumped by her boyfriend on-air and soon has her break up exposed even further by a shock jock, she needs to regain her confidence while working with the same man who humiliated her in front of his viewers.
