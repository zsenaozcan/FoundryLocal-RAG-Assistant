Title: The Babysitters (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: David Ross
Main Cast: John Leguizamo, Katherine Waterston, Cynthia Nixon, Andy Comeau, Denis O'Hare
Keywords: sex, adultery, nudity, babysitter, cabin, daughter, high school, business idea, independent film, train

Overview: Seventeen-year-old Shirley (Katherine Waterston) is a good student who works as a babysitter in order to make money for college. One night Michael (John Leguizamo), a father Shirley works for, confesses he's unhappy with married life. Shirley has a crush on Michael, and seizes this moment to kiss him. Michael is so happy he presents Shirley with a big tip, which gives her an idea. Shirley plans to make extra money by setting up her teenage friends with other unhappy fathers.
