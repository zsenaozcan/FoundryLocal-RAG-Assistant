Title: The Three Caballeros (1944)
Genres: Animation, Family, Music
Director: William Roberts
Main Cast: Sterling Holloway, Clarence Nash, Joaquin Garay, José Oliveira, Aurora Miranda
Keywords: part live action, south america, pinata, live action and animation

Overview: For Donald's birthday he receives a box with three gifts inside. The gifts, a movie projector, a pop-up book, and a pinata, each take Donald on wild adventures through Mexico and South America.
