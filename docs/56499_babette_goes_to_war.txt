Title: Babette Goes to War (1959)
Genres: Comedy, War
Director: Christian-Jaque
Main Cast: Brigitte Bardot, Jacques Charrier, Ronald Howard, Michael Cramer, Hannes Messemer
Keywords: london england, french girl, time of war

Overview: Comedy about a naive French country girl in London who helps the war effort by parachuting into German-occupied France to help kidnap an important German general. She bungles through to a heroic finish of plot and counter-plot.
