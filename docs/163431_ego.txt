Title: Ego (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lisa James Larsson
Main Cast: Martin Wallström, Mylaine Hedreul, Sissela Kyle, Peter Andersson, Emil Johnsen
Keywords: woman director

Overview: For Sebastian Silverberg life is all about his appearance, partying and his enormous ego. Things are going smoothly when an accident leaves him blind, forcing him to re-evaluate his life and to seek meaning beyond appearances.
