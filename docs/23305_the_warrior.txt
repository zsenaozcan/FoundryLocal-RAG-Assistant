Title: The Warrior (2001)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Drama, Action, Foreign
Director: Asif Kapadia
Main Cast: Irrfan Khan, Puru Chibber, Aino Annuddin, Manoj Mishra, Nanhe Khan

Overview: In feudal India, a warrior (Khan) who renounces his role as the longtime enforcer to a local lord becomes the prey in a murderous hunt through the Himalayan mountains.
