Title: Escanaba in da Moonlight (2001)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jeff Daniels
Main Cast: Jeff Daniels, Harve Presnell, Joey Albright, Wayne David Parker, Randall Godwin
Keywords: sport, independent film

Overview: A macho man in a family on the eve of deer-hunting season must deal with the eldest son's curse of never having bagged a buck.
