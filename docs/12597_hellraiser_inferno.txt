Title: Hellraiser: Inferno (2000)
Genres: Crime, Horror, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Scott Derrickson
Main Cast: Craig Sheffer, Nicholas Turturro, James Remar, Doug Bradley, Nicholas Sadler
Keywords: loss of sense of reality, nightmare, pinhead, vision

Overview: A shady police detective becomes embroiled in a strange world of murder, sadism and madness after being assigned a murder investigation against a madman known only as "The Engineer".
