Title: Kidnapping, Caucasian Style (1967)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Romance
Director: Leonid Gayday
Main Cast: Aleksandr Demyanenko, Natalya Varley, Vladimir Etush, Frunzik Mkrtchyan, Ruslan Akhmetov

Overview: In this comic but dated story, nerdy Shurik travels to Caucas in search of native legends and folklore. But what he finds is a beautiful girl whom, due to intoxication and deceit of the local "gang", he ends up literally stealing for the local deceitful governor. All the time Shurik thinks that it is all just a one old Caucasian custom. When he, finally, realizes what he did he goes out in search for the girl of his dreams. Written by Ptah Hoteb
