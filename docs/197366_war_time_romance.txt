Title: War-Time Romance (1983)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Pyotr Todorovskiy
Main Cast: Nikolay Burlyaev, Natalya Andreychenko, Inna Churikova, Zinoviy Gerdt, Viktor Proskurin

Overview: This melodrama revolves around the post-war meeting reunion an intelligent front-line officer, now happily married, and a woman street vendor. This encounter reawakens in them submerged feelings of gratitude and tenderness as the officer recalls how they met during the war and what she used to mean to him. Now he learns that she is alone with a small daughter, the girl's father having been killed at the front.
