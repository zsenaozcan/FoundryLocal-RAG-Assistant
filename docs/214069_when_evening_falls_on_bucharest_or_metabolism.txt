Title: When Evening Falls on Bucharest or Metabolism (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Corneliu Porumboiu
Main Cast: Diana Avramut, Bogdan Dumitrache, Mihaela Sirbu, Alexandru Papadopol, Alex Jitea
Keywords: bucharest

Overview: A director with two weeks left on his latest production fakes an ulcer to pursue a romance with his lead actress.
