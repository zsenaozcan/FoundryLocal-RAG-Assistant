Title: Piccadilly Incident (1946)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Herbert Wilcox
Main Cast: Anna Neagle, Michael Wilding, Frances Mercer, Coral Browne, Edward Rigby

Overview: A newly married WREN, presumed drowned when her ship is torpedoed, spends three years on a tropical island before returning to England to find her husband remarried with a baby son.
