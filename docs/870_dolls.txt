Title: Dolls (2002)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Takeshi Kitano
Main Cast: Miho Kanno, Hidetoshi Nishijima, Tatsuya Mihashi, Chieko Matsubara, Kyoko Fukada
Keywords: japan, broken engagement, suicide attempt, allegory, new love, yakuza, pop star, bunraku, murder, eternal love

Overview: Dolls is a film about Japanese doll theater and a human love story illustrated through beautiful imagery. This film is a lot more sensitive than most Kitano films.
