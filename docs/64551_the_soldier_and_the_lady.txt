Title: The Soldier and the Lady (1937)
Genres: Adventure, History, Romance, War
Director: George Nichols Jr.
Main Cast: Adolf Wohlbrück, Akim Tamiroff, Elizabeth Allan, Margot Grahame, Fay Bainter
Keywords: war correspondent, rescue, impersonation

Overview: Based on a story by Jules Verne and featuring battle footage from a French film version of the tale, this epic action-adventure follows a courageous courier of the Czar Alexander II as he struggles to deliver vital information to Russian troops fighting a losing battle against the invading Tartar hoards in Siberia.
