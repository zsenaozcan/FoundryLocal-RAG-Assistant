Title: Mantervention (2014)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Stuart Acher
Main Cast: Nick Roux, Travis Van Winkle, Chloe Bridges, Max Carver, Sarah Baldwin

Overview: Spencer turns to his best friend who's planned an unorthodox "mantervention" filled with debauchery to convert him from hopeless romantic to forever player, and in the process they discover that being in love is not so bad after all.
