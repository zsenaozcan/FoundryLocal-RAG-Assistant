Title: Rebirth (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: James Whitaker
Main Cast: Nicholas Chirls, Tanya Villanueva Tepper, Ling Young, Tim Brown
Keywords: september 11 2001

Overview: Filmed over the ensuing years after the attack on New York's World Trade Center, this documentary takes a look a the physical and emotional healing process involved in the aftermath of such a tragedy.
