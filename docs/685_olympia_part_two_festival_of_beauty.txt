Title: Olympia Part Two: Festival of Beauty (1938)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Leni Riefenstahl
Main Cast: Jack Beresford, Ralf Berzsenyi, Ferenc Csík, Richard Degener, Willemijntje den Ouden
Keywords: gymnastics, nationalism, olympic games, nazis, national socialism, national socialist party, sport, nazi germany, olympia, woman director

Overview: The Second part of Olympia, a documentary about the 1936 Olympic games in Berlin by German Director Leni Riefenstahl. The film played in theaters in 1938 and again in 1952 after the fall of the Nazi Regime.
