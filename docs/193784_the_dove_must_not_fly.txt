Title: The Dove Must Not Fly (1970)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Sergio Garrone
Main Cast: Horst Buchholz, Sylva Koscina, William Berger, Riccardo Garrone, Giuseppe Castellano

Overview: An undercover Nazi in Libya aims to steal fuel and deliver it to a specific location in the desert where an Italian bomber would refuel on its way to Allied target.
