Title: The 24 Hour War (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Adam Carolla
Main Cast: Mario Andretti, Bob Bondurant, Ralph Nader, David Hobbs
Keywords: ferrari, le mans, ford

Overview: In the early 1960s, Henry Ford II and Enzo Ferrari went to war on the battlefield of Le Mans. This epic battle saw drivers lose their lives, family dynasties nearly collapse, and the development of a new car that changed racing.
