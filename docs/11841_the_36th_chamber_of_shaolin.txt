Title: The 36th Chamber of Shaolin (1978)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Liu Chia-Liang
Main Cast: Gordon Liu Chia-Hui, Lo Lieh, John Cheung Ng-Long, Wilson Tong, Wa Lun
Keywords: martial arts, kung fu, government, shaolin, kammer, shaolin kung fu, teachers and students

Overview: The anti-Ching patriots, under the guidance of Ho Kuang-han, have secretly set up their base in Canton, disguised as school masters. During a brutal Manchu attack, Lui manages to escape, and devotes himself to learning the martial arts in order to seek revenge.
