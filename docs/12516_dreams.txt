Title: Dreams (1990)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Akira Kurosawa
Main Cast: Akira Terao, Mitsuko Baisho, Toshie Negishi, Mieko Harada, Mitsunori Isaki
Keywords: life and death, artist, volcano, nuclear power plant, dream world, catastrophe, child

Overview: A collection of magical tales based upon the actual dreams of director Akira Kurosawa.
