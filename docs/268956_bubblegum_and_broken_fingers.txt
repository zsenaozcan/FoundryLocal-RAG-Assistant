Title: Bubblegum and Broken Fingers (2011)
Genres: Western, Crime, Drama
Director: Sean Jackson
Main Cast: Camme Tyla, Mandy Williams, Brenna Daly, Jason Nious, Paige Lauren Billiot

Overview: "If you don't have your own plan, you'll damn sure be a part of someone else's." That quote kicks off the first of multiple story lines, in the crime ensemble "Bubblegum &amp; Broken Fingers." This character-driven collage of sex, violence and survival is equal parts western, gangster and love stories. We follow the journey of a mysterious silver briefcase and witness the havoc it brings each new owner.
