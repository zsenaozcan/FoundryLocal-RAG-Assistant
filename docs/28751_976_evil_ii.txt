Title: 976-Evil II (1992)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jim Wynorski
Main Cast: Debbie James, Rene Assa, Patrick O'Bryan, Rod McCary, Philip McKeon
Keywords: telephone call

Overview: After being bestowed with demonic powers following a phone call to Hell, a psychotic teacher begins a rampage of death and destruction in a small town, forcing a teen and her boyfriend to fight him off so that they can get away.
