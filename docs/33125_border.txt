Title: Border (1997)
Genres: Action, Foreign, History
Director: J.P. Dutta
Main Cast: Sunny Deol, Jackie Shroff, Sunil Shetty, Akshaye Khanna, Puneet Issar

Overview: The year is 1971 when the Pakistani Army is at war with the Indian Soldiers. The Indian battalion is posted in Longewala region in Punjab and are just 150 in number while the Pakistani Army consists of 2000 armed soldiers with tanks.
