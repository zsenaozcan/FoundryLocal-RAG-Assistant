Title: Twenty Million Sweethearts (1934)
Genres: Music, Comedy
Director: Ray Enright
Main Cast: Pat O’Brien, Dick Powell, Ginger Rogers, The Mills Brothers, Allen Jenkins
Keywords: radio, musical, singer, fired from the job, waiter

Overview: Unscrupulous agent Pat O'Brien makes singing waiter Dick Powell a big radio star while Ginger Rogers, who has lost her own radio show, helps Powell.
