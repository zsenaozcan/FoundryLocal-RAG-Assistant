Title: Spine Tingler! The William Castle Story (2007)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jeffrey Schwarz
Main Cast: Forrest J. Ackerman, John Badham, Diane Baker, Sidney D. Balkin, Steve Bickel

Overview: Chronicles the last great American showman, filmmaker William Castle, a master of ballyhoo who became a brand name in movie horror with his outrageous audience participation gimmicks.
