Title: Million Dollar Crocodile (2012)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Lin Li-Sheng
Main Cast: Barbie Hsu, Guo Tao, Lam Suet, Shi Zhao-Qi, Xiong Xin-Xin

Overview: The film tells the story of an eight meter long crocodile on the rampage in Hangzhou. Xiao befriends Amao, a 36 foot crocodile, who lives on his fathers croc sanctuary. Soon his father is forced to sell the crocodiles to a gangster who plans to use them for high priced meals. The story begins.
