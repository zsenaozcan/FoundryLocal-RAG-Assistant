Title: Amelia Earhart: The Final Flight (1994)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Yves Simoneau
Main Cast: Diane Keaton, Rutger Hauer, Bruce Dern, Paul Guilfoyle, David Carpenter

Overview: A telling of the story of the famous U.S. aviatrix Amelia Earhart who attempted to fly solo around the world in 1937. She disappeared during the process.
