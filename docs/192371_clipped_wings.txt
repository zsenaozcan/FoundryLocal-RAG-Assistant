Title: Clipped Wings (1953)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Edward Bernds
Main Cast: Leo Gorcey, Huntz Hall, Bernard Gorcey, June Vincent, Mary Treen
Keywords: u.s. air force, slapstick, comedy, classic, bowery boys

Overview: Slip and correspondence school pilot Sach, go to an air base to help a pal out...but find themselves in when they mistakenly sign enlistment forms. Can the Air Force turn these dodos into eagles? The guys may be airborne airheads, But just watch them find a way to ground a spy network that's infiltrated the base.
