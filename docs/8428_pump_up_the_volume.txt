Title: Pump up the Volume (1990)
Genres: Music, Drama
Director: Allan Moyle
Main Cast: Christian Slater, Samantha Mathis, Annie Ross, Scott Paulin, Mimi Kennedy
Keywords: suicide, authority, hero, arizona, radio, nudity, chaos, freedom, cult, high school

Overview: Mark is an intelligent but shy teenager who has just moved to Arizona from the East Coast. His parents give him a short-wave radio so he can talk to his pals, but instead he sets up shop as pirate deejay Hard Harry, who becomes a hero to his peers while inspiring the wrath of the local high school principal.
