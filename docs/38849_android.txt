Title: Android (1982)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Aaron Lipstadt
Main Cast: Klaus Kinski, Brie Howard, Norbert Weisser, Crofton Hardester, Kendra Kirchner
Keywords: android, spaceship, independent film, escape, fugitive

Overview: Eccentric scientist Dr .Daniel and his shy assistant Max lead a quiet life on their space station, carrying out illegal research on androids, until they receive an unwelcome visit from three fugitives one of whom is female. Both Dr. Daniel and Max show an interest in her, but one of the other visitors has more sinister intentions.
