Title: Dirkie - Lost in the Desert (1969)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Jamie Uys
Main Cast: Wynand Uys, Jamie Uys, Lady Frolic of Belvedale, Wilhelm Esterhuizen, Sue Burman
Keywords: kalahari

Overview: An 8-year-old boy and his dog are left to face a vast desert wasteland alone after a plane crash, while an army of men and machines penetrate the desert searching for them. Based on true events.
