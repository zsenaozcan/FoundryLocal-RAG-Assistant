Title: Wild Bill (1995)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Walter Hill
Main Cast: Jeff Bridges, Ellen Barkin, John Hurt, Diane Lane, Keith Carradine
Keywords: opium, loss of eyesight, biography, sioux, wild bill hickok, calamity jane

Overview: Biopic about famous gunslinger Wild Bill Hickock. The early career of legendary lawman is telescoped and culminates in his relocation in Deadwood and a reunion with Calamity Jane.
