Title: ID:A (2011)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Christian E. Christiansen
Main Cast: Flemming Enevold, Carsten Bjørnlund, Arnaud Binard, Jens Jørn Spottag, Tuva Novotny

Overview: A woman wakes up in a river. Wounded and without memory, then races to elude mysterious followers and recover from amnesia.
