Title: The Uninvited (1996)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Horror
Director: Larry Shaw
Main Cast: Sharon Lawrence, Beau Bridges, Shirley Knight, Alex D. Linz, Lawrence Pressman

Overview: A family has just moved to its new home. Soon they begin to feel the presence of paranormal phenomena. Horror film, produced directly for television, which explains the classic story of owning a home. The life of a normal family will be shaken by forces beyond.
