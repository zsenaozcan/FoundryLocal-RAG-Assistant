Title: Duck Amuck (1953)
Genres: Animation
Director: Chuck Jones
Main Cast: Mel Blanc
Keywords: transformation, pencil, surrealism, bugs bunny, surprise ending, breaking the fourth wall, drawing, daffy duck, doppelganger, reference to longfellow

Overview: The short-tempered Daffy Duck must improvise madly as the backgrounds, his costumes, the soundtrack, even his physical form, shifts and changes at the whim of the animator.
