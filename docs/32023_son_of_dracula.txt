Title: Son of Dracula (1943)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror
Director: Robert Siodmak
Main Cast: Lon Chaney Jr., Louise Allbritton, Robert Paige, Frank Craven, J. Edward Bromberg
Keywords: sheriff, cemetery, monster, coffin, grave, vampire, voodoo, dracula, transformation, swamp

Overview: Carpathian Count Alucard is invited to the U.S. by a young "morbid" heiress. Her boyfriend and local officials are suspicious of the newcomer, who is interested in the "virile" soil of the new world.
