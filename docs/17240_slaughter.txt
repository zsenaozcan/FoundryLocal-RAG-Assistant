Title: Slaughter (2009)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Stewart Hopewell
Main Cast: Lucy Holt, Amy Shiels, David Sterne, Antonia Bernath, Andrei Araditz
Keywords: atlanta, barn, farm, slaughterhouse

Overview: A young woman looks to escape her abusive life by moving to her family's farm near Atlanta. Unfortunately, she learns her place of supposed comfort offers more terrifying forms of abuse.
