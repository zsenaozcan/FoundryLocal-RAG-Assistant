Title: We Think the World of You (1988)
Genres: Drama
Director: Colin Gregg
Main Cast: Alan Bates, Gary Oldman, Max Wall, Frances Barber, Liz Smith
Keywords: bisexuality, dog, parolee

Overview: An aimless young man, Johnny, is sent prison. He entrusts his beloved dog, Evie, to the care of his former lover and best friend, Frank. When he gets out of prison, he has to face difficulties at home. Added to this, is the fact that he may have to give up Evie to Frank.
