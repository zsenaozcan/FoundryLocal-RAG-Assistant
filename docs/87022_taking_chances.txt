Title: Taking Chances (2011)
Genres: Foreign
Director: Nicole van Kilsdonk
Main Cast: Johnny de Mol, Rifka Lodeizen, Ruben van der Meer, Pippa Allen
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Kiek is worried as her father works in a war zone. To lengthen the odds of her father getting hurt, she comes up with a strange and unique idea: she needs a dead dog and a dead mouse, because Kiek doesn't know one person who has a dead mouse, a dead dog and a dead father. Surely the odds against that are enormous?
