Title: Bunraku (2010)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Drama
Director: Guy Moshe
Main Cast: Josh Hartnett, Demi Moore, Woody Harrelson, Ron Perlman, Gackt Camui
Keywords: bartender, revenge, drifter, uncle nephew relationship

Overview: In a world with no guns, a mysterious drifter, a bartender and a young samurai plot revenge against a ruthless leader and his army of thugs, headed by nine diverse and deadly assassins.
