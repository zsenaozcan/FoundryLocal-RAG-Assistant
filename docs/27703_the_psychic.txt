Title: The Psychic (1977)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Horror
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Jennifer O'Neill, Gabriele Ferzetti, Marc Porel, Ida Galli, Gianni Garko
Keywords: murder, serial killer, clairvoyant

Overview: A woman with psychic powers has a vision of a murder that took place in a house owned by her husband.
