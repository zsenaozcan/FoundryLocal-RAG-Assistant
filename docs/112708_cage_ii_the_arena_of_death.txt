Title: Cage II: The Arena of Death (1994)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Lang Elliott
Main Cast: Lou Ferrigno, Reb Brown, Shannon Lee, James Shigeta, Gerald Okamura
Keywords: cage fighting, tournament

Overview: After being tricked into thinking his best friend, and caretaker is dead, Vietnam Vet, and mentally disabled person has no other choice but to enter The Arena Of Death!
