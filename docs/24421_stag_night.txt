Title: Stag Night (2008)
Genres: Action, Horror, Thriller
Director: Peter A. Dowling
Main Cast: Scott Adkins, Kip Pardue, Vinessa Shaw, Breckin Meyer, Karl Geary
Keywords: suspense, subway tunnel, nyc, cannibalism, no escape

Overview: Four guys on a bachelor party get off the subway at a station that shut down in the 70's and, after watching a transit cop get brutally murdered, find themselves running for their lives beneath the streets of NY.
