Title: Chinese Roulette (1976)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Rainer Werner Fassbinder
Main Cast: Anna Karina, Margit Carstensen, Brigitte Mira, Ulli Lommel, Alexander Allerson
Keywords: castle, mute, game, crutches, cheat on wife

Overview: A husband and wife lie to each other about their weekend travel plans, only to both show up at the family's country house with their lovers.
