Title: Lola (2006)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Javier Rebollo
Main Cast: Michaël Abiteboul, Lola Dueñas, Carmen Machi, Lucienne Deschamps, Victoria Sáez

Overview: The film is a French-Spanish production and most of the dialog is in French. 'Lo que se de Lola' - in English 'What I know about Lola' - is about a lonely guy, living an uneventful life in Paris. He takes care of his mother. One day, a girl with a more exciting life moves in his apartment building...
