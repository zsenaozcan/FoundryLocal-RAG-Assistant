Title: The Ring Finger (2005)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Diane Bertrand
Main Cast: Olga Kurylenko, Marc Barbé, Stipe Erceg, Edith Scob, Hanns Zischler
Keywords: sex, obsession, nudity, fetish, shoe, woman director

Overview: When she is slightly hurt in the factory where she works, Iris quits her job and finds a new one as an assistant in a laboratory of a very peculiar kind. Without fully grasping what is at play around her, she gradually engages in a disturbing love affair with her enigmatic employer.
