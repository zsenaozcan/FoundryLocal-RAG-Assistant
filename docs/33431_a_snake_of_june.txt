Title: A Snake of June (2002)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Romance, Foreign
Director: Shinya Tsukamoto
Main Cast: Shinya Tsukamoto, Asuka Kurosawa, Yûji Kôtari, Tomorowo Taguchi, Yukino Asai

Overview: Set in an anonymous Japanese metropolis, the film tells the tale of shy career woman, Rinko and Shigehiko, her hygiene-obsessed, workaholic husband. The couple explore their sexuality in a number of ways, causing their lives to be disrupted.
