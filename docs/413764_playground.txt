Title: Playground (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Bartosz M. Kowalski
Main Cast: Michalina Swistun, Nicolas Przygoda, Przemyslaw Balinski, Patryk Swiderski

Overview: Final day of school in a small Polish town. It's the very last chance for 12-year-old Gabrysia to tell her classmate that she has fallen in love with him. She sets up a secret meeting and blackmails the boy to show up. But what was supposed to be an intimate talk spins out of control and leads to an unexpected ending.
