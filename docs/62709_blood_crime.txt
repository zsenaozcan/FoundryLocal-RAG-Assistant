Title: Blood Crime (2002)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: William A. Graham
Main Cast: James Caan, Johnathon Schaech, Lisa Lackey, David Field, Paul Glover
Keywords: suspense

Overview: For burned-out Seattle cop Daniel Pruitt, a camping trip turns hellish when his wife, Jessica, is savagely attacked in the forest. En route to the hospital, the Pruitts collide with a semi, and Jessica identifies the driver as her assailant. Enraged, Pruitt pummels the man.
