Title: Patchwork (2015)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Tyler MacIntyre
Main Cast: Tory Stolper, Tracey Fairaway, Maria Blasucci, Corey Sorenson, James Phelps
Keywords: revenge, reference to frankenstein

Overview: A delightfully dark, Frankenstein-themed horror comedy about a re-animated corpse, made from the stitched together body parts of three murdered young women, that decides to go on a bloody quest to find their killer and avenge their deaths!
