Title: Chasing Beauty (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Brent Huff
Main Cast: Kelly Anderson, Jo Baker, William Barney, Dawn Ann Billings, Traci Bingham

Overview: CHASING BEAUTY provides a rare glimpse into the intriguing and complex world of modeling. Behind the glossy covers of Vogue and Glamour lie the rarely talked about, uncensored stories of what models endure and sacrifice to become a top model. CHASING BEAUTY examines body image and the psychological effects of the beauty business on young women and men.
