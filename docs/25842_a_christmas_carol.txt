Title: A Christmas Carol (1938)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Family
Director: Edwin L. Marin
Main Cast: Reginald Owen, Gene Lockhart, Kathleen Lockhart, Terry Kilburn, Barry MacKay
Keywords: holiday, scrooge, charles dickens

Overview: Reginald Owen portrays Charles Dickens' holiday humbug Ebenezer Scrooge, the miser's miser who has a huge change of heart after spirits whisk him into the past, present and future.
