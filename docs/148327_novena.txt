Title: Novena (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bernard Émond
Main Cast: Élise Guilbault, Patrick Drolet, Marie-Josee Bastien, Claude Binet, Luc Bourgeois
Keywords: quebec, loneliness, religion, silence, crisis of faith

Overview: Two men bond as one recovers from an attack and the other deals with the impending death of his grandmother.
