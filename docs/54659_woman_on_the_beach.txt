Title: Woman on the Beach (2006)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Hong Sang-soo
Main Cast: Kim Seung-woo, Song Seon-mi, Kim Tae-woo, Choi Ban-Ya, Ko Hyun-jung

Overview: Stymied by writer's block while crafting his latest script, director Kim Jung-rae persuades his friend Won Chang-wook to drive him to a beach resort—where he promptly becomes involved with Chang-wook's girlfriend. Abandoning her and taking up with another woman, Jung-rae winds up creating enough drama to inspire his writing.
