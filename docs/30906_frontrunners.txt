Title: Frontrunners (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Caroline Suh
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: competition, high school, documentary, election, candidate, woman director

Overview: A documentary on the competition for student body president at New York's Stuyvesant High School. As the notoriously competitive school's election draws near, the campaign becomes a microcosm for the nation at large, with race, gender and appearance vying for attention with real issues.
