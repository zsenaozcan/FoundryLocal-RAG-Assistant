Title: The Man Who Watched Trains Go By (1952)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Harold French
Main Cast: Claude Rains, Marius Goring, Märta Torén, Ferdy Mayne, Herbert Lom
Keywords: train ride, brit noir

Overview: A meek teller thinks he's killed his boss. He flees with a box of cash hoping for a new life with his younger mistress.
