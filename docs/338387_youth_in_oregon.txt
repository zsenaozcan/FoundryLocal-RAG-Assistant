Title: Youth in Oregon (2017)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Joel David Moore
Main Cast: Frank Langella, Billy Crudup, Christina Applegate, Nicola Peltz, Mary Kay Place
Keywords: oregon, cross country, reason for living, euthanized

Overview: A man is tasked with driving his embittered 80-year-old father-in-law cross country to be legally euthanized in Oregon, while along the way helping him rediscover a reason for living.
