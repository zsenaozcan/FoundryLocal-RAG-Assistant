Title: The 7 Grandmasters (1977)
Genres: Action, Foreign
Director: Joseph Kuo
Main Cast: Li Yi-Min, Jack Lung, Mark Lung, Corey Yuen Kwai, Nancy Yen Nan-See
Keywords: martial arts

Overview: An aged Kung Fu practitioner travels across China, challenging the best Grandmaster from each province to prove his mastery of martial arts. Meanwhile is a plot developing behind his back.
