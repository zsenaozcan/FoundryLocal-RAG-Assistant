Title: Nature of the Beast (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Romance, Thriller
Director: Rodman Flender
Main Cast: Eddie Kaye Thomas, Autumn Reeser, Eric Mabius, Paula Boudreau, George Buza

Overview: Julia and Rich are about to get married, but Rich has a hairy secret. Will Julia still marry him when she finds out about the monster "inside"?
