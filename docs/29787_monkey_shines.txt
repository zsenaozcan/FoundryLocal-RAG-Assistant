Title: Monkey Shines (1988)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: George A. Romero
Main Cast: Jason Beghe, John Pankow, Kate McNeil, Joyce Van Patten, Christine Forrest
Keywords: based on novel, paralysis, revenge, rage, monkey, disability, animal experimentation, science experiment, science run amok, animal testing

Overview: A quadriplegic man is given a trained monkey help him with every day activities, until the little monkey begins to develop feelings, and rage, against its new master and those who get too close to him.
