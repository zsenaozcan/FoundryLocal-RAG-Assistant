Title: In This Our Life (1942)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: John Huston
Main Cast: Bette Davis, Olivia de Havilland, George Brent, Dennis Morgan, Frank Craven
Keywords: melodrama

Overview: A young woman dumps her fiancée and runs off with her sister's husband. They marry, settle in Baltimore, and Stanley ultimately drives Peter to drink and suicide. Stanley returns home to Richmond only to learn that her sister Roy and old flame Craig have fallen in love and plan to marry. The jealous and selfish Stanley attempts to win back Craig's affections, but her true character is revealed when, rather than take the rap herself, she attempts to pin a hit and run accident on the young black clerk who works in Craig's law office.
