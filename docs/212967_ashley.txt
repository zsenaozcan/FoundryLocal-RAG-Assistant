Title: Ashley (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Dean Matthew Ronalds
Main Cast: Michael Madsen, Jennifer Taylor, Lauren C. Mayhew, Alex Arleo, Nicole Fox

Overview: A teenage girl, distraught from her vain attempt to connect with her estranged mother, resorts to cutting herself. When she develops an online relationship with an older woman, she learns to accept her sexuality and the endless solitude of sprawling suburbia.
