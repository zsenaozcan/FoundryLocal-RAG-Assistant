Title: Girl in Woods (2016)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Jeremy Benson
Main Cast: Juliet Reeves, Charisma Carpenter, Jeremy London, Lee Perkins, John Still
Keywords: suicide, female nudity, marriage proposal, nudity, trauma, cabin, mental breakdown, childhood trauma, forest, alone

Overview: After a tragic accident Grace is lost and alone in the Smoky Mountains. Grace's struggle for survival is made more complicated by her troubled past. Battling the demons in her mind may be the only way to come out alive.
