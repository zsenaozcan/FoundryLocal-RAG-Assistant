Title: The Discreet Charm of the Bourgeoisie (1972)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Luis Buñuel
Main Cast: Fernando Rey, Delphine Seyrig, Paul Frankeur, Bulle Ogier, Stéphane Audran
Keywords: upper class, date, terrorist, dream, restaurant, nightmare, bourgeoisie, guest, satire, dinner

Overview: A surreal, virtually plotless series of dreams centered around six middle-class people and their consistently interrupted attempts to have a meal together.
