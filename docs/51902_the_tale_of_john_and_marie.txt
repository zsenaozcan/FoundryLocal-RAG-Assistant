Title: The Tale of John and Marie (1980)
Genres: Animation, Adventure, Fantasy, Family
Director: Karel Zeman
Main Cast: Otakar Brousek sr., Antonín Procházka, Naďa Konvalinková, Jaroslav Moučka, František Filipovský

Overview: Young knight John travels the world in search of fame and fortune, but also to help others and prove himself. However, things go dire fast and he becomes burdened with life's hardships. But then he meets a pretty water nymph, Mary.
