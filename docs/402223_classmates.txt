Title: Classmates (2016)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Dmitriy Suvorov
Main Cast: Olga Kuzmina, Svetlana Khodchenkova, Ekaterina Vilkova, Valentina Mazunina, Roman Yunusov

Overview: Sochi, Russia. The day before wedding Katya finds her fiancé on a date with a hot blonde, so her classmates Vika, Sveta, and Dasha decide to find Katya a new husband. They have one night only. Will they succeed?
