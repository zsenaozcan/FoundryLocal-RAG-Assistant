Title: Katt Williams: American Hustle (2007)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Katt Williams
Keywords: live comedy, dark comedy

Overview: Stand-up comedian Katt Williams stars in this feature-length comedy. The film follows Katt as he takes a trip to Hollywood, where he hopes to break into the movie industry. But Katt's dreams lie in tatters when he is cast in increasingly ridiculous and stereotyped roles, so he remembers where his real loyalties lie, and sets off on a stand-up comedy road trip.
