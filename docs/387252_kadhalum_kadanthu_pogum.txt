Title: Kadhalum Kadanthu Pogum (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Nalan Kumarasamy
Main Cast: Vijay Sethupathi, Madonna Sebastian, Samuthirakani, RJ Ramesh Thilak, Rindhu Ravi
Keywords: street gang, interview, gangster, unemployment, goon

Overview: An unlikely romance brews between a tough-acting thug with a tender heart, and a recently single, IT consultant neighbor, in this lighthearted and wacky twist of a romantic comedy.
