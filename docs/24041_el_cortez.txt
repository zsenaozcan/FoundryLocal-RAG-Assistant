Title: El Cortez (2006)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Stephen Purvis
Main Cast: Lou Diamond Phillips, Bruce Weitz, Glenn Plummer, Tracy Middendorf, James McDaniel
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A man just released from a mental institution gets involved in a gold mine scheme while trying to avoid the cops, a wrathful drug dealer, and a sultry femme fatale.
