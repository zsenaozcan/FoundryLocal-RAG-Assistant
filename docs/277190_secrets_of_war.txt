Title: Secrets of War (2014)
Genres: War, Family, Drama
Director: Dennis Bots
Main Cast: Nils Verkooijen, Loek Peters, Maas Bronkhuyzen, Eva Duijvestein, Pippa Allen
Keywords: world war ii

Overview: Tuur and Lambert are best friends. But the war is closing in and is about to change their lives forever. Tuurs dad joined the resistance and even his big brother seems so be part of it. Lamberts family on the other hand choose to obey the Germans. Then a new girls from the city shows up, befriending the boys but telling her secret to only one of them. A choice that separates the boys and ultimately gets her in trouble.
