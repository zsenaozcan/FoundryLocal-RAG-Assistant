Title: The Ugly Duckling and Me! (2006)
Genres: Family, Animation, Comedy
Director: Michael Hegner
Main Cast: Morgan C. Jones, Paul Tylak, Anna Nugent, Gary Hetzler, Danna Davis
Keywords: surrealism, animation

Overview: The Hans Christian Anderson tale gets a new treatment, this time with a rat trying to exploit the talents of a little ugly duckling for profit.
