Title: Hot to Trot (1988)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michael Dinner
Main Cast: Bobcat Goldthwait, John Candy, Dabney Coleman, Virginia Madsen, Cindy Pickett

Overview: Fred P. Chaney receives as inheritance after the death of his mother a speaking horse that also has good knowledge about the stock-market. With the help of this horse Fred gains a lot at the stock-market of Chicago.
