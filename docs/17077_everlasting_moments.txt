Title: Everlasting Moments (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jan Troell
Main Cast: Maria Heiskanen, Mikael Persbrandt, Jesper Christensen, Emil Jensen, Callin Öhrvall
Keywords: camera, wine

Overview: In a time of social change and unrest, war and poverty, a young working class woman, Maria, wins a camera in a lottery. The decision to keep it alters her whole life.
