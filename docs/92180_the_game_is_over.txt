Title: The Game Is Over (1966)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Roger Vadim
Main Cast: Jane Fonda, Michel Piccoli, Peter McEnery, Tina Aumont, Jacques Monod
Keywords: france, nudity, revenge, deception

Overview: Renee Saccard is a pampered, selfish young wife of a middle-aged Parisian businessman who falls in love with her stepson but is driven to the point of madness when her husband tricks the stepson into betraying her.
