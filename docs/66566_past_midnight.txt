Title: Past Midnight (1991)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Romance, Thriller
Director: Jan Eliasberg
Main Cast: Rutger Hauer, Natasha Richardson, Clancy Brown, Kibibi Monie, Tom Wright
Keywords: social worker, police officer killed, woman director

Overview: A man gets out of prison after 15 years for stabbing his wife to death, and his social worker becomes convinced he was innocent. As she researches his case, and interviews other people who were involved, she begins to fall in love with him, as her friends warn her that he's dangerous.
