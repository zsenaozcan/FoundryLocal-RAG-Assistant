Title: Endless Poetry (2016)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama
Director: Alejandro Jodorowsky
Main Cast: Adan Jodorowsky, Brontis Jodorowsky, Pamela Flores, Leandro Taub, Alejandro Jodorowsky
Keywords: biography, surrealism, autobiographical

Overview: A portrait of Alejandro Jodorowsky’s young adulthood, set in the 1940s and 50s, in the electric capital city of Santiago. There, he decides to become a poet and is introduced, by destiny, into the foremost bohemian and artistic circle of the time.
