Title: Va Savoir (Who Knows?) (2001)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Jacques Rivette
Main Cast: Jeanne Balibar, Marianne Basler, Hélène de Fougerolles, Catherine Rouvel, Sergio Castellitto

Overview: Quick-witted, well-read cultured types revolve around each other in a delightful potpourri of theatre, romanticism and theft.
