Title: The Marrying Kind (1952)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: George Cukor
Main Cast: Judy Holliday, Aldo Ray, Madge Kennedy, Sheila Bond, John Alexander
Keywords: judge, marriage, divorce

Overview: Florence and Chet Keefer have had a troublesome marriage. Whilst in the middle of a divorce hearing the judge encourages them to remember the good times they have had hoping that the marriage can be saved.
