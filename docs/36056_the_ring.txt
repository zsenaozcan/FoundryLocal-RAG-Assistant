Title: The Ring (1927)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alfred Hitchcock
Main Cast: Carl Brisson, Lillian Hall-Davis, Ian Hunter, Forrester Harvey, Gordon Harker
Keywords: love triangle, boxer, woman between two men, silent film, prize fighter

Overview: Both Jack Sander and Bob Corby are boxers in love with Mabel. Jack and Mabel wed, but their marriage is flat. The young wife looks to Bob for comfort.
