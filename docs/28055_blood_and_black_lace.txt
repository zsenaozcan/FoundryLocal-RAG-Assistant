Title: Blood and Black Lace (1964)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Mario Bava
Main Cast: Cameron Mitchell, Eva Bartok, Thomas Reiner, Ariana Gorini, Dante DiPaolo
Keywords: blackmail, murder, giallo, masked killer, surprise ending, model, killer, psycho killer, proto-slasher

Overview: Inspector Sylvester is assigned to investigate the violent murder of a fashion house model Isabella, by a masked assailant. As the investigation proceeds all of the house’s various sins, including corruption, abortions, blackmail and drug addiction, begin to come to light. It turns out that Isabella had kept a diary detailing these vices, and now almost every employee becomes nervous.
