Title: Gangs (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Rainer Matsutani
Main Cast: Jimi Blue Ochsenknecht, Wilson Gonzalez Ochsenknecht, Emilia Schüle, Sina Tkotsch, Jannis Niewöhner

Overview: This is the story of 17-year-old Flo and his gang, the Rox. A story of great romance and hard choices. When his brother, the leader of the Rox, gets into trouble, Flo has to decide between the love of his life or his flesh and blood. It's a matter of life and death.
