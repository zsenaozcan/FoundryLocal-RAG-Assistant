Title: Fratricide (2005)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Yilmaz Arslan
Main Cast: Xevat Gectan, Erdal Celik, Bulent Buyukasik, Nurretin Celik, Yusef Geçtan

Overview: Two young Kurdish refugees, recently arrived in Germany struggle to make their way through the harshness of the occidental city. Their encounter with a gang of Turks will rapidly led them into an inevitable tragical spiral filled with death and revenge.
