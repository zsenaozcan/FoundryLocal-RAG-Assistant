Title: The Thrill of Brazil (1946)
Genres: Music
Director: S. Sylvan Simon
Main Cast: Evelyn Keyes, Keenan Wynn, Ann Miller, Allyn Joslyn, Tito Guízar
Keywords: dancing, dancer, brazil, musical

Overview: Steve, revue producer in Rio de Janeiro, is still in love with his ex-wife Vicki, his star Linda is in love with Steve and Tito is in love with Linda. Because of this they all get small problems.
