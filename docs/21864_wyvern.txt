Title: Wyvern (2009)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Steven R. Monroe
Main Cast: Nick Chinlund, Erin Karpluk, Barry Corbin, Don S. Davis, Elaine Miles
Keywords: dragon

Overview: The residents of a small Alaskan town find themselves under attack by a flying reptile known in medieval mythology as a Wyvern. It has thawed from its ancient slumber by melting icecaps caused by global warming.
