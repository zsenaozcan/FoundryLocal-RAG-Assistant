Title: Almost Human (2013)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Joe Begos
Main Cast: Graham Skipper, Josh Ethier, Vanessa Leigh, Susan T. Travers, Anthony Amaral III
Keywords: slasher, alien invasion, alien infection, axe murder

Overview: Mark Fisher disappeared from his home in a brilliant flash of blue light almost two years ago. His friend Seth Hampton was the last to see him alive. Now a string of grisly, violent murders leads Seth to believe that Mark is back, and something evil is living inside of him.
