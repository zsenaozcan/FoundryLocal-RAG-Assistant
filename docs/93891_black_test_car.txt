Title: Black Test Car (1962)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Foreign
Director: Yasuzô Masumura
Main Cast: Jirô Tamiya, Junko Kanô, Eiji Funakoshi, Hideo Takamatsu, Ichirô Sugai
Keywords: spy, confession, war veteran, highway, business man, playground, whiskey, crash, promotion, corporate crime
