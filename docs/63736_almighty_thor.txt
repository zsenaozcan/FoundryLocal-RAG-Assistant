Title: Almighty Thor (2011)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Christopher Ray
Main Cast: Cody Deal, Patricia Velásquez, Richard Grieco, Kevin Nash, Jess Allen

Overview: When the demon god Loki destroys the fortress of Valhalla and steals the Hammer of Invincibility, only the young hero Thor can protect Earth from armageddon.
