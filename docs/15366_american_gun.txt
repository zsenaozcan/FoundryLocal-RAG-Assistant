Title: American Gun (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Aric Avelino
Main Cast: Forest Whitaker, Marcia Gay Harden, Donald Sutherland, Lisa Long, Chris Warren, Jr.
Keywords: independent film, on the road

Overview: Seemingly disparate portraits of people -- among them a single mother, a high school principal, and an ace student -- Distinctly American -- all affected by the proliferation of guns in American society.
