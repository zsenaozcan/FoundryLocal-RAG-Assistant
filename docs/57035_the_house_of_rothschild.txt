Title: The House of Rothschild (1934)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Alfred L. Werker
Main Cast: George Arliss, Boris Karloff, Loretta Young, C. Aubrey Smith, Robert Young
Keywords: rothschild, financial empire

Overview: The story of the rise of the Rothschild financial empire founded by Mayer Rothschild and continued by his five sons. From humble beginnings the business grows and helps to finance the war against Napoleon, but it's not always easy, especially because of the prejudices against Jews.
