Title: Day and Night (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Simon Staho
Main Cast: Mikael Persbrandt, Maria Bonnevie, Sam Kessel, Tuva Novotny
Keywords: suicide, mother, childhood trauma, sister, alcoholism, mistress, illegal prostitution

Overview: DAY AND NIGHT is about people that love and want to be loved. It's the story of a father, his young son, his unfaithful wife, her secret lover, his young mistress, his lonely sister, his forgetful mother, a fanatic football coach, a pregnant whore and an angel disguised as an old man. They are all looking for the answer to the same question: "If love is the answer - what is the question?"
