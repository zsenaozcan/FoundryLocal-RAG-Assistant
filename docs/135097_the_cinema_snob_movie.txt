Title: The Cinema Snob Movie (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ryan Mitchelle
Main Cast: Brad Jones, Jake Norvell, Noah Antwiler, Zachary La Voy, Jillian Zurawski

Overview: When small time exploitation filmmakers Craig (Brad Jones) and Neil (Jake Norvell) are denied filming permits by the snooty head of the local film commission (Ryan Mitchelle), Craig’s only option is to go undercover as a pretentious film snob. But once accepted by the group Craig finds himself thrust into a strange and deadly mystery surrounding the very secretive club.
