Title: Pizza 2: Villa (2013)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Deepan Chakravarthy
Main Cast: Ashok Selvan, Sanchita Shetty, Nassar, S. J. Surya, Ramdoss
Keywords: supernatural, writer

Overview: Jebin is a writer who is trying to get his work published. He comes to understand his financial status after his father's death. There is also a villa in Pondycherry that has been bequeathed to him by his dad about which he had no clue till then. His ensuing journey to the villa sets the ball rolling for interesting events that get unfurled slowly.
