Title: Ga-ga: Glory to the Heroes (1986)
Genres: Comedy, Science Fiction, Horror, Drama
Director: Piotr Szulkin
Main Cast: Katarzyna Figura, Jerzy Trela, Daniel Olbrychski, Dorota Stalińska, Andrzej Chichłowski

Overview: In the 21st century, prisoners aboard penitentiary space ships explore unknown worlds. Scope, one of the prisoners is sent on a planet though to be lifeless, until he found "Humans" on it.
