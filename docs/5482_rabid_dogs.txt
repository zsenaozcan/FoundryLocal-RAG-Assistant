Title: Rabid Dogs (1974)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Thriller
Director: Mario Bava
Main Cast: Riccardo Cucciolla, Don Backy, Lea Lander, Maurice Poli, George Eastman
Keywords: rape, killer

Overview: Following a bungled robbery, three violent criminals take a young woman, a middle-aged man, and a child hostage and force them to drive them outside Rome to help them make a clean getaway.
