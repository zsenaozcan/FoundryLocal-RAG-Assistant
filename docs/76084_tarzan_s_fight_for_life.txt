Title: Tarzan's Fight for Life (1958)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: H. Bruce Humberstone
Main Cast: Gordon Scott, Eve Brent, Rickie Sorensen, Woody Strode, Jil Jarmyn
Keywords: tarzan, jungle, witch doctor

Overview: Dr. Sturdy is trying to establish a modern hospital in the jungle. His efforts are strongly opposed by Futa, the witch doctor, and Ramo, a native warrior.
