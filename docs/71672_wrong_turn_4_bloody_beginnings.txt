Title: Wrong Turn 4: Bloody Beginnings (2011)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Declan O'Brien
Main Cast: Jennifer Pudavick, Tenika Davis, Kaitlyn Wong, Terra Vnesa, Ali Tataryn
Keywords: murder, lesbian relationship, snow

Overview: Follows a group of friends that decide to go snowmobiling during their winter break. They make a "wrong turn", getting lost in a storm.
