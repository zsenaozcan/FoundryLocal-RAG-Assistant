Title: The End of Evangelion (1997)
Genres: Animation, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Hideaki Anno
Main Cast: Megumi Ogata, Megumi Hayashibara, Kotono Mitsuishi, Yuko Miyamura, Fumihiko Tachiki
Keywords: philosophy, post-apocalyptic, insanity, mecha, violence, dismemberment, psychological

Overview: NERV faces a brutal attack from SEELE, but with Asuka in a coma, and Shinji in a nervous breakdown, things soon turn into the surreal. This movie provides a concurrent ending to the final two episodes of the show "Neon Genesis Evangelion".
