Title: The Breakfast Club (1985)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: John Hughes
Main Cast: Emilio Estevez, Anthony Michael Hall, Judd Nelson, Molly Ringwald, Ally Sheedy
Keywords: high school, tardy hall, teenager, detention

Overview: Five high school students, all different stereotypes, meet in detention, where they pour their hearts out to each other, and discover how they have a lot more in common than they thought.
