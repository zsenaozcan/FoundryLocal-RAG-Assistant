Title: Giulia Doesn't Date at Night (2009)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Giuseppe Piccioni
Main Cast: Valerio Mastandrea, Valeria Golino, Sonia Bergamasco, Lidia Vitale, Chiara Nicola

Overview: Guido, an acclaimed author, leads an idyllic life with his beautiful wife and teenage daughter. But despite his seemingly perfect existence, Guido's restless search for inspiration leads him into the arms of Giulia, a charming and mysterious swim instructor, who is hiding a secret from her past.
