Title: Charles Darwin and the Tree of Life (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: David Attenborough
Main Cast: David Attenborough
Keywords: animal species, natural history, biology, evolution, science, darwin, natural selection, fossil

Overview: Darwin's great insight – that life has evolved over millions of years by natural selection – has been the cornerstone of all David Attenborough’s natural history series. In this documentary, he takes us on a deeply personal journey which reflects his own life and the way he came to understand Darwin’s theory.
