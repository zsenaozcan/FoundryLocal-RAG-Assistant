Title: Ju-on: The Curse (2000)
Genres: Horror
Director: Takashi Shimizu
Main Cast: Yūrei Yanagi, Yue, Ryôta Koyama, Yumi Yoshiyuki, Kazushi Andô
Keywords: haunted house, stalker, horror, curse, fear, vengeful ghost, ghost

Overview: A teacher visits the house of one of his students after the boy goes missing, only to have a horrifying excuse for his absence from school.
