Title: Innocence (1997)
Genres: Drama
Director: Zeki Demirkubuz
Main Cast: Güven Kıraç, Haluk Bilginer, Derya Alabora, Yalcin Cakmak, Iskender Altin

Overview: Yusuf is released from prison after serving a ten year sentence. He is scared of life outside as he goes to an address given to him by another prisoner.
