Title: The Severed Arm (1973)
Genres: Horror
Director: Thomas S. Alderman
Main Cast: Deborah Walley, Paul Carr, Marvin Kaplan, John Crawford, Vince Martorano
Keywords: cave, murder, slasher, violence, axe murder, radio dj, proto-slasher

Overview: Trapped in a cave, five men cut the arm off of another companion in order to ward off starvation. After they are saved, their victim seeks revenge on them one by one.
