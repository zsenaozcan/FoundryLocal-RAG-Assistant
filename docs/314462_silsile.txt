Title: Silsile (2014)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ozan Aciktan
Main Cast: Nehir Erdoğan, Ilker Kaleli, Tardu Flordun

Overview: Cenk has just arrived back to Istanbul from the United States. Suppressed love slowly begins to resurface after he encounters Ece, a woman whom he had a romantic relationship with in the past. Suddenly, there is a robbery attempt in a quiet and gloomy house, which results in a crime being committed.
