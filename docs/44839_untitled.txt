Title: Юленька (2009)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Horror
Director: Aleksandr Strizhenov
Main Cast: Marat Basharov, Darya Balabanova, Aleksandr Strizhenov, Irina Kupchenko

Overview: A university professor, who wants to slow down a little, moves with his wife and daughter, from the big city to a small town and starts a new job as a teacher in a girls' gymnasium. Something weird is happening with the class, they seem to hide a secret. As it turns out, these girls don't play with dolls...
