Title: The Cliff (2016)
Genres: Romance, Thriller
Director: Helena Taberna
Main Cast: Daniel Grao, Juana Acosta, Ingrid García Jonsson, Jon Kortajarena, Goya Toledo
Keywords: kidnapping, disappearance, woman director

Overview: A mass suicide involving members of a cult takes place in the Canary Islands. Gabriel's younger sister, Cordelia, who he hasn't seen for years, was one of the cult followers. Gabriel decides to travel there and find out what happened.
