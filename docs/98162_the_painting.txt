Title: The Painting (2011)
Genres: Fantasy, Animation
Director: Jean-François Laguionie
Main Cast: Jean Barney, Chloé Berthier, Julien Bouanich, Serge Faliu, Thierry Jahn
Keywords: france, painter, painting, worlds collide, class struggle

Overview: Three characters living in an unfinished painting venture out into the real world in search of their creator to convince him to finish his work.
