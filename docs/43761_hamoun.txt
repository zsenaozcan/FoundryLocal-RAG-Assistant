Title: Hamoun (1990)
Genres: Drama
Director: Dariush Mehrjui
Main Cast: Ezzatolah Entezami, Bita Farahi, Sedigheh Kianfar, Bita Farahi, Turan Mehrzad

Overview: Hamoon's wife is leaving him. He is also unsuccessfully trying to finish his Ph.D. thesis. He is forced to reexamine his life. In a series of flashbacks and dreams, Hamoon tries to figure out what he did wrong.
