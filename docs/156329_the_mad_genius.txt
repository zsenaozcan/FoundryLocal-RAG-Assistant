Title: The Mad Genius (1931)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Romance
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: John Barrymore, Marian Marsh, Charles Butterworth, Donald Cook, Luis Alberni
Keywords: cabaret, puppeteer, puppet, ballet, ballerina

Overview: A crippled puppeteer rescues an abused young boy and turns the boy into a great ballet dancer. Complications ensue when, as a young man, the dancer falls in love with a young woman the puppeteer is also in love with.
