Title: Everyone Loves Alice (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: Richard Hobert
Main Cast: Mikael Persbrandt, Marie Göranzon, Marie Richardsson, Lena Endre, Anastasios Soulis

Overview: A love triangle between a man, two women and three children. When love suddenly explodes in these people's everyday lives, it creates a pressure wave that changes the lives of everyone.
