Title: The Secret of the Third Planet (1981)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Roman Kachanov
Main Cast: Olga Gromova, Vsevolod Larionov, Yuri Volyntsev, Vasiliy Livanov, Grigoriy Shpigel
Keywords: starships, invisible, space adventure

Overview: At the end of the 22nd century Alisa Seleznyova, her father Professor Seleznyov and pilot Zelyony go on a space expedition to find rare animals for Moscow Zoo. On the way they seem to encounter a mysterious conspiracy led by Doctor Verhovtsev against legendary Two Captains Kim and Buran. The only clue is a talking bird Сhatterer [Govorun] that our heroes accidentally took possession of.
