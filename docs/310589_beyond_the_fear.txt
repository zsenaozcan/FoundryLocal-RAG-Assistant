Title: Beyond the Fear (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Maria Kravchenko
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: israel, yitzhak rabin, yigal amir

Overview: Delving deep into the mind of Yigal Amir, the rightwing Israeli radical who assassinated Prime Minister Yitzhak Rabin, the directors pore over his life-altering decisions, opening a window onto humankind’s capacity for both good and evil.
