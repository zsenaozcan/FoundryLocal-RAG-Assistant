Title: Bang Gang (A Modern Love Story) (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Eva Husson
Main Cast: Marilyn Lima, Daisy Broom, Finnegan Oldfield, Lorenzo Lefèbvre, Fred Hotier
Keywords: sexuality, skinny dipping, teenage sexuality, sex party

Overview: Biarritz. 16 year-old George, the high-school hottie, falls in love with Alex. To get his attention, she initiates a group game with Alex, Nikita, Laetitia and Gabriel. They will discover, test, and push the limits of their sexuality.
