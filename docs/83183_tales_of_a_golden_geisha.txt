Title: Tales of a Golden Geisha (1990)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Juzo Itami
Main Cast: Nobuko Miyamoto, Masahiko Tsugawa, Hiroshi Ôkôchi, Ryunosuke Kaneda, Mitsuko Ishii
Keywords: geisha

Overview: The film stars two of Itami's regular actors, Nobuko Miyamoto as a geisha who brings luck to the men with whom she sleeps, and Masahiko Tsugawa as her unfaithful, sometimes partner. As well as showing her relationships with the man she loves and the men who employ her, it satirizes corruption and the influence of money in Japanese politics.
