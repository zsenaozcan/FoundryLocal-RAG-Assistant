Title: Species: The Awakening (2007)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Nick Lyon
Main Cast: Edy Arellano, Marco Bacuzzi, Ben Cross, Roger Cudney, Cynthia Francesconi
Keywords: mexico, alien, pregnancy, scientist, bullet time, alien dna, direct to video sequel to theatrical movie

Overview: A scientist, Dr Holander, takes his niece Miranda to Mexico in an attempt to reverse the effects of the alien DNA he used to create her. However the treatment goes horribly wrong, and sets Miranda on a killing spree as she sets out to find a mate.
