Title: A Bag of Marbles (2017)
Genres: Drama
Director: Christian Duguay
Main Cast: Dorian Le Clech, Batyste Fleurial, Patrick Bruel, Elsa Zylberstein, Christian Clavier
Keywords: 1940s, occupied france

Overview: In occupied France, Maurice and Joseph, two young Jewish brothers left to their own devices demonstrate an incredible amount of cleverness, courage, and ingenuity to escape the enemy invasion and to try to reunite their family once again.
