Title: Down Here (2014)
Genres: Mystery, Crime, Drama
Director: Teach Grant
Main Cast: Dean Wray, Tantoo Cardinal, Martin Cummins, Michael Rogers, Brent Stait

Overview: When an alcoholic detective fights to find a murderer targeting street workers in Vancouver's Downtown Eastside, he struggles to escape the haunting memories of his past and unexpectedly finds solace within the fragile community he endeavours to protect.
