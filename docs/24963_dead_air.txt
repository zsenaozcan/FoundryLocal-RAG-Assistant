Title: Dead Air (2009)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Corbin Bernsen
Main Cast: Bill Moseley, Patricia Tallman, Navid Negahban, David Moscow, Joshua Feinman
Keywords: radio transmission, suspense, terrorism, rage, virus

Overview: Logan Burnhardt is the ego-king of the airwaves, but his unflappable persona is put to the test when a terrorist bio-attack unleashes a plague of flesh-ripping maniacs on Los Angeles.
