Title: Goodbye Bafana (2007)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Bille August
Main Cast: Joseph Fiennes, Dennis Haysbert, Diane Kruger, Shiloh Henderson, Patrick Lyster
Keywords: jail guard, prison cell, south africa, apartheid, nelson mandela, escape, xenophobia

Overview: The true story of a white South African racist whose life was profoundly altered by the black prisoner he guarded for twenty years. The prisoner's name was Nelson Mandela.
