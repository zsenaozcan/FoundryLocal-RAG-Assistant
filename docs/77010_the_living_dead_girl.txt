Title: The Living Dead Girl (1982)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jean Rollin
Main Cast: Marina Pierro, Françoise Blanchard, Mike Marshall, Carina Barone, Fanny Magier
Keywords: chemical, castle, living dead, female vampire

Overview: A toxic spill revives a beautiful, dead heiress who, with the help of her childhood friend, must quench her insatiable thirst for blood
