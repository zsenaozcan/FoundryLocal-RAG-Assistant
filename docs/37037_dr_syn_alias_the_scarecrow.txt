Title: Dr. Syn, Alias the Scarecrow (1963)
Genres: Adventure, Family
Director: James Neilson
Main Cast: Patrick McGoohan, George Cole, Tony Britton, Michael Hordern, Geoffrey Keen
Keywords: scarecrow, british army, costume, taxes, smuggling, animation, oppression, press gang

Overview: This Disney drama, originally broadcast as a three-part TV episode, tells the story of a vicar's double life. Outwardly, he is the model of upstanding citizenship and loyalty to an oppressive British government. But he is also a notorious smuggler who uses his ill-gotten gains to benefit his impoverished village.
