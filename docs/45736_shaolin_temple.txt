Title: Shaolin Temple (1976)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Chang Cheh
Main Cast: Alexander Fu Sheng, Chi Kuan-Chun, Wai Wang, David Chiang, Ti Lung

Overview: There is no place more hallowed in the martial art world than China's Shaolin Temple. This special place deserves a special epic, which is what the martial arts maestro delivers in this battle between a brave brand of Chinese boxers and literally thousands of Ching troops - complete with betrayals, intrigues, and such novel fighting machines as 108 wooden robots. The conflicts grow in complexity, intensity and even suspense as monks struggle to stay alive in the face of overwhelming odds.
