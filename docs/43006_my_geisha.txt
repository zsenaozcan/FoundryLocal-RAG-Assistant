Title: My Geisha (1962)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Jack Cardiff
Main Cast: Shirley MacLaine, Yves Montand, Edward G. Robinson, Robert Cummings, Yoko Tani
Keywords: geisha

Overview: A director's (Yves Montand) wife (Shirley MacLaine) poses as a geisha to win the lead in his Japanese production of "Madame Butterfly."
