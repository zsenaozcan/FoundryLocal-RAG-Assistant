Title: Day of the Cobra (1980)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime, Foreign
Director: Enzo G. Castellari
Main Cast: Franco Nero, Sybil Danning, Massimo Vanni, Sasha D'Arc, Ennio Girolami
Keywords: italian, private detective

Overview: An American narcotics agent is murdered in Italy. The head of the department decides to hire Larry Stanziani, 'The Cobra'. Larry, a former agent and now a third-rate private detective, uses the opportunity to get even with an old enemy, but soon he finds out he's facing a relentless organization
