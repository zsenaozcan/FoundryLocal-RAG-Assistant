Title: My Life Without Me (2003)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Isabel Coixet
Main Cast: Sarah Polley, Amanda Plummer, Scott Speedman, Leonor Watling, Deborah Harry
Keywords: farewell, responsability, dying and death, night shift, daughter, secret love, mother daughter relationship, woman director

Overview: A Pedro Almodovar production in which a fatally ill mother with only two months to live creates a list of things she wants to do before she dies with out telling her family of her illness.
