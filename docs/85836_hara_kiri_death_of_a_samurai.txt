Title: Hara-Kiri: Death of a Samurai (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Takashi Miike
Main Cast: Koji Yakusho, Naoto Takenaka, Eita, Hikari Mitsushima, Ebizo Ichikawa
Keywords: samurai

Overview: An tale of revenge, honor and disgrace, centering on a poverty-stricken samurai who discovers the fate of his ronin son-in-law, setting in motion a tense showdown of vengeance against the house of a feudal lord.
