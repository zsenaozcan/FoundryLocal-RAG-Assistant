Title: Rafa (2012)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Family
Director: João Salaviza
Main Cast: Rodrigo Perdigão, Joana de Verona
Keywords: skateboarding, coming of age, kids

Overview: Rafa is a 13 years old kid concerned with his mother, held in a police station for driving without a license.  The director shows a day in the life of a teenager who lives on a problematic area and discovers that her mother was arrested because of an automobile accident.
