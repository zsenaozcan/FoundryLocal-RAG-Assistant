Title: (Absolutions) Pipilotti's Mistakes (1988)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Pipilotti Rist
Main Cast: Pipilotti Rist
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Precisely edited to the start-stop rhythm of a martial beat and post-punk rock music, Absolutions glories in organized disjunction, juxtaposing images of the artist collapsing to the ground with bursts of wildly scrambled electronic distortion.
