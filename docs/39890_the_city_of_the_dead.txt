Title: The City of the Dead (1960)
Genres: Mystery, Horror
Director: John Llewellyn Moxey
Main Cast: Christopher Lee, Dennis Lotis, Patricia Jessel, Tom Naylor, Venetia Stevenson
Keywords: witch, burning of witches, witch burning, witchcraft

Overview: A young coed (Nan Barlow) uses her winter vacation to research a paper on witchcraft in New England. Her professor recommends that she spend her time in a small village called Whitewood. He originally cam from that village so he also recommends she stay at the "Raven's Inn," run by a Mrs. Newlis. She gets to the village and notices some weird happenings, but things begin to happen in earnest when she finds herself "marked" for sacrifice by the undead coven of witches. It seems that the innkeeper is actually the undead spirit of Elizabeth Selwyn, and the "guests" at the inn are the other witches who have come to celebrate the sacrifice on Candalmas Eve. As one of them said when Nan walked away, "HE will be PLEASED."
