Title: When Night Falls on the Reeperbahn (1967)
Genres: Thriller, Crime
Director: Rolf Olsen
Main Cast: Erik Schumann, Fritz Wepper, Jürgen Draeger, Marianne Hoffmann, Heinz Reincke

Overview: A crime reporter (Erik Schumann) uncovers a lurid plot by a Hamburg gang to dose impressionable young girls with LSD in order to enslave them into prostitution. Featuring mad killers, psychedelic acid trips and ladies mud wrestling!
