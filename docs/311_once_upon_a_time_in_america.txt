Title: Once Upon a Time in America (1984)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Sergio Leone
Main Cast: Robert De Niro, James Woods, Elizabeth McGovern, Joe Pesci, Burt Young
Keywords: life and death, corruption, street gang, rape, sadistic, lovesickness, sexual abuse, money laundering, opium

Overview: A former Prohibition-era Jewish gangster returns to the Lower East Side of Manhattan over thirty years later, where he once again must confront the ghosts and regrets of his old life.
