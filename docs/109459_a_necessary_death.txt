Title: A Necessary Death (2008)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Daniel Stamm
Main Cast: G.J. Echternkamp, Matthew Tilley, Valerie Hurt, Michael Traynor, Konima Parkinson-Jones
Keywords: suspense

Overview: "Documentary Filmmaker looking for suicidal individual to follow from first preparation to final act." Cut from 142 video tapes, this project sheds light on the tragedy following the infamous Internet ad.
