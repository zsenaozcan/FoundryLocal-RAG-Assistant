Title: Spaghetti Story (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ciro De Caro
Main Cast: Valerio Di Benedetto, Cristian Di Sante, Sara Tosti

Overview: Nowadays, four Italian young adults longing to change their lives, yet stuck upon the shelf: Valerio and Kookie, dreaming of their big break, yet still not being independent; Giovanna and Serena, pretending to be adult, yet never having dared to live. Then, the encounter with Mei Mei, a young Chinese prostitute, shines a light on the truth: the gain of one’s freedom is, above all, an inside job.
