Title: The Cossacks (1928)
Genres: Drama
Director: George W. Hill
Main Cast: John Gilbert, Renée Adorée, Ernest Torrence, Nils Asther, Paul Hurst
Keywords: silent film

Overview: Stirring romance, hard riding, desperate fighting with the Cossacks playing their game of war and chivalry. A mighty picturization of Count Leo Tolstoi's famous novel of the same name.
