Title: Sweeney Todd (2006)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller, Music
Director: David Moore
Main Cast: Ray Winstone, Essie Davis, David Warner, Tom Hardy, David Bradley
Keywords: tv movie, barber

Overview: A BBC adaptation of the Victorian "penny dreadful" tale of 18th century "demon barber" Sweeney Todd, of Fleet Street, who cuts the throats of unsuspecting clients in his London shop.
