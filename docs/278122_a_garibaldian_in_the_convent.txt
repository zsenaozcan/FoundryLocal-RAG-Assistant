Title: A Garibaldian in the Convent (1942)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Vittorio De Sica
Main Cast: Leonardo Cortese, María Mercader, Carla Del Poggio, Fausto Guerzoni, Olga Vittoria Gentilli

Overview: An old woman's poignant reminiscence of her youth in a convent school, the happy moments and the sad, and her tragic love for a Garibaldian.
