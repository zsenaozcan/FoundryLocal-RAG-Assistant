Title: Last Night (2010)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Massy Tadjedin
Main Cast: Keira Knightley, Sam Worthington, Eva Mendes, Guillaume Canet, Griffin Dunne
Keywords: new york, infidelity, ex-girlfriend, loyalty, trip, marriage, party, business, writer, trust

Overview: The story follows a married couple, apart for a night while the husband takes a business trip with a colleague to whom he's attracted. While he's resisting temptation, his wife encounters her past love.
