Title: Cas & Dylan (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jason Priestley
Main Cast: Tatiana Maslany, Richard Dreyfuss, Aaron Poole, Natalie Radford

Overview: A dying Doctor, who plans to check out on his own terms, takes a reluctant detour when he inadvertently winds up on the lam with an 'anything-but-normal' 22-year-old girl.
