Title: Hiding Out (1987)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Drama, Family, Thriller
Director: Bob Giraldi
Main Cast: Jon Cryer, Keith Coogan, Annabeth Gish, Tim Quill, John Spencer
Keywords: high school, youth

Overview: A very successful stock broker is called to court to testify against a mob boss who was into some inside trading. Andrew Morenski must become Max Hauser and go back to high school for protection from the mob.
