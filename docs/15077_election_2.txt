Title: Election 2 (2006)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Johnnie To
Main Cast: Louis Koo, Simon Yam, Nick Cheung, Gordon Lam Ka-Tung, Lam Suet
Keywords: suspense, organized crime, gangster, criminal, triad

Overview: As election time nears, current Triad chairman Lok (Simon Yam) faces competition from his godsons. At the same time, Jimmy (Louis Koo) looks to increase his business relations with mainland China.
