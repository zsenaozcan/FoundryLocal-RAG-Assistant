Title: Two Mules for Sister Sara (1970)
Genres: Western
Director: Don Siegel
Main Cast: Shirley MacLaine, Clint Eastwood, Alberto Morin, John Kelly, Enrique Lucero
Keywords: gunslinger, mexico, french, treasure hunt, illegal prostitution

Overview: Hogan, on his way to do some reconnaissance for a future mission to capture a French fort, encounters Sister Sara, a nun in trouble. Before he knows it, Hogan is accompanying Sister Sara in the dangerous frontier while she seeks to achieve a hidden goal.
