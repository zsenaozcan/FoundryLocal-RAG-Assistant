Title: One Magic Christmas (1985)
Genres: Family, Fantasy
Director: Phillip Borsos
Main Cast: Mary Steenburgen, Harry Dean Stanton, Gary Basaraba, Elisabeth Harnois, Arthur Hill
Keywords: holiday, mother daughter relationship, christmas

Overview: Ginny Grainger, a young mother, rediscovers the joy and beauty of Christmas, thanks to the unshakable faith of her six-year-old daughter Abbie and Gideon, Ginny's very own guardian angel.
