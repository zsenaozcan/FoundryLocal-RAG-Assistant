Title: Another World (2014)
Genres: Action, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Eitan Reuven
Main Cast: Zach Cohen, Susanne Gschwendtner, Davina Kevelson, David Lavenski, Carl McCrystal
Keywords: zombie, brutality, biological warfare, female warrior

Overview: The movie is a post-apocalyptic horror/science fiction film. the setting is in a near post apocalyptic future where a biological warfare program goes wrong, and turns most of humanity to mindless, murderous creatures.
