Title: An Alternative Reality: The Football Manager Documentary (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Louis Myles
Main Cast: Vicky McClure
Keywords: video game, american football

Overview: "It’s the story of the people who escape to the alternative reality of being a football manager, and the effect this has had on their lives. It’s also the story of how a computer game made by football fans has become a part of the world it set out to replicate." - Miles Jacobson
