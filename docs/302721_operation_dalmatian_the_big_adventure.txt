Title: Operation Dalmatian: The Big Adventure (1997)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Michael Paul Girard
Main Cast: Jo Clark, Nick Lashaway, Jessica-Snow Wilson, Dee Hengstler, Ken Tipton

Overview: What do you get when you cross a cute rambunctious girl, a nutty professor, a mischievous medicine man and a colorful dalmatian with a stuffy dog show... pure puppy pandemonium! Travels back in time with Grandma Julietta to her humble childhood, when she found her beloved Dalmatian, Baskerville. Join the outrageous cast of characters on this great big adventure!
