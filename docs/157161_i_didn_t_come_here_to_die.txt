Title: I Didn't Come Here to Die (2010)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Bradley Scott Sullivan
Main Cast: Kurt Cole, Madi Goff, Indiana Adams, Niko Red Star, Emmy Robbin

Overview: The story of six young volunteers working on a humanitarian project in the woods. Horrific accidents, rash decisions and the unpredictability of human nature leads them all to the same disturbing conclusion.
