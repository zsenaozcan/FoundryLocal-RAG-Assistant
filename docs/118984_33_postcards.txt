Title: 33 Postcards (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Pauline Chan
Main Cast: Guy Pearce, Zhu Lin, Claudia Karvan, Elaine Jin, Rhys Muldoon
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Dean Randall has sponsored a young Chinese orphan Mei Mei for many years, when she arrives in Sydney out of the blue to thank him, their lives are changed forever.
