Title: Four Bags Full (1956)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Claude Autant-Lara
Main Cast: Jean Gabin, Bourvil, Jeannette Batti, Louis de Funès, Georgette Anys
Keywords: paris, black market, suitcase

Overview: Two men, a painter and a poor guy have to cross over Paris by night during world war II and nazi occupation to delivery black market meat. As they walk along dark parisian streets they encounter various characters and adventures until they are arrested by German police.
