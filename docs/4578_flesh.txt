Title: Flesh (1968)
Genres: Drama
Director: Paul Morrissey
Main Cast: Joe Dallesandro, Geraldine Smith, Patti D'Arbanville, Louis Waldon, Barry Brown
Keywords: male nudity, hustler, punter, family secrets, independent film, illegal prostitution, penis, unemployment

Overview: A heroin junkie works as a prostitute to support his habit and fund an abortion needed by the girlfriend of his lesbian wife. His seedy encounters with delusional and damaged clients, and dates with drag queens and hustlers are heavy on sex, drugs and decadence.
