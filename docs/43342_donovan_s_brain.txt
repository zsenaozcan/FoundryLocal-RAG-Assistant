Title: Donovan's Brain (1953)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Felix E. Feist
Main Cast: Lew Ayres, Gene Evans, Nancy Reagan, Steve Brodie, Tom Powers
Keywords: mad doctor, brain surgery

Overview: A scientist takes a the brain of dead man and revives it via electrodes as it lays suspended in a tank of liquid. Soon, the brain grows to possess enormous psychic powers and inflicts its personality upon the doctor who saved creating a "Jekyll and Hyde" paradigm.
