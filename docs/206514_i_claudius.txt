Title: I, Claudius (1976)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Herbert Wise
Main Cast: Derek Jacobi, Siân Phillips, George Baker, John Hurt, Brian Blessed
Keywords: emperor, ancient rome, british, epic, history, betrayal, miniseries, political intrigue 

Overview: Tracing the lives of several Roman emperors, this is an epic of ruthless ambition, shocking debauchery and murderous intrigue set in one of history's most fascinating eras. Bearing witness to the saga is Claudius, whose stutter and limp have marked him a fool - yet whom prophecies have foretold will one day rule Rome.
