Title: American Movie (1999)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Chris Smith
Main Cast: Mark Borchardt, Mike Schank, Tom Schimmels, Monica Borchardt, Alex Borchardt
Keywords: underdog, obsession, wisconsin, ambition, beer, independent film, filmmaker

Overview: AMERICAN MOVIE is the story of filmmaker Mark Borchardt, his mission, and his dream. Spanning over two years of intense struggle with his film, his family, financial decline, and spiritual crisis, AMERICAN MOVIE is a portrayal of ambition, obsession, excess, and one man's quest for the American Dream.
