Title: Ground Control (1998)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Thriller
Director: Richard Howard
Main Cast: Kiefer Sutherland, Kristy Swanson, Robert Sean Leonard, Kelly McGillis, Margaret Cho
Keywords: new year's eve, airplane, suspense, storm, air traffic control
