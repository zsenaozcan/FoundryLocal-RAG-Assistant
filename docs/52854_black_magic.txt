Title: Black Magic (1975)
Genres: Horror
Director: Ho Meng-Hua
Main Cast: Ti Lung, Lo Lieh, Tanny Tien-Ni, Lily Li Li-Li, Ku Feng
Keywords: black magic, good vs evil, breast milk

Overview: A magician makes money by charging people to cast love spells on the objects of their affection. Complications arise when he decides that he wants a customer's bride for himself.
