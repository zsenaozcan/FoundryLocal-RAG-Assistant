Title: The Sign of Venus (1955)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Dino Risi
Main Cast: Franca Valeri, Sophia Loren, Vittorio De Sica, Raf Vallone, Tina Pica

Overview: The Sign of Venus (Italian: Il segno di Venere) is a 1955 Italian comedy film directed by Dino Risi and starring Sophia Loren. It was entered into the 1955 Cannes Film Festival.
