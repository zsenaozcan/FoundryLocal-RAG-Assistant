Title: Chrysalis (2014)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: John Klein
Main Cast: Sara Gorsky, Cole Simon, Tanya Thai McBride, Clayton Stamper, Kate Black-Spence
Keywords: terror, hope, post-apocalyptic, suspense, gore, zombie, zombie apocalypse

Overview: Joshua and Penelope are survivors of a deadly infection that laid waste to humanity 25 years ago. When they encounter fellow survivor Abira, their lives are forever changed as they fight off the remnants of the infected.
