Title: Sheitan Warrior (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Horror
Director: Stevan Filipović
Main Cast: Radovan Vujović, Staša Koprivica, Miloš Tanasković, Vladimir Tešović, Vujadin Milošević

Overview: Trouble usually starts when the power falls into the wrong hands. That's how the book that hides an ancient evil fell to Stanislav. It does not matter whether Stanislav is a nice guy who helps old ladies cross the street, a psychopath, nerd, or a blood-thirsty young man.
