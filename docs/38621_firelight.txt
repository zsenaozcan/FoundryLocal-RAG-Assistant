Title: Firelight (1997)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: William Nicholson
Main Cast: Sophie Marceau, Stephen Dillane, Dominique Belcourt, Kevin Anderson, Joss Ackland
Keywords: england, estate, governess, 19th century

Overview: Firelight is a period romance/drama film written and directed by William Nicholson. It was Nicholson's first, and to date only, film as director.
