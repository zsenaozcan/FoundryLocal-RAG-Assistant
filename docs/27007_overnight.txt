Title: Overnight (2003)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Mark Brian Smith
Main Cast: Troy Duffy, Billy Connolly, Norman Reedus, Jake Busey, Emmanuelle Béart
Keywords: from rags to riches, filmmaker, filmmaking, riches to rags

Overview: Alternately hilarious and horrifying, Overnight chronicles one man's misadventures of making a Hollywood movie. It starts out as a rags to riches story as Troy Duffy, a Boston-bred bartender, sells his first screenplay for The Boondock Saints.
