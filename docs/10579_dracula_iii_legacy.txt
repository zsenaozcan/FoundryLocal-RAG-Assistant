Title: Dracula III: Legacy (2005)
Genres: Horror
Director: Patrick Lussier
Main Cast: Alexandra Wescourt, Jason Scott Lee, Jason London, Diane Neal, Claudiu Bleont
Keywords: suicide, female nudity, rebel, helicopter, whip, shotgun, orgy, sword, horse, rocket launcher

Overview: In the near future, Uffizi and Luke travel to the remote reaches of war torn Romania to rescue Elizabeth and finish the vampire once and for all. Along the way, they encounter TV news journalist and a corps of rebels trying to fight the vampire uprising which plagues their country.
