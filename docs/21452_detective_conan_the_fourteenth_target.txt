Title: Detective Conan: The Fourteenth Target (1998)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Kenji Kodama
Main Cast: Minami Takayama, Kappei Yamaguchi, Akira Kamiya, Wakana Yamazaki, Chafurin
Keywords: revenge, murder, mystery

Overview: Ran has a nightmare about her mother dying, but starts to remember what really happened involving herself, her mother, and her father from 10 years ago. Meanwhile, people are being injured by a certain culprit.
