Title: Honeydripper (2007)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: John Sayles
Main Cast: Danny Glover, Lisa Gay Hamilton, Charles S. Dutton, Brent Jennings, Yaya DaCosta
Keywords: alabama

Overview: In 1950s Alabama, the owner of the Honeydripper juke joint finds his business dropping off and against his better judgment, hires a young electric guitarist in a last ditch effort to draw crowds during harvest time.
