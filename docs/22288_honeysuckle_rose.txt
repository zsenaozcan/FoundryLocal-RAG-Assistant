Title: Honeysuckle Rose (1980)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance
Director: Jerry Schatzberg
Main Cast: Willie Nelson, Dyan Cannon, Amy Irving, Slim Pickens, Joey Floyd
Keywords: texas, on the road

Overview: Buck Bonham (Willie Nelson) is a country singer, with a good family, struggling to find national fame. He juggles his music career with his responsibilities to his wife and son. He has everything going his way until the daughter of his former guitarist joins his tour. The road leads to temptation, which leads to his downfall. The only question is will his family and friends stand by him?
