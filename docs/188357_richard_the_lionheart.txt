Title: Richard The Lionheart (2013)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Stefano Milla
Main Cast: Malcolm McDowell, Gregory Chandler Maness, Burton Perez, Umberto Procopio

Overview: King Henry II tests the loyalty and honor of his son Richard sending him to a secret castle known as the Knight's Martyr. There, the Prince must fight against adversaries representing the virtues of a knight.
