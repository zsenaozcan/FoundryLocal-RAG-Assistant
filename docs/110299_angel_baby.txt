Title: Angel Baby (1961)
Genres: Drama
Director: Paul Wendkos
Main Cast: George Hamilton, Mercedes McCambridge, Joan Blondell, Henry Jones, Burt Reynolds
Keywords: faith, blindness, southern, faith healer

Overview: A woman who believes she has been chosen by God to heal people is taken in by a greedy promoter and his shrewish wife to make the rounds of the rural South - she to save souls and heal the sick, he to make as much money as he possibly can.
