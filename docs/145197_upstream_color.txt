Title: Upstream Color (2013)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Shane Carruth
Main Cast: Amy Seimetz, Shane Carruth, Andrew Sensenig, Thiago Martins, Carolyn King
Keywords: sadness, hypnosis, hope, loss, romance, independent film, drugged, parasite

Overview: A man and woman are drawn together, entangled in the lifecycle of an ageless organism. Identity becomes an illusion as they struggle to assemble the loose fragments of wrecked lives.
