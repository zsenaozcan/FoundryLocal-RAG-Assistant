Title: Mouth to Mouth (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Foreign
Director: Manuel Gómez Pereira
Main Cast: Javier Bardem, Aitana Sánchez-Gijón, Josep Maria Flotats, María Barranco, Myriam Mézières

Overview: For financial reasons an unemployed aspiring actor works for a phone-sex enterprise. One day he receives a call from the mysterious wife of one of his best clients.
