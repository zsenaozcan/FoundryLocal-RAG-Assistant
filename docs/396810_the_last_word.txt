Title: The Last Word (2017)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Mark Pellington
Main Cast: Shirley MacLaine, Amanda Seyfried, Anne Heche, Thomas Sadoski, Philip Baker Hall
Keywords: suicide attempt, vandalism, friendship, road trip, advertising executive, obituary, radio dj, obituary writer, essay writer

Overview: A retired businesswoman – who tries to control everything around her – decides to write her own obituary. A young journalist takes up the task of finding out the truth, and the result is a life-altering friendship.
