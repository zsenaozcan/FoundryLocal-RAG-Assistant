Title: Katt Williams: The Pimp Chronicles Pt. 1 (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Gary Binkow
Main Cast: Katt Williams, Anthony Anderson, Snoop Dogg, Lil' Jon
Keywords: comedian, stand-up comedy, live comedy, duringcreditsstinger

Overview: Comedian Katt Williams (aka Money Mike) showcases his laugh out loud comedic talents in his first ever HBO stand-up comedy DVD taped in front of a live audience. A native of Cincinnati, Ohio, Williams worked his way up the comedy club ladder before landing key television and film roles that displayed his flashy, sassy, streetwise style.
