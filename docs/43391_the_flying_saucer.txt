Title: The Flying Saucer (1950)
Genres: Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Mikel Conrad
Main Cast: Mikel Conrad, Pat Garrison, Hantz von Teuffen, Earle Lyon, Lester Sharpe
Keywords: spy, cold war, flying saucer, secret agent, alaska, independent film, espionage

Overview: The CIA sends playboy Mike Trent to Alaska with agent Vee Langley, posing as his "nurse," to investigate flying saucer sightings. At first, installed in a hunting lodge, the two play in the wilderness. But then they sight a saucer. Investigating, our heroes clash with an inept gang of Soviet spies, also after the saucer secret.
