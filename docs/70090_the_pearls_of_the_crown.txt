Title: The Pearls of the Crown (1937)
Genres: Comedy, History
Director: Sacha Guitry
Main Cast: Jean-Louis Barrault, Arletty, Marcel Dalio, Sacha Guitry, Renée Saint-Cyr
Keywords: mary queen of scots, episodic, anne boleyn

Overview: The story of the seven pearls of the English Crown, from Henry VIII to 1937; three of them missing.
