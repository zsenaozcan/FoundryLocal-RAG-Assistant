Title: Blue Is the Warmest Color (2013)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Abdellatif Kechiche
Main Cast: Adèle Exarchopoulos, Léa Seydoux, Salim Kechiouche, Aurélien Recoing, Catherine Salée
Keywords: france, homophobia, love, lesbian relationship, gay interest, coming of age, lesbian, teenage girl, sexual attraction, teenage love
