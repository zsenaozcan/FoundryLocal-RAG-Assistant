Title: Tuya's Marriage (2006)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Wang Quan'an
Main Cast: Nan Yu, Bater, Sen'ge, Zhaya
Keywords: suicide attempt, self sacrifice, shepherd, marriage proposal, sheep, snow storm, bridegroom, camel, petrol, paralysis

Overview: Set in Inner Mongolia, a physical setback causes a young woman to choose a suitor who can take care of her, as well as her disabled husband.
