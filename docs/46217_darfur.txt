Title: Darfur (2009)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller
Director: Uwe Boll
Main Cast: Edward Furlong, Billy Zane, Kristanna Loken, David O'Hara, Noah Danby

Overview: American journalists in Sudan are confronted with the dilemma of whether to return home to report on the atrocities they have seen, or to stay behind and help some of the victims they have encountered.
