Title: Billy Budd (1962)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Peter Ustinov
Main Cast: Terence Stamp, Robert Ryan, Peter Ustinov, Melvyn Douglas, Paul Rogers
Keywords: homicide, mutiny, war ship, court case

Overview: Billy is an innocent, naive seaman in the British Navy in 1797. When the ship's sadistic master-at-arms is murdered, Billy is accused and tried.
