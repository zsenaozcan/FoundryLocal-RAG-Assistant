Title: Alligator Eyes (1990)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: John Feldman
Main Cast: Annabelle Larsen, Roger Kabler, Mary McLain, Allen McCullough
Keywords: seduction, b movie, hitchhiker

Overview: Three young New York men decide to travel South for a few days away from the Big Apple. They pick up a beautiful, mysterious young sight less woman with a strange and unsettling past. All three are captivated and fascinated by her, but can she be trusted?
