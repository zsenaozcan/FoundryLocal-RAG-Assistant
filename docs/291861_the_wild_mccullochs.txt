Title: The Wild McCullochs (1975)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Max Baer, Jr.
Main Cast: Forrest Tucker, Julie Adams, Max Baer, Jr., Janice Heiden, Dennis Redfield
Keywords: overbearing father, father child relationship
