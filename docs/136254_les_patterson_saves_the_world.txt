Title: Les Patterson Saves the World (1987)
Genres: Comedy
Director: George T. Miller
Main Cast: Barry Humphries, Pamela Stephenson, Thaao Penghlis, Andrew Clarke, Joan Rivers
Keywords: middle east, ambassador, scientist, toilet seat

Overview: Fat middle aged 24/7 drunkard Les Patterson represents Australia at the UN where his fart literally incinerates an Arab ambassador. Patterson is reassigned to the Middle East so he can be tortured to death by the country he insulted. Patterson's arrival is the prefect distract of a coup and he is spared. At a bar Patterson meets a bio weapons scientist who's developed a horrific disease for the KGB whom plan to distribute it to the Pentagon via toilet seats. Patterson of course is far too drunk to understand anything happening and teams up with Dane Edna to save the world.
