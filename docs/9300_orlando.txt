Title: Orlando (1992)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Sally Potter
Main Cast: Tilda Swinton, Billy Zane, Lothaire Bluteau, John Wood, Charlotte Valandrey
Keywords: london england, queen, queen elizabeth i, androgyny, breaking the fourth wall, nobility, woman director, gender bender

Overview: In 1600, nobleman Orlando inherits his parents' house, thanks to Queen Elizabeth I, who commands the young man to never change. After a disastrous affair with Russian princess Sasha, Orlando looks for solace in the arts before being appointed ambassador to Constantinople in 1700, where war is raging. One morning, Orlando is shocked to wake up as a woman and returns home, struggling as a female to retain her property as the centuries roll by.
