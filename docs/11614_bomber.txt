Title: Bomber (1982)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama
Director: Michele Lupo
Main Cast: Bud Spencer, Jerry Calà, Stefano Mingardo, Kallie Knoetze, Bobby Rhodes
Keywords: transporter, boxer, training, fistfight, gangster

Overview: Bomber is an unemployed boat captain. One day he meets Jerry, a manager of boxers who are struck by the force of his fists. That is when they see the chance to win big money.
