Title: Totò, lascia o raddoppia? (1956)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Camillo Mastrocinque
Main Cast: Totò, Mike Bongiorno, Valeria Moriconi, Carlo Croccolo, Gabriele Tinti

Overview: The penniless Duke decides to participate in "Lascia o raddoppia", a TV quiz show, in order to win five million lira. Then two gangsters bet between themselves on his success and, alas, the Duke is kidnapped.
