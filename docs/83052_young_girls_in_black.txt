Title: Young Girls in Black (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jean-Paul Civeyrac
Main Cast: Elise Lhomeau, Léa Tissier, Elise Caron, Roger Jendly, Thierry Paret

Overview: Noémie and Priscilla, two teenage girls from working class backgrounds, cultivate the same violence, the same contempt of the world. They are a source of serious concern for family and friends, who sense them capable of going to extremes. Noémie has already tried to kill herself once when with the work of German Romanic writer Kleist ringing true to her, convinces equally unhappy best friend Priscilla to make a suicide pact. The two of them can see no reason to go on living but the practicalities and opportunity to go through with that plan is harder than they envisage.
