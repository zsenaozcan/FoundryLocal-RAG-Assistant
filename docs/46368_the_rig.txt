Title: The Rig (2010)
Genres: Action, Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Peter Atencio
Main Cast: William Forsythe, Stacey Hinnen, Serah D'Laine, Marcus T. Paulk, Carmen Perez
Keywords: sea, monster, oil rig 

Overview: In the midst of a tropical storm, the crew of an offshore oil rig must survive the rampage of a creature after invading its undersea habitat.
