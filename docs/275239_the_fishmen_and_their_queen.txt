Title: The Fishmen and Their Queen (1995)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror, Adventure
Director: Sergio Martino
Main Cast: Ramona Badescu, Giuliano Gensini, Donald Hodson, Antonella Troise

Overview: A "sequel" to Island of the Fishmen (L'isola degli uomini pesce) that opts for a kitchen sink approach blending Italian post-apocalyptic, fantasy and kids movie genres.
