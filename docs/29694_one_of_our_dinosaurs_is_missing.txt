Title: One of Our Dinosaurs Is Missing (1975)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Drama, Family, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Robert Stevenson
Main Cast: Derek Nimmo, Hugh Burden, Bernard Bresslaw, Helen Hayes, Joan Sims
Keywords: based on novel, nanny, museum

Overview: Escaping from China with a microfilm of the formula for the mysterious "Lotus X", Lord Southmere, a Queen's Messenger, is chased by a group of Chinese spies.
