Title: Aftermath (1994)
Genres: Horror, Crime
Director: Nacho Cerdà
Main Cast: Pep Tosar, Xevi Collellmir, Jordi Tarrida, Ángel Tarris
Keywords: morgue, necrophile

Overview: When the others leave for the night, the last mortician begins to fondle the corpses. He quickly moves to the corpse of a young woman who died in a car crash.
