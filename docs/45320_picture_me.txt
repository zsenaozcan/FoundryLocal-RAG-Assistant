Title: Picture Me (2010)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ole Schell
Main Cast: Sara Ziff, Caitriona Balfe, Nicole Miller, Karl Lagerfeld
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A documentary filmmaker follows a model for several years, chronicling her rise from a fresh face to one that adorns billboards and magazines around the world. Go behind the scenes and chronicle the glitzy world of high fashion modeling, from photo shoots with celebrated photographers to runway shows in New York, Milan, and Paris.
