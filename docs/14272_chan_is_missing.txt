Title: Chan Is Missing (1982)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Wayne Wang
Main Cast: Wood Moy, Marc Hayashi, Laureen Chew, Peter Wang, Presco Tabios
Keywords: chinatown, enigma

Overview: Two cabbies search San Francisco's Chinatown for a mysterious character who has disappeared with their $4000. Their quest leads them on a humorous, if mundane, journey which illuminates the many problems experienced by Chinese-Americans trying to assimilate into contemporary American society.
