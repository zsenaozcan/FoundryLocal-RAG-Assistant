Title: The Diaries of Vaslav Nijinsky (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: Paul Cox
Main Cast: Derek Jacobi, Delia Silvan, Chris Haywood, Hans Sonneveld
Keywords: biography

Overview: Dramatization of Russian ballet star Vaclav Nijinsky's diaries which detail his madness as well as his homosexual relationship with Ballet Russe impresario Sergei Diaghilev and his marriage to his Hungarian wife.
