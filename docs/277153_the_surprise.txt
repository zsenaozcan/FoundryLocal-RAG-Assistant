Title: The Surprise (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Mike van Diem
Main Cast: Georgina Verbaan, Jeroen van Koningsbrugge, Jan Decleir, Henry Goodman, Pierre Bokma

Overview: An eccentric multimillionaire signs an agreement to have his life ended. While selecting his coffin he meets a young woman who has signed up for the same arrangement. Trouble ensues when the couple falls in love and wishes to get out of the contract.
