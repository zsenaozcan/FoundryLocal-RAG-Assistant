Title: Millionaires Express (1986)
Genres: Comedy, Action, Foreign
Director: Sammo Hung
Main Cast: Sammo Hung, Yuen Biao, Kenny Bee, Olivia Cheng Man-Nga, Hsiao Ho
Keywords: train, bank robbery, unlikely hero

Overview: What happens when a glamorous express, with high government officials, wealthy merchants, concubines and a gang of brigands on board, speeds towards the small town of Hanshui, where escaping bank robbers, corrupt officials, and gamblers await? Well, let's just say the Titanic had a smoother maiden voyage.
