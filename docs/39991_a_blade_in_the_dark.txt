Title: A Blade in the Dark (1983)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Lamberto Bava
Main Cast: Andrea Occhipinti, Anny Papa, Fabiola Toledo, Michele Soavi, Valeria Cavalli
Keywords: secret, stalker, murder, killer, tuscany

Overview: A killer stalks a composer staying at a posh Tuscany villa while writing the score to a horror film which has an incriminating clue to the killer's identity.
