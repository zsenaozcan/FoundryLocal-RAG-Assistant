Title: Armored Car Robbery (1950)
Genres: Crime
Director: Richard Fleischer
Main Cast: Charles McGraw, Adele Jergens, William Talman, Douglas Fowley, Steve Brodie
Keywords: armored car, film noir

Overview: The film tells the story of a well-planned robbery of an armored car when it stops at a sports stadium. Yet, the heist goes awry, and a tough Los Angeles cop named Cordell (Charles McGraw) is in hot pursuit.
