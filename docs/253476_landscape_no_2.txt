Title: Landscape No. 2 (2009)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Vinko Möderndorfer
Main Cast: Marko Mandić, Janez Hočevar, Slobodan Ćustić, Barbara Cerar, Maja Martina Merljak

Overview: The burglars Sergej and Polde steal a valuable painting, Landscape No.2. By coincidence Sergej also takes a mysterious document dating back to the end of the World War II. Instructor is ordered to recover the stolen painting and the document, which triggers a diabolic mechanism of the past.
