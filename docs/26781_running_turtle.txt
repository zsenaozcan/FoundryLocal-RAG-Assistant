Title: Running Turtle (2009)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Jung Kyung-ho, Seo Dong-Soo, Kim Yoon-seok

Overview: Phil-sung is a lazy countryside detective. He is suspended from office due to his mistakes during examination process. Ki-tae is a legendary prison breaker and continues his merciless killing rampage. Phil-sung accidentally runs into Ki-tae but he is much too slow to catch the killer. He’s fired due to this error and now he makes it his personal mission to bring in Ki-tae at all costs.
