Title: Grace of My Heart (1996)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Music
Director: Allison Anders
Main Cast: Illeana Douglas, John Turturro, Eric Stoltz, Matt Dillon, Bridget Fonda
Keywords: singer, songwriter, woman director

Overview: An aspiring singer, Denise Waverly/Edna Buxton, sacrifices her own singing career to write hit songs that launch the careers of other singers. The film follows her life from her first break, through the pain of rejection from the recording industry and a bad marriage, to her final triumph in realizing her dream to record her own hit album.
