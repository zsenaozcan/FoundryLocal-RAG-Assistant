Title: Elephants Dream (2006)
Genres: Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Bassam Kurdali
Main Cast: Tygo Gernandt, Cas Jansen
Keywords: dream, future

Overview: Emo and Proog are on a journey in the folds of a giant Machine, exploring the twisted and dark complex of wires, gears and cogs. Until one moment a conflict arises that throws out all their assumptions. This is the world's first open source movie, created using the FOSS program Blender.
