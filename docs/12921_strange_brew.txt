Title: Strange Brew (1983)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Rick Moranis
Main Cast: Dave Thomas, Rick Moranis, Max von Sydow, Paul Dooley, Lynne Griffin
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Something is rotten at the Elsinore Brewery. Bob and Doug Mackenzie (as seen on SCTV) help the orphan Pam regain the brewery founded by her recently-deceased father. But to do so, they must confront the suspicious Brewmeister Smith and two teams of vicious hockey players.
