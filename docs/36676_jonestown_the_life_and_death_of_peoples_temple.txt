Title: Jonestown: The Life and Death of Peoples Temple (2006)
Genres: Documentary, History
Director: Stanley Nelson
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: jim jones, mass suicide

Overview: Featuring never-before-seen footage, this documentary delivers a startling new look at the Peoples Temple, headed by preacher Jim Jones who, in 1978, led more than 900 members to Guyana, where he orchestrated a mass suicide via tainted punch.
