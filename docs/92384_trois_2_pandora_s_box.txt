Title: Trois 2: Pandora's Box (2004)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Romance, Foreign
Director: Rob Hardy
Main Cast: Michael Jai White, Monica Calhoun, Kristoff St. John, Josephine Lawrence, Tyson Beckford

Overview: Stuck in a dull marriage, successful shrink Mia DuBois follows a patient's advice and exposes her wild side at a private underground club, where she meets a handsome stranger and begins an affair that may cost her more than just her husband.
