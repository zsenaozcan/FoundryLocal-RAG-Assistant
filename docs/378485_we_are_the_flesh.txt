Title: We Are the Flesh (2016)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Fantasy
Director: Emiliano Rocha Minter
Main Cast: Noe Hernandez, María Cid, Diego Gamaliel, María Evoli, Gabino Rodríguez
Keywords: post-apocalyptic

Overview: After wandering a ruined city for years in search of food and shelter, two siblings find their way into one of the last remaining buildings. Inside, they find a man who will make them a dangerous offer to survive the outside world.
