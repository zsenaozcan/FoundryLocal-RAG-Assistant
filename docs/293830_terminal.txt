Title: Terminal (1996)
Genres: Horror, Drama
Director: Larry Elikann
Main Cast: Doug Savant, Nia Peeples, Michael Ironside, Roy Thinnes, Jenny O'Hara
Keywords: based on novel, hospital

Overview: A hospital in need of funds announces a cure for a form of brain cancer, having saved several wealthy men's lives. When a young girl with the cancer dies, one doctor notices that she and all the cured men had been treated with exactly the same medicine. Investigating further, she uncovers a plot by the administration to locate wealthy men already suffering from a serious condition, and give them the cancer, which can then be treated successfully.
