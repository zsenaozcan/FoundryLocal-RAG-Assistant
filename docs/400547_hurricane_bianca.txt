Title: Hurricane Bianca (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Matt Kugelman
Main Cast: Roy Haylock, RuPaul, Rachel Dratch, Willam Belli, Alan Cumming
Keywords: gay, drag queen, homophobia, teacher, lgbt, discrimination

Overview: Hurricane Bianca is a revenge comedy about a New York teacher who moves to a small town in Texas, gets fired for being gay, and returns disguised in drag to get revenge on the people who were nasty to him.
