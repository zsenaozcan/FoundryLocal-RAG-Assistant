Title: I Am a Sex Addict (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Caveh Zahedi
Main Cast: Caveh Zahedi, Rebecca Lord, Emily Morse, Amanda Henderson, Valentine Fillol-Cordier
Keywords: sex, eroticism, sex addiction, marriage, prostitution, illegal prostitution

Overview: Just moments before his third wedding, Zahedi relates with utter sincerity and astonishing candor his obsession with prostitutes. He retraces his romantic and sexual history, including his ideological commitment to open relationships, that led to two disastrous marriages and several very pissed off ex-girlfriends. I Am a Sex Addict is Zahedi's unique brand of comedy at its confessional best.
