Title: Hurlyburly (1998)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Anthony Drazan
Main Cast: Sean Penn, Kevin Spacey, Robin Wright, Chazz Palminteri, Garry Shandling
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Based on a play by David Rabe, Hurlyburly is about the intersecting lives of several Hollywood players and wannabes, whose dysfunctional personal lives are more interesting than anything they're peddling to the studios.
