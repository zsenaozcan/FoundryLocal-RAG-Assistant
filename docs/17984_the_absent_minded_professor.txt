Title: The Absent-Minded Professor (1961)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Science Fiction
Director: Robert Stevenson
Main Cast: Fred MacMurray, Nancy Olson, Keenan Wynn, Tommy Kirk, Leon Ames

Overview: Hailed as one of Walt Disney's most hilarious comedies, The Absent-Minded Professor contains two essential elements for laughter - Fred MacMurray and Flubber, his gravity-defying formula for flying rubber!
