Title: Gloria (1999)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: Sidney Lumet
Main Cast: Sharon Stone, Jeremy Northam, Cathy Moriarty, Jean-Luke Figueroa, Mike Starr
Keywords: on the run, streetwise, cigarette smoke, moll

Overview: Sharon Stone plays a street-wise, middle-aged moll standing up against the mobs, all of which is complicated by a 6 year old urchin with a will of his own who she reluctantly takes under her wing after his family has been gunned down.
