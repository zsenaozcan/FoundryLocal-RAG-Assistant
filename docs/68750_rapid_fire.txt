Title: Rapid Fire (2006)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Kari Skogland
Main Cast: David Cubitt, Kristin Lehman, Jason Gedrick, Brendan Fletcher, Jessica Steen
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A desperate band of Militia men attempts a daylight bank robbery in the sleepy semi-rural City of Norco, California, and leads the local Police on the longest, most violent running gun battle in Law Enforcement history. Based on the true events.
