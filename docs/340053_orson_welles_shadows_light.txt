Title: Orson Welles: Shadows & Light (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Elisabeth Kapnist
Main Cast: Henry Jaglom, Joseph McBride, David Thomson
Keywords: biography

Overview: Giant of cinema, the embodiment of creation, Orson Welles is the man who reinvents the film language at 24-years old. Who is hidding behind this impressive figure? This movie is a journey towards the man behind the legend. It drags us into the labyrinth with multiple mirrors that Welles erases and recreates at the mercy of his imagination.
