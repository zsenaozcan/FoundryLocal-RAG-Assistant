Title: The Last of Sheila (1973)
Genres: Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Herbert Ross
Main Cast: Richard Benjamin, Dyan Cannon, James Coburn, Joan Hackett, James Mason
Keywords: yacht, scavenger hunt, whodunit, ensemble cast, murder mystery, weekend getaway

Overview: A year after Sheila is killed in a hit-and-run, her multimillionaire husband invites a group of friends to spend a week on his yacht playing a scavenger hunt-style mystery game. The game turns out to be all too real and all too deadly.
