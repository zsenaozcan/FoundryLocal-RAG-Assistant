Title: Here and Elsewhere (1976)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jean-Luc Godard
Main Cast: Jean-Luc Godard, Anne-Marie Miéville
Keywords: revolution, plo, marxism, multiculturalism, war, domestic life, film grammar, woman director

Overview: Godard, Miéville and Gorin (aka the "Dziga Vertov Group") examine the parallel lives of two families - one French, one Palestinian - using an exploratory combination of film and video.
