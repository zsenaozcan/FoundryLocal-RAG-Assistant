Title: Summer Interlude (1951)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Ingmar Bergman
Main Cast: Maj-Britt Nilsson, Birger Malmsten, Alf Kjellin, Annalisa Ericson, Georg Funkquist

Overview: Ballet dancer Marie reminisce of a summer 13 years ago when she was a teenager and met Henrik on vacation in the archipelago.
