Title: The In-Laws (1979)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Arthur Hiller
Main Cast: Peter Falk, Alan Arkin, Richard Libertini, Nancy Dussault, Penny Peyser
Keywords: cia, wedding, in-laws, armored car robbery, fictional country

Overview: In preparation for his daughter's wedding, dentist Sheldon Kornpett meets Vince Ricardo, the groom's father. Vince, a manic fellow who claims to be a government agent, then proceeds to drag Sheldon into a series of chases and misadventures from New York to Central America.
