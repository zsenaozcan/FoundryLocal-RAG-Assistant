Title: Gas Station Queen (1962)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Aleksei Mishurin
Main Cast: Nadezhda Rumyantseva, Yuri Belov, Aleksey Kozhevnikov, Andrei Sova, Nonna Koperzhinskaya

Overview: Lyudmila doesn't have any luck in finding the perfect job. She tried to be an air hostess, newscaster, model, ice skater, but fate had something else in store for her - a petrol station, where her incredible positivity and fiery personality charm everyone around her.
