Title: Ride Him, Cowboy (1932)
Genres: Action, Western, Romance
Director: Fred Allen
Main Cast: John Wayne, Ruth Hall, Henry B. Walthall, Otis Harlan, Harry Gribbon
Keywords: rancher

Overview: John Drury saves Duke, a wild horse accused of murder, and trains him. When he discovers that the real murderer, a bad guy known as The Hawk, is the town's leading citizen, Drury arrested on a fraudulent charge.
