Title: Dark Journey (1937)
Genres: Romance, Adventure, Thriller
Director: Victor Saville
Main Cast: Vivien Leigh, Conrad Veidt, Joan Gardner, Anthony Bushell, Ursula Jeans
Keywords: spy, world war i, baron, espionage, female spy

Overview: Madeline Goddard (Leigh), is a British double agent who meets and falls in love with a German spy Baron Karl Von Marwitz (Veidt) during World War I. This tale of espionage blends high adventure and romance making perfect order from wartime chaos and growing in faith from despair.
