Title: Stanley & Iris (1990)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Martin Ritt
Main Cast: Jane Fonda, Robert De Niro, Swoosie Kurtz, Martha Plimpton, Jamey Sheridan
Keywords: cook, education, widow, bakery, love, tutoring

Overview: An illiterate cook at a company cafeteria tries for the attention of a newly widowed woman. As they get to know one another, she discovers his inability to read. When he is fired, she takes on trying to teach him to read in her kitchen each night.
