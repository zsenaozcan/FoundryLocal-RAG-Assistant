Title: Dark Space (2013)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Emmett Callinan
Main Cast: Alana Dietze, Steve West, Avital Ash, Joseph Darden, Keith Reay
Keywords: sci-fi horror

Overview: College friends Devin, Jack, Shelly, Kristy, Shaun, and Flower rent a spacecraft to autopilot them to Centauri Five for a holiday break. Impatient over the rental agency restricting the ship’s speed to 10x, Devin and the others convince Shaun to remove the craft’s constrictor device. Doing so damages the computer’s automated systems, as well as the ability to send a distress beacon, causing the six friends to crash land on an uncharted alien planet.
