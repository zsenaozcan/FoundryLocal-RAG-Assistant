Title: Taste of Fear (1961)
Genres: Horror, Mystery
Director: Seth Holt
Main Cast: Susan Strasberg, Ronald Lewis, Ann Todd, Christopher Lee, John Serret
Keywords: wheelchair, father, suspense, dead body, psychological thriller, hammer horror

Overview: A wheelchair-bound young girl returns to her father's estate after ten years, and although she's told he's away, she keeps seeing his dead body on the estate.
