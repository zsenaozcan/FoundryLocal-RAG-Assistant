Title: The Discoverers (2014)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Justin Schwarz
Main Cast: Griffin Dunne, Madeleine Martin, Cara Buono, Devon Graye, Dreama Walker

Overview: Washed-up history professor Lewis Birch takes his begrudging teenagers Zoe and Jack on a road trip to a conference in hopes of jumpstarting his career and reconnecting with his kids. But, when Lewis’s estranged father Stanley goes missing on a Lewis and Clark historical reenactment trek, Lewis is forced to make a family detour. The Birch family find themselves on a journey of discovery and connection as they make their own passage west.
