Title: The Thing That Couldn't Die (1958)
Genres: Horror
Director: Will Cowan
Main Cast: William Reynolds, Andra Martin, Jeffrey Stone, Robin Hughes, Carolyn Kearney
Keywords: ranch, grave, psychic, dowsing rod

Overview: A psychically gifted young woman discovers a centuries-old crate buried on her aunt's ranch. Opening it, her family discovers the living head of Gideon Drew, a 16th century devil worshiper who was beheaded by Sir Francis Drake.
