Title: Tartuffe (1925)
Genres: Drama
Director: F.W. Murnau
Main Cast: Emil Jannings, Hermann Picha, Rosa Valetti, André Mattoni, Werner Krauss
Keywords: fraud, french

Overview: Young man shows his millionaire grandfather a film based on Molière's Tartuffe, in order to expose the old man's hypocritical governess who covets his own inheritance.
