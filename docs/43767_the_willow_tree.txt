Title: The Willow Tree (2005)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Majid Majidi
Main Cast: Parviz Parastui, Roya Taymourian, Afarin Obeisi, Mohammad Amir Naji, Melika Eslafi
Keywords: hospital, blindness, religious fundamentalism, iranian

Overview: Youssef, a blind university professor, is suddenly diagnosed with a fatal disease and must undergo treatment in France. Back home, will he find the life he had before?
