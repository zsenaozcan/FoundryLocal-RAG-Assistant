Title: 300 Miles to Heaven (1989)
Genres: Drama
Director: Maciej Dejczer
Main Cast: Wojciech Klata, Rafał Zimowski, Jadwiga Jankowska-Cieślak, Andrzej Mellin, Kama Kowalewska
Keywords: denmark, social worker, truck, based on true story, number in title, children, poland, communism

Overview: Film based on a true story dating back to 1985 when two Polish boys, a teenager and his little brother, escaped from communist Poland. Hiding beneath a lorry they reached Scandinavia - against all odds.
