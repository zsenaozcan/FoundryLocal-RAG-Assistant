Title: Dombivali Fast (2006)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Nishikant Kamat
Main Cast: Sandeep Kulkarni, Shilpa Tulaskar, Sandesh Jadhav

Overview: It is the story of a middle class bank employee, Madhav Apte, an ordinary, law-abiding and honest citizen who faces constant frustration with the injustice and corruption that pervades in all walks of his life. The film portrays Apte's mental breakdown as he reaches his breaking point, and his rampage as a vigilante across Mumbai to set things right.
