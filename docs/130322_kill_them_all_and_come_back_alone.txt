Title: Kill Them All and Come Back Alone (1968)
Genres: Western
Director: Enzo G. Castellari
Main Cast: Chuck Connors, Frank Wolff, Franco Citti, Leo Anchóriz, Giovanni Cianfriglia
Keywords: spaghetti western

Overview: During the American Civil War, a Confederate prisoner, Clyde McKay, attempts to steal a box of gold from a Union prison camp. He is aided by a group of prisoners and a prison guard but he is double-crossed along the way.
