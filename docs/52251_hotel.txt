Title: Hotel (2004)
Genres: Horror, Drama, Thriller
Director: Jessica Hausner
Main Cast: Franziska Weisz, Birgit Minichmayr, Marlene Streeruwitz, Rosa Waissnix, Christopher Schärf
Keywords: woman director

Overview: When Irene takes a position at a hotel deep in the woods of the Austrian Alps, she soon discovers the girl she replaced vanished without a trace. Is there a murderer on site, or are there even darker forces at work?
