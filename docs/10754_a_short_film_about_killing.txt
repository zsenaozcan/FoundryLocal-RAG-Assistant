Title: A Short Film About Killing (1989)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Krzysztof Kieślowski
Main Cast: Mirosław Baka, Jerzy Zass, Aleksander Bednarz, Jan Tesarz, Krzysztof Globisz
Keywords: prison, taxi driver, court, jurist, murder, lawyer, warsaw

Overview: Jacek climbs into the taxi driven by Waldemar, tells him to drive to a remote location, then brutally strangles him, seemingly without motive.
