Title: Inside Moves (1980)
Genres: Drama
Director: Richard Donner
Main Cast: John Savage, David Morse, Diana Scarwid, Amy Wright, Tony Burton
Keywords: san francisco, based on novel, waitress, bar, suicide attempt, wheelchair, bartender, basketball, independent film, hospital

Overview: After a failed suicide attempt leaves him partially crippled, Rory begins spending a lot of time at a neighborhood bar full of interesting misfits. When Jerry the bartender suddenly finds himself playing basketball for the Golden State Warriors, Rory and the rest of the bar regulars hope his success will provide a lift to their sagging spirits. Will Jerry forget his friends? What about his junkie hooker girlfriend and her pimp?
