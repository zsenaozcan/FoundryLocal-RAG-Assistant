Title: Charlie Chan's Secret (1936)
Genres: Crime, Mystery, Drama, Thriller
Director: Gordon Wiles
Main Cast: Warner Oland, Henrietta Crosman, Arthur Edmund Carewe, Astrid Allwyn, Rosina Lawrence
Keywords: charlie chan

Overview: Allen Colby, heir to a huge fortune, is presumed drowned after an ocean liner sinks off the coast of Honolulu. Mysteriously, Colby reappears at his mansion only to be murdered soon after. When his body is discovered during a seance, everyone in attendance becomes a suspect, and it's up to Chan to find the murderer before he or she strikes again.
