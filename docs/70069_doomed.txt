Title: Doomed (2007)
Genres: Horror
Director: Michael Su
Main Cast: Drew Russell, Kara Schaaf, Edwin Villa, Aaron Gaffey, Mary Christina Brown
Keywords: zombie

Overview: In the future, death row criminals are given a chance to gain a pardon, on a "survivor" style reality show, only to find the island is full of zombie ex-contestants. (It's as bad as it sounds)
