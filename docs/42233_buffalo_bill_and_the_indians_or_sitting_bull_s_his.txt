Title: Buffalo Bill and the Indians, or Sitting Bull's History Lesson (1976)
Genres: Western, Comedy
Director: Robert Altman
Main Cast: Paul Newman, Joel Grey, Harvey Keitel, Kevin McCarthy, Allan F. Nicholls
Keywords: wild west, wild west show, buffalo bill

Overview: Buffalo Bill plans to put on his own Wild West sideshow, and Chief Sitting Bull has agreed to appear in it. However, Sitting Bull has his own hidden agenda, involving the President and General Custer.
