Title: Marie Antoinette (2006)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Sofia Coppola
Main Cast: Kirsten Dunst, Jason Schwartzman, Judy Davis, Rip Torn, Rose Byrne
Keywords: shoe, voice over, rite, theatre audience, bride and groom, death of king, applause, woman director

Overview: The retelling of France's iconic but ill-fated queen, Marie Antoinette. From her betrothal and marriage to Louis XVI at 15 to her reign as queen at 19 and ultimately the fall of Versailles.
