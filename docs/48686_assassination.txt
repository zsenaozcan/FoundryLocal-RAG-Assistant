Title: Assassination (1987)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller
Director: Peter R. Hunt
Main Cast: Charles Bronson, Jill Ireland, Stephen Elliott, Jan Gan Boyd, James Staley

Overview: Jay Killon is the bodyguard of the recently elected US president, but he is assigned to the first lady (Lara Royce). Lara hates Killon so she does all she can to escape. The story complicates when someone tries to kill Lara.
