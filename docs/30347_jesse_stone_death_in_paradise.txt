Title: Jesse Stone: Death in Paradise (2006)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Robert Harmon
Main Cast: Tom Selleck, Viola Davis, Kohl Sudduth, Orla Brady, Gary Basaraba
Keywords: cop, new england, jesse stone

Overview: Small-time police chief and struggling alcoholic Jesse Stone looks into the murder of a teen-age girl whose body is found floating in a local lake. The case brings the former LAPD cop up against the Boston mob and into the affluent world of a bestselling writer who exploits troubled teens.
