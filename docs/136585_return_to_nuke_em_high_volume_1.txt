Title: Return to Nuke 'Em High Volume 1 (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Lloyd Kaufman
Main Cast: Asta Parades, Catherine Corcoran, Zac Amico, Vitto Triggo, Babette Bombshell
Keywords: satire, sequel, gore, campy, shock humor, gore movie

Overview: Return to the Class of Nuke 'Em High follows a young couple that are up against the school glee club. Unfortunately, the glee club has mutated into a gang called The Cretins. When the other students begin to undergo mutations, our couple must solve the mystery and save Tromaville High School
