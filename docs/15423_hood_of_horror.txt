Title: Hood of Horror (2006)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror
Director: Stacy Title
Main Cast: Snoop Dogg, Ernie Hudson, Danny Trejo, Anson Mount, Daniella Alonso
Keywords: graffiti, hell, anthology, shot in the crotch, woman director

Overview: A hip hop horror anthology of three tales of terror told by the Hound of Hell (Snoop Dogg) that revolve around the residents of an inner-city neighborhood whose actions determine where they will go in the afterlife.
