Title: Wodehouse In Exile (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Tim Fywell
Main Cast: Tim Pigott-Smith, Zoë Wanamaker, Paul Ritter, Flora Montgomery, Paul Mallon

Overview: An all-star cast heads up this intimate film about how author, P.G.Wodehouse, came to face a charge of treason during the Second World War and how this quintessential Englishman, creator of Jeeves and Wooster, became an exile from his own country and never set foot on English soil again.
