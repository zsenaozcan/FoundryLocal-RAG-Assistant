Title: La Terra Trema (1948)
Genres: Drama
Director: Luchino Visconti
Main Cast: Antonio Arcidiacono, Giuseppe Arcidiacono, Venera Bonaccorso, Nicola Castorino, Rosa Catalano
Keywords: sicily, fish, boat, fisherman

Overview: In rural Sicily, the fishermen live at the mercy of the greedy wholesalers. One family risks everything to buy their own boat and operate independently.
