Title: The Body (2003)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Tomasz Konecki
Main Cast: Rafal Królikowski, Emilia Krakowska, Piotr Adamczyk
Keywords: siamese twins, mafia, dead body, coincidence, poland

Overview: One day a commuter, who happens to be a burglar, finds a dead body on a train. As he was just returning from a burglary and not wanting to draw attention to himself, he decides to get rid of the corpse himself. Little does he know that the body is about to embark on one hell of a journey...
