Title: Kuka (2007)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Yaroslav Chevazhevskiy
Main Cast: Anastasiya Dobrynina, Dina Korzun, Dmitriy Dyuzhev, Aleksandr Polovtsev, Pavel Derevyanko

Overview: A woman and a young girl in different cities are struggling to find their way in this world. Will their paths intersect and will that change their destinies?
