Title: The Kill Hole (2012)
Genres: Action
Director: Mischa Webley
Main Cast: Chadwick Boseman, Tory Kittles, Billy Zane, Peter Greene, Brian Siefkes

Overview: The shadow of war follows a troubled Iraq War vet as he is forced to pursue one of his own into the Pacific Northwest wilderness to finally confront a war crime that has haunted them both.
