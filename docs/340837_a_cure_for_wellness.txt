Title: A Cure for Wellness (2017)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Gore Verbinski
Main Cast: Dane DeHaan, Jason Isaacs, Mia Goth, Adrian Schiller, Celia Imrie
Keywords: switzerland, therapist, health, darkness, wellness, employee, patient, violence, drug, remote
