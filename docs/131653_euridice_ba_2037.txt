Title: Euridice BA 2037 (1975)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nikos Nikolaidis
Main Cast: Vera Tschechowa, John Moore, Niki Triantafillidi, Manolis Logiadis

Overview: Euridice in Hades, code number ΒΑ 2037, is waiting for Orpheus to free her and lead her to the Upper World. She’s waiting for orders that will allow her to move out of her prison/home. Her destination is not mentioned. A voice from a computer orders her to move out.
