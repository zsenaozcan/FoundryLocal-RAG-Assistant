Title: Panic (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Henry Bromell
Main Cast: William H. Macy, John Ritter, Neve Campbell, Donald Sutherland, Tracey Ullman
Keywords: dark comedy, independent film, criminal

Overview: Alex is going through a midlife crisis and it has become a very difficult time for him. His marriage is struggling, he's worried about his son, and his job of killing people for his family has become the most stressful part of his life. He seeks the help of a therapist and meets a woman in the waiting room that he connects with.
