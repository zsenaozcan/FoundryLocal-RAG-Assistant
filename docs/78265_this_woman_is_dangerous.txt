Title: This Woman Is Dangerous (1952)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Felix Feist
Main Cast: Joan Crawford, Dennis Morgan, David Brian, Richard Webb, Mari Aldon
Keywords: gangster, criminal mastermind

Overview: Elizabeth is losing her sight, so while her lover go into hiding, she checks in to the hospital for extensive surgery to recover her eyesight. There she is treated by a handsome young doctor, Ben Hellack (Dennis Morgan). As expected not only the doctor successfully open her eyes, he also opened her heart for him.
