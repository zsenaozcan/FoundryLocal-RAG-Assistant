Title: An American Ghost Story (2012)
Genres: Horror
Director: Derek Cole
Main Cast: Stephen Twardokus, Liesel Kopp, Jon Gale, Wendy Haines
Keywords: haunted house, independent film, ghost

Overview: When Paul, an unemployed writer, decides to rent and live in a house that's rumored to be haunted, he puts his life and his relationships in grave danger as he obsessively attempts to get the story that will finally make his career.
