Title: The Present (2014)
Genres: Animation, Comedy
Director: Jacob Frey
Main Cast: Samantha Brown, Quinn Nealy
Keywords: based on comic, gift, computer games, mother son relationship, short

Overview: The short tells the story of a boy who rather spends his time indoors playing videogames instead of discovering whats waiting in front of the door. One day his Mum decides to get a little surprise for her son, which makes it hard for him to concentrate on his video game.
