Title: Every Stewardess Goes to Heaven (2002)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Daniel Burman
Main Cast: Alfredo Casero, Ingrid Rubio, Emilio Disi, Valentina Bassi, Verónica Llinás

Overview: Teresa is a stewardess who lives a perfect life in the midst of his impeccable suits world clean and prepared foods. However the world scares land: men, motherhood, love ... Meanwhile, Julian, a young doctor, must travel to Ushuaia to throw the ashes of his dead wife in the place where they fell in love.
