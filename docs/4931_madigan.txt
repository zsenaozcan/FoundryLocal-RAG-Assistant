Title: Madigan (1968)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Don Siegel
Main Cast: Richard Widmark, Inger Stevens, Henry Fonda, Harry Guardino, Susan Clark
Keywords: marriage, neglect of one's spouse

Overview: Policemen Bonaro and Madigan lose their guns to fugitive Barney Benesch. As compensation, the two NYC detectives are given a weekend to bring Benesch to justice. While Bonaro and Madigan follow up on various leads, Police Commissioner Russell goes about his duties, including attending functions, meeting with aggrieved relatives, and counseling the spouses of fallen officers.
