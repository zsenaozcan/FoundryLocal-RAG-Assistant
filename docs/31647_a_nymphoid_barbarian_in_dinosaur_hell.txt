Title: A Nymphoid Barbarian in Dinosaur Hell (1990)
Genres: Fantasy, Science Fiction, Horror
Director: Brett Piper
Main Cast: Linda Corwin, Paul Guzzi, Alex Pirnie, Mark Deshaies, Al Hodder

Overview: In a post-Armageddon world, a young woman finds herself in a fight for survival against mutant cavemen, dinosaurs and other prehistoric animals.
