Title: Charlie Chan in Honolulu (1938)
Genres: Mystery, Crime, Drama, Comedy, Thriller
Director: H. Bruce Humberstone
Main Cast: Sidney Toler, Phyllis Brooks, Victor Sen Yung, Eddie Collins, John 'Dusty' King
Keywords: charlie chan

Overview: While Charlie is distracted with the birth of his first grandchild, son Jimmy impersonates his father in order to investigate a murder aboard a freighter in the harbor.
