Title: Dead Rising: Watchtower (2015)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Zach Lipovsky
Main Cast: Jesse Metcalfe, Meghan Ory, Keegan Connor Tracy, Virginia Madsen, Dennis Haysbert
Keywords: chain saw, infection, quarantine, zombie, based on video game, u.s. military, plague

Overview: Based on the game, Dead Rising takes place during a large-scale zombie outbreak. When a mandatory government vaccine fails to stop the infection from spreading, the four leads must evade infection while also pursuing the root of the epidemic, with all signs pointing to a government conspiracy.
