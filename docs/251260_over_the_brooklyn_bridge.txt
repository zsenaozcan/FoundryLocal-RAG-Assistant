Title: Over the Brooklyn Bridge (1984)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Menahem Golan
Main Cast: Elliott Gould, Margaux Hemingway, Sid Caesar, Carol Kane, Burt Young
Keywords: jewish, deli

Overview: A put-upon Jewish deli owner in Brooklyn dreams of getting out from underneath the thumb of his domineering father and his haughty fashion-model girlfriend by buying his own restaurant in midtown Manhattan.
