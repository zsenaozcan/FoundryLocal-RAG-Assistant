Title: Behind the Screen (1916)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Charlie Chaplin
Main Cast: Eric Campbell, Charlie Chaplin, Edna Purviance, Albert Austin, Lloyd Bacon
Keywords: dynamite, strike, costume, boss, love, falling in love, pie, black eye, burp, handkerchief

Overview: Charlie is an overworked labourer at a film studio who helps a young woman find work even while his coworkers strike against his tyrannical boss.
