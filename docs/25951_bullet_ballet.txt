Title: Bullet Ballet (1998)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Thriller
Director: Shinya Tsukamoto
Main Cast: Shinya Tsukamoto, Kirina Mano, Tatsuya Nakamura, Takahiro Murase, Kyōka Suzuki
Keywords: gun, punk, revenge, surrealism

Overview: After his girlfriend commits suicide, a man (Shinya Tsukamoto) becomes embroiled in gang warfare attempting to obtain a gun in hopes to kill himself.
