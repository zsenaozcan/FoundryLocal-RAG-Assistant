Genres: Drama
Director: D.W. Griffith
Main Cast: Lillian Gish, Mae Marsh, Robert Harron, F.A. Turner, Sam De Grasse
Keywords: usa, naivety, intolerance, mill, marriage, wedding, massacre, protestant, silent film, multiple storylines
