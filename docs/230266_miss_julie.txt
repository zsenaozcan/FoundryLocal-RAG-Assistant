Title: Miss Julie (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Liv Ullmann
Main Cast: Jessica Chastain, Colin Farrell, Samantha Morton, Nora McMenamy
Keywords: love triangle, love, irish, valet, woman director, 19th century
