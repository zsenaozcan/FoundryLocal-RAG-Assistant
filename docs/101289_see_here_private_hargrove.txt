Title: See Here, Private Hargrove (1944)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, War
Director: Wesley Ruggles
Main Cast: Robert Walker, Donna Reed, Keenan Wynn, Ray Collins, Bob Crosby

Overview: Journalist Marion Hargrove enters the Army intending to supplement his income by writing about his training experiences. He muddles through basic training at Fort Bragg with the self-serving help of a couple of buddies intent on cutting themselves in on that extra income.
