Title: Le révélateur (1968)
Genres: Drama
Director: Philippe Garrel
Main Cast: Laurent Terzieff, Bernadette Lafont, Stanislas Robiolles
Keywords: experimental film

Overview: A 4-year-old child is the element from and around which the action develops, and brings sentiments and emotions to light. (The French word "révélateur"/developer describes the product to develop or "reveal" film negatives.)
