Title: Lips of Blood (1975)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Romance, Foreign
Director: Jean Rollin
Main Cast: Jean-Loup Philippe, Annie Belle, Natalie Perrey, Catherine Castel, Marie-Pierre Castel
Keywords: vampire, female vampire

Overview: Frederick sees a photograph of a ruined seaside castle, which triggers a strange childhood memory. He then goes on a strange quest, aided by four female vampires, to find the castle and the beautiful woman who lives there.
