Title: Orgasm Inc. (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Elizabeth Canner
Main Cast: Andrilla Chakrabarti, Nan Cochran, John Griesemer, Kim Airs, Susan Bennett
Keywords: pharmaceutical industry, viagra, woman director

Overview: Extraordinary behind-the-scenes access reveals a drug company's fevered race to develop the first FDA-approved Viagra for women - and offers a humorous but sobering look inside the cash-fueled pharmaceutical industry.
