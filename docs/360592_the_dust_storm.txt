Title: The Dust Storm (2016)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Music
Director: Ryan Lacen
Main Cast: Colin O'Donoghue, Kristen Gutoskie, Chris Carmack, Jim O'Heir, J.T. Hodges

Overview: On a business trip to Nashville, Brennan finds himself reunited with the "Girl" who had previously broken his heart. Now, for one weekend in the city's music scene, a past and present romance will be reborn and challenged.
