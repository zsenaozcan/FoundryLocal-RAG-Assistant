Title: Super Stooges vs the Wonder Women (1974)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Science Fiction, Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Alfonso Brescia
Main Cast: Aldo Canti, Marc Hannibal, Elliot Ngok, Malisa Longo, Aldo Bufi Landi
Keywords: amazon, sword fight, peplum, supernatural power

Overview: Three men with supernatural powers interfere in a tribal warfare to prevent evil Amazons destroying the inhabitants of a village.
