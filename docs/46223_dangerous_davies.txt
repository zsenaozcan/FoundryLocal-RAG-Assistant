Title: Dangerous Davies (1981)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Val Guest
Main Cast: Bernard Cribbins, Bill Maynard, Joss Ackland, Bernard Lee, John Leyton

Overview: When D. C. Dangerous Davies, not held in high regard by his superiors, is assigned to find a notorious criminal kingpin, he uncovers the details of 15 year old cold case.
