Title: The Grim Reaper (1962)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery, Foreign
Director: Bernardo Bertolucci
Main Cast: Carlotta Barilli, Lorenza Benedetti, Clorinda Celani, Vincenzo Ciccora, Alvaro D'Ercole

Overview: Near the Tiber river, in a Roman park, a prostitute was killed. The police tracks down people that were inside the park during that night. They are questioned and have to explain why they were there. One of them is the killer.
