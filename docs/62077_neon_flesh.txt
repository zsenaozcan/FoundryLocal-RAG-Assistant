Title: Neon Flesh (2010)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Comedy
Director: Paco Cabezas
Main Cast: Mario Casas, Vicente Romero, Macarena Gómez, Luciano Cáceres, Ángela Molina

Overview: Hoping to earn his mother's respect, a young hustler-turned-businessman tries to open a brothel with his two useless buddies.
