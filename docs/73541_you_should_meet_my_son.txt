Title: You Should Meet My Son (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Keith Hartman
Main Cast: Joanne McGee, Carol Goans, Stewart Carrico, Ginger Pullman, Steve Snyder

Overview: A comedy about a conservative Southern mom who discovers that her only son is gay. Determined that he won’t go through life alone and miserable, she sets out to find him the perfect husband!
