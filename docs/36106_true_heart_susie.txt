Title: True Heart Susie (1919)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Drama
Director: D.W. Griffith
Main Cast: Lillian Gish, Robert Harron, Wilbur Higby, Loyola O'Connor, George Fawcett

Overview: True Heart Susie (Gish) secretly loves her neighbor, William Jenkins (Harron), but neither, it seems, can confess their feelings for each other.
