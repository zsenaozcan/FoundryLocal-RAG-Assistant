Title: Ice Quake (2010)
Genres: Thriller, Science Fiction, Action
Director: Paul Ziller
Main Cast: Brendan Fehr, Holly Elissa Lamaro, Jodelle Ferland, Ryan Grantham, Rob LaBelle

Overview: As the permafrost melts in Alaska, underground rivers of volatile liquid methane form which sets off a series of devastating earthquakes. Forced apart by this violent occurrence, one family must find each other during the holidays and work to stop the deadly rivers that could cause a worldwide catastrophy.
