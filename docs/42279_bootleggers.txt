Title: Bootleggers (1962)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Leonid Gayday
Main Cast: Yuriy Nikulin, Georgiy Vitsin, Yevgeni Morgunov, Vladimir Pitsek
Keywords: police, alcoholism, slapstick, dog, moonshine

Overview: This is the second silent (save for a song) slapstick comedy short about adventures of Worldly, Coward, and Fool. In a small hunting lodge three friends are making illegal moonshine. Bottled "product" fills shelves quickly. Life is good. But their dog Barbos doesn't understand that bringing a moonshine condenser coil to a police station is a bad idea...
