Title: A Christmas Horror Story (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: Brett Sullivan
Main Cast: William Shatner, George Buza, Rob Archer, Zoé De Grand Maison, Alex Ozerov
Keywords: santa claus, christmas, krampus

Overview: Christmas is supposed to be a time of joy, peace and goodwill. But for some folks in the small town of Bailey Downs, it turns into something much less festive.
