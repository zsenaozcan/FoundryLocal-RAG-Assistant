Title: The Man in the Wall (2015)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Evgeny Ruman
Main Cast: Gilad Kahana, Yoav Donat, Tamar Alkan
Keywords: relationship

Overview: One night, one apartment and one mystery. Shir wakes up in the middle of the night. Her husband hasn’t come back after taking the dog out. The dog has. But where is Rami? Friends, relatives, neighbours and police come round and with each visit more marital secrets are revealed.
