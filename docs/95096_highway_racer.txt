Title: Highway Racer (1977)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Stelvio Massi
Main Cast: Maurizio Merli, Giancarlo Sbragia, Angelo Infanti, Lilli Carati

Overview: Maurizio Merli stars as a hot-shot police driver who has more guts than brains, often landing him in hot water with his middle-aged mentor, who was once a legendary police interceptor responsible for numerous large scale arrests.
