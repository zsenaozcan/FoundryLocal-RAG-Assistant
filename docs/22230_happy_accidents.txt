Title: Happy Accidents (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Brad Anderson
Main Cast: Vincent D'Onofrio, Marisa Tomei, Nadia Dajani, Holland Taylor, Tovah Feldshuh
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A comedy that pays tribute to the science fiction genre -- specifically, the sub-genre of time travel. But here the alternate reality is contemporary New York City where past and future experiences of trust, commitment and denial are cleverly put to the test. Just as Ruby is beginning to relish her first-ever healthy relationship, Sam begins muttering about being a time traveler from the year 2470.
