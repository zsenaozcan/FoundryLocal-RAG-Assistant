Title: Wolves (1999)
Genres: Documentary
Director: David Douglas
Main Cast: Robbie Robertson
Keywords: wolf, nature, imax

Overview: A look at the great wolf debate with comments and views from people on both sides. It also contains footage of Natives dressing up &amp; doing tribal dances. The link between wolves, bison, and Native Americans; as well as white man's reasoning behind their determination to eliminate bison and wolves from the landscape.
