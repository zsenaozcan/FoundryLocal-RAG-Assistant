Title: Ghosts Before Breakfast (1928)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Hans Richter
Main Cast: Werner Graeff, Walter Gronostay, Paul Hindemith, Darius Milhaud, Madeleine Milhaud
Keywords: surrealism, experimental film, avant-garde, dadaism

Overview: Hans Richter, noted for his abstract shorts, has everyday objects rebelling against their daily routine.
