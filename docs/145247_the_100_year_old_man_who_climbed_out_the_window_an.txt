Title: The 100 Year-Old Man Who Climbed Out the Window and Disappeared (2013)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Drama
Director: Felix Herngren
Main Cast: Robert Gustafsson, Iwar Wiklander, David Wiberg, Mia Skäringer, Jens Hultén

Overview: After living a long and colorful life, Allan Karlsson finds himself stuck in a nursing home. On his 100th birthday, he leaps out a window and begins an unexpected journey.
