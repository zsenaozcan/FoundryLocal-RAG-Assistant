Title: A Fine Pair (1968)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Crime
Director: Francesco Maselli
Main Cast: Rock Hudson, Claudia Cardinale, Tomás Milián, Leon Askin, Ellen Corby
Keywords: austria, caper, jewel thief

Overview: A detective gets involved with the beautiful daughter of an old friend. The daughter turns out to be a jewel thief, who in turn gets the detective involved in a caper in Austria.
