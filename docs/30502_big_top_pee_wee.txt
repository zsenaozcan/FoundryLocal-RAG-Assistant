Title: Big Top Pee-wee (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Randal Kleiser
Main Cast: Paul Reubens, Penelope Ann Miller, Kris Kristofferson, Valeria Golino, Benicio del Toro
Keywords: pig, relationship problems, travelling circus, beautiful girl, animal lover, creepy neighbor

Overview: Paul Reubens stars as Pee-wee Herman in his second full-length film about a farmer who joins the circus after a storm drops a big tent in his front yard. Pee-wee, along with an outlandish cast of animals and circus performers, puts on the best show ever.
