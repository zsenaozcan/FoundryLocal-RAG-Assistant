Title: The Biggest Fan (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Michael Criscione
Main Cast: Kaila Amariah, Marissa Tait, Richard Moll, Pat Morita, Shanelle Workman

Overview: Young girl (Kayla Amariah) falls in love with Chris Trousdale of popular boys band, Dream Street, in a sweet, highly comedic hand-holding romance.
