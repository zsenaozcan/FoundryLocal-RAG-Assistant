Title: Secret Sunshine (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lee Chang-dong
Main Cast: Jeon Do-yeon, Song Kang-ho, Park Myung-shin, Cho Youg-jin, Kim Young-jae
Keywords: loss of son, suicide attempt, loss of husband, faith, independent film, south korea

Overview: Sin-ae moves with her son Jun to Miryang, the town where her dead husband was born. As she tries to come to herself and set out on new foundations, another tragic event overturns her life.
