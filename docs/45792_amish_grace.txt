Title: Amish Grace (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Gregg Champion
Main Cast: Kimberly Williams-Paisley, Tammy Blanchard, Matt Letscher, Fay Masterson, Darcy Rose Byrnes

Overview: When a gunman killed five Amish children and injured five others in a Nickel Mines, Pennsylvania schoolhouse shooting in October of 2006, the world media attention rapidly turned from the tragic events to the extraordinary forgiveness demonstrated by the Amish community.
