Title: Heart of the Dragon (1985)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Sammo Hung
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Sammo Hung, Emily Chu, Mang Hoi, Melvin Wong
Keywords: martial arts, mentally disabled, brothers

Overview: Story of a cop who forsakes his dreams of sailing around the world so that he can care for his mentally retarded brother. Innocently caught up in a gangland fight, the brother is kidnapped to force the cop to turn over a police informant.
