Title: Wild Bill (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Dexter Fletcher
Main Cast: Charlie Creed-Miles, Will Poulter, Sammy Williams, Leo Gregory, Andy Serkis
Keywords: drug dealer, british, parole, ex-con

Overview: Out on parole after 8 years inside Bill Hayward returns home to find his now 11 and 15 year old sons abandoned by their mother and fending for themselves. Unwilling to play Dad, an uncaring Bill is determined to move on.
