Title: Helga, She Wolf of Spilberg (1977)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Patrice Rhomm
Main Cast: Patrizia Gori, Malisa Longo, Dominique Aveline, Alban Ceray, Jacques Marbeuf
Keywords: sadism, nazi exploitation film

Overview: Helga, a woman who runs a strict prison camp, forces her female prisoners into slave labor and to be love toys for her own personal pleasure, as well as for her soldiers. Issuing torture and whippings to anyone who dares defy her, man or woman.
