Title: In the Basement (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ulrich Seidl
Main Cast: Alessa Duchek, Gerald Duchek, Inge Ellinger, Manfred Ellinger, Walter Holzer
Keywords: basement, weird

Overview: Filmmaker Ulrich Seidl explores of the dark underside of the human psyche by entering Austrian basements fitted out as private domains for secrets and fetishes.
