Title: Kung Fu Rascals (1992)
Genres: Comedy, Action
Director: Steve Wang
Main Cast: Steve Wang, Troy Fromin, Johnnie Saiko, Les Claypool

Overview: In the tradition of Bruce Lee, Jackie Chan and the Three Stooges comes this wildly funny action comedy from director Steve Wang. When chased by mutated monsters, crafty ninjas and a 300 foot tall stone god, the bumbling heroes battle against the powers of darkness in search of "The Power Most Big".
