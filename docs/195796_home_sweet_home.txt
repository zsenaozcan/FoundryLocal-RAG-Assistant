Title: Home Sweet Home (2013)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: David Morley
Main Cast: Marty Adams, Shaun Benson, Meghan Heffern, Adam MacDonald
Keywords: gun, sword, mask, murder, gore, serial killer, masked killer, home invasion, cell phone, hand
