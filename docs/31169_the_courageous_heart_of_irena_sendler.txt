Title: The Courageous Heart of Irena Sendler (2009)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: John Kent Harrison
Main Cast: Anna Paquin, Marcia Gay Harden, Goran Visnjic, Nathaniel Parker, Steve Speirs
Keywords: holocaust, world war ii, biography, nazi germany

Overview: Irena Sendler is a Catholic social worker who has sympathized with the Jews since her childhood, when her physician father died of typhus contracted while treating poor Jewish patients. When she initially proposes saving Jewish children from the Warsaw Ghetto, her idea is met with skepticism by fellow workers, her parish priest, and even her own mother Janina.
