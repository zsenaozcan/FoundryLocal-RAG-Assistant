Title: Rascals (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Foreign
Director: David Dhawan
Main Cast: Sanjay Dutt, Ajay Devgn, Kangana Ranaut, Arjun Rampal, Lisa Haydon

Overview: Rascals movie is a comedy about the joys of conning where Sanjay Dutt and Ajay Devgn play two cool competing conmen trying to out-scheme each other in consistently amusing and surprising setups. But when both fall for the same girl, the competition reaches an all new level as both men constantly attempt to outwit each other to win over the surprisingly elusive Kangana!
