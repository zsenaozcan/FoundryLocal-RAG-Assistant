Title: Preacher's Kid (2010)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Stan Foster
Main Cast: Letoya Luckett, Tammy Townsend, Carlos Davis
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Small-town girl Angie King leaves the church and her home to pursue a dream of singing stardom. Luckett plays Angie, the daughter of a stern but loving bishop, whose attraction to the hunky star (Tank) of a traveling gospel show takes her on the road…and into romance, heartbreak and the realization that happiness may lie in the home she left behind.
