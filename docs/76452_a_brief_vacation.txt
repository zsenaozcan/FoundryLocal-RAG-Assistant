Title: A Brief Vacation (1973)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Vittorio De Sica
Main Cast: Florinda Bolkan, Renato Salvatori, Daniel Quenaud

Overview: Vittorio De Sica's A Brief Vacation (Una Breva Vacanza) stars Florinda Bolkan as a downtrodden working woman. Forced to support herself, her children, her physically incapacitated husband and her obtrusive brother and mother, Bolkan contracts tuberculosis. She is granted a brief vacation at a health spa, where a whole new world--and potential new life--is opened up to her.
