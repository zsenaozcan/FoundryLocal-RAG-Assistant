Title: The Idle Class (1921)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Charlie Chaplin
Main Cast: Charlie Chaplin, Edna Purviance, Mack Swain, Henry Bergman, Al Ernest Garcia

Overview: A tramp sneaks into a upper class golf resort. The tramp meets a rich woman who is having an argument with her drunken husband. Complications arise when she mistakes the tramp for her husband.
