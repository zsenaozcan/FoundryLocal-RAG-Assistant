Title: Miracle In Lane 2 (2000)
Genres: TV Movie, Family, Drama
Director: Greg Beeman
Main Cast: Frankie Muniz, Rick Rossovich, Molly Hagan

Overview: Young tween Justin Yoder, who's known for his outgoing demeanor and wit despite being confined to a wheelchair, dreams to be like his athletic older brother and propels himself into the world of soapbox derby racing. It's a field he's sure he has a chance in. Unfortunately, he finds that because of his condition, not everyone is eager to see him compete.
