Title: The Arrival (1996)
Genres: Action, Mystery, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: David Twohy
Main Cast: Charlie Sheen, Lindsay Crouse, Richard Schiff, Ron Silver, Teri Polo
Keywords: greenhouse effect, global warming, extraterrestrial, astronomer, conspiracy, scientist, foot chase, desert, climatologist, observatory

Overview: Zane Ziminski is an astrophysicist who receives a message that seems to have extraterrestrial origins. Eerily soon after his discovery, Zane is fired. He then embarks on a search to determine the origins of the transmission that leads him into a Hitchcockian labyrinth of paranoia and intrigue.
