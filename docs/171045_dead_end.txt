Title: Dead End (2012)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Nicholas David Lean
Main Cast: Jamie Foreman, Wil Johnson, Jon Campling, Angela Dixon, Frances Speedie
Keywords: terror, carnival, road trip, diner

Overview: Carl Foster takes off on a well-deserved weekend break with his family knowing only too well that focus on work has impacted his relationship with his wife, young son, and teenage step-daughter. But after a short but restful break in the journey Carl awakens to find himself tied and bound in an old roadside diner, his family trussed and gagged next to him, and a disparate group of dirty, disheveled, vagrant-like undesirables keeping them captive. Only time will reveal who they are and what they want, but things are not everything they might seem.
