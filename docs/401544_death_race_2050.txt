Title: Death Race 2050 (2017)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: G.J. Echternkamp
Main Cast: Manu Bennett, Malcolm McDowell, Marci Miller, Burt Grinstead, Folake Olowofoyeku
Keywords: female nudity, car race, black humor, overpopulation, remake, murder, grindhouse, corporate world, dystopic future, political satire

Overview: The year 2050 the planet has become overpopulated, to help control population the government develops a race. The Death Race. Annually competitors race across the country scoring points for killing people with their vehicles.
