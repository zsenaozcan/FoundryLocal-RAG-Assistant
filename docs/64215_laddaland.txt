Title: Laddaland (2011)
Genres: Horror, Foreign
Director: Sophon Sakdaphisit
Main Cast: Saharat Sangkapreecha, Piyathida Woramusik, Sutatta Udomsilp, Athipich Chutiwatkajornchai, Deuntem Salitul

Overview: A family moves into a new house where they gradually begin to encounter paranormal and horror events.
