Title: Howling III: The Marsupials (1987)
Genres: Horror, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Philippe Mora
Main Cast: Barry Otto, William Yang, Imogen Annesley, Deby Wightman, Lee Biolos

Overview: A strange race of human-like marsupials appear suddenly in Australia, and a sociologist who studies these creatures falls in love with a female one. Is this a dangerous combination?
