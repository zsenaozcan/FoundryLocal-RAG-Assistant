Title: The Star Witness (1931)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: Walter Huston, Grant Mitchell, Frances Starr, Charles 'Chic' Sale, Edward Nugent
Keywords: war veteran, grandfather, police protection, murder, gang, old man, shootout, fear, gangster, district attorney

Overview: A tough District Attorney (Walter Huston) goes after a murderous crime gang, only to find that his witnesses, an innocent family, have clammed up in fear of reprisals.
