Title: Tapia (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Eddie Alcazar
Main Cast: Johnny Tapia

Overview: Albuquerque-born boxer Johnny Tapia's life was a maelstrom of turmoil. The glory of his punishing ring prowess and handful of world titles across three weight classes forever jockeyed with personal demons: his mother's kidnapping and murder when he was 8, drug addiction, mental illness and suicide attempts.
