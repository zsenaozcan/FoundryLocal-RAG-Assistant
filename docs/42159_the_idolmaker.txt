Title: The Idolmaker (1980)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Music
Director: Taylor Hackford
Main Cast: Ray Sharkey, Peter Gallagher, Joe Pantoliano, Paul Land, JoJo D'Amore
Keywords: musical, rock music

Overview: Based on the life of rock promoter/producer Bob Marucci, who discovered, among others, Frankie Avalon and Fabian.
