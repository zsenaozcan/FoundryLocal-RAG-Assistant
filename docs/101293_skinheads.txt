Title: Skinheads (1989)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Action, Crime
Director: Greydon Clark
Main Cast: Chuck Connors, Barbara Bain, Jason Culp, Brian Brophy, Bill Kohne
Keywords: skinhead

Overview: A group of students traveling to California are menaced by a vicious group of skin-heads in the Colorado mountains. A WW2 vet living in the mountains comes to their rescue.
