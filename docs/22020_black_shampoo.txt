Title: Black Shampoo (1976)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Greydon Clark
Main Cast: John Daniels, Tanya Boyd, Joseph Carlo, Skip E. Lowe, Gary Allen
Keywords: hairdresser, blaxploitation

Overview: John Daniels stars as Jonathan Knight, the owner of "Mr. Jonathans", the most successful hair salon for women on the Sunset Strip. Everything is cool for Jonathan until he messes with the mob in an effort to protect his young, attractive receptionist from her former boss.
