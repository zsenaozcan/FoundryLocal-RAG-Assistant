Title: Crazy on the Outside (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Tim Allen
Main Cast: Tim Allen, Sigourney Weaver, Ray Liotta, J.K. Simmons, Julie Bowen
Keywords: ex-detainee, duringcreditsstinger

Overview: A recently paroled ex-con who has trouble adjusting to the wacky normalcy of life outside of prison. He has spent the last three years behind bars after getting caught committing a crime and taking the rap for his much more dangerous pal.
