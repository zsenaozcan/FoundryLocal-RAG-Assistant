Title: The Yes Men (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary
Director: Dan Ollman
Main Cast: Mike Bonanno, Andy Bichlbaum, Michael Moore, Patrick Lichty, Sal Salamone
Keywords: globalization, third world, market economy, political activism, anti-globalisation activist, conference, interview, discussion, speech, global economy

Overview: A comic, biting and revelatory documentary following a small group of prankster activists as they gain worldwide notoriety for impersonating the World Trade Organization (WTO) on television and at business conferences around the world.
