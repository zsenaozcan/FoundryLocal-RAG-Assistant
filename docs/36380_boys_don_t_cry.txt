Title: Boys Don't Cry (2000)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Olaf Lubaszenko
Main Cast: Cezary Pazura, Maciej Stuhr, Michał Milowicz, Bohdan Łazuka

Overview: One of the best Polish comedies of the late 1990's, "Boys Don't Cry" is a satirical look at the gangsters of Poland and some teens who accidentally get involved with them.
