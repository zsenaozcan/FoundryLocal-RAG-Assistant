Title: Two Smart People (1946)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Jules Dassin
Main Cast: Lucille Ball, John Hodiak, Lloyd Nolan, Hugo Haas, Lenore Ulric
Keywords: con man

Overview: A con woman (Lucille Ball) and a detective (Lloyd Nolan) follow a prison-bound swindler (John Hodiak) on his five-day gourmet binge.
