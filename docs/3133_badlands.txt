Title: Badlands (1973)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Terrence Malick
Main Cast: Martin Sheen, Sissy Spacek, Ramon Bieri, Warren Oates, Alan Vint
Keywords: mass murder, on the run, fugitive, killing spree, gritty, south dakota, badlands

Overview: Dramatization of the Starkweather-Fugate killing spree of the 1950s, in which a teenage girl and her twenty-something boyfriend slaughtered her entire family and several others in the Dakota badlands.
