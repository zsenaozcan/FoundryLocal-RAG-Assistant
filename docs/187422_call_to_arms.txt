Title: Call to Arms (1973)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Foreign
Director: San Kong
Main Cast: Chung Wa, Wang Hsieh, Chan Shen, Ha Faan, Cheung Ban
Keywords: sacrifice, exile, palace intrigue, assassination attempt

Overview: The aggressive and dangerous Chin empire occupies the put-upon Chao kingdom while the hero-filled Wei empire is paralysed by an indecisive ruler.
