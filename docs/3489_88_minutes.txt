Title: 88 Minutes (2007)
Genres: Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Jon Avnet
Main Cast: Al Pacino, Alicia Witt, Leelee Sobieski, Amy Brenneman, William Forsythe
Keywords: fbi, professor, ladies' man, medical examiner, serial killer, lecture

Overview: "88 Minutes" focuses on a college professor (Pacino) who moonlights as a forensic psychiatrist for the FBI and receives a death threat claiming he has only 88 minutes to live.
