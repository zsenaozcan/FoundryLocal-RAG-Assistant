Title: Fast, Cheap & Out of Control (1997)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Errol Morris
Main Cast: Dave Hoover, George Mendonça, Raymond A. Mendez, Rodney Brooks
Keywords: circus, lion, eccentric, robot

Overview: Hilarious, sad, absurd, eerie and beautiful, "FAST, CHEAP &amp; OUT OF CONTROL" is a film like no other. Starting as a darkly funny contemplation of the Sisyphus-like nature of human striving, it ultimately becomes a profoundly moving meditation on the very nature of existence.
