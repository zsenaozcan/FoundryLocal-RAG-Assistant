Title: The Gunfighter (1950)
Genres: Western
Director: Henry King
Main Cast: Gregory Peck, Helen Westcott, Millard Mitchell, Jean Parker, Karl Malden
Keywords: marshal, saloon, bartender, western, shootout, gunfighter, bar shootout, street shootout

Overview: Gregory Peck stars as an aging notorious gunslinger, Johnny Ringo. Sick of killing he tries to avoid trouble, but when a cocksure young man named Eddie draws on him, Ringo has no choice but to kill him. Now Eddie's three brothers are after him. Ringo decides to return East to see his estranged wife and young son before the brothers catch up with him.
