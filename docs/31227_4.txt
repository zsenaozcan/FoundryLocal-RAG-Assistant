Title: 4 (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ilya Khrzhanovsky
Main Cast: Yuri Laguta, Konstantin Murzenko, Sergey Shnurov, Marina Vovchenko, Shavkat Abdusalamov
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Two men and a woman happen to meet in a bar. We learn from their conversations both the intriguing and banal details of their lives. But is anyone really telling the truth?
