Title: Salem Witch Trials (2002)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Joseph Sargent
Main Cast: Kirstie Alley, Henry Czerny, Gloria Reuben, Jay O. Sanders, Kristin Booth
Keywords: history, puritan, witch hunt

Overview: The film recounts the sobering tale of how 19 innocent lives were taken in a community experiencing paranoid hysteria regarding the suspected - but unproved - presence of the occult in the small New England town.
