Title: Karol: A Man Who Became Pope (2005)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Giacomo Battiato
Main Cast: Piotr Adamczyk, Malgorzata Bela, Ken Duken, Hristo Shopov, Ennio Fantastichini

Overview: The life of the pope John-Paul II, from his youth as a writer, actor, and athlete in war-torn occupied Poland to his election as Pope at the age of 58.
