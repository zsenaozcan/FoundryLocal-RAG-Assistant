Title: Saboteur (1942)
Genres: Thriller, War
Director: Alfred Hitchcock
Main Cast: Priscilla Lane, Robert Cummings, Otto Kruger, Alan Baxter, Clem Bevans
Keywords: falsely accused, suspense, aircraft factory

Overview: Aircraft factory worker Barry Kane goes on the run across the United States when he is wrongly accused of starting a fire that killed his best friend.
