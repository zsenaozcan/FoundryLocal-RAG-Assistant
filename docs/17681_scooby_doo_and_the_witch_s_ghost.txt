Title: Scooby-Doo! and the Witch's Ghost (1999)
Genres: Animation, Comedy
Director: Jim Stenstrum
Main Cast: Scott Innes, Mary Kay Bergman, Frank Welker, B. J. Ward, Kimberly Brooks
Keywords: crime solving

Overview: A famous horror writer, Ben Ravencroft invites the Scooby gang to his home town of Oakhaven where they find the ghost of "witch" Sarah Ravencroft, a relative of Ben's, is haunting the town.
