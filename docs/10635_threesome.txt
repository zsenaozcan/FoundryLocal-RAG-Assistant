Title: Threesome (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family, Romance
Director: Andrew Fleming
Main Cast: Josh Charles, Stephen Baldwin, Lara Flynn Boyle, Alexis Arquette, Martha Gehman
Keywords: crush, pillow fight, catcher in the rye, odd couple, water balloon, pregnancy scare, water gun

Overview: Eddy and Stuart share two-thirds of a dormitory suite. Due to bureaucratic error, a woman named Alex is added to their room. At first, relations among the three are tense. Soon, however, Alex falls for Eddy, and Stuart lusts after Alex.
