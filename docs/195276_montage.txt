Title: Montage (2013)
Genres: Thriller, Mystery, Crime
Director: Chung Keun-sup
Main Cast: Uhm Jung-hwa, Kim Sang-kyung, Song Young-chang, Jo Hee-bong, Yoo Seung-mok

Overview: A kidnapper disappeared 15 years ago without a trace. Five days before the case's statute of limitations expires, someone anonymously leaves a flower at the crime scene. A few days later, another kidnapping takes place using the same method on a similar target.
