Title: Since Otar Left (2003)
Genres: Drama
Director: Julie Bertuccelli
Main Cast: Esther Gorintin, Nino Khomassouridze, Dinara Drukarova, Temur Kalandadze, Rusudan Bolkvadze
Keywords: independent film, family, death, woman director, tbilisi

Overview: The one joy in the lives of a mother and daughter comes from the regular letters sent to them from Paris from the family's adored son, Otar. When the daughter finds out that Otar has died suddenly, she tries to conceal the truth from her mother, changing the course of their lives forever.
