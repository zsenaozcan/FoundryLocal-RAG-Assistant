Title: A Song Is Born (1948)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Howard Hawks
Main Cast: Danny Kaye, Virginia Mayo, Benny Goodman, Louis Armstrong, Tommy Dorsey
Keywords: musical

Overview: The story of seven scholars (including Danny Kaye and Benny Goodman) in search of an expert to teach them about swing music. They seem to have found the perfect candidate in winsome nightclub singer Honey Swanson (Virginia Mayo). But Honey's gangster boyfriend doesn't want to give her up.
