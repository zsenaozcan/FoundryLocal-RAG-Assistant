Title: Batman: The Dark Knight Returns, Part 1 (2012)
Genres: Action, Animation
Director: Jay Oliva
Main Cast: Peter Weller, Ariel Winter, David Selby, Wade Williams, Michael McKean
Keywords: dc comics, future, based on graphic novel, dystopic future, super powers

Overview: Batman has not been seen for ten years. A new breed of criminal ravages Gotham City, forcing 55-year-old Bruce Wayne back into the cape and cowl. But, does he still have what it takes to fight crime in a new era?
