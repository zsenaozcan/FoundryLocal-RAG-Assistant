Title: The Art of Travel (2008)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: Thomas Whelan
Main Cast: Christopher Masterson, Brooke Burns, Johnny Messner, James Duval
Keywords: independent film

Overview: High school grad. Conner Layne is about to marry his first love, but when wedding plans fail, he goes solo on his honeymoon to Central America, finding adventure with a ragtag group of foreigners who attempt to cross the Darien Gap in record time.
