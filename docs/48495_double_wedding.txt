Title: Double Wedding (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Craig Pryce
Main Cast: Tia Mowry, Tamera Mowry, Ardon Bess, Robin Brûlé, Sandra Caldwell

Overview: Two sisters, no love lives. Both end up dating the same man, and inviting him to meet the family on December 17th. He doesn’t know there are two sisters or that he committed to two different dates on the same day.
