Title: The Good Die Young (1954)
Genres: Crime
Director: Lewis Gilbert
Main Cast: Laurence Harvey, Gloria Grahame, Richard Basehart, Joan Collins, John Ireland
Keywords: brit noir, film noir

Overview: 1954 British crime thriller by Lewis Gilbert, based on a novel by American novelist Richard Macaulay. Four men  in post-war London, two Brits and two Americans, feel forced to turn to crime by their financial problems, and decide to rob a mail van together.
