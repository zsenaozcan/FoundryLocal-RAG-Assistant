Title: Dossier 51 (1978)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Michel Deville
Main Cast: François Marthouret, Roger Planchon, Patrick Chesnais, Jean Martin, Daniel Mesguich

Overview: French diplomat Dominique Auphal is put under surveillance by an unnamed secret service. They wish to find a weakness in his life in order to control him politically. Auphal becomes "File no. 51": his private life is spied, analysed and commented.
