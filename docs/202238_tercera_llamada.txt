Title: Tercera Llamada (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Francisco Franco
Main Cast: Irene Azuela, Ricardo Blume, Anabel Ferreira, Karina Gidi, Rebecca Jones
Keywords: theatre play, theatre director, backstage, theatrical producer

Overview: Turmoil and tumultuousness become the order of the day when a Mexican theatrical company begins its rehearsals of absurdist philosopher Albert Camus's "Caligula" for an influential upcoming international theatre festival.
