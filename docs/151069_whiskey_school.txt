Title: Whiskey School (2005)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Peter Masterson
Main Cast: Olympia Dukakis, Alexandra Eite, Carlin Glynn, Lainie Kazan, Denise Lute

Overview: Leopold De Angeli is a talented playwright, but like many scribes he fancies the drink a little too much. In the hopes of saving Leopold's life, his theater friends come together to stage an intervention for him.
