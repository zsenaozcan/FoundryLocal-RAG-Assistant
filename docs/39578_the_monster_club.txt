Title: The Monster Club (1981)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Roy Ward Baker
Main Cast: Vincent Price, Donald Pleasence, John Carradine, Stuart Whitman, Britt Ekland
Keywords: based on novel, monster, vampire, stripper, anthology, independent film, ghost

Overview: A writer of horror stories is invited to a "monster club" by a mysterious old gentleman. There, three gruesome stories are told to him; between each story some musicians play their songs. In the end, it's recognized he's the greatest monster of all ...
