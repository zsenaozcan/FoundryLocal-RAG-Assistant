Title: The Babe (1992)
Genres: Drama
Director: Arthur Hiller
Main Cast: John Goodman, Kelly McGillis, Trini Alvarado, Randy Steinmeyer, James Cromwell
Keywords: baseball, sport, historical figure, new york yankees

Overview: "The Babe" chronicles Ruth's phenomenal story--from his hard knock beginnings at a Baltimore orphanage, to his meteoric rise to baseball superstardom and his poignant retirement from the game. His amazing career included seven American League pennants, four World Series championships, two tempestuous marriages and a wild lifestyle that earned him numerous suspensions.
