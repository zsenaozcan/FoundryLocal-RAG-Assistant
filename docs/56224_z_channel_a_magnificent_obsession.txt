Title: Z Channel: A Magnificent Obsession (2004)
Genres: History, Documentary
Director: Xan Cassavetes
Main Cast: Quentin Tarantino, Robert Altman, Jim Jarmusch, Paul Verhoeven, Alexander Payne
Keywords: cinephilia, television, interview, discussion, history, independent film, eccentric, memory, los angeles, retrospective

Overview: A documentary on the Z Channel, one of the first pay cable stations in the US, and its programming chief, Jerry Harvey. Debuting in 1974, the LA-based channel's eclectic slate of movies became a prime example of the untapped power of cable television.
