Title: Love Is My Profession (1958)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Crime
Director: Claude Autant-Lara
Main Cast: Jean Gabin, Brigitte Bardot, Edwige Feuillère, Nicole Berger, Franco Interlenghi

Overview: Married French lawyer Andre defends succesfully the case of Yvette, who committed a robbery. He falls in love with her, but she isn't true to him.
