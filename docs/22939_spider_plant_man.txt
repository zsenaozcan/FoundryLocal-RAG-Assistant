Title: Spider-Plant Man (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Rowan Atkinson, Rachel Stevens, Tony Robinson, Jim Broadbent, Mackenzie Crook

Overview: Spider-Plant Man is a parody of Spider-Man, made for the Comic Relief 2005 appeal and aired on BBC One. It featured Rowan Atkinson as Peter Piper/Spider-Plant Man and Rachel Stevens as his love-interest Jane-Mary (parodying Mary-Jane Watson). Jim Broadbent also made an appearance, portraying Batman, and Tony Robinson as Robin.
