Title: The Story of G.I. Joe (1945)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: Burgess Meredith, Robert Mitchum, Freddie Steele, Wally Cassell, Jimmy Lloyd
Keywords: journalist, italian, world war ii, german, northern africa, u.s. army, army, infantry, reporter

Overview: War correspondent Ernie Pyle joins Company C, 18th Infantry as this American army unit fights its way across North Africa in World War II. He comes to know the soldiers and finds much human interest material for his readers back in the States.
