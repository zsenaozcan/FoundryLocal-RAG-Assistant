Title: There Will Come a Day (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Giorgio Diritti
Main Cast: Jasmine Trinca, Anne Alvaro, Pia Engleberth, Sonia Gessner, Federica Fracassi

Overview: Painful family events lead Augusta to leave Italy. On a small boat, immersed in the Amazon Forest grandness, she begins a travel between Indios villages. From the favela to the isolation in the Forest, Augusta will go in search of herself.
