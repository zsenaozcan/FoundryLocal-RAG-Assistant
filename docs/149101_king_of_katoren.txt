Title: King of Katoren (2012)
Genres: Family, Adventure
Director: Ben Sombogaart
Main Cast: Abbey Hoes, Glenn Durfort, Frits Lambrechts, Mingus Dagelet, Urmie Plein
Keywords: corruption, based on novel, assignment, male female relationship, reporter, king

Overview: The seventeen year old Stach is faced with five impossible assignments he has to complete in order to become king. Things do not go as easily as the boisterous Stach had expected, especially when he is confronted with matters of life and death.
