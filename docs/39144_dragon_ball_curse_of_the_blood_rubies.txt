Title: Dragon Ball: Curse of the Blood Rubies (1986)
Genres: Action, Animation
Director: Daisuke Nishio
Main Cast: Tomiko Suzuki, Hiromi Tsuru, Masako Nozawa, Naoko Watanabe, Naoki Tatsuta
Keywords: anime, dragon ball,

Overview: A retelling of Dragon Ball's origins, this is a different version of the meeting of Goku, Bulma, Oolong, and Yamucha. They are all looking for the dragon balls for different reasons when they cross paths with an evil king named Gourmeth, who is also looking for the dragon balls.
