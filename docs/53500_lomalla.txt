Title: Lomalla (2000)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Aleksi Mäkelä
Main Cast: Juha Veijonen, Outi Mäenpää, Kärt Tomingas, Samuli Edelmann, Karri Termonen

Overview: The feature film produced by Solar Films has the title The South. It is a comedy of crime, romance and deception played out against the background of a vacation under the Mediterranean sun.
