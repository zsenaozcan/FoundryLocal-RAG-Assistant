Title: Red Passport (2003)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Albert Xavier
Main Cast: Frank Molina, Frank Medrano, Sharon Angela, Maite Bonilla, Héctor Rosario
Keywords: biography

Overview: Fabio Reyes, a former counterfeit artist who after serving 10 years of a 25 year sentence, wants nothing more than to rekindle his relationship with his long-lost daughter and live peacefully. But life isn't that fair and demons never die. As Fabio embarks on a roller-coaster ride of deception, lies, suspense and murder, nothing turns out to be as it seems.
