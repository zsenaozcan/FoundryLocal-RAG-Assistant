Title: The Insect Woman (1963)
Genres: Drama
Director: Shôhei Imamura
Main Cast: Emiko Aizawa, Masumi Harukawa, Sachiko Hidari, Emiko Higashi, Daizaburo Hirata
Keywords: buddhism, japan, prostitute, adultery, confession, brothel, commune, greed, cynicism, independent film

Overview: A woman, Tome, is born to a lower class family in Japan in 1918. The title refers to an insect, repeating its mistakes, as in an infinite circle. Imamura, with this metaphor, introduces the life of Tome, who keeps trying to change her poor life.
