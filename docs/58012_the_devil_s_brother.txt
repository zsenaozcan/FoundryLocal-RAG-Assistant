Title: The Devil's Brother (1933)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Hal Roach
Main Cast: Stan Laurel, Oliver Hardy, Dennis King, Lucile Browne, Thelma Todd
Keywords: innkeeper, musical, wine, firing squad, finger wiggle

Overview: The Devil's Brother or Bogus Bandits or Fra Diavolo is a 1933 comedy film starring Laurel and Hardy. It is based on Daniel Auber's operetta Fra Diavolo about the Italian bandit Fra Diavolo.
