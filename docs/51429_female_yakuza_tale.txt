Title: Female Yakuza Tale (1973)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Teruo Ishii
Main Cast: Reiko Ike, Makoto Ashikawa, Arumi Kuri, Meika Seri, Jun Midorikawa
Keywords: samurai, eroticism, yakuza, suspense

Overview: Ocho is accidentally captured by a drug trafficking cartel who use Chinese women to smuggle drugs into Japan by hiding it in their vaginas. She is tortured, and manages to escape, fighting both the male yakuzas and a gang of female thieves.
