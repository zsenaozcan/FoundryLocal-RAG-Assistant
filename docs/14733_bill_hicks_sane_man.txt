Title: Bill Hicks: Sane Man (1989)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Kevin Booth
Main Cast: Bill Hicks
Keywords: comedian, honesty, stand-up comedy, cult comedy, comedy

Overview: The amazing comedian Bill Hicks passed away in 1994, but his legend continues to grow. Sane Man was filmed before Bill recorded "Dangerous," his first comedy album, and is a turning point in Hicks' career.
