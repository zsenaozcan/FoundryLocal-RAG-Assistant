Title: The Drummer (2007)
Genres: Drama, Action, Foreign
Director: Kenneth Bi
Main Cast: Jaycee Chan, Tony Leung Ka-Fai, Angelica Lee, Roy Cheung

Overview: When Sid insults a powerful triad boss, his father sends him to Taiwan for safety. Bored and restless, he discovers a community of Zen drummers high in the hills, and joins up - a decision inspired by a pretty face that soon becomes a challenge.
