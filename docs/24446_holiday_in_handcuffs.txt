Title: Holiday in Handcuffs (2007)
Genres: TV Movie, Comedy, Romance, Family
Director: Ron Underwood
Main Cast: Melissa Joan Hart, Mario López, Markie Post, June Lockhart, Timothy Bottoms
Keywords: family holiday, christmas

Overview: A ne er-do-well thirty-something (Melissa Joan Hart) attempts to appease her family by kidnapping herself an attractive boyfriend (Mario Lopez) for the family Christmas. Despite unlikely odds and dysfunctional family moments, the two fall in love and share a magical Christmas.
