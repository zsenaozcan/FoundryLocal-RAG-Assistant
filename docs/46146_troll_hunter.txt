Title: Troll Hunter (2010)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror
Director: André Øvredal
Main Cast: Otto Jespersen, Hans Morten Hansen, Tomas Alf Larsen, Johanna Mørck, Knut Nærum
Keywords: hunter, mountain, wilderness, adventure, forest, secret government organization, hunting trip, wildlife, conspiracy, troll

Overview: A group of students investigates a series of mysterious bear killings, but learns that there are much more dangerous things going on. They start to follow a mysterious hunter, learning that he is actually a troll hunter.
