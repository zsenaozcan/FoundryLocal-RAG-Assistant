Title: A Return to Salem's Lot (1987)
Genres: Horror
Director: Larry Cohen
Main Cast: Michael Moriarty, Ricky Addison Reed, Samuel Fuller, Andrew Duggan, Ronee Blakley
Keywords: vampire, new england

Overview: Joe Weber is an anthropologist who takes his son on a trip to the New England town of Salem's Lot unaware that it is populated by vampires. When the inhabitants reveal their secret, they ask Joe to write a bible for them.
