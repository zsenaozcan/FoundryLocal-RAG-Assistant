Title: Personal Property (1937)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: W.S. Van Dyke
Main Cast: Jean Harlow, Robert Taylor, Reginald Owen, Una O'Connor, Henrietta Crosman

Overview: Raymond Dabney returns to his family after trouble with the law. He convinces the sheriff to give him a job watching the house and furniture of widow Crystal Wetherby without knowing she is engaged to his brother.
