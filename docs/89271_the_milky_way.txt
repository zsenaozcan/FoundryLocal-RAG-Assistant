Title: The Milky Way (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lina Chamie
Main Cast: Marco Ricca, Alice Braga, Fernando Alves Pinto, Juliane Elting

Overview: A modern love story set in Sao Paulo about Heitor, a writer and literature professor, and Julia, a theatre student turned veterinarian. A fight over the telephone propels Heitor into a journey through the chaotic city streets to reconcile with Julia. Their personal history unfolds against the backdrop of the city and its people in a series of events representing the struggle between poetry and science.
