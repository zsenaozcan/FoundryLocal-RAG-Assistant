Title: Dead Men Tell (1941)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Crime, Drama, Comedy
Director: Harry Lachman
Main Cast: Sidney Toler, Sheila Ryan, Robert Weldon, Victor Sen Yung, Donald Douglas

Overview: A treasure map in four pieces, the ghost of a hanged pirate, a talking parrot, and a ship full of red herrings complicate Charlie's search for a murderer on board a docked ship.
