Title: Ossos (1997)
Genres: Drama
Director: Pedro Costa
Main Cast: Vanda Duarte, Nuno Vaz, Isabel Ruth, Inês de Medeiros
Keywords: drug, life in the slums

Overview: After a suicidal teenage girl gives birth, she misguidedly entrusts her baby’s safety to the troubled, deadbeat father, whose violent actions take the viewer on a tour of the foreboding, crumbling shantytown in which they live.
