Title: Boycott (2001)
Genres: History, Drama, TV Movie
Director: Clark Johnson
Main Cast: Jeffrey Wright, Terrence Howard, CCH Pounder, Carmen Ejogo, Reg E. Cathey
Keywords: martin luther king, civil rights movement , african american

Overview: This made-for-TV movie dramatizes the historic boycott of public buses in the 1950s, led by civil rights leader Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr.
