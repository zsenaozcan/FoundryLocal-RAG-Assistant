Title: Clubbed (2008)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: Neil Thompson
Main Cast: Mel Raido, Scot Williams, Maxine Peake, Ronnie Fox, Colin Salmon
Keywords: suicide, drug dealer, clubbing, beaten to death

Overview: An underworld drama set in the early 1980s, about a lonely factory worker whose life is transformed when he becomes a nightclub doorman.
