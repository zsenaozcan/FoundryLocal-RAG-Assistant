Title: George & A.J. (2009)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Josh Cooley
Main Cast: Steve Purcell, Jason Topolski, Bob Peterson
Keywords: short, pixar animation

Overview: George &amp; A.J. is a short film created by Pixar which uses characters from the film Up to tell what Nurses George and A.J. did after Carl Fredricksen left with his house tied to balloons in the feature film.
