Title: Summer Rental (1985)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Carl Reiner
Main Cast: John Candy, Karen Austin, Kerri Green, Joey Lawrence, Aubrey Jene
Keywords: rivalry, family vacation, suffering parent, time off, run down house

Overview: Jack Chester, an overworked air traffic controller, takes his family on vacation to the beach. Things immediately start to go wrong for the Chesters, and steadily get worse. Jack ends up in a feud with a local yachtsman, and has to race him to regain his pride and family's respect.
