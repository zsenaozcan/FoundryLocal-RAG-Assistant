Title: What War May Bring (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Claude Lelouch
Main Cast: Audrey Dana, Samuel Labarthe, Dominique Pinon, Anouk Aimée

Overview: A theater projectionist's daughter is involved in the Resistance in WWII, but she doesn’t share his political involvement &amp; only believes in passion. She follows the men she loves unconditionally, suffering betrayal and hardship.
