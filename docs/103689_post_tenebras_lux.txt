Title: Post Tenebras Lux (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Carlos Reygadas
Main Cast: Adolfo Jimenez Castro, Willebaldo Torres, Rut Reygadas, Eleazar Reygadas, Nathalia Acevedo

Overview: Juan and his urban family live in the Mexican countryside, where they enjoy and suffer a world apart. And nobody knows if these two worlds are complementary or if they strive to eliminate one another.
