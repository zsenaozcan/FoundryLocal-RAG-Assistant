Title: A Dry White Season (1989)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: Euzhan Palcy
Main Cast: Donald Sutherland, Janet Suzman, Zakes Mokae, Jürgen Prochnow, Susan Sarandon
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Taking place during the 1976 Soweto uprising, the story follows a white school teacher whose life and values are threatened when he asks questions about the death of a young black boy who died in police custody.
