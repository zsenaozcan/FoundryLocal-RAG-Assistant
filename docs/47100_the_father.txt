Title: The Father (1996)
Genres: Drama
Director: Majid Majidi
Main Cast: Hassan Sadeghi, Parivash Nazarieh, Mohammad Kasebi, Hossein Abedini
Keywords: father son relationship

Overview: Mehrollah is a 14-year-old boy who is forced to find a job to support his family after his father dies. He travels to the southern parts of Iran, looking for work. Upon his return to his hometown, he notices certain changes in his family.
