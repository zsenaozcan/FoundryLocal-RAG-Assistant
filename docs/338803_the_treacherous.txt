Title: The Treacherous (2015)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Min Kyu-dong
Main Cast: Ju Ji-hoon, Kim Kang-woo, Lim Ji-yeon, Lee You-young, Cheon Ho-jin
Keywords: korean movie

Overview: The story of Joseon's tyrant king Yeonsan who exploits the populace for his own carnal pleasures, his seemingly loyal retainer who controls him and all court dealings, and a woman who seeks vengeance.
