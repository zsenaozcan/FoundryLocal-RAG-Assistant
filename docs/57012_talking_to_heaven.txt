Title: Talking to Heaven (2002)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction, Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Stephen Gyllenhaal
Main Cast: Ted Danson, Mary Steenburgen, Jack Palance, Diane Ladd, Michael Moriarty

Overview: A man begins to have disturbing visions of dead people, among whom are his mother and victims of a local serial killer. All telling their story of how they died, and how their murderer is still out there. The detective investigating the murders looks to him for help in solving the case.
