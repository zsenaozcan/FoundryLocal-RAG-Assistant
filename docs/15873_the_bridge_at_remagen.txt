Title: The Bridge at Remagen (1969)
Genres: Action, History, War
Director: John Guillermin
Main Cast: George Segal, Ben Gazzara, Robert Vaughn, Bradford Dillman, Peter van Eyck
Keywords: world war ii, bridge, soldier, explosion

Overview: In March of 1945, as the War in Europe is coming to a close, fighting erupts between German and American troops at the last remaining bridgehead across the Rhine.
