Title: North West Mounted Police (1940)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, History, Romance, Western
Director: Cecil B. DeMille
Main Cast: Gary Cooper, Madeleine Carroll, Paulette Goddard, Preston Foster, Robert Preston
Keywords: canada, texas, murder, rebellion

Overview: Texas Ranger Dusty Rivers ("Isn't that a contradiction in terms?", another character asks him) travels to Canada in the 1880s in search of Jacques Corbeau, who is wanted for murder. He wanders into the midst of the Riel Rebellion, in which Métis (people of French and Native heritage) and Natives want a separate nation. Dusty falls for nurse April Logan, who is also loved by Mountie Jim Brett. April's brother is involved with Courbeau's daughter Louvette, which leads to trouble during the battles between the rebels and the Mounties. Through it all Dusty is determined to bring Corbeau back to Texas (and April, too, if he can manage it.)
