Title: Spellbound (2011)
Genres: Horror, Romance, Comedy
Director: Hwang In-Ho
Main Cast: Son Ye-jin, Lee Min-ki, Park Cheol-min, Hwang Seung-Eon, Kim Hyun-Sook
Keywords: 오싹한 연애

Overview: A magician meets a weird girl and offers her to work together in his magic show. It's only until a year later that he starts to know her personally and develops a feeling towards her despite her own problems.
