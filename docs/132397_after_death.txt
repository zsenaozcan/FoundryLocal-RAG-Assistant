Title: After Death (1915)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yevgeni Bauer
Main Cast: Vitold Polonsky, Olga Rakhmanova, Georg Asagaroff, Vera Karalli, Marfa Kassatskaya
Keywords: suicide, unrequited love, haunting, spirit, guilt, silent film, ghost

Overview: Young scholar Andrei, fascinated by haunting actress Zoya Kadmina, is surprised when she sends him a note. The two have a brief scheduled meeting, then three months later Andrei is shocked to learn she has died. He becomes obsessed with her memory and decides he must find out all that he can about her.
