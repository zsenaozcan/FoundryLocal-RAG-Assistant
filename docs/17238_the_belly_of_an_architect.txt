Title: The Belly of an Architect (1987)
Genres: Drama
Director: Peter Greenaway
Main Cast: Brian Dennehy, Chloe Webb, Lambert Wilson, Sergio Fantoni, Stefania Casini
Keywords: architect, independent film, illness, grotesque, stomach

Overview: The American architect Kracklite arrives in Italy, supervising an exhibiton for a French architect, Boullée, famous for his oval structures. Tirelessly dedicated to the project, Kracklite's marriage quickly dissolves along with his health.
