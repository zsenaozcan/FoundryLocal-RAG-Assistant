Title: Captains of April (2000)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Maria de Medeiros
Main Cast: Stefano Accorsi, Maria de Medeiros, Joaquim de Almeida, Frédéric Pierrot, Fele Martínez
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Story of the 1974 coup that overthrew the right-wing Portuguese dictatorship--which continued the fascist policies of long-time dictator Antonio Salazar--and of two young army captains who were involved in it.
