Title: It's Hot In Paradise (1960)
Genres: Horror
Director: Fritz Böttger
Main Cast: Alexander D'Arcy, Barbara Valentin, Rainer Brandt, Harald Maresch, Elfie Wagner
Keywords: island, spider, stranded, cabin in the woods, creature feature

Overview: A plane carrying a troupe of beautiful dancers crashes on a deserted island inhabited by radioactive spiders whose bite turns you into a murderous monster.
