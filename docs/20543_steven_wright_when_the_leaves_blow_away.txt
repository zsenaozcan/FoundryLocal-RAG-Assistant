Title: Steven Wright: When the Leaves Blow Away (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michael Drumm
Main Cast: Steven Wright
Keywords: comedian, stand-up comedy, live comedy

Overview: Dry, wry and hilariously deadpan, Steven Wright unleashes a torrent of ironic and hysterical thought-provoking one-liners in the quirky stand-up style that has become his trademark. From Toronto's historic Elgin Theater, this Oscar-winning comedian keeps an adoring audience in stitches with random observations on everything from baby monitors as a form of wiretapping to his addiction to placebos.
