Title: Beep: A Documentary History of Game Sound (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Karen Collins
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: video game

Overview: A documentary of game sound from the Victorian arcades through to today, with a special focus on video game sound, but also including mechanical games and pinball. Beep is a feature film, but also a series of webisodes and DVD extras, a book, and a collection of resources found on the Beep website.
