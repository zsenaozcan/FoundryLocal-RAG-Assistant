Title: All the World's Memory (1956)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Alain Resnais
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: library

Overview: Toute la mémoire du monde is a documentary about the Bibliothèque Nationale in Paris. It presents the building, with its processes of cataloguing and preserving all sorts of printed material, as both a monument of cultural memory and as a monstrous, alien being.
