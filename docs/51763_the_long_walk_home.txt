Title: The Long Walk Home (1990)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Richard Pearce
Main Cast: Sissy Spacek, Whoopi Goldberg, Dwight Schultz, Ving Rhames
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Two women, black and white, in 1955 Montgomery Alabama, must decide what they are going to do in response to the famous bus boycott lead by Martin Luther King.
