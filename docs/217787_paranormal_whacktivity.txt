Title: Paranormal Whacktivity (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Roger Roth
Main Cast: Aneliese Roettger, Chanel Ryan, Melinda Y. Cohen, Stephanie Danielson, Brie Doffing
Keywords: sex, parody, couple, bump in the night

Overview: A sexy and hilarious spoof about a couple who have just moved into a new home to consummate their relationship, but find peril around every corner due to an evil presence that haunts them whenever they attempt to be intimate.
