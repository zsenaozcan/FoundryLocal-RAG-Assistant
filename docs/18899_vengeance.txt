Title: Vengeance (2009)
Genres: Thriller, Action
Director: Johnnie To
Main Cast: Johnny Hallyday, Anthony Wong, Gordon Lam Ka-Tung, Lam Suet, Simon Yam
Keywords: revenge, heroic bloodshed

Overview: A French chef swears revenge after a violent attack on his daughter's family in Hong Kong, during which her husband and her two children are murdered. To help him find the killers, he hires three local hit-men working for the mafia.
