Title: The Trials of Cate McCall (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Karen Moncrieff
Main Cast: Kate Beckinsale, Nick Nolte, James Cromwell, Mark Pellegrino, Clancy Brown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In order to be reinstated to the bar and recover custody of her daughter, a hotshot lawyer, now in recovery and on probation, must take on the appeal of a woman wrongfully convicted of murder.
