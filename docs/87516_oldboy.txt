Title: Oldboy (2013)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery, Action
Director: Spike Lee
Main Cast: Josh Brolin, Elizabeth Olsen, Samuel L. Jackson, Sharlto Copley, Lance Reddick
Keywords: imprisonment, remake of korean film

Overview: An everyday man has only three and a half days and limited resources to discover why he was imprisoned in a nondescript room for 20 years without any explanation.
