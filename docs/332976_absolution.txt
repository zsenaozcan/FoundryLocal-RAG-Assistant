Title: Absolution (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Adventure, Crime
Director: Keoni Waxman
Main Cast: Steven Seagal, Byron Mann, Josh Barnett, Adina Stetcu, Massimo Dobrovic

Overview: When a contract killer (Steven Seagal) encounters a girl on the run from a dangerous mob boss (Vinnie Jones) with powerful political ties, running a human trafficking operation, he is torn between protecting the girl, and remaining loyal to the government agency that hired him.
