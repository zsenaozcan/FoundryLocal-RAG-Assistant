Title: Atelier Fontana (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Riccardo Milani
Main Cast: Alessandra Mastronardi, Anna Valle, Federica De Cola

Overview: The drama recounts the life and career of unparalleled three sisters Fountain. Micol is just a dressmaker with 500 pounds in his pocket when she arrives in Rome with her sisters Giovanna and Zoe. The effort of the beginnings is compensated  by a passion for fashion that never fails, until the day when the whole world notices them.
