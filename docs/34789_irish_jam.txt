Title: Irish Jam (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Eddie Griffin, Christopher Dunne, Anna Friel, Dudley Sutton, Kevin McNally

Overview: Upon discovering that their town is up for sale, crafty Irish villagers scheme to raise the money to prevent the buy-out. They hold a poetry contest with a tempting grand prize -- the deed to their local pub. But what could happen when a duplicitous American rapper emerges as the best poet around?
