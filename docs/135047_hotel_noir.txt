Title: Hotel Noir (2012)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Sebastian Gutierrez
Main Cast: Carla Gugino, Danny DeVito, Malin Åkerman, Rufus Sewell, Robert Forster
Keywords: hotel, detective, killer, 1950s, hotel guests

Overview: Los Angeles, 1958: a detective holes up in a downtown hotel awaiting killers to come get him. During the course of one night he will meet various occupants of the hotel and the truth of how he came to be in his present situation will be revealed.
