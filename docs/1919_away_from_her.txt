Title: Away from Her (2006)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Sarah Polley
Main Cast: Julie Christie, Michael Murphy, Gordon Pinsent, Olympia Dukakis, Kristen Thomson
Keywords: nurse, canada, professor, love of one's life, forgiveness, alzheimer, marriage, past, independent film, nursing home

Overview: A man coping with the institutionalization of his wife because of Alzheimer's disease faces an epiphany when she transfers her affections to another man, a wheel chair-bound mute who also is a patient at the nursing home.
