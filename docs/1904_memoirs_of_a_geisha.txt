Title: Memoirs of a Geisha (2005)
Genres: Drama, History, Romance
Director: Rob Marshall
Main Cast: Zhang Ziyi, Gong Li, Youki Kudoh, Tsai Chin, Suzuka Ohgo
Keywords: japan, prostitute, sister sister relationship, brothel, world war ii, geisha

Overview: A sweeping romantic epic set in Japan in the years before World War II, a penniless Japanese child is torn from her family to work as a maid in a geisha house.
