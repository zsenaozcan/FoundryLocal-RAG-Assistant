Title: Android Cop (2014)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Mark Atkins
Main Cast: Michael Jai White, Charles S. Dutton, Kadeem Hardison, Randy Wayne, Larissa Vereza

Overview: In the year 2045, a Los Angeles Police Department detective and his new android partner enter the Zone, a forbidden section of the city plagued with an unknown disease. There, they discover the source of the illness and attempt to stop it using the android's advanced technology and weaponry.
