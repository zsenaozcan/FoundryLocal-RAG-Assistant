Title: Gravy (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: James Roday
Main Cast: Michael Weston, Jimmi Simpson, Sutton Foster, Lily Cole, Molly Ephraim
Keywords: hostage, halloween, cannibal, cantina

Overview: It's All Hallow's Eve. A trio of costumed misfits with very special dietary requirements seizes a Mexican cantina and force the staff to engage in a late night of gaming, food and libations. The only caveat is what's on the menu.
