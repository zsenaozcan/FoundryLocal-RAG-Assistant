Title: Sharktopus vs Pteracuda (2014)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, TV Movie
Director: Kevin O'Neill
Main Cast: Robert Carradine, Tony Evangelista, Rib Hillis, Conan O'Brien, Hensy Pichardo
Keywords: monster, octopus, giant monster, decapitation, scientist, shark, sea monster, tentacles

Overview: SHARKTOPUS VS PTERACUDA finds an American scientist up to no good (as usual) by creating the half-pterodactyl, half-barracuda: Pteracuda. When the creature inevitably escapes, it's up to Sharktopus to stop him.
