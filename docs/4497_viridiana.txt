Title: Viridiana (1961)
Genres: Drama
Director: Luis Buñuel
Main Cast: Silvia Pinal, Francisco Rabal, Fernando Rey, José Calvo, Margarita Lozano
Keywords: spain, rape, nun, faith, wine, devoutness, orphan, guilt, mother superior, convent
