Title: Ben-Hur (2016)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action
Director: Timur Bekmambetov
Main Cast: Jack Huston, Toby Kebbell, Rodrigo Santoro, Nazanin Boniadi, Ayelet Zurer
Keywords: ancient rome, betrayal, vengeance
