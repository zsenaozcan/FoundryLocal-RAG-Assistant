Title: The Ghosts in Our Machine (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Liz Marshall
Main Cast: Jo-Anne McArthur, Marcel Sabat, Lori Reese, Perrie Wardell, Martin Rowe

Overview: Through the heart and photographic lens of international photographer Jo-Anne McArthur,  we become intimately familiar with a cast of non-human animals. The film follows Jo-Anne over the course of a year as she photographs several animal stories in parts of Canada,  the U.S. and in Europe. Each story is a window into global animal industries:  Food, Fashion, Entertainment and Research.
