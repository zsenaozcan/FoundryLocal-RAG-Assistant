Title: Ghost in the Shell Arise - Border 5: Pyrophoric Cult (2015)
Genres: Action, Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Kazuchika Kise
Main Cast: Maaya Sakamoto, Ken'ichirô Matsuda, Shunsuke Sakuya
Keywords: cyberpunk

Overview: Motoko and her squad are finally getting closer to Fire-Starter's broker, but things get tense when they're instructed to take a backseat role on a sting op.  Part five of the OVA series, containing the materials developed for Alternative Architecture's final two episodes.
