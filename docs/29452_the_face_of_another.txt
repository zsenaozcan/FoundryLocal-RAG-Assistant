Title: The Face of Another (1966)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Hiroshi Teshigahara
Main Cast: Tatsuya Nakadai, Machiko Kyô, Mikijiro Hira, Kyoko Kishida, Eiji Okada
Keywords: japanese new wave

Overview: A businessman with a disfigured face obtains a lifelike mask from his doctor, but the mask starts altering his personality.
