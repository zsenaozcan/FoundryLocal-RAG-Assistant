Title: Batman & Mr. Freeze: SubZero (1998)
Genres: Animation, Action, Crime
Director: Boyd Kirkland
Main Cast: Kevin Conroy, Michael Ansara, Loren Lester, Efrem Zimbalist, Jr., George Dzundza
Keywords: super powers, mr. freeze, batman

Overview: When a desperate Mr. Freeze, to save his dying wife, kidnaps Barbara (Batgirl) Gordon as an involuntary organ donor, Batman and Robin must find her before the operation can begin.
