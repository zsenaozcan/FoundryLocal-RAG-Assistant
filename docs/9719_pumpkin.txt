Title: Pumpkin (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Anthony Abrams
Main Cast: Christina Ricci, Hank Harris, Brenda Blethyn, Dominique Swain, Marisa Coughlan
Keywords: independent film, disabled, sorority girl, filipino american, poetry workshop, community college, stepford wife, relay race, physically handicapped, s.a.t.

Overview: When perfect, perky Carolyn (Christina Ricci) and her Alpha Omega Pi sisters decide to coach mentally challenged athletes for the regional Challenged Games, Carolyn's assigned to coach Pumpkin (Hank Harris), a young disabled man who touches her heart. She's terrified at first, but to the horror of her friends -- and Pumpkin's overprotective mother (Brenda Blethyn) -- Carolyn starts to falls in love.
