Title: Blood Games (1990)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Tanya Rosenberg
Main Cast: Gregory Scott Cummins, Laura Albert, Shelley Abblett, Ken Carpenter, Ross Hagen
Keywords: rape, exploitation, woods, redneck, survival, rape attempt, rape and murder, softball, survival horror, rape and revenge

Overview: A team of softball players get lost in the woods after their bus breaks down. They get attacked, beaten, raped and murdered by some psychotic men. The women fight back with baseball bats and bows and arrows.
