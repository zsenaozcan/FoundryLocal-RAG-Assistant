Title: Rewind (2013)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Jack Bender
Main Cast: Jeff Fahey, David Cronenberg, Keisha Castle-Hughes, Jennifer Ferrin, Shane McRae
Keywords: time travel

Overview: Revolves around a team of military field operatives and civilian scientists who must use untested technology to travel back in time to alter past events in order to change the future and avoid a devastating terrorist attack. Feature length pilot for failed tv series, released as a made for TV movie.
