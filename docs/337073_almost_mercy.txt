Title: Almost Mercy (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: Tom DeNucci
Main Cast: Kane Hodder, Bill Moseley, Danielle Guldin, Jesse Dufault, Eva Senerchia

Overview: Jackson and Emily aren't like the other kids. Two burgeoning sociopaths on the brink of total meltdown. Ticking time bombs seeking revenge. Who will unravel first?
