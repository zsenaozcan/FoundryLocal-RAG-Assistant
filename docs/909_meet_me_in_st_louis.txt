Title: Meet Me in St. Louis (1944)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Vincente Minnelli
Main Cast: Judy Garland, Margaret O'Brien, Mary Astor, Lucille Bremer, Leon Ames
Keywords: musical, moving, boy next door, americana, togetherness, fiddle, chapter headings, straw hat, st. louis missouri, 20th century

Overview: In the year before the 1904 St Louis World's Fair, the four Smith daughters learn lessons of life and love, even as they prepare for a reluctant move to New York.
