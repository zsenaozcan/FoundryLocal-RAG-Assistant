Title: Summer in the City (2016)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Vic Sarin
Main Cast: Julianna Guill, Marc Bendavid, Vivica A. Fox, Marla Sokoloff, Natasha Henstridge
Keywords: fashion, manhattan, new york city, clothing store

Overview: When a small town woman with southern charm is given a big promotion managing a store in the Big Apple, she tries to adopt a big city personality and it leads to disastrous results.
