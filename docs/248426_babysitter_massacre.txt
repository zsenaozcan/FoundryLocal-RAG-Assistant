Title: Babysitter Massacre (2013)
Genres: Horror
Director: Henrique Couto
Main Cast: Erin R. Ryan, Marylee Osborne, Joni Durian, Tara Clark, Odette Despairr
Keywords: halloween, revenge, slasher, blonde woman

Overview: A group of young women teamed together in their youth to run a babysitting business, when one of their friends died suddenly the group fell apart. Now years later they are all going their separate ways as college approaches, but on Halloween night someone is torturing and killing every member of the babysitter club...
