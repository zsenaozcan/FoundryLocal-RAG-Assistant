Title: Spa Night (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Andrew Ahn
Main Cast: Joe Seo, Haerry Kim, Janice Pak, Youn Ho Cho, Tae Song
Keywords: korea, spa

Overview: A young Korean-American man works to reconcile his obligations to his struggling immigrant family with his burgeoning sexual desires in the underground world of gay hookups at Korean spas in Los Angeles.
