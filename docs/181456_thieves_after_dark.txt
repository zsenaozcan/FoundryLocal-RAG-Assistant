Title: Thieves After Dark (1984)
Genres: Thriller, Romance, Crime, Drama
Director: Samuel Fuller
Main Cast: Véronique Jannot, Bobby Di Cicco, Victor Lanoux, Stéphane Audran, Camille de Casabianca

Overview: A crime story set in Paris about a Bonnie-and-Clyde couple -- how they got together and how they are pursued for a murder they never committed.
