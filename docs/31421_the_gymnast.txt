Title: The Gymnast (2006)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Ned Farr
Main Cast: Dreya Weber, Addie Yungmee, John Lee Ames, Andrew Ableson, Tony Horton
Keywords: dance contest, independent film, gay man, lesbian interest

Overview: The stunning Dreya Weber stars as a former top gymnast who discovers love and a new life path when she teams up with a dancer (played by former L.A. Lakers cheerleader Addie Yungmee) for an ambitious Las Vegas aerial act show.
