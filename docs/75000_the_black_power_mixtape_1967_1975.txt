Title: The Black Power Mixtape 1967-1975 (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Göran Olsson
Main Cast: Abiodun Oyewole, Talib Kweli, Angela Davis, Harry Belafonte, Stokely Carmichael
Keywords: black power

Overview: Examines the evolution of the Black Power Movement in American society from 1967 to 1975. It features footage of the movement shot by Swedish journalists in America during that period and includes the appearances of Bobby Seale, Huey P. Newton, Eldridge Cleaver, and other activists, artists, and leaders central to the movement.
