Title: Barbie of Swan Lake (2003)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Owen Hurley
Main Cast: Kelly Sheridan, Mark Hildreth, Kelsey Grammer, Maggie Wheeler, Venus Terzo
Keywords: musical, barbie, children, animation, based on toy, ballet, animal, unicorn, swan lake

Overview: Barbie comes to life in her third animated movie, based on the beloved fairy tale and set to the brilliant music of Tchaikovsky. Barbie as Odette, the young daughter of a baker, follows a unicorn into the Enchanted Forest and is transformed into a swan by an evil wizard intent on defeating the Fairy Queen.
