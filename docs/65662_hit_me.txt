Title: Hit Me (1996)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Steven Shainberg
Main Cast: Elias Koteas, Laure Marsac, Jay Leggett, Bruce Ramsay, Kevin J. O'Connor
Keywords: suspense

Overview: Sonny lives with his retarded older brother, and works as a bellhop at a second-rate hotel. This changes when Monique a beautiful, suicidal nut-case checks in.
