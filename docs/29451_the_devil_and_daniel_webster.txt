Title: The Devil and Daniel Webster (1941)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Fantasy
Director: William Dieterle
Main Cast: Edward Arnold, Walter Huston, Jane Darwell, Simone Simon, Gene Lockhart
Keywords: pact with the devil, devil, farmer

Overview: A down-on-his-luck farmer makes a deal with the devil for seven years of prosperity. When Mr. Scratch comes to collect, orator and hero of the common man Daniel Webster comes to the rescue.
