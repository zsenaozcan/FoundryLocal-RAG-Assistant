Title: Valley Girl (1983)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Martha Coolidge
Main Cast: Nicolas Cage, E.G. Daily, Deborah Foreman, Michael Bowen, Cameron Dye
Keywords: alcohol, sex, nudity, punk, music, party, love, friends, romance, teenager

Overview: Julie, a girl from the valley, meets Randy, a punk from the city. They are from different worlds and find love. Somehow they need to stay together in spite of her trendy, shallow friends.
