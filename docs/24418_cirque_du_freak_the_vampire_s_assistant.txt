Title: Cirque du Freak: The Vampire's Assistant (2009)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Action, Thriller
Director: Paul Weitz
Main Cast: John C. Reilly, Josh Hutcherson, Chris Massoglia, Patrick Fugit, Salma Hayek
Keywords: vampire, spider, wolfman, stew, best friend, antidote, based on young adult novel

Overview: Darren Shan is a regular teenage kid. He and his friend Steve find out about a Freak Show coming to town and work hard at trying to find tickets. They do, and together they go to "Cirque du Freak" where they see many strange acts including a wolf-man and a bearded lady
