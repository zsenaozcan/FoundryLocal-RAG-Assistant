Title: Eros + Massacre (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yoshishige Yoshida
Main Cast: Mariko Okada, Toshiyuki Hosokawa, Yûko Kusunoki, Kazuko Ineno, Etsushi Takahashi
Keywords: japan, philosophy, sex, fire, obsession, camera, revolution, nudity, earthquake, society

Overview: Two interwoven stories. The first is a biography of anarchist Sakae Osugi which follows his relationship with three women in the 1920s. The second centers around two 1960s students researching Osugi's theories.
