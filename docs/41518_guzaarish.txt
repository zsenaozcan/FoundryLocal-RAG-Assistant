Title: Guzaarish (2010)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Sanjay Leela Bhansali
Main Cast: Hrithik Roshan, Aishwarya Rai Bachchan, Shernaz Patel, Aditya Roy Kapoor
Keywords: bodily disabled person, love, romance

Overview: Ethan, a magician rendered paralyzed in an accident, sets off a furor when he makes a petition to the court to end his own life.
