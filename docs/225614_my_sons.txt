Title: My Sons (1991)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Yôji Yamada
Main Cast: Masatoshi Nagase, Rentarô Mikuni, Emi Wakui, Mieko Harada, Ryûzô Tanaka

Overview: Tetsuo is a young man living in Tokyo, who falls in love with a deaf-mute factory girl. He has always felt jealous of his college- educated brother, but ultimately wins both the girl and his father's acceptance and support in a touching and refreshing way.
