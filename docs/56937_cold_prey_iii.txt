Title: Cold Prey III (2010)
Genres: Horror
Director: Mikkel Brænne Sandemose
Main Cast: Ida Marie Bakkerud, Kim S. Falck-Jørgensen, Pål Stokka, Julie Rusti, Arthur Berning
Keywords: norway, young boy, teenager, cruelty, killer

Overview: Takes place in the '80s where a group of teenagers go to visit an abandoned hotel, only to find themselves hunted by a psychotic killer through the Norwegian woods.
