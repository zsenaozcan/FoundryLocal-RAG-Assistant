Title: Island of Fire (1990)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Action, Thriller
Director: Chu Yen-ping
Main Cast: Andy Lau, Jackie Chan, Sammo Hung, Tony Leung Ka-Fai, Jimmy Wang Yu

Overview: Someone in a prison run by a corrupt warden fakes the deaths of convicts to later use them as expendable assassins. A police officer is sent into the prison to gather evidence of the corruption.
