Title: The King's Whore (1990)
Genres: Romance, History, Drama
Director: Axel Corti
Main Cast: Timothy Dalton, Valeria Golino, Stéphane Freiss, Robin Renucci, Margaret Tyzack

Overview: Set in the 17th-century, an Italian nobleman weds an impoverished countess, who is wooed by the King of Piedmont and faces pressure from his entire court to succumb to his wishes.
