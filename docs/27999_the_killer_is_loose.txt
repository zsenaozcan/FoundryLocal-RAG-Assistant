Title: The Killer Is Loose (1956)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Budd Boetticher
Main Cast: Joseph Cotten, Rhonda Fleming, Wendell Corey, Alan Hale Jr., Michael Pate
Keywords: prison, detective, revenge, escape, trial, bank robbery attempt

Overview: A savings-and-loan bank is robbed; later, a police wiretap identifies teller Leon Poole as inside man. In capturing him, detective Sam Wagner accidentally kills Poole's young wife, and at his trial Poole swears vengeance against Wagner. Poole begins his plans to get revenge when he escapes his captors.
