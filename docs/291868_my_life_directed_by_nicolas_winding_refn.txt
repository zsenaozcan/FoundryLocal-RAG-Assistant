Title: My Life Directed by Nicolas Winding Refn (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Liv Corfixen
Main Cast: Nicolas Winding Refn, Liv Corfixen, Ryan Gosling, Alejandro Jodorowsky, Kristin Scott Thomas
Keywords: bangkok, marriage, parenthood, filmmaker, filmmaking, woman director

Overview: A documentary directed by Winding Refn's wife, Liv Corfixen, and it follows the Danish-born filmmaker during the making of his 2013 film Only God Forgives.
