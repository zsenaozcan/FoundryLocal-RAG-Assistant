Title: Ritual in Transfigured Time (1946)
Genres: Mystery
Director: Maya Deren
Main Cast: Rita Christiani, Maya Deren, Anaïs Nin, Frank Westbrook, Gore Vidal
Keywords: dance, woman director, short

Overview: A social event choreographed in the manner of a dance, illuminated by concepts drawn from Greek legend; one of filmmaker Maya Deren’s most intriguing works.
