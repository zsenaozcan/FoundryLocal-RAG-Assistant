Title: Consolation Marriage (1931)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Paul Sloane
Main Cast: Irene Dunne, Pat O’Brien, John Halliday, Myrna Loy, Lester Vail
Keywords: marriage of convenience, melodrama

Overview: A sportswriter (Pat O'Brien) jilted by his globe-trotting girlfriend (Myrna Loy) marries a woman (Irene Dunne) jilted by her boyfriend.
