Title: Eu Não Faço a Menor Ideia do que eu Tô Fazendo Com a Minha Vida (2013)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Matheus Souza
Main Cast: Clarice Falcão, Rodrigo Pandolfo, Leandro Hassum, Daniel Filho, Nelson Freitas

Overview: Clara doesn't know what she wants to do with her life, until one day she meets Guilherme who helps her to find her own path.
