Title: Terror in a Texas Town (1958)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Joseph H. Lewis
Main Cast: Sterling Hayden, Sebastian Cabot, Carol Kelly, Eugene Mazzola, Nedrick Young
Keywords: b movie, moral courage, whaler

Overview: Armed with a harpoon, a Swedish whaler is out for revenge after the death of his father. A greedy oil man trying to buy up the Swede's land might be the guilty party.
