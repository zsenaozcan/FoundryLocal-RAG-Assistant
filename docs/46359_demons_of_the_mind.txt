Title: Demons of the Mind (1972)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Peter Sykes
Main Cast: Robert Hardy, Shane Briant, Gillian Hills, Yvonne Mitchell, Paul Jones
Keywords: secret, castle, murder, violence, gothic, incest

Overview: A physician discovers that two children are being kept virtually imprisoned in their house by their father. He investigates, and discovers a web of sex, incest and satanic possession.
