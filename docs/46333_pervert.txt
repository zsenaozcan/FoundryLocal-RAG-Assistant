Title: Pervert! (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Jonathan Yudis
Main Cast: Mary Carey, Sean Andrews, Darell Sandeen, Juliette Clarke, Sally Jean
Keywords: erotic movie

Overview: While visiting his Bible-thumping yet lecherous father in the desert, randy college student James (Sean Andrews) tries everything he can to hook up with the local curvaceous cuties, but his sex quest stalls when a bloodthirsty killer targets every buxom beauty in sight. Porn star and onetime California gubernatorial candidate Mary Carey stars in this lusty nod to 1960s exploitation films.
