Title: The Third Wheel (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Jordan Brady
Main Cast: Luke Wilson, Denise Richards, Jay Lacopo, Meredith Salenger, Ben Affleck

Overview: The clumsy and shy Stanley is a nice fellow, beloved by his colleagues in the office. He has a crush on the gorgeous new-hire Diana, but he has difficulties to get close to her. When Stanley finally invites Diana for a date, he hits the homeless Phil with his Taurus, and his planned night turns upside down.
