Title: Magnum Cop (1978)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Stelvio Massi
Main Cast: Maurizio Merli, Joan Collins, Franco Ressel, Werner Pochath, Annarita Grapputo
Keywords: blackmail, cyanide, vehicular homicide, poliziotteschi

Overview: A low-rent private investigator and former cop is sent to Austria on a job, and while there sees a number of seemingly unrelated coincidences that lead him into a strange web of corruption and decadence.
