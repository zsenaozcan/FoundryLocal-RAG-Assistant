Title: Gold (1974)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Thriller
Director: Peter R. Hunt
Main Cast: Roger Moore, Susannah York, Ray Milland, John Gielgud, Bradford Dillman
Keywords: aviation, gold mine

Overview: Rod Slater is the newly appointed general manager of the Sonderditch gold mine, but he stumbles across an ingenious plot to flood the mine, by drilling into an underground lake, so the unscrupulous owners to make a killing in the international gold market.
