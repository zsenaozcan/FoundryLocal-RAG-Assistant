Title: The Flaying (2012)
Genres: Horror, Fantasy, Thriller
Director: Nicolás Amelio-Ortiz
Main Cast: Olivia Tykocki, Juan Sebastian Alvarez Windey, Celeste Subatin, Emilio Martin

Overview: Four teenage friends go on a road trip through Argentina. On their way, they find a castle left abandoned in the middle of a forest. They decide to investigate further, only to discover it's gruesome secrets and former residents.
