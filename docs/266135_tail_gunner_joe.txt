Title: Tail Gunner Joe (1977)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Jud Taylor
Main Cast: Peter Boyle, John Forsythe, Burgess Meredith, Patricia Neal, Jean Stapleton
Keywords: communist, red scare

Overview: A dramatization of the life of Sen. Joseph McCarthy, the alcoholic senator from Wisconsin whose tactics of accusing prominent people of Communist sympathies were initially designed to give him a national power base when he later planned to run for President.
