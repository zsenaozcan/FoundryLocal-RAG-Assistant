Title: My Grandfather's People (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family, History
Director: Çağan Irmak
Main Cast: Çetin Tekindor, Hümeyra, Zafer Algöz, Yiğit Özşener, Gökçe Bahadır

Overview: A feature film that tells the story of the director's grandfather who was forced to leave Crete in the 1920s during the Greek-Turkish population exchange.
