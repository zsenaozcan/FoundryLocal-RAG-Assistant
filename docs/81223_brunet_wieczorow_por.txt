Title: Brunet wieczorową porą (1976)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Stanisław Bareja
Main Cast: Krzysztof Kowalewski, Wojciech Pokora, Janina Traczykówna, Bohdan Łazuka, Wieslaw Golas

Overview: Modest editor, has shipped his wife and kids for the weekend, and is trying to relax in his house at the outskirts of Warsaw. His quiet evening is only disturbed by the accidental forecast made by a Gypsy woman, that at evening time he will murder a mysterious brunet.
