Title: Living 'til the End (2005)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Amanda Goodwin
Main Cast: Sean Maher, Jaime Ray Newman, Janet Carroll, Stephen Tobolowsky, John Getz
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Told by a psychic of his imminent death on his next birthday, Jack lives out the year, as a hopeless agoraphobic, until he meets a young woman who slowly coaxes him out of his self imposed bondage.
