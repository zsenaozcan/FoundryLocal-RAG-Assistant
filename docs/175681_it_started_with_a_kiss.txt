Title: It Started With A Kiss (1959)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: George Marshall
Main Cast: Glenn Ford, Debbie Reynolds, Fred Clark, Eva Gabor, Gustavo Rojo
Keywords: newlywed

Overview: While on leave in New York, a serviceman both weds a chorus girl and wins a red convertible in a charity raffle. Both his wife and the car turn out to be problematic. Comedy.
