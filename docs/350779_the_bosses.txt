Title: The Bosses (2015)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Chiva Rodriguez
Main Cast: Roman Leonardo Rodriguez, Eduardo Dávalos de Luna, César Renato Suárez Morales, Fernando Sosa

Overview: Poncho is a student at a prestigious college in northern Mexico who is immersed along a single day on a whirlwind adventure through the various levels of the narc world after accepting the help of "Greñas", a guy working in the parking lot of his college, who will explain him how to buy marijuana on the campus for the first time.
