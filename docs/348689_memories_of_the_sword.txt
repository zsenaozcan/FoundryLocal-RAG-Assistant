Title: Memories of the Sword (2015)
Genres: History, Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: Park Heung-sik
Main Cast: Lee Byung-hun, Jeon Do-yeon, Kim Go-eun, Lee Jun-ho, Lee Kyoung-young
Keywords: sword fight

Overview: While in medieval Korea, a young girl sets out to revenge the betrayal and the death of her mother. But therefore she must face one of the most powerful men and warriors of the Goryo Dynasty.
