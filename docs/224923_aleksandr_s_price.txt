Title: Aleksandr's Price (2013)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Pau Masó
Main Cast: Pau Masó, Anatoli Grek, Josh Berresford, Samantha Glovin, Keith Dougherty
Keywords: russian, gay man, escort

Overview: The life of an illegal Russian boy who, after losing his family, is pushed into becoming an escort - ultimately trying to come to terms with who he thinks he is.
