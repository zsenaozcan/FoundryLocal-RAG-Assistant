Title: Doctor at Sea (1955)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ralph Thomas
Main Cast: Dirk Bogarde, James Robertson Justice, Brigitte Bardot, Brenda De Banzie, Maurice Denham

Overview: The second of the seven "Doctor" films, based on Richard Gordon's novels and released between 1954 and 1970. A bachelor doctor goes to sea to escape the boredom of shore practice, but studies the nurses more than medicine, and Brigitte Bardot is around.
