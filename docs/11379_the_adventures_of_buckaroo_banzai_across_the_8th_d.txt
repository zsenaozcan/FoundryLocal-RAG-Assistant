Title: The Adventures of Buckaroo Banzai Across the 8th Dimension (1984)
Genres: Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: W.D. Richter
Main Cast: Peter Weller, John Lithgow, Ellen Barkin, Jeff Goldblum, Christopher Lloyd
Keywords: menace, talent, intellectually gifted, space war, time travel, creativity, genius

Overview: Adventurer/surgeon/rock musician Buckaroo Banzai and his band of men, the Hong Kong Cavaliers, take on evil alien invaders from the 8th dimension.
