Title: Love on the Sidelines (2016)
Genres: TV Movie, Romance, Comedy
Director: Terry Ingram
Main Cast: Emily Kinney, John Reardon, Joe Theismann, Luisa D'Oliveira, Elise Gatien
Keywords: american football, fashion designer

Overview: A down-on-her-luck young woman who falls into a job as a personal assistant to a star quarterback sidelined with an injury. He's never had a female assistant before and she knows nothing about football.
