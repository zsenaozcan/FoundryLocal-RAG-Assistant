Title: Assault on a Queen (1966)
Genres: Adventure, Crime, Thriller
Director: Jack Donohue
Main Cast: Frank Sinatra, Virna Lisi, Anthony Franciosa, Richard Conte, Alf Kjellin
Keywords: submarine, ocean liner, torpedo, u boat

Overview: A group of adventurers refloat a WWII German submarine and prepare to use it to pull a very large heist; The Queen Mary which they plan to rob on the high seas.
