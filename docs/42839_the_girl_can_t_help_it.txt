Title: The Girl Can't Help It (1956)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Frank Tashlin
Main Cast: Jayne Mansfield, Tom Ewell, Edmond O'Brien, Julie London, Ray Anthony
Keywords: blonde

Overview: A down-and-out gangster hires an alcoholic press agent to make his blonde bombshell girlfriend a recording star in 6 weeks. But what is he going to do when he finds out that she has no talent? And what is going to happen when the two fall in love?
