Title: Rangeela (1995)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Ram Gopal Varma
Main Cast: Aamir Khan, Jackie Shroff, Urmila Matondkar, Gulshan Grover, Rajeev Mehta
Keywords: bollywood, actor

Overview: A poor young woman, who dreams of Bollywood fame, is caught in a love triangle between her childhood friend and a famous actor.
