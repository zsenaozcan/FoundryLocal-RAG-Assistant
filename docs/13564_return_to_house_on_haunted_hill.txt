Title: Return to House on Haunted Hill (2007)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Víctor García
Main Cast: Amanda Righetti, Cerina Vincent, Erik Palladino, Tom Riley, Jeffrey Combs
Keywords: haunted house, horror, evil

Overview: Eight years have passed since Sara Wolfe and Eddie Baker escaped the House on Haunted Hill. Now the kidnapped Ariel, Sara's sister, goes inside the house with a group of treasure hunters to find the statue of Baphomet, worth millions and believed to be the cause of the House's evil.
