Title: A Man with Warranty (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Artyom Aksyonenko
Main Cast: Aleksandr Oleshko, Nonna Grishaeva, Andrey Fedortsov, Pyotr Fyodorov, Dmitriy Nagiev

Overview: A big mall security guard becomes friend with the attractive woman. The only problem: he doesn't know yet that she is a mall owner.
