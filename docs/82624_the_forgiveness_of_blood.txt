Title: The Forgiveness of Blood (2011)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Joshua Marston
Main Cast: Refet Abazi, Tristan Halilaj, Sindi Lacej, Ilire Vinca Celaj, Zana Hasaj
Keywords: seclusion, vendetta, blood feud

Overview: After his father and uncle kill a neighbor, an Albanian teen (Tristan Halilaj) finds that his own life may be forfeited in a legal blood feud.
