Title: Orson Welles: The One-Man Band (1995)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Vassili Silovic
Main Cast: Oja Kodar, Orson Welles, Ingrid Bergman, Tim Brooke-Taylor, Michael Bryant
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Orson Welles' archives of unfinished/never released movies and the last years of his life from the perspective of Oja Kodar (life and artistic partner of Orson Welles in his last years).
