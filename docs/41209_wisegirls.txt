Title: WiseGirls (2002)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Thriller
Director: David Anspaugh
Main Cast: Mira Sorvino, Mariah Carey, Melora Walters, Arthur J. Nascarella, Saul Stein
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A new waitress working at an Italian restaurant in New York City finds herself entangled in a mob-run underworld of drug dealing and murder.
