Title: October Gale (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Romance
Director: Ruba Nadda
Main Cast: Patricia Clarkson, Scott Speedman, Tim Roth, Callum Keith Rennie
Keywords: gunshot wound, cottage, doctor, woman director

Overview: A doctor takes in a mysterious man who washes ashore at her remote cottage with a gunshot wound. Quickly they both learn the killer has arrived to finish the job, while a storm has cut them off from the mainland.
