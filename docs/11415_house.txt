Title: House (1986)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Steve Miner
Main Cast: William Katt, George Wendt, Richard Moll, Kay Lenz, Mary Stavin
Keywords: vietnam war, haunted house, author

Overview: Roger Cobb is a author who has just separated from his wife. He moves into a new house and tries to work on a novel based on his experiences in the Vietnam War. Strange things start happening around him; little things at first, but as they become more frequent, Cobb becomes aware that the house resents his presence.
