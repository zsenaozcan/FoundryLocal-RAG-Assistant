Title: The Enchanted Cottage (1945)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: John Cromwell
Main Cast: Dorothy McGuire, Robert Young, Herbert Marshall, Mildred Natwick, Spring Byington
Keywords: maid

Overview: A homely maid and a scarred ex-GI meet at the cottage where she works and where he was to spend his honeymoon prior to his accident. The two develop a bond and agree to marry, more out of loneliness than love. The romantic spirit of the cottage, however, overtakes them. They soon begin to look beautiful to each other, but no one else.
