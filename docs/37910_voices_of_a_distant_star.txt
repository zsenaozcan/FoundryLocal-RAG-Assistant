Title: Voices of a Distant Star (2002)
Genres: Animation, Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Makoto Shinkai
Main Cast: Mika Shinohara, Makoto Shinkai, Sumi Mutoh, Chihiro Suzuki, Donna Burke
Keywords: love, based on manga, text message, long distance relationship, true love

Overview: Voices of a Distant Star chronicles a long-distance relationship between two close friends who communicate by sending emails via their mobile phones across interstellar space.
