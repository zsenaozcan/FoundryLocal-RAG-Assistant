Title: The Grey (2012)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Joe Carnahan
Main Cast: Liam Neeson, Dermot Mulroney, Frank Grillo, Dallas Roberts, Joe Anderson
Keywords: alcohol, isolation, wolf, wilderness, forest, stranded, alaska, survival, airplane crash, fear

Overview: An oil drilling team struggles to survive after a plane crash strands them in the wilds of Alaska. Hunting them is a pack of wolves that sees them as intruders.
