Title: Stolen (2009)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Anders Anderson
Main Cast: Jon Hamm, Josh Lucas, Rhona Mitra, James Van Der Beek, Jessica Chastain
Keywords: loss of child, grieving widower

Overview: A detective becomes obsessed with solving a child's 50-year-old murder, uncovering striking similarities between the case and his son's disappearance.
