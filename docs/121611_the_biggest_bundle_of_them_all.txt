Title: The Biggest Bundle of Them All (1968)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Ken Annakin
Main Cast: Vittorio De Sica, Raquel Welch, Robert Wagner, Godfrey Cambridge, Davy Kaye
Keywords: kidnapping, farce, mafia, train robbery

Overview: A kidnapped mobster (Vittorio De Sica) persuades his captors to help him rob platinum ingots from a train.
