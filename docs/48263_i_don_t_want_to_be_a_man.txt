Title: I Don't Want to Be a Man (1918)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Ernst Lubitsch
Main Cast: Ossi Oswalda, Curt Goetz, Ferry Sikla, Margarete Kupfer, Victor Janson
Keywords: gender, lgbt

Overview: A teenage tomboy, tired of being bossed around by her strict guardian, impersonates a man so she can have more fun. She quickly discovers that being the opposite sex isn’t as easy as she had hoped for. What ensues is a gender-bending comedy decades ahead of its time.
