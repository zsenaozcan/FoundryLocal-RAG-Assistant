Title: Chronicles of the Ghostly Tribe (2015)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Thriller
Director: Lu Chuan
Main Cast: Yao Chen, Mark Chao, Rhydian Vaughan, Li Chen, Tiffany Tang
Keywords: based on novel

Overview: In 1979, a young soldier is working in China's snowcapped mountains when an explosion reveals bizarre fossils hidden deep in the mountain caverns. What they discover next will change his life and human history forever.
