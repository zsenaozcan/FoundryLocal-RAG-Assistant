Title: Hothead (1979)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jean-Jacques Annaud
Main Cast: Patrick Dewaere, France Dougnac, Dorothée Jemma, Maurice Barrier, Jean Bouise
Keywords: prison, prisoner, soccer field, soccer

Overview: Delightful French comedy about a talented soccer player whose foul temper causes him to be cut from the team, lose his job and even be banned from his favorite bar.
