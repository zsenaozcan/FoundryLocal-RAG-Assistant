Title: Female (2005)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Comedy
Director: Shinya Tsukamoto
Main Cast: Nene Otsuka, Naoyuki Morita , Nomura Eri, Hiroyuki Ikeuchi, Chihiro Otsuka
Keywords: woman director

Overview: 'Female' is comprised of five short films adapted from five novels by female authors. Directed by Shinya Tsukamoto, Tetsuo Shinohara, Ryuichi Hiroki, Miwa Nishikawa, and Suzuki Matsuo.
