Title: Killer Klowns from Outer Space (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Stephen Chiodo
Main Cast: Grant Cramer, Suzanne Snyder, John Allen Nelson, John Vernon, Royal Dano
Keywords: clown, circus tent, ice cream truck, lovers lane, shadow puppet, net, drinking straw, pie in the face, evil clown
