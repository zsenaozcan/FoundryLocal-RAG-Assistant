Title: Strait-Jacket (1964)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: William Castle
Main Cast: Joan Crawford, Diane Baker, Leif Erickson, Howard St. John, John Anthony Hayes
Keywords: asylum, ax, mother daughter relationship, axe murder, proto-slasher

Overview: After a 20 year stay at an asylum for a double murder, a mother returns to her estranged daughter where suspicions arise about her behaviour.
