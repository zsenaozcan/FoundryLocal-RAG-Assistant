Title: Diamond Men (2000)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Dan Cohen
Main Cast: Robert Forster, Donnie Wahlberg, Bess Armstrong, Jasmine Guy, KaDee Strickland

Overview: After 30 years on the road, a veteran jewellery salesman is forced to show his young replacement the tricks of the trade. But when the kid introduces him to the ladies of the "Altoona Riding Club," the old dog is introduced to a whole new set of "tricks."
