Title: Children Shouldn't Play with Dead Things (1973)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Bob Clark
Main Cast: Alan Ormsby, Valerie Mamches, Jeff Gillen, Anya Ormsby, Paul Cronin
Keywords: island, zombie, cult film, living dead, graveyard

Overview: Six actors go to graveyard on a remote island to act out a necromantic ritual. The ritual works, and soon the dead are walking about and chowing down on human flesh.
