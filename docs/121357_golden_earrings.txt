Title: Golden Earrings (1947)
Genres: Adventure, Romance
Director: Mitchell Leisen
Main Cast: Ray Milland, Marlene Dietrich, Murvyn Vye, Bruce Lester, Dennis Hoey
Keywords: forest, black and white

Overview: A British colonel (Ray Milland) escapes from the Gestapo to the Black Forest and poses as a Gypsy's (Marlene Dietrich) mate.
