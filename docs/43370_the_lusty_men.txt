Title: The Lusty Men (1952)
Genres: Action, Drama, Western
Director: Nicholas Ray
Main Cast: Susan Hayward, Robert Mitchum, Arthur Kennedy, Arthur Hunnicutt, Frank Faylen
Keywords: rodeo, injury, rodeo cowboy

Overview: Retired rodeo champion Jeff McCloud agrees to mentor novice rodeo contestant Wes Merritt against the wishes of Merritt's wife who fears the dangers of this rough sport.
