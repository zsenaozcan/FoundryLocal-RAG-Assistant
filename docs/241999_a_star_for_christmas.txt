Title: A Star for Christmas (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance, TV Movie
Director: Michael Feifer
Main Cast: Briana Evigan, Corey Sevier, Brooke Burns, Travis Van Winkle
Keywords: holiday, christmas

Overview: The only thing that is working in Cassie's world is her small town business. Her Christmas Cupcakery is a huge success, but her love life is a mess. Cassie's whole universe is turned upside down when she falls in love with a co-worker who is revealed to be the famous and notoriously troubled actor Alex Gray. There's no denying that Alex and Cassie are in love, but can their relationship survive the pressure of Hollywood and the holidays?
