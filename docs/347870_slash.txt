Title: Slash (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Clay Liford
Main Cast: Michael Ian Black, Burnie Burns, Violett Beane, Hannah Marks, Missi Pyle

Overview: Freshman Neil's Vanguard stories are all he cares about...until he meets the older Julia, who pushes him to put his own fan fic online. When the website's moderator takes a special interest in Neil's work, it opens up a whole new universe.
