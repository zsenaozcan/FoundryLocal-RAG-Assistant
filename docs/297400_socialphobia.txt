Title: Socialphobia (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Mystery, Drama
Director: Hong Seok-Jae
Main Cast: Byun Yo-han, Lee Ju-seung, Ha Yoon-kyung, Ryu Jun-yeol, Park Keun-rok

Overview: A woman is attacked by Internet users after posting provocative remarks online. Using her personal information, they go to her place but what they find is her body; she took her own life. Who made her kill herself? Or, was she killed by someone else?
