Title: New York Confidential (1955)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Russell Rouse
Main Cast: Broderick Crawford, Richard Conte, Marilyn Maxwell, Anne Bancroft, J. Carrol Naish
Keywords: b movie, crime family, film noir

Overview: Story follows the rise and subsequent fall of the notorious head of a New York crime family, who decides to testify against his pals in order to avoid being killed by his fellow cohorts.
