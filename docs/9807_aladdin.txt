Title: Aladdin (1986)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Bruno Corbucci
Main Cast: Bud Spencer, Luca Venantini, Julian Voloshin, Diamy Spencer, Janet Ågren
Keywords: miami, wonderlamp, flying carpet, wish

Overview: Updating of the old "Aladdin" Arabian Nights tale, as a teenager in modern-day Miami finds a magic lantern and out pops a genie, who's been asleep for 200 years and in his gratitude grants the boy several wishes.
