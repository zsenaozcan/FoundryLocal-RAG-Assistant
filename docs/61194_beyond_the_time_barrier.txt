Title: Beyond the Time Barrier (1960)
Genres: Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Edgar G. Ulmer
Main Cast: Robert Clarke, Darlene Tompkins, Vladimir Sokoloff, Boyd 'Red' Morgan, Stephen Bekassy
Keywords: deaf-mute, near future, time traveler, test pilot, segregation, mutants, plague survivors, underground city

Overview: A pilot testing an experimental new rocket powered craft manages to fly into the future and land at the now deserted airbase he left. He ends up in a city with people who are suspicious he is a spy and who want to keep him to procreate with the rulers daughter because the majority of the inhabitants are sterile. He manages to escape and return to his own time but ages dramatically.
