Title: You'll Never Get Rich (1941)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Sidney Lanfield
Main Cast: Fred Astaire, Rita Hayworth, Robert Benchley, John Hubbard, Osa Massen
Keywords: musical, army, broadway

Overview: A Broadway choreographer (Fred Astaire) gets drafted and puts on a GI show with his girlfriend (Rita Hayworth) and producer (Robert Benchley).
