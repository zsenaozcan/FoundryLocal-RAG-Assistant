Title: Anne of the Thousand Days (1969)
Genres: Drama, Romance, History
Director: Charles Jarrott
Main Cast: Richard Burton, Geneviève Bujold, Irene Papas, Anthony Quayle, John Colicos
Keywords: england, king, henry viii

Overview: Henry VIII of England discards one wife, Katharine of Aragon, who has failed to produce a male heir, in favor of the young and beautiful Anne Boleyn.
