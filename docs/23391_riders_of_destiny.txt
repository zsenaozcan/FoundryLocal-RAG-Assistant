Title: Riders of Destiny (1933)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Robert N. Bradbury
Main Cast: John Wayne, Cecilia Parker, Forrest Taylor, George 'Gabby' Hayes
Keywords: romance

Overview: John Wayne portrays Singin' Sandy Saunders and has a reputation as the most notorious gunman since Billy the Kid. That's somewhat ironic though, since it's later revealed that he's a special Secret Service agent sent from Washington to investigate a land swindle scheme under the direction of town boss James Kincaid (Forrest Taylor).
