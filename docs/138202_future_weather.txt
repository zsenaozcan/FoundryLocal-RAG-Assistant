Title: Future Weather (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jenny Deller
Main Cast: Perla Haney-Jardine, Marin Ireland, Lili Taylor, Amy Madigan, William Sadler
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Abandoned by her dreamer single mom, a teenage loner becomes obsessed with ecological disaster, forcing her and her grandmother, a functioning alcoholic, to rethink their futures.
