Title: Man in the Saddle (1951)
Genres: Western
Director: André De Toth
Main Cast: Randolph Scott, Joan Leslie, Ellen Drew, Alexander Knox, Richard Rober
Keywords: rancher, gunman, the end, windy

Overview: A small farmer and rancher is being harassed by his mighty and powerfull neighbour. When the neighbour even hires gunmen to intimidate him he has to defend himself and his property by means of violence.
