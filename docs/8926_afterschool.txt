Title: Afterschool (2008)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: Antonio Campos
Main Cast: Ezra Miller, Jeremy Allen White, Emory Cohen, Michael Stuhlbarg, Rosemarie DeWitt
Keywords: overdose, video camera

Overview: A prep-school student (Ezra Miller) accidentally films the drug-related deaths of two classmates, then is asked to put together a memorial video.
