Title: In This Corner of the World (2016)
Genres: Animation, Drama
Director: Sunao Katabuchi
Main Cast: Non, Yoshimasa Hosoya, Daisuke Ono, Mayumi Shintani, Shigeru Ushiyama
Keywords: war, based on manga

Overview: Newlywed Suzu Urano moves to her new life in Kure City on the coast of Hiroshima Prefecture, where she perseveres through World War II with pluck and determination.
