Title: TV Junkie (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Michael Caine
Main Cast: Rick Kirkham
Keywords: drug addiction

Overview: Rick Kirkham was a reporter for Inside Edition who appeared on a segment called "Inside Adventure". From the age of 14, he filmed more than 3,000 hours of a video diary; this included footage during his tenure on Inside Edition during which he was addicted to crack cocaine.
