Title: The Brute (1953)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Luis Buñuel
Main Cast: Pedro Armendáriz, Katy Jurado, Rosita Arenas, Andrés Soler, Paco Martínez
Keywords: solidarity, fight, butcher's shop, community, independent film, neighbor, escape, landlord, slaughterhouse, orphan

Overview: A tough young man, who helps to kick poor people out of their houses, falls in love with a girl. She lives with her father in the building about to be demolished.
