Title: Spy School (2008)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Family
Director: Mark Blutman
Main Cast: Forrest Landis, AnnaSophia Robb, Rider Strong, Lea Thompson, Roger Bart

Overview: Spy School is the story of a twelve year old boy known for telling tall tales who overhears a plot to kidnap the President's daughter. When he goes public with his story, no one believes him, and he is forced to save her on his own.
