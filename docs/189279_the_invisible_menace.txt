Title: The Invisible Menace (1938)
Genres: Mystery
Director: John Farrow
Main Cast: Boris Karloff, Marie Wilson, Eddie Craven, Regis Toomey, Henry Kolker
Keywords: island, army, murder

Overview: Army Private Eddie Pratt smuggles his new bride into camp in hopes of having a happy wedding night. Instead they discover a murder. Colonel Rogers of Army Intelligence arrives to take over the case. The prime suspect, Jevries, is well-known to Rogers, who sets out to get a confession from Jevries even though there are plenty of other suspects.
