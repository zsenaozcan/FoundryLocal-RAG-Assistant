Title: Rânia (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Roberta Marques
Main Cast: Graziela Felix, Mariana Lima, Nataly Rocha, Rob Das, Demick Lopes
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Rânia is a teenage girl who lives on Santa Teresinha hill in the city of Fortaleza. Her busy days are also occupied by the dream of being a dancer. Rânia's best friend, Zizi, introduces her to 'Sereia da Noite', a local joint where fun, dance, money and sex rock the night. When Rânia meets the choreographer Estela, she will be torn between the possibility to make money in the nightclub and the will to become a "real dancer".
