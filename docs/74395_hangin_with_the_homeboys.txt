Title: Hangin' With The Homeboys (1991)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Joseph B. Vasquez
Main Cast: Doug E. Doug, Mario Joyner, John Leguizamo, Nestor Serrano, Kimberley Russell
Keywords: new york, italian, subway, police, friends, bronx

Overview: Two African-Americans and two Puerto-Ricans (though one pretends to be Italian) go out on the town on a Friday night. They will be forced to get to know each other, and even worst, learn to like each other as friends
