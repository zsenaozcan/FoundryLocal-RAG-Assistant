Title: Madman at War (1985)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Dino Risi
Main Cast: Coluche, Beppe Grillo, Bernard Blier, Fabio Testi, Claudio Bisio

Overview: Story about a doctor in a Libyan military hospital who is mad, but no-one wants to declare him unfit, and he becomes the captain of a fighting unit. Through him the Germans risk losing a truce with the British, and therefore kill him and give him a hero's funeral.
