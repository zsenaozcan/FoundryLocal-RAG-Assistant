Title: The Russian Game (2007)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Pavel Chukhray
Main Cast: Giuliano Di Capua, Sergey Garmash, Sergei Makovetsky, Andrey Merzlikin, Dimitri Vysotsky

Overview: Italian gambler who is well-skilled in the card trade owes people in authority, and leaves his country to win the money. Arriving in a small Russian town, he tries to show off his tricks to the local card swindlers but is caught and ends up agreeing to a friendship and a cooperation. It is hard not to find oneself liking the gallant Italian.
