Title: The Cutting Edge (1992)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Paul Michael Glaser
Main Cast: D. B. Sweeney, Moira Kelly, Roy Dotrice, Terry O'Quinn, Dwier Brown
Keywords: olympic games, sport, romantic comedy, skating, figure skating

Overview: Two former Olympians, one a figure skater and the other a hockey player, pin their hopes of one last shot at Olympic glory on one another. That is, of course, if they can keep from killing each other in the process...
