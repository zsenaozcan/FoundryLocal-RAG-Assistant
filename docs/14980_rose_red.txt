Title: Rose Red (2002)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Craig R. Baxley
Main Cast: Nancy Travis, Matt Keeslar, Kimberly J. Brown, David Dukes, Judith Ivey
Keywords: upper class, professor, haunted house, psychic power

Overview: Dr. Joyce Reardon, a psychology professor, leads a team of psychics into the decrepit mansion known as Rose Red. Her efforts unleash the spirit of former owner Ellen Rimbauer and uncover the horrifying secrets of those who lived and died there.
