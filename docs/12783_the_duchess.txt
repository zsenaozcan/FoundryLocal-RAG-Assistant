Title: The Duchess (2008)
Genres: Drama, History, Romance
Director: Saul Dibb
Main Cast: Keira Knightley, Ralph Fiennes, Aidan McArdle, Simon McBurney, Charlotte Rampling
Keywords: england, adultery, duke, gambling debts, marriage crisis

Overview: A chronicle of the life of 18th century aristocrat Georgiana, Duchess of Devonshire, who was reviled for her extravagant political and personal life.
