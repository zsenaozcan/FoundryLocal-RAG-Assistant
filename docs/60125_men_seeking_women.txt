Title: Men Seeking Women (1997)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Jim Milio
Main Cast: Maureen Teefy, Anthony Palermo, Grant Shaud, Lisa Wilcox, Nia Vardalos
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Three friends, finding themselves single on their 33'rd birthdays, agree to bet $2000 each to see who can get a girlfriend and live together for three months.
