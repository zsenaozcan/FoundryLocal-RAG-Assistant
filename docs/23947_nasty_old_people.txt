Title: Nasty Old People (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hanna Sköld
Main Cast: Karin Bertling, Cecile Anckarswärd, Rune Bergman, Torkel Petersson
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Member of a neo-Nazi gang, her day job is to take care of four crazy old people that all are just waiting to die. Her life becomes a journey into a burlesque fairytale, where the rules of the game are created by Mette herself. Mette is indifferent about her way of life, until she one night assaults a man, kicking him senseless. Waking up the day after, she realizes that something is wrong.
