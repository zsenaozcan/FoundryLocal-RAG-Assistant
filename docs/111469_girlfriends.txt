Title: Girlfriends (1978)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Claudia Weill
Main Cast: Melanie Mayron, Anita Skinner, Christopher Guest, Bob Balaban, Eli Wallach
Keywords: photographer, roommate, rabbi, single, woman director

Overview: A photographer and her girlfriend are roommates. She is stuck with small-change shooting jobs and dreams of success. When her roommate decides to get married and leave, she feels hurt and has to learn how to deal with living alone.
