Title: Evaporating Borders (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Iva Radivojevic
Main Cast: Iva Radivojevic
Keywords: illegal immigration, immigrant, woman director, cyprus

Overview: Evaporating Borders is a poetically photographed and rendered film on tolerance and search for identity. Told through 5 vignettes portraying the lives of migrants on the island of Cyprus, it passionately weaves themes of displacement and belonging.
