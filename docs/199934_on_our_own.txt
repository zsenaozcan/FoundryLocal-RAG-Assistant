Title: On Our Own (1998)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Lone Scherfig
Main Cast: Pernille Kaae Høier, Ann Eleonora Jørgensen, Birthe Neumann, Bjarne Henriksen, Lars Kaalund
Keywords: denmark, copenhagen, children, female prisoner, single mother, children on the run, childhood, woman director

Overview: Three siblings ingeniously avoid being sent off to a children's home while their solo-mother serves a short sentence in a prison for shoplifting. Rather than have the news leak out and have to be escorted off with the eccentric welfare officer, they invent a 'never present' dad who is looking after them.
