Title: Miss Pettigrew Lives for a Day (2008)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Bharat Nalluri
Main Cast: Frances McDormand, Amy Adams, Lee Pace, Ciarán Hinds, Clare Clifford
Keywords: london england, world war ii, singer, governess, piano

Overview: Guinevere Pettigrew, a middle-aged London governess, finds herself unfairly dismissed from her job. An attempt to gain new employment catapults her into the glamorous world and dizzying social whirl of an American actress and singer, Delysia Lafosse.
