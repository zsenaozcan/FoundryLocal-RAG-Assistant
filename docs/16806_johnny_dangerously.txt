Title: Johnny Dangerously (1984)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Thriller, Crime
Director: Amy Heckerling
Main Cast: Michael Keaton, Peter Boyle, Griffin Dunne, Marilu Henner, Joe Piscopo
Keywords: organized crime, mobster, woman director

Overview: Set in the 1930s, an honest, goodhearted man is forced to turn to a life of crime to finance his neurotic mother's skyrocketing medical bills.
