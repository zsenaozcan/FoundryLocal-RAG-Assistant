Title: The Program (1993)
Genres: Drama
Director: David S. Ward
Main Cast: James Caan, Halle Berry, Omar Epps, Craig Sheffer, Kristy Swanson
Keywords: american football, sport

Overview: Several players from different backgrounds try to cope with the pressures of playing football at a major university. Each deals with the pressure differently, some turn to drinking, others to drugs, and some to studying.
