Title: Demon Witch Child (1975)
Genres: Horror
Director: Amando de Ossorio
Main Cast: Julián Mateos, Marián Salgado, Fernando Sancho, Lone Fleming, Ángel del Pozo
Keywords: exorcism, priest

Overview: An old witch seeks vengence through the daughter of the politician who had her thrown in jail. Soon the little girl's head is spinning around, and an old priest is called in to perform an exorcism.
