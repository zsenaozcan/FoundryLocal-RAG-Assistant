Title: W.W. and the Dixie Dancekings (1975)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Music
Director: John G. Avildsen
Main Cast: Burt Reynolds, Conny Van Dyke, Jerry Reed, Ned Beatty, Art Carney
Keywords: country music, pursuit, triumph

Overview: Story of a happy-go-lucky small time crook who runs into a country music group looking for their big break. They end up helping each other out in unexpected ways.
