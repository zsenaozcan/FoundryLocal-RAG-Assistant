Title: Girl vs. Monster (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Science Fiction
Director: Stuart Gillard
Main Cast: Olivia Holt, Brendan Meyer, Kerris Dorsey, Katherine McNamara, Tracy Dawson
Keywords: monster, halloween, teen movie, teenager, fighting supernatural

Overview: Skylar finds out that her parents are monster hunters after she accidentally releases some monsters from a secret containment chamber; so she and her techno friends must recapture all the monsters and also save her mom and dad from these monsters who are out for revenge.
