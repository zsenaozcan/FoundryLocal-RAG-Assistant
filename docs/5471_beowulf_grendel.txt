Title: Beowulf & Grendel (2005)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Sturla Gunnarsson
Main Cast: Gerard Butler, Spencer Wilding, Stellan Skarsgård, Ingvar Eggert Sigurðsson, Hringur Ingvarsson
Keywords: hero, mythology, nordic mythology, mythical creature, viking, weapon, swordsman, mythological beast, norse mythology, sword and sorcery

Overview: The blood-soaked tale of a Norse warrior's battle against the great and murderous troll, Grendel. Heads will roll. Out of allegiance to the King Hrothgar, the much respected Lord of the Danes, Beowulf leads a troop of warriors across the sea to rid a village of the marauding monster.
