Title: Threshold (1981)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Richard Pearce
Main Cast: Donald Sutherland, Jeff Goldblum, Mare Winningham, Sharon Acker, John Marley
Keywords: heart, heart transplant, artificial heart

Overview: The celebrated heart surgeon Dr. Vrain supports the research of the offbeat scientist Aldo Gehring, who is inventing an artificial heart. Dr. Vrain performs the first artificial human heart transplant against the advice of the Ethics Committee.
