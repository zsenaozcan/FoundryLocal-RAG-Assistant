Title: Will Success Spoil Rock Hunter? (1957)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Frank Tashlin
Main Cast: Tony Randall, Jayne Mansfield, Joan Blondell, Betsy Drake, John Williams
Keywords: ambition, marketing, advertising expert, engagement

Overview: To save his career, an ad man wants a sex symbol to endorse a lipstick. But she wants something too: he has to pretend to be her new lover.
