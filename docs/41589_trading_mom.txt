Title: Trading Mom (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Tia Brelis
Main Cast: Sissy Spacek, Anna Chlumsky, Aaron Michael Metchik, Asher Metchik, Merritt Yohnka
Keywords: mother, magic, family relationships, woman director, tween girl

Overview: The Martin kids learn of a magic spell that will take them to the 'mommy market' so they can get a more user friendly model. After giving a few other moms a try, they want their own back. But that is not so easy.
