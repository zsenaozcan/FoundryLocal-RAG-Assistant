Title: Paradise Lost: The Child Murders at Robin Hood Hills (1996)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Joe Berlinger
Main Cast: Joe Berlinger, Bruce Sinofsky, Jessie Miskelly, Jason Baldwin, Damien Wayne Echols
Keywords: child murderer, right and justice

Overview: A horrific triple child murder leads to an indictment and trial of three nonconformist boys based on questionable evidence.
