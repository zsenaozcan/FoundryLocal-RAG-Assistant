Title: P2 (2007)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Franck Khalfoun
Main Cast: Wes Bentley, Rachel Nichols, Simon Reynolds, Philip Akin, Miranda Edwards
Keywords: kidnapping, parking garage, basement garage, psychopath, christmas eve, security guard, executive

Overview: Angela, a corporate climber, gets stuck working late on Christmas Eve and finds herself the target of an unhinged security guard. With no help in sight, the woman must overcome physical and psychological challenges to survive.
