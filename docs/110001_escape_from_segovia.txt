Title: Escape from Segovia (1981)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Imanol Uribe
Main Cast: Xabier Elorriaga, Mario Pardo, Ovidi Montllor, Chema Muñoz, José Pedro Carrión
Keywords: prison, journalist, based on novel, franco regime, escape from prison, eta, terrorism, jailbreak, based on true events, euskal zinema

Overview: Narrates the escape of 29 political prisoners, led by ETA, from Segovia prison. Based on actual events.
