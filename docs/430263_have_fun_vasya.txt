Title: Have Fun, Vasya! (2017)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Roman Karimov
Main Cast: Efim Petrunin, Lyubov Aksyonova, Sofya Rayzman, Roman Kurtsyn, Sergey Abroskin

Overview: Contemporary Russia. Mitya and Alisa are set to get married. The problem is that Mitya is still married to Vasya, who refuses to grant him a divorce. Can Mitya and his friends convince Vasya to change her mind?
