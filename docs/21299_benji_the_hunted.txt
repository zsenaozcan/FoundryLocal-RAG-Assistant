Title: Benji the Hunted (1987)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Family
Director: Joe Camp
Main Cast: Red Steagall, Frank Inn, Nancy Francis
Keywords: hunter, wilderness, cougar, lost at sea, benji

Overview: Benji has become stranded on a remote island after a boating accident. He finds himself struggling to survive in the wilderness, avoiding close encounters with a wolf, a bear, and a territorial female cougar with her cub.
