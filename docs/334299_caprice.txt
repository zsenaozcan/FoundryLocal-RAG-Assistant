Title: Caprice (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Emmanuel Mouret
Main Cast: Virginie Efira, Anaïs Demoustier, Laurent Stocker, Emmanuel Mouret, Thomas Blanchard

Overview: An average guy meets an actress who is more beautiful than he could ever imagine. But then a pesky girl materializes to make his life a living hell. His perfect girlfriend now thinks that he is involved with this Caprice.
