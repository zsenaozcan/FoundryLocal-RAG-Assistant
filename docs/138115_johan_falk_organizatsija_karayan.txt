Title: Johan Falk: Organizatsija Karayan (2012)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Richard Holm
Main Cast: Jakob Eklund, Marie Richardson, Johan Hedenberg, Mikael Tornving, Joel Kinnaman

Overview: Johan Falk's stepdaughter Nina has gotten in contact with her biological father, Orjan, discovering that he is in debt to an Estonian construction company in Gothenburg, leading to Nina's kidnapping.
