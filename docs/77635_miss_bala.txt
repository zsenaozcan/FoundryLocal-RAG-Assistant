Title: Miss Bala (2011)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Gerardo Naranjo
Main Cast: Stephanie Sigman, Noe Hernandez, Irene Azuela, Jose Yenque, James Russo
Keywords: rape, killing, handcuffs, press conference, drug cartel, hiding under a bed

Overview: The story of a young woman clinging on to her dream to become a beauty contest queen in a Mexico dominated by organized crime.
