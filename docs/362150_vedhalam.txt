Title: Vedhalam (2015)
Genres: Action, Family
Director: Siva
Main Cast: Ajith Kumar, Shruti Haasan, Lakshmi Menon, Soori, Thambi Ramaiah
Keywords: supernatural, revenge, double act, sentimental

Overview: Ganesh, a cab driver and a doting brother to his sister Tamizh, is hunting down three notorious criminals in Kolkata. Who is he actually and what's his motive?
