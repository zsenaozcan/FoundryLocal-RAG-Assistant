Title: Unit 7 (2012)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Alberto Rodríguez
Main Cast: Antonio de la Torre, Mario Casas, Julián Villagrán, José Manuel Poga, Inma Cuesta
Keywords: bad cop

Overview: Angel, a young intelligent and kind, aims to be a police inspector. Rafael  instead  is an expeditious, forceful and arrogant policeman. Meanwhile, Miguel and Mateo are part of Group 7, a group of rogue cops ready to do anything to achieve their goals.
