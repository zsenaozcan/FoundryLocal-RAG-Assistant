Title: Mindhorn (2017)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sean Foley
Main Cast: Julian Barratt, Simon Farnaby, Essie Davis, Steve Coogan, Andrea Riseborough
Keywords: amateur detective, murderer, aging actor, police investigation, isle of man, crime spoof, failed actor

Overview: A washed up actor who played Mindhorn, a secret agent with a bionic eye, returns to the Isle of Man, the area where his most famous role was set, to help catch a killer.
