Title: Third Star (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hattie Dalton
Main Cast: Tom Burke, Benedict Cumberbatch, J. J. Feild, Hugh Bonneville, Eros Vlahos
Keywords: friendship, cancer, woman director

Overview: James and his three closest lifelong friends go on an ill-advised trip to the stunning coastal area of Barafundle Bay in West Wales. What follows is a touching and comical adventure dealing with friendship, heroism and love.
