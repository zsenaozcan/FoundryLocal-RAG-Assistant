Title: Done the Impossible (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Tony Hadlock
Main Cast: Adam Baldwin, Jewel Staite, Alan Tudyk, Christina Hendricks, Christopher Buchanan
Keywords: fan culture, documentary, science fiction, firefly, joss whedon, fandom
