Title: The Man with the Golden Arm (1955)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Otto Preminger
Main Cast: Frank Sinatra, Kim Novak, Eleanor Parker, Arnold Stang, Darren McGavin
Keywords: prison, poker, gambling, underground world, sense of guilt, jazz, drug dealer, rehabilitation, junkie, wheelchair

Overview: Frankie is a heroin addict and sits in prison. He wants a different life but the rehabilitation isn’t as easy as he had thought. Director Otto Preminger films one of the first social studies of an addict in this 1955 film.
