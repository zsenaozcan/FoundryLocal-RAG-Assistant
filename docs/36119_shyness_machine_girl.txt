Title: Shyness Machine Girl (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Action, Science Fiction
Director: Noboru Iguchi
Main Cast: Noriko Kijima, Kentarô Shimazu, Minase Yashiro, Demo Tanaka, Asami
Keywords: cyborg, resurrection, girl heroine, female protagonist, woman with gun, schoolgirl, gatling gun

Overview: Ami's friend, Yoshie, was murdered and desecrated by the Kimura Gang. She was saved and, like Ami in the previous film, received modifications from the same mechanics. Remembering her past, Yoshie decides to avenge herself and Ami.
