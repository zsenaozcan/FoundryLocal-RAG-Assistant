Title: A Murder of Crows (1999)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Rowdy Herrington
Main Cast: Cuba Gooding Jr., Tom Berenger, Marianne Jean-Baptiste, Eric Stoltz, Mark Pellegrino
Keywords: book, louisiana, murder, suspense, independent film, lawyer, killer

Overview: After a lawyer gets disbared, he goes off to write a book about his experience. He meets a man who lets him read his manuscript. The man dies and the lawyer passes the book off as his. Just when the book becomes a big success. He gets arrested for the true life murders of the five lawyers in his book. He then finds himself, trying to prove that he is innocent of the murders
