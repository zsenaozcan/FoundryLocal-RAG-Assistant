Title: King of Kings (1961)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nicholas Ray
Main Cast: Jeffrey Hunter, Siobhán McKenna, Hurd Hatfield, Viveca Lindfors, Robert Ryan
Keywords: jesus christ, biography, epic

Overview: Who is Jesus, and why does he impact all he meets? He is respected and reviled, emulated and accused, beloved, betrayed, and finally crucified. Yet that terrible fate would not be the end of the story.
