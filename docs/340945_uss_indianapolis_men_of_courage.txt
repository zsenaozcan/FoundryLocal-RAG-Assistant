Title: USS Indianapolis: Men of Courage (2016)
Genres: Action, War
Director: Mario Van Peebles
Main Cast: Nicolas Cage, Tom Sizemore, Matt Lanter, Thomas Jane, Emily Tennant
Keywords: world war ii, based on true story, 1940s

Overview: The harrowing true story of the crew of the USS Indianapolis, who were stranded in the Philippine Sea for five days after delivering the atomic weapons that would eventually end WWII. As they awaited rescue, they endured extreme thirst, hunger, and relentless shark attacks.
