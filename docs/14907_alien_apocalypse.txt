Title: Alien Apocalypse (2005)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Comedy, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Josh Becker
Main Cast: Bruce Campbell, Renee O'Connor, Michael Cory Davis, Peter Jason, Neda Sokolovska
Keywords: alien life-form, space invasion, alien, astronaut

Overview: An astronaut doctor Ivan Hood and his fellow astronaut Kelly return from their mission in space to find the world has been taken over by aliens. Now Dr. Ivan Hood and Kelly must lead a revolution to free the human slaves from their alien masters.
