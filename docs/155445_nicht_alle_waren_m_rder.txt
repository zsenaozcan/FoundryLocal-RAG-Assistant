Title: Nicht alle waren Mörder (2006)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Jo Baier
Main Cast: Nadja Uhl, Axel Prahl, Hannelore Elsner, Dagmar Manzel, Merab Ninidze
Keywords: world war ii, lie, deportation, bombing, child play, kz sachsenhausen

Overview: Based on the childhood memories of actor Michael Degen, the movie deals with the everyday struggle to survive as a Jewish boy in Nazi Germany. As his father had died in 1940 after being released from the Sachsenhausen concentration camp, Michael and his mother fear to be deported themselves. They manage to live in Berlin with false names and faked papers, hidden by several, often broken, people...
