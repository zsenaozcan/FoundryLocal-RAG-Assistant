Title: The Samaritan (2012)
Genres: Thriller
Director: David Weaver
Main Cast: Samuel L. Jackson, Luke Kirby, Ruth Negga, Tom Wilkinson, Alan C. Peterson
Keywords: prison, sex, revenge, incest, grifter, paternity revealed, parole officer

Overview: After twenty years in prison, Foley is finished with the grifter's life. When he meets an elusive young woman named Iris, the possibility of a new start looks real. But his past is proving to be a stubborn companion.
