Title: Knockout (2000)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Lorenzo Doumani
Main Cast: Sophia Adella Luke, Eduardo Yáñez, Tony Plana, Paul Winfield, William McNamara

Overview: Bell decides to follow the footsteps of his father, devoted to boxing to become champion. But not only must fight against his opponents but against a world where it seems that nobody wants to see it succeed.
