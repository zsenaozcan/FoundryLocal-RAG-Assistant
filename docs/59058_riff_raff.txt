Title: Riff-Raff (1991)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Ken Loach
Main Cast: Robert Carlyle, Emer McCourt, George Moss, Ricky Tomlinson, Jim R. Coleman
Keywords: independent film, poor class

Overview: The story of Stevie, a construction worker, and his girlfriend, an unemployed pop singer, serves to show the living conditions of the British poor class
