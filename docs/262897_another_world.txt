Title: Another World (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Thomas Torelli
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: human evolution, modern society, quantum mechanics, spirituality, environment, native peoples, science, consciousness

Overview: A feature documentary about the journey of mankind to discover our true force and who we truly are. It is a quest through science and consciousness, individual and planetary, exploring our relationships with ourselves, the world around us and the universe as a whole.
