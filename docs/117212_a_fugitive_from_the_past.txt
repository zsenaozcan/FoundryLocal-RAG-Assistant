Title: A Fugitive from the Past (1965)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery
Director: Tomu Uchida
Main Cast: Rentarô Mikuni, Sachiko Hidari, Kôji Mitsui, Yoshi Katô, Sadako Sawamura

Overview: Three robbers escape with loot from a heist before one of them shoots them; their corpses wash up near the aftermath of a maritime calamity, provoking a policeman's interest.
