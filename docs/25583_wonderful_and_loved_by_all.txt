Title: Wonderful and Loved by All (2007)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Hannes Holm
Main Cast: Martina Haag, Nikolaj Coster-Waldau, Ellen Mattsson, Reine Brynolfsson, Katrin Sundberg

Overview: Isabella Eklöf is an unemployed actress in her early thirties. Her friends all enjoy stable relationships, children and successful careers. In order to jump-start her life she decides to lie a bit on her next job application with unexpected consequences.
