Title: Half the Sky: Turning Oppression Into Opportunity for Women Worldwide (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Maro Chermayeff
Main Cast: Eva Mendes, Nicholas Kristof, Dipti Mehta, Meg Ryan, Gabrielle Union
Keywords: woman director

Overview: PBS series documentary based on a book of the same name that argues the oppression of women worldwide is the "paramount moral challenge" of our time.
