Title: A Girl Walks Home Alone at Night (2014)
Genres: Horror, Romance
Director: Ana Lily Amirpour
Main Cast: Sheila Vand, Arash Marandi, Marshall Manesh, Dominic Rains, Rome Shadanloo
Keywords: vampire, female vampire, iranian, woman director

Overview: In the Iranian ghost-town Bad City, a place that reeks of death and loneliness, the townspeople are unaware they are being stalked by a lonesome vampire.
