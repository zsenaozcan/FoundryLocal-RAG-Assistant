Title: How to Be Louise (1990)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Anne Flournoy
Main Cast: Lea Floden, Steve Ahern, Dan Bonnell, Maggie Burke, Eugene Corey
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Haunted by the death of her mother and without role models, dreamer Louise scours her world for someone to imitate. With the help of James Joyce's Ulysses and a taxi-driving poet, Louise discovers she is the woman she thought she would never be.
