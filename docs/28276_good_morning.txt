Title: Good Morning (1959)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Drama
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Main Cast: Yoshiko Kuga, Chishu Ryu, Kuniko Miyake, Haruko Sugimura, Kôji Shitara
Keywords: japan, tv, neighborhood, television set

Overview: Two boys beg their parents for a television set, nagging them until all patience is lost. The parents order the boys to be quiet and the boys do exactly that--refusing to utter a word. The boys' silence ultimately puts the whole neighborhood into turmoil.
