Title: Weird Woman (1944)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Reginald Le Borg
Main Cast: Lon Chaney Jr., Anne Gwynne, Evelyn Ankers, Ralph Morgan, Elisabeth Risdon
Keywords: voodoo, b movie, radio series

Overview: While on a South Seas trip, a professor falls in love with marries an exotic native woman. What he doesn't know is that she was raised by superstitious natives who believe her to be some kind of supernatural being.
