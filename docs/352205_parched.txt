Title: Parched (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Leena Yadav
Main Cast: Radhika Apte, Tannishtha Chatterjee, Sayani Gupta, Surveen Chawla, Adil Hussain
Keywords: fire, tradition, fight, mobile phone, cave, fireworks, sheep, teenage girl, rural setting, female protagonist
