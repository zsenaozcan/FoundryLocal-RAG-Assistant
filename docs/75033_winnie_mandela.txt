Title: Winnie Mandela (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Darrell James Roodt
Main Cast: Elias Koteas, Terrence Howard, Jennifer Hudson, Wendy Crewson, Angelique Pretorius
Keywords: biography, nelson mandela

Overview: A drama that chronicles the life of Winnie Mandela from her childhood through her marriage and her husband's incarceration.
