Title: Kill Squad (1982)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Patrick G. Donahue
Main Cast: Jean Glaudé, Jeff Risk, Sean P. Donahue, Cameron Mitchell

Overview: A wealthy business man is a victim of an assault and is shot and wheelchair bound while his wife is gang raped and murdered. He then assembles his motley squad of Vietnam buddies to kung fu their way to revenge and get those muthas back.
