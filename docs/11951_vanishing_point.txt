Title: Vanishing Point (1971)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Drama
Director: Richard C. Sarafian
Main Cast: Barry Newman, Cleavon Little, Dean Jagger, Victoria Medlin, Gilda Texter
Keywords: san francisco, bet, denver, police, auto-tuning

Overview: Kowalski works for a car delivery service. He takes delivery of a 1970 Dodge Challenger to take from Colorado to San Francisco, California. Shortly after pickup, he takes a bet to get the car there in less than 15 hours.
