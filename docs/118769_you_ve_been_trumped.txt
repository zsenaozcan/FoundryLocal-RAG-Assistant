Title: You've Been Trumped (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Anthony Baxter
Main Cast: Michael Forbes, Donald Trump, Anthony Baxter, Sheila Forbes
Keywords: scandal, tycoon, political satire, environmental conservation, environmental destruction, sustainable development, ruthlessness, police harassment, sand dune, common good

Overview: In this David and Goliath story for the 21st century, a group of proud Scottish homeowners take on celebrity tycoon Donald Trump as he buys up one of Scotland's last wilderness areas to build a golf resort.
