Title: Lady Liberty (1971)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mario Monicelli
Main Cast: Sophia Loren, Gigi Proietti, William Devane, Beeson Carroll, David Doyle

Overview: The story concerns the difficulties and reactions of Maddalena (Sophia Loren), an Italian visitor to New York City. She has come to the country carrying a huge mortadella sausage which she intends as a gift for her fiancé. But Customs do not allow processed meat to enter the U.S., so she is held at JFK airport...
