Title: Joan of Paris (1942)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: Robert Stevenson
Main Cast: Michèle Morgan, Paul Henreid, Thomas Mitchell, Laird Cregar, May Robson
Keywords: france, gestapo

Overview: An RAF squadron is brought down over occupied France. The flyers get to Paris in spite of the fact that the youngest, Baby, is injured. He must be hidden and his wounds cared for. The Gestapo has already issued orders for their arrest.
