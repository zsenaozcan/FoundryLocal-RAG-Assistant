Title: The Bellboy (1960)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jerry Lewis
Main Cast: Jerry Lewis, Alex Gerry, Milton Berle, Bob Clayton, Sonnie Sands
Keywords: hotel, slapstick, gag humor, madcap comedy, bellhop  , entourage

All that he displays are facial expressions and a comedic slapstick style. And anything that can go wrong - does go wrong when Stanley is involved.
