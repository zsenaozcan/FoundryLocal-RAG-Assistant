Title: The Scribbler (2014)
Genres: Thriller
Director: John Suits
Main Cast: Katie Cassidy, Eliza Dushku, Sasha Grey, Michelle Trachtenberg, Gina Gershon
Keywords: based on graphic novel, multiple personality, psychiatric

Overview: Suki is a young woman confronting her destructive mental illness using "The Siamese Burn," an experimental machine designed to eliminate multiple personalities. The closer Suki comes to being "cured," she's haunted by a thought... what if the last unwanted identity turns out to be her?
