Title: 18 Again! (1988)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy
Director: Paul Flaherty
Main Cast: George Burns, Charlie Schlatter, Tony Roberts, Miriam Flynn, Red Buttons
Keywords: grandfather, wish, role reversal, body-swap, wishes come true, vejez

Overview: 18 Again! is a 1988 comedy film starring George Burns and Charlie Schlatter. The plot involves a grandson switching souls with his grandfather by means of an accident. This was one of a series of unrelated films, including Like Father, Like Son and Vice Versa, produced in the late 1980s involving a similar plotline.
