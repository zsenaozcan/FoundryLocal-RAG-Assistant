Title: The Saga of Gosta Berling (1924)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mauritz Stiller
Main Cast: Greta Garbo, Lars Hanson, Gerda Lundeqvist, Karin Swanström, Jenny Hasselqvist
Keywords: based on novel, silent film

Overview: Gösta Berling is a young and attractive minister. Because of his alcoholism and his daring sermons, he is finally defrocked. He becomes a tutor of countess Marta's stepdaughter and they fall in love. But the countess has a plan of her own ... Plot by  Aljaz Ciber.
