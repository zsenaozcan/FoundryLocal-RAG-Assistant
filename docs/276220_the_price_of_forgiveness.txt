Title: The Price of Forgiveness (2001)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mansour Sora Wade
Main Cast: Hubert Koundé, Gora Seck, Alioune Ndiaye, Nar Sene, Rokhaya Niang

Overview: Mythical story about a fishing village on the south coast of Senegal. Two men in the village are both in love with the same beautiful girl.
