Title: Lightning Jack (1994)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Western
Director: Simon Wincer
Main Cast: Paul Hogan, Beverly D'Angelo, Cuba Gooding Jr., Pat Hingle, Richard Riehle
Keywords: lightning, jack

Overview: Lightning Jack Kane is an Australian outlaw in the wild west. During a bungled bank robbery he picks up mute Ben Doyle as a hostage. The two become good friends, with Jack teaching Ben how to rob banks, while they plan Jack's last heist.
