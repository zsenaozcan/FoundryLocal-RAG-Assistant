Title: Beautiful Thing (1996)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Hettie MacDonald
Main Cast: Linda Henry, Glen Berry, Scott Neal, Tameka Empson, Ben Daniels
Keywords: london england, gay, coming out, homosexuality, summer, gay man, lgbt, woman director

Overview: A pair of teenage boys growing up in a working-class neighbourhood of Thamesmead, in south-east London, become aware of their homosexuality. While both were vaguely aware they might be gay, neither had ever acted on their impulses. Once they decide that they're attracted to each other, neither is sure just what to do.
