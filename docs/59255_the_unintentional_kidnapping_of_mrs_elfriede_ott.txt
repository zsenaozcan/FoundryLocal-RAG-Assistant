Title: The Unintentional Kidnapping of Mrs. Elfriede Ott (2010)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Andreas Prochaska
Main Cast: Michael Ostrowski, Andreas Kiendl, Elfriede Ott, Gerhard Liebmann, Angelika Niedetzky

Overview: Horst receives the monthly pension from his grandmother. The only problem is, that she is dead. As a local politician wants to congratulate the grandmother to her birthday Horst "borrows" an old lady from a hospital nearby, not recognizing that he just kidnapped actress Elfriede Ott.
