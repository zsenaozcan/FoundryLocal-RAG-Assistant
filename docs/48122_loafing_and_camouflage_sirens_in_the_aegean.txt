Title: Loafing and Camouflage: Sirens in the Aegean (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Nikos Perakis
Main Cast: Orfeas Avgoustidis, Yannis Tsimitselis, Ioannis Papazisis, Socratis Patsikas, Stelios Xanthoudakis

Overview: A group of Greek solders, who serve their military service in Kos, is sent to guard the skerry of Pitta. Everything looked normal until a Turkish boat disembarks 4 castaways on Pitta.
