Title: The Green Years (1963)
Genres: Drama
Director: Paulo Rocha
Main Cast: Rui Gomes, Isabel Ruth, Paulo Renato

Overview: Julio, aged nineteen, has just left the provinces to settle down in the outskirts of Lisbon. He lives there in a poor area with his uncle Raul and starts working as an apprentice shoemaker. At the shop, he gets to know Ilda, a young housemaid and regular customer. Ilda is pretty, joyful and modern and Julio falls for her.
