Title: Master and Tatyana (2015)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Giedrė Žickytė
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: photographer, 1970s, soviet union, biography, lithuania, woman director

Overview: Lithuanian photographer, the legend of Soviet Sixties' generation Vitas Luckus tragically passed away in 1987. Yet the life and times of the talented rebel still impassion and lead us to a journey questioning why, at all times, we are wary of those who are really free.
