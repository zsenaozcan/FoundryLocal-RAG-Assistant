Title: Standing Tall (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Emmanuelle Bercot
Main Cast: Rod Paradot, Catherine Deneuve, Sara Forestier, Benoît Magimel, Diane Rouxel
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The film tells the story of Malony and his education as he grows from a six-year-old into an 18-year-old. A minors’ judge and a caseworker work tirelessly to try to save the young offender.
