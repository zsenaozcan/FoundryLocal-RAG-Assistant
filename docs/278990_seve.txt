Title: Seve (2014)
Genres: Drama, Documentary
Director: John-Paul Davidson
Main Cast: José Luis Gutiérrez, José Navar, Maria Molins, Alvar Gordejuela, Adrián Salzedo
Keywords: golf, biography, sport

Overview: Despite being blocked at almost every turn in pursuit of the sport he loved, Seve Ballesteros fought against adversity to become the most spectacular and charismatic golfer to ever play the game.
