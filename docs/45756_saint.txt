Title: Saint (2010)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Dick Maas
Main Cast: Huub Stapel, Egbert Jan Weeber, Caro Lenssen, Bert Luppes, Escha Tanihatu
Keywords: bishop, saint nicholas

Overview: A horror film that depicts St. Nicholas as a murderous bishop who kidnaps and murders children when there is a full moon on December 5.
