Title: Two Men in Town (1973)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: José Giovanni
Main Cast: Jean Gabin, Alain Delon, Mimsy Farmer, Michel Bouquet, Cécile Vassort
Keywords: rehabilitation, rehabilitation centre, shadowing, spying, police, guillotine, torture by the police, loss of wife

Overview: A former bank robber is released after 10 years in prison. He gets help from a social-worker, but gets harassed by an old cop from his past.
