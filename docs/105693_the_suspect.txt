Title: The Suspect (1975)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Francesco Maselli
Main Cast: Gian Maria Volonté, Annie Girardot, Renato Salvatori, Gabriele Lavia, Bruno Corazzari

Overview: Fascism has forced the leadership of the Italian Communist Party to settle in Paris. In Italy arrests of militants are decimating the organization, so Emilio is sent on a mission in the area of Turin, to put out of harm whistleblowers.
