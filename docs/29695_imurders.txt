Title: iMurders (2008)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Robbie Bryan
Main Cast: Gabrielle Anwar, William Forsythe, Tony Todd, Frank Grillo, Terri Conn
Keywords: computer program, suspense, independent film, cyber, online chat

Overview: A mysterious love triangle leads to a tragic shooting. Months later, eight members of a MySpace-esque chat room are being gruesomely murdered in the privacy of their own homes.
