Title: Naanum Rowdydhaan (2015)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Vignesh Shivan
Main Cast: Vijay Sethupathi, Nayanthara, Balaji Patturaj, Radhika Sarathkumar, Anandharaj
Keywords: revenge, gangster, deaf, don, rowdy

Overview: Pandian, the son of a cop and a wannabe rowdy, falls in love with Kadambarai, a hearing-impaired girl, who wants him to take on Killivalavan, a gangster-politician who has murdered her parents.
