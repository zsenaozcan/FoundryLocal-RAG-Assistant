Title: Earth vs. the Flying Saucers (1956)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Fred F. Sears
Main Cast: Hugh Marlowe, Joan Taylor, Donald Curtis, Thomas Browne Henry, Morris Ankrum
Keywords: flying saucer, alien invasion, death ray

Overview: After aliens fail to effectively manage their time-space communications devices and botch an attempt to contact rocket scientist Dr. Russell Marvin and his charming wife Carol, they start to destroy Earth targets, suck the knowledge out of military brains, and issue ultimatums. Fortunately, Dr. Marvin and his fellow scientists have a few tricks of their own.
