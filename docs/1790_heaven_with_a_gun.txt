Title: Heaven with a Gun (1969)
Genres: Western
Director: Lee H. Katzin
Main Cast: Glenn Ford, Carolyn Jones, Barbara Hershey, John Anderson, David Carradine
Keywords: gunslinger, ranch, johnson county war

Overview: Glenn Ford plays Jim Killian, a preacher who arrives in a town divided between cattlemen and sheep herders. But Killian isn't just any preacher. He is a former fast gun who has set upon a different path.
