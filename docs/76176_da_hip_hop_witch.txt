Title: Da Hip Hop Witch (2000)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Dale Resteghini
Main Cast: Eminem, Vanilla Ice, Pras, Ja Rule, Mobb Deep

Overview: 5 teenage rappers gets notice of a mysterious witch that supposedly lurks in the ghetto and those who are attacked by her gets a successful hip-hop career. In their search for the witch, they come across various rappers whom already been attacked and retells their experiences.
