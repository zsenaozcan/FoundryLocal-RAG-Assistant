Title: Lost in New York (1989)
Genres: Mystery, Horror, Fantasy
Director: Jean Rollin
Main Cast: Adeline Abitbol, Catherine Herengt, Funny Abitbol, Catherine Lesret, Sophie Maret

Overview: It is about two Alices who transport themselves through a mirror of fictions; the Wonderland as it turns out is eerie, desolate New York. A memory of a journey past? A fanciful, mysterious flight inwards? He frames before and after with an essay on the imaginative mind weaving narratives, fictions, cinema. Mirrors permit the journey inwards, masks.
