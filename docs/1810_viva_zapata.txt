Title: Viva Zapata! (1952)
Genres: History, Drama, Western
Director: Elia Kazan
Main Cast: Marlon Brando, Jean Peters, Anthony Quinn, Joseph Wiseman, Arnold Moss
Keywords: usa president, mexican revolution, presidential election, peasant

Overview: The story of Mexican revolutionary Emiliano Zapata, who led a rebellion against the corrupt, oppressive dictatorship of president Porfirio Diaz in the early 20th century.
