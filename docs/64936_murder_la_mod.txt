Title: Murder à la Mod (1968)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy, Crime, Mystery
Director: Brian De Palma
Main Cast: Andra Akers, William Finley, Margo Norton, Jared Martin, Ken Burrows
Keywords: film maker, murder, violence

Overview: Naive young lady Karen wants to help her struggling amateur filmmaker boyfriend Christopher raise enough money so he can divorce his wife. Meanwhile, jolly psycho prankster Otto stalks the building where Christopher is shooting a low-grade adult movie in order to keep himself afloat.
