Title: House of Clocks (1989)
Genres: Horror, Foreign
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Keith Van Hoven, Karina Huff, Paolo Paoloni, Bettine Milne, Peter Hintz
Keywords: time, zombie, home invasion

Overview: A gang of vicious young punks break into the house of an elderly couple, terrorize, and then murder them. Suddenly, the clocks in the house begin to run backwards, and the dead come back for revenge!
