Title: Dead Time (2007)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Joko Anwar
Main Cast: Fachry Albar, Donny Alamsyah, Ario Bayu, Shanty, Fahrani
Keywords: journalist, cop

Overview: A nameless country is in a state of chaos as natural disasters, corruption, and street justice rise. Part of the people grow more violent, the other part hope for the arrival of a promised leader.
