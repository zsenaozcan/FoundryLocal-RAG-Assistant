Title: It Rains in My Village (1968)
Genres: Music, Drama, Foreign
Director: Aleksandar Petrović
Main Cast: Annie Girardot, Ivan Palúch, Mija Aleksić, Eva Ras, Dragomir 'Gidra' Bojanić

Overview: A bizarre and tragic love story involving swineherd, village fool, teacher and an agricultural pilot. The story unfolds in a remote village in the communist ruled Yugoslavia at the down of Soviet occupation of Czechoslovakia in 1968.
