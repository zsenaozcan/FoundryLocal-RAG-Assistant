Title: Frenchmen 2 (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Marc Esposito
Main Cast: Bernard Campan, Gérard Darmon, Jean-Pierre Darroussin, Marc Lavoine, Catherine Wilkening

Overview: Alex, Antoine, Jeff and Manu. Four friends, four years later. Their relationships, friendship, shared secrets, feelings of guilt and their desire to change and improve.
