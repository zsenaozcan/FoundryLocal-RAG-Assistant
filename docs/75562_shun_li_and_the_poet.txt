Title: Shun Li and the Poet (2011)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Andrea Segre
Main Cast: Zhao Tao, Marco Paolini, Roberto Citran, Giuseppe Battiston, Rade Serbedzija

Overview: A study of the friendship between a Chinese woman and a fisherman who came to Italy from Yugoslavia many years ago, who live in a small city-island in the Veneto lagoon.
