Title: Riki-Oh: The Story of Ricky (1991)
Genres: Comedy, Action, Thriller
Director: Lam Nai-Choi
Main Cast: Fan Siu-Wong, Gloria Yip, Yukari Ôshima, Frankie Chin, Kôichi Sugisaki
Keywords: martial arts, violence, held captive, death, animal abuse, knocked out, megalomaniac

Overview: Hard man Ricky is incarcerated in a futuristic prison where ultra-violence is his only means of survival in the corrupt, sadistic system. He must battle his way quite literally 'through' the feared 'gang of four', and undergo multiple tortures before facing the governor in one of the goriest climaxes ever seen.
