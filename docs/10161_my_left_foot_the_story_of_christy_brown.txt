Title: My Left Foot: The Story of Christy Brown (1989)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jim Sheridan
Main Cast: Daniel Day-Lewis, Brenda Fricker, Alison Whelan, Declan Croghan, Eanna MacLiam
Keywords: bodily disabled person, biography, poet, foot, author, irish, working class, disabled, cerebral palsy, flashback

Overview: In this true story told through flashbacks, Christy Brown is born with crippling cerebral palsy into a poor, working-class Irish family. Able only to control movement in his left foot and to speak in guttural sounds, he is mistakenly believed to be retarded for the first ten years of his life.
