Title: Saving Mr. Wu (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Crime
Director: Ding Sheng
Main Cast: Andy Lau, Wang Qian-Yuan, Cai Lu, Liu Ye, Wu Ruo-Fu

Overview: In this suspenseful crime thriller, Mr. Wu, a Hong Kong movie star, is kidnapped by six unpredictable criminals disguised as police officers. The story is based on the 2004 real-life celebrity kidnapping case in China.
