Title: The Spin Kid (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Joe Lee
Main Cast: Blue Cheng-Lung Lan, Nikki Hsin-Ying Hsieh, Po Tai
Keywords: buddhism, techno

Overview: Blending modern techno music with traditional Buddhist ceremonial dance on the big screen, The Spin Kid is one young man's redemptive journey from alleyway punk to dance floor pioneer.
