Title: The Ultimate Warrior (1975)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Robert Clouse
Main Cast: Yul Brynner, Max von Sydow, Joanna Miles, William Smith, Richard Kelton
Keywords: dystopia, apocalypse, zombie, new york city, post nuclear, post apocalypse

Overview: Only a few people still live in New York in 2015. They are organized in gangs with their own turf. One of them is led by Baron, another one by Carrot, and they are constantly at war with each other.
