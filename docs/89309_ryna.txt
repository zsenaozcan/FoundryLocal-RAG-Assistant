Title: Ryna (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ruxandra Zenide
Main Cast: Dorotheea Petre, Valentin Popescu, Nicolae Praida, Mathieu Roze, Aura Călărașu
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In a small village in the Danube Delta, where traditions are confronted with new material purposes, a teenage girl is becoming a woman. This is the story of Ryna, a 16 year old girl, working as an motor mechanic in Sulina, who strives to find her identity next to a father who is firmly denying her femininity.
