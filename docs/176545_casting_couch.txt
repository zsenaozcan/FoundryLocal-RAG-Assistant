Title: Casting Couch (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jason Lockhart
Main Cast: Mindy Robinson, Mayra Leal, Jessica Lee Rose, Kayla Collins, Chasty Ballesteros

Overview: Desperate to meet new girls, six down-on-their-luck guys come up with the ultimate plan to hook up - cast a fake movie. When tons of hot chicks show up for the audition, it's a matter of who's willing to go the farthest to get the part.
