Title: Hundhotellet (2000)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Mystery
Director: Per Åhlin
Main Cast: Hans Alfredson

Overview: On their way to find Paris, Sture the dog (Hasse Alfredsson) and his friend Picasso end up on a hotel in the middle of nowhere. Along with Miss Mops, Sture tries to sniff out who is causing all the strange events of the hotel.
