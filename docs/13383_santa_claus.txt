Title: Santa Claus (1959)
Genres: Drama, Family, Fantasy
Director: René Cardona
Main Cast: José Elías Moreno, Cesáreo Quezadas, José Luis Aguirre, Armando Arriola, Lupita Quezadas
Keywords: mexico, santa claus, children, devil, doll
