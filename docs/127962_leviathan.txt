Title: Leviathan (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Lucien Castaing-Taylor
Main Cast: Declan Conneely, Johnny Gatcombe, Adrian Guillette, Brian Jannelle, Clyde Lee
Keywords: fishing, atlantic ocean, fisherman, woman director, commercial fishing

Overview: Set aboard a hulking fishing vessel as it navigates the treacherous waves off the New England coast. The very waters that once inspired Moby Dick, the film captures the harsh, unforgiving world of the fishermen in starkly haunting, yet beautiful detail.
