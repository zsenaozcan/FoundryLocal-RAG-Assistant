Title: The War Against Mrs. Hadley (1942)
Genres: Drama
Director: Harold S. Bucquet
Main Cast: Edward Arnold, Fay Bainter, Richard Ney, Jean Rogers, Van Johnson
Keywords: society matron

Overview: Wealthy American society matron, Stella Hadley (Fay Bainter) refuses to sacrifice her material comforts to aid the war effort until she realizes that her selfishness is cheating the boys overseas who are fighting for her freedom.
