Title: The Body Farm (2011)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Tara Fitzgerald, Keith Allen, Wunmi Mosaku, Mark Bazeley, Finlay Robertson

Overview: Dr Eve Lockhart is one of Britain's leading forensic pathologists and runs a state-of-the-art forensic research facility called The Body Farm with her business partner Mike Phillips. Theirs is the only facility in the country using real human body-donors to examine the complex and fascinating journey the body goes through as it decays and returns to the earth.
