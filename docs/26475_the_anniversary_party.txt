Title: The Anniversary Party (2001)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Alan Cumming
Main Cast: Alan Cumming, Jennifer Jason Leigh, John Benjamin Hickey, Parker Posey, Phoebe Cates
Keywords: infidelity, ecstasy, party, independent film, woman director

Overview: While celebrating their reconciliation and six years of marriage, the American actress Sally Nash (Jennifer Jason Leigh) and the British novelist Joe Therrian (Alan Cumming) receive their close friends, some colleagues and their next door neighbors in a party. Under the effect of Ecstasy, revelations are disclosed and relationships deteriorate among the group.
