Title: Dead Sea (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Science Fiction, Horror
Director: Brandon Slagle
Main Cast: James Duval, Devanny Pinn, Chanel Ryan, Candace Kita, Tawny Amber Young
Keywords: legend, excavation, artifact, u.s. marine

Overview: A Marine Biologist is assigned to investigate the mysterious deaths of marine life in an inland salt water lake that have been attributed to a creature thought to have been the stuff of legend.
