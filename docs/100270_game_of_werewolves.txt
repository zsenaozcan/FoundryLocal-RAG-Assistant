Title: Game of Werewolves (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Juan Martínez Moreno
Main Cast: Carlos Areces, Luis Zahera, Mabel Rivera, Gorka Otxoa, Secun de la Rosa

Overview: After 15 years, Tomas, a moderately successful writer, returns to his family's ancestral village in Galicia to receive a civic award, and write his next book. Neither of those things happens.
