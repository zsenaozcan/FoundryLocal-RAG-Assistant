Title: Funny Bones (1995)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Peter Chelsom
Main Cast: Jerry Lewis, Oliver Platt, Lee Evans, Leslie Caron, Richard Griffiths

Overview: Tommy Fawkes wants to be a successful comedian but his Las Vegas debut is a failure. He goes back to Blackpool, UK, where his father, also a comedian started and where he spent the summers of his childhood. He starts to search for a partner, a comic relief, with whom he can be famous.
