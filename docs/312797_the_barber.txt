Title: The Barber (2015)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Basel Owies
Main Cast: Scott Glenn, Stephen Tobolowsky, Kristen Hager, Chris Coy, Max Arciniega
Keywords: obsession, barber

Overview: Eugene is a small town barber, beloved by the local community, but hiding a deadly secret. John is hunting for a serial killer not to expose him but to learn the business! They form a twisted bond and turn the town upside down as Eugene teaches John to kill.
