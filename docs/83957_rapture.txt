Title: Rapture (1965)
Genres: Drama
Director: John Guillermin
Main Cast: Melvyn Douglas, Patricia Gozzi, Dean Stockwell, Gunnel Lindblom, Leslie Sands

Overview: Agnes, a lonely teenage girl, and her father befriend an escaped convict, named Joseph, who arrives at their farm in Brittany, France. When Joseph develops an attraction to Agnes, her father threatens to break up the union.
