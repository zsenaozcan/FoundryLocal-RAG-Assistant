Title: Cardcaptor Sakura: The Sealed Card (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Animation, Adventure, Fantasy, Romance
Director: Morio Asaka
Main Cast: Sakura Tange, Kumai Motoko, Aya Hisakawa, Masaya Onosaka, Megumi Ogata
Keywords: japan, elementary school, sixth grader, card, video tape

Overview: All of the Clow Cards have been captured, and Sakura Kinomoto, the new Master of the Cards, is preparing to play the lead in the play for the town festival. However, a new evil force is causing mysterious events all over Tomoeda, including the disappearance of Sakura's cards. With Syaoran's help, Sakura must figure out the cause of these events, and save her town.
