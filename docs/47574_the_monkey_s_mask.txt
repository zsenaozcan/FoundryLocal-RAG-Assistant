Title: The Monkey's Mask (2000)
Genres: Drama
Director: Samantha Lang
Main Cast: Susie Porter, Kelly McGillis, Abbie Cornish, John Noble, Billy Mitchell
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The Monkey's Mask is a 2000 thriller film directed by Samantha Lang. It stars Susie Porter and Kelly McGillis. Porter plays a lesbian private detective who falls in love with a suspect (McGillis) in the disappearance of a young woman. The film is based on the verse novel of the same name by Australian poet Dorothy Porter.
