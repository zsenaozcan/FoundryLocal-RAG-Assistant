Title: The Last Time (2006)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Michael Caleo
Main Cast: Michael Keaton, Brendan Fraser, Daniel Stern, Amber Valletta, Richard Kuhlman

Overview: Ted Ryker is the top salesman in the New York office of a business machine company; the corporate stock lives by quarterly sales numbers, the competition is keen, and the economy may be in a downturn. Ted's company is marking time until a new product is ready - probably in a few months. Into the mix comes a new hire, a callow Midwesterner named Jamie, who's come East with his fiancée Belisa.
