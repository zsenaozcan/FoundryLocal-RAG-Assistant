Title: The Jack Bull (1999)
Genres: TV Movie, Action, Drama, Western
Director: John Badham
Main Cast: John Cusack, John Goodman, L.Q. Jones, Miranda Otto, John Savage
Keywords: wyoming, justice, horse trader
