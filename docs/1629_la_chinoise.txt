Title: La Chinoise (1967)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jean-Luc Godard
Main Cast: Anne Wiazemsky, Jean-Pierre Léaud, Juliet Berto, Michel Semeniako, Lex De Bruijn
Keywords: paris, roommate, discussion, politics, independent film, communism, may 68
