Title: Palme (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Kristina Lindström
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: sweden, society, politics, democracy, woman director

Overview: Olof Palme was openly shot to death on a February evening 1986 on the streets of Stockholm. In one night, the country of Sweden was transfigured. “Palme” is about his life, his time, and about the Sweden he had created. About a man who altered history.
