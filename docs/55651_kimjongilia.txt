Title: Kimjongilia (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: N.C. Heikin
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: North Korea is one of the world's most isolated nations. For sixty years, North Koreans have been governed by a totalitarian regime that controls all information entering and leaving the country. A cult of personality surrounds its two recent leaders: first, Kim Il Sung, and now his son, Kim Jong Il...
