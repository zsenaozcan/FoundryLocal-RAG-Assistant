Title: The Pretty One (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jenée LaMarque
Main Cast: Zoe Kazan, Jake Johnson, Ron Livingston, Danny Pudi, Sterling Beaumon
Keywords: woman director

Overview: When her idolized twin sister Audrey dies in a car accident, Laurel decides to take her place. But, as she eases into the life she has always wanted, she must decide between continuing the lie or revealing herself.
