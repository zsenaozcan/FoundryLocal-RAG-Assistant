Title: Prospect (2014)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Christopher Caldwell
Main Cast: Thomas Castle, Tony Doupe, Callie Harlow, Joshua Miles
Keywords: space suit, alien planet, father daughter relationship, natural resources

Overview: Prospect is the unusual coming-of-age story of a teenage girl on a toxic alien planet. She and her father hunt for precious materials aiming to strike it rich. When the father is attacked by a roving bandit, the daughter must take control.
