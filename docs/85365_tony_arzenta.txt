Title: Tony Arzenta (1973)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Duccio Tessari
Main Cast: Alain Delon, Roger Hanin, Richard Conte, Carla Gravina, Marc Porel
Keywords: torino, poliziotteschi

Overview: A mob hitman wants to retire, but his bosses don't think that's a good idea. Complications--and many bloody shootouts-ensue.
