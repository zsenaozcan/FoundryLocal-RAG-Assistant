Title: My Beloved Bodyguard (2016)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Sammo Hung
Main Cast: Sammo Hung, Jacqueline Chan, Andy Lau, Feng Jia-Yi, Li Qinqin

Overview: A retired bodyguard who has settled into a corner of the world where China, Russia and North Korea meet as he suffers from early dementia finds a new friend in a young girl. When her life is threatened by her father's connection with a local crime lord, .the bodyguard must call upon his long forgotten skills to save her.
