Title: Jennifer Eight (1992)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery
Director: Bruce Robinson
Main Cast: Andy García, Lance Henriksen, Uma Thurman, Graham Beckel, Kathy Baker
Keywords: fight, blindness and impaired vision, victim, suspense, serial killer, falling in love, series of murders, police officer, surveillance

Overview: A big city cop from LA moves to a small town police force and immediately finds himself investigating a murder. Using theories rejected by his colleagues, the cop, John Berlin, meets a young blind woman named Helena, who he is attracted to. Meanwhile, a serial killer is on the loose and only John knows it.
