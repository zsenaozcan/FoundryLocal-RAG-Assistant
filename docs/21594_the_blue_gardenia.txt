Title: The Blue Gardenia (1953)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Fritz Lang
Main Cast: Anne Baxter, Richard Conte, Ann Sothern, Raymond Burr, Jeff Donnell
Keywords: classic noir

Overview: Norah Larkin is a single woman whose heart is broken after receiving a "Dear John" letter. After a night of drinking she passes out in the apartment of womaniser Harry Prebble, awaking to find herself accused of his murder.
