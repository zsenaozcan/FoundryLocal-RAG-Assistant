Title: The Ladies Man (1961)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jerry Lewis
Main Cast: Jerry Lewis, Helen Traubel, Kathleen Freeman, Pat Stanley, George Raft
Keywords: slapstick, comedy, madcap comedy

Overview: After his girl leaves him for someone else, Herbert gets really depressed and starts searching for a job. He finally finds one in a big house which is inhabited by many, many women. Can he live in the same home with all these females?
