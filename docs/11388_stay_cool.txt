Title: Stay Cool (2009)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michael Polish
Main Cast: Winona Ryder, Chevy Chase, Sean Astin, Mark Polish, Jon Cryer
Keywords: independent film, writer, hometown

Overview: The film centers on a successful author who is forced to confront an unrequited high school crush when he returns home to deliver a commencement address to graduating seniors. Shasta O'Neil, a sexy high school senior flirts with the visiting author and invites him to the prom.
