Title: Falling Up (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: David M. Rosenthal
Main Cast: Joseph Cross, Sarah Roemer, Snoop Dogg, Rachael Leigh Cook, Claudette Lali
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A nursing student (Cross) forced to quit school for family reasons winds up taking a job as a doorman in an elite apartment building in New York City, where he sparks to one of his residents (Roemer).
