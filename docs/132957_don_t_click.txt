Title: Don't Click (2012)
Genres: Horror, Crime, Mystery
Director: Kim Tae-kyung
Main Cast: Park Bo-young, Joo Won, Ko Won-hee, Kang Byul

Overview: A horror movie about the mysterious happenings that happen to a pair of sisters after watching an unidentified video. Directed by Kim Tae-kyeong, Park Bo-yeong plays Se-hee who tries to save her sister Jeong-mi, her boyfriend Joo Won and Kang Byeol plays the sister.
