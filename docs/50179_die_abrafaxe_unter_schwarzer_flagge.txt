Title: Die Abrafaxe - Unter schwarzer Flagge (2001)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Comedy, Science Fiction, Family
Director: Gerhard Hahn
Main Cast: Ilona Schulz, David Turba, Kim Hasper, Nena Kerner, Helmut Krauss

Overview: The Pirates Of Tortuga :Under The Black Flag Set sail with three young boys on an adventure where time travel, gold treasure and battling villainous pirates are all in a day’s work! On a trip to the city museum, three young boys, Alex, Max and Califax discover a golden bowl — part of a legendary Aztec treasure.
