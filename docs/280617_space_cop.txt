Title: Space Cop (2016)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Jay Bauman
Main Cast: Rich Evans, Mike Stoklasa, Jocelyn Ridgely, Chike Johnson, Jay Bauman
Keywords: time travel, satire, space, alien, spoof, police officer, ironic

Overview: Space Cop is the story of a cop from the future of space who travels back in time to the present and is teamed up with a cop from the past who is unfrozen in the present. Together, they must defeat evil aliens with a sinister plan. Out of time and out of place, these two unwitting heroes must work together to save the world from a group of renegade aliens and the re-animated brain of a mad scientist bent on global extinction.
