Title: Abraham Lincoln vs. Zombies (2012)
Genres: Horror, Action, Thriller
Director: Richard Schenkman
Main Cast: Bill Oberst Jr., Baby Norman, Jason Vail, Jason Hughley, Don McGraw
Keywords: usa president, president, thriller, zombie, horror movie

Overview: As Abraham Lincoln labors over the Gettysburg address, the importance of which he is fully aware, he learns that a menace from his past has returned, threatening to tear the already fractured nation to pieces. He must journey behind enemy lines to face an foe far more fearsome than the Confederate army: the walking dead.
