Title: Wet Dreams (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nicholas Ray
Main Cast: Melvin Miracle
Keywords: sex

Overview: Experimental anthology film consisting of nine segments - Contrasts, The Janitor, The Plumber, Another Wet Dream, The Happy Necrophiliacs, On a Sunday Afternoon, A Face, Politfuck, Flames - all focused on 70s sex, love and politics.
