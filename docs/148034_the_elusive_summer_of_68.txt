Title: The Elusive Summer of '68 (1984)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Goran Paskaljević
Main Cast: Slavko Štimac, Danilo 'Bata' Stojković, Mira Banjac, Mija Aleksić, Neda Arnerić

Overview: For the young man who lives in Serbian province town, the maturing coincides with the turbulent political events of the year 1968.
