Title: A Tale of Lost Times (1964)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Family
Director: Aleksandr Ptushko
Main Cast: Oleg Anofriev, Savely Kramarov, Rina Zelyonaya, Lyudmila Shagalova, Sergei Martinson

Overview: Four evil wizards succeeded to become young by using a time stolen from lazy kids. Now these kids who became seniors must act fast if they want to return to their real age.
