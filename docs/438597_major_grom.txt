Title: Major Grom (2017)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Vladimir Besedin
Main Cast: Aleksandr Gorbatov, Ivan Fominov, Anton Kuznetsov, Danila Yakushev, Oysel More Despaygne

Overview: Igor Grom, a skilled detective from St. Petersburg, happened to be at the scene of a bank robbery and decides to stop them.
