Title: Flesh and Blood (1922)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Irving Cummings
Main Cast: Lon Chaney, Edith Roberts, Noah Beery, DeWitt Jennings, Ralph Lewis

Overview: A convict hiding in Chinatown assumes the identity of a cripple to track down a businessman who framed him 15 years previously. He discovers that his daughter has fallen in love with the businessman's son.
