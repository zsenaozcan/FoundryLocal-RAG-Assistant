Title: Going to Kansas City (1998)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Pekka Mandart
Main Cast: Mikko Nousiainen, Melissa Galianos, Michael Ironside, Susan Almgren, Daniel Pilon

Overview: An exchange student from Finland arrives in Kansas City. He is expecting to spend a year in the city, but ends up living on a farm in a small town. He falls in love with the sheriff’s daughter and soon finds himself in trouble.
