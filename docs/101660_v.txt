Title: V (1983)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction, Action, Adventure
Director: Kenneth Johnson
Main Cast: Marc Singer, Faye Grant, Jane Badler, Michael Durrell, Blair Tefkin
Keywords: resistance, spacecraft, cover-up, extraterrestrial, mother ship, reptilian

Overview: Aliens pretending to be friendly come to Earth and are received openly. The aliens have masqueraded themselves to look just like humans. When it is discovered that the aliens' planet is dying and that they have come to rape the Earth of its natural resources, the war for Earth begins.
