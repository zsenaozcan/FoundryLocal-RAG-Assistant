Title: Kevin Smith: Burn in Hell (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Zak Knutson
Main Cast: Kevin Smith
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: In this hour and a half special, writer/director/comedian/podcaster Kevin Smith conducts a riotous and outrageous Q&amp;A session about the aftermath of his latest film, Red State. Topics include such misadventures as his pissing off the entire movie blogger community at the Sundance Film Festival and being followed around the country by the highly devout, and highly angered, Phelps Family.
