Title: Freedom Strike (1998)
Genres: Action
Director: Jerry P. Jacobs
Main Cast: Michael Dudikoff, Tone Loc, Felicity Waterman, Jay Anthony, Nicolas Coster

Overview: When a peace treaty between America and Syria is thwarted, the renegade head of the Syrian army retaliates in a major way. He steals a nuclear weapon that only the Freedom Strike team can stop! Cue an action-packed race against time to save the world.
