Title: The Strange Affair of Uncle Harry (1945)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Robert Siodmak
Main Cast: George Sanders, Geraldine Fitzgerald, Ella Raines, Sara Allgood, Moyna MacGill
Keywords: melodrama, b movie, film noir

Overview: George Sanders stars in this engrossing melodrama about a very domineering sister who holds a tight grip on her brother -- especially when he shows signs of falling in love.
