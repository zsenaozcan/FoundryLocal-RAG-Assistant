Title: Desperate Journey (1942)
Genres: Action, Drama, War
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: Errol Flynn, Ronald Reagan, Raymond Massey, Alan Hale, Nancy Coleman
Keywords: germany, factory, escape, bombing

Overview: When Flight Lt Forbes and his crew are shot down after bombing their target, they discover valuable information, about a hidden German aircraft factory, that must get back to England. In their way across Germany, they try and cause as much damage as possible. Then with the chasing Germans about to pounce, they come up with an ingenious plan to escape.
