Title: Deadly Chase (1978)
Genres: Crime
Director: Franco Prosperi
Main Cast: Luc Merenda, Janet Ågren, Patrizia Gori, Luciana Paluzzi, Chris Avram

Overview: Police Commissioner Verrazzano (Luc Merenda) is visited by charming Giulia Medici (Janet Agren) who claims that her brother, banker Valter Medici didn't commit suicide but was murdered. The investigation leads Verrazzano to dark secrets of loan sharks.
