Title: The Foundling (1939)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Tatyana Lukashevich
Main Cast: Veronika Lebedeva, Faina Ranevskaya, Pyotr Repnin, Rostislav Plyatt, Rina Zelyonaya

Overview: A little girl is lost in Moscow and hits the road making fun (not intentionally) of everybody she meets. She'll be back home soon but she will change the life of at least one man forever...
