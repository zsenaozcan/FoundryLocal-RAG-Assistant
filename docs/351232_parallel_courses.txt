Title: Parallel Courses (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rafael Montero
Main Cast: Ludwika Paleta, Iliana Fox, Fernanda Castillo, Michel Brown, Arturo Barba

Overview: Gaby, Fer's mother and Silvia, Diego's mother, live their lives happily, until one day, due to Diego's kidney disease they find out that the kids were switched at birth. In order to save Diego’, both mothers end up in a legal battle to see who will have the custody of which kid.
