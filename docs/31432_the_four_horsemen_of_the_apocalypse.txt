Title: The Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse (1921)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Rex Ingram
Main Cast: Rudolph Valentino, Pomeroy Cannon, Josef Swickard, Bridgetta Clark, Virginia Warwick
Keywords: infidelity, silent film, firing squad

Overview: Set immediately before World War I, it deals with a rich Argentine family, one branch recently arrived from France, the other from Germany.
