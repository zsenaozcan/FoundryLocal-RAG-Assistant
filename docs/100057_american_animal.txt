Title: American Animal (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Matt D'Elia
Main Cast: Brendan Fletcher, Mircea Monroe, Angela Sarafyan, Matt D'Elia

Overview: When Jimmy (Matt D'Elia) finds out his best friend and roommate (Fletcher) is leaving, he sees this as a betrayal of their perfect way of life. Over the course of a night full of drinks, drugs and women, the two men engage in a classic battle of wills as James prepares to enter the real world and Jimmy falls deeper and deeper into his world of isolation and make-believe.
