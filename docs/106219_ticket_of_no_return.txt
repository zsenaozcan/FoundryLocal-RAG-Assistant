Title: Ticket of No Return (1979)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ulrike Ottinger
Main Cast: Tabea Blumenschein , Lutze , Orpha Termin , Monika von Cube, Nina Hagen
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A sartorially resplendent woman arrives in Berlin with plans to live out the rest of her days as a drunkard in this experimental film. The film provides the viewer with a tour of the city and some of the more colorful characters, but the appeal may be limited to those who are mavens of the experimental style.
