Title: Somewhere in the Night (1946)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Joseph L. Mankiewicz
Main Cast: John Hodiak, Nancy Guild, Lloyd Nolan, Richard Conte, Josephine Hutchinson
Keywords: amnesia, film noir

Overview: George Taylor returns from the WWII with amnesia. Back home in Los Angeles, he tries to track down his old identity, stumbling into a 3-year old murder case and a hunt for a missing $2 million.
