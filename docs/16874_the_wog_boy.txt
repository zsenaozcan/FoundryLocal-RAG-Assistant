Title: The Wog Boy (2000)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Aleksi Vellis
Main Cast: Nick Giannopoulos, Lucy Bell, Vince Colosimo, Abi Tucker, Geraldine Turner
Keywords: australian, cult comedy

Overview: Steve liked Celia from the moment they met. But following a clash with her boss, Raelene Beagle-Thorpe, Minister for Employment, he finds himself on national television branded as Australia's biggest dole bludger. Now Steve has to prove to Celia, to himself, and to the whole country, that there's more to him than meets the eye. With a little help from his friends, he might just do it.
