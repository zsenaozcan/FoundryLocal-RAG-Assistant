Title: Len and Company (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Tim Godsall
Main Cast: Rhys Ifans, Juno Temple, Jack Kilmer, Kathryn Hahn, Keir Gilchrist
Keywords: music business, illegal drugs, divorced parents, estranged son

Overview: A successful music producer quits the industry and exiles himself in upstate New York, but the solitude he seeks is shattered when his estranged son and the pop star he's created come looking for answers.
