Title: The Adventures of Barry McKenzie (1972)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Bruce Beresford
Main Cast: Barry Crocker, Barry Humphries, Peter Cook, Paul Bertram

Overview: Barry McKenzie sets off for England with his aunt, Edna Everage, to advance his cultural education. Bazza is an innocent abroad, fond of beer, Bondi and beautiful sheilas, but he soon settles into the Australian ghetto in Earls Court, where his old mate Curly has a flat.
