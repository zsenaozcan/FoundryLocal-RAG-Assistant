Title: Blockade (1938)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: William Dieterle
Main Cast: Madeleine Carroll, Henry Fonda, Leo Carrillo, John Halliday, Vladimir Sokoloff
Keywords: spanish civil war

Overview: A simple peasant is forced to take up arms to defend his farm during the Spanish Civil War. Along the way he falls in love with Russian whose father is involved in espionage.
