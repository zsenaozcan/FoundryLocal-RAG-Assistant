Title: Absent (2011)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Marco Berger
Main Cast: Carlos Echevarría, Javier De Pietro, Antonella Costa, Alejandro Barbero, Rocío Pavón
Keywords: obsession, crush, unrequited love, gay interest, argentina, falling from height, lgbt

Overview: Sebastiano teaches high school gym classes and coaches swimming. He’s got a job, a girlfriend, and a pretty normal life. But as young Martin starts to infiltrate Sebastiano’s life, his world will get turned upside down. Eerie from the first frame, this Hitchcockian thriller portrays sexual obsession with terrifying realism.
