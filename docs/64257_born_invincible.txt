Title: Born Invincible (1978)
Genres: Foreign, Action
Director: Joseph Kuo
Main Cast: Carter Wong, Lo Lieh, Mark Lung, Jack Lung, Nancy Yen Nan-See

Overview: A very arrogant white haired Thai Chi martial artist and two of his cronies wreaks havoc in a small village, terrorizing people and their families. Three local heroes team up to defeat the villainous three, but they have to find a secret weak point, which the Thai Chi master can choose and change at will.
