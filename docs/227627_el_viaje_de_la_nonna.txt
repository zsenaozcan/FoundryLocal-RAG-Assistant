Title: El Viaje de la Nonna (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Batan Silva
Main Cast: Ana Ofelia Murguía, Verónica Langer, Rodrigo Murray, Julio Bracho, Ximena Ayala
Keywords: italy, mexico, travel

Overview: A Mexican family, wanting to grant their Grandmother her last wish, embarks on a make believe trip to a small town in Italy ... in Mexico!
