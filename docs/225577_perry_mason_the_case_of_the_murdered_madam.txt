Title: Perry Mason: The Case of the Murdered Madam (1987)
Genres: Mystery
Director: Ron Satlof
Main Cast: Raymond Burr, Barbara Hale, William Katt, Daphne Ashbrook, John Rhys-Davies
Keywords: detective, murder, courtroom

Overview: Perry investigates the murder of a former madam and uncovers a multimillion dollar banking fraud in the process.
