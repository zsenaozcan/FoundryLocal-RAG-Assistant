Title: Juliet of the Spirits (1965)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Federico Fellini
Main Cast: Giulietta Masina, Sandra Milo, Mario Pisu, Valentina Cortese, Valeska Gert
Keywords: eccentric, memory, middle aged woman, psychic

Overview: Visions, memories, and mysticism all help a 40-something woman to find the strength to leave her cheating husband.
