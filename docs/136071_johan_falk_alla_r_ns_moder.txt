Title: Johan Falk: Alla råns moder (2012)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Anders Nilsson
Main Cast: Jakob Eklund, Mikael Tornving, Jens Hultén, Joel Kinnaman, Meliz Karlge

Overview: GSI second in command, Sophie Nordh, flips gangster leader Seth Rydell and convinces him to infiltrate a rogue military group. For the first time GSI Chief Patrick Agrell realizes that he is using a ruthless psychopath as an infiltrator. Can Seth be trusted or does he have his own agenda?
