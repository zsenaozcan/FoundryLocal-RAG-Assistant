Title: The Daisy Chain (2008)
Genres: Thriller, Foreign
Director: Aisling Walsh
Main Cast: Samantha Morton, Steven Mackintosh, Mhairi Anderson, David Bradley, Eva Birthistle
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A grieving couple move to a remote Irish village in the wake of their baby daughter's death. They soon take in an orphaned autistic girl, only to become involved in a series of strange occurrences.
