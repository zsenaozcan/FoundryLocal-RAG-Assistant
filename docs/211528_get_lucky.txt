Title: Get Lucky (2013)
Genres: Action
Director: Sacha Bennett
Main Cast: Luke Treadaway, Emily Atack, Craig Fairbrass, James Cosmo, Terry Stone

Overview: Lucky is a small time criminal and he's happy just getting by until he agrees to look after some counterfeit money for a friend who is in a bit of trouble with the law Two months go by without a word from him so Lucky burns the 'funny money' to avoid problems for himself.
