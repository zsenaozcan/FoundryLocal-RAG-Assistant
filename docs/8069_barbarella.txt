Title: Barbarella (1968)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Roger Vadim
Main Cast: Jane Fonda, John Phillip Law, Anita Pallenberg, Marcel Marceau, Claude Dauphin
Keywords: sexual fantasy, alien planet, distant future, cult classic, female mercenary

Overview: In the far future, a highly sexual woman is tasked with finding and stopping the evil Durand-Durand. Along the way she encounters various unusual people.
