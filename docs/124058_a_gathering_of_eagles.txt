Title: A Gathering of Eagles (1963)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Delbert Mann
Main Cast: Rock Hudson, Rod Taylor, Mary Peach, Barry Sullivan, Kevin McCarthy
Keywords: u.s. air force

Overview: Rock Hudson plays an Air Force Colonel who has just been re-assigned as a cold war B-52 commander who must shape up his men to pass a grueling inspection that the previous commander had failed, and had been fired for. He is also recently married, and as a tough commanding officer doing whatever he has to do to shape his men up, his wife sees a side to him that she hadn't seen before.
