Title: Snuff 102 (2007)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Mariano Peralta
Main Cast: Yamila Greco, Eduardo Poli, Andrea Alfonso, Silvia Paz, Rodrigo Bianco

Overview: A young Argentinian reporter is writing an article about snuff films - which depict real torture, rape and murder of people. But seeking the films and those behind them might backfire. She herself is in danger of becoming one of the suffering.
