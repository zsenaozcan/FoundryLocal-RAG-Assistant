Title: Çalgı Çengi (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Selçuk Aydemir
Main Cast: Ahmet Kural, Murat Cemcir, Erdal Tosun, Tuna Orhan, Hazal Kaya

Overview: Salih and Gürkan, two cousins from Ankara, are struggling musicians that are trying to make a living by performing at private events such as bachelorette parties. Nihat, a low profile event manager, finds a gig for the duo which leads them to encounter with unexpected guests in their "dressing room".
