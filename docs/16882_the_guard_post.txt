Title: The Guard Post (2008)
Genres: Horror, Mystery
Director: Su-chang Kong
Main Cast: Young-hoon Lee, Jeong-heon Lee, Cheon Ho-jin, Park Won-sang, Cho Jin-woong
Keywords: videoband, army

Overview: The plot concerns Guard Point 506, where an entire squad of soldiers have been killed in mysterious circumstances, bar one survivor who's in a coma. Military Investigators are dispatched to the base, and discover they're dealing with a virus that turns humans into ravenous flesh-eating killers!
