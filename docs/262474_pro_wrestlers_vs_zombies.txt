Title: Pro Wrestlers vs Zombies (2014)
Genres: Horror, Action, Comedy
Director: Cody Knotts
Main Cast: Roddy Piper, Jim Duggan, Matt Hardy, Kurt Angle, Adrienne Fischer
Keywords: wrestler, zombie

Overview: The Franchise Shane Douglas accidentally kills a wrestler in the ring. The wrestler's brother Angus seeks revenge by surrendering his soul to an ancient demon to gain the power to raise the undead. Angus then pays for a private show at an empty prison to lure Douglas and his friend Rowdy Roddy Piper into a death trap.
