Title: All Good Things (2010)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller, Crime, Romance
Director: Andrew Jarecki
Main Cast: Ryan Gosling, Kirsten Dunst, Frank Langella, Lily Rabe, Philip Baker Hall
Keywords: difficult childhood, patriarch, childhood trauma, dysfunctional marriage

Overview: Newly-discovered facts, court records and speculation are used to elaborate the true love story and murder mystery of the most notorious unsolved murder case in New York history.
