Title: Three Plus Two (1963)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Genrikh Oganisyan
Main Cast: Natalya Kustinskaya, Natalya Fateeva, Andrey Mironov, Yevgeni Zharikov, Gennadi Nilov

Overview: Three best friend are going to spend a vacation on a sea shore. But when they arrive to their usual place they suddenly find that the spot is already occupied - by two beautiful girls.
