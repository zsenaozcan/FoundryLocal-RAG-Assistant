Title: Two Heads Are Better Than None (2000)
Genres: Family, Comedy
Director: Michael Grossman
Main Cast: Kenan Thompson, Kel Mitchell, Milton Berle, Time Winters, Bonnie Hellman

Overview: Two Heads Are Better Than None also known as Kenan &amp; Kel: The Movie is a 2000 American made-for-television film that stars the cast of the Nickelodeon sitcom Kenan &amp; Kel. It is the last feature film of comic legend Milton Berle.
