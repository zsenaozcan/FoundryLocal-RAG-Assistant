Title: Åke and His World (1984)
Genres: Drama
Director: Allan Edwall
Main Cast: Martin Lindström, Loa Falkman, Gunnel Fred, Katja Blomquist, Ulla Sjöblom

Overview: Aake and his world is a long, lyrical study of a Swedish country doctor of the 1930s. Aake is the doctor's six year old son, from whose point of view the film is told. His best friend is Kalle Nubb. Aake is very frightened of the janitor Bergstroem as well as the lunatic Anne-Marie.
