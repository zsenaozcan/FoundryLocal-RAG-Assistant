Title: Joseph: King of Dreams (2000)
Genres: Music, Family, Animation
Director: Rob LaDuca
Main Cast: Ben Affleck, Mark Hamill, Richard Herd, Maureen McGovern, Jodi Benson
Keywords: bible, faith, christian film, ancient egypt

Overview: In this animated retelling of the story from the Bible's Book of Genesis, Joseph's gift of dream interpretation and his brilliantly colored coat inspires jealousy in his brothers.
