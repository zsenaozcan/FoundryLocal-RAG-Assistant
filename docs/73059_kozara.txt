Title: Kozara (1962)
Genres: War
Director: Veljko Bulajić
Main Cast: Bert Sotlar, Velimir „Bata“ Živojinović, Milena Dravić, Olivera Marković, Dragomir Felba
Keywords: world war ii, partisan

Overview: In June of 1942 Germans and their collaborators decide to get rid of partisans and their stronghold in the woods of Mount Kozara in Northern Bosnia. They encircle the mountain and begin the mop up operation. Out gunned and outnumbered the partisans must not only take care of themselves but try to protect thousands of refugees too.
