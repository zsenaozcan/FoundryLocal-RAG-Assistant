Title: Ten (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: Abbas Kiarostami
Main Cast: Mania Akbari, Amin Maher, Kamran Adl, Roya Arabshahi
Keywords: sister sister relationship, prayer, divorcee, illegal prostitution

Overview: A visual social examination in the form of ten conversations between a driving woman and her various pick-ups and hitchhikers.
