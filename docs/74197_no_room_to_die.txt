Title: No Room To Die (1969)
Genres: Western
Director: Sergio Garrone
Main Cast: Anthony Steffen, William Berger, Mario Brega, Nicoletta Machiavelli, Riccardo Garrone
Keywords: spaghetti western

Overview: Mexican's are being smuggled over the border to work as cheap labour for wealthy land baron Fargo. Hi's gang is made up of known criminals with bounties on their heads, this greatly interests two bounty hunters who may have to team up to achieve their goals.
