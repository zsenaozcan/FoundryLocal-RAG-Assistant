Title: What Ever Happened to Baby Jane? (1962)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Robert Aldrich
Main Cast: Bette Davis, Joan Crawford, Victor Buono, Wesley Addy, Julie Allred
Keywords: based on novel, sister sister relationship, aging, insanity, murder, hollywood, mental illness, invalid, vaudeville, recluse

Overview: Two aging film actresses live as virtual recluses in an old Hollywood mansion. Jane Hudson, a successful child star, cares for her crippled sister Blanche, whose career in later years eclipsed that of Jane. Now the two live together, their relationship affected by simmering subconscious thoughts of mutual envy, hate and revenge.
