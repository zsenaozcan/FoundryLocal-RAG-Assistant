Title: Shocker (1989)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy, Horror
Director: Wes Craven
Main Cast: Michael Murphy, Mitch Pileggi, Peter Berg, Camille Cooper, Sam Scarber
Keywords: terror, sadistic, detective, blackout, psychopath, murder, independent film, gore, capital punishment, slasher

Overview: After being sent to the electric chair, a serial killer uses electricity to come back from the dead and carry out his vengeance on the football player who turned him in to the police.
