Title: Pharaoh (1966)
Genres: History
Director: Jerzy Kawalerowicz
Main Cast: Jerzy Zelnik, Barbara Brylska, Krystyna Mikołajewská, Ewa Krzyżewska, Wiesława Mazurkiewicz
Keywords: insurrection, pharao

Overview: Young Pharaoh Ramses XIII clashes with Egypt's clergy over influence on the affairs of the state and its coffers.
