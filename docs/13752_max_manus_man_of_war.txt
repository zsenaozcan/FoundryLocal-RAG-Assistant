Title: Max Manus: Man of War (2008)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Joachim Rønning
Main Cast: Aksel Hennie, Agnes Kittelsen, Nicolai Cleve Broch, Knut Joner, Christian Rubeck
Keywords: bomb, resistance, norway, world war ii, nazis, trauma, norwegian, saboteur, idealism, escape

Overview: Max Manus is a Norwegian 2008 biographic war film based on the real events of the life of resistance fighter Max Manus (1914–96), after his contribution in the Winter War against the Soviet Union. The story follows Manus through the outbreak of World War II in Norway until peacetime in 1945.
