Title: Flying (1986)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Paul Lynch
Main Cast: Olivia d'Abo, Keanu Reeves, Rita Tushingham, Jessica Steen, Sean McCann
Keywords: gymnastics

Overview: TV-generation feel good story about a teenage gymnast aiming for the championship despite an abusive stepfather, an ailing mother, and injury. Reeves is notable as the supportive boyfriend.
