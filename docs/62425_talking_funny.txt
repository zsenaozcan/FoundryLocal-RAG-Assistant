Title: Talking Funny (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: John Moffitt
Main Cast: Jerry Seinfeld, Louis C.K., Chris Rock, Ricky Gervais

Overview: What does it mean to be a successful comedian? How far can the boundaries of taste be pushed to get a laugh? Four top comics, Ricky Gervais, Louis C.K., Jerry Seinfeld and Chris Rock, discuss and debate their craft.
