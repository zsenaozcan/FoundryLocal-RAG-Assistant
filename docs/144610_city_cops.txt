Title: City Cops (1989)
Genres: Action
Director: Lau Kar-Wing
Main Cast: Cynthia Rothrock, Kiu Wai Miu, Shing Fui-On, Suki Kwan, Michiko Nishiwaki
Keywords: martial arts, kung fu, police, mafia, triad

Overview: Cindy is an American FBI agent who is currently in Hong Kong seeking arch-criminal Kent Tong. In order to bring down Tong and his criminal organization, she teams up with local HK cops David and Johnny.
