Title: Lourdes (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jessica Hausner
Main Cast: Sylvie Testud, Léa Seydoux, Elina Löwensohn, Bruno Todeschini, Gilette Barbier
Keywords: miracle, pyrenees, lourdes france, envy, silence, woman director

Overview: In order to escape her isolation, wheelchair-bound Christine makes a life changing journey to Lourdes, the iconic site of pilgrimage in the Pyrenees Mountains.
