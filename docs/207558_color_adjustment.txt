Title: Color Adjustment (1992)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Marlon Riggs
Main Cast: Norman Lear, Tim Reid, Daphne Reid
Keywords: 1970s, history, documentary, african american, tv, 1980s, 1950s, 1960s, pbs

Overview: From Amos 'n' Andy to Nat King Cole, from Roots to The Cosby Show, blacks have played many roles on primetime television. Brilliantly weaving clips from classic TV shows with commentary from TV producers, black actors and scholars, Marlon Riggs blends humor, insight, and thoughtful analysis to explore the evolution of black/white relations as reflected by America's favorite addiction.
