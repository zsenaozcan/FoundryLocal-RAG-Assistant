Title: Heroes (2010)
Genres: Drama, Adventure
Director: Pau Freixas
Main Cast: Eva Santolaria, Àlex Brendemühl, Àlex Monner, Ferran Rull, Mireia Vilapuig

Overview: A young publicist, with a successful professional life but with an empty personal life, finds himself on a trip against time to get to an important business meeting. During the trip, he meets a girl and, although they are very different and have opposite ways of life, they click when recalling the most mythical and emotional time of their childhood: the last summer they spent with their gang
