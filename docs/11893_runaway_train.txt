Title: Runaway Train (1985)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Andrei Konchalovsky
Main Cast: Jon Voight, Eric Roberts, Rebecca De Mornay, Kyle T. Heffner, John P. Ryan
Keywords: train accident, attempt to escape, prison warden, train, prison escape

Overview: A hardened convict and a younger prisoner escape from a brutal prison in the middle of winter only to find themselves on an out-of-control train with a female railway worker while being pursued by the vengeful head of security.
