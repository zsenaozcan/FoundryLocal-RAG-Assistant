Title: Clannad: The Motion Picture (2007)
Genres: Animation, Drama
Director: Osamu Dezaki
Main Cast: Kenji Nojima, Mai Nakahara, Ryou Hirohashi, Houko Kuwashima, Daisuke Sakaguchi

Overview: Clannad is set in a high-school located in some Japanese town. Okazaki Tomoya is a third-year student who doesn't take his studies seriously. Always late for class, he's seen as a delinquent by the rest of his classmates who are busy preparing for their entrance examinations. Needless to say, he hasn't too many close friends either.
