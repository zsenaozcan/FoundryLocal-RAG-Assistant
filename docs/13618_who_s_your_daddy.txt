Title: Who's Your Daddy? (2004)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Andy Fickman
Main Cast: Brandon Jack James, Colleen Camp, Kadeem Hardison, Christine Lakin, Ali Landry
Keywords: sex

Overview: An adopted Ohio high school senior discovers he is the inherited heir to a porn empire. Dropped into a bitter power struggle, his new flock of beautiful co-workers come to his aid.
