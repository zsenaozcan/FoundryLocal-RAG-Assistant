Title: Guyana Tragedy: The Story of Jim Jones (1980)
Genres: Drama, TV Movie
Director: William A. Graham
Main Cast: Powers Boothe, Ned Beatty, Irene Cara, Veronica Cartwright, Brad Dourif
Keywords: suicide, poison, commune, cult, jim jones, guyana
