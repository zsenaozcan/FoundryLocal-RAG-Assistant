Title: The Serpent's Egg (1977)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Mystery
Director: Ingmar Bergman
Main Cast: David Carradine, Liv Ullmann, Heinz Bennent, Gert Fröbe, Isolde Barth
Keywords: germany, suicide, circus, suspense

Overview: The Serpent's Egg follows a week in the life of Abel Rosenberg, an out-of-work American circus acrobat living in poverty-stricken Berlin following Germany's defeat in World War I.
