Title: The Spy with Ten Faces (1966)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Alberto De Martino
Main Cast: Paul Hubschmid, Karin Dor, Vivi Bach, Nando Gazzolo, Rosalba Neri
Keywords: spy, eurospy

Overview: Paul Hubsschmid is Paul Finney aka Upperseven, master of disguise. He's out to stop a diamond smuggling operation which fronts for a missile building project.
