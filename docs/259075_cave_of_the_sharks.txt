Title: Cave of the Sharks (1978)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Horror
Director: Tonino Ricci
Main Cast: Andrés García, Janet Ågren, Arthur Kennedy, Pino Colizzi, Máximo Valverde
Keywords: supernatural, smuggling, bermuda triangle, shark, missing person, illegal drugs

Overview: When Andres and his partner are hired to recover some valuables from an airplane that went down in the Bermuda Triangle, they face not only human treachery but also the mysterious powers of an underwater civilization.
