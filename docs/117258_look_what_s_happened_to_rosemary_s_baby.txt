Title: Look What's Happened to Rosemary's Baby (1976)
Genres: Horror, TV Movie
Director: Sam O'Steen
Main Cast: Stephen McHattie, Patty Duke, Broderick Crawford, Ruth Gordon, Lloyd Haynes
Keywords: sequel, occult, satanic ritual, child birth

Overview: Having been adopted by the madam of a southwestern brothel, a now adult Adrian must cope with the fact that he's Satan's kid, and not living up to his expectations.
