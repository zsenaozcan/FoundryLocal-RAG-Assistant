Title: Graveyard of Honor (2002)
Genres: Action, Crime, Foreign, Thriller
Director: Takashi Miike
Main Cast: Narimi Arimori, Yoshiyuki Daichi, Hirotaro Honda, Harumi Inoue, Renji Ishibashi
Keywords: yakuza, remake

Overview: A barkeeper saves a Yakuza boss' life and thus makes his way up in the organization. However, his fear of nothing soon causes problems.
