Title: Ringmaster (1998)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Neil Abramson
Main Cast: Jerry Springer, Jaime Pressly, Molly Hagan, William McNamara, Michael Dudikoff
Keywords: talk show, raunchy

Overview: Jerry Springer stars as more or less himself, the host of a raunchy, controversial and popular Los Angeles talk show which features everyday people with problems and who frequently vent thrir problems on the air.
