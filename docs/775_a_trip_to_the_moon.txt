Title: A Trip to the Moon (1902)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Georges Méliès
Main Cast: Georges Méliès, Henri Delannoy, Bleuette Bernon, Brunnet, Victor André
Keywords: moon, mushroom, dream, expedition, cannon, star, earth, satire, alien, astronomer

Overview: A Trip to The Moon is a science fiction film from the French film pioneer Georges Méliès from the year 1902 about a trip to the moon. The film was the first film with a self-contained plot and was the beginning of the Science Fiction genre.
