Title: Berberian Sound Studio (2012)
Genres: Horror, Drama, Thriller
Director: Peter Strickland
Main Cast: Toby Jones, Tonia Sotiropoulou, Susanna Capellaro, Cosimo Fusco, Hilda Péter
Keywords: italy, film making, 1970s, sound production

Overview: In the 1970s, a British sound technician is brought to Italy to work on the sound effects for a gruesome horror film. His nightmarish task slowly takes over his psyche, driving him to confront his own past.
