Title: Escape in the Fog (1945)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Budd Boetticher
Main Cast: Otto Kruger, Nina Foch, William Wright, Konstantin Shayne, Ivan Triesault
Keywords: san francisco, b movie, film noir

Overview: A nurse recovering in a hospital from a nervous breakdown keeps having dreams where she sees two men trying to murder a third. When she meets the the officer in charge of psychology at the hospital, she is astounded to discover that he is the man in her dream who is the intended murder victim.
