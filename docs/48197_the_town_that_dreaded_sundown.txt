Title: The Town That Dreaded Sundown (1976)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller, Crime
Director: Charles B. Pierce
Main Cast: Ben Johnson, Andrew Prine, Dawn Wells, Jimmy Clem, Jim Citty
Keywords: based on true story, serial killer, faux documentary, proto-slasher

Overview: A Texas Ranger hunts for a hooded serial killer terrorizing the residents of a small town, set in 1946 Arkansas. Loosely based on a true story.
