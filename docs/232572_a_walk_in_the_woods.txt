Title: A Walk in the Woods (2015)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Drama
Director: Ken Kwapis
Main Cast: Robert Redford, Nick Nolte, Emma Thompson, Nick Offerman, Kristen Schaal
Keywords: mountain, hiking, backpacker, climbing, friends, appalachian trail

Overview: After spending two decades in England, Bill Bryson returns to the U.S., where he decides the best way to connect with his homeland is to hike the Appalachian Trail with one of his oldest friends.
