Title: Johnny Skidmarks (1998)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama, Mystery
Director: John Raffo
Main Cast: Peter Gallagher, Frances McDormand, John Lithgow, John Kapelos, Jack Black
Keywords: car accident, dead woman, dead woman with eyes open

Overview: Johnny Scardino is working for blackmailers, photographing wealthy guys in seedy motels. One such assignment turns the wrong way and blackmailers die one by one. Is Johnny the next on the list?
