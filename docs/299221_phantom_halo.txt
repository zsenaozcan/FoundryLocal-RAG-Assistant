Title: Phantom Halo (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: Antonia Bogdanovich
Main Cast: Rebecca Romijn, Thomas Brodie-Sangster, Luke Kleintank, Jordan Dunn, Tobin Bell
Keywords: counterfeiting, woman director

Overview: Two brothers barely scrape by as their father continues to gamble and drink away the money they do bring home. They hatch a plan for a robbery but at what price?
