Title: The Private Life of a Cat (1944)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Alexander Hammid
Main Cast: Maya Deren
Keywords: cat, woman director, short

Overview: Life of a domestic cat, filmed from a cat’s-eye view. This film was circulated in two versions: a silent version without narration and a somewhat longer sound version with a narration read by filmmaker Alexander Hammid’s then-wife, Maya Deren.  Takes place over a period of months as the cat gives birth to a litter of kittens and she cares for them as they grow.
