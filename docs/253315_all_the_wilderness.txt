Title: All the Wilderness (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael James Johnson
Main Cast: Danny DeVito, Isabelle Fuhrman, Virginia Madsen, Kodi Smit-McPhee, Evan Ross

Overview: Keeping to himself in the wake his father's death, James Charm finds refuge in solitary walks and creating morbid sketches — until a charismatic new friend and a quirky young woman begin to draw him out of his shell.
