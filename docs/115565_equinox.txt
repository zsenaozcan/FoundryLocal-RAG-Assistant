Title: Equinox (1992)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Alan Rudolph
Main Cast: Matthew Modine, Lara Flynn Boyle, Fred Ward, Marisa Tomei, Tyra Ferrell
Keywords: dream, adoption, nudity, investigation, murder, inheritance, writer, gangster, twins, discovery

Overview: Henry Petosa and Freddy Ace are twins who were separated being babies, and they do not know each other. Henry was adopted by a honest man, while Freddy becomes a gangster. Henry is very shy and has a lot of mental troubles. The film melts the two stories by a young writer who discovers that they were sons of an european noble and they own a large inheritance.
