Title: Sleeper (1973)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Woody Allen
Main Cast: Woody Allen, Diane Keaton, John Beck, Mary Gregory, Brian Avery
Keywords: sex, revolution, future, dystopia, government, control, satire, independent film, robot, tyranny

Overview: Miles Monroe, a clarinet-playing health food store proprietor, is revived out of cryostasis 200 years into a future world in order to help rebels fight an oppressive government regime.
