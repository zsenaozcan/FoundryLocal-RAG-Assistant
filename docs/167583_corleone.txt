Title: Corleone (1978)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Pasquale Squitieri
Main Cast: Giuliano Gemma, Claudia Cardinale, Francisco Rabal, Michele Placido, Stefano Satta Flores
Keywords: italy, corruption, sicily, based on novel, friendship, politics, murder, gun battle, survival, mafia

Overview: Two friends grow up together in the Sicily of the '50s. Two different destiny, two different way of life. Could their friendship survive to the mafia shadow?
