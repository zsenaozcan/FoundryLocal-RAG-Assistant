Title: The Zodiac Killer (1971)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Horror, Mystery
Director: Tom Hanson
Main Cast: Hal Reed, Bob Jones, Ray Lynch, Mary Darrington, Frank Sanabek
Keywords: female nudity, killing, nudity, murder, gore, serial killer, masked killer, slasher, violence, psychotic

Overview: The San Francisco area is beset by a series of seemingly random murders without motive or pattern. The police are taunted by phone calls and letters. Could the maniac be the violent, truck driver, or the seemingly mild-mannered mailman, or even a cop?
