Title: Village People (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Marja Pyykkö
Main Cast: Iina Kuustonen, Iina Kuustonen, Aake Kalliala, Marjaana Maijala
Keywords: president, finland, woman director, presidential visit

Overview: Urho Kaleva Kekkonen, the long-time president of the Republic of Finland, is scheduled to visit Alli’s Bar on his way to northern Finland. He is blindly worshiped and the nearer the visit draws, the more envy, competition and dirty tricks come to bear in this small town setting. No one is spared the brutality!
