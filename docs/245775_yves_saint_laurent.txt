Title: Yves Saint Laurent (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jalil Lespert
Main Cast: Pierre Niney, Guillaume Gallienne, Nikolai Kinski, Charlotte Le Bon, Xavier Lafitte
Keywords: biography, fashion designer, fashion

Overview: A look at the life of French designer Yves Saint Laurent from the beginning of his career in 1958 when he met his lover and business partner, Pierre Berge.
