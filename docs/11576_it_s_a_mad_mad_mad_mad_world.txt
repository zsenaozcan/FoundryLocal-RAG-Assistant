Title: It's a Mad, Mad, Mad, Mad World (1963)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Crime
Director: Stanley Kramer
Main Cast: Spencer Tracy, Milton Berle, Sid Caesar, Buddy Hackett, Mickey Rooney
Keywords: competition, prison, california, chase, airplane, humor, national park, treasure hunt, slapstick, money

Overview: A group of strangers come across a man dying after a car crash who proceeds to tell them about the $350,000 he buried in California. What follows is the madcap adventures of those strangers as each attempts to claim the prize for himself.
