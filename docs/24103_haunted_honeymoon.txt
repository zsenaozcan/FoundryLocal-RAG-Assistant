Title: Haunted Honeymoon (1986)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Music
Director: Gene Wilder
Main Cast: Gene Wilder, Gilda Radner, Dom DeLuise, Jonathan Pryce, Eve Ferret
Keywords: radio program

Overview: Larry Abbot, speaker in the radio horror shows of Manhattan Mystery Theater wants to marry. For the marriage he takes his fiancée home to the castle where he grew up among his eccentric relatives. His uncle decides that he needs to be cured from a neurotic speech defect and exaggerated bursts of fear: he gives him a shock therapy with palace ghosts.
