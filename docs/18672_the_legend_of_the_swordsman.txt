Title: The Legend of the Swordsman (1992)
Genres: Action, Fantasy
Director: Ching Siu-Tung
Main Cast: Brigitte Lin, Jet Li, Rosamund Kwan, Michelle Reis, Waise Lee
Keywords: wuxia

Overview: Jet Li plays Ling, a student from the Blue Mountain school of martial arts, who, along with fellow students and Kiddo, find themselves embroiled in a country-wide political/clan war with the Highlander clan in the forefront of the battle.
