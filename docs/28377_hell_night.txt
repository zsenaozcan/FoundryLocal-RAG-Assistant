Title: Hell Night (1981)
Genres: Horror
Director: Tom DeSimone
Main Cast: Linda Blair, Vince Van Patten, Peter Barton, Suki Goodwin, Kevin Brophy
Keywords: haunted house, college, abandoned house, serial killer, slasher, ghost, fraternity initiation, scream queen, deformed man, trespassing

Overview: Four college pledges are forced to spend the night in a deserted old mansion where they get killed off one by one by the monstrous surviving members of a family massacre years earlier for trespassing on their living grounds.
