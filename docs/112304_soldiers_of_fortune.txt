Title: Soldiers of Fortune (2012)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Maksim Korostyshevsky
Main Cast: Christian Slater, Ving Rhames, Colm Meaney, Freddy Rodríguez, Charlie Bewley

Overview: Wealthy thrill-seekers pay huge premiums to have themselves inserted into military adventures, only this time things don't go exactly to plan.
