Title: The Texas Chain Saw Massacre (1974)
Genres: Horror
Director: Tobe Hooper
Main Cast: Marilyn Burns, Allen Danziger, Paul A. Partain, William Vail, Teri McMinn
Keywords: gas station, texas, van, gore, midnight movie, surprise ending, shock in the end, leatherface, hitchhiker, slaughterhouse
