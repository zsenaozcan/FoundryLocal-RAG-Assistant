Title: The Forest (2002)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Leonel Vieira
Main Cast: Diogo Morgado, Karra Elejalde, Maitê Proença, Chico Diaz, Carlos Santos

Overview: Young man, of Portuguese nobility ascendancy, starts working in a rubber plantation in the Amazon, in 1912, and falls in love with pretty Yayá, a married woman.
