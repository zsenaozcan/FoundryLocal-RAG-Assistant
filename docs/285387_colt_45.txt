Title: Colt 45 (2014)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Action, Crime
Director: Fabrice Du Welz
Main Cast: Gérard Lanvin, JoeyStarr, Ymanol Perset, Simon Abkarian, Alice Taglioni
Keywords: police, violence

Overview: The young police man Vincent is the best in his class, and denies to be recruited to the special forces. But when the corrupt Milo manages to get a grip on him, his life is turned into a living nightmare.
