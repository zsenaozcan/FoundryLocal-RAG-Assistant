Title: Prisoners of the Lost Universe (1983)
Genres: Adventure, Science Fiction, Action
Director: Terry Marcel
Main Cast: Richard Hatch, Kay Lenz, John Saxon, Peter O'Farrell, Ray Charleson
Keywords: monster, bathing, teleportation, warlord, scientist, attempted rape, parallel world, little people, kicked in the groin, sword and sorcery

Overview: Three people are transported into a parallel universe. There they find that they must use modern technology, but medieval weapons, in order to save the citizenry from a murderous warlord.
