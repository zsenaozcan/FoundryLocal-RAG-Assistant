Title: Bait Shop (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: C. B. Harding
Main Cast: Bill Engvall, Jim R. Coleman, Billy Ray Cyrus, Mary Rachel Dudley, Jessica Blackmore
Keywords: sport

Overview: Its Barbershop for Rednecks. Bill is a bait shop owner who has to make a balloon payment of $15,000 or else the bank will close his shop. Unable to make the payment from the proceeds of the shop, Bill must compete in the annual Bass Tournament, which he pathetically loses every year, to win the money.
