Title: Gun Fury (1953)
Genres: Crime, Action, Adventure, Western
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: Rock Hudson, Donna Reed, Lee Marvin, Philip Carey, Roberta Haynes
Keywords: arizona, gang, stagecoach

Overview: After a stagecoach holdup, Frank Slayton's notorious gang leave Ben Warren for deasd and head off with his fiancée. Warren follows, and although none of the townspeople he comes across are prepared to help, he recruits two others who have sworn revenge on the ruthless Slayton.
