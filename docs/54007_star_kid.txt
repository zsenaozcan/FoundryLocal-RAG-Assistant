Title: Star Kid (1997)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Family, Science Fiction
Director: Manny Coto
Main Cast: Joseph Mazzello, Richard Gilliland, Corinne Bohrer, Alex Daniels, Joey Simmrin
Keywords: meteor, superhero, cybersuit

Overview: Shy seventh-grader Spencer Griffith's life changes when the meteor falls into local junkyard and he finds a Cybersuit - the wise and strong robot from another galaxy. Spencer puts Cybersuit on and becomes a different kind of guy
