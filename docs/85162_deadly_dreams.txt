Title: Deadly Dreams (1988)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Kristine Peterson
Main Cast: Mitchell Anderson, Juliette Cummins, Xander Berkeley, Thom Babbes, Troy Evans
Keywords: nightmare, murder, double crossed, slasher, throat slitting, double cross, woman director

Overview: From the director of the highly acclaimed "Body Chemistry," comes a frightening excursion into terror. Alex is caught in a web of distrust between his brother, his best friend, a beautiful stranger and the renewed dreams of the slaughter of his family.
