Title: Frances Ha (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Noah Baumbach
Main Cast: Greta Gerwig, Mickey Sumner, Adam Driver, Michael Zegen, Michael Esper
Keywords: friendship, loneliness, black and white, responsibility, late coming of age

Overview: An aspiring dancer moves to New York City and becomes caught up in a whirlwind of flighty fair-weather friends, diminishing fortunes and career setbacks.
