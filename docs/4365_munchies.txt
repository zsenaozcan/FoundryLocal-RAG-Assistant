Title: Munchies (1987)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Tina Hirsch
Main Cast: Nadine Van der Velde, Charlie Stratton, Harvey Korman, Alix Elias, Charlie Phillips
Keywords: monster, beer, peru, junk food

Overview: Simon Watterman, a space archaeologist, discovers the "Munchies" in a cave in Peru. Cecil Watterman, Simon's evil twin brother and snack food entrepreneur, kidnaps the creature. What Cecil does not know is that the creature, when chopped up, regenerates into many new creatures and are they mean!
