Title: Small Town Saturday Night (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ryan Craig
Main Cast: Shawn Christian, Chris Pine, Bre Blair, John Hawkes, Lin Shaye
Keywords: prostitute, bar, small town, gas station, deputy sheriff, musician, friends, slice of life, independent film, rural setting

Overview: Just days before Rhett (Chris Pine) is leaving for Nashville to pursue a lifelong dream of being a singer/songwriter, his girlfriend Samantha (Bre Blair) realizes that "his" future may not be "their" future. This news comes as a shock to Rhett, and he is faced with having to choose between following his dream and staying in his hometown in order to be with the woman he loves.
