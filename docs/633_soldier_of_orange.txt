Title: Soldier of Orange (1977)
Genres: Drama, History, Thriller, War
Director: Paul Verhoeven
Main Cast: Rutger Hauer, Jeroen Krabbé, Susan Penhaligon, Edward Fox, Lex van Delden
Keywords: netherlands, world war ii, brotherhood, student

Overview: This film depicts World War II through the eyes of several Dutch students. It follows them through the beginning of the war, the Nazi occupation and the liberation.
