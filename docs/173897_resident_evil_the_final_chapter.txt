Title: Resident Evil: The Final Chapter (2016)
Genres: Action, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Paul W.S. Anderson
Main Cast: Milla Jovovich, Iain Glen, Ali Larter, Shawn Roberts, Eoin Macken
Keywords: helicopter, monster, underground, mutant, post-apocalyptic, dystopia, sequel, end of the world, female soldier, woman with gun

Overview: Picking up immediately after the events in Resident Evil: Retribution, Alice (Milla Jovovich) is the only survivor of what was meant to be humanity's final stand against the undead. Now, she must return to where the nightmare began - The Hive in Raccoon City, where the Umbrella Corporation is gathering its forces for a final strike against the only remaining survivors of the apocalypse.
