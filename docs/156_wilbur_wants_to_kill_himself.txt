Title: Wilbur Wants to Kill Himself (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Lone Scherfig
Main Cast: Jamie Sives, Adrian Rawlins, Shirley Henderson, Lisa McKinlay, Mads Mikkelsen
Keywords: love triangle, suicide, brother brother relationship, bookshop, glasgow, irony, depression, optimism, suicide attempt, cleaning lady

Overview: The strange comedy film of two close brothers; one, Wilbur, who wants to kill himself, and the other, Harbour, who tries to prevent this. When their father dies leaving them his bookstore they meet a woman who makes their lives a bit better yet with a bit more trouble as well.
