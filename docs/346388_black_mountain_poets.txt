Title: Black Mountain Poets (2016)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Jamie Adams
Main Cast: Alice Lowe, Dolly Wells, Tom Cullen, Rosa Robson, Richard Elis

Overview: Two professional con artist sisters go on the run and assume the identities of The Wilding Sisters, guest stars of a poetry retreat in the depths of the Black Mountains. A romantic comedy drama about love, crime, spirituality, and soul
