Title: Repentance (2014)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Philippe Caland
Main Cast: Forest Whitaker, Anthony Mackie, Sanaa Lathan, Nicole Ari Parker, Mike Epps

Overview: An earnest life-coach/author, Thomas Carter, is mysteriously abducted by a deranged client, Angel Sanchez, who delves into Thomas' teachings and uses his spiritual messages of Karma - action and reaction (Vipaka), against him to terrorize him and his family for their past sins.
