Title: The Twin (1984)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Yves Robert
Main Cast: Pierre Richard, Camilla More, Carey More, Jean-Pierre Kalfon, Andréa Ferréol

Overview: Matthias Duval is in love, but he can't choose between the two twin sisters Betty and Liz Kerner. To pick up the two sisters, he invents his own twin brother and will play both characters.
