Title: Hard Luck (2007)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Mario Van Peebles
Main Cast: Wesley Snipes, Jackie Quinones, Cybill Shepherd, James Hiroyuki Liao, Aubrey Dollar
Keywords: stolen money, money, serial killer

Overview: Three converging story lines involving bootleggers, a serial killer and drug dealers are followed. A former drug dealer tries to go straight, but comes across a stash of stolen drugs. Meanwhile, a middle-aged suburban housewife hides a sadistic and vicious streak.
