Title: The Hand (1981)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Oliver Stone
Main Cast: Michael Caine, Andrea Marcovicci, Annie McEnroe, Bruce McGill, Viveca Lindfors
Keywords: murder, severed hand, comic book artist

Overview: Jon Lansdale is a comic book artist who loses his right hand in a car accident. The hand was not found at the scene of the accident, but it soon returns by itself to follow Jon around, and murder those who anger him.
