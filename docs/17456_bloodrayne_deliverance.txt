Title: BloodRayne: Deliverance (2007)
Genres: Action, Horror, Western
Director: Uwe Boll
Main Cast: Natassia Malthe, Zack Ward, Michael Paré, Brendan Fletcher, Chris Coppola
Keywords: based on video game

Overview: Rayne, a half-human half-vampire warrior, is in the America's 1880's Wild West to stop the vampired Billy the Kid and his posse of vampire cowboys.
