Title: Revengeance (2017)
Genres: Comedy, Action, Animation
Director: Bill Plympton
Main Cast: Charley Rossman, Robert LuJane

Overview: A low-rent bounty hunter named Rod Rosse, The One Man Posse, gets entangled in a web of danger when he takes on a job from an ex-biker/ex-wrestler turned U.S. senator named "Deathface."
