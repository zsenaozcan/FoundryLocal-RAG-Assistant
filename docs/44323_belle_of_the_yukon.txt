Title: Belle of the Yukon (1944)
Genres: Drama, Action, Comedy, Western, Music
Director: William A. Seiter
Main Cast: Randolph Scott, Gypsy Rose Lee, Dinah Shore, Bob Burns, Charles Winninger
Keywords: gold rush, straight

Overview: Left by a con man (Randolph Scott), a dancer (Gypsy Rose Lee) finds him in gold-rush Alaska running an honest casino/dance hall.
