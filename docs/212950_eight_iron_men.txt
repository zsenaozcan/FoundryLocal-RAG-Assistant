Title: Eight Iron Men (1952)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Edward Dmytryk
Main Cast: Bonar Colleano, Lee Marvin, Arthur Franz, Richard Kiley, Nick Dennis
Keywords: world war ii, machine gun, dream sequence

Overview: Stanley Kramer's WW-II character study has Lee Marvin as the Sergeant of a small squad laid over during fighting in Italy. During the otherwise boring time between battles, tensions arise as they are ordered not to rescue a squad mate pinned down by the enemy, for fear of risking more lives. Based on the stage play "A Sound of Hunting", by Harry Brown.
