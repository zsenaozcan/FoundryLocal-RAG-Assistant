Title: Moving Day (1936)
Genres: Animation
Director: Ben Sharpsteen
Main Cast: Billy Bletcher, Walt Disney, Pinto Colvig, Clarence Nash
Keywords: mickey mouse, disney short, donald duck, pegleg pete, goofy

Overview: Donald and Mickey are overdue on their rent, so the sheriff is preparing to evict them and sell their belongings. Goofy the ice-man comes by and helps them move out before the sale, but their piano doesn't want to stay on his truck. Meanwhile, Donald has a fight with a plunger and a fishbowl after removing a heater from the gas line.
