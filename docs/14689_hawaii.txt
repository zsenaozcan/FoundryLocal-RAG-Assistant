Title: Hawaii (1966)
Genres: Drama, Adventure, Romance
Director: George Roy Hill
Main Cast: Julie Andrews, Max von Sydow, Richard Harris, Gene Hackman, Carroll O'Connor
Keywords: hawaii, epic

Overview: Abner Hale, a rigid and humorless New England missionary, marries the beautiful Jerusha Bromley and takes her to the exotic island kingdom of Hawaii, intent on converting the natives. But the clash between the two cultures is too great and instead of understanding there comes tragedy.
