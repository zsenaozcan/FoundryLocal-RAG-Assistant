Title: Killer Tomatoes Eat France! (1992)
Genres: Horror, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: John De Bello
Main Cast: John Astin, Tom Katsis, Marc Price, Steve Lundquist, Costa Dillon
Keywords: paris, monster, tomato, sequel

Overview: After being busted out of jail by his tomatoes, Professor Mortimer Gangrene begins a new plot to take over the world by inserting his hapless lackey Igor into the throne of the King of France.
