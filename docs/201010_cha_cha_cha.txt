Title: Cha cha cha (2013)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Marco Risi
Main Cast: Luca Argentero, Eva Herzigová, Claudio Amendola, Pippo Delbono, Pietro Ragusa

Overview: An accident involving a 16-years old prompts private eye Corso to an investigation that will unearth a deep web of corruption in today's Rome.
