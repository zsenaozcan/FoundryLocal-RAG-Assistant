Title: The Big Combo (1955)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Joseph H. Lewis
Main Cast: Cornel Wilde, Jean Wallace, Brian Donlevy, Richard Conte, Lee Van Cleef
Keywords: detective, police corruption

Overview: Police Lt. Leonard Diamond vies to bring a clever, well connected, and sadistic gangster to justice all the while obsessing over the gangsters' girlfriend.
