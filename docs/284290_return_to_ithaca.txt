Title: Return to Ithaca (2014)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Laurent Cantet
Main Cast: Isabel Cristina Santos Téllez, Jorge Perugorría, Fernando Hechavarría, Néstor Jiménez Rodríguez, Pedro Julio Díaz Ferrán

Overview: Sun is setting on Havana. Five friends are gathered to celebrate the return of Amadeo after 16 years of exile in Madrid.
