Title: Elvira, Mistress of the Dark (1988)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: James Signorelli
Main Cast: Cassandra Peterson, Susan Kellermann, Jeff Conaway, Daniel Greene, Edie McClurg
Keywords: hostess

Overview: When her great aunt dies famed horror hostess Elvira heads for the uptight new England town to Falwell to claim her inheritance of a haunted house a witches cookbook and a punk rock poodle. But once the stuffy locals get an eyeful of the scream queens ample assets all hell busts out &amp; breaks loose.
