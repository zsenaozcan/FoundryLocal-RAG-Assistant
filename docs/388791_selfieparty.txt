Title: SelfieParty (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lubomir Levitski
Main Cast: Nadiya Lukavetska 

Overview: Four friends who are all students, wake up at the police station, not remembering the events of yesterday. They are accused of murdering a dwarf, whose body was found on the lawn of the house where the boys partied last night. To figure out what happened, the boys decide to collect all the photos and videos from yesterday's mayhem.
