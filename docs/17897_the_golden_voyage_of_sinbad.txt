Title: The Golden Voyage of Sinbad (1973)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Gordon Hessler
Main Cast: John Phillip Law, Caroline Munro, Tom Baker, Douglas Wilmer, Martin Shaw
Keywords: tattoo, sinbad

Overview: Sinbad and his crew intercept a homunculus carrying a golden tablet. Koura, the creator of the homunculus and practitioner of evil magic, wants the tablet back and pursues Sinbad. Meanwhile Sinbad meets the Vizier who has another part of the interlocking golden map, and they mount a quest across the seas to solve the riddle of the map.
