Title: Igor (2008)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Family, Fantasy
Director: Anthony Leondis
Main Cast: John Cusack, Myleene Klass, Robin Walsh, Matt McKenna, John Cleese
Keywords: experiment, evil genius

Overview: Animated fable about a cliché hunchbacked evil scientist's assistant who aspires to become a scientist himself, much to the displeasure of the rest of the evil science community.
