Title: The Sea Bat (1930)
Genres: Action, Romance, Thriller
Director: Lionel Barrymore
Main Cast: Raquel Torres, Charles Bickford, Nils Asther, George F. Marion, John Miljan
Keywords: monster, island, paganism, escaped convict, diver, sponge

Overview: The sister (Raquel Torres) of a sponge diver killed by a stingray loves an escaped convict (Charles Bickford) posing as a priest.
