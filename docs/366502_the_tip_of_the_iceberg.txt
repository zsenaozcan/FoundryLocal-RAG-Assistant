Title: The Tip of the Iceberg (2016)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: David Cánovas
Main Cast: Maribel Verdú, Fernando Cayo, Bárbara Goenaga, Jorge Calvo, Jesús Castejón
Keywords: employment

Overview: A large, multinational Company is badly shaken when three of its employees commit suicide. Sofía Cuevas, one of the company’s top executives, is chosen to prepare an in-house report to try and shed some light on the events.
