Title: Doc Savage: The Man of Bronze (1975)
Genres: Adventure
Director: Michael Anderson
Main Cast: Ron Ely, Paul Gleason, William Lucking, Michael Miller, Eldon Quick
Keywords: campy

Overview: In the Fabulous Thirties, Doc Savage and his five Amazing Adventurers are sucked into the mystery of Doc's father disappearing in the wilds of South America. The maniacal Captain Seas tries to thwart them at every turn as they travel to the country of Hidalgo to investigate Doc's father's death and uncover a vast horde of Incan gold.
