Title: The Godless Girl (1929)
Genres: Drama
Director: Cecil B. DeMille
Main Cast: Lina Basquette, Tom Keene, Marie Prevost, Noah Beery, Eddie Quillan
Keywords: silent film, reform school

Overview: High school students led by the Girl and Boy turn from Christianity toward secret atheistic meetings. When a girl is accidentally killed by a stairway collapse, the Girl and Boy go to reform school where they are treated brutally.
