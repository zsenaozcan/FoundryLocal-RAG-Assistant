Title: Honey Night (2015)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Ivo Trajkov
Main Cast: Nikola Ristanovski, Verica Nedeska, Aleksandar Mikić, Нина Јанковић, Mitko Apostolovski

Overview: The story of this political thriller takes place in the 90s and follows one night of the life of the Deputy Minister Nikola and his wife Ana.  Nikola Ristanovski and Verica Nedeska star in the main roles of the couple.
