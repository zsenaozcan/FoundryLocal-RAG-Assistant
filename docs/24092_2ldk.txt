Title: 2LDK (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Yukihiko Tsutsumi
Main Cast: Maho Nonami, Eiko Koike

Overview: Nozomi and Lana share an apartment in Tokyo. They have both auditioned for the same role in a movie, and know that the shortlist has been cut down to just the two of them. As they wait the night before finding out who will get the role, their personality clashes erupt into an all-out battle.
