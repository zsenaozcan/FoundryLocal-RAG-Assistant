Title: Excision (2012)
Genres: Horror
Director: Richard Bates Jr.
Main Cast: AnnaLynne McCord, Traci Lords, Ariel Winter, Roger Bart, Jeremy Sumpter
Keywords: surgeon, teenager, domineering mother, awkwardness, social outcast, lung transplant 

Overview: Alienated teen Pauline struggles with the pressures of fitting into high school, pleasing her mother and a burning desire to lose her virginity. With a grotesque curiosity for the darker side of life, Pauline is considered a social outcast by everyone around her. Enticed by flesh, she retreats into her own fantasies and hopes to become a great surgeon - that is, if she doesn't go insane first.
