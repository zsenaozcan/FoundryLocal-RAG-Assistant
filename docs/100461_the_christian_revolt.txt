Title: The Christian Revolt (1962)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Nagisa Ōshima
Main Cast: Hashizo Okawa, Satomi Oka, Rentarô Mikuni, Ryutaro Otomo
Keywords: japanese new wave

Overview: In the year 1637 in Shimabara of Tokugawa-era Japan, oppressed peasant Christians revolt against the shogunate with the aid of a charismatic Christian rebel leader Shiro Amakusa.
