Title: Girl in Progress (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Patricia Riggen
Main Cast: Eva Mendes, Matthew Modine, Landon Liboiron, Patricia Arquette, Raini Rodriguez
Keywords: latina, coming of age, mother daughter relationship, teenage girl, teenager, single mother, woman director
