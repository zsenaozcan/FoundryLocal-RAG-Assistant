Title: The Little Drummer Boy (1968)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Jules Bass
Main Cast: Ted Eccles, José Ferrer, Paul Frees, June Foray, Greer Garson
Keywords: holiday, orphan, based on song, poem or rhyme

Overview: In this touching Christmas classic a young orphaned drummer boy is kidnapped. After he escapes he searches for his camel and finds him in the Nativity of the Baby Jesus. He gives Baby Jesus the only gift he has a song on his drum. This song is the only gift Baby Jesus responds to.
