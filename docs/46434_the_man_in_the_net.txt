Title: The Man in the Net (1959)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: Alan Ladd, Carolyn Jones, Diane Brewster, John Lupton, Charles McGraw

Overview: A artist living in a quiet Connecticut is the main suspect in the disappearance of his shrew wife. Things really turn ugly when the townsfolk attempt to take the law into their own hands.
