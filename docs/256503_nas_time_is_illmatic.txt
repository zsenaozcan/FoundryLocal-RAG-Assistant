Title: Nas: Time Is Illmatic (2014)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: One9
Main Cast: Nas, Alicia Keys, Busta Rhymes, Q-Tip, Pete Rock
Keywords: rap music, music documentary

Overview: Time Is Illmatic is a feature length documentary film that delves deep into the making of Nas' 1994 debut album, Illmatic, and the social conditions that influenced its creation.
