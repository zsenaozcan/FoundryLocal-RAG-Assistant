Title: Fiasco in Milan (1959)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Nanni Loy
Main Cast: Vittorio Gassman, Nino Manfredi, Tiberio Murgia, Claudia Cardinale, Renato Salvatori
Keywords: sequel, heist, soccer

Overview: The usual gang of robbers in engaged by a thief from Milan to steal a suitcase full of money, but troubles will menace the success of the operation.
