Title: Senn (2013)
Genres: Science Fiction, Mystery, Adventure
Director: Josh Feldman
Main Cast: Zach Eulberg, Taylor Lambert, Paul R. Frommer

Overview: Strange phenomena begin to plague Senn, a factory worker on the forgotten world, Pyom, when an enigmatic alien intelligence arrives to deliver him to the Polychronom. This ancient object has surreptitiously chosen him for a mysterious purpose that could mean his undoing.
