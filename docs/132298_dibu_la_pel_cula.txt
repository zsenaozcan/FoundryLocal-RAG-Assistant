Title: Dibu: La Película (1997)
Genres: Animation, Comedy
Director: Alejandro Stoessel
Main Cast: Germán Kraus, Stella Maris Closas, Alberto Anchart, Facundo Espinosa, Andrés Ispani

Overview: When Caro and Marce go on vacation they find a new friend, Buji, and just like Dibu, she is a cartoon animated kid. Abu, Pepe, Víctor, Leo and Dibu are left alone at home and they have quite an adventure when they decide to sign-up Dibu in a go-cart race.
