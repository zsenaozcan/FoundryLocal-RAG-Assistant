Title: Flatfoot in Egypt (1980)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Steno
Main Cast: Bud Spencer, Enzo Cannavale, Baldwyn Dakile, Angelo Infanti, Cinzia Monreale
Keywords: prosecution, petrol, investigation, fistfight, kommissar geiger

Overview: Police Commissioner Rizzo, Bigfoot (Piedone by nickname) and Marshal Caputo go to Egypt and look for Professor Cerullo, who is missing. The professor has discovered an insect that can smell where oil is and lots of criminals are interested in it. Written by Salvatore Santangelo
