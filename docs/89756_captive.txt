Title: Captive (1998)
Genres: TV Movie, Thriller
Director: Matt Dorff
Main Cast: Richard Grieco, Marie-Josée Croze, Paul Hopkins, Michele Greene, Lawrence Arcouette
Keywords: boy, prison guard, child kidnapping

Overview: Joe Goodies has just entered an advertising agency after recovering from the death of his girlfriend in a car accident. Ruined by the compensation he has to pay for the accident, he decides, with his new girlfriend, to kidnap the son of the president of the agency and ask for a bailout.
