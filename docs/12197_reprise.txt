Title: Reprise (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Joachim Trier
Main Cast: Anders Danielsen Lie, Espen Klouman-Høiner, Viktoria Winge, Christian Rubeck, Henrik Elvestad
Keywords: depression, music, friendship, author

Overview: Two competitive friends, fueled by literary aspirations and youthful exuberance, endure the pangs of love, depression and burgeoning careers.
