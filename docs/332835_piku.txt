Title: Piku (2015)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Shoojit Sircar
Main Cast: Amitabh Bachchan, Deepika Padukone, Irrfan Khan, Jisshu Sengupta

Overview: A road trip to Kolkata brings Piku, an architect, closer to her ageing but nagging father Bhaskor Banerjee despite their disparate ideologies and fights over trivial issues.
