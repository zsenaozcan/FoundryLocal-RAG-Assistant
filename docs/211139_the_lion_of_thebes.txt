Title: The Lion of Thebes (1964)
Genres: Drama, Action, Adventure
Director: Giorgio Ferroni
Main Cast: Mark Forest, Yvonne Furneaux, Massimo Serato, Pierre Cressoy, Nerio Bernardi
Keywords: sword and sandal, peplum

Overview: Fleeing Troy in the wake of its destruction, fair-faced Helen and her faithful protector Arion run into the pharaoh Ramses, who sets his sights on Helen -- and is subsequently murdered. Now it's up to Arion to save Helen from those who wish her dead.
