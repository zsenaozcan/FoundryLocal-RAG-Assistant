Title: The Ambushers (1967)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Henry Levin
Main Cast: Dean Martin, Senta Berger, Janice Rule, Kurt Kasznar, Beverly Adams
Keywords: spy, matt helm

Overview: A  U.S. government built flying saucer is launched into space on a top secret test flight, but is brought down by exiled ruler of an outlaw nation who uses a remote control device to cause the ship to land at his secret base in Mexico. Agent Matt Helm is assigned to find the saucer and has to contend with an enemy agent and an Arab assassin.
