Title: The Colonel (2006)
Genres: Crime, Drama, History
Director: Laurent Herbiet
Main Cast: Olivier Gourmet, Robinson Stévenin, Cécile de France, Charles Aznavour

Overview: A "Reformed Colonel" is found dead in Paris, a couple of decades after Algeria's struggle for independence was won from France. Lieutenant Galois is assigned the investigation of this murder. She receives the diary of Lieutenent Guy Rossi who served under The Colonel in Algeria in 1956, and has been reported as missing in action since 1957. The revelations found in Rossi's diary go far beyond The Colonel's actions in Algeria, and give an insight on how dirty Algeria's War for Independence really was.
