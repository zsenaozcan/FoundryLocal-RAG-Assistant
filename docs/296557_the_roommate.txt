Title: The Roommate (2008)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Nagaoka Hisaaki
Main Cast: Ryosuke Watabe, Midori Tahara, Akira Kuruta, Sawaco, Sayori Shiozaki
Keywords: roommate, murder, tokyo japan, demonic possession, roommate relationship

Overview: A paranormal horror thriller involving two young women who start off as roommates in Tokyo but become much more. These women have a such have such a wonderful time living together until an uninvited "guest" possesses one of them. The demonic entity forces the woman to do things against her will including murder.
