Title: The Super Cops (1974)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Action
Director: Gordon Parks
Main Cast: Ron Leibman, David Selby, Sheila Frazier, Pat Hingle, Barton Heyman
Keywords: based on novel, based on true story, police officer
