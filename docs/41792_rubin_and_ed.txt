Title: Rubin and Ed (1991)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Trent Harris
Main Cast: Crispin Glover, Howard Hesseman, Karen Black, Michael Greene, Anna Louise Daniels
Keywords: road trip

Overview: Reclusive Rubin Farr teams up with vocal but unsuccessful multi-level salesman Ed Tuttle on a quest to bury Rubin's dead cat in the "perfect spot." Their trip takes them across Utah's desert where they have run-ins with Ed'd ex-wife Rula and an elusive Andy Warhol critic.
