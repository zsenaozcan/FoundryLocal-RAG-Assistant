Title: Alice Sweet Alice (1976)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Alfred Sole
Main Cast: Linda Miller, Mildred Clinton, Paula E. Sheppard, Niles McMaster, Jane Lowry
Keywords: single parent, church service, burned alive, psychopathic killer, proto-slasher

Overview: Alice is a withdrawn 12-year-old who lives with her mother and her younger sister, Karen, who gets most of the attention from her mother, leaving Alice out of the spotlight. But when Karen is found brutally murdered in a church, suspicions start to turn toward Alice. But could a 12-year-old girl really be capable of such savagery?
