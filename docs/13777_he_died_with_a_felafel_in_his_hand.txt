Title: He Died with a Felafel in His Hand (2001)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Richard Lowenstein
Main Cast: Noah Taylor, Emily Hamilton, Romane Bohringer, Alex Menglet, Brett Stewart
Keywords: based on novel, boarder, independent film

Overview: A search for love, meaning and bathroom solitude. Danny goes through a series of shared housing experiences in a succession of cities on the east coast of Australia. Together these vignettes form a narrative that is surprisingly reflective.
