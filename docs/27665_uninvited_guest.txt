Title: Uninvited Guest (1999)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Timothy Wayne Folsome
Main Cast: Mekhi Phifer, Mari Morrow, Malinda Williams, Mel Jackson, Wanya Morris
Keywords: suspense

Overview: During a small gathering, a smooth, seductive stranger is invited into a couple's house party and falls in love with the wife. Turns out the stranger is an escaped convict who demands that the wife leave the country with him and threatens to kill anyone who stands in his way.
