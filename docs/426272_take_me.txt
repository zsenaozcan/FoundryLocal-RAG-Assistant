Title: Take Me (2017)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Pat Healy
Main Cast: Taylor Schilling, Pat Healy, Alycia Delmore, Brooke Dillman, Jim O'Heir

Overview: Ray is a fledgling entrepreneur who specializes in high-end simulated abductions. He jumps at the chance when a mysterious client contracts him for a weekend kidnapping with a handsome payday at the end. But the job isn't all that it seems.
