Title: A Long Way Down (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Pascal Chaumeil
Main Cast: Pierce Brosnan, Aaron Paul, Imogen Poots, Toni Collette, Sam Neill

Overview: Four lost souls decide to end their lives on the same night, New Year's Eve. When they meet unintentionally at the same suicide hotspot, they mutually agree to call off their plans for six weeks, forming an unconventional, dysfunctional family.
