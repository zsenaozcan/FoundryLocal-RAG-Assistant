Title: Silent Madness (1984)
Genres: Horror
Director: Simon Nuchtern
Main Cast: Viveca Lindfors, Belinda Montgomery, Solly Marx, Rick Aiello, Elizabeth Kaitan
Keywords: psychiatrist, sorority house, homicidal maniac

Overview: A homicidal maniac is accidentally released from a hospital because of a computer error and heads to the site (a sorority) of his past murders to continue his penchant for mayhem.
