Title: Scorned (2013)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Mark Jones
Main Cast: Viva Bianca, AnnaLynne McCord, Billy Zane, Juan Pablo Veza, Sadie Katz
Keywords: revenge

Overview: Sadie and Kevin have decided to spend a romantic weekend together at his lake house.  But when an unexpected- and unfortunate- text from her best friend Jennifer to Kevin reveals a lurid love affair between the two, Sadie spirals a into a hunger for revenge.
