Title: George Carlin: Complaints & Grievances (2001)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary, TV Movie
Director: Rocco Urbisci
Main Cast: George Carlin
Keywords: concert, stand-up comedy, live comedy, dark comedy

Overview: George Carlin performs a hilarious set of never-before released material in "Complaints and Grievances." His 12th HBO special was recorded live at the Beacon Theater in New York City on November 17, 2001. In "Complaints and Grievances," Carlin shamelessly exposes the people and subjects that irritate him the most.
