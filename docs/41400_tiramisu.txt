Title: Tiramisu (2002)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Foreign, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Dante Lam
Main Cast: Karena Lam, Eason Chan, Candy Lo, Vincent Kok, Nicholas Tse

Overview: Is the story of a man, Fung, in his early twenties living his life as a mail-man during the day and a janitor at night. One day, through fate and destiny, he manages to meet a beautiful girl, Jane, on a subway train while delivering a package. From this moment on his life will change forever.
