Title: Kite (1998)
Genres: Animation, Crime, Action, Thriller
Director: Yasuomi Umetsu
Main Cast: Konami Yoshida

Overview: Sawa may seem innocent and naive, but don’t be fooled. She is a cold-blooded killer, and if you’re on the wrong side of the law, you may be her next target. Not content to just watch as the imperfect justice system lets more and more criminals go loose every day, a detective decides to train the young Sawa to be his instrument of justice.
