Title: The Last Man on Earth (1964)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Ubaldo Ragona
Main Cast: Vincent Price, Franca Bettoia, Emma Danieli, Giacomo Rossi-Stuart, Umberto Raho
Keywords: vampire, night, survivor, hammer, end of the world, blood, woman with gun, cigarette smoking, corpse, disease

Overview: Robert Morgan is the last man on earth, as far as he can tell. A plague killed everyone else on the planet several years ago. He was immune to it, and can only guess why. Vampires that were formerly human attack Morgan's home every night
