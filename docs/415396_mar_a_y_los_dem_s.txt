Title: María (y los demás) (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Nely Reguera
Main Cast: Bárbara Lennie, Vito Sanz, Julián Villagrán
Keywords: drama, woman director

Overview: Ever since her mum died when she was fifteen, Maria has taken care of her father and her siblings. Responsible and in control, she's always been a rock for the family and feels proud of her efforts. That's why her father's sudden love for his nurse and the announcement of their marriage brings Maria's world crashing down around her. At the age of 35 and unable to find a steady partner, she'll have to take the plunge and dare to change her own fate.
