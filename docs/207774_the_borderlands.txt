Title: The Borderlands (2013)
Genres: Horror
Director: Elliot Goldner
Main Cast: Robin Hill, Gordon Kennedy, Aidan McArdle, Patrick Godfrey, Luke Neal
Keywords: suicide, miracle, paganism, church, found footage, catholic priest, folk horror

Overview: Vatican investigators are sent to the British West Country to investigate paranormal activity, and they find the events are more disturbing than they first imagined.
