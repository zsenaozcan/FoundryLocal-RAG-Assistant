Title: The Rough House (1917)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Buster Keaton
Main Cast: Roscoe 'Fatty' Arbuckle, Al St. John, Buster Keaton, Alice Lake, Agnes Neilson

Overview: Roscoe, his wife and his mother-in-law run a seaside resort. Buster plays a gardener who puts out a fire started by Roscoe, then a delivery boy who fights with the cook St. John, then a cop.
