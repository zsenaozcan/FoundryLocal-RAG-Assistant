Title: Naked Childhood (1968)
Genres: Drama
Director: Maurice Pialat
Main Cast: Michel Terrazon, Linda Gutemberg, Raoul Billerey, Pierrette Deplanque
Keywords: childhood

Overview: Handed over to foster care by his mother—who's unwilling to give up permanent custody—the now-adolescent François understands that nothing in life is permanent, and his increasingly erratic actions reflect this knowledge. Shining a light on both the destructive behavior borne of abandonment and the complicated nature of foster care relationships, this 1968 film marked the directorial debut of Maurice Pialat.
