Title: The Hamiltons (2006)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Phil Flores
Main Cast: Cory Knauf, Samuel Child, Joseph McKelheer, Mackenzie Firgens, Rebekah Hoyle
Keywords: misfit, homosexuality, video camera

Overview: The Hamilton's seem to be an ordinary American family, living in a small town in Northern California and dealing with the problems of everyday life. They've also been recently adjusting to the untimely death of their parents, but since have moved on.
