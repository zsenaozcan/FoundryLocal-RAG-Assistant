Title: The Jester's Tale (1964)
Genres: History, Adventure, Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Karel Zeman
Main Cast: Petr Kostka, Miloslav Holub, Emília Vášáryová, Valentina Thielová, Eduard Kohout

Overview: An anti-war black comedy set during the Thirty Years' War. The film combines live action with animation to suggest the artistic style of the engraver Matthäus Merian.
