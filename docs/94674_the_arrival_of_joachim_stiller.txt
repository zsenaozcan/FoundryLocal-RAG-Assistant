Title: The Arrival of Joachim Stiller (1976)
Genres: Mystery
Director: Harry Kümel
Main Cast: Hugo Metsers, Cox Habbema, Ward de Ravet, Charles Janssens, Peter Strynckx
Keywords: belgium

Overview: The film tells the story of Freek Groenevelt and Simone Marijnissen whose lives are taken over by Joachim Stiller.
