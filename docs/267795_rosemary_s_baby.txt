Title: Rosemary's Baby (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Horror, Mystery
Director: Agnieszka Holland
Main Cast: Zoe Saldana, Patrick J. Adams, Carole Bouquet, Christina Cole, Jason Isaacs
Keywords: supernatural, woman director

Overview: Modern 3 hour mini-series adaptation of the classic novel by Ira Levin focusing on young Rosemary Woodhouse's suspicions that her neighbors may belong to a Satanic cult who are hell bent on getting one thing: the baby she is carrying.
