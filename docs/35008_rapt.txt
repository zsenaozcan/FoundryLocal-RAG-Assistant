Title: Rapt (2009)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Lucas Belvaux
Main Cast: Yvan Attal, Anne Consigny, Françoise Fabian, André Marcon, Alex Descas

Overview: A rich industrialist is brutally kidnapped. While he physically and mentally degenerates in imprisonment, the kidnappers, police and the board of the company of which he is director negotiate about the ransom of 50 million euro.
