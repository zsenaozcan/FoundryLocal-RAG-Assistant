Title: The Blood Spattered Bride (1972)
Genres: Horror
Director: Vicente Aranda
Main Cast: Simón Andreu, Maribel Martín, Alexandra Bastedo, Dean Selmier, Ángel Lombarte
Keywords: bride, mutilation

Overview: A young husband's sexual fantasies frighten his new wife and cause her to seek advice from Carmilla, a descendent of Mircalla de Karnstein. Carmilla seduces the young bride and forces her to commit gory acts of mutilation.
