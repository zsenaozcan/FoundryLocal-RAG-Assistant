Title: Point Zero (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: José Pedro Goulart
Main Cast: Sandro Aliprandini, Eucir de Souza, Patrícia Selonk, Camila Vergara, Larissa Tavares

Overview: Young Ênio is having to deal with a lot at home. His family is falling apart, whilst his sister's friends bully him at school. Over the course of one fateful night Ênio goes on an adventure, in a tour-de-force debut about growing up.
