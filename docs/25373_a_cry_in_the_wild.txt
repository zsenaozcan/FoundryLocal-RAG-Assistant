Title: A Cry in the Wild (1990)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Mark Griffiths
Main Cast: Jared Rushton, Ned Beatty, Pamela Sue Martin, Deke Anderson

Overview: 13-year-old Brian is the sole survivor of an unreported plane crash. Alone in the Yukon wilderness, Brian must learn to survive by his wits, find food and shelter, and brave wild, hungry animals until or if he is found.
