Title: Sibling Rivalry (1990)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Carl Reiner
Main Cast: Kirstie Alley, Bill Pullman, Carrie Fisher, Jami Gertz, Scott Bakula
Keywords: hotel room, police, salesman, sibling rivalry

Overview: Marjorie Turner is suffocating. Her younger sister Jeanine has no ambition, and her 8 year marriage to Harry puts her in constant competition with his family (all doctors)..
