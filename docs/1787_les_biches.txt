Title: Les Biches (1968)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Claude Chabrol
Main Cast: Jean-Louis Trintignant, Jacqueline Sassard, Stéphane Audran, Nane Germon, Serge Bento
Keywords: holiday, love triangle, gambling, lgbt
