Title: Arthur (1981)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Steve Gordon
Main Cast: Dudley Moore, Liza Minnelli, John Gielgud, Geraldine Fitzgerald, Jill Eikenberry
Keywords: new york city, comic drunk

Overview: Arthur is the most quotable drunk millionaire that is likely to ever steal your heart. Dudley Moore portrays Arthur, a thirty year old child who will inherit 750 million dollars if he complies with his family's demands and marries the woman of their choosing.
