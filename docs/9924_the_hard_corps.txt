Title: The Hard Corps (2006)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Romance, Thriller
Director: Sheldon Lettich
Main Cast: Jean-Claude Van Damme, Razaaq Adoti, Vivica A. Fox, Peter Bryant, Ron Bottitta
Keywords: bodyguard, special car, business man, rapper

Overview: An Army veteran assembles a team of bodyguards to protect a former boxer. Complications arise when the boxer suspects his sister may be romantically involved with the bodyguard.
