Title: Extraordinary Stories (2008)
Genres: Mystery, Drama
Director: Mariano Llinás
Main Cast: Mariano Llinás, Walter Jakob, Agustín Mendilaharzu, Klaus Dietze, Héctor Díaz
Keywords: argentina

Overview: In this adventurous experiment in storytelling, secret identities, missing persons, lost treasures, exotic beasts and desperate criminals are only a few of the elements woven into a grand tapestry of mysteries.
