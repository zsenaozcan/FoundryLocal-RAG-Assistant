Title: Step Up All In (2014)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Music
Director: Trish Sie
Main Cast: Ryan Guzman, Briana Evigan, Stephen Boss, Misha Gabriel, Izabella Miko
Keywords: street artist, dance contest, woman director
