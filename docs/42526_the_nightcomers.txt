Title: The Nightcomers (1971)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Michael Winner
Main Cast: Marlon Brando, Stephanie Beacham, Thora Hird, Harry Andrews, Verna Harvey
Keywords: corruption, sex, bondage, nudity, exploitation, prequel, murder, children, mansion, cruelty

Overview: Prequel to the Henry James classic "Turn of the Screw" about the events leading up to the deaths of Peter Quint and Ms. Jessel, and the the slow corruption of the children in their care.
