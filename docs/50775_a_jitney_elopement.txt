Title: A Jitney Elopement (1915)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Charlie Chaplin
Main Cast: Charlie Chaplin, Edna Purviance, Ernest Van Pelt, Leo White, Lloyd Bacon

Overview: Edna's father wants her to marry wealthy Count He-Ha. Charlie, Edna's true love, impersonates the Count at dinner, but the real Count shows up and Charlie is thrown out.
