Title: The Bride Came C.O.D. (1941)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: William Keighley
Main Cast: James Cagney, Bette Davis, Stuart Erwin, Eugene Pallette, Jack Carson
Keywords: kidnapping, pilot

Overview: A financially-strapped charter pilot hires himself to an oil tycoon to kidnap his madcap daughter and prevent her from marrying a vapid band leader.
