Title: Love Me No More (2008)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Jean Becker
Main Cast: Albert Dupontel, Marie-Josée Croze, Pierre Vaneck, Alessandra Martines, François Marthouret

Overview: Antoine Méliot is around 40 years old and has everything he needs to be happy: a beautiful wife, two adorable children, friends he can count on, a pretty house in the Yvelines and money. But one day he decides to ruin everything in one weekend.
