Title: Macaroni (1985)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Ettore Scola
Main Cast: Jack Lemmon, Marcello Mastroianni, Daria Nicolodi, Patrizia Sacchi

Overview: A businessman from the United States returns to Italy for the first time in four decades only to discover that an old girlfriend of his, along with her brother, have involved him in a massive hoax.
