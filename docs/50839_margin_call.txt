Title: Margin Call (2011)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: J.C. Chandor
Main Cast: Kevin Spacey, Paul Bettany, Jeremy Irons, Zachary Quinto, Ashley Williams
Keywords: capitalism, brokerage, wall street, downsizing, financial crisis, stock market, stock market crash, 21st century, investment

Overview: A thriller that revolves around the key people at an investment bank over a 24-hour period during the early stages of the financial crisis.
