Title: The Second Circle (1990)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alexander Sokurov
Main Cast: Петр Александров, Надежда Роднова, Тамара Тимофеева, Александр Быстряков, Сергей Выборнов

Overview: A man tries to come to terms with his father's death and to deal with the mundane details of his burial in a society cut off from spirituality.
