Title: Aanmodderfakker (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michiel ten Horn
Main Cast: Gijs Naber, Yannick van de Velde, Anniek  Pheifer, Roos Wiltink

Overview: Aanmodderfakker is a coming-of-age film, but 15 years too late. The film is about the 32-year old student Thijs who fils his life with beer, chicks and working in a large electronics store. But then something grows between Thijs and Lisa, the 16-year old baby-sitter at Thijs' sister.
