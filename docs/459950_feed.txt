Title: Feed (2017)
Genres: Drama
Director: Tommy Bertelsen
Main Cast: Troian Bellisario, Tom Felton, Ben Winchell, James Remar, Paula Malcomson
Keywords: twins, valedictorian, eating disorder, highschool kids

Overview: Olivia and Matthew Grey, 18-year-old twins born into a world of privilege and high expectations. There are almost no boundaries between them - even their dreams are connected.
