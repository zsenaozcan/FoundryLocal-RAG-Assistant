Title: Il bell'Antonio (1960)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mauro Bolognini
Main Cast: Marcello Mastroianni, Claudia Cardinale, Pierre Brasseur, Rina Morelli, Tomás Milián
Keywords: impotence, marriage, gossip, consumation, virility

Overview: Problems arise for Antonio Magnano when he is unable to consummate his marriage to the beautiful Barbara Puglisi and his virility is called into question. Despite the fact that he loves his beautiful wife and they have otherwise been happily married for a year, his problem becomes a source of contention for all concerned.
