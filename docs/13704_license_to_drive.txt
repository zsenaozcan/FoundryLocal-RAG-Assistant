Title: License to Drive (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Greg Beeman
Main Cast: Corey Haim, Corey Feldman, Carol Kane, Heather Graham, Richard Masur
Keywords: champagne, ferrari, driving test, underage drinking, drunk driving, teem

Overview: Teenager Les Anderson thinks his life can't get any worse after he flunks his driver's exam, but he's wrong. Even though he didn't receive his license, Les refuses to break his date with the cool Mercedes Lane, and he decides to lift his family's prize luxury car for the occasion. Unfortunately, Mercedes sneaks some booze along and passes out drunk, and a confused Les makes the bad decision of enlisting his rebellious friend, Dean, to help.
