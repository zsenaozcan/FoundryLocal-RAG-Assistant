Title: Yongary, Monster from the Deep (1967)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Foreign
Director: Kim Ki-duk
Main Cast: Yeong-il Oh, Jeong-im Nam, Kwang Ho Lee, Cho Kyoung-min

Overview: Earthquakes in central Korea turn out to be the work of Yongary, a prehistoric gasoline-eating reptile that soon goes on a rampage through Seoul.
