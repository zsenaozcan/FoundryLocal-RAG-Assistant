Title: Tru Loved (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Stewart Wade
Main Cast: Najarra Townsend, Jake Abel, Matthew Thompson, Alexandra Paul, Tye Olson

Overview: Recently relocated from San Francisco to conservative suburbia by her lesbian mothers, Tru struggles like all teens to fit in and find love, but her quest is complicated by sexual politics, closed minds, and closeted friends as she seeks to establish her school's first Gay-Straight Alliance.
