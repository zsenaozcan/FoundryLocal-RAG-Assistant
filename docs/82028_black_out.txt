Title: Black Out (2012)
Genres: Crime, Action, Comedy
Director: Arne Toonen
Main Cast: Raymond Thiry, Kim van Kooten, Bas Keijzer, Renee Fokker, Katja Schuurman

Overview: After ruthless gangsters wrongfully accuse him of stealing from them, a retired criminal has 24 hours to creep back into his past life and retrieve a bag of coke before his fiancée is killed.
