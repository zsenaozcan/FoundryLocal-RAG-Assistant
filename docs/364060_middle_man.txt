Title: Middle Man (2017)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Crime, Thriller
Director: Ned Crowley
Main Cast: Jim O'Heir, Andrew J. West, Josh McDermitt, Anne Dudek, Tracey Walter
Keywords: road trip, stand-up comedy, murder, las vegas

Overview: When a hapless, unfunny, wanna-be comedian picks up a mysterious hitchhiker on his way to Vegas to audition for the Monte Guy show, he unwittingly becomes trapped in a desert-town killing spree that also has a very unexpected consequence - it improves his comedy.
