Title: Grimm Love (2006)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Martin Weisz
Main Cast: Keri Russell, Thomas Kretschmann, Thomas Huber, Rainier Meissner, Marcus Lucas
Keywords: videoband, kanibalismus, victim, murder, killer

Overview: In Germany, as graduate student Katie Armstrong researches cannibal killer Oliver Hagen for her thesis, she becomes obsessed with her subject and ultimately plunges into a lifestyle similar to Hagen's and the thousands of people like him.
