Title: Papanasam (2015)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Family, Thriller
Director: Jeethu Joseph
Main Cast: Kamal Haasan, Gowthami, Kalabhavan Mani, Asha Sarath, Niveda Thomas
Keywords: innocence, revenge, family relationships, remake of malayalam film

Overview: A cinephile tries to protect his family by hiding a heat-of-the-moment murder committed by his daughter by taking cues from the films he has watched. With the victim the son of a cop, can he get away with it?
