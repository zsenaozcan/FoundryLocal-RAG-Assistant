Title: Tales from Muppetland: The Frog Prince (1971)
Genres: Fantasy, Family, Music
Director: Jim Henson
Main Cast: Gordon Thomson, Jim Henson, Frank Oz, Jerry Nelson, Carl Banas

Overview: A retelling of the classic fairy tale of The Frog Prince featuring Kermit the Frog as the narrator, Kermit's nephew Robin as the Frog Prince, Sir Robin, and Sweetums, among others.
