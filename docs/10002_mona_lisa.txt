Title: Mona Lisa (1986)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Romance
Director: Neil Jordan
Main Cast: Bob Hoskins, Cathy Tyson, Michael Caine, Robbie Coltrane, Clarke Peters
Keywords: london england, prostitute, ex-detainee, chauffeur, hoodlum

Overview: George has just been released from prison, and manages to get a job driving an expensive call girl from customer to customer. Initially they don't get on - he doesn't fit in with the high class customers Simone services. Will they ever get on?
