Title: Merantau (2009)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Drama
Director: Gareth Evans
Main Cast: Iko Uwais, Sisca Jessica, Christine Hakim, Mads Koudal, Yusuf Aulia

Overview: In Minangkabau, West Sumatera, Yuda a skilled practitioner of Silat Harimau is in the final preparations to begin his "Merantau" a century's old rites-of-passage to be carried out by the community's young men that will see him leave the comforts of his idyllic farming village and make a name for himself in the bustling city of Jakarta.
