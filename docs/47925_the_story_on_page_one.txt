Title: The Story on Page One (1959)
Genres: Drama
Director: Clifford Odets
Main Cast: Rita Hayworth, Anthony Franciosa, Gig Young, Mildred Dunnock, Hugh Griffith
Keywords: lawyer, trial, widower, courtroom

Overview: An adulterous couple is accused of murder after the woman's husband is shot and killed during a scuffle. A high-profile court case tells the story.
