Title: The Amazing Dr. Clitterhouse (1938)
Genres: Drama, Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Anatole Litvak
Main Cast: Edward G. Robinson, Claire Trevor, Humphrey Bogart, Allen Jenkins, Donald Crisp
Keywords: robbery, paranoia, psychology, research, society, jewelry, sociopath, dark comedy, doctor, gangster

Overview: A wealthy society doctor decides to research the medical aspects of criminal behaviour by becoming one himself. He joins a gang of thieves and proceeds to wrest leadership of the gang away from it's extremely resentful leader.
