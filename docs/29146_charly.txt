Title: Charly (1968)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Ralph Nelson
Main Cast: Cliff Robertson, Claire Bloom, Lilia Skala, Leon Janney, Ruth White
Keywords: experiment, mouse, intelligence test, genius

Overview: An experiment on a simpleton turns him into a genius. When he discovers what has been done to him he struggles with whether or not what was done to his was right.
