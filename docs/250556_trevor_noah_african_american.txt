Title: Trevor Noah: African American (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ryan Polito
Main Cast: Trevor Noah
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Internationally acclaimed comedian Trevor Noah challenges the status quo through his unique perspective, breaking down cultural flaws on all sides and finding our mutual connections with laughter. Born a mixed-race child in South Africa under Apartheid, 'Trevor Noah: African American' is Noah¿s search for identity in a world obsessed with labels.
