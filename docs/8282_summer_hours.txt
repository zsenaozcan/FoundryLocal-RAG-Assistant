Title: Summer Hours (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Olivier Assayas
Main Cast: Juliette Binoche, Charles Berling, Jérémie Renier, Edith Scob, Dominique Reymond
Keywords: brother sister relationship, summer, family

Overview: Two brothers and a sister witness the disappearance of their childhood memories when they must relinquish the family belongings to ensure their deceased mother's succession.
