Title: Who the Hell Is Juliette? (1997)
Genres: Documentary, Foreign
Director: Carlos Marcovich
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Cinematographer Carlos Macovich met Yuliet Ortega, a young "jinetera" (prostitute) from Havana, when he shot a video in Cuba, starring model Fabiola Quiroz. When he realized that the two women had not seen their respective fathers for many years, he made this documentary, which is also a reflection on the process of filmmaking.
