Title: Every-Night Dreams (1933)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mikio Naruse
Main Cast: Sumiko Kurishima, Tatsuo Saitô, Teruko Kojima, Takeshi Sakamoto, Chôko Iida
Keywords: bar, son, tokyo japan, single mother, silent film, husband

Overview: In the formally ravishing Every-Night Dreams, set in the dockside neighborhoods of Tokyo, a single mother works tirelessly as a Ginza bar hostess to ensure a better life for her young son—until her long-lost husband returns.
