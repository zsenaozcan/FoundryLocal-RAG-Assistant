Title: The Detective (1968)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Gordon Douglas
Main Cast: Frank Sinatra, Lee Remick, Jacqueline Bisset, Ralph Meeker, Jack Klugman
Keywords: police detective, gay man, gritty

Overview: Police detective Joe Leland investigates the murder of a homosexual man. While investigating, he discovers links to official corruption in New York City in this drama that delves into a world of sex and drugs.
