Title: Dracula's Widow (1988)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Christopher Coppola
Main Cast: Sylvia Kristel, Josef Sommer, Lenny Von Dohlen, Marc Coppola, Stefan Schnabel
Keywords: vampire, dracula, independent film, wax museum, female vampire
