Title: Phantom Pain (2009)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Matthias Emcke
Main Cast: Til Schweiger, Jana Pallaske, Stipe Erceg, Julia Brendler, Alwara Höfels

Overview: Marc is a passionate cyclist and urban slacker who avoids work and responsibility. Marc's life changes from one moment to the next when he suffers a life-changing accident. He can either wallow in self-pity or tackle the beast called life with the help of love and willpower.
