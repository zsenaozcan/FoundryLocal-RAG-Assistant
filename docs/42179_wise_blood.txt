Title: Wise Blood (1979)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: John Huston
Main Cast: Brad Dourif, John Huston, Harry Dean Stanton, Dan Shor, Ned Beatty
Keywords: christianity, southern usa, based on novel, redemption, preacher, blindness

Overview: A Southerner--young, poor, ambitious but uneducated--determines to become something in the world. He decides that the best way to do that is to become a preacher and start up his own church.
