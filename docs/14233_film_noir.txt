Title: Film Noir (2007)
Genres: Mystery, Animation, Crime
Director: D. Jud Jones
Main Cast: Mark Keller, Bettina Devin
Keywords: amnesia, nudity

Overview: Private detective Sam Ruben's clever plan falls apart with the onset of amnesia. Everyone is trying to kill him and he doesn't know why. The classic film noir milieu now comes to you in the form of an animated feature.
