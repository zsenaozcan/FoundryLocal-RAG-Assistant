Title: Tubelight (2017)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Kabir Khan
Main Cast: Salman Khan, Zhu Zhu, Sohail Khan, Om Puri, Yashpal Sharma
Keywords: history

Overview: Laxman Singh Bisht (Salman) is nicknamed tube light by his neighbours because he is feeble-minded. Despite being special, Laxman lives by one life-lesson; keep your faith alive and you can do almost anything, even stop a war.
