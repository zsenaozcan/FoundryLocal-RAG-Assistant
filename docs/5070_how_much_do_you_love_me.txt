Title: How Much Do You Love Me? (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Bertrand Blier
Main Cast: Monica Bellucci, Bernard Campan, Gérard Depardieu, Jean-Pierre Darroussin, François Rollin
Keywords: prostitute, illegal prostitution, sadomasoquismo

Overview: After winning the lottery, François goes to a bar in Pigalle and offers one hundred thousand Euros per month to a prostitute named Daniela to live with him as his wife until his money runs out.
