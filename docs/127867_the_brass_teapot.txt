Title: The Brass Teapot (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Thriller
Director: Ramaa Mosley
Main Cast: Juno Temple, Michael Angarano, Alexis Bledel, Billy Magnussen, Alia Shawkat
Keywords: fantasy, based on comic, money, magical object, woman director

Overview: When a couple discovers that a brass teapot makes them money whenever they hurt themselves, they must come to terms with how far they are willing to go.
