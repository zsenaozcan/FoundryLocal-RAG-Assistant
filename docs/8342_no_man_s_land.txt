Title: No Man's Land (2001)
Genres: Drama, Action, History
Director: Danis Tanović
Main Cast: Branko Đurić, Rene Bitorajac, Filip Šovagović, Georges Siatidis, Sacha Kremer
Keywords: bosnia and herzegovina, recruit, bosnian war of 1992-1995

Overview: Two soldiers from opposite sites get stuck between the front lines in the same trench. The UN is asked to free them and both sides agree on a ceasefire, but will they stick to it?
