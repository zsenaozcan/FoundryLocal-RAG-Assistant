Title: Japanese Girls at the Harbor (1933)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hiroshi Shimizu
Main Cast: Michiko Oikawa, Yukiko Inoue, Ureo Egawa, Ranko Sawa, Yumeko Aizome
Keywords: harbor

Overview: Set in the port city of Yokohama, two girls, Sunako and Dora who attend a Christian school, pledge to be friends. But when a youth named Henry appears on his motorcycle and offers to take Sunako for a ride, we know that this friendship won't last and that the lives of both girls will change in ways they are barely able to comprehend, and can do little to change.
