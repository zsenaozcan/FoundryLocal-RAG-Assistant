Title: Some Dogs Bite (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Marc Munden
Main Cast: Aaron D. Taylor, Thomas Brodie-Sangster, Kian Smith, Cory Smith, Lewis Ryan Dickinson

Overview: Halliday (19), Casey (14) and baby Severino have been split up after their mothers death. Casey is in care; Severino has been fostered with a view to adoption, and no one really cares what Halliday does; even his mum thought he was a waste of space.
