Title: Jacob's Ladder (1990)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Horror
Director: Adrian Lyne
Main Cast: Tim Robbins, Elizabeth Peña, Danny Aiello, Matt Craven, Pruitt Taylor Vince
Keywords: violence, postal worker, figment of imagination, oneiric
