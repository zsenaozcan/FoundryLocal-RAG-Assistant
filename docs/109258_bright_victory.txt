Title: Bright Victory (1951)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Mark Robson
Main Cast: Arthur Kennedy, Peggy Dow, Julie Adams, James Edwards, Will Geer
Keywords: blind, veteran

Overview: A soldier blinded in war returns home and attempts to adjust to civilian life. Director Mark Robson's 1951 drama stars Arthur Kennedy (Academy Award nomination, Best Actor, for his performance), Peggy Dow, James Edwards, Will Geer, Nana Bryant, Julie Adams, Jim Backus, Richard Egan and Murray Hamilton.
