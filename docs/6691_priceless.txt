Title: Priceless (2006)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Pierre Salvadori
Main Cast: Gad Elmaleh, Audrey Tautou, Marie-Christine Adam, Vernon Dobtcheff, Jacques Spiesser
Keywords: broken engagement, bartender, shortage of money, greed, engagement
