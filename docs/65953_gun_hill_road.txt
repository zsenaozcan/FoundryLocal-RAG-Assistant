Title: Gun Hill Road (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rashaad Ernesto Green
Main Cast: Judy Reyes, Isiah Whitlock Jr., Esai Morales, Tyrone Brown, Reinaldo Marcus Green
Keywords: puerto rican, based on true story, independent film, family relationships,  

Overview: An ex-con returns home to the Bronx after three year in prison to discover his wife estranged and his teenage son exploring a sexual transformation that will put the fragile bonds of their family to the test.
