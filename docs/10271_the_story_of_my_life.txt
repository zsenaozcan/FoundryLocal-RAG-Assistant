Title: The Story Of My Life (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Laurent Tirard
Main Cast: Edouard Baer, Clovis Cornillac, Marie-Josée Croze, Alice Taglioni, Eric Berger
Keywords: paris

Overview: Raphael is a ghostwriter who takes a job writing for famous footy player Kevin. To his delight and his girlfriend, Murials horror, Kevins current girlfriend is an old (easily rekindled) flame of Raphaels. A freak accident leaves a close friend dead and Raphael is forced to reconsider his priorities.
