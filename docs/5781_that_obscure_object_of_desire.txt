Title: That Obscure Object of Desire (1977)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Luis Buñuel
Main Cast: Carole Bouquet, Ángela Molina, Fernando Rey, Milena Vukotic, Julien Bertheau
Keywords: paris, bomb, mistake in person, bucket, lausanne, love, independent film, mother daughter relationship, train, maid

Overview: Set in Spain and France against the backdrop of a terrorist insurgency, the film tells the story of an aging Frenchman who falls in love with a young woman who repeatedly frustrates his romantic and sexual desires.
