Title: The Bishop's Bedroom (1977)
Genres: Crime, Comedy, Drama
Director: Dino Risi
Main Cast: Ugo Tognazzi, Patrick Dewaere, Ornella Muti, Lia Tanzi, Gabriella Giacobbe
Keywords: italy, female nudity, sex, based on novel, boat, yacht, storm, sister-in-law, sex comedy, nude sunbathing

Overview: Mario (Tognazzi), a rich and eccentric war hero befriends Marco (Dewaere), a loner with a sailboat and takes him home to meet his estranged wife Cleofe (Lia Tanzi Gabriella) and sexually repressed sister in law Matilde (Muti). Mario confesses his love for Matilde and so ensues a love triangle.
