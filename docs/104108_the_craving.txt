Title: The Craving (2008)
Genres: Horror
Director: Sean Dillon
Main Cast: Tricia Allen, Grayson Berry, Jesse C. Boyd, Anselm Clinard, Wallis Herst

Overview: A group of college students embarking on a cross country road trip to the Burning Man Festival find themselves stranded in desert. Come nightfall a vicious predatory monster comes out searching for a meal. Will anyone survive the nocturnal attacks from this ferocious beast?
