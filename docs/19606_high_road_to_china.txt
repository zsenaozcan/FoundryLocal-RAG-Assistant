Title: High Road to China (1983)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, History

Main Cast: Tom Selleck, Bess Armstrong, Wilford Brimley, Jack Weston, Robert Morley
Keywords: china, nepal, pilot, afghanistan, aerial combat, alcoholic, socialite, biplane, declared dead
