Title: Fools in the Mountains (1957)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Edith Carlmar
Main Cast: Leif Juster, Unni Bernhoft, Frank Robert, Nanna Stenersen, Anne Lise Wang
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The story takes place on Hurlumhei skiing hotel, where the porter Poppe, unforgettably played by Leif Juster, is experiencing one complication after the other with a rich cast of characters. Fjols til Fjells ("Fools in the mountains") is a movie about white phones, disguised Piccolos, charming actors and alcoholic businessmen.
