Title: Beck 30 - Sjukhusmorden (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Stephan Apelgren
Main Cast: Mikael Persbrandt, Peter Haber, Ingvar Hirdwall, Rebecka Hemse, Måns Nathanaelson

Overview: An elderly woman dies in a hospital in Stockholm and it turns out that she suffered from an incurable disease. Her son refuses to believe that it was the disease that was the primary cause of her death.
