Title: Somers Town (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Shane Meadows
Main Cast: Thomas Turgoose, Piotr Jagiello, Elisa Lasowski, Kate Dickie, Perry Benson
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Two teenagers, both newcomers to London, forge an unlikely friendship over the course of a hot summer. Tomo (Thomas Turgoose) is a runaway from Nottingham; Marek (Piotr Jagiello) lives in the district of Somers Town, between King's Cross and Euston stations, where his dad is working on a new rail link.
