Title: The Wistful Widow of Wagon Gap (1947)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Western
Director: Charles Barton
Main Cast: Bud Abbott, Lou Costello, Marjorie Main, Audrey Young, George Cleveland
Keywords: montana, photograph

Overview: Chester Wooley (Lou Costello) and Duke Egan (Bud Abbott) are traveling salesmen who make a stopover in Wagon Gap, Montana while enroute to California. During the stopover, a notorious criminal, Fred Hawkins, is murdered, and the two are charged with the crime.
