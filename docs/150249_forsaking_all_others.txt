Title: Forsaking All Others (1934)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: W.S. Van Dyke
Main Cast: Robert Montgomery, Joan Crawford, Clark Gable, Charles Butterworth, Billie Burke
Keywords: cabin, wedding, new york city, elopement, adirondack mountains

Overview: Dill leaves Mary standing at the altar in order to marry his old flame, Connie, instead. Knowing that Mary still has feelings for Dill, Jeff keeps quiet about his own love for her..
