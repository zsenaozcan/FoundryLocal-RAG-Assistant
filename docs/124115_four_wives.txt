Title: Four Wives (1939)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: Priscilla Lane, Rosemary Lane, Lola Lane, Gale Page, Claude Rains
Keywords: wife

Overview: In this sequel to Four Daughters, Adam Lemp and his daughters have gone on with life after the death of Mickey Borden. Ann, Mickey's widow, falls in love with Felix Dietz, but on the day of her engagement discovers that she carries Mickey's child.
