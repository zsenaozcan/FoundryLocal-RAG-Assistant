Title: Absence of Malice (1981)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Sydney Pollack
Main Cast: Paul Newman, Sally Field, Bob Balaban, Melinda Dillon, Luther Adler
Keywords: prosecutor, newspaper reporter

Overview: A prosecutor leaks a false story that liquor warehouse owner Michael Gallagher is involved in the murder of a union head, and as newspaper reporter Megan Carter picks up the story and attempts to find the real truth, Gallagher's life begins to unravel.
