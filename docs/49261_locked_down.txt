Title: Locked Down (2010)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Daniel Zirilli
Main Cast: Vinnie Jones, Tony Schiena, Rashad Evans, Kimbo Slice, Forrest Griffin
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Danny, a respected cop, is setup after an investigation goes wrong. While laying low in his new jail cell, Danny is forced to get involved in the inmates underground cage fighting circuit. With the help of Irving, a former mixed martial arts champion, Danny devises a scheme to bring justice and innocence to his family's name, if he can get out of the cage alive.
