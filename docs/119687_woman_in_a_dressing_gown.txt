Title: Woman in a Dressing Gown (1957)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: J. Lee Thompson
Main Cast: Sylvia Syms, Anthony Quayle, Yvonne Mitchell, Andrew Ray, Carole Lesley
Keywords: divorce, extramarital affair, desertion

Overview: A married, middle-aged woman is shocked to discover that her husband, who she thought was content in their marriage, has become infatuated with a beautiful younger woman and is planning to leave his family for her.
