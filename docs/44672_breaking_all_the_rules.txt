Title: Breaking All the Rules (1985)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: James Orr
Main Cast: Carl Marotte, Carolyn Dunn, Thor Bishopric, Rachel Hayward, Michael Rudder
Keywords: female nudity, detective, breakdance, fireworks, nudity, independent film, summer, criminal, amusement park, roller coaster
