Title: The Last Valley (1971)
Genres: Action, Drama, War
Director: James Clavell
Main Cast: Michael Caine, Omar Sharif, Florinda Bolkan, Nigel Davenport, Per Oscarsson
Keywords: germany, war, 17th century

Overview: People in a small German village in the last valley to remain untouched by the devastating Thirty Years' War try to exist in peace with a group of soldiers occupying the valley.
