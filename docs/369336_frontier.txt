Title: Frontier (2001)
Genres: Adventure, Action
Director: David Zellner
Main Cast: Wiley Wiggins, David Zellner, Nathan Zellner

Overview: Strangers in a strange land, Bulbovia’s dimmest explorers attempt to dutifully colonize a new world but soon find their buttoned-up shirts and rational thoughts melting away into beetle-eating, tree-molesting, wife-swapping chaos.
