Title: Captive Women (1952)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Stuart Gilmore
Main Cast: Robert Clarke, Margaret Field, Ron Randell, Gloria Saunders, Stuart Randall
Keywords: new york, mutant, post-apocalyptic

Overview: In a post-apocalyptic New York City, three tribes of mutants (the Norms, the Mutates and the Upriver people) battle each other to survive.
