Title: The Mask (1988)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Fiorella Infascelli
Main Cast: Helena Bonham Carter, Michael Maloney, Feodor Chaliapin Jr.
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A debauched nobleman offers himself to a beautiful woman, but she is repelled by his advances. He dons a mask and tries again, and this time is more successful. But the mask cannot conceal the evil in his soul.
