Title: Gypsy (1993)
Genres: TV Movie, Comedy, Drama, Music
Director: Emile Ardolino
Main Cast: Bette Midler, Peter Riegert, Cynthia Gibb, Ed Asner, Christine Ebersole
Keywords: musical, drama, dysfunctional family, based on play or musical, domineering mother

Overview: Based on the autobiography of Gypsy Rose Lee, this made-for-TV movie - previously a Broadway smash and a 1962 film - depicts the life and times of a stripper and her domineering mother.
