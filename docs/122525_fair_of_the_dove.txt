Title: Fair of the Dove (1935)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Benito Perojo
Main Cast: Miguel Ligero, Roberto Rey, Raquel Rodrigo, Sélica Pérez Carpio, Dolores Cortés

Overview: A young printer's relationship with his girlfriend is in trouble after she accepts another suitor's invitation to the fair. This adaption of a popular operetta aims to please with a simple storyline and goofy slapstick.
