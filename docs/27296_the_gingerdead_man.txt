Title: The Gingerdead Man (2005)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Comedy
Director: Charles Band
Main Cast: Gary Busey, Robin Sydney, Ryan Locke, Larry Cedar, Jonathan Chase
Keywords: puppet, revenge, slasher, killer, gingerbread man

Overview: An evil yet adorable Gingerbread man comes to life with the soul of a convicted killer, and this real life cookie monster wreaks havoc on the girl who sent the killer to the electric chair.
