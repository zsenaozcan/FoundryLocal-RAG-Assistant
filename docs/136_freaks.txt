Genres: Drama, Horror

Main Cast: Harry Earles, Daisy Earles, Olga Baclanova, Henry Victor, Leila Hyams
Keywords: circus, dwarves, infidelity, attempted murder, clown, beauty, siamese twins, revenge, midnight movie, newlywed
