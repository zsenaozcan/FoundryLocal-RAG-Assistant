Title: Kevin & Perry Go Large (2000)
Genres: Music, Comedy
Director: Ed Bye
Main Cast: Harry Enfield, Kathy Burke, Rhys Ifans, James Fleet, Laura Fraser
Keywords: holiday, disc jockey, becoming an adult, puberty, first time, virgin, longing, beatnik, ibiza, youth

Overview: When stubborn, spotty Kevin and his equally hopeless best friend Perry go on holiday to the party island Ibiza, they see it as their big chance to become superstar club DJs and, more importantly, to lose their virginities. But they aren't prepared for the interference of top DJ Eyeball Paul, not to mention the embarrassment factor of Kevin's long-suffering parents.
