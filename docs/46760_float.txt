Title: Float (2007)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Kareem Mortimer
Main Cast: Paul Cowderoy, Daria Delancey, Rukenya Demeritte, Sania Johnson, Jonathan Murray
Keywords: independent film

Overview: The story of a young painter from the Bahamas, Jonny Roberts, who travels to an exotic island to find inspiration but finds unexpected love and adventure in a complex and tortured Romeo.
