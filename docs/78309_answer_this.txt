Title: Answer This! (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Christopher Farah
Main Cast: Christopher Gorham, Arielle Kebbel, Chris Parnell, Nelson Franklin
Keywords: trivia

Overview: Brilliant trivia whiz Paul Tarson is great at answering little questions but horrible at answering the big ones – like what’s he going to do after grad school? When his father withdraws his funding, Paul is forced to confront his future and much procrastinated dissertation deadline…until he discovers the First Annual Ann Arbor Pub Trivia Tournament.
