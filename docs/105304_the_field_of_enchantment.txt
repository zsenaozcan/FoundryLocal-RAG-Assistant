Title: The Field of Enchantment (2011)
Genres: Documentary, Family
Director: Claude Nuridsany
Main Cast: Simon Delagnes, Lindsey Henocque, Jean-Claude Ayrinhac, Denis Podalydès
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A man remembers holidays at his uncle in a little village in the French countryside when he was something like 10. He feels so bored until he finds a pond and starts discovering the life in it.
