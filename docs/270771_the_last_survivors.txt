Title: The Last Survivors (2014)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, Action
Director: Thomas S. Hammock
Main Cast: Haley Lu Richardson, Booboo Stewart, Max Charles, Nicole Fox, Michael Welch
Keywords: post-apocalyptic, revenge, murder, survival, drought

Overview: In the near future, society collapses and water becomes scarce. When a greedy water baron starts violently clearing out survivors, Kendal, a 17-year-old teenager, fights the baron's henchman to keep a well open.
