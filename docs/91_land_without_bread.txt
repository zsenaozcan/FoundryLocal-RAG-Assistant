Title: Land Without Bread (1933)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Luis Buñuel
Main Cast: Abel Jacquin, Alexandre O'Neill
Keywords: spain, river, suffering, work, misery, desolateness, remoteness, hygiene, village, teacher

Overview: Las Hurdes – Tierra Sin Pan / Land Without Bread / Unpromised Land is a surrealist documentary filmed in Spain in 1932. It is the result of a two month trip through the Las Hurdes region. The film shows the contrasts between the village and the towns in the north of Extremadura, Salamanca and along the border of Portugal.
