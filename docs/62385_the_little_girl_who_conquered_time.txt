Title: The Little Girl Who Conquered Time (1983)
Genres: Science Fiction, Romance
Director: Nobuhiko Ôbayashi
Main Cast: Tomoyo Harada, Ryôichi Takayanagi, Toshinori Omi, Yukari Tsuda, Ittoku Kishibe

Overview: Yoshiyama Kazuko is in the 3rd grade of junior high. One day, she's cleaning in the laboratory, she notices of the smell of lavender, and fainted. Since then, she acquires a mysterious power to travel time.
