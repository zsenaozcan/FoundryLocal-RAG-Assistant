Title: 911 Nightmare (2016)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Craig Moss
Main Cast: Fiona Gubelmann, Drew Fuller, Scott Bailey, Heather Mazur, Laura Niemi

Overview: Officer Chris McCullers was gravely injured in a shootout and now works on 911-dispatch. Resentful that her career has ended up here, Chris receives a call from a scared kid and dismisses it as a prank. The next day, she learns that the call was all too real: there's been a murder.
