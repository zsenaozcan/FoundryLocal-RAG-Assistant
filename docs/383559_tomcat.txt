Title: Tomcat (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Klaus Händl
Main Cast: Lukas Turtur, Philipp Hochmair, Thomas Stipsits, Manuel Rubey, Gabriela Hegedüs
Keywords: gay, cat, homosexuality, gay relationship, gay interest, gay marriage, gay man, animal lover, lgbt, cat lover

Overview: Andreas and Stefan lead a happy and passionate life: Together with their beloved tomcat Moses, they live in a beautiful old house in Vienna's vineyards. They work as a musician and as a scheduler in the same orchestra and they love their large circle of friends. An unexpected and inexplicable outburst of violence suddenly shakes up the relationship and calls everything into question - the blind spot that resides in all of us.
