Title: The Devils (1971)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Ken Russell
Main Cast: Oliver Reed, Vanessa Redgrave, Dudley Sutton, Max Adrian, Gemma Jones
Keywords: christianity, inquisition, hysteria, biography, controversy, priest, torture, historical, banned film, catholic church

Overview: A dramatised historical account of the rise and fall of Urbain Grandier, a 17th-century Roman Catholic priest accused of  witchcraft following alleged demonic possessions of sexually repressed nuns.
