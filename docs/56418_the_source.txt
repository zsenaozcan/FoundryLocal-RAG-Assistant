Title: The Source (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Radu Mihaileanu
Main Cast: Leïla Bekhti, Hafsia Herzi, Biyouna, Sabrina Ouazani, Saleh Bakri
Keywords: wife husband relationship, village life, female protagonist

Overview: A comedy/drama set in a village and centered on a battle of the sexes, where women threaten to withhold sexual favours as long as the men refuse to install a water pipe.
