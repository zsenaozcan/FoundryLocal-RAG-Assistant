Title: The Laughing Policeman (1973)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Stuart Rosenberg
Main Cast: Walter Matthau, Bruce Dern, Louis Gossett, Jr., Albert Paulsen, Anthony Zerbe
Keywords: mass murder, murder, suspense, mystery, killer

Overview: It's tough beat for San Francisco police lieutenant Jake Martin (Walter Matthau) when he has to investigate a city bus massacre in which a madman opened machine gunfire on eight people.
