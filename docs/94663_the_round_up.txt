Title: The Round-Up (1966)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Miklós Jancsó
Main Cast: Zoltán Latinovits, Görbe János, Tibor Molnár, Gábor Agárdi, András Kozák
Keywords: detention camp, austria-hungary
