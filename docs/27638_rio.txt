Title: Rio (1939)
Genres: Crime
Director: John Brahm
Main Cast: Basil Rathbone, Victor McLaglen, Sigrid Gurie, Robert Cummings, Leo Carrillo
Keywords: rio de janeiro, escape from prison

Overview: Diabolical French capitalist Paul Reynard is forced to leave Irene, his bride of one year, when he is arrested for the crimes of forgery and embezzlement and sentenced to a penal colony off the coast of South America.
