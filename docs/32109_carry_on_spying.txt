Title: Carry On Spying (1964)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Gerald Thomas
Main Cast: Kenneth Williams, Barbara Windsor, Bernard Cribbins, Charles Hawtrey, Eric Barker
Keywords: spy, villain, undercover agent, british, farce, espionage, carry on, spoof, britain, female spy

Overview: Carry On favourite Barbara Windsor makes her debut in this outrageous send-up of the James Bond movies. Fearless agent Desmond Simpkins and James Bind, aided and abetted by the comely Agent Honeybutt and Agent Crump, battle against the evil powers of international bad guys STENCH and their three cronies.
