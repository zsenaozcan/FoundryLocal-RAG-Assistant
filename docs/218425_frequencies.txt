Title: Frequencies (2013)
Genres: Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Darren Paul Fisher
Main Cast: Daniel Fraser, Eleanor Wyld, Owen Pugh, David Broughton-Davies, Emma Powell

Overview: This science fiction love story follows the forbidden relationship between a 'low born' boy and a 'high born' girl in an alternate reality where every person's relationships and life worth are determined by their innate "frequencies".
