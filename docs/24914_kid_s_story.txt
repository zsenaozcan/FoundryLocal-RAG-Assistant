Title: Kid's Story (2003)
Genres: Science Fiction, Animation
Director: Shinichiro Watanabe
Main Cast: Clayton Watson, Keanu Reeves, Carrie-Anne Moss, Kevin Michael Richardson, John DeMita

Overview: A high school student is haunted by thoughts of "The Matrix" and a person named "Neo". Part of the Animatrix collection of animated shorts set in the Matrix universe.
