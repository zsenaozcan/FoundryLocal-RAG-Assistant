Title: Accident (2009)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Soi Cheang
Main Cast: Louis Koo, Richie Ren, Michelle Ye Xuan, Stanley Fung, Lam Suet
Keywords: perfect crime, hong kong

Overview: A self-styled accident choreographer, the Brain is a professional hitman who kills his victims by trapping them in well crafted accidents that look like unfortunate mishaps but are in fact perfectly staged acts of crime. When the teams next assignment goes disastrously wrong, Brain begins to suspect that someone else has planned an ‘accident’ on them.
