Title: Mr. India (1987)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Science Fiction
Director: Shekhar Kapur
Main Cast: Anil Kapoor, Sridevi Kapoor, Amrish Puri, Satish Kaushik, Sharat Saxena
Keywords: orphanage, super powers

Overview: A poor but big-hearted man takes orphans into his home. After discovering his scientist father's invisibility device, he rises to the occasion and fights to save his children and all of India from the clutches of a megalomaniac.
