Title: Hangar 10 (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Daniel Simpson
Main Cast: Danny Shayler, Abbie Salt, Robert Curtis
Keywords: found footage

Overview: 33 years after the infamous Rendlesham Forest UFO incident, three metal detector enthusiasts hunting for Saxon gold in the same region, capture incredible footage of UFO's whilst filming their expedition. As night falls and with their navigation equipment failing, they find themselves facing a terrifying encounter with an unforgiving alien presence.
