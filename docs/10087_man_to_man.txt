Title: Man to Man (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Régis Wargnier
Main Cast: Joseph Fiennes, Kristin Scott Thomas, Hugh Bonneville, Lomama Boseki, Cécile Bayiha
Keywords: scotland, africa, pygmy, colonisation, explorer

Overview: An epic about anthropologists who hunt and capture pygmies for study back in Europe, in an attempt to illustrate the link between man and ape.
