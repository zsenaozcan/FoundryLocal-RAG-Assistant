Title: Magic Hunter (1994)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ildikó Enyedi
Main Cast: Gary Kemp, Sadie Frost, Aleksandr Kaydanovskiy, Péter Vallai, Andor Lukáts
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A battle-scarred, has-been Hungarian cop, tormented by his memories of accidentally killing an innocent woman in his custody, enters into a Faustian pact in which he trades his soul for a handful of "magic" bullets that always hit the mark. A mysterious and mythical story.
