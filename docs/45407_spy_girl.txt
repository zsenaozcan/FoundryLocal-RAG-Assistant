Title: Spy Girl (2004)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Romance
Director: Park Han-jun
Main Cast: Kim Jung-hwa, Gong Yoo, Nam Sang-mi, Jadu, Baek Il-seob
Keywords: spy

Overview: The plot of this film has a young beautiful spy from North Korea going south to capture another rogue spy. She goes undercover at Burger King and soon she's battling with her co-workers for the affections of the local boys, dealing with a culture clash between North and South, and a very cute young man who's posted pictures of her on a website of local "angels" possibly blowing her cover.
