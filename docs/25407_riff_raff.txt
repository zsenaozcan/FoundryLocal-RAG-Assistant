Title: Riff-Raff (1947)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Ted Tetzlaff
Main Cast: Pat O’Brien, Anne Jeffreys, Walter Slezak, Percy Kilbride, Jerome Cowan
Keywords: panama, classic noir, film noir

Overview: A private detective foils the plans of villains attempting to take over Panamanian oilfields when he hides a valuable map in plain sight.
