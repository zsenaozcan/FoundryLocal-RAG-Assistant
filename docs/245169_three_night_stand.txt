Title: Three Night Stand (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Pat Kiely
Main Cast: Sam Huntington, Meaghan Rath, Emmanuelle Chriqui, Reagan Pasternak, Jonathan Cherry
Keywords: ex-girlfriend, cheating husband, ski lodge, feel bad comedy

Overview: A man's plans for a romantic weekend go awry when he learns that his ex-girlfriend, whom he still secretly loves, manages the ski lodge where he and his wife are staying.
