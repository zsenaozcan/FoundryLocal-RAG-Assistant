Title: Blood and Roses (1960)
Genres: Horror, Romance
Director: Roger Vadim
Main Cast: Mel Ferrer, Elsa Martinelli, Annette Vadim, Alberto Bonucci
Keywords: based on novel, vampire, 20th century

Overview: Young Carmilla is jealous of her friend's engagement, and her obsession leads her to the tomb of a female vampire. The vampire possesses her and leads her to kill and terrorise the inhabitants of the estate. But is it all in her mind, or is she really under the control of an ancient vampire ancestor?
