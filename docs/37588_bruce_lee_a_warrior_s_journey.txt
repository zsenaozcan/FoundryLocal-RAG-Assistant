Title: Bruce Lee: A Warrior's Journey (2000)
Genres: Documentary
Director: John Little
Main Cast: Bruce Lee, Jackie Chan, Sammo Hung, Yuen Biao, Chuck Norris
Keywords: martial arts, behind the scenes, asian, asian man, asian lead

Overview: Legendary martial artist Bruce Lee is the subject of this thoughtful documentary by Lee aficionado John Little. Using interviews, behind-the-scenes footage and action sequences from Lee's last (unfinished) film, Game of Death, Little paints a textured, complex portrait of the world's most famous action hero
