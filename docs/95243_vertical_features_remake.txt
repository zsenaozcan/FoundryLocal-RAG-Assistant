Title: Vertical Features Remake (1978)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Peter Greenaway
Main Cast: Colin Cantlie
Keywords: experimental film

Overview: Vertical Features Remake is a film by Peter Greenaway. It portrays the work of a fictional Institute of Reclamation and Restoration as they attempt to assemble raw footage taken by ornithologist Tulse Luper into a short film, in accordance with his notes and structuralist film theory.
