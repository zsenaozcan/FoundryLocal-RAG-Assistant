Title: Deliver Us from Eva (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Gary Hardwick
Main Cast: LL Cool J, Gabrielle Union, Kym Whitley, Meagan Good, Duane Martin
Keywords: romantic comedy

Overview: Eva Dandridge is a very uptight young woman who constantly meddles in the affairs of her sisters and their husbands. Her in-laws, who are tired of Eva interfering in their lives, decide to set her up with someone so she can leave them alone. They end up paying Ray, the local "playboy," $5,000 to date her. The plan goes by smoothly, but troubles comes when Ray actually falls in love with Eva.
