Title: Our Little Sister (2015)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Hirokazu Koreeda
Main Cast: Haruka Ayase, Kaho, Suzu Hirose, Shinichi Tsutsumi, Ryo Kase
Keywords: funeral, family, step sister, step siblings

Overview: A story that revolves around three sisters who live in their grandmother's home and the arrival of their 13-year-old half sister.
