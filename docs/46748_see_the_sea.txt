Title: See the Sea (1997)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: François Ozon
Main Cast: Sasha Hails, Marina de Van, Samantha, Paul Raoux

Overview: Sasha, a young British woman, is living with her baby daughter at Ile d'Yeu, a peaceful beach community. A stranger appears. Her name is Tatiana, she's passing through, and pitches her tent in Sasha's yard. The two women build an odd rapport, and tension builds as events unfold.
