Title: Max & Leon (2016)
Genres: War, Comedy
Director: Jonathan Barré
Main Cast: Grégoire Ludig, David Marsais, Bernard Farcy, Dominique Pinon, Julien Pestel

Overview: In this WW2 epic comedy – in the vein of “La Grande Vadrouille” – our two heroes, Max and Léon, two lazy and partier pals, will try by all possible means to avoid going in the battle zone… This will lead the duo to demented and wild adventures.
