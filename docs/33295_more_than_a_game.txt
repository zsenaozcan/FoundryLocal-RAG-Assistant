Title: More Than a Game (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Kristopher Belman
Main Cast: LeBron James, Romeo Travis, Sian Cotton, Willie McGee, Dru Joyce
Keywords: sport, high school, basketball, high school sports

Overview: This documentary follows NBA superstar LeBron James and four of his talented teammates through the trials and tribulations of high school basketball in Ohio and James' journey to fame.
