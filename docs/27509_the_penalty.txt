Title: The Penalty (1920)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Horror
Director: Wallace Worsley
Main Cast: Charles Clary, Doris Pawn, Claire Adams, Lon Chaney, Jim Mason
Keywords: silent film

Overview: Blizzard, deranged from a childhood operation in which both his legs were perhaps needlessly amputated after an accident, becomes a vicious criminal, and eventually mob leader of the San Francisco underworld. Out for revenge against the surgeon who performed his operation, he undergoes brain surgery which has a chance of altering his anti-social behavior.
