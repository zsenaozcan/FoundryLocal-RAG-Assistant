Title: George's Island (1989)
Genres: Family, Adventure, Drama
Director: Paul Donovan
Main Cast: Ian Bannen, Sheila McCarthy, Maury Chaykin, Nathaniel Moreau, Vickie Ridler
Keywords: island, halloween, pirate, canuxploitation

Overview: This is a tale about two children who are put in a foster home, and then on Halloween they breakout and are rescued by their eccentric grandfather who is in a Halloween costume of an eyeball. They then go to George's island to try and find the treasure of Captain Kidd. Written by Andrew Hazeden
