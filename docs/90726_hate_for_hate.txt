Title: Hate for Hate (1967)
Genres: Western
Director: Domenico Paolella
Main Cast: Antonio Sabàto, John Ireland, Piero Vida, Nadia Marconi, Fernando Sancho
Keywords: spaghetti western

Overview: After being captured during a bank robbery, a cowboy is sent to a prison located in a swamp, where he contracts malaria. He soon escapes and, with the help of a Mexican, sets out to track down his partner, who escaped from the bank robbery with all the money.
