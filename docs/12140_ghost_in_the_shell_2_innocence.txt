Title: Ghost in the Shell 2: Innocence (2004)
Genres: Animation, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Mamoru Oshii
Main Cast: Atsuko Tanaka, Akio Ohtsuka, Kouichi Yamadera, Tamio Ohki, Yutaka Nakano
Keywords: cyborg, android, japanese, future, elite unit, futuristic, sexual violence, cyberpunk, hong kong

Overview: Groundbreaking director Mamoru Oshii continues to push the boundaries of art and anime with this universally acclaimed cyber thriller following cyborg detective Batou as he tries to unravel the reasons for a murderous robot revolt in the year 2032. A quest for a killer that becomes a mind bending search for the meaning of life.
