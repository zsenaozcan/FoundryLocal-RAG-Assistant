Title: The Sea Vultures (1915)
Genres: Drama
Director: Victor Sjöström
Main Cast: Richard Lund, Rasmus Rasmussen, Greta Almroth, John Ekman, Nils Elffors
Keywords: silent film

Overview: Two smugglers meet two customs officers at sea, and happen to kill them. The story continues fifteen years later when a son of one of the officers and a smuggler's daughter fall in love with each other.
