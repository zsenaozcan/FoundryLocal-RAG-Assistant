Title: Holidaze: The Christmas That Almost Didn't Happen (2006)
Genres: Family, Animation
Director: David H. Brooks
Main Cast: Jonathan Prince, Fred Savage, Fred Willard, Edie McClurg, John O'Hurley

Overview: Rusty Reindeer (Savage) stumbles into a support group where the Easter Bunny, Cupid, and other down-and-out holiday icons congregate.
