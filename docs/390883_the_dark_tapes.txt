Title: The Dark Tapes (2017)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Vincent J. Guastini
Main Cast: Cortney Palm, David Hull, Emilia Zoryan, Brittany Underwood, David Banks
Keywords: kidnapping, webcam, anthology, paranormal, serial killer, creature, demon, ghost hunting, ghoul, ghost

Overview: A genre-defying mixture of horror, sci-fi, myth, mystery and thrills told as four interlocking tales in one intelligent anthology. Ghosts, spirits, creatures, demons and more from the paranormal world collide with rational curiosity.
