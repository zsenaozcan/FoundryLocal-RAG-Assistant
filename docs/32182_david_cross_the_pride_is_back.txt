Title: David Cross: The Pride Is Back (1999)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Troy Miller
Main Cast: David Cross, Molly Brenner, Amiira Ruotola
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Taped in July before a live audience at the Showbox Theatre in Seattle, Cross pushes his brash humor to new extremes, offering uncensored remarks on the Virgin Mary, trendy advertising, violence in the media, airports and pornography, Dr. Kevorkian, organ donations, High Times magazine and religious fundamentalists.
