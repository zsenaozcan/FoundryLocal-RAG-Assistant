Title: Jedda (1955)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: Charles Chauvel
Main Cast: Ngarla Kunoth, Robert Tudawali, Betty Suttor, Paul Reynall, George Simpson-Lyttle
Keywords: australia, aborigine

Overview: An aboriginal girl is brought up by a white family that adopts her. As a young woman, she is mysteriously drawn to go "Walkabout" as people of her tribe have for hundreds of years.
