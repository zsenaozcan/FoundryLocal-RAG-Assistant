Title: Hercules Against the Barbarians (1964)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Domenico Paolella
Main Cast: Mark Forest, José Greci, Ken CLARKE, Glorai MILLAND

Overview: The eternal Hercules in this film has gone way beyond his usual time and place in ancient Greece and is now helping Poland free itself of Mongol invaders in the 12th century. Mark Forest is the star of this peplum epic, Hercules Against The Barbarians.
