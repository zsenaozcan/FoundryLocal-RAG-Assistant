Title: The Thirteenth Guest (1932)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Albert Ray
Main Cast: Ginger Rogers, Lyle Talbot, J. Farrell MacDonald, Paul Hurst
Keywords: guest, police, dinner

Overview: Thirteen years after a dinner party in which the thirteenth guest failed to arrive, the remaining guests are being murdered one by one, and their bodies being placed at the same dinner table in the appropriate seats they occupied thirteen years prior.
