Title: Kill List (2011)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, Crime
Director: Ben Wheatley
Main Cast: Neil Maskell, MyAnna Buring, Harry Simpson, Michael Smiley, Struan Rodger
Keywords: hotel, wife husband relationship, hitman, infection, forest, cult, priest, murderer, brutality, bonfire

Overview: Nearly a year after a botched job, a hitman takes a new assignment with the promise of a big payoff for three killings. What starts off as an easy task soon unravels, sending the killer into the heart of darkness.
