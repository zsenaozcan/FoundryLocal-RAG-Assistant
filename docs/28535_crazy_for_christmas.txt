Title: Crazy for Christmas (2005)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Eleanor Lindo
Main Cast: Howard Hesseman, Jason Spevack, Andrea Roth
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A girl loses her parents and husband and is left driving a limo trying to get by raising her young son. An interesting but strange rich man hires her limo on Christmas eve and has her drive him to various ice rinks where he gives out $100 bills to the people there. The news media catches on and starts following him. Some interesting twists follow, but the movie is somewhat predictable.
