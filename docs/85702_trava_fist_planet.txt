Title: Trava: Fist Planet (2003)
Genres: Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Takeshi Koike
Main Cast: Sie Kohinata, Yoshiyuki Morishita, Kanji Tsuda

Overview: Ace pilot Trava and his personal mechanic buddy Shinkai, on their way to mark an out-of-the-way planet, pick up Mikuru, a girl with no memory. The three are about to discover that the planet is more than it seems.  A four-part OVA that was part of the "Grasshoppa!" video anthology/DVD magazine.
