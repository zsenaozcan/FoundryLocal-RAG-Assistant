Title: The Marriage Chronicles (2012)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Paul D. Hannah
Main Cast: Jazsmin Lewis, Darrin Henson, Terri J. Vaughn, Persia White

Overview: Three couples trying to spice up their marriages go on a retreat run by a sexy psychologist and her handsome husband at a tranquil Malibu estate. Encouraged to share their most innermost feelings, the fun begins when secrets are revealed and hearts are challenged. How far will each couple go to save their marriage? (Redbox.com)
