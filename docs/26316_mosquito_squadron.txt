Title: Mosquito Squadron (1969)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Boris Sagal
Main Cast: David McCallum, Suzanne Neve, Charles Gray, David Buck, David Dundas
Keywords: world war ii, mosquito, squadron

Overview: In WW2, a RAF squadron leader mourns the death of a comrade and receives a bombing mission against a secret Nazi V-2 rocket testing facility in France.
