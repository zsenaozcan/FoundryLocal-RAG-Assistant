Title: Proof of Life (2000)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Taylor Hackford
Main Cast: Meg Ryan, Russell Crowe, David Morse, Pamela Reed, David Caruso
Keywords: hostage, new love, suspense, agent

Overview: Alice hires a professional negotiator to obtain the release of her engineer husband, who has been kidnapped by anti-government guerrillas in South America.
