Title: Husk (2011)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Brett Simmons
Main Cast: Devon Graye, Wes Chatham, C.J. Thomason, Tammin Sursok, Ben Easter
Keywords: terror, scarecrow, hallucination, flashlight, ritual, supernatural, friends, murder, curse, blood

Overview: A group of friends stranded near a desolate cornfield find shelter in an old farmhouse, though they soon discover the dwelling is the center of a supernatural ritual.
