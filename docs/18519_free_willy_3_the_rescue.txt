Title: Free Willy 3: The Rescue (1997)
Genres: Adventure
Director: Sam Pillsbury
Main Cast: Jason James Richter, August Schellenberg, Annie Corley, Vincent Berry, Patrick Kilpatrick
Keywords: ship, whale, friends, killer whale, whaler

Overview: Willy the whale is back, this time threatened by illegal whalers making money off sushi. Jesse, now 16, has taken a job on an orca-researching ship, along with old friend Randolph and a sarcastic scientist, Drew.
