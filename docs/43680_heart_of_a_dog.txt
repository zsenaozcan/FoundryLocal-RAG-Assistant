Title: Heart of a Dog (1988)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Vladimir Bortko
Main Cast: Evgeniy Evstigneev, Boris Plotnikov, Vladimir Tolokonnikov, Roman Kartsev, Nina Ruslanova

Overview: Old Prof. Preobrazhensky and his young colleague Dr. Bormental inserted the human's hypophysis into a dog's brain. Couple of weeks later the dog became "human looking". The main question is "Is anybody who is looking like a man, A REAL MAN?"
