Title: Where Are My Children? (1916)
Genres: Drama
Director: Phillips Smalley
Main Cast: Tyrone Power Sr., Helen Riaume, Marie Walcamp, Cora Drew, Rena Rogers
Keywords: regret, seduction, childlessness, womanizer, tragedy, doctor, district attorney, silent film, court trial, devastation

Overview: While prosecuting a physician for the death of a client after an abortion, the district attorney discovers that his wife helped her society friends and the daughter of her maid obtain and pay for abortions from the physician (and was perhaps herself also a client).
