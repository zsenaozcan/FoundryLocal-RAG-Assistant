Title: Darling (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Johan Kling
Main Cast: Michelle Meadows, Michael Segerström, Richard Ulfsäter, Lis Nilheim, Erik Johansson 
Keywords: alienation, mcdonald's restaurant, opposites attract, clothing store

Overview: Eva is beautiful, irresponsible, self-absorbed and emotionally disconnected. Her life is all about the right clothes, the right people and the right places. Her life is turned upside down when she loses her job and alienates her friends by cheating on her boyfriend. She soon finds herself among "common" people where she makes a surprising new acquaintance.
