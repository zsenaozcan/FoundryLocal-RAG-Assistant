Title: The Crow's Nest (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Arturo Menéndez
Main Cast: Salvador Solís, Karla Valencia, Leandro Sanchez Arauz, Marcela Santamaria, Mercy Flores
Keywords: el salvador

Overview: The life of a simple piñata salesman named Don Cleo is turned upside down when he falls victim to an extortionist he can’t possibly afford to pay. The harder he tries to raise the funds, the deeper into trouble he  gets. If Don Cleo hopes to survive, he’ll have to face his fears and stand up to his tormentors.
