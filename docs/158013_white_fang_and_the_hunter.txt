Title: White Fang and the Hunter (1975)
Genres: Western, Adventure, Drama, Family
Director: Alfonso Brescia
Main Cast: Robert Woods, Ignazio Spalla, Malisa Longo, Claudio Undari

Overview: Courageous Alaskan canine White Fang and Daniel, his fur-trapping master are beset by wolves and later help save a widow who is being forced to marry a man she despises.
