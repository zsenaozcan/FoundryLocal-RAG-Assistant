Title: A Better Tomorrow III: Love and Death in Saigon (1989)
Genres: War, Action, Thriller
Director: Tsui Hark
Main Cast: Chow Yun-fat, Anita Mui, Tony Leung Ka-Fai, Saburô Tokitô, Sek Kin

Overview: The year is 1974. A young Hong Kong Chinese, Mark (Chow Yun-Fat), travels to Saigon to make his fortune. There, he encounters a mysterious femme fatale, the lovely Kit. As he becomes more involved in her various underworld deals, a tragic romance develops. On the eve of the outbreak of war, Kit's past returns to endanger her, and Mark must risk everything for the one true love of his life...
