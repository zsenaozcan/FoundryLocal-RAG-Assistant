Title: The Crawling Hand (1963)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Herbert L. Strock
Main Cast: Peter Breck, Kent Taylor, Rod Lauren, Alan Hale Jr., Allison Hayes
Keywords: astronaut, violence

Overview: After an astronaut space capsule is detonated in orbit, a teenager finds a severed arm among wreckage on earth. Soon the thing returns to life to murder and posses the young man's mind.
