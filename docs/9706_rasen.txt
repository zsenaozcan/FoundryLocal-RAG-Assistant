Title: Rasen (1998)
Genres: Horror
Director: George Iida
Main Cast: Koichi Sato, Miki Nakatani, Hinako Saeki, Shingo Tsurumi, Shigemitsu Ogi
Keywords: code, pathology, sequel, serial killer, car accident, videotape, rebirth, murder hunt

Overview: A young pathologist seeks answers to the mysterious death of a friend and soon comes into contact with the same cursed videotape that caused the death of the friend's wife and son, which is haunted by the curse of Sadako, a relentless spirit.
