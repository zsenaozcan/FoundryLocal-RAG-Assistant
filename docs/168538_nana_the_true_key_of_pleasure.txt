Title: Nana, the True Key of Pleasure (1983)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Dan Wolman
Main Cast: Katya Berger, Jean-Pierre Aumont, Yehuda Efroni, Yehuda Efroni, Massimo Serato

Overview: In Zola's Paris, an ingenue arrives at a tony bordello: she's Nana, guileless, but quickly learning to use her erotic innocence to get what she wants. She's an actress for a soft-core filmmaker and soon is the most popular courtesan in Paris, parlaying this into a house, bought for her by a wealthy banker. She tosses him and takes up with her neighbor, a count of impeccable rectitude, and with the count's impressionable son. The count is soon fetching sticks like a dog and mortgaging his lands to satisfy her whims.
