Title: White Hell of Pitz Palu (1929)
Genres: Drama
Director: G.W. Pabst
Main Cast: Gustav Diessl, Leni Riefenstahl, Ernst Petersen, Ernst Udet, Mizzi Götzel
Keywords: mountain, stranded, rescue, snow, mountain climbing

Overview: A man climbs a 12,000-foot mountain to search for his wife, who was lost on their honeymoon. Another couple makes the dangerous climb with him.
