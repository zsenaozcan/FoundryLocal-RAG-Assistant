Title: Moonfleet (1955)
Genres: Adventure
Director: Fritz Lang
Main Cast: Stewart Granger, George Sanders, Joan Greenwood, Viveca Lindfors, Jon Whiteley
Keywords: treasure, pirate, rum

Overview: set in the eighteenth century, the film is about John Mohune, a young orphan, played by Jon Whiteley, who is sent to the Dorset village of Moonfleet to stay with an old friend of his mother's, Jeremy Fox. Fox, played by Stewart Granger, is a morally ambiguous character, an elegant gentleman involved with smugglers and pirates.
