Title: Survival Family (2017)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Shinobu Yaguchi
Main Cast: Fumiyo Kohinata, Eri Fukatsu, Yuki Izumisawa

Overview: A world wide electrical outage occurs. Everything that requires electricity comes to a stop. Tokyo is nearly ruined. Yoshiyuki Suzuki (Fumiyo Kohinata) decides to escape from Tokyo with his family.
