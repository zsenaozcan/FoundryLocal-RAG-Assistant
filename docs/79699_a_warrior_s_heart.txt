Title: A Warrior's Heart (2011)
Genres: Action, Drama, Family
Director: Michael F. Sears
Main Cast: Ashley Greene, Kellan Lutz, Gabrielle Anwar, Chord Overstreet, William Mapother

Overview: After his father’s death, high school lacrosse star Conor Sullivan becomes self-destructive but through a new love interest and his passion for this sport, Conor discovers the warrior within himself.
