Title: Password: Uccidete agente Gordon (1966)
Genres: Adventure, Action
Director: Sergio Grieco
Main Cast: Roger Browne, Helga Liné, Miguel de la Riva, Franco Ressel, Rosalba Neri
Keywords: espionaje

Overview: A tough CIA agent is called in to put a stop to a shipment of weapons to the Viet Cong from an arms smuggling ring in this intercontinental spy thriller.
