Title: Don't Open Till Christmas (1984)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Mystery, Comedy
Director: Edmund Purdom
Main Cast: Edmund Purdom, Belinda Mayne, Mark Jones, Gerry Sundquist, Alan Lake
Keywords: scotland yard, santa claus, investigation, murder, dark comedy, gore, dark humor, british horror, christmas horror, christmas

Overview: Somebody with very little Christmas spirit is killing anyone in a Santa suit one London holiday season, and Scotland Yard has to stop him before he makes his exploits an annual tradition.
