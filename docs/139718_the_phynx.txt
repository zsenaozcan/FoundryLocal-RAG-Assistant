Title: The Phynx (1970)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lee H. Katzin
Main Cast: Joan Blondell, Martha Raye, George Tobias, Maureen O'Sullivan, Ed Sullivan
Keywords: spy, rock band, celebrity cameo

Overview: A rock band becomes embroiled in foreign affairs when they're sent to go on tour in Albania as a cover to find hostages in a remote castle held by communist enemies of the USA.
