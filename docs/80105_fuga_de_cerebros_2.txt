Title: Fuga de cerebros 2 (2011)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Carlos Therón
Main Cast: Adrián Lastra, Patricia Montero, Gorka Lasaosa, Alberto Amarilla, Canco Rodríguez
Keywords: harvard university, university

Overview: Alfonso, pursues the girl of his life to Harvard, determined to conquer her under the same friends and outlandish plans that his brother used in Oxford. But when you least expect it appears Martha, the girl he fell in love while at school. Sequel to "Fuga de cerebros".
