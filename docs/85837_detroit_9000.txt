Title: Detroit 9000 (1973)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime
Director: Arthur Marks
Main Cast: Alex Rocco, Vonetta McGee, Hari Rhodes, Ella Edwards, Scatman Crothers
Keywords: hitman, police, party, murder, money, blaxploitation, prostitution, violence, flashback

Overview: After a fundraiser for a black politician is robbed, Detroit police put two detectives, one white and one black, on the case, who try to work together under boiling political pressure.
