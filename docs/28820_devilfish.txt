Title: Devilfish (1984)
Genres: Action, Horror, Thriller
Director: Lamberto Bava
Main Cast: Michael Sopkiw, Valentine Monnier, Gianni Garko, William Berger, Iris Peynado
Keywords: octopus, shark attack, water monster, grindhouse

Overview: A marine biologist, a dolphin trainer, a research scientist, and a local sheriff try to hunt down a large sea monster, a shark/octopus hybrid, that is devouring swimmers and fishermen off a south Florida coast.
