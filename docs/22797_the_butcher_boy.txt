Title: The Butcher Boy (1997)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Neil Jordan
Main Cast: Eamonn Owens, Alan Boyle, Fiona Shaw, Andrew Fullerton, Stephen Rea
Keywords: suicide, angel, difficult childhood, insane asylum, pig, friendship, boarding school, alcoholism, murder, dark comedy

Overview: Francie and Joe live the usual playful, fantasy filled childhoods of normal boys. However, with a violent, alcoholic father and a manic depressive, suicidal mother the pressure on Francie to grow up are immense. When Francie's world turns to madness, he tries to counter it with further insanity, with dire consequences.
