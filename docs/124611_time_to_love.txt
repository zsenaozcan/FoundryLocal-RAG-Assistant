Title: Time to Love (1965)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Metin Erksan
Main Cast: Müsfik Kenter, Sema Özcan, Süleyman Tekcan, Oya Bulaner, Ayban Erkmen
Keywords: painter, mansion

Overview: A poor painter falls in love with a photograph of a woman while at work in one of the massive villas on Istanbul's Princes' Islands.
