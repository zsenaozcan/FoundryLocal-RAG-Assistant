Title: Neighbor (2009)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Robert A. Masciantonio
Main Cast: America Olivo, Christian Campbell, Mink Stole, Lauren Rooney, Pete Postiglione
Keywords: amputation, torture, electrocution, extreme violence, needle

Overview: A mysterious new girl arrives in posh suburban neighborhood and quickly sets out to terrorize the town. As she starts breaking into homes and torturing the occupants, they begin to realize that she isn't just another girl next door.
