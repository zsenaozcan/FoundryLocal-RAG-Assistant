Title: Och, Karol (1985)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Roman Zaluski
Main Cast: Dorota Kamińska, Marta Klubowicz, Danuta Kowalska, Jan Piechociński

Overview: Karol has everything. He has three mistresses in addition to the bride. He is charming, loves sex and enslaves women in the blink of an eye. He's also well aware of that women like and uses it for his benefits.
