Title: The Possibilities Are Endless (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: James Hall
Main Cast: Edwyn Collins, Grace Maxwell, Yasmin Paige, William Collins, Findlay Adams

Overview: Scottish musician, Edwyn Collins' world was shattered by a devastating stroke. After fighting back from the brink of death, he discovers that life, love and language mean even more to him that he could ever have imagined.
