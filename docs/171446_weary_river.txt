Title: Weary River (1929)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Frank Lloyd
Main Cast: Richard Barthelmess, Betty Compson, William Holden, George E. Stone, Louis Natheaux
Keywords: prison, shootout, warden

Overview: A gangster is put in prison, but finds salvation through music while serving his time. Again on the outside, he finds success elusive and temptations abound.
