Title: Two: The Story of Roman & Nyro (2013)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Heather M. Winters
Main Cast: Desmond Child, Curtis Shaw Child, Roman Shaw Child, Nyro Shaw Child, Jon Bon Jovi
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Combining over twelve years of footage and narrated by their twin sons, TWO: The Story of Roman &amp; Nyro, follows legendary songwriter Desmond Child and his lifelong partner's loving journey to create their new modern family.
