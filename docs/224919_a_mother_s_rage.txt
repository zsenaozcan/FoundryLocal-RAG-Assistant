Title: A Mother's Rage (2013)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Oren Kaplan
Main Cast: Lori Loughlin, Kristen Dalton, Ted McGinley, Jordan Hinson, Alix Elizabeth Gitter

Overview: A mother and her daughter, Conner, embark on a journey to Conner's new school - both unaware of how many "bumps" they may find along the way.
