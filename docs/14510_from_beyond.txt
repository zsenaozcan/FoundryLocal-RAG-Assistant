Title: From Beyond (1986)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Fantasy
Director: Stuart Gordon
Main Cast: Jeffrey Combs, Barbara Crampton, Ken Foree, Ted Sorel, Carolyn Purdy-Gordon
Keywords: human experimentation, h.p. lovecraft, parallel world

Overview: A group of scientists have developed the Resonator, a machine which allows whoever is within range to see beyond normal perceptible reality. But when the experiment succeeds, they are immediately attacked by terrible life forms.
