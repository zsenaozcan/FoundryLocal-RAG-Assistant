Title: If I Should Die Before I Wake (1952)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Carlos Hugo Christensen
Main Cast: Néstor Zavarce, Blanca del Prado, Homero Cárpena, Floren Delbene

Overview: During a prank an 11 year old boy gets information about the identity of a kidnapper of girls in the vicinity but does not reveal it to his father, a policeman, not to confess his own fault and it jeopardizes his life.
