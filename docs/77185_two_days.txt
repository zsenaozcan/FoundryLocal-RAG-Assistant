Title: Two Days (2011)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Avdotya Smirnova
Main Cast: Fyodor Bondarchuk, Kseniya Rappoport, Boris Kamorzin, Boris Khlebnikov, Anna Mikhalkova

Overview: A two days from the life of Pyotr Drozdov, a highly ranked official from Moscow, which he spends with young museum employee Masha.
