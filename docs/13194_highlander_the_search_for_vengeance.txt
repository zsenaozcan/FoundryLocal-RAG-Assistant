Title: Highlander: The Search for Vengeance (2007)
Genres: Action, Animation, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Yoshiaki Kawajiri
Main Cast: Alistair Abell, Hank Banks, Kathleen Barr, Zachary Samuels, Janyse Jaud
Keywords: new love, future, immortality, revenge, highlander

Overview: Colin MacLeod hunts for the man responsible for the death of his love. He chases the immortal Marcus Octavius across centuries as his quest threatens to take over his life and heart. In his quest, he meets a woman who reminds him of his slain beauty. But it's his final battle with Marcus that will decide not only his fate, but also the fate of everyone in the world.
