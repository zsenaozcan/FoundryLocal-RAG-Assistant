Title: None But the Lonely Heart (1944)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Clifford Odets
Main Cast: Cary Grant, Ethel Barrymore, Barry Fitzgerald, June Duprez, Jane Wyatt
Keywords: slum, unrequited love, cancer

Overview: When an itinerant reluctantly returns home to help his sickly mother run her shop, they're both tempted to turn to crime to help make ends meet.
