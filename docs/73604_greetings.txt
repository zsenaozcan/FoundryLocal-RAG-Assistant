Title: Greetings (1968)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Brian De Palma
Main Cast: Jonathan Warden, Robert De Niro, Gerrit Graham

Overview: An offbeat, episodic film about three friends, Paul, a shy love-seeker, Lloyd, a vibrant conspiracy nut, and Jon, an aspiring filmmaker and peeping tom. The film satirizes free-love, the Kennedy assassination, Vietnam, and amateur film-making.
