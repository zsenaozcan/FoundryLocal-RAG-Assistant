Title: I due della legione (1962)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Franco Franchi, Ciccio Ingrassia, Alighiero Noschese, Rosalba Neri, Aldo Giuffrè

Overview: Ciccio and Franco flee Naples because they are wrongly accused of having murdered a local camorra boss and enlist in the Foreign Legion. They are inept but are mistaken for brave people by their commander and sent to break a weapon smuggling ring.
