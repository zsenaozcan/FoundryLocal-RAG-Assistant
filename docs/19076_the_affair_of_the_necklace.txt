Title: The Affair of the Necklace (2001)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Charles Shyer
Main Cast: Hilary Swank, Simon Baker, Christopher Walken, Hayden Panettiere, Jonathan Pryce
Keywords: marie antoinette, revenge

Overview: In pre-Revolutionary France, a young aristocratic woman left penniless by the political unrest in the country, must avenge her family's fall from grace by scheming to steal a priceless necklace.
