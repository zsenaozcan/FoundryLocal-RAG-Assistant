Title: The High Frontier (2016)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Wojciech Kasperski
Main Cast: Marcin Dorociński, Andrzej Chyra, Andrzej Grabowski, Bartosz Bielenia, Kuba Henriksen

Overview: A father and his two teenage sons travel to a small mountain cabin for a male bonding adventure. When a lost tourist arrives at the cabin, their male-bonding outing turns into a struggle for survival.
