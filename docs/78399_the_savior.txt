Title: The Savior (1971)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michel Mardore
Main Cast: Horst Buchholz, Muriel Catalá, Hélène Vallier, Roger Lumont, Henri Vilbert

Overview: This is rural France. It's the summer of 1943, the weather is fine and sunny and life is sweet. On one of these beautiful days, Nanette, a fourteen-year-old peasant girl, meets a slightly injured young man near the farm she lives on. Her life is about to change forever.
