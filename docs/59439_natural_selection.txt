Title: Natural Selection (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Robbie Pickering
Main Cast: Rachael Harris, Jon Gries, Matt O'Leary, John Diehl

Overview: When a dutiful, albeit barren Christian housewife discovers that her devout husband has suffered a stroke at a sperm bank where he's been secretly donating his seed for the past 25 years, she leaves her sheltered world and starts off on a journey to find his eldest biological son - a mullet-headed, foul-mouthed ex-con with whom she develops an odd but meaningful relationship.
