Title: Barbie in the Pink Shoes (2013)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Owen Hurley
Main Cast: Trevor Devall, Brett Dier, Katie Crown, Ali Liebert, Bill Mondy
Keywords: love triangle, talent, prince, costume, barbie, princess, talent-scout, based on toy, ballet, best friend

Overview: Dance your way to a magical adventure with Barbie as Kristyn, a ballerina with big dreams! When she tries on a pair of sparkling pink shoes, she and her best friend, Hailey, are whisked away to a fantastical ballet world. There, Kristyn discovers she must dance in her favorite ballets in order to defeat an evil Snow Queen. With performances to the legendary Giselle and Swan Lake ballets, it's a wonderful journey where if you dance with your heart, dreams come true!
