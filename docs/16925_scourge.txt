Title: Scourge (2008)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery, Science Fiction
Director: Jonas Quastel
Main Cast: Russell Ferrier, Robyn Ledoux, Nic Rhind, Jason Harder, Marina Pasqua
Keywords: parasite, public hazard, person to person

Overview: An ancient pestilence called The Scourge has been set free in a small town after being entombed in a church's masonry for a century and a half. As bodies rapidly pile up, nothing stands between the spawning Scourge and the rest of the town except our young heroes Scott and Jessie who rediscover their love for each other in the process
