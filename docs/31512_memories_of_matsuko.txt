Title: Memories of Matsuko (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Tetsuya Nakashima
Main Cast: Miki Nakatani, Eita, Yusuke Iseya, Teruyuki Kagawa, Mikako Ichikawa
Keywords: musical

Overview: While combing through the belongings of his recently deceased aunt, Matsuko, nephew Sho pieces together the crucial events that sank Matsuko's life into a despairing tragedy.
