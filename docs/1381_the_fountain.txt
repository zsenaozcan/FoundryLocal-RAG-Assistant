Title: The Fountain (2006)
Genres: Drama, Adventure, Science Fiction, Romance
Director: Darren Aronofsky
Main Cast: Hugh Jackman, Rachel Weisz, Ellen Burstyn, Mark Margolis, Stephen McHattie
Keywords: brain tumor, operation, queen, love of one's life, surgeon, tree, dying and death, transience, immortality, maya civilization
