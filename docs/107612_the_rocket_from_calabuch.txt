Title: The Rocket from Calabuch (1956)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Luis García Berlanga
Main Cast: Edmund Gwenn, Valentina Cortese, Juan Calvo, Franco Fabrizi

Overview: Professor Hamilton, a naive Nuclear scientist who once thought bombs were good for humanity, realizes his mistake and runs away from his inventions. He hides in Calabuch, a beautiful seaside town where people still believe in humor and friendship.
