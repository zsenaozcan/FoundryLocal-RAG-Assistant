Title: Johan Falk: Kodnamn Lisa (2013)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Charlotte Brandström
Main Cast: Jakob Eklund, Joel Kinnaman, Ruth Vega Fernandez, Jens Hultén, Mikael Tornving
Keywords: undercover agent, police, organized crime, mafia, crime, mole, woman director

Overview: Five men break into Frank Wagner's apartment. Frank is able to escape and seeks out the help of Johan Falk. Has someone leaked that Frank is working with the police? Frank and John do not know who knows what and who they can trust.
