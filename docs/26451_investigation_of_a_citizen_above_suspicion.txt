Title: Investigation of a Citizen Above Suspicion (1970)
Genres: Drama
Director: Elio Petri
Main Cast: Gian Maria Volonté, Florinda Bolkan, Gianni Santuccio, Salvo Randone, Orazio Orlando
Keywords: politics, police, giallo, political, crime investigation

Overview: A chief of detectives, homicide section, commits a murder and deliberately leaves clues to prove his own responsibility for the crime.
