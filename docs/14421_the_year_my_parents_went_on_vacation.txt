Title: The Year My Parents Went On Vacation (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Cao Hamburger
Main Cast: Germano Haiut, Michel Joelsas, Paulo Autran, Simone Spoladore, Eduardo Moreira
Keywords: brazilian, drama
