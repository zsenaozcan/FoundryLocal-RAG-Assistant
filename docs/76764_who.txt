Title: Who? (1974)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Jack Gold
Main Cast: Elliott Gould, Trevor Howard, Joseph Bova, Edward Grover, John Lehne
Keywords: cyborg

Overview: After American scientist gets severely injured and scarred in a car crash in USSR, Russians use metal implants to save him. Once back in the States no one can tell if it's really him so a specialist must determine who is under the "mask".
