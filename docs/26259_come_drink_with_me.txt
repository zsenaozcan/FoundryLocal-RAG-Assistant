Title: Come Drink with Me (1966)
Genres: Adventure, Action
Director: King Hu
Main Cast: Cheng Pei-Pei, Jackie Chan, Yuen Siu-Tin, Mars, Elliot Ngok
Keywords: kung fu, showdown, hero, kidnapping, warrior woman, fistfight, waterfall, forest, murder, gore

Overview: Cheng Pei-pei plays Golden Swallow, a fighter-for-hire who has been contracted by the local government to retrieve the governor's kidnapped son. Holding him is a group of rebels who are demanding that their leader be released from prison in return for the captured son. After a brief encounter with the gang at a local restaurant, Golden Swallow is joined by an inebriated wanderer Drunken Cat (Yueh Hua) who aids her in her mission.
