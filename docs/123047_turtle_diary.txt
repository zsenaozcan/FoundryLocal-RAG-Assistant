Title: Turtle Diary (1985)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: John Irvin
Main Cast: Glenda Jackson, Ben Kingsley, Michael Gambon, Nigel Hawthorne, Richard Johnson

Overview: Two separate people, a man and a woman, find something very stirring about the sea turtles in their tank at the London Zoo. They meet and form an odd, but sympathetic camaraderie as they plan to steal two of the turtles and free them into the ocean.
