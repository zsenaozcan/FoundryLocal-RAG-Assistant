Title: Look Who's Talking Too (1990)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Amy Heckerling
Main Cast: John Travolta, Kirstie Alley, Bruce Willis, Roseanne Barr, Gilbert Gottfried
Keywords: baby, brother sister relationship, pregnancy, family, woman director, talking baby

Overview: Mollie and James are together and raising a family, which now consists of an older Mikey and his baby sister, Julie. Tension between the siblings arises, and as well with Mollie and James when Mollie's brother Stuart moves in. Mikey is also learning how to use the toilet for the first time.
