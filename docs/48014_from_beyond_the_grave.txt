Title: From Beyond the Grave (1974)
Genres: Horror
Director: Kevin Connor
Main Cast: Peter Cushing, David Warner, Donald Pleasence, Ian Ogilvy, Ian Carmichael
Keywords: anthology, antiques shop

Overview: Four customers purchase (or take) items from Temptations Limited, an antiques shop whose motto is "Offers You Cannot Resist". A nasty fate awaits all of them—particularly those who cheat the shop's Proprietor.
