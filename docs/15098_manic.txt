Title: Manic (2001)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jordan Melamed
Main Cast: Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Don Cheadle, Elden Henson, Cody Lightning, Sara Rivas
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Lyle is filled with rage. After badly beating a classmate during a schoolyard fight, Lyle is committed to a juvenile mental institution in lieu of criminal prosecution. Inside the hospital, Lyle encounters a group of adolescents struggling with a variety of illnesses.
