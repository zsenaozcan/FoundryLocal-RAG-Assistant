Title: 16 Years of Alcohol (2003)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Richard Jobson
Main Cast: Kevin McKidd, Laura Fraser, Susan Lynch, Iain De Caestecker, Ewen Bremner
Keywords: skinhead, scotland, punk, independent film, alcoholic, swearing, brawl

Overview: 16 Years of Alcohol is a 2003 drama film written and directed by Richard Jobson, based on his 1987 novel. The film is Jobson's first directorial effort, following a career as a television presenter on BSkyB and VH-1, and as the vocalist for the 1970s punk rock band The Skids.
