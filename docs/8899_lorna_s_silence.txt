Title: Lorna's Silence (2008)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Luc Dardenne
Main Cast: Arta Dobroshi, Jérémie Renier, Fabrizio Rongione, Alban Ukaj, Morgan Marinne
Keywords: belgium, taxi driver, fictitious marriage, junkie, gangster, conscience

Overview: In order to become the owner of a snack bar with her boyfriend, Lorna, a young Albanian woman living in Belgium, becomes an accomplice to a diabolical plan devised by mobster Fabio.
