Title: Girl in the Sunny Place (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Fantasy
Director: Takahiro Miki
Main Cast: Juri Ueno, Jun Matsumoto, Tetsuji Tamayama, Masaki Suda, Koji Okura
Keywords: high school reunion

Overview: A new salesman named Kosuke Okuda happens to meet Mao Watarai, a friend from his middle school days. Back then, Mao was called stupid and bullied, but now she is an attractive woman. Kosuke and Mao fall in love and decide to marry, but Mao has a secret…
