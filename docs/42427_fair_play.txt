Title: Fair Play (2006)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Lionel Bailliu
Main Cast: Benoît Magimel, Jérémie Renier, Mélanie Doutey, Marion Cotillard, Jean-Pierre Cassel
Keywords: tennis, manipulation, career

Overview: A company, where manipulation and harassment is a problem between colleagues, decides to organize a canyoning trip for its employees. The building tensions between co-workers might actually explode during this trip.
