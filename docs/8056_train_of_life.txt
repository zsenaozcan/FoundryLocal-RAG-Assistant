Title: Train of Life (1998)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, War
Director: Radu Mihaileanu
Main Cast: Lionel Abelanski, Rufus, Clément Harari, Agathe de La Fontaine, Michel Muller
Keywords: concentration camp, holocaust, jewish life, deportation, jew, nazis, eastern europe, fool, village, nazi germany
