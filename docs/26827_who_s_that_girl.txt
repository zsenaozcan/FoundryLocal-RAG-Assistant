Title: Who's That Girl (1987)
Genres: Comedy
Director: James Foley
Main Cast: Madonna, Griffin Dunne, Bibi Besch, John McMartin, John Mills
Keywords: new york, falsely accused, cult comedy

Overview: An uptight New York tax lawyer gets his life turned upside down, all in a single day, when he's asked to escort a feisty and free-spirited female ex-convict whom asks him to help prove her innocence of her crime.
