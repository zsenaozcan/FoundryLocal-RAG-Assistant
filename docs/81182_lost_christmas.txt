Title: Lost Christmas (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: John Hay
Main Cast: Eddie Izzard, Jason Flemyng, Connie Hyde, Brett Fancy, Adlyn Ross
Keywords: christmas

Overview: Urban fairytale set in Manchester. A series of tragic events that blight a young boy's life are reversed one Christmas Eve, giving him and those around him the happy ending that they were destined to have.
