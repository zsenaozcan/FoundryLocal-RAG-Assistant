Title: The Brave Little Toaster Goes to Mars (1998)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Robert C. Ramirez
Main Cast: Deanna Oliver, Thurl Ravenscroft, Roger Kabler, Carol Channing, Stephen Tobolowsky
Keywords: musical

Overview: Based on the book by Thomas M. Disch and intended as the third film in the series, this sequel was finished and released prior to 'The Brave Little Toaster To The Rescue'. Whilst trying to protect their new "Little Master" the anthropomorphic appliances set off on an epic adventure and make many new friends along the way.
