Title: The Barretts of Wimpole Street (1934)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Sidney Franklin
Main Cast: Norma Shearer, Fredric March, Charles Laughton, Maureen O'Sullivan, Katharine Alexander
Keywords: biography, poet, family relationships, church

Overview: Elizabeth Barrett's tyrannical father has forbidden any of his family to marry. Nevertheless, Elizabeth falls in love with the poet Robert Browning.
