Title: Chandu the Magician (1932)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy, Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: William Cameron Menzies
Main Cast: Edmund Lowe, Irene Ware, Bela Lugosi, Herbert Mundin, Henry B. Walthall
Keywords: scientist, madman

Overview: When delusional madman Roxor kidnaps a scientist in hopes of using his death ray to achieve world dominance, he is opposed by Chandu, a powerful hypnotist and yogi.
