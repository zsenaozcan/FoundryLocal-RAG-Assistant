Title: Attack of the 50 Ft. Woman (1993)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy
Director: Christopher Guest
Main Cast: Daryl Hannah, Frances Fisher, William Windom, Cristi Conaway, Paul Benedict
Keywords: made for cable tv, alien, giant monster
