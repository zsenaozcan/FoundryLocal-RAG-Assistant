Title: Kuffs (1992)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Bruce A. Evans
Main Cast: Christian Slater, Milla Jovovich, Tony Goldwyn, Bruce Boxleitner, Troy Evans
Keywords: dance, police protection, organized crime, breaking the fourth wall, gunfight, talking to the audience, funny hero, ugly shirts

Overview: George Kuffs didn't finish high-school, just lost his job, and his college-age girlfriend is pregnant. To top it off, George's brother Brad is killed and George inherits Brad's "patrol special" privatized police district and all the problems that come with it.
