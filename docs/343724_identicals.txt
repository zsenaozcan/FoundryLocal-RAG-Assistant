Title: Identicals (2015)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Simon Pummell
Main Cast: Nick Blood, Nora-Jane Noone, Tony Way, Lachlan Nieboer, Michelle Asante
Keywords: relationship, parallel world, parallel lives

Overview: Identicals is a highly unconventional romantic thriller: an obsessive love story stripped down and re-arranged into the looping logic of a nightmare.
