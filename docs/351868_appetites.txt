Title: Appetites (2015)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Cameron Casey
Main Cast: Lauren Parkinson, Bret Roberts, Travis Eberhard, James Duval, Bonnie Rotten
Keywords: psychopath

Overview: Daisy is the all-American girl next door with a hunger for true love and an appetite for murder. Then one day Daisy meets the man of her dreams, only to discover that his own lust for killing might make her his next victim.
