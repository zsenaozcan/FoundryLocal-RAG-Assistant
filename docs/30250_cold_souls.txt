Title: Cold Souls (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Sophie Barthes
Main Cast: Paul Giamatti, Emily Watson, Dina Korzun, David Strathairn, Katheryn Winnick
Keywords: soul selling, woman director

Overview: Paul Giamatti stars as himself, agonizing over his interpretation of "Uncle Vanya." Paralyzed by anxiety, he stumbles upon a solution via a New Yorker article about a high-tech company promising to alleviate suffering by extracting souls. Giamatti enlists their service -- only to discover that his soul is the shape and size of a chickpea.
