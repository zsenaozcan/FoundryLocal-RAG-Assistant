Title: Don't Eat the Pictures: Sesame Street at the Metropolitan Museum of Art (1983)
Genres: Fantasy, Family
Director: Jon Stone
Main Cast: Caroll Spinney, Frank Oz, Jerry Nelson, Martin P. Robinson, Brian Muehl

Overview: As the Metropolitan Museum of Art closes, Big Bird decides to leave his Sesame Street friends behind in search of Snuffy. Once locked inside for the night, educational hilarity ensues as Big Bird and Snuffy team up to help a small Egyptian boy solve a riddle - as the rest of the cast searches for their big, yellow friend.
