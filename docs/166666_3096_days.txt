Title: 3096 Days (2013)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Sherry Hormann
Main Cast: Antonia Campbell-Hughes, Thure Lindhardt, Trine Dyrholm, Amelia Pidgeon, Dearbhla Molloy
Keywords: hostage, kidnapping, woman director

Overview: A young Austrian girl is kidnapped and held in captivity for eight years. Based on the real-life case of Natascha Kampusch.
