Title: The First Man (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Gianni Amelio
Main Cast: Jacques Gamblin, Maya Sansa, Denis Podalydès, Régis Romele, Catherine Sola

Overview: Based on a novel that Albert Camus was working on when he died, we follow Jacques Comery as he travels back to Algeria in 1957, a place full of childhood memories. The country is split between those wanting to remain a part of France, and those demanding independence. Reminiscences of his mother, his stern grandmother and a young Arab boy come flooding back.
