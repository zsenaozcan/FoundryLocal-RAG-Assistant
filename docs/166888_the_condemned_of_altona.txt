Title: The Condemned of Altona (1962)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Vittorio De Sica
Main Cast: Sophia Loren, Maximilian Schell, Fredric March, Robert Wagner, Françoise Prévost
Keywords: nazis, wealth

Overview: A dying German magnate invites his youngest son and daughter-in-law home to discuss the future of the family's shipbuilding empire. There, the daughter-in-law stumbles upon a secret of the family's Nazi past.
