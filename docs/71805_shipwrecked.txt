Title: Shipwrecked (1990)
Genres: Adventure, Family
Director: Nils Gaup
Main Cast: Stian Smestad, Gabriel Byrne, Louisa Milwood-Haigh, Bjørn Sundquist, Joachim Rafaelsen
Keywords: island, ship, pirate, sailor, cabin boy, 19th century
