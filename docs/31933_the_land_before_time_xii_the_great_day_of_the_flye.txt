Title: The Land Before Time XII: The Great Day of the Flyers (2006)
Genres: Animation, Family, Science Fiction
Director: Charles Grosvenor
Main Cast: John Ingle, Nick Price

Overview: As the Great Day of the Flyers nears, the Great Valley's flying youngsters are eager to participate in the annual exhibition to show off their skills. Everyone, that is, except free-spirited pterodactyl Petrie, whose individualism causes problems when it comes to staying in formation. Enter his dinosaur pals Littlefoot, Cera, Spike and Ducky, who encourage Petrie to embrace his uniqueness.
