Title: Tiger Eyes (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lawrence Blume
Main Cast: Willa Holland, Tatanka Means, Amy Jo Johnson, Elise Eberle, Cynthia Stevenson

Overview: After Davey's father is killed in a hold-up, she and her mother and younger brother visit relatives in New Mexico. Here Davey is befriended by a young man who helps her find the strength to carry on and conquer her fears.
