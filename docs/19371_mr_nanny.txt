Title: Mr. Nanny (1993)
Genres: Family, Comedy, Action
Director: Michael Gottlieb
Main Cast: Hulk Hogan, David Johansen, Madeline Zima, Robert Hy Gorman, Sherman Hemsley
Keywords: nanny, wrestler
