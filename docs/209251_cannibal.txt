Title: Cannibal (2013)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Manuel Martín Cuenca
Main Cast: Antonio de la Torre, María Alfonsa Rosso, Olimpia Melinte, Joaquín Núñez, Delphine Tempels

Overview: Carlos is the most prestigious tailor in Granada, but he's also a murderer in the shadows. He feels no remorse, no guilt, until Nina appears in his life and love awakens.
