Title: The Pee-Wee Herman Show on Broadway (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Marty Callner
Main Cast: Paul Reubens, Phil LaMarr, Jesse Garcia, Lexy Fridell, Josh Meyers

Overview: Subversive humor and childlike wonder based on both Reubens' original stage show, "The Pee-wee Herman Show," and the Emmy-winning Saturday morning TV show, "Pee-wee's Playhouse."
