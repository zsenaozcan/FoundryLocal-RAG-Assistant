Title: Third Person (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Paul Haggis
Main Cast: Liam Neeson, Mila Kunis, James Franco, Olivia Wilde, Adrien Brody
Keywords: female nudity, hotel room, trauma, death of a child, love, con artist, writer, maid, incest, unfaithful husband

Overview: An acclaimed novelist struggles to write an analysis of love in one of three stories, each set in a different city, that detail the beginning, middle and end of a relationship.
