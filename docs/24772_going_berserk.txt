Title: Going Berserk (1983)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: David Steinberg
Main Cast: John Candy, Joe Flaherty, Eugene Levy, Alley Mills, Pat Hingle
Keywords: humor, john candy

Overview: John Bourgignon, a drummer/chauffeur engaged to the daughter of a United States senator encounters a sleazy film director, the leader of an aerobics cult, and other crazed characters in the days leading up to his wedding.
