Title: An Act of War (2015)
Genres: Mystery, Drama
Director: Ryan M. Kennedy
Main Cast: Russ Russo, Natasha Alam, Doug E. Doug, Joseph R. Gannascoli, Robert Miano

Overview: Battling insomnia and undiagnosed PTSD, a war veteran works nights as a projectionist at a decrepit theater. While struggling to adapt to civilian life, he soon finds himself tangled in an inescapable web of seduction, addiction, and violence.
