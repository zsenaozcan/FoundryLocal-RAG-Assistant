Title: The Bridge to Nowhere (2009)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Blair Underwood
Main Cast: Ving Rhames, Bijou Phillips, Danny Masterson, Ben Crowley, Thomas Ian Nicholas
Keywords: underground, men, friends, illegal prostitution

Overview: A group of 20-something blue-collar workers in Pittsburgh cook up a scheme to make extra money that involves prostitution. Their business takes off, then gets way out of hand.
