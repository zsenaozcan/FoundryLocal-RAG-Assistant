Title: The Apple (1980)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Science Fiction, Music
Director: Menahem Golan
Main Cast: Catherine Mary Stewart, Grace Kennedy, Allan Love, Vladek Shaybal, Joss Ackland
Keywords: musical

Overview: Musical set in the 'future', e.g. 1994. A young couple enters the world of the music industry, but also the world of drugs.
