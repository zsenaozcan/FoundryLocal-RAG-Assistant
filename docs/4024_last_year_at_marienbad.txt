Title: Last Year at Marienbad (1961)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Alain Resnais
Main Cast: Delphine Seyrig, Giorgio Albertazzi, Sacha Pitoëff, Françoise Bertin, Luce Garcia-Ville
Keywords: hotel, amnesia, mystery, extramarital affair

Overview: Takes place in a chateau, an ambiguous story of a man and a woman who may or may not have met last year at Marienbad.
