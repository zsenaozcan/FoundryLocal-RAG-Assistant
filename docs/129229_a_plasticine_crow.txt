Title: A Plasticine Crow (1981)
Genres: Animation
Director: Aleksandr Tatarskiy
Main Cast: Leonid Bronevoy, Aleksandr Levenbuk, Lev Shimelov, Grigoriy Gladkov, Alyosha Pavlov
Keywords: plasticine

Overview: Short animation of a Russian folk tale, made in 1981. A parody of the fable by Ivan Krylov "The Crow and the Fox".
