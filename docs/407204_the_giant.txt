Title: The Giant (2016)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Johannes Nyholm
Main Cast: Christian Andrén, Johan Kylén, Anna Bjelkerud, Linda Faith, Amin Alabadi
Keywords: autism, sport, tournament, game, disabled, petanque, disfigured

Overview: A film about Rikard, age 30. He is autistic and severely disfigured. He lives in a home for disabled people. He was separated from his mother as a three-year-old, and this continues to torment him today. To deal with life's trials, Rikard escapes into a fantasy world in which he's a 50-meter-tall giant.
