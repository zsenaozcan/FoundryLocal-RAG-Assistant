Title: The Magic of Heineken (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Freddy Heineken, Charlene de Carvalho-Heineken, Michel Ray, Lucille Cummins-Heineken, Louisa de Carvalho

Overview: Take a captivating ride through nearly 150 years of entertaining history telling the story of the world's largest family controlled brewer. Meet Gerard Adriaan Heineken, who founded the first Heineken Brewery in 1864 and follow this extraordinary tale of pioneering adventure, relentless obsession for quality, persistent innovation, environmental sustainability, and celebrated sports and music events.
