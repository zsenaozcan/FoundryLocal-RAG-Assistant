Title: Sherlock Holmes in New York (1976)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Boris Sagal
Main Cast: Roger Moore, John Huston, Patrick Macnee, Charlotte Rampling, David Huddleston
Keywords: new york, sherlock holmes

Overview: An affectionate bow to the master sleuth in this lavishly produced original that has Holmes rushing to New York City after discovering that his old nemesis, Moriarty, has kidnapped the son of the detective's long-time love, actress Irene Adler.
