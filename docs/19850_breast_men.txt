Title: Breast Men (1997)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Comedy
Director: Lawrence O'Neil
Main Cast: David Schwimmer, Chris Cooper, Emily Procter, Matt Frewer, Terry O'Quinn
Keywords: surgeon, plastic surgery, stripper, breast

Overview: We follow the two Texas doctors who invented the modern breast implant and its surgical procedure. However, when success and money come their way, they split up and follow different paths. One becomes the surgeon of the everyday woman while the other's career freefalls and has to settle with strippers and actresses. The film covers their history, and their invention's, from the sixties until today
