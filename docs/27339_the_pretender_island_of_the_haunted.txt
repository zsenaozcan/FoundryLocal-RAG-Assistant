Title: The Pretender: Island of the Haunted (2001)
Genres: TV Movie, Action, Crime, Mystery
Director: Frederick King Keller
Main Cast: Michael T. Weiss, Andrea Parker, Patrick Bauchau, Jon Gries, Harve Presnell

Overview: Through a series of bizarre circumstances, Jarod and Miss Parker find themselves trapped together on a strange island in a life and death situation, out of which come some of the most important revelations about their pasts.
