Title: Candy (1968)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Adventure
Director: Christian Marquand
Main Cast: Ewa Aulin, Charles Aznavour, Marlon Brando, Richard Burton, James Coburn
Keywords: california, sex, obsession, seduction, satire

Overview: Based on Terry Southern's satirical novel, a send-up of Voltaire's Candide. Young Candy is a high school girl who seeks truth and meaning in life, encountering a variety of kookie characters and humorous sexual situations in the process.
