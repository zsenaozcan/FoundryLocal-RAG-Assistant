Title: The Fabulous Baron Munchausen (1962)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Fantasy
Director: Karel Zeman
Main Cast: Miloš Kopecký, Jana Brejchová, Rudolf Jelínek, Karel Höger, Eduard Kohout
Keywords: liar, baron, turkish army

Overview: The outrageous Baron Munchausen tells of his many adventures, from meeting the Man in the Moon to defeating a Turkish army all by himself.
