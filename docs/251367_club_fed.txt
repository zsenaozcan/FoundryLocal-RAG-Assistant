Title: Club Fed (1990)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Nat Christian
Main Cast: Burt Young, Allen Garfield, Judy Landers, Lance Kinsey, Sherman Hemsley

Overview: A powerful gangster is murdered. However, All of his holdings were in his girlfriend's name. So she is charged and sent to a minimum-security prison, where all of the prisoners are filthy rich. There she meets and falls in love with an undercover FBI agent.
