Title: Witchfinder General (1968)
Genres: Drama, Horror, History
Director: Michael Reeves
Main Cast: Vincent Price, Ian Ogilvy, Rupert Davies, Hilary Dwyer, Nicky Henson
Keywords: witch, burning of witches, sorcery

Overview: England is torn in civil strife as the Royalists battle the Parliamentary Party for control. This conflict distracts people from rational thought and allows unscrupulous men to gain local power by exploiting village superstitions. One of these men is Matthew Hopkins, who tours the land offering his services as a persecutor of witches.
