Title: All Blossoms Again: Pedro Costa, Director (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Aurélien Gerbault
Main Cast: Pedro Costa

Overview: With this movie, Aurélien Gerbault invites us to know the portuguese filmmaker Pedro Costa and to witness the process of shooting of his movie Colossal Youth (2006). The nature of Costa's cinema is revealed to us: the criation of an intimate space in the hardness of reality.
