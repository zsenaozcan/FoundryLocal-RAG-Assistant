Title: Long Time Dead (2002)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Marcus Adams
Main Cast: Joe Absolom, Lara Belmont, Melanie Gutteridge, Lukas Haas, James Hillier
Keywords: horror, ouija, spirit, game

Overview: A group of British students embark on summoning spirits on a Ouija board after a night of clubbing. But someone breaks the link before they have finished and now a demon is trapped in their world and the only way to banish it, is for all the people who summoned it to die.
