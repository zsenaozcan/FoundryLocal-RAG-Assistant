Title: Explosive City (2004)
Genres: Action
Director: Tak-Sam Leong
Main Cast: Kim-Fai Che, Sonny Chiba, Alex Fong Chung-Sun, Joe Cheung Tung-Cho, Simon Yam
Keywords: hit man

Overview: A young Japanese woman has been trained to become a professional killer since childhood. Two cops pursue the girl, who has assassinated a Hong Kong official.
