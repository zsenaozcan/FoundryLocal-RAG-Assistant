Title: The Run of the Country (1995)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Peter Yates
Main Cast: Albert Finney, Matt Keeslar, Victoria Smurfit, Anthony Brophy, David Kelly
Keywords: ireland, strained relationship

Overview: An Irish lad (Matt Keeslar) who fled from his oppressive, widowed father (Albert Finney) falls for a girl (Victoria Smurfit) from an affluent family.
