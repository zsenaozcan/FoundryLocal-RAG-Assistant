Title: The Loreley's Grasp (1974)
Genres: Horror
Director: Amando de Ossorio
Main Cast: Tony Kendall, Helga Liné, Silvia Tortosa, Loreta Tovar, Josefina Jartin
Keywords: monster, nudity, murder, blood, creature, violence, reptile

Overview: The legendary Loreley has been living for centuries in a grotto beneath the river Rhein in Germany. Every night when the moon is full, she turns into a reptile-like creature craving for human blood. When one girl after another of a nearby boarding school is killed by her, a hunter named Sirgurd is engaged to kill the monster.
