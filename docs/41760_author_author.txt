Title: Author! Author! (1982)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Arthur Hiller
Main Cast: Al Pacino, Dyan Cannon, Tuesday Weld, Bob Dishy, Bob Elliott
Keywords: marriage, children, playwright

Overview: A broadway playwright is burning the candle at both ends. He is dealing with pressure from a production nearing premiere, a wife who is leaving him, and 5 children 4 of which belong to her.
