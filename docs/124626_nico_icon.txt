Title: Nico Icon (1995)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Susanne Ofteringer
Main Cast: Nico, Tina Aumont, Jackson Browne, John Cale, Paul Morrissey

Overview: A look into the many lives of Christa Päffgen, otherwise known as Nico; from cutie German mädchen to the first of the supermodels, to glamorous diva of the Velvet Underground, to cult item, junkie and hag. Many faces for the same woman, whom, you realize, just couldn't bring herself to care enough to live.
