Title: Walking, Walking (1983)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Ermanno Olmi
Main Cast: Alberto Fumigalli, Antonio Cucciarrè, Eligio Martellucci, Renzo Samminiatesi, Marco Bartolini

Overview: Italian maestro Ermanno Olmi tells the tale of the journey of the Magi in this lyrical odyssey. The film focuses on a wise man who interprets his sighting of a comet streaking through the sky as a spiritual message. Joined by two associates, soldiers, villagers and others, the Magi sets out on a journey that tests his beliefs and strength.
