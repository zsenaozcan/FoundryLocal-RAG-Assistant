Title: Dare (2009)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Adam Salky
Main Cast: Emmy Rossum, Zach Gilford, Ashley Springer, Ana Gasteyer, Rooney Mara
Keywords: high school, independent film, male female relationship
