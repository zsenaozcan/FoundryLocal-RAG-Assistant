Title: Along Came a Nanny (2014)
Genres: Mystery
Director: Michael Scott
Main Cast: Cameron Mathison, Sarah Lancaster, Jessica Harmon, Brendan Penny, Leah Cairns
Keywords: undercover, nanny, police detective

Overview: Hoping to catch a burglar, a cop (Cameron Mathison) goes under cover as a nanny in an upscale community. As he looks for leads, he becomes involved with a high-maintenance family and a pretty caregiver (Sarah Lancaster) nearby.
