Title: After the Reality (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: David Anderson
Main Cast: Matthew Morrison, Sarah Chalke, Jon Dore, Laura Bell Bundy, Jane Lynch
Keywords: cabin, death of father, reality show, estranged sister, contestant

Overview: The life of a contestant on a 'Bachelorette' style reality show is thrown into turmoil when the sudden death of his father forces him to quit the series prematurely and reconnect with his estranged sister at the family cabin.
