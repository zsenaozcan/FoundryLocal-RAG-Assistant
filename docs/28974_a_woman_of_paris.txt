Title: A Woman of Paris (1923)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Charlie Chaplin
Main Cast: Edna Purviance, Clarence Geldart, Carl Miller, Lydia Knott, Charles K. French
Keywords: pearl necklace, high society, railroad station

Overview: When Marie St. Clair believes she has been jilted by her artist fiance Jean, she decides to leave for Paris on her own. After spending a year in the city as a mistress of the wealthy Pierre Revel, she is reunited with Jean by chance. This leaves her with the choice between a glamorous life in Paris, and the true love she left behind.
