Title: The Last Light (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Andrew Hyatt
Main Cast: Edward Furlong, Tahyna Tozzi, Yvonne Zima, Jeff Fahey, Ed Quinn

Overview: Seven strangers find themselves trapped inside an abandoned hospital after an unexplainable apocalyptic event. In addition to being haunted by what they've lost, the strangers must also fight off mysterious creatures that hunt them down one by one.
