Title: Between the Folds (2008)
Genres: Documentary, Family
Director: Vanessa Gould
Main Cast: Dr. Erik D. Demaine, Martin L. Demaine, Vincent Floderer, Miri Golan, Vanessa Gould
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Depicts a cast of fine artists and eccentric scientists (from MIT and NASA) who have devoted their lives to the unlikely medium of modern origami. Through their determination to reinterpret the world in paper, they arouse a fascinating mix of sensibilities towards art, form, expressiveness, creativity and meaning
