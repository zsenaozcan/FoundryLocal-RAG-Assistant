Title: Tomie: Unlimited (2011)
Genres: Horror
Director: Noboru Iguchi
Main Cast: Moe Arai, Miu Nakamura
Keywords: japan

Overview: Tsukiko is part of the photography club at school, but has always felt less than her sister Tomie. She's cute and very popular among boys. However, one day, Tomie dies in a horrible accident, triggering endless nightmares for Tsukiko. One year later, right when Tomie was supposed to turn 18, someone knocks on the door. Tomie is back. Little by little, Tomie will start to show her darkest side to Tsukiko, making her nightmares come to life.
