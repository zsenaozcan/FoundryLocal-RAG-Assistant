Title: Sauna (2010)
Genres: Horror
Director: Antti-Jussi Annila
Main Cast: Ville Virtanen, Tommi Eronen, Viktor Klimenko, Rain Tolk, Kari Ketonen
Keywords: border, russian, ritual, village, redemption, gothic horror, post war, medieval, orthodox, 16th century

Overview: It is 1595. Brutal wars have just ended in an uneasy peace between Protestant Sweden and Orthodox Russia. We focus on the spiritual defeats of two conquered Finnish brothers, one a hardened near-psychopathic war hero, the other a gentle scientist in an age with no use for such men. They find themselves in the swampy interior, demarcating the new border with a unit of sadistic Russians.
