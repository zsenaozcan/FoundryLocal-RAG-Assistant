Title: Amen (2013)
Genres: Fantasy, Music, Romance
Director: Lijo Jose Pellissery
Main Cast: Fahadh Faasil, Indrajith Sukumaran, Kalabhavan Mani, Swathi Reddy, Joy Mathew
Keywords: competition, musician, music, love, church, band, malayalam

Overview: Solomon, who belongs to a band, is in love with Soshanna, who hails from an upper class family. The story spells how Solomon manages to win over all the obstacles to marry Soshanna.
