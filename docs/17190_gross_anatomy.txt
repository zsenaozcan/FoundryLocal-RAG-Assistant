Title: Gross Anatomy (1989)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Thom Eberhardt
Main Cast: Matthew Modine, Christine Lahti, Todd Field, Daphne Zuniga, Zakes Mokae

Overview: It Takes Hard Work, Discipline And Commitment To Get Through Med School. But Joe's Never Done Anything By The Book. Matthew Modine stars as Joe Slovak, a brilliant first-year med student whose casual, nonconforming approach to life gets tested when he enrolls in Gross Anatomy, the toughest course in med school.
