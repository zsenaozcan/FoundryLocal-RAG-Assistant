Title: Hollywood Boulevard (1976)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Joe Dante
Main Cast: Candice Rialson, Mary Woronov, Dick Miller, Jeffrey Kramer, Jonathan Kaplan
Keywords: filmmaking

Overview: Joe Dante directs this story of the glamour, the glitter, the magical allure of Hollywood... and not a speck of it rubs off on Miracle Pictures, where "If it's a good picture, it's a Miracle." This is a hilarious tribute to the unsung heroes who grind out the B movies massacred by critics, but nursed fondly in the hearts of film fans everywhere.
