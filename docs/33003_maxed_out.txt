Title: Maxed Out (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: James D. Scurlock
Main Cast: Beth Naef, Mike Hudson, Louis C.K.
Keywords: banking, credit card, debt, economics, corporation, poor

Overview: Maxed Out takes us on a journey deep inside the American debt-style, where everything seems okay as long as the minimum monthly payment arrives on time. Sure, most of us may have that sinking feeling that something isn't quite right, but we're told not to worry. After all, there's always more credit!
