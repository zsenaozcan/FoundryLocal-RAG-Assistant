Title: I'm Afraid (1982)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Pekka Hyytiäinen
Main Cast: Pekka Valkeejärvi, Liisa Halonen, Heta Hyytiäinen, Matti Kanerva

Overview: In the near future, an out-of-control military drill shatters the peace of a family spending their summer vacation. War appears to be breaking out – or are the shocking events just a fabrication of the protagonists’ imagination?
