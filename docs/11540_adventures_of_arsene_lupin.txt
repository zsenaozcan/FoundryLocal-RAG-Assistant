Title: Adventures of Arsene Lupin (2004)
Genres: Romance, Action, Adventure, Crime, Mystery
Director: Jean-Paul Salomé
Main Cast: Romain Duris, Kristin Scott Thomas, Eva Green, Pascal Greggory, Robin Renucci
Keywords: paris, transformation, crook, thief, falling in love

Overview: As the daring thief Arsène Lupin (Duris) ransacks the homes of wealthy Parisians, the police, with a secret weapon in their arsenal, attempt to ferret him out.
