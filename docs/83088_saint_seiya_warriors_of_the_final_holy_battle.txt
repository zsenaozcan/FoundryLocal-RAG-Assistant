Title: Saint Seiya: Warriors of the Final Holy Battle (1989)
Genres: Action, Animation, Fantasy
Director: Masayuki Akehi
Main Cast: Tōru Furuya, Hirotaka Suzuoki, Kōichi Hashimoto, Ryou Horikawa, Hideyuki Hori
Keywords: mythology, fighter, manga, suit of armor, anime

Overview: The plot involves the coming of Lucifer to the Sanctuary, where his underlings systematically dispose of the surviving Gold Saints. Athena goes to Lucifer to ask for peace, placing herself in peril. The Bronze Saints must then come to her rescue, destroying Lucifer's Fallen Angels (Seima Tenshi in Japanese) in the process.
