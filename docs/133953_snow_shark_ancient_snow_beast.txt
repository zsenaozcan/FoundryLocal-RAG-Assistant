Title: Snow Shark: Ancient Snow Beast (2011)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror
Director: Sam Qualiana
Main Cast: Sam Qualiana, Michael O'Hear, Jackey Hall, C.J. Qualiana, Kathy Murphy
Keywords: monster, shark attack

Overview: 12 years ago during a scientific expedition 3 animal biologists stumbled upon a great discovery that ended in tragedy. Whatever killed them has awoken and now the legend of the Ancient Snow Beast could prove to be more than just a legend.
