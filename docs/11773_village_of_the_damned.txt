Title: Village of the Damned (1960)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Wolf Rilla
Main Cast: George Sanders, Barbara Shelley, Martin Stephens, Michael Gwynn, Laurence Naismith
Keywords: suicide, england, based on novel, parent child relationship, youngster, parish, village, car crash, children, pregnant

Overview: In a small English village everyone suddenly falls unconscious. When they awake every woman of child bearing age is pregnant. The resulting children have the same strange blond hair, eyes and a strong connection to each other.
