Title: Halloweentown II: Kalabar's Revenge (2001)
Genres: Adventure, Family, Fantasy, TV Movie
Director: Mary Lambert
Main Cast: Kimberly J. Brown, Debbie Reynolds, Daniel Kountz, Judith Hoag, Phillip Van Dyke
Keywords: witch, magic, sequel, warlock, disney channel, woman director, children's adventure

Overview: The Cromwell clan live in the real world, except for their grandmother who lives in Halloweentown, a place where monsters go to escape reality. But now the son of the Cromwells' old enemy Kalabar has a plan to use the grandmother's book to turn Halloweentown into a grey dreary version of the real world, while transform the denizens of the real world into monsters.
