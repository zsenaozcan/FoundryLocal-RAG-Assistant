Title: The Great Waldo Pepper (1975)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: George Roy Hill
Main Cast: Robert Redford, Susan Sarandon, Geoffrey Lewis, Bo Svenson, Bo Brundin
Keywords: world war i, aerial combat, red baron
