Title: Romanoff and Juliet (1961)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Peter Ustinov
Main Cast: Peter Ustinov, Sandra Dee, John Gavin, Akim Tamiroff, Alix Talton
Keywords: united nations, geofiction

Overview: Political satire has Shakespeare's Romeo &amp; Juliet story updated for the cold war era, with the children of the US and Russian ambassadors falling in love.
