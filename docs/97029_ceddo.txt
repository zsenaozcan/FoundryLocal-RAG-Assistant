Title: Ceddo (1977)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ousmane Sembene
Main Cast: Mamadou Dioumé, Ousmane Sembene, Tabata Ndiaye, Moustapha Yade, Ismaila Diagne

Overview: When King Demba War sides with the Muslims, the Ceddo kidnap his daughter, Princess Dior Yacine, to protest their forcible conversion to Islam.
