Title: Marie Curie (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Marie Noëlle
Main Cast: Karolina Gruszka, Arieh Worthalter, Izabela Kuna, Charles Berling, Daniel Olbrychski
Keywords: biopic

Overview: The most turbulent five years in the life of a genius woman: Between 1905, where Marie Curie comes with Pierre Curie to Stockholm to be awarded the Nobel Prize for the discovery of the radioactivity, and 1911, where she receives her second Nobel Prize, after challenging France's male-dominated academic establishment both as a scientist and a woman.
