Title: Sällskapsresan II - Snowroller (1985)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lasse Åberg
Main Cast: Lasse Åberg, Jon Skolmen, Cecilia Walton, Eva Millberg, Ingrid Wallin
Keywords: holiday, switzerland, winter, picnic, hang gliding, alps, swiss alps, ski resort, ski lift, photo booth

Overview: Stig-Helmer takes another vacation with his norwegian friend Ole. This time it's time for a skiing vacation in the Alps. Of course, Stig-Helmer has never learnt downhill skiing, but he attends a ski school. And together they manage to charm two women also on vacation.
