Title: Elsewhere (2009)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Nathan Hope
Main Cast: Anna Kendrick, Paul Wesley, Tania Raymonde, Olivia Dawn York, Jeff Daniel Phillips
Keywords: corruption, waitress, secret, small town, dream, kidnapping, nightmare, investigation, party, murder

Overview: A teen girl disappears after trying to meet men online in order to escape her small town. Apparently, only her best friend worries enough to investigate the mystery.
