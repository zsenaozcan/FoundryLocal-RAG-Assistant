Title: What's Between Us (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Claudia Lorenz
Main Cast: Antonio Buíl, Dominique Jann, Ursina Lardi, Linda Olsansky, Nicolas Rosat
Keywords: woman director

Overview: After eighteen years of marriage, Alice finds herself confronted with the fact that her husband Frank feels more and more attracted to men. Not only Frank, but also she and their three children undergo an irreversible change.
