Title: Seven Thieves (1960)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Henry Hathaway
Main Cast: Edward G. Robinson, Rod Steiger, Joan Collins, Eli Wallach, Alexander Scourby
Keywords: robbery, monte carlo, heist

Overview: A discredited professor and a sophisticated thief decide to join together and pick a team to pull off one last job--the casino vault in Monte Carlo.
