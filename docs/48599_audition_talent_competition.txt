Title: Audition/Talent Competition (1964)
Genres: Music, Documentary
Director: Miloš Forman
Main Cast: Jiří Suchý, Jiří Šlitr, Vladimír Pucholt, Marketa Krotka, Ladislav Jakim

Overview: Two closely related episodes. Youths make problems for two local orchestras about to compete nationally, and in a talent competition a young girl gets stage fright, while another lies to her boss to compete.
