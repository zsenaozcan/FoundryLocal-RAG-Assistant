Title: Oil City Confidential (2009)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Julien Temple
Main Cast: Lee Brilleaux, Wilko Johnson, John Martin, John B. Sparkes, Christopher Fenwick
Keywords: rock and roll, punk rock

Overview: Director Julien Temple's film celebrates Canvey Island's Dr Feelgood, the Essex R 'n' B band that exploded out of the UK in the prog era of the early Seventies, delivering shows and albums that helped pave the way for pub rock and punk.
