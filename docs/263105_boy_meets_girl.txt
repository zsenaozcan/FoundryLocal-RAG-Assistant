Title: Boy Meets Girl (2015)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Comedy
Director: Eric Schaeffer
Main Cast: Michael Welch, Michelle Hendley, Alexandra Turshen, Michael Galante, Joseph Ricci
Keywords: transsexuality, romance, gay relationship, relationship, transsexual

Overview: Boy Meets Girl is a funny, tender, sex positive romantic comedy that explores what it means to be a real man or woman, and how important it is to live a courageous life not letting fear stand in the way of going after your dreams
