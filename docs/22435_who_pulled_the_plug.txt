Title: Who Pulled the Plug? (1981)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Hans Iveberg
Main Cast: Kim Anderzon, Janne 'Loffe' Carlsson, Stig Ossian Ericson, Yvonne Lombard, Nils Eklund
Keywords: debt collector, boat chase, canal, race, boating accident, arab sheik

Overview: To secure an order for 1 000 cruise ships from an Arab prince, two companies have to compete by racing each other by canal from Stockholm to Gothenburg. A small family owned company on the brink of bankruptcy is pitted against their major competitor and they both have to resort to dirty tricks in order to reach the goal line first. Plot by Mattias Thuresson.
