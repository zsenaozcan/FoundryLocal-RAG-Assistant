Title: The Sleeping Dictionary (2003)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Guy Jenkin
Main Cast: Jessica Alba, Hugh Dancy, Brenda Blethyn, Christopher Ling Lee Ian, Emily Mortimer
Keywords: forbidden love

Overview: A young Englishman is dispatched to Sarawak to become part of the British colonial government. He encounters some unorthodox local traditions, and finds himself faced with tough decisions of the heart involving the beautiful Selima, the unwitting object of his affections.
