Title: Acts of Worship (2001)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Rosemary Rodriguez
Main Cast: Ana Reeder, Michael Hyatt, Christopher Kadish, Michael Buscemi, John Ahearn
Keywords: independent film, woman director

Overview: Alix is taken in by a photographer, Digna, who despite her friends' protests, tries to help Alix piece her life back together and overcome her addictions.
