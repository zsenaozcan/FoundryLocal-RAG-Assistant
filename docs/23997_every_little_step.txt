Title: Every Little Step (2009)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: James D. Stern
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: competition, new jersey, documentary, dance contest, chorus line, audition, casting director, choreographer, dance class, leotard

Overview: Every Little Step follows the plight of real-life dancers as they struggle through auditions for the Broadway revival of "A Chorus Line" and also investigates the history of the show and the creative minds behind the original and current incarnations.
