Title: Sodom and Gomorrah (1962)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, History
Director: Robert Aldrich
Main Cast: Stewart Granger, Stanley Baker, Pier Angeli, Rossana Podestà, Rik Battaglia
Keywords: sodom and gomorrah, biblical

Overview: Sex, torture and betrayal in Biblical Sodom and Gomorrah. Lot, leader of the Hebrews, believes his people can co-exist with the Sodomites, a disastrous decision.
