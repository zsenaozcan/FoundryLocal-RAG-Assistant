Title: Chelsea Walls (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ethan Hawke
Main Cast: Uma Thurman, Guillermo Díaz, Vincent D'Onofrio, Kris Kristofferson, Rosario Dawson
Keywords: independent film

Overview: This movie tells five stories set in a single day at the famed Chelsea Hotel in New York City, involving an ensemble cast of some 30-35 characters.
