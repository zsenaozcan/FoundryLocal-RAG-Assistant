Title: Ken (1964)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Kenji Misumi
Main Cast: Raizô Ichikawa, Yûsuke Kawazu, Hisaya Morishige, Akio Hasegawa, Noriko Sengoku

Overview: Kokubu Jiro is the captain of his university's Kendo team. Often standoffish, mild, stoic and minimalist, Kokubu is a mystery to those who know him. Kagawa wants to understand Kokubu, but being arrogant and flashy, has a hard time connecting with Kokubu.
