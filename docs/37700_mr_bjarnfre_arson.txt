Title: Mr. Bjarnfreðarson (2009)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Family
Director: Ragnar Bragason
Main Cast: Jón Gnarr, Pétur Jóhann Sigfússon, Jörundur Ragnarsson, Helga Braga Jónsdóttir

Overview: Centers on Georg Bjarnfreðarson, an over-educated know-it-all who has just been released from jail after a ten year sentence for manslaughter, but is still a prisoner of his own past.
