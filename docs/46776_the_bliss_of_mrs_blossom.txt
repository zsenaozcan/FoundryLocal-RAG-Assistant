Title: The Bliss of Mrs. Blossom (1968)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Joseph McGrath
Main Cast: Shirley MacLaine, Richard Attenborough, James Booth, Freddie Jones, William Rushton
Keywords: secret, lover, hidden, bored housewife

Overview: A housewife, bored with her bra-manufacturer husband, who spends most of his time obsessed with classical music, strikes up an affair with one of his employees whom she hides in the attic--something both she and the lover find quite a suitable arrangement.
