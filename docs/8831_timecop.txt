Title: Timecop (1994)
Genres: Thriller, Science Fiction, Action, Crime
Director: Peter Hyams
Main Cast: Jean-Claude Van Damme, Mia Sara, Ron Silver, Bruce McGill, Gloria Reuben
Keywords: martial arts, time travel, science fiction, alternative reality

Overview: An officer for a security agency that regulates time travel, must fend for his life against a shady politician who has a tie to his past.
