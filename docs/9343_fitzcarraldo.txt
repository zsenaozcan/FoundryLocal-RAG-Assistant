Title: Fitzcarraldo (1982)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: Werner Herzog
Main Cast: Klaus Kinski, José Lewgoy, Miguel Ángel Fuentes, Paul Hittscher, Claudia Cardinale
Keywords: plantation, peru, amazon, opera house, rubber plantation, riverboat

Overview: The story of Brian Sweeney Fitzgerald, an extremely determined man who intends to build an opera house in the middle of a jungle.
