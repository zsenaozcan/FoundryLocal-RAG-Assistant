Title: Tin Pan Alley (1940)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Walter Lang
Main Cast: Alice Faye, Betty Grable, John Payne, Jack Oakie, Esther Ralston
Keywords: musical

Overview: Songwriters Calhoun and Harrigan get Katie and Lily Blane to introduce a new one. Lily goes to England, and Katy joins her after the boys give a new song to Nora Bayes. All are reunited when the boys, now in the army, show up in England.
