Title: Roadkill (1989)
Genres: History, Adventure, Comedy, Drama
Director: Bruce McDonald
Main Cast: Valerie Buhagiar, Gerry Quigley, Larry Hudson, Bruce McDonald, Shaun Bowring
Keywords: canada, road trip, easter, independent film, rock band

Overview: Ramona works in a concert agency and is sent after the rock band 'Children of Paradise' who have disappeared. Since she can't drive, she has to take the train and bus and eventually hitchhike. On her way through the Canadian back country she meets the weirdest people - and learns to drive, what leads to... roadkill.
