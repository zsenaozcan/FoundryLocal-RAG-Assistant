Title: The Survivors (1983)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Michael Ritchie
Main Cast: Robin Williams, Walter Matthau, Jerry Reed, James Wainwright, Kristen Vigard
Keywords: robbery, secret identity, gas station, daughter, survivalist, unemployment

Overview: Having both lost their jobs, two strangers become unlikely friends after a run in with a would be robber, who is actually a hitman with a grudge against the two.
