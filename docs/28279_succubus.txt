Title: Succubus (1968)
Genres: Horror, Foreign
Director: Jesús Franco
Main Cast: Janine Reynaud, Jack Taylor, Adrian Hoven, Howard Vernon, Nathalie Nort

Overview: Janine Reynaud stars as a nightclub stripper who free-floats through a spectral 60's landscape littered with dream-figures, dancing midgets and bizarre S&amp;M games.
