Title: Sing Sing (1983)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sergio Corbucci
Main Cast: Adriano Celentano, Enrico Montesano, Vanessa Redgrave, Désirée Nosbusch, Marina Suma

Overview: Eduardo accidentally discovers that he is adopted. His real father is dying, but before his death he said that his mother was the "Queen of England", failing to clarify that this is the nickname of Italian prostitute. But Eduardo is already rushing to the capital of England...
