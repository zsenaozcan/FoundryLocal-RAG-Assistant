Title: Bachelors (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Kenny Young
Main Cast: Colin Egglesfield, David Faustino, Drew Fuller, Anna Hutchison, Noel Gugliemi

Overview: Womanizing advertising executive Aaron tries to stop best friend Sean from falling into the "eternal damnation" of marriage by throwing a wild bachelor party to remind Sean of the joys of bachelorhood.
