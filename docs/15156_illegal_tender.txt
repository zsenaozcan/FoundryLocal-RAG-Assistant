Title: Illegal Tender (2007)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: Franc. Reyes
Main Cast: Rick Gonzalez, Wanda De Jesus, Dania Ramirez, Delilah Cotto, Manny Pérez

Overview: After the gangsters who killed his father come to settle a score, a teenage boy and his mother turn the tables on the killers - one Latino family's quest for honor and revenge as the hunted become the hunters.
