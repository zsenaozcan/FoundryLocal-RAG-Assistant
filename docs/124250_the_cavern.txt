Title: The Cavern (1964)
Genres: Adventure, War, Drama
Director: Edgar G. Ulmer
Main Cast: John Saxon, Rosanna Schiaffino, Larry Hagman, Peter Marshall, Nino Castelnuovo
Keywords: italy, world war ii, trapped underground

Overview: Adventure drama during WW2 in Italy where a mixed group of people get trapped inside a cave after a bomb raid. But can they co-operate? And will they survive?
