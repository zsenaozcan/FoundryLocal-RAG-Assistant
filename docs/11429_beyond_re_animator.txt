Title: Beyond Re-Animator (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Brian Yuzna
Main Cast: Jeffrey Combs, Jason Barry, Elsa Pataky, Enrique Arce, Nico Baixas
Keywords: prison, nurse, monster, experiment, nudity, prison cell, human experimentation, revitalization, insanity, sequel

Overview: Once again tampering with mother nature to disastrous results, Dr. Herbert West continues his research while serving time in a maximum security prison for his previous exploits. West's limited prison-cell experiments are suddenly interrupted by the arrival of a new prison doctor and the brother of the girl who suffered from West's experiments 13 years earlier.
