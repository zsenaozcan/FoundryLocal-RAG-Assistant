Title: Turn Me On, Dammit! (2011)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Jannicke Systad Jacobsen
Main Cast: Helene Bergsholm, Malin Bjørhovde, Beate Støfring, Matias Myren, Julia Schacht
Keywords: best friend, woman director

Overview: In Skoddeheimen, Norway, 15-year-old Alma is consumed by her hormones and fantasies that range from sweetly romantic images of Artur, the boyfriend she yearns for, to daydreams about practically everybody she lays eyes on.
