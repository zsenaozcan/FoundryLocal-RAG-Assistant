Title: The Little Ghost (2013)
Genres: Family, Fantasy
Director: Alain Gsponer
Main Cast: Uwe Ochsenknecht, Herbert Knaup, Jonas Holdenrieder, Emily Kusche, Bettina Stucky
Keywords: ghost, gespenst

Overview: The Little Ghost lives in the castle over looking a small town and awakens for precisely one hour after the clock strikes midnight. Follow him on this adventure to see his first sunrise ever!
