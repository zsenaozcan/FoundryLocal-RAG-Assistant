Title: Deported Women of the SS Special Section (1975)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, War
Director: Rino Di Silvestro
Main Cast: Lina Polito, Erna Schürer, John Steiner, Sara Sperati, Solvi Stübing
Keywords: prisoners of war, nazi exploitation film, women in prision

Overview: Young women in Nazi-occupied countries are packed onto a train and shipped off to a prison camp, where the sadistic commandant uses them as rewards for his lesbian guards and perverted and deviate troops.
