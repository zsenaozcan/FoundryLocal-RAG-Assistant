Title: To Sir, with Love (1967)
Genres: Drama
Director: James Clavell
Main Cast: Sidney Poitier, Suzy Kendall, Christian Roberts, Judy Geeson, Lulu
Keywords: london england, education, teacher, school

Overview: Idealistic engineer-trainee and his experiences in teaching a group of rambunctious white high school students from the slums of London's East End.
