Title: Once Upon a Spy (1980)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller, TV Movie
Director: Ivan Nagy
Main Cast: Ted Danson, Mary Louise Weller, Christopher Lee, Eleanor Parker, Leonard Stone

Overview: Computer genius and chess champion Ted Danson is lured into service by secret agent Eleanor Parker and becomes a reluctant hero thanks to a number of exploits with villainous Christopher Lee and his army of lethal blue-eyed blonde women.
