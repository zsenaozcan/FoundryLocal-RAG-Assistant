Title: Real Men (1987)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Dennis Feldman
Main Cast: Jim Belushi, John Ritter, Barbara Barrie, Mark Herrier, Bill Morey

Overview: Jim Belushi plays a super-competent secret agent on the trail of Russian thugs. John Ritter plays a milquetoast dad who gets mixed up in the caper. The story follows their adventures over the course of a week, in which Ritter develops some guts and Belushi gets in touch with his sensitive side.
