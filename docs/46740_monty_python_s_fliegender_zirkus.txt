Title: Monty Python's Fliegender Zirkus (1971)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Graham Chapman, John Cleese, Eric Idle, Terry Jones, Michael Palin
Keywords: monty python

Overview: It is essentially the same concept as an English Monty Python's episode, but in German. The paintings by Albrecht Dürer, fall victim to silly animations. Little Red Riding Hood shows up too. A number of familiar sketches, including the Lumberjack song, all in German.
