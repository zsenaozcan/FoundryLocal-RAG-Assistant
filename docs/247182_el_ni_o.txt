Title: El niño (2014)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Daniel Monzón
Main Cast: Luis Tosar, Jesús Castro, Eduard Fernández, Sergi López, Bárbara Lennie
Keywords: spain, drug traffic, police, smuggling, hashish, drug, narcotics cop

Overview: The story stars two teenagers on the one side, El Niño and El Compi, who want to enter the world of drug trafficking, and two police officers on the other, Jesús and Eva, who have been trying to eradicate the drug trafficking network for years. A small-time trafficker working in the Gibraltar Straits.
