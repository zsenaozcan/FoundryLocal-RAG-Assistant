Title: Everybody Dies But Me (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Valeriya Gay Germanika
Main Cast: Polina Filonenko, Agniya Kuznetsova, Olga Shuvalova, Yuliya Aleksandrova, Donatas Grudovich
Keywords: high school, school, woman director

Overview: One Monday morning Katya, Vika and Zhanna learn that there will be a school disco, their first disco, on the coming Saturday night. The girls feverishly start preparing for the event, which rapidly becomes the most important moment ever in their universe, and looks like the ideal way to escape their daily lives...
