Title: Nicholas on Holiday (2014)
Genres: Family, Comedy
Director: Laurent Tirard
Main Cast: Valérie Lemercier, Kad Merad, Daniel Prévost, François Damiens, Francis Perrin

Overview: It's the end of the school year. The long-awaited moment of the holidays is arrive. The little Nicolas, her parents and Grandma take the road towards the sea, and settle in for some time at the Hotel Beau-Rivage.
