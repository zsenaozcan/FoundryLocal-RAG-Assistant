Title: Ryan (2004)
Genres: Fantasy, Documentary, Animation, Drama
Director: Chris Landreth
Main Cast: Ryan Larkin, Chris Landreth, Felicity Fanjoy, Derek Lamb

Overview: Ryan is a 2004 animated documentary by Chris Landreth about the Canadian animator Ryan Larkin, who in later years lived on skid row in Montreal following a history of drug and alcohol abuse.
