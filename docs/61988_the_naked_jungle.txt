Title: The Naked Jungle (1954)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Romance
Director: Byron Haskin
Main Cast: Charlton Heston, Eleanor Parker, William Conrad, Abraham Sofaer, Romo Vincent
Keywords: plantation, ants

Overview: The Leiningen South American cocoa plantation is threatened by a 2-mile-wide, 20-mile-long column of army ants.
