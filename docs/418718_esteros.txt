Title: Esteros (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Papu Curotto
Main Cast: Ignacio Rogers, Esteban Masturini, Joaquín Parada, Blas Finardi Niz, Renata Calmon
Keywords: gay, social control, friendship, past, love, homosexuality, memories

Overview: Matias and Jeronimo know each other since childhood. Their friendship takes a new turn during the holiday before starting high school when they both experience their sexual awakening.
