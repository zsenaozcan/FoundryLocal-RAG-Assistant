Title: August 32nd on Earth (1998)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Denis Villeneuve
Main Cast: Paule Baillargeon, Emmanuel Bilodeau, Pascale Bussières, Joanne Côté, Frédéric Desager

Overview: Young Simone gets hit by a near fatal car crash, and as she questions her mortality, she also decides to have a baby. Her candidate for a father is her best friend Phillipe who happens to be seeing someone. He agrees, as long as they conceive in Salt Lake City, in the desert. The trip teaches many lessons about love, solitude, and self-discovery.
