Title: Free the Nipple (2014)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Lina Esco
Main Cast: Lola Kirke, Casey LaBow, Lina Esco, Monique Coleman, Zach Grenier
Keywords: woman director

Overview: An army of passionate women launch a revolution to "Free the Nipple" and decriminalize the female body. Based on a true story, this mass movement of topless women, armed with First Amendment lawyers, graffiti installations and national publicity stunts, invade New York City to protest the backwards censorship laws in the USA.
