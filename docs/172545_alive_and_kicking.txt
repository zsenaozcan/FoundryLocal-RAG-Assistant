Title: Alive and Kicking (1996)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Nancy Meckler
Main Cast: Jason Flemyng, Antony Sher, Dorothy Tutin, Anthony Higgins, Bill Nighy
Keywords: independent film, gay interest, gay man, woman director

Overview: A passionately committed young dancer is forced to re-examine his career and life when faced with death, finding hope through an older man who becomes his lover, mentor and companion.
