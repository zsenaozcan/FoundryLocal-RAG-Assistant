Title: Alligator (1980)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Lewis Teague
Main Cast: Robert Forster, Robin Riker, Michael V. Gazzo, Dean Jagger, Sydney Lassick
Keywords: hunter, mutation, alligator, underwear, toxic, briefs, scientist, sewer, child eaten, timebomb

Overview: A baby alligator is flushed down a Chicago toilet and survives by eating discarded lab rats, injected with growth hormones. The now gigantic animal, escapes the city sewers, and goes on a rampage, pursued by a cop and a big-game hunter.
