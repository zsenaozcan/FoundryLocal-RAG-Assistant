Title: Sundown: The Vampire in Retreat (1989)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Western
Director: Anthony Hickox
Main Cast: David Carradine, Morgan Brittany, Bruce Campbell, Jim Metzler, Deborah Foreman
Keywords: vampire, humor

Overview: Reclusive vampires lounge in a lonely American town. They wear sun cream to protect themselves. A descendant of Van Helsing arrives with hilarious consequences.
