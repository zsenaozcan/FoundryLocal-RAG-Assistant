Title: Reverse (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Thriller, Romance
Director: Borys Lankosz
Main Cast: Agata Buzek, Krystyna Janda, Marcin Dorociński, Anna Polony, Adam Woronowicz

Overview: A story about women, set in the present and in 1950s Warsaw. Sabina, a quiet, shy woman who has just turned thirty lacks a man in her life. Her mother knows all about it and tries at all costs to find her daughter a good candidate for a husband. The whole situation is controlled by the grandmother, an eccentric lady with a sharp tongue from whom no secrets can be kept.
