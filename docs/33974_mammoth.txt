Title: Mammoth (2009)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Lukas Moodysson
Main Cast: Gael García Bernal, Michelle Williams, Marife Necesito, Sophie Nyweide, Tom McCarthy
Keywords: mother, thailand, philippines, independent film, unfaithfulness

Overview: While on a trip to Thailand, a successful American businessman tries to radically change his life. Back in New York, his wife and daughter find their relationship with their live-in Filipino maid changing around them. At the same time, in the Philippines, the maid's family struggles to deal with her absence.
