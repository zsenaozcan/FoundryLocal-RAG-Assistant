Title: The Wildcat (1921)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Ernst Lubitsch
Main Cast: Pola Negri, Victor Janson, Paul Heidemann, Wilhelm Diegelmann, Hermann Thimig
Keywords: silent film, masochist, action heroine

Overview: A charismatic lieutenant newly assigned to a remote fort is captured by a group of mountain bandits, thus setting in motion a madcap farce that is Lubitsch at his most unrestrained. A wonderfully anarchic and playfully subversive satire of military life from one of the great comedy filmmakers.
