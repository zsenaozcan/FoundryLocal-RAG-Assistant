Title: Nice People (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Karin af Klintberg
Main Cast: Filip Hammar, Fredrik Wikingsson
Keywords: somalia, sport, xenophobia, woman director, bandy

Overview: As the first African team ever, Somalia has just signed up for the Bandy World Championships. The young players doesn't live in Somalia. They live in Borlänge, Sweden, where xenophobia has taken hold.
