Title: The PyraMMMid (2011)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Eldar Salavatov
Main Cast: Aleksey Serebryakov, Pyotr Fyodorov, Fyodor Bondarchuk, Ekaterina Vilkova, Yuriy Tsurilo

Overview: Russia, early 90s. A fictional story inspired by financier Sergey Mavrodi, his securities company "MMM", and the pyramid scheme he ran that left upwards of fifteen million investors with nothing when it crashed.
