Title: The Testaments (2000)
Genres: Drama
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Rick Macy, Jeremy Hoop, Arianna Marsden, Joy Gardner, Mowava Pryor

Overview: This film depict events from the Life and ministry of Jesus Christ as recorded in the New Testament and the Book of Mormon. The story culminates with a portrayal of the Ressurected Savior's visit to the ancient Americas as seen through the eyes of a fictional family. Jesus' ministry to the members of His fold in both the Holy Land and the ancient Americas powerfully demonstrates that He is the Good Shepherd of all who hear His voice.
