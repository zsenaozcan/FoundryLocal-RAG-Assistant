Title: Brain Donors (1992)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Dennis Dugan
Main Cast: Mel Smith, Bob Nelson, John Turturro, Teri Copley, George de la Peña
Keywords: idiot, slapstick, vaudeville

Overview: Three manic idiots; a lawyer, cab driver and a handyman team up to run a ballet company to fulfill the will of a millionaire. Stooge-like antics result as the trio try to outwit the rich widow and her scheming big-shot lawyer, who also wants to run the ballet.
