Title: Treasure Island (1982)
Genres: Adventure, Family
Director: Vladimir Vorobyov
Main Cast: Fyodor Stukov, Vladislav Strzhelchik, Oleg Borisov, Valeriy Zolotukhin, Nikolai Karachentsov

Overview: Young Jim Hawkins is caught up with the pirate Long John Silver in search of the buried treasure of the buccaneer Captain Flint, in this adaptation of the classic novel by Robert Louis Stevenson.
