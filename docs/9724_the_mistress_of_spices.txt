Title: The Mistress of Spices (2005)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Gurinder Chadha
Main Cast: Aishwarya Rai Bachchan, Dylan McDermott, Nitin Ganatra, Adewale Akinnuoye-Agbaje, Caroline Chikezie
Keywords: san francisco, new love, spice, woman director

Overview: Tilo, the titular "Mistresses of Spices" is a shopkeeper and an immigrant from India who helps customers satisfy their needs and desires with spices. Her life changes when she falls in love with an American man.
