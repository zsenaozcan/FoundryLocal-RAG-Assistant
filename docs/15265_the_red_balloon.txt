Title: The Red Balloon (1956)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Family
Director: Albert Lamorisse
Main Cast: Pascal Lamorisse, Georges Sellier, Paul Perey, Vladimir Popov
Keywords: paris, balloon

Overview: In this deceptively simple, nearly wordless tale, a young boy discovers a stray balloon, which seems to have a mind of its own, on the streets of Paris. The two become inseparable, yet the world’s harsh realities finally interfere. With its glorious palette and allegorical purity, this Academy Award-winning short film has enchanted young and old for generations.
