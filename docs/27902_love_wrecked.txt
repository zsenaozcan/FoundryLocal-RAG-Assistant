Title: Love Wrecked (2005)
Genres: Family, Romance
Director: Randal Kleiser
Main Cast: Amanda Bynes, Chris Carmack, Jonathan Bennett, Jamie Lynn Sigler, Susan Duerden
Keywords: beach, island, rock star, shipwreck, stranded, teenage crush, marooned, resort, lying

Overview: On a Caribbean cruise, Jenny (Bynes) is marooned on a beach with her rock and roll idol. Deliriously in love with the idea of time alone with him, she manages to hide the fact that they're a stone's throw away from their resort.
