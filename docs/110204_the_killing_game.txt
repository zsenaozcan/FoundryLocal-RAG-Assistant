Title: The Killing Game (2011)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, TV Movie
Director: Bobby Roth
Main Cast: Laura Prepon, Ty Olsson, Brian Markinson, Kavan Smith, Greyston Holt

Overview: Eve Duncan is contacted, 10 years after the disappearance and murder of her 7-year-old daughter Bonnie, by a man claiming to be her killer. Taunted by his clues about Bonnie's case and his threats to kill again, she throws herself into her career as a forensic sculptor and works to close the case.
