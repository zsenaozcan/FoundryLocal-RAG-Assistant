Title: They Found Hell (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: Nick Lyon
Main Cast: Chris Schellenger, Katy Reece, Austin Scott, Kabby Borders, Mirela Burke
Keywords: teleportation, hell, portal, demon

Overview: When a group of gifted college students run a secret teleportation experiment, they accidentally open a portal to another dimension, trapping them in Hell. One by one they are hunted, tortured and killed by the denizens of Hell who are bent on stealing their souls.
