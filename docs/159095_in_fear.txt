Title: In Fear (2013)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Jeremy Lovering
Main Cast: Alice Englert, Iain De Caestecker, Allen Leech
Keywords: psychopath, stalker, fear, sadist, stranger

Overview: Driving, lost and tormented in the night, primal fears of the dark and the unknown give way to fear that you have let the evil in, or that it is already there.
