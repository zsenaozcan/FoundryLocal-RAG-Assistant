Title: Hijacking Catastrophe: 9/11, Fear & the Selling of American Empire (2004)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jeremy Earp
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: george w. bush, neocons

Overview: This documentary places the Bush Administration's original justifications for war in Iraq within the larger context of a two-decade struggle by neo-conservatives to dramatically increase military spending while projecting American power and influence globally by means of force.
