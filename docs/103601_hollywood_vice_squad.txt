Title: Hollywood Vice Squad (1986)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Penelope Spheeris
Main Cast: Ronny Cox, Frank Gorshin, Leon Isaac Kennedy, Trish Van Devere, Carrie Fisher
Keywords: police operation, woman director

Overview: A mother goes to Hollywood to find her runaway daughter. She discovers that the girl has become involved in the pornography industry, and goes to the police to get help in finding her.
