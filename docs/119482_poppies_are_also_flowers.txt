Title: Poppies Are Also Flowers (1966)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery
Director: Terence Young
Main Cast: Senta Berger, Stephen Boyd, Yul Brynner, Angie Dickinson, Georges Géret
Keywords: opium, narcotics cop

Overview: In an attempt to stem the heroin trade from Iran, a group of narcotics agents working for the UN inject a radioactive compound into a seized shipment of opium, in the hopes that it will lead them to the main heroin distributor in Europe. Along the way, they encounter a mysterious woman doing her own investigating of the smuggling operation.
