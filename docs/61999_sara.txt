Title: Sara (1997)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime, Romance, Family
Director: Maciej Slesicki
Main Cast: Bogusław Linda, Agnieszka Włodarczyk

Overview: Forty-something Leon is an alcoholic, ex-special forces barely scraping by when he's hired by a mobster to protect his 16 year old daughter. Not only does Leon have to clean up his act, but also handle the romantic advances of his ward and the keen attention of his dangerous employers.
