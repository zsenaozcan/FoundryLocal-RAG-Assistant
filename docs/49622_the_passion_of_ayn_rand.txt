Title: The Passion of Ayn Rand (1999)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Christopher Menaul
Main Cast: Helen Mirren, Eric Stoltz, Julie Delpy, Peter Fonda

Overview: The rather eccentric (especially in her thinking) author of "The Fountainhead" and "Atlas Shrugged" becomes involved with a much younger, and married man... to the dismay of those close to her.
