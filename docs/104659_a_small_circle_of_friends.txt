Title: A Small Circle of Friends (1980)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Rob Cohen
Main Cast: Brad Davis, Karen Allen, Jameson Parker, Shelley Long, John Friedrich

Overview: In the late 1960s, three Harvard students Jessica (Karen Allen), Leo (Brad Davis) and Nick (Jameson Parker) grow close as they undergo personal changes, but their friendship is jeopardized by romantic feelings both men develop for Jessica.
