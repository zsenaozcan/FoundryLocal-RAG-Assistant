Title: Born to Be Bad (1934)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Lowell Sherman
Main Cast: Cary Grant, Loretta Young, Jackie Kelk, Marion Burns, Henry Travers

Overview: Letty, a young woman who ended up pregnant, unmarried and on the streets at fifteen is bitter and determined that her child will not grow up to be taken advantage of. Letty teaches her child to lie, steal, cheat and anything else he'll need to be street smart.
