Title: The Memphis Belle: A Story of a Flying Fortress (1944)
Genres: Documentary
Director: William Wyler
Main Cast: Robert Morgan, Vince Evans, Jacob L. Devers, Ira C. Eaker, Haywood Hansell

Overview: Documentary about the 25th and last bombing mission of a B17, the "Memphis Belle". The "Memphis Belle" took part in a great bombing raid on sub-pens in Wilhelmshafen, Germany.  If she makes it back from this one, she'll be retired from combat and sent home. That is, if she makes it!
 Fly with the brave men of The Great Flying Fortress on this genuine combat mission! The true story of the real plane and the real crew - actually filmed as it was happening!
