Title: Count Dracula (1970)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jesús Franco
Main Cast: Christopher Lee, Klaus Kinski, Herbert Lom, Maria Rohm, Soledad Miranda
Keywords: dracula, blood, gothic horror

Overview: Jess Franco's version of the Bram Stoker classic has Count Dracula as an old man who grows younger whenever he dines on the blood of young maidens.
