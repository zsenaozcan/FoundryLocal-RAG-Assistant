Title: The Battalion (2015)
Genres: War, Action, Drama, History
Director: Dmitri Meskhiyev
Main Cast: Mariya Aronova, Mariya Kozhevnikova, Irina Rakhmanova, Marat Basharov, Evgeniy Dyatlov

Overview: Russia, 1917, WWI. This is the story of the 1st Russian Women's Battalion of Death, formed as part of an ill-conceived propaganda ploy by the Russian Provisional Government in late May of 1917.
