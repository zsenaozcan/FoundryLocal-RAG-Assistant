Title: Tree Without Leaves (1986)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Kaneto Shindô
Main Cast: Nobuko Otowa, Ichirô Zaitsu, Meiko Kaji, Kotoe Hatsui, Taiji Tonoyama
Keywords: japanese, drama, asian

Overview: Haru, an aging scriptwriter, has isolated himself somewhere in the woods of Nagano to work on his first novel. As the last surviving member of his kin, he intends to chronicle the family he grew up in.
