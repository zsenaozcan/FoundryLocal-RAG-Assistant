Title: Springtime in the Sierras (1947)
Genres: Western
Director: William Witney
Main Cast: Roy Rogers, Jane Frazee, Andy Devine, Stephanie Bachelor, Harold Landon
Keywords: horse, poacher, trucolor

Overview: Jean Loring has her men illegally killing and selling game. Roy suspects her and gets himself invited to stay at her ranch. Investigating he finds the freezer where the slaughtered game are kept. But he is caught, tied up, and left to freeze.
