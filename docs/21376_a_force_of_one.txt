Title: A Force of One (1979)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Paul Aaron
Main Cast: Chuck Norris, Jennifer O'Neill, Clu Gulager, Ron O'Neal, Bill Wallace
Keywords: karate, special unit

Overview: Karate champion Matt Logan is enlisted by the police to train officers in self-defense after narcotics agents are killed by an assailant using the martial arts.
