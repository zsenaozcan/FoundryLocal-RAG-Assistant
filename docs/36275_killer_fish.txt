Title: Killer Fish (1979)
Genres: Adventure, Horror
Director: Antonio Margheriti
Main Cast: Lee Majors, Karen Black, Margaux Hemingway, Marisa Berenson, James Franciscus
Keywords: treasure, water, betrayal, jewel, jewel thief, piranha

Overview: Jewel thieves attempt to recover treasure from piranha infested waters. Mistrust and betrayals happen amongst the gang in the quest for gold.
