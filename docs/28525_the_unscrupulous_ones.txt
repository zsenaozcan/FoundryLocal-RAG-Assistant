Title: The Unscrupulous Ones (1962)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ruy Guerra
Main Cast: Jece Valadão, Daniel Filho, Norma Bengell, Hugo Carvana, Lucy de Carvalho
Keywords: beach, convertible

Overview: This film captures the criminal behavior of two young Copacabana thugs (the "cafajestes"). The two plan a blackmail coup against a rich old man, using nude pictures of a lady on a deserted beach. But things don't always happen as planned.
