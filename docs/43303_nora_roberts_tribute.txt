Title: Nora Roberts' Tribute (2009)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Horror, Romance
Director: Martha Coolidge
Main Cast: Brittany Murphy, Jason Lewis, Christian Oliver, Diana Scarwid, Tippi Hedren
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A former child star buys her grandmother's house to rescue it from ruin but her hope for serenity is soon eclipsed by haunting dreams of her famous grandmother, who died of a supposed overdose in the house more than 30 years ago.
