Title: Fat, Sick & Nearly Dead 2 (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Kurt Engfehr
Main Cast: Joe Cross
Keywords: lifestyle, diet, sequel, advocacy

Overview: Joe Cross took viewers on his journey from overweight and sick to healthy and fit via a 60-day juice fast in the award-winning Fat Sick and Nearly Dead. With Fat, Sick &amp; Nearly Dead 2, he looks at keeping healthy habits long-term.
