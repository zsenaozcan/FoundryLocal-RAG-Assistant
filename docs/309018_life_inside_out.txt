Title: Life Inside Out (2014)
Genres: Music, Drama
Director: Jill D'Agnenica
Main Cast: Maggie Baird, Finneas O'Connell, Lori Nasso, David Cowgill, William Dennis Hunt
Keywords: music, family, woman director, teenage son

Overview: When a mother returns to her musical roots, she rediscovers the passion of her youth, and finds a way to connect with her troubled youngest son.
