Title: English, August (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Dev Benegal
Main Cast: Rahul Bose, Tanvi Azmi, Mita Vasisht

Overview: Agasyta, an urban bengali who seamlessly shuttles between Ella Fitzgerald and Rabindra Sangeet, joins the Indian administration service and gets posted in the lap of India's hinterland - a dingy little town called Madna.
