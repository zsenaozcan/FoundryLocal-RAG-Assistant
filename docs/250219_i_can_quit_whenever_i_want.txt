Title: I Can Quit Whenever I Want (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sydney Sibilia
Main Cast: Valeria Solarino, Valerio Aprea, Paolo Calabresi, Libero De Rienzo, Pietro Sermonti

Overview: A university researcher is fired because of the cuts to university. To earn a living he decides to produce drugs recruiting his former colleagues, who despite their skills are living at the margins of society.
