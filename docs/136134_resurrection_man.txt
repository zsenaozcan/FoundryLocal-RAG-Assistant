Title: Resurrection Man (1998)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: Marc Evans
Main Cast: Stuart Townsend, John Hannah, James Nesbitt, Brenda Fricker, Derek Thompson
Keywords: journalist, murder, ireland, protestant

Overview: Belfast, in 1970s. Victor Kelly is a young protestant man who hates the Catholics so much that one night he begins to brutally murder them. A reporter soon tries to uncover the murder and obtained prestige for himself, while Victor sinks deeper into madness.
