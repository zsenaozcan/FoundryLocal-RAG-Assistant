Title: Street Fighter Alpha - The Movie (1999)
Genres: Action, Animation
Director: Shigeyasu Yamauchi
Main Cast: Kane Kosugi, Kazuya Ichijō, Yumi Touma, Tomomichi Nishimura, Ai Orikasa
Keywords: video game, martial arts, street fighter, based on video game

Overview: Increasingly disturbed by the dark energies building within him, Ryu is confronted by Shun, a boy claiming to be the brother he never knew. But before Ryu can consider whether Shun's timely appearance might be more than coincidental, Shun is kidnapped by Shadowlaw. To recover Shun, Ryu must undertake the ultimate journey of self-discovery and learn to control the power threatening to consume him.
