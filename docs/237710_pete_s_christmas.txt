Title: Pete's Christmas (2013)
Genres: Fantasy, Family
Director: Nisha Ganatra
Main Cast: Zachary Gordon, Molly Parker, Bailee Madison, Bruce Dern, Rick Roberts
Keywords: woman director

Overview: An overlooked middle child finds himself in the unexpected spotlight when he realizes his family's terrible Christmas day keeps repeating. As the only one experiencing the day over and over, he decides to use his unique gift to give the holidays a makeover and his family a Christmas they will never forget.
