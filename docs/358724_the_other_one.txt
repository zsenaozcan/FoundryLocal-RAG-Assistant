Title: The Other One (2014)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Josef Steiff
Main Cast: Grace McPhilips, Nancy Sellers, Jesse Bob Harper, Caitlin Bidwell, Brian Crawford
Keywords: dementia

Overview: A teacher, haunted by the death of her husband, returns to her childhood home to care for her ill mother. But as her mother's dementia deepens, dark secrets tumble out, including a family secret that will turn her life upside-down forever.
