Title: Frozen Land (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Aku Louhimies
Main Cast: Jasper Pääkkönen, Mikko Leppilampi, Pamela Tola, Petteri Summanen, Matleena Kuusniemi
Keywords: alcohol, depression, helsinki, alcoholism, counterfeit, alcoholic

Overview: When a schoolteacher is sacked, he projects his bad mood at his troubled teenage son. The son, in turn, buys a CD player from a pawnshop with counterfeit money. This starts a chain reaction of misery as every victim projects his problems on to another person.
