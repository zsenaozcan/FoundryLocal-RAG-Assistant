Title: Kill Ratio (2016)
Genres: Action
Director: Paul Tanter
Main Cast: Tom Hopper, Amy Huberman, Nick Dunning, Lacy Moore, Michelle Lehane
Keywords: government, president, covert agency, military

Overview: An attack on the new President of a fledgling Eastern European democracy pits an American covert operative against the country’s ruthless military leader determined to seize control of the government.
