Title: Cinderella (1899)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Science Fiction, Family
Director: Georges Méliès
Main Cast: Georges Méliès, Barral, Bleuette Bernon, Carmely, Jeanne d'Alcy
Keywords: fairy tale, fairy godmother

Overview: A fairy godmother magically turns Cinderella's rags to a beautiful dress, and a pumpkin into a coach. Cinderella goes to the ball, where she meets the Prince - but will she remember to leave before the magic runs out?  Méliès based the art direction on engravings by Gustave Doré. First known example of a fairy-tale adapted to film, and the first film to use dissolves to go from one scene to another.
