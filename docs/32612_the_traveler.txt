Title: The Traveler (2010)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Michael Oblowitz
Main Cast: John Cassini, Val Kilmer, Camille Sullivan, Dylan Neal, Paul McGillion

Overview: On a dark Christmas Eve in a small town, the lone Sheriffs on the night shift encounter a mysterious man who goes by the name of Mr. Nobody. As the night progresses, the Sheriffs discover that this isn't just a nobody, but a vengeful killer whose past threatens to haunt them all.
