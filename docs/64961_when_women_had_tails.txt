Title: When Women Had Tails (1970)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Pasquale Festa Campanile
Main Cast: Senta Berger, Giuliano Gemma, Frank Wolff, Renzo Montagnani, Lino Toffolo
Keywords: cavemen, sex comedy

Overview: Cavemen Ulli (Giuliano Gemma), Kao (Lando Buzzanca), Grrr, Put, Zog, Maluo and Uto meet Filli (Senta Berger) with the light-brown tail.
