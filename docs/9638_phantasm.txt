Title: Phantasm (1979)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Don Coscarelli
Main Cast: Angus Scrimm, A. Michael Baldwin, Bill Thornbury, Reggie Bannister, Kathy Lester
Keywords: evil, ice cream man  , mausoleum, tall man, sentinals, sphere
