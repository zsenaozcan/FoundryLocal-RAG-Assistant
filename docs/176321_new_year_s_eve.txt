Title: New Year's Eve (1924)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Lupu Pick
Main Cast: Eugen Klöpfer, Edith Posca, Frida Richard, Karl Harbacher, Julius E. Herrmann

Overview: The film is set in a bar on New Year's Eve. We see plenty of Germans swilling beer non-stop and the owner of the bar and his wife await the arrival of his mother for a little celebration together.
