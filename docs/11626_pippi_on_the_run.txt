Title: Pippi on the Run (1970)
Genres: Family
Director: Olle Hellbom
Main Cast: Inger Nilsson, Maria Persson, Pär Sundberg, Hans Alfredson, Öllegård Wellton
Keywords: runaway, sweden, strong woman, child hero, folk hero
