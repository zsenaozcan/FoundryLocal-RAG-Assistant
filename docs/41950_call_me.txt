Title: Call Me (1988)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Sollace Mitchell
Main Cast: Patricia Charbonneau, Stephen McHattie, Boyd Gaines, Steve Buscemi, Sam Freed
Keywords: bar, mistake, murder, meeting, boyfriend, phone, call, obscene

Overview: A journalist receives an obscene phonecall that she mistakes for her boyfriend. She then agrees to meet with the caller at a bar, but instead of meeting the boyfriend, she witnesses a murder.
