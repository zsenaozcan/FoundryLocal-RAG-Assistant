Title: The Student (2011)
Genres: Foreign, Drama
Director: Santiago Mitre
Main Cast: Romina Paula, Esteban Lamothe, Ricardo Felix, Valeria Correa, Martín Tchira

Overview: Roque starts University in Buenos Aires but he is not particularly interested in attending classes or working towards a degree. Instead, he dedicates his time to one of the many groups vying for control of the university, motivated less by grand political ideals than by a wish to get close to Paula, an attractive young teacher heavily involved in internal university politics.
