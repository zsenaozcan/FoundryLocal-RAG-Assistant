Title: Shakma (1990)
Genres: Horror
Director: Tom Logan
Main Cast: Christopher Atkins, Amanda Wyss, Ari Meyers, Roddy McDowall, Robb Edward Morris
Keywords: independent film, ape, baboon, dungeons and dragons, animal attack

Overview: A murderous baboon escapes from a laboratory and roams the research building, and begins to kill some teenagers who are also in the building playing a Dungeons-and-Dragons type game.
