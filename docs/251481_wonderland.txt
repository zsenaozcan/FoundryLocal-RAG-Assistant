Title: Wonderland (1997)
Genres: Documentary
Director: John O'Hagan
Main Cast: Bill Griffith

Overview: Welcome to Levittown, New York - America's first cookie-cutter community. From wife-swapping to bomb shelters, to flag burning, Wonderland takes a hilarious and unforgettable look at life in a town where thousands of identical-looking houses were assigned to their residents in alphabetical order.
