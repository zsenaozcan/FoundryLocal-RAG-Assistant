Title: Zero. Lilac Lithuania (2006)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Emilis Velyvis
Main Cast: Mandaugas Papinigis, Andrius Paulavičius, Justas Vencius, Ramūnas Rudokas

Overview: ZERO is an 0 sucking in a young deserter, a casino victim and three heroin brothers. That is a destiny, a mixture of objectives and desires of heroes. That is a lifestyle balancing on the edge - bullets, numbers and needles.
