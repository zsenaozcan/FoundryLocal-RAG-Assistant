Title: Port of Call (1948)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ingmar Bergman
Main Cast: Nine-Christine Jönsson, Bengt Eklund, Mimi Nelson, Birgitta Valberg, Sif Ruud
Keywords: factory, reformatory

Overview: A suicidal factory girl out of reformatory school, anxious to escape her overbearing mother, falls in love with a sailor who can't forgive her past.
