Title: Casanova (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lasse Hallström
Main Cast: Heath Ledger, Sienna Miller, Jeremy Irons, Oliver Platt, Lena Olin
Keywords: venice, female nudity, women, lover (female), carnival, man between two women, casanova, extramarital affair, catholicism

Overview: With a reputation for seducing members of the opposite sex, regardless of their marital status, a notorious womanizer discovers a beauty who seems impervious to his charms. However, as he continues to pursue the indifferent lady, he finds himself falling in love.
