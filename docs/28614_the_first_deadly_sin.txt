Title: The First Deadly Sin (1980)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Brian G. Hutton
Main Cast: Frank Sinatra, Faye Dunaway, David Dukes, George Coe, Brenda Vaccaro
Keywords: suspense

Overview: A serial killer is stalking New York. Inspector Edward X. Delaney is an NYPD detective, nearing retirement, who is trying to put together the pieces of the case. Are the victims somehow linked? What does the brutal method of death signify?
