Title: Backfire (1964)
Genres: Crime, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Jean Becker
Main Cast: Jean-Paul Belmondo, Jean Seberg, Enrico Maria Salerno, Renate Ewert, Jean-Pierre Marielle
Keywords: smuggling

Overview: A greedy gold smuggler hires a handsome hero to transport a stolen fortune to a new hideout accompanied by the smuggler's sexy girlfriend.
