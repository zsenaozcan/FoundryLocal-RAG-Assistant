Title: The Best Man (1964)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Franklin J. Schaffner
Main Cast: Henry Fonda, Cliff Robertson, Edie Adams, Margaret Leighton, Shelley Berman
Keywords: presidential election, politics, election campaign

Overview: The other party is in disarray. Five men vie for the party nomination for president. No one has a majority as the first ballot closes and the front-runners begin to decide how badly they want the job.
