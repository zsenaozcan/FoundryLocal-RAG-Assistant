Title: Kannathil Muthamittal (2002)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, War
Director: Mani Ratnam
Main Cast: Madhavan, Simran, P. S. Keerthana, Nandita Das, J. D. Chakravarthi
Keywords: civil war, refugee, war victim, sri lanka, suicide bomber, extremist, ltte

Overview: A little girl is told by her parents that she is adopted. Determined to find her birth mother, her family eventually agrees to take her to Sri Lanka, where they are abducted by the terrorist group known as the Tamil Tigers.
