Title: The Man Who Would Be King (1975)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: John Huston
Main Cast: Sean Connery, Michael Caine, Christopher Plummer, Saeed Jaffrey, Doghmi Larbi
Keywords: robbery, journalist, gold, cheating, treasure, coronation, con man, british army, british empire, adventure

Overview: A robust adventure about two British adventurers who take over primitive Kafiristan as "godlike" rulers, meeting a tragic end through their desire for a native girl. Based on a short story by Rudyard Kipling.
