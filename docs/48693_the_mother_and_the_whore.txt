Title: The Mother and the Whore (1973)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Jean Eustache
Main Cast: Bernadette Lafont, Jean-Pierre Léaud, Françoise Lebrun, Isabelle Weingarten
Keywords: love triangle, french, conflict, cafe

Overview: In this sexually frank French drama, the aimless young Alexandre  juggles his relationships with his girlfriend, Marie, and a casual lover named Veronika. The dialogue-heavy film focuses intently on the love triangle, with Marie increasingly jealous of Alexandre's fling with Veronika. As the trio continues their unsustainable affair, the emotional stakes get higher, leading to conflict and unhappiness.
