Title: Le Grand Restaurant (1966)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jacques Besnard
Main Cast: Louis de Funès, Bernard Blier, Maria-Rosa Rodriguez, Venantino Venantini, Juan Ramírez
Keywords: restaurant

Overview: A great French restaurant's owner, Monsieur Septime (Louis de Funès), is thrust into intrigue and crime, when one of his famous guests disappears.
