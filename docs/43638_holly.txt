Title: Holly (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Guy Moshe
Main Cast: Ron Livingston, Chris Penn, Thuy Nguyen, Virginie Ledoyen, Udo Kier
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Shot on location in Cambodia, including many scenes in actual brothels in the notorious red light district of Phnom Penh, HOLLY is a captivating, touching and emotional experience. Patrick, an American card shark and dealer of stolen artifacts, has been 'comfortably numb' in Cambodia for years, when he encounters Holly, a 12-year-old Vietnamese girl, in the K11 red light village. The girl has been sold by her impoverished family and smuggled across the border to work as a prostitute.
