Title: In a Glass Cage (1987)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: Agustí Villaronga
Main Cast: Günter Meisner, David Sust, Marisa Paredes, Gisèle Echevarría, Imma Colomer
Keywords: nurse, war crimes, darkness, insanity, iron lung, nazi

Overview: A Nazi doctor who had a fetish for young boys feels guilt after torturing and murdering his latest victim, and throws himself off a roof. Some years later, the doctor, confined to an iron lung, accepts as his nurse a boy who secretly witnessed that killing.
