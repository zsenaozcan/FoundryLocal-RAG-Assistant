Title: The Blue Light (1932)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Mystery
Director: Leni Riefenstahl
Main Cast: Leni Riefenstahl, Mathias Wieman, Beni Führer, Max Holzboer, Martha Mair
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A young woman, Junta (Riefenstahl), lives apart from her village and, for her solitude and strangeness, is considered to be a witch; when she comes to the village for one reason or another, the townsfolk chase her away. They feel that she may in some way be responsible for the deaths of several young men of the village, who have felt compelled, one by one, to climb the local mountain - and fall to their deaths - on nights when the moon is full.
