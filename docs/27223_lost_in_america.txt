Title: Lost in America (1985)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Albert Brooks
Main Cast: Albert Brooks, Julie Hagerty, Michael Greene, Garry Marshall, Maggie Roswell
Keywords: gambling, yuppie, roulette, satire, winnebago, new york city, las vegas, fired from the job, recreational vehicle, road movie

Overview: David and Linda Howard are successful yuppies from LA. When he gets a job disappointment, David convinces Linda that they should quit their jobs, liquidate their assets, and emulate the movie Easy Rider, spending the rest of their lives traveling around America...in a Winnebago.
