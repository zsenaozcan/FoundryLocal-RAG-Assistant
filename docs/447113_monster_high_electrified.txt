Title: Monster High: Electrified (2017)
Genres: Family, Animation
Director: Avgousta Zourelidi
Main Cast: Katy Townsend, Debi Derryberry, Cassandra Morris, Michael Sorich, Cristina Milizia
Keywords: monster high, draculaura, mattel

Overview: When Clawdeen dreams of opening a salon made for monsters and normies, Frankie has the perfect place - an abandoned power station outside town. But the idea sparks negative voltage when Moanica plots to ruin the whole thing, and replace the celebration with something a little more... shocking!
