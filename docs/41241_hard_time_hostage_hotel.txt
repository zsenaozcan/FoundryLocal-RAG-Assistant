Title: Hard Time: Hostage Hotel (1999)
Genres: TV Movie, Action, Thriller
Director: Hal Needham
Main Cast: Burt Reynolds, Charles Durning, Keith Carradine, Michael Buie, David Rasche

Overview: A California congressman's wife and daughter are abducted and held hostage in a historic hotel and Logan McQueen (Reynolds) is rushed to the scene to assist his former partner, Charlie Duffy (Durning), who has been taken captive trying to broker the ransom deal. McQueen must risk his life to face the cunning killer (Carradine) and save the hostages and his long-time friend.
