Title: Bonjour Tristesse (1958)
Genres: Drama
Director: Otto Preminger
Main Cast: Jean Seberg, Deborah Kerr, David Niven, Mylène Demongeot, Geoffrey Horne
Keywords: holiday, based on novel, lover (female), mediterranean, daughter, french riviera

Overview: Cecile, decadent young girl who lives with her rich playboy father Raymond. When Anne, Raymond's old love interest, comes to Raymond's villa, Cecile is afraid for her way of life.
