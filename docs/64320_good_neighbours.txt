Title: Good Neighbours (2011)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Jacob Tierney
Main Cast: Jay Baruchel, Scott Speedman, Emily Hampshire, Gary Farmer, Xavier Dolan

Overview: Takes place in 1995, the year of the second referendum on the separation of Quebec. In the dead of winter, a serial killer is on the loose in the small Montreal neighborhood of Notre Dame de Grace. The tenants of an old apartment house must figure out who they can trust and who they can't.
