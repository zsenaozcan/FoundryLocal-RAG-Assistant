Title: Our Folks (1967)
Genres: Comedy, Foreign
Director: Sylwester Checinski
Main Cast: Wacław Kowalski, Zdzislaw Karczewski, Eliasz Kuziemski, Ilona Kusmierska, Jerzy Janeczek
Keywords: neighbor, conflict

Overview: Two quarreling peasant families, who were forced to leave their lands after the war, are settled by accident on two neighbouring farms.
