Title: Charro! (1969)
Genres: Drama, Action, Western
Director: Charles Marquis Warren
Main Cast: Elvis Presley, Ina Balin, Victor French, Barbara Werle, Solomon Sturges
Keywords: cannon, gunfight, mexican

Overview: Jess Wade is innocently accused of having stolen a cannon from the Mexican revolutionary forces. He tries to find the real culprits, a gang of criminals.
