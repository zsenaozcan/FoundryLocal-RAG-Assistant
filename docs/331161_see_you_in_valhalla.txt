Title: See You In Valhalla (2015)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Jarret Tarnol
Main Cast: Sarah Hyland, Steve Howey, Odeya Rush, Jake McDorman, Beau Mirchoff

Overview: After the bizarre death of her brother, Johana Burwood must return home after four years, to face her strange siblings, her out of touch father and her very touchy past.
