Title: Bananas!* (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Fredrik Gertten
Main Cast: Byron Rosales Romero, Juan J. Dominguez, Duane Miller, Rick McKnight, David Delorenzo
Keywords: pesticide, banana plantation, david vs. goliath

Overview: Juan “Accidentes” Dominguez is on his biggest case ever. On behalf of twelve Nicaraguan banana workers he is tackling Dole Food in a ground-breaking legal battle for their use of a banned pesticide that was known by the company to cause sterility. Can he beat the giant, or will the corporation get away with it?
