Title: A Pistol for Ringo (1965)
Genres: Western, Action, Drama
Director: Duccio Tessari
Main Cast: Giuliano Gemma, Fernando Sancho, Lorella De Luca, Nieves Navarro, Antonio Casas
Keywords: hostage, ranch, violence, mexican, mexican border, hopscotch

Overview: Giuliano Gemma stars as a cool, cocky mercenary enlisted by a sheriff to infiltrate a ruthless gang of outlaws. Ennio Morricone provided the score to this early spaghetti western hit.
