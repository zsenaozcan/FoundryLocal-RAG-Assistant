Title: Mister Blot's Academy (1984)
Genres: Fantasy, Family
Director: Krzysztof Gradowski
Main Cast: Piotr Fronczewski, Slavek Wronka, Leon Niemczyk, Wiesław Michnikowski, Bronisław Pawlik
Keywords: magic, wizardry, school

Overview: Thanks to the powers of magic, Adaś Niezgódka is transported to the fairy tale land of Mr. Blot – the protagonist of Jan Brzechwa’s classic book. Mr. Blot is a teacher and a magician in one. His lessons cannot be boring. Especially when bIotology is the subject. Adaś learns about the miracles of the enchanted land, but also gets to know its dark secrets.
