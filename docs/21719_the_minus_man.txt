Title: The Minus Man (1999)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Hampton Fancher
Main Cast: Owen Wilson, Brian Cox, Sheryl Crow, Dwight Yoakam, Dennis Haysbert

Overview: The Minus Man is a 1999 film based on the novel by Lew McCreary. It was directed by Hampton Fancher, who also wrote the screenplay. The film centers on a psychotic killer whom Fancher describes as "a cross between Psycho's Norman Bates, Melville's Billy Budd and Being There's Chauncey Gardner"
