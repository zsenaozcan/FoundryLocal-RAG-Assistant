Title: Shadowheart (2009)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Romance, Western
Director: Dean Alioto
Main Cast: Justin Ament, William Sadler, Daniel Baldwin, Ines Dali

Overview: James Conners comes home to the town of Legend, New Mexico in 1865 to avenge his fathers death against the ruthless Will Tunney. Along the way he re-captures his long lost love Mary Cooper. With the chance to take Will Tunney he must choose revenge or redemption.
