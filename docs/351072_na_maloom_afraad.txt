Title: Na Maloom Afraad (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Javed Sheikh, Fahad Mustafa, Urwa Hocane, Mohsan Abbas Haider, Kubra Khan

Overview: Na Maloom Afraad is a story of three reckless poor struggling souls, running after their individual ambitions and desires, brought together by one incident which makes their not so simple life into a thrilling roller coaster ride of numerous ironic twist &amp; turns. The three characters run around in the chaotic city of Karachi bringing out the craziest plan to save their love, life and asses.
