Title: The War Boys (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ron Daniels
Main Cast: Benjamin Walker, Peter Gallagher, Brian J. Smith, Victor Rasuk, Greg Serano
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Three young vigilantes huddle on la linea ready to chase illegals back across the border into Mexico... but they soon learn that there are borderlines deep within each one of them that each of them has to cross.
