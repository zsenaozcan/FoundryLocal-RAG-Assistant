Title: Underworld (1927)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Josef von Sternberg
Main Cast: George Bancroft, Evelyn Brent, Clive Brook, Fred Kohler, Helen Lynch
Keywords: death sentence, revenge, organized crime, alcoholic, silent film, jail break

Overview: Boisterous gangster kingpin Bull Weed rehabilitates his former lawyer from his alcoholic haze, but complications arise when he falls for Weed's girlfriend.
