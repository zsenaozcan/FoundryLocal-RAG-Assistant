Title: Days of Grace (2011)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Everardo Gout
Main Cast: Tenoch Huerta, Carlos Bardem, José Alberto Solorzano, Mario Zaragoza, Sonia Couoh

Overview: Mexico City. 2002, 2006, 2010. A cop. A hostage. A wife. Corruption, violence, vengeance. Three destinies, during 30 days, during three Soccer World Cups. Three ways to fight in order to survive.
