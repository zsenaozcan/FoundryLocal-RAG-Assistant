Title: Godsend (2004)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Nick Hamm
Main Cast: Greg Kinnear, Rebecca Romijn, Robert De Niro, Cameron Bright, Zoie Palmer
Keywords: schizophrenia, clone, loss of son, nightmare, doctor

Overview: A couple agree to have their deceased son cloned under the supervision of an enigmatic doctor, but bizarre things start to happen years after his rebirth.
