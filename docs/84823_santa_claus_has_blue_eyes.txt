Title: Santa Claus Has Blue Eyes (1966)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jean Eustache
Main Cast: Jean-Pierre Léaud, Gérard Zimmermann, Henri Martinez, René Gilson, Michèle Maynard

Overview: Daniel needs some money to buy a duffle coat that is in fashion, so he agrees to work for a photographer by dressing up as Santa Claus. He discovers that it is much easier to meet girls when he is in his costume.
