Title: Oddball (2015)
Genres: Family, Adventure
Director: Stuart McDonald
Main Cast: Alan Tudyk, Sarah Snook, Coco Jack Gillies, Shane Jacobson, Deborah Mailman

Overview: The story of Allan "Swampy" Marsh and his Maremma dog Oddball. Swampy convinced local authorities to allow Oddball to protect a colony of Little Penguins on Middle Island from wild cats and dogs. Oddball succeeds in his campaign and becomes a front runner for Maremma dogs being used to protect other endangered Australian wildlife.
