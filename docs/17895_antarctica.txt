Title: Antarctica (1983)
Genres: Adventure
Director: Koreyoshi Kurahara
Main Cast: Ken Takakura, Eiji Okada, Masako Natsume, Keiko Oginome, Takeshi Kusaka
Keywords: expedition, based on true story, antarctic

Overview: Two Japanese scientists, Ushioda and Ochi, develop a bond with their sled dogs while on an expedition in Antarctica.
