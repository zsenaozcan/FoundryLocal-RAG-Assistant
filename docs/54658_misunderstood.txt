Title: Misunderstood (1984)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jerry Schatzberg
Main Cast: Gene Hackman, Henry Thomas, Rip Torn, Huckleberry Fox, Maureen Kerwin
Keywords: father son relationship, family drama

Overview: A workaholic father becomes sole parent to his 2 young sons, and finds it hard to relate to them, consumed as he is with his own grief.
