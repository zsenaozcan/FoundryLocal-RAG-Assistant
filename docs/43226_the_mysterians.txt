Title: The Mysterians (1957)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Ishirô Honda
Main Cast: Kenji Sahara, Yumi Shirakawa, Akihiko Hirata, Takashi Shimura, Momoko Kôchi
Keywords: spaceman, flying saucer, alien, giant robot, outer space, tokusatsu, alien invasion, kaiju, ray gun

Overview: In Japan, scientifically advanced invaders from the war-destroyed planet Mysteroid cause an entire village to vanish, then send a giant robot out to storm the city by night, after which they request a small patch of land on Earth and the right to marry earthling women, claiming to be pacifists.  Mankind must decide whether to capitulate or to resist.
