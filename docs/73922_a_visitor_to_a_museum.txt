Title: A Visitor to a Museum (1989)
Genres: Drama
Director: Konstantin Lopushanskiy
Main Cast: Viktor Mikhaylov, Vera Mayorova, Vadim Lobanov, Irina Rakshina, Aleksandr Rasinsky
Keywords: post-apocalyptic

Overview: It is the future, the world is in ruins and a large portion of the population consists of deformed mutants living in reservations. In this world a man decides to spend his vacation visiting the ruins of a museum that is now buried under the sea.
