Title: My Prairie Home (2013)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Chelsea McMullan
Main Cast: Rae Spoon
Keywords: canada, folk music, prairie, transgender, woman director

Overview: A true Canadian iconoclast, acclaimed transgender country/electro-pop artist Rae Spoon revisits the stretches of rural Alberta that once constituted “home” and confronts memories of growing up queer in an abusive, evangelical household.
