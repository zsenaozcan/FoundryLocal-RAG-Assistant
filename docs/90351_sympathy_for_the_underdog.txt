Title: Sympathy for the Underdog (1971)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime, Foreign
Director: Kinji Fukasaku
Main Cast: Koji Tsuruta, Noboru Andô, Kenji Imai, Akiko Kudô, Kenjiro Morokado
Keywords: okinawa

Overview: A yakuza gang gets driven out of Yokohama by a big gang from Tokyo. They relocate to Okinawa to violently start over.
