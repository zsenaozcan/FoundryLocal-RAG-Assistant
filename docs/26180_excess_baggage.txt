Title: Excess Baggage (1997)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Romance
Director: Marco Brambilla
Main Cast: Alicia Silverstone, Benicio del Toro, Christopher Walken, Jack Thompson, Harry Connick Jr.
Keywords: trunk, attempted kidnapping

Overview: A rich brat (Alicia Silverstone) fakes her own kidnapping, but in the process ends up locked in the trunk of a car that gets stolen.
