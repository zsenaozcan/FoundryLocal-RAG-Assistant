Title: Girl Most Likely (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Shari Springer Berman
Main Cast: Kristen Wiig, Annette Bening, Matt Dillon, Darren Criss, Christopher Fitzgerald
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A failed New York playwright stages a suicide in an attempt to win back her ex, only to wind up in the custody of her gambling-addict mother.
