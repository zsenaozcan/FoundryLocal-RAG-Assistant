Title: Breaking In (1989)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Bill Forsyth
Main Cast: Burt Reynolds, Casey Siemaszko, Sheila Kelley, Lorraine Toussaint

Overview: Professional thief Ernie takes Mike on as an apprentice, but while Mike clearly has "larceny in his heart", it will take him a long time to get as good as Ernie.
