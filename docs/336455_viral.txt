Title: Viral (2016)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Henry Joost
Main Cast: Sofia Black-D'Elia, Analeigh Tipton, Travis Tope, Machine Gun Kelly, Michael Kelly
Keywords: sister, quarantine, outbreak, family, virus

Overview: Following the outbreak of a virus that wipes out the majority of the human population, a young woman documents her family's new life in quarantine and tries to protect her infected sister.
