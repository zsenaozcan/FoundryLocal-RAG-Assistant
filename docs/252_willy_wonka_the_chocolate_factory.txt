Title: Willy Wonka & the Chocolate Factory (1971)
Genres: Family, Fantasy
Director: Mel Stuart
Main Cast: Gene Wilder, Jack Albertson, Peter Ostrum, Roy Kinnear, Denise Nickerson
Keywords: chocolate, factory worker, based on novel, candy, tv addicted person, overweight child, grandfather grandson relationship, factory, musical, single

But does Wonka have an agenda hidden amid a world of Oompa Loompas and chocolate rivers?
