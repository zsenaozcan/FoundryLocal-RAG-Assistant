Title: Imagine I'm Beautiful (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Meredith Edwards
Main Cast: Natalie Knepp, Alexandra Turshen, Lucas Salvagno, Yury Yakor, Naomi McDougall Jones
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Hoping to leave behind the trauma of her mother's death, a young woman moves to New York City, where she becomes close to her similarly haunted roommate -- until a disturbing revelation changes everything.
