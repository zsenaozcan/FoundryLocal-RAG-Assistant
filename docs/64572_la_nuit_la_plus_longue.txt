Title: La nuit la plus longue (1965)
Genres: Unknown
Director: José Bénazéraf
Main Cast: Willy Braque, Yves Duffaut, Annie Josse, Virginie Solenn, Alain Tissier

Overview: A beautiful heiress is kidnapped and held hostage until her wealthy father comes up with a ransom. The heiress develops an attraction to one of her kidnappers, but another member of the gang tries to rape her, leading to a deadly triangle.
