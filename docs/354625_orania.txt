Title: Orania (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Tobias Lindner
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Orania is a remote village in the barren centre of South Africa, an "intentional community" where only white Afrikaans people live - a culturally homogeneous place in a multicultural country. What lies beneath this peculiar societal experiment? A feature-length documentary about cultural identity and the thin line between self-determination and discrimination.
