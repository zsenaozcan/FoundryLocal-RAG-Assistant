Title: Offspring (2009)
Genres: Horror
Director: Andrew van den Houten
Main Cast: Jessica Butler, Leigh Shannan, Holter Graham, Stephen Grey, Amy Hargreaves
Keywords: terror, gore

Overview: Survivors of a feral flesh-eating clan are chowing their way through the locals. Amy Halbard and Claire Carey strive to survive their abduction by the cannibals and save their children. A subplot involving Claire's despicable husband, Steven, gives an opportunity to cleverly compare predatory civilized folk to the appetite-driven primitives.
