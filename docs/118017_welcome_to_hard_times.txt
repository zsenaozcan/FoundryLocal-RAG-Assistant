Title: Welcome to Hard Times (1967)
Genres: Western
Director: Burt Kennedy
Main Cast: Henry Fonda, Janice Rule, Keenan Wynn, Janis Paige, Warren Oates
Keywords: rape, saloon, mayor

Overview: A sociopathic stranger all but destroys a small hardscrabble town but the 'mayor' convinces its survivors to stay and rebuild.
