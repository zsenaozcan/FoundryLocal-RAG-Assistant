Title: Lady in White (1962)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Arne Mattsson
Main Cast: Nils Asther, Anita Björk, Jan Malmsjö, Karl-Arne Holmsten, Sif Ruud
Keywords: detective, murder, ghost

Overview: A ghost called Vita Frun (White Lady) is accused of several murders. Detective Hillman is contacted to resolve the case.
