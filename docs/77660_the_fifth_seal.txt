Title: The Fifth Seal (1976)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Zoltán Fábri
Main Cast: Őze Lajos, László Márkus, Ferenc Bencze, Sándor Horváth, István Dégi

Overview: In Budapest in 1944, a watchmaker, a book seller and a carpenter are drinking in a bar with the owner. They are joined by a stranger who asks a hypothetical question that will change their lives.
