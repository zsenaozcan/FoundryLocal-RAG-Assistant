Title: My Last Five Girlfriends (2009)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Julian Kemp
Main Cast: Brendan Patricks, Naomie Harris, Kelly Adams, Cécile Cassel
Keywords: independent film

Overview: My Last Five Girlfriends traces the romantic journey of thirtysomething bachelor Duncan. As the film opens, the despondent Duncan is apparently preparing to take his own life, but not before taking the time to send a message to the last five women he dated. Taking the form of a whirlwind tour through Duncan World, the film is a comedic exploration of his failed relationships.
