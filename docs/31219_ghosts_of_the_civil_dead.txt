Title: Ghosts... of the Civil Dead (1989)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: John Hillcoat
Main Cast: David Field, Mike Bishop, Chris DeRose, Kevin Mackey, Dave Mason

Overview: The inmates and guards of a modern, clean and efficient maximum security wing are slowly and increasingly brutalized until they erupt in violence. Dark and macabre, and based in truth, the story is told in a traditional dramatic style combined with telephone interviews and narration.
