Title: Gabi on the Roof in July (2010)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lawrence Michael Levine
Main Cast: Sophia Takal, Lawrence Michael Levine, Brooke Bloom, Louis Cancelmi, Amy Seimetz

Overview: An ambitious painter on the verge of a big break confronts his moral decay when his idealist, hell-raising, younger sister comes to stay with him in New York City for the summer.
