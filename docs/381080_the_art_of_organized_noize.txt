Title: The Art of Organized Noize (2016)
Genres: Music, Documentary
Director: Quincy Jones III
Main Cast: André Benjamin, Ludacris, Sean Combs, Cee Lo Green, Big Boi

Overview: Organized Noize shaped the landscape of Hip Hop music with a distinct sound created in the confines of a dungeon. They're responsible for the careers of Outkast, CeeLo, Goodie Mob, and the Dungeon Family. This is the story of the Art of Organized.
