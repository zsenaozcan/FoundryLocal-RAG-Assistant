Title: The Final Master (2015)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Xu Haofeng
Main Cast: Liao Fan, Song Jia, Song Yang, Jiang Wenli, Chin Shih-Chieh

Overview: Determined to pass down his art, the Final Master of Wing Chun is caught in a power struggle with malicious local officials and ultimately must choose between personal honor and his master’s dying wish.
