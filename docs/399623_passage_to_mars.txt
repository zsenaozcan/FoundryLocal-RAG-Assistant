Title: Passage to Mars (2016)
Genres: Adventure, Documentary, Science Fiction
Director: Jean-Christophe Jeauffre
Main Cast: Zachary Quinto, Charlotte Rampling, Pascal Lee, Buzz Aldrin, Jean-Christophe Jeauffre
Keywords: nasa, expedition, scientific expedition

Overview: A NASA Arctic expedition designed to be the first Martian road trip on Earth becomes an epic two-year odyssey of human adventure and survival.
