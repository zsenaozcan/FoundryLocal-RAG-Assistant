Title: Blacker than the Night (1975)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Horror, Fantasy
Director: Carlos Enrique Taboada
Main Cast: Claudia Islas, Susana Dosamantes, Helena Rojo, Lucía Méndez, Alicia Palacios
Keywords: terror, mexico, cinema, haunted house, film and cinema, horror, black cat

Overview: When four women move into an old house left by one woman's aunt, strange things begin to happen. Bizarre voices, visions of ghosts, and mysterious noises lead them to discover the darkest powers of evil and a horror and agony beyond terror.
