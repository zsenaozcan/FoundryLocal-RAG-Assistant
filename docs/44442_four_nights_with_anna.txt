Title: Four Nights with Anna (2008)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Foreign, Thriller
Director: Jerzy Skolimowski
Main Cast: Artur Steranko, Kinga Preis, Jerzy Fedorowicz, Redbad Klijnstra, Jakub Snochowski

Overview: A crematorium worker repeatedly breaks into a woman's house at night to help with housework.
