Title: Familiar Strangers (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Zackary Adler
Main Cast: Shawn Hatosy, DJ Qualls, Tom Bower, Nikki Reed, Ann Dowd
Keywords: independent film

Overview: "Familiar Strangers" asks the question. "Is it really possible to relate to ones parents and siblings after being replaced by the family dog? . . . Perhaps as friends - weird friends?" An off beat - funny - heartfelt story of family negotiating the changing relationships between parents and children, especially as those children grow into adulthood.
