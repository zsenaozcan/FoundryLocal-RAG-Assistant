Title: Eden Log (2007)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Franck Vestiel
Main Cast: Clovis Cornillac, Vimala Pons, Zohar Wexler, Sifan Shao, Arben Bajraktaraj
Keywords: amnesia, cave, plant, independent film

Overview: A man wakes up deep inside a cave. Suffering amnesia, he has no recollection of how he came to be here or of what happened to the man whose body he finds beside him. Tailed by a mysterious creature, he must continue through this strange and fantastic world. Enclosed, Tolbiac has no other option to reach the surface than to use REZO ZERO, secret observing cells in this cemetery-like abandoned mine.
