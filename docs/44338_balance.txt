Title: Balance (1989)
Genres: Drama, Animation
Director: Christoph Lauenstein
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: teamwork, greed, surrealism, stop motion, political, selfishness, balance, fishing pole, short

Overview: The setting is on a floating platform where a group of evenly and carefully placed men live. Each man is aware that the platform is not stable and in order not to fall to their deaths, they maintain a careful balance of weight to prevent the platform from tipping too far and cause them all to fall.
