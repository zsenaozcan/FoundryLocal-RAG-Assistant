Title: Train of Shadows (1997)
Genres: Drama
Director: José Luis Guerin
Main Cast: Jessica Andrieu, Anne Céline Auche, Juliette Gautier, Marc Montserrat, Ivon Orvain
Keywords: homage, 1920s, silent cinema

Overview: A silent homage to the origins of cinema, recreating the apparent disappearance of a French photographer in the 1920s. Experimental.
