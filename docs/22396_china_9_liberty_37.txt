Title: China 9, Liberty 37 (1978)
Genres: Action, Western, Foreign
Director: Monte Hellman
Main Cast: Fabio Testi, Warren Oates, Jenny Agutter, Sam Peckinpah, Isabel Mestres
Keywords: violence, spaghetti western

Overview: Gunslinger Clayton Drumm (Testi) is about to be hanged when he is given a chance to live if he will agree to murder Matthew (Oates), a miner who has steadfastly refused to sell his land to the railroad company. Matthew’s refusal is a major obstacle to the railroad’s plans for expansion.
