Title: British Intelligence (1940)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Terry O. Morse
Main Cast: Boris Karloff, Margaret Lindsay, Bruce Lester, Leonard Mudie, Holmes Herbert
Keywords: london england, german spy

Overview: During WWI pretty German master spy Helene von Lorbeer is sent undercover to London to live with the family of a high-placed British official where she is to rendezvous with the butler Valdar, also a spy, and help him transmit secret war plans back to Germany.
