Title: A Covenant with Death (1967)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lamont Johnson
Main Cast: George Maharis, Earl Holliman, Laura Devon, Katy Jurado, Gene Hackman
Keywords: judge

Overview: An innocent man is found guilty of murder and sentenced to death, but as he's about to be hanged he accidentally kills his executioner. He now faces a new trial, presided over by a young and inexperienced judge.
