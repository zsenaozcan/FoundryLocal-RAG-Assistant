Title: The Reconquest (2016)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Jonás Trueba
Main Cast: Francesco Carril, Itsaso Arana, Aura Garrido, Pablo Hoyos, Candela Recio

Overview: Manuela and Olmo meet in a future as they had promised fifteen years earlier as teenagers, when they lived their first love. Based on this romantic situation, La Reconquista is in fact a film on a quest for time; or about the awareness of time: of time lost and time recovered; about what we remember about ourselves and what we don't remember; about the words, gestures and feelings to which we remain faithful, because they define and compel us today, yesterday and tomorrow.
