Title: Live and Become (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Radu Mihaileanu
Main Cast: Moshe Abebe, Moshe Agazai, Sirak M. Sabahat, Roni Hadar, Yaël Abecassis
Keywords: paris, underdog, post traumatic stress  disorder, father son relationship, loss of mother, refugee camp, medicine, immigration, love of one's life, adoption

Overview: In 1980 the black Falashas in Ethiopia are recognised as genuine Jews and are secretly carried to Israel. The day before the transport the son of a Jewish mother dies. In his place and with his name (Schlomo) she takes a Christian 9-year-old boy.
