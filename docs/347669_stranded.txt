Title: Stranded (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: José Corbacho
Main Cast: Lola Dueñas, Carlos Areces, Núria Gago, Miki Esparbé, Rossy de Palma

Overview: The last train travel of the year from Barcelona to Madrid, suddenly stops by a voltage drop in the middle of nowhere during a fierce snowstorm. Because of the peculiarity of its passengers, will begin to emerge hilarious conflicts between them.
