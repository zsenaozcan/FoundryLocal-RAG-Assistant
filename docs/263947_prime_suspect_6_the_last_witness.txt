Title: Prime Suspect 6: The Last Witness (2004)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller, TV Movie
Director: Tom Hooper
Main Cast: Helen Mirren, Oleg Menshikov, Ben Miles, Robert Pugh, Clare Holman

Overview: Detective Superintendent Jane Tennison's investigation of the murder of a Bosnian refugee leads her to one, or possibly two, Serbian war criminals determined to silence the last witness to a massacre a decade before.
