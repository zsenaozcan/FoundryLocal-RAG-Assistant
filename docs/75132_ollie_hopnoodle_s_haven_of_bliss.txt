Title: Ollie Hopnoodle's Haven of Bliss (1988)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Dick Bartlett
Main Cast: Jerry O'Connell, James Sikking, Dorothy Lyman, Jean Shepherd

Overview: From Jean Shepherd (A Christmas Story) comes the story of 14 year old Ralphie's first job, and the family vacation the family is planning to a rustic fishing cabin on Lake Michigan. Originally made for the Disney Channel, this film is a delightful family comedy.
