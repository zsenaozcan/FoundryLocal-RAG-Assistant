Title: The Mangler (1995)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Tobe Hooper
Main Cast: Robert Englund, Ted Levine, Jeremy Crutchley, Daniel Matmor, Vanessa Pike
Keywords: crushed, living machine

Overview: When an accident involving a folding machine at an old laundry happens, detective John Hunton investigates. While he tries to solve the mystery, Bill Gartley, the owner, wants to find new victims for his machine.
