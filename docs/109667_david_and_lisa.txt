Title: David and Lisa (1962)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Frank Perry
Main Cast: Keir Dullea, Janet Margolin, Howard Da Silva, Neva Patterson, Clifton James
Keywords: schizophrenia, independent film, mental illness

Overview: The emotional story of a young man in a mental institution for teens who begins to understand his psychosis in the environment of others with mental and emotional problems. He finds intimacy with Lisa, a young woman suffering from schizophrenia.
