Title: The Lovers (1994)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Family, Fantasy
Director: Tsui Hark
Main Cast: Charlie Yeung, Nicky Wu, Carrie Ng, Elvis Tsui, Lau Shun
Keywords: lovers, ancient china, 1st century

Overview: In 3A.D., during the Eastern Jin Dynasty, parents dress a very pretty, very privileged girl like a boy so she may be educated in a local boarding school. There, she falls in love with a poor, but handsome and industrious young man, but their short love affair ends in disaster. Written by
