Title: Deadfall (1993)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Christopher Coppola
Main Cast: Michael Biehn, Sarah Trigger, Nicolas Cage, James Coburn, Peter Fonda
Keywords: murder, dying wish, twins, danger, neo-noir

Overview: After he accidentally kills his father, Mike, during a sting, Joe tries to carry out Mike's dying wish by recovering valuables that Mike's twin brother Lou stole from him years earlier. But Uncle Lou is also a confidence artist, and Joe is soon drawn into his increasingly dangerous schemes.
