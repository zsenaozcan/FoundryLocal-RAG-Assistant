Title: Louis Theroux: Extreme Love - Autism (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jamie Pickup
Main Cast: Louis Theroux
Keywords: autism

Overview: Louis travels to one of the most innovative autism schools of its kind – the DLC Warren in New Jersey – opened in 2007 at a cost of $54 million. There he finds out how specialised intervention can help both the children and the families that care for them.
