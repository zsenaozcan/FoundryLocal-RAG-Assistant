Title: The Coalition (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Monica Mingo
Main Cast: Denyce Lawton, Eddie Goines, Adrienne Bailon, J.R. Ramirez, Ingrid Clay
Keywords: battle of the sexes, woman director, feel bad comedy, idle rich

Overview: A group of women form an unlikely alliance to get their revenge on a pro athlete and his three friends who played them. The guys are used to getting what they want when they want it, but that's all about to change.
