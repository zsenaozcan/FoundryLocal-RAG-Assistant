Title: The Butterfly Tattoo (2009)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Phil Hawkins
Main Cast: Duncan Stuart, Jessica Blake, Aidan Magrath, Christopher Dane, Dan Morgan
Keywords: british crime

Overview: "Chris Marshall met the girl he was going to kill on a warm night in early June, when one of the colleges in Oxford was holding its summer ball." A chance meeting with Jenny at an Oxford party leaves seventeen-year-old Chris with hope for a summer romance - and no premonition of trouble.
