Title: Cruel Story of Youth (1960)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Nagisa Ōshima
Main Cast: Yûsuke Kawazu, Miyuki Kuwano, Yoshiko Kuga, Fumio Watanabe, Shinji Tanaka
Keywords: japanese new wave

Overview: A budding gangster enthralls a freeloading young woman, soon taking advantage of her knack for hitch-hiking to rob middle-class, middle-aged men.
