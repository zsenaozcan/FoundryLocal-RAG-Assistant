Title: The Pack (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Nick Robertson
Main Cast: Anna Lise Phillips, Jack Campbell, Katie Moore, Hamish Phillips, Kieran Thomas McNamara
Keywords: survival, dog, isolated farmhouse, australian outback

Overview: Man’s best friend becomes his worst nightmare when a horde of bloodthirsty wild dogs descends upon a family’s farmhouse in a fang-bearing fight for survival.
