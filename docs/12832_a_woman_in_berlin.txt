Title: A Woman in Berlin (2008)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Max Färberböck
Main Cast: Nina Hoss, Evgeniy Sidikhin, Juliane Köhler, August Diehl, Irm Hermann
Keywords: berlin, rape, world war ii, love of one's life, strong woman, soviet troops, war, secret love

Overview: A nameless woman keeps a diary as the Russians invade Berlin in the spring of 1945. She is in her early 30s, a patriotic journalist with international credentials; her husband, Gerd, a writer, is an officer at the Russian front. She speaks Russian and, for a day or two after the invasion, keeps herself safe, but then the rapes begin. She resolves to control her fate
