Title: Jack the Giant Killer (2013)
Genres: Action, Fantasy
Director: Mark Atkins
Main Cast: Ben Cross, Jane March, Jamie Atkins, Vicki Glover, Tanya Winsor
Keywords: fairy tale, fantasy world, giant

Overview: A giant beanstalk brings Jack to a land in the clouds filled with snarling, evil beasts. When the creatures make their way to the ground, Jack must figure out how to get back down before they destroy earth and everyone in it.
