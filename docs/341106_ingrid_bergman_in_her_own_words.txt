Title: Ingrid Bergman: In Her Own Words (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Stig Björkman
Main Cast: Alicia Vikander, Isabella Rossellini, Roberto Rossellini, Isotta Rossellini, Fiorella Mariani
Keywords: biography, ingrid bergman
