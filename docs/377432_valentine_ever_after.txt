Title: Valentine Ever After (2016)
Genres: Romance, TV Movie
Director: Don McBrearty
Main Cast: Autumn Reeser, Vanessa Matsui, Eric Johnson, Damon Runyan, Ron Lea

Overview: Big city girls Julia and her best friend Sydney take a trip to a dude ranch in Wyoming for a fun weekend getaway, but after a brawl at the local bar, the girls are sentenced to stay in town and perform community service. Along the way, Julia finds love and her true calling in life.
