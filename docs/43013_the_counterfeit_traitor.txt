Title: The Counterfeit Traitor (1962)
Genres: War, Thriller, Drama
Director: George Seaton
Main Cast: William Holden, Lilli Palmer, Hugh Griffith, Carl Raddatz, Ernst Schröder
Keywords: spy, sweden, world war ii

Overview: Blacklisted in modern day WW2, a Swedish oil trader opts to assist British Allies, by means of infiltrating and surveying Nazi Germany.
