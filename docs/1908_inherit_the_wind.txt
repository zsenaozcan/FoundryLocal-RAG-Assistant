Title: Inherit the Wind (1960)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Stanley Kramer
Main Cast: Spencer Tracy, Fredric March, Gene Kelly, Dick York, Donna Anderson
Keywords: politician, teacher, lawyer, trial, courtroom, evolution, science, telephone call  , rhetoric, scopes monkey trial

Overview: This gripping adaptation of the Jerome Lawrence-Robert E. Lee play examines an issue that still causes great controversy: the role religion should play in the schools.
