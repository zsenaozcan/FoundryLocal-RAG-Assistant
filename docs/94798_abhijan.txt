Title: Abhijan (1962)
Genres: Drama
Director: Satyajit Ray
Main Cast: Waheeda Rehman, Soumitra Chatterjee, Ruma Guha Thakurta, Gyanesh Mukherjee, Charuprakash Ghosh
Keywords: taxi driver

Overview: The story revolves around a taxi driver, Narsingh, who attempts to reinvent his life by visiting his native place, but instead gets embroiled in a local Marwari businessman's smuggling and human trafficking business.
