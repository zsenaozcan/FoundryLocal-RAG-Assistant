Title: Downhill Racer (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Ritchie
Main Cast: Sylvester Stallone, Robert Redford, Gene Hackman, Camilla Sparv, Karl Michael Vogler
Keywords: sport, teammate, narcissism, injury, skiing

Overview: An ambitious young skier, determined to break all existing records, is contemptuous of the teamwork advocated by the US coach when they go to Europe for the Olympics.
