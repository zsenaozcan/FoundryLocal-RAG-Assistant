Title: Sandakan No. 8 (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kei Kumai
Main Cast: Komaki Kurihara, Yoko Takahashi, Kinuyo Tanaka, Ken Tanaka, Masayo Umezawa
Keywords: japan, exploitation, misery, forced prostitution

Overview: A young journalist interviews an elderly woman about being forced into prostitution in Borneo at a brothel called Sandakan No. 8.
