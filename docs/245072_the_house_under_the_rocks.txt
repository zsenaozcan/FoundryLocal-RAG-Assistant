Title: The House Under the Rocks (1959)
Genres: Drama
Director: Károly Makk
Main Cast: Görbe János, Irén Psota, Margit Bara, József Bihari

Overview: Three people – a returned prisoner of war, his beautiful second wife, and the possessive, hunchbacked spinster who is his sister-in-law by his first marriage – are isolated in a little house under an extinct volcano, where each strives for personal happiness but is suffocated by their dependence on the others.
