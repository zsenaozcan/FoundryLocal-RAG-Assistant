Title: From Beginning to End (2009)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Aluizio Abranches
Main Cast: João Gabriel Vasconcellos, Rafael Cardoso, Júlia Lemmertz, Fábio Assunção, Jean Pierre Noher
Keywords: brazilian, drama, romance, independent film, estranged

Overview: Two brothers develop a very close relationship as they are growing up in an idyllic and happy family. When they are young adults their relationship becomes very intimate, romantic, and sexual.
