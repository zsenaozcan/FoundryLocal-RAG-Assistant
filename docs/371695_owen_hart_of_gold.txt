Title: Owen Hart of Gold (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Owen Hart, Bret Hart, Brian James, Monty Sopp, Mark Henry
Keywords: wrestling

Overview: The youngest of 12 from the famous Hart Wrestling Family, and one of the most likeable personalities in sports entertainment, Owen Hart was a champion who won the hearts of fans around the world. For the first time ever, hear from his brothers, sisters, friends, and colleagues as they fondly look back on the life and career of one of sports entertainment's greatest WWE Superstars.
