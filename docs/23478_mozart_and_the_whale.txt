Title: Mozart and the Whale (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Petter Næss
Main Cast: Josh Hartnett, Radha Mitchell, Gary Cole, Sheila Kelley, Erica Leerhsen
Keywords: autism, taxi driver

Overview: A love story between two savants with Asperger's syndrome, a kind of autism, whose conditions sabotage their budding relationship.
