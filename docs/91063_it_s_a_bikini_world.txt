Title: It's a Bikini World (1967)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Stephanie Rothman
Main Cast: Deborah Walley, Tommy Kirk, Bobby Pickett, Suzie Kaye, Sid Haig
Keywords: photographer, bikini, harem, woman director, camel race

Overview: Male chauvinist Mike attempts to get an intelligent burgeoning feminist Delilah into his harem. When she resists, Mike then poses as his brother Herbert, but in the process falls in love with Delilah.
