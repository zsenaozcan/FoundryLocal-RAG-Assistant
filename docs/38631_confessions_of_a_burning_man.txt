Title: Confessions of a Burning Man (2003)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Paul Barnett
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: desert scientist, rave, carnival, party

Overview: The experience shared by four first-timers demonstrates how Burning Man dissolves the barriers between races, nationalities and economic classes. A beautiful piece of film-making which inspires and entertains as it provides some understanding about why people return year after year.
