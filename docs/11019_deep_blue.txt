Title: Deep Blue (2003)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Alastair Fothergill
Main Cast: Michael Gambon, Pierce Brosnan, Frank Glaubrecht, Jacques Perrin, Dalik Volonitz
Keywords: diving, sea, fish, atlantic ocean, deep sea, water, whale, shark

Overview: Deep Blue is a major documentary feature film shot by the BBC Natural History Unit. An epic cinematic rollercoaster ride for all ages, Deep Blue uses amazing footage to tell us the story of our oceans and the life they support.
