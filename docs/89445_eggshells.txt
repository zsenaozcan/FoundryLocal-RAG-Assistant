Title: Eggshells (1969)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama
Director: Tobe Hooper
Main Cast: Ron Barnhart, Pamela Craig, Allen Danziger, Sharon Danziger, Mahlon Foreman
Keywords: hippies

Overview: Experimental allegorical story about a group of hippie students in Austin, Texas, who move into an old big house in the woods. However, something else is there and it's influencing them.
