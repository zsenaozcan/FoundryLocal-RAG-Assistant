Title: Unconquered (1947)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, History, Western, Romance
Director: Cecil B. DeMille
Main Cast: Gary Cooper, Paulette Goddard, Howard Da Silva, Boris Karloff, Cecil Kellaway

Overview: Intrepid frontiersman Chris Holden foils the political and personal ambitions of renegade Martin Garth in the Ohio Valley following the French and Indian War.
