Title: Falling Leaves (1966)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Otar Iosseliani
Main Cast: Ramaz Giorgobiani, Gogi Kharabadze, Marina Kartsivadze, Aleksandre Omiadze, Baadur Tsuladze

Overview: A young idealist takes a job at a local state run winery only to discover and become disillusioned by the corruption of the Soviet State.
