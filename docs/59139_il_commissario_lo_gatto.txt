Title: Il commissario Lo Gatto (1987)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Dino Risi
Main Cast: Lino Banfi, Maurizio Ferrini, Maurizio Micheli, Isabel Russinova, Galeazzo Benti
Keywords: investigation, disappearance

Overview: Police commissioner Lo Gatto is in charge of the local Italian police station within Vatican State. During an investigation, following the murder of a Vatican priest, he decides to question the Pope!!! For this reason Lo Gatto is sent to a Sicilian remote island Favignana. There is very little for the commissioner to do on the quiet island, so Lo Gatto decides to investigate the vanishing of a tourist which quickly becomes a very complicated affair.
