Title: Janeane Garofalo: If I May (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jay Chapman
Main Cast: Janeane Garofalo
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Filmed live in San Francisco, Janeane Garofalo takes on society’s intolerance of gluten and House Hunters International. With her unapologetic attitude, Janeane proudly states her AARP membership, her love for Febreze but the disdain for their commercials, and her disinterest in doctors.
