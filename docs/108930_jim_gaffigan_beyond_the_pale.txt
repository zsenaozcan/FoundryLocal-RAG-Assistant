Title: Jim Gaffigan: Beyond the Pale (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michael Drumm
Main Cast: Jim Gaffigan
Keywords: stand-up comedy, clean comedy

Overview: The affable, towheaded comic demonstrates his hysterical brand of self-effacing comedy and deadpan delivery at two sold-out shows at Chicago's Vic Theater. It's OK to laugh at this pale white guy...'cause nobody's laughing at Jim Gaffigan harder than Jim Gaffigan!
