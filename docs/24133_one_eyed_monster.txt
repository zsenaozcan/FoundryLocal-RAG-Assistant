Title: One-Eyed Monster (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Adam Fields
Main Cast: Jason Graham, Charles Napier, Jeff Denton, Veronica Hart, Bart Fletcher

Overview: In February 2007, ten people went to the remote mountains of Northern California to shoot an adult movie. What happened next was something no one expected, but everyone saw coming. Amber Benson, Charles Napier, and Ron Jeremy star in an homage to "Alien", "The Thing", and porn.
