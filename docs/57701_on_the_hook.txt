Title: On the Hook! (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Natalya Uglitskikh
Main Cast: Ekaterina Vilkova, Konstantin Kryukov, Marat Basharov, Igor Ugolnikov, Svetlana Antonova

Overview: Rita, a figure skating coach, wants to prevent her school bankruptcy and closing by trying to push the famous millionaire to fall in love with her.
