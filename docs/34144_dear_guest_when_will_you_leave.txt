Title: Dear Guest, When Will You Leave? (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Foreign
Director: Ashwani Dhir
Main Cast: Ajay Devgn, Sanjay Mishra, Paresh Rawal, Konkona Sen Sharma, Mukesh Tiwari

Overview: Atithi Tum Kab Jaoge? is the story of Puneet and Munmun, a happily married couple living in Mumbai whose lives take an interesting turn when a distant relative, Chachaji turns up unannounced at their doorstep from a far off village. The guest overstays his welcome so much so that the exasperated couple come up with various ploys to hasten his departure to hilarious results.
