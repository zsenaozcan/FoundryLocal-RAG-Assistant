Title: Alles stroomt (2009)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Danyael Sugawara
Main Cast: Anneke Blok, Marnie Blok, Manuel Broekman, Lidewij Mahler, Ronald Top

Overview: A story on the trials and tribulations of a mother-and-son relationship, which goes downhill after son Damiaan passes his school exams and almost immediately moves into a squat, while his widowed mother Aleid falls for the charms of a skipper and moves in with him just as soon.
