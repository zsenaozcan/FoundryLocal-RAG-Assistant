Title: I am Von Höfler Variation on Werther (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Forgács Péter
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: How does the process of film-making—like a huge carpet, of a 250 years saga of the Hungarian von Höfler family—render the chosen subject topical, re-liveable? And what is the topical signifier? is it merely a fleeting (a momentary) décollage — which is neither a piece of news, nor official history?
