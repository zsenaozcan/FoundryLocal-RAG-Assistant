Title: Hey There, It's Yogi Bear (1964)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Family, Adventure
Director: Joseph Barbera
Main Cast: Daws Butler, Don Messick, Julie Bennett, Mel Blanc, Paul Frees
Keywords: bear, picnic baskets

Overview: Yogi Bear and his pal Boo Boo are shipped off to the San Diego Zoo by Jellystone National Park's Ranger Smith who is tired of Yogi's "pick-a-nick" basket stealing. Yogi escapes by convincing a bear named Cornpone to switch places with him and go to sunny California and returns to the park.
