Title: The Prize of Peril (1983)
Genres: Action, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Yves Boisset
Main Cast: Gérard Lanvin, Michel Piccoli, Marie-France Pisier, Bruno Cremer, Andréa Ferréol

Overview: In a futuristic society, contestants pit their survival skills against each other in a fight to the death for cash prizes, and the contest is aired live on television.
