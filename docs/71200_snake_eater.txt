Title: Snake Eater (1989)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: George Erschbamer
Main Cast: Lorenzo Lamas, Josie Bell, Ronnie Hawkins, Ben DiGregorio

Overview: The Snake Eaters are an elite division of the Marines especially trained for search and destroy missions. This actioner chronicles the exploits of one of them who has become a cop. Known as a tough loner, he returns to find the band of backwoods bad-guys who killed his parents and abducted his sister.
