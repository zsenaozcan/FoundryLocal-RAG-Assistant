Title: The Class (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ilmar Raag
Main Cast: Vallo Kirs, Pärt Uusberg, Lauri Pedaja, Paula Solvak, Mikk Mägi
Keywords: high school, violence in schools, based on true story

Overview: An average guy of an Estonian high-school decides to defend his bullied classmate. This starts war between him and the informal leader of the class. As teenagers' honour is a touchy thing, everything ends in bloodshed.
