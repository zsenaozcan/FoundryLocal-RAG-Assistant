Title: Asignatura pendiente (1977)
Genres: Drama
Director: José Luis Garci
Main Cast: José Sacristán, Fiorella Faltoyano, Antonio Gamero, Silvia Tortosa, Héctor Alterio

Overview: Joseph and Helen, who had been boyfriends, there meeting some years later and fall madly in love again, but now the two are married. His hard and clandestine love story addition is determined by the circumstances of the times in which they have lived: the last years of the Franco regime and the beginning of the transition.
