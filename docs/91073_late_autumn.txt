Title: Late Autumn (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kim Tae-yong
Main Cast: Hyun Bin, Tang Wei, Jun Kim, John Wu, Danni Lang

Overview: Anna learns in prison that her mother has passed away in Seattle. Prison officials grants Anna a three day furlough to attend her mother's funeral. Anna embarks on a long trip to Seattle. Hoon is a Korean immigrant who works as a gigolo. Hoon is now on the run from a wealthy client's furious husband. These two seemingly lost souls are about to share three memorable days together.
