Title: Monsieur Hire (1989)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Patrice Leconte
Main Cast: Michel Blanc, Sandrine Bonnaire, Luc Thuillier, André Wilms, Philippe Dormoy
Keywords: french, suspense, murder suspect

Overview: A French man spies on a lovely younger woman across the way. When he's spotted by the woman shortly after being questioned by the police about a local murder, the man simple life becomes more complicated.
