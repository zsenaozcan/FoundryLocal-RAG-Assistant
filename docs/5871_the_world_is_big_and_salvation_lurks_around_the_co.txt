Title: The World is Big and Salvation Lurks Around the Corner (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Stephan Komandarev
Main Cast: Carlo Ljubek, Predrag 'Miki' Manojlović, Hristo Mutafchiev, Ana Papadopulu, Lyudmila Cheshmedzhieva
Keywords: migration, backgammon

Overview: The story of Alex, who, with the help of his charismatic grandfather, embarks on a journey in search of his real self.
