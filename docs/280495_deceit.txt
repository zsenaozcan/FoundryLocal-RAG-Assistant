Title: Deceit (1992)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Albert Pyun
Main Cast: Samantha Phillips, Norbert Weisser, Scott Paulin, Diane Defoe, Christian Andrews
Keywords: blonde, alien

Overview: Two outer-space aliens visit Earth with the intention of blowing it up, but they meet a hot blonde and decide to postpone the planet's destruction in order to try to score with her.
