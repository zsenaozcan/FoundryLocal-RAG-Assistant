Title: Protektor (2009)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Marek Najbrt
Main Cast: Jana Plodková, Marek Daniel, Klára Melíšková, Sandra Nováková, Jan Budař
Keywords: radio, nudity, nazis, propaganda, jewish

Overview: A Czech journalist joins a Prague radio station what broadcasts Nazi propaganda in order to protect his Jewish wife. However, as the Nazi rule over Czechoslovakia calls for more and more collaboration, his relationship with his wife spirals downward.
