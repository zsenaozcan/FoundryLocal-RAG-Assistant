Title: The Blood on Satan's Claw (1971)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Piers Haggard
Main Cast: Patrick Wymark, Linda Hayden, Barry Andrews, James Hayter, Michele Dotrice
Keywords: england, rape, nudity, sacrifice, satanism, village, murder, gore, devil, witchcraft

Overview: The accidental unearthing of Satan’s earthly remains causes the children of 17th-century English village to slowly convert into a coven of devil worshipers.
