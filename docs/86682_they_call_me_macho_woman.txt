Title: They Call Me Macho Woman (1991)
Genres: Action
Director: Patrick G. Donahue
Main Cast: Debra Sweaney, Brian Oldfield, Sean P. Donahue, Mike Donahue, Jerry Johnson

Overview: A young widow on a trip to the backwoods stumbles upon the operation of a gang of drug smugglers. They attempt to kill her in order to keep their operation a secret, but she turns out to be more resourceful than they thought, and starts to turn the tables on them.
