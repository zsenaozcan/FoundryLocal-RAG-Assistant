Title: Complicit (2013)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Niall MacCormick
Main Cast: David Oyelowo, Stephen Campbell Moore, Sebastian Armesto, Monica Dolan, Paul Ritter
Keywords: terrorism, politcs

Overview: An MI5 officer's attempts to foil a possible terrorist plot are undermined by bureaucracy and moral dilemmas. Will he make the world a safer place or be complicit in making a tense situation even worse?
