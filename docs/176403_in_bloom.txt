Title: In Bloom (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nana Ekvtimishvili
Main Cast: Lika Babluani, Mariam Bokeria, Zurab Gogaladze, Data Zakareishvili, Giorgi Aladashvili
Keywords: woman director
