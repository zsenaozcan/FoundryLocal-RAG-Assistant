Title: They All Laughed (1981)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Thriller, Mystery, Romance
Director: Peter Bogdanovich
Main Cast: Audrey Hepburn, Ben Gazzara, Patti Hansen, John Ritter, Dorothy Stratten

Overview: A mad cap private-eye caper about a team of detectives who are following, and are being followed by, a group of beautiful women.
