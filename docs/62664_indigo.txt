Title: Indigo (2008)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Fantasy
Director: Roman Prygunov
Main Cast: Yuriy Kutsenko, Mikhail Efremov, Elena Drobysheva, Anastasiya Richi, Mariya Shukshina

Overview: This story takes place in Moscow during 2006. The film is about new generation of kids, called Indigo, who have more mental and spiritual abilities than others. A company of such kids gather together in hidden places. Each of them can do unbelievable things, such as : reading other people thoughts, communicating with animals, breaking computer codes. They are different from ordinary people and they are happy with each other. But some adults want to use their talents for old-time perverted purposes...
