Title: The Fiction Makers (1968)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Roy Ward Baker
Main Cast: Roger Moore, Caron Gardner, Sylvia Syms, Justine Lord, Kenneth J. Warren

Overview: Simon Templar is hired by a friend in the book publishing trade to protect one of his stars, a secretive recluse named Amos Klein who writes a popular (and lucrative) series of adventure novels about a manly and suave spy.
