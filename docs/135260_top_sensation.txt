Title: Top Sensation (1969)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Ottavio Alessi
Main Cast: Edwige Fenech, Rosalba Neri, Eva Thulin, Maud De Belleroche, Maurizio Bonuglia

Overview: Attempting to cure her mentally handicapped son's virginity, his mother and several friends embark on a cruise which strands them on nearly deserted island where lust and murder are on the menu!
