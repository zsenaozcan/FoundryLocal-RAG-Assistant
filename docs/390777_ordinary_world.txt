Title: Ordinary World (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Lee Kirk
Main Cast: Billie Joe Armstrong, Fred Armisen, Judy Greer, Selma Blair, Chris Messina

Overview: Perry is a happily married father of two living a comfortable but sedate life in the suburbs. On the occasion of his 40th birthday, he seeks to revisit his former life as the lead singer in a popular punk band though his middle-aged reality quickly (and hilariously) clashes with the indulgences of his youth.
