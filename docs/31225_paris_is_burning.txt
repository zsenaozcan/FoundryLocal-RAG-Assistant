Title: Paris is Burning (1990)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jennie Livingston
Main Cast: Dorian Corey, Pepper LaBeija, Willi Ninja, Octavia St. Laurent, Anji Xtravaganza
Keywords: transvestism, drag queen, homophobia, black, homosexuality, african american, new york city, transgender, gay man, transsexual

Overview: A chronicle of New York City's drag scene in the 1980s, focusing on balls, voguing and the ambitions and dreams of those who gave the era its warmth and vitality.
