Title: The Short Game (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Josh Greenbaum
Main Cast: Edd Hall, Jennifer Van Horn
Keywords: competition, north carolina, documentary, kids, sport competition

Overview: Each year, the world’s best 7 year-old golfers descend on Pinehurst, North Carolina to compete in the World Championships of Junior Golf. The Short Game follows eight of these very young athletes on their quest to become the sport’s next phenom.
