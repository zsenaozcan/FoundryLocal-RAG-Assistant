Title: The Battle for Marjah (2010)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Anthony Wonke
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Tells the story of Operation Moshtarak, the biggest operation since the start of the Afghanistan war, whose main target was the Taliban stronghold of Marjah. For two months, VBS correspondent Ben Anderson got to know the American forces behind Operation Moshtarak and filmed the brutal realities of their life on the front lines.
