Title: A Bullet for the General (1966)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Damiano Damiani
Main Cast: Gian Maria Volonté, Klaus Kinski, Martine Beswick, Lou Castel, Aldo Sambrell
Keywords: mexican revolution, spaghetti western

Overview: El Chuncho's bandits rob arms from a train, intending to sell the weapons to Elias' revolutionaries. They are helped by one of the passengers, Bill Tate, and allow him to join them, unware he is an assassin working for the Mexican government.
