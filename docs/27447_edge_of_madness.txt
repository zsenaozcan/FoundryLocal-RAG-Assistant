Title: Edge of Madness (2002)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Anne Wheeler
Main Cast: Caroline Dhavernas, Paul Johansson, Brendan Fehr, Corey Sevier, Jonas Chernick
Keywords: suspense, woman director

Overview: 1851, Manitoba's Red River Valley. As winter sets in, a young woman on the edge of madness arrives exhausted at the fort, a wilderness station, claiming she murdered her husband. She's placed in a cell; for the next several months, she sews while the local prefect, Henry Mullen, investigates.
