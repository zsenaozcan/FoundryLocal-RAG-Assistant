Title: Kill and Pray (1967)
Genres: Western
Director: Carlo Lizzani
Main Cast: Lou Castel, Mark Damon, Pier Paolo Pasolini, Barbara Frey, Rossana Martini
Keywords: skull, spaghetti western, mexican, shooting game

Overview: Requiescant is a 1967 spaghetti western directed by Carlo Lizzani. After surviving his family being massacred, a young boy is taken in and raised by a preacher. Years later he comes face to face with the man that killed his family and he is tempted into back into violence.
