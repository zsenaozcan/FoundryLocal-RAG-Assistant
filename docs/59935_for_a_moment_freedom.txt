Title: For a Moment, Freedom (2008)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Arash T. Riahi
Main Cast: Navíd Akhavan, Pourya Mahyari, Elika Bozorgi, Sina Saba, Payam Madjlessi
Keywords: refugee, road movie

Overview: Centers on a group of weary Middle Eastern refugees who have made their way to Turkey to apply for European visas.
