Title: Three Men and a Little Lady (1990)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Emile Ardolino
Main Cast: Tom Selleck, Steve Guttenberg, Ted Danson, Nancy Travis, Robin Weisman
Keywords: london england, marriage proposal, daughter

Overview: Sylvia's work increasingly takes her away from the three men who help bring up Mary, her daughter. When she decides to move to England and take Mary with her, the three men are heartbroken at losing the two most important females in their lives.
