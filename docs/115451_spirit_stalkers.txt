Title: Spirit Stalkers (2012)
Genres: Horror
Director: Steve Hudgins
Main Cast: Steve Hudgins, PJ Woodside, Jessica Dockrey, Nick Faust, Michael Coon
Keywords: haunted house

Overview: The Spirit Stalkers reality show, once a big hit, faces cancellation without a ratings boost, which leads the investigative team to Gloria Talman, whose house is rampant with unexplained ghostly activity.
