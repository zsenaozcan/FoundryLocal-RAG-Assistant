Title: Louise-Michel (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Benoît Delépine
Main Cast: Yolande Moreau, Bouli Lanners, Benoît Poelvoorde, Albert Dupontel, Mathieu Kassovitz

Overview: In this radical and endearing black comedy, a group of retrenched female factory workers decide to pool their compensation money… and hire a hit man to liquidate their boss.
