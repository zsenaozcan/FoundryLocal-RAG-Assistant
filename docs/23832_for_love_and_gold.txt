Title: For Love and Gold (1966)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Mario Monicelli
Main Cast: Vittorio Gassman, Gian Maria Volonté, Gianluigi Crescenzi, Ugo Fangareggi, Catherine Spaak
Keywords: italy, knight, bear, cult favorite, contagion spread, pirate

Overview: A group of rogues steal a scroll granting its bearer the property of the land of Aurocastro in Apulia (south of Italy). They elect a shaggy knight, Brancaleone from Norcia, as their leader, and decide to get possession of this supposedly wealthy land. Many adventures will occurr during the journey.
