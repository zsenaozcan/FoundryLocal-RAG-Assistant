Title: The Scenic Route (1978)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mark Rappaport
Main Cast: Randy Danson, Marilyn Jones, Kevin Wade, Grant Stewart, Arthur Ginsberg
Keywords: love triangle, independent film, avant-garde

Overview: Spins the tale of a woman, her sister, and the man who completes the triangle. Told through such fertile sources as grand opera, classical painting, and Victorian melodrama.
