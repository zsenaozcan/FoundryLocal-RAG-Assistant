Title: The Colour of Magic (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, TV Movie
Director: Vadim Jean
Main Cast: David Jason, Sean Astin, Tim Curry, Jeremy Irons, Brian Cox
Keywords: based on novel, university, discworld

Overview: As Rincewind involuntarily becomes a guide to the naive tourist Twoflower, they find themselves forced to flee the city of Ankh-Morpork to escape a terrible fire, and begin on a journey across the Disc. Unknown to them, their journey &amp; fate is being decided by the Gods playing a board game the whole time.
