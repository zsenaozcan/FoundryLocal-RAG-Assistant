Title: A Touch of Class (1973)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Melvin Frank
Main Cast: George Segal, Glenda Jackson, Paul Sorvino, K Callan, Cec Linder
Keywords: resort, affair

Overview: Steve, a happily married American man living in London meets Vicki, an English divorcée and run off to Marbella for a rollicking week of sex. They then return to London to set up a cozy menage, despite the fact that he loves his wife and children, and now realize that he and Vicki have also fallen in love.
