Title: Inbred (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Alex Chandon
Main Cast: Jo Hartley, Seamus O'Neill, James Doherty, James Burrows, Neil Leiper
Keywords: chainsaw, torture, farm house, inbreeding

Overview: Four young offenders and their workers spend a weekend in the remote Yorkshire village of Mortlake, which prides on keeping itself to itself. A minor incident with locals rapidly escalates into a blood-soaked, deliriously warped nightmare.
