Title: Aurora (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Robert Kouba
Main Cast: Carmen Argenziano, Eileen Grubba, Julian Schaffner, Jeannine Wacker, Pavlo Bubryak

Overview: In 2020 super-computer Kronos concludes that human beings are the biggest threat to the planet. 60 years later, Andrew finds himself lost in a world run by machines when he meets Calia, a girl on the way to a place called Aurora.
