Title: Ballerina (2016)
Genres: Animation, Family, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Eric Summer
Main Cast: Elle Fanning, Dane DeHaan, Carly Rae Jepsen, Maddie Ziegler, Mel Brooks
Keywords: paris, flying, inventor, dance, opera, musical, life's dream, ballet, orphan, statue of liberty
