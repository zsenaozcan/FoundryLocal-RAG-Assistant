Title: Oh, God! Book II (1980)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy
Director: Gilbert Cates
Main Cast: George Burns, Suzanne Pleshette, David Birney, Louanne, John Louie
Keywords: god, divorced parents

Overview: Second 'Oh God' movie has God appearing before 11-year-old Tracy Richards to ask for her help to spread his word and influence over the world which she suggests the slogan 'Think God.' Naturaly, Tracy's divorced parents Paula and Don think Tracy's just crazy and plot to halt her 'heaven-sent' mission to spread God's word. Written by Matthew Patay
