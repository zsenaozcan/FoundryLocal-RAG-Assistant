Title: The Professional (1981)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Thriller
Director: Georges Lautner
Main Cast: Jean-Paul Belmondo, Robert Hossein, Elisabeth Margoni, Jean-Louis Richard, Jean Desailly
Keywords: paris, prison, africa, homeless person, wife husband relationship, intelligence, revenge, escape from prison

Overview: French secret agent Joss Baumont is sent to kill the president Njala of an African country. However, a counter-order is given before he can execute his mission and he is abandoned in the hands of foreign authorities. He escapes and seeks revenge by pursuing what was his original mission.
