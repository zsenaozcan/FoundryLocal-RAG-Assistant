Title: Ripley Under Ground (2005)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Roger Spottiswoode
Main Cast: Barry Pepper, Willem Dafoe, Jacinda Barrett, Tom Wilkinson, Alan Cumming
Keywords: cocaine, suspense, london

Overview: After his friend, a hot young artist, is killed, a resourceful American man living in London covers up the crime and tries to keep the friend's name alive in order to exploit his legacy and reap millions in the process.
