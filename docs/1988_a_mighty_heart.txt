Title: A Mighty Heart (2007)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Michael Winterbottom
Main Cast: Angelina Jolie, Dan Futterman, Irrfan Khan, Archie Panjabi, Denis O'Hare
Keywords: terror, journalism, new love, sadness, fbi, despair, hostage drama, support, hope, friendship
