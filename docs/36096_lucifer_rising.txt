Title: Lucifer Rising (1974)
Genres: Mystery, Fantasy
Director: Kenneth Anger
Main Cast: Kenneth Anger, Bobby Beausoleil, Donald Cammell, Marianne Faithfull, Myriam Gibril
Keywords: occult, short

Overview: Lucifer Rising is a short film by director Kenneth Anger. The film was completed in 1972 and starred British singer Marianne Faithfull, but only widely distributed in 1980. The film's soundtrack was composed by Bobby Beausoleil.
