Title: Razortooth (2007)
Genres: Action, Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Patricia Harrington
Main Cast: Kathleen LaGue, Doug Swander, Matt Holly, Simon Page, Tim Colceri
Keywords: sheriff, eel, animal horror

Overview: The concept is based on a true story concerning an exotic species of eels that are released in the southeast from Asia. They breathe air and can survive on land.
