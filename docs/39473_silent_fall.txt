Title: Silent Fall (1994)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Bruce Beresford
Main Cast: Richard Dreyfuss, Linda Hamilton, John Lithgow, J.T. Walsh, Ben Faulkner
Keywords: autism, double murder

Overview: An autistic boy witnesses his parents' double murder. Richard Dreyfuss as a controversial therapist, seeks to probe the child's mind in order to solve the case.
