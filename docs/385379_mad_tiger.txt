Title: Mad Tiger (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Michael Haertlein
Main Cast: Kengo Hioki, Kotaro Tsukada, Yumiko Hioki, Akihiko Naruse, Akiteru Ito

Overview: Two best friends spent the last fifteen years touring the country in their performance art punk band. When one of them decides to quit, they both face deeper challenges than expected.
