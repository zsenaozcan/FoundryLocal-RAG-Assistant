Title: The Hands (2006)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Alejandro Doria
Main Cast: Graciela Borges, Jorge Marrale, Duilio Marzio,  Belen Blanco, Carlos Portaluppi

Overview: The priest Mario Pantaleo discovers he has some power to diagnose and cure diseases through the laying on of hands. But the police and the church hierarchy will against him.
