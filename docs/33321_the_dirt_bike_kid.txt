Title: The Dirt Bike Kid (1985)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family, Fantasy
Director: Hoite C. Caston
Main Cast: Peter Billingsley, Stuart Pankin, Anne Bloom, Patrick Collins, Sage Parker
Keywords: kids and family

Overview: When his mother sends Jack off with money to buy groceries, he comes home with a magic supercharged dirt bike instead. His mother is furious, but when Jack uses the magic bike to save the local hot dog stand from the clutches of corrupt big business, he becomes the town hero. Written by Concorde - New Horizons (with permission).
