Title: Surf Nazis Must Die (1987)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Peter George
Main Cast: Gail Neely, Robert Harden, Barry Brenner, Dawn Wildsmith, Michael Sonye

Overview: When the grandson of a gun wielding woman is murdered by neo-nazi surf punks in the post-apocalyptic future, this grandma hunts them down for some bloodthirsty revenge.
