Title: Grantham and Rose (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Kristin Hanggi
Main Cast: Jake T. Austin, Marla Gibbs, Tessa Thompson, Ryan Spahn, Jon Lee Cope
Keywords: woman director

Overview: When a petty crime thrusts him into the company of a feisty eighty-one-year-old African-American woman named Rose Price, Grantham and Rose push the boundaries of their relationship, their lives, and what it means to love, as they take a road trip back to their roots.
