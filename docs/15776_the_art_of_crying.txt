Title: The Art of Crying (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Peter Schønau Fog
Main Cast: Jannik Lorenzen, Jesper Asholt, Julie Kolbech, Hanne Hedelund, Thomas Knuth-Winterfeldt

Overview: Life is not easy for 11-year-old Allan living in South Jutland during the early 1970s. His mentally unstable father frequently threatens suicide and his mother has long since given up. It's up to Allan to keep the family together. When a rival family threatens his father's livelihood, Allan starts committing atrocious acts.
