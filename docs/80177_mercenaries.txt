Title: Mercenaries (2011)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Paris Leonti
Main Cast: Robert Fucilla, Billy Zane, Kirsty Mitchell, Vas Blackwood, Geoff Bell

Overview: Andy Marlow, an ex British S.A.S serviceman turned mercenary, is sent into the Balkans after a military coup has arisen to rescue a U.S ambassador and his aide.
