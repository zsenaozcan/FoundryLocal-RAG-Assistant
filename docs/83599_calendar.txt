Title: Calendar (1993)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Atom Egoyan
Main Cast: Arsinée Khanjian, Atom Egoyan, Ashot Adamyan, Michelle Bellerose

Overview: A photographer and his wife travel across Armenia photographing churches for a calendar project. Travelling with them is a local man acting as their driver and guide. As the project nears completion, the distance between husband and wife grows.
