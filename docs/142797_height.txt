Title: Height (1957)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Comedy
Director: Aleksandr Zarkhi
Main Cast: Nikolai Rybnikov, Inna Makarova, Gennadi Karnovich-Valua, Marina Strizhenova, Vasili Makarov

Overview: The romantic story set during the construction of a huge blast furnace that will supply thousands of jobs in a depressed community. As the local citizenry pulls together to assemble the furnace in record time, several romances develop. As a contrast, one of the workers is derelict in his duty, suffering mightily as a result. Based on a novel by Yevgeni Vorobyov.
