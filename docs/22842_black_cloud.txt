Title: Black Cloud (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ricky Schroder
Main Cast: Eddie Spears, Russell Means, Julia Jones, Tim McGraw, Ricky Schroder
Keywords: boxer, independent film, native american

Overview: Black Cloud, is an inspirational story about a young Navajo, Native American boxer, who overcomes personal challenges as he comes to terms with his heritage, while fighting his way for a spot on the US Olympic boxing team.
