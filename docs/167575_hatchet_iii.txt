Title: Hatchet III (2013)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, Comedy, Action
Director: BJ McDonnell
Main Cast: Danielle Harris, Kane Hodder, Zach Galligan, Caroline Williams, Derek Mears
Keywords: horror, sequel, gore, slasher, violent death, victor crowley, kill count

Overview: A search and recovery team heads into the haunted swamp to pick up the pieces and Marybeth learns the secret to ending the voodoo curse that has left Victor Crowley haunting and terrorizing Honey Island Swamp for decades.
