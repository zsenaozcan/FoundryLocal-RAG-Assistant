Title: Hercules and the Lost Kingdom (1994)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy, TV Movie
Director: Harley Cokeliss
Main Cast: Kevin Sorbo, Anthony Quinn, Renee O'Connor, Robert Trebor, Eric Close
Keywords: hero, mythology, greek mythology, legend, gods, hercules, warrior

Overview: Hercules comes to the aid of a young woman who is seeking the lost city of Troy. Eventually, Hercules leads her to a camp of refugees from the city, which has been taken over by Hera's Blue Priests. Hercules helps the refugees take back the city. "Hercules and the Lost Kingdom" is the second movie-length pilot episode of the television series "Hercules: The Legendary Journeys".
