Title: Apartment for Peggy (1948)
Genres: Drama
Director: George Seaton
Main Cast: Jeanne Crain, William Holden, Edmund Gwenn, Gene Lockhart, Griff Barnett
Keywords: college, hospital, pregnant woman, maternity ward, college life

Overview: Professor Henry Barnes decides he's lived long enough and contemplates suicide. His attitude is changed by Peggy Taylor, a chipper young mother-to-be who charms him into renting out his attic as an apartment for her and her husband Jason, a former GI struggling to finish college.
