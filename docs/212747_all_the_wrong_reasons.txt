Title: All the Wrong Reasons (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Gia Milani
Main Cast: Cory Monteith, Kevin Zegers, Emily Hampshire, Karine Vanasse, Denis Theriault
Keywords: trauma, woman director

Overview: An ensemble film about four everyday people: a store manager, a security guard, a fire fighter and a clerk who struggle in the aftermath of trauma.
