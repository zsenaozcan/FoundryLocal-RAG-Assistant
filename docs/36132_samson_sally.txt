Title: Samson & Sally (1984)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Jannik Hastrup
Main Cast: Jesper Klein, Helle Hertz, Per Pallesen, Bodil Udsen, Poul Thomsen
Keywords: adventure, whale, whale catch

Overview: Samson is a young whale that doesn't have friends. He spends his time listening to his mother's stories about the legendary whale Moby Dick. Samson becomes fascinated about it and decides to go on a journey to find Moby Dick.
