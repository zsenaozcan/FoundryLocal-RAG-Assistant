Title: An Empress and the Warriors (2008)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Ching Siu-Tung
Main Cast: Kelly Chen, Donnie Yen, Leon Lai, Guo Xiaodong, Kau Jan-Hoi
Keywords: loss of father, betrayal, empress, swordsman, ancient china, poison dart
