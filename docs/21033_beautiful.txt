Title: Beautiful (2009)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Dean O'Flaherty
Main Cast: Deborra-Lee Furness, Peta Wilson, Aaron Jeffery, Asher Keddie, Erik Thomson
Keywords: suspense

Overview: Searing and luscious, "Beautiful" tells the story of Daniel, a shy and introverted 14 year old boy residing in Sunshine Hills, a suburb living in the grip of fear following the rumoured abductions of three teenage girls. Daniel's two main obsessions are photography and Suzy, a 17 year old Lolita, a dangerous combination of youth and sexuality.
