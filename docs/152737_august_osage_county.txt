Title: August: Osage County (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: John Wells
Main Cast: Meryl Streep, Julia Roberts, Chris Cooper, Ewan McGregor, Margo Martindale
Keywords: suicide, drug addiction, funeral, dysfunctional family, based on play or musical, midwest, southern, bleak comedy, mental disorders

Overview: A look at the lives of the strong-willed women of the Weston family, whose paths have diverged until a family crisis brings them back to the Midwest house they grew up in, and to the dysfunctional woman who raised them.
