Title: Yellow Sky (1948)
Genres: Crime, Western
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: Gregory Peck, Anne Baxter, Richard Widmark, Robert Arthur, John Russell
Keywords: bank robber, desert

Overview: In 1867, a gang led by James "Stretch" Dawson robs a bank and flees into the desert. Out of water, the outlaws come upon a ghost town called Yellow Sky and its only residents, a hostile young woman named Mike and her grandpa. The story is a Western adaptation of William Shakespeare's "The Tempest".
