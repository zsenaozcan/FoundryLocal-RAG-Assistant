Title: And God Said to Cain (1970)
Genres: Thriller, Mystery, Western, Action
Director: Antonio Margheriti
Main Cast: Klaus Kinski, Peter Carsten, Marcella Michelangeli, Guido Lollobrigida, Antonio Cantafora
Keywords: spaghetti western, euro western

Overview: An innocent man sentenced to ten years in prison for a crime he did not commit, is released from jail, promising to seek revenge on the guilty.
