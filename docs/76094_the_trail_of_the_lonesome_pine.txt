Title: The Trail of the Lonesome Pine (1936)
Genres: Action, Romance
Director: Henry Hathaway
Main Cast: Sylvia Sidney, Henry Fonda, Fred Stone, Nigel Bruce, Beulah Bondi

Overview: A well-established tale of a long-running feud between two mountain clans. The features of this production were the new three-strip Technicolor process showing the outdoor Californian settings and an energetic young cast. It is said that Li'l Abner was based on Henry Fonda's performance.
