Title: Captains of the Clouds (1942)
Genres: Action, Drama, War
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: James Cagney, Dennis Morgan, Brenda Marshall, Alan Hale, George Tobias
Keywords: canada, world war ii, pilot, royal air force

Overview: Inspired by Churchill's Dunkirk speech, brash, undisciplined Canadian bush pilot Brian MacLean and three friends enlist in the RCAF.
