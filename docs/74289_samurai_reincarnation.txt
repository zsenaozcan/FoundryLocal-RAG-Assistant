Title: Samurai Reincarnation (1981)
Genres: Action, Fantasy, Horror
Director: Kinji Fukasaku
Main Cast: Sonny Chiba, Kenji Sawada, Akiko Kana, Ken Ogata, Hiroyuki Sanada
Keywords: samurai, undead

Overview: After surviving the slaughter of many Christians 350 years ago, a samurai denounces God for ignoring the pleas of believers. He sells his soul to Satan and receives the power to resurrect the dead to join him in a murderous rampage.
