Title: Provocateur (1998)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Drama
Director: Jim Donovan
Main Cast: Jane March, Stephen Mendel, Lillo Brancato, Nick Mancuso, Cary-Hiroyuki Tagawa
Keywords: spy, secret, technology, intelligence, nudity, mission, korea, espionage, agent

Overview: In 1994 South Korea, a female North Korean spy integrates herself as a housekeeper for U.S. military officer to steal classified documents, but soon worries about her cover when she falls for the teenage son of the officer.
