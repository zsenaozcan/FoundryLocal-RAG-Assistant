Title: Taken in Broad Daylight (2009)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Crime
Director: Gary Yates
Main Cast: James Van Der Beek, Sara Canning, LeVar Burton, Diana Reis, Tom Anniko
Keywords: based on true story, child kidnapping

Overview: Every 24 hrs. after a child's been kidnapped by a stranger, chances of finding them alive diminishes to almost 100% ... The true and inspiring survival story of kidnapped teen, Anne Sluti, and how she manages to stay alive by manipulating her captor, engineering her own rescue and negotiating her safe release after 6 days of hell.
