Title: Dadnapped (2009)
Genres: TV Movie, Action, Adventure, Comedy, Family
Director: Paul Hoen
Main Cast: Emily Osment, David Henrie, Jason Earles, Jonathan Keltz

Overview: Melissa has a bad case of sibling rivalry, only her competition is a fictional character in her father's best-selling novel about a teenage super spy. When her father is "dadnapped" by a group of overzealous fans, it's up to Melissa to help him by tapping her inner superhero.
