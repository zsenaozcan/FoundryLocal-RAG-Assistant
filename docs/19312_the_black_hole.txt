Title: The Black Hole (2006)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller, TV Movie
Director: Tibor Takács
Main Cast: Kristy Swanson, Judd Nelson, David Selby, Heather Dawn Rally, Jennifer Lyn Quackenbush
Keywords: alien, mankind annihilated, experiment gone wrong, colossal black hole

Overview: It's 2 A.M. in St. Louis when a routine scientific experiment goes terribly wrong and an explosion shakes the city. A scientific team investigates, clashing with an intergalactic, voltage-devouring creature that vaporizes them.
