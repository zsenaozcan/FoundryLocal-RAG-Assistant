Title: Drive-In Horrorshow (2009)
Genres: Horror
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: A low-budget horror anthology made of 5 short films, each belonging to a different horror sub-genre. These segments are introduced by a ghoulish host of the sort that used to present late-night horror movies on TV. The five films are Pig, The Closet, Fall Apart, Meat Man, and Watcher.
