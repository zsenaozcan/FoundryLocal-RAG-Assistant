Title: The Castle of Fu Manchu (1969)
Genres: Crime, Adventure, Horror, Thriller
Director: Jesús Franco
Main Cast: Christopher Lee, Richard Greene, Howard Marion-Crawford, Günther Stoll, Rosalba Neri
Keywords: ice, opium, iceberg, ocean liner, secret formula

Overview: The evil mastermind Fu Manchu plots his latest scheme to basically freeze over the Earth's oceans with his diabolical new device. Opposing him is his arch-nemesis, Interpol's very British Dr. Nayland Smith.
