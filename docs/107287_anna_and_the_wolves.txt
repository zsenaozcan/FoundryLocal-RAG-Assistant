Title: Anna and the Wolves (1973)
Genres: Drama
Director: Carlos Saura
Main Cast: Geraldine Chaplin, José María Prada, José Vivó, Rafaela Aparicio, Fernando Fernán Gómez
Keywords: countryside, dysfunctional family

Overview: The young but traveled Ana arrives in a manor in the countryside of Spain to work as nanny of three girls and finds a dysfunctional family.
