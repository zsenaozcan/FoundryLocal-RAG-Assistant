Title: Basil (1998)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Romance
Director: Radha Bharadwaj
Main Cast: Jared Leto, Christian Slater, Claire Forlani, Rachel Pickup
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A lonely young aristocrat in turn-of the century England struggles to meet the approval of his over-bearing, class-conscious father while trying to please the selfish woman he loves.
