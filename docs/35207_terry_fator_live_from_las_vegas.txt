Title: Terry Fator: Live from Las Vegas (2009)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mark Goffman
Main Cast: Terry Fator, Taylor Dew
Keywords: stand-up comedy, puppetry

Overview: Experience why America has fallen in love with Terry Fator's irresistible combination of humor and music and his hilarious cast of characters! The million-dollar winner of "America's Got Talent" and his endearing puppets deliver spot-on performances in the styles of many favorite singers.
