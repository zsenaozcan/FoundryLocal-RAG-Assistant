Title: Shanghai Triad (1995)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Zhang Yimou
Main Cast: Gong Li, Li Bao-Tian, Wang Xiaoxiao, Li Xuejian, Chun Sung
Keywords: servant, cabaret, shanghai, diva, uncle, mistress, umbrella

Overview: A provincial boy related to a Shanghai crime family is recruited by his uncle into cosmopolitan Shanghai in the 1930s to be a servant to a ganglord's mistress.
