Title: Reunion in France (1942)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: Jules Dassin
Main Cast: Joan Crawford, John Wayne, Philip Dorn, Reginald Owen, Albert Bassermann
Keywords: france, industrialist, nazis

Overview: Frenchwoman Michele de la Becque, an opponent of the Nazis in German-occupied Paris, hides a downed American flyer, Pat Talbot, and attempts to get him safely out of the country.
