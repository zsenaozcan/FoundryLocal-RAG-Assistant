Title: Highwaymen (2004)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Robert Harmon
Main Cast: Jim Caviezel, Rhona Mitra, Frankie Faison, Colm Feore, Gordon Currie
Keywords: car crash, serial killer, series of murders

Overview: James Cray watched as his wife was killed by Fargo, a hit-and-run serial murderer. After severely injuring Fargo and going to prison for several years, James is now determined to avenge his wife's death. He drives across the country looking for Fargo's 1972 Cadillac Eldorado, which the now-disabled killer has turned into a rolling death trap. James' search is helped by a state traffic officer and a singer with her own agenda.
