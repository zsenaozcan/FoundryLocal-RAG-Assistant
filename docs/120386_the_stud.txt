Title: The Stud (1978)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Quentin Masters
Main Cast: Joan Collins, Sue Lloyd, Walter Gotell, Oliver Tobias, Mark Burns
Keywords: disco, club

Overview: Fontaine Khaled is the wife of a wealthy but boring businessman. She spends his money on her nightclub, The Hobo, and partying. She hires a manager, Tony, to run her club, but it is understood that his job security is dependent on him satisfying her nymphomaniac demands.
