Title: The Roosevelts: An Intimate History (2014)
Genres: History, Documentary
Director: Ken Burns
Main Cast: Meryl Streep, Paul Giamatti, Edward Herrmann, Peter Coyote, Doris Kearns Goodwin
Keywords: usa president, theodore roosevelt, historical figure, franklin delano roosevelt, history, documentary, miniseries, historical, american history, usa politics

Overview: A documentary that weaves together the stories of Theodore, Franklin and Eleanor Roosevelt, three members of one of the most prominent and influential families in American politics.
