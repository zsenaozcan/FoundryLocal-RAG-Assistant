Title: Choking Man (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Steve Barron
Main Cast: Octavio Gómez Berríos, Eugenia Yuan, Aaron Paul, Mandy Patinkin, Kate Buddeke
Keywords: independent film

Overview: The social anxiety of a morbidly shy Ecuadorian dishwasher working in a Queens diner provides the psychological engine that powers this blend of drama and magical realism.
