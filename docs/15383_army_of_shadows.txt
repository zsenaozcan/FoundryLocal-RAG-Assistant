Title: Army of Shadows (1969)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Jean-Pierre Melville
Main Cast: Lino Ventura, Paul Meurisse, Jean-Pierre Cassel, Simone Signoret, Paul Crauchet
Keywords: paris, concentration camp, world war ii, german, czech, nazis, french resistance, german occupation

Overview: Betrayed by an informant, Philippe Gerbier (Lino Ventura) finds himself trapped in a torturous Nazi prison camp. Though Gerbier escapes to rejoin the Resistance in occupied Marseilles, France, and exacts his revenge on the informant, he must continue a quiet, seemingly endless battle against the Nazis in an atmosphere of tension, paranoia and distrust.
