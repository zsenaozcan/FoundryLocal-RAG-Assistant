Title: Reservation Road (2007)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Thriller
Director: Terry George
Main Cast: Joaquin Phoenix, Jennifer Connelly, Elle Fanning, Sean Curley, Mark Ruffalo
Keywords: loss of son, sense of guilt, baseball, sadness, dying and death, funeral, police, klienten, obsequies, revenge

Overview: Two fathers' lives intersect when one of them is involved in a terrible and sudden hit-and-run car accident that leaves the other's son dead. In response, the two men (Joaquin Phoenix and Mark Ruffalo) react in unexpected ways as a reckoning looms in the near future.
