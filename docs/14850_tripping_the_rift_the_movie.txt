Title: Tripping the Rift: The Movie (2008)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Bernie Denk
Main Cast: Gayle Garfinkle, Rick Jones, Maurice LaMarche, Jenny McCarthy, John Melendez
Keywords: surreal, clowning

Overview: What begins for Chode and crew as a routine mission to protect a pissed-off princess will soon become a filth- splattered saga of dismembered royalty, indestructible clown assassins and desperately horny housewives.
