Title: CBGB (2013)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Randall Miller
Main Cast: Alan Rickman, Rupert Grint, Malin Åkerman, Johnny Galecki, Stana Katic
Keywords: night life, music, punk rock, ramones, nyc, talking head, blondie, iggy pop, patti smith

Overview: CBGB looks at New York's dynamic punk rock scene through the lens of the ground-breaking Lower East Side club started by eccentric Hilly Kristal (Alan Rickman) in 1973 which launched thousands of bands.
