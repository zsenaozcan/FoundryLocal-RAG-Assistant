Title: Tobruk (2008)
Genres: War, History
Director: Václav Marhoul
Main Cast: Jan Meduna, Petr Vaněk, Robert Nebřenský, Michal Novotný, Radim Fiala

Overview: It is the story of Jiri and Jan, two Czech soldiers, battling alongside the allied forces against the Germans, during World War II in Tobruk, Libya. Jiri Pospichal, eighteen years old, signs up as a volunteer in the Czechoslovak army. His naive ideas about heroism are rawly confronted with the hell of the African desert, complicated relationships in his unit and the ubiquitous threat of death.
