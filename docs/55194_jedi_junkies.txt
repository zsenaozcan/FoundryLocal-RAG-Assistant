Title: Jedi Junkies (2010)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Mark Edlitz
Main Cast: Ray Park, Peter Mayhew, Jeremy Bulloch, Olivia Munn, Jeremy Bulloch
Keywords: pop culture, fan culture, documentary, science fiction, space opera, fandom

Overview: A film about the world's most dedicated Star Wars fans. From lightsaber wielding martial arts academies to a filmmaker who built the world's only life-size Millennium Falcon, from a Monster Garage-esque sculptor whose professional livelihood is building custom lightsabers to metal-bikini wearing dancers who embody Slave Leia, the film offers viewers a rare glimpse into rabid fans' personal and professional self expression that borders on obsession.
