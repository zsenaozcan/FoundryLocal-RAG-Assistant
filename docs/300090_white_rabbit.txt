Title: White Rabbit (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Tim McCann
Main Cast: Britt Robertson, Sam Trammell, Ryan Lee, Nick Krause, Denise Williamson
Keywords: trauma, high school, revenge, student, teenager, violence, rabbit, dark, mental illness, imagination

Overview: A bullied student sees visions of a rabbit he was forced to kill as a child, and those visions propel him into a state where his imagination causes him to carry out violent acts.
