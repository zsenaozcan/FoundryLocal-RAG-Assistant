Title: Lady of Csejte (2015)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Horror
Director: Andrei Konst
Main Cast: Svetlana Khodchenkova, Pavel Derevyanko, Isabelle Allen, Claudiu Trandafir, Ada Condeescu

Overview: Based on a story of Countess Bathory, a serial killer in 16th century Transylvania who supposedly killed hundreds of children.
