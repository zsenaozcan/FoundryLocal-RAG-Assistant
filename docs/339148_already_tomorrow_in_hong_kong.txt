Title: Already Tomorrow in Hong Kong (2015)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Emily Ting
Main Cast: Jamie Chung, Bryan Greenberg, Richard Ng, Sarah Lian, Ines Laimins
Keywords: hong kong, expatriate, woman director

Overview: An attraction forms when a Chinese American girl visiting Hong Kong for the first time meets an American expat who shows her the way, but timing may not quite be on their side.
