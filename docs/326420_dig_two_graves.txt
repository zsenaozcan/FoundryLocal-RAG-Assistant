Title: Dig Two Graves (2014)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Fantasy
Director: Hunter Adams
Main Cast: Ted Levine, Samantha Isler, Danny Goldring, Troy Ruptash, Rachael Drummond

Overview: A girl's obsession with her brother's disappearance leads her on a nightmarish journey through a small town's Gothic landscape where she is faced with a deadly proposition. How far will she go to save the people she loves?
