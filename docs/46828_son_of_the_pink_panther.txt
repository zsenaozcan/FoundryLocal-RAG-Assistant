Title: Son of the Pink Panther (1993)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Blake Edwards
Main Cast: Roberto Benigni, Herbert Lom, Claudia Cardinale, Shabana Azmi, Debrah Farentino

Overview: The eighth and last of the Pink Panther series. The illegitimate son of Inspector Clouseau is on the case of the kidnapped Princess Yasmin.
