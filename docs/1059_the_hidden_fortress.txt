Title: The Hidden Fortress (1958)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action, Comedy
Director: Akira Kurosawa
Main Cast: Toshirō Mifune, Takashi Shimura, Minoru Chiaki, Misa Uehara, Kamatari Fujiwara
Keywords: japan, gold, peasant, assault, hostile

Overview: Lured by gold, two greedy peasants escort a man and woman across enemy lines. However, they do not realize that their companions are actually a princess and her general.
