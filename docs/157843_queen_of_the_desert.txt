Title: Queen of the Desert (2015)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Werner Herzog
Main Cast: Nicole Kidman, James Franco, Robert Pattinson, Damian Lewis, Jenny Agutter
Keywords: middle east, persia, adventurer

Overview: A chronicle of Gertrude Bell's life, a traveler, writer, archaeologist, explorer, cartographer, and political attaché for the British Empire at the dawn of the twentieth century.
