Title: Quarantine L.A. (2013)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror
Director: Filip Maciejewicz
Main Cast: Bo Linton, Eugenia Kuzmina, Adrian Voo, Adia Dinh, Dillaran Martin

Overview: Seven strangers try to survive in the aftermath of a devastating outbreak. They must band together to escape from an isolated Los Angeles that has been hit by a virus that dramatically alters infected humans.
