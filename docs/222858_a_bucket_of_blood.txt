Title: A Bucket of Blood (1995)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy, Horror
Director: Michael McDonald
Main Cast: Anthony Michael Hall, Justine Bateman, Shadoe Stevens, Sam Lloyd, Jesse D. Goins
Keywords: artist, sculptor

Overview: Walter Paisley, a busboy at a cappuccino bar called the Jabberjaw, is praised as a genius after he kills his landlady's cat and covers it in plaster. Pressured to produce more work, he goes after bigger subjects.
