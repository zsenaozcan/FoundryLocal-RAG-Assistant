Title: Kung Pow: Enter the Fist (2002)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Steve Oedekerk
Main Cast: Hui Lou Chen, Tad Horino, Steve Oedekerk, Jennifer Tung, Ming Lo
Keywords: loss of family, loss of parents, supernatural powers, invulnerability, spoof, evil, murder hunt

Overview: A movie within a movie, created to spoof the martial arts genre. Writer/director Steve Oedekerk uses contemporary characters and splices them into a 1970s kung-fu film, weaving the new and old together.
 As the main character, The Chosen One, Oedekerk sets off to avenge the deaths of his parents at the hands of kung-fu legend Master Pain. Along the way, he encounters some strange characters
