Title: The Reagan Show (2017)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Sierra Pettengill
Main Cast: Ronald Reagan, Nancy Reagan, Barbara Walters, Walter Cronkite, Ted Koppel
Keywords: usa president, president, ronald reagan, usa politics

Overview: Comprised entirely of archival footage taken during those pre-reality-television years, The Reagan Show looks at how Ronald Reagan redefined the look and feel of what it means to be the POTUS.
