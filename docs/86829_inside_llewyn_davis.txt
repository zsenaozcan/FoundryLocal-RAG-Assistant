Title: Inside Llewyn Davis (2013)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Joel Coen
Main Cast: Oscar Isaac, Carey Mulligan, Justin Timberlake, Jerry Grayson, Robin Bartlett
Keywords: folk music, performance, aspiring singer, new york city, recording, merchant marine, 1960s

Overview: In Greenwich Village in the early 1960s, gifted but volatile folk musician Llewyn Davis struggles with money, relationships, and his uncertain future following the suicide of his singing partner.
