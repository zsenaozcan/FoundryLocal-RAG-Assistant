Title: The Mack (1973)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Campus
Main Cast: Max Julien, Don Gordon, Richard Pryor, Carol Speed, George Murdock
Keywords: pimp, blaxploitation

Overview: Goldie returns from five years at the state pen and winds up king of the pimping game. Trouble comes in the form of two corrupt white cops and a crime lord who wants him to return to the small time.
