Title: Macon County Line (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: Richard Compton
Main Cast: Max Baer, Jr., Geoffrey Lewis, Leif Garrett, Alan Vint, Jesse Vint
Keywords: sheriff, independent film

Overview: A vengeful Southern sheriff is out for blood after his wife is brutally killed by a pair of drifters. Low-budget film set in Georgia in 1953 and based on fact.
