Title: Night Editor (1946)
Genres: Drama
Director: Henry Levin
Main Cast: William Gargan, Janis Carter, Jeff Donnell, Coulter Irwin, Charles D. Brown
Keywords: dirty cop, reporter, film noir

Overview: "Night Editor" was based on the already existing radio program in which a newspaper editor would recount the 'inside story' of some bit newspaper story, and later became a television series.
