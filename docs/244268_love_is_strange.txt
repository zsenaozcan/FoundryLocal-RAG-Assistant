Title: Love Is Strange (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ira Sachs
Main Cast: John Lithgow, Alfred Molina, Marisa Tomei, Darren E. Burrows, Charlie Tahan
Keywords: homosexuality, independent film, wedding, new york city

Overview: After 39 years together, Ben and George finally tie the knot, but George loses his job as a result, and the newlyweds must sell their New York apartment and live apart, relying on friends and family to make ends meet.
