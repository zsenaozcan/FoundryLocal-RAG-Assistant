Title: Roller Boogie (1979)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance, Family
Director: Mark L. Lester
Main Cast: Linda Blair, Jim Bray, Beverly Garland, Roger Perry, James Van Patten
Keywords: rollerskates, disco, rollerskating

Overview: Teen lovers Bobby and Terry band together with other roller skaters to try and prevent a powerful mobster taking over the land their favourite skating rink sits on, and compete in the Boogie Contest.
