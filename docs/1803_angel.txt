Title: Angel (1984)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: Robert Vincent O'Neill
Main Cast: Donna Wilkes, Cliff Gorman, Susan Tyrrell, Dick Shawn, Rory Calhoun
Keywords: female nudity, prostitute, sex, detective, nudity, exploitation, high school, vigilante, murder, independent film

Overview: 15 year-old Molly is top of her high school class. But nobody suspects that the model pupil earns her money at night: as a prostitute on Sunset Blvd. The well-organized separation of her two lives is shattered when two of her friends are slain by a serial killer. She's the only eye witness and becomes a target herself.
