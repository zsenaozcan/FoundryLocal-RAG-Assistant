Title: Eagles Over London (1969)
Genres: Drama, Action, History, Foreign
Director: Enzo G. Castellari
Main Cast: Frederick Stafford, Van Johnson, Francisco Rabal, Ida Galli, Luigi Pistilli

Overview: The British High Command finds itself in the thick of a huge dilemma when it is realized that they have long been infiltrated by spies from a German intelligence group. This all happens during the preliminary stages of the Battle of Britain.
