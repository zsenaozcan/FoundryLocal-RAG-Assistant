Title: The Last Remake of Beau Geste (1977)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Comedy
Director: Marty Feldman
Main Cast: Ann-Margret, Marty Feldman, Michael York, Peter Ustinov, James Earl Jones
Keywords: foreign legion, camel, parody

Overview: Digby Geste (Feldman) and his ‘identical’ twin brother Beau (York) compete with their stepmother (Ann-Margeret) over possession of a priceless family heirloom.
