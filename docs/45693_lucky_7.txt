Title: Lucky 7 (2003)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Harry Winer
Main Cast: Kimberly Williams-Paisley, Patrick Dempsey, Brad Rowe, Brian Markinson, Emily Holmes

Overview: Before Amy Myer's mother died when Amy was 7, she planned out the little girl's life on a timeline, including the fact that Amy would marry her 7th boyfriend. When Amy falls in love with #6, she's thrown into a tailspin, because all of her mother's advice had worked perfectly. Now she must decide whether to follow her mother's advice and wait for #7, or follow her own heart.
