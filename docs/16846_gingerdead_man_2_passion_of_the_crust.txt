Title: Gingerdead Man 2: Passion of the Crust (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Pieter Christian Colson
Main Cast: Michelle Bauer, K-von, Frank Nicotero, Gregory Nicotero, John Vulich
Keywords: darkness, beatnik, murder

Overview: The horrifying yet delicious and chewy Gingerdead Man causes murder and mayhem on the set of a horrible low budget movie set. It will take the determination of the studio's young new owner to save both his company and well as the lives of his young new friends. Including a terminally ill young boy whose final wish it was to meet the studios stars - The Tiny Terrors.
