Title: Recipe for a Perfect Christmas (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sheldon Larry
Main Cast: Carly Pope, Christine Baranski, Bobby Cannavale

Overview: The holiday season is turned upside down for a neophyte food critic JJ (Pope) when her uninhibited mother (Baranski) shows up unannounced. In an attempt to occupy her mom's time, JJ works out a deal with a young chef (Cannavale) that stirs up some matters of the heart.
