Title: The Colony (2013)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction, Horror
Director: Jeff Renfroe
Main Cast: Laurence Fishburne, Kevin Zegers, Bill Paxton, John Tench, Atticus Mitchell
Keywords: ice age, dystopia, global warming, starvation, cannibalism

Overview: Forced underground by the next ice age, a struggling outpost of survivors must fight to preserve humanity against a threat even more savage than nature.
