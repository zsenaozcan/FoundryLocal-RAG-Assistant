Title: Tractor Drivers (1939)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Comedy
Director: Ivan Pyryev
Main Cast: Marina Ladynina, Nikolay Kryuchkov, Boris Andreyev, Stepan Kayukov, Pyotr Aleynikov

Overview: The story takes place in a Soviet placed in what is now Ukraine. A mechanic arrives in the Soviet, lead by a young independent woman driving tractors and, between many comedy sketches and propaganda mottoes, a love comes to light.
