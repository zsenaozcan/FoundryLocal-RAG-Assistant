Title: Spring in a Small Town (1948)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Fei Mu
Main Cast: Wei Wei, Yu Shi, Wei Li, Chaoming Cui, Hongmei Zhang
Keywords: small town, spring

Overview: After eight years of marriage to Liyan - once rich but now a shadow of his former self following a long, ruinous war - Yuwen does little except deliver his daily medication. A surprise visit from Liyan's friend Zhang re-energises the household, but also stirs up dangerously suppressed longings and resentments.
