Title: Good for Nothing (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Gianni Di Gregorio
Main Cast: Gianni Di Gregorio, Marco Marzocca, Marco Messeri, Valentina Lodovini, Daniela Giordano

Overview: Easygoing Gianni looks forward to retirement - until he is informed that a change in the law obliges him to work three more years. Faced with the outrageous possibility of having to do a full day's work, he sets about finding a way to achieve a quiet life
