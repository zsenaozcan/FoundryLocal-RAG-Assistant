Title: Dirty Deeds (2002)
Genres: Crime, Comedy, Drama, Thriller
Director: David Caesar
Main Cast: Bryan Brown, Toni Collette, John Goodman, Sam Neill, Sam Worthington

Overview: Set in 1960's Sydney, this is the story of an Australian gangster whose booming business, buoyed by the influx of U.S. soldiers in town for R&amp;R during their tours in Vietnam, attracts the attention of first the Chicago mafia, and then their East Coast competitors.
