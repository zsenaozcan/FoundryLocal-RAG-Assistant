Title: The Verdict (2013)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Jan Verheyen
Main Cast: Koen De Bouw, Johan Leysen, Veerle Baetens, Jappe Claes, Joke Devynck
Keywords: law, justice

Overview: When his wife's murderer is released due to a procedural error, Luc will do everything in his power to stop it from happening... He is going to take justice into his own hands and will then compete with the constitutional state which let him down.
