Title: The Land Before Time XI: Invasion of the Tinysauruses (2005)
Genres: Fantasy, Animation, Science Fiction, Family
Director: Charles Grosvenor
Main Cast: Aaron Spann, Jeff Bennett, Aria Curzon, Michael Clarke Duncan, Miriam Flynn
Keywords: family relationships, dinosaur

Overview: While trying to obtain "Tree Sweets", Littlefoot into the tree and knocks down all the blossoms, attracting the Tinysauruses. They devour the plants and disappear. But Littlefoot isn't blamed for the disappearance, instead he accidentally makes the adults believe the tiny dinosaurs were the main cause. Now Littlefoot and his friends must protect the tiny herd and learn the value of honesty.
