Title: Raakh (1989)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Aditya Bhattacharya
Main Cast: Aamir Khan, Supriya Pathak, Pankaj Kapur, Jagdeep

Overview: After witnessing the public humiliation &amp; abuse of a female friend, a young man vows to avenge her by going after the hoodlums responsible. he is guided by a burnt out, cynical cop, who shows him the ropes as well as the realities of vigilante life.
