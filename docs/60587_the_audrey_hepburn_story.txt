Title: The Audrey Hepburn Story (2000)
Genres: Drama
Director: Steven Robman
Main Cast: Jennifer Love Hewitt, Frances Fisher, Eric McCormack, Emmy Rossum, Keir Dullea
Keywords: biography, hollywood, tv movie, actress

Overview: The film spans from Hepburn's early childhood to the 1950s which details her life as a Dutch ballerina, coming to grips with her parents' divorce, and enduring life in the Nazi-occupied Netherlands during World War II. She then settles in the U.S. where she succeeds in making it big as a movie actress, in such movies as Breakfast at Tiffany's.
