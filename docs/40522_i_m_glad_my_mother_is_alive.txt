Title: I’m Glad My Mother Is Alive (2009)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Claude Miller
Main Cast: Sophie Cattani, Christine Citti, Yves Verhoeven, Maxime Renard, Olivier Guéritée
Keywords: cinema, foster child, toddler, auto mechanic

Overview: This finely acted, delicate and provocative new film by one of France’s most impressive filmmakers—working here with his son, Nathan—depicts the troubled life of an adopted boy who, as a taciturn adult, visits his birth mother and strikes up a relationship fraught with tension and emotion.
