Title: Ghosts Can't Do It (1989)
Genres: Fantasy, Action, Comedy, Science Fiction, Romance
Director: John Derek
Main Cast: Bo Derek, Anthony Quinn, Don Murray, Julie Newmar, Donald Trump

Overview: Elderly Scott kills himself after a heart attack wrecks his body, but then comes back as a ghost and convinces his loving young hot wife Kate to pick and kill a young man in order for Scott to possess his body and be with her again.
