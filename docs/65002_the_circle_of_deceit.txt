Title: The Circle of Deceit (1981)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Volker Schlöndorff
Main Cast: Bruno Ganz, Hanna Schygulla, Jerzy Skolimowski, Jean Carmet, Gila von Weitershausen

Overview: Georg Laschen leaves his family in West Germany to go work as a war correspondent in Beirut during the fights between Christians and Palestinians.
