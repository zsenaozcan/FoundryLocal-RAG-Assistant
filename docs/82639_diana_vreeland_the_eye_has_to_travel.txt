Title: Diana Vreeland: The Eye Has to Travel (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Lisa Immordino Vreeland
Main Cast: Diana Vreeland
Keywords: woman director

Overview: This intimate and loving portrait of the legendary arbiter of fashion, art and culture illustrates the many stages of Vreeland's remarkable life. Born in Paris in 1903, she was to become New York's "Empress of Fashion" and a celebrated Vogue editor.
