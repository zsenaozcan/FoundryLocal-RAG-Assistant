Title: Hoodoo Ann (1916)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Lloyd Ingraham
Main Cast: Mae Marsh

Overview: A teenage orphan (Mae Marsh) is taken in by a childless couple and quickly falls for the boy next door (Robert Harron). Director Lloyd Ingraham's 1916 silent film also stars Wilbur Higby, Loyola O'Connor and Anna Hernandez.
