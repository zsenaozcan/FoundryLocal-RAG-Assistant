Title: Precious Cargo (2016)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Max Adams
Main Cast: Bruce Willis, Claire Forlani, Mark-Paul Gosselaar, John Brotherton, Lydia Hull
Keywords: crime boss, heist

Overview: After a botched heist, Eddie  a murderous crime boss, hunts down the seductive thief Karen who failed him. In order to win back Eddie’s trust, Karen recruits her ex-lover and premier thief Jack to steal a cargo of rare precious gems. But when the job goes down, allegiances are betrayed and lines are crossed as Jack, Karen, and Eddie face off in a fateful showdown.
