Title: Net Worth (1995)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jerry Ciccoritti
Main Cast: Aidan Devine, Kevin Conway, Robin Gammell, Carl Marotte, Richard Donat
Keywords: sport, trade union, labor organizer, tv movie, sports history
