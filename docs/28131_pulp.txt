Title: Pulp (1972)
Genres: Mystery, Crime, Drama
Director: Mike Hodges
Main Cast: Michael Caine, Mickey Rooney, Lionel Stander, Lizabeth Scott, Nadia Cassini
Keywords: funeral, clairvoyance, suspense, author, writer

Overview: A seedy writer of sleazy pulp novels is recruited by a quirky, reclusive ex-actor to help him write his biography at his house in Malta.
