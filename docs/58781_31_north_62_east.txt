Title: 31 North 62 East (2009)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Tristan Loraine
Main Cast: Heather Peace, Lee Godfrey, Aurélie Bargème, John Rhys-Davies, Marina Sirtis

Overview: A psychological thriller about how an elite SAS unit's position is revealed by the British Prime Minister to ensure an arms deal goes ahead and to secure his re-election.
