Title: The Rickshaw Man (1958)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hiroshi Inagaki
Main Cast: Toshirō Mifune, Hiroshi Akutagawa, Chishu Ryu, Chôko Iida, Hideko Takamine
Keywords: widow, rickshaw, father figure, remake of japanese film, social differences, feudal japan, drunkeness, unattainable desire

Overview: The story of Muhōmatsu, a rickshaw man who becomes a surrogate father to the child of a recently widowed woman.  1958 remake of a 1943 film (same title, same director)
