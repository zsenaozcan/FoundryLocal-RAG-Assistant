Title: Faust (1926)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Horror
Director: F.W. Murnau
Main Cast: Gösta Ekman, Emil Jannings, Camilla Horn, Frida Richard, William Dieterle
Keywords: pest, sale of soul, pact with the devil, ewige jugend, faust, love, romance, youth, death, cult film

Overview: God and Satan war over earth; to settle things, they wager on the soul of Faust, a learned and prayerful alchemist.
