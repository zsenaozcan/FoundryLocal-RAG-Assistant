Title: I'll Follow You Down (2013)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama, Mystery
Director: Richie Mehta
Main Cast: Gillian Anderson, Haley Joel Osment, Rufus Sewell, Victor Garber, Kiara Glasco

Overview: After the disappearance of a young scientist on a business trip, his son and wife struggle to cope, only to make a bizarre discovery years later - one that may bring him home.
