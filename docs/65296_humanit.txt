Title: Humanité (1999)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bruno Dumont
Main Cast: Emmanuel Schotté, Séverine Caneele, Philippe Tullier, Ghislain Ghesquère
Keywords: unsimulated sex, new french extremism

Overview: When an 11-year-old girl is brutally raped and murdered in a quiet French village, a police detective who has forgotten how to feel emotions--because of the death of his own family in some kind of accident--investigates the crime, which turns out to ask more questions than it answers.
