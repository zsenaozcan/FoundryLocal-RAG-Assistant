Title: Hidden Secrets (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Carey Scott
Main Cast: John Schneider, David A.R. White, Staci Keanan, Corin Nemec, Gregg Binkley

Overview: A young man finds himself torn between his current girlfriend and an old flame at a funeral of friend where lifestyles and attitudes clash over a long weekend in this faith-based dramedy.
