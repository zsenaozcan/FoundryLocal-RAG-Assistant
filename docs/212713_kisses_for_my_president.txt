Title: Kisses For My President (1964)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Curtis Bernhardt
Main Cast: Fred MacMurray, Polly Bergen, Eli Wallach, Arlene Dahl, Edward Andrews
Keywords: president

Overview: When the women of America join together on election day and elect a Leslie McCloud as the US President, things get a little awkward. Especially for her husband Thad McCloud. He, as First Husband, must take over the job as The First Lady, in the women's groups and garden parties.
