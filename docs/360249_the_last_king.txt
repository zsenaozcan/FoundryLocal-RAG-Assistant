Title: The Last King (2016)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: Nils Gaup
Main Cast: Kristofer Hivju, Jakob Oftebro, Pål Sverre Hagen, Thorbjørn Harr, Nikolaj Lie Kaas
Keywords: civil war, norway, kingdom, viking, snow, vikings

Overview: Year 1206. Norway is ravaged by civil war. The King's illegitimate son is guarded in deep secret. A boy that half of the kingdom wants to kill and two men will protect to the death. A boy named Earl Håkonsson. Birkebeiners is the story of the escape that changed Norway's history forever.
