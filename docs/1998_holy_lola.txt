Title: Holy Lola (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bertrand Tavernier
Main Cast: Isabelle Carré, Frédéric Pierrot, Lara Guirao, Maria Pitarresi, Jean-Yves Roan
Keywords: corruption, hotel, bestechung, adoption, cambodia, sterility, human trafficking, auvergne

Overview: The story of a young couple, Pierre and Geraldine, and their desire for a child, which leads them on a journey of initiation to Cambodia.  On their difficult and transformative adventure, they must contend with obstructive authorities and the jealousies and mistrust of a small community of would-be adoptive parents.
