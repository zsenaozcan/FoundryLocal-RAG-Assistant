Title: From the Terrace (1960)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Mark Robson
Main Cast: Paul Newman, Joanne Woodward, Ted de Corsia, Ina Balin, Elizabeth Allen
Keywords: new york, business, financial

Overview: Alfred Eaton, an ambitious young executive, climbs to the top of New York's financial world as his marriage crumbles. At the brink of attaining his career goals, he is forced to choose between business success, married to the beautiful, but unfaithful Mary and starting over with his true love, the much younger Natalie.
