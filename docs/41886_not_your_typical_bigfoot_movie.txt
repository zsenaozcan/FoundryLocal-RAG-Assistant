Title: Not Your Typical Bigfoot Movie (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jay Delaney
Main Cast: Dallas Gilbert, Wayne Burton, C. Thomas Biscardi
Keywords: bigfoot

Overview: Through the experiences of two amateur Bigfoot researchers in Appalachian Ohio, we see how the power of a dream can bring two men together and provide a source of hope and meaning that transcend the harsh realities of life in a dying steel town.
