Title: Mantis in Lace (1968)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: William Rotsler
Main Cast: Susan Stewart, Steve Vincent, James Brand, Vic Lance, Pat Barrington
Keywords: murder, gore, exploitation film, topless dancing

Overview: A topless dancer attracts, seduces, then murders the men she sleeps with. She does it with a twist, however; she kills them with garden tools.
