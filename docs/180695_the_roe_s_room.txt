Title: The Roe's Room (1997)
Genres: Music
Director: Lech Majewski
Main Cast: Mieczyslaw Czepulonis, Elzbieta Mazur, Rafał Olbrychski, Agnieszka Wróblewska

Overview: Bound to a suffocating existence, a young man embarks on a marvelous journey of the imagination, transforming ordinary details of his room into fantastic images of natural beauty, where the cycles of life mysteriously play themselves out.
