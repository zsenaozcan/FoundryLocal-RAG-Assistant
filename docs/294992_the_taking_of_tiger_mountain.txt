Title: The Taking of Tiger Mountain (2014)
Genres: Thriller, War, Adventure
Director: Tsui Hark
Main Cast: Zhang Hanyu, Tony Leung Ka-Fai, Tong Liya, Lin Gengxin, Nan Yu
Keywords: civil war, chinese communists, 3d

Overview: Based on the real life story of an incident in 1946 during the Chinese Civil War, the film involves a communist reconnaissance team soldier Yang Zirong who disguised himself as a bandit to infiltrate a local gang of bandits, eventually helping the main communist force to destroy the bandits. Based on the novel "Tracks in the Snowy Forest" by Qu Bo.
