Title: The Silence Before Bach (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Pere Portabella
Main Cast: Àlex Brendemühl, Féodor Atkine, Christian Brembeck, Daniel Ligorio, Georg C. Biller
Keywords: bach

Overview: This latest feature from the eccentrically experimental Catalan director Portabella is a beautiful, sometimes faintly bonkers celebration and contemplation of the role Bach’s music plays in the world today. Blending historical reconstruction with very loosely linked ‘dramatic’ scenes and documentary sequences, the film constitutes a playful, painterly sequence of variations on the argument that Johann Sebastian changed the way the world hears thanks to his extraordinary ear for harmony.
