Title: Diggstown (1992)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama
Director: Michael Ritchie
Main Cast: James Woods, Louis Gossett, Jr., Bruce Dern, Oliver Platt, Heather Graham
Keywords: transporter, con man, boxing match, sport

Overview: Gabriel Caine has just been released from prison when he sets up a bet with a business man. The business man owns most of a boxing-mad town called Diggstown. The bet is that Gabe can find a boxer that will knock out 10 Diggstown men, in a boxing ring, within 24 hours. "Honey" Roy Palmer is that man - although at 48, many say he is too old.
