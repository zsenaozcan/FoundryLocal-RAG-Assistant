Title: Nightmare Man (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Thriller
Director: Rolfe Kanefsky
Main Cast: Tiffany Shepis, Blythe Metz, Luciano Szafir, Aaron Sherry, Jack Sway
Keywords: mask, demon

Overview: After receiving a mysterious demonic African mask in the mail, Ellen Morris is attacked by a "being" she refers to as THE NIGHTMARE MAN. Her doctors and husband, William believe Ellen is a paranoid schizophrenic and needs to spend some quality time at a mental facility for further examination.
