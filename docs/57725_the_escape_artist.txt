Title: The Escape Artist (1982)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Caleb Deschanel
Main Cast: Griffin O'Neal, Raúl Juliá, Teri Garr, Joan Hackett, Desi Arnaz
Keywords: children

Overview: The young and self-confident Danny blufs at the local police-station that he will escape out of prison within one hour. What follows is a flashback about his childhood with his uncle and aunt, which are 'vaudeville'-artists themselves.
