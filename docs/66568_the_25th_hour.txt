Title: The 25th Hour (1967)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Henri Verneuil
Main Cast: Anthony Quinn, Serge Reggiani, Virna Lisi, Grégoire Aslan, Jacques Marin
Keywords: world war ii, jew persecution

Overview: A Romanian peasant fights to get back to his family after he's imprisoned by the Nazis.The picture is based on real events. It includes Hungary's government in collaboration with the Nazis, the encroachment of Romania by Stalin's troopers, and other happenings.
