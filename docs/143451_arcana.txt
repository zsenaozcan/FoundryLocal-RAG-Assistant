Title: Arcana (1972)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery
Director: Giulio Questi
Main Cast: Lucia Bosé, Maurizio Degli Esposti, Tina Aumont, Renato Paracchi, Gianfranco Pozzi
Keywords: mother, rape, supernatural, sorcerer, blood, psychic, death, clairvoyant, powers

Overview: A Sicilian widow earns her living as a clairvoyant, in Milan, but she hasn't got any power at all. Her son instead holds supernatural powers and with the help of his mother he becomes a strong sorcerer. But he fails perhaps to understand the real strength he possesses inside and unbinds uncontrolled forces that lead people that surround him to go mad.
