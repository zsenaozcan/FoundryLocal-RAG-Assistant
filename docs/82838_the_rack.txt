Title: The Rack (1956)
Genres: War, Drama, Romance
Director: Arnold Laven
Main Cast: Paul Newman, Wendell Corey, Walter Pidgeon, Edmond O'Brien, Anne Francis
Keywords: korean war

Overview: Captain Edward Hall returns to the USA after two years in a prison camp in the korean war. In the camp he was brainwashed and helped the Chinese convince the other prisoners that they were fighting an unjust war. When he comes back he is charged for collaboration with the enemy. Where does loyalty end in a prison camp, when the camp is a living hell?
