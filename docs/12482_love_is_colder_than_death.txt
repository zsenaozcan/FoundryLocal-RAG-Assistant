Title: Love Is Colder Than Death (1970)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Rainer Werner Fassbinder
Main Cast: Ulli Lommel, Hanna Schygulla, Katrin Schaake, Liz Soellner, Rainer Werner Fassbinder
Keywords: hitman, falling in love, criminal, illegal prostitution, crime syndicate

Overview: Unusual gangster story, in which a small-time pimp Franz, who is torn between his mistress and Bruno the gangster sent after him by the syndicate that he has refused to join.
