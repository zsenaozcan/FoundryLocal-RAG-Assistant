Title: A Life Among Whales (2005)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Bill Haney
Main Cast: Roger Payne, John Perry, Paul Winter, Xianfeng Zhang
Keywords: whales

Overview: Weaving together natural history and biography, this film is a fascinating exploration into the unique relationship between humans and whales as told by renowned whale biologist and activist Dr. Roger Payne. A pioneer in whale song research, Payne has passionately promoted the protection of these majestic sea creatures for four decades and continues to do so by studying ocean pollution.
