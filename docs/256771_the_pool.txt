Title: The Pool (2014)
Genres: Horror
Director: Chris W. Mitchell
Main Cast: Carine Crutzen, Gijs Scholten van Aschat, Katja Herbers, Jamie Grant, Bart Klever

Overview: Two families go camping illegally in a forest, and set up their tents near a beautiful pond, far away from the daily hubbub. However, they soon discover that the pond contains a mysterious force, which will not allow them to leave. Rot and decay strike, and madness follows.
