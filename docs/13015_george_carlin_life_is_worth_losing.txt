Title: George Carlin: Life Is Worth Losing (2005)
Genres: Documentary, Comedy, TV Movie
Director: Rocco Urbisci
Main Cast: George Carlin
Keywords: comedian, religion and supernatural, dying and death, concert, politics, made for cable tv, stand-up comedy, stand-up comedian, tv movie, tv special

Overview: Carlin returns to the stage in his 13th live comedy stand-up special, performed at the Beacon Theatre in New York City for HBO®. His spot-on observations on the deterioration of human behavior include Americans’ obsession with their two favorite addictions - shopping and eating; his creative idea for The All-Suicide Channel, a new reality TV network; and the glorious rebirth of the planet to its original pristine condition - once the fires and floods destroy life as we know it.
