Title: The Conformist (1970)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bernardo Bertolucci
Main Cast: Jean-Louis Trintignant, Stefania Sandrelli, Gastone Moschin, Dominique Sanda, Enzo Tarascio
Keywords: paris, italy, assassin, fascism, benito mussolini

Overview: A weak-willed Italian man becomes a fascist flunky who goes abroad to arrange the assassination of his old teacher, now a political dissident.
