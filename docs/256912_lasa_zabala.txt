Title: Lasa & Zabala (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Crime
Director: Pablo Malo
Main Cast: Unax Ugalde, Ricard Sales, Francesc Orella, Oriol Vila, Pep Tosar

Overview: In October 1983, members of ETA Lasa and Zabala disappear in Bayonne. Twelve years later, their bodies, tortured and buried in quicklime by the GAL (Anti-Terrorist Liberation Groups) are identified. Then begins a process in which the lawyer for the two families (Unax Ugalde) and his assistant try to justice and that the murderers sit on the dock.
