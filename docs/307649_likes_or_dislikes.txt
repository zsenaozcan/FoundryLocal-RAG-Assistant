Title: Likes or Dislikes (2014)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Klim Shipenko
Main Cast: Maksim Matveev, Svetlana Khodchenkova, Lyubov Aksyonova, Sergey Gazarov, Maksim Vitorgan

Overview: Aleksey must decide what's better for him - a regular life with his fiancé Alyona or adventurous life with a famous journalist Irina.
