Title: Battle for Brooklyn (2011)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Michael Galinsky
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: An intimate look at the very public and passionate fight waged by residents and business owners of Brooklyn’s historic Prospect Heights neighborhood facing condemnation of their property to make way for the polarizing Atlantic Yards project, a massive plan to build 16 skyscrapers and a basketball arena for the New Jersey Nets.
