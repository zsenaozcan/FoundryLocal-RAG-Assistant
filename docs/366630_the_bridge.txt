Title: The Bridge (2015)
Genres: Drama, TV Movie
Director: Mike Rohl
Main Cast: Katie Findlay, Wyatt Nash, Andrea Brooks, Faith Ford, Ted McGinley
Keywords: based on novel

Overview: Karen Kingsbury's The Bridge is the sweeping tale of Molly Callens (Findlay) and Ryan Kelly (Nash), two young students who share a profound friendship their first semester in college, a time that becomes the defining moment of their lives.
