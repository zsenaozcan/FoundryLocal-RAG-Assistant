Title: Yuppi Du (1975)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Adriano Celentano
Main Cast: Adriano Celentano, Claudia Mori, Charlotte Rampling, Rosita Celentano, Gino Santercole

Overview: Felice Della Pietà is indifferent to money and the turbulences of life. His wife Sylvia simulates suicide in order not to live in poverty with him.
