Title: Blood Lake (2014)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Jason Cullen Bressack
Main Cast: Shannen Doherty, Jason Brooks, Zack Ward, Christopher Lloyd, Ciara Hanna
Keywords: lake, water monster

Overview: After chomping through the fish population, thousands of starved lampreys begin attacking the citizens of a sleepy lake town, and the community scrambles to stay alive.
