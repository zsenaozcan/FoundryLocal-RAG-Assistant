Title: Unbowed (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Chung Ji-young
Main Cast: Lee Kyoung-young, Jin Kyung, Kim Eung-Soo, Ahn Sung-Ki, Kim Ji-ho
Keywords: 부러진 화살

Overview: Inspired by the true story of Kim Kyung-ho, a math professor who was arrested for shooting a crossbow at the presiding judge of his appeal against unfair dismissal.
