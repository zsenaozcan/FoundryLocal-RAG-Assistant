Title: People Will Talk (1951)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Joseph L. Mankiewicz
Main Cast: Cary Grant, Jeanne Crain, Finlay Currie, Hume Cronyn, Walter Slezak
Keywords: romantic comedy

Overview: Successful and well-liked, Dr. Noah Praetorius becomes the victim of a witchhunt at the hands of Professor Elwell, who disdains Praetorius's unorthodox medical views and also questions his relationship with the mysterious, ever-present Mr. Shunderson.
