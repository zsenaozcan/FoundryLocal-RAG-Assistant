Title: Men in White (1998)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy
Director: Scott P. Levy
Main Cast: Thomas F. Wilson, Karim Prince, M. Emmet Walsh, Brion James, Wigald Boning

Overview: National Lampoon's Men in White is a straight-to-television satirical parody of contemporary science fiction movies, mainly spoofing Men in Black and Independence Day. It debuted in 1998 on the Fox Family Channel.
