Title: Adopt a Sailor (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Charles Evered
Main Cast: Bebe Neuwirth, Peter Coyote, Ethan Peck
Keywords: relationship problems, independent film, sailor

Overview: Adopt A Sailor is about Patricia and Richard, a successful and hip couple from New York City who inadvertently "adopt a sailor" during Fleet Week in New York City and the young man form Turkey Scratch, Arkansas who changes their lives forever.
