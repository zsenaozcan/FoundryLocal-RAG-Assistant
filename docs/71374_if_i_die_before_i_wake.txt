Title: If I Die Before I Wake (2001)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Brian Katkin
Main Cast: Stephanie Jones, Muse Watson, Michael McCleery, Anthony Nicosia, Mary Kathleen Gordon

Overview: A suburban family is held hostage and terrorized by three intruders while their sixteen year-old daughter first hides, then exacts her own brand of terror on the assailants.
