Title: The Black Sheep (1968)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Luciano Salce
Main Cast: Vittorio Gassman, Lisa Gastoni, Adrienne La Russa, Giampiero Albertini, Ennio Balbo
Keywords: twin brother

Overview: Episode film about identical twin brothers. Mario is an honest government-minded deputy. Filippo lives by his wits, not quite legally. Eventually Filippo ruins his brother's career and mind, absorbs his wife and finally steps into his shoes as an honorable member of Parliament.
