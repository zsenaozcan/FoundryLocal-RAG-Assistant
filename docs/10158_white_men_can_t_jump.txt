Title: White Men Can't Jump (1992)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Ron Shelton
Main Cast: Wesley Snipes, Woody Harrelson, Rosie Perez, Tyra Ferrell, Cylk Cozart
Keywords: white trash, male friendship, friendship, sport, basketball, racial segregation, streetball, jeopardy, los angeles, hoodlum

Overview: Billy Hoyle (Woody Harrelson) and Sidney Deane (Wesley Snipes) are an unlikely pair of basketball hustlers. They team up to con their way across the courts of Los Angeles, playing a game that's fast dangerous - and funny.
