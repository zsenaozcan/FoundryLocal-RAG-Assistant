Title: Red-Headed Woman (1932)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Jack Conway
Main Cast: Jean Harlow, Chester Morris, Lewis Stone, Leila Hyams, Una Merkel
Keywords: chauffeur, mistress, divorce, pre-code, coal, gold digger

Overview: Lil works for the Legendre Company and causes Bill to divorce Irene and marry her. She has an affair with businessman Gaerste and uses him to force society to pay attention to her. She has another affair with the chauffeur Albert.
