Title: Ringers - Lord of the Fans (2005)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Carlene Cordova
Main Cast: Dominic Monaghan, Elijah Wood, Peter Jackson, Viggo Mortensen
Keywords: pop culture, fantasy, fan culture, documentary, the lord of the rings, cosplay, fandom
