Title: Indru Netru Naalai (2015)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy
Director: R. Ravi Kumar
Main Cast: Vishnu Vishal, Mia George, Karunakaran, Jayaprakash, P. Ravi Shankar
Keywords: time travel

Overview: Elango and his astrologer friend Pulivetti Arumugam come in possession of a time machine and start profiting from it. However, their meddling with time prevents the death of a gangster, who begins to wreck havoc in their lives.
