Title: Dying Room Only (1973)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, TV Movie
Director: Philip Leacock
Main Cast: Cloris Leachman, Ross Martin, Ned Beatty, Dana Elcar, Louise Latham
Keywords: hotel, murder, desert, tv movie, missing husband

Overview: A married couple are traveling on a deserted desert road at night. They stop at a diner and the husband goes to the men's room. He never returns and the wife begins to suspect serious foul play.
