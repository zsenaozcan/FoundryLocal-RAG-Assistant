Title: The Beatles: The First U.S. Visit (1991)
Genres: Music
Director: Albert Maysles
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The Beatles First US Visit uniquely chronicles the inside story of the two remarkable weeks when Beatlemania first ignited America. The pioneering Maysles Brothers who filmed at the shoulders of John, Paul, George and Ringo, innovated an intimate documentary style of film-making which set the benchmark for rock and roll cinematography that remains to this day.
