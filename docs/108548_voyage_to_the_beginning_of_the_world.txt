Title: Voyage to the Beginning of the World (1997)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Manoel de Oliveira
Main Cast: Marcello Mastroianni, Jean-Yves Gautier, Leonor Silveira, Diogo Dória, Isabel de Castro

Overview: Manoel is aging film director who travels with the film crew through Portugal in search of the origins of Afonso, a famous French actor whose father emigrated from Portugal to France and in process remembers his own youth.
