Title: Ride or Die (2003)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Thriller, Crime, Music, Romance
Director: Craig Ross Jr.
Main Cast: Duane Martin, Vivica A. Fox, Meagan Good, Michael Taliferro, Jadakiss

Overview: A slick private eye (Duane Martin) set out to investigate the death of a young rapper, only to find himself knee-deep in drugs, guns, and double crosses. Vivica A. Fox and real-life rappers Jadakiss and Sticky Fingaz co-star.
