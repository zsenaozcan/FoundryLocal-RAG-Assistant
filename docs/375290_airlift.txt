Title: Airlift (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Drama, History
Director: Raja Menon
Main Cast: Akshay Kumar, Nimrat Kaur, Feryna Wazheir, Lena, Purab Kohli
Keywords: evacuation, war, based on true story, iraq war

Overview: When Iraq invades Kuwait in August, 1990, a callous Indian businessman becomes the spokesperson for more than 170,000 stranded countrymen.
