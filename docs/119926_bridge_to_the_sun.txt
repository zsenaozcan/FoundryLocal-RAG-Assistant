Title: Bridge to the Sun (1961)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: Etienne Périer
Main Cast: Carroll Baker, James Shigeta, James Yagi, Tetsurō Tamba, Yôko Takahashi
Keywords: interracial marriage

Overview: Tells the true story of Gwen Terasaki, who falls in love with, then marries a Japanese diplomat. When war breaks out they find animosity and trouble from both sides.
