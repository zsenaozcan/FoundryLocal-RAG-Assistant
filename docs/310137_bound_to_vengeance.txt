Title: Bound to Vengeance (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: José Manuel Cravioto
Main Cast: Tina Ivlev, Richard Tyson, Bianca Malinowski, Kris Kjornes, Dustin Quick
Keywords: kidnapping, van, revenge, murder, gore, escape, blood, violence, captive, death

Overview: A young woman, Eve, fights back and manages to escape a malicious abductor. However, after discovering she may not be the only victim, Eve unravels a darker truth and decides to turn the tables on her captor.
