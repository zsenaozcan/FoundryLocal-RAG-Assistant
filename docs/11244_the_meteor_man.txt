Title: The Meteor Man (1993)
Genres: Fantasy, Action, Comedy, Science Fiction, Family
Director: Robert Townsend
Main Cast: Robert Townsend, Marla Gibbs, Eddie Griffin, Robert Guillaume, James Earl Jones
Keywords: street gang, hero, music teacher, supernatural powers, superhero

Overview: One night Jefferson Reed gets hit in the chest by a souped-up chunk of meteor. So he can fly, but he's scared of heights. He can master the information in any book ... for about fifteen minutes. Now his friends and family want him to protect their community from the dreaded Golden Lords.
