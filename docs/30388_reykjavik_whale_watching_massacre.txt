Title: Reykjavik Whale Watching Massacre (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Júlíus Kemp
Main Cast: Gunnar Hansen, Pihla Viitala, Nae, Terence Anderson, Miranda Hennessy

Overview: An epic tale about a group of whale watchers, whose ship breaks down and they get picked up by a whale fisher vessel. The Fishbillies on the vessel has just gone bust, and everything goes out of control.
