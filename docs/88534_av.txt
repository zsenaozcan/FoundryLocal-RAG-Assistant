Title: Avé (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Konstantin Bojanov
Main Cast: Anjela Nedyalkova, Ovanes Torosian, Martin Brambach, Svetla Yancheva
Keywords: bulgaria, teenager, hitchhiking

Overview: While hitchhiking from Sofia to Ruse, Kamen meets Avé, a 17-year-old runaway girl. With each ride they hitch, Avé invents new identities for them, and her compulsive lies get Kamen deeper and deeper into trouble. Reluctantly drawn into this adventure, Kamen begins to fall in love with the fleeting Avé.
