Title: Intruders (2016)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Horror
Director: Adam Schindler
Main Cast: Beth Riesgraf, Martin Starr, Jack Kesy, Rory Culkin, Leticia Jimenez
Keywords: agoraphobia

Overview: Anna suffers from agoraphobia so crippling that when a trio of criminals break into her house, she cannot bring herself to flee. But what the intruders don't realize is that agoraphobia is not her only psychosis.
