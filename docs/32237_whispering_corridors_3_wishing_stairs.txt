Title: Whispering Corridors 3: Wishing Stairs (2003)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jae-yeon Yun
Main Cast: Park Han-byul, Hong Soo-a, Song Ji-hyo, Park Ji-yeon, Wu-jin Jang
Keywords: dance, private school, wish, high school, rivalry, paranormal, obesity, ballet, korea

Overview: A staircase leading to the dormitory of a remote boarding school usually has 28 stairs, but every so often there appears to be 29. When someone steps on the mysterious extra stair, the horror begins.
