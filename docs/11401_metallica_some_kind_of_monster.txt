Title: Metallica: Some Kind of Monster (2004)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Joe Berlinger
Main Cast: James Hetfield, Lars Ulrich, Kirk Hammett, Bob Rock, Robert Trujillo
Keywords: rock star, heavy metal, recording studio, psychologist, conflict, music band

Overview: Some Kind of Monster is a music documentary about Metallica's making of their album St. Anger and the difficulties they had to go through in the process. The directors shot over 1200 hours and followed the band around night and day for over a year to create this documentary.
