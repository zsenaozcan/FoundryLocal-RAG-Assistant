Title: The Rat Race (1960)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Robert Mulligan
Main Cast: Tony Curtis, Debbie Reynolds, Jack Oakie, Kay Medford, Don Rickles
Keywords: roommate, jazz musician

Overview: Tender romantic comedy about an aspiring musician who arrives in New York in search of fame &amp; fortune. He soon meets a taxi dancer, moves in with her, and before too long a romance develops.
