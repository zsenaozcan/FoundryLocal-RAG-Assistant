Title: David Golder (1931)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Julien Duvivier
Main Cast: Harry Baur, Paule Andral, Jackie Monnier, Jean Bradin, Gaston Jacquet
Keywords: drama

Overview: David is a poor but ambitious Polish Jew who reinvents himself as a powerful New York business magnate. After gaining wealth, he relocates to Paris, only to have his selfish and demanding wife squander his fortune.
