Title: Trémulo (2015)
Genres: Romance
Director: Roberto Fiesco
Main Cast: Benny Emmanuel, Axel Arenas, Alfonso Bravo, Pascacio López, Giovanna Zacarías
Keywords: dancing, mexico, piggy bank, independence, apprentice, kiss, soldier, haircut, spanish, barber shop

Overview: Carlos sweeps and mops the floor of an old-time barbershop. The day before Independence Day, among the customers is Julio. Their eyes meet, and when the barbershop closes Julio comes back for Carlos. They spend the night together, eating, chatting and even dancing, knowing full well that this encounter will be brief and short-lived.
