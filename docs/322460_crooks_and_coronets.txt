Title: Crooks and Coronets (1969)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jim O'Connolly
Main Cast: Telly Savalas, Edith Evans, Warren Oates, Cesar Romero, Harry H. Corbett
Keywords: heist

Overview: Two crooks are hired to rob an eccentric old lady's estate, but once they get to know her, they can't bring themselves to do it.
