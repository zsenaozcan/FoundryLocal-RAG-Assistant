Title: Detour (2009)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Severin Eskeland
Main Cast: Jens Hultén, Jeppe Beck Laursen, Severin Eskeland, Malin King, Mikkel Gaup
Keywords: snuff, woods, blood

Overview: Driving back to Norway, Lina and Martin reach a roadblock where a policeman tells them to take a detour deep into the Swedish forest. But soon one creepy incident after another leaves them stranded in the dark woods and everything seems much too bizarre to be accidental.
