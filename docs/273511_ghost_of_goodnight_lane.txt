Title: Ghost of Goodnight Lane (2014)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Alin Bijan
Main Cast: Lacey Chabert, Danielle Harris, Billy Zane, Matt Dallas, Christine Bently
Keywords: investigation, supernatural, paranormal

Overview: When the staff inside a renovated film studio finds a co-worker dead one morning, the pieces of a forty year puzzle add up to an angry ghost who has let the last person step inside her house. But will they ever get out alive?
