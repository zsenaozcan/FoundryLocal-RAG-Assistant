Title: Artificial Paradises (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Marcos Prado
Main Cast: Nathalia Dill, Luca Bianchi, Lívia de Bueno, Bernardo Melo Barreto

Overview: Artificial Paradises tells the story of Nando and Erika, two young people in their twenties who meet several times without noticing. It tells the story of the subculture of electronic music and rave parties and drugs like MDMA.
