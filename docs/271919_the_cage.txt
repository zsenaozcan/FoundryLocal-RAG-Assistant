Title: The Cage (1975)
Genres: Drama
Director: Pierre Granier-Deferre
Main Cast: Lino Ventura, Ingrid Thulin, William Sabatier, Sophie Sam, Jean Turlier

Overview: The story of a woman who is suddenly visited by her estranged ex-husband. The ex-hubby wants to buy her country house, which she received in the divorce settlement. Instead he finds himself locked in a cage in the cellar.
