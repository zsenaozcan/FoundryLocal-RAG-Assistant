Title: i hate myself :) (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Joanna Arnow
Main Cast: Joanna Arnow, James Kepple
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Nebbishy filmmaker Joanna Arnow documents her yearlong relationship with racially charged poet-provocateur James Kepple. What starts out as an uncomfortably intimate portrait of a dysfunctional relationship and protracted mid-twenties adolescence, quickly turns into a complex commentary on societal repression, sexuality and self-confrontation through art.
