Title: Cold Harbour (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Crime
Director: Carey McKenzie
Main Cast: Tony Kgoroge, Fana Mokoena, Nan Yu, Deon Lotz, Thomas Gumede
Keywords: woman director

Overview: While investigating a smugglers' turf war in Cape Town, township cop Sizwe discovers police corruption. He must set loyalty aside to act with integrity, alone.
