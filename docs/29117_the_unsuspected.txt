Title: The Unsuspected (1947)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: Joan Caulfield, Claude Rains, Audrey Totter, Constance Bennett, Hurd Hatfield
Keywords: radio, radio presenter, murder, film noir

Overview: A girl has been murdered. A woman cannot remember a man who claims to be her husband. Her uncle hosts a radio murder mystery show called "The Unsuspected". Who killed the girl? Why? And who is this mystery husband?
