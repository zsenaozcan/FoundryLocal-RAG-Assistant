Title: Athena (1954)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Richard Thorpe
Main Cast: Jane Powell, Debbie Reynolds, Virginia Gibson, Edmund Purdom, Vic Damone
Keywords: bodybuilder

Overview: A stuffy young lawyer's outlook on life drastically changes when he meets a perky health food enthusiast and her wacky family. This 1954 MGM musical, directed by Richard Thorpe, stars Jane Powell, Edmund Purdom, Debbie Reynolds, Vic Damone, Louis Calhern, Evelyn Varden, Linda Christian and Steve Reeves.
