Title: The Birth of the Tramp (2013)
Genres: Documentary, TV Movie
Director: Serge Bromberg
Main Cast: Peter Hudson, Kate Guyonvarch, David Robinson, Charlie Chaplin, Edna Purviance
Keywords: charlie chaplin, silent film star

Overview: A look back at Charlie Chaplin's early life and career, from his rough childhood and music hall success in England to his early Hollywood days and the development of his enormously popular "Little Tramp" character.
