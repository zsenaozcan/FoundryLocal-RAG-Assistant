Title: Guidance (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Pat Mills
Main Cast: Pat Mills, Alexandria Benoit, Zahra Bentham, Jennifer Goodhue, Kevin Hanchard

Overview: Fabricating credentials to score a last-ditch job as a high school guidance counsellor, a boozing, drug-addled former child star becomes an improbable hit with his students by dispensing the worst advice possible, in this hilarious reprobate comedy from writer-director-star Pat Mills.
