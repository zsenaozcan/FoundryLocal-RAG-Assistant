Title: The First Grader (2010)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Justin Chadwick
Main Cast: Naomie Harris, Tony Kgoroge, Nick Reding, Oliver Litondo, Alfred Munyua
Keywords: education, uniform, village, kenya, independent film, teacher, school, student

Overview: The true story of an 84 year-old Kenyan villager and ex Mau Mau freedom fighter who fights for his right to go to school for the first time to get the education he could never afford.
