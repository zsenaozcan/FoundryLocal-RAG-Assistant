Title: Death Is a Woman (1966)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: Frederic Goode
Main Cast: Trisha Noble, Mark Burns, Shaun Curry, William Dexter, Wanda Ventham
Keywords: female nudity, heroin, smuggling, bikini, spy spoof

Overview: Narcotics agent Dennis goes undercover in the Mediterranean Islands to investigate a smuggling operation, and gains the confidence of a woman who works for the ringleader. But Dennis ends up arrested for murder when the crime boss turns up dead.
