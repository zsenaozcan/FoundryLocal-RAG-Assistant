Title: Royal Flash (1975)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Romance
Director: Richard Lester
Main Cast: Malcolm McDowell, Alan Bates, Florinda Bolkan, Oliver Reed, Tom Bell
Keywords: sword fight, battle, cad

Overview: Cowardly rogue Harry Flashman's (Malcolm McDowell) schemes to gain entry to the royal circles of 19th-century Europe go nowhere until he meets a pair of devious nobles with their own agenda. At their urging, Flashman agrees to re-create himself as a bogus Prussian nobleman to woo a beautiful duchess. But the half-baked plan quickly comes unraveled, and he's soon on the run from several new enemies who are all calling for the rapscallion's head.
