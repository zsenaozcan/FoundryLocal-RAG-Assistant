Title: Fantozzi Retires (1988)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Neri Parenti
Main Cast: Paolo Villaggio, Milena Vukotic, Gigi Reder, Plinio Fernando, Anna Mazzamauro

Overview: After thirty years in the big corporation, Ugo Fantozzi retires. Suddenly, he needs things to do in everyday life and he tries a number of activities: helping Pina shopping; babysitting grand-daughter Uga; a trip to Venice; learning golf. He then fakes documents to get a new job, but in the end he becomes a hypochondriac and doesn't even take a long-awaited chance with Miss Silvani.
