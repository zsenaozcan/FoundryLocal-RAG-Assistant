Title: Face The Music (1993)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Carol Wiseman
Main Cast: Molly Ringwald, Patrick Dempsey, Lysette Anthony, Rebecca Potok, Danny Green

Overview: Divorced couple Charlie and Lisa Hunter once a song writing team decide to spend a weekend in the French countryside to inspire them to write one last song together. But there is one person who isn't too crazy about the idea... Charlie's fiance!
