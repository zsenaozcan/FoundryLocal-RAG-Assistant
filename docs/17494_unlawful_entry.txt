Title: Unlawful Entry (1992)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Jonathan Kaplan
Main Cast: Kurt Russell, Ray Liotta, Madeleine Stowe, Roger E. Mosley, Ken Lerner
Keywords: corruption, prostitute, sex, jealousy, fight, nightclub, nudity, police, stalker, murder

Overview: After a break-in at their house, a couple gets help from one of the cops that answered their call. He helps them install the security system, and begins dropping by on short notice and unofficial patrol, and spends a lot of time discussing the couple's problems with the wife. The husband begins wondering if they're getting too much help.
