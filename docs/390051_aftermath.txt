Title: Aftermath (2017)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Elliott Lester
Main Cast: Arnold Schwarzenegger, Scoot McNairy, Maggie Grace, Judah Nelson, Larry Sullivan
Keywords: airport, depression, secret, sadness, forgiveness, airplane, mental breakdown, investigation, vigilante, flight

Overview: A fatal plane crash changes the lives of Roman and Jake forever. Roman loses his wife and daughter in the accident, while Jake, his mind as he happens to be the air traffic controller, who fails to avert the nightmare. Rage and revenge engulfs Roman and Jake finds himself swamped with guilt and regret. Can the two make peace with the past?
