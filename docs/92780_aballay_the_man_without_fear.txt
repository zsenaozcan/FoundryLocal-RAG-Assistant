Title: Aballay, the Man without Fear (2010)
Genres: Drama, Western
Director: Fernando Spiner
Main Cast: Nazareno Casero, Pablo Cedrón, Claudio Rissi, Luis Ziembrowski, Mariana Anghileri
Keywords: argentinia, gaucho

Overview: Aballay was a bad tempered gaucho. After killing a man, the terrified look of the victim's son raised his consciousness about his savagery. Years go by, that kid's look doesn't leave him. Aballay knows that the kid will look for him.
