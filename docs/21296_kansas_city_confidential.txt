Title: Kansas City Confidential (1952)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Phil Karlson
Main Cast: John Payne, Coleen Gray, Preston Foster, Neville Brand, Lee Van Cleef
Keywords: florist, armored car, heist, armored car robbery, police shootout, film noir

Overview: A down-on-his-luck ex-GI finds himself framed for an armored car robbery. When he's finally released for lack of evidence--after having been beaten up and tortured by the police--he sets out to discover who set him up, and why. The trail leads him into Mexico and a web of hired killers and corrupt cops.
