Title: Chronically Metropolitan (2017)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Xavier Manrique
Main Cast: Shiloh Fernandez, Ashley Benson, Addison Timlin, Mary-Louise Parker, Chris Noth

Overview: A cynical and clever coming of age film about first time novelist Fenton Dillane, who, unannounced returns to New York City to confront his family, his ex- girlfriend and a few lingering childhood fears.
