Title: Charlie Chan at Monte Carlo (1937)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Eugene Forde
Main Cast: Warner Oland, Keye Luke, Virginia Field, Sidney Blackmer, Harold Huber
Keywords: monte carlo, charlie chan

Overview: Although Charlie and Lee are in Monaco for an art exhibit, they become caught up in a feud between rival financiers which involves the Chan's in a web of blackmail and murder.
