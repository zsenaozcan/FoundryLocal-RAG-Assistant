Title: Slaughterhouse (1987)
Genres: Horror
Director: Rick Roessler
Main Cast: Joe B. Barton, Don Barrett, Sherry Leigh, Jeff Grossi, Jane Higginson
Keywords: psychopathic killer, slaughterhouse, teenager, trespassing

Overview: The owner of a slaughterhouse facing foreclosure instructs his 350lbs, mentally retarded son to go on a killing spree against the people who want to buy his property.
