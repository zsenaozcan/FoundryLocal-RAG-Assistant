Title: Hadj Cheriff (1894)
Genres: Unknown
Director: William K.L. Dickson 
Main Cast: Hadji Cheriff
Keywords: silent film, acrobats

Overview: Hadji Cheriff, a performer known for a variety of unusual abilities, demonstrates part of his act in the Thomas Edison studio. He has a large knife in his hand at the opening of the act. He then hurls the knife away and begins a rapid series of dance-like motions, executing numerous cartwheels and whirling movements.
