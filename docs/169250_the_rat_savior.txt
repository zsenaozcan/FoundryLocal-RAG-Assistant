Title: The Rat Savior (1976)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror, Drama
Director: Krsto Papić
Main Cast: Ivica Vidović, Mirjana Majurec, Relja Bašić, Fabijan Sovagovic, Ilija Ivezić
Keywords: secret organization, writer, rich poor, rat

Overview: A poor writer discovers that a species of rats has banded together to impersonate humans and supplant them unnoticed, in a manner reminiscent of the transformations in Eugene Ionesco's Rhinoceros, or the covert conspiracy of pod-people in The Invasion of the Body Snatchers. This film's story is based on a book by the Soviet writer Alexander Greene.
