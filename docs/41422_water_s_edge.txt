Title: Water's Edge (2003)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Harvey Kahn
Main Cast: Nathan Fillion, Chandra West, Emmanuelle Vaugier, Daniel Baldwin

Overview: Robert wanted an escape from the big city. He thought a small town by the water's edge would give him a new start. He couldn't have been more wrong. This little town has dirty secrets, and Robert just found the worst of them...
