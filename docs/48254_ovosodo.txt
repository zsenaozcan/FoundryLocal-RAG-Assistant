Title: Ovosodo (1997)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Paolo Virzì
Main Cast: Edoardo Gabbriellini, Malcolm Lunghi, Nicoletta Braschi, Claudia Pandolfi, Alessio Fantozzi

Overview: The story of Piero, from when he was a kid to fatherhood, over the background of the town of Livorno, Tuscany, where the director Virzì was born.
