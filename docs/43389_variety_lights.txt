Title: Variety Lights (1950)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Music
Director: Federico Fellini
Main Cast: Giulietta Masina, Peppino De Filippo, Carla Del Poggio, John Kitzmiller, Folco Lulli

Overview: A beautiful and ambitious young woman joins a traveling troupe of third-rate vaudevillians and inadvertently causes jealousy and emotional crises.
