Title: The Singing Ringing Tree (1957)
Genres: Fantasy, Family
Director: Francesco Stefani
Main Cast: Christel Bodenstein, Eckart Dux, Richard Krüger, Dorothea Thiesing, Günther Polensen
Keywords: prince

Overview: Ein Märchen der Gebrüder Grimm.  In this East German fairy tale classic, a brave prince must seek the fabled singing, ringing tree in order to win the heart of a beautiful princess.
