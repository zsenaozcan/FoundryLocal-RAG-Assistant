Title: A Run for Your Money (1949)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Charles Frend
Main Cast: Donald Houston, Meredith Edwards, Moira Lister, Alec Guinness, Hugh Griffith

Overview: Two Welsh coal-mining brothers win a trip to London to claim a monetary prize. They are supposed to meet a newspaper reporter who will be their escort. Instead, the brothers are launched into an adventure with some London riff-raff. It is up to the reporter to look out for the brothers, and what a job it turns out to be!
