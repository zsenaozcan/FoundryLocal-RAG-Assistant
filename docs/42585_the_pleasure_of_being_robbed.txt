Title: The Pleasure of Being Robbed (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Joshua Safdie
Main Cast: Joshua Safdie, Ariel Schulman, Eléonore Hendricks, Wayne Chin, Dawn Glickman
Keywords: thief, independent film, comedy

Overview: A curious and lost Eleonore looks for something everywhere, even in the bags of strangers who find themselves sadly smiling only well after she's left their lives. They owe her their thanks.
