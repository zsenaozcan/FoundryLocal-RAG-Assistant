Title: Circle of Eight (2009)
Genres: Mystery, Horror
Director: Stephen Cragg
Main Cast: Austin Highsmith, Jesse Johnson, Ryan Doom, Josh Kelly, Katie Lowes
Keywords: horror, mystery, t-horror

Overview: Jessica moves into a loft on the eighth floor of a Los Angeles apartment building called The Dante. The other tenants all seem friendly at first, but when she starts witnessing horrible deaths, she must team up with her neighbor Evan (Doom) to solve the mystery -- and uncover her own connection to the place.
