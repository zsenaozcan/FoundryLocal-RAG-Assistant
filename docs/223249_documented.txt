Title: Documented (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jose Antonio Vargas
Main Cast: Jose Antonio Vargas
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In 2011, Pulitzer Prize-winning journalist Jose Antonio Vargas outed himself as an undocumented immigrant in the New York Times Magazine. 'Documented' chronicles his journey to America from the Philippines as a child; his journey through America as an immigration reform activist/provocateur; and his journey inward as he re-connects with his mother, whom he hasn't seen in 20 years.
