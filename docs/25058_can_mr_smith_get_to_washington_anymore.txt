Title: Can Mr. Smith Get to Washington Anymore? (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Joan Barry, Charles Brennan, Russ Carnahan, Howard Dean, Martin Duggan

Overview: This documentary follows the 2004 Missouri Democratic primary to replace retiring 28-year veteran and former House Majority Leader Dick Gephardt. It is told from inside the campaign of Jeff Smith, a 29-year old part-time political science instructor at Washington University.
