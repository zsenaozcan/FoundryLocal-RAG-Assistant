Title: The Terror Within (1989)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Thierry Notz
Main Cast: George Kennedy, Andrew Stevens, Starr Andreeff, Terri Treas, John Lafayette
Keywords: monster, mutant, biological weapon, post-apocalyptic, laboratory, vaccine, desert, walkie talkie, flame thrower, rattlesnake

Overview: Experiments in biological warfare have destroyed all but a few remnants of the human race. Alone in a lab, eight students work feverishly to create a vaccine before they are forced outside in search for food. It is then that the surviving scientists discover creatures mutated by the plague. Now they prepare to do battle against their worst fear: The Terror Within.
