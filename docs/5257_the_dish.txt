Title: The Dish (2000)
Genres: History, Comedy, Drama
Director: Rob Sitch
Main Cast: Sam Neill, Patrick Warburton, Kevin Harrington, Tom Long, Eliza Szonert
Keywords: nasa, australia, moon landing, independent film, moon walk

Overview: The true story of how the Parkes Radio Telescope was used to relay the live television of man's first steps on the moon, during the Apollo 11 mission in 1969.
