Title: Night School (1981)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Ken Hughes
Main Cast: Leonard Mann, Rachel Ward, Drew Snyder, Joseph R. Sicari, Nicholas Cairis
Keywords: boston, nudity, suspicion, machete, college, chase, ritual, surprise, investigation, love

Overview: A Boston police detective investigates a series of gruesome decapitations of various college coeds commited by a helmeted, black-leather clad serial killer which leads him to suspect a well known anthropology professor as well as his female live-in assistant.
