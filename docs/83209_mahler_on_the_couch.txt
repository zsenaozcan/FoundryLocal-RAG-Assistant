Title: Mahler on the Couch (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Percy Adlon
Main Cast: Johannes Silberschneider, Barbara Romaner, Karl Markovics, Friedrich Mücke, Eva Mattes
Keywords: infidelity, psychoanalysis, biography, music

Overview: Alma Mahler's affair with the young architect Walter Gropius sets in motion a marital drama that forces her husband Gustav Mahler to seek advice from Sigmund Freud.
