Title: Clown (2014)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jon Watts
Main Cast: Peter Stormare, Laura Allen, Elizabeth Whitmere, Christian Distefano, John MacDonald
Keywords: clown, boy, murder, blood, child murder, killer clown

Overview: A loving father finds a clown suit for his son's birthday party, only to realize the suit is part of an evil curse that turns its wearer into a killer.
