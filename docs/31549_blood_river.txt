Title: Blood River (2009)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Adam Mason
Main Cast: Andrew Howard, Tess Panzer, Ian Duncan, Sarah Essex, Dillon Borowski
Keywords: horror

Overview: A psychological thriller following a successful young married couple on their way to visit family. After a blowout on a desolate stretch of highway in Nevada, they head to the next town only to discover it long abandoned. Here they meet a mysterious stranger who seems to know decidedly more than he is sharing.
