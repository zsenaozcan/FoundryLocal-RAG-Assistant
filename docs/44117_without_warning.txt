Title: Without Warning (1994)
Genres: Action, Drama, Science Fiction, TV Movie
Director: Robert Iscove
Main Cast: Sander Vanocur, Jane Kaczmarek, Bree Walker, John de Lancie, Philip Baker Hall
Keywords: suspense

Overview: A television program is interupted by a news network announcing that three meteors have hit the United States, France and China. At first it seems natural but after interviews by scientists and eyewitness seems to suggest that it is not. Three more meteors are coming and the various Earth governments combine forces to stop them.
