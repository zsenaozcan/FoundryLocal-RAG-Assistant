Title: 5ive Days to Midnight (2004)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller, Drama, Action
Director: Michael W. Watkins
Main Cast: Timothy Hutton, Randy Quaid, Kari Matchett, Hamish Linklater, Angus Macfadyen

Overview: A physicist discovers a briefcase containing postdated documents and evidence which indicate he will die five days in the future.
