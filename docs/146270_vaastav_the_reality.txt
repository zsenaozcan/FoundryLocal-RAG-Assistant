Title: Vaastav: The Reality (1999)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Mahesh Manjrekar
Main Cast: Sanjay Dutt, Namrata Shirodkar, Ekta Sohini, Kashmira Shah, Mohnish Behl

Overview: "The Reality" as described by the film's tagline, refers to the harsh realities of life in the Mumbai underworld. The film is said to be loosely based on the life of Mumbai underworld gangster Chota Rajan.
