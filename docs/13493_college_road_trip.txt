Title: College Road Trip (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Roger Kumble
Main Cast: Martin Lawrence, Kym Whitley, Raven-Symoné, Brenda Song, Margo Harshman
Keywords: road trip

Overview: When an overachieving high school student decides to travel around the country to choose the perfect college, her overprotective cop father also decides to accompany her in order to keep her on the straight and narrow.
