Title: Ambush at Cimarron Pass (1958)
Genres: Action, War, Western
Director: Jodie Copelan
Main Cast: Scott Brady, Margia Dean, Clint Eastwood, Irving Bacon, Frank Gerstle
Keywords: mexican, confederate, southern accent, civil war veteran

Overview: A small Army patrol unit and a couple of former Confederates reluctantly throw in their lot together after being attacked by a band of Native Americans.
