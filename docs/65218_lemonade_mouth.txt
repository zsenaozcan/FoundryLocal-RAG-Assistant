Title: Lemonade Mouth (2011)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Patricia Riggen
Main Cast: Bridgit Mendler, Adam Hicks, Hayley Kiyoko, Naomi Scott, Blake Michael
Keywords: guitar, cat, narration, musical, violin, lemonade, detention, father daughter relationship, school assembly, woman director

Overview: The story follows five disparate high school students - Olivia, Mo, Charlie, Stella, and Wen who meet in detention. They realize they are destined to rock, and ultimately form a band that becomes a champion for students sidelined by the high school elite.
