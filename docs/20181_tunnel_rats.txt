Title: Tunnel Rats (2008)
Genres: Action, Drama, War
Director: Uwe Boll
Main Cast: Michael Paré, Wilson Bethel, Mitch Eakins, Erik Eidem, Brandon Fobbs

Overview: During the Vietnam War [1959-1975] a special US combat unit is sent out to hunt and kill the Viet Cong soldiers in a man-to-man combat in the endless tunnels underneath the jungle of Vietnam. Suicide squads of a special kind.
