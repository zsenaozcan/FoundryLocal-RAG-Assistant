Title: Juggernaut (1974)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Crime
Director: Richard Lester
Main Cast: Richard Harris, Omar Sharif, David Hemmings, Anthony Hopkins, Shirley Knight
Keywords: bomb, sea, ransom, interrogation

Overview: A bomber has planted 7 bombs on a transatlantic cruise ship. While a crack bomb squad team try to defuse the bombs the police attempt to track down the mad bomber.
