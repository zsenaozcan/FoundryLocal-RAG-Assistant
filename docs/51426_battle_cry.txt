Title: Battle Cry (1955)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: Van Heflin, Aldo Ray, Mona Freeman, James Whitmore, Nancy Olson
Keywords: world war ii, u.s. marine

Overview: The dramatic story of US marines in training, in combat and in love during World War II. The story centres on a major who guides the raw recruits from their training to combat. Based on the novel by Leon Uris.
