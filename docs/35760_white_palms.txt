Title: White Palms (2006)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Szabolcs Hajdu
Main Cast: Zoltán Miklós Hajdu, Kyle Shewfelt, Gheorghe Dinică, Andor Lukáts, Oana Pellea
Keywords: gymnastics, hungary, canada, rivalry, athlete, teacher, student, hungarian, communism, gymnast

Overview: Having suffered as a boy under a brutal Communist-era coach, champion Hungarian gymnast Miklos moves to Canada years later in search of a new start - only to find himself unwittingly perpetuating the very same cycle of abuse among his own pupils.
