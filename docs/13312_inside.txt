Title: Inside (2007)
Genres: Horror
Director: Alexandre Bustillo
Main Cast: Béatrice Dalle, Alysson Paradis, Nathalie Roussel, François-Régis Marchasson, Jean-Baptiste Tabourin
Keywords: baby, suspense, gore, pregnant woman, new french extremism

Overview: Four months after the death of her husband, a woman on the brink of motherhood is tormented in her home by a strange woman who wants her unborn baby.
