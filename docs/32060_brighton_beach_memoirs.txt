Title: Brighton Beach Memoirs (1986)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Gene Saks
Main Cast: Jonathan Silverman, Blythe Danner, Stacey Glick, Judith Ivey, Bob Dishy

Overview: Eugene, a young teenage Jewish boy, recalls his memoirs of his time as an adolescent youth. He lives with his parents, his aunt, two cousins, and his brother, Stanley, whom he looks up to and admires. He goes through the hardships of puberty, sexual fantasy, and living the life of a poor boy in a crowded house.
