Title: Between Two Worlds (1944)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy
Director: Edward A. Blatt
Main Cast: John Garfield, Paul Henreid, Eleanor Parker, Edmund Gwenn, Sydney Greenstreet
Keywords: ship, afterlife, fate, explosion, bombing, ship passengers

Overview: Passengers on an ocean liner can't recall how they got onboard or where they are going. Soon it becomes apparent that they all have something in common.
