Title: United (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Magnus Martens
Main Cast: Håvard Lilleheie, Berte Rommetveit, Severin Eskeland, Sondre Sørheim, Ole-Jørgen Nilsen

Overview: Kåre is a youth coach with aspirations to become the greatest football player ever like his idol, Bryan Robson. However, his dreams are starting to take a toll on his relationship with his girlfriend, Anna.
