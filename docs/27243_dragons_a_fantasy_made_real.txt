Title: Dragons: A Fantasy Made Real (2004)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: Justin Hardy
Main Cast: Tom Chadbon, Catherine Bach, Paul Hilton, Bradley Lavelle
Keywords: cave, mythology, museum, skeleton, sketch, computer animation, knight, dragon, scientist, scientific experiment

Overview: "Dragon's World: A Fantasy Made Real" is a nature mockumentary about a British scientific team that attempts to understand the unique the incredible beasts that have fascinated people for ages. CGI is used to create the dragons.
