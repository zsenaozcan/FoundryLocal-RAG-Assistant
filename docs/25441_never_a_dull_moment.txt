Title: Never a Dull Moment (1968)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Family
Director: Jerry Paris
Main Cast: Dick Van Dyke, Edward G. Robinson, Dorothy Provine, Henry Silva, Tony Bill
Keywords: artwork, mistaken identity, spectacle

Overview: When practicing for a role, actor Jack is mistaken for the killer Ace. He doesn't realize this until it's too late and is carried off to gangster boss Leo Smooth, who wants Ace to do a job for him. Fearing for his life, Jack plays his role, but always searching for a way out of the well-guarded house. - By: Tom Zoerner
