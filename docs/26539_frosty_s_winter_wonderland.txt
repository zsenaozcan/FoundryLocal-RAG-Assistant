Title: Frosty's Winter Wonderland (1976)
Genres: Animation
Director: Arthur Rankin, Jr.
Main Cast: Andy Griffith, Jackie Vernon, Paul Frees, Shelley Winters, Dennis Day
Keywords: holiday, snowman

Overview: Years have passed since Frosty left for the North Pole, but his promise is kept when he hears news of the first snowfall of the season, and decides to return.
