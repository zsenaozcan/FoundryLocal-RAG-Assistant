Title: The Most Wonderful Evening of My Life (1972)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Mystery
Director: Ettore Scola
Main Cast: Alberto Sordi, Charles Vanel, Claude Dauphin, Janet Ågren, Michel Simon

Overview: A successful businessman whose destiny leads him to a secluded Swiss chalet where his life is put on trial by a group of retired law professionals. The men assemble to analyze Sordi's rise to power and his increasingly immoral behavior as he attained success, and the warped perceptions of right and wrong he has adopted to remain successful.
