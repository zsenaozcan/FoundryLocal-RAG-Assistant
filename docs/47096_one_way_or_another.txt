Title: One Way or Another (1976)
Genres: Drama
Director: Elio Petri
Main Cast: Gian Maria Volonté, Marcello Mastroianni, Mariangela Melato, Ciccio Ingrassia, Franco Citti
Keywords: suicide, confession, sin, religion, sexual repression

Overview: Set during a retreat of Christian Democrat politicians who practice spiritual exercises together, it is an allegory of corrupted power. Disturbing, claustrophobic settings are the background to a series of mysterious crimes.
