Title: The Devil's Widow (1970)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Roddy McDowall
Main Cast: Ava Gardner, Ian McShane, Richard Wattis, Cyril Cusack, Stephanie Beacham
Keywords: witchcraft, swinging 60s

Overview: Based on an ancient Scottish folk song, an older woman uses witchcraft to keep her young jet-set friends.
