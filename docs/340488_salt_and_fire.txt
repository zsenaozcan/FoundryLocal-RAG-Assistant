Title: Salt and Fire (2016)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Werner Herzog
Main Cast: Michael Shannon, Veronica Ferres, Gael García Bernal, Werner Herzog, Anita Briem
Keywords: airport, volcano, ecology, south america, environmental
