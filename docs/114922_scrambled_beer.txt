Title: Scrambled Beer (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Foreign
Director: Cristobal Valderrama
Main Cast: Diego Muñoz, Nicolás Saavedra, Javiera Díaz de Valdés, Manuela Martelli

Overview: After moving into a new house with his friend, Vladimir starts to notice that he awakes every day in a different week as if he has traveled trough time. Could it be the popular local drink -- beer with eggs?
