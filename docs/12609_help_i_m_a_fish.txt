Title: Help! I'm A Fish (2000)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Comedy
Director: Stefan Fjeldmark
Main Cast: Alan Rickman, Terry Jones, Paprika Steen, Morten Kern Nielsen, Aaron Paul
Keywords: parent child relationship, fish, transformation, house, mad scientist, underwater

Overview: Three children accidentally get turned into fish after drinking a potion made by an eccentric scientist. The kids end up in the sea, with one problem. They must find and drink the antidote within 48 hours, or forever remain as fish.
