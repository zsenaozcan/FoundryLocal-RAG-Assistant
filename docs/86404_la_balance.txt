Title: La balance (1982)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Bob Swaim
Main Cast: Nathalie Baye, Philippe Léotard, Richard Berry, Maurice Ronet, Bernard Freyd
Keywords: paris, prostitute, detective

Overview: A Paris police detective (Richard Berry) plays rough with a prostitute (Nathalie Baye) and her pimp/lover (Philippe Léotard), whom he wants as an informant.
