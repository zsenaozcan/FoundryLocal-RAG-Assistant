Title: Barcelona (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Whit Stillman
Main Cast: Taylor Nichols, Chris Eigeman, Mira Sorvino, Tushka Bergen, Pep Munné
Keywords: barcelona spain, navy, independent film

Overview: During the 1980s, uptight Ted Boynton is a salesman working in the Barcelona office of a Chicago-based company. He receives an unexpected visit from his cousin Fred, a naval officer who has come to Spain on a public relations mission for a U.S. fleet. Not exactly friends in the past, Ted and Fred strike up relationships with women in the Spanish city and experience conflicts -- Ted with his employer, and Fred with the Barcelona community.
