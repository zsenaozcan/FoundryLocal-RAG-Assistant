Title: R.O.D - Read or Die (2001)
Genres: Science Fiction, Animation, Foreign, Action, Adventure, Mystery
Director: Koji Masunari
Main Cast: Rieko Miura, Michiko Neya, Masami Iwasaki, Hozumi Gouda, Hiroki Takahashi
