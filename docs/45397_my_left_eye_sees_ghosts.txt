Title: My Left Eye Sees Ghosts (2002)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Comedy, Science Fiction, Foreign
Director: Johnnie To
Main Cast: Sammi Cheng, Lau Ching-Wan, Cherrie Ying, Lee San-San, Lam Suet

Overview: A car accident gives a spoiled, troubled young woman the ability to see ghosts, including one of a former classmate.
