Title: She Was Like a Wild Chrysanthemum (1955)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Keisuke Kinoshita
Main Cast: Chishu Ryu, Haruko Sugimura, Takahiro Tamura, Toshio Kobayashi, Kappei Matsumoto

Overview: Now an old man, Masao returns to his childhood home and begins to recall his upbringing in this abandoned sector of Japan.
