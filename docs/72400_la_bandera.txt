Title: La Bandera (1935)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Julien Duvivier
Main Cast: Jean Gabin, Annabella, Margo Lion, Reine Paulet, Viviane Romance

Overview: Pierre Gilieth has committed a murder in Paris. He flees to Barcelona, where he runs out of money. So he joins the Spanish Foreign Legion. He meets there two fellow countrymen, Mulot and Lucas. He tries to forget his fault... but Lucas's friendship soon appears to be less unselfish...
