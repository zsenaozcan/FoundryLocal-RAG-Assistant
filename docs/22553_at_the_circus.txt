Title: At the Circus (1939)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Edward Buzzell
Main Cast: Groucho Marx, Chico Marx, Harpo Marx, Kenny Baker, Florence Rice
Keywords: circus, musical, aunt, money, lawyer, strong man

Overview: Jeff Wilson, the owner of a small circus, owes his partner Carter $10,000. Before Jeff can pay, Carter lets his accomplices steal the money, so he can take over the circus. Antonio Pirelli and Punchy, who work at the circus, together with lawyer Loophole try to find the thief and get the money back.
