Title: Fate (2001)
Genres: Drama
Director: Zeki Demirkubuz
Main Cast: Zeynep Tokuş, Serdar Orçin, Engin Gunaydin, Demir Karahan, Feridun Koc
Keywords: turkey, fate

Overview: Fate (Turkish: Yazgı) is a 2001 Turkish drama film directed and screen-written by Zeki Demirkubuz based on the Albert Camus novel L'Étranger... It was screened in the Un Certain Regard section at the 2002 Cannes Film Festival.
