Title: A Soldier's Sweetheart (1998)
Genres: TV Movie, War, Drama
Director: Thomas Michael Donnelly
Main Cast: Kiefer Sutherland, Skeet Ulrich, Georgina Cates, Daniel London, Louis Vanaria

Overview: An Army medic brings his girlfriend to stay with him at an out of the way Vietnam outpost in 1967, the woman disappears one day and he begins searching for her.
