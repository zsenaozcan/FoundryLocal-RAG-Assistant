Title: The Gray Man (2007)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Scott L. Flynn
Main Cast: Patrick Bauchau, Jack Conley, John Aylward, Jillian Armenante, Silas Weir Mitchell
Keywords: serial killer, child murder

Overview: Early 20th century. Albert Fish, a seemingly benevolent father and grandfather who reared his family by himself after his wife deserted them, turns out to be a serial child molester and murderer. Based on a true story.
