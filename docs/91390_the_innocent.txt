Title: The Innocent (1993)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: John Schlesinger
Main Cast: Anthony Hopkins, Isabella Rossellini, Campbell Scott, Ronald Nitschke, James Grant

Overview: A young engineer is sent to post-WWII Berlin to help the Americans in spying on the Russians. In a time and place where discretion is still a man’s best friend, he falls in love with a mysterious woman who will take him on the dark side of evil.
