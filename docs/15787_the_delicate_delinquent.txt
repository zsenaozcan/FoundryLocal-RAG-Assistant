Title: The Delicate Delinquent (1957)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Don McGuire
Main Cast: Jerry Lewis, Darren McGavin, Martha Hyer, Robert Ivers, Horace McMahon
Keywords: delinquency, juvenile delinquent, theremin

Overview: Sidney Pythias (Jerry Lewis) is the a bumbling janitor picked up by cop Darren McGavin as a teenage gang member worth saving from delinquency. With McGavin's help, Sidney works his way through the Police Academy to become a cop too.
