Title: Septembers of Shiraz (2015)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Wayne Blair
Main Cast: Adrien Brody, Salma Hayek, Gabriella Wright, Shohreh Aghdashloo, Bashar Rahal

Overview: In this adaptation of the critically acclaimed debut novel by Iranian American author Dalia Sofer, a secular Jewish family is caught up in the maelstrom of the 1979 Iranian Revolution.
