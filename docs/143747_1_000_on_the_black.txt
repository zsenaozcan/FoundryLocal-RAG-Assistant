Title: $1,000 on the Black (1966)
Genres: Western
Director: Alberto Cardone
Main Cast: Anthony Steffen, Gianni Garko, Erika Blanc, Franco Fantasia, Sieghardt Rupp

Overview: Johnny Liston has just been released from prison where he has wrongly served twelve years for a murder. He returns to Campos, the village of his birth, where the whole town is terrorized by Johnny's brother, Sartana, who has taken Manuela, Johnny's fiancèe to be his lover. With his only friend a mute, Johnny must seize all opportunity to find out why he was framed, who was involved and why he is not welcome. source: SWDB www.spaghetti-western.net
