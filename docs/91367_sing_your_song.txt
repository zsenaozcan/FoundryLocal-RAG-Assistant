Title: Sing Your Song (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Susanne Rostock	
Main Cast: Harry Belafonte, Sidney Poitier, Marge Champion, Fran Scott Attaway, Julian Bond
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Most people know the lasting legacy of Harry Belafonte, the entertainer. This film unearths his significant contribution to and his leadership in the civil rights movement in America and to social justice globally.
