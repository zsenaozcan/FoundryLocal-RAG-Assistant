Title: Mother Carey's Chickens (1938)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Rowland V. Lee
Main Cast: Fay Bainter, Anne Shirley, Ruby Keeler, James Ellison, Walter Brennan
Keywords: wife husband relationship, navy, widow

Overview: Fay Bainter stars in this 1938 film, based on the novel by Kate Douglas Wiggin, about a financially-strapped mother and her children who relocate from the city to a small rural town. It was remade by Walt Disney in 1963 as "Summer Magic".
