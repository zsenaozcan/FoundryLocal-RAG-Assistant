Title: Gaslight (1940)
Genres: Thriller, Mystery
Director: Thorold Dickinson
Main Cast: Adolf Wohlbrück, Diana Wynyard, Frank Pettingell, Cathleen Cordell, Robert Newton
Keywords: marriage, murder, driven mad, rubies

Overview: Twenty years after the murder of Alice Barlow, her house is finally occupied again. However, the husband of the couple who have moved in has a secret which he will do anything to keep hidden.
