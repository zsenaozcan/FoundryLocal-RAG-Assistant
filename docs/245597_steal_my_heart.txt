Title: Steal My Heart (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Lee Hyeon-jong
Main Cast: Kim Ah-joong, Joo Won, Ju Jin-mo, Baek Do-bin, Bae Seong-woo

Overview: "Catch Me" is about Lee Ho-tae (Joo Won) who meets the first love of his dreams, Yoon Jin-sook (Kim Ah-joong) for the first time in ten years but is shocked to find out she's a hit-and-runner.
