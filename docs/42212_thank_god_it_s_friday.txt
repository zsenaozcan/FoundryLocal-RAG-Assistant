Title: Thank God It's Friday (1978)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Robert Klane
Main Cast: Jeff Goldblum, Ray Vitte, Debra Winger, Valerie Landsburg, Terri Nunn
Keywords: dance contest, disco

Overview: It's Friday and everyone is going to the hot new disco. The Commodore's are scheduled to play if Floyd shows up with the instruments and Nicole dreams of becoming a disco star. Other characters are there to win the dance contest, or to put a little excitement into a fifth anniversary.
