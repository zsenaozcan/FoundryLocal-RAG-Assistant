Title: Cold Hell (2017)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Stefan Ruzowitzky
Main Cast: Violetta Schurawlow, Tobias Moretti, Robert Palfrader, Sammy Sheik, Friedrich von Thun
Keywords: martial arts, serial killer

Overview: A young Turkish woman living in Vienna feels increasingly lonely after she witnesses a murder and finds herself next on the killer's agenda in this smart and gritty thriller from the director of ANATOMY and the Oscar-winning THE COUNTERFEITERS.
