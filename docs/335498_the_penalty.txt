Title: The Penalty (1941)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Harold S. Bucquet
Main Cast: Edward Arnold, Lionel Barrymore, Marsha Hunt, Robert Sterling, Gene Reynolds
Keywords: farm, on the run, bank robbery

Overview: In this crime drama, a ruthless gangster's son is soon following in his father's footsteps. When his daddy kills an FBI agent and a cabby, the boy sees it all. Fortunately the courts intervene and send the lad off to live with a family of farmers.
