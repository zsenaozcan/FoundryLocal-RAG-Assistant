Title: Honeymoon (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jan Hřebejk
Main Cast: Anna Geislerová, Kristýna Nováková, Stanislav Majer, Juraj Nvota, Daniela Choderová

Overview: A wedding is supposed to be a joyous occasion, but things can go awry. When an uninvited guest turns up at the church and later at the reception as well, the newly-weds’ day turns sour. Shadows of the past can blight the most sumptuous of banquets and rend the most affectionate of embraces.
