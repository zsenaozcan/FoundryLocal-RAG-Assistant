Title: The Giants (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bouli Lanners
Main Cast: Zacharie Chasseriaud, Martin Nissen, Paul Bartel, Marthe Keller, Karim Leklou
Keywords: drug dealer, summer, teenager

Overview: Brothers Danny and Zak, 15 and 13 &amp; 3/4 are spending the summer in their deceased grandfather's house, waiting in vain for their mother, who is otherwise busy, and running low on cash. To make some money they decide to rent out the house to a local drug dealer, but things don't go exactly as planned...
