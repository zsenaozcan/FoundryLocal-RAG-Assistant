Title: Stars & Bars (1988)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Pat O'Connor
Main Cast: Daniel Day-Lewis, Harry Dean Stanton, Joan Cusack, Kent Broadhurst, Maury Chaykin

Overview: Henderson Dores (Daniel Day Lewis), a New York based art dealer from England, travels to Georgia to persuade patriarch, Harry Dean Stanton, to sell a Monet previously thought lost.
