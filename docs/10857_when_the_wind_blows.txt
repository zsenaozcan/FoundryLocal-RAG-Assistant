Title: When the Wind Blows (1986)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Drama, War
Director: Jimmy T. Murakami
Main Cast: Peggy Ashcroft, John Mills, Robin Houston, David Dundas
Keywords: england, rain, firemen, dying and death, disaster, adult animation, devastation, radiation sickness, fallout, nuclear catastrophe

Overview: With the help of government-issued pamphlets, an elderly British couple build a shelter and prepare for an impending nuclear attack, unaware that times and the nature of war have changed from their romantic memories of World War II
