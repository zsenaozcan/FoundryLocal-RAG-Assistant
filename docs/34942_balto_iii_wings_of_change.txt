Title: Balto III: Wings of Change (2004)
Genres: Family, Adventure, Animation
Director: Phil Weinstein
Main Cast: Sean Astin, Jodi Benson, Keith Carradine, Bill Fagerbakke, Charles Fleischer

Overview: Balto and the other sled dogs are feeling dejected because the mail that used to be delivered by dogsled is now being delivered by airplanes. But when a mail plane crashes in the mountains, the dogs come to the rescue.
