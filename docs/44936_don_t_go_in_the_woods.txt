Title: Don't Go In the Woods (1981)
Genres: Horror
Director: James Bryan
Main Cast: Jack McClelland, Mary Gail Artz, Angie Brown, Ken Carter, James P. Hayden
Keywords: slasher, violence, camper, video nasty

Overview: Four backpackers decide to take a hike in the mountains of Utah. But within the woods lurks a killer. But who...or what...is it? The lazy local sheriff blames bears. But the escalating body count seems to point to a human killer. Ignoring the warning signs, our campers remain lost in the woods...alone...awaiting their fate.
