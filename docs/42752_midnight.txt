Title: Midnight (1934)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Chester Erskine
Main Cast: Sidney Fox, O.P. Heggie, Henry Hull, Margaret Wycherly, Lynne Overman
Keywords: death sentence

Overview: Jury foreman Edward Weldon's questioning leads to the death sentence for Ethel Saxon. His daughter Stella claims to have killed her lover, the gangster Garboni, just as Saxon was to sit in the electric chair.
