Title: When Night Falls (2007)
Genres: Foreign, Horror, Thriller
Director: Alex Galvin
Main Cast: Tania Nolan, Rosella Hart, Kevin Keys, Sylvia Rands, Gerald Bryan

Overview: A Thriller set in 1932 about two nurses trapped in a country mansion with their invalid patient whilst a killer is on the loose. A series of unexplainable events start to occur and the nurses begin to wonder if they are no longer alone.
