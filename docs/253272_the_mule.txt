Title: The Mule (2014)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Tony Mahony
Main Cast: Hugo Weaving, Angus Sampson, Ewen Leslie, Leigh Whannell, Georgina Haig
Keywords: drug mule, drug

Overview: In 1983, a naive man is detained by Australian Federal Police with lethal narcotics hidden in his stomach. After being apprehended, ‘The Mule’ makes a desperate choice... to defy his bodily functions and withhold the evidence – literally.
