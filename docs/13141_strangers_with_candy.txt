Title: Strangers with Candy (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Paul Dinello
Main Cast: Amy Sedaris, Stephen Colbert, Paul Dinello, Greg Hollimon, Philip Seymour Hoffman
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A prequel to the critically acclaimed series featuring Jerri Blank, a 46 year-old ex-junkie, ex-con who returns to high school in a bid to start her life over.
