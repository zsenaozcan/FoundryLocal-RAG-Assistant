Title: The Hope Factory (2014)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Natalya Meshchaninova
Main Cast: Stepan Devonin, Daria Savelieva, Polina Shanina, Daniil Steklov, Maxim Stoyanov

Overview: Industrial city of Norilsk: factories, cold, chemical air. The only desire of young people living here is to leave, against all odds. A docu-style, emotionally-driven drama about a young girl desperately fighting for an escape which is so blurry, and for love which is so insecure.
