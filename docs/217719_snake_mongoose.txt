Title: Snake & Mongoose (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Wayne Holloway
Main Cast: Jesse Williams, Susie Abromeit, Noah Wyle, Ian Ziering, John Heard

Overview: The untold story of how Southern California drag racers, Don "The Snake" Prudhomme and Tom "The Mongoose" McEwen combined with corporate giants to change the face of sports and ultimately became the most famous rivalry in racing history.
