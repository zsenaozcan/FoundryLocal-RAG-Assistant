Title: Marine Boy (2009)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Jong-seok Yoon
Main Cast: Kim Kang-woo, Jo Jae-hyeon, Park Si-yeon, Kim Joon-bae

Overview: Marine Boy is the story of a former national swimmer who finds himself in debt, and out of desperation is employed as a mule by a gangster boss, smuggling drugs by sea.
