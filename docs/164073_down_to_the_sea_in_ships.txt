Title: Down to the Sea in Ships (1949)
Genres: Drama
Director: Henry Hathaway
Main Cast: Richard Widmark, Lionel Barrymore, Dean Stockwell, Cecil Kellaway, Gene Lockhart
Keywords: whaling, ship captain

Overview: A re-make of the 1922 silent film that details the lives of the people in a small Quaker community and the adventures of a whaling ship.
