Title: Dog (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Esen Isik
Main Cast: Salih Bademci, Cemal Toktas, Hakan Karsak, Baris Atay, Cagla Akalin
Keywords: city, transgender, woman director, episode film

Overview: An ordinary day in the megacity of Istanbul: Ten-year-old Cemo sells paper tissues in the streets, Hayat is controlled by her husband and transsexual Ebru sells her body. All three have a secret love and they do everything that satisfies their longing, if only for a moment. An authentic, delicate and unsettling story about love and death and the Turkish society at the beginning of the 21st century.
