Title: Sunshine on Leith (2013)
Genres: Music, Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Dexter Fletcher
Main Cast: George MacKay, Kevin Guthrie, Freya Mavor, Antonia Thomas, Jane Horrocks
Keywords: musical

Overview: Davy and Ally have to re-learn how to live life in Edinburgh after coming home from serving in Afghanistan. Both struggle to learn to live a life outside the army and to deal with the everyday struggles of family, jobs and relationships. Sunshine on Leith is based on the sensational stage hit of the same name, featuring music by pop-folk band The Proclaimers.
