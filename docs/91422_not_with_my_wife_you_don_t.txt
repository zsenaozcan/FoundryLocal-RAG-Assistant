Title: Not With My Wife, You Don't! (1966)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Norman Panama
Main Cast: Tony Curtis, Virna Lisi, George C. Scott, Carroll O'Connor, Richard Eastham
Keywords: pilot

Overview: During the Korean War, Italian nurse Virna Lisi falls in love with two American fliers, Tony Curtis and George C. Scott. Lisi marries Curtis after he convinces her that Scott has been killed in a plane crash. She soon discovers Scott is alive, but remains happily married to Curtis until Scott re-enters their lives 14 years later. Written by Jeanne Armintrout
