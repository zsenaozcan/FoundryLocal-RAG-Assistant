Title: M (1951)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Joseph Losey
Main Cast: David Wayne, Howard Da Silva, Martin Gabel, Luther Adler, Steve Brodie
Keywords: child killer, film noir

Overview: Remake of the 1931 original. In the city, someone is murdering children. The Police search is so intense, it is disturbing the 'normal' criminals, and the local hoods decide to help find the murderer as quickly as possible.
