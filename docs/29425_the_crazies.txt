Title: The Crazies (1973)
Genres: Action, Horror, Thriller
Director: George A. Romero
Main Cast: Lane Carroll, Will MacMillan, Harold Wayne Jones, Lynn Lowry, Lloyd Hollar
Keywords: pennsylvania, small town, biological weapon, quarantine, murder, independent film, blood, disease, scientist, epidemic

Overview: Citizens of a small town are infected by a biological weapon that causes its victims to become violently insane. As uninfected citizens struggle to survive, the military readies its own response.
