Title: Attraction (2017)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Fyodor Bondarchuk
Main Cast: Rinal Mukhametov, Sergey Garmash, Oleg Menshikov, Irina Starshenbaum, Nikita Kukushkin
Keywords: ufo, extraterrestrial, alien, ufo crash site, alien ship
