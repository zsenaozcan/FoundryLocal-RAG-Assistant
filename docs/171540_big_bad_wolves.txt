Title: Big Bad Wolves (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Navot Papushado
Main Cast: Lior Ashkenazi, Kais Nashif, Tzahi Grad, Dvir Benedek, Ami Weinberg
Keywords: suspense, series of murders

Overview: A series of brutal murders puts the lives of three men on a collision course: The father of the latest victim now out for revenge, a vigilante police detective operating outside the boundaries of law, and the main suspect in the killings - a religious studies teacher arrested and released due to a police blunder.
