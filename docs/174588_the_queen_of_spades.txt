Title: The Queen of Spades (1916)
Genres: Horror, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Yakov Protazanov
Main Cast: Iwan Mosschuchin, Vera Orlova, George Azagarov, Tatiana Duva, Nikolai Panov

Overview: While hosting a game of cards one night, Narumov tells his friends a story about his grandmother, a Countess. As a young woman, she had once incurred an enormous gambling debt, which she was able to erase by learning a secret that guaranteed that she could win by playing her cards in a certain order...
