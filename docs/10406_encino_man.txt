Title: Encino Man (1992)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Les Mayfield
Main Cast: Sean Astin, Brendan Fraser, Pauly Shore, Megan Ward, Robin Tunney
Keywords: stone age, prehistoric, los angeles, cavemen, prehistoric man

Overview: High school misfits Stoney and Dave discover a long-frozen primeval man from the past in their back yard. But the thawed-out Link, as the boys have named him, quickly becomes a wild card in the teens' already zany Southern California lives. After a shave and some new clothes, Link's presence at school makes the daily drudgery a lot more interesting.
