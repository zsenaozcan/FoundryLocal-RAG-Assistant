Title: The Girl and the Fox (2011)
Genres: Animation, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Tyler J. Kupferer
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: fox, snow

Overview: Ilona is a nine-year-old girl who lives in the wilderness with her mother and father. Food is running low, and when a mysterious fox starts killing their livestock, she has no choice but to track down the strange creature in order to ensure the survival of her family.
