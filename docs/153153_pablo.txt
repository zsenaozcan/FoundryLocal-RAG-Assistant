Title: Pablo (2012)
Genres: Animation, Documentary
Director: Richard Goldgewicht
Main Cast: Jeff Bridges, Andy García, Jon Voight, Anjelica Huston, Robert Downey Sr.
Keywords: biography

Overview: Pablo blends documentary and animation elements to tell the saga of "famous unknown" Pablo Ferro, a man with a personal journey that spans from Havana, during the pre-Cuban revolution to his current home, in the garage behind his son's house.
