Title: Beware the Slenderman (2016)
Genres: Crime, Documentary
Director: Irene Taylor Brodsky
Main Cast: Richard Dawkins
Keywords: documentary, crime, woman director, slender man

Overview: In this horrifyingly modern fairytale lurks an online Boogeyman and two 12-year-old girls who would kill for him. The entrance to the internet quickly leads to its darkest basement. How responsible are our children for what they find there?
