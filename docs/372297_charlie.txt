Title: Charlie (2015)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Martin Prakkat
Main Cast: Parvathy Thiruvothu, Dulquer Salmaan, Aparna Gopinath, Chemban Vinod Jose, Nedumudi Venu
Keywords: travelling person, comic book, arts, love, suspense, mystery, jinn, faked death, malayalam, hippy

Overview: A young, nonconforming woman named Tessa gets entangled in a cat-and-mouse chase in the by-lanes of Kerala, hunting for a mysterious artist who previously lived in her apartment.
