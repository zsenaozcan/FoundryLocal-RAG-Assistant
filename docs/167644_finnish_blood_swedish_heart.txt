Title: Finnish Blood Swedish Heart (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Mika Ronkainen
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Rocker Kai Latvalehto has a wife and a son, almost everything a man could wish for. Still, he feels that something is missing. Accompanied by his father, he drives across Sweden to refresh his childhood memories of life in Gothenburg in the 1970’s. This journey of identities brings the two men closer.
