Title: The Bannen Way (2010)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Thriller
Director: Jesse Warren
Main Cast: Mark Gantt, Vanessa Marcil, Gabriel Tigerman, Michael Ironside, Robert Forster

Overview: The Bannen Way is a slick, sexy, action-packed show about Neal Bannen, a charming con-man with a police chief for a father, a mob boss for an uncle and a weakness for beautiful women, who wants to turn his life around and leave the criminal lifestyle forever.
