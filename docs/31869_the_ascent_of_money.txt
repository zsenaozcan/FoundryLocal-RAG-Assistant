Title: The Ascent of Money (2009)
Genres: History, Documentary
Director: Adrian Pennick
Main Cast: Niall Ferguson
Keywords: capitalism, global economy, banking, money, debt, economics, financial crisis, stock market, corporation, federal reserve

Overview: British historian and author Niall Ferguson explains how big money works today as well as the causes of and solutions to economic catastrophes in this extended version The Ascent of Money documentary. Through interviews with top experts, such as former Federal Reserve Chairman Paul Volcker and American currency speculator George Soros, the intricate world of finance, including global commerce, banking and lending, is examined thoroughly.
