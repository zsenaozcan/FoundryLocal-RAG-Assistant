Title: Looking: The Movie (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Andrew Haigh
Main Cast: Jonathan Groff, Frankie J. Alvarez, Murray Bartlett, Lauren Weedman, Raúl Castillo
Keywords: gay, san francisco, friendship, love, romance, lgbt, queer

Overview: Patrick returns to San Francisco for the first time in almost a year to celebrate a momentous event with his old friends. In the process, he must face the unresolved relationships he left behind and make difficult choices about what’s important to him.
