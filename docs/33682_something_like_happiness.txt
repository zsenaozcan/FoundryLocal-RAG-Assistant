Title: Something Like Happiness (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bohdan Sláma
Main Cast: Tatiana Vilhelmová, Pavel Liška, Anna Geislerová, Marek Daniel

Overview: Over the course of a year, in a small town scarred and beaten down by industrialization, Tonda and Monika, friends since childhood, come to experience what life might be like if they could be with one another – which they seemingly can't. Or can they?
