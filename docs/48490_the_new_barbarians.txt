Title: The New Barbarians (1983)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller, Action
Director: Enzo G. Castellari
Main Cast: Giancarlo Prete, Fred Williamson, George Eastman, Anna Kanakis, Ennio Girolami

Overview: Two mercenaries help wandering caravans fight off an evil and aimless band of white-clad bikers after the nuclear holocaust.
