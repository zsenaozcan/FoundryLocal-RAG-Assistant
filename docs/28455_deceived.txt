Title: Deceived (1991)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Damian Harris
Main Cast: Goldie Hawn, John Heard, Tom Irwin, Kate Reid, Robin Bartlett
Keywords: bigamy, suspense, violence, intrigue, stolen identity

Overview: A marriage that seemed perfect comes crashing down after the supposed death of Jack Saunders, husband of Adrienne Saunders. After his supposed death, strange developments begin to be discovered by Adrienne regarding Jack's past. Developments that lead her to believe she has been Deceived.
