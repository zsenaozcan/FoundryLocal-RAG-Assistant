Title: Who's Minding the Store? (1963)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Frank Tashlin
Main Cast: Jerry Lewis, Jill St. John, Ray Walston, John McGiver, Agnes Moorehead
Keywords: department store, chaos, slapstick, romance

Overview: Jerry Lewis plays Norman Phiffer, a proud man in a humble life, who doesn't know that his girlfriend, Barbara, is heir to the Tuttle Department Store dynasty. Mrs. Tuttle, Barbara's mother, is determined to split the two lovers, and hires Norman in an attempt to humiliate him enough that Barbara leaves him. Will she ruin their love, or will he ruin her store?
