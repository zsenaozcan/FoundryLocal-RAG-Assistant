Title: Harvest: 3,000 Years (1976)
Genres: Drama
Director: Haile Gerima
Main Cast: Kasu Asfaw, Gebru Kasa, Worke Kasa, Melaku Makonen, Worke Kasa
Keywords: ethiopia

Overview: In this meditative film, the everyday lives of poor Ethiopian peasants are shown using documentary as well as storytelling techniques, and the drama arises out of the timeless but still contemporary issues of their lives.
