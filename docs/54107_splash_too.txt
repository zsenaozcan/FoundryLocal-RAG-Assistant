Title: Splash, Too (1988)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Family
Director: Greg Antonacci
Main Cast: Todd Waring, Amy Yasbeck, Donovan Scott, Dody Goodman, Rita Taggart

Overview: A sequel to the popular romantic mermaid drama, in which Allen and Madison return to New York - one to save his business, the other to save a Dolphin in captivity. You can guess which does which.
