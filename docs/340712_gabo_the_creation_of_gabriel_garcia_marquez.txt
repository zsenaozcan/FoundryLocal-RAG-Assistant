Title: Gabo: The Creation of Gabriel Garcia Marquez (2015)
Genres: History, Documentary
Director: Justin Webster
Main Cast: Juan Gabriel Vásquez, Jon Lee Anderson, Xavi Ayén, Carmen Balcells, Bill Clinton
Keywords: biography

Overview: 'Gabo, The Magic of Reality' is a story about the incredible power of human imagination, which follows the interwoven threads of Gabriel García Márquez's life and work - "Gabo" to all of Latin America - with the narrative tension of an investigation.
