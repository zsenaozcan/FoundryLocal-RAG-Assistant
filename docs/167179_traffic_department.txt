Title: Traffic Department (2013)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Wojciech Smarzowski
Main Cast: Bartłomiej Topa, Arkadiusz Jakubik, Julia Kijowska, Eryk Lubos, Robert Wabich
Keywords: adultery, politics, police, mafia, interrogation, poland, bribery, polish

Overview: Seven policemen, seven deadly sins, a murder case, secrets and the filth of everyday police work: Traffic Department transports the viewer into the darkest Warsaw streets.
