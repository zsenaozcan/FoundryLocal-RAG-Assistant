Title: Back to the Jurassic (2015)
Genres: Adventure, Family, Animation
Director: John Kafka
Main Cast: Jane Lynch, Melanie Griffith, Rob Schneider, William Baldwin, Stephen Baldwin

Overview: Ernie, Julia, and Max accidentally start a time-machine and find themselves back in time 65 million years, where they are adopted by a doting dinosaur mom and a rambunctious dinosaur brother while, back in the present day, their parents plot their rescue.
