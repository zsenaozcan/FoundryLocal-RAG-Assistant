Title: The Hardy Bucks Movie (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mike Cockayne
Main Cast: Martin Maloney, Chris Tordoff, Owen Colgan, Peter Cassidy, Tom Kilgallon

Overview: Eddie Durkan, the self-proclaimed leader of the 'Bucks is dreading spending another summer bored out of his skull. Ireland has qualified for the Euros in Poland, but with no money and apathetic mates, the task falls to Eddie to get them out of Castletown and onto the road in search of football and the craic.
