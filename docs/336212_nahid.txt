Title: Nahid (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ida Panahandeh
Main Cast: Sareh Bayat, Pejman Bazeghi, Navid Mohammad Zadeh, Milad Hossein Pour, Pouria Rahimi
Keywords: woman director
