Title: Son of Saul (2015)
Genres: War, Drama, Thriller
Director: László Nemes
Main Cast: Géza Röhrig, Levente Molnár, Urs Rechn, Todd Charmont, Jerzy Walczak
Keywords: prisoner, auschwitz, rabbi, son, hungarian, auschwitz-birkenau, sonderkommando

Overview: In the horror of 1944 Auschwitz, a prisoner forced to burn the corpses of his own people finds moral survival trying to save from the flames the body of a boy he takes for his son.
