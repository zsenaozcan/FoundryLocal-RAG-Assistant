Title: Titanic: The Final Word with James Cameron (2012)
Genres: Documentary, History
Director: Tony Gerber
Main Cast: James Cameron, Don Lynch, Parks Stephenson, Bernard Hill, Ewan Stewart
Keywords: titanic

Overview: Cameron brings together some the world's leading Titanic experts, including engineers, naval architects, artists and historians, to solve the lingering mysteries of why and how the 'unsinkable' ship sank.
