Title: Love Stories (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Tomas Portella
Main Cast: Dani Calabresa, Gregório Duvivier, Verônica Debom, Clarice Falcão, Zezé Polessa
Keywords: brazilian

Overview: Eduardo discovers he has a split personality: a buttoned-up São Paulo executive and beach-loving weekend playboy in Rio de Janeiro called Duca. Unconsciously, he becomes entangled with a woman although he's already engaged to another.
