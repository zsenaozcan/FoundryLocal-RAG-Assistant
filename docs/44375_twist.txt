Title: Twist (2003)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jacob Tierney
Main Cast: Nick Stahl, Joshua Close, Gary Farmer, Michèle-Barbara Pelletier, Andre Noble
Keywords: independent film

Overview: In a gritty retelling of the Dickens's classic, Twist takes Oliver and the Artful Dodger out of the poorhouse and onto the streets, where junk is the currency and hustling is the game.
