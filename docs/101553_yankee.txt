Title: Yankee (1966)
Genres: Western
Director: Tinto Brass
Main Cast: Adolfo Celi, Mirella Martin, Tomás Torres, Víctor Israel, Valentino Macchi
Keywords: male nudity, hood, raft, spaghetti western, hung upside down, shave

Overview: A man referred to only as "Yankee" rides into a dying, desolate town in frontier New Mexico which is completely controlled by a man called the "Grand Cougar." Almost immediately, a battle for dominance ensues.
