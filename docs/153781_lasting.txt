Title: Lasting (2013)
Genres: Foreign, Drama, Romance
Director: Jacek Borcuch
Main Cast: Jakub Gierszał, Magdalena Berus, Ángela Molina, Andrzej Chyra, Joanna Kulig

Overview: Lasting is an emotional love story about Michał and Karina, a pair of Polish students who meet and fall in love with each other while working summer jobs in Spain. An unexpected nightmare brutally breaks into their carefree time in the heavenly landscape and throws their lives into chaos.
