Title: The Kingdom (1994)
Genres: Horror, Mystery
Director: Lars von Trier
Main Cast: Ernst-Hugo Järegård, Laura Christensen, Baard Owe, Kirsten Rolffes, Holger Juul Hansen
Keywords: hospital

Overview: The first part of a Danish miniseries that has been edited into two massive films, "Riget" is set in the neurosurgical ward of Copenhagen's Rigshospitalet, the city and country's main hospital, nicknamed "Riget". The show follows a number of characters, both staff and patients, as they encounter bizarre phenomena, both human and supernatural.
