Title: The African Queen (1951)
Genres: Adventure, War, Romance
Director: John Huston
Main Cast: Humphrey Bogart, Katharine Hepburn, Robert Morley, Peter Bull, Theodore Bikel
Keywords: africa, missionary, river, desperation, boat, patriotism, world war i, hippopotamus, giraffe, methodist church

Overview: At the start of the first World War, in the middle of Africa’s nowhere, a gin soaked riverboat captain is persuaded by a strong-willed missionary to go down river and face-off a German warship.
