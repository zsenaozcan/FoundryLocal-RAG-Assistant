Title: Paradise (2015)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Sina Ataeian Dena
Main Cast: Dorna Dibaj, Fariba Kamran, Dorsa Khanmohammad, Fatemeh Naghavi, Roya Afshar

Overview: Hanieh, a young teacher, lives in the household of her married sister in Tehran.  Increasingly exhausted by her paralysing everyday life and the two-faced reality she subliminally experiences as a woman, an employee, a younger sister, and unhappy partner, Hanieh struggles to find her way out.
