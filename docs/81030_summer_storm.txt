Title: Summer Storm (1944)
Genres: Drama
Director: Douglas Sirk
Main Cast: Linda Darnell, George Sanders, Anna Lee, Hugo Haas, Lori Lahner
Keywords: melodrama, russian peasant

Overview: Linda Darnell plays a beautiful Russian peasant in this moody Sirk melodrama, based on Chekhov's "The Shooting Party." Trying to pull herself out of serf-ish poverty, she works her charms on an engaged aristocrat (George Sanders) with tragic results.
