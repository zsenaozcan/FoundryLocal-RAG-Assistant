Title: October (Ten Days that Shook the World) (1928)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Sergei M. Eisenstein
Main Cast: Vladimir Popov, Vasili Nikandrov, Layaschenko, Boris Livanov, Mikholyev
Keywords: monarchy, russian revolution 1917, lenin, tzar, government, coup d'etat

Overview: October is Sergei M. Eisenstein's docu-drama about the 1917 October Revolution in Russia. Made ten years after the events and edited in Eisenstein's 'Soviet Montage' style, it re-enacts in celebratory terms several key scenes from the revolution.
