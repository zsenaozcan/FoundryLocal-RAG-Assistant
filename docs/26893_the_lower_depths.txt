Title: The Lower Depths (1957)
Genres: Drama
Director: Akira Kurosawa
Main Cast: Toshirō Mifune, Isuzu Yamada, Kyôko Kagawa, Ganjiro Nakamura, Minoru Chiaki
Keywords: independent film

Overview: In a run-down Edo tenement, an elderly man (Rokubei) and his bitter wife (Osugi) rent out rooms and beds to the poor. The tenants are gamblers, prostitutes, petty thieves and drunk layabouts, all struggling to survive.
