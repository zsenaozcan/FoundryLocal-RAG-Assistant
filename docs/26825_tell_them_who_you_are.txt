Title: Tell Them Who You Are (2004)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Mark Wexler
Main Cast: Mark Wexler, Haskell Wexler, Simon Wexler, Martin Sheen, Jane Fonda
Keywords: father son relationship, film making

Overview: The son of acclaimed cinematographer Haskell Wexler confronts his complex father by turning the camera on him. What results is a portrait of a difficult genius and a son's path out of the shadow of a famous father.
