Title: Occupant (2011)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Henry Miller
Main Cast: Van Hansis, Cody Horn, Thorsten Kaye, Jamie Harrold
Keywords: sanity, lawyer, psychological drama, landlord tenant relationship

Overview: 25 year old Danny Hill's grandmother just died giving Danny the chance to move into her enormous rent controlled apartment in Manhattan. Danny must lock himself in for twelve days before he can take over the lease. There's just one problem -- he may not be the only occupant.
