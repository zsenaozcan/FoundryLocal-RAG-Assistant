Title: Terry Pratchett: Choosing to Die (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Charlie Russell
Main Cast: Terry Pratchett
Keywords: euthanasia

Overview: In a frank and personal documentary, author Sir Terry Pratchett considers how he might choose to end his life. Diagnosed with Alzheimer's in 2008, Terry wants to know whether he might be able to end his life before his disease takes over.
