Title: Labyrinth (2002)
Genres: Adventure, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Miroslav Lekić
Main Cast: Dragan Nikolić, Maja Sabljić, Branislav Lečić, Katarina Radivojević, Svetozar Cvetković

Overview: The story evolves around the Arian sect. During the III century AD they have seriously shaken the very foundations of Christian world. It is also known that in this region they had a stronghold between towns of Sirmium and Belgrade. "Labyrinth" is not only the story about our times, but a story of our roots.
