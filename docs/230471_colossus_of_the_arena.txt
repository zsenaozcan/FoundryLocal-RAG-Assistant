Title: Colossus of the Arena (1962)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Drama
Director: Michele Lupo
Main Cast: Mark Forest, Scilla Gabel, Jon Chevron, José Greci, Erno Crisa

Overview: A powerful man posing as a gladiator in Rome's fourth century discovers a plan to put the beautiful Queen in prison, which he thwarts by exposing a sinister duke as a traitor.
