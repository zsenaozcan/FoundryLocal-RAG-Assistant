Title: Machines (2017)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Rahul Jain
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: factory, textile industry, india, machine, sweatshop

Overview: This portrayal of the rhythm of life and work in a gigantic textile factory in Gujarat, India, moves through the corridors and bowels of the enormously disorienting structure—taking the viewer on a journey of dehumanizing physical labor and intense hardship.
