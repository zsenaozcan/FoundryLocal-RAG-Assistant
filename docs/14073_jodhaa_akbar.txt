Title: Jodhaa Akbar (2008)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, History, Romance
Director: Ashutosh Gowariker
Main Cast: Hrithik Roshan, Aishwarya Rai Bachchan, Sonu Sood, Kulbhushan Kharbanda, Rajesh Vivek
Keywords: wife husband relationship, emperor, hindu, marriage crisis, epic, sword fight, domineering mother, mughal empire, rajput princess, marriage of alliance

Overview: Jodhaa Akbar is a sixteenth century love story about a marriage of alliance that gave birth to true love between a great Mughal Emperor, Akbar and a Rajput princess, Jodhaa.
