Title: Beer for My Horses (2008)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Michael Salomon
Main Cast: Toby Keith, Rodney Carrington, Greg Serano, Claire Forlani, Ted Nugent

Overview: "Beer for My Horses" tells the story of two best friends that work together as deputies in a small town. The two defy the Sheriff and head off on an outrageous road trip to save the protagonist's girlfriend from drug lord kidnappers.
