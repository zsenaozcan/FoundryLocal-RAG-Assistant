Title: Asterix the Gaul (1967)
Genres: Family, Animation, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Ray Goossens
Main Cast: Roger Carel, Jacques Morel, Jacques Jouanneau, Pierre Tornade, Lucien Raimbourg
Keywords: rome, roman empire, magic, roman, ancient rome, galier, gallien, asterix

Overview: In the year 50 BC, Gaul is occupied by the Romans - nearly. But the small village of Asterix and his friends still resists the Roman legions with the aid of their druid's magic potion, which gives superhuman strength. Learning of this potion, a Roman centurion kidnaps the druid to get the secret formula out of him.
