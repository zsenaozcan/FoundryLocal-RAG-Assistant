Title: Hell (2005)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror
Director: Tanit Jitnukul
Main Cast: Nathawan Woravit, Kom Chauncheun, Punyapon Dhajsonk, Wuttinan Maikan, Dollaya Polthipattayakul
Keywords: hell, thai legend

Overview: While traveling, a crew of documentary makers met a horrible fate when their van fell off a cliff. Though only one of them really died, the rest were 'accidentally' dragged into hell where each were tortured according to their sins.
