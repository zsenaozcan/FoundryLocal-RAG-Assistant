Title: The Twelve Gold Medallions (1970)
Genres: Action, Drama, Foreign
Director: Cheng Kang
Main Cast: Elliot Ngok, Chin Ping, Cheng Miu, Wang Hsieh, Lisa Chiao Chiao

Overview: As a valiant Chinese general and his men battle Tartar invaders, patriot swordsmen attempt to stop the delivery of 12 medallions sent by a traitor that could spell the doom of the Sung Dynasty. When a powerful kung fu master assumes responsibility for the deliveries, his daughter and a former student turn against him in order to save China.
