Title: No Escape (2015)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: John Erick Dowdle
Main Cast: Owen Wilson, Lake Bell, Pierce Brosnan, Sterling Jerins, Claire Geare
Keywords: hotel, revolution, race against time, engineer, coup, survival, rebellion, execution, american, family
