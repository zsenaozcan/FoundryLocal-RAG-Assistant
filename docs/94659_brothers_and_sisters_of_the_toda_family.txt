Title: Brothers and Sisters of the Toda Family (1941)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Main Cast: Mieko Takamine, Shin Saburi, Hideo Fujino, Ayako Katsuragi, Mitsuko Yoshikawa
Keywords: japan, yoshikawa, yasujirō ozu
