Title: Cinderella (2015)
Genres: Romance, Fantasy, Family, Drama

Main Cast: Lily James, Cate Blanchett, Richard Madden, Helena Bonham Carter, Stellan Skarsgård
Keywords: cinderella, magic, prince, fairy tale, kingdom, royalty, orphan, lost shoe, evil stepmother, retelling
