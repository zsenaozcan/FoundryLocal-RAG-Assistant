Title: Gurren Lagann The Movie: Childhood's End (2008)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Animation, Comedy
Director: Hiroyuki Imaishi
Main Cast: Tetsuya Kakihara, Katsuyuki Konishi, Marina Inoue, Yukari Fukui, Nobuyuki Hiyama

Overview: Simon and Kamina live in an underground city monitored by the village chief. When Simon stumbles upon an artifact and beastmen invade from the surface, Simon and Kamina rebel against them.
