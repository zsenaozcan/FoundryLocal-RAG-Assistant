Title: Dickson Greeting (1891)
Genres: Documentary
Director: William K.L. Dickson 
Main Cast: William K.L. Dickson 
Keywords: silent film

Overview: William K.L. Dickson brings his hat from his one hand to the other and moves his head slightly, as a small nod toward the audience. This was the first film produced by the Edison Manufacturing Company to be shown to public audiences and the press.
