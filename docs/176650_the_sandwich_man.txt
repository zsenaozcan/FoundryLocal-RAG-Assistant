Title: The Sandwich Man (1983)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hou Hsiao-hsien
Main Cast: Bor Jeng Chen, Yang Li-Yin
Keywords: based on novel

Overview: Composed of three separate stories, the film vividly portrays Taiwan during the cold war period when the country developed its economy with help from the United States. It is regarded as a hallmark of Taiwanese New Cinema.
