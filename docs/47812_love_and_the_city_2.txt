Title: Love and the City 2 (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Marius Balchunas
Main Cast: Vera Brezhneva, Anastasiya Zadorozhnaya, Vladimir Zelenskiy, Filipp Kirkorov, Anastasiya Stotskaya

Overview: Our three hapless heroes - Igor, Artyom, and Sauna - return for another lesson from St. Valentine. This time they must learn the true value of fatherhood.
