Title: Sand Castles (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: Clenet Verdi-Rose
Main Cast: Jordon Hodges, Anne Winters, Saxon Trainor, Daniella Grace, Scott Jemison
Keywords: indiana, kidnapping, mute

Overview: In rural Indiana, Noah and his impoverished family wrestle with the mysterious return of his now mute sister, Lauren, who was kidnapped and held captive for over a decade.
