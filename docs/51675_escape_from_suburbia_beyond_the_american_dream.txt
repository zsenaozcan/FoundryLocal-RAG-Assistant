Title: Escape from Suburbia: Beyond the American Dream (2007)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Gregory Greene
Main Cast: James Howard Kunstler, Michael Ruppert, Richard Heinberg, Roscoe Bartlett, Jeremy Rifkin

Overview: After condemning America's oil dependency in his 2004 documentary The End of Suburbia, filmmaker Gregory Greene here addresses the solutions that will avert catastrophe, outlining the issues actively moving the energy crisis from theory to reality. Spurred to action by the realities of peak oil, Greene focuses his camera on individuals across the country brave enough to challenge and instigate their communities into serious change.
