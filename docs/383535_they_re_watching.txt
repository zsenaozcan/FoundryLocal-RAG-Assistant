Title: They're Watching (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy, Horror
Director: Jay Lender
Main Cast: Brigid Brannagh, Carrie Genzel, David Alpay, Kris Lemche, Dimitri Diatchenko

Overview: An American TV crew gets trapped in a centuries-old web of revenge, horror, and blood, when their home improvement show is attacked by angry Eastern European villagers out to kill the show's star.
