Title: Why We Fight: The Battle of China (1944)
Genres: War, Documentary
Director: Frank Capra
Main Cast: Anthony Veiller, Walter Huston

Overview: The sixth film illustrates Japan's occupation of China, including Madame Chiang Kai-Shek's stirring address before congress, the rape of Naking, the great 2,000 mile migration, and Claire Chennault's Flying Tigers.
