Title: Not Quite Human (1987)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Science Fiction
Director: Steven Hilliard Stern
Main Cast: Jay Underwood, Alan Thicke, Robyn Lively, Joseph Bologna, Robert Harper
Keywords: android

Overview: Dr. Jonas Carson, a scientist, invents Chip, an android teenager. Dr. Carson sends Chip to school with his daughter Becky to see whether an android could interact with others. But his former employer decides to try and make a profit by stealing the mechanical boy.
