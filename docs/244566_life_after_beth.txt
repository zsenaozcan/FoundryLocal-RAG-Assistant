Title: Life After Beth (2014)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Horror
Director: Jeff Baena
Main Cast: Aubrey Plaza, Dane DeHaan, John C. Reilly, Molly Shannon, Cheryl Hines

Overview: Zach is devastated by the unexpected death of his girlfriend, Beth. When she mysteriously returns, he gets a second chance at love. Soon his whole world turns upside down...
