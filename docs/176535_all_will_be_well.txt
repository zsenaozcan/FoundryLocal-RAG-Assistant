Title: All Will Be Well (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Tomasz Wiszniewski
Main Cast: Robert Więckiewicz, Adam Werstak, Izabela Dąbrowska, Beata Kawka, Daniel Mąkolski

Overview: A teenage boy, whose mother is terminally ill, and his alcohol-addicted sports teacher set out on a pilgrimage to Czestochowa.
