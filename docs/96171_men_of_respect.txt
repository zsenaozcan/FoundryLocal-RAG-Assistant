Title: Men of Respect (1990)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime
Director: William Reilly
Main Cast: John Turturro, Rod Steiger, Katherine Borowitz, Dennis Farina, Peter Boyle

Overview: In this modern day version of Shakespeare's Macbeth, a hitman heeds a spiritualist's prophesies that he will rise to the head of his family. He starts his ascension by clandestinely executing the heads of the family and casting the blame on others. However, with power comes consequences that are also predicted by the seer.
