Title: Schultze Gets the Blues (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Foreign
Director: Michael Schorr
Main Cast: Horst Krause, Harald Warmbrunn, Karl-Fred Müller, Ursula Schucht, Hannelore Schubert
Keywords: southern usa, musical, retirement, polka

Overview: Schultze is an accordion player and newly without work. When the local music club celebrates its 50th anniversary, his taste of music changes unexpectedly.
