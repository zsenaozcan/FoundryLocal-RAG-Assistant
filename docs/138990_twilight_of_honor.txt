Title: Twilight of Honor (1963)
Genres: Drama
Director: Boris Sagal
Main Cast: Richard Chamberlain, Nick Adams, Claude Rains, Joan Blackman, James Gregory
Keywords: law, murder, courtroom

Overview: A young lawyer defends a drifter accused of murder. Director Boris Sagal's 1963 courtroom drama stars Richard Chamberlain, Claude Rains, Nick Adams (Oscar nominee, Best Supporting Actor), Joan Blackman, James Gregory, Joey Heatherton, Pat Buttram, Jeanette Nolan, James Bell, Donald Barry, Vaughn Taylor, Arch Johnson and Linda Evans.
