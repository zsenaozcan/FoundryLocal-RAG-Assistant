Title: The Strange Love of Martha Ivers (1946)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lewis Milestone
Main Cast: Barbara Stanwyck, Van Heflin, Lizabeth Scott, Kirk Douglas, Judith Anderson
Keywords: alcoholic, district attorney, film noir

Overview: A ruthless, domineering woman is married to an alcoholic D.A., her childhood companion who is the only living witness to her murder of her rich aunt seventeen years earlier.
