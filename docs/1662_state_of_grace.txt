Title: State of Grace (1990)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: Phil Joanou
Main Cast: Sean Penn, Gary Oldman, Ed Harris, Robin Wright, John Turturro
Keywords: new york, undercover agent, irish mob

Overview: Terry Noonan returns home to New York's Hells Kitchen after a ten year absence. He soon hooks up with childhood pal Jackie who is involved in the Irish mob run by his brother Frankie. Terry also rekindles an old flame with Jackie's sister Kathleen. Soon, however, Terry is torn between his loyalty to his friends and his loyalties to others.
