Title: The Eye Above the Well (1988)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Johan van der Keuken
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: well

Overview: A poetic depiction of life and ritual in the south Indian state of Kerala. We see how knowledge is passed down from generation to generation: within the family, through the village economy, and especially from teachers to students. Performance footage shows how song, dance, martial arts, and religion constitute the building blocks of a culture.
