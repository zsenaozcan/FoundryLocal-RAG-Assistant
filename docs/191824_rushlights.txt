Title: Rushlights (2013)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Antoni Stutz
Main Cast: Beau Bridges, Aidan Quinn, Josh Henderson, Haley Webb, Jordan Bridges

Overview: Young lovers Billy and Sarah journey from L.A. to Texas with plans to make a false claim to the valuable assets left behind by a dead friend. But when their voyage brings them to Tremo, Texas, they find a bizarre and sinister world awaiting them.
