Title: Madagascar Skin (1995)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Chris Newby
Main Cast: Bernard Hill, John Hannah
Keywords: gay, independent film

Overview: This is the story of Harry and Flint, an unlikely couple. Harry is a shy young gay man who can't seem to fit into his local bar scene. Flint is a crusty, older, and seemingly straight man with a questionable background. They meet on a gorgeous coastline, and evolve from distrust to deep love.
