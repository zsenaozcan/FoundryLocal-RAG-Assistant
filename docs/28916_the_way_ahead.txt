Title: The Way Ahead (1944)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Carol Reed
Main Cast: David Niven, Stanley Holloway, James Donald, John Laurie, Leslie Dwyer
Keywords: group dynamics, army life, unity, flashbacks

Overview: A mismatched collection of conscripted civilians find training tough under Lieutenant Jim Perry  and Sergeant Ned Fletcher when they are called up to replace an infantry battalion that had suffered casualties at Dunkirk.
