Title: Dangerous Game (1993)
Genres: Drama
Director: Abel Ferrara
Main Cast: Harvey Keitel, Madonna, James Russo, Nancy Ferrara, Reilly Murphy
Keywords: independent film, drug, divorce, filmmaking, psychological drama

Overview: A New York film director, working on his latest movie in Los Angeles, begins to reflect the actions in his movie and real life, especially when he begins an affair with the lead actress.
