Title: America 3000 (1986)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Science Fiction
Director: David Engelbach
Main Cast: Chuck Wagner, Laurene Landon, William Wallace, Sue Giosa, Victoria Barrett

Overview: 900 years after a nuclear war in the USA, mankind is back to the stone ages. Amazon women rule the tribes, while the men are weak and dumb - either kept as slaves or living wild as animals. Only Korvis and a friend are intelligent enough to flee and found a tribe of their own. Will they manage to teach the women love to men again?
