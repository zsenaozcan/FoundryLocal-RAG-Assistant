Title: Maladies (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Carter
Main Cast: James Franco, Catherine Keener, David Strathairn, Fallon Goodson, Alan Cumming
Keywords: mental illness, actor

Overview: A talented and successful actor retires at a young age due to a perceived mental illness. Now living in a small town with his deranged sister and his best friend, we watch as their Maladies intertwine.
