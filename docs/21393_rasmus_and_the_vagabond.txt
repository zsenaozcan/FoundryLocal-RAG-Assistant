Title: Rasmus and the Vagabond (1981)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Family
Director: Olle Hellbom
Main Cast: Allan Edwall, Erik Lindgren, Håkan Serner, Olof Bergström, Rolf Larsson
Keywords: homeless person

Overview: 9-year old orphan called Rasmus runs away from the orphanage. He meets a vagabond they call Paradise Oskar who likes to sing and play his accordion. Oskar makes the Rasmus world seem like a better place.
