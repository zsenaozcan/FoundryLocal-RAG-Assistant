Title: Windwalker (1980)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Western
Director: Kieth Merrill
Main Cast: Trevor Howard, Nick Ramus, James Remar, Serene Hedin, Dusty McCrea
Keywords: mountain, kidnapping, wind, wolf, narration, snow, twins, death, missing son, indigenous peoples

Overview: An ancient Indian warrior who has reached the end of his life is brought back from his 'death' to save his family from a raiding party of enemy Indians in this unique story of 'Indians without a single cowboy.
