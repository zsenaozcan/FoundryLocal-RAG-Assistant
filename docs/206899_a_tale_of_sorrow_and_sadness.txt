Title: A Tale of Sorrow and Sadness (1977)
Genres: Drama
Director: Seijun Suzuki
Main Cast: Yoko Shiraki, Kyôko Enami, Yoshio Harada, Masumi Okada, Jô Shishido
Keywords: blackmail, golf, surreal, sport, female stalker

Overview: A model (Yoko Shiraki) must cope with the stress of fame after a marketing executive (Yoshio Harada) transforms her into a sports celebrity.
