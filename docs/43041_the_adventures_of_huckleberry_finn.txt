Title: The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn (1960)
Genres: Drama, Family, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: Tony Randall, Archie Moore, Eddie Hodges, Patty McCormack, Neville Brand
Keywords: mississippi

Overview: From chicken thief to cabin boy, riverboat pilot to circus performer, Huck Finn (Eddie Hodges) outsmarts everyone on his way down the muddy Mississippi. This 1960 movie was the first color version of the Mark Twain classic.
