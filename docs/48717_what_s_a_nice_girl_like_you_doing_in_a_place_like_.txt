Title: What's a Nice Girl Like You Doing in a Place Like This? (1963)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Martin Scorsese
Main Cast: Sarah Braveman, Zeph Michelis, Fred Sica, Mimi Stark, Robert Uricola

Overview: A writer named Algernon (but called Harry by his friends) buys a picture of a boat on a lake, and his obsession with it renders normal life impossible.
