Title: Puff, Puff, Pass (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mekhi Phifer
Main Cast: Ronnie Warner, Danny Masterson, Mekhi Phifer, Ashley Scott, Terry Crews

Overview: Danny Masterson (TV's 'That '70s Show') leads a hilarious ensemble cast in a tale about two hapless stoners who get involved in a scheme to rip off a shady character named Mr. Big after the duo sours on rehab.
