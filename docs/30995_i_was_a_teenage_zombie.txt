Title: I Was a Teenage Zombie (1987)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: John Elias Michalakis
Main Cast: Michael Rubin, Steve McCoy, George Seminara, Robert C. Sabin, Peter Bush
Keywords: independent film, spoof, zombie

Overview: A high-school student (Michael Ruben) and a drug pusher (Steve McCoy) land in a nuclear-wasted river and come out zombies.
