Title: End of the World (2013)
Genres: Science Fiction, Action, Comedy, Adventure
Director: Steven R. Monroe
Main Cast: Caroline Cave, Brad Dourif, Greg Grunberg, Neil Grayston, Mark Hildreth

Overview: In End of the World, the fate of humanity lies in the hands of two guys who run a video rental store and are “sci-fi obsessed” movie fans.  And who better to save the world, than guys who have seen all the movies and all the “out there” ways to save it!?
