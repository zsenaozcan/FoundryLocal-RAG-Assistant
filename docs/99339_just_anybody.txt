Title: Just Anybody (2008)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Jacques Doillon
Main Cast: Clémentine Beaugrand, Gérald Thomassin, Guillaume Saurrel, Gwendoline Godquin, Jany Garachana

Overview: Camille, a 20-something dissatisfied with her life, craves the opportunity to give her love to the person who needs it the most. She finds this opportunity in the form of Costa, a homeless ne’er do well, deadbeat dad, and rejected son. Even as their relationship begins to take its toll on them both, Camille finds happiness in her efforts to rescue him from his own self-destruction.
