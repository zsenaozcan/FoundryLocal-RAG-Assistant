Title: Cries in the Night (1980)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: William Fruet
Main Cast: Kay Hawtrey, Lesleh Donaldson, Barry Morse, Dean Garbett, Stephen E. Miller
Keywords: cellar, funeral, grandfather, independent film, morgue, slasher, maniac, occult, grandmother, canuxploitation

Overview: A young woman arrives at her grandmother's house, which used to be a funeral home, to help her turn the place into a bed-and-breakfast inn. After they open, however, guests begin disappearing or turning up dead.
