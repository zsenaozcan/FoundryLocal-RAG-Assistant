Title: My Night at Maud's (1969)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Éric Rohmer
Main Cast: Jean-Louis Trintignant, Françoise Fabian, Marie-Christine Barrault, Antoine Vitez, Léonide Kogan
Keywords: catholic, devout

Overview: The rigid principles of a devout Catholic man are challenged during a one-night stay with Maud, a divorced woman with an outsize personality.
