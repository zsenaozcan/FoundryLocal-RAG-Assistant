Title: Allan Quatermain and the Lost City of Gold (1986)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Gary Nelson
Main Cast: Richard Chamberlain, Sharon Stone, James Earl Jones, Henry Silva, Robert Donner
Keywords: brother brother relationship, africa, riddle, gold, treasure, treasure hunt, goldmine, archaeologist, missing person, archeology 

Overview: After his brother Robeson disappears without a trace while exploring Africa in search of a legendary 'white tribe', Allan Quatermain decides to follow in his footsteps to learn what became of him. Soon after arriving, he discovers the Lost City of Gold, controlled by the evil lord Agon, and mined by his legions of white slaves.
