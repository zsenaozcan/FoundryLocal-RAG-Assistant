Title: The Red Chapel (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary, Foreign
Director: Mads Brügger
Main Cast: Simon Jul Jørgensen, Jacob Nossell, Mads Brügger

Overview: Two Danish comics, one of them a spastic and both born in Korea, join the director on a trip to North Korea, where they have been allowed access under the pretext of wanting to perform a vaudeville act.
