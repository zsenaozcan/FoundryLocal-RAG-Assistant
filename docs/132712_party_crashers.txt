Title: Party Crashers (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Andrucha Waddington
Main Cast: Marcelo Adnet, Eduardo Sterblitch, Andréa Beltrão, Mariana Ximenes, Caio Junqueira

Overview: "Party Crashers" chronicles the amusing encounter between Marco Polo (Marcelo Adnet), a Copacabana's trickster, and Beto (Eduardo Sterblitch), a boy passionate and somewhat pixilated, on the New Year's Eve in Rio. Together they crashes at parties and create a lot of confusion in search of the beautiful Laura.
