Title: Dark Summer (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Paul Solet
Main Cast: Peter Stormare, Stella Maeve, Grace Phipps, Keir Gilchrist, Maestro Harrell
Keywords: swimming pool, house arrest

Overview: Follows the tale of a 17-year-old on house arrest for the summer. When his mother is away on business, a horrifying incident occurs, followed by an even more terrifying presence in the house.
