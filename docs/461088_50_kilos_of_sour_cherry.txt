Title: 50 Kilos of Sour Cherry (2016)
Genres: Family, Drama, Comedy
Director: Mani Haghighi
Main Cast: هستی مهدوی‌فر, Farhad Ayish, Vishka Asayesh, Behnoosh Bakhtiari, Afsaneh Bayegan

Overview: When the corrupted groom starts shooting in the wedding ceremony, guests try to run away. Aida and Davoud accidentally end up in a car that crashes a police car. Aida lies to the inspector that Davoud is her husband and that's where the story begins.
