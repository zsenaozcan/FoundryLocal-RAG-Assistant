Title: An Education (2009)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Lone Scherfig
Main Cast: Carey Mulligan, Olivia Williams, Peter Sarsgaard, Alfred Molina, Cara Seymour
Keywords: adolescence, age difference, parent child relationship, self-discovery, coming of age, love affair, youth, family, woman director, teaching the ways of the world

Overview: A coming-of-age story about a teenage girl in 1960s suburban London, and how her life changes with the arrival of a playboy nearly twice her age.
