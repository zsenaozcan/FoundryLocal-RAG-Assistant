Title: All Around Us (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ryosuke Hashiguchi
Main Cast: Lily Franky, Tae Kimura, Akira Emoto, Susumu Terajima, Tamae Ando

Overview: Lily Franky plays the role of a courtroom sketch artist named Kanao, and actress Tae Kimura plays the role of his wife Shoko. The story takes place in the 1990s, involving real-life events from that time.
