Title: How Strange to be Named Federico: Scola Narrates Fellini (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ettore Scola
Main Cast: Federico Fellini, Vittorio Viviani, Ernesto D'Argenio, Antonella Attili

Overview: On the 20th anniversary of Federico Fellini’s death, Ettore Scola, a devoted admirer of the incomparable maestro, commemorates the lesser-known aspects of Fellini’s personality, employing interviews, photographs, behind-the-scenes footage as well as Fellini’s drawings and film clips.
