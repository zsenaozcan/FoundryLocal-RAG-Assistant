Title: Crush the Skull (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy, Horror
Director: Viet Nguyen
Main Cast: Katie Savoy, Chris Riedell, Tim Chiou, Lauren Reeder, Leonard Wu

Overview: A couple of master thieves find themselves trapped within a house they intended to rob, only to discover they've inadvertently wandered into the lair of a deranged serial killer.
