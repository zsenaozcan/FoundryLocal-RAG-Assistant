Title: Roads to Koktebel (2003)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Boris Khlebnikov
Main Cast: Глеб Пускепалис, Igor Chernevich, Yevgeni Syty, Вера Сандрыкина, Владимир Кучеренко

Overview: A widowed aeronautics engineer, who has lost his job, travels with his son hopping freight trains from Moscow to Koktebel, a town by the Black Sea, to start a new life with the father's sister.
