Title: I Hate But Love (1962)
Genres: Romance, Action, Comedy, Drama
Director: Koreyoshi Kurahara
Main Cast: Yûjirô Ishihara, Ruriko Asaoka, Hiroyuki Nagato, Izumi Ashikawa, Tamio Kawaji

Overview: A celebrity (Yujiro Ishihara), dissatisfied with his personal and professional lives, impulsively leaves fast-paced Tokyo to deliver a much-needed jeep to a remote village. When his controlling manager, the woman he loves (Ruriko Asaoka), follows, the two must reconcile while dodging reporters.
