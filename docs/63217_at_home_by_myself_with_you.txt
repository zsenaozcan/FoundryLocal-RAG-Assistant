Title: At Home by Myself... with You (2009)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Kris Booth
Main Cast: Kristin Booth, Aron Abrams, Ryan Blakely, Raoul Bhaneja, Shauna MacDonald

Overview: A multi phobia-plagued single woman who hasn't left her apartment in six years finds her carefully organized existence disrupted by her hot new 'on-the-go' neighbor.
