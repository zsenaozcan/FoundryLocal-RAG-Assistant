Title: Extasis (1996)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Mariano Barroso
Main Cast: Javier Bardem, Federico Luppi, Sílvia Munt, Leire Berrocal, Alfonso Lussón

Overview: Three friends, two young men and a young woman, are bored by the normal world of their parents and want to flee in order to start living somewhere else. Thus they make a pervert plan: rob their own families. After that they flee without any destination and without having really planned where to go to.
