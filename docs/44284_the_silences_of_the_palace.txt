Title: The Silences of the Palace (1994)
Genres: Drama
Director: Moufida Tlatli
Main Cast: Amel Hedhili, Najia Ouerghi, Fatima Ben Saïdane, Zahira Ben Ammar, Sabah Bouzouita
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The death of a prince brings a young woman back to the palace where she was born into servitude. The lingering legacy of the harem is brought into light from behind frosted windows and velvet curtains.
