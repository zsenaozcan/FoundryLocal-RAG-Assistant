Title: Warning Shadows (1923)
Genres: Thriller, Fantasy, Drama, Horror
Director: Arthur Robison
Main Cast: Alexander Granach, Fritz Kortner, Ruth Weyher, Gustav von Wangenheim, Eugen Rex

Overview: During a dinner, given by a wealthy baron and his wive, attended by four of her suitors in a 19th century German manor, a shadow-player rescues the marriage by giving all the guests a vision what might happen tonight if the baron stays jealous and the suitors do not reduce their advances towards his beautiful wife. Or was it a vision?
