Title: The Bronze Buckaroo (1939)
Genres: Western
Director: Richard C. Kahn
Main Cast: Herb Jeffries, Lucius Brooks, Artie Young, F.E. Miller, Spencer Williams
Keywords: singing cowboy

Overview: Bob Blake and his sidekick and four singing cowboys arrive at the Jackson ranch where Bob learns from Betty Jackson that her brother, Joe, is missing. Bob investigates and learns that there is gold on the Jackson ranch, and the neighboring rancher has kidnapped Joe in order to get his land.
