Title: Hitched for the Holidays (2012)
Genres: Romance
Director: Michael Scott
Main Cast: Emily Hampshire, Joey Lawrence, Linda Darlow, Marilu Henner, Serge Houde
Keywords: holiday, pretend relationship

Overview: A couple of single adults agree to pose as each others  significant other throughout the holidays to keep their meddling families from harassing them, only to find that their feelings for each other are real.
