Title: Revenge (1989)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yermek Shinarbayev
Main Cast: Kasym Zhakibayev, Lyubov Germanova, Oleg Li, Juozas Budraitis, Maksim Munzuk
Keywords: poetry, revenge, murder

Overview: Enraged, a teacher murders a young female pupil. Over the years, another boy is bred for one sole purpose: to avenge his sister’s death.
