Title: Bounty Hunters (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Action, Adventure
Director: Shin Tae-Ra
Main Cast: Lee Min-ho, Wallace Chung, Tiffany Tang, Karena Ng, Fan Siu-Wong
Keywords: bounty hunter, asia

Overview: Lee Shan and Ayo are ex-Interpol agents who are now bounty hunters, chasing fugitives for cash rewards. When the two of them are framed for a hotel-bombing, they join hands with a legendary bounty hunter named Cat, along with her teammates, to find the real bomber.
