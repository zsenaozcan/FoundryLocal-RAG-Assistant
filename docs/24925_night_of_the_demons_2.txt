Title: Night of the Demons 2 (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Brian Trenchard-Smith
Main Cast: Cristi Harris, Darin Heames, Robert Jayne, Merle Kennedy, Amelia Kinkade

Overview: Angela, the universe's most unpleasant party crasher, returns! Angela's sister, Mouse, is taken by her bullying Catholic school classmates to a party at Angela's favorite haunt, and before long, everybody's being turned into demons and only a butt-kickin' nun, who wields her ruler like a mighty sword of steel, can save the day.
