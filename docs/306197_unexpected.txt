Title: Unexpected (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Kris Swanberg
Main Cast: Cobie Smulders, Gail Bean, Anders Holm, Elizabeth McGovern, Robyn Coffin
Keywords: woman director

Overview: An inner-city high school teacher discovers she is pregnant at the same time as one of her most promising students and the two develop an unlikely friendship while struggling to navigate their unexpected pregnancies.
