Title: Bad Behavior (2013)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Lisa Hamil
Main Cast: Hallee Hirsh, Austin Rogers, Linda Hamilton, Ted McGinley, Jeremy Dozier
Keywords: mental illness, woman director

Overview: Trapped overnight by an unknown assailant, a babysitter struggles to stay safe. As the hours tick by, she realizes that the greatest threat might be from the very children she's trying to protect.
