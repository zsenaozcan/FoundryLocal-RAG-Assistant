Title: Rimini, Rimini: A Year Later (1988)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Bruno Corbucci
Main Cast: Renzo Montagnani, Eva Grimaldi, Maurizio Micheli, Gianfranco D'Angelo, Gastone Moschin

Overview: Funny, entertaining comedy with a few storylines. All of them have one thing in common - a resort town of Rimini in Italy.
