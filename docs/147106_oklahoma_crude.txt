Title: Oklahoma Crude (1973)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Western
Director: Stanley Kramer
Main Cast: George C. Scott, Faye Dunaway, Jack Palance, John Mills, William Lucking
Keywords: oklahoma, father, oil

Overview: In 1913, in Oklahoma, oil derrick owner Lena Doyle, aided by her father and a hobo, is stubbornly drilling for oil despite the pressure from major oil companies to sell her land.
