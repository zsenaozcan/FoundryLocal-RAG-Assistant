Title: The Clown (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Selton Mello
Main Cast: Selton Mello, Paulo José, Larissa Manoela, Giselle Motta, Teuda Bara
Keywords: brazilian, drama, comedy

Overview: The life of the owners of Circus Esperança, Puro Sangue e Pangaré, father and son, who plays two clowns. At a certain point, Pangaré starts showing tiredness and sadness for being an unhappy clown.
