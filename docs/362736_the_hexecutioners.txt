Title: The Hexecutioners (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jesse Thomas Cook
Main Cast: Liv Collins, Sarah Power, Timothy Burd, Ari Millen, Tony Burgess

Overview: Two disparate young women working for a company that conducts euthanasia are dispatched to a remote estate to perform a custom assisted-suicide ritual, but unwittingly summon the wraiths of a Death Cult that once inhabited the property.
