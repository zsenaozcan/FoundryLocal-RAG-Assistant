Title: Arundhati (2009)
Genres: Horror, Fantasy, Thriller
Director: Kodi Rama Krishna
Main Cast: Sonu Sood, Arjan Bajwa, Manorama, Anushka Shetty, Sayaji Shinde
Keywords: suspense

Overview: Arundhati (Anushka) belongs to Gadwal samsthanam in 1920’s. She is known for her bravery in saving the Samstanam by killing Pasupathi (Sonu Sood) an evildoer. But the spirit of Pasupathi haunts that place. Another girl is born in the same lineage after 3 generations and she is named as Arundhati. The spirit of Pasupati is after Arundhati to take the revenge.
