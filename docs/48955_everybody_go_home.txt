Title: Everybody Go Home (1960)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Luigi Comencini
Main Cast: Alberto Sordi, Eduardo De Filippo, Serge Reggiani, Martin Balsam, Alex Nicol

Overview: Everybody Go Home (Italian: Tutti a casa) is a 1960 Italian film directed by Luigi Comencini. It features an international cast including the U.S. actors Martin Balsam, Alex Nicol and the Franco-Italian Serge Reggiani. Nino Manfredi was rejected for the starring role because Alberto Sordi wanted it.
