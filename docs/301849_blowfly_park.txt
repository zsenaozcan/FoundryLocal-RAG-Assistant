Title: Blowfly Park (2014)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Jens Östberg
Main Cast: Sverrir Gudnason, Peter Andersson, Malin Buska, Leonard Terfelt, Joar Hennix Raukola

Overview: Blowfly Park is a psychological thriller which follows Christian Kretschmann, a day-nursery teacher, who may have committed a violent murder. This is the film debut of choreographer Jens Östberg.
