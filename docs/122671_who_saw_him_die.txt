Title: Who Saw Him Die? (1968)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jan Troell
Main Cast: Per Oscarsson, Ann-Marie Gyllenspetz, Kerstin Tidelius, Bengt Ekerot, Catti Edfeldt
Keywords: teacher

Overview: Sören Mårtensson is a middle school teacher. His classroom occupied by half-grown children. His work is tormenting him and at night he is haunted by nightmares.
