Title: Mars and April (2012)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Martin Villeneuve
Main Cast: Jacques Languirand, Caroline Dhavernas, Paul Ahmarani, Robert Lepage, Stéphane Demers

Overview: Set in a futuristic environment, "Mars et Avril" tells the story of an elderly musician and his instrument maker who both become obsessed with the same woman. She agrees to be the model for their next musical instrument but then gets lost in a virtual world on the way to Mars.
