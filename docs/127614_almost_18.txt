Title: Almost 18 (2012)
Genres: Foreign, Drama, Comedy
Director: Maarit Lalli
Main Cast: Henrik Mäki-Tanila, Anton Thomson Coon, Karim Al-Rifai, Ben Thomson Coon
Keywords: male nudity, shower, difficult childhood, woman director, difficult parents

Overview: Almost 18 is a charming and unaffected film about five 17-year-old guys from Helsinki in 2010. Karri, Peter, André, Akseli and Joni dream, party, love and boast like only teenage males can.
