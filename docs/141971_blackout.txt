Title: Blackout (2008)
Genres: Thriller, Mystery
Director: JP Siili
Main Cast: Petteri Summanen, Ismo Kallio, Eppu Salminen, Irina Björklund, Hannu-Pekka Björkman

Overview: Recovering from a nail gun shot to the head and 13 months of coma, doctor Pekka Valinta starts to unravel the mystery of his past, still suffering from total amnesia.
