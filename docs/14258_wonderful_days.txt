Title: Wonderful Days (2003)
Genres: Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Kim Moon-saeng
Main Cast: Joon-ho Chung, Won-Je Tak
Keywords: resistance, island, pollution, energy

Overview: Civilization has been destroyed by war and pollution, but the survivors have built the last city of Ecoban. As most natural resources have been exhausted, Ecoban is powered by pollution. The citizens of Ecoban need to continue creating this pollution leading them into conflict with the inhabitants of Marr while one man just wants to clear away the clouds and see the sky.
