Title: The Temptations (1998)
Genres: Music, Drama
Director: Allan Arkush
Main Cast: Charles Malik Whitfield, D.B. Woodside, Terron Brooks, Christian Payton, Jenifer Lewis
Keywords: motown, biography, pop music, flashback

Overview: The true story of The Temptations, the Soul vocal group of the 1960s, as seen from the viewpoint of the last surviving member, Otis Williams. Beginning from their humble origins in the late 50s and continuing through the 90s and the deaths of the other 4 members.
