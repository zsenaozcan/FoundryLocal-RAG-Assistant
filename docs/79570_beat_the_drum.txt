Title: Beat the Drum (2003)
Genres: Drama
Director: David Hickson
Main Cast: Junior Singo, Mary Twala, Adelaide Shabalala

Overview: Young Musa is orphaned after a mysterious illness strikes his village in KwaZulu Natal. To help his grandmother, Musa sets out for Johannesburg with his father's last gift, a tribal drum, in search of work and his uncle. The journey confronts him with the stark realities of urban life, but his indomitable spirit never wavers; he returns with a truth and understanding his elders have failed to grasp.
