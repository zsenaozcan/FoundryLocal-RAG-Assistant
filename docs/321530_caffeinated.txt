Title: Caffeinated (2015)
Genres: Documentary, Adventure
Director: Hanh Nguyen
Main Cast: Jeremy Adams, Andrew Alcala, Danny Glover, Katie Leclerc, John Savage
Keywords: lifestyle, coffee, caffeine

Overview: This documentary explores a vivid and unmatched perspective to the existence of coffee in our daily lives. Expanding production to United States, Italy, India, Guatemala, Honduras and Nicaragua, the film takes coffee enthusiasts on a trip that will transcend their knowledge of the beverage and get them close to a human reality they haven't experienced before.
