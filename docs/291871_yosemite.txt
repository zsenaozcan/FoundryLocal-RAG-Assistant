Title: Yosemite (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Gabrielle Demeestere
Main Cast: James Franco, Henry Hopper, Steven Wiig, Barry Del Sherman, George Maguire
Keywords: based on novel, childhood friends, woman director, mountain lion, palo alto

Overview: It's the fall of 1985. The intertwining tales of three 5th grade friends, Chris, Joe and Ted, unfold in the suburban paradise of Palo Alto, as the threat of a mountain lion looms over the community.
