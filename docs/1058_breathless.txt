Title: Breathless (1983)
Genres: Action, Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Jim McBride
Main Cast: Richard Gere, Valérie Kaprisky, Art Metrano, John P. Ryan, William Tepper
Keywords: mexico, eroticism, quickie, suspicion of murder, motel, seduction, final exam, police, suspense, los angeles

Overview: Jesse has to get out of Las Vegas quickly, and steals a car to drive to L.A. On the way he shoots a police man. When he makes it to L.A. he stays with Monica, a girl he has only known for a few days. As the film progresses, the police get closer to him, and the crimes escalate.
