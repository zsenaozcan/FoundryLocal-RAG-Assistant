Title: To Die Like a Man (2009)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: João Pedro Rodrigues
Main Cast: Chandra Malatitch, John Jesus Romão, Ivo Barroso, Gonçalo Ferreira De Almeida, Miguel Loureiro
Keywords: fantasy, forest, transsexual

Overview: A transvestite tries to erase any past history of herself as a male. Struggling with a young male lover and a problematic son.
