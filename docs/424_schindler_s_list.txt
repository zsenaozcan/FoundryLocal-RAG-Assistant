Genres: Drama, History, War

Main Cast: Liam Neeson, Ben Kingsley, Ralph Fiennes, Caroline Goodall, Jonathan Sagall
Keywords: factory, concentration camp, hero, holocaust, world war ii, nazis, defense industry, biography, the holocaust
