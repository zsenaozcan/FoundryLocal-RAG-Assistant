Title: The Fighter (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: David O. Russell
Main Cast: Mark Wahlberg, Christian Bale, Amy Adams, Melissa Leo, Mickey O'Keefe
Keywords: sport, irish american, documentary crew, lowell massachusetts, blue collar worker, church bells, documentary filmmaking, boxer shorts, jumping rope, shadow boxing

Overview: The Fighter, is a drama about boxer "Irish" Micky Ward's unlikely road to the world light welterweight title. His Rocky-like rise was shepherded by half-brother Dicky, a boxer-turned-trainer who rebounded in life after nearly being KO'd by drugs and crime.
