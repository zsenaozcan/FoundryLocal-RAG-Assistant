Title: The Redemption of General Butt Naked (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Daniele Anastasion
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Can a ruthless mass killer become a messenger of peace? This spellbinding documentary traces the rise and fall of General Butt Naked—feared warlord of Liberia’s 14-year civil war—and his self-proclaimed quest to heal his country and redeem his own soul as a newborn evangelist.
