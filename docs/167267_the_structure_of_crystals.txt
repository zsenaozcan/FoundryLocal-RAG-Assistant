Title: The Structure of Crystals (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Krzysztof Zanussi
Main Cast: Jan Myslowicz, Barbara Wrzesińska, Andrzej Zarnecki, Wladyslaw Jarema, Adam Debski
Keywords: university, science, old friends, moral dilemma, provincial life

Overview: Physicist John worked as a meteorologist. He lives in a small village with his wife, a teacher. Meanwhile, his friend from university, Mark, is doing a research career. Trying to get John to return to academic work in the capital. Mark is not convinced by the arguments of John. Clash of two attitudes, characters, ideas about the meaning of life.
