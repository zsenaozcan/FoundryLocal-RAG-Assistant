Title: Champagne (1928)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Alfred Hitchcock
Main Cast: Betty Balfour, Gordon Harker, Jean Bradin, Ferdinand von Alten, Fanny Wright
Keywords: riches

Overview: Betty, rebellious daughter of a millionaire, decides to marry the pennyless Jean, against her father's will. She runs away to France and she starts living a life of luxury on the profits from her father's business. The rich man decides to put a stop to her behavior pretending his business crashed. Betty now has to find money by herself and she gets a job in a night club.
