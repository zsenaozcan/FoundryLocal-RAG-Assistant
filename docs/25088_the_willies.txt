Title: The Willies (1990)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Brian Peck
Main Cast: Sean Astin, Jason Horst, James Karen, Joshua Miller, Kathleen Freeman
Keywords: camping, tall tales

Overview: Two brothers camping with their cousin try to frighten each other by telling stories. They start with urban legends, but then there are two main narrations: one involves strange happenings at an elementary school; the other, a teenage boy with a peculiar interest.
