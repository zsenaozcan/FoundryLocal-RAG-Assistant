Title: Clay Pigeon (1971)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Lane Slate
Main Cast: Tom Stern, Telly Savalas, Robert Vaughn, John Marley, Burgess Meredith
Keywords: fbi, ex soldier, smoking a joint

Overview: An ex-soldier is recruited by the FBI to go undercover in L.A. and find other ex-soldiers who are part of a drug-dealing gang.
