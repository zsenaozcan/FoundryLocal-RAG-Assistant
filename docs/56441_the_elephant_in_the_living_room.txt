Title: The Elephant in the Living Room (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Michael Webber
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: lion cub, suburbia, suburb, python, deadly snake

Overview: A film about the controversial world of exotic animal ownership within the suburbs of the United States. "The Elephant in the Living Room" offers an unprecedented glimpse into the fascinating subculture of trading and raising the most deadly and exotic animals in the world as common household pets.
