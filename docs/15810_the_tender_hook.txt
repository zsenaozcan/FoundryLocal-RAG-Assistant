Title: The Tender Hook (2008)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery
Director: Jonathan Ogilvie
Main Cast: Rose Byrne, Tyler Coppin, John Batchelor, Hugo Weaving, Matthew Le Nevez
Keywords: love triangle, jazz age, 1920s, 20th century

Overview: The story is about Iris' rise to the apex of a love/power triangle that includes her roguish English lover, McHeath and Art, an earnest young boxer. Within the flawed moral landscape, each character struggles to establish their sovereignty.
