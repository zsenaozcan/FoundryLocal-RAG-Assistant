Title: The Jolly Boys' Last Stand (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Foreign
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: independent film

Overview: The story revolves around a group of "Lads". The "leader" of this group of twenty somethings starts to head away the group's usual juvenile direction and head into career and marriage pursuits. The Best Man decides to create a video of the friends as a wedding present. In reality the video is supposed to help bring the leader back into the fold.
