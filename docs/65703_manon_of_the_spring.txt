Title: Manon of the Spring (1952)
Genres: Drama
Director: Marcel Pagnol
Main Cast: Jacqueline Pagnol, Rellys, Henri Poupon, Raymond Pellegrin

Overview: Marcel Pagnol's adaptation of his own novel Manon des sources, the story of a shepherdess who exacts her revenge on the townsfolk she blames for killing her father, in two parts: Manon des sources and Ugolin.
