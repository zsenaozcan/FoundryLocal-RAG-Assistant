Title: The Survivor (1981)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: David Hemmings
Main Cast: Robert Powell, Jenny Agutter, Joseph Cotten, Angela Punch McGregor, Peter Sumner
Keywords: based on novel, pilot, independent film, airplane crash

Overview: When a 747 crashes shortly after take-off, the sole survivor is the pilot. Virtually unhurt, he and the investigators look for the answers to the disaster. Meanwhile mysterious deaths occur in the community and only a psychic, in touch with the supernatural, can help the pilot unravel the mystery surrounding the doomed plane.
