Title: Holocaust 2000 (1977)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Alberto De Martino
Main Cast: Kirk Douglas, Simon Ward, Agostina Belli, Anthony Quayle, Alexander Knox
Keywords: nuclear power plant, nuclear holocaust, armageddon

Overview: An executive in charge of a nuclear power plant in the Mid-East must stop his son-- who turns out to be the Anti Christ -- from blowing it up.
