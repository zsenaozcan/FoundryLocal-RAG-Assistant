Title: Latitudes (2014)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Felipe Braga
Main Cast: Alice Braga, Daniel de Oliveira, Elisa Volpatto, Michel Noher
Keywords: paris, london england, trip, buenos aires, sao paulo, porto, venezia, josé ignácio, istambul

Overview: Olívia, a fashion executive, and José, a photographer, gets together in Paris, having casual sex and talking a little about relationships. And, in some days, they meet again in another city. And another one. And another... Latitudes is a trip around the world, the love and the poem of living.
