Title: Hannibal Buress: Animal Furnace (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michael Dimich
Main Cast: Hannibal Buress
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Hannibal is a Chicago native, currently living in New York City where he regularly performs and lives alone with no pets. Animal Furnace was recorded in December of 2011. Hannibal's credits include writing for 30 Rock, SNL and performing in several basements of bars in NYC and Chicago. His jokes cover topics like personal stories, current events, the streets and even food.
