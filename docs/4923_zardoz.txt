Title: Zardoz (1974)
Genres: Fantasy, Action, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: John Boorman
Main Cast: Sean Connery, Charlotte Rampling, Sara Kestelman, John Alderton, Sally Anne Newton
Keywords: sex, future, dystopia, immortality, murder, savage

Overview: In the far future, a savage trained only to kill finds a way into the community of bored immortals that alone preserves humanity's achievements.
