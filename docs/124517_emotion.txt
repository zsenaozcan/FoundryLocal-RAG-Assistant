Title: Emotion (1966)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy
Director: Nobuhiko Ôbayashi
Main Cast: Sari Akasaka, Kyôko Hanyû, Sakio Hirata, Jinichi Isizaki, Jusin Kitamura
Keywords: coming of age, avant-garde, experimental

Overview: Experimental short film depicting the life, perhaps real, perhaps a dream, of a young girl named Emi. Emi travels to the city where she encounters her counterpart, Sari, and falls in love with…a vampire?
