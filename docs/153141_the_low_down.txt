Title: The Low Down (2001)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Jamie Thraves
Main Cast: Aidan Gillen, Kate Ashfield, Dean Lennox Kelly, Tobias Menzies, Rupert Procter
Keywords: friendship, love

Overview: Frank is a restless young man in his late twenties whose life revolves around his friends and his work. When he becomes involved with Ruby, her optimistic and fresh approach to life and its problems begins to have a dramatic effect on him.
