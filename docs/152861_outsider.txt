Title: Outsider (1997)
Genres: Drama
Director: Andrej Košak
Main Cast: Davor Janjić, Nina Ivanič, Uroš Potočnik, Zijah Sokolović, Miranda Caharija
Keywords: yugoslavia, 1970s, police, basement, punk rock, military officer, punk rocker, communism, punk band

Overview: A teenager, son of an army officer moves with his family to Ljubljana in the fall of 1979. In the new surroundings, he'll fall in love, make friends and discover the world of punk rock. The latter will put him into the troubles with his strict father.
