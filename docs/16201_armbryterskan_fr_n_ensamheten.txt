Title: Armbryterskan från Ensamheten (2004)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Helen Ahlsson
Main Cast: Heidi Andersson, Jeanette Andersson, Kent Andersson, Björn Ferry
Keywords: sport, arm wrestling, woman director

Overview: The tiny village in the far north of Sweden called Ensamheten (Solitude) has sixteen inhabitants. They all share an unusual passion - armwrestling.
