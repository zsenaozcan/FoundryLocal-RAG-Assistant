Title: Boeing, Boeing (1965)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: John Rich
Main Cast: Jerry Lewis, Tony Curtis, Thelma Ritter, Christiane Schmidtmer, Dany Saval
Keywords: pilot, womanizer, based on play or musical, american abroad, newspaper man, airline, stewardesses

Everything works fine as long as they only come home every third day. But when there's a change in their working schedule, they will be able to be home every second day instead. Bernard's carefully structured life is breaking apart
