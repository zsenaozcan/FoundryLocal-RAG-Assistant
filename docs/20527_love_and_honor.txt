Title: Love and Honor (2006)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Romance
Director: Yôji Yamada
Main Cast: Takuya Kimura, Rei Dan, Takashi Sasano, Kaori Momoi, Nenji Kobayashi

Overview: A look at the relationship between a young blind samurai (Kimura) and his wife, who will make a sacrifice in order to defend her husband's honor.
