Title: A Tale of Two Cities (1980)
Genres: TV Movie, Romance, Drama, History
Director: Jim Goddard
Main Cast: Chris Sarandon, Peter Cushing, Alice Krige, Kenneth More, Barry Morse
Keywords: based on novel, french revolution, charles dickens

Overview: Dissolute barrister Sydney Carton becomes enchanted and then hopelessly in love with the beautiful Lucie Manette. But Lucie loves and marries Charles Darnay, and remains oblivious to Carton's undimmed devotion to her. When Darnay is ensnared in the deadly web of the French Revolution and condemned to die by the guillotine, Sydney Carton concocts a dangerous plot to free the husband of the woman he loves.
