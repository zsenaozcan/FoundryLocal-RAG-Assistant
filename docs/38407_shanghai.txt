Title: Shanghai (2010)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Romance
Director: Mikael Håfström
Main Cast: John Cusack, Gong Li, Chow Yun-fat, Jeffrey Dean Morgan, Ken Watanabe
Keywords: suspense

Overview: An American man returns to a corrupt, Japanese-occupied Shanghai four months before Pearl Harbor and discovers his friend has been killed. While he unravels the mysteries of the death, he falls in love and discovers a much larger secret that his own government is hiding.
