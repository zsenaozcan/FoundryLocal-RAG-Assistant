Title: A Girl in Every Port (1952)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Chester Erskine
Main Cast: Groucho Marx, Marie Wilson, William Bendix, Don DeFore, Gene Lockhart
Keywords: navy, blonde, sailor

Overview: After two sailors are conned into buying a lame race-horse, they go ashore to sort out the problem, but when they realize that the horse is one of a pair of identical twins, their plan for revenge becomes more complicated.
