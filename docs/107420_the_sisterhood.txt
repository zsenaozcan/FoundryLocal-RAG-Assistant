Title: The Sisterhood (1988)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Science Fiction
Director: Cirio H. Santiago
Main Cast: Rebecca Holden, Chuck Wagner, Lynn-Holly Johnson, Barbara Patrick, Robert Dryer
Keywords: post-apocalyptic, amazon, independent film, desert, child murder

Overview: The year is 2021 AD. Women have been enslaved by a brutal army of men who survived the nuclear holocaust. Their only hope for freedom is in the hands of a nomadic band of fierce she-warriors: The Sisterhood.
