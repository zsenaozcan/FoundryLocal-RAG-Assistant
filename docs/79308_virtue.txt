Title: Virtue (1932)
Genres: Drama
Director: Edward Buzzell
Main Cast: Carole Lombard, Pat O’Brien, Ward Bond, Shirley Grey, Mayo Methot
Keywords: waitress, taxi driver, brooklyn new york city

Overview: A relationship gradually develops between a savvy New York street girl and a good-hearted cab driver--who first meet when she stiffs him for the fare--but other matters keep getting in their way, including financial problems and a murder.
