Title: Canadian Bacon (1995)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Michael Moore
Main Cast: John Candy, Alan Alda, Rhea Perlman, Kevin Pollak, Rip Torn

Overview: Canadian Bacon is the only fictitious film from Director Michael Moore. This comedy is about an American president who decides to go to war to bring the public’s opinion of him up during election time. Canadian Bacon is actor John Candy’s last film.
