Title: Sheep & Wolves (2016)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Comedy, Family, Fantasy, Romance
Director: Maxim Volkov
Main Cast: Elizaveta Boyarskaya, Sergei Bezrukov, Yuriy Galtsev , Nikita Prozorovsky, Andrei Rozhkov
Keywords: sheep, furry, anthropomorphism, wolves

Overview: In a magical faraway land, in a picturesque little village nestled among green meadows and rolling hills, lives a flock of carefree sheep. But their pastoral and stress-free life is interrupted when a pack of wolves sets up camp in the nearby ravine.
