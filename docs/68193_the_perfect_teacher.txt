Title: The Perfect Teacher (2010)
Genres: TV Movie, Thriller, Drama
Director: Jim Donovan
Main Cast: Megan Park, David Charvet, Boti Bliss, Keeva Lynk, Amanda Tilson
Keywords: love triangle, teenage girl

Overview: A spoiled, selfish teenager becomes infatuated with her teacher. She befriends his daughter as a way of worming her way into the family, and sets about manipulating every aspect of his life.
