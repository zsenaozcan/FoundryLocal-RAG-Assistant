Title: Jail Bait (1954)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Edward D. Wood Jr.
Main Cast: Lyle Talbot, Herbert Rawlinson, Steve Reeves, Dolores Fuller, Clancy Malone
Keywords: blackmail, plastic surgeon

Overview: Don Gregor, the son of famous plastic surgeon Dr. Boris Gregor, begins to hang around with young criminal Vic Brady and carry a gun. The pair attempt an armed holdup, and when things start to go wrong Gregor accidentally kills a night watchman. Fearing that Gregor plans to turn himself in, Brady kills him and blackmails Dr. Gregor into giving him a new face.
