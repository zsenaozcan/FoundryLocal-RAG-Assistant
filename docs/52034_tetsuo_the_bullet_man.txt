Title: Tetsuo: The Bullet Man (2009)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction, Horror, Thriller
Director: Shinya Tsukamoto
Main Cast: Eric Bossick, Akiko Monô, Yûko Nakamura, Stephen Sarrazin, Tiger Charlie Gerhardt
Keywords: android, child murderer, sequel, metal, mutilations, japanese cyberpunk, tetsuo, part of trilogy

Overview: An American named Anthony is living and working in Tokyo and married to a Japanese woman. When their son is killed by the same driver who creates the Tetsuos in previous films, he makes the transformation into Tetsuo.
