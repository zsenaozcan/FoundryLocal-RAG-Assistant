Title: Immortal Beloved (1994)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance
Director: Bernard Rose
Main Cast: Gary Oldman, Jeroen Krabbé, Isabella Rossellini, Johanna ter Steege, Marco Hofschneider
Keywords: deafness, sister-in-law, character study, uncle nephew relationship, für elise

Overview: The life and death of the legendary Ludwig van Beethoven. Beside all the work he is known for, the composer once wrote a famous love letter to a nameless beloved and the movie tries to find out who this beloved was. Not easy as Beethoven has had many women in his life.
