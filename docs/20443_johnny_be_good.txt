Title: Johnny Be Good (1988)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Bud Smith 
Main Cast: Anthony Michael Hall, Robert Downey Jr., Uma Thurman, Paul Gleason, Steve James
Keywords: sport

Overview: It's recruiting time and despite being short and scrawny, Johnny Walker is America's hottest young football prospect. His dilemma: should he take one of the many offers from college talent scouts or should he attend the local state college with his girlfriend and give up his football career?
