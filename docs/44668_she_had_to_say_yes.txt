Title: She Had to Say Yes (1933)
Genres: Drama
Director: George Amy
Main Cast: Loretta Young, Winnie Lightner, Lyle Talbot, Regis Toomey, Hugh Herbert
Keywords: taxi, engagement, new york city

Overview: Stenographers at a department store office are recruited to be "customer girls" who entertain out-of-town buyers. When Loretta Young becomes one of the girls things turn ugly with a customer.
