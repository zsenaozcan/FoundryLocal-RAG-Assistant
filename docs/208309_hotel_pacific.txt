Title: Hotel Pacific (1975)
Genres: Drama
Director: Janusz Majewski
Main Cast: Marek Kondrat, Roman Wilhelmi, Roman Skamene, Cestmír Randa, Michał Pawlicki

Overview: Set in the early 1930s, a young man finds a job as a dishwasher in a hotel and quickly works his way up the ladder. Loosely based on the novel by Henryk Worcell.
