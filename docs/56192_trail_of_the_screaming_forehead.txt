Title: Trail of the Screaming Forehead (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Larry Blamire
Main Cast: Daniel Roebuck, Susan McConnell, Fay Masterson, Andrew Parks, H.M. Wynant

Overview: A small town infestation of crawling alien foreheads that begin attaching to people and taking them over collides with a scientist's experiments to extract foreheadazine and things go horribly horribly wrong.
