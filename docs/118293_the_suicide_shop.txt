Title: The Suicide Shop (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Animation
Director: Patrice Leconte
Main Cast: Bernard Alane, Isabella Spade, Kacey Mottet Klein, Isabelle Giami, Laurent Gendron
Keywords: depression, baby, suicide attempt, musical, animation, suicide by gunshot, suicide thoughts

Overview: A city where suicide is the only growing business. A shop where you can get everything to cater this urge. In the hand of one family for centuries.. till they get a happy baby. And he has to power to make all around him happy as well.
