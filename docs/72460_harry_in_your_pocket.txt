Title: Harry in Your Pocket (1973)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Bruce Geller
Main Cast: James Coburn, Michael Sarrazin, Trish Van Devere, Walter Pidgeon, Michael C. Gwynne

Overview: A master thief (James Coburn) and his drug-addicted partner teach two aspiring crooks (Michael Sarrazin, Trish Van Devere) how to steal wallets.
