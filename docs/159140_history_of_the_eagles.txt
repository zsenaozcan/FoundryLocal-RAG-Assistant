Title: History of the Eagles (2013)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Alison Ellwood
Main Cast: Don Henley, Glenn Frey, Timothy B. Schmit, Joe Walsh, David Geffen
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Alison Ellwood’s intimate, meticulously crafted patchwork of rare archival material, concert footage, and unseen home movies explores the evolution and enduring popularity of one of America’s truly defining bands.
