Title: Hounddog (2007)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Deborah Kampmeier
Main Cast: Dakota Fanning, Oliver Clayton-Luce, Isabelle Fuhrman, Cody Hanford, Piper Laurie
Keywords: independent film, woman director

Overview: A drama set in the American South, where a precocious, troubled girl finds a safe haven in the music and movement of Elvis Presley.
