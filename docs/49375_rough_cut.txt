Title: Rough Cut (1980)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Crime, Mystery, Romance
Director: Don Siegel
Main Cast: Burt Reynolds, Lesley-Anne Down, David Niven, Timothy West, Patrick Magee
Keywords: netherlands, jewel, jewel thief

Overview: A romantic comedy with action and suspense. Two sophisticated jewel thieves join forces to steal $30 million in uncut jewels. Despite a continuous exchange of quips they eventually become romantically involved.
