Title: Pretty Maids All in a Row (1971)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Roger Vadim
Main Cast: Rock Hudson, Angie Dickinson, Telly Savalas, John David Carson, Roddy McDowall
Keywords: california, high school, suspense, teacher, series of murders

Overview: In a California high school, a married teacher is the athletic coach and faculty advisor. He starts to have sex flings with his female students and eventually has to kill several of the girls to keep them quiet.
