Title: Tony 10 (2012)
Genres: Foreign, Family
Director: Mischa Kamp
Main Cast: Carlo Boszhard, Anna Drijver, Rifka Lodeizen, Loek Peters
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A family film which tells the unlikely and timeless story about almost ten year old Tony, whose father rises from being a crane driver to Secretary of State. As a result his parents get divorced and Tony does everything he can to bring them back together. He even calls in the help of the queen.
