Title: The Red Badge of Courage (1951)
Genres: Action, Drama, War, Western
Director: John Huston
Main Cast: Audie Murphy, Bill Mauldin, Douglas Dick, Robert Easton, Arthur Hunnicutt
Keywords: civil war

Overview: Truncated adaptation of Stephen Crane's novel about a Civil War Union soldier who stuggles to find the courage to fight in the heat of battle.
