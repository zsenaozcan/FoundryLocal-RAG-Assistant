Title: The Robber Symphony (1937)
Genres: Music
Director: Friedrich Fehér
Main Cast: Françoise Rosay, Magda Sonja, George Graves, Jim Gérald, Henri Valbel

Overview: A bag of loot is stashed in a piano that belongs to a family of traveling entertainers. A gang of robbers tries to get the loot back, and though they kddnap two members of the troop, the third, a young boy, has run off with the piano.
