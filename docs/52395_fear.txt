Title: Fear (1990)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Rockne S. O'Bannon
Main Cast: Ally Sheedy, Lauren Hutton, Michael O'Keefe, Stan Shaw, Dina Merrill
Keywords: carnival, strip club, knife, police, murder, serial killer, psychic, amusement park, ferris wheel, clairvoyant

Overview: Psychic Ally Sheedy helps police solve murders by mentally linking with the murderer. Then she discovers a murderer with the same talent - who wants to share the fear of his victims with her!
