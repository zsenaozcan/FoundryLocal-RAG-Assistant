Title: Stop Making Sense (1984)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Jonathan Demme
Main Cast: David Byrne, Tina Weymouth, Chris Frantz, Jerry Harrison, Steven Scales
Keywords: concert

Overview: David Byrne walks onto the stage and does a solo "Psycho Killer". Jerry Harrison, Tina Weymouth and Chris Frantz join him for two more songs. The crew is busy, still setting up. Then, three more musicians and two back-up singers join the band. Everybody sings, plays, harmonizes, dances, and runs. In this concert film, the Talking Heads hardly talk, don't stop, and always make sense.
