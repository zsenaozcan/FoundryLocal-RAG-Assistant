Title: Angels of the Universe (2000)
Genres: Drama
Director: Friðrik Þór Friðriksson
Main Cast: Ingvar Eggert Sigurðsson, Baltasar Kormákur, Björn Jörundur Friðbjörnsson, Hilmir Snær Guðnason, Þór Tulinius
Keywords: schizophrenia, suicide, mentally disabled, based on novel

Overview: The story of an Icelandic man and his slow descent into madness. Along his journey he meets Dagný, the initial cause of his breakdown. Other people he meets in the asylum have been committed for various reasons, such as signing cheques for Adolf Hitler and, believing themselves to be writing songs for the Beatles and telepathically transmitting to the band.
