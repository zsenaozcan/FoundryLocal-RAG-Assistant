Title: Attack of the Killer Tomatoes! (1978)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: John De Bello
Main Cast: David Miller, George Wilson, Sharon Taylor, J. Stephen Peace, Ernie Meyers
Keywords: mutation, aggression by plant, musical, tomato, vegetable, low-budget, parody, mad scientist, b movie, spoof

Overview: Attack of the Killer Tomatoes is a 1978 comedy film directed by John De Bello and starring David Miller. The film is a spoof of B-movies. Made on a budget of less than USD $100,000, the story involves tomatoes becoming sentient by unknown means and revolting against humanity.
