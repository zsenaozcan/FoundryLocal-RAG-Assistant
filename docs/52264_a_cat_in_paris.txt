Title: A Cat in Paris (2010)
Genres: Animation, Comedy
Director: Alain Gagnol
Main Cast: Dominique Blanc, Bruno Salomone, Jean Benguigui, Bernadette Lafont, Oriane Zani
Keywords: cat, walking on roof, mute girl, notre dame cathedral

Overview: A thrilling mystery that unfurls in the alleys and on the rooftops of the French capital, Paris, over the course of one adventurous evening.
