Title: Thomas in Love (2000)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Science Fiction, Romance, Foreign
Director: Pierre-Paul Renders
Main Cast: Benoît Verhaert, Aylin Yay, Magali Pinglaut, Micheline Hardy, Frédéric Topart
Keywords: phobia, agoraphobia

Overview: Thomas Thomas is agoraphobic, has a computer-generated girlfriend, and hasn't left his home in years. Can a prostitute convince him to leave cyberspace and his home for the real world?
