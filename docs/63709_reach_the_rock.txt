Title: Reach The Rock (1998)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: William Ryan
Main Cast: William Sadler, Alessandro Nivola, Bruce Norris, Karen Sillas, Brooke Langton

Overview: A small town troublemaker (Alessandro Nivola), directionless and alienated, ends up spending a night in a jail cell, where he and the police chief (William Sadler) engage in a battle of wills and wit.
