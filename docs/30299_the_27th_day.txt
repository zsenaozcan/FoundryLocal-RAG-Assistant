Title: The 27th Day (1957)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: William Asher
Main Cast: Gene Barry, Valerie French, George Voskovec, Arnold Moss, Stefan Schnabel
Keywords: decision, alien

Overview: Five individuals from five nations, including the "Superpowers," USA, USSR, and China, suddenly find themselves on an alien spacecraft. An alien gives each a container holding capsules. No power on earth can open a given container except a mental command from the person to whom it is given. Each person has been provided with the power of life and death.
