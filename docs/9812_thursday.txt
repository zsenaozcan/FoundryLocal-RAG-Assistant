Title: Thursday (1998)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Skip Woods
Main Cast: Thomas Jane, Paula Marshall, Aaron Eckhart, James Le Gros, Paulina Porizkova
Keywords: drug dealer, marijuana

Overview: Casey has given up drug dealing for a suburban idyll in Houston, a job as an architect and a new wife. They are even planning to adopt a child. But Casey's past arrives on the doorstep in the shape of Nick, an old business partner. And all hell breaks loose!
