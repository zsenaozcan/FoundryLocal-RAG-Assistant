Title: Long Live Ghosts! (1977)
Genres: Family, Comedy, Music, Fantasy
Director: Oldřich Lipský
Main Cast: Jiří Sovák, Vlastimil Brodský, Dana Vávrová, Jiří Procházka, Lubomír Lipský
Keywords: fairy tale, musical, pohádka

Overview: A trespassing gang of boys encounter a ghost in the ruins of a castle. When they rescue the ghost who is caught in a trapset, they become allies in a fight to prevent ruins from being turned into mushroom farm by the city authorities.
