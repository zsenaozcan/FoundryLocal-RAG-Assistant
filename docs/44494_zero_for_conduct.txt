Title: Zero for Conduct (1933)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jean Vigo
Main Cast: Jean Dasté, Robert le Flon, Du Verron, Delphin, Léon Larive
Keywords: boarding school, cigarette smoking, dinner, revolt, boys' school

Overview: In a repressive boarding school with rigid rules of behavior, four boys decide to rebel against the director on a celebration day.
