Title: Nightfall (2012)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Crime
Director: Roy Chow Hin-Yeung
Main Cast: Simon Yam, Nick Cheung, Kay Tse, Janice Man, Michael Wong
Keywords: thriller, mystery

Overview: Inspector Lam investigates the brutal murder of Tsui, a famous musician. Wong, the designated suspect, just spent 20 years in jail for the murder of Tsui's daughter but has always claimed his innocence.
