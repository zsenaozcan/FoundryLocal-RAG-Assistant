Title: The Sleep Room (1998)
Genres: Drama
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Nicola Cavendish, Eric Peterson, Nicholas Campbell

Overview: At the height of the cold war, the C.I.A. secretly funded Dr. Ewen Cameron, director of the Allan Memorial Institute, and his experimental research into brainwashing techniques. Twenty-five years later, the last nine survivors of those gruesome experiments fight to expose the truth...of The Sleep Room.
