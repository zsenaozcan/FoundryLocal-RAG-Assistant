Title: Deep in the Valley (2009)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Christian Forte
Main Cast: Rachel Specter, Denise Richards, Brendan Hines, Chris Pratt, Blanca Soto
Keywords: sex comedy

Overview: Best friends, Carl and Lester, find themselves magically transported into an alternate universe straight out of a real-life adult movie. Hilarity ensues as they embark upon a journey of adult-themed mayhem while Carl ultimately finds true love in the most unlikely of places.
