Title: The Last Joint Venture (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama, Mystery
Director: Ulrik Imtiaz Rolfsen
Main Cast: Kristoffer Joner, Nicolai Cleve Broch, Thomas Bo Larsen, Kåre Conradi, Gard B. Eidsvold
Keywords: drug smuggle, drama, cannabis, crude humor, stoned, money, marijuana, adult humor, drug humor, drug use

Overview: The decade of the hippies is at an end and the 80's are approaching fast. Carl and Robert are two doped out guys who lives in harmony with the world around them and let life pass by in an eternal hash high. They have been peddling hashish for a decade, hitting an all time high with this last batch: 45 Kilos of pure, high grade Nepalese Hashish. Following the delivery, the police are on their tail.
