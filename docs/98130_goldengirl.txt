Title: Goldengirl (1979)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Joseph Sargent
Main Cast: Susan Anton, James Coburn, Robert Culp, Curd Jürgens, Leslie Caron
Keywords: olympic games

Overview: A neo-Nazi Doctor tries to make a superwoman of his daughter who has been specially fed, exercised &amp; Conditioned since she was a child to run in the Olympics.
