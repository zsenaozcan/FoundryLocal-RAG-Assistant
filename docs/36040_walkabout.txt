Title: Walkabout (1971)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Nicolas Roeg
Main Cast: Jenny Agutter, Luc Roeg, David Gulpilil, John Meillon, Robert McDarra
Keywords: suicide, male nudity, female nudity, hunter, based on novel, brother sister relationship, picnic, wilderness, camel, australia

Overview: Under the pretense of having a picnic, a geologist takes his teenage daughter and 6-year-old son into the Australian outback and attempts to shoot them. When he fails, he turns the gun on himself, and the two city-bred children must contend with harsh wilderness alone. They are saved by a chance encounter with an Aborigine boy who shows them how to survive, and in the process underscores the disharmony between nature and modern life.
