Title: Joan the Maid 1: The Battles (1994)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Jacques Rivette
Main Cast: Sandrine Bonnaire, Tatiana Moukhine, Baptiste Roussillon, Jean-Marie Richier, Bruno Wolkowitch

Overview: A part of Joan of Arc's life. At the beginning, Jeanne (Joan) has already left Domremy, she is trying to convince a captain to escort her to the Dauphin. It ends during Jeanne's first battle, at Orleans. Meanwhile, Jeanne is depicted more as a warrior than a saint (all cliches are avoided), with only her faith for strength.
