Title: In a Better World (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Susanne Bier
Main Cast: Mikael Persbrandt, Trine Dyrholm, Ulrich Thomsen, William Jøhnk Nielsen, Markus Rygaard
Keywords: africa, friendship, bully, hospital, woman director

Overview: The lives of two Danish families cross each other, and an extraordinary but risky friendship comes into bud. But loneliness, frailty and sorrow lie in wait.
