Title: Ten Minutes Older: The Cello (2002)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama, Romance
Director: Jean-Luc Godard
Main Cast: Valeria Bruni Tedeschi, Amit Arroz, Tarun Bedi, Maria Ludovica Bernardi, Chiara Mastalli
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Collection of short films the summaries of which include; a foreign man moving to Italy, getting married and having a child; a four split scene short involving plot-less images of old people with television sets for heads, a beautiful woman having sex, and overall confusion; and an old man reminiscing over his youth.
