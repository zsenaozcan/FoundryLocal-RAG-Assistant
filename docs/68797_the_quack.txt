Title: The Quack (1982)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Jerzy Hoffman
Main Cast: Jerzy Binczycki, Anna Dymna, Bożena Dykiel, Arkadiusz Bazak

Overview: A famous surgeon is beaten by drunken bullies, loses his memory and cannot recollect who he was before. He gets to a village, lives in a not so well to do family and becomes the Quack - he slowly regains his talent for medicine and saves the lives of several village patients.
