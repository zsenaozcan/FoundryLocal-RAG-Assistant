Title: Il Divo (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Paolo Sorrentino
Main Cast: Toni Servillo, Anna Bonaiuto, Giulio Bosetti, Flavio Bucci, Carlo Buccirosso
Keywords: italy, diva, politics, independent film, mafia

Overview: The narration spans the period since the seventh election of Andreotti as Prime Minister of Italy in 1992, until the trial in which he was accused of collusion with the Mafia.
