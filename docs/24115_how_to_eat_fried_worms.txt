Title: How to Eat Fried Worms (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Bob Dolman
Main Cast: Luke Benward, Hallie Kate Eisenberg, Adam Hicks, Austin Rogers, Alexander Gould

Overview: During the first day of his new school year, a fifth grade boy squares off against a bully and winds up accepting a dare that could change the balance of power within the class.
