Title: Becoming Cary Grant (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Mark Kidel
Main Cast: Cary Grant, Jonathan Pryce, Judy Balaban, Mark Glancy, Barbara Jaynes
Keywords: from rags to riches, hollywood, old hollywood

Overview: For the first time one of Hollywood's greatest stars tells his own story, in his own words. From a childhood of poverty to global fame, Cary Grant, the ultimate self-made star, explores his own screen image and what it took to create it.
