Title: The Angriest Man in Brooklyn (2014)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Phil Alden Robinson
Main Cast: Robin Williams, Mila Kunis, Peter Dinklage, Melissa Leo, James Earl Jones
Keywords: dark comedy

Overview: After learning that a brain aneurysm will kill him in about 90 minutes, a perpetually unhappy man struggles to come to terms with his fate and make amends with everyone he has ever hurt.
