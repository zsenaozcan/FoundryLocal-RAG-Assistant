Title: Miss Sadie Thompson (1953)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Curtis Bernhardt
Main Cast: Rita Hayworth, José Ferrer, Aldo Ray, Russell Collins, Diosa Costello
Keywords: missionary, musical, south pacific, u.s. marine

Overview: Sadie Thompson winds up stranded on an island and while her boat is being quarantined, she manages to stir up the blood of every marine on the base.
