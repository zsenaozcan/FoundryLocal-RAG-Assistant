Title: Bloody Reunion (2006)
Genres: Mystery, Horror, Thriller
Director: Dae-wung Lim
Main Cast: Oh Mi-hee, Jang Seong-won, Kim Eung-Soo, Kim Yeong-seon, Lee Ji-hyeon

Overview: Mi-ja, who has been taking care of a retired teacher, Ms Park, invites several of her former students to her house 16 years after their graduation. Since the end of her teaching career, Park has been residing in the countryside, suffering from an illness while an ex-student, the kind and pretty Mi-ja cares for her.
