Title: Camille Claudel 1915 (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bruno Dumont
Main Cast: Juliette Binoche, Jean-Luc Vincent, Robert Leroy, Armelle Leroy-Rolland, Emmanuel Kauffman
Keywords: france, nun, biography, sculpture, mental asylum, persecution, based on true events, separation from family, estranged brother, religious institution

Overview: Winter, 1915. Confined by her family to an asylum in the South of France - where she will never sculpt again - the chronicle of Camille Claudel's reclusive life, as she waits for a visit from her brother, Paul Claudel.
