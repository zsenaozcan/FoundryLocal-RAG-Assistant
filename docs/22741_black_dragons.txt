Title: Black Dragons (1942)
Genres: Horror, War
Director: William Nigh
Main Cast: Bela Lugosi, Joan Barclay, George Pembroke, Clayton Moore
Keywords: nazis, embassy, sabotage

Overview: It is prior to the commencement of World War II, and Japan's fiendish Black Dragon Society is hatching an evil plot with the Nazis. They instruct a brilliant scientist, Dr. Melcher, to travel to Japan on a secret mission. There he operates on six Japanese conspirators, transforming them to resemble six American leaders.
