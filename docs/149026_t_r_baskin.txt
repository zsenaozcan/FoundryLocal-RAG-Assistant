Title: T.R. Baskin (1971)
Genres: Drama
Director: Herbert Ross
Main Cast: Candice Bergen, Peter Boyle, James Caan, Marcia Rodd, Erin Allin O'Reilly
Keywords: runaway, cold, city, confrontation

Overview: Enthusiastic young woman runs away to Chicago to start a new life. She is soon confronted with the emotional coldness of the big city and has to search for her place in the scheme of things.
