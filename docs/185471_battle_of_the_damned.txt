Title: Battle of the Damned (2013)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction, Adventure
Director: Christopher Hatton
Main Cast: Dolph Lundgren, Matt Doran, David Field, Melanie Zanetti, Jen Sung
Keywords: rescue mission, zombie, robot, virus

Overview: Following a deadly viral outbreak, private military soldier Max Gatling leads a handful of survivors and a ragtag band of robots against an army of the infected.
