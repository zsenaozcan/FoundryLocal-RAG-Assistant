Title: Freejack (1992)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Geoff Murphy
Main Cast: Emilio Estevez, Mick Jagger, Rene Russo, Anthony Hopkins, Jonathan Banks
Keywords: future, chase, time travel, dystopia, immortality

Overview: Time-traveling bounty hunters find a doomed race-car driver in the past and bring him to 2009 New York, where his mind will be replaced with that of a terminally ill billionaire.
