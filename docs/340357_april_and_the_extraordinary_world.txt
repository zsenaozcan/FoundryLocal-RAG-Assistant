Title: April and the Extraordinary World (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Adventure, Animation, Comedy, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Franck Ekinci
Main Cast: Marion Cotillard, Jean Rochefort, Olivier Gourmet, Philippe Katerine, Marc-André Grondin
Keywords: france, cat, grandfather, scientist, electricity, talking cat
