Title: Gamera (1965)
Genres: Action, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Noriaki Yuasa
Main Cast: Eiji Funakoshi, Harumi Kiritachi, Junichirô Yamashiko, Yoshiro Uchida, Michiko Sugata
Keywords: japan, monster, turtle, giant monster, tokusatsu, kaiju, fire breathing, gamera, giant turtle

Overview: A nuclear explosion in the far north unleashes Gamera, the legendary flying turtle, from his sleep under the ice. In his search for energy, Gamera wreaks havoc over the entire world, and it's up to the scientists, assisted by a young boy with a strange sympathic link to the monster, to put a stop to Gamera's rampage.
