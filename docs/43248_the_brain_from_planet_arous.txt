Title: The Brain from Planet Arous (1957)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Nathan H. Juran
Main Cast: John Agar, Joyce Meadows, Robert Fuller, Thomas Browne Henry, Henry Travis
Keywords: alien, independent film, b movie, desert, axe murder, evil brain

Overview: An evil alien brain, bent on world domination, takes over the body of an atomic scientist, while a "good" alien brain inhabits the body of the scientist's dog and waits for an opportunity to defeat the evil brain.
