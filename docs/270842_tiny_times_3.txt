Title: Tiny Times 3 (2014)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Jingming Guo
Main Cast: Yang Mi, Amber Kuo, Haden Kuo, Evonne Hsieh, Kai Ko
Keywords: shanghai, female friendship, melodrama, fashion, fashion magazine

Overview: Lin Xiao, Gu Li, Nan Xiang, Tang Wanru - these four best friends, after waving goodbye to their school campus and entering the workforce, they are faced with various challenges in their life: friendship, love, and career. Together, they become lost in life, having long forgotten their past courage.
