Title: Shalako (1968)
Genres: Western
Director: Edward Dmytryk
Main Cast: Sean Connery, Brigitte Bardot, Jack Hawkins, Stephen Boyd, Peter van Eyck
Keywords: native american, gunfighter, hunting party

Overview: Sean Connery is Shalako, a guide in the old West who has to rescue an aristocratic British hunting party from Indians and bandits.
