Title: The Hound of the Baskervilles (1939)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Sidney Lanfield
Main Cast: Richard Greene, Basil Rathbone, Wendy Barrie, Nigel Bruce, Lionel Atwill
Keywords: sherlock holmes, sequel, basil rathbone

Overview: On his uncle's death Sir Henry Baskerville returns from abroad and opens up the ancestral hall on the desolate moors of Devonshire. Holmes uncovers a plot to have Sir Henry murdered by a terrible trained hound. First casting of Rathbone and Bruce as Holmes and Watson.
