Title: Hey, Boo: Harper Lee & To Kill a Mockingbird (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Mary McDonagh Murphy
Main Cast: Mary Badham, Tom Brokaw, Rosanne Cash, Alice Finch Lee, James McBride
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Hey, Boo explores the life of reclusive author Nelle Harper Lee, shedding light on the context and history of her Pulitzer Prize-winning novel To Kill a Mockingbird.
