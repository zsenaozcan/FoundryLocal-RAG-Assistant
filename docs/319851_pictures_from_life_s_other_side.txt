Title: Pictures from Life's Other Side (1971)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Jim McBride
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: A sequel to My Girlfriend's Wedding. Pictures from Life’s Other Side  focuses almost entirely on a later cross-country trip, this one also including Clarissa’s illegitimate, preadolescent son Joe and a couple of dogs, with Clarissa and Joe taking over the commentary and the whole family trading off various sound and camera duties
