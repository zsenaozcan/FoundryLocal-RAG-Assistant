Title: Khadak (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Peter Brosens
Main Cast: Batzul Khayankhyarvaa, Tsetsegee Byamba, Banzar Damchaa, Tserendarizav Dashnyam, Dugarsuren Dagvadorj
Keywords: countryside, adolescence, shepherd, meditation, revolution, vagabund, living together, religion and supernatural, cultural revolution, resettlement

Overview: Set in the frozen steppes of Mongolia, a young nomad confronted with his destiny after animals fall victim to a plague which threatens to eradicate nomadism.
