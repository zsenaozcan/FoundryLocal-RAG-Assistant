Title: Sandor slash Ida (2005)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Henrik Georgsson
Main Cast: Andrej Lunusjkin, André Lidholm, Adam Lundgren, Aliette Opheim

Overview: Ida lives hard and fast with too much drinking and a string of boyfriends, Sandor has very few friends and his mother insists that he practices ballet. They meet in a chat room and despite appearances they discover they have much in common. Sandor and Ida lives in different cities and when Sandor unexpectedly visits her it ends with disaster.
