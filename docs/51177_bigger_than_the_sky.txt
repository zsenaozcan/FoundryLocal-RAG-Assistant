Title: Bigger Than the Sky (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Al Corley
Main Cast: Amy Smart, Sean Astin, John Corbett

Overview: After being dumped by his girlfriend, a man stuck in a deadend life decides to audition for a small role in a local community theatre's production of Cyrano de Bergerac. Despite having no experience as an actor, he lands the lead role, which wreaks havoc upon his life.
