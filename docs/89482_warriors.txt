Title: Warriors (2002)
Genres: Adventure, War, Drama
Director: Daniel Calparsoro
Main Cast: Eloy Azorín, Eduardo Noriega, Rubén Ochandiano, Carla Pérez, Jordi Vilches
Keywords: kosovo, platoon

Overview: Union general William T. Sherman said, "War is all hell." He might easily have been referring to the conflict in Kosovo. Director Daniel Calparsoro lenses a tense drama that revolves around a platoon of Spanish soldiers, following the innocent young men as they step from the training field into a hell for which nothing could have prepared them.
