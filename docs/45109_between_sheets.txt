Title: Between Sheets (2008)
Genres: Romance
Director: Michael DeLuise
Main Cast: Reynaldo Gianecchini, Paolla Oliveira

Overview: Two strangers meet in a nightclub and end up spending the night together in a hotel. Roberto has just came out of a failed relationship and Paula wants to enjoy her last moments of freedom before getting married the next day. After making a pact of sincerity, they spend a night filled with interesting conversations and passion.
