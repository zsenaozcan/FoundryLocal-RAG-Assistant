Title: Love at First Hiccup (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Barbara Topsøe-Rothenborg
Main Cast: Devon Werkheiser, Scout Taylor-Compton, Tania Verafield, Ken Luckey, Adam J. Bernstein

Overview: A high school romantic comedy about freshman Victor who has contracted a case of Anya-itis, (acute and incurable love passion for high school senior Anja.) And why shouldn't he?
