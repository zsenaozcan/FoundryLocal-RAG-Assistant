Title: Family Guy Presents: Seth and Alex's almost live comedy show (2009)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Music
Director: Jackson Douglas
Main Cast: Seth MacFarlane, Alex Borstein, Walter Murphy

Overview: A commercial-free variety special starring Seth MacFarlane and his "Family Guy" co-star Alex Borstein. The half-hour special will highlight the duo's subversive and unique comedic sensibilities and feature original animation, live-action performances of "Family Guy's" most memorable musical numbers, comedy sketches and surprise celebrity guests.
