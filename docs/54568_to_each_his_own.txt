Title: To Each His Own (1946)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mitchell Leisen
Main Cast: Olivia de Havilland, Mary Anderson, Roland Culver, Phillip Terry, Bill Goodwin
Keywords: small town, cosmetics, adopted son

Overview: During World War I, small-town girl Josephine Norris has an illegitimate son by an itinerant pilot. After a scheme to adopt him ends up giving him to another family, she devotes her life to loving him from afar.
