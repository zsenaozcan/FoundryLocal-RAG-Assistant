Title: The Match (1999)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Mick Davis
Main Cast: Max Beesley, Richard E. Grant, Laura Fraser, Isla Blair, James Cosmo
Keywords: sport

Overview: The Match is a romantic comedy set against the story of a grudge football match between two pubs. The prize for the winner of the centenary match is the the closure of their opponent's bar. The Match was mainly filmed around Straiton in Ayrshire.
