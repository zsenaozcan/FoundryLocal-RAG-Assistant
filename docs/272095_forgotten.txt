Title: Forgotten (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Carlos Bolado
Main Cast: Damián Alcázar, Carla Ortiz, Rafael Ferro, Ana Celentano, Olga Fonda

Overview: José, a Bolivian retired general, is lying on his deathbed after a heart attack. Tormented by memories of his involvement in Operation Condor and invaded by deep remorse,  he decides to tell his only son his biggest secrets in search of redemption.
