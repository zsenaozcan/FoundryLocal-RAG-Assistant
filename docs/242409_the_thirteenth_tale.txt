Title: The Thirteenth Tale (2013)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: James Kent
Main Cast: Vanessa Redgrave, Olivia Colman, Sophie Turner, Alexandra Roach, Steven Mackintosh

Overview: Margaret Lea, a young biographer, attempts to interview writer Vida Winter, whose health is deteriorating. Vida has previously denied access to the secrets of her life and childhood home. Vida tells her story to Margaret, helping Margaret to confront her past.
