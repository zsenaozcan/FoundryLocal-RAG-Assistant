Title: My Sweet Charlie (1970)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Lamont Johnson
Main Cast: Patty Duke, Al Freeman, Jr., Ford Rainey, William Hardy, Chris Wilson
Keywords: interracial romance

Overview: A pregnant white Southern girl and a black New York lawyer, both on the run in rural Texas, meet up in a boarded-up, abandoned house and realize they both need each other in order to survive.
