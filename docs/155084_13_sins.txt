Title: 13 Sins (2014)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Daniel Stamm
Main Cast: Mark Webber, Devon Graye, Rutina Wesley, Ron Perlman, Pruitt Taylor Vince

Overview: A bright but meek salesman, drowning in debt as he's about to get married, receives a mysterious phone call informing him that he's on a hidden camera game show where he must execute 13 tasks to receive a multi-million dollar cash prize.
