Title: Miracle in Harlem (1948)
Genres: Romance, Mystery
Director: Jack Kemp
Main Cast: Sheila Guyse, Stepin Fetchit, Hilda Offley, Creighton Thompson, Kenneth Freeman
Keywords: extortion, store, swindle, harlem, poverty row, 1940s

Overview: A crooked real estate tycoon tricks a trusting young woman out of her small candy store. When he is found dead, the girl is suspected of the crime.
