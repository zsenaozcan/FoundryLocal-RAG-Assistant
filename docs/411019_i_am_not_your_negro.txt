Title: I Am Not Your Negro (2017)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Raoul Peck
Main Cast: Samuel L. Jackson, James Baldwin, Malcolm X, Martin Luther King, Medgar Evers
Keywords: race politics, martin luther king, documentary, racism, african american, interviews, james baldwin, malcom x

Overview: Working from the text of James Baldwin’s unfinished final novel, director Raoul Peck creates a meditation on what it means to be Black in the United States.
