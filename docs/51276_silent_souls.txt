Title: Silent Souls (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Aleksey Fedorchenko
Main Cast: Yuliya Aug, Igor Sergeev, Viktor Sukhorukov, Yuriy Tsurilo, Vyacheslav Melekhov
Keywords: female nudity, river, russia, narration, water, dead body, chubby woman, typewriter, ancient culture, love for wife

Overview: Present days. A man and his companion go on a journey to cremate the dead body of the former beloved wife, on a riverbank in the area where they spent their honeymoon.
