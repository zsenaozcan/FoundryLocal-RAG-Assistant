Title: The Perfect Assistant (2008)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Douglas Jackson
Main Cast: Josie Davis, Chris Potter, Rachel Hunter, Veronique Natalie Szalankiewicz, Jason Harper

Overview: Rachel Parsons is the perfect assistant, but she's completely in love with her boss. She's got a secret obsession: be his wife and have kids. The problem is that he already has a family.
