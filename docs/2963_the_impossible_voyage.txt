Title: The Impossible Voyage (1904)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Georges Méliès
Main Cast: Georges Méliès, Fernande Albeny, Jeanne d'Alcy, May de Lavergne
Keywords: moon, space travel, space, steampunk, crash, silent film, voyage, catastrophe, short

Overview: Using every known means of transportation, several savants from the Geographic Society undertake a journey through the Alps to the Sun which finishes under the sea.
