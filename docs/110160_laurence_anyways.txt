Title: Laurence Anyways (2012)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Xavier Dolan
Main Cast: Melvil Poupaud, Suzanne Clément, Nathalie Baye, Monia Chokri, Yves Jacques
Keywords: homosexuality, transgender, family, lgbt, drag, romantic drama, social issues, self-liberation, fearless, sex change operation

Overview: Set in the 1980s and 1990s, a man tries to salvage his relationship with his fiancée after revealing to her his aspirations of becoming a woman.  This is the story of a wild and unusual love.
