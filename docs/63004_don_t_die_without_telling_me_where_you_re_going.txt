Title: Don't Die Without Telling Me Where You're Going (1995)
Genres: Drama
Director: Eliseo Subiela
Main Cast: Darío Grandinetti, Mariana Arias
Keywords: magic

Overview: It is the fantastic story of Leopoldo, a lonely film projectionist who has invented a machine to record his dreams. Through a series of odd events, Leopoldo discovers that he is the reincarnation of one of the inventors of cinema, and that a woman he sees in his dreams is his eternal companion on a trip through centuries.
