Title: Shake It (2001)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Hella Joof
Main Cast: Mads Mikkelsen, Troels Lyby, Charlotte Munck, Jesper Lohmann, Nikolaj Steen
Keywords: gay, infidelity, bisexuality, marriage, homosexuality, gay relationship, woman director

Overview: Jacob is a young man used to getting everything he wants. For several years, he has been living in a happy homosexual partnership with Jørgen, and one night Jacob decides to pop the big question to Jørgen. Jørgen happily accepts Jacobs marriage proposal, but then something happens: Jacob falls in love with a girl, and not just any girl. The girl is Caroline, married to Jørgens younger brother Tom.
