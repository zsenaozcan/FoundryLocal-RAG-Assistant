Title: Tongan Ninja (2002)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Jason Stutter
Main Cast: Jemaine Clement, Sam Manu, Linda Tseng, Raybon Kan, David Fane
Keywords: ninja fighter

Overview: The Tongan Ninja is dispatched to the island nation of New Zealand in order to help a brother of his master with his floundering Chinese restaurant. But the mysterious Mister Big stands in the eatery's way as he sends numerous villains such as Knife Man, Gun Man, and the super-sexy Action Fighter who may know a lot about the hero.
