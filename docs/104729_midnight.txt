Title: Midnight (1982)
Genres: Horror, Comedy, Romance
Director: John A. Russo
Main Cast: Melanie Verlin, Lawrence Tierney, John Hall, Charles Jackson

Overview: A teenage girl runs away from home because police officer/stepfather puts the moves on her. Hitchhiking to California, she's picked up by two guys who are also traveling cross-country. Along the way, they decide to camp out in the woods and run across a family of Satanists who keep their dead mother in the attic.
