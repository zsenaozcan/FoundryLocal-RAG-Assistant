Title: Kisaragi (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Mystery
Director: Yûichi Satô
Main Cast: Shun Oguri, Yūsuke Santamaria, Keisuke Koide, Muga Tsukaji, Teruyuki Kagawa

Overview: The fanclub of a deceased Japanese idol gather together for the one year anniversary of her death. By combining forces they try to unearth the true circumstances surrounding her supposed suicide.
