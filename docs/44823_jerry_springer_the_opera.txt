Title: Jerry Springer: The Opera (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Peter Orton
Main Cast: David Bedella, David Soul, Leon Craig, Carrie Ellis, Alison Jiear
Keywords: musical

Overview: 'Jerry Springer-The Opera' tells the story of a day in the job of world famous talk-show host, Jerry Springer. Jerry has to sort out a number of guests problems; including a man who wants to dress up as a baby, a man with 3 lovers (one of them a transexual) and a fat woman who wants to be a pole dancer. However, on this particular day, something out of the ordinary will happen...
