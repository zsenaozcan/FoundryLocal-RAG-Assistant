Title: The Blue Elephant (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Horror, Mystery
Director: Marwan Hamed
Main Cast: Karim Abdel Aziz, Khaled El Sawy, Lebleba, Sherine Reda, Dareen Haddad

Overview: Psychiatrist Dr. Yehia returns to El-Abbaseya Hospital five years after the death of his family. His return coincide with the arrival of a new patient, Sherif, a friend from college, who is accused of murder.
