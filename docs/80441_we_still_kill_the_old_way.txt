Title: We Still Kill the Old Way (1967)
Genres: Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Elio Petri
Main Cast: Gian Maria Volonté, Irene Papas, Gabriele Ferzetti, Salvo Randone, Laura Nucci
Keywords: love triangle, political killing

Overview: A leftist professor wants the truth about two men killed during a hunting party; but the mafia, the Church and corrupt politicians don't want him to learn it.
