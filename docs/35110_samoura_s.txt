Title: Samouraïs (2002)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Giordano Gederlini
Main Cast: Cyril Mourali, Maï Anh Le, Yasuaki Kurata, Saïd Serrari, Santi Sudaros

Overview: An ageless Demon Warrior is magically impregnated into the womb of a Parisian Kung-Fu Master's girlfriend. But what is the Demon's relationship with a new-release blockbuster fighting game?
