Title: Murphy's Romance (1985)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Martin Ritt
Main Cast: Sally Field, James Garner, Brian Kerwin, Corey Haim, Dennis Burkley
Keywords: single mother, divorce, horse ranch, eligible bachelor, horse boarder

Overview: Emma is a divorced woman with a teen aged boy who moves into a small town and tries to make a go of a horse ranch. Murphy is the town druggist who steers business her way. Things are going along predictably until her ex husband shows up, needing a place to stay. The three of them form an intricate circle, Emma's son liking Murphy, but desperately wanting his father back.
