Title: Ditirambo (1969)
Genres: Mystery, Comedy, Drama
Director: Gonzalo Suárez
Main Cast: Gonzalo Suárez, Yelena Samarina, José María Prada, Charo López, Luis Ciges
Keywords: honest

Overview: Considered by some as the first modern Spanish cinema film, “Ditirambo” retrieves a character who had already been Gonzalo Suárez’s literary life in his novel.
