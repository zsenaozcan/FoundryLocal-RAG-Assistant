Title: Keep 'Em Flying (1941)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Arthur Lubin
Main Cast: Bud Abbott, Lou Costello, Martha Raye, Carol Bruce, William Gargan
Keywords: musical

Overview: When a barnstorming stunt pilot decides to join the air corps, his two goofball assistants decide to go with him. Since the two are Abbott &amp; Costello, the air corps doesn't know what it's in for.
