Title: Chasing Madoff (2010)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jeff Prosserman
Main Cast: Frank Casey, Neil Chelo, Gaytri Kachroo, Harry Markopolos
Keywords: corruption, court case, scandal, fraud, investigation, wall street, black money, economics, ponzi scheme, stock market

Overview: A look at how one investigator spent ten years trying to expose Bernie Madoff's massive Ponzi scheme that scammed an estimated $18 billion from investors.
