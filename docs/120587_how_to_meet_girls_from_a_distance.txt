Title: How to Meet Girls from a Distance (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Dean Hewison
Main Cast: Richard Falkner, Scarlet Hemingway, Jonathan Brugh

Overview: The peeping tom rom-com ‘How To Meet Girls From A Distance’ is a funny and heartwarming film that looks at how far we go for a crush. Premiering in Wellington as part of the New Zealand International Film Festival, How To Meet Girls From A Distance tells the story of Toby. Trying to win the heart of the beautiful Phoebe, he is determined to become the man of her dreams.
