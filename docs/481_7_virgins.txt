Title: 7 Virgins (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alberto Rodríguez
Main Cast: Juan José Ballesta, Jesús Carroza, Antonio Dechent, Loles León, Muriel
Keywords: adolescence, hip-hop, socially deprived family, juvenile prison, grandmother grandson relationship, concrete slabs building, class, teacher, wedding, suburb

Overview: Tano is 16-years-old and is already sitting in jail. In 48 hours he’s a free man and off to the wedding of his brother. In the two days he recounts his neighborhood in a section of Sevilla.
