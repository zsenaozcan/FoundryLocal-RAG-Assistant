Title: Farmageddon (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Kristin Canty
Main Cast: Linda Faillace, Mark McAfee, Joel Salatin, Jackie Stowers, Eric Wagoner
Keywords: milk, organic agriculture

Overview: The story of a mom whose son healed from all allergies and asthma after consuming raw milk, and real food from farms. It depicts people all over the country who formed food co-ops and private clubs to get these foods, and how they were raided by state and local governments.
