Title: The White Storm (2013)
Genres: Action, Drama, Mystery, Crime
Director: Benny Chan
Main Cast: Nick Cheung, Lau Ching-Wan, Louis Koo, Yuan Quan, Ben Lam
Keywords: brotherhood, undercover cop, gunfight, drug trade

Overview: An operation against the notorious druglord in Thailand changed the destiny of a HK narcotic team- the workaholic captain lost his men and bright career; the loyal officer was abandoned and went missing; the undercover cop who was salvaged chose to walk away from the past. Several years later, a drug dealing-related crime brought them together for a final face-off.
