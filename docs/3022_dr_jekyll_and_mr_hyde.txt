Title: Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde (1941)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Victor Fleming
Main Cast: Spencer Tracy, Ingrid Bergman, Lana Turner, Donald Crisp, Ian Hunter
Keywords: double life, classic horror, jekyll and hyde

Overview: Dr. Jekyll believes good and evil exist in everyone and creates a potion that allows his evil side, Mr. Hyde, to come to the fore. He faces horrible consequences when he lets his dark side run amok.
