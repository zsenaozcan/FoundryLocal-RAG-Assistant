Title: Nobody Will Speak of Us When We're Dead (1995)
Genres: Drama
Director: Agustín Díaz Yanes
Main Cast: Victoria Abril, Pilar Bardem, Federico Luppi, Ángel Alcázar, María Asquerino
Keywords: drug mule, prostitution

Overview: Escaping gangsters trying to kill her because of being witness to a crime, Gloria (Victoria Abril), a young woman of lower class, comes back to Madrid, Spain and to her family. There she tries to find work and earn some money.
