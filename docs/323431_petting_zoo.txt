Title: Petting Zoo (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Micah Magee
Main Cast: Devon Keller, Deztiny Gonzales, Jocko Sims, Kiowa Tucker, Adrienne Harrell
Keywords: poverty, teenage pregnancy, woman director

Overview: A story of love, sex and teen pregnancy in San Antonio, Texas. Petting Zoo is the portrait of a young woman coming into her own, in an environment that does not always present ideal circumstances.
