Title: Iron Jawed Angels (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Katja von Garnier
Main Cast: Hilary Swank, Anjelica Huston, Molly Parker, Margo Martindale, Frances O'Connor
Keywords: independent film, woman director

Overview: Defiant young activists take the women's suffrage movement by storm, putting their lives at risk to help American women win the right to vote.
