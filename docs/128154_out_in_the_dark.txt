Title: Out in the Dark (2012)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Michael Mayer
Main Cast: Michael Aloni, Nicholas Jacob, Jamil Khoury, Loai Nofi, Alon Pdut
Keywords: homosexuality

Overview: Two young men — a Palestinian grad student and an Israeli lawyer — meet and fall in love amidst personal and political intrigue in this striking debut feature from Israeli director Michael Mayer.
