Title: Grandma Got Run Over by a Reindeer (2000)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Phil Roman
Main Cast: Susan Blu, Alex Doduk, Jim Fisher

Overview: Based on the hit song that became a worldwide holiday phenomenon, Grandma Got Run Over By A Reindeer is a wild and wacky escapade that will knock you off your feet! When his beloved Grandma appears to be missing on Christmas Eve, young Jake Spankenheimer goes on the misadventure of a lifetime to try and prove that Santa Claus is indeed real. With his spirit, smarts and determination, Jake must outmaneuver his greedy and curvaceous Cousin Mel (Michele Lee) and the all-powerful businessman Austin Bucks to the disbelief of his doting parents, rebellious sister and goofy Grandpa. All the while, it is up to Jake to save Santa from his fretful fate in this colorful Christmas mystery. This is one hilarious high-flying adventure that will bring joy to the world and laughs for the entire family. Narrated by Elmo Shrosphire (A.K.A. "Dr. Elmo").
