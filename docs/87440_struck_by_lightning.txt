Title: Struck by Lightning (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Brian Dannelly
Main Cast: Dermot Mulroney, Sarah Hyland, Chris Colfer, Allison Janney, Christina Hendricks
Keywords: lightning, blackmail, grandmother grandson relationship, high school, narration from grave, electrocution, dead boy, death

Overview: After being struck and killed by lightning, a young man recounts the way he blackmailed his fellow classmates into contributing to his literary magazine.
