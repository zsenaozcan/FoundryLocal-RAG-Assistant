Title: Touch of Death (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Brett Halsey, Zora Kerova, Al Cliver, Sacha Darwin, Ria De Simone
Keywords: chain saw, kanibalismus, hit-and-run driving , dismemberment of human beings, dark comedy, gore, serial killer

Overview: Lester Parson is a lonely gigolo who dates and kills off middle-aged women in ultra-gory fashion just for the pleasure of watching them die, as well as to take their money and jewelery as well as cook and eat their remains for his dinner.
