Title: Cine Holliúdy (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Halder Gomes
Main Cast: Edmilson Filho, Miriam Freeland, Joel Gomes, Falcão, Roberto Bomtempo
Keywords: brazilian, comedy

Overview: During the seventies, the massive arrival of television in the Brazilian countryside puts in jeopardy the business of running small movie theaters. Francisgleydisson is the owner of Cine Holliudy, and he must be extremely creative to keep his passion for cinema alive.
