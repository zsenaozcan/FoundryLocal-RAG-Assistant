Title: The Magnificent Seven Ride! (1972)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: George McCowan
Main Cast: Lee Van Cleef, Stefanie Powers, Michael Callan, Mariette Hartley, Luke Askew
Keywords: wife husband relationship, dynamite, sequel, mexican, wagon

Overview: Marshal Chris Adams turns down a friend's request to help stop the depredations of a gang of Mexican bandits. When his wife is killed by bank robbers and his friend is killed capturing the last thief, Chris feels obligated to take up his friend's cause and recruits a writer and five prisoners to destroy the desperadoes.The last in the original series of four "Magnificent Seven" movies.
