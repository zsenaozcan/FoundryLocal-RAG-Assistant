Title: One Week and a Day (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Asaph Polonsky
Main Cast: Shai Avivi, Jenya Dodina, Uri Gavriel, Sharon Alexander, Evgenia Dodina
Keywords: cemetery, funeral, joint, cannabis, cancer, neighbor, hospital, death, face slap, mourning

Overview: When Eyal finishes the week of mourning for his late son, his wife urges him to return to their routine but instead he gets high with a young neighbor and sets out to discover that there are still things in his life worth living for.
