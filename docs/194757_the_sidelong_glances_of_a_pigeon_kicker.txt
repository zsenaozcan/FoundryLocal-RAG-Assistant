Title: The Sidelong Glances of a Pigeon Kicker (1970)
Genres: Comedy
Director: John Dexter
Main Cast: Sylvester Stallone, Jordan Christopher, Jill O'Hara, Robert Walden, Kate Reid
Keywords: from rags to riches

Overview: An aimless college graduate drives a taxi while rebelling against his parents, the Establishment and whatever else he happens to loathe on any given day (including pigeons). Then he meets a girl.
