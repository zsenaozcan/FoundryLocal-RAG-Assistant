Title: Deadtime Stories (1986)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Jeffery Delman
Main Cast: Scott Valentine, Nicole Picard, Matt Mitler, Cathryn de Prume, Melissa Leo
Keywords: witch, cemetery, monster, babysitter, fairy tale, independent film, surprise ending, impalement, werewolf, cult film

Overview: A babysitting uncle tells his charges three horror stories--about a killer witch, Little Red Riding Hood and a werewolf, and a story about "Goldi Lox" and the three bears.
