Title: Across the River (2013)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Mystery
Director: Lorenzo Bianchini
Main Cast: Renzo Gariup, Marco Marchese, Lidia Zabrieszach
Keywords: trapped, biologist

Overview: After a wildlife biologist (Marco Marchese) becomes trapped on the wrong side of the river while collecting data, he comes across an abandoned village. Taking shelter, he begins to suspect he may be sharing the grounds with more than just boar and deer.
