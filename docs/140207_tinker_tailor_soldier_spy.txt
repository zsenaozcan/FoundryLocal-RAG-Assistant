Title: Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy (1979)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Mystery
Director: John Irvin
Main Cast: Alec Guinness, Michael Jayston, Anthony Bate, Bernard Hepton, Ian Richardson
Keywords: spy, cold war, british, espionage, mole

Overview: In the bleak days of the Cold War, espionage veteran George Smiley is forced from semi-retirement to uncover a Soviet agent within MI6's echelons.
