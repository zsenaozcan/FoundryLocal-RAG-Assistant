Title: Stage Struck (1925)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Allan Dwan
Main Cast: Gloria Swanson, Lawrence Gray, Gertrude Astor, Oliver Sandys, Ford Sterling

Overview: Stage Struck (1925) is a silent comedy film starring Gloria Swanson, Lawrence Gray, Gertrude Astor, and Ford Sterling. The film was directed by Allan Dwan, and released by Paramount Pictures with sequences filmed in the early two-color Technicolor. The film, including its Technicolor sequences was restored by the George Eastman House film archive in 2004.
