Title: I Don't Kiss (1991)
Genres: Drama
Director: André Téchiné
Main Cast: Philippe Noiret, Emmanuelle Béart, Hélène Vincent, Ivan Desny, Manuel Blanc
Keywords: france, french, prostitution, loner

Overview: A good-looking but naive lad leaves his mountain village to try and make his way in Paris. He thinks maybe he will try acting but things don't go too well for him. Eventually he is forced to sell himself to men and though he meets an attractive girl she is herself a prostitute with a vicious pimp.
