Title: Berserk: The Golden Age Arc 2 - The Battle for Doldrey (2012)
Genres: Animation, Action, Fantasy
Director: Toshiyuki Kubooka
Main Cast: Hiroaki Iwanaga, Takahiro Sakurai, Toa Yukinari, Takahiro Fujiwara, Yuuki Kaji
Keywords: mercenary, sorcery, manga, sword fight, gore, blood, demon, middle ages

Overview: The Band of the Hawk participates in the Midland war campaign. On the bloody battlefield, they conquer decisive victories that lead them to Doldrey, an old fortress that will decide the outcome of the war.
