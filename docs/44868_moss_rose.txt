Title: Moss Rose (1947)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Gregory Ratoff
Main Cast: Peggy Cummins, Victor Mature, Ethel Barrymore, Vincent Price, Margo Woode
Keywords: victorian england

Overview: When a music-hall dancer is murdered, a moss rose marks the page of a Bible next to her body. Luckily, another chorus girl (Peggy Cummins) saw a gentleman (Victor Mature) leaving the lodgings. She approaches him directly, saying she'll go to the police if he doesn't meet her demands, but he brushes her off contemptuously. When he learns she's dead serious, he tries to buy her off with a thick wad of pound notes. But it's not money she's after; all she wants is two weeks at his country estate, living the life of a `lady.'
