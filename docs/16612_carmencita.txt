Title: Carmencita (1894)
Genres: Documentary
Director: William K.L. Dickson 
Main Cast: Carmencita
Keywords: dancer, silent film

Overview: The first woman to appear in front of an Edison motion picture camera and possibly the first woman to appear in a motion picture within the United States. In the film, Carmencita is recorded going through a routine she had been performing at Koster &amp; Bial's in New York since February 1890.
