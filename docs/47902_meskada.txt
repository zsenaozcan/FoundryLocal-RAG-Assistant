Title: Meskada (2010)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Nick Stahl, Rachel Nichols, Kellan Lutz, Jonathan Tucker, Laura Benanti
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Small-town detective Noah Cordin is called to solve a juvenile homicide that occurred during a home burglary in his affluent town of Hilliard. The dead boy's mother, Allison Connor, is a member of the Meskada County Board of Commissioners, and a powerful woman in Hilliard; and the entire township rallies together in solidarity - to support her and Detective Cordin's efforts to find the killers.
