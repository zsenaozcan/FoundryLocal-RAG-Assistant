Title: Kryptonita (2015)
Genres: Fantasy, Science Fiction, Comedy
Director: Nicanor Loreti
Main Cast: Pablo Rago, Diego Capusotto, Nicolás Vázquez, Diego Velázquez, Juan Palomino
Keywords: bizarre comedy, sci-fi comedy

Overview: Movie adaptation of Leonardo Oyola's novel, which tells the story of the legendary DC comics superhero, Superman, if he, instead of falling in Smallville from Krypton, would have landed in the heart of Isidro Casanova, in La Matanza, deep in the west side of Buenos Aires.
