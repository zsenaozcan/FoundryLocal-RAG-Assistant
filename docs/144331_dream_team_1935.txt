Title: Dream Team 1935 (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, History
Director: Aigars Grauba
Main Cast: Jānis Amanis, Inga Alsiņa, Vilis Daudziņš, Mārcis Maņjakovs, Jānis Vimba

Overview: A story about the Latvian basketball team, which won the first-ever European Championships in 1935 and its enthusiastic head coach Valdemars Baumanis.
