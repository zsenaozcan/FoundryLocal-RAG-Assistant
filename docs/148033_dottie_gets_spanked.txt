Title: Dottie Gets Spanked (1993)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Todd Haynes
Main Cast: J. Evan Bonifant, Barbara Garrick, Julie Halston, Robert Pall, Harriet Sansom Harris
Keywords: gay, obsession, television, queer cinema

Overview: Paying homage to his childhood fascination with "I Love Lucy", Haynes' film tells the story of a highly feminine six-year old boy in pre-hippie 1960s America who endures ridicule from his schoolmates and worry from his parent over his fixation with a TV star named Dottie.
