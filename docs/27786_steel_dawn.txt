Title: Steel Dawn (1987)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Science Fiction
Director: Lance Hool
Main Cast: Patrick Swayze, Lisa Niemi, Anthony Zerbe, Christopher Neame, Brion James
Keywords: post-apocalyptic, independent film, water shortage, desert

Overview: In a post-apocalyptic world, a warrior wandering through the desert comes upon a group of settlers who are being menaced by a murderous gang that is after the water they control.
