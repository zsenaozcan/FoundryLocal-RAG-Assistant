Title: The Tommyknockers (1993)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: John Power
Main Cast: Jimmy Smits, Marg Helgenberger, John Ashton, Allyce Beasley, Robert Carradine
Keywords: ufo, alien

Overview: The small town of Haven becomes a hot-bed of inventions all run by a strange green power device. The whole town is digging something up in the woods, and only an alcoholic poet can discover the secret of the Tommyknocker
