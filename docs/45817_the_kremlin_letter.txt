Title: The Kremlin Letter (1970)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller
Director: John Huston
Main Cast: Richard Boone, Nigel Green, Dean Jagger, Lila Kedrova, Патрик О’Нил

Overview: A network of older spies from the West recruits a young intelligence officer with a photographic memory to accompany them on a mission inside Russia. They must recover a letter written by the CIA that promises American assistance to Russia if China gets the atomic bomb.
