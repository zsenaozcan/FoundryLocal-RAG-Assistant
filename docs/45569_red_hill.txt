Title: Red Hill (2010)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Western
Director: Patrick Hughes
Main Cast: Ryan Kwanten, Steve Bisley, Tom E. Lewis, Claire van der Boom, Christopher Davis
Keywords: suicide, rape, fire, small town, horse, jukebox, australia, panther, revenge, murder

Overview: Young police officer Shane Cooper's first day on duty, after relocating to the small town of Red Hill, rapidly turns into a nightmare. News of a prison break, involving convicted murderer Jimmy Conway, sends the local law enforcement officers - led by the town's ruling presence, Old Bill - into a panic and leads to a terrifying and bloody confrontation.
