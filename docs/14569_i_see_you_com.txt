Title: I-See-You.Com (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Eric Steven Stahl
Main Cast: Beau Bridges, Rosanna Arquette, Mathew Botuchis, Héctor Elizondo, Doris Roberts
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A 17-year-old boy buys mini-cameras and displays the footage online at I-see-you.com. The cash rolls in as the site becomes a major hit. Everyone seems to have fun until it all comes crashing down....
