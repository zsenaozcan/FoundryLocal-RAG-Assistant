Title: The Innocent (1976)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Luchino Visconti
Main Cast: Giancarlo Giannini, Laura Antonelli, Jennifer O'Neill, Marc Porel, Rina Morelli
Keywords: suicide, eroticism, fascism, aristocrat, illegitimate son, turn of the century

Overview: Tullio Hermil is a chauvinist aristocrat who flaunts his mistress to his wife, but when he believes she has been unfaithful he becomes enamored of her again.
