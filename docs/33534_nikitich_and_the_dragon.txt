Title: Nikitich and The Dragon (2006)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Comedy, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Ilya Maksimov
Main Cast: Yekaterina Gorokhovskaya, Yuri Tarasov, Valeri Solovyev
Keywords: new love, adventure, fairy-tale figure, dragon

Overview: Dobrinya Nikitich goes on a quest to save the royal niece and finds out whether his old friend Zmey Gorinich is loyal to him. During the adventures, he is accompanied by the royal messenger who's in love with the royal niece.
