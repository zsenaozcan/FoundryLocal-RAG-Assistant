Title: Shark Swarm (2008)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller, Action
Director: James A. Contner
Main Cast: Daryl Hannah, John Schneider, Armand Assante, Solly Assa, F. Murray Abraham

Overview: A fisherman and his family fight to take down a greedy real estate developer who has released toxins into the ocean, turning the area's sharks into bloodthirsty hunters.
