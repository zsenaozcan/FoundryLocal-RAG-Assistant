Title: A Dark Truth (2012)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Damian Lee
Main Cast: Andy García, Kim Coates, Deborah Kara Unger, Eva Longoria, Forest Whitaker
Keywords: corruption, water purification, epiphany, ecuador, natural resources, field agent, water conflict, eco thriller

Overview: In the jungles of Ecuador, blood taints the waters. A multinational conglomerate's unholy alliance with a bloodthirsty military regime has resulted in a massacre. Only the rebel Francisco Franco and his determined wife Mia can prove the truth. To settle a personal debt, former CIA agent Jack Begosian takes on the freelance assignment to rescue Francisco and risks everything in a brutal battle to expose the cover-up.
