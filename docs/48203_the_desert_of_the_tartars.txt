Title: The Desert of the Tartars (1976)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Valerio Zurlini
Main Cast: Vittorio Gassman, Giuliano Gemma, Philippe Noiret, Francisco Rabal, Fernando Rey
Keywords: based on novel, fortress

Overview: Lieutenant Giovanni Drogo is assigned to the old Bastiani border fortress where he expects an imminent attack by nomadic fearsome Tartars.
