Title: Witching & Bitching (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Álex de la Iglesia
Main Cast: Hugo Silva, Mario Casas, Carmen Maura, Javier Botet, Carolina Bang

Overview: In this heist film turned horror fest, director Álex de la Iglesia's love of mayhem is on full display as a gang of gold thieves lands in a coven of witches who are preparing for an ancient ritual -- and in need of a sacrifice.
