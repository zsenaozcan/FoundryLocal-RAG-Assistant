Title: The Disorderly Orderly (1964)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Frank Tashlin
Main Cast: Jerry Lewis, Karen Sharpe, Glenda Farrell, Everett Sloane, Kathleen Freeman
Keywords: hospital

Overview: The Disorderly Orderly is a 1964 American comedy film released by Paramount Pictures, and starring Jerry Lewis. The film was produced by Paul Jones with a screenplay by director Frank Tashlin, based on a story by Norm Liebermann and Ed Haas.
