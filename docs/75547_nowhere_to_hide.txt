Title: Nowhere to Hide (2009)
Genres: Thriller
Director: John Murlowski
Main Cast: Meredith Monroe, Lochlyn Munro, Brian Krause, Brian Dietzen

Overview: After the murder of a police inspector, his widow discovers with horror that, in addition, her only daughter has been kidnapped. While driving in her search, the voice of the technical roadside assistance that comes through the intercom installed in her vehicle, reports that he is who has kidnapped the girl.
