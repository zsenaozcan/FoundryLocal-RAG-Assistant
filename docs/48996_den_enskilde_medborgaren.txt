Title: Den enskilde medborgaren (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Claes Eriksson
Main Cast: Örjan Ramberg, Anders Eriksson, Claes Eriksson, Kerstin Granlund, Björn Granath
Keywords: scandal, greed, satire, evil corporation

Overview: Insurance Director Stig Roxby is worth a hundred times more than an ordinary high school teacher. But he doesn't think that's enough, but sees himself forced to work with four other director colleagues to lift the bonus cap to be even more valuable. After some complications, Stig Roxby realize the brutal truth: he has everything but the essentials...
