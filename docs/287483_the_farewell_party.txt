Title: The Farewell Party (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Tal Granit
Main Cast: Ze'ev Revach, Levana Finkelstein, Alisa Rozen, Ilan Dar, Rafael Tabor
Keywords: dark comedy, woman director

Overview: A group of friends at a Jerusalem retirement home build a machine for self-euthanasia in order to help their terminally ill friend. When rumors of the machine begin to spread, more and more people ask for their help, and the friends are faced with an emotional dilemma
