Title: Bedeviled (2017)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Abel Vang
Main Cast: Saxon Sharbino, Bonnie Morgan, Brandon Soo Hoo, Robyn Cohen, Victory Van Tuyl
Keywords: supernatural, cell phone, entity, evil, app

Overview: A group of friends download a Siri-like App which, at first, seems like a harmless way to get directions or a restaurant recommendation. But the sinister nature of the App soon reveals itself. The App not only knows each person’s deepest, darkest fears, but is able to manifest these fears into the real world to literally scare the kids to death.
