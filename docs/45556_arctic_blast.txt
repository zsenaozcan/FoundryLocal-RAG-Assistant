Title: Arctic Blast (2010)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Brian Trenchard-Smith
Main Cast: Michael Shanks, Alexandra Davies, Saskia Hampele, Bruce Davison, Indiana Evans
Keywords: solar eclipse, divorce, freezing, worldwide disaster, cry wolf

Overview: When a solar eclipse sends a colossal blast of super chilled air towards the earth, it then sets off a catastrophic chain of events that threatens to engulf the world in ice and begin a new Ice Age.
