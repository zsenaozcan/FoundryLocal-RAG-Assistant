Title: The Secret in Their Eyes (2009)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Romance
Director: Juan José Campanella
Main Cast: Ricardo Darín, Soledad Villamil, Pablo Rago, Javier Godino, Guillermo Francella
Keywords: rape, secret, writing, homicide, kidnapping, passion, suspect, investigation, police, partner

Overview: A retired legal counselor writes a novel hoping to find closure for one of his past unresolved homicide cases and for his unreciprocated love with his superior - both of which still haunt him decades later.
