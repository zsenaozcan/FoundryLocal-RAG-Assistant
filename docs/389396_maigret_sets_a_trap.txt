Title: Maigret Sets a Trap (2016)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Mystery
Director: Ashley Pearce
Main Cast: Rowan Atkinson, Shaun Dingwall, Lucy Cohu, David Dawson, Gillian Bevan

Overview: A serial killer strikes in post-war France and it's up to Jules Maigret, a dedicated police commissioner, to hunt the murderer down.
