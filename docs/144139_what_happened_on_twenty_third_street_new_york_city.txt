Title: What Happened on Twenty-Third Street, New York City (1901)
Genres: Documentary, Comedy
Director: Edwin S. Porter
Main Cast: A.C. Abadie, Florence Georgie
Keywords: silent film, candid camera

Overview: A street level view from the sidewalk, looking along the length of 23rd Street. Following actuality footage of pedestrians and street traffic, the actors, a man in summer attire and a woman in an ankle-length dress, walk toward the camera.
