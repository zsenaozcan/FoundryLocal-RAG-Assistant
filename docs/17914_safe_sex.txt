Title: Safe Sex (1999)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Thanasis Papathanasiou
Main Cast: Mina Adamaki, Alexandros Antonopoulos, Vladimiros Kiriakidis, Ieroklis Michaelidis, Renia Louizidou

Overview: A satire of life in modern Greece, presented through a series of different stories about sex. We see several couples and their relation with sex in parallel stories that come together in a hilarious way as the film progresses.
