Title: The Dark Valley (2014)
Genres: Western, Drama
Director: Andreas Prochaska
Main Cast: Sam Riley, Paula Beer, Tobias Moretti, Clemens Schick, Hans-Michael Rehberg
Keywords: revenge

Overview: Through a hidden path a lone rider reaches a little town high up in the Alpes. Nobody knows where the stranger comes from, nor what he wants there. But everyone knows that they don't want him to stay.
