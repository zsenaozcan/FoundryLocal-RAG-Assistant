Title: Where the Lilies Bloom (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: William A. Graham
Main Cast: Julie Gholson, Jan Smithers, Matthew Burrill, Harry Dean Stanton, Rance Howard
Keywords: north carolina, backwoods

Overview: A family of children decide not to tell anyone their father has died, and to live on their own in the backwoods of rural North Carolina. If the state finds out they are on their own, they will be split up and sent to live in foster homes.
