Title: The King of Havana (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Agustí Villaronga
Main Cast: Maykol David, Yordanka Ariosa, Héctor Medina, Jazz Vilá, Chanel Terrero
Keywords: adolescence

Overview: Recently escaped from reformatory, Reinaldo struggles to get by in the streets of Havana in the late 90s, one of the worst decades for Cuban society. Hopes, disillusionment, rum, good humor and above all hunger, accompany him in his wanderings, until he meets Magda and Yunisleidy, survivors like himself. In one or the other's arms, he will try to escape the material and moral misery surrounding him, living love, passion, tenderness and uninhibited sex to the limit.
