Title: From A to B (2015)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Drama
Director: Ali F. Mostafa
Main Cast: Fahad Albutairi, Shadi Alfons, Fadi Rifaai, Yosra El Lozy, Maha Abou Ouf

Overview: Three estranged childhood friends (Omar, Ramy, Jay), travel on a road trip from Abu Dhabi to Beirut in memory of their lost friend. If what happens en route doesn't make them crazy, it might just bring them closer.
