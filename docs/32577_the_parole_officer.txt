Title: The Parole Officer (2001)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: John Duigan
Main Cast: Steve Coogan, Lena Headey, Ben Miller, Om Puri, Steven Waddington
Keywords: bank, murder, framed, cctv

Overview: A hapless parole officer is framed for murder by a crooked police chief. To prove his innocence he must entice his former clients away from the law abiding lives they are now living to recover the evidence that will save him.
