Title: Darkest Day (2015)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Horror
Director: Dan Rickard
Main Cast: Dan Rickard, Chris Wandell, Samantha Bolter, Richard Wilkinson, Christianne van Wijk
Keywords: beach, british, b movie, memory loss, zombie, virus

Overview: Dan wakes up on a beach with no memory. He discovers an empty city ravaged by a deadly virus. After befriending a small group of survivors it soon becomes clear the army are hunting him down, and the group are forced on a dangerous journey to escape.
