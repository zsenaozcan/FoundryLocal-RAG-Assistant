Title: Say Uncle (2005)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Peter Paige
Main Cast: Peter Paige, Kathy Najimy, Anthony Clark, Melanie Lynskey, Gabrielle Union

Overview: A young artist (Peter Paige), desperate to replace the relationship he had with his recently relocated godson, is targeted by a neighborhood mom (Kathy Najimy) as a potential threat to the community.
