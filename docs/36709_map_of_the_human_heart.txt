Title: Map of the Human Heart (1993)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Romance, War
Director: Vincent Ward
Main Cast: Jason Scott Lee, Robert Joamie, Anne Parillaud, Annie Galipeau, Patrick Bergin
Keywords: world war ii, inuit, interracial romance

Overview: Fantastic improbabilities, happenstance and the undying bridge of love are part of this romantic fantasy about an Inuit who crosses years, oceans and the ravages of WWII to find his childhood love, a Metis girl, but finds that their cultures are the most difficult spaces to gap.
