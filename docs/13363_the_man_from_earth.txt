Title: The Man from Earth (2007)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Richard Schenkman
Main Cast: David Lee Smith, Tony Todd, John Billingsley, Ellen Crawford, Annika Peterson
Keywords: philosophy, secret, birthday, professor, psychology, bible, time, legend, immortality, history

Overview: An impromptu goodbye party for Professor John Oldman becomes a mysterious interrogation after the retiring scholar reveals to his colleagues he never ages and has walked the earth for 14,000 years.
