Title: Yes or No (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Saratswadee Wongsomphet
Main Cast: Arisara Thongborisut, Supanart Jittaleela, Sushar Manaying, Soranut Yupanun, Inthira Yeunyong
Keywords: love, lesbian, female homosexuality

Overview: Pie is a sweet girl who moves into a new college dorm room where she finds out that her new roommate Kim, is a tomboy who looks and dress like a boy. As their friendship develops, Pie and Kim begin to wonder if the feeling they feel for one another is just an ordinary friendship or true love.
