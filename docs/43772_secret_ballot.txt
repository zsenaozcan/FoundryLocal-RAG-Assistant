Title: Secret Ballot (2001)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Babak Payami
Main Cast: Nassim Abdi, Cyrus Abidi, Youssef Habashi, Farrokh Shoja, Gholbahar Janghali
Keywords: iran, socio-political drama, minimalism

Overview: A female election agent and a gun-toting soldier try to collect votes among the local islanders with mixed success.
