Title: Rain or Shine (1930)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Comedy, Music
Director: Frank Capra
Main Cast: Joe Cook, Louise Fazenda, Joan Peers, Dave Chasen, Tom Howard
Keywords: circus, strike, song, performer

Overview: Young Mary Rainey takes the reins of her deceased father's failing circus. With the help of the Inimitable Smiley Johnson, she hopes to bring fortune back to her ragtag band of ragged shoeleather performers.
