Title: Svengali (1931)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Archie Mayo
Main Cast: John Barrymore, Marian Marsh, Donald Crisp, Bramwell Fletcher, Carmel Myers
Keywords: artist, model, hypnotism, svengali

Overview: Through hypnotism and telepathic mind control, a sinister music maestro controls the singing voice, but not the heart, of the woman he loves.
