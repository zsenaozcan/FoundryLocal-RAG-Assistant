Title: Live at the Foxes Den (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Kristoff
Main Cast: Jackson Rathbone, Jocelin Donahue, Jack Holmes, Caity Lotz, Xander Berkeley

Overview: A corporate attorney quits his job at a high profile law firm to become the singer at a an old cocktail lounge called the Foxes Den, becoming deeply involved in the lives of its staff and regulars.
