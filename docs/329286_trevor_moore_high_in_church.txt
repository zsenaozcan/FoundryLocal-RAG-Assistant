Title: Trevor Moore: High In Church (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ryan Polito
Main Cast: Trevor Moore, Owen Benjamin
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Trevor Moore recorded his first solo one-hour special, High In Church, at The Gramercy Theatre in New York. Accompanied by a live band, dancing girls and music videos, Trevor performs an hour of brand-new sketches and songs spanning all musical genres.
