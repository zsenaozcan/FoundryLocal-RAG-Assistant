Title: Dead of the Nite (2013)
Genres: Horror
Director: S.J. Evans
Main Cast: Tony Todd, Cicely Tennant, Joseph Millson

Overview: When a group of ghost hunters investigate the infamous Jericho Manor, they soon realise it's not just ghosts that go bump in the night! As people get murdered, the survivors need to discover who or what's killing them before it's too late.
