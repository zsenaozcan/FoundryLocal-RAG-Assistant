Title: Take Away (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Marc Gracie
Main Cast: Vince Colosimo, Stephen Curry, Rose Byrne, Nathan Phillips, Matthew Dyktynski
Keywords: rivalry, fish and chip shop, takeaway, melbourne

Overview: Tony Stilano and Trev Spackneys both own, live over and work in adjoining take-away fish shops in Melbourne. Although they have fallen into a habitual rivalry based on a cause long forgotten, the pair unite when the multinational fast-food outlet "Burgies" unveils a new store directly opposite the twin fish &amp; chips shops.
