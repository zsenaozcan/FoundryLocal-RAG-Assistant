Title: Giants and Toys (1958)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Yasuzô Masumura
Main Cast: Hiroshi Kawaguchi, Hitomi Nozoe, Yûnosuke Itô, Michiko Ono, Kyû Sazanka
Keywords: japanese new wave
