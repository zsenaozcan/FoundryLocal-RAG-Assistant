Title: Zero Tolerance (1999)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Anders Nilsson
Main Cast: Jakob Eklund, Peter Andersson, Marie Richardson, Jacqueline Ramel, Hanna Alsterlund
Keywords: robbery, robber, witness, chase, police, revenge, murder, shootout, gangster, violence

Overview: Johan is a two-fisted Gothenburg cop who finds himself in a shoot-out with jewel robbers. After the smoke has cleared, one robber, shot by his accomplice, and an innocent bystander, are dead. Three witnesses, including Helen, identify thug extraordinaire Leo Gaut as being the dead crook's trigger-happy colleague. Gaut soon threatens the three witnesses, and only Johan, the badge-wearing hero, can save them.
