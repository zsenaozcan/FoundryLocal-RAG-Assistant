Title: Six Degrees of Separation (1993)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Comedy
Director: Fred Schepisi
Main Cast: Will Smith, Donald Sutherland, Stockard Channing, Ian McKellen, Mary Beth Hurt
Keywords: con man, new york city, art, art dealer

Overview: The story of a young, gay black con artist who, posing as the son of Sidney Poitier, cunningly maneuvers his way into the lives of a white, upper-class New York family.
