Title: The Bonesetter (2004)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Brett Kelly
Main Cast: Anne-Marie Frigon, Mark Courneyea, Sherry Thurig, Tara Rheaume, Josh Grace
Keywords: low-budget, urban legend, farmhouse, missing child

Overview: One hundred years ago, an evil bonesetter was killed for his crimes against children. Now in the present a single mother and a shy librarian must discover who is behind a rash of child abductions, is the Bonesetter back to finish his depraved work?
