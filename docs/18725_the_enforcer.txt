Title: The Enforcer (1995)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: Corey Yuen Kwai
Main Cast: Jet Li, Anita Mui, Tse Miu, Blacky Ko Sau-Leung, Yu Rong-Guang

Overview: An undercover cop struggling to provide for his son and ailing wife, must infiltrate a ruthless gang. But things turn sour when another cop blows his cover and he quickly finds himself battling for his life and the lives of his family.
