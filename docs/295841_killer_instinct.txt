Title: Killer Instinct (1988)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Cirio H. Santiago
Main Cast: Robert Patrick, Robert Dryer, William Steis, Morgan Douglas, Lydie Denier

Overview: For America, the Vietnam War is over. Johnny doesn't see it that way. This Special Forces soldier battles vicious Vietcong, a sadistic Russian advisor and his own peacenik girlfriend as he embarks on a deadly mission behind enemy lines.
