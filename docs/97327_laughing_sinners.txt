Title: Laughing Sinners (1931)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Harry Beaumont
Main Cast: Joan Crawford, Neil Hamilton, Clark Gable, Marjorie Rambeau, Guy Kibbee
Keywords: salvation army

Overview: After being dumped by her traveling salesman boyfriend, nightclub dancer Joan Crawford falls for a Salvation Army worker. This 1931 drama, directed by Harry Beaumont, also stars Clark Gable, Neil Hamilton, Marjorie Rambeau and Guy Kibbee.
