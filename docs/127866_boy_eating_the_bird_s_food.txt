Title: Boy Eating the Bird's Food (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ektoras Lygizos
Main Cast: Yiannis Papadopoulos

Overview: The astonishing debut feature from Greek filmmaker Ektoras Lygizos updates Knut Hamsun's classic 1890 novel Hunger to the modern day, as it follows an alienated young man desperately trying to survive on the streets of Athens.
