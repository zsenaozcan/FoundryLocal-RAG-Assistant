Title: The Thief of Bagdad (1940)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Family, Fantasy
Director: Alexander Korda
Main Cast: Conrad Veidt, Sabu, June Duprez, Rex Ingram, John Justin
Keywords: toy, wretch, sultan, flying carpet, bagdad, orient

Overview: When Prince Ahmad (John Justin) is blinded and cast out of Bagdad by the nefarious Jaffar (Conrad Veidt), he joins forces with the scrappy thief Abu (the incomparable Sabu, in his definitive role) to win back his royal place, as well as the heart of a beautiful princess (June Duprez).
