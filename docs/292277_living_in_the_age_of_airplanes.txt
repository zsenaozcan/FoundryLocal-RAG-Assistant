Title: Living in the Age of Airplanes (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Brian J. Terwilliger
Main Cast: Harrison Ford
Keywords: airplane, earth, travel, documentary, aviation, world

Overview: LIVING IN THE AGE OF AIRPLANES offers a fresh perspective on a modern-day miracle that many of us take for granted: flying. Narrated by Harrison Ford and featuring an original score from Academy Award® winning composer James Horner, the film takes viewers to 18 countries across all seven continents to illuminate how airplanes have empowered a century of global connectedness our ancestors could never have imagined.
