Title: The Kingdom of Fairies (1903)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Georges Méliès
Main Cast: Bleuette Bernon, Georges Méliès
Keywords: fairy, fairy tale, silent film, short

Overview: At the royal court, a prince is presenting the princess whom he is pledged to marry, when a witch suddenly appears. Though driven off, the witch soon returns, summons some of her servants, and carries off the princess. A rescue party is quickly organized, but the unfortunate captive has been taken to a strange, forbidding realm, from where it will be impossible to rescue her without some special help. (IMDb)
