Title: My Daughter, the Socialist (1966)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Alekos Sakellarios
Main Cast: Aliki Vougiouklaki, Dimitris Papamichael, Stavros Xenidis, Labros Konstadaras, Nikitas Platis

Overview: Liza falls in love with one of her father's employees. Her socialistic ideas about employment drive her to collide with her father and the sociopolitical status of the time.
