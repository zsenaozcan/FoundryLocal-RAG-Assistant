Title: The Hanging Tree (1959)
Genres: Western
Director: Delmer Daves
Main Cast: Gary Cooper, Maria Schell, Karl Malden, George C. Scott, Karl Swenson
Keywords: doctor, criminal, hanging

Overview: Character study of a Doctor who saves a local criminal from a mob who are trying to hang him, but then tries to control the life of the young man, realising that he can exploit his secret.
