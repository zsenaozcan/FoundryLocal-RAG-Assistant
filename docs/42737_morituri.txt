Title: Morituri (1965)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller, War
Director: Bernhard Wicki
Main Cast: Marlon Brando, Yul Brynner, Janet Margolin, Trevor Howard, Martin Benrath
Keywords: submarine

Overview: A German living in India during World War II is blackmailed by the English to impersonate an SS officer on board a cargo ship leaving Japan for Germany carrying a large supply of rubber for tyres. His mission is to disable the scuttling charges so the captain cannot sink the ship if they are stopped by English warships.
