Title: Prozac Nation (2001)
Genres: Drama
Director: Erik Skjoldbjærg
Main Cast: Christina Ricci, Jason Biggs, Anne Heche, Michelle Williams, Jonathan Rhys Meyers
Keywords: depression, harvard university, rolling stone, writer, therapy, mental illness, young adult, degradation of lifestyle, lou reed

Overview: Elizabeth is on the verge of losing her grip on life after she leaves her emotionally fraught home to start college. Quickly, her life takes a turn for the worse: She clashes with her roommate and decides her boyfriend, Rafe, is her sole salvation. Her psychiatrist prescribes Prozac … but is that her only choice?
