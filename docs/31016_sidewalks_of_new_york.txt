Title: Sidewalks of New York (2001)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Edward Burns
Main Cast: Edward Burns, Heather Graham, Rosario Dawson, Dennis Farina, Brittany Murphy
Keywords: independent film

Overview: The film follows the marital and dating lives of three men and three women who unknowingly form a tangled web of relationships. Interspersing "man on the street" interviews with scenes from the six characters' lives, the film weaves a humorous and biting commentary on the game of love -- easy to start, hard to finish.
