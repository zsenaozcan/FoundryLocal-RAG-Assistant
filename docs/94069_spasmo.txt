Title: Spasmo (1974)
Genres: Thriller, Mystery
Director: Umberto Lenzi
Main Cast: Robert Hoffmann, Suzy Kendall, Ivan Rassimov, Adolfo Lastretti, Franco Silva
Keywords: brother brother relationship, hereditary disease, hitman, murder, mysterious woman

Overview: While walking along the beach, Christian and his girlfriend discover a mysterious woman washed ashore. The following day, Christian meets the woman again at a yacht party and soon finds himself entangled in a web of lust, intrigue and murder.
