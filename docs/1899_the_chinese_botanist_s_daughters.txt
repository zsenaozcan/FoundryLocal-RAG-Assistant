Title: The Chinese Botanist's Daughters (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sijie Dai
Main Cast: Mylène Jampanoï, Li Xiaoran, Dong Fu Lin, Wei-chang Wang, Như Quỳnh Nguyễn
