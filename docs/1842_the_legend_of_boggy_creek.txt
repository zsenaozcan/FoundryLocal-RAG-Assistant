Title: The Legend of Boggy Creek (1972)
Genres: Mystery, Horror, Drama
Director: Charles B. Pierce
Main Cast: Vern Stierman, Chuck Pierce Jr., William Stumpp, Willie E. Smith, Lloyd Bowen
Keywords: bigfoot, arkansas, based on true story, monsters of the wild, cryptozoology, docudrama, southern sasquatch, fouke monster

Overview: A documentary-style drama based on true accounts of the Fouke Monster in Arkansas. It focuses on the lives of back country people and their culture while detailing certain sightings of the monster. This title was a drive-in B movie favorite for it's time.
