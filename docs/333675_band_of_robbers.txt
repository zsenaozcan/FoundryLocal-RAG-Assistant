Title: Band of Robbers (2016)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Crime, Thriller
Director: Adam Nee
Main Cast: Kyle Gallner, Adam Nee, Matthew Gray Gubler, Hannibal Buress, Melissa Benoist
Keywords: robbery, treasure, coming of age, arrested development, mexican laborer

Overview: A comedic thriller that re-imagines Mark Twain's iconic literary characters of "Huckleberry Finn" and "Tom Sawyer" as grown men in current day.
