Title: Teen Spirit (2011)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Comedy
Director: Gil Junger
Main Cast: Cassandra Scerbo, Lindsay Shaw, Chris Zylka, Elena Varela, Rhoda Griffis
Keywords: high school, prom queen

Overview: The story is about Amber, a mean popular girl who gets electrocuted and dies and is not allowed to enter into heaven unless she helps the least popular girl in school become Prom Queen within a week, but things do not go as planned.
