Title: My Name Is Emily (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Simon Fitzmaurice
Main Cast: Evanna Lynch, Michael Smiley, George Webster, Deirdre Mullins, Cathy Belton

Overview: Packed off to a foster home after her father is insti­tu­tion­al­ized, a rebellious young Irish girl resolves to bust her dad out of the hospital where he's been confined.
