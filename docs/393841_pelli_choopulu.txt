Title: Pelli Choopulu (2016)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Tharun Bhascker Dhaassyam
Main Cast: Vijay Deverakonda, Ritu Varma, Nandoo, Priyadarshi Pullikonda, Abhay Bethiganti
Keywords: arranged marriage, marriage, love, first date

Overview: Raj Kandukuri and BiG Ben Films in association with Vinoothna Geetha presents #Pellichoopulu, a Telugu romantic comedy about two individuals Prashanth and Chitra who meet at a 'Pellichoopulu'.  The story is about a modern day couple anchored down by their traditional roots. When fate plays havoc in their lives, they set off on a journey to find their dreams, face their fears and ultimately find true love.  Directed by Tharun Bhascker of Anukokunda and Sainma fame, the film stars Vijay Deverakonda and Ritu Varma.
