Title: The Return of the Vampire (1943)
Genres: Horror
Director: Lew Landers
Main Cast: Bela Lugosi, Frieda Inescort, Nina Foch, Miles Mander, Roland Varno
Keywords: coffin, vampire, grave digger, london blitz

Overview: In 1918, an English family are terrorized by a vampire, until they learn how to deal with it. They think their troubles are over, but German bombs in WWII free the monster. He reclaims the soul of his wolfman ex-servant, and assuming the identity of a scientist who has just escaped from a concentration camp, he starts out on a plan to get revenge upon the family.
