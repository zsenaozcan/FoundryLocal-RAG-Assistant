Title: Fellini's Casanova (1976)
Genres: Drama, History, Romance
Director: Federico Fellini
Main Cast: Donald Sutherland, Tina Aumont, Cicely Browne, Carmen Scarpitta, Clara Algranti
Keywords: biography, casanova, libertine, memoir

Overview: Casanova is a libertine, collecting seductions and sexual feats. But he is really interested in someone, and is he really an interesting person? Is he really alive?
