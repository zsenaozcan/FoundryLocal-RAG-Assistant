Title: Variety (1925)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: E.A. Dupont
Main Cast: Emil Jannings, Maly Delschaft, Lya De Putti, Warwick Ward, Alice Hechy
Keywords: prison, son, silent film, trapeze artist

Overview: The murderer “Boss” Huller – after having spent ten years in prison – breaks his silence to tell the warden his story.
