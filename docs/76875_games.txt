Title: Games (1967)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Curtis Harrington
Main Cast: Simone Signoret, James Caan, Katharine Ross, Don Stroud, Kent Smith
Keywords: games, mind game

Overview: A mysterious woman (Simone Signoret) in black moves in with married Manhattan thrill-seekers (James Caan, Katharine Ross) and helps one trick the other.
