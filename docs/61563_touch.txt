Title: Touch (1997)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Foreign, Romance
Director: Paul Schrader
Main Cast: LL Cool J, Gina Gershon, Conchata Ferrell, John Doe, Christopher Walken

Overview: When Juvenal, a presumed miracle worker, appears on the scene Bill Hill attempts to exploit him but his plans go astray with the untimely intervention of August Murray and the developing relationship between Juvenal and Lynn Faulkner.
