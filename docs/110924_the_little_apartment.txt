Title: The Little Apartment (1959)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Marco Ferreri
Main Cast: José Luis López Vázquez, Mary Carrillo, Concha López Silva, Ángel Álvarez, María Luisa Ponte
Keywords: apartment

Overview: Rodolfo and Petrita each live in separate quarters in dilapidated Madrid, while looking to have a little apartment (or "pisito", in Spanish dialect). Unfortunately their low salaries prevent them from acquiring one. Soon, Rodolfo's co-workers urge him to marry the old and frail Doña Martina, who is the main tenant in the apartment he boards in. According to Spanish rent-control law, he could inherit the lease from his spouse. Thus begin his misgivings and Petrita's. Written by Emilio
