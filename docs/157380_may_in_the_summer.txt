Title: May in the Summer (2013)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Cherien Dabis
Main Cast: Cherien Dabis, Hiam Abbass, Bill Pullman, Alia Shawkat, Nadine Malouf
Keywords: middle east, marriage, independent film, divorce, woman director, new yorker, bride-to-be
