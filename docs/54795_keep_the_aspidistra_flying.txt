Title: Keep the Aspidistra Flying (1997)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Robert Bierman
Main Cast: Helena Bonham Carter, Liz Smith, Jim Carter, Richard E. Grant
Keywords: england, poet, poverty, writer

Overview: Gordon Comstock is a copywriter at an ad agency, and his girlfriend Rosemary is a designer. Gordon believes he is a genius, a marvelous poet and quits the ad agency, trying to live on his poems, but poverty soon comes to him.
