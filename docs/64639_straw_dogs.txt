Title: Straw Dogs (2011)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Rod Lurie
Main Cast: James Marsden, Kate Bosworth, Alexander Skarsgård, Dominic Purcell, Laz Alonso
Keywords: southern usa, rape, bar, wife husband relationship, barn, machismo, rifle, home invasion

Overview: L.A. screenwriter David Sumner relocates with his wife, Amy, to her hometown in the deep South. There, while tensions build between them, a brewing conflict with locals becomes a threat to them both.
