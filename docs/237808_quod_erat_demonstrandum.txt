Title: Quod erat demonstrandum (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Andrei Gruzsniczki
Main Cast: Ofelia Popii, Florin Piersic Jr., Sorin Leoveanu, Medeea Marinescu, Tora Vasilescu

Overview: A scholar of mathematics would like to publish his research outside Ceaucescu's Romania, but getting it through the border and the State censorship is not easy.
