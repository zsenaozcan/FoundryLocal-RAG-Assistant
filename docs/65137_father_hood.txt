Title: Father Hood (1993)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Family
Director: Darrell James Roodt
Main Cast: Patrick Swayze, Halle Berry, Sabrina Lloyd, Brian Bonsall, Michael Ironside
Keywords: kidnapping, highway, police, road trip, based on true story, reporter, criminal, convict

Overview: Deadbeat dad kidnaps his kids from the bus to an abusive foster home and they go on a run. The cops are after them, they bicker constantly and his idea of a way out is grim, yet this dangerous ride might just turn them into a family.
