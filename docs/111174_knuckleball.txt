Title: Knuckleball! (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ricki Stern
Main Cast: Tim Wakefield, R.A. Dickey, Charles Hough, Phil Niekro
Keywords: baseball, woman director

Overview: Follows the Boston Red Sox' Tim Wakefield and the New York Mets' R.A. Dickey - the only two major league pitchers who use the unpredictable knuckleball - during the 2011 season.
