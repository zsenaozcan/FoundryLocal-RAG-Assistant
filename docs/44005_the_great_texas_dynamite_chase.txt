Title: The Great Texas Dynamite Chase (1976)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Michael Pressman
Main Cast: Claudia Jennings, Jocelyn Jones, Tara Strohmeier, Johnny Crawford, Miles Watkins
Keywords: texas, white trash, independent film, trailer trash, pursuit, car chase

Overview: Candy and Ellie Jo are a pair of sexy bank robbers who blast their way into small-town banks with a carload of dynamite! When they take Slim hostage, it begins a thrill-packed crime spree across the state of Texas.
