Title: La Notte (1961)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michelangelo Antonioni
Main Cast: Marcello Mastroianni, Jeanne Moreau, Monica Vitti, Bernhard Wicki, Rosy Mazzacurati
Keywords: party, hospital, writer, socialite party
