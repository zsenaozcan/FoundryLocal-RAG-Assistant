Title: Genghis Blues (1999)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Roko Belic
Main Cast: Paul Pena, Kongar-ol Ondar
Keywords: blues, music, throatsinging, tuva

Overview: Blind blues musician Paul Pena is perhaps best known for his song "Jet Airliner". In 1993, Pena heard Tuvan throat singing over his shortwave radio and subsequently taught himself how to reproduce these extraordinary sounds. This documentary follows him to Tuva, where he takes part in a throat singing competition.
