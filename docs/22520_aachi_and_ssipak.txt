Title: Aachi and Ssipak (2006)
Genres: Action, Animation, Horror
Director: Jo Beom-jin
Main Cast: Ryoo Seung-bum, Hyun Young, Hae-chol Shin, Im Chang-Jung

Overview: Sometime in the future, mankind has depleted all energy and fuel sources, however they have engineered a way to use human excrement as fuel. To reward production, the government hands out extremely addictive, popsicle-like "Juicybars", which in turn also act as a laxative. Aachi and Ssipak are street hoodlums who struggle to survive by trading these bars in the black market.
