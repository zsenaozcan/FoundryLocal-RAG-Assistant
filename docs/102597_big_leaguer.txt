Title: Big Leaguer (1953)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Robert Aldrich
Main Cast: Edward G. Robinson, Vera-Ellen, Jeff Richards, Richard Jaeckel, William Campbell
Keywords: baseball, sport, new york giants baseball

Overview: John Lobert runs a training camp in Florida for the New York Giants. Every year, he evaluates the 18-22 year old hopefuls to pick the best for a minor league contract.
