Title: Silent Witness (2011)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Crime
Director: Peter Markle
Main Cast: Dermot Mulroney, Anne Heche, Judd Hirsch, Michael Cudlitz, Anthony Ruivivar

Overview: Prominent defense attorney Tony Lord returns to his hometown to defend an old friend, a teacher accused of murdering one of his students. The case re-opens the heartache from Tony's own high school days, when he was a student falsely accused of murdering his first love.
