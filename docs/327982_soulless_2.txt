Title: Soulless 2 (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Roman Prygunov
Main Cast: Danila Kozlovsky, Dominique Pinon, Miloš Biković, Mariya Andreeva

Overview: Max Andreev thought the best place to start a new life would be at the other end of the world. What he didn't know, is that you can't outrun your past.
