Title: Chevolution (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Trisha Ziff
Main Cast: Gerry Adams, Antonio Banderas, Gael García Bernal, Ernesto 'Che' Guevara, Tom Morello
Keywords: politics, photograph, woman director

Overview: Examines the history and legacy of the photo Guerrillero Heroico taken by famous Cuban photographer Alberto Díaz Gutiérrez. This image has thrived for the decades since Che Guevara's death and has evolved into an iconic image, which represents a multitude of ideals. The documentary film explores the story of how the photo came to be, its adoption of multiple interpretations and meanings, as well as the commercialization of the image of Ernesto "Che" Guevara.
