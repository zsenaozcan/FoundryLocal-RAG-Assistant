Title: Godard's Passion (1982)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jean-Luc Godard
Main Cast: Isabelle Huppert, Hanna Schygulla, Michel Piccoli, László Szabó, Jerzy Radziwiłowicz

Overview: While shooting a film, the director becomes interested in the unfolding struggle of a young factory worker that has been laid off by a boss who did not like her union activities.
