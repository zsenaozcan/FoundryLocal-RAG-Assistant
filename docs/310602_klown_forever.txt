Title: Klown Forever (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mikkel Nørgaard
Main Cast: Casper Christensen, Frank Hvam, Adam Levine, Nikolaj Coster-Waldau, Mia Lyhne
Keywords: male nudity

Overview: Frank and Casper's friendship is put to a test, when Casper decides to leave Denmark to pursue a solo career in Los Angeles. Determined to win his best friend back Frank chooses to follow Casper insuring an eventful trip.
