Title: Castle Freak (1995)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Horror, Mystery
Director: Stuart Gordon
Main Cast: Jeffrey Combs, Barbara Crampton, Jonathan Fuller, Jessica Dollarhide, Massimo Sarchielli
Keywords: castle, voyeurism

Overview: John has inherited a castle and Italy and he moves there with his wife Susan and their blind daughter Rebecca. What they don't realize is that there is somebody else in the castle. An abused child left to die in the basement who has now become the castle freak and is out to wreak havoc.
