Title: Ejecta (2014)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Chad Archibald
Main Cast: Julian Richings, Lisa Houle, Adam Seybold, Mark Gibson, Justin Darmanin
Keywords: truth, alien, survival, violence, universe, solar storm, hunted, anonymous

Overview: Two men witness an unexplainable event in the sky as a historic solar storm approaches, and they try to survive as a terrifying life form hunts them.
