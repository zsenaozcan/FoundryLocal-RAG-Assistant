Title: Classmates (2016)
Genres: Animation
Director: Shouko Nakamura
Main Cast: Hiroshi Kamiya, Kenji Nojima, Hideo Ishikawa
Keywords: boy's choir, high school, slice of life, coming of age, based on manga, anime, watercolour style, shounen ai

Overview: The story begins during music class before the chorus festival, when male student Kusakabe Hikaru notices that the bespectacled honor student Sajou Rihito is not singing.  Although he thinks that Rihito just doesn't like singing, he finds him practicing alone in the classroom after school. Without even thinking, Hikaru calls out to him.
