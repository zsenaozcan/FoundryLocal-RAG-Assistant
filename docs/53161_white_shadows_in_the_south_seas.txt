Title: White Shadows in the South Seas (1928)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: W.S. Van Dyke
Main Cast: Monte Blue, Raquel Torres, Robert Anderson, Renee Bush, Napua
Keywords: shipwreck, greed, doctor, native peoples

Overview: An alcoholic doctor on a Polynesian island, disgusted by white exploitation of the natives, finds himself marooned on a pristinely beautiful island.
