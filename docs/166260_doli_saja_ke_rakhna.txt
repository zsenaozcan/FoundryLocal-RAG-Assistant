Title: Doli Saja Ke Rakhna (1998)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Priyadarshan
Main Cast: Akshaye Khanna, Jyothika, Paresh Rawal, Moushumi Chatterjee, Anupam Kher

Overview: Inderjit Bansal comes from a wealthy family. His father and mother, Chandrika, would like him to get married and settle down,but he goes to another city to do his MBA and stays with friends. During this time, he meets Pallavi Sinh (Jyothika) and they fall in love. She reciprocates his feelings, but trouble comes in the form of her tyrannical trio of brothers
