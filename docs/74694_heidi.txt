Title: Heidi (1993)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Michael Ray Rhodes
Main Cast: Noley Thornton, Jason Robards, Jane Seymour, Lexi Randall, Patricia Neal

Overview: After charming her reclusive grandfather and falling in love with the beautiful mountain he calls home, Heidi is uprooted and sent to Frankfurt where she befriends Klara, a young girl confined to a wheelchair.
