Title: The Red Dwarf (1998)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Yvan Le Moine
Main Cast: Jean-Yves Tual, Anita Ekberg

Overview: Lucien is a dwarf, who works hard in a large legal office. His only friend is trapeze circus artist Isis. One day he gets the attention of singer Paola Bendoni and falls in love with her.
