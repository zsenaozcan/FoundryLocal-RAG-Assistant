Title: Frogs (1972)
Genres: Horror, Mystery
Director: George McCowan
Main Cast: Ray Milland, Sam Elliott, Joan Van Ark, Adam Roarke, Judy Pace
Keywords: poison, photographer, florida, snake, island, leech, wheelchair, bird, human vs nature, alligator

Overview: Jason Crockett is an aging, grumpy, physically disabled millionaire who invites his family to his island estate for his birthday celebration. Pickett Smith is a free-lance photographer who is doing a pollution layout for an ecology magazine. Jason Crockett hates nature, poisoning anything that crawls on his property. On the night of his birthday the frogs and other members of nature begin to pay Crockett back.
