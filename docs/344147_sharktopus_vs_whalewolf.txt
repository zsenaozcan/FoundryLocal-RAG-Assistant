Title: Sharktopus vs. Whalewolf (2015)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction, Adventure
Director: Kevin O'Neill
Main Cast: Casper Van Dien, Catherine Oxenberg, Akari Endo, Jorge Eduardo De Los Santos, Tony Almont
Keywords: monster, mutant, whale, sequel, shark

Overview: When a mad scientist mixes the genes of a killer whale and a wolf, it creates the Whalewolf, and it's up to Sharktopus to stop it.
