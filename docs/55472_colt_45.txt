Title: Colt .45 (1950)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Edwin L. Marin
Main Cast: Randolph Scott, Ruth Roman, Zachary Scott, Lloyd Bridges, Alan Hale
Keywords: gun

Overview: Scott plays Steve Farrell, a Colt .45 salesman who has two of the prized guns stolen by Jason Brett (Zachary Scott). Armed with the guns, Brett begins a reign of terror, leading a gang of robbers and murderers.
