Title: The Three Fantastic Supermen (1967)
Genres: Action, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Gianfranco Parolini
Main Cast: Tony Kendall, Brad Harris, Aldo Canti, Carlo Tamberlani, Rossella Bergamonti
Keywords: martial arts, mad scientist, superhero team, eurospy

Overview: FBI agent Brad joins Tony and Nick, the self styled Supermen who battle crime wearing bullet-proof super-suits. They are on a case involving radioactive counterfeit money and people who can be broken down into precious jewels.
