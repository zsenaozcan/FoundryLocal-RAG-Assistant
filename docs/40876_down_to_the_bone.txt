Title: Down to the Bone (2004)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Debra Granik
Main Cast: Vera Farmiga, Hugh Dillon, Clint Jordan, Caridad 'La Bruja' De La Luz, Jasper Daniels
Keywords: new york, cheating, cocaine, marriage, addiction, children, independent film, infertility, drug, extramarital affair
