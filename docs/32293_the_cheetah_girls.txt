Title: The Cheetah Girls (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family, Music, TV Movie
Director: Oz Scott
Main Cast: Raven-Symoné, Adrienne Bailon, Kiely Williams, Sabrina Bryan, Lynn Whitfield
Keywords: musical, high school, talent show, best friend, teenager, girl group, based on young adult novel

Overview: A four-member teen girl group named the Cheetah Girls go to a Manhattan High School for the Performing Arts and try to become the first freshmen to win the talent show in the school's history. During the talent show auditions, they meet a big-time producer named Jackal Johnson, who tries to make the group into superstars, but the girls run into many problems.
