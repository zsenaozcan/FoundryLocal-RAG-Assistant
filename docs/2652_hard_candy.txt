Title: Hard Candy (2005)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: David Slade
Main Cast: Patrick Wilson, Ellen Page, Sandra Oh, Odessa Rae, G.J. Echternkamp
Keywords: suicide, rape, age difference, photographer, ice, shower, menace, lie, pedophilia, manipulation

Overview: A mature 14-year old girl meets a charming 32-year old photographer on the Internet. Suspecting that he is a pedophile, she goes to his home in an attempt to expose him.
