Title: Abzurdah (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Daniela Goggi
Main Cast: María Eugenia Suárez, Esteban Lamothe, Gloria Carrá, Rafael Spregelburd, Tomás Ottaviano
Keywords: based on novel, biography, argentina, based on memoir or autobiography, internet chat, troubled teen, woman director, internet culture
