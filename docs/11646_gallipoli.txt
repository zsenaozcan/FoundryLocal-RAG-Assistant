Title: Gallipoli (1981)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, History, War
Director: Peter Weir
Main Cast: Mel Gibson, Mark Lee, Bill Kerr, Gerda Nicolson, Harold Hopkins
Keywords: world war i, battle assignment, troops, australia, turk, gallipoli campaign

Overview: As World War I rages, brave and youthful Australians Archy and Frank, both agile runners, become friends and enlist in the Australian and New Zealand Army Corps together. They later find themselves part of the Dardanelles Campaign on the Gallipoli peninsula, a brutal eight-month conflict which pit the British and their allies against the Ottoman Empire and left over 500,000 men dead.
