Title: Don't Grow Up (2015)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Thierry Poiraud
Main Cast: Fergus Riordan, Madeleine Kelly, McKell David, Darren Evans, Natifa Mai
Keywords: island, growing up, zombie

Overview: The story about a group of youths who can't face the thought of growing up because anyone who does becomes a rampaging zombie.
