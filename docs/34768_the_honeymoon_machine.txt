Title: The Honeymoon Machine (1961)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Richard Thorpe
Main Cast: Steve McQueen, Jim Hutton, Paula Prentiss, Brigid Bazlen, Dean Jagger
Keywords: casino, hotel, computer, roulette, admiral

Overview: Lieutenant Fergie Howard, Ensign Beau Gilliam and navy scientist Jason Elderidge use their ships computer, designed to calculate missile trajectories, to calculate a roulette ball's trajectory and hit the Venice Casino. Things get complex when Admiral Fitch arrives at the same Casino.
