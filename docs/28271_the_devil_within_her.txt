Title: The Devil Within Her (1975)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Peter Sasdy
Main Cast: Joan Collins, Donald Pleasence, Ralph Bates, Caroline Munro, Eileen Atkins
Keywords: evil baby

Overview: A woman gives birth to a baby, but this is no ordinary little tyke. The child is seemingly possessed by the spirit of a freak dwarf who the mother once spurned. Cue a spate of strange deaths, the one common factor being the presence of a baby in pram at the scene...
