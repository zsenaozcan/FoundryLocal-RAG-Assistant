Title: The River Why (2010)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Matthew Leutwyler
Main Cast: William Hurt, Amber Heard, Zach Gilford, Dallas Roberts, Gattlin Griffith
Keywords: based on novel, fly fishing

Overview: A young man abandons his family for a solitary life of fly-fishing. His goal was to find his own way in the fishing world and thereby find himself and love.
