Title: Breaking the Maya Code (2008)
Genres: Documentary, Mystery
Director: David Lebrun
Main Cast: Michael D. Coe, Ian Graham, Dr. Nikolai Grube, Peter Mathews, CCH Pounder

Overview: The complex and beautiful hieroglyphic script of the ancient Maya was until recently one of the last great undeciphered writing systems. Based on the best-selling book by Michael Coe, called by the New York Times "one of the great stories of 20th century scientific discovery", Breaking the Maya Code traces the epic quest to unlock the secrets of the script across 200 years, nine countries and three continents.
