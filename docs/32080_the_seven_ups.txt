Title: The Seven-Ups (1973)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Drama, Crime
Director: Philip D'Antoni
Main Cast: Roy Scheider, Jerry Leon, Tony Lo Bianco, Victor Arnold, Ken Kercheval
Keywords: car chase

Overview: A tough detective who is part of an elite New York City unit is trying to find out who killed his partner, but uncovers a plot to kidnap mobsters for money.
