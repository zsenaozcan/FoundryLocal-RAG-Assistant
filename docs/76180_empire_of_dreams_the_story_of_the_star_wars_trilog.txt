Title: Empire of Dreams: The Story of the Star Wars Trilogy (2004)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Kevin Burns
Main Cast: Robert Clotworthy, Kenny Baker, Jim Bloom, Leo Braudy, Ben Burtt
Keywords: film history, audition, making of, space opera

Overview: Empire of Dreams: The Story of the Star Wars Trilogy is a 2004 documentary film directed by Kevin Burns and narrated by Robert Clotworthy. It documents the making of the original Star Wars trilogy.
