Title: Chinese Puzzle (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Cédric Klapisch
Main Cast: Romain Duris, Audrey Tautou, Cécile de France, Kelly Reilly, Sandrine Holt
Keywords: chinatown, immigration, friendship, sham marriage, frenchman abroad

Overview: Xavier is a 40-year-old father of two who still finds life very complicated. When the mother of his children moves to New York, he can't bear them growing up far away from him and so he decides to move there as well.
