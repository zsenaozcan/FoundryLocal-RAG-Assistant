Title: In the Room (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Eric Khoo
Main Cast: Josie Ho, Shô Nishino, Choi Woo-shik, Kim Kkobbi, Gillian Tan
Keywords: drug abuse, nudity, interracial romance, sexual joke, sex talk, singapore

Overview: This sensitive and sensual film draws together several narratives spanning several decades, all of them transpiring in the same room of the same Singaporean hotel — and all of them involving sex.
