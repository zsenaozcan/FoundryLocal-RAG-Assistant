Title: Girlfriends of Christmas Past (2016)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Jake Helgren
Main Cast: Tammin Sursok, Brent Bailey, Lindsey McKeon, Abigail Klein, John Brotherton
Keywords: love, christmas

Overview: When three hilarious, strong-willed women join forces to seek the ultimate revenge on their cheating ex at his upcoming holiday retreat, love struck event planner Livvy meets Carter, the property manager organizing it all, leaving Livvy to question if she can leave off their revenge scheme long enough to finally forgive her ex and let a real holiday romance blossom.
