Title: Before You Say 'I Do' (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Drama, Fantasy, TV Movie
Director: Paul Fox
Main Cast: David Sutcliffe, Jennifer Westfeldt, Lauren Holly, Brad Borbridge, Jeff Roop

Overview: George Murray's fiancée Jane Gardner gets cold feet after accepting his ring, terrorized by her first wedding with Doug, who cheated that very day with their wedding coordinator. After a car crash, George finds himself 10 years in the past, just days before Doug's day.
