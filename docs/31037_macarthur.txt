Title: MacArthur (1977)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Joseph Sargent
Main Cast: Gregory Peck, Ivan Bonar, Ward Costello, Nicolas Coster, Marj Dusay
Keywords: world war ii, history

Overview: The film portrays MacArthur's (Gregory Peck) life from 1942, before the Battle of Bataan, to 1952, the time after he had been removed from his Korean War command by President Truman (Ed Flanders) for insubordination, and is recounted in flashback as he visits West Point.
