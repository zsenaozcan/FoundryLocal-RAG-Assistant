Title: Hansel and Gretel (1988)
Genres: Family, Fantasy
Director: Len Talan
Main Cast: Hugh Pollard, Nicola Stapleton, David Warner, Emily Richard, Cloris Leachman
Keywords: witch, fairy tale, evil witch, hansel and gretel, wood

Overview: Hansel and Gretel tells the tale of two young children driven from home by their scolding mother. Losing their way in the dark forest they stumble upon the cottage of a kindly old woman. But is this kindly old woman everything she seems...?
