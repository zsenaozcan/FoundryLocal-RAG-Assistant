Title: Private Eyes (1953)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Edward Bernds
Main Cast: Leo Gorcey, Huntz Hall, Bernard Gorcey, Robert Osterloh, Joyce Holden
Keywords: bowery boys

Overview: After being punched in the nose, Sach finds out that he has the ability to read minds. Slip and the gang start up a detective agency try to cash in on Sach's new powers.
