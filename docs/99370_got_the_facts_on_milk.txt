Title: Got the Facts on Milk? (2011)
Genres: Drama, Documentary
Director: Shira Lane
Main Cast: T.C. Campbell, Matthew Osborne, James LeQuanq, Shira Lane

Overview: Got the facts on Milk? (also known as "The Milk Documentary" is an entertaining, award winning feature documentary that dares to question the conventional wisdom of the much publicized health benefits of milk and dairy products.  Addressing myth, truth and all in-between, the film is a humorous yet shocking exposition that provokes serious thought about this everyday staple.
