Title: Christmas Holiday (1944)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Robert Siodmak
Main Cast: Deanna Durbin, Gene Kelly, Richard Whorf, Dean Harens, Gladys George
Keywords: airplane, murder, reporter, catholic church, flashback, film noir

Overview: Don't be fooled by the title. Christmas Holiday is a far, far cry from It's a Wonderful Life. Told in flashback, the story begins as Abigail Martin marries Southern aristocrat Robert Monette. Unfortunately, Robert has inherited his family's streak of violence and instability, and soon drags Abigail into a life of misery.
