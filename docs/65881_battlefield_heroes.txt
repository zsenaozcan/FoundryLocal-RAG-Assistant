Title: Battlefield Heroes (2011)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, History
Director: Lee Joon-ik
Main Cast: Jung Jin-young, Lee Mun-sik, Ryu Seung-ryong, Yoon Je-moon, Sunwoo Sun

Overview: This story unfolds 8 years after 'Battle of Hwangsanbeol', which destroyed Baekjae, when Silla-Tang Dynasty union attacks Goguryeo.
