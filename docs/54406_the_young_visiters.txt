Title: The Young Visiters (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family, Romance
Director: David Yates
Main Cast: Jim Broadbent, Hugh Laurie, Lyndsey Marshal, Bill Nighy, Geoffrey Palmer

Overview: The Young Visiters, written in twelve days by nine-year-old Daisy Ashford in 1890, is a surreal blend of naiveté, precocious perception and inadvertent social satire.
