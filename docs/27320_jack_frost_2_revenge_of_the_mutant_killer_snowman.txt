Title: Jack Frost 2: Revenge of the Mutant Killer Snowman (2000)
Genres: Horror
Director: Michael Cooney
Main Cast: Christopher Allport, Eileen Seeley, Chip Heller, Scott MacDonald, David Allen Brooks
Keywords: snowman

Overview: The sheriff and his deputies from the first movie decide to take a vacation in the Caribbean. Their holiday will be short-lived, however, as the thawed murderer gets inadvertently re-frozen and brought back to life. As if that weren't bad enough, he now has the ability to remain frozen even in tropical temperatures, and he's headed south to settle some old scores.
