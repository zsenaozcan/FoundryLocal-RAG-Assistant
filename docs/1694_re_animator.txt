Title: Re-Animator (1985)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy, Horror
Director: Stuart Gordon
Main Cast: Jeffrey Combs, Bruce Abbott, Barbara Crampton, David Gale, Robert Sampson
Keywords: overdose, human experimentation, black humor, insanity, reanimation, laboratory, mad scientist, h.p. lovecraft, decapitation, severed head

Overview: A dedicated student at a medical college and his girlfriend become involved in bizarre experiments centering around the re-animation of dead tissue when an odd new student arrives on campus.
