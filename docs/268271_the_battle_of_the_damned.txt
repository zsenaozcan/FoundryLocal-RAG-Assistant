Title: The Battle of the Damned (1969)
Genres: War
Director: Roberto Bianchi Montero
Main Cast: Dale Cummings, Maurice Poli, Herb Andress, Fabio Testi, Luciano Catenacci
Keywords: world war ii

Overview: A small band of misfit American commandos are assigned to head across the North African desert to blow up a huge German fuel depot.
