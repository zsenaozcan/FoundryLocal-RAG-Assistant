Title: The Sign of Four (2001)
Genres: Mystery, Crime
Director: Rodney Gibbons
Main Cast: Matt Frewer, Kenneth Welsh, Sophie Lorain, Marcel Jeannin, Michel Perron
Keywords: sherlock holmes

Overview: Greed, betrayal and vengeance set the stage for this Sir Arthur Conan Doyle classic. Mary Morstan, a young governess, has been receiving a rare and lustrous pearl annually from an anonymous benefactor.
