Title: Lusers (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Adventure
Director: Ticoy Rodriguez
Main Cast: Felipe Izquierdo, Pablo Granados, Gaby Espino, Cristián de la Fuente, Carlos Alcantara
Keywords: world cup, road trip, comedy

Overview: Three guys from differents countries (Peru, Argentina and Chile) come together in a funny adventure through the jungle in order to get to the Final of the FIFA World Cup Brasil 2014.
