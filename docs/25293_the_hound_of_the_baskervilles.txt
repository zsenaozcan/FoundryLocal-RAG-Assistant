Title: The Hound of the Baskervilles (2000)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Rodney Gibbons
Main Cast: Matt Frewer, Kenneth Welsh, Jason London, Emma Campbell, Gordon Masten
Keywords: detective, sherlock holmes, suspense, curse

Overview: The mysterious death of Sir Charles Baskerville is blamed on a longstanding curse that has followed the Baskerville family for two hundred years. Enigmatic sleuth Sherlock Holmes is on the case to uncover the truth about a monstrous, supernatural hound who roams the moors, waiting to attack the latest heir to the Baskerville estate.
