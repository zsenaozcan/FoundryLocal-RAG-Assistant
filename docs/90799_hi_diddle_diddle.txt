Title: Hi Diddle Diddle (1943)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Andrew L. Stone
Main Cast: Martha Scott, Adolphe Menjou, Pola Negri, Billie Burke, Dennis O'Keefe
Keywords: musical, animated sequence, screwball comedy

Overview: When the bride's mother is supposedly swindled out of her money by a spurned suitor, the groom's father orchestrates a scheme of his own to set things right. He is aided by a cabaret singer, while placating a jealous wife.
