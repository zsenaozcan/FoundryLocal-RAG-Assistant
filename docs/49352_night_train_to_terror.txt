Title: Night Train to Terror (1985)
Genres: Horror
Director: Gregg C. Tallas
Main Cast: John Phillip Law, Cameron Mitchell, Richard Moll, Tony Giorgio, Marc Lawrence
Keywords: god, anthology, satan, train crash, horror anthology

Overview: God and Satan are on a train discussing the fate of three individuals. The stories of the people in question are told in a trio of very strange vignettes. One involves an insane anylum with some very interesting treatment plans. Another involves a 'death club'. The final story shows us the adventures of a server of Satan.
