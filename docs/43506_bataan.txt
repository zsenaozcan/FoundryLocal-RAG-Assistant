Title: Bataan (1943)
Genres: Action, Drama, War
Director: Tay Garnett
Main Cast: Robert Taylor, George Murphy, Thomas Mitchell, Lloyd Nolan, Lee Bowman
Keywords: philippines, last stand, bataan

Overview: Japan has just invaded the Phillipines and the US Army attempts a desperate defence. Thirteen men are chosen to blow up a bridge on the Bataan peninsula and keep the Japanese from rebuilding it.
