Title: Dear Diary (1993)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Nanni Moretti
Main Cast: Nanni Moretti, Renato Carpentieri, Antonio Neiwiller, Claudia Della Seta, Lorenzo Alessandri
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Writer/director/actor Nanni Moretti offers a three-part film diary which takes a sharply satiric look at Italian life.
