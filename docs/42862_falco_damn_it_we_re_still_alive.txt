Title: Falco: Damn It, We're Still Alive! (2008)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Thomas Roth
Main Cast: Manuel Rubey, Patricia Aulitzky, Christian Tramitz, Martin Loos, Nicholas Ofczarek
Keywords: biography, falco

Overview: Deals with the life, work, and tragic death of Johann "Falco" Hölzel. The biopic portrays his work through his early days, as member of the Viennese band "Drahdiwaberl", and later with his own hits as solo artist, like "Der Kommissar", "Wiener Blut", and his worldwide smash-hit "Rock Me Amadeus".
