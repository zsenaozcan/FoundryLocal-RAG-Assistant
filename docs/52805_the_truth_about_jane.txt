Title: The Truth About Jane (2000)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Lee Rose
Main Cast: Stockard Channing, Ellen Muth, Kelly Rowan, Jenny O'Hara, RuPaul
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Jane, a high school teenager, tries to deal with the discovery that she is a lesbian after developing an intense friendship with another girl who makes her discover her true sexuality, which is only the start of Jane's troubles when Jane's unaccepting mother, Janice, struggles with her surprising revelation of brought forth by her only daughter.
