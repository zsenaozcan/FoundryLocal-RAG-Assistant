Title: 12:01 PM (1990)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Jonathan Heap
Main Cast: Kurtwood Smith, Jane Alden, Don Amendolia, John Bachelder, Laura Harrington
Keywords: time travel

Overview: Kurtwood Smith plays Myron Castleman, an everyman who keeps repeating the same hour of his life, from 12:01 PM to 1:00 PM.
