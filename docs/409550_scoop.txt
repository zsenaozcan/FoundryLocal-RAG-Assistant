Title: Scoop! (2016)
Genres: Mystery
Director: Hitoshi Ohne
Main Cast: Masaharu Fukuyama, Fumi Nikaido, Yo Yoshida, Kenichi Takito, Lily Franky

Overview: Shizuoka Miyakonojo is a middle-aged paparazzi photographer. His skills as a cameraman are good, but he gambles and is in debt. When he worked in the editorial department for weekly picture magazine 'Scoop!,' he landed numerous scoops. He now works as a freelance photographer and chases after celebrity scandals.  Shizuoka Miyakonojo works with Nobi Namekawa. She is a rookie reporter for 'Scoop!' and they land a scoop.
