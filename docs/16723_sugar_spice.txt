Title: Sugar & Spice (2001)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Francine McDougall
Main Cast: Marla Sokoloff, Marley Shelton, Melissa George, Mena Suvari, Rachel Blanchard
Keywords: rebel, american football, robbery, bank, mask, cheerleader, high school, love, money, teenager

Overview: When Jack (James Marsden) and Diane (Marley Shelton) find themselves in an unexpected adult situation, the A-Squad comes to their rescue. In order to help their friend Diane, the A-Squad goes where no cheerleader has gone before: taking on a little after-school project known as bank robbery. But the A-Squad does things their way -- with sugar and spice -- forever changing their friendship, their future and the nation's notion of teen spirit.
