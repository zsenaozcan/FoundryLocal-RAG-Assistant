Title: Feeding Frenzy (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Mike Stoklasa
Main Cast: Ron Lipski, Gillian Bellinger, Mike Stoklasa, Mike Johnson, Jack Packard

Overview: A mysterious old man named Mr. Plinkett is keeping a deep dark secret locked away in the basement of a small-town hardware store: vicious, flesh-eating creatures with a mysterious past. Once these creatures escape, all hell breaks loose and it's up to Jesse, an incompetent hardware store employee, to stop them from turning the town into a feeding frenzy!
