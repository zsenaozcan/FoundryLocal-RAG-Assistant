Title: Life (2017)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Daniel Espinosa
Main Cast: Jake Gyllenhaal, Rebecca Ferguson, Ryan Reynolds, Hiroyuki Sanada, Ariyon Bakare
Keywords: mars, alien life-form, space, astronaut, space station, intelligent, trapped in space, extraterrestrial life

Overview: The six-member crew of the International Space Station is tasked with studying a sample from Mars that may be the first proof of extra-terrestrial life, which proves more intelligent than ever expected.
