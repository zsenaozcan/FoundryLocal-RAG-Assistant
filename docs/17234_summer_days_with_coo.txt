Title: Summer Days With Coo (2007)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Romance
Director: Keiichi Hara
Main Cast: Kazato Tomizawa, Takahiro Yokokawa, Naoki Tanaka, Tamaki Matsumoto, Ken'ichi Nagira
Keywords: river, swimming, sumo, back in time, corrupt official

Overview: A small, lone Kappa miraculously survives over 200 years into modern day Japan, when he is found by young Koichi. Coo, who is secretly adopted by Koichi's family, searches with his new human friend for unpopulated places in hopes of finding any remaining of his kind.
