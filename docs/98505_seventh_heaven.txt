Title: Seventh Heaven (1997)
Genres: Drama
Director: Benoît Jacquot
Main Cast: Sandrine Kiberlain, Vincent Lindon, François Berléand, Philippe Magnan, Francine Bergé
Keywords: kleptomania, hypnosis, marital problem, hypnotherapy
