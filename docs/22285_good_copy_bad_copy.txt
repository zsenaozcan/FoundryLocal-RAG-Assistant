Title: Good Copy Bad Copy (2007)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Andreas Johnsen
Main Cast: Danger Mouse, Girl Talk, Dr. Lawrence Ferrara, Paul V. Licalsi, Jane Peterer
Keywords: brazil, music, freedom of the press, copyright, intellectual property, nollywood, nigerian cinema, creative commons, tecno brego, music sampling, mpaa
