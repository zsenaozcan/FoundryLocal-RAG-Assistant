Title: Killer Crocodile (1989)
Genres: Horror
Director: Fabrizio De Angelis
Main Cast: Richard Anthony Crenna, Ann Douglas, Ennio Girolami, Bill Wohrman, Sherrie Rose

Overview: A group of environmentalists arrives at a faraway tropical delta where toxic waste is being dumped. However the water also hides a giant crocodile. The corrupt local officials don't help much either.
