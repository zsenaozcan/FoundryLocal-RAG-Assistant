Title: Enclave (2015)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Goran Radovanović
Main Cast: Nebojša Glogovac, Anica Dobra, Miodrag Krivokapić, Milena Jakšić, Filip Šubarić
Keywords: serbian, post war, albanian

Overview: Nenad, ten years Christian boy from a Serbian enclave, determined to create a proper community burial for his late grandfather, crosses enemy lines and makes friends among the Muslim majority in deeply divided, war-torn Kosovo.
