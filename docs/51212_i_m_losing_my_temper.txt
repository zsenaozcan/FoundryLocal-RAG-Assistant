Title: I'm Losing My Temper (1974)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Claude Zidi
Main Cast: Pierre Richard, Jane Birkin, Claude Piéplu, Jean Martin, Danielle Minazzoli

Overview: Pierre is a clumsy, overly serious math teacher at an all-girls high school. His life is thrown into chaos after encountering a beautiful British actress and the paparazzi that follow her around.
