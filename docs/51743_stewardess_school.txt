Title: Stewardess School (1986)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ken Blancato
Main Cast: Brett Cullen, Mary Cadorette, Don Most, Sandahl Bergman, Wendie Jo Sperber
Keywords: female nudity, stewardess, sexploitation, school, teen comedy, airline

Overview: A group of varied misfits (including a former prostitute/stripper and a bumbler who can't see more than 6 inches in front of his face) enter a school to become flight attendants. Somehow, the group makes it through to the final test: a cross-country flight.
