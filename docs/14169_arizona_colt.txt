Title: Arizona Colt (1966)
Genres: Western
Director: Michele Lupo
Main Cast: Giuliano Gemma, Fernando Sancho, Roberto Camardiel, Corinne Marchand, Nello Pazzafini
Keywords: woman shaves a man, union suit

Overview: Bandit Gordon frees a group of prisoners, forcing them to join his gang or die. Arizona Colt, declining to do either, heads for Blackstone City where Gordon is planning a robbery. When one of Gordon's men murders saloon girl Dolores, Arizona offers to hunt down the killer for the price of five hundred dollars and Dolores' sister, Jane.
