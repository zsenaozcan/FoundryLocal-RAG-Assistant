Title: Haider (2014)
Genres: Action, Drama, Crime, Romance
Director: Vishal Bhardwaj
Main Cast: Shahid Kapoor, Tabu, Shraddha Kapoor, Kay Kay Menon, Irrfan Khan
Keywords: suspense, death of father, kashmir conflict, avenge

Overview: A young man returns to Kashmir after his father's disappearance to confront his uncle - the man he suspects to have a role in his father's fate.
