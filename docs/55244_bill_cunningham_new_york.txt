Title: Bill Cunningham New York (2010)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Richard Press
Main Cast: Bill Cunningham, Tom Wolfe, Anna Wintour, Carmen Dell'Orefice, Iris Apfel
Keywords: fashion, manhattan, new york city, milliner, new york city ballet, poncho, haute couture, hat shop, traffic, new york times, landlord tenant relationship

Overview: Chronicles a man who is obsessively interested in only one thing, the pictures he takes that document the way people dress. The 80-year-old New York Times photographer has two columns in the paper's Style section, yet nobody knows who he is.
