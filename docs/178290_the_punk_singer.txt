Title: The Punk Singer (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Sini Anderson
Main Cast: Kathleen Hanna, Adam Horovitz, Joan Jett, Jennifer Baumgardner, Johanna Fateman
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A look at the life of activist, musician, and cultural icon Kathleen Hanna, who formed the punk band Bikini Kill and pioneered the "riot grrrl" movement of the 1990s.
