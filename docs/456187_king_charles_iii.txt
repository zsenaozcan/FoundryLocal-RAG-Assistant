Title: King Charles III (2017)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rupert Goold
Main Cast: Tim Pigott-Smith, Charlotte Riley, Oliver Chris, Adam James, Richard Goulding

Overview: Prince Charles' accession to the throne following the Queen's death. When he refuses to sign a controversial bill into law, political chaos ensues: a constitutional crisis, rioting on the streets and a tank in front of Buckingham Palace.
