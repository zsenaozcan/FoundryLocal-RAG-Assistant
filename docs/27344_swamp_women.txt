Title: Swamp Women (1956)
Genres: Crime, Adventure, Drama, Thriller
Director: Roger Corman
Main Cast: Marie Windsor, Carole Mathews, Beverly Garland, Mike Connors, Susan Cummings
Keywords: swamp, b movie

Overview: An undercover policewoman helps three female convicts escape from prison so that they can lead her to a stash of stolen diamonds hidden in a swamp.
