Title: A Thousand Acres (1997)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jocelyn Moorhouse
Main Cast: Michelle Pfeiffer, Jessica Lange, Jason Robards, Jennifer Jason Leigh, Colin Firth
Keywords: tragedy, family, woman director

Overview: A THOUSAND ACRES is a drama about an American family who meets with tragedy on their land. It is the story of a father, his daughters, and their husbands, and their passion to subdue the history of their land and its stories.
