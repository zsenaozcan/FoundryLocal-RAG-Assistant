Title: The Rutles 2: Can't Buy Me Lunch (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary, Foreign
Director: Eric Idle
Main Cast: Eric Idle, Neil Innes, Ricky Fataar, John Halsey, David Bowie
Keywords: fab four, parody

Overview: Twenty-three years after the release of the original Beatles mockumentary, 'The Rutles: All You Need Is Cash', famous artists, actors and musicians speak out on how The Rutles influenced them.
