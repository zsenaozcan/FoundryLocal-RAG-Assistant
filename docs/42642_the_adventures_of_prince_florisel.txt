Title: The Adventures of Prince Florisel (1979)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Mystery
Director: Yevgeni Tatarsky
Main Cast: Oleg Dal, Donatas Banionis, Igor Dmitriev, Lyubov Polishchuk, Boris Novikov

Overview: A mostly comical tale of all-powerful and wealthy Bavarian prince adventures that started when prince was seeking to deal with the boredom. Joining the Club of Suicide society, he uncovered the mastermind criminal behind it, and vowed to get rid of him by any means necessary, following deaths of several servants when dreaded president of club makes dashing escape. During that point, story changes into nearly second movie, as prince has to uncover yet another crime.
