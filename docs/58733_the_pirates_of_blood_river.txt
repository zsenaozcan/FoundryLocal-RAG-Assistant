Title: The Pirates of Blood River (1962)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: John Gilling
Main Cast: Christopher Lee, Glenn Corbett, Kerwin Mathews, Oliver Reed, Dennis Waterman
Keywords: pirate, penal colony

Overview: A group of ruthless pirates attack a 17th Century Huguenot settlement on the Isle of Devon in search of treasure and will stop at nothing to obtain it.
