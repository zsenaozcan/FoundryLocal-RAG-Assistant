Title: Rat (2001)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Comedy, Family
Director: Steve Barron
Main Cast: Imelda Staunton, Frank Kelly, David Wilmot, Kerry Condon, Andrew Lovern

Overview: After a night of drinking Guiness at the local watering hole, an ordinary, working-class, family man in Dublin's life is turned upside-down when he wakes up as a rat.
