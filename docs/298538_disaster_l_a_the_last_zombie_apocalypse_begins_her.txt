Title: Disaster L.A.: The Last Zombie Apocalypse Begins Here (2014)
Genres: Action, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Turner Clay
Main Cast: Justin Ray, Stefanie Estes, Jerod Meagher, Ali Williams, Ron Hanks
Keywords: zombie apocalypse

Overview: After a meteor shower strikes Los Angeles, toxic smoke is released across the city causing people to physically change and become violent. A small group of survivors plans to vacate the city by heading to the coast.
