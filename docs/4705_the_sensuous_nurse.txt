Title: The Sensuous Nurse (1975)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Nello Rossati
Main Cast: Ursula Andress, Duilio Del Prete, Mario Pisu, Daniele Vargas, Carla Romanelli
Keywords: female nudity, nurse, heart attack, legacy hunter, beautiful woman
