Title: Just a Gigolo (1979)
Genres: Drama
Director: David Hemmings
Main Cast: David Bowie, Kim Novak, Marlene Dietrich, Maria Schell, Curd Jürgens
Keywords: gigolo

Overview: After World War I, a war hero returns to Berlin to find that there's no place for him--he has no skills other than what he learned in the army, and can only find menial, low-paying jobs. He decides to become a gigolo to lonely rich women.
