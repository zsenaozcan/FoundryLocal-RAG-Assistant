Title: Hellboy Animated: Blood and Iron (2007)
Genres: TV Movie, Fantasy, Animation, Horror, Action, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Victor Cook
Main Cast: Ron Perlman, Selma Blair, John Hurt, Doug Jones, Peri Gilpin
Keywords: vampire, manga, horror, sequel, adult animation, dark horse comics, hecate

Overview: When Hellboy, Liz Sherman, and Abe Sapien are assigned to investigate the ghost-infested mansion of a publicity-hound billionaire, they uncover a plot to resurrect a beautiful yet monstrous vampire from Professor Bruttenholm’s past. But before they can stop her bloodbath, Hellboy will have to battle harpies, hellhounds, a giant werewolf, and even the ferocious goddess Hecate herself.
