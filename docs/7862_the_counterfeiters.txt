Title: The Counterfeiters (2007)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Stefan Ruzowitzky
Main Cast: Karl Markovics, August Diehl, Devid Striesow, Martin Brambach, August Zirner
Keywords: world war ii, sachsenhausen, counterfeit, nazi germany

Overview: The story Jewish counterfeiter, Salomon Sorowitsch, coerced into assisting the Nazi operation of the Sachsenhausen concentration camp during World War II.
