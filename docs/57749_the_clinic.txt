Title: The Clinic (2010)
Genres: Mystery, Horror, Thriller, Crime
Director: James Rabbitts
Main Cast: Tabrett Bethell, Freya Stafford, Andy Whitfield, Clare Bowen, Sophie Lowe

Overview: While traveling across country with her fiancé, Beth wakes alone in an isolated clinic to a mother's worst nightmare. Just how far will she go to save her child?
