Title: BUtterfield 8 (1960)
Genres: Drama
Director: Daniel Mann
Main Cast: Elizabeth Taylor, Laurence Harvey, Eddie Fisher, Dina Merrill, Mildred Dunnock
Keywords: fashion model, marrying money

Overview: The story about Gloria Wandrous, a promiscuous fashion model, who falls in love with Weston Liggett, the hard drinking son of a working class family who has married into money.
