Title: The Speak (2011)
Genres: Horror
Director: Anthony Pierce
Main Cast: Tom Sizemore, Kristina Anapau, Tina Casciani, Brett Donowho, Michael Klinger

Overview: A small TV crew make their way into the most haunted hotel in the US for the last program in their web series on the paranormal. With the help of a native-American medium, they perform ‘the speak’, a ceremony that brings forth more spirits and demons than the crew bargained for.
