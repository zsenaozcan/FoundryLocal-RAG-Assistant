Title: Vietnam in HD (2011)
Genres: War
Director: Sammy Jackson
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: vietnam

Overview: Vietnam in HD is a 6-part series that immerses viewers in the sights, sounds and stories of the Vietnam War as it has never before been seen. Thousands of hours of uncensored footage--much of it shot by soldiers in action--will detail every critical chapter of the conflict.  The war will unfold onscreen through the gripping firsthand accounts of 13 brave men and women who were forever changed by their experience in Vietnam.
