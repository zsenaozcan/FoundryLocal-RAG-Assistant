Title: The Invisible Man's Revenge (1944)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Ford Beebe
Main Cast: Jon Hall, John Carradine, Evelyn Ankers, Leon Errol, Alan Curtis
Keywords: monster, blackmail, sequel, revenge, fugitive, mad doctor, reporter, invisible man, invisibility

Overview: An eccentric scientist helps a fugitive from the law become invisible, unwittingly giving him the power to exact revenge on his former friends.
