Title: A Crush on You (2011)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Allison Anders
Main Cast: Brigid Brannagh, Sean Patrick Flanery, Christine Scott Bennett, Tug Coker, Connor Gibbs
Keywords: woman director
