Title: The Magic of Belle Isle (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Rob Reiner
Main Cast: Morgan Freeman, Virginia Madsen, Madeline Carroll, Emma Fuhrmann, Nicolette Pierini

Overview: In an effort to tap into his original talent, a wheelchair-bound author moves to a rural town, where he befriends a single mother and her three kids, who help reignite his passion for writing.
