Title: A Summer to Remember (1960)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Family
Director: Georgiy Daneliya
Main Cast: Boris Barkhatov, Irina Skobtseva, Lyubov Sokolova, Sergey Bondarchuk, Vasiliy Merkurev

Overview: Based on the Vera Panova book about a complicated relationships between 8-years boy and his new stepfather.
