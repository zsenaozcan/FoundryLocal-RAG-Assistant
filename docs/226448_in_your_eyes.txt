Title: In Your Eyes (2014)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Brin Hill
Main Cast: Zoe Kazan, Michael Stahl-David, Nikki Reed, Jennifer Grey, Mark Feuerstein
Keywords: telepathy, new mexico, loneliness, ex-con, offbeat

Overview: Two seemingly unconnected souls from different corners of the United States make a telepathic bond that allows them to see, hear and feel the other's experiences, creating a bond that apparently can't be broken.
