Title: The Big Heat (1988)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: Johnnie To
Main Cast: Waise Lee, Joey Wong, Matthew Wong Hin-Mung, Philip Kwok Chun-Fung, Lionel Lo King-Wah

Overview: A cop (Waise Lee) is losing the control of his right hand and cannot pull the trigger on time anymore. Before any accident happens, he decides to resign. Meanwhile his friend and informer is brutally murdered in Malaysia. Before leaving the force, he decides to find the killers with the help of his buddy, a young cop and an inspector from Kuala Lumpur.
