Title: In My Country (2004)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: John Boorman
Main Cast: Samuel L. Jackson, Juliette Binoche, Brendan Gleeson, Langley Kirkwood, Harriet Lenabe
Keywords: journalism, apartheid, justice

Overview: An American reporter and an Afrikaans poet meet and fall in love while covering South Africa's Truth and Reconciliation Commission hearings.
