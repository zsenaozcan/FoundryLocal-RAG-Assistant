Title: Reflections of a Blender (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: André Klotzel
Main Cast: Selton Mello, Fabiula Nascimento, Ana Lúcia Torre, Germano Haiut, Zé Carlos Machado

Overview: After receiving a patch, a blender gains consciousness and begin to think about life, the human and the lifetime of electronics. When a man disappears, the blender remains the only company of his wife.
