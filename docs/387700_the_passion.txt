Title: The Passion (2016)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Peter Barsocchini
Main Cast: Tyler Perry, Jencarlos Canela, Trisha Yearwood, Chris Daughtry, Prince Royce

Overview: Tyler Perry narrates a musical portrait of the last days of Jesus Christ, from the Last Supper to the Resurrection, which plays out on the streets of New Orleans through Bible passages and contemporary pop songs.
