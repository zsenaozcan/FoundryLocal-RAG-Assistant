Title: Mr. Moto Takes a Chance (1938)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Norman Foster
Main Cast: Peter Lorre, Rochelle Hudson, Robert Kent, J. Edward Bromberg, Chick Chandler
Keywords: radio series, mr. moto

Overview: In the jungle near Angkor Wat in Cambodia, Mr. Moto poses as an ineffectual archaeologist and a venerable holy man with mystical powers to help foil two insurgencies against the government.
