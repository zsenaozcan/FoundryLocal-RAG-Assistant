Title: Oppressed Majority (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Éléonore Pourriat
Main Cast: Pierre Bénéziti, Marie-Lorna Vaconsin, Jamel Barbouche, Céline Menville, Marie Favasuli
Keywords: feminism, sexism, sexual harassment, gender roles

Overview: On what seems to be just another ordinary day, a man is exposed to sexism and sexual violence in a society ruled by women.
