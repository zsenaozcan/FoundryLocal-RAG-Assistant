Title: Papillon (1973)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Franklin J. Schaffner
Main Cast: Steve McQueen, Dustin Hoffman, Victor Jory, Don Gordon, Anthony Zerbe
Keywords: prison, escape from prison, devil's island

Overview: A man befriends a fellow criminal as the two of them begin serving their sentence on a dreadful prison island, which inspires the man to plot his escape.
