Title: Love by Chance (2016)
Genres: Romance, TV Movie
Director: Gary Harvey
Main Cast: Beau Garrett, Brenda Strong, Benjamin Ayres, Garwin Sanford, Peter Graham-Gaudreau
Keywords: restaurant, romantic comedy, matchmaking, chef

Overview: Claire, an ambitious pastry chef, is busy running her new restaurant, but her meddling mom is preoccupied with her lack of love life. Without her knowledge, Claire’s mother finds her the perfect man, but when Claire finds out it wasn’t fate that brought them together, it could ruin the relationship.
