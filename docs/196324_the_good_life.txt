Title: The Good Life (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Andrés Wood
Main Cast: Aline Küppenheim, Eduardo Paxeco, Roberto Farías

Overview: In the city of Santiago, four characters struggle to reach their goals: a psychologist who wants to help other women and save their lives, a hairdresser who wants to buy a car, a musician who wants to play in a philharmonic orchestra, and a young woman who simply survives in the city, but each of them obtains something unexpected and different from what they wanted.
