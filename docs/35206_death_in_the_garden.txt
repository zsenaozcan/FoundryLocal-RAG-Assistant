Title: Death in the Garden (1956)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Luis Buñuel
Main Cast: Simone Signoret, Charles Vanel, Georges Marchal, Michel Piccoli, Tito Junco
Keywords: based on novel, revolution, coup d'etat, jungle, shootout, violence, diamond, gold mine, military, latin america

Overview: Amid a revolution in a South American mining outpost, a band of fugitives - a roguish adventurer, a local hooker, a priest, an aging diamond miner and his deaf-mute daughter - are forced to flee for their lives into the jungle. Starving, exhausted and stripped of their old identities, they wander desperately lured by one deceptive promise of salvation after another.
