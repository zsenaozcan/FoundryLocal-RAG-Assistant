Title: Brother 2 (2000)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Aleksey Balabanov
Main Cast: Sergei Bodrov Jr., Viktor Sukhorukov, Aleksandr Dyachenko, Kirill Pirogov, Gary Houston

Overview: Arriving in Moscow, Chechen veteran Danila (Sergei Bodrov Jr) meets Konstantin, an old friend who tells him that his twin brother has been forced into signing a crooked contract with a US ice hockey team. Soon after this meeting, Danila discovers Konstantin dead and he sets out to avenge his death; a journey that leads him to Chicago and a whole new experience.
