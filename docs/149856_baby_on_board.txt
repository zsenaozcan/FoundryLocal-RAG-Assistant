Title: Baby on Board (1992)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Francis Schaeffer
Main Cast: Judge Reinhold, Carol Kane

Overview: Maria (Carol Kane) is the wife of a murdered Mafia bookkeeper. With her baby in tow, she tracks the hitman to the airport, intending to shoot the man. A pickpocket causes the gun to discharge prematurely, and now it's Maria who is being pursued. She commandeers a cab driven by Ernie (Judge Reinhold), and the chase is on.
