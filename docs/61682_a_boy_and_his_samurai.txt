Title: A Boy and His Samurai (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Yoshihiro Nakamura
Main Cast: Ryo Nishikido, Rie Tomosaka, Fuku Suzuki, Hiroki Konno, Keisuke Horibe
Keywords: samurai, baking

Overview: A samurai from the Edo Period time travels to present day Japan where he meets a divorcee and her son.  Soon, he discovers a passion for making pastries.
