Title: Three Guys Named Mike (1951)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Charles Walters
Main Cast: Jane Wyman, Van Johnson, Howard Keel, Barry Sullivan, Phyllis Kirk
Keywords: stewardess, pilot, suitor, scientist, airline

Overview: A stewardess becomes romantically involved with an airline pilot, a college professor, and a successful businessman...all of whom are named Mike. When the three find out about each other, she has to decide which one she loves the most.
