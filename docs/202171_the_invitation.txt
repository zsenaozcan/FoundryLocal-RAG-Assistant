Title: The Invitation (2003)
Genres: Horror, Drama, Thriller
Director: Pat Bermel
Main Cast: Lance Henriksen, Christopher Shyer

Overview: The Invitation, a 2003 movie starring Lance Henriksen. When an author invites his friends to his home on a private island, the guests realize they've been poisoned at dinner. The only way to receive the antidote from their twisted host will be to confess to all the lies they've ever told.
