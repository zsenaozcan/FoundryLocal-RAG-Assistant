Title: Jeanne Dielman, 23, Quai du Commerce 1080 Bruxelles (1975)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Chantal Akerman
Main Cast: Delphine Seyrig, Jan Decorte, Henri Storck, Jacques Doniol-Valcroze, Yves Bical
Keywords: independent film, woman director

Overview: A lonely widowed housewife does her daily chores, takes care of her apartment where she lives with her teenage son, and turns the occasional trick to make ends meet. Slowly, her ritualized daily routines begin to fall apart.
