Title: Karlsson on the Roof (1974)
Genres: Family
Director: Olle Hellbom
Main Cast: Lars Söderdahl, Mats Wikström, Catrin Westerlund, Stig Ossian Ericson, Staffan Hallerstam
Keywords: stockholm, based on children's book

Overview: Svante is a young boy who lives with his family in a Stockholm apartment. One day he meets Karlsson, a chubby little man in the prime of his life, who can fly using a propeller on his back and who lives in his little house on the roof.
