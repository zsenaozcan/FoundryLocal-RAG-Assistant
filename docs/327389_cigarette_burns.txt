Title: Cigarette Burns (2005)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: John Carpenter
Main Cast: Norman Reedus, Colin Foo, Udo Kier, Christopher Redman, Chris Gauthier
Keywords: angel, collector, gun in mouth, wings

Overview: With a torrid past that haunts him, a movie theatre owner is hired to search for the only existing print of a film so notorious that its single screening caused the viewers to become homicidally insane.
