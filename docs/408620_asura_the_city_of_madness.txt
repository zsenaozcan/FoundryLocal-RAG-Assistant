Title: Asura: The City of Madness (2016)
Genres: Action, Crime
Director: Kim Sung-su
Main Cast: Jung Woo-sung, Hwang Jung-min, Ju Ji-hoon, Kwak Do-won, Jeong Man-sik
Keywords: politics, police, korea, prosecutor

Overview: A shady cop finds himself in over his head when he gets caught between Internal Affairs and the city’s corrupt mayor, in this scintillating crime drama from Korean maestro Kim Sung-soo (The Warrior).
