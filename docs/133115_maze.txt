Title: Maze (2000)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Rob Morrow
Main Cast: Rob Morrow, Laura Linney, Craig Sheffer, Rose Gregorio, Robert Hogan
Keywords: tourette syndrome, obsessive compulsive disorder

Overview: Artist Lyle Maze is reconciled to a life without romance thanks to his dual afflictions: Tourette's Syndrome and obsessive-compulsive disorder. But his life takes an unexpected turn when his friend Mike departs on a humanitarian mission, leaving behind his pregnant girlfriend Callie. She leans on Lyle for help, and before long he's Lamaze coach, pinch-hitting father-to-be and in love with Callie.
