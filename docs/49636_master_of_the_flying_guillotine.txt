Title: Master of the Flying Guillotine (1976)
Genres: Action, Foreign, Fantasy
Director: Jimmy Wang Yu
Main Cast: Jimmy Wang Yu, Kam Kong, Doris Lung Chun-Erh, Sham Chin-Bo, Lung Fei

Overview: A one-armed martial arts master is being stalked by an Imperial assassin, the master of two fighters (the Tibetan Lamas) who were killed in the previous film. When the One-Armed Boxer is invited to attend a martial arts tournament, his efforts to lay low are unsuccessful, and the assassin soon tracks him down with the help of his three subordinates competing in the tournament: a Thai boxer, a yoga master, and a kobojutsu user.
