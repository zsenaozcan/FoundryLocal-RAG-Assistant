Title: Necrosis (2009)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Jason Stephens
Main Cast: James Kyson, George Stults, Penny Drake, Tiffany, Danielle De Luca
Keywords: spirit

Overview: In 2009, six friends arrive at an isolated cabin to enjoy a long weekend in the snow. An epic snowstorm interrupts their vacation, trapping them on the mountain and resurrecting the haunting ghosts of the Donner Party. But, are they true 'entities' or is it simply 'cabin fever' that brings out their fears and darkness, causing friends to turn against each other as reality deteriorates around them.
