Title: Cold in July (2014)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Jim Mickle
Main Cast: Michael C. Hall, Don Johnson, Sam Shepard, Vinessa Shaw, Nick Damici
Keywords: based on novel, cemetery, self-defense, flashlight, revenge, murder, home invasion, intruder

Overview: How can a split-second decision change your life? While investigating noises in his house one balmy Texas night in 1989, Richard Dane puts a bullet in the brain of low-life burglar Freddy Russell. Although he’s hailed as a small-town hero, Dane soon finds himself fearing for his family’s safety when Freddy’s ex-con father, Ben, rolls into town, hell-bent on revenge.
