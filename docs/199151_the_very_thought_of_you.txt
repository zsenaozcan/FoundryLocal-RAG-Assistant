Title: The Very Thought of You (1944)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Delmer Daves
Main Cast: Dennis Morgan, Eleanor Parker, Dane Clark, Faye Emerson, Beulah Bondi
Keywords: expectant mother, pregnant wife, reconciliation

Overview: Army sergeants Dave and "Fixit" spend a three-day pass in Pasadena, where they meet Janet and Cora, two young women who work in a parachute factory.
