Title: Pyaar Impossible (2010)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance, Foreign
Director: Jugal Hansraj
Main Cast: Uday Chopra, Priyanka Chopra, Dino Morea, Rahul Vohra

Overview: romantic comedy in which a geek and the most sought-after college beauty are paired together by a twist of fate. And under all odds, an impossible love story begins.
