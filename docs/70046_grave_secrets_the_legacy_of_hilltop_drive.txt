Title: Grave Secrets: The Legacy of Hilltop Drive (1992)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, TV Movie
Director: John Patterson
Main Cast: Patty Duke, David Selby, Kiersten Warren, Blake Clark, Kelly Rowan

Overview: Jean and Shag Williams locate a newly built house and decide it's perfect for them to buy. One thing the developers forgot to tell them about was that it is built on a graveyard. Within days toilets start to flush by themselves and the garage door moves up and down by it's own accord. Will Jean and Shag realize that the place may be haunted by ghosts before it's too late.
