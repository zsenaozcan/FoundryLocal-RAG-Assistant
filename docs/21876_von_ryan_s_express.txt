Title: Von Ryan's Express (1965)
Genres: Action, Drama, History, Thriller, War
Director: Mark Robson
Main Cast: Frank Sinatra, Trevor Howard, Raffaella Carrà, Brad Dexter, Sergio Fantoni
Keywords: italian, prisoners of war, german, frontier, escape, pow, train

Overview: Von Ryan's Express stars Frank Sinatra as a POW colonel who leads a daring escape from Nazi Germany by taking over a freight train, but he has to win over the British soldiers he finds himself commanding.
