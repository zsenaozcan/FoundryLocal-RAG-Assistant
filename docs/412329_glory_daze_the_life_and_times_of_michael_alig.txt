Title: Glory Daze: The Life and Times of Michael Alig (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ramon Fernandez
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: new york city, subculture, illegal drugs, lgbt, murder investigation, clubs

Overview: A review of the wild New York City nightlife of the 90s. The cast of characters who made up the infamous Club Kids speak candidly about that era, culminating with Alig's release from incarceration.
