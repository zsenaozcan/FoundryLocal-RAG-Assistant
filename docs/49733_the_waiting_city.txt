Title: The Waiting City (2010)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Claire McCarthy
Main Cast: Radha Mitchell, Joel Edgerton, Samrat Chakrabarti, Isabel Lucas
Keywords: woman director

Overview: An outwardly happy Australian couple journey to Calcutta to collect their adopted baby, but on arrival find that the arrangements have yet to be finalized. Soon, the intoxicating mystic power of the Indian city pulls them in separate and unexpected directions, and the vulnerability of their marriage begins to reveal itself.
