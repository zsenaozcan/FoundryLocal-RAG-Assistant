Title: The New Year (2010)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Brett Haley
Main Cast: Trieste Kelly Dunn, Victor Aminger, Lance Brannon, Ryan Hunter
Keywords: feel bad comedy, life changes, sick parent

Overview: A young woman is forced to return to her hometown to take care of her ailing father and, in turn, finds herself living a life she never imagined for herself.
