Title: Mistrial (1996)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Action
Director: Heywood Gould
Main Cast: Bill Pullman, Robert Loggia, Blair Underwood, Leo Burmester, Roma Maffia

Overview: When a cop-killer goes free, a detective's search for personal justice veers dangerously out of control. Declaring a mistrial, he takes the accused, the judge and the jury hostage...and begins a trial of his own.
