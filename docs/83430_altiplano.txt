Title: Altiplano (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Peter Brosens
Main Cast: Jasmin Tabatabai, Magaly Solier, Olivier Gourmet, Arturo Anacarino Zarate, Malku Choquehuillca
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In the Andes, a Belgian doctor and his photojournalist wife become ensnared in a native tribe's struggle with a mining company.
