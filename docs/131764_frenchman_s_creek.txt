Title: Frenchman's Creek (1944)
Genres: Romance, Adventure
Director: Mitchell Leisen
Main Cast: Joan Fontaine, Arturo de Córdova, Basil Rathbone, Nigel Bruce, Cecil Kellaway
Keywords: pirate

Overview: An English lady falls madly in love with a French Pirate after he kidnaps her from her ancestral home on the coast of Cornwall.
