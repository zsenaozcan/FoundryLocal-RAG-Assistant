Title: The Enchanted World of Danny Kaye: The Emperor's New Clothes (1972)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Jules Bass
Main Cast: Danny Kaye, Cyril Ritchard, Imogene Coca, Allen Swift, Bob McFadden

Overview: This is one of the 'Animagical' titles from the children's film archive of Rankin/Bass. The story line is reminiscent of an earlier 1966 R/B Animagic film, 'The Daydreamer', both of which chronicles the fairy tales of Danish author, Hans Christian Andersen.
