Title: Olive Kitteridge (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lisa Cholodenko
Main Cast: Frances McDormand, Richard Jenkins, Bill Murray, Ann Dowd, Martha Wainwright
Keywords: miniseries, woman director

Overview: The story focuses on a middle-school math teacher Olive and her relationship with Henry which spans 25 years over the four parts. It is based on Elizabeth Strout’s Pulitzer Prize-winning novel.
