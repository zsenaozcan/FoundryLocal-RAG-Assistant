Title: Big Mommas: Like Father, Like Son (2011)
Genres: Crime, Comedy, Action
Director: John Whitesell
Main Cast: Martin Lawrence, Brandon T. Jackson, Portia Doubleday, Mari Morrow, Faizon Love
Keywords: undercover, fbi, sequel, comedy, disguise, fbi agent, impersonation, duringcreditsstinger

Overview: FBI agent Malcolm Turner and his 17-year-old son, Trent, go undercover at an all-girls performing arts school after Trent witnesses a murder. Posing as Big Momma and Charmaine, they must find the murderer before he finds them.
