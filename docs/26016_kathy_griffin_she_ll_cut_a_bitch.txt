Title: Kathy Griffin: She'll Cut a Bitch (2009)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Paul Miller
Main Cast: Kathy Griffin
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Two-time Emmy Award-winning and self proclaimed D-Lister Kathy Griffins latest comedy special takes you backstage in Hollywood: from the Emmys to the Grammys and even to the Malibu mansion of Cher! Appearing before a sold out crowd at the Arlene Schnitzer Concert Hall in Portland, Oregon, Griffin is in top form dishing about scandalous misbehaving celebrities and her own uproarious escapades in Tinsel Town.
