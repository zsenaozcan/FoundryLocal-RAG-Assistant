Title: Road to Yesterday (2015)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Ishaya Bako
Main Cast: Genevieve Nnaji, Oris Erhuero, Chioma Omeruah, Majid Michel, Ebele Okaro-Onyiuke
Keywords: death in family, estranged couple, road movie, flashback

Overview: Victoria returns to Nigeria from the UK to help estranged husband Izu cope with his uncle's death. Hoping to save his marriage, Izu brings Victoria on his trip to the funeral, during which they reflect on their courtship, secrets and regrets.
