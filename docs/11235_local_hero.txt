Title: Local Hero (1983)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Bill Forsyth
Main Cast: Burt Lancaster, Peter Riegert, Fulton Mackay, Denis Lawson, Norman Chancer
Keywords: scotland, small town, village life, petrol, village, millionaire

Overview: An American oil company sends a man to Scotland to buy up an entire village where they want to build a refinery. But things don't go as expected.
