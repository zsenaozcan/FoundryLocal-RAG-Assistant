Title: Wishmaster 3: Beyond the Gates of Hell (2001)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Chris Angel
Main Cast: John Novak, A.J. Cook, Tobias Mehler, Jason Connery, Emmanuelle Vaugier
Keywords: sequel, djinn, lifting person in air

Overview: The evil Djinn is back at again, this time wreaking havoc on the students of Illinois' Baxter University. His victim is a beautiful, innocent and studious teenage girl named Diana Collins who accidentally opened up the Djinn's tomb and released him.
