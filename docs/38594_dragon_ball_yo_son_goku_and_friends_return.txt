Title: Dragon Ball: Yo! Son Goku and Friends Return!! (2008)
Genres: Animation, Action, Adventure
Director: Yoshihiro Ueda
Main Cast: Masakazu Morita, Ryou Horikawa, Takeshi Kusao, Masako Nozawa, Masako Nozawa

Overview: A 35-minute Dragon Ball animated short film that premiered at the Jump Super Anime Tour in September 2008. It is the first animated Dragon Ball feature in twelve years, following the tenth anniversary film The Path to Power.
