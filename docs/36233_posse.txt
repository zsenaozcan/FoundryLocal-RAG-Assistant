Title: Posse (1993)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Western
Director: Mario Van Peebles
Main Cast: Mario Van Peebles, Stephen Baldwin, Tom Lister Jr., Big Daddy Kane, Billy Zane
Keywords: racial tension, street shootout, gun fight

Overview: A group of mostly black infantrymen return from the Spanish-American War with a cache of gold. They travel to the West where their leader searches for the men who lynched his father.
