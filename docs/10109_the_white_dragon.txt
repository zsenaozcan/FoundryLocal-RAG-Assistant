Title: The White Dragon (2004)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Drama, Fantasy, Romance
Director: Wilson Yip
Main Cast: Cecilia Cheung, Avi K. Garg, Lei Liu, Andy On, Francis Ng
Keywords: dragon, female martial artist

Overview: In this swordfighting comedy, a young noblewoman falls in love with a prince of the Imperial House. By accident, she acquires the martial arts skills of the White Dragon. New in her power, she learns that there are definite advantages in performing "good deeds" as the Little White Dragon.
