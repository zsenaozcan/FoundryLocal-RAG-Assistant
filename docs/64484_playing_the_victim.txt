Title: Playing the Victim (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Kirill Serebrennikov
Main Cast: Yuri Chursin, Vitaliy Khaev, Aleksandr Ilin, Anna Mikhalkova, Marat Basharov

Overview: A young man drops out of university and goes to the police. He's done nothing wrong he just wants a job. A particular job. Playing the victim in murder reconstructions. Maybe by getting close to death he can manage to cheat on his own.
