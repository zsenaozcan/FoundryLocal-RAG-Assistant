Title: Z.P.G. (1972)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Michael Campus
Main Cast: Oliver Reed, Geraldine Chaplin, Don Gordon, Diane Cilento, Eugene Blau
Keywords: pregnancy and birth, dystopia, overpopulation, pollution, distant future, robot, birth control

Overview: In the not too distant future, a very smoggy and overpopulated Earth government makes it illegal to have children for a generation. One couple, unsatisfied with their substitute robot baby, breaks the rules and gets in a lot of trouble. (Z.P.G. stands for Zero Population Growth.)
