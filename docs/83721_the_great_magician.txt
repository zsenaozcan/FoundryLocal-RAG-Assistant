Title: The Great Magician (2011)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: Derek Yee Tung-Sing
Main Cast: Tony Leung Chiu-Wai, Lau Ching-Wan, Zhou Xun, Paul Chun Pui, Wang Ya-Chao

Overview: In the early 1920’s, on the streets of Beijing, the nation’s most talented performers have gathered to show off their most spectacular skills. One day, a challenge is set: 50 silver dollars to whoever can reproduce the incredible magic trick “Eight Immortals’ Treat”. Zhang Xian appears amidst the waiting crowd and decides to try his hand at it.
