Title: Crystal Lake Memories: The Complete History of Friday the 13th (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Daniel Farrands
Main Cast: Corey Feldman, Robert Englund, Kane Hodder, Elizabeth Kaitan, Monica Keena
Keywords: jason voorhees

Overview: Taking inspiration from Peter M. Bracke's definitive book of the same name, this epic 7-hour documentary (by the same team behind Never Sleep Again: The Elm Street Legacy) dives into the making of all 12 Friday the 13th films, with all-new interviews from cast and crew.
