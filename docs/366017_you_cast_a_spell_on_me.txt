Title: You Cast A Spell On Me (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Romance
Director: Tosca Musk
Main Cast: Nikki DeLoach, Ryan McPartlin, Carolyn Hennesy, Briana Lane, Jonathan Patrick Moore
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Despite agreeing to an arranged marriage to a childhood friend, a successful businessman tries to track down a beautiful woman that he met at a Halloween party.
