Title: Lovesick (1983)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Marshall Brickman
Main Cast: Dudley Moore, Elizabeth McGovern, Alec Guinness, Christine Baranski, John Huston
Keywords: sigmund freud

Overview: Forget about accountants who want to fly, professors whose brains are being zapped by aliens and bored housewives passing the time. The one all-consuming problem Manhattan psychiatrist Saul Benjamin has is himself! Saul (Dudley Moore) and Chloe (Elizabeth McGovern), one of his patients, are in love. The situation could get him bounced from his profession but for lovers of romantic comedy, it's all delightful.
