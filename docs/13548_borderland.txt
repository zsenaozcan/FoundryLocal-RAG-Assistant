Title: Borderland (2007)
Genres: Crime, Horror, Thriller
Director: Zev Berman
Main Cast: Brian Presley, Rider Strong, Jake Muxworthy, Sean Astin, Beto Cuevas
Keywords: mexico, prostitute, tarot cards, machete, butcher, victim of murder, cult, insanity, blood splatter, based on true story

Overview: Three college students, Phil, Ed, and Henry take a road trip into Mexico for a week of drinking and carefree fun only to have Phil find himself a captive of a group of satanic Mexican drug smugglers who kill tourists and whom are looking for a group of new ones to prepare for a sacrifice.
