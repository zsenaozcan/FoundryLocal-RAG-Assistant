Title: Men Behind the Sun (1988)
Genres: War, History
Director: Mou Tun-fei
Main Cast: Hsu Gou, Tie Long Jin, Zhaohua Mei, Zhe Quan
Keywords: japan, china, japanese, war crimes, mass murder, biological weapon, world war ii, prisoners of war, concentration camp prisoner, human experimentation

Overview: The film is a graphic depiction of the war atrocities committed by the Japanese at Unit 731, the secret biological weapons experimentation unit of the Imperial Japanese Army during World War II. The film details the various cruel medical experiments Unit 731 inflicted upon the Chinese and Soviet prisoners at the tail-end of the war.
