Title: Dostana (2008)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Tarun Mansukhani
Main Cast: John Abraham, Priyanka Chopra, Bobby Deol, Abhishek Bachchan, Shilpa Shetty
Keywords: miami, bollywood

Overview: Kunal and Sameer, two straight guys who pretend to be a gay couple to secure an posh Miami apartment, but both of them fall for their gorgeous room-mate Neha, hilarity ensures as they strive to convince one and all they are gay! While secretly trying to win Neha's heart.
