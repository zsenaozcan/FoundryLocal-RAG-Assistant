Title: The Man Who Will Come (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Giorgio Diritti
Main Cast: Alba Rohrwacher, Greta Zuccheri Montanari, Maria Grazia Naldi, Eleonora Mazzoni, Maya Sansa

Overview: A group of Italian villagers struggle to survive during a tumultuous time in 1943, debating how much assistance to give the partisans with the impending arrival of the Germans. Based on True Events.
