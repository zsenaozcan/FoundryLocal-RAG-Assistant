Title: Youth of the Son (1952)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Masaki Kobayashi
Main Cast: Akira Ishihama, Chishu Ryu, Kuniko Miyake, Yôko Kosono, Ryuji Kita

Overview: The story of a father and two teenaged sons, and the rivalry between the two siblings as they begin to discover the attraction of girls.
