Title: Traded (2016)
Genres: Western
Director: Timothy Woodward Jr.
Main Cast: Kris Kristofferson, Trace Adkins, Michael Paré, Tom Sizemore, Martin Kove
Keywords: bar, rancher, sharpshooter

Overview: In the 1880s western "Traded", a father must leave his ranch for Dodge City to save his daughter from an old enemy, putting his reputation as the fastest draw in the west to the test.
