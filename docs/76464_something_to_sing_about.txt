Title: Something to Sing About (1937)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Victor Schertzinger
Main Cast: James Cagney, Evelyn Daw, William Frawley, Mona Barrie, Gene Lockhart
Keywords: musical, bandleader

Overview: James Cagney has a rare chance to show his song-and-dance-man roots in this low-budget tale of a New York bandleader struggling with a Hollywood studio boss.
