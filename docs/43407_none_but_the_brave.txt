Title: None But the Brave (1965)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Frank Sinatra
Main Cast: Tatsuya Mihashi, Takeshi Katô, Homare Suguro, Kenji Sahara, Masahiko Tanimura
Keywords: japanese, world war ii, u.s. army, stranded, jungle, infantry, pacific island

Overview: American and Japanese soldiers, stranded on a tiny Pacific island during World War II, must make a temporary truce and cooperate to survive various tribulations. Told through the eyes of the American and Japanese unit commanders, who must deal with an atmosphere of growing distrust and tension between their men.
