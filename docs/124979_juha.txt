Title: Juha (1937)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nyrki Tapiovaara
Main Cast: Hannes Närhi, Irma Seikkula, Walle Saikko, Tuulikki Paananen, Aino Haverinen
Keywords: runaway, womanizer, 18th century

Overview: Eastern Finland in 18th century. Farmer Juha has raised an orphan girl Marja and married her. Karelian trader Shemeikka visits the farm and starts to make advances to young and beautiful Marja, tempting her to run away with him.
