Title: Gun Hill (2014)
Genres: Action
Director: Reggie Rock Bythewood
Main Cast: Larenz Tate, Tawny Cypress, Emayatzy Corinealdi, Hisham Tawfiq, Michael Aronov

Overview: Gun Hill is a BET Premiere Cinema original film produced and directed by Reggie Rock Bythewood (Biker Boyz, Daddy's Girl, New York Undercover, Get on the Bus). The film stars Larenz Tate as Bird Stevens – a con who assumes the identity of his twin brother, Trane, a cop, after he's killed and goes on a search for his own redemption.
