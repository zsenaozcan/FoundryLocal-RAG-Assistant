Title: Go Goa Gone (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Krishna D.K.
Main Cast: Saif Ali Khan, Anand Tiwari, Vir Das, Pooja Gupta
Keywords: hindi, zombie, zombie apocalypse

Overview: A rave party off the coast of Goa, goes horrifyingly and hilariously wrong when the island is overrun with zombies.
