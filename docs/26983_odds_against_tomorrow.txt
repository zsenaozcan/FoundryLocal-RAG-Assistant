Title: Odds Against Tomorrow (1959)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Robert Wise
Main Cast: Harry Belafonte, Robert Ryan, Shelley Winters, Ed Begley, Gloria Grahame
Keywords: robbery, explosive, hold-up robbery, exploding building, suspense

Overview: An old-time crook plans a heist. When one of his two partners is found out to be a black man tensions flare.
