Title: Druid Peak (2014)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Marni Zelnick
Main Cast: Andrew Wilson, Spencer Treat Clark, Rachel Korine, Damian Young, Nathaniel Brown
Keywords: wyoming, wolf, teenage boy, woman director, yellowstone

Overview: A troubled teen is sent to live with his estranged father, a park ranger. During his time there, he develops an unusual affinity with and passion for the wolves in a local pack.
