Title: Raanjhanaa (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Anand L Rai
Main Cast: Dhanush, Sonam Kapoor, Abhay Deol, Swara Bhaskar, Mohammed Zeeshan Ayyub
Keywords: unrequited love, teenage love, inter religion relationship, tragic love story

Overview: A small-town boy needs to break through the class divide to gain acceptance from his childhood sweetheart who is in love with big city ideals.
