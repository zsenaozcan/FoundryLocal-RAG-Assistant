Title: Playdate (2012)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Andrew C. Erin
Main Cast: Marguerite Moreau, Richard Ruccolo, Abby Brammell, Natalie Alyn Lind

Overview: A new family moves into the house next door to the Valentine family. While they become fast friends, Emilie Valentine starts to notice bruises and odd behavior from the two sons in the new family. Are they victims of child abuse? Or are there twists and turns to finding out the real reason?
