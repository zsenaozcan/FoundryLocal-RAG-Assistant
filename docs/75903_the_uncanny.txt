Title: The Uncanny (1977)
Genres: Horror
Director: Denis Héroux
Main Cast: Peter Cushing, Ray Milland, Joan Greenwood, Susan Penhaligon, Donald Pleasence
Keywords: cat

Overview: Wilbur Gray, a horror writer, has stumbled upon a terrible secret, that cats are supernatural creatures who really call the shots. In a desperate attempt to get others to believe him, Wilbur spews three tales of feline horror.
