Title: Touching the Void (2003)
Genres: Documentary, Action, Adventure
Director: Kevin Macdonald
Main Cast: Brendan Mackey, Nicholas Aaron, Richard Hawking, Ollie Ryall, Joe Simpson
Keywords: wound, mountaineer, peru, sport, glacier, climbing, rescue, snow, cordillera huayhuash

Overview: A documentary based on the book of the same name by Joe Simpson about Simpson's and Simon Yates' disastrous and near-fatal attempt to climb 6,344m Siula Grande in the Cordillera Huayhuash in the Peruvian Andes in 1985.
