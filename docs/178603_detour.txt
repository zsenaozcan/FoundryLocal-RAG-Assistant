Title: Detour (2013)
Genres: Thriller, Mystery
Director: William Dickerson
Main Cast: Neil Hopkins, Brea Grant, John Forest, Ptolemy Slocum, Deborah Snyder
Keywords: survival

Overview: Trapped inside his car by a mudslide, smooth talking Jackson Alder suddenly finds himself in a situation he can't talk his way out of. With no hope of rescue, he must defy the odds; battling Mother Nature for his survival.
