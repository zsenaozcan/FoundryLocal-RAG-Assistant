Title: Planet of Dinosaurs (1977)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller, Action
Director: James K. Shea
Main Cast: Mary Appleseth, Derna Wylde, Max Thayer, Chuck Pennington, Charlotte Speer
Keywords: alien planet, dinosaur, planet, astronaut, space exploration

Overview: A space-ship gets lost and is forced to make an emergency landing on an unknown planet. The planet looks much like Earth, only with no trace of civilization. Soon the crew discovers that there are dinosaurs on the planet, and blood-thirsty buggers at that. The crew hopes to be found and rescued, but they have to struggle to survive until then
