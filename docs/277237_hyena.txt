Title: Hyena (2014)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Gerard Johnson
Main Cast: Peter Ferdinando, Stephen Graham, Neil Maskell, Elisa Lasowski, MyAnna Buring
Keywords: london england, bad cop

Overview: Good policing doesn't necessarily mean doing everything by the book. But as the business of crime in London turns to favour the Albanians and Turks, how does a "good" policeman survive?
