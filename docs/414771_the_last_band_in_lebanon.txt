Title: The Last Band in Lebanon (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ben Bachar
Main Cast: Ofer Shechter, Ori Laizerouvich, Ofer Hayoun, Israel Katorza
Keywords: israel, lebanon, war, army, comedy, band, israeli defense force, military

Overview: A comedy about three military band reservists who wake to discover that the Israeli army has withdrawn from Lebanon and left them behind.
