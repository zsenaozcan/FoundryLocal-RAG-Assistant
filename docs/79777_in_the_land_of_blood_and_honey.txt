Title: In the Land of Blood and Honey (2011)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: Angelina Jolie
Main Cast: Zana Marjanović, Goran Kostić, Branko Đurić, Dzana Pinjo, Miloš Timotijević
Keywords: muslim, sister sister relationship, sarajevo, bosnian war of 1992-1995, violence, united nations, woman director

Overview: During the Bosnian War, Danijel, a soldier fighting for the Serbs, re-encounters Ajla, a Bosnian who's now a captive in his camp he oversees. Their once promising connection has become ambiguous as their motives have changed.
