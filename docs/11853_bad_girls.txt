Title: Bad Girls (1994)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Drama, Western
Director: Jonathan Kaplan
Main Cast: Madeleine Stowe, Mary Stuart Masterson, Andie MacDowell, Drew Barrymore, James Russo
Keywords: female nudity, revenge, wild west, blonde woman, fast draw, tough  girl, gun fu, pistol duel, small western town

Overview: Four former harlots try to leave the wild west (Colorado, to be exact) and head north to make a better life for themselves. Unfortunately someone from Cody's past won't let it happen that easily.
