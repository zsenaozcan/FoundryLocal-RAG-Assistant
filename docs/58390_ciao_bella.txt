Title: Ciao Bella (2007)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Mani Maserrat Agah
Main Cast: Chanelle Lindell, Poyan Karimi, Mina Azarian, Arash Bolouri

Overview: Mustafa Moradi is aching to find a girlfriend. On the other end of the city, Linnea is desperately searching for a guy with style, courage and a burning passion.
