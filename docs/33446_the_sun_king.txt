Title: The Sun King (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Tomas Villum Jensen
Main Cast: Nikolaj Lie Kaas, Birthe Neumann, Thomas Bo Larsen, Niels Olsen, Lotte Andersen

Overview: While changing the pipes in the tanningbeds at Golden Sun, Tommy meets the owner. A middleaged former Miss Fyn called Susse. Slowly an unusual love affair begins. Tommy's two friends Ole &amp; Flemming give him tips on how to give Golden Sun some masculine appeal. Susse just happens to have a sleazy lawyer that does not appreciate the new look.
