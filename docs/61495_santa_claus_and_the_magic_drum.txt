Title: Santa Claus and the Magic Drum (1996)
Genres: Comedy, Animation, Family
Director: Mauri Kunnas
Main Cast: Esa Saario, Ulla Tapaninen, Henna Haverinen, Olli Parviainen, Aarre Karén
Keywords: santa claus, christmas

Overview: A few days before Christmas Eve the last sack of letters arrives to Santa. At the bottom is a letter that is a bit older than the others, it's a drawing. While the curious Santa tries to figure out what the drawing looks like, strange things begin to happen.
