Title: The 3 Penny Opera (1931)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Music
Director: G.W. Pabst
Main Cast: Rudolf Forster, Carola Neher, Reinhold Schünzel, Fritz Rasp, Valeska Gert
Keywords: corruption, prostitute, musical, victorian england, social satire, criminal underground, stage play, beggar clan, brechtian theatre

Overview: In London at the turn of the century, the bandit Mack the Knife marries Polly without the knowledge of her father, Peachum, the 'king of the beggars'.
