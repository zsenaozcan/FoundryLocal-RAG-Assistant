Title: Revenge of the Stepford Wives (1980)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, TV Movie, Science Fiction
Director: Robert Fuest
Main Cast: Don Johnson, Sharon Gless, Julie Kavner, Audra Lindley, Mason Adams
Keywords: small town, housewife, suburb, subservience

Overview: A TV reporter arrives in the quiet town of Stepford to launch an investigation into why the town has the lowest divorce and crime rates in America. However, she begins to notice some bizarre behavior in the women of the town, discovering that Stepford is not as clean cut as it seems.
