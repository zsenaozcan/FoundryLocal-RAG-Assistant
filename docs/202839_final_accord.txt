Title: Final Accord (1936)
Genres: Music, Drama
Director: Douglas Sirk
Main Cast: Willy Birgel, Lil Dagover, Maria Koppenhöfer, Theodor Loos, Peter Bosse

Overview: A young, impoverished German woman named Hanna gives her infant up for adoption and emigrates to American to live with her husband. When her husband commits suicide, Hanna returns to Germany and works her way into becoming the live-in maid and nurse to her child being raised by an orchestra conductor and his wife.
