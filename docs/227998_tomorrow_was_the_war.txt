Title: Tomorrow Was the War (1987)
Genres: Family, Drama, War
Director: Yuriy Kara
Main Cast: Sergey Nikonenko, Nina Ruslanova, Natalya Negoda, Vera Alentova, Vladimir Zamanskiy

Overview: A drama a life in a small Russian provincial town in 1940 - one year before Germany invaded the Soviet Union. Two school girls for the first time in their lives discover truth about Stalin repressions against innocent people. Based on a novel by Boris Vasiliev.
