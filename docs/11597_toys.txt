Title: Toys (1992)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Barry Levinson
Main Cast: Robin Williams, Michael Gambon, Joan Cusack, Robin Wright, Jack Warden
Keywords: brother brother relationship, loss of brother, loss of father, toy, company, uncle, toy shop, technical toy, inheritance
