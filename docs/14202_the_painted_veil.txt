Title: The Painted Veil (2006)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: John Curran
Main Cast: Edward Norton, Juliet Howland, Naomi Watts, Ian Renwick, Liev Schreiber
Keywords: china, cholera, foreign aid, epidemic, loveless marriage

Overview: A British medical doctor fights a cholera outbreak in a small Chinese village, while also being trapped at home in a loveless marriage to an unfaithful wife.
