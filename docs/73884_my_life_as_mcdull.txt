Title: My Life as McDull (2001)
Genres: Animation, Drama, Comedy
Director: Toe Yuen
Main Cast: Lee Chun-Wai, Jan Lamb, Sandra Ng Kwun-Yu, Anthony Wong, Dejay Choi

Overview: McDull is not the brightest kid on the block, but he continuously tries to do his best to please his mother. Still it seems he may not be destined for great things like she wishes, but McDull strives to try anyway.
