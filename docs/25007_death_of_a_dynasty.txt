Title: Death of a Dynasty (2005)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Damon Dash
Main Cast: Ebon Moss-Bachrach, Capone Lee, Loon, Robert Stapleton, Rashida Jones

Overview: A story which centers around Dave Katz, a journalist at a hip-hop magazine who is sent on assignment to uncover the often comedic underworld of Roc-a-Fella Records run by Damon Dash and Jay-Z.
