Title: Another Earth (2011)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Mike Cahill
Main Cast: William Mapother, Brit Marling, Matthew-Lee Erlbach, DJ Flava, Meggan Lennon
Keywords: earth, tragedy, janitor, planet, duplicate, cosmology, ego, solar system
