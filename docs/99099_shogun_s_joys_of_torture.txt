Title: Shogun's Joys of Torture (1968)
Genres: Horror
Director: Teruo Ishii
Main Cast: Yuki Kagawa, Reiko Mikasa, Miki Obana, Masumi Tachibana, Fumio Watanabe

Overview: One of the most famous of cult director Ishii Teruo's films. A young magistrate recalls  three tales of heinous crimes committed by women and brutal punished that ensued.  Not for the faint of heart.
