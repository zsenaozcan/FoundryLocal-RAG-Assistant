Title: The Old Mill (1937)
Genres: Animation
Director: Wilfred Jackson
Main Cast: Louise Myers, Jean MacMurray, Jerry Philips, Marie Arbuckle, Marta Nelson
Keywords: mill, disney short, silly symphony

Overview: We see the various birds, mice, and bats that have moved into an old windmill, followed by the frogs, crickets, and fireflies making their music in an adjacent pond. Then a storm comes, shaking loose parts in mill and threatening everything we've seen.
