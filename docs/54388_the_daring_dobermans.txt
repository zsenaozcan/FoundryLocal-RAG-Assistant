Title: The Daring Dobermans (1973)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Byron Chudnow
Main Cast: Charles Robinson, Tim Considine, Joan Caulfield, David Moses, Claudio Martinez
Keywords: dog, doberman

Overview: In this sequel to The Doberman Gang, Three men track down a pack of dobermans and along with a young Native American boy, train the dobermans to rob the campaign funds of a politician.
