Title: Dirty Deeds (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: David Kendall
Main Cast: Milo Ventimiglia, Lacey Chabert, Zoe Saldana, Arielle Kebbel, Charles Durning
Keywords: american football, nudity, cheerleader, challenge, high school, party, prank, teacher, bully
