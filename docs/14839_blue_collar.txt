Title: Blue Collar (1978)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Paul Schrader
Main Cast: Richard Pryor, Harvey Keitel, Yaphet Kotto, Ed Begley Jr., Harry Bellaver
Keywords: robbery, bar, blackmail, drinking, union, blue collar worker, assembly line

Overview: Fed up with mistreatment at the hands of both management and union brass, and coupled with financial hardships on each man's end, three auto assembly line workers hatch a plan to rob a safe at union headquarters.
