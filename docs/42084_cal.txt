Title: Cal (1984)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Romance
Director: Pat O'Connor
Main Cast: John Lynch, Helen Mirren, Donal McCann, Ray McAnally, John Kavanagh

Overview: Cal, a young man on the fringes of the IRA, falls in love with Marcella, a Catholic woman whose husband, a Protestant policeman, was killed one year earlier by the IRA.
