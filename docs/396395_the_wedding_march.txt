Title: The Wedding March (2016)
Genres: Romance, TV Movie
Director: Neill Fearnley
Main Cast: Jack Wagner, Josie Bissett, Emily Tennant, Sarah Grey, Cameron Bancroft

Overview: When former college sweethearts Olivia Parsons and Mick Turner are unexpectedly reunited the weekend of Olivia's wedding, the former couple initially locks horns but as the events of the weekend unfold, Olivia and Mick find old feelings rising to the surface. Will the bride-to-be walk down the aisle as planned or will she decide to give her first love a second chance?
