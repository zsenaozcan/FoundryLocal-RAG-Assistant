Title: Sadie McKee (1934)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Clarence Brown
Main Cast: Joan Crawford, Gene Raymond, Franchot Tone, Edward Arnold, Esther Ralston
Keywords: cook, maid, alcoholic, millionaire

Overview: A maid (Joan Crawford) has romances with a two-timer (Gene Raymond), a boozing millionaire and the master (Franchot Tone) of the house.
