Title: The Neon Bible (1995)
Genres: Drama
Director: Terence Davies
Main Cast: Jacob Tierney, Drake Bell, Gena Rowlands, Diana Scarwid, Denis Leary
Keywords: southern usa, based on novel, singer, south

Overview: While on a train, a teenage boy thinks about his life and the flamboyant aunt whose friendship acted as an emotional shield from his troubled family. This film evokes the haunting quality of memory while creating a heartfelt portrait of a boy's life in a rural 1940s Southern town.
