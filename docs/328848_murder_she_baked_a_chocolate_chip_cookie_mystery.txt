Title: Murder, She Baked: A Chocolate Chip Cookie Mystery (2015)
Genres: TV Movie, Mystery
Director: Mark Jean
Main Cast: Alison Sweeney, Cameron Mathison, Barbara Niven, Gabriel Hogan, Lisa Durupt

Overview: Based on the New York Times bestselling book, Chocolate Chip Cookie Murder by Joanne Fluke, the film will take viewers on a mouthwatering mystery that centers on Hannah Swensen, shop owner of the Cookie Jar where much of the town's gossip percolates along with the strong coffee. But when a mysterious murder occurs, Hannah turns into a culinary detective and finds herself trying to solve the crime while getting caught in an unexpected romantic mystery of her own.
