Title: Fitzwilly (1967)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Delbert Mann
Main Cast: Dick Van Dyke, Barbara Feldon, John McGiver, Edith Evans, Harry Townes
Keywords: butler, wealthy, secretary, philanthropist

Overview: When Miss Vicki's father dies, she becomes the world's greatest philanthropist. Unfortunately, she is flat broke! Her loyal butler, Claude Fitzwilliam, leads the household staff to rob from various businesses by charging goods to various wealthy people and misdirecting the shipments, all to keep Miss Vicki's standard of living.
