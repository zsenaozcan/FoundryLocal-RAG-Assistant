Title: Running from Crazy (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Barbara Kopple
Main Cast: Mariel Hemingway, Dree Hemingway, Margaux Hemingway, Langley Hemingway
Keywords: suicide, depression, woman director, bipolar, ernest hemingway, bipolar disorder

Overview: 'Running from Crazy' is a documentary examining the personal journey of model and actress Mariel Hemingway, the granddaughter of Ernest Hemingway, as she strives for a greater understanding of her family history of suicide and mental illness.
