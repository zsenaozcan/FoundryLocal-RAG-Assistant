Title: Belly 2: Millionaire Boyz Club (2008)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Ivan Frank
Main Cast: Jayceon Terrell Taylor, Taye Hansberry, Ski Carr, Michael Kenneth Williams, Adam Ambruso

Overview: Born into a life of crime, G has just spent 8 years in the pen. Now out on the streets he's back to his old ways wondering if a con can ever really straighten out. Things become more complex when G hooks up with Alexis who makes her living upholding the law.
