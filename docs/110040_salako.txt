Title: Salako (1974)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Atıf Yılmaz
Main Cast: Kemal Sunal, Meral Zeren, İhsan Yüce, Oktar Durukan, Talat Gözbak

Overview: In this romantic comedy Kemal Sunal plays the title role of Salakoglan("the fool" in English). He is the village idiot in which the boss (or Aga) of the village has a very beautiful daughter, Emine... whom Salako falls desperately in love with.
