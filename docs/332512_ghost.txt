Title: Ghost (2015)
Genres: Family, Comedy
Director: Aleksandr Voytinskiy
Main Cast: Fyodor Bondarchuk, Yan Tsapnik, Anna Antonova, Igor Ugolnikov, Semyon Treskunov

Overview: An aircraft designer becomes a ghost after a sudden death. Now he needs to engage a help of seven-grader in order to finish all his unaccomplished tasks.
