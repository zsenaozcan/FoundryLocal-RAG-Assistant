Title: Modigliani of Montparnasse (1958)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Jacques Becker
Main Cast: Gérard Philipe, Lilli Palmer, Lea Padovani, Gérard Séty, Lino Ventura
Keywords: painter, based on novel, depression, artist, biography, montparnasse, alcoholic, drunkenness

Overview: Biographic film chronicling the last year of the life of the Italian painter Amedeo Modigliani, 1919, who falls in love with a girl from a wealthy family. Her parents are against this relationship and stop financial help. Modigliani worked and died in abject poverty in the Montparnasse Quarter of Paris, France.
