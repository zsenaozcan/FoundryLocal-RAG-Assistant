Title: Todd Barry: The Crowd Work Tour (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lance Bangs
Main Cast: Todd Barry
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: A Todd Barry show consists of two things: amazing jokes and amazing crowd work. In September 2013, he went on a tour without the amazing jokes and did entire shows of riffing and bantering with the audience. Filmed in seven west coast cities, “Todd Barry: The Crowd Work Tour” was directed by Lance Bangs and produced by Louis CK.
