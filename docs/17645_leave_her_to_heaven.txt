Title: Leave Her to Heaven (1945)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: John M. Stahl
Main Cast: Gene Tierney, Cornel Wilde, Jeanne Crain, Vincent Price, Mary Philips
Keywords: boat, femme fatale, murder, film noir

Overview: A young novelist, Richard Harland, meets beautiful Ellen Berent on a train where they fall in love and are soon married. When tragedies take first his handicapped young brother, then his unborn son from him, Harland gradually realises that his wife's insane jealousy may be the cause of the tragedies in his life. Yet another shock awaits them all, as Ellen's emotions become uncontrollable.
