Title: Fanny & Alexander (1982)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Mystery
Director: Ingmar Bergman
Main Cast: Allan Edwall, Ewa Fröling, Erland Josephson, Bertil Guve, Pernilla Allwin
Keywords: brother sister relationship, sweden, loss of child, loss of father, bishop, dying and death, funeral, child prodigy

Overview: Through the eyes of ten-year-old Alexander, we witness the delights and conflicts of the Ekdahl family, a sprawling bourgeois clan in turn-of-the-twentieth-century Sweden.
