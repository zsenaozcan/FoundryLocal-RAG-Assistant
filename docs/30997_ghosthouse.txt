Title: Ghosthouse (1988)
Genres: Horror
Director: Umberto Lenzi
Main Cast: Lara Wendel, Greg Rhodes, Mary Sellers, Ron Houck, Martin Jay
Keywords: haunted house, possessed, severed head, undertaker, doll, ghost

Overview: A group of unlikely companions receive a radio call leading to a deserted house with a grisly past. After exploring the house, the group makes the foolish decision to split up, leading to a trail of death and destruction as the house unleashes its deadly past. Along the way expect severed heads, exploding light bulbs, demonic clown dolls, and creepy children.
