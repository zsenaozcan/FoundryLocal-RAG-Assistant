Title: Silent Retreat (2013)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Tricia Lee
Main Cast: Chelsea Jenish, Sofia Banzhaf, Robert Nolan, Jen Pogue, Matthew Romantini
Keywords: woods, brainwashing, creature, gender roles, silence, woman director, retreat, canuxploitation

Overview: Janey arrives at a silent retreat in the middle of the woods for rehabilitation, only to discover that the men who run it aren't afraid to show her what lurks beyond the trees.
