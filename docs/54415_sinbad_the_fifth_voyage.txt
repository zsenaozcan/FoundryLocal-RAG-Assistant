Title: Sinbad: The Fifth Voyage (2014)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: Shahin Sean Solimon
Main Cast: Patrick Stewart, Said Faraj, Lorna Raver, Isaac C. Singleton Jr., Shahin Sean Solimon

Overview: When the Sultan's first born is taken by an evil sorcerer, Sinbad is tasked with traveling to a desert of magic and creatures to save her.
