Title: On Approval (1944)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Clive Brook
Main Cast: Clive Brook, Beatrice Lillie, Googie Withers, Roland Culver, O.B. Clarence
Keywords: scotland, duke, victorian england, farce, heiress, trial marriage

Overview: Two wealthy Victorian widows are courted tentatively by two impoverished British aristocrats. When one of the dowagers suggests that her beau go away with her for a month to see if they are compatible, the fireworks begin.
