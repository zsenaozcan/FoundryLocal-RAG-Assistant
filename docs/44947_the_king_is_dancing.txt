Title: The King Is Dancing (2000)
Genres: Drama, History, Music
Director: Gérard Corbiau
Main Cast: Benoît Magimel, Boris Terral, Tchéky Karyo, Colette Emmanuelle, Cécile Bois

Overview: Louis XIV, the French sun-king has two passions, establishing absolute rule over the realm -after decades of religious/civil wars- by divine right and artistic brilliancy as a dancer
