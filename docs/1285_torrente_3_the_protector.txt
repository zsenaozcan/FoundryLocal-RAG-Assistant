Title: Torrente 3 The Protector (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Santiago Segura
Main Cast: Santiago Segura, Yvonne Sciò, Javier Gutiérrez, José Mota, Tony Leblanc
Keywords: detective

Overview: A popular and beautiful politician plans to expose the evil-doings of a multinational corporation. Hoping to silence her, the corporation's top executives decide to hire the most incompetent detective they can find to act as her bodyguard.
