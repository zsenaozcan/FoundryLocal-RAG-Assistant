Title: Chato's Land (1972)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Western
Director: Michael Winner
Main Cast: Charles Bronson, Jack Palance, James Whitmore, Simon Oakland, Ralph Waite
Keywords: rape, posse, dead horse, canteen, carcass

Overview: A posse pursues Pardon Chato (Charles Bronson) a mestizo indian after he killed a US marshal in self-defense. As they get deeper into Indian territory, just who is hunting who.
