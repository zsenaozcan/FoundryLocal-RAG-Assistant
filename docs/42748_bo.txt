Title: Bo (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hans Herbots
Main Cast: Ella-June Henrard, Laura Ballyn, Peter Bastiaensen, Mia Boels, Pierre Callens
Keywords: antwerp, escort girl

Overview: Fifteen year old Deborah meets Jennifer who steers her into a career as an escort. Under the alias Bo she begins a new life that she cannot handle and starts a downward spiral that only she can end.
