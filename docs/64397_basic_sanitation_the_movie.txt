Title: Basic Sanitation, the Movie (2007)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jorge Furtado
Main Cast: Fernanda Torres, Wagner Moura, Camila Pitanga, Bruno Garcia, Janaína Kremer
Keywords: brazilian, comedy

Overview: A small community of descendants of Italian immigrants in the interior of the state of Rio Grande do Sul resorts to making a video to try to solve the problems of basic sanitation that plague their village.
