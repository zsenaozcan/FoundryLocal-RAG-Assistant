Title: Much Loved (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nabil Ayouch
Main Cast: Loubna Abidar, Asmaa Lazrak, Halima Karaouane, Sara Elmhamdi-Elalaoui, Abdellah Didane

Overview: A social drama about four marginalized prostitutes in Marrakech and their complex relations with their families and society at large.
