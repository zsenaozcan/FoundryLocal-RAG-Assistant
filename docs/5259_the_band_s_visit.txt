Title: The Band's Visit (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Eran Kolirin
Main Cast: Sasson Gabai, Ronit Elkabetz, Khalifa Natour, Saleh Bakri, Shlomi Avraham
Keywords: heterosexual, ladykiller, empowerment, strong woman, orchestra, music lover, police band, independent film

Overview: Once-not long ago-a small Egyptian police band arrived in Israel. Not many remember this... It wasn't that important. A band comprised of members of the Egyptian police force head to Israel to play at the inaugural ceremony of an Arab arts center, only to find themselves lost in the wrong town.
