Title: Under the Shadow (2016)
Genres: Horror
Director: Babak Anvari
Main Cast: Narges Rashidi, Avin Manshadi, Bobby Naderi, Ray Haratian, Hamid Djavadan
Keywords: mother, daughter, evil, 1980s, tehran

Overview: As a mother and daughter struggle to cope with the terrors of the post-revolution, war torn Tehran of the 80s, a mysterious evil begins to haunt their home.
