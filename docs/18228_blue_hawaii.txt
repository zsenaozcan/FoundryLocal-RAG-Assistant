Title: Blue Hawaii (1961)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Norman Taurog
Main Cast: Elvis Presley, Joan Blackman, Angela Lansbury, Nancy Walters, Roland Winters
Keywords: hawaii, musical, romantic comedy, tiki culture

Overview: Chad Gates has just gotten out of the Army, and is happy to be back in Hawaii with his surf-board, his beach buddies, and his girlfriend.
