Title: City Limits (1984)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Aaron Lipstadt
Main Cast: John Stockwell, Darrell Larson, Rae Dawn Chong, James Earl Jones, Kim Cattrall
Keywords: future, gang war, post-apocalyptic, biker, rivalry, rampage, gang, los angeles, motorcycle, post nuclear

Overview: A series of teenage gangs struggle against each other in a not-so-distant future. Eventually they united against an evil corporation, as represented by evil CEO Robby Benson who wants to control everything.
