Title: Rufus 2 (2017)
Genres: Family, Fantasy, Comedy
Director: Savage Steve Holland
Main Cast: Jace Norman, Davis Cleveland, Haley Tju, Jade Pettyjohn
Keywords: children, anthropomorphism

Overview: Rufus is a dog but turns into a human with a pendant. He meets a girl named Kat who asks him to go out. Kat is a cat who can also turn into a human as well.
