Title: The Guvnors (2014)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Gabe Turner
Main Cast: Harley Sylvester, Doug Allen, Jay Simpson, Vas Blackwood, Lee Nicholas Harris
Keywords: london england, violence, clans, suburban, two generation

Overview: The Guvnors is a violent thriller set amongst the clans and firms of South East London, bringing two generations together in brutal conflict.
