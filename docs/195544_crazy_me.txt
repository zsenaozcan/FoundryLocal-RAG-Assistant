Title: Crazy Me (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Fausto Brizzi
Main Cast: Francesco Mandelli, Loretta Goggi, Valeria Bilello, Chiara Francini, Marina Rocco

Overview: Andrea is having a bad luck with girls. Maybe because he's only man in his big family which include mother, three sisters, grandma, nurse and a dog which is of course female also.
