Title: Footprints (1975)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Luigi Bazzoni
Main Cast: Florinda Bolkan, Peter McEnery, Nicoletta Elmi, Klaus Kinski, Lila Kedrova
Keywords: giallo, nonlinear timeline

Overview: Alice, a young translator, finds the real world slowly merging with her recurring nightmares as she tries to solve the puzzle of her recent memory loss.  A postcard leads her to the island of Garma where the locals seems to know her.  Is she who she thinks she is?  And what significance does her dream of an astronaut abandoned on the moon have?  A haunting, beautiful and unconventional giallo.
