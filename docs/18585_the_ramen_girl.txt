Title: The Ramen Girl (2008)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Robert Allan Ackerman
Main Cast: Brittany Murphy, Tammy Blanchard, Gabriel Mann, Toshiyuki Nishida, Sohee Park

Overview: An American woman is stranded in Tokyo after breaking up with her boyfriend. Searching for direction in life, she trains to be a râmen chef under a tyrannical Japanese master.
