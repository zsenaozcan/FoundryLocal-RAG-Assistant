Title: Prague (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ole Christian Madsen
Main Cast: Mads Mikkelsen, Milan Duchek, Stine Stengade, Jana Plodková, Bořivoj Navrátil
Keywords: father son relationship, prague, marriage, love, hospital

Overview: Christoffer and Maja's trip to Prague to bring back Chistoffer's deceased father, evolves into the story of a brake-up. With the dead father lurking in the background, secrets gradually emerge threatening to destroy their marriage.
