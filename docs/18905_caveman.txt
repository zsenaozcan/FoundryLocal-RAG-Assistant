Title: Caveman (1981)
Genres: Comedy, Adventure
Director: Carl Gottlieb
Main Cast: Ringo Starr, Dennis Quaid, Shelley Long, Jack Gilford, Barbara Bach
Keywords: stone age, slapstick, cavemen, prehistoric adventure, prehistoric times, prehistoric man

Disgraced and cast out of his tribe for lusting after Lana, the mate of tribe's head muscle man, Atouk stumbles along gathering other misfits and learning a bit about the world outside of his cave.
