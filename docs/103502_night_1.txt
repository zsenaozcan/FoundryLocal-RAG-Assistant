Title: Night #1 (2011)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Anne Émond
Main Cast: Dimitri Storoge, Catherine de Léan, Véronique Rebizov, Raphaël Boulanger, Mika Pluviose
Keywords: one-night stand, quebec, conversation, slacker, woman director

Overview: Clara and Nikolai meet at a sweat-soaked rave and end their night at his apartment. The first part of the film is an erotic and candid portrait of their lovemaking.  When Clara tries to sneak out without saying goodbye, this typical hookup takes an unexpected turn...
