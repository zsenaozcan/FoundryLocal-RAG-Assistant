Title: Blackbird (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Adam Rapp
Main Cast: Gillian Jacobs, Paul Sparks, Danny Hoch, Michael Shannon, Annie Parisse

Overview: In 1990s New York City, teen runaway Froggy is caught in the throes of heroin addiction as she falls in love with a fellow junky, war veteran Baylis. The couple finds comfort in each other, yet they are already far too deep in a wild downward spiral.
