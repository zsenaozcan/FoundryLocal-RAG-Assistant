Title: Dig! (2004)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Ondi Timoner
Main Cast: Anton Newcombe, Courtney Taylor-Taylor, Joel Gion, Matt Hollywood, Peter Holmström
Keywords: bus, junkie, heroin, career, musical, romantic rivalry, rock, flush, woman director

Overview: A documentary on the once promising American rock bands The Brian Jonestown Massacre and The Dandy Warhols. The friendship between respective founders, Anton Newcombe and Courtney Taylor, escalated into bitter rivalry as the Dandy Warhols garnered major international success while the Brian Jonestown Massacre imploded in a haze of drugs.
