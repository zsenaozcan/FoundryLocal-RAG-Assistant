Title: Chopper Chicks in Zombietown (1989)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Dan Hoskins
Main Cast: Jamie Rose, Catherine Carlen, Lycia Naff, Vicki Frederick, Kristina Loggia
Keywords: femme fatale, zombie, motorcycle gang, girls with guns

Overview: Riding around on their motorbikes, a gang of tough women bikers are the only thing that stands between a crowd of zombies, which have been accidentally let out of their secure cave, and those still alive in the town.
