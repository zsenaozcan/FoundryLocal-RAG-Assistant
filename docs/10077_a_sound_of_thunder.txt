Title: A Sound of Thunder (2005)
Genres: Thriller, Science Fiction, Adventure, Action
Director: Peter Hyams
Main Cast: Heike Makatsch, Armin Rohde, David Oyelowo, Wilfried Hochholdinger, Edward Burns
Keywords: dying and death, time travel, romance, dinosaur

Overview: When a hunter sent back to the prehistoric era runs off the path he must not leave, he causes a chain reaction that alters history in disastrous ways.
