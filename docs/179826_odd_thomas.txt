Title: Odd Thomas (2013)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Stephen Sommers
Main Cast: Anton Yelchin, Addison Timlin, Willem Dafoe, Gugu Mbatha-Raw, Nico Tortorella
Keywords: small town, supernatural, horror, romance, comedy, supernatural ability

Overview: In a California desert town, a short-order cook with clairvoyant abilities encounters a mysterious man with a link to dark, threatening forces.
