Title: I Motorizzati (1962)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Camillo Mastrocinque
Main Cast: Ugo Tognazzi, Nino Manfredi, José Luis López Vázquez, Walter Chiari, Mario Brega

Overview: A guy is about to become the millionth citizen to buy a car in Rome. Frightened, he decides to remain pedestrian, and recalls several stories, with new car owners as protagonists. Episodic comedy.
