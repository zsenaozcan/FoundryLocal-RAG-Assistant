Title: The Seventh Continent (1989)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Haneke
Main Cast: Birgit Doll, Dieter Berner, Leni Tanzer, Silvia Fenz, Robert Dietl
Keywords: continents

Overview: Chronicles three years of a middle class family seemingly caught up in their daily routines, only troubled by minor incidents. Behind their apparent calm and repetitive existence however, they are actually planning something sinister.
