Title: Fool Circle (2014)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Vincent Mariette
Main Cast: Ludivine Sagnier, Laurent Lafitte, Vincent Macaigne, Noémie Lvovsky, Dominique Reymond

Overview: Two, very different, brothers are reunited for their father's funeral. But, when they arrive at the crematorium nobody is there, not even the corpse, except for Chloe who introduces herself as their sister.
