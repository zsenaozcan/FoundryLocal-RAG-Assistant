Title: Vanishing Waves (2012)
Genres: Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Kristina Buozyte
Main Cast: Jurga Jutaite, Marius Jampolskis, Brice Fournier, Šarūnas Bartas, Arnoldas Eisimantas
Keywords: experiment, woman director

Overview: Lucas is a scientist who works near the European experimental project that focus on advanced ‘human neuron research’. The project is to transfer neuron information from one human mind to another. Lukas is chosen to connect his mind with a comatose woman he named Aurora.
