Title: Rob the Mob (2014)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Raymond De Felitta
Main Cast: Michael Pitt, Nina Arianda, Andy García, Ray Romano, Griffin Dunne
Keywords: runaway, fbi, love, mobster, couple

Overview: The true-life story of a crazy-in-love Queens couple who robbed a series of mafia social clubs and got away with it… for a while… until they stumble upon a score bigger than they ever planned and become targets of both the mob and the FBI.
