Title: Triple Dog (2010)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Pascal Franchot 
Main Cast: Scout Taylor-Compton, Britt Robertson, Alexia Fast, Janel Parrish, Emily Tennant
Keywords: suspense, independent film

Overview: On the night of a sleepover, a group of teenage girls venture out in a competitive game of challenging dares. As the antics escalate, and the dares become more extreme, the girls unravel the truth behind a former student's rumored suicide.
