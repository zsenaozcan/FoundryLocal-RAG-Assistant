Title: History of Cinema in Popielawy (1998)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Jan Jakub Kolski
Main Cast: Krzysztof Majchrzak, Bartosz Opania, Grażyna Błęcka-Kolska, Mariusz Saniternik, Maria Gładkowska

Overview: The story is narrated by ten-year old Staszek, who writes in his diary about his school-friend Jozef. Jozef comes from a family of blacksmiths, all of whom bear the name Jozef. Since he is the sixth consecutive son to be called Jozef, he is nicknamed Szustek (meaning "sixth one").
