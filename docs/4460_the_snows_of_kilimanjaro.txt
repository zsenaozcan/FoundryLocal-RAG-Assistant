Title: The Snows of Kilimanjaro (1952)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Romance
Director: Henry King
Main Cast: Gregory Peck, Susan Hayward, Ava Gardner, Hildegard Knef, Leo G. Carroll
Keywords: africa, ex-lover, safari, adventure, fever, countess

Overview: Writer Harry Street reflects on his life as he lies dying from an infection while on safari in the shadow of Mount Kilimanjaro.
