Title: Summer and Smoke (1961)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Peter Glenville
Main Cast: Laurence Harvey, Geraldine Page, Rita Moreno, John McIntire, Una Merkel

Overview: In a small Mississippi town in 1916, an eccentric spinster battles her romantic yearnings for the randy boy next door. A 1961 film, adapted from a Tennessee Williams play, starring Geraldine Page, Laurence Harvey, John McIntire, Una Merkel and Rita Moreno.
