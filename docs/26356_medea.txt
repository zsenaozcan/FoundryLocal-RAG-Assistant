Title: Medea (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Pier Paolo Pasolini
Main Cast: Maria Callas, Giuseppe Gentile, Laurent Terzieff, Margareth Clémenti, Massimo Girotti
Keywords: greek mythology, ritual, marriage crisis, medea, revenge, infanticide

Overview: Based on the plot of Euripides' Medea. Medea centers on the barbarian protagonist as she finds her position in the Greek world threatened, and the revenge she takes against her husband Jason who has betrayed her for another woman.
