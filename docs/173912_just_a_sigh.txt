Title: Just a Sigh (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Jérôme Bonnell
Main Cast: Emmanuelle Devos, Gabriel Byrne, Gilles Privat, Aurélia Petit, Laurent Capelluto
Keywords: relationship

Overview: In the short break between performances in Calais, stage actress Alix makes a quick escape to Paris. On the train she meets a mysterious English stranger and, for the most fleeting of afternoons, imagines what the future could hold down a different road.
