Title: 36 Hours (1964)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: George Seaton
Main Cast: James Garner, Eva Marie Saint, Rod Taylor, Werner Peters, John Banner
Keywords: europe, world war ii, german, interrogation, mind games

Overview: Germans kidnap an American major and try to convince him that World War II is over, so that they can get details about the Allied invasion of Europe out of him.
