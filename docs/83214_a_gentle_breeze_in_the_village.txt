Title: A Gentle Breeze in the Village (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Nobuhiro Yamashita
Main Cast: Kaho, Masaki Okada, Erisa Yanagi, Shôko Fujimura, Yui Natsukawa

Overview: Based on a popular manga, the film is a tender coming-of-age love story set deep in Japan's countryside. When eighth grade cool guy Hiromi transfers from Tokyo to a small elementary/middle school with only six kids enrolled, wholesome and honest Soyo becomes enthralled with his sophisticated world.
