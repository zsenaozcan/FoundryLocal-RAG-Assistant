Title: Voice from the Stone (2017)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Romance, Thriller
Director: Eric D. Howell
Main Cast: Emilia Clarke, Marton Csokas, Caterina Murino, Kate Linder, Lisa Gastoni
Keywords: female nudity, fairy tale, suspense, haunting, hitchcockian, tuscany, 1950s

Overview: Set in 1950s Tuscany, Voice from the Stone is the haunting and suspenseful story of Verena, a solemn nurse drawn to aid a young boy who has fallen silent since the sudden passing of his mother.
