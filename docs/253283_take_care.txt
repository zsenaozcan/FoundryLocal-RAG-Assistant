Title: Take Care (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Liz Tuccillo
Main Cast: Leslie Bibb, Thomas Sadoski, Betty Gilpin, Michael Stahl-David, Marin Ireland
Keywords: romance, cancer, comedy, auto accident, woman director, awkward
