Title: Hold Your Man (1933)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Sam Wood
Main Cast: Jean Harlow, Clark Gable, Stuart Erwin, Dorothy Burgess, Muriel Kirkland
Keywords: blackmail, con artist

Overview: Ruby falls in love with small-time con man Eddie. During a botched blackmail scheme, Eddie accidentally kills the man they were setting up. Eddie takes off and Ruby is sent to a reformatory for two years.
