Title: Street Mobster (1972)
Genres: Crime, Action, Thriller, Foreign
Director: Kinji Fukasaku
Main Cast: Bunta Sugawara, Noboru Andô, Mayumi Nagisa, Asao Koike, Noboru Mitani
Keywords: prison, rape, yakuza, nudity, murder, gang, street life

Overview: The Japanese yakuza films certainly have a well-earned reputation of being violent and chaotic. Some are more frenetic than others and Street Mobster is unbelievable high on savagery and energy. It dawned the re-birth of the genre, telling the story of low-level punks who will do anything without compromise and little sense of honor.
