Title: Expresso Bongo (1959)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Val Guest
Main Cast: Laurence Harvey, Sylvia Syms, Yolande Donlan, Cliff Richard
Keywords: based on play or musical

Overview: A seedy London promoter turns a naive, working-class teenager into a pop singing sensation. Director Val Guest's 1959 British drama stars Laurence Harvey, Cliff Richard, Sylvia Syms, Yolande Donlan, Hermione Baddeley, Susan Hampshire and Avis Bunnage.
