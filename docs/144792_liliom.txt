Title: Liliom (1930)
Genres: Drama
Director: Frank Borzage
Main Cast: Rose Hobart, Estelle Taylor, H.B. Warner, Lee Tracy, Walter Abel
Keywords: robbery, heaven, carousel , hell, afterlife, redemption, foreign language adaptation

Overview: A carousel barker falls in love with a young woman. Both are fired from their jobs, and when the young woman becomes pregnant, the carousel barker tries to help pull off a robbery, which goes wrong. Because of the robbery, he dies, and after spending time in hell, is sent back to earth for one day to try to make amends.
