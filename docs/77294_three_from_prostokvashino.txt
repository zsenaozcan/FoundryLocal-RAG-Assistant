Title: Three from Prostokvashino (1978)
Genres: Animation
Director: Vladimir Popov
Main Cast: Mariya Vinogradova, Oleg Tabakov, Lev Durov, Valentina Talyzina, Boris Novikov

Overview: Little Fedor brings a cat to home despite his mother's distaste for cats. He runs away with his talking cat, to make more friends on the way.
