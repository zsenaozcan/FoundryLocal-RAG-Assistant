Title: Daltry Calhoun (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Music
Director: Katrina Holden Bronson
Main Cast: Johnny Knoxville, Elizabeth Banks, Juliette Lewis, David Koechner, Kick Gurry
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In small town Tennessee, a ne'er-do-well man (Knoxville) wrestling for control over his fading golf club is reunited with his estranged daughter, a 14-year-old musical prodigy.
