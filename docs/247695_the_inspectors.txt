Title: The Inspectors (1998)
Genres: Mystery, Crime
Director: Brad Turner
Main Cast: Louis Gossett, Jr., Jonathan Silverman, Samantha Ferris, Ty Olsson
Keywords: criminal investigation

Overview: U.S Postal Inspectors are called in when a suburban couple are killed by a mail bomb. Suspicion immediately falls on the couple's estranged and heavily in debt son, who also just happens to be a Navy munitions expert. But investigations reveal that he is on the run from a past event in his life that is associated with the bombings.
