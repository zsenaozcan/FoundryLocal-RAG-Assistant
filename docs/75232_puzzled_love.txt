Title: Puzzled Love (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Bruno Sarabia
Main Cast: Marcel Borràs, Saras Gil, Artur Busquets, Irene C. Rodríguez
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Sun from Chicago (USA) and Lucas from Mallorca (Spain) meet each other in a students's flat in Barcelona. They fall in love and try to enjoy it without thinking of their separation at the term's ending. Will their love prevail?
