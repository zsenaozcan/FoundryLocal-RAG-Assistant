Title: My Reputation (1946)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Curtis Bernhardt
Main Cast: Barbara Stanwyck, George Brent, Lucile Watson, Scotty Beckett, Bobby Cooper
Keywords: widow, gossip, domineering mother, skiing, mother child relationship, army major, social convention, christmastime

Overview: Tongues begin to wag when a lonely widow becomes romantically involved with a military man. Problems arise when the gossip is filtered down to her own children.
