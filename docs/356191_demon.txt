Title: Demon (2015)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Marcin Wrona
Main Cast: Itay Tiran, Agnieszka Żulewska, Tomasz Schuchardt, Andrzej Grabowski, Tomasz Ziętek
Keywords: possession, wedding party

Overview: A bridegroom is possessed by an unquiet spirit in the midst of his own wedding celebration, in this clever take on the Jewish legend of the dybbuk.
