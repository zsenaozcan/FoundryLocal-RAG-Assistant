Title: Savannah Smiles (1982)
Genres: Family
Director: Pierre De Moro
Main Cast: Bridgette Andersen, Mark Miller, Donovan Scott, Michael Parks, Peter Graves
Keywords: runaway, ransom, kidnapping, neglect, politician, police, escaped convict

Overview: The young daughter of a politician runs away due to lack of attention. She hides in the car of two not too bright crooks who are slowly converted into parent figures as the police web closes down on the supposed kidnappers.
