Title: Next of Kin (1982)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Tony Williams
Main Cast: Jacki Kerin, John Jarratt, Alex Scott, Gerda Nicolson, Charles McCallum
Keywords: cemetery, dream, diary, violence, mirror, ghost

Overview: In a rest home for elderly people, a daughter reads her mother's diary. Soon events that are mentioned in the mother's diary begin to happen to the daughter.
