Title: Trap (2007)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Foreign
Director: James Bonner
Main Cast: Amanda Lamberti, Heather Kozlakowski, Patrick Moug, Edward Nahhat, Bradley K. Ross

Overview: After Amy convinces Nicole to kill her abusive husband, Nicole travels to her on-line friend's home -- only to find Amy's own abused spouse, Chandler. And he's got blood on his hands, too. TRAP is a painfully funny look inside love, marriage, and body disposal.
