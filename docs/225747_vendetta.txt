Title: Vendetta (2013)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Stephen Reynolds
Main Cast: Danny Dyer, Roxanne McKee, Vincent Regan, Josef Altin, Bruce Payne

Overview: Special ops interrogation officer Jimmy Vickers (Danny Dyer) tracks down a gang who slaughtered his parents while at the same time avoiding being tracked down by his old unit and the cops. With police closing in and his old unit on his trail, he has to evade capture long enough to complete his gruesome crusade.
