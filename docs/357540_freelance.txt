Title: Freelance (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Drama
Director: Nawapol Thamrongrattanarit
Main Cast: Sunny Suwanmethanon, Davika Hoorne, Violette Wautier, Nottapon Boonprakob, Torpong Chantabubpha

Overview: A hard-worker freelancer who falls in love with a doctor. While his life is getting worse every second. He must find the way to release his life to the beyond of everything.
