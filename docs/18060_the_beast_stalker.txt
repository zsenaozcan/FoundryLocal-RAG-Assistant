Title: The Beast Stalker (2008)
Genres: Action
Director: Dante Lam
Main Cast: Nicholas Tse, Nick Cheung, Zhang Jingchu, Miao Pu, Sherman Chung
Keywords: ransom, police, suspense

Overview: Sergeant Tong is wracked with guilt after he unwittingly kills a young girl whilst capuring a criminal named Cheung. When the girl's sister is later kidnapped in a ploy to get Cheung released, Sergeant Tong vows to find and rescue her before she comes to harm.
