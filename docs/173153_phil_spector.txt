Title: Phil Spector (2013)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: David Mamet
Main Cast: Helen Mirren, Al Pacino, Jeffrey Tambor, Chiwetel Ejiofor, Rebecca Pidgeon

Overview: A drama centered on the relationship between Phil Spector and defense attorney Linda Kenney Baden while the music business legend was on trial for the murder of Lana Clarkson.
