Title: The Rain Fairy (2010)
Genres: Family, Fantasy
Director: Milan Cieslar
Main Cast: Jakub Gottwald, Miroslav Donutil, Simona Stašová, Lenka Vlasáková, Vica Kerekes

Overview: The elements that rule Earth - Air, Fire, Earth and Water - are in their "regular inspection tour". We are following Water, in the form of the Rain Fairy. Traversing the land dressed like a poor woman, she learns that people really have begun focusing mainly on money and that love and goodness are disappearing.
