Title: The Married Couple of the Year Two (1971)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, History
Director: Jean-Paul Rappeneau
Main Cast: Jean-Paul Belmondo, Marlène Jobert, Laura Antonelli, Sami Frey, Pierre Brasseur
Keywords: french revolution, ex husband, guillotine, relationship, divorce, aristocrat, ex-wife

Overview: Nicolas Philibert goes to America after killing a French aristocrat. On his return he tries to divorce his wife, Charlotte, but when he sees others trying to woo her his own interest is rekindled.
