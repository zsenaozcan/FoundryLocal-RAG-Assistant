Title: Lowlands (1954)
Genres: Drama
Director: Leni Riefenstahl
Main Cast: Leni Riefenstahl, Bernhard Minetti, Aribert Wäscher, Maria Koppenhöfer, Karl Skraup
Keywords: spain, jealousy, rejection, woman director, 20th century

Overview: Set in the early part of 20th century Europe. There lived a dancer who becomes the romantic bone of contention between a humble shepherd and an imperious marquis.
