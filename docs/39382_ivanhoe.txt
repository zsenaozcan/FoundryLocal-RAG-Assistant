Title: Ivanhoe (1982)
Genres: Drama, History, Adventure
Director: Douglas Camfield
Main Cast: Anthony Andrews, James Mason, Sam Neill, Michael Hordern, Julian Glover
Keywords: middle ages

Overview: This is a remake of Walter Scott's Ivanhoe. Ivanhoe, a worthy and noble knight, the champion of justice returns to England after the holy wars. He find England under the reign of Prince John and his henchmen and finds himself being involved in the power-struggle for the throne of England. Will justice prevail and will all fair ladies in distress be rescued?
