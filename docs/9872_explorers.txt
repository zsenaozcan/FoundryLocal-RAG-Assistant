Title: Explorers (1985)
Genres: Adventure, Family, Fantasy, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Joe Dante
Main Cast: Ethan Hawke, River Phoenix, Bobby Fite, Bradley Gregg, Georg Olden
Keywords: dream, spacecraft, space travel, bullying, best friend, young love, circuit board

Overview: The visionary dreams of three curious and adventuresome young boys become an exciting reality in Explorers, the action-fantasy from director Joe Dante, who combines keen humor, warmth and fantasy with unexpected twists. In their makeshift laboratory, the boys use an amazing discovery and their ingenuity to build their own spaceship and launch themselves on a fantastic interplanetary journey.
