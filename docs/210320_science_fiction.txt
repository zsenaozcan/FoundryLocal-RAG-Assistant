Title: Science Fiction (2002)
Genres: Adventure, Thriller, Family, Science Fiction
Director: Danny Deprez
Main Cast: David Geclowicz, Fran Michiels, Jurre Baguet
Keywords: airport, alien, teacher, school

Overview: Nine-year old Andreas Decker is the new kid in town. He tells his class that he has lived all over the world on account of his parents being top scientific researchers. Yet classmate Vero finds Andreas's workaholic parents really weird. She suggests to spy on them. When Andreas overhears their conversation about conquering the world and becoming leaders, their can only be one conclusion for the baffled children: Andreas's parents are aliens.
