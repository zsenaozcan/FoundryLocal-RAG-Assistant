Title: Bowery at Midnight (1942)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Horror, Action
Director: Wallace Fox
Main Cast: Bela Lugosi, John Archer, Wanda McKay, Tom Neal, Vince Barnett
Keywords: back from the dead, soup, soup kitchen

Overview: Soup kitchen operator and criminologist Bela Lugosi operates a soup kitchen as a front for a criminal gang who commit a series of robberies and murders.
