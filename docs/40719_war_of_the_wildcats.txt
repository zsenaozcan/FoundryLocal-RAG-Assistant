Title: War of the Wildcats (1943)
Genres: Western
Director: Albert S. Rogell
Main Cast: John Wayne, Martha Scott, Albert Dekker, George 'Gabby' Hayes, Marjorie Rambeau
Keywords: oklahoma, native american, oil, cowboy, turn of the century

Overview: Cowboy Dan Somers and oilman Jim "Hunk" Gardner compete for oil lease rights on Indian land in Oklahoma, as well as for the favors of schoolteacher Cathy Allen.
