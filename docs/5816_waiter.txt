Title: Waiter (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Alex van Warmerdam
Main Cast: Alex van Warmerdam, Ariane Schluter, Jaap Spijkers, Mark Rietman, Thekla Reuten
Keywords: loss of lover, wife husband relationship, netherlands, lover (female), restaurant, lovers, relationship problems, little boy, author

Overview: Edgar works as a waiter in 'Het Westen', a restaurant. He is married and has a lover. His neighbors terrorize him with loud music and garbage. One day after he is mistreated by some customers he is sick of it all and goes to talk to his creator, the scenario writer.
