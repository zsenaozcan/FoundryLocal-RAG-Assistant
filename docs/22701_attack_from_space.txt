Title: Attack from Space (1965)
Genres: Action, Foreign, Science Fiction
Director: Teruo Ishii
Main Cast: Ken Utsui, Sachihiro Ohsawa, Junko Ikeuchi, Minako Yamada, Shôji Nakayama
Keywords: superhero, space

Overview: The superhero Starman is sent by the Emerald Planet to protect Earth from belligerent aliens from the Sapphire Galaxy. The Sapphireans (or "Spherions") kidnap Dr. Yamanaka and force him to use his spaceship against the Earth.
