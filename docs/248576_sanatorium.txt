Title: Sanatorium (2013)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Brant Sersen
Main Cast: Kate Riley, Don Fanelli, DJ Hazard, Megan Neuringer
Keywords: sanatorium

Overview: On a bitterly cold December night, a paranormal investigations team has set their sights on the bloody Hillcrest Sanatorium to answer the age old question: Is there life after death? With rumours of hauntings and local children gone missing, they may just get their answer the hard way when members of the team mysteriously disappear, leaving behind unnerving evidence.
