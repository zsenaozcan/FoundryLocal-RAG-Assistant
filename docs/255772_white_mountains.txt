Title: White Mountains (1964)
Genres: Drama
Director: Melis Ubukeyev
Main Cast: Shaarbek Kobaganov, A. Abayeva, Baken Kydykeyeva, Muratbek Ryskulov, Bolot Beyshenaliev

Overview: Mukash is chased by officials, learns of the devastation of war from a blind woman and helps her daughter to freedom beyond the river crossing, he, having to choose a tragic solution.
