Title: Pigs with a P.38 (1978)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Gianfranco Pagani
Main Cast: Marc Porel, Laura Belli, Raymond Pellegrin, Luciano Pigozzi, Gabriele Ferzetti
Keywords: poliziotteschi, eurocrime

Overview: After an elderly crime boss retires from business, his three lieutenants each inherit a piece of his empire: Max is given the kidnapping ring, while the prostitution racket and the drug trafficking go to the others. Max is not satisfied with his share, and after killing the old boss he gets rid of the other two lieutenants. However, a police officer finds a lighter at the murder scene which could lead to Max, who then kidnaps the policeman's daughter.
