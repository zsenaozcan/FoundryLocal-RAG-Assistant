Title: Little Odessa (1994)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: James Gray
Main Cast: Edward Furlong, Tim Roth, Moira Kelly, Vanessa Redgrave, Maximilian Schell
Keywords: russian mafia, dysfunctional family, brothers

Overview: The film follows the personal relationship between a father and his two sons, one of whom is a hit-man for the Russian mafia in Brooklyn.
