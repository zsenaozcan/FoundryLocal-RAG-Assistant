Title: Look for a Woman (1982)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Alla Surikova
Main Cast: Sofiko Chiaureli, Leonid Kuravlyov, Sergei Yursky, Yelena Solovey, Aleksandr Abdulov

Overview: The story begins when Alice Postique, who had to stay late at work, discovers her boss Roche with a knife in his back. When the police detective arrives, Alice recognizes him as her old friend.
