Title: Gung Ho! (1943)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, War
Director: Ray Enright
Main Cast: Randolph Scott, Alan Curtis, Noah Beery, Jr., J. Carrol Naish, Sam Levene
Keywords: island, pearl harbor, marine

Overview: A true-life epic that revolves around an exclusive bataillon of the U.S. Marine Corps during World War II, "Carlson's Raiders," whose assignment is to take control of a South Pacific island once possessed by the United States but now under Japanese command.
