Title: The Last Desperate Hours (1974)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Giorgio Stegani
Main Cast: Antonio Sabàto, Silvia Monti, Pier Paolo Capponi, Nicoletta Rizzi

Overview: Paolo Mancuso is a gang boss whose rivals want dead. During a failed assassination attempt, he is bitten by an infected lab rat. In the short time he has left to live, he tracks down his enemies and kills them one by one.
