Title: President (2006)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Lionel Delplanque
Main Cast: Albert Dupontel, Jérémie Renier, Mélanie Doutey, Claude Rich, Carlo Brandt

Overview: State secrets, sincere convictions, ecstatic crowds, a regal lifestyle, prying journalists, suspicious disappearances: what goes on behind the scenes in the halls of power or the daily life of a President.
