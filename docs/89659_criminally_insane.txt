Title: Criminally Insane (1975)
Genres: Horror
Director: Nick Millard
Main Cast: Priscilla Alden, Michael Flood, Jane Lambert, Robert Copple, George Buck Flower
Keywords: prostitution, killing spree, exploitation film, mental illness, meat cleaver, obese

Overview: An obese woman recently released from an insane asylum kills anyone who attempts to get her to stop eating.
