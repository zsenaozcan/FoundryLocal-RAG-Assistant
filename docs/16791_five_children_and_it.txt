Title: Five Children and It (2004)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Drama, Science Fiction, Family
Director: John Stephenson
Main Cast: Tara Fitzgerald, Freddie Highmore, Alex Jennings, Jonathan Bailey, Jessica Claridge
Keywords: fairy, wish fulfillment

Overview: A Psammead is 'It', an ancient, irritable, ugly sand fairy, which five children find one day in a gravel pit. As a reward for finding him, It grants the children one wish a day, the results of which will last until sunset.
