Title: Hot Pepper (1973)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Les Blank
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: new orleans, cajun

Overview: Thrilling musical portrait of Zydeco King Clifton Chenier, who combines the pulsating rhythms of Cajun dance music and black R&amp;B with African overtones, belting out his irresistible music in the sweaty juke joints of South Louisiana.
