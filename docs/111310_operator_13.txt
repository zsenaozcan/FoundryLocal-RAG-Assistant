Title: Operator 13 (1934)
Genres: Drama, Western, War, Romance, History
Director: Richard Boleslawski
Main Cast: Marion Davies, Gary Cooper, Jean Parker, Katharine Alexander, Ted Healy
Keywords: civil war, spy, espionage

Overview: Union spy Gail Loveless impersonates a black maid in the early days of the Civil War, but complications arise when she falls in love with a Confederate officer.
