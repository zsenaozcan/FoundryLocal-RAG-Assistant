Title: Children of the Corn (1984)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Horror, Thriller
Director: Fritz Kiersch
Main Cast: Peter Horton, Linda Hamilton, R. G. Armstrong, John Franklin, Courtney Gains
Keywords: small town, sacrifice, children gang, human sacrifice, paganism, evil preacher, preacher, cornfield, religious cult, killer child

Overview: A boy preacher named Isaac goes to a town in Nebraska called Gatlin and gets all the children to murder every adult in town.
