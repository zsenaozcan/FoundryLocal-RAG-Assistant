Title: Women Who Flirt (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Pang Ho-cheung
Main Cast: Zhou Xun, Huang Xiaoming, Sonia Sui, Evonne Hsieh
Keywords: romantic comedy, flirting, self-help, flirtation

Overview: When Zhang Hui is told by long-time best friend Xiao Gong that he has a new girlfriend, she is determined to learn new tricks to gain him back. Based on the novel written by Luo Fuman, "Everyone Loves Tender Women".
