Title: Finding Altamira (2016)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Hugh Hudson
Main Cast: Antonio Banderas, Golshifteh Farahani, Rupert Everett, Pierre Niney, Henry Goodman
Keywords: spain, cave, history, discovery, altamira

Overview: The story of nine-year old Maria and her father Marcelino who, in 1879, found the first pre-historic cave paintings at the now world famous Altamira cave.
