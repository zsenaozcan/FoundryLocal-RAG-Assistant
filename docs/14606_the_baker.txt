Title: The Baker (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Gareth Lewis
Main Cast: Damian Lewis, Kate Ashfield, Nikolaj Coster-Waldau, Michael Gambon, Dyfan Dwyfor
Keywords: hitman, black humor, bakery, rural setting

Overview: Milo (Damian Lewis) is a professional hit man living on the edge. When failing to fulfil a contract for the first time, Milo escapes the city to avoid the wrath of his employers. Hiding out in a remote rural village, the locals mistake him for the new baker.
