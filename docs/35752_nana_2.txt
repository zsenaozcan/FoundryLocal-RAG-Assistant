Title: Nana 2 (2006)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Kentarô Ôtani
Main Cast: Mika Nakashima, Yui Ichikawa, Hiroki Narimiya, Kanata Hongo, Yuna Ito
Keywords: female friendship, friendship

Overview: Two girls with the same name but very different personalities share an apartment in this sequel to Nana. The rising fame of Nana Osaki's band, the Black Stones, is beginning to take a toll on the best friends' relationship. Meanwhile, Nana Komatsu struggles to make sense of her love triangle with Black Stones' guitarist Nobu and rival group Trapnest's bassist Takumi.
