Title: Come Play with Me (1968)
Genres: Drama
Director: Salvatore Samperi
Main Cast: Lisa Gastoni, Lou Castel, Gabriele Ferzetti, Luisa De Santis, Massimo Sarchielli

Overview: Young Alvise's body is paralyzed, or at least he thinks so. He is infatuated by his beautiful aunt Lea. What starts as innocent therapy and care soon develops into strange erotic and psychological battle between them.
