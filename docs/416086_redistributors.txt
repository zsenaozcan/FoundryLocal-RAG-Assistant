Title: Redistributors (2016)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Adrian Tanner
Main Cast: James Allen, Natan Barreto, Tim Bentinck, Robert Boulton, Alexandra Evans
Keywords: london england

Overview: A city PR girl goes on the run accused of leaking information from the military supply company where she works. Chased by ex-soldiers she hides with her brother, a member of a shadowy hacker group, and has to choose between her luxury life - and the Redistributors.
