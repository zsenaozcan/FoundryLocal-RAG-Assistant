Title: Dog Eat Dog (2009)
Genres: Action, Drama, Foreign, Romance, Thriller
Director: Carlos Moreno
Main Cast: Marlon Moreno, Andrés Parra, Alvaro Rodriguez, Blas Jaramillo, John Alex Castillo
Keywords: hotel room, colombia, black magic, mafia boss, curse, chainsaw, torture, hotel manager, barking dog, cali colombia
