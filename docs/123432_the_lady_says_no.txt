Title: The Lady Says No (1952)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Frank Ross
Main Cast: Joan Caulfield, David Niven, Lenore Lonergan, Frances Bavier, James Robertson Justice

Overview: The feminist author of a national best-seller titled "The Lady Says No" meets a sexist magazine photographer and decides she'd rather say yes. Director Frank Ross' 1952 comedy stars David Niven, Joan Caulfield, James Robertson Justice, Frances Bavier, Lenore Lonergan, Peggy Maley, Henry Jones and Jeff York.
