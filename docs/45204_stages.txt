Title: Stages (2007)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Mijke de Jong
Main Cast: Elsie de Blauw, Marcel Musters, Stijn Koomen, Jeroen Willems, Joan Nederhof
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Stages is a film about loneliness, anxiety, struggle and love, which in an unconventional way shows the survival of two former lovers in their forties and their seventeen year-old son.
