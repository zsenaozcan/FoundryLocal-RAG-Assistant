Title: Days of Santiago (2004)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Josué Méndez
Main Cast: Pietro Sibille, Milagros Vidal, Marisela Puicón, Sandra Vergara, Ricardo Mejía

Overview: Santiago returns home from the Peruvian army ill-prepared to cope with the realities of life. Haunted by his violent military past, he is conflicted by his desire for education and his temptation to join his comrades in a decadent life of crime.
