Title: Zig Zag (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: David S. Goyer
Main Cast: John Leguizamo, Wesley Snipes, Oliver Platt, Natasha Lyonne, Luke Goss
Keywords: independent film

Overview: An autistic 15-year-old boy steals money from his boss to provide rent for his abusive father, who uses the money to repay a loan shark.
