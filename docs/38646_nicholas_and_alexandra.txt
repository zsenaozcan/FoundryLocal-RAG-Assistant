Title: Nicholas and Alexandra (1971)
Genres: Romance, Drama, War
Director: Franklin J. Schaffner
Main Cast: Michael Jayston, Janet Suzman, Roderic Noble, Ania Marson, Lynne Frederick
Keywords: tzar, epic, rasputin

Overview: Biography of the last Russian Monarch, Tsar Nicholas II and his Tsarina, Alexandra.Czar Nicholas II, the inept monarch of Russia, insensitive to the needs of his people, is overthrown and exiled to Siberia with his family.
