Title: The Ghost Galleon (1974)
Genres: Horror
Director: Amando de Ossorio
Main Cast: Maria Perschy, Jack Taylor, Bárbara Rey, Carlos Lemos, Manuel de Blas
Keywords: rape, ship, ghost ship, sequel, undead, zombie, evil, coast, galleon

Overview: After a pair of models go missing from a boat, a rescue party discover an empty galleon carrying the coffins of the long dead Knights Templar. The rescue party board the galleon and then discover their own boat has vanished. The survivors struggle to fight off the spectral knights with what little knowledge they have of them.
