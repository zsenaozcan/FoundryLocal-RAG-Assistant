Title: The Dancing Masters (1943)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Malcolm St. Clair
Main Cast: Stan Laurel, Oliver Hardy, Trudy Marshall, Robert Bailey, Matt Briggs
Keywords: inventor, ballet, dance instructor

Overview: The Dancing Masters is a 1943 Laurel and Hardy feature film. The plot involves the team running a ballet school, and getting involved with an inventor. A young Robert Mitchum has an uncredited cameo role as a fraudulent insurance salesman.
