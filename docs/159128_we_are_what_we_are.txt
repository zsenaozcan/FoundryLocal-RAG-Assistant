Title: We Are What We Are (2013)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Jim Mickle
Main Cast: Bill Sage, Ambyr Childers, Julia Garner, Michael Parks, Wyatt Russell
Keywords: rain, funeral, bone, prayer, storm, religious, dog, horror movie remade, creek, cannibalism

Overview: In this reimagining of the 2010 Mexican film of the same name, director Jim Mickle paints a gruesome portrait of an introverted family struggling to keep their macabre traditions alive, giving us something we can really sink our teeth into.
