Title: Sam's Song (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jordan Leondopoulos
Main Cast: Robert De Niro, Jarred Mickey, Jennifer Warren, Terrayne Crawford, Martin J. Kelley

Overview: A political filmmaker finds himself in Long Island for a weekend where he finds himself entangled with a high-living, jet set crowd. At first it is exciting, but soon he finds himself disillusioned by their shallowness.
