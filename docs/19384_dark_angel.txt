Title: Dark Angel (1990)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Science Fiction, Crime, Horror
Director: Craig R. Baxley
Main Cast: Dolph Lundgren, Brian Benben, Betsy Brantley, Matthias Hues, Jay Bilas
Keywords: evidence, overdose, heroin, cop, alien, shootout, undercover cop, drug

Overview: Jack Caine is a Houston vice cop who's forgotten the rule book. His self-appointed mission is to stop the drugs trade and the number one supplier Victor Manning. Whilst involved in an undercover operation to entrap Victor Manning, his partner gets killed, and a sinister newcomer enters the scene...
