Title: Skylark (1941)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Mark Sandrich
Main Cast: Claudette Colbert, Ray Milland, Brian Aherne, Binnie Barnes, Walter Abel
Keywords: wife husband relationship, wedding anniversary

Overview: As her fifth wedding anniversary approaches, a woman realizes that she is fed up with always coming in second to her husband's advertising business. Just at the moment when she is trying to decide what to do, she meets a handsome attorney, and their innocent flirtation begins to turn into something a bit more serious.
