Title: Chained Heat (1983)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Paul Nicholas
Main Cast: Linda Blair, John Vernon, Sybil Danning, Tamara Dobson, Stella Stevens
Keywords: sadism, sexploitation, lesbian sex, women in prison

Overview: Linda Blair plays Carol, a young woman who must serve 18 months in prison after drink driving and killing a man. The prison turns out to be brimming with decadence, corruption and sleaze, where the other female inmates are sadistic crack-selling lesbian rapists and the guards and warden are no better.
