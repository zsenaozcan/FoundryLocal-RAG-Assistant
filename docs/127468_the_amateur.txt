Title: The Amateur (1999)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Juan Bautista Stagnaro
Main Cast: Mauricio Dayub, Pedro Heredia, Vando Villamil, Arturo Goetz, Carlos Lanari

Overview: On the outskirts of a provincial town, Pajaro is determined to enter the Guinness Book of Records by breaking the longest bike-ride record. He rides around the fountain in the square with the help of his friend, Lopecito. He is willing to face many challenges, in attaining this new record. Will this race open the path to love for him, and for his redemption?
