Title: Russian Roulette (1975)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: Lou Lombardo
Main Cast: George Segal, Cristina Raines, Bo Brundin, Denholm Elliott, Gordon Jackson
Keywords: spy, cia, hitman, kgb, firearm, mafia, conspiracy, sniper rifle, explosion, soviet

Overview: An RCMP officer is ordered to discreetly take a Russian immigrant into custody in advance of a state visit by the Soviet premier. When his prisoner is kidnapped, the officer is drawn into a complicated assasination scheme.
