Title: Mother Küsters Goes to Heaven (1975)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rainer Werner Fassbinder
Main Cast: Brigitte Mira, Ingrid Caven, Margit Carstensen, Karlheinz Böhm, Irm Hermann
Keywords: suicide, communist, widow, anarchist, death of father, mother daughter relationship, working class, class differences, distortion, media manipulation

Overview: Frau Kusters is preparing dinner late one seemingly ordinary afternoon in her seemingly ordinary kitchen in Frankfurt, Germany. Mrs. Kusters wants to add canned sausages to the stew, her annoying daughter-in-law thinks otherwise. The point, we soon find out, is moot: Mr. Kusters has murdered the personnel director at the soap factory where he works before committing suicide.
