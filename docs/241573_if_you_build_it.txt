Title: If You Build It (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Patrick Creadon
Main Cast: Matthew Miller, Emily Pilloton
Keywords: education, architecture, construction

Overview: A year in the life of one of America's most innovative classrooms where students design &amp; build to transform their hometown community. The film follows Emily Pilloton and Matt Miller as they teach the fundamentals of design, architecture and construction to a class of high school juniors in rural North Carolina.
