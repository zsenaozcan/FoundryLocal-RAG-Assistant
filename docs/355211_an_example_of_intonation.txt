Title: An Example of Intonation (1991)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Alexander Sokurov
Main Cast: Boris Yeltsin, Alexander Sokurov

Overview: The second film by Sokurov featuring Boris Yeltsin as the principal character. Now he is the President of Russia, invested with power, bearing the full responsibility for the destinies of his distant compatriots as well as his closest kin and friends.
