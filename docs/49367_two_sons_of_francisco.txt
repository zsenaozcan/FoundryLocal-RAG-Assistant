Title: Two Sons of Francisco (2005)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Breno Silveira
Main Cast: Ângelo Antônio, Dira Paes, Márcio Kieling , José Dumont, Paloma Duarte
Keywords: brazilian, brazil, obsession, success, peasant, from rags to riches, based on true story, family relationships, car accident, death
