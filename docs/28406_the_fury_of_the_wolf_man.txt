Title: The Fury of the Wolf Man (1972)
Genres: Horror
Director: José María Zabalza
Main Cast: Paul Naschy, Perla Cristal, Verónica Luján, Miguel de la Riva, Pilar Zorrilla
Keywords: werewolf

Overview: A man has had a werewolf curse cast upon him. If he doesn't get rid of it, he turns into a killer werewolf when the moon is full.
