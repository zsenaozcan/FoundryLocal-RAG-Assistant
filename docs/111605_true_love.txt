Title: True Love (1989)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Nancy Savoca
Main Cast: Annabella Sciorra, Ron Eldard, Aida Turturro, Roger Rignack
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Donna and Michael are getting married. But first, they have to plan the reception, get the tux, buy the rings, and cope with their own uncertainty about the decision. Michael fears commitment. Donna has her doubts about Michael's immaturity. Both are getting cold feet.
