Title: An All Dogs Christmas Carol (1998)
Genres: Animation, Family, Comedy
Director: Paul Sabella
Main Cast: Ernest Borgnine, Dom DeLuise, Sheena Easton, Taylor Emerson, Bebe Neuwirth
Keywords: musical, dog, christmas

Overview: Charlie B. Barker and Itchy Ford are back with Sasha and the gang having a Dicken's of a time as they try to save Christmas from Carface and an evil spirit that wishes to use dogs all over the world to ruin Christmas forever.
