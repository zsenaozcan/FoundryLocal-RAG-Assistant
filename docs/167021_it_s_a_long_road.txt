Title: It's a Long Road (1998)
Genres: Drama
Director: Pantelis Voulgaris
Main Cast: Thanasis Vengos, Giorgos Armenis, Dimitris Katalifos, Kostas Kazanas

Overview: Three stories dealing with three men and their stance towards life, given their personal background. All stories are held in Northern Greece (Macedonia and Thrace), covering all the spectrum from urban to rural settings.
