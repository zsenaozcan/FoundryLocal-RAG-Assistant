Title: The Most Charming and Attractive (1985)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Gerald Bezhanov
Main Cast: Irina Muravyova, Tatyana Vasileva, Aleksandr Abdulov, Mikhail Kokshenov, Leonid Kuravlyov

Overview: Nadya Klyueva is a single woman. Persuaded by her friend, she decides to charm her co-worker whom she doesn't really love, but who is the most popular man around.
