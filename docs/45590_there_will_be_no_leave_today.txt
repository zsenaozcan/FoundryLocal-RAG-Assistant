Title: There Will Be No Leave Today (1959)
Genres: Drama
Director: Andrei Tarkovsky
Main Cast: A. Alekseev, Pyotr Lyubeshkin, Oleg Mokshantsev, Vladimir Marenkov, Igor Kosukhin
Keywords: suspense

Overview: Soldiers undertake the perilous task of removing a stockpile of World War II bombshells discovered during roadworks under the ground of a small village.
