Title: Rampart (2011)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Oren Moverman
Main Cast: Woody Harrelson, Steve Buscemi, Ben Foster, Robin Wright, Jon Bernthal
Keywords: confession, one-night stand, police corruption, racism, brutality, district attorney, racial tension, gunshot, ex-wife, bigotry
