Title: Murk (2005)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Jannik Johansen
Main Cast: Nikolaj Lie Kaas, Laura Drasbæk, Nicolas Bro, Lisbet Lundquist, Lærke Winther Andersen

Overview: The psychological thriller "Murk" tells the story of Jacob, who is investigating into the circumstances surrounding his sister's death on her wedding night.
