Title: War Machine (2017)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, War
Director: David Michôd
Main Cast: Brad Pitt, Anthony Hayes, John Magaro, Anthony Michael Hall, Emory Cohen
Keywords: journalist, based on novel, afghanistan, exposé, satirical, fictionalized history, u.s. general, born leader, charismatic, nato forces

Overview: A rock star general bent on winning the “impossible” war in Afghanistan takes us inside the complex machinery of modern war. Inspired by the true story of General Stanley McChrystal.
