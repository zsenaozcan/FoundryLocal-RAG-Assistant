Title: Lady Chatterley's Lover (1981)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Just Jaeckin
Main Cast: Sylvia Kristel, Shane Briant, Nicholas Clay, Ann Mitchell, Elizabeth Spriggs
Keywords: male nudity, female nudity, nurse, sex, adultery, based on novel, servant, infidelity, country estate, eroticism

Overview: A film adaptation of D.H. Lawrence's novel. After a crippling injury leaves her husband impotent, Lady Chatterly is torn between her love for her husband and her physical desires. With her husband's consent, she seeks out other means of fulfilling her needs.
