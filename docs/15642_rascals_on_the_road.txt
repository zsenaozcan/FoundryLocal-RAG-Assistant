Title: Rascals on the Road (2006)
Genres: Family, Comedy
Director: Michael Steiner
Main Cast: Manuel Häberli, Janic Halioua, Dominic Hänni, Alex Niederhäuser, Mike Müller

Overview: Four kids - Eugen, Wrigley, Bäschteli and Eduard - leave for an adventure around Switzerland, searching for the fabulous treasure of Titicaca Lake, discovered by Fritzli Bühler.
