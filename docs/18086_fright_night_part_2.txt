Title: Fright Night Part 2 (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Tommy Lee Wallace
Main Cast: Roddy McDowall, William Ragsdale, Traci Lind, Julie Carmen, Jon Gries

Overview: After three years of therapy Charley Brewster, now a college student, is convinced that Jerry Dandridge was a serial killer posing as a vampire. But when Regine, a mysterious actress and her entourage move into Peter Vincent's apartment block, the nightmare starts again - and this time it's personal!
