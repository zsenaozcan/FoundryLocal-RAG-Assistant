Title: The Chipmunk Adventure (1987)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Family
Director: Janice Karman
Main Cast: Ross Bagdasarian, Jr., Janice Karman, Dody Goodman, Susan Tyrrell, Anthony De Longis
Keywords: adventure, woman director, animated

Overview: Alvin has entered himself and Simon and Theodore in a hot-air balloon race around the world against the Chipettes to deliver diamonds for a group of diamond smugglers. The winners will collect a prize of $100,000. Kids and adults will enjoy this film made with musical numbers by the Chipmunks and the Chipettes.
