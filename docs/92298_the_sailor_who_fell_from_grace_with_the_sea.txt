Title: The Sailor Who Fell from Grace with the Sea (1976)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Lewis John Carlino
Main Cast: Kris Kristofferson, Sarah Miles, Jonathan Kahn, Margo Cunningham, Earl Rhodes
Keywords: nudity, revenge, sailor, the sea

Overview: When a widowed mother falls in love with an American sailor, her troubled young son is pressured by the bullying leader of his clique to seek revenge.
