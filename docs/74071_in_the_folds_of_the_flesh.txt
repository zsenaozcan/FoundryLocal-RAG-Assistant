Title: In the Folds of the Flesh (1970)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Sergio Bergonzelli
Main Cast: Eleonora Rossi Drago, Pier Angeli, Fernando Sancho, Alfredo Mayo, Emilio Gutiérrez Caba
Keywords: villa, blackmail, mystery, giallo, incest, host, hotel guests

Overview: The guests of a villa are killed off one by one by their hosts. Incest, decapitations and a cyanide bath feature amongst the other bizarre delights.
