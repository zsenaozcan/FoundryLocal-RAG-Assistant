Title: Choose (2011)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Marcus Graves
Main Cast: Katheryn Winnick, Nicholas Tucci, Kevin Pollak, Bruce Dern, Richard Short
Keywords: killer, father daughter relationship, criminal investigation, sadistic psychopath, college student

Overview: Fiona Wagner, a journalism student, tracks a cold-blooded killer with the help of her detective father and a therapist.
