Title: Emanuelle in America (1977)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Joe D'Amato
Main Cast: Laura Gemser, Gabriele Tinti, Roger Browne, Riccardo Salvino, Lars Bloch
Keywords: female nudity, sex, journalist, orgy, snuff, sexploitation, hardcore, exploitation film, corrupt politician, zoophilia

Overview: An American journalist travels throughout the world in search of a good story by joining a modern-day harem and traveling to Venice to see what really goes on at diplomatic parties. While trying to expose a corrupt government official, Emanuelle stumbles upon a group that uses kidnapped girls to make and sell snuff films
