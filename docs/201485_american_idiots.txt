Title: American Idiots (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Robert Taleghany
Main Cast: Jeffrey T. Schoettlin, Sean Muramatsu, Caroline D'Amore, Nick Faltas, Madeline Merritt
Keywords: on the road, race against the clock

Overview: Four friends embark on a cross-country journey to Las Vegas in an old RV to stop a wedding and save a friend from losing the love of his life.
