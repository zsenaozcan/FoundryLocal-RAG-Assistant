Title: Crashing (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Gary Walkow
Main Cast: David Cross, Caleigh White, Campbell Scott, Alex Kingston, Izabella Miko
Keywords: independent film

Overview: It sounds like a budding writer's dream: a bestselling first novel, a luxurious house in Malibu, and a trophy wife... But it all unravels when writer's block and a failed marriage send Richard McMurray (Campbell Scott, The Exorcism of Emily Rose) out into the streets.
