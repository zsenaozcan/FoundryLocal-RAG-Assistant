Title: The Year My Voice Broke (1987)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Romance
Director: John Duigan
Main Cast: Noah Taylor, Loene Carmen, Ben Mendelsohn, Graeme Blundell, Lynette Curran

Overview: Coming-of age feature, set in a New South Wales country town in the 1960s, about a 15 year-old boy. It follows his loves and how he copes with life's problems such as death and departure.
