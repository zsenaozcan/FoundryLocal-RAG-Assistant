Title: The Demoniacs (1974)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jean Rollin
Main Cast: Joëlle Coeur, John Rico, Willy Braque, Paul Bisciglia, Lieva Lone
Keywords: male nudity, female nudity, rape, drowning, nudity, clown, skeleton, sadism, revenge, murder

Overview: A gang of pirates rape the two sole survivors of a ship wreck. The violated girls are rescued by the strange inhabitants of a supposedly haunted island, where they are granted supernatural powers to strike revenge against the pirates.
