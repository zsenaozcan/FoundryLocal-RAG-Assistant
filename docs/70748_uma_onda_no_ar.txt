Title: Uma Onda no Ar (2002)
Genres: Drama, Action, Foreign
Director: Helvécio Ratton
Main Cast: Babú Santana, Adolfo Moura, Benjamim Abras, Alexandre Moreno, Edyr de Castro
Keywords: slum, pirate radio

Overview: Based on the true story of four friends who decide to install a communal pirate radio station called Rádio Favela in a shanty-town, to give voice to the outcasts' complaints.
