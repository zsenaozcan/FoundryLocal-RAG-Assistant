Title: Last Man Standing (2011)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller
Director: Ernest R. Dickerson
Main Cast: Anthony Michael Hall, Catherine Bell, Mekhi Phifer, Ele Bardha, Chris Torres

Overview: Abby Collins's quiet life as a wife and mother shows no trace of her past work as a military special-ops expert -- until the day her husband is kidnapped. With no alternative, Abby must resort to her long-dormant lethal skills to save her spouse.
