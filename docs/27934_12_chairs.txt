Title: 12 Chairs (1976)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Mark Zakharov
Main Cast: Andrey Mironov, Anatoli Papanov, Rolan Bykov, Oleg Tabakov, Tatyana Pelttser

Overview: A former aristocrat Ippolit Vorobyaninov leads a miserable life in Soviet Russia. His mother-in-law reveals a secret to him - she hid family diamonds in one of the twelve chairs they once had. Vorobyaninov in cooperation with a young con artist Ostap Bender start a long search for the diamonds.
