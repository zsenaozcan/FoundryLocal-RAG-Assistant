Title: The Sea Around Us (1953)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Irwin Allen
Main Cast: Don Forbes, Theodore von Eltz
Keywords: anthology, underwater world

Overview: Irwin Allen explores the mysteries of the deep blue sea in this Technicolor documentary. Based on Rachel L. Carson's famous study, this Oscar winning project investigates everything under the sea, from sharks, whales and octopuses to microscopical creatures and their coexistence in this vast underwater world.
