Title: The Dukes of Hazzard: Reunion! (1997)
Genres: TV Movie, Action, Adventure
Director: Lewis Teague
Main Cast: Tom Wopat, John Schneider, Catherine Bach, Denver Pyle, James Best

Overview: Mama Josephine Max wants to build a theme park in Hazzard, right on the Duke family farm! To stop her, Bo and Luke have to win a cross-country moonshine race. Because that's how things get settled in Hazzard.
