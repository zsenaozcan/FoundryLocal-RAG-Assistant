Title: Last Run (2001)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Anthony Hickox
Main Cast: Jürgen Prochnow, Armand Assante, Ornella Muti, Corey Johnson, Anthony Hickox
Keywords: thriller, crime

Overview: Frank Banner used to specialize in helping Soviet spies defect safely to the West. Banner's wife was shot to death aiding such an escape. Now, with the Cold War over, Banner is called upon to safely escort an ex-K.G.B. man who is wanted dead by both the CIA and the Russian Mafia
