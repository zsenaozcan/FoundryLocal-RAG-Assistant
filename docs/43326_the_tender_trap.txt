Title: The Tender Trap (1955)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Charles Walters
Main Cast: Frank Sinatra, Debbie Reynolds, David Wayne, Celeste Holm, Jarma Lewis
Keywords: musical, theater agent

Overview: Charlie Reader is a successful theater agent. He is also successful with young ladies. One day he is visited by his old friend Joe, married with three children. Joe falls in love with Charlie's girl Sylvia while Charlie spends his time with young actress Julie. Written by Mattias Thuresson
