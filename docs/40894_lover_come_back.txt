Title: Lover Come Back (1961)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Delbert Mann
Main Cast: Rock Hudson, Doris Day, Tony Randall, Edie Adams, Jack Oakie
Keywords: madison avenue

Overview: Rock Hudson and Doris Day are together again! Jerry Webster (Hudson) and Carol Templeton (Day) are rival Madison Avenue advertising executives who each dislike each other’s methods. After he steals a client out from under her cute little nose, revenge prompts her to infiltrate his secret "VIP" campaign in order to persuade the mystery product’s scientist to switch to her firm.
