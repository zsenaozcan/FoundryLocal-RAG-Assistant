Title: The Sporting Club (1971)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Larry Peerce
Main Cast: Robert Fields, Nicolas Coster, Margaret Blye, Jack Warden, Richard Dysart

Overview: This exploitative melodrama is set in northern Michigan where an exclusive private hunting club is located. There some of the country's richest, most powerful men come to relax and get closer to nature. Unfortunately, that means that they become engaged in debauchery and become brutal, amoral killers.
