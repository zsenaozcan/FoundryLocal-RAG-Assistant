Title: Sodoma's Ghost (1988)
Genres: Foreign, Horror
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Claudio Aliotti, Maria Concetta Salieri, Robert Egon, Jessica Moore, Teresa Razzaudi
Keywords: female nudity, orgy, nazis, haunted house, soldier, ghost

Overview: The spirits of Nazis who were killed by bombs while having an orgy return 50 years later to terrorize a group of six teens who stumble upon the house of their death site.
