Title: Zyzzyx Road (2006)
Genres: Thriller
Director: John Penney
Main Cast: Katherine Heigl, Leo Grillo, Tom Sizemore, Rickey Medlocke, Yorlin Madera
Keywords: suspense

Overview: A married man (Leo Grillo) meets a beautiful woman (Katherine Heigl) in a Las Vegas casino and allows her to seduce him. When her jealous boyfriend (Tom Sizemore) finds them together, a scuffle results in the boyfriend's death. The lovers head to the desert to bury the corpse, but it disappears.
