Title: The Double Hour (2009)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Romance, Thriller
Director: Giuseppe Capotondi
Main Cast: Kseniya Rappoport, Filippo Timi, Antonia Truppo, Gaetano Bruno, Fausto Russo Alesi
Keywords: suicide, sex, hotel, dream, psychology, loss, cop, love, microphone, photograph

Overview: A chambermaid and a former cop meet at a speed dating event and a romance develops. But during a romantic getaway things suddenly take a turn for the worse when her mysterious past is revealed.
