Title: The Bewitched Inn (1897)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: Georges Méliès
Main Cast: Georges Méliès
Keywords: french, horror, comedy, silent film, georges melies

Overview: A weary traveler stops at an inn along the way to get a good night's sleep, but his rest is interrupted by odd happenings when he gets to his room--beds vanishing and re-appearing, candles exploding, pants flying through the air and his shoes walking away by themselves.
