Title: New Tale of Zatôichi (1963)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Tokuzô Tanaka
Main Cast: Shintarô Katsu, Mikiko Tsubouchi, Seizaburô Kawazu, Fujio Suga, Mieko Kondo
Keywords: samurai, sequel, murder, zatoichi

Overview: Wishing to find peace, Zatoichi travels to his old village but only finds trouble when he ends up in a love triangle and finds old scores have followed him home.
