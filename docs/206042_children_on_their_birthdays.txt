Title: Children on Their Birthdays (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Romance
Director: Mark Medoff
Main Cast: Sheryl Lee, Christopher McDonald, Tom Arnold, Tania Raymonde, Jesse Plemons

Overview: Havoc is created in a small Southern community when a 12-year-old shows up, causing a couple 13-year-old friends to fall in love with her, thus possibly jeopardizing their friendship.
