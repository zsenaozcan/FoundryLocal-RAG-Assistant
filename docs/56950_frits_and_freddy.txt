Title: Frits and Freddy (2010)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Guy Goossens
Main Cast: Peter van den Begin, Tom van Dyck, Tania Kloek, Wim Opbrouck, Frank Aendenboom

Overview: Frits and Freddy Frateur are brothers who, after a confrontation with the white collar criminal Carlo Mus, kidnap his wife, Gina. The situation for the brothers goes from bad to worse as more and more people get involved, and all want a share of the ransom money.
