Title: Island in the Sky (1953)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: John Wayne, Lloyd Nolan, Walter Abel, James Arness, Andy Devine
Keywords: radio, airplane, survival, snow, food rationing, navigation

Overview: A C-47 transport plane, named the Corsair, makes a forced landing in the frozen wastes of Labrador, and the plane's pilot, Captain Dooley, must keep his men alive in deadly conditions while waiting for rescue.
