Title: The other me (2017)
Genres: Thriller, Mystery
Director: Sotiris Tsafoulias
Main Cast: Pigmalion Dadakaridis, Dimitris Katalifos, Manos Vakousis, Ioanna Kolliopoulou, Giorgos Chrysostomou

Overview: A lonely criminology professor attempts to solve the mystery behind five murders by decoding the puzzle of five Pythagorean theorems, in a crime story that features renowned French actor François Cluzet in a key-role.
