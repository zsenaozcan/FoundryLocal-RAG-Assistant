Title: Vlad the Impaler: The True Life of Dracula (1979)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Doru Nastase
Main Cast: Stefan Sileanu, Ernest Maftei, Emanoil Petrut, Teofil Vâlcu, Alexandru Repan
Keywords: ottoman empire
