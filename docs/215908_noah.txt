Title: Noah (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Walter Woodman
Main Cast: Sam Kantor, Nina Iordanova, Caitlin McConkey-Pirie, Nahan Fahey, Mackenzie Hannan
Keywords: social media, short

Overview: In a story that plays out entirely on a teenager's computer screen, Noah follows its eponymous protagonist as his relationship takes a rapid turn for the worse in this fascinating study of behavior (and romance) in the digital age.
