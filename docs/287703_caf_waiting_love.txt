Title: Café. Waiting. Love (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Chiang Chin-Lin
Main Cast: Vivian Sung, Bruce Bu Lu-Si, Megan Lai, Marcus Chang, Lee Luo
Keywords: coffee shop

Overview: College freshman Si-ying gets a part-time job at “Cafe. Waiting. Love” coffee shop, where she befriends Abusi, a tomboyish barista who can make any coffee the customers request, the beautiful and mysterious cafe owner, and Zeyu, the boy who always sits in the same spot in the cafe who seems to be very popular with girls and on whom Si-ying develops an instant crush.
