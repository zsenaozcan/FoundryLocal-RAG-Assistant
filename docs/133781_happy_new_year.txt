Title: Happy New Year (1987)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Romance
Director: John G. Avildsen
Main Cast: Peter Falk, Charles Durning, Tom Courtenay, Tracy Brooks Swope, Ruben Rabasa
Keywords: new year's eve, robbery, robber, antiquary, new year's day, jewelry, antique, jewel, jewel heist, jewel thief

Overview: Small time crooksters Nick and Charlie have an elaborate plan to rob an exclusive jewelers store. Using a variety of disguises and posing as rich old men and women they begin the set-up, but then Nick falls for the owner of a neighboring antique store and things get a little complicated.
