Title: Disappearing Acts (2000)
Genres: TV Movie, Romance, Drama
Director: Gina Prince-Bythewood
Main Cast: Sanaa Lathan, Wesley Snipes, CCH Pounder
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Zora Banks is a school teacher and aspiring singer hoping to become a successful star while taking a break from heartache. Franklin Swift is a down-on-his-luck construction worker and not-quite divorced father of two hoping to start his own business. The two meet and fall in love and during the course of the stormy relationship, they both come to some startling conclusions about love and each other.
