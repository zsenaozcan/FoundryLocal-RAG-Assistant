Title: The Bloodstained Shadow (1978)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Antonio Bido
Main Cast: Lino Capolicchio, Stefania Casini, Craig Hill, Massimo Serato, Juliette Mayniel
Keywords: giallo

Overview: A murder mystery set on an island near Venice. A schoolgirl was murdered seven years ago, and the case was never solved; now, the murderer seems to be back.
