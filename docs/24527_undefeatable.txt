Title: Undefeatable (1993)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Godfrey Ho
Main Cast: Cynthia Rothrock, Don Niam, Donna Jason, Sunny David, Emille Davazac
Keywords: martial arts, obsession, cop, serial murder, psychologist, mental illness, streetfighting, waiting, bills

Overview: The film follows Kristi Jones (Cynthia Rothrock) who, along with her gang, take part in Mafia-run street fights to earn money for her sister's college education. Kristi's sister hopes to become a doctor and pay for Kristi's education.
