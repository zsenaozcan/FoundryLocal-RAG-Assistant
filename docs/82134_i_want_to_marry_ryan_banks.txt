Title: I Want to Marry Ryan Banks (2004)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Sheldon Larry
Main Cast: Jason Priestley, Bradley Cooper, Emma Caulfield, Mark L. Walberg, Paige Jones

Overview: Ryan Banks's manager and old friend, Todd, comes up with the idea to have Ryan be the bachelor on a reality dating show in order to clean up his image. The only problem is, Todd falls in love with Charlie, the girl Ryan has chosen to propose to.
