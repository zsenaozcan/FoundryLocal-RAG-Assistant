Title: Dawn of the Dead (1978)
Genres: Horror
Director: George A. Romero
Main Cast: David Emge, Ken Foree, Scott H. Reiniger, Gaylen Ross, David Crawford
Keywords: helicopter, news broadcast, materialism, machete, bite, chaos, martial law, state of emergency, rock gang, infection

Overview: During an ever-growing epidemic of zombies that have risen from the dead, two Philadelphia SWAT team members, a traffic reporter, and his television-executive girlfriend seek refuge in a secluded shopping mall.
