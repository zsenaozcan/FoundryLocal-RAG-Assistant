Title: Accepted (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Steve Pink
Main Cast: Justin Long, Jonah Hill, Blake Lively, Adam Herschman, Columbus Short
Keywords: hot dog costume, electro shock, fake school, strait jacket, croquet, sprinkler system
