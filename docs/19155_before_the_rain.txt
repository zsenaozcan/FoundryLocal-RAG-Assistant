Title: Before the Rain (1994)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Milčo Mančevski
Main Cast: Katrin Cartlidge, Rade Serbedzija, Grégoire Colin, Labina Mitevska, Phyllida Law
Keywords: monk, mountain, restaurant, war photographer, christian, fashion photographer, girl, violence, albanian, macedonia

Overview: The circularity of violence seen in a story that circles on itself. In Macedonia, during war in Bosnia, Christians hunt an ethnic Albanian girl who may have murdered one of their own. A young monk who's taken a vow of silence offers her protection. In London, a photographic editor who's pregnant needs to talk it out with her estranged husband and chooses a toney restaurant.
