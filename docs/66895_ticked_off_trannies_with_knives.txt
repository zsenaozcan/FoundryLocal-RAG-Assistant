Title: Ticked-Off Trannies with Knives (2010)
Genres: Horror
Director: Israel Luna
Main Cast: Krystal Summers, Willam Belli, Kelexis Davenport, Erica Andrews, Jenna Skyy

Overview: A group of trannies are violently bashed and left for dead. The surviving ladies regain consciousness, confidence, and courage ready to seek out revenge on the ones who attacked them.
