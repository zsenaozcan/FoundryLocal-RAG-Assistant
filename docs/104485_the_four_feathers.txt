Title: The Four Feathers (1929)
Genres: Drama, Adventure
Director: Merian C. Cooper
Main Cast: Richard Arlen, Fay Wray, Clive Brook, William Powell, Theodore von Eltz

Overview: An Englishman (Richard Arlen) fights in the Sudan after receiving white feathers of cowardice from his fiancee (Fay Wray) and friends.
