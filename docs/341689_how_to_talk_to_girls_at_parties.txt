Title: How to Talk to Girls at Parties (2017)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: John Cameron Mitchell
Main Cast: Elle Fanning, Nicole Kidman, Ruth Wilson, Matt Lucas, Mark Douglas

Overview: A couple of British 1970s teen-aged boys, Enn and Vic, go to a party to meet girls, only to find that the girls are very different from the boys' expectations.
