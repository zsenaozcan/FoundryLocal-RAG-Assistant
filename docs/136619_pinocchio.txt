Title: Pinocchio (2012)
Genres: Animation
Director: Enzo D'Alò
Main Cast: Gabriele Caprio, Rocco Papaleo, Paolo Ruffini, Maurizio Micheli, Lucio Dalla

Overview: Geppetto the carpenter carves the object of his hidden desires out of a log: a puppet that will keep him company and will be like a son to him. He will call him Pinocchio. Imagine his surprise when he discovers that the puppet moves, as if by magic, and is gifted with a life on its own.
