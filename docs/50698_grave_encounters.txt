Title: Grave Encounters (2011)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Colin Minihan
Main Cast: Sean Rogerson, Juan Riedinger, Ashleigh Gryzko, Mackenzie Gray, Arthur Corber
Keywords: camcorder, reality, insane asylum, paranormal, haunting, psychiatric hospital, ghost hunting, paranormal investigation, labyrinth, mental asylum

Overview: A crew from a paranormal reality television show lock themselves in a haunted psychiatric hospital. They search for evidence of paranormal activity as they shoot what ends up becoming their final episode.
