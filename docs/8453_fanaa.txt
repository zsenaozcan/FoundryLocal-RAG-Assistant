Title: Fanaa (2006)
Genres: Action, Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Kunal Kohli
Main Cast: Kajol, Aamir Khan, Rishi Kapoor, Kirron Kher, Shiney Ahuja
Keywords: blindness and impaired vision, new delhi india, musical, tourist guide, kashmir conflict

Overview: Zooni Ali Beg (Kajol) is a blind Kashmiri girl who travels without her parents for the first time with a dance troupe to Delhi to perform in a ceremony for independence day. On her journey, she meets Rehan Khan (Aamir Khan), a casanova and tour guide who flirts with her. Although her friends warn Zooni about him, she cannot resist falling in love with him and he takes her on a private tour of New Delhi. But there is more to Rehan than meets the eye and Zooni will have to make a heartbreaking decision.
