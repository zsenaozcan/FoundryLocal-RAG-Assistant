Title: Jane Eyre (1996)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Franco Zeffirelli
Main Cast: Anna Paquin, Charlotte Gainsbourg, William Hurt, Joan Plowright, Nic Knight
Keywords: aunt, gentleman

Overview: Jane Eyre is an orphan cast out as a young girl by her aunt, Mrs. Reed, and sent to be raised in a harsh charity school for girls. There she learns to be come a teacher and eventually seeks employment outside the school. Her advertisement is answered by the housekeeper of Thornfield Hall, Mrs. Fairfax.
