Title: Milton's Secret (2016)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Barnet Bain
Main Cast: Michelle Rodriguez, Mia Kirshner, Donald Sutherland, David Sutcliffe, Ella Ballentine

Overview: 11 year-old Milton Adams is growing up in an uncertain world. With his parents constantly stressing about their careers and finances, and the neighborhood bully tormenting him, Milton feels his whole world is in crisis. But when his Grandpa Howard comes to visit, he discovers anxiety about the past and worrying about the future only make things worse and prevent him from finding true happiness moment to moment.
