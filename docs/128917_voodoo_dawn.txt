Title: Voodoo Dawn (1991)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Steven Fierberg
Main Cast: Raymond St. Jacques, Theresa Merritt, Gina Gershon, Kirk Baily, Billy 'Sly' Williams

Overview: Group of immigrant Haitian farm workers tries to fight off an evil Haitian voodoo priest who tries to kill them & use their body parts to make up a zombie army.
