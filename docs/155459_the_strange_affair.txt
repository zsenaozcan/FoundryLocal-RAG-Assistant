Title: The Strange Affair (1968)
Genres: Drama
Director: David Greene
Main Cast: Michael York, Susan George, Jeremy Kemp, George A. Cooper, Jack Watson
Keywords: corruption, crime

Overview: When rookie P.C. Strange falls for an under aged girl, he is unknowingly compromised by a pair of pornographers. Meanwhile, seasoned Det. Pierce is out to catch mob boss Quince and soon both plots intertwine.
