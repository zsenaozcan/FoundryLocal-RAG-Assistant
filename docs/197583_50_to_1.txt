Title: 50 to 1 (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jim Wilson
Main Cast: Skeet Ulrich, Christian Kane, William Devane, Madelyn Deutch, Todd Lowe
Keywords: underdog, horse race, horse, based on true story

Overview: A misfit group of New Mexico cowboys find themselves on the journey of a lifetime when they learn their crooked-footed racehorse qualifies to run in the Kentucky Derby. Based on the true story of Mine That Bird, the cowboys must overcome impossible odds even before they reach Churchill Downs and the land of Kentucky's blue bloods.
