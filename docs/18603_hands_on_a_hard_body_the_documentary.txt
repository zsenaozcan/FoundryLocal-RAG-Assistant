Title: Hands on a Hard Body: The Documentary (1997)
Genres: Documentary
Director: S.R. Bindler
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: competition, texas, documentary, contest, endurance, pickup truck

Overview: Hands on a Hard Body: The Documentary is a 1997 film documenting an endurance competition that took place in Longview, Texas. The yearly competition pits twenty-four contestants against each other to see who can keep their hand on a pickup truck for the longest amount of time. Whoever endures the longest without leaning on the truck or squatting wins the truck.
