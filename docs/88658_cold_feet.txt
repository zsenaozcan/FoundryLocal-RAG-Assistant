Title: Cold Feet (1989)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Robert Dornhelm
Main Cast: Keith Carradine, Sally Kirkland, Tom Waits, Bill Pullman, Rip Torn

Overview: The leader of a group of misfits, his girlfriend and an assassin have devised a bizarre plan to smuggle stolen jewelry over the US/Mexican border. They implant the jewelry in a racing horse.
