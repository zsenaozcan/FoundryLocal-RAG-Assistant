Title: The Devil in a Convent (1899)
Genres: Horror, Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Georges Méliès
Main Cast: Georges Méliès
Keywords: french, religion, devil, silent film, nuns, georges melies

Overview: A priest is officiating at a convent, when suddenly he is transformed into the devil, who frightens away the nuns and turns the place into a outlet of Pandemonium.
