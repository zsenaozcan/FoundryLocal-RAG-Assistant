Title: Short Term 12 (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Destin Cretton
Main Cast: Brie Larson, John Gallagher Jr., Kaitlyn Dever, Rami Malek, Keith Stanfield
Keywords: child abuse, suicide attempt, social worker, based on short film, pregnancy, violence, parole, father daughter relationship, incest, foster child

Overview: Grace, a compassionate young supervisor at a foster care facility, works with her boyfriend and colleague, Mason, to help at-risk teens. But when a new charge dredges up memories of her own troubled past, Grace's tough exterior begins eroding.
