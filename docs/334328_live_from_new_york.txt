Title: Live from New York! (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary
Director: Bao Nguyen
Main Cast: Tom Broecker, Tina Fey, Amy Poehler, Alec Baldwin, John Goodman
Keywords: saturday night live

Overview: Saturday Night Live has been reflecting and influencing life in the United States for forty years. LIVE FROM NEW YORK! goes deep inside this television phenomenon exploring the laughter that pulses through American politics, tragedy, and pop culture.
