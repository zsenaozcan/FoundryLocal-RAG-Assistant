Title: Motorway (2012)
Genres: Action, Crime
Director: Soi Cheang
Main Cast: Shawn Yue, Anthony Wong, Guo Xiaodong, Michelle Ye Xuan, Gordon Lam Ka-Tung

Overview: Two police pursuit drivers, a hothead rookie and his long-suffering, almost-retired mentor, face off against an escape car driver from the latter's past.
