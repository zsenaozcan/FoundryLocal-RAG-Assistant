Title: 7:19 (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Jorge Michel Grau
Main Cast: Demián Bichir, Héctor Bonilla, Carmen Beato, Octavio Michel, Azalia Ortiz

Overview: At 7:19 a.m., on September 19th of 1985, the most destructive earthquake hit Mexico's City. Inside what's left of a building, a group of survivors fight for their lives waiting for rescue.
