Title: Big Brown Eyes (1936)
Genres: Comedy, Mystery
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: Cary Grant, Joan Bennett, Walter Pidgeon, Lloyd Nolan, Alan Baxter
Keywords: jewel theft, manicurist, police reporter

Overview: Sassy manicurist Eve Fallon is recruited as an even more brassy reporter and she helps police detective boyfriend Danny Barr break a jewel theft ring and solve the murder of a baby.
