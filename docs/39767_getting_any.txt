Title: Getting Any? (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Foreign
Director: Takeshi Kitano
Main Cast: Dankan, Moeko Ezawa, Hakuryu, Hideo Higashikokubaru, Akiji Kobayashi

Overview: A slapstick comedy, about a goofy middle-aged man who is obsessed with the idea of having car sex. While pursuing his 'dream', he makes all the wrong moves possible, and ends up enrolling in a number of crazy escapades.
