Title: Crossfire Trail (2001)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Thriller, Western, Action
Director: Simon Wincer
Main Cast: Tom Selleck, Virginia Madsen, David O'Hara, Christian Kane, Ken Pogue

Overview: Rafe Covington is as good as his word, and he's determined to keep his promise to a dying man that he'll look after the man's widow and Wyoming ranch. But the widow doubts the integrity of drifter Covington. And an unscrupulous land grabber and his gunmen are sizing up the ranch the way a spider eyes a fly.
