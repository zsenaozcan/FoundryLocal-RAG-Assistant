Title: All of My Heart (2015)
Genres: Romance, TV Movie
Director: Peter DeLuise
Main Cast: Lacey Chabert, Brennan Elliott, Ed Asner, Greyston Holt, Daniel Cudmore
Keywords: roommate, romantic comedy, inheritance, opposites attract, home renovation

Overview: A young caterer’s life suddenly changes course when she inherits a country home and learns she must share it with a career obsessed Wall Street trader. At first, these opposites do not attract, but feelings begin to change when they find themselves having to work side-by-side to restore their newly acquired home.
