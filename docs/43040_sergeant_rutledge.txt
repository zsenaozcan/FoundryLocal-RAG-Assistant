Title: Sergeant Rutledge (1960)
Genres: Crime, Western
Director: John Ford
Main Cast: Jeffrey Hunter, Woody Strode, Constance Towers, Billie Burke, Juano Hernández
Keywords: rape, court martial

Overview: Respected black cavalry Sergeant Brax Rutledge stands court-martial for raping and killing a white woman and murdering her father, his superior officer.
