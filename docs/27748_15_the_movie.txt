Title: 15: The Movie (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama, Foreign
Director: Royston Tan
Main Cast: Melvin Chen, Erick Chun, Melvin Lee, Vynn Soh, Shaun Tan
Keywords: drug dealer, drug smuggle, self-inflicted injury, friendship, piercing, drug scene, young prostitute, misfit, controversial, gang

Overview: Fast, frenetic, and furious best describe the story of five teenage boys all but abandoned by the system, estranged from any parents, and discarded by life in general.  They build a world of there own in which gangs, drugs, fighting, body piercing, self-harm, and even suicide are considered commonplace.  The film highlights their harrowing place in time and this small world; where brotherhood is valued above all else.  Impressively acted by actual street kids, the movie highlights a gritty side of modern-day Singaporean life.
