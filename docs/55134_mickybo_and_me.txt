Title: Mickybo and Me (2005)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Terry Loane
Main Cast: Adrian Dunbar, Ciarán Hinds, Gina McKee, Julie Walters, John Joe McNeill
Keywords: based on play or musical, belfast

Overview: The film tells the story of two boys who become friends at the start of the Troubles in 1970. The boys share an obsession with Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid, with the consequence that they run away to Australia.
