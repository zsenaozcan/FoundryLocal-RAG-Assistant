Title: Valentin and Valentina (1985)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Georgi Natanson
Main Cast: Marina Zudina, Nikolay Stotskiy, Tatyana Doronina, Nina Ruslanova, Zinaida Dekhtyaryova

Overview: Valentin and Valentina love each other and want to marry. But their mothers are strongly against this marriage. So strong is their hate that they can use any possible means.
