Title: Drone Wars (2016)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Jack Perez
Main Cast: Corin Nemec, Jude Lanston, Jake Allyn, Jolene Andersen, Paige Lauren Billiot
Keywords: invasion, alien, drone, los angeles, alien invasion
