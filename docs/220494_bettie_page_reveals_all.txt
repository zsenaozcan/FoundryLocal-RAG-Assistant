Title: Bettie Page Reveals All (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Mark Mori
Main Cast: Bettie Page, Dita Von Teese, Hugh M. Hefner, Rebecca Romijn, Tricia Helfer
Keywords: biography, pin-up

Overview: The world's greatest pinup model and cult icon, Bettie Page, recounts the true story of how her free expression overcame government witch-hunts to help launch America's sexual revolution.
