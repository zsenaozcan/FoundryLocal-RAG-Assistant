Title: Whistle Down the Wind (1961)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Bryan Forbes
Main Cast: Alan Bates, Hayley Mills, Bernard Lee, Norman Bird, Diane Clare
Keywords: village life, kids and family, fugitive, farming, wife murderer, religon
