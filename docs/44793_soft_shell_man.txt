Title: Soft Shell Man (2001)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: André Turpin
Main Cast: David La Haye, Isabelle Blais, Emmanuel Bilodeau, Chantal Giroux, Pascale Desrochers
Keywords: girlfriend, travel, best friend, underwater

Overview: An emotionally immature underwater photographer returns home to an affair with his best friend's deaf girlfriend and unresolved issues with the wife he left six months before.
