Title: Tibetana (1969)
Genres: Drama, Adventure
Director: John Peyser
Main Cast: Pernell Roberts, Alexandra Bastedo, Gloria Cámara, Leonard Bell, Alejandro de Enciso
Keywords: independent film, chinese

Overview: One step ahead of Chinese communists, a couple of friends hook up with an American adventurer who promises to lead them safely out of Tibet through the Himalayas.
