Title: Comforting Skin (2011)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Fantasy, Horror
Director: Derek Franson
Main Cast: Victoria Bidewell, Tygh Runyan, Jane Sowerby, Philip Granger, Jenn Griffin
Keywords: suicide, tattoo, sex, nudity, supernatural, independent film

Overview: A lonely young woman's desperate need for emotional and sexual companionship draws her into a surreal and ultimately destructive relationship with a shifting and whispering tattoo she has willed to life on her skin.
