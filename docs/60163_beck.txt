Title: Beck (2010)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Yukihiko Tsutsumi
Main Cast: Hiro Mizushima, Kenta Kiritani, Aoi Nakamura, Osamu Mukai, Takeru Satoh

Overview: Yukio is your average high school student who spends his days going to class and avoiding bullies. That is until he meets Ryusuke, a guitar prodigy. Together with Taira, Chiba, and Saku they form the rock band BECK. As their popularity increases so does the danger as Ryusuke's past catches up with him.
