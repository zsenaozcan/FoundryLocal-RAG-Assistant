Title: No Path Through Fire (1968)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Gleb Panfilov
Main Cast: Inna Churikova, Mikhail Kononov, Анатолій Солоніцин, Mikhail Gluzskiy, Maya Bulgakova

Overview: A talented girl is trying to find happiness amidst the Russian revolution of 1917 and the civil war that split the nation.
