Title: Marvellous (2015)
Genres: Drama, TV Movie
Director: Julian Farino
Main Cast: Toby Jones, Gemma Jones, Tony Curran, Nicholas Gleaves, Greg McHugh
Keywords: drama, soccer, tv movie, intellectual disability

Overview: Dramatisation of the true story of the life of Neil "Nello" Baldwin.  Born with a mild learning disability but without the burden of social embarrassment &amp; how his inexhaustible ability to see the good in any situation overcame any stigma society tried to label him with.
