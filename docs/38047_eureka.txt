Title: Eureka (2000)
Genres: Drama
Director: Shinji Aoyama
Main Cast: Koji Yakusho, Aoi Miyazaki, Masaru Miyazaki, Yôichirô Saitô, Sayuri Kokushô
Keywords: serial killer

Overview: In Kyushu, southwest Japan, one hot summer morning, a municipal bus is hijacked. In the carnage only three people survive: the driver, a school girl, and her older brother.
