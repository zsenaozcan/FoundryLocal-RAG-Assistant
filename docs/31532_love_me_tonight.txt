Title: Love Me Tonight (1932)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Rouben Mamoulian
Main Cast: Maurice Chevalier, Jeanette MacDonald, Charles Ruggles, Charles Butterworth, Myrna Loy
Keywords: paris

Overview: A Parisian tailor finds himself posing as a baron in order to collect a sizeable bill from an aristocrat, only to fall in love with an aloof young princess.
