Title: Zeher (2005)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Mohit Suri
Main Cast: Emraan Hashmi, Ninad Kamat, Shamita Shetty, Udita Goswami, Sameer Kochhar
Keywords: adultery, infidelity

Overview: Police Inspector Sonia Mehra is married to her subordinate Siddharth, and both are employed by the Goa Police Force. They share a fairly harmonious relationship. Then one day, a woman named Anna Verghese goes missing and is believed dead. To add to this mystery, there is also the question of a suitcase full of cash that is also missing.
