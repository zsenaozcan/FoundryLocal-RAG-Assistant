Title: The Young Poisoner's Handbook (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Benjamin Ross
Main Cast: Tobias Arnold, Ruth Sheen, Roger Lloyd Pack, Hugh O'Conor, Norman Caro
Keywords: london england, poison, based on true story, murder, serial killer, mental institution, teenager, psychiatrist, stepmother, social misfit

Overview: A sinister tale of genius gone wrong, The Young Poisoner's Handbook chronicles a young man's descent into madness against the absurd backdrop of suburban English life. Hugh O'Conor plays Graham Young, a schoolboy from the London suburbs whose deadly obsession with toxic substances causes him to dabble in experimental murder.
