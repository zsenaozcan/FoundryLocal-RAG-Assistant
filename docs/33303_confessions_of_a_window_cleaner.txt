Title: Confessions of a Window Cleaner (1974)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Val Guest
Main Cast: Robin Askwith, Tony Booth, Sheila White, Linda Hayden, Dandy Nichols
Keywords: british

Overview: Young Timmy starts as a window cleaner in the little company of his brother. Soon he learns that some female customers expect additional service. Young and curious as he is, he reluctantly accepts the juicy duty. However his heart belongs to Liz, who demands the highest commitment until she lets him go all the way.
