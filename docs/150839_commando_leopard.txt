Title: Commando Leopard (1985)
Genres: Action, War
Director: Antonio Margheriti
Main Cast: Lewis Collins, Klaus Kinski, Cristina Donadio, Manfred Lehmann, John Steiner

Overview: A cruel dictator rules a Latin American state. Corruption, brutality and exploitation are present every day. A few people begin to organise resistance. Under the leader "El Leopardo" a small group of guerillas fights against the violent government.
