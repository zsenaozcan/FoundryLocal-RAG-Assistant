Title: The Hitch Hikers Guide to the Galaxy (1981)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Alan J.W. Bell
Main Cast: Peter Jones, Simon Jones, David Dixon, Sandra Dickinson, Mark Wing-Davey

Overview: An Earth Man and his alien friend escape an exploding Earth, and set forth on an odd adventure across the universe with a known fugitive. Based on the book by Douglas Adams, the dialog follows the book closely. This was originally a 6-part series from the BBC
