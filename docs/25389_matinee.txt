Title: Matinee (1993)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Joe Dante
Main Cast: John Goodman, Cathy Moriarty, Simon Fenton, Omri Katz, Lisa Jakub
Keywords: florida, cold war, movie theater, premiere, bomb shelter, film in film

Overview: A showman introduces a small coastal town to a unique movie experience and capitalises on the Cuban Missile crisis hysteria with a kitschy horror extravaganza combining film effects, stage props and actors in rubber suits in this salute to the B-movie.
