Title: Just Ask My Children (2001)
Genres: Drama, TV Movie
Director: Arvin Brown
Main Cast: Virginia Madsen, Jeffrey Nordling, Graham Beckel, Robert Joy, Barbara Tarbuck
Keywords: based on true story

Overview: Based on the true story of a modern-day witch hunt, in which a couple is falsely accused and imprisoned for molesting their own children. Twelve years later, the couple's children come forward with the truth that they were coerced by the police to testify against their parents.
