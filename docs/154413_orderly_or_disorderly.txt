Title: Orderly or Disorderly (1981)
Genres: Drama
Director: Abbas Kiarostami
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: chaos, stability

Overview: A sense of order is the necessary basis of any good social organisation. To illustrate this axiom, Kiarostami presents a series of paired scenes in this educational short film in which the same action is first shown in an organised way and then in an anarchic one. The film crew, however, finds it difficult to organise disorder.
