Title: The Cool Ones (1967)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Gene Nelson
Main Cast: Roddy McDowall, Debbie Watson, Gil Peterson, Phil Harris, Robert Coote
Keywords: dancer, cable car, teen idol

Overview: A young, millionaire rock promoter creates a new boy/girl team for his teen TV dance show. Will the ambitious go-go dancer and has-been pop star fall in love for real?
