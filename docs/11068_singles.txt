Title: Singles (1992)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Cameron Crowe
Main Cast: Bridget Fonda, Campbell Scott, Kyra Sedgwick, Sheila Kelley, Jim True-Frost
Keywords: free love, lovesickness, roommate, cheating, ex-boyfriend, ex-girlfriend, love of one's life, seattle, blind date, grunge

Overview: Romantic comedy about six of Seattle's young people, most of whom live in the same apartment building and whose lives revolve around the city's ever-expanding music scene. The interrelated stories about each character's progress through the singles scene are intriguing and often very funny, and the soundtrack is a grunge fanatic's dream, with the likes of Soundgarden, Pearl Jam and Mudhoney.
