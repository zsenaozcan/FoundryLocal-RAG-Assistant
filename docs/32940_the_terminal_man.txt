Title: The Terminal Man (1974)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Mike Hodges
Main Cast: George Segal, Joan Hackett, Richard Dysart, Donald Moffat, Michael C. Gwynne
Keywords: mind control

Overview: As the result of a head injury, brilliant computer scientist Harry Benson begins to experience violent seizures. In an attempt to control the seizures, Benson undergoes a new surgical procedure in which a microcomputer is inserted into his brain. The procedure is not entirely successful.
