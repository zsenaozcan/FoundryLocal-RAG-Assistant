Title: The Rock-afire Explosion (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Brett Whitcomb
Main Cast: Chris Thrash, Aaron Fechter, Travis Schafer, Mike Scherpenberg, Damon E. Breland

Overview: "The Rock-afire Explosion" is the story of a small-town disc-jockey, a struggling inventor, and an animatronic rock band, that quickly becomes an eccentric portrait of childhood memories, broken dreams, and the resilience of the human spirit.
