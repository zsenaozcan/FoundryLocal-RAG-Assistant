Title: Sister Helen (2002)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Rebecca Cammisa
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In this emotionally compelling documentary, Sister Helen opens a private home for recovering addicts and alcoholics in the South Bronx after the death of her husband and two sons. The film's fly-on-the-wall technique succeeds in capturing the day-to-day existence of Sister Helen and the various residents of the home.
