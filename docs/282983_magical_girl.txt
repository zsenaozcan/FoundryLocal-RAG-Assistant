Title: Magical Girl (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Carlos Vermut
Main Cast: Bárbara Lennie, José Sacristán, Luis Bermejo, Lucía Pollán, Javier Botet
Keywords: thriller

Overview: A desperate man (Luis Bermejo) blackmails a mentally ill woman (Bárbara Lennie) to buy a dress for his terminally ill daughter (Lucía Pollán).
