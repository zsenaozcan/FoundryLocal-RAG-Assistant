Title: The Sleeping Beauty (2010)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Foreign
Director: Catherine Breillat
Main Cast: Carla Besnaïnou, Julia Artamonov, Kerian Mayan, David Chausse, Luna Charpentier
Keywords: prince, woman director

Overview: On the heels of the mesmeric “Bluebeard” comes this wonderfully idiosyncratic, heady and erotic exploration of another classic Charles Perrault fairy tale by French cinema’s endlessly probing and brilliant provocateuse. Time-tripping with a truly precocious heroine, we follow a once-cursed six-year-old princess as she awakens a century later at the ripe old age of 16.
