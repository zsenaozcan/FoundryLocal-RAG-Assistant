Title: This Way Up (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Animation
Director: Adam Foulkes
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: funeral, dead body, casket, funeral director, funeral home, funeral parlor

Overview: A.T Shank &amp; Son have a bad day at the parlor when a falling boulder flattens their hearse. Emotional and literal pitfalls lie in wait for the odd couple as they make their way cross country with just a coffin for company. This short animated caper puts the fun back into funeral as their journey and relationship unravel on an epic scale.
