Title: Ohyaku: The Female Demon (1968)
Genres: Action
Director: Yoshihiro Ishikawa
Main Cast: Junko Miyazono, Tomisaburō Wakayama, Kunio Murai, Hôsei Komatsu, Gannosuke Ashiya

Overview: Ohyaku is a grifter who falls in love with a thief, who recruits her for his plan to rob a shipment of government gold.  When this plan goes wrong and Ohyaku ends up in prison, she resolves to do anything to escape and claim revenge on the ones who betrayed her and her lover.
