Title: Jimmy the Gent (1934)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: James Cagney, Bette Davis, Allen Jenkins, Alan Dinehart, Alice White
Keywords: assumed identity, racketeer, heir to fortune, sham marriage, unethical business, chiseler, competing businesses

Overview: An unpolished racketeer, whose racket is finding heirs for unclaimed fortunes, affects ethics and tea-drinking manners to win back the sweetheart who now works for his seemingly upright competitor.
