Title: A Perfect Couple (1979)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Robert Altman
Main Cast: Paul Dooley, Marta Heflin, Titos Vandis, Belita Moreno, Henry Gibson

Overview: Offbeat but endearing romantic comedy about unlikely match-up (by computer dating) of straitlaced Dooley, under the thumb of overbearing father, and singer Heflin, whose life is wrapped up with her familial rock group.
