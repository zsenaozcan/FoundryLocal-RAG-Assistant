Title: TekWar: TekJustice (1994)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Jerry Ciccoritti
Main Cast: Greg Evigan, Eugene Clark, Torri Higginson, William Shatner, Sandahl Bergman
Keywords: futuristic, tv movie

Overview: 21st Century detective Jake Cardigan is arrested for murdering the husband of his ex-wife. It seems like a frame up, yet Cardigan refuses to let the authorities investigate further or even deny any involvement in the killing.
