Title: Bangkok Adrenaline (2009)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Raimund Huber
Main Cast: Daniel O'Neill, Priya Suandokemai, Gwion Jacob Miles, Conan Stevens, Raimund Huber

Overview: Four backpackers arrive in Thailand to party and drink. A gambling game goes wrong and with their lives on the line they desperately decide to kidnap a billionaires daughter. Things go wrong when her 'father' doesn't play ball and prefers to use the kidnapping to further his own interests.
