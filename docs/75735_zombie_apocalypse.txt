Title: Zombie Apocalypse (2011)
Genres: Action, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Nick Lyon
Main Cast: Ving Rhames, Taryn Manning, Lesley-Ann Brandt, Eddie Steeples, Johnny Pacar
Keywords: tv movie, zombie apocalypse, virus
