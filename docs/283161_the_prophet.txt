Title: The Prophet (2014)
Genres: Animation
Director: Roger Allers
Main Cast: Salma Hayek, Liam Neeson, John Krasinski, Alfred Molina, Frank Langella
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Exiled artist and poet Mustafa embarks on a journey home with his housekeeper and her daughter; together the trio must evade the authorities who fear that the truth in Mustafa's words will incite rebellion.
