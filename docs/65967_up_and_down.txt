Title: Up and Down (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Foreign
Director: Jan Hřebejk
Main Cast: Petr Forman, Jan Tříska, Emília Vášáryová, Nataša Burger, Ingrid Timková

Overview: Milan and Goran are two criminals who smuggle illegal immigrants. One night after they complete a smuggle, they discover that one of the immigrants has left a baby behind. Milan and Goran decide to sell the baby to Lubos and Eman, who are responsible for running an illegal baby adoption center. Lubos and Eman make attempts to sell the baby to Miluska and Frantisek, a barren couple.
