Title: Anatomy of a Murder (1959)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Otto Preminger
Main Cast: James Stewart, Lee Remick, Ben Gazzara, Arthur O'Connell, Eve Arden
Keywords: rape, adultery, jealousy, michigan, judge, hays code, jazz, jurors, court case, lawyer

Overview: The film pits a humble small-town lawyer against a hard-headed big city prosecutor. Emotions flare as a jealous army lieutenant pleads innocent to murdering the rapist of his seductive, beautiful wife.
