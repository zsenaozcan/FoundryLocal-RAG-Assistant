Title: Cartagena (2009)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Alain Monne
Main Cast: Sophie Marceau, Rodolfo De Souza, Margarita Rosa de Francisco, Linnett Hernandez Valdes, Christopher Lambert

Overview: Muriel is beautiful, free-spirited and bed-ridden since a horrific accident. Leo is a drunk middle-aged ex-boxer. Desperate for work and unqualified, he interviews for Muriel, who hires him to cook and care for her against her better judgment. Initially out of his depth, Leo slowly wins Muriel's trust. As Muriel teaches him to read, he forces her to confront the joys beyond her window.
