Title: La Femme Nikita (1990)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Luc Besson
Main Cast: Anne Parillaud, Marc Duret, Patrick Fontana, Alain Lathière, Jean Reno
Keywords: secret identity, special unit, romance, government assassin

Overview: A beautiful felon, sentenced to life in prison for the murder of a policeman, is given a second chance - as a secret political assassin controlled by the government.
