Title: Wings (1927)
Genres: Action, Drama, Romance, War
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: Clara Bow, Charles 'Buddy' Rogers, Richard Arlen, Gary Cooper, El Brendel
Keywords: paris, world war i, friendship, battlefield, aerial combat, warfare, silent film, u.s. military, fighter pilots

Overview: Two young men, one rich, one middle class, both in love with the same woman (Jobyna Ralston), become US Air Corps fighter pilots and, eventually, heroic flying aces during World War I. Devoted best friends, their mutual love of the girl eventually threatens their bond. Meanwhile, a hometown girl (Clara Bow) who's the love struck lifelong next door neighbor of one of them, pines away.
