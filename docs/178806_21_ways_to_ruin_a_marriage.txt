Title: 21 Ways to Ruin a Marriage (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Johanna Vuoksenmaa
Main Cast: Pamela Tola, Aku Hirviniemi, Vesa Vierikko, Mari Perankoski, Jarkko Niemi
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The film follows Sanna, who sets out to document the countless ways there are to ruin a marriage for her doctoral thesis.
