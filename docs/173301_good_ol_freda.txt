Title: Good Ol’ Freda (2013)
Genres: Documentary, History
Director: Ryan White
Main Cast: Freda Kelly, George Harrison, John Lennon, Paul McCartney, Ringo Starr
Keywords: musical, secretary, the beatles, rock band

Overview: The story of Freda Kelly, a shy Liverpudlian teenager asked to work for a young local band hoping to make it big: The Beatles. Their loyal secretary from beginning to end, Freda tells her tales for the first time in 50 years.
