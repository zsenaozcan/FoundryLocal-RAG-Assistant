Title: Botched (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Horror, Thriller
Director: Kit Ryan
Main Cast: Stephen Dorff, Sean Pertwee, Jamie Foreman, David Heap, Alan Smyth

Overview: Ritchie Donovan is a professional thief whose luck has just run out. The only survivor from a heist that goes terribly wrong, Ritchie is forced to take the rap and is sent to Russia to steal a priceless antique cross locked in a safe on the penthouse floor of a Moscow skyscraper.
