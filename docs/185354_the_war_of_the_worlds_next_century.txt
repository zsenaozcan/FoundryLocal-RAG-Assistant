Title: The War of the Worlds: Next Century (1981)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller, War
Director: Piotr Szulkin
Main Cast: Roman Wilhelmi, Krystyna Janda, Jerzy Stuhr
Keywords: police, martian, rock concert, martian invasion

Overview: Film opens on December 18, 1999, just a few days before the dawn of the new century. A local reporter, Iron Idem, announces that the Martians have landed. Shortly after that his program loses its independence: he is given the script telling the crowds how to welcome the invaders.
