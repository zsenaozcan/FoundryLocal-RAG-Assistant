Title: Buzzkill (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Steven Kampmann
Main Cast: Daniel Raymont, Krysten Ritter, Darrell Hammond, Reiko Aylesworth, Mike Starr
Keywords: karaoke, dark comedy, fame, writer, murderer, theft, script

Overview: Failed writer Ray Wyatt is ready to take his own life when a notorious serial murderer steals his latest movie script and begins publicizing it. Revived by the sudden deluge of celebrity, Ray starts tracking the killer to recover the manuscript.
