Title: The Prodigal (1955)
Genres: Drama
Director: Richard Thorpe
Main Cast: Lana Turner, Edmund Purdom, Louis Calhern, Audrey Dalton, James Mitchell
Keywords: faith, pagan

Overview: A wealthy young Hebrew traveling in Damascus renounces his faith after is seduced by an alluring pagan priestess and cheated of his fortune by the High Priest as well.
