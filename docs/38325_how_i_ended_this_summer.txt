Title: How I Ended This Summer (2010)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Aleksei Popogrebsky
Main Cast: Grigoriy Dobrygin, Sergey Puskepalis, Artyom Tsukanov, Igor Chernevich, Ilya Sobolev
Keywords: isolation, radiation, radio, metereologist, arctic, desolation, very little dialogue

Overview: Two men at a remote Arctic base begin mistrusting each other after an important radio message.
