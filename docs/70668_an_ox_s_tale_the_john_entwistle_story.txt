Title: An Ox's Tale: The John Entwistle Story (2006)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Glenn Aveni
Main Cast: John Entwistle, Pete Townshend, Roger Daltrey, Peter Frampton, Ray Manzarek

Overview: An intimate portrait of the legendary bass guitarist for The Who. This documentary includes the last footage ever recorded with John Entwistle, and features interviews with Pete Townshend, as well as other major figures in rock history.
