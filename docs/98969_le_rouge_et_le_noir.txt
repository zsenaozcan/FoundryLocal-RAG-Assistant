Title: Le rouge et le noir (1954)
Genres: Drama
Director: Claude Autant-Lara
Main Cast: Gérard Philipe, Danielle Darrieux, Antonella Lualdi, Jean Mercure, Antoine Balpêtré

Overview: It's no holds barred for Julian in pursuit of upward mobility. Although expected to channel career aspirations into the Church of the post-Napoleonic era, his intensely romantic liaisons propel him forward at a pace he cannot control.
