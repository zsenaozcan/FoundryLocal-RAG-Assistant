Title: Tell Me a Riddle (1980)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lee Grant
Main Cast: Melvyn Douglas, Lila Kedrova, Brooke Adams, Dolores Dorn, Bob Elross
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Touching story of elderly couple David and Eva who go on one last journey across the USA when they discover Eva is dying, ending up with their granddaughter Jeannie in San Francisco.
