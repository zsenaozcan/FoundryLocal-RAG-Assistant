Title: Enid Is Sleeping (1991)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Maurice Phillips
Main Cast: Elizabeth Perkins, Judge Reinhold, Jeffrey Jones
Keywords: sister sister relationship, infidelity, new mexico, dark comedy, shootout, police officer, car explosion

Overview: Enid only looks like she's sleeping. In fact, she's dead. Shortly after Enid caught her husband Harry in bed with her sister June, a row ensued, whereupon June accidently killed Enid. June's problem now is to hide the truth from the authorities, including her police-officer husband Floyd. Then she decides to reveal Enid's demise, albeit rearranging the damning evidence to make the whole thing look like the accident it really was.
