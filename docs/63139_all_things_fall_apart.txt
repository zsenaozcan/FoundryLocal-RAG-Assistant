Title: All Things Fall Apart (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mario Van Peebles
Main Cast: Ray Liotta, 50 Cent, Mario Van Peebles, Lynn Whitfield, Ambyr Childers
Keywords: sport

Overview: 50 Cent stars as gifted college running back Deon in this touching drama. Deon's world turns upside down when an unexpected crisis jeopardizes his professional ambitions -- and causes him to look at himself and his life in a new way.
