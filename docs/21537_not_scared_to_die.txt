Title: Not Scared to Die (1973)
Genres: Action, Drama, History
Director: Chu Mu
Main Cast: Wong Ching, Jackie Chan, Yuen Qiu, Alex Lung, Chiang Nan
Keywords: japan, china, martial arts, world war ii, japanese occupation

Overview: Historical movie set during the Japanese occupation of China during WWII. Jackie Chan is one of the good guys but has nothing much more than a supporting role.
