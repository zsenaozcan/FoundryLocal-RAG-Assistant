Title: Summer of Sam (1999)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Crime, Romance
Director: Spike Lee
Main Cast: John Leguizamo, Adrien Brody, Mira Sorvino, Jennifer Esposito, Anthony LaPaglia
Keywords: distrust, intolerance, punk rock, italian american, summer, bigotry, bronx

Overview: Spike Lee's take on the "Son of Sam" murders in New York City during the summer of 1977 centering on the residents of an Italian-American South Bronx neighborhood who live in fear and distrust of one another.
