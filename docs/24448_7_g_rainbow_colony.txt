Title: 7/G Rainbow Colony (2004)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: K. Selvaraghavan
Main Cast: Ravi Krishna, Sonia Agarwal, Vijayan, Suman Shetty, Sudha
Keywords: tragic love, interracial love, unemployed

Overview: Kathir is a young underachiever, frequently venting his frustrations through public outbursts. When a north Indian girl, Anitha, moves into his neighborhood, he falls head over heels in love with her, and aggressively expresses interest. When she does not respond favorably, things get out of hand.
