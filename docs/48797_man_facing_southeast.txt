Title: Man Facing Southeast (1986)
Genres: Mystery, Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Eliseo Subiela
Main Cast: Lorenzo Quinteros, Hugo Soto, Inés Vernengo, Cristina Scaramuzza, Tomas Voth
Keywords: insane asylum, insanity, psychiatric hospital, psychological thriller, psychiatry, telekenesis

Overview: A new patient mysteriously appears in a psychiatric ward. He claims to come from another planet to study humans and their behavior. The alien is gentle but criticizes humans for their harsh treatment of each other. The assigned psychiatrist is himself unhappy, and affected by the patient's insight. But he is ordered to treat the patient according to institutional procedure.
