Title: Dog Days (2001)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ulrich Seidl
Main Cast: Maria Hofstätter, Alfred Mrva, Franziska Weisz, Christine Jirku, Viktor Hennemann
Keywords: alcohol, sex, jealousy, new love, suburbian idyll, heat, longing, bad luck, fate, unsimulated sex

Overview: In a suburb of Vienna during some hot summer days: A teacher who is in bondage to a sleazy pimp, a very importunate hitchhiker, a private detective on the run for some car vandals, a couple with a serious marriage problem and an old man, whose wife died long before on the search for some sexual entertainment live their lives while their lifelines cross from time to time.
