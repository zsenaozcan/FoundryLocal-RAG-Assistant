Title: Air Raid Wardens (1943)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Edward Sedgwick
Main Cast: Stan Laurel, Oliver Hardy, Edgar Kennedy, Jacqueline White, Stephen McNally
Keywords: sabotage, laurel and hardy

Overview: Two bumblers, failures as businessmen and air raid wardens, stumble across a nest of Nazi saboteurs bent on blowing up the local magnesium plant.
