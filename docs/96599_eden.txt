Title: Eden (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Megan Griffiths
Main Cast: Jamie Chung, Beau Bridges, Matt O'Leary, Tantoo Cardinal, Naama Kates
Keywords: slavery, human trafficking, moral ambiguity, prostitution, woman director

Overview: The true story of Chong Kim who, as a young teen, was abducted into the sex trade, and the complicated moral choices she had to make in order to survive as her situation grew more desperate.
