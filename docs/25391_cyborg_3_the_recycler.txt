Title: Cyborg 3: The Recycler (1995)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Michael Schroeder
Main Cast: Zach Galligan, Khrystyne Haje, Richard Lynch, Andrew Bryniarski, Malcolm McDowell
Keywords: community, battle, motorcycle gang, forcefield

Overview: Prepare yourself for the all too deadly future. Cash, the heroine of Cyborg 2, is living safe in the free zone. But not for long. Biomechanical problems are taking down her systems and a visit to a doctor in Silica confirms her worst fears. She is more then a marvel of cyborg technology. She is the first of her kind to become a creator-she is pregnant.
