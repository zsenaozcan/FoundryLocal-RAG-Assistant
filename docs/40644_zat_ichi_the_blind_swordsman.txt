Title: Zatôichi: The Blind Swordsman (1989)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Shintarô Katsu
Main Cast: Shintarô Katsu, Kanako Higuchi, Takanori Jinnai, Ryuutarô Gan, Yuya Uchida
Keywords: samurai, swordsman, samurai era, blind

Overview: This 26th film installment of the Zatoichi series sees Shintaro Katsu return to this classic role after a 10 year hiatus. The blind masseur Ichi gets caught between rival Yakuza clans.
