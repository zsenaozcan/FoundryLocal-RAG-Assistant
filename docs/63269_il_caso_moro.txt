Title: Il caso Moro (1986)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Giuseppe Ferrara
Main Cast: Gian Maria Volonté, Mattia Sbragia, Bruno Zanin, Consuelo Ferrara, Enrica Maria Modugno
Keywords: terrorism, italian politics

Overview: On March 16 1978, the Red Brigade kidnap the Chief of the Christian Democraty, the party in power in Italy since the end of the war. Fifty-five days later, his corpse was found in the trunk of a red Renault.
