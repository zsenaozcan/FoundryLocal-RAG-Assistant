Title: Thirteen Women (1932)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: George Archainbaud
Main Cast: Irene Dunne, Ricardo Cortez, Myrna Loy, Jill Esmond, Mary Duncan
Keywords: melodrama, schoolmates, swami, proto-slasher

Overview: Thirteen women who were schoolmates send to a swami for their horoscopes. Little do they realize that Ursula, a half-breed Asian, is using her hypnotic powers over the swami and them to lead them or their families to their deaths. It seems that she too went to their school, but was forced to leave by their bigotry, and is exacting revenge. Will she be stopped in time to save Laura's son, Bobby?
