Title: The Tunnel (2001)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Roland Suso Richter
Main Cast: Heino Ferch, Nicolette Krebitz, Sebastian Koch, Alexandra Maria Lara, Claudia Michelsen
Keywords: berlin, berlin wall, german democratic republic, border, german-german border, escape agent, east berlin, west berlin, escape, tunnel

Overview: Inspired by true events, Olympic swimmer Harry Melchior defects from East Germany in the 1960s and hatches a daring plot to help his sister and others flee East Berlin through a 145-yard underground tunnel.
