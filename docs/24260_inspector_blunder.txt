Title: Inspector Blunder (1980)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Claude Zidi
Main Cast: Coluche, Gérard Depardieu, Dominique Lavanant, Julien Guiomar, Richard Bohringer

Overview: A detective, fresh out of the academy (lowest mark in class), gets the assignment to bodyguard a spoiled rich girl reporter. She's been interviewed on TV and has questioned the virility of a mobster who kidnaps her later.
