Title: The North Avenue Irregulars (1979)
Genres: Family, Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Bruce Bilson
Main Cast: Barbara Harris, Susan Clark, Karen Valentine, Michael Constantine, Cloris Leachman
Keywords: minister, neighbor, gang, policeman

Overview: When crooks set up operations in a traditional town, a minister and a group of church ladies are willing to do anything, no matter how wacky, to get them out.
