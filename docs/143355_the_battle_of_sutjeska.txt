Title: The Battle of Sutjeska (1973)
Genres: War
Director: Stipe Delić
Main Cast: Richard Burton, Ljuba Tadić, Velimir „Bata“ Živojinović, Irene Papas, Milena Dravić
Keywords: yugoslavia, bosnia and herzegovina, world war ii, partisan

Overview: The film tells the story of the Battle of Sutjeska which occurred in Nazi-occupied Bosnia and Herzegovina in 1943.
