Title: A Bigger Splash (2015)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Luca Guadagnino
Main Cast: Tilda Swinton, Matthias Schoenaerts, Ralph Fiennes, Dakota Johnson, Aurore Clément
Keywords: italy, fish, rock star, vacation, rock concert
