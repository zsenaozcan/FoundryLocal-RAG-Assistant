Title: Casanova '70 (1965)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Mario Monicelli
Main Cast: Marcello Mastroianni, Virna Lisi, Marisa Mell, Michèle Mercier, Enrico Maria Salerno
Keywords: officer, nato, extramarital affair

Overview: The amorous adventures of Andrea Rossi-Colombotti, an army officer who finds pleasure with beautiful women in life-threatening situations.
