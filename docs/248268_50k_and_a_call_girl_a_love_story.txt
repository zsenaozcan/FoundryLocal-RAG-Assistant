Title: $50K and a Call Girl: A Love Story (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Seth Grossman
Main Cast: Ross Patterson, Jessie Wiseman, Seth Grossman, Lauren Aboulafia, Asher Roth

Overview: When Ross is diagnosed with terminal brain cancer and given six weeks to live, his newly engaged older brother Seth offers to spend his $50,000 wedding fund on a final trip of a lifetime. Their plans are complicated when Ross invites a call girl to join the group and Seth’s uptight fiancée insists on tagging along. This raucous road trip comedy features hip-hop star Asher Roth.
