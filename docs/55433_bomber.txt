Title: Bomber (2009)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Paul Cotter
Main Cast: Shane Taylor, Eileen Nicholas, Ulrike Arndt, Michael Asmussen, Emil Austermann
Keywords: germany, nightmare, son, road movie

Overview: An 83 year-old man returns to Germany for a long planned journey of atonement. When Ross, his useless son agrees to drive him there, a nightmare family road trip ensues.
