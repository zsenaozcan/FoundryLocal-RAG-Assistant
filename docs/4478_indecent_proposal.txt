Title: Indecent Proposal (1993)
Genres: Drama
Director: Adrian Lyne
Main Cast: Robert Redford, Demi Moore, Woody Harrelson, Seymour Cassel, Oliver Platt
Keywords: gambling, adultery, love of one's life, shortage of money, millionaire

Overview: Robert Redford stars as billionaire John Gage, who offers a down-on-his-luck yuppie husband (Woody Harrelson) $1 million for the opportunity to spend the night with the man's wife (Demi Moore).
