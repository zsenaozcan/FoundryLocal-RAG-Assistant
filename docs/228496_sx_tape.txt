Title: Sx_Tape (2014)
Genres: Horror
Director: Bernard Rose
Main Cast: Caitlyn Folley, Julie Marcus, Ian Duncan, Daniel Faraldo, Diana García
Keywords: supernatural, horror, subjective camera, found footage

Overview: From the director of CANDYMAN and the producers of PARANORMAL ACTIVITY comes a found-footage nightmare of lust, possession, and destruction. Jill's an artist. Ian's a filmmaker. And their love life is off the chain. There's no experience too wild, no dare too dangerous—not even when Jill lets Ian strap her to a gurney in the abandoned hospital they're scoping out for their next art show. But he shouldn't have left her alone. Not even as a joke. Now, Jill's hookup with horror has awakened something in that place. Something with a lust for more than flesh.
