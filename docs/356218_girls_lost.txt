Title: Girls Lost (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alexandra-Therese Keining
Main Cast: Tuva Jagell, Wilma Holmén, Louise Nyvall, Emrik Öhlander, Vilgot Ostwald Vesterlund
Keywords: coming of age, lgbt, woman director, gender identity

Overview: Kim, Bella and Momo are three fourteen-year-old girls who discover a fantastic flower with magic qualities: by drinking its nectar they are transformed into boys and they enter a new world. At first they enjoy their newly found freedom, but soon Bella and Momo realize that there are downsides to it. Kim however gets seriously addicted…
