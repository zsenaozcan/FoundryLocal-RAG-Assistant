Title: Forks Over Knives (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Lee Fulkerson
Main Cast: Lee Fulkerson, Matthew Lederman, Alona Pulde, T. Colin Campbell, Caldwell Esselstyn Jr.
Keywords: vegetarian, food

Overview: Examines the profound claim that most, if not all, of the degenerative diseases that afflict us can be controlled, or even reversed, by rejecting our present menu of animal-based and processed foods.
