Title: Baby Face (1933)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Alfred E. Green
Main Cast: Barbara Stanwyck, George Brent, Donald Cook, Alphonse Ethier, Henry Kolker
Keywords: paris, ambition, friedrich nietzsche, new york city, pre-code, speakeasy, golddigger
