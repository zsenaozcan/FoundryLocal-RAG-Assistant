Title: Mannequin Two: On the Move (1991)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Science Fiction, Romance
Director: Stewart Raffill
Main Cast: Kristy Swanson, William Ragsdale, Meshach Taylor, Terry Kiser, Stuart Pankin
Keywords: foreigner, department store, magic, mannequin, prince, hot air balloon, time travel, sword fight, chase scene, credit card

Overview: Although Jason works as a department store clerk, he is also a reincarnated prince. Long ago, his beloved Jessie was snatched away from him by an evil wizard who used his powers to transform her into wooden statue. Now Jessie is in Jason's department store as a mannequin. When he encounters her, she awakens from her thousand-year sleep. They quickly revive their romance, but the evil wizard has been reincarnated as well, and he's up to no good.
