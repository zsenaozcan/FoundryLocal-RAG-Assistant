Title: Jonathan Livingston Seagull (1973)
Genres: Drama, Family, Science Fiction
Director: Hall Bartlett
Main Cast: James Franciscus, Juliet Mills, Philip Ahn, David Ladd, Dorothy McGuire

Overview: Jonathan is sick and tired of the boring life in his sea-gull clan. He rather experiments with new, always more daring flying techniques. Since he doesn't fit in, the elders expel him from the clan. So he sets out to discover the world beyond the horizon in quest for wisdom.
