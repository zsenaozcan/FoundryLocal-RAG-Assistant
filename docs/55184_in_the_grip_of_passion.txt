Title: In the Grip of Passion (1947)
Genres: Drama
Director: Teuvo Tulio
Main Cast: Regina Linnanheimo, Kullervo Kalske, Eric Gustafson, Aku Peltonen, Elli Ylimaa
Keywords: forbidden love, alcoholism, unhappy marriage

Overview: A young woman is forced into an arranged marriage by her family in hopes of securing their future, but her husband turns out to be an alcoholic, brute of a man and she longs for the man she truly loves.
