Title: Thirty Day Princess (1934)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Marion Gering
Main Cast: Sylvia Sidney, Cary Grant, Edward Arnold, Henry Stephenson, Vince Barnett
Keywords: loan, impersonation

Overview: A European princess arrives in New York City to secure a much-needed loan for her country. She contracts the mumps, and an actress who looks exactly like her is hired to impersonate her.
