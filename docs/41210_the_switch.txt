Title: The Switch (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Drama
Director: Josh Gordon
Main Cast: Jason Bateman, Jennifer Aniston, Jeff Goldblum, Juliette Lewis, Todd Louiso
Keywords: alcohol, single parent, baby, restaurant, aquarium, sperm, little boy, male female relationship, pregnant, artificial insemination
