Title: The Bride Wore Black (1968)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Romance, Thriller, Crime
Director: François Truffaut
Main Cast: Jeanne Moreau, Michel Bouquet, Jean-Claude Brialy, Charles Denner, Claude Rich
Keywords: repayment, femme fatale, french noir

Overview: Julie Kohler is prevented from suicide by her mother. She leaves the town. She will track down, charm and kill five men who do not know her. What is her goal ? What is her purpose?
