Title: Under The Sign Of Scorpio (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Paolo Taviani
Main Cast: Gian Maria Volonté, Lucia Bosé, Giulio Brogi, Samy Pavel, Daniele Dublino
Keywords: island, volcano

Overview: An island has been decimated by a volcanic eruption and the few survivors escape to a nearby island. Led by Giulio Brogi, they know that their new home is equally susceptible to such a catastrophe via its own volcano, but they are unable to convince the current inhabitants of Island #2 to flee with them to a mainland.
