Title: Living on Love (1937)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Lew Landers
Main Cast: James Dunn, Whitney Bourne, Joan Woodbury, Franklin Pangborn, Solly Ward
Keywords: zebra, truck driver

Overview: A man and woman, who've never met, are forced by circumstances to share the same apartment. A remake of the 1933 film "Rafter Romance".
