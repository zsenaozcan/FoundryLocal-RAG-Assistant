Title: Battle Royale (2000)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Adventure
Director: Kinji Fukasaku
Main Cast: Takeshi Kitano, Tsuyako Kinoshita, Eri Ishikawa, Sayaka Ikeda, Tomomi Shimaki
Keywords: island, television, survivor, soldier, battle, death, game of death, school class

Overview: In the future, the Japanese government captures a class of ninth-grade students and forces them to kill each other under the revolutionary "Battle Royale" act.
