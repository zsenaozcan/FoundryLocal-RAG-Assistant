Title: Last Night At The Alamo (1984)
Genres: Drama
Director: Eagle Pennell
Main Cast: Sonny Carl Davis, Lou Perryman, Steven Mattila, Tina-Bess Hubbard, Amanda Lamar

Overview: The events on the last night in the existence of a little suburban Houston pub. It has to be closed down for development reasons but one of the regular customers, Cowboy, seems to have friends in high places.
