Title: Wild America (1997)
Genres: Family
Director: William Dear
Main Cast: Jonathan Taylor Thomas, Scott Bairstow, Devon Sawa, Frances Fisher, Danny Glover
Keywords: animal, naturalist

Overview: Three brothers - Marshall, Marty and Mark dream of becoming naturalists and portraying animal life of America. One summer their dream comes true, they travel through America, filming alligators, b  ears and moose.
