Title: They Were Expendable (1945)
Genres: Drama, Action, War
Director: John Ford
Main Cast: Robert Montgomery, John Wayne, Donna Reed, Jack Holt, Ward Bond
Keywords: pearl harbor, navy, philippines

Overview: Shortly after Pearl Harbor, a squadron of PT-boat crews in the Philippines must battle the Navy brass between skirmishes with the Japanese. The title says it all about the Navy's attitude towards the PT-boats and their crews.
