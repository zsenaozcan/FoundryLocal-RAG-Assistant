Title: Blood Rage (1987)
Genres: Horror
Director: John Grissmer
Main Cast: Louise Lasser, Mark Soper, Marianne Kanter, Julie Gordon, Jayne Bentzen
Keywords: machete, thanksgiving, insanity, psychopath, revenge, murder, drive-in theatre, serial killer, decapitation, slasher

Overview: As kids, Todd is institutionalized for a murder whilst his twin goes free. 10 years later, on Thanksgiving, Todd escapes and a killing spree begins in his neighborhood.
