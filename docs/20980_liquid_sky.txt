Title: Liquid Sky (1982)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy
Director: Slava Tsukerman
Main Cast: Anne Carlisle, Paula E. Sheppard, Susan Doukas, Otto von Wernherr, Bob Brady
Keywords: rape, sex, cocaine, heroin, bisexuality, punk, alien, androgyny, drug, fashion

Overview: Invisible aliens in a tiny flying saucer come to Earth looking for heroin. They land on top of a New York apartment inhabited by a drug dealer and her female, androgynous, bisexual nymphomaniac lover, a fashion model. The aliens soon find the human pheromones created in the brain during orgasm preferable to heroin, and the model's casual sex partners begin to disappear.
