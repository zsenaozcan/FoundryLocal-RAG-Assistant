Title: Churchill: The Hollywood Years (2004)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Peter Richardson
Main Cast: Christian Slater, Neve Campbell, Harry Enfield, Jon Culshaw, Leslie Phillips

Overview: A group of American executives making a film about World War II decide that since their lead is rather unglamorous, they will draft in an American G.I. to play the part of Winston Churchill. Their take on the war depicts a handsome Churchill falling in love with Princess Elizabeth, who is herself involved in the war as an undercover agent.
