Title: Daylight's End (2016)
Genres: Action, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: William Kaufman
Main Cast: Johnny Strong, Lance Henriksen, Louis Mandylor, Hakeem Kae-Kazim, Farah White
Keywords: plague, blood thirsty creatures

Overview: Years after a mysterious plague has devastated the planet and turned most of humanity into blood-hungry creatures, a rogue drifter on a vengeful hunt stumbles across a band of survivors in an abandoned police station and reluctantly agrees to try to help them defend themselves and escape to the sanctuary they so desperately need.
