Title: Roman Polanski: Wanted and Desired (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Marina Zenovich
Main Cast: Istvan Bajzat, Steve Barshop, Marilyn Beck, Madeline Bessmer, Pierre-André Boutang
Keywords: independent film, woman director

Overview: Examines the public scandal and private tragedy which led to legendary director Roman Polanski's sudden flight from the United States.
