Title: Sicarivs: The Night and the Silence (2015)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Thriller
Director: Javier Muñoz
Main Cast: Víctor Clavijo, Fernando Gil, Nahia Laiz, Israel Elejalde, María Cecilia Sánchez
Keywords: sicario

Overview: A hit man receives the assignment to kill a woman. But what seems as another job complicates when he is about to execute her, but does not. He will have to go after those who gave him the contract, for, in his profession, those who do not comply with their duty end up dead. But the question remains, why did he not kill the woman?
