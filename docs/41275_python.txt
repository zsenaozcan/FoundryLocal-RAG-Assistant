Title: Python (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Richard Clabaugh
Main Cast: Frayne Rosanoff, Robert Englund, Casper Van Dien, William Zabka, Dana Barron
Keywords: snake, airplane, scientist, military

Overview: Sleepy New Haven California is a small town with a big problem. A sixty foot slithering horror has arrived and shattered the town's tranquillity on it's path of death and destruction... Growing violent and more savage with each attack the gigantic creature soon becomes an unstoppable feeding machine raging beyond control of it's creator, leaving only the stripped bones of it's victims in it's wake.
