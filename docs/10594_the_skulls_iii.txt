Title: The Skulls III (2004)
Genres: Thriller
Director: J. Miles Dale
Main Cast: Clare Kramer, Barry Bostwick, Steve Braun, Maria del Mar, Len Cariou
Keywords: suspicion of murder, brotherhood, college, sexism, conspiracy of murder

Overview: A young college co-ed tries joining the elite, all-male, secret society, the Skulls, and in so doing, she uncovers some unscrupulous methods used by some of the members to get what they want.
