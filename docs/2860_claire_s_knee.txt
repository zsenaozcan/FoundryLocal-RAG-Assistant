Title: Claire's Knee (1970)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Éric Rohmer
Main Cast: Jean-Claude Brialy, Laurence de Monaghan, Béatrice Romand, Aurora Cornu, Michèle Montel
Keywords: knee, independent film, sexual desire

Overview: On the eve of his wedding, on holiday on the Lake Annecy shore, a career diplomat visits an old acquaintance, perhaps a former girlfriend. Through her he meets an intense teenager, Laura, and then lusts after her sister, Claire. Whilst Laura attempts to flirt with him, his fantasy becomes focused on wanting to caress Claire's knee.
