Title: Watermarks (2004)
Genres: History, Documentary, Foreign
Director: Yaron Zilberman
Main Cast: Hanni Lux, Judith Haspel, Anni Lampl, Elisheva Susz, Trude Hirschler

Overview: The story of the Hakoah Vienna Jewish womens swim team of the 1930s, their forced separation, and their reunion decades later.
