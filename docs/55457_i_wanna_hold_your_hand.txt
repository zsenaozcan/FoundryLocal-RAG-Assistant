Title: I Wanna Hold Your Hand (1978)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Robert Zemeckis
Main Cast: Nancy Allen, Bobby Di Cicco, Wendie Jo Sperber, Theresa Saldana, Marc McClure
Keywords: music, reference to the beatles, friend

Overview: If they missed Beatles' first appearance in the U.S.A. they would hate themselves for the rest of their lives! So six young girls from New Jersey set off even though they don't have tickets for the show! The journey is full of surprises and misfortunes but the young ladies are determined to reach their idols.
