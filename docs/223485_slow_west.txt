Title: Slow West (2015)

Main Cast: Michael Fassbender, Kodi Smit-McPhee, Ben Mendelsohn, Caren Pistorius, Rory McCann
Keywords: fire, bounty hunter, shotgun, horseback riding, horse, cabin, frontier, outlaw, rifle, unrequited love
