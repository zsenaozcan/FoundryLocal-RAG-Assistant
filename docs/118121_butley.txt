Title: Butley (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: Harold Pinter
Main Cast: Alan Bates, Jessica Tandy, Michael Byrne, Georgina Hale, Simon Rouse
Keywords: based on play or musical, lgbt, theater

Overview: Alan Bates recreates his award-winning stage role in director Harold Pinter's 1974 film verson of Simon Gray's play, about a university professor whose private life is in freefall. The cast also includes Jessica Tandy, Richard O'Callaghan, Susan Engel and Georgina Hale.
