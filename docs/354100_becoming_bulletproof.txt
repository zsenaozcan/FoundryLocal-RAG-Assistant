Title: Becoming Bulletproof (2015)
Genres: Western, Documentary
Director: Michael Barnett
Main Cast: Vanessa Halby, Peter Halby, Ila Halby, Ajani A.J. Murray, Jeremy Vest

Overview: A diverse group of disabled people from across the U.S. take on leading roles in a magical rip-roaring costume drama Western, filmed on vintage Hollywood locations. This riveting film within a film immerses us in a dynamic, inclusive world of discipline and play, raising questions about why we so rarely see real disabled actors on the big screen?
