Title: Some Girl(s) (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Daisy von Scherler Mayer
Main Cast: Adam Brody, Kristen Bell, Zoe Kazan, Mía Maestro, Jennifer Morrison
Keywords: woman director

Overview: On the eve of his wedding, a successful writer travels around the country to meet up with ex-lovers in an attempt to make amends for his wrongdoings.
