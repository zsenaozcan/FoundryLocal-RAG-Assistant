Title: Failan (2001)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Song Hae-sung
Main Cast: Cecilia Cheung, Choi Min-sik, Son Byung-ho, Gong Hyung-jin, Kim Su-hyeon

Overview: After losing both her parents, Failan emmigrates to Korea to seek her only remaining relatives. Once she reaches Korea, she finds out that her relatives have moved to Canada well over a year ago. Desperate to stay and make a living in Korea, Failan is forced to have an arranged marriage through a match-making agency.
