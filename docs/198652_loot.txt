Title: Loot (1970)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Silvio Narizzano
Main Cast: Richard Attenborough, Lee Remick, Hywel Bennett, Milo O'Shea, Roy Holder
Keywords: robbery, coffin, caper, loot

Overview: Based on the play by 'Joe Orton' this film follows the adventures of two pals who have pulled off a bank robbery and have to hide the loot. Fortunately (?) one of them works in a funeral parlour and they have a coffin to spare.
