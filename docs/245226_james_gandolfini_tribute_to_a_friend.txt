Title: James Gandolfini: Tribute To A Friend (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: James Gandolfini, Tony Sirico, Edie Falco, Steve Buscemi, David Chase
Keywords: interview, biography, tribute, friends, actor

Overview: "James Gandolfini: Tribute To A Friend" will debut Sunday, December 22 at 8:30 PM on HBO, his old The Sopranos network. In the half-hour tribute, friends and colleagues remember the three-time Emmy winner, who died June 19 at age 51. The special features clips of Gandolfini’s work as well as behind-the-scenes footage.
