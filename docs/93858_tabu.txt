Title: Tabu (2012)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Miguel Gomes
Main Cast: Teresa Madruga, Laura Soveral, Henrique Espírito Santo, Carloto Cotta, Isabel Muñoz Cardoso
Keywords: tragic love, black and white, silent film

Overview: When Pilar's befriended neighbour Aurora dies, Pilar starts to dig into Aurora's past and learns about Aurora's very tragic love story.
