Title: The Frozen North (1922)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Buster Keaton
Main Cast: Buster Keaton, Joe Roberts, Sybil Seely, Bonnie Hill, Freeman Wood
Keywords: physical comedy, short

Overview: This satirical parody of William S. Hart's melodramatic films finds Buster in the frozen north, "last stop on the subway." He uses a wanted poster as his partner in robbing a gambling house. When he thinks he spies his wife making love to another man he shoots them both only to learn it isn't his cabin after all.
