Title: The Weekend Murders (1970)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Mystery
Director: Michele Lupo
Main Cast: Anna Moffo, Ida Galli, Gastone Moschin, Peter Baldwin, Lance Percival
Keywords: giallo, english countryside, serial murders

Overview: A large family meet at the family estate for the reading of their father's will. Each expects to be the recipient of a tidy sum, but apart from the local constable and a few other small awards to the help, the entire estate goes to the favorite daughter.
