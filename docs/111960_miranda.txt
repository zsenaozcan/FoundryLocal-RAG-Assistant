Title: Miranda (1948)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Fantasy, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Ken Annakin
Main Cast: Glynis Johns, Googie Withers, Griffith Jones, John McCallum, Margaret Rutherford
Keywords: love triangle, jealousy, fisherman, mermaid

Overview: A young married physician discovers a mermaid, and gives into her request to be taken to see London. Comedy and romantic entanglements ensue soon after.
