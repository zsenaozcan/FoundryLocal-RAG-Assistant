Title: Lianna (1983)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: John Sayles
Main Cast: Linda Griffiths, Jane Hallaren, Jon DeVries, Jo Henderson, Jessica Wight MacDonald
Keywords: lesbian relationship, homosexuality

Overview: A happily married woman comes to realize herself of being a repressed lesbian after she has an affair with a female college professor, and then tries to come to terms with her newfound lifestyle.
