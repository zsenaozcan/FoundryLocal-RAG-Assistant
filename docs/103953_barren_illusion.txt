Title: Barren Illusion (1999)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama, Romance
Director: Kiyoshi Kurosawa
Main Cast: Shinji Takeda, Miako Tadano, Taro Suwa, Yamamoto Naoki, Shinji Aoyama

Overview: In a near future where allergies have become an epidemic, music producer Haru half-heartedly dates post office clerk and mail thief Michi. He searches for violent ways to reactivate his life, she dreams of escaping abroad; both volunteer to test a new drug which may provide a cure.
