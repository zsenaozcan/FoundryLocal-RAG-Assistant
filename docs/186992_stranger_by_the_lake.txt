Title: Stranger by the Lake (2013)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Alain Guiraudie
Main Cast: Pierre Deladonchamps, Christophe Paou, Patrick d'Assumçao, Jérôme Chappatte, Mathieu Vervisch
Keywords: male nudity, suspense, homosexuality, unsimulated sex, skinny dipping, lgbt, murder hunt

Overview: Summertime. A cruising spot for men, tucked away on the shores of a lake. Franck falls in love with Michel, an attractive, potent and lethally dangerous man. Franck knows this, but wants to live out his passion anyway.
