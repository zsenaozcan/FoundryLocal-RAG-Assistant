Title: Borotalco (1982)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Carlo Verdone
Main Cast: Carlo Verdone, Eleonora Giorgi, Mario Brega, Christian De Sica, Roberta Manfredi

Overview: Sergio Benvenuti is a shy seller of contracts for a Roman company of music, but because of his character he cannot find even a customer, so he asks for help from a fellow named Nadia.
