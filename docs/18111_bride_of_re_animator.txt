Title: Bride of Re-Animator (1989)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Brian Yuzna
Main Cast: Jeffrey Combs, Bruce Abbott, Claude Earl Jones, Fabiana Udenio, David Gale
Keywords: desperation, nudity, human experimentation, bat, sequel, basement, stop motion, reanimation, dark comedy, laboratory

Overview: Herbert West once again revives the dead. This time, he brings Dan's ex-girlfriend's heart back inside a 'perfect' body. Dr. Hill returns as the evil nemesis who lost his head.
