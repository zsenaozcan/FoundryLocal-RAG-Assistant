Title: Summers Downstairs (2015)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Tom Sommerlatte
Main Cast: Sebastian Fräsdorf, Godehard Giese, Karin Hanczewski, Alice Pehlivanyan, William Peiro

Overview: Brothers David and Matthias have contrasting personalities. Long since grown up – one with a wife, the other a girlfriend – they unintentionally meet at their parents' holiday home. Games and plots about power and dominance with unforeseen consequences.
