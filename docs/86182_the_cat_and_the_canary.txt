Title: The Cat and the Canary (1979)
Genres: Thriller, Foreign, Mystery, Horror, Comedy
Director: Radley Metzger
Main Cast: Honor Blackman, Edward Fox, Michael Callan, Wendy Hiller, Olivia Hussey
Keywords: remake, based on play or musical, black cat

Overview: British remake of the classic comedy thriller play. A group of potential heirs gather in a forbidding old house to learn which of them will inherit a fortune. Later, they learn that a flesh-rending maniac is loose.
