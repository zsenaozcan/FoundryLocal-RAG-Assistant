Title: Journey to the West: Conquering the Demons (2013)
Genres: Action, Fantasy, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Stephen Chow
Main Cast: Wen Zhang, Shu Qi, Huang Bo, Show Luo, Lee Sheung-Ching
Keywords: folklore, demon hunter

Overview: In a world plagued by demons who cause great human suffering, young demon hunter Xuan Zang risks his all to conquer a water demon, a pig demon and the demon of all demons, Sun Wukong.
