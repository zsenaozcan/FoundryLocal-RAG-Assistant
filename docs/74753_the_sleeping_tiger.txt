Title: The Sleeping Tiger (1954)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Joseph Losey
Main Cast: Dirk Bogarde, Alexis Smith, Alexander Knox, Hugh Griffith, Patricia McCarron
Keywords: psychiatrist, brit noir, film noir

Overview: A petty thief breaks into the home of a psychiatrist and gets caught in a web of a doctor who wishes to experiment on him and a doctor's wife who wishes to seduce him.
