Title: Last Cowboy Standing (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Zaida Bergroth
Main Cast: Lauri Tilkanen, Iiro Panula, Tommi Korpela, Elina Knihtilä, Ilmari Järvenpää
Keywords: woman director
