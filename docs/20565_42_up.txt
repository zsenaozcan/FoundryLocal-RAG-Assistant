Title: 42 Up (1998)
Genres: Documentary, Foreign
Director: Michael Apted
Main Cast: Bruce Balden, Jacqueline Bassett, Symon Basterfield, Andrew Brackfield, John Brisby
Keywords: docudrama, east end, archival footage

Overview: Director Michael Apted revisits the same group of British-born adults after a 7 year wait. The subjects are interviewed as to the changes that have occurred in their lives during the last seven years.
