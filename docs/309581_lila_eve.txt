Title: Lila & Eve (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Charles Stone III
Main Cast: Viola Davis, Jennifer Lopez, Shea Whigham, Aml Ameen, Andre Royo

Overview: Lila, a grief-stricken mother reeling from her son’s murder, attends a support group where she meets Eve, who urges her to take matters into her own hands to track down her son’s killers.
