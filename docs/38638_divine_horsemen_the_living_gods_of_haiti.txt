Title: Divine Horsemen: The Living Gods of Haiti (1985)
Genres: Documentary, Horror
Director: Maya Deren
Main Cast: John Genke, Joan Pape
Keywords: independent film, woman director

Overview: This intimate ethnographic study of Voudoun dances and rituals was shot by Maya Deren during her years in Haiti (1947-1951); she never edited the footage, so this “finished” version was made by Teiji Ito and Cherel Ito after Deren’s death.
