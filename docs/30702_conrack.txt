Title: Conrack (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: Martin Ritt
Main Cast: Jon Voight, Paul Winfield, Madge Sinclair, Tina Andrews, Antonio Fargas
Keywords: island, south carolina, teacher, school, racism

Overview: A young, white teacher is assigned to an isolated island off the coast of South Carolina populated mostly by poor black families. He finds that the basically illiterate, neglected children there know so little of the world outside their island.
