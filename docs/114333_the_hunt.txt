Title: The Hunt (1966)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Carlos Saura
Main Cast: Ismael Merlo, Alfredo Mayo, José María Prada, Emilio Gutiérrez Caba, Fernando Sánchez Polack
Keywords: rabbit hunting

Overview: Three men go hunting rabbits during a hot day. Heat and talking about events happened in the past make them angry, until they go totally crazy.
