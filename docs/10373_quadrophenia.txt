Title: Quadrophenia (1979)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Franc Roddam
Main Cast: Phil Daniels, Leslie Ash, Phil Davis, Mark Wingett, Timothy Spall
Keywords: england, fab four, music, woodstock, music festival, mods

Overview: Based on the 1973 rock opera album of the same name by The Who, this is the story of 60s teenager Jimmy. At work he slaves in a dead-end job. While after, he shops for tailored suits and rides his scooter as part of the London Mod scene.
