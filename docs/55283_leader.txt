Title: Leader (2010)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Romance
Director: Sekhar Kammula
Main Cast: Rana Daggubati, Harshavardhan, Priya Anand, Subbaraju Santosh, Richa Gangopadhyay
Keywords: corruption, inspiration, politics

Overview: Upon the death of his father, Arjun Prasad campaigns to become the CM of Andhra. He hopes to eradicate corruption from Indian Politics.
