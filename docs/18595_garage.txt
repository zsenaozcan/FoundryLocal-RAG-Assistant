Title: Garage (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Lenny Abrahamson
Main Cast: Pat Shortt, John Keogh, Anne-Marie Duff, George Costigan, Conor Ryan
Keywords: gas station, garage, village, independent film, ireland, solitude

Overview: A tragicomedy set in the world of gas stations in rural Ireland, where over-diligent employee of the garage searches for intimacy during the course of a life-changing summer.
