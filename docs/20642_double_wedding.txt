Title: Double Wedding (1937)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Richard Thorpe
Main Cast: William Powell, Myrna Loy, Florence Rice, John Beal, Jessie Ralph
Keywords: double wedding, romantic comedy, trailer, milquetoast

Overview: A bohemian free spirit (William Powell) helps meek Waldo (John Beal) win back his fiancée (Florence Rice) and falls in love with her over-controlling sister (Myrna Loy) in the process.
