Title: Kizumonogatari Part 3: Reiketsu (2017)
Genres: Animation, Fantasy, Mystery
Director: Tatsuya Oishi
Main Cast: Hiroshi Kamiya, Yui Horie, Maaya Sakamoto, Takahiro Sakurai
Keywords: vampire, based on light novel, anime

Overview: With help from Meme Oshino, the apparition specialist, Koyomi defeats the three powerful vampire hunters: Dramaturgy, Episode and Guillotinecutter. Koyomi takes back all the limbs of Kiss-shot Acerola-orion Heart-under-blade in order to become a human again.
