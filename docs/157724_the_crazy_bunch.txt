Title: The Crazy Bunch (1974)
Genres: Western, Comedy
Director: Giuliano Carnimeo
Main Cast: George Hilton, Chris Huerta, Antonio Monselesan, Memmo Carotenuto, Nello Pazzafini

Overview: Tricky Dicky is after a gold treasure with his companion. He tracks the treasure down in insane asylums, Ku Klux Klan rallies, and finally, the outlaw's hideout.
