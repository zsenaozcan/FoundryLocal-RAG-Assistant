Title: The Anarchist Cookbook (2002)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Thriller
Director: Jordan Susman
Main Cast: Devon Gummersall, Dylan Bruno, Gina Philips, Johnny Whitworth, Katharine Towne
Keywords: independent film, anarchism

Overview: A movie about a young honors student-turned-anarchist, Puck, and his group of anarchist friends living peacefully in a Dallas commune until a nihilist, Johnny Black, appears with The Anarchist Cookbook and completely destroys their way of life.
