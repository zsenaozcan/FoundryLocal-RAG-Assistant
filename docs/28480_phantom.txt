Title: Phantom (1922)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy
Director: F.W. Murnau
Main Cast: Alfred Abel, Grete Berger, Lil Dagover, Lya De Putti, Anton Edthofer
Keywords: road accident, infatuation, shopping spree

Overview: Lorenz Lubota is a city clerk with no direction in life. One day on his way to work he is run over by a woman driving a chariot and he is immediately infatuated with her.
