Title: City Hunter (1993)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Romance
Director: Wong Jing
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Joey Wong, Richard Norton, Kumiko Goto, Chingmy Yau
Keywords: martial arts, terrorist, boat, hostage, spoof, based on manga

Overview: Extremely silly comedy about a self-indulgent private investigator who winds up on a cruise ship full of rich patrons, gorgeous women, murderous terrorists, and scarce food.
