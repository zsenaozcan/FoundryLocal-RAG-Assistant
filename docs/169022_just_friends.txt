Title: Just Friends? (2009)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Kim Jho Gwang-soo
Main Cast: Lee Je-hoon, Yeon Woo Jin

Overview: Min-soo is on leave from the military and visits his boyfriend Seok-yi. While having fun out on the town, they run into Min-soo mother's mother! When the Min-soo's mother asks about their relationship, Min-soo replies "... just friends." Making things more complicated, Min-soo's mother ends up sleeping between them that night!
