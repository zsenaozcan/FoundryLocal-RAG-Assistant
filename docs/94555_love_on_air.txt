Title: Love On-Air (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kwon Chil-in
Main Cast: Lee Min-jung, Lee Jung-Jin, Jeong Yu-mi, Kim Jeong-tae, Lee Kwang-Soo

Overview: Former K-pop idol-turned-radio DJ Shin Jin Ah must find a way to revive her failing show with an overbearing boss who won't stand for her diva-like ways.
