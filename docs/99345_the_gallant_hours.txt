Title: The Gallant Hours (1960)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Robert Montgomery
Main Cast: James Cagney, Dennis Weaver, Ward Costello, Vaughn Taylor, Richard Jaeckel
Keywords: u.s. navy, american, admiral

Overview: A semi-documentary dramatization of five weeks in the life of Vice Admiral William F. "Bull" Halsey, Jr., from his assignment to command the U.S. naval operations in the South Pacific to the Allied victory at Guadalcanal.
