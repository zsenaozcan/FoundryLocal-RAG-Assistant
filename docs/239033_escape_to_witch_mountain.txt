Title: Escape to Witch Mountain (1995)
Genres: Adventure, Family, Fantasy
Director: Peter Rader
Main Cast: Robert Vaughn, Elisabeth Moss, Erik von Detten, Sam Horrigan, Lynne Moody
Keywords: brother sister relationship, orphan, superpower

Overview: The tale of two orphan twins with special powers who go looking for their origins. The two are hindered by an avaricious tycoon who only wants to exploit their abilities.
