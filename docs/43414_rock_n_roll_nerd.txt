Title: Rock n' Roll Nerd (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Documentary
Director: Rhian Skirving
Main Cast: Tim Minchin

Overview: ROCK N ROLL NERD is an intimate tale that charts Tim Minchin’s meteoric rise from obscurity to celebrity. Capturing every moment of Tim’s first frenzied break onto the comedy scene the documentary weaves never-before-seen performance footage, backstage antics, home footage and candid interviews into a funny and deeply personal story of love and ambition.
