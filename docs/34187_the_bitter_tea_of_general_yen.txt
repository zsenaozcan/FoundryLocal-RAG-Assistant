Title: The Bitter Tea of General Yen (1933)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Frank Capra
Main Cast: Barbara Stanwyck, Nils Asther, Toshia Mori, Walter Connolly, Gavin Gordon
Keywords: missionary

Overview: A U.S. missionary (Barbara Stanwyck) is gradually seduced by a courtly warlord (Nils Asther) holding her in Shanghai.
