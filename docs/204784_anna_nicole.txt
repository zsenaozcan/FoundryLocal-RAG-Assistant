Title: Anna Nicole (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mary Harron
Main Cast: Agnes Bruckner, Martin Landau, Virginia Madsen, Adam Goldberg, Cary Elwes
Keywords: celebrity, blonde, playboy, tabloid, woman director

Overview: Voluptuous beauty Anna Nicole Smith marries an elderly millionaire and poses for Playboy, but after her husband's death, her excessive drinking, pill-popping and weight fluctuations take their toll.
