Title: Interceptors (1999)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Phillip J. Roth
Main Cast: Olivier Gruner, Brad Dourif, Ernie Hudson, William Zabka, Glenn Plummer

Overview: In the near future the US Government has created an elite team of soldiers specially trained for action in the event of a hostile alien encounter. A multi-national force, they are the last line of defense
