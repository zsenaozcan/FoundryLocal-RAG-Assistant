Title: For Better and Worse (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Kjell Sundvall
Main Cast: Magdalena in de Betou, Katarina Ewerlöf, Johan Ulveson, Ellen Bergström, Adam Pålsson
Keywords: wedding, family, failing marriage

Overview: A couple on the brink of divorce attempts to hide their crisis at a grand wedding, but it turns out they are not the only ones with false appearances.
