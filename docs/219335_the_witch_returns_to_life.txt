Title: The Witch Returns to Life (1952)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Comedy
Director: Roland af Hällström
Main Cast: Mirja Mane, Toivo Mäkelä, Hillevi Lagerstam	, Sakari Jurkka, Helge Herala
Keywords: female nudity, witch, seduction, curse

Overview: An archaeological team unearths a body of a young woman, who was told to be a witch buried in the bog some 300 years ago. Soon a naked woman appears and drives the men of the village crazy...
