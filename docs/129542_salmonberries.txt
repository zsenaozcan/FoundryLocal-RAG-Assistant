Title: Salmonberries (1991)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Percy Adlon
Main Cast: Angayuqaq Oscar Kawagley, k.d. lang, Rosel Zech, Chuck Connors

Overview: A young orphaned woman, named Kotzebue, is trying to find out who her parents are in the icy landscapes of Alaska. Kotzebue (K.d. Lang) is helped by a east-german librarian (Rosel Zech), whose husband was killed while fleeing from the GDR. Although both women could not be more different from each other, a fragile relationship forms.
