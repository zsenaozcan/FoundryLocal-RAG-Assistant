Title: The Poseidon Adventure (2005)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Thriller, Action
Director: John Putch
Main Cast: Adam Baldwin, Rutger Hauer, Steve Guttenberg, Bryan Brown, C. Thomas Howell
Keywords: capsized ship

Overview: A cruise ship succumbs to a terrorist act and capsizes on New Year's eve. A rag-tag group of survivors, spearheaded by a priest and a homeland security agent, must journey through the upside down vessel and attempt an escape.
