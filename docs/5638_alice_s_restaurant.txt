Title: Alice's Restaurant (1969)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Music
Director: Arthur Penn
Main Cast: Arlo Guthrie, Patricia Quinn, James Broderick, Tina Chen, Geoff Outlaw
Keywords: hippie, free love, restaurant, junkie, commune, military service, vorstrafe, wedding, church, drug

Overview: Alice's Restaurant is a 1969 movie adapted from a song by Arlo Guthrie. The song is Arlo Guthrie's most famous work, a talking blues based on a true story that began on Thanksgiving Day 1965. The movie reproduces the events of the song, in addition to other scenes.
