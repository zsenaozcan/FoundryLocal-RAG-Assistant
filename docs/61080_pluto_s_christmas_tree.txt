Title: Pluto's Christmas Tree (1952)
Genres: Animation, Comedy
Director: Jack Hannah
Main Cast: James MacDonald, Dessie Flynn, Clarence Nash, Ruth Clifford, Pinto Colvig
Keywords: mickey mouse, disney short, chip n dale, pluto

Overview: Pluto comes bounding outside to help Mickey get a Christmas tree. Chip 'n Dale see him and make fun of him, but the tree they take refuge in is the one Mickey chops down. They like the decorations, especially the candy canes and Mickey's bowl of mixed nuts. But Pluto spots them and goes after them long before Mickey spots them. Minnie, Donald, and Goofy drop by to sing carols.
