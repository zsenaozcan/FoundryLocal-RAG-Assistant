Title: Infernal Affairs II (2003)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: Andrew Lau
Main Cast: Anthony Wong, Eric Tsang, Carina Lau, Francis Ng, Edison Chen
Keywords: undercover, gangster boss, chinese mafia, police, triade, murder

Overview: In this prequel to Mou gaan dou (2002), Chan Wing Yan has just become an undercover cop in the triads while Lau Kin Ming joins the police force. Both the triads and the police find an enemy in a rival crime boss.
