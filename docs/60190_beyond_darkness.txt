Title: Beyond Darkness (1990)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Claudio Fragasso
Main Cast: Gene LeBrock, David Brandon, Barbara Bingham, Michael Stephenson, Stephen Brown
Keywords: religion and supernatural, demonic possession

Overview: A priest &amp; his family move into a new house, without knowing that it was built over the place where twenty witches were burnt at the stake. Soon the terror begins, with the house terrorizing its inhabitants with the elements that lie within the construction, like a possessed radio and a flying cleaver.
