Title: Maniacts (2001)
Genres: Unknown
Director: C.W. Cressler
Main Cast: Jeff Fahey, Kellie Waymire

Overview: Two serial killers meet and fall in love in an asylum for the criminally insane. Upon escaping their corrupt captors, they flee to the country to try and lead a happy, normal life. Unfortunately, "normal" turns out to be a state of mind...an elusive state of mind.
