Title: 95 Miles to Go (2006)
Genres: Documentary, Comedy
Director: Tom Caltabiano
Main Cast: Ray Romano, Tom Caltabiano, Roger Lay, Jr., Jearlyn Steele

Overview: Ray Romano's eight-day drive through the south on a stand-up comedy tour becomes more than he bargains for when longtime friend and opening act, Tom Caltabiano, brings a film student along to document their thousand-mile journey. Together, all three struggle with Ray's obsessions, phobias, and insecurities in this unscripted exploration of newfound fame
