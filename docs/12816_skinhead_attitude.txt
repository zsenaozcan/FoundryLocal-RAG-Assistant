Title: Skinhead Attitude (2003)
Genres: Documentary, Foreign
Director: Daniel Schweizer
Main Cast: Laurel Aitken, Gavin Watson, Buster Bloodvessel, Jimmy Pursey, Ian Stuart
Keywords: skinhead, renee, ska, youth culture
