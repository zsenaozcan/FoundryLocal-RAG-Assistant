Title: Collective Invention (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Oh-Kwang Kwon
Main Cast: Lee Chun-hee, Lee Kwang-Soo, Park Bo-young, Jang Gwang, Lee Byung-joon

Overview: Park Goo (Lee Kwang-Soo) mutates into a man-fish due to side effects from an experimental drug. He receives heavy publicity and becomes a star. A conspiracy by a pharmaceutical company leads Park Goo to being possibly expelled from the world.
