Title: Tokyo Fiancée (2014)
Genres: Romance
Director: Stefan Liberski
Main Cast: Pauline Étienne, Taichi Inoue, Julie Le Breton, Alice de Lencquesaing

Overview: A Japanophile young Belgian woman in Tokyo falls into a whirlwind romance with a Francophile Japanese student, in this charming and tender tale of young love and cultural discovery.
