Title: The Optimists of Nine Elms (1973)
Genres: Drama
Director: Anthony Simmons
Main Cast: Peter Sellers, Donna Mullane, John Chaffey, Marjorie Yates, David Daker
Keywords: busker, nine elms

Overview: A retired entertainer makes his living as a street musician on the streets of London. Two young children befriend the old musician, brightening his otherwise colorless life
