Title: Blackout (1985)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Douglas Hickox
Main Cast: Keith Carradine, Kathleen Quinlan, Richard Widmark, Michael Beck, Jerry Wasserman
Keywords: stalker, serial murders

Overview: A police officer suspects that a local husband and father who has recently undergone facial surgery because of injuries received in a car accident is in reality the same man who committed a quadruple murder several years before.
