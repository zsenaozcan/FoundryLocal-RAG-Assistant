Title: Straight A's (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: James Cox
Main Cast: Anna Paquin, Ryan Phillippe, Luke Wilson, Powers Boothe, Riley Thomas Stewart
Keywords: brother brother relationship, drug addiction, sister-in-law

Overview: Pressured by his deceased mother's ghost to return home to the family he abandoned, a former addict grabs a bag of pills and a sack of marijuana and hits the road to Shreveport.
