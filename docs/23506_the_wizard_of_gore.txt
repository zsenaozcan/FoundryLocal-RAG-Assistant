Title: The Wizard of Gore (1970)
Genres: Horror
Director: Herschell Gordon Lewis
Main Cast: Ray Sager, Judy Cler, Wayne Ratay, Phil Laurenson, Jim Rau
Keywords: detective, magic, hypnosis, illusion, independent film, gore, blood, grindhouse, torture, extreme violence

Overview: A TV talk-show hostess and her boyfriend investigate a shady magician whom has the ability to hypnotize and control the thoughts of people in order to stage gory on-stage illusions using his powers of mind bending.
