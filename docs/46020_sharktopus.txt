Title: Sharktopus (2010)
Genres: Horror, Comedy, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Declan O'Brien
Main Cast: Eric Roberts, Sara Malakul Lane, Ralph Garman, Kerem Bursin, Liv Boughn
Keywords: beach, monster, deep sea, octopus, shark attack, animal horror

Overview: The U.S. Navy's special group "Blue Water" builds a half-shark, half-octopus for combat. But the sharktopus escapes and terrorizes the beaches of Puerto Vallarta.
