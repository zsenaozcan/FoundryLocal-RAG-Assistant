Title: Lo sgarbo (1975)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Marino Girolami
Main Cast: Leonard Mann, Gianni Russo, Karin Schubert, Hélène Chanel, Guido Celano

Overview: A glamorous playboy thug's brutal trail of murder, terrorism and sexual blackmail across Las Vegas, Rome, Paris and London quickly wins him a prominent place in the Rome syndicate loosely run by an aging and decrepit Mafia Don. He even wins over the Don's much younger wife.
