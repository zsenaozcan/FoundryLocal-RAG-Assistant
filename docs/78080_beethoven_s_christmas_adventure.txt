Title: Beethoven's Christmas Adventure (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: John Putch
Main Cast: Curtis Armstrong, Tom Arnold, John Cleese, Kim Rhodes, Kyle Massey
Keywords: holiday, christmas

Overview: A Christmas elf accidentally takes off in Santa's sleigh, crash lands in a small town, and loses the magic toy bag. Beethoven must rescue the elf, recover the bag from greedy crooks, and return the sleigh to Santa in time to save Christmas.
