Title: This Is Not an Exit: The Fictional World of Bret Easton Ellis (1998)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Gerald Fox
Main Cast: Dechen Thurman, Rachel Weisz, Jason Bushman, Paul Blackthorne
Keywords: reenactment

Overview: When "American Psycho" was released early in 2000 it reaffirmed author Bret Easton Ellis as the controversial "bad boy" of contemporary American Fiction. "This is Not an Exit" reveals the world inhabited by Ellis.
