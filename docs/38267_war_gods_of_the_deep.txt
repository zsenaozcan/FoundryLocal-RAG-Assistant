Title: War-Gods of the Deep (1965)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Jacques Tourneur
Main Cast: Vincent Price, David Tomlinson, Tab Hunter, Susan Hart, John Le Mesurier
Keywords: underwater, lost world

Overview: The plot concerns the discovery of a lost city beneath the sea off the coast of Cornwall. Price is the captain overseeing a group of sailors who have lived there for more than a century where the peculiar mix of gases has allowed them to extend their lifespan.
