Title: The War in Space (1977)
Genres: Adventure, Science Fiction, Action
Director: Jun Fukuda
Main Cast: Kensaku Morita, Yûko Asano, Ryô Ikebe, Masaya Oki, Hiroshi Miyauchi
Keywords: sequel, alien, outer space, alien invasion

Overview: Earth is attacked by alien spacecraft from Venus. But a Japanese scientist has constructed a space craft, the Gohten, with which he may save humanity.
