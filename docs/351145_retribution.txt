Title: Retribution (2015)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Dani de la Torre
Main Cast: Luis Tosar, Elvira Mínguez, Javier Gutiérrez, Fernando Cayo, Goya Toledo
Keywords: bomb, money, phone, retribution

Overview: Carlos, a bank executive, begins his morning routine by taking his kids to school. As he starts the car he receives an anonymous phone call informing him that there's a bomb under his seat. A stranger's voice tells him that he only has a few hours to gather together a large amount of cash: if he doesn't, his car will blow up.
