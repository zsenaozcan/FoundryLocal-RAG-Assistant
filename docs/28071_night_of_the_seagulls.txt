Title: Night of the Seagulls (1975)
Genres: Horror
Director: Amando de Ossorio
Main Cast: Víctor Petit, María Kosty, Sandra Mozarowsky, Julia Saly, José Antonio Calvo
Keywords: revenge, torture, knight templars

Overview: The Knight Templars return in this fourth installment of the Blind Dead seris. On this outing, the Templars haunt a fishing village, where they rise seven nights every seven years to claim their sacrificial offerings in return for the safety of the townspeople.
