Title: Kiss or Kill (1997)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Bill Bennett
Main Cast: Tiffany Peters, Syd Brisbane, Geoff Revell, Matt Day, Frances O'Connor
Keywords: hotel room, murder, scam, neo-noir

Overview: Two lovers, Nikki and Al, have a scam in which Nikki allows herself to be picked up by older men, drugs them, and, with Al's help, robs them. After accidentally killing one of her victims with an overdose, Nikki and Al are on the run.
