Title: Watch the Shadows Dance (1987)
Genres: Action
Director: Mark Joffe
Main Cast: Nicole Kidman, Tom Jennings, Vince Martin, Joanne Samuel, Jeremy Shadlow

Overview: Robbie and his girlfriend, Amy, are among a group of students who play their private night games in the romantic thriller that catapulted Kidman to center stage.  On screen throughout, Kidman discovers what will happen when a friendly game becomes real life ... and real life becomes a deadly game.
