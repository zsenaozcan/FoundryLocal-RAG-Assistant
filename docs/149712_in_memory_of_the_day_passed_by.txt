Title: In Memory of the Day Passed By (1990)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Šarūnas Bartas
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: The film is a day in the life that passes by, even if it seems neverending. In the morning the streets are alive with people, pedestrians and cars, with loud and exultant noise. Such sounds accompany the restless walk of a woman and her child across a dusty street, while Bartas’ gaze wanders through many different perspectives.
