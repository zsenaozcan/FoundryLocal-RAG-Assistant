Title: The Shoe (1998)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Laila Pakalnina
Main Cast: Ivars Brakovskis, Igors Buraks, Viktors Cestnovs, Andrejs Garnavl, Vadims Grossmans
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Every night, Soviet tractors comb the coast of Latvia looking for signs of anyone who could have infiltrated the Soviet border from the sea. One morning, three Soviet patrolmen discover a woman’s shoe in the sand and footsteps leading to the quaint little village of Liepaja.
