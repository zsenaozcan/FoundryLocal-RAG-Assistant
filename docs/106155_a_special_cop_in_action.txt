Title: A Special Cop in Action (1976)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Crime
Director: Marino Girolami
Main Cast: Maurizio Merli, John Saxon, Mirella D'Angelo, Raymond Pellegrin, Toni Ucci

Overview: A school bus with young children being kidnapped. Commissario Betti will solve the case. High action is promised, including hostage, bank robberies, car chases, prison scenes and mafia bosses!
