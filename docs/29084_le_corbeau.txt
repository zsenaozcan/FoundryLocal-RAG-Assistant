Title: Le Corbeau (1943)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Henri-Georges Clouzot
Main Cast: Pierre Fresnay, Ginette Leclerc, Micheline Francey, Héléna Manson, Jeanne Fusier-Gir
Keywords: france, poison, doctor, gossip, social outcast, rumor, abortion

Overview: French village doctor becomes target of poison-pen letters sent to village leaders, accusing him of affairs and practicing abortion.
