Title: Take the Trash (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Rasmus Heide
Main Cast: Thure Lindhardt, Sidse Babett Knudsen, Mick Øgendahl, Troels Lyby, Beate Bille
Keywords: recycling, community service, salesman

Overview: Jesper Jensen is a successful IT salesman who stops at nothing, even if it means backstabbing his best friend. Being number one in the company and having a beautiful wife and a lovely home, Jesper seems to lead a perfect life, but one day his arrogance and self confidence hits him in the face like a boomerang and he loses everything overnight. Jesper is sentenced to do community service at a recycling center with the odd and funny characters Dion, Theodor, and Lotte. At first he looks down on these people and doesn't want to be associated with them. When he begins to loosen up, however, he discovers that he can learn a lot from their straight forward and honest approach to life.
