Title: Bunny and the Bull (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Paul King
Main Cast: Edward Hogg, Simon Farnaby, Verónica Echegui, Julian Barratt, Noel Fielding
Keywords: independent film, agoraphobia

Overview: A young shut-in takes an imaginary road trip inside his apartment, based on mementos and memories of a European trek from years before.
