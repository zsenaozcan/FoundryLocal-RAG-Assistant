Title: Looking for Eric (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Ken Loach
Main Cast: Eric Cantona, Steve Evets, Stephanie Bishop, John Henshaw, Gerard Kearns
Keywords: ex-lover, independent film, social satire, soccer

Overview: A man trying to put his life back on track gets some advice from an unexpected benefactor (the ex football player Eric Cantona) in this comedy-drama from acclaimed British director Ken Loach.
