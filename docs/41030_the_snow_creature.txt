Title: The Snow Creature (1954)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: W. Lee Wilder
Main Cast: Paul Langton, Leslie Denison, Teru Shimada, William Phipps, Lock Martin
Keywords: yeti, monster movie

Overview: A botanical expedition to the Himalayas captures a Yeti and brings it back alive to Los Angeles, where it escapes and runs amok, seeking food.
