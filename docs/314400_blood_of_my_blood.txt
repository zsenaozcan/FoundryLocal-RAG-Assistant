Title: Blood of My Blood (2015)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Marco Bellocchio
Main Cast: Alba Rohrwacher, Filippo Timi, Toni Bertorelli, Ivan Franěk, Roberto Herlitzka

Overview: Northern Italy, 17th century. In a monastery, a nun accused of witchcraft tries to seduce a young confessor who refuses to yield to his searing temptation.
