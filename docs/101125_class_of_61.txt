Title: Class of '61 (1993)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Drama
Director: Gregory Hoblit
Main Cast: Clive Owen, Laura Linney, Christien Anholt, Andre Braugher, Dan Futterman

Overview: Three West Point 1861 generation cadets and friends go on opposite sides after the breakout of The Civil War, with tragic consequences. A subplot involves Lucius, a Shelby Peyton's slave, who kills a slave trader and goes on the run.
