Title: Home Care (2015)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Slávek Horák
Main Cast: Tatiana Vilhelmová, Bolek Polívka, Alena Mihulová, Sára Venclovská, Zuzana Krónerová
Keywords: medicine, health care, death, alternative medicine

Overview: Dedicated home care nurse Vlasta attends her whimsical patients in Czech wine country and lives for her husband Lada and her daughter, but one day things change and Vlasta is forced to reach outside of her comfort zone. Drama and gentle humor intertwine as Vlasta realizes, for the first time in her life, that she might need some care, too.
