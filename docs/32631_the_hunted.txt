Title: The Hunted (1995)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: J. F. Lawton
Main Cast: Christopher Lambert, John Lone, Joan Chen, Yoshio Harada, Yoko Shimada

Overview: A businessman on a trip to Tokyo ends up in the middle of a centuries old feud between a criminal Ninja cult and a Samurai when he witnesses a assassination performed by the Ninja-cult leader.
