Title: Lorelei: The Witch of the Pacific Ocean (2005)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Shinji Higuchi
Main Cast: Koji Yakusho, Satoshi Tsumabuki, Yuu Kashii, Shinichi Tsutsumi, Toshirō Yanagiba

Overview: A drama set during World War II where a submarine carrying a secret weapon attempts to stop a planned third atomic bombing of Japan. Based on Harutoshi Fukui's novel Shuusen no Lorelei.
