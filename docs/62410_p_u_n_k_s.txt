Title: P.U.N.K.S (1999)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Science Fiction
Director: Sean McNamara
Main Cast: Jessica Alba, Patrick Renna, Brandon Baker, Tim Redwine, Randy Quaid
Keywords: misfit, teenager, scientist, super strength

Overview: A group of misfit teens evade a corrupt scientist when they steal and attempt to return a suit that delivers supernatural strength to its wearer.
