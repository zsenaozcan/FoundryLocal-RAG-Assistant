Title: The 36 Crazy Fists (1977)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Chen Chi-Hwa
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Paul Chun Pui, Fung Hak-On, Tony Leung Siu-Hung, Yen Shi-Kwan

Overview: When a young man's village is destroyed by a band of thugs, he seeks help from a great kung-fu master, but his real lessons come from a drunk old man, he basically learns kung fu by accident and seeks his revenge.
