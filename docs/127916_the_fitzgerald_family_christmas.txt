Title: The Fitzgerald Family Christmas (2012)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Edward Burns
Main Cast: Kerry Bishé, Edward Burns, Heather Burns, Marsha Dietlein, Caitlin Fitzgerald
Keywords: holiday, brother sister relationship, irish-american, family gatherings, christmas

Overview: The siblings of the Fitzgerald family must decide if the dad who abandoned them 20 years ago can come home for Christmas. Can the big Irish clan get past their grievances to be a family again?
