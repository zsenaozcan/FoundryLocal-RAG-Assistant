Title: The Erotic Man (2010)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Jørgen Leth
Main Cast: Jørgen Leth, Sue Prado, Rokhaya Niang, Angeli Bayani
Keywords: eroticism

Overview: The film has been ten years in the making, and over time it has grown to become what the director himself has called an artistic testament. It is simultaneously his most personal and most provocative film. A film about growing older, about losing, about the special moments one remembers, and about the director's own circling around the essence of eroticism.
