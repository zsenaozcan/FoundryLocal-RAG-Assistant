Title: October Sky (1999)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Joe Johnston
Main Cast: Jake Gyllenhaal, Chris Cooper, Laura Dern, Chris Owen, William Lee Scott
Keywords: father son relationship, based on novel, small town, satellite, biography, rocket, west virginia, teacher, student, coal mine

Overview: Based on the true story of Homer Hickam, a coal miner's son who was inspired by the first Sputnik launch to take up rocketry against his father's wishes, and eventually became a NASA scientist.
