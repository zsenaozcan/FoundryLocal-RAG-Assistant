Title: The Mating Game (1959)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: George Marshall
Main Cast: Debbie Reynolds, Tony Randall, Paul Douglas, Una Merkel, Fred Clark
Keywords: maryland, taxes, tax inspector, tax collector, rural setting, farm life

Overview: Tax collector Lorenzo Charlton comes to the Larkins' farm to ask why Pop Larkins hasn't paid his back taxes. Charlton has to stay for a day to try to estimate the income from the farm, but it isn't easy to calculate when the farmer has such a lovely daughter, Mariette... Plot by Mattias Thuresson.
