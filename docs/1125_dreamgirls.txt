Title: Dreamgirls (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bill Condon
Main Cast: Jamie Foxx, Beyoncé Knowles, Eddie Murphy, Danny Glover, Jennifer Hudson
Keywords: music record, manager, black people, adultery, soul, show business, drug addiction, oscar award, musical, deceived wife
