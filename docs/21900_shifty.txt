Title: Shifty (2009)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Eran Creevy
Main Cast: Riz Ahmed, Daniel Mays, Jason Flemyng, Francesca Annis, Nitin Ganatra
Keywords: cocaine, drug dealer, heroin, police

Overview: Shifty, a young crack cocaine dealer in London, sees his life quickly spiral out of control when his best friend returns home. Stalked by a customer desperate to score at all costs, and with his family about to turn their back on him for good, Shifty must out-run and out-smart a rival drug dealer, intent on setting him up for a big fall.
