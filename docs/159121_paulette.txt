Title: Paulette (2013)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Jérôme Enrico
Main Cast: Bernadette Lafont, Carmen Maura, Dominique Lavanant, Françoise Bertin, André Penvern
Keywords: drug dealer, marijuana, weed+, old woman

Overview: Paulette lives alone in a housing project in the Paris suburbs. With her meager pension, she can no longer make ends meet.
