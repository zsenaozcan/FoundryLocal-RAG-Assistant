Title: Underworld: Endless War (2011)
Genres: Action, Animation, Horror
Director: Juno John Lee
Main Cast: Trevor Devall, Brian Dobson, Paul Dobson, Laura Harris, Mark Oliver
Keywords: paris, france, vampire, night, woman with gun, werewolf

Overview: Underworld: Endless War is a collection of three short, anime-style tie-in stories-—whose creation was supervised by Len Wiseman, director of the first 2 Underworld movies and producer of Underworld 3 &amp; 4--each tell a different story, and at different periods of history to highlight new events of the Underworld mythology. These stories also tell of what happened before the events in Underworld: Awakening
