Title: The Skeptic (2009)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Tennyson Bardwell
Main Cast: Tim Daly, Tom Arnold, Zoe Saldana, Andrea Roth, Edward Herrmann

Overview: After the mysterious death of his Aunt, a confirmed skeptic lawyer, Bryan Becket, dismisses reports that her house is haunted and moves in. Immediately occurrences begin he cannot explain. And beyond the occurrences there is something about the house which gnaws at Becket - some strange connection he senses he has with the house's past. Soon, the haunting turns personal.
