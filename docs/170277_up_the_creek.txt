Title: Up the Creek (1958)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Val Guest
Main Cast: David Tomlinson, Peter Sellers, Wilfrid Hyde-White, Vera Day, Lionel Jeffries

Overview: Bumbling navy officer Lieutenant Humphrey Fairweather (David Tomlinson) is transferred to HMS Berkeley, an old World War II destroyer, to keep him out of harm's way. But together with Chief Petty Officer Doherty (Peter Sellers), Fairweather gets into more trouble than might be thought possible, with events coming to a riotous conclusion when the Admiralty turn up for an inspection of the ship.
