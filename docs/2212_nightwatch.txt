Title: Nightwatch (1997)
Genres: Action, Drama, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Ole Bornedal
Main Cast: Ewan McGregor, Patricia Arquette, Nick Nolte, Josh Brolin, Lauren Graham
Keywords: expensive restaurant, night watchman, law, investigation, suspense, student, best friend

Overview: A law student, who takes a job as a night watchman at a morgue, begins to discover clues that implicate him as the suspect of a series of murders.
