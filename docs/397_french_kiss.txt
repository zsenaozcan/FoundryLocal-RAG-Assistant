Title: French Kiss (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Lawrence Kasdan
Main Cast: Meg Ryan, Kevin Kline, Timothy Hutton, Jean Reno, Adam Brooks
Keywords: paris, airport, in love with enemy, canadian, necklace, fear of flying, deal, vineyard, smuggling

Overview: French Kiss is director Kasdan’s best film since the Bodyguard. The plot involves a couple in love and one woman’s attempt to fly to Paris to get her lover back from a business trip and marry him. On the way she unknowingly smuggles something of value that has a petty thief chasing her across France as she chases after her future husband.
