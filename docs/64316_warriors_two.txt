Title: Warriors Two (1978)
Genres: Action, Drama, Foreign
Director: Sammo Hung
Main Cast: Sammo Hung, Leung Kar-Yan, Casanova Wong, Yuen Biao, Eric Tsang

Overview: In an attempt to save his village from being taken over by brutes, Wah is beaten to a pulp and his mother brutally murdered. Determined to take revenge, Wah learns the art of Wing Chun and enters into a showdown with the nasty villains.
