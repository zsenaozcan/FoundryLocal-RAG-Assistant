Title: The Rich Man's Wife (1996)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Amy Holden Jones
Main Cast: Clive Owen, Halle Berry, Peter Greene, Frankie Faison, Charles Hallahan
Keywords: fantasy, surprise, marriage, murder, suspense, rich, stranger, woman director

Overview: A rich man's wife finds she has a bad prenuptial agreement with an even worse husband. Over drinks with a stranger, she fantasizes about doing her husband in to void the prenupt. The stranger decides to turn her imagination into reality much to the wife's surprise.
