Title: 1900 (1976)
Genres: Drama, History, Romance
Director: Bernardo Bertolucci
Main Cast: Robert De Niro, Gérard Depardieu, Dominique Sanda, Stefania Sandrelli, Laura Betti
Keywords: italy, fascism, kommunistischer widerstand, epic, execution, death, catholicism, communism

Overview: Set in Bertolucci's ancestral region of Emilia, the film chronicles the lives of two men during the political turmoils that took place in Italy in the first half on the 20th century.
