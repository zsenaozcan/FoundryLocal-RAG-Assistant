Title: How to Make Money Selling Drugs (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Matthew Cooke
Main Cast: Matthew Cooke, Woody Harrelson, Susan Sarandon, Barry Cooper, Eminem
Keywords: war on drugs, cocaine, drug dealer, marijuana, drug bust, dea agent, law enforcement
