Title: The Kung Fu Instructor (1979)
Genres: Action, Foreign
Director: Sun Chung
Main Cast: Ti Lung, Wong Yu, Ku Feng, Johnny Wang Lung-Wei

Overview: Two rival clans have unsuccessfully tried to hire the master of the good clan to teach his clan. Not willing to take no for an answer, they frame the master for a dirty deed that he didn't commit, which forces him to kill a man in battle. The townspeople attempt to kill him and he is forced to flee to the evil clan.
