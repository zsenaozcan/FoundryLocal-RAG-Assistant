Title: The Day They Robbed the Bank of England (1960)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: John Guillermin
Main Cast: Aldo Ray, Elizabeth Sellars, Peter O'Toole, Hugh Griffith, Kieron Moore
Keywords: ira, bank robbery

Overview: London at the turn of the century. Three men is on a mission from the IRA to steal all the gold in the vaults of the Bank of England. Norgate, their leader, discovers the bank's weak spot: an old forgotten sewer straight under the vaults.
