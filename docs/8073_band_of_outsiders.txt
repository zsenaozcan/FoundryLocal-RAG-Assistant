Title: Band of Outsiders (1964)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Jean-Luc Godard
Main Cast: Anna Karina, Claude Brasseur, Sami Frey, Danièle Girard, Louisa Colpeyn
Keywords: robbery, louvre, woman between two men, bastille, nouvelle vague
