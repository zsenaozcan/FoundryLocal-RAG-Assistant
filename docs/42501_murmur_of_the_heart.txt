Title: Murmur of the Heart (1971)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Louis Malle
Main Cast: Lea Massari, Benoît Ferreux, Daniel Gélin, Michael Lonsdale, Ave Ninchi
Keywords: jazz, incest, boy scout, resort sex

Overview: In the end of the first Indochina War, an open-minded teenage boy finds himself between the urge to discover love and the ever-present, dominating affection of his mother.
