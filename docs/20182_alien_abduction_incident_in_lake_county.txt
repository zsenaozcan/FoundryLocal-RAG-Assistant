Title: Alien Abduction: Incident in Lake County (1998)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Dean Alioto
Main Cast: Benz Antoine, Kristian Ayre, Gillian Barber, Michael Buie, Emmanuelle Chriqui
Keywords: alcoholism, spaceship, alien, conspiracy, disappearance, alien abduction, found footage, abduction, smoke alarm
