Title: A Blast (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Syllas Tzoumerkas
Main Cast: Angeliki Papoulia, Vassilis Doganis, Maria Filini, Giorgos Biniaris, Efthymis Papadimitriou

Overview: Running away on the highway, Maria is alone in her roaring SUV. Behind her, fire and a case full of money. In front of her, the hopeless vastness of the motorway. Only a day before she was a caring mother, a loving wife, a responsible daughter. Today she has gone rogue.
