Title: River Queen (2005)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, War
Director: Vincent Ward
Main Cast: Samantha Morton, Kiefer Sutherland, Cliff Curtis, Stephen Rea, Temuera Morrison
Keywords: new zealand

Overview: An intimate story set during the 1860s in which a young Irish woman Sarah and her family find themselves on both sides of the turbulent wars between British and Maori during the British colonization of New Zealand.
