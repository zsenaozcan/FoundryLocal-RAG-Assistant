Title: Return to Mayberry (1986)
Genres: TV Movie, Comedy
Director: Bob Sweeney
Main Cast: Andy Griffith, Ron Howard, Don Knotts, Jim Nabors, Hal Smith
Keywords: tv series

Overview: After being away for awhile, Andy Taylor returns home to Mayberry to visit Opie, now an expectant father. While there he ends up helping Barney Fife mount a campaign for sheriff.
