Title: Sade (2000)
Genres: History, Drama, Crime
Director: Benoît Jacquot
Main Cast: Daniel Auteuil, Marianne Denicourt, Jeanne Balibar, Isild Le Besco, Jean-Pierre Cassel
Keywords: biography

Overview: A man prepares himself to be transferred to a detention center and rest home where he will relive one more time the highlights of his youth.
