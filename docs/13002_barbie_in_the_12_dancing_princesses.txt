Title: Barbie in the 12 Dancing Princesses (2006)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Greg Richardson
Main Cast: Nicole Oliver, Lalainia Lindbjerg, Kathleen Barr, Chiara Zanni, Kelly Sheridan
Keywords: sister sister relationship, dance, barbie, princess, ballet

Overview: King Randolph sends for his cousin Duchess Rowena to help turn his daughters, Princess Genevieve and her 11 sisters, into better ladies. But the Duchess takes away all the sisters fun, including the sisters favorite pastime: dancing.Thinking all hope is lost they find a secret passageway to a magical land were they can dance the night away.
