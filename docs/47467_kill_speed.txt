Title: Kill Speed (2010)
Genres: Crime, Action, Thriller
Director: Kim Bass
Main Cast: Andrew Keegan, Brandon Quinn, Natalia Cigliuti, Nick Carter, Robert Patrick
Keywords: sex, surfing

Overview: KILL SPEED is a high-octane, youth oriented, TOP GUN meets FAST &amp; FURIOUS tale about best friends who fly home-built, high-tech planes to deliver Mexican manufactured crystal meth throughout rural California in order to fund their Hollywood, rock-star lifestyle.
