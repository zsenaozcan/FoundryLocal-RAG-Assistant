Title: Mrs. Parker and the Vicious Circle (1994)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alan Rudolph
Main Cast: Jennifer Jason Leigh, Campbell Scott, Matthew Broderick, Gwyneth Paltrow, Jennifer Beals
Keywords: new york, alcohol, prohibition, biography, romance, independent film, theater, flashback, 1920s
