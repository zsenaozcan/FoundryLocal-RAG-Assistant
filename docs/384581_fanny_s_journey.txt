Title: Fanny's Journey (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lola Doillon
Main Cast: Léonie Souchaud, Fantine Harduin, Juliane Lepoureau, Cécile de France, Stéphane De Groodt
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Fanny is a Jewish girl in a French orphanage in 1943. When she and her friends are no longer safe from the Nazis, they try to flee to Switzerland. After their guide disappears, Fanny has to take the lead and help the other kids make it over the mountains.
