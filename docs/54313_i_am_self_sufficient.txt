Title: I Am Self-Sufficient (1976)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Nanni Moretti
Main Cast: Nanni Moretti, Simona Frosi, Andrea Pozzi, Fabio Traversa, Beniamino Placido

Overview: Michele Apicella lives in Rome, in an apartment paid for by his father. Abandoned by his wife and his son Andrea, he occupies his time acting in an experimental theater company under the artistic direction of his friend Fabio.
