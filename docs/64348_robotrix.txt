Title: Robotrix (1991)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Jamie Luk Kim-Ming
Main Cast: Chikako Aoyama, Amy Yip, David Wu, Hui Hiu-Daan, Billy Chow
Keywords: sex, cyborg, martial arts, resurrection, female protagonist, policewoman killing

Overview: A mad scientist transfers his mind to a wicked robot, which then embarks on a program of kidnaping, rape and murder, during which a female detective is killed. To fight the robot, the police woman's corpse is then made into a robotrix.
