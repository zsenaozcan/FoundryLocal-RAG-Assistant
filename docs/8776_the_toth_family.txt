Title: The Toth Family (1969)
Genres: Drama, War, Comedy
Director: Zoltán Fábri
Main Cast: Zoltán Latinovits, Sinkovits Imre, Márta Fónay, Vera Venczel, István Dégi
Keywords: hungary, fascism, world war ii, guest, major, military officer, family, oppression

Overview: The Toth family resides in Northern Hungary. The couple has a daughter and a son, the latter a member of the armed forces. When his weary major is ordered to take a vacation, the son talks him into a visit to his family home. Comedy ensues when the Toths go overboard trying to make things pleasant for the visiting major in hopes of an easier life for their son the soldier.
