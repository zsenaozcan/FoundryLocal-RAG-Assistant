Title: Desire (1936)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Frank Borzage
Main Cast: Marlene Dietrich, Gary Cooper, John Halliday, William Frawley, Ernest Cossart
Keywords: paris, spain, pearl necklace

Overview: Madeleine steals a string of pearls in Paris and uses US engineer Tom, who is driving on his vacation to Spain, to get the pearls out of France. But getting the pearls back from him proves to be difficult without falling in love.
