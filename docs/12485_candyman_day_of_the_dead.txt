Title: Candyman: Day of the Dead (1999)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror
Director: Turi Meyer
Main Cast: Tony Todd, Donna D'Errico, Jsu Garcia, Wade Williams, Alexia Robinson
Keywords: life and death, relatives, murder, los angeles, mirror, evil

Overview: The Candyman returns to try to convince his female descendent, an artist, to join him as a legendary figure. To this end, he frames her for a series of hideous murders of her friends and associates so that she has nowhere else to turn to.
