Title: The Hunters of the Golden Cobra (1982)
Genres: Crime, Adventure, Action, War
Director: Antonio Margheriti
Main Cast: David Warbeck, Almanta Suska, Luciano Pigozzi, Protacio Dee, Rene Abadeza
Keywords: treasure, snake, fistfight, jungle, airplane crash, supernatural power, cobra

Overview: An American soldier and a British intelligence agent try to track down an ancient relic called the Golden Cobra, rumored to have supernatural powers. An early Raiders rip-off directed Antonio Margheriti.
