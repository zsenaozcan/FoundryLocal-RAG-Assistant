Title: Sandesham (1991)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Sathyan Anthikad
Main Cast: Thilakan, Sreenivasan, Jayaram Subramaniam, Oduvil Unnikrishnan, Kaviyoor Ponnamma
Keywords: communist, politician, political party, lawyer, social satire, brother against brother, malayalam, railwayman

Overview: After his retirement, Raghavan Nair (Thilakan) is back at his home. His long cherished dream to spend his retired life along with his family is thwarted after seeing his two sons brawling each other over their political differences.
