Title: Deep Red (1975)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Dario Argento
Main Cast: David Hemmings, Daria Nicolodi, Gabriele Lavia, Macha Méril, Eros Pagni
Keywords: murder, giallo, reporter, crime, psychic, pianist, english, foreign, proto-slasher

Overview: A musician witnesses the murder of a famous psychic, and then teams up with a fiesty reporter to find the killer while evading attempts on their lives by the unseen killer bent on keeping a dark secret buried.
