Title: The Fear (1995)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Vincent Robert
Main Cast: Eddie Bowz, Heather Medway, Ann Turkel, Vince Edwards, Darin Heames
Keywords: mannequin, supernatural, b movie, cult film, wood

Overview: A weekend of 'fear therapy' turns into a nightmare of terror when a mannequin comes to life. Only those with the courage to confront their demons will survive.
