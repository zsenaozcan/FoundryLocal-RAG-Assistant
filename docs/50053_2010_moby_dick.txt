Title: 2010: Moby Dick (2010)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Trey Stokes
Main Cast: Barry Bostwick, Renee O'Connor, Bart Baggett, Matt Lagan, Adam Grimes

Overview: That infamous whale is bigger, badder and a whole lot stronger in this sci-fi reimagining of Herman Melville’s classic tale of the battle between man, sea and sea creature starring “Xena” alum Rene O’Connor as the (traditionally male) narrator. But the boat — now a high-tech submarine — is also bigger, and Capt. Ahab is as determined as ever to settle the score and take down the mighty sea mammal that maimed him.
