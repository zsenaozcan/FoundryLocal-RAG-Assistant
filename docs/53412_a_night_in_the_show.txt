Title: A Night in the Show (1915)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Charlie Chaplin
Main Cast: Charlie Chaplin, Edna Purviance, Charlotte Mineau, Dee Lampton, Leo White

Overview: Mr. Pest tries several theatre seats before winding up in front in a fight with the conductor. He is thrown out. In the lobby he pushes a fat lady into a fountain and returns to sit down by Edna. Mr. Rowdy, in the gallery, pours beer down on Mr. Pest and Edna. He attacks patrons, a harem dancer, the singers Dot and Dash, and a fire-eater.
