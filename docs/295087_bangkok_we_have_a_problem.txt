Title: Bangkok, We Have A Problem! (2014)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Olivier Baroux
Main Cast: Kad Merad, Alice Taglioni, Peter Coyote, Claude Perron, Chawanrut Janjittranon

Overview: Serge Renart, a television journalist who's now a has-been, and Natacha Bison, a war reporter who has been laid off because she posed too much of a danger to her colleagues, find themselves forced to investigate a story together, which will take them to Thailand, in search of one of the best kept secrets of contemporary history.
 What really happened during the televised broadcast of the Apollo 11 mission, when man first walked on the moon?
