Title: Punching Henry (2017)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Gregori Viens
Main Cast: Henry Phillips, J.K. Simmons, Sarah Silverman, Mike Judge, Michaela Watkins
Keywords: comedian, los angeles, songwriter, struggling career, tv producer, ambushed

Overview: Comedian Henry Phillips is lured to LA by a renowned TV producer who wants to bring his story of failure to the screen. But when a major network gets involved, Henry must decide whether he wants to make jokes for a living, or be the butt of them.
