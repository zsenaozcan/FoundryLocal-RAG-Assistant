Title: Whistle If You Come Back (1993)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Orhan Oğuz
Main Cast: Derya Alabora, Fikret Kuskan, Mevlüt Demiryay, Menderes Samancılar

Overview: A dwarf and a travesty, both are excluded from the society and doomed to exist at night. Their lives overlap in a strange encounter and this becomes the start of their friendship.
