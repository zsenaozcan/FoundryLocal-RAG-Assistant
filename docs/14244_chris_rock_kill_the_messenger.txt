Title: Chris Rock: Kill the Messenger (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Marty Callner
Main Cast: Chris Rock
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: An HBO special edited from three performances from Chris Rock's 2008 comedy tour: London (dark suit, dark shirt), Johannesburg (black suit, white shirt) and New York (shiny jacket). Topics include the ongoing presidential campaign, the possibility of a black president, George W. Bush, gas prices, low-paid jobs, ringtones and bottled water, sex, relationships and the correct use of the n-word
