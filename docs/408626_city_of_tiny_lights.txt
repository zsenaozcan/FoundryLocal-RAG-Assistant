Title: City of Tiny Lights (2017)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Pete Travis
Main Cast: Riz Ahmed, Billie Piper, James Floyd, Cush Jumbo, Roshan Seth
Keywords: based on novel, political intrigue , missing persons case, private eye, london, religious fanaticism

Overview: In the teeming, multicultural metropolis of modern-day London, a seemingly straightforward missing-person case launches a down-at-heel private eye into a dangerous world of religious fanaticism and political intrigue.
