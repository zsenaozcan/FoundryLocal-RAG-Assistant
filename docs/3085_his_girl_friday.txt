Title: His Girl Friday (1940)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Howard Hawks
Main Cast: Cary Grant, Rosalind Russell, Ralph Bellamy, Gene Lockhart, Alma Kruger
Keywords: suicide, prison, journalist, journalism, fugitive, wager, newspaper editor, insurance agent, fiancé fiancée relationship, crooked politician

Overview: Hildy, the journalist former wife of newspaper editor Walter Burns, visits his office to inform him that she's engaged and will be getting remarried the next day. Walter can't let that happen and frames the fiancé, Bruce Baldwin, for one thing after another, to keep him temporarily held in prison, while trying to steer Hildy into returning to her old job as his employee.
