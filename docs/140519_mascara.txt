Title: Mascara (1999)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Linda Kandel
Main Cast: Ione Skye, Lumi Cavazos, Amanda de Cadenet, Steven Schub, Steve Jones
Keywords: middle age

Overview: Mascara is the story of three very different women who rediscover the value of their friendship at a time when their lives are in turmoil. Panic sets in as they approach their thirtieth birthdays and nothing is going according to plan.
