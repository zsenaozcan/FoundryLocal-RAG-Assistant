Title: Fatal Fury: The Motion Picture (1994)
Genres: Action, Animation
Director: Masami Ôbari
Main Cast: Kazukiyo Nishikiori, Keiichi Nanba, Kotono Mitsuishi, Nobuyuki Hiyama, Shin'ichirou Miki
Keywords: based on video game

Overview: The Armor of Mars -- a suit of armor consisting of six pieces. If anyone from the bloodline of the ancient warrior Godimus were to find all six pieces for himself, he would receive power equal to the level of a god. The Curse of Godimus, however, also dictates that this same person would lose all sense of himself and become an insane killing machine.
