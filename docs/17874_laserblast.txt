Title: Laserblast (1978)
Genres: Horror, Action, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Michael Rae
Main Cast: Kim Milford, Cheryl Smith, Gianni Russo, Ron Masak, Eddie Deezen
Keywords: mutation, alien life-form, spaceship, alien, stop motion, independent film, desert, laser gun, exploding car, machine gun

Overview: Loner teenager Billy Duncan (Kim Milford) stumbles across a lost alien ray gun while wandering the desert one day. Billy uses the destructive weapon to get revenge on his enemies, but quickly finds himself beginning to turn into a violent, alien creature who destroys anyone who offends him.
