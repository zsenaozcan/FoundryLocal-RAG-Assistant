Title: Bunker Palace Hotel (1989)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Enki Bilal
Main Cast: Jean-Louis Trintignant, Carole Bouquet, Maria Schneider, Roger Dumas, Yann Collette
Keywords: dystopia

Overview: In an imaginary dictature of a futuristic world, rebellion has broken out. The men in power scramble to the Bunker Palace Hotel, a bunker built long ago for just this kind of contingency. But a rebel spy sneaks in, and although her nature is very quickly suspected, she is left to observe the raving of the decadent power class, who keeps wondering what happened to their leader, who has failed to show up. Plot written by Jay Han
