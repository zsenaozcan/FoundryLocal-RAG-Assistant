Title: Stuff for the Rich (1987)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sergio Corbucci
Main Cast: Paolo Villaggio, Serena Grandi, Maurizio Micheli, Aldo Ralli, Lino Banfi

Overview: 3 episodes set in Monte Carlo. In the first one a priest is compelled to become the lover of a princess. In the second, a con woman tries to cheat an insurance man. In the third, a man has to deal with his wife's suicidal attempts.
