Title: The Secret Ways (1961)
Genres: Adventure
Director: Phil Karlson
Main Cast: Richard Widmark, Sonja Ziemann, Walter Rilla, Charles Regnier, Senta Berger
Keywords: vienna austria

Overview: Vienna, 1956. After Soviet tanks crush the Hungarian uprising, soldier-of-fortune Mike Reynolds is hired to help a threatened Hungarian scientist (Prof. Jansci) escape from Budapest. He and Julia, the professor's daughter, cross the boulder posing as journalists, but they encounter a problem. The staunch freedom fighter doesn't want to go!
