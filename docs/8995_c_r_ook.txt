Title: C(r)ook (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Thriller
Director: Pepe Danquart
Main Cast: Moritz Bleibtreu, Henry Hübchen, Corinna Harfouch, Karlheinz Hackl, Josef Hader
Keywords: cooking, release from prison, psychologist, falling in love, gangster, police officer, psychotherapy, hoodlum
