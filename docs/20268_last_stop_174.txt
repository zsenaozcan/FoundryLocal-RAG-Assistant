Title: Last Stop 174 (2008)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Bruno Barreto
Main Cast: Cris Vianna, Marcello Melo Jr., Michel Gomes, Gabriela Luiz
Keywords: brazilian

Overview: Sandro was a boy who loved to sing rap, to kiss, to stare the statue of Christ the Redeemer and dreamed to go visit Copacabana. Growing up on streets, the story culminates at the infamous episode of June 12th 2000, when Sandro hijacked bus 174.
