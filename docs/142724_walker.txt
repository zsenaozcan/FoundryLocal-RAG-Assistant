Title: Walker (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Tsai Ming-liang
Main Cast: Lee Kang-Sheng

Overview: A metaphor for mourning as much as it is a reminder to slow down, Tsai Ming-liang's stunningly beautiful Walker features his acteur fétiche Lee Kang-Sheng as a red-robed monk barely locomoting through the bustling streets of Hong Kong.
