Title: Toute une nuit (1982)
Genres: Drama
Director: Chantal Akerman
Main Cast: Aurore Clément, Natalia Akerman, Paul Allio, Angelo Abazoglou, Frank Aendenboom
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Following over two dozen different people in the almost wordless atmosphere of a dark night in a Brussels town, Akerman examines acceptance and rejection in the realm of romance.
