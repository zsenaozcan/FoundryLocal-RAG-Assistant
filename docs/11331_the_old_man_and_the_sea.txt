Title: The Old Man and the Sea (1958)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: John Sturges
Main Cast: Spencer Tracy, Felipe Pazos, Harry Bellaver, Don Diamond, Mary Hemingway
Keywords: sea, cuba, mockery, human vs nature, fisherman

Overview: Based on one of Hemingway's most famous works, it centers upon Santiago, an aging, down on his luck Cuban fisherman. After catching nothing for nearly 3 months, he hooks a huge Marlin and struggles to land it far out in the Gulf Stream.
