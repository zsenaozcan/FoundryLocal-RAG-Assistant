Title: The Lawyer (1970)
Genres: Crime, Mystery, Drama
Director: Sidney J. Furie
Main Cast: Barry Newman, Diana Muldaur, Harold Gould, Booth Colman, Ken Swofford
Keywords: lawyer, socialite

Overview: Wilma Harrison had it all. A wealthy doctor for a husband. A big playboy for a lover. A beautiful home. And a horrible death.
