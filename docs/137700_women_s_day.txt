Title: Women's Day (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Maria Sadowska
Main Cast: Katarzyna Kwiatkowska, Eryk Lubos, Klara Bielawka, Ewa Konstancja Bułhak, Agata Kulesza
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Helina, a modest checkout assistant at the Butterfly retail chain, dreams about getting a better life for herself and her daughter. An opportunity presents itself when Helina becomes a store manager. However, she soon discovers that the price for better wages and an improved standard of living is dishonesty, employee exploitation, and fraud.
