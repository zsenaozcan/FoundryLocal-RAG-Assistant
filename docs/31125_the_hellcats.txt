Title: The Hellcats (1968)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Robert F. Slatzer
Main Cast: Ross Hagen, Dee Duffy, Sharyn Kinzie, Del 'Sonny' West, Robert F. Slatzer
Keywords: biker film

Overview: The brother and fiancee of a dead policeman infiltrate a female-led biker gang to uncover his murderer.
