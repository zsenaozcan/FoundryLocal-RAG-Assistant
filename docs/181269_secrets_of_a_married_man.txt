Title: Secrets of a Married Man (1984)
Genres: Drama
Director: William A. Graham
Main Cast: William Shatner, Michelle Phillips, Glynn Turman, Cybill Shepherd, Jackson Davies
Keywords: prostitute, pimp, harassment, police, crisis, lingerie

Overview: A man (Shatner) going through a mid-life crisis, starts patronizing prostitutes. Eventually, he meets a very expensive one (Shepherd) and he thinks he has it all. That is until her pimp starts hounding him.
