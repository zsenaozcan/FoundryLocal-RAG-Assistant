Title: The Carpenter (1988)
Genres: Horror, Thriller

Main Cast: Wings Hauser, Lynne Adams, Pierre Lenoir, Barbara Jones, Louise-Marie Mennier
Keywords: mental breakdown, house, cheating husband, carpenter, ghost, canuxploitation, restoration
