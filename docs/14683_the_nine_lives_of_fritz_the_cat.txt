Title: The Nine Lives of Fritz the Cat (1974)
Genres: Animation, Comedy
Director: Robert Taylor
Main Cast: Skip Hinnant, Reva Rose, Bob Holt, Robert Ridgely
Keywords: sex, cat, 1970s, cheating, lust and impulsiveness, joint, sexual revolution, hell, sequel, cult comedy

Overview: Fritz, now married and with a son, is desperate to escape from the domestic hell he now finds himself in. Lighting up a joint, he begins to dream about his eight other lives, hoping to find one that will provide a pleasant distraction.
