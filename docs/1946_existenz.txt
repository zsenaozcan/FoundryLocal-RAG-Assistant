Title: eXistenZ (1999)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Science Fiction, Horror
Director: David Cronenberg
Main Cast: Jennifer Jason Leigh, Jude Law, Ian Holm, Willem Dafoe, Don McKellar
Keywords: video game, hacker, bodyguard, pop star, virtual reality, dystopia, virtual fight

Overview: A game designer on the run from assassins must play her latest virtual reality creation with a marketing trainee to determine if the game has been damaged.
