Title: Maidan (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Sergey Loznitsa
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: A chronicle of the civil uprising against the regime of Ukrainian president Viktor Yanukovych that took place in Kiev in the winter of 2013/14. The film follows the progress of the revolution: from peaceful rallies, half a million strong in the Maidan square, to the bloody street battles between protesters and riot police.
