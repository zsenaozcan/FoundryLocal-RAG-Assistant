Title: Hammer of the Gods (2013)
Genres: History, Action, Adventure
Director: Farren Blackburn
Main Cast: Charlie Bewley, Clive Standen, James Cosmo, Elliot Cowan, Ivan Kaye
Keywords: viking

Overview: Action epic sees a passionate young man transform into a brutal warrior as he travels the unforgiving landscape in search of his long lost brother Hakan The Ferrocious, whose people are relying on him to restore order to their kingdom.
