Title: Donald Cried (2017)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Kris Avedisian
Main Cast: Louisa Krause, Allie Marshall, Ariana DeFusco, Alexander Cook, Ted Arcidi
Keywords: hometown, journey, death of grandmother, childhood friend, favor

Overview: Returning home to working class Warwick, Rhode Island, Peter Latang encounters childhood friend Donald Treebeck for what starts as a simple favor and turns into a long van ride into two friends past.
