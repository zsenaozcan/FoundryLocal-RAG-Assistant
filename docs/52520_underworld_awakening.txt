Title: Underworld: Awakening (2012)
Genres: Fantasy, Action, Horror
Director: Måns Mårlind
Main Cast: Kate Beckinsale, Stephen Rea, Michael Ealy, Theo James, India Eisley
Keywords: vampire, daughter, hybrid, child vampire, werewolf, imax, lab experiment, werewolf child, fang vamp
