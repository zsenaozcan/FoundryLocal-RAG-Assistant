Title: Miss Peregrine's Home for Peculiar Children (2016)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Adventure
Director: Tim Burton
Main Cast: Eva Green, Asa Butterfield, Samuel L. Jackson, Judi Dench, Rupert Everett
Keywords: time travel, orphan, superpower

Overview: A teenager finds himself transported to an island where he must help protect a group of orphans with special powers from creatures intent on destroying them.
