Title: How About You... (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Anthony Byrne
Main Cast: Vanessa Redgrave, Brenda Fricker, Joss Ackland, Hayley Atwell, Orla Brady
Keywords: independent film

Overview: The story of Ellie, a young woman left in charge of the residential home run by her older sister, over the Christmas period. Whilst most of the residents have left to spend the festive period with their families, four residents, known as 'the hardcore' remain.
