Title: Henry V (1944)
Genres: Drama, Action, History
Director: Laurence Olivier
Main Cast: Laurence Olivier, Renée Asherson, Ralph Truman, Ernest Thesiger, Frederick Cooper

Overview: Olivier mustered out of the navy to film this adaptation of Shakespeare’s history. Embroiled in World War II, Britons took courage from this tale of a king who surmounts overwhelming odds and emerges victorious.
