Title: Blood Alley (1955)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Thriller
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: John Wayne, Lauren Bacall, Paul Fix, Joy Kim, Berry Kroeger
Keywords: orient, merchant marine

Overview: A merchant marine captain, rescued from the Chinese Communists by local visitors, is "shanghaied" into transporting the whole village to Hong Kong on an ancient paddle steamer.
