Title: A Streetcar Named Desire (1984)
Genres: Unknown
Director: John Erman
Main Cast: Ann-Margret, Treat Williams, Beverly D'Angelo, Randy Quaid, Dan Hewitt Owens

Overview: Blanche Dubois goes to visit her pregnant sister and husband Stanley in New Orleans. Stanley doesn't like her, and starts pushing her for information on some property he know was left to the sisters. He discovers she has mortgaged the place and spent all the money, and wants to find out all he can about her. Even more friction develops between the two while they are in the apartment together...
