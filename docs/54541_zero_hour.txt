Title: Zero Hour! (1957)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Hall Bartlett
Main Cast: Dana Andrews, Linda Darnell, Sterling Hayden, Elroy 'Crazylegs' Hirsch, Geoffrey Toone
Keywords: airport, canada, airplane, disaster, food poisoning, doctor, crash landing

Overview: A routine flight turns into a major emergency as passengers and crew succumb to food poisoning - is there anyone on board who knows how to fly a plane? If that sounds vaguely familiar, it's because 'Airplane' was a send-up of this forerunner of the 1970s disaster movie. Written by Michael Brooke
