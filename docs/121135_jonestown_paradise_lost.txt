Title: Jonestown: Paradise Lost (2007)
Genres: History, Documentary
Director: Tim Wolochatiuk
Main Cast: Ted Biggs, Rick Roberts, Quentin Krog, Brendan Murray, Alon Nashman

Overview: Jonestown: Paradise Lost was a 2007 documentary played on the History Channel about the final days of Jonestown, the Peoples Temple, and Jim Jones. From eyewitness and survivor accounts, the program recreates the last week before the mass murder-suicide on November 18, 1978.
