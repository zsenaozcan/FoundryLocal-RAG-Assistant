Title: Company (2011)
Genres: Music
Director: Lonny Price
Main Cast: Neil Patrick Harris, Patti LuPone, Martha Plimpton, Anika Noni Rose, Jim Walton

Overview: Following five couples and their friend Robert (Neil Patrick Harris), the perpetual bachelor, Company explores the true meaning of being in a relationship through a series of vignettes. Winner of the 1971 Tony Award for Best Musical, Neil Patrick Harris (How I Met Your Mother, Rent) will led an all-star cast in a sold out event at the New York Philharmonic.
