Title: Run & Jump (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Steph Green
Main Cast: Maxine Peake, Will Forte, Edward MacLiam, Sharon Horgan, Brendan Morris
Keywords: wife, irish, stroke, woman director, recovery

Overview: After a stroke leaves her husband disabled and fundamentally changed, a spirited Irish wife struggles to keep her family members together. All the while they are under the microscope of an American researcher documenting their recovery process.
