Title: Missionary Man (2007)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Thriller
Director: Dolph Lundgren
Main Cast: Dolph Lundgren, Sophia Taylor Ali, Chelsea Ricketts, Matthew Tompkins, James Chalke
Keywords: modern-day western, modern western

Overview: A mysterious stranger rolls into town on a unique motorcycle. All he carries is the bible and a desire for justice. Past vengeance collides as Ryder rights an injustice from his past and liberates the small town from a malicious oppressor.
