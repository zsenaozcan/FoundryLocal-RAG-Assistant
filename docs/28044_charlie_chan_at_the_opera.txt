Title: Charlie Chan at the Opera (1936)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: H. Bruce Humberstone
Main Cast: Warner Oland, Boris Karloff, Keye Luke, William Demarest, Guy Usher
Keywords: opera, charlie chan

Overview: A dangerous amnesiac escapes from an asylum, hides in the opera house, and is suspected of getting revenge on those who tried to murder him 13 years ago.
