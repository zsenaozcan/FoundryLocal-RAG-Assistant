Title: Gloss (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Andrei Konchalovsky
Main Cast: Yuliya Vysotskaya, Aleksandr Domogarov, Aleksey Serebryakov, Irina Rozanova, Tatyana Arntgolts

Overview: A pretty, young girl from a coal-mining town comes to Moscow with dreams of becoming a supermodel in this satirical look at the fashion industry.
