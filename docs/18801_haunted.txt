Title: Haunted (1995)
Genres: Mystery, Horror
Director: Lewis Gilbert
Main Cast: Kate Beckinsale, Aidan Quinn, Anthony Andrews, John Gielgud, Anna Massey
Keywords: based on novel, secret, professor, nudity, supernatural, murder, haunting, family, doubt

Overview: Professor David Ash exposes false spiritulists and mediums. He is invited to Edbrook to resolve the fears and torments within its secretive family. Soon after arriving Ash begins to doubt his own senses, and watching the strange behaviour of its residents does not make his task any easier. In time, he finds there's more to Edbrook than even he can debunk.
