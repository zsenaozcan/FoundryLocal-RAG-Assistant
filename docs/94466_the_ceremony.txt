Title: The Ceremony (1971)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Nagisa Ōshima
Main Cast: Kenzô Kawarasaki, Atsuko Kaku, Nobuko Otowa, Kei Satô, Maki Takayama
Keywords: post war japan

Overview: Oshima’s magisterial epic, centering on the ambivalent surviving heir of the Sakurada clan, uses ritual and the microcosm of the traditional family to trace the rise and fall of militaristic Japan across several decades.
