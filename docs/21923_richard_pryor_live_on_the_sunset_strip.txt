Title: Richard Pryor: Live on the Sunset Strip (1982)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Joe Layton
Main Cast: Richard Pryor, Gene Cross, Julie Hampton, Jesse Jackson
Keywords: stand-up comedian, laughter, celebrity impersonation

Overview: The third and most successful of four stand-up act movies release by Richard Pryor on film. The stand-up act includes Pryor's frank discussion about his freebasing addiction, as well as the infamous night on June 9, 1980 that he caught on fire.
