Title: Avenging Angel (1985)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Robert Vincent O'Neill
Main Cast: Betsy Russell, Rory Calhoun, Susan Tyrrell, Ossie Davis, Ross Hagen
Keywords: prostitute, revenge

Overview: Molly, former baby prostitute "Angel" from Sunset Boulevard, has managed to leave her street life with help of Lt. Andrews. She studies law at an university and aims to become attorney. When she learns that Andrews was shot during a failed observation by brutal gangsters, she returns downtown to take revenge
