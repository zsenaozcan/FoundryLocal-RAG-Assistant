Title: Blues Harp (1998)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Takashi Miike
Main Cast: Mickey Curtis, Seiichi Tanabe, Hiroyuki Ikeuchi, Saori Sekino

Overview: Ambitious yakuza Kenji befriends harmonica-playing bartender Chuji, who moonlights as a part-time drug-dealer for the opposing gang. Their friendship is threatened by Kenji's plans for advancement, as well as by his bodyguards growing jealousy of Chuji.
