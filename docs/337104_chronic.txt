Title: Chronic (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michel Franco
Main Cast: Tim Roth, Bitsie Tulloch, David Dastmalchian, Claire van der Boom, Tate Ellington
Keywords: nurse, architect, man, past, terminal illness, mystery, moral ambiguity, patient, guilt, character study
