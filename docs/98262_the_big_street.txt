Title: The Big Street (1942)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Irving Reis
Main Cast: Henry Fonda, Lucille Ball, Barton MacLane, Eugene Pallette, Agnes Moorehead
Keywords: unrequited love, runyonesque characters

Overview: Runyonesque busboy Little Pinks (Henry Fonda) hitchhikes to Florida with a gangster's (Barton MacLane) ex-moll (Lucille Ball) in a wheelchair.
