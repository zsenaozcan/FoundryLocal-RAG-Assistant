Title: The Second Jungle Book: Mowgli & Baloo (1997)
Genres: Adventure, Family
Director: Dee McLachlan
Main Cast: Gulshan Grover, Jamie Williams, Billy Campbell, Roddy McDowall, David Paul Francis
Keywords: circus, boy, bear, jungle, monkey, woman director

Overview: In this exciting live-action adventure, young Mowgli, an orphan raised by wolves, is spotted by a scout for a giant circus. Accompanied by a cruel hunter and a snake charmer, the scout sets out to trap Mowgli. But with the help of Baloo the bear and Bagheera the panther, little Mowgli leads the adults into his biggest and wildest adventure yet! A fun-filled movie every member of the family will enjoy.
