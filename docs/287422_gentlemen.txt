Title: Gentlemen (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Romance
Director: Mikael Marcimain
Main Cast: David Dencik, Ruth Vega Fernandez, Sverrir Gudnason, David Fukamachi Regnfors, Pernilla August

Overview: Beaten up, bruised, and scared, a young writer hides in a Stockholm apartment, writing the story of its disappeared inhabitants: the flamboyant and charismatic Morgan brothers.
