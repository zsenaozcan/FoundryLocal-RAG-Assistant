Title: Tromeo & Juliet (1996)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Lloyd Kaufman
Main Cast: Jane Jensen, Will Keenan, Valentine Miele, William Beckwith, Debbie Rochon
Keywords: shakespeare

Overview: A modern, punk adaptation of Shakespeare's classic. Told irreverently, this film attempts to impact the viewer in the same way theatre-goers were effected in Shakespeare's time. Bawdy, Violent, Humorous, and Romantic.
