Title: Runaway (1984)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Science Fiction
Director: Michael Crichton
Main Cast: Tom Selleck, Cynthia Rhodes, Gene Simmons, Kirstie Alley, Stan Shaw
Keywords: technology, future, chase, revenge, murder, conspiracy, robot, explosion, violence

Overview: In the near future, a police officer specializes in malfunctioning robots. When a robot turns out to have been programmed to kill, he begins to uncover a homicidal plot to create killer robots... and his son becomes a target.
