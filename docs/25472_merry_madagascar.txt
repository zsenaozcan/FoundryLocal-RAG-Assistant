Title: Merry Madagascar (2009)
Genres: Family, Animation
Director: David Soren
Main Cast: Cedric the Entertainer, Andy Richter, Carl Reiner, David Schwimmer, Danny Jacobs
Keywords: holiday

Overview: The Zoosters are back in an all-new holiday adventure. When Santa and his reindeer crash onto the island of Madagascar it's up to Alex, Marty, Gloria, Melman and those wacky penguins to save Christmas. Get ready for a sleigh full of laughs in this hilarious new holiday classic.
