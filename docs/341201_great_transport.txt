Title: Great Transport (1983)
Genres: War
Director: Veljko Bulajić
Main Cast: James Franciscus, Steve Railsback, Edward Albert, Robert Vaughn
Keywords: world war ii, partisan

Overview: This WW2 epic was one of the last movies of that kind made in former Yugoslavia. It tells the true story of great transport of Partizans from Vojvodina to Bosnia in 1943.
