Title: The Convent (1995)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Manoel de Oliveira
Main Cast: Catherine Deneuve, John Malkovich, Luís Miguel Cintra, Leonor Silveira

Overview: The journey of Michael Padovic, an American professor who arrives with his wife, Helene, at a Portuguese convent where he expects to find the documents needed to prove his theory: Shakespeare was born in Spain; not in England.
