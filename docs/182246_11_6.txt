Title: 11.6 (2013)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Philippe Godeau
Main Cast: François Cluzet, Bouli Lanners, Corinne Masiero, Juana Acosta, Johan Libéreau

Overview: In November 2009, French security van driver Toni Musulin (François Cluzet) makes off with nearly 12 million euros, then turns himself over to the authorities a week later.
