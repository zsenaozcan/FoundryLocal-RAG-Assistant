Title: Miracle in Cell No. 7 (2013)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Lee Hwan-kyung
Main Cast: Ryu Seung-ryong, Park Shin-hye, Kal So-won, Jung Jin-young, Oh Dal-su
Keywords: prison cell, father daughter relationship

Overview: A story about a mentally ill man wrongfully accused of murder and his relationship with his lovingly adorable 6 year old daughter.
