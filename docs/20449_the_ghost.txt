Title: The Ghost (2008)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Karen Oganesyan
Main Cast: Konstantin Khabenskiy, Vladimir Mashkov, Chulpan Khamatova, Armen Dzhigarkhanyan, Vitaly Kishchenko
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A novelist struggling with writer's block enlists the aid of an assassin for some inspiration -- fully unaware that he is being set up to take the blame for a murder.
