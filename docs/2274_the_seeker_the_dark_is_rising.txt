Title: The Seeker: The Dark Is Rising (2007)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Fantasy, Family, Thriller
Director: David L. Cunningham
Main Cast: Ian McShane, Christopher Eccleston, Gregory Smith, Jonathan Jackson, Wendy Crewson
Keywords: fight, dynasty, chosen one, earth, immortality, bad power, good vs evil, based on young adult novel
