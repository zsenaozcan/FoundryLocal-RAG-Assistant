Title: Beach Red (1967)
Genres: Action, War
Director: Cornel Wilde
Main Cast: Cornel Wilde, Rip Torn, Burr DeBenning, Patrick Wolfe, Jean Wallace
Keywords: japanese, beach, world war ii, army, jungle, pacific island

Overview: American troops storm ashore on a Japanese-held island and push inland while their enemies plan a counterattack in this look at warfare. Soldiers on both sides are haunted by memories of home and the horrifying, sickening images they find in combat.
