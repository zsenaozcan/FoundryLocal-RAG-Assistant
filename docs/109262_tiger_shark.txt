Title: Tiger Shark (1932)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Howard Hawks
Main Cast: Edward G. Robinson, Richard Arlen, Zita Johann, Leila Bennett, J. Carrol Naish
Keywords: shipwreck, fisherman, tuna

Overview: A Portuguese tuna fisherman (Edward G. Robinson) catches his bride (Zita Johann) with his first mate (Richard Arlen).
