Title: Hussar Ballad (1962)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Eldar Ryazanov
Main Cast: Larisa Golubkina, Yuriy Yakovlev, Igor Ilyinsky, Nikolay Kryuchkov, Tatyana Shmyga
Keywords: musical

Overview: A young girl decides to join a Husar squadron and fight against Napoleon. Dressed as a man she has a hard time adjusting to the rude Husar life styles.
