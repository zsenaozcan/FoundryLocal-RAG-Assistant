Title: The Member of the Wedding (1952)
Genres: Drama
Director: Fred Zinnemann
Main Cast: Ethel Waters, Julie Harris, Brandon De Wilde, Arthur Franz, Nancy Gates
Keywords: runaway, wedding, running away

Overview: Carson  McCullers stage hit brought to the screen by Fred Zinnemann starring Ethel Waters and Julie Harris. Tomboy Julie Harris dreams of running away with her brother and new fiancee away from the Deep South.
