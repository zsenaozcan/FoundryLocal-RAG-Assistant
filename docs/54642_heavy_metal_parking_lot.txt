Title: Heavy Metal Parking Lot (1986)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Jeff Krulik
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: interview, heavy metal, maryland, fan culture, music fan, short

Overview: Heavy Metal Parking Lot documents heavy metal music fans tailgating in the parking lot outside the Capital Centre (since demolished) in Landover, Maryland, on May 31, 1986, before a Judas Priest concert (with opening act Dokken).
