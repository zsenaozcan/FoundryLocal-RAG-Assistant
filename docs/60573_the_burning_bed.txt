Title: The Burning Bed (1984)
Genres: TV Movie, Crime, Drama
Director: Robert Greenwald
Main Cast: Farrah Fawcett, Paul Le Mat, Richard Masur, Grace Zabriskie, Penelope Milford
Keywords: domestic violence, killer

Overview: Abused battered wife has had enough of husband beating up on her. Everywhere she turns for help, there's not much anyone will do. After he rapes one night, she sets the bed on fire with him in it while asleep.
