Title: Coogan's Bluff (1968)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Don Siegel
Main Cast: Clint Eastwood, Lee J. Cobb, Susan Clark, Tisha Sterling, Don Stroud
Keywords: escape, fish out of water, chrysler building

Overview: Coogan (Clint Eastwood) an Arizona deputy sheriff goes to New York to pick up a prisoner. While escorting the prisoner to the airport he escapes and Coogan heads into the City to recapture him.
