Title: A Black Veil for Lisa (1968)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Massimo Dallamano
Main Cast: John Mills, Luciana Paluzzi, Robert Hoffmann, Renate Kasché, Tullio Altamura
Keywords: sex, adultery, detective, hitman, narcotics

Overview: When a narcotics officer suspects that his beautiful wife, who is a former criminal, is having an affair, he becomes so obsessed with this that he has major problems to manage their work. In the end, his obsession with devastating consequences when he captures the assassin he hunts.
