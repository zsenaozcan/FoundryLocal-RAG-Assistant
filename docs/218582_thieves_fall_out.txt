Title: Thieves Fall Out (1941)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ray Enright
Main Cast: Eddie Albert, Joan Leslie, Jane Darwell, Alan Hale, Anthony Quinn

Overview: Eddie Barnes, tired of being a nobody and living with his parents, decides to cash in his mother's legacy and use the money to buy a business. Unfortunately, Eddie's mother has to die before the broker can collect the full value of the policy and the broker's gangster partner doesn't want to wait for nature to take its course.
