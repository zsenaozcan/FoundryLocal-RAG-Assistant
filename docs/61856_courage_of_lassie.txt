Title: Courage of Lassie (1946)
Genres: Family, Adventure, Drama
Director: Fred M. Wilcox
Main Cast: Elizabeth Taylor, Frank Morgan, Tom Drake, Selena Royle, Harry Davenport
Keywords: amnesia, army, car crash, rancher, military life, lassie

Overview: Bill's separated from his litter, making friends with the wild creatures until he's found and adopted by young Kathie. An accident separates him from her, and he's drafted into K-9 duty in the trenches until battle fatigue takes its toll and he turns vicious. And even though he finds his way back home, he may be condemned as a killer.
