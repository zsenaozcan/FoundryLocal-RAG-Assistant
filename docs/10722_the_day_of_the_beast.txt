Title: The Day of the Beast (1995)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Horror, Crime
Director: Álex de la Iglesia
Main Cast: Alex Angulo, Armando De Razza, Santiago Segura, Terele Pávez, Nathalie Seseña
Keywords: neo-nazi, prophecy, madrid, telecaster, virgin, black magic, heavy metal, hallucinogen, anti-christ, murder

Overview: The story revolves around a Basque Roman Catholic priest dedicated to committing as many sins as possible (Angulo), a death metal salesman from Carabanchel (Segura), and the Italian host of a TV show on the occult (De Razza). These go on a literal "trip" through Christmas-time Madrid to hunt for and prevent the reincarnation of the Antichrist.
