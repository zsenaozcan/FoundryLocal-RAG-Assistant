Title: The Double (1971)
Genres: Thriller, Mystery
Director: Romolo Guerrieri
Main Cast: Ewa Aulin, Jean Sorel, Lucia Bosé, Silvano Tranquilli, Sergio Doria

Overview: A man is shot in an underground car-park by a mysterious bearded man. As he dies he recollects the events that led him to this situation, including adulterous liaisons and jealous envy.
