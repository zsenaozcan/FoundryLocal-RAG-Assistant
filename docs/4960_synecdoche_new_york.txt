Title: Synecdoche, New York (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Charlie Kaufman
Main Cast: Philip Seymour Hoffman, Samantha Morton, Jennifer Jason Leigh, Michelle Williams, Catherine Keener
Keywords: new york, man-woman relation, writer

Overview: A theater director struggles with his work, and the women in his life, as he attempts to create a life-size replica of New York inside a warehouse as part of his new play.
