Title: Flu Bird Horror (2008)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Leigh Scott
Main Cast: Clare Carey, Lance Guest, Sarah Butler, Jonathon Trent, Rebekah Kochan
Keywords: monster, teenager, eaten alive, virus, h5n1 bird flu

Overview: A group of teenagers go on a excursion to the mountains. There, they are attacked by birds infected with a lethal virus. When the teens reach a nearby village, the haunting birds start passing their virus on to all the dwellers.
