Title: The Blue Dahlia (1946)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: George Marshall
Main Cast: Alan Ladd, Veronica Lake, William Bendix, Howard Da Silva, Doris Dowling
Keywords: femme fatale, framed for murder, film noir

Overview: This film noir stars Alan Ladd as a war veteran framed for the murder of his own wife and follows his search for the real murderer. Veronica Lake plays the sultry femme fatale who provides unexpected help.
