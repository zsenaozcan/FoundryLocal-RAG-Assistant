Title: Dollybirds (1997)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Péter Tímár
Main Cast: János Gálvölgyi, Sándor Almási, Anita Tóth, Reviczky Gábor, Éva Igó
Keywords: socialism

Overview: The movie is set in the actual "Ki Mit Tud?" talent contest in 1962. In reality the contest in dance music category was won by an army brass band. "Omega" which later became one of the most successful Hungarian rock bands came out in the second place.
