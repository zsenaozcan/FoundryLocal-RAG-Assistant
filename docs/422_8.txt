Title: 8½ (1963)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama
Director: Federico Fellini
Main Cast: Marcello Mastroianni, Claudia Cardinale, Anouk Aimée, Sandra Milo, Rossella Falk
Keywords: individual, scapegoat, adultery, screenplay, cinecittà, film making, film producer, depression, suicide attempt, sun glasses

Overview: With 8 ½ Frederico Fellini leaves a self-portrait where dreams and reality are a mix. With help from a most excellent cast and unique scenery this self reflecting film is one of his master works.
