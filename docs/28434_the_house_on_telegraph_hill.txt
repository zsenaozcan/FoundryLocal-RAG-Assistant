Title: The House on Telegraph Hill (1951)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Robert Wise
Main Cast: Richard Basehart, Valentina Cortese, William Lundigan, Fay Baker, Gordon Gebert
Keywords: san francisco, old house, film noir

Overview: Concentration camp survivor Victoria Kowelska finds herself involved in mystery, greed, and murder when she assumes the identity of a dead friend in order to gain passage to America.
