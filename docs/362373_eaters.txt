Title: Eaters (2015)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Johnny Tabor
Main Cast: Marcelle Bowman, Robert Dean, Tristan Parrish Moore, Jonathan Haltiwanger
Keywords: kidnapping, biker

Overview: After their friend goes missing, five road-tripping friends must confront a violent biker gang, but after they narrowly escape, their nightmare goes from bad to spine-chilling.
