Title: Rid Of Me (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: James Westby
Main Cast: Katie O’Grady, John Keyser, Cora Benesh, John Breen, Orianna Herrman
Keywords: self-discovery, dark comedy, break-up, divorce

Overview: A scathing black comedy of embarrassment that charts the emotional breakdown and rebirth of a woman ripe for self-discovery.
