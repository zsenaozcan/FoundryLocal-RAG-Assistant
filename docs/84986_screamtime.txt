Title: Screamtime (1983)
Genres: Horror
Director: Michael Armstrong
Main Cast: Vincent Russo, Michael Gordon, Marie Scinto, Robin Bailey, Jonathon Morris
Keywords: puppeteer, garden gnome, times square

Overview: Compilation of three short horror films: "That's The Way To Do It", "Dreamhouse" and "Do You Believe In Fairies?" plus some new linking material.
