Title: Haunted Castle (2001)
Genres: Animation, Horror, Thriller
Director: Ben Stassen
Main Cast: Jasper Steverlinck, Kyoko Baertsoen, Harry Shearer

Overview: A young musician and singer named Johnny has been notified by a law firm that his mother, an aging rock star whom Johnny hasn't seen or heard from since he was 3 years old, has died in a helicopter accident. Johnny has been willed her castle and all of her property and money, but he must visit the actual castle to claim these things.
