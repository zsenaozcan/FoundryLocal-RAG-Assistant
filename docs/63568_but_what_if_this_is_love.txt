Title: But What If This Is Love? (1961)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Yuli Raizman
Main Cast: Zhanna Prokhorenko, Igor Pushkaryov, Aleksandra Nazarova, Andrey Mironov, Yevgeni Zharikov

Overview: High school students Ksenya and Boris are in love but all the world id against them. Could they fight it and will the first love win?
