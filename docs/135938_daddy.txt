Title: Daddy (1995)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Maciej Ślesicki
Main Cast: Bogusław Linda, Dorota Segda, Aleksandra Maliszewska, Cezary Pazura, Krystyna Janda

Overview: Daddy is the story of a divorced father fighting for the right to raise his 7-year-old daughter. When his marriage falls apart, he decides to kidnap his daughter rather than let the court award custody to his mentally ill wife, whom he deems unfit to raise their child. But as he quickly finds out, it's easier to be a real man than it is to be a real father.
