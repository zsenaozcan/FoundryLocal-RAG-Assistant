Title: Black Snow (2017)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Crime, Thriller
Director: Martín Hodara
Main Cast: Ricardo Darín, Leonardo Sbaraglia, Laia Costa, Dolores Fonzi, Federico Luppi
Keywords: isolation, cabin, snow, hunt, dark past

Overview: Accused of killing his brother during adolescence, Salvador lives alone in the middle of Patagonia. Several decades later, his brother Marcos and his sister-in-law Laura, come to convince him to sell the lands they share by inheritance.
