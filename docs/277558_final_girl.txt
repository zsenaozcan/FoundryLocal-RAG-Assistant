Title: Final Girl (2015)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Tyler Shields
Main Cast: Abigail Breslin, Alexander Ludwig, Wes Bentley, Cameron Bright, Logan Huffman

Overview: Veronica, the new girl in town, is lured into the woods by a group of senior boys looking to make her a victim. But the boys don't know that Veronica's been trained to handle herself in surprisingly lethal ways.
