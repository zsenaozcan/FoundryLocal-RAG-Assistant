Title: The Beach (1954)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Alberto Lattuada
Main Cast: Martine Carol, Raf Vallone, Carlo Bianco

Overview: A prostitute goes on vacation to a beach resort with her young daughter (who's being schooled by nuns). Everything is cheerful and pleasant at the seashore; the customers at the hotel think she's a poor widow  - she always wears black - and treat her like a lady. But when one of her former clients arrives, her carefree days on the beach are numbered.
