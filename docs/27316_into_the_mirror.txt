Title: Into the Mirror (2003)
Genres: Crime, Horror, Mystery
Director: Kim Sung-ho
Main Cast: Yoo Ji-tae, Kim Hye-na, Gi Ju-bong, Kim Myoeng-su, Lee Young-jin

Overview: Woo Yeong-min retired from the police force after trying to save his partner and causing his death. After a series of mysterious deaths in the shopping mall in which his uncle has allowed him to work as a security guard prior to its reopening, he must face both his own fear of mirrors and the mystery surrounding the fire that closed down the mall.
