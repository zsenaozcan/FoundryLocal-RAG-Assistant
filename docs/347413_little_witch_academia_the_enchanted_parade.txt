Title: Little Witch Academia: The Enchanted Parade (2015)
Genres: Action, Animation, Fantasy, Comedy
Director: Yoh Yoshinari
Main Cast: Megumi Han, Fumiko Orikasa, Michiyo Murase, Youko Hikasa, Noriko Hidaka
Keywords: witch, magic, magical girl, anime

Overview: In Enchanted Parade, Akko, Lotte and Sucy, having gotten in trouble yet again, are forced to team up with three other troublemakers; Amanda O'Neill, Constance Braunschweig Albrechtsburger and Jasmineka Antonenko, and must make an annual witch parade a success or else face expulsion.
