Title: Nico and Dani (2000)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Cesc Gay
Main Cast: Fernando Ramallo, Jordi Vilches, Marieta Orozco, Esther Nubiola, Chisco Amado
Keywords: gay, sea, coming out, first time, best friend, summer vacation

Overview: A Spanish coming of age story focusing on the antics of two 17 year olds, who have a posh beach house almost all to themselves one summer. This is also a summer of sexual awakenings.
