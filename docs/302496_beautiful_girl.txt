Title: Beautiful Girl (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Stevie Long
Main Cast: Brendan Sexton III, Diora Baird, Matthew Gray Gubler, Stevie Long, Will Sasso
Keywords: porno star

Overview: Odessa is a beautiful girl addicted to the attention and money that being an adult film star brings. William, a lonely mechanic, has a crush on her, and she indulges his fantasy and leads him on. Thinking he can save her and help her find a normal life, he befriends her drug-fueled bodyguard, Angry Jack, to get close to her.
