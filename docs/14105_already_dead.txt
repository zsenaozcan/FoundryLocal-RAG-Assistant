Title: Already Dead (2007)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Joe Otting
Main Cast: Ron Eldard, Til Schweiger, Patrick Kilpatrick, Marisa Coughlan, Christopher Plummer
Keywords: prison, babysitter, revenge, grief, suspense

Overview: When Thomas Archer's happy &amp; successful life is all but ruined following a horrific attack he is presented with a perfect opportunity to exact physical revenge upon the man who caused all his grief. However the immediate consequences of his actions and inactions are much more complicated than he imagined.
