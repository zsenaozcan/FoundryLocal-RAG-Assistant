Title: Men & Chicken (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Anders Thomas Jensen
Main Cast: Mads Mikkelsen, Nikolaj Lie Kaas, David Dencik, Nicolas Bro, Søren Malling

Overview: Men &amp; Chicken is a black comedy about two outcast brothers, who by getting to know their unknown family also discover a horrible truth about themselves and their relatives.
