Title: Guilty Hearts (2006)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: George Augusto
Main Cast: Kathy Bates, Julie Delpy, Andrea Di Stefano, Anna Faris, Frank Gallegos
Keywords: independent film, woman director

Overview: Over the course of this drama's six separate stories, a variety of couples struggle with the infidelity, dishonesty and heartbreak that plagues their varied pasts. In overcoming their challenges, these diverse individuals embrace a brighter future.
