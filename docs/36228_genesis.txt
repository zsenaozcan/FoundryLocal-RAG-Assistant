Title: Genesis (1998)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Romance, Foreign
Director: Nacho Cerdà
Main Cast: Pep Tosar, Trae Houlihan

Overview: A sculptor is traumatized by the death of his wife in a car accident. He builds a sculpture in her memory. As the lifelike sculpture begins to bleed through the cracks of clay, the sculptor's flesh mutates and crumbles away...
