Title: Dungeons & Dragons: The Book of Vile Darkness (2012)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Gerry Lively
Main Cast: Charlotte Hunter, Jack Derges, Anthony Howell, Eleanor Gecks, Habib Nasib Nader
Keywords: heroic mission, sword and sorcery

Overview: A noble warrior must battle dragons and demons while upholding his moral code as he covertly joins a group of villains to rescue his kidnapped father from Shathrax, the Mind Flayer, who threatens to destroy the world.
