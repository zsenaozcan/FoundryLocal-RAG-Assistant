Title: Wild Roomies (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Oliver Robins
Main Cast: A.J. Buckley, Holly Fields, Jennifer Lyons, Joel Michaely, David Wheir

Overview: After his last girlfriend slept with his last roommate, Reno Rizzolla moves to L.A. with his new girlfriend... and chaos ensues when the debt on the house forces him to get roommates.
