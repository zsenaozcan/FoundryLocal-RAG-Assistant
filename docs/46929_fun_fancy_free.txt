Title: Fun & Fancy Free (1947)
Genres: Animation, Family, Music
Director: Jack Kinney
Main Cast: Edgar Bergen, Dinah Shore, Mortimer Snerd, Luana Patten, Walt Disney

Overview: Jiminy Cricket hosts two Disney animated shorts: "Bongo," about a circus bear escaping to the wild, and "Mickey and the Beanstalk," a take on the famous fairy tale.
