Title: Bright Eyes (1934)
Genres: Drama, Family, Music
Director: David Butler
Main Cast: Shirley Temple, James Dunn, Jane Darwell, Judith Allen, Lois Wilson
Keywords: uncle, maid, godfather, aviator

Overview: An orphaned girl is taken in by a snobbish family at the insistence of their rich, crotchety uncle, even as her devoted aviator godfather fights for custody.
