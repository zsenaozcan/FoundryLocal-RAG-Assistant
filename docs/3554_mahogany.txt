Title: Mahogany (1975)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Berry Gordy
Main Cast: Diana Ross, Billy Dee Williams, Anthony Perkins, Marisa Mell, Jean-Pierre Aumont
Keywords: slum, chicago, rome, ghetto, mannequin, supermodel

Overview: Tracy, an aspiring designer from the slums of Chicago puts herself through fashion school in the hopes of becoming one of the world's top designers. Her ambition leads her to Rome spurring a choice between the man she loves or her newfound success.
