Title: The Liquidator (1965)
Genres: Comedy, Action, Adventure
Director: Jack Cardiff
Main Cast: Rod Taylor, Trevor Howard, Jill St. John, Wilfrid Hyde-White, Eric Sykes
Keywords: spy

Overview: Spy spoof about Boysie Oakes, a British secret agent who specialises in Liquidating. In actual fact he contracts out the work and pretends it is was himself. This leads to complications.
