Title: Trinity And Beyond: The Atomic Bomb Movie (1995)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Peter Kuran
Main Cast: William Shatner
Keywords: atomic bomb, atomic bomb test, energiestrahlenwaffe, nuclear explosion

Overview: "Trinity and Beyond" is an unsettling yet visually fascinating documentary presenting the history of nuclear weapons development and testing between 1945-1963. Narrated by William Shatner and featuring an original score performed by the Moscow Symphony Orchestra, this award-winning documentary reveals previously unreleased and classified government footage from several countries.
