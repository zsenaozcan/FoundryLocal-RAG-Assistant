Title: The Taste of Money (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Im Sang-soo
Main Cast: Kim Kang-woo, Baek Yun-Shik, Youn Yuh-jung, Maui Taylor, Kim Hyo-jin
Keywords: 돈의 맛, the taste of money

Overview: In this suspenseful erotic drama, the private male secretary and lover of Madam Baek, a Korean conglomerate's middle-aged matriarch, falls for her more genuine daughter. Meanwhile, Madam Baek's husband has an affair with the Filipino nanny.
