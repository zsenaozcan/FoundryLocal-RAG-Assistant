Title: The Legend of Mor'du (2012)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Brian Larsen
Main Cast: Julie Walters, Steve Purcell, Callum O'Neil
Keywords: transformation

Overview: The witch from Pixar's Brave uses magical illustrations to tell the legend of a power-obsessed prince who sought a magic spell that would allow him to wrest control of a kingdom from his brothers, only to destroy that kingdom and his own fate as well.
