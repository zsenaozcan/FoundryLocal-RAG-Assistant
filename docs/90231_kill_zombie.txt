Title: Kill Zombie! (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Erwin van den Eshof
Main Cast: Sergio Hasselbaink, Mimoun Ouled Radi, Carlo Boszhard, Nadia Poeschmann, Gigi Ravelli
Keywords: amsterdam, dutch, jail guard, jail, rescue mission, zombie

Overview: The bizarre story takes place in Amsterdam-West, where a virus turns people into bloodthirsty zombies. Although much blood is flowing and many limbs chopped off, there is a lot to laugh at in this bizarre horror comedy.
