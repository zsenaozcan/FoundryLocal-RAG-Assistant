Title: Moonlight Tariff (2001)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ralf Huettner
Main Cast: Gruschenka Stevens, Tim Bergmann, Jasmin Tabatabai, Bettina Zimmermann, Rüdiger Klink
Keywords: eroticism, one-night stand, longing, man of one's dreams, telephone, best friend, call

Overview: To call or not to call? For young Cora it's not a question. In fact, after the first date you may never call the guy, even if it was the best sex of your life! It's him who must beg for a meeting - and not later than 72 hours after. Otherwise - delete it from memory.
