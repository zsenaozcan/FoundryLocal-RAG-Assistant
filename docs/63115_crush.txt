Title: Crush (1992)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Alison Maclean
Main Cast: Marcia Gay Harden
Keywords: new zealand, woman director

Overview: On the way to interview a novelist, Lane and Christina are involved in a car crash which leaves literary critic Christina brain-damaged. Lane undertakes the assignment and becomes attracted to the novelist's 15 year old daughter, leading to stormy emotions.
