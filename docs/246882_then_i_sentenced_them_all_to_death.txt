Title: Then I Sentenced Them All to Death (1972)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Sergiu Nicolaescu
Main Cast: Amza Pellea, Cristian Sofron, Ion Besoiu, Gheorghe Dinică, Ioana Bulca
Keywords: gun, wehrmacht, murder, tank, reprisal

Overview: A young boy is adopted by a priest and his wife during the Nazi occupation of Romania during the Second World War. He  befriends the village idiot, who's despised by everyone else. When a German is killed in the last days of the war, the village is threatened with total destruction unless the murderer is handed over. The villagers decide to hand over the idiot.
