Title: Last of the Dogmen (1995)
Genres: Adventure, Mystery, Thriller, Western
Director: Tab Murphy
Main Cast: Tom Berenger, Barbara Hershey, Kurtwood Smith, Steve Reevis, Andrew Miller
Keywords: bounty hunter, mountain, forest, anthropologist, native american tribe

Overview: A Montana bounty hunter is sent into the wilderness to track three escaped prisoners. Instead he sees something that puzzles him. Later with a female Native Indian history professor, he returns to find some answers.
