Title: The Secret (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Drew Heriot
Main Cast: Bob Proctor, Joe Vitale, John Assaraf, Loral Langemeier, Marie Diamond
Keywords: scam, pseudoscience

Overview: Interviews with leading authors, philosophers, scientists, with an in-depth discussion of the Law of Attraction. The audience is shown how they can learn and use 'The Secret' in their everyday lives.
