Title: Juke Girl (1942)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery
Director: Curtis Bernhardt
Main Cast: Ann Sheridan, Ronald Reagan, Richard Whorf, George Tobias, Gene Lockhart
Keywords: tomato, hitchhiker, framed for murder

Overview: A hitchhiker (Ronald Reagan) and a jukebox-joint hostess (Ann Sheridan) are framed for murder in Florida tomato country.
