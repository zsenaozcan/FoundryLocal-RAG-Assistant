Title: India (1993)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Paul Harather
Main Cast: Josef Hader, Alfred Dorfer, Maria Hofstätter, Roger Murbach, Ursula Rojek
Keywords: austria, province, male friendship, hygiene, approach, friendship, guesthouse, tumor, cancer, dialect

Overview: Heinzi Boesel and Kurt Fellner are two Austrian health inspectors forced to work together, traveling through Austria. Over time a beautiful friendship evolves between the odd couple who couldn't stand each other initially; a friendship that even overcomes the boundaries of great tragedy.
