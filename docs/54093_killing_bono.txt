Title: Killing Bono (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Nick Hamm
Main Cast: Ben Barnes, Robert Sheehan, Pete Postlethwaite, Krysten Ritter, Ralph Brown
Keywords: rivalry, u2, teenage boy, long hair

Overview: Neil and Ivan McCormick, two Irish brothers who attempt to become rock stars but can only look on as their high school friends U2 become the biggest band in the world.
