Title: The Five Heartbeats (1991)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Robert Townsend
Main Cast: Robert Townsend, Michael Wright, Leon Robinson, Harry Lennix, Tico Wells

Overview: In the early 1960's, a quintet of hopeful, young African-American men form an amateur vocal group called The Five Heartbeats. After an initially rocky start, the group improves, turns pro, and rises to become a top flight music sensation. Along the way, however, the guys learn many hard lessons about the reality of the music industry.
