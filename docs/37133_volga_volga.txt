Title: Volga - Volga (1938)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Grigori Aleksandrov
Main Cast: Lyubov Orlova, Igor Ilyinsky, Vladimir Volodin, Pavel Olenev, Sergei Antimonov
Keywords: musical

Overview: Widely claimed to be Joseph Stalin's favorite movie, this classic musical comedy is a must-see. The action takes place on a steamboat on the iconic Volga River, as two groups of performers travel to Moscow to perform in the Moscow Musical Olympiad.
