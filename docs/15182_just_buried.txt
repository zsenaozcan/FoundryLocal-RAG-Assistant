Title: Just Buried (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Chaz Thorne
Main Cast: Jay Baruchel, Rose Byrne, Graham Greene, Nigel Bennett
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A young man inherits a nearly bankrupt funeral home from his estranged father. He falls in love with the alluring young mortician, only to find out she's offing people to keep the place in business!
