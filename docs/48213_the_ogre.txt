Title: The Ogre (1996)
Genres: Drama
Director: Volker Schlöndorff
Main Cast: John Malkovich, Agnès Soral, Volker Spengler, Gottfried John, Armin Mueller-Stahl
Keywords: hitler youth, nazi germany, fable

Overview: Frenchman Abel Tiffauges likes children, and wants to protect them against the grown-ups. Falsely suspected as child molester, he's recruited as a soldier in the 2nd World War, but very soon he is taken prisoner of war.
