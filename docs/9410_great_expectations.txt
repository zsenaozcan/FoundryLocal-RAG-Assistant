Title: Great Expectations (1998)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Alfonso Cuarón
Main Cast: Gwyneth Paltrow, Ethan Hawke, Chris Cooper, Anne Bancroft, Robert De Niro
Keywords: artist, artists' life, childhood memory

Overview: Loosely based on the Charles Dickens' classic novel, "Great Expectations" is a sensual tale of a young man's unforgettable passage into manhood, and the three individuals who will undeniably change his life forever. Through the surprising interactions of these vivid characters, "Great Expectations" takes a unique and contemporary look at life's great coincidences.
