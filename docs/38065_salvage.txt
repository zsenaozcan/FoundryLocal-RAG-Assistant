Title: Salvage (2006)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jeff Crook
Main Cast: Lauren Currie Lewis, Cody Darbe, Chris Ferry, Maureen Olander, Jessica Delong
Keywords: time loop, brutal death, killer, death

Overview: Claire Parker is going to die. At the hands of a sadistic and depraved killer, she will endure a terrifying, unimaginable brutal death--and it will all happen again. After being beaten, dragged, sliced, and stabbed, Claire awakens at work--where it all began--untouched and unharmed. But the hellish ordeal is far from over. The madman is back and he's ready for more blood...
