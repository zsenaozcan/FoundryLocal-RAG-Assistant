Title: The Clique (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Michael Lembeck
Main Cast: Elizabeth McLaughlin, Ellen Marlow, Sophie Anna Everhard, Samantha Boscarino, Bridgit Mendler
Keywords: group of friends

Overview: A young girl tries to fit in with a clique of popular middle school girls after moving into the guest house of one of their homes.
