Title: The Pool (2008)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Chris Smith
Main Cast: Venkatesh Chavan, Jhangir Badshah, Ayesha Mohan, Nana Patekar

Overview: A boy in abject poverty works in a hotel and becomes obsessed with a swimming pool in the opulent hills of Panjim, Goa, India. His life gets turned upside-down when he attempts to meet the mysterious family who lives at the house.
