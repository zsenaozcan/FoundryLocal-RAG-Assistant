Title: Scorchers (1991)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: David Beaird
Main Cast: Faye Dunaway, Denholm Elliott, James Earl Jones
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Bayou La Teche, Louisiana sizzles as the Cajun town celebrates the wedding of Splendid and Dolan. The trouble comes on the wedding night when Splendid is determined to maintain her innocence. On the other side of town Splendids' cousin has her own problems. Her man has been sleeping with Thais, the town hooker. She heads to the Tiger Cafe with gun in hand to get back her man.
