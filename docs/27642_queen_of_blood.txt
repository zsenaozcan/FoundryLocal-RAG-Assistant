Title: Queen of Blood (1966)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Curtis Harrington
Main Cast: Florence Marly, Basil Rathbone, John Saxon, Judi Meredith, Dennis Hopper
Keywords: moon, mars, vampire, spaceship, space, alien, rescue, blood, space exploration

Overview: A spaceship is sent to Mars after a alien distress signal is picked up. They find one survivor, but when a crew member is found drained of blood it's evident they have rescued a bloodsucking monster.
