Title: Elevator (2011)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Stig Svendsen
Main Cast: Anita Briem, John Getz, Shirley Knight, Michael Mercurio, Amanda Pace
Keywords: greed, tension, racism, stranger, trapped in an elevator

Overview: Nine people are stuck in an elevator. One of them has a ticking bomb that can't be defused. The other eight will do anything to survive. There is no escape and no promise of rescue. As the tension in the elevator mounts the unthinkable soon becomes the only reasonable solution.
