Title: Man on a Tightrope (1953)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Elia Kazan
Main Cast: Fredric March, Terry Moore, Gloria Grahame, Cameron Mitchell, Adolphe Menjou

Overview: Elia Kazan's 1953 film stars Fredric March as the owner of an impoverished circus in Communist-ruled Czechoslovokia who plots to flee across the border to freedom, taking his entire troupe of performers and wild animals with him. The cast also includes Gloria Grahame, Terry Moore, Cameron Mitchell, Richard Boone and Adolphe Menjou.
