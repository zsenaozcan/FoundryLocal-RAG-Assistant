Title: Aimy in a Cage (2016)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Hooroo Jackson
Main Cast: Crispin Glover, Allisyn Ashley Arm, Terry Moore, Paz de la Huerta, Michael William Hunter
Keywords: surrealism, steampunk, female protagonist, brain surgery

Overview: A creative teenage girl is forced to undergo a mind-altering procedure while a virus spreads throughout the globe. Crispin Glover co-stars in this black comedy-fusion-surreal plague thriller. A delicious, phantasmagoric fantasy, directed by Hooroo Jackson.
