Title: Larks on a String (1990)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jiří Menzel
Main Cast: Rudolf Hrušínský, Jitka Zelenohorská, Václav Neckář, Vlastimil Brodský, Jaroslav Satoranský
Keywords: love of one's life

Overview: Set in the late 1940s, the film concerns the treatment of suspect "bourgeois elements", a professor, a saxophonist, and a milkman, who are put to work in a junkyard for rehabilitation.
