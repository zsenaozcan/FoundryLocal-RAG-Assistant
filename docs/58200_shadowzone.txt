Title: Shadowzone (1990)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: J.S. Cardone
Main Cast: Louise Fletcher, David Beecroft, James Hong, Frederick Flynn, Shawn Weatherly

Overview: After someone is killed in the subterranean project called "Shadowzone," a NASA captain is called in to investigate. In the project, sleeping subjects are induced into a deep EDS state whereby they become portals to a parallel universe. Unfortunately this causes adverse reactions in the subject, and something gets through the portal, the consequence of which is an attrition problem.
