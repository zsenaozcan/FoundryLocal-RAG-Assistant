Title: Scarfies (1999)
Genres: Comedy, Thriller
Director: Robert Sarkies
Main Cast: Willa O'Neill, Taika Waititi, Charlie Bleakley, Neill Rea, Ashleigh Seagar

Overview: This dark comedy is set in Dunedin, New Zealand. A university student finds an old "abandoned" house and proceeds to invite other students to share. It even has running electricity. But what is the catch?
