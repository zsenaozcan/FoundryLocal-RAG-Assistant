Title: Fuck You Goethe (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Bora Dağtekin
Main Cast: Elyas M’Barek, Karoline Herfurth, Katja Riemann, Jana Pallaske, Alwara Höfels
Keywords: teacher, school

Overview: A comedy that follows an ex-con who lands a position at a school that sits over the spot where money from one of his earlier robberies was stashed.
