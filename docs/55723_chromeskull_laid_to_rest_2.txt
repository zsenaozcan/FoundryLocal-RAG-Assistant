Title: ChromeSkull: Laid to Rest 2 (2011)
Genres: Horror
Director: Robert Hall
Main Cast: Brian Austin Green, Thomas Dekker, Mimi Michaels, Owain Yeoman, Danielle Harris
Keywords: torture

Overview: "ChromeSkull" is the sequel to the 2009 horror hit "Laid to Rest." It brings back ChromeSkull, who barely escaped death in the first movie and is hell-bent on continuing where he left off... and forging a new path of terror and destruction.
