Title: Tenchi Forever! (1999)
Genres: Animation, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Hiroshi Negishi
Main Cast: Masami Kikuchi, Ai Orikasa, Yumi Takada, Chisa Yokoyama, Kikuko Inoue

Overview: One day, Tenchi disappears in the forest near his house. Six months later, Ayeka and Ryoko locate Tenchi living in a city, but with a mysterious woman. What's more, Tenchi appears to have aged several years. Whenever Ayeka and Ryoko catch up to him, he disappears into thin air, apparently existing in a fabricated alternate dimension where he has no knowledge of his past.
