Title: Are You Scared 2 (2009)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Horror
Director: John Lands
Main Cast: Adam Busch, Tristan Wright, Chad Guerrero, Kathy Gardiner, Andrea Monier

Overview: Dallas, Andrew, his girlfriend Taryn and Reese compose of the DNA Team in an Internet game where the objective is to find treasures in hidden caches. While searching the last treasure to beat the game's record score, they are chased by two psychopaths controlled by the sick owner of an underground site that is filming their movements and they have to fight to survive.
