Title: Double Indemnity (1973)
Genres: Crime, Drama, TV Movie, Thriller
Director: Jack Smight
Main Cast: Richard Crenna, Lee J. Cobb, Samantha Eggar, Robert Webber, Arch Johnson
Keywords: insurance fraud, remake

Overview: A scheming wife lures an insurance investigator into helping murder her husband and then declare it an accident. The investigator's boss, not knowing his man is involved in it, suspects murder and sets out to prove it.
