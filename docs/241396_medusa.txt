Title: Medusa (1998)
Genres: Fantasy, Thriller
Director: George Lazopoulos
Main Cast: Thanos Amorginos, Eleni Filini, Vana Rambota, Arto Apartian
Keywords: based on myths or folklore

Overview: In this wry retelling of the ancient Medusa myth, strange, clothed statues of men are appearing all over Greece. Only Perseus, a leader of a gang of modern Athenian thieves, with a strange childhood, holds the answer to the mystery--and it has something to do with beautiful, long-haired women in black. One night his group breaks into the house of one such creature. Filled with low-key humor and suspense, Medusa unfolds in a simple and tantalizing way
