Title: The Fighting Sullivans (1944)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Lloyd Bacon
Main Cast: Anne Baxter, Thomas Mitchell, Selena Royle, Edward Ryan, Trudy Marshall
Keywords: world war ii, biography

Overview: The lives of a close-knit group of brothers growing up in Iowa during the days of the Great Depression and of World War II and their eventual deaths in action in the Pacific theater are chronicled in this film based on a true story.
