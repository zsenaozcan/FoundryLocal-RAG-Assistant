Title: I Hate Valentine's Day (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Nia Vardalos
Main Cast: Nia Vardalos, John Corbett, Jason Mantzoukas, Judah Friedlander, Zoe Kazan
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A love story set in Manhattan, where a florist who abides by a strict five-date-limit with any man finds herself wanting more with the new restaurateur in town.
