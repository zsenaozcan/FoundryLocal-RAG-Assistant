Title: I Accuse! (1958)
Genres: Drama
Director: José Ferrer
Main Cast: José Ferrer, Viveca Lindfors, Emlyn Williams, Adolf Wohlbrück, Leo Genn
Keywords: anti semitism, wrongful conviction

Overview: The famous "Dreyfus Case" where a Jewish captain in the French Army is falsely accused of treason. He is sentenced to imprisonment on Devil's Island. When the real traitor is found the French Army tries to hide the truth.
