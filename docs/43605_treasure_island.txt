Title: Treasure Island (1934)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Family
Director: Victor Fleming
Main Cast: Wallace Beery, Jackie Cooper, Lionel Barrymore, Otto Kruger, Lewis Stone
Keywords: sailboat, pirate gang, treasure hunt, sunken treasure, treasure map, pirate

Overview: In this early film adaptation of the classic novel by Robert Louis Stevenson, young Jim Hawkins is caught up with the pirate Long John Silver in search of buccaneer Captain Flint's buried treasure.
