Title: Pawn (2013)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: David A. Armstrong
Main Cast: Forest Whitaker, Nikki Reed, Sean Faris, Ray Liotta, Michael Chiklis
Keywords: hostage drama, criminal past, hostage negotiator

Overview: A petty robbery spirals into a tense hostage situation after three gunmen hold up a diner that's a front for the mob.
