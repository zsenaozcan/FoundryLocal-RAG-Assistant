Title: Princess (2006)
Genres: Action, Animation, Drama, Foreign, Romance, Thriller
Director: Anders Morgenthaler
Main Cast: Thure Lindhardt, Stine Fischer Christensen, Jens Arentzen, Liv Corfixen, Ida Dwinger
Keywords: child abuse, adult animation, social issues

Overview: The story of August who loses his beloved sister Christina, a former porn star known as The Princess. He adopts Christina's five-year-old daughter Mia. Weighed down by grief and guilt, August breaks down and with Mia in tow, he embarks on a mission of vengeance to erase Christina's pornographic legacy.
