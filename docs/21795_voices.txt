Title: Voices (2007)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Oh Ki-hwan
Main Cast: Yoon Jin-Seo, Park Ki-Woong, Yu-seon Ham, Kim So-eun, Lee Ki-Woo
Keywords: fencing, high school, wedding party
