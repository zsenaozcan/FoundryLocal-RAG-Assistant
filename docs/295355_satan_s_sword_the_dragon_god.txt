Title: Satan's Sword: The Dragon God (1960)
Genres: Drama, Action, History
Director: Kenji Misumi
Main Cast: Raizô Ichikawa, Kojiro Hongo, Tamao Nakamura, Fujiko Yamamoto, Yutaka Nakamura

Overview: The sequel to Daibosatsu tôge  (1960) and the second of the trilogy follows the adventures of Ryunosuke Tsukue after he is blinded.
