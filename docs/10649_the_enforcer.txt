Title: The Enforcer (1976)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: James Fargo
Main Cast: Clint Eastwood, Tyne Daly, Harry Guardino, Bradford Dillman, John Mitchum
Keywords: san francisco, alcatraz, damage, inspector, magnum, dirty harry
