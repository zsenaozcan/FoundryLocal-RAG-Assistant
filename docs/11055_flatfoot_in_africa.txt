Title: Flatfoot in Africa (1978)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Steno
Main Cast: Bud Spencer, Enzo Cannavale, Werner Pochath, Joe Stewardson, Dagmar Lassander
Keywords: safari, south africa, kommissar geiger, drug

Dying he shows Rizzo a picture of his little son Bodo. Riyyo travels to Johannesburg to find out what the policeman was working on and to find Bodo.
