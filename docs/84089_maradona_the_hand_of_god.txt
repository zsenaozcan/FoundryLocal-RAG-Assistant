Title: Maradona, the Hand of God (2007)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Marco Risi
Main Cast: Marco Leonardi, Juan Leyrado, Rolly Serrano, Julieta Díaz
Keywords: sport

Overview: Dramatizing of the many shocking highs and lows of Argentine soccer hero Diego Maradona, an extraordinary athlete and arguably the greatest player in the history of the sport.
