Title: In Vogue: The Editor's Eye (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Fenton Bailey
Main Cast: Anna Wintour, Grace Coddington, Hamish Bowles, Polly Allen Mellen, Tonne Goodman
Keywords: fashion, fashion magazine

Overview: Documentary film takes a look at some of the world's most influential fashion images as conceived by the magazine's iconic fashion editors.
