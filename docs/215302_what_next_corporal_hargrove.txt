Title: What Next, Corporal Hargrove? (1945)
Genres: Comedy, War
Director: Richard Thorpe
Main Cast: Keenan Wynn, Robert Walker, Jean Porter, Chill Wills, Hugo Haas
Keywords: sequel, corporal, chaplain
