Title: Nadia and Sarra (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Moufida Tlatli
Main Cast: Hiam Abbass, Nejia Ouerghi, Mohamed Ali Ben Jemaa, Hichem Rostom, Nadia Saiji
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Nadia is a 45 year old female professor at the University of Tunis. She is married and has a daughter of 18, Sarra. For some time, Nadia has problems in her marriage also symptoms of menopause are inseparable. The physical and psychological state deteriorates inexorably Nadia.
