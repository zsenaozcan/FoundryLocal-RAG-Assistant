Title: Movie Days (1994)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Friðrik Þór Friðriksson
Main Cast: Örvar Jens Arnarson, Rúrik Haraldsson, Sigrún Hjálmtýsdóttir, Orri Helgason, Jón Gnarr

Overview: This Icelandic tale, loosely based on the real-life experiences of director Fridrik Fridriksson tells the saga of a boyhood spent in Iceland in the 1960s.
