Title: Leolo (1992)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jean-Claude Lauzon
Main Cast: Gilbert Sicotte, Maxime Collin, Ginette Reno, Julien Guiomar, Giuditta Del Vecchio
Keywords: sicily, peasant, daydream, insanity, montreal, falling in love, madness, family conflict, psychiatric

Overview: The story of an imaginative boy who pretends he is the child of a sperm-laden Sicilian tomato upon which his mother accidentally fell.
