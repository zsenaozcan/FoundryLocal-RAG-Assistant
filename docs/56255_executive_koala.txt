Title: Executive Koala (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama, Foreign, Thriller
Director: Minoru Kawasaki
Main Cast: Eiichi Kikichi, Lee Ho, Arthur Kuroda, Hironobu Nomura, Hideki Saijô

Overview: Minoru Kawasaki directs this comedic psychological thriller that follows a large koala as he looks for help from several of his closest friends, which include a giant rabbit and frog. A hardworking executive at a pickle company, Mr. Tamura stands out from other employees because he's a koala bear who stands six feet tall. When his human girlfriend is found murdered, the blackout-prone Tamura goes on the run and tries to solve the mystery.
