Title: Man Hunt (1941)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, War
Director: Fritz Lang
Main Cast: Walter Pidgeon, Joan Bennett, George Sanders, John Carradine, Roddy McDowall
Keywords: sniper, on the run

Overview: British hunter Thorndike vacationing in Bavaria has Hitler in his gun sight. He is captured, beaten, left for dead, and escapes back to London where he is hounded by German agents and aided by a young woman.
