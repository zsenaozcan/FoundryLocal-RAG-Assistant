Title: A Good Man (2014)
Genres: Action
Director: Keoni Waxman
Main Cast: Steven Seagal, Victor Webster, Tzi Ma, Iulia Verdes, Massimo Dobrovic

Overview: After an illustrious special ops career ends in disaster, Alexander goes off the grid and attempts to lead a quiet life as a handyman at an apartment complex. But when one of his tenants and her family fall under the thumb of a Russian gangster, Alexander is dragged into an all-out war between rival Chinese and Russian gangs; forcing him to not only defend the family, but bringing him face to face with an old foe, and giving him one more chance to reconcile his past.
