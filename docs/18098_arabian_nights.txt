Title: Arabian Nights (2000)
Genres: Drama, Family, Fantasy
Director: Steve Barron
Main Cast: Mili Avital, Dougray Scott, Jason Scott Lee, Vanessa Mae, Alan Bates

Overview: Scheherezade puts herself in danger to save Sultan Schariar, her childhood friend, from the madness that has gripped him since the death of his cheating wife at his own hands.
