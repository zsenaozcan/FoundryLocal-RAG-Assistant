Title: Witchcraft (1989)
Genres: Horror
Director: Rob Spera
Main Cast: Anat Topol, Gary Sloan, Mary Shelley, Deborah Scott, Alexander Kirkwood
Keywords: witchcraft

Overview: A new mother and her child move into her mother-in-law's dark old mansion. She soon begns to suspect that neither the house nor her mother-in-law are quite what they seem to be.
