Title: Dream Boy (2008)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: James Bolton
Main Cast: Stephan Bender, Maximillian Roeg, Thomas Jay Ryan, Diana Scarwid, Owen Beckman

Overview: The story of Nathan, a young teenager who tries to flourish in a romantic relationship with neighbour Roy. The two young men will have to face the brutal reality of the rural south of the United States in the late 1970s.
