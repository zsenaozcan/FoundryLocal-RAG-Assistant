Title: Multiple Maniacs (1970)
Genres: Crime, Comedy, Horror
Director: John Waters
Main Cast: Divine, David Lochary, Mary Vivian Pearce, Mink Stole, Cookie Mueller
Keywords: murder, church, lobster

Overview: A traveling sideshow called the Cavalcade of Perversion draws in and terrorizes unsuspecting spectators. The leader of the troupe of misfits is Lady Divine, who goes out for blood after discovering her lover's affair.
