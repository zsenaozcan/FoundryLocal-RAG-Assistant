Title: El asombroso mundo de Borjamari y Pocholo (2004)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Enrique López Lavigne
Main Cast: Santiago Segura, Javier Gutiérrez, Pilar Castro, Guillermo Toledo, Carmen de la Maza
Keywords: joke, friendship, disco, preppie

Overview: In the 80's, Pocholo and Borjamari are kings in the disco "Aguacates". Twenty years later, nothing has changed. Only now, the two brothers are nothing in "Aguacates" disco. Now they are object of derision and mockery. His cousin Pelayo, who used to laugh and abuse in adolescence, reappears in their lives and come to the old cruelties and Pocholo Borjamari announcing that Mecano will meet in a surprise concert...
