Title: Freaks of Nature (2015)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy, Horror
Director: Robbie Pickering
Main Cast: Nicholas Braun, Mackenzie Davis, Ed Westwick, Vanessa Hudgens, Keegan-Michael Key
Keywords: small town, vampire, alien, zombie, teenager

Overview: In the town of Dillford, humans, vampires and zombies were all living in peace - until the alien apocalypse arrived. Now three teenagers-one human, one vampire, and one zombie-have to team up to figure out how to get rid of the visitors.
