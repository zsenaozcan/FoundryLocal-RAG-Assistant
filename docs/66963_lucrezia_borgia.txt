Title: Lucrezia Borgia (1935)
Genres: Drama, History, Romance
Director: Abel Gance
Main Cast: Edwige Feuillère, Gabriel Gabrio, Maurice Escande, Roger Karl
Keywords: borgia

Overview: French silent film pioneer Abel Gance directs this 1935 classic about Lucrezia Borgia, her brother, Cesare. and her father, Pope Alexander VI -- one of history's most ruthless and ambitious crime families.
