Title: Ryuzo and the Seven Henchmen (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Action
Director: Takeshi Kitano
Main Cast: Tatsuya Fuji, Masaomi Kondo, Akira Nakao, Tôru Shinagawa, Ben Hiura

Overview: Ryuzo (Tatsuya Fuji) and his 7 former henchmen are all retired yakuza, but they now live as regular old men. One day, Ryuzo becomes the victim of a phishing fraud. He calls his 7 men together to reform their society.
