Title: Gracie's Choice (2004)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Peter Werner
Main Cast: Kristen Bell, Diane Ladd, Shedrack Anderson III, Anne Heche, Kristin Fairlie
Keywords: teenage girl

Overview: For as long as she can remember, 16 year-old Gracie has been raising her four siblings, each of whom has a different, absent father and their mother is on the fast track to self-destruction. When these children's lives are about to be pulled apart, Gracie will have to do the impossible and make the ultimate sacrifices to keep her family together.
