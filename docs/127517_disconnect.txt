Title: Disconnect (2012)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Henry Alex Rubin
Main Cast: Jason Bateman, Hope Davis, Frank Grillo, Paula Patton, Michael Nyqvist
Keywords: technology, relationship problems, internet, cell phone, gesellschaft

Overview: Disconnect interweaves multiple storylines about people searching for human connection in today’s wired world. Through poignant turns that are both harrowing and touching, the stories intersect with surprising twists that expose a shocking reality into our daily use of technology that mediates and defines our relationships and ultimately our lives.
