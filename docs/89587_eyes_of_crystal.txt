Title: Eyes of Crystal (2004)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Eros Puglielli
Main Cast: Luigi Lo Cascio, Desislava Tenekedjieva, Simón Andreu, José Ángel Egido, Lucía Jiménez
Keywords: giallo

Overview: While hunting a cultured, intelligent and vicious psychopath, Inspector Amaldi comes face to face with the physical and moral decay of his city as well as the ghosts of his own past.
