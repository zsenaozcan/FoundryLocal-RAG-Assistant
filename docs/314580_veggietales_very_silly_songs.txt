Title: VeggieTales: Very Silly Songs (1997)
Genres: Family, Animation
Director: Phil Vischer
Main Cast: Daniel Anderson, Mike Nawrocki, Jim Poole, Mike Sage, Lisa Vischer
Keywords: kids, religious humour, kids music

Overview: In every VeggieTales video, Larry the Cucumber sings one of his special "Silly Songs with Larry". This video contains many of the most popular Silly Songs, hosted by The Pirates Who Don't Do Anything (who debut a Silly Song of their own).
