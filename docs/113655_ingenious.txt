Title: Ingenious (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Jeff Balsmeyer
Main Cast: Dallas Roberts, Jeremy Renner, Ayelet Zurer, Marguerite Moreau, Amanda Anka

Overview: A rags-to-riches story of two friends, a small-time inventor and a sharky salesman, who hit rock bottom before coming up with a gizmo that becomes a worldwide phenomenon.
