Title: The Hunters (1977)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Theo Angelopoulos
Main Cast: Mairi Hronopoulou, Stratos Pahis, Vangelis Kazan, Giorgos Danis, Christoforos Nezer
Keywords: hunters

Overview: During a hunting party on New Year's Eve 1976, five representatives of the bourgeoisie encounter with their companion the body of a partisan from the Civil War of the late forties. What they are most confused about is the fact that the corpse that lies at their feet is still bleeding…
