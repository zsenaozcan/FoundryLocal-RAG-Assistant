Title: Salvage (2010)
Genres: Horror
Director: Lawrence Gough
Main Cast: Shahid Ahmed, Sufian Ashraf, Ben Batt, Linzey Cocker, Trevor Hancock
Keywords: mutant, paranoia, mother daughter relationship, violence, witness elimination

Overview: When a container washes ashore the residents of a sleepy cul-de-sac are plunged into violence, terror and paranoia. Ring fenced by the military a single mother must overcome all the odds to save her daughter.
