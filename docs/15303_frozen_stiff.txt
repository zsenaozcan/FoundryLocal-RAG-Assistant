Title: Frozen Stiff (2002)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Milorad Milinković
Main Cast: Nikola Đuričko, Sonja Kolačarić, Tara Manić, Srđan Todorović, Nenad Jezdić
Keywords: truck, dead body, refrigerator

Overview: The basic plot revolves around drug dealer Limeni (Tin Man) who is said to have no heart, and two men, Lemi and Kiza, who are trying to transport their dead grandfather for burial, until their car breaks down and they end up struggling to get him home onboard a train. This is when these two plots intersect and all hell breaks loose.
