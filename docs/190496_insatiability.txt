Title: Insatiability (2003)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Wiktor Grodecki
Main Cast: Cezary Pazura, Marcin Dorociński, Katarzyna Gniewkowska, Anna Wendzikowska, Leon Niemczyk

Overview: The film Nienasycenie / INSATIABILITY, directed by Wiktor Grodecki based on the novel of the same title by Stanislaw Ignacy Witkiewicz, was selected for the Berlin International Film Festival. The film will be screened as part of the festival's Panorama section.
