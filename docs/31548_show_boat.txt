Title: Show Boat (1936)
Genres: Music, Comedy, Drama
Director: James Whale
Main Cast: Irene Dunne, Allan Jones, Charles Winninger, Paul Robeson, Helen Morgan
Keywords: musical, mississippi river, new year's eve party, showboat

Overview: Despite her mother's objections, the naive young daughter of a show boat captain is thrust into the limelight as the company's new leading lady.
