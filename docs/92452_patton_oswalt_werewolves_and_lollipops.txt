Title: Patton Oswalt: Werewolves and Lollipops (2007)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jason Woliner
Main Cast: Patton Oswalt
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: This DVD is a recording of the show Patton Oswalt performed at the 40 Watt Club in Athens, Georgia . It's a version of his Werewolves and Lollipops show, second solo album, and he ranges from talking about Kentucky Fried Chicken's famous bowls to his work as television writer.
