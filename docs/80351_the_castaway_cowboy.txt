Title: The Castaway Cowboy (1974)
Genres: Adventure, Family, Comedy, Western
Director: Vincent McEveety
Main Cast: James Garner, Vera Miles, Eric Shea, Robert Culp
Keywords: children, classic

Overview: Wayward Texas cowboy (James Garner) washes up on the beaches of Hawaii and is taken home by an fatherless boy. He saves the family's business while romancing the single mom (Vera Miles).
