Title: The Touch of Her Flesh (1967)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Michael Findlay
Main Cast: Suzanne Marre, Angelique, Michael Findlay, Vivian Del Rio, Peggy Steffans
Keywords: sexploitation, erotic movie

Overview: A distraught weapons dealer is involved in an automobile accident after catching his stripper wife in bed with another man. Upon recovering, he goes on a killing spree, bumping off exotic dancers and hookers while plotting revenge on his wife.
