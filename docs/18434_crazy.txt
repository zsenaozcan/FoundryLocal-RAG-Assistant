Title: Crazy (2008)
Genres: Music, Drama, Romance
Director: Rick Bieber
Main Cast: Waylon Payne, Ali Larter, Lane Garrison, Scott Michael Campbell, David Conrad
Keywords: assassination, guitar, jazz, vororte, doctor

Overview: Crazy is the story of a legendary guitar player who emerged from Nashville in the 1950s. Blessed with incomparable, natural talent, Hank Garland quickly established his reputation as the finest sessions player in Nashville.
