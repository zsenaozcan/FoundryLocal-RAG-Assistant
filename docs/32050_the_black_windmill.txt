Title: The Black Windmill (1974)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Crime
Director: Don Siegel
Main Cast: Michael Caine, Donald Pleasence, Delphine Seyrig, Clive Revill, John Vernon
Keywords: paris, london england, terror, spy, secret, ransom, underground, subway, nudity, chase

Overview: A British agent's son is kidnapped and held for a ransom of diamonds. The agent finds out that he can't even count on the people he thought were on his side to help him, so he decides to track down the kidnappers himself.
