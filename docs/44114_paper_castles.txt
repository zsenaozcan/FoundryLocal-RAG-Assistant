Title: Paper Castles (2009)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Salvador García Ruiz
Main Cast: Biel Durán, Adriana Ugarte, Nilo Mur, Guillermo Eced

Overview: In an attempt to help his friend with his impotence, Jaime sets up two friends together. But after the failure, he comes in to help. What first appeared to be just a sexual experience evolves to a full romantic relationship of three people.
