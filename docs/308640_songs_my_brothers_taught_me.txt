Title: Songs My Brothers Taught Me (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Chloé Zhao
Main Cast: Irene Bedard, Eléonore Hendricks, Taysha Fuller, Travis Lone Hill, Dakota Brown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: This complex portrait of modern-day life on the Pine Ridge Indian Reservation explores the bond between a brother and his younger sister, who find themselves on separate paths to rediscovering the meaning of home.
