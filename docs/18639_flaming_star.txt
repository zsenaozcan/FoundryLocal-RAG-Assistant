Title: Flaming Star (1960)
Genres: Action, Drama, Romance, Western
Director: Don Siegel
Main Cast: Elvis Presley, Steve Forrest, Barbara Eden, Dolores del Río, Rodolfo Acosta
Keywords: native american

Overview: Sam Burton's second wife Neddy is Indian, their son Pacer a half-breed. As struggle starts between the whites and the Kiowas, the Burton family is split between loyalties.
