Title: Thief (2006)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller, TV Movie
Director: John David Coles
Main Cast: Andre Braugher, Clifton Collins Jr, Mae Whitman, Yancey Arias, Linda Hamilton

Overview: Andre Braugher stars as Nick Atwater, the leader of a robbery crew balancing his personal life with the planning of a major heist. The crew is being targeted by a relentless cop and the Chinese mafia every step of the way, while Atwater struggles at home with his teenage stepdaughter (Mae Whitman).
