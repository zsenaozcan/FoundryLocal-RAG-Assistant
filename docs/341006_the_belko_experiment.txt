Title: The Belko Experiment (2016)
Genres: Action, Horror, Thriller
Director: Greg McLean
Main Cast: John Gallagher Jr., Tony Goldwyn, Adria Arjona, John C. McGinley, Melonie Diaz
Keywords: gun, office, experiment, colombia, voice, group, blood splatter, murder, dark comedy, gore

Overview: In a twisted social experiment, a group of 80 Americans are locked in their high-rise corporate office in Bogotá, Colombia and ordered by an unknown voice coming from the company's intercom system to participate in a deadly game of kill or be killed.
