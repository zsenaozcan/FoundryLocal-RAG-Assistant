Title: ¿Quién mató a Bambi? (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Santi Amodeo
Main Cast: Quim Gutiérrez, Julián Villagrán, Clara Lago, Úrsula Corberó, Enrico Vecchi
Keywords: kidnapping, remake, dark comedy, relationship, crime, seville

Overview: It tells the story of two young friends who must find a way for the president of the company in which they work (and father of one of them) safe return home, and that is enclosed strange circumstances naked in the trunk of his car.
