Title: The Red Snowball Tree (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: Vasiliy Shukshin
Main Cast: Vasiliy Shukshin, Lidiya Fedoseyeva-Shukshina, Ivan Ryzhov, Aleksey Vanin, Mariya Vinogradova

Overview: A former thief is released from a prison. He tries to start a new life with his penfriend - a good village woman, but his past doesn't let him go.
