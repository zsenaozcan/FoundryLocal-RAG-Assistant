Title: Archive 253 (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: Abe Rosenberg Fishbein
Main Cast: Anna Cetti, Mario Escalante, Michel Chauvet, Juan Luis Tovar
Keywords: psychiatric hospital, found footage

Overview: In 2013 a psychiatric clinic which served for more than 50 years caring for patients in Mexico City was demolished. A few months before its demolition, four young men came to investigate the clinic, determined to discover the truth about rumors of strange and paranormal events, recording everything with which they encounter. Today, the whereabouts of these four young men are unknown, remaining only fragments of their recordings.
