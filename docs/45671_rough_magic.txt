Title: Rough Magic (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance, Fantasy
Director: Clare Peploe
Main Cast: Bridget Fonda, Russell Crowe, Jim Broadbent, D.W. Moffett, Kenneth Mars
Keywords: mexico, shaman, woman director
