Title: Cat in the Brain (1990)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Lucio Fulci, David L. Thompson, Jeoffrey Kennedy, Malisa Longo, Paola Cozzo
Keywords: cat, orgy, film director, nightmare, chain saw, pig, insanity, delusion, gore, cannibalism

Overview: A horror film director is stalked by a mad psychiatrist/serial killer bent on killing people to model the killings after the director's gory death scenes from his movies.
