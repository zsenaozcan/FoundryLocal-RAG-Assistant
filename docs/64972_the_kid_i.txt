Title: The Kid & I (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Penelope Spheeris
Main Cast: Tom Arnold, Eric Gores, Linda Hamilton, Joe Mantegna, Henry Winkler
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Aaron Roman (Gores) is a teenager with cerebral palsy who dreams of starring in a big-time action movie. When his father (Mantegna) grants Aaron his wish for his 18th birthday, he experiences the reality a bit hard to manage.
