Title: Funny Girl (1968)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: William Wyler
Main Cast: Barbra Streisand, Omar Sharif, Kay Medford, Anne Francis, Walter Pidgeon
Keywords: comedian, musical, marriage, jewish, divorce

Overview: The life of comedienne Fannie Brice, from her early days in the Jewish slums of the Lower East Side, to the height of her career with the Ziegfeld Follies, including her marriage to and eventual divorce from her first husband, Nick Arnstein.
