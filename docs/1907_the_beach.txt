Title: The Beach (2000)
Genres: Drama, Adventure, Romance, Thriller
Director: Danny Boyle
Main Cast: Leonardo DiCaprio, Guillaume Canet, Tilda Swinton, Virginie Ledoyen, Staffan Kihlbom
Keywords: hippie, exotic island, beach, map, group dynamics, shark attack, leader, thailand, community, backpacker

Overview: Twenty-something Richard travels to Thailand and finds himself in possession of a strange map. Rumours state that it leads to a solitary beach paradise, a tropical bliss - excited and intrigued, he sets out to find it.
