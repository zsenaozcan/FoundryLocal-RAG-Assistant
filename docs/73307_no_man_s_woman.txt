Title: No Man's Woman (1955)
Genres: Crime
Director: Franklin Adreon
Main Cast: Marie Windsor, John Archer, Patric Knowles, Nancy Gates, Jil Jarmyn
Keywords: femme fatale, b movie, film noir

Overview: A greedy, scheming woman is found murdered in her studio, and the police find that there is no shortage of suspects who wanted to see her dead--among them a rich husband she wouldn't divorce unless he paid her a huge settlement, a lover she caused to be fired from his job and an assistant whose fiancé she tried to seduce.
