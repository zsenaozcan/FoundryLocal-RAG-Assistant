Title: Head-On (2004)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Fatih Akin
Main Cast: Birol Ünel, Sibel Kekilli, Catrin Striebeck, Güven Kıraç, Meltem Cumbul
Keywords: alcohol, drug abuse, religious education, homicide, fictitious marriage, living together, religion and supernatural, love, family, hamburg germany

Overview: Head On is German director Fatih Akin’s story of the alcoholic Cahit and Turkish Sibel. Akin tells the story of a love that can never be between two people who are too alike to not be together.
