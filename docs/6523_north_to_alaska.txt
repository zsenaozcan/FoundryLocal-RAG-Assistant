Title: North to Alaska (1960)
Genres: Western
Director: Henry Hathaway
Main Cast: John Wayne, Stewart Granger, Ernie Kovacs, Fabian, Capucine
Keywords: gold, gold rush, alaska

Overview: Sam and George strike gold in Alaska. George sends Sam to Seattle to bring George's fiancé back to Alaska. Sam finds she is already married, and returns instead with Angel. Sam, after trying to get George and Angel together, finally romances Angel, who, in the meantime, is busy fighting off the advances of George's younger brother, Billy.
