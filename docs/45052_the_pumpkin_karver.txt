Title: The Pumpkin Karver (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Robert Mann
Main Cast: Amy Weber, Michael Zara, Minka Kelly, Terrence Evans, David Austin

Overview: A feature film based on a story in which a young man is stabbed to death on the evening of October 31, 2003. One year later, at a Halloween party, six people were brutally butchered in a remote location. The victims faces were carved and mutilated beyond recognition. The crime is still unsolved and is currently under investigation. Written by Robert Mann (Writer/Director)
