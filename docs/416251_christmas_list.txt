Title: Christmas List (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, TV Movie
Director: Paul A. Kaufman
Main Cast: Alicia Witt, Gabriel Hogan, Peter Benson
Keywords: christmas

Overview: Isobel Gray plans a storybook Christmas with her boyfriend, including a snow-covered cottage in the Northwest, and a carefully composed bucket list of classic holiday traditions. But when the boyfriend goes AWOL, the list proves challenging and a tempting new romance turns her life upside down. Will Isobel have a White Christmas ending under the mistletoe?
