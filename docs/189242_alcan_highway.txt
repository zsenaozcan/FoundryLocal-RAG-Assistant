Title: Alcan Highway (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Aleksi Salmenperä
Main Cast: Jon Ayres, Rhys Palmer, Heikki Tolonen
Keywords: road movie

Overview: Alcan Highway is a filmed diary of a one dream on its way to fulfillment. The Documentary depicts the story of Hese making his dream come true; with the help of his friends he attempts to fix a truck, build a home on top of it, and drive it from Wasilla, Alaska to the Vancouver island. Hese is trying finally to get a home.
