Title: Alabama's Ghost (1973)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Fredric Hobbs
Main Cast: Christopher Brooks, Steven Kent Browne, E. Kerrigan Prescott, Ken Grantham, Karen Ingenthron
Keywords: magic, bone car

Overview: A nightclub janitor discovers the personal effects of a magician and uses them to become a popular magician himself. Tampering with the secrets of the beyond has its price, however, as he soon learns when he is confronted by voodoo, vampirism, and an eviscerated ghost.
