Title: The Grand Duel (1972)
Genres: Western
Director: Giancarlo Santi
Main Cast: Lee Van Cleef, Marc Mazza, Klaus Grunberg, Horst Frank, Alberto Dentice
Keywords: bounty hunter, saloon, spaghetti western

Overview: A grizzled ex-sheriff helps a man framed for murder to confront the powerful trio of brothers who want him dead.
