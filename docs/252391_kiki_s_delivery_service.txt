Title: Kiki's Delivery Service (2014)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: Takashi Shimizu
Main Cast: Fuka Koshiba, Machiko Ono, Hirofumi Arai, Rie Miyazawa, Michitaka Tsutsui
Keywords: witch, fantasy, broom, talking cat

Overview: A young girl named Kiki (Fuka Koshiba) must leave her home for a year to begin training in witchcraft. She leaves on her broom, but first says goodbye to her friends and family. Kiki then begins her new life with her trusted cat Jiji.
