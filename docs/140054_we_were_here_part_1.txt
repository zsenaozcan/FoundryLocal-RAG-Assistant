Title: We Were Here: Part 1 (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Takahiro Miki
Main Cast: Toma Ikuta, Yuriko Yoshitaka, Sousuke Takaoka, Yuika Motokariya, Manami Higa

Overview: "We Were There" follows the 10 year romance between Motoharu Yano (Toma Ikuta) &amp; Nanami Takahashi (Yuriko Yoshitaka). During their high school days, Motoharu Yano was the most popular boy in school, but can't get over the death of his girlfriend from a car crash. Nanami Takahashi falls in love with Motoharu Yano and must deal with his inability to let go of the past.
