Title: Peeping Toms (1973)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Uri Zohar
Main Cast: Arik Einstein, Uri Zohar, Sima Eliyahu, Tzvi Shissel

Overview: Gote and Eli are two aging friends who don't want to age. Gote is a lifeguard who's fighting peepers on the Tel-Aviv beach. Eli is a guitar player who dreams of building a night club in altman's restaurant.
