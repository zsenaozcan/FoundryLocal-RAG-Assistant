Title: The Bird with the Crystal Plumage (1970)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Dario Argento
Main Cast: Tony Musante, Suzy Kendall, Enrico Maria Salerno, Eva Renzi, Umberto Raho
Keywords: based on novel, psychopath, serial killer, giallo, throat slitting, voyeurism, homicidal maniac, black gloved killer

Overview: In Italy, the American writer Sam Dalmas (Tony Musant) witnesses an attempt of murder of the owner of an art gallery, a couple of days before his foreseen return to the United States of America. He decides to stay with his girlfriend Julia (Suzy Kendall) and help the police in the investigation, while the serial killer stalks Julia and him.
