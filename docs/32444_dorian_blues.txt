Title: Dorian Blues (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Tennyson Bardwell
Main Cast: Michael McMillian, Lea Coco, Steve Fletcher, Mo Quigley
Keywords: gay, brother brother relationship, coming out, independent film, coming of age, lgbt child

Overview: Dorian realizes he is gay in his final year of high school. He meets another gay youth locally, but remains confused. He starts therapy, then resorts to confession in the Church, and finally comes out to his brother. Dorian then decides to come out to his father; he gets kicked out of the house.
