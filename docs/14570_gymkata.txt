Title: Gymkata (1985)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Robert Clouse
Main Cast: Kurt Thomas, Tetchie Agbayani, Richard Norton, Edward Bell, John Barrett
Keywords: kung fu, karate, asian, gymnast

Overview: Johnathan Cabot is a champion gymnast. In the tiny, yet savage, country of Parmistan, there is a perfect spot for a "star wars" site. For the US to get this site, they must compete in the brutal "Game". The government calls on Cabot, the son of a former operative, to win the game. Cabot must combine his gymnastics skills of the west with fighting secrets of the east and form GYMKATA!
