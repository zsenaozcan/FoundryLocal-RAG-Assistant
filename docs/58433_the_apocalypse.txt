Title: The Apocalypse (2002)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: Raffaele Mertes
Main Cast: Richard Harris, Benjamin Sadler, Christian Kohlund, Vittoria Belvedere, Erol Sander
Keywords: christianity, opression, slavery, jesus christ, apostle, bible, ancient rome, christian, historical figure, god

Overview: It is 90 AD, and the Roman Empire is being run by the Emperor Domitian, who has declared himself to be God and ruler over heaven and earth. The Christians, who do not recognize his divinity, are a thorn in his side and he is having them cruelly persecuted. The small village in Asia Minor to which the aged apostle John has withdrawn is also attacked by Roman soldiers.
