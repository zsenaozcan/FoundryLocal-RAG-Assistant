Title: Foreign Exchange (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Danny Roth
Main Cast: Ryan Pinkston, Tania Raymonde, Jennifer Coolidge, Curtis Armstrong, Clint Howard
Keywords: high school, masturbation

Overview: Four high school friends plan to ride out their senior year taking easy classes, including a program for housing foreign exchange students and eventually all attending Ohio State. Hurdles befall each of the four: grades, higher aspirations, love. Is it the Foreign Exchange students that learn from their host or the hosts that do more of the learning?
