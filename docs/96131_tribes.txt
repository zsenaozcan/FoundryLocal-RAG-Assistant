Title: Tribes (1970)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Joseph Sargent
Main Cast: Darren McGavin, Earl Holliman, Jan-Michael Vincent, John Gruber, Danny Goldman
Keywords: hippie, recruit, drill instructor, individuality

Overview: A Marine Corps drill instructor who is disgusted by the fact that the Corps now accepts draftees finds himself pitted against a hippie who has been drafted but refuses to accept the military's way of doing things.
