Title: Broken Sky (2006)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Julián Hernández
Main Cast: Miguel Ángel Hoppe, Fernando Arroyo
Keywords: forbidden love, friends, betrayal, new mexican cinema

Overview: First-love movie involving love triangle. Gerardo is deeply in love with longtime lover Jonas. When Jonas falls for a stranger he met at a local nightclub, heartbroken Gerardo soon seeks solace in the arms of Sergio (Alejandro Rojo). Despite other interests, Gerardo and Jonas can't bring themselves to end it.
