Title: Shadows Over Chinatown (1946)
Genres: Mystery, Comedy, Crime, Thriller
Director: Terry O. Morse
Main Cast: Sidney Toler, Mantan Moreland, Victor Sen Yung, Tanis Chandler, John Gallaudet
Keywords: charlie chan

Overview: In San Francisco's Chinatown, Charlie helps two different people search for their missing relatives and uncovers a murder for insurance scheme.
