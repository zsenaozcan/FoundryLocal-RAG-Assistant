Title: Eden and After (1970)
Genres: Mystery, Drama
Director: Alain Robbe-Grillet
Main Cast: Catherine Jourdan, Lorraine Rainer, Sylvain Corthay, Richard Leduc, Pierre Zimmer
Keywords: allegory, nihilism, eroticism, painting, surrealism, torture, violence, avant-garde, fantasy sequence, desert

Overview: A group of French students are drawn into the psychological and sexual games of a mysterious Dutchman. Once they sample his "fear powder" the students experience a series of hallucinations.
