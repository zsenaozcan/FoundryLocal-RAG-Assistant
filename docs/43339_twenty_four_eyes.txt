Title: Twenty-Four Eyes (1954)
Genres: Drama
Director: Keisuke Kinoshita
Main Cast: Hideki Gôko, Itsuo Watanabe, Hideko Takamine, Makoto Miyagawa, Takeo Terashita
Keywords: patriotism, bus ride, sino japanese war, school, poverty, blind, anti war, schoolteacher, snare, class reunion

Overview: Schoolteacher Hisako Oishi struggles to imbue her students with a positive view of the world and their place in it, despite the fact that she knows full well that most of them will die in the war.
