Title: Servants of Twilight (1991)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Jeffrey Obrow
Main Cast: Bruce Greenwood, Jarrett Lennon, Belinda Bauer, Grace Zabriskie, Richard Bradford
Keywords: single parent, based on novel, anti-christ, independent film

Overview: Based on the novel by Dean R. Koontz, this action packed thriller features Bruce Greenwood as a private detective hired to protect a little boy from a fanatical religious cult that believe he is the antichrist fortold in the book of Revelations.
