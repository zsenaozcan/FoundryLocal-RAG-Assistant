Title: More Than a Secretary (1936)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Alfred E. Green
Main Cast: Jean Arthur, George Brent, Lionel Stander, Ruth Donnelly, Reginald Denny

Overview: When the co-owner of a secretarial school visits a magazine editor to find out why he runs through secretaries, she's mistaken for an applicant. Drawn to him, she accepts the position.
