Title: Mr. Jones (1993)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Mike Figgis
Main Cast: Richard Gere, Lena Olin, Anne Bancroft, Tom Irwin, Delroy Lindo
Keywords: depression, therapist, symphony, piano, psychiatrist patient relationship, bipolar disorder
