Title: Devil Girl from Mars (1954)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: David MacDonald
Main Cast: Hugh McDermott, Patricia Laffan, Hazel Court, Peter Reynolds, Adrienne Corri
Keywords: killer robot, flying saucer, invasion, ufo, beautiful woman, robot as menace

Overview: An uptight, leather-clad female alien, armed with a raygun and accompanied by a menacing robot, comes to Earth to collect Earth's men as breeding stock
