Title: The Moon Is Blue (1953)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Otto Preminger
Main Cast: David Niven, William Holden, Maggie McNamara, Tom Tully, Dawn Addams
Keywords: virgin

Overview: Two aging playboys are both after the same attractive young woman, but she fends them off by claiming that she plans to remain a virgin until her wedding night. Both men determine to find a way around her objections.
