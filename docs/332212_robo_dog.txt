Title: Robo-Dog (2015)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Jason Murphy
Main Cast: Olivia d'Abo, Wallace Shawn, James Arnold Taylor, Patrick Muldoon, Steve Heinz

Overview: ROBO-DOG is a real-world fantasy film for the whole family. When Tyler's furry best friend dies tragically, his inventor Dad creates a new dog to take his place - complete with mechanical powers and robotic abilities to keep everyone on their toes.
