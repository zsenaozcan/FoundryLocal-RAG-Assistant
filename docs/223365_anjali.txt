Title: Anjali (1990)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Mani Ratnam
Main Cast: Raghuvaran, Revathi, Shamili, Prabhu Ganesan, Tarun
Keywords: family drama, tamil

Overview: Anjali (Shamili) is a story of a mentally disabled child and the emotional trauma experienced by her family of four – a young civil engineer Shekar (Raghuvaran), his wife, Chitra (Revathi) and their two young children Arjun (Tarun) and Anu (Shruti).
