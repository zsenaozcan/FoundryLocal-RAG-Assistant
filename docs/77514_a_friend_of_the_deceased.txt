Title: A Friend of the Deceased (1997)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Leonid Boyko
Main Cast: Aleksandr Lazarev Jr., Anzhelika Nevolina, Elena Korikova, Tetyana Kryvytska Stang Lund, Evgeniy Pashin
Keywords: prostitute, hitman, translator

Overview: Tolla is an unemployed translator whose wife is leaving him. Despondent and weak, he submits to the suggestion of an acquantance to have a contract placed on the man that his wife is seeing. Instead, however, he arranges for the hit to be placed on himself. Before the contract is executed, he develops a relationship with a prostitute, and then changes his mind. In order to survive he takes the obvious course of action, which turns out to have possibly been unnecessary, and then he must deal with the guilt.
