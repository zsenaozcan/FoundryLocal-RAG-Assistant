Title: The Last Brickmaker in America (2001)
Genres: Drama
Director: Gregg Champion
Main Cast: Sidney Poitier, Wendy Crewson, Jay O. Sanders, Mary Alice, Bernie Casey
Keywords: friendship, brick making

Overview: A man must cope with the loss of his wife and the obsolescence of his job before finding redemption by becoming a role model to an equally lost 13-year-old.
