Title: A Nightmare in Las Cruces (2011)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Hafid Abdelmoula, Shannon Caruso, Charles Comer, Dora Cothern , Howard Cothern

Overview: In winter 1990, a friendly bowling match turned into a deadly nightmare when a pair of robbers walked into the Las Cruces, New Mexico, lanes, emptied the safe and then shot the bowlers. This documentary takes a close look at the still-unsolved case.
