Title: Here Without Me (2011)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Bahram Tavakoli
Main Cast: Saber Abbar, Negar Javaherian, Fatemah Motamed-Aria, Parsa Pirouzfar
Keywords: iranian

Overview: The story is about the world of a small family with familiar dreams and not so remarkable problems. The mother is trying to lead everything to save her family, but small events disarrange all her plans.
