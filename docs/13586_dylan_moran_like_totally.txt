Title: Dylan Moran: Like, Totally... (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michael Matheson
Main Cast: Dylan Moran
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Dylan Moran, creator of Channel 4's BAFTA award-winning "Black Books" returns with an all-new stand-up show. Unpredictable, startling, bizarre, elegiac, but above all brilliant and hilariously funny, Moran is a master of comedy.
