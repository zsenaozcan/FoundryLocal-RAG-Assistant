Title: Hard Sun (2014)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Canyon Prince
Main Cast: Robyn Buck, John Bain, Ben Begley, Tamara Carey
Keywords: brother sister relationship, death of parent, special needs, child care, legal guardian

Overview: After the death of her parents, a young woman assumes primary guardianship of her special needs brother. But as she attempts to balance her new life with her brother and her own blossoming romance, it becomes painfully clear that life will only make room for one.
