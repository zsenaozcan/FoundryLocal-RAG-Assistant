Title: The Day the Sky Exploded (1958)
Genres: Science Fiction, Action
Director: Paolo Heusch
Main Cast: Paul Hubschmid, Fiorella Mari, Madeleine Fischer, Ivo Garrani, Dario Michaelis

Overview: Scientists discover that a group of meteors are hurtling on a collison course with Earth, and if they hit, the planet will be destroyed.
