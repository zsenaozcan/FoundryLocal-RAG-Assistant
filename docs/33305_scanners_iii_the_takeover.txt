Title: Scanners III: The Takeover (1992)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Action
Director: Christian Duguay
Main Cast: Liliana Komorowska, Valérie Valois, Steve Parrish, Colin Fox, Daniel Pilon
Keywords: computer, drug

Overview: A young female scanner turns from a sweet young thing into a murderous, power-crazed villain after she takes an experimental drug developed by her father. Her brother, who is also a scanner, is the only one powerful enough to stop her.
