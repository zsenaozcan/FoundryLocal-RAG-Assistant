Title: Hoot (2006)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Wil Shriner
Main Cast: Luke Wilson, Logan Lerman, Brie Larson, Tim Blake Nelson, Cody Linley
Keywords: owl, teenager, animal protection, based on young adult novel

Overview: A young man (Lerman) moves from Montana to Florida with his family, where he's compelled to engage in a fight to protect a population of endangered owls.
