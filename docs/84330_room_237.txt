Title: Room 237 (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Rodney Ascher
Main Cast: Tom Cruise, Jack Nicholson, Stephen King, Nicole Kidman, Liam Neeson
Keywords: conspiracy theory, the shining, kubrick

Overview: A subjective documentary that explores various theories about hidden meanings in Stanley Kubrick's classic film The Shining. Five very different points of view are illuminated through voice over, film clips, animation and dramatic reenactments.
