Title: Attack the Gas Station! (1999)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Kim Sang-jin
Main Cast: Lee Sung-jae, Yu Oh-seong, Kang Seong-jin, Yoo Ji-tae, Park Young-gyu
Keywords: robbery, anarchist, petrol station robbery, hostage-taking, hooligan

Overview: A quartet of disaffected Korean youths have robbed a Seoul gas station. After taking the gas station over, their wacky antics ensue; forcing the manager to sing, kidnapping customers that complain about the service, and staging fist-fights between street gang members and gas station employees; all of these reflect their own gripes against society.
