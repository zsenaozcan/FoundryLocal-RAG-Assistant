Title: Kounterfeit (1996)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Action, Crime
Director: John Asher
Main Cast: Gil Bernardy, Corbin Bernsen, Bobbie Brown, Hilary Swank, Andrew Hawkes
Keywords: undercover, deal, sister, cop, money, counterfeit, shootout, criminal, killer

Overview: Flush with $3 million in counterfeit bills, a pair of small-time criminals hope to make the big time by trading it in for a cool million in authentic cash. However, when the trade goes awry, three men end up murdered- one a cop with a vengeful sister--and the hapless buddies find themselves caught between the police and mob hit men after the money.
