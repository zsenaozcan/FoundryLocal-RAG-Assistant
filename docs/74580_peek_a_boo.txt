Title: Peek-a-boo (1954)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jean Loubignac
Main Cast: Robert Dhéry, Louis de Funès, Colette Brosset, Raymond Bussières, Jacqueline Maillan

Overview: A small-town policeman is informed that "naked women" are dancing in a revue at a local variety theater. Being the guardian of public morals that he is, he decides to stroll on down there and check it out for himself.
