Title: The Greasy Strangler (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Thriller
Director: Jim Hosking
Main Cast: Michael St. Michaels, Sky Elobar, Elizabeth De Razzo, Gil Gex, Abdoulaye N'Gom
Keywords: competition, affection, slasher, maniac, disco

Overview: Ronnie runs a Disco walking tour with his son, Brayden. When a sexy woman takes the tour, it begins a competition between father and son for her love. It also signals the arrival of an oily strangler who stalks the streets at night.
