Title: Korgoth of Barbaria (2006)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Genndy Tartakovsky
Main Cast: Diedrich Bader, Corey Burton, Craig Raisner, John DiMaggio, Tom Kenny
Keywords: failed tv pilot

Overview: In a dark future wasteland, the great cities have risen and fallen, primordial beasts have reclaimed the wilderness and thieves and savages populate sparse, dirty towns. From the frozen north emerges a warrior known as Korgoth, and his merciless savagery may be his only key to survival.
