Title: Rara (2016)
Genres: Family, Drama
Director: Pepa San Martín
Main Cast: Julia Lübbert, Emilia Ossandón, Mariana Loyola, Agustina Muñoz, Coca Guazzini
Keywords: mother role, based on true story, coming of age, family, lgbt, queer, struggle

Overview: Since their parents split up, Sara and her younger sister live with their mother, whose new partner is a woman. Everyday life for the four of them is hardly any different than it is for other families. But not everyone sees it that way – Sara's father in particular has his doubts. As Sara’s 13th birthday approaches, she feels rather overwhelmed: her first crush, a body in the midst of changes and to top it off, conflicts over loyalty with her parents.
