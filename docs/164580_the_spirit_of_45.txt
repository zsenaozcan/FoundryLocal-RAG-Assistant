Title: The Spirit of '45 (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ken Loach
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Ken Loach’s 2013 documentary about social change in Britain in the aftermath of the Second World War, including the nationalisation of industries and the formation of the welfare state. Made almost entirely in black &amp; white, so B&amp;W archive footage from the 1940s blend in with interviews made today.
