Title: My Queen Karo (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Dorothée Van Den Berghe
Main Cast: Anna Franziska Jäger, Matthias Schoenaerts, Déborah François, Nico Sturm, Maria Kraakman
Keywords: free love, commune, baptism, squatter

Overview: Nine-year-old Karo grows up with her parents in an Amsterdam commune in the Seventies. She leads a carefree existence in this utopia-for-adults. Everything is shared in the squat, but not everyone is able to honor these ideals. Karo gets confused because of the internal conflicts that start to divide the group. Karo slowly realizes that nothing can stay the same forever.
