Title: El cimarrón (2006)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Iván Dariel Ortiz
Main Cast: Fernando Allende, Mara Croatto, Modesto Lacen, Dolores Pedro, Pedro Telémaco

Overview: El cimarrón is a love story about a young African couple, Marcos and Carolina, that takes place in the slavery era at the turn of the nineteen-century in a Caribbean island. After several futile escape plans, Marcos finally escaped. Now free, he returns to liberate his beloved Carolina.
