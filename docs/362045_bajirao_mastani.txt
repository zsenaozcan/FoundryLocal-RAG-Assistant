Title: Bajirao Mastani (2015)
Genres: War, History, Romance
Director: Sanjay Leela Bhansali
Main Cast: Ranveer Singh, Deepika Padukone, Priyanka Chopra, Tanvi Azmi, Vaibhav Tatwawadi
Keywords: любовь, битва, воин

Overview: A historical account of the romance between the Maratha general, Baji Rao I and Mastani, a Muslim princess.
