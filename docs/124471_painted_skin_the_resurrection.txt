Title: Painted Skin: The Resurrection (2012)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy
Director: Wuershan
Main Cast: Zhou Xun, Chen Kun, Zhao Wei, Yang Mi, William Feng

Overview: According to demon lore, it takes hundreds of years to attain human form. Even then, lacking a human heart, a demon cannot experience the true pains and passions of existence. However, there is a legend that if a pure human heart is freely offered to a demon, it can become a mortal and experience true life.
 Sequel of Painted Skin (2008).
