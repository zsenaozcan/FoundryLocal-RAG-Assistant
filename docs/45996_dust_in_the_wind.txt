Title: Dust in the Wind (1986)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Hou Hsiao-hsien
Main Cast: Yang Lin, Mei Fang, Mei-Feng, Chien-wen Wang, Shufen Xin
Keywords: mining town

Overview: A-yuan and A-yun are both from the small mining town of Jio-fen. In the city, A-yuan is an apprentice by day and goes to night school, and A-yun works as a helper at a tailors. Everyone thinks they are meant for each other, so do they. They fail to see time and fate are beyond their control.
