Title: Legends of the Hidden Temple (2016)
Genres: Adventure, Family
Director: Joe Menendez
Main Cast: Isabela Moner, Dee Bradley Baker, Daniel Cudmore, Michael Benyaer, James Black
Keywords: temple, mission, jungle
