Title: Fireworks (1947)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kenneth Anger
Main Cast: Kenneth Anger, Gordon Gray, Bill Seltzer
Keywords: homosexuality, erotic movie

Overview: Fireworks revolves around a young man (played by Anger himself) associating with various navy sailors, who eventually turn on him, stripping him naked and beating him to death, ripping open his chest to find a clock ticking inside. Several fireworks then explode, accompanied by a burning Christmas tree and the final shot shows the young man lying in bed next to another topless man.
