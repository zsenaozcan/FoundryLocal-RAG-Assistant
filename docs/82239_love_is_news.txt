Title: Love Is News (1937)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Tay Garnett
Main Cast: Tyrone Power, Loretta Young, Don Ameche, George Sanders, Elisha Cook Jr.
Keywords: reporter, heiress, scoop

Overview: When a crafty reporter uses false pretenses to get a story out of heiress Tony Gateson, she turns the tables on him, telling the press that they are engaged. Suddenly he's front page news, every salesman is at his doorstep, and he loses his job. A series of misadventures ensues with him alternately back on his job and fired and her ex-fiance showing up. Can an heiress be a human being, and can a reporter get a scoop?
