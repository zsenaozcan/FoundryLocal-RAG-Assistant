Title: Badlapur (2015)
Genres: Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Sriram Raghavan
Main Cast: Varun Dhawan, Nawazuddin Siddiqui, Huma Qureshi, Yami Gautam, Divya Dutta
Keywords: revenge

Overview: After his wife and son are killed, a young man finds out the man behind the murder and fights against him, along committing some big mistakes.
