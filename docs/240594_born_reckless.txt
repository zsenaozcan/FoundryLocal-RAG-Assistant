Title: Born Reckless (1937)
Genres: Adventure, Crime
Director: Gustav Machatý
Main Cast: Rochelle Hudson, Brian Donlevy, Barton MacLane, Robert Kent, Harry Carey
Keywords: fight, racketeer

Overview: Racketeer Jim Barnes is trying to force the independent taxicab-drivers to join his "protection service" at the cost of five bucks a day. Champion race-car driver, Bob Kane, joins with his friends Lee and "Dad" Martin in a fight for the street rights of a big city.
