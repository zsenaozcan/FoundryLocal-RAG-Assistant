Title: Hobgoblins (1988)
Genres: Horror, Action, Comedy, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Rick Sloane
Main Cast: Tom Bartlett, Paige Sullivan, Steven Boggs, Kelley Palmer, Billy Frank

Overview: A group of hobgoblins, who allow you to live out your fantasies but kill you in the process, escape from a studio vault, and a security guard and his friends must stop them before dawn.
