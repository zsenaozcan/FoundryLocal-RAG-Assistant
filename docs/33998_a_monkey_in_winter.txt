Title: A Monkey in Winter (1962)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Henri Verneuil
Main Cast: Jean Gabin, Jean-Paul Belmondo, Suzanne Flon, Noël Roquevert, Paul Frankeur
Keywords: french movies

Overview: The story of a man who has stopped drinking- and then dreaming- and who thanks to a young man who tries to forget a lost love in alcohol will turn drunk again during an exciting night.
