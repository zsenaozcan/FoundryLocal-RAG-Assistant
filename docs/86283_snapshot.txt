Title: Snapshot (1979)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Simon Wincer
Main Cast: Chantal Contouri, Robert Bruning, Sigrid Thornton, Hugh Keays-Byrne, Denise Drysdale
Keywords: female nudity, photographer, obsession, stalker, murder, photograph, model, advertising, ex-boyfriend ex-girlfriend relationship, modeling

Overview: Angela is a young hairdresser thrown out of home by her puritantical mother after too many nights out. She quickly falls in with the modeling crowd and does some modeling work herself, while it seems she is being stalked by a mysterious figure driving an ice-cream van.
