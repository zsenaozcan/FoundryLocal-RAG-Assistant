Title: Boy 7 (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Science Fiction
Director: Lourens Blok
Main Cast: Matthijs van de Sande Bakhuyzen, Ella-June Henrard, Tygo Gernandt, Yannick Jozefzoon, Halina Reijn
Keywords: based on novel, memory loss

Overview: When Sam regains consciousness in the middle of a crowded subway, he doesn't know how he got there, where he came from nor his own name. He franticly searches for his identity, using the contents of his backpack. Slowly but surely he realizes his life is in great danger.
