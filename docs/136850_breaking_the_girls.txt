Title: Breaking the Girls (2013)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Jamie Babbit
Main Cast: Shawn Ashmore, Madeline Zima, Agnes Bruckner, Shanna Collins, Kate Levering
Keywords: lesbian, woman director, murder plot

Overview: A naive college student loses a scholarship at the hands of a classmate and makes a pact with a mysterious friend to kill off each other's enemies.
