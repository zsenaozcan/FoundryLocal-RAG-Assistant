Title: 99 Homes (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ramin Bahrani
Main Cast: Andrew Garfield, Laura Dern, Michael Shannon, Tim Guinee, J.D. Evermore
Keywords: florida, motel, finances, single father, mother son relationship, real estate agent, eviction, financial crisis, foreclosure, real estate
