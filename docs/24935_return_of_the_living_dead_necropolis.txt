Title: Return of the Living Dead: Necropolis (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Ellory Elkayem
Main Cast: Aimee-Lynn Chadwick, Cory Hardrict, John Keefe, Jana Kramer
Keywords: undead, zombie

Overview: A group of teenagers who, in an attempt to rescue their friend from an evil corporation, end up releasing a horde of blood thirsty zombies.
