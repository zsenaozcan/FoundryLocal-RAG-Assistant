Title: Dooman River (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lu Zhang
Main Cast: Jian Cui , Lan Yin , Jin-Long Lin 

Overview: Chang-ho, 12, becomes friend with a North-Korean immigrant about the same age who just crossed the Dooman river, border between North-Korea and China. His mute sister and his wise grand-father will get along with him in a series of misfortunes.
