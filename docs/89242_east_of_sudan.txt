Title: East of Sudan (1964)
Genres: Romance, Adventure, Drama
Director: Nathan H. Juran
Main Cast: Anthony Quayle, Sylvia Syms, Derek Fowlds, Jenny Agutter, Johnny Sekka

Overview: A British soldier escapes from 1880s Khartoum and goes down the Nile river with a fellow soldier, a governess and the daughter of an emir.
