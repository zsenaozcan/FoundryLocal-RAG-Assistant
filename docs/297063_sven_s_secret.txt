Title: Sven's Secret (1995)
Genres: Drama
Director: Roland Suso Richter
Main Cast: Katharina Meinecke, Richy Müller, Katharina Schüttler, Nikolaus Paryla, Daniel Brühl

Overview: Sven is a twelve year old pocket-picker and works for Jens, boss of his gang in school. Teacher Vera Loewe becomes one of his victims. When she realizes that he's one of the pupils in her new class, she confronts him and wants to help, even against his will.
