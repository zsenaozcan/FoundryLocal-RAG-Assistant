Title: Ugetsu (1953)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Mystery
Director: Kenji Mizoguchi
Main Cast: Masayuki Mori, Machiko Kyô, Kinuyo Tanaka, Eitarô Ozawa, Ikio Sawamura
Keywords: japan, based on novel, samurai, greed, bigamy, tragedy, rural setting, ghost, sengoku period, warring states period

Overview: In the civil wars of 16th century Japan, two ambitious peasants in Nakanogo want to make their fortunes. The potter Genjuro intends to sell his wares for vast profits in the local city, Omizo, while his brother-in-law Tobei wishes to become a samurai. A respected sage warns them against seeking profit in times of war but his advice goes unheeded, and their insistency leads to the ruin of their families.
