Title: Young Guns (1988)
Genres: Crime, Action, Adventure, Drama, Western
Director: Christopher Cain
Main Cast: Emilio Estevez, Kiefer Sutherland, Lou Diamond Phillips, Charlie Sheen, Dermot Mulroney
Keywords: corruption, sheriff, deputy sheriff, ranch, billy the kid, outlaw, neighbor

Overview: A group of young gunmen, led by Billy the Kid, become deputies to avenge the murder of the rancher who became their benefactor. But when Billy takes their authority too far, they become the hunted.
