Title: OC87: The Obsessive Compulsive, Major Depression, Bipolar, Asperger's Movie (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Bud Clayman
Main Cast: Jeff Bell, Maurice Benard, Wayne Bickerstaff, Carol Caruso, Buddy Clayman
Keywords: obsessive compulsive disorder

Overview: Director Bud Clayman documents his struggle with OCD and Asperger's Syndrome and how it derailed his plan to become a filmmaker.
