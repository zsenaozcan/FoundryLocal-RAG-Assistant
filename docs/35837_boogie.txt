Title: Boogie (2009)
Genres: Action, Animation
Director: Gustavo Cova
Main Cast: Pablo Echarri, Nancy Dupláa, Nicolás Frías, Marcelo Armand
Keywords: animation

Overview: Based on the comic "Boogie, el aceitoso".In a city where crime rules the streets, Boogie shows up. only to make things brutally worse. A hired thug, with a blatant disregard for human life – especially those that are less fortunate, and an unmatched lack of respect for women, Boogie is the ultimate anti-hero. Boogie is an action-packed, jaw-dropping, animated feature, exploding with sadistic humour,violence and non-stop bullets.
