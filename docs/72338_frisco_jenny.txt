Title: Frisco Jenny (1932)
Genres: Drama
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: Ruth Chatterton, Louis Calhern, Helen Jerome Eddy, Donald Cook, Hallam Cooley
Keywords: san francisco, brothel madam

Overview: Frisco Jenny was orphaned by the 1906 earthquake and fire and has become the madame of prosperous bawdy house. She puts her son up for adoption and he rises to prominence as district attorney dedicated to closing down such houses. When her underling Dutton proposes killing the DA, she kills the underling and must face execution.
