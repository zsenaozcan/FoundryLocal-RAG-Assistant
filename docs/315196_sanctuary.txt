Title: Sanctuary (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Marc Brummund
Main Cast: Louis Hofmann, Katharina Lorenz, Uwe Bohm, Alexander Held, Max Riemelt
Keywords: boy, coming of age, incest overtones, mother son relationship, based on true events, school for delinquent boys, mother son french kiss, stepfather, borstal, youth correctional facility

Overview: Summer 1968. 14-year-old Wolfgang is deported from his family in the secluded Church Welfare Institution sanctuary. One thing is clear for Wolfgang: His yearning for freedom, he will not soon buried in the bog.
