Title: Bread and Chocolate (1974)
Genres: Comedy, Foreign
Director: Franco Brusati
Main Cast: Nino Manfredi, Johnny Dorelli, Anna Karina, Paolo Turco, Ugo D'Alessio
Keywords: switzerland, worker, immigrant, hardship, xenophobia, foreigners

Overview: Italian immigrant tries to become a member of Swiss society but fails as a waiter and even as a chicken plucker. He then becomes involved with shady wealthy character and tries to hide his Italian identity. He refuses to give up no matter how awful his situation.
