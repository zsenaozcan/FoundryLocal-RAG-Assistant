Title: Camelot (1967)
Genres: Adventure, Music, Romance
Director: Joshua Logan
Main Cast: Richard Harris, Vanessa Redgrave, Franco Nero, David Hemmings, Lionel Jeffries
Keywords: camelot, musical, knight, king arthur, sir lancelot

Overview: King Arthur establishes the ideals and laws of Camelot but is tragically forced to enforce these laws against his own wife.
