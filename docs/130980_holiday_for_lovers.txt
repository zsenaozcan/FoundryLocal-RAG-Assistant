Title: Holiday For Lovers (1959)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Henry Levin
Main Cast: Clifton Webb, Jane Wyman, Jill St. John, Carol Lynley, Paul Henreid

Overview: Clifton Webb as a strict, conservative father heads the cast of this 1959 comedy, about an American family vacationing in South America. Directed by Henry Levin, the film also features Jane Wyman, Jill St. John, Carol Lynley, Paul Henreid, Gary Crosby, Henny Backus, Wally Brown, Gardner McKay and Jose Greco.
