Title: The Red Ball Express (1952)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Budd Boetticher
Main Cast: Jeff Chandler, Alex Nicol, Charles Drake, Judith Braun, Sidney Poitier
Keywords: france, driver

Overview: August 1944: proceeding with the invasion of France, Patton's Third Army has advanced so far toward Paris that it cannot be supplied. To keep up the momentum, Allied HQ establishes an elite military truck route.
