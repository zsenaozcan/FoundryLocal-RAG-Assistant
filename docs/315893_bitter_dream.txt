Title: Bitter Dream (2004)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Mohsen Amiryousefi
Main Cast: Yadollah Anvari, Abbas Esfandiari, Delbar Ghasri, Mohsen Rahimi, Safar-Ali Safari
Keywords: iranian new wave

Overview: For forty years, Esfandiar has been unconcernedly preparing corpses for the journey to their final resting place. One day, whilst preparing a burial service, he's taken ill. Might he too be mortal?
