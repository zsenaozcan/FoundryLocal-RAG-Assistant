Title: The Sicilian Connection (1972)
Genres: Thriller, Crime
Director: Ferdinando Baldi
Main Cast: Ben Gazzara, Silvia Monti, Fausto Tozzi, Jess Hahn, José Greci
Keywords: smuggling, drug

Overview: A small time hood smuggles drugs from Turkey to Italy in an effort to make it into the big time, but along the way everyone has to have their cut.
