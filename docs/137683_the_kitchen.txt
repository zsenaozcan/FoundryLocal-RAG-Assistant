Title: The Kitchen (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Ishai Setton
Main Cast: Laura Prepon, Bryan Greenberg, Dreama Walker, Matt Bush, Tate Ellington
Keywords: infidelity, birthday, fight, kitchen, beer, birthday party, cigarette smoking, band, abortion, feel bad comedy

Overview: Jennifer's thirtieth birthday party is supposed to be a special day. But what starts out as a day of celebration quickly spirals into a most ill-fated day Jennifer wishes she could forget, in this ensemble comedy set entirely in a kitchen.
