Title: A Little Chaos (2014)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Alan Rickman
Main Cast: Kate Winslet, Alan Rickman, Stanley Tucci, Matthias Schoenaerts, Jennifer Ehle
Keywords: paris, dancing, france, adultery, jealousy, infidelity, corset, suppressed past, garden, society

Overview: A landscape gardener is hired by famous architect Le Nôtre to construct the grand gardens at the palace of Versailles. As the two work on the palace, they find themselves drawn to each other and are thrown into rivalries within the court of King Louis XIV.
