Title: The Invisible Man Returns (1940)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Joe May
Main Cast: Cedric Hardwicke, Vincent Price, Nan Grey, John Sutton, Cecil Kellaway
Keywords: scotland yard, monster, insanity, sequel, murder, scientist, madness, invisible man

Overview: The owner of a coal mining operation, falsely imprisoned for fratricide, takes a drug to make him invisible, despite its side effect: gradual madness.
