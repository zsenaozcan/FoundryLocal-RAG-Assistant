Title: What Happened, Miss Simone? (2015)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Liz Garbus
Main Cast: Nina Simone, Lisa Simone Kelly, Dick Gregory, Stanley Crouch, Elisabeth Henry-Macari
Keywords: voice, child prodigy, singer, racism, civil rights, blues music, woman director, miles davis, spousal abuse, manic depression
