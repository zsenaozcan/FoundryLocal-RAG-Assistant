Title: Zombie Nightmare (1987)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jack Bravman
Main Cast: Adam West, Jon Mikl Thor, Tia Carrere, Manuska Rigaud, Frank Dietz
Keywords: voodoo, revenge, teenager, canuxploitation

Overview: Tony Washington is killed by a gang of rampant trendy teenagers. Molly Mokembe is a voodoo lady who brings him back from the dead to seek revenge on his killers so he can rest in peace.
