Title: Through the Mist (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jean-Philippe Duval
Main Cast: Sébastien Ricard, Joseph Mesiano, Dimitri Storoge, Bénédicte Décary, David Quertigniez
Keywords: musical, drama

Overview: This is the life of Dédé Fortin. Signer,writer and creator of a very popular french-quebec band called "Les Colocs". This is a bio-movie of the life of this late signer .
