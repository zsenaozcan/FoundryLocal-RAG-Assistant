Title: Our Little Differences (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Sylvie Michel
Main Cast: Wolfram Koch, Bettina Stucky, Leo Bruckmann, Silvia Petkova, Anne Ratte-Polle
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In this acutely observed and sharply written debut feature from director Sylvie Michel, the contrasting parenting philosophies of a well-to-do divorcee and his immigrant cleaning lady becomes an angry debate over deeper issues of social class and cultural values when their teenage children disappear after a night on the town. (TIFF)
