Title: You'll Find Out (1940)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: David Butler
Main Cast: Kay Kyser, Boris Karloff, Peter Lorre, Bela Lugosi, Helen Parrish
Keywords: musical, old mansion

Overview: The manager of Kay Kyser's band books them for a birthday party bash for an heiress at a spooky mansion, where sinister forces try to kill her.
