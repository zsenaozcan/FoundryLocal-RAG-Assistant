Title: Afro Samurai (2007)
Genres: Animation, Adventure, Action, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Fuminori Kizaki
Main Cast: Samuel L. Jackson, Phil LaMarr, Ron Perlman, Yuri Lowenthal

Overview: A Black samurai goes on a mission to avenge the wrongful death of his father in a futuristic feudal Japan.In the Afro Samurai world there are many headbands and they signify the best fighters in the world. If a person should hold number 1, they are referred to as a god among combatants, killers, and assassins. Afro Samurai's father was number one. That was until a cowboy mutant gunslinger named Justice shot him in the head. Now as number two, Afro seeks sweet, pure, and bloody revenge.
