Title: Why Does Herr R. Run Amok? (1970)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rainer Werner Fassbinder
Main Cast: Lilith Ungerer, Kurt Raab, Lilo Pempeit, Franz Maron, Hanna Schygulla
Keywords: attempted murder, unexpected happiness, married couple, work, success, uxoricide, marriage problems, colleague

Overview: Herr Raab is a technical draftsman. His wife pushes him to seek a promotion, which may be a long shot after he gives a dull and tipsy toast at an office dinner. But why would Herr Raab run amok?
