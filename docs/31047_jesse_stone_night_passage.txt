Title: Jesse Stone: Night Passage (2006)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Thriller, Crime
Director: Robert Harmon
Main Cast: Tom Selleck, Saul Rubinek, Viola Davis, Kohl Sudduth, Polly Shannon
Keywords: cop, new england, jesse stone

Overview: In this prequel to 'Stone Cold,' Tom Selleck reprises his role as Jesse Stone, an L.A. cop who relocates to a small town only to find himself immersed in one mystery after the other.
