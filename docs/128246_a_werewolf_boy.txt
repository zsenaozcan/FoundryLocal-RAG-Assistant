Title: A Werewolf Boy (2012)
Genres: Fantasy, Romance
Director: Jo Sung-hee
Main Cast: Song Joong-ki, Park Bo-young, Yoo Yeon-seok, Jang Young-nam, Kim Hyang-gi
Keywords: single parent, werewolf, orphan

Overview: Summoned by an unexpected phone call, an elderly woman visits the country cottage she lived in as a child. Memories of an orphan boy she knew 47 years ago come flooding back to her.
