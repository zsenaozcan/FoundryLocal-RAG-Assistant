Title: A Wife Confesses (1961)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: Yasuzô Masumura
Main Cast: Ayako Wakao, Hiroshi Kawaguchi, Eitarô Ozawa, Haruko Mabuchi, Jun Negami

Overview: Tied to a mountain between her brutalizing spouse and her secret lover, disaster strikes; the authorities accuse her of murder and prompt a confession.
