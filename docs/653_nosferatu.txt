Title: Nosferatu (1922)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror
Director: F.W. Murnau
Main Cast: Max Schreck, Gustav von Wangenheim, Greta Schröder, Alexander Granach, Georg H. Schnell
Keywords: transylvania, shapeshifting, self sacrifice, castle, vampire, ghost ship, loss of wife, silent film, seashore, vampire bat

Overview: Vampire Count Orlok is interested in a new residence and in real estate agent Hutter's young wife. Silent horror classic based on the Bram Stoker novel "Dracula".
