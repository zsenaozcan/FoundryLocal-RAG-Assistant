Title: Didi, the Goofy Cupid (2003)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Family
Director: Alexandre Boury
Main Cast: Renato Aragão, Daniel, Jackeline Petkovic, Tadeu Mello, Helen Ganzarolli

Overview: A clumsy angel is sent away from heaven by God Himself. He may only return after he becomes a good Cupid. But the first couple he tries to connect is a real challenge: the man is poor, and the girl comes from a rich family. And their names are Romeu and Julieta.
