Title: Queen of Spades (2016)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Pavel Lungin
Main Cast: Kseniya Rappoport, Ivan Yankovskiy, Igor Mirkurbanov, Mariya Kurdenevich, Evgeniy Zelenskiy

Overview: Once upon a time, the great soprano Sofia Mayer conquered the world with her voice, her beauty and the legend she carefully built around herself. Now only the legend remains — the diva herself hasn't performed for years, nor been seen in the glittering circles of society she once dominated. But the woman who fascinated and thrilled the world for so long would like to crown her career with one more triumph. And she'll use every dirty trick she knows to achieve it.
