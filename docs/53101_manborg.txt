Title: Manborg (2011)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Steven Kostanski
Main Cast: Meredith Sweeney, Matthew Kennedy, Adam Brooks, Jeremy Gillespie, Stephen Gomori
Keywords: cyborg, dystopic future

Overview: The evil Count Draculon and his army of demons have conquered Earth, but a young soldier who lost his life in the first war against Hell has reawakened in the future as MANBORG! Together with a crew of post-apocalyptic rebels, the half-man half-machine hero will fight to defeat Draculon and take back the planet!
