Title: Clean (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Olivier Assayas
Main Cast: Maggie Cheung, Nick Nolte, Béatrice Dalle, Jeanne Balibar, Don McKellar
Keywords: addiction, recovering drug addict, estranged son

Overview: After losing her husband to a heroin overdose, Emily Wang fights to overcome her own addictions and to be reconciled with her estranged son.
