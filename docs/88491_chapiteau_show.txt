Title: Chapiteau-Show (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sergei Loban
Main Cast: Pyotr Mamonov, Vera Strokova, Sergei Popov, Алексей Подольский, Valeri Zavodovsky
Keywords: musical

Overview: Four parts and four travels to the sea, four crossed short stories: Love, Friendship, Respect and Cooperation. Heroes of each of the short stories arrive to seaside town, and go through turning fragments of their life...
