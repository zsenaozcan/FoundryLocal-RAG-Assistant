Title: Cut! (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Mystery
Director: David Rountree
Main Cast: Sam Scarber, Dahlia Salem, Gabrielle Stone, David Banks, David Rountree

Overview: Nothing is as it seems as an ex-con and aspirant filmmaker set out to manufacture a horror film by scaring real people; however, when it goes too far and someone actually dies, the pair decide that killing for real on film is the way to make a truly terrifying movie.
