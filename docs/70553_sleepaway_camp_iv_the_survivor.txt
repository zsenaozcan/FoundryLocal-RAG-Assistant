Title: Sleepaway Camp IV: The Survivor (1992)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jim Markovic
Main Cast: Carrie Chambers, Victor Campos, John Lodico, Felissa Rose, Jonathan Tiersten

Overview: In the forest, she is grabbed by two large hands. Expecting the hands to be Jack's, she is surprised to meet Eugene (John Gallhager), a large, oafish hunter. After the enraged Alison explains her situation with Jack to him, Eugene vows to help her "take care" of him. Eugene has reasons of his own for disliking Jack - being a hunter. Jack has always tried to keep him off the property which Eugene's family has hunted on for generations.
