Title: Pray the Devil Back to Hell (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Gini Reticker
Main Cast: Janet Johnson Bryant, Etweda Cooper, Vaiba Flomo, Leymah Gbowee, Asatu Bah Kenneth
Keywords: strong woman, peace, war, woman director, liberia

Overview: Pray the Devil Back to Hell chronicles the remarkable story of the Liberian women who came together to end a bloody civil war and bring peace to their shattered country.
