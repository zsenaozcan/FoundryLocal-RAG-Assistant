Title: Kids in America (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Josh Stolberg
Main Cast: Gregory Smith, Stephanie Sherrin, Caitlin Wachs, Nicole Richie, Julie Bowen
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Inspired by real events, this ribald comedy pits an unlikely gang of students against their principal after she bars safe-sex activities on campus. Protesting Principal Weller's muzzling of free speech, the teens stage a bold and hilarious rebellion.
