Title: Desmundo (2002)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Alain Fresnot
Main Cast: Simone Spoladore, Osmar Prado, Caco Ciocler, Berta Zemel, Beatriz Segall

Overview: The story of Orisbela, a young and religious woman who comes from Portugal to Brazil to marry Francisco de Albuquerque, a rude sugar-cane plantation owner, in 1570.
