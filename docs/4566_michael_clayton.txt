Title: Michael Clayton (2007)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Crime
Director: Tony Gilroy
Main Cast: George Clooney, Tom Wilkinson, Tilda Swinton, Sydney Pollack, Michael O'Keefe
Keywords: killing, restaurant, chambers of a barrister, scandal, pretended suicide, lawsuit, car bomb, business ethics, crooked lawyer
