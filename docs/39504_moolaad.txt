Title: Moolaadé (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ousmane Sembene
Main Cast: Fatoumata Coulibaly, Maimouna Hélène Diarra, Salimata Traoré, Dominique Zeïda, Rasmane Ouedraogo
Keywords: africa, tradition, magic, feminism, sexism, polygamy

Overview: When a woman shelters a group of girls from suffering female genital mutilation, she starts a conflict that tears her village apart.
