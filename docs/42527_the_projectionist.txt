Title: The Projectionist (1971)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Harry Hurwitz
Main Cast: Chuck McCann, Rodney Dangerfield, Ina Balin, Jára Kohout, Harry Hurwitz
Keywords: projectionist, loner, film buff

Overview: A projectionist bored with his everyday life begins fantasizing about his being one of the superheroes he sees in the movies he shows.
