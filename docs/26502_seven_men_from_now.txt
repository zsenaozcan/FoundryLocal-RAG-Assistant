Title: Seven Men from Now (1956)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Western
Director: Budd Boetticher
Main Cast: Randolph Scott, Gail Russell, Lee Marvin, Walter Reed, John Larch
Keywords: sheriff, outlaw, wells fargo

Overview: Aging lawman Ben Stride burns a trail of murderous revenge across a hardscrabble landscape searching for his wife's murderers.
