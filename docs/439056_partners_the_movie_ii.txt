Title: Partners: The Movie II (2010)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Seiji Izumi
Main Cast: Jun Kunimura, Yukiyoshi Ozawa, Takehiro Hira, Yutaka Mizutani, Ittoku Kishibe
Keywords: police

Overview: "Partners: The Movie II" is the second of the movies based on the superpopular Japanese show "Aibou". In this one, the kidnapping of some chiefs of police by a rogue ex-policeman is just the beginning for a much much bigger story that it seems at first sight.
