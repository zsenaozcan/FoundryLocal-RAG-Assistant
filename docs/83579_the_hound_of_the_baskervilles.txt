Title: The Hound of the Baskervilles (1983)
Genres: Horror, Crime, Mystery
Director: Douglas Hickox
Main Cast: Ian Richardson, Donald Churchill, Denholm Elliott
Keywords: sherlock holmes

Overview: Sherlock Holmes comes to the aid of his friend Henry Baskerville, who is under a family curse and menaced by a demonic dog that prowls the bogs near his estate and murders people.
