Title: Shadows in the Sun (2005)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Brad Mirman
Main Cast: Harvey Keitel, Joshua Jackson, Claire Forlani, Armando Pucci, Giancarlo Giannini
Keywords: italy, london england, isolation, author, loss of wife

Overview: An aspiring young writer (Jackson) tracks a literary titan (Keitel) suffering from writers block to his refuge in rural Italy and learns about life and love from the irascible genius and his daughters.
