Title: I Believe in Unicorns (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Leah Meyerhoff
Main Cast: Natalia Dyer, Peter Vack, Julia Garner, Amy Seimetz, Toni Meyerhoff
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Follows the lyrical journey of an imaginative teenage girl who runs away from home with an older punk rock drifter, but not even unicorns can save her now.
