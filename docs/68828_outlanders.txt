Title: Outlanders (1986)
Genres: Animation, Science Fiction, Comedy
Director: Katsuhisa Yamada
Main Cast: Fumi Hirano, Mitsuo Iwata, Akira Kamiya, Kenji Utsumi, Emi Shinohara

Overview: In the midst of a violent invasion of Earth by unknown alien forces, photo-journalist Wakatsuki Tetsuya comes across a scantily-clad alien woman, cutting a swath of death through the Terran ranks with her sword. After a chaotic struggle, Tetsuya is knocked unconscious, only to awaken aboard her starship.
