Title: Stan Lee Presents: Mosiac (2007)
Genres: Action, Animation, Fantasy
Director: Roy Allen Smith
Main Cast: Anna Paquin, Kirby Morrow, Cam Clarke, Garry Chalk, Nicole Oliver
Keywords: secret agent, interpol

Overview: A break-in and murder at a New York City museum sparks an investigation by Interpol Agent Nathan Nelson. When he finds an ancient artifact, Nelson takes it home to conduct more research. A lightning storm, a pet chameleon and the artifact gives his daughter, Maggie, mystical powers to shape-shift her appearance at will which includes the ability to mimic other people and animals.
