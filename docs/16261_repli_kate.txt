Title: Repli-Kate (2002)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Frank Longo
Main Cast: Ali Landry, James Roday, Desmond Askew, Eugene Levy, Todd Robert Anderson
Keywords: female nudity, clone, journalist, human experimentation, perfectionist, white woman

Overview: A scientist and his sidekick accidentally clone a journalist and decide to turn the mistake into Repli-Kate, their idea of the perfect woman, who loves, beer, football, and frat parties.
