Title: Private Valentine: Blonde & Dangerous (2008)
Genres: Comedy, War, Adventure, Drama
Director: Steve Miner
Main Cast: Jessica Simpson, Vivica A. Fox, Steve Guttenberg, Aimee Garcia, Olesya Rulin

Overview: When fluffy, bubble gum movie star Megan Valentine suddenly finds herself broke and humiliated in the public eye, she wanders from the wreckage of a car accident and witlessly enlists in the U.S. Army hoping in vain that it will change her life.
