Title: Toomorrow (1970)
Genres: Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Val Guest
Main Cast: Olivia Newton-John, Benny Thomas, Vic Cooper, Karl Chambers, Roy Dotrice
Keywords: musical

Overview: A group of students pay their way through school by forming a pop band called Toomorrow. Sonic vibrations from a special instrument called a ‘tonaliser’ cause an extraterrestrial to abduct the group, and have them entertain the Alphoid population.
