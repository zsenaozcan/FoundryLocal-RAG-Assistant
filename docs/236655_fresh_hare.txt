Title: Fresh Hare (1942)
Genres: Comedy, Animation
Director: Friz Freleng
Main Cast: Mel Blanc, Arthur Q. Bryan, The Sportsmen Quartet
Keywords: bugs bunny, short

Overview: Bugs Bunny is wanted "dead or alive" by the Mounted Police, led by Elmer Fudd. The "Fresh Hare" episode was banned from television for almost 30 years because it was considered too racey for the time.
