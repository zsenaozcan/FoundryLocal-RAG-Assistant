Title: With a Song in My Heart (1952)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Walter Lang
Main Cast: Susan Hayward, Rory Calhoun, David Wayne, Thelma Ritter, Robert Wagner
Keywords: musical

Overview: Jane Froman (Susan Hayward), an aspiring songstress, lands a job in radio with help from pianist Don Ross (David Wayne), whom she later marries. Jane's popularity soars, and she leaves on a European tour... but her plane crashes in Lisbon, and she is partially crippled. Unable to walk without crutches, Jane nevertheless goes on to entertain the Allied troops in World War II.
