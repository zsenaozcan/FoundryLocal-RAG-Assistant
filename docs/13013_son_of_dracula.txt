Title: Son of Dracula (1974)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Music
Director: Freddie Francis
Main Cast: Harry Nilsson, Ringo Starr, Freddie Jones, Suzanna Leigh, Dennis Price
Keywords: musical

Overview: Due to be crowned King of the Netherworld by his mentor Merlin the Magician at a monster's convention Count Downe, the son of Count Dracula, falls in love with the beautiful but human Amber and finds himself in conflict with Baron Frankenstein who is vying for the same honorary title.
