Title: The Point Men (2001)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: John Glen
Main Cast: Christopher Lambert, Vincent Regan, Cal Macaninch, Nicolas de Pruyssenaere, Donald Sumpter

Overview: Some time after their botched operation to capture a known Palestinian terrorist, a team of Israeli agents starts to get killed off one by one. Their leader must get to the bottom of things before the killer(s) plan is complete.
