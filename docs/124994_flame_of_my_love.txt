Title: Flame of My Love (1949)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Kenji Mizoguchi
Main Cast: Kinuyo Tanaka, Mitsuko Mito, Kuniko Miyake, Ichirô Sugai, Eitarô Ozawa

Overview: A woman's struggle for equality in Japan in the 1880s. Eiko Hirayama leaves Okayama for Tokyo, where she helps the fledgling Liberal Party and falls in love with its leader Kentaro Omoi, just as the party is being disbanded by the government.
