Title: Le Bal (1983)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ettore Scola
Main Cast: Marc Berman, Christophe Allwright, Étienne Guichard, Régis Bouquet, Francesco De Rosa

Overview: The 50 year story of a ballroom in France, from the 1920s. The people who go there is always the same, even the musicians. You can see all kind of people dancing all the fashion dances (depending on the age).
