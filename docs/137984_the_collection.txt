Title: The Collection (1976)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Michael Apted
Main Cast: Laurence Olivier, Alan Bates, Malcolm McDowell, Helen Mirren, Maurice O'Conner

Overview: Set in the rarefied world of West End boutique owners and fashion designers, The Collection takes as its departure point the moment when four elegant lives are suddenly shaken by the suggestion of infidelity. The sinister anonymous phone call that disturbs Harry Kane at four o'clock in the morning becomes increasingly hard to establish...
