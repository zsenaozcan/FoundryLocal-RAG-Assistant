Title: Maximum Overdrive (1986)
Genres: Action, Drama, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Stephen King
Main Cast: Emilio Estevez, Pat Hingle, Laura Harrington, Yeardley Smith, John Short
Keywords: man vs machine, gas station, roadhouse, apparats and technics, siege, lorry, comet, automation, truck, arcade

Overview: When a comet passes close to the earth, machines all over the world come alive and go on homicidal rampages. A group of people at a desolate truck stop are held hostage by a gang of homicidal 18-wheelers. The frightened people set out to defeat the killer machines ... or be killed by them.
