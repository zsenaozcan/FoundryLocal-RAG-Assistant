Title: Ironclads (1991)
Genres: TV Movie, History, War
Director: Delbert Mann
Main Cast: Virginia Madsen, Alex Hyde-White, Reed Diamond, Philip Casnoff, E.G. Marshall
Keywords: american civil war

Overview: Ironclads is a 1991 made-for-television movie produced by Ted Turner's TNT company about the events behind the creation of the CSS Virginia from the remains of the USS Merrimack and the battle between the Virginia and the USS Monitor in the Battle of Hampton Roads, March 8, 1862-March 9, 1862.
