Title: Lethal Hostage (2012)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Cheng Er
Main Cast: Sun Honglei, Wang Luodan, Mo Zhang, Ni Dahong
Keywords: thriller

Overview: Produced by Crazy Racer director Ning Hao, Lethal Hostage revolves around a drug dealer who falls in love with his hostage and decides to quit the business following one final deal.
