Title: Mortuary (2005)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Tobe Hooper
Main Cast: Dan Byrd, Stephanie Patton, Alexandra Adi, Rocky Marquette, William Alva
Keywords: long abandoned, evil lurks there

Overview: A family moves to a small town in California where they plan on starting a new life while running a long-abandoned funeral home. The locals fear the place, which is suspected to be on haunted ground
