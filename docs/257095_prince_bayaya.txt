Title: Prince Bayaya (1950)
Genres: Animation
Director: Jiří Trnka
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: stop motion

Overview: The first fairy tale transformed into a full-length picture by Jiri Trnka. It is based on the charming story about prince Bajaja by Bozena Nemcova. Bajaja saves three princesses from a dragon’s claws and then marries the youngest of them.
