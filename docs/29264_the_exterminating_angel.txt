Title: The Exterminating Angel (1962)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Luis Buñuel
Main Cast: Silvia Pinal, Enrique Rambal, Claudio Brook, José Baviera, Augusto Benedico
Keywords: upper class, room, sheep, water, guest, quarantine, surrealism, lamb, mansion, pretension

Overview: The guests at an upper-class dinner party find themselves unable to leave.  The Exterminating Angel is a 1962 surrealist film, written and directed by Luis Buñuel, starring Silvia Pinal. Sharply satirical and allegorical, the film contains a view of human nature suggesting "mankind harbors savage instincts and unspeakable secrets". It is considered one of the best 1,000 films by The New York Times.
