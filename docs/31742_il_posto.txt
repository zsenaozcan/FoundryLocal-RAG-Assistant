Title: Il Posto (1961)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ermanno Olmi
Main Cast: Loredana Detto, Sandro Panseri, Tullio Kezich, Mara Revel, Guido Spadea
Keywords: job, corporation, college graduate

Overview: Domenico and Antonietta are two suburban Italian youths who meet while seeking "a job for life" from a big city corporation.
