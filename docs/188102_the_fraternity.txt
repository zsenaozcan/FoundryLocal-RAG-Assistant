Title: The Fraternity (2002)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Sidney J. Furie
Main Cast: Treat Williams, Robin Dunne, Malin Åkerman, Dylan Trowbridge, Gianpaolo Venuta
Keywords: high school, clique, preparatory school, elitism, cheating at exam

Overview: Some students at Runcie prep school form an elite club. The group is implicated in a cheating scandal and one of the club members dies mysteriously. Another member decides to try and discover the dangerous truth about Runcie.
