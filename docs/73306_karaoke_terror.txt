Title: Karaoke Terror (2003)
Genres: Horror, Drama
Director: Tetsuo Shinohara
Main Cast: Ryuhei Matsuda, Kayoko Kishimoto, Hiroyuki Ikeuchi, Yôichirô Saitô, Sawa Suzuki

Overview: One of a gang of karaoke loving middle aged women is murdered by a young man. Her friends track him down and kill him. His friends track them down and kill the killer ..... and it escalates!
