Title: Celine and Julie Go Boating (1974)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Jacques Rivette
Main Cast: Juliet Berto, Dominique Labourier, Bulle Ogier, Marie-France Pisier, Barbet Schroeder
Keywords: anarchic comedy

Overview: A mysteriously linked pair of young women find their daily lives pre-empted by a strange boudoir melodrama that plays itself out in a hallucinatory parallel reality.
