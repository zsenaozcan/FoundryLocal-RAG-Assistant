Title: Stuck in Love (2012)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Josh Boone
Main Cast: Greg Kinnear, Jennifer Connelly, Lily Collins, Nat Wolff, Logan Lerman
Keywords: love, independent film, relationship, family, novelist, unhappiness

Overview: An acclaimed writer, his ex-wife, and their teenaged children come to terms with the complexities of love in all its forms over the course of one tumultuous year.
