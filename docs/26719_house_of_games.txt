Title: House of Games (1987)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: David Mamet
Main Cast: Lindsay Crouse, Joe Mantegna, Mike Nussbaum, J.T. Walsh, Ricky Jay
Keywords: con man, fraud, gambling debts, noir

Overview: A psychiatrist comes to the aid of a compulsive gambler and is led by a smooth-talking grifter into the shadowy but compelling world of stings, scams, and con men.
