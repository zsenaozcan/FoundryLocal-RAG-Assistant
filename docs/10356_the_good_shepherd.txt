Title: The Good Shepherd (2004)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Lewin Webb
Main Cast: Christian Slater, Molly Parker, Stephen Rea, Gordon Pinsent, Nancy Beatty
Keywords: sexual abuse, messe, murder, priest, church

Overview: CHRISTIAN SLATER plays a worldly and urbane priest who is forced to challenge his comfortable existence as an ecclesiastical spin-doctor when he comes to believe in the innocence of a young priest accused of murder. His only ally, a journalist, is also his former sweetheart.
