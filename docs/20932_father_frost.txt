Title: Father Frost (1964)
Genres: Family, Fantasy, Comedy, Romance
Director: Aleksandr Rou
Main Cast: Aleksandr Khvylya, Natalya Sedykh, Eduard Izotov, Inna Churikova, Pavel Pavlenko
Keywords: jealousy, winter, magic, russian, fairy tale, step mother, love, money, mother daughter relationship, old man

Overview: A fairy tale about a conceited young man and a young woman with a tyrannical step-mother, who must overcome magical trials in order to be together.
