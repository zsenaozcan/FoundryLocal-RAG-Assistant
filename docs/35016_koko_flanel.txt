Title: Koko Flanel (1990)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Stijn Coninx
Main Cast: Urbanus, Bea Van Der Maet, Willeke van Ammelrooy, Herbert Flack, Jan Decleir

Overview: Placide's dad tells him on his deathbed he'll haunt Placide if he doesn't find a wife soon. Placide cautiously agrees, but he only wants to settle for a very beautiful woman. That might be easier said than done, since he is a vagabond. When he accidentally enters a photo shoot, Placide meets Sarah. He falls in love, but she is way out of his reach.
