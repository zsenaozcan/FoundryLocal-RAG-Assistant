Title: The Glass Agency (1999)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ebrahim Hatamikia
Main Cast: Parviz Parastui, Reza Kianian, Habib Rezaei

Overview: The Glass Agency is the story of a war veteran living in post war Iran. It depicts veterans who are suffering from social problems after the war. Society does not understand them and the standard social norms are not in harmony with their personalities.
