Title: Penumbra (2012)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Adrián García Bogliano
Main Cast: Cristina Brondo, Camila Bordonaba, Berta Muñiz, Arnaldo André, Mirella Pascual

Overview: A woman hesitantly rents an apartment to an eerie man who she soon realizes has a part in the solar eclipse that is taking place.
