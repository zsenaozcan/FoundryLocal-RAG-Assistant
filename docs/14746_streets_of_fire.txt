Title: Streets of Fire (1984)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Music
Director: Walter Hill
Main Cast: Michael Paré, Diane Lane, Rick Moranis, Amy Madigan, Willem Dafoe
Keywords: fan, rescue, singer, motorcycle, motorcycle gang

Overview: Raven Shaddock and his gang of merciless biker friends kidnap rock singer Ellen Aim. Ellen's former lover, soldier-for-hire Tom Cody, happens to be passing through town on a visit. In an attempt to save his star act, Ellen's manager hires Tom to rescue her. Along with a former soldier, they battle through dangerous cityscapes, determined to get Ellen back.
