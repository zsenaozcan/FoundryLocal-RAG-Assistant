Title: Two on a Guillotine (1965)
Genres: Mystery, Horror
Director: William Conrad
Main Cast: Connie Stevens, Dean Jones, Cesar Romero, Parley Baer, Virginia Gregg
Keywords: magic, inheritance, mansion

Overview: The daughter of a dead magician who accidentally killed his wife, her mother, while performing a guillotine trick must spend the night in his house in order to collect her inheritance. Is the house haunted or is it all magic?
