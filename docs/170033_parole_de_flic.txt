Title: Parole de flic (1985)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Action, Crime
Director: José Pinheiro
Main Cast: Alain Delon, Jacques Perrin, Fiona Gélin, Eva Darlan, Jean-François Stévenin

Overview: Cop's Honor (Parole de Flic) is a 1985 French crime movie directed by José Pinheiro and starring Alain Delon as retired police officer Daniel Pratt. His teenager daughter was killed by a gang of mysterious hooded killers so Pratt began his own investigation to avenge the killers and their backroom leader.
