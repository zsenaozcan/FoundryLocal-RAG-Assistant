Title: Night of the Comet (1984)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Thom Eberhardt
Main Cast: Catherine Mary Stewart, Robert Beltran, Kelli Maroney, Sharon Farrell, Mary Woronov
Keywords: post-apocalyptic, comet, end of the world

Overview: Two girls from the Valley wake up to find that a passing comet has eradicated their world and left behind a mysterious red-dust and a pack of cannibal mutants. With the help of a friendly truck driver, the girls save the earth from a villainous "think tank," karate chop their way through flesh-eating zombies, and, of course, find time to go to the mall.
