Title: White Tiger (2012)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy, War
Director: Karen Shakhnazarov
Main Cast: Vladimir Ilin, Gerasim Arkhipov, Aleksandr Vakhov, Vilmar Bieri, Dmitri Bykovsky
Keywords: obsession, world war ii, eastern front, tank battle

Overview: Great Patriotic War, early 1940s. After barely surviving a battle with a mysterious, ghostly-white Tiger tank, Red Army Sergeant Ivan Naydenov becomes obsessed with its destruction.
