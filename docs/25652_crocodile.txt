Title: Crocodile (1996)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kim Ki-duk
Main Cast: Jo Jae-hyeon, Jeon Mu-song, Woo Yun-Kyeong, Ahn Jae-hong, Han Tae-il
Keywords: suicide, korea

Overview: Crocodile is a 1996 South Korean film. It was the directorial debut of Ki-duk Kim and stars Cho Jae-hyun as "Crocodile".The film tells the story of a man living at the edge of the Han River in Seoul, who saves a woman trying to commit suicide. He then proceeds to rape and abuse her until an odd relationship develops between them.
