Title: Darkweb (2016)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Bruno Vaussenat
Main Cast: Danny Glover, Nina Seul, Tristan Robin, Petra Silander, Olivier Gruner
Keywords: filmed killing, most dangerous game, hunting party

Overview: A mysterious group has created a sadistic venture. Kidnapping young women, they auction the rights to hunt them to millionaires with a thirst for blood - and broadcast the hunt on the dark web for the world to see.
