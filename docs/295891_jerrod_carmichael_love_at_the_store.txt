Title: Jerrod Carmichael: Love at the Store (2014)
Genres: Comedy, TV Movie
Director: Spike Lee
Main Cast: Jerrod Carmichael
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Rising comedy star Jerrod Carmichael takes to the stage of The Comedy Store in Hollywood, CA where he comically subverts such subjects as poverty, wealth, crime and race and presents his unique take on national tragedies, female empowerment, and more.
