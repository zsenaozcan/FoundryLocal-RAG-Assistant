Title: Statues Also Die (1953)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Chris Marker
Main Cast: Jean Négroni
Keywords: africa, politics, anthropology, racism, art, colonialism

Overview: Short documentary ordered by the magazine "Présence Africaine". From the question "Why is the african in the Human museum while Greek or Egyptian art are in Le Louvre?", the two directors expose and criticise the lack of consideration for African art. The film was censored in France for eight years because of its anti-colonial perspective.
