Title: The Deadly Tower (1975)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Action
Director: Jerry Jameson
Main Cast: Kurt Russell, Ned Beatty, Richard Yniguez, Pernell Roberts, Clifton James
Keywords: shooting, tower

Overview: The real-life story of Charles Whitman's deadly shooting spree at the University of Texas is retold.  In August 1966, after killing his wife and mother, Whitman climbed to the top of the school's tower and opened fire on passers-by, killing 13 and wounding many others.
