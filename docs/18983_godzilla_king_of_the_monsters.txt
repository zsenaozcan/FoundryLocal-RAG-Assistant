Title: Godzilla, King of the Monsters! (1956)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror
Director: Ishirô Honda
Main Cast: Raymond Burr, Frank Iwanaga, Takashi Shimura, Momoko Kôchi, Akira Takarada
Keywords: godzilla, giant monster, reporter, tokyo japan, kaiju, animal horror

Overview: A 400-foot  dinosaur-like beast attacks Tokyo. Filmed in 1954 as Gojira, this grandaddy of all Japanese giant-reptile epics was picked up for American distribution two years later, at which time several newly filmed inserts, featuring Raymond Burr as reporter Steve Martin, were edited into the original footage.
