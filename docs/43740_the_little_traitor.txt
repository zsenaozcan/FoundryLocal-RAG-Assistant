Title: The Little Traitor (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lynn Roth
Main Cast: Alfred Molina, Ido Port, Zachi Noy, Anat Klausner

Overview: Based on the novel "Panther in the Basement" by the world-renowned author, Amos Oz, the movie takes place in Palestine in 1947, just a few months before Israel becomes a state. Proffy Liebowitz, a militant yet sensitive eleven year old wants nothing more than for the occupying British to get the hell out of his land.
