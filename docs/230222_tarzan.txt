Title: Tarzan (2013)
Genres: Animation, Action, Adventure, Family
Director: Reinhard Klooss
Main Cast: Kellan Lutz, Spencer Locke, Les Bubb, Joe Cappelletti, Brian Huskey
Keywords: based on novel, tarzan, motion capture

Overview: One of the most classic and revered stories of all time, Edgar Rice Burroughs' Tarzan returns to the big screen for a new generation. Tarzan and Jane face a mercenary army dispatched by the evil CEO of Greystoke Energies, a man who took over the company from Tarzan's parents, after they died in a plane crash in the African jungle.
