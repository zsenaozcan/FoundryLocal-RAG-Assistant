Title: Tarzan Escapes (1936)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: Johnny Weissmuller, Maureen O'Sullivan, John Buckler, Benita Hume, William Henry
Keywords: tarzan

Overview: White hunter Captain Fry tries to take Tarzan back to civilization, caged for public display. He arrives in the jungle with Jane's cousins, Eric and Rita who want Jane's help in claiming a fortune left her.
