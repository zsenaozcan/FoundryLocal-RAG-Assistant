Title: Two Sisters From Boston (1946)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Henry Koster
Main Cast: Kathryn Grayson, June Allyson, Jimmy Durante, Peter Lawford, John George

Overview: Abigail Chandler has written her stuffy Boston relatives that she's a successful opera singer in New York. In reality, she works at a burlesque house and is billed as High-C Susie. When her sister Martha comes for a visit, Abigail tries to hide the truth from her.
