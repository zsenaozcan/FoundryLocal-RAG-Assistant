Title: Here Comes the Devil (2012)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Adrián García Bogliano
Main Cast: Francisco Barreiro, Giancarlo Ruiz, Laura Caro, Alan Martinez, Michele Garcia
Keywords: demonic possession, tijuana

Overview: A married couple loses their children while on a family trip near some caves in Tijuana. The kids eventually reappear without explanation, but it becomes clear that they are not who they used to be, that something terrifying has changed them.
