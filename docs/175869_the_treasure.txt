Title: The Treasure (1923)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: G.W. Pabst
Main Cast: Albert Steinrück, Lucie Mannheim, Ilka Grüning, Werner Krauss, Hans Brausewetter
Keywords: slovenia, bell maker

Overview: On the surface a straightforward tale of the search for a buried treasure, the film is a textbook example of German expressionism, with the passions of the protagonists conveyed as much through symbolism as action.
