Title: Narcissus (1983)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Norman McLaren
Main Cast: Jean-Louis Morin, Sylvie Kinal, Sylvain Lafortune
Keywords: dance, short

Overview: In this short film by Norman McLaren, dancers enact the Greek tragedy of Narcissus, the beautiful youth whose excessive self-love condemned him to a trapped existence. Skilfully merging film, dance and music, the film is a compendium of the techniques McLaren acquired over a lifetime of experimentation.
