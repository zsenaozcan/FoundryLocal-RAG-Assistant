Title: Slumber Party Massacre III (1990)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Sally Mattison
Main Cast: Keely Christian, Brittain Frye, Maria Ford, Hope Marie Carlton, Brandi Burkett
Keywords: slasher, woman director

Overview: After a hard day of volleyball at the beach, a teen whose parents are away decides to have a slumber party with her girlfriends. Their boyfriends predictably show up to scare them, but a stranger from the beach is also seen lurking around the house. Soon the group begins experiencing an attrition problem.
