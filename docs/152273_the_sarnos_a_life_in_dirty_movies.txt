Title: The Sarnos: A Life in Dirty Movies (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Wiktor Ericsson
Main Cast: Joseph W. Sarno, Peggy Steffans, John Waters, Annie Sprinkle, Jamie Gillis

Overview: A love story about the legendary sexploitation director Joe Sarno and his loyal wife and business partner Peggy. The film relates their part in the history of sex film, their lives between New York and Sweden, and their struggle to produce one last erotic film.
