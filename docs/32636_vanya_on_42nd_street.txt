Title: Vanya on 42nd Street (1994)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Louis Malle
Main Cast: Wallace Shawn, Julianne Moore, Larry Pine, Brooke Smith, George Gaynes
Keywords: independent film, chekhov

Overview: An uninterrupted rehersal of Chekhov's "Uncle Vanya" played out by a company of actors. The setting is their run down theater with an unusable stage and crumbling ceiling. The play is shown act by act with the briefest of breaks to move props or for refreshments. The lack of costumes, real props and scenery is soon forgotten.
