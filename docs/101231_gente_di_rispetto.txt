Title: Gente Di Rispetto (1975)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: Luigi Zampa
Main Cast: Jennifer O'Neill, Franco Nero, James Mason, Aldo Giuffrè, Franco Fabrizi

Overview: A female school teacher is implicated in a murder in a Sicilian town only hours after her arrival. The dead man insulted her on the bus on the way into town. As the mystery unfolds, it becomes clear that the town is hiding some very sinister secrets.
