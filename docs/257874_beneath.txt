Title: Beneath (2013)
Genres: Horror
Director: Ben Ketai
Main Cast: Brent Briscoe, Kurt Caceres, Eric Etebari, Jeff Fahey, Joey Kern
Keywords: underground, mine, trapped

Overview: A crew of coal miners becomes trapped 600 feet below ground after a disastrous collapse.  As the air grows more toxic and time runs out, they slowly descend into madness and begin to turn on one another.  Inspired by true events.
