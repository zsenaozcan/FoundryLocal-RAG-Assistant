Title: Carnival of Sinners (1943)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror
Director: Maurice Tourneur
Main Cast: Pierre Fresnay, Josseline Gaël, Noël Roquevert, Guillaume de Sax, Pierre Palau
Keywords: pact with the devil, supernatural, devil
