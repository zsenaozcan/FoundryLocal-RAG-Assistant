Title: Chained (2012)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Jennifer Chambers Lynch
Main Cast: Vincent D'Onofrio, Eamon Farren, Julia Ormond, Gina Philips, Evan Bird
Keywords: rap music, serial killer, incest overtones, protégé, desolation, serial rapist, woman director

Overview: A serial killer kidnaps a young boy after murdering his mother, then raises him to be his accomplice. After years in captivity, the boy must choose between escaping or following in his captor's bloody footprints.
