Title: Yesterday (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Darrell James Roodt
Main Cast: Leleti Khumalo, Kenneth Khambula, Harriet Lenabe, Lihle Mvelase, Camilla Walker
Keywords: aids, mine, south africa, dying and death, johannesburg, infection, kral, independent film, school
