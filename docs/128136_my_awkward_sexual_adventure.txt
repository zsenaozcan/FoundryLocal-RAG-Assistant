Title: My Awkward Sexual Adventure (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Sean Garrity
Main Cast: Jonas Chernick, Emily Hampshire, Sarah Manninen, Vik Sahay
Keywords: underwear, masseuse, threesome, dildo, sex lesson

Overview: A hyper-repressed and schlubby accountant (Jonas Chernick) strikes a deal with a worldly but disorganized stripper (Emily Hampshire): he'll help her with her crushing debt if she helps him become a better lover. Sharp direction by the versatile Sean Garrity and a very funny script by Chernick ensure for an uproarious — and surprisingly educational — sex comedy. (TIFF)
