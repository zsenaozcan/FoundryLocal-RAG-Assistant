Title: Tender Age (2000)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Sergey Solovyov
Main Cast: Dmitri Solovyov, Yelena Kamayeva, Nikolay Chindyaykin, Valentin Gaft, Sergey Garmash

Overview: A movie which at first seems like a simple life story of an ordinary young man slowly evolves into an extraordinarily told ballade with twofold meaning - sad and optimistic at the same time...
