Title: Innocent Lies (1995)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery, Foreign
Director: Patrick Dewolf
Main Cast: Gabrielle Anwar, Stephen Dorff, Joanna Lumley, Keira Knightley, Adrian Dunbar

Overview: In September 1938 a British detective comes to a small French coastal town in order to investigate the death of a colleague. Prime suspects are the members of English aristocratic family with plenty of skeletons in the closet. This is a loose adaptation of the Agatha Christie novel Towards Zero.
