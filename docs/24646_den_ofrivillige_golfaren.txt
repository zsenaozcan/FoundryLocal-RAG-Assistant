Title: Den ofrivillige golfaren (1991)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lasse Åberg
Main Cast: Lasse Åberg, Jon Skolmen, Hege Schøyen, Ulf Eklund, Marianne Scheja
Keywords: golf, sport

Overview: I bet anyone can get better at golf than you are in one week, a golfer says to her golfing businessman for a boyfriend in a quarrel. Unfortunately Stig-Helmer is standing nearby when the boyfriend accepts the bet, and he is chosen to be the competitor. Stig-Helmer has never played the game before, but his Norwegian friend Ole promises to help him.
