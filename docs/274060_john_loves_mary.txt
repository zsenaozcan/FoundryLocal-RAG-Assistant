Title: John Loves Mary (1949)
Genres: Comedy
Director: David Butler
Main Cast: Ronald Reagan, Jack Carson, Wayne Morris, Edward Arnold, Virginia Field
Keywords: senator, sergeant, war bride, usher, wedding plans

Overview: After four long years apart, there are so many things returning World War II soldier John Lawrence wants to tell his sweetheart, Mary McKinley. That he loves her. That he's missed her. And that he's married.
