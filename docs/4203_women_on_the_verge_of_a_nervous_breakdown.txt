Title: Women on the Verge of a Nervous Breakdown (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Pedro Almodóvar
Main Cast: Carmen Maura, Antonio Banderas, Julieta Serrano, María Barranco, Rossy de Palma
Keywords: madrid, airport, terrorist, suicide attempt, pregnancy, relationship, ex-wife, spectacle, sleeping pill
