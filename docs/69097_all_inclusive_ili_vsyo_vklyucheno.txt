Title: All Inclusive ili Vsyo Vklyucheno (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Eduard Radzyukevich
Main Cast: Marina Aleksandrova, Anna Ardova, Mikhail Bespalov, Anfisa Chekhova, Viktoriya Dayneko

Overview: Andrey is a handsome veterinarian with a successful Moscow pet clinic. Popular with ladies among his upscale clientele, he is forced to leave town when the jealous husband of one of them hires a hitman.
