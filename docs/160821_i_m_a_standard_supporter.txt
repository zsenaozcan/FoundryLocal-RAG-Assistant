Title: I'm a Standard Supporter (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Riton Liebman
Main Cast: Riton Liebman, Léa Drucker, David Murgia, Guy Staumont, Samir Guesmi
Keywords: sport, romance

Overview: Milou is a football addict. His life is dedicated to his local team: Le Standard de Liège. Milou has no limit when it comes to his football club. But, when he meets Martine, he will soon find good reasons to start his football therapy..
