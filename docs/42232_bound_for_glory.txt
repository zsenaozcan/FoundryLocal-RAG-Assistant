Title: Bound for Glory (1976)
Genres: Music
Director: Hal Ashby
Main Cast: David Carradine, Ronny Cox, Melinda Dillon, Gail Strickland, John Lehne
Keywords: folk music, based on memoir or autobiography, dust bowl, labor union, hoedown

Overview: A biography of Woody Guthrie, one of America's greatest folk singers. He left his dust-devastated Texas home in the 1930s to find work, discovering the suffering and strength of America's working class.
