Title: Venus & Fleur (2004)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Emmanuel Mouret
Main Cast: Isabelle Pirès, Veroushka Knoge, Julien Imbert, Frédéric Niedermayer, Gilbert Mouret

Overview: Fleur, a shy young Parisian in Marseilles, meets Vénus, a flamboyant but lost Russian girl. They have nothing in common, save for their wish to meet the ideal boy.
