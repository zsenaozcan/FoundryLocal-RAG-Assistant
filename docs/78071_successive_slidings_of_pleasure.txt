Title: Successive Slidings of Pleasure (1974)
Genres: Thriller, Fantasy, Horror
Director: Alain Robbe-Grillet
Main Cast: Anicée Alvina, Olga Georges-Picot, Michael Lonsdale, Jean Martin, Marianne Eggerickx
Keywords: female nudity, eroticism, murder, female homosexuality

Overview: A young woman is questioned by the police and the judges, suspected of being a modern witch. The girl who shared her apartment has been found dead, and a pair of scisors impaled through her heart, as she lay attached to the bedposts. Apparently, the girl does have powers, to make all people around her fall prey to her spell, glissing progressively into desire, lust, and the unknown.
