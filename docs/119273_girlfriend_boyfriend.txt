Title: Girlfriend Boyfriend (2012)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Ya-che Yang
Main Cast: Joseph Chang Hsiao-Chuan, Gwei Lun-mei, Rhydian Vaughan

Overview: Mabel, Liam and Aaron have been friends since childhood. Their bonds of friendship are tested when, years later, they realize their friendship is the only reason they have made it through emotional hardships and extreme tragedies.
