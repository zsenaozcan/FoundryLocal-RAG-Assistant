Title: Hanna's Journey (2014)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Julia von Heinz
Main Cast: Karoline Schuch, Suzanne von Borsody, Max Mauff, Lore Richter, Doron Amit
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A German girl travels to Israel to help people with disabilities, where she learns a lot about the role of her grandparents in WWII and meets a man who wants to move to Berlin.
