Title: Sink or Swim (1997)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Gary Rosen
Main Cast: Stephen Rea, Illeana Douglas, Jason Priestley, Olivia d'Abo, Lisa Kudrow

Overview: Brian is a television writer-producer who has to script a 22-episode anthology, but lacks inspiration. He witnesses a strange romantic encounter between two figures on the balcony of hotel near his flat and decides to write scripts with his writer friends based on what he saw.
