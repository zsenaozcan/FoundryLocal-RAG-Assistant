Title: The Dorm (2014)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Rachel Talalay
Main Cast: Alexis Knapp, Jake Croker, Cassie Steele
Keywords: college, woman director, young adult

Overview: The film follows a student, Vivian (Alexis Knapp) who begins college as a shy, self-consciousness freshman but who, after becoming a dorm mate of the popular Sarah (Cassie Steele), begins a complete transformation.
