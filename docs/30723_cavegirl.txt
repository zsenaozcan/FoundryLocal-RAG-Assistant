Title: Cavegirl (1985)
Genres: Comedy
Director: David Oliver
Main Cast: Daniel Roebuck, Cynthia Thompson, Darren Young, Saba Moor-Doucette, Jeff Chayette
Keywords: female nudity, time travel, cave woman

Overview: On a class excursion to a cave with stone age paintings the clumsy Rex gets lost. A mysterious crystal opens a gateway in time and sets him back to the stone ages, where he meets a group of prehistoric fellows and the gorgeous Eba. While teaching her English and doing some pseudo-research on her fellows, his main goal is to get her sleeping with him. However the curiosity of the clan chief disturbs his attempts.
