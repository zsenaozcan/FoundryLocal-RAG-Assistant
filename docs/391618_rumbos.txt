Title: Rumbos (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Manuela Burló Moreno
Main Cast: Carmen Machi, Ernesto Alterio, Pilar López de Ayala, Miki Esparbé, Nora Navas
Keywords: woman director

Overview: During a long night of August, some people have different experiences that leave a deep impression in them. Linked by a radio night show, all them cross the city of Barcelona, not knowing that their fates will collide with each other in unexpected ways.
