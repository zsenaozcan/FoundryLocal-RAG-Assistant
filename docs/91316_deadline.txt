Title: Deadline (2012)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Curt Hahn
Main Cast: Eric Roberts, Steve Talley, Anna Felix, Lauren Jenkins, J.D. Souther

Overview: Deadline is the story of the murder of an African American youth in rural Alabama that has gone uninvestigated, unsolved, and unpunished for almost twenty years. That changes when Nashville Times reporter Matt Harper (Steve Talley) meets an idealistic young blueblood bent on discovering the truth. Harper undertakes the investigation despite the opposition of his publisher, violent threats from mysterious forces, a break-up with his fiancée, and his father's cancer diagnosis.
