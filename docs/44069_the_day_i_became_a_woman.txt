Title: The Day I Became a Woman (2000)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Marzieh Makhmalbaf
Main Cast: Fatemeh Cherag Akhar, Hassan Nebhan, Shahr Banou Sisizadeh, Ameneh Passand, Shabnam Toloui
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A girl reaches the age of nine and is supposed to act as a grown woman according to her family. A girl participates in a bike race against the will of her husband. An elderly woman decides she wants to buy all the things she always wanted but could never get.
