Title: All This Panic (2017)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jenny Gage
Main Cast: Dusty Rose Ryan, Lena M., Ginger Leigh Ryan, Olivia Cucinotta, Sage Adams
Keywords: coming of age, teenager, woman director

Overview: Shot over a three-year period with unparalleled intimacy and access, ALL THIS PANIC is a feature length documentary that takes an intimate look at the interior lives of a group of teenage girls as they come of age in Brooklyn. A potent mix of vivid portraiture and vérité, we follow the girls as they navigate the ephemeral and fleeting transition between childhood and adulthood.
