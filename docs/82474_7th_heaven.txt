Title: 7th Heaven (1927)
Genres: War, Drama, Romance
Director: Frank Borzage
Main Cast: Janet Gaynor, Charles Farrell, Gladys Brockwell, David Butler, Ben Bard
Keywords: optimism, love, sewer, presumed dead, street cleaners

Overview: A dejected Parisian sewer worker feels his prayers have been answered when he falls in love with a street waif.
