Title: ファンタスティポ (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Shogo Yabuuchi
Main Cast: Taichi Kokubun, Tsuyoshi Domoto

Overview: Two brothers must take the helm of their family's company, Armadillo, Inc., after their father steps down from his position as the company's director. Through trials and tribulations, the two slowly begin to learn the true meaning of family.
