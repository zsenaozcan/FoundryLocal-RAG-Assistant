Title: Apartment Troubles (2014)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Jess Weixler
Main Cast: Jess Weixler, Jennifer Prediger, Megan Mullally, Will Forte, Jeffrey Tambor
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Two codependent roommates, on the verge of eviction, flee New York for the promise of sunshine in Los Angeles where their friendship is tested by a chance at fame, a fortune teller and an amorous wealthy aunt.
