Title: Man of the World (1931)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Richard Wallace
Main Cast: William Powell, Carole Lombard, Wynne Gibson, Lawrence Gray, Guy Kibbee
Keywords: paris, marly horse, con trick

Overview: A young American girl visits Paris accompanied by her fiancee and her wealthy uncle. There she meets and is romanced by a worldly novelist; what she doesn't know is that he is a blackmailer who is using her to get to her uncle.
