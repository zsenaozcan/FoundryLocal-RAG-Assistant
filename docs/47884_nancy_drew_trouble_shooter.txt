Title: Nancy Drew... Trouble Shooter (1939)
Genres: Comedy, Mystery
Director: William Clemens
Main Cast: Bonita Granville, Frankie Thomas, John Litel, Aldrich Bowker, Charlotte Wynters
Keywords: lake, uncle, vacation, murder, nancy drew

Overview: Nancy Drew clears her Uncle Matt of murder charges when she exposes the real murderers. She and Ted find themselves in many dangerous positions such as trying to fly an airplane after the pilot parachuted out and being face-to-face with the murderer.
