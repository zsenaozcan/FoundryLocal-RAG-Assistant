Title: Betty Blue (1986)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Jean-Jacques Beineix
Main Cast: Jean-Hugues Anglade, Béatrice Dalle, Gérard Darmon, Consuelo De Haviland, Clémentine Célarié
Keywords: self-destruction, mental breakdown, kiss, self-inflicted injury, borderline personality disorder, femme fatale

Overview: Betty Blue, a mentally unbalanced and sexually aggressive free spirit who becomes involved with Zorg, a repairman moonlighting as a writer. The two engage in a variety of sexual encounters, and grow more passionate toward each other. Betty finds Zorg’s book and is aggressively supportive; over time, her instability begin to catch up with her, leading to a grisly and shocking conclusion.
