Title: Guilty of Romance (2011)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Horror
Director: Sion Sono
Main Cast: Miki Mizuno, Makoto Togashi, Megumi Kagurazaka, Satoshi Nikaido, Ryûju Kobayashi

Overview: A detective probes the brutal murder of a woman in a red light district while a housewife hides her double life as a prostitute from her husband.
