Title: Scum (1979)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Alan Clarke
Main Cast: Ray Winstone, Mick Ford, Julian Firth, John Blundell, Phil Daniels
Keywords: suicide, prison, england, corruption, riot, power, controversy, revenge, racism, violence

Overview: This is the hard and shocking story of life in a British Borstal for young offenders. The brutal regime made no attempt to reform or improve the inmates and actively encouraged a power struggle between the 'tough' new inmate and the 'old hands'. The film was originally made as a BBC play but it was banned before ever being shown.
