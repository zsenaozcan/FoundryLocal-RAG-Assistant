Title: Closed Curtain (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kambuzia Partovi
Main Cast: Jafar Panahi, Hadi Saeedi, Maryam Moqadam, Azadeh Torabi, Abolghasem Sobhani

Overview: A man, his dog, a young woman and a filmmaker in a house by the Caspian Sea. All three are wanted, but they are also in search of each other. Thus begins an absurd game in which reality and fiction merge.
