Title: Darkness (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Eduardo Villanueva Jiménez
Main Cast: Adelelmo Jimenez, Carlota Rodriquez, Alfredo Llamas Herrera, Jesús Sernas
Keywords: rural setting

Overview: In Penumbra, the second feature film by writer, producer and filmmaker Eduardo Villanueva Jiménez, an experienced old hunter shows his world within a rural Mexico lost in time: a place of suspense and mystery where he creates a fascinating and illusory world together with the spirits of the forest.
