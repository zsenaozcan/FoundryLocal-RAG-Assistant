Title: Doctor in the House (1954)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ralph Thomas
Main Cast: Dirk Bogarde, Muriel Pavlow, Kenneth More, Donald Sinden, Kay Kendall
Keywords: student of medicine, student

Overview: The first of the seven "Doctor" films, based on Richard Gordon's novels and released between 1954 and 1970.  Simon Sparrow is a newly arrived medical student at St Swithin's hospital in London. Falling in with three longer-serving hopefuls he is soon immersed in the wooing, imbibing and fast sports-car driving that constitute 1950's medical training. There is, however, always the looming and formidable figure of chief surgeon Sir Lancelot Spratt to remind them of their real purpose.
