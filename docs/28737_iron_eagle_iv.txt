Title: Iron Eagle IV (1995)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Sidney J. Furie
Main Cast: Louis Gossett, Jr., Jason Cadieux, Joanne Vannicola, Max Piersig, Karen Gayle

Overview: While Chappy Sinclair is saddled with a bunch of misfits and delinquents for his flight school, he turns to his protégé Doug Masters to assist him in rounding them into shape for an important competition. During their training, they stumble upon a group of subversive air force officers who are dealing in toxic waste as a sideline.
