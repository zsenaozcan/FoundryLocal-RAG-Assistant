Title: Silent Night, Deadly Night III: Better Watch Out! (1989)
Genres: Horror
Director: Monte Hellman
Main Cast: Richard Beymer, Monte Hellman, Robert Culp, Bill Moseley, Samantha Scully
Keywords: santa claus, sequel, killer santa claus, holiday horror, christmas

Overview: Ricky Caldwell, the notorious 'Killer Santa Claus', awakens from a six-year coma after being kept alive on life-support by a slightly crazed doctor experimenting with ESP and other special abilities. Ricky targets a young, clairvoyant blind woman, named Laura, whom is traveling with her brother Chris, and his girlfriend Jerri to their grandmother's house for Christmas Eve, and Ricky decides to go after her, leaving a trail of dead bodies in his wake.
