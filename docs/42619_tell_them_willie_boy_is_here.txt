Title: Tell Them Willie Boy Is Here (1969)
Genres: Drama, Western
Director: Abraham Polonsky
Main Cast: Robert Redford, Katharine Ross, Robert Blake, Susan Clark, Barry Sullivan
Keywords: california, native american, on the run, posse

Overview: Based on true events that happened in Banning,California, Tell Them Willie Boy Is Here, tells the story of one of the last Western manhunts, in 1909. Willie Boy, a Native American, kills his girlfriend's father in self defense, and the two go on the run, pursued by a search posse led by Sheriff Christopher Cooper.
