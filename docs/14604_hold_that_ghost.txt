Title: Hold That Ghost (1941)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Arthur Lubin
Main Cast: Bud Abbott, Lou Costello, Richard Carlson, Joan Davis, Mischa Auer
Keywords: haunted house, will

Overview: Two bumbling service station attendants are left as the sole beneficiaries in a gangster's will. Their trip to claim their fortune is sidetracked when they are stranded in a haunted house along with several other strangers.
