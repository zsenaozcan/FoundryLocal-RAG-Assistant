Title: The Truth About Bebe Donge (1952)
Genres: Drama
Director: Henri Decoin
Main Cast: Jean Gabin, Gabrielle Dorziat, Claude Génia, Marcel André, Jacques Castelot
Keywords: suspense, film noir

Overview: François Donge, a rich industrialist and womaniser, meets a girl nicknamed Bébé who he marries. Ten years later, poisoned by his wife and dying in hospital, he recalls his married life and understands how his wife who adored him suffered from his many affairs and indifference.
