Title: On My Way (2013)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Emmanuelle Bercot
Main Cast: Catherine Deneuve, Claude Gensac, Dominique Rocheteau, Gérard Garouste, Hafsia Herzi
Keywords: road movie, woman director

Overview: Deneuve plays sassy grandmother Cathy who takes to the road after being betrayed by her lover and learning her business is on the verge of bankruptcy on the same day. During a weeklong odyssey across France, she spends time with a grandson she hardly knows and reconnects with her past as former Miss Brittany through a reunion for former beauty queens.
