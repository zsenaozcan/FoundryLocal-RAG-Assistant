Title: Kiss of the Damned (2012)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Romance
Director: Xan Cassavetes
Main Cast: Joséphine de la Baume, Milo Ventimiglia, Roxane Mesquida, Michael Rapaport, Riley Keough
Keywords: vampire, sibling rivalry, female vampire, woman director

Overview: Beautiful vampire Djuna tries to resist the advances of the handsome, human screenwriter Paolo, but eventually gives in to their passion. When her seductive and highly volatile sister Mimi unexpectedly comes to visit, she threatens Djuna's new relationship, and the whole vampire community becomes endangered.
