Title: Flyin' Ryan (2003)
Genres: Family
Director: Linda Shayne
Main Cast: Andy Weiss, Connor Synder, Geneve Rupert, Chris McGurrin, Damon Schoeffler
Keywords: woman director

Overview: When twelve-year-old Ryan is forced to move to a new town, he soon discovers the kids there are mean and tough. And they really don't like strangers. But when they steal his dog Theo, Ryan is given a strange pair of magical shoes and finds that the sky's the limit.
