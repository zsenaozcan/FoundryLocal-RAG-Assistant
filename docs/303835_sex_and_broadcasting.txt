Title: Sex and Broadcasting (2014)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Tim K. Smith
Main Cast: Matt Groening, Adam Horovitz, Patton Oswalt, Tom Scharpling

Overview: SEX AND BROADCASTING is a feature length documentary about New Jersey's WFMU, the world's strangest and most unique radio station, and one man's attempt to keep it alive in the face of recession, the persistent threat of commercial media, and the challenges that come with keeping a rebellious group of outsiders together.
