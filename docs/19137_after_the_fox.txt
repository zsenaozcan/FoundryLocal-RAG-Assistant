Title: After the Fox (1966)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Vittorio De Sica
Main Cast: Peter Sellers, Victor Mature, Britt Ekland, Martin Balsam, Akim Tamiroff
Keywords: escapee convict, gold bar, film crew

Overview: Criminal mastermind 'The Fox' sets up a phony film production and masquerades as it's director as part of a plan to smuggle a stolen gold shipment into Italy.
