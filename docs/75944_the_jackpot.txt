Title: The Jackpot! (1991)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Claude Zidi
Main Cast: Thierry Lhermitte, Miou-Miou, Eddy Mitchell, Michel Boujenah, Hélène Zidi

Overview: To his family, François Voisin is nothing more than a humdrum civil servant. In truth, for the past twenty years he has been working as a secret agent. All goes well until circumstances put his secret in danger.
