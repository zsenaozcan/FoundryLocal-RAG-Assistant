Title: Il Sorpasso (1962)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Dino Risi
Main Cast: Vittorio Gassman, Catherine Spaak, Jean-Louis Trintignant, Claudio Gora, Luciana Angiolillo
Keywords: drive, summer, seaside

Overview: Roberto, a shy law student in Rome, meets Bruno, a forty-year-old exuberant, capricious man, who takes him for a drive through the Roman and Tuscany countries in the summer.
