Title: Friday (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Evgeniy Shelyakin
Main Cast: Danila Kozlovsky, Sergey Burunov, Evgeniy Stychkin, Pavel Derevyanko, Natalya Bardo

Overview: Friday night is almost like a shortened New year's night. One can expect every kind of surprises on that night. Friday night has its special magic.
