Title: The Cow (1969)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Dariush Mehrjui
Main Cast: Ezzatolah Entezami, Mahin Shahabi, Ali Nassirian, Jamshid Mashayekhi, Firouz Behjat-Mohamadi
Keywords: iranian new wave

Overview: An old villager deeply in love with his cow goes to the capital for a while. While he's there, the cow dies and now the villagers are afraid of his possible reaction to it when he returns.
