Title: The Decent One (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Vanessa Lapa
Main Cast: Tobias Moretti, Sophie Rois, Antonia Moretti, Lotte Ledl, Florentín Groll
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Through previously undiscovered private letters, photos and diaries that were found in the Himmler family house in 1945, the "The Decent One" exposes a unique and at times uncomfortable access to the life and mind of the merciless "Architect of the Final Solution" Heinrich Himmler.
