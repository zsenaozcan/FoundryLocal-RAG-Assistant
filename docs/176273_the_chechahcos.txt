Title: The Chechahcos (1924)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Lewis H. Moomaw
Main Cast: William Dills, Albert Van Antwerp, Eva Gordon

Overview: A fire engulfs a shipload of prospectors and adventurers making their way to Alaska. In the confusion, Mrs. Stanlaw is separated from her young daughter, who ends up in the care of 'Horseshoe' Riley and Bob Dexter.
