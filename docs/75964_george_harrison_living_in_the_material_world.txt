Title: George Harrison: Living in the Material World (2011)
Genres: Music, Documentary
Director: Martin Scorsese
Main Cast: George Harrison, Paul McCartney, John Lennon, Ringo Starr, Olivia Harrison
Keywords: guitar, composer, song, fame, the beatles, electric guitar, ringo starr

Overview: Director Martin Scorsese profiles former Beatle George Harrison in this reverent portrait that mixes interviews and archival footage, featuring commentary from the likes of Paul McCartney, Eric Clapton, Ringo Starr and Yoko Ono.
