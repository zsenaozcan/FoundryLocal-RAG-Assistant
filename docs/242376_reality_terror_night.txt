Title: Reality Terror Night (2013)
Genres: Horror
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Martin Kove, Vincent Rivera, Oliver Rayon, Lucy Treadway, Lucy Treadway

Overview: 5 beautiful girls win a contest to star in a new reality show. Spend a night in a haunted house, and the first one to make contact with a ghost will win. But the house is really haunted by a killer who intends that none of them will win.
