Title: Alice in Wonderland (1966)
Genres: Family, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Jonathan Miller
Main Cast: Anne-Marie Mallik, Freda Dowie, Jo Maxwell Muller, Wilfrid Brambell, Peter Sellers
Keywords: alice in wonderland, rabbit, tea party

Overview: Alice in Wonderland (1966) is a BBC television play based on Alice's Adventures in Wonderland by Lewis Carroll. It was directed by Jonathan Miller, then most widely known for his appearance in the long-running satirical revue Beyond the Fringe.
