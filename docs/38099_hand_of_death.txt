Title: Hand of Death (1976)
Genres: Action
Director: John Woo
Main Cast: Dorian Tan Tao-Liang, Jackie Chan, Sammo Hung, Yuen Biao, James Tien
Keywords: martial arts

Overview: A survivor of an attack on a rebel group opposing the Manchu invasion of China creates the Goose Fist fighting technique and tries for revenge on a traitor.
