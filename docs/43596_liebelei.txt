Title: Liebelei (1933)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Max Ophüls
Main Cast: Paul Hörbiger, Magda Schneider, Luise Ullrich, Gustaf Gründgens, Olga Tschechowa
Keywords: monarchy, duel, soldier, vienna, schnitzler

Overview: Vienna in the beginning of the twentieth century. Cavalry Lieutenant Fritz Lobheimer is about to end his affair with Baroness Eggerdorff when he meets the young Christine, the daughter of an opera violinist. Baron Eggerdorff however soon hears of his past misfortune...
