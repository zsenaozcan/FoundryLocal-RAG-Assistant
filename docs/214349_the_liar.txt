Title: The Liar (1981)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Mika Kaurismäki
Main Cast: Aki Kaurismäki, Pirkko Hämäläinen, Juuso Hirvikangas, Juuso Hirvikangas

Overview: Talkative, hyperactive young drifter Ville Alfa goes around Helsinki, basically trying to borrow money from friends and strangers by means of an incessant delivery of quirky and snappy quasi-intellectual lines and fabricated excuses.
