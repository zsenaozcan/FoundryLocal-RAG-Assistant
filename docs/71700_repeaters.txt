Title: Repeaters (2010)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller, Drama
Director: Carl Bessai
Main Cast: Dustin Milligan, Amanda Crew, Richard de Klerk, Alexia Fast, Anja Savcic
Keywords: recovering drug addict, group of friends

Overview: A gritty mind-bending thriller about three twenty-somethings who find themselves in an impossible time labyrinth, where each day they awaken to the same terrifying day as the preceding one.
