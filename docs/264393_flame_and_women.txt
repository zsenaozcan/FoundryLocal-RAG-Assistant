Title: Flame and Women (1967)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Yoshishige Yoshida
Main Cast: Mariko Okada, Isao Kimura, Mayumi Ogawa, Takeshi Kusaka, Kazuo Kitamura

Overview: Shingo and Ritsuko have a baby: Takashi. They happen to be a happy couple, but soon Ritsuko wants to know who is the true father of Takashi, born by artificial insemination.
