Title: LasseMajas detektivbyrå - Stella Nostra (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Walter Söderlund
Main Cast: Amanda Pajus, Lukas Holgersson, Nassim Al Fakir, Sara Sommerfeld, Tomas Norström
Keywords: sweden, small town, mafia, crime, private detective

Overview: The sleepy little town Valleby is getting ready for the cafe owners Dino Panini's and Sara Bernard's wedding, and our popular young detectives Lasse and Maja stand ready to ensure that nothing will go wrong. But when the wedding couple's respective relatives arrive, they prove to belong to rival mafia families.
