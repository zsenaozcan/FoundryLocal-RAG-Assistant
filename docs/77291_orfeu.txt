Title: Orfeu (1999)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Music, Romance, Foreign
Director: Carlos Diegues
Main Cast: Toni Garrido, Patrícia França, Murilo Benício, Zezé Motta, Milton Gonçalves
Keywords: carnival, violence

Overview: Orfeu is a popular composer from a samba school. He lives in the favela and falls madly in love when he meets Euridice, a newcomer to the neighborhood. But the local drug boss Lucinho stands between them and will drastically change both their lives.
