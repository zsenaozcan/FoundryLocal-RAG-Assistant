Title: Thirst (1949)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ingmar Bergman
Main Cast: Eva Henning, Birger Malmsten, Birgit Tengroth, Hasse Ekman, Mimi Nelson
Keywords: homosexuality, ballet

Overview: A needy couple in a bad marriage travel back to Stockholm after a trip to Italy. Meanwhile, a widow resists seductions from two different persons - her psychiatrist and a lesbian friend.
