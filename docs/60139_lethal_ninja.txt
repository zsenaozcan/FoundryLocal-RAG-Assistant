Title: Lethal Ninja (1993)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Yossi Wein
Main Cast: Ross Kettle, Norman Coombes, David Webb, Krayn Hill, Kimberleigh Stark
Keywords: island, nazis, ninja, american ninja warrior
