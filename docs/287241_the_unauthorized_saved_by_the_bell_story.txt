Title: The Unauthorized Saved by the Bell Story (2014)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Comedy
Director: Jason Lapeyre
Main Cast: Sam Kindseth, Dylan Everett, Alyssa Lynch, Tiera Skovbye, Julian works

Overview: The Unauthorized Saved by the Bell Story delves into the experiences of six unknown young actors placed into the Hollywood spotlight, exposing the challenges of growing up under public scrutiny.
