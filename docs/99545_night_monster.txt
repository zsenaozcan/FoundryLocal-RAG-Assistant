Title: Night Monster (1942)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Family
Director: Ford Beebe
Main Cast: Ralph Morgan, Don Porter, Irene Hervey, Fay Helm, Nils Asther
Keywords: mystic, fog, old dark house

Overview: There's a'doin's transpirin' at the Old Dark Manse of Old Man Ingston where, one-by-one, invited guests are being mysteriously murdered by the monster of the fog-shrouded moors.
