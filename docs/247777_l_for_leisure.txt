Title: L for Leisure (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lev Kalman
Main Cast: Gabriel Abrantes, Melissa Barrera, Bene Coopersmith, Benjamin Crotty, Carolyn Cutillo
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A dreamy, ironic comedy about a generation of hedonistic students in the 1990s. Their favourite activity: talking endlessly. During a series of vacation days filled with sun-drenched naps, squabbling, waterskiing competitions and love-making, the amateur actors discuss their concerns in dry dialogues. For instance, semi-intellectual reflections on the end of time and how Michael Jordan plays basketball.
