Title: Hot Pursuit (1987)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Steven Lisberger
Main Cast: John Cusack, Robert Loggia, Jerry Stiller, Wendy Gazelle, Monte Markham
Keywords: boat, chase, girlfriend, smuggling, rescue

Overview: Young Danny is following his rich girlfriend's family to the Caribbean. But suddenly he simply must make a chemistry test and cannot go with them. After they have left, he gets a leave from his professor and takes a plane to find them. But he is not quite sure where they are, and meets smugglers, crazy captains and murderers.
