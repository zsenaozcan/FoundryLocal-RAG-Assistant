Title: The Scopone Game (1972)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Luigi Comencini
Main Cast: Alberto Sordi, Silvana Mangano, Bette Davis, Joseph Cotten, Mario Carotenuto

Overview: An aging American millionairess journeys to Rome each year with her chauffeur George to play the card game scopone with destitute Peppino and his wife Antonia.
