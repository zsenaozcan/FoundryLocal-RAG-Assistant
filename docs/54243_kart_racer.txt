Title: Kart Racer (2003)
Genres: Action, Drama, Family
Director: Stuart Gillard
Main Cast: Randy Quaid, Will Rothhaar, Jennifer Wigmore, David Gallagher, Amanda de Martinis

Overview: 14-year-old Watts Davies is estranged from his dad, a former International Karting Federation (IKF) champion. Watts's resolve to race in the upcoming IKF Regional Championships rekindles their relationship as they pursue the dream together.
