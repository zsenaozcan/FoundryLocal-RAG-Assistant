Title: The Virgin of Nuremberg (1963)
Genres: War, Horror
Director: Antonio Margheriti
Main Cast: Rossana Podestà, Georges Rivière, Christopher Lee, Jim Dolen, Anny Degli Uberti
Keywords: castle, murder

Overview: Women are being tortured to death with various torture devices in the dungeon of an old castle by a deformed, hooded, holocaust survivor.
