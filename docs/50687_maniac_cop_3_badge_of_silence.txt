Title: Maniac Cop 3: Badge of Silence (1993)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Horror
Director: Joel Soisson
Main Cast: Robert Davi, Robert Z'Dar, Caitlin Dulany, Paul Gleason, Jackie Earle Haley

Overview: A female cop is gunned down and wrongly accused of using excessive force in a hostage rescue attempt. Maniac cop returns from the dead once more to seek revenge, destroying everthing and anyone that stands in his way.
