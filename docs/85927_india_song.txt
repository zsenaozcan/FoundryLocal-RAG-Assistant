Title: India Song (1975)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Marguerite Duras
Main Cast: Delphine Seyrig, Mathieu Carrière, Michael Lonsdale, Claude Mann, Vernon Dobtcheff
Keywords: woman director

Overview: India, 1937. Anne-Marie Stretter is the wife of the French ambassador and leads a solitary yet privileged life in Calcutta. The tedium of her existence is relieved by numerous illicit love affairs with government officials, young men who find her an object of desire and fascination. The Vice Consul is driven insane by his love for her and, expelled from the ambassador’s palace, cries like a sick animal. Life continues for Anne-Marie Stretter, the same tedious existence…
