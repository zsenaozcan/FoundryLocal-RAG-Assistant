Title: Robbery at 3 O'clock (1962)
Genres: Comedy, Foreign
Director: José María Forqué
Main Cast: José Luis López Vázquez, Cassen, Gracita Morales, Katia Loritz, Manuel Alexandre
Keywords: bank robbery, bank employee

Overview: Galindo works in a bank. One day he convinces the other employees in the bank to plan a hold-up in the bank they are working. They prepare everything carefully. However, real robbers come just before the fake ones.
