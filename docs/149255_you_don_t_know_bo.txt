Title: You Don't Know Bo (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Michael Bonfiglio
Main Cast: Bo Jackson, James Andrews, Bomani Jones, Boomer Esiason, Mike Greenberg
Keywords: american football player, baseball player, nfl, mlb

Overview: A close look at two-sport athlete Bo Jackson and the creation of a legend. Even without winning a Super Bowl or World Series, Bo will forever be known as a cultural icon and one of the most famous athletes of all time. This film will examine the truths and tall tales that surround Jackson, and how his seemingly impossible feats captured our collective imagination for an all-too-brief moment in time.
