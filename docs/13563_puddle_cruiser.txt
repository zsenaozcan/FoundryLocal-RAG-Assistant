Title: Puddle Cruiser (1996)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Jay Chandrasekhar
Main Cast: Jay Chandrasekhar, Kevin Heffernan, Steve Lemme, Paul Soter, Erik Stolhanske
Keywords: broken lizard, independent film

Overview: A student falls in love with a fellow student who is defending his buddies in university court. He "proves his love" (more like his masculinity) to her by playing rugby against her ex-boyfriend.
