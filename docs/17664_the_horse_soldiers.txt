Title: The Horse Soldiers (1959)
Genres: War, Western
Director: John Ford
Main Cast: John Wayne, William Holden, Constance Towers, Judson Pratt, Hoot Gibson
Keywords: war hero, cavalry, standoff, confederate

Overview: A Union Cavalry outfit is sent behind confederate lines in strength to destroy a rail supply center. Along with them is sent a doctor who causes instant antipathy between him and the commander. The secret plan for the mission is overheard by a southern belle who must be taken along to assure her silence.
