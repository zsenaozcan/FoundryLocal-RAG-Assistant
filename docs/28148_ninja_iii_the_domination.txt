Title: Ninja III: The Domination (1984)
Genres: Horror, Action
Director: Sam Firstenberg
Main Cast: Sho Kosugi, Lucinda Dickey, Jordan Bennett, David Chung, Dale Ishimoto
Keywords: revenge, ninja, possessed

Overview: A female aerobic instructor is possessed by an evil spirit of a fallen ninja when coming to his aid. The spirit seeks revenge on those who killed him and uses the female instructor's body to carry out his mission. The only way the spirit will leave aerobic instructor's body is with the help of another ninja.
