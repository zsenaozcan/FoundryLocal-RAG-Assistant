Title: The Revenge of Frankenstein (1958)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Terence Fisher
Main Cast: Peter Cushing, Francis Matthews, Eunice Gayson, Michael Gwynn, John Welsh
Keywords: frankenstein, hammer horror

Overview: Baron Frankenstein, working under the protective pseudonym Dr. Victor Stein, together with his assistant Dr. Kleve, transplants a dwarf's brain into another body and unleashes a deranged being.
