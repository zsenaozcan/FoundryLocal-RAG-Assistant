Title: The Blue Max (1966)
Genres: War, Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: John Guillermin
Main Cast: George Peppard, James Mason, Ursula Andress, Jeremy Kemp, Karl Michael Vogler
Keywords: world war i, fighter pilot, biplane, flying ace

Overview: A young pilot in the German air force of 1918, disliked as lower-class and unchivalrous, tries ambitiously to earn the medal offered for 20 kills.
