Title: Kristy (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Olly Blackburn
Main Cast: Ashley Greene, Haley Bennett, Lucas Till, Chris Coy, Mike Seal
Keywords: fight, mask, college, thanksgiving, campus, outcast, gang, fear, target
