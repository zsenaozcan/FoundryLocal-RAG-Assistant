Title: Very Semi-Serious (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Leah Wolchok
Main Cast: Bob Mankoff
Keywords: woman director, new yorker, cartoonist

Overview: The New Yorker is the benchmark for the single-panel cartoon. This light-hearted and sometimes poignant look at the art and humor of the iconic drawings shows why they have inspired and even baffled us for decades. Very Semi-Serious is a window into the minds of cartooning legends and hopefuls, including editor Bob Mankoff, shedding light onto how their humor evolves.
