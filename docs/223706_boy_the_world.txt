Title: Boy & the World (2014)
Genres: Animation, Adventure, Family
Director: Alê Abreu
Main Cast: Vinicius Garcia, Lu Horta, Marco Aurélio Campos, Felipe Zilse, Alê Abreu
Keywords: brazilian, modernity, travel, coming of age

Overview: Suffering because of his father departure to the big city, a boy leaves his village and discovers a fantastic world dominated by bug-engines and strange beings. An unusual animation with various artistic techniques that portrays the issues of the modern world through the eyes of a child.
