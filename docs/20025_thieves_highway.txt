Title: Thieves' Highway (1949)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Jules Dassin
Main Cast: Richard Conte, Valentina Cortese, Lee J. Cobb, Barbara Lawrence, Jack Oakie
Keywords: classic noir, truck driver, produce, truck crash, apple picking, film noir

Overview: War-veteran truck driver "Nick" Garcos (Conte) arrives at home to find his father, a farmer, crippled. He learns that his father was crippled at the hands of an amoral produce dealer in San Francisco, Mike Figlia (Cobb). Garcos vows revenge.
