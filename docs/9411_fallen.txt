Title: Fallen (1998)
Genres: Thriller, Fantasy, Crime, Drama
Director: Gregory Hoblit
Main Cast: Denzel Washington, John Goodman, Donald Sutherland, Embeth Davidtz, James Gandolfini
Keywords: angel, riddle, detective, bible, police, supernatural, psychopath, murder, curse, religion

Overview: Homicide detective John Hobbes witnesses the execution of serial killer Edgar Reese. Soon after the execution the killings start again, and they are very similar to Reese's style.
