Title: Miracle at St. Anna (2008)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Spike Lee
Main Cast: Derek Luke, Michael Ealy, Laz Alonso, John Turturro, Valentina Cervi
Keywords: black people, world war ii, toscana, village, soldier, partisan

Overview: Miracle at St. Anna chronicles the story of four American soldiers who are members of the all-black 92nd "Buffalo Soldier" Division stationed in Tuscany, Italy during World War II.
