Title: Eden of the East Movie II: Paradise Lost (2010)
Genres: Animation, Mystery
Director: Kenji Kamiyama
Main Cast: Ryohei Kimura, Sakiko Tamagawa, Takuya Eguchi, Motoyuki Kawahara, Nobuyuki Hiyama

Overview: Accused of terrorism and feeling the heat, Saki's high-tech crew is in the crosshairs. Meanwhile, the shroud of mystery covering Takizawa is lifted as he and his fellow Selecao make their final moves. Every game must come to an end. For those playing Mr. Outside's twisted game of conspiracy thrills, the end comes now.
