Title: The Education of Mohammad Hussein (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Heidi Ewing
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: THE EDUCATION OF MOHAMMAD HUSSEIN is an intimate look at how the largest Muslim community in America responds to the provocations of an anti-Islamic preacher. Through the eyes of children, the film examines what it is like to come of age as a Muslim in the United States ten years post 9/11.
