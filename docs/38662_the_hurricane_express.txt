Title: The Hurricane Express (1932)
Genres: Adventure
Director: J.P. McGowan
Main Cast: John Wayne, Tully Marshall, Conway Tearle, Shirley Grey, Edmund Breese
Keywords: movie serial

Overview: The Wrecker wrecks trains on the L & R Railroad. One of his victims is Larry Baker's father. Baker wants to find the evildoer, among a host of suspects, but it will be difficult since the Wrecker can disguise himself to look like almost anyone
