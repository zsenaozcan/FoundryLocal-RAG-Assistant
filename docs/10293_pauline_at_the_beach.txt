Title: Pauline at the Beach (1983)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Éric Rohmer
Main Cast: Amanda Langlet, Arielle Dombasle, Pascal Greggory, Féodor Atkine, Simon de La Brosse
Keywords: holiday, sea, beach, flirt, love of one's life, girlfriend, divorce, marriage problems

Overview: Fifteen year old Pauline and her older cousin, model-shaped Marion, go to the emtying Atlantic coast for an autumn holiday . Marion ignores the approaches of a surfer and falls for Henri, a hedonist who is only interested in a sexual adventure and drops her soon. Pauline's little romance with a young man (Sylvain) is also spoiled by Henri.
