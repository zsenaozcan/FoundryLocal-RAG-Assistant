Title: Battle of Britain (1969)
Genres: Action, Drama, History, War
Director: Guy Hamilton
Main Cast: Harry Andrews, Michael Caine, Trevor Howard, Curd Jürgens, Ian McShane
Keywords: world war ii, nazis, winston churchill, aviation, royal air force, air battle, luftwaffe

Overview: In 1940, the Royal Air Force fights a desperate battle against the might of the Luftwaffe for control of the skies over Britain, thus preventing the Nazi invasion of Britain.
