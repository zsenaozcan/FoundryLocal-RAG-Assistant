Title: Smash Cut (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Lee Demarbre
Main Cast: Sasha Grey, Jesse Buck, David Hess, Michael Berryman, Ray Sager

Overview: Television news celebrity April Carson turns to the services of private investigator Isaac Beaumonde to seek her missing sister, a stripper known as Gigi Spot. Carson assumes a role in a horror movie in the process, eventually learning that the movie's director, Able Whitman, is not only the culprit, but that he has rendered her sister's body into props for the production.
