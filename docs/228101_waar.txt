Title: Waar (2013)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Bilal Lashari
Main Cast: Shaan Shahid, Ayesha Khan, Hamza Ali Abbasi, Shamoon Abbasi, Meesha Shafi
Keywords: foreign

Overview: The efforts of the Pakistani security forces in their fight against terrorism and how the lives of security officials are affected. A retired security officer returns to save Pakistan from a major terrorist attack.
