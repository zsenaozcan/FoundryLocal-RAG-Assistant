Title: Internet Story (2010)
Genres: Animation, Drama
Director: Adam Butcher
Main Cast: Shaun French, Duncan Wigman
Keywords: urban legend, mystery, internet

Overview: A fast-paced and thought-provoking film told through fragments of internet videos, animations, blogs and news articles. A series of shocking events unfolds when a young man creates a public treasure hunt for his own amusement and a video blogger decides to pursue the riddles across country.
