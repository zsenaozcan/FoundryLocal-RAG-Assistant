Title: XXY (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lucía Puenzo
Main Cast: Ricardo Darín, Valeria Bertuccelli, Carolina Pelleritti, Germán Palacios, Martín Piroyansky
Keywords: transsexuality, intersexuality, youth, woman director

Overview: Alex, an intersexed 15-year-old, is living as a girl, but she and her family begin to wonder whether she's emotionally a boy when another teenager's sexual advances bring the issue to a head. As Alex faces a final decision regarding her gender, she meets both hostility and compassion.
