Title: Suburban Gothic (2014)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Richard Bates Jr.
Main Cast: Matthew Gray Gubler, Ray Wise, Kat Dennings, Sally Kirkland, Jeffrey Combs
Keywords: supernatural, evil spirit, spirit, paranormal activity

Overview: An awkward, unemployed man who can talk to the dead teams up with a rebellious bartender to find the vengeful ghost that's been terrorizing their town.
