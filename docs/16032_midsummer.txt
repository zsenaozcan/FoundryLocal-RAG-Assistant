Title: Midsummer (2003)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Carsten Myllerup
Main Cast: Kristian Leth, Laura Christensen, Jon Lange, Nicolai Jandorf Klok, Julie R. Ølgaard
Keywords: suicide, denmark, secret, nightmare, cabin, vacation, party, ouija board, car accident, guilt

Overview: It is the last day of school for Christian and his younger sister Sophie. They are heading to a party at his friend Trina. High school graduation is just around the corner and after the freedom and future. But behind the idyllic facade lurks tragedy and secrets. That evening Sophie commits suicide.
