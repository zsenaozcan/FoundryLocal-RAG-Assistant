Title: Roma (1972)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Federico Fellini
Main Cast: Peter Gonzales Falcon, Fiona Florence, Pia De Doses, Marne Maitland, Renato Giovannoli
Keywords: rome, subway, theatre milieu, illegal prostitution, child

Overview: A virtually plotless, gaudy, impressionistic portrait of Rome through the eyes of one of its most famous citizens.
