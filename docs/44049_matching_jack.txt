Title: Matching Jack (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nadia Tass
Main Cast: Jacinda Barrett, Tom Russell, James Nesbitt, Richard Roxburgh, Kodi Smit-McPhee
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A woman struggles with her son's illness and her husband's infidelity, but, after a chance encounter with an Irish sailor and his son, her life is turned upside down in a love story that defies explanation and breaks all the rules.
