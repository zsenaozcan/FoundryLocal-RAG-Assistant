Title: Amsterdam Heavy (2011)
Genres: Action
Director: Michael Wright
Main Cast: Michael Madsen, Alison Carroll, Vincent van Ommen, Amy Huckabay, Fajah Lourens

Overview: CIA Agent Martin Keele (Michael Madsen - Reservoir Dogs Kill Bill) sets the wheels spinning in this gritty urban action thriller, as mysterious gangster J.D. goes on the rampage in Amsterdam, stopping at nothing to uncover those who have betrayed him
