Title: It's Only Money (1962)
Genres: Comedy, Thriller
Director: Frank Tashlin
Main Cast: Jerry Lewis, Jack Weston, Mae Questel, Jesse White

Overview: Lester is a clumsy and awkward TV repair man who is nevertheless gifted technically. In helping out a friend, he is drawn into a mystery involving a missing heir in a rich family. He begins to notice little things, like how much those family portraits look like him. Surely..no..he can't be...can he ?
