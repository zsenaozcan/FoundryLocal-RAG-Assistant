Title: Berkshire County (2014)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Audrey Cummings
Main Cast: Alysa King, Madison Ferguson, Christophe Gallander, Samora Smallwood, Bart Rochon
Keywords: babysitter, halloween, high school, horror, home invasion, horror movie, pig mask, woman director

Overview: Kylie Winters, a bullied and self-loathing teen, reluctantly agrees to babysit at an isolated country mansion on Halloween night. When a small boy in a pig mask appears at the door trick-or-treating, Kylie's night transforms into a horrifying and violent cat-and-mouse game. She must go beyond what she ever thought possible if she and the children are to survive the night.
