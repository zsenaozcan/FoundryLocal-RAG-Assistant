Title: Mezzo Forte (2000)
Genres: Animation, Action, Thriller
Director: Yasuomi Umetsu
Main Cast: Tomoko Kotani, Takumi Yamazaki, Taichirō Hirokawa, Shozo Iizuka
Keywords: female nudity, sex, killing, baseball, nudity, female killer, murder, gore, teenage girl, cruelty

Overview: Double the Girls, Double the Guns!! For Mikura, danger is routine and killing is just like breathing. Sleek and sexy, she was born with a gun in each hand and a smile on her face. She specializes in taking risks, but things get a little too hot when her company takes a kidnapping job. The target is a wealthy baseball team owner named Momokichi, but he turns out to be a powerful underworld boss. Mikura and her team are beset by countless heavily armed bodyguards and Momokichi's vicious daughter, Momomi. She's used to getting her way, and with world-class marksmanship skills, she may be the perfect match for Mikura!
