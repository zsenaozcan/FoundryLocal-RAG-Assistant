Title: Harem (1985)
Genres: History, Drama, Romance
Director: Arthur Joffé
Main Cast: Nastassja Kinski, Ben Kingsley, Zohra Sehgal, Dennis Goldson, Michel Robin

Overview: Diane is a sophisticated trainee on the New York Stock Exchange who is suddenly kidnapped and held captive in a North African desert hideaway by Selim, an Arab mogul.
