Title: Animal Love (1996)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ulrich Seidl
Main Cast: Franz Dolesch, Renée Felden, Franz Holzschuh, Peter Kristek, Fritzi Schmied
Keywords: pet, interview, love, dog, animal

Overview: In conurbations where hundreds of thousands live alongside one another, in the era of a highly technological society, in which communication has never played such a significant role, man has become lonely. Disappointed by his fellow human beings, he turns to animals. Dogs and other domestic animals serve him as companions, life partners, cuddly objects and bedfellows.
