Title: Sleepless (2001)
Genres: Crime, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Dario Argento
Main Cast: Max von Sydow, Stefano Dionisi, Chiara Caselli, Gabriele Lavia, Rossella Falk
Keywords: giallo, police detective, psycho killer

Overview: An elderly and retired police detective and a young amateur sleuth team up to find a serial killer whom has resumed a killing spree in Turin, Italy after a 17-year hiatus.
