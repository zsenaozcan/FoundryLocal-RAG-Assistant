Title: Sissi (1955)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Ernst Marischka
Main Cast: Romy Schneider, Karlheinz Böhm, Magda Schneider, Uta Franz, Gustav Knuth
Keywords: love at first sight, sister sister relationship, wife husband relationship, marriage proposal, attachment to nature, love of one's life, anonymity, bavaria, emperor, mistake in person

Overview: The young Bavarian princess Elisabeth, who all call Sissi, goes with her mother and older sister Néné to Austria where Néné will be wed to an emperor named Franz Joseph, Yet unexpectedly Franz runs into Sissi while out fishing and they fall in love.
