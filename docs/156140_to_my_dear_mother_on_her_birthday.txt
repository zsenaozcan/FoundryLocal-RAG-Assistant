Title: To My Dear Mother on Her Birthday (1974)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Luciano Salce
Main Cast: Paolo Villaggio, Lila Kedrova, Eleonora Giorgi, Antonino Faà di Bruno, Renato Chiantoni

Overview: A funny story of count Alfredo aka Didino (little Alfred) and countess Mafalda - his bloodsucking and over-controlling mother.
