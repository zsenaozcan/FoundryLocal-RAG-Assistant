Title: D.C. Cab (1983)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Crime
Director: Joel Schumacher
Main Cast: Max Gail, Adam Baldwin, Mr. T, Gary Busey, Gloria Gifford
Keywords: yellow cab

Overview: The tale of a hapless group of cabbies and a rundown cab company owned by Harold. Albert comes to town with a dream of starting his own cab company but needs to motivate Harold's employees to want to make something out of themselves. It is only when Albert is kidnapped that the cabbies must decide whether or not they are loyal to Albert and his cause.
