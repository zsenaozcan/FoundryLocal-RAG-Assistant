Title: Casanova's Big Night (1954)
Genres: Comedy, Adventure
Director: Norman Z. McLeod
Main Cast: Bob Hope, Joan Fontaine, Audrey Dalton, Basil Rathbone, Hugh Marlowe
Keywords: italy, widow, musical, casanova, tailor, man dressed as woman

Overview: Italy 1757, Pippo Popolino, a lowly tailor, disguises himself as the great Casanova in order to romance the attractive widow Francesca. He little suspects what awaits him... Locked into the incongruous role by the desperation of the real Casanova's creditors, Pippo must journey to Venice on a delicate mission far beyond his capabilities.
