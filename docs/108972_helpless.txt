Title: Helpless (2012)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Byun Young-Joo
Main Cast: Kim Min-hee, Lee Sun-kyun, Cho Seong-ha, Song Ha-yoon, Choi Duk-moon
Keywords: secret identity, missing person, woman director

Overview: A woman suddenly disappears. Her fiance then sets out to find her and, in the process, uncovers layers of dark hidden secrets.
