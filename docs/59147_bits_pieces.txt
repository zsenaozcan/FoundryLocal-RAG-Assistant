Title: Bits & Pieces (1996)
Genres: Drama
Director: Antonello Grimaldi
Main Cast: Asia Argento, Luca Barbareschi, Margherita Buy, Roberto Citran, Enrico Lo Verso

Overview: Dive into The Eternal City - see Rome like you’ve never seen it before. Storefront robberies, bizarre murders, career dreamers, and cameos from Italy’s greatest directors and actors feature in this star-studded omnibus tale about life and love.
