Title: Human Resources (1999)
Genres: Drama
Director: Laurent Cantet
Main Cast: Jalil Lespert, Jean-Claude Vallod, Didier Emile-Woldemard, Chantal Barré, Véronique de Pandelaère

Overview: Set in Limoges, the movie tells the story of "good son" Franck (Jalil Lespert), who returns to his hometown to do a trainee managerial internship in the Human Resources department of the factory where his anxious, taciturn father has worked for 23 years.
