Title: Neverwas (2005)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Thriller
Director: Joshua Michael Stern
Main Cast: Ian McKellen, Aaron Eckhart, Brittany Murphy, Nick Nolte, Jessica Lange
Keywords: secret, book, psychiatrist, novelist, schizophrenic

Overview: Zach Riley (Aaron Eckhart) is a psychiatrist, who leaves a job at a prestigious university, to take up a job at the privately run mental institution, 'Millwood'. What he doesnt reveal at the time of his appointment is that this was the very place where his novelist father, T.L. Pierson (Nick Nolte), spent many years of his life.
