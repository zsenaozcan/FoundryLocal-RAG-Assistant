Title: Firewall (2006)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Richard Loncraine
Main Cast: Harrison Ford, Paul Bettany, Virginia Madsen, Carly Schroeder, Jimmy Bennett
Keywords: bank, technology, blackmail, hacker, seattle, computer hacker, firewall
