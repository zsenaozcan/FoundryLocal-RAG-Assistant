Title: When Trumpets Fade (1998)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: John Irvin
Main Cast: Ron Eldard, Zak Orth, Frank Whaley, Dylan Bruno, Devon Gummersall
Keywords: france, europe, world war ii, u.s. army, nazi germany, infantry

Overview: In WWII Western Germany, Private David Manning reluctantly leaves behind a mortally wounded fellow soldier and searches for survivors from his platoon, only to learn from commanding officer Captain Pritchett that they have all been killed in action. Despite requesting a discharge on the grounds of mental disability, Manning is promoted to sergeant and assigned to lead a new platoon of young inductees.
