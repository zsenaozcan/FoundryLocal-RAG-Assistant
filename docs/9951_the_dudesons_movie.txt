Title: The Dudesons Movie (2006)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Jarno Laasala
Main Cast: Jukka Hildén, Jarppi Leppälä, Hannu-Pekka Parviainen, Jarno Laasala, Bam Margera
Keywords: polar zone, stuntman, best friend, finland

Overview: "The Dudesons Movie" is an extreme stunts comedy that features the wild antics of four hilarious, lifelong friends and their crazy lives in the Arctic Circle.
