Title: E=mc² (2002)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Olaf Lubaszenko
Main Cast: Cezary Pazura, Olaf Lubaszenko, Agnieszka Włodarczyk, Renata Dancewicz, Jan Nowicki

Overview: The film tells about mainly three people - Max, a bored, smart, poor University Professor of Philosophy; Ramzes, a mafia leader, cool, strong, merciless bully and Stella, Ramzes girlfriend.
