Title: Friend (1988)
Genres: Drama
Director: Leonid Kvinikhidze
Main Cast: Sergey Shakurov, Vasiliy Livanov, Anatoli Romashin, Yelena Solovey, Igor Yasulovich

Overview: Looking for the money for a drink, an alcoholic gets a Newfoundland dog as a present from a stranger at a bird fair. He'll soon find out that the animal is not only able to speak, but to get him to give up drinking as well. Now he's determined to get rid of the dog, but that won't be an easy task.
