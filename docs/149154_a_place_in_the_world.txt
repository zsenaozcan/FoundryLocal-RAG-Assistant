Title: A Place in the World (2001)
Genres: Drama
Director: Artur Aristakisyan
Main Cast: Anna Chernova, Vitali Khayev, Nonna Grishayeva, Inna Verbitskaya

Overview: The film describes the life of homeless people who form a community in the center of modern-day Moscow. It relates the story of six couples who love each other and try to remain together despite the poverty and despair of their existence.
