Title: The Lesser Blessed (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Anita Doron
Main Cast: Joel Evans, Kiowa Gordon, Chloe Rose, Benjamin Bratt, Tamara Podemski
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Larry, a 16-year-old Tlicho Indian, lives in the small northern town of Fort Simmer. He loves heavy metal music and has a crush on his classmate, the high school's hottie, Juliet Hope. Larry's past holds a variety of terrors—his father is abusive and he once had an accident that nearly killed him. When Johnny Beck , a young Métis from Hay River, moves to town, things heat up.
