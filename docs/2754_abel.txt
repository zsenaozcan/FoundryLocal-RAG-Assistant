Title: Abel (1986)
Genres: Comedy, Foreign
Director: Alex van Warmerdam
Main Cast: Alex van Warmerdam, Henri Garcin, Olga Zuiderhoek, Annet Malherbe, Loes Luca
Keywords: mama's boy, cowardliness

Overview: An extremely weird comedy about the life of 31- year-old Abel, who has never left home. After failing with doctors and psychiatrists, Abel's father Victor brings home Christine, a friend, in an attempt to teach Abel basic social skills. After that trouble starts and Abel is thrown out into the street.
