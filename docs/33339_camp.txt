Title: Camp (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Todd Graff
Main Cast: Daniel Letterle, Joanna Chilcoat, Robin de Jesus, Tiffany Taylor, Alana Allen
Keywords: gay, summer camp, independent film, coming of age, lgbt child, kids

Overview: Misfits in their lives back home, a group of young people live it up at musical-theater camp. While the sports counselor is completely ignored, the kids' spend all their time in rehearsal for a grueling schedule that involves a new show every two weeks. Several personal stories come to the fore.
