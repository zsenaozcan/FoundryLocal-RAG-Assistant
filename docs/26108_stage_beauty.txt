Title: Stage Beauty (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Richard Eyre
Main Cast: Claire Danes, Billy Crudup, Derek Hutchinson, Mark Letheren, Tom Wilkinson

Overview: Humble Maria, who outfits top London theater star Ned Kynaston, takes none of the credit for the male actor's success at playing women. And because this is the 17th century, Maria, like other females, is prohibited from pursuing her dream of acting. But when powerful people support her, King Charles II lifts the ban on female stage performers. And just as Maria aided Ned, she needs his help to learn her new profession.
