Title: Ten Zan - Ultimate Mission (1988)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Drama
Director: Ferdinando Baldi
Main Cast: Frank Zagarino, Mark Gregory, Romano Kristoff, Jinny Rockers, Sabrina Siani
Keywords: terrorist, asia, mercenary, human experimentation, agent, child kidnapping

Overview: The story in short: Some kind of evil people, have found out a way to extract bodily fluids from the base of the brain of unsuspecting victims. This fluid is then used for some reason or another. A couple of western heroes have to stop this in true 80's action style, big explosions and violence ensue.
