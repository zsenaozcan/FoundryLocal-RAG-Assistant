Title: A Frozen Flower (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yoo Ha
Main Cast: Jo In-sung, Joo Jin-Mo, Song Ji-hyo, Shim Ji-ho, Seo Young-Joo
Keywords: adultery, sword fight, romance, betrayal, tragedy

Overview: A historical drama set in the Koryo dynasty and focused on the relationship between a king and his bodyguard.  Under the dominance of Yuan Dynasty China, the king of Goryeo Dynasty Korea is pressured to produce a successor to the throne. Unable to make love to his queen because he is in love with his bodyguard, Hong Lim, the king asks the two of them to sleep together instead. This sexual contact formulates a forbidden love between Hong Lim and the Queen.
