Title: The Pentagon Wars (1998)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, War
Director: Richard Benjamin
Main Cast: Kelsey Grammer, Cary Elwes, Viola Davis, John C. McGinley, Clifton Powell
Keywords: pentagon, gulf war, vietnam war, u.s. army, army, based on true story, dark comedy, tank, bradley fighting vehicle, u.s. department of defense

Overview: From the director of “Made In America” and “The Money Pit” comes a hilarious look at one of the most expensive blunders in military history. Over 17 years and almost as many billion dollars have gone into devising the BFV (Bradley Fighting Vehicle). There's only one problem. . . it doesn't work.
