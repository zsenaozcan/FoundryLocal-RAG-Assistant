Title: Ulysses (1967)
Genres: Drama
Director: Joseph Strick
Main Cast: Barbara Jefford, Milo O'Shea, Maurice Roeves, T. P. McKenna, Martin Dempsey

Overview: Dublin; June 16, 1904. Stephen Dedalus, who fancies himself as a poet, embarks on a day of wandering about the city during which he finds friendship and a father figure in Leopold Bloom, a middle-aged Jew. Meanwhile, Bloom's day, illuminated by a funeral and an evening of drinking and revelry that stirs paternal feelings toward Stephen, ends with a rapprochement with Molly, his earthy wife.
