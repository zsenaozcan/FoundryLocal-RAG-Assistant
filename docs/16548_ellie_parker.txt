Title: Ellie Parker (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Scott Coffey
Main Cast: Naomi Watts, Scott Coffey, Jennifer Syme, Mark Pellegrino, Greg Freitas
Keywords: alcohol, sex, infidelity, nightclub, nudity, cinematographer, satire, liar, sociopath, independent film

Overview: An aspiring actress from Australia, Ellie Parker (Naomi Watts), lives a hectic Hollywood lifestyle, perpetually trying to land the role that will elevate her career. Living with her lothario musician boyfriend, Justin (Mark Pelligrino), Ellie is far from happy, finding support primarily from her friend Sam (Rebecca Rigg). When Ellie meets Chris (Scott Coffey) after a minor traffic accident, she sees new potential for both romance and her life in general.
