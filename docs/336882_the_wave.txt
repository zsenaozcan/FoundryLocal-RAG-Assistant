Title: The Wave (2015)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Roar Uthaug
Main Cast: Kristoffer Joner, Thomas Bo Larsen, Ane Dahl Torp, Fridtjov Såheim, Laila Goody
Keywords: hotel, norway, fjord, tsunami, disaster, underwater, family in peril, avalanche, new job, disaster movie

Overview: Based on the fact that mountain party Åkneset, located in the Geiranger fjord in Norway, one day will race out and create a violent tsunami of over 80 meters that will crush everything in its path before it hits land in Geiranger. A geologist gets caught in the middle of it and a race against time begins.
