Title: Kept and Dreamless (2005)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Vera Fogwill
Main Cast: Vera Fogwill, Lucia Snieg, Mirta Busnelli, Edda Díaz
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Florence, a young irresponsible mother and her precocious daughter, Eugenia, have an unusual relationship. The roles between the two are reversed. Florence is totally inept at caring for a daughter, whereas Eugenia, only 10 years old, shows maturity and responsibility far beyond what her age might lead you to suppose.
