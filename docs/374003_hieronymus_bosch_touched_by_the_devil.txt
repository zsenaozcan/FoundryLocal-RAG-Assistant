Title: Hieronymus Bosch: Touched by the Devil (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Pieter van Huystee
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: In 2016, the Noordbrabants Museum in the Dutch city of Den Bosch held a special exhibition devoted to the work of Hieronymus Bosch, who died 500 years ago. This late-medieval artist lived his entire life in the city, causing uproar with his fantastical and utterly unique paintings in which hell and the devil always played a prominent role.
