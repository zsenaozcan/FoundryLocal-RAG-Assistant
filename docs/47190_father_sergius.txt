Title: Father Sergius (1918)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Alexandre Volkoff
Main Cast: Iwan Mosschuchin, Olga Kondorova, V. Dzheneyeva, Vladimir Gajdarov, Nikolai Panov

Overview: The story of Prince Stepán Kasátsky discovering his fiancée was the mistress of the Czar, so he then becomes a monk.
