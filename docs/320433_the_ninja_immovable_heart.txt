Title: The Ninja Immovable Heart (2014)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Rob Baard
Main Cast: Rob Baard, Roger Neave, Danny Glover, Cassandra Gava
Keywords: ninja

Overview: Reese, an elite member of a covert unit of Ninja Warriors, is captured. Being tortured for information, Reese recalls the lessons learned from his training in order to fight his way free and stop an evil conglomerate from arming a dangerous weapon.
