Title: Happy Valley (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Amir Bar-Lev
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: american football, child molester

Overview: The children of "Happy Valley" were victimized for years, by a key member of the legendary Penn State college football program. But were Jerry Sandusky’s crimes an open secret? With rare access, director Amir Bar-Lev delves beneath the headlines to tell a modern American parable of guilt, redemption, and identity.
