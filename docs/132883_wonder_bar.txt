Title: Wonder Bar (1934)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Music, Romance
Director: Lloyd Bacon
Main Cast: Al Jolson, Kay Francis, Dolores del Río, Ricardo Cortez, Dick Powell
Keywords: death, pre-code, black face

Overview: Harry and Inez are a dance team at the Wonder Bar. Inez loves Harry, but he is in love with Liane, the wife of a wealthy business man. Al Wonder and the conductor/singer Tommy are in love with Inez. When Inez finds out that Harry wants to leave Paris and is going to the USA with Liane, she kills him.
