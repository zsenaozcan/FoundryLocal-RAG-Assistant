Title: The Story of Temple Drake (1933)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Stephen Roberts
Main Cast: Miriam  Hopkins, William Gargan, Jack La Rue, Florence Eldridge, Guy Standing
Keywords: bootlegger, southern belle

Overview: A wealthy but neurotic Southern belle finds herself trapped in the hideout of a gang of vicious bootleggers. The gang's leader lusts after her, and is determined not to let anything stand in the way of his having her.
