Title: The Secret Garden (1949)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Fred M. Wilcox
Main Cast: Margaret O'Brien, Dean Stockwell, Herbert Marshall, Gladys Cooper, Elsa Lanchester
Keywords: secret, garden

Overview: When Cholera takes the parents of Mary Lennox, she is shipped from India to England to live with her Uncle Craven. Mary changes the lives of those she encounters at her Uncle's remote estate.
