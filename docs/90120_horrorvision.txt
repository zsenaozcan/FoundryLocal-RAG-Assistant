Title: Horrorvision (2001)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Danny Draven
Main Cast: Len Cordova, Maggie Rose Fleck, Josh Covitt, James Black, Brinke Stevens

Overview: The website "horrorvision.com" has a mysterious secret...anyone who logs onto it winds up dead. After Dez, a web programmer, logs in his girlfriend and others are attacked. Only Dez and a mysterious man named Bradbury can stop the ominous forces intent on ruling the cyber-world.
