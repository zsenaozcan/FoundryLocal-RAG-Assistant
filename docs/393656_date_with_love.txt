Title: Date with Love (2016)
Genres: Comedy, TV Movie, Romance
Director: Ron Oliver
Main Cast: Bailee Madison, Shenae Grimes, Andrew W. Walker, Quinn Lord, Andrea Brooks
Keywords: prom, teen comedy

Overview: When a boy’s video to a female star goes viral, it scores him a date with his celebrity crush to the prom. Complications arise when the famous starlet falls for the boy’s small-town teacher.
