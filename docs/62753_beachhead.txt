Title: Beachhead (1954)
Genres: War, Action, Drama
Director: Stuart Heisler
Main Cast: Tony Curtis, Frank Lovejoy, Mary Murphy, Eduard Franz, Skip Homeier
Keywords: world war ii

Overview: On a Japanese-occupied island during World War II, only two soldiers remain alive after a mission attempt goes horribly wrong. Trapped on the island, they must escort a scientist and his daughter to the other side of the island where their ship awaits. They must battle nature, hard terrain, and advancing Japanese troops.
