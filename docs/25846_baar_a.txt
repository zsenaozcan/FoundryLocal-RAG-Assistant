Title: Baarìa (2009)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Giuseppe Tornatore
Main Cast: Francesco Scianna, Margareth Madè, Lina Sastri, Ángela Molina, Nicole Grimaudo
Keywords: sicily, fascism, mafia, communism

Overview: Giuseppe Tornatore traces three generations of a Sicilian family in in the Sicilian town of Bagheria (known as Baarìa in the local Sicilian dialect), from the 1930s to the 1980s, to tell the story of the loves, dreams and delusions of an unusual community.
