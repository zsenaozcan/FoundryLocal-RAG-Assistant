Title: Trailer Park Boys: Don't Legalize It (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mike Clattenburg
Main Cast: John Paul Tremblay, Robb Wells, Mike Smith, John Dunsworth, Jonathan Torrens

Overview: Trailer Park Boys: Don't Legalize It is the third film in the Trailer Park Boys franchise, and a sequel to Trailer Park Boys: Countdown to Liquor Day (2009). In the film, Ricky (Robb Wells), Julian (John Paul Tremblay) and Bubbles (Mike Smith) attempt a series of get-rich-quick schemes after being released from prison, but are again pursued by former Sunnyvale Trailer Park supervisor Jim Lahey (John Dunsworth).
