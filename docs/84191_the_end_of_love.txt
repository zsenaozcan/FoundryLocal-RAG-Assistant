Title: The End of Love (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mark Webber
Main Cast: Mark Webber, Isaac Love, Shannyn Sossamon, Amanda Seyfried, Jason Ritter
Keywords: father son relationship, single parent, single father, toddler

Overview: Mark is a struggling actor stuck between the life he once knew and the one waiting for him. When the mother of his two-year-old son suddenly passes away, Mark is forced to confront his shortcomings. Their fates, now intertwined, hang in the balance as Mark grapples with his ability to grow up. When he meets Lydia, a young mother, he is no longer able to live in the comfort of denial.
