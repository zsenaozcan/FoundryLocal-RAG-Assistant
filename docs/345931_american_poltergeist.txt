Title: American Poltergeist (2015)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Mike Rutkowski
Main Cast: Simona Fusco, Donna Spangler, Ashley Green Elizabeth, Nikole Howell, Arthur Richardson
Keywords: haunting

Overview: A group of friends move into a vacation home with a sinister past in Fall River, Massachusetts. Suspicious of the home's owner, Taryn feels a strong connection to the house and finds herself at the center of one of the most deadly poltergeists in American history.
