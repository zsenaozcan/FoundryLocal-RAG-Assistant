Title: Shahid (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hansal Mehta
Main Cast: Rajkummar Rao, Kay Kay Menon, Prabhleen Sandhu, Baljinder Kaur, Tigmanshu Dhulia
Keywords: biography

Overview: This compelling drama tells the remarkable true story of slain human rights activist Shahid Azmi, who became a powerful voice against the intercommunal violence that has engulfed Mumbai since the early 1990s.
