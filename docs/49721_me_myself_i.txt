Title: Me Myself I (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Pip Karmel
Main Cast: Rachel Griffiths, David Roberts, Sandy Winton, Yael Stone, Shaun Loseby

Overview: Pamela Drury is unhappy, and alone. On her birthday she stumbles across a photo of Robert Dickson, and wonders what would've happened had she said yes to his proposal. A freak accident causes Pamela to live out the life she could've had, but is the grass on the other side always greener?
