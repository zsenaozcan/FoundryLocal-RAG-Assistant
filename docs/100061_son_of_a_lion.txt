Title: Son of a Lion (2007)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Benjamin Gilmour
Main Cast: Niaz Khan Shinwari, Sher Alam Miskeen Ustad

Overview: In the Pakistani tribal village of Darra Adam Khel, a young Pashtun boy Niaz Afridi defies the expectations of his father to continue in the family business making weapons and calls for an education.
