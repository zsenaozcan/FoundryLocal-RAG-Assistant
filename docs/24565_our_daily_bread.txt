Title: Our Daily Bread (2006)
Genres: Documentary, Foreign
Director: Nikolaus Geyrhalter
Main Cast: Claus Hansen Petz, Arkadiusz Rydellek, Tibor Korom, András Szarvas

Overview: Welcome to the world of industrial food production and high-tech farming! To the rhythm of conveyor belts and immense machines, the film looks without commenting into the places where food is produced in Europe: monumental spaces, surreal landscapes and bizarre sounds - a cool, industrial environment which leaves little space for individualism.
