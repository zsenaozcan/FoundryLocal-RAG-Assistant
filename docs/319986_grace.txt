Title: Grace (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Heath Jones
Main Cast: Annika Marks, Sharon Lawrence, Chase Mowen, Cindy Joy Goggins, Liam Springthorpe
Keywords: addiction, drunkenness

Overview: Gracie knows hangovers. She's intimately acquainted with them. But this one? Why did she wake up, half-dressed, on a Florida beach, 1100 miles from home? And this time her father - who also knows about the tragedy of addiction from his struggles with Gracie's mom - isn't going to clean things up.
