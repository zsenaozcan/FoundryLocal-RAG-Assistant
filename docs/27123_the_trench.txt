Title: The Trench (1999)
Genres: War, Action, Drama
Director: William Boyd
Main Cast: Daniel Craig, Danny Dyer, James D'Arcy, Paul Nicholls, Julian Rhind-Tutt
Keywords: world war i, trauma, bravery, friendship, battlefield

Overview: The Trench tells the story of a group of young British soldiers on the eve of the Battle of the Somme in the summer of 1916, the worst defeat in British military history. Against this ill-fated backdrop, the movie depicts the soldiers' experience as a mixture of boredom, fear, panic, and restlessness, confined to a trench on the front lines.
