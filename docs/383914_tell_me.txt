Title: Tell Me (1980)
Genres: TV Movie, Documentary
Director: Chantal Akerman
Main Cast: Chantal Akerman, Natalia Akerman
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Chantal Akerman meets with elderly Jewish women in Paris, all of them survivors of the Shoah, and listens to their family stories. Between interviews, Akerman's mother Natalia speaks of her own family. Made for a French miniseries on grandmothers.
