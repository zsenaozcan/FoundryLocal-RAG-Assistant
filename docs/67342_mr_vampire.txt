Title: Mr. Vampire (1985)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Horror
Director: Ricky Lau
Main Cast: Lam Ching-Ying, Ricky Hui, Chin Siu Ho, Moon Lee, Huang Ha
Keywords: martial arts, vampire, possession, ghost, supernatural power, taoism

Overview: The planned reburial of a village elder goes awry as the corpse resurrects into a hopping, bloodthirsty vampire, threatening mankind. Therefore, a Taoist Priest and his two disciples attempt to stop the terror.
