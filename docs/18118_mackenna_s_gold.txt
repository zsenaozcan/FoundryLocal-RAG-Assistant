Title: Mackenna's Gold (1969)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Western
Director: J. Lee Thompson
Main Cast: Gregory Peck, Omar Sharif, Camilla Sparv, Julie Newmar, Telly Savalas
Keywords: gold, marshal

Overview: The gangster Colorado kidnaps Marshal McKenna. He believes that McKenna has seen a map which leads to a rich vein of gold in the mountains and forces him to show him the way. But they're not the only ones who're after the gold; soon they meet a group of "honorable" citizens and the cavalry crosses their way too - and that is even before they enter Indian territory.
