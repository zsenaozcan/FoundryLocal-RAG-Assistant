Title: Poor Little Rich Girl (1936)
Genres: Adventure, Family, Music
Director: Irving Cummings
Main Cast: Shirley Temple, Jack Haley, Gloria Stuart, Alice Faye, Michael Whalen
Keywords: musical, business man, doll, soap opera

Overview: Cossetted and bored, Barbara Barry is finally sent off to school by her busy if doting widowed soap manufacturer father. When her nurse is injured en route, Barbara finds herself alone in town, ending up as part of radio song-and-dance act Dolan and Dolan sponsored by a rival soap company.
