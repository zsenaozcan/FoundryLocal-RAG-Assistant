Title: Little Nemo: Adventures In Slumberland (1989)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Family, Fantasy
Director: Masami Hata
Main Cast: Gabriel Damon, Mickey Rooney, Rene Auberjonois, Danny Mann, Laura Mooney

Overview: A little boy whose dreams transcend reality is sucked into his own fantasy, which is everything he has dreamed of, until he unleashes an old secret that may not only destroy this perfect dream world but reality itself.
