Title: A Midsummer Night's Dream (1999)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Michael Hoffman
Main Cast: Rupert Everett, Calista Flockhart, Kevin Kline, Michelle Pfeiffer, Stanley Tucci
Keywords: shakespeare, fairy, lover, promise, luck, puck

Overview: Shakespeare's comedy about two couples in love with the wrong partners, and how they are finally brought together rightly, thanks in part to the bungling work of Puck. It is completely in the language of the Bard, with Pfeiffer as the Fairy Queen and Kline as the one turned into her evening's lover with donkey ears.
