Title: Cruel Summer (2016)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Phillip Escott
Main Cast: Richard Pawulski, Danny Miller, Reece Douglas, Natalie Martins, Grace Dixon

Overview: Young Autism sufferer Danny enjoys the serenity and solitude of camping. That's all about to change when enraged teens Nicholas, Calvin and Julia find him, each with their own agenda for wanting to see Danny suffer.
