Title: Silk (2006)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller, Science Fiction, Mystery
Director: Chao-Bin Su
Main Cast: Yosuke Eguchi, Chang Chen, Karena Lam, Barbie Hsu, Chen Bolin

Overview: A scientist uses his invention - the "Menger Sponge" - to capture the energy of a dead child's spirit in an old building. In trying to determine why the energy of the ghost does not dissipate, the team discovers the identity and the dramatic story of the boy.
