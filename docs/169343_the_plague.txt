Title: The Plague (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Neus Ballús
Main Cast: Rosemarie Abella, Maribel Martí, Raül Molist, Maria Ros, Iurie Timbur
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Raül, a farmer that tries to grow organic food, hires Iurie,  a Moldavian wrestling fighter, to help him in the fields. Slowly, their personal histories intertwine with those of three solitary women.
