Title: Mulholland Drive (2001)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Mystery
Director: David Lynch
Main Cast: Naomi Watts, Laura Harring, Ann Miller, Dan Hedaya, Justin Theroux
Keywords: schizophrenia, identity, amnesia, loss of sense of reality, suppressed past, trauma, key, bisexuality, hallucination, surreal

Overview: After a car wreck on the winding Mulholland Drive renders a woman amnesic, she and a perky Hollywood-hopeful search for clues and answers across Los Angeles in a twisting venture beyond dreams and reality.
