Title: Pernicious (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: James Cullen Bressack
Main Cast: Ciara Hanna, Emily O'Brien, Jackie Moore, Byron Gibson, Russell Geoffrey Banks
Keywords: revenge, dismemberment, ghost, torture porn

Overview: It was supposed to be an adventure of a lifetime as three young girls spend the summer in Thailand. But their adventure quickly becomes a nightmare when the trio unleashes the spirit of a murdered child with only one thing on her mind - revenge.
