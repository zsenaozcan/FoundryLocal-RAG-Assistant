Title: The Red Lily (1924)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Fred Niblo
Main Cast: Ramon Novarro, Enid Bennett, Wallace Beery, Frank Currier, Mitchell Lewis
Keywords: paris, prison, escape, silent film, lost love

Overview: Jean and Marise, two young lovers, are forced from their homes by disapproving parents. To escape the oppression in their homes, the couple flees to Paris. When they arrive, Jean leaves Marise briefly to arrange their wedding when he is arrested for theft from his own father. The couple is irrevocably separated, and their lives deviate into the slums and hard labor of low-class French society. All the while, the two desperately search for their lost love.
