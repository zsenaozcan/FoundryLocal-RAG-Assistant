Title: Vavien (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Thriller, Drama
Director: Durul Taylan
Main Cast: Engin Gunaydin, Binnur Kaya, Settar Tanriogen, İlker Aksum, Günes Berberoglu

Overview: Celal, lives an unhappy family life with his wife Sevilay and his child in a small town. Celal and his brother Cemal, running an electrician shop which doesn't go well. They are in debt. The only fun they have is going to the night clubs in Samsun. Celal's love for Sibel Ceylan who works in the night club, will cause him trouble. Celal's wife, Sevilay, saves her money, sent by her father who lives in Germany. She is unaware of the fact that Celal knows her secret.
