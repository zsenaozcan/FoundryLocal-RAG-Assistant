Title: Street Thief (2006)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Malik Bader
Main Cast: Frank Zieger, Wesley Walker
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Kaspar Carr is a professional burglar who lives and works in Chicago. Carr loves his job and is good at it, and two documentary filmmakers are granted access to his private world when he agrees to let them make a film about him.
