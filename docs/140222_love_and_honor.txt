Title: Love and Honor (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: Danny Mooney 
Main Cast: Liam Hemsworth, Teresa Palmer, Aimee Teegarden, Chris Lowell, Wyatt Russell
Keywords: vietnam war, u.s. soldier, anti war, awol, win girl back

Overview: When a young soldier in Vietnam gets dumped by his hometown girl, he and his best friend decide to go AWOL and return to the States to win her back.
