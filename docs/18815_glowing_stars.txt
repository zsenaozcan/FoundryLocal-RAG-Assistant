Title: Glowing Stars (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lisa Siwe
Main Cast: Josefine Mattsson, Mika Berndtsdotter Ahlen, Annika Hallin, Anki Lidén, Samuel Haus
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Young adolescent girl Jenna's mother is dying, while at the same time Jenna is turning form a young girl into a young woman. So it is difficult for her to admit that she is afraid to lose her mother, instead she behaves contrary to how one would expect from her.
