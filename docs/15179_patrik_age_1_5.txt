Title: Patrik, Age 1.5 (2008)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Ella Lemhagen
Main Cast: Gustaf Skarsgård, Torkel Petersson, Tom Ljungman, Annika Hallin, Amanda Davin
Keywords: gay, adoption, woman director

Overview: Göran and Sven have been cleared for adoption and they have a possibility to adopt a swedish orphan, Patrik 1,5. But when Patrik arrives he turns out to be someone else, not the little boy they were expecting. A comma had been misplaced, and in comes a 15-year-old homophobic with a criminal past.
