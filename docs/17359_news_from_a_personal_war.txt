Title: News from a Personal War (1999)
Genres: Documentary, Crime
Director: Kátia Lund
Main Cast: Nilton Cerqueira, Carlos Luis Gregório, Paulo Lins, Hélio Luz, Rodrigo Pimentel
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Documentary about urban violence in Brazil, especially in Rio de Janeiro. Policemen, drug dealers, and shantytown dwellers get trapped into a daily war that knows no winners.
