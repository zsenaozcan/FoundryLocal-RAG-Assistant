Title: Trailer Park of Terror (2008)
Genres: Horror
Director: Steven Goldmann
Main Cast: Nichole Hiltz, Ryan Carnes, Trace Adkins, Priscilla Barnes, Stefanie Black
Keywords: sex, trailer park, bus, murder, redneck, undead, truck driver

Overview: Based on the Imperium Comics series, Trailer Park of Terror. Six troubled high school students and their chaperon, an optimistic youth ministries Pastor, return from an outdoor character building retreat in the mountains. During a raging storm, their bus crashes, hopelessly stranding them in the middle of the Trucker's Triangle, a forgotten locus of consummate evil in the middle of nowhere.
