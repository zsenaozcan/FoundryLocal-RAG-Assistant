Title: The Nostalgist (2014)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Giacomo Cimini
Main Cast: Lambert Wilson, Samuel Joslin, Darrell D'Silva
Keywords: android, future, virtual reality, dystopia, victorian england, steampunk, children, robot, dystopic future, short

Overview: In the futuristic city of Vanille, with properly tuned ImmerSyst Eyes &amp; Ears the world can look and sound like a paradise. But the life of a father and his young son threatens to disintegrate when the father's device begins to fail. Desperate to avoid facing his own traumatic reality, the man must venture outside to find a replacement, into a city where violence and danger lurk beneath a skim of beautiful illusion.
