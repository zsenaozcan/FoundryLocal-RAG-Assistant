Title: Human Trafficking (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Christian Duguay
Main Cast: Mira Sorvino, Donald Sutherland, Robert Carlyle, Emma Campbell, Larry Day
Keywords: corruption, eastern europe, philippines, small community

Overview: When a sixteen-year-old girl from the Ukraine, a single mother from Russia, an orphaned seventeen-year-old girl from Romania, and a twelve-year-old American tourist become the victims of international sex slave traffickers, a specialized team of Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) struggles to expose the worldwide network that has enslaved them.
