Title: Escape from the Planet of the Apes (1971)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Don Taylor
Main Cast: Roddy McDowall, Kim Hunter, Bradford Dillman, Natalie Trundy, Eric Braeden
Keywords: spacecraft, pacifism, human being, cage, dystopia, politician, chimpanzee, tease, caving
