Title: Artists Under the Big Top: Perplexed (1968)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alexander Kluge
Main Cast: Hannelore Hoger, Sigi Graue, Alfred Edel, Klaus Schwarzkopf, Bernd Höltz
Keywords: circus, television

Overview: A young circus director ends up going into television after her father, a trapeze performer, dies in a circus accident.
