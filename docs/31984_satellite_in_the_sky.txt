Title: Satellite in the Sky (1956)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Paul Dickson
Main Cast: Kieron Moore, Lois Maxwell, Donald Wolfit, Bryan Forbes, Jimmy Hanley
Keywords: sacrifice, stowaway, time limit

Overview: A bomb dooms the first space satellite, manned by a selfless crew, a stowaway reporter (Lois Maxwell) and a mad scientist (Donald Wolfit).
