Title: The Scarf (1951)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: E.A. Dupont
Main Cast: John Ireland, Mercedes McCambridge, James Barton, Emlyn Williams, Lloyd Gough
Keywords: amnesia, escape, psychiatrist

Overview: John Ireland stars in this thriller as a man who breaks out of an asylum for the criminally insane, where he has been committed for strangling a girl with a scarf.
