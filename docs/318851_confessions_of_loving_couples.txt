Title: Confessions of Loving Couples (1967)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Wim Verstappen
Main Cast: Ramses Shaffy, Kitty Courbois, Michael York, Shireen Strooker, Marina Schaper
Keywords: writing, love

Overview: Author Frank's debut novel is successful, but he needs the proceeds to pay off a debt and his girlfriend Mascha ends their relationship. He meets another woman, but he and Mascha also seem to make amends. These amorous vicissitudes give him inspiration for a new book, which will deal with eroticism in the Netherlands.
