Title: Jamie Marks Is Dead (2014)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Carter Smith
Main Cast: Morgan Saylor, Noah Silver, Cameron Monaghan, Brett DelBuono, Fred Tolliver Jr.
Keywords: high school, haunting, death, troubled teen, ghost

Overview: No one seemed to care about Jamie Marks until after his death. Hoping to find the love and friendship he never had in life, Jamie’s ghost visits former classmate Adam McCormick, drawing him into the bleak world between the living and the dead.
