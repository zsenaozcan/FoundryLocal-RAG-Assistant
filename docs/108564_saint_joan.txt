Title: Saint Joan (1957)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Otto Preminger
Main Cast: Jean Seberg, Richard Widmark, Richard Todd, Adolf Wohlbrück, John Gielgud
Keywords: france, trial, saint, joan of arc

Overview: Young Joan of Arc comes to the palace in France to make The Dauphin King of France and is appointed to head the French Army. After winning many battles she is not needed any longer and soon she is thought of as a witch.
