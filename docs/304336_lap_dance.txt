Title: Lap Dance (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Greg Carter
Main Cast: Carmen Electra, Briana Evigan, Stacey Dash, Nia Peeples, James Remar
Keywords: based on true story, cancer, lap dance, female stripper, exotic dancer

Overview: An aspiring actress makes a pact with her fiancé to take a job as an exotic dancer to care for her cancer stricken father. Once the pact the couple made is broken, their lives are changed forever.
