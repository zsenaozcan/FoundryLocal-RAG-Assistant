Title: Life Risking Romance (2016)
Genres: Romance, Thriller, Comedy
Director: Song Min-gyu
Main Cast: Ha Ji-won, Chun Jung-myung, Chen Bolin, Yoon So-hee, Oh Jung-se

Overview: Je-In (Ha Ji-Won) is a mystery novel writer. She chases after a serial killer with her longtime friend Rok-Hwan (Chun Jung-Myung). Rok-Hwan is a detective. A handsome and mysterious man, Jason (Chen Bolin), appears in front of them.
