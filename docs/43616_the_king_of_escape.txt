Title: The King of Escape (2009)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Alain Guiraudie
Main Cast: Hafsia Herzi, Ludovic Berthillot, Luc Palun, Pascal Aubert, Jean Toscane

Overview: Armand, a man who sells farm machines in the country, is a popular middle-aged homosexual. Just as he was getting sick of life, he falls in love with a young girl called, Curlie, and goes on the run from her parents and the police.
