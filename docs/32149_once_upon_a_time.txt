Title: Once Upon a Time (2008)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Jeong Yong-ki
Main Cast: Lee Bo-young, Park Yong-woo, Kim Su-hyeon, Sung Dong-il, Kim Eung-Soo

Overview: The film is a heist comedy set in 1940s Korea, and stars Park Yong-woo and Lee Bo-young as a con artist and jazz singer who each plot to steal a valuable diamond from the Japanese authorities.
