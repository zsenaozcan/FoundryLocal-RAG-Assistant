Title: Supernatural (1933)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Horror
Director: Victor Halperin
Main Cast: Carole Lombard, Alan Dinehart, Vivienne Osborne, Randolph Scott, H.B. Warner
Keywords: murderer, seance, spiritualist

Overview: In the course of a seance meant to contact her dead brother, heiress Roma Courtenay is instead possessed by the spirit of recently executed murderess Ruth Rogan.
