Title: Thin Ice (2011)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Jill Sprecher
Main Cast: Greg Kinnear, Lea Thompson, Billy Crudup, Alan Arkin, Scott Crouch
Keywords: cold, independent film, salesman, woman director

Overview: A dishonest insurance salesman's life quickly disintegrates during a Wisconsin winter when he teams up with a psychopath to steal a rare violin at the home of a reclusive farmer.
