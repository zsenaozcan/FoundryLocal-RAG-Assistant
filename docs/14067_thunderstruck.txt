Title: Thunderstruck (2004)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Darren Ashton
Main Cast: Damon Gameau, Stephen Curry, Ryan Johnson, Callan Mulvey, Sam Worthington
Keywords: australia, road trip, best friends

Overview: After a near death experience, five Boys, all devoted AC/DC fans, make a pact to bury their best friend next to the grave of Bon Scott. 12 years later, having gone their different ways, they come together to fulfill the promise.
