Title: Savageland (2017)
Genres: Horror, Mystery
Director: Phil Guidry
Main Cast: Monica Davis, Edward L. Green, Patrick Pedraza, VaLynn Rain
Keywords: social commentary, mockumentary, apocalypse, found footage, mexican border

Overview: When a small town near the Arizona-Mexico border is wiped out overnight, suspicion falls on the lone survivor. But a roll of photos the survivor took that night tells a different story.
