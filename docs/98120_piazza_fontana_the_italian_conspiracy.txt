Title: Piazza Fontana: The Italian Conspiracy (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Marco Tullio Giordana
Main Cast: Valerio Mastandrea, Pierfrancesco Favino, Bob Marchese, Michela Cescon, Laura Chiatti
Keywords: bomb, milan

Overview: A chronicle of the 1969 bombing at a major national bank in Milan and its aftermath.
