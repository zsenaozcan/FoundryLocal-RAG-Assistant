Title: Manufacturing Dissent (2007)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Debbie Melnyk 
Main Cast: Michael Moore, Debbie Melnyk , Rick Caine
Keywords: woman director

Overview: "Michael Moore doesn't like documentaries. That's why he doesn't make them." A documentary that looks to distinguish what's fact, fiction, legend, and otherwise as a camera crew trails Michael Moore as he tours with his film, Fahrenheit 9/11.
