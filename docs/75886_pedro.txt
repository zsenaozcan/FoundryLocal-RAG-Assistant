Title: Pedro (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nick Oceano
Main Cast: Alex Loynaz, Justina Machado, Hale Appleman, DaJuan Johnson, Matt Barr
Keywords: gay, aids, mtv, hiv, lgbt history

Overview: An intimate biopic of Pedro Zamora, an HIV-positive Cuban-American, who was cast for the MTV reality show, The Real World: San Francisco, in 1994. Due to his experience on the Real World, Pedro became a celebrity and a sympathetic face of the AIDS epidemic for millions of Americans who had never met anyone with HIV/AIDS.
