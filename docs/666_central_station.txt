Title: Central Station (1998)
Genres: Drama
Director: Walter Salles
Main Cast: Fernanda Montenegro, Marília Pêra, Vinícius de Oliveira, Othon Bastos, Otávio Augusto
Keywords: brazilian, brazil, rio de janeiro, letter, wilderness, teacher, alcoholic, railroad, missing father, long lost relative

Overview: An emotive journey of a former school teacher, who writes letters for illiterate people, and a young boy, whose mother has just died, as they search for the father he never knew.
