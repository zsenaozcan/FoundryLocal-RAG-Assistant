Title: Buried Alive (1990)
Genres: TV Movie, Horror, Thriller
Director: Frank Darabont
Main Cast: Tim Matheson, Jennifer Jason Leigh, William Atherton, Hoyt Axton
Keywords: poison, buried alive, love affair

Overview: Clint is an every day working man whose wife Joanna is having an affair with a doctor. They plot to kill him and get the insurance money. Only trouble the drug overdose they give him doesn't kill him. Lucky for Clint he's buried in a cheap wooden box and he unburies himself. Just remember, Hell hath no fury like a man buried alive!
