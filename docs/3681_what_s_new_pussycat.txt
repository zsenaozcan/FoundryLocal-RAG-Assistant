Title: What's New Pussycat? (1965)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Clive Donner
Main Cast: Peter Sellers, Peter O'Toole, Romy Schneider, Capucine, Paula Prentiss
Keywords: journalist, ladykiller, gigolo, sex therapy, fashion journal

Overview: A playboy who refuses to give up his hedonistic lifestyle to settle down and marry his true love seeks help from a demented psychoanalyst who is having romantic problems of his own.
