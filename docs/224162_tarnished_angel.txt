Title: Tarnished Angel (1938)
Genres: Drama
Director: Leslie Goodwins
Main Cast: Sally Eilers, Lee Bowman, Ann Miller, Alma Kruger, Paul Guilfoyle
Keywords: robbery, police, showgirl, vaudeville

Overview: A showgirl with a dubious reputation flees the cops and transforms herself into a phony evangelist offering "cures" to the sick and disabled.
