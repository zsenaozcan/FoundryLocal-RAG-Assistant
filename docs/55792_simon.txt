Title: Simon (1980)
Genres: Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Marshall Brickman
Main Cast: Alan Arkin, Madeline Kahn, William Finley, Wallace Shawn, Max Wright
Keywords: television, brainwashing, scientist, evolution, psychosis

Overview: A group of scientist take Simon, a psychology professor, as a test person for a brainwash experiment. After that they try to convince him that he was a living-being from another planet
