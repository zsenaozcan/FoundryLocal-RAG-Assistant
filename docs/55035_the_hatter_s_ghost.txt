Title: The Hatter's Ghost (1982)
Genres: Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Claude Chabrol
Main Cast: Michel Serrault, Charles Aznavour, Monique Chaumette, François Cluzet, Isabelle Sadoyan
Keywords: serial killer

Overview: A hatter in a provincial town (Michel Serrault) leads the life of a respectable citizen but is in fact a serial murderer. The only person to suspect this is his neighbour the tailor (Charles Asnavour).
