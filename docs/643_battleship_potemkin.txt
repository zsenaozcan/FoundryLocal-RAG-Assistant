Title: Battleship Potemkin (1925)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Sergei M. Eisenstein
Main Cast: Aleksandr Antonov, Vladimir Barsky, Grigori Aleksandrov, Ivan Bobrov, Mikhail Gomorov
Keywords: staircase, russian revolution 1905, odessa, baby carriage, panic, violence, rage, sailor, silent film, revolt

Overview: A dramatized account of a great Russian naval mutiny and a resulting street demonstration which brought on a police massacre. The film had an incredible impact on the development of cinema and was a masterful example of montage editing.
