Title: Funky Forest: The First Contact (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Katsuhito Ishii
Main Cast: Tadanobu Asano, Ryo Kase, Rinko Kikuchi, Yoshiyuki Morishita, Hideaki Anno
Keywords: fantasy

Overview: The Taste of Tea director Katsuhito Ishii collaborates with filmmakers Shinichiro Miki and Hajime Ishimine for this outrageous collection of surreal, short attention span non-sequiturs largely revolving around Guitar Brother.
