Title: The Arena (1974)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Steve Carver
Main Cast: Pam Grier, Margaret Markov, Lucretia Love, Paul Müller, Daniele Vargas
Keywords: female nudity, gladiator, gladiator fight, nudity, sword fight, arrow

Overview: Female gladiators fight to the death. Inspired by the story of Spartacus, follow the adventures of a bevy of slave girls who, upon finding themselves thrust into the gladiator ring, mount a vicious rebellion to fight their way to freedom.
