Title: Nothing Lasts Forever (1984)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Tom Schiller
Main Cast: Zach Galligan, Apollonia van Ravenstein, Lauren Tom, Dan Aykroyd, Bill Murray
Keywords: moon, holland tunnel

Overview: An artist fails a test and is required to direct traffic in New York City's Holland Tunnel. He winds up falling in love with a beautiful woman, who takes him to the moon on a Lunar Cruiser.
