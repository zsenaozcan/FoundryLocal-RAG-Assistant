Title: Nuns on the Run (1990)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jonathan Lynn
Main Cast: Eric Idle, Robbie Coltrane, Camille Coduri, Janet Suzman, Doris Hare
Keywords: drug traffic, chinese mafia, crook couple, flight, crook, stolen money, hospital, gangster, peekaboo, monastery

Overview: Brian and Charlie work for a gangster. When the boss learns they want to "leave" he sets them up to be killed, after they help rob the local Triads of their drug dealing profits. B&amp;C decide to steal the money for themselves, but when their escape doesn't go to plan, they have to seek refuge in a Nuns' teacher training school.
