Title: Blood Father (2016)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Jean-François Richet
Main Cast: Mel Gibson, Erin Moriarty, William H. Macy, Michael Parks, Thomas Mann
Keywords: drug dealer, independent film, father daughter relationship, family reunion, ex-con
