Title: Mozart's Sister (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: René Féret
Main Cast: Marie Féret, Marc Barbé, Delphine Chuillot, David Moreau
Keywords: royal court, overbearing father, harpsichord, 18th century

Overview: A re-imagined account of the early life of Maria Anna 'Nannerl' Mozart, five years older than Wolfgang and a musical prodigy in her own right.
