Title: King for a Day (1983)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Nikolai Volev
Main Cast: Todor Kolev, Yordanka Stefanova, Itzhak Finzi, Stoyan Gudev, Nikola Pashov

Overview: This film is a comedy about a poor man named Purko. Purko always fails in his attempt to escape from poverty. The film illustrates the social and the moral atmosphere in Bulgaria during the 1930's.
