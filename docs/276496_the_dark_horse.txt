Title: The Dark Horse (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: James Napier Robertson
Main Cast: Cliff Curtis, James Rolleston, Kirk Torrance, Sia Trokenheim, Andrew Grainger
Keywords: chess, new zealand, based on true story, bipolar

Overview: One-time Maori speed-chess champ, Genesis Potini, lives with a bi-polar disorder and must overcome prejudice and violence in the battle to save his struggling chess club, his family and ultimately, himself.
