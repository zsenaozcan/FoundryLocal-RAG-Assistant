Title: Alpine Climbers (1936)
Genres: Animation
Director: David Hand
Main Cast: Pinto Colvig, Walt Disney, Clarence Nash
Keywords: mickey mouse, eagle, st. bernard's dog, disney short, donald duck, pluto, mountaineering, cartoon goat

Overview: Donald, Mickey, and Pluto climb the Alps. While up top, Donald has a run-in with a mountain goat over some edelweiss, Mickey has a row with an eagle over its eggs; one of them hatches, and gives Pluto some trouble (as does the grog a Saint Bernard gives him when he falls into a snowbank).
