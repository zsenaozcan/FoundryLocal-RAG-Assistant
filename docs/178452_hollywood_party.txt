Title: Hollywood Party (1934)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: George Stevens
Main Cast: Stan Laurel, Oliver Hardy, Jimmy Durante, Jack Pearl, Polly Moran
Keywords: musical, party, hollywood

Overview: Jimmy Durante is jungle star Schnarzan the Conqueror, but the public is tiring of his fake lions. So when Baron Munchausen comes to town with real man-eating lions, Durante throws a big party with so that he might use the lions in his next movie. His film rival sneaks into the party to buy the lions before Durante.
