Title: LOL (Laughing Out Loud) (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lisa Azuelos
Main Cast: Sophie Marceau, Christa Théret, Alexandre Astier, Jérémy Kapone, Marion Chabassol
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Lola, a striking teenaged girl who is on the cusp of adulthood, who longs to rush into the adult world of independence, freedom and sexual exploits, but is tenaciously held back by her mother.
