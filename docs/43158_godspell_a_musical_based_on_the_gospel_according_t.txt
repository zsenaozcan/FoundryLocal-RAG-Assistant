Title: Godspell: A Musical Based on the Gospel According to St. Matthew (1973)
Genres: Music, Family
Director: David Greene
Main Cast: Victor Garber, David Haskell, Katie Hanley, Merrell Jackson, Joanne Jonas

Overview: A modern day musical telling of the ministry of Jesus Christ set in New York. The Apostles, portrayed as an acting troupe re-enact the parables and teachings of Jesus.
