Title: Gloria (1980)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: John Cassavetes
Main Cast: Gena Rowlands, Julie Carmen, John Adames, Buck Henry, Lupe Garnica
Keywords: neighbor, mobster, gangster, new york city

Overview: When a young boy's family is killed by the mob, their tough neighbor Gloria becomes his reluctant guardian. In possession of a book that the gangsters want, the pair go on the run in New York.
