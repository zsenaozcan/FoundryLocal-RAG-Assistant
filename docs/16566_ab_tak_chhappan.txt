Title: Ab Tak Chhappan (2004)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Shimit Amin
Main Cast: Nana Patekar, Yashpal Sharma, Prasad Purandhare, Nakul Vaid, Kunal Vijaykar
Keywords: dirty cop, police department

Overview: Sadhu Agashe, a police officer in Mumbai, makes a name for himself by killing criminals in sting operations instead of locking them up in prison. The film follows his method of working, gives an insight into the Mumbai Police Crime Branch, and Agashe's alleged connection with the underworld.
