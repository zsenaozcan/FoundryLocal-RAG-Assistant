Title: My Mother's Castle (1990)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Yves Robert
Main Cast: Julien Ciamaca, Philippe Caubère, Nathalie Roussel, Victorien Delamare, Didier Pain
Keywords: provence, biography, marcel pagnol

Overview: My Mother's Castle (Le chateau de ma mere) is a sequel and companion piece to My Father's Glory (La Gloire de Mon Pere), both based on the childhood recollections of Marcel Pagnol.  Like its predecessor, the movie explores the adventures of the young Marcel (Julien Ciamaca) during his summers at the family summer home in Provence.
