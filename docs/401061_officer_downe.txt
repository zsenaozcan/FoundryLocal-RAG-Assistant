Title: Officer Downe (2016)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Shawn Crahan
Main Cast: Kim Coates, Tyler Ross, Lauren Vélez, Samuel Witwer, Lindsay Pulsipher
Keywords: resurrection, vigilante, cop, based on graphic novel

Overview: A rookie cop is tasked with shadowing Officer Downe, a no-nonsense LAPD cop with regenerative powers, as he wages an ultra-violent war against the nefarious villains of Los Angeles.
