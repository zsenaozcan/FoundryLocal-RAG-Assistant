Title: A Perfect Ending (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nicole Conn
Main Cast: Barbara Niven, Jessica Clark, John Heard, Morgan Fairchild, Rebecca Staab
Keywords: homosexuality, lesbian interest, lgbt, woman director

Overview: This intimate drama follows Rebecca, a woman who has kept her sexuality a secret from her friends but chooses to reveal it to a stranger. While Rebecca's revelations may not yield the results she expects, a perfect ending is still in reach.
