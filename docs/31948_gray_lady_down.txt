Title: Gray Lady Down (1978)
Genres: Thriller, Adventure, History, Drama
Director: David Greene
Main Cast: Charlton Heston, David Carradine, Stacy Keach, Ned Beatty, Stephen McHattie
Keywords: submarine, drowning, based on novel, rescue, disaster, underwater, disaster film, collision

Overview: The USS Neptune, a nuclear submarine, is sunk off the coast of Connecticut after a collision with a Norwegian cargo ship. The navy must attempt a potentially dangerous rescue in the hope of saving the lives of the crew.
