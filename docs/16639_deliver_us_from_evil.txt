Title: Deliver Us from Evil (2009)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Ole Bornedal
Main Cast: Pernille Vallentin, Lene Nystrøm, Lasse Rimmer, Fanny Bornedal, Jacob Ottensten

Overview: In search of a new lifestyle, Johannes and his wife Pernille leave the city and move with their two children to Johannes’ childhood town in the country. When Johannes’ drunken brother Lars accidently runs over Anna, a local, and plants false evidence pointing at the emigrant Alain, Johannes stands up to defend Alain. But it comes with a price.
