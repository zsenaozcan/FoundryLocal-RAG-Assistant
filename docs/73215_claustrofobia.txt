Title: Claustrofobia (2011)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Bobby Boermans
Main Cast: Dragan Bakema, Carolien Spoor, Thijs Römer, Frits Lambrechts, Rogier Philipoom

Overview: When Eva opens her eyes a young girl finds herself chained terrified for being abused. Her fears become reality in a way no one could ever imagine.
