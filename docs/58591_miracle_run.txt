Title: Miracle Run (2003)
Genres: Drama
Director: Gregg Champion
Main Cast: Mary-Louise Parker, Aidan Quinn, Zac Efron, Bubba Lewis, Jake Cherry
Keywords: autism, motherly love

Overview: Single mom Corrine discovers that her twins are autistic. She refuses to give up on their potential, even though it seems that the rest of the world has turned its back on them. Thanks to this incredible mother's support, her boys accomplish the unimaginable.
