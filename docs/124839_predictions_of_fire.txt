Title: Predictions of Fire (1996)
Genres: Music, Documentary
Director: Michael Benson
Main Cast: Laibach, Peter Mlakar, Slavoj Žižek
Keywords: philosophy, politics, art, nsk

Overview: A visceral documentary focusing on the Slovenian collective art movement known as NSK ('Neue Slowenische Kunst') and its varied branches: 'Laibach', 'Irwin', and 'Red Pilot'.
