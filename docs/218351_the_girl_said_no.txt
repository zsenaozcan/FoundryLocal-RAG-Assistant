Title: The Girl Said No (1937)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Andrew L. Stone
Main Cast: Robert Armstrong, Irene Hervey, Paula Stone, Edward Brophy, William Danforth
Keywords: bookie, dance hall girl

Overview: Jimmie Allen, a shady bookie, is in love with Pearl Proctor, a greedy dance hall girl. He schemes to get her back after she rejects him; and along the way, he revives a failing Gilbert and Sullivan troupe.
