Title: Eva (1962)
Genres: Drama
Director: Joseph Losey
Main Cast: Jeanne Moreau, Stanley Baker, Giorgio Albertazzi, James Villiers, Virna Lisi
Keywords: venice, novelist

Overview: Best-selling author Tyvian Jones has a life of leisure in Venice, Italy, until he has a chance encounter with sultry Frenchwoman Eva Olivier. He falls for her instantly, despite already having wedding plans with Francesca Ferrara. Winning Eva's affection proves elusive; she's more interested in money than in love. But Tyvian remain steadfast in his obsession, going after Eva with a fervor that threatens to destroy his life.
