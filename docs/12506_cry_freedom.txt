Title: Cry Freedom (1987)
Genres: Drama
Director: Richard Attenborough
Main Cast: Denzel Washington, Kevin Kline, Penelope Wilton, Kate Hardie, John Matshikiza
Keywords: journalist, 1970s, male friendship, south africa, apartheid

Overview: A dramatic story, based on actual events, about the friendship between two men struggling against apartheid in South Africa in the 1970s. Donald Woods is a white liberal sell out in South Africa who begins to follow the activities of Stephen Biko, an arrogant and outspoken black anti-apartheid activist.
