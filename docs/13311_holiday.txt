Title: Holiday (2006)
Genres: Drama, Action, Comedy, Crime, Romance, Family, Foreign
Director: Yang Yun-ho
Main Cast: Lee Sung-jae, Choi Min-soo, Yeo Hyun-soo, Lee Hyun-woo, Jo An

Overview: A (Based On True Events) movie about prisoners escaping during the heyday of Korean nationalism and international image-consciousness, the Seoul World Olympics of 1988.
