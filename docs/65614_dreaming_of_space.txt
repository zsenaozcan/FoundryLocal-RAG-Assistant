Title: Dreaming of Space (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Aleksey Uchitel
Main Cast: Evgeny Mironov, Irina Pegova, Yevgeni Tsyganov, Elena Lyadova, Sergei Kachanov

Overview: A story of a simple, naive Russian man Konek and the people around him: his love and her sister and a mysterious man. The film is set in 1957, time of changes, time of waiting for something big to happen.
