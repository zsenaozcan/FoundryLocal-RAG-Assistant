Title: Liar's Dice (2014)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Geethu Mohandas
Main Cast: Nawazuddin Siddiqui, Geetanjali Thapa, Manya Gupta
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The film follows Kamala, a young woman from Chitkul village and her girl child Manya, who embarks on a journey leaving their native land in search for her missing husband. Along this journey she encounters Nawazudin, a free spirited army deserter who helps them to get to their destination with his own selfish motive.
