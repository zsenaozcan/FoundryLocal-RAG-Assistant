Title: Virumandi (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kamal Haasan
Main Cast: Kamal Haasan, Abhirami, Napoleon, Pasupathy, Rohini

Overview: A reporter researching the death penalty in India is recounted the story of a farm owner Virumandi whose role in a clan feud between Naicker and Kothalla Devar leads him to death row. As the story unfolds, there is a riot in the jail, and the only one who can get the reporter safely out of the prison is Virumandi.
