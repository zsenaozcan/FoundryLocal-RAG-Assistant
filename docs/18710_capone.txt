Title: Capone (1975)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Steve Carver
Main Cast: Ben Gazzara, Harry Guardino, Susan Blakely, Sylvester Stallone, John Cassavetes
Keywords: chicago, prohibition, bodyguard, rise and fall, revenge, murder, bootlegging, gangster, vendetta, bribe

Overview: The story of the rise and fall of the infamous Chicago gangster Al Capone and the control he exhibited over the city during the prohibition years.
