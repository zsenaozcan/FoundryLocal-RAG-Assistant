Title: One Wild Oat (1951)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Charles Saunders
Main Cast: Robertson Hare, Stanley Holloway, Andrew Crawford, Vera Pearce, Audrey Hepburn

Overview: A barrister (Robertson Hare) attempts to discourage his daughter's infatuation for a philanderer by revealing his past. The plan backfires when the daughter's would-be father-in-law (Stanley Holloway) threatens to reveal the barrister's shady background.
