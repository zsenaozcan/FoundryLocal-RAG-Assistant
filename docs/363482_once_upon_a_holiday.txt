Title: Once Upon A Holiday (2015)
Genres: Family, Romance, TV Movie
Director: James Head
Main Cast: Briana Evigan, Paul Campbell, Jacqueline Samuda, Greg Evigan, Tony Alcantar
Keywords: princess, christmas

Overview: When an actual, honest-to-goodness royal princess runs off from her official obligations and duties for a couple of days to see how the other half lives, she winds up falling for a good Samaritan who is unaware of her real identity during the holiday season. Will her true love still feel the same way once he learns the truth?
