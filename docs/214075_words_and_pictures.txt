Title: Words and Pictures (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Fred Schepisi
Main Cast: Clive Owen, Juliette Binoche, Keegan Connor Tracy, Bruce Davison, Adam DiMarco
Keywords: painter, rivalry, teacher, school, battle, art, arrogance

Overview: An art instructor and an English teacher form a rivalry that ends up with a competition at their school in which students decide whether words or pictures are more important.
