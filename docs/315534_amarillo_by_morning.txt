Title: Amarillo By Morning (1998)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Spike Jonze
Main Cast: Clint Barr, Spike Jonze
Keywords: rodeo, texas, houston, youth, cowboy, rodeo cowboy

Overview: While filming professional bullriders for a commercial at the national rodeo in Houston, Texas, Spike Jonze befriended two suburban teenagers who aspired to be cowboys. The documentary chronicles an afternoon in their lives.
