Title: Woyzeck (1979)
Genres: Drama
Director: Werner Herzog
Main Cast: Klaus Kinski, Eva Mattes, Wolfgang Reichmann, Willy Semmelrogge, Josef Bierbichler
Keywords: becoming an adult, child hero, childhood trauma, leipzig, murder, youth, child

Overview: Franz Woyzeck (Kinski) is a hapless, hopeless soldier, alone and powerless in society, assaulted from all sides by forces he cannot control.
