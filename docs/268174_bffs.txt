Title: BFFs (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Andrew Putschoegl
Main Cast: Tara Karsian, Andrea Grano, Larisa Oleynik, Sean Maher, Pat Carroll
Keywords: couples therapy, relationship

Overview: Kat receives a weekend at a couples retreat as a gift from her mother, even though she and her boyfriend had broken up six month prior. Kat's friend Samantha proposes that they pretend to be a couple and go there anyway.
