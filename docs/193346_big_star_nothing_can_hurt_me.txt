Title: Big Star: Nothing Can Hurt Me (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Drew DeNicola
Main Cast: Rick Clark, Jon Auer, Chris Bell, Alex Chilton, Andy Hummel
Keywords: rock music

Overview: Big Star: Nothing Can Hurt Me is a feature-length documentary film about the dismal commercial failure, subsequent massive critical acclaim, and enduring legacy of pop music's greatest cult phenomenon, Big Star.
