Title: Everybody's Got Somebody... Not Me (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Raúl Fuentes
Main Cast: Andrea Portal, Naian Daeva, Isabel Aerenlund, Luis Andree Arias, Gimena Gómez
Keywords: lesbian

Overview: Alejandra is sick of her daily life and her past relationships that have not worked out. Then she meets María, an adolescent, with whom she has an affair. At first everything runs smoothly, however Alejandra´s personality and her emotional needs prove increasingly demanding; being around her becomes unbearable. At this point, they ask themselves whether they should continue the oppressive relationship, or go on with their ordinary lives.
