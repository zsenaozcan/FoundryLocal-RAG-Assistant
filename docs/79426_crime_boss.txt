Title: Crime Boss (1972)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Alberto De Martino
Main Cast: Telly Savalas, Antonio Sabàto, Paola Tedesco, Giuliano Persico, Guido Lollobrigida
Keywords: poliziotteschi

Overview: Hit man Antonio Mancuso travels to Italy to make the big time. He meets and befriends Mafia boss Don Vincenzo. Mancurso becomes part of the organization and the more he gets to know Vincenzo the more he wants his job but Mancurso will learn that success can come at a severe cost.
