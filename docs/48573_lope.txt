Title: Lope (2010)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Andrucha Waddington
Main Cast: Leonor Watling, Sônia Braga, Luis Tosar, Alberto Ammann, Pilar López de Ayala

Overview: A chronicle of the life of Spanish playwright and poet Lope de Vega, who dominated Spain’s early Golden Age of Theatre in the 16th Century. Lope returns home from war and enters the theatre world when a producer’s beautiful daughter takes a shine to him. They embark on a passionate affair as his plays begin to win popular acclaim.
