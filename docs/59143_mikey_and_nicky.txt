Title: Mikey and Nicky (1976)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime
Director: Elaine May
Main Cast: Peter Falk, John Cassavetes, Ned Beatty, Rose Arrick, Carol Grace
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In Philadelphia, a small-time bookie who stole mob money is in hiding and he begs a childhood friend to help him evade the hit-man who's on his trail.
