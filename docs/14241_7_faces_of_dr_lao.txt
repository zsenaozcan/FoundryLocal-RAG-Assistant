Title: 7 Faces of Dr. Lao (1964)
Genres: Drama, Family, Fantasy, Mystery, Western
Director: George Pal
Main Cast: Tony Randall, John Ericson, Barbara Eden, Kevin Tate, Arthur O'Connell
Keywords: circus, magic, medusa, chinese man

Overview: An old Chinese man rides into the town of Abalone, Arizona and changes it forever, as the citizens see themselves reflected in the mirror of Lao's mysterious circus of mythical beasts.
