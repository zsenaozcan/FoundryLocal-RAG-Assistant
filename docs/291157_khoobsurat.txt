Title: Khoobsurat (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Shashanka Ghosh
Main Cast: Fawad Afzal Khan, Sonam Kapoor, Aditi Rao Hydari, Kirron Kher, Ratna Pathak

Overview: A vibrant, hopelessly romantic physiotherapist meets a handsome young Rajput prince who is the complete opposite of her – and is engaged to someone else.
