Title: Tormented (2011)
Genres: Horror
Director: Takashi Shimizu
Main Cast: Hikari Mitsushima, Teruyuki Kagawa, Takeru Shibuya, Nao Omori, Tamaki Ogawa
Keywords: horror movie, alternate world, stuffed toy rabbit

Overview: To his mute sister's dismay, a young boy puts a dying rabbit out of its misery with a brick -- and soon the siblings end up in a world of nightmares.
