Title: The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari (1920)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller, Crime
Director: Robert Wiene
Main Cast: Werner Krauss, Conrad Veidt, Friedrich Fehér, Lil Dagover, Hans Heinrich von Twardowski
Keywords: insane asylum, silent film, unreliable narrator, expressionism, somnambulist, unreliable flashback, megalomania, madman, enigma, somnambulism

Overview: The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari is eerie and expressionistic, silent and surreal. It has become not only a classic of German Expressionist cinema, but a landmark in film history, with creative scenery and an unusual ending.
