Title: The Bullfighters (1945)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Malcolm St. Clair
Main Cast: Stan Laurel, Oliver Hardy, Margo Woode, Richard Lane, Carol Andrews
Keywords: matador, bullfighting

Overview: Bumbling detective Stan Laurel disguises himself as a famous matador in order to hide from the vengeful Richard K. Muldoon, who spent time in prison on Stan's bogus testimony.
