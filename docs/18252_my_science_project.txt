Title: My Science Project (1985)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Jonathan R. Betuel
Main Cast: John Stockwell, Dennis Hopper, Danielle von Zerneck, Fisher Stevens, Richard Masur
Keywords: time travel, dinosaur, science

Overview: Michael and Ellie break into a military junkyard to find a science project for Michael's class, and discover a strange glowing orb which absorbs electricity. When the orb begins to blend past, present, and future, its up to Michael and Ellie to stop the orb and save mankind.
