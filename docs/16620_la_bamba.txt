Title: La Bamba (1987)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Luis Valdez
Main Cast: Lou Diamond Phillips, Danielle von Zerneck, Elizabeth Peña, Rosanna DeSoto, Esai Morales
Keywords: musical, death, dying young, nostalgic, mexican american, rising star, marital rape
