Title: Side Street (1950)
Genres: Crime
Director: Anthony Mann
Main Cast: Farley Granger, Cathy O'Donnell, James Craig, Paul Kelly, Jean Hagen
Keywords: bag of money, city life

Overview: A struggling young father-to-be gives in to temptation and impulsively steals an envelope of money from the office of a corrupt attorney. Instead of a few hundred dollars it contains $30,000 and when he decides to return the money things go wrong and that is only the beginning of his troubles.
