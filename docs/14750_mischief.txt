Title: Mischief (1985)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Mel Damski
Main Cast: Doug McKeon, Catherine Mary Stewart, Kelly Preston, Chris Nash, D.W. Brown
Keywords: female nudity, rebel, sex, high school, romantic comedy, teen movie, teenager, first love, 1950s

Overview: 1956: The shy Jonathan's luck with girls changes when he wins the rebellious Gene as a friend in his last year of high school. Gene is adored by many girls and manages to teach Jonathan a few lessons. Gene himself would rather just be with one girl: his girlfriend Bunny. But since his father is poor, her parents don't accept him.
