Title: Rhymes for Young Ghouls (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jeff Barnaby
Main Cast: Roseanne Supernault, Mark Antony Krupa, Devery Jacobs, Arthur Holden, Glen Gould

Overview: In 1976, a Mi'gMaq teenager plots revenge against the sadistic Indian agent who imprisoned her in a residential school where rape and abuse are common.
