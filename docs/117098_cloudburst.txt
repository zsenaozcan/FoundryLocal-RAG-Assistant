Title: Cloudburst (2011)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Comedy, Thriller, Foreign
Director: Thom Fitzgerald
Main Cast: Olympia Dukakis, Brenda Fricker, John Dunsworth, Kristin Booth, Ryan Doucette
Keywords: comedy

Overview: When Dot (Brenda Fricker)'s granddaughter puts her into a nursing home, Stella (Olympia Dukakis) stages a breakout, and takes Dot to Canada so they can get married. They pick up a hitchhiker along the way.
