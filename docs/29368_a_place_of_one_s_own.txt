Title: A Place of One's Own (1945)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Bernard Knowles
Main Cast: Margaret Lockwood, James Mason, Barbara Mullen, Dennis Price, Helen Haye
Keywords: possession, murder, old house, haunted manor

Overview: An elderly couple move into an old, supposedly haunted abandoned house. A young girl comes to live with the pair as a companion for the wife. However, soon the girl is possessed by the spirit of another girl, a wealthy woman who had once lived in the house but who had been murdered there.
