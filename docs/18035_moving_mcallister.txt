Title: Moving McAllister (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Andrew Black
Main Cast: Ben Gourley, Mila Kunis, Jon Heder, Rutger Hauer, Hubbel Palmer
Keywords: pig, road trip, independent film, hitchhiker, intern

Overview: Rick Robinson, a law intern, is scheduled to take the bar exam in just four days. Anxious to score points with his boss Mr. McAllister, Rick unwisely agrees to help the man move. The next day, Rick finds himself in a moving van from Miami to Los Angeles, accompanied by McAllister's spoiled niece and her pet pig.
