Title: The Bandit (1946)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Alberto Lattuada
Main Cast: Anna Magnani, Amedeo Nazzari, Carla Del Poggio, Carlo Campanini, Eliana Banducci
Keywords: robbery, neorealism

Overview: Among others, Ernesto returns to Turin after the war from a German POW camp; his household destroyed and family deceased, he tries making an honest living.
