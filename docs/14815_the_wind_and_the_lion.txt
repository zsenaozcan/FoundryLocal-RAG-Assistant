Title: The Wind and the Lion (1975)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, History
Director: John Milius
Main Cast: Sean Connery, Candice Bergen, Brian Keith, John Huston, Geoffrey Lewis
Keywords: morocco, theodore roosevelt

Overview: At the beginning of the 20th century an American woman is abducted in Morocco by Berbers. The attempts to free her range from diplomatic pressure to military intervention.
