Title: Bring Me the Head of Alfredo Garcia (1974)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller, Western
Director: Sam Peckinpah
Main Cast: Warren Oates, Isela Vega, Robert Webber, Gig Young, Helmut Dantine
Keywords: female nudity, mexico, prostitute, bounty, bounty hunter, nudity, bartender, buried alive, revenge, murder
