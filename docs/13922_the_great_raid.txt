Title: The Great Raid (2005)
Genres: Action, History, War
Director: John Dahl
Main Cast: Benjamin Bratt, James Franco, Connie Nielsen, Marton Csokas, Joseph Fiennes
Keywords: based on novel, world war ii, prisoners of war, narration, archive footage, rescue mission, soldier, 1940s, inspired by true events, fictionalized history
