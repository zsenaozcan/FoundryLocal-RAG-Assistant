Title: The Dust Factory (2004)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Science Fiction, Family
Director: Eric Small
Main Cast: Armin Mueller-Stahl, Hayden Panettiere, Ryan Kelley, George de la Peña, Michael Angarano
Keywords: grandfather grandson relationship, bridge, water, contamination

Overview: Ryan is a teenager who lacks the ability to speak. One day, Ryan falls off a bridge and he finds himself transported to a strange fantasy world where he encounters his Grandpa Randolph (Armin Mueller-Stahl) and a pretty girl named Melanie (Hayden Panettiere); together, Ryan and Melanie learn to help one another with their problems, and they both discover the wisdom they can gain from elders like Grandpa Randolph.
