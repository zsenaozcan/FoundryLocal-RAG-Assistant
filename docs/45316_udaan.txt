Title: Udaan (2010)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Vikramaditya Motwane
Main Cast: Rajat Barmecha, Aayan Boradia, Ronit Roy, Ram Kapoor, Manjot Singh

Overview: After being abandoned for eight straight years in boarding school, Rohan returns to the small industrial town of Jamshedpur and finds himself closeted with an authoritarian father and a younger half brother who he didn't even know existed. Forced to work in his father's steel factory and study engineering against his wishes, he strives to forge his own life out of his given circumstances and pursue his dream of being a writer.
