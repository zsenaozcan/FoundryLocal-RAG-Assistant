Title: The New Centurions (1972)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Action
Director: Richard Fleischer
Main Cast: George C. Scott, Stacy Keach, Jane Alexander, Scott Wilson, Rosalind Cash
Keywords: los angeles, rookie cop, lapd

Overview: An idealistic rookie cop joins the LAPD to make ends meet while finishing law school, and is indoctrinated by a seasoned veteran. As time goes on, he loses his ambitions and family as police work becomes his entire life.
