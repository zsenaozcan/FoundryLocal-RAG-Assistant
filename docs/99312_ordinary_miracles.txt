Title: Ordinary Miracles (2005)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Family
Director: Michael Switzer
Main Cast: Jaclyn Smith, Lyndsy Fonseca, C. Thomas Howell, Sarah Aldrich, Corbin Bernsen

Overview: A tough judge takes in a foster child with nowhere to go. Attempts to reunite child with long lost father end badly with the rebellious child running away.
