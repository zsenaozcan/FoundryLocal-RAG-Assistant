Title: Watch Me When I Kill (1977)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Antonio Bido
Main Cast: Corrado Pani, Paola Tedesco, Franco Citti, Fernando Cerulli, Paolo Malco
Keywords: female nudity, murder, holocaust survivor, giallo, slasher, chemist, psycho killer

Overview: A pharmacist is murdered, and a woman happens to see the culprit leave the scene. She soon finds herself being stalked by the killer.
