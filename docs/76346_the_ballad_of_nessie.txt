Title: The Ballad of Nessie (2011)
Genres: Animation, Family, Fantasy
Director: Stevie Wermers
Main Cast: Billy Connolly
Keywords: scotland, golf, disney short, loch ness monster, rubber duck

Overview: A legendary and friendly creature named Nessie lives happily in a small pond with her friend MacQuack, a rubber duck. When a rich developer takes the pond and land surrounding it to build a miniature golf course, Nessie is forced to search for a new home.
