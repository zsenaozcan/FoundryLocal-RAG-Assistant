Title: Voices (1979)
Genres: Romance, Music
Director: Robert Markowitz
Main Cast: Michael Ontkean, Amy Irving, Alex Rocco, Barry Miller, Herbert Berghof
Keywords: rock star, deaf

Overview: Oscar-nominee Amy Irving ("Crossing Delancy," "Carrie") stars as a deaf woman who falls in love with an aspiring rock singer, even though she is unable to hear him sing.
