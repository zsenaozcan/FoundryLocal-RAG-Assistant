Title: Posthumous Memories (2001)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: André Klotzel
Main Cast: Reginaldo Faria, Marcos Caruso, Stepan Nercessian, Sônia Braga, Otávio Muller

Overview: Free adaptation of Machado de Assis' classic. The narrator is a rich dead man, who tells us about his life and times, making fun of both.
