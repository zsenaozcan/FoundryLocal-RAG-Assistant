Title: Kanchenjungha (1962)
Genres: Drama
Director: Satyajit Ray
Main Cast: Chhabi Biswas, Karuna Bannerjee, Pahadi Sanyal, Anil Chatterjee, Anubha Gupta
Keywords: family vacation

Overview: Played out in real time, several complex family dramas intersect in the landscape of the Himalayan foothills, allowing the Bengali auteur to examine the class and generational differences of postcolonial India while celebrating the hopes of a society in transition.
