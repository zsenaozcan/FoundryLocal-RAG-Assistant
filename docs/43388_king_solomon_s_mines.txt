Title: King Solomon's Mines (1950)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Romance
Director: Andrew Marton
Main Cast: Stewart Granger, Deborah Kerr, Richard Carlson, Hugo Haas, Lowell Gilmore
Keywords: africa, diamond mine, allan quatermain

Overview: Adventurer Allan Quartermain leads an expedition into uncharted African territory in an attempt to locate an explorer who went missing during his search for the fabled diamond mines of King Solomon.
