Title: Manson (1973)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Laurence Merrick
Main Cast: Charles Manson, Vincent Bugliosi, Lynette Fromme, Bruce Davis, Paul Watkins
Keywords: counter-culture, disciples, multiple murders

Overview: Prosecuting attorney Vincent Bugliosi and Manson "family" members Lynette Fromme, Mary Brunner and Sandra Good discuss the Tate-LaBianca murders.
