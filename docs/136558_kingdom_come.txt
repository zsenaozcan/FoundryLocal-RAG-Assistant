Title: Kingdom Come (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Paiman Kalayeh
Main Cast: Daniel Gillies, Rachael Leigh Cook, Seth Green, John Lyons Murphy, Selma Blair

Overview: The documentary, Kingdom Come follows a first-time director (Daniel Gillies) as he tries to raise a million dollars to finance his first film, Broken Kingdom. This emotionally-charged journey is interwoven with over 30 rare interviews from acclaimed indie darlings including Mark Ruffalo, Illeana Douglas, Don Cheadle, Kevin Smith, Edward Burns, Tim Roth, Morgan Spurlock, Selma Blair, Robert Townsend, Bill Pullman and many more.
