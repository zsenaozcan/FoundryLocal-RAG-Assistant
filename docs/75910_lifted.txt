Title: Lifted (2010)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Lexi Alexander
Main Cast: Uriah Shelton, Dash Mihok, Nicki Aycox, Ruben Studdard, Trace Adkins
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The chance to shine in a singing competition offers much-needed hope for a Southern middle-schooler who's drowning in worries, from bullies in the halls to turmoil at home -- and the news that his reservist father has been called back to Afghanistan.
