Title: Catherine of Russia (1963)
Genres: Drama, History, Romance
Director: Umberto Lenzi
Main Cast: Hildegard Knef, Sergio Fantoni, Giacomo Rossi-Stuart, Ángela Cavo, Ennio Balbo

Overview: Caterina finds out that her husband Peter Tzar of Russia, is plotting to kill her. She sets Count Orlov free from prison, Peter's sworn enemy, becomes empress of Russia and leads the Cossacks army against him.
