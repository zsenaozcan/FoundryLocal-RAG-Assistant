Title: Samson and Delilah (1996)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: Nicolas Roeg
Main Cast: Eric Thal, Elizabeth Hurley, Dennis Hopper, Diana Rigg, Ben Becker

Overview: Mara and Manoa are both upstanding and religious Israelites living under the harsh and unjust rule of the Philistines. One day, a mysterious stranger appears to Mara and promises her that she will bear a son whom she is to call Samson.
