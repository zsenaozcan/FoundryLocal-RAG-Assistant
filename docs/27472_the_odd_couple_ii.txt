Title: The Odd Couple II (1998)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Howard Deutch
Main Cast: Jack Lemmon, Walter Matthau, Richard Riehle, Jonathan Silverman, Lisa Waltz
Keywords: road trip, wedding date
