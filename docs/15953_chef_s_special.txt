Title: Chef's Special (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Nacho G. Velilla
Main Cast: Javier Cámara, Lola Dueñas, Fernando Tejero, Benjamín Vicuña, Luis Varela

Overview: Maxi thinks is life is perfect. He is a famous cook who own a top hype restaurant in Chueca. He is living his homosexual life without complex. But when his son Edu and his daughter Alba appears, and comes a new neighbor which is an attractive ex famous Argentinian soccer player, they will punch his regular life and change his valors.
