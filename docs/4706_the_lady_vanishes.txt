Title: The Lady Vanishes (1979)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Anthony Page
Main Cast: Cybill Shepherd, Elliott Gould, Angela Lansbury, Herbert Lom, Arthur Lowe
Keywords: spy, secret agent, search for witnesses, train

Overview: Remake of Alfred Hitchcock's 1938 classic. On the eve of the Second World War, a train carrying an assortment of passengers, pulls out of a small town in Bavaria. When one of the passengers, a kindly old lady, mysteriously disappears the other passengers are led into confrontation with the Nazis and a desperate race for freedom.
