Title: Home Among Strangers, a Stranger Among His Own (1974)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Nikita Mikhalkov
Main Cast: Yuri Bogatyryov, Nikita Mikhalkov, Sergey Shakurov, Aleksandr Kalyagin, Aleksandr Kaydanovskiy

Overview: The setting is post-Russian Civil War, during the reconstruction of the young Soviet republic. During the war, Shilov, Sarichev, Kungorov, Zabelin and Lipyagin had become great friends.
