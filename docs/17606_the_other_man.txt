Title: The Other Man (2008)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery, Romance
Director: Richard Eyre
Main Cast: Romola Garai, Antonio Banderas, Laura Linney, Liam Neeson, Abigail Canton
Keywords: woman between two men, betrayal

Overview: The story of a husband who suspects his wife of adultery, and sets out to track down the other man in her life.
