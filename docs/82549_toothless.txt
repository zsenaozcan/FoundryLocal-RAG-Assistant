Title: Toothless (1997)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Romance
Director: Melanie Mayron
Main Cast: Kirstie Alley, Dale Midkiff, Ross Malinger, Lynn Redgrave, Eileen Brennan
Keywords: afterlife, tooth fairy, dentist, woman director

Overview: A female dentist is cast into limbo after her death in an accident and is given the assignment to act as the Tooth Fairy as her action to be admitted into heaven.
