Title: Grey Gardens (1975)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Albert Maysles
Main Cast: Edith Bouvier Beale, Brooks Hyers, Norman Vincent Peale, Jack Helmuth, Albert Maysles
Keywords: woman director

Overview: An old mother and her middle-aged daughter, the aunt and cousin of Jacqueline Kennedy Onassis, live their eccentric lives in a filthy, decaying mansion in East Hampton.
