Title: Tales from the Vienna Woods (1979)
Genres: Drama
Director: Maximilian Schell
Main Cast: Helmut Qualtinger, Birgit Doll, Hanno Pöschl, Jane Tilden, André Heller
Keywords: literature, love affair, swimming, family affairs, danube, lower austria, literaturverfilmung, wachau, heuriger

Overview: Based on a popular 1931 play, the film tells the fate of a naive young woman named Marianne, who breaks off her reluctant engagement with Oskar the butcher after falling in love with a fop named Alfred who, however, has no serious interest in returning her love. For this error, she must pay bitterly.
