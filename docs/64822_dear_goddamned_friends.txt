Title: Dear Goddamned Friends (1994)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mario Monicelli
Main Cast: Paolo Villaggio, Paolo Hendel, Massimo Ceccherini, Vittorio Rap, Marco Graziani

Overview: In 1944 in Tuscany, a group of boxers facing the disasters of the Second World War, during the intrusive presence of the Germans and the Americans, organizes rigged matches to make some money and travel from town to town, hoping to participate in local fairs. A young black American soldier, believed missing, and a girl who refused to marry accompany the ramshackle group.
