Title: King Richard And The Crusaders (1954)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Music
Director: David Butler
Main Cast: Rex Harrison, Virginia Mayo, George Sanders, Laurence Harvey, Robert Douglas
Keywords: saladin, king richard

Overview: Based on Sir Walter Scott's The Talisman, this is the story of the romantic adventures of Christians and Muslims during the battle for the Holy Land in the time of King Richard the Lionheart.
