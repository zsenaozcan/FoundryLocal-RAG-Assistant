Title: The Comedy of Terrors (1963)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Jacques Tourneur
Main Cast: Vincent Price, Peter Lorre, Boris Karloff, Joyce Jameson, Joe E. Brown
Keywords: undertaker

Overview: An undertaker who hasn't had any 'customers' in a long time is forced the pay one year's back-rent. To get money he starts to kill people in order to get new clients.
