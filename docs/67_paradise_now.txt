Title: Paradise Now (2005)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: Hany Abu-Assad
Main Cast: Ali Suliman, Amer Hlehel, Hiam Abbass, Ashraf Barhom, Mohammad Bustami
Keywords: individual, terror, civil war, israel, palestine, middle east, muslim, middle east conflict, friendship, suicide mission

Overview: Paradise Now is filmed from the perspective of two Palestinian men who are preparing to perform a suicide attack in Israel. This is the first film to deal with the subject of suicide bombers.
