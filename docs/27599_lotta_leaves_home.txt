Title: Lotta Leaves Home (1993)
Genres: Family
Director: Johanna Hald
Main Cast: Grete Havnesköld, Linn Gloppestad, Beatrice Järås, Claes Malmberg, Martin Andersson
Keywords: children, woman director

Overview: A feisty five-year-old girl Lotta decides to move away from home. And no, she doesn't want to put on the stupid sweater.
