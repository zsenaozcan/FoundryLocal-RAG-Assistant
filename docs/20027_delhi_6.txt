Title: Delhi-6 (2009)
Genres: Action, Drama, Foreign
Director: Rakeysh Omprakash Mehra
Main Cast: Abhishek Bachchan, Sonam Kapoor, Waheeda Rehman, Rishi Kapoor, Om Puri
Keywords: bollywood
