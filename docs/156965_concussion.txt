Title: Concussion (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Stacie Passon
Main Cast: Robin Weigert, Maggie Siff, Johnathan Tchaikovsky, Julie Fain Lawrence, Laila Robins
Keywords: lesbian, woman director

Overview: After a blow to the head, Abby decides she can't do it anymore. Her life just can't be only about the house, the kids and the wife. She needs more: she needs to be Eleanor.
