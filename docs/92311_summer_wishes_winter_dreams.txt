Title: Summer Wishes, Winter Dreams (1973)
Genres: Drama
Director: Gilbert Cates
Main Cast: Joanne Woodward, Martin Balsam, Sylvia Sidney, Tresa Hughes, Dori Brenner
Keywords: gay son

Overview: Rita, a middle aged New York City homemaker, finds herself in an emotional crisis which forces her to re-examine her life, as well as her relationships with her mother, her eye doctor husband, her alienated daughter and estranged son.
