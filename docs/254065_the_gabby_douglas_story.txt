Title: The Gabby Douglas Story (2014)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Gregg Champion
Main Cast: Regina King, S. Epatha Merkerson, Imani Hakim, Sydney Mikayla, Brian Tee
Keywords: gymnastics, olympic games

Overview: The true story of Gabby Douglas who becomes the first African American to be named Individual All-Around Champion in artistic gymnastics at the Olympic Games.
