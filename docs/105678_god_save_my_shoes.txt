Title: God Save My Shoes (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Julie Benasra
Main Cast: Manolo Blahnik, Fergie, Christian Louboutin, Dita Von Teese, Kelly Rowland
Keywords: woman director

Overview: God Save My Shoes is the first documentary film to explore the intimate relationship between women and shoes, questioning why shoes are the most addictive item in a woman's closet and how shoes have become a totem object.
