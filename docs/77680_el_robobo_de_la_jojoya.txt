Title: El robobo de la jojoya (1991)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Álvaro Sáenz de Heredia
Main Cast: Josema Yuste, Millán Salcedo, Esther Del Prado, Anabel Alonso, Valeriano Andrés
Keywords: prison, brother brother relationship, robbery, museum, cop, art gallery, jewel heist, jewel thief

Overview: Two brothers plan to steal the precious jewel of Nefertiti's eye in a famous exhibition hall, but they are not the only ones who want it. There is a scuffle in which a policeman dies and one of the robbers falls on the dead man.
