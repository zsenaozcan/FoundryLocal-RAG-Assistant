Title: Those Daring Young Men in Their Jaunty Jalopies (1969)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Ken Annakin
Main Cast: Bourvil, Lando Buzzanca, Walter Chiari, Peter Cook, Tony Curtis
Keywords: cheating, smuggling, automobile racing, monte carlo rally

Overview: Sequel to "Those Magnificent Men In Their Flying Machines". This time an international car rally from England to Monte Carlo provides the comedic farce.
