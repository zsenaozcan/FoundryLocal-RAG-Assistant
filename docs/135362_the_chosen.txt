Title: The chosen (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Theu Boermans
Main Cast: Pierre Bokma, Kees Prins, Tijn Docter, Katja Herbers, Monic Hendrickx
Keywords: brotherhood, fraud, betrayal, religious fundamentalism

Overview: The two brothers Van der Laan, of strict religion, have been running Holland's most successful software company for 10 years when suddenly their financial manager leaves the firm. The lives of the brothers and their families get turned upside down when they hire a young hot shot financial specialist, in order to pursue their goal to bring the company to the NASDAQ.
