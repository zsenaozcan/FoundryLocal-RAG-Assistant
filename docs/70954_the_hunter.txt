Title: The Hunter (2011)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Adventure
Director: Daniel Nettheim
Main Cast: Willem Dafoe, Frances O'Connor, Sam Neill, Jacek Koman, Callan Mulvey
Keywords: hunter, mercenary, wilderness, little boy, crying, sole survivor, tasmanian tiger

Overview: Martin, a mercenary, is sent from Europe by an anonymous biotech company to the Tasmanian wilderness on a hunt for the last Tasmanian tiger.
