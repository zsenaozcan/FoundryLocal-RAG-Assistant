Title: The Naughty Flirt (1931)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Edward F. Cline
Main Cast: Alice White, Paul Page, Myrna Loy, Robert Agnew, Douglas Gilmore
Keywords: socialite

Overview: Coquettish socialite Kay Elliott has set her cap for Alan Ward, an associate in her father's law firm. But while pursuing Alan, she must fend off the advances of a greedy fortune-hunter, who is aided and abetted by his equally voracious sister.
