Title: Burnt (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: John Wells
Main Cast: Bradley Cooper, Sienna Miller, Lily James, Alicia Vikander, Uma Thurman
Keywords: london england, cook, restaurant, diva, career, kitchen, redemption, drug, drug addict, chef
