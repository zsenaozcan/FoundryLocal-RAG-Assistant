Title: The Only Son (1936)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Main Cast: Chôko Iida, Shin'ichi Himori, Masao Hayama, Yoshiko Tsubouchi, Mitsuko Yoshikawa
Keywords: tokyo japan, mother son relationship, 1930s

Overview: A poor factory worker, visiting the Tokyo son she has skimped to educate, finds even a college degree doesn't make a difference in Depression era Japan.
