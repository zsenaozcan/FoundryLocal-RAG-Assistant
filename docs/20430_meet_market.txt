Title: Meet Market (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Charlie Loventhal
Main Cast: Krista Allen, Elizabeth Berkley, Susan Egan, Suzanne Krull, Julian McMahon
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Lonely aspirants on the LA dating scene hook up at thier local grocery store, where they can see and be seen by some of their dubiously eligible fellow singles, all the while hoping for that one magical relationship that will finally click.
