Title: Eating Out: Drama Camp (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Q. Allan Brocka
Main Cast: Chris Salvatore, Daniel Skelton, Aaron Milo, Ronnie Kroell, Harmony Santana
Keywords: gay

Overview: With raging boy hormones and the laugh quotient turned on full, Chris Salvatore and Daniel Skelton return, along with Rebekah Kochan and cult icon Mink Stole in the fourth installment of Q. Allan Brocka's hilarious and over-the-top sexual antic-laden Eating Out series!
