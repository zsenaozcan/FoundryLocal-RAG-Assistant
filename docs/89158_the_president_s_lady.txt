Title: The President's Lady (1953)
Genres: Drama
Director: Henry Levin
Main Cast: Susan Hayward, Charlton Heston, John McIntire, Fay Bainter, Ruth Attaway
Keywords: president, romance, historical, andrew jackson

Overview: The story of president Andrew Jackson from his early years, through his meeting with and subsequent marriage to Rachel Donelson Robards. The plot concentrates on the later scandal concerning the legality of their marriage and how they overcame the difficulties.
