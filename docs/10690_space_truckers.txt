Title: Space Truckers (1996)
Genres: Comedy, Science Fiction, Thriller, Adventure
Director: Stuart Gordon
Main Cast: Dennis Hopper, Stephen Dorff, Debi Mazar, George Wendt, Charles Dance
Keywords: killer robot, space war, dangerous, state of emergency

Overview: John Canyon is one of the last independent space transport entrepreneurs. Rough times force him to carry suspicious cargo to Earth without questions being asked. During the flight the cargo turns out to be multitude of unstoppable and deadly killer robots.
