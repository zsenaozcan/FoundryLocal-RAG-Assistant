Title: April Story (1998)
Genres: Romance
Director: Shunji Iwai
Main Cast: 松隆子, Seiichi Tanabe, Kazuhiko Kato, Rumi, Kanji Tsuda

Overview: In spring, a girl leaves the island of Hokkaido to attend university in Tokyo. Once there, she is asked to reveal why she wanted to go there in the first place.
