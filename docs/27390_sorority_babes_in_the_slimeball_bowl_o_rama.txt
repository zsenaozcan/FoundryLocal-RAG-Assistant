Title: Sorority Babes in the Slimeball Bowl-O-Rama (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: David DeCoteau
Main Cast: Linnea Quigley, Andras Jones, Robin Stille, Brinke Stevens, Michelle Bauer
Keywords: female nudity, bowling, robbery, monster, deafness, shower, ax, cut-off head, wish, biker

Overview: When three college guys get caught spying on a sorority ritual, they're forced to accompany the pledges on their next assignment: stealing a trophy from a bowling alley. But the token they pinch has a devilish imp who makes their lives a living hell.
