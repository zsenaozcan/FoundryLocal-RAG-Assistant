Title: Ghostheads (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Brenden Mertens
Main Cast: Robin Shelby, Dan Aykroyd, Sigourney Weaver, Ivan Reitman, Paul Feig
Keywords: fandom

Overview: Ghostheads is a documentary that explores the extreme side of the Ghostbusters fandom. Join us as we travel the world meeting extreme Ghostbusters fans. Every Ghosthead is unique. Every Franchise is its own. Every pop culture fandom should learn how to give back to the community.
