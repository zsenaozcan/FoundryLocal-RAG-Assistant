Title: 2:22 (2008)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Phillip Guzman
Main Cast: Robert Miano, Aaron Gallagher, Jorge A. Jimenez, Peter Dobson, Mick Rossi
Keywords: suspect, delinquent, crime boss, criminal

Overview: The plan was easy; the job was not. On a snowy night a tight crew of four criminals plan to pull off a routine heist. When things go horribly wrong, friendship, loyalty and trust are pushed to the limit.
