Title: They're a Weird Mob (1966)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michael Powell
Main Cast: Walter Chiari, Claire Dunne, Chips Rafferty, Alida Chelli, Ed Devereaux

Overview: Nino Culotta is an Italian immigrant, newly arrived in Australia. Much of the story is taken up with Nino's attempts to understand the aspirational values and social rituals of everyday urban Australians of the 1950's and 60's, and assimilate.
