Title: The Jade Mask (1945)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Phil Rosen
Main Cast: Sidney Toler, Mantan Moreland, Edwin Luke, Hardie Albright, Frank Reicher
Keywords: detective, charlie chan

Overview: The latest assignment for respected yet goofy detective Charlie Chan has come directly from the government and involves the disappearance of a scientist named Harper, who was working on an extremely important serum. When the scientist is killed, Chan must sort through a crazy cast of characters who are all very likely suspects, including the man's sister and his butler.
