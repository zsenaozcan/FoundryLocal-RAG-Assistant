Title: Colt Comrades (1943)
Genres: Action, Western, Adventure
Director: Lesley Selander
Main Cast: William Boyd, Andy Clyde, Jay Kirby, Teddi Sherman, Victor Jory
Keywords: cattle rustling, hopalong cassidy

Overview: While oil drilling, Hoppy and California discover an underground well - a potential threat to Jebb Hardin's monopoly on water in the region.
