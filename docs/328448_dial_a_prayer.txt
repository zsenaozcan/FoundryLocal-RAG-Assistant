Title: Dial a Prayer (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Maggie Kiley
Main Cast: Brittany Snow, William H. Macy, Glenne Headly, Tom Lipinski, Stephanie Koenig
Keywords: prayer, call center, woman director

Overview: A troubled young woman working at a prayer call center makes a difference in other people's lives, forcing her to reconcile with her troubled past with the faith she brings out in others.
