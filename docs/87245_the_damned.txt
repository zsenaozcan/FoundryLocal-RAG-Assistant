Title: The Damned (1947)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: René Clément
Main Cast: Henri Vidal, Florence Marly, Fosco Giachetti, Paul Bernard, Jo Dest
Keywords: submarine, cat, mutiny, world war ii, nazis, murder, doctor, violence

Overview: Oslo, April 19th 1945, as the Third Reich is living its last day, a group of Nazis and sympathizers (a Wehrmacht general, an SS leader and his "assistant", an Italian industrialist and his wife who is also the general's lover, a French collaborator) board a submarine that will take them to South America, where they hope to find shelter. While they sail off the shores of liberated Royan they manage to kidnap a French doctor to have him look after a wounded passenger. Dr Guilbert will be forced to share the restricted space of the submarine with the fugitives. The atmosphere soon becomes unbreathable…
