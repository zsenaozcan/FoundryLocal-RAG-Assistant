Title: The Last Keepers (2013)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: Maggie Greenwald
Main Cast: Aidan Quinn, Virginia Madsen, Zosia Mamet, Olympia Dukakis, Sam Underwood
Keywords: witch, culture clash, artist, gallery, vanity project, woman director

Overview: This romantic fantasy movie is about two people who are the last survivors of an ancient line of goddess worshipers, who sell art at a shop. When their teenage daughter Rhea falls in love with a poetically inclined boy , she begins to develop magical powers of her own throwing everything into chaos.
