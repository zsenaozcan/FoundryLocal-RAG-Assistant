Title: Godfather (1991)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Lal
Main Cast: N.N. Pillai, Mukesh, Innocent, Philomina, Thilakan
Keywords: malayalam

Overview: The story of Anjooran (N. N. Pillai), and his four sons Balaraman (Thilakan), Swaminathan (Innocent), Premachandran (Bheeman Raghu) and Ramabhadran (Mukesh) are in severe enmity with the Anappara family.
