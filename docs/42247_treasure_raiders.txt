Title: Treasure Raiders (2007)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action, Thriller, Foreign
Director: Brent Huff
Main Cast: Alexander Nevsky, Steven Brand, David Carradine, Sherilyn Fenn, Robert Madrid

Overview: Michael, an American professor teaching history at Moscow University, finances his passion for treasure hunting with competitive street racing. His racing nemesis Wolf becomes his ally as they both embark on a quest to search for a famous ancient Russian treasure.
