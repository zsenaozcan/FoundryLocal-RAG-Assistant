Title: Guest of Cindy Sherman (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Tom Donahue
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: arts, photography, new york art world, art scene

Overview: Videographer Paul H-O must confront issues of ego and identity when he begins a relationship with the reclusive Cindy Sherman. With unprecedented access, the documentary places us in the company of the artist and offers a critique of the ever-inflated New York art market and the culture of celebrity. Written by Anonymous
