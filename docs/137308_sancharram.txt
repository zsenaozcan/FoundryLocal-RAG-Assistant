Title: Sancharram (2004)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Ligy J. Pullappally
Main Cast: Suhasini V. Nair, Shruthy Menon, K P A C Lalitha, Valsala Menon, Syam Seethal
Keywords: lesbian relationship

Overview: In Sancharram ("The Journey"), Kiran is mortified by her growing lesbian desire for the effervescent Delilah, in an idyllic Indian village where arranged marriage is the only acceptable form of coupling.
