Title: The Battle Of The Villa Fiorita (1965)
Genres: Drama
Director: Delmer Daves
Main Cast: Maureen O'Hara, Rossano Brazzi, Richard Todd, Phyllis Calvert, Martin Stephens
Keywords: villa, lake, children, pianist

Overview: Two British children travel to Italy in an attempt to break up their runaway mother's affair with an Italian concert pianist.
