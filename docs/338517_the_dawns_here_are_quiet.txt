Title: The Dawns Here Are Quiet (2015)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Renat Davletyarov
Main Cast: Pyotr Fyodorov, Anastasiya Mikulchina, Evgenia Malakhova, Agniya Kuznetsova, Sofia Lebedeva

Overview: It is late spring of 1942, and the Great Patriotic War is in full swing. A long way off from the front-line, at some God-forgotten junction, the Germans make an air landing operation in an attempt to get through to the Kirov railway and the White Sea - the Baltic Sea Canal. These aren't just ordinary paratroopers. This is a team of seasoned and highly trained infiltrators, the elite of the Waffen-SS, superhumans. The only thing in their way is an anti-aircraft artillery unit of corporal Vaskov and five young women in training. It may seem like a fight of local significance, but the countrys main strategic transportation artery is at stake. Can the corporal and his 'petite newbies' prevent Nazi sabotage and at what cost?
