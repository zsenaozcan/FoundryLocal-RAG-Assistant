Title: 100 Feet (2008)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Eric Red
Main Cast: Famke Janssen, Bobby Cannavale, Ed Westwick, Michael Paré, Patricia Charbonneau
Keywords: house arrest, haunting, vengeful ghost

Overview: After Marnie Watson kills her abusive husband in self-defense, she is condemned to house arrest... only to discover that the house is possessed by the enraged and violent spirit of her dead husband.
