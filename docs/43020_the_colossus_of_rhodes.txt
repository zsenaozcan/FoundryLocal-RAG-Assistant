Title: The Colossus of Rhodes (1961)
Genres: History, Adventure, Drama
Director: Sergio Leone
Main Cast: Rory Calhoun, Lea Massari, Georges Marchal, Conrado San Martín, Ángel Aranda
Keywords: greek mythology, plot

Overview: While on holiday in Rhodes, Athenian war hero Darios becomes involved in two different plots to overthrow the tyrannical king, one from Rhodian patriots and the other from sinister Phoenician agents.
