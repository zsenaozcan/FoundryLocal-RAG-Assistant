Title: Reincarnation (2005)
Genres: Horror
Director: Takashi Shimizu
Main Cast: Yûka, Kippei Shiina, Tetta Sugimoto, Shun Oguri, Marika Matsumoto

Overview: A Japanese actress begins having strange visions and experiences after landing a role in a horror film about a real-life murder spree that took place over forty years ago.
