Title: Into a Dream (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sion Sono
Main Cast: Tetsushi Tanaka, Yûna Natsuo, Joe Odagiri, Jun Murakami, Miwako Ichikawa

Overview: Low-profile theater troop actor Mutsugoro Suzuki begins an oneiric journey back to his hometown, marked by his quest to find the one responsible of infecting him with a STD.
