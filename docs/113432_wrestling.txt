Title: Wrestling (2007)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Grímur Hákonarson
Main Cast: Helga Braga Jónsdóttir, María Guðmundsdóttir, Halldór Gylfason, Björn Ingi Hilmarsson
Keywords: small town, iceland, wrestling, escape, rural setting, short

Overview: A love story about two gay wrestlers living in rural Iceland who must keep their relationship a secret from the inner world of Iceland's national and very macho sport.
