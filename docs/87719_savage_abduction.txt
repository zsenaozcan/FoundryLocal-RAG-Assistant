Title: Savage Abduction (1973)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: John Lawrence
Main Cast: Tom Drake, Steve Oliver, Joe Turkel, Sean Kenney, Amy Thomson
Keywords: blackmail, murder, serial killer, motorcycle gang

Overview: A serial killer murders a businessman's wife, then blackmails the man into procuring young girls for him to murder. The businessman hires a gang of renegade motor-cyclists to kidnap girls for him.
