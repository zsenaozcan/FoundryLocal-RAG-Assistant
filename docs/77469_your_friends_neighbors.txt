Title: Your Friends & Neighbors (1998)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Neil LaBute
Main Cast: Amy Brenneman, Aaron Eckhart, Ben Stiller, Nastassja Kinski, Jason Patric
Keywords: impotence, male friendship, extramarital affair, unhappiness

Overview: This adult comedy follows six characters, three men and three women from a cross-section of social groups, as they play sexual power games. When an affair fires up between 2 of the married characters, it sparks a chain of consequences for all of them, including one of the wives falling for another woman!
