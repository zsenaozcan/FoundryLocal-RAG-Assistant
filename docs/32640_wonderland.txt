Title: Wonderland (1999)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Winterbottom
Main Cast: Shirley Henderson, Gina McKee, Molly Parker, Ian Hart, John Simm

Overview: There's little wonder in the working-class lives of Bill, Eileen, and their three grown daughters. They're lonely Londoners. Nadia, a cafe waitress, places personal ads, looking for love; Debbie, a single mom, entertains men at the hair salon after hours; her son spends part of the weekend with her ex, a man with a hair-trigger temper. Molly is expecting her first baby and its father acts as if the responsibility is too much for him.
