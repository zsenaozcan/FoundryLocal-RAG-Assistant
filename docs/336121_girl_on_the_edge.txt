Title: Girl on the Edge (2015)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Jay Silverman
Main Cast: Taylor Spreitler, Peter Coyote, Gil Bellows, Amy Price-Francis, Shane Graham
Keywords: rape, trauma, online predator, alternative healing center, tramatic childhood, trama center
