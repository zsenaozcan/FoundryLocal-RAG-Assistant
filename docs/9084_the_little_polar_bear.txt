Title: The Little Polar Bear (2001)
Genres: Animation, Family, Foreign
Director: Piet De Rycker
Main Cast: Mijail Verona, Wolfgang Völz, Jeanette Biedermann, Dirk Bach, Mike Krüger
Keywords: polar bear, best friend, antarctic

Overview: This charming animated adventure follows a young polar bear, Lars, as he befriends Robbie, a seal. Together, these two form a friendship that proves different breeds of animals can get along perfectly well.
