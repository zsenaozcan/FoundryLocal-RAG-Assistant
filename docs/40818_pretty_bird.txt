Title: Pretty Bird (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Paul Schneider
Main Cast: Paul Giamatti, Daniel Stewart Sherman, Kristen Wiig, Elizabeth Marvel, Daniel Cameron Talbott
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A comic tale of three would-be entrepreneurs who set out to invent a rocket belt. The clash of their mismatched personalities soon dissolves the business into a morass of recriminations and retaliations, kidnapping, and murder in this parable of American dreams and delusions.
