Title: Begotten (1990)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror
Director: E. Elias Merhige
Main Cast: Brian Salzberg, Donna Dempsey, Stephen Charles Barry, James Gandia
Keywords: nihilism, razor, pregnancy and birth, bizarre, necrophilia, woods, surrealism, independent film, gore, experimental film

Overview: Begotten is the creation myth brought to life, the story of no less than the violent death of God and the (re)birth of nature on a barren earth.
