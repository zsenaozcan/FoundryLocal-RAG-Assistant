Title: Mother Joan of the Angels (1961)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: Jerzy Kawalerowicz
Main Cast: Lucyna Winnicka, Mieczysław Voit, Anna Ciepielewska, Maria Chwalibóg, Kazimierz Fabisiak
Keywords: nun, possession

Overview: A priest is sent to a small parish in the Polish countryside which is believed to be under demonic possession and there he finds his own temptations awaiting.
