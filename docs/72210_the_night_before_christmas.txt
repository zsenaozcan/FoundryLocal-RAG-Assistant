Title: The Night Before Christmas (1951)
Genres: Animation, Fantasy
Director: Valentina Brumberg
Main Cast: Vladimir Gribkov, Aleksei Gribov, Liliya Gritsenko
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A cartoon based on the works of Nikolay Gogol. Handsome blacksmith Vakula who fell in love with village beauty, Oksana, is ready to do anything for her liking. Even to fly on the devil's back to Saint-Petersburg and ask the Empress for the slippers that he can present his sweetheart.
