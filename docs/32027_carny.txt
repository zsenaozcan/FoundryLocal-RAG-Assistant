Title: Carny (1980)
Genres: Drama
Director: Robert Kaylor
Main Cast: Gary Busey, Jodie Foster, Robbie Robertson, Meg Foster, Kenneth McMillan
Keywords: waitress, hustler, carnival, games, travel

Overview: Tired of being a small-town waitress, Donna departs with the latest carnival show, living with entertainers Frankie and Patch in a tense, emotional triangle.
