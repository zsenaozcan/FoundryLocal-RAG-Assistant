Title: Easy Come, Easy Go (1967)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Music
Director: John Rich
Main Cast: Elvis Presley, Dodie Marshall, Pat Priest, Pat Harrington, Jr., Skip Ward
Keywords: diving, treasure, navy, musical

Overview: Lieutenant Ted Jackson spends his lat day on active duty in the Navy disarming an underwater mine. While disarming the explosive he sees what appears to be a sunken ship. Overcome with curiosity, he investigates and finds a treasure chest filled with what he thinks are Spanish gold coins.
