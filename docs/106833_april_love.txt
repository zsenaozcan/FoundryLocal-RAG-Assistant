Title: April Love (1957)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Henry Levin
Main Cast: Pat Boone, Shirley Jones, Arthur O'Connell, Dolores Michaels, Matt Crowley
Keywords: musical

Overview: A teenager (Pat Boone), recently in trouble with the police, is sent to live with his aunt and uncle on their Kentucky farm in order to rediscover life's values. Director Henry Levin's 1957 musical remake of "Home In Indiana" also stars Shirley Jones, Arthur O'Connell, Jeanette Nolan and Dolores Michaels.
