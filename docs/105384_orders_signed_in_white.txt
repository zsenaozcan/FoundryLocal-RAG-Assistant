Title: Orders Signed in White (1974)
Genres: Thriller, Crime
Director: Gianni Manera
Main Cast: Herb Andress, Paola Arduini, Lucy Chevalier, Ivano Davoli, Giovanni De Angelis
Keywords: heist, hideout, poliziottesco

Overview: A gang raid a bank, making off with a nice tidy sum and head off to a hideout in the mountains to take refuge (with a few women in tow to keep them company).
