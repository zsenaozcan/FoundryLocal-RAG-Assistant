Title: The Philadelphia Experiment (1984)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Stewart Raffill
Main Cast: Michael Paré, Nancy Allen, Eric Christmas, Bobby Di Cicco, Louise Latham
Keywords: experiment, intelligence, time travel, aftermath, biography, suspense, falling in love, philadelphia experiment

Overview: Based on an "actual event" that took place in 1943. About a US Navy Destroyer Escort that disappeared from the Philadelphia Naval Shipyard, and sent two men 40 years into the future to 1984.
