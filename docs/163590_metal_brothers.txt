Title: Metal Brothers (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ulf Malmros
Main Cast: Lotta Tejle, Johan Östling, Björn Starrin, Kjell Bergqvist, Mia Skäringer

Overview: Bröderna Hårdrock is a film about two brothers who are planning a heavy metal convention in their sleepy village. Their mother, the village priest, worries about them and wants them to settle down and have families. A young female teacher and line dance instructor in the village sets both their hearts beating faster and, unaware of each other's feelings, they enter a battle to win her affections.
