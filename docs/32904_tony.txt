Title: Tony (2010)
Genres: Horror, Drama, Thriller
Director: Gerard Johnson
Main Cast: Peter Ferdinando, Ricky Grover, Neil Maskell, Lorenzo Camporese, Vicky Murdock
Keywords: prostitute, isolation, frustration, pub, police, murder, loneliness, violence, strangulation, gay man

Overview: Unemployed and unemployable, Tony is a sympathetic recluse with severe social problems, an addiction to VHS action films and a horrible moustache. Occasionally he snaps and murder is the result…
