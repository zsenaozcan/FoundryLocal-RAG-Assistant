Title: Hush (2008)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Action
Director: Mark Tonderai
Main Cast: William Ash, Christine Bottomley, Andreas Wisniewski, Claire Keelan, Stuart McQuarrie
Keywords: sensor beam, mysterious truck, violence

Overview: A young couple on a motorway journey are drawn into a game of cat and mouse with a truck driver when they see something disturbing in the back of his vehicle.
