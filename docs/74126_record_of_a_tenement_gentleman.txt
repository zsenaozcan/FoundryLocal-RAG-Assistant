Title: Record of a Tenement Gentleman (1947)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Main Cast: Chôko Iida, Hohi Aoki, Mitsuko Yoshikawa, Chishu Ryu, Eitarô Ozawa
Keywords: working class, childhood friends, simple pleasures, war orphans

Overview: An errant salaryman's son gets lost until a man from the Tokyo tenements brings him to vendor Tane, who's reluctant to let the kid board.
