Title: Sparkle (1976)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Sam O'Steen
Main Cast: Philip Michael Thomas, Irene Cara, Lonette McKee, Dwan Smith, Mary Alice
Keywords: singer, drug, girl group

Overview: Three sisters (Sister and the Sisters) from Harlem who become singers Sister (Lonette McKee) becomes involved with drugs and Sparkle ends up being the one who gets famous. This movie tells about how drugs ruined Sister's relationships and eventually ended her life.
