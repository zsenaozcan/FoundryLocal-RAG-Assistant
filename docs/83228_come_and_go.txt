Title: Come and Go (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: João César Monteiro
Main Cast: João César Monteiro, Rita Pereira Marques, Joaquina Chicau, Manuela de Freitas, Lígia Soares
Keywords: bus ride, funeral, snow white

Overview: Driven by his struggle against the illness that would ultimately prove fatal, Monteiro fervently devoted himself to his deeply personal last work, a musically structured meditation on spirituality and desire in which he heroically cast himself in the lead role as an eccentric libertine drifting wide-eyed through a sun-drenched Lisbon. A consciously terminal film, Come and Go offers a poetic summation of Monteiro’s fascination with the body as the most irreverent and mysterious of temples, staging a series of playfully austere encounters between the visibly infirm and prematurely aged director and the women with whom he engages in erotic, linguistic and theological debates.
