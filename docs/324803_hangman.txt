Title: Hangman (2015)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Adam Mason
Main Cast: Jeremy Sisto, Kate Ashfield, Ty Simpkins, Ryan Simpkins, Amy Smart
Keywords: found footage

Overview: Returning from vacation, the Miller family find their home has been broken into. After cleaning up the mess they continue with their lives, shaking off the feeling of being violated. But little do they know the nightmare has just begun.
