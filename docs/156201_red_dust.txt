Title: Red Dust (1990)
Genres: War, Romance
Director: Yim Ho
Main Cast: Brigitte Lin, Maggie Cheung, Han Chin, Josephine Koo, Richard Ng

Overview: Stretching across the canvas of the Sino-Japanese War of the 30s, the subsequent Japanese surrender in 1945, and the onslaught of Communism, this film depicts an ill-fated romance between a talented lady novelist and a Chinese traitor working with the Japanese who fall victim to the mayhem of war and their tragic inability to reconcile political differences.
