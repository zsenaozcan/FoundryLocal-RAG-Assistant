Title: Eddie Izzard: Force Majeure Live (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Eddie Izzard

Overview: Force Majeure (French) (pronounced: fors mah-ZHur), "superior force", "chance occurrence, unavoidable accident": 2 years and 27 countries, Force Majeure Live was filmed during Eddie's mammoth 2013/2014 tour and takes you on a truly hilarious journey, offering a rare glimpse in to the mind of the master of surrealism.
