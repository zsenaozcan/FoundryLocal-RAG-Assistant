Title: Moscow Chill (2007)
Genres: Thriller, Action
Director: Chris Solimine
Main Cast: Norman Reedus, Konstantin Yushkevich, Slava Schoot, Xenia Buravsky, Aleksandr Lenkov

Overview: A loner American computer hacker is brought to Russia to commit bank fraud, only to find a family and love in the incomprehensible, violent, and chaotic Moscow underworld.
