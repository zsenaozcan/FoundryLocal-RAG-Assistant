Title: Trespassing (2004)
Genres: Horror
Director: James Merendino
Main Cast: Daniel Gillies, Estella Warren, Clayne Crawford, Maryam d'Abo, Jeff Galpin

Overview: A graduate student preparing his thesis on mythology leads his friends on a research expedition to an old plantation estate on the outskirts of the Big Easy. The site is reputed to mysteriously cause madness and death to all who enter it.
