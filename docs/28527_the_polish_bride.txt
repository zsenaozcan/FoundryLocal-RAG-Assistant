Title: The Polish Bride (1998)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Karim Traïdia
Main Cast: Monic Hendrickx, Jaap Spijkers, Roef Ragas

Overview: Drama on the dawning love between a Polish woman and a farmer. Anna is forced to work in a brothel, but manages to escape. She's found, exhausted and scared, by Henk (the farmer) who offers her a place to stay, but her past chases her.
