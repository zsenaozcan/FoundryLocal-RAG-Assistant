Title: Pumpkinhead (1988)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror
Director: Stan Winston
Main Cast: Lance Henriksen, Jeff East, John D'Aquino, Kimberly Ross, Joel Hoffman
Keywords: witch, cabin, death of a child, murder, pumpkin, demon, evil, storekeeper, vengeance

Overview: A man conjures up a gigantic vengeance demon called Pumpkinhead to destroy the teenagers who accidentally killed his son.
