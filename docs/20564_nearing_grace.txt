Title: Nearing Grace (2005)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Rick Rosenthal
Main Cast: Gregory Smith, Jordana Brewster, Ashley Johnson, David Moscow, Chad Faust
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Did you ever wish you could get the hottest girl in school? This is the story of how non-jock Henry (Gregory Smith) indeed gets the girl, Grace (Jordana Brewster), but not without complications.
