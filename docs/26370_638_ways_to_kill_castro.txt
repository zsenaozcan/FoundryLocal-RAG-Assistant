Title: 638 Ways to Kill Castro (2006)
Genres: History, Documentary, Foreign
Director: Dollan Cannell
Main Cast: Fidel Castro, Wayne Smith, Enrique Encinosa, Enrique Ovares, Fabian Escalante

Overview: Dollan Cannell's documentary on the hundreds of alleged plots to assassinate Fidel Castro, and a look at the evolution of Cuban politics. If the title of this extraordinary film sounds ludicrous, don't be fooled. This film looks at the incredible story of the 638 alleged plots by the CIA and Cuban exiles to kill the Cuban leader Fidel Castro.
