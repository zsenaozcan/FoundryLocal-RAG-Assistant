Title: The Vixen (1969)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance

Main Cast: Brigitte Bardot, Maurice Ronet, Christina Holme, Anny Duperey, Jean-Pierre Marielle
Keywords: sex, nudity, secretary, train, writer
