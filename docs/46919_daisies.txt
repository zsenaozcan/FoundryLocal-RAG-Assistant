Title: Daisies (1966)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Věra Chytilová
Main Cast: Ivana Karbanová, Jitka Cerhová, Marie Cesková, Jirina Myskova, Marcela Brezinova
Keywords: eroticism, feminism, collage, farce, teenage girl, flapper, woman director, repeated line, phallic symbol, dumbwaiter

Overview: Two teenage girls, both named Marie, decide that since the world is spoiled they will be spoiled as well; accordingly they embark on a series of destructive pranks in which they consume and destroy the world about them. This freewheeling, madcap feminist farce was immediately banned by the government.
