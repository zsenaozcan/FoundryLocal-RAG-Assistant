Title: Breakdown Lane (2017)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Bob Schultz
Main Cast: Whitney Moore, William 'Bill' Connor, Owen Conway, Monica Engesser, Clint James
Keywords: infection, undead, survival, zombie, desert, suv, zombie apocalypse, coyote, dehydration

Overview: When Kirby Lane's SUV breaks down in the middle of the desert, she must overcome the dehydration, coyotes, and lurking undead to find her way home.
