Title: Rhino Season (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bahman Ghobadi
Main Cast: Behrouz Vossoughi, Monica Bellucci, Yılmaz Erdoğan, Caner Cindoruk, Belcim Bilgin Erdogan
Keywords: iranian, kurdish, released from prison, estranged, reconnect

Overview: Kurdish-Iranian poet Sahel has just been released from a thirty-year prison sentence in Iran. Now the one thing keeping him going is the thought of finding his wife, who thinks he's been dead for over twenty years.
