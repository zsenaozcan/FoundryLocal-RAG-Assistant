Title: Bill Hicks: Relentless (1992)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Chris Bould
Main Cast: Bill Hicks
Keywords: comedian, stand-up comedy, cult comedy, observational comedy, live comedy, dark comedy

Overview: Outspoken and raw, not mincing matters, Bill Hicks tells us that, just like smokers, non-smokers also die every day - with jogging instructor Jim Fixx as an example -, asks women what's so terrible about giving head, talks us through what really happened during the 1990-1991 Gulf War and how the used technology could have also been used to feed the poor of the world.
