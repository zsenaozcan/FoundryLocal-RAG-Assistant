Title: Back Stage (1919)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Roscoe 'Fatty' Arbuckle
Main Cast: Roscoe 'Fatty' Arbuckle, Buster Keaton, Al St. John, Molly Malone

Overview: Roscoe and Buster give a bullying Strongman the what-for, and after the performance troupe quits it's up to Fatty and Buster and St. John to keep the show going.
