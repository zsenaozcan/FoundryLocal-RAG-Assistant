Title: Broadway Melody of 1940 (1940)
Genres: Music, Romance
Director: Norman Taurog
Main Cast: Fred Astaire, Eleanor Powell, George Murphy, Frank Morgan, Ian Hunter
Keywords: musical

Overview: Johnny Brett and King Shaw are an unsuccessful dance team in New York. A producer discovers Brent as the new partner for Clare Bennett, but Brett, who thinks he is one of the people they lent money to gives him the name of his partner.
