Title: Lucky Miles (2007)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Michael James Rowland
Main Cast: Geoff Morrell, Rithy Dourng, Deborah Mailman
Keywords: immigration

Overview: It's 1990 and an Indonesian fishing boat abandons Iraqi and Cambodian refugees in a remote part of the Western Australia. Although most are quickly caught by officials, three men with nothing in common but their misfortune and determination to escape arrest, begin an epic journey into the heart of Australia.
