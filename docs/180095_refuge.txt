Title: Refuge (2013)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Andrew Robertson
Main Cast: Sebastian Beacon, Chris Kies, Carter Roy, Amy Rutberg, Eva Grace Kellner
Keywords: post-apocalyptic

Overview: A post-apocalyptic thriller set in the ruins of a collapsed America, Refuge follows a family’s struggle to survive and rebuild a life in the absence of civilization. In the wake of a catastrophic plague, the family lives a lonely existence, struggling to maintain a sense of normalcy amidst a lawless world of roaming gangs and dwindling resources.
