Title: Dirty Linen (1999)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Mario Monicelli
Main Cast: Gigi Proietti, Paolo Bonacelli, Marina Confalone, Alessandro Haber, Ornella Muti

Overview: The Razzi family owns a small factory of sweets in Macerata. Their desire to enlarge their business to cope with the new Unified European Market brings to a string of fatal mistakes that will ultimately doom their wealth.
