Title: Arranged (2007)
Genres: Drama, Action, Comedy, Thriller, Romance
Director: Stefan C. Schaefer
Main Cast: Zoe Lister-Jones, Francis Benhamou
Keywords: roman, romance, woman director

Overview: ARRANGED centers on the friendship between an Orthodox Jewish woman and a Muslim woman who meet as first-year teachers at a public school in Brooklyn. Over the course of the year they learn they share much in common - not least of which is that they are both going through the process of arranged marriages.
