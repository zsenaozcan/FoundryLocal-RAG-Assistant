Title: Sallie Gardner at a Gallop (1878)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Eadweard Muybridge
Main Cast: Sallie Gardner, Gilbert Domm
Keywords: horse, stop motion, animation, black and white, silent film, short, chronophotography

Overview: Sallie Gardner at a Gallop was one of the earliest silent films — a production experiment by the photographer Eadward Muybridge on June 15, 1878 that led to the development of motion pictures. The motion picture consists of 24 photographs in a fast-motion series that were shown on a zoopraxiscope.
