Title: Night of the Living Dorks (2004)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Mathias Dinter
Main Cast: Tino Mewes, Thomas Schmieder, Manuel Cortez, Collien Ulmen-Fernandes, Hendrik Borgmann
Keywords: voodoo, car crash, morgue, zombie

Overview: Three not-so-cool school friends decide to try a old voodoo ritual. Later, they die in a car accident, but live on as zombies. But being a zombie has advantages, too...
