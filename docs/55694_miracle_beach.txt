Title: Miracle Beach (1992)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Science Fiction, Romance
Director: Skott Snider
Main Cast: Dean Cameron, Ami Dolenz, Felicity Waterman, Brian Perry, Alexis Arquette
Keywords: beach, wish, bottle, bikini, contest

Overview: A romantic comedy set in the Southern California beach scene. Down on his luck, Scotty McKay becomes the master of a very lonely genie, who brings him wealth, power and true love. Miracles can happen!
