Title: The Dependent (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Leonardo Favio
Main Cast: Walter Vidarte, Graciela Borges, Nora Cullen, Fernando Iglesias 'Tacholas', Martín Andrade
Keywords: clerk, romance, loneliness, lonely man

Overview: Fernandez is a lonely man leading a lonely life. All he does is work for an old man in a hardware store. But all that changes, when he meets the girl of his dreams... and her family.
