Title: Blood and Sand (1989)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Javier Elorrieta
Main Cast: Sharon Stone, Christopher Rydell, Ana Torrent, Guillermo Montesinos, Albert Vidal
Keywords: spain, adultery, bullfight, based on novel, nudity

Overview: Remake of the tale of an acclaimed matador who finds himself involved with a beautiful woman, jeopardizing both his marriage and career.
