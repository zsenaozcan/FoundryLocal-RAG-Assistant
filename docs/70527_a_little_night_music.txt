Title: A Little Night Music (2002)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Dejan Zečević
Main Cast: Гордан Кичић, Vuk Kostić, Feđa Stojanović, Vlastimir Velisavljević, Dragan Petrović
Keywords: swimming pool, mafia, mortuary

Overview: Spasoje and Cole are cute and silly boys who constantly find themselves in risky situations. They work in a morgue. One night they heard mobile phone that rings in your pocket just dead guy. Of course, they can not resist, and answer the call. On the other side man voice ordered them to go to a specific address and get some money. Cole and Spasoje don't reject easy profit...
