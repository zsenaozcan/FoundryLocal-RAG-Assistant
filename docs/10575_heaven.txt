Title: Heaven (2002)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Romance, Crime
Director: Tom Tykwer
Main Cast: Cate Blanchett, Giovanni Ribisi, Remo Girone, Stefania Rocca, Alessandro Sperduti
Keywords: suicide, bomb, assassination, sex, terrorist, nudity, court, police, love, revenge

Overview: A woman takes the law into her own hands after police ignore her pleas to arrest the man responsible for her husband's death, and finds herself not only under arrest for murder but falling in love with an officer.
