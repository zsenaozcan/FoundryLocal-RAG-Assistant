Title: Office Romance. Our time (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Sarik Andreasyan
Main Cast: Vladimir Zelenskiy, Svetlana Khodchenkova, Marat Basharov, Anastasiya Zavorotnyuk, Pavel Volya

Overview: Anatoli Yefremovich Novoseltsev works in a ratings company, whose director is an unattractive and bossy woman. An old friend of his, Yuri Grigorievich Samokhvalov, who gets appointed assistant director, wants to make Novoseltsev the manager but encounters objections from Ludmila Prokopievna Kalugina, the director. Samokhvalov then advises Novoseltsev to lightly hit on the boss. Ironically, Novoseltsev and Kalugina fall in love with each other...
