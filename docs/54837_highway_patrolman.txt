Title: Highway Patrolman (1991)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Alex Cox
Main Cast: Roberto Sosa, Bruno Bichir, Vanessa Bauche, Zaide Silvia Gutiérrez, Pedro Armendáriz Jr.
Keywords: mexico, patrol, drug cartel

Overview: A naïve rookie in the Mexican highway patrol has to adapt to survive as he contends with dangerous drug-runners and widespread corruption.
