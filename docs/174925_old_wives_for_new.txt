Title: Old Wives for New (1918)
Genres: Drama
Director: Cecil B. DeMille
Main Cast: Elliott Dexter, Florence Vidor, Sylvia Ashton, Wanda Hawley, Theodore Roberts

Overview: Charles Murdock neglects his fat and lazy wife in favor of Juliet Raeburn but, when Juliet's name is involved in murder, he marries Viola and takes her to Paris.
