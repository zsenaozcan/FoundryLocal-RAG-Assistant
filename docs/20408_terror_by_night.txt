Title: Terror by Night (1946)
Genres: Adventure, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Roy William Neill
Main Cast: Basil Rathbone, Nigel Bruce, Alan Mowbray, Dennis Hoey, Renee Godfrey
Keywords: mathematics, sherlock holmes, mystery, playing gin, gin rummy

Overview: Holmes is hired by Roland Carstairs to prevent the theft of the Star of Rhodesia, an enormous diamond owned by Carstairs' mother, Lady Margaret. Believing the diamond will be stolen on a train trip from London to Edinburgh, Holmes deftly switches diamonds with Lady Margaret while in her compartment. Soon after, Roland is murdered and the fake diamond is stolen.
