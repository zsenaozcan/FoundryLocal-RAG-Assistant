Title: The Honey Pot (1967)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Comedy, Thriller
Director: Joseph L. Mankiewicz
Main Cast: Rex Harrison, Susan Hayward, Capucine, Maggie Smith, Cliff Robertson
Keywords: greed, murder, inheritance, stage performance

Overview: In Venice, the millionaire benefactor Cecil Fox watches a Seventeenth Century play 'Volpone' and plots a practical joke to play on his three former greedy mistresses. He hires the unemployed actor William McFly to act as his butler and stage manager and sends letters telling the mistresses he is terminally ill. The prime intention of Rex is to see the reaction of the women after the reading of his will but things do not go as planned.
