Title: Legendary (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mel Damski
Main Cast: John Cena, Patricia Clarkson, Devon Graye, Danny Glover, Madeleine Martin
Keywords: wrestling, sport, independent film, family relationships, family reunion

Overview: A book-smart teenager joins his school's wrestling team as a way to reunite his surviving family members, who split apart after the death of his father, a college wrestling legend, 10 years ago.
