Title: Scream, Pretty Peggy (1973)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery, Thriller, TV Movie
Director: Gordon Hessler
Main Cast: Ted Bessell, Sian Barbara Allen, Bette Davis, Charles Drake, Allan Arbus

Overview: A sculptor hires young college girls to take care of his elderly mother and his supposedly insane sister, both of whom live in the old family mansion with him.
