Title: Remains (2011)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Action, Thriller
Director: Colin Theys
Main Cast: Grant Bowler, Miko Hughes, Lance Reddick, Tawny Cypress, Evalena Marie

Overview: The story centers on two lone survivors of a bizarre accident that reduced most of the world's population to zombies. They take refuge in a vacant casino and fight a losing battle against the undead.
