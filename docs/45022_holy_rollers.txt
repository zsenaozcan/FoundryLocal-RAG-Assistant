Title: Holy Rollers (2010)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Kevin Asch
Main Cast: Jesse Eisenberg, Justin Bartha, Danny A. Abeckaser, Ari Graynor, Jason Fuchs

Overview: Inspired by actual events in the late nineties when Hasidic Jews were recruited as mules to smuggle ecstasy from Europe into the United States.
