Title: The Devil's in the Details (2013)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Waymon Boone
Main Cast: Ray Liotta, Emilio Rivera, Joel Mathews, Raymond J. Barry, Noel Gugliemi

Overview: 7 minutes from now in Nogales, Arizona, Thomas Conrad's life will forever change when he gets caught up in a Mexican cartels poetic game of drug mule chess.
