Title: You Wont Miss Me (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Ry Russo-Young
Main Cast: Stella Schnabel, Gina Abatemarco, Nick Ackerman, Carlen Altman, David Anzuelo
Keywords: independent film, woman director

Overview: A kaleidoscopic film portrait of Shelly Brown, a twenty-three year-old alienated urban misfit recently released from a psychiatric hospital.
