Title: Dead of Night (1974)
Genres: Horror
Director: Bob Clark
Main Cast: John Marley, Lynn Carlin, Richard Backus, Henderson Forsythe, Anya Ormsby
Keywords: suicide, vietnam veteran, vampire, murder, independent film, drive-in theatre, blood, hitchhiker, zombie, soldier

Overview: A young soldier is killed in the line of duty in Vietnam. That same night, he returns home, brought back by his mother's wishes. Upon his return, Andy sits in his room, refusing to see his friends or family, venturing out only at night. The vampiric horror is secondary to the terror that comes from the disintegration of a typical American family.
