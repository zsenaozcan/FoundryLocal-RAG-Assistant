Title: When Ladies Meet (1933)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Harry Beaumont
Main Cast: Ann Harding, Robert Montgomery, Myrna Loy, Alice Brady, Frank Morgan
Keywords: widow, writer, publisher

Overview: Mary, a writer working on a novel about a love triangle, is attracted to her publisher. Her suitor Jimmy is determined to break them up; he introduces Mary to the publisher's wife without telling Mary who she is.
