Title: Cemetery Without Crosses (1969)
Genres: Western
Director: Robert Hossein
Main Cast: Robert Hossein, Michèle Mercier, Guido Lollobrigida, Daniele Vargas, Serge Marquand
Keywords: kidnapping, horse, murder, gunfight, spaghetti western, street shootout, evil man

Overview: Maria seeks revenge on the killers of her husband. She enlists the help of her husband's best friend, Manual, a reluctant, but skilled, gunfighter.
