Title: Harvest (1999)
Genres: Drama
Director: Stuart Burkin
Main Cast: Jeffrey DeMunn, John Slattery, James Van Der Beek, Frederick Weller, Mary McCormack

Overview: A DEA agent and a local sheriff have to wrestle with their consciences as they start raids on local farmers, who have started growing marijuana simply to keep their farms operational. Story focuses on a young man, who accidentally discovers that his straight-laced parents are involved in the marijuana cultivation.
