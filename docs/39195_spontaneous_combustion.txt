Title: Spontaneous Combustion (1990)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Tobe Hooper
Main Cast: Brad Dourif, Cynthia Bain, Jon Cypher, André De Toth, John Landis
Keywords: spontaneous combustion

Overview: A young man finds out that his parents had been used in an atomic-weapons experiment shortly before he was born, and that the results have had some unexpected effects on him.
