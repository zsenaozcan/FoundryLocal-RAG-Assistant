Title: Kammatipaadam (2016)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Rajeev Ravi
Main Cast: Dulquer Salmaan, Vinayakan, Manikandan R Achari, Anil Nedumangad, Shine Tom Chacko
Keywords: bodyguard, friendship, brother against brother, city, malayalam, accident, kochi

Overview: Krishnan, who has been living away from his family and friends in Mumbai, gets a call from his friend Ganga in Kerala, out of the blue. He senses danger, and leaves for Kammattipadam, where both of them grew up.
