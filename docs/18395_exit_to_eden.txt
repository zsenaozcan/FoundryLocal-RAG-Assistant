Title: Exit to Eden (1994)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy, Crime, Romance
Director: Garry Marshall
Main Cast: Dana Delany, Paul Mercurio, Rosie O'Donnell, Dan Aykroyd, Héctor Elizondo
Keywords: female nudity, sexuality, island, love, mistress, kinky, smuggler, bdsm

Overview: Elliot is going to the island of Eden to live out his submissive fantasies, but inadvertently photographs diamond smugglers at work. Smugglers, and detectives, follow him to the island, where they try to retrieve the film. Elliot begins falling in love with Lisa, the head mistress of the island, and Lisa must evaluate her feelings about Elliot and her own motivations.
