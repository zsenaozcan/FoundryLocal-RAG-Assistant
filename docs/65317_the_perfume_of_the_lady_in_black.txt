Title: The Perfume of the Lady in Black (1974)
Genres: Foreign, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Francesco Barilli
Main Cast: Mimsy Farmer, Maurizio Bonuglia, Mario Scaccia, Jho Jhenkins, Nike Arrighi
Keywords: hallucination, giallo, doctor

Overview: Sylvia, an industrial scientist, is troubled by strange hallucinations related to the tragic suicide of her mother.
