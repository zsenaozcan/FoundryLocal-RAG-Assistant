Title: The Situation (2006)
Genres: Drama, History, Romance, Thriller
Director: Philip Haas
Main Cast: Connie Nielsen, Damian Lewis, Mido Hamada
Keywords: iraq war

Overview: The first U.S.-made film drama set during the Iraq war, THE SITUATION chronicles the tragic death of an Iraqi teenager at the hands of U.S. soldiers. The incident sets off an "investigation," a cover-up, and complications involving Iraqi mayor Sheikh Tahsin (Saïd Amadis), who has a complex relationship with the Americans.
