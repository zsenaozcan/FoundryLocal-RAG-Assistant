Title: Le Donk & Scor-zay-zee (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary, Foreign
Director: Shane Meadows
Main Cast: Olivia Colman, Dean Palinczuk, Paddy Considine, Shane Meadows
Keywords: independent film, roadie, artic monkeys, music business

Overview: An improvised comedy, shot over five days by Shane Meadows, devised with and starring Paddy Considine. Rock roadie and failed musician, Le Donk has lived, loved and learned. Along the way he's lost a girlfriend but he has found a new sidekick in up-and-coming rap prodigy Scor-zay-zee. With Meadows' fly-on-the-wall crew in tow, Donk sets out to make Scor-zay-zee a star...
