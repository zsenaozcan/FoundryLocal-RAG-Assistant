Title: The Pacific (2010)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, History, War
Director: Graham Yost
Main Cast: James Badge Dale, Joseph Mazzello, Jon Seda, Ashton Holmes, William Sadler
Keywords: world war ii, miniseries

Overview: A 10-part mini-series from the creators of "Band of Brothers" telling the intertwined stories of three Marines during America's battle with the Japanese in the Pacific during World War II.
