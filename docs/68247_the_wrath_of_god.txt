Title: The Wrath of God (1972)
Genres: Western, Adventure
Director: Ralph Nelson
Main Cast: Robert Mitchum, Frank Langella, Rita Hayworth, John Colicos, Victor Buono
Keywords: central america, bandit

Overview: Set in the 1920s, several foreigners held by a South American military group are offered possible freedom if they accept to topple a local crazed military leader.
