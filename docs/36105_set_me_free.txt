Title: Set Me Free (1999)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Léa Pool
Main Cast: Karine Vanasse, Pascale Bussières, Predrag 'Miki' Manojlović, Alexandre Mérineau, Charlotte Christeler
Keywords: prostitute, puberty, teacher, prostitution, woman director

Overview: A coming-of-age tale centered around Hannah, a young girl who is living a troubled family life. Set in 1963, Hannah develops a fascination with Jean-Luc Godard's then-recent film "Vivre sa vie". As she begins to model herself after the film's lead role, Hannah slowly begins to explore the confusing nature of her sexuality.
