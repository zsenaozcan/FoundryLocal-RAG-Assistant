Title: Money for Nothing (1993)
Genres: Drama, Action, Comedy, Thriller, Crime
Director: Ramón Menéndez
Main Cast: John Cusack, Debi Mazar, Michael Madsen, Benicio del Toro, Michael Rapaport

Overview: When unemployed dockworker Joey Coyle finds $1.2 million that fell off of an armored car, he decides to do the logical thing: take the money and run. After all, he says, finders keepers. He turns to his ex-girlfriend Monica, who works in an investment firm, for advice, before turning to the mob for help laundering the money. While Joey makes plans to leave the country, however, a detective is following his ever-warmer trail in order to recover the cash.
