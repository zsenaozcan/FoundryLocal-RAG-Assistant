Title: Return to Reason (1923)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Man Ray
Main Cast: Kiki of Montparnasse
Keywords: art, avant-garde

Overview: Experimental film, white specks and shapes gyrating over a black background, the light-striped torso of Kiki of Montparnasse (Alice Prin), a gyrating eggcrate. One of the first Dadaist films.
