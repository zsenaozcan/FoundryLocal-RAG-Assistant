Title: Blood Curse (2006)
Genres: Horror, Mystery
Director: Tiago Guedes
Main Cast: Adriano Luz, Manuela Couto, Sara Carinhas, Afonso Pimentel, João Santos
Keywords: portugal, horror, mystery, haunting, possessed

Overview: An urban family inherits an old country house located in a small village. Upon their arrival they try to adapt to the new lifestyle, but they soon learn that the village is overshadowed by superstition and mysterious folklore. As they are plagued by strange events, they learn more about the history of their family, and slowly begin to believe by inheriting the house, they also inherited a curse.
