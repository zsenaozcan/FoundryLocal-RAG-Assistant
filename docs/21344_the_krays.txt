Title: The Krays (1990)
Genres: Drama, Crime, History
Director: Peter Medak
Main Cast: Gary Kemp, Martin Kemp, Billie Whitelaw, Tom Bell, Susan Fleetwood
Keywords: gangster, twins

Overview: The Krays is a film based on the lives and crimes of the British gangsters Ronald and Reginald Kray, twins who are often referred to as The Krays and were active in London in the 1960s.
