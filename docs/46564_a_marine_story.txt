Title: A Marine Story (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ned Farr
Main Cast: Dreya Weber, Paris Pickard, Christine Mourad, Anthony Michael Jones, Jason Williams
Keywords: lesbian relationship, homosexuality, independent film

Overview: Marine officer Alexandra is tough enough to kick any guy's ass in a bar fight, but there's one opponent she can't beat: military policy. When she returns to her conservative hometown from Iraq with a mysterious personal life, she finds herself charged with preparing a tempestuous teenage girl to boot camp.
