Title: The Sticky Fingers of Time (1997)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Hilary Brougher
Main Cast: Terumi Matthews, Nicole Zaray, Belinda Becker, James Urbaniak, Thomas Pasley
Keywords: future, bisexuality, time travel, woman director

Overview: A 1950s author (Terumi Matthews) is transported to 1990s Brooklyn, where she meets a woman (Nicole Zaray) who reads about her life.
