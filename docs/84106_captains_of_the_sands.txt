Title: Captains of the Sands (2011)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Foreign
Director: Cecília Amado
Main Cast: Jean Luís Amorim, Ana Graciela, Robério Lima, Paulo Abade, Israel Gouvêia
Keywords: brazilian, woman director

Overview: About the life and adventures of a gang of abandoned street kids known as "Capitães da Areia" (Captains of the Sands), in Salvador, Bahia, during the 1950s.
