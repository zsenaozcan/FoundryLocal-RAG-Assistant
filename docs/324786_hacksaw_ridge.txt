Title: Hacksaw Ridge (2016)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Mel Gibson
Main Cast: Andrew Garfield, Sam Worthington, Vince Vaughn, Luke Bracey, Teresa Palmer
Keywords: japan, world war ii, violent father, pacifism, bible, biography, war, conscientious objector, based on true story, gore
