Title: Two Weeks with Love (1950)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Roy Rowland
Main Cast: Jane Powell, Ricardo Montalban, Debbie Reynolds, Ann Harding, Louis Calhern
Keywords: musical, singing

Overview: The Robinson family are spending two weeks of summer vacation at a resort in the Catskills. Older daughter Patti vies with her friend, Valeria, for the affections of Demi Armendez but Patti is at a disadvantage because her parents think she is too young for boys. But with Patti singing at an amateur show and a dance, her adventures in quest of Armendez ends happily.
