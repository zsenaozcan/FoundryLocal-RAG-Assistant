Title: Appleseed Alpha (2014)
Genres: Animation, Science Fiction, Action
Director: Shinji Aramaki
Main Cast: Luci Christian, David Matranga, Wendel Calvert, Adam Gibbs, Elizabeth Bunch
Keywords: post-apocalyptic, war, mecha, cyberpunk

Overview: Based on the comic book by the creator of Ghost in the Shell, a young female soldier Deunan and her cyborg partner Briareos survive through the post World War 3 apocalyptic New York in search of human's future hope, the legendary city of Olympus.
