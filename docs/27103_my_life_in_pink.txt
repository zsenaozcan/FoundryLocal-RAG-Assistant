Title: My Life in Pink (1997)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Alain Berliner
Main Cast: Michèle Laroque, Jean-Philippe Écoffey, Hélène Vincent, Georges Du Fresne, Daniel Hanssens
Keywords: transvestism, gay, belgium, coming out, family relationships, coming of age, transgender, gender, lgbt child

Overview: Ludovic is a small boy who cross-dresses and generally acts like a girl, talks of marrying his neighbor's son and can not understand why everyone is so surprised about it. His actions lead to problems for him and his family.
