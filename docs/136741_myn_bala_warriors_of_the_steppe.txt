Title: Myn Bala: Warriors of the Steppe (2012)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: Akan Satayev
Main Cast: Kuralay Anarbekova, Aliya Anuarbek, Aliya Telebarisova, Assylkhan Tolepov, Asylkhan Tolepov

Overview: A universal story about the freedom of the human spirit and the struggle against slavery and despotism, about love, loss and betrayal. It is seen through the eyes of simple Kazakh kids and teenagers.
