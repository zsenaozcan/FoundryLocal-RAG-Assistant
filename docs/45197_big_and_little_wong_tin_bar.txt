Title: Big and Little Wong Tin Bar (1962)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Jim-Yuen Yu
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Sammo Hung, Li Li-Hua, Yuen Wah, Yu Kai
Keywords: martial arts, black and white, chinese opera, seven little fortunes

Overview: A child learns martial arts in order to become a Kung Fu warrior. Features the Seven Little Fortunes, and is the debut film of Jackie Chan.
