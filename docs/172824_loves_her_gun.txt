Title: Loves Her Gun (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Geoff Marslett
Main Cast: Trieste Kelly Dunn, Francisco Barreiro, Ashley Spillers, Melissa Bisagni, John Merriman

Overview: Allie moves to Texas and embraces the slower pace of life and gun culture. She tries to maintain healthy relationships as the weapons she uses to protect herself lead her to a paranoid withdrawal from life.
