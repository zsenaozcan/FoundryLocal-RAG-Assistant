Title: Innocence Unprotected (1968)
Genres: Drama, Documentary, Foreign
Director: Dusan Makavejev
Main Cast: Dragoljub Aleksić, Bratoljub Gligorijević, Vera Jovanović-Šegović, Ana Milosavljević, Pera Milosavljević
Keywords: balcony, liberation, nudity, nazis, propaganda, locksmith, belgrad, serbian, love, documentary

Overview: Documentary about the famous Serbian athlete and movie enthusiast who made a feature film during the Nazi occupation of Belgrade and had some problems after the liberation because of that.
