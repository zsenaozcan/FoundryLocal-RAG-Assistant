Title: Ploy (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Pen-Ek Ratanaruang
Main Cast: Apinya Sakuljaroensuk, Ananda Everingham, Lalita Panyopas, Porntip Papanai, Pornwut Sarasin
Keywords: bathroom, sex, hotel, depression, hotel room, jealousy, dream, eroticism, melancholy, song
