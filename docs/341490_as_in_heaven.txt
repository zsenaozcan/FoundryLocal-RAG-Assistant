Title: As in Heaven (1992)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Kristín Jóhannesdóttir
Main Cast: Tinna Gunnlaugsdóttir, Pierre Vaneck, Sigursteinn Brynjólfsson, Christian Charmetant, Valdimar Örn Flygenring
Keywords: iceland

Overview: Set on a remote farm on the west coast of Iceland in late summer 1936. Hrefna, encouraged by her grandmother’s stories, begins to mix past and future in her imagination, travelling back to the 14th century.
