Title: Magadheera (2009)
Genres: Action
Director: S.S. Rajamouli
Main Cast: Ram Charan, Kajal Agarwal, Dev Gill, Srihari, Chiranjeevi

Overview: When Harsha, a bike stunter from Vishakapatnam, accidentally meets Indu, he discovers his warrior life Kala Bhairava in his last birth, the remaining story portrays how Indu and Kala Bhairava are connected.
