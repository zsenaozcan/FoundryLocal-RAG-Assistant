Title: Tropical Fish (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Chen Yu-Hsun
Main Cast: Yi-fan Chen, Wen Ying, Akio Chen, Lin Cheng-sheng, Lien Pi-tung

Overview: Young boy, Ah-Jiang, a school failure and day dreamer witnesses the kidnapping of a child. After being taken hostage by a corrupt family, he begins an unusual adventure away from home.
