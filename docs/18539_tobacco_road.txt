Title: Tobacco Road (1941)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: John Ford
Main Cast: Charley Grapewin, Marjorie Rambeau, Gene Tierney, William Tracy, Elizabeth Patterson
Keywords: georgia, banker, marriage, hillbilly

Overview: Shiftless Jeeter Lester and his family of hillbilly stereotypes live in a rural backwater where their ancestors were once wealthy planters. Their slapstick existence is threatened by a bank's plans to take over the land for more profitable farming.
