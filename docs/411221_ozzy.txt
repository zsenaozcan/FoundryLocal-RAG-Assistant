Title: Ozzy (2016)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Alberto Rodríguez
Main Cast: Elsa Pataky, Michelle Jenner, Dani Rovira, Carlos Areces, Fernando Tejero
Keywords: dog, beagle

Overview: Ozzy, a friendly, peaceful beagle has his idyllic life turned upside down when the Martins leave on a long and distant trip. There's only one problem: no dogs allowed! Unable to bring their beloved Ozzy along for the ride, they settle on the next best thing, a top-of-the-line canine spa called Blue Creek.
