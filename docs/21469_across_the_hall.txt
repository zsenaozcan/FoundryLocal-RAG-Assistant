Title: Across the Hall (2009)
Genres: Thriller, Mystery, Romance
Director: Alex Merkin
Main Cast: Mike Vogel, Brittany Murphy, Danny Pino, Brad Greenquist, Arie Verveen
Keywords: hotel, neo-noir

Overview: Convinced that his fiancee is cheating on him, a man follows her to a hotel and calls his best friend to help him avoid a calamity.
