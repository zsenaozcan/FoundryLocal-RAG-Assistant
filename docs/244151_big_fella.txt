Title: Big Fella (1937)
Genres: Unknown
Director: J. Elder Wills
Main Cast: Paul Robeson, Elisabeth Welch, Roy Emerton, James Hayter, Lawrence Brown

Overview: In this musical comedy, Paul Robeson stars as Joe, a Marseilles docker hired by a wealthy English couple to find their missing son. When Joe finds him, he learns he escaped of his own will, and takes him to stay with a local singer. They offer him a refuge from his repressed white parents.
