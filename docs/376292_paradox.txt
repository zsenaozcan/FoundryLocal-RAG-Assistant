Title: Paradox (2016)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Michael Hurst
Main Cast: Zoë Bell, Adam Huss, Malik Yoba, Bjørn Alexander Brem, Brian Flaccus
Keywords: time travel

Overview: A group of scientists are experimenting with time travel, and they manage to send one of their group ahead in time one hour. But when he comes back, he tells them that they’ll all be dead within the next hour unless they shut the machine down.
