Title: A Man Called Horse (1970)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Western
Director: Elliot Silverstein
Main Cast: Richard Harris, Judith Anderson, Jean Gascon, Manu Tupou, Corinna Tsopei
Keywords: ritual, aristocrat, bear attack, sioux tribe, rites of passage, native american captive

Overview: In 1825 an English aristocrat is captured by Indians. He lives with them and begins to understand/accept their lifestyles. Eventually he is accepted as part of the tribe and becomes their leader.
