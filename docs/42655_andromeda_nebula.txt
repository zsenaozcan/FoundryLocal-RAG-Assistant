Title: Andromeda Nebula (1967)
Genres: Science Fiction, Romance
Director: Yevgeni Sherstobitov
Main Cast: Sergei Stolyarov, Vija Artmane, Nikolai Kryukov, Lyudmila Chursina, Marina Yurasova

Overview: Humans on spaceship named "Tantra" are exploring the unknown part of the Universe. The spaceship gets trapped by the mysterious Iron Star. The Iron Star's powerful force of gravity is to hold the spaceship for 20 years. The humans are facing a very tough survival challenge... Based on famous novel by Ivan Efremov.
