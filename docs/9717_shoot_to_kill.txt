Title: Shoot to Kill (1988)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Crime, Thriller
Director: Roger Spottiswoode
Main Cast: Sidney Poitier, Tom Berenger, Kirstie Alley, Clancy Brown, Richard Masur
Keywords: mountain, canada, fbi, kidnapping, horse, wilderness, mountaineer, waterfall, hiking, police

Overview: Sidney Poitier makes his long-overdue return to films in this 1988 thriller, playing an FBI agent on the trail of an elusive killer. Reluctantly teamed with tracker Tom Berenger, the city-dwelling Poitier braves the wilds of the Pacific Northwest in search of his quarry.
