Title: Gringuito (1998)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Gringuito tells the story of Ivan, a kid whose family decides to go back to Chile from New York, after at least a decade away. Ivan of course is not quite the Chilean kid his grandparents would like him to be: he sulks, misses the Opera, finds things odd and ugly, acting like a real "little gringo" (hence the title).
