Title: 9 Souls (2003)
Genres: Crime, Comedy, Drama, Adventure
Director: Toshiaki Toyoda
Main Cast: Yoshio Harada, 松田龙平, Itsuji Itao, Kiyohiko Shibukawa, Kôji Chihara

Overview: After discovering a hole in their crowded cell, nine prisoners escape their confinement to track down the key of the universe, which a fellow prisoner known as the Counterfeit King said he had hidden.
