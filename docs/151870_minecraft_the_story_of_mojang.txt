Title: Minecraft: The Story of Mojang (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Paul Owens
Main Cast: Markus Persson, Jens Bergensten, Jakob Porser, Carl Manneh, Peter Molyneux

Overview: Follow Markus "Notch" Persson and his co-workers during the first years on the independent game studio "Mojang" after releasing the successful debut game, "Minecraft".
