Title: Alien Nation: The Enemy Within (1996)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Kenneth Johnson
Main Cast: Gary Graham, Eric Pierpoint, Michele Scarabelli, Terri Treas, Lauren Woodland
Keywords: detective, alien, prejudice, newcomer

Overview: When detectives Sikes and Francisco are presented with the mysterious death of an Eeno, Matt is stupefied to discover that George rudely snubs the case. He, like most newcomers, reviles the outcast Eenos. As the case unfolds, George has to reassess his prejudices, and George's family help save the city from an alien threat originating in an Eeno waste disposal facility.
