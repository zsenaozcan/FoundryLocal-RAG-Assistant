Title: Blind Husbands (1919)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Erich von Stroheim
Main Cast: Erich von Stroheim, Gibson Gowland, Sam De Grasse, Francelia Billington, Fay Holderness
Keywords: mountain, rescue, mountain climbing, philanderer

Overview: An Austrian military officer and roue' attempts to seduce the wife of a surgeon. The two men confront each other in a test of abilities that ends surprisingly.
