Title: The Power of Kangwon Province (1998)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Hong Sang-soo
Main Cast: Baek Jong-hak, Chun Jae-Hyun, Im Sun-Young, Kim Yu-seok, Oh Yun-Hong

Overview: Recently separated lovers (Baek Jong-hak, Oh Yun-hong) have a series of close encounters at a popular mountain retreat.
