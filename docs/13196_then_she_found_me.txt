Title: Then She Found Me (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Helen Hunt
Main Cast: Helen Hunt, Bette Midler, Colin Firth, Matthew Broderick, Ben Shenkman
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A New York schoolteacher hits a midlife crisis when, in quick succession, her husband leaves, her adoptive mother dies and her biological mother, an eccentric talk show host, materializes and turns her life upside down as she begins a courtship with the father of one of her students.
