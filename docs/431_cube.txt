Title: Cube (1997)
Genres: Thriller, Science Fiction, Mystery
Director: Vincenzo Natali
Main Cast: Nicole de Boer, Nicky Guadagni, David Hewlett, Andrew Miller, Julian Richings
Keywords: riddle, autism, claustrophobia, maze, prime number, entrapment, mathematics, murder, escape, violence

Overview: Seven strangers are taken out of their daily lives and placed mysteriously in a deadly cube where they all agree they must find their way out.
