Title: The Erotic Misadventures of the Invisible Man (2003)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy
Director: Rolfe Kanefsky
Main Cast: Scott Coppola, Gabriella Hall, Doug Merril, Holly Hollywood, Kim Dawson
Keywords: sex, erotic movie

Overview: When Norman (Scott Coppola), an aspiring actor, can't find an open door in Hollywood, he gets rather dispirited. But he gets a new lease on life when he discovers an invisibility formula and learns that women don't really care whether they can see him or not! This erotic comedy is based on the comic books by Milo Manera.
