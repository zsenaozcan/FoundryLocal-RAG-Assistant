Title: Leningrad Cowboys Go America (1989)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Aki Kaurismäki
Main Cast: Matti Pellonpää, Kari Väänänen, Sakke Järvenpää, Heikki Keskinen, Pimme Korhonen
Keywords: rock and roll, dream, american dream, career, success, musical, outfit, music band, finland, road movie

Overview: The Leningrad Cowboys, a group of Finnish musicians, and their manager, travel to America seeking fame and fortune. As they cross the country, trying to get to a wedding in Mexico, they are followed by the village idiot, who wishes to join the band.
