Title: The Legend of Kaspar Hauser (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director:  Davide Manuli
Main Cast: Vincent Gallo, Claudia Gerini, Elisa Sednaoui, Fabrizio Gifuni, Silvia Calderoni

Overview: Arriving on a deserted beach in the Mediterranean sea, in a time and a place unspecified, Kaspar Hauser is forced to confront the evil of a Grand Duchess who feels threatened by the power she exercises over the community.
