Title: Murder-Rock: Dancing Death (1984)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Olga Karlatos, Ray Lovelock, Cosimo Cinieri, Giuseppe Mannajuolo, Claudio Cassinelli
Keywords: murder, giallo, student, dance studio

Overview: A series of murders rock a dance academy in New York in which the mysterous directress, Candice, sets out to find the kiler, with a little assistance from a shady male model, named George, who has a hidden agenda for her as does she for him.
