Title: The Fallen Idol (1948)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Carol Reed
Main Cast: Ralph Richardson, Michèle Morgan, Sonia Dresdel, Bobby Henrey, Denis O'Dea
Keywords: butler, suspicion, witness, embassy, boy

Overview: The film is told through the naive eyes of a diplomat's young son, Phillipe, who idolises his best friend, the diplomat's butler Baines. Baines has constructed a heroic persona, full of exotic adventures, that fascinates the boy.
