Title: Dangerous Days: Making Blade Runner (2007)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Charles de Lauzirika
Main Cast: Ridley Scott, Sean Young, Rutger Hauer, Edward James Olmos, Joanna Cassidy
Keywords: behind the scenes, making of

Overview: The definitive three-and-a-half hour documentary about the troubled creation and enduring legacy of the science fiction classic "Blade Runner," culled from 80 interviews and hours of never-before-seen outtakes and lost footage.
