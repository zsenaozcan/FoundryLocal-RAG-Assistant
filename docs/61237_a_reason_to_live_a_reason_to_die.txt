Title: A Reason to Live, a Reason to Die (1972)
Genres: Western
Director: Tonino Valerii
Main Cast: James Coburn, Telly Savalas, Bud Spencer, Georges Géret, Reinhard Kolldehoff
Keywords: spaghetti western

Overview: A dishonored Union Army officer (James Coburn) leads a group of convicts to retake Fort Holman from the Confederate Army.
