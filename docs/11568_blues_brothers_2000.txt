Title: Blues Brothers 2000 (1998)
Genres: Music, Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: John Landis
Main Cast: Dan Aykroyd, John Goodman, Joe Morton, J. Evan Bonifant, B.B. King
Keywords: prison, blues, half-brother, orphanage, band

Overview: Elwood, the now lone "Blues Brother" finally released from prison, is once again enlisted by Sister Mary Stigmata in her latest crusade to raise funds for a children's hospital. Once again hitting the road to re-unite the band and win the big prize at the New Orleans Battle of the Bands, Elwood is pursued cross-country by the cops, led by Cabel the Curtis' son
