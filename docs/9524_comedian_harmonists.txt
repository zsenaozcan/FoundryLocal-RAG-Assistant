Title: Comedian Harmonists (1997)
Genres: Music, Drama
Director: Joseph Vilsmaier
Main Cast: Ben Becker, Heino Ferch, Ulrich Noethen, Heinrich Schafmeister, Max Tidof
Keywords: berlin, jew, nazis, musical, biography, anti semitism

Overview: Comedian Harmonists tells the story of a famous, German male sextet, five vocals and piano, the "Comedian Harmonists", from the day they meet first in 1927 to the day in 1934, when they become banned by the upcoming Nazis, because three of them are Jewish.
