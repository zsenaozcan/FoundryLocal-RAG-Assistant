Title: High Sierra (1941)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Thriller, Crime
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: Ida Lupino, Humphrey Bogart, Alan Curtis, Arthur Kennedy, Joan Leslie
Keywords: prison, robbery, shooting, on the run, little dog
