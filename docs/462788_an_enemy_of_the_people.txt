Title: An Enemy of the People (1966)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Paul Bogart
Main Cast: James Daly, Kate Reid, Philip Bosco, Tim Daly, Barbara Dana
Keywords: based on play or musical

Overview: Set in a Norwegian hamlet, an idealistic physician discovers that the town's hot springs are contaminated. But with the community relying on the spa for tourist dollars, his warning to the powers that be fall on deaf ears.
