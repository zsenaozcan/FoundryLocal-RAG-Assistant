Title: Glen or Glenda (1953)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Edward D. Wood Jr.
Main Cast: Bela Lugosi, Lyle Talbot, Edward D. Wood Jr., Dolores Fuller, Timothy Farrell
Keywords: transvestism, independent film, midnight movie

Overview: A psychiatrist tells two stories: one of a transvestite (Glen or Glenda), the other of a pseudohermaphrodite (Alan or Anne).
