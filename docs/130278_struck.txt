Title: Struck (2008)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Taron Lexton
Main Cast: David Aranovich, Lynsey Bartilson, Jamie Bernadette, Nancy Cartwright, Kelly Preston
Keywords: bow, cupid

Overview: On his way to work one day, Joel is impaled through the chest by a three-foot arrow. But it doesn’t harm him. And it won’t come out. So Joel has to learn to deal – both with his newfound protrusion, and his own painful loneliness. He tries to go to work, to date women, but no one seems ready to accept his strange flaw. Little does he know, his life is about to change forever...
