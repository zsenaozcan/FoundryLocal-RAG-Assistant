Title: Made in Bangkok (2015)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Flavio Florencio
Main Cast: Morgana Love, Irene Sue, Niwat Kaewpeer, Preecha Tiewtranon, Noa Herrera

Overview: Morgana is a Mexican transgender opera singer with a dream: a sex reassignment surgery. We follow her odyssey all the way to Bangkok as she fights for the identity she has been struggling all her life to construct.
