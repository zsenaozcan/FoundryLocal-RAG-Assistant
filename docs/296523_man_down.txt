Title: Man Down (2016)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Dito Montiel
Main Cast: Shia LaBeouf, Jai Courtney, Gary Oldman, Kate Mara, Tory Kittles
Keywords: dream, post-apocalyptic, illusion, delusion, apocalypse, alternate reality, post war
