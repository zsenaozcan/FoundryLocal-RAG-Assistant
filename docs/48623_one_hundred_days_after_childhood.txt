Title: One Hundred Days After Childhood (1975)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Sergey Solovyov
Main Cast: Boris Tokarev, Tatyana Drubich, Irina Malysheva, Yuri Agilin, Sergey Shakurov

Overview: A group of Russian teenagers spend their summer at a summer camp. Mitia falls in love and gets so occupied with his own problems that he forgets about everything else.
