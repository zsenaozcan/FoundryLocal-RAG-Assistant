Title: One Sunday Afternoon (1933)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Stephen Roberts
Main Cast: Gary Cooper, Fay Wray, Frances Fuller, Roscoe Karns, Neil Hamilton
Keywords: prison, unrequited love, dentist

Overview: Middle-aged dentist Biff Grimes reminisces about his unrequited love for beautiful Virginia Brush and her husband Hugo, his ex-friend, who betrayed him.
