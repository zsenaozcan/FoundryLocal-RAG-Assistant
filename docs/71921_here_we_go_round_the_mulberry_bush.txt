Title: Here We Go Round the Mulberry Bush (1968)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Clive Donner
Main Cast: Barry Evans, Judy Geeson, Angela Scoular, Sheila White, Adrienne Posta
Keywords: england, obsession, virgin, swinging 60s, sex comedy

Overview: Jamie McGregor (Barry Evans) is a virginal sixth-former in suburbia delivering groceries for the local supermarket, but he is more interested in other matters - Mary, Linda, Paula and Caroline. He tries to seduce the girls of his dreams in the swinging sixties.
