Title: Undertaking Betty (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Nick Hurran
Main Cast: Brenda Blethyn, Alfred Molina, Christopher Walken, Naomi Watts, Robert Pugh
Keywords: competition, small town, coffin, politician, run away, falling in love, grave digger
