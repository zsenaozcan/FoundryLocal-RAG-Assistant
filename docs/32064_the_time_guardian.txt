Title: The Time Guardian (1987)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Brian Hannant
Main Cast: Tom Burlinson, Nikki Coghill, Dean Stockwell, Carrie Fisher, Peter Merrill
Keywords: time travel, space

Overview: In the distant future, the human race nears extinction and a new race of beast-like creatures rule the earth. The few surviving people live in the City, a huge protected construction with the ability to travel in both space and time. The City travels back to our time to save humanity...
