Title: The Zookeeper's Wife (2017)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Niki Caro
Main Cast: Jessica Chastain, Johan Heldenbergh, Daniel Brühl, Timothy Radford, Efrat Dor
Keywords: holocaust, world war ii, nazis, biography, jewish, zookeeper, poland

Overview: The account of keepers of the Warsaw Zoo, Jan and Antonina Zabinski, who helped save hundreds of people and animals during the Nazi invasion.
