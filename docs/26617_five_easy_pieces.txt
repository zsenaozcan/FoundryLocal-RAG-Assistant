Title: Five Easy Pieces (1970)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bob Rafelson
Main Cast: Jack Nicholson, Susan Anspach, Karen Black, Billy Green Bush, William Challee
Keywords: road trip, drifter, class differences, rebelliousness, pacific northwest

Overview: A drop-out from upper-class America picks up work along the way on oil-rigs when his life isn't spent in a squalid succession of bars, motels, and other points of interest.
