Title: A Muppets Christmas: Letters to Santa (2008)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Kirk R. Thatcher
Main Cast: Steve Whitmire, Dave Goelz, Bill Barretta, Eric Jacobson, Michael Bloomberg
Keywords: holiday, christmas

Overview: When Gonzo forgets to mail three letters to Santa, he convinces Kermit and the gang to help him deliver the notes to the North Pole. Along the way, they discover that Christmas is the time to be with those you care about most, as they dash home to make a friends Christmas wish come true.
