Title: The Last Days of the World (2011)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Comedy, Science Fiction, Foreign
Director: Eiji Uchida
Main Cast: Chieko Imaizumi, Ryo Iwamatsu, Jyonmyon Pe, Kiyohiko Shibukawa, Tetsushi Tanaka
Keywords: cosplay

Overview: The life of a student named Kanou is transformed when a tiny man informs him that Earth will soon be annihilated. Kanou decides to do just as he pleases with the time he has left, leading to strange adventures that could be real -- or just a dream.
