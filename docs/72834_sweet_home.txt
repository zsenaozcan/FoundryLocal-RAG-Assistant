Title: Sweet Home (1989)
Genres: Horror
Director: Kiyoshi Kurosawa
Main Cast: Nobuko Miyamoto, Shingo Yamashiro, Tsutomu Yamazaki
Keywords: painter, japanese, haunted house, darkness, murder, evil spirit, mansion, horror movie, based on video game, old mansion

Overview: A TV production crew are making a documentary about the infamous painter Mamiya Ichiro. When they start filming at his old home, they come under attack from the ghost of the painter's wife.
