Title: Street People (1976)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime
Director: Maurizio Lucidi
Main Cast: Roger Moore, Stacy Keach, Fausto Tozzi, Ivo Garrani, Ennio Balbo
Keywords: smuggling, mafia, driver

Overview: A Mafia boss is enraged when he is suspected of smuggling a heroin shipment into San Francisco. He dispatches his nephew, a hotshot Anglo-Sicilian lawyer, to identify the real culprit. The lawyer also enlists the aid of his best friend, a grand prix driver with an adventurous streak.
