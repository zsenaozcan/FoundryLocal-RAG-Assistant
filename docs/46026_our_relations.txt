Title: Our Relations (1936)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Harry Lachman
Main Cast: Stan Laurel, Oliver Hardy, Alan Hale, Sidney Toler, Daphne Pollard
Keywords: captain, fight, restaurant, attempted murder, nightclub, letter, bartender, ship, beer, brother

Overview: Two sailors get caught in a mountain of mix-ups when they meet their long-lost twins. Laurel and Hardy play themselves and their twins.
