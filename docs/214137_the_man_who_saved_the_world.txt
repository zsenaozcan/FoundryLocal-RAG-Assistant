Title: The Man Who Saved the World (2014)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Peter Anthony
Main Cast: Robert De Niro, Kevin Costner, Stanislav Petrov, Sergey Shnyryov, Matt Damon
Keywords: cold war, nuclear war, nuclear weapons

Overview: The Man Who Saved the World is feature documentary film about Stanislav Petrov, a former lieutenant colonel of the Soviet Air Defence Forces.
