Title: H.H. Holmes: America's First Serial Killer (2004)
Genres: Crime, Documentary, History
Director: John Borowski
Main Cast: Tony Jay, Willy Laszlo
Keywords: criminal

Overview: Torture chambers, acid vats, greased chutes and gassing rooms were just some of the devices of death designed by the Torture Doctor, H.H. Holmes in his castle of horrors. Follows Holmes' entire life as a criminal mastermind.
