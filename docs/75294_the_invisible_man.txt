Title: The Invisible Man (1984)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Aleksandr Zakharov
Main Cast: Andrey Kharitonov, Romualdas Ramanauskas, Leonid Kuravlyov, Natalya Danilova, Nina Agapova
Keywords: based on novel

Overview: Based upon the famous novel by H.G.Wells. A poor scientist named Griffin discovers a way to make things invisible. Since he has no money to continue his research, he decides to perform his only experiment on himself. After becoming invisible, Griffin has a lot of trouble trying to conceal this from other people.
