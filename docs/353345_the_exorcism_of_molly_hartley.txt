Title: The Exorcism of Molly Hartley (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: Steven R. Monroe
Main Cast: Sarah Lind, Devon Sawa, Gina Holden, Peter MacNeill, Tom McLaren

Overview: Taking place years after The Haunting of Molly Hartley, who now, as an adult, has fallen under the possession of an evil spirit and must be exorcised by a fallen priest before the devil completely takes her.
