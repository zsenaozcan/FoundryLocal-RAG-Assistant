Title: Little Big Master (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Adrian Kwan
Main Cast: Miriam Yeung, Louis Koo, Richard Ng, Stanley Fung, Winnie Ho
Keywords: education, headmaster, kindergarten teacher

Overview: The story of a hopeful headmaster who perseveres in running a kindergarten for underprivileged children in Yuen Long, despite many challenges and little reward. Based on true events.
