Title: Becky Sharp (1935)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Rouben Mamoulian
Main Cast: Miriam  Hopkins, Frances Dee, Cedric Hardwicke, Billie Burke, Alison Skipworth
Keywords: technicolor

Overview: The first feature length film to use three-strip Technicolor film. Adapted from a play that was adapted from William Makepeace Thackeray's book "Vanity Fair", the film looks at the English class system during the Napoleonic Wars era.
