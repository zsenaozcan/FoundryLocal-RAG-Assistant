Title: Kidnapped (1960)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Family
Director: Robert Stevenson
Main Cast: Peter Finch, James MacArthur, Bernard Lee, John Laurie, Niall MacGinnis

Overview: Kidnapped and cheated out of his inheritance, young David Balfour falls in with a Jacobite adventurer, Alan Breck Stewart. Falsely accused of murder, they must flee across the Highlands, evading the redcoats.
