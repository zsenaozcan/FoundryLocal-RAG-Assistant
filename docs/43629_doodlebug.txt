Title: Doodlebug (1997)
Genres: Fantasy, Mystery
Director: Christopher Nolan
Main Cast: Jeremy Theobald
Keywords: student movie, bug

Overview: A man is trying to catch some sort of bug running around his room. He takes his shoes off and intends to crush it under the heel of his loafer. However, as he slowly begins to track the bug down and trap it things change dramatically, but the man continues his course of action.
