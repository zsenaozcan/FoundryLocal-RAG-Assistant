Title: The Condemned 2 (2015)
Genres: Action
Director: Roel Reiné
Main Cast: Randy Orton, Eric Roberts, Wes Studi, Steven Michael Quezada, Monique Candelaria

Overview: A former bounty hunter who finds himself on the run as part of a revamped Condemned tournament, in which convicts are forced to fight each other to the death as part of a game that's broadcast to the public.
