Title: The Man Who Loved Women (1977)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: François Truffaut
Main Cast: Charles Denner, Brigitte Fossey, Nelly Borgeaud, Geneviève Fontanel, Leslie Caron
Keywords: paris, women, prostitute, bachelor, lover (female), ladykiller, heart-throb, author, extramarital affair

Overview: At Bertrand Morane's burial there are many of the women that the 40-year-old engineer loved. In flashback Bertrand's life and love affairs are told by himself while writing an autobiographical novel.
