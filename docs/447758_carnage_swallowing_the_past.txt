Title: Carnage: Swallowing the Past (2017)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy, Documentary
Director: Simon Amstell
Main Cast: Simon Amstell, Linda Bassett, Martin Freeman, Eileen Atkins, Joanna Lumley

Overview: It's 2067, the UK is vegan, but older generations are suffering the guilt of their carnivorous past. Simon Amstell asks us to forgive them for the horrors of what they swallowed.
