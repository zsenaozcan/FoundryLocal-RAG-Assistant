Title: Born American (1986)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Renny Harlin
Main Cast: Mike Norris, Steve Durham, David Coburn, Thalmus Rasulala, Albert Salmi
Keywords: russia, finland

Overview: Three American students vacationing in Finland, cross the border into Russia for fun of it. When they are spotted by the Russian soldiers who are shooting to kill, it's not fun anymore. Captured and thrown in jail, they find it's not fun either. It's a nightmare.
