Title: Frankie and Johnny (1966)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Music, Romance
Director: Frederick de Cordova
Main Cast: Elvis Presley, Donna Douglas, Harry Morgan, Nancy Kovack, Robert Strauss
Keywords: riverboat

Overview: Elvis plays Johnny, a riverboat entertainer with a big gambling problem. Donna Douglas plays Johnny's girl, Frankie. A fortune teller tells Johnny how he can change his luck. Enter a new lady luck played by Nancy Kovack and the cat fight begins.
