Title: Big Stan (2007)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Rob Schneider
Main Cast: Rob Schneider, David Carradine, Jennifer Morrison, Scott Wilson, Richard Kind
Keywords: prison, corruption, martial arts, anti hero, fraud, slapstick, gang, cons and scams, prison life

Overview: Rob Schneider writes and stars in this comedy about a nerdy con man whose swindling ways land him a stiff prison sentence. Terrified at the prospect of being raped in prison he enlists the aid of a mysterious kung-fu expert to teach him how to fight.
