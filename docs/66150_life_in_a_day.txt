Title: Life in a Day (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Kevin Macdonald
Main Cast: Cindy Baer, Moica, Caryn Waechter, Drake Shannon
Keywords: daily life, non fiction, clip show, fear, talking to the camera, one day, nonlinear timeline

Overview: A documentary shot by filmmakers all over the world that serves as a time capsule to show future generations what it was like to be alive on the 24th of July, 2010.
