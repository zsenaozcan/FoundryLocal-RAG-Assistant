Title: Perry Mason: The Case of the Lethal Lesson (1989)
Genres: Mystery, Drama
Director: Christian I. Nyby II
Main Cast: Raymond Burr, Barbara Hale, William R. Moses, Alexandra Paul, Brian Keith
Keywords: detective, murder, courtroom

Overview: Ken Malansky, a law student of Perry Mason's, is accused of murdering a fellow student. Perry is reluctant to take on his case as the victim was the son of a close friend.
