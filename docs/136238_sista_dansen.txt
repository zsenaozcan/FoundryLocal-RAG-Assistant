Title: Sista dansen (1993)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Colin Nutley
Main Cast: Helena Bergström, Reine Brynolfsson, Ewa Fröling, Peter Andersson, Rikard Wolff

Overview: Blackpool, a dancing contest, a dead woman. This is the opening scene of this movie, which then continues by telling us the story preceding this sad accident - or is it a murder? We will get to know the tensed relationship of the dancing contestant couple Tove and Claes, and see how there lives are affected by Liselott and Lennart, their opponents and friends.
