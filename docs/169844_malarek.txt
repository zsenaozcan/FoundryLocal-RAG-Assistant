Title: Malarek (1988)
Genres: Action
Director: Roger Cardinal
Main Cast: Elias Koteas, Kerrie Keane, Al Waxman, Daniel Pilon
Keywords: cover-up, juvenile delinquent

Overview: Malarek is a film directed by Roger Cardinal in 1989. Ex-juvenile offender Victor Malarek catches a break when he's hired as a cub reporter for the Montreal Star. After witnessing a cop murder a street kid, Malarek dedicates himself to exposing corruption in the social welfare system.
