Title: Little Otik (2001)
Genres: Animation, Fantasy, Drama, Horror, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Jan Švankmajer
Main Cast: Veronika Žilková, Jan Hartl, Jaroslava Kretschmerová, Pavel Nový, Kristina Adamcová
Keywords: baby, childless couple

Overview: When a childless couple learn that they cannot have children, it causes great distress. To ease his wife's pain, the man finds a piece of root in the backyard and chops it and varnishes it into the shape of a child. However the woman takes the root as her baby and starts to pretend that it is real.
