Title: Maniac (1980)
Genres: Horror
Director: William Lustig
Main Cast: Joe Spinell, Caroline Munro, Abigail Clayton, Kelly Piper, Rita Montone
Keywords: shotgun, mannequin, sadism, gore, cult favorite, grindhouse, park, model, violence, rage

Overview: A psychotic man, troubled by his childhood abuse, loose in NYC, kills young women and takes their scalps as trophies. Will he find the perfect woman in photographer Anna, and end his killing spree?
