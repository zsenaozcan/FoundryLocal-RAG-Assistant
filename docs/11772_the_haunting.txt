Title: The Haunting (1963)
Genres: Mystery, Horror, Thriller
Director: Robert Wise
Main Cast: Julie Harris, Claire Bloom, Richard Johnson, Russ Tamblyn, Fay Compton
Keywords: alcohol, haunted house, supernatural powers, disappearance, orphan

Overview: Dr. Markway, doing research to prove the existence of ghosts, investigates Hill House, a large, eerie mansion with a lurid history of violent death and insanity.
