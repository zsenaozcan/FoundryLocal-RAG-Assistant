Title: Lloyd's of London (1936)
Genres: Drama, History, Romance, War
Director: Henry King
Main Cast: Freddie Bartholomew, Madeleine Carroll, Guy Standing, Tyrone Power, C. Aubrey Smith
Keywords: ship, insurance, battle of trafalger

Overview: Blake is in love with an aristocratic woman whose husband seriously injures him. Blake's friendship with Lord Nelson provides the basis for Blake's part in the growth of Lloyd's insurance business following the Battle of Trafalgar. Only very slightly based on history.
