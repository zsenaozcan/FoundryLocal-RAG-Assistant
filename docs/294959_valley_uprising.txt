Title: Valley Uprising (2014)
Genres: Adventure, Documentary
Director: Nick Rosen
Main Cast: Peter Sarsgaard, Alex Honnold, Royal Robbins, Lynn Hill, Dean Potter
Keywords: tourist, ranger, national park, climbing, yosemite, rivalry, airplane crash, illegal drugs, climbing up a wall, rock climbing

Overview: In the shady campgrounds of Yosemite valley, climbers carved out a counterculture lifestyle of dumpster-diving and wild parties that clashed with the conservative values of the National Park Service. And up on the walls, generation after generation has pushed the limits of climbing, vying amongst each other for supremacy on Yosemite's cliffs. "Valley Uprising" is the riveting, unforgettable tale of this bold rock climbing tradition in Yosemite National Park: half a century of struggle against the laws of gravity -- and the laws of the land.
