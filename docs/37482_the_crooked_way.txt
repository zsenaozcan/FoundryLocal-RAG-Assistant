Title: The Crooked Way (1949)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Robert Florey
Main Cast: John Payne, Sonny Tufts, Ellen Drew, Rhys Williams, Percy Helton
Keywords: amnesia, gangster, post war, veterans hospital, film noir

Overview: A war veteran suffering from amnesia, returns to Los Angeles from a San Francisco veterans hospital hoping to learn who he is and discovers his criminal past.
