Title: Zombie Resurrection (2014)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jake Hawkins
Main Cast: Eric Colvin, Jim Sweeney, Danny Brown, Simon Burbage, Jade Colucci
Keywords: zombie

Overview: 15 months after the apocalypse, a group of survivors are forced to take refuge in an abandoned school, where they encounter a mysterious zombie with the power to bring the undead back to life.
