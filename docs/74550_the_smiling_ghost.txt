Title: The Smiling Ghost (1941)
Genres: Comedy, Mystery
Director: Lewis Seiler
Main Cast: Wayne Morris, Brenda Marshall, Willie Best, Alexis Smith, Alan Hale
Keywords: ghost

Overview: Elinor Bentley Fairchild's previous three grooms-to-be have either died or been maimed. Her aunt hires Lucky Downing to become engaged to her for a month to break the curse. But Lucky becomes a target of what appears to be the ghost of one of the former fiances.
