Title: Daleks' Invasion Earth: 2150 A.D. (1966)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Gordon Flemyng
Main Cast: Peter Cushing, Bernard Cribbins, Ray Brooks, Andrew Keir, Roberta Tovey
Keywords: time travel

Overview: Doctor Who and his companions are hurled into the future and make a horrifying discovery: the Daleks have conquered Earth! The metal fiends have devastated entire continents and turned the survivors into Robomen.
