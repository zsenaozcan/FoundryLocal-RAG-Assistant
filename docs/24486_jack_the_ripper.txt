Title: Jack the Ripper (1988)
Genres: Horror, Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: David Wickes
Main Cast: Michael Caine, Armand Assante, Ray McAnally, Lewis Collins, Ken Bones
Keywords: scotland yard, jack the ripper, serial killer, killer

Overview: Jack the Ripper was a 1988 two-part television movie/mini-series portraying a fictionalized account of the hunt for Jack The Ripper, the killer responsible for the Whitechapel murders of 1888. The series coincided with the 100th anniversary of the murders. Using historical characters involved in the genuine 1888 hunt for the killer, the film was written by Derek Marlowe and David Wickes
