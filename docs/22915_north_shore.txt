Title: North Shore (1987)
Genres: Action, Drama, Romance
Director: William Phelps
Main Cast: Matt Adler, Gregory Harrison, Nia Peeples, John Philbin, Gerry Lopez
Keywords: surfing

Overview: On a small stretch of coastline as powerful as a man's will, Rick Kane came to surf the big waves. He found a woman who would show him how to survive, and a challenge unlike any other.
