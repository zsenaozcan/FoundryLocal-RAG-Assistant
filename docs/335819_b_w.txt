Title: B/W (2015)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy, Crime
Director: Evgeniy Shelyakin
Main Cast: Aleksey Chadov, Merab Ninidze, Sergei Makovetsky, Yekaterina Rednikova, Kakhi Kavsadze

Overview: Nurick and Yaroslav are trying to robe a criminal boss. Unfortunately Nurick dies but returns to the world with a mission: to be the guardian angel of Yaroslav.
