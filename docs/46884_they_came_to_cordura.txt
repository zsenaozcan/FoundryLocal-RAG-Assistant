Title: They Came to Cordura (1959)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, War
Director: Robert Rossen
Main Cast: Gary Cooper, Rita Hayworth, Van Heflin, Tab Hunter, Richard Conte
Keywords: cavalry, medal of honor

Overview: An army major, himself guilty of cowardice, is asked to recommended soldiers for the Congressional Medal of Honor during the Mexican Border Incursion of 1916.
