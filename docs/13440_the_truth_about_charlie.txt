Title: The Truth About Charlie (2002)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Jonathan Demme
Main Cast: Mark Wahlberg, Thandie Newton, Tim Robbins, Christine Boisson, Lisa Gay Hamilton
Keywords: suspense

Overview: The Truth About Charlie is a 2002 remake of the 1963 film Charade (which starred Cary Grant and Audrey Hepburn). It is also an homage to François Truffaut's Shoot the Piano Player and that film's star Charles Aznavour appeared as himself and sang his song "Quand tu m'aimes" (English version).
