Title: Headless (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: Arthur Cullipher
Main Cast: Shane Beasley, Kaden Miller, Kelsey Carlisle, Ellie Church, Dave Parker
Keywords: exploitation

Overview: In this "lost slasher film from 1978," a masked killer wages an unrelenting spree of murder, cannibalism, and necrophilia. But when his tortured past comes back to haunt him, he plunges to even greater depths of madness and depravity, consuming the lives of a young woman and those she holds dear.
