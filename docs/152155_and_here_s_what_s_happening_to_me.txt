Title: And Here's What's Happening to Me (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Viktor Shamirov
Main Cast: Yuriy Kutsenko, Viktor Shamirov, Aleksandra Petrova, Nikolay Chindyaykin, Olesya Zheleznyak

Overview: December, 31st. Impossible Moscow traffic, an endless rush leading to nothing, people trying to help but not making things any better. Small talk… about small things that fill one’s life — and make it pass us by too quickly. About Dad, who’s only got a few weeks left to live — and there’s nothing you can do to help... About plans for life of a 15-year-old girl — should she become a cancer specialist or a food designer?
