Title: The Hive (2008)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Peter Manus
Main Cast: Tom Wopat, Kal Weber, Elizabeth Healey, Mark Ramsey, Jessica Reavis
Keywords: island, invasion, ants, animal attack, eaten alive, swarms, flame thrower, animal horror

Overview: When ants, displaying never-before-seen behavior, seize an island, the controversial Thorax Team is called in to stop the massive threat, only to discover that the ants are controlled by something beyond this world.
