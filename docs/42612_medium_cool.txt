Title: Medium Cool (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Haskell Wexler
Main Cast: Robert Forster, Verna Bloom, Peter Bonerz, Marianna Hill, Harold Blankenship
Keywords: violence, tv news

Overview: John Cassellis is the toughtest TV-news-reporter around. He becomes an expert in reporting about violence in the ghetto and racial tensions. But he discovers that his network helps the FBI by letting them look at his tapes to find suspects. When he protests he is fired and goes to the 1968 Democratic National Convention in Chicago.
