Title: Everything Will Be All Right (1995)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Comedy
Director: Dmitriy Astrakhan
Main Cast: Anatoli Zhuravlyov, Olga Ponizova, Mark Goronok, Aleksandr Zbruev, Irina Mazurkevich

Overview: This charming comedy tracks the lives of several romantic pairs through trials and tribulations. The main focus of the story is a young soldier with a good heart but little ambition and his fiancée, who feels torn when a charming and sophisticated intellectual enters her life and sweeps her off her feet. There are also several side stories, also all dealing with relationships, most significantly the soldier's mother, whose comfortable but unexceptional marriage is threatened when a past love returns to her life.
