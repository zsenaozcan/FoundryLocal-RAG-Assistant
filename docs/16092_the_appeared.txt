Title: The Appeared (2008)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Paco Cabezas
Main Cast: Ruth Díaz, Javier Pereira, Héctor Bidonde, Paco Cabezas, Luciano Cáceres
Keywords: diary, argentinia, past
