Title: The Samurai That Night (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Masaaki Akahori
Main Cast: Masato Sakai, Takayuki Yamada, Sakura Ando, Hirofumi Arai, Go Ayano

Overview: A widower sends daily reminders to the petty criminal who killed his wife in a traffic accident that on the anniversary of her death, he will kill him.
