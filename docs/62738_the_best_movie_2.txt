Title: The Best Movie 2 (2009)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Oleg Fomin
Main Cast: Garik Kharlamov, Mikhail Galustyan, Dmitriy Khrustalev, Mikhail Efremov, Yuriy Galtsev 

Overview: The Best Movie 2 is a 2009 Russian comedy film from Monumental Pictures (Sony Pictures Entertainment) and Comedy Club Production, continuation of 2008 The Best Movie film, spoofing such famous Russian films as Zhara, The Irony of Fate 2 and TV-programs: Malakhov+, King of Ring and Taxi.
