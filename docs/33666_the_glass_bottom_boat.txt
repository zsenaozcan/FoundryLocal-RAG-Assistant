Title: The Glass Bottom Boat (1966)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Frank Tashlin
Main Cast: Doris Day, Rod Taylor, Arthur Godfrey, John McGiver, Paul Lynde
Keywords: spy, widow

Overview: Bruce (Rod Taylor), the owner of a aerospace company, is infatuated with Jennifer (Doris Day) and hires her to be his biographer so that he can be near her and win her affections. Is she actually a Russian spy trying to obtain aerospace secrets?
