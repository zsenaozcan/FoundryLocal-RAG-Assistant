Title: Beloved Sisters (2014)
Genres: Romance, Drama, History
Director: Dominik Graf
Main Cast: Florian Stetter, Henriette Confurius, Hannah Herzsprung, Ronald Zehrfeld, Andreas Pietschmann
Keywords: love triangle, writer, aristocrat, 18th century, riedrich schiller

Overview: A love triangle forms between post-Enlightenment writer Friedrich Schiller and two sisters -- one who became his wife, and the other, his biographer.
