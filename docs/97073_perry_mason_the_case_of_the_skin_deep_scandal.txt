Title: Perry Mason: The Case of the Skin-Deep Scandal (1993)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: Christian I. Nyby II
Main Cast: Raymond Burr, Barbara Hale, William R. Moses, Carmen Argenziano, Polly Bergen
Keywords: perry mason

Overview: The owner of a cosmetics company is unveiling a new cream which she claims she's been using. She's been keeping her age a secret and now reveals that she's 60 and owes her appearance to the cream. She's later killed and the formula missing. Her husband is arrested and Perry defends him.
