Title: Dabangg (2010)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Abhinav Kashyap
Main Cast: Salman Khan, Sonakshi Sinha, Arbaaz Khan, Sonu Sood, Vinod Khanna
Keywords: fight, slow motion scene, hindi, bollywood, india, police officer

Overview: Chulbul Pandey is a corrupt but golden-hearted police officer in the small town of Uttar Pradesh. He dotes on his mother but hates his stepfather and stepbrother. He is at loggerheads with the local politician, Cheddi Singh, who sets the two half brothers against one another.
