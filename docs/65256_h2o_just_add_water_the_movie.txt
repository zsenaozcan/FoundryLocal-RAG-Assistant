Title: H2O Just Add Water - The Movie (2011)
Genres: Fantasy, Adventure
Director: Jonathan M. Shiff
Main Cast: Cariba Heine, Phoebe Tonkin, Claire Holt, Angus McLaren

Overview: When three normal teenage girls stumble upon a ancient cave they undergo a transformation that will change their lives forever. H20 - Just Add Water, sees three very different girls facing everyday teen problems with an added twist - they're mermaids with incredible powers over water.
