Title: An Angel Named Billy (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Greg Osborne
Main Cast: Dustin Belt, Allison Fleming, Amy London, Matt Prokop, Robin Dionne

Overview: Mark Anderson, a recent stroke victim who is frail and weak. Also, his best friend Thomas, a classic over the hill drag queen desperate for love and happiness. Mark and Thomas have a master plan, to find Mark's son, James Anderson the love of his life once and for all.
