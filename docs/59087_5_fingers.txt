Title: 5 Fingers (1952)
Genres: Drama
Director: Joseph L. Mankiewicz
Main Cast: James Mason, Danielle Darrieux, Michael Rennie, Walter Hampden, Oskar Karlweis
Keywords: secret, world war ii, german

Overview: During WWII the valet to the British Ambassador to Ankara sells British secrets to the Germans while trying to romance a refugee Polish countess.
