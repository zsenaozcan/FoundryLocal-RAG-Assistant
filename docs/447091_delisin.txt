Title: Delisin (1975)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ergin Orbey
Main Cast: Necla Nazır, Tarık Akan, Adile Naşit, Hulusi Kentmen, Ali Şen

Overview: A shy introvert falls for the photographer who took her pictures during high school. Confused and worried, she doubts that love will elapse and can never open up to her lover. Yet she cannot runaway from love.
