Title: The Love Bug (1968)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Fantasy
Director: Robert Stevenson
Main Cast: Dean Jones, Michele Lee, Buddy Hackett, Joe Flynn, Benson Fong
Keywords: car race, suicide attempt, sport, golden gate bridge, racecar driver, bug, car, cult film, car trouble, automobile racing

Overview: Herbie is a car - but no ordinary car. The story follows the Volkswagen Beetle with a mind of its own from the showroom to the race track, with various close escapes in between. Three further Herbie movies were to follow.
