Title: The Ape (2009)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Foreign
Director: Jesper Ganslandt
Main Cast: Olle Sarri, Françoise Joyce, Sean Pietrulewicz, Niclas Gillis, Samuel Haus

Overview: A descent into hell, Jesper Ganslandt's disturbing and suspenseful second feature begins with a man waking up in unfamiliar surroundings, only to find the life he knew the day before is gone.
