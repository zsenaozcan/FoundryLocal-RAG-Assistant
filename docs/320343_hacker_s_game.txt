Title: Hacker's Game (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Romance
Director: Cyril Morin
Main Cast: Pom Klementieff, Chris Schellenger, Mark Wallace, Alena von Stroheim, Gayla Johnson
Keywords: hacker, love story

Overview: A love story between two hackers, Soyan and Loise. Like many other hackers, Soyan works for a company he previously hacked.
