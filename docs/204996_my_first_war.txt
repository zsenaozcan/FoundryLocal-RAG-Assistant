Title: My first war (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Yariv Mozer
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: At the age of 28, I was drafted as a soldier in the Second Lebanese War. Instinctively, I grabbed my video camera. The movie recalls the lack of justice in this unnecessary war and the loss of valuable lives. This was my personal earthquake and the nation as a whole. (Yariv mozer)
