Title: Fun in Acapulco (1963)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Richard Thorpe
Main Cast: Elvis Presley, Ursula Andress, Elsa Cárdenas, Teri Hope, Alejandro Rey
Keywords: musical, acapulco mexico

Overview: Mike works on a boat in Acapulco. When the bratty daughter of the boat owner gets him fired, Mike must find new work. Little boy Rauol helps him get a job as a lifeguard and singer at a local hotel. Clashes abound when Mike runs into the rival lifeguard, who is the champion diver of Mexico.
