Title: Somewhere Under the Broad Sky (1954)
Genres: Drama
Director: Masaki Kobayashi
Main Cast: Keiji Sada, Yoshiko Kuga, Hideko Takamine, Akira Ishihama, Minoru Ôki

Overview: This drama of middle-class life in postwar Japan tells the story lower-middle-class workers in the city of Kawasaki, and their troubles and travails.
