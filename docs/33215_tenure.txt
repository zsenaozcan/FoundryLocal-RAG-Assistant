Title: Tenure (2009)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mike Million
Main Cast: Luke Wilson, Gretchen Mol, David Koechner, Sasha Alexander, Bob Gunton
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Despite his outstanding intellect, associate professor Charlie Thurber is a chronic underachiever and has never received university tenure. Aided by his nutty best friend, Charlie launches a final effort to make the grade at Gray College. But a beautiful new teacher whose ascending star threatens to eclipse him shakes up Charlie's plans.
