Title: Lisztomania (1975)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Music
Director: Ken Russell
Main Cast: Roger Daltrey, Sara Kestelman, Paul Nicholas, Ringo Starr, Rick Wakeman
Keywords: composer, richard wagner

Overview: A send-up of the bawdy life of Romantic composer/piano virtuoso Franz Liszt, with ubiquitous phallic imagery and a good portion of the film devoted to Liszt's "friendship" with fellow composer Richard Wagner. The film begins during the time when Franz would give piano performance to a crowd of shrieking teenage fans while maintaining affairs with his (multiple!) mistresses. He eventually seeks Princess Carolyne of St. Petersburg (at her invitation), elopes, and, after their marriage is forbidden by the Pope, he embraces the monastic life as an abbé.
