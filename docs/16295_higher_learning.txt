Title: Higher Learning (1995)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Crime
Director: John Singleton
Main Cast: Omar Epps, Kristy Swanson, Michael Rapaport, Jennifer Connelly, Ice Cube
Keywords: racism, violence, date rape, athletic scholarship, black militant, resentment, school shooting

Overview: Malik (Omar Epps) is an African-American student attending on a track scholarship; academics are not his strong suit, and he goes in thinking that his athletic abilities will earn him a free ride through college. Fudge (Ice Cube), a "professional student" who has been at Columbus for six years so far, becomes friendly with Malik and challenges his views about race and politics in America.
