Title: The Midnight Hour (1985)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Romance
Director: Jack Bender
Main Cast: Shari Belafonte, LeVar Burton, Jonelle Allen, Jonna Lee, Kevin McCarthy

Overview: Phil, Melissa, Mitch, Mary, and Vinnie are high school friends, who unwittingly raise the dead on Halloween night. Once the dead have returned, Pitchford Cove will never be the same again....or will it?
