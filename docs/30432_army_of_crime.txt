Title: Army of Crime (2009)
Genres: War, Drama, History
Director: Robert Guédiguian
Main Cast: Simon Abkarian, Virginie Ledoyen, Robinson Stévenin, Lola Naymark, Adrien Jolivet
Keywords: paris, resistance, world war ii, ss, armenian genocide, ss commander

Overview: This gripping historical drama recounts the story of Armenian-born Missak Manouchian, a woodworker and political activist who led an immigrant laborer division of the Parisian Resistance on 30 operations against the Nazis in 1943. The Nazis branded the group an Army of Crime, an anti-immigrant propaganda stunt that backfired as the team's members became martyrs for the Resistance.
