Title: Cheyenne (1947)
Genres: Western
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: Dennis Morgan, Jane Wyman, Janis Paige, Bruce Bennett, Alan Hale
Keywords: gambler

Overview: A gambler is determined to capture the outlaw bandit responsible for a series of stagecoach robberies. Director Raoul Walsh's 1947 western stars Dennis Morgan and Jane Wyman.
