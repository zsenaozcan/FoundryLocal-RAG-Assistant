Title: Time Limit (1957)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, War
Director: Karl Malden
Main Cast: Richard Widmark, Richard Basehart, Dolores Michaels, June Lockhart, Martin Balsam
Keywords: general, korea

Overview: Military investigator Colonel Edwards is assigned a case involving Major Cargill, a Korean War POW who is accused of treason. Although Cargill admits his guilt and Edwards' superiors are impatiently pushing Edwards to move this case to court martial, Edwards becomes convinced of Cargill's innocence.
