Title: Kiss the Abyss (2012)
Genres: Horror
Director: Ken Winkler
Main Cast: Nikki Moore, Scott Mitchell Nelson, James Mathers, Mark Craig, Scott Wilson

Overview: When a young woman is brutally killed by an intruder, her husband and estranged father conspire to bring her back from the dead with the help of a mysterious desert dweller. Soon after she awakens, she begins to realize that something is horribly wrong...
