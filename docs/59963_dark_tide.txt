Title: Dark Tide (2012)
Genres: Thriller, Adventure, Drama
Director: John Stockwell
Main Cast: Halle Berry, Olivier Martinez, Ralph Brown, Mark Elderkin, Luke Tyler
Keywords: suspense, shark, diver, diver instructor

Overview: A traumatized shark expert (Halle Berry) must battle her own fears to lead a thrill-seeking businessman on a dive into a dangerous section of water known as "Shark Alley."
