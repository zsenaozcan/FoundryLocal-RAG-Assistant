Title: I Am Afraid (1977)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Damiano Damiani
Main Cast: Gian Maria Volonté, Erland Josephson, Mario Adorf, Angelica Ippolito, Bruno Corazzari

Overview: During a wave of political violence in Italy, a policeman who's already survived an assassination is assigned to act as a bodyguard to a morally upright judge. He feels increasingly fearful as the links of the upper echelons of police and government to the violence become clear.
