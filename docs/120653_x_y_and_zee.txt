Title: X, Y and Zee (1972)
Genres: Drama
Director: Brian G. Hutton
Main Cast: Elizabeth Taylor, Michael Caine, Susannah York, Margaret Leighton
Keywords: wife husband relationship, romance, foreign

Overview: The venomous and amoral wife of a wealthy architect tries, any way she can, to break up the blossoming romance between her husband and his new mistress; a good-natured young widow who holds a dark past.
