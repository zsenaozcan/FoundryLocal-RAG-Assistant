Title: Brother's Keeper (1992)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Bruce Sinofsky
Main Cast: Connie Chung, John Teeple, Delbert Ward, Roscoe Ward, Lyman Ward

Overview: This documentary by Joe Berlinger and Bruce Sinofsky details the murder trial of Delbert Ward. Delbert was a member of a family of four elderly brothers, working as semi-literate farmers and living together in isolation from the rest of society until William's death.
