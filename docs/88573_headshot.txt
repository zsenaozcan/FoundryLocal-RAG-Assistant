Title: Headshot (2011)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime
Director: Pen-Ek Ratanaruang
Main Cast: Sirin Horwang, Chanokporn Sayoungkul, Apisit Opasaimlikit, Kiat Punpiputt, Theeradanai Suwannahom

Overview: A hitman named Tul is shot in the head during an assignment. When he wakes up from a two month coma Tul discovers that he literally sees everything upside down.
