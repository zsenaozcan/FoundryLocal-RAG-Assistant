Title: An Afghan Love Story (2013)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Barmak Akram
Main Cast: Wajma Bahar, Mustafa Abdulsatar
Keywords: horror, student, kabul

Overview: It’s snowing in Kabul, and gregarious waiter Mustafa charms a pretty student named Wajma. The pair begin a clandestine relationship—they’re playful and passionate but ever mindful of the societal rules they are breaking. After Wajma discovers she is pregnant, her certainty that Mustafa will marry her falters, and word of their dalliance gets out. Her father must decide between his culturally held right to uphold family honor and his devotion to his daughter.
