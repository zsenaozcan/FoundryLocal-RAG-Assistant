Title: 15 Years and One Day (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Gracia Querejeta
Main Cast: Maribel Verdú, Belén López, Fernando Valverde, Arón Piper
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Jon is a troubled teenager who has been expelled from school. His mother decides to teach him a lesson sending him away with his grandfather Max, a retired military man who lives in a village by the seaside. But the time they will spend together won’t be that simple, Jon likes to flirt with danger and bad companies, and the veteran has now settled down into a comfortable life.
