Title: North Face (2008)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action, History, Thriller
Director: Philipp Stölzl
Main Cast: Benno Fürmann, Florian Lukas, Johanna Wokalek, Georg Friedrich, Simon Schwarz
Keywords: switzerland, bavaria, human vs nature, national socialism, climbing, mountain climbing, alps, mountaineering

Overview: North Face tells the story of two German climbers Toni Kurz and Andreas Hinterstoisser and their attempt to scale the deadly North Face of the Eiger.
