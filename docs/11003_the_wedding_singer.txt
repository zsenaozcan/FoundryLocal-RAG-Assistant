Title: The Wedding Singer (1998)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Frank Coraci
Main Cast: Adam Sandler, Drew Barrymore, Christine Taylor, Allen Covert, Jon Lovitz
Keywords: wedding vows, marriage proposal, love of one's life, bride, bridegroom

Overview: Robbie, a local rock star turned wedding singer, is dumped on the day of his wedding. Meanwhile, waitress Julia finally sets a wedding date with her fiancée Glenn. When Julia and Robbie meet and hit it off, they find that things are more complicated than anybody thought.
