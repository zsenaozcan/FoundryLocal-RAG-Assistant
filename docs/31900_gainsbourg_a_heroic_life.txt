Title: Gainsbourg: A Heroic Life (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Joann Sfar
Main Cast: Eric Elmosnino, Lucy Gordon, Laetitia Casta, Doug Jones, Anna Mouglalis
Keywords: father son relationship, beach, composer, jazz, seduction, pop star, musical, song, biography, singer

Overview: A glimpse at the life of French singer Serge Gainsbourg, from growing up in 1940s Nazi-occupied Paris through his successful song-writing years in the 1960s to his death in 1991 at the age of 62.
