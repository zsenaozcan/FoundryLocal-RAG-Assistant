Title: Beat Street (1984)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Stan Lathan
Main Cast: Guy Davis, Jon Chardiet, Rae Dawn Chong, Leon W. Grant, Saundra Santiago
Keywords: disc jockey, rap music, hip-hop, rhyme battle, subway, dance, breakdance, graffiti

Overview: An aspiring DJ, from the South Bronx, and his best friend, a promoter, try to get into show business by exposing people to hip-hop music and culture.
