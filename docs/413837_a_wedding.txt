Title: A Wedding (2017)
Genres: Drama
Director: Stephan Streker
Main Cast: Lina El Arabi, Sébastien Houbani, Babak Karimi, Olivier Gourmet, Alice de Lencquesaing

Overview: Zahira, 18, is close to her family until her parents ask her to follow Pakistani tradition to choose a husband. Torn between family customs and her western lifestyle, the young woman turns for help to her brother and confidant Amir.
