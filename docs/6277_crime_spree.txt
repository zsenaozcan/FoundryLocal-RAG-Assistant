Title: Crime Spree (2003)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Brad Mirman
Main Cast: Gérard Depardieu, Harvey Keitel, Johnny Hallyday, Renaud, Saïd Taghmaoui
Keywords: chicago, fbi, gangster boss, hoodlum, american mafia

Overview: An out-of-town heist becomes a nightmare for a crew of French burglars when they mistakenly rob the head of the Chicago mafia. Unaccustomed to the ways of the American underworld, it is not long before they have the mafia, the FBI and a couple of street gangs on their backs as they attempt to make their way back to Paris.
