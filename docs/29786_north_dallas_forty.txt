Title: North Dallas Forty (1979)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Ted Kotcheff
Main Cast: Nick Nolte, Mac Davis, Charles Durning, Dayle Haddon, Bo Svenson
Keywords: american football, sport

Overview: A semi-fictional account of life as a professional Football (American-style) player. Loosely based on the Dallas Cowboys team of the early 1970s.
