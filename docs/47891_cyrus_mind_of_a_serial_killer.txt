Title: Cyrus: Mind of a Serial Killer (2010)
Genres: Crime, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Mark Vadik
Main Cast: Brian Krause, Danielle Harris, Lance Henriksen, Anne Leighton, Kim Rhodes
Keywords: serial killer, mockumentary, tv reporter, cannibalism, meat market

Overview: A small independent news crew investigates a series of unexplained disappearances in a small Midwestern county. They find themselves interviewing a man who possesses an all too intimate knowledge of the details of "The County Line Cannibal" - Cyrus. Based on a true story.
