Title: Shock 'Em Dead (1991)
Genres: Music, Horror, Thriller
Director: Mark Freed
Main Cast: Traci Lords, Stephen Quadros, Troy Donahue, Aldo Ray, Tim Moffett
Keywords: female nudity, nudity, rock star, voodoo, heavy metal, pact with the devil, independent film, rock music, guitar playing

Overview: Martin is a total loser, who nobody cares for. When he fails to get a position as guitar player in Lindsay's band and loses his job on the way, he makes a deal with a Voodoo priestess. She promises him the fulfillment of all his dreams if he swears obedience to her. He becomes a rock star and has many women - but to stay alive, he has to kill other people.
