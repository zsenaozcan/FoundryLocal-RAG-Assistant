Title: Gregory's Two Girls (1999)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Bill Forsyth
Main Cast: John Gordon Sinclair, Maria Doyle Kennedy, Carly McKinnon, Dougray Scott, Fiona Bell
Keywords: american football, women's soccer

Overview: Twenty years after his teenage crush on a football-mad schoolgirl, Gregory is back at his old school, teaching English. When two of his pupils uncover evil practices at a local factory they want their teacher to help them expose the wrong-doer, who happens to be Greg's old schoolfriend.
