Title: Naked Souls (1996)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Lyndon Chubbuck
Main Cast: Brian Krause, Pamela Anderson, David Warner, Dean Stockwell, Clayton Rohner
Keywords: murder, serial killer, dream girl

Overview: Edward is busy trying to unlock the secrets of reading and recording people's thoughts. He is very involved with his work leaving little room for girlfriend Britt. Longstreet comes along and offers Edward a place to do his research and have unlimited funding. There are, however, ulterior motives as Edward is also somehow unlocking the secrets of eternal life.
