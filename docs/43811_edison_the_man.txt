Title: Edison, the Man (1940)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Clarence Brown
Main Cast: Spencer Tracy, Charles Coburn, Lynne Overman, Rita Johnson, Gene Lockhart
Keywords: historical figure, light bulb, thomas edison

Overview: In flashback, fifty years after inventing the light bulb, an 82-year-old Edison tells his story starting at age twenty-two with his arrival in New York. He's on his way with invention of an early form of stock market ticker.
