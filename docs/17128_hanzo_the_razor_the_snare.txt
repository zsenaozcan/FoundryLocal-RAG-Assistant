Title: Hanzo the Razor: The Snare (1973)
Genres: History, Action, Drama, Foreign
Director: Yasuzô Masumura
Main Cast: Shintarô Katsu, Toshio Kurosawa, Kô Nishimura, Kei Satô, Kazuko Ineno
Keywords: samurai

Overview: Against the backdrop of the Edo treasury devaluing currency and driving many into poverty, Hanzo Itami enforces the law without regard to status. He shows inadequate respect to the treasurer, who wants him dead.
