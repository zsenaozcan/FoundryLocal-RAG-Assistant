Title: Spies (1928)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Fritz Lang
Main Cast: Rudolf Klein-Rogge, Willy Fritsch, Paul Hörbiger, Lien Deyers, Gerda Maurus
Keywords: chase, criminal mastermind

Overview: The mastermind behind a ubiquitous spy operation learns of a dangerous romance between a Russian lady in his employ and a dashing agent from the government's secret service.
