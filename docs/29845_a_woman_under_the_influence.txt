Title: A Woman Under the Influence (1974)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: John Cassavetes
Main Cast: Peter Falk, Gena Rowlands, Fred Draper, Lady Rowlands, Katherine Cassavetes
Keywords: mental breakdown, unfaithfulness

Overview: Mabel, a mother and wife, is on the brink of insanity as her blue-collar husband Nick attempts to understand her plight.
