Title: Suddenly (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Johan Brisinger
Main Cast: Michael Nyqvist, Catherine Hansson, Moa Gammel, Anastasios Soulis, Sten Ljunggren

Overview: The mother and the younger son in a happy family dies in a car-accident. The father and the teenage-son Jonas survives the accident. Now they have to move on with their lives together.
