Title: Hotel Meina (2007)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Carlo Lizzani
Main Cast: Majlinda Agaj, Eugenio Allegri, Marta Bifano, Veronica Bruni, Ursula Buschhorn
Keywords: hotel, refugee

Overview: How will the Germans react to the armistice of the 8th of September 1943, due to which Italy betrays them and sides with the allied forces? This is the distressing questions that a group of 16 Italian - Greek Jews evacuated at The Meina Hotel ask themselves.
