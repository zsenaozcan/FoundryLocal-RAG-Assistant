Title: Tizoc (1956)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Ismael Rodríguez
Main Cast: María Félix, Pedro Infante, Andrés Soler, Alicia del Lago, Eduardo Fajardo
Keywords: revenge, native american, melodrama, poverty, hatred, racial stereotype, discrimination, scarf

Overview: The story of an indigenous who gave his life for the love of a white woman, whom he confused, because of her beauty, with Maria the virgin, fighting against the society, the prejudices, and the race.
