Title: Khottabych (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Pyotr Tochilin
Main Cast: Vladimir Tolokonnikov, Marius Jampolskis, Boris Shcherbakov, Liva Kruminya, Mila Lipner

Overview: Young programmer and hacker Gena meets Khottabych - a genie who can't quite understand today life realities after spending thousands of years in a lamp.
