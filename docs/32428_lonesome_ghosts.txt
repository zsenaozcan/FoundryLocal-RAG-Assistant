Title: Lonesome Ghosts (1937)
Genres: Animation, Comedy
Director: Burt Gillett
Main Cast: Walt Disney, Pinto Colvig, Clarence Nash, Billy Bletcher
Keywords: ghostbuster, haunted house, mickey mouse, surrealism, haunting

Overview: Four bored ghosts in a haunted house who've scared everyone away call up Ghost Hunters Mickey, Donald, and Goofy in hopes to have a little fun scaring them off.
