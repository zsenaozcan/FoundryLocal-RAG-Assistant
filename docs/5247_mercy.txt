Title: Mercy (2000)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Damian Harris
Main Cast: Ellen Barkin, Wendy Crewson, Peta Wilson, Karen Young, Julian Sands
Keywords: sadomasochism, murder, erotic movie

Overview: Detective Catherine Palmer (Ellen Barkin) is on the trail of an elusive serial killer. During her investigation she meets Vickie Kittrie (Peta Wilson), who belongs to an exclusive club of women who engage in secret sessions of bondage and S&amp;M. Matters become even more complicated when Palmer finds herself attracted to Kittrie, leading to a brief lesbian encounter. Palmer soon learns that each victim belonged to this club of prominent, sexually experimental women. In order to catch the killer, Catherine must trust Vickie to guide her through the dangerous and illicit underground.
