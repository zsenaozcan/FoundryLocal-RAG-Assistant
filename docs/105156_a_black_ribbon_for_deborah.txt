Title: A Black Ribbon for Deborah (1974)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Horror
Director: Marcello Andrei
Main Cast: Bradford Dillman, Marina Malfatti, Gig Young, Delia Boccardo, Micaela Esdra
Keywords: supernatural, psychic, birth

Overview: A young woman with psychic powers is obsessed with having a child. Even though she is unable to conceive, her desire to do so creates a supernatural force determined to fulfill her wishes
