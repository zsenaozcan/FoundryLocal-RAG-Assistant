Title: Skin Game (1971)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Western
Director: Gordon Douglas
Main Cast: James Garner, Louis Gossett, Jr., Susan Clark, Brenda Sykes, Ed Asner
Keywords: con man, slave

Overview: Quincy Drew (Garner) and Jason O'Rourke (Gossett) travel from town to town in the south of the United States during the slavery era. Drew claims to be a down-on-his-luck slave owner who is selling O'Rourke as a slave. Quincy gets the bidding rolling, sells Jason, and the two later meet up to split the profit. Jason was born a free man in New Jersey and is very well educated.
