Title: Torch Song (1953)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Charles Walters
Main Cast: Joan Crawford, Gig Young, Michael Wilding, Marjorie Rambeau, Harry Morgan
Keywords: diva, female protagonist, broadway star

Overview: Jenny Stewart is a tough Broadway musical star who doesn't take criticism from anyone. Yet there is one individual, Tye Graham, a blind pianist who may be able to break through her tough exterior.
