Title: Hillbillys in a Haunted House (1967)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Music
Director: Jean Yarbrough
Main Cast: Ferlin Husky, Joi Lansing, Don Bowman, John Carradine, Lon Chaney Jr.
Keywords: hillbilly

Overview: Country singers on their way to Nashville have car trouble, forcing them to stop at an old haunted mansion. Soon they realize that the house is not only haunted, but is also the headquarters of a ring of international spies after a top secret formula for rocket fuel.
