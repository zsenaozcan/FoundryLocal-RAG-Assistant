Title: The Haunted House (1921)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Buster Keaton
Main Cast: Buster Keaton, Virginia Fox, Joe Roberts, Edward F. Cline, Natalie Talmadge
Keywords: haunted house

Overview: Buster Keaton is a bank teller who becomes involved with a hold-up, counterfeiters, and a theatrical troupe posing as spooks in a haunted house.
