Title: Screaming Masterpiece (2005)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ari Alexander Ergis Magnússon
Main Cast: Hilmar Örn Hilmarsson, Björk, Damon Albarn, Barði Jóhannsson, Walter Durkacz
Keywords: musical

Overview: A documentary about the bustling Icelandic musical scene. This documentary covers some of Iceland's most talented and well-known musicians.
