Title: Rage (2009)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Foreign, Romance
Director: Sebastián Cordero
Main Cast: Martina García, Icíar Bollaín, Yon González, Gustavo Sánchez Parra, Fernando Tielve

Overview: A romantic thriller about a construction worker in hiding for killing his foreman who hides in the mansion where his girlfriend works as a maid.
