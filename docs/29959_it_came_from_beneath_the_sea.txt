Title: It Came from Beneath the Sea (1955)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Robert Gordon
Main Cast: Kenneth Tobey, Faith Domergue, Donald Curtis, Ian Keith, Dean Maddox Jr.
Keywords: submarine, san francisco, radiation, octopus, water monster, stop motion, scientist, animal horror

Overview: A Giant Octopus, whose feeding habits have been affected by radiation from H-Bomb tests, rises from the Mindanao Deep to terrorize the California Coast.
