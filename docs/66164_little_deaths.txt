Title: Little Deaths (2011)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, Romance
Director: Sean Hogan
Main Cast: Luke de Lacey, Holly Lucas, Siubhan Harrison, James Oliver Wheatley, Jodie Jameson
Keywords: sex, tramp, sadistic sex, pet dog

Overview: Composed of three disturbingly sensual and terrifying short narratives, unified by the twin themes of sex and death.
