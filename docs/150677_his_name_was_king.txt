Title: His Name was King (1971)
Genres: Western
Director: Giancarlo Romitelli
Main Cast: Richard Harrison, Klaus Kinski, Anne Puskin, Goffredo Unger, Lorenzo Fineschi
Keywords: gunfight, smugglers

Overview: The Benson brothers, who are smuggling guns across the Mexican border, kill King's brother and rape his wife. King must stop their illegal activities, find who is behind them and gain revenge for his family.
