Title: El Estudiante (2010)
Genres: Foreign, Drama
Director: Roberto Girault
Main Cast: Jorge Lavat, Jeannine Derbez, Pablo Cruz Guerrero, Norma Lazareno, Jeronimo Medina

Overview: After retiring to the beautiful Mexican town of Guanajuato, a 70 year old decides to follow his dreams and enroll at the university where he stumbles upon a new generation and they are bound together by the novel Don Quijote de la Mancha.
