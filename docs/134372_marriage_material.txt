Title: Marriage Material (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Joe Swanberg
Main Cast: Kentucker Audley, Joe Swanberg, Kris Swanberg, Caroline White, Adam Wingard

Overview: Emily and Andrew, a young couple living in Memphis, agree to babysit their friend’s 6-month-old for a day. The experience causes them to examine their own relationship and their feelings about marriage and children.
