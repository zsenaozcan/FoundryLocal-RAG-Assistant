Title: Anarchy Parlor (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: Kenny Gage
Main Cast: Robert LaSardo, Jordan James Smith, Sara Fabel, Tiffany DeMarco, Claire Garvey
Keywords: tattoo, tattooing

Overview: Six young college hopefuls vacationing and partying in Lithuania get more than they bargained for when they unwittingly get caught up in a maniacal tattoo artist's fiendish side business.
