Title: Panic Button (1964)
Genres: Comedy
Director: George Sherman
Main Cast: Maurice Chevalier, Eleanor Parker, Jayne Mansfield, Mike Connors, Akim Tamiroff
Keywords: farce, filmmaking, shakespeare's romeo and juliet

Overview: A businessman comes up with a scheme to make money by financing a film version of "Romeo and Juliet" that's destined to fail, thus giving him some major tax breaks.
