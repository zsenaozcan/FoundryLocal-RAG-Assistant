Title: The Grinch Grinches the Cat in the Hat (1982)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Bill Perez
Main Cast: Mason Adams, Bob Holt, Frank Welker, Joe Eich

Overview: The Cat in the Hat is all set for a lovely picnic, but the evil Grinch changes his plans by inventing a contraption that captures noise and makes it sound ferocious. The Cat has to save the world from the clutches of the Grinch and the only way to do it is to reach Grinch's soft spot.
