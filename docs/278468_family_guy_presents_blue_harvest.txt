Title: Family Guy Presents: Blue Harvest (2007)
Genres: Animation
Director: Dominic Polcino
Main Cast: Seth MacFarlane, Alex Borstein, Seth Green, Mila Kunis, Mike Henry

Overview: With the Griffins stuck at home during a blackout, Peter begins to tell a story, which leads to a Star Wars flashback. Acting out scenes from Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope.
