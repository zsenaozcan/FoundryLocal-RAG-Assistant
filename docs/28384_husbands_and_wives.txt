Title: Husbands and Wives (1992)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Woody Allen
Main Cast: Woody Allen, Mia Farrow, Judy Davis, Sydney Pollack, Juliette Lewis
Keywords: musical, marriage, dialogue driven, teacher student relationship, fake documentary

Overview: When Jack and Sally announce that they're splitting up, this comes as a shock to their best friends Gabe and Judy. Maybe mostly because they also are drifting apart and are now being made aware of it. So while Jack and Sally try to go on and meet new people, the marriage of Gabe and Judy gets more and more strained, and they begin to find themselves being attracted to other people.
