Title: The Kid Stays in the Picture (2002)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Nanette Burstein
Main Cast: Robert Evans, Norma Shearer, Ali MacGraw, Ernest Hemingway, Catherine Deneuve
Keywords: cocaine, arranged marriage, love, hollywood, woman director

Overview: Documentary about legendary Paramount producer Robert Evans (the film shares the same name as Evans's famous 1994 autobiography).
