Title: Lulu in the Nude (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Sólveig Anspach
Main Cast: Karin Viard, Bouli Lanners, Claude Gensac, Pascal Demolon, Philippe Rebbot
Keywords: woman director

Overview: After a job interview gone wrong, Lulu sets sail leaving her husband and three children behind. But being an adventurer is easier said than done.
