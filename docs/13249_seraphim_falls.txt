Title: Seraphim Falls (2006)
Genres: Western, Drama
Director: David Von Ancken
Main Cast: Liam Neeson, Pierce Brosnan, Michael Wincott, Xander Berkeley, Ed Lauter
Keywords: frontier, colonel, storm, bullet, remedy, union soldier, post civil war

Overview: The Civil War has ended, but Colonel Morsman Carver is on one final mission – to kill Gideon, no matter what it takes. Launched by a gunshot and propelled by rage, the relentless pursuit takes the two men through frigid snow-capped mountains and arid deserts, far from the comforts and codes of civilisation, into the bloodiest recesses of their own souls.
