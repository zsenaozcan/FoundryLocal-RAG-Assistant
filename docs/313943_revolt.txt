Title: Revolt (2017)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Joe Miale
Main Cast: Lee Pace, Bérénice Marlohe, Jason Flemyng, Sibulele Gcilitshana, Wandile Molebatsi
Keywords: alien invasion

Overview: The story of humankind's last stand against a cataclysmic alien invasion. Set in the war-ravaged African countryside, a U.S. soldier and a French foreign aid worker team up to survive the alien onslaught. Their bond will be tested as they search for refuge across a crumbling world.
