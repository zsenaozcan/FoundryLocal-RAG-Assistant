Title: Flashback (1969)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Raffaele Andreassi
Main Cast: Fred Robsahm, Pilar Castel

Overview: Heinz is a German soldier stationed as a sniper overlooking his own army from a tree. When he falls asleep, his troops are gone and he is left alone to defend the incoming enemy invasion. Flashbacks recall his wartime experiences and his transition from a human being into a sadistic murderer and rapist.
