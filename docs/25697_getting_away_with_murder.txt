Title: Getting Away with Murder (1996)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Harvey Miller
Main Cast: Jack Lemmon, Dan Aykroyd, Bonnie Hunt, J.C. Quinn, Jerry Adler
Keywords: german, instructor, war criminal

Overview: When the very moralistic college ethics instructor Jack Lambert finds himself living next door to an accused German death camp commander, he takes it upon himself to rid the world of this man.
