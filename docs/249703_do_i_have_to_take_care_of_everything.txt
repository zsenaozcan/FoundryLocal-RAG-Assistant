Title: Do I Have to Take Care of Everything? (2014)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Selma Vilhunen
Main Cast: Joanna Haartti, Santtu Karvonen, Jukka Kärkkäinen, Ella Toivoniemi, Ranja Omaheimo

Overview: A comedy about a chaotic morning in a family with kids, and a mother who is determined that it's best to take care of everything herself.
