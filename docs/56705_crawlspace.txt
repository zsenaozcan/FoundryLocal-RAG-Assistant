Title: Crawlspace (1986)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: David Schmoeller
Main Cast: Klaus Kinski, Talia Balsam, Barbara Whinnery, Carole Francis, Tane McClure

Overview: A man who runs an apartment house for women is the demented son of a Nazi surgeon who has the house equipped with secret passageways, hidden rooms and torture and murder devices.
