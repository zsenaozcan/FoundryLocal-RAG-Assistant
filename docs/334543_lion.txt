Title: Lion (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Garth Davis
Main Cast: Sunny Pawar, Dev Patel, Rooney Mara, David Wenham, Nicole Kidman
Keywords: based on novel, indian lead, adoption, australia, based on true story, india, missing child, long lost relative, tasmania, passenger train

Overview: A five-year-old Indian boy gets lost on the streets of Calcutta, thousands of kilometers from home. He survives many challenges before being adopted by a couple in Australia; 25 years later, he sets out to find his lost family.
