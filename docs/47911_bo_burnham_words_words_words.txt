Title: Bo Burnham: Words, Words, Words (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Shannon Hartman
Main Cast: Bo Burnham
Keywords: one-man show, stand-up comedy

Overview: Bo Burnham is back with a new one-man show full of his patented songs and wordplay, as well as haikus, dramatic readings, blasphemy, and so much more in his first hour-long special, shot live in his home town of Boston.
