Title: Comedian (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary
Director: Christian Charles
Main Cast: Jerry Seinfeld, Orny Adams, Greg Giraldo, Jim Norton, Sherrod Small
Keywords: comedian, stand-up comedy

Overview: A look at the work of two stand-up comics, Jerry Seinfeld and a lesser-known newcomer, detailing the effort and frustration behind putting together a successful act and career while living a life on the road.
