Title: Pop Skull (2007)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Adam Wingard
Main Cast: Lane Hughes, Brandon Carroll, Maggie Gwin, Hannah Hughes, Jeff Dylan Graham
Keywords: independent film, drug, prescription drug abuse

Overview: Addled prescription drug addict Daniel finds himself unraveling further under the stress of a recent breakup. Worse yet, he lives in a house haunted by nightmarish events from the past, images of which torment him in terrifying dreams. This hallucinatory horror film leaps off the screen with its disturbingly vivid visuals.
