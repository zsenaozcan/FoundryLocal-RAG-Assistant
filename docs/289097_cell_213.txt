Title: Cell 213 (2011)
Genres: Horror
Director: Stephen T. Kay
Main Cast: Bruce Greenwood, Eric Balfour, Michael Rooker, Deborah Valente
Keywords: reference to god, location in title, two word title

Overview: Cocky young attorney Michael Gray finds himself framed for murder when an inmate he is defending violently kills himself during their interview at South River State Penitentiary. Now locked in the same nightmarish Cell 213 where his client died, he soon realizes that unnatural forces are behind a string of inmate suicides, making matters of guilt and innocence not as cut and dry as they seem.
