Title: Hulchul (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Priyadarshan
Main Cast: Akshaye Khanna, Kareena Kapoor, Arshad Warsi, Sunil Shetty, Paresh Rawal
Keywords: remake of malayalam film
