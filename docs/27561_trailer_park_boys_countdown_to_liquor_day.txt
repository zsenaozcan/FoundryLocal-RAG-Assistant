Title: Trailer Park Boys: Countdown to Liquor Day (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Mike Clattenburg
Main Cast: John Paul Tremblay, Robb Wells, Mike Smith, John Dunsworth, Lucy Decoutere
Keywords: nudity, white trash, jack daniels, marijuana

Overview: Ricky, Julian and Bubbles are about to get out of jail, and this time, Julian vows to go straight, even open a legit business. Soon the Boys will all be rich. At least that's what they've told the parole board. But when they arrive back at the park, they find it's not the same old Sunnyvale - and it's not the same old Jim Lahey, Trailer Park Supervisor.
