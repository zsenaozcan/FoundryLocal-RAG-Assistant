Title: Moana (1926)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Robert J. Flaherty
Main Cast: Ta'avale, Fa'amgase, Tama, T'ugaita, Pe'a
Keywords: south seas, coconut, tattoo art, dance ritual, native

Overview: Robert J. Flaherty's South Seas follow-up to Nanook of the North is a Gauguin idyll moved by "pride of beauty... pride of strength."
