Title: Pleasure Cruise (1933)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Frank Tuttle
Main Cast: Genevieve Tobin, Roland Young, Ralph Forbes, Una O'Connor, Herbert Mundin
Keywords: cruise, marriage, vacation

Overview: Roland Young and Genevieve Tobin have been married for one year, and they feel as if they are drifting apart in their marriage. They decide that they will take separate vacations away from each other to sort the marriage out. Tobin decides to go on a pleasure cruise, and while aboard many available men begin to hit on her, and she begins to contemplate having a quick shipboard fling with one of the men.
