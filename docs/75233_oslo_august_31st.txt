Title: Oslo, August 31st (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Joachim Trier
Main Cast: Anders Danielsen Lie, Hans Olav Brenner, Ingrid Olava, Tone Beate Mostraum, Malin Crépin
Keywords: old friends, suicide thoughts

Overview: Anders is a recovering drug addict in an Oslo rehab clinic. On the 30th August, he is given a day’s leave to attend a job interview in the city centre.
