Title: Dark Water (2002)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Hideo Nakata
Main Cast: Hitomi Kuroki, Mirei Oguchi, Fumiyo Kohinata, Asami Mizukawa, Rio Kanno
Keywords: nightmare, water, mother daughter relationship, family, ghost, ghost child, child custody, new apartment

Overview: A woman in the midst of an unpleasant divorce moves to an eerie apartment building with her young daughter. The ceiling of their apartment has a dark and active leak.
