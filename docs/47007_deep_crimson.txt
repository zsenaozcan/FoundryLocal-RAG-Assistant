Title: Deep Crimson (1996)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Crime
Director: Arturo Ripstein
Main Cast: Regina Orozco, Daniel Giménez Cacho, Marisa Paredes, Julieta Egurrola, Sherlyn
Keywords: gigolo, widow, lonely hearts

Overview: The life of a man who preys on unsuspecting women for a living is changed when he finds an accomplice in the woman who loves and controls him.
