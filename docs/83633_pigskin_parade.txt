Title: Pigskin Parade (1936)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: David Butler
Main Cast: Stuart Erwin, Patsy Kelly, Jack Haley, Johnny Downs, Betty Grable
Keywords: american football, hillbilly, yale

Overview: Bessie and Winston "Slug" Winters are married coaches whose mission is to whip their college football team into shape. Just in time, they discover a hillbilly farmhand and his sister. But the hillbilly farmhand's ability to throw melons enables him to become their star passing ace.
