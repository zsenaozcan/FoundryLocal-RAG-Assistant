Title: 10+10 (2011)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Sylvia Chang
Main Cast: Shu Qi, Gwei Lun-mei, Lawrence Ko, Leon Dai, Joseph Chang Hsiao-Chuan
Keywords: woman director

Overview: 10+10 is a project initiated by the Taipei Golden Horse Film Festival to demonstrate the solidarity between Taiwanese film-makers. 20 directors are invited to make a 5-minute short film each on the theme of the “Uniqueness of Taiwan,” but allowed total freedom in all other aspects.
