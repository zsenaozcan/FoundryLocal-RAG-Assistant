Title: The Breed (2006)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Comedy
Director: Nicholas Mastandrea
Main Cast: Michelle Rodriguez, Taryn Manning, Eric Lively, Oliver Hudson, Hill Harper
Keywords: exotic island, baseball bat, vacation, electrocution, cabin in the woods, dog, german shepherd, remote island, animal attack, dog attack

Overview: Brothers John and Matt have inherited an island cabin from their recently deceased uncle. Along with Matt's girlfriend, Nicki, and other mutual friends, the siblings travel to the cabin for a relaxing weekend getaway. But, not long after arriving, the group is besieged by ravenous dogs. They watch in horror as another vacationer, Luke, is eaten alive. Soon, they discover a training facility where the dogs have been bred to kill.
