Title: Dinotopia: Quest for the Ruby Sunstone (2005)
Genres: Fantasy, Science Fiction, Family
Director: Davis Doi
Main Cast: Alyssa Milano, Jamie Kennedy, Kathy Griffin, Wayne Knight, Malcolm McDowell

Overview: An Orphaned Boy sets out in search of adventure, but when a shipwreck lands him on an island where Dinosaurs and Humans coexist, he finds not only adventure but a family as well.
