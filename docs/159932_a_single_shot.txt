Title: A Single Shot (2013)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Thriller
Director: David M. Rosenthal
Main Cast: Sam Rockwell, William H. Macy, Jeffrey Wright, Kelly Reilly, Ted Levine
Keywords: based on novel, hunt

Overview: The tragic death of a beautiful young girl starts a tense and atmospheric game of cat and mouse between hunter John Moon and the hardened backwater criminals out for his blood.
