Title: A Cinderella Story: Once Upon a Song (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Romance
Director: Damon Santostefano
Main Cast: Lucy Hale, Missi Pyle, Megan Park, Freddie Stroma, Titus Makin Jr.
Keywords: cinderella, music, high school, aspiring singer, orphan, teenager, teenage romance, modern fairy tale

Overview: In this modern telling of the classic tale, aspiring singer Katie Gibbs falls for the new boy at her performing arts high school. But Katie's wicked stepmother and stepsister are scheming to crush her dream before she can sing her way into his heart.
