Title: Leviathan: The Story of Hellraiser and Hellbound: Hellraiser II (2015)
Genres: Horror, Documentary
Director: Kevin McDonagh
Main Cast: Doug Bradley, Clare Higgins, Christopher Figg, Simon Bamford, William Hope
Keywords: cenobite

Overview: Leviathan: The Story of Hellraiser and Hellbound: Hellraiser II is a feature length documentary uncovering the history and the making of Clive Barker's Hellraiser and Hellbound: Hellraiser II films.
