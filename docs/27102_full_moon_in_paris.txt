Title: Full Moon in Paris (1984)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Éric Rohmer
Main Cast: Pascale Ogier, Tchéky Karyo, Fabrice Luchini, Virginie Thévenet, Christian Vadim
Keywords: relationship problems, alienation, best friend, existentialism

Overview: "He who has two women loses his soul, he who has two houses loses his mind." So it goes with Louise played by Pascale Ogier who is dissatisfied with her mundane life in a bleak Parisian new-town. She rents a pied-à-terre in the city so she can experience independence.
