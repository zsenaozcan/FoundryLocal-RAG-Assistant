Title: Silent Ones (2013)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ricky Rijneke
Main Cast: Orsolya Tóth, Roland Rába, Fatih Dervisoglu

Overview: A young Hungarian woman (played by Hungarian actress Orsi Tóth) wakes up inside a crashed car in the middle of nowhere, not knowing where her brother Isti is. Upset and alone, she leaves aboard a cargo ship to keep a promise she made to him. Once at sea, she withdraws into a dream world and loses grip on her life completely.
