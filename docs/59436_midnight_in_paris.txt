Title: Midnight in Paris (2011)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Romance
Director: Woody Allen
Main Cast: Owen Wilson, Rachel McAdams, Kurt Fuller, Mimi Kennedy, Michael Sheen
Keywords: paris, painter, detective, based on novel, screenwriter, forbidden love, time travel, midnight, nostalgia, past
