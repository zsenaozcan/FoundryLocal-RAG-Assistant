Title: Born in China (2017)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Lu Chuan
Main Cast: John Krasinski, Zhou Xun
Keywords: china, documentary, animal, nature

Overview: Venturing into the wilds of China, "Born in China" captures intimate moments with a panda bear and her growing cub, a young golden monkey who feels displaced by his baby sister, and a mother snow leopard struggling to raise her two cubs.
