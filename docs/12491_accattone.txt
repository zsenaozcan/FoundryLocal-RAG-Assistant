Title: Accattone (1961)
Genres: Drama
Director: Pier Paolo Pasolini
Main Cast: Franco Citti, Franca Pasut, Silvana Corsini, Paola Guidi, Adriana Asti
Keywords: prostitute, rome, robbery, pimp, poverty, violence
