Title: 009 Re: Cyborg (2012)
Genres: Action, Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Kenji Kamiyama
Main Cast: Hisao Egawa, Toshiko Fujita, Hiroshi Kamiya, Houko Kuwashima, Hikaru Midorikawa
Keywords: cyborg, futuristic

Overview: 009 Re:Cyborg follows a group of nine cyborgs, each of them created by a shadowy organization for use as weapons against humanity. The group turns on their creators to protect the population instead, using the powers given them to fight their creators.
