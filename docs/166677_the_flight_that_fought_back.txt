Title: The Flight That Fought Back (2004)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Bruce Goodison
Main Cast: Greg Benson, Toni M. Youngblood, Jason LeGrande, Bryce Wagoner
Keywords: muslim, terrorist, flight, terrorism, hijack, 9/11

Overview: A docudrama about the efforts of the passengers aboard United Airlines Flight 93 to derail the 9/11 terrorists plan for the hijacked airliner.
