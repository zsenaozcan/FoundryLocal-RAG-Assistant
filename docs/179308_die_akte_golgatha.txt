Title: Die Akte Golgatha (2010)
Genres: Adventure
Director: Zoltan Spirandelli
Main Cast: Marco Girnth, Katharina Schüttler, Nina Hartmann, Helmfried von Lüttichau, Rolf Kanies

Overview: Die Akte Golgatha ​​(or: Akte Golgatha) is a TV movie from 2010, produced by UFA TV production. The film is based on a novel by Philipp Vandenberg and combines elements of an adventure film with a comedy.
