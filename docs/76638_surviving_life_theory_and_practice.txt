Title: Surviving Life (Theory and Practice) (2010)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Jan Švankmajer
Main Cast: Václav Helšus, Klára Issová, Daniela Bakerová, Zuzana Krónerová

Overview: Eugene, an aging man, leads a double life – one real, the other in his dreams. He goes to see a psychoanalyst, who tries to interpret the meaning of his dreams. Eugene finds a way of entering his dream-world at will and finds out about his childhood and what really happened to his parents.
