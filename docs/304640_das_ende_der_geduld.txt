Title: Das Ende der Geduld (2014)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Christian Wagner
Main Cast: Martina Gedeck, Jörg Hartmann, Sascha Alexander Geršak, Sesede Terziyan, Mohamed Issa
Keywords: juvenile delinquent, female judge, juvenile crime

Overview: Corinna Kleist is a juvenile court judge who, after a leave of absence following the suicide of a convicted teenager, is appointed to a 'problem area' in Berlin. She now tries harder than ever to change the system in order to prevent troubled kids from being turned into vicious criminals.
