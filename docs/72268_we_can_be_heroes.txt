Title: We Can Be Heroes! (2002)
Genres: Family, Drama
Director: Ulf Malmros
Main Cast: Ariel Petsonk, Zamand Hägg, Michael Nyqvist, Anna Pettersson, Vilma Rogsten-Zammel
Keywords: bullying, soccer

Overview: Two kids - about 11 years old - Marcello and Fatima, meet and like each other very much. Both have dreams and problems. Marcello wants to fly but feels like an absolutely useless human being, Fatima wants to play soccer - which is not acceptable for her Muslim family. But then they start to support each other in a very delicate and subtle manner...
