Title: North and South, Book II (1986)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Kevin Connor
Main Cast: Patrick Swayze, Philip Casnoff, Kirstie Alley, Genie Francis

Overview: The continuing saga of the Hazards and the Mains. The Hazards are from the North and the Mains from the South. George Hazard and Orry Main met at West Point and fought in the Mexican American war together becoming fast friends. Now they find themselves fighting on opposite sides of the Civil War and struggle to maintain their friendship through these troubled times.
