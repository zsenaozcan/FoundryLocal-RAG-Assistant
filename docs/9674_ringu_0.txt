Title: Ringu 0 (2000)
Genres: Horror
Director: Norio Tsuruta
Main Cast: Yukie Nakama, Seiichi Tanabe, Kumiko Asou, Yoshiko Tanaka, Takeshi Wakamatsu
Keywords: journalist, dying and death, theatre group, wizardry, prequel, killer, parapsychology

Overview: Taking place thirty years before the events of Ringu, Ringu 0 provides the shocking background story of how the girl on the video became a deadly, vengeful spirit.
