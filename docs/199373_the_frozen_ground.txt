Title: The Frozen Ground (2013)
Genres: Thriller, Crime
Director: Scott Walker
Main Cast: Nicolas Cage, Vanessa Hudgens, John Cusack, Radha Mitchell, Jodi Lyn O'Keefe
Keywords: gun, escape, serial killer, man hunt, hunting
