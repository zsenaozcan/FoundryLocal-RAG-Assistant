Title: The Land Before Time IX: Journey to the Big Water (2002)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Charles Grosvenor
Main Cast: John Ingle, Kenneth Mars, Miriam Flynn, Thomas Dekker, Anndi McAfee

Overview: Beloved dinosaurs Littlefoot, Cera, Spike, Petrie and Ducky return in this story about the wonders of discovery that features new songs, including Donny Osmond's "No One Has to Be Alone."
