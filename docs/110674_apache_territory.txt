Title: Apache Territory (1958)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Ray Nazarro 
Main Cast: Rory Calhoun, Barbara Bates, John Dehner, Carolyn Craig, Tom Pittman
Keywords: water, apache, drifter, soldier, dust storm

Overview: Logan Cates (Rory Calhoun) sets out to rescue a white woman captured by Apache Indians and prevent a war. On the way he is joined by a few civilians and a small band of soldiers at a water hole. They are ambushed and laid siege to by Apache. As their food and water supplies dwindle a storm arrives which enables Cates to put an escape plan into action.
