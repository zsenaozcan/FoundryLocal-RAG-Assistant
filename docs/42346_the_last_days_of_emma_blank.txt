Title: The Last Days of Emma Blank (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Foreign
Director: Alex van Warmerdam
Main Cast: Marlies Heuer, Eva van de Wijdeven, Annet Malherbe, Eugene Bervoets, Marwan Kenzari

Overview: A woman living in a large country home drives her servants to mutiny with her outrageous demands as she waits for death to come for her.
