Title: Magician: The Astonishing Life and Work of Orson Welles (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Chuck Workman
Main Cast: Orson Welles, Peter Bogdanovich, Julie Taymor, Steven Spielberg, Elvis Mitchell
Keywords: film director, biography, orson welles, theater, actor

Overview: Combining extensive filmmaker interviews and rare archival footage, Chuck Workman’s documentary takes us through the life of one of cinema’s greatest masters: Orson Welles.
