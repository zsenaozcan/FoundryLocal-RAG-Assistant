Title: Becoming Me (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Mina Laamo
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: blogger

Overview: Becoming Me is a film about three young women, Elli, Laura and Juulia. Each of them writes a blog on her life on the Internet, all of them sharing their lives with strangers. Each of them is struggling with the real or imagined demands and pressures of today’s society.
