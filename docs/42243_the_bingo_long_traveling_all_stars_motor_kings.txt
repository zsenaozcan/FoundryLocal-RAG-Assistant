Title: The Bingo Long Traveling All-Stars & Motor Kings (1976)
Genres: Comedy
Director: John Badham
Main Cast: Billy Dee Williams, James Earl Jones, Richard Pryor, DeWayne Jessie
Keywords: baseball, sport, negro league

Overview: Tired of the slave-like treatment of his team's owner, charismatic star Negro League pitcher Bingo Long takes to the road with his band of barnstormers through the small towns of the Midwest in the 1930's.
