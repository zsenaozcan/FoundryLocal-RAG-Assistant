Title: Zatôichi the Outlaw (1967)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Drama
Director: Satsuo Yamamoto
Main Cast: Shintarô Katsu, Rentarô Mikuni, Kô Nishimura, Yuko Hamada, Toshiyuki Hosokawa
Keywords: samurai, yakuza, sequel, jail, zatoichi

Overview: When a local gambling house kidnaps some peasants because they failed to pay their debts, a rival gambling house pays their debts and sets them free.
