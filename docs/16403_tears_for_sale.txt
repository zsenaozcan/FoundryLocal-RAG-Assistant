Title: Tears for Sale (2008)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Uroš Stojanović
Main Cast: Sonja Kolačarić, Katarina Radivojević, Nenad Jezdić, Stefan Kapičić, Nada Šargin

Overview: Story of two sisters that grew up in a small Serbian village in the beginning of the 1930s. The village is torn up by wars and years long blood oath. There are no men left in the village. Our heroines, Ognjenka and Mala Boginja decide to go to the city, kidnap men and return life to their village. The lights of the metropolis dazzle them and there starts this little amusing and sentimental adventure.
