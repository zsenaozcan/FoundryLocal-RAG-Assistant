Title: Tampopo (1985)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Juzo Itami
Main Cast: Tsutomu Yamazaki, Nobuko Miyamoto, Ken Watanabe, Koji Yakusho, Rikiya Yasuoka
Keywords: widow, food, truck driver, hoodlum, noodle

Overview: A pair of truck drivers happen onto a decrepit roadside fast food stop selling ramen noodles. The widowed owner, Tampopo, pleads them to help her turn her establishment into a paragon of the "art of noodle soup making".
