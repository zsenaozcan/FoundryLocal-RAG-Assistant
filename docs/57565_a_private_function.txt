Title: A Private Function (1984)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Malcolm Mowbray
Main Cast: Michael Palin, Maggie Smith, Richard Griffiths, Denholm Elliott, Pete Postlethwaite

Overview: Life isn't easy in 1947 Yorkshire, where strict post-war rationing is putting the squeeze on the townspeople. But when a working-class couple discover that the town's wealthy elite has been secretly fattening up an illegal pig for their own selfish gluttony, they hatch an outlandish kidnapping scheme that gives new meaning to the phrase bringing home the bacon!
