Title: Smorgasbord (1983)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Jerry Lewis
Main Cast: Jerry Lewis, Milton Berle, Sammy Davis, Jr., Herb Edelman, Foster Brooks
Keywords: slapstick

Overview: Warren Nefron is a hopeless klutz who has some of the worst luck in the world: when he tries to end it all with a foolproof suicide plan, he still manages to mess it up. In desperation, he goes to a psychiatrist to see if there is some way for him to end his troubles.
