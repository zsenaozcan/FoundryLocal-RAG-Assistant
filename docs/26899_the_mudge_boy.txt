Title: The Mudge Boy (2003)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Burke
Main Cast: Emile Hirsch, Tom Guiry, Richard Jenkins, Pablo Schreiber, Zachary Knighton
Keywords: chicken, bullying, rape of a male, misfit, homosexuality, independent film, coming of age, gay sex

Overview: Duncan Mudge, mid-teens, lives apart from his rural world populated by his distant father and rough local kids. His main companionship is a chicken left after his mother's death until the neighbor's son befriends him.
