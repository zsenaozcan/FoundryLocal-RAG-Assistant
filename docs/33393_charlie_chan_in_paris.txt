Title: Charlie Chan in Paris (1935)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Lewis Seiler
Main Cast: Warner Oland, Mary Brian, Thomas Beck, John Miljan, Erik Rhodes
Keywords: charlie chan

Overview: Charlie's visit to Paris, ostensibly a vacation, is really a mission to investigate a bond-forgery racket. But his agent, apache dancer Nardi is killed before she can tell him much. The case, complicated by a false murder accusation for banker's daughter Yvette, climaxes with a strange journey through the Paris sewers.
