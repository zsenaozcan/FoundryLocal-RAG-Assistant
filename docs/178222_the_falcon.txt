Title: The Falcon (1981)
Genres: History, Drama, Action
Director: Vatroslav Mimica
Main Cast: Franco Nero, Dragan Nikolić, Sanja Vejnović, Rade Serbedzija, Nikola 'Kole' Angelovski
Keywords: knight, medieval times

Overview: Banovic Strahinja takes place during the middle ages in Serbia. While estate owner Strahinja is hunting, a Turkish bandit, Alija, attacks his castle. The Turks kill his servants and kidnap his beloved Andja. When he discovers what has happened, Strahinja gathers a together a group of scoundrels and chases the bandits.
