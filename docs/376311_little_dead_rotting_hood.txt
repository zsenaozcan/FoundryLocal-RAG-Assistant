Title: Little Dead Rotting Hood (2016)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Romance
Director: Jared Cohn
Main Cast: Eric Balfour, Bianca A. Santos, Patrick Muldoon, Heather Tom, Brendan Wayne
Keywords: wolf, mythology, undead, werewolf, red riding hood

Overview: The residents of a small town discover that something more sinister than killer wolves is lurking in the backwoods: first the wolves start turning up dead...then people.
