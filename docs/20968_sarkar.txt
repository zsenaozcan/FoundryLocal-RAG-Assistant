Title: Sarkar (2005)
Genres: Crime, Action, Drama
Director: Ram Gopal Varma
Main Cast: Amitabh Bachchan, Abhishek Bachchan, Katrina Kaif, Anupam Kher, Kay Kay Menon
Keywords: corruption, politics, political corruption

Overview: The authority of a man, who runs a parallel government in Mumbai, is challenged. His son rises to face his enemies.
