Title: Shadows (2007)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Thriller, Horror
Director: Milčo Mančevski
Main Cast: Borce Nacev, Vesna Stanojevska, Sabina Ajrula, Salaetin Bilal, Ratka Radmanovic
Keywords: dream, car crash
