Title: Wildlike (2014)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Thriller
Director: Frank Hall Green
Main Cast: Bruce Greenwood, Ella Purnell, Brian Geraghty, Ann Dowd, Nolan Gerard Funk
Keywords: runaway, camping, wilderness, grief, alaska, nature, uncle niece relationship, runaway teen, backpacking

Overview: Fourteen-year-old Mackenzie is sent to live with her uncle in Juneau when her mother can’t care for her anymore. The living situation quickly takes a turn for the worse, and she runs away to rejoin her mother in Seattle. While on her dangerous journey of sleeping in cars and breaking into hotel rooms, she’s drawn to Rene, a lonesome backpacker looking for tranquility in the wilderness.
