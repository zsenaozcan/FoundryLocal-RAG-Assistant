Title: Unrest (2006)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jason Todd Ipson
Main Cast: Joshua Alba, Scot Davis, Corri English, Marisa Petroro, Jay Jablonski
Keywords: prostitute, murder of prostitute, medical examiner, pathology, hospital, student

Overview: A young pathology med student suspects that the spirit of a dead cadaver in the hospital morgue where she works is killing off all those who handle or desecrate the body.
