Title: Be with Me (2010)
Genres: Horror, Drama, Thriller, Romance
Director: Kim Jho Gwang-soo
Main Cast: Han Ye-ri, Hong Jong-hyun, Hwang Byeong-guk, Shin Ji-soo, Kim Kkobbi
Keywords: ghost

Overview: Seo-hee, Nam-hee and Lan. These three girls happen to read their tarot cards. Tarot cards tell them once-in-a-life-time opportunities will be coming to each of them simply by holding tarot cards and chanting magic spells. And the magic spells are nothing but their own names! Once their names are said, very special events begin to unfold.
