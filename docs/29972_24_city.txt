Title: 24 City (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jia Zhangke
Main Cast: Joan Chen, Liping Lü, Zhao Tao, Chen Jianbin
Keywords: china, factory, flat, beautiful woman, independent film

Overview: Chengdu nowadays. The state owned factory 420 shuts down to give way to a complex of luxury apartments called "24 CITY". Three generations, eight characters : old workers, factory executives and yuppies, their stories melt into the History of China.
