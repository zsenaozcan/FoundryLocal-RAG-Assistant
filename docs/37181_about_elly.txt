Title: About Elly (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Asghar Farhadi
Main Cast: Golshifteh Farahani, Shahab Hosseini, Taraneh Alidoosti, پیمان معادی, Mani Haghighi
Keywords: sea, fiancé, volleyball, middle class, purse, friends, thriller, mystery, death by accident, guilt
