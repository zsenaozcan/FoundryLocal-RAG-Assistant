Title: When We Leave (2010)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Feo Aladag
Main Cast: Sibel Kekilli, Florian Lukas, Nizam Schiller, Derya Alabora, Settar Tanriogen
Keywords: marital rape, woman director, abusive father

Overview: Umay is a young woman of Turkish descent, fighting for an independent and self-determined life in Germany against the resistance of her family. Her struggle initiates a dynamic, which results in a life-threatening situation.
