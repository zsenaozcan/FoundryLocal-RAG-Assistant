Title: Hells Angels on Wheels (1967)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: Richard Rush
Main Cast: Adam Roarke, Jack Nicholson, Sabrina Scharf, Jana Taylor, Richard Anders
Keywords: jealousy, hells angels, murder, gang, motorcycle, violence

Overview: At first gas station attendant Poet is happy when the rockers gang “Hell’s Angels” finally accepts him. But he’s shocked when he learns how brutal they are – not even murder is a taboo to them. He gets himself in trouble when the leader’s girlfriend falls in love with him – and he welcomes her approaches.
