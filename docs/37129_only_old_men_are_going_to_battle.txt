Title: Only Old Men Are Going to Battle (1973)
Genres: War, Drama, Comedy
Director: Leonid Bykov
Main Cast: Leonid Bykov, Aleksei Smirnov, Sergei Ivanov, Sergei Podgornyj, Rustam Sagdullayev
Keywords: music, war, love, aviation

Overview: Bogdan Titarenko is very talented pilot and brave leader of "Singing group". He also must look after some new cadets and fight together with them against German Luftwaffe planes. Close to Bogdan always are his friends, they are all from different parts of the country, but they all became real brothers.
