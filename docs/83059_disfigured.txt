Title: Disfigured (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Deidra Edwards, Staci Lawrence, Ryan C. Benson

Overview: Lydia is an overweight sales clerk in a trendy home furnishings store, nearing 30. Though she is a member of a Fat Acceptance Group (a movement dedicated to fighting prejudice against overweight people), she is still struggling with complex feelings about her body and its place in the world. Darcy, a recovering-anorexic real estate agent in her mid-20s, is struggling with the same issues from a very different perspective.
