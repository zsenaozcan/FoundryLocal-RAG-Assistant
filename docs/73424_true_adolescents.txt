Title: True Adolescents (2009)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Craig Johnson
Main Cast: Mark Duplass, Melissa Leo, Bret Loehr, Carr Thompson, Emma Dumont

Overview: At 34, struggling Seattle musician Sam finds himself broke, jobless and losing touch with the person he wants to become. When his girlfriend kicks him out, he's forced to crash with his Aunt Sharon and is reluctantly enlisted to take her teen son, Oliver, and his friend Jake camping.
