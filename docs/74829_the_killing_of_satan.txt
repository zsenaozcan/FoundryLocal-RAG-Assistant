Title: The Killing of Satan (1983)
Genres: Horror, Action
Director: Efren C. Piñon
Main Cast: Ramon Revilla Sr., Elizabeth Oropesa, George Estregan, Paquito Diaz, Cecille Castillo
Keywords: snake, uncle, satan, torture, battle

Overview: A Filipino-made horror-fantasy movie about a man who is chosen by his dead uncle to battle the forces of evil. He fights a being called "The Prince of Magic," who shoots rays from his hands, makes heads spin around and hypnotizes people. He finally faces Satan himself and engages him in a battle of magic
