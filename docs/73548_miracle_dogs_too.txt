Title: Miracle Dogs Too (2006)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Richard Gabai
Main Cast: Dustin Hunter Evans, Janine Turner, Patrick Muldoon, Lesley Ann Warren, Charles Durning

Overview: Zack, a young boy, finds two cocker spaniels caged in the woods and takes them home. The animals, Sissy and Buddy, have magical healing powers that change the people of a small town.
