Title: The Comedians of Comedy: Live at The Troubadour (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary
Director: Chad Callner
Main Cast: Patton Oswalt, Brian Posehn, Maria Bamford, Zach Galifianakis, David Cross
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: It's a night of comic anarchy as 15 of today's edgiest comedians perform at Los Angeles' legendary Troubadour rock 'n' roll club! You're guaranteed to laugh, and occasionally gasp with shock, as new comic frontiers are crossed. Staring; Patton Oswalt, Brian Posehn, Zach Galifianakis, Maria Bamford, David Cross, Sarah Silverman, and many more.
