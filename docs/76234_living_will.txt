Title: Living Will... (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Matthew Lauyer
Main Cast: Ryan Dunn, April Scott, Gerard Haitz, Noam Harary, Chris Ready
Keywords: supernatural ability

Overview: Will's best friend and roommate Belcher, (Ryan Dunn) a party bum slacker, returns from the dead as a cocky, mischievous, and perverted ghost. He soon discovers his cousin, Krista, has shacked up and moved in with his best friend, Will. Belcher will do anything and everything to destroy this relationship and get his prank buddy back.
