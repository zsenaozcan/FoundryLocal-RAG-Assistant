Title: Agatha and the Storm (2004)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Silvio Soldini
Main Cast: Licia Maglietta, Giuseppe Battiston, Silvana Bosi, Emilio Solfrizzi, Marina Massironi

Overview: The story of a middle-aged bookshop owner, who becomes embroiled in a surreal swirl of romance and family secrets of infidelity, her energy levels registering in bursts of electrical interference - causing lightbulbs, traffic lights, toasters and computers to spontaneously spark and malfunction.
