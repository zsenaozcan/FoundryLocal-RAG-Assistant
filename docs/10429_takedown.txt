Title: Takedown (2000)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Joe Chappelle
Main Cast: Skeet Ulrich, Angela Featherstone, Donal Logue, Christopher McDonald, Tom Berenger
Keywords: hacker, internet, hacking, computer hacker

Overview: Kevin Mitnick is quite possibly the best hacker in the world. Hunting for more and more information, seeking more and more cyber-trophies every day, he constantly looks for bigger challenges. When he breaks into the computer of a security expert and an ex-hacker, he finds one - and much more than that...
