Title: Picnic on the Grass (1959)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Jean Renoir
Main Cast: Paul Meurisse, Catherine Rouvel, Jacqueline Morane, Fernand Sardou, Jean-Pierre Granval

Overview: Picnic on the Grass is a 1959 film directed by Jean Renoir. It shares a title with the painting by Édouard Manet.
