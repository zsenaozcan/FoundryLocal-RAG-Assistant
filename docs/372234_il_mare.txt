Title: Il mare (1962)
Genres: Drama
Director: Giuseppe Patroni Griffi
Main Cast: Françoise Prévost, Umberto Orsini, Dino Mele

Overview: A well known actor comes to off-season Capri to unwind and meets a teenaged boy. The attraction is immediate and mutual but before their relationship can get off the ground, an alluring woman with a spontaneous sexuality and care free attitude joins the triangle and the boy is slowly pushed out of the picture.
