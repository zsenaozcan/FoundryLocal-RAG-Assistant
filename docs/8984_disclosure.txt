Title: Disclosure (1994)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime, Mystery, Romance
Director: Barry Levinson
Main Cast: Michael Douglas, Demi Moore, Donald Sutherland, Dylan Baker, Jacqueline Kim
Keywords: employee, workplace, sexual harassment, intrigue

Overview: A computer specialist is sued for sexual harassment by a former lover turned boss who initiated the act forcefully, which threatens both his career and his personal life.
