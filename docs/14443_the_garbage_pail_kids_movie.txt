Title: The Garbage Pail Kids Movie (1987)
Genres: Adventure, Science Fiction, Comedy
Director: Rod Amateau
Main Cast: Anthony Newley, Mackenzie Astin, Katie Barberi, Phil Fondacaro, Ron MacLachlan
Keywords: monster, garbage, musical, friendship, toilet humor

Overview: Seven disgusting kids but nevertheless of interesting personality are being made of the green mud coming out of garbage can. Once alive their master gives them rules to obey although they think that life is funnier without following stupid regulations like no television or no candy. Naturally this will cause some conflicts.
