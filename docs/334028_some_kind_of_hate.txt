Title: Some Kind Of Hate (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: Adam Egypt Mortimer
Main Cast: Grace Phipps, Sierra McCormick, Lexi Atkins, Spencer Breslin, Noah Segan
Keywords: suicide, bullying, revenge

Overview: When a troubled teen is subjected to severe bullying, he accidentally conjures the vengeful ghost of Moira Karp. Once a teenage girl pushed to suicide, Moira is now an unstoppable force on a mission of gruesome retribution. But when she goes to far, he must prevent her from spiraling out of control in this passionate and vividly violent supernatural thriller.
