Title: Slayers: The Motion Picture (1995)
Genres: Fantasy, Animation, Comedy, Adventure
Director: Kazuo Yamazaki
Main Cast: Megumi Hayashibara, Maria Kawamura, Chafurin, Keiji Fujiwara, Tesshou Genda
Keywords: sorcery, sorcerer

Overview: The first Slayers movie is a prequel to the TV series, following the exploits of a couple of sorceresses with too much time on their hands and too many violent tendencies. The main mage is Lina Inverse, known for her fireballs, love of seafood, and small breasts.
