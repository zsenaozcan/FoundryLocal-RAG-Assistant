Title: Forgetting the Girl (2012)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Nate Taylor
Main Cast: Christopher Denham, Lindsay Beamish, Elizabeth Rice, Anna Camp, Phyllis Somerville

Overview: Kevin is obsessed with finding a girl who can help him forget his unpleasant past. However, all his encounters with the opposite sex inevitably go afoul. As the rejections mount, Kevin's futile search for happiness and love becomes overwhelmingly turbulent, forcing him to take desperate measures.
