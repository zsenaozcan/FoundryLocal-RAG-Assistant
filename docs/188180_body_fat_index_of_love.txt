Title: Body Fat Index of Love (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mikko Kuparinen
Main Cast: Mikko Nousiainen, Mari Perankoski, Mirja Oksanen, Sanna-Kaisa Palo, Kristo Salminen

Overview: Timo Stigu Mertala is a celebrated, award-winning adman, and sworn to singlehood. He is a well known figure in the Helsinki nightlife.
