Title: Psychic Killer (1975)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Ray Danton
Main Cast: Jim Hutton, Julie Adams, Nehemiah Persoff, Neville Brand, Della Reese
Keywords: coma, telepathy, telekinesis, revenge, psychic power, murder, suspense, independent film, gore, psychiatric hospital

Overview: Mental patient Arnold Masters, hospitalized for a murder he didn't commit, learns astral projection--the art of leaving one's physical body and transporting the soul someplace else--from a fellow inmate. Upon his release, Arnold uses his new powers to bump off the people he holds responsible for his arrest, his mother's death while he was imprisoned and the price of meat! Lt. Morgan and Lt. Anderson are the cops on his trail, while his caring shrink, Dr. Scott, tries to prevent any more deaths.
