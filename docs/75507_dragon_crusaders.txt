Title: Dragon Crusaders (2011)
Genres: Fantasy, Action, Science Fiction
Director: Mark Atkins
Main Cast: Nia Ann, Jason Beeston, Carys Eleri, Cecily Fay
Keywords: fire, wales

Overview: In this sword-and-sorcery extravaganza, a group of marauding Knights Templar suffers a curse that transforms them into monsters, driving them to square off against an evil dragon obsessed with annihilating the entire world.
