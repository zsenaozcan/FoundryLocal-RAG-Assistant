Title: Hellraiser: Revelations (2011)
Genres: Horror
Director: Víctor García
Main Cast: Steven Brand, Jolene Andersen, Nick Eversman, Jay Gillespie, Stephan Smith Collins
Keywords: pinhead, demon, puzzle box, cenobites

Overview: Two friends in Mexico discover the Lament Configuration and unleash Pinhead, but one decides to try to survive by swapping himself with someone else. Once they go missing, family members go in search of them, but find Pinhead instead.
