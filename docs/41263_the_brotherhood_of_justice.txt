Title: The Brotherhood of Justice (1986)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Charles Braverman
Main Cast: Kiefer Sutherland, Lori Loughlin, Joe Spano, Keanu Reeves, Darren Dalton
Keywords: high school, vigilante, gang, student, violence, tv movie

Overview: A group of high school students, led by a rich boy Derek, is sick of school violence and decides to become underground vigilantes named "Brotherhood of Justice". It starts with the idea "watching people", but things quickly get out of control. "Brotherhood of Justice" turns out another gang of violence. Derek wants to stop it but it is too late, so he takes responsibility for it and gives himself to the law.
