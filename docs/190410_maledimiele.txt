Title: Maledimiele (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Marco Pozzi
Main Cast: Gianmarco Tognazzi, Sonia Bergamasco, Isa Barzizza, Benedetta Gargari
Keywords: anorexia, eating disorder

Overview: Maledimiele tells the story of Sara, a teenage girl who sinks – slowly but inevitably – into the abyss of anorexia.
