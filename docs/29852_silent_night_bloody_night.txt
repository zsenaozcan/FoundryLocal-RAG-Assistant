Title: Silent Night, Bloody Night (1972)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Theodore Gershuny
Main Cast: James Patterson, Mary Woronov, Патрик О’Нил, John Carradine, Astrid Heeren
Keywords: insane asylum, burned alive, christmas, holidays

Overview: A man inherits a mansion, which once was a mental home. He visits the place and begins to investigate some crimes that happened in old times, scaring the people living in the region.
