Title: The Sandpiper (1965)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Vincente Minnelli
Main Cast: Elizabeth Taylor, Richard Burton, Eva Marie Saint, Charles Bronson, Robert Webber
Keywords: deer, love, school, unwed mother, big sur

Overview: A free-spirited artist (Elizabeth Taylor), who lives near California's Big Sur, meets the administrator (Richard Burton) of the parochial school that her young son attends and lures the cleric away from his wife (Eva Marie Saint).
