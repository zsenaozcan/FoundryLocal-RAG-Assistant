Title: Sleeping Beauty (2014)
Genres: Fantasy, Adventure
Director: Casper Van Dien
Main Cast: Michael York, Finn Jones, Grace Van Dien, Catherine Oxenberg, Casper Van Dien
Keywords: fairy, castle, forest, sleeping beauty, dragon

Overview: When a young Prince and his trusted aid learn of a beautiful Princess's cursed eternal slumber, they embark on a journey to rescue her. They must battle an evil queen and legions of undead monsters before she will be free.
