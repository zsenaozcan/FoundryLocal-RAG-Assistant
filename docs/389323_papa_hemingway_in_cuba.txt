Title: Papa Hemingway in Cuba (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bob Yari
Main Cast: Giovanni Ribisi, Joely Richardson, Adrian Sparks, Minka Kelly, James Remar
Keywords: biography, hemmingway

Overview: In 1959, a young journalist ventures to Havana, Cuba to meet his idol, the legendary Ernest Hemingway who helped him find his literary voice, while the Cuban Revolution comes to a boil around them.
