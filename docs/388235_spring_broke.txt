Title: Spring Broke (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Alison Ellwood
Main Cast: Robin Leach, Dave Barry, Ed Lover, Pauly Shore
Keywords: mtv, daytona, spring break

Overview: Documentary charting the raucous history of the infamous spring vacation revelries in Daytona, which started in the early 60s, and by the 1980s led to the arrival of tens of thousands of college students, lured by lust, booze, fun in the sun and eventually, the hope to make it onto MTV.
