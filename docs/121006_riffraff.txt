Title: Riffraff (1936)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: J. Walter Ruben
Main Cast: Spencer Tracy, Jean Harlow, Una Merkel, Joseph Calleia, Victor Kilian
Keywords: cannery

Overview: Fisherman Dutch marries cannery worker Hattie. After he is kicked out of his union and fired from his job he leaves Hattie who steals money for him and goes to jail. He gets a new job, foils a plot to dynamite the ship, and promises to wait for Hattie.
