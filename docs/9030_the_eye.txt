Title: The Eye (2008)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: David Moreau
Main Cast: Jessica Alba, Alessandro Nivola, Parker Posey, Rade Serbedzija, Fernanda Romero
Keywords: sister sister relationship, blindness and impaired vision, eye operation, eyesight, dying and death, eye, classical music, sister, eye specialist, remake

Overview: Violinist Sydney Wells was accidentally blinded by her sister Helen when she was five years old. She submits to a cornea transplantation, and while recovering from the operation, she realizes that she is seeing dead people.
