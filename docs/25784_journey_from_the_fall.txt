Title: Journey from the Fall (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ham Tran
Main Cast: Kiều Chinh, Long Nguyen, Diem Lien, Jayvee Mai The Hiep, Khanh Doan
Keywords: vietnam, independent film

Overview: Thirteen years after the end of the Vietnam War, a family who was tragically affected by the war are forced to emigrate to America.
