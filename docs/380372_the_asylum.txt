Title: The Asylum (2013)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Science Fiction, Mystery, Horror
Director: Filip Maciejewicz
Main Cast: Jonathan Rosenthal, Adrian Voo, Patrick Edward Wynne, Kathinka van Putten, Bo Linton

Overview: In an abandoned mental institution, doctors carry out secret experiments on unsuspecting patients. The experiments, codenamed Limes, are designed to cure violent episodes within the inmates through new methods of brainwashing and mind manipulation. It is soon discovered by the families of the patients that this is no ordinary hospital.
