Title: The Selling (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Emily Lou
Main Cast: Gabriel Diani, Janet Varney, Jonathan Klein, Etta Devine, Nancy Lenehan

Overview: For most people affected by the recent housing market crash, the impact was financial. Super nice real estate agent Richard Scarry has an additional burden: the paranormal. This startlingly funny debut feature takes many of the tropes of haunted house films and employs them to exciting, witty, and original ends.
