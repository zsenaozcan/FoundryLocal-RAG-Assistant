Title: Pony Soldier (1952)
Genres: Western
Director: Joseph M. Newman
Main Cast: Tyrone Power, Cameron Mitchell, Thomas Gomez, Penny Edwards, Robert Horton
Keywords: canada, cree indian, mountie, mounted police

Overview: Duncan MacDonald, a 19th-century Royal Canadian Mountie, has to escort a group of Cree Indians back to their above-the-border reservation. His guide in this endeavor is the not-too-trustworthy half-breed Natayo.
