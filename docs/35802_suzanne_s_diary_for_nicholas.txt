Title: Suzanne's Diary for Nicholas (2005)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Richard Friedenberg
Main Cast: Christina Applegate, Johnathon Schaech, Kathleen Rose Perkins, John Dunsworth, Kevin Jubinville
Keywords: infant, letter, loss, terminal illness, romance

Overview: A woman discovers the truth about her former lover from the diary that his first wife wrote to their son, Nicholas.
