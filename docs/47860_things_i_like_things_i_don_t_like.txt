Title: Things I Like, Things I Don't Like (1989)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jean-Pierre Jeunet
Main Cast: Dominique Pinon

Overview: A 9 minute comedy starring Dominique Pinon (Delicatessen). Featuring muted colors with a sepia black and white, Pinon takes the viewer through various examples of what he "likes and dislikes". The music is by Carlos D'Alessio and Special Thanks is given to Claudie Ossard.
