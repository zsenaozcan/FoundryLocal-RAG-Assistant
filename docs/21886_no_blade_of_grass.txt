Title: No Blade of Grass (1970)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Cornel Wilde
Main Cast: Nigel Davenport, Jean Wallace, John Hamill, Lynne Frederick, Patrick Holt
Keywords: dystopia, virus

Overview: A strange new virus has appeared, which only attacks strains of grasses such as wheat and rice, and the world is descending into famine and chaos. Architect John, along with his family and friends, is making his way from London to his brother's farm in northern England where there will hopefully be food and safety for all of them.  Adaptation of John Christopher's novel The Death of Grass (1956)
