Title: French Fried Vacation (1978)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Patrice Leconte
Main Cast: Thierry Lhermitte, Marie-Anne Chazel, Michel Blanc, Josiane Balasko, Christian Clavier
Keywords: free love, hotel, animateur, flirt, ladykiller, one-night stand, summer vacation, extramarital affair

Overview: Holidaymakers arriving in a Club Med camp on the Ivory Coast are determined to forget their everyday problems and emotional disappointments. Games, competitions, outings, bathing and sunburn accompany a continual succession of casual affairs.
