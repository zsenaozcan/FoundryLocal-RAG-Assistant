Title: Blood Hook (1987)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Jim Mallon
Main Cast: Mark Jacobs, Lisa Jane Todd, Patrick Danz, Sara Hauser, Christopher Whiting
Keywords: fishing, horror, slasher

Overview: During a local fishing contest, people are being mysteriously dragged into the lake and killed by a giant fish hook. After a sufficient number of deaths, the killer is finally revealed.
