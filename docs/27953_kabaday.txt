Title: Kabadayı (2007)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Ömer Vargı
Main Cast: Kenan İmirzalıoğlu, Şener Şen, İsmail Hacıoğlu, Aslı Tandoğan, Rasim Öztekin
Keywords: femme fatale

Overview: Ali Osman is a former bully of city of Istanbul. But lately he gives up bully and starts to operate a synthetic pitch. He often meets his old friends, former bullies, too. One day Ali Osman receives a news which is related with his previous passionate life. Then the whole action stats as a chain of events.
