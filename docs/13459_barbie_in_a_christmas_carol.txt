Title: Barbie in 'A Christmas Carol' (2008)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: William Lau
Main Cast: Kelly Sheridan, Morwenna Banks, Melissa Lyons, Prudence Edwards

Overview: On Christmas Eve, Kelly is reluctant to go to a Christmas Eve ball, so Barbie tells her the story of Eden Starling, a glamourous singing diva in the Victorian England and the owner of a theatre house. However, Eden is self-centred and loves only herself. She is frequently accompanied by her snooty cat, Chuzzlewit. She does not believe in Christmas and orders all her employees to work on Christmas.
