Title: The Legend of Ben Hall (2016)
Genres: Action, Drama, History, Western
Director: Matthew Holmes
Main Cast: Jack Martin, Callan McAuliffe, Arthur Angel, Angus Pilakui, Andy McPhee
Keywords: biography, australia, independent film, directorial debut, revisionist history, hall, new south wales, bushranger

Overview: Ben Hall is drawn back into bushranging by the reappearance of his old friend John Gilbert. Reforming the gang, they soon become the most wanted men in Australian history.
