Title: Hôtel Terminus: The Life and Times of Klaus Barbie (1988)
Genres: War, Documentary
Director: Marcel Ophüls
Main Cast: Klaus Barbie, Marcel Ophüls
Keywords: holocaust, nazis

Overview: Winner of a Best Documentary Academy Award, Marcel Ophuls' riveting film details the heinous legacy of the Gestapo head dubbed "The Butcher of Lyon." Responsible for over 4,000 deaths in occupied France during World War II, Barbie would escape--with U.S. help--to South America in 1951, where he lived until a global manhunt led to his 1983 arrest and subsequent trial.
