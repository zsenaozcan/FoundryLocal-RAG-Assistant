Title: Tokyo Twilight (1957)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Main Cast: Setsuko Hara, Ineko Arima, Chishu Ryu, Isuzu Yamada, Teiji Takahashi
Keywords: daughter, post war japan

Overview: Two sisters find out the existence of their long-lost mother, but the younger cannot take the truth of being abandoned as a child.
