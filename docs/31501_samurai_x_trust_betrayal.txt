Title: Samurai X: Trust & Betrayal (1999)
Genres: Animation, Action, Adventure, Drama, Romance
Director: Kazuhiro Furuhashi
Main Cast: Mayo Suzukaze, Junko Iwao, Nozomu Sasaki, Masami Suzuki, Shūichi Ikeda

Overview: Taken by slavers when he was a child, Himura Kenshin is rescued only when an encounter with bandits kills off everyone but him. He is found by a master of the Divine Justice School of Swordmanship, a school so deadly that to train in it, means death for either the master or student, there can only be one master....
