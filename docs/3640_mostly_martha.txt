Title: Mostly Martha (2001)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Comedy
Director: Sandra Nettelbeck
Main Cast: Martina Gedeck, Maxime Foerste, Sergio Castellitto, August Zirner, Sibylle Canonica
Keywords: italy, competition, loss of mother, loss of sister, cook, cooking, restaurant, bars and restaurants, mother role, kitchen

Overview: Martha is a single woman who lives for one passion: cooking. The head chef at a chic restaurant, Martha has no time for anything - or anyone - else. But Martha's solitary life is shaken when a fateful accident brings her sister's eight-year-old daughter, Lina, to her doorstep.
