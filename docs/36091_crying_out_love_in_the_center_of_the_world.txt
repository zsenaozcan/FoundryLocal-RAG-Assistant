Title: Crying Out Love in the Center of the World (2004)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Isao Yukisada
Main Cast: Takao Osawa, Kou Shibasaki, Tsutomu Yamazaki, Kanji Tsuda, Rio Kanno
Keywords: love, flashback, fiancée, flirtatious woman, athletic girl

Overview: While searching for his fiancee Ritsuko, Sakutarou rediscovers through flashbacks the void deep within him caused by the events from his high school days.
