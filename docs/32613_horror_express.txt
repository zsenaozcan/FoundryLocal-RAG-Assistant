Title: Horror Express (1972)
Genres: Horror
Director: Eugenio Martín
Main Cast: Christopher Lee, Peter Cushing, Telly Savalas, Alberto de Mendoza, Silvia Tortosa
Keywords: monk, monster, expedition, russia, cossack, loss of husband, alien, train, creature, gothic horror

Overview: Mysterious and unearthly deaths start to occur while Professor Saxton is transporting the frozen remains of a primitive humanoid creature he found in Manchuria back to Europe.
