Title: Five Wives, Three Secretaries and Me (1999)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Tessa Blake
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Filmmaker Teresa Blake's portrait of an 88-year-old man with a trail of Texas Exes: Houston oil man, Hollywood playboy, hotshot lawyer and serial monogamist Tommy Blake on a quirky and intimate journey, a daughter looks beyond myth, money, and society -- and all that hair -- in seeking the truth of her father.
