Title: Great World of Sound (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Music
Director: Craig Zobel
Main Cast: Pat Healy, Kene Holliday, John Baker, Robert Longstreet, Libertad Green

Overview: When a man answers an ad to train as a record producer, he's excited by the prospect of signing undiscovered artists only to discover his new job isn't all it's cracked up to be.
