Title: Rally 'Round the Flag, Boys! (1958)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Leo McCarey
Main Cast: Paul Newman, Joanne Woodward, Joan Collins, Jack Carson, Dwayne Hickman
Keywords: protest, army, connecticut, flag, bonfire, rally

Overview: Harry Bannerman, a Connecticut suburbanite who becomes involved in various shenanigans with his wife Grace Oglethorpe, leads a protest movement against a secret army plan to set up a missile base in their community.
