Title: The Prince and the Pauper (1962)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Don Chaffey
Main Cast: Guy Williams, Laurence Naismith, Donald Houston, Sean Scully, Niall MacGinnis

Overview: In the London of 1537, two boys resembling each other exactly meet accidentally and exchange "roles" for a short while. After many adventures, the prince regains his rightful identity and graciously makes his "twin" a ward of the court.
