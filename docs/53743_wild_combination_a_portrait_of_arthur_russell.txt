Title: Wild Combination: A Portrait of Arthur Russell (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Matt Wolf
Main Cast: Bob Blank, Ernie Brooks , Allen Ginsberg, Arthur Russell

Overview: Wild Combination is Matt Wolf’s acclaimed documentary on seminal avant-garde composer, singer-songwriter, cellist, and disco producer Arthur Russell. Before his death in 1992, Arthur created music that spanned both pop and the transcendent possibilities of abstract art.
