Title: Two Girls and a Sailor (1944)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Richard Thorpe
Main Cast: June Allyson, Gloria DeHaven, Van Johnson, Tom Drake, Henry Stephenson
Keywords: sister sister relationship, musical, sailor

Overview: A sailor helps two sisters start up a service canteen. The sailor soon becomes taken with gorgeous sister Jean, unaware that her sibling Patsy is also in love with him.
