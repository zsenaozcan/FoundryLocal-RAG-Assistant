Title: Tapped Out (2014)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Allan Ungar
Main Cast: Michael Biehn, Cody Hackman, Anderson Silva, Lyoto Machida, Martin Kove
Keywords: sport, mixed martial arts

Overview: A disgruntled teenager, sent to do community service at a rundown Karate school, enters an MMA tournament to face the man who killed his parents.
