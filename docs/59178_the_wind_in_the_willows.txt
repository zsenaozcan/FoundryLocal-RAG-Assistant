Title: The Wind in the Willows (1995)
Genres: Family, Animation, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Dave Unwin
Main Cast: Alan Bennett, Michael Palin, Michael Gambon, Rik Mayall, Vanessa Redgrave

Overview: Kenneth Grahame's literary classic about an enchanting world along the Riverbank has delighted readers for nearly a century. Now, this enduring beloved tale comes to life in this beautifully animated feature film from the producers of "The World of Peter Rabbit and Friends" and "The Snowman".
