Title: The Mark of the Whistler (1944)
Genres: Thriller
Director: William Castle
Main Cast: Richard Dix, Janis Carter, Porter Hall, Paul Guilfoyle, John Calvert
Keywords: radio program, b movie, radio series, the whistler, film noir

Overview: A drifter claims the money in an old bank account. Soon he finds himself the target of two men who turn out to be the sons of the man's old partner, who is now in prison because of a conflict with him over the money in that account.
