Title: Caramel (2007)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Nadine Labaki
Main Cast: Nadine Labaki, Yasmine Al Massri, Joanna Moukarzel, Gisèle Aouad, Adel Karam
Keywords: women, hairdresser, sister sister relationship, new love, unexpected happiness, virgin, beauty, dressmaker, cosmetics and hygiene, strafzettel

Overview: In a beauty salon in Beirut the lives of five women cross paths. The beauty salon is a colorful and sensual microcosm where they share and entrust their hopes, fears and expectations.
