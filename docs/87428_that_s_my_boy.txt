Title: That's My Boy (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sean Anders
Main Cast: Adam Sandler, Susan Sarandon, Eva Amurri Martino, Leighton Meester, Andy Samberg
Keywords: deadbeat dad, cheating fiancée, teacher student sex, brother sister incest
