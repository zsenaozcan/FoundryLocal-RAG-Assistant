Title: Tangled (2001)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Jay Lowi
Main Cast: Rachael Leigh Cook, Shawn Hatosy, Jonathan Rhys Meyers, Estella Warren, Lorraine Bracco
Keywords: love triangle, detective, nudity, underwear, investigation, disappearance, flashback

Overview: A young man is found bruised, beaten and stumbling down a secluded road. As the police try to piece together what happened, the convoluted relationship between a young woman and her two suitors gradually emerges.
