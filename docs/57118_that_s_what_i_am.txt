Title: That's What I Am (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Michael Pavone
Main Cast: Ed Harris, Molly Parker, Amy Madigan, Randy Orton, Chase Ellison

Overview: A coming-of-age story that follows 12-year-old Andy Nichol, a bright student who, like most kids his age, will do anything to avoid conflict for fear of suffering overwhelming ridicule and punishment from his junior high school peers.
