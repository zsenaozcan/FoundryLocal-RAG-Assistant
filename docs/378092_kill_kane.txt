Title: Kill Kane (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Crime
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Vinnie Jones, Sean Cronin, Nicole Faraday, Sarah Alexandra Marks

Overview: A teacher's world is torn apart when his wife and children are brutally murdered at the hands of a ruthless gang. Left for dead and with no one to turn to, he takes matters into his own hands and hits the streets in search of justice.
