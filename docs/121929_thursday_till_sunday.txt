Title: Thursday Till Sunday (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Dominga Sotomayor Castillo
Main Cast: Santi Ahumada, Emiliano Freifeld, Francisco Pérez-Bannen, Paola Giannini, Axel Dupré
Keywords: woman director

Overview: As she and her family embark on a short vacation, a Chilean teenager slowly comes to the realization that her parents might be splitting up.
