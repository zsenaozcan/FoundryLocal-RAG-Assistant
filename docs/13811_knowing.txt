Title: Knowing (2009)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Mystery, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Alex Proyas
Main Cast: Nicolas Cage, Rose Byrne, Chandler Canterbury, Ben Mendelsohn, Nadia Townsend
Keywords: cataclysm, code, suspense, end of the world, time capsule, astrophysicist, grieving widower, lexington massachusetts, westford massachusetts, prediction

Overview: A teacher opens a time capsule that has been dug up at his son's elementary school; in it are some chilling predictions -- some that have already occurred and others that are about to -- that lead him to believe his family plays a role in the events that are about to unfold.
