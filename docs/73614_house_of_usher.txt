Title: House of Usher (2008)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: David DeCoteau
Main Cast: Michael Cardelle, Frank Mentier, Jaimyse Haft, Ian Shaw, Jack Carlisle

Overview: Victor Reynolds arrives at the notorious House of Usher, whereupon he is greeted by old acquaintances Roderick and Madeline Usher and their servant, Markus. As Victor uncovers more about the history of the house and the disappearances of those that entered it previously, he begins to realize that he is in mortal danger.
