Title: Tuvalu (1999)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Comedy, Science Fiction, Romance, Family, Foreign
Director: Veit Helmer
Main Cast: Denis Lavant, Philippe Clay, Terrence Gillespie, E.J. Callahan, Djoko Rosic
Keywords: swimming pool

Overview: Set in a dilapidated indoor swimming pool (the Central Baths in Sofia), the film details the efforts of Anton, a clueless dreamer who yearns to sail the world, and Martha, the button obsessed cashier, to maintain the illusion for Anton's blind father that business is thriving. Working to sabotage their efforts is Gregor - Anton's brother - an amoral developer who is determined to raze the entire town and construct a sprawling condominium complex. Gregor engineers an accident that seems certain to doom the business and in the process steals away Eva, the beautiful woman of Anton's dreams. Will Gregor's dastardly plan succeed?
