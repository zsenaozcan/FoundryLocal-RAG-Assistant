Title: Mount St. Elias (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Gerald Salmina 
Main Cast: Axel Naglich, Jon Johnston, Peter Ressmann
Keywords: sport

Overview: At an altitude of 18,000 feet, Alaska's Mount St. Elias is the destination for a trio of mountaineers determined to reach the mountain's summit, and to ski back down as well. Mount St. Elias documents their journey as they trek the fine line between bravery and madness.
