Title: Mr. Nice (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Bernard Rose
Main Cast: Rhys Ifans, Chloë Sevigny, David Thewlis, Elsa Pataky, Olivia Grant
Keywords: prison, sex, cia, wales, biography, elite, conspiracy, drug use, student, terrorism

Overview: Biopic about 1970s Welsh marijuana trafficker Howard Marks, whose inventive smuggling schemes made him a huge success in the drug trade, as well as leading to dealings with both the IRA and British Intelligence. Based on Marks' biography with the same title.
