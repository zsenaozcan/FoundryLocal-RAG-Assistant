Title: Eddie and the Cruisers (1983)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Martin Davidson
Main Cast: Tom Berenger, Michael Paré, Joe Pantoliano, Ellen Barkin, Matthew Laurance
Keywords: music

Overview: Michael Paré stars as Eddie Wilson of the rock band Eddie and Cruisers. The band is at the height of their career in the 1960s when front man Eddie drives his car off a bridge in an apparent suicide -- but the body was never found.
