Title: Ultrasonic (2012)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Rohit Colin Rao
Main Cast: Silas Gordon Brigham, Sam Repshas, Cate Buscher, Josh Davidson, Ricardo Kingsbury
Keywords: paranoia, conspiracy, sound waves

Overview: A musician with hyper-sensitive hearing goes in search of an ominous sound that plagues him, but is inaudible to everyone else.
