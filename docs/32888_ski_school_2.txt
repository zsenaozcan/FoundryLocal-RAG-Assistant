Title: Ski School 2 (1994)
Genres: Comedy
Director: David Mitchell
Main Cast: Dean Cameron, Heather Campbell, Doug Copithorne, Bil Dwyer, Wendy Hamilton
Keywords: female nudity, sport, sequel, wedding, skiing, ski resort

Overview: Dave has been enjoying life until he receives an ex-girlfriend's wedding invitation. Even though Beth dumped him years ago, Dave knows it's their destiny to be together. So Dave embarks on a quest: to save Beth from certain death-by-boredom, return balance to the universe, and maybe get in a little awesome skiing. Dave convinces his buddies to join him in his quest to make things right.
