Title: Albatross (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Niall MacCormick
Main Cast: Jessica Brown Findlay, Felicity Jones, Sebastian Koch, Julia Ormond, Peter Vaughan
Keywords: literature, marriage crisis, friendship, family relationships, coming of age, affair

Overview: Beth, a bookish teenager, befriends Emilia, an aspiring novelist who has just arrived in town. Emilia soon begins an affair with Beth's father that threatens to have devastating consequences.
