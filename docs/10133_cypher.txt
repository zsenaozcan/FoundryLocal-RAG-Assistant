Title: Cypher (2002)
Genres: Thriller, Science Fiction, Mystery
Director: Vincenzo Natali
Main Cast: Jeremy Northam, Lucy Liu, Nigel Bennett, Timothy Webber, David Hewlett
Keywords: double life, undercover, lie, wife, company, industry, femme fatale, conspiracy, espionage, memory loss

Overview: An unsuspecting, disenchanted man finds himself working as a spy in the dangerous, high-stakes world of corporate espionage. Quickly getting way over-his-head, he teams up with a mysterious femme fatale.
