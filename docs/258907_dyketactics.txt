Title: Dyketactics (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: Barbara Hammer
Main Cast: Barbara Hammer
Keywords: experimental film, avant-garde, lgbt, woman director

Overview: Born in Los Angeles but a New Yorker by choice, Barbara Hammer is a whole genre unto herself. Her pioneering 1974 short film Dyketactics, a four-minute, hippie wonder consisting of frolicking naked women in the countryside, broke new ground for its exploration of lesbian identity, desire and aesthetic.
