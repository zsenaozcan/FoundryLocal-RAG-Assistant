Title: The Treasure of the Amazon (1985)
Genres: Thriller, Action
Director: René Cardona Jr.
Main Cast: Stuart Whitman, John Ireland, Donald Pleasence, Bradford Dillman, Sonia Infante
Keywords: female nudity, nazis, amazon, diamond

Overview: A ragtag group of adventurers, including a former Nazi, is led by a half-maddened grizzled treasure hunter who wants a second chance to find the mythical treasure of the Amazon, after his first mission went terrible wrong.
