Title: Jerry Seinfeld: I'm Telling You for the Last Time (1998)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Marty Callner
Main Cast: Jerry Seinfeld, Michael Barryte, Grace Bustos, George Carlin, Alan King
Keywords: stand-up comedy, live comedy

Overview: Taped for HBO in August 1998, on the final date of Jerry Seinfeld's tour appearances at New York City's Broadhurst Theater, I'm Telling You for the Last Time presents the standup comedian's so-called "final" standup, or at least his final tour with the standup material that made him famous.
