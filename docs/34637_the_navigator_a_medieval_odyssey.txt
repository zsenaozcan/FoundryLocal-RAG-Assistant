Title: The Navigator: A Medieval Odyssey (1988)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Drama, Fantasy, Mystery
Director: Vincent Ward
Main Cast: Bruce Lyons, Chris Haywood, Hamish McFarlane, Marshall Napier, Noel Appleby
Keywords: suspense

Overview: In the 14th century a village seeking escape from the Black Death are guided by a boy's vision to tunnel into an abandoned mine and emerge into 20th century New Zealand.
