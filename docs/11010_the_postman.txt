Title: The Postman (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Michael Radford
Main Cast: Philippe Noiret, Massimo Troisi, Maria Grazia Cucinotta, Renato Scarpa, Linda Moretti
Keywords: island, letter, postman, fisherman, poet

Overview: Simple Italian postman learns to love poetry while delivering mail to a famous poet; he uses this to woo local beauty Beatrice.
