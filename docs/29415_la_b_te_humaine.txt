Title: La Bête Humaine (1938)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jean Renoir
Main Cast: Jean Gabin, Simone Simon, Fernand Ledoux, Blanchette Brunoy, Gérard Landry
Keywords: freight train, lovers, murder, independent film

Overview: In this classic adaptation of Emile Zola's novel, a tortured train engineer falls in love with a troubled married woman who has helped her husband commit a murder.
