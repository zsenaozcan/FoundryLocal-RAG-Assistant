Title: KM 31: Kilometer 31 (2006)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Mystery
Director: Rigoberto Castañeda
Main Cast: Iliana Fox, Adrià Collado, Raúl Méndez, Luisa Huertas, Fernando Becerril

Overview: While driving through the kilometer 31 of a lonely road, Agata Hameran hits a boy. She leaves her car to help the victim and another car runs over her and she falls in a deep coma. Her twin sister Catalina telepathically feels the pain of Agata and hears her whispering for help. Together with her boyfriend Nuno and Agata's mate Omar, they return to the km 31 of the road, and find out that the place is surrounded by supernatural accidents caused by the ghost of a mother that lost her boy many years ago. Further, Catalina discloses that the spirit of Agata is trapped between the worlds of the living and the dead.
