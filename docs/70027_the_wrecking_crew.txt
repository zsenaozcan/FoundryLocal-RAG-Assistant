Title: The Wrecking Crew (2008)
Genres: Music, Documentary
Director: Denny Tedesco
Main Cast: Lou Adler, Herb Alpert, Hal Blaine, Glen Campbell, Al Casey
Keywords: rock and roll, musician, music, pop music

Overview: A celebration of the musical work of a group of session musicians known as "The Wrecking Crew", a band that provided back-up instrumentals to such legendary recording artists as Frank Sinatra, The Beach Boys and Bing Crosby.
