Title: Shy People (1987)
Genres: Drama
Director: Andrei Konchalovsky
Main Cast: Jill Clayburgh, Barbara Hershey, Martha Plimpton, Merritt Butrick, John Philbin
Keywords: sister sister relationship, swamp, cajoons

Overview: A New York journalist visits her distant cousin for the first time to write an article about her hard life in the bayous of Louisiana. The journalist's wild, drug-addicted daughter adds tensions between the two relatives' cultures.
