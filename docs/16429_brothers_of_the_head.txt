Title: Brothers of the Head (2005)
Genres: Music, Drama
Director: Keith Fulton
Main Cast: Luke Treadaway, Harry Treadaway, Sean Harris, Edward Hogg, Jonathan Pryce
Keywords: independent film

Overview: In the 1970s a music promoter plucks Siamese twins from obscurity and grooms them into a freakish rock'n'roll act. A dark tale of sex, strangeness and rock music.
