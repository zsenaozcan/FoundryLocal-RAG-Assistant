Title: The Kindergarten Teacher (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nadav Lapid
Main Cast: Sarit Larry, Avi Shnaidman, Lior Raz, Gilad ben David, Ester Rada

Overview: A teacher discovers in a five year-old child a prodigious gift for poetry. Amazed and inspired by this young boy, she decides to protect his talent in spite of everyone.
