Title: Devil's Angels (1967)
Genres: Action
Director: Daniel Haller
Main Cast: John Cassavetes, Beverly Adams, Mimsy Farmer, Maurice McEndree, Leo Gordon
Keywords: biker film

Overview: An exiled band of Hell's Angels strike a bargain with the Sheriff of a local town, let them stay and the town is safe. But a local girl strays into their lair and sparks off a full scale Angel war.
