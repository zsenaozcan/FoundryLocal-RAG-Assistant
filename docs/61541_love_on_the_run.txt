Title: Love on the Run (1936)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Romance
Director: W.S. Van Dyke
Main Cast: Joan Crawford, Clark Gable, Franchot Tone, Reginald Owen, Mona Barrie
Keywords: spy, prince, reporter

Overview: A runaway bride and an undercover reporter get caught up in political intrigue as they lead a merry chase across Europe and uncover a spy plot.
