Title: Kathleen (1941)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Harold S. Bucquet
Main Cast: Shirley Temple, Herbert Marshall, Laraine Day, Gail Patrick, Felix Bressart
Keywords: dream, psychologist

Overview: Kathleen is a 12 year old who lives in a big house with a nanny, a butler, maids, no mother and a father who is working most of the time. She dreams of a family with a mother, father and her, and tells everyone that she has such a family. Because of this story, she cannot invite any friends over as they will see that it is not true.
