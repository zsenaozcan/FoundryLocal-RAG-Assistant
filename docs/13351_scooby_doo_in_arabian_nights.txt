Title: Scooby-Doo in Arabian Nights (1994)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Jun Falkenstein
Main Cast: Don Messick, Casey Kasem, Greg Burson, Allan Melvin
Keywords: woman director, crime solving

Overview: Scooby-Doo and Shaggy travel to Arabia to become the Caliph's Royal Food Tasters. But they bite off more than they can chew and are forced to run for their lives! It's a wild magic carpet ride as Scooby-Doo, Shaggy and their genie (Yogi Bear) and a jolly sailor named Sinbad (Magilla Gorilla) take you on an adventure of mistaken identities, exotic locations and fun-filled action and surprises!
