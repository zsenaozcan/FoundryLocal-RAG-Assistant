Title: Letters from Iwo Jima (2006)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, War
Director: Clint Eastwood
Main Cast: Ken Watanabe, Kazunari Ninomiya, Tsuyoshi Ihara, Ryo Kase, Shidou Nakamura
Keywords: world war ii, cave, dying and death, pacific theater, japanese army, imperial japan

Overview: The story of the battle of Iwo Jima between the United States and Imperial Japan during World War II, as told from the perspective of the Japanese who fought it.
