Title: Honey and Clover (2006)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Masahiro Takada
Main Cast: Yu Aoi, Yusuke Iseya, Ryo Kase, Megumi Seki, Gin Pun Chou

Overview: Adapted from the popular manga of the same name, director Masahiro Takada's coming of age drama follows five Hama Art College students as they prepare to venture out into the real world on a great voyage of self-discovery.
