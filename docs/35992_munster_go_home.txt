Title: Munster, Go Home! (1966)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Earl Bellamy
Main Cast: Fred Gwynne, Yvonne De Carlo, Al Lewis, Hermione Gingold, Terry-Thomas
Keywords: based on tv series

Overview: Herman discovers he's the new lord of Munster Hall in England. The family sails to Britain, where they receive a tepid welcome from Lady Effigy and Freddie Munster, who throws tantrums because he wasn't named Lord Munster. An on-board romance had blossomed between Marilyn and Roger, but on land Marilyn discovers Roger's family holds a longstanding grudge against the Munsters.
