Title: Ankhon Dekhi (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Foreign
Director: Rajat Kapoor
Main Cast: Rajat Kapoor, Sanjay Mishra, Seema Pahwa, Maya Sarao
Keywords: hindi

Overview: Raje Bauji is a man in his late fifties, living out a dreary but eventful life in a small house in old Delhi where he lives with his extended family.  A random incident is going to change his life in a dramatic way- though he does not realize it  yet.
