Title: The Night That Panicked America (1975)
Genres: Drama, TV Movie
Director: Joseph Sargent
Main Cast: Vic Morrow, Cliff De Young, Michael Constantine, Walter McGinn, Eileen Brennan
Keywords: docudrama, based on true events

Overview: A dramatization of the Oct. 30, 1938 mass panic that Orson Welles' radio play, "The War of the Worlds" accidentally provoked.
