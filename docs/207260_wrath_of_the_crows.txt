Title: Wrath of the Crows (2013)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Ivan Zuccon
Main Cast: Tiffany Shepis, Debbie Rochon, Tara Cardinal, Domiziano Arcangeli, Suzi Lorraine

Overview: Wrath of the crows is very gory and brutal, but tied by a strong concept and the whole story is focused on the characters' development. So there's not only splatter sequences, but also atmospheric moments, a few dream-like sequences and a final twist that will shock you all.
