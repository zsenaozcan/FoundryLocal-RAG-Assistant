Title: The Fish Child (2009)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Romance, Foreign
Director: Lucía Puenzo
Main Cast: Inés Efron, Mariela Vitale, Pep Munné, Diego Velázquez, Carlos Bardem
Keywords: judge, bus ride, scandal, legend, puppy, lesbian, road map, woman director

Overview: A desperate love story between two young girls of extremely different social backgrounds who, unable to find a place for their love in the world they live in, are pushed to commit a crime.
