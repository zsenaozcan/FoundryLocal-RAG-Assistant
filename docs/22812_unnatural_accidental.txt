Title: Unnatural & Accidental (2006)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Mystery
Director: Carl Bessai
Main Cast: Tantoo Cardinal, Na'Ku'Set Shepherd, Tinsel Korey

Overview: Unnatural &amp; Accidental is the feature length dramatic film adaptation of the stage play entitled, The Unnatural and Accidental Women by renowned Metis playwright Marie Clements and is based loosely around the "unnatural and accidental" downing deaths of native women living on skid row.
