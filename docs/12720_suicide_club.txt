Title: Suicide Club (2001)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Sion Sono
Main Cast: Ryo Ishibashi, Akaji Maro, Masatoshi Nagase, Sayako Hagiwara, Hideo Sako
Keywords: japan, suicide, investigation, website, youth, incomprehensible plot

Overview: When 54 high school girls throw themselves in front of a subway train it appears to be only the beginning of a string of suicides around the country. Does the new all-girl group Desert have anything to do with it? Detective Kuroda tries to find the answer, which isn't as simple as he had hoped.
