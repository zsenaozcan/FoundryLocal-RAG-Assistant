Title: The Murder Pact (2015)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Colin Theys
Main Cast: Alexa PenaVega, Beau Mirchoff, Renee Olstead, John Heard, Michael J. Willett

Overview: Four friends at a prestigious university whose promising futures are jeopardized when they become connected to the accidental death of a classmate. When another student says she knows about their involvement, the friends panic and start to plot her murder.
