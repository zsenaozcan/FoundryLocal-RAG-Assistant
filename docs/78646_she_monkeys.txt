Title: She Monkeys (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lisa Aschan
Main Cast: Mathilda Paradeiser, Linda Molin, Isabella Lindquist, Sergej Merkusjev, Adam Lundgren
Keywords: lesbian relationship, homosexuality, woman director

Overview: When Emma meets Cassandra, they initiate a relationship filled with physical and psychological challenges. Emma does whatever it takes to master the rules of the game. Lines are crossed and the stakes get higher and higher. Despite this, Emma can't resist the intoxicating feeling of total control.
