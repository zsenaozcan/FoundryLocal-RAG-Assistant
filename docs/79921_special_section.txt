Title: Special Section (1975)
Genres: Drama, War, History, Mystery
Director: Costa-Gavras
Main Cast: Louis Seigner, Michael Lonsdale, Claude Piéplu, Pierre Dux, Heinz Bennent
Keywords: based on novel, world war ii, drama, suspense, thriller, judgment, political, courtroom, nazism

Overview: In occupied France during the WWII, a German officer is murdered. The collaborationist Vichy government decides to pin the murder on six petty criminals. Loyal judges are called in to convict them as quickly as possible.
