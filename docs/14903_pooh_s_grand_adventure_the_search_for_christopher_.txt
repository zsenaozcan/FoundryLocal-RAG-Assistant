Title: Pooh's Grand Adventure: The Search for Christopher Robin (1997)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Karl Geurs
Main Cast: Jim Cummings, John Fiedler, Ken Sansom, Andre Stojka, Peter Cullen

Overview: A full length animated film for all the family to enjoy. Pooh gets confused when Christopher Robin leaves him a note to say that he has gone back to school after the holidays. So Pooh, Piglet, Tigger, Eeyore and Rabbit go in search of Christopher Robin which leads to a big adventure.
