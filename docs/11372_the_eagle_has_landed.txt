Title: The Eagle Has Landed (1976)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action, Thriller, War
Director: John Sturges
Main Cast: Michael Caine, Donald Sutherland, Robert Duvall, Jenny Agutter, Donald Pleasence
Keywords: england, world war ii, nazis, adolf hitler, winston churchill, benito mussolini, nazi germany

Overview: When the Nazi high command learns in late 1943 that Winston Churchill will be spending time at a country estate in Norfolk, it hatches an audacious scheme to kidnap the prime minister and spirit him to Germany for enforced negotiations with Hitler.
