Title: Winning (1969)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: James Goldstone
Main Cast: Paul Newman, Joanne Woodward, Robert Wagner, Richard Thomas, David Sheiner
Keywords: sport, racing car

Overview: Frank Capua is a rising star on the race circuit who dreams of winning the big one - the Indianapolis 500. But to get there he runs the risk of losing his wife Elora to his rival, Luther Erding, and strains the relationship with his stepson.
