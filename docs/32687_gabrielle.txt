Title: Gabrielle (2005)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Foreign, Romance, Thriller
Director: Patrice Chéreau
Main Cast: Isabelle Huppert, Pascal Greggory, Claudia Coli, Thierry Hancisse, Chantal Neuwirth

Overview: A seemingly ideal marriage is thrown into embarrassing turmoil in Patrice Chéreau's period drama, based on the short story The Return by Joseph Conrad.
