Title: Alan & Naomi (1992)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Sterling Van Wagenen
Main Cast: Lukas Haas, Vanessa Zaoui, Michael Gross, Amy Aquino, Kevin Connolly
Keywords: post traumatic stress  disorder, world war ii, male female relationship

Overview: When Naomi, a young refugee from Nazi-occupied Paris, moves into Alan Silverman’s building in New York, he does his best to avoid her. But despite Naomi's strange behavior and the language barrier, they slowly develop a deep and touching friendship.
