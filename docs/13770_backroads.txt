Title: Backroads (1977)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Phillip Noyce
Main Cast: Bill Hunter, Gary Foley, Zack Martin, Terry Camilleri, Julie McGregor
Keywords: australian, xenophobia

Overview: Two strangers - one white, one black - steal a car in western NSW and head for the coast. Jack is abrasive, cunning and disparaging about Aborigines. Gary doesn’t really care – he just wants to escape. En route, they pick up Gary’s Uncle Joe, a French hitchhiker and a young woman who’s running away. Their petty crimes escalate as they go, heading towards disaster.
