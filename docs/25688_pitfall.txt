Title: Pitfall (1948)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: André De Toth
Main Cast: Dick Powell, Lizabeth Scott, Jane Wyatt, Raymond Burr, John Litel
Keywords: blackmail, los angeles, extramarital affair, classic noir, film noir

Overview: Based on Jay Dratler's novel The Pitfall, André de Toth's gripping film is a classic L.A. noir in the tradition of Detour, and shows the spiraling, epic consequences resulting from one fateful decision.
