Title: The Russians Are Coming, The Russians Are Coming (1966)
Genres: Comedy, War
Director: Norman Jewison
Main Cast: Carl Reiner, Eva Marie Saint, Alan Arkin, Brian Keith, Paul Ford
Keywords: cold war, russian, new england

Overview: Without hostile intent, a Soviet sub runs aground off New England. Men are sent for a boat, but many villagers go into a tizzy, risking bloodshed.
