Title: This Night I'll Possess Your Corpse (1967)
Genres: Horror
Director: José Mojica Marins
Main Cast: José Mojica Marins, Tina Wohlers, Nadia Freitas, Antonio Fracari, Jose Lobo
Keywords: coffin, corpse

Overview: Coffin Joe is still looking for the perfect woman to give birth to a son of his, and, cleared of the past crimes in the first film (At Midnight I'll Take Your Soul), keeps terrorizing the people in his small town with his iconoclast and sadistic practices.
