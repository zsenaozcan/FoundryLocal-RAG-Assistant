Title: Lone Wolf and Cub: Baby Cart to Hades (1972)
Genres: Drama, Action

Main Cast: Tomisaburō Wakayama, Yuko Hamada, Isao Yamagata, Michitaro Mizushima, Akihiro Tomikawa
