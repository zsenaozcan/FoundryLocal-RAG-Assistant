Title: We're Not Married! (1952)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Edmund Goulding
Main Cast: Ginger Rogers, Victor Moore, Fred Allen, Marilyn Monroe, David Wayne

Overview: A Justice of the Peace performed weddings a few days before his license was valid. A few years later five couples learn they have never been legally married. Annabel Norris, already Mrs. Mississippi and ready to enter the Mrs. America contest, is now free to enter the Miss Mississippi contest.
