Title: Bullet for a Badman (1964)
Genres: Western
Director: R.G. Springsteen
Main Cast: Audie Murphy, Darren McGavin, Ruta Lee, Skip Homeier, George Tobias
Keywords: romance, betrayal, grudge

Overview: Former Texas Rangers Sam Ward and Logan Keliher become enemies when Sam turns bank robber and Logan marries Sam's ex-wife.
