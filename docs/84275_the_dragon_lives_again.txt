Title: The Dragon Lives Again (1977)
Genres: Fantasy, Action
Director: Law Kei
Main Cast: Bruce Leung Siu-Lung, Sin Il-Ryong, Tang Ching, Alexander Grand, Nick Cheung Lik
Keywords: dracula, hell, parody, bruce lee, bruceploitation

Overview: Everyone's favorite martial artist ""Bruce"" winds up fighting for his life and soul in hell against an evil Warlord intent on making him mincemeat - and he has recruited some of the 1970s most iconic B-movie characters to help dispatch the great one. But when that won't do the trick, he uses monsters, mummies and beautiful women to get the little dragon at any cost.
