Title: Oddsac (2010)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Danny Perez
Main Cast: Kyrian Friedenberg, Bill Haber, Rosemary Howard, Josh Dibb, Raigan Irons

Overview: Opening with torch-wielding villagers and a wall bleeding oil, this experimental film attaches vivid scenery and strange characters to the wonderful melodic wavelengths of the band Animal Collective, revitalizing the lost form of the "visual album."
