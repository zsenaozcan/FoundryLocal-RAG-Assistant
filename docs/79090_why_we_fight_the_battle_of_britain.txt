Title: Why We Fight: The Battle of Britain (1943)
Genres: Documentary, War, History
Director: Frank Capra
Main Cast: Walter Huston, Frieda Inescort, John B. Hughes

Overview: Exciting newsreel footage highlights this award-winning documentary of Britain's gallant stand against the encroaching Nazi horde. As the British people fight bravely, Hitler orders the Luftwaffe to begin the London Blitz in the hope of bringing England to her knees.
