Title: Fourteen Hours (1951)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Henry Hathaway
Main Cast: Paul Douglas, Richard Basehart, Barbara Bel Geddes, Debra Paget, Agnes Moorehead
Keywords: suicide, police, reporter, ledge, ex fiancée

Overview: A young man, morally destroyed by his parents not loving him and by the fear of being not capable to make his girlfriend happy, rises on the ledge of a building with the intention of committing suicide. A policeman makes every effort to argue him out of it.
