Title: The Bastard (1968)
Genres: Crime, Action
Director: Duccio Tessari
Main Cast: Rita Hayworth, Giuliano Gemma, Klaus Kinski, Margaret Lee, Claudine Auger
Keywords: gangster, armored car robbery, revenge plot

Overview: A criminal (Giuliano Gemma) seeks vengeance when his double-dealing brother (Klaus Kinski) steals his jewels and mutilates his shooting hand.
