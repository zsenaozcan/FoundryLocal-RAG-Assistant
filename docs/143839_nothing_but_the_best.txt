Title: Nothing But the Best (1964)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Clive Donner
Main Cast: Alan Bates, Harry Andrews, Millicent Martin, Denholm Elliott, Pauline Delaney
Keywords: satire, con artist

Overview: Success has James Brewster's name written all over it, and he also has his heart set on his boss's daughter. A con artist hires him to help out on a bank scheme, but then again, James will do anything to get rich and be the most successful businessman in Britian-even if it means murder!!!
