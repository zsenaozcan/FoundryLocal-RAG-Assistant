Title: Kurt & Courtney (1998)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Nick Broomfield
Main Cast: Nick Broomfield, Dylan Carlson, Kurt Cobain, El Duce, Larry Flynt
Keywords: singing, embryo, living under the bridge, music rights

Overview: After rocker Kurt Cobain's death, ruled a suicide, a film crew arrives in Seattle to make a documentary. Director Nick Broomfield talks to lots of people. Portraits emerge: a shy, slight Kurt, weary of touring, embarrassed by fame, hooked on heroin; an out-going Courtney, dramatic, controlling, moving from groupie to star.
