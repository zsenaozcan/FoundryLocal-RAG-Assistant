Title: Project S (1993)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Crime
Director: Stanley Tong
Main Cast: Michelle Yeoh, Yu Rong-Guang, Emil Chow, Jackie Chan, Eric Tsang
Keywords: martial arts

Overview: As a crime wave sweeps through Hong Kong, the police call Jessica Yang (Yeoh), a rising star in the ranks, to help stop a notorious gang of thieves! What Jessica doesn't realize is that her boyfriend - recently discharged from the force - is the leader of this ruthless crime ring!
