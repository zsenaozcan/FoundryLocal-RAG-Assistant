Title: Eugene Mirman: An Evening of Comedy in a Fake Underground Laboratory (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ryan Polito
Main Cast: Kristen Schaal, Eugene Mirman
Keywords: newspaper, stand-up comedy

Overview: When it comes to satirical observational comedy, Eugene Mirman is a wizard at finding humor in what ordinary people find mundane. Tune in to find out why you should get your daughter a neck tattoo and how to make ten Saudi Arabian men give you $40 each. And if you know what a theremin is, you'll be excited to know that there's one in this special.
