Title: Summer Holiday (1948)
Genres: Music
Director: Rouben Mamoulian
Main Cast: Mickey Rooney, Gloria DeHaven, Walter Huston, Frank Morgan, Jackie 'Butch' Jenkins
Keywords: small town, musical, vacation, valedictorian, puppy love

Overview: Danville, Connecticut at the turn of the century. Young Richard Miller lives in a middle-class neighborhood with his family. He is in love with the girl next-door, Muriel, but her father isn't too happy with their puppy-love, since Richard always share his revolutionary ideas with her.
