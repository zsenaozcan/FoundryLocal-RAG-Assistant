Title: When the Road Bends: Tales of a Gypsy Caravan (2006)
Genres: Music, Documentary
Director: Jasmine Dellal
Main Cast: Fanfare Ciocarlia, Taraf de Haidouks, Antonio El Pipa, Maharaja, Esma Redzepova
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A brief look into Romany culture and Rom (Gypsies) from around the globe as five famous Romany groups tour the USA.
