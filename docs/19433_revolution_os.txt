Title: Revolution OS (2001)
Genres: Documentary
Director: J.T.S. Moore
Main Cast: Susan Egan, Linus Torvalds, Richard M. Stallman, Eric S. Raymond, Bruce Perens
Keywords: hacker, history, linux, gnu, free software, open source

Overview: REVOLUTION OS tells the inside story of the hackers who rebelled against the proprietary software model and Microsoft to create GNU/Linux and the Open Source movement.
