Title: The Big Steal (1990)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Nadia Tass
Main Cast: Ben Mendelsohn, Claudia Karvan, Steve Bisley, Marshall Napier
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Danny wants two things in life more than anything else, one is a Jaguar and the other is Joanna Johnson. After Danny is conned into trading his Nissan Cedric for a 1973 Jaguar, he plots to win Joanna and get revenge against the sleazy car dealer Gordon Farkes.
