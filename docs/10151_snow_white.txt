Title: Snow White (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Samir
Main Cast: Stefan Gubser, Sunnyi Melles, Carlos Leal, Julie Fournier, Xaver Hutter
Keywords: paris, upper class, female nudity, rap music, hip-hop, zurich, suicide attempt, cocaine, lie, nudity

Overview: The life of 20-year-old Nico from upper class Zurich is all party, sex and drugs when she meets Paco, the front man of a rap band. She finds herself drawn to his independent, creative and profound way of life, so different from her own. With naiveté and infinite self-confidence, she tries to adopt his lifestyle - without anticipating the consequences.
