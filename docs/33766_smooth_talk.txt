Title: Smooth Talk (1985)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Joyce Chopra
Main Cast: Laura Dern, Treat Williams, Mary Kay Place, Margaret Welsh, Sara Inglis
Keywords: suspense, coming of age, teenage girl, summer, sexual awakening, woman director, female sexuality, loss of innocence

Overview: Based on the short story, "Where Are You Going, Where Have You Been", by Joyce Carol Oates, this film chronicles a 15-year-old girl, Connie's, sexual awakening in the Northern California suburbs. Her experimenting begin to get out of hand when the mysterious Arnold Friend takes an interest in her.
