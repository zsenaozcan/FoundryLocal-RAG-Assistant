Title: A Summer at Grandpa's (1984)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Hou Hsiao-hsien
Main Cast: Wong Kai-Gwong, Lee Suk-Ching, Nai-Chu Ting, Lin Hsiu-Ling, Mei Fang
Keywords: grandparents, slice of life, family, childhood, life

Overview: A coming-of-age story about a young brother and sister whom spend a pivotal summer in the country with their grandparents.
