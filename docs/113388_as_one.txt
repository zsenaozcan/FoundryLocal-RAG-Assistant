Title: As One (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Moon Hyun-sung
Main Cast: Ha Ji-won, Bae Doona, Han Ye-ri, Park Cheol-min, Kim Eung-Soo
Keywords: ping pong, sport

Overview: A unified team representing the two Koreas competed at the 1991 Chiba International Table Tennis Championships. It was the first such sport team since the division of the Korean peninsula. The unified team won the group competition event, beating the front runner, China. HA Ji-won plays the South Korean table tennis player, HYEON Jeong-hwa and her North Korean counterpart, LEE Bun-hee, is played by BAE Doona.
