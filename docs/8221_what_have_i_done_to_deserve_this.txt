Title: What Have I Done to Deserve This? (1984)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Pedro Almodóvar
Main Cast: Carmen Maura, Ángel de Andrés López, Verónica Forqué, Chus Lampreave, Gonzalo Suárez
Keywords: prostitute, madrid, wife husband relationship, taxi driver, vororte, hustler, nudity, housewife, sadism, telekinesis

Overview: Almodóvar has described his fourth film as a homage to Italian Neorealism, although this tribute also involves jokes about paedophilia, prostitution, and a telekenetic child. The film, set in the towerblocks around Madrid, depicts female frustration and family breakdown.
