Title: Alonzo Bodden: Historically Incorrect (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: John Asher
Main Cast: Alonzo Bodden
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Alonzo Bodden returns with a comedic look at the historical inaccuracies of the present and past in an all-new stand-up comedy event that exposes the hypocrisy - and hilarity - of modern day culture.
