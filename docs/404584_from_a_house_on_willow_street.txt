Title: From a House on Willow Street (2017)
Genres: Action, Horror
Director: Alastair Orr
Main Cast: Sharni Vinson, Carlyn Burchell, Steve John Ward, Zino Ventura, Nicole De Klerk
Keywords: kidnapping, supernatural, possession, gore, violence, demon
