Title: Mostly Ghostly (2008)
Genres: Family, Fantasy, Horror
Director: Rich Correll
Main Cast: Sterling Beaumon, Madison Pettis, Luke Benward, Brian Stepanek, David DeLuise
Keywords: based on children's book, ghost

Overview: Based on the successful book series by R.L. Stine, this spooky tale finds 11-year-old Max making a deal with the ghosts who haunt his home. If Max helps them find what was responsible for their parents' disappearance, they'll help him transform from a social nobody to the most popular kid in school.
