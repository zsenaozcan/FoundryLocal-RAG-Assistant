Title: The Beast of the City (1932)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Charles Brabin
Main Cast: Walter Huston, Jean Harlow, Wallace Ford, Jean Hersholt, Dorothy Peterson
Keywords: police chief, organized crime, film noir

Overview: Police Chief Jim Fitzpatrick is after gangster Sam Belmonte. He uses his corrupt brother Ed to watch over Daisy who was associated with Belmonte.
