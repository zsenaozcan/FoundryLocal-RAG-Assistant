Title: Fear of a Black Hat (1994)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Rusty Cundieff
Main Cast: Larry B. Scott, Mark Christopher Lawrence, Rusty Cundieff, Kasi Lemmons, G. Smokey Campbell
Keywords: hip-hop, musician, mockumentary, spoof

Overview: Chronicling the controversial career of bad boys N.W.H. (Niggaz With Hats), this uproarious 'mockumentary' lampoons all of hardcore rap's hot-button issues. This underground laugh riot recounts the rise, fall and resurrection of a clueless bunch of would-be rappers, Ice Cold, Tone-Def &amp; Tasty Taste performing as N.W.H.
