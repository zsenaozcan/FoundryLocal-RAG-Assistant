Title: Opera (1987)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Mystery
Director: Dario Argento
Main Cast: Cristina Marsillach, Ian Charleson, Urbano Barberini, Daria Nicolodi, Coralina Cataldi-Tassoni
Keywords: giallo, killer

Overview: A young opperata is stalked by a deranged fan bent on killing the people associated with her to claim her for himself.
