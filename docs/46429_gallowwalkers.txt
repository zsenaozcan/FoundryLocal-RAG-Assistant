Title: Gallowwalkers (2012)
Genres: Action, Horror
Director: Andrew Goth
Main Cast: Wesley Snipes, Riley Smith, Tanit Phoenix, Simona Brhlikova, Steven Elder
Keywords: nun, hair, zombie, skin, gunman, heaven and hell

Overview: When a nun broke her covenant with God to save the life of  her unborn son, Aman, he was cursed for life. As an adult, Aman has killed those who have crossed him. But his curse brings his victims back to life and they pursue him for revenge, so Aman enlists a young gunman to fight by his side against his undead victims.
