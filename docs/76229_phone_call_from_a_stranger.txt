Title: Phone Call from a Stranger (1952)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jean Negulesco
Main Cast: Gary Merrill, Shelley Winters, Michael Rennie, Keenan Wynn, Evelyn Varden

Overview: Four strangers board a plane and become fast friends, but a catastrophic crash leaves only one survivor. He then sets off on a journey to discover who these people were, but ultimately discovers the devastating truth about himself.
