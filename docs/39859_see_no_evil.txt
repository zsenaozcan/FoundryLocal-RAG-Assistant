Title: See No Evil (1971)
Genres: Crime, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Richard Fleischer
Main Cast: Mia Farrow, Dorothy Alison, Robin Bailey, Diane Grayson, Brian Rawlinson
Keywords: murder, blind girl

Overview: Sarah is a blind girl who has returned to her home, a country manor in which all of the occupants are dead. She unknowingly sleeps overnight, among a houseful of corpses, arising the next morning to quietly creep out of bed, in order not to awaken the other members of the household.
