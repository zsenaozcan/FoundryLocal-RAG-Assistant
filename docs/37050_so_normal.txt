Title: So Normal (2003)
Genres: Comedy
Director: José Alvarenga Jr.
Main Cast: Luiz Fernando Guimarães, Fernanda Torres, Marisa Orth, Evandro Mesquita
Keywords: brazilian, comedy

Overview: The film shows the day when Rui and Vani first met. It was at their wedding (with other partners). Vani was going to marry Sérgio, and Rui would marry Marta in the same church, following Vani's marriage. While waiting for the ceremony, they begin to talk. Complications ensue.
