Title: Raiders of the Lost Ark - The Adaptation (1989)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Eric Zala
Main Cast: Chris Strompolos, Angela Rodriguez, Eric Zala, Michael Bales, Ted Ross
Keywords: fan film

Overview: After seeing Raiders of the Lost Ark in 1981, three 12 year old friends, Chris Strompolos, Eric Zala and Jayson Lamb, began filming their own shot-by-shot adaptation of the Spielberg classic in the backyards of their Mississippi homes. After 6 years, the film was ready.
