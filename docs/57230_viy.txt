Title: Viy (1967)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Horror
Director: Georgiy Kropachyov
Main Cast: Leonid Kuravlyov, Natalya Varley, Aleksey Glazyrin, Vadim Zakharchenko, Nikolay Kutuzov
Keywords: witch

Overview: A young priest is ordered to preside over the wake of a witch in the church of a remote village. This means spending three nights alone with the corpse with only his faith to protect him.
