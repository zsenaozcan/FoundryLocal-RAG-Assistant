Title: Maternal Half-Brothers (1988)
Genres: Drama
Director: Zdravko Šotra
Main Cast: Žarko Laušević, Mira Furlan, Slavko Štimac, Goran Visnjic, Sonja Savić

Overview: This story about two maternal half-brothers, a Croat and a Serb. Although they never met, and both lose their loved ones in ethnic clashes, there is a bond between them. Filmed in 1988, it prophetically forsees the war that would engulf former Yugoslavia three years later.
