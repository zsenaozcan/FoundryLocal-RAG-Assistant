Title: Mission to Lars (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: William Spicer
Main Cast: Lars Ulrich, Jasmin St. Claire, William Spicer, Tom Spicer, Kate Spicer
Keywords: adventure

Overview: Kate and Will Spicer's brother, Tom, has Fragile X Syndrome, the most common form of inherited learning disability. He is also a massive fan of Lars Ulrich from Metallica. They made a promise to Tom that they would get him to meet Lars. Tom's dream is their promise. Together they went on a Mission to Lars.
