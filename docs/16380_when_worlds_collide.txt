Title: When Worlds Collide (1951)
Genres: Action, Fantasy, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Rudolph Maté
Main Cast: Richard Derr, Barbara Rush, Larry Keating, John Hoyt, Frank Cady
Keywords: space travel, rocket

Overview: When a group of astronomers calculate a star is on a course to slam into Earth, a few days before, it's accompanying planet will first pass close enough to the Earth to cause havoc on land and sea. They set about building a rocket so a few selected individuals can escape to the planet.
