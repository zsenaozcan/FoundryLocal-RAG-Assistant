Title: Bones (2001)
Genres: Action, Horror, Thriller
Director: Ernest R. Dickerson
Main Cast: Snoop Dogg, Pam Grier, Michael T. Weiss, Clifton Powell, Katharine Isabelle
Keywords: black people, drug dealer, dying and death, skeleton, crack

Overview: Over 20 years after his death by a gunshot, Jimmy Bones comes back as a ghost to wreak revenge on those who killed him and to clean up his neighborhood.
