Title: Quatermass II (1957)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Val Guest
Main Cast: Brian Donlevy, John Longden, Sid James, Bryan Forbes, William Franklyn
Keywords: monster, factory, alien life-form, meteor, rocket, sequel, scientist

Overview: Quatermass is the director of an important scientific base, where he builds nuclear rockets for the colonization of the Moon. A strange fall of meteorites in a nearby village leads to the discovery of a huge base under a strict military control, officially a factory of artificial food. Quatermass reveals that the factory is actually breeding alien creatures.
