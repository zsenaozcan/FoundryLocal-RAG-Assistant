Title: Wonderwall (1968)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Joe Massot
Main Cast: Jack MacGowran, Jane Birkin, Irene Handl, Richard Wattis
Keywords: surrealism

Overview: The eccentric professor Collins lives completely secluded in his chaotic apartment. When the model Penny moves in next to him, he becomes fascinated of her. He drills holes in her walls and ceiling and peeps on her day and night. He loses himself in daydreams and delusions
