Title: See You in Montevideo (2014)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Dragan Bjelogrlić
Main Cast: Miloš Biković, Petar Strugar, Armand Assante, Viktor Savić, Nebojša Ilić
Keywords: sport, soccer

Overview: A football team from Belgrade, former Republic of Yugoslavia gets a chance to go to the First World Football Championship, but things get complicated along the way.
