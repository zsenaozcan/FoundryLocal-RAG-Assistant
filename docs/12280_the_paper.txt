Title: The Paper (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Ron Howard
Main Cast: Michael Keaton, Robert Duvall, Glenn Close, Marisa Tomei, Randy Quaid
Keywords: new york, research, success, tageszeitung, murder

Overview: Henry Hackett is the editor of a New York City tabloid. He is a workaholic who loves his job, but the long hours and low pay are leading to discontent. Also, publisher Bernie White faces financial straits, and has hatchetman Alicia Clark, Henry's nemesis, impose unpopular cutbacks.
