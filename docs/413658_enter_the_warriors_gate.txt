Title: Enter the Warriors Gate (2016)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Matthias Hoene
Main Cast: Francis Ng, Kara Hui, Uriah Shelton, Sienna Guillory, Ni Ni

Overview: Epic fantasy-adventure meets martial arts action in this thrilling film written by Luc Besson &amp; Robert Mark Kamen. After a mysterious chest opens a gateway through time, teen gamer Jack (Uriah Shelton) is transported to an ancient empire terrorized by a cruel barbarian king (former WWE superstar Dave Bautista). Jack will need all of his gaming skills as he battles to defeat the barbarian, protect a beautiful princess, and somehow find his way back home.
