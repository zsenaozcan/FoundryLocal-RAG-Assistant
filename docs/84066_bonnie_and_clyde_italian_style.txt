Title: Bonnie and Clyde Italian Style (1982)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Steno
Main Cast: Paolo Villaggio, Ornella Muti, Jean Sorel, Nando Murolo, Antonio Allocca

Overview: Two stupid lowlifes are "forced" into a life of violent crime on the streets of Italy. What follows is a mocking takeoff of the American crime classic "Bonnie and Clyde."
