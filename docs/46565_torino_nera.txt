Title: Torino nera (1972)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Carlo Lizzani
Main Cast: Bud Spencer, Marcel Bozzuffi, Françoise Fabian, Andrea Balestri, Saro Urzì
Keywords: mafia

Overview: Mino and Lello, two sons of a worker wrongly accused of murder, try to find out evidence of his innocence and begin to investigate mafia business in Torino.
