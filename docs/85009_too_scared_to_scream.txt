Title: Too Scared to Scream (1985)
Genres: Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Tony Lo Bianco
Main Cast: Mike Connors, Anne Archer, Leon Isaac Kennedy, Ian McShane, Ruth Ford
Keywords: wheelchair, slasher, manhattan, new york city

Overview: A killer of young women is loose in an exclusive apartment complex and a detective is out to stop him. The chief suspect is a Shakespeare-spouting doorman who lives with his invalid mother. Meanwhile, an undercover cop moves into the apartment house. This move is complicated by her relationship with the detective.
