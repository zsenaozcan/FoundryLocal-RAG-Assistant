Title: Laid to Rest (2009)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Robert Hall
Main Cast: Bobbi Sue Luther, Kevin Gage, Lena Headey, Sean Whalen, Richard Lynch
Keywords: psychopathic killer, slaughter

Overview: A terrifying story of a young girl who wakes up in a casket with a traumatic head injury and no memory of her identity. She quickly realizes she was abducted by a Deranged Serial Murderer and in an isolated rural town she must survive the night and outsmart the technologically inclined killer who is hellbent on finishing what he started.
