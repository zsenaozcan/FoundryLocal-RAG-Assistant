Title: Geronimo (1962)
Genres: History, Western
Director: Arnold Laven
Main Cast: Chuck Connors, Kamala Devi, Pat Conway, Armando Silvestre, Adam West

Overview: In 1883, the Apache Indians lead by Geronimo reluctantly surrender to the attacks of American and Mexican troops, in exchange for a territory and food for their warriors. Soon though, Geronimo escapes the camps and declares war against the Americans.
