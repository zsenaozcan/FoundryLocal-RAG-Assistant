Title: Branded to Kill (1967)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Seijun Suzuki
Main Cast: Jô Shishido, Kôji Nanbara, Isao Tamagawa, Anne Mari, Mariko Ogawa
Keywords: assassin, hitman, organized crime

Overview: After botching his latest assignment, a third-ranked Japanese hit man becomes the target of another assassin.
