Title: The Tango Lesson (1997)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance, Foreign
Director: Sally Potter
Main Cast: Sally Potter, Morgane Maugran, Pablo Verón, Géraldine Maillet, Katerina Mechera
Keywords: dancer, dance, tango, woman director

Overview: On a trip to Paris Sally meets Pablo, a tango dancer. He starts teaching her to dance then she returns to London to work on some "projects". She visits Buenos Aires and learns more from Pablo's friends. Sally and Pablo meet again but this time their relationship changes, she realises they want different things from each other. On a trip to Buenos Aires they cement their friendship.
