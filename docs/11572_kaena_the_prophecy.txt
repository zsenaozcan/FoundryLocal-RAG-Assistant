Title: Kaena: The Prophecy (2003)
Genres: Animation, Adventure
Director: Chris Delaporte
Main Cast: Cécile de France, Michael Lonsdale
Keywords: cloud, plant, cgi, mortal danger, dying world, high priest, mysterious force

Overview: Compelled by a mysterious force, Kaena, a rebellious, high-spirited teenage girl will defy the High Priest and her people's ancestral beliefs to take the perilous journey through the Axis and discover what dark secrets lie beyond the clouds.
