Title: The Witches of Eastwick (1987)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Comedy
Director: George Miller
Main Cast: Jack Nicholson, Cher, Susan Sarandon, Michelle Pfeiffer, Veronica Cartwright
Keywords: witch, new england

Overview: Three single women in a picturesque village have their wishes granted - at a cost - when a mysterious and flamboyant man arrives in their lives.
