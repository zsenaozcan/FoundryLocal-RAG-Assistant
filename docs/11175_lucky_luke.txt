Title: Lucky Luke (1991)
Genres: Comedy, Western
Director: Terence Hill
Main Cast: Terence Hill, Nancy Morgan, Fritz Sperberg, Dominic Barto, Bo Greigh
Keywords: saloon, horse, brother, arrest, spaghetti western

Overview: Lucky Luke becomes the Sheriff of Daisy Town and runs out all the criminals. Then the Dalton brothers arrive and try to get the Indians to break the peace treaty and attack the town.
