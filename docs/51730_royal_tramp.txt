Title: Royal Tramp (1992)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Wong Jing
Main Cast: Stephen Chow, Sharla Cheung Man, Deric Wan Siu-Lun, Chingmy Yau, Ng Man-Tat
Keywords: china, queen, emperor, eunuch

Overview: The story of Wilson Bond, a pimp who after saving Chan, the leader of the Heaven and Earth society, a revolutionary group, is made a member. After a botched first assignment for the group, he is made a servant to the Prince, the very person the Heaven and Earth society want to overthrow. What follows is plenty of mayhem and laughter.
