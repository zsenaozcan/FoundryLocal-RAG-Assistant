Title: Return of the Living Dead: Rave to the Grave (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Ellory Elkayem
Main Cast: Aimee-Lynn Chadwick, Cory Hardrict, John Keefe, Jenny Mollen, Peter Coyote
Keywords: halloween, undead, zombie

Overview: A college student creates and sells a drug called 'Z' on campus which resurrects the living dead, who wreak havoc at a Halloween rave.
