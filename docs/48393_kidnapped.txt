Title: Kidnapped (2010)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Miguel Ángel Vivas
Main Cast: Fernando Cayo, Ana Wagener, Manuela Vellés, Dritan Biba, Martijn Kuiper
Keywords: rape, long take, violence

Overview: Three hooded Eastern-European criminals burst into a home in a Madrid gated community, holding the family hostage in its own home, and forcing the father to empty his credit cards. But the family fights back - brutally.
