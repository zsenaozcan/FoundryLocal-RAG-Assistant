Title: Cupcakes (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Eytan Fox
Main Cast: Ofer Shechter, Anat Waxman, Yael Bar Zohar, Efrat Dor, Dana Ivgy
Keywords: musical, tel aviv, drama, romantic comedy

Overview: A group of friends in a Tel Aviv suburb get together to watch Universong, a Eurovision-like television song contest. They gather to watch and are depressed by the lifelessness of the Israeli entry, a parody of many recent offerings, a flashy, grating song about "amour." Realizing that Anat is distraught over the crisis in her marriage, they decide to compose a song to cheer her up. As a lark, they enters their cellphone video of it in next year's contest, and it becomes Israel's entry.
