Title: Sense and Sensibility (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: John Alexander
Main Cast: Hattie Morahan, Charity Wakefield, David Morrissey, Janet McTeer, Dan Stevens

Overview: This is the acclaimed 2008 BBC adaptation of the famous Jane Austen novel. While it originally aired as a 3-part miniseries, this home video release includes a single uninterrupted version of the entire film.
