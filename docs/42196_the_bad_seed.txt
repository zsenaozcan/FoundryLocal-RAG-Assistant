Title: The Bad Seed (1956)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Mervyn LeRoy
Main Cast: Patty McCormack, Eileen Heckart, Henry Jones, Nancy Kelly, Evelyn Varden
Keywords: evil child

Overview: Young Rhoda Penmark is sweet, smart … and inherently evil! After a school chum dies during a picnic, no one suspects Rhoda, except the janitor of her apartment building. But when Rhoda's mother finds out that her own mother was a cold-blooded killer, she begins to suspect Rhoda might be the victim of some faulty genetics.
