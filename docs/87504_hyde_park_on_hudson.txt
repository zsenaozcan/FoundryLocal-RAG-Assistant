Title: Hyde Park on Hudson (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, History
Director: Roger Michell
Main Cast: Bill Murray, Olivia Williams, Laura Linney, Olivia Colman, Elizabeth Marvel
Keywords: queen, franklin delano roosevelt, park, hyde park, queen of england, king of england, 1930s

Overview: The story of the love affair between FDR and his distant cousin Margaret Stuckley, centered around the weekend in 1939 when the King and Queen of the United Kingdom visited upstate New York.
