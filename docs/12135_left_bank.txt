Title: Left Bank (2008)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Horror, Thriller
Director: Pieter Van Hees
Main Cast: Eline Kuppens, Matthias Schoenaerts, Sien Eggers, Tom Dewispelaere, Frank Vercruyssen
Keywords: antwerp, legend, foot race, flemish

Overview: A chilling thriller about a woman, who upon moving in with her new boyfriend, becomes obsessed with the fate of the previous tenant and descends into madness.
