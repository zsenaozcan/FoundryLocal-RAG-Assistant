Title: My Blue Heaven (1950)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Henry Koster
Main Cast: Betty Grable, Dan Dailey, David Wayne, Jane Wyatt, Mitzi Gaynor
Keywords: musical

Overview: Radio star Kitty Moran, long married to partner Jack, finds she's pregnant, but miscarries. For a change, the couple turn their act into a series on early TV and try to adopt a baby. Finally they acquiring a girl in a somewhat back alley manner.
