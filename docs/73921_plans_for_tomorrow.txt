Title: Plans for Tomorrow (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Juana Macías
Main Cast: Carme Elias, Goya Toledo, Ana Labordeta, Aura Garrido
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Three stories intertwined which occur in a single day. The day Ines discovers she is pregnant, Antonia decides to abandon her family and Marian realizes she is going to die.
