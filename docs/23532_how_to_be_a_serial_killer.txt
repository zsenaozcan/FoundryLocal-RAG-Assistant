Title: How to Be a Serial Killer (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Luke Ricci
Main Cast: Dameon Clarke, Laura Regan, George Wyner, Ryan Smith, Mary Jo Catlett
Keywords: independent film

Overview: HOW TO BE A SERIAL KILLER is the story of Mike Wilson, a charismatic, educated, and articulate young man who has found his life's purpose in exterminating people. Mike is determined to spread his message about the joy of serial killing and recruits a lost soul named Bart to be his pupil.
