Title: The Miracle Worker (2000)
Genres: Drama, TV Movie
Director: Nadia Tass
Main Cast: Hallie Kate Eisenberg, Alison Elliott, David Strathairn, Lucas Black, Kate Greenhouse
Keywords: blindness and impaired vision, education, woman director

Overview: Devoted teacher Anne Sullivan (Alison Elliott) leads deaf, blind and mute Helen Keller (Hallie Kate Eisenberg) out of solitude and helps integrate her into the world.
