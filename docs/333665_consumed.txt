Title: Consumed (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Daryl Wein
Main Cast: Zoe Lister-Jones, Victor Garber, Beth Grant, Taylor Kinney, Kunal Nayyar

Overview: When Sophie's son, Garrett, develops a mysterious illness, she embarks on a search for answers. This leads her into the controversial world of Genetically Modified Organisms (GMOs) where a sociopolitical battle rages between organic farmers and big biotech corporations. As her desperation grows, so too does her quest for knowledge. And the deeper she goes, a more heightened sense of danger develops that preys on her state of mind, as she attempts to discover the root cause of her son's illness.
