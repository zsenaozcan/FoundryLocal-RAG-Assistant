Title: Bellissima (1951)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Luchino Visconti
Main Cast: Anna Magnani, Walter Chiari, Tina Apicella, Gastone Renzelli, Tecla Scarano
Keywords: satire

Overview: Bellissima is a satire of the film industry, and centers around a mother and daughter after the latter attends an audition.
