Title: The Asphyx (1972)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Science Fiction, Foreign
Director: Peter Newbrook
Main Cast: Robert Powell, Robert Stephens, Jane Lapotaire, Alex Scott
Keywords: soul, experiment, supernatural

Overview: Hugo is a brilliant turn-of-the-century scientist, loved and respected by his family and friends, admired by his colleagues. But he is a man quickly becoming obsessed with a curious and frightening question... what is the mysterious apparition found in the photographs of his dying subjects?
