Title: Blind Faith (1998)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ernest R. Dickerson
Main Cast: Charles S. Dutton, Courtney B. Vance, Kadeem Hardison, Garland Whitt, Lonette McKee
Keywords: homophobia, law, gay son, african american, bronx, criminal defence lawyer

Overview: in 1957 an African-American criminal lawyer (Courtney B. Vance) defends his nephew, who faces the death penalty for murdering a white boy. Charles S. Dutton, Kadeem Hardison, Garland Whitt and Lonette McKee costar in this drama of racial and social tensions.
