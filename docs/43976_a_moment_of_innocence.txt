Title: A Moment of Innocence (1996)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Foreign
Director: Mohsen Makhmalbaf
Main Cast: Mirhadi Tayebi, Mohsen Makhmalbaf, Ali Bakhsi, Ammar Tafti, Maryam Mohamadamini
Keywords: gun, winter, marriage proposal, flower, policeman

Overview: A semi-autobiographical account of Makmahlbaf's experience as a teenager when, as a 17-year-old, he stabbed a policeman at a protest rally. Two decades later, he tracks down the policeman he injured in an attempt to make amends.
