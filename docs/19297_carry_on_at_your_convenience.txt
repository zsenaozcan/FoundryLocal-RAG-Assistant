Title: Carry On at Your Convenience (1971)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Gerald Thomas
Main Cast: Sid James, Kenneth Williams, Charles Hawtrey, Joan Sims, Hattie Jacques
Keywords: factory, trade union, toilet, coach, romance, brighton, pier, seaside, innuendo, picketing

Overview: This is the tale of industrial strife at WC Boggs' Lavatory factory. Vic Spanner is the union representative who calls a strike at the drop of a hat; eventually everyone has to get fed up with him. This is also the ideal opportunity for lots of lavatorial jokes...
