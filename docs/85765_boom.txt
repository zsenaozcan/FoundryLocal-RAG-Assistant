Title: Boom! (1968)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Joseph Losey
Main Cast: Elizabeth Taylor, Richard Burton, Noël Coward, Joanna Shimkus, Michael Dunn
Keywords: island, mysterious man, death, rich

Overview: Explores the confrontation between the woman who has everything, including emptiness, and a penniless poet who has nothing but the ability to fill a wealthy woman's needs.
