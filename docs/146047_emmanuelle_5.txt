Title: Emmanuelle 5 (1987)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Walerian Borowczyk
Main Cast: Monique Gabrielle, Crofton Hardester, Dana Burns Westburg, Yaseen Khan, Heidi Paine
Keywords: erotic movie

Overview: Emmanuelle, the sexiest woman in the world, endures a streak of bad luck that begins when she's stripped by a mob of adoring fans at an international film festival. Emmanuelle's lousy luck continues when she's abducted from her yacht off the South of France and forced to submit to the erotic desires of an Arab sheik.
