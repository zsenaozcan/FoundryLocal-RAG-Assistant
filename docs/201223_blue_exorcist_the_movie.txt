Title: Blue Exorcist: The Movie (2012)
Genres: Animation, Fantasy, Mystery, Horror
Director: Atsushi Takahashi
Main Cast: Jun Fukuyama, Kana Hanazawa, Kazuya Nakai, Koji Yusa, Yuuki Kaji
Keywords: satan, demon, anime

Overview: Rin Okumura is raised by a famous exorcist named Father Fujimoto. After an argument between the two, Rin discovers he is the son of the devil. Rin decides to fight his fate by joining the True Cross Academy to become an exorcist and defeat demons. However, when he draws his father's sword a dark power is released.
