Title: Mostly Ghostly 3: One Night in Doom House (2016)
Genres: Family, Fantasy, Horror
Director: Ron Oliver
Main Cast: Corey Fogelmanis, Sophie Reynolds, Blake Michael, Danny Trejo, Olivia Ryan Stern
Keywords: halloween, haunted house, based on children's book, ghost

Overview: Just one enchanted jewel stands between earth and an army of evil spirits led by the devious ghoul, Phears. With the help of his new girlfriend and ghost pals, Max Doyle races to find the crystal and save the world.
