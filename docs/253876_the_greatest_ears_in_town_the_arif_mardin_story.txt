Title: The Greatest Ears in Town: The Arif Mardin Story (2010)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Doug Biro
Main Cast: Arif Mardin, Phil Collins, Aretha Franklin, Bette Midler, Daryl Hall

Overview: The Greatest Ears in Town is an insightful documentary and testimonial to Arif Mardin; the producer, arranger, musician and multi Grammy award winner.
