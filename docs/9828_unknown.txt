Title: Unknown (2006)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Simon Brand
Main Cast: Jim Caviezel, Greg Kinnear, Bridget Moynahan, Joe Pantoliano, Barry Pepper
Keywords: ransom, factory, amnesia, kidnapping, men, police, past, toxic, survival, photographic memory
