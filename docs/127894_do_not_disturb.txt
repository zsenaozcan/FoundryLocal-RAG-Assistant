Title: Do Not Disturb (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Yvan Attal
Main Cast: Asia Argento, Yvan Attal, Laetitia Casta, François Cluzet, Charlotte Gainsbourg
Keywords: remake, porno filmmaking

Overview: In this lively French remake of Humpday, best buds reunite and revive their friendship on a questionable dare. Can two straight guys really make a gay sex film, together?
