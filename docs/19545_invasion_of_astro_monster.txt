Title: Invasion of Astro-Monster (1965)
Genres: Romance, Science Fiction, Action, Adventure
Director: Ishirô Honda
Main Cast: Nick Adams, Akira Takarada, Jun Tazaki, Akira Kubo, Kumi Mizuno
Keywords: japan, monster, godzilla, sequel, ufo, alien, giant monster, tokusatsu, alien invasion, gojira

Overview: In the 6th Godzilla movie, mysterious aliens from the Planet X ask earth's people to help them save their world from the dreaded Ghidorah (aka "Monster Zero") by letting them "borrow" Godzilla and Rodan. Monster mayhem ensues on Planet X, but it's soon discovered that the aliens have other intentions for the monsters.
