Title: Carousel (1956)
Genres: Music
Director: Henry King
Main Cast: Gordon MacRae, Shirley Jones, Barbara Ruick, Cameron Mitchell, Claramae Turner
Keywords: carnival, carousel , musical, barker, fairground

Overview: Billy Bigelow has been dead for 15 years. Now outside the pearly gates, he long ago waived his right to go back to Earth for a day. He has heard that there is a problem with his family: namely with his wife Julie Bigelow, née Jordan, and his child he hasn't met. He would now like to head back to Earth to assist in rectifying the problem; but before he may go, he has to get permission from the gatekeeper by telling him his story. Adapted from the Rodgers and Hammerstein hit Broadway musical.
