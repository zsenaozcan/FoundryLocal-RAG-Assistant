Title: The Seventh Victim (1943)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery
Director: Mark Robson
Main Cast: Kim Hunter, Tom Conway, Jean Brooks, Hugh Beaumont, Erford Gage
Keywords: paranoia, satanism, murder, betrayal, punishment, noose, dead man, val lewton, dying woman, satanic cult

Overview: A young woman searches for her missing sister who, unknown to her, has become involved with a group of Satan worshippers in Greenwich Village.
