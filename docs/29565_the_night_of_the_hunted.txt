Title: The Night of the Hunted (1980)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, Romance, Foreign
Director: Jean Rollin
Main Cast: Brigitte Lahaie, Dominique Journet, Rachel Mhas, Bernard Papineau, Vincent Gardère

Overview: A woman is taken to a mysterious clinic whose patients have a mental disorder in which their memories and identities are disintegrating as a result of a strange environmental accident.
