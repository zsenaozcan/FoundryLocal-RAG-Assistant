Title: Conagher (1991)
Genres: Action, Western, Romance
Director: Reynaldo Villalobos
Main Cast: Sam Elliott, Katharine Ross, Barry Corbin, Billy Green Bush, Ken Curtis
Keywords: cowboy, homestead

Overview: Mrs. Evie Teale is struggling to stay alive while raising her two children alone on a remote homestead. Conn Conagher is a honest, hardworking cowboy. Their lives are intertwined as they fight the elements, indians, outlaws, and loneliness.
