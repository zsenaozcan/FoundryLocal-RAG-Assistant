Title: The Snow Queen (1957)
Genres: Animation, Family, Fantasy
Director: Lev Atamanov
Main Cast: Vladimir Gribkov, Mariya Babanova, Yanina Zhejmo, Sergei Martinson, Aleksei Konsovsky

Overview: When the Snow Queen, a lonely and powerful fairy, kidnaps the human boy Kay, his best friend Gerda must overcome many obstacles on her journey to rescue him.
