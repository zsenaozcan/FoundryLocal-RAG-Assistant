Title: To Die For (1995)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Comedy, Thriller
Director: Gus Van Sant
Main Cast: Nicole Kidman, Matt Dillon, Joaquin Phoenix, Casey Affleck, Illeana Douglas
Keywords: adultery, winter, obsession, television, new hampshire, narcissistic personality disorder

Overview: Susan wants to work in television and will therefore do anything it takes, even if it means killing her husband. A very dark comedy from independent director Gus Van Sant with a brilliant Nicole Kidman in the leading role.
