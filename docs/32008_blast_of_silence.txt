Title: Blast of Silence (1961)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Allen Baron
Main Cast: Allen Baron, Molly McCarthy, Larry Tucker, Bill DePrato, Peter H. Clune
Keywords: new york, hitman

Overview: A hired killer from Cleveland has a job to do on a second-string mob boss in New York. But a special girl from his past, and a fat gun dealer with pet rats, each gets in his way.
