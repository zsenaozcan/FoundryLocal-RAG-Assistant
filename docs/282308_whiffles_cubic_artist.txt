Title: Whiffles, Cubic Artist (1912)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Georges Monca
Main Cast: Charles Prince, Gabrielle Lange

Overview: After visiting the first Cubist exposition in Paris, Rigadin (Charles Prince) is struck by his discovery: A egg is a rhombus, the earth a cube! Unfortunately, he is engaged to Miss Rondebosse (Gabrielle Lange), whose father is head of the Tradition...
