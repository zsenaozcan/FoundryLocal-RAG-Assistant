Title: Where Is Parsifal? (1985)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Henri Helman
Main Cast: Tony Curtis, Berta Domínguez D., Erik Estrada, Peter Lawford, Ron Moody

Overview: Tucked away in his castle, a hypochondriac inventor plays generous host to a revolving cast of wacky guests. But to pay off his mounting debts, he must sell either a powerful businessman or a rich gypsy on his latest creation: a laser skywriter.
