Title: The Black Rose (1950)
Genres: War, Adventure, History
Director: Henry Hathaway
Main Cast: Tyrone Power, Orson Welles, Cecile Aubry, Jack Hawkins, Michael Rennie

Overview: In the time of the crusades, a Saxon youth is forced to run away from England. He goes with his loyal retainer who brings along a British long bow. The two go all the way to China where they become involved in intrigues in the court of Kubla Kahn.
