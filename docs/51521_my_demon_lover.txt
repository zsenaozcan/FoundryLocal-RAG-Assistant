Title: My Demon Lover (1987)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Horror, Romance
Director: Charlie Loventhal
Main Cast: Scott Valentine, Michele Little, Robert Trebor, Gina Gallego, Alan Fudge
Keywords: demon

Overview: Denny has yet again been left by her thug boyfriend Chip. It seems as if she is doomed to be stuck with awful guys, this time she has to choose between nerdy Charles and the strange Kaz, who turns into a monster when sexually aroused.
