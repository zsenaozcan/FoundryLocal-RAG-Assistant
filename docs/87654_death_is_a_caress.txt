Title: Death Is a Caress (1949)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Edith Carlmar
Main Cast: Claus Wiese, Bjørg Riiser-Larsen, Ingolf Rogde, Gisle Straume, Einar Vaage
Keywords: femme fatale, murder, woman director, film noir

Overview: The story of a working man, who's got a gorgeous fiancée but who falls for a rich older woman - who turns out to be a femme fatale.
