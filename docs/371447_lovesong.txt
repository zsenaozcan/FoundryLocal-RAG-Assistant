Title: Lovesong (2017)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: So Yong Kim
Main Cast: Jena Malone, Riley Keough, Jessie Ok Gray, Sky Ok Gray, Brooklyn Decker
Keywords: female friendship, road trip, loneliness, wedding, unspoken love, woman director

Overview: Neglected by her husband, Sarah embarks on an impromptu road trip with her young daughter and her best friend, Mindy. Along the way, the dynamic between the two friends intensifies before circumstances force them apart. Years later, Sarah attempts to rebuild their intimate connection in the days before Mindy’s wedding.
