Title: The Happiest Days of Your Life (1950)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Frank Launder
Main Cast: Alastair Sim, Margaret Rutherford, Guy Middleton, Joyce Grenfell, Edward Rigby

Overview: Nutbourne College, an old established, all-boys, boarding school is told that another school is to be billeted with due to wartime restrictions. The shock is that it's an all-girls school that has been sent. The two head teachers are soon battling for the upper hand with each other and the Ministry. But a crisis (or two) forces them to work together.
