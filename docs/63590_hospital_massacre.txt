Title: Hospital Massacre (1981)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Boaz Davidson
Main Cast: Barbi Benton, Charles Lucia, Jon Van Ness, John Warner Williams, Den Surles
Keywords: female nudity, nurse, murder, valentine, stalking, serial killer, decapitation, surprise ending, hospital, slasher
