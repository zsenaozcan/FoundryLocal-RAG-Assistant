Title: Borderline (1980)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Action
Director: Jerrold Freedman
Main Cast: Charles Bronson, Bruno Kirby, Bert Remsen, Michael Lerner, Kenneth McMillan
Keywords: illegal immigration, border patrol, united states–mexico barrier, human trafficking, border crossing

Overview: Jeb Maynard is a patrolman guarding the U.S.-Mexican border, whose partner and buddy Scooter has just been murdered. Maynard knows that a smuggler of illegal aliens is responsible for Scooter's death, but the feds insist that drug dealers committed the crime.
