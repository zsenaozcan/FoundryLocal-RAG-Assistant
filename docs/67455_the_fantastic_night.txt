Title: The Fantastic Night (1942)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Marcel L'Herbier
Main Cast: Fernand Gravey, Micheline Presle, Saturnin Fabre, Charles Granval

Overview: Denis, a poor student in philosophy, works as a night porter in the Paris market of Les Halles in order to pay for his studies. Constantly weary, he falls asleep and dreams of a beautiful girl in white, Irène, with whom he falls in love.
