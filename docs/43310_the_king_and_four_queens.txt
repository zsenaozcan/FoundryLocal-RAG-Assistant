Title: The King and Four Queens (1956)
Genres: Adventure, Western, Mystery, Comedy
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: Clark Gable, Eleanor Parker, Jean Willes, Jo Van Fleet, Barbara Nichols
Keywords: con man, widow

Overview: Opportunistic con man Dan Kehoe ingratiates himself with the cantankerous mother of four outlaws and their beautiful widows in order to find their hidden gold.
