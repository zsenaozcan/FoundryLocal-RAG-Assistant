Title: Prophet's Prey (2015)
Genres: Crime, Documentary
Director: Amy Berg
Main Cast: Sam Brower, Nick Cave, Andrew Chatwin, Lori Chatwin
Keywords: marriage, polygamy, religion, prophet, church, woman director, abuse

Overview: Filmmaker Amy Berg sheds light on the sexual, financial and spiritual abuses heaped upon members of the Fundamentalist Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints by their former leader, Warren Jeffs.
