Title: Infection (2004)
Genres: Horror, Foreign, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Masayuki Ochiai
Main Cast: Michiko Hada, Mari Hoshino, Tae Kimura, Yoko Maki, Kaho Minami
Keywords: nurse, infection, mass lunacy, hospital

Overview: Infection takes place in a dark, isolated hospital, where one doctors mistake has led to dire consequences for a patient. In a hospital death is just a breath away.
