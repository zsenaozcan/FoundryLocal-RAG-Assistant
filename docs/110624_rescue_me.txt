Title: Rescue Me (1993)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Arthur Allan Seidelman
Main Cast: Michael Dudikoff, Stephen Dorff, Ami Dolenz, Peter DeLuise, William Lucking
Keywords: kidnapping, high school, motorcycle

Overview: Fraser's unrequited love for the beautiful and rich Ginny gets a chance to prove itself, when she is suddenly kidnapped. He teams up with rebel Mac, who's got a score to settle with the kidnappers. Together they cross the country, looking for Ginny and the bad guys.
