Title: The Big Trail (1930)
Genres: Adventure, Western, Romance
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: John Wayne, Marguerite Churchill, El Brendel, Tully Marshall, Tyrone Power Sr.
Keywords: mississippi, wagon train

Overview: Breck Coleman leads a wagon train of pioneers through Indian attack, storms, deserts, swollen rivers, down cliffs and so on while looking for the murder of a trapper and falling in love with Ruth Cameron.
