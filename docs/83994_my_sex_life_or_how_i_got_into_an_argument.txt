Title: My Sex Life... or How I Got Into an Argument (1996)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Arnaud Desplechin
Main Cast: Mathieu Amalric, Emmanuelle Devos, Emmanuel Salinger, Marianne Denicourt, Thibault de Montalembert

Overview: Paul Dedalus is at a crossroads in his life. He has to make several decisions; should he complete his doctorate, does he want to become a full professor, does he really love his long-standing girlfriend, or should he re-start with one of his other lovers?
