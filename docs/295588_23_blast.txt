Title: 23 Blast (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Dylan Baker
Main Cast: Mark Hapka, Bram Hoover, Stephen Lang, Max Adler, Alexa PenaVega
Keywords: sport, family

Overview: When a high school football star is suddenly stricken with irreversible total blindness, he must decide whether to live a safe handicapped life or bravely return to the life he once knew and the sport he still loves.
