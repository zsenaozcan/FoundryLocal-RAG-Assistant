Title: Black Moon (1975)
Genres: Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Louis Malle
Main Cast: Cathryn Harrison,  Therese Giehse, Alexandra Stewart, Joe Dallesandro
Keywords: sex, allegory, dream, radio, nudity, reality, brother, sister, war, independent film

Overview: There is a war in the world between the men and the women. A young girl tries to escape this reality and comes to a hidden place where a strange unicorn lives with a family: Sister, Brother, many children and an old woman that never leaves her bed but stays in contact with the world through her radio...
