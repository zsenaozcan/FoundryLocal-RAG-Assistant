Title: La Marcia Su Roma (1962)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Dino Risi
Main Cast: Vittorio Gassman, Ugo Tognazzi, Roger Hanin, Mario Brega

Overview: March on Rome (Italian: La marcia su Roma) is a 1962 comedy film by Dino Risi with Vittorio Gassman and Ugo Tognazzi, aimed at describing the March on Rome of Benito Mussolini's black shirts from the point of view of two newly recruited, naïve black shirts
