Title: Grand Masti (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Indra Kumar
Main Cast: Ritesh Deshmukh, Vivek Oberoi, Aftab Shivdasani, Suresh Menon, Karishma Tanna

Overview: Amar, Prem and Meet are longtime friends who have gone through every phase of growing up together and are now bored of their insipid married lives. To add a little spice to their lives they plan a wild adventure at their college reunion! When fun turns into something more insidious and deceitful, they find themselves being led astray and right into a seductive web of danger. Packed with innuendo and double meaning, this is an adult comedy based on a strong undertone of sex.
