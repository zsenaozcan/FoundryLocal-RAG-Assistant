Title: The Deadly Mantis (1957)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Nathan H. Juran
Main Cast: Craig Stevens, William Hopper, Alix Talton, Donald Randolph, Pat Conway
Keywords: monster, paleontology, paleontologist, arctic, giant monster, new york city, giant insect, man eating monster, prehistoric creature, mantis

Overview: The calving of an Arctic iceberg releases a giant praying mantis, trapped in suspended animation since prehistoric times. It first attacks military outposts to eat their occupants, then makes its way to the warmer latitudes of Washington and New York. A paleontologist works together with military units to try to kill it. (copied from IMDB)
