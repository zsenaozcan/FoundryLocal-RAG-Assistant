Title: School for Love (1955)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Marc Allégret
Main Cast: Jean Marais, Brigitte Bardot, Isabelle Pia, Yves Robert, Denise Noël
Keywords: depression, teacher, emotional blackmail

Overview: In this erotic French romance two lovely women compete for the love of the same man. The film is also known as Joy of Loving.
