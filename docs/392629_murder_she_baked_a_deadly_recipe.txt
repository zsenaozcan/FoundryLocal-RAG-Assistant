Title: Murder, She Baked: A Deadly Recipe (2016)
Genres: TV Movie, Mystery
Director: Kristoffer Tabori
Main Cast: Alison Sweeney, Cameron Mathison, Barbara Niven, Gabriel Hogan, Toby Levins

Overview: Bakery owner Hannah Swensen just can't keep her hands out of the batter when murder stirs things up in Lake Eden, Minnesota, leaving the sheriff dead, a deputy accused, and a killer on the loose.
