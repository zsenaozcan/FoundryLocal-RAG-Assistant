Title: Witchboard III: The Possession (1995)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: Peter Svatek
Main Cast: David Nerman, Elizabeth Lambert, Cedric Smith, Donna Sarrasin, Danette Mackay
Keywords: exorcism, supernatural, sequel, ouija, demon, possesion

Overview: Brian, an out-of-work stockbroker, is introduced by his landlord Mr. Redman to a new source of insider information: the Ouija! The Ouija makes him rich, but when Mr. Redman kills himself and Brian suffers a freak accident, Brian's wife starts noticing changes in her husband...
