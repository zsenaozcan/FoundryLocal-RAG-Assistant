Title: The Making of 'The Terminator': A Retrospective (1992)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jeff McQueen
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: making of

Overview: An interview session with Arnold Schwarzenegger and director James Cameron gives us a better understanding of how hard it was making Terminator, The (1984) on such a low budget. Clips from the film are present along with funny and interesting stories from behind the scenes.
