Title: God Help the Girl (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Music
Director: Stuart Murdoch
Main Cast: Emily Browning, Olly Alexander, Hannah Murray, Pierre Boulanger, Cora Bissett
Keywords: anorexia, psychiatric hospital, pop music, lifeguard

Overview: Eve is a catastrophe—low on self-esteem but high on fantasy, especially when it comes to music. Over the course of one Glasgow summer, she meets two similarly rootless souls: posh Cass and fastidious James, and together they form a group.
