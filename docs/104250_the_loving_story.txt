Title: The Loving Story (2011)
Genres: Documentary, Drama, History
Director: Nancy Buirski
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: civil rights movement , supreme court, interracial, woman director

Overview: This documentary film tells the dramatic story of Richard and Mildred Loving, an interracial couple living in Virginia in the 1950s, and their landmark Supreme Court Case, Loving v. Virginia, that changed history.
