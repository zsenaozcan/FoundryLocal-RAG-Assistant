Title: Hellraiser: Deader (2005)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Rick Bota
Main Cast: Kari Wuhrer, Paul Rhys, Simon Kunz, Marc Warren, Georgina Rylance
Keywords: suicide, pinhead, puzzle box, snuff films, puzzle box creator, revive dead

Overview: In London, after investigating crack addicted junkies for an article in her newspaper, Amy Klein watches a bizarre videotape where an underground group of youngsters in Bucharest apparently becoming zombies. Amy finds Marla dead with a puzzle cube in her hands. She brings the object to her hotel room, and opens it, beginning her journey to hell.
