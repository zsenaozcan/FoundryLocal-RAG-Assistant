Title: The Brass Bottle (1964)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Harry Keller
Main Cast: Tony Randall, Burl Ives, Barbara Eden, Kamala Devi, Edward Andrews
Keywords: genie

Overview: Comedy about the proverbial genie who comes out of a bottle (a table lamp in this instance) to serve his new master. The only problem is that instead of helping his master, the genie (Burl Ives) tends to get his master (Tony Randall) into more predicaments than he gets him out of.
