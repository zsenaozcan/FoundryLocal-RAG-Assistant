Title: Diary of a Hitman (1991)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: Roy London
Main Cast: Forest Whitaker, Jim Belushi, Sharon Stone, Sherilyn Fenn, Peter White
Keywords: hitman

Overview: Hitman Dekker is on his last job, out to kill the wife (Jain) of his client, but he starts to waiver. The longer he takes to do the job the more difficult it becomes.
