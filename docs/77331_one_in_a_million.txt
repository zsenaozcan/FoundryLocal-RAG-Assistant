Title: One in a Million (1995)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Måns Herngren
Main Cast: Tomas von Brömssen, Ulla Skoog, Gösta Ekman, Tintin Anderzon, Per Fritzell
Keywords: money, unemployment

Overview: Torsten Södergren lives in a normal house with his normal family in a completely normal city. Every morning he greets his normal neighbours before getting into his normal car and drives the usual way to his ordinary work at an ordinary bank. But one day he is fired from his work after twenty years of loyal service. Now he is prepared to do anything to get it back...
