Title: Song of the Exile (1990)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ann Hui
Main Cast: Maggie Cheung, Siu-Kwong Chung, Tan Lang Jachi Tian, Waise Lee, Zi Xiong Li
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Set in the early 1970s, it tells the story of a Chinese-Japanese student who returns to her native Hong Kong after graduating from a university in London. Once she arrives back home, she and her family begins to fight, largely due to cultural and societal conflicts between her mother and herself.
