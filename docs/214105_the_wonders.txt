Title: The Wonders (2013)
Genres: Mystery
Director: Avi Nesher
Main Cast: Efrat Gosh, Ori Hizkiah, Yehuda Levi, Adir Miller, Yuval Scharf

Overview: Avi Nesher's new film tracks the complex relationship between a Jerusalem Graffiti artist and a mysterious modern day prophet who is imprisoned in an abandoned apartment across the Artist's window.
