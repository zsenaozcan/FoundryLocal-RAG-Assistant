Title: Piranha (1995)
Genres: Horror
Director: Scott P. Levy
Main Cast: William Katt, Alexandra Paul, Monte Markham, Darleen Carr, Mila Kunis
Keywords: piranha

Overview: Doctor Baines has been conducting genetic experiments on piranhas and has made them virtually unstoppable. Unfortunately, his assistants, Maggie and Paul, accidentally release the hybrids into the Lost River Lake threatening to destroy everyone in their path. Can they prevent the flesh-eating piranhas from escaping into the ocean and spawning?
