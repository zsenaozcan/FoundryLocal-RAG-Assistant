Title: Eating Out 2: Sloppy Seconds (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Phillip J. Bartell
Main Cast: Brett Chukerman, Rebekah Kochan, Jim Verraros, Emily Brooke Hands, Scott Vickaryous

Overview: After Marc dumps him, Kyle unites with Gwen and Tiffani to land sexually confused art model Troy by pretending to be straight. However, Marc wants Troy, too, and members from a notorious "ex-gay" group are slipping for the both of them.
