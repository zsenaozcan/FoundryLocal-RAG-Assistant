Title: The Burglars (1971)
Genres: Action, Crime
Director: Henri Verneuil
Main Cast: Jean-Paul Belmondo, Omar Sharif, Dyan Cannon, Robert Hossein, Nicole Calfan
Keywords: burglar, gang of thieves, suspense, theft, french noir

Overview: In Athens a collection of emeralds is successfully stolen by a team of robbers, led by safe-cracker Azad. Things go smoothly until they miss the ship by which they planned their escape; a police chief pursues Azad while he waits for the next ship to set off.
