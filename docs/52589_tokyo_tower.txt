Title: Tokyo Tower (2005)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Takashi Minamoto
Main Cast: Hitomi Kuroki, Junichi Okada, Jun Matsumoto, Shinobu Terajima, Kento Handa

Overview: Toru Kojima and his friend Koji are young student boys with one thing in common - they both love to date older women. Koji is a playboy with several women, young and older, whereas Toru is a romantic with his heart set on on certain lady.
