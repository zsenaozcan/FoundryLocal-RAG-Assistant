Title: Case départ (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Thomas Ngijol
Main Cast: Thomas Ngijol, Fabrice Eboué, Joséphine de Meaux, Etienne Chicot, Eriq Ebouaney
Keywords: time travel, adventure, comedy

Overview: Two contempo Frenchmen of Antillean descent visit their ancestor's time as well as their land in the slavery-themed French era.
