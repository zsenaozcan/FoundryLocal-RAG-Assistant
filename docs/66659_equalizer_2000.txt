Title: Equalizer 2000 (1987)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Science Fiction
Director: Cirio H. Santiago
Main Cast: Robert Patrick, Richard Norton, Don Gordon Bell, Corinne Wahl, William Steis
Keywords: post-apocalyptic, wasteland, desert, machine gun, biker gang

Overview: In a bleak, postnuclear future world, warring factions struggle to claim the Equalizer 2000, the one weapon powerful enough to guarantee survival.
