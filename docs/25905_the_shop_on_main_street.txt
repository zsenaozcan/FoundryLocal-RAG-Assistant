Title: The Shop on Main Street (1965)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Ján Kadár
Main Cast: Ida Kaminska, Jozef Kroner, Martin Gregor, Hana Slivková, Martin Hollý
Keywords: holocaust, nazis, slovakia, time of war

Overview: Slovakia during WW2. Tono lives a poor life, but the authorities offer him a take over the Jewish widow Lautman's little shop for sewing material.
