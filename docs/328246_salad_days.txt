Title: Salad Days (2015)
Genres: Music, Documentary
Director: Scott Crawford
Main Cast: Fred Armisen, Dave Grohl, Ian MacKaye, Thurston Moore, Henry Rollins
Keywords: punk rock, music documentary

Overview: 'Salad Days' is an insightful documentary film that examines the Washington, DC punk scene from the early 1980s to the decade's end. The city played an integral part in shaping the alternative music explosion of the 1990s and its impact on popular culture continues today.
