Title: Charley Varrick (1973)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Don Siegel
Main Cast: Walter Matthau, Joe Don Baker, Felicia Farr, Andrew Robinson, Sheree North
Keywords: suicide, sheriff, based on novel, small town, new mexico, hitman, pilot, mafia, desert, bank heist

Overview: Charley Varrick robs a bank in a small town with his friends. Instead of obtaining a small amount of money they discover they stole a very large amount of money belonging to the mob. Charley must now come up with a plan to not only evade the police but the mob as well.
