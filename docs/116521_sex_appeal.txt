Title: Sex Appeal (1986)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Chuck Vincent
Main Cast: Louie Bonanno, Tally Chanel, Jerome Brenner, Marcia Karr, Marie Sawyer

Overview: This is the story of young Tony Cannelloni as he attempts to break away from his overbearing mom and strike out on his own. He sets himself up with a new apartment, look, and lifestyle to improve his "sex appeal". Through a series of encounters with a variety of women he gets ever closer to achieving his goal. However, a never-ending series of calamities always seem to interrupt his progress.
