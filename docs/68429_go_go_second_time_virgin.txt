Title: Go, Go Second Time Virgin (1969)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Kōji Wakamatsu
Main Cast: Michio Akiyama, Mimi Kozakura, Hiroshi Imaizumi

Overview: After being raped in an unknown rooftop, nineteen year-old girl Poppo meets a mysterious boy, and both share their sexual traumas and fears, with fatal consequences.
