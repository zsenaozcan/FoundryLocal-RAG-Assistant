Title: Miami Blues (1990)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: George Armitage
Main Cast: Alec Baldwin, Jennifer Jason Leigh, Fred Ward, Cecilia Pérez-Cervera, Georgie Cranford
Keywords: miami, assault, thief, false teeth, charles willeford

Overview: When Fred gets out of prison, he decides to start over in Miami, where he starts a violent one-man crime wave. He soon meets up with amiable college student Susie. Opposing Fred is Sgt Hoke Moseley, a cop who is getting a bit old for the job, especially since the job of cop in 1980's Miami is getting crazier all the time.
