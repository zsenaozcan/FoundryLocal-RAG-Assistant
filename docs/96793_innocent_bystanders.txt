Title: Innocent Bystanders (1972)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Peter Collinson
Main Cast: Stanley Baker, Geraldine Chaplin, Donald Pleasence, Dana Andrews, Warren Mitchell
Keywords: spy, redemption, russian scientist

Overview: Washed-up agent John Craig is given the task of proving his worth by tracking down a Russian scientist on the run. Cross and double-cross is the name of the game.
