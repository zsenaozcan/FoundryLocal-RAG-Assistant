Title: Uzumasa Limelight (2014)
Genres: Foreign, Drama, Action
Director: Ken Ochiai
Main Cast: Seizô Fukumoto, Chihiro Yamamoto, Masashi Goda, Hirotaro Honda, Hisako Manda
Keywords: samurai, katana, katana sword, chanbara

Overview: A moving, nostalgic portrait of the men behind the golden age of chanbara (sword-fighting dramas and films) that goes behind the scenes of the distinctive film genre for which Japan is most famous, with dominant performance by real-life kirare-yaku Seizo Fukumoto.
