Title: River (2016)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Jamie M. Dagg
Main Cast: Rossif Sutherland, Sara Botsford, Ted Atherton, Douangmany Soliphanh, David Soncin
Keywords: river, bangkok, thailand, volunteer, laos, murder, fugitive, doctor, sexual assault, accused of murder

Overview: In the south of Laos, an American volunteer doctor becomes a fugitive after he intervenes in the sexual assault of a young woman. When the assailant's body is pulled from the Mekong River, things quickly spiral out of control.
