Title: Badges of Fury (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Action, Crime
Director: Wong Tsz Ming
Main Cast: Wen Zhang, Jet Li, Liu Shishi, Michelle Chen, Liu Yan

Overview: When a spate of eerie murders erupts across HK two troub/emaking cops are assigned to the case. Young maverick Wang and grizzled vet Huang who is fed up with his reckless younger partner always landing them in hot water. Reaching a dead end after discovering all the victims were former boyfriends of aspiring starlet Liu, the detectives must play a deadly game. One of them must go undercover as Liu’s lover to lure the killer out.
