Title: Never Travel on a One Way Ticket (1987)
Genres: Thriller, Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Håkan Alexandersson 
Main Cast: Nina Antskog, Krister Broberg, Mats Flink, Christine Floderer, Karl-Erik Forsgårdh

Overview: A hardboiled story that takes place in a far future when most things are over. What’s left is greed, cruelty, sexuality, fire and a tad of poetry.
