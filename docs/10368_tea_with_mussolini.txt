Title: Tea with Mussolini (1999)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, War

Main Cast: Cher, Judi Dench, Joan Plowright, Maggie Smith, Lily Tomlin
Keywords: italy, hotel, loss of mother, ambassador, world war ii, loss of parents, widow, benito mussolini, independent film
