Title: The 13 Roses (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Emilio Martínez Lázaro
Main Cast: Pilar López de Ayala, Verónica Sánchez, Gabriella Pession, Nadia de Santiago, Teresa Hurtado de Ory
Keywords: spanish civil war, based on true story, execution, 1930s

Overview: True story of thirteen totally normal young women that suffered harsh questioning and were put in prison under made up charges of helping the rebellion against Franco back in the 1940s. Despite of their innocence, the thirteen were soon executed without even a trace of evidence of any wrong doing.
