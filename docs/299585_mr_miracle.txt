Title: Mr. Miracle (2014)
Genres: Drama, Family, TV Movie
Director: Carl Bessai
Main Cast: Rob Morrow, Michelle Harrison, Brittney Irvin, Kendra Anderson, Andrew Francis
Keywords: angel, christmas

Overview: Heavenly angel Harry Mills is sent to Earth on a trial assignment to intervene in the life of a woman who needs help getting her life on track after the death of her father. With a deadline of Christmas day, he attempts to help her heal in order to embrace a new future, and an unexpected love, just in time to celebrate the miracle of the holiday season.
