Title: Doomed! The Untold Story of Roger Corman's The Fantastic Four (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Marty Langford
Main Cast: Roger Corman, Michael Bailey Smith, Rebecca Staab, Jay Underwood, Alex Hyde-White
Keywords: comic book, superhero, b movie

Overview: A history of the ill-fated 1994 production of “The Fantastic Four” that was executive produced by Roger Corman.
