Title: 21 Years: Richard Linklater (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Tara Wood
Main Cast: Joey Lauren Adams, Jack Black, Miranda Cosgrove, Julie Delpy, Jay Duplass
Keywords: film maker

Overview: Documentary that takes a behind the scenes tour of groundbreaking director Richard Linklater's last 21 years creating his iconic legacy of films from the unconventional breakout 'Slacker' to the innovative 'Boyhood'. Features candid conversations with actors Ethan Hawke, Julie Delpy, Matthew McConaughey and others.
