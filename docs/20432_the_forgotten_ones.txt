Title: The Forgotten Ones (2008)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: Jörg Ihle
Main Cast: Jewel Staite, Justin Baldoni, Marc Bacher, Nikki Griffin, Kellan Lutz
Keywords: yacht

Overview: When a devastating boat crash shipwrecks a group of friends in the jungles of an uncharted island, they are savagely picked off one-by-one by a cannibalistic enemy that evolution forgot. A horrifying action-adventure in the vein of Predator and Lost.
