Title: Inferno (1953)
Genres: Romance, Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: Roy Ward Baker
Main Cast: Robert Ryan, Rhonda Fleming, William Lundigan, Larry Keating, Henry Hull
Keywords: survival, desert, survival skills, 3d

Overview: A spoiled and alcoholic millionaire, Robert Ryan, breaks his leg falling off of a horse, and is left to die in the desert by his cheating wife, Rhonda Fleming, and her lover, William Lundigan.
