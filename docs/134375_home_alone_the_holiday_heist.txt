Title: Home Alone: The Holiday Heist (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Family
Director: Peter Hewitt
Main Cast: Jodelle Ferland, Christian Martyn, Ellie Harvie, Peter DaCunha, Doug Murray

Overview: 8-year-old Finn who is terrified to learn his family is relocating from sunny California to Maine in the scariest house he has ever seen! Convinced that his new house is haunted, Finn sets up a series of elaborate traps to catch the “ghost” in action. Left home alone with his sister while their parents are stranded across town, Finn’s traps catch a new target – a group of thieves who have targeted Finn’s house.
