Title: Flesh and Bone (1993)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Romance
Director: Steve Kloves
Main Cast: Meg Ryan, Dennis Quaid, James Caan, Gwyneth Paltrow, Julia McNeal
Keywords: gambling, sex, robbery, texas, nudity, roadhouse, liquor, funeral, murder, romance

Overview: Some thirty years after Arlis witnesses his father murdering a family, he runs into Kay, who happens to be the family's baby who was spared. Kay and Arlis suspect nothing about each other, but when his father returns, old wounds are reopened.
