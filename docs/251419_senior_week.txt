Title: Senior Week (1988)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Stuart A. Goldman
Main Cast: Michael St. Gerard, George Robert Klek, Jennifer Gorey, Gary Kerr, Leesa Bryte

Overview: Everett and his party-hungry buddies embark on an all-night road-trip to the Florida beaches, for a week of fun, sun, and beautiful women. Mayhem ensues when 2 jealous girl-friends show up unexpectedly, along with a psycho teacher who's hunting down Everett because he owes her an overdue term-paper.
