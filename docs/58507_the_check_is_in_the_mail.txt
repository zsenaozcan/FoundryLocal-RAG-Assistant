Title: The Check Is in the Mail... (1986)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Joan Darling
Main Cast: Brian Dennehy, Anne Archer, Hallie Todd, Michael Bowen, Chris Hebert
Keywords: woman director, fighting back

Overview: Frustrated with all the minor irritants of the rat-race Richard Jackson decides to buy a goat, disconnect the electricity and become self-sufficient. But will this life be any less annoying?
