Title: 305 (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Daniel Holechek
Main Cast: Tim Larson, Brandon Tyra, Ed Portillo, B.J. Bales, Sunny Peabody

Overview: '305' is a mockumentary detailing the misadventures of five not-so-brave members of the Spartan army charged with guarding a seemingly ordinary goat path. But when their actions lead to the death of King Leonidas and his army of 300 men, the five must find a way to redeem themselves and save Sparta from invasion.
