Title: Finding Mr. Destiny (2010)
Genres: Family, Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Chang You-jeong
Main Cast: Im Soo-jung, Gong Yoo, Cheon Ho-jin, Lee Chung-a, Jang Young-nam
Keywords: south korea

Overview: A woman named Seo Ji-Woo (Im Soo-Jung) traveled to India and met her first true love Kim Jong-Ok. Since that time Ji-Woo can't forget her first love. She then turns the company "첫사랑 찾기 주식회사" - which means literally "Finding Your First True Love Company" to help track down Kim Jong-Ok. Han Gi-Joon (Gong Yoo) started "Finding Your First True Love Company" and he takes on Seo Ji-Woo is the company's first client.
