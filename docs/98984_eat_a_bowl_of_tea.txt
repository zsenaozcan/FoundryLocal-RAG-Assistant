Title: Eat a Bowl of Tea (1989)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Wayne Wang
Main Cast: Victor Wong, Lau Siu-Ming, Russell Wong, Cora Miao

Overview: A study in culture bridging, including ... a new US-born husband, trying to work within the traditional ways, a new China-born wife, eager to join the "dream" of America, two family-minded fathers, lots of gender-related social bifurcations.
