Title: Shooting Gallery (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Keoni Waxman
Main Cast: Roselyn Sánchez, Freddie Prinze Jr., Ving Rhames, Angus Macfadyen, Devon Sawa

Overview: A street-smart pool player falls in with a pack of hustlers. As he rises in the underground circuit, he lands in the middle of a match between his boss and a crooked cop.
