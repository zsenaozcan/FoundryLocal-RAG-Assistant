Title: Beyond Hypothermia (1996)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Patrick Leung
Main Cast: Jacklyn Wu , Lau Ching-Wan, Shirley Wong Sa-Lee, Han Jae-suk
Keywords: girls with guns

Overview: A mysterious and highly successful hitwoman in Hong Kong is paid to assassinate top-level crime bosses. After beginning a relationship with a noodle vendor, she decides to travel to South Korea to complete one last job. Unfortunately, the dead man's bodyguard is out for revenge.
