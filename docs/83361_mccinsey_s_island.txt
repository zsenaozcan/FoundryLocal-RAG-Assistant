Title: McCinsey's Island (1998)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Sam Firstenberg
Main Cast: Hulk Hogan, Grace Jones, Robert Vaughn
Keywords: machete, bandana, palms

Overview: Hulk Hogan and Grace Jones star in this adventure tale about a onetime secret agent who finds a treasure map on the shell of a turtle. Soon he's on a chase to recover the loot, just one step ahead of other seekers of wealth.
