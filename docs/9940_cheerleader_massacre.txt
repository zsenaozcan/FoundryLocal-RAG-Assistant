Title: Cheerleader Massacre (2003)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Jim Wynorski
Main Cast: GiGi Erneta, Tamie Sheffield, Charity Rahmer, Erin Byron, April Flowers
Keywords: female nudity, sex, nudity, cheerleader, murder, serial killer, cheerleading, slasher

Overview: Five high school cheerleaders, their coach and a couple of adolescent sex-crazed guys travel to a cabin in the woods for a weekend getaway only to be killed off one by one by an unseen maniac.
