Title: Windows (1980)
Genres: Horror, Drama, Thriller
Director: Gordon Willis
Main Cast: Talia Shire, Elizabeth Ashley, Joe Cortese, Kay Medford, Michael Gorrin
Keywords: telescope, lesbian

Overview: Shire is the subject of a perverse obsession by a Lesbian neighbor, Andrea, who not only is in lust with her but hires a rapist in order to get audio tapes of her moaning. Ashley turns pepping tom and watches Shire with a telescope as she begins an affair with Det. Cortese.
