Title: Santa Claws (1996)
Genres: Horror
Director: John A. Russo
Main Cast: Debbie Rochon, Grant Kramer, John Mowod, Marilyn Eastman, Dawn Michelucci

Overview: A young man finds his divorced mother having sex with a man in a Santa Clause hat and shoots them both dead. Years later, now thinking he is Santa Clause, the man develops an obsession with an erotic horror film star named Raven and begins stalking her.
