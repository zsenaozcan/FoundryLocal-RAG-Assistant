Title: Eddie Murphy Raw (1987)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Robert Townsend
Main Cast: Eddie Murphy, Tatyana Ali, Billie Allen, Eyde Byrde, Birdie M. Hale
Keywords: stand-up comedy, live comedy

Overview: Eddie Murphy delights, shocks and entertains with dead-on celebrity impersonations, observations on '80s love, sex and marriage, a remembrance of Mom's hamburgers and much more.
