Title: Marina (2013)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Music
Director: Stijn Coninx
Main Cast: Matteo Simoni, Cristiaan Campagna, Luigi Lo Cascio, Evelien Bosmans
Keywords: biography

Overview: The true story of Rocco Granata, an Italian coal miner's son who followed his passion for music against his father's wishes and social barriers.
