Title: Just Like Home (2007)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Lone Scherfig
Main Cast: Pernille Vallentin, Bodil Jørgensen, Ann Eleonora Jørgensen, Peter Gantzler, Mia Lyhne
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The tale of a troubled small town, and the brave few who made it their mission to keep the community together. As the citizens of a secluded Danish town gradually loose their trust in one and other, the sight of a naked man walking through town in the early hours of the morning sets off an unsettling wave of paranoia.
