Title: Vigil in the Night (1940)
Genres: Drama
Director: George Stevens
Main Cast: Carole Lombard, Brian Aherne, Anne Shirley, Julien Mitchell, Robert Coote
Keywords: nurse

Overview: Nurse Anne Lee blames herself for a fatal mistake of her sister Lucy, who also is a nurse. Anne loses her job, and gets a new one at a poorly equipped country hospital. There she falls in love with Dr. Prescott, who is battling with Mr. Bowly, the chairman of the local hospital board, who also makes Anne's life miserable. But then a virulent epidemic begins...
