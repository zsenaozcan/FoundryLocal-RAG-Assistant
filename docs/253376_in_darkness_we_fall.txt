Title: In Darkness We Fall (2014)
Genres: Horror
Director: Alfredo Montero
Main Cast: Marta Castellote, Xoel Fernández, Eva García-Vacas, Marcos Ortiz, Jorge López Páez

Overview: Group of Spanish friends on the tropical island holiday of a lifetime. Discovering a secret cave adds adventure. They enthusiastically go underground with their camera, but soon become hopelessly lost. Nail-biting tension in the tradition of recent Spanish horror films.
