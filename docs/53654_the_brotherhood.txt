Title: The Brotherhood (1968)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Martin Ritt
Main Cast: Kirk Douglas, Alex Cord, Irene Papas, Luther Adler, Susan Strasberg
Keywords: brother brother relationship, vietnam veteran, mafia

Overview: The son of a powerful Mafia don comes home from his army service in Vietnam and wants to lead his own life, but family tradition, intrigues and powerplays involving his older brother dictate otherwise, and he finds himself being slowly drawn back into that world.
