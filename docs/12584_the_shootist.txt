Title: The Shootist (1976)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Western
Director: Don Siegel
Main Cast: John Wayne, Lauren Bacall, Ron Howard, James Stewart, Richard Boone
Keywords: based on novel, sniper, widow, retirement

Overview: Afflicted with a terminal illness John Bernard Brooks, the last of the legendary gunfighters, quietly returns to Carson City for medical attention from his old friend Dr. Hostetler. Aware that his days are numbered, the troubled man seeks solace and peace in a boarding house run by a widow and her son.However, it is not Brooks' fate to die in peace, as he becomes embroiled in one last valiant battle.
