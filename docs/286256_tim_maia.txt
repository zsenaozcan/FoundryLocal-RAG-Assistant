Title: Tim Maia (2014)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Mauro Lima
Main Cast: Robson Nunes, Babú Santana, Alinne Moraes, Cauã Reymond, Valdineia Soriano
Keywords: brazilian, biography, music, drama, documentary, singer

Overview: Biopic of Brazilian singer Tim Maia, from his childhood in Rio de Janeiro until his death at age 55, including his passage by the US, where he discovers a new style of music and is arrested for theft and drug possession.
