Title: Suing The Devil (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Thriller
Director: Timothy A. Chey
Main Cast: Malcolm McDowell, Shannen Fields, Corbin Bernsen, Tom Sizemore, Bart Bronson

Overview: Luke O'Brien, a washed-up salesman turned night law student, decides to sue Satan for $8 trillion dollars. On the last day before Luke files a default judgment, Satan appears to defend himself. On Satan's legal team are 10 of the country's best trial lawyers. The entire world watches on Legal TV to see who will win the Trial of the Century
