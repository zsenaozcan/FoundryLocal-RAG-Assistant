Title: Army (1944)
Genres: Drama
Director: Keisuke Kinoshita
Main Cast: Chishu Ryu, Kinuyo Tanaka, Ken Mitsuda, Kazumasa Hoshino, Haruko Sugimura
Keywords: japanese, family, military, time of war

Overview: A widow raises her sickly son to be strong enough to join the army and fight on the front lines.
