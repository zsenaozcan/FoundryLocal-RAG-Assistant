Title: Fat People (2009)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Daniel Sánchez Arévalo
Main Cast: Verónica Sánchez, Antonio de la Torre, Roberto Enríquez, Raúl Arévalo, Leticia Herrero
Keywords: overweight man, overweight woman

Overview: "GORDOS" is a comedy about life's excesses and deficiencies; about our insecurities, phobias, obsessions, traumas, mistakes, fears, blame, desires, hopes, challenges, concessions, goals, relationships, love, sex, health, family... about survival in the widest and "largest" sense of the word.
