Title: Almost Blue (2000)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Alex Infascelli
Main Cast: Lorenza Indovina, Claudio Santamaria, Andrea Di Stefano, Rolando Ravello, Dario D'Ambrosi
Keywords: serial killer

Overview: A serial killer, called the Iguana, is terrorizing Bologna. He is able to change continuously identity. Grazia is investigating trying to find out the truth about the Iguana.
