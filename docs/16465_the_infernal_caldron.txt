Title: The Infernal Caldron (1903)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror
Director: Georges Méliès
Main Cast: Georges Méliès
Keywords: hell, silent film

Overview: The Infernal Boiling Pot is a 1903 silent French fantasy film directed by Georges Méliès. The film shows a green-skinned demon who places a woman and then two courtiers into a burning cauldron.
