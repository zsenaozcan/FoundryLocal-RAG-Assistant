Title: Nancy Drew (2002)
Genres: Mystery
Director: James Frawley
Main Cast: Maggie Lawson, Jill Ritchie, Lauren Birkell, Marieh Delfino
Keywords: based on novel

Overview: In this movie, Maggie Lawson plays Nancy Drew. A young woman who has just entered college. While rushing with her friends Bess and George, the star football player, Jesse, goes into a coma. Nancy can sense there is something more to the story, so she starts investigating. In only a short period of time she gets arrested, asked out by a cop, almost expelled and gets one of her friends expelled. But that doesn't stop her from finding out what happened to Jesse. Written by Kate Price!!!
