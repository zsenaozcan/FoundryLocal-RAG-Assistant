Title: The Warped Ones (1960)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Koreyoshi Kurahara
Main Cast: Tamio Kawaji, Noriko Matsumoto, Yuko Chishiro, Kôjirô Kusanagi, Hiroyuki Nagato
Keywords: striptease, street walker

Overview: A juvenile delinquent gets out of the pen and immediately embarks on a rampage of untethered anger, most of it directed at the girlfriend of the journalist who helped send him up.
