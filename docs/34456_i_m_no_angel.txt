Title: I'm No Angel (1933)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Wesley Ruggles
Main Cast: Mae West, Cary Grant, Gregory Ratoff, Edward Arnold, Ralf Harolde
Keywords: romantic comedy

Overview: The bold Tira works as dancing beauty and lion tamer at a fair. Out of an urgent need of money, she agrees to a risky new number: she'll put her head into a lion's muzzle! With this attraction the circus makes it to New York and Tira can persue her dearest occupation: flirting with rich men and accepting expensive presents.
