Title: The Video Dead (1987)
Genres: Horror
Director: Robert Scott
Main Cast: Roxanna Augesen, Rocky Duvall, Michael St. Michaels, Jennifer Miro, Libby Russler
Keywords: zombie

Overview: A supernatural TV is mistakenly delivered to a suburban family instead of a research lab. After the family mysteriously turn up dead, a new family moves in and finds the TV hidden in the basement. Soon they discover the TV is actually a gateway for the undead.
