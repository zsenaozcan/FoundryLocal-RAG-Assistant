Title: The New Godfathers (1979)
Genres: Crime
Director: Alfonso Brescia
Main Cast: Mario Merola, Antonio Sabàto, Gianni Garko, Jeff Blynn, Edmund Purdom
Keywords: mafia, drug

Overview: A revolution in Iran halts a heroin shipment, but an alliance of crime families is set on getting it to the US.  They decide to run the drug through an unsuspecting network of good-natured, local smugglers in Naples, while an international anti-narcotics agent rushes to shut their operation down.  Betrayals and divided loyalties lead to a  final, bloody confrontation in America.
