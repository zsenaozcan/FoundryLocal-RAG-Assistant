Title: Empire of the Sun (1987)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Steven Spielberg
Main Cast: Christian Bale, John Malkovich, Miranda Richardson, Nigel Havers, Ben Stiller
Keywords: japan, stadium, harmonica, bravery, peasant, pow, japanese occupation, sergeant, photograph, chinese

Overview: Jamie Graham, a privileged English boy, is living in Shanghai when the Japanese invade and force all foreigners into prison camps. Jamie is captured with an American sailor named Basie, who looks out for him while they are in the camp together. Even though he is separated from his parents and in a hostile environment, Jamie maintains his dignity and youthful spirits, providing a beacon of hope for the others held captive with him.
