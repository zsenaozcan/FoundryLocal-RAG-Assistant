Title: Two Way Stretch (1960)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Robert Day
Main Cast: Peter Sellers, Maurice Denham, Lionel Jeffries, David Lodge, Bernard Cribbins

Overview: Three criminals plan to break out of prison the day before their release in order to carry out a daring jewel robbery, intending to establish the perfect alibi by returning to jail afterwards. First however they must get out, a task made more difficult by a new, stricter prison officer.
