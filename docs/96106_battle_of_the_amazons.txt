Title: Battle of the Amazons (1973)
Genres: Fantasy, Action
Director: Alfonso Brescia
Main Cast: Lincoln Tate, Paola Tedesco, Mirta Miller, Benito Stefanelli, Genie Woods
Keywords: female nudity, warrior woman, amazon, amazon tribe

Overview: A tribe of vicious female warriors terrorizes the countryside, and especially the males, until one day the men and some local villagers decide to fight back.
