Title: Heavy Petting (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Marcel Sarmiento
Main Cast: Malin Åkerman, Brendan Hines, Kevin Sussman, Mike Doyle, Juan Carlos Hernández
Keywords: human animal relationship, independent film, dog, girl dog relationship

Overview: Charlie thinks he's met the perfect woman, but in order to be with her, he must first get past her dog. Just when he thinks all is lost, he realizes that he loves the dog too! Now he must do everything he can to keep them both.
