Title: Horace and Pete (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Louis C.K.
Main Cast: Louis C.K., Steve Buscemi, Edie Falco, Alan Alda, Jessica Lange
Keywords: new york, bar, family business 

Overview: Horace and Pete (stylized as Horace and Pete's Est. 1916) is an American comedy-drama web series created by Louis C.K. starring himself and Steve Buscemi as Horace and Pete, co-owners of an Irish bar, Horace and Pete's.
