Title: Monsterwolf (2010)
Genres: TV Movie, Fantasy, Horror
Director: Todor Chapkanov
Main Cast: Jason London, Robert Picardo, Leonor Varela, Nicole Barré, Amber Bartlett

Overview: A creature of ancient legend manifests, bound to protect the ecological balance of the land and killing anyone that threatens it. This elusive guardian is initially both feared and celebrated by the locals, but when a deadly curse affects them all, they must unite and recapture the monster wolf’s spirit or face their ultimate doom.
