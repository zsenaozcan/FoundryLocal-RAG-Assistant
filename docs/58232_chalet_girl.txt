Title: Chalet Girl (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Phil Traill
Main Cast: Felicity Jones, Ed Westwick, Brooke Shields, Bill Nighy, Tamsin Egerton
Keywords: competition, snowboarding, birthday party, chalet, snowboarding competition, skiing, engagement party

Overview: While working a job at an exclusive ski resort to support her Dad, Kim learns to snowboard and is so good at it that she enters a competition with a huge cash prize. She has to dig deep to overcome her fears, but her life gets more complicated through her spoken-for boss, Jonny.
