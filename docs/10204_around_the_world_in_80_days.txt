Title: Around the World in 80 Days (2004)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Frank Coraci
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Steve Coogan, Cécile de France, Robert Fyfe, Jim Broadbent
Keywords: paris, london england, new york, jules verne, san francisco, istanbul, hot air balloon, journey round the world
