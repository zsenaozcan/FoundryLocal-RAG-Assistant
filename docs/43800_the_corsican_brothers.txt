Title: The Corsican Brothers (1941)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Gregory Ratoff
Main Cast: Douglas Fairbanks Jr., Ruth Warrick, Akim Tamiroff, J. Carrol Naish, H.B. Warner
Keywords: siamese twins, sword fight, brother against brother, doctor, beautiful  woman, bandit, vendetta, carrier pigeon, antidote, twins separated at birth

Overview: The bloody hostility of two noble families on Corsica shapes the destiny of twins born conjoined, who were surgically separated as newborns and then raised apart. Upon reaching adulthood they learn of one another and join forces to avenge their parents' murders.
