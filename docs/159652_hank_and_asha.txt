Title: Hank and Asha (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: James E. Duff
Main Cast: Mahira Kakkar, Andrew Pastides, Brian Sloan, Anna Tydlitátová, Bianca Butti

Overview: An Indian student in Prague and a lonely New Yorker correspond online through video letters. A voyeuristic love story about aching for human connection in a hyper-connected world.
