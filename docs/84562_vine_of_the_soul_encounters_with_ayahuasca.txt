Title: Vine of the Soul: Encounters with Ayahuasca (2010)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Richard Meech
Main Cast: Guillermo Arévalo, Gabor Mate, Dennis J. McKenna, Kenneth Tupper
Keywords: canadian, ayahuasca, shaman, fish out of water, ceremony, healing process, amazon rainforest, shamanism

Overview: On a quest for emotional healing and spiritual awakening, a naturopathic doctor and an accountant join others in the Peruvian Amazon to drink a psychedelic brew called ayahuasca.
