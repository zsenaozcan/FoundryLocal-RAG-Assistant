Title: Buffalo '66 (1998)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Vincent Gallo
Main Cast: Vincent Gallo, Christina Ricci, Ben Gazzara, Mickey Rourke, Rosanna Arquette
Keywords: prison, gambling, compulsive gambling
