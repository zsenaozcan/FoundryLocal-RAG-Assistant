Title: Kołysanka (2010)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Juliusz Machulski
Main Cast: Robert Więckiewicz, Janusz Chabior, Krzysztof Kiersznowski, Jacek Koman, Ewa Ziętek

Overview: Lullaby is a menacing story full of humor and suspense from the master of the genre, Juliusz Machulski. Two policemen investigate mysterious disappearances in a small scenic town. People keep disappearing, but the investigation brings no results. The tension grows and step by step a dark mystery unravels.
