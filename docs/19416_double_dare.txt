Title: Double Dare (2004)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Amanda Micheli
Main Cast: Jeannie Epper, Zoë Bell, Quentin Tarantino
Keywords: stuntwoman, stuntperson, indiana jones, woman director

Overview: With being thrown off buildings an occupational hazard, professional stuntwomen Jeannie Epper and Zoë Bell (the alter egos of Wonder Woman and Xena, respectively) would seem well-equipped for any challenges Hollywood might dish out. But finding roles -- and respect -- in a male-dominated field can prove more harrowing than dodging punches.
