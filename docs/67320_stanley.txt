Title: Stanley (1972)
Genres: Horror
Director: William Grefe
Main Cast: Chris Robinson, Alex Rocco, Steve Alaimo, Susan Carroll, Mark Harris
Keywords: snake, revenge

Overview: Tim Ochopee, a shell-shocked Seminole Indian has just returned from a tour of Vietnam. He lives a peaceful life deep in the Everglades with his pet snake Stanley. Upon his return, he finds out his father has passed away. When he learns how he was killed, Tim lets Stanley and his brood loose on the people who've done him wrong, leading to a thrilling climax.
