Title: Kiss of the Spider Woman (1985)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hector Babenco
Main Cast: William Hurt, Raúl Juliá, Sônia Braga, José Lewgoy, Walter Breda
Keywords: gay, male nudity, prison, based on novel, homophobia, politics, war, homosexuality, torture, lgbt

Overview: Luis Molina and Valentin Arregui are cell mates in a South American prison. Luis, a homosexual, is found guilty of immoral behaviour and Valentin is a political prisoner. To escape reality Luis invents romantic movies, while Valentin tries to keep his mind on the situation he's in. During the time they spend together, the two men come to understand and respect one another.
