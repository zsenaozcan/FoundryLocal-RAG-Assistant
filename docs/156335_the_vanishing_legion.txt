Title: The Vanishing Legion (1931)
Genres: Action, Mystery
Director: Ford Beebe
Main Cast: Harry Carey, Edwina Booth, William Desmond, Frankie Darro, Philo McCullough
Keywords: secret identity, movie serial

Overview: A mysterious master criminal known as The Voice plots with his gang to sabotage the Milesburg Oil Company, but the rightful heir has a secret army of her own to protect her rights.
