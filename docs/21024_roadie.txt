Title: Roadie (1980)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Alan Rudolph
Main Cast: Meat Loaf, Art Carney, Alice Cooper, Kaki Hunter, Deborah Harry
Keywords: roadie, rock music

Overview: A young Texas good ol' boy has a knack with electronic equipment, and that talent gets him a job as a roadie with a raucous traveling rock-and-roll show.
