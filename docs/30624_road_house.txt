Title: Road House (1948)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Romance
Director: Jean Negulesco
Main Cast: Ida Lupino, Cornel Wilde, Celeste Holm, Richard Widmark, O.Z. Whitehead
Keywords: bowling, singer, night club, female singer

Overview: A night club owner becomes infatuated with a torch singer and frames his best friend/manager for embezzlement when the chanteuse falls in love with him.
