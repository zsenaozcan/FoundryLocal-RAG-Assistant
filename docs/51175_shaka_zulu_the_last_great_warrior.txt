Title: Shaka Zulu: The Last Great Warrior (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Joshua Sinclair
Main Cast: Henry Cele, Omar Sharif, Grace Jones, David Hasselhoff, Karen Allen

Overview: The saga of legendary African warrior Shaka Zulu comes to the screen in this continuation of the timeless tale from original miniseries creator Joshua Sinclair. From the moment he was born, Shaka Zulu was bound for greatness. When his homeland of Africa begins to fall prey to England's rapidly expanding empire, Shaka Zulu leads a bloody struggle to keep his people free. Jason Buchanan, Rovi
