Title: Jesse Stone: Benefit of the Doubt (2012)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Robert Harmon
Main Cast: Tom Selleck, Kathy Baker, Kohl Sudduth, Gloria Reuben, Stephen McHattie
Keywords: jesse stone

Overview: Jesse finds himself struggling to get his job back as the Paradise police chief, and he is forced to rely on his cop intuition to sort through a maze of misleading clues and hidden meanings as he attempts to solve a shocking and horrifying mob-related double homicide.
