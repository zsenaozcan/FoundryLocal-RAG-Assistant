Title: The Magnificent Ambersons (1942)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Orson Welles
Main Cast: Joseph Cotten, Dolores Costello, Anne Baxter, Tim Holt, Agnes Moorehead
Keywords: jealousy, small town, marriage proposal, love of one's life, only child, marriage crisis

Overview: The spoiled young heir to the decaying Amberson fortune comes between his widowed mother and the man she has always loved.
