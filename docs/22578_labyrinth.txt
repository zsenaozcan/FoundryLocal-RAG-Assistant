Title: Labyrinth (2003)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: René Manzor
Main Cast: Lambert Wilson, Sylvie Testud, Frédéric Diefenthal, Michel Duchaussoy, Edouard Montoute
Keywords: intellectual, murder, psychologist, patient, guilty, dice

Overview: Psychologist Dr. Brennac is asked by a colleague to help with the case of Claude, a patient on trial for multiple murders. Is Claude really guilty of the crimes, or is he a victim of his own mind?
