Title: Au Revoir les Enfants (1987)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Louis Malle
Main Cast: Gaspard Manesse, Raphael Fejtö, Francine Racette, Philippe Morier-Genoud, Stanislas Carré de Malberg
Keywords: france, resistance, parent child relationship, vichy-régime, german, deportation, jew persecution, auschwitz, jew, national socialism

Overview: A French boarding school run by priests seems to be a haven from World War II until a new student arrives. He becomes the roommate of top student in his class. Rivals at first, the roommates form a bond and share a secret.
