Title: The Glass Cell (1978)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Hans W. Geißendörfer
Main Cast: Brigitte Fossey, Helmut Griem, Dieter Laser, Günter Strack, Walter Kohut
Keywords: release from prison

Overview: A man is wrongfully imprisoned for five years. Once out, he hears about his wife's supposed adventures outside of their marriage and becomes increasingly jealous.
