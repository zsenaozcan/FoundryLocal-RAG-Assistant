Title: An Honest Liar (2014)
Genres: History, Comedy, Documentary
Director: Tyler Measom
Main Cast: James Randi, Adam Savage, Bill Nye, Uri Geller, Penn Jillette
Keywords: magic, fraud, biography, paranormal, psychic, skeptic

Overview: An Honest Liar tells the incredible story of the world-famous magician, escape artist, and world-renowned enemy of deception, James 'The Amazing' Randi. The film brings to life Randi's intricate investigations that publicly exposed psychics, faith healers, and con-artists with quasi-religious fervor. A master deceiver who came out of the closet at the age of 81, Randi created fictional characters, fake psychics, and even turned his partner of 25 years, Jose Alvarez, into a sham guru names Carlos.
