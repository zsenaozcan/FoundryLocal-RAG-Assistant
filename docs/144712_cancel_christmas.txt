Title: Cancel Christmas (2011)
Genres: Family, Fantasy
Director: John Bradshaw
Main Cast: Judd Nelson, Justin Landry, Natalie Brown, John Fleming, Sante Scaletta
Keywords: christmas

Overview: Santa Claus has a mission to convince three problematic guys of Riverside Academy to change their attitude, especially Adam, who after being confined to a wheelchair has become a bitter guy. The misconduct of the three is causing serious problems in the academy and the task of Santa is led back they on the right track.
