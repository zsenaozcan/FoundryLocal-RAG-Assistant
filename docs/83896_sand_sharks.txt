Title: Sand Sharks (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Mark Atkins
Main Cast: Corin Nemec, Brooke Hogan, Vanessa Evigan, Eric Scott Woods, Gina Holden
Keywords: beach, monster, killer shark

Overview: Just when you thought it was safe to go back to the beach, it appears that our large fishy friends have found a way to chomp on you when you're chilling out on the sand too!
