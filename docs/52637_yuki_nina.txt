Title: Yuki & Nina (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nobuhiro Suwa
Main Cast: Noë Sampy, Arielle Moutel, Tsuyu Shimizu, Hippolyte Girardot, Marilyne Canto
Keywords: child of divorce

Overview: When Yuki finds out that her parents are separating and she is moving to Japan with her mother, she and her best friend Nina devise ways to reunite the feuding adults.
