Title: Mindscape (2013)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Jorge Dorado
Main Cast: Mark Strong, Taissa Farmiga, Brian Cox, Saskia Reeves, Richard Dillane
Keywords: teenager

Overview: A man with the ability to enter peoples' memories takes on the case of a brilliant, troubled sixteen-year-old girl to determine whether she is a sociopath or a victim of trauma.
