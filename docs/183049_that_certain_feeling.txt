Title: That Certain Feeling (1956)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Melvin Frank
Main Cast: Bob Hope, Eva Marie Saint, George Sanders, Pearl Bailey, David Lewis

Overview: When Larry Larkin's comic strip needs some freshening up, he calls in ghost-writer Francis X. Dignan to help him with the strip. Things get complicated when Francis rekindles his love for his ex-wife, who happens to be Larkin's secretary and soon-to-be wife.
