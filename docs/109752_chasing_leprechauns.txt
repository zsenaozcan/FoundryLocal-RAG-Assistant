Title: Chasing Leprechauns (2013)
Genres: TV Movie, Comedy, Romance
Director: Kevin Conner
Main Cast: Adrian Pasdar, Amy Huberman, Sean Duggan, Frank O'Sullivan, Pascal Scott

Overview: Before an American company can build a smelting plant outside a small Irish village, they have to send their top troubleshooter to find a way around an antiquated law that protects the leprechauns supposedly living on the property.
