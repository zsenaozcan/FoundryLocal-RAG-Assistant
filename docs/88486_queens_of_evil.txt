Title: Queens Of Evil (1970)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror
Director: Tonino Cervi
Main Cast: Ida Galli, Ray Lovelock, Silvia Monti, Haydée Politoff, Gianni Santuccio
Keywords: claustrophobia, dark, deserted house

Overview: A young hippie kills a man, and seeks refuge at the lakeside house of three beautiful sisters, who seem to be hiding a dark secret.
