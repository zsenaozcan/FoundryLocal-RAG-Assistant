Title: The Burrowers (2008)
Genres: Action, Horror, Western
Director: J.T. Petty
Main Cast: Doug Hutchison, Clancy Brown, William Mapother, Sean Patrick Thomas, Karl Geary
Keywords: monster, underground, dakota, funeral, paralysis, buried alive, native american, crucifix, cavalry

Overview: It is 1879 in the Dakota Territories, a band of men who set out to find and recover a family of settlers that has mysteriously vanished from their home. Expecting the offenders to be a band of fierce natives, but they soon discover that the real enemy stalks them from below.
