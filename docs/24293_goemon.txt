Title: Goemon (2009)
Genres: Action, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Kazuaki Kiriya
Main Cast: Yosuke Eguchi, Ryoko Hirosue, Takao Osawa, Jun Kaname, Mikijiro Hira
Keywords: samurai, hero, katana, sword, ninja

Overview: Follow the infamous ninja bandit named Ishikawa Goemon (Yosuke Eguchi) in an epic story. One night while looting treasure from Nanban (Southern Barbarians), Goemon finds a mysterious box hidden inside a secret grave. A forgotten relic from the past containing untold powers.
