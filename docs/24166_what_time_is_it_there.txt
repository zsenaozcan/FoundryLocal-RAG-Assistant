Title: What Time Is It There? (2001)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Tsai Ming-liang
Main Cast: Lee Kang-Sheng, Chen Shiang-Chyi, Lu Yi-Ching, Tien Miao, Cecilia Yip

Overview: When a young street vendor with a grim home life meets a woman on her way to Paris, they forge an instant connection. He changes all the clocks in Taipei to French time.
