Title: Spiritual Kung Fu (1978)
Genres: Action
Director: Lo Wei
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, James Tien, Mo Man-Sau, Li Tong-Chun, Lee Kwan
Keywords: martial arts, temple, training, shaolin

Overview: Jackie Chan plays the part of the class clown in a shaolin temple whose deadliest secret is stolen. All is lost until Jackie's character discovers dancing blue ghosts with bright red hair who haunt the library.
