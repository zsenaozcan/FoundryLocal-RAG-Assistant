Title: RoboGeisha (2009)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Noboru Iguchi
Main Cast: Aya Kiguchi, Hitomi Hasebe, Takumi Saito, Tarô Shigaki, Etsuko Ikuta
Keywords: sex, cyborg, assassin, geisha, resurrection, female protagonist

Overview: Sisters Yoshie and Kikue are two Geisha who are abducted by a mysterious organization to be transformed into murderous cyborg assassins.
