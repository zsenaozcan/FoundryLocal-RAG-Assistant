Title: Mythica: The Darkspore (2015)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Anne K. Black
Main Cast: Melanie Stone, Kevin Sorbo, Adam Johnson, Jake Stormoen, Rocky Myers
Keywords: sword, magic, sorcery, necromancer, woman director

Overview: When Teela’s sister is murdered and a powerful relic stolen, Marek and her friends face a sinister new enemy – Kishkumen, a foreign mystic bent on reclaiming the Darkspore for his master Szorlok. Armed with twin maps, Marek and her team race Kishkumen and his horde through creature-infested lands, to a long abandoned underground city – all the while pursued by bounty hunters intent on returning Marek to slavery.
