Main Cast: Minami Takayama, Rei Sakuma, Kappei Yamaguchi, Keiko Toda, Mieko Nobusawa
Keywords: clock tower, witch, cat, bicycle accident, broom, female friendship, bakery, dog, baking, seaside town
