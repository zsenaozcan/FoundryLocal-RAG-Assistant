Title: Fresh Horses (1988)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: David Anspaugh
Main Cast: Molly Ringwald, Andrew McCarthy, Viggo Mortensen, Molly Hagan, Ben Stiller

Overview: A Cincinnati college student breaks off his engagement to his wealthy fiancée after he falls in love with a backwoods Kentucky girl he meets at a party. She says she's 20, but he finds out she's 16 and married to an abusive husband.
