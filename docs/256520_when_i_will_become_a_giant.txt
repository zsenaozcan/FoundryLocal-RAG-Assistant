Title: When I Will Become a Giant (1978)
Genres: Romance, Family
Director: Inna Tumanyan
Main Cast: Mikhail Efremov, Natalya Sezeman, Liya Akhedzhakova, Inna Ulyanova, Marina Shimanskaya

Overview: Inspired by Edmond Rostand's play Cyrano de Bergerac, 8th grader Petya Kopeykin tries to prove everyone that bravery and honor still exist in today's world.
