Title: The Halloween That Almost Wasn't (1979)
Genres: Horror, Comedy, Family
Director: Bruce Bilson
Main Cast: Judd Hirsch, Mariette Hartley, Henry Gibson, Jack Riley, Josip Elic
Keywords: witch, monster, halloween, dracula

Overview: Upset by rumors that Halloween may end, Dracula calls his mild-mannered monsters together and delivers an ultimatum - either regain their frightening image, or leave his Transylvania castle forever.
