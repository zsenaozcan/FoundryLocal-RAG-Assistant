Title: Barren Lives (1963)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Romance
Director: Nelson Pereira dos Santos
Main Cast: Átila Iório, Maria Ribeiro, Orlando Macedo, Joffre Soares, Gilvan Lima
Keywords: brazilian, brazil, hunger, socially deprived family, aridity, peasant, independent film, teacher, cinema novo

Overview: 'Vidas secas' tells in vivid images the almost documentary like story of a drover and his family in the northern interior states of Brazil during the drought. A family in the search of a new hope and destiny.
