Title: First a Girl (1935)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Victor Saville
Main Cast: Jessie Matthews, Sonnie Hale, Anna Lee, Griffith Jones, Alfred Drayton
Keywords: musical, cross dressing

Overview: First a Girl is a 1935 British comedy film directed by Victor Saville and starring Jessie Matthews. First a Girl was adapted from the 1933 German film Viktor und Viktoria written and directed by Reinhold Schünzel.
