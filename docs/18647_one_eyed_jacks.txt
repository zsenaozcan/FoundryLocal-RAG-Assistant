Title: One-Eyed Jacks (1961)
Genres: Western, Action
Director: Marlon Brando
Main Cast: Marlon Brando, Karl Malden, Katy Jurado, Pina Pellicer, Ben Johnson
Keywords: ambush, gold, stolen gold

Overview: Running from the law after a bank robbery in Mexico, Dad Longworth finds an opportunity to take the stolen gold and leave his partner Rio to be captured. Years later, Rio escapes from the prison where he has been since, and hunts down Dad for revenge. Dad is now a respectable sheriff in California, and has been living in fear of Rio's return.
