Title: One Kiss (2016)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Ivan Cotroneo
Main Cast: Rimau Grillo Ritzberger, Valentina Romani, Leonardo Pazzagli, Simonetta Solder, Giorgio Marchesi

Overview: Lorenzo, Blue and Antonio have a lot in common: they are sixteen, attending the same class in the same school in a small town in the northeast, each have a family that loves them. And all three, though for different reasons, have come to be isolated from other peers. Their new friendship helps them to resist, until the mechanical attraction and fear the judgment of others do not grasp them unprepared.
