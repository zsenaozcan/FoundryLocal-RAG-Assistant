Title: The Confession (2001)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Daniel Lind Lagerlöf
Main Cast: Magnus Krepper, Jonas Karlsson, Johanna Sällström, Ralph Carlsson, Michael Nyqvist

Overview: A couple from Estonia visits a town in Sweden. One of the locals visits them a few times until one night things get out of hand after a few drinks. From then on we see how the police investigates a double murder and what complexions arise during this investigation.
