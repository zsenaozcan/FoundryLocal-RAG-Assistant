Title: True Crime (1996)
Genres: Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Pat Verducci
Main Cast: Alicia Silverstone, Kevin Dillon, Bill Nunn, Tara Subkoff, Michael Bowen
Keywords: investigation, murder, suspense, serial killer, police corruption, female detective, murder investigation, child murder

Overview: Mary Giordano is a bright, intelligent student who goes to a catholic school. She also has a addiction to mystery novels and detective magazines (hence the title of the movie), which inspire her to do her own detective work. When she starts snooping around on the case of a murderer of teenage girls, it gets her in hot water with her mentor Detective Jerry Gunn. But it also starts a team up with police cadet Tony Campbell. The two work together to find the murderer. But the closer Mary gets to solving the murder, the more danger she puts herself in of being the next victim.
