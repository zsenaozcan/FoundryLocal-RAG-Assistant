Title: In the Mirror of Maya Deren (2002)
Genres: Documentary, Foreign
Director: Martina Kudlácek
Main Cast: Maya Deren, Stan Brakhage, Judith Malina, Alexander Hammid, Chao Li Chi
Keywords: feminism, documentary, independent film, woman director

Overview: Documentary about the life of avant-garde filmmaker Maya Deren, who led the independent film movement of the 1940s.
