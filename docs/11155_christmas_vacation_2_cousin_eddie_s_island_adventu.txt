Title: Christmas Vacation 2: Cousin Eddie's Island Adventure (2003)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Nick Marck
Main Cast: Randy Quaid, Miriam Flynn, Dana Barron, Jake Thomas, Fred Willard
Keywords: christmas

Overview: Though Eddie's fired right at Christmastime, his boss sends him and his family on a South Pacific vacation, hoping Eddie won't sue him after being bitten by a lab monkey. When the Tuttle family winds up trapped on a tropical island, however, Eddie manages to provide for everyone and prove himself a real man.
