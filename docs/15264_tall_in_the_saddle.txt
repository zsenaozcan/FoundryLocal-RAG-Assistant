Title: Tall in the Saddle (1944)
Genres: Western
Director: Edwin L. Marin
Main Cast: John Wayne, Ella Raines, Ward Bond, George 'Gabby' Hayes, Audrey Long
Keywords: poker, sheriff, saloon, ranch, inheritance, shot in the back, rancher, stagecoach, wild west, stagecoach driver

Overview: When Rocklin (John Wayne) arrives in a western town he finds that the rancher who hired him as a foreman has been murdered. He is out to solve the murder and thwart the scheming to take the ranch from its rightful owner.
