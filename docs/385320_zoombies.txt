Title: Zoombies (2016)
Genres: Science Fiction, Action, Horror
Director: Glenn Miller
Main Cast: Ione Butler, Andrew Asper, Lala Nestor, Kim Nielsen, Marcus Anderson
Keywords: zoo, undead, zombie, virus, animals gone wild

Overview: When a strange virus quickly spreads through a safari park and turns all the zoo animals undead, those left in the park must stop the creatures before they escape and zombify the whole city.
