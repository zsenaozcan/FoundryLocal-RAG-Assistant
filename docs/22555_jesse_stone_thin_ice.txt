Title: Jesse Stone: Thin Ice (2009)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Robert Harmon
Main Cast: Tom Selleck, Kathy Baker, Kohl Sudduth, Leslie Hope, Stephen McHattie
Keywords: based on novel, police chief, sequel, jesse stone

Overview: Jesse Stone and Captain Healy are shot during an unauthorized stake-out in Boston. Meanwhile, a cryptic letter sent from Paradise leads the mother of a kidnapped child to Stone. Though her son was declared dead, she hopes he will reopen the case.
