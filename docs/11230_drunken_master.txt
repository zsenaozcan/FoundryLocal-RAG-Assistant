Title: Drunken Master (1978)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Yuen Woo-ping
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Yuen Siu-Tin, Hwang Jang-Lee, Dean Shek, Lam Kau
Keywords: trainer, sake, uncle, dojo, master, kung fu classic, drunken master

Overview: Jackie Chan stars as Wong Fei-Hung, whose mischievous antics land him in hot water. Having tolerated enough of his son's mishaps, Fei-Hung's dad enlists his sadistic uncle, who specializes in drunken-style kung fu, to teach the lad some discipline. This Hong Kong martial-arts comedy helped establish the slapstick fighting style that would become Chan's trademark.
