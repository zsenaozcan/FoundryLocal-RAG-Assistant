Title: The Age of Stupid (2009)
Genres: War, Documentary, History
Director: Franny Armstrong
Main Cast: Pete Postlethwaite
Keywords: post-apocalyptic, global warming, woman director

Overview: The Age of Stupid is the new movie from Director Franny Armstrong (McLibel) and producer John Battsek (One Day In September). Pete Postlethwaite stars as a man living alone in the devastated future world of 2055, looking at old footage from 2008 and asking: why didn’t we stop climate change when we had the chance?
