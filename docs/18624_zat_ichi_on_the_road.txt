Title: Zatôichi on the Road (1963)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: Kimiyoshi Yasuda
Main Cast: Shintarô Katsu, Shiho Fujimura, Ryûzô Shimada, Reiko Fujiwara, Matasaburô Niwa

Overview: Zatoichi is sworn to protect the life of a young girl and without any real allies finds himself in the middle of a bloody turf war.
