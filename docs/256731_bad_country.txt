Title: Bad Country (2014)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime
Director: Chris Brinker
Main Cast: Matt Dillon, Willem Dafoe, Neal McDonough, Amy Smart, Chris Marquette
Keywords: police, baton rouge

Overview: When Baton Rouge police detective Bud Carter busts contract killer Jesse Weiland, he convinces Jesse to become an informant and rat out the South's most powerful crime ring.
