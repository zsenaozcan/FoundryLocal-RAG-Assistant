Title: Casual Encounters (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Zackary Adler
Main Cast: Brooklyn Decker, David Arquette, David Krumholtz, Taran Killam, Mark Boone Junior

Overview: When Justin's girlfriend of 5 years leaves him heartbroken and embarrassed after a public breakup, his trying to be helpful but somewhat misguided friends talk him into the strange world of on-line dating.
