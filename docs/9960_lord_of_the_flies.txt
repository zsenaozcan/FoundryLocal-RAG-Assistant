Title: Lord of the Flies (1963)
Genres: Thriller, Adventure, Drama
Director: Peter Brook
Main Cast: James Aubrey, Tom Chapin, Hugh Edwards, Roger Elwin, Tom Gaman
Keywords: child murderer, deserted island, dead children, south sea island, all male cast, flogging, survival skills, degeneration, adolescent boy, swine

Overview: Amidst a nuclear war, a plane carrying a group of schoolboys crash lands on a deserted island. With no adult survivors, the boys are forced to fend for themselves. At first they cooperate, but when the they split into two separate camps -- one led by the pragmatic Ralph and the other by militaristic Jack -- their society falls into disarray, leading to a disturbing examination of human nature and a chilling conclusion.
