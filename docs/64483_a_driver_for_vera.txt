Title: A Driver for Vera (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Pavel Chukhray
Main Cast: Igor Petrenko, Elena Babenko, Bogdan Stupka, Andrei Panin, Marina Golub

Overview: The film is set during 1962 in Sevastopol, Crimea, then a secret Navy Base in the Soviet Union. General Serov hires Viktor, a cadet from the Kremlin Guard to work as his private chauffeur. In a jet-black "ZIM" limo, Viktor is chauffeuring the General's disabled daughter Vera. Viktor is oblivious to the hidden agenda of the KGB agent Saveliev, who manipulates everyone behind the scenes in the old rivalry between the Army and KGB.
