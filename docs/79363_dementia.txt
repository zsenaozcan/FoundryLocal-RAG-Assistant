Title: Dementia (1955)
Genres: Crime, Horror
Director: John Parker
Main Cast: Adrienne Barrett, Ben Roseman, Bruno VeSota, Ed Hinkle, Richard Barron
Keywords: female killer, cult film, film noir

Overview: Shot entirely without dialogue and filled with suggestive violence and psycho-sexual imagery, it's like a skid row expressionist thriller following the nocturnal prowling of a young woman haunted by homicidal guilt.
