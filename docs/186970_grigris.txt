Title: Grigris (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mahamat-Saleh Haroun
Main Cast: Souleymane Démé, Anaïs Monory, Cyril Gueï, Marius Yelolo, Hadje Fatime N'Goua
Keywords: africa, petrol exploitation, republic of chad, paralyzed

Overview: A 25 year old young man with dreams of becoming a dancer despite the fact that he's paralyzed from the waist down. His dreams are shattered when his uncle falls seriously ill. To save him, he decides to go work for petrol traffickers.
