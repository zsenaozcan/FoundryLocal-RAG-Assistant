Title: It's Alive III: Island of the Alive (1987)
Genres: Horror
Director: Larry Cohen
Main Cast: Michael Moriarty, Karen Black, Laurene Landon, James Dixon, Gerrit Graham
Keywords: mutant, new baby, mutant baby

Overview: The mutant babies have been placed by court order on a deserted island. Appalled by the cynicism and exploitation of the children by the legal system and the media, the man responsible for them leads an expedition to the island to free them.
