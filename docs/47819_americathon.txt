Title: Americathon (1979)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Neal Israel
Main Cast: John Ritter, Harvey Korman, Fred Willard, Chief Dan George, Richard Schaal
Keywords: government, near future, telethon

Overview: In a story told in narrative flashbacks, a young TV consultant is hired by the President of a bankrupt USA to organize a telethon in order to prevent the country from being repossessed by wealthy Native Americans.
