Title: King of the Roaring 20's: The Story of Arnold Rothstein (1961)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Joseph M. Newman
Main Cast: David Janssen, Dianne Foster, Jack Carson, Diana Dors, Dan O'Herlihy
Keywords: biography, machine gun

Overview: Gambler Arnold Rothstein (David Janssen) marries an actress (Dianne Foster), avenges his buddy (Mickey Rooney) and meets an underworld fate.
