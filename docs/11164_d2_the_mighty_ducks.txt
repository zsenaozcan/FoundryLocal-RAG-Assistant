Title: D2: The Mighty Ducks (1994)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Sam Weisman
Main Cast: Emilio Estevez, Kathryn Erbe, Michael Tucker, Jan Rubes, Carsten Norgaard
Keywords: competition, adversary, sport, ice hockey, hockey player, hockey coach, youth

Overview: After Gordon Bombay's hockey comeback is cut short he is named coach of Team USA Hockey for the Junior Goodwill Games. Bombay reunites the Mighty Ducks and introduces a few new players, however, he finds himself distracted by his newfound fame and must regather if the Ducks are to defeat tournament favourites Iceland.
