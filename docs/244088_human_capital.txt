Title: Human Capital (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Paolo Virzì
Main Cast: Fabrizio Bentivoglio, Valeria Bruni Tedeschi, Fabrizio Gifuni, Valeria Golino, Luigi Lo Cascio

Overview: The destinies of two families are irrevocably tied together after a cyclist is hit off the road by a jeep in the night before Christmas Eve.
