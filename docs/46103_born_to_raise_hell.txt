Title: Born to Raise Hell (2010)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Lauro Chartrand
Main Cast: Steven Seagal, Darren Shahlavi, D. Neil Mark, Dan Badarau, Claudiu Bleont

Overview: A hard core Interpol Agent is assigned to an Eastern European task force to target gun trafficking and dope running throughout the Balkans. While investigating a Russian gun dealer, his team is caught in a bloody street war between a Gypsy gang and the Russians, leaving one task force member dead. Fueled with vengeance, he leads us on an action packed thrill ride while avenging his friend's death.
