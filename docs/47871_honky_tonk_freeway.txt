Title: Honky Tonk Freeway (1981)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: John Schlesinger
Main Cast: David Rasche, Paul Jabara, Howard Hesseman, Teri Garr, Peter Billingsley

Overview: Ticlaw, a small town in Florida, has only one attraction: a safari park. The government constructs a freeway that passes near Ticlaw, but decides not to put an exit into the town. The people of Ticlaw, leaded by it's Mayor, will do anything in order to convince the governor to alter the project.
