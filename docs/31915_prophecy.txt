Title: Prophecy (1979)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: John Frankenheimer
Main Cast: Talia Shire, Robert Foxworth, Armand Assante, Richard Dysart, Victoria Racimo
Keywords: monster, mutant, toxic, native american, environmental, animal horror

Overview: A Savage beast, grown to monstrous size and driven mad by toxic wastes that are poisoning the waters, spreads terror and death on a Maine countryside.
