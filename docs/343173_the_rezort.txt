Title: The ReZort (2016)
Genres: Horror
Director: Steve Barker
Main Cast: Dougray Scott, Jessica De Gouw, Martin McCann, Elen Rhys, Jassa Ahluwalia
Keywords: resort, zombie, zombie apocalypse

Overview: The ReZort, a post apocalyptic safari, offers paying guests the opportunity to kill zombies in the wake of an outbreak.
