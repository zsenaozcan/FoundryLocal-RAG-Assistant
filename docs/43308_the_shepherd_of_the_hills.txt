Title: The Shepherd of the Hills (1941)
Genres: Western, Drama, Romance
Director: Henry Hathaway
Main Cast: John Wayne, Betty Field, Harry Carey, Beulah Bondi, James Barton
Keywords: fight, hate, moonshine

Overview: Young Matt Masters, an Ozark Mountains moonshiner, hates the father he has never seen, who apparently deserted Matt's mother and left her to die. His obsession contributes to the hatred rampant in the mountains. However, the arrival of a stranger, Daniel Howitt, begins to positively affect the mountain people, who learn to shed their hatred under his gentle influence
