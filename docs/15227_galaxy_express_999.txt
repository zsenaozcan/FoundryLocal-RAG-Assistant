Title: Galaxy Express 999 (1979)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Rintaro
Main Cast: Masako Nozawa, Masako Ikeda, Yôko Asagami, Miyoko Asô, Toshiko Fujita
Keywords: cyborg, space travel, revenge, space, transhumanism, train

Overview: In the future, one can achieve immortality by obtaining a mechanized body. Orphaned, young Tetsuro hitches a ride on the space train Galaxy Express 999 in the hope of obtaining a cyborg body to avenge his mother's death. Along the way, he meets Matael, who is the spitting image of his dead mother.
