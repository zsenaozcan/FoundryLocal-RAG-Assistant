Title: The Prime of Miss Jean Brodie (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ronald Neame
Main Cast: Maggie Smith, Robert Stephens, Pamela Franklin, Celia Johnson, Gordon Jackson
Keywords: edinburgh, eccentric, teacher, school

Overview: A headstrong young teacher in a private school in 1930s Edinburgh ignores the curriculum and influences her impressionable 12 year old charges with her over-romanticized world view.
