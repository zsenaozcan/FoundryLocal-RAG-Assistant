Title: The Westerner (1940)
Genres: Action, Western, Romance
Director: William Wyler
Main Cast: Gary Cooper, Walter Brennan, Doris Davenport, Fred Stone, Forrest Tucker
Keywords: horse thief

Overview: Drifter Cole Harden is accused of stealing a horse and faces hanging by self-appointed Judge Roy Bean, but Harden manages to talk his way out of it by claiming to be a friend of stage star Lillie Langtry, with whom the judge is obsessed, even though he has never met her. Tensions rise when Harden comes to the defense of a group of struggling homesteaders who Judge Bean is trying to drive away.
