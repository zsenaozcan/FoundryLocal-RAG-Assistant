Title: Greaser's Palace (1972)
Genres: Western, Comedy
Director: Robert Downey Sr.
Main Cast: Allan Arbus, Albert Henderson, Luana Anders, Michael Sullivan, Hervé Villechaize
Keywords: surrealism, wild west, christ allegory

Overview: A parable based on the life of Christ. This ain't your father's Bible story, full of references about the destruction of the world through massive constipation and a New Mexican setting.
