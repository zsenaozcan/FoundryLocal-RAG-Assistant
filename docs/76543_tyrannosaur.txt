Title: Tyrannosaur (2011)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Paddy Considine
Main Cast: Peter Mullan, Olivia Colman, Eddie Marsan, Paul Popplewell, Samuel Bottomley
Keywords: pub, urination

Overview: The story of Joseph a man plagued by violence and a rage that is driving him to self-destruction. As Joseph's life spirals into turmoil a chance of redemption appears in the form of Hannah, a Christian charity shop worker. Their relationship develops to reveal that Hannah is hiding a secret of her own with devastating results on both of their lives.
