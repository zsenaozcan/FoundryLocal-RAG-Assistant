Title: Woman of the Lake (1966)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yoshishige Yoshida
Main Cast: Mariko Okada, Shigeru Tsuyuguchi, Tamotsu Hayakawa, Keiko Natsu, Hiroko Masuda
Keywords: adultery, based on novel, blackmail

Overview: A married woman lets her lover take naked pictures of her. The photos end up in possession of a man who starts blackmailing the couple.
