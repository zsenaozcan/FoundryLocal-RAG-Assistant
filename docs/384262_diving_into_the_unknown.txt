Title: Diving Into the Unknown (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Juan Reina
Main Cast: Patrik Grönqvist, Kai Känkänen, Sami Paakkarinen, Vesa Rantanen
Keywords: rescue mission, death, accident, underwater cave, diving accident

Overview: After a terrible accident deep inside an underwater cave, the survivors are forced to risk their own lives to bring the bodies of their friends home.
