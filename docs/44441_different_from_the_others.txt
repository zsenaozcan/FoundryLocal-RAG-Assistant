Title: Different from the Others (1919)
Genres: Drama
Director: Richard Oswald
Main Cast: Conrad Veidt, Anita Berber, Magnus Hirschfeld, Reinhold Schünzel, Fritz Schulz
Keywords: homosexuality, docudrama, lgbt

Overview: Conrad Veidt plays a famous musician who is blackmailed for being gay. Eventually he stands trial and is convicted. At the end the film pleads for the abolition of §175 (the paragraph which punishes homosexuality).
