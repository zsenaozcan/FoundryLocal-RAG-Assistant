Title: Wasted Youth (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Argyris Papadimitropoulos
Main Cast: Haris Markou, Ieronimos Kaletsanos, Arthouros Kiviliov, Jason Wastor, Syllas Tzoumerkas
Keywords: youth, unemployment, financial crisis

Overview: Harris, a 16 year old boy spends his day skateboarding aimlessly around Athens with his friends. Vassilis, a middle aged cop, is frustrated with his dead-end job and crammed in his small apartment with his family.
