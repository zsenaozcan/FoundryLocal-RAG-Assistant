Title: Mike's Murder (1984)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: James Bridges
Main Cast: Debra Winger, Mark Keyloun, Darrell Larson, Brooke Alderson, Paul Winfield
Keywords: tennis, investigation, coach, murder, los angeles, drug, neo-noir

Overview: Betty has a crush on her tennis coach Mike. He keeps on promising to call, but never does – she doesn’t know that he’s a little dealer. After a failed deal in someone else’s district he has to disappear for three months. He contacts Betty again, but she waits in vain – he’s killed before they meet. Now she tries to find out what happened, asks around among his friends. This leads her right into the drug scenery.
