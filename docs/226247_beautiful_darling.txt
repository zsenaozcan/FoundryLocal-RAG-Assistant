Title: Beautiful Darling (2010)
Genres: Documentary
Director: James Rasin
Main Cast: Candy Darling, Andy Warhol, Fran Lebowitz, Paul Morrissey, Julie Newmar
Keywords: andy warhol, the factory, candy darling

Overview: James Rasin's documentary “Beautiful Darling” honors American Transgender actress and best-known Warhol Superstar, Candy Darling, and her all-too-brief life and career, with a combination of current and vintage interview material, rarely seen archival photos and footage, and extracts from Darling's movies.
