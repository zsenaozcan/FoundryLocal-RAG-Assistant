Title: Dreamland (2007)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror, Mystery
Director: James P. Lay
Main Cast: Jackie Kreisler, Shane Elliott, Mark Bernier, Jonathan Breck, Kyle Saylors
Keywords: independent film

Overview: In the Nevada mountains between Las Vegas and Reno in the desolate nuclear testing grounds of Dreamland (Area 51), a young couple Megan (Jackie Kreisler) and Dylan (Shane Elliott) stop in a greasy spoon cafe where they learn about the Area 51 government base a few miles away. After they get back on the road, Dylan turns on the radio. The only broadcast he can find is a speech from Adolf Hitler at the 1936 Olympic Games. The car dies and a visitor appears from another moment in time. When Megan and Dylan look closely they realise that it is Hitler from the past.
