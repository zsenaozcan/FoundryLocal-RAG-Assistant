Title: Behind Office Doors (1931)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Melville W. Brown
Main Cast: Mary Astor, Robert Ames, Ricardo Cortez, Catherine Dale Owen, Kitty Kelly
Keywords: hotel, secretary

Overview: Mary Linden is the secretary who is the unheralded power behind successful executive James Duneen. He takes her for granted until rival Wales tries to take her away from him.
