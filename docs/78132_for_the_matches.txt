Title: For the Matches (1980)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Risto Orko
Main Cast: Evgeni Leonov, Vyacheslav Nevinnyy, Georgiy Vitsin, Galina Polskikh, Sergei Filippov

Overview: In Eastern Finland, in Liperi to be exact, Anna-Liisa Ihalainen asks her husband Antti to get some matches from the Ihalainen household. Unfortunately that trip takes a little longer than expected...
