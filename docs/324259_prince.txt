Title: Prince (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Sam de Jong
Main Cast: Ayoub Elasri, Oussama Addi, Achraf Meziani, Elsie de Brauw, Sigrid ten Napel
Keywords: youth gang, street life

Overview: A troubled teenager attempts to conquer the girl he's in love with, who seems out of reach. He's lured into criminal activities and becomes alienated from family and friends.
