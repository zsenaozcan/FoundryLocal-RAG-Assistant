Title: The Client List (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Eric Laneuville
Main Cast: Jennifer Love Hewitt, Teddy Sears, Sonja Bennett, Lynda Boyd, Chelah Horsdal
Keywords: prostitution, based on true events, inspired by a true story
