Title: Armitage: Dual Matrix (2002)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Animation, Science Fiction, Adventure
Director: Katsuhito Akiyama
Main Cast: Ryōka Yuzuki, Hikaru Hanada, Yuka Imai, Mitsuki Saiga, Jouji Nakata
Keywords: sequel, cyberpunk, robot, violence, toddler

Overview: Naomi Armitage and Ross Sylibus have changed their names and live with their daughter Yoko as a happy and normal family on Mars — until an android riot breaks out at an anti-matter plant on Earth.
