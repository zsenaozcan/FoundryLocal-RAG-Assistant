Title: Sweet Home (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: Rafa Martínez
Main Cast: Ingrid García Jonsson, Bruno Sevilla, José María Blanco, Eduardo Lloveras, Luka Peroš
Keywords: murder, tenant, apartment building

Overview: A couple decides to spend a romantic evening in a floor of a semi-abandoned building that slip because she works as a consultant for the council house and got the keys. During the evening they discover that a hooded murderer is the only tenant left in the building...and they have become the new target.
