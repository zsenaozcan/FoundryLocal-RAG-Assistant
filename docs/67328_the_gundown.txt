Title: The Gundown (2011)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Dustin Rikert
Main Cast: Andrew W. Walker, Peter Coyote, Veronica Milagros, Sheree J. Wilson, Tyler Kain

Overview: Seeking revenge and justice, Cole Brandt finds himself in the lawless town of Dead River where he is faced with one last bloody showdown for freedom in order to protect The Majestic Saloon and a beautiful woman.
