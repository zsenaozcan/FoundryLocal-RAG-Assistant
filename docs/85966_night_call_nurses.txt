Title: Night Call Nurses (1972)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jonathan Kaplan
Main Cast: Patty Byrne, Alana Stewart, Mittie Lawrence, Clint Kimbrough, Felton Perry
Keywords: nurse, exploitation film

Overview: The sweet and perky Barbara, the sunny Janis, and the responsible Sandra are a trio of young and attractive nurses who work in the psych ward at a hospital. The threesome really have their hands full dealing with nutty patients, creepy stalkers, and black revolutionaries
