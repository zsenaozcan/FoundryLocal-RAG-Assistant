Title: ABCD (Any Body Can Dance) (2013)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Remo D'Souza
Main Cast: Prabhu Deva, Ganesh Acharya, Kay Kay Menon, Lauren Gottlieb, Salman Yusuff Khan
Keywords: underdog, competition, dance, musical, drama

Overview: When a capable dancer is provoked by the evil design of his employer, naturally he will be out to prove his mettle.
