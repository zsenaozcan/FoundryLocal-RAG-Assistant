Title: Birds, Orphans and Fools (1969)
Genres: War, Comedy, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Juraj Jakubisko
Main Cast: Philippe Avron, Jirí Sýkora, Magda Vášáryová , Míla Beran, Augustín Kubán

Overview: In the aftermath of war, two men and a woman begin acting more like children than adults, leading to tragedy.
