Title: Stone (2012)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Vyacheslav Kaminsky
Main Cast: Sergey Svetlakov, Olesya Sudzilovskaya, Nikolai Kozak, Yelena Koreneva, Valda Bičkutė

Overview: When a 7-year-old son of the well-known businessman is kidnapped, he has no idea what awaits him! It turns out that the mysterious kidnapper does not need any ransom, but has only one claim: during the next day father must decide who will die of the two: his child or himself...
