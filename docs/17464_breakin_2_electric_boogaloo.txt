Title: Breakin' 2: Electric Boogaloo (1984)
Genres: Drama, Music

Main Cast: Lucinda Dickey, Adolfo Quinones, Michael Chambers, Harry Caesar, Susie Coelho
Keywords: breakdance, sequel
