Title: Diverted (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alex Chapple
Main Cast: Shawn Ashmore, David Suchet, Joanne Whalley, Colin Buchanan, Anita Majumdar

Overview: On September 11th, 2001, 38 planes headed to New York City were diverted to Gander, Newfoundland, Canada. A town of 9,000 took in 7,000 passengers for 4 days until American airspace reopened.
