Title: Alter Ego (2007)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Nicholas Dimitropoulos
Main Cast: Sakis Rouvas, Danai Skiadi, Alexandros Logothetis, Doretta Papadimitriou

Overview: Alter Ego: a successful Greek rock group, with a potential that surpasses the limits of the local music industry and hundreds of thousands of fans getting wild at every concert and public appearance of theirs. Fame, success, money, in a world of glamor and vanity. But what's hidden behind the mirror? How do the members of Alter Ego experience their success, their day to day life, their dreams?
