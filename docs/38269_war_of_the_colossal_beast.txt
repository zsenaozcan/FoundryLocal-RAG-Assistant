Title: War of the Colossal Beast (1958)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror
Director: Bert I. Gordon
Main Cast: Duncan 'Dean' Parkin, Sally Fraser, Roger Pace, Russ Bender, Rico Alaniz
Keywords: mutant, giant man

Overview: Glenn Manning, "The Amazing Colosasal Man," believed dead after falling from the Hoover Dam, reemerges in rural Mexico, brain damaged, disfigured, and very angry.
