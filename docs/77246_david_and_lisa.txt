Title: David and Lisa (1998)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Romance
Director: Lloyd Kramer
Main Cast: Brittany Murphy, Lukas Haas, Sidney Poitier, Debi Mazar, Allison Janney

Overview: It was adapted as a stage play in 1967, and was remade as a television movie in 1998 starring Lukas Haas, Sidney Poitier and Brittany Murphy.
