Title: The Little Giant (1933)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Thriller, Crime, Romance
Director: Roy Del Ruth
Main Cast: Edward G. Robinson, Mary Astor, Helen Vinson, Russell Hopton, Kenneth Thomson
Keywords: prohibition, bootlegger, bootlegging, mansion, gangster, class differences, millionaire, socialite, real estate agent, swindle

Overview: Prohibition is ending so bootlegger Bugs Ahearn decides to crack California society. He leases a house from down-on-her-luck Ruth and hires her as social secretary. He rescues Polly Cass from a horsefall and goes home to meet her dad who sells him some phony stock certificates. When he learns about this he sends to Chicago for mob help.
