Title: Trivial (2007)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Sophie Marceau
Main Cast: Sophie Marceau, Christopher Lambert, Nicolas Briançon, Simon Abkarian, Robert Hossein
Keywords: suppressed past, normandy, spirit, woman director

Overview: An inspector who just suffered a family tragedy is looking for a missing older man. Man's family is of no help, or are they hiding something? He is helped by a ghost of an actress who died 30 years ago, or is he slowly going insane?
