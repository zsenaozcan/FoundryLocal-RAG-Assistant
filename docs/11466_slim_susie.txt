Title: Slim Susie (2003)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ulf Malmros
Main Cast: Tuva Novotny, Jonas Rimeika, Björn Starrin, Kjell Bergqvist, Lotta Tejle
Keywords: brother sister relationship, sweden, success, autonomy, rural setting

Overview: When Erik, a Stockholm urbanite, learns that his beauty-queen sister, Susie, is missing, he goes to their country roots to look for her. But after talking to the eccentric locals -- including a shy video store clerk and a corrupt police officer -- Erik finds a woman who is not at all like the girl he left behind. Award-winning director Ulf Malmros helms this black comedy infused with hipster flair.
