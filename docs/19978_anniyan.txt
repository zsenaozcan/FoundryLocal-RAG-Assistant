Title: Anniyan (2005)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: S. Shankar
Main Cast: Vikram, Sadha, Vivek, Nassar, Prakash Raj
Keywords: vigilante, romantic comedy, lawyer, split personality, multiple personality

Overview: Ambi, a meek lawyer, ends up being a vigilante named Anniyan, and a supermodel named Remo, after suffering with multiple personality disorder due to the society.
