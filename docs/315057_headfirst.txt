Title: Headfirst (2014)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Antti Heikki Pesonen
Main Cast: Armi Toivanen, Eero Ritala, Mimosa Willamo, Kai Lehtinen

Overview: Headfirst is a black comedy about a single mother with anger management problems and her teenage daughter who is always in trouble at school. With yet another school comes a new teacher and a blast from the past for the mother. Before long, an accidental pickpocket and a notoriously lousy storyteller teams up with this one dysfunctional sort of a family. Nothing is the same anymore. Headfirst is a story about missing teeth, strange friends, hidden dreams and long-gone love. And about a hare.
