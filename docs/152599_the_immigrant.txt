Title: The Immigrant (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: James Gray
Main Cast: Marion Cotillard, Jeremy Renner, Joaquin Phoenix, Dagmara Domińczyk, Gabriel Rush
Keywords: new york, corruption, sister sister relationship, magic, immigrant, nudity, nightmare, ship, quarantine, love

Overview: An innocent immigrant woman is tricked into a life of burlesque and vaudeville until a dazzling magician tries to save her and reunite her with her sister who is being held in the confines of Ellis Island.
