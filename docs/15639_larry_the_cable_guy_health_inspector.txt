Title: Larry the Cable Guy: Health Inspector (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Trent Cooper
Main Cast: Larry the Cable Guy, Iris Bahr, Tony Hale, David Koechner, Brooke Dillman
Keywords: health inspector, food intoxication

Overview: A slovenly cable repairman becomes a big-city health inspector and is tasked with uncovering the source of a food poisoning epidemic.
