Title: Once Upon a Time in China II (1992)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Tsui Hark
Main Cast: Jet Li, Rosamund Kwan, Max Mok Siu-Chung, Donnie Yen, Jackie Chan
Keywords: china, martial arts, kung fu, congress, colonisation, martial arts master, sect, kung fu master

Overview: In the sequel to the Tsui Hark classic, Wong Fei-Hung faces The White Lotus society, a fanatical cult seeking to drive the Europeans out of China through violence, even attacking Chinese who follow Western ways. Wong must also defend Dr. Sun Yat Sen, a revolutionary, from the military.
