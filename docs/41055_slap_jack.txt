Title: Slap-Jack (1992)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Péter Tímár
Main Cast: Vera Pap, Gáspár Sándor, Károly Eperjes, Enikő Eszenyi, Koltai Róbert

Overview: The story happens in 1989 in the suburbs of Budapest, where the neighbor maintains an underground worker's guard training base, while the youngsters fall victim to a mistakenly posted letter.
