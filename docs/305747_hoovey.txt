Title: Hoovey (2015)
Genres: Family, Drama
Director: Sean McNamara
Main Cast: Patrick Warburton, Lauren Holly, Cody Linley, Alyson Stoner, Charles Robinson

Overview: HOOVEY is the true and inspiring story of the Elliott family, who refused to surrender, instead turning to one another and their faith in the worst of circumstances, to rebuild their lives, their boy, and their dreams... together. Step by grueling step, with faith, love, and the tireless work of a mother and father to save their family, their farm, their dream, Hoovey learns to walk again. To read again. To dribble and shoot a basketball again. And yes, to dream again himself. Then stepping out in his faith, back onto the hard court, to live that dream of playing this game once more...
