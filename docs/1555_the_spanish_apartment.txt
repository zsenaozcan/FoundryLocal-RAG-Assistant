Title: The Spanish Apartment (2002)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Cédric Klapisch
Main Cast: Romain Duris, Audrey Tautou, Kelly Reilly, Cécile de France, Cristina Brondo
Keywords: paris, barcelona spain, alcohol, roommate, single, travel, crush, student, relationship, break-up

Overview: A strait-laced French student moves into an apartment in Barcelona with a cast of six other characters from all over Europe. Together, they speak the international language of love and friendship.
