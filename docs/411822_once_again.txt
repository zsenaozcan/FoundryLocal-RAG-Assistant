Title: Once Again (2016)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Adoor Gopalakrishnan
Main Cast: Dileep, Kavya Madhavan, Subodh Bhave, Nedumudi Venu, Srinda Arhaan
Keywords: teacher, family, jobless, malayalam

Overview: After years spent living off the modest wealth of his in-laws, a man hatches a desperate plan that draws his respectable middle-class family into a vortex of crime, in this first feature in eight years from south Indian master Adoor Gopalakrishnan.
