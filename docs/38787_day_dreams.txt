Title: Day Dreams (1922)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Buster Keaton
Main Cast: Buster Keaton, Renée Adorée, Joe Roberts, Joe Keaton, Edward F. Cline

Overview: In order to impress the father of a girl he is keen on, Buster goes to the city in search of work. In his letters home he writes of his various jobs which her imagination expands into much nobler ones than those that he is actually attempting.
