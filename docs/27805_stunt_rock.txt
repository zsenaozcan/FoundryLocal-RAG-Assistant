Title: Stunt Rock (1978)
Genres: Action, Drama, Music
Director: Brian Trenchard-Smith
Main Cast: Grant Page, Monique van de Ven, Margaret Gerard, Paul Haynes, Curtis Hyde
Keywords: stuntman, rock band, daredevil

Overview: Australian stuntman Grant Page goes to Los Angeles to work on a television series. He uses his spare time to lend his expertise to rock band Sorcer. Page helps the band develop pyrotechnic magic tricks for their shows, and also recounts to his own exploits as a stuntman and daredevil as well as various stunts by other greats.
