Title: The Notorious Landlady (1962)
Genres: Comedy, Mystery, Romance, Thriller
Director: Richard Quine
Main Cast: Kim Novak, Jack Lemmon, Fred Astaire, Lionel Jeffries, Estelle Winwood
Keywords: attempted murder, diplomat, landlady

Overview: An American junior diplomat in London rents a house from, and falls in love with, a woman suspected of murder.
