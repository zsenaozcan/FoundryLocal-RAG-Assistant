Title: Homeboy (1988)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Seresin
Main Cast: Mickey Rourke, Christopher Walken, Jon Polito, Debra Feuer, Thomas Quinn
Keywords: transporter, boxer

Overview: Johnny Walker is a cowboy and a boxer. He is very shy and a bit of a fool. He is in love with Ruby, but he cannot tell her. He is also a bit old to keep on boxing, but its the only thing he does well.
