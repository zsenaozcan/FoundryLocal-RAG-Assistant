Title: The Girl Next Door (1999)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Christine Fugate
Main Cast: Stacy Valentine, Jack Gallagher, Veronica Hart, Julian, Ron Jeremy
Keywords: porno star, behind the scenes, porn industry, girl next door

Overview: In 1995, at the urging of her then-husband, an Oklahoma housewife submitted a topless photo of herself to a men's magazine, thus launching her phenomenal career as porn star Stacy Valentine. In this eye-opening and refreshingly even-handed film, Valentine candidly discusses the inner-workings and occupational hazards of the adult entertainment world, touching on the glamour -- and frequent emptiness -- of life in "the business."
