Title: C.S.A.: The Confederate States of America (2004)
Genres: War, Comedy, Drama
Director: Kevin Willmott
Main Cast: Greg Kirsch, Rupert Pate, Ryan L. Carroll, Brian Paulette, Larry Peterson
Keywords: civil war, slavery, mockumentary, racism, confederate, american civil war

Overview: Through the eyes of a British "documentary", this film takes a satirically humorous, and sometimes frightening, look at the history of an America where the South won the Civil War.
