Title: Bull Durham (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Ron Shelton
Main Cast: Kevin Costner, Susan Sarandon, Tim Robbins, Trey Wilson, Robert Wuhl
Keywords: love triangle, poem, jealousy, age difference, baseball, trainer, sports team, pitcher, stadium, flirt

Overview: Veteran catcher Crash Davis is brought to the minor league Durham Bulls to help their up and coming pitching prospect, "Nuke" Laloosh. Their relationship gets off to a rocky start and is further complicated when baseball groupie Annie Savoy sets her sights on the two men.
