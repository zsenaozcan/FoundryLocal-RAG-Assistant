Title: Crossplot (1969)
Genres: Thriller, Action
Director: Alvin Rakoff
Main Cast: Roger Moore, Martha Hyer, Alexis Kanner, Claudie Lange, Derek Francis
Keywords: assassination plot

Overview: A successful London ad-exec hires a beautiful Hungarian girl to pose for some modeling shots, little realising that she has overheard an assassination plot and is now being hunted by some dangerous killers.
