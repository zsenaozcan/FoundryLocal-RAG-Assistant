Title: Peculiarities of the National Fishing (1998)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Aleksandr Rogozhkin
Main Cast: Aleksei Buldakov, Viktor Bychkov, Semyon Strugachyov, Ville Haapasalo, Sergei Russkin

Overview: General Ivolgin, forester Kuzmich, and good-natured Lyova lose their way on a fishing trip and wind up in a neighboring country, where they decide to have a good time anyway but end up leaving their vodka and fishing equipment behind.
