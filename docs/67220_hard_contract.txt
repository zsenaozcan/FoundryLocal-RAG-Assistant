Title: Hard Contract (1969)
Genres: Crime, Romance
Director: S. Lee Pogostin
Main Cast: James Coburn, Lee Remick, Lilli Palmer, Burgess Meredith, Patrick Magee
Keywords: europe, hitman, love, morality

Overview: A cold hearted American hit man goes to Europe for 'one last score'. His encounter with a beautiful young woman casts self doubt on his lifeblood, and influences him to resist carrying out the contract
