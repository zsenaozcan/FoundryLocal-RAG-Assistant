Title: Listen to Britain (1942)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Humphrey Jennings
Main Cast: Chesney Allen, Leonard Brockington, Bud Flanagan, Myra Hess

Overview: A depiction of life in wartime England during the Second World War. Director Humphrey Jennings visits many aspects of civilian life and of the turmoil and privation caused by the war, all without narration.
