Title: Zelig (1983)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Woody Allen
Main Cast: Woody Allen, Mia Farrow, John Buckwalter, Patrick Horgan, Marvin Chatinover
Keywords: paris, great depression, celebrity, mockumentary, chameleon, psychiatrist, newsreel footage, electroconvulsive therapy, 1920s, 1930s

Overview: Fictional documentary about the life of human chameleon Leonard Zelig, a man who becomes a celebrity in the 1920s due to his ability to look and act like whoever is around him. Clever editing places Zelig in real newsreel footage of Woodrow Wilson, Babe Ruth, and others.
