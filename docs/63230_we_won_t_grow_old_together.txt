Title: We Won't Grow Old Together (1972)
Genres: Drama
Director: Maurice Pialat
Main Cast: Marlène Jobert, Jean Yanne, Harry-Max, Christine Fabréga, Jacques Galland

Overview: Jean has been married to Francoise for years, but his relationship with his wife has been all but over for a long time. She's hardly ever around, always traveling to Russia for work, and she drifts in and out of the film as well, reflecting the ephemeral nature of their marriage.
