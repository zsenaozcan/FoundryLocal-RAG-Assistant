Title: Pride of the Bowery (1940)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Drama
Director: Joseph H. Lewis
Main Cast: Leo Gorcey, Bobby Jordan, Kenneth Howell, Mary Ainslee, Bobby Stone
Keywords: sport, gang, east side kids, boxing

Overview: Muggs (Leo Gorcey) is tricked into entering a Civilian Conservation Corps camp by Danny (Bobby Jordan) in order to get in shape. Muggs resists and battles with the camp captain and with other campers. He also becomes involved in trying to help one of his friends get out of trouble.
