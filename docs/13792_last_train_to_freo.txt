Title: Last Train to Freo (2006)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Jeremy Sims
Main Cast: Steve Le Marquand, Tom Budge, Gigi Edgley, Glenn Hazeldine, Gillian Jones
Keywords: psychology, con man, night, train ride, psychopath, night train, train

Overview: Two thugs from the Perth suburb of Midland catch the last train to Fremantle. When a young woman boards the train a few stops later, they begin talking and find out not everyone on the train is who they seem to be.
