Title: Ogaryova Street, Number 6 (1980)
Genres: Crime, Mystery
Director: Boris Grigoryev
Main Cast: Vasili Lanovoy, Georgiy Yumatov, Yevgeni Gerasimov, Vsevolod Kuznetsov, Vsevolod Larionov

Overview: Detective Kostenko is traveling between Moscow and Sukhumi trying to find the responsible for double homicide.
