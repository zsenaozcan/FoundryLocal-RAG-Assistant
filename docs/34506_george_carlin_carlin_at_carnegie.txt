Title: George Carlin: Carlin at Carnegie (1982)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary, TV Movie
Director: Steven J. Santos
Main Cast: George Carlin
Keywords: concert, performance, made for cable tv, stand-up comedy, adult humor, tv special

Overview: Recorded at Carnegie Hall, New York City in 1982, released in 1983. Most of the material comes from his A Place for My Stuff, the album released earlier that same year. The final performance of "Seven Dirty Words," his last recorded performance of the routine, features Carlin's updated list.
