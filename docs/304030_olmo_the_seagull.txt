Title: Olmo & the Seagull (2015)
Genres: Drama, Documentary
Director: Petra Costa
Main Cast: Olivia Corsini, Serge Nicolaï
Keywords: woman director

Overview: 'Olmo and the Seagull' is a poetic and existential dive into an actress's mind during the nine months of her pregnancy as she must confront her most fiery inner demons while trying to rewrite a new philosophy of life, identity and love. Underlying this hybrid film is mounting tension over what is real and what is enacted when one is performing one's own life.
