Title: Desperate Measures (1998)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Barbet Schroeder
Main Cast: Michael Keaton, Andy García, Brian Cox, Marcia Gay Harden, Erik King
Keywords: donation, paranoia, terminal illness, leukemia, psychological thriller, criminal, convict, confrontation, captive, prison escape

Overview: San Francisco police officer Frank Connor is in a frantic search for a compatible bone marrow donor for his gravely ill son. There's only one catch the potential donor is convicted multiple murderer Peter McCabe who sees a trip to the hospital as the perfect opportunity to get what he wants most freedom. With McCabe's escape, the entire hospital becomes a battleground and Connor must pursue and, ironically, protect the deadly fugitive who is his son's only hope for survival.
