Title: The Man Without a Map (1968)
Genres: Mystery
Director: Hiroshi Teshigahara
Main Cast: Shintarô Katsu, Etsuko Ichihara, Osamu Ôkawa, Kiyoshi Atsumi, Tamao Nakamura

Overview: A private detective is hired to find a missing man by his wife. While his search is unsuccessful, the detective's own life begins to resemble the man for whom he is searching.
