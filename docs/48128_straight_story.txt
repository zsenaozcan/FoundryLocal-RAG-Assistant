Title: Straight Story (2006)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy
Director: Efi Mouriki
Main Cast: Christos Hatzipanayotis, Vladimiros Kiriakidis, Anthem Moss, Kanellina Menouti, Meni Konstantinidou
Keywords: dream, restaurant, alternate reality, straight

Overview: This film is about an upside down world, where being homosexual is considered "normal" and heterosexual is considered "not normal".
