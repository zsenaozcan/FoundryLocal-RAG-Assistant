Title: Midnight Sun (2014)
Genres: Family, Adventure
Director: Roger Spottiswoode
Main Cast: Dakota Goyo, Goran Visnjic, Bridget Moynahan, Linda Kash, Kendra Leigh Timmins
Keywords: canada, winter, human animal relationship, polar bear, bear, northern canada, animal rescue

Overview: In northern Canada, a young boy sets a goal for himself to to reunite an abandoned polar bear cub with its mother. Goran Visnjic joins the cast as Muktuk, a half Inuit and half Canadian, who knows the terrain where the polar bears live and who agrees to help the boy.
