Title: The Deathhead Virgin (1974)
Genres: Horror
Director: Norman Foster
Main Cast: Jock Gaynor, Larry Ward, Diane McBain, Vic Diaz, Kim Ramos
Keywords: possession, curse, sunken treasure

Overview: A treasure hunter finds a sunken Spanish galleon off the coast of a Philippine island. What he doesn't know is that the ship is guarded by the spirit of an ancient Moro princess
