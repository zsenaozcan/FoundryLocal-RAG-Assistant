Title: Buzzard (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Horror
Director: Joel Potrykus
Main Cast: Joshua Burge, Joel Potrykus, Teri Ann Nelson, Joe Anderson, Alan Longstreet
Keywords: hotel, bank, spaghetti, punk, nintendo, slacker, demon, scam artist, power glove

Overview: Paranoia forces small-time scam artist Marty to flee his hometown and hide out in a dangerous Detroit. With nothing but a pocket full of bogus checks, his Power Glove, and a bad temper, the horror metal slacker lashes out.
