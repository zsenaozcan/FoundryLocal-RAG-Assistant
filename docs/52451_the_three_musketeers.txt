Genres: Adventure, Action, Thriller
Director: Paul W.S. Anderson
Main Cast: Milla Jovovich, Orlando Bloom, Logan Lerman, Ray Stevenson, Matthew Macfadyen
Keywords: number in title, historical fiction, musketeer, 17th century, 3d
