Title: Pursuit to Algiers (1945)
Genres: Mystery, Adventure, Crime
Director: Roy William Neill
Main Cast: Basil Rathbone, Nigel Bruce, Marjorie Riordan, Rosalind Ivan, Morton Lowry
Keywords: sherlock holmes

Overview: Holmes and Watson on a transatlantic ocean liner escorting Nikolas, heir to a foreign throne. Also on board are a number of assassins, plotting against their sovereign.
