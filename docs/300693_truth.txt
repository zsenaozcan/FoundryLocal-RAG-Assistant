Title: Truth (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: James Vanderbilt
Main Cast: Robert Redford, Cate Blanchett, Elisabeth Moss, Topher Grace, Dennis Quaid
Keywords: document, biography, tv reporter, presidential elections, political scandal

Overview: A behind-the-scenes look at news anchor Dan Rather during his final days at C.B.S. News when he broadcast a damaging report about how President Bush relied on privilege and family connections to avoid fighting in the Vietnam War.
