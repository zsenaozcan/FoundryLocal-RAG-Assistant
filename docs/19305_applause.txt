Title: Applause (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Martin Zandvliet
Main Cast: Paprika Steen, Michael Falch, Otto Leonardo Steen Rieks, Noel Koch-Søfeldt, Sara-Marie Maltha

Overview: When the critically acclaimed, tough and coming of age actress Thea Barfoed ends her rehab, she confronts a hard choice. During her heavy drinking period she divorced and lost custody of her two boys. Now she wants them to be a part of her life again.
