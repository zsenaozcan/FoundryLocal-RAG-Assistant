Title: Elle (2016)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Paul Verhoeven
Main Cast: Isabelle Huppert, Laurent Lafitte, Anne Consigny, Charles Berling, Virginie Efira
Keywords: france, video game, female nudity, rape, nudity, assault, death of father, car accident, violence, birth

Overview: When Michelle, the CEO of a gaming software company, is attacked in her home by an unknown assailant, she refuses to let it alter her precisely ordered life. She manages crises involving family, all the while becoming engaged in a game of cat and mouse with her stalker.
