Title: Out of the Furnace (2013)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Crime
Director: Scott Cooper
Main Cast: Christian Bale, Zoe Saldana, Woody Harrelson, Sam Shepard, Willem Dafoe
Keywords: prison, brother brother relationship, drug dealer, brother, revenge, drug use, car accident, justice, family, bare knuckle fighting

Overview: Two brothers live in the economically-depressed Rust Belt, when a cruel twist of fate lands one in prison. His brother is then lured into one of the most violent crime rings in the Northeast.
