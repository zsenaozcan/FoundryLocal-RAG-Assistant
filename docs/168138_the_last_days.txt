Title: The Last Days (2013)
Genres: Science Fiction, Adventure, Thriller
Director: Àlex Pastor
Main Cast: Quim Gutiérrez, José Coronado, Marta Etura , Leticia Dolera, Ivan Massagué
Keywords: dystopia

Overview: 2013. A mysterious epidemic spreads across the planet. Humanity develops an irrational fear of open spaces that causes instant death. Soon, the world population is trapped inside buildings. As Barcelona descends into chaos, Marc sets off on a quest to find Julia, his missing girlfriend, without ever going outside.
