Title: The Strawberry Blonde (1941)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: James Cagney, Olivia de Havilland, Rita Hayworth, Jack Carson, Alan Hale
Keywords: remake, revenge, barber shop, gay nineties, graft, beer garden, 19th century

Overview: Biff Grimes is desperately in love with Virginia, but his best friend Hugo marries her and manipulates Biff into becoming involved in his somewhat nefarious businesses. Hugo appears to have stolen Biff's dreams, and Biff has to deal with the realisation that having what he wants and wanting what another has can be very different things.
