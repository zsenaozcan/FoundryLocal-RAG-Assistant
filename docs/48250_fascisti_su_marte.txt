Title: Fascisti su Marte (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Corrado Guzzanti
Main Cast: Corrado Guzzanti, Marco Marzocca, Caterina Guzzanti, Andrea Blarzino, Pasquale Petrolo
Keywords: political satire

Overview: In 1938 a group of fascists led by the general Gaetano Maria Barbagli decides to invade the planet of Mars.
