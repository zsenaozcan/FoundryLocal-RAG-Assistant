Title: Night Terrors (1993)
Genres: Horror
Director: Tobe Hooper
Main Cast: Robert Englund, Zoe Trilling, Chandra West, Alona Kimhi, Juliano Mer-Khamis
Keywords: descendant, marquis de sade

Overview: A young girl travels to Cairo to visit her father, and becomes unwillingly involved with a bizarre sadomasochistic cult led by the charismatic Paul Chevalier, who is a descendant of the Marquis de Sade.
