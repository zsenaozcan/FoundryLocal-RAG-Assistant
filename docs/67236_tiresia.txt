Title: Tiresia (2003)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bertrand Bonello
Main Cast: Laurent Lucas, Clara Choveaux, Thiago Telès, Célia Catalifo
Keywords: prostitute, transsexuality, greek mythology

Overview: Based on the legend of Tiresias, it tells of a transsexual who is kidnapped by a man and left to die in the woods. She is then saved by a family and receives the gift of telling the future.
