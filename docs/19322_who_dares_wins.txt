Title: Who Dares Wins (1982)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Ian Sharp
Main Cast: Lewis Collins, Judy Davis, Richard Widmark, Edward Woodward, Robert Webber

Overview: When SAS Captain Peter Skellen is thrown out of the service for gross misconduct due to unnecessary violence and bullying, he is soon recruited by The People's Lobby, a fanatical group aiming to hold several US dignitaries hostage. But Skellen's dismissal is a front to enable him to get close to the terrorist group. Can he get close enough to stop the Lobby from creating an international incident?
