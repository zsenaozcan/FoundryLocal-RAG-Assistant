Title: Flatfoot in Hong Kong (1975)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Thriller, Drama
Director: Steno
Main Cast: Bud Spencer, Al Lettieri, Enzo Cannavale, Renato Scarpa, Robert Webber
Keywords: heroin, gangster boss, bangkok, police, hong kong

Overview: Piedone (Bigfoot) Rizzo is accused of drug trafficking. In order to clear his name he has to find out who is the person, from a Mafia ring, who has infiltrated his police department.
