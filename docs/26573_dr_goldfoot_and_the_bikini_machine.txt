Title: Dr. Goldfoot and the Bikini Machine (1965)
Genres: Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Norman Taurog
Main Cast: Vincent Price, Frankie Avalon, Dwayne Hickman, Jack Mullaney, Fred Clark
Keywords: secret agent, bikini, mad scientist, robot, world domination

Overview: In this campy spy movie spoof Dr. Goldfoot (Vincent Price) has invented an army of bikini-clad robots who are programmed to seek out wealthy men and charm them into signing over their assets. Secret agent Craig Gamble (Frankie Avalon) and millionaire Todd Armstrong set out to foil his fiendish plot.
