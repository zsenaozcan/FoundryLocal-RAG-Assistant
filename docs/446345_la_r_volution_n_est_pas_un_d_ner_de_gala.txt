Title: La révolution n'est pas un dîner de gala (2015)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Youri Tchao-Débats
Main Cast: Coralie Russier, Vincente Perez, Jean Morel

Overview: Lola is hitch-hiking on a highway rest area when she meets Karim. He agrees to drive her all the way to Paris. Lola wants to become a film actress. Karim wants to change the course of History. The revolution will happen with or without them.
