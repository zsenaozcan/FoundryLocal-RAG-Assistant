Title: Satanik (1968)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Piero Vivarelli
Main Cast: Magda Konopka, Julio Peña, Umberto Raho, Luigi Montini, Armando Calvo

Overview: A youth potion transforms an old crone into a ravishing beauty. Unfortunately, her new-found gorgeousness forces the heroine to form a pact with the Devil.
