Title: Babes on Broadway (1941)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Busby Berkeley
Main Cast: Mickey Rooney, Judy Garland, Fay Bainter, Virginia Weidler, Ray McDonald
Keywords: musical, teenager, show

Overview: Penny Morris and Tommy Williams are both starstruck young teens but nobody seems to give them any chance to perform. Instead they decide to put up their own show to collect money for a summer camp for the kids.
