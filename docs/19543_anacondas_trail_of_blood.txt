Title: Anacondas: Trail of Blood (2009)
Genres: Action, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Don E. FauntLeRoy
Main Cast: John Rhys-Davies, Crystal Allen, Linden Ashby, Danny Midwinter, Calin Stanciu
