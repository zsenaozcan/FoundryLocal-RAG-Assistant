Title: Home from Home – Chronicle of a Vision (2013)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Edgar Reitz
Main Cast: Jan Dieter Schneider, Antonia Bill, Maximilian Scheidt, Marita Breuer, Rüdiger Kriese

Overview: Follow-up to the TV trilogy "Heimat", this time for cinemas, set again in the fictional village Schabbach in the Hunsrück region of Rhineland-Palatinate.
