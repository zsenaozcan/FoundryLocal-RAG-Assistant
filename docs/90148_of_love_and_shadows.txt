Title: Of Love and Shadows (1994)
Genres: Drama
Director: Betty Kaplan
Main Cast: Jennifer Connelly, Antonio Banderas, Stefania Sandrelli, Diego Wallraff, Camilo Gallardo
Keywords: dictatorship, woman reporter, torture, woman director

Overview: Irene is a magazine editor living under the shadow of the Pinochet dictatorship in Chile. Francisco is a handsome photographer and he comes to Irene for a job. As a sympathizer with the underground resistance movement, Francisco opens her eyes and her heart to the atrocities being committed by the state.
