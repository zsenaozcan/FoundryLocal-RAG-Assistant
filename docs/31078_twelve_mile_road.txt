Title: Twelve Mile Road (2003)
Genres: Drama
Director: Richard Friedenberg
Main Cast: Tom Selleck, Maggie Grace, Wendy Crewson, Anna Gunn, Patrick John Flueger
Keywords: summer vacation, father daughter relationship, farmer, troubled teen

Overview: A divorced farmer takes in his troubled teenage daughter for the summer, a summer which changes the lives of the two of them, and their friends and family.
