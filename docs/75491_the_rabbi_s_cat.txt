Title: The Rabbi's Cat (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Animation
Director: Joann Sfar
Main Cast: François Morel, Hafsia Herzi, Maurice Bénichou, Jean-Pierre Kalfon, Daniel Cohen
Keywords: cat, rabbi, racism, father daughter relationship, french colonialism, jewish culture, talmud, french colony, inter religion relationship, casbah
