Title: Fort Vengeance (1953)
Genres: Western
Director: Lesley Selander
Main Cast: James Craig, Rita Moreno, Keith Larsen, Reginald Denny, Charles Irwin
Keywords: native american, fortress

Overview: Two brothers flee America and join the Canadian North West Mounted Police. One brother is good, the other bad, both men on a collision course just as trouble starts to brew with the Indians.
