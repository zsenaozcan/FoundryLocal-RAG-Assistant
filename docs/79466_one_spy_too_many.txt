Title: One Spy Too Many (1966)
Genres: Adventure, Crime
Director: Joseph Sargent
Main Cast: Robert Vaughn, James Hong, Rip Torn, Cal Bolder, Dorothy Provine
Keywords: spy, based on tv series

Overview: The men from U.N.C.L.E." are back! This time Robert Vaughn and David McCallum must stop the meglomanic Alexander (Rip Torn) from committing the world's greatest crimes.
