Title: The One I Love (2014)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Charlie McDowell
Main Cast: Mark Duplass, Elisabeth Moss, Ted Danson, Kiana Cason, Kaitlyn Dodson
Keywords: weekend, therapist, marriage, dilemma, escape, couple, discovery, retreat, help, feelings

Overview: On the brink of separation, Ethan and Sophie escape to a beautiful vacation house for a weekend getaway in an attempt to save their marriage. What begins as a romantic and fun retreat soon becomes surreal, when an unexpected discovery forces the two to examine themselves, their relationship, and their future.
