Title: Some Velvet Morning (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Neil LaBute
Main Cast: Stanley Tucci, Alice Eve
Keywords: prostitute, infidelity

Overview: Fred (Stanley Tucci) arrives at the doorstep of his beautiful young mistress Velvet (Alice Eve) after four years apart, claiming to have finally left his wife. But when she rejects his attempts to rekindle their romance, his persistence evolves into obsession — and a dark history between the former lovers comes into focus.
