Title: How The West Was Fun (1994)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Mary-Kate Olsen, Ashley Olsen, Martin Mull

Overview: Twins Suzy and Jessica visit granny Natty's dude ranch in the Wild West. The ranch has too few paying visitors and granny may have to sell the ranch to evil developer Bart unless "somebody" can help her. The twins also find the diary of their deceased mother.
