Title: The Polar Bear King (1991)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Romance, Family
Director: Ola Solum
Main Cast: Maria Bonnevie, Tobias Hoesl, Anna-Lotta Larsson, Jack Fjeldstad, Monica Nordquist
Keywords: supernatural, polar bear, curse, king, witchcraft

Overview: After his father is killed, Prince Valemon ascends the throne, only to be turned into a polar bear by a bitter witch who wants to be his queen.
