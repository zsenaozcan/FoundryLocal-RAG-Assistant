Title: Maz Jobrani: I'm Not a Terrorist But I've Played One on TV (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Maz Jobrani
Main Cast: Maz Jobrani
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Comedian Maz Jobrani follows up his bestselling book with a hilarious comedy special of the same title, where he takes on political issues, social issues and his kids through the lens of his Iranian-American background to show that we have a lot more in common than we think.
