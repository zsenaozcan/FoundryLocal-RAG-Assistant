Title: The Lookout (2012)
Genres: Action, Crime
Director: Michele Placido
Main Cast: Violante Placido, Daniel Auteuil, Mathieu Kassovitz, Arly Jover, Olivier Gourmet
Keywords: suspense, theft

Overview: A major police operation to take down a notorious gang of bank robbers goes horribly wrong when a rooftop sniper opens fire, killing several police officers and causing an explosive distraction in which the robbers make their getaway. Commissioner Mattei launches a large-scale manhunt for the sniper and his team, and is thrown into the seedy Parisian underworld of criminals and fallen souls.
