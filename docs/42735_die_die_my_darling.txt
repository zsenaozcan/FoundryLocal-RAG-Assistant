Title: Die! Die! My Darling! (1965)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Silvio Narizzano
Main Cast: Tallulah Bankhead, Stefanie Powers, Peter Vaughan, Donald Sutherland, Maurice Kaufmann
Keywords: mother-in-law, insanity, hammer horror, religious fanatic

Overview: A young woman is terrorized by her fiance's demented mother who blames her for her son's death.
