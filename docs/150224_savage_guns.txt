Title: Savage Guns (1971)
Genres: Western
Director: Demofilo Fidani
Main Cast: Robert Woods, Dino Strano, Benito Pacifico, Simonetta Vitelli, Piera Bruni
Keywords: knife throwing, spaghetti western, opening action scene, gatling gun, revenge plot

Overview: Sam is wounded by the Mash Flanigan gang who enters a saloon and kills everybody in sight including his brother. Walbash chases Flanigan to Golden City for revenge, leading to the obligatory showdown. One of the clever scenes is when gunfighters Gordon Mitchell, Lincoln Tate and Peter Martell interview for jobs and are introduced with their acting names Mitchell, Tate and Martell.
