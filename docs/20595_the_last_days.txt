Title: The Last Days (1998)
Genres: Documentary, Drama, History, War
Director: James Moll
Main Cast: Bill Basch, Martin Basch, Randolph Braham
Keywords: prisoner, u.s. soldier, banana, childhood home, buchenwald, menorah 

Overview: Five Jewish Hungarians, now U.S. citizens, tell their stories: before March, 1944, when Nazis began to exterminate Hungarian Jews, months in concentration camps, and visiting childhood homes more than 50 years later. An historian, a Sonderkommando, a doctor who experimented on Auschwitz prisoners, and US soldiers who were part of the liberation in April, 1945.
