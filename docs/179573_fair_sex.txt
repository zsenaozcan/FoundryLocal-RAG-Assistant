Title: Fair Sex (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Martin Laroche
Main Cast: Marie-Evelyne Lessard, Marc-André Brunet, Normand Daoust, Stephanie Dubois, Alexandre Dubois
Keywords: quebec, fair, romance, gender, womens issues, female genital mutilation

Overview: Would-be filmmaker Sophie left Africa for Canada as a child. After graduating, she films the daily doings as part of a summer job at a fair. When pointing the camera at herself, she reveals painful secrets from the past.
