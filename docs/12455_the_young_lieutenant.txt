Title: The Young Lieutenant (2005)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Xavier Beauvois
Main Cast: Nathalie Baye, Jalil Lespert, Roschdy Zem, Antoine Chappey, Jacques Perrin
Keywords: homicide, le havre, recovering alcoholic, police academy, russian immigrant 

Overview: A rookie policeman from provincial Le Havre volunteers for the high pressure Parisian homicide bureau and is assigned to a middle-aged woman detective.
