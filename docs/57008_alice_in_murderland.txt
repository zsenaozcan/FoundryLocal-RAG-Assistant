Title: Alice in Murderland (2010)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Dennis Devine
Main Cast: Malerie Grady, Marlene Mc'Cohen, Kelly Kula, Heath Butler

Overview: It's Alice's birthday and her sorority girlfriends throw her a themed party. Everyone comes as their favorite, sexy character from Wonderland. The Jabberwocky wasn't invited and brings murder and mayhem to the girls' night out.
