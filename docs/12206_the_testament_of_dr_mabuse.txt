Title: The Testament of Dr. Mabuse (1933)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Fritz Lang
Main Cast: Rudolf Klein-Rogge, Oscar Beregi Sr., Paul Bernd, Henry Pleß, Otto Wernicke
Keywords: hypnosis, victim, delinquent, nervenklinik, independent film, doctor, crime, criminal

Overview: Fritz Lang directed this sequel to his nearly four-hour Dr. Mabuse silent of 1922. The film opens with Detective Hofmeister spying on the activities of a criminal syndicate. Not realizing he has been seen, Hofmeister is attacked by the thugs and later turns up out of his mind.
