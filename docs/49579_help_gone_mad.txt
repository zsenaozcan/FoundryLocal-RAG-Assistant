Title: Help Gone Mad (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Foreign
Director: Boris Khlebnikov
Main Cast: Yevgeni Syty, Sergei Dreiden, Anna Mikhalkova, Igor Chernevich

Overview: Kind and lazy Jenya comes to Moscow from small village in Belarus for earnings. Criminal incident unexpectedly separates Jenya from companions and leaves him alone without money and documents. He has no friends or relatives in this big and hostile city and he is about to end as a homeless bum.
