Title: Persuasion (2007)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Adrian Shergold
Main Cast: Sally Hawkins, Rupert Penry-Jones, Alice Krige, Anthony Stewart Head, Julia Davis
Keywords: love triangle, poetry, captain, navy, love

Overview: Persuasion is the newest adaptation of the classic Jane Austen novel of the same name published in 1818.Anne (Sally Hawkins) fell deeply in love with handsome young naval officer Frederick Wentworth (Rupert Penry-Jones) at the age of nineteen.
