Title: 11/11/11 (2011)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Keith Allen Burns
Main Cast: Jon Briddell, Erin Coker, Hayden Byerly, Rebecca Sigl, Scott McKinley
Keywords: anti-christ, conspiracy

Overview: Jack and Melissa are frightened by their son's bizarre and violent behavior; they soon learn that he is the gateway to the Apocalypse, and it will happen on his birthday, 11-11-11.
