Title: The Star of Bethlehem (2007)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Stephen Vidano
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: bible, star, bethlehem, christian

Overview: Scholars debate whether the Star of Bethlehem is a legend created by the early church or a miracle that marked the advent of Christ. Is it possible that the star was a real, astronomical event? From Producer Stephen McEveety (The Passion of the Christ) comes an amazing documentary on the Star of Bethlehem.
