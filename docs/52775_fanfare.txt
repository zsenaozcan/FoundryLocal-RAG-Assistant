Title: Fanfare (1958)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Bert Haanstra
Main Cast: Hans Kaart, Bernard Droog, Ineke Brinkman, Wim Van den Heuvel, Andrea Domburg

Overview: After a fight the brass band in a small village splits up into two separate bands. They both want to win a contest and will do anything to prevent the other band from winning it.
