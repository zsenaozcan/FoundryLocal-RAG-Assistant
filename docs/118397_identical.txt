Title: Identical (2011)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Daniel Bollag
Main Cast: Jonathan Togo, Pascal Yen-Pfister, Emily Baldoni, Kelly Baugher

Overview: Identical twins are born, one is good and one is evil. They need each-other to exist, but they deeply resent the others existence. They both fall in love with the same woman. A love triangle forms which leads to murder.
