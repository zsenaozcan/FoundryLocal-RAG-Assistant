Title: Horoscope for Good Luck (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Arman Gevorgyan
Main Cast: Dmitriy Endaltsev, Svetlana Khodchenkova, Anna Chipovskaya, Yuriy Kutsenko, Dmitriy Nagiev
