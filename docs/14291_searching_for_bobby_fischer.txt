Title: Searching for Bobby Fischer (1993)
Genres: Drama
Director: Steven Zaillian
Main Cast: Max Pomeranc, Joe Mantegna, Joan Allen, Ben Kingsley, Laurence Fishburne
Keywords: chess, hustler, child prodigy, new york city

Overview: A prepubescent chess prodigy refuses to harden himself in order to become a champion like the famous but unlikable Bobby Fischer.
