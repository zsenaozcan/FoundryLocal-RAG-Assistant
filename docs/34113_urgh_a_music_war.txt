Title: Urgh! A Music War (1981)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Derek Burbidge
Main Cast: Belinda Carlisle, Klaus Nomi, Sting, Gang of Four, Pere Ubu

Overview: Urgh! A Music War is a British film released in 1981 featuring performances by punk rock, new wave, and post-punk acts of the late 70s and early 80s.
