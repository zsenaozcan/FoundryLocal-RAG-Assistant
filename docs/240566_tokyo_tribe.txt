Title: Tokyo Tribe (2014)
Genres: Music, Science Fiction, Action, Drama, Crime
Director: Sion Sono
Main Cast: Ryohei Suzuki, Young Dais, Seino Nana, Ryuta Sato, Shôko Nakagawa
Keywords: yakuza, katana, musical, manga, sword fight, gang, based on manga, hiphop

Overview: In an alternate Japan, territorial street gangs form opposing factions collectively known as the Tokyo Tribes. The simmering tension between them is about to boil over into all-out war.
