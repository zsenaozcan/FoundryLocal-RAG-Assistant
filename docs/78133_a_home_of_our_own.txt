Title: A Home of Our Own (1993)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Tony Bill
Main Cast: Edward Furlong, Kathy Bates, T.J. Lowther, Soon-Tek Oh, Clarissa Lassig
Keywords: child abuse, widow, biography, family relationships, child's point of view

Overview: The story is located in Los Angeles in the sixties. An energetic widow, Frances Lacey, with her six children try to make a dream of theirs come true: to have a home of their own. Therefore they leave Los Angeles and head for the countryside, while facing all kinds of difficulties during their journey.
