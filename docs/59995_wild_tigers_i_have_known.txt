Title: Wild Tigers I Have Known (2006)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Cam Archer
Main Cast: Malcolm Stumpf, Patrick White, Fairuza Balk, Kim Dickens, Tom Gilroy
Keywords: gay, independent film, coming of age

Overview: A lyrical telling of the coming of age of a 13-year-old boy who learns to cope with his new found sexuality and his unrequited love for the cool kid in school.
