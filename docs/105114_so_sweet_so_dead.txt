Title: So Sweet, So Dead (1972)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Horror, Mystery, Romance, Thriller
Director: Roberto Bianchi Montero
Main Cast: Farley Granger, Sylva Koscina, Jessica Dublin, Krista Nell, Silvano Tranquilli
Keywords: sex, adultery, giallo, photograph, police inspector

Overview: A serial killer is on the loose. His victims are unfaithful wives and he always leaves compromising photographs at the crime scene.
