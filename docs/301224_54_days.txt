Title: 54 Days (2014)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Tim Lea
Main Cast: Dianna La Grassa, Stephanie May, Matt James, Michela Carattini, Byron Sakha
Keywords: australian, invasion, dystopia, post nuclear

Overview: 5 people trapped in a bunker after a nuclear and biological attack are forced to make an impossible decision - either one dies or they all die.
