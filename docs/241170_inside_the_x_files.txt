Title: Inside The X-Files (1998)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Glen Kasper
Main Cast: Chris Carter, David Duchovny, Gillian Anderson, William B. Davis, Sheila Larken

Overview: Inside the X-Files for a behind-the-scenes look at the show. Also included are interviews with the cast and creator Chris Carter, never before seen segments from the show, outtakes and a sneak preview of the upcoming feature film.
