Title: La Novia (2015)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Paula Ortiz
Main Cast: Inma Cuesta, Álex García, Asier Etxeandia, Leticia Dolera, Luisa Gavasa
Keywords: love triangle, based on play or musical, melodrama, love affair, family feud, woman director, play adaptation

Overview: Based on the famous play "Bodas de sangre" (Blood Wedding) by Federico García Lorca, La novia tells the story of a passionate love triangle. A bride leaves her groom, no longer able to withstand the surging desires of her lover, Leonordo.
