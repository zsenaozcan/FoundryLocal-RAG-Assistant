Title: The Pee-wee Herman Show (1981)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Paul Reubens
Main Cast: Paul Reubens, Phil Hartman, Lynne Marie Stewart, John Paragon, Edie McClurg

Overview: An adult-oriented version of what would eventually become an award-winning children's classic. This version of the show features Pee-wee's playhouse and many of the characters of the later series, but with adult and sexual overtones and jokes including "mirror shoes" and others.
