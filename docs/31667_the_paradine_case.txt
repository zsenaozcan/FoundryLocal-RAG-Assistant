Title: The Paradine Case (1947)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Thriller
Director: Alfred Hitchcock
Main Cast: Gregory Peck, Ann Todd, Charles Laughton, Charles Coburn, Ethel Barrymore
Keywords: femme fatale, lawyer

Overview: The beautiful Mrs. Paradine is accused of poisoning her older, blind husband. She hires married Anthony Keane as her lawyer and when he begins to fall in love with her, she encourages him.
