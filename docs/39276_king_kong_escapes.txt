Title: King Kong Escapes (1967)
Genres: Action, Fantasy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Ishirô Honda
Main Cast: Rhodes Reason, Mie Hama, Linda Miller, Akira Takarada, Hideyo Amamoto
Keywords: giant monster, kaiju

Overview: King Kong is brought in by an evil ruler to dig for precious gems in a mine when the robot MechaKong is unable to do the task. This leads to the machine and the real Kong engaging in a tremendous battle that threatens to level Japan.
