Title: Aurore (2005)
Genres: Drama, Action, Foreign
Director: Luc Dionne
Main Cast: Marianne Fortier, Serge Postigo, Hélène Bourgeois Leclerc, Yves Jacques, Rémy Girard
Keywords: court case, quebec, step mother

Overview: After the sudden death of her mother, Aurore Gagnon is abused by her disturbed step-mother as her town remains in the silence followed by her death. Based on a true story.
