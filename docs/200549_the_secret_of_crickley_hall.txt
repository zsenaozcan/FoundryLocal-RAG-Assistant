Title: The Secret of Crickley Hall (2012)
Genres: Drama, Horror, TV Movie
Director: Joe Ahearne
Main Cast: Suranne Jones, Tom Ellis, Douglas Henshall, David Warner, Sarah Smart
Keywords: haunted house, miniseries

Overview: A year after their son goes missing, a family moves to Crickley Hall. When supernatural events begin to take place, Eve feels the house is somehow connected to her lost son.
