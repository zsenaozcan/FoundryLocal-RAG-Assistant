Title: S21: The Khmer Rouge Death Machine (2003)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Rithy Panh
Main Cast: Chum Mey, Khieu 'Poev' Ches, Yeay Cheu, Nhiem Ein, Houy Him
Keywords: cambodia, red khmer, khmer rouge

Overview: Documentary of the S-21 genocide prison in Phnom Penh with interviews of prisoners and guards. On the search for reasons why this could have happened.
