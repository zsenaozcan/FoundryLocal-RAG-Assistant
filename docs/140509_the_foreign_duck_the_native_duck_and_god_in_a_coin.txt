Title: The Foreign Duck, the Native Duck and God in a Coin Locker (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yoshihiro Nakamura
Main Cast: Eita, Megumi Seki, Gaku Hamada, Masaki Okada, Nene Otsuka
Keywords: kidnapping, illiteracy, zoo, tape recorder, textbook

Overview: A university student finds himself wrapped up in the bizarre world of his next door neighbor, learning about his history and relationship with a girl who changed his life.
