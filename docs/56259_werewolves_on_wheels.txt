Title: Werewolves on Wheels (1971)
Genres: Action, Horror
Director: Michel Levesque
Main Cast: Steve Oliver, D.J. Anderson, Gene Shane, Billy Gray, Gray Johnson
Keywords: satanism, biker, independent film, blood, werewolf, motorcycle gang, desert, occult

Overview: A biker gang visits a monastery where they encounter black-robed monks engaged in worshipping Satan. When the monks try to persuade one of the female bikers, Helen, to become a satanic sacrifice the bikers smash up the monastery and leave. The monks have the last laugh, though, as Helen, as a result of the satanic rituals, is now possessed and at night changes into a werewolf, with dire results for the biker gang.
