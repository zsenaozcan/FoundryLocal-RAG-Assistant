Title: Robert the Doll (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: Andrew Jones
Main Cast: Lee Bane, Megan Lockhurst, Flynn Allen, Suzie Frances Garton, Judith Haley
Keywords: based on true story, evil doll, haunting, possessed

Overview: In this chilling story based on real life events a family experience terrifying supernatural occurrences when their son acquires a vintage doll called Robert.
