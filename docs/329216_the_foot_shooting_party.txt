Title: The Foot Shooting Party (1994)
Genres: Drama
Director: Annette Haywood-Carter
Main Cast: Jake Busey, Bradley Gregg, Darin Heames, Merle Kennedy, Sarah Lassez
Keywords: vietnam, vietnam war, lead singer, woman director

Overview: The lead singer of a band gets drafted for war in Vietnam. So as not to break up the band, he decides to shoot his own foot.
