Title: Twigson (2009)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Family
Director: Åsleik Engmark
Main Cast: Adrian Grønnevik Smith, Åsleik Engmark, Pernille Sørensen, Jan Gunnar Røise, Petrus Andreas Christensen

Overview: The film Twigson is all about a tough, naughty, quick-witted and brave boy. Lillebror has just moved, and has not made any new friends yet, when his imaginary friend – the wooden twig Knerten – appears in the middle of a pile of firewood...
