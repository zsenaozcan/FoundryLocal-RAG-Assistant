Title: Elevator Girl (2010)
Genres: TV Movie, Comedy, Family
Director: Bradford May
Main Cast: Lacey Chabert, Ryan Merriman, Hedy Burress, Jonathan Bennett, Patty McCormack

Overview: A smart, uptight attorney and a disorganized yet passionate and creative woman meet in a stuck elevator, and sparks ignite instantly. Will opposites attract, or are they simply passing time before getting off on different floors?
