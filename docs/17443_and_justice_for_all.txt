Title: ...And Justice for All (1979)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Norman Jewison
Main Cast: Al Pacino, Jack Warden, John Forsythe, Christine Lahti, Lee Strasberg
Keywords: suspense, lawyer, courtroom, extramarital affair

Overview: An ethical Baltimore defense lawyer disgusted with rampant legal corruption is asked to defend a judge he despises in a rape trial. But if he doesn't do it, the judge will have him disbarred.
