Title: Bounce (2000)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Don Roos
Main Cast: Ben Affleck, Gwyneth Paltrow, Joe Morton, Natasha Henstridge, Tony Goldwyn
Keywords: chance, widow, stroke of fate, encounter, destiny

Overview: A man switches plane tickets with another man who dies in that plane in a crash. The man falls in love with the deceased one's wife.
