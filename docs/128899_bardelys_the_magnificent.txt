Title: Bardelys the Magnificent (1926)
Genres: Action, Drama, Romance
Director: King Vidor
Main Cast: John Gilbert, Eleanor Boardman, Roy D'Arcy, Lionel Belmore, Emily Fitzroy
Keywords: silent film, king louis xiii

Overview: Rafael Sabatini's story of the swashbuckling era and of Bardeleys, the handsome courtier who could win any woman he set his mind to...and was not above boasting about it to all who would listen.
