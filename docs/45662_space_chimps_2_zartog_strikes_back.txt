Title: Space Chimps 2: Zartog Strikes Back (2010)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: John H. Williams
Main Cast: Tom Kenny, Zack Shada, Patrick Warburton, Cheryl Hines, Carlos Alazraqui

Overview: Comet longs to be taken seriously as a full-fledged space chimp. He journeys to the fantastical Planet Malgor and bonds with the adorable alien Kilowatt, living out his ultimate fantasy. However, it's time for Comet to prove himself when the feared alien ruler Zartog takes over Mission Control! Comet must show he has the right stuff, and join fellow chimps Ham, Luna and Titan, to save the day.
