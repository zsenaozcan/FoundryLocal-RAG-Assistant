Title: The Cross of Lorraine (1943)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Tay Garnett
Main Cast: Jean-Pierre Aumont, Gene Kelly, Cedric Hardwicke, Peter Lorre, Hume Cronyn
Keywords: spy, nazis, false accusations

Overview: French soldiers (Jean-Pierre Aumont, Gene Kelly) surrender to lying Nazis and are herded into a barbaric prison camp.
