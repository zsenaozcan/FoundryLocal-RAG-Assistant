Title: Girl Rising (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Richard Robbins
Main Cast: Cate Blanchett, Priyanka Chopra, Selena Gomez, Anne Hathaway, Salma Hayek
Keywords: cultural difference, inspiring, international, gender differences

Overview: Nine filmmakers each profile a young girl from a different part of the world to weave a global tapestry of youth in the 21st century.
