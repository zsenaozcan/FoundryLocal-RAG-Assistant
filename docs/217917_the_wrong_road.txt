Title: The Wrong Road (1937)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: James Cruze
Main Cast: Richard Cromwell, Helen Mack, Lionel Atwill, Horace McMahon, Russ Powell
Keywords: wife husband relationship, release from prison, bank robbery, insurance investigator, hidden loot

Overview: A young married couple whose plans for their life together haven't turned out as expected decide to rob the bank where the husband works of $100,000, then hide the money in a safe place and return for it after they serve out their sentences. All goes according to plan until they get out of prison, when they find that they're being trailed by an insurance investigator and the husband's old cellmate, who has decided that he wants a cut of the money.
