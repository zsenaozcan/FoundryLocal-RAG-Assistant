Title: The Sparrow's Fluttering (1988)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Gianfranco Mingozzi
Main Cast: Philippe Noiret, Ornella Muti, Nicola Farron, Chiara Argelli, Sabrina Ferilli

Overview: A woman, who has been the mistress of a man who has just died, thinks about leaving the little Italian village where they lived and moving to another place. An old and rich man offers her a place in his house, however, she will have to listen to him telling his past love stories.
