Title: Princess Arete (2001)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Fantasy
Director: Sunao Katabuchi
Main Cast: Houko Kuwashima, Tsuyoshi Koyama, Yūsuke Numata, Minami Takayama, Satomi Kōrogi
Keywords: anime

Overview: Imprisoned by a sorcerer who fears she will rob him of eternal life, guileless young Princess Arete befriends a villager who helps her plan an escape.
