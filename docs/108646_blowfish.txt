Title: Blowfish (2011)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Chi Y. Lee
Main Cast: Vicci Pan, Kang Jen Wu, Lu Yi-Ching, Angel Yao, Hong Xiu Wu

Overview: A woman leaves her cheating boyfriend and goes to a remote village in the Taiwaneese mountains to deliver a blowfish to a man who won it on an online auction. Immediately, a quiet and unusual relationship develops between the two.
