Title: Zatôichi the Fugitive (1963)
Genres: Drama, Adventure, Action
Director: Tokuzô Tanaka
Main Cast: Shintarô Katsu, Miwa Takada, Masayo Banri, Katsuhiko Kobayashi, Jun'ichirô Narita
Keywords: ichi, zatoichi

Overview: After arriving in the town of Shimonita, Ichi finds that a price has been put on his head by a local yakuza boss. He's drawn into a trap, but after hearing of the slaying of a former love, Ichi furiously fights his way through the entire clan to face the killer, a hired ronin.
