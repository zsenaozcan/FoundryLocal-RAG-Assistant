Title: Archangel (1990)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance, War
Director: Guy Maddin
Main Cast: Michael Gottli, David Falkenburg, Michael O'Sullivan, Margaret Anne MacLeod, Ari Cohen
Keywords: silent cinema

Overview: A surrealistic film in which a strangely assorted group of people come together in the Russian Arctic at the height of the revolution and World War One.
