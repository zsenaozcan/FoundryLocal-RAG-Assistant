Title: The Pills - Sempre meglio che lavorare (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Luca Vecchi
Main Cast: Luigi Di Capua, Matteo Corradini, Luca Vecchi, Giancarlo Esposito, Mattia Coluccia

Overview: Three life-long friends struggle with an early middle-age crisis as they start to try to get a job for the first time. An ironic quest to avoid becoming an adult and accept responsibilities.
