Title: Gonin (1995)
Genres: Action, Crime, Foreign, Thriller
Director: Takashi Ishii
Main Cast: Koichi Sato, Masahiro Motoki, Takeshi Kitano, Jinpachi Nezu, Kippei Shiina

Overview: Five men plot to steal a large sum of money from the local yakuza, but everything does not go as planned and the men find themselves hunted down by contract killers
