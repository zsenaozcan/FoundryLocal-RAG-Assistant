Title: Torchy Blane in Panama (1938)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: William Clemens
Main Cast: Lola Lane, Paul Kelly, Tom Kennedy, Anthony Averill, Larry Williams
Keywords: panama, torchy blane

Overview: Torchy, Steve, and Gahagan are on the trail of a bank robber aboard an ocean liner traveling from New York to L.A. via the Panama Canal.
