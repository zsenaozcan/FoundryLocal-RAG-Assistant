Title: Dawn of the Mummy (1981)
Genres: Horror
Director: Frank Agrama
Main Cast: Brenda Siemer Scheider, Barry Sattels, George Peck, John Salvo, Ibrahim Khan
Keywords: mummy, gore, curse, zombie, tomb

Overview: A group of fashion models disturb the tomb of a mummy and revive an ancient curse. Along with the mummy rising, slaves who were buried in the desert thousands of years before, also rise, with a craving for human flesh.
