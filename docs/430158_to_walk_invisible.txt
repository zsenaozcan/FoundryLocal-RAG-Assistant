Title: To Walk Invisible (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sally Wainwright
Main Cast: Finn Atkins, Chloe Pirrie, Charlie Murphy, Adam Nagaitis, Jonathan Pryce
Keywords: victorian england, writer, gothic, novelist, englishwoman

Overview: To Walk Invisible takes a new look at the extraordinary Brontë family, telling the story of these remarkable women who, despite the obstacles they faced, came from obscurity to produce some of the greatest novels in the English language.
