Title: Traveling Saleslady (1935)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Ray Enright
Main Cast: Joan Blondell, Glenda Farrell, William Gargan, Hugh Herbert, Grant Mitchell
Keywords: inventor, salesman, toothpaste

Overview: In this comedy, a toothpaste magnate's mischievous daughter, tired of her father's traditional ways of conducting business, joins forces with her father's rival and a crazy inventor. Together they create "Cocktail Toothpaste." The new concoction tastes like whiskey in the morning, a martini at suppertime, and champagne at night.
