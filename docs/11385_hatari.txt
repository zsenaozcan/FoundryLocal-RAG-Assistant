Title: Hatari! (1962)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Howard Hawks
Main Cast: John Wayne, Hardy Krüger, Elsa Martinelli, Red Buttons, Gérard Blain
Keywords: africa, giraffe, elephant, safari, chase, dangerous
