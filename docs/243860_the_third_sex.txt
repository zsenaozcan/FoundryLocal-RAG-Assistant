Title: The Third Sex (1957)
Genres: Drama
Director: Veit Harlan
Main Cast: Paula Wessely, Ingrid Stenn, Christian Wolff, Friedrich Joloff, Günther Theil
Keywords: gay, gay son, gay man, lgbt

Overview: Klaus is a young man in post-war Berlin. He is drawn to his friend Manfred and, under the encouragement of their acquaintance, Dr. Winkler, explore the underground world of gay clubs and electronic music. His family begins to learn of his other life and do everything they can to set him straight.
