Title: SeaFood (2011)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Family, Adventure
Director: Aun Hoe Goh
Main Cast: Steven Bone, Colin Chong, Chi-Ren Choong, Jason Cottom, Kennie Dowle

Overview: Pup, a bamboo shark, discovers egg sacs while playing around the ocean with his friends. When he sees human poachers stealing the eggs from his reef, Pup makes it his mission to save his family. As he leaves the sea and enters the beautiful, yet dangerous, world of humans he'll prove he isn't too small to save his family. Written by Elizabeth Obermeier, Marketing Manager
