Title: Carnival (1982)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Tatyana Lioznova
Main Cast: Irina Muravyova, Yuriy Yakovlev, Klara Luchko, Aleksandr Abdulov, Vera Vasileva
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A young Russian girl is in love with show business and wants to leave her small town for Moscow and make it big in the big city. She encounters her long missing, remarried father and gets a boy friend who uses her and then leaves her.
