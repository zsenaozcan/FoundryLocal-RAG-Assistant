Title: The Phenom (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Noah Buschel
Main Cast: Johnny Simmons, Paul Giamatti, Ethan Hawke, Alison Elliott, Yul Vazquez
Keywords: baseball, sport

Overview: Major-league rookie pitcher Hopper Gibson has lost his focus. After choking on the mound, he’s sent down to the minors and prescribed sessions with an unorthodox sports therapist, who pushes him to uncover the origins of his anxiety.
