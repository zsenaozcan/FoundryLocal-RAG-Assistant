Title: Foxy Brown (1974)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Crime
Director: Jack Hill 
Main Cast: Pam Grier, Antonio Fargas, Peter Brown, Terry Carter, Kathryn Loder
Keywords: blaxploitation, drug use, prostitution, afro

Overview: A voluptuous black woman takes a job as a high-class prostitute in order to get revenge on the mobsters who murdered her boyfriend.
