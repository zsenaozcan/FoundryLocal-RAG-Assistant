Title: Playing (2007)
Genres: Drama, Documentary
Director: Eduardo Coutinho
Main Cast: Marília Pêra, Andréa Beltrão, Fernanda Torres, Aleta Gomes Vieira, Mary Sheila

Overview: Following a newspaper ad, ordinary women tell part of their life stories to director Eduardo Coutinho, which are then re-enacted by actresses, blurring the barriers between truth, fiction and interpretation.
