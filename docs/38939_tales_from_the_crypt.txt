Title: Tales from the Crypt (1972)
Genres: Horror
Director: Freddie Francis
Main Cast: Joan Collins, Peter Cushing, Roy Dotrice, Richard Greene, Ian Hendry
Keywords: santa claus, wish, immortality, supernatural, anthology, pet dog, tomb, booby trap, crypt keeper, jingle bells
