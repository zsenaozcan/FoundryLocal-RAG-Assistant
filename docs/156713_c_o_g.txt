Title: C.O.G. (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Kyle Patrick Alvarez
Main Cast: Jonathan Groff, Troian Bellisario, Corey Stoll, Dale Dickey, Dean Stockwell

Overview: A cocky young man travels to Oregon to work on an apple farm. Out of his element, he finds his lifestyle and notions being picked apart by everyone who crosses his path.
