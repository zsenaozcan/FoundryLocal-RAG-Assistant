Title: The Pursuit of Unhappiness (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sherry Hormann
Main Cast: Johanna Wokalek, Iris Berben, David Kross, Richy Müller, Benjamin Sadler
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Based on the best-selling psychology book by Paul Watzlawick, the film tells the story of Tiffany Blechschmid, a neurotic, moony, superstitious, contradictory and, no surprises here, single young woman in her late-twenties.
