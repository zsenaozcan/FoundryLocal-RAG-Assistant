Title: Duel of Champions (1961)
Genres: Adventure, History
Director: Terence Young
Main Cast: Alan Ladd, Robert Keith, Alana Ladd, Franca Bettoia, Franco Fabrizi
Keywords: ancient rome, duel

Overview: A Roman nobleman, Horatius leads an imperial legion during the long and bloody war between the Romans and the Albans.  A desperate arrangement is agreed on how to settle the war.  Three valiant brothers are chosen from each side to fight one last fierce and bloody duel...
