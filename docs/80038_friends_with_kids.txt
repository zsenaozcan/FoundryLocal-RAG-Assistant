Title: Friends with Kids (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Jennifer Westfeldt
Main Cast: Adam Scott, Jennifer Westfeldt, Jon Hamm, Maya Rudolph, Chris O'Dowd
Keywords: relationship problems, friendship, best friend, new york city, best friends in love, woman director

Overview: In the wake of their friends' marriages and eventual offspring, longtime pals Julie and Jason decide to have a child together without becoming a couple. By becoming "time-share" parents, they reason, they can experience the joys of parenthood without significantly curbing their personal freedom. However, when Julie and Jason both become involved with others, they discover that they secretly harbor romantic feelings for each other.
