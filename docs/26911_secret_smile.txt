Title: Secret Smile (2005)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Christopher Menaul
Main Cast: David Tennant, Kate Ashfield, Claire Goose, Jill Baker, John Bowe

Overview: When Miranda splits up with her creepy boyfriend Brendan because he attacked her she finds out that he is now going out with her sister Kerry and later proposes to her. Miranda warns her sister, as Brendan swims his way into their family ruining peoples lives. This drama story turns into a Ultimate Terror Twist.
