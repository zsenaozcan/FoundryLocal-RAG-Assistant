Title: Wake of the Red Witch (1948)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Edward Ludwig
Main Cast: John Wayne, Gail Russell, Gig Young, Adele Mara, Luther Adler
Keywords: based on novel, captain, fight, love, shipping magnate

Overview: Captain Ralls fights Dutch shipping magnate Mayrant Sidneye for the woman he loves, Angelique Desaix, and for a fortune in gold aboard the Red Witch.
