Title: Legend of the Galactic Heroes: Overture to a New War (1993)
Genres: Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Keizou Shimizu
Main Cast: Kei Tomiyama, Ryou Horikawa, Gorō Naya, Hiroshi Itoh, Hideyuki Tanaka

Overview: Based on a series of sci-fi novels, this series tells the story of a massive conflict between the Prussia-like Galactic Empire and the Free Planets Alliance. It describes all levels of the war, from the tactical to the strategic, but focuses most on the two oppposing militaries' leaders - Reinhard Von Lohengram and Yang Wen-Li.
