Title: Torso (1973)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Sergio Martino
Main Cast: Suzy Kendall, Tina Aumont, Luc Merenda, John Richardson, Roberto Bisacco
Keywords: nudity, swamp, psychopath, murder, serial killer, giallo, masked killer, decapitation, prostitution, black gloved killer

Overview: A series of sex murders shock a college campus, and four beautiful young girlfriends head for the safety of an isolated country villa. But as they succumb to their own erotic desires, their weekend of pleasure becomes a vacation to dismember at the hands - and blade - of the lecherous maniac.
