Title: Hornet's Nest (1970)
Genres: War, Action, Drama
Director: Phil Karlson
Main Cast: Rock Hudson, Sylva Koscina, Sergio Fantoni, Giacomo Rossi-Stuart, Jacques Sernas

Overview: An American commando who's the sole survivor of a parachute jump into WWII-era Italy leads a group of children in a campaign of sabotage against the Nazis.
