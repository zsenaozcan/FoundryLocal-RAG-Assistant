Title: Santa's Slay (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Horror
Director: David Steiman
Main Cast: Bill Goldberg, Douglas Smith, Emilie de Ravin, Robert Culp, Dave Thomas
Keywords: holiday, angel, bet, grandfather grandson relationship, mass murder, santa claus, killer santa claus, christmas horror

Overview: Santa Claus is actually a demon who lost a bet with an Angel, so he becomes the giver of toys and happiness. But when the bet is off, he returns to his evil ways.
