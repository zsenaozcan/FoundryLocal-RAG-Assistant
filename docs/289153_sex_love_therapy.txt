Title: Sex, Love & Therapy (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Tonie Marshall
Main Cast: Sophie Marceau, Patrick Bruel, Alexia Barlier, André Wilms, François Morel
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A romantic comedy about two Parisian therapists who are so obsessed about getting into the sack with anyone they meet, they can’t ever get it on with each other.
