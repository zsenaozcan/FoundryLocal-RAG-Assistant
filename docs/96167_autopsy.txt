Title: Autopsy (1975)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Armando Crispino
Main Cast: Mimsy Farmer, Barry Primus, Ray Lovelock, Carlo Cattaneo, Angela Goodwin
Keywords: murder, priest, student

Overview: A pathology med student and a priest team up to investigate a wave of suicides blamed on sun spots and discover a number of them to be actual murders.
