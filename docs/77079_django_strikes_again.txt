Title: Django Strikes Again (1987)
Genres: Western
Director: Nello Rossati
Main Cast: Franco Nero, Christopher Connelly, Licinia Lentini, William Berger, Roberto Posse
Keywords: spaghetti western, django

Overview: Former gunfighter Django has become a monk and abandoned his violent former ways. His daughter is kidnapped by rogue Hungarian soldiers using slave labor to run a silver mine. Django casts off his habit and digs up his machine gun to practice a little liberation theology.
