Title: The Hearse (1980)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Drama
Director: George Bowers
Main Cast: Trish Van Devere, Joseph Cotten, David Gautreaux, Donald Hotton, Med Flory
Keywords: haunted house, supernatural, possession, killer car, occult, ghost

Overview: Jane Hardy moves into the house left to her by her deceased aunt. After moving in Jane is haunted by a hearse following her around.
