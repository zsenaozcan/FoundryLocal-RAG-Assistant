Title: Touch and Go (1986)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Robert Mandel
Main Cast: Michael Keaton, María Conchita Alonso, Ajay Naidu, John Reilly, Maria Tucci
Keywords: bar, celebrity, hockey player, party, love, athlete, gang, threat

Overview: An ice hockey star is accosted by a youth gang who attempt to rob him; after he chases them off he catches the youngest member and gives him a ride home, where he meets the boy's mother. A romantic plot ensues, as well as a confrontation with the gang leaders.
