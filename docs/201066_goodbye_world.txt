Title: Goodbye World (2013)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Denis Hennelly
Main Cast: Caroline Dhavernas, Adrian Grenier, Ben McKenzie, Gaby Hoffmann, Mark Webber

Overview: When a mysterious cyber-attack cripples civilization, a group of old college friends and lovers retreat to a remote country cabin, where they must cope with an uncertain future while navigating the minefield of their shared past.
