Title: Student Seduction (2003)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Peter Svatek
Main Cast: Elizabeth Berkley, Corey Sevier, Rick Roberts, Sarah Allen

Overview: Christie Dawson always wanted to be the "cool" teacher. But her world is shaken up after a pupil's crush spirals out of control and he sexually assaults her. Suddenly, her colleagues, neighbors and even her hubby are wondering whether she crossed the line and tempted her student. But school isn't out for Christine just yet she's gonna fight back, and big time!
