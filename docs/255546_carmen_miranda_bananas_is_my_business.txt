Title: Carmen Miranda: Bananas Is My Business (1995)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Helena Solberg
Main Cast: Carmen Miranda, Aurora Miranda, Cesar Romero, Rita Moreno, Alice Faye
Keywords: latin, profile, woman director

Overview: A biography of the Portuguese-Brazilian singer Carmen Miranda, whose most distinctive feature was her tutti frutti hat. She came to the US as the "Brazilian Bombshell" and was a Broadway and Hollywood star in the 1940s.
