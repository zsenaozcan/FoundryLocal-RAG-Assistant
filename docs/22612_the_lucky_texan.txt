Title: The Lucky Texan (1934)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Romance, Western
Director: Robert N. Bradbury
Main Cast: John Wayne, Barbara Sheldon, Lloyd Whitlock, George 'Gabby' Hayes, Yakima Canutt

Overview: Jerry Mason, a young Texan, and Jake Benson, an old rancher, become partners and strike it rich with a gold mine. They then find their lives complicated by bad guys and a woman.
