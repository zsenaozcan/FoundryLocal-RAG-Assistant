Title: Carrie (1952)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: William Wyler
Main Cast: Laurence Olivier, Jennifer Jones, Miriam  Hopkins, Eddie Albert, Basil Ruysdael

Overview: Carrie's dreams of adventure in the big city are quickly squashed as she discovers all that awaits her there is a bleak life of grueling and poorly-paid factory work. That is, until a traveling salesman named Drouet steps into her life and changes her outlook...
