Title: Sunrise: A Song of Two Humans (1927)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: F.W. Murnau
Main Cast: George O'Brien, Janet Gaynor, Margaret Livingston, Bodil Rosing, J. Farrell MacDonald
Keywords: love triangle, adultery, lake, country life, planned murder, indian summer , marriage crisis, rural setting, storm, city

Overview: A married farmer falls under the spell of a slatternly woman from the city, who tries to convince him to drown his wife.
