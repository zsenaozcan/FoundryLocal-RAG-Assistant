Title: Cloud 9 (2014)
Genres: Action, Drama, Romance, TV Movie
Director: Paul Hoen
Main Cast: Dove Cameron, Luke Benward, Kiersey Clemons, Mike C. Manning, Dillon Lane

Overview: Set high atop snow-capped mountains in the adrenaline-fueled world of competitive snowboarding, the Disney Channel Original Movie “Cloud 9″ tells the inspiring story of two snowboarders who must overcome self-doubt to learn that achieving their dreams is possible.
