Title: EM (2008)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Tony Barbieri
Main Cast: Nathan Wetherington, Stef Willen, Sean Kaysen, Bre Blair, Jenny Gabrielle

Overview: When Josh and Amanda make eye contact at the airport, they both experience an instant attraction, and soon they're living together. But one day Josh awakens from his romantic haze to find his new life partner hospitalized for bipolar disorder.
