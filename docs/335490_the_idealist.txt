Title: The Idealist (2015)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Christina Rosendahl
Main Cast: Peter Plaugborg, Søren Malling, Arly Jover, Thomas Bo Larsen, Henrik Birch
Keywords: journalist, cold war, hydrogen bomb, based on true story, greenland, thule base
