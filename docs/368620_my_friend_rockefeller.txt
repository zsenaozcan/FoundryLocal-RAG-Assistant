Title: My Friend Rockefeller (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Steffi Kammerer
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: biography, crime, woman director

Overview: Christian Karl Gerhartsreiter lived a life of deception and crime before settling on his ultimate scam - impersonating a Rockefeller. How was Gerhartsreiter able to dupe so many people from so many walks of life? A story that begins in a Bavarian village, continues in the most exclusive clubs on the American East Coast, - and ends in a Los Angeles court.
