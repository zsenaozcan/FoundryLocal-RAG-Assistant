Title: The Fearless Four (1997)
Genres: Animation
Director: Michael Coldewey
Main Cast: Oleta Adams, Mario Adorf, Dagmar Altrichter, Peer Augustinski, Tom Deininger

Overview: Four aging animals, a dog, a cat, a donkey and a rooster escaped from their former homes because of they weren't as productive and useful for their owners as they used to be. To avoid death they all moved out to Bremen to find a better life...
