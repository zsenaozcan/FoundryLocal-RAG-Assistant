Title: Neon Maniacs (1986)
Genres: Horror
Director: Joseph Mangine
Main Cast: Clyde Hayes, Leilani Sarelle, Donna Locke, Andrew Divoff, Marta Kober
Keywords: crime

Overview: A group of teenagers in San Francisco discover a nest of homicidal monsters living in a tower of the Golden Gate Bridge, but when they try to tell authorities, no one believes them.
