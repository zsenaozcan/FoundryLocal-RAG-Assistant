Title: A White Dress for Marialé (1972)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Romano Scavolini
Main Cast: Ida Galli, Ivan Rassimov, Luigi Pistilli, Pilar Velázquez, Ezio Marano
Keywords: dream, murder, giallo

Overview: When Marile is a little girl, she sees her father murder the two lovers and then shoot himself in the head. About 30 years later, she lives in a mansion and has mental problems. She invites a number of friends and one by one they become murdered.
