Title: Act of Violence (2008)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Lars Henning Jung
Main Cast: Vinzenz Kiefer, Tobias Schenke, Anna Bertheau, Alice Dwyer, Natalie Spinell

Overview: After finishing school, a group of three guys and three girls set off to spend their last holidays together in a remote cottage. What started as a great adventure soon turns into a nightmare of explosive violence. Once you start taking the game seriously, it is not a game anymore...
