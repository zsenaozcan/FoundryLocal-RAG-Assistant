Title: Dheepan (2015)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Jacques Audiard
Main Cast: Jesuthasan Antonythasan, Kalieaswari Srinivasan, Claudine Vinasithamby, Vincent Rottiers, Faouzi Bensaïdi
Keywords: paris, france, freedom fighter, sri lanka, tamil

Overview: Dheepan is a Sri Lankan Tamil warrior who flees to France and ends up working as a caretaker outside Paris.
