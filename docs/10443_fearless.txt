Title: Fearless (1993)
Genres: Drama
Director: Peter Weir
Main Cast: Jeff Bridges, Isabella Rossellini, Rosie Perez, Tom Hulce, John Turturro
Keywords: grief, disaster, guilt, mental health

Overview: After a terrible air disaster, survivor Max Klein emerges a changed person. Unable to connect to his former life or to wife Laura, he feels godlike and invulnerable. When psychologist Bill Perlman is unable to help Max, he has Max meet another survivor, Carla Rodrigo, who is racked with grief and guilt since her baby died in the crash which she and Max survived.
