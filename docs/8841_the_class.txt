Title: The Class (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Laurent Cantet
Main Cast: François Bégaudeau, Franck Keïta, Esmeralda Ouertani, Rachel Regulier, Wei Huang
Keywords: paris, socially deprived family, literature, illegal immigration, immigrant, education, boredom, class society, violence in schools, republicanism

Overview: Teacher and novelist François Bégaudeau plays a version of himself as he negotiates a year with his racially mixed students from a tough Parisian neighborhood.
