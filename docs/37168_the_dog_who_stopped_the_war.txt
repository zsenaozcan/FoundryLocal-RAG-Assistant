Title: The Dog Who Stopped the War (1984)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Comedy, Family
Director: André Mélançon
Main Cast: Cédric Jourde, Marie-Pierre A. D'Amour, Julien Elie, Minh Vu Duc, Maryse Cartwright

Overview: During Christmas' holidays, the children of a village split in two gang to play a snowball war. But that half-tone war scattered some bitterness and make more difficult the mutual attirance between Luc, the chief of the assailant and Sophie one leader of the snow castle defenders.
