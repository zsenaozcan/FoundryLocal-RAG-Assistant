Title: Three Resurrected Drunkards (1968)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Nagisa Ōshima
Main Cast: Kazuhiko Kato, Osamu Kitayama, Norihiko Hashida, Kei Satô, Cha Dei-dang
Keywords: korea, alternative reality

Overview: Three students spend their holidays at the seaside where they are mistaken for Koreans, a minority which is looked down on in Japan. The action develops into a crime story.
