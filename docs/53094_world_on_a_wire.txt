Title: World on a Wire (1973)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Rainer Werner Fassbinder
Main Cast: Klaus Löwitsch, Barbara Valentin, Karl-Heinz Vosgerau, Wolfgang Schenck, Günter Lamprecht
Keywords: dystopia, cyberpunk

Overview: Cybernetics engineer Fred Stiller uncovers a massive corporate conspiracy involving a virtual reality computer project.
