Title: It Happened on Fifth Avenue (1947)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Roy Del Ruth
Main Cast: Don DeFore, Ann Harding, Charles Ruggles, Victor Moore, Gale Storm
Keywords: homeless person, musical, new year's day, mansion, millionaire, bum, assumed identity, homeless man

Overview: A New Yorker hobo moves into a mansion and along the way he gathers friends to live in the house with him. Before he knows it, he is living with the actual home owners.
