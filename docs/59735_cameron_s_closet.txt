Title: Cameron's Closet (1988)
Genres: Horror
Director: Armand Mastroianni
Main Cast: Cotter Smith, Mel Harris, Chuck McCann, Leigh McCloskey, Kim Lankford
Keywords: telekinesis, psychokinesis, psychic, demon, mayan

Overview: A father who experiments with his sons psychokinetic powers, is unaware that these experiments release a demon from hell, which lives in his sons closet, preparing to take over the young boys soul.
