Title: Little Accidents (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sara Colangelo
Main Cast: Elizabeth Banks, Boyd Holbrook, Chloë Sevigny, Jacob Lofland, Josh Lucas
Keywords: small town, woman director, miners, mining accident

Overview: In a small American coal town living in the shadow of a recent mining accident, the disappearance of a teenage boy draws three people together—a surviving miner, the lonely wife of a mine executive, and a local boy—in a web of secrets.
