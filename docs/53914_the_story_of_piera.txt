Title: The Story of Piera (1983)
Genres: Drama
Director: Marco Ferreri
Main Cast: Isabelle Huppert, Hanna Schygulla, Marcello Mastroianni, Angelo Infanti, Tanya Lopert
Keywords: eroticism, biography, lesbian relationship, homosexuality, incest

Overview: This sometimes confusing erotic drama about the incestuous relationship of a mother and daughter is based on the autobiography of Italian theater actress Piera Degli Esposti though it focuses more on her mother Eugenia.
