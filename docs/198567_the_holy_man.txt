Title: The Holy Man (1965)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Satyajit Ray
Main Cast: Charuprakash Ghosh, Prasad Mukherjee, Gitali Roy, Satindra Bhattacharya, Somen Bose

Overview: A wandering baba initiates a widower layer and his youngest daughter, irritating her boyfriend Satya and the ever-skeptical Nibaran.
