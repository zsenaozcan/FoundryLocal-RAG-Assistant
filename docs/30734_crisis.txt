Title: Crisis (1946)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ingmar Bergman
Main Cast: Inga Landgré, Stig Olin, Marianne Löfgren, Dagny Lind, Allan Bohlin
Keywords: piano teacher, foster daughter

Overview: A small-town piano teacher is shocked by the arrival of her foster daughter's real mother, whose young lover soon follows and causes further disruption.
