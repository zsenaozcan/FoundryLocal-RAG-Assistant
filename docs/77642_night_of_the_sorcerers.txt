Title: Night of the Sorcerers (1974)
Genres: Horror
Director: Amando de Ossorio
Main Cast: María Kosty, Simón Andreu, Kali Hansa, Loreta Tovar, José Thelman
Keywords: vampire, jungle

Overview: A group of researchers gathering material for a magazine article on endangered wildlife encounter vampiric leopard women in the heart of the African jungle.
