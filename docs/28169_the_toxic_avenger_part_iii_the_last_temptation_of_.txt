Title: The Toxic Avenger Part III: The Last Temptation of Toxie (1989)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Action, Science Fiction
Director: Michael Herz
Main Cast: Ron Fazio, John Altamura, Rick Collins, Phoebe Legere, Lisa Gaye
Keywords: nudity, satire, sequel, superhero, gore, camp, spoof, splatter

Overview: Toxie finds he has nothing to do as a superhero, as he has ridden his city of evil. So he decides to go to work for a major corporation, which he discovers may be the evilest of all his adversaries.
