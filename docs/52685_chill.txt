Title: Chill (2007)
Genres: Horror
Director: Serge Rodnunsky
Main Cast: Thomas Calabro, Ashley Laurence, Shaun Kurtz, James Russo, Victor Grant

Overview: In this classic retro horror thriller, Sam, an aspiring writer, takes a job as a clerk in an L.A. inner city grocery market owned by the sinister Dr. Munoz, a former scientist who claims to have a rare skin condition.
