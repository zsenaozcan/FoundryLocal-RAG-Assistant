Title: Year of the Devil (2002)
Genres: Music, Comedy, Documentary
Director: Petr Zelenka
Main Cast: Jaromír Nohavica, Karel Plíhal, Jan Prent, Karel Holas, František Černý
Keywords: mockumentary

Overview: Documentary maker Jan Holman follows the ultrafamous singer/songwriter Jaromír Novahica during his tour with the band Cechomor. The combination is very succesful, but it draws a heavy toll on the band members and Jan Holman.
