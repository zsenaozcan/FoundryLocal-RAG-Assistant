Title: The Lone Ranger (1956)
Genres: Adventure, Western
Director: Stuart Heisler
Main Cast: Clayton Moore, Jay Silverheels, Lyle Bettger, Bonita Granville, Perry Lopez
Keywords: marshal, dynamite, murder, native american reservation, abilene kansas

Overview: The territorial governor asks the Lone Ranger to investigate mysterious raids on settlers by Indians who ride with saddles. Wealthy rancher Reese Kilgore wants to mine silver on Spirit Mountain which is sacred to the Indians.
