Title: Summer of Blood (2014)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Onur Tukel
Main Cast: Onur Tukel, Anna Margaret Hollyman, Dakota Goldhor, Dustin Guy Defa, Melodie Sisk

Overview: Lousy lover and egocentric Eric Sparrow is dumped by his girlfriend after rejecting her marital proposal. After a few failed dates his luck turns when a chance encounter with a vampire turns him into a sex god.
