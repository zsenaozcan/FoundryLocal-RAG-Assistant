Title: Film Geek (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: James Westby
Main Cast: Melik Malkasian, Ritah Parrish, John Breen, Dennis Adkins, Tara Walker
Keywords: independent film

Overview: The story of Scotty Pelk, a socially inept video store clerk who gets fired from his job and becomes a sensation as an online film critic.
