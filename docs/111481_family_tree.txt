Title: Family tree (2010)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Olivier Ducastel
Main Cast: Guy Marchand, Françoise Fabian, Sabrina Seyvecou, Yannick Rénier

Overview: When Frédérick, the patriarch of the Alsatian Muller family, is conspicuously absent from his son Charles' funeral, Frédérick's surviving son and his granddaughter, raw from their loss, await an explanation. Once revealed, Frédérick's reasons and the painful secret Charles harbored for years threaten the foundations of the entire family.
