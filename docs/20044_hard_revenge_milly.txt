Title: Hard Revenge, Milly (2008)
Genres: Horror, Action, Thriller, Foreign
Director: Takanori Tsujimoto
Main Cast: Miki Mizuno, Hirotsugu Imamura, Mitsuki Koga, Tetsuya Nakamura, Saaya

Overview: At some point in the near future gun and weapon control laws are deregulated by public order. Asia quickly becomes the epicenter of criminal activity in the world. In Yokohama City this violence becomes so extreme that the population drops as people try to escape the criminal element that remain. When The Jack Brothers murder Milly's husband and daughter in front of her, she vows revenge.
