Title: The Kiss (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hilde Van Mieghem
Main Cast: Marie Vinck, Fedja van Huêt, Hilde Van Mieghem, Jan Decleir, Veerle Baetens
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Sarah, a beautiful and gifted 15-year-old from a wealthy but chaotic family, holds on to one dream: to run away from home and to become a professional ballet dancer.
