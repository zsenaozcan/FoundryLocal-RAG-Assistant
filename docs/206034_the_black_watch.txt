Title: The Black Watch (1929)
Genres: Drama, Adventure
Director: John Ford
Main Cast: Victor McLaglen, Myrna Loy, David Torrence, David Rollins, Cyril Chadwick

Overview: Captain Donald King of the British Army goes to India just as World War I breaks out, convincing his comrades that he is a coward. In reality, he is on a secret mission to rescue British soldiers held prisoner there.
