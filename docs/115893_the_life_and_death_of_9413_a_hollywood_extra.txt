Title: The Life and Death of 9413, a Hollywood Extra (1928)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Robert Florey
Main Cast: Jules Raucourt, George Voya, Robert Florey, Adriane Marsh
Keywords: satire, silent film, expressionist cinema

Overview: This short experimental film tells the story of a man who comes to Hollywood to become a star, only to fail and be dehumanized (he is identified by the number 9413 written on his forehead).
