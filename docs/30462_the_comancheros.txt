Title: The Comancheros (1961)
Genres: Western
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: John Wayne, Stuart Whitman, Ina Balin, Nehemiah Persoff, Lee Marvin
Keywords: renegade, texas ranger, gambler

Overview: Texas Ranger Jake Cutter arrests gambler Paul Regret, but soon finds himself teamed with his prisoner in an undercover effort to defeat a band of renegade arms merchants and thieves known as Comancheros.
