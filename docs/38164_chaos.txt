Title: Chaos (1984)
Genres: Mystery, Romance, Drama, Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Vittorio Taviani
Main Cast: Franco Franchi, Ciccio Ingrassia, Omero Antonutti, Claudio Bigagli, Massimo Bonetti

Overview: Five stories center around a werewolf, a feudal landlord, peasants, a ghost, and a mother (Margarita Lozano) and her sons.
