Title: Eline Vere (1991)
Genres: Drama
Director: Harry Kümel
Main Cast: Marianne Basler, Monique van de Ven, Johan Leysen, Thom Hoffman, Aurore Clément

Overview: This melodramatic film follows Eline Vere, as she attempts to break free from the confines of her narrow existence in The Hague through three tumultuous and ultimately disastrous courtships. Adaptation of Louis Couperus' novel Eline Vere.
