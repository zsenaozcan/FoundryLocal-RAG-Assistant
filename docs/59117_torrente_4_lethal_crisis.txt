Title: Torrente 4: Lethal crisis (2011)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Santiago Segura
Main Cast: Santiago Segura, Yon González, Ana Obregón, Maria Lapiedra, Javier Gutiérrez

Overview: In Lethal Crisis, the world's most uncouth private eye is framed for a crime he didn't commit in the riotous fourth installment of the Torrente series.
