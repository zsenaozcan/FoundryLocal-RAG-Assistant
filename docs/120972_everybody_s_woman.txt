Title: Everybody's Woman (1934)
Genres: Drama
Director: Max Ophüls
Main Cast: Isa Miranda, Memo Benassi, Tatyana Pavlova, Friedrich Benfer, Franco Coop
Keywords: suicide attempt, patriarchy, love

Overview: Gaby is expelled from school after a married teacher commits suicide after telling her he can't live without her. Though she has done nothing, she is punished for his act.
