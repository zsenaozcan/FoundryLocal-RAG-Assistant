Title: Hammy's Boomerang Adventure (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Animation
Director: Will Finn
Main Cast: Bruce Willis, Garry Shandling, Steve Carell, Wanda Sykes, Sami Kirkpatrick

Overview: RJ the raccoon produces a nature video, which turns out to be an excuse for him and the porcupine children to play pranks on Hammy the squirrel.
