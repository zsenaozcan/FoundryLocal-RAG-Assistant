Title: Escape by Night (1960)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Roberto Rossellini
Main Cast: Giovanna Ralli, Renato Salvatori, Leo Genn, Sergey Bondarchuk, Peter Baldwin

Overview: In Nazi-occupied Rome, a beautiful bootlegger, to the chagrin of her lover, gives sanctuary to three escaped POWs: an American pilot, a Russian sergeant and a British major
