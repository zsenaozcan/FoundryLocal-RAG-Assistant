Title: The Patent Leather Kid (1927)
Genres: War, Drama, Romance
Director: Alfred Santell
Main Cast: Richard Barthelmess, Molly O'Day, Lawford Davidson, Matthew Betz, Arthur Stone

Overview: The Patent Leather Kid is a 1927 silent film which tells the story of a boxer who scoffs at fighting outside the ring... particularly for the United States once it enters World War I. Eventually, he is drafted, is shipped overseas, and performs a heroic act, which results in his being severely wounded.
