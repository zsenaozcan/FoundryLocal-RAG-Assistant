Title: The Bride Comes Home (1935)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Wesley Ruggles
Main Cast: Claudette Colbert, Fred MacMurray, Robert Young, William Collier Sr., Donald Meek
Keywords: magazine, socialite

Overview: A penniless socialite is hired by two young men as a front in their plan to start a magazine. Soon, however, they find themselves more interested in her than in their publishing venture.
