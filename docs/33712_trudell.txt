Title: Trudell (2005)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Heather Rae
Main Cast: Robert Redford, Sam Shepard, Kris Kristofferson, Val Kilmer, John Trudell
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Trudell is a 2005 documentary film about the life of author and American Indian activist John Trudell. The film traces Trudell from his childhood in Omaha, Nebraska, to his role in the American Indian Movement, and finally to his rebirth as a musician and spoken word poet.
