Title: Scooby-Doo and the Ghoul School (1988)
Genres: TV Movie, Animation, Family
Director: Charles A. Nichols
Main Cast: Don Messick, Casey Kasem, Remy Auberjonois, Susan Blu, Jeff Cohen
Keywords: animation, based on tv series, cartoon dog

Overview: Scooby, Shaggy and Scrappy Doo are on their way to a Miss Grimwood's Finishing School for Girls, where they have been hired as gym teachers. Once there, however, they find that it is actually a school for girl ghouls.
