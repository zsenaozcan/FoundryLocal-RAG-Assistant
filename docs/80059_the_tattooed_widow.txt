Title: The Tattooed Widow (1998)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Lars Molin
Main Cast: Mona Malm, Gösta Bredefeldt, Erland Josephson, Jan Malmsjö, Sven Wollter

Overview: Ester is the perfect grandmother everyone expects her to be. But she unexpectedly inherits an old aunt and gets a big apartment and a lot of money. She decides to leave her husband, children and grandchildren to start her life over. Before long, she has four men who court her and she feels young once again.
