Title: Bad Kids Go To Hell (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Matthew Spradlin
Main Cast: Judd Nelson, Ben Browder, Mike Gassaway, Cameron Deane Stewart, Marc Donato
Keywords: sex, detention, based on graphic novel, preparatory school

Overview: On a stormy Saturday afternoon, six students from Crestview Academy begin to meet horrible fates as they serve out their detentions. Is a fellow student to blame, or perhaps Crestview's alleged ghosts are behind the terrible acts?
