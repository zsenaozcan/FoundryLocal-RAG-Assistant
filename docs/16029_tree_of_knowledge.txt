Title: Tree of Knowledge (1981)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nils Malmros
Main Cast: Eva Gram Schjoldager, Jan Johansen, Lone Elliot, Line Arlien-Søborg, Brian Theibel
Keywords: adolescence, teenager, young girl

Overview: As a group of Danish children pass into adolescence, gradually the most popular girl becomes an outcast, for trivial reasons that she never understands.
