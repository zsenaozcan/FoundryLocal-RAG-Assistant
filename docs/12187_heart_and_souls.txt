Title: Heart and Souls (1993)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Comedy, Science Fiction, Romance
Director: Ron Underwood
Main Cast: Robert Downey Jr., Charles Grodin, Kyra Sedgwick, Elisabeth Shue, Tom Sizemore
Keywords: afterlife

Overview: A businessman is reunited with the four lost souls who were his guardian angels during childhood, all with a particular purpose to joining the afterlife.
