Title: Learning to Drive (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Isabel Coixet
Main Cast: Patricia Clarkson, Ben Kingsley, Jake Weber, Sarita Choudhury, Grace Gummer
Keywords: woman director

Overview: As her marriage dissolves, a Manhattan writer takes driving lessons from a Sikh instructor with marriage troubles of his own. In each other's company they find the courage to get back on the road and the strength to take the wheel.
