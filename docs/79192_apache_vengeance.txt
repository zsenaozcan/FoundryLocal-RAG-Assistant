Title: Apache Vengeance (1971)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Ron Joy
Main Cast: Michele Carey, Henry Silva, Keenan Wynn, John Anderson, Joe Turkel
Keywords: female nudity, rape, apache, native american, wild west, gold theft, rape revenge

Overview: A woman tracks down the three men who raped her, helped by an Apache. Traveling through the Old West to her new home, young schoolteacher Alice McAndrew is abducted by five outlaws headed by robber Pudge Elliott. The thugs rape Alice before leaving her for dead, but she is rescued by Chatto, an Apache chief. He restores her health and aids her in her bloodthirsty quest for revenge. With her sanity wavering, will Alice be able to find the men who tortured her?
