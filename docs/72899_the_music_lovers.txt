Title: The Music Lovers (1970)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Ken Russell
Main Cast: Richard Chamberlain, Glenda Jackson, Max Adrian, Kenneth Colley, Christopher Gable
Keywords: music, historical figure, homosexuality, hipersexualidad

Overview: Composer, conductor and teacher Peter Ilych Tchaikovsky struggles against his homosexual tendencies by marrying, but unfortunately he chooses a wonky, nymphomaniac girl whom he cannot satisfy.
