Title: Silent Night, Deadly Night (1984)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Charles E. Sellier Jr.
Main Cast: Lilyan Chauvin, Gilmer McCormick, Toni Nero, Robert Brian Wilson, Britt Leach
Keywords: holiday, santa claus, boy, killer santa claus, holiday horror, christmas horror, christmas

Overview: Little Billy witnesses his parents being brutally murdered by Santa. When, years later, he has to fill in for an absent in-store Santa Claus, his traumas materialise once again.
