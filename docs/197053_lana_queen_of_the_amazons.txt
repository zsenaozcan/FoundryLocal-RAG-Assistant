Title: Lana: Queen of the Amazons (1964)
Genres: Adventure
Director: Cyll Farney
Main Cast: Anton Diffring, Catherine Schell, Christian Wolff, Michael Hinz, Yara Lex
Keywords: jungle, amazon women, jungle girl

Overview: A scientist and his nephew are hosts of Lana, queen of the Amazons, while other crew men try to find and take hold of the legendary Amazons' treasure.
