Title: The Bad Mother's Handbook (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family, Foreign, Romance
Director: Robin Sheppard
Main Cast: Catherine Tate, Robert Pattinson, Anne Reid, Holliday Grainger, Steve Pemberton
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Karen Cooper wants to domineer her family and believes she's its pillar. In fact she does everything wrong. Thus she messes up all their lives and futures, rather then help her loved-ones.
