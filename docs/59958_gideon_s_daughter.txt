Title: Gideon's Daughter (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Stephen Poliakoff
Main Cast: Bill Nighy, Miranda Richardson, Emily Blunt, Robert Lindsay, Tom Hardy
Keywords: celebrity, love, summer

Overview: Bill Nighy and Miranda Richardson star in a story of grief and celebrity, set in the intense spring and summer of New Labour's election victory and Diana's death. Nighy is a PR guru who has to stop and re-evaluate his world when his daughter threatens to leave his life, perhaps as revenge for his serial infidelities. Richardson plays a mother trying to bury her grief in an unconventional way after the loss of her young son.
