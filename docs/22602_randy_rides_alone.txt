Title: Randy Rides Alone (1934)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Harry L. Fraser
Main Cast: John Wayne, Alberta Vaughn, George 'Gabby' Hayes, Yakima Canutt, Earl Dwire
Keywords: outlaw, murder, posse, damsel in distress

Overview: Bandits lead by Matt the Mute enter a bar and kill multiple people. Randy Bowers comes to town and is framed by Matt the Mute, who is working with the sheriff (who doesn't know Matt is really a criminal). Randy escapes with the help of the niece of the dead owner of the bar. Bowers ends up running from the sheriff, and ends up in the cave in which the bandits have their hide-out…
