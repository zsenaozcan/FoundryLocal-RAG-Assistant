Title: The Secret Scripture (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jim Sheridan
Main Cast: Rooney Mara, Theo James, Vanessa Redgrave, Eric Bana, Jack Reynor
Keywords: based on novel, ireland, 1920s, 1930s

Overview: The hidden memoir of an elderly woman confined to a mental hospital reveals the history of her passionate yet tortured life, and of the religious and political upheavals in Ireland during the 1920s and 30s.
