Title: The Garden of Sinners - Chapter 1: Overlooking View (2007)
Genres: Crime, Animation, Action
Director: Ei Aoki
Main Cast: Maaya Sakamoto, Kenichi Suzumura, Takako Honda, Rie Tanaka, Ayumi Fujimura
Keywords: supernatural

Overview: When an odd string of suicides begins to occur at a local high rise building, most of the townsfolk treat it as an unfortunate series of events. One girl, however, senses something more sinister at work - especially when a close friend of hers falls mysteriously ill.
