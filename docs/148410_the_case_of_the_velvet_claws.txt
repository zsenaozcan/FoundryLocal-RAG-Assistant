Title: The Case of the Velvet Claws (1936)
Genres: Mystery, Comedy, Crime
Director: William Clemens
Main Cast: Warren William, Claire Dodd, Wini Shaw, Bill Elliott, Joe King
Keywords: honeymoon, perry mason

Overview: Perry and Della are finally married by his old friend, Judge Mary. They plan to go on a honeymoon, but before it can start, Perry is retained by a woman with a gun and $5000.
