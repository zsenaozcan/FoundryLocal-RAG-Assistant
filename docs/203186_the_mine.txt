Title: The Mine (2013)
Genres: Adventure, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Jeff Chamberlain
Main Cast: Alexa PenaVega, Reiley McClendon, Saige Thompson, Charan Prabhakar, Adam Hendershott
Keywords: sacrifice, supernatural, haunting, paranormal activity

Overview: Five school friends seek adventure on Halloween night in an abandoned, haunted mine, only to find to their horror that the ghostly rumors may be true as they fight for survival.
