Title: The Scarecrow (1972)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Boris Sagal
Main Cast: Nina Foch, Norman Lloyd, Blythe Danner, Will Geer, Gene Wilder

Overview: Just before the Salem Witch Trials, an embittered old woman, who has learned witchcraft, teams up with the Devil, and brings a scarecrow to life as part of her diabolical revenge on the judge who was once her lover.
