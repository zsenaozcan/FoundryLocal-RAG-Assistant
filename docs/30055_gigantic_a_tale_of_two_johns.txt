Title: Gigantic (A Tale Of Two Johns) (2002)
Genres: Documentary
Director: AJ Schnack
Main Cast: John Flansburgh, John Linnell

Overview: Gigantic: A Tale of Two Johns is a rock documentary about Brooklyn's finest, They Might Be Giants. Filmed in 2001, the film contains concert footage, interviews, humor, animation, coffee, humor, lyric readings, people wondering about the subject matter of their lyrics, information on our eleventh and sixteenth presidents, and much, much more.
