Title: Uncle Kent (2011)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Joe Swanberg
Main Cast: Kent Osborne, Jennifer Prediger, Josephine Decker, Joe Swanberg, Kevin Bewersdorf

Overview: A pothead kid's show cartoonist in Los Angeles spends a weekend trying to sleep with his visiting house guest - a woman from New York who he met on Chatroulette.
