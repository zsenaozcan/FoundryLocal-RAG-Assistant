Title: The Hitcher II: I've Been Waiting (2003)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Louis Morneau
Main Cast: C. Thomas Howell, Kari Wuhrer, Jake Busey, Shaun Johnston, Marty Antonini
Keywords: suspense

Overview: A sadistic serial killer terrorizes a couple driving on a rural highway in Texas while killing numerous people and framing them for his killings.
