Title: Her Best Move (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Norm Hunter
Main Cast: Leah Pipes, Scott Patterson, Lisa Darr, Drew Tyler Bell, Lalaine
Keywords: sport, soccer, teenager
