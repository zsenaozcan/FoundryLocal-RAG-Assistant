Title: The Frame (2014)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Jamin Winans
Main Cast: Christopher Soren Kelly, Cal Bartlett, Megan Heffernan, Marty Lindsey

Overview: Two strangers find their lives colliding in an impossible way. Alex is a methodical cargo thief working for a dangerous cartel. Sam is a determined paramedic trying to save the world while running from her past.
