Title: The Incubus (1982)
Genres: Horror
Director: John Hough
Main Cast: John Cassavetes, John Ireland, Helen Hughes, Kerrie Keane, Erin Noble
Keywords: rape, demon, new england

Overview: In the small New England town of Galen, a young teenage boy claims he has dreams of young women being brutally raped and murdered. A doctor and the local sheriff discover that the boy's dreams are real and that a sinister occult might be behind the brutal murders
