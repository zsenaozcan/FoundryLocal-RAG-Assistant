Title: The Mole People (1956)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Virgil W. Vogel
Main Cast: John Agar, Cynthia Patrick, Hugh Beaumont, Alan Napier, Nestor Paiva
Keywords: mushroom, expedition, earthquake, ritual, glacier, prologue, creature, archaeologist, albino, hidden civilization

Overview: A party of archaeologists discovers the remnants of a mutant five millennia-old Sumerian civilization living beneath a glacier atop a mountain in Mesopatamia.
