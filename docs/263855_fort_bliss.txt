Title: Fort Bliss (2014)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Claudia Myers
Main Cast: Michelle Monaghan, Emmanuelle Chriqui, Pablo Schreiber, Ron Livingston, Freddy Rodríguez
Keywords: war veteran, woman director, afghanistan veteran

Overview: After returning home from an extended tour in Afghanistan, a decorated U.S. Army medic and single mother struggles to rebuild her relationship with her young son.
