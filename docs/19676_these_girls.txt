Title: These Girls (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: John Hazlett
Main Cast: David Boreanaz, Caroline Dhavernas, Amanda Walsh, Holly Lewis
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Based on the humorously edgy play by Vivienne Laxdal, These Girls is the story of three teenage girls who, in turn, seduce and then attempt to share Keith, an older married man. It is a tale of innocence, jealousy and friendship told as a provocative coming-of-age story.
