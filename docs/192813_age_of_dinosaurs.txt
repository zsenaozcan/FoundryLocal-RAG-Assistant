Title: Age of Dinosaurs (2013)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Joseph J. Lawson
Main Cast: Treat Williams, Ronny Cox, Jillian Rose Reed, Joshua Michael Allen, Max Aria
Keywords: genetics, dinosaur, genetic engineering

Overview: Using breakthrough flesh-regeneration technology, a biotech firm creates a set of living dinosaurs. But when the creatures escape their museum exhibit and terrorize Los Angeles, a former firefighter must rescue his teenage daughter from the chaos brought on by the Age of Dinosaurs
