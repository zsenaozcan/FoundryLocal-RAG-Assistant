Title: Devil's Playground (2010)
Genres: Horror, Action
Director: Mark McQueen
Main Cast: MyAnna Buring, Danny Dyer, Craig Fairbrass, Sean Pertwee, Colin Salmon
Keywords: mercenary, zombie apocalypse

Overview: As the world succumbs to a zombie apocalypse, Cole - a hardened mercenary - is chasing the one person who can provide a cure. Not only to the plague but to Cole's own incumbent destiny.
