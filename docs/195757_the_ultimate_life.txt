Title: The Ultimate Life (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Michael Landon Jr.
Main Cast: Peter Fonda, Bill Cobbs, Ali Hillis, Lee Meriwether, Logan Bartholomew
Keywords: hope, inspiration, feel-good ending, inspiring

Overview: Despite his best intentions, billionaire Jason Stevens can’t find enough time to keep his beloved Alexia a priority. But when he discovers his late grandfather’s journal, he is transported back to Red Stevens’ incredible world. With everything he loves hanging in the balance, Jason Stevens hopes the past will prepare him well for the future.
