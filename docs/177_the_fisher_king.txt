Title: The Fisher King (1991)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Terry Gilliam
Main Cast: Jeff Bridges, Robin Williams, Mercedes Ruehl, Amanda Plummer, Michael Jeter
Keywords: holy grail, homeless person, loss of lover, mental disease, sense of guilt, suppressed past, yuppie, cynic, fantasy, self-discovery

Overview: Two troubled men face their terrible destinies and events of their past as they join together on a mission to find the Holy Grail and thus to save themselves.
