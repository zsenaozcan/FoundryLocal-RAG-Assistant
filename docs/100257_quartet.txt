Title: Quartet (1948)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ken Annakin
Main Cast: W. Somerset Maugham, Basil Radford, Naunton Wayne, Mai Zetterling, Ian Fleming

Overview: Somerset Maugham introduces four of his tales in this anthology film: "The Facts of Life," "The Alien Corn," "The Kite," and "The Colonel's Lady."
