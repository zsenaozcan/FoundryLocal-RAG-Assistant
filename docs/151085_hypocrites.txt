Title: Hypocrites (1915)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lois Weber
Main Cast: Myrtle Stedman, Courtenay Foote, Herbert Standing, Adele Farrington, A.D. Blake
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The story of St. Gabriel, who was killed by an ignorant mob for making a nude statue representing Purity, who is also represented by a ghostly naked girl that flits through the film.
