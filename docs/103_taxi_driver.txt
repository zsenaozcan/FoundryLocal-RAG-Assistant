Title: Taxi Driver (1976)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Martin Scorsese
Main Cast: Robert De Niro, Cybill Shepherd, Harvey Keitel, Jodie Foster, Peter Boyle
Keywords: vietnam veteran, taxi, obsession, drug dealer, night shift, vigilante, alienation, misanthrope, shot to death, illegal prostitution

Overview: A mentally unstable Vietnam War veteran works as a night-time taxi driver in New York City where the perceived decadence and sleaze feeds his urge for violent action, attempting to save a preadolescent prostitute in the process.
