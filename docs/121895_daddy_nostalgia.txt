Title: Daddy Nostalgia (1990)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bertrand Tavernier
Main Cast: Dirk Bogarde, Odette Laure, Jane Birkin, Emmanuelle Bataille, Charlotte Kady
Keywords: french riviera, father daughter relationship

Overview: A half-French, half-British writer (Jane Birkin) learns her father (Dirk Bogarde), who neglected her when she was young, has been taken seriously ill and goes to visit him and her mother (Odette Laure).
