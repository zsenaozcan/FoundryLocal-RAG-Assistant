Title: Rebound: The Legend of Earl 'The Goat' Manigault (1996)
Genres: Drama
Director: Eriq La Salle
Main Cast: Don Cheadle, James Earl Jones, Daryl Mitchell, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Eriq La Salle
Keywords: sport

Overview: A dramatization of the life of Earl 'The Goat' Manigault (Don Cheadle), with a lot of factual based occurrences. A reformed junkie returns from prison to clean up his act and devote the rest of his life to the young kids of Harlem. 1996 was the 25th anniversary of the first tournament named after him.
