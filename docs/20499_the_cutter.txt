Title: The Cutter (2005)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Thriller
Director: William Tannen
Main Cast: Chuck Norris, Joanna Pacula, Daniel Bernhardt, Bernie Kopell, Todd Jensen
Keywords: shootout, diamond cutter, shoulder holster

Overview: A former cop turned Los Angeles P.I. takes on a case of a missing diamond cutter that leads him on an adventure of love and villainy spanning of mob to the present day Jewelry District.
