Title: Freedom Strikes a Blow (1973)
Genres: Action
Director: Ta Huang
Main Cast: Michael Chan Wai-Man, Jackie Chan, Yuen Biao, Corey Yuen Kwai, Chung Fat
Keywords: kung fu

Overview: A martial arts fighter, haunted by his past, takes a job as a dock worker in a small village. His vow never to fight again is tested by the cruel owner of the pier.
