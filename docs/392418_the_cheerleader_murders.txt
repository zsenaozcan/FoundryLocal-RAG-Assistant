Title: The Cheerleader Murders (2017)
Genres: TV Movie, Thriller
Director: David Jackson
Main Cast: Amanda Leighton, Samantha Boscarino, Hannah Kasulka, Austin Lyon, Irene Chiang
Keywords: cheerleader

Overview: A high school student whose father and sister were recently murdered begins her own investigation after two of her fellow cheerleaders are kidnapped. She sets out on a rescue mission, but the killer may already be hot on her trail.
