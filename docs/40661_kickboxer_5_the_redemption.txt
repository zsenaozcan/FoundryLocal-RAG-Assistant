Title: Kickboxer 5: The Redemption (1995)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Kristine Peterson
Main Cast: Mark Dacascos, James Ryan, Geoff Meed, Tony Caprari, Greg Latter
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Kickboxing champion Matt Reeves witnesses the murder of his friend Johnny. While investigating the crime, he learns that a Mr. Negaal in South Africa has founded a new kickboxing association and does not leave international champions much of a chance. Johnny had to pay with his life when he did not want to join. Neegal seems to kill everybody who is in his way especially David Sloan from the previous Kickboxer films. Matt is on his own now, but suddenly finds an unexpected ally.
