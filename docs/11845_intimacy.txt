Title: Intimacy (2001)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Patrice Chéreau
Main Cast: Mark Rylance, Kerry Fox, Timothy Spall, Alastair Galbraith, Marianne Faithfull
Keywords: hotel room, blow job, bartender, unsimulated sex, new french extremism

Overview: Jay, a failed musician, walked out of his family and now earns a living as head bartender in a trendy London pub. Every Wednesday afternoon a woman comes to his house for graphic, almost wordless, sex. One day Jay follows her and finds out about the rest of her life (and that her name is Claire). This eventually disrupts their relationship.
