Title: Gourmet Detective: A Healthy Place to Die (2015)
Genres: Drama, TV Movie, Mystery, Crime
Director: Scott Smith
Main Cast: Dylan Neal, Brooke Burns, Steve Valentine, Matthew Kevin Anderson, Marc Senior

Overview: When Henry is invited as a guest speaker at a luxury resort spa, he quickly finds himself at the center of another murder. With Maggie at his side, our favorite duo must not only find the killer but be careful they don't become his latest victim.
