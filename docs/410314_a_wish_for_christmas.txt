Title: A Wish for Christmas (2016)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Romance
Director: Christie Will
Main Cast: Lacey Chabert, Paul Greene, Andrea Brooks, Colleen Wheeler
Keywords: holiday, tv movie, woman director

Overview: Sara Shaw is the type of woman who prefers to sit on the sidelines at work, but when her big idea for a Christmas initiative is stolen, she makes a wish to Santa that she'll finally have the courage to stand up for herself. Santa grants her wish, but only gives her 48 hours. As the clock ticks, Sara will discover how to channel the Christmas magic and speak her mind all on her own.
