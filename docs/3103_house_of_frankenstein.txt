Title: House of Frankenstein (1944)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Erle C. Kenton
Main Cast: Boris Karloff, J. Carrol Naish, Lon Chaney Jr., Elena Verdugo, John Carradine
Keywords: dracula, frankenstein

Overview: An evil scientist and a hunchback escape from prison and encounter Dracula, the Wolf Man and Frankenstein's Monster.
