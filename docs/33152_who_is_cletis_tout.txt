Title: Who Is Cletis Tout? (2001)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Chris Ver Wiel
Main Cast: Christian Slater, Tim Allen, Portia de Rossi, Richard Dreyfuss, Billy Connolly
Keywords: dark comedy

Overview: A comedy about a hitman who sees everything in terms of the movies and how mistaken identity is getting in the way of recovering a stash of diamonds that was stolen and subsequently hidden more than 20 years earlier.
