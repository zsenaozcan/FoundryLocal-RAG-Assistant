Title: 50 Children: The Rescue Mission of Mr. and Mrs. Kraus (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Steven Pressman
Main Cast: Alan Alda, Mamie Gummer

Overview: In the spring of 1939, Gilbert and Eleanor Kraus embarked on a risky and unlikely mission. Traveling into the heart of Nazi Germany, they rescued 50 Jewish children from Vienna and brought them to the United States.
