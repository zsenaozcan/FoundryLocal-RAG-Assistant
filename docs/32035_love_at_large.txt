Title: Love at Large (1990)
Genres: Comedy, Mystery, Romance, Thriller
Director: Alan Rudolph
Main Cast: Tom Berenger, Elizabeth Perkins, Anne Archer, Kate Capshaw, Annette O'Toole
Keywords: jealousy, private detective

Overview: Vampish miss Dolan hires hardboiled P.I. Harry Dobbs to tail her shady boyfriend. Harry realizes that the man leads a double life but then his client disappears. Harry teams up with his own tail, P.I. Stella Wynkowski, to clear things up.
