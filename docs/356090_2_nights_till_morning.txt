Title: 2 Nights Till Morning (2015)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Mikko Kuparinen
Main Cast: Marie-Josée Croze, Mikko Nousiainen, Arly Jover, Andrius Ziurauskas

Overview: Caroline, a French architect in Vilnius, Lithuania, on business, has to spend an extra night in the city due to a delayed work meeting. At the bar of her hotel she meets Jaakko, a Finnish DJ who is in Vilnius for a gig. Caroline lets him understand that she doesn’t speak English, and Jaakko doesn’t speak French. Although they lack a common language, they find themselves enjoying each other’s company...
