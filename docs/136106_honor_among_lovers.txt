Title: Honor Among Lovers (1931)
Genres: Drama
Director: Dorothy Arzner
Main Cast: Claudette Colbert, Fredric March, Monroe Owsley, Charles Ruggles, Ginger Rogers
Keywords: secretary, woman director

Overview: A businessman (Fredric March) falls for his secretary (Claudette Colbert), but she marries a shady character (Monroe Owsley) who causes trouble for both of them.
