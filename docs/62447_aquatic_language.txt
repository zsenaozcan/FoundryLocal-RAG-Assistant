Title: Aquatic Language (2002)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Yasuhiro Yoshiura
Main Cast: Rina Matsufuji, Yasuhiro Yoshiura

Overview: Several people are spending their evening in a café. Two gossiping girls, two debating men, a bookworm and a boy, who just broke up with his girlfriend. The café's waitress starts a chat with him, persuading him to take a look at certain easel, while other customers begin to notice unusual things.
