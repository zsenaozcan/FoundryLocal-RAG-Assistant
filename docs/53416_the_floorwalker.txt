Title: The Floorwalker (1916)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Charlie Chaplin
Main Cast: Charlie Chaplin, Eric Campbell, Edna Purviance, Lloyd Bacon, Albert Austin
Keywords: slapstick

Overview: Chaplin, in his traditional Tramp persona, is a customer who creates chaos in a department store and becomes inadvertently entangled in the nefarious scheme of the store manager and the store's floorwalker to embezzle money from the establishment.
