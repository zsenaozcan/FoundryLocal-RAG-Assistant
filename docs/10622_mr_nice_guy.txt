Title: Mr. Nice Guy (1997)
Genres: Crime, Action, Comedy
Director: Sammo Hung
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Richard Norton, Miki Lee, Gabrielle Fitzpatrick, Karen McLymont
Keywords: journalist, martial arts, cook, drug dealer

Overview: A Chinese chef accidentally gets involved with a news reporter who filmed a drug bust that went awry and is now being chased by gangs who are trying to get the video tape.
