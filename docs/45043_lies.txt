Title: Lies (1999)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jang Sun-Woo
Main Cast: Sang Hyun Lee, Kim Tae-yeon, Hyun Joo Choi, Kwon Taek Han
Keywords: age difference, erotic movie

Overview: A story about the bizarre sexual relations of 38-year-old married man J and 18-year-old student Y. After an initial encounter, they embark on a sexual odyssey that visits the realms of obsession and sadomasochism.
