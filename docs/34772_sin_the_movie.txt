Title: Sin: The Movie (2000)
Genres: Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Yasunori Urata
Main Cast: Kaori Yamagata, Kikuko Inoue, Kouji Ishii, Ayako Kawasumi, Hiroya Ishimaru
Keywords: cyberpunk, based on video game

Overview: It is the near future, and the city of Freeport teeters on the verge of total collapse. The twin tides of rampant crime and ruinous graft face only one barrier: the elite strike force HARDCORPS. Led by Colonel John Blade, HARDCORPS is the last force for justice in the darkening city.
