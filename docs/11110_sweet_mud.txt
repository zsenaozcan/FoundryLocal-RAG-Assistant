Title: Sweet Mud (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Dror Shaul
Main Cast: Tomer Steinhof, Ronit Yudkevitz, Henri Garcin, Shai Avivi, Gal Zaid
Keywords: suicide, israel, sex, becoming an adult, new love, grandmother grandson relationship, psychological stress, bird, tree, love letter

Overview: Set in mid-70's, 12-year old Dvir Avni navigates between the equality values of his home-born Kibbutz and the relationship with his undermined mother, whom the Kibbutz members will to denounce.
