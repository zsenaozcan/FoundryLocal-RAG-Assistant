Title: My Father and My Son (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Çağan Irmak
Main Cast: Çetin Tekindor, Fikret Kuskan, Binnur Kaya, Özge Özberk, Tuba Buyukustun

Overview: A Turkish man, whose wife died while giving birth to his son during a military coup, finally returns home. Estranged from his father for turning his back on the family farm, he takes his 8 year-old and tries to repair relations.
