Title: Babylon XX (1979)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ivan Mykolaichuk
Main Cast: Ivan Mykolaichuk, Lyubov Polishchuk, Les Serdyuk, Yaroslav Gavrilyuk, Taisiya Litvinenko

Overview: The small town of Babylon in Ukraine hears of the recent Russian Revolution, but attempts to communize it are met with resistance from the rich people living in the town.
