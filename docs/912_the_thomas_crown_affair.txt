Title: The Thomas Crown Affair (1968)
Genres: Romance, Crime, Thriller, Drama
Director: Norman Jewison
Main Cast: Steve McQueen, Faye Dunaway, Paul Burke, Jack Weston, Biff McGuire
Keywords: in love with enemy, hold-up robbery, boredom, insurance salesman, business man, insurance fraud, plan, heist, theft, millionaire

Overview: Young businessman, Thomas Crown is bored and decides to plan a robbery and assigns a professional agent with the right information to the job. However, Crown is soon betrayed yet cannot blow his cover because he’s in love.
