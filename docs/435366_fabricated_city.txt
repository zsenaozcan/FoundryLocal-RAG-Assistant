Title: Fabricated City (2017)
Genres: Action
Director: Park Kwang-hyun
Main Cast: Ji Chang-wook, Shim Eun-kyung, Ahn Jae-hong, Oh Jung-se, Kim Sang-ho
Keywords: video game, murder, gamer

Overview: In real life, Kwon Yoo is unemployed, but in the virtual game world he is the best leader. Kwon Yoo is then framed for a murder. With the help of hacker Yeo-Wool, he tries to uncover the truth behind the murder case.
