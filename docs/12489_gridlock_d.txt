Title: Gridlock'd (1997)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Comedy
Director: Vondie Curtis-Hall
Main Cast: Tupac Amaru Shakur, Tim Roth, Thandie Newton, Charles Fleischer, Howard Hesseman
Keywords: aids, drug addiction, suspicion of murder, police, escape, withdrawal

Overview: After a friend overdoses, Spoon and Stretch decide to kick their drug habits and attempt to enroll in a government detox program. Their efforts are hampered by seemingly endless red tape, as they are shuffled from one office to another while being chased by drug dealers and the police.
