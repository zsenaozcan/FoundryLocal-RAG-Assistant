Title: Buck (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Cindy Meehl
Main Cast: Buck Brannaman, Robert Redford, Scarlett Johansson
Keywords: horseback riding, training, biography, duringcreditsstinger, woman director

Overview: An examination of the life of acclaimed 'horse whisperer' Buck Brannaman, who recovered from years of child abuse to become a well-known expert in the interactions between horses and people.
