Title: Mariachi Gringo (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Tom Gustafson
Main Cast: Shawn Ashmore, Martha Higareda, Kate Burton, Adriana Barraza, Fernando Becerril

Overview: A stifled, small-town man stuck in a dead end life, runs away to Mexico to be a mariachi singer. MARIACHI GRINGO is a musical tour-de-force exploring the reality of "following your dreams" across cultural, personal, social and geographical borders.
