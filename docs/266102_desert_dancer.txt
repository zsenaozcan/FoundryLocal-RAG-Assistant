Title: Desert Dancer (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Richard Raymond
Main Cast: Freida Pinto, Reece Ritchie, Tom Cullen, Nazanin Boniadi, Marama Corlett
Keywords: dance, ban from profession, biography, iran, oppression, tehran iran
