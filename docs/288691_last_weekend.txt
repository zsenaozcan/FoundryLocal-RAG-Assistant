Title: Last Weekend (2014)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Tom Dolby
Main Cast: Patricia Clarkson, Zachary Booth, Joseph Cross, Rutina Wesley, Julio Oscar Mechoso

Overview: When an affluent matriarch gathers her dysfunctional family for a holiday at their Northern California lake house, her carefully constructed weekend begins to come apart at the seams, leading her to question her own role in the family.
