Title: The Scenesters (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Todd Berger
Main Cast: Sherilyn Fenn, Blaise Miller, Suzanne May

Overview: Someone is killing beautiful young Hipsters in East L.A. Charlie is a crime scene cleaner working for Aftershocks Inc. with a penchant for Sherlock Holmes type deduction. Wallace is an out-of-luck filmmaker working as a crime scene videographer. The detectives covering the murders are apathetic at best but with good names (Henry and Carlita, particularly Carlita). Good names, bad attitudes.
