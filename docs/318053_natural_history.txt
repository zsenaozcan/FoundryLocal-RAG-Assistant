Title: natural history (2014)
Genres: Unknown
Director: James Benning
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: In 54 static shots, Benning provides a picture of the Natural History Museum in Vienna. With the exception of the first three shots, he chooses areas that are normally closed to the public: offices, store rooms and empty corridors. A characteristically meditative Benning, even though this time he chooses to stay indoors.
