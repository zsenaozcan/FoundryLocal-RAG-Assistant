Title: What Love Is (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Mars Callahan
Main Cast: Cuba Gooding Jr., Matthew Lillard, Sean Astin, Terrence 'T.C.' Carson, Anne Heche

Overview: It's Valentine's Day and Tomhas big plans. He's about to get engaged and has a surprise party planned to share the good news with friends. But things take a turn for the worse. Tom returns home only to discover his girlfriend has packed up and left him. Now, instead of a surprise party, the surprise is on Tom. Fortunately, his friends are there to help him make sense of it all.
