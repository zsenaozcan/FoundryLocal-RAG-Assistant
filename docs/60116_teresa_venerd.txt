Title: Teresa Venerdì (1941)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Vittorio De Sica
Main Cast: Vittorio De Sica, Adriana Benetti, Anna Magnani

Overview: A comedy of errors in which the sweetly incompetent Dr. Pietro Vignali (de Sica) has been run deep into debt by his girlfriend, Loletta Prima (Magnani). After his creditors threaten to sell his belongings, he takes a job as an orphanage health inspector to pay his debts and ends up engaged to wealthy Lilli, daughter of a mattress tycoon, and chased after by the orphan Teresa Venerdí, while trying to keep his life in order.
