Title: Jungle Captive (1945)
Genres: Horror
Director: Harold Young
Main Cast: Otto Kruger, Vicky Lane, Amelita Ward, Phil Brown, Jerome Cowan
Keywords: mad doctor, ape, paula dupree

Overview: Once again Paula ape woman is brought back to life, this time by a mad doctor and his disfigured assistant, who also kidnaps a nurse in order to have a female blood donor. By this time, Paula's been brain damaged by experiences in the last film, so there's not much for her to do except wander around.
