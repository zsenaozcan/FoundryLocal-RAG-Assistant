Title: White Coffin (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Action
Director: Daniel De La Vega
Main Cast: Julieta Cardinali, Rafael Ferro, Eleonora Wexler, Fiorela Duranda, Verónica Intile
Keywords: desperation, kidnapping, ritual, brutal

Overview: Virginia will do the impossible in order to rescue her little daughter who has been abducted. After a terrible accident, she has the chance to live one day more to save her. On her path, she will find out that there are worst thing than death.
