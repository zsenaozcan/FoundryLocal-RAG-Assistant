Title: Krakatoa, East of Java (1969)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Thriller
Director: Bernard L. Kowalski
Main Cast: Maximilian Schell, Diane Baker, Barbara Werle, Brian Keith, Marc Lawrence
Keywords: disaster

Overview: A team of maritime salvage workers are about to embark on a recovery dive. However the 1883 Krakatoa Volcano eruption provides more pressing problems.
