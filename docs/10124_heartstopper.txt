Title: Heartstopper (2006)
Genres: Horror
Director: Bob Keen
Main Cast: Nathan Stephenson, James Binkley, Robert Englund, Meredith Henderson, Michael Cram
Keywords: autopsy, wheelchair user   , medical examiner, serial killer, hospital

Overview: Two hospitalized young people discover that things can get even worse when the dilapidated institution is stranded by a severe storm and a maniac stalks the corridors butchering the patients and staff.
