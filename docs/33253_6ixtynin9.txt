Title: 6ixtynin9 (1999)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Thriller
Director: Pen-Ek Ratanaruang
Main Cast: Lalita Panyopas, Sirisin Siripornsmathikul, Prompop Lee, Tasanawalai Ongartittichai, Jaran 'See Tao' Petcharoen
Keywords: airport, drug dealer, buddhist, mistaken identity, money, escape, serial killer, old man, crying, break-up
