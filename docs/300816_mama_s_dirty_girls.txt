Title: Mama's Dirty Girls (1974)
Genres: Thriller, Crime
Director: John Hayes
Main Cast: Gloria Grahame, Paul Lambert, Sondra Currie, Candice Rialson, Christopher Lofton

Overview: Mama loves men, but she loves money even more. She's trained her three teenager daughters to meet, marry and murder men for their money. But soon the meet Harold and he got other plans.
