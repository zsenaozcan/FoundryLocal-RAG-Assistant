Title: On Earth as It Is in Heaven (1995)
Genres: Comedy
Director: José Luis Cuerda
Main Cast: Francisco Rabal, Fernando Fernán Gómez, Jesús Bonilla, Luis Ciges, Agustín González
Keywords: spain, bible, heaven, rural setting, apocalypse

Overview: Matacanes dies and goes to heaven just to discover that it was not as he expected, and everyone there is revolutionized lately. Peter explains that God was concerned about the progress of the world, and decided to send a second son to Earth. However, Jesus hears and does not agree, as they would have to rewrite history.
