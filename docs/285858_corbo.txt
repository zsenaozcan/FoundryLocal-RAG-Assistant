Title: Corbo (2014)
Genres: Crime, Drama, History
Director: Mathieu Denis
Main Cast: Anthony Therrien, Antoine L'Écuyer, Karelle Tremblay, Tony Nardi, Marie Brassard
Keywords: terrorist, political activism, quebec, flq, october crisis, 1960s

Overview: A teenage Quebecer in the 1960s evolves from pro-independence activist to radical terrorist, in this gripping chronicle of the origins of the FLQ in the decade preceding the 1970 October Crisis.
