Title: Leonard Cohen: I'm Your Man (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Lian Lunson
Main Cast: Leonard Cohen, Martha Wainwright, Rufus Wainwright, Beth Orton, Jarvis Cocker
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Poet, singer / songwriter and ladies man Leonard Cohen is interviewed in his home about his life and times. The interview is interspersed with archive photos and exuberant praise and live perfomances from an eclectic mix of musicians, including: Jarvis Cocker, Rufus &amp; Martha Wainwright, Teddy Thompson, Antony Hegarty, The Handsome Family and U2's Bono and The Edge.
