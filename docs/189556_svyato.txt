Title: Svyato (2005)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Victor Kossakovsky
Main Cast: Svyatoslav Kossakovsky

Overview: In Russian, "Svyato" means "happy". But it is also a nickname for Svyatoslav, the son of director Kossakovsky, who for two years covered mirrors from Svyato. For the first time in his life, Svyato is going to watch himself on a mirror.
