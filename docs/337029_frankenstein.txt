Title: Frankenstein (2015)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Bernard Rose
Main Cast: Carrie-Anne Moss, Danny Huston, Xavier Samuel, Tony Todd, Mckenna Grace
Keywords: frankenstein

Overview: After he is artificially created, then left for dead by a husband-and-wife team of eccentric scientists, Adam is confronted with nothing but aggression and violence from the world around him. This perfect creation-turned disfigured monster must come to grips with the horrific nature of humanity.
