Title: One Hundredth of a Second (2006)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Susan Jacobson
Main Cast: Heather Cameron-McLintock, Emma Cleasby, Richard Dillane, Luciano Dodero, Julie Legrand
Keywords: woman director
