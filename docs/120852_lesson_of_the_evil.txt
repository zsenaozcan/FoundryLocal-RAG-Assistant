Title: Lesson of the Evil (2012)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Takashi Miike
Main Cast: Hideaki Ito, Takayuki Yamada, Takehiro Hira, Mitsuru Fukikoshi, Shota Sometani
Keywords: high school, serial killer, student

Overview: A popular high school teacher concocts an extreme plan to deal with the rise of bullying and bad behavior among the student body.
