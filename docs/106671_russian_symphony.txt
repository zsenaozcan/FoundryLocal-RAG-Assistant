Title: Russian Symphony (1994)
Genres: Drama
Director: Konstantin Lopushanskiy
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: The protagonist finds out that some children were left behind in a sinking school, and is slowly driven mad as he tries to save them. A parable on the theme of the Last Judgment, numerous catastrophic events reveal a certain ambiguity in their origins, accompanied by the terrible suspicion that the things going on are some kind of a performance or theatrical production.
