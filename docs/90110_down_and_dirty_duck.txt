Title: Down and Dirty Duck (1974)
Genres: Animation, Comedy
Director: Charles Swenson
Main Cast: Howard Kaylan, Mark Volman, Robert Ridgely, Cynthia Adler, Walker Edmiston
Keywords: adult animation, anti establishment

Overview: Willard, a mild mannered insurance adjuster, teams up with a foul-mouthed fowl who takes Willard on a surreal quest to become less uptight - and possibly get laid in the process.
