Title: A Day in the Country (1936)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Jean Renoir
Main Cast: Sylvia Bataille, Jane Marken, Georges D'Arnoux, Jacques B. Brunius, André Gabriello
Keywords: flash forward, rowboat, fishing poll

Overview: The family of a Parisian shop-owner spends a day in the country. The daughter falls in love to a man at the inn, where they spend the day.
