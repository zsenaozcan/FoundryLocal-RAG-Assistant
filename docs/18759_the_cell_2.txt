Title: The Cell 2 (2009)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Tim Iacofano
Main Cast: Tessie Santiago, Chris Bruno, Frank Whaley, Bart Johnson, Michael Flynn
Keywords: amnesia, suspense, serial killer, psychic, investigator, shot in the face

Overview: The Cusp is a serial killer who kills his victims and then brings them back to life; over and over again; until they beg to die! Maya is a psychic investigator who gained her powers after a 1 year coma after she was the Cusp's first victim! Now the Cusp Killer is back and Maya has little time to do what she has never done before: go into the mind of a killer unprotected and save his latest victim.
