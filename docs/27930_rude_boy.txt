Title: Rude Boy (1980)
Genres: Music
Director: Jack Hazan
Main Cast: Joe Strummer, Mick Jones, Topper Headon, Jimmy Pursey, Ray Gange
Keywords: punk rock, music band

Overview: Rude Boy is a semi-documentary, part character study, part 'rockumentary', featuring a British punk band, The Clash. The script includes the story of a fictional fan juxtposed with actual public events of the day, including political demonstrations and Clash concerts.
