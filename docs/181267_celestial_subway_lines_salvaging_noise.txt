Title: Celestial Subway Lines/Salvaging Noise (2005)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ken Jacobs
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: In Celestial Subway Lines/Salvaging Noise its story is told by director Ken Jacobs but without the conventional storyline. Using a modified magic lantern, an early type of image projector developed in the 17th century, he morphs, flickering images that look like photo-negatives.
