Title: Southie (1998)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller
Director: John Shea
Main Cast: Donnie Wahlberg, Rose McGowan, Anne Meara, Will Arnett, Amanda Peet
Keywords: boston, mafia, southie

Overview: South Boston Irish bad boy Danny Quinn returns back home from New York and gets stuck between his pals, who are supported by one Irish mafia clan, and his family, which are members of another.
