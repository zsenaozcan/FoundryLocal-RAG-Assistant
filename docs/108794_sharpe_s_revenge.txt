Title: Sharpe's Revenge (1997)
Genres: Action, Adventure, History
Director: Tom Clegg
Main Cast: Sean Bean, Daragh O'Malley, Abigail Cruttenden, Philip Whitchurch, Cecile Paoli

Overview: Sharpe is framed as the thief who stole Napolean's gold, and he must clear his name to avoid execution. Meanwhile his wife Jane - urged on by a friend - makes some questionable choices.
