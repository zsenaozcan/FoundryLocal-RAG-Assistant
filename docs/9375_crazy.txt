Title: Crazy (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Hans-Christian Schmid
Main Cast: Robert Stadlober, Tom Schilling, Oona-Devi Liebich, Julia Hummer, Dagmar Manzel
Keywords: puberty, bodily disabled person, friendship, boarding school, teenage crush

Overview: Benjamin is a 16 year old half-side spastic with lousy grades in math, who switches to a boarding school to reach grammar school. Acclimatization to the new environment is difficult in the first place and he has to struggle with the various problems of coming of age, when he falls in love to the school's dream girl Malen.
