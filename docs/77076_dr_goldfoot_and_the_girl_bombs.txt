Title: Dr. Goldfoot and the Girl Bombs (1966)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Mario Bava
Main Cast: Vincent Price, Fabian, Franco Franchi, Ciccio Ingrassia, Francesco Mulé
Keywords: robot, scientist

Overview: The foppish mad scientist Dr. Goldfoot (Vincent Price) plots another mad scheme to take over the world by killing off the major military leaders of every country; to that end, he creates in his secret lab a bevy of bodacious girl bombs; full-length, life-size robots that explode when embraced.
