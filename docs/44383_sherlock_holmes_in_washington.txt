Title: Sherlock Holmes in Washington (1943)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Roy William Neill
Main Cast: Basil Rathbone, Nigel Bruce, Marjorie Lord, Henry Daniell, George Zucco
Keywords: sherlock holmes

Overview: In World War II, a British secret agent carrying a vitally important document is kidnapped en route to Washington. The British government calls on Sherlock Holmes to recover it.
