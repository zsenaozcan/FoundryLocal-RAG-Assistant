Title: The Organizer (1963)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Mario Monicelli
Main Cast: Marcello Mastroianni, Renato Salvatori, Gabriella Giorgelli, Folco Lulli, Bernard Blier
Keywords: italy, factory, solidarity, strike, misery, prostitution, turin italy, sweatshop, socialists, turn of the century

Overview: Professor Sinigaglia (Marcello Mastroianni) is the title character in this darkly comedic Italian drama about mistreated factory workers in the city of Turin. Toiling away in appalling conditions in a textile mill, these employees have no one to stand up for them until Sinigaglia puts his academic career on the line by helping them to start a strike. Although the teacher comes under scrutiny by unsympathetic authorities, he maintains his dedication to the workers' cause.
