Title: The Slumber Party Massacre (1982)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Amy Holden Jones
Main Cast: Michelle Michaels, Robin Stille, Michael Villella, Debra Deliso, Andree Honore
Keywords: female nudity, sleepover, cannabis, neighbor, serial killer, slumber party, slasher, drill, woman director, murder spree

Overview: An 18-year-old high school girl is left at home by her parents and decides to have a slumber party. Meanwhile, a mass murderer with a propensity for power tools has escaped from prison, and eventually makes his way to the party where the guests begin dropping off one by one.
