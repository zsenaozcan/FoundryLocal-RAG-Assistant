Title: Carlin on Campus (1984)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary, TV Movie
Director: Steven J. Santos
Main Cast: George Carlin
Keywords: concert, one-man show, made for cable tv, stand-up comedy, adult humor, tv special

Overview: George Carlin hits the boards with the former Hippie-Dippie Weatherman's take on Brooklynese pronunciations of the names of sexually transmitted disease ("hoipes"), plus a prayer for the separation of church and state, feuds between breakfast foods, and the absurdity of wearing jungle camouflage in a desert.
