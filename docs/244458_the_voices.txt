Title: The Voices (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Horror, Thriller
Director: Marjane Satrapi
Main Cast: Ryan Reynolds, Gemma Arterton, Anna Kendrick, Jacki Weaver, Ella Smith
Keywords: violence, psychiatrist, dark, evil, woman director

Overview: A mentally unhinged factory worker must decide whether to listen to his talking cat and become a killer, or follow his dog's advice to keep striving for normalcy.
