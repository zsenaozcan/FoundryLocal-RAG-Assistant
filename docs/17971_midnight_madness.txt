Title: Midnight Madness (1980)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Michael Nankin
Main Cast: Michael J. Fox, David Naughton, Stephen Furst, Maggie Roswell, Eddie Deezen
Keywords: scavenger hunt

Overview: A genius grad student organizes an all-night treasure hunt in which five rival teams composed of colorful oddballs furiously match wits with one another while trying to locate and decipher various cryptic clues planted ingeniously around Los Angeles.
