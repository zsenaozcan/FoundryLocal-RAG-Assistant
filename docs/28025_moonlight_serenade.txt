Title: Moonlight Serenade (2009)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Giancarlo Tallarico
Main Cast: Amy Adams, Scott G. Anderson, Moon Bloodgood
Keywords: musical

Overview: A jazz musician performs alongside a coat check girl with a beautiful voice in this musical drama from director Giancarlo Tallarico. By day Nate earns his living as a financial manager, but when night falls, he helps the girl with her singing career at the jazz club, where she performs one night a week. In time both realize they share something special other than the music.
