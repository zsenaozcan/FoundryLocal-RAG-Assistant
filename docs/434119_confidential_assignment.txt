Title: Confidential Assignment (2017)
Genres: Action, Drama, Comedy
Director: Sung-hoon Kim
Main Cast: Hyun Bin, Yoo Hae-jin, Kim Joo-hyuk, Jang Young-nam, Im Yoon-ah

Overview: When a crime organization from North Korea crosses borders and enters South Korean soil, a South Korean detective must cooperate with a North Korean detective to investigate their whereabouts.
