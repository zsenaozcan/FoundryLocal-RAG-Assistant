Title: Nocturna (2007)
Genres: Animation
Director: Adrià García
Main Cast: Imanol Arias, Natalia Rodríguez, Carlos Sobera

Overview: In the aging orphanage the days pass very uneventfully, but the nights are something quite different; at least for Tim they are. The light reflected from the stars is the only cure for his fear of the dark. One night this fear leads him to the orphanage rooftop.
