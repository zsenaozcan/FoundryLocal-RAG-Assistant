Title: Death Valley Rangers (1943)
Genres: Western
Director: Robert Emmett Tansey
Main Cast: Ken Maynard, Hoot Gibson, Bob Steele, Weldon Heyburn, Linda Brent
Keywords: stagecoach, gold mine, death valley

Overview: When a fed-up businessman tires of watching gold shipments disappear without a trace, he calls in the Trail Blazers (Ken Maynard, Hoot Gibson and Bob Steele) -- a legendary trio of law enforcers -- to find the gold and figure out who's behind the thefts.
