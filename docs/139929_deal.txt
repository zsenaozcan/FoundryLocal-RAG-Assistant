Title: Deal (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Eddy Terstall
Main Cast: Roberta Petzoldt, Teun Kuilboer, Frank Sheppard, Fouad Mourigh, Peer Mascini

Overview: After a couple of drinks two twenty-something students make a deal to have sex with each other for 2000 Euros. The deal takes them to Barcelona. There, their mutual embarrassment over the deal complicates matters, but brings them closer together.
