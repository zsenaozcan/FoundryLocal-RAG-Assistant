Title: Heart Like A Wheel (1983)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jonathan Kaplan
Main Cast: Bonnie Bedelia, Beau Bridges, Leo Rossi, Hoyt Axton, Bill McKinney
Keywords: biography, sport, drag racer

Overview: Shirley Muldowney is determined to be a top-fuel drag racer, although no woman has ever raced them before. Despite the high risks of this kind of racing and the burden it places on her family life, she perseveres in her dream.
