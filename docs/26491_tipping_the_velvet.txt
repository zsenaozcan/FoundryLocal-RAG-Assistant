Title: Tipping the Velvet (2002)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Geoffrey Sax
Main Cast: Rachael Stirling, Keeley Hawes, Anna Chancellor, Jodhi May, John Bowe
Keywords: victorian england, homosexuality, miniseries, period drama, lesbian interest

Overview: Set in the 1890s, Tipping the Velvet tells the lesbian love affair between male impersonator music hall star Kitty Butler and Nan Astley.
