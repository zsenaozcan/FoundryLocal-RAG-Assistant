Title: One Day Like Rain (2007)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Paul Todisco
Main Cast: Jesse Eisenberg, Samantha Figura, Trevor Zacharias, Dylan Kussman, Marisa Petroro
Keywords: independent film, science fiction

Overview: A teenage girl living in California suburbia devises a metaphysical experiment designed to save the world from what she sees as an impending doom...but the results of such an experiment prove to be both beneficial and destructive.
