Title: Almost You (1985)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Drama
Director: Adam Brooks
Main Cast: Brooke Adams, Griffin Dunne, Karen Young, Marty Watt, Dana Delany
Keywords: nurse, adultery, midlife crisis, wall street, independent film, injured leg, troubled marriage

Overview: Alex and Erica Boyer's marriage is in a crisis: job and wife bore Alex. When Erica has an accident that has her staying in a wheel chair for some time, it changes their life: Alex meets Erica's young therapy assistant Lisa and gets the idea that she'd be the end of his boredom and he could start over with her. However Lisa's boyfriend feels what's going on and isn't idle.
