Title: Orphans (1998)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Peter Mullan
Main Cast: Douglas Henshall, Gary Lewis, Rosemarie Stevenson, Stephen McCole, Ann Swan

Overview: The star of Ken Loach's MY NAME IS JOE, Mullan proves that his talent isn't relegated to acting. As a writer/director, he has crafted a supremely entertaining motion picture. ORPHANS tells the grittily realistic, hysterical, and deeply moving tale of a group of siblings who reunite in Glasgow on the eve of their mother's funeral. The four children mourn their mother's passing in a variety of ways, some of which are heartfelt and some of which are bizarre. As a potential thunderstorm threatens to damage the city, the situation compounds itself even further.
