Title: The Devil's Carnival (2012)
Genres: Horror
Director: Darren Lynn Bousman
Main Cast: Sean Patrick Flanery, Briana Evigan, Bill Moseley, Jessica Lowndes, Dayton Callie
Keywords: clown, musical, lucifer, fable, dark carnival

Overview: Sinners are invited to a theme park where they endure the repetition of their transgressions. What chances do a conniving kleptomaniac, a gullible teenager, and an obsessed father stand when facing their own moral failings? Lucifer and his colorful cast of singing carnies invite you to grab a ticket to The Devil’s Carnival to find out!
