Title: Jerry and Tom (1998)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Crime
Director: Saul Rubinek
Main Cast: Joe Mantegna, Sam Rockwell, Maury Chaykin, Ted Danson, Charles Durning

Overview: Tom and Jerry are two hit men, they work by day at a third-rate second-hand car dealership. Tom is a veteran and Jerry is a novice in their business, and their attitude toward their profession differs a lot. It shows when Tom is required to kill his old friend Karl.
