Title: You're Killing Me Susana (2016)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Roberto Sneider
Main Cast: Gael García Bernal, Verónica Echegui, Ashley Grace, Hlyunr Harraldson, Barbara Garrick

Overview: It is the journey of an educated man from Mexico City to the center of the United States, with its rigid society, its frigid winter, but most of all, it is an inner journey to face the contradictions between his love for his wife and the macho baggage he carries with him. It is a voyage of growth, funny and painful.
