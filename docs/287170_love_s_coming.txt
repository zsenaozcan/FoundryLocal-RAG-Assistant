Title: Love's Coming (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Naphat Chaithiangthum
Main Cast: Suttinut Uengtrakul, Norrapat Sakulsong, Chanon Santinatornkul, Suraphat Kirivichien, Korn Khunatipapisiri
Keywords: teenage love

Overview: The storyline revolves around a group of schoolboys who suspect two of their number are in love with each other. They come up with ways to prove the secret love affair, but things get more complicated when a girl comes into play.
