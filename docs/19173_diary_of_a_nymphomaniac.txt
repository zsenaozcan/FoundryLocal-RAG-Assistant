Title: Diary of a Nymphomaniac (2008)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Christian Molina
Main Cast: Belén Fabra, Leonardo Sbaraglia, Llum Barrera, Geraldine Chaplin, Ángela Molina
Keywords: nymphomaniac, prostitution, red dress, female genitalia, promiscuous past, nymphomania

Overview: A chronicle of the life of a middle-class French girl's sexual adventures, her then fall into prostitution, and her ultimate redemption.
