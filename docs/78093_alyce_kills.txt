Title: Alyce Kills (2011)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Jay Lee
Main Cast: Jade Dornfeld, Tamara Feldman, James Duval, Larry Cedar, Eddie Rouse

Overview: After accidentally knocking her best friend off a roof, Alyce is haunted by guilt and delves into a brutal nightmare wonderland of sex, drugs and violence, her mind tearing itself apart… along with anyone else who gets in her way.
