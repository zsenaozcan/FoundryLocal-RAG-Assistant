Title: 9 (2005)
Genres: Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Shane Acker
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: soul, fight, trap, end of world, rag doll, short

Overview: A rag doll fights a monster that has been stealing the souls of his people.  This film was Shane Acker's thesis project at UCLA film school.  11 min short, not to be confused with the 79 min 2009 feature version.
