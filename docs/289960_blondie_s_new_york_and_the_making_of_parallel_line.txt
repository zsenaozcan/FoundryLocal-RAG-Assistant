Title: Blondie's New York and the Making of Parallel Lines (2014)
Genres: Music, Documentary
Director: Alan Ravenscroft
Main Cast: Deborah Harry, Chris Stein, Clem Burke
Keywords: new york, music, blondie

Overview: The story behind Blondie's album Parallel Lines, which sold 16 million copies and captured the spirit of 1970s New York at a time of poverty, crime and an exploding artistic life.
