Title: With Heart & Soul (2009)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Pantelis Voulgaris
Main Cast: Vangelis Mourikis, Giorgos Symeonidis, Victoria Haralabidou, Kostas Kleftogiannis, Vasilis Nanakis

Overview: Set during the Greek Civil War, "With Heart &amp; Soul" centers on two brothers who are fighting on opposite sides.
