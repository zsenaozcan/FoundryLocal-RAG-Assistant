Title: High School U.S.A. (1983)
Genres: TV Movie, Romance, Comedy
Director: Rod Amateau
Main Cast: Crystal Bernard, Michael J. Fox, Nancy McKeon, Todd Bridges, Angela Cartwright
Keywords: high school, teen movie

Overview: Set in a senior high school class, J.J. (Michael J. Fox) pursues the girlfriend of a rival from a higher clique which culminates in a race at the end of the movie between the two rivals in this light comedy.
