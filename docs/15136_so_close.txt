Title: So Close (2002)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Corey Yuen Kwai
Main Cast: Shu Qi, Zhao Wei, Karen Mok, Song Seung-heon, Michael Wai
Keywords: martial arts, sense of guilt, hacker, death of father, aerial combat, high tech surveillance, global positioning, female protagonist, sadist, female cop
