Title: The Young Master (1980)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Jackie Chan
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Wai Pak, Yuen Biao, Sek Kin, Lily Li Li-Li
Keywords: kung fu, police, fighter

Overview: Youthful martial arts master Lung is searching for his missing brother, when he is mistaken for a criminal on the run.  He must prove his innocence by solving the case himself, while local lawmen and merciless mercenaries are hot on his trail.
