Title: Common Wealth (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Álex de la Iglesia
Main Cast: Carmen Maura, Eduardo Antuña, María Asquerino, Jesús Bonilla, Marta Fernández Muro
Keywords: treasure, estate agent, greed, stolen money, neighbor, conflict, inheritance fight

Overview: Julia finds 300 million pesetas hidden in a dead man's house while selling an apartment. She's a 40-ish real estate agent now forced to face the wrath of a very peculiar community (of neighbors), headed by an unscrupulous administrator. Black humor gives way to suspense, closely followed by horror that doesn't take long in coming to a head in undisguised pandemonium.
