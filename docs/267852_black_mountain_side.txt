Title: Black Mountain Side (2014)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Nick Szostakiwskyj
Main Cast: Shane Twerdun, Michael Dickson, Carl Toftfelt, Marc Anthony Williams, Timothy Lyle
Keywords: suicide, amputation, canada, winter, night, murder, h.p. lovecraft, snow, creature, psychological horror

Overview: A group of archaeologists uncover a strange structure in Northern Canada, dating over ten thousand years before the present. The team finds themselves isolated when their communication systems fail and it's not long before they begin to feel the effects of the solitude.
