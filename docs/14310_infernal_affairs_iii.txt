Title: Infernal Affairs III (2003)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Action
Director: Andrew Lau
Main Cast: Andy Lau, Tony Leung Chiu-Wai, Leon Lai, Chen Dao-Ming, Anthony Wong
Keywords: sequel, flashback sequence

Overview: Infernal Affairs III is a 2003 Hong Kong crime thriller film directed by Andrew Lau and Alan Mak. It is the third installment in the Infernal Affairs film series, and is both a sequel and a semi-prequel to the original film, as it intercuts events before and after the events in the original.
