Title: The Surgeon (1995)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Carl Schenkel
Main Cast: Isabel Glasser, James Remar, Sean Haberle, Peter Boyle, Malcolm McDowell

Overview: Dr. Julian Mater is suspended and gets his license revoked for performing experiments on dying patients in cellular regeneration. A couple of years later, he returns to the hospital that condemned his work to begin practicing his grizzly experiments once more.
