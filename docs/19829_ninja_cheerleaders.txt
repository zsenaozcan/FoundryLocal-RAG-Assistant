Title: Ninja Cheerleaders (2008)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: David Presley
Main Cast: Trishelle Cannatella, Ginny Weirick, Maitland McConnell, George Takei, Michael FitzGibbon
Keywords: cheerleader, ninja, police chase, female ninja, deed

Overview: 3 college cheerleaders use their martial arts know-how to save their Sensei from mafia kidnappers, but must keep their extra curricular activities a secret in order to make it into an Ivy League school.
