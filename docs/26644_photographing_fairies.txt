Title: Photographing Fairies (1997)
Genres: Mystery, Fantasy, Drama
Director: Nick Willing
Main Cast: Toby Stephens, Emily Woof, Ben Kingsley, Frances Barber, Bernard Gallagher

Overview: Photographer Charles Castle is numbed with grief following the death of his beautiful bride. He goes off to war, working in the trenches as a photographer. Following the war and still in grief Charles is given some photographs purporting to be of fairies. His search for the truth leads him to Burkinwell, a seemingly peaceful village seething with secrets
