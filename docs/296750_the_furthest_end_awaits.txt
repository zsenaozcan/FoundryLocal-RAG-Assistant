Title: The Furthest End Awaits (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Chiang Hsiu-Chiung
Main Cast: Hiromi Nagasaku, Nozomi Sasaki, Hiyori Sakurada, Masatoshi Nagase, Jun Murakami
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Set at the edge of the Noto Peninsula in Ishikawa Prefecture, Japan, with its beautiful scenic views, the film revolves around two women from different backgrounds who develop a friendship, and how they begin to influence and change each other’s lives.
