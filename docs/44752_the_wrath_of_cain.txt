Title: The Wrath of Cain (2010)
Genres: Action
Director: Ryan Combs
Main Cast: Ving Rhames, Nipsey Hustle, Gillie Da Kid, Robert Patrick, Erick Nathan

Overview: A former kingpin of the streets, Miles “Cain” Skinner is doing life in prison. He is feared and respected among the inmates. When Cain learns that his sentence will be shortened only by brain cancer, his urgency to change his ways goes into overdrive.
