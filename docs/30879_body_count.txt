Title: Body Count (1987)
Genres: Horror
Director: Ruggero Deodato
Main Cast: Bruce Penhall, Mimsy Farmer, David Hess, Luisa Maneri, Nicola Farron
Keywords: mountain, camping, ax, forest, vacation, violence

Overview: A bodybuilder, a junk-food addict and a wild blonde nymph and their friends are stalked by a terrifying figure. An horrific tale of murder as a fun-loving group of college students explore the Colorado wilderness.
