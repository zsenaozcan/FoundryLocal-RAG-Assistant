Title: All About the Feathers (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Neto Villalobos
Main Cast: Marvin Acosta, Allan Cascante, Sylvia Sossa, Erlan Vásquez, David Ríos
Keywords: feather

Overview: A black comedy about Chalo, a lone security guard who acquires his first gamecock and discovers the bittersweet taste of friendship.
