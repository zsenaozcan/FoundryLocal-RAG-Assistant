Title: Carmen (1984)
Genres: Music, Drama
Director: Francesco Rosi
Main Cast: Julia Migenes, Ruggero Raimondi, Faith Esham, Plácido Domingo, Lillian Watson
Keywords: opera, musical

Overview: A film version of the famous Bizet opera, where a soldier (Don Jose) falls in love with a beautiful factory worker (Carmen), but she does not reciprocate his feelings.
