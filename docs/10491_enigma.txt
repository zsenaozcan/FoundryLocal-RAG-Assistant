Title: Enigma (2001)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Thriller, Romance, War
Director: Michael Apted
Main Cast: Dougray Scott, Kate Winslet, Saffron Burrows, Jeremy Northam, Nikolaj Coster-Waldau
Keywords: lover (female), world war ii, radio transmission, leave, friendship, u boat, cryptography, secret formula
