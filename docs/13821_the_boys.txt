Title: The Boys (1998)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rowan Woods
Main Cast: David Wenham, Toni Collette, Lynette Curran, John Polson, Anthony Hayes
Keywords: prisoner, australian, brother, sydney, australia, psychopath, parole board

Overview: Brett Sprague is a violent and psychopathic man, who is released on parole after serving a sentence for assault. As he returns to his family house and we watch him and his brothers, Stevie and Glenn, for the next 24 hours, it becomes clear this day will not end well.
