Title: Daniel Tosh: People Pleaser (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Marcus Raboy
Main Cast: Daniel Tosh

Overview: Daniel Tosh is brutally honest in his newest stand-up special filmed at the Wilshire Ebell Theater in Los Angeles. The comedian takes piercing shots at everyone, including couples who can't conceive, parents of sick children, hoarders, marathoners, and his fans.
