Title: Death of a Salesman (2000)
Genres: Drama, TV Movie
Director: Kirk Browning
Main Cast: Brian Dennehy, Elizabeth Franz, Ron Eldard, Ted Koch, Howard Witt
Keywords: salesman, live performance

Overview: An aging salesman is fired from his job after a long career in it. Broken, without much to look forward to, he tries reconnecting with his wife and kids who he had always put down as he dedicated himself to work.
