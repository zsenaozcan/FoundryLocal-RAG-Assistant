Title: The Housemaid (1960)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Kim Ki-young
Main Cast: Lee Eun-shim, Ju Jeung-nyeo, Kim Jin-kyu, Um Aing-ran, Ahn Sung-Ki
Keywords: housemaid

Overview: A piano composer's family moves into a new house; when his pregnant wife collapses from working to support the family, he hires a housemaid to help with housework.
