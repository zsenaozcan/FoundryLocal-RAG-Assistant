Title: The Odds (2012)
Genres: Crime, Mystery
Director: Simon Davidson
Main Cast: Tyler Johnston, Julia Maxwell, Jaren Brandt Bartlett, Calum Worthy, Robert Moloney
Keywords: poker, apparent suicide, handgun, illegal gambling, betting

Overview: A murder mystery set in the world of illegal teenage gambling. A 17-year old must find his best friends' killer before the game is exposed.
