Title: The Pervert's Guide to Cinema (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Sophie Fiennes
Main Cast: Slavoj Žižek
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The Pervert's Guide to Cinema offers an introduction into some of Žižek's most exciting ideas on fantasy, reality, sexuality, subjectivity, desire, materiality and cinematic form. Whether he is untangling the famously baffling films of David Lynch, or overturning everything you thought you knew about Hitchcock, Žižek illuminates the screen with his passion, intellect, and unfailing sense of humour.
