Title: I Am Here (2014)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Anders Morgenthaler
Main Cast: Kim Basinger, Jordan Prentice, Sebastian Schipper, Peter Stormare, Robert Hunger-Bühler
Keywords: prostitute, border, black market, business woman, mortal danger

Overview: Successful businesswoman Maria has achieved everything except what she wants the most - a baby of her own. She decides to deal with the matter by herself and embarks on a desperate and dangerous journey in order to make her dream come true.
