Title: The Dark (1979)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: John 'Bud' Cardos
Main Cast: William Devane, Cathy Lee Crosby, Keenan Wynn, Richard Jaeckel, Warren J. Kemmerling
Keywords: police, murder, alien, city

Overview: At night the Mangler stalks the streets of Los Angeles, killing and mutilating random victims. On the trail are a TV reporter, the father of one of the victims, and a police detective, but despite their efforts only the mysterious psychic DeRenzy knows what the killer is and how to stop it.
