Title: The Door (2009)
Genres: Fantasy, Thriller, Science Fiction, Mystery
Director: Anno Saul
Main Cast: Mads Mikkelsen, Jessica Schwarz, Valeria Eisenbart, Heike Makatsch, Tim Seyfi

Overview: A successful artist loses control of his life after his young daughter's death. A chance for a new start appears, but all is not what it seems.
