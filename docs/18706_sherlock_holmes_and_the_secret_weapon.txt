Title: Sherlock Holmes and the Secret Weapon (1942)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Roy William Neill
Main Cast: Basil Rathbone, Nigel Bruce, Lionel Atwill, Kaaren Verne, William Post Jr.
Keywords: sherlock holmes

Overview: Starting in Switzerland, Sherlock Holmes rescues the inventor of a bomb-sight which the allies want to keep from the Nazis. Back in London it seems that the inventor is not all that he seemed.
