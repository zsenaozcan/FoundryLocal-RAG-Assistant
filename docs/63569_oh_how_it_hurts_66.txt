Title: Oh How It Hurts 66 (1966)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Rolan Bykov
Main Cast: Rolan Bykov, Oleg Efremov, Aleksei Smirnov, Frunzik Mkrtchyan, Lidiya Knyazeva
Keywords: fairy tale

Overview: A fantasy story based on Kornei Chukovsky classics about a brave doctor Aybolit and his adventures on the land and on the sea.
