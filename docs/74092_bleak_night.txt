Title: Bleak Night (2010)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Yoon Seong-hyeon
Main Cast: Lee Je-hoon, Seo Jun-young, Park Jung-Min, Cho Seong-ha, Lee Cho-hee

Overview: There were three friends. Someone became the assailant and someone became the victim. Another swings his fists in anger at their broken friendship. Through the director’s creation of young actors and looking into the life of high school boys, the assailant and the victim come dialectically. It was a time when everyone got hurt.
