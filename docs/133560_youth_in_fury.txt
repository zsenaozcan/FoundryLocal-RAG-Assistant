Title: Youth in Fury (1960)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Masahiro Shinoda
Main Cast: Shinichirô Mikami, Shima Iwashita, Kayoko Honoo, Kazuya Kosaka, Junichiro Yamashita
Keywords: youth, student protest

Overview: A reckless student contemplates terrorism in a prescient film that confirmed Shinoda as a fearless member of Shochiku’s iconoclastic New Wave. At the height of student protests, Shimojo (Shinichiro Mikami) takes his aggressions to another level, beset by seemingly insoluble feelings of alienation.
