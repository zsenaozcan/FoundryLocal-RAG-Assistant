Title: Reform School Girls (1986)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Tom DeSimone
Main Cast: Linda Carol, Wendy O. Williams, Pat Ast, Sybil Danning, Charlotte McGinnis
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Jenny is sent to a women's reform school. It is run by evil warden Sutter and her henchwoman Edna. Jenny will stop at nothing to escape but she also has to deal with Charlie the bully.
