Title: The Woman in Question (1952)
Genres: Mystery
Director: Anthony Asquith
Main Cast: Jean Kent, Dirk Bogarde, John McCallum, Susan Shaw, Hermione Baddeley
Keywords: hitchcockian

Overview: Director Anthony Asquith, working in the style of a Hitchcock whodunit, deftly moves between multiple points of view in this gritty look at life in a seaport town. Jean Kent stars as Agnes "Astra" Huston, a fortune teller at a run-down fair, who is found strangled in her bedroom. As the police question five suspects their interactions with her are shown in flashbacks from their point of view.
