Title: Death In Love (2008)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: Boaz Yakin
Main Cast: Josh Lucas, Jacqueline Bisset, Adam Brody, Morena Baccarin, Lukas Haas
Keywords: new york, sex, adultery, experiment, nudity, nazis, greed, war, independent film, memory

Overview: Death in Love is a psychosexual-thriller about a love affair between a Jewish woman and a doctor overseeing human experimentation at a Nazi German concentration camp, and the impact this has on her sons' lives in the 1990s.
