Title: La Sfida (1958)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Francesco Rosi
Main Cast: José Suárez, Rosanna Schiaffino, Nino Vingelli, Decimo Cristiani, José Jaspe

Overview: Vito Polara is ambitious and wants to get as more power and money as possible. He decides to leave the cigarette smuggling and try to get the total control of the regional fruit and vegetable distribution considered more profitable. He looks for the help of a rural crime Boss.
