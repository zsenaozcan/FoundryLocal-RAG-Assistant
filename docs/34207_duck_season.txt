Title: Duck Season (2004)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Fernando Eimbcke
Main Cast: Daniel Miranda, Diego Cataño, Danny Perea, Enrique Arreola
Keywords: video game, homophobia, flat, pizza delivery, pizza boy, marijuana

Overview: Flama and Moko are fourteen years old; they have been best friends since they were kids. They have everything they need to survive yet another boring Sunday: an apartment without parents, videogames, porn magazines, soft drinks and pizza delivery.
