Title: The Journey to Melonia (1989)
Genres: Animation, Family, Adventure
Director: Per Åhlin
Main Cast: Allan Edwall, Robyn Carlsson, Olle Sarri, Tomas von Brömssen, Ernst Günther

Overview: When a ship sinks during a storm, a slave from the industrial island of Plutonia is washed up on the beaches of paradise island Melonia, where the "all-powerful" wizard Prospero and his strange friends reside.
