Title: Scooby-Doo! and the Reluctant Werewolf (1988)
Genres: TV Movie, Animation, Family, Mystery, Comedy
Director: Ray Patterson
Main Cast: Don Messick, Casey Kasem, Hamilton Camp, Jim Cummings, Ed Gilbert

Overview: Shaggy is turned into a werewolf, and it's up to Scooby, Scrappy and Shaggy's girlfriend to help him win a race against other monsters, and become human again.
