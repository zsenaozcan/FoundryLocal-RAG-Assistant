Title: Bokeh (2017)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Geoffrey Orthwein
Main Cast: Maika Monroe, Matt O'Leary, Arnar Jónsson, Gunnar Helgason, Berglind Rós Sigurôardóttir

Overview: On a romantic getaway to Iceland, a young American couple wake up one morning to discover every person on Earth has disappeared. Their struggle to survive and to reconcile the mysterious event lead them to reconsider everything they know about themselves and the world.
