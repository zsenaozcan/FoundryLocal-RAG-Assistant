Title: Jasper, Texas (2003)
Genres: Horror, Drama
Director: Jeffrey W. Byrd
Main Cast: Roy T. Anderson, Demore Barnes, John Bayliss, James Bearden, Samantha Bee

Overview: In 1998, three white men in the small town of Jasper, Texas, chained a black man to the back of their pickup truck and dragged him to his death. This film relates that story and how it affected all of the residents of the town, both black and white.
