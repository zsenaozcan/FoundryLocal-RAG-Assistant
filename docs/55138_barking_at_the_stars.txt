Title: Barking at the Stars (1998)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Romance, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Zdravko Šotra
Main Cast: Dragan Mićanović, Nataša Tapušković, Velimir „Bata“ Živojinović, Nikola Simić, Dragan Jovanović
Keywords: 1960s, barking

Overview: Comedy about teachers and students at a high school in a small provincial town. Mihailo tries to win the heart of a girl his brother is also chasing.
