Title: The Old Dark House (1932)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: James Whale
Main Cast: Boris Karloff, Melvyn Douglas, Charles Laughton, Lilian Bond, Ernest Thesiger
Keywords: terror, servant, humor, stalker, dysfunctional family, mute, eccentric, storm, centenarian, old dark house

Overview: Seeking shelter from a pounding rainstorm in a remote region of Wales, several travellers are admitted to a gloomy, foreboding mansion belonging to the extremely strange Femm family. Trying to make the best of it, the guests must deal with their sepulchral host, Horace Femm and his obsessive, malevolent sister Rebecca. Things get worse as the brutish manservant Morgan gets drunk and runs amok.
