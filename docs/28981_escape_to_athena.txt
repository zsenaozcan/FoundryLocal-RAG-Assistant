Title: Escape to Athena (1979)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, War
Director: George P. Cosmatos
Main Cast: Roger Moore, Telly Savalas, David Niven, Stefanie Powers, Claudia Cardinale
Keywords: island, escape

Overview: During the World War II, the prisoners of a German camp in a greek island are trying to escape. They don't want only their freedom, but they also seek for an ineffable treasure hidden in a monastery at the top of the island's mountain.
