Title: Life Goes On (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Eugenio Martín
Main Cast: Julio Iglesias, Jean Harrington, Charo López, Florinda Chico, Mayrata O'Wisiedo

Overview: A promising goalkeeper of Real Madrid sees his chance when a teammate is injured and is summoned to replace him. But the misfortune is primed with him when an untimely car accident damaged her spine and is forced to give up that had to be the game of his life. Tired of so much bad luck, he retreats to a seaside hotel, where chance will go up to the stage.
