Title: Darling (2010)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: A. Karunakaran
Main Cast: Prabhas, Kajal Agarwal, Shraddha Das, Prabhu Ganesan, Mukesh Rishi

Overview: Prabhas (Prabhas) has a childhood sweetheart named Nandini (Kajal Agarwal) and they are separated during their childhood. Nandini migrates to Swiss along with her father. Meanwhile Prabhas rejects love proposals from other girls as his heart belongs to Nandini. Prabhas gets a chance to meet Nandini for the first time after the separation during the reunion of their respective parents who have studied together. The rest of the story is all about how Prabhas wins the love of Nandini.
