Title: Nothing Personal (1996)
Genres: Drama
Director: Thaddeus O'Sullivan
Main Cast: Ian Hart, John Lynch, James Frain, Michael Gambon, Gary Lydon

Overview: A raw depiction of the Belfast 'troubles' as savage tribal warfare. Set shortly after the 1975 cease fire, the film focuses on the tribulations of Kenny, Protestant leader of a group of Shankill Road Loyalists, and his one-time friend Liam, a Catholic.
