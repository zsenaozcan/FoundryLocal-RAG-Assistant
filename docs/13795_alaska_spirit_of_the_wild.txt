Title: Alaska: Spirit of the Wild (1998)
Genres: Documentary
Director: George Casey
Main Cast: Charlton Heston
Keywords: wilderness, whale, alaska, bear, nature, imax

Overview: Alaska... Here, in this vast and spectacularly beautiful land teeming with abundant wildlife, discover the "Spirit of the Wild." Experience it in the explosive calving of glaciers, the celestial fires of the Aurora Borealis. Witness it in the thundering stampede of caribou, the beauty of the polar bear and the stealthful, deadly hunt of the wolf pack.
