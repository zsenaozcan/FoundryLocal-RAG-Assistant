Title: The Land of OZ (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Vasili Sigarev
Main Cast: Yana Troyanova, Yuriy Kutsenko, Aleksandr Bashirov, Yevgeni Tsyganov, Inna Churikova

Overview: In Yekaterinburg over the Christmas period, shop assistant Lenka Shabadinova experiences a number of incredible events and unexpected meetings.
