Title: No Place to Hide (1970)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Thriller
Director: Robert Allen Schnitzer
Main Cast: Sylvester Stallone, Henry G. Sanders, Tony Page, Rebecca Grimes, Vickie Lancaster
Keywords: fbi, bomber, independent film, terrorism, cult film

Overview: In New York in the late 60s, a politically motivated group of students plans bombings of company offices who do business with dictators in Middle American countries. But when they contact a known terrorist and bombing specialist, the FBI gets on their track.
