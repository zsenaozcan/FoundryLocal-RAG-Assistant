Title: Pitbull Tough Women (2016)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Patryk Vega
Main Cast: Sebastian Fabijański, Joanna Kulig, Piotr Stramowski, Alicja Bachleda-Curuś, Maja Ostaszewska

Overview: Two new female police officers struggle to survive a force filled with corrupt superiors and the brutal world of the criminal mafia.
