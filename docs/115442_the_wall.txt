Title: The Wall (2012)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Julian Pölsler
Main Cast: Martina Gedeck, Ulrike Beimpold, Karlheinz Hackl, Wolfgang Maria Bauer, Julia Gschnitzer
Keywords: shelter, loneliness, survival, food

Overview: A woman inexplicably finds herself cut off from all human contact when an invisible, unyielding wall suddenly surrounds the countryside. Accompanied by her loyal dog Lynx, she becomes immersed in a world untouched by civilization and ruled by the laws of nature.
