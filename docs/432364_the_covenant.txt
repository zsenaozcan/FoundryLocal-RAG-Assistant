Title: The Covenant (2017)
Genres: Horror
Director: Robert Conway
Main Cast: Monica Engesser, Clint James, Maria Olsen, Owen Conway, Sanford Gibbons
Keywords: isolation, possession, demon, evil force

Overview: After the tragic deaths of her husband and daughter, Sarah Doyle moves back to her childhood home with her estranged brother, Richard. It's not long before Sarah begins to experience supernatural phenomena of a violent and hostile nature.
