Title: Every Which Way But Loose (1978)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Romance
Director: James Fargo
Main Cast: Clint Eastwood, Sondra Locke, Beverly D'Angelo, Geoffrey Lewis, Ruth Gordon
Keywords: bare knuckle boxing, waitress, fishing, camping, overweight, motel, beer, biker, orangutan, monkey

Overview: Philo Beddoe is your regular, easygoing, truck-driving guy. He's also the best bar-room brawler west of the Rockies. And he lives with a 165-pound orangutan named Clyde. Like other guys, Philo finally falls in love - with a flighty singer who leads him on a screwball chase across the American Southwest. Nothing's in the way except a motorcycle gang, and legendary brawler Tank Murdock.
