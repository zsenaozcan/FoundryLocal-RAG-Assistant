Title: Swamp Water (1941)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Jean Renoir
Main Cast: Walter Brennan, Walter Huston, Anne Baxter, Dana Andrews, Virginia Gilmore
Keywords: hunter, swamp, on the run, fugitive, quicksand, dog

Overview: A hunter happens upon a fugitive and his daughter living in a Georgia swamp. He falls in love with the girl and persuades the fugitive to return to town.
