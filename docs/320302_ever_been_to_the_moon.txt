Title: Ever Been to the Moon? (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Paolo Genovese
Main Cast: Liz Solari, Raoul Bova, Simone Dell'Anna, Giulia Michelini, Pietro Sermonti

Overview: Giulia is thirty years old and unmarried. She has a good job which allows her to travel and lead a luxurious life in Milan and Paris. She is convinced that she has everything she needs for happiness, but after meeting Renzo, a charming Apulian farmer, Giulia understands that she's missing the most important thing in her life - true love.
