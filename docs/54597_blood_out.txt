Title: Blood Out (2011)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Thriller
Director: Jason Hewitt
Main Cast: 50 Cent, Val Kilmer, Luke Goss, Vinnie Jones, AnnaLynne McCord

Overview: When big city detectives refuse to further investigate his kid brother's gang related murder, small town Sheriff Michael Spencer drops the badge and goes undercover to find his brother's killer and avenge his death.
