Title: Addicted (2002)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery, Romance, Foreign
Director: Park Young-hoon
Main Cast: Lee Byung-hun, Lee Mi-yeon, Lee Eol, Park Sun-young, Kang Ye-won

Overview: Two brothers get into a sudden tragic accident and they both fall into a coma at the same time. A year later, the younger brother Dae-jin wakes up believing he is his older brother Ho-jin.
