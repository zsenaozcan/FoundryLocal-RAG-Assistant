Title: Biology 101 (2011)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Christopher R. Smith
Main Cast: David Christian Welborn, Noelle DuBois, Deborah O'Brien, Emily Bicks, Gregory MacTavish
Keywords: pornography, stalker, teacher, new student, married

Overview: A College professor finds his life unraveling when a new student joins his class, a student who may or may not be the very subject of his darkest secret. His obsession could kill him and his family.
