Title: The Box (2007)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: AJ Kparr
Main Cast: Yul Vazquez, A.J. Buckley, Gabrielle Union, Giancarlo Esposito, Anjelah N. Johnson
Keywords: interview, deception

Overview: A disgraced former LAPD detective leads a home invasion in search of millions in stolen money. The plan goes awry and homicide detectives seeking answers interrogate the only survivors; a thief and a victim.
