Title: River of Grass (1995)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Kelly Reichardt
Main Cast: Lisa Bowman, Larry Fessenden, Michael Buscemi, Greg Schroeder, Santo Fazio
Keywords: fugitive, road movie, woman director

Overview: Cozy, a dissatisfied housewife, meets Lee at a bar. A drink turns into a home break-in, and a gun shot sends them on the run together, thinking they've committed murder.
