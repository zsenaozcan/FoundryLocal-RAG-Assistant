Title: Kill Bill: Vol. 1 (2003)
Genres: Action, Crime
Director: Quentin Tarantino
Main Cast: Uma Thurman, Lucy Liu, Vivica A. Fox, Daryl Hannah, David Carradine
Keywords: japan, coma, martial arts, kung fu, underworld, yakuza, sword, bride, revenge, gore
