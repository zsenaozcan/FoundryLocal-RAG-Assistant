Title: Tamara (2005)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Thriller
Director: Jeremy Haft
Main Cast: Jenna Dewan, Marc Devigne, Chad Faust, Katie Stuart, Bryan Clark
Keywords: vampire, dying and death, high school, revenge

Overview: Tamara, an unattractive girl who is picked on by her peers, returns after her death as a sexy seductress to enact revenge.
