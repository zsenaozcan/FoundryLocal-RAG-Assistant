Title: V2: Dead Angel (2007)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Aleksi Mäkelä
Main Cast: Juha Veijonen, Hannu-Pekka Björkman, Jussi Lampi, Johanna Kokko, Lotta Lindroos

Overview: The tough Detective Vares, get hired by a friend accused of murdering a girl. His job is to find the real killer, but people aren't talking. Before he can get traction another homicide takes place- and things move toward a dire conclusion.
