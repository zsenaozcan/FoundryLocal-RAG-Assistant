Title: Gas Food Lodging (1992)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Allison Anders
Main Cast: Brooke Adams, Ione Skye, Fairuza Balk, James Brolin, Robert Knepper
Keywords: waitress, sister sister relationship, new mexico, mother daughter relationship, diner, single mother, teenage daughter, unwanted pregnancy, woman director

Overview: In the boring desert of New Mexico, a single mother raises her two teenage daughters, Shade and Trudi, whose deepest desire is to leave the dead calm town. Shade is the type to escape in her extravagant fantasies while Trudi is so rebelious it could drive her away.
