Title: Forget Paris (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Billy Crystal
Main Cast: Billy Crystal, Debra Winger, Joe Mantegna, Cynthia Stevenson, Richard Masur
Keywords: paris, dallas, basketball, crisis, falling in love, referee

Overview: Mickey Gordon is a basketball referee who travels to France to bury his father. Ellen Andrews is an American living in Paris who works for the airline he flies on. They meet and fall in love, but their relationship goes through many difficult patches. The story is told in flashback by their friends at a restaurant waiting for them to arrive.
