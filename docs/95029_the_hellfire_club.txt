Title: The Hellfire Club (1961)
Genres: Adventure
Director: Robert S. Baker
Main Cast: Keith Michell, Adrienne Corri, Peter Cushing, Peter Arne, Kai Fischer
Keywords: victim, relatives, rescue, danger, ancestral home

Overview: Years after fleeing his ancestral home with his mother, Jason returns home to claim his birthright, only to find his way blocked by his evil cousin Thomas. In order to reclaim his title, Jason must do battle with his cousin, who calls upon the members of the deadly Hellfire Club to stop him.
