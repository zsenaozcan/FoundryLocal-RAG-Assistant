Title: All Eyez on Me (2017)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Benny Boom
Main Cast: Demetrius Shipp Jr., Danai Gurira, Kat Graham, Jamal Woolard, Dominic L. Santana
Keywords: biography, biopic

Overview: All Eyez on Me chronicles the life and legacy of Tupac Shakur, including his rise to superstardom as a hip-hop artist, actor, poet and activist, as well as his imprisonment and prolific, controversial time at Death Row Records. Against insurmountable odds, Tupac rose to become a cultural icon whose career and persona both continue to grow long after his passing.
