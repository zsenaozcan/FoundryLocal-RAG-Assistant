Title: Bug (1975)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror, Mystery
Director: Jeannot Szwarc
Main Cast: Bradford Dillman, Joanna Miles, Richard Gilliland, Jamie Smith-Jackson, Alan Fudge
Keywords: fire, mutation, wall crawling, disaster

Overview: An earthquake releases a strain of mutant cockroaches with the ability to start fires, which proceed to cause destructive chaos in a small town. The studies carried out by scientist James Parmiter, however, reveal an intent with much more far-reaching consequences.
