Title: Evita (1996)
Genres: History, Drama, Music
Director: Alan Parker
Main Cast: Madonna, Antonio Banderas, Jonathan Pryce, Jimmy Nail, Victoria Sus
Keywords: prostitute, deification, dancehall hostess, perónism, argentine president, rise to power, singing narrator, soccer ball

Overview: The hit musical based on the life of Evita Duarte, a B-movie Argentinian actress who eventually became the wife of Argentinian president and dictator Juan Perón, and the most beloved and hated woman in Argentina.
