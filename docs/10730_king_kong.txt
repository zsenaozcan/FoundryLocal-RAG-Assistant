Title: King Kong (1976)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: John Guillermin
Main Cast: Jeff Bridges, Jessica Lange, Charles Grodin, John Randolph, Rene Auberjonois
Keywords: indigenous, island, science fiction, king kong, giant animal, giant ape

Overview: In this remake of the 1933 classic about the giant ape, an oil company expedition disturbs the peace of Kong and brings him back to New York to exploit him. Even though a woman somewhat tames Kong, he finally breaks loose and terrorizes the city, and as the military attempt to stop him, he falls to his death from the top of the World Trade Center.
