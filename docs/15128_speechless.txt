Title: Speechless (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Ron Underwood
Main Cast: Michael Keaton, Geena Davis, Christopher Reeve, Bonnie Bedelia, Ernie Hudson
Keywords: politics, love, speech writer

Overview: A romantic comedy. Michael Keaton and Geena Davis are speechwriters for competing political campaigns. Witty and amusing for the political junkies amongst us.
