Title: The Band Concert (1935)
Genres: Animation
Director: Wilfred Jackson
Main Cast: Clarence Nash
Keywords: mickey mouse, disney short, donald duck, goofy, william tell overture, music concert

Overview: Mickey is trying to lead a concert of The William Tell Overture, but he's continually disrupted by ice cream vendor Donald, who uses a seemingly endless supply of flutes to play Turkey in the Straw instead. After Donald gives up, a bee comes along and causes his own havoc. The band then reaches the Storm sequence, and the weather also starts to pick up; a tornado comes along, but they keep playing.
