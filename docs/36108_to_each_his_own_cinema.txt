Title: To Each His Own Cinema (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Aki Kaurismäki
Main Cast: Isabelle Adjani, Pegah Ahangarani, Anouk Aimée, Leonid Alexeenko, Taraneh Alidoosti
Keywords: woman director
