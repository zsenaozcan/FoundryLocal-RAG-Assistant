Title: The Ron Clark Story (2006)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Randa Haines
Main Cast: Matthew Perry, Judith Buchan, Griffin Cork, Ernie Hudson, C.J. Jackman-Zigante
Keywords: biography, woman director

Overview: Matthew Perry is real-life inspiration Ron Clark, a passionate and innovative teacher who leaves his small hometown to teach in one of Harlem's toughest schools. But to break through to this students, Clark must use unconventional methods, including his ground-breaking classroom rules, to drive them toward their potential.
