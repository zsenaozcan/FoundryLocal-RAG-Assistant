Title: Troubleshooter (2010)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Foreign
Director: Kwon Hyeok-Jae
Main Cast: Sol Kyung-gu, Lee Jung-Jin, Oh Dal-su, Ju Jin-mo, Lee Sung-min

Overview: Kang Tae-sik is a private detective who would do anything for money. One day, he gets framed for a murder by a ruthless boss of the criminal underworld and a mysterious caller watches and controls his every move. He must use a wide array of techno-gadgetry at his disposal to clear himself and exact revenge within 24 hours given to him.
