Title: Alleycats (2016)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Ian Bonhôte
Main Cast: Josh Whitehouse, Eleanor Tomlinson, John Lynch, Sam Keeley, Sonoya Mizuno
Keywords: corruption, cycling, bike racing

Overview: When bike courier Chris witnesses what looks like a murder, his first instinct is to cut and run. But when his curiosity draws him back in, he's soon embroiled in a world of corruption, political power, and illegal bike racing.
