Title: Imaginary Crimes (1994)
Genres: Drama
Director: Anthony Drazan
Main Cast: Harvey Keitel, Fairuza Balk, Kelly Lynch, Vincent D'Onofrio, Diane Baker
Keywords: indiana, hustler, father, coming of age

Overview: A recently widowed, small-time hustler struggles to raise his two teenage daughters on his own and still make a dishonest living in 1950s Indiana.
