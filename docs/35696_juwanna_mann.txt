Title: Juwanna Mann (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jesse Vaughan
Main Cast: Miguel A. Núñez, Jr., Vivica A. Fox, Kevin Pollak, Tommy Davidson, Ginuwine
Keywords: sport, basketball, cross dressing, man dressed as woman

Overview: A basketball star is booted out of the NBA when his on-court antics go too far, so he poses as a woman and joins the WUBA.
