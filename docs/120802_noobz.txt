Title: Noobz (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Adventure
Director: Blake Freeman
Main Cast: Jason Mewes, Blake Freeman, Moisés Arias, Matt Shively, Casper Van Dien
Keywords: video game

Overview: Four friends hit the road to LA to compete in the Cyberbowl Video Game Championship, but will they be able to compete with the worst hangovers of their lives?
