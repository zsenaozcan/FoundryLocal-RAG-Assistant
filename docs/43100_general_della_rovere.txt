Title: General Della Rovere (1959)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Roberto Rossellini
Main Cast: Vittorio De Sica, Hannes Messemer, Sandra Milo, Vittorio Caprioli, Herbert Fischer
Keywords: italy, resistance, general, brothel, nazis, con man, bravery, crook, swindler, partisan

Overview: The Gestapo forces con man Victorio Bardone (Vittorio De Sica) to impersonate a dead partisan general in order to extract information from his fellow inmates.
