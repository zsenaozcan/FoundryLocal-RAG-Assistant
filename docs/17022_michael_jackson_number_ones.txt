Title: Michael Jackson: Number Ones (2003)
Genres: Music
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Michael Jackson, Quincy Jones, George Clinton, Mary J. Blige, Jennifer Lopez
Keywords: pop, musical

Overview: Pop king Michael Jackson solidifies his title with this release of fifteen past number one hits. Songs include "Don't Stop 'Til You Get Enough," "Thriller," "Black or White," and "Blood on the Dancefloor."
