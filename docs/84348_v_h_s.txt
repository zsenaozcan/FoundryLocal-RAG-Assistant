Title: V/H/S (2012)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Joe Swanberg
Main Cast: Calvin Lee Reeder, Lane Hughes, Adam Wingard, Hannah Fierman, Mike Donlan
Keywords: webcam, anthology, vhs, burglary, split screen, found footage, mumblecore, ghost child, boxer shorts, vcr
