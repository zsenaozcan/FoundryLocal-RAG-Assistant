Title: Jack and the Beanstalk (1902)
Genres: Adventure, Family, Fantasy
Director: George S. Fleming
Main Cast: Thomas White

Overview: Porter's sequential continuity editing links several shots to form a narrative of the famous fairy tale story of Jack and his magic beanstalk. Borrowing on cinematographic methods reminiscent of 'Georges Melies' , Porter uses animation, double exposure, and trick photography to illustrate the fairy's apparitions, Jack's dream, and the fast growing beanstalk.
