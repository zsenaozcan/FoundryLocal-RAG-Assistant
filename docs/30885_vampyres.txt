Title: Vampyres (1974)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: José Ramón Larraz
Main Cast: Marianne Morris, Anulka Dziubinska, Murray Brown, Brian Deacon, Sally Faulkner
Keywords: vampire, lesbian

Overview: A lesbian vampire couple haunt an abandoned castle and the English country side around it. They prey on passing motorists, whom they seduce, and murder.
