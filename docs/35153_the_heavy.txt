Title: The Heavy (2010)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Adventure
Director: Marcus Warren
Main Cast: Gary Stretch, Vinnie Jones, Stephen Rea, Shannyn Sossamon, Christopher Lee
Keywords: revenge, suspense, sibling rivalry

Overview: Sibling rivalry turned into betrayal between two brothers. One a prime candidate for Prime Minister, the other a henchman for a successful, yet shady businessman. Presented with an opportunity to take revenge against his brother, our anti-hero must come to terms with the truth in a world where you can trust no one and loyalty is rare.
