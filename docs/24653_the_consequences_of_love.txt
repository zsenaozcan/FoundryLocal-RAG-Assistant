Title: The Consequences of Love (2004)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Paolo Sorrentino
Main Cast: Toni Servillo, Olivia Magnani, Adriano Giannini, Antonio Ballerio, Gianna Paola Scaffidi
Keywords: italy, switzerland, loneliness, mafia

Overview: Titta di Girolamo has been living in a small hotel in Lugano for ten years, he leads a dull and rigid live and has apparently lost his capability to feel human emotion. This all changes when he decides to talk to Sofia, the Barmaid.
