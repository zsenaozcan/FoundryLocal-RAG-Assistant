Title: Get Crazy (1983)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Allan Arkush
Main Cast: Malcolm McDowell, Allen Garfield, Daniel Stern, Gail Edwards, Miles Chapin
Keywords: new year's eve party

Overview: Mega-promoter Colin Beverly plans to sabotage the New Year's 1983 concert of small-time operator Max Wolfe. Wolfe's assistants Neil Allen and Willie Loman find romance while trying to save the drugs, violence, and rock and roll from Beverly's schemes.
