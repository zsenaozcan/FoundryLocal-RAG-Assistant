Title: Surrender, Dorothy (2006)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Romance
Director: Charles McDougall
Main Cast: Diane Keaton, Tom Everett Scott, Alexa Davalos, Lauren German, Josh Hopkins

Overview: When her daughter Sara unexpectedly passes away, Natalie retreats to the summer home where she and Sara used to visit. Time with her best friends and some of Sara's friends help her deal with her loss.
