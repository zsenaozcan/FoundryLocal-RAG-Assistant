Title: The Dark Mirror (1946)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Robert Siodmak
Main Cast: Olivia de Havilland, Lew Ayres, Thomas Mitchell, Richard Long, Charles Evans
Keywords: evil twin, film noir

Overview: De Havilland plays two roles in this entertaining psychological drama. A sister and her disturbed twin are implicated in a Hollywood murder and a police detective must figure out which one's the killer.
