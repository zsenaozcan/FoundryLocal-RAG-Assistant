Title: O Kadhal Kanmani (2015)
Genres: Drama, Family, Romance
Director: Mani Ratnam
Main Cast: Dulquer Salmaan, Nithya Menen, Prakash Raj, Leela Samson, Prabhu Lakshman
Keywords: living together, love, open relationship, sexual relationship, sentiments

Overview: Adhi and Tara are in a live-in relationship and but they are both unwilling to get married. How do they realize that marriage is just a natural progression of their relationship?
