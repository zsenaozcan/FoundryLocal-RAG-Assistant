Title: The Big Mouth (1967)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Action
Director: Jerry Lewis
Main Cast: Jerry Lewis, Harold J. Stone, Susan Bay, Buddy Lester, Del Moore
Keywords: fisherman, smuggling, look-alike, diamond

Overview: A fisherman crosses paths with a diamond-smuggling gangster who is his double and inadvertently takes his place at a resort hotel where he meets a special girl.
