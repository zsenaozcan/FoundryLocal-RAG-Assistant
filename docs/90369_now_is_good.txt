Title: Now Is Good (2012)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Ol Parker
Main Cast: Dakota Fanning, Jeremy Irvine, Kaya Scodelario, Olivia Williams, Paddy Considine
Keywords: cancer, teenager, teen drama, bucket list, based on young adult novel

Overview: A girl dying of leukemia compiles a list of things she'd like to do before passing away. Topping the list is her desire to lose her virginity.
