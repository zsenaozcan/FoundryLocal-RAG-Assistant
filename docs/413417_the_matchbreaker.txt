Title: The Matchbreaker (2016)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Caleb Vetter
Main Cast: Wesley Elder, Christina Grimmie, Osric Chau, Victoria Jackson, Ashley Shelton

Overview: When an idealistic romantic gets fired from his day job, he is offered a "one-time gig" to break up a girl's relationship for her disapproving parents. This "one-time" gig spreads through word-of-mouth and he ends up becoming a professional match-breaker. However, he ends up falling for one of his clients and must figure out how to balance his secret job with his love-life.
