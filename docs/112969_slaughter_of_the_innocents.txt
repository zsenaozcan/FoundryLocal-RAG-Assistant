Title: Slaughter of the Innocents (1993)
Genres: Horror, Action, Thriller, Mystery
Director: James Glickenhaus
Main Cast: Scott Glenn, Jesse Cameron-Glickenhaus, Sheila Tousey, Darlanne Fluegel, Zitto Kazann
Keywords: nun, fbi, kidnapping, murder, priest

Overview: A seasoned FBI Agent's child-genius son assists him on catching a child-killer, a schizophrenic mohab nut who believes he's been chosen by god to be a new Noah.
