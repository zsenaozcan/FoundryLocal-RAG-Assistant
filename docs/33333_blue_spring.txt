Title: Blue Spring (2001)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Action
Director: Toshiaki Toyoda
Main Cast: 松田龙平, Hirofumi Arai, Sousuke Takaoka, Yusuke Oshiba, Yuta Yamazaki

Overview: Soon after being named the new leader of his high school's gang system, Kujo grows bored with the violence and hatred that surround him. He wants desperately to abandon his post… but his once-enviable position of power has a strange way of making him feel powerless.
