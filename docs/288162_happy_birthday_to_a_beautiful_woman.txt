Title: Happy Birthday to a Beautiful Woman (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Mickalene Thomas
Main Cast: Sandra Bush II
Keywords: woman director

Overview: African-American painter Mickalene Thomas pays tribute to the life and longings of her mother and muse Sandra Bush in this documentary short. A former runway model with a poignant backstory filled with dreams, regrets and redemption, Sandra is the focus of this emotional portrait for her New York-based artist-daughter Mickalene, for whom she serves as a model and inspiration.
