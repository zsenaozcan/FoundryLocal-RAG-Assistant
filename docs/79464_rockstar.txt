Title: Rockstar (2011)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance
Director: Imtiaz Ali
Main Cast: Ranbir Kapoor, Nargis Fakhri, Shammi Kapoor, Aditi Rao Hydari, Kumud Mishra
Keywords: musical, rebellious youth, rumi  , rock musical, rockstar

Overview: An uninhibited youth woos the hottest about-to-be-married college girl. He rises to become a rock-star - then self-destructs.
