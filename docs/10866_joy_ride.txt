Title: Joy Ride (2001)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Drama
Director: John Dahl
Main Cast: Steve Zahn, Paul Walker, Leelee Sobieski, Jessica Bowman, Stuart Stone
Keywords: colorado, friendship, stalker, pokies, truck driver, cb radio, strange
