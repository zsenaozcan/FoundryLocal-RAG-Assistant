Title: The White Bus (1967)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Lindsay Anderson
Main Cast: Patricia Healey, Arthur Lowe, John Sharp, Julie Perry, Stephen Moore

Overview: An unnamed young woman (Patricia Healey) who is bored working in a London office takes a train to Manchester and ends up inexplicably deciding to board a white tourist bus that is packed with an eclectic assortment of international eccentrics. The bus stops at various locations including factories and the group is escorted about by the Lord Mayor (Arthur Lowe) who is decked out in full regalia.
