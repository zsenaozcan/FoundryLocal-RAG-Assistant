Title: The One Man Band (1970)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Serge Korber
Main Cast: Louis de Funès, Olivier De Funès, Noëlle Adam, Franco Volpi, Paola Tedesco

Overview: Evan Evans, the director of a ballet troupe, is rehearsing his next show in Monaco, in preparation for a worldwide tour. When one member of his troupe leaves to get married, Evans imposes a regime of strict discipline on his remaining dancers. The latter get their revenge by presenting Evans’ nephew Philippe, the only male member of the group, with a baby and a note claiming he is the father…
