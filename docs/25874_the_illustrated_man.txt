Title: The Illustrated Man (1969)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Jack Smight
Main Cast: Rod Steiger, Claire Bloom, Robert Drivas, Don Dubbins, Jason Evers
Keywords: africa, lion, future, story teller, hallucination, virtual reality, tramp, stranded, alternate dimension, spaceship

Overview: A man who has a body almost completely covered in tattoos is searching for the woman who cursed him with the "skin illustrations". Each tattoo reveals a bizarre story, which is experienced by staring at the scene depicted. When the illustrated man meets a fellow tramp on the road a strange voyage begins.
