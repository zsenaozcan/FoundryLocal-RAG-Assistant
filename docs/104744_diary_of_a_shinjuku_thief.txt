Title: Diary of a Shinjuku Thief (1969)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Nagisa Ōshima
Main Cast: Tadanori Yokoo, Rie Yokoyama, Fumio Watanabe, Kei Satô, Rokkô Toura
Keywords: city

Overview: This is the story of a bookstore thief named Birdy, who is led through various adventures in Tokyo's Shinjuku district by salesgirl Umeko.
