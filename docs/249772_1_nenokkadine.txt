Title: 1 - Nenokkadine (2014)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Romance
Director: Sukumar
Main Cast: Mahesh Babu, Kriti Sanon, Nassar, Anu Hasan, Kelly Dorji

Overview: Gautham is a rock star suffering from a mental disorder due to which he loses grip over reality. But he's convinced his parents were murdered by three men and his only goal is revenge.
