Title: Belladonna of Sadness (1973)
Genres: Animation, Drama, Fantasy, Music
Director: Eiichi Yamamoto
Main Cast: Aiko Nagayama, Tatsuya Nakadai, Katsuyuki Itô, Masaya Takahashi, Shigako Shimegi
Keywords: pact with the devil, unique visuals, loss of innocence

Overview: A peasant woman is banished from her village and makes a deal with the devil to gain magical powers.
