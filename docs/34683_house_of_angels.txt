Title: House of Angels (1992)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Colin Nutley
Main Cast: Helena Bergström, Rikard Wolff, Sven Wollter, Reine Brynolfsson, Ernst Günther
Keywords: countryside, small town, gay interest, inheritance, conservatism, free spirit, cottage, motorcycle

Overview: In a small village in Västergötland, everything is turned upside-down when the local loner dies and his cottage is inherited by his unknown relative Fanny. She rides into tow on a big black motorcycle with her androgenic boyfriend Zack, wearing black leather and spikes. When they decide to stay, Flogfält's plans to buy the cottage are upset.
