Title: The Jungle Book (1994)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Family
Director: Stephen Sommers
Main Cast: Jason Scott Lee, Cary Elwes, Lena Headey, Sam Neill, John Cleese
Keywords: indian lead, tiger, feral child, british, panther, bear, jungle, falling in love

Overview: Raised by wild animals since childhood, Mowgli is drawn away from the jungle by the beautiful Kitty. But Mowgli must eventually face corrupt Capt. Boone, who wants both Kitty's hand and the treasures of Monkey City – a place only Mowgli can find.
