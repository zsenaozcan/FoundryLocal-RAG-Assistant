Title: Senso (1954)
Genres: Romance, Drama, History
Director: Luchino Visconti
Main Cast: Alida Valli, Farley Granger, Heinz Moog, Rina Morelli, Christian Marquand
Keywords: italian, countess

Overview: A troubled and neurotic Italian Countess betrays her entire country for a self-destructive love affair with an Austrian Lieutenant.
