Title: Anna Karenina (1935)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Clarence Brown
Main Cast: Greta Garbo, Fredric March, Freddie Bartholomew, Maureen O'Sullivan, May Robson
Keywords: based on novel, russia, marriage, love, train

Overview: In 19th century Russia a woman in a respectable marriage to a doctor must grapple with her love for a dashing soldier.
