Title: The Silent Mountain (2014)
Genres: War, Adventure, Drama, Romance
Director: Ernst Gossner
Main Cast: William Moseley, Eugenia Costantini, Claudia Cardinale, Werner Daehn, Corrado Invernizzi
Keywords: adventure, war, suspense

Overview: A young Austrian soldier in World War I fights his way through the Alps to rescue his Italian girlfriend and escape the impending explosion that will rock the mountain.
