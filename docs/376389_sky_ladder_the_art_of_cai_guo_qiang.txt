Title: Sky Ladder: The Art of Cai Guo-Qiang (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Kevin Macdonald
Main Cast: Cai Guo-Qiang
Keywords: fireworks, portrait of an artist

Overview: Known for his spectacular pyrotechnic displays, Chinese artist Cai Guo-Qiang creates his most ambitious project yet: Sky Ladder, a visionary, explosive event that he pulls off in his hometown in China after 20 years of failed attempts.
