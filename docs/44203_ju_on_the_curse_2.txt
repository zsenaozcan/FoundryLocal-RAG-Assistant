Title: Ju-on: The Curse 2 (2000)
Genres: Horror
Director: Takashi Shimizu
Main Cast: Yūrei Yanagi, Takako Fuji, Takashi Matsuyama, Yûko Daike, Hua Rong Weng
Keywords: haunted house, sequel, vengeful ghost, ghost, ju-on

Overview: On his request, the sensitive sister of a real estate agent visits a house he intends to put on sell, only to cross paths with its resident curse.
