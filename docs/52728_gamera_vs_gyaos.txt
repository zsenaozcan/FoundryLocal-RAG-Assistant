Title: Gamera vs. Gyaos (1967)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Noriaki Yuasa
Main Cast: Kojiro Hongo, Kichijirô Ueda, Reiko Kasahara, Naoyuki Abe, Taro Marui
Keywords: japan, monster, bat, turtle, sequel, giant monster, scientist, kaiju, gamera, giant turtle

Overview: Gamera's back, and just in time to save Japan from Gaos, a mysterious bat-like creature awakened by a volcanic eruption. As in the first Gamera movie, a young boy establishes an emotional link with Gamera, and the two work together, with the help of the world's scientists, to put and end to Gaos' violent rampage.
