Title: Future by Design (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: William Gazecki
Main Cast: Jacque Fresco, Roxanne Meadows, William Gazecki, Larry King

Overview: Future by Design shares the life and far-reaching vision of Jacque Fresco, considered by many to be a modern day Da Vinci. Peer to Einstein and Buckminster Fuller, Jacque is a self-taught futurist who describes himself most often as a "generalist" or multi-disciplinarian -- a student of many inter-related fields.
