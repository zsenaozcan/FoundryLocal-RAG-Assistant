Title: The Feast of the Goat (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Luis Llosa
Main Cast: Tomás Milián, Isabella Rossellini, Juan Diego Botto, Paul Freeman, Stephanie Leonidas

Overview: Urania Cabral returns to Santo Domingo, after several years, and remembers her and her family's relationship to Rafael Trujillo, the Dominican dictator, as well as the events surrounding his assassination.
