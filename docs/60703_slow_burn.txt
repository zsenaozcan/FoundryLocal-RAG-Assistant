Title: Slow Burn (2000)
Genres: Action, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Rodney Gibbons
Main Cast: Pam Grier, Rutger Hauer, Romano Orzari, Simone-Elise Girard, John Dunn-Hill
Keywords: detective, pharmaceutical industry, suspense, independent film, series of murders, female detective

Overview: The murder of the ex-wife of Doctor Sam Charney (Rutger Hauer) leads Detective Della Wilder (Pam Grier) to uncover a series of women's murders somehow linked to a big pharmaceutical company.
