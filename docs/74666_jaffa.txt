Title: Jaffa (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Keren Yedaya
Main Cast: Dana Ivgy, Moni Moshonov, Ronit Elkabetz, Mahmud Shalaby, Hussein Yassin Mahajne
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In the city of Jaffa; a young girl plans to run away with her secret lover, when a tragedy forever changes the course of their lives.
