Title: Tender Scoundrel (1966)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Jean Becker
Main Cast: Jean-Paul Belmondo, Philippe Noiret, Robert Morley, Geneviève Page, Nadja Tiller

Overview: Jean-Paul Belmondo is a lovable lothario who delights in his womanizing ways in this ribald comedy adventure. When two women can't get enough of him, he is chased to Tahiti and back to Paris by admiring females. His experiences are exhausting to the point that he considers giving up his life as a ladies man.
