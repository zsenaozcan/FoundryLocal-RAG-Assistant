Title: Creature from the Black Lagoon (1954)
Genres: Adventure, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Jack Arnold
Main Cast: Richard Carlson, Julie Adams, Richard Denning, Antonio Moreno, Nestor Paiva
Keywords: monster, boat, paleontology, expedition, chase, ship, amazon, water monster, creature, scientist

Overview: A scientific expedition searching for fossils along the Amazon River discover a prehistoric Gill-Man in the legendary Black Lagoon. The explorers capture the mysterious creature, but it breaks free. The Gill-Man returns to kidnap the lovely Kay, fiancée of one of the expedition, with whom it has fallen in love.
