Title: Envy (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Zeki Demirkubuz
Main Cast: Serhat Tutumluer, Berrak Tuzunatac, Nergis Öztürk, Bora Cengiz, Hasibe Eren
Keywords: turkey, envy

Overview: Seniha lives with her brother Halit and his wife Mükerrem. When Nüzhet, the handsome son of the richest family in town, sets his eyes on the married Mükerrem, she is at first disgusted with Nüzhet’s overt sexual innuendo. However, it isn’t long before Mükerrem yields to her lust and thus they embark on an affair. When Seniha finally reveals her sister-in-law’s un-virtuousness to her brother, Seniha has no idea what the consequences will be and how cruelly her destiny will change.
