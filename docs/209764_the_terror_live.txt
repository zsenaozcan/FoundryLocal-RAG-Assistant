Title: The Terror Live (2013)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Byungwoo Kim
Main Cast: Ha Jung-woo, Lee Kyoung-young, Jeon Hye-Jin, Lee Da-wit, Kim So-jin

Overview: "I'll blow up Mapo Bridge." An anonymous call comes into a radio station one day. When it turns out to be legitimate, a terrorist's threats end up getting broadcast live throughout the country.
