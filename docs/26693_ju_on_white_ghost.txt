Title: Ju-on: White Ghost (2009)
Genres: Horror
Director: Ryuta Miyake
Main Cast: Hiroki Suzuki, Ichirôta Miyakawa, Natsuki Kasa, Mihiro, Akina Minami
Keywords: curse, vengeful ghost, ju-on

Overview: Akane begins seeing visions of a female ghost wearing the same yellow hat and red satchel she wore as a school child.
