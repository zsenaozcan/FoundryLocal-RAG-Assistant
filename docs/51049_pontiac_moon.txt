Title: Pontiac Moon (1994)
Genres: Drama
Director: Peter Medak
Main Cast: Ted Danson, Mary Steenburgen, Ryan Todd, Eric Schweig, Cathy Moriarty
Keywords: road trip, absent minded, pontiac

Overview: An absent-minded-professor father and his son take off in an old Pontiac to bond during a symbolic road trip through the Western U.S. This while his wife tries to overcome her neuroses to save the family.
