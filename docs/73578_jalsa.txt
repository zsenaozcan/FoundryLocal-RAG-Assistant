Title: Jalsa (2008)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Pawan Kalyan, Ileana D'Cruz, Brahmanandam, Prakash Raj, Kamalinee Mukherjee

Overview: Sanjay (Pawan Kalyan) falls in love with Indu (Kamalini Mukherjee). Her father Rammohan Reddy (Prakash Raj) doesnt approve of it &amp; she is married to someone else. Later, Bhagyamathi (Ileana) falls for him &amp; he goes to meet her father who turns out to be Rammohan Reddy. Somewhere in between Sanjays life as a naxal is depicted.
