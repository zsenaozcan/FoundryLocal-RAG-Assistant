Title: Underground: The Julian Assange Story (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Robert Connolly
Main Cast: Callan McAuliffe, Anthony LaPaglia, Alex Williams, Laura Wheelwright, Rachel Griffiths
Keywords: technology, hacker, asylum, biography, political, computer hacker

Overview: Julian Assange is one of the most significant figures of the twenty first century. But before he was famous, before WikiLeaks, before the internet even existed, he was a teenage computer hacker in Melbourne. This is his story.  In 1989, known as ‘Mendax’, Assange and two friends formed a group called the ‘International Subversives’. Using early home computers and defining themselves as ‘white hat hackers’ - those who look but don’t steal – they broke into some of the world’s most powerful and secretive organisations. They were young, brilliant, and in the eyes of the US Government, a major threat to national security.
