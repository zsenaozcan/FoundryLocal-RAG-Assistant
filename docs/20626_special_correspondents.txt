Title: Special Correspondents (2009)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Frédéric Auburtin
Main Cast: Gérard Lanvin, Gérard Jugnot, Omar Sy, Anne Marivin, Valérie Kaprisky

Overview: R2I, a famous news radio station, sends its best reporting duo to Iraq: Frank, journalist, and Poussin, sound engineer. Very quickly, millions of listeners follow their highly documented stories, reflecting the hot intensity of the fighting and the difficult survival of the population.
