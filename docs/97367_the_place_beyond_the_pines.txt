Title: The Place Beyond the Pines (2013)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Derek Cianfrance
Main Cast: Ryan Gosling, Bradley Cooper, Eva Mendes, Dane DeHaan, Emory Cohen
Keywords: corruption, father son relationship, carnival, mechanic, motorcycle, bank robbery
