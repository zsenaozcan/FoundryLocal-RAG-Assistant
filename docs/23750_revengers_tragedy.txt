Title: Revengers Tragedy (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Foreign, Science Fiction
Director: Alex Cox
Main Cast: Christopher Eccleston, Eddie Izzard, Derek Jacobi, Diana Quick, Marc Warren
Keywords: dystopia, revenge, wedding

Overview: Revengers Tragedy is a film adaptation of the 1606 play The Revenger's Tragedy It was directed by Alex Cox and adapted for the screen by Cox's fellow Liverpudlian, Frank Cottrell Boyce. The film stars Christopher Eccleston as the revenge-obsessed Vindice, with Derek Jacobi and Eddie Izzard.
