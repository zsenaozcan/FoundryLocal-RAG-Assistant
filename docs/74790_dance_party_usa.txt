Title: Dance Party, USA (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Aaron Katz
Main Cast: Cole Pennsinger, Anna Kavan, Ryan White, Sarah Bing, Natalie Buller
Keywords: drama

Overview: Jessica and Gus, two apathetic teenagers, drift aimlessly from one day to the next until they meet each other. They make a tenuous and fleeting connection when Gus confides in Jessica about his dark past.
