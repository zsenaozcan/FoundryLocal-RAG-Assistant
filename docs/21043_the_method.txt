Title: The Method (2005)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Marcelo Piñeyro
Main Cast: Eduardo Noriega, Najwa Nimri, Eduard Fernández, Pablo Echarri, Ernesto Alterio
Keywords: sex, career, ambition, treason

Overview: In Madrid seven candidates report for a job interview that uses "the Grönholm method" of selection, as protestors rise up in public protest in the street over the IMF-World Bank Summit attempting globalisation of workers' unions.
