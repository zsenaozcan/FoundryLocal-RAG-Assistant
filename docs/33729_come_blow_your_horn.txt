Title: Come Blow Your Horn (1963)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Bud Yorkin
Main Cast: Frank Sinatra, Lee J. Cobb, Molly Picon, Tony Bill, Barbara Rush
Keywords: new york, jewish family

Overview: The story of a young man's decision to leave the home of his parents for the bachelor pad of his older brother who leads a swinging '60s lifestyle.
