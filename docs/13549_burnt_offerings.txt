Title: Burnt Offerings (1976)
Genres: Horror
Director: Dan Curtis
Main Cast: Karen Black, Oliver Reed, Burgess Meredith, Bette Davis, Eileen Heckart
Keywords: coffin, old dark house, old house

Overview: A couple and their 12-year-old son move into a giant house for the summer. Things start acting strange almost immediately. It seems that every time some gets hurt on the grounds the beat-up house seems to repair itself.
