Title: Life During Wartime (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Todd Solondz
Main Cast: Ally Sheedy, Paul Reubens, Shirley Henderson, Allison Janney, Michael Kenneth Williams
Keywords: dark comedy, independent film

Overview: Friends, family, and lovers struggle to find love, forgiveness, and meaning in an almost war-torn world riddled with comedy and pathos. Follows Solondz's film Happiness (1998).
