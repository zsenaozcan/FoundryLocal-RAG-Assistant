Title: Journey to the Center of the Earth (1999)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Action, Science Fiction, Family
Director: George T. Miller
Main Cast: Treat Williams, Jeremy London, Tushka Bergen, Hugh Keays-Byrne, Bryan Brown
Keywords: dinosaur

Overview: Remake of Jules Verne's classic story finds adventurers seeking a mysterious hidden land. They are joined by the wife of another man, who had previously gone on a similar expedition and disappeared.
