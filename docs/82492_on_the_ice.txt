Title: On the Ice (2011)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Andrew Okpeaha MacLean
Main Cast: Josiah Patkotak , Frank Qutuq Irelan, Teddy Kyle Smith, Adamina Kerr, Sierra Jade Sampson
Keywords: grief, alaska, based on short film, death

Overview: In Barrow, Alaska, teenagers Qalli and Aivaaq find their bond tested when a seal-hunting trip goes wrong, resulting in the death of their friend.
