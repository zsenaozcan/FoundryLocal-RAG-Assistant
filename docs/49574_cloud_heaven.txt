Title: Cloud Heaven (1990)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Nikolai Dostal
Main Cast: Andrei Zhigalov, Sergey Batalov, Irina Rozanova, Alla Kliouka, Anna Ovsyannikova

Overview: In this gentle comedy, an unpopular resident in a Russian village has his life completely changed when he announces, entirely on a whim and just to upset things a bit, that he's moving to the Pacific coast.
