Title: Always in My Heart (1942)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Jo Graham
Main Cast: Kay Francis, Walter Huston, Gloria Warren, Patti Hale, Frankie Thomas
Keywords: prison, musical, daughter, melodrama

Overview: A man is pardoned from prison and returns to Santa Rita, CA to be with his family, but discovers his children have been told he's dead and his wife is in love with another man.
