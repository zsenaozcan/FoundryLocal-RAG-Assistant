Title: Why (1971)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Nanni Loy
Main Cast: Alberto Sordi, Elga Andersen, Andrea Aureli, Nazzareno Natale, Michele Gammino
Keywords: bureaucracy, prison, italian, law, innocence, satire, injustice, victimization

Overview: Giuseppe Di Noi, an Italian surveyor living in Switzerland, gets arrested at the border while going back to Italy with his family for a vacation. But can someone tell the man WHY?
