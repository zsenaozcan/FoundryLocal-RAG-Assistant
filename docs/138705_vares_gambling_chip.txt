Title: Vares: Gambling Chip (2012)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Lauri Törhönen
Main Cast: Antti Reini, Minna Haapkylä, Risto Kaskilahti, Ilkka Heiskanen, Kaarina Hazard

Overview: Jussi Vares falls in love with a beautiful brunette - only to lose her in a horrible way. After dating Sole a few weeks, her body is found in a bird-watching tower. The investigations come to a standstill, until Vares promises to solve a mystery of messy denunciation letters. Before the letters reveal their secret, a lot of things happen. A gun-for-hire, Stahanov, gets an assignment. Sole's brother finds his wife in the arms of a wrong man and goes on a rampage, accompanied by a black cat.
