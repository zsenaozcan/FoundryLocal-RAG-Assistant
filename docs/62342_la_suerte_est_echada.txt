Title: La suerte está echada (2005)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Sebastián Borensztein
Main Cast: Gastón Pauls, Marcelo Mazzarello, Leticia Brédice, Lucrecia Capello, Gerardo Chendo

Overview: Felipe, an aspiring actor, believes he is jinxed after an unfortunate event. His struggle to find the cause and cure of his jinx. His half-brother is not doing much better, but among the two of them embark on a mission to change their lives and fulfill their father's dream.
