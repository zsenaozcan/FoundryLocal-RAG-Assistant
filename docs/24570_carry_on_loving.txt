Title: Carry On Loving (1970)
Genres: Romance, Comedy

Main Cast: Sid James, Kenneth Williams, Charles Hawtrey, Joan Sims, Hattie Jacques

Its owner, Sidney Bliss, has enough complications in his own love life, but still produces a pamphlet called "The Wit to Woo".
