Title: New World (2013)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Park Hoon-Jung
Main Cast: Lee Jung-jae, Choi Min-sik, Park Sung-woong, Hwang Jung-min, Song Ji-hyo
Keywords: undercover cop, gangster, south korea, gang warfare, criminal gang, neo-noir

Overview: A detective infiltrates into one of the biggest gang organizations in the country and gets involved in a fight for the heir to the gang after the boss dies and in between the second in charge who trusts him with his life and the high police officials who think of him only as bait.
