Title: Vovka in the Kingdom of Far Far Away (1965)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Family, Fantasy
Director: Boris Stepantsev
Main Cast: Rina Zelyonaya, Klara Rumyanova, Mikhail Yanshin, Elena Ponsova, Emma Treyvas

Overview: A schoolboy gets into a fairy tale with the help of a librarian and DIY book. But life in the magical land is not as easy as it seems.
