Title: Percentage (2013)
Genres: Action, Crime
Director: Alex Merkin
Main Cast: Cam'ron, Omar Gooding, Macy Gray, Ving Rhames, Malinda Williams

Overview: After a drug deal gone wrong, two New York City hustlers relocate to Miami, where they set up a lucrative credit card fraud operation. Unfortunately, their new business not only attracts the police, but the city's top criminals -- and both want a percentage of the take.
