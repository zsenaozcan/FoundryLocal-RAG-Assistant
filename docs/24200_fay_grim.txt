Title: Fay Grim (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Thriller
Director: Hal Hartley
Main Cast: Parker Posey, D.J. Mendel, Liam Aiken, Megan Gay, Jasmin Tabatabai
Keywords: cia, fugitive

Overview: Many years after her notorious husband, Henry Fool, fled after killing a neighbor, Fay Grim receives a visit from CIA agent Fulbright, who tells her that Henry is dead, but that some of his journals have been unearthed in France. She sets forth on a globe-trotting odyssey that soon leads to the discovery that he is alive, and his journals are more than they appear to be.
