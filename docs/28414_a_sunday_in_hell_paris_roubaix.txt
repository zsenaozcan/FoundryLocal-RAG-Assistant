Title: A Sunday in Hell: Paris-Roubaix (1977)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jørgen Leth
Main Cast: Roger De Vlaeminck, Marc Demeyer, Freddy Maertens, Eddy Merckx, Ole Ritter
Keywords: sport, cycling

Overview: A Sunday in Hell (original title: En Forårsdag i Helvede) is a 1977 Danish documentary directed by Jørgen Leth. The film is a chronology of the 1976 Paris-Roubaix bicycle race from the perspective of participants, organizers and spectators.
