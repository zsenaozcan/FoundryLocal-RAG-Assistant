Title: First Position (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary, Drama
Director: Bess Kargman
Main Cast: Aran Bell, Rebecca Houseknecht, Joan Sebastian Zamora, Miko Fogarty, Jules Jarvis Fogarty
Keywords: dancer, ballet, duringcreditsstinger, woman director
