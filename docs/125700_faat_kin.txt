Title: Faat Kiné (2001)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ousmane Sembene
Main Cast: Venus Seye, Mame Ndoumbé Diop, Ndiagne Dia, Mariama Balde, Awa Sene Sarr
Keywords: feminism, single mother

Overview: A forty-year-old woman refuses to give into the stigma of unwed motherhood and climbs the ladder of success in a male dominated field.
