Title: Amer (2009)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Hélène Cattet
Main Cast: Marie Bos, Delphine Brual, Cassandra Forêt, Charlotte Eugène-Guibbaud, Jean-Michel Vovk
Keywords: sex, razor, suspense, sensuality, gloves, bitterness, woman director

Overview: Searing memories and carnal desires rule the mind of Ana, a young woman in thrall to her own fantasies whose visions and obsessions draw her toward deeper eroticism -- and deeper danger, in this modern take on a Giallo.
