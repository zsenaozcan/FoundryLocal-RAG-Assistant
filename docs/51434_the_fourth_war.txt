Title: The Fourth War (1990)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: John Frankenheimer
Main Cast: Roy Scheider, Jürgen Prochnow, Lara Harris, Harry Dean Stanton, Dale Dye
Keywords: germany, eastern germany, cold war, border, czechoslovakia

Overview: Cold War drama about two gung-ho border commanders (Roy Scheider, Jurgen Prochnow) who carry out their own private war against each other on the German - Czechoslovakia border.
