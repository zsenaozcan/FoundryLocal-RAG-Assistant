Title: Rock, Paper, Scissors: The Way of the Tosser (2007)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Tim Doiron, April Mullen, Ryan Tilley, Peter Pasyk, Mairtin O'Carrigan

Overview: This hilarious genre-bending comedy follows Gary Brewer, along with girlfriend Holly Brewer (no relation), and best friend Trevor Morehouse as Gary quests to reign supreme at the 2006 Rock, Paper, Scissors World Championships.
