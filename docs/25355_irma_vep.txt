Title: Irma Vep (1996)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Horror
Director: Olivier Assayas
Main Cast: Maggie Cheung, Jean-Pierre Léaud, Nathalie Richard, Antoine Basler, Nathalie Boutefeu
Keywords: independent film, anarchic comedy

Overview: A Chinese movie actress, in France to star in a remake of "Les Vampires", finds petty intrigues and clashing egos on the set.
