Title: El patrullero 777 (1978)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Miguel M. Delgado
Main Cast: Cantinflas, Ana Bertha Lepe, Valeria Pani, Wolf Ruvinskis, Ramón Menéndez
Keywords: pimp, cabaret, investigation, arrest, police, melodrama, undercover cop, police department

Overview: Follows Diogenes as he goes about his day as a police officer. He's prone to do things the right way, surrounded by a pretty much corrupt &amp; citizen distant police department, &amp; always resolves his duties with a personal method.
