Title: Dating Games People Play (2008)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Stefan Marc
Main Cast: Austin Peck, Leslie Bega, Stephanie Brown, Herb Armstrong

Overview: Nick Jenkins finds himself back in the single scene as his relationship with his fiancee abruptly fall apart. With his roommate and best friend Jed Rollins, Nick jumps into the dating world. Mona, Jed, and Robin accompany him as they meander throught the quagmire of awkward situations they encounter on their dating escapades.
