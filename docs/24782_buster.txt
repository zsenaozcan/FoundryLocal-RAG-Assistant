Title: Buster (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: David Green
Main Cast: Phil Collins, Julie Walters, Larry Lamb, Ralph Brown, Christopher Ellison
Keywords: mexico, robbery, scotland yard, heist, train, 1960s

Overview: Buster is a small time crook who pulls a big time job. When he finds that the police will not let the case drop, he goes into hiding and can't contact his wife and child. He arranges to meet them in Mexico where he thinks they can begin again, but finds that he must choose between his family and freedom.
