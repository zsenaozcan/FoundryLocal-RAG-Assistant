Title: White Water Summer (1987)
Genres: Family, Adventure, Drama
Director: Jeff Bleckner
Main Cast: Kevin Bacon, Sean Astin, Jonathan Ward, K.C. Martel, Matt Adler
Keywords: wilderness, friendship, survival, coming of age, summer, nature, guide, tough love

Overview: An experienced guide (Vic) accompanies a city boy (Alan) and his three friends on their first wilderness experience. Hoping to teach the four boys lessons not only about the wilderness, but about themselves, Vic pushes them to the limit. Soon after alienating the boys, Vic finds himself in desperate need of help and must rely on his students in order to survive.
