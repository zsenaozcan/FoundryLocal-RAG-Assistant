Title: ...tick... tick... tick... (1970)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Ralph Nelson
Main Cast: Jim Brown, George Kennedy, Fredric March, Lynn Carlin, Don Stroud
Keywords: racial tension, racial prejudice, black cop, small town sheriff, deep south

Overview: Racial tensions threaten to explode when a black man is elected sheriff of a small, racially divided town in the Deep South.
