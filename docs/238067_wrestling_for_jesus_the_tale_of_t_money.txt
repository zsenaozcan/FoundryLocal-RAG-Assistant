Title: Wrestling For Jesus: The Tale of T-Money (2011)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Nathan Clarke
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: christianity, jesus christ, wrestling, rural setting

Overview: A man from rural South Carolina tries to find solace from the demons of his past by starting a Christian professional wrestling league. His goal is to use wrestling to evangelize his neighbors. However his passion and vision for his ministry are tested when his personal life begins to disintegrate.
