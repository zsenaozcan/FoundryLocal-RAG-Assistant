Title: Snake Woman's Curse (1968)
Genres: Horror
Director: Nobuo Nakagawa
Main Cast: Yukiko Kuwahara, Yukie Kagawa, Seizaburô Kawazu, Kô Nishimura, Kunio Murai
Keywords: rape, snake, virgin, bride, horror movie, reptilian scale

Overview: An old man dies of heartbreak when a cruel landlord is about to repossess his land. The old man haunts the landlord from the grave. Since the man died in debt, his wife and daughter are indentured servants and are forced to work in the landlord's factory.
