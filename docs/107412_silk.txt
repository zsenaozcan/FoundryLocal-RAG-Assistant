Title: Silk (1986)
Genres: Action, Drama, Crime
Director: Cirio H. Santiago
Main Cast: Cec Verrell, Bill McLaughlin, Joe Mari Avellana, Frederick Bailey

Overview: Silk, the toughest cop in Honolulu, busts small time smugglers only to reveal a larger syndicate smuggling Asian mobsters into the States by buying the identities of Hawaiian citizens.
