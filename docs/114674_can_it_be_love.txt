Title: Can It Be Love (1992)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Peter Maris
Main Cast: Charles Klausmeyer, Richard Beaumont, Mary Ann Mixon, Jennifer Langdon, Blake Pickett

Overview: Tim and David want to have fun during vacation so they join a private detective agency. Their first job is to find a delta girl who has inherited a fortune. But the job seems easier than it turned out to be... Written by Volker Boehm
