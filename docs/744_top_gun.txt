Title: Top Gun (1986)
Genres: Action, Romance, War
Director: Tony Scott
Main Cast: Tom Cruise, Kelly McGillis, Val Kilmer, Anthony Edwards, Tom Skerritt
Keywords: lovesickness, loss of lover, fighter pilot, self-discovery, pilot, ejection seat, dying and death, officer, training camp, air force

Overview: For Lieutenant Pete 'Maverick' Mitchell and his friend and Co-Pilot Nick 'Goose' Bradshaw being accepted into an elite training school for fighter pilots is a dream come true.  A tragedy, as well as personal demons, threaten Pete's dreams of becoming an Ace pilot.
