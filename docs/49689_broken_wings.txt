Title: Broken Wings (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nir Bergman
Main Cast: Orly Silbersatz Banai, Maya Maron, Daniel Magon, Vladimir Friedman, Dana Ivgy
Keywords: haifa israel

Overview: Daphne and her four children try to cope with the abrupt death of husband/father. As the family seems to fall apart, a sudden incident gives them a chance to heal their 'broken wings'.
