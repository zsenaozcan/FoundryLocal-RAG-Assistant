Title: Around June (2011)
Genres: Animation, Drama, Fantasy, Romance
Director: James Savoca
Main Cast: Samaire Armstrong, Brad William Henke, Jon Gries, Oscar H. Guerrero, Michael A. Goorjian

Overview: A love story set in the shadow of the shipyards of San Francisco. A young woman living with her much adored uncle Henry and domineering, abusive father, meets a penniless illegal immigrant, whose love gives her the insight and courage to finally break free and live her own life.
