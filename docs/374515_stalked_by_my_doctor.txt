Title: Stalked by My Doctor (2015)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Doug Campbell
Main Cast: Eric Roberts, Brianna Joy Chomer, Deborah Zoe, Jon Briddell, Carson Boatman

Overview: When a teenage girl is miraculously saved by a heart surgeon, the doctor begins to flirt with her. Her father doesn’t believe her and unbeknownst to all, the doctor is obsessed with the girl.
