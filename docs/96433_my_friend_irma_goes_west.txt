Title: My Friend Irma Goes West (1950)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Hal Walker
Main Cast: Marie Wilson, Diana Lynn, Dean Martin, Jerry Lewis, John Lund
Keywords: show business, musical

Overview: Singer Steve, friend Seymour and fiance Jane, along with her dizzy blonde room mate Irma, have a series of misadventures on a California-bound train and end up involved with a gang of murderous gangsters in Las Vegas.
