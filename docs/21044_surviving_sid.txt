Title: Surviving Sid (2008)
Genres: Animation
Director: Galen T. Chu
Main Cast: John Leguizamo, Chris Wedge, Shane Baumel, Paul Butcher, Sean S. Cunningham

Overview: Sid, the sloth, takes a school of children out on a camping trip from home, only to find that in typical Sid style, he is not a very good guide and the children he takes with him don't have a very good time.
