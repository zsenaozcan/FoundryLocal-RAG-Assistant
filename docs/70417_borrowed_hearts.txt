Title: Borrowed Hearts (1997)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Romance
Director: Ted Kotcheff
Main Cast: Roma Downey, Eric McCormack, Shawn Alex Thompson, Janet Bailey, Kevin Hicks
Keywords: christmas

Overview: Single mom Kathleen Russell (Roma Downey) and her daughter Zoey pretend to be Kathleen's boss's "family" so he can close a major business deal with the mysterious Mexican financier Javier Del Campo.
