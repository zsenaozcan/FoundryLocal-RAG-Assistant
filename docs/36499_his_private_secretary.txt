Title: His Private Secretary (1933)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Phil Whitman
Main Cast: Evalyn Knapp, John Wayne, Reginald Barlow, Alec B. Francis, Arthur Hoyt
Keywords: office, gold digger

Overview: Dick Wallace wants to marry a minister's grand-daughter but his father, who wants him to get work on his company's business, is opposed. She takes a job with the company to prove she's okay.
