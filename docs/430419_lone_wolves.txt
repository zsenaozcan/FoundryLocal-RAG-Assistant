Title: Lone Wolves (2016)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Christopher Jacobs
Main Cast: Dan Purdey, Warren Otteraa, Alana Tranter, David Leeming
Keywords: monster, space station, post apocalypse

Overview: After an apocalypse of questionable origin, Private James Conroy is living in an abandoned shack at the base of a radio tower. When Conroy finally contacts Garry Freeman, marooned in a space station, they have to learn to work together if they're going to save themselves, and maybe all of civilization.
