Title: The Miracle of Morgan’s Creek (1944)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, War
Director: Preston Sturges
Main Cast: Eddie Bracken, Betty Hutton, Diana Lynn, William Demarest, Porter Hall
Keywords: u.s. soldier, small town girl

Overview: A small-town girl with a soft spot for American soldiers wakes up the morning after a wild farewell party for the troops to find that she married someone she can't remember.
