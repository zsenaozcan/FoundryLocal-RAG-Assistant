Title: The Emperor of California (1936)
Genres: Drama, Western
Director: Luis Trenker
Main Cast: Luis Trenker, Viktoria von Ballasko, Elise Aulinger, Bernhard Minetti, Werner Kunig

Overview: The film follows the life story of Johann Augustus Suter, the owner of Sutter's Mill, famous as the birthplace of the great California Gold Rush of 1848.
