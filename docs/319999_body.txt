Title: Body (2015)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Małgorzata Szumowska
Main Cast: Janusz Gajos, Maja Ostaszewska, Justyna Suwala, Ewa Dalkowska, Roman Gancarczyk
Keywords: regret, therapist, anorexia, dead child, lawyer, psychiatrist, dog, father daughter relationship, death of mother, suicidal thoughts

Overview: A busy attorney, worried that his anorexic daughter Olga might try to harm herself, since she's still grieving over her recently deceased mother, sends her to see a psychiatrist, Anna, who's dealing with her own loss in an unusual way.
