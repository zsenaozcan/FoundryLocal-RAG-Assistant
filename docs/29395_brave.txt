Title: Brave (1994)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Richard Stanley
Main Cast: Josie Ayers, Russell Copley, Raymond Sawyer, Anthony Story, Jema Hewitt
Keywords: suicide, depression, homeless person, suicide attempt, amnesia, paranoia, homelessness, musical, marillion, psychopathy

Overview: Cult director Richard Stanley brings Marillion's music to the screen in the 50-minute BRAVE. A teenager believed to be suicidal is discovered wandering near the Severn Bridge. Suffering from severe memory loss, she seeks information about the mysterious events that led to her condition. This work of fiction was inspired from the true story of an amnesiac woman found at the bridge.
