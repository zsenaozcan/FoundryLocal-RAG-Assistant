Title: Beyond the Hill (2012)
Genres: Mystery, Drama
Director: Emin Alper
Main Cast: Tamer Levent, Reha Özcan, Mehmet Ozgur, Berk Hakman, Banu Fotocan
Keywords: turkey, family

Overview: In the quiet foothills of Turkey, Faik lives an isolated existence. When his second son brings his boys for a visit, Faik takes the opportunity to pontificate about the law of the land, as he sees it. He shares one unsolicited thought after the next, most particularly focusing on the elusive nomads whom he suspects have been trespassing on his property. The day and night wear on, and each member of the clan takes his turn entrusting the film's audience with his own dark secret.
