Title: Bal-Can-Can (2005)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Crime, Drama, War
Director: Darko Mitrevski
Main Cast: Vlado Jovanovski, Adolfo Margiotta, Zvezda Angelovska, Branko Đurić, Antonella Troise
Keywords: gangster

Overview: A Macedonian military deserter and his Italian blood-brother are searching for a dead grandmother wrapped up in a stolen carpet, all over the Balkan's criminal underworld.
