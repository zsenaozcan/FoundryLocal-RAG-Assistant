Title: One Dark Night (1983)
Genres: Horror
Director: Tom McLoughlin
Main Cast: Meg Tilly, Melissa Newman, Robin Evans, Leslie Speights, Donald Hotton
Keywords: telekinesis, initiation

Overview: A strange man named Karl Rhamarevich dies shortly after discovering a way to become even more powerful in death through telekinesis. On the night of his burial in a crypt, Julie is to spend the night there as part of an initiation rite, supervised by two other girls. The crypt becomes a scene of horror as Raymar returns to life and deploys his horrifying telekinetic powers.
