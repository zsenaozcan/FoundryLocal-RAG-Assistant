Title: Asterix and Cleopatra (1968)
Genres: Family, Animation, Adventure, Comedy
Director: René Goscinny
Main Cast: Roger Carel, Jacques Morel, Lucien Raimbourg, Micheline Dax, Pierre Tornade
Keywords: cairo, magic, palace, cleopatra, galier, julius caesar

Overview: Popular animated hero Asterix and his faithful sidekick Obelix travel to ancient Egypt to help Cleopatra build a new summer home. Cleopatra and Julius Caesar have made a bet, with Caesar wagering the project cannot be completed in a few weeks time. With the help of a magic potion, Asterix comes to the rescue of the Queen of the Nile as Caesar and an angry architect plot against them.
