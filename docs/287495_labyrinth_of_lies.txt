Title: Labyrinth of Lies (2014)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Giulio Ricciarelli
Main Cast: Alexander Fehling, André Szymanski, Friederike Becht, Johann von Bülow, Hansi Jochmann
Keywords: world war ii

Overview: A young prosecutor in postwar West Germany investigates a massive conspiracy to cover up the Nazi pasts of prominent public figures.
