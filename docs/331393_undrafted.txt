Title: Undrafted (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Joseph Mazzello
Main Cast: Tyler Hoechlin, Chace Crawford, Aaron Tveit, Manny Montana, Toby Hemingway
Keywords: baseball, sport

Overview: Joe Mazzello, best known for his acting roles in Jurassic Park, HBO's The Pacific, The Social Network, etc, wrote and made his directorial debut with this story based on his brother's experience as a collegiate baseball star who was skipped over in the Major League Baseball draft. Story centers around an intramural baseball game with his misfit teammates that becomes incredibly important to him as he tries to come to grips with his dashed dream.
