Title: Here Comes Peter Cottontail (1971)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Jules Bass
Main Cast: Danny Kaye, Vincent Price, Casey Kasem, Paul Frees, Joan Gardner
Keywords: holiday

Overview: Peter Cottontail wants to be the #1 chief Easter Bunny, and everyone in April Valley agrees...except for Evil Irontail. Peter must deliver more eggs than this archrival to earn the top spot...and save Easter for children everywhere!
