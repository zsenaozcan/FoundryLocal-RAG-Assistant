Title: Asura (2012)
Genres: Animation, Drama, History, Horror
Director: Kei'ichi Sato
Main Cast: Masako Nozawa, Hiroaki Hirata, Kinya Kitaoji, Megumi Hayashibara
Keywords: war, growing up, beast, anime

Overview: Abandoned in the barren wasteland of Kyoto, a savage, enraged orphan does whatever it takes to survive in the wild. When he crosses paths with civilization, he must learn to tame the beast within.
