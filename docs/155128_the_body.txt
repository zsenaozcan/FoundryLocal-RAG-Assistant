Title: The Body (1974)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Luigi Scattini
Main Cast: Carroll Baker, Zeudi Araya Cristaldi, Leonard Mann, Enrico Maria Salerno, Luigi Antonio Guerra
Keywords: love triangle, female nudity, island, murder, abusive husband

Overview: A beautiful woman who lives on an island with her abusive husband, entices a young man into a murder-for-money plot.
