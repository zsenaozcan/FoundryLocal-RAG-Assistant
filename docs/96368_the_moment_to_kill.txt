Title: The Moment to Kill (1968)
Genres: Western
Director: Giuliano Carnimeo
Main Cast: George Hilton, Walter Barnes, Horst Frank, Loni von Friedl, Carlo Alighiero
Keywords: spaghetti western, django

Overview: Two famous gunmen, Lord and Bull are called to a southern western town by a judge to retrace a gold reserve, worth $500.000 which was hidden in the last days of the Civil War, by a Confederate colonel and people have been looking for it ever since.
