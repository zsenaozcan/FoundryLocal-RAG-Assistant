Title: Back in the Day (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Paul Borghese
Main Cast: Alec Baldwin, Michael Madsen, Shannen Doherty, Danny Glover, Annabella Sciorra
Keywords: mafia boss, boxing

Overview: A young boxer is taken under the wing of a mob boss after his mother dies and his father is run out of town for being an abusive alcoholic.
