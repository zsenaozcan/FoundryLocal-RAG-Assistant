Title: How the West Was Won (1962)
Genres: History, Western, Adventure
Director: John Ford
Main Cast: Carroll Baker, Lee J. Cobb, Henry Fonda, Carolyn Jones, Karl Malden
Keywords: river, settler, caravan, epic, frontier, outlaw, native american, colonisation, family, riverboat

Overview: The epic tale of the development of the American West from the 1830s through the Civil War to the end of the century, as seen through the eyes of one pioneer family.
