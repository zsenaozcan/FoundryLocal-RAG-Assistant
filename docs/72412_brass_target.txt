Title: Brass Target (1978)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: John Hough
Main Cast: Sophia Loren, John Cassavetes, Max von Sydow, George Kennedy, Robert Vaughn
Keywords: gold

Overview: General George S. Patton died in a car accident in 1945. However, now speculates that his death was actually a murder carefully prepared by his staff to cover a large theft of gold.
