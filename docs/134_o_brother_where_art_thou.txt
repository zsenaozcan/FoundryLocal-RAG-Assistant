Title: O Brother, Where Art Thou? (2000)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Joel Coen
Main Cast: George Clooney, John Turturro, Tim Blake Nelson, John Goodman, Holly Hunter
Keywords: prophecy, southern usa, music record, country music, mississippi, seduction, musical, fraud
