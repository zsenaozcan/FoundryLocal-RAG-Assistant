Title: The Guyver: Bio-Booster Armor (1989)
Genres: Science Fiction, Animation, Foreign, Action, Horror
Director: Koichi Ishiguro
Main Cast: Takeshi Kusao, Yuko Mizutani, Hideyuki Tanaka, Hirotaka Suzuoki, Kôzô Shioya

Overview: Sho and his friend Tetsurou stumble upon an odd alien artifact while walking through the woods. Then, the alien artifact breaks free of its metallic bonds and enters Sho's body, turning him into the Guyver. With this new power, Sho must do battle with the evil Chronos corporation and their genetically enhanced Zoanoids, who seek to get the Guyver back into their labs. No one close to Sho is safe from Chronos. He must fight.
