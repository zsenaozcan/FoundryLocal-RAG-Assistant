Title: Night of the Sharks (1989)
Genres: Horror, Action, Drama
Director: Tonino Ricci
Main Cast: Treat Williams, Janet Ågren, Antonio Fargas, John Steiner, Stelio Candelli
Keywords: killer shark

Overview: David must fight for his life against the gangsters who killed his brother for a CD filled with proof of their illegal activities. When David gets possession of the CD they go down to Mexico where David lives as a shark hunter. Who will get David first, the gangsters or the shark?
