Title: The Visual Bible: The Gospel of John (2003)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Philip Saville
Main Cast: Christopher Plummer, Henry Ian Cusick, Stuart Bunce, Daniel Kash, Stephen Russell
Keywords: miracle, jesus christ, last supper, bible, christian, god, sermon on the mount
