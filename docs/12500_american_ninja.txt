Title: American Ninja (1985)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Sam Firstenberg
Main Cast: Michael Dudikoff, Steve James, Judie Aronson, Guich Koock, John Fujioka
Keywords: rebel, sword, wilderness, daughter, ninja, soldier, gaijin

Overview: Joe Armstrong, an orphaned drifter will little respect for much other than martial arts, finds himself on an American Army base in The Philippines after a judge gives him a choice of enlistment or prison. On one of his first missions driving a convoy, his platoon is attacked by a group of rebels who try to steal the weapons the platoon is transporting and kidnap the base colonel's daughter.
