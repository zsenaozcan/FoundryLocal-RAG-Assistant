Title: Scandalous John (1971)
Genres: Western, Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Robert Butler
Main Cast: Brian Keith, Alfonso Aráu, Michele Carey, Rick Lenz, Harry Morgan
Keywords: ranch, rancher

Overview: A crotchety old ranch owner fights to be able to live his life the way he wants to, and not the way other people--and the law--tell him he has to.
