Title: Deep Core (2000)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Rodney McDonald
Main Cast: Craig Sheffer, Terry Farrell, Bruce McGill, Harry Van Gorkum, Wil Wheaton

Overview: A powerful laser machine rips the core of the earth causing earthquakes and tsunamis, and only one man and his team can save the planet from destruction. Will they succeed? Action, suspense and disaster for fans of the genre.
