Title: El tren de la memoria (2005)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Marta Arribas
Main Cast: Josefina Cembrero, Leonor Mediavilla, Victoria Toro
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Josefina's personal journey back to Nuremberg, Germany, where she arrived as an immigrant from Spain being only 18, along with other 2 million Spaniards who left to Europe in order to find a future.
