Title: The Victim (2006)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Monthon Arayangkoon
Main Cast: Pitchanart Sakakorn, Apasiri Nitibhon, Chokchai Charoensuk, Kiradej Ketakinta, Penpak Sirikul

Overview: Ting, an actress for murder illustration is hired to simulate a real-life murder case of Min, a former Miss Thailand who was brutally killed.
