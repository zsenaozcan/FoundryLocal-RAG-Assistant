Title: Starter for 10 (2006)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Tom Vaughan
Main Cast: James McAvoy, Alice Eve, Dominic Cooper, Simon Woods, Rebecca Hall
Keywords: sex, fight, television, nudity, protest, costume, politics, scholarship, quiz show, party

Overview: In 1985, against the backdrop of Thatcherism, Brian Jackson enrolls in the University of Bristol, a scholarship boy from seaside Essex with a love of knowledge for its own sake and a childhood spent watching "University Challenge," a college quiz show. At Bristol he tries out for the Challenge team and falls under the spell of Alice, a lovely blond with an extensive sexual past.
