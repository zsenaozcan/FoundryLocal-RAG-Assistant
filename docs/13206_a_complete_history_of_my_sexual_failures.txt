Title: A Complete History of My Sexual Failures (2008)
Genres: Documentary, Romance, Foreign
Director: Chris Waitt
Main Cast: Chris Waitt, Alexandra Boyarskaya, Danielle McLeod
Keywords: warp x

Overview: The egocentric documentary-maker Chris Waitt traces his romantic ineptitude and sexual impotence through awkward interviews with irate ex-girlfriends and stunts involving S&amp;M parlours, Harley Street doctors and Viagra overdoses. The results are often hilarious, sometimes moving and speak directly to the hapless paramour in all of us.
