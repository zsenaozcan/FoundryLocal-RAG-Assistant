Title: Miss Rose White (1992)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Joseph Sargent
Main Cast: Kyra Sedgwick, Maureen Stapleton, Maximilian Schell, Amanda Plummer, Penny Fuller

Overview: Rose White (Sedgwick) is a modern young career woman in post-World War II New York City who has largely relegated her Jewish heritage to scrapbooks and memories. Born in Poland but fortunate enough to escape the country before the Nazi occupation and the Holocaust wiped out her family, she is stunned to learn her older sister somehow survived the horror and is coming to America. The sisters' reunion is complicated by Lusia's (Amanda Plummer) memories of her struggles to survive and the revelation of past family secrets.
