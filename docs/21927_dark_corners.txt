Title: Dark Corners (2006)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Ray Gower
Main Cast: Thora Birch, Toby Stephens, Christien Anholt, Joanna Hole, Jonny Coyne
Keywords: fear, madness, emergency room

Overview: A troubled young woman (Birch) wakes up one day as a different person - someone who is stalked by creatures.
