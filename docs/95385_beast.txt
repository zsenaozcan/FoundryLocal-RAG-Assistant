Title: Beast (2011)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Christoffer Boe
Main Cast: Nicolas Bro, Marijana Jankovic, Nikolaj Lie Kaas, Oliver Gustav, Sofia Oskarsson

Overview: Bruno loves his wife Maxine, but his love and body are slowly undergoing a transformation. Something is beginning to stir inside him and there is nothing he can do to stop it. Because when you first delve into the dark side of love to find power, aggression and hatred, there is no turning back.
