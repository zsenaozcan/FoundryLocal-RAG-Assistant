Title: Smokey and the Bandit (1977)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Hal Needham
Main Cast: Burt Reynolds, Sally Field, Jerry Reed, Jackie Gleason, Mike Henry
Keywords: sheriff, bride, smuggling, road movie
