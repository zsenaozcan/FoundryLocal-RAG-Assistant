Title: Gangsters and Philantropists (1963)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Jerzy Hoffman
Main Cast: Gustaw Holoubek, Wiesław Michnikowski, Hanna Bielicka, Marian Kociniak, Roman Kłosowski

Overview: The movie consists of two satirical novel based on the same idea: both the "gangsters" and "philanthropists" end up in the courtroom.
