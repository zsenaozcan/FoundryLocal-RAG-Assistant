Title: A Change of Heart (1998)
Genres: Drama
Director: Arvin Brown
Main Cast: Jean Smart, John Terry, Gretchen Corbett, Phillip Geoffrey Hough, Shawna Waldron
Keywords: gay, chick flick

Overview: Beneath Jim Marshall's veneer as the all-American father and husband, lies a long suppressed desire that is about to ruin his idyllic family. When Elaine Marshall (Jean Smart) catches her husband and his lover in a hotel room, her anger quickly turns to shock as she discovers that Jim's paramour is a young man. What ensues is painful soul-searching for Jim, Elaine, and their family.
