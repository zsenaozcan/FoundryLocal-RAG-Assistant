Title: Hal (2013)
Genres: Animation, Romance
Director: Ryoutarou Makihara
Main Cast: Yoshimasa Hosoya, Youko Hikasa, Mamoru Miyano, Shinpachi Tsuji, Tamio Ohki
Keywords: android, future, science fiction, robot, loss of loved one, heartbreak, aftercreditsstinger, duringcreditsstinger, repressed memory, flashback

Overview: Kurumi’s heart was broken by the sudden death of her boyfriend in a tragic airplane accident. By melding futuristic technology with the binary equivalent of human emotion, a brilliant scientist created an ultra-lifelike robotic surrogate to take Hal’s place – and lure Kurumi from her shroud of solitude.
