Title: Rumble Fish (1983)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Francis Ford Coppola
Main Cast: Matt Dillon, Mickey Rourke, Diane Lane, Dennis Hopper, Diana Scarwid
Keywords: street gang, billard, colour-blindness, gang
