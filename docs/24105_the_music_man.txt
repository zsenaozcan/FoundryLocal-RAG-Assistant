Title: The Music Man (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance, Music, Family
Director: Jeff Bleckner
Main Cast: Matthew Broderick, Kristin Chenoweth, Victor Garber, Debra Monk, Molly Shannon

Overview: Contemporary rethinking of the legendary Broadway musical and 1962 film, updated to reflect a few early twenty-first-century sensibilities: A masterful con artist tries to bilk a staid Midwestern community, with unexpected results.
