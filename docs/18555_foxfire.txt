Title: Foxfire (1996)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Annette Haywood-Carter
Main Cast: Angelina Jolie, Hedy Burress, Jenny Lewis, Jenny Shimizu, Sarah Rosenberg
Keywords: tattoo, prison, based on novel, hostage, fight, nudity, harassment, friendship, high school, independent film

Overview: The story of five teenage girls who form an unlikely bond after beating up a teacher who has sexually harassed them. They build a solid friendship but their wild ways begin to get out of control.
