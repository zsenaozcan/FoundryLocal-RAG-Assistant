Title: The Evening Star (1996)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Family, Comedy
Director: Robert Harling
Main Cast: Shirley MacLaine, Jack Nicholson, Bill Paxton, Juliette Lewis, Miranda Richardson
Keywords: terminal illness, sequel, family relationships, death

Overview: Continuing the story of Aurora Greenway in her latter years. After the death of her daughter, Aurora struggled to keep her family together, but has one grandson in jail, a rebellious granddaughter, and another grandson living just above the poverty line.
