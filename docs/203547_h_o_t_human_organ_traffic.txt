Title: H.O.T. Human Organ Traffic (2009)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Roberto Orazi
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Roberto Orazi turns the camera on the lives of some of those involved in the scourge of the global traffic in human organs: lives united by a momentary hope and separated by that abstract yet insurmountable border called the North-South divide. This shocking documentary exposes the players who claim they know nothing of the crimes they commit.
