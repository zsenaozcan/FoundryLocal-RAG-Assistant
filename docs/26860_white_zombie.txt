Title: White Zombie (1932)
Genres: Horror
Director: Victor Halperin
Main Cast: Bela Lugosi, John Harron, Madge Bellamy, Joseph Cawthorn, Robert Frazer
Keywords: plantation, voodoo, zombie, cult film, newlywed, pre-code, poverty row

Overview: In Haiti, a wealthy landowner convinces a sorcerer to lure the American woman he has fallen for away from her fiance, only to have the madman decide to keep the woman for himself, as a zombie.
