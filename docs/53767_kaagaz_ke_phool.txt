Title: Kaagaz Ke Phool (1959)
Genres: Drama
Director: Guru Dutt
Main Cast: Waheeda Rehman, Guru Dutt, Kumari Naaz, Johnny Walker, Mahesh Kaul
Keywords: rain shower, flowers

Overview: The film tells, in flashback, the story of Suresh Sinha (Guru Dutt), a famous film director and his relationship with an aspiring actress.
