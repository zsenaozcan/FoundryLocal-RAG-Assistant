Title: Mystery Woman (2003)
Genres: Mystery, Crime, Thriller
Director: Walter Klenhard
Main Cast: Kellie Martin, Constance Zimmer, Amy Locane, J.E. Freeman, William R. Moses

Overview: A famous writer is found hanged in his office. At first, all police investigations conclude that it was suicide, but when Samantha Kinsey, owner of Seller 'Mystery Woman' starts his own investigation, he discovers that there is chance that has been committed murder. The film is the first in a long series of American TV movies called" Mystery Woman", in which Samantha Kinsey, the owner of a bookshop specializing in thrillers and suspense is involved in the resolution of real cases.
