Title: Nothing to Lose (1994)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Izidore K. Musallam
Main Cast: Alexandra Paul, Youssef Abed-Alnour, Paul Gleason, Michael V. Gazzo, Juliano Mer-Khamis

Overview: A young street fighter seeks revenge against a powerful drug lord who killed his entire family. Using of his many special combat skills, he's about to take down a whole criminal operation, reaching his goal whatever the consequences.
