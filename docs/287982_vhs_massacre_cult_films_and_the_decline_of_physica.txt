Title: VHS Massacre: Cult Films and the Decline of Physical Media (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Thomas Edward Seymour
Main Cast: Lloyd Kaufman, Ellen Muth, Juliette Danielle, Greg Sestero, Debbie Rochon
Keywords: vhs, cult film, indie film

Overview: This lively documentary explores the rise and fall of physical media from the origin of film all the way through the video store era into digital media, focusing on B-movie and cult films. With icons like Joe Bob Briggs (MonsterVision), Lloyd Kaufman (Toxic Avenger), Greg Sestero (The Room), Debbie Rochon (Return to Nuke 'Em High), Deborah Reed (Troll 2), Mark Frazer (Samurai Cop), James Nguyen (Birdemic) and many others.
