Title: Keane (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lodge Kerrigan
Main Cast: Damian Lewis, Abigail Breslin, Amy Ryan, Tina Holmes, Stephen Henderson
Keywords: schizophrenia, kidnapping, daughter, loss of daughter, search for witnesses, independent film, search, missing girl

Overview: A mentally ill man searches New York for his missing eight year old daughter. He recreates her steps each day hoping for some clue to her disappearance, until he meets and befriend a woman with a daughter the same age. Could she help him with the missing piece of the puzzle?
