Title: Drömkåken (1993)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Peter Dalle
Main Cast: Björn Skifs, Suzanne Reuter, Zara Zetterqvist, Mikael Håck, Lena Nyman

Overview: Göran buys a house in a suburb. It is in need of some repairs but Göran believes himself capable of repairing it himself and with the help of some cheap craftsmen. However, the house is much worse than he thought. At the same time, a gang of crooks is searching for the dynamite the hid in the basement some time ago. Göran has to rescue both his wallet from greedy craftsmen as well as his marriage with Tina,
