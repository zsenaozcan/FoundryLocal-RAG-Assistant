Title: unINDIAN (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Anupam Sharma
Main Cast: Tannishtha Chatterjee, Nicholas Brown, Brett Lee, Supriya Pathak kapur, Arka Das
Keywords: indian lead, australian, romantic comedy, bollywood, immigrant family

Overview: Single mum Meera is an Australian of Indian origin. Smart and independent, she has carved out a successful life for herself and her daughter...despite family pressure to find a 'nice Indian match'. Then Meera meets Will, a blonde, blue-eyed Aussie charmer. But falling in love with an Australian is not only scandalous - it's unIndian! Does she do what her family wishes, or follow her heart?
