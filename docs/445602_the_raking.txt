Title: The Raking (2017)
Genres: Horror
Director: Bryan Brewer
Main Cast: Cree Kelly, Allie Rivera, Thatcher Robinson, Marshal Hilton, Marisa Davila
Keywords: monster, horror, urban legend, creature, college student, camping trip

Overview: THE RAKING tells the story of a group of college students who set out to debunk an urban legend in Joshua Tree, California for their anthropology thesis, only to find that they become part of the legend themselves.
