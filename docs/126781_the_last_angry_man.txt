Title: The Last Angry Man (1959)
Genres: Drama
Director: Daniel Mann
Main Cast: Paul Muni, David Wayne, Betsy Palmer, Luther Adler, Claudia McNeil
Keywords: newspaper, tv show, doctor, brooklyn new york city, good samaritan

Overview: As the fiercely dedicated general practitioner who tries to help the sick, the poor and the unfortunate in his decrepit neighborhood, Paul Muni is the testy old man who faces life without compromise and David Wayne is the troubled television producer fighting to preserve his career.
