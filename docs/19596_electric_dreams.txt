Title: Electric Dreams (1984)
Genres: Music, Comedy, Drama
Director: Steve Barron
Main Cast: Lenny Von Dohlen, Virginia Madsen, Maxwell Caulfield, Bud Cort, Don Fellows
Keywords: architect, cello, computer

Overview: Miles buys himself a state-of-the-art computer that starts expressing thoughts and emotions after a having champagne spilled down on him. Things start getting out of hand when both Miles and Edgar, how the computer calls himself, fall in love with Madeline, an attractive neighbour.
