Title: Fire, Ice & Canned Beer (2002)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mathias Dinter
Main Cast: Rick Kavanian, Axel Stein, Eva Habermann, Christoph M. Ohrt, Jonas Gruber
Keywords: alp, civil service, ischgl, amusement park, ski

Overview: Two friends who are doing civil service flee to the Austrian Alps to escape being forced into the army. While there, one of them rediscovers his past and they realise that it's going to be more fun than they thought.
