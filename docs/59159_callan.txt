Title: Callan (1974)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Don Sharp
Main Cast: Edward Woodward, Eric Porter, Carl Möhner, Catherine Schell, Peter Egan

Overview: David Callan, secret agent, is called back to the service after his retirement, to handle the assasination of a german businessman, but Callan refuses to co-operate until he finds out why this man is marked for death.
