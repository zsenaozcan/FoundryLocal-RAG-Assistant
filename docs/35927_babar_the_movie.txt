Title: Babar The Movie (1989)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Family, Fantasy
Director: Alan Bunce
Main Cast: Gordon Pinsent, Elizabeth Hanna, Lisa Yamanaka, Marsha Moreau, Bobby Becken
Keywords: slavery, elephant, childhood memory, musical, rhinoceros, war, escape from prison

Overview: Children's book authors Jean and Laurent de Brunhoff's most beloved elephant comes to the big screen in this animated family tale. Elephant monarch King Babar tells the tale, that unfolds via flashback, of how a much-younger Barbar and his girlfriend Celeste save her village from the pugnacious rhinoceroses that have come to raid it.
