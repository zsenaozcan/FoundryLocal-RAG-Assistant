Title: The Divided Heaven (1964)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Konrad Wolf
Main Cast: Renate Blume, Eberhard Esche, Hans Hardt-Hardtloff, Hilmar Thate, Martin Flörchinger
Keywords: eastern germany, gdr

Overview: 1964 DEFA, East German Film by Konrad Wolf based on the novel by Christa Wolf.  Tale of star-crossed lovers divided by worldviews in a divided Berlin.
