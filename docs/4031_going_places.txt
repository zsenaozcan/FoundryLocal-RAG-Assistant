Title: Going Places (1974)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Bertrand Blier
Main Cast: Gérard Depardieu, Patrick Dewaere, Miou-Miou, Jeanne Moreau, Brigitte Fossey
Keywords: suicide, male nudity, female nudity, bisexuality, menage a trois, release from prison, hoodlum, anarchic comedy

Overview: Two whimsical, aimless thugs harass and assault women, steal, murder, and alternately charm, fight, or sprint their way out of trouble. They take whatever the bourgeois characters value: whether it's cars, peace of mind, or daughters. Marie-Ange, a jaded, passive hairdresser, joins them as lover, cook, and mother confessor. She's on her own search for seemingly unattainable sexual pleasure.
