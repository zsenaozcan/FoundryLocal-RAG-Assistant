Title: Le Garçu (1995)
Genres: Drama
Director: Maurice Pialat
Main Cast: Gérard Depardieu, Géraldine Pailhas, Dominique Rocheteau, Fabienne Babe, Elisabeth Depardieu

Overview: A self-centered man (Gérard Depardieu) with many diversions occasionally visits his 4-year-old son (Antoine Pialat) and the boy's mother (Géraldine Pailhas).
