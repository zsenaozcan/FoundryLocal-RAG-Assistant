Title: Youngblood (1986)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Peter Markle
Main Cast: Rob Lowe, Cynthia Gibb, Patrick Swayze, Ed Lauter, Jim Youngs
Keywords: frustration, sport, ice hockey, coach, beating, hazing, locker room

Overview: A skilled young hockey prospect hoping to attract the attention of professional scouts is pressured to show that he can fight if challenged during his stay in a Canadian minor hockey town. His on-ice activities are complicated by his relationship with the coach's daughter.
