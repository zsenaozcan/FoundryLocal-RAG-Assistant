Title: After Death (2012)
Genres: Mystery, Comedy, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Martin Gooch
Main Cast: Claira Watson Parr, Ben Shockley, David Wayman, Nicola Goodchild, Linal Haft

Overview: Following the sudden death of their last surviving parent - their Dad, two brothers and two sisters return to the family home after many years to face each other, face facts and face the future. But what do they know of the past?
