Title: Thirst (2009)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Park Chan-wook
Main Cast: Song Kang-ho, Kim Ok-bin, Kim Hae-sook, Shin Ha-kyun, Song Young-chang
Keywords: self mutilation, childhood friends, blood transfusion, confessional, catholic guilt, lesion, jumping off a building

Overview: Sang-hyun (Song Kang-ho), a respected priest, volunteers for an experimental procedure that may lead to a cure for a deadly virus. He gets infected and dies, but a blood transfusion of unknown origin brings him back to life as a vampire. Now, Sang-hyun is torn between faith and bloodlust, and has a newfound desire for Tae-ju (Kim Ok-vin), the wife of his childhood friend.
