Title: How to Keep the Red Lamp Burning (1965)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Gilles Grangier
Main Cast: Louis de Funès, Mireille Darc, Bernard Blier, Jean Lefebvre, Philippe Castelli

Overview: The film consists of three novels. The film begins with the fact that the Bernard Blier hero removes a lantern from the entrance to a brothel. The second part is about how the lantern and jewelery were stolen from a young baroness. And in the third part the hero of Louis de Funes hangs a lantern at the entrance to his house.
