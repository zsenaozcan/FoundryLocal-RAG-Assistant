Title: Love Ghost (2001)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Kazuyuki Shibuya
Main Cast: 松田龙平, Kumiko Akiyoshi, Risa Goto, Shuntaro Hani, Hirotaro Honda

Overview: Teenager Midori's family moves back to the city where she lived as a child and is relieved to discover that her old friend Ryusuke still lives there. Her terrifying nightmares begin intersecting with her everyday life. Do her nightly dreams about an anonymous fortune-teller have anything to do with the tragedies plaguing her high school?
