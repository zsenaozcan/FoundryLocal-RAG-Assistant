Title: Idiot Box (1996)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Foreign, Thriller
Director: David Caesar
Main Cast: Ben Mendelsohn, Jeremy Sims, John Polson, Graeme Blundell, Deborah Kennedy
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Mick and Kev – bored, unemployed and aimless in the western suburbs of Sydney – decide to rob a bank, more or less for the fun of it.
