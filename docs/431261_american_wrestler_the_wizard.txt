Title: American Wrestler: The Wizard (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alex Ranarivelo
Main Cast: George Kosturos, Jon Voight, William Fichtner, Lia Marie Johnson, Kevin G. Schmidt
Keywords: hostility, sport, iran, teenage boy, 1980s, inspired by true events, wrestling team

Overview: In 1980, a teenage boy escapes the unrest in Iran only to face more hostility in America, due to the hostage crisis. Determined to fit in, he joins the school's floundering wrestling team.
