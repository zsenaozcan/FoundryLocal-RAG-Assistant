Title: Thumb Wars: The Phantom Cuticle (1999)
Genres: Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Steve Oedekerk
Main Cast: Steve Oedekerk, Andrea Fears, Ross Shafer, Rob Paulsen, Paul Greenberg
Keywords: thumb, hologram, space war, warp speed, good vs evil

Overview: The hilarious story of a restless young farm-thumb, Loke Groundrunner, and his tasty companion, Princess Bunhead, who go on an quest to combat Black Helmet Man and the Evil Thumbpire.
