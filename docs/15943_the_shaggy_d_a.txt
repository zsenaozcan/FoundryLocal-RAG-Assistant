Title: The Shaggy D.A. (1976)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Robert Stevenson
Main Cast: Dean Jones, Suzanne Pleshette, Tim Conway, Keenan Wynn, Jo Anne Worley
Keywords: lawyer, dog, district attorney

Overview: Wilby Daniels, a successful lawyer running for District Attorney, suddenly finds himself being transformed into an English sheepdog. Somehow he has to keep his change a secret and find just what is causing it, all the while eluding the local dog catcher.
