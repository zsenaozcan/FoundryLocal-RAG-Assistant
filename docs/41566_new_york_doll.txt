Title: New York Doll (2005)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Greg Whiteley
Main Cast: Arthur Kane, Nina Antonia, Clem Burke, David Johansen, Leee Black Childers
Keywords: new york, punk, the bowery, jetboy

Overview: A recovering alcoholic and recently converted Mormon, Arthur "Killer" Kane, of the rock band The New York Dolls, is given a chance at reuniting with his band after 30 years.
