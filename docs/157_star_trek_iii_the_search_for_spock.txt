Title: Star Trek III: The Search for Spock (1984)
Genres: Science Fiction, Action, Adventure, Thriller
Director: Leonard Nimoy
Main Cast: William Shatner, Leonard Nimoy, DeForest Kelley, James Doohan, George Takei

Overview: Admiral Kirk and his bridge crew risk their careers stealing the decommissioned Enterprise to return to the restricted Genesis planet to recover Spock's body.
