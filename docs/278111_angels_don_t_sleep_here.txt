Title: Angels Don't Sleep Here (2002)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Paul Cade
Main Cast: Dana Ashbrook, Kelly Rutherford, Roy Scheider, Robert Patrick, Kari Wuhrer
Keywords: mayor, revenge, murder, twins, father daughter conflict

Overview: A man is accused of murder and manipulated into a web of deceit as his thought-to-be dead twin brother plots revenge on those who tried to kill him.
