Title: Early Spring (1956)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Main Cast: Chikage Awashima, Takako Fujino, Ryô Ikebe, Daisuke Katô, Keiko Kishi
Keywords: japanese culture

Overview: A young salary man and his wife struggle within the confines of their passionless relationship while he has an extramarital affair.
