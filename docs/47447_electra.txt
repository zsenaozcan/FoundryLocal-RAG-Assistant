Title: Electra (1962)
Genres: Action, History
Director: Mihalis Kakogiannis
Main Cast: Irene Papas, Notis Peryalis, Takis Emmanuel, Manos Katrakis, Giannis Fertis

Overview: Living in exile after the death of their father, the grown children of a murdered and usurped king converge to exact eye-for-an-eye revenge.
