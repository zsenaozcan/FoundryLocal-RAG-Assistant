Title: VeggieTales: Where's God When I'm Scared (1993)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Phil Vischer
Main Cast: Daniel Anderson, G Bock, Mike Nawrocki, Mike Sage, Lisa Vischer
Keywords: inspiration, peace, fear

Overview: Whether it's a giant Frankencelery from a scary movie or a hungry den of lions from Ancient Babylon, Junior Asparagus learns we can trust God with all of our fears and that He really is "bigger than the boogey-man!"
