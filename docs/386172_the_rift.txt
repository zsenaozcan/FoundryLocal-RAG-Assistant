Title: The Rift (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Dejan Zečević
Main Cast: Katarina Čas, Ken Foree, Monte Markham, Dragan Mićanović, Denis Murić
Keywords: boy, space, astronaut, death, rift

Overview: The satellite has vanished and the only clue is a trail leading to a small village nestled on the outskirts of forest line. As the team begins to investigate they quickly discover that something else came back from space, something not of this world. As their time runs out, the team must battle to unlock the terrifying truth behind the ill-fated mission.
