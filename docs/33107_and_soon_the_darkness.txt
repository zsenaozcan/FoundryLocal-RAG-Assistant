Title: And Soon the Darkness (2010)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Marcos Efron
Main Cast: Odette Annable, Amber Heard, Karl Urban, Adriana Barraza, César Vianco
Keywords: kidnapping, remake, murder, argentina, torture
