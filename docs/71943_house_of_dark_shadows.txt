Title: House of Dark Shadows (1970)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller, Romance
Director: Dan Curtis
Main Cast: Jonathan Frid, Grayson Hall, Joan Bennett, John Karlen, Louis Edmonds
Keywords: vampire, love, blood, lost love, cure

Overview: The story of vampire Barnabas Collins, the possible cure offered by Dr. Julia Hoffman, and his search for love amidst the horror.
