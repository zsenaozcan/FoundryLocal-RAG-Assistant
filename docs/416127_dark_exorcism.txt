Title: Dark Exorcism (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: David Spaltro
Main Cast: Grace Folsom, Lynn Justinger, Fiona Horrigan, Catherine Cobb Ryan, Kayla Leasure
Keywords: exorcism

Overview: A skeptical grad student and a renowned paranormal specialist investigate a potentially haunted home and the troubled woman inside whose affliction may be beyond the capacity of either of them.
