Title: Soldier of Fortune (1955)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action, Romance
Director: Edward Dmytryk
Main Cast: Clark Gable, Susan Hayward, Michael Rennie, Gene Barry, Tom Tully

Overview: An American woman arrives in Hong Kong to unravel the mystery of her missing photographer husband. After getting nowhere with the authorities, she is led by some underground characters to an American soldier of fortune working in the area against the Communists. He promises to help find her husband.
