Title: Fida (2004)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Romance
Director: Ken Ghosh
Main Cast: Kareena Kapoor, Shahid Kapoor, Fardeen Khan, Kim Sharma, Akhilendra Mishra

Overview: Fida tells the story of Jai who one day happen to meet a young and beautiful woman called Neha and he falls in love with her at first sight.
