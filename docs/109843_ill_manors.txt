Title: Ill Manors (2012)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Ben Drew
Main Cast: Riz Ahmed, Ed Skrein, Natalie Press, Anouska Mond, Lee Allen
Keywords: gang, united kingdom

Overview: Ensemble film revolving around characters living in Forest Gate, London. Over the course of a few days, six inter-linking stories explore issues of drug use, prostitution and urban poverty.
