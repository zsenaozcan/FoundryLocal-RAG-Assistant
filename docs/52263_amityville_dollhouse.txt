Title: Amityville: Dollhouse (1996)
Genres: Horror
Director: Steve White
Main Cast: Robin Thomas, Starr Andreeff, Allen Cutler, Rachel Duncan, Jarrett Lennon
Keywords: female nudity, haunted house, sequel, occult, dollhouse, ghost, amityville horror

Overview: A dollhouse that is a replica of the infamous Amityville haunted house is given to a little girl. Soon after, all sorts of horrible unexplained accidents start to happen. The family must work together to fight off the terrifying evil that has inhabited their lives.
