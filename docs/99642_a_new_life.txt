Title: A New Life (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: Philippe Grandrieux
Main Cast: Zachary Knighton, Anna Mouglalis, Marc Barbé, Zsolt Nagy
Keywords: new french extremism

Overview: A 2002 French experimental film directed by Philippe Grandrieux and starring Zachary Knighton and Anna Mouglalis. The story involves a young American who falls obsessively in love with a mysterious courtesan named Melania against the backdrop of a dilapidated Eastern European landscape.
