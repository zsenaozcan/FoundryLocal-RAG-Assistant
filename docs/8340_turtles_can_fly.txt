Title: Turtles Can Fly (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bahman Ghobadi
Main Cast: Soran Ebrahim, Avaz Latif, Saddam Hossein Feysal, Hiresh Feysal Rahman, Abdol Rahman Karim
Keywords: muslim, refugee, landmine, bicycle, boy, village, iraq, war, dead child, tank

Overview: Turtles can fly tells the story of a group of young children near the Turkey-Iran border. They clean up mines and wait for the Saddam regime to fall.
