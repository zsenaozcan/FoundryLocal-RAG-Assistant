Title: Black Heaven (2010)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Gilles Marchand
Main Cast: Grégoire Leprince-Ringuet, Melvil Poupaud, Pauline Étienne, Louise Bourgoin, Pierre Niney

Overview: An innocent young man becomes enamored with a mysterious girl. He is lured into "Black Hole" - a dark, obscure video game world of avatars with deadly serious intentions in the real world.
