Title: Snake-Crane Secret (1978)
Genres: Action
Director: Wu Ma
Main Cast: Dorian Tan Tao-Liang, Meng Fei, Dean Shek, Wang Hsieh
Keywords: martial arts

Overview: Snake and Crane Secret is a fast paced martial arts action film about a familys battle for survival. A family tries to uphold their honor while keeping their Sacred Kung Fu book from getting into the wrong hands. A tale of deceit mistrust and traditional values unfolds along with spectacular fight sequences necessary to keep the Snake Crane Secret.
