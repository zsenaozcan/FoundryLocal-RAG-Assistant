Title: Around the World (1943)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Allan Dwan
Main Cast: Kay Kyser, Mischa Auer, Joan Davis, Marcy McGuire, Wally Brown
Keywords: musical, australia

Overview: Bandleader Kay Kyser takes his troupe of nutty musicians, goofball comics and pretty girl singers on a tour around the world to entertain the troops during World War II.
