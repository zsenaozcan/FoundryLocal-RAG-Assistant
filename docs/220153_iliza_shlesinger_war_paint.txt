Title: Iliza Shlesinger: War Paint (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jay Chapman
Main Cast: Iliza Shlesinger
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Her innocent appearance is just a cover for Last Comic Standing winner Iliza Shlesinger's acerbic, stream-of-conscious comedy that she unleashes on an unsuspecting audience in her hometown of Dallas in "War Paint," her first stand-up special.
