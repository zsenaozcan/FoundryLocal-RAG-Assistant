Title: Adrenaline Drive (1999)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Romance, Foreign
Director: Shinobu Yaguchi
Main Cast: Masanobu Ando, Reila Aphrodite, Hikari Ishida, Yukata Matsushige, Yu Tokui
Keywords: japan, yakuza, orderly, stolen money, dark comedy

Overview: A gas leak explosion at a yakuza hideout provides a shy nurse and a rental car clerk with the opportunity to take a briefcase full of money. A cross-country chase ensues.
