Title: No Retreat, No Surrender 2: Raging Thunder (1988)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Corey Yuen Kwai
Main Cast: Cynthia Rothrock, Loren Avedon, Max Thayer, Matthias Hues, Nirut Sirichanya

Overview: In this sequel, Scott Wylde, an American kickboxer, must go to Cambodia to rescue his Vietnamese girlfriend from Russian and Vietnamese troops.
