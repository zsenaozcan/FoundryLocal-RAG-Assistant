Title: Sh! The Octopus (1937)
Genres: Mystery, Comedy
Director: William C. McGann
Main Cast: Hugh Herbert, Allen Jenkins, Marcia Ralston, John Eldredge, George Rosener
Keywords: octopus, lighthouse

Overview: Comedy-mystery finds Detectives Kelly and Dempsey trapped in a deserted lighthouse with a group of strangers who are being terrorized by a killer octopus AND a mysterious crime figure named after the title sea creature.
