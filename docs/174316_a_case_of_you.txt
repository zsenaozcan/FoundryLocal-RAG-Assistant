Title: A Case of You (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Kat Coiro
Main Cast: Justin Long, Evan Rachel Wood, Keir O'Donnell, Busy Philipps, Sienna Miller
Keywords: woman director, internet dating

Overview: Young writer Sam has a crush on Birdie, the cute and quirky barista at his local coffee shop. When his conventional attempts to woo her crash and burn, he takes his efforts online, creating an Internet profile embellished with all of the details that would make him Birdie’s dream guy. When the harebrained scheme is a surprise success and Birdie falls for his exaggerated alter ego, Sam must keep up the act or lose his dream girl forever.
