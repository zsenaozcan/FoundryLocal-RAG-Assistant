Title: Retro Puppet Master (1999)
Genres: Horror
Director: David DeCoteau
Main Cast: Greg Sestero, Brigitte Dau, Stephen Blackehart, Jack Donner, Guy Rolfe
Keywords: paris, puppet

Overview: Toulon runs a puppet theatre in the heart of Paris and meets the sorcerer (the mysterious Afzel).  When his life is saved by the lovely Swiss Ambassador's daughter Ilsa, we bear witness to the origin of the Puppet Master.
