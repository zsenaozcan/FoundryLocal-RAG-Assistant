Title: Binta and the Great Idea (2004)
Genres: Family, Drama
Director: Javier Fesser
Main Cast: Zeynabou Diallo, Agnile Sambou, Aminata Sane
Keywords: education, village, senegal

Overview: Binta, a little girl from Senegal, tells us about the everyday life in her village, the importance of education for the girls, and about her father's great idea to make the world a better place.
