Title: I Will Walk Like a Crazy Horse (1973)
Genres: Drama
Director: Fernando Arrabal
Main Cast: Emmanuelle Riva, George Shannon, Hachemi Marzouk, Marco Perrin, François Chatelet
Keywords: epilepsy, police, surrealism, desert, incest, cannibalism, breastfeeding, transvestite

Overview: Running away from the police, Aden goes to the desert where he meets an uncivilized man who has a special link with Mother-Earth. He ends up by convincing the hermit to come along with him into another desert... the big town!
