Title: Ferocious (2013)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Robert Cuffley
Main Cast: Amanda Crew, Kim Coates, Dustin Milligan, Michael Eklund, Amy Matysio

Overview: Amanda Crew stars as Leigh Parrish, a successful actress on a trip home to visit her small town roots.  While dodging the careful eye of her manager (Dustin Milligan), Leigh, steps out from the lights and cameras and into the shadows of her old life, confronting her unsavory past and ex-employer, Maurice (Kim Coates).
