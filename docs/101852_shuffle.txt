Title: Shuffle (2012)
Genres: Thriller, Mystery, Romance
Director: Kurt Kuenne
Main Cast: T. J. Thyne, Paula Rhodes, Chris Stone, Meeghan Holaway, JoJo Henrickson
Keywords: time travel

Overview: Lovell Milo is a man who begins experiencing his life out of order; every day he wakes up at a different age, on a different day of his life, never knowing where or when he’s going to be once he falls asleep. He’s terrified and wants it to stop - until he notices a pattern in his experience, and works to uncover why this is happening to him - and what or who is behind it.
