Title: Herbie Goes To Monte Carlo (1977)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Family, Action, Adventure
Director: Vincent McEveety
Main Cast: Dean Jones, Don Knotts, Julie Sommars, Jacques Marin, Roy Kinnear
Keywords: auto mechanic, automobile racing, smart car, monte carlo rally, the love bug

Overview: Herbie, the Volkswagen Beetle with a mind of its own, is racing in the Monte Carlo Rally. But thieves have hidden a cache of stolen diamonds in Herbie's gas tank, and are now trying to get them back.
