Title: The Legend of the 7 Golden Vampires (1974)
Genres: Action, Horror
Director: Roy Ward Baker
Main Cast: Peter Cushing, David Chiang, Julie Ege, Robin Stewart, Shih Szu
Keywords: martial arts, vampire, dracula, vampire hunter, hammer horror

Overview: Professor Van Helsing had been asked to help against the tyranny of skeletal creatures that are responsible for terror and death amongst the peasants in rural China. He is the only person qualified to deal with the cause of these phenomena, for the undead are controlled by the most diabolical force of all.... Count Dracula. But he is not alone- to aid him comes a mystical brotherhood of seven martial arts warriors.
