Title: The Sacrament (2013)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Ti West
Main Cast: Joe Swanberg, AJ Bowen, Kentucker Audley, Gene Jones, Amy Seimetz
Keywords: poison, journalist, lethal injection, interview, cult, based on true story, suspense, thriller, brainwashing, massacre

Overview: A fashion photographer is traveling to meet his sister at Eden Parish. Once there, his friends begin to film interviews with the Eden Parish inhabitants, all of whom speak of the commune in glowing terms. However, they soon discover that there is a sinister edge to the commune that belies the seemingly peaceful setting.
