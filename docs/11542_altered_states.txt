Title: Altered States (1980)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Fantasy, Science Fiction, Thriller, Horror
Director: Ken Russell
Main Cast: William Hurt, Blair Brown, Bob Balaban, Charles Haid, Thaao Penghlis
Keywords: male nudity, female nudity, based on novel, subconsciousness, nudity, ritual, genetics, suspense, university, drug

Overview: A research scientist (William Hurt) explores the boundaries and frontiers of consciousness. Using sensory deprivation and hallucinogenic mixtures from native American shamans, he explores these altered states of consciousness and finds that memory, time, and perhaps reality itself are states of mind.
