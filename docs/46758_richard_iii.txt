Title: Richard III (1912)
Genres: Drama
Director: André Calmettes
Main Cast: Robert Gemp, Frederick Warde, Albert Gardner, James Keane, George Moss

Overview: Shakespeare's tragedy of the hump-backed Duke of Gloucester, who rises to the throne of England by chicanery, treachery, and brilliance, only to find that his own methods have prepared the groundwork for his downfall.
