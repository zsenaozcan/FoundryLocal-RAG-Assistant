Title: Alien Nation: Millennium (1996)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Kenneth Johnson
Main Cast: Gary Graham, Eric Pierpoint, Michele Scarabelli, Terri Treas, Lauren Woodland
Keywords: undercover, alien, enlightenment

Overview: It's 1999, and as the end of the millenium approaches, people are attempting to find spiritual enlightenment. But a few people want to skip all the work that entails, and a holy Tenktonese relic in the hands of a heretic is giving them a shortcut. But it's not quite as easily controlled as she says.
