Title: Yellow Submarine (1968)
Genres: Animation, Fantasy, Music
Director: George Dunning
Main Cast: Paul Angelis, John Lennon, Paul McCartney, George Harrison, Ringo Starr
Keywords: submarine, fab four, colours, music, blase, the beatles

Overview: The Blue Meanies take over Pepperland, draining it of all its color and music, firing anti-music missiles, bonking people with green apples, and turning the inhabitants to stone by way of the pointed finger of a giant blue glove. As the only survivor, the Lord Admiral escapes in the yellow submarine and goes to Liverpool to enlist the help of the Beatles.
