Title: Gun Woman (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Action
Director: Kurando Mitsutake
Main Cast: Asami, Kairi Narita, Noriaki Kamata, Matthew Floyd Miller, Dean Simone
Keywords: gun, thriller, gun battle, gore, woman, action

Overview: A brilliant doctor on a quest for revenge buys a young woman and trains her to be the ultimate assassin, implanting gun parts in her body that she must later assemble and use to kill her target before she bleeds to death.
