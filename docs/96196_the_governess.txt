Title: The Governess (1998)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Sandra Goldbacher
Main Cast: Minnie Driver, Tom Wilkinson, Harriet Walter, Jonathan Rhys Meyers, Florence Hoath
Keywords: scotland, egg, loss of virginity, kissing, woman director, gentile

Overview: When the father of privileged Rosina da Silva violently dies, she decides to pass herself off as a gentile and finds employment with a family in faraway Scotland. Soon she and the family father, Charles, start a passionate secret affair.
