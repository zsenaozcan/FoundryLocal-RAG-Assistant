Title: Bad Guy (2002)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Romance
Director: Kim Ki-duk
Main Cast: Jo Jae-hyeon, Seo Won, Yun-tae Kim, Choi Duk-moon, Choi Yoon-young
Keywords: eroticism, prostitution

Overview: A criminal who runs a brothel becomes obsessed with a beautiful college student. When she rejects him he sets her up by introducing her to a loan shark and when she can't pay, she is forced into working in the brothel. But when she gets the chance to escape she realizes she cannot return to her former life.
