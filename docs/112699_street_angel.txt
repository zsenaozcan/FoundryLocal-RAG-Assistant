Title: Street Angel (1937)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Music
Director: Muzhi Yuan
Main Cast: Dan Zhao, Heling Wei, Zhou Xuan, Jiting Wang, Zhicheng Feng
Keywords: prostitute, newspaper seller

Overview: In old Shanghai, two sisters, a prostitute and a singer, tried to escape from the local scoundrels with the help of a trumpet player and a newspaper seller.
