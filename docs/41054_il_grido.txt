Title: Il Grido (1957)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michelangelo Antonioni
Main Cast: Steve Cochran, Alida Valli, Betsy Blair, Gabriella Pallotta, Dorian Gray
Keywords: depression, mechanic, oil refinery, valley

Overview: A sugar-refinery worker (Steve Cochran) flees his Northern Italy town after a woman (Alida Valli) refuses his marriage proposal.
