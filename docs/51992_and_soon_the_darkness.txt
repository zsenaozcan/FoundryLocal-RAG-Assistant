Title: And Soon the Darkness (1970)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Robert Fuest
Main Cast: Pamela Franklin, Michele Dotrice, Sandor Elès, John Nettleton, Clare Kelly
Keywords: france, language barrier, bicycle, murder, suspense, independent film, cycling

Overview: Two young English women go on a cycling tour of the French countryside. When one of them goes missing, the other begins to search for her. But who can she trust?
