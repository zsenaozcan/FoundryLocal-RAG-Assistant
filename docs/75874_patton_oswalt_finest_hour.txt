Title: Patton Oswalt: Finest Hour (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jason Woliner
Main Cast: Patton Oswalt
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Patton Oswalt brings his deeply insightful, creative comedy to topics such as trying to be a good example for his daughter, his bewilderment at why we still have circuses, and his run-ins with crack addicts in New York.
