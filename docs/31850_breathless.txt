Title: Breathless (2008)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Yang Ik-june
Main Cast: Yang Ik-june, Kim Kkobbi, Jeong Man-sik, Lee Hwan, Yun Seung-hun
Keywords: gangster, south korea, loan shark

Overview: Sang-Hoon is a thirty something scamp collecting money from debtors for a loan shark. One day, he encounters Youn-Hee, a young hot-tempered high school student. Their strong wills causes the two strangers to clash at first, but an unique friendship then develops as they realize they have far more in common ...
