Title: Gervaise (1956)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: René Clément
Main Cast: Maria Schell, François Périer, Jany Holt, Mathilde Casadesus, Suzy Delair

Overview: An adaptation of Émile Zola’s 1877 masterpiece L’assommoir, the film is an uncompromising depiction of a lowly laundress’s struggles to deal with an alcoholic husband while running her own business.
