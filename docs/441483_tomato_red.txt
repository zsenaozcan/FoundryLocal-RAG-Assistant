Title: Tomato Red (2017)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Juanita Wilson
Main Cast: Julia Garner, Jake Weary, Anna Friel, Nick Roux, Douglas M. Griffin

Overview: When small town drifter Sammy Barlach drives into town on the search for his next cold beer and the bunch that'll have him, he gets a lot more than he bargained for.
