Title: Only When I Laugh (1981)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Glenn A. Jordan
Main Cast: Marsha Mason, Kristy McNichol, James Coco, Joan Hackett, David Dukes
Keywords: mother daughter relationship, new york city, drunk, actress

Overview: A boozy Broadway actress comes out of a 12-week cure to face the problems of her best friends as well as her needy daughter. She tries to balance the terrors of returning to work with the demands of all around her with humor and insight, while staying off the booze.
