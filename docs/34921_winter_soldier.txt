Title: Winter Soldier (1972)
Genres: War, Documentary
Director: Michaël Weill
Main Cast: Rusty Sachs, Kenneth Campbell, Joe Bangert, Scott Shimabukuro, Scott Camil
Keywords: vietnam war, testimony, atrocity, soldier

Overview: For three days in 1971, former US soldiers who were in Vietnam testify in Detroit about their war experiences. Nearly 30 speak, describing atrocities personally committed or witnessed, telling of inaccurate body counts, and recounting the process of destroying a village.
