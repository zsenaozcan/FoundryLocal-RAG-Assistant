Title: Who Killed Captain Alex? (2010)
Genres: Action
Director: Nabwana I.G.G.
Main Cast: Kakule Wilson, Sserunya Ernest, G. Puffs, Kavubu Muhammed, Kasumba Isma
Keywords: africa, drug cartel, police raid

Overview: Recognized as Uganda’s first action film, Who Killed Captain Alex? is about the aftermath of a police raid in Kampala in which a police captain (Alex) and a drug racketeer’s brother are killed. Both sides seek revenge, escalating into war.
