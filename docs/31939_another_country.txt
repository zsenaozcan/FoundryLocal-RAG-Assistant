Title: Another Country (1984)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Marek Kanievska
Main Cast: Rupert Everett, Colin Firth, Michael Jenn, Robert Addie, Rupert Wainwright
Keywords: gay, based on play or musical, independent film, gay relationship, britain, lgbt child

Overview: Set in the 1930s, Guy Bennett and Tommy Judd are friends because they are both outsiders in their own ways. Bennett is openly gay, Judd is a Marxist. It explores his homosexuality and exposure to Marxism, while examining the hypocrisy and snobbery of the English public school system. The film is loosely based on the life of the spy and double agent Guy Burgess.
