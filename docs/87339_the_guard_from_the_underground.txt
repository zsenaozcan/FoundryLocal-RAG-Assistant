Title: The Guard from the Underground (1992)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller, Crime
Director: Kiyoshi Kurosawa
Main Cast: Makiko Kuno, Yutaka Matsushige, Hatsunori Hasegawa, Ren Osugi, Taro Suwa
Keywords: murderer, security guard

Overview: One of the contemporary masters of Japanese film, Kiyoshi Kurosawa here fashions a dark tale that is both a sharp satire of corporate life and a B-movie thriller. A former sumo wrestler now working as a security guard goes on a murderous rampage in the company that's employed him.
