Title: Gideon's Trumpet (1980)
Genres: Drama, Crime, History
Director: Robert L. Collins
Main Cast: Henry Fonda, José Ferrer, John Houseman, Fay Wray, Sam Jaffe
Keywords: prison, oldtimer

Overview: True story of Clarence Gideon's fight to be appointed counsel at the expense of the state. This landmark case led to the Supreme Court's decision which extended this right to all criminal defendants.
