Title: What Have You Done to Solange? (1972)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Massimo Dallamano
Main Cast: Fabio Testi, Karin Baal, Joachim Fuchsberger, Cristina Galbó, Camille Keaton
Keywords: scotland yard, secret, lover, edgar wallace, girls' boarding school

Overview: After several coeds are murdered at a college, a professor (Fabio Testi) who is involved with a student becomes a suspect, along with several other male and female teachers.
