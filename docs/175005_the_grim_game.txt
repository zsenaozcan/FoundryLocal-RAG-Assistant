Title: The Grim Game (1919)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Irvin Willat
Main Cast: Harry Houdini, Thomas Jefferson, Ann Forrest, Augustus Phillips, Tully Marshall
Keywords: kidnapping, killer, escape artist

Overview: Jailed unjustly for a murder he did not commit, a young man uses his amazing powers of escape to free himself and pursue the actual killers, who hold his fiancée captive.
