Title: The Trumpet Of The Swan (2001)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Richard Rich
Main Cast: Jason Alexander, Mary Steenburgen, Reese Witherspoon, Seth Green, Carol Burnett
Keywords: trumpet, swan

Overview: The adventures of a young Trumpeter swan who cannot speak. With the help of a human boy and the love of his family and friends, Louie discovers his own unique talents which help him find his place in the world.
