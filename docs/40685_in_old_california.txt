Title: In Old California (1942)
Genres: Action, Romance, Western
Director: William C. McGann
Main Cast: John Wayne, Binnie Barnes, Albert Dekker, Helen Parrish, Patsy Kelly
Keywords: gold rush, sacramento

Overview: Boston pharmacist Tom Craig comes to Sacramento, where he runs afoul of local political boss Britt Dawson, who exacts protection payment from the citizenry. Dawson frames Craig with poisoned medicine, but Craig redeems himself during a Gold Rush epidemic.
