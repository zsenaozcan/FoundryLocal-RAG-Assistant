Title: Schlussmacher (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Matthias Schweighöfer
Main Cast: Matthias Schweighöfer, Milan Peschel, Nadja Uhl, Anna Bederke, Heiner Lauterbach
Keywords: love, money, break-up

Overview: An employee at a professional separation agency -- a business that helps couples break up -- embarks on his toughest assignment yet: helping his own parents part ways.
