Title: Lupin the 3rd (2014)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Ryûhei Kitamura
Main Cast: Shun Oguri, Meisa Kuroki, Go Ayano, Tadanobu Asano, Tetsuji Tamayama
Keywords: detective inspector, thief, art thief, live action

Overview: Interpol Inspector Zenigata begins a world-wide hunt for the infamous thief Lupin The 3rd. But together, they face a powerful enemy and form the Lupin team in response. The Lupin team then faces the challenge of retrieving the "Crimson Heart of Cleopatra" locked away in a fort like safe. The treasure is thought to allow its owner to control the world.
