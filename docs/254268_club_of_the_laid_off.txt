Title: Club of the Laid Off (1989)
Genres: Animation, Fantasy
Director: Jiří Barta
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: mannequin, czechoslovakia, stop motion

Overview: Laid-off old mannequins spend their cracked and broken lives in an old, abandoned warehouse. New mannequins are brought to the warehouse. They are old as well, but from a younger generation. The two groups must live together, but it's not easy at all.
