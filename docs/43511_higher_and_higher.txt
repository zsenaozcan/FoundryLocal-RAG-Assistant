Title: Higher and Higher (1943)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Tim Whelan
Main Cast: Michèle Morgan, Jack Haley, Frank Sinatra, Leon Errol, Barbara Hale
Keywords: musical

Overview: 1943 musical comedy with Frank Sinatra. A valet to a bankrupt millionaire plans to rebuild his boss's fortune by passing a scullery maid off as a high-society debutante. Based on a 1940 Broadway musical.
