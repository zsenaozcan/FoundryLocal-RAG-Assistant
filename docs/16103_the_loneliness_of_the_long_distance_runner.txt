Title: The Loneliness of the Long Distance Runner (1962)
Genres: Drama
Director: Tony Richardson
Main Cast: Michael Redgrave, Tom Courtenay, Avis Bunnage, Alec McCowen, James Bolam
Keywords: prison, rebel, running, sport

Overview: A rebellious youth, sentenced to a boy's reformatory for robbing a bakery, rises through the ranks of the institution through his prowess as a long distance runner. During his solitary runs, reveries of his life and times before his incarceration lead him to re-evaluate his privileged status as the Governor's prize runner.
