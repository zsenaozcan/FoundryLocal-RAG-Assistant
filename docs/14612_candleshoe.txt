Title: Candleshoe (1977)
Genres: Family
Director: Norman Tokar
Main Cast: David Niven, Jodie Foster, Helen Hayes, Leo McKern, Veronica Quilligan

Overview: Candleshoe is a 1977 Walt Disney Productions live action movie based on the Michael Innes novel Christmas at Candleshoe and starring Jodie Foster, Helen Hayes in her last screen appearance, David Niven and Leo McKern.
