Title: Repulsion (1965)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Roman Polanski
Main Cast: Catherine Deneuve, Ian Hendry, John Fraser, Yvonne Furneaux, Patrick Wymark
Keywords: london england, schizophrenia, post traumatic stress  disorder, hallucination, rent, loneliness, psychosis

Overview: In Roman Polanski's first English-language film, beautiful young manicurist Carole suffers from androphobia (the pathological fear of interaction with men). When her sister and roommate, Helen, leaves their London flat to go on an Italian holiday with her married boyfriend, Carole withdraws into her apartment. She begins to experience frightful hallucinations, her fear gradually mutating into madness.
