Title: The Barkleys of Broadway (1949)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Charles Walters
Main Cast: Fred Astaire, Ginger Rogers, Oscar Levant, Billie Burke, Jacques François
Keywords: dance, musical

Overview: Josh (Astaire) and Dinah (Rogers) Barkley are a successful musical-comedy team, known for their stormy (but passionate) relationship. Dinah feels overshadowed by Josh, and limited by the lighthearted musical roles he directs her in, and decides to stretch her skills by taking a role in a serious drama, directed by another man.
