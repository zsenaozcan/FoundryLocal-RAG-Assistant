Title: Like It Is (1998)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Paul Oremland
Main Cast: Steve Bell, Ian Rose, Roger Daltrey, Dani Behr, Jude Alderson
Keywords: gay, bare knuckle boxing, boxer, underclass, aspiring singer

Overview: A young, bare-knuckle boxer Craig (played by real-life boxer Steve Bell) moves from Blackpool to London, where he falls for a twenty-something music producer, Matt. Trouble ensues when shy, awkward Craig tries squeezing into Matt's glib lifestyle and Matt's scheming boss (played by Roger Daltrey) tries to break up the couple.
