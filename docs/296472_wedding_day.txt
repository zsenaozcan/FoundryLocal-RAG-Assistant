Title: Wedding Day (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: André Gordon
Main Cast: David Koechner, C. Thomas Howell, Jennifer Keller, Stephanie Drapeau, André Gordon

Overview: This thriller from directors Dale Fabrigar and André Gordon concerns two couples who decide to marry on the same day - readily anticipating the most joyous event of their young lives. All hell breaks loose, however, when three men - including a minister, a drug addict and a jealous brother - unleash violence on the ceremonies.
