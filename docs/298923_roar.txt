Title: Roar (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Adventure
Director: Kamal Sadanah
Main Cast: Pulkit Jawahar, Himarsha Venkatsamy, Pranay Dixit, Abhinav Shukla, Achint Kaur

Overview: After his photojournalist brother gets killed by a white tigress in the jungles of the Sundarbans, Pandit and his team of commandos enter the prohibited core area of the forest to avenge his death.
