Title: Verbo (2011)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Adventure
Director: Eduardo Chapero-Jackson
Main Cast: Alba García, Miguel Ángel Silvestre, Najwa Nimri, Víctor Clavijo, Macarena Gómez

Overview: Sara is a young woman who fails to understand the world she lives in. Trying to find meaning in life she would come to a mysterious place to undertake an adventure: it is a parallel universe, dark and fantastic, where life and death hang in the balance.
