Title: La sedia della felicità (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Carlo Mazzacurati
Main Cast: Valerio Mastandrea, Isabella Ragonese, Giuseppe Battiston, Antonio Albanese, Fabrizio Bentivoglio
Keywords: italy, small town

Overview: Dino craft practice tattoos, Bruna is a beautician; their studies are facing each other. They learn of a mysterious treasure hidden in a chair that belonged to a woman now dead.
