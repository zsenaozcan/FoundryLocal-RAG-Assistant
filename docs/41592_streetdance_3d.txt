Title: StreetDance 3D (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Max Giwa
Main Cast: Nichola Burley, Rachel McDowall, Richard Winsor, Eleanor Bron, Patrick Baladi
Keywords: ballet dancer, street dance, woman director, dance group

Overview: In order to win the Street Dance Championships, a dance crew is forced to work with ballet dancers from the Royal Dance School in exchange for rehearsal space.
