Title: I Love You Too (2001)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ruud van Hemert
Main Cast: Angela Schijf, Antonie Kamerling, Beau van Erven Dorens, Florence Kasumba, Dorothée Capelluto

Overview: While camping in France, Eric and Eddy fall in with two women. Eric talks to Silke, a Ugandan studying medicine in Holland. She tells him about her life and asks about the book he's writing: it's the story of his love affair with Reza. He tells her the story and we see it in flashbacks: Reza explodes into his boring life, she's unpredictable and their relationship becomes intense.
