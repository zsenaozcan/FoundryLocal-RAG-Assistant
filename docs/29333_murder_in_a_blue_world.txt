Title: Murder in a Blue World (1973)
Genres: Thriller, Crime
Director: Eloy de la Iglesia
Main Cast: Sue Lyon, Christopher Mitchum, Jean Sorel, Ramón Pons, Charly Bravo
Keywords: surreal, human experimentation, gay interest, serial killer, sexual harassment, motorcycle

Overview: Set in the future, the story follows a nurse who tries to bring her own style of relief to people condemned to die. Her identity is a mystery and she may not be quite what she seems.
