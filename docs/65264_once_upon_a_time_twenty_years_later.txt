Title: Once Upon a Time Twenty Years Later (1981)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Yuri Yegorov
Main Cast: Natalya Gundareva, Viktor Proskurin, Oleg Efremov, Igor Yasulovich, Valentin Smirnitskiy

Overview: During the twenty years school reunion classmates suddenly learn that one of them - Nadezhda Kruglova - is a proud mother of ten children.
