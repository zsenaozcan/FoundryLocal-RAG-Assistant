Title: Daddy Day Care (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Steve Carr
Main Cast: Eddie Murphy, Jeff Garlin, Steve Zahn, Regina King, Kevin Nealon
Keywords: competition, success, kindergarten, children, unemployment

Overview: Two men get laid off and have to become stay-at-home dads when they can't find jobs, which inspires them to open their own day-care center.
