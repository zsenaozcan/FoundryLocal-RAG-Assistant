Title: Alaska: Silence And Solitude (2005)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Bob Swerer Jr.
Main Cast: Dick Proenneke, Bob Swerer Jr., Bob Swerer Sr.
Keywords: wilderness, log cabin, hermit

Overview: Alaska Silence &amp; Solitude is the follow up to Alone in the Wilderness, filmed 20 years later. Bob Swerer and Bob Swerer Sr. visit Dick Proenneke at his famous cabin on Twin Lakes where the wildlife is still abundant and the scenery is spectacular.
