Title: Man of Her Dreams (1997)
Genres: Horror, Drama
Director: Martin Kitrosser
Main Cast: William R. Moses, Lysette Anthony, Patrick Cassidy, Wanda Acuna, Gordon Thomson
Keywords: psychopath

Overview: After suspecting her husband is playing the field, a nervous woman finds a sympathetic ear in the form of a strange man. The drawback is that her sensitive new-found friend is an obsessive psychopath.
