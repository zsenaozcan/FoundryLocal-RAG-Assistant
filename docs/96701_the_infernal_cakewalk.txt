Title: The Infernal Cakewalk (1903)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy
Director: Georges Méliès
Main Cast: Georges Méliès
Keywords: dancing, silent film, trick film

Overview: Pluto, having seen the earth, comes back home amazed at the success of that well-known dance, the "cake-walk." He has brought back with him two noted well-known dancers, who start their favorite dance amidst the flames.
