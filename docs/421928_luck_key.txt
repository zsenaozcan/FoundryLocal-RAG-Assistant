Title: Luck-Key (2016)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Gye-byeok Lee
Main Cast: Yoo Hae-jin, Lee Joon, Jo Yoon-hee, Lim Ji-yeon, Jo Han-cheol

Overview: A reverse comedy that tells the story of a perfectionist assassin who falls and hits his head in a sauna, giving him amnesia. When a down-and-out actor switches locker keys with him, they switch lives until the hit-man, who soon becomes an action hero on TV, starts to remember things.
