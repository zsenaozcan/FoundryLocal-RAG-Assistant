Title: The Manhattan Project (1986)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Marshall Brickman
Main Cast: John Lithgow, Christopher Collet, Cynthia Nixon, Jill Eikenberry, John Mahoney
Keywords: bomb, helicopter, plutonium, laser, heist, fugitive, teenager, scientist, nuclear, science

Overview: Named after the World War II-era program, the plot revolves around a gifted high school student who decides to construct a nuclear bomb for a national science fair. The film's underlying theme involves the Cold War of the 1980s when government secrecy and mutually assured destruction were key political and military issues.
