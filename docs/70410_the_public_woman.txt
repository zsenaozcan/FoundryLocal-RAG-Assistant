Title: The Public Woman (1984)
Genres: Drama
Director: Andrzej Żuławski
Main Cast: Valérie Kaprisky, Francis Huster, Lambert Wilson, Patrick Bauchau, Giselle Pascal
Keywords: paris, sex, immigrant, nudity, reality, wife, photography, political, erotic movie

Overview: An inexperienced young actress is invited to play a role in a film based on Dostoyevsky's 'The Possessed'. The film director, a Czech immigrant in Paris, takes over her life, and in a short time she is unable to draw the line between acting and reality. She winds up playing a real-life role posing as the dead wife of another Czech immigrant, who is manipulated by the filmmaker into commiting a political assasination.
