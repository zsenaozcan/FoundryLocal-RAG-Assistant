Title: Fisherman's Wharf (1939)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Music
Director: Bernard Vorhaus
Main Cast: Bobby Breen, Leo Carrillo, Henry Armetta, Lee Patrick, Tommy Bupp
Keywords: father son relationship, san francisco, adoption, fisherman, italian american, golden gate bridge, wharf
