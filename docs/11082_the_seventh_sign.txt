Title: The Seventh Sign (1988)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, Mystery, Fantasy
Director: Carl Schultz
Main Cast: Demi Moore, Michael Biehn, Jürgen Prochnow, Peter Friedman, Manny Jacobs
Keywords: christianity, prophecy, sacrifice, apocalypse, catholicism, mysticism, biblical plagues, gnosticism

Overview: Abby is a pregnant woman with a curious new boarder in the apartment over her garage. Turns out he's heaven-sent and is speeding along the Apocalypse by bloodying rivers, egging on plagues and following scripture word for word.
