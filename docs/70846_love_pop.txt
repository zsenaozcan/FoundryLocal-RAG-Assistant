Title: Love & Pop (1998)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Hideaki Anno
Main Cast: Tadanobu Asano, Kirari, Hirono Kudo, Asumi Miwa, Leo Morimoto
Keywords: camera

Overview: Pretty Tokyo teen Hiromi Yoshii (Asumi Miwa) and her girlfriends struggle with rivalries, clothes and boyfriends, just like any other high school clique. But out for kicks and blind to the consequences, the girls of Love &amp; Pop hide a dirty little secret. From phone booths and street corners, in department stores and restaurants, the girls practice enjo-kosai or "compensated dating" -- arranging liaisons with older men drawn to their evident nubile charms and schoolgirl uniform innocence.
