Title: Prehysteria! (1993)
Genres: Family, Drama, Fantasy, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Charles Band
Main Cast: Brett Cullen, Colleen Morris, Samantha Mills, Austin O'Brien, Tony Longo
Keywords: single parent, egg, dinosaur

Overview: A young boy and his family embark on a series of adventures when the boy finds some mysterious eggs which hatch to reveal a brood of baby dinosaurs.
