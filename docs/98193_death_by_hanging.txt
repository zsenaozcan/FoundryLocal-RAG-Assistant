Title: Death by Hanging (1968)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama, Foreign
Director: Nagisa Ōshima
Main Cast: Kei Satô, Fumio Watanabe, Toshirô Ishidô, Masao Adachi, Rokkô Toura
Keywords: satire

Overview: A Korean man is sentenced to death by hanging, but he survives the execution. For the following two hours, his executioners try to work out how to handle the situation in this black farce.
