Title: Ash Wednesday (2002)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime
Director: Edward Burns
Main Cast: Edward Burns, Brian Burns, Vincent Rubino, Jimmy Cummings, Elijah Wood
Keywords: bar, gun, hitman, irish mob, priest, tears, church, drink, vendetta, deli

Overview: Ash Wednesday is set in the Manhattan of the early 1980's and is about a pair of Irish-American brothers who become embroiled in a conflict with the Irish Mob.
