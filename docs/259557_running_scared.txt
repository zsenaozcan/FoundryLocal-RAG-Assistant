Title: Running Scared (1980)
Genres: Drama, Action, Adventure
Director: Paul Glickler
Main Cast: Ken Wahl, Judge Reinhold, Annie McEnroe, Bradford Dillman, John Saxon
Keywords: spy, florida, cold war, swamp, independent film, escape, boat chase, u.s. military, manhunt

Overview: It's 1961. Two servicemen smuggle a box of military gear to USA. Leroy tries out a military camera and accidentally takes a picture of some military facilities. Army finds one of the pictures and thinks they're communist spies.
