Title: Marked for Death (1990)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Dwight H. Little
Main Cast: Steven Seagal, Basil Wallace, Keith David, Tom Wright, Joanna Pacula
Keywords: voodoo, cop, murder, family

Overview: Just retired from the Drug Enforcement Agency, John Hatcher (Seagal) returns to his hometown and quickly discovers that drugs have infiltrated his old neighborhood. Determined to drive the dealers out, Hatcher crosses pathes with a ferocious Jamaican druglord who vowes that Hatcher and his family are now marked for death.
