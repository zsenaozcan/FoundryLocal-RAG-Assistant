Title: George Lopez: Tall, Dark & Chicano (2009)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Marty Callner
Main Cast: George Lopez
Keywords: comedian, stand-up comedy, live comedy

Overview: One of the premier comedic talents in the entertainment industry, George Lopez stars in his second HBO solo special. George Lopez: Tall, Dark &amp; Chicano is a live stand-up special performed in front of a packed arena crowd at the AT&amp;T Center in San Antonio, TX.
