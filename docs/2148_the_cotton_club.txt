Title: The Cotton Club (1984)
Genres: Music, Drama, Crime, Romance
Director: Francis Ford Coppola
Main Cast: Richard Gere, Gregory Hines, Diane Lane, Lonette McKee, Bob Hoskins
Keywords: jazz, jazz musician, musical, mafia

Overview: The story of the people that frequented Harlem's famous nightclubs, 'The Cotton Club', and those that ran it.
