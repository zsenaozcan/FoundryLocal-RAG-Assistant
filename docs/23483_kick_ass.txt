Title: Kick-Ass (2010)
Genres: Action, Crime
Director: Matthew Vaughn
Main Cast: Aaron Taylor-Johnson, Chloë Grace Moretz, Christopher Mintz-Plasse, Nicolas Cage, Mark Strong
Keywords: crime fighter, secret identity, comic book, superhero, murder, comedy, mafia, teenager, violence, realism
