Title: The Bad Bunch (1973)
Genres: Drama
Director: Greydon Clark
Main Cast: Greydon Clark, Tom Johnigarn, Jacqueline Cole, Bambi Allen, Aldo Ray
Keywords: ghetto, vietnam, hostility, blaxploitation, soldier, violence

Overview: A white ex-GI goes to a black ghetto to deliver a letter from his buddy, a black soldier who died in Vietnam. When he arrives there he encounters hostility and trouble from all sides.
