Title: Los Olvidados (1950)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Luis Buñuel
Main Cast: Alfonso Mejía, Estela Inda, Miguel Inclán, Roberto Cobo, Alma Delia Fuentes
Keywords: slum, mexico, homelessness, fair, delinquent, approved school , teacher, rebellious youth, alcoholic, older woman younger man relationship

Overview: A group of juvenile delinquents live a violent and crime-filled life in the festering slums of Mexico City, and the morals of young Pedro are gradually corrupted and destroyed by the others.
