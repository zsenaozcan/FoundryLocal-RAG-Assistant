Title: Greg Fitzsimmons: Life on Stage (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Tyler Spindel
Main Cast: Greg Fitzsimmons

Overview: Comedy Central Special Released 18th of August 2013.  Life On The Stage finds Greg back in his hometown of Tarrytown, NY to give a cutting standup set of twisted life advice. Nothing is off-limits in his advice for getting older, and stories of his life on and off stage.
