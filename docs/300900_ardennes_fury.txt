Title: Ardennes Fury (2014)
Genres: War, Action
Director: Joseph J. Lawson
Main Cast: Tom Stedham, Bill Voorhees, Tino Struckmann, Larry Gamell Jr., Lauren Vera
Keywords: world war ii, tank, battle of the bulge, mockbuster

Overview: As the Battle of the Bulge rages on, an American tank unit gets trapped behind Nazi lines. With just hours before the bombs of Operation Ardennes Fury fall, the tank's commander makes the risky decision to rescue an orphanage.
