Title: Solyaris (1968)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Lidiya Ishimbaeva
Main Cast: Vasili Lanovoy, Antonina Pilyus, Vladimir Etush, Viktor Zozulin, Vyacheslav Dugin
Keywords: based on novel

Overview: Kris Kelvin joins the space station orbiting the planet Solaris, only to find its two crew members plagued by "phantoms," creations of Solaris. Kelvin is soon confronted with his own phantom, taking the shape of his dead wife Hari.
