Title: The Hawk Is Dying (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Julian Goldberger
Main Cast: Paul Giamatti, Michelle Williams, Michael Pitt, Rusty Schwimmer, Robert Wisdom
Keywords: kids and family, independent film, hawk

Overview: A Gainesville Florida auto upholsterer (Giamatti) attempts to subvert his mundane life by training a wild, red-tailed hawk.
