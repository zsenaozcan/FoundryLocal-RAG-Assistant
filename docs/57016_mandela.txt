Title: Mandela (1996)
Genres: Documentary, History
Director: Angus Gibson
Main Cast: Nelson Mandela, Winnie Mandela, F.W. de Klerk, Eugene Terre'Blanche
Keywords: africa, south africa, apartheid, protest, biography, nelson mandela, politics, independent film, democracy, racial tension

Overview: A documentary that chronicles the life of South African leader Nelson Mandela. Mandela is probably best known for his 27 years of imprisonment, and for bringing an end to apartheid. But this film also sheds light on the little-known early period of Mandela's life.
