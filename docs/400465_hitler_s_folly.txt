Title: Hitler's Folly (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Animation
Director: Bill Plympton
Main Cast: Dana Ashbrook, Kevin Kolack, Bill Plympton, Nate Steinwachs
Keywords: nazis, adolf hitler, satire, mockumentary

Overview: "Hitler's Folly" explores what might have happened if Adolf Hitler's art career had been more successful and instead of becoming an evil dictator, he was inspired to become an animator like Walt Disney.
