Title: Ticking Clock (2011)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Science Fiction, Mystery
Director: Ernie Barbarash
Main Cast: Cuba Gooding Jr., Neal McDonough, Austin Abrams, Yancey Arias, Danielle Nicolet

Overview: A reporter stumbles upon the journal of a murderer replete with plans to butcher specific people, and investigates on his own, finding that every trail leads to a 9-year-old orphan living in a group home.
