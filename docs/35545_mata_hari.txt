Title: Mata Hari (1931)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Romance
Director: George Fitzmaurice
Main Cast: Greta Garbo, Ramon Novarro, Lionel Barrymore, Lewis Stone, C. Henry Gordon
Keywords: spy, world war i

Overview: A semi-fictionalized account of the life of Mata Hari, an exotic dancer who was accused of spying for Germany during World War I.
