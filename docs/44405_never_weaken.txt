Title: Never Weaken (1921)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Fred C. Newmeyer
Main Cast: Harold Lloyd, Mildred Davis, Roy Brooks, Mark Jones, Charles Stevenson
Keywords: silent film

Overview: Our hero (Lloyd) is infatuated with a girl in the next office. In order to drum up business for her boss, an osteopath, he gets an actor friend to pretend injuries that the doctor "cures", thereby building a reputation. When he hears that his girl is marrying another, he decides to commit suicide and spends the bulk of the film in thrilling, failed attempts.
