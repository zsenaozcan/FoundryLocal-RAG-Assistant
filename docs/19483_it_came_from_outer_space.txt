Title: It Came from Outer Space (1953)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Jack Arnold
Main Cast: Richard Carlson, Barbara Rush, Charles Drake, Joe Sawyer, Russell Johnson
Keywords: small town, spacecraft, arizona, alien life-form, mind control, meteorite, astronomer

Overview: Author &amp; amateur astronomer John Putnam and schoolteacher Ellen Fields witness an enormous meteorite come down near a small town in Arizona, but Putnam becomes a local object of scorn when, after examining the object up close, he announces that it is a spacecraft, and that it is inhabited...
