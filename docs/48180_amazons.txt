Title: Amazons (1986)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Action, Science Fiction, Foreign
Director: Alejandro Sessa
Main Cast: Ty Randolph, Penelope Reed, Joseph Whipp, Danitza Kingsley, Wolfram Hoechst

Overview: An epic from the dark ages about the legendary lost tribe of warrior women! The girls fly into danger, come up against fierce tribes, fall prey to sorcery, put to rest a family rivalry of centuries past and battle to victory!
