Title: A Dangerous Method (2011)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: David Cronenberg
Main Cast: Keira Knightley, Viggo Mortensen, Michael Fassbender, Sarah Gadon, Vincent Cassel
Keywords: based on novel, psychoanalysis, sigmund freud, biography, spanking, based on play or musical, cigar smoking, cheating husband, based on true events
