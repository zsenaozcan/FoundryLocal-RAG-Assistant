Title: Mon oncle (1958)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jacques Tati
Main Cast: Jacques Tati, Jean-Pierre Zola, Adrienne Servantie, Alain Bécourt, Lucien Frégis
Keywords: paris, father son relationship, city portrait, factory worker, parent child relationship, factory, new building, brother-in-law, modernity, car crash

Overview: With many idea rich comic situations French director Jacques Tati uses his poetic ways and knowledge of the discrepancy between heart and technique to tell a gentle business satiric tale that won him the Oscar for best foreign film in 1959.
