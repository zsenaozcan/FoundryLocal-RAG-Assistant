Title: Peggy Sue Got Married (1986)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Fantasy, Romance
Director: Francis Ford Coppola
Main Cast: Nicolas Cage, Jim Carrey, Kathleen Turner, Helen Hunt, Catherine Hicks
Keywords: time travel, high school reunion

Overview: Peggy Sue faints at a Highschool reunion. When she wakes up she finds herself in her own past, just before she finished school.
