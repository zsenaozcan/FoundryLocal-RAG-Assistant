Title: Thorne: Scaredycat (2010)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Benjamin Ross
Main Cast: David Morrissey, Aidan Gillen, Aidan Gillen, Eddie Marsan, O.T. Fagbenle

Overview: D.I. Tom Thorne's latest case puts him on the hunt for not one, but two serial killers, who seem to be working together, yet killing separately.
