Title: Zotz! (1962)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy
Director: William Castle
Main Cast: Tom Poston, Julia Meade, Fred Clark, Jim Backus, Cecil Kellaway
Keywords: based on novel, professor, magic, spying, coin

Overview: Jonathan Jones, a professor of ancient languages, comes into possession of an ancient coin. He translates its inscription, which gives him three powers: to inflict pain, slow down time or kill. Soon, he's pursued by enemy spies who have learned about the magic coin.
