Title: Dark of the Sun (1968)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, War
Director: Jack Cardiff
Main Cast: Rod Taylor, Yvette Mimieux, Peter Carsten, Jim Brown, Kenneth More
Keywords: based on novel, mercenary, heist, chainsaw, congo

Overview: A band of mercenaries led by Captain Curry travel through the Congo across deadly terrain, battling rival armies, to rescue $25 million in uncut diamonds.
