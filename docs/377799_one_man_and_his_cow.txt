Title: One Man and his Cow (2015)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Mohamed Hamidi
Main Cast: Fatsah Bouyahmed, Lambert Wilson, Jamel Debbouze, Julia Piaton, Hajar Masdouki
Keywords: fair, cow, agriculture

Overview: An Algerian man's life-long dream finally comes true when he receives an invitation to take his cow Jacqueline to the Paris International Agriculture Fair.
