Title: Where the Boys Are '84 (1984)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Hy Averback
Main Cast: Lynn-Holly Johnson, Lisa Hartman, Lorna Luft, Wendy Schaal, Russell Todd

Overview: Where the Boys Are '84 is a 1984 remake of the 1960 teen sex comedy film Where the Boys Are, starring Lisa Hartman, Lorna Luft, Wendy Schaal and Lynn-Holly Johnson. Directed by Hy Averback and produced by Allan Carr, it was the first film released by Tri-Star Pictures. Source: http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Where_the_Boys_Are_%2784
