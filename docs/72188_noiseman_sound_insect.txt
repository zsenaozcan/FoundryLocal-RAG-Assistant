Title: Noiseman Sound Insect (1997)
Genres: Animation, Science Fiction, Fantasy
Director: Kouji Morimoto
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: The film is set in the distant future in a city called Cahmpon. A scientist creates a synthetic life-form called Noiseman, which erases music from the airwaves by turning it into crystals. A group of Biker street kids rebels against this tyranny.
