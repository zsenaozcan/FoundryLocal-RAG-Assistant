Title: Basket Case (1982)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Frank Henenlotter
Main Cast: Kevin Van Hentenryck, Terri Susan Smith, Beverly Bonner, Robert Vogel, Diana Browne
Keywords: siamese twins, basket, mood ring eyes

Overview: A young man carrying a big basket that contains his deformed Siamese-twin brother seeks vengeance on the doctors who separated them against their will.
