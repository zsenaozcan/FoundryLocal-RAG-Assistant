Title: Bite the Bullet (1975)
Genres: Action, Western, Adventure
Director: Richard Brooks
Main Cast: Gene Hackman, Candice Bergen, James Coburn, Ben Johnson, Ian Bannen
Keywords: horse race, horse, endurance, ex prostitute, mexican

Overview: At the beginning of the 20th century, a newspaper organizes an endurance horse race : 700 miles to run in a few days. 9 adventurers are competing, among them a woman, Miss Jones, a Mexican, an Englishman, a young cow-boy, an old one and two friends, Sam Clayton and Luke Matthews. All those individualists will learn to respect each other.
