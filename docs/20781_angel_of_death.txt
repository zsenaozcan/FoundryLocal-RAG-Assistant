Title: Angel of Death (2009)
Genres: Crime, Action, Thriller
Director: Paul Etheredge
Main Cast: Zoë Bell, Jake Abel, Vail Bloom, Justin Huen, Doug Jones
Keywords: hitman

Overview: In the tradition of Grindhouse, Kill Bill and Sin City, award-winning comic book writer Ed Brubaker (Incognito, The Death of Captain America, Daredevil) teams up with stuntwoman-turned-cult star Zoe Bell (Death Proof, TV's "Lost") to deliver a stark, stylish pulp thriller about a very bad girl gone "good."
