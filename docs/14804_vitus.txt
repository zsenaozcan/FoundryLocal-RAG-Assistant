Title: Vitus (2006)
Genres: Music, Drama
Director: Fredi M. Murer
Main Cast: Fabrizio Borsani, Teo Gheorghiu, Julika Jenkins, Urs Jucker, Bruno Ganz
Keywords: child prodigy

Overview: Vitus tells the story of a highly-gifted boy (played by real-life piano prodigy Teo Gheorghiu) whose parents have demanding and ambitious plans for him.
