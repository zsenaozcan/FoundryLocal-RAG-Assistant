Title: Architecture 101 (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Lee Yong-joo
Main Cast: Uhm Tae-woong, Han Ga-in, Lee Je-hoon, Bae Suzy, Jo Jung-suk

Overview: When Seung Min was on his first year at the Academy of Architecture, he met Seo Yeon. She was a musician student, and Seung Min totally fell in love with her. Years have passed, and now he meets Seo Yeon again - she asks him to rebuild her father's old house.
