Title: Candy Razors (1980)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Eloy de la Iglesia
Main Cast: José Luis Manzano, Isela Vega, Verónica Castro, José Sacristán, Enrique San Francisco
Keywords: prostitute, robbery, journalist, madrid, delinquency, juvenile delinquent, drug, based on true events, cine quinqui

Overview: Jose Manuel Gomez Perales, "El Jaro", lives alone in Madrid, with no other company than his band and his "girlfriends." One day he meets Mercedes, a prostitute of Mexican origin. Mercedes falls for him and offers him her home to take him apart from his life of crime.
