Title: Jo pour Jonathan (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Maxime Giroux
Main Cast: Raphaël Lacaille, Jean-Sébastien Courchesne, Éliane Gagnon, Vanessa Pilon, Jean-Alexandre Létourneau
Keywords: brother brother relationship, car race, quebec, car accident

Overview: Jo lives in the shadow of his older brother. Amidst a world of delinquency, they become interested in illegal car racing. One night they participate in of them, never reaching the finish line. Jo will become Jonathan.
