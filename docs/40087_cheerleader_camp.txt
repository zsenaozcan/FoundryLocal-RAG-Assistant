Title: Cheerleader Camp (1988)
Genres: Horror, Comedy, Thriller
Director: John Quinn
Main Cast: Betsy Russell, Leif Garrett, Lucinda Dickey, Lorie Griffin, George Buck Flower
Keywords: female nudity, nudity, cheerleader, nightmare, summer camp, mascot, bikini, murder, slow motion scene, independent film

Overview: Alison follows her friends to a summer camp for cheerleaders. But she is having bad nightmares. Her boyfriend has followed her to the camp but he seems to be more interested in the other girls, girls who sooner or later are found brutally murdered. Alison starts to believe that she has a split-personality who kills them.
