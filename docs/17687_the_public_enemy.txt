Title: The Public Enemy (1931)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: James Cagney, Jean Harlow, Edward Woods, Joan Blondell, Donald Cook
Keywords: best friend, gangster, grapefruit

Overview: James Cagney star's as Tom Powers, a trouble-ridden individual who rises from the position of cheap thug to that of a powerful Prohibition gangster. When his best friend is murdered, Powers self-destructively seeks deadly retaliation.
