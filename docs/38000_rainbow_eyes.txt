Title: Rainbow Eyes (2007)
Genres: Thriller, Romance, Foreign
Director: Yang Yun-ho
Main Cast: Kim Kang-woo, Kim Gyu-ri

Overview: Officers Kyung-yoon CHO and Eun-joo PARK are on the hunt a killer following duo of ghastly murders. The victims carry a common military past, and the secret of their past must be unraveled to find the killer.
