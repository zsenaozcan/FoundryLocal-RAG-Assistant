Title: Vacancy (2007)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Nimród Antal
Main Cast: Kate Beckinsale, Luke Wilson, Ethan Embry, Scott G. Anderson, Frank Whaley
Keywords: motel, hidden camera, remoteness, deception, psychological thriller, killer, trapped, survival horror, estranged couple, broken down car

Overview: A young married couple becomes stranded at an isolated motel and find hidden video cameras in their room. They realize that unless they escape, they'll be the next victims of a snuff film.
