Title: Final Destination (2000)
Genres: Horror
Director: James Wong
Main Cast: Devon Sawa, Ali Larter, Kerr Smith, Tony Todd, Seann William Scott
Keywords: omen, airplane accident, corpse, death, near-death experience

Overview: After a teenager has a terrifying vision of him and his friends dying in a plane crash, he prevents the accident only to have Death hunt them down, one by one.
