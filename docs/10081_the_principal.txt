Title: The Principal (1987)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Crime, Drama
Director: Christopher Cain
Main Cast: Jim Belushi, Louis Gossett, Jr., Rae Dawn Chong, Sean Michael Allen, Kelly Jo Minter
Keywords: resistance, drug traffic, teacher, youth gang, violence, school life

Overview: Burglary. Drugs. Assault. Rape. The students at Brandel High are more than new Principal Rick Latimer bargained for. Gangs fight to control the school using knives - even guns - when they have to. When Latimer and the head of security try to clean up the school and stop the narcotics trade, they run up against a teenage mafia. A violent confrontation on the campus leads to a deadly showdown with the drug dealer's gang, and one last chance for Latimer to save his career... and his life.
