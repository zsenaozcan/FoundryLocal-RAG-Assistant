Title: The Swedish Moment (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Tommi Korpela
Main Cast: Tommi Korpela, Minna Haapkylä, Elina Knihtilä, Hannu-Pekka Björkman, Johannes Holopainen
Keywords: dark comedy

Overview: Pop singer Kari and his girl dump a tightly wrapped mattress on a frozen lake. Things don’t quite go as planned, and the two go to Kari’s old drummer friend for help. Paranoia, jealousy and a series of increasingly absurd events ensue.
