Title: Resurrecting the Street Walker (2009)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: James Powell

Overview: Whilst documenting his life as a lowly intern, James Parker (James Powell) uncovers the long forgotten film, The Street Walker. Desperate to make a mark on the film industry and to prove his ever-doubting parents wrong, James endeavours to complete the unfinished horror movie, resulting in a murderous obsession.
