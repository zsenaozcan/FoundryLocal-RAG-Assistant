Title: Out for a Kill (2003)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Michael Oblowitz
Main Cast: Steven Seagal, Мишель Го, Corey Johnson, Том Ву, Брюс Ванг

Overview: An unsuspecting university professor is an unwitting accomplice in a foiled Chinese cocaine deal. Wrongly imprisoned, he escapes to take his revenge and prove his innocence.
