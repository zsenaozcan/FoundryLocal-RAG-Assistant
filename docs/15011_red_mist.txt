Title: Red Mist (2008)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Paddy Breathnach
Main Cast: Arielle Kebbel, Sarah Carter, Stephen Dillane, Andrew-Lee Potts, Alex Wyndham
Keywords: coma, revenge, mental institution, scar, loner, out of body experience, slamming a door, glass

Overview: A young doctor in a US hospital administers a powerful and untested cocktail of drugs to a coma victim. But instead of curing him, it triggers a powerful "out-of-body" experience and enables the patient - a depraved and dangerous loner - to inhabit other people's bodies and, through them, take revenge on the bullying medical students who were accidentally responsible for his condition.
