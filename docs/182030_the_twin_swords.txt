Title: The Twin Swords (1965)
Genres: Action, Foreign
Director: Hsu Tseng-Hung
Main Cast: Jimmy Wang Yu, Chin Ping, Ivy Ling Po, Petrina Fung Bo-Bo, Petrina Fung Bo-Bo
Keywords: martial arts

Overview: Kwei Wu happens upon a kidnapping with his wife Kan Lien-chu at the Red Lotus Temple. Kan sends Kwei to go for reinforcements while she stays to fight the kidnappers. Fortunately, the mysterious Scarlet Maid is surreptitiously helping her.
