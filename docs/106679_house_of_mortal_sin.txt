Title: House of Mortal Sin (1976)
Genres: Horror
Director: Pete Walker
Main Cast: Anthony Sharp, Susan Penhaligon, Stephanie Beacham, Norman Eshley, Sheila Keith
Keywords: confession, murder, priest, church, proto-slasher

Overview: A troubled young girl goes to confession at the local church. Unfortunately, the sexually frustrated priest she confesses to becomes obsessed with her. At first, the priest stalks the girl, but later it is revealed that he will stop at nothing, including blackmail and murder, just to get close to her
