Title: Lucid Dream (2017)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Kim Joon-Sung
Main Cast: Go Soo, Sol Kyung-gu, Park Yoo-chun, Kang Hye-jung, Park In-hwan
Keywords: child kidnapping

Overview: Dae-ho, an investigative journalist, seeks to track down the whereabouts of his son who was abducted three years ago. With the help of a detective and a psychiatrist friend, he will retrace his memory of the incident through the use of lucid dreaming techniques.
