Title: Jamon Jamon (1992)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Bigas Luna
Main Cast: Javier Bardem, Penélope Cruz, Jordi Mollà, Stefania Sandrelli, Tomás Martín
Keywords: male nudity, female nudity, father son relationship, prostitute, adultery, sexuality, jealousy, wife husband relationship, infidelity, symbolism

Overview: Jose Luis is an executive at his parents underwear factory where his girlfriend Sylvia works on the shop floor. When Sylvia becomes pregnant, Jose Luis promises her that he will marry her, most likely against the wishes of his parents. Jose Luis' mother is determined to break her son's engagement to a girl from a lower-class family, and hires Raul, a potential underwear model and would-be bullfighter to seduce Sylvia.
