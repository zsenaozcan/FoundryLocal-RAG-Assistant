Title: The Skinny (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Patrik-Ian Polk
Main Cast: Jussie Smollett
Keywords: black lgbt

Overview: Feature film from award-winning writer/director Patrik-Ian Polk, tells the story of five black Brown University classmate s- four gay men and one lesbian - reuniting in the Big Apple for a weekend of sin, fun, secrets, lies and drama.
