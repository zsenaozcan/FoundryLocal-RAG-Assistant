Title: Route 181: Fragments of a Journey in Palestine-Israel (2004)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Michel Khleifi
Main Cast: Michel Khleifi, Eyal Sivan

Overview: Route 181 is the epic record of a road trip undertaken in the summer of 2002 by two filmmakers, one Palestinian and one Israeli, along sections of what had been designated as the border between Israel and Palestine by U.N. Resolution 181 in 1947.
