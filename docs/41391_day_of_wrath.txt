Title: Day of Wrath (1943)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Carl Theodor Dreyer
Main Cast: Preben Lerdorff Rye, Lisbeth Movin, Thorkild Roose, Olaf Ussing, Preben Neergaard
Keywords: denmark, witchcraft

Overview: In a 17th-century Danish village, an old woman is accused of witchcraft. In the shadow of her flight, capture, confession, and burning at the stake, the young wife of the town's aging pastor falls in love with the pastor's son. Her confession of this illicit affair to her husband brings on her husband's death. At the funeral the pastor's mother denounces the young widow as a witch. Will the widow's lover come to her defense, or has the day of wrath returned?
