Title: Metropia (2009)
Genres: Animation, Drama, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Tarik Saleh
Main Cast: Vincent Gallo, Juliette Lewis, Udo Kier, Stellan Skarsgård, Alexander Skarsgård
Keywords: future, dystopia, mind control, independent film, adult animation, dystopic future

Overview: In the near future, oil reserves are nearly depleted and Europe is connected by series of underground tunnels. While navigating these tunnels, Roger hears voices, one in particular. Seeking a way to rid himself of the voice only leads Roger deeper into a bizarre conspiracy of control - mind and body.
