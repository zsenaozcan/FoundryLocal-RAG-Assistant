Title: 4 Horror Tales - Roommates (2006)
Genres: Horror
Director: Eun-kyung Kim
Main Cast: Jin Yong Heo, Kim Joo-ryoung, Ri-na Kim, Yeong-jin Sin, Joo-hee Yoo
Keywords: roommate, vision

Overview: Roommates Yoo-jin, Eun-soo, Bo-ram, and Da-young are cramming for a college entrance exam. It's difficult for them to adapt to the stifling atmosphere of the women's lodging institute - and to get along with each other due to their differing personalities. Yoo-jin has the most difficulty with the stuffy institute life. she begins to have visions of events that took place at the institute in the past - like the tragic fire that occurred years ago. Yoo-jin gradually becomes consumed with fear, and the relationship among the four intimate friends begins to suffer.
