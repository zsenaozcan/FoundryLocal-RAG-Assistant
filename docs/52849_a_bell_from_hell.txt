Title: A Bell From Hell (1973)
Genres: Foreign, Horror
Director: Claudio Guerín
Main Cast: Renaud Verley, Viveca Lindfors, Alfredo Mayo, Maribel Martín, Nuria Gimeno
Keywords: asylum, aunt, revenge, inheritance

Overview: A young man is released from an asylum and returns home for revenge on his aunt and her three daughters, who had him declared insane in order to steal his inheritance.
