Title: Mrs. Dalloway (1997)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Marleen Gorris
Main Cast: Vanessa Redgrave, Natascha McElhone, Michael Kitchen, Alan Cox, Sarah Badel
Keywords: female nudity, flower, impalement, woman director
