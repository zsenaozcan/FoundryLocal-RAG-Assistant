Title: The Promise (2017)
Genres: History, Romance, Drama
Director: Terry George
Main Cast: Oscar Isaac, Charlotte Le Bon, Christian Bale, Daniel Giménez Cacho, Shohreh Aghdashloo
Keywords: love triangle, journalist, artist, genocide, armenian genocide, medical student, ottoman empire

Overview: Set during the last days of the Ottoman Empire, a love triangle develops between Mikael, a brilliant medical student, the beautiful and sophisticated artist Ana, and Chris, a renowned American journalist based in Paris.
