Title: Twelve (2010)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Action, Crime
Director: Joel Schumacher
Main Cast: Chace Crawford, Rory Culkin, 50 Cent, Emily Meade, Emma Roberts
Keywords: drug dealer, murder

Overview: A young drug dealer watches as his high-rolling life is dismantled in the wake of his cousin's murder, which sees his best friend arrested for the crime.
