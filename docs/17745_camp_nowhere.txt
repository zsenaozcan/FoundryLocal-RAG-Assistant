Title: Camp Nowhere (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Jonathan Prince
Main Cast: Jonathan Jackson, Christopher Lloyd, Melody Kay, Andrew Keegan, Marnette Patterson
Keywords: parent child relationship, cutting the cord, con man, summer camp

Overview: Morris "Mud" Himmel has a problem. His parents desperately want to send him away to summer camp. He hates going to summer camp, and would do anything to get out of it. Talking to his friends, he realizes that they are all facing the same sentence: a boring summer camp. Together with his friends, he hatches a plan to trick all the parents into sending them to a camp of their own design.
