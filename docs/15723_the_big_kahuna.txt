Title: The Big Kahuna (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: John Swanbeck
Main Cast: Kevin Spacey, Danny DeVito, Peter Facinelli, Paul Dawson, Christopher Donahue
Keywords: religion and supernatural, independent film, lubricant, salesman

Overview: Three salesmen working for a firm that makes industrial lubricants are waiting in the company's "hospitality suite" at a manufacturers' convention for a "big kahuna" named Dick Fuller to show up, in hopes they can persuade him to place an order that could salvage the company's flagging sales.
