Title: The Incredible Melting Man (1977)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: William Sachs
Main Cast: Alex Rebar, Burr DeBenning, Myron Healey, Michael Alldredge, Ann Sweeny
Keywords: astronaut, crashing through glass

Overview: An astronaut exposed to cosmic rays outside of Saturn's rings returns to Earth and begins to melt away. Escaping from the hospital, he wanders around the backwoods looking for human flesh to eat.
