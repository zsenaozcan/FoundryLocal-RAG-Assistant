Title: Progression (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Tommy Caldwell, Johanna Ernst, Alex Honnold, Kevin Jorgeson, Paul Robinson
Keywords: sport, climbing, bouldering, rock climbing

Overview: Behind every breakthrough in the progression of climbing, there's a true story of doubt and determination, perseverance in the face of failure. From boulders, to big walls, to competition podiums, the climbers at the top of the game share a commitment to do whatever it takes to achieve their vision.
