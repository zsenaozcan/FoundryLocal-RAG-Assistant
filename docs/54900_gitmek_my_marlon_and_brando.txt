Title: Gitmek: My Marlon and Brando (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Huseyin Karabey
Main Cast: Ayca Damgaci
Keywords: narration, turkey

Overview: Ayça and Hama Ali are together in spirit, but divided by geography. With the outbreak of the Iraq War leaving him stranded, she embarks on an odyssey from Turkey to be reunited with him.
