Title: My Apprenticeship (1939)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Mark Donskoy
Main Cast: Aleksei Lyarsky, Irina Zarubina, Varvara Massalitinova, Ye. Lilina, Ivan Kudryavtsev
Keywords: autobiography

Overview: My Apprenticeship (V lyudyakh) was the second entry in Russian director Mark Donskoy's "Maxim Gorki" trilogy. Picking up where 1938's My Childhood left off, the story covers the years in Gorki's life when the future writer (Alexei Lyarsky) was on his own, looking for a purpose and place in life.
