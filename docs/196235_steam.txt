Title: Steam (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kyle Schickner
Main Cast: Ruby Dee, Ally Sheedy, Kate Siegel, Alan Ritchson, Zach Mills
Keywords: sauna, widow, female friendship, lesbian, single mother, divorcee, catholic

Overview: Three women — a young coed, a forty-something single mother, and one a senior-aged widow — meet in the sauna of the local gym, where they gradually get to know one another and bond over their respective trials and tribulations.
