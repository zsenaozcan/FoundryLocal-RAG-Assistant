Title: A Kid in Aladdin's Palace (1998)
Genres: Family, Adventure
Director: Robert L. Levy
Main Cast: Thomas Ian Nicholas, Rhona Mitra

Overview: Calvin, a young pizza delivery boy, is mistaken for the "Great Deliverer" by Aladdin, an eighth century genie imprisoned in a lamp. Thinking that the boy is his only hope for deliverance, the genie lures him back to the eigth century where he must join forces with Ali Baba in order to save Aladdin from the evil hands of Luxor.
