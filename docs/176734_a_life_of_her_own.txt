Title: A Life of Her Own (1950)
Genres: Drama
Director: George Cukor
Main Cast: Lana Turner, Ray Milland, Tom Ewell, Louis Calhern, Ann Dvorak
Keywords: love, new york city, model

Overview: Glossy melodrama starring Lana Turner as a young woman from Kansas who moves to New York, becomes highly successful at a prestigious modeling agency, and falls in love with a married man.
