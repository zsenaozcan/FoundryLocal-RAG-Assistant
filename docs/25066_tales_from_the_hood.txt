Title: Tales from the Hood (1995)
Genres: Crime, Horror, Thriller
Director: Rusty Cundieff
Main Cast: Clarence Williams III, Corbin Bernsen, David Alan Grier, Wings Hauser, Duane Whitaker
Keywords: prison, child abuse, ku klux klan, police brutality, ghetto, anthology, evil doll, murder, hood, dirty cop

Overview: A strange funeral director tells four strange tales of horror with an African American focus to three drug dealers he traps in his place of business.
