Title: Intimidation (1960)
Genres: Crime
Director: Koreyoshi Kurahara
Main Cast: Jun Hamamura, Nobuo Kaneko, Yôko Kosono, Kôjirô Kusanagi, Kô Nishimura
Keywords: intimidation, japanese noir

Overview: Koreyoshi Kurahara’s ingeniously plotted, pocket-size noir concerns the intertwined fates of a desperate bank manager, blackmailed for book-cooking, and his resentful but timid underling, passed over for a promotion. The marvelously moody Intimidation (Aru kyouhaku) is an elegantly stripped-down and carefully paced crime drama.
