Title: The Schoolboy (2015)
Genres: Mystery, Drama
Director: Paloma Lommel
Main Cast: Kim Evans, Charlie Heaton, Sarah Lamesch, Emily Aitcheson, Emma Nicole Bailey

Overview: The Schoolboy tells the story of Claire Adams, a former teacher and her struggle with survivor's guilt. In the aftermath of a school shooting, she wants return to her old job, however the memories of her past keep haunting her.
