Title: The Face on the Milk Carton (1995)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Waris Hussein
Main Cast: Kellie Martin, Sharon Lawrence, Richard Masur, Jill Clayburgh, Edward Herrmann

Overview: Based on the book by Caroline B. Cooney, a girl happens to look down at a milk carton one day and she sees herself on the back! Could her parents really have kidnapped her?
