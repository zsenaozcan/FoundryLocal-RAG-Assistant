Title: The Secret of Evil (2014)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Dorian Fernández-Moris
Main Cast: Bruno Espejo, Lupita Mora, Eduardo Ramos, Willy Gutiérrez, Miguel Baldeón
Keywords: haunted house, haunting, ghost, found footage

Overview: Video footage depicting a supernatural encounter is all that remains of a filmmaker and his crew who disappeared while exploring a haunted house.
