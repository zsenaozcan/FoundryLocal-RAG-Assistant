Title: The Driller Killer (1979)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: Abel Ferrara
Main Cast: Abel Ferrara, Carolyn Marz, Baybi Day, Harry Schultz, Alan Wynroth
Keywords: female nudity, artist, hallucination, insomnia, painting, concert, murder, dark comedy, punk rock, blood

Overview: An artist slowly goes insane while struggling to pay his bills, work on his paintings, and care for his two female roommates, which leads him taking to the streets of New York after dark and randomly killing derelicts with a power drill.
