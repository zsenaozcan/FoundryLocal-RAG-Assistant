Title: Conquest (1983)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy, Horror
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Jorge Rivero, Andrea Occhipinti, Sabrina Siani, Conrado San Martín, Gioia Scola
Keywords: monster, magic, fog, archer, premonition, bromance, archery, atmospheric, evil, doppelganger

Overview: A young man, armed with a magical bow and arrows, embarks on a mystical journey through a mystical land to rid it of all evil and joins forces with an outlaw to take down an evil witch bent on claiming the magic bow for evil.
