Title: Empty Nest (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Daniel Burman
Main Cast: Cecilia Roth, Oscar Martinez, Carlos Bermejo, Inés Efron, Arturo Goetz
Keywords: jewry, aging, separation

Overview: Empty Nest tells the story of a couple who have to struggle to find themselves after their children grow up and move out. It starts out as a fairly simple story of a couple but becomes complicated by a series of events that may or may not be occurring only in Leonardo’s mind.
