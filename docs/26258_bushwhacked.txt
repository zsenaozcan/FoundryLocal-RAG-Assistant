Title: Bushwhacked (1995)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Comedy, Crime, Family
Director: Greg Beeman
Main Cast: Daniel Stern, Jon Polito, Brad Sullivan, Ann Dowd, Anthony Heald
Keywords: cave, waterfall, children, mistaken identity, boy scouts leader

Overview: A inept courier realises he has been used by criminals to deliver money. On the run from both the criminals and police, he poses as a Scout leader and leads a scout group on a hike through the mountains.
