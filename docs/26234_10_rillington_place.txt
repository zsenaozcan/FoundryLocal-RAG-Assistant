Title: 10 Rillington Place (1971)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Richard Fleischer
Main Cast: Richard Attenborough, John Hurt, Judy Geeson, Pat Heywood, Isobel Black
Keywords: biography, british, based on true story, serial killer

Overview: The story of British serial killer John Christie, who committed most or all of his crimes in the titular terraced house, and the miscarriage of justice involving Timothy Evans.
