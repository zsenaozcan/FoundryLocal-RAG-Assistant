Title: While She Was Out (2008)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Susan Montford
Main Cast: Kim Basinger, Lukas Haas, Craig Sheffer, Luke Gair, Leonard Wu
Keywords: suspense, survival

Overview: A suburban housewife is forced to fend for herself when she becomes stranded in a desolate forest with four murderous thugs.
