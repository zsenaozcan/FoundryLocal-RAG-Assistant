Title: My Italian Secret: The Forgotten Heroes (2014)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Oren Jacoby
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: MY ITALIAN SECRET tells a heroic story that was all but lost to history, until now. The film recounts how WWII bicycling idol Gino Bartali, physician Giovanni Borromeo and other Italians worked with Jewish leaders and high-ranking officials of the Catholic Church, risking their lives by defying the Nazis to save thousands of Italy’s Jews.
