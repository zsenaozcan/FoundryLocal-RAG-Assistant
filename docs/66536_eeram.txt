Title: Eeram (2009)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Arivazhagan Venkatachalam
Main Cast: Aadhi, Nandha, Sindhu Menon, Saranya Mohan, Srinath
Keywords: attempted murder

Overview: Earam is a full of love and thrill.Aadhi is playing the role of Assistant Commissioner of Police. The romance between him and Sindhu Menon happens in the temple premises. Nandha plays an engineer who romances with Charanya Mohan. All the crime and chasing happens and heightens between the four young people.
