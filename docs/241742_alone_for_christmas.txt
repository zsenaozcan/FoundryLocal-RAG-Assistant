Title: Alone For Christmas (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Joseph J. Lawson
Main Cast: Kevin Sorbo, David DeLuise, Kim Little, Davis Cleveland, Jeremy Mascia
Keywords: talking dog, dog, home alone

Overview: When a family visits Grandma's house on Christmas Eve, they leave their dog at home alone. And when burglars try to take the presents from under the tree, the dog must use every trick it knows to stop them.
