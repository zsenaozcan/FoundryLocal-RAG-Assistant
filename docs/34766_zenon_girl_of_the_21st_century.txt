Title: Zenon: Girl of the 21st Century (1999)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Family, TV Movie
Director: Kenneth Johnson
Main Cast: Kirsten Storms, Raven-Symoné, Stuart Pankin, Holly Fulger, Frederick Coffin
Keywords: future, teenager, based on children's book, space station

Overview: Zenon Kar, a 13-year-old girl who lives on a space station in the year 2049, gets into some trouble and is banished to Earth. With help from some Earth friends she must find her way back.
