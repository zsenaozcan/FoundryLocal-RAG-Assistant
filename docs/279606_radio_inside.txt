Title: Radio Inside (1994)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Jeffrey Bell
Main Cast: William McNamara, Elisabeth Shue, Dylan Walsh, Peewee Love, Steve Zurk
Keywords: nightmare, surrealism, pool, undressing, lifeguard

Overview: As two brothers fall in love with the same woman they must come to grips with the accidental death of their father in this sensitive drama set in Miami.
