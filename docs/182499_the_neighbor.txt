Title: The Neighbor (1993)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Rodney Gibbons
Main Cast: Rod Steiger, Linda Kozlowski, Ron Lea, Jane Wheeler, Bruce Boa

Overview: A young couple, Mary and John, awaiting a baby is in search for a house when they decide to buy that lovely old villa they found by coincidence. What they both do not know is that Myron, the owner of that villa and their new neigbour, is a dangerous psychopath and that Mary reminds him of his dead mother. Mary begins to wonder about Myron's odd behaviour, but everybody thinks Mary's fears are due to her being pregnant.
