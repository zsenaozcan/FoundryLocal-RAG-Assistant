Title: Prince of the City (1981)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime
Director: Sidney Lumet
Main Cast: Treat Williams, Jerry Orbach, Richard Foronjy, Don Billett, Carmine Caridi
Keywords: new york, corruption, 1970s, police

Overview: New York City detective Daniel Ciello agrees to help the United States Department of Justice help eliminate corruption in the police department, as long as he will not have to turn in any close friends. In doing so, Ciello uncovers a conspiracy within the force to smuggle drugs to street informants
