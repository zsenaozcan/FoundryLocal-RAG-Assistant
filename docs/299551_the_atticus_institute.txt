Title: The Atticus Institute (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: Chris Sparling
Main Cast: William Mapother, Rya Kihlstedt, Sharon Maughan, Anne Betancourt, John Rubinstein
Keywords: experiment, psychology, government, weapon, supernatural, possession, mockumentary, entity, military, classified

Overview: In the early 1970s, Dr. Henry West creates an institute to find people with supernatural abilities. When Judith Winstead comes to the facility, she exhibits amazing abilities that the military wants to turn into a weapon.
