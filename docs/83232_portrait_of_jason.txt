Title: Portrait of Jason (1967)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Shirley Clarke
Main Cast: Jason Holliday, Shirley Clarke, Carl Lee
Keywords: lgbt, woman director, black lgbt

Overview: Interview with Jason Holliday aka Aaron Payne, house boy, would be cabaret performer, and self proclaimed hustler giving one man's gin-soaked pill-popped, view of what it was like to be coloured and gay in 1960's America.
