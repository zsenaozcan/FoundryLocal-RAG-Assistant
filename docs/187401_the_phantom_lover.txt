Title: The Phantom Lover (1995)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ronny Yu
Main Cast: Leslie Cheung, Jacklyn Wu 

Overview: In 1936 China, a nearly bankrupt drama troupe starts performing in a burned-out theater where the great actor Song Danping was killed. One of the actors, Wei Qing, starts seeing strange apparitions that could revive his troupe and deliver him to the same fate as Song Danping.
