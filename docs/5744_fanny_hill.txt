Title: Fanny Hill (1964)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Russ Meyer
Main Cast: Letícia Román, Veronica Ericson, Miriam  Hopkins, Ulli Lommel, Chris Howland
Keywords: illegal prostitution

Overview: Cult filmmaker Russ Meyer takes on directorial duties in Zugsmith s adaptation of the notorious erotic classic Fanny Hill. Set in pre-Victorian London, young Fanny finds herself taken in by a madame at one of the city s most elite brothels. A strange mix of Zugsmith s surreal slapstick and Meyer s trademark buxom beauties and thoughtful satire, Fanny Hill is an over-the-top saga of low-rent thrills in high-brow settings.
