Title: American Son (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Neil Abramson
Main Cast: Nick Cannon, Melonie Diaz, Matt O'Leary, Tom Sizemore, Percy Daggs III
Keywords: independent film, marine

Overview: The story of a young Marine, fresh from Camp Pendleton, who is forced to confront the complexities of adulthood and a volatile home life during a four-day Thanksgiving leave.
