Title: A Tale from the Past (1987)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Dhimitër Anagnosti
Main Cast: Elvira Diamanti, Admir Sorra, Xhevdet Ferri, Robert Ndrenika, Mimika Luca
Keywords: arranged marriage, older woman younger man relationship, generation gap, albanian

Overview: To meet the desires and needs of the parents, a marriage is arranged between 14-year-old Gjino and 20-year-old Marigo. Gjino reluctantly obeys, but Marigo, who loves another, tries various devious methods to free herself from this bizarre marriage agreement.
