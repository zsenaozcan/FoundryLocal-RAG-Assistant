Title: Where a Good Man Goes (1999)
Genres: Action, Drama, Foreign, Thriller
Director: Johnnie To
Main Cast: Lau Ching-Wan, Ruby Wong, Yiu-Cheung Lai, Lam Suet, Raymond Wong

Overview: Fresh from a prison term, a former Triad boss resides in a hotel in Macau, where he befriends its owner and her young son.
