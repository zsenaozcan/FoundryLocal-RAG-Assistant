Title: Hercules and the Circle of Fire (1994)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy, TV Movie
Director: Doug Lefler
Main Cast: Kevin Sorbo, Anthony Quinn, Tawny Kitaen, Mark Ferguson
Keywords: hero, mythology, greek mythology, legend, gods, hercules

Overview: When all the earth's fire begins to go out, Hercules and Deianeira must go in search for fire to stop the world from cold.  "Hercules and the Circle of Fire" is the third movie-length pilot episode of the television series "Hercules: The Legendary Journeys".
