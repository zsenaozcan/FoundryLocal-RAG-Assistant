Title: Donkey X (2007)
Genres: Family, Adventure, Animation
Director: Jose Pozo
Main Cast: David Fernández, Andreu Buenafuente, José Luis Gil, Sancho Gracia
Keywords: donkey

Overview: The donkey, Rucio, tells the true story of Don Quixote and defends the idea that he wasn't insane, but a very intelligent, passionate and enthusiastic fellow.
