Title: A Show of Force (1990)
Genres: Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Bruno Barreto
Main Cast: Amy Irving, Andy García, Lou Diamond Phillips, Robert Duvall, Kevin Spacey

Overview: In this thriller, television reporter Kate Ryan de Meléndez investigates the death of two radical Puerto Rican activists, whom she begins to believe may have been framed and murdered by undercover American agents.
