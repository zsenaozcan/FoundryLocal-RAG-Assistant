Title: The Way Home (2002)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Lee Jeong-hyang
Main Cast: Eul-boon Kim, Yoo Seung-Ho, Hyo-hee Dong, Kyung-hyun Min, Eun-kyung Yim
Keywords: grandmother grandson relationship, woman director

Overview: The story begins on a fine summers morning, when San-woo and his mother board a bus to the country. It is soon clear that the unsophisticated rural passengers annoy the seven-year-old urban boy. His mother is taking him to live with his 78-year-old mute, but not deaf, grandmother while she looks for a new job after a business venture failed in Seoul.
