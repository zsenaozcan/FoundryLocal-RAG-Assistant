Title: Snow on the Pines (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: پیمان معادی
Main Cast: Mahnaz Afshar, Vishka Asayesh, Saber Abbar, Hassan Majooni, Anahita Afshar

Overview: Piano teacher Roya (Mahnaz Afshar) realizes that her marriage to Ali is in a deadlock when she discovers some of her husband's secrets. In the meantime she meets a young musician (Saber Abar) and has to make a painful choice.
