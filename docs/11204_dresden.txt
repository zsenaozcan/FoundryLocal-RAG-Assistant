Title: Dresden (2006)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Roland Suso Richter
Main Cast: Felicitas Woll, John Light, Benjamin Sadler, Heiner Lauterbach, Marie Bäumer
Keywords: world war ii, assault, dresden, bombing

Overview: At a Dresden hospital in 1945, nurse Anna Mauth (Felicitas Woll) cares for badly injured British pilot Robert Newman (John Light), whom Anna believes to be a German deserter. As Allied forces close in, Anna grows close to Robert despite her engagement to Dr. Alexander Wenninger (Benjamin Sadler). The gripping historical romance won a 2006 German Television Award.
