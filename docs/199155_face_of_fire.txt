Title: Face of Fire (1959)
Genres: Drama
Director: Albert Band
Main Cast: James Whitmore, Royal Dano, Bettye Ackerman, Cameron Mitchell, Miko Oscard
Keywords: small town, burned alive, fear, disfigured face

Overview: A local handyman saves a child in a fire, but the burns he receives disfigure his face so much that the townspeople avoid him.
