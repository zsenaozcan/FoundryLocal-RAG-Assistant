Title: Vendetta for the Saint (1969)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Jim O'Connolly
Main Cast: Roger Moore, Rosemary Dexter, Ian Hendry, Aimi MacDonald, Fulton Mackay

Overview: Simon Templar is on holiday in Naples when a small uproar on a lunch table draws his attention. An English tourist attempts to greet an Italian businessman as an old friend, but the Italian refuses to acknowledge the greeting and claims never to have met him.
