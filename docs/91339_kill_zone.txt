Title: Kill Zone (1993)
Genres: Action, Thriller, War
Director: Cirio H. Santiago
Main Cast: David Carradine, Tony Dorsett, Rob Youngblood, Vic Trevino, James Gregory Paolleli
Keywords: b movie

Overview: An American colonel will pay any price to defeat the Viet Cong. When his unauthorized fighting force in Cambodia is discovered, he becomes a one man army, fighting a war of his own for a cause he knows is just.
