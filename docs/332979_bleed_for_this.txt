Title: Bleed for This (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ben Younger
Main Cast: Miles Teller, Aaron Eckhart, Katey Sagal, Ciarán Hinds, Ted Levine
Keywords: underdog, boxer, comeback, biography, rhode island, sport, fighter, come back

Overview: The inspirational story of World Champion Boxer Vinny Pazienza, who after a near fatal car crash, which left him not knowing if he'd ever walk again, made one of sports most incredible comebacks.
