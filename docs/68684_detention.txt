Title: Detention (2011)
Genres: Horror, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Joseph Kahn
Main Cast: Shanley Caswell, Josh Hutcherson, Spencer Locke, Dane Cook, A.D. Johnson
Keywords: high school, murder, slasher, horror spoof

Overview: As a killer named Cinderhella stalks the student body at the high school in Grizzly Lake, a group of co-eds band together to survive while they're all serving detention.
