Title: Oscar (1991)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Thriller, Family
Director: John Landis
Main Cast: Sylvester Stallone, Ornella Muti, Don Ameche, Peter Riegert, Tim Curry
Keywords: honesty, business man

Overview: Angelo "Snaps" Provolone made his dying father a promise on his deathbed: he would leave the world of crime and become an honest businessman. Despite having no experience in making money in a legal fashion, Snaps sets about to keep his promise.
