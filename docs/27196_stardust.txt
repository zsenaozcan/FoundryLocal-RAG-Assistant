Title: Stardust (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Apted
Main Cast: David Essex, Adam Faith, Larry Hagman, Rosalind Ayres, Marty Wilde

Overview: Sequel to 1973's That'll Be The Day. Jim McLain is now enjoying the nomadic 'gigs and groupies' life on tour with his band. When he achieves all his wildest dreams of international stardom, the sweet taste of success begins to turn sour.
