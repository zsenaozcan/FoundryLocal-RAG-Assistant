Title: From Prada to Nada (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Angel Gracia
Main Cast: Camilla Belle, Alexa PenaVega, April Bowlby, Wilmer Valderrama, Nicholas D'Agosto
Keywords: tattoo, cooking, bankrupt, sacrifice, latina, janitor, student, mariachi, law student, portrait

Overview: A whimsical fish-out-of-water story of two spoiled sisters: Nora (Camilla Belle), a law student, and Mary (Alexa Vega), an undergrad party girl, living with their father in a luxurious mansion in Beverly Hills. Mary has become so "90210" she refuses to admit she is of Mexican decent. When dad suddenly passes away, their posh lives are turned upside down. They discover they have been left penniless and are forced to move into their estranged aunt Aurelia's (Adriana Barraza) modest but lively home in the Latino-centric Boyle Heights neighborhood of East LA.
