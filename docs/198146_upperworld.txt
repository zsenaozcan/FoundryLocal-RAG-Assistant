Title: Upperworld (1934)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Roy Del Ruth
Main Cast: Warren William, Mary Astor, Ginger Rogers, Andy Devine, J. Carrol Naish
Keywords: blackmail, chauffeur, disappointment, scandal, marriage, loneliness, tragedy, love affair, millionaire, showgirl

Overview: A railroad tycoon, disillusioned with his marriage (his wife has no time for him - she's too busy giving parties and sailing on yachts), starts seeing a showgirl. Things go agreeably until the girl's manager, seeing his chance to make some big money, decides to blackmail the millionaire.
