Title: Love in the Afternoon (1957)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Billy Wilder
Main Cast: Gary Cooper, Audrey Hepburn, Maurice Chevalier, John McGiver, Van Doude
Keywords: hotel, playboy, love, private detective

Overview: Lovestruck conservatory student Adriane pretends to be just as much as a cosmopolitan lover as the worldly mature Frank Flannag hoping that l’amour will take hold.
