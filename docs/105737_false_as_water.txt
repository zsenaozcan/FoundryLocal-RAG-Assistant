Title: False as Water (1985)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Hans Alfredson
Main Cast: Malin Ek, Sverre Anker Ousdal, Marie Göranzon, Stellan Skarsgård, Örjan Ramberg
Keywords: jealousy, death, publisher

Overview: John is the owner of a small publishing company. One day John meets the poet Clara and falls in love with her. A movie about jealousy and death.
