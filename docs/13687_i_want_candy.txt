Title: I Want Candy (2007)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Stephen Surjik
Main Cast: Carmen Electra, Tom Riley, Tom Burke, Eddie Marsan, Michelle Ryan

Overview: Two hopeful lads from Leatherhead trying to break into the movies stumble upon the opportunity of a lifetime. Frustrated by their arty film teacher, wannabe producer Joe and his talented but neurotic director friend Baggy head to London to sell what they know is a script made of gold.
