Title: Ants in the Pants (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Marc Rothemund
Main Cast: Tobias Schenke, Axel Stein, Luise Helm, Mina Tander, Sissi Perlinger
Keywords: puberty, first time, teenage crush

Overview: Flo is not quite a normal teenager: While all his friends only think about sex he can't even pronounce the word "S - E - X". But everything changes completely when one morning Flo wakes up from a strange call. His penis, excited for the first time, begins to talk to him. He claims that Flo severely neglected him for 15 years and it's time to release him.
