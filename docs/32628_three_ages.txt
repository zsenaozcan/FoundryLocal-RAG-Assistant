Title: Three Ages (1923)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Edward F. Cline
Main Cast: Buster Keaton, Margaret Leahy, Wallace Beery, Joe Roberts, Lillian Lawrence
Keywords: silent film

Overview: "The Three Ages," Buster Keaton's first feature-length film after a number of comedy shorts, is his parody of Griffith's "Intolerance." Keaton tells three parallel stories about the perils of romance, one set in the Stone Age, one during the Roman Empire, and one during the 20th century.
