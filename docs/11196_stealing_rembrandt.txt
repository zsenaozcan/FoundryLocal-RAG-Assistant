Title: Stealing Rembrandt (2003)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Jannik Johansen
Main Cast: Lars Brygmann, Jakob Cedergren, Nikolaj Coster-Waldau, Nicolas Bro, Sonja Richter
Keywords: father son relationship, robbery, museum, prosecution, painting

Overview: Two bumbling scrap metal thieves - father and son - steal the wrong painting during a museum heist. The painting turns out to be the only original Rembrandt painting in Denmark, and all hell breaks loose. What do you do when you've got Interpol, the Danish police and the entire Danish underworld on your heels? And who was this Rembrandt guy anyway?
