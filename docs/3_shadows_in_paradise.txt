Title: Shadows in Paradise (1986)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Aki Kaurismäki
Main Cast: Matti Pellonpää, Kati Outinen, Sakari Kuosmanen, Esko Nikkari, Kylli Köngäs
Keywords: salesclerk, helsinki, garbage, independent film
