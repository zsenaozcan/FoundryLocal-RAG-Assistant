Title: The Girl in the Café (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance, TV Movie
Director: David Yates
Main Cast: Bill Nighy, Kelly Macdonald, Meneka Das, Anton Lesser, Paul Ritter

Overview: Lawrence, an aging, lonely civil servant falls for Gina, an enigmatic young woman. When he takes her to the G8 Summit in Reykjavik, however, their bond is tested by Lawrence's professional obligations.
