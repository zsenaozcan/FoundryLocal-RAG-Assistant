Title: Miss Marple: The Moving Finger (1985)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Crime
Director: Roy Boulting
Main Cast: Joan Hickson, Michael Culver, Elizabeth Counsell, Deborah Appleby, Lucy Gleeson
Keywords: detective, based on novel

Overview: The residents of a quiet English village begin to receive nasty, threatening letters. The wife of the local vicar calls in her friend Miss Marple to investigate.
