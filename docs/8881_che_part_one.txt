Title: Che: Part One (2008)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Steven Soderbergh
Main Cast: Benicio del Toro, Demián Bichir, Rodrigo Santoro, Santiago Cabrera, Julia Ormond
Keywords: che guevara

Overview: The Argentine, begins as Che and a band of Cuban exiles (led by Fidel Castro) reach the Cuban shore from Mexico in 1956. Within two years, they mobilized popular support and an army and toppled the U.S.-friendly regime of dictator Fulgencio Batista.
