Title: Veronika Decides to Die (2009)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Emily Young
Main Cast: Sarah Michelle Gellar, David Thewlis, Jonathan Tucker, Erika Christensen, Melissa Leo
Keywords: new york, sex, asylum, controversial, independent film, psychiatrist, treatment, woman director, mental

Overview: After a frantic suicide attempt, Veronika awakens inside a mysterious mental asylum. Under the supervision of an unorthodox psychiatrist who specializes in controversial treatment, Veronika learns that she has only weeks to live.
