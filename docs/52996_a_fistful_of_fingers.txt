Title: A Fistful of Fingers (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Western
Director: Edgar Wright
Main Cast: Graham Low, Oli van der Vijver, Nicola Stapleton, Martin Curtis, Jeremy Beadle
Keywords: cowboy, western spoof

Overview: Follow the exploits of taciturn hero No Name and his stereotypical Indian side-kick Running Sore as they search for the nefarious villain The Squint.
