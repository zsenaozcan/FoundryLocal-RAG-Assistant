Title: Caliber 9 (1972)
Genres: Action, Crime
Director: Fernando Di Leo
Main Cast: Gastone Moschin, Barbara Bouchet, Mario Adorf, Frank Wolff, Luigi Pistilli
Keywords: blackmail, police, organized crime, gunfight, gangster, brutality

Overview: Just out of prison, ex-con Ugo Piazza meets his former employer, a psychopathic gangster Rocco who enjoys sick violence and torture. Both the gangsters and the police believe Ugo has hidden $300,000 that should have gone to an American drug syndicate boss.
