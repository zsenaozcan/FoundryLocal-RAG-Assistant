Title: Living Death (2006)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Erin Berry
Main Cast: Kristy Swanson, Joshua Peace, Kelsey Matheson, Neil Foster, Marc Hickox

Overview: Torture is the ultimate seduction when Victor, a powerful and dangerous millionaire, discovers that his beautiful wife Elizabeth (Kristy Swanson) is having a steamy affair with his best friend. And when the lovers' risky plan to get rid of him backfires, Victor's relentless anger and terrifying rage sends him over the edge on a shockingly sadistic quest for total revenge.
