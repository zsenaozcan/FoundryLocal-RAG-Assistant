Title: The Legend of Hell House (1973)
Genres: Horror, Mystery
Director: John Hough
Main Cast: Pamela Franklin, Roddy McDowall, Clive Revill, Gayle Hunnicutt, Roland Culver
Keywords: isolation, medium, afterlife, physicist, haunting, psychic, paranormal investigation

Overview: A team consisting of a physicist, his wife, a young female psychic and the only survivor of the previous visit are sent to the notorious Hell House to prove/disprove survival after death. Previous visitors have either been killed or gone mad, and it is up to the team to survive a full week in isolation, and solve the mystery of the Hell House.
