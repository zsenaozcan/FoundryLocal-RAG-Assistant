Title: Crossing the Bridge: The Sound of Istanbul (2005)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Fatih Akin
Main Cast: Orhan Gencebay, Baba Zula

Overview: Award-winning director Fatih Akin takes us on a journey through Istanbul, the city that bridges Europe and Asia, and challenges familiar notions of east and west. He looks at the vibrant musical scene which includes traditional Turkish music plus rock and hip-hop.
