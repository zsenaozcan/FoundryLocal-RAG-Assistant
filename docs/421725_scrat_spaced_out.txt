Title: Scrat: Spaced Out (2016)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy, Animation
Director: Galen T. Chu
Main Cast: Neil deGrasse Tyson, Chris Wedge, Karen Disher

Overview: In the main events of Ice Age: Collision Course, Scrat is captured by Scratazons who take away his acorn. Now, Scrat must rescue his Acorn and escape before the aliens can catch him.
