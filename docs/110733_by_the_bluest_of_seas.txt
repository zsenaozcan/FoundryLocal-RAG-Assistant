Title: By the Bluest of Seas (1936)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Music
Director: Boris Barnet
Main Cast: Alexei Dolinin, Nikolay Kryuchkov, Yelena Kuzmina, Lyalya Sateyeva, Semyon Svashenko

Overview: Yussuf and Aliosha are two shipwrecked sailor on an island in the Caspian Sea. They start working has sailor and mechanic for the fishboats of the "Lights of the Communism" kolkhoz. Here the two friends will fall in love for the beautiful Misha.
