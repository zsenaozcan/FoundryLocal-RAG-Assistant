Title: The Arrow (1997)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Don McBrearty
Main Cast: Sara Botsford, Aidan Devine, Nigel Bennett, Jonathan Whittaker, Ian D. Clark
Keywords: history, aviation, airplane manufacturer, avro arrow

Overview: This series tells the story of the world's fastest fighter plane ever built, in 1950's Canada, and how the project was dropped due to political pressure from the United States.
