Title: Sword of the Stranger (2007)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Animation
Director: Masahiro Andô
Main Cast: Tomoya Nagase, Yuri Chinen, Kouichi Yamadera, Naoto Takenaka, Akio Ohtsuka
Keywords: swordsman, espionage, dog

Overview: Hunted by the Ming from China, young Kotaro and his dog meet a nameless samurai "Nanashi" who is haunted constantly by memories of his past which has led him to avoid drawing his sword ever again. Among the Ming is a fearsome Western fighter named Raro, whose only desire is to find a worthy opponent.
