Title: Two Men in Town (2014)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Rachid Bouchareb
Main Cast: Forest Whitaker, Harvey Keitel, Brenda Blethyn, Luis Guzmán, Dolores Heredia
Keywords: sheriff, revenge, ex-con

Overview: A convict is released from prison in a small town in New Mexico. 18 years ago he killed a deputy. The then-and-now sheriff seeks revenge.
