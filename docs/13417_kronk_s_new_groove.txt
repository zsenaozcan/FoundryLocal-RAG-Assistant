Title: Kronk's New Groove (2005)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Family
Director: Saul Blinkoff
Main Cast: Patrick Warburton, Tracey Ullman, Eartha Kitt, John Goodman, Wendie Malick
Keywords: competition, friendship, sequel, kids and family

Overview: Kronk, now chef and Head Delivery Boy of Mudka's Meat Hut, is fretting over the upcoming visit of his father. Kronk's father always disapproved of young Kronk's culinary interests and wished that Kronk instead would settle down with a wife and a large house on a hill.
