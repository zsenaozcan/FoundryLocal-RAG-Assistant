Title: Russia 88 (2009)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Pavel Bardin
Main Cast: Pyotr Fyodorov, Michail Poljakow, Wera Sergejewna Strokowa, Alexander Prokofjewitsch Makarow, Artschibald Artschibaldowitsch

Overview: A gang of skinheads 'Russia 88' are filming propaganda videos in order to place it on the internet. At the same time the camera records the life of the gang, they become accustomed to this and stop paying attention to it. The leader of the gang 'Blade' discovers that his sister is dating a guy from the Caucausus. This family drama develops into a tragedy.
