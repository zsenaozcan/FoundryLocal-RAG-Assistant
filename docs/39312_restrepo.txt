Title: Restrepo (2010)
Genres: Documentary, War
Director: Sebastian Junger
Main Cast: Juan "Doc" Restrepo, Dan Kearney, LaMonta Caldwell, Aron Hijar
Keywords: guitar, radio, afghanistan, cow, army, grief, independent film, dead soldier, explosion, duringcreditsstinger

Overview: Winner of the 2010 Sundance Film Festival Grand Jury Prize for a Documentary, Restrepo chronicles the deployment of a U.S. platoon of courageous American soldiers in Afghanistan's Korengal Valley, considered to be one of the most dangerous postings in the U.S. military.
