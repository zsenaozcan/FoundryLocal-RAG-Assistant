Title: The Butterfly Effect 2 (2006)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: John R. Leonetti
Main Cast: Eric Lively, Erica Durance, Dustin Milligan, Gina Holden, David Lewis
Keywords: suicide, brain tumor, underwear, time travel

Overview: After his girlfriend, Julie and two best friends are killed in a tragic auto accident, Nick struggles to cope with his loss and grief. Suffering from migraine-like seizures, Nick soon discovers that he has the power to change the past via his memories. However, his time-traveling attempts to alter the past and save his one true love have unexpected and dire consequences.
