Title: El Gringo (2012)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action, Thriller
Director: Eduardo Rodríguez
Main Cast: Scott Adkins, Yvette Yates, Christian Slater, Israel Islas, Erando González
Keywords: small town, outlaw, gunfight, violence, cowboy, neo-western, neo-noir

Overview: A man crossing into Mexico with a satchel of $2,000,000 – and a bloody past – suddenly finds himself under attack in the sleepy town of El Fronteras.
