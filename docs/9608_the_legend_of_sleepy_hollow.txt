Title: The Legend of Sleepy Hollow (1999)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Horror, Thriller
Director: Pierre Gang
Main Cast: Brent Carver, Rachelle Lefevre, Vlasta Vrána, Kathleen Fee, Paul Lemelin
Keywords: legend, leave, village, greed, marriage, suspense, falling in love, headless horseman

Overview: Ichabod Crane, a Yankee wanderer, arrives in Sleepy Hollow and becomes the new schoolmaster. He meets Katrina Van Tassel, and blissfully fantasizes about how can marry her, ultimately, inherit her father's rich estate.
