Title: Joy House (1964)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Crime
Director: René Clément
Main Cast: Alain Delon, Jane Fonda, Lola Albright, Sorrell Booke, Carl Studer
Keywords: french riviera

Overview: A small-time con man on the run from the gangster husband of his girlfriend hides out in a strange, brooding mansion run by two mysterious women. There he finds himself trapped in deception between the two women. Shot on location in the French Riviera.
