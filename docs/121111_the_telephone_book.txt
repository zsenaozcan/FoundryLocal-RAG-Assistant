Title: The Telephone Book (1971)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Drama
Director: David Weisman
Main Cast: Sarah Kennedy, Norman Rose, Barry Morse, Jill Clayburgh, Ultra Violet
Keywords: sex, surreal, satire

Overview: The story of a day in the life of a lonely, sensitive, exhuberent, attractive, young woman. Her exploits, encounters, and frustrations as she attempts to find a "special" someone, a caller who has "class", as she puts it.
