Title: Company Man (2000)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Douglas McGrath
Main Cast: Alan Cumming, Anthony LaPaglia, Denis Leary, Douglas McGrath, John Turturro
Keywords: duringcreditsstinger

Overview: Set in the 1960's, a school teacher pretends to be a CIA spy to get his nagging wife off his back. He helps a Russian ballet dancer defect and is then sent to Cuba to locate "Agent X" for the CIA.
