Title: Sex in Chains (1928)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: William Dieterle
Main Cast: William Dieterle, Gunnar Tolnæs, Mary Johnson, Paul Henckels, Hans Heinrich von Twardowski
Keywords: prison, lgbt

Overview: A young man is convicted of manslaughter and sentenced to a term in prison. There he forms a close relationship with his cellmate and upon his release his wife is concerned as to how prison has changed the man she married.
