Title: A Wedding Suit (1976)
Genres: Drama
Director: Abbas Kiarostami
Main Cast: Mohammad Fassih, Mehdi Nekoueï, Massoud Zand, Reza Hashemi, Hashem Arkan
Keywords: karate, tailor, suit

Overview: A woman orders a suit from a tailor for her young son to wear to her sister's wedding. The tailor's apprentice, together with two other teenage boys who work in the same building, devise a plan to try on the suit at night to see what it feels like.
