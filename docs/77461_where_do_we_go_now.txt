Title: Where Do We Go Now? (2011)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Nadine Labaki
Main Cast: Claude Msawbaa, Leyla Hakim, Nadine Labaki, Yvonne Maalouf, Antoinette Noufaily
Keywords: religious conflict, hijab, religious differences, virgin mary statue, woman director

Overview: On a remote, isolated, unnamed Lebanese village inhabited by both Muslims and Christians. The village is surrounded by land mines and only reachable by a small bridge. As civil strife engulfed the country, the women in the village learn of this fact and try, by various means and to varying success, to keep their men in the dark, sabotaging the village radio, then destroying the village TV.
