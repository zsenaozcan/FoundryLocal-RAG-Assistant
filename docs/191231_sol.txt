Title: Sol (2012)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Benjamin Carland
Main Cast: Jake White, Jake Brown, Caleb Courtney, Sky King, Aaron Kuban
Keywords: stranded, alien planet, alien world

Overview: A group of students are sent to a faraway planet to participate in an annual competition between schools.  Unfortunately, once there they discover that things have gone awry.  Most of them don't survive the transport, and those that do find themselves stranded on a hostile alien world.
