Title: Piranha Sharks (2015)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Leigh Scott
Main Cast: Collin Galyean, John Wells, Josh Hammond, Ramona Mallory, Frederic Doss
Keywords: creature

Overview: Great white sharks bio-engineered to be the size of piranhas with the purpose of living in rich peoples exotic aquariums, terrorize New York City when they get into the water supply and do what great white sharks do best.
