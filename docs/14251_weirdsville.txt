Title: Weirdsville (2007)
Genres: Drama, Action, Comedy
Director: Allan Moyle
Main Cast: Scott Speedman, Taryn Manning, Wes Bentley, Matt Frewer, Greg Bryk
Keywords: satanism, morphine, drive-in theatre

Overview: A pair of slackers get in way over their heads when they try to dump the body of a dead girlfriend in the basement of a drive-in movie theater where a satanic cult performs ritual sacrifices.
