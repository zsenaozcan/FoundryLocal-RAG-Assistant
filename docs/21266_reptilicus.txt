Title: Reptilicus (1961)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Poul Bang
Main Cast: Asbjørn Andersen, Ann Smyrner, Mimi Heinrich, Carl Ottosen, Bent Mejding
Keywords: denmark, monster, professor, lapland, bridge, panic, giant monster, scientist, man eaten by monster, man eating monster

Overview: A portion of the tail of a prehistoric reptile is discovered in Denmark. It regenerates into the entire reptile, which proceeds to destroy buildings and property and generally make a nuisance of itself. It can fly, swim, and walk, and has impenetrable scales, which makes it difficult to kill.
