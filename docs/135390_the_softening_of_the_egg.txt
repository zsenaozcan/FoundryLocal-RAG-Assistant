Title: The Softening of the Egg (1975)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Hans Alfredson
Main Cast: Gösta Ekman, Max von Sydow, Birgitta Andersson, Hans Alfredson, Börje Ahlstedt
Keywords: oedipus complex

Overview: A fairy tale and satire about a wealthy industrialist, whose son has been subdued and oppressed and totally uninterested in his father's business.
