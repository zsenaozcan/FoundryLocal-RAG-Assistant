Title: Night Nurses (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Aleksei Muradov
Main Cast: Aleksey Makarov, Olga Lomonosova, Aleksey Kortnev, Angelina Mirimskaya, Maksim Konovalov

Overview: After an accident, an unconscious and charming millionaire from Moscow lands in a provincial boarding house where he is surrounded with loving care by four sexy nurses.
