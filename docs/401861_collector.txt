Title: Collector (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Aleksey Krasovskiy
Main Cast: Konstantin Khabenskiy, Evgeniy Stychkin, Kseniya Buravskaya, Polina Agureyeva, Kirill Pletnev

Overview: An unscrupulous debt collector who is a prominent member of the Moscow elite has just a few hours to refute an accusation that might cost him his job and his life.
