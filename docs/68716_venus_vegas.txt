Title: Venus & Vegas (2010)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Demian Lichtenstein
Main Cast: Eddie Kaye Thomas, Donald Faison, Eddie Guerra, Jaime Pressly, Abraham Benrubi
Keywords: casino, heist, las vegas
