Title: Everything Relative (1996)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sharon Pollack
Main Cast: Monica Bell, Carol Schneider, Ellen McLaughlin, Gabriella Messina, Olivia Negron
Keywords: lesbian, female homosexuality, class reunion, college reunion

Overview: Old college chums get together for a weekend reunion that is bound to open old wounds and perhaps heal them. New romances find a spark while old ones rekindle.
