Title: The Diamond Arm (1968)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Crime
Director: Leonid Gayday
Main Cast: Yuriy Nikulin, Andrey Mironov, Anatoli Papanov, Nina Grebeshkova, Nonna Mordyukova

Overview: Semyon Gorbunkov goes on a cruise with a smuggler on board. In Istanbul, he slips and breaks his arm. What he didn't know is that this was a signal for the gang of smugglers.
