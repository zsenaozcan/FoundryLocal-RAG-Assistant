Title: The Cutlass (1973)
Genres: Adventure, Family
Director: Nikolai Kalinin
Main Cast: Sergei Shevkunenko, Vladimir Dichkovsky, Emmanuil Vitorgan, Viktor Sergachyov, Zoya Fyodorova

Overview: A Red Army officer Polevoy possesses a navy dirk with a secret message encrypted in its handle. The second part of a message belongs to a White army officer Nikitskiy. It's up to three young friends of Polevoy to uncover the mystery.
