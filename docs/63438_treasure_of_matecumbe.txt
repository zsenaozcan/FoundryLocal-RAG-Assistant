Title: Treasure of Matecumbe (1976)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Vincent McEveety
Main Cast: Robert Foxworth, Joan Hackett, Peter Ustinov, Vic Morrow, Johnny Doran
Keywords: florida, treasure, buried treasure

Overview: In post-Civil War Kentucky, young David Burnic becomes the unexpected heir to the family secret, a map leading to buried treasure on the Florida isle of Matecumbe.
