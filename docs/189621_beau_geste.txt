Title: Beau Geste (1926)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Herbert Brenon
Main Cast: Ronald Colman, Neil Hamilton, Ralph Forbes, Alice Joyce, Mary Brian

Overview: Michael "Beau" Geste leaves England in disgrace and joins the infamous French Foreign Legion. He is reunited with his two brothers in North Africa, where they face greater danger from their own sadistic commander than from the rebellious Arabs.
