Title: Stage Struck (1958)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Sidney Lumet
Main Cast: Henry Fonda, Susan Strasberg, Christopher Plummer, Joan Greenwood, Herbert Marshall
Keywords: theater

Overview: A small-town actress (Susan Strasberg) meets a New York producer (Henry Fonda) and playwright, and replaces their show's moody star (Joan Greenwood).  This film marked Christopher Plummer's film debut, and was a remake of the 1933 “Morning Glory” with Katherine Hepburn. Douglas Fairbanks, Jr., and Adolphe Menjou in the corresponding three lead roles.
