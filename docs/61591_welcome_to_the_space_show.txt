Title: Welcome to the Space Show (2010)
Genres: Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Masaaki Yuasa
Main Cast: Yoshinaga Takuto, Tomoyo Kurosawa, Tamaki Matsumoto, Keiji Fujiwara, Honoka Ikezuki
Keywords: moon, space, alien

Overview: Five children save the life of a dog-like alien while at a self-run summer camp. He attempts to reward them by taking them to an alien colony on the Moon. Events take a turn for the worse when his report on that attack that injured him causes passage from the Moon to the Earth to be banned, and children are stranded in space. The children need to find a way back home before camp ends and their parents discover that they are missing. They also have to avoid the poachers that injured their alien friend, and now seem to be stalking them all.
