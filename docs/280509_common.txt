Title: Common (2014)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: David Blair
Main Cast: Nico Mirallegro, Susan Lynch, Daniel Mays, Jodhi May, Andrew Tiernan
Keywords: law, british, mystery

Overview: Multi-award-winning writer Jimmy McGovern's brand new film based on the UK's controversial joint enterprise law.  Jimmy McGovern's gritty drama stars Nico Mirallegro as guileless 17 year old Johnjo O'Shea, who goes from innocent bystander to accessory to premeditated murder after giving a few friends a lift.
