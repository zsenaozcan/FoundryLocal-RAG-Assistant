Title: C.H.O.M.P.S. (1979)
Genres: Family, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Don Chaffey
Main Cast: Valerie Bertinelli, Red Buttons, Conrad Bain, Wesley Eure, Jim Backus
Keywords: invention, robot, dog, strength, x-ray

Overview: A young man invents a robot dog that has super strength, x-ray vision and can detect crimes being committed. A greedy businessman tries to steal the boy's invention from him.
