Title: Teenagers from Outer Space (1959)
Genres: Crime, Horror, Science Fiction, Romance, Thriller
Director: Tom Graeff
Main Cast: David Love, Bryan Grant, Harvey B. Dunn, Tom Graeff, King Moody
Keywords: flying saucer, teenager, ray gun

Overview: A young alien falls for a pretty teenage Earth girl and they team up to try to stop the plans of his invading cohorts, who intend to use Earth as a food-breeding ground for giant lobsters from their planet.
