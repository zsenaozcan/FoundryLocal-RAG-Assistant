Title: Bonnie Scotland (1935)
Genres: Comedy
Director: James W. Horne
Main Cast: Stan Laurel, Oliver Hardy, June Lang, William Janney, Anne Grey
Keywords: love triangle, scotland, accordion, disappointment, marching band, india, heiress, lodging house, laurel and hardy, marching

Overview: Stan and Ollie stow away to Scotland expecting to inherit the MacLaurel estate. When things don't quite turn out that way, they unwittingly enlist in the Scottish army and are posted to India.
