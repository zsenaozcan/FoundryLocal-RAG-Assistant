Title: The Alamo: Thirteen Days to Glory (1987)
Genres: TV Movie, Western
Director: Burt Kennedy
Main Cast: James Arness, Brian Keith, Alec Baldwin, David Ogden Stiers, Jim Metzler

Overview: Against orders and with no help of relief Texas patriots led by William Travis, Jim Bowie, and Davy Crockett defend the Alamo against overwhelming Mexican forces.
