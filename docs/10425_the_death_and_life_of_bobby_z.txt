Title: The Death and Life of Bobby Z (2007)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: John Herzfeld
Main Cast: Paul Walker, Laurence Fishburne, Olivia Wilde, Jason Lewis, Michael Bowen
Keywords: vineyard, rifle, drug trade, violence, shot in the stomach, double cross, estate, face slap, tdrug lord, faked death

Overview: A DEA agent provides former Marine Tim Kearney with a way out of his prison sentence: impersonate Bobby Z, a recently deceased drug dealer, in a hostage switch with a crime lord. When the negotiations go awry, Kearney flees, with Z's son in tow.
