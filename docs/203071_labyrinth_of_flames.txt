Title: Labyrinth of Flames (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Action
Director: Katsuhiko Nishijima
Main Cast: Susumu Chiba, Yukari Tamura, Kōichi Tōchika, Yuki Masuda
Keywords: sex, animation, women fight, ecchi

Overview: When Galan's princess girlfriend gives him the gift of an ancient sword, strange events unfold, and even stranger people drop out of the sky to attack. Now Galan must overcome his ineptitude and join a bunch of beautiful women in a wacky romp through a kingdom that time forgot.
