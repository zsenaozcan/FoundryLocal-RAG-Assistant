Title: Montana (1998)
Genres: Drama, Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Jennifer Leitzes
Main Cast: Kyra Sedgwick, Stanley Tucci, Robin Tunney, John Ritter, Philip Seymour Hoffman
Keywords: boss, murder, cancer, shootout, mistress, woman director

Overview: A seasoned enforcer (Kyra Sedgwick) is given the seemingly routine task of finding the Boss's runaway mistress, Kitty. However Kitty is involved in plans to overthrow the Boss and this routine task gets very complicated.
