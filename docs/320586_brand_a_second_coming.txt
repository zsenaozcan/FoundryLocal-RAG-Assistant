Title: Brand: A Second Coming (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ondi Timoner
Main Cast: Russell Brand, Katy Perry, David Lynch, Stephen Merchant, Oliver Stone
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Comic Russell Brand uses drugs, sex and fame in a quest for happiness, only to find it remains elusive. As he explores iconic figures such as Gandhi, Malcolm X, Che Guevara, and Jesus, he transforms himself into a political antagonist.
