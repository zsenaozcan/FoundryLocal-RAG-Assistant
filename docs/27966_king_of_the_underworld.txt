Title: King of the Underworld (1939)
Genres: Romance, Crime, Drama
Director: Lewis Seiler
Main Cast: Humphrey Bogart, Kay Francis, James Stephenson, John Eldredge, Jessie Busley
Keywords: underworld, on the run

Overview: Niles and Carol Niles are married doctors who save one of ganglord Gurney's men. Gurney exploits them, then kills Niles leaving Carol to take the fall. He next kidnaps writer Bill Forrest to write his biography. Knowing Bill will be killed when the biography is finished, Carol takes matters into her own hands.
