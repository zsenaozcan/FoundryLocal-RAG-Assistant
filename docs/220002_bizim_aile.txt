Title: Bizim Aile (1975)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Drama
Director: Engin Orbey
Main Cast: Münir Özkul, Adile Naşit, Tarık Akan, Şener Şen, Halit Akçatepe
Keywords: big family, family relationships

Overview: A love story between a poor man and a rich girl. The rich girl's father tries to prevent their marriage and he declares a war against the poor boy's family.
