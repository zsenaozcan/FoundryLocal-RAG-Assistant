Title: Enchantment (1921)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Robert G. Vignola
Main Cast: Marion Davies, Edith Shayne, Tom Lewis, Arthur Rankin, Corinne Barker
Keywords: silent film, flapper

Overview: The frothy experiences of a vain little flapper. Her father induces an actor friend to become a gentlemanly cave man and the film becomes another variation of the 'Taming of the Shrew' theme.
