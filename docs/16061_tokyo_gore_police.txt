Title: Tokyo Gore Police (2008)
Genres: Adventure, Horror, Action, Science Fiction
Director: Yoshihiro Nishimura
Main Cast: Eihi Shiina, Itsuji Itao, Yukihide Benny, Jiji Bû, Ikuko Sawada
Keywords: mutant, police chief, engineer, blood splatter, remake, revenge, engineering, head wound, japanese cyberpunk, crooked cops

Overview: Set in a future-world vision of Tokyo where the police have been privatized and bitter self-mutilation is so casual that advertising is often specially geared to the "cutter" demographic, this is the story of samurai-sword-wielding Ruka and her mission to avenge her father's assassination. Ruka is a cop from a squad who's mission is to destroy homicidal mutant humans known as "engineers" possessing the ability to transform any injury to a weapon.
