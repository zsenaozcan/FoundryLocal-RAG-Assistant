Title: Perry Mason: The Case of the Avenging Ace (1988)
Genres: Mystery
Director: Christian I. Nyby II
Main Cast: Raymond Burr, Barbara Hale, William Katt, Patty Duke, Erin Gray
Keywords: murder, crime, courtroom

Overview: Mason defends a man he had sentenced to prison when he was an appellate-court judge. Now, 18 months after the sentencing, a new witness turns up to prove the man's innocence. Mason steps in to defend the man.
