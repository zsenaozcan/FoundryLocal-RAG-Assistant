Title: Vision – From the Life of Hildegard von Bingen (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Margarethe von Trotta
Main Cast: Barbara Sukowa, Heino Ferch, Hannah Herzsprung, Devid Striesow, Sunnyi Melles
Keywords: independent film, woman director

Overview: Hildegard von Bingen was truly a woman ahead of her time. A visionary in every sense of the word, this famed 12th-century Benedictine nun was a Christian mystic, composer, philosopher, playwright, poet, naturalist, scientist, physician, herbalist and ecological activist.
