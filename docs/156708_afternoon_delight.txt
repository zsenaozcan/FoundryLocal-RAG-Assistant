Title: Afternoon Delight (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jill Soloway
Main Cast: Kathryn Hahn, Juno Temple, Josh Radnor, Jane Lynch, Annie Mumolo
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Rachel is a quick-witted and lovable stay-at-home mom. Frustrated with the realities of preschool auctions, a lackluster sex life and career that's gone kaput, Rachel visits a strip club to spice up her marriage and meets McKenna, a stripper she adopts as her live-in nanny.
