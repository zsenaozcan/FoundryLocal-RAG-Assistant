Title: Down in the Delta (1998)
Genres: Drama
Director: Maya Angelou
Main Cast: Alfre Woodard, Al Freeman, Jr., Esther Rolle, Mary Alice, Loretta Devine
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Rosa Lynn sends her druggie daughter Loretta and her children Thomas and Tracy away from the big city to live with their uncle Earl in the ancestral home in rural Mississippi. Earl puts Loretta to work in his restaurant, Just Chicken, while also telling them about the generations of their family, the Sinclairs, dating back to their time in slavery before the the Civil War.
