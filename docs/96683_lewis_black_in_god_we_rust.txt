Title: Lewis Black: In God We Rust (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Adam Dubin
Main Cast: Lewis Black

Overview: The Emmy nominated, Grammy-winning Lewis Black hits the historic State Theatre stage in Minneapolis for a rant-filled, cathartic ride through through the issues of our baffling world, from inept politicians and the shortcomings of technology to the absurdity of social media. No topic is left unexplored in this sold out performance by the bestselling author, actor, playwright and The Daily Show contributor.
