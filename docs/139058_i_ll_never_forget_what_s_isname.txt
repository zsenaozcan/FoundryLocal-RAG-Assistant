Title: I'll Never Forget What's'isname (1967)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Michael Winner
Main Cast: Orson Welles, Oliver Reed, Carol White, Harry Andrews, Michael Hordern
Keywords: advertising

Overview: Advertising golden boy Andrew Quint is fed up with his fabulously successful life. In very dramatic fashion, he quits his job to return to writing for a small literary magazine. He wants to leave his former life behind, going as far as saying good-bye to his wife and mistresses. He finds, however, that it's not so easy to escape the past.
