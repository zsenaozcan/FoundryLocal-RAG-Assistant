Title: Sharkwater (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Rob Stewart
Main Cast: Patrick Moore, Erich Ritter, Paul Watson, Rob Stewart, Boris Worm
Keywords: shark

Overview: Driven by passion fed from a life-long fascination with sharks, Stewart Stewart debunks historical stereotypes and media depictions of sharks as bloodthirsty, man-eating monsters and reveals the reality of sharks as pillars in the evolution of the seas.
