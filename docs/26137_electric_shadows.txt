Title: Electric Shadows (2004)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Jiang Xiao
Main Cast: Xia Yu, Haibin Li, Zhengjia Wang, Haogi Zhang
Keywords: china, woman director

Overview: For no apparent reason, a mute young woman assaults a youth who delivers water on his bicycle, injuring him and ruining his bike. Surprisingly, she asks him to feed her fish while she is in custody. Her tiny apartment, he discovers, is a shrine to his favorite escape, the movies.
