Title: Chicago Cab (1997)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: John Tintori
Main Cast: Paul Dillon, Tim Gamble, Olivia Trevino, Moira Sinise, Rana Khan
Keywords: chicago, taxi driver, cab driver

Overview: A day in the life of a Chicago cab driver is examined as he picks up fares from the good and bad parts of the city and emotionally connects to many of his passengers.
