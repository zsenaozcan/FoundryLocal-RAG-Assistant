Title: Hideous Kinky (1998)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Gillies MacKinnon
Main Cast: Kate Winslet, Saïd Taghmaoui, Bella Riza, Carrie Mullan, Pierre Clémenti
Keywords: hippie, culture clash, morocco, arabian, lover, sister, independent film
