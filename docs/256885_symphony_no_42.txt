Title: Symphony No. 42 (2014)
Genres: Mystery, Animation
Director: Réka Bucsi
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: (animated short) The film applies an unconventional narrative. It presents a subjective world through 47 scenes. The small events, interlaced by associations, express the irrational coherence of our surroundings. The surreal situations are based on the interactions of humans and nature.
