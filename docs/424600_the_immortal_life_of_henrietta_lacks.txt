Title: The Immortal Life of Henrietta Lacks (2017)
Genres: Drama
Director: George C. Wolfe
Main Cast: Rose Byrne, Renée Elise Goldsberry, Oprah Winfrey, Ninja N. Devoe, Lisa Arrindell
Keywords: medical research, medical drama

Overview: An African-American woman becomes an unwitting pioneer for medical breakthroughs when her cells are used to create the first immortal human cell line in the early 1950s.
