Title: Elevator Movie (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Zeb Haradon
Main Cast: Zeb Haradon, Robin Ballard, Alissa Hooper, Andrew Hibbard
Keywords: trapped in an elevator

Overview: Jim is a shy, withdrawn loner, pervert, and virgin. Lana is a friendly and outgoing born again Christian who has renounced her promiscuous drug filled past. They have never met until they step into an elevator together one day. The elevator gets stuck between floors, and they start to get to know each other...
