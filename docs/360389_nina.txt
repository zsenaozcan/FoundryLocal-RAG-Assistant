Title: Nina (2016)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Cynthia Mort
Main Cast: Zoe Saldana, David Oyelowo, Mike Epps, Ella Thomas, Ronald Guttman
Keywords: jazz, biography, based on true story, woman director

Overview: The story of the late jazz musician and classical pianist Nina Simone including her rise to fame and relationship with her manager Clifton Henderson.
