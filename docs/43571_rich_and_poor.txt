Title: Rich and Poor (1983)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Pasquale Festa Campanile
Main Cast: Renato Pozzetto, Ornella Muti, Piero Mazzarella, Ugo Gregoretti, Giulio Massimini

Overview: Pozzetto is a rich man obsessed by the idea of becoming poor. So, his psychiatric suggests him to leave home and live just like a poor man for some days. He will then become familiar with this way of living, and fear less the idea of becoming poor. So he does, and his old family &amp; friends soon forget about him. They don't miss him at all.
