Title: The Pawnbroker (1965)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sidney Lumet
Main Cast: Rod Steiger, Geraldine Fitzgerald, Brock Peters, Jaime Sánchez, Thelma Oliver
Keywords: holocaust, jew persecution, pawnbroker

Overview: A Jewish pawnbroker, a victim of Nazi persecution, loses all faith in his fellow man until he realizes too late the tragedy of his actions.
