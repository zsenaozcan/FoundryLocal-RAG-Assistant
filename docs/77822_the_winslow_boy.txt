Title: The Winslow Boy (1948)
Genres: Drama
Director: Anthony Asquith
Main Cast: Robert Donat, Cedric Hardwicke, Basil Radford, Margaret Leighton, Kathleen Harrison

Overview: In pre-WW1 England, a youngster is expelled from a naval academy over a petty theft, but his parents raise a political furor by demanding a trial.
