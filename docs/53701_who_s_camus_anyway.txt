Title: Who's Camus Anyway? (2005)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Mitsuo Yanagimachi
Main Cast: Ai Maeda, Meisa Kuroki, Hinano Yoshikawa, Shûji Kashiwabara, Tetsuji Tamayama

Overview: A group of eccentric students decide to make a movie. But, when their star suddenly quits, this witty ensemble cast begins to live the film, including murder, deception and true love.
