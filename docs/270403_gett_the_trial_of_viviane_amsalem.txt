Title: Gett: The Trial of Viviane Amsalem (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ronit Elkabetz
Main Cast: Ronit Elkabetz, Simon Abkarian, Menashe Noy, Gabi Amrani, Dalia Beger
Keywords: israel, judge, divorce, woman director

Overview: The trial story of Viviane Amsalem's five year fight to obtain her divorce in front of the only legal authority competent for divorce cases in Israel, the Rabbinical Court.
