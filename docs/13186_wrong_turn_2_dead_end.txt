Title: Wrong Turn 2: Dead End (2007)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Joe Lynch
Main Cast: Erica Leerhsen, Henry Rollins, Texas Battle, Daniella Alonso, Steve Braun
Keywords: west virginia, woods, reality show, cannibal

Overview: Retired military commander Colonel Dale Murphy hosts the simulated post-apocalyptic reality show where participants are challenged to survive a remote West Virginia wasteland. But the show turns into a nightmarish showdown when each realizes they are being hunted by an inbred family of cannibals determined to make them all dinner!
