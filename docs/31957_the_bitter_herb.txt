Title: The bitter herb (1985)
Genres: History
Director: Kees Van Oostrum
Main Cast: Ester Spitz, Gerard Thoolen, Kitty Courbois, Frank Rigter, Marion Van Wijk
Keywords: world war ii

Overview: This film is about the Jewish girl Sara and the ruin of her family during World War II. Her parents have to live in the Jewish ghetto in Amsterdam, but later they are arrested and deported. Sara decides to flee with her brother and his girlfriend to Palestine. Based on the book by Marga Minco.
