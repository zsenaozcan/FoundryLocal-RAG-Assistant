Title: Lassie (1994)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Family
Director: Daniel Petrie
Main Cast: Tom Guiry, Helen Slater, Jon Tenney, Brittany Boyd, Frederic Forrest
Keywords: lassie

Overview: An update of the Lassie legend stars Thomas Guiry as a troubled city kid whose family retreats to the country, where he befriends the famous collie and changes for the better. Conflict develops when a ruthless sheep rancher causes trouble for everyone.
