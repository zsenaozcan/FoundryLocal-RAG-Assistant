Title: Tommy's Honour (2017)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Jason Connery
Main Cast: Peter Mullan, Jack Lowden, Ophelia Lovibond, Sam Neill, Peter Ferdinando
Keywords: biography, sport

Overview: In every generation, a torch passes from father to son. And that timeless dynamic is the beating heart of Tommy's Honor - an intimate, powerfully moving tale of the real-life founders of the modern game of golf.
