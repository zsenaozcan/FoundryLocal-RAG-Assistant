Title: 1001 Grams (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bent Hamer
Main Cast: Ane Dahl Torp, Laurent Stocker, Magne-Håvard Brekke, Per Christian Ellefsen, Peter Hudson

Overview: When Norwegian scientist Marie attends a seminar in Paris on the actual weight of a kilo, it is her own measurement of disappointment, grief and, not least, love, that ends up on the scale. Finally Marie is forced to come to terms with how much a human life truly weighs and which measurements she intends to live by.
