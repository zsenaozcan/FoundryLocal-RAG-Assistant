Title: The Inhabitants (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Shawn Rasmussen
Main Cast: Elise Couture, Michael Reed, India Pearl, Edmund Donovan, Erica Derrickson
Keywords: haunted house, bed and breakfast place, new england, young couple
