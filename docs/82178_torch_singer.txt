Title: Torch Singer (1933)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Music
Director: Alexander Hall
Main Cast: Claudette Colbert, Ricardo Cortez, David Manners, Lyda Roberti, Charley Grapewin
Keywords: singer, illegitimate child

Overview: When she can't support her illegitimate child, an abandoned young woman puts her up for adoption and pursues a career as a torch singer.
