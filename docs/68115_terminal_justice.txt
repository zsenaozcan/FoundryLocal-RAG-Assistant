Title: Terminal Justice (1996)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Rick King
Main Cast: Lorenzo Lamas, Chris Sarandon, Peter Coyote, Kari Wuhrer, Barry Flatman
Keywords: nudity, virtual reality, sequel, cyberpunk

Overview: In 2008 cybersex can only be surpassed by one thing: Cloned women, especially created for their clients. This puts Pamela Travis, a famous and much sought-after cybersex star in great danger - Dr. Vivyan, scientist and genius in the field of biotechnology wants her DNA to clone her for his many customers. Sergeant Bobby Chase is assigned to protect her, but can't prevent her from being kidnapped. In order to free Pamela, Chase has to risk his life in a deadly virtual reality game called "Hellraiser"
