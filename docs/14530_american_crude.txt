Title: American Crude (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Craig Sheffer
Main Cast: Michael Clarke Duncan, Jennifer Esposito, Ron Livingston, Rob Schneider, Claudia Choi
Keywords: pornography, sex, american dream, stag night, party, intersexuality, independent film, pot smoking, los angeles, drug

Overview: Quite a bit can happen over the course of a single day in L.A., and when smooth-talking scam artist Johnny decides to throw a bachelor party for his best friend Bill the events of the day take a bizarre turn. As the festivities get underway, the appearance of a transvestite prostitute, a trigger-happy ex-con, and a notorious porn king signal that this isn't going to be a typical night out.
