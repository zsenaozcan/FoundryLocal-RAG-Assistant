Title: Hannie Caulder (1971)
Genres: Drama, Western, Crime

Main Cast: Raquel Welch, Robert Culp, Ernest Borgnine, Jack Elam, Strother Martin
Keywords: gunslinger, mexico, rape, bounty hunter, texas, bank robber, revenge, independent film, shootout, bank robbery
