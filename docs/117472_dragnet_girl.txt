Title: Dragnet Girl (1933)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Crime
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Main Cast: Kinuyo Tanaka, Joji Oka, Sumiko Mizukubo, Kôji Mitsui, Yumeko Aizome

Overview: Tokiko leads a double-life as an office typist and the mistress of a retired champion boxer and small-time ringleader named Jyoji. Hiroshi, a new recruit to the gang, hero worships Jyoji and neglects his studies. Hiroshi's sister Kazuko begs Jyoji to spare her brother from their shady dealings, but inadvertently casts a spell on Jyoji. After several reversals, Jyoji returns to Tokiko's arms. They decide to come clean, but not before pulling one last job to help Hiroshi and Kazuko.
