Title: Daylight (2013)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller
Director: Diederik Van Rooijen
Main Cast: Angela Schijf, Fedja van Huêt, Monique van de Ven, Derek de Lint, Maartje van de Wetering
Keywords: autism, imprisonment, murder, family

Overview: A successful lawyer finds out that she has a half-brother who is imprisoned in a secure hospital after he brutally murdered his neighbor and their baby.
