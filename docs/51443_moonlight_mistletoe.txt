Title: Moonlight & Mistletoe (2008)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Comedy, Romance, Family
Director: Karen Arthur
Main Cast: Tom Arnold, Candace Cameron Bure, Christopher Wiehl, Barbara Niven, Matt Walton
Keywords: santa claus, business, woman director

Overview: After three years, Nick's estranged daughter Holly returns home to find the spirit of Christmas in Santaville has dwindled, taking the family business with it. What starts out as an attempt to bail out her dad, ends up rekindling Holly's belief in Christmas all over again.
