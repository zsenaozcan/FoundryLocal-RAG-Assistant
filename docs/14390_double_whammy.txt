Title: Double Whammy (2001)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Tom DiCillo
Main Cast: Denis Leary, Elizabeth Hurley, Luis Guzmán, Victor Argo, Chris Noth
Keywords: sex, detective, insult, rivalry, independent film, violence, guilt, tabloid

Overview: Ray Pluto has many problems. He is satirized in the tabloids as the "loser cop." His partner is starting to seem suspiciously attracted to him. A pair of screenwriters across the hall keep bugging him for help. The superintendent of his building is stabbed by hoodlums hired by his own rebellious daughter. To top it off, a sexually aggressive chiropractor may just be Ray's undoing.
