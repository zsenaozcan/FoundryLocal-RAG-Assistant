Title: Sherlock Holmes: Incident at Victoria Falls (1992)
Genres: TV Movie, Adventure, Crime, Drama
Director: Bill Corcoran
Main Cast: Christopher Lee, Patrick Macnee, Jenny Seagrove, Joss Ackland, Richard Todd

Overview: King Edward ask Sherlock Holmes to perform one more task before his retirement: to safeguard the Star of Africa on a trip to Cape Town. Soon the fabled jewel is stolen and several people end up being murdered.
