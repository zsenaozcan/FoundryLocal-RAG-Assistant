Title: Behind the Planet of the Apes (1998)
Genres: Documentary, Science Fiction
Director: Kevin Burns
Main Cast: Roddy McDowall, Charlton Heston, Richard D. Zanuck, John Chambers, Kim Hunter
Keywords: interview, earth, ape man

Overview: This documentary was shown as part of the American Movie Classics (AMC) cable TV channel's celebration of the 30th anniversary of the release of Planet of the Apes (1968). We learn how the original French novel was transformed into the first film.
