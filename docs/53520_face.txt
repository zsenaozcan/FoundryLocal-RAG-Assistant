Title: Face (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Tsai Ming-liang
Main Cast: Lee Kang-Sheng, Lu Yi-Ching, Fanny Ardant, Jean-Pierre Léaud, Laetitia Casta
Keywords: paris, france, louvre, filmmaker, face
