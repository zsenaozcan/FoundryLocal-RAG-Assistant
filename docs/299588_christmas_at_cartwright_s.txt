Title: Christmas at Cartwright's (2014)
Genres: Romance, Family
Director: Graeme Campbell
Main Cast: Alicia Witt, Gabriel Hogan, Wallace Shawn, T.J. McGibbon, Linda Kash
Keywords: christmas

Overview: With Christmas approaching, a struggling single mom finds herself working as a department store Santa Claus, as a real-life angel delivers good fortune and the possibility of holiday romance.
