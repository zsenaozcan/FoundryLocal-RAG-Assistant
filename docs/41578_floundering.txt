Title: Floundering (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Peter McCarthy
Main Cast: James Le Gros, John Cusack, Ethan Hawke, Steve Buscemi, Billy Bob Thornton
Keywords: slacker, independent film, venice beach california, unemployed
