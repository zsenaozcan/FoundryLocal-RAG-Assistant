Title: Blood and Sand (1941)
Genres: Action, Drama, Romance
Director: Rouben Mamoulian
Main Cast: Tyrone Power, Linda Darnell, Rita Hayworth, Alla Nazimova, Anthony Quinn
Keywords: bullfight, bullfighter

Overview: Bullfighter Juan Gallardo falls for socialite Dona Sol, turning from the faithful Carmen who nevertheless stands by her man as he continues to face real danger in the bullring.
