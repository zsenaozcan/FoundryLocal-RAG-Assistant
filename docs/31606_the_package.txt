Title: The Package (1989)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Andrew Davis
Main Cast: Gene Hackman, Joanna Cassidy, Tommy Lee Jones, John Heard, Dennis Franz
Keywords: chicago, prisoner, hitman, suspense, sergeant

Overview: Experienced Green Beret sergeant Johnny Gallagher is escorting a prisoner, Airborne Ranger Thomas Boyette, back to the US, but Boyette escapes and Gallagher must risk life and limb to catch him.
