Title: The Last Blitzkrieg (1959)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Arthur Dreifuss
Main Cast: Van Johnson, Kerwin Mathews, Dick York, Larry Storch, Lise Bourdin

Overview: War - In the last days of WW2, a German soldier named Kroner, leads a band of saboteurs on a mission to infiltrate the Allied troops. When his true identity is revealed, Kroner must make a choice; stay true to the Third Reich or to break from all that he has known and lead a "last blitzkrieg". -  Van Johnson, Kerwin Mathews, Dick York
