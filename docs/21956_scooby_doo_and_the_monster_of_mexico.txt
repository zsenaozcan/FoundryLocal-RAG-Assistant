Title: Scooby-Doo! and the Monster of Mexico (2003)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Family, Mystery
Director: Scott Jeralds
Main Cast: Frank Welker, Nicole Jaffe, Heather North, Casey Kasem

Overview: A friend of Fred's, Alejo Otero invites the Scooby gang to Veracruz Mexico where they find the monster, El Chupacabra is terrorizing the town.
