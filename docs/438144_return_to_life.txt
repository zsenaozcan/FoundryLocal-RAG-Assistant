Title: Return to Life (1937)
Genres: War, Documentary
Director: Henri Cartier-Bresson
Main Cast: Pierre Unik
Keywords: spanish civil war

Overview: In this propaganda film intended to raise money for republicans fighting in the Spanish Civil War, Henri Cartier-Bresson first presents the achievements of the Spanish Republic in the field of public health. He then shows how members of the public and organizations across the world were supporting the fighters.
