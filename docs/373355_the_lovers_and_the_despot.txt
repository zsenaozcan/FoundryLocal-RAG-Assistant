Title: The Lovers and the Despot (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Robert Cannan
Main Cast: Paul Courtenay Hyu
Keywords: dictator, kidnapping, love

Overview: After the collapse of their glamorous romance, a famous director and actress are kidnapped by movie-obsessed dictator Kim Jong-il. Forced to make films in the world's weirdest state, they get a second chance at love, but only one chance at escape.
