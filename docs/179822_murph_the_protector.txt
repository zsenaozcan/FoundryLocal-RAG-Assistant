Title: MURPH: The Protector (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Scott Mactavish
Main Cast: John McElhone, Daniel Murphy, Jason Keenan, John Murphy

Overview: A documentary based on the honor, courage and commitment of Navy SEAL LT Michael P. Murphy, who gave his life for his men in 2005 and was posthumously awarded the Medal of Honor in 2007.
