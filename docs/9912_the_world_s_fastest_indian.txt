Title: The World's Fastest Indian (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Roger Donaldson
Main Cast: Anthony Hopkins, Iain Rea, Tessa Mitchell, Aaron Murphy, Tim Shadbolt
Keywords: new zealand, utah, life's dream, motor sport, independent film, motorcycle, trailer, mortgage

Overview: The life story of New Zealander Burt Munro, who spent years building a 1920 Indian motorcycle -- a bike which helped him set the land-speed world record at Utah's Bonneville Salt Flats in 1967.
