Title: The Sunchaser (1996)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Cimino
Main Cast: Woody Harrelson, Talisa Soto, Anne Bancroft, Jon Seda, Alexandra Tydings
Keywords: kidnapping, navajo, oncologist

Overview: A young half-Navajo convict (Jon Seda) dying of cancer forces a yuppie (Woody Harrelson) doctor to drive him to a magic healing lake.
