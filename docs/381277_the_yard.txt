Title: The Yard (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Måns Månsson
Main Cast: Anders Mossling, Hilal Shoman, Axel Roos, Robert Bengtsson, Alexandra Zetterberg

Overview: Based on Kristian Lundberg's novel about Anders, who goes from acclaimed author to physical worker at the bottom of society. He can no longer support himself through his cultural work and is forced to start anew, as a worker in Malmö harbour, at a place called 'The Yard'.
