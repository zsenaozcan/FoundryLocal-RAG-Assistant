Title: San Andreas Quake (2015)
Genres: TV Movie, Action, Science Fiction
Director: John Baumgartner
Main Cast: Jhey Castles, Jason Woods, Grace Van Dien, Elaine Partnow, Lane Townsend
Keywords: earthquake, los angeles, family, tv, seismologist

Overview: When a discredited L.A. Seismologist warns of an impending 12.7 earthquake, no one takes her seriously. Now on her own, she races desperately to get her family to safety before the earthquake breaks Los Angeles apart from the mainland.
