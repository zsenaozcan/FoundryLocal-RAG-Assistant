Title: The Texas Chainsaw Massacre (2003)
Genres: Horror
Director: Marcus Nispel
Main Cast: Jessica Biel, Jonathan Tucker, Erica Leerhsen, Mike Vogel, Eric Balfour
Keywords: trauma, psychopath, stalker, friends, murder, gore, blood, hitchhiker, chainsaw, torture

Overview: After picking up a traumatized young hitchhiker, five friends find themselves stalked and hunted by a deformed chainsaw-wielding killer and his family of equally psychopathic killers.
