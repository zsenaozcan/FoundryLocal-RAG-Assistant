Title: Troublemaker (1988)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Andy Bausch
Main Cast: Thierry van Werveke, Ender Frings, Nicole Max, Conrad Scheel, Jochen Senf
Keywords: gangster, luxembourg

Overview: With the American Dream set in their minds, two pathetic gangsters, one Luxembourgish, the other German, go through various trials and tribulation in the South of Luxembourg.
