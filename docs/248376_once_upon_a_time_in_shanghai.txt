Title: Once Upon a Time in Shanghai (2014)
Genres: Action, Crime
Director: Wong Ching-Po
Main Cast: Philip Ng, Andy On, Sammo Hung, Jiang Lu-Xia, Michelle Hu Ran
Keywords: martial arts, hong kong movie

Overview: A laborer moves to Shanghai in the hope of becoming rich. But ends up using his kung fu skills to survive. Remake of The Boxer From Shantung.
