Title: A Christmas Melody (2015)
Genres: Romance, Music, TV Movie, Comedy
Director: Mariah Carey
Main Cast: Lacey Chabert, Mariah Carey, Brennan Elliott, Kathy Najimy, Kevin Chamberlin
Keywords: christmas

Overview: "A Christmas Melody" revolves around Kristin, a beautiful, stylish divorcee who is a talented clothing designer and her young daughter. Kristin has just had to close her small Manhattan boutique to return to her Ohio home town and live in her parents' former home. It's an adjustment for Kristin and Emily - especially when she runs into her former high school rival Melissa.
