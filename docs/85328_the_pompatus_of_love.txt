Title: The Pompatus of Love (1996)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Drama
Director: Richard Schenkman
Main Cast: Adrian Pasdar, Jon Cryer, Tim Guinee, Adam Oliensis, Mia Sara
Keywords: beer, love, drinking

Overview: Four guys sit around drinking beer and talking, trying to figure out the meaning of "the pompatus of love" (from the Steve Miller song "Joker") and analyzing their relationships with women.
