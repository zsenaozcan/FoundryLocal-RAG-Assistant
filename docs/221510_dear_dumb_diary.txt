Title: Dear Dumb Diary (2013)
Genres: Family
Director: Kristin Hanggi
Main Cast: Emily Alyn Lind, Sterling Griffith, David Mazouz, Natalie Alyn Lind, Mary-Charles Jones
Keywords: based on novel, female protagonist, middle school, woman director

Overview: Based on the best selling series "Dear Dumb Diary" by Jim Benton. Follow Jamie Kelly as she navigates Mackeral Middle School with the help of her best friend Isabella, her nemesis Angeline and the boy of her dreams Hudson.
