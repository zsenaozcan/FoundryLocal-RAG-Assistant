Title: Hana and Alice (2004)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Shunji Iwai
Main Cast: Anne Suzuki, Yu Aoi, Tomohiro Kaku, Shôko Aida, Hiroshi Abe
Keywords: train station, amnesia, high school, crush, best friend
