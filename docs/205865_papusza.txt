Title: Papusza (2013)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Krzysztof Krauze
Main Cast: Jowita Budnik, Joanna Niemirska, Antoni Pawlicki, Artur Steranko, Andrzej Walden
Keywords: poetry, poet, gipsy, woman director

Overview: The rise and fall of the most distinguished Polish-Gypsy poetess Bronislawa Wajs, widely known as Papusza, and her relationship with her discoverer, writer Jerzy Ficowski.
