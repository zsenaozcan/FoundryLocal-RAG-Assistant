Title: Position Among the Stars (2011)
Genres: Documentary, History
Director: Leonard Retel Helmrich
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: globalization, family history, global economy, indonesia

Overview: Through the eyes of grandmother Rumidjah, a poor old Christian woman living in the slums of Jakarta, we see the economical changing society of Indonesia and the influence of globalization reflected in the life of her juvenile granddaughter Tari and her sons Bakti and Dwi.
