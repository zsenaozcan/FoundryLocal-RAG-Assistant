Title: Gli equilibristi (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ivano De Matteo
Main Cast: Valerio Mastandrea, Barbora Bobuľová, Maurizio Casagrande, Rolando Ravello, Rosabell Laurenti Sellers

Overview: Having cheated on his wife, Giulio, a municipal employee, moves out, but has to find a solution on how to still support his family, and himself.
