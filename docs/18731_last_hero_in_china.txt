Title: Last Hero in China (1993)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Wong Jing
Main Cast: Jet Li, Sharla Cheung Man, Alan Chui Chung-San, Dicky Cheung Wai-Kin, Leung Kar-Yan

Overview: Jet Li stars in this comic spectacle as a Chinese "Robin Hood" who stumbles upon a kidnapping scheme after unwittingly opening a martial arts school next to a brothel!
