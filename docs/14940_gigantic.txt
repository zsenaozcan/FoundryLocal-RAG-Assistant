Title: Gigantic (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Matt Aselton
Main Cast: Zooey Deschanel, John Goodman, Paul Dano, Ed Asner, Jane Alexander
Keywords: department store, adoption, chinese woman, stalker, independent film, stalking

Overview: Young mattress salesman Brian decides to adopt a baby from China but is distracted when he forms a relationship with quirky, wealthy Harriet whom he meets at his mattress store. As their relationship flourishes, unbeknownst to them, a hitman is trying to kill Brian.
