Title: Pokémon: The Movie 2000 (1999)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Animation, Science Fiction, Family
Director: Michael Haigney
Main Cast: Veronica Taylor, Rachael Lillis, Ed Paul, Ikue Ōtani, Eric Stuart
Keywords: bird, collector, fighter, duel, nature, anime

Overview: Ash Ketchum must put his skill to the test when he attempts to save the world from destruction. The Greedy Pokemon collector Lawrence III throws the universe into chaos after disrupting the balance of nature by capturing one of the Pokemon birds that rule the elements of fire, lightning and ice. Will Ash have what it takes to save the world?
