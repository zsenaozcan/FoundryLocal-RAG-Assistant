Title: The Legend of Seven Cutter (2006)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Kwon Nam-Ki
Main Cast: Lee Jung, Ahn Jae-mo, Yoon Eun-hye, Hyun Young, Jeong Jun-ha

Overview: Han-soo is a new transfer student at Seonggji High School, who is thought to be the legendary "Seven Cutter", a badass who beat up an entire gang, attracting the attention of school bully Sung-gi and his lackeys.
