Title: Death Ship (1980)
Genres: Adventure, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Alvin Rakoff
Main Cast: George Kennedy, Richard Crenna, Nick Mancuso, Sally Ann Howes, Kate Reid
Keywords: ocean, shipwreck, nazis, ship, ghost ship, survivor, possession, rescue, independent film, blood

Overview: Survivors of a tragic shipping collision are rescued by a mysterious black ship which appears out of the fog. Little do they realise that the ship is actually a Nazi torture ship which has sailed the seas for years, luring unsuspecting sailors aboard and killing them off one by one.
