Title: Ride (1998)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Millicent Shelton
Main Cast: Malik Yoba, Melissa De Sousa, John Witherspoon, Fredro Starr, Cedric the Entertainer
Keywords: miami, bus ride, road trip, independent film, bus trip, woman director, harlem

Overview: A group of young people leave Harlem for a bus trip down to Miami. The voyage starts off with problems, but it ends up becoming a learning experience, as they end up learning things about each other they had not previously known.
