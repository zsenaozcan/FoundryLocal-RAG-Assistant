Title: Jail Bait (1973)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rainer Werner Fassbinder
Main Cast: Harry Baer, Eva Mattes, Ruth Drexel, Irm Hermann, Klaus Löwitsch
Keywords: erotic movie

Overview: Based on Franz Xaver Kroetz's play, which is in turn based on a true story, this film by Rainer Werner Fassbinder tells the story of a very young girl who, after persuading a local boy to become her lover, induces the anger of her father, whose incestuous sexual attentions to her have grown unbearable.
