Title: Richard Pryor: Here and Now (1983)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Richard Pryor
Main Cast: Richard Pryor, Paul Mooney
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Part live stand-up performance, part documentary, this film is one of comedian Richard Pryor's later stand-up performances. As foul-mouthed as ever, Pryor touches on most of the same topics as in his previous live shows.
