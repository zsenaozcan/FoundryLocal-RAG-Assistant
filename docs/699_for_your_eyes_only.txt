Title: For Your Eyes Only (1981)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Thriller
Director: John Glen
Main Cast: Roger Moore, Carole Bouquet, Chaim Topol, Lynn-Holly Johnson, Julian Glover
Keywords: london england, submarine, england, sea, assassin, mountain, undercover, olympic games, drug traffic, secret mission
