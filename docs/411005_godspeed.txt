Title: Godspeed (2016)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Comedy, Thriller
Director: Chung Mong-Hong
Main Cast: Michael Hui, Leon Dai, Tou Chung-Hua, Matt Wu Zhong-Tian, Na-Do
Keywords: taxi, drug

Overview: A Taiwanese drug mule hires an eccentric cab driver to take him down South so that he can deliver bags of heroin. Things don't go quite to plan as the worst impulses of man plague everyone and everything, and the moments of brevity are so important among the torture and sadism.
