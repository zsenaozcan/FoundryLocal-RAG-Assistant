Title: Searching for Sugar Man (2012)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Malik Bendjelloul
Main Cast: Dennis Coffey, Rodriguez, Stephen Segerman, Mike Theodore, Regan Rodriguez
Keywords: 1970s, mayor, south africa, musician, icon, music business, election, detroit, reading, recording
