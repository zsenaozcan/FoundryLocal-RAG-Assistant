Title: Cartels (2016)
Genres: Action
Director: Keoni Waxman
Main Cast: Steven Seagal, Luke Goss, Georges St-Pierre, Bruce Crawford, Lauro Chartrand
Keywords: ambush, assault, drug lord, luxury hotel, dea

Overview: An elite team of DEA agents are assigned to protect a dangerous drug lord and take refuge in a luxury hotel while they await extraction. They soon find themselves at the center of an ambush as the drug lord's former associates launch an explosive assault on the hotel.
