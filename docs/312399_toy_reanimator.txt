Title: Toy Reanimator (2002)
Genres: Science Fiction, Fantasy
Director: Hakubun
Main Cast: Shugo Oshinari, Akaji Maro, Rena Tanaka

Overview: Set in a toy store specialized in antique toys, a boy works in the toy store and is obsessed with tin toys. There's one particular tin toy he loves more than the rest. In walks a girl wearing shades, and starts telling her own story, a weird and fascinating experience she had with a toy.
