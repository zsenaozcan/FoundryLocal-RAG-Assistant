Title: Vedam (2010)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Krish (Radhakrishna Jagarlamudi)
Main Cast: Allu Arjun, Manoj Kumar Manchu, Anushka Shetty, Manoj Bajpayee, Saranya Ponvannan

Overview: Cable Raju is a slum dweller who works as a cable operator; Saroja is a prostitute; Ramulu is a debt-ridden weaver from Sircilla; Vivek Chakravarthy is an upcoming rock star; Raheemuddin Qureshi is a Muslim from Old Basti in Hyderabad. The stories of these five characters are interwoven into one.
