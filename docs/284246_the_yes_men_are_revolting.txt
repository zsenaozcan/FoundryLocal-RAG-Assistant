Title: The Yes Men Are Revolting (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Laura Nix
Main Cast: Andy Bichlbaum, Mike Bonanno
Keywords: political activism, social activism, woman director, activist

Overview: Activist-pranksters Andy Bichlbaum and Mike Bonnano pull the rug out from under mega-corporations, government officials and a complacent media in a series of outrageous stunts designed to draw awareness to the issue of climate change.
