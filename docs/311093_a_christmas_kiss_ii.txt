Title: A Christmas Kiss II (2014)
Genres: TV Movie, Romance
Director: Kevin Conner
Main Cast: Elisabeth Harnois, Adam Mayfield, Lola Glaudini, Jonathan Bennett, Elisabeth Röhm
Keywords: holiday, christmas

Overview: At a Christmastime event, Jenna shares an impromptu, unforgettable kiss with the dashing billionaire, Cooper Montgomery. Unaware of his intentions and fearful of getting hurt in another relationship, Jenna vows to resist his charms, but begins to realize his affection is real as the two spend more time together.
