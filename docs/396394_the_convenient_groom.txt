Title: The Convenient Groom (2016)
Genres: TV Movie, Romance
Director: David Winning
Main Cast: Vanessa Marcil, David Sutcliffe, Lisa Durupt, Karen Holness, Aaron Craven

Overview: Dr. Kate Lawrence, a celebrity relationship expert, plans to publicly announce her engagement to Bryan, a handsome and perfectly polished businessman. As Kate prepares to share the news, Bryan shocks her by breaking up with her and calling off the wedding. Wanting to save her from humiliation and protect her public image, Lucas Wright, Kate's childhood friend steps in and pretends to be Kate's fiancé.
