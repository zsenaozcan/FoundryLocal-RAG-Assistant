Title: The Iceman Cometh (1973)
Genres: Drama
Director: John Frankenheimer
Main Cast: Lee Marvin, Fredric March, Robert Ryan, Bradford Dillman, Sorrell Booke

Overview: Set in 1912, the entire film takes place inside a dive bar named The Last Chance Saloon, where its destitute patrons eagerly await the arrival of Hickey, who arrives annually and props everyone up with free drinks and spirited stories of his travels. However, when Hickey does show up this year, it is with a message of temperance and an exhortation to give up hopeless dreams and face reality.
