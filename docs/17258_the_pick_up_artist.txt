Title: The Pick-up Artist (1987)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: James Toback
Main Cast: Molly Ringwald, Robert Downey Jr., Dennis Hopper, Danny Aiello, Harvey Keitel
Keywords: womanizer, debt, alcoholic father
