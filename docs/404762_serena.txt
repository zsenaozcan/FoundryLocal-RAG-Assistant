Title: Serena (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ryan White
Main Cast: Serena Williams, Venus Williams, John McEnroe, Roger Federer, Novak Djokovic
Keywords: sport

Overview: An intimate portrait of four-time Olympic gold medalist and international sports icon Serena Williams, focusing on the external pressures and vulnerabilities Williams faces in her quest to achieve four Grand Slams in a row.
