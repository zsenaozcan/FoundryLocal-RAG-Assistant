Title: Intermezzo: A Love Story (1939)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Gregory Ratoff
Main Cast: Leslie Howard, Ingrid Bergman, Edna Best, John Halliday, Cecil Kellaway
Keywords: concert, scholarship, violinist, love affair, piano

Overview: A concert violinist becomes charmed with his daughter's talented piano teacher. When he invites her to go on tour with him, they make beautiful music away from the concert hall as well. He soon leaves his wife so the two can go off together.
