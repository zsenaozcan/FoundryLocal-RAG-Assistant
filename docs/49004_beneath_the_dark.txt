Title: Beneath the Dark (2010)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Chad Feehan
Main Cast: Josh Stewart, Jamie Lynn Sigler, Chris Browning, Angela Featherstone
Keywords: suspense

Overview: Driving to a wedding in Los Angeles through the Mojave Desert, Paul and Adrienne pull off the highway and into Roy's Motel and Cafe. This roadside artifact proves to be a strange and surreal place with an unsettling mix of travelers, who force our couple to discover the secret hidden between them and ultimately, the horrifying reality of their current situation.
