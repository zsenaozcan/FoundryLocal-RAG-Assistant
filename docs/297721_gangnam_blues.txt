Title: Gangnam Blues (2015)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Yoo Ha
Main Cast: Lee Min-ho, Kim Rae-won, Jung Jin-young, Kim Ji-soo, Kim Seol-hyun
Keywords: gangster

Overview: A "gangster noir" set in the 1970s, when Gangnam, Seoul goes under development. Political powers and darker organizations collide as they seek to grab control of the influential and exclusive area.
