Title: The Unbelievers (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Gus Holwerda
Main Cast: Richard Dawkins, Lawrence Krauss, Ayaan Hirsi Ali, Woody Allen, Cameron Diaz
Keywords: atheist, history, religion, science, skepticism, atheism, secularism

Overview: Scientists Richard Dawkins and Lawrence Krauss travel the globe promoting a scientific worldview and the rational questioning of religious belief.
