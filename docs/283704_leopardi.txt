Title: Leopardi (2014)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Mario Martone
Main Cast: Elio Germano, Michele Riondino, Anna Mouglalis, Isabella Ragonese, Massimo Popolizio
Keywords: poetry, poet

Overview: A luminously beautiful biopic of the celebrated eighteenth-century Italian poet, essayist and philosopher Giacomo Leopardi, who created immortal verse whilst struggling with a debilitating illness.
