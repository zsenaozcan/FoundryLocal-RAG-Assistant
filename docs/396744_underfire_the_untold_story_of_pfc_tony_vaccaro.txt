Title: Underfire: The Untold Story of Pfc. Tony Vaccaro (2016)
Genres: War, Documentary, Drama, History
Director: Max Lewkowicz
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: The remarkable story of WWII infantryman and photographer Tony Vaccaro, who created one of the most comprehensive, haunting and intimate photographic records of the war using a smuggled $47 camera while developing the negatives in his helmet at night.
