Title: Madame Bovary (1949)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Vincente Minnelli
Main Cast: Jennifer Jones, James Mason, Van Heflin, Louis Jourdan, Alf Kjellin
Keywords: lover, trial, adulteress

Overview: A provincial doctor's wife's romantic illusions about life and social status lead her to betray her naive husband, take on lovers and run up ruinous debts.
