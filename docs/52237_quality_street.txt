Title: Quality Street (1937)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Drama
Director: George Stevens
Main Cast: Katharine Hepburn, Franchot Tone, Eric Blore, Fay Bainter, Cora Witherspoon
Keywords: school, maid, spinster

Overview: In the 1810s, an old maid poses as her own niece in order to teach her onetime beau a lesson.
