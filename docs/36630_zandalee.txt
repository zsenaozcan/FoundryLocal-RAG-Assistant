Title: Zandalee (1991)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Sam Pillsbury
Main Cast: Nicolas Cage, Erika Anderson, Judge Reinhold, Joe Pantoliano, Viveca Lindfors
Keywords: love triangle, sex, adultery, wife husband relationship, infidelity, boat, new orleans, nudity, passion, louisiana

Overview: Bored with her marriage to burnt out poet turned corporate executive Thierry, Zandalee falls prey to an old friend of her husband, the manipulative and egotistical Johhny and becomes enmeshed in a sensual, passionate and destructive affair.
