Title: Bad Hair (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mariana Rondón
Main Cast: Beto Benitez, Samantha Castillo, Samuel Lange Zambrano, Nelly Ramos, María Emilia Sulbarán
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A nine-year-old boy’s preening obsession with straightening his hair elicits a tidal wave of homophobic panic in his hard-working mother, in this tender but clear-eyed coming-of-age tale.
