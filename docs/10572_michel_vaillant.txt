Title: Michel Vaillant (2003)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Louis-Pascal Couvelaire
Main Cast: Sagamore Stévenin, Peter Youngblood Hills, Diane Kruger, Jean-Pierre Cassel, Béatrice Agenin
Keywords: competition, running, sports team, racing car, saboteur

Overview: Michel Vaillant is # 1 of pilots, undisputed champion in rallying in all circuits in the world. His success arouses admiration and envy. Ruth Wong, director of Team Leader, is determined to break his streak and avenge the memory of his father, founder of Leader. She is capable of anything to achieve her goal.
