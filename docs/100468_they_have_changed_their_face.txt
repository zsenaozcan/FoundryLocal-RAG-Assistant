Title: They Have Changed Their Face (1971)
Genres: Mystery, Horror
Director: Corrado Farina
Main Cast: Adolfo Celi, Geraldine Hooper, Giuliano Esperanti, Francesca Modigliani, Lorenzo Rapazzini
Keywords: capitalism, allegory, industrialist, vampire, automobile industry, advertising

Overview: In this allegory on capitalism, director of a known car corporation invites one of his employees to his country villa to give him the good news. He just got promoted. However, the old man is not what he seems and promotion has a price.
