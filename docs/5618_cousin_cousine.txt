Title: Cousin, Cousine (1975)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Jean-Charles Tacchella
Main Cast: Marie-Christine Barrault, Victor Lanoux, Guy Marchand, Marie-France Pisier, Ginette Garcin
Keywords: female nudity, cousin, friendship, wedding

Overview: Two distant cousins meet at a wedding banquet for an elderly couple. Over time, a close friendship develops between them, but their spouses begin to think that they are more than just friends.
