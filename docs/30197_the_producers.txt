Title: The Producers (1967)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mel Brooks
Main Cast: Gene Wilder, Zero Mostel, Dick Shawn, Kenneth Mars, Lee Meredith
Keywords: adolf hitler, musical, theatrical producer, eva braun, playwright, scam, broadway

Overview: Broadway producer, Max Bialystock and his accountant, Leo Bloom plan to make money by charming little old ladies to invest in a production many times over what it will actually cost, and then put on a sure-fire flop, so nobody will ask for their money back – and what can be a more certain flop than a tasteless musical celebrating Hitler.
