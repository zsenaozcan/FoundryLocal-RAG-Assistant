Title: Amadeus (1984)
Genres: Drama, History, Music
Director: Miloš Forman
Main Cast: F. Murray Abraham, Tom Hulce, Elizabeth Berridge, Roy Dotrice, Simon Callow
Keywords: italy, composer, opera, talent, musician, marriage crisis, god, murder, vienna austria, envy

Overview: The incredible story of genius musician Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, told in flashback by his peer and secret rival Antonio Salieri – now confined to an insane asylum.
