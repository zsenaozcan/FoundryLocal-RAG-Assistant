Title: Slam Dunk Ernest (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family, Romance
Director: John R. Cherry III
Main Cast: Jim Varney, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Richard Leacock, Miguel A. Núñez, Jr.
Keywords: sport

Overview: Ernest P. Worrell becomes a basketball star after an angel bearing an uncanny resemblance to Kareem Abdul-Jabbar gives him a pair of magic sneakers.
