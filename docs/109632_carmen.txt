Title: Carmen (1915)
Genres: Drama
Director: Cecil B. DeMille
Main Cast: Geraldine Farrar, Wallace Reid, Pedro de Cordoba, Horace B. Carpenter, William Elmer
Keywords: gypsy

Overview: In order to help her smuggler kinsmen, a sultry gypsy seduces and corrupts an officer of the Civil Guard turning him into a traitor and murderer.
