Title: The Edge of Dreaming (2010)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Amy Hardie
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: This is the story of a rational, skeptical woman, a mother and wife, who does not remember her dreams. Except once, when she dreamed her horse was dying. She woke so scared she went outside in the night. She found him dead. The next dream told her she would die herself, when she was 48.
