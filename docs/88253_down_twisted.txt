Title: Down Twisted (1987)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Albert Pyun
Main Cast: Carey Lowell, Charles Rocket, Trudy Dochterman, Thom Mathews, Norbert Weisser

Overview: Maxine (Cary Lowell) is a waitress with big dreams who inadvertently gets mixed up with a gang of smugglers who are trying to get their hands on a stolen artefact called 'The Crucible Of San Lucas'.
