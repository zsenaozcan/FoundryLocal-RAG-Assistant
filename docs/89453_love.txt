Title: Love (2005)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Vladan Nikolić
Main Cast: Sergej Trifunović, Geno Lechner, Peter Gevisser

Overview: Told through a looping, non-linear narrative from each of the characters' points of view, the film reconstructs the stories of a Yugoslav hit man, his former lover and her police officer boyfriend, as their paths cross in New York.
