Title: Babysitter's Black Book (2015)
Genres: Drama, TV Movie
Director: Lee Friedlander
Main Cast: Spencer Locke, Angeline Appel, Lauren York, Steffani Brass, David Shatraw
Keywords: prostitute, babysitter, escort service, high school, lifetime, inspired by true events

Overview: When her parents' financial problems threaten Ashley's dreams for college, the high school whiz kid takes matters into her own hands. She and her girlfriends turn their babysitting business into a wildly successful escort service for dads. But in this small suburban community, nothing stays secret for long.
