Title: Attack of the 50 Foot Woman (1958)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Nathan H. Juran
Main Cast: Allison Hayes, William Hudson, Yvette Vickers, Roy Gordon, George Douglas
Keywords: revenge, alien, b movie, cheating husband, violence, woman, giant, chrysler imperial

Overview: When an abused wife grows to giant size because of an alien encounter and an aborted murder attempt, she goes after her cheating husband with revenge on her mind.
