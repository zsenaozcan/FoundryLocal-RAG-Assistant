Title: Pearls of the Deep (1966)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jiří Menzel
Main Cast: Ferdinand Krůta, Alois Vachek, Milos Ctrnacty, Frantisek Havel, Josef Hejl
Keywords: fairy tale, bride, insurance salesman, avant-garde, railway, woman director, boasting, riff raff, new wave, gypsy girl

Overview: A manifesto of sorts for the Czech New Wave, this five-part anthology shows off the breadth of expression and the versatility of the movement’s directors. Based on stories by the legendary writer Bohumil Hrabal, the shorts range from the surreally chilling to the caustically observant to the casually romantic, but all have a cutting, wily view of the world.
