Title: Relentless (1989)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Horror, Crime
Director: William Lustig
Main Cast: Judd Nelson, Robert Loggia, Leo Rossi, Meg Foster, Patrick O'Bryan
Keywords: serial killer, police officer, dead woman with eyes open

Overview: Two Los Angeles police detectives, cynical veteran Malloy and cocky rookie Dietz, hunt for a serial killer, an ex-cop named Taylor, whom randomly chooses his victims from a phone directory.
