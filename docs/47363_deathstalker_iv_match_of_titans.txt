Title: Deathstalker IV: Match of Titans (1991)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Howard R. Cohen
Main Cast: Rick Hill, Brett Baxter Clark, Michelle Moffett, Anya Pencheva, Kosta Karageorgiev
Keywords: castle, sequel, independent film, infiltration, duel, bdsm, sword and sorcery

Overview: It begins as a contest of strength the challenge of competition luring fighters from far away to a hilltop castle. Deathstalker leads the combatants but only to discover that the winners are disappearing one by one. Now, in his last and greatest battle, Deathstalker must defend his remaining comrades, his life, and his newfound love in battle against an evil army of stone warriors and the wicked queen who created them.
