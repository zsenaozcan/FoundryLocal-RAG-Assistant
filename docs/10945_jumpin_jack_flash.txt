Title: Jumpin' Jack Flash (1986)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Thriller
Director: Penny Marshall
Main Cast: Whoopi Goldberg, Stephen Collins, Jonathan Pryce, John Wood, Carol Kane
Keywords: cia, kgb, computer, chat, mole, agent, woman director

Overview: Terry works for a bank, and uses computers to communicate with clients all over the world. One day she gets a strange message from an unknown source. The message is coded. After decoding the message, Terry becomes embroiled in an espionage ring. People are killed, and Terry is chased. Throughout she remains in contact with this unknown person, who needs Terry to help save his life.
