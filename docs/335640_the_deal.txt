Title: The Deal (2015)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Son Yong-ho
Main Cast: Kim Sang-kyung, Kim Sung-kyun, Park Sung-woong, Jo Jae-yoon, Kim Eui-sung

Overview: Serial killer Kang-Cheon made several victims. Among them, the younger sister of Detective Tae-Soo and the wife of another ordinary man named Seung-Hyun. The two men then get together for revenge upon Kang-Cheon.
