Title: Hunter Will Get You (1976)
Genres: Crime, Action, Thriller
Director: Philippe Labro
Main Cast: Jean-Paul Belmondo, Bruno Cremer, Jean Négroni, Patrick Fierry
Keywords: france, bounty, government, drug

Overview: L'Alpagueur is a free-lance spy from the French secret agency. He's put on the investigation about L'epervier, a serial-killer who employs young boys to help him robbing banks before killing them.
