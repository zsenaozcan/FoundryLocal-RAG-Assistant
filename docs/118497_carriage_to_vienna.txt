Title: Carriage to Vienna (1966)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Karel Kachyňa
Main Cast: Iva Janžurová, Jaromír Hanzlík, Luděk Munzar, Vladimír Ptácek, Ivo Niederle

Overview: A WW2 story of a young Austrian soldier running from the Russian army and a woman whom he forces to come along in order to save his wounded mate.
