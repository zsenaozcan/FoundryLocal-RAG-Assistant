Title: The Toast of New Orleans (1950)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance
Director: Norman Taurog
Main Cast: Kathryn Grayson, Mario Lanza, David Niven, J. Carrol Naish, James Mitchell
Keywords: musical, fisherman, opera singer

Overview: Snooty opera singer meets a rough-and-tumble fisherman in the Louisiana bayous, but this fisherman can sing! Her agent lures him away to New Orleans to teach him to sing opera, but comes to regret this rash decision when the singers fall in love.
