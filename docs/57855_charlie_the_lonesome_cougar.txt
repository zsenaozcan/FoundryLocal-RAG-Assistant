Title: Charlie, the Lonesome Cougar (1967)
Genres: Adventure, Family
Director: Winston Hibler
Main Cast: Ron Brown, Bryan Russell, Linda Wallace, Jim Wilson, Clifford Peterson
Keywords: wilderness, friendship, wild animal

Overview: A heartwarming Disney classic in which a cougar, who was rescued as a cub and raised by a group of loggers in the Pacific Northwest, reverts back to his natural instincts, leading to hilarious (and dangerous) consequences.
