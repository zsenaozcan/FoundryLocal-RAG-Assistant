Title: The Gift of Love (1978)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Don Chaffey
Main Cast: Marie Osmond, Timothy Bottoms, Bethel Leslie, June Lockhart, Donald Moffat
Keywords: watch, hair, newlywed

Overview: Inspired by O. Henry's short story about a young bride and groom, each of whom foolishly--but quite lovingly--sacrifices a treasured possession to buy the perfect Christmas gift for their mate.
