Title: Amuck! (1972)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Horror
Director: Silvio Amadio
Main Cast: Farley Granger, Barbara Bouchet, Rosalba Neri, Petar Martinovitch, Dino Mele
Keywords: stalker, giallo, madness

Overview: A young American girl arrives on an island near Venice to begin her new job as secretary to famous novelist Richard Stuart. However, it turns out that her predecessor disappeared without a trace....
