Title: Dorian Gray (1970)
Genres: Drama
Director: Massimo Dallamano
Main Cast: Helmut Berger, Richard Todd, Herbert Lom, Marie Liljedahl, Margaret Lee
Keywords: gay, shakespeare, nightclub, painting, soul selling, vanity
