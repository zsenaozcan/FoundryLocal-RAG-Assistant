Title: The Fabulous World of Jules Verne (1958)
Genres: Science Fiction, Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Karel Zeman
Main Cast: Lubor Tokoš, Arnošt Navrátil, Miroslav Holub, František Šlégr, Jana Zatloukalová
Keywords: jules verne

Overview: This marvelous film from Czech master animator Karel Zeman is a partial adaptation of the Jules Verne novel "Facing the Flag." The story treads the well-worn path of world domination as attempted by a piratical mastermind, who uses a morally myopic scientist's high explosive.
