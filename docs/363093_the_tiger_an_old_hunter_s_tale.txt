Title: The Tiger: An Old Hunter's Tale (2015)
Genres: Action, Adventure, History, Thriller
Director: Park Hoon-Jung
Main Cast: Choi Min-sik, Jeong Man-sik, Kim Sang-ho, Sung Yu-bin, Jung Suk-won
Keywords: hunter, mountain, tiger, snow, korean movie
