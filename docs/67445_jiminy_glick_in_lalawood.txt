Title: Jiminy Glick in Lalawood (2004)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Vadim Jean
Main Cast: Martin Short, Jan Hooks, Linda Cardellini, Elizabeth Perkins, John Michael Higgins

Overview: "La La Wood" follows the legacy of Jiminy Glick, first introduced on "The Martin Short Show," who went on to get (non)-critical acclaim for his talk show "Primetime Glick," where Mr. Glick interviewed countless celebrities (which usually ended in verbally--sometimes physically--insulting/assaulting them). Now comes "La La Wood"--Jiminy Glick's home. This is his story (sort of).
