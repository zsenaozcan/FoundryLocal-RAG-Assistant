Title: Berth Marks (1929)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lewis R. Foster
Main Cast: Stan Laurel, Oliver Hardy, Paulette Goddard, Charlie Hall

Overview: The story involves Stan and Ollie as two musicians attempting to travel by train to Pottsville (presumably Pennsylvania, a very popular vaudeville performance location). It was only their second sound film, but a silent version was also made for cinemas at the time that were not equipped to show talkies.
