Title: The Kiss of Her Flesh (1968)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Michael Findlay
Main Cast: Uta Erickson, Janet Banzet, Michael Findlay, Donna Stone, Rita Vance
Keywords: sex, dancer, nudity, stripper, murder, violence

Overview: A weapons expert who murdered his unfaithful stripper wife and her lover goes on a killing spree, bumping off exotic dancers and hookers, while plotting the demise of his wife's swinging bisexual sister and her kinky boyfriend.
