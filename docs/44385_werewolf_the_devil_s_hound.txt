Title: Werewolf: The Devil's Hound (2007)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Gregory C. Parker
Main Cast: Michael Wrann, Michael Dionne, Tamara Malawitz, Phil Gauvin, Christy O. Cianci
Keywords: werewolf

Overview: An escaped werewolf - who’s determined to start a family of her own - sinks her claws into Kevin, leaving him with an uncontrollable desire to kill. Can his family save him before his lycan nature takes over for good?
