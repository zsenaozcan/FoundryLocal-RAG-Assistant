Title: Saw (2003)
Genres: Crime, Horror, Thriller
Director: James Wan
Main Cast: Leigh Whannell, Paul Moder, Katrina Mathers, Dean Francis
Keywords: kidnapping, paralysis, hospital, torture, short

Overview: Saw is an Australian nine-and-a-half-minute short subject horror film that was later expanded to a full feature film. The story is about David, an orderly at a hospital, who tells his horrific story of being kidnapped and forced to play a vile game of survival.
