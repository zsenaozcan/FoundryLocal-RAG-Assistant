Title: La Grande Bouffe (1973)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Marco Ferreri
Main Cast: Marcello Mastroianni, Ugo Tognazzi, Michel Piccoli, Philippe Noiret, Andréa Ferréol
Keywords: suicide, callgirl, sex, eating and drinking, childhood friends

Overview: A group of men hire some prostitutes and go to a villa in the countryside. There, they engage in group sex and resolve to eat themselves to death.
