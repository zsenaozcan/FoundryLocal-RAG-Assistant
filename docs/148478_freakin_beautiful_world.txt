Title: Freakin' Beautiful World (1997)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jarmo Lampela
Main Cast: Joonas Bragge, Arttu Kapulainen, Pihla Penttinen, Ilkka Koivula, Kati Outinen
Keywords: drug dealer, young people, drug use, teenager, youth

Overview: Ippe and Papu are two teenagers spending the summer, when they get into trouble with their drug-dealer. They are forced to act as drug-mules. Along the way they meet a new girl, Mia.
