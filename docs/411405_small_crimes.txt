Title: Small Crimes (2017)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Thriller, Crime
Director: E.L. Katz
Main Cast: Nikolaj Coster-Waldau, Jacki Weaver, Robert Forster, Gary Cole, Molly Parker
Keywords: based on novel, ex-cop, suspense, redemption, released from prison, disgraced, delightful, blackly comic tale
