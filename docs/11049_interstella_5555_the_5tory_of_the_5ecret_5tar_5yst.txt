Title: Interstella 5555: The 5tory of the 5ecret 5tar 5ystem (2003)
Genres: Science Fiction, Music, Animation
Director: Kazuhisa Takenôchi
Main Cast: Romanthony
Keywords: spaceman, musical, record producer, space, rescue, music band

Overview: A sci-fi anime House-musical movie collaboration between Daft Punk--and their music--and designer Leiji Matsumoto. During the recording of their DISCOVERY album and using the themes of sci-fi celebrity, decadence and space travel, Daft Punk--with help from Cedric Hervet--wrote the story and inspired seasoned Japanese animators to symbiotically create this stunning space musical.
