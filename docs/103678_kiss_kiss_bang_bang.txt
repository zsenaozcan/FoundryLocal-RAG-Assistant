Title: Kiss Kiss... Bang Bang (1966)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Duccio Tessari
Main Cast: Giuliano Gemma, George Martin, Lorella De Luca, Nieves Navarro, Daniele Vargas
Keywords: spy, farce, breaking the fourth wall, james bond spoof, eurospy

Overview: Kirk Warren, a former spy, is to be executed because he tried to steal a million dollars. But he is saved by Colonel Smithson because Sir Wilcox has prepared a mission for him. Warren has to steal a secret formula in Switzerland and try to expose the real identity of Mr. X. Kirk leaves London and Alina, his girl, together with three clumsy friends to accomplish his not so secret mission. But Kirk's great love is always a million dollars and not a secret formula.
