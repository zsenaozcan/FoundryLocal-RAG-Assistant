Title: The Rose Seller (1998)
Genres: Drama
Director: Víctor Gaviria
Main Cast: Lady Tabares, Marta Correa, Mileider Gil, Diana Murillo, Liliana Giraldo
Keywords: colombia

Overview: 13-year-old Monica leads a street life, making her living by selling flowers to couples in local nightspots, she is joined by 10-year-old Andrea who runs out of her house after her mother beats her.
