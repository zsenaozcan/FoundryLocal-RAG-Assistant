Title: Teleios (2017)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Ian Truitner
Main Cast: Sunny Mabrey, Lance Broadway, T.J. Hoban, Christian Pitre, Mykel Shannon Jenkins
Keywords: artificial intelligence, isolation, genetic enhancement, deep space

Overview: A deep space mining vessel has been adrift for two years. It is suspected the crew brutally killed each other, but the reason for the bloodbath is unknown. A rescue crew is sent to find if there are any survivors, what happened and why.
