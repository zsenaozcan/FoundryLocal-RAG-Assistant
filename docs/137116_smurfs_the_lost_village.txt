Title: Smurfs: The Lost Village (2017)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Comedy, Family
Director: Kelly Asbury
Main Cast: Julia Roberts, Ariel Winter, Ellie Kemper, Mandy Patinkin, Jeff Dunham
Keywords: sequel, kids

Overview: In this fully animated, all-new take on the Smurfs, a mysterious map sets Smurfette and her friends Brainy, Clumsy and Hefty on an exciting race through the Forbidden Forest leading to the discovery of the biggest secret in Smurf history.
