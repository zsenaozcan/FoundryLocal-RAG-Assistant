Title: Come On, Get Happy: The Partridge Family Story (1999)
Genres: Unknown
Director: David Burton Morris
Main Cast: Eve Gordon, Rodney Scott, Kathy Wagner, Shawn Pyfrom, Michael Chieffo

Overview: The behind the scenes story of "The Partridge Family" TV show, told from the point of view of young Danny Bonaduce. Problems include Danny's jealous father, David Cassidy's overnight fame, and even conflicts with the Brady Bunch!
