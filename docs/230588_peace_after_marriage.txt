Title: Peace After Marriage (2013)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Bandar Albuliwi
Main Cast: Hiam Abbass, Omer Barnea, Ghazi Albuliwi, Assef Cohen, Assaf Cohen

Overview: Desperate for companionship, a lonely, young Palestinian-American man agrees to marry an Israeli woman in need of a Green Card, forcing them to re-examine their respective cultural and familial traditions.
