Title: Thesis on a Homicide (2013)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Crime
Director: Hernán Goldfrid
Main Cast: Ricardo Darín, Alberto Ammann, Calu Rivero, Arturo Puig
Keywords: suspense

Overview: Roberto is a specialist in Criminal Law whose life is thrown into chaos when he becomes convinced that Gonzalo, one of his best students, has committed a brutal murder right in front of the Faculty of Law. Determined to uncover the truth he begins a personal investigation that will soon become an obsession.
