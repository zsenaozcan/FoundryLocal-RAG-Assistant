Title: Northern Lights (2009)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Mystery, Romance
Director: Mike Robe
Main Cast: LeAnn Rimes, Eddie Cibrian, Rosanna Arquette, Jayne Eastwood, William MacDonald

Overview: Hoping to leave behind troubled days in the Baltimore city police department, Nate Burns (Eddie Cibrian) journeys to Alaska, where he takes up a quiet life as a small-town sheriff and begins a romance with spirited bush pilot Meg Galligan (LeAnn Rimes). But when Meg's father turns up dead, Burns finds himself thrust into the limelight of a dangerous murder investigation. Rosanna Arquette co-stars in this drama based on the novel by Nora Roberts.
