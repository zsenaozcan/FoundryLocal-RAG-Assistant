Title: Behind Convent Walls (1978)
Genres: Drama
Director: Walerian Borowczyk
Main Cast: Marina Pierro, Ligia Branice, Gabriella Giacobbe, Loredana Martínez, Renato Rossini
Keywords: nun, nudity, convent

Overview: A zealous, handsome priest, who is the confessor for a convent full of women, encourages the equally zealous abbess of the institution to enforce strict rules on these unfortunate women. At the same time, a particularly disturbed nun manages to poison herself and many of the other novitiates in yet another scandal which is covered up by church authorities.
