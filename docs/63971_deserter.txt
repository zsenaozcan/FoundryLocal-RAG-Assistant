Title: Deserter (2002)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Martin Huberty
Main Cast: Paul Fox, Tom Hardy, Kate Maberly, Aitor Merino, Félicité Du Jeu

Overview: Unlikely friends in a melting pot of confusion. Simon Murray fights for the French Foreign Legion. Pascal Dupont fights for himself. War torn men question honour, hope, morality...because you can desert everything...except yourself.
