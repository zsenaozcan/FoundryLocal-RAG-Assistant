Title: Journey Back to Christmas (2016)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Mel Damski
Main Cast: Candace Cameron Bure, Oliver Hudson, Tom Skerritt, Brooke Nevin, Meghan Heffern
Keywords: world war ii, christmas tree, christmas, christmas songs

Overview: WWII has ended and Hanna is still grieving over her husband's disappearance. When the Christmas Comet appears for the first time in 70 years, Hanna is caught in a terrible storm and knocked unconscious. When she wakes up, she's in 2016.
