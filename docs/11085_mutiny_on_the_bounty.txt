Title: Mutiny on the Bounty (1962)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, History
Director: Lewis Milestone
Main Cast: Trevor Howard, Marlon Brando, Richard Harris, Hugh Griffith, Percy Herbert
Keywords: exotic island, captain, mutiny, heart-throb, tahiti, import

Overview: The Bounty leaves Portsmouth in 1787. Its destination: to sail to Tahiti and load bread-fruit. Captain Bligh will do anything to get there as fast as possible, using any means to keep up a strict discipline. When they arrive at Tahiti, it is like a paradise for the crew, something completely different than the living hell aboard the ship. On the way back to England, officer Fletcher Christian becomes the leader of a mutiny.
