Title: 90 Minutes (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Eva Sørhaug
Main Cast: Aksel Hennie, Pia Tjelta, Bjørn Floberg, Mads Ousdal, Kaia Varjord
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Director Eva Sørhaug (Cold Lunch) reveals the rage and violence lurking beneath seemingly tranquil domesticity in her bold and uncompromising sophomore feature.
