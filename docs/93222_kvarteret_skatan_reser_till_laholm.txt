Title: Kvarteret Skatan reser till Laholm (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mikael Syrén
Main Cast: Sofia Bach, David Batra, Johan Glans, Olle Jansson, Rachel Mohlin
Keywords: marriage proposal, camping, funeral, dysfunctional marriage

Overview: For a long time, a group of friends have been on a yearly summer trip to Laholm. This year, they want to go somewhere else, at least everyone except Ulf, who force the others to join the trip - by saying he has cancer.
