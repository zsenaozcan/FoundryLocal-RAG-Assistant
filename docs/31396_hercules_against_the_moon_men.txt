Title: Hercules Against the Moon Men (1964)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Giacomo Gentilomo
Main Cast: Alan Steel, Jany Clair, Anna Maria Polani, Nando Tamberlani, Jean-Pierre Honoré
Keywords: moon, mythology, horse, sacrifice, princess, sword fight, alien, strong man, evil queen, hercules

Overview: Hercules is summoned to oppose the evil Queen Samara, who has allied herself with aliens and is sacrificing her own people in a bid to awaken a moon goddess.
