Title: Children of Hiroshima (1952)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kaneto Shindô
Main Cast: Nobuko Otowa, Osamu Takizawa, Masao Shimizu, Jukichi Uno, Akira Yamanouchi

Overview: Shows the devastation caused by the Atomic bomb, and by use of a fictional storyline, portrays the struggle of the ordinary Japanese people in dealing with the aftermath.
