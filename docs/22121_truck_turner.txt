Title: Truck Turner (1974)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Jonathan Kaplan
Main Cast: Isaac Hayes, Yaphet Kotto, Alan Weeks, Annazette Chase, Nichelle Nichols
Keywords: bounty hunter, revenge, organized crime, blaxploitation

Overview: Ex-football star turned skip-tracer, Turner specializes in bringing in the criminals police are too scared to chase, and when he's hired to capture sociopathic pimp Gator, he finds himself confronted by the most vicious killers in the underground scene.
