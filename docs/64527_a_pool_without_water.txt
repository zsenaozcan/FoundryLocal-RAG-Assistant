Title: A Pool Without Water (1982)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Kōji Wakamatsu
Main Cast: Mie, Reiko Nakamura, Yumiko Fujita, Yuya Uchida
Keywords: eroticism

Overview: The man has a monotonous job punching tickets for the Japanese railway. One night, while walking home, he saves Jun from getting raped by two hoodlums. A few days later, he sneaks into her house but gets discovered. Later, as he's watches his son subdue an insect with a chemical, the man gets an idea to subdue women while they're sleeping so he won't be discovered sneaking into their home. His new hobby spirals out of control as his confidence grows.
