Title: Les Héritiers (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Marie-Castille Mention-Schaar
Main Cast: Ariane Ascaride, Ahmed Dramé, Noémie Merlant, Geneviève Mnich, Stéphane Bak
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Based on a true story.  In Léon Blum high school in Créteil (France), a history teacher decides to have her weakest 10th grade class take a national history competition. This will change them.
