Title: A Long Ride from Hell (1968)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Camillo Bazzoni
Main Cast: Steve Reeves, Wayde Preston, Guido Lollobrigida, Mimmo Palmara, Silvana Venturelli
Keywords: based on novel, revenge, train robbery, spaghetti western

Overview: Mike Sturges and his younger brother, Roy, are sentenced to Yuma Penitentiary on a trumped-up train robbery charge. Both endure cruel treatment before Mike escapes to extract revenge on their enemies.
