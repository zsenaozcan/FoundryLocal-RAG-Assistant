Title: Robinson Crusoe on Mars (1964)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Byron Haskin
Main Cast: Paul Mantee, Victor Lundin, Adam West, The Woolley Monkey
Keywords: mars, space mission, astronaut, mission to mars, robinson crusoe

Overview: Stranded on Mars with only a monkey as a companion, an astronaut must figure out how to find oxygen, water, and food on the lifeless planet.
