Title: The Venetian Affair (1967)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Jerry Thorpe
Main Cast: Robert Vaughn, Felicia Farr, Karlheinz Böhm, Luciana Paluzzi, Boris Karloff
Keywords: spy, secret agent

Overview: Former CIA man, Bill Fenner, now a downbeat, loner journalist, is sent to Venice to investigate the shock suicide bombing by an American diplomat at a peace conference.
