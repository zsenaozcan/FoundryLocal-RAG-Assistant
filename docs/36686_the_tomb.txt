Title: The Tomb (2009)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Michael Staininger
Main Cast: Wes Bentley, Kaitlin Doubleday, Sofya Skya, Cary-Hiroyuki Tagawa, Mackenzie Rosman

Overview: Successful writer and scholar Jonathan Merrick falls under the spell of the irresistible, bewitchingly beautiful Ligeia. She's fighting a fatal illness and she will stop at nothing to defeat death, her one true enemy.
