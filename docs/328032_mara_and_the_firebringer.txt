Title: Mara and the Firebringer (2015)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: Tommy Krappweis
Main Cast: Lilian Prent, Jan Josef Liefers, Esther Schweins, Christoph Maria Herbst, Eva Habermann

Overview: Mara Lorbeer, a fifteen year old girl, finds out that she has to save the world because the Norse god Loki is threatening to break free of his chains.
