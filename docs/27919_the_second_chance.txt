Title: The Second Chance (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Steve Taylor
Main Cast: Michael W. Smith, Jeff Obafemi Carr, J. Don Ferguson, Lisa Arrindell, David Alford

Overview: Ethan Jenkins (Michael W. Smith) and Jake Sanders (introducing Jeff Obafemi Carr) are both passionate pastors who worship the same God from the same book--but that's where the similarity ends. When they are suddenly thrown together in a tough neighborhood and forced to work side by side, Ethan discovers there is no boundary between the streets and the sanctuary.Written by Tracey Zemitis.
