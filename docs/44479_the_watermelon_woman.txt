Title: The Watermelon Woman (1996)
Genres: Drama
Director: Cheryl Dunye
Main Cast: Cheryl Dunye, Guinevere Turner, Valarie Walker, Lisa Marie Bronson, Cheryll Clarke
Keywords: lesbian relationship, independent film, woman director, black lgbt

Overview: Cheryl, a young black lesbian, works a day job in a video store while trying to make a film about a black actress from the 1930s known for playing the stereotypical "mammy" roles relegated to black actresses during the period. It was the first feature film directed by a black lesbian.
