Title: Eklavya: The Royal Guard (2007)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Vidhu Vinod Chopra
Main Cast: Amitabh Bachchan, Saif Ali Khan, Sanjay Dutt, Vidya Balan, Jackie Shroff

Overview: As the kingdom of Devigarh comes apart at the seams, an aging bodyguard attempts to protect the Royal Family, as well as keep its darkest secrets from ever coming to light.
