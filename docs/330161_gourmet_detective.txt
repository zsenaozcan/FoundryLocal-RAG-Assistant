Title: Gourmet Detective (2015)
Genres: TV Movie, Mystery
Director: Scott Smith
Main Cast: Dylan Neal, Brooke Burns, Alissa Skovbye

Overview: Famed culinary consultant Henry Ross is a charming food industry insider in San Francisco. When suspicious  sabotage ends in a shocking murder at his friend’s five-star restaurant, Henry is put on the case with strong-willed  police detective and single mom, Maggie Price. Despite Maggie’s protests, they must work together to decipher  the clues and investigate all of San Francisco’s top culinary pros.
