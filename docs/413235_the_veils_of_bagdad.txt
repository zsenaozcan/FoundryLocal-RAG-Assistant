Title: The Veils of Bagdad (1953)
Genres: Drama, Action, Adventure
Director: George Sherman
Main Cast: Victor Mature, Mari Blanchard, Virginia Field, Guy Rolfe, James Arness
Keywords: harem, tribe, ottoman empire, pasha

Overview: Antar is sent by Suleiman, head of the Ottoman Empire, to Bagdad to prevent Hammam, Pasha of Bagdad, from purchasing the services of local leader Mustapha to unite the hill tribes and overthrow the emperor. The intrigue mounts as Antar falls in love with dancer Selima, who tries to avenge her father's death against Hammam's right-hand-man Kasseim, whose wife Rosanna has fallen in love with Antar!
