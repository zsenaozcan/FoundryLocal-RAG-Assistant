Title: The Seasoning House (2012)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Paul Hyett
Main Cast: Rosie Day, Sean Pertwee, Kevin Howarth, Anna Walton, Jemma Powell
Keywords: revenge, land war, forced prostitution, balkans

Overview: The Seasoning House - where young girls are prostituted to the military. An orphaned deaf mute is enslaved to care for them. She moves between the walls and crawlspaces, planning her escape. Planning her ingenious and brutal revenge.
