Title: The Ardor (2014)
Genres: Western, Drama
Director: Pablo Fendrik
Main Cast: Gael García Bernal, Alice Braga, Claudio Tolcachir, Chico Diaz, Jorge Sesán

Overview: Kaí is a mysterious shaman who emerges from the Rio Paraná to help a poor farmer and his daughter, who are threatened by a band of cold-blooded mercenaries hired to force them to sell their land.
