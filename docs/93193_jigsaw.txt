Title: Jigsaw (1962)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery
Director: Val Guest
Main Cast: Jack Warner, Yolande Donlan, Michael Goodliffe, John Le Mesurier, John Barron
Keywords: brighton, brit noir

Overview: A woman is found murdered in a house along the coast from Brighton. Local detectives Fellows and Wilks lead an investigation methodically following up leads and clues mostly in Brighton and Hove but also further afield.
