Title: Noroi: The Curse (2005)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Kôji Shiraishi
Main Cast: Jin Muraki, Rio Kanno, Tomono Kuga, Maria Takagi, Hiroshi Aramata
Keywords: journalist, tv show, curse, disappearance, demon, occult, found footage, dark secret

Overview: A documentary filmmaker explores seemingly unrelated paranormal incidents connected by the legend of an ancient demon called the "kagutaba."
