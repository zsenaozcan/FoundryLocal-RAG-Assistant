Title: The Fearless Vampire Killers (1967)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Roman Polanski
Main Cast: Jack MacGowran, Roman Polanski, Alfie Bass, Jessie Robins, Sharon Tate
Keywords: professor, vampire

Overview: A noted professor and his dim-witted apprentice fall prey to their inquiring vampires, while on the trail of the ominous damsel in distress.
