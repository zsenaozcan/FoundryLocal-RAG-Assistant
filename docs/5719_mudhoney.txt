Title: Mudhoney (1965)
Genres: Drama
Director: Russ Meyer
Main Cast: Hal Hopper, Antoinette Cristiani, John Furlong, Rena Horten, Princess Livingston
Keywords: sex, based on novel, depression, prohibition, nudity, murder

Overview: In this Depression-era tale, Calef is traveling from Michigan to California and stops in Spooner, Missouri, where Lute hires him for odd jobs. Calef gets involved with Lute's niece, Hannah. But she is married to Sidney, a wife-beating drunk who hopes to inherit his uncle-in-law's money. Sidney and an eccentric preacher plot against Calef, who finds it difficult to conceal his mysterious past and his growing affection for Sidney's wife.
