Title: 99 and 44/100% Dead (1974)
Genres: Action, Crime, Comedy, Adventure
Director: John Frankenheimer
Main Cast: Richard Harris, Edmond O'Brien, Bradford Dillman, Ann Turkel, Constance Ford

Overview: Uncle Frank Kelly calls on Harry Crown to help him in a gang war. The war becomes personal when Harry's new girlfriend is kidnapped by Uncle Frank's enemy, Big Eddie.
