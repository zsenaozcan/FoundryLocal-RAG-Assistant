Title: Belle du Seigneur (2012)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Glenio Bonder
Main Cast: Jonathan Rhys Meyers, Natalia Vodianova, Ed Stoppard, Marianne Faithfull

Overview: English-language adaptation of Albert Cohen's epic Swiss tale of a tortured love affair between a high-ranking Jewish official and the protestant wife of one of his employees.
