Title: The Twonky (1953)
Genres: Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Arch Oboler
Main Cast: Hans Conried, Janet Warren, William H. Lynn, Edwin Max, Gloria Blondell
Keywords: sister sister relationship, satire, television set

Overview: A college professor, left alone by his wife for the weekend, discovers his new TV set is not only alive, but determined to take control of his entire life.
