Title: Texas Killing Fields (2011)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Ami Canaan Mann
Main Cast: Sam Worthington, Jessica Chastain, Chloë Grace Moretz, Jeffrey Dean Morgan, Stephen Graham
Keywords: torture, series of murders, multiple murders, woman director

Overview: In the Texas bayous, a local homicide detective teams up with a cop from New York City to investigate a series of unsolved murders.
