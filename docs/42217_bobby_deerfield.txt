Title: Bobby Deerfield (1977)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Sydney Pollack
Main Cast: Al Pacino, Marthe Keller, Anny Duperey, Walter McGinn, Romolo Valli
Keywords: love triangle, terminal illness, race car driver

Overview: Bobby Deerfield, a famous American race car driver on the European circuit, falls in love with the enigmatic Lillian Morelli, who is terminally ill.
