Title: Cecil B. Demented (2000)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy, Crime
Director: John Waters
Main Cast: Melanie Griffith, Stephen Dorff, Alicia Witt, Adrian Grenier, Lawrence Gilliard Jr.
Keywords: heroin, blindfold, bong, perversion, burned alive, independent film
