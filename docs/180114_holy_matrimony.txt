Title: Holy Matrimony (1943)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: John M. Stahl
Main Cast: Monty Woolley, Gracie Fields, Laird Cregar, Una O'Connor, Alan Mowbray
Keywords: artist, valet, assumed identity, spinsters

Overview: An artist returning from years abroad takes the identity of his dead valet and gets married, but then there are complications.
