Title: Poor Pretty Eddie (1975)
Genres: Horror, Drama
Director: Richard Robinson
Main Cast: Leslie Uggams, Shelley Winters, Michael Christian, Slim Pickens, Ted Cassidy
Keywords: rape, redneck, backwoods, car

Overview: A wrong turn on a jazz singer's road trip results in her car breaking down near an isolated lodge run by a faded starlet and a young, homicidal Elvis impersonator.
