Title: The Water Diviner (2014)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Russell Crowe
Main Cast: Russell Crowe, Olga Kurylenko, Yılmaz Erdoğan, Cem Yılmaz, Jai Courtney
Keywords: istanbul, australia, post world war i, farmer, missing in action, missing son, gallipoli campaign, inspired by true events

Overview: In 1919, Australian farmer Joshua Connor travels to Turkey to discover the fate of his three sons, reported missing in action. Holding on to hope, Joshua must travel across the war-torn landscape to find the truth and his own peace.
