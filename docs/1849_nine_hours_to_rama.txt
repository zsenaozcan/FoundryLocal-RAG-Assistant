Title: Nine Hours to Rama (1963)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Mark Robson
Main Cast: Horst Buchholz, José Ferrer, Valerie Gearon, Don Borisenko, Robert Morley
Keywords: assassination, reference to mahatma gandhi, indian history

Overview: José Ferrer and Horst Buchholz star in this fictionalised account of events leading up to the assassination of Indian spiritual leader and independence campaigner Mahatma Gandhi.
