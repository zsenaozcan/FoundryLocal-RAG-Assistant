Title: A Trick of the Light (1995)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Wim Wenders
Main Cast: Daniele Drobny, Florian Gallenberger, Jürgen Jürges, Udo Kier, Otto Kuhnle
Keywords: biography

Overview: A rare gem of cinematic storytelling that weaves docudrama, fictional reenactment, and experimental photography into a powerful, reflective work on the early days of German cinema. The film tells the story of the Skladanowsky Brothers, the German-born duo responsible for inventing the "bioskop", an early version of the film projector.
