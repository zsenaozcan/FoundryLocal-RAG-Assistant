Title: Skylark (1993)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Joseph Sargent
Main Cast: Glenn Close, Christopher Walken, Lexi Randall, Chris Bell, Malgorzata Zajaczkowska
Keywords: famine, farm, kansas, rural setting, drought

Overview: Jacob's farm is in trouble from a severe drought. Jacob and Sarah begin to wonder if Sarah can stay, and what will happen to Jacob if she and the children have to leave the farm.
