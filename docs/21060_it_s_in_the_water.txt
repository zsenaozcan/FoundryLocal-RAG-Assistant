Title: It's in the Water (1997)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Kelli Herd
Main Cast: Keri Jo Chapman, Teresa Garrett, Derrick Sanders, Timothy Vahle, Barbara Lasater
Keywords: gay, aids, homophobia, coming out, lesbian relationship, homosexuality, independent film, lesbian, woman director

Overview: When hordes of gays and lesbians come out "of the closet" in the fictional town of Azalea Springs, Texas, intolerant residents go into a panic about the water supply.
