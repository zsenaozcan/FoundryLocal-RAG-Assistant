Title: Paris Under Watch (2012)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Cédric Jimenez
Main Cast: Mélanie Doutey, Olivier Barthelemy, Francis Renaud, Valérie Sibilia, Féodor Atkine
Keywords: paris, france, camera, security camera, webcam, terrorism, surveillance

Overview: An anonymous hacker has hacked all of Paris' cameras and observes the city unbeknownst to its inhabitants. Petty crimes and moments of stolen intimacy, he sees everything. Until the day that an explosion lays wasted to the Gare d'Austerlitz. The police starts tracking down an Al Queda satellite group. The hacker succeeds in finding images of the explosion and discovers that it was a young couple who planted the bomb. Using the city's cameras, he decides to hunt down the criminals.
