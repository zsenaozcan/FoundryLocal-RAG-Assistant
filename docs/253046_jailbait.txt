Title: Jailbait (2014)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Jared Cohn
Main Cast: Sara Malakul Lane, Samantha Cardona, Erin O'Brien, Jennifer Robyn Jacobs, Rebecca Rowley
Keywords: women in prison

Overview: A gritty coming of age thriller about a young girl sent to juvenile prison for the murder of her abusive step father. The film follows Anna Nix's journey into the dark world of an all girls jail where she discovers complex relationships, drugs, mental illness and her eventual search for redemption.
