Title: Loose Ankles (1930)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Ted Wilde
Main Cast: Loretta Young, Douglas Fairbanks Jr., Louise Fazenda, Otis Harlan, Ethel Wales
Keywords: scandal, uncle, aunt, inheritance

Overview: A young woman tries to scandalize her snotty family by romancing a gigolo. Director Ted Wilde's 1930 comedy stars Loretta Young, Douglas Fairbanks Jr., Louise Fazenda, Ethel Wales and Daphne Pollard.
