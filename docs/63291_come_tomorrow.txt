Title: Come Tomorrow... (1962)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Yevgeni Tashkov
Main Cast: Ekaterina Savinova, Aleksandr Shirvindt, Anatoli Papanov, Yuri Belov, Antonina Maksimova
Keywords: musical

Overview: Young girl from the country come to Moscow, she wants to get into University, but it's too late... Anyway, she won't give up and will do everything to get a meeting with dean and sing for him.
