Title: Firecracker (2005)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Steve Balderson
Main Cast: Karen Black, Mike Patton, Susan Traylor, Kathleen Wilhoite, Jak Kendall

Overview: A tale of murder in small-town Kansas. When Jimmy is lured away from his abusive family by a traveling sideshow carnival, he encounters Sandra, one of the main attractions. The happiness they find together causes them to confront the darkness in their lives
