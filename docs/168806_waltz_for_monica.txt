Title: Waltz for Monica (2013)
Genres: Music, Drama
Director: Per Fly
Main Cast: Edda Magnason, Sverrir Gudnason, Kjell Bergqvist, Vera Vitali, Cecilia Ljung
Keywords: biography, single mother, jazz singer, nightclub singer

Overview: "Monica Z" is a biopic about the Swedish singer and actress Monica Zetterlund focusing on her journey from a job as a telephone operator in a small town in Sweden to stardom in the clubs of New York and Stockholm.
