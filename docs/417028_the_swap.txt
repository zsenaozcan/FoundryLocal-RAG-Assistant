Title: The Swap (2016)
Genres: Comedy, TV Movie
Director: Jay Karas
Main Cast: Peyton List, Jacob Bertrand, Claire Rankin, Callan Potter, Jesse Bostick
Keywords: high school, ice hockey, body-swap, based on young adult novel, body switching, rhythmic gymnastics

Overview: Based on the book by Megan Shull, The Swap follows the adventures of a Rhythmic Gymnast named Ellie with a Make-it-or-Break-it Competition, and the younger brother named Jack in a Hockey Family, who's vying for a varsity spot on his school team. But when a Simple Text causes the two to Swap Bodies, their paths take an Unexpected Cross.
