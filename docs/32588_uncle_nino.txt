Title: Uncle Nino (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Robert Shallcross
Main Cast: Joe Mantegna, Anne Archer, Pierrino Mascarino, Trevor Morgan, Gia Mantegna

Overview: A distant, slightly dysfunctional family is brought closer together when the father's long-estranged Uncle Nino comes from Italy to Chicago for a surprise visit.
