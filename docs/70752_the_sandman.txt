Title: The Sandman (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Peter Luisi
Main Cast: Fabian Krüger, Irene Brügger, Beat Schlatter, Florine Elena Deplazes, Kaspar Weiss

Overview: One fine morning, Benno finds sand in his bed. While he tries to ignore this at first, he soon must realize that he himself is loosing the sand.
