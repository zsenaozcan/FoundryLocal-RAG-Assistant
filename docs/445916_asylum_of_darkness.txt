Title: Asylum of Darkness (2017)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jay Woelfel
Main Cast: Tim Thomerson, Richard Hatch, Tiffany Shepis, Scott Summitt, Nick Baldasare
Keywords: supernatural, remake, escape, madness

Overview: After awakening in a mental asylum, a patient plans an escape to freedom, but finds an even more disturbing, supernatural world on the outside, one that threatens to keep him trapped in madness forever.
