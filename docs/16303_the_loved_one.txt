Title: The Loved One (1965)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Tony Richardson
Main Cast: Robert Morse, Jonathan Winters, Anjanette Comer, Dana Andrews, Milton Berle
Keywords: funeral home

Overview: Newly arrived in Hollywood from England, Dennis Barlow finds he has to arrange his uncle's interment at the highly-organised and very profitable Whispering Glades funeral parlour. His fancy is caught by one of their cosmeticians, Aimee Thanatogenos. But he has three problems - the strict rules of owner Blessed Reverand Glenworthy, the rivalry of embalmer Mr Joyboy, and the shame of now working himself at The Happy Hunting Ground pets' memorial home.
