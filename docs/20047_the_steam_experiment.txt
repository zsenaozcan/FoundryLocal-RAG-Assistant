Title: The Steam Experiment (2009)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Philippe Martinez
Main Cast: Val Kilmer, Armand Assante, Eric Roberts, Megan Brown, Patrick Muldoon
Keywords: global warming, steam room

Overview: A deranged scientist locks 6 people in a steam room and threatens to turn up the heat if the local paper doesn't publish his story about global warming.
