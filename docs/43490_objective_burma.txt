Title: Objective, Burma! (1945)
Genres: Action, Drama, War
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: Errol Flynn, James Brown, William Prince, George Tobias, Henry Hull

Overview: A group of men parachute into Japanese-occupied Burma with a dangerous and important mission: to locate and blow up a radar station. They accomplish this well enough, but when they try to rendezvous at an old air-strip to be taken back to their base, they find Japanese waiting for them, and they must make a long, difficult walk back through enemy-occupied jungle.
