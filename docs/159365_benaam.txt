Title: Benaam (1974)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Mystery
Director: Narendra Bedi
Main Cast: Amitabh Bachchan, Moushumi Chatterjee, Satyendra Kapoor, Madan Puri, Iftekhar

Overview: After helping a stabbing victim to the hospital, a man begins to receive anonymous threats. The only way to save his wife and child is to unravel the mystery of the killer's identity.
