Title: Beyond Justice (1992)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action, Thriller, Foreign
Director: Duccio Tessari
Main Cast: Rutger Hauer, Carol Alt, Omar Sharif, Donald Hodson, Kabir Bedi

Overview: A woman's ex-husband, who is the son of an Arab chieftain, kidnaps their teenage son and brings him back to Morocco, where the boy is to be made the leader of the tribe. The child's mother hires a mercenary to get her son and bring him back to her.
