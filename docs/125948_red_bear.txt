Title: Red Bear (2002)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Adrián Caetano
Main Cast: Julio Chávez, Soledad Villamil

Overview: Bear has never gotten over the separation from his wife and daughter after having been convicted for armed robbery and homicide and sent to prison. Now he is out, to finally get his cut of the money and reunite with his family, or at least avenge the damage done.
