Title: The Amazons (1973)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: Terence Young
Main Cast: Alena Johnston, Sabine Sun, Rosanna Yanni, Helga Liné, Malisa Longo
Keywords: sword fight, sword and sandal, amazon women

Overview: lena Johnston is Antione, the War Goddess of the Amazons, who must mate with the Greek king Theseus to propogate her race. Traditionally this is done only out of duty, but this time she falls in love with her "boy toy"... a definite no-no for an Amazon queen. Helmed by James Bond director Terence Young and based on a story co-authored by Robert Graves, this sexy and spectacular entry in the sword-and-sandal genre features an incredible bevy of Euro-cult starlets including Rosanna Yanni, Helga Line and Luciana Paluzzi, plus a lyrical score by Academy Award-winner Riz Ortolani.
