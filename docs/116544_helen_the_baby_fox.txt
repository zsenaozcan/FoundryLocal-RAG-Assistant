Title: Helen the Baby Fox (2006)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Keita Kono
Main Cast: Takao Osawa, Yasuko Matsuyuki, Arashi Fukasawa, Ryoko Kobayashi, Shunji Fujimura

Overview: A young boy named Taichi Ogawara finds a fox cub on the side of the road and takes it home as a pet. He discovers it is blind, deaf, and mute. Because of this, he names it Helen, after Helen Keller.
