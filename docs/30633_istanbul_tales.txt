Title: Istanbul Tales (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Erdem Akakce, Azra Akın, Erkan Can, Şevket Çoruh, Altan Erkekli

Overview: Five interconnected stories set in modern-day Istanbul based on the fairytales Snow White, Cinderella, Pied Piper, Sleeping Beauty and Little Red Riding Hood.
