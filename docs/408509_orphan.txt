Title: Orphan (2017)
Genres: Drama
Director: Arnaud des Pallières
Main Cast: Adèle Exarchopoulos, Adèle Haenel, Solène Rigot, Gemma Arterton, Jalil Lespert

Overview: A young woman who moves to Paris and has a brush with disaster. The grown-up at last, an accomplished woman, who thought she was safe from her own past. Gradually, these characters come together to form a single heroine.
