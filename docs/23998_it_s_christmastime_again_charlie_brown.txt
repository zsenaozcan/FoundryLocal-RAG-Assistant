Title: It's Christmastime Again, Charlie Brown (1992)
Genres: Animation
Director: Bill Melendez
Main Cast: Jamie E. Smith, John Christian Graas, Marnette Patterson, Mindy Ann Martin, Phillip Lucier
Keywords: holiday

Overview: As the holiday season rolls around and all the Peanuts gang are getting ready for it. Whether it be Charlie Brown struggling to raise money for his girlfriend or Sally and Peppermint Patty struggling to rehearse and memorize their one word lines for the Christmas pageant, these kids try to keep with the Christmas spirit while Snoopy has his mischief to do.
