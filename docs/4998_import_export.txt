Title: Import/Export (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ulrich Seidl
Main Cast: Ekateryna Rak, Paul Hofmann, Michael Thomas, Susanne Lothar, Natalya Baranova
Keywords: ukraine, pflegeheim, exploitation, independent film, teacher, sexual violence, unsimulated sex, illegal prostitution, unemployment, emigrant

Overview: A nurse from the Ukraine searches for a better life in the West, while an unemployed security guard from Austria heads East for the same reason. Both are looking for work, a new beginning, an existence, struggling to believe in themselves, to find a meaning in life..
