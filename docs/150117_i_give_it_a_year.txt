Title: I Give It a Year (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Dan Mazer
Main Cast: Rafe Spall, Rose Byrne, Anna Faris, Jason Flemyng, Stephen Merchant
Keywords: marriage, newlywed, first anniversary

Overview: After a quick courtship, two lovers hastily decide to tie the knot. As their first year of marriage unfolds, temptation and incompatibility put their relationship in jeopardy.
