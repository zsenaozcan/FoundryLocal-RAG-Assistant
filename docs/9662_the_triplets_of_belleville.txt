Title: The Triplets of Belleville (2003)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Comedy, Drama
Director: Sylvain Chomet
Main Cast: Béatrice Bonifassi, Lina Boudreau, Michèle Caucheteux, Jean-Claude Donda, Mari-Lou Gauthier
Keywords: france, kidnapping, boy, biker, tour de france, mafia, dog, triplet, band singer, silent film

Overview: When her grandson is kidnapped during the Tour de France, Madame Souza and her beloved pooch Bruno team up with the Belleville Sisters--an aged song-and-dance team from the days of Fred Astaire--to rescue him.
