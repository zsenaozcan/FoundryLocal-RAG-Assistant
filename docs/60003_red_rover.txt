Title: Red Rover (2006)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Marc S. Grenier
Main Cast: Jodi Lyn O'Keefe, William Baldwin, Brenda James, Francis X. McCarthy, Heidi von Palleske

Overview: Two siblings played by William Baldin and Jodi Lyn O'Keefe travel to their families old, ancestrial estate upon the death of their father. They soon find out the hard way about their families ancient secret of witchcraft and the occult.
