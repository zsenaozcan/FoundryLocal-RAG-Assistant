Title: Support Your Local Gunfighter (1971)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Western
Director: Burt Kennedy
Main Cast: James Garner, Suzanne Pleshette, Jack Elam, Harry Morgan, Joan Blondell

Overview: A con artist arrives in a mining town controlled by two competing companies. Both companies think he's a famous gunfighter and try to hire him to drive the other out of town.
