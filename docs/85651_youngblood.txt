Title: Youngblood (1978)
Genres: Drama
Director: Noel Nosseck
Main Cast: Lawrence Hilton-Jacobs, Bryan O'Dell, Renn Woods, Tony Allen, Vince Cannon
Keywords: ghetto, coming of age, gang, drug, social issues

Overview: A gang war/drug story set in a Los Angeles ghetto about the coming of age of a 15-year-old black youth.
