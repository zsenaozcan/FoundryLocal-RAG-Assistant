Title: Grbavica: The Land of My Dreams (2006)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Jasmila Zbanic
Main Cast: Mirjana Karanović, Luna Mijovic, Leon Lučev, Kenan Catic, Jasna Beri
Keywords: rape, depression, war crimes, sense of guilt, becoming an adult, suppressed past, sarajevo, bosnia and herzegovina, right and justice, mother daughter relationship
