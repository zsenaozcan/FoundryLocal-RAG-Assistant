Title: Love, Rosie (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Christian Ditter
Main Cast: Lily Collins, Sam Claflin, Suki Waterhouse, Christian Cooke, Tamsin Egerton
Keywords: based on novel, male female relationship, teenage pregnancy, best friends in love, missed opportunity

Overview: Rosie and Alex are best friends. They are suddenly separated when Alex and his family move from Dublin to America. Can their friendship survive years and miles? Will they gamble everything for true love?
