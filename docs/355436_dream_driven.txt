Title: Dream Driven (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Timo Peltokangas
Main Cast: Arman Alizad, Mikael Hautala, Misa Jokisalo, Heikki Lamula, Juho Leppänen

Overview: 3 young men. 19 countries. 20,000 kilometers. 1 social movement.  Juho dreams of a more just world. During an operation in Asia, he has been in contact with the Nepalese Dalit population's poor conditions and have not been able to forget what he saw. Juho wants to find a way to help them.
