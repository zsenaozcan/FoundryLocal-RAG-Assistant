Title: The Mysterious Lady (1928)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Harold S. Bucquet
Main Cast: Greta Garbo, Conrad Nagel, Gustav von Seyffertitz, Albert Pollet, Edward Connelly
Keywords: silent film, russian spy, austrian officer, ploy

Overview: A beautiful Russian spy seduces an Austrian military officer in order to obtain secret plans. When she falls in love with him, both are placed in danger.
