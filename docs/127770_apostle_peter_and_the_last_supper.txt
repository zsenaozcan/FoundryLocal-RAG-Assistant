Title: Apostle Peter and the Last Supper (2012)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Gabriel Sabloff
Main Cast: Robert Loggia, Bruce Marchiano, Laurence Fuller, Ryan Alosio, Sarah Prikryl
Keywords: prison, jesus christ, apostle, last supper, conversion

Overview: The film portrays Saint Peter reflecting on his time with Jesus and his fellow Apostles during his final imprisonment in Rome. In particular, Peter attempts to convert one of his jailers, Martinian, by relating the life, teachings, and sacrifice of Jesus.
