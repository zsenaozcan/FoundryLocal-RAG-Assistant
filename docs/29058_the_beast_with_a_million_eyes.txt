Title: The Beast with a Million Eyes (1958)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Roger Corman
Main Cast: Paul Birch, Lorna Thayer, Dona Cole, Dick Sargent, Chester Conklin
Keywords: california, farm, extraterrestrial, alien, dysfunctional family, independent film, mute, bird attack, dog, desert

Overview: An alien space craft lands in the desert. The alien takes over the minds of some of the local humans and animals and is able to see through them. The animals attack and the terror begins.
