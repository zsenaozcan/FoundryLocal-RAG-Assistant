Title: Cencoroll (2009)
Genres: Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Atsuya Uki
Main Cast: Kana Hanazawa, Hiro Shimono, Ryohei Kimura, Satomi Moriya
Keywords: japan, supernatural powers, alien, aftercreditsstinger, contemporary fantasy, short

Overview: A giant monster sends a town's citizens into a panic, except for a girl named Yuki and her schoolmate Tetsu. Tetsu happens to have his own strange creature named Cenco as a pet. Another boy named Shuu controls the monster threatening the town, and the stage is set for a battle.
