Title: House Calls (1978)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Howard Zieff
Main Cast: Walter Matthau, Art Carney, Richard Benjamin, Candice Azzara, Dick O'Neill

Overview: Charley is a surgeon who's recently lost his wife; he embarks on a tragicomic romantic quest with one woman after another until he meets up with Ann, a singular woman, closer to his own age, who immediately and unexpectedly captures his heart.
