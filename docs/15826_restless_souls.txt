Title: Restless Souls (2005)
Genres: Horror, Drama
Director: Martin Schmidt
Main Cast: Andrea Vagn Jensen, Anne Birgitte Lind, Jakob Cedergren, Dejan Čukić, Thure Lindhardt
Keywords: danish horror

Overview: A young couple and their daughter move into a rambling old house. Soon, an increasingly alarming string of events and supernatural disturbances connects the house, and them, with a series of unsolved murders committed three years earlier. They are the only living witnesses, but for how long?
