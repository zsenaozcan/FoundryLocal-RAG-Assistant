Title: Rest Stop (2006)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: John Shiban
Main Cast: Jaimie Alexander, Joey Mendicino, Deanna Russo, Diane Salinger, Curtis Taylor
Keywords: roadhouse, lorry, truck driver

Overview: The film follows Nicole Carrow, a young woman who is threatened by a maniac serial killer, after her boyfriend Jess, is abducted in a rest stop.
