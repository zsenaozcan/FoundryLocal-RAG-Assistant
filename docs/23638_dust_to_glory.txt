Title: Dust to Glory (2005)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Dana Brown
Main Cast: Mario Andretti, Sal Fish, James Garner, Robby Gordon, Mike McCoy
Keywords: competition, wilderness, racing, independent film

Overview: An action-adventure documentary chronicling the most notorious and dangerous race in the world--the Tecate SCORE Baja 1000. Rivaling the Indy 500 and 25 Hours of Daytona, the race across Baja's peninsula is unpredictable, grueling and raw--just like the uncharted American West of yesteryear.
