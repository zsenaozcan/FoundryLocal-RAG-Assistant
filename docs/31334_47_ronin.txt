Title: 47 Ronin (1994)
Genres: Action, Drama, Foreign
Director: Kon Ichikawa
Main Cast: Ken Takakura, Kiichi Nakai, Rie Miyazawa, Kôji Ishizaka, Ruriko Asaoka
Keywords: samurai

Overview: Kon Ichikawa's retelling of the classic true story of Samurai honor. When a young clan lord is forced to commit seppuku (ritual suicide), his loyal followers (now Ronin, masterless Samurai) dedicate their lives to avenging his death.
