Title: Shikshanachya Aaicha Gho (2010)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mahesh Manjrekar
Main Cast: Bharat Jadhav, Siddharth Jadhav, Sachin Khedekar, Saksham Kulkarni, Mahesh Manjrekar

Overview: Shikshanacha Aaicha Gho is a 2010 Indian Marathi film directed by Mahesh Manjrekar starring Sachin Khedekar, Bharat Jadhav, Saksham Kulkarni, Gauri Vaidya, Siddharth Jadhav and Kranti Redkar. The film was released on 15 January 2010.[2] Films music composed by trio Ajit-Atul-Sameer. After Astitva with 9 years long gap Mahesh Manjrekar directed Marathi film.This film was later remade in Tamil &amp; Telugu as Dhoni and in Bengali as "Chalo Paltai" starring Prosenjit Chatterjee.
