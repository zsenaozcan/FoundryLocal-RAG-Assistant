Title: The Bachelor (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Antonis Sotiropoulos
Main Cast: Yannis Zouganelis, Yannis Tsimitselis, Katerina Geronikolou, Apostolis Totsikas, Thanasis Viskadourakis
Keywords: bachelor

Overview: A mistake made by the bride rejoins 4 old friends of disaster and a father in law! 5 guys go to Thassaloniki to tear everything down. A father in law gets in a hearse in order to kill a love in the making. An ex-girlfriend dances tango with her memories as a partner. A cancelled wedding and a relationship that starts with the brides vail floating at the wind of independence.
