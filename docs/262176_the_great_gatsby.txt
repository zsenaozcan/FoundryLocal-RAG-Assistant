Title: The Great Gatsby (1926)
Genres: Drama
Director: Herbert Brenon
Main Cast: Warner Baxter, Lois Wilson, Neil Hamilton, Georgia Hale, William Powell
Keywords: tragic love

Overview: Nick Carraway, a young Midwesterner now living on Long Island, finds himself fascinated by the mysterious past and lavish lifestyle of his neighbor, the nouveau riche Jay Gatsby. He is drawn into Gatsby's circle, becoming a witness to obsession and tragedy.
