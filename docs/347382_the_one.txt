Title: The One (2015)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Kirill Belevich
Main Cast: Andrey Merzlikin, Ilya Korobko, Anna Prus, Arina Borisova, Mikhail Evlanov

Overview: During WWII a Soviet artillery unit is fighting Nazis while trying to do everything to save lives of a group of deaf children near their position.
