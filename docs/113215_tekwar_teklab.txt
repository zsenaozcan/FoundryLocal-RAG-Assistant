Title: TekWar: TekLab (1994)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Timothy Bond
Main Cast: Greg Evigan, Eugene Clark, William Shatner, Michael York, Joel Bissonnette
Keywords: futuristic

Overview: The actual sword of Excalibur has been stolen in London, and futuristic detectives Jake Cardigan and Sid Gomez are assigned to track it down and to find out who is trying to block the British reign from its rightful heir.
