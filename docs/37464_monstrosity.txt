Title: Monstrosity (1963)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Joseph V. Mascelli
Main Cast: Marjorie Eaton, Frank Gerstle, Frank Fowler, Erika Peters, Judy Bamber
Keywords: transplantation, cat, experiment, murder, mad scientist, independent film, grave robbing, brain transplant

Overview: A rich but unscrupulous old woman plots with a scientist to have her brain implanted in the skull of a sexy young woman.
