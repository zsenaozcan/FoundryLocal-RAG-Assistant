Title: American Gun (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alan Jacobs
Main Cast: James Coburn, Virginia Madsen, Barbara Bain, Alexandra Holden, Brittany Krall
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A brilliant story with an unexpected twist. A father uses the serial number on a hand gun in an attempt to track down his daughter's killer. The truth he uncovers is an unsettling reminder of the dangers of keeping handguns. Stars the legendary James Coburn and the incredibly gifted Virginia Madsen. Written and directed by Alan Jacobs.
