Title: Car Trouble (1986)
Genres: Unknown
Director: David Green
Main Cast: Julie Walters, Ian Charleson, Vincenzo Ricotta, Stratford Johns, Hazel O'Connor

Overview: In the throes of a midlife crisis, a man buys a new Jaguar, and it immediately becomes his new love. What he doesn't know is that his wife is as attracted to the Jaguar salesman as he is to the car.
