Title: The Wild One (1953)
Genres: Drama
Director: László Benedek
Main Cast: Marlon Brando, Mary Murphy, Robert Keith, Lee Marvin, Timothy Carey
Keywords: teenager, motorcycle, motorcycle gang

Overview: A gang of forty motorcyclists, the Black Rebels Motorcycle Club, gate-crash a legitimate motorcycle race. They are eventually thrown out, but one of the gang steals the second prize trophy and gives it to their leader, Johnny.
