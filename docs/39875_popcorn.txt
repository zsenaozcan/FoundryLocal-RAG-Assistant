Title: Popcorn (1991)
Genres: Horror
Director: Mark Herrier
Main Cast: Jill Schoelen, Dee Wallace, Kelly Jo Minter, Karen Lorre, Ray Walston
Keywords: revenge, murder, serial killer, student, series of murders, movie theater
