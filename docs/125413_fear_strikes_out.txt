Title: Fear Strikes Out (1957)
Genres: Drama
Director: Robert Mulligan
Main Cast: Anthony Perkins, Karl Malden, Norma Moore, Adam Williams, Perry Wilson
Keywords: baseball, red sox, mental health

Overview: Director Robert Mulligan's 1957 biographical drama stars Anthony Perkins as Jimmy Piersall, the baseball player whose career was interrupted by his battle with mental illness. The cast also includes Karl Malden, Norma Moore, Adam Williams and Perry Wilson.
