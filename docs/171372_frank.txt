Title: Frank (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Mystery
Director: Lenny Abrahamson
Main Cast: Michael Fassbender, Maggie Gyllenhaal, Domhnall Gleeson, Scoot McNairy, Hayley Derryberry
Keywords: identity, musician, music, friendship, mysterious, mental problems

Overview: A comedy about a young wannabe musician (Domhnall Gleeson) who discovers he has bitten off more than he can chew when he joins an eccentric pop band led by the mysterious and enigmatic Frank (Michael Fassbender).
