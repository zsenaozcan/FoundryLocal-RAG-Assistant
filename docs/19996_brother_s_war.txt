Title: Brother's War (2009)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Jerry Buteyn
Main Cast: Tino Struckmann, Hugh Daly, Hayley Carr, Olivier Gruner, Michael Berryman
Keywords: world war ii, society, freemason, friendship, war, slaughter, russian front, eastern front

Overview: The story is set in the latter days of World War 2, against the backdrop of fierce combat on the eastern front. Brother's War is based on real events.
