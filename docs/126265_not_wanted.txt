Title: Not Wanted (1949)
Genres: Drama
Director: Elmer Clifton
Main Cast: Sally Forrest, Keefe Brasselle, Leo Penn, Dorothy Adams, Wheaton Chambers
Keywords: unwed pregnancy, woman director

Overview: After a beautiful but unsophisticated girl is seduced by a worldly piano player and gives up her out-of-wedlock baby, her guilt compels her to kidnap another child.
