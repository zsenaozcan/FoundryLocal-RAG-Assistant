Title: In the Good Old Summertime (1949)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Buster Keaton
Main Cast: Judy Garland, Van Johnson, Spring Byington, S.Z. Sakall, Clinton Sundberg
Keywords: musical, pen pals, romantic comedy, music store

Overview: In this musical remake of The Shop Around the Corner, feuding co-workers in a small music shop do not realize they are secret romantic pen pals.
