Title: APT. (2006)
Genres: Horror
Director: Ahn Byeong-ki
Main Cast: Ko So-Young, Kang Seong-jin, Jang Hee-jin, Park Ha-sun, Ha-eun Kim
Keywords: apartment, voyeurism

Overview: Se-jin, a lonely department store employee, moves into a high-rise apartment building. One day she notices that the lights in the windows of the building opposite all start to mysteriously flicker at exactly 9:56pm. She begins to suspect that these occurrences are somehow linked to a series of suspicious deaths in the neighbourhood.
