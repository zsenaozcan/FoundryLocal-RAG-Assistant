Title: The Postman Always Rings Twice (1981)
Genres: Romance, Crime, Drama
Director: Bob Rafelson
Main Cast: Jack Nicholson, Jessica Lange, John Colicos, Michael Lerner, Anjelica Huston
Keywords: sex, adultery, confession, based on novel, obsession, nudity, remake, murder, suspense, conspiracy

Overview: This remake of the 1946 movie of the same name accounts an affair between a seedy drifter and a seductive wife of a roadside cafe owner. This begins a chain of events that culminates in murder. Based on a novel by James M. Cain.
