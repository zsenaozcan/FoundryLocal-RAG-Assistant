Title: The Winning of Barbara Worth (1926)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Western
Director: Henry King
Main Cast: Ronald Colman, Vilma Banky, Gary Cooper, Charles Willis Lane, Paul McAllister
Keywords: water, engineer, wasteland, desert, silent film, land developer, irrigation

Overview: While building an irrigation system for a Southwestern desert community, an engineer vies with a local cowboy for the affections of a rancher's daughter.
