Title: Domingo de carnaval (1945)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Edgar Neville
Main Cast: Conchita Montes, Guillermo Marín, Fernando Fernán Gómez, Julia Lajos, Juana Mansó

Overview: The same morning that carnival begins, a serene finds in Madrid the corpse of a rich and greedy lender who has been murdered.
