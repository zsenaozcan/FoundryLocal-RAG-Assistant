Title: Zombie Christ (2010)
Genres: Horror
Director: Bill Zebub
Main Cast: Jessica Alexandra Green, Jordana Leigh, Caitlin Burdi, Taylor Trash, Kathy Rice
Keywords: skeleton, druid

Overview: Druids resurrect the partially mummified remains of Jesus. They force the zombified Christ to commit unspeakable acts as their sect prepares to rule a new age of mankind.
