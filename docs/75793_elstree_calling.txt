Title: Elstree Calling (1930)
Genres: Music, Comedy
Director: Alfred Hitchcock
Main Cast: Anna May Wong, Will Fyffe, Lily Morris, Gordon Begg, Tommy Handley
Keywords: musical, vaudeville, blackface

Overview: A series of 19 musical and comedy "vaudeville" sketches presented in the form of a live broadcast hosted by Tommy Handley (as himself). There are two "running gags" which connect the sketches. In one, an actor wants to perform Shakespeare, but he is continually denied air-time. The other gag has an inventor trying to view the broadcast on television.
