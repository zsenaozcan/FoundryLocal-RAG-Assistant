Title: The Exclusive : Beat the Devil's Tattoo (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: No Deok
Main Cast: Jo Jung-suk, Lee Ha-Na, Lee Mi-sook, Tae In-ho, Bae Seong-woo

Overview: An unsuccessful journalist becomes a star in the media after he wrote an article about the memo of an unknown serial killer. As he learns that the memo was just part of a novel, he decides to cover up everything.
