Title: Wild Side (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sébastien Lifshitz
Main Cast: Stéphanie Michelini, Yasmine Belmadi, Edouard Nikitine, Josiane Stoléru
Keywords: paris, prostitute, transsexuality, eroticism, mother daughter relationship, prostitution

Overview: A transsexual who survives prostituting herself in Paris, returns – with her two male lovers in town – to her family home in the countryside to look after her dying mother.
