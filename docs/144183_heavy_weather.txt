Title: Heavy Weather (1995)
Genres: Comedy, TV Movie
Director: Jack Gold
Main Cast: Judy Parfitt, Roy Hudd, Richard Briers, Peter O'Toole, David Bamber
Keywords: country estate

Overview: At Blandings Castle, the Earl of Emsworth only cares about his prize pig 'The Empress' and is wilfully ignorant of the fact that his brother is planning to publish a book which might ruin the family name forever. Moreover, the Earl's nephew might cause the family some major damage by getting married to a terribly unsuitable chorus girl. An adaptation of P.G. Wodehouse's novel of the same name.
