Title: Gifted Hands: The Ben Carson Story (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Thomas Carter
Main Cast: Cuba Gooding Jr., Kimberly Elise, Aunjanue Ellis, Ele Bardha, Loren Bass
Keywords: biography

Overview: Gifted Hands: The Ben Carson Story is a movie based on the life story of world-renowned neurosurgeon Ben Carson from 1961 to 1987.
