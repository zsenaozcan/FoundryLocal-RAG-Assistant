Title: Beau Brummell: This Charming Man (2006)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Philippa Lowthorpe
Main Cast: James Purefoy, Hugh Bonneville, Phil Davis, Elliot Levey, Zoe Telford
Keywords: dandy, woman director

Overview: This BBC historical drama stars James Purefoy as Beau Brummell, the original sharp-dressed dandy of 18th-century London. A socialite responsible for inventing the modern suit, Brummell befriends and restyles Prince Regent of Wales.
