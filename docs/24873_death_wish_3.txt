Title: Death Wish 3 (1985)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Michael Winner
Main Cast: Charles Bronson, Deborah Raffin, Ed Lauter, Martin Balsam, Gavin O'Herlihy
Keywords: rocket launcher, punk, vigilante, revenge

Overview: Paul Kersey returns to New York to visit his friend Charley, who lives in one of the worst parts of New York City. But when Paul arrives at Charley's apartment, he finds Charley dying after a vicious beating by a gang led by Manny Fraker, and the police enter the apartment and find Paul standing over Charley's body. Paul is arrested for the murder, but police chief Richard S. Shriker is like Paul.
