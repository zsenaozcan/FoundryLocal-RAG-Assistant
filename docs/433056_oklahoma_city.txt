Title: Oklahoma City (2017)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Barak Goodman
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: terrorism, bombing

Overview: The bombing of the Alfred P. Murrah Federal Building in Oklahoma City in April 1995 is the worst act of domestic terrorism in American history. This documentary explores how a series of deadly encounters between American citizens and federal law enforcement—including the standoffs at Ruby Ridge and Waco—led to it.
