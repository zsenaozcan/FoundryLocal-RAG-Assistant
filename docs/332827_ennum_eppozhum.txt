Title: Ennum Eppozhum (2015)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Sathyan Anthikad
Main Cast: Mohanlal, Manju Warrier, Innocent, Jacob Gregory, Reenu Mathews
Keywords: photographer, interview, lawyer, divorce, malayalam

Overview: Vineeth, a senior reporter, tries to interview Deepa, a junior family court lawyer for the new edition of a magazine. However, the interview seems impossible due to Deepa's hectic schedule.
