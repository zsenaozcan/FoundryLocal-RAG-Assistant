Title: Buried Secrets (1996)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: Michael Toshiyuki Uno
Main Cast: Tiffani Thiessen, Tim Matheson, Melinda Culea, Erika Flores, Kelly Rutherford
Keywords: haunted house, murder, mother daughter relationship, dead father

Overview: A young woman moves with her mother to her mother's hometown and is haunted by the ghost of a dead teenager.
