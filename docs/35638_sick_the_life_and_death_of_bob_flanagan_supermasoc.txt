Title: Sick: The Life and Death of Bob Flanagan, Supermasochist (1997)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Kirby Dick
Main Cast: Bob Flanagan, Kirby Dick, Kathe Burkhart, Sheree Rose, Rita Valencia
Keywords: fetishism, funeral, sadomasochism, terminal illness, documentary, disease, bdsm, terminal illnessdying man
