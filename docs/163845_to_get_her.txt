Title: To.get.her (2011)
Genres: Mystery, Drama
Director: Erica Dunton
Main Cast: Jazzy De Lisser, Chelsea Logan, Adwoa Aboah, Audrey Speicher, Jami Eaton
Keywords: suspense, woman director

Overview: Five teenage girls with a shared secret get together for a weekend of "No Consequences." The sex, drugs and rock n'roll they intend pale in comparison to the more dangerous path they find themselves on.
