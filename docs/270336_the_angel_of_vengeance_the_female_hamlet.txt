Title: The Angel of Vengeance - The Female Hamlet (1977)
Genres: Drama
Director: Metin Erksan
Main Cast: Fatma Girik, Sevda Ferdag, Reha Yurdakul, Ahmet Sezerel
Keywords: foreign language adaptation, hamlet

Overview: An extraordinary interpretation of Hamlet from the phenomenal director of the Turkish Cinema: Metin Erksan. In this presentation Hamlet is female and search vengeance of her father. The relotionship of mother and son has a metamorphosis in that situation and force She-Hamlet to look for the lost father-and-daughter affection.
