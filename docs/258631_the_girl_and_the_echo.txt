Title: The Girl and The Echo (1964)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Arūnas Žebriūnas
Main Cast: Lina Braknyte, Valeri Zubarev, Bronius Babkauskas, Hugo Laur
Keywords: children

Overview: The movie, based on a story by Yuri Nagibin, depicts a young girl Vika enjoying the last days of summer vacations in a sea resort somewhere in the south.
