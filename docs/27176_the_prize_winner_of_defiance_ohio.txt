Title: The Prize Winner of Defiance, Ohio (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jane Anderson
Main Cast: Julianne Moore, Woody Harrelson, Laura Dern, Trevor Morgan, Ellary Porterfield
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Based on the true story of Evelyn Ryan, a midwestern housewife who managed to rescue her family from debt by entering contests for ad slogans sponsored by consumer product companies.
