Title: Double Or Nothing (1937)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Theodore Reed
Main Cast: Bing Crosby, Martha Raye, Andy Devine, Mary Carlisle, William Frawley

Overview: In this lively musical, an eccentric philanthropist's will dictates that four people receive $5,000 with the stipulation that the first one who can double the amount-- without dishonesty-- will win a cool million. Hindering the four are the avaricious relatives of the late millionaire. Songs include: "It's On, It's Off," "Double or Nothing," "Listen My Children," "Smarty," "The Moon Got in My Eyes" and "After You."
