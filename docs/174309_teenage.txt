Title: Teenage (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Matt Wolf
Main Cast: Ben Whishaw, Jena Malone, Julia Hummer, Jessie Usher, Ben Rosenfield

Overview: Teenagers did not exist before the 20th century. Not until the early 1950s did the term gain widespread recognition, but with Teenage, Matt Wolf offers compelling evidence that “teenagers” had a tumultuous effect on the previous half-decade.
