Title: Enemy Empire (2013)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Science Fiction, Western
Director: Michael Ryan Hahn
Main Cast: Tristan James Butler, Elisabeth Meurer
Keywords: surreal, post-apocalyptic

Overview: Sol, a bold fugitive lost in a dangerous post-apocalyptic desert world, searches for a missing woman named Catherine and her illusive captor, the Nomad King. With enemies at every turn, Sol's only chance of finding Catherine is with the help of a rogue and mysterious wanderer, Cleo.
