Title: Bill Burr: You People Are All The Same (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jay Karas
Main Cast: Bill Burr
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Taking the stage in Washington, D.C., funnyman Bill Burr brings his stinging brand of humor to the spotlight, uncorking a profanity-laced, incisive routine that pokes fun at plastic surgery, reality TV, gold diggers and more.
