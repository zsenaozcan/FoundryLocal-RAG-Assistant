Title: Lost on Journey (2010)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Raymond Yip Wai-Man
Main Cast: Xu Zheng, Wang Baoqiang, Li Man, Li Xiaolu, Zuo Xiaoqing
Keywords: road movie

Overview: A boss of a toy corporation, Chenggong Li, tries to head back to Chan Sar to celebrate the Chinese New Year with his family. However, plans don't go as smoothly after he crosses paths with a stranger, Geng Niu.
