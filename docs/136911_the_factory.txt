Title: The Factory (2012)
Genres: Crime, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Morgan O'Neill
Main Cast: John Cusack, Jennifer Carpenter, Mae Whitman, Dallas Roberts, Sonya Walger
Keywords: kidnapping, betrayal, serial killer, police detective, psychotic villain

Overview: An obsessed cop is on the trail of a serial killer prowling the streets of Buffalo, N.Y. but when his teenage daughter disappears, he drops any professional restraint to get the killer.
