Title: Marie-Jo and Her 2 Lovers (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: Robert Guédiguian
Main Cast: Ariane Ascaride, Jean-Pierre Darroussin, Julie-Marie Parmentier, Jacques Boudet, Yann Trégouët
Keywords: love triangle, wife husband relationship, woman between two men, extramarital affair

Overview: Marie-Jo and Her Two Lovers (French: Marie-Jo et ses deux amours) is a 2002 French drama film directed by Robert Guédiguian. It was entered into the 2002 Cannes Film Festival.
