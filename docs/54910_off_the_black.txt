Title: Off the Black (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: James Ponsoldt
Main Cast: Nick Nolte, Trevor Morgan, Rosemarie DeWitt, Noah Fleiss, Johnathan Tchaikovsky
Keywords: independent film

Overview: An aging, disillusioned alcoholic (Nick Nolte) gets a younger friend (Trevor Morgan) and wants him to pose as his son at a school reunion.
