Title: Winnie-the-Pooh Goes Visiting (1971)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Fyodor Khitruk
Main Cast: Evgeni Leonov, Iya Savvina, Anatoliy Shchukin, Vladimir Osenev
Keywords: based on children's book, cartoon rabbit, cartoon pig, cartoon bear

Overview: This was the second of the Russian Winnie the Pooh series. This one had Pooh and Piglet visiting Rabbit for a meal with honey.
