Title: Leif (1987)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Claes Eriksson
Main Cast: Anders Eriksson, Kerstin Granlund, Claes Eriksson, Knut Agnred, Per Fritzell
Keywords: factory, musical, hospital, parade, car chase, sleepwalking

Overview: The weaponfactory in Rotum is selling weapons illegal, a person called 'Leif' writes in the local newspaper. He must be stopped, even if what he has written happens to be true. Gunnar Volt and some of the other workers at the factory sets out to catch him.
