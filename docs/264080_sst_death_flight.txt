Title: SST: Death Flight (1977)
Genres: Thriller, TV Movie, Drama, Action
Director: David Lowell Rich
Main Cast: Barbara Anderson, Bert Convy, Peter Graves, Lorne Greene, Season Hubley
Keywords: airplane accident, sabotage, tv movie, disaster film, supersonic jet

Overview: On its maiden flight, the crew of America's first supersonic transport learns that it may not be able to land, due to an act of sabotage and a deadly flu onboard.
