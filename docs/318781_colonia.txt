Title: Colonia (2016)
Genres: Drama, Romance, History
Director: Florian Gallenberger
Main Cast: Emma Watson, Daniel Brühl, Michael Nyqvist, Richenda Carey, Vicky Krieps
Keywords: kidnapping, cult, chile, religious fanatic, political unrest

Overview: A young woman's desperate search for her abducted boyfriend that draws her into the infamous Colonia Dignidad, a sect nobody ever escaped from.
