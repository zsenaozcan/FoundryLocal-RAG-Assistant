Title: The Ouija Experiment 2: Theatre of Death (2015)
Genres: Horror
Director: Israel Luna
Main Cast: Justin Armstrong, Swisyzinna, Israel Luna, Nicole Holt, Israel Luna

Overview: A fun filled weekend turns deadly when a group of unsuspecting theatre goers are trapped inside, after a ravenous spirit is resurrected and released from a Ouija board. They must confront their most terrifying fears and destroy the spirit before it finds and kills them first.
