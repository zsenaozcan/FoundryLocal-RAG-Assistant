Title: Seven Dead in the Cat's Eye (1973)
Genres: Crime, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Antonio Margheriti
Main Cast: Jane Birkin, Hiram Keller, Françoise Christophe, Venantino Venantini, Doris Kunstmann
Keywords: female nudity, gorilla, based on novel, cat, nudity, sadism, blood splatter, murder, giallo, subjective camera
