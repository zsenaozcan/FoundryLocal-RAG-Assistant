Title: Nothing But the Truth (2008)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Rod Lurie
Main Cast: Kate Beckinsale, Matt Dillon, Angela Bassett, Alan Alda, Preston Bailey

Overview: When reporter Rachel Armstrong writes a story that reveals the identity of a covert CIA operative, the government demands that Rachel reveal her source. She defies the special prosecutor and is thrown in jail. Meanwhile, her attorney, Albert Burnside argues her case all the way to the U.S. Supreme Court.
