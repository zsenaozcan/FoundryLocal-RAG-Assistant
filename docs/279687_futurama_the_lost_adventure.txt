Title: Futurama: The Lost Adventure (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Animation
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Billy West, Katey Sagal, John DiMaggio, Tress MacNeille, Maurice LaMarche

Overview: The cut-scenes from Futurama (2003) (VG), edited together to form an episode. Mom attempts to take over the universe and it's up to the Planet Express crew to stop her.
