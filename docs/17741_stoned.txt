Title: Stoned (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Stephen Woolley
Main Cast: Leo Gregory, Paddy Considine, David Morrissey, Ben Whishaw, Tuva Novotny
Keywords: rock star, musician, rolling stones, drug, death, 1960s, inspired by celebrity

Overview: A chronicle of the sordid life and suspicious death of Rolling Stones co-founder Brian Jones, who was found in the bottom of his swimming pool weeks after being let go from the band.
