Title: Next Time I'll Aim for the Heart (2014)
Genres: Crime, Thriller, Drama
Director: Cédric Anger
Main Cast: Guillaume Canet, Ana Girardot, Jean-Yves Berteloot, Cédric le Maoût, Alice de Lencquesaing
Keywords: 1970s, police, based on true story, murder, serial killer, rogue cop

Overview: Between 1978 and 1979, the inhabitants of the Oise are in fear of a maniac who kills several hitchhikers and escape the police. He was then dubbed "the killer of the Oise" is actually a shy young policeman who will investigate his own murder, only to lose control of the situation.
