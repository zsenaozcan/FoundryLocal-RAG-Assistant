Title: In Between (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Maysaloun Hamoud
Main Cast: Mouna Hawa, Shaden Kanboura, Sana Jammalieh, Mahmoud Shalaby
Keywords: woman director

Overview: IN BETWEEN captures the daily duality of three young Palestinian women in Tel Aviv, caught between hometown tradition and big city abandon, and the price they must pay for a lifestyle that seems obvious to many: the freedom to work, party, fuck, and choose.
