Title: UFO Abduction (1989)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Dean Alioto
Main Cast: Tommy Giavocchini, Patrick Kelley, Shirly McCalla, Stacey Shulman, Christine Staples
Keywords: alien, mockumentary, found footage

Overview: On a typical fall evening in 1983, a young man was videotaping his niece's 5th birthday party. As the night's strange occurrences took place, he kept his video camera running, recording the entire event.
