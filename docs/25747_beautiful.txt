Title: Beautiful (2008)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery, Foreign
Director: Juhn Jai-hong
Main Cast: Lee Chun-hee, Cha Soo-yeon, Choi Moo-seong, Kim Min-Soo, Lee Min

Overview: Eun-young is a woman cursed with great beauty. Wherever she goes, she gets unwanted admiration from men and jealousy from women. One day, one of her stalking admirer invades her place.
