Title: Le Papillon Fantastique (1909)
Genres: Horror, Fantasy
Director: Georges Méliès
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: magic, nightmare, spider, silent film, butterfly, hand tinted

Overview: Surviving fragment of a longer film. A magician makes a butterfly woman appear, and a woman in a star.  Exhausted, the magician falls asleep and the star woman turns into a spider, dragging the butterfly into her web.
