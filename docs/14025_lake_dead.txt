Title: Lake Dead (2007)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: George Bessudo
Main Cast: Alex A. Quinn, Kelsey Crane, Jim Devoti, Kelsey Wedeen, Tara Gerard
Keywords: lake, motel, incest overtones, extreme violence

Overview: Three sisters and a group of their friends take a trip to the home of the recently deceased grandfather -- who died a particularly grisly death -- to learn more about the promise of an inheritance, only to encounter a family of psychos who have taken up residence in the old man's cabin.
