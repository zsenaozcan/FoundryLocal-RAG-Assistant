Title: The Age of Shadows (2016)
Genres: Action, Drama, History, Thriller
Director: Kim Jee-woon
Main Cast: Song Kang-ho, Gong Yoo, Han Ji-min, Shingo Tsurumi, Um Tae-goo
Keywords: japan, korea, japanese occupation, 1920s, korean resistance

Overview: Movie follows the activities of the Heroic Corps, an anti-Japanese independence organization that existed under the Japanese colonial period in South Korea. The group used violent means to achieve Korean independence.
