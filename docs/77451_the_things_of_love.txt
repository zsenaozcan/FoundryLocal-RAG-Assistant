Title: The Things of Love (1989)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Jaime Chávarri
Main Cast: Ángela Molina, Ángel de Andrés López, Manuel Bandera
Keywords: musical

Overview: In 1940's Madrid. Juan plays piano for Pepita and her on-stage partner Mario. Although Mario really wants to steal Juan for himself, Juan is not interested and Mario resorts to a string of lovers as consolation. When he loves (and leaves) a young nobleman, the young man wants revenge.
