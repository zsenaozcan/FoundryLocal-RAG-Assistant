Title: Close (2004)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Marcus Lenz
Main Cast: Christoph Bach, Jule Böwe, Julia Richter, Julia Jäger, Joachim Paul Assböck

Overview: Jost roams through the streets of Berlin. He opposes the world enraged, aggressively and furiously in a self-destructive way. He continuously provokes situations proving to himself that everybody hates him. He is convinced he does not need anybody and does not let anyone get close to him.  Anna is walled in by her fear and unable to leave her apartment. She manages to keep this situation from all the others in a system of lies. Her reserves draw to a close. She heads for a catastrophe.  One day, Jost shows up at her door.
