Title: The Mind Snatchers (1972)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Bernard Girard
Main Cast: Christopher Walken, Ronny Cox, Joss Ackland, Ralph Meeker, Marco St. John
Keywords: remote control, psychological experiment, electrodes

Overview: A German scientist works on a way of quelling overly aggressive soldiers by developing implants that directly stimulate the pleasure centers of the brain.
