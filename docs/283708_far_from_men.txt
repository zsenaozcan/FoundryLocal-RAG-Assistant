Title: Far from Men (2014)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: David Oelhoffen
Main Cast: Viggo Mortensen, Reda Kateb, Djemel Barek, Vincent Martin, Nicolas Giraud
Keywords: fire, mountain, classroom, hostage, rain, horse, rifle, children, teacher, school
