Title: Soldier's Girl (2003)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Crime
Director: Frank Pierson
Main Cast: Lee Pace, Troy Garity, Shawn Hatosy, Andre Braugher, Philip Eddolls

Overview: The true story of Soldier’s Girl, which takes place in Fort Campbell, KY, tells the heart-wrenching story of the life and tragic death of soldier Barry Winchell. His love for Calpernia Addams, a beautiful transgendered nightclub performer was misunderstood by fellow soldiers and eventually leads to his brutal death.
