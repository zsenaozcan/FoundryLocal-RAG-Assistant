Title: A Knight in Camelot (1998)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Comedy, Science Fiction, Family
Director: Roger Young
Main Cast: Whoopi Goldberg, Michael York, Paloma Baeza, Simon Fenton, James Coombes
Keywords: experiment, time travel, knight, king arthur

Overview: Yet another variation on Mark Twain's "A Connecticut Yankee At King Arthur's Court." Here, a computer malfunction causes a science researcher to be sent back in time with her laptop, which she uses to amaze the court.
