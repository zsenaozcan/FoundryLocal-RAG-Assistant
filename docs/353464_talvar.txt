Title: Talvar (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Mystery
Director: Meghna Gulzar
Main Cast: Irrfan Khan, Konkona Sen Sharma, Neeraj Kabi, Sohum Shah, Atul Kumar
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Dramatization of the notorious "Noida Double Murder Case", which set off a media frenzy around the world in 2008.
