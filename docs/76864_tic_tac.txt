Title: Tic Tac (1997)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Daniel Alfredson
Main Cast: Oliver Loftéen, Tuva Novotny, Jacob Nordenson, Tintin Anderzon, Emil Forselius
Keywords: multiple storylines

Overview: During one single day a number of life destinies cross one another. It becomes a night for the involved they will never forget, a night when the coincidences are going to change their lives forever.
