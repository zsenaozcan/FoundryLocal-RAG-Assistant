Title: Snow White and Russian Red (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Xawery Żuławski
Main Cast: Borys Szyc, Roma Gąsiorowska, Maria Strzelecka, Sonia Bohosiewicz, Anna Prus

Overview: An aggressive thug has problems with his girlfriend. Over the course of several chaotic days he walks around the hood, takes drugs and confronts himself with other women.
