Title: The Riddle of the Sands (1979)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Tony Maylam
Main Cast: Simon MacCorkindale, Michael York, Jenny Agutter, Alan Badel, Jürgen Andersen
Keywords: germany, yacht, war, north sea

Overview: In the early years of the 20th Century, two British yachtsmen (Michael York and Simon MacCorkindale) stumble upon a German plot to invade the east coast of England in a flotilla of specially designed barges. They set out to thwart this terrible scheme, but must outwit not only the cream of the German Navy, but the feared Kaiser Wilhelm himself.
