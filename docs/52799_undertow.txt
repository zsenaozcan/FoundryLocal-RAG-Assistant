Title: Undertow (2009)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Javier Fuentes-León
Main Cast: Cristian Mercado, Manolo Cardona, Tatiana Astengo, José Chacaltana, Attilia Boschetti
Keywords: gay, sea, lover, ritual, fisherman, peru, supernatural, homosexuality, gay relationship, in the closet

Overview: An unusual ghost story set on the Peruvian seaside; a married fisherman struggles to reconcile his devotion to his male lover within his town's rigid traditions.
