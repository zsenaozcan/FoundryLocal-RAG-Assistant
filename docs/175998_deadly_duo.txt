Title: Deadly Duo (1962)
Genres: Mystery
Director: Reginald Le Borg
Main Cast: Marcia Henderson, Craig Hill, Robert Lowery, Irene Tedrow, Dayton Lummis
Keywords: twin sister

Overview: Two identical twin sisters...one is very,very good--one is very, very bad. The good twin is due some big bucks. The bad twin wants 'em. Since they look just alike...look out.
