Title: The Love of the Three Pomegranates (1979)
Genres: Drama
Director: João César Monteiro
Main Cast: Margarida Gil, Helena Domingos, Joana Oliveira, Sílvia Gomes Ferreira, Pedro Ferreira
Keywords: folklore

Overview: In this highly theatrical TV production, Monteiro again draws on the world of folklore – and, more precisely, on the widespread sexual connotation of the pomegranate – to tell a tale of love, envy, treason and mistaken/double identities.
