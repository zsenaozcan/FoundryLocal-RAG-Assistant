Title: The Legend of Lylah Clare (1968)
Genres: Drama
Director: Robert Aldrich
Main Cast: Kim Novak, Peter Finch, Ernest Borgnine, Valentina Cortese, Michael Murphy
Keywords: hollywood, psychological thriller, movie star, actress

Overview: A dictatorial film director (Finch) hires an unknown actress (Novak) to play the lead role in a planned movie biography of a late, great Hollywood star.
