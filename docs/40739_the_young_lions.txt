Title: The Young Lions (1958)
Genres: Action, Drama, War
Director: Edward Dmytryk
Main Cast: Marlon Brando, Montgomery Clift, Dean Martin, Hope Lange, Barbara Rush
Keywords: world war ii, german, soldier, american

Overview: The Young Lions follows the lives of three soldiers: one German and two Americans, paralleling their experiences in World War II until they meet up at the end for a confrontation
