Title: Way of the Wicked (2014)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Kevin Carraway
Main Cast: Vinnie Jones, Christian Slater, Emily Tennant, Jake Croker, Aren Buchholz
Keywords: religion, demon, evil, satanic

Overview: Lawrence Salva's script centers on a detective (Facinelli), on the trail of a murderer, who is led to believe that a local teenager, harboring some sort of strange supernatural power, may be involved. Slater plays the man's disbelieving boss, while Hauer plays a man-of-the-cloth who aids the cop on his mission.
