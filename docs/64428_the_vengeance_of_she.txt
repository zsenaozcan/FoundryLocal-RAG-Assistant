Title: The Vengeance of She (1968)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Cliff Owen
Main Cast: John Richardson, Olga Schoberová, Edward Judd, Colin Blakely, Jill Melford
Keywords: beauty, sequel, lost city, possesion

Overview: Beautiful young European girl, Carol, is possessed by the spirit of Ayesha – “She, who must be obeyed” – and led to the lost city of Kuma, where she is destined to become queen.
