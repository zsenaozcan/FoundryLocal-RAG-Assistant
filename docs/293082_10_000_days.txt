Title: 10,000 Days (2014)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Eric Small
Main Cast: Riley Smith, John Schneider, Kim Myers, Peter Wingfield, Lisa Pelikan

Overview: 10,000 Days ago, Comet 23 struck Earth with the magnitude of all the nuclear weapons in the world sending the planet into a deep freeze. Now, 27 years in the future, those who survived are locked in an epic battle of life or death.
