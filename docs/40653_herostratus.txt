Title: Herostratus (1967)
Genres: Drama
Director: Don Levy
Main Cast: Michael Gothard, Gabriella Licudi, Peter Stephens, Antony Paul, Mona Chin

Overview: When Max, a young poet hires a marketing company to turn his suicide-by-jumping into a mass-media spectacle, he finds that his subversive intentions are quickly diluted into a reactionary gesture, and his motivations are revealed as a desperate attempt to seek attention through celebrity.
