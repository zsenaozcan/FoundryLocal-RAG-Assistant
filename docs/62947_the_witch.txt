Title: The Witch (1966)
Genres: Foreign, Horror
Director: Damiano Damiani
Main Cast: Richard Johnson, Rosanna Schiaffino, Gian Maria Volonté, Sarah Ferrati, Margherita Guzzinati
Keywords: love triangle, witch, italian, film noir

Overview: A historian goes to a castle library to translate some ancient erotic literature. While there he discovers what he believes to be supernatural forces at work.
