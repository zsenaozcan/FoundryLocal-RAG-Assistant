Title: The Cradle (2007)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Tim Brown
Main Cast: Lukas Haas, Emily Hampshire, Amanda Smith
Keywords: nightmare, curse

Overview: When Frank and Julie move to a small isolated town with their son Sam, both Julie and Sam become ill. Driven by terrifying nightmares of torture that come true Frank begs a local midwife Helen to care for Sam as he desperately searches for a way to stop this curse on his family.
