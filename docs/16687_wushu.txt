Title: Wushu (2008)
Genres: Action, Drama, Foreign
Director: Antony Szeto
Main Cast: Sammo Hung, Liu Feng-Chao, Wang Fei, Wang Wen-Jie, Wang Ya-Chao
Keywords: showdown, sword, coach, medal, tournament, duel, kendo, staff, spear, combat

Overview: Ten years ago, five students joined a martial arts school and learned the disciplines of Wushu and the bonds of friendship. Today, both will be put to the test after they and a former student stumble into a child kidnapping ring
