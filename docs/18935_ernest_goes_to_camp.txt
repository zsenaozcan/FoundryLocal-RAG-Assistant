Title: Ernest Goes to Camp (1987)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: John R. Cherry III
Main Cast: Jim Varney, Victoria Racimo, John Vernon, Gailard Sartain, Daniel Butler
Keywords: ernest p. worrell

Overview: A group of juvenile criminals is sent for vacation to Kamp Kikakee. The clumsy Ernest has to care for them, although he doesn't even know how to take care of himself. The other children at the camp show enmity against them, but the group knows very well how to defend themselves. They do also help the Indian owner of the camp when a brutal mining corporation wants to tear down the camp to mine a rare mineral.
