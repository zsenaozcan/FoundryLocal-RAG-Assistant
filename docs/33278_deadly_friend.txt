Title: Deadly Friend (1986)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Wes Craven
Main Cast: Matthew Laborteaux, Kristy Swanson, Michael Sharrett, Anne Twomey, Anne Ramsey
Keywords: artificial intelligence, child abuse, basketball, resurrection, revenge, teenager, robot, alcoholic, brawl, circuit board

Overview: Paul is a new kid in town with a robot named "BB". He befriends Samantha and the three of them have a lot of good times together. That is, until Samantha's abusive father throws her down some stairs and kills her. In an effort to save her life, Paul implants BB's computer brain into Samantha's human brain.
