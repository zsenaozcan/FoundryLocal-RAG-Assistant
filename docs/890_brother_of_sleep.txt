Title: Brother of Sleep (1995)
Genres: Drama
Director: Joseph Vilsmaier
Main Cast: André Eisermann, Dana Vávrová, Ben Becker, Jochen Nickel, Jürgen Schornagel
Keywords: suicide, brother brother relationship, sex, lovesickness, jealousy, austria, new love, village life, mountain village, pregnancy and birth

Overview: Schlafes Bruder tells the story a few exceptionally talented individuals who grew up in a remote mountain town in the Alps. The film, based on an internationally successful novel by Robert Schneider of the same name, depicts the slow withering of their unique skills.
