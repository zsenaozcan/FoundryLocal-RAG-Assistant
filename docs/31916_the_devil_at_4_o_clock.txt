Title: The Devil at 4 O'Clock (1961)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Drama
Director: Mervyn LeRoy
Main Cast: Spencer Tracy, Frank Sinatra, Kerwin Mathews, Jean-Pierre Aumont, Grégoire Aslan
Keywords: prisoner, island, volcano, rescue, priest, hospital, quicksand

Overview: A crusty, eccentric priest recruits three reluctant convicts to help him rescue a children's leper colony from a Pacific island menaced by a smoldering volcano.
