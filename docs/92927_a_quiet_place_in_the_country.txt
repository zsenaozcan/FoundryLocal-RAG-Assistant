Title: A Quiet Place in the Country (1968)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: Elio Petri
Main Cast: Franco Nero, Vanessa Redgrave, Georges Géret, Gabriella Boccardo, Madeleine Damien
Keywords: obsession, psychic

Overview: A painter facing a creative block arranges to spend the weekend in the country at his mistress's villa. While staying there, his sanity begins to disintegrate.
