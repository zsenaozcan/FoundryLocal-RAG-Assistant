Title: Pokémon the Movie: Genesect and the Legend Awakened (2013)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Animation, Family
Director: Kunihiko Yuyama
Main Cast: Sarah Natochenny, Eileen Stevens, Jason Griffith, Jason Flores Contreras, Eli James
Keywords: power, pokémon

Overview: A group of five Genesect have invaded the big city and pose a threat to the supply of electricity, which attracts the attention of the legendary Pokémon Mewtwo. Ash, Pikachu and his friends must come to the rescue when the powerful leader, a red Genesect, faces Mewtwo.
