Title: American Me (1992)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Edward James Olmos
Main Cast: Edward James Olmos, William Forsythe, Sal Lopez, Dyana Ortelli, William Smith
Keywords: juvenile prison, jail, east los angeles, hood, gang, racism, violence, mexican american, lowrider

Overview: Edward J. Olmos made his directorial bow with the powerhouse crime saga American Me. Olmos stars as street-gang leader Santana, who during his 18 years in Folsom Prison rules over all the drug-and-murder activities behind bars.
