Title: Cat on a Hot Tin Roof (1958)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Richard Brooks
Main Cast: Elizabeth Taylor, Paul Newman, Burl Ives, Judith Anderson, Jack Carson
Keywords: individual, suicide, southern usa, adultery, mississippi, depression, jealousy, wife husband relationship, dying and death, alcoholic

Overview: Brick, an alcoholic ex-football player, drinks his days away and resists the affections of his wife, Maggie. His reunion with his father, Big Daddy, who is dying of cancer, jogs a host of memories and revelations for both father and son.
