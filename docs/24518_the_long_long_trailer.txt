Title: The Long, Long Trailer (1954)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Vincente Minnelli
Main Cast: Lucille Ball, Desi Arnaz, Marjorie Main, Keenan Wynn, Gladys Hurlbut
Keywords: based on novel, mountain, honeymoon, marriage, road trip, trailer, road movie

Overview: A newly wed couple, Tacy and Nicky, travel in a trailer for their honeymoon. The journey is a humorous one that could end up destroying their marriage.
