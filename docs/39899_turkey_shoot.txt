Title: Turkey Shoot (1982)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Brian Trenchard-Smith
Main Cast: Steve Railsback, Olivia Hussey, Michael Craig, Carmen Duncan, Noel Ferrier
Keywords: male nudity, female nudity, rape, sex, nudity, future, machete, sadism, dystopia, satire

Overview: In the near future, after an unspecified holocaust, survivors are herded into prison camps. There, they are hunted for sport by the leaders of the camp. Paul, one of the newest prisoners, is determined not to go down as quietly as the others.
