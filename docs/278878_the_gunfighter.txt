Title: The Gunfighter (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Western
Director: Eric Kissack
Main Cast: Shawn Parsons, Scott Beehner, Brace Harris, Eileen O'Connell, Jordan Black
Keywords: parody, farce, breaking the fourth wall

Overview: In the tradition of classic westerns, a narrator sets up the story of a lone gunslinger who walks into a saloon. However, the people in this saloon can hear the narrator and the narrator may just be a little bit bloodthirsty.
