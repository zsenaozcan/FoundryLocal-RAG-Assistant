Title: Summer with Monika (1953)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Ingmar Bergman
Main Cast: Harriet Andersson, Lars Ekborg, Dagmar Ebbesen, Åke Fridell, Naemi Briese
Keywords: stockholm, pregnancy, young love, archipelago, midsummer

Overview: Monika from Stockholm falls in love with Harry, a young man on holiday. When she becomes pregnant they are forced into a marriage, which begins to fall apart soon after they take up residence in a cramped little flat.
