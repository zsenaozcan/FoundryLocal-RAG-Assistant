Title: Abar, the First Black Superman (1977)
Genres: Action
Director: Frank Packard
Main Cast: Tobar Mayo, J. Walter Smith, Roxie Young, Gladys Lum, Tony Rumford
Keywords: blaxploitation

Overview: Upon moving into a bigoted neighborhood, the scientist father of a persecuted black family gives a superpower elixir to a tough bodyguard, who thus becomes a superpowered crimefighter.
