Title: The Boys (1962)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Sidney J. Furie
Main Cast: Richard Todd, Robert Morley, Dudley Sutton, Ronald Lacey, Jess Conrad

Overview: A night watchman at a garage is found murdered, and four teddy boys are put on trial for the crime. Witnesses and suspects give differing accounts of the lead-up to the crime, and the truth emerges.
