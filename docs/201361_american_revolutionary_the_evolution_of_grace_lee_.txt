Title: American Revolutionary: The Evolution of Grace Lee Boggs (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Grace Lee
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Grace Lee Boggs is an activist and philosopher in Detroit who has dedicated her life to the next American Revolution and the possibility of a better, more just future for all of humanity. At age 97, she has been building movements and developing strategies for social change for most of her life -- reminding us that revolution is not only possible and necessary, but a process that must always be in motion.
