Title: Walking Tall Part II (1975)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Crime
Director: Earl Bellamy
Main Cast: Bo Svenson, Luke Askew, John Davis Chandler, Robert DoQui, Leif Garrett
Keywords: vigilante

Overview: Sheriff Buford Pusser continues his one-man war against moonshiners and a ruthless crime syndicate after the murder of his wife in late 1960's Tennessee.
