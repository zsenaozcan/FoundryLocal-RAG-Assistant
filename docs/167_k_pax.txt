Title: K-PAX (2001)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Iain Softley
Main Cast: Jeff Bridges, Kevin Spacey, Mary McCormack, Alfre Woodard, Ajay Naidu
Keywords: robbery, dream, hypnosis, investigation, murder, alien, hospital, planet, patient, medication

Overview: Prot is a patient at a mental hospital who claims to be from a far away Planet. His psychiatrist tries to help him, only to begin to doubt his own explanations.
