Title: The Zone (2007)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Rodrigo Plá
Main Cast: Daniel Giménez Cacho, Maribel Verdú, Alan Chávez, Carlos Bardem, Marina de Tavira
Keywords: slum, mexico, robbery, militia, vigilante, murder, escape, crime, violence, neighborhood

Overview: Residents of an enclosed neighborhood in the middle of Mexico DF are shocked by a violent crime, and for one resident in particular, young Alejandro, the drama is ratcheted up when he encounters the lone kid who escaped the event and is hiding out within the neighborhood's borders.
