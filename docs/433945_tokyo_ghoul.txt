Title: Tokyo Ghoul (2017)
Genres: Action, Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Kentaro Hagiwara
Main Cast: Masataka Kubota, Yu Aoi, Fumika Shimizu, Nobuyuki Suzuki, Yo Oizumi
Keywords: based on manga

Overview: Ken Kaneki (Masataka Kubota) is a university student. He becomes injured by Rize, a human eating ghoul. Ken is saved from the ghoul when a steel frame falls on Rize. They are both sent to the hospital. Ken receives an organ transplant from Rize and becomes a half ghoul.
