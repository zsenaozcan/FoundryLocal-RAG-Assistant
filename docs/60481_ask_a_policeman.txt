Title: Ask a Policeman (1939)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Marcel Varnel
Main Cast: Will Hay, Graham Moffatt, Moore Marriott, Glennis Lorimer, Peter Gawthorne
Keywords: english countryside, bumbling cops

Overview: The mirthful adventures of Police-Sergeant Samuel Dudfoot and his two constables, Albert Brown and Jeremias Harbottle, who stage a fabricated crime-wave to save their jobs---and then find themselves involved in the real thing.
