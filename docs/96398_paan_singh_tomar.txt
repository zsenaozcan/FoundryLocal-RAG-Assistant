Title: Paan Singh Tomar (2012)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Tigmanshu Dhulia
Main Cast: Irrfan Khan, Mahie Gill, Zakir Hussain, Nawazuddin Siddiqui, Brijendra Kala
Keywords: rebel, sport, army, athlete

Overview: Paan Singh Tomar (Hindi: पान सिंह तोमर) is an Indian biographical film, based on the true story of an athlete Paan Singh Tomar employed by the Indian Army and who won a gold medal at Indian National Games, but was forced to become a notorious bandit .
