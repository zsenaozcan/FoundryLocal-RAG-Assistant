Title: A Bird of the Air (2011)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Margaret Whitton
Main Cast: Rachel Nichols, Jackson Hurst, Linda Emond, Buck Henry
Keywords: highway, parrot, orphan, dog, basset hound, woman director

Overview: A man in search of his past, and a woman who lives in the moment, are brought together when they pursue the origins of a stray parrot in this comedic and romantic drama.
