Title: A Far Off Place (1993)
Genres: Drama, Adventure
Director: Mikael Salomon
Main Cast: Reese Witherspoon, Ethan Embry, Jack Thompson, Sarel Bok, Robert John Burke
Keywords: murder, survival, explosion

Overview: Thrown together under incredible circumstances, two strangers must discover courage and strength when they begin a journey across the treacherous African desert! Equipped only with their wits and the expertise of a native bushman who befriends them, they are determined to triumph over impossible odds and reach their destination. But along the way, the trio face a primitive desert wilderness.
