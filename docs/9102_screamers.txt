Title: Screamers (1995)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Christian Duguay
Main Cast: Peter Weller, Roy Dupuis, Jennifer Rubin, Andrew Lauer, Liliana Komorowska
Keywords: man vs machine, alien planet, struggle for survival

Overview: (SIRIUS 6B, Year 2078) On a distant mining planet ravaged by a decade of war, scientists have created the perfect weapon: a blade-wielding, self-replicating race of killing devices known as Screamers designed for one purpose only -- to hunt down and destroy all enemy life forms.
