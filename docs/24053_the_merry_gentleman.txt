Title: The Merry Gentleman (2008)
Genres: Drama, Action
Director: Michael Keaton
Main Cast: Michael Keaton, Kelly Macdonald, Tom Bastounes, Mike Bradecich, Debbi Burns
Keywords: detective, assassin, jacket, coat

Overview: A woman who leaves an abusive relationship to begin a new life in a new city, where she forms an unlikely and ironic relationship with a suicidal hit man (unbeknownst to her). Enter a worn, alcoholic detective to form the third party in a very unusual triangle as this story begins to unfold.
