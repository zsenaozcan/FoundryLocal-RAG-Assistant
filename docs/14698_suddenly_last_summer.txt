Title: Suddenly, Last Summer (1959)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Mystery
Director: Joseph L. Mankiewicz
Main Cast: Elizabeth Taylor, Katharine Hepburn, Montgomery Clift, Albert Dekker, Mercedes McCambridge
Keywords: spain, operation, hunger, lie, post, widow, turtle, dysfunctional family, memory, hospital

Overview: The only son of wealthy widow Violet Venable dies while on vacation with his cousin Catherine. What the girl saw was so horrible that she went insane; now Mrs. Venable wants Catherine lobotomized to cover up the truth.
