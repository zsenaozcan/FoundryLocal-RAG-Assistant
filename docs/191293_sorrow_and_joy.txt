Title: Sorrow and Joy (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nils Malmros
Main Cast: Jakob Cedergren, Helle Fagralid, Nicolas Bro, Ida Dwinger, Søren Pilmark

Overview: Filmmaker Johannes and his wife, schoolteacher Signe, experience the biggest sorrow and misfortune one can ever imagine. Nevertheless, in all the hopelessness they must try to reach for mutual and mature love in order to continue life after death.
