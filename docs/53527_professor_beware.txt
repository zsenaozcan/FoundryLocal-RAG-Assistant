Title: Professor Beware (1938)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Elliott Nugent
Main Cast: Harold Lloyd, Phyllis Welch, Raymond Walburn, Lionel Stander, William Frawley

Overview: Egyptologist, Dean Lambert (Lloyd), accused of car-theft, skips bail and begins a cross-country trek to join a group in New York headed for Egypt. With the police close on his trail he gets in and out of scrapes along the way.
