Title: Adult Beginners (2014)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Ross Katz
Main Cast: Nick Kroll, Rose Byrne, Bobby Cannavale, Joel McHale, Caitlin Fitzgerald
Keywords: new york, nanny, loser, pregnant

Overview: A young, hipster entrepreneur crashes and burns on the eve of his company’s big launch. With his entire life in disarray, he leaves Manhattan to move in with his estranged pregnant sister, brother-in-law and three year-old nephew in the suburbs — only to become their manny.  Faced with real responsibility, he may finally have to grow up — but not without some bad behavior first.
