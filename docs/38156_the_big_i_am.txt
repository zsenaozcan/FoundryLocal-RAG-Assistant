Title: The Big I Am (2010)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: Nic Auerbach
Main Cast: Leo Gregory, Vincent Regan, Michael Madsen, Robert Fucilla, Steven Berkoff

Overview: A cruel twist of fate catapults small time crook Mickey Skinner into the big league, as head of a brutal London gang poised on the brink of a lucrative human-trafficking deal.
