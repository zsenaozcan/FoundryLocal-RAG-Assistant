Title: Samurai I: Musashi Miyamoto (1954)
Genres: Drama, Action, Adventure
Director: Hiroshi Inagaki
Main Cast: Toshirō Mifune, Rentarô Mikuni, Mariko Okada, Kuroemon Onoe, Kaoru Yachigusa
Keywords: japanese, samurai, classic
