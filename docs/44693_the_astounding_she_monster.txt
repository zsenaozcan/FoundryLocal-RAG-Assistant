Title: The Astounding She-Monster (1958)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Ronald V. Ashcroft
Main Cast: Robert Clarke, Kenne Duncan, Shirley Kilpatrick, Marilyn Harvey, Jeanne Tatum
Keywords: kidnapping, meteor, forest ranger, ufo, alien, independent film, b movie, gangster
