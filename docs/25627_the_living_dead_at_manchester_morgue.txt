Title: The Living Dead at Manchester Morgue (1974)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Jorge Grau
Main Cast: Cristina Galbó, Ray Lovelock, Arthur Kennedy, Aldo Massasso, Giorgio Trestini
Keywords: england, hippie, countryside, detective, farm, morgue, undead, zombie, video nasty

Overview: A cop chases two hippies suspected of a series of Manson family-like murders; unbeknown to him, the real culprits are the living dead, brought to life with a thirst for human flesh after government officials conduct pest control experiments using subsonic waves in the area.
