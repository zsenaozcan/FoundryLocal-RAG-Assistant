Title: The Earth Dies Screaming (1964)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Terence Fisher
Main Cast: Willard Parker, Virginia Field, Dennis Price, Thorley Walters, Vanda Godsell
Keywords: killer robot, post-apocalyptic, robot, alien invasion

Overview: A crack space pilot returns to earth to find the planet has been devastated by some unknown forces. There are a few survivors, so he organizes them in a plan to ward off control by a group of killer robots.
