Title: Scared To Death (1981)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction, Horror
Director: William Malone
Main Cast: John Stinson, Diana Davidson, Jonathan David Moses, Toni Jannotta, Walker Edmiston
Keywords: serial killer, genetic engineering

Overview: A series of hideous murders is taking place, and Inspector Capell and cop-turned-novelist Lonergan are investigating. The murders are found to be the work of an out-of-control experiment in genetic engineering. The two men must descend into the city's sewer systems to destroy the horrific miscreation. It won't be hard to find, as it's already looking for its next victims...
