Title: The Slap (1974)
Genres: Foreign, Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Claude Pinoteau
Main Cast: Lino Ventura, Isabelle Adjani, Annie Girardot, Francis Perrin, Jacques Spiesser

Overview: A Parisian teacher (Lino Ventura) loses his cool when his teenage daughter tells him she plans to drop out of school and move in with her boyfriend.
