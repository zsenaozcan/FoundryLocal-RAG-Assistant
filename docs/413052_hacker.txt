Title: Hacker (2016)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Akan Satayev
Main Cast: Callan McAuliffe, Lorraine Nicholson, Daniel Eric Gold, Clifton Collins Jr, Zachary Bennett
Keywords: fbi, hacker, hustler, black market, identity theft, financial market, street-wise, trading, dark web

Overview: Alex, an immigrant from Ukraine comes to Canada and becomes involved with an online criminal organization called Darkweb. What starts off as a way to help his parents financially, soon becomes a personal vendetta against the entire banking system, when his mother is fired from her job at the bank
