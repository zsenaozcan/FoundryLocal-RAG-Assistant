Title: Pinocchio and the Emperor of the Night (1987)
Genres: Animation, Adventure, Family
Director: Hal Sutherland
Main Cast: Scott Grimes, Tom Bosley, Rickie Lee Jones, James Earl Jones

Overview: Pinocchio and his friends, a glow worm and a marionette, search for a magic music box. However, so are the evil Scalawag and the Emperor of the Night.
