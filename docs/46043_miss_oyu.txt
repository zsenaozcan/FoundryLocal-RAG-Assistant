Title: Miss Oyu (1951)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kenji Mizoguchi
Main Cast: Kinuyo Tanaka, Nobuko Otowa, Yûji Hori, Kiyoko Hirai, Reiko Kongo
Keywords: japan, sister sister relationship, death in childbirth

Overview: Shinnosuke is introduced to Shizu as a prospective marriage partner, but he falls in love with her widowed sister Oyu. Convention forbids Oyu to marry because she has to raise her son as the head of her husband's family. Oyu convinces Shinnosuke and Shizu to marry so that she can remain close to Shinnosuke.
