Title: 10 Billion: What's On Your Plate (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Valentin Thurn
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: food, environmentalism, sustainable development, natural resources, landgrabbing

Overview: How can we best meet every earth citizens need for healthy food facing our limited resources? Regarding the almost 10 billion humans living on earth by 2050, we have to decide now how we want to shape the future of agriculture.
