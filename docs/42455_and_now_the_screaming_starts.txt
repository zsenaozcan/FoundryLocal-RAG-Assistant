Title: And Now The Screaming Starts (1973)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Roy Ward Baker
Main Cast: Stephanie Beacham, Peter Cushing, Patrick Magee, Herbert Lom, Ian Ogilvy
Keywords: old house, severed hand

Overview: In the late 18th century, two newlyweds move into the stately mansion of husband Charles Fengriffen. The bride, Catherine, falls victim to a curse placed by a wronged servant on the Fengriffen family and all its descendants.
