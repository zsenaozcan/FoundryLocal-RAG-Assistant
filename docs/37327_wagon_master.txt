Title: Wagon Master (1950)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: John Ford
Main Cast: Ben Johnson, Joanne Dru, Harry Carey, Jr., Ward Bond, Charles Kemper
Keywords: native american, cowboy, mormon, san juana valley, pre-credit sequence
