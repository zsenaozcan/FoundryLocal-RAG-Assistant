Title: Reeker (2005)
Genres: Horror, Mystery
Director: Dave Payne
Main Cast: Devon Gummersall, Derek Richardson, Tina Illman, Scott Whyte, Arielle Kebbel
Keywords: bad smell, rave, blood splatter

Overview: Strangers trapped at an eerie travel oasis in the desert must unravel the mystery behind their visions of dying people while they are preyed upon by a decaying creature.
