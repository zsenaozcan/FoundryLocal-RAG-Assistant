Title: Standoff (1998)
Genres: Crime
Director: Andrew Chapman
Main Cast: Robert Sean Leonard, Dennis Haysbert, Natasha Henstridge, Tricia Vessey, Paul Ben-Victor
Keywords: fanatic, siege

Overview: The FBI and Texas police make ready to storm the headquarters of a heavily armed sect of religious fanatics. But the operation goes horribly wrong and a couple of agents seek protection at an abandoned farm. But they are not the only ones there.
