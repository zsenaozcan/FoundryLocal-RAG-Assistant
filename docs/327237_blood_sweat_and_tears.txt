Title: Blood, Sweat and Tears (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Diederick Koopal
Main Cast: Martijn Fischer, Hadewych Minis, Fedja van Huêt, Loek Peters, Marcel Hensema
Keywords: biography, singer

Overview: Bloed, Zweet en Tranen (Blood, Sweat and Tears) is a story about the life of Andre Hazes, a Dutch populair singer. This movie shows three crucial phases in the life of Hazes: his youth (60's), his breakthrough (80's) and the last year of his life (early '00).
