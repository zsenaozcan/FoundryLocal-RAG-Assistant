Title: Man from Deep River (1972)
Genres: Adventure, Horror
Director: Umberto Lenzi
Main Cast: Ivan Rassimov, Me Me Lai, Prasitsak Singhara, Sulallewan Suxantat, Ong Ard
Keywords: photographer, river, thailand, violence, cannibal, video nasty, savage

Overview: A photographer in the rain forest is captured by wild natives, and after months of living with them, he marries the chief's daughter and helps protect the village from a vicious cannibal tribe.
