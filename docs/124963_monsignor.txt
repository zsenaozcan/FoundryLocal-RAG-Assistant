Title: Monsignor (1982)
Genres: Crime, Drama, War
Director: Frank Perry
Main Cast: Christopher Reeve, Geneviève Bujold, Fernando Rey, Jason Miller, Adolfo Celi
Keywords: pope, nun, black market, scandal, priest, money, catholic, sicilian, catholic church

Overview: The vows of an ambitious young American priest are tested during World War II. Not only does Father John Flaherty get involved with the black market to raise money for the Vatican, he also falls in love with a young French nun.
