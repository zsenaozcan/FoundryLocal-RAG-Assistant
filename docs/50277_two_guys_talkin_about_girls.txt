Title: Two Guys Talkin' About Girls (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Steven Pearl
Main Cast: Dan Cortese, Jonathan Silverman, Allison Smith, Mariangela Pino, Monte Markham
Keywords: bar, diner, apartment, storytelling, episodic structure

Overview: When longtime friends Joey Fortone and Lenny Kaminski get together, the race is on to get one up on the other. Their fantasies abound and their results lag far behind.
