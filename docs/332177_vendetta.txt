Title: Vendetta (2015)
Genres: Action
Director: Jen Soska
Main Cast: Dean Cain, Kyra Zagorsky, Benjamin Hollingsworth, Paul Wight, Michael Eklund
Keywords: prison, detective, prisoner, prison warden, revenge, woman director

Overview: A hard-nosed detective deliberately commits a crime to get thrown in prison, allowing him the chance to seek vengeance on a criminal serving a life sentence for brutally murdering his wife.
