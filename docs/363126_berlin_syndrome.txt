Title: Berlin Syndrome (2017)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Drama, Horror
Director: Cate Shortland
Main Cast: Teresa Palmer, Max Riemelt, Matthias Habich, Emma Bading, Elmira Bahrami
Keywords: berlin, stockholm syndrome, based on novel, hostage, photojournalist, woman director

Overview: A passionate holiday romance leads to an obsessive relationship when an Australian photojournalist wakes one morning in a Berlin apartment and is unable to leave.
