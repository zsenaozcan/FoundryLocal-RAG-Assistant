Title: The Guitar Mongoloid (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ruben Östlund
Main Cast: Erik Rutström, Ola Sandstig, Britt-Marie Andersson, Julia Persdotter, P-A Emanielsson

Overview: The Guitar Mongoloid is a 2004 Swedish film directed by Ruben Östlund, about different people living outside the norms in the fictional city Jöteborg, strikingly similar to real-life Göteborg. Although not a documentary, most of the people seen in the film are non-actors more or less playing themselves.
