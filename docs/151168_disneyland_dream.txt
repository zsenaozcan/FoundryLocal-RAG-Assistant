Title: Disneyland Dream (1956)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Robbins Barstow
Main Cast: Daniel Barstow, David Barstow, Mary Barstow, Meg Barstow, Robbins Barstow
Keywords: home movie, disneyland, disney, travelogue, short

Overview: A home movie made by Robbins and Meg Barstow that documents their family's free trip to the newly opened Disneyland. The one-week trip was a prize that they won in a contest sponsored by Scotch tape.
