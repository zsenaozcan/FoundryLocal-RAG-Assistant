Title: Mary and Rhoda (2000)
Genres: TV Movie, Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Barnet Kellman
Main Cast: Mary Tyler Moore, Valerie Harper, Elon Gold, Christine Ebersole, Bethany Joy Lenz

Overview: The old friends from "The Mary Tyler Moore Show," Mary and Rhoda, are reunited, only to discover that Mary has a daughter named Rose and Rhoda's daughter is named Meredith.
