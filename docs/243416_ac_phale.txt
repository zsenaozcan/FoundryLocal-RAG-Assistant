Title: Acéphale (1969)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Patrick Deval
Main Cast: Patrick Deval, Jackie Raynal

Overview: In common with other Zanzibar films, Patrick Deval’s Acéphale (1968) eschews conventional narrative in favour of an experimental arrangement of austerely executed but intensely hallucinatory episodes that build into a nightmarish fever of isolation and hopelessness.
