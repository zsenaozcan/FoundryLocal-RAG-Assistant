Title: Forest of the Damned (2005)
Genres: Horror, Foreign
Director: Johannes Roberts
Main Cast: Tom Savini, Nicole Petty, Daniel Maclagan, Sophie Holland, Richard Cambridge

Overview: A group of friends go on a roadtrip and come to a stop in a forest where legend has it a group of naked bisexual female monsters lurk. Will they awake them? And if so will they survive?
