Title: The Smiling Madame Beudet (1923)
Genres: Drama
Director: Germaine Dulac
Main Cast: Germaine Dermoz, Alexandre Arquillière, Jean d'Yd, Yvette Grisier, Madeleine Guitty
Keywords: woman director

Overview: One of the first feminist movies, this is the story of an intelligent woman trapped in a loveless marriage.
