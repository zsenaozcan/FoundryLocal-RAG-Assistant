Title: 10.5 (2004)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Drama
Director: John Lafia
Main Cast: Kim Delaney, Beau Bridges, Fred Ward, Ivan Sergei, Dulé Hill
Keywords: miniseries

Overview: An earthquake reaching a 10.5 magnitude on the Richter scale, strikes the west coast of the U.S. and Canada. A large portion of land falls into the ocean, and the situation is worsened by aftershocks and tsunami.
