Title: Knock Knock (2008)
Genres: Horror
Director: Joseph Ariola
Main Cast: Nicole Abisinio, Chris Bashinelli, Kat Castaneda, Suzi Lorraine

Overview: Knock Knock is a horror film that combines fear with revenge, hate with justice and responsibility with sacrifice. The bible says an "Eye for an Eye." Rico was just a big fun loving son of a hard working neighborhood business man. The problem is his dad is an undertaker. Some popular high school Students thought he was weird because of it.
