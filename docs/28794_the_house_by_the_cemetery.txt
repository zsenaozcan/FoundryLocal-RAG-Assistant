Title: The House by the Cemetery (1981)
Genres: Horror
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Catriona MacColl, Paolo Malco, Ania Pieroni, Giovanni Frezza, Silvia Collatina
Keywords: haunted house, mad scientist, gore, zombie, video nasty

Overview: A deranged killer lives in the basement of an old mansion and pops out occasionally to commit grisly murders that include be-headings.
