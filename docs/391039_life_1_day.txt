Title: Life+1 Day (2016)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Saeed Roustayi
Main Cast: پیمان معادی, Parinaz Izadyar, Navid Mohammadzadeh, Shabnam Moghadami, Rima Raminfar
Keywords: middle east, drug dealer, family relationships, tragedy, iran

Overview: Somaieh, the youngest daughter of an indigent family, is getting married and fear is overwhelming each and every member of the family regarding how to overcome their difficulties after she's gone.
