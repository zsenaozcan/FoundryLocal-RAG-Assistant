Title: The Cockleshell Heroes (1955)
Genres: War
Director: José Ferrer
Main Cast: Trevor Howard, José Ferrer, Anthony Newley, Victor Maddern, Percy Herbert
Keywords: world war ii, mission, behind enemy lines, u.s. marine, royal marines
