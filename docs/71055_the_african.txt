Title: The African (1983)
Genres: Comedy, Adventure
Director: Philippe de Broca
Main Cast: Catherine Deneuve, Philippe Noiret, Jean-François Balmer, Jacques François, Jean Benguigui

Overview: Charlotte (Catherine Deneuve) flies to East-Africa in order to build a touristic center near the Lake Williams where the pygmys live. Here she meets her husband Victor (Philippe Noiret), a devoted conservationist who left her three years ago to live in the jungle. Can you imagine his enthusiasm when she arrives at the conclusion that the ideal place to built this holiday resort is his kitchen garden?
