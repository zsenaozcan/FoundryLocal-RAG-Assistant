Title: Living Is Easy With Eyes Closed (2013)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: David Trueba
Main Cast: Javier Cámara, Natalia de Molina, Francesc Colomer, Ariadna Gil, Jorge Sanz
Keywords: based on true story, the beatles, hitchhiking

Overview: The unforgettable friendship between three people: an English teacher who travels to Almería when he hears that John Lennon is making a film there, a 16-year-old boy who has run away from home and a young girl who also seems to be running away from something.
