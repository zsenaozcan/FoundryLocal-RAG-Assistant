Title: The Hottie & The Nottie (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Tom Putnam
Main Cast: Paris Hilton, Joel David Moore, Christine Lakin, Johan Urb, Adam Kulbersh
Keywords: california, yoga, friends, crush

Overview: Nate moves to L.A. to track down Cristabel, the woman he's been in love with since childhood, only to discover that his plan to woo her only has one hurdle to overcome: what to do with June, Cristabel's ever-present, not-so-hot best friend? What's even more complicating is Nate's growing feelings for June, whose true beauty starts to emerge.
