Title: Harper Valley P.T.A. (1978)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Richard C. Bennett
Main Cast: Barbara Eden, Ronny Cox, Woody Harrelson, Nanette Fabray, Audrey Christie
Keywords: revenge, school

Overview: Stella Johnson is a single mother living in the town of Harper Valley. Most of the townsfolk, particularly those on the school PTA, think she is a little too liberal so they're making things tough for her and her daughter. So she runs for the position of PTA President, which infuriates them. Stella decides to get revenge with the help of her friends.
