Title: Mi mejor enemigo (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Foreign
Director: Alex Bowen
Main Cast: Ezequiel Abeijón, Nicolás Saavedra, Miguel Dedovich, Jorge Román, Felipe Braun

Overview: After losing their bearings, a group of Chilean soldiers learns some lessons about their enemy's humanity in this dark comedy set during the 1970s war between Chile and Argentina. Digging in where they are, the troop soon discovers a nearby Argentinean platoon. Unsure how to handle the situation, the two groups start passing notes via a stray dog, and eventually achieve a wary camaraderie.
