Title: Unstrung Heroes (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Diane Keaton
Main Cast: Andie MacDowell, John Turturro, Michael Richards, Nathan Watt, Maury Chaykin
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Steven Lidz, unhappy with his home life since his mother got sick, goes and lives with his two crazy Uncles. There he changes and gets closer to his Uncles, but his parents want him home even though he is finally happy and popular. Written by Todd Weiser
