Title: The Lost World (1960)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Irwin Allen
Main Cast: Michael Rennie, Jill St. John, David Hedison, Claude Rains, Fernando Lamas
Keywords: central and south america, professor, dinosaur

Overview: Professor Challenger leads an expedition of scientists and adventurers to a remote plateau deep in the Amazonian jungle to verify his claim that dinosaurs still live there.
