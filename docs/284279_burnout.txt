Title: Burnout (2014)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Hallvard Bræin
Main Cast: Anders Baasmo Christiansen, Ida Husøy, Sven Nordin, Otto Jespersen, Jenny Skavlan
Keywords: street race, family, car, race, illegal street racing

Overview: For all who remember Cannonball Run. Here is a modern twist on the subject. The longest, wildest and funniest car race, ever. From Oslo to the North Cape. The only rule is getting there first!
