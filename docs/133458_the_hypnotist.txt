Title: The Hypnotist (2012)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Lasse Hallström
Main Cast: Tobias Zilliacus, Mikael Persbrandt, Lena Olin, Helena af Sandeberg, Jonatan Bökman
Keywords: trauma, psychologist, hypnotist, broken ice, hypnotize

Overview: After a young woman and her parents are murdered by a killer determined to wipe out the entire family, Detective Inspector Joona Linna works with a psychiatrist to hypnotize the son who narrowly escaped death in order to find the one surviving daughter before the murderer does.
