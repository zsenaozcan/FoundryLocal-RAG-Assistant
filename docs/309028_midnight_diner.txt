Title: Midnight Diner (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Joji Matsuoka
Main Cast: Kaoru Kobayashi, Toshiki Ayata, Mansaku Fuwa, Yutaka Matsushige, Tamae Ando
Keywords: restaurant, based on tv series

Overview: Every night, Master (Kaoru Kobayashi) is busy with customers at the restaurant he runs. He has a worried face, looking at the funerary urn which someone left at his restaurant. His customers also talk about the funerary urn.
