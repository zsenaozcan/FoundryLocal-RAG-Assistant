Title: Clouds of Sils Maria (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Olivier Assayas
Main Cast: Juliette Binoche, Kristen Stewart, Chloë Grace Moretz, Lars Eidinger, Brady Corbet
Keywords: suicide, mountain, aging, divorce, gender ideals, actress

Overview: A veteran actress comes face-to-face with an uncomfortable reflection of herself when she agrees to take part in a revival of the play that launched her career 20 years earlier.
