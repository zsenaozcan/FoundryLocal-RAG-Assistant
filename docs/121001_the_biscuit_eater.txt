Title: The Biscuit Eater (1940)
Genres: Family, Drama
Director: Stuart Heisler
Main Cast: Billy Lee, Cordell Hickman, Richard Lane, Lester Matthews, Helene Millard
Keywords: boy, father

Overview: Two little boys have faith in a dog they name Promise, so much faith that they enter him in the championship trials for bird dogs. The favorite is Georgia Boy bred and trained by the boys' fathers. And if Georgia Boy doesn't win, the fathers may both lose their jobs.
