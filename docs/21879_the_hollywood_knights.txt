Title: The Hollywood Knights (1980)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Floyd Mutrux
Main Cast: Robert Wuhl, Tony Danza, Fran Drescher, Michelle Pfeiffer, Joyce Hyser
Keywords: teen movie

Overview: Led by their comedic and pranking leader, Newbomb Turk, the Hollywood Knights car gang raise hell throughout Beverly Hills on Halloween Night, 1965. Everything from drag racing to Vietnam to high school love.
