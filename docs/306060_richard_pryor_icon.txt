Title: Richard Pryor: Icon (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Richard Pryor, George Lopez, Tracy Morgan, George Wallace, Louie Anderson
Keywords: stand-up comedy, dark comedy

Overview: Richard Pryor's impact on the craft of comedy and today's top comics is legendary and unrivaled. This program surveys the profound and enduring influence of one of the greatest American comics of all time.
