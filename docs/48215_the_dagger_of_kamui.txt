Title: The Dagger of Kamui (1985)
Genres: Animation, Action
Director: Rintaro
Main Cast: Hiroyuki Sanada, Taro Ishida, Yuriko Yamamoto, Ichirô Nagai, Kaneto Shiozawa
Keywords: based on novel, treasure, revenge, murder, shinobi, shogunate

Overview: Young Jiro finds his mother and sister murdered at home; falsely accused, he flees to a new life and trains to be an assassin.
