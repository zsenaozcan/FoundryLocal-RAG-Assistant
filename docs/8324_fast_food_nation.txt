Title: Fast Food Nation (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Thriller
Director: Richard Linklater
Main Cast: Catalina Sandino Moreno, Wilmer Valderrama, Ana Claudia Talancón, Greg Kinnear, Juan Carlos Serrán
Keywords: usa, texas, illegal immigration, illegal employment, fast food restaurant, butcher, hamburger, junk food

Overview: Inspired by author Eric Schlosser's New York Times best-seller of the same name, director Richard Linklater's ensemble drama examines the health issues and social consequences of America's love affair with fast food and features an all-star cast that includes Greg Kinnear, Ethan Hawke, Kris Kristofferson, Patricia Arquette, and Luis Guzman.
