Title: According to Greta (2009)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Nancy Bardawil
Main Cast: Hilary Duff, Evan Ross, Ellen Burstyn, Michael Murphy, Tsianina Joelson
Keywords: suicide, grandparents, love, romance, summer, teenager, woman director, rebellious teenager

Overview: Greta is a waitress who falls for an ambitious cook at the restaurant where they work. But as their summer romance heats up, she has to overcome the concerns of her grandparents about her boyfriend's criminal past.
