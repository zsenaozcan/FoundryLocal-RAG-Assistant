Title: For Sale (1998)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Mystery
Director: Laetitia Masson
Main Cast: Sandrine Kiberlain, Sergio Castellitto, Aurore Clément, Chiara Mastroianni, Mireille Perrier
Keywords: detective, interview, french, mystery, disappearance, wedding, woman director

Overview: A Private detective is hired to trace a woman who ran away and disappeared on her wedding day. The movie follows him and recounts the story of her life through her eyes and the eyes of those interviewed by the detective.
