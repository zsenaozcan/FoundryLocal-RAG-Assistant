Title: Death Clique (2014)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Doug Campbell
Main Cast: Tina Ivlev, Barbara Alyn Woods, Lexi Ainsworth, Brittany Underwood
Keywords: jealousy, manipulation, murder

Overview: Inspired by true events, a friendship rivalry between three high school girls escalates into a shocking act of violence, and soon one of them is dead. Now the dead girl's mom is determined to find her missing child... and get justice for her daughter.
