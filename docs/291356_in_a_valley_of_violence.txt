Title: In a Valley of Violence (2016)
Genres: Crime, Thriller, Western, Action
Director: Ti West
Main Cast: Ethan Hawke, Taissa Farmiga, James Ransone, Karen Gillan, John Travolta
Keywords: civil war, revenge, murder, violence, killer, dog, cowboy

Overview: The story of a drifter named Paul who arrives in a small town seeking revenge on the thugs who murdered his friend. Sisters Mary Anne and Ellen, who run the town's hotel, help Paul in his quest for vengeance.
