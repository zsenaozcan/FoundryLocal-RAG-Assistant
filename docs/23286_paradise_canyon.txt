Title: Paradise Canyon (1935)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Carl Pierson
Main Cast: John Wayne, Marion Burns, Reed Howes, Earle Hodgins, Gino Corrado
Keywords: counterfeiting

Overview: John Wyatt is a government agent sent to smash a counterfeiting operation near the Mexican border. Joining Doc Carter's medicine show they arrive in the town where Curly Joe, who once framed Carter, resides.
