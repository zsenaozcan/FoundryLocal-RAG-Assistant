Title: Gorillas in the Mist (1988)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Apted
Main Cast: Sigourney Weaver, Bryan Brown, Julie Harris, John Omirah Miluwi, Iain Cuthbertson
Keywords: gorilla, menace, poacher, jungle, sympathy, extinction, gorillas

Overview: The story of Dian Fossey, a scientist who came to Africa to study the vanishing mountain gorillas, and later fought to protect them.
