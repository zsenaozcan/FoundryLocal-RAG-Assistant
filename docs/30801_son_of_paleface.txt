Title: Son of Paleface (1952)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Western
Director: Frank Tashlin
Main Cast: Bob Hope, Jane Russell, Roy Rogers, Trigger, Bill Williams
Keywords: gold, sequel, street shootout

Overview: In this sequel to "The Paleface", Peter Potter Jr. and Mike 'The Torch' Delroy return as the lead characters. Junior Potter returns to claim his father's gold, which is nowhere to be found; Mike 'The Torch' Delroy is the luscious head of a gang of thieves; and Roy Barton is a federal marshal hot on Mike's trail.
