Title: Peacock (2010)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Michael Lander
Main Cast: Cillian Murphy, Ellen Page, Susan Sarandon, Josh Lucas, Bill Pullman
Keywords: schizophrenia

Overview: A man with a split personality fools his small town into believing his two alter egos are man and wife, although a struggling young mother holds the key to his past and sparks a battle between the personalities.
