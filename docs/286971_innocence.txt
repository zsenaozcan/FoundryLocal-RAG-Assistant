Title: Innocence (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Mystery, Adventure, Romance, Horror, Fantasy
Director: Hilary Brougher
Main Cast: Sophie Curtis, Kelly Reilly, Graham Phillips, Linus Roache, Sarah Sutherland
Keywords: high school, blood, car accident, woman director

Overview: After losing her mother in a tragic accident, Beckett Warner realizes that her troubles may be far worse—her school is run by a coven of beautiful women who perpetuate their youth by drinking the blood of virgins.
