Title: Don Quijote cabalga de nuevo (1973)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Roberto Gavaldón
Main Cast: Fernando Fernán Gómez, Cantinflas, María Fernanda D'Ocón, Paca Gabaldón, Ricardo Merino
Keywords: spain, based on novel, horse, castle, adventure, knight, don quixote, imaginary, sancho panza, old age

Overview: A new vision of the Knight of the sad figure, which lives obsessed by the chivalry and its codes of honor. Accompanied by his unusual squire Sancho Panza, Don Quixote recalls some of the adventures they've shared.
