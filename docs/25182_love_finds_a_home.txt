Title: Love Finds A Home (2009)
Genres: Drama, Action, Western, Family
Director: David S. Cass Sr.
Main Cast: Sarah Jones, Haylie Duff, Jordan Bridges, Patty Duke

Overview: In this sequel, Lillian has been adopted and it's several years later.  Annie, married and pregnant, visits her fellow doctor friend, Dr. Owen.  Dr Owen desperately wants a baby,  but seems unable to become pregnant. Lillian finds a love interest,  an assistant to her adoptive dad, the latter has become quite overprotective.
