Title: The Waiting Room (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Zeki Demirkubuz
Main Cast: Zeki Demirkubuz, Nilüfer Açıkalın, Nurhayat Kavrak

Overview: Zeki Demirkubuz plays the lead character Ahmet who wants to make a film about Dostoyevsky's 'Crime and Punishment'. He falls into a deep depression, loses interest in the film and life, pushes those who love him away and cannot complete the film.
