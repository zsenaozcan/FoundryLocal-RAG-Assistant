Title: Careless Love (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: John Duigan
Main Cast: Nammi Le, Peter O'Brien, Andrew Hazzard, David Field, Geraldine Hakewill

Overview: Linh (Nammi Le) is a Vietnamese Australian university student who secretly starts part-time work as an escort. She develops a close rapport with one of her clients, an enigmatic American art dealer, who books her on a regular basis. For a time she manages to keep her two lives in separate compartments. But when she falls for a fellow student her worlds collide and she must deal with the emotional chaos that follows.
