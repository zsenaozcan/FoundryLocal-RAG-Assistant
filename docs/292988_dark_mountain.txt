Title: Dark Mountain (2013)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Tara Anaïse
Main Cast: Sage Howard, Shelby Stehlin, Andrew Simpson
Keywords: mountain, arizona, mine, woman director

Overview: In March of 2011, three filmmakers disappeared in the Superstition Mountains of Arizona while documenting their search for the Lost Dutchman mine. Their bodies were never found... but their camera was.
