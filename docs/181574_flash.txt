Title: Flash (1997)
Genres: Family, Drama
Director: Simon Wincer
Main Cast: Lucas Black, Brian Kerwin, Tom Nowicki, Shawn Toovey, Von Coulter
Keywords: horse, horse racing

Overview: A boy falls in love with a horse named Flash that's for sale. He gets a job to earn the money to buy the horse, but he's forced to sell when the family falls upon hard times.
