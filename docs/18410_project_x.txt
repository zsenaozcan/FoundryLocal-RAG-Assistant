Title: Project X (1987)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jonathan Kaplan
Main Cast: Matthew Broderick, Helen Hunt, Willie, William Sadler, Johnny Ray McGhee
Keywords: chimp

Overview: A young inductee into the military is given the task of looking after some chimpanzees used in the mysterious "Project X". Getting to know the chimps fairly well, he begins to suspect there is more to the secret project than he is being told.
