Title: Scarlet Street (1945)
Genres: Drama
Director: Fritz Lang
Main Cast: Edward G. Robinson, Joan Bennett, Dan Duryea, Margaret Lindsay, Jess Barker
Keywords: painter, roommate, pimp, death sentence, femme fatale, murder, electric chair, scam, new york city, classic noir

Overview: A man in mid-life crisis befriends a young woman after rescuing her from an attack. She believes he is rich and her boyfriend persuades her to con him out of his fortune.
