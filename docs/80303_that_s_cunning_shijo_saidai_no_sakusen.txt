Title: That's Cunning! Shijo Saidai no Sakusen? (1996)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Hiroshi Sugawara
Main Cast: Tatsuya Yamaguchi, Namie Amuro

Overview: Comedy about a group of chemistry students who organize a cheating scam during their final exams. Amuro Namie, the top pop music sensation, plays one of the students.
