Title: Woman in the Moon (1929)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Science Fiction, Romance
Director: Fritz Lang
Main Cast: Willy Fritsch, Gerda Maurus, Klaus Pohl, Fritz Rasp, Gustav von Wangenheim
Keywords: moon, future, space travel

Overview: A scientist discovers that there's gold on the moon, he builds a rocket to fly there, but there's too much rivalry among the crew to have a sucessful expedition.
