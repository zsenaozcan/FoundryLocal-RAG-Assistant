Title: Bachelor Party 2: The Last Temptation (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: James Ryan
Main Cast: Josh Cooke, Harland Williams, Greg Pitts, Sara Foster, Emmanuelle Vaugier

Overview: Ron Simmons (Josh Cooke) is ready to settle down and get married, but first, he must survive his out-of-control bachelor party. With his best buds shoving booze, women and more booze in his face.Stripper fights, a sex addicts' convention and a suddenly-coed shake-your-booty competition all lead to solid laughs in this outrageous sequel to the 1984 comedy hit Bachelor Party.
