Title: The Devil's Hand (2014)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Christian E. Christiansen
Main Cast: Rufus Sewell, Alycia Debnam-Carey, Adelaide Kane, Leah Pipes, Thomas McDonell
Keywords: supernatural, suspense

Overview: Five young Amish girls accused of being Satan's children must fight for their lives when their devout community elders insist they be "cleansed" before turning 18.
