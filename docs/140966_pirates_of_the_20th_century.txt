Title: Pirates of the 20th Century (1980)
Genres: Adventure, Action
Director: Boris Durov
Main Cast: Nikolai Yeryomenko Ml., Pyotr Velyaminov, Talgat Nigmatulin, Georgi Martirosyan, Vladimir Episkoposyan

Overview: A Soviet cargo ship carrying medical opium gets attacked by pirates of an unknown nationality. The crew is left to die on a sinking ship but they manage to escape and now must fight the pirates for survival.
