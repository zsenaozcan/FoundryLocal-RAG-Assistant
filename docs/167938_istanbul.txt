Title: Istanbul (1985)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Marc Didden
Main Cast: Brad Dourif, Dominique Deruddere, Ingrid De Vos

Overview: Willy, a student, quits his vacationjob to go to Istanbul with the sinister yet facinating Klamski. On the way from Ostend to Arlon some chance meetings unpuzzle the truth about Klamski, but Willy turns out to have a dark side as well.
