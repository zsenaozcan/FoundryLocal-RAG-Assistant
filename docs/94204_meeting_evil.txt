Title: Meeting Evil (2012)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Chris Fisher
Main Cast: Leslie Bibb, Samuel L. Jackson, Luke Wilson, Peyton List

Overview: Follows disillusioned young family man John as a mysterious stranger, Richie takes him on a murder-fueled ride that transforms the weak-willed John into a desperate hero willing to go to any length to protect his family.
