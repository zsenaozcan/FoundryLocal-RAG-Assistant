Title: Murder, Inc. (1960)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Stuart Rosenberg
Main Cast: Stuart Whitman, May Britt, Peter Falk, Henry Morgan, David J. Stewart
Keywords: organized crime

Overview: Chronicles the rise and fall of the organised crime syndicate known as Murder, Incorporated, focusing on powerful boss Lepke and violent hit man Reles.
