Title: Ballerina (1937)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Jean Benoît-Lévy
Main Cast: Yvette Chauviré, Mia Slavenska, Jeanine Charrat

Overview: As its title indicates, La Mort du Cygne (The Dying Swan) is set in the special world of the ballet. Young dance student Janine Charrat idolizes her teacher-role-model Yvette Chauvire, and will do anything to help Chauvire further herself.
