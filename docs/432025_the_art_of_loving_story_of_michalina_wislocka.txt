Title: The Art of Loving. Story of Michalina Wislocka (2017)
Genres: Drama
Director: Maria Sadowska
Main Cast: Magdalena Boczarska, Piotr Adamczyk, Justyna Wasilewska, Eryk Lubos, Jaśmina Polak
Keywords: biography, sex education

Overview: Michalina Wislocka, the most famous and recognized sexologist of communist Poland, fights for the right to publish her book, which will change the sex life of Polish people forever.
