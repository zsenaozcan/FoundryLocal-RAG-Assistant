Title: One Night Surprise (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Jin Yimeng
Main Cast: Fan Bingbing, Aarif Lee, Pace Wu, Jiang Jinfu, Daniel Henney
Keywords: woman director

Overview: After her birthday party, ad executive Michelle finds herself caught between cheerful Tony, dreamy Bill, youthful Jeb, and rich Tiger. Overwhelmed with stress, another complication quietly forms in her life.
