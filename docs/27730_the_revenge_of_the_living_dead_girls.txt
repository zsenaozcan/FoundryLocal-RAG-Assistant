Title: The Revenge of the Living Dead Girls (1987)
Genres: Horror, Foreign
Director: Pierre B. Reinhard
Main Cast: Cornélia Wilms, Véronique Catanzaro, Patrick Guillemin, Kathryn Charly, Sylvie Novak
Keywords: zombie

Overview: A company, not wanting to pay for their chemicals to be dumped properly, put some into a truck of milk, which eventually kills three girls. The girls then return from the dead to seek bloody revenge.
