Title: Jasmine Women (2004)
Genres: Drama, Family, Romance
Director: Hou Yong
Main Cast: Zhang Ziyi, Joan Chen, Jiang Wen, Liu Ye, Lu Yi
Keywords: shanghai, family saga

Overview: Zhang Ziyi plays the youngest of three generations of women who leads lives in Shanghai. Joan Chen plays the great-grandmother, grandmother, and mother. The film recounts this family, the mistakes they make, and a cycle that the granddaughter breaks out of.
