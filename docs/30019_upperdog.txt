Title: Upperdog (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Sara Johnsen
Main Cast: Hermann Sabado, Agnieszka Grochowska, Mads Sjøgård Pettersen, Bang Chau, Yngvild Støen Grotmol
Keywords: woman director

Overview: As young children, half-siblings Axel and Yanne are adopted to Norway. They are separated on arrival, he to material wealth on Oslo's west side, she to an average family on the east side. In contrast to her younger brother, Yanne remembers their journey to Norway, but she has no idea where he might be now.
