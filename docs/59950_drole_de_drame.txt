Title: Drole de Drame (1937)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Marcel Carné
Main Cast: Françoise Rosay, Louis Jouvet, Jean-Louis Barrault, Michel Simon, Jean-Pierre Aumont

Overview: A French farce set in Victorian London where a botanist and his wife get into trouble when they pretend to go missing in order to hide from their sanctimonious cousin - an Anglican bishop who is leading a campaign against such writing.
