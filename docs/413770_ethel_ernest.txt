Title: Ethel & Ernest (2016)
Genres: War, Animation, Drama
Director: Roger Mainwood
Main Cast: Jim Broadbent, Brenda Blethyn, Luke Treadaway, Pam Ferris, Roger Allam

Overview: This hand drawn animated film, based on the award winning graphic novel by Raymond Briggs, is an intimate and affectionate depiction of the life and times of his parents, two ordinary Londoners living through extraordinary events.
