Title: Katie Tippel (1975)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Paul Verhoeven
Main Cast: Monique van de Ven, Rutger Hauer, Andrea Domburg, Hannah de Leeuwe, Jan Blaaser

Overview: In the late 19th century, a young woman moves to Amsterdam with her family and tries to make a living. Preyed upon by various men, she nonetheless rises in society.
