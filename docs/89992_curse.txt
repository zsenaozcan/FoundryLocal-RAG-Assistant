Title: Curse (1987)
Genres: Horror
Director: Kong Yeung
Main Cast: Susan Brandy, Maria Isabel Lopez, Maria Yuen Chi-Wai, Eliza Yue Chi-Wai, Hung San-Nam

Overview: A woman tries to attract her reincarnated lover from her previous life but eventually turns to the aid of an evil sorcerer who rules over a tribe of cannibal midgets.
