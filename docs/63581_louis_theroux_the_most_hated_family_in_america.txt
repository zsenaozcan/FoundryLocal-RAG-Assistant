Title: Louis Theroux: The Most Hated Family in America (2007)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Geoffrey O'Connor
Main Cast: Louis Theroux, Fred Phelps
Keywords: bible, protest, god, homosexuality, southern baptist, biblical code, dead soldier

Overview: Louis meets the Phelps family — the people at the heart of the controversial Westboro Baptist Church. The Phelps have rabid anti-homosexual beliefs, and often campaign at the funerals of American soldiers. They believe that every tragedy in the world is God's punishment for homosexuality.
