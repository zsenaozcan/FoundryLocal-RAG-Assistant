Title: Old Ironsides (1926)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: James Cruze
Main Cast: Charles Farrell, Esther Ralston, Wallace Beery, George Bancroft, Charles Hill Mailes
Keywords: sea, ship, epic

Overview: The film is a wide-ranging ensemble piece, with Farrell and Beery playing equally large roles, and detailed subplots about the country's atmosphere before the conflict as well as Stephen Decatur's early naval exploits.
