Title: Santa Fe Trail (1940)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: Errol Flynn, Olivia de Havilland, Alan Hale, Ronald Reagan, John Litel
Keywords: fight, slavery, fanatic, war

Overview: As a penalty for fighting fellow classmates days before graduating from West Point, J.E.B. Stuart, George Armstrong Custer and four friends are assigned to the 2nd Cavalry, stationed at Fort Leavenworth. While there they aid in the capture and execution of the abolitionist, John Brown following the Battle of Harper's Ferry.
