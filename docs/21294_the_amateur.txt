Title: The Amateur (1981)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Charles Jarrott
Main Cast: John Savage, Christopher Plummer, Marthe Keller, Arthur Hill, Nicholas Campbell
Keywords: suspense

Overview: A researcher for the CIA who convinces his superiors to send him to the eastern bloc in order to avenge the murder of his wife by enemy agents discovers a web of deception underneath his wife's death.
