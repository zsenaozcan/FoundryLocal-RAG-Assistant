Title: Cry Wilderness (1987)
Genres: Fantasy, Adventure, Family
Director: Jay Schlossberg-Cohen
Main Cast: Eric Foster, Maurice Grandmaison, John Tallman, Griffin Casey, Faith Clift
Keywords: bigfoot, independent film

Overview: A Bigfoot-type creature befriends Paul, a young student. His father is a park ranger trying to capture an escaped tiger. Everyone in town is on edge and wants the tiger killed. David tries to keep Bigfoot a secret.
