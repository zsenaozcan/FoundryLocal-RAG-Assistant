Title: It's Impossible to Learn to Plow by Reading Books (1988)
Genres: Drama
Director: Richard Linklater
Main Cast: Richard Linklater, James Goodwin, Dan Kratochvil, Linda Finney, Tracy Crabtree
Keywords: independent film

Overview: In its minimal and non-traditional plot, the main character travels about the country meeting with various acquaintances when not taking part in various mundane, day-to-day activities. There is no real rising action or climax, and Linklater's character does not change substantially throughout the course of the film.
