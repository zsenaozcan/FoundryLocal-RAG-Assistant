Title: Pocahontas II: Journey to a New World (1998)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Romance, Family
Director: Bradley Raymond
Main Cast: Irene Bedard, Jim Cummings, Donal Gibson, Finola Hughes, Linda Hunt

Overview: When news of John Smith's death reaches America, Pocahontas is devastated. She sets off to London with John Rolfe, to meet with the King of England on a diplomatic mission: to create peace and respect between the two great lands. However, Governor Ratcliffe is still around; he wants to return to Jamestown and take over. He will stop at nothing to discredit the young princess.
