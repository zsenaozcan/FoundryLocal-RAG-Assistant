Title: A Very Brady Sequel (1996)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Arlene Sanford
Main Cast: Shelley Long, Gary Cole, Christopher Daniel Barnes, Christine Taylor, Paul Sutera
Keywords: hawaii, wedding, family vacation, family, woman director

Overview: A man claiming to be Carol Brady's long-lost first husband, Roy Martin, shows up at the suburban Brady residence one evening. An impostor, the man is actually determined to steal the Bradys' familiar horse statue, a $20-million ancient Asian artifact.
