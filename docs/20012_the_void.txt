Title: The Void (2001)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Gilbert M. Shilton
Main Cast: Adrian Paul, Amanda Tapping, Andrew McIlroy, Kirsten Robek, Roger Cross
Keywords: black hole, physicist

Overview: Physicist Eva Soderstrom discovers greedy industrialist Thomas Abernathy is on the verge of creating an artificial black hole in a laboratory on Earth. It's the same experiment that killed her father years earlier, except bigger. With the help of Dr. Price, Eva tries to stop Abernathy and, possibly, save the planet
