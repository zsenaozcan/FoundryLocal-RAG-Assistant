Title: Kill Me Please (2010)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Olias Barco
Main Cast: Aurélien Recoing, Virginie Efira, Benoît Poelvoorde, Bouli Lanners, Philippe Nahon
Keywords: black humor, suicidal

Overview: Doctor Kruger dreams to insert “the suicide in modernity”. He offers to his patients the service of a private clinic where one can die in all peace, champagne glass to the hand. But in the private clinic of “ideal death”, nothing occurs as envisaged.
