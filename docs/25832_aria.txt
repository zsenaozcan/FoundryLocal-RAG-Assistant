Title: Aria (1987)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Music
Director: Jean-Luc Godard
Main Cast: Theresa Russell, Bridget Fonda, Buck Henry, Elizabeth Hurley, Stephanie Lane
Keywords: suicide, gambling, sex, assassin, infidelity, secret, eroticism, opera, nightclub, nudity

Overview: Ten short pieces directed by ten different directors, including Ken Russell, Jean-Luc Godard, Robert Altman, Bruce Beresford, and Nicolas Roeg. Each short uses an aria as soundtrack/sound (Vivaldi, Bach, Wagner), and is an interpretation of the particular aria.
