Title: Strings (2004)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Anders Rønnow Klarlund
Main Cast: James McAvoy, Catherine McCormack, Julian Glover, Derek Jacobi, Ian Hart

Overview: Strings is a mythological story about the son of a king, Hal Tara, who sets out on a journey to avenge the death of his father. To his surprise he discovers the truth of his own people - and where he least expects it - he finds true love.
