Title: The Fighting Temptations (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Jonathan Lynn
Main Cast: Beyoncé Knowles, Cuba Gooding Jr., Wendell Pierce, LaTanya Richardson, Faith Evans
Keywords: church choir

Overview: The Fighting Temptations stars Cuba Gooding, Jr. as the film's protagonist as he attempts to revive a church choir in order to enter a gospel competition with the help of a beautiful lounge singer (Beyoncé Knowles) with whom he soon falls in love.
