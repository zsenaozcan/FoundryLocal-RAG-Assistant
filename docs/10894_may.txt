Title: May (2002)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller, Romance
Director: Lucky McKee
Main Cast: Angela Bettis, Jeremy Sisto, Anna Faris, James Duval, Nichole Hiltz
Keywords: difficult childhood, psychoterror, murder, loneliness, artificial

Overview: Psychological horror about a lonely young woman traumatized by a difficult childhood, and her increasingly desperate attempts to connect with the people around her.
