Title: Child of God (2014)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: James Franco
Main Cast: Scott Haze, Wade Williams, James Franco, Tim Blake Nelson, Jim Parrack
Keywords: necrophilia, deep south

Overview: A dispossessed, violent man's life is a disastrous attempt to exist outside the social order. Successively deprived of parents and homes and with few other ties, Ballard descends to the level of a cave dweller as he falls deeper into crime and degradation.
