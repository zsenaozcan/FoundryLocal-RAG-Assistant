Title: West of Zanzibar (1928)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: Tod Browning
Main Cast: Lon Chaney, Lionel Barrymore, Mary Nolan, Warner Baxter, Jacqueline Gadsden
Keywords: magic, silent film

Overview: Silent film (with musical score) about the vengefulness of a cuckolded magician (Lon Chaney) paralyzed in a brawl with his rival (Lionel Barrymore).
