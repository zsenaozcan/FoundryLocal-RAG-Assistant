Title: The Magic Lantern (1903)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: Georges Méliès
Main Cast: Georges Méliès
Keywords: silent film, trick film

Overview: Two impish clowns construct a magic lantern. They prop it up at an angle, and use it to project pictures onto a wall. When the picture show ends, they open up the lantern to reveal a group of dancing girls inside - and this is only the first of the indications that this lantern really is magical.
