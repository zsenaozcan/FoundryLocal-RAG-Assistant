Title: A Yank in the R.A.F. (1941)
Genres: War, Action, Drama, Romance
Director: Henry King
Main Cast: Tyrone Power, Betty Grable, John Sutton, Reginald Gardiner, Donald Stuart
Keywords: royal air force, bomber pilot

Overview: Tyrone Power is a pilots' pilot, but he doesn't believe in anything beyond his own abilities. He gets into trouble by flying a new fighter directly to Canada instead of to New York and letting it be towed across as the law demands, but is offered a new job ferrying bombers to war torn England. While on a layover he finds Betty Grable, an old flame, has joined the RAF as a WREN in her attempt to fight for democracy. Power joins up to impress her and in the course of his several missions begins to develope an understanding of what they are fighting for.
