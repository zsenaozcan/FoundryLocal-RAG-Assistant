Title: A Complicated Girl (1969)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Damiano Damiani
Main Cast: Florinda Bolkan, Catherine Spaak, Jean Sorel, Gigi Proietti, María Cuadra

Overview: Alberto intercepts a call between two troubled women. Intrigued by the situation, he decides to meet one of two girls, Claudia, and becomes her lover. The two begin a relationship the leads from one twisted game to another.
