Title: The Fighting Seabees (1944)
Genres: Action, War
Director: Edward Ludwig
Main Cast: John Wayne, Susan Hayward, Dennis O'Keefe, William Frawley, Leonid Kinskey
Keywords: japanese, world war ii, u.s. navy, pacific island

Overview: Construction workers in World War II in the Pacific are needed to build military sites, but the work is dangerous and they doubt the ability of the Navy to protect them. After a series of attacks by the Japanese, something new is tried, Construction Battalions (CBs=Seabees). The new CBs have to both build and be ready to fight.
