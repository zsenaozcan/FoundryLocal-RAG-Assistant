Title: The Guardian (1990)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: William Friedkin
Main Cast: Jenny Seagrove, Dwier Brown, Carey Lowell, Brad Hall, Miguel Ferrer

Overview: The idyllic lives of Phil and Kate seem complete when they select the winsome young Camilla as a live-in nanny for their newborn child. But the lovely young Camilla s not what she appears to be.
