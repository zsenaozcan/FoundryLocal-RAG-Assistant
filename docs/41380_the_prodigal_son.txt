Title: The Prodigal Son (1981)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Sammo Hung
Main Cast: Yuen Biao, Lam Ching-Ying, Frankie Chan Fan-Kei, Sammo Hung, Peter Chan Lung
Keywords: martial arts, kung fu, hand to hand combat

Overview: A rich man's son (Yuen Biao) believes himself to be the best kung fu fighter in Canton. Unfortunately, his father, anxious for his son's safety, bribes all his opponents to lose. After a humiliating defeat at the hands of an actor in a traveling theatre company, the son resolves to find a better teacher. Furious kung fu battles and slapstick comedy.
