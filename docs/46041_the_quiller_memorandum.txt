Title: The Quiller Memorandum (1966)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Michael Anderson
Main Cast: George Segal, Alec Guinness, Max von Sydow, Senta Berger, George Sanders
Keywords: spy

Overview: Two British agents are murdered by a mysterious Neonazi organization in West Berlin. The British Secret Service sends agent Quiller to investigate. Soon Quiller is confronted with Neonazi chief "Oktober" and involved in a dangerous game where each side tries to find out the enemy's headquarters at any price.
