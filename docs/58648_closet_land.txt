Title: Closet Land (1991)
Genres: Drama
Director: Radha Bharadwaj
Main Cast: Alan Rickman, Madeleine Stowe
Keywords: suspense, woman director

Overview: A young writer is interrogated by a sadistic secret policeman. She is accused of embedding political messages in her children's stories. The entire movie takes place in one room, with only the two actors. The movie is set in an unidentified, modern police state.
