Title: The Howards of Virginia (1940)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Frank Lloyd
Main Cast: Cary Grant, Martha Scott, Cedric Hardwicke, Alan Marshal, Richard Carlson
Keywords: government, surveyor, virginian

Overview: Beautiful young Virginian Jane steps down from her proper aristocratic upbringing when she marries down-to-earth surveyor Matt Howard. Matt joins the Colonial forces in their fight for freedom against England. Matt will meet Jane's father in the battlefield.
