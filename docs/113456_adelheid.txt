Title: Adelheid (1970)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: František Vláčil
Main Cast: Petr Čepek, Emma Cerná, Jan Vostrčil, Jana Krupičková, Pavel Landovský
Keywords: locomotive, czechoslovakia, comeback, forest, german occupation, post war, military, old mansion, demographic movements, inscribed cross

Overview: In the aftermath of World War II, a former Czech soldier takes charge of a manor formerly owned by a German family. He falls in love with the daughter, who is now a maid, and is forced to confront the stress between his love and his conscience when he discovers her sheltering her German-soldier brother.
