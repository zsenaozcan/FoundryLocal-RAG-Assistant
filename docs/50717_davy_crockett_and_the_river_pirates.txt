Title: Davy Crockett and the River Pirates (1956)
Genres: Comedy, Action, Adventure, Family
Director: Norman Foster
Main Cast: Fess Parker, Buddy Ebsen, Jeff York, Kenneth Tobey, Clem Bevans
Keywords: river, davy crockett

Overview: Davy Crockett and his sidekick Georgie compete against boastful Mike Fink ("King of the River") in a boat race to New Orleans. Later, Davy and Georgie, allied with Fink, battle a group of river pirates trying to pass themselves off as Native Americans. Written by Ken Miller
