Title: Down House (2001)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Roman Kachanov
Main Cast: Fyodor Bondarchuk, Ivan Okhlobystin, Anna Buklovskaya, Aleksandr Bashirov, Mikhail Vladimirov
Keywords: based on novel

Overview: The plot is set in modern Moscow, in the 1990s, with "New Russians", Hummer H1 SUVs, bribery, violence, truck fulls of tinned stew as a dowry, etc.
