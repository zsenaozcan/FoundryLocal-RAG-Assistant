Title: Hallettsville (2009)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Andrew Pozza
Main Cast: GiGi Erneta, Gary Busey, Derek Lee Nixon, Katie Fountain, Jordan Brower

Overview: Something awaits Tyler Jensen and his friends in the Hallettsville house. When Tyler's lifelong friends start dying, he must figure out a way to save himself, his girlfriend and the rest of the crew before the demons come.
