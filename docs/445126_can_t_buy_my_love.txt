Title: Can't Buy My Love (2017)
Genres: Romance
Director: Farhad Mann
Main Cast: Adelaide Kane, Benjamin Hollingsworth, Jean Louisa Kelly, James Remar
Keywords: emt

Overview: Lilly, a hard working EMT, is only focused on her job and getting into medical school. So when wealthy slacker Jeff enters her life it throws her off course. At first, Lilly wants nothing to do with Jeff or his sense of entitlement and lack of direction. As she spends more time with him, she starts to realize that you can’t always judge a book by its cover.
