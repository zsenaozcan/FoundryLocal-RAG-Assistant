Title: Firehouse Dog (2007)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Family
Director: Todd Holland
Main Cast: Josh Hutcherson, Bruce Greenwood, Bill Nunn, Scotch Ellis Loring, Teddy Sears
Keywords: human animal relationship, dog, arsonist

Overview: Rexxx, Hollywood's top canine star, gets lost and is adopted into a shabby firehouse. He teams up with a young kid (Hutcherson) to get the station back on its feet.
