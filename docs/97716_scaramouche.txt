Title: Scaramouche (1923)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Rex Ingram
Main Cast: Ramon Novarro, Alice Terry, Lewis Stone, Lloyd Ingraham, Julia Swayne Gordon
Keywords: seduction, french revolution, duel, silent film, king louis xvi, aristocracy

Overview: A law student becomes an outlaw French revolutionary when he decides to avenge the unjust killing of his friend. To get close to the aristocrat who has killed his friend the student adopts the identity of Scaramouche the clown.
