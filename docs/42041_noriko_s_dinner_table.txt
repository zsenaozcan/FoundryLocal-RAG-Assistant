Title: Noriko's Dinner Table (2005)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Horror
Director: Sion Sono
Main Cast: Kazue Fukiishi, Tsugumi, Yuriko Yoshitaka, Shirô Namiki, Sanae Miyata
Keywords: suicide, cult, internet

Overview: A teenager called Noriko Shimabara runs away from her family in Tokoyama, to meet Kumiko, the leader of an Internet BBS, Haikyo.com. She becomes involved with Kumiko's "family circle", which grows darker after the mass suicide of 54 high school girls.
