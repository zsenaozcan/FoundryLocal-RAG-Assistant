Title: Blancanieves (2012)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy
Director: Pablo Berger
Main Cast: Maribel Verdú, Macarena García, Daniel Giménez Cacho, Ángela Molina, Inma Cuesta
Keywords: matador, reference to snow white, bull fight

Overview: A black and white silent movie, based on the Snow White fairy tale, that is set in a romantic version of 1920s Seville and centered on a female bullfighter.
