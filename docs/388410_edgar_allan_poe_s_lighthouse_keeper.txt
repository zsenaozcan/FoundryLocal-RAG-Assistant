Title: Edgar Allan Poe's Lighthouse Keeper (2016)
Genres: Horror
Director: Benjamin Cooper
Main Cast: Vernon Wells, Matt O'Neill, Rachel Riley, Monty Wall, Carl Edge

Overview: Marooned on a remote peninsula and haunted by frightening specters, a young man must confront the grotesque denizens of the night, or heed the Lighthouse Keeper's cryptic warning to, 'Always keep a light burning!'
