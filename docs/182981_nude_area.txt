Title: Nude Area (2014)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Urszula Antoniak
Main Cast: Sammy Boonstra, Imaan Hammam, Benjamin de Wit
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Naomi, a fifteen year-old Dutch girl from South-Amsterdam, develops a crush on a beautiful Arabic girl of the same age living in the poor quarters of Amsterdam West. During one magical summer in Amsterdam, the two girls play an emotional chess game of love, seduction and attraction.
