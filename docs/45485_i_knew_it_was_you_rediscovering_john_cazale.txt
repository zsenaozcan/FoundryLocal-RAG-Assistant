Title: I Knew It Was You: Rediscovering John Cazale (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Richard Shepard
Main Cast: John Cazale, Al Pacino, Meryl Streep, Robert De Niro, Olympia Dukakis
Keywords: 1970s, biography, friendship, tribute, cancer, film, john cazale, actor, short

Overview: John Cazale was in only five films - The Godfather, The Conversation, The Godfather, Part Two, Dog Day Afternoon, and The Deer Hunter - each was nominated for Best Picture. Yet today most people don't even know his name. I KNEW IT WAS YOU is a fresh tour through movies that defined a generation.
