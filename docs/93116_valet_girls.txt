Title: Valet Girls (1987)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Rafal Zielinski
Main Cast: Mary Kohnert, Jack DeLeon, Jon Sharp, Michael Karm, Steve Lyon
Keywords: musician, malibu, aspiring singer, female protagonist, valet, female singer, female musician
