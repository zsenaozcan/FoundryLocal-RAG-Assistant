Title: Captain Blood (1960)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: André Hunebelle
Main Cast: Jean Marais, Bourvil, Elsa Martinelli, Pierrette Bruno, Lise Delamare

Overview: Le capitan is a 1960 French-Italian swashbuckler film directed by André Hunebelle and starring Jean Marais, Bourvil, Elsa Martinelli and Lise Delamare. It is based on a novel by Michel Zévaco.
