Title: The Professional (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Dušan Kovačević
Main Cast: Borivoje Todorović, Branislav Lečić, Наташа Нинковић, Dragan Jovanović, Josif Tatić

Overview: Till recently a University professor, a bohemian writer, a member of Belgrade's intellectual circles and a passionate opponent of the Milosevic's regime meets the man, unknown to him, but who knows every tiny detail of his life. Man tells him a story and plot unfolds unimaginably.
