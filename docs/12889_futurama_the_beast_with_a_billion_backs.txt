Title: Futurama: The Beast with a Billion Backs (2008)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Science Fiction, Fantasy
Director: Peter Avanzino
Main Cast: Billy West, Katey Sagal, John DiMaggio, Maurice LaMarche, Lauren Tom
Keywords: saving the world

Overview: Fresh off ripping space-time a new one at the end of "Bender's Big Score," the Planet Express crew is back to mend the tear in reality, or (hopefully) at least not make it worse.  Beyond the tear, though, lurks a being of inconceivable...tentacularity.  What will become of Earth, and indeed, our universe, when faced with the Beast with a Billion Backs?
