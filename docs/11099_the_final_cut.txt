Title: The Final Cut (2004)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Omar Naim
Main Cast: Robin Williams, Mira Sorvino, Jim Caviezel, Mimi Kuzyk, Genevieve Buechner
Keywords: star, microchip, dying and death, boss, filmteam

Overview: Set in a world with memory implants, Robin Williams plays a cutter, someone with the power of final edit over people's recorded histories. His latest assignment is one that puts him in danger.
