Title: The Chocolate Soldier (1941)
Genres: Music
Director: Roy Del Ruth
Main Cast: Nelson Eddy, Risë Stevens, Nigel Bruce, Florence Bates, Dorothy Raye
Keywords: musical, opera singer

Overview: Maria and Karl Lang are the singing duo of Vienna. Maria is very flirtatious and Karl very jealous. Karl decides to masquerade as a Russian guardsman and attempts to make Maria flirt with him - to test her loyalty to him - as the Russian, Karl makes a vigorous attempt to seduce Maria. For a moment she accepts then rejects. Karl is left in turmoil...
