Title: One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest (1975)
Genres: Drama
Director: Miloš Forman
Main Cast: Jack Nicholson, Louise Fletcher, Danny DeVito, William Redfield, Scatman Crothers
Keywords: individual, rebel, self-destruction, wheelchair, lunatic asylum, dying and death, rage and hate, freedom, insanity, basic rights and human rights

Overview: While serving time for insanity at a state mental hospital, implacable rabble-rouser, Randle Patrick McMurphy inspires his fellow patients to rebel against the authoritarian rule of head nurse, Mildred Ratched.
