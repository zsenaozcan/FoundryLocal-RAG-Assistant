Title: Thunderball (1965)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Thriller
Director: Terence Young
Main Cast: Sean Connery, Claudine Auger, Adolfo Celi, Luciana Paluzzi, Rik Van Nutter
Keywords: paris, florida, fighter pilot, sanatorium, secret organization, nuclear missile, coral reef, bahamas, scuba diving, scuba
