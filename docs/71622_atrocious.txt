Title: Atrocious (2010)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Fernando Barreda Luna
Main Cast: Rafael Amaya, Jose Masegosa, Chus Pereiro, Cristian Valencia
Keywords: country house, supernatural, woods, paranormal, blood, family vacation, found footage

Overview: Quintanilla family was found dead in their country side house. Police reported the existence of 37 hours of recorded evidence. The family traveled to their old farm house, where they investigated about the Legend of the Girl in the Garraf woods. They used to record whatever they did. Atrocious shows the last five days of this family, and the strange situations that they lived in those moments and which remain unexplained.
