Title: The Houses Are Full of Smoke (1987)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Allan Francovich
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: A powerful three-part documentary studying the US involvement in Guatemala, El Salvador and Nicaragua. The differing factions - Sandinista leaders, Guatemalan campesinos, CIA operatives, Contras and US government apologists - are interviewed and, in the absence of a controlling narration, the audience is encouraged to draw its own conclusions.
