Title: Slacker 2011 (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Amy Grappell
Main Cast: Sonny Carl Davis, David Zellner, Nathan Zellner, Don Hertzfeldt
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Richard Linklater's SLACKER inspired a generation of American filmmakers by exploring the subculture of Austin, Texas in a loose narrative with a tapestry of quirky characters. Celebrating the 20th anniversary of that iconic movie, 24 of Austin's top filmmakers banded together to update SLACKER with their own perspectives on the city.
