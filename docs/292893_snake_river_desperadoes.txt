Title: Snake River Desperadoes (1951)
Genres: Western
Director: Fred F. Sears
Main Cast: Charles Starrett, Don Reynolds, Tommy Ivo, Monte Blue, Smiley Burnette
Keywords: the durango kid

Overview: Starrett tries to prevent a range war between settlers and the Native Americans. Blue and his fellow scoundrels think they can profit from the bloodshed,but the Durango Kid along with a couple of precocious youngsters put an end to Blue's terrorism.
