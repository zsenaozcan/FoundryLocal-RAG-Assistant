Title: When Love Is Not Enough: The Lois Wilson Story (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: John Kent Harrison
Main Cast: Winona Ryder, Barry Pepper, John Bourgeois, Rosemary Dunsmore, Paul Popowich

Overview: Based on the true story of the enduring but troubled love between Lois Wilson, co-founder of Al-Anon, and her alcoholic husband Bill Wilson, co-founder of Alcoholics Anonymous.
