Title: Beatriz at Dinner (2017)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Miguel Arteta
Main Cast: Salma Hayek, John Lithgow, Connie Britton, Jay Duplass, Amy Landecker
Keywords: immigrant, dark comedy, billionaire

Overview: Beatriz, an immigrant from a poor town in Mexico, has drawn on her innate kindness to build a career as a health practitioner. Doug Strutt is a cutthroat, self-satisfied billionaire. When these two opposites meet at a dinner party, their worlds collide, and neither will ever be the same.
