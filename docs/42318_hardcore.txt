Title: Hardcore (2004)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Dennis Iliadis
Main Cast: Katerina Tsavalou, Danai Skiadi, Ioannis Papazisis, Omiros Poulakis
Keywords: lesbian relationship, prostitution

Overview: Leaving behind a hard life with their families, two young girls end up in a brothel, fall in love and support one another against the adversities and violence of the night.
