Title: The Three Stooges in Orbit (1962)
Genres: Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Edward Bernds
Main Cast: Moe Howard, Larry Fine, Joe DeRita, Emil Sitka, Carol Christensen
Keywords: three stooges

Overview: The fate of the planet in the hands of Larry, Moe and Curly Joe? That's exactly the situation the trio finds themselves in when they befriend a wacky scientist and must defend his secret invention from a pair of malevolent Martians. Sight gags, slapstick and plenty of nyuks abound as the Stooges bumble their way through an adventure of intergalactic proportions.
