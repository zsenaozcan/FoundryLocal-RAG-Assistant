Title: An American Tail: The Treasure of Manhattan Island (1998)
Genres: Family, Animation, Adventure
Director: Larry Latham
Main Cast: Thomas Dekker, Dom DeLuise, Pat Musick, Nehemiah Persoff, Erica Yohn

Overview: Fievel and his friend Tony Toponi find a map that they believe points to a treasure buried somewhere beneath Old New York, and the plucky rodent is determined to find it. However, what he discovers under the city is a tribe of Native American mice who were driven underground by prejudiced European immigrants.
