Title: The Billionaire (2011)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Comedy
Director: Songyos Sugmakanan
Main Cast: Pachara Chirathivat, Walanlak Kumsuwan, Pieak Poster, Somboonsuk Niyomsiri
Keywords: business start-up

Overview: The Billionaire tells the story of Aitthipat Kulapongvanich and how he, at the age of nineteen, dropped out from university to launch a packaged fried seaweed business that is now Taokaenoi Food &amp; Marketing and became one of Thailand's youngest (baht) billionaires
