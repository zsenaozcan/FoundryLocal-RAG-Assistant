Title: Maria (2003)
Genres: Drama
Director: Călin Peter Netzer
Main Cast: Diana Dumbrava, Horatiu Malaele, Serban Ionescu, Luminița Gheorghiu, Rona Hartner

Overview: When a child steals candles from the cemetery, in order to provide some light means for his family sheltered into a damp basement of a block of flats, this circumstance can say a lot about the sufferance imposed by transition period of time in Romania. This child is one of the seven that Maria has got. She is a 33 years old ordinary woman, whose husband (Ion) is unemployed and has become a drunkard and violently acting, maybe because of his despairing and lack of prospective.
