Title: The Bolshoi (2017)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Valery Todorovsky
Main Cast: Alisa Freyndlikh, Aleksandr Domogarov, Nicolas Le Riche, Margarita Simonova, Anna Isaeva

Overview: Julia is incredibly talented. She can become a great ballerina. But for this purpose she must give up everything: the interests of the family, her first love, friendship. Over and over again the heroine will face a complex choice between the dream of beauty and real life.
 The history of the rise of a young provincial girl to the stage of the Bolshoi Theatre. A film about dreams, beauty, and ballet.
