Title: Invaders from Mars (1953)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: William Cameron Menzies
Main Cast: Jimmy Hunt, Arthur Franz, Helena Carter, Leif Erickson, Hillary Brooke
Keywords: flying saucer, invasion, alien life-form, mind control, ufo, spaceship, alien, independent film, martian, surprise ending

Overview: In the early hours of the night, young David Maclean sees a flying saucer land and disappear into the sand dunes just beyond his house. Slowly, all of the adults, including his once loving parents, begin to act strangely.
