Title: The Men Who Tread on the Tiger's Tail (1945)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Akira Kurosawa
Main Cast: Denjirô Ôkôchi, Susumu Fujita, Kenichi Enomoto, Masayuki Mori, Takashi Shimura
Keywords: farce

Overview: Yoshitsune Minamoto, disguised with his retinue as monks, must make do with a comical porter as their guide through hostile territory en route to safety.
