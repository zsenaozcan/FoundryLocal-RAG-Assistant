Title: Black Legion (1937)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: Humphrey Bogart, Ann Sheridan, Dick Foran, Erin O'Brien-Moore, Helen Flint
Keywords: inventor, immigrant, drugstore, fear, radio broadcast, hate group, machinist, foreman, machine shop, polish american

Overview: When a hard-working machinist loses a promotion to a Polish-born worker, he is seduced into joining the secretive Black Legion, which intimidates foreigners through violence.
