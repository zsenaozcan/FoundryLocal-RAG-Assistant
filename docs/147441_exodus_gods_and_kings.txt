Title: Exodus: Gods and Kings (2014)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action
Director: Ridley Scott
Main Cast: Christian Bale, Joel Edgerton, John Turturro, Aaron Paul, Ben Mendelsohn
Keywords: moses, bible, ancient egypt, 3d, ramses
