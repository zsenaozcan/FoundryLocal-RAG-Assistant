Title: Dead Man's Shoes (2004)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime
Director: Shane Meadows
Main Cast: Paddy Considine, Gary Stretch, Toby Kebbell, Jo Hartley, Seamus O'Neill
Keywords: rage and hate, brother, revenge, punishment, home movie footage
