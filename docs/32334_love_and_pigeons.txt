Title: Love and Pigeons (1984)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Vladimir Menshov
Main Cast: Aleksandr Mikhaylov, Nina Doroshina, Sergei Yursky, Natalya Tenyakova, Lyudmila Gurchenko
Keywords: rural setting

Overview: Shown a life in the remote siberian village. Vasia, who never leave his family, village, comes to pass summer holiday on the Black Sea. New world, new people - so, hi's temted by a half-crazy woman (Raisa Zaharovna). And after short time Vasia returns to his wife and children.
