Title: The Iron Commissioner (1978)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Stelvio Massi
Main Cast: Maurizio Merli, Janet Ågren, Ettore Manni, Chris Avram

Overview: Pretty solid Merli/Massi collaboration. The plot is a simple one that centers on various kidnapping episodes. With each scenario, the stakes increase for Merli's character and he must race against the clock to resolve each standoff.
