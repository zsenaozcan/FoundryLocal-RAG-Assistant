Title: Raffles (1930)
Genres: Adventure, Crime, Romance
Director: George Fitzmaurice
Main Cast: Ronald Colman, Kay Francis, Bramwell Fletcher, Frances Dade, David Torrence

Overview: A distinguished English gentleman has a secret life--he is the notorious jewel thief the press has dubbed "The Amateur Cracksman". When he meets a woman and falls in love he decides to "retire" from that life, but an old friend comes to him with a predicament that entails him committing one last job.
