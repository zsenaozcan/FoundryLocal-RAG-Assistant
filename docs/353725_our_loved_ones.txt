Title: Our Loved Ones (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Anne Émond
Main Cast: Maxim Gaudette, Karelle Tremblay, Valerie Cadieux, Mickaël Gouin, Louise Turcot
Keywords: suicide, depression, quebec, family secrets, death of father, family drama, woman director, life challenges

Overview: 1978. In a small village in Bas-St-Laurent, Guy’s tragic death is a shock for the Leblanc family. For many years, the real cause of his death is kept hidden from some members of the family, including his son David. The latter in turn starts his own family with his wife Marie. He lovingly raises his children Laurence and Frédéric, but deep within him harbours a persistent melancholy.
