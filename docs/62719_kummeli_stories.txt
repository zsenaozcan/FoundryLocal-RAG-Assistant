Title: Kummeli Stories (1995)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Matti Grönberg
Main Cast: Heikki Hela, Timo Kahilainen, Heikki Silvennoinen, Mari Turunen, Heikki Vihinen

Overview: The war between Luxembourg and Finland sets the outline to a set of comedic stories about a rally team, gay couple's fishing trip, two hoodlums' last school days in the spring and a haunted hotel among others.
