Title: Täällä Pohjantähden alla II (2010)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Timo Koivusalo
Main Cast: Ilkka Koivula, Vera Kiiskinen, Risto Tuorila, Hannu-Pekka Björkman

Overview: Akseli Koskela returns from prison and tries to learn living again. He's not allowed to take part in politics anymore, but he sees a lot of injustice around him. He raises a family with his wife Elina while another war is around the corner...
