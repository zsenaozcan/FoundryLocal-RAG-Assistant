Title: It’s Only Make Believe (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Arild Østin Ommundsen
Main Cast: Silje Salomonsen, Vegar Hoel, Tomas Alf Larsen, Ole Romsdal, Egil Birkeland
Keywords: prison

Overview: After serving 10 years in prison for murder, Jenny returns to society. She wants to live a quiet life and resume her responsibilities as a mother, but soon shadows from the past start to appear.
