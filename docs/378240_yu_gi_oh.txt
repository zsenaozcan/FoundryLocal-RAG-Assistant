Title: Yu☆Gi☆Oh! (1999)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Fantasy
Director: Junji Shimizu
Main Cast: Yumi Kakazu, Konomi Maeda, Hikaru Midorikawa, Toshiyuki Morikawa, Yukana Nogami
Keywords: card game, shounen, anime
