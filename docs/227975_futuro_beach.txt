Title: Futuro Beach (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Karim Aïnouz
Main Cast: Wagner Moura, Clemens Schick, Jesuíta Barbosa, Sabine Timoteo, Sophie Charlotte Conrad
Keywords: germany, gay, portugal, drowning, nudity, travel, brothers

Overview: Donato fails in his attempt to save a drowning man, and meets one of the man's friends. He decides to start his life over, but pieces of his past keep coming after him.
