Title: The Killings at Outpost Zeta (1980)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller

Main Cast: Gordon Devol, Jacqueline Ray, Jackson Bostwick, Stan Wojno, Hildy Brooks
Keywords: monster, future, spaceship, creature, z movie
