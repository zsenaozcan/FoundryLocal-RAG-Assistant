Title: The Similars (2015)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Isaac Ezban
Main Cast: Gustavo Sánchez Parra, Luis Alberti, Carmen Beato, Fernando Becerril, Humberto Busto

Overview: A science fiction thriller that takes place in 1968´s Mexico and deals with identity in a metaphorical way, as the plot involves a mysterious condition that makes all persons locked inside a bus station on a rainy night to adopt the same face and features.
