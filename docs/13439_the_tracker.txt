Title: The Tracker (2002)
Genres: Western, Drama, Foreign, History
Director: Rolf de Heer
Main Cast: David Gulpilil, Gary Sweet, Damon Gameau, Grant Page, Noel Wilton
Keywords: independent film

Overview: The film is set in the early 20th century Australian outback, where four men are following an Aboriginal man who has been accused of murder. The characters in the film are referred to by their characters rather than by name.
