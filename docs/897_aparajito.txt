Title: Aparajito (1956)
Genres: Drama
Director: Satyajit Ray
Main Cast: Kanu Bannerjee, Karuna Bannerjee, Smaran Ghosal, Pinaki Sengupta, Santi Gupta
Keywords: based on novel, loss of mother, loss of father, varanasi, pneumonia, loss of husband, scholarship, india

Overview: The Unvanquished is the second part of the Bangladesh Apu film series about a boy named Apu who gets a scholarship to study at a College in Calcutta. His mother is alone and torn between wanting him to be successful and not wanting him to leave her alone.
