Title: The Girl on the Train (2014)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Larry Brand
Main Cast: Henry Ian Cusick, Nicki Aycox, Stephen Lang, Charles Aitken

Overview: When documentary filmmaker Danny Hart boards a train at Grand Central Station he has no reason to expect anything more than a pleasant ride to upstate New York to interview the subjects for his latest project. But a chance encounter with a mysterious young woman leads him on a journey of a very different sort.
