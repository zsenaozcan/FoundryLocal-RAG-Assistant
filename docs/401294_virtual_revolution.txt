Title: Virtual Revolution (2016)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Guy-Roger Duvert
Main Cast: Mike Dopud, Jane Badler, Jochen Hägele, Maximilien Poullein, Kaya Blocksage
Keywords: paris, hitman, virtual reality, virtual world

Overview: Paris 2047. Most of the population spend all their time online, connected into virtual worlds, and don't care anymore about reality. A shadow agent, Nash, working for one of the multinational companies behind these virtual worlds, is tracking down terrorists who threaten the system...
