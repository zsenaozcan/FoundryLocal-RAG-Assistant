Title: The Art of Seduction (2005)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Comedy
Director: Oh Ki-hwan
Main Cast: Song Il-gook, Son Ye-jin, Ahn Sun-yeong, Noh Joo-hyun, Park Jun-gyu
Keywords: adversary, thief, romance, cons and scams, grifters, serial dating

Overview: Min-Jun and Ji-Won are first-rate "players" who are dating gurus with 100% success rates in any dating pursuits. Following her usual systematic dating rules, Ji-Won fakes a car accident to capture Min-Jun's attention and successfully approaches him. However, her smooth-sailing dating life finally encounters turbulence. Min-Jun is also overwhelmed by the understanding that he has met his match.
