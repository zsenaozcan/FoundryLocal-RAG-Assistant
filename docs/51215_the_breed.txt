Title: The Breed (2001)
Genres: Horror, Action
Director: Michael Oblowitz
Main Cast: Adrian Paul, Bokeem Woodbine, Bai Ling, Péter Halász, James Booth
Keywords: vampire

Overview: Vampires have come out of the shadows and are living as normal citizens. Two policemen, one a vampire, are assigned to track down a serial killer who tears the throat of his victims and drains their blood.
