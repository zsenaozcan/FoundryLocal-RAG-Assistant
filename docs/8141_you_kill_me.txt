Title: You Kill Me (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Thriller, Romance
Director: John Dahl
Main Cast: Ben Kingsley, Téa Leoni, Luke Wilson, Bill Pullman, Dennis Farina
Keywords: new york, san francisco, alcoholism, serial killer, alcoholic
