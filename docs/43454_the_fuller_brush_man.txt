Title: The Fuller Brush Man (1948)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Crime, Romance
Director: S. Sylvan Simon
Main Cast: Red Skelton, Janet Blair, Don McGuire, Hillary Brooke, Adele Jergens
Keywords: suspect, trap, war surplus, fuller brush

Overview: Poor Red Jones gets fired from every job he tries. His fiancée gives him one last chance to make good when he becomes a Fuller Brush man. His awkward attempts at sales are further complicated when one of his customers is murdered and he becomes the prime suspect.
