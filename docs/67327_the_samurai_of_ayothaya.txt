Title: The Samurai of Ayothaya (2010)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Nopporn Watin
Main Cast: Seigi Ozeki, Kanokkorn Jaicheun, Sorapong Chatree, Winai Kraibutr

Overview: Based on a true historic figure, Yamada Nagamasa, a Japanese adventurer who went to Ayothaya in the 16th century and became a soldier in King Naresuan's army. After the war in Burma it's restless in Ayothaya, because of a mysterious group of men that terrorizes and plunders the city. Yamada goes after them and discovers that the men are rebelious Japanese samurai. Now he has to fight his own countrymen.
