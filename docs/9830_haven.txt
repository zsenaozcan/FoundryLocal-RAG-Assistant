Title: Haven (2004)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Frank E. Flowers
Main Cast: Orlando Bloom, Anthony Mackie, Bill Paxton, Zoe Saldana, Razaaq Adoti
Keywords: business man, daughter, cayman islands, tax evasion

Overview: During a weekend, two shady businessmen flee to the Cayman Islands to avoid federal prosecution. But their escape ignites a chain reaction that leads a British native to commit a crime that changes the nation.
