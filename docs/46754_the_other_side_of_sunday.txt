Title: The Other Side of Sunday (1996)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Berit Nesheim
Main Cast: Marie Theisen, Hildegun Riise, Bjørn Sundquist, Sylvia Salvesen, Martin Dahl Granfalk
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The Other Side of Sunday, also known in Norwegian as "Søndagsengler", is a movie that criticizes the small, and often tight, church community. We follow the Preacher's Daughter, Maria, on her journey to liberate herself from the stiff church community and her father.
