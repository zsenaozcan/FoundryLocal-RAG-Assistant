Title: The Romantics (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Galt Niederhoffer
Main Cast: Katie Holmes, Anna Paquin, Josh Duhamel, Dianna Agron, Adam Brody
Keywords: cheating, wedding, romantic literature, woman director

Overview: Seven close friends reunite for the wedding of two of their friends. Problems arise because the bride and the maid of honor have had a long rivalry over the groom.
