Title: Just a Little Harmless Sex (1999)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Rick Rosenthal
Main Cast: Alison Eastwood, Robert Mailhouse, Jonathan Silverman, Kimberly Williams-Paisley, William Ragsdale
Keywords: farce, teenager, sex comedy, thong

Overview: Just a Little Harmless Sex examines a young married couple that faces a serious break-up when the husband is caught committing adultery with a prostitute.
