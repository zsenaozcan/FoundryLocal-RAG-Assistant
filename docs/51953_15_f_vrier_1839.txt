Title: 15 Février 1839 (2001)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Pierre Falardeau
Main Cast: Luc Picard, Sylvie Drapeau, Pierre Rivard , Thérèse Perreault, Frédéric Gilles
Keywords: death sentence, lower canada, 19th century

Overview: In 1839, months after the British army has repressed the insurrection of the Patriots, hundreds of rebels rot in prison. The morning of February 14, Marie-Thomas Chevalier De Lorimier and Charles Hindelang learn that they will be hung in 24 hours, with three other comrades. While they await their hour of death the condemned spend time and consult with companions and loved ones as well as entrust to those their last wills. At dawn, the five Patriots find the courage to walk with dignity towards the scaffold, knowing that will they die in the name of a just causewww.tribute.ca
