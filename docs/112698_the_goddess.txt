Title: The Goddess (1934)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yonggang Wu
Main Cast: Ruan Lingyu, Tian Jian, Zhang Zhi-Zhi, Keng Li, Junpan Li

Overview: The story of a young prostitute who is trying to give her infant son a good start in life, fighting against the prejudices of others and the attentions of a pimp.
