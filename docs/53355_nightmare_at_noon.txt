Title: Nightmare at Noon (1988)
Genres: Action, Horror, Thriller
Director: Nico Mastorakis
Main Cast: Wings Hauser, Bo Hopkins, George Kennedy, Brion James, Kimberly Beck
Keywords: helicopter, sheriff, experiment, water, water supply, violence
