Title: The Disappearance of Eleanor Rigby: Her (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ned Benson
Main Cast: Jessica Chastain, James McAvoy, Viola Davis, Isabelle Huppert, William Hurt
Keywords: new york, couple, loss of a child

Overview: Told from the female perspective, the story of a couple trying to reclaim the life and love they once knew and pick up the pieces of a past that may be too far gone.
