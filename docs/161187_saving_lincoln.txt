Title: Saving Lincoln (2013)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Salvador Litvak
Main Cast: Tom Amandes, Lea Coco, Penelope Ann Miller, Bruce Davison, Creed Bratton
Keywords: civil war, biography

Overview: The almost entirely true story of Abraham Lincoln and his self-appointed bodyguard, U.S. Marshal Ward Hill Lamon - a banjo-playing Southerner who foiled repeated attempts on the President's life, and kept him functioning during the darkest hours of the Civil War.
