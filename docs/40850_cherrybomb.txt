Title: Cherrybomb (2009)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Lisa Barros D'Sa
Main Cast: Rupert Grint, Robert Sheehan, Kimberley Nixon, James Nesbitt, Kathy Kiera Clarke
Keywords: independent film, woman director

Overview: Cherry Bomb follows teenagers Luke, Malachy, and Michelle as they embark on a wild weekend of drink, drugs, shop-lifting and stealing cars. But what starts out as a game turns deadly serious when the three discover that they can't get off the wild ride they've set in motion.
