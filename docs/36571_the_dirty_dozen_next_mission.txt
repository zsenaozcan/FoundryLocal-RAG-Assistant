Title: The Dirty Dozen: Next Mission (1985)
Genres: Action, War
Director: Andrew V. McLaglen
Main Cast: Lee Marvin, Ernest Borgnine, Ken Wahl, Larry Wilcox, Sonny Landham
Keywords: prisoner, world war ii, nazis, army, sequel, soldier, convict, tv movie, assassination plan

Overview: Major Reisman is "volunteered" to lead another mission using convicted army soldiers, sentenced to either death or long prison terms. This time their mission is to kill a Nazi general who plans to assassinate Hitler.
