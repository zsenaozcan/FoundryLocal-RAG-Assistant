Title: L'Amour Fou (1969)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Jacques Rivette
Main Cast: Bulle Ogier, Jean-Pierre Kalfon, Josée Destoop, Celia, André S. Labarthe
Keywords: long take, improvisation, director, play

Overview: L'Amour Fou follows the dissolution of the marriage between Claire, an actress (played by Bulle Ogier), and Sebastien, her director (Jean-Pierre Kalfon).
