Title: The Bloodstained Butterfly (1971)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Duccio Tessari
Main Cast: Helmut Berger, Giancarlo Sbragia, Ida Galli, Wendy D'Olive, Günther Stoll
Keywords: giallo, murder investigation, piano player, miscarriage of justice

Overview: A girl is murdered in a park. The man is tried and convicted for the crime but the killings suddenly continue.
