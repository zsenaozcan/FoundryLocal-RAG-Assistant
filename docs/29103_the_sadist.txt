Title: The Sadist (1963)
Genres: Horror
Director: James Landis
Main Cast: Arch Hall Jr., Richard Alden, Marilyn Manning, Helen Hovey, Don Russell
Keywords: sadism

Overview: Three people driving into Los Angeles for a Dodgers game have car trouble and pull off into an old wrecking yard where they are held at bay by a bloodthirsty psycho and his crazy girlfriend.
