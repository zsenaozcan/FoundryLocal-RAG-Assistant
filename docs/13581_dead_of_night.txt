Title: Dead of Night (1945)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Alberto Cavalcanti
Main Cast: Mervyn Johns, Roland Culver, Mary Merrall, Googie Withers, Frederick Valk
Keywords: dream, nightmare, insanity, ventriloquist, anthology, evil doll, suspense, ventriloquist dummy, recurring dream

Overview: Architect Walter Craig, seeking the possibility of some work at a country farmhouse, soon finds himself once again stuck in his recurring nightmare. Dreading the end of the dream that he knows is coming, he must first listen to all the assembled guests' own bizarre tales.
