Title: Too Hot to Handle (1977)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Drama
Director: Don Schain
Main Cast: Cheri Caffaro, Vic Diaz, Aharon Ipalé, Corinne Calvet, John van Dreelen
Keywords: sex, detective, assassin, nudity, exploitation, philippines, violence

Overview: Gorgeous blonde assassin Samantha Fox accepts a contract to liquidate a quintet of gangsters in the Philippines. Problems ensue when she falls in love with the Manila detective investigating the killings.
