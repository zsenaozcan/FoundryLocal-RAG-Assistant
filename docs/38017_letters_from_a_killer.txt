Title: Letters From a Killer (1998)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: David Carson
Main Cast: Patrick Swayze, Gia Carides, Roger E. Mosley, Kim Myers, Olivia Birkelund
Keywords: suspense

Overview: A man is falsely convicted of the murder of his wife. During his time in jail, he finds comfort from four women with whom he corresponds. After his second court appearance, he is finally freed from prison only to be framed for yet 2 more murders which he did not commit. With the help of his former prison guard, he once again sets out to clear his name
