Title: Sinbad (1971)
Genres: Drama
Director: Zoltán Huszárik
Main Cast: Zoltán Latinovits, Éva Ruttkai, Dajka Margit, Anna Nagy, Eva Leelossy

Overview: Based on the stories of Hungarian writer Gyula Krúdy, this iconic film is a lush and sensuous depiction of the life, loves and memories of serial seducer Szindbád.
