Title: Cell 211 (2009)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Drama
Director: Daniel Monzón
Main Cast: Luis Tosar, Alberto Ammann, Antonio Resines, Carlos Bardem, Félix Cubero
Keywords: prison cell, penitentiary, prison guard, walkie talkie, prison riot, surveillance camera, wrist slitting, forced to strip, flashback, gagged

Overview: The story of two men on different sides of a prison riot -- the inmate leading the rebellion and the young guard trapped in the revolt, who poses as a prisoner in a desperate attempt to survive the ordeal.
