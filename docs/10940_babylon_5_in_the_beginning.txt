Title: Babylon 5: In the Beginning (1998)
Genres: Action, Drama, Science Fiction, Adventure
Director: Michael Vejar
Main Cast: Bruce Boxleitner, Mira Furlan, Richard Biggs, Andreas Katsulas, Peter Jurasik
Keywords: earth, alien phenomenons, war, assault, space centre
