Title: Orwell Rolls in His Grave (2003)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Robert Kane Pappas
Main Cast: Charles Lewis, Robert McChesney, Mark Crispin Miller, Bernie Sanders, Danny Schechter

Overview: Has America entered an Orwellian world of doublespeak where outright lies can pass for truth? The country's leading intellectuals discuss and examine the mix of businesses, politics and ideology that is the mainstream media.
