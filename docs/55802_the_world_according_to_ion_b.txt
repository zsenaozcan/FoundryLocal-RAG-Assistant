Title: The World According to Ion B. (2009)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Alexander Nanau
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: artist, art, homeless man, romania

Overview: The fascinating portrait of Ion Bârlàdeanu. The touching and inspiring story of a man who literally lived in the gutter for 20 years - and in the meantime managed to create paintings and collages which are now exhibited alongside works by Andy Warhol or Marcel Duchamp.
