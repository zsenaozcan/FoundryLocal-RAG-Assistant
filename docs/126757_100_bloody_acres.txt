Title: 100 Bloody Acres (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Cameron Cairnes
Main Cast: Damon Herriman, Angus Sampson, Anna McGahan, John Jarratt, Oliver Ackland
Keywords: brother, australia, murder, gore, dog, incest

Overview: Reg and Lindsay run an organic fertiliser business. They need a fresh supply of their "secret ingredient" to process through the meat grinder. Reg comes across two guys and a girl with a broken-down vehicle on their way to a music festival.
