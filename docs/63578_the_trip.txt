Title: The Trip (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Michael Winterbottom
Main Cast: Steve Coogan, Rob Brydon, Claire Keelan, Margo Stilley, Paul Popplewell
Keywords: restaurant, independent film, on the road

Overview: When Steve Coogan is asked by The Observer to tour the country's finest restaurants, he envisions it as the perfect getaway with his beautiful girlfriend. But, when she backs out on him, he has no one to accompany him but his best friend and source of eternal aggravation, Rob Brydon.
