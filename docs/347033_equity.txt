Title: Equity (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Meera Menon
Main Cast: Anna Gunn, James Purefoy, Sarah Megan Thomas, Alysia Reiner, Sophie von Haselberg
Keywords: corruption, wall street, woman director, investment banker

Overview: Senior investment banker Naomi Bishop’s world of high-power big money is brutal and fierce, and one she thrives in. When a controversial IPO threatens the fragile balance of power and confidentiality, Naomi finds herself entangled in a web of politics and deception.
