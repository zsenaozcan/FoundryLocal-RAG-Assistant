Title: Only Two Can Play (1962)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Sidney Gilliat
Main Cast: Peter Sellers, Mai Zetterling, Virginia Maskell, Raymond Huntley, John Le Mesurier

Overview: John Lewis is bored by his librarian's job and henpecked at home. Then Liz, wife of a local councillor, sets her sights on him. But this is risky stuff in a Welsh valleys town - if he and Liz ever manage to consummate their affair, that is.
