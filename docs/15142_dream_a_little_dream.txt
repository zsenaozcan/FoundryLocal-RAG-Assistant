Title: Dream a Little Dream (1989)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Science Fiction, Romance
Director: Marc Rocco
Main Cast: Corey Feldman, Corey Haim, Jason Robards, Meredith Salenger, Piper Laurie

Overview: Bobby Kellar has a crush on Lainie Diamond, girlfriend of school jerk Joel. Coleman is working on an experiment which will help him move into a place where Dreams are reality. When an accident occurs Coleman finds himself in Bobby's body and can only contact Bobby in his dreams.
