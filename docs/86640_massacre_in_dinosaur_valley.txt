Title: Massacre in Dinosaur Valley (1985)
Genres: Horror, Action, Adventure
Director: Michele Massimo Tarantini
Main Cast: Michael Sopkiw, Suzane Carvalho, Marta Anderson, Joffre Soares, Leonidas Bayer
Keywords: tribe, jungle, airplane crash, extreme violence, cannibal, piranha, sex slave

Overview: A plane crashes in the Amazon jungle, and its passengers must battle their way through cannibals, slave traders, wild animals and murderous piranha fish to safety.
