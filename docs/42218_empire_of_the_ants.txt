Title: Empire of the Ants (1977)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Bert I. Gordon
Main Cast: Joan Collins, Robert Lansing, John David Carson, Albert Salmi, Jacqueline Scott
Keywords: florida, mutant, everglades, radioactive, independent film, b movie, ants, giant insect, real estate, florida everglades

Overview: Sleazy scam artist Joan Collins tries to sell phony real estate deals down in the Florida everglades. What she and her unsuspecting buyers don't know is the area has been taken over by giant ants!
