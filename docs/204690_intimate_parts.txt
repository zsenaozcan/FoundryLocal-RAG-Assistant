Title: Intimate Parts (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Alexey Chupov
Main Cast: Yuliya Aug, Olesya Sudzilovskaya, Nikita Tarasov, Yuri Kolokolnikov
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Contemporary Moscow, home to several people who apparently lead a totally carefree life. They have all acquired status in society, and at first sight seem to have everything they desire. But each of them is lacking something, and each has their own intimate secret.
