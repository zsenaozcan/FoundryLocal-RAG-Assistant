Title: Who Can Kill a Child? (1976)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Narciso Ibáñez Serrador
Main Cast: Lewis Fiander, Prunella Ransome, Antonio Iranzo, Miguel Narros, María Luisa Arias
Keywords: holiday, spain, based on novel, island, murder, children, small village, evil children

Overview: A couple of English tourists arrive on an island where all the children have gone crazy and are murdering the adults.
