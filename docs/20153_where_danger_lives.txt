Title: Where Danger Lives (1950)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: John Farrow
Main Cast: Robert Mitchum, Faith Domergue, Claude Rains, Maureen O'Sullivan, Charles Kemper
Keywords: on the run, memory loss, doctor, film noir

Overview: A young doctor falls in love with a disturbed young woman and apparently becomes involved in the death of her husband. They head for Mexico trying to outrun the law.
