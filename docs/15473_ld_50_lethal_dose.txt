Title: LD 50 Lethal Dose (2003)
Genres: Horror
Director: Simon De Selva
Main Cast: Katharine Towne, Melanie Brown, Tom Hardy, Ross McCall, Toby Fisher

Overview: Re-formed by a coded message to their web site, a group of animal rights activists set off to free an imprisoned colleague from a terrifying ordeal. Their rescue mission leads them to a disused lab, but what should have been a simple raid turns into a series of twisted and mind bending incidents where the free and the caged switch places, in this tormented psychological horror.
