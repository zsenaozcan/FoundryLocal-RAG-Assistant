Title: The Cheat (1915)
Genres: Drama
Director: Cecil B. DeMille
Main Cast: Fannie Ward, Jack Dean, Sessue Hayakawa, James Neill, Yutaka Abe

Overview: A venal, spoiled stockbroker's wife impulsively embezzles $10,000 from the charity she chairs and desperately turns to a Burmese ivory trader to replace the stolen money.
