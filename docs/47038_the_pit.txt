Title: The Pit (1981)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Lew Lehman
Main Cast: Sammy Snyders, Jeannie Elias, Sonja Smits, Laura Press, Laura Hollingsworth
Keywords: forest, revenge, canuxploitation

Overview: Twelve year-old Jamie Benjamin is a solitary misunderstood boy in his preteens. His classmates pick on him, his neighbors think he's weird and his parents ignore him. But now Jamie has a secret weapon: deep in the woods he has discovered a deep pit full of man-eating creatures he calls Trogs... and it isn't long before he gets an idea for getting revenge and feeding the Trogs in the process!
