Title: Growing Op (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Michael Melski
Main Cast: Steven Yaffee, Jon Cor, Wallace Langham, Rosanna Arquette, Katie Boland
Keywords: independent film

Overview: The story of a teenage boy coming of age in a suburban grow-operation, where every day is paradise or fresh hell. But it's always a trip. Sheltered all his life and home-schooled by loving parents who are also committed criminals, Quinn Dawson yearns to experience the normalcy of the suburban world which surrounds him.
