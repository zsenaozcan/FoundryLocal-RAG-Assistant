Title: Just Like a Woman (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rachid Bouchareb
Main Cast: Sienna Miller, Golshifteh Farahani, Bahar Soomekh, Tim Guinee, Roschdy Zem
Keywords: dancing, chicago, medicine, secret, immigrant, passion, highway, misery, market, marriage

Overview: Two women get on the highway heading to Santa Fe. Marilyn dreams of winning a contest held by a famous belly dancing company, while her friend, Mona, has a secret: she's a fugitive from justice - accused of her mother-in-law's death.
