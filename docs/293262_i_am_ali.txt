Title: I Am Ali (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Clare Lewins
Main Cast: Muhammad Ali, Muhammad Ali Jr., Hana Ali, Maryum Ali, Rahaman Ali
Keywords: biography, wrestling, sport, woman director

Overview: Unprecedented access to Muhammad Ali's personal archive of "audio journals" as well as interviews and testimonials from his inner circle of family and friends are used to tell the legend's life story.
