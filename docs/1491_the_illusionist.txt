Title: The Illusionist (2006)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Thriller, Romance
Director: Neil Burger
Main Cast: Edward Norton, Jessica Biel, Paul Giamatti, Rufus Sewell, Eddie Marsan
Keywords: magic, princess, love, rivalry, vienna austria, crown prince, duchess, supernatural power, childhood sweetheart, vienna

Overview: With his eye on a lovely aristocrat, a gifted illusionist named Eisenheim uses his powers to win her away from her betrothed, a crowned prince. But Eisenheim's scheme creates tumult within the monarchy and ignites the suspicion of a dogged inspector.
