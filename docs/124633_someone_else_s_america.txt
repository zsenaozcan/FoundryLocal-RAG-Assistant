Title: Someone Else's America (1995)
Genres: Drama
Director: Goran Paskaljević
Main Cast: Tom Conti, Predrag 'Miki' Manojlović, María Casares, Zorka Manojlović, Sergej Trifunović
Keywords: yugoslavia, immigration, american dream, serbia

Overview: This tale takes place in a bar. The Spanish Alonso and his blind mother run this place. Bay, who is Alonso's friend live here too. This story tells something about Alonso and Bay and the "American Dream".
