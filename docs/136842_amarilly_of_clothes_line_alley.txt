Title: Amarilly of Clothes-Line Alley (1918)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Marshall Neilan
Main Cast: Mary Pickford, William Scott, Kate Price, Ida Waterman, Norman Kerry

Overview: Amarilly comes from a large family in a working-class neighborhood. She is happy with her family and her boyfriend Terry, a bartender in a cafe. But one day she meets Gordon, a sculptor who comes from a rich family, and she begins to be drawn into the world of the upper class.
