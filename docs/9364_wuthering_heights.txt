Title: Wuthering Heights (2011)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Andrea Arnold
Main Cast: Kaya Scodelario, James Northcote, Amy Wren, Nichola Burley, Steve Evets
Keywords: england, based on novel, foster sister, moor, foster family, woman director

Overview: A poor boy of unknown origins is rescued from poverty and taken in by the Earnshaw family where he develops an intense relationship with his young foster sister, Cathy. Based on the classic novel by Emily Bronte.
