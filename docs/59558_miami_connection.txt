Title: Miami Connection (1987)
Genres: Action, Crime
Director: Y.K. Kim
Main Cast: Y.K. Kim, Vincent Hirsch, Joseph Diamand, Maurice Smith, Angelo Janotti
Keywords: ninja, blood, falling from height, computer class

Overview: In a world where ninjas dream of being rock stars and rock stars dream of being ninjas, a martial arts rock band goes up against a band of motorcycle ninjas who have tightened their grip on Florida's narcotics trade.
