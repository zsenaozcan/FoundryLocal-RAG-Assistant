Title: The Madness of King George (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, History
Director: Nicholas Hytner
Main Cast: Nigel Hawthorne, Helen Mirren, Ian Holm, Rupert Graves, Amanda Donohoe
Keywords: queen, kingdom, biography, victorian england, psychopathy

Overview: Nicholas Hytner’s much awarded 1994 drama, based on Alan Bennett's West End play. George III's erratic behaviour leads to a plot in Parliament to have him declared insane and removed from the throne.
