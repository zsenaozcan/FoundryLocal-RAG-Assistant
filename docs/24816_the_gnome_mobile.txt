Title: The Gnome-Mobile (1967)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Family
Director: Robert Stevenson
Main Cast: Walter Brennan, Matthew Garber, Karen Dotrice, Richard Deacon, Sean McClory
Keywords: dancing, brother sister relationship, laughing, magic

Overview: An eccentric millionaire and his grandchildren are embroiled in the plights of some forest gnomes who are searching for the rest of their tribe. While helping them, the millionaire is suspected of being crazy because he's seeing gnomes! He's committed, and the niece and nephew and the gnomes have to find him and free him.
