Title: American Kickboxer (1991)
Genres: Action
Director: Frans Nel
Main Cast: John Barrett, Keith Vitali, Terry Norton, Brad Morris, Ted Le Plat
Keywords: competition, martial arts, journalism, beach, training, relationship problems, sport, fighter, alcoholism, jail

Overview: Kickboxing champion B.J. is jailed for an accidental murder thanks to the testimony of his arch-nemesis Denard. A year later, B.J. is released and then challenged by Denard for $100,000. Will B.J. accept and get even with Denard?
