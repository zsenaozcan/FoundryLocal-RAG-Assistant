Title: Sex and the Teenage Mind (2002)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Donald L. Gold
Main Cast: Jay Michael Ferguson, Jordan Belfi, Allison Lange, Richard Karn, Dee Wallace

Overview: High school hottie Kellie Guthrie would never dream of giving nerdy classmate Virgil Heitmeyer a second look -- but after he takes the fall for Kellie when she's caught cheating, she's ready to reward him with an intimate thank-you.
