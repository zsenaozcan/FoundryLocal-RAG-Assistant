Title: State Fair (1933)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Henry King
Main Cast: Janet Gaynor, Will Rogers, Lew Ayres, Sally Eilers, Norman Foster
Keywords: naivety, rural setting, contest, love affair

Overview: The Frake family attend the Iowa State Fair. Father Abel enters his Hampshire boar, Blue Boy, in the hog contents. Mother Melissa enters the mincemeat competition. And their children, Margy and Wayne, find love with newspaper reporter Pat Gilbert and trapeze artist Emily Joyce. But will everyone return home safe and happy or will hearts be broken?
