Title: Hype! (1996)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Doug Pray
Main Cast: Valerie M. Agnew, Carrie Akre, Jeff Ament, Michael Anderson, Mark Arm
Keywords: grunge, rock music

Overview: This documentary examines the Seattle scene as it became the focus of a merging of punk rock, heavy metal, and innovation. Building from the grass roots, self-promoted and self-recorded until break-out success of bands like Nirvana brought the record industry to the Pacific Northwest, a phenomenon was born.
