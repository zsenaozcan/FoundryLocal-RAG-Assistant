Title: Rhapsody in August (1991)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Foreign
Director: Akira Kurosawa
Main Cast: Richard Gere, Sachiko Murase, Hisashi Igawa, Narumi Kayashima, Tomoko Ôtakara

Overview: The story centers on an elderly hibakusha, who lost her husband in the 1945 atomic bombing of Nagasaki, caring for her four grandchildren over the summer. She learns of a long-lost brother, Suzujiro, living in Hawaii who wants her to visit him before he dies.
