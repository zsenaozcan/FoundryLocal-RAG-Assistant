Title: Blue Thunder (1983)
Genres: Science Fiction, Action, Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: John Badham
Main Cast: Roy Scheider, Warren Oates, Candy Clark, Daniel Stern, Paul Roebling
Keywords: vietnam veteran, helicopter, mayor, vietnam war, security camera, los angeles

Overview: Officer Frank Murphy, an experienced LAPD helicopter pilot, is given command of the advanced new "Blue Thunder" chopper. But he begins to wonder why the LAPD would need a helicopter so powerful and why it is such a secret.
