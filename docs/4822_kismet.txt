Title: Kismet (1955)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Romance
Director: Vincente Minnelli
Main Cast: Howard Keel, Ann Blyth, Dolores Gray, Vic Damone, Sebastian Cabot
Keywords: musical, beggar, bagdad, impostor

Overview: Like a tale spun by Schehrazade, Kismet follows the remarkable and repeated changes of fortune that engulf a poor poet. It all happens in one incredible day when Kismet (Fate) takes a hand.
