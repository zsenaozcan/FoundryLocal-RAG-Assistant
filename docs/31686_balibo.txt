Title: Balibo (2009)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Robert Connolly
Main Cast: Anthony LaPaglia, Oscar Isaac, Nathan Phillips, Damon Gameau, Nick Farnell
Keywords: mass murder, war correspondent, investigation, east timor

Overview: As Indonesia prepares to invade the tiny nation of East Timor, five Australian based journalists go missing. Four weeks later, veteran foreign correspondent Roger East is lured to East Timor by the young and charismatic José Ramos-Horta to tell the story of his country and investigate the fate of the missing men.
