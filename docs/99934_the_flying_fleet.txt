Title: The Flying Fleet (1929)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Romance
Director: George W. Hill
Main Cast: Ramon Novarro, Ralph Graves, Anita Page, Alfred Allen, Wade Boteler
Keywords: united states naval academy

Overview: Six friends are to graduate the next day from the United States Naval Academy. They all hope to become aviators. When the officer of the day becomes sick, Tommy Winslow (Ramon Navarro) has to take his place, while the others go out and celebrate.
