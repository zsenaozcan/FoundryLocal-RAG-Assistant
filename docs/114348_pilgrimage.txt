Title: Pilgrimage (1933)
Genres: Drama
Director: John Ford
Main Cast: Henrietta Crosman, Heather Angel, Norman Foster, Lucille La Verne, Maurice Murphy
Keywords: arkansas

Overview: The story of an Arkansas farm woman and her son. When the son expresses his desire to marry a girl who comes from a family that the mother thinks is trash, she enrolls him in the army.
