Title: Restless Blood (1946)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Teuvo Tulio
Main Cast: Regina Linnanheimo, Eino Katajavuori, Toini Vartiainen, Harriett Philipson, Hans Strååt
Keywords: love triangle, sister sister relationship

Overview: Regina Linnanheimo plays Sylvi, whose child is run over by a bus, prompting her to drink poison. She goes blind in the process, causing her husband to lust after her much younger sister. After undergoing a dangerous operation to regain her sight, she hides her repaired vision behind dark eyeglasses, so she can spy on her sister and husband as they carry on their affair.
