Title: Karam (2005)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Sanjay F. Gupta
Main Cast: John Abraham, Priyanka Chopra, Bharat Dabholkar, Vishwajeet Pradhan, Shiney Ahuja
Keywords: bollywood

Overview: John, a retired assassin is caught in a crossfire when his boss, "Captain," is faced with threats from a rival don, Yunus. Now that everything, including his wife Shalini is at stake he must decide whether to fight back.
