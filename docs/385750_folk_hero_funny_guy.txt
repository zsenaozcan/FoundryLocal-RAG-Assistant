Title: Folk Hero & Funny Guy (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jeff Grace
Main Cast: Alex Karpovsky, Wyatt Russell, Meredith Hagner, Melanie Lynskey, Hannah Simone
Keywords: comedian, singer-songwriter, opening act, solo tour

Overview: Two artistically inclined childhood friends, a comedian and a folk-rocker respectively, set out on a tour together in hopes of regaining their "mojo" and finding love in the process.
