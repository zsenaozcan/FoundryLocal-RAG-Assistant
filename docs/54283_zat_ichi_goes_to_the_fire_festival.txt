Title: Zatôichi Goes to the Fire Festival (1970)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Drama
Director: Kenji Misumi
Main Cast: Shintarô Katsu, Tatsuya Nakadai, Reiko Ôhara, Masayuki Mori, Pîtâ
Keywords: samurai, katana, zatoichi, ronin

Overview: Zatoichi is mentored by the blind leader of a secret organization as he contends with both the Yakuza and a jealous husband.
