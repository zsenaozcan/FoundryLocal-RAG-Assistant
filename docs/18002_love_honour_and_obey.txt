Title: Love, Honour and Obey (2000)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Thriller, Crime
Director: Dominic Anciano
Main Cast: Jonny Lee Miller, Jude Law, Ray Winstone, Sean Pertwee, Rhys Ifans
Keywords: london england, gang war, ambition, organized crime, gang, crime

Overview: Jonny dreams of leaving his dead-end job as a courier. Through his childhood best friend, nephew of the notorious crime lord Ray Kreed, he wins his way into the toughest gang in North London. Hungry for action, Jonny sparks a feud between Ray's gang and a rival firm in South London headed by drug kingpin Sean and his lieutenant Matthew.
