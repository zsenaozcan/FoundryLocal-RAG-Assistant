Title: The War of the Worlds (1953)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Byron Haskin
Main Cast: Gene Barry, Ann Robinson, Les Tremayne, Lewis Martin, Robert Cornthwaite
Keywords: lasergun, space marine, dystopia, space invasion, alien invasion

Overview: The residents of a small town are excited when a flaming meteor lands in the hills, but their joy is short-lived when they discover it has passengers who are not very friendly.
