Title: Mafioso (1962)
Genres: Drama, Action, Comedy, Crime, Foreign
Director: Alberto Lattuada
Main Cast: Alberto Sordi, Norma Bengell, Carmelo Oliviero, Gabriella Conti
Keywords: factory, sicilian mafia

Overview: When a good-natured factory supervisor living in Milan with his Northern wife returns to his native Sicily, a decades' old oath forces him to fulfill a nightmarish obligation.
