Title: Fidel: The Untold Story (2001)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Estela Bravo
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Documentary about Fidel Castro, covering 40 years of Cuban Revolution. Rare Fidel Castro footage: he appears swimming with a bodyguard, visiting his childhood home and school, playing with his friend Nelson Mandela, meeting kid Elián Gonzalez, and celebrating his birthday with the Buena Vista Social Club group.
