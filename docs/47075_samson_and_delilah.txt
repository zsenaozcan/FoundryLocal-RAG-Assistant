Title: Samson and Delilah (2009)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Romance
Director: Warwick Thornton
Main Cast: Rowan McNamara, Marissa Gibson, Mitjili Napanangka Gibson, Scott Thornton, Matthew Gibson
Keywords: hunger, independent film

Overview: Samson and Delilah's world is small- an isolated community in the Central Australian desert. When tragedy strikes they turn their backs on home and embark on a journey of survival. Lost, unwanted and alone the discover that life isn't always fair, but love never judges.
