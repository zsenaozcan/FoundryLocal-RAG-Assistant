Title: Edith Stein: The Seventh Chamber (1996)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Márta Mészáros
Main Cast: Maia Morgenstern, Adriana Asti, Jan Nowicki, Elide Melli, Giovanni Capalbo
Keywords: woman director, life of saint

Overview: An expressionist biography of Edith Stein, who converted from the Jewish faith to the Catholic one and became a Carmelite sister.  She would die in a German concentration camp.
