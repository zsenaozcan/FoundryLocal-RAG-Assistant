Title: Heavenly Shift (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Márk Bodzsár
Main Cast: Ötvös András, Rába Roland, Keresztes Tamás, Sándor Zsótér, Natasa Stork
Keywords: ambulance

Overview: Death does not select, man does. Set in Budapest, Heavenly Shift offers an eerie insight into the everyday lives of a rather extraordinary ambulance crew. The films main character is Milan, a young refugee from the Balkan War, who joins a team of paramedics but inadvertently ends up involved in the funeral business in order to finance his fiancee s rescue from the hostilities.
