Title: Rock 'n' Roll High School (1979)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Allan Arkush
Main Cast: P.J. Soles, Vince Van Patten, Clint Howard, Mary Woronov, Dey Young
Keywords: rock and roll, punk, bong, rebellion, camp, ramones

Overview: A group of rock-music-loving students, with the help of the Ramones, take over their school to combat its newly installed oppressive administration.
