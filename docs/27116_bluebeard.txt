Title: Bluebeard (1972)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Horror
Director: Edward Dmytryk
Main Cast: Richard Burton, Raquel Welch, Virna Lisi, Nathalie Delon, Karin Schubert
Keywords: castle, murder

Overview: Baron von Sepper is an Austrian aristocrat noted for his blue-toned beard, and his appetite for beautiful wives. His latest spouse, an American beauty named Anne, discovers a vault in his castle that's filled with the frozen bodies of several beautiful women.
