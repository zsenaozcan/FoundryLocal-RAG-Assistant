Title: The Heart is Deceitful Above All Things (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Asia Argento
Main Cast: Asia Argento, Jimmy Bennett, Jeremy Renner, Winona Ryder, Ornella Muti
Keywords: prostitute, alcohol, rape, sex, fire, dancer, nudity, fanatic, officer, police

Overview: The dysfunctional twenty-three years old Sarah takes her six year old natural son Jeremiah from the home of his beloved foster parents with the support of the social service to live with her. Along the years, the boy shares her insane and lowlife style and is introduced to booze and drugs and mentally, physically and sexually abused by Sarah, her lovers and her religiously fanatic family.
