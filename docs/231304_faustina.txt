Title: Faustina (1968)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Luigi Magni
Main Cast: Vonetta McGee, Enzo Cerusico, Renzo Montagnani, Franco Acampora, Clara Bindi
Keywords: love triangle, interracial romance

Overview: Faustina, the offspring of a black G.I. and a Roman woman who met during the conflict of World War II, must choose between two Italian men who desperately vie for her attention.
