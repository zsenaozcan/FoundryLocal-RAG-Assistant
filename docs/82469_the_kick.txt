Title: The Kick (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Action
Director: Prachya Pinkaew
Main Cast: Jo Jae-hyeon,  Ye Ji-won, Do Shi-gang, JeeJa Yanin, Tae-joo Na
Keywords: robbery, thailand, taekwondo

Overview: Korean family made up of taekwondo experts moves to Thailand, where they set up a taekwondo gym. However, one member of the family, Taeju, wants to become a famous pop singer instead. The family becomes famous after stopping treasure robbers.
