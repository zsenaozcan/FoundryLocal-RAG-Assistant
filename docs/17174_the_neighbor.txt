Title: The Neighbor (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Eddie O'Flaherty
Main Cast: Matthew Modine, Michèle Laroque, Katie Walder, Richard Kind, Danielle Bisutti
Keywords: architect

Overview: A businessman (Modine) watches as his life begins to unravel after learning his ex-wife is going to marry his best friend. A distracting battle of wills with a real estate developer, however, might just be the thing that turns his life around.
