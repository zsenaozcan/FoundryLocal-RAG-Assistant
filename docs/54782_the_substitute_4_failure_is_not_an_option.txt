Title: The Substitute 4: Failure Is Not an Option (2001)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Robert Radler
Main Cast: Treat Williams, Angie Everhart, Patrick Kilpatrick, Grayson Fricke, Simon Rhee

Overview: A former mercenary joins a military academy as a teacher. He soon discovers a neo-Nazi group, code-named Werewolves, formed among the cadets. He also quickly finds that they have military leadership among the officers at the Academy.
