Title: God's Sandbox (2002)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Doron Eran
Main Cast: Razia Israeli, Meital Dohan, Amos Lavi, Orli Perl

Overview: A successful author, Liz, searches for her daughter, Rachel, in the Sinai Desert. There she meets a Bedouin storyteller who relays the unusual love story of a Western tourist and the son of a Bedouin Sheik named Najim. Their relationship is torn apart when the American is exposed to a tribal ritual.
