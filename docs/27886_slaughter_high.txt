Title: Slaughter High (1986)
Genres: Horror
Director: George Dugdale
Main Cast: Caroline Munro, Simon Scuddamore, Carmine Iannaccone, Donna Yeager, Gary Martin
Keywords: nerd, slasher, high school reunion

Overview: Eight different people are invited to their 10-year high school reunion at their now-closed down high school where a former student, disfigured from a prank gone wrong, is there to seek revenge.
