Title: Flesh Gordon (1974)
Genres: Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Michael Benveniste
Main Cast: Jason Williams, Suzanne Fields, Joseph Hudgins, William Dennis Hunt, Candy Samples
Keywords: earth, interior of the earth, kaiser wang, parody, erotic movie

Overview: Emperor Wang (the Perverted) is leader of the planet Porno and sends his mighty "Sex Ray" towards Earth, turning everyone into sex-mad fiends. Only one man can save the Earth, football player Flesh Gordon. Along with his girlfriend Dale Ardor and Professor Flexi-Jerkoff, they set off towards the source of the Sex Ray, unaware of the perils that face them!
