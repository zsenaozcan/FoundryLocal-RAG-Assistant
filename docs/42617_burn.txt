Title: Burn! (1969)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Gillo Pontecorvo
Main Cast: Marlon Brando, Evaristo Márquez, Norman Hill, Thomas Lyons, Renato Salvatori
Keywords: england, rebel, bank, bread, revolution, island, plantation, british, president, rebellion

Overview: The professional mercenary Sir William Walker instigates a slave revolt on the Caribbean island of Queimada in order to help improve the British sugar trade. Years later he is sent again to deal with the same rebels that he built up because they have seized too much power that now threatens British sugar interests.
