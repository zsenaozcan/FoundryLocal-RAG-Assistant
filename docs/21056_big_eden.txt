Title: Big Eden (2000)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Thomas Bezucha
Main Cast: Arye Gross, Eric Schweig, Tim DeKay, Louise Fletcher, Nan Martin
Keywords: gay, montana, independent film, gay relationship, native american

Overview: Big Eden is a tiny fictional town in northwestern Montana, as Preston Sturges or Frank Capra might have envisioned it. Timber and Cowboy country. This is the story of Henry Hart, a successful New York Artist, who returns to the town of his childhood to care for the ailing grandfather who raised him. Back in Big Eden, Henry must come to terms with his relationship to Dean Stewart, his best friend.
