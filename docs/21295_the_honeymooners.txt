Title: The Honeymooners (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: John Schultz
Main Cast: Cedric the Entertainer, Mike Epps, Gabrielle Union, Regina Hall, Eric Stoltz
Keywords: cult comedy

Overview: Working class New York bus driver Ralph Kramden is always coming up with get-rich-quick schemes for him and his best friend, Ed Norton, who's always around to help him get in (and out of) trouble.
