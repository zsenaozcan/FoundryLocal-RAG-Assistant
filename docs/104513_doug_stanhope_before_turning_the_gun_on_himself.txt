Title: Doug Stanhope: Before Turning the Gun on Himself (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Pierre Lamoureux
Main Cast: Doug Stanhope

Overview: Doug Stanhope has been a stand-up comic since 1990. Recorded in Salt Lake City, UT at The Complex on July 19, 2011, this is the follow up to the widely praised Oslo: Burning The Bridge To Nowhere.
