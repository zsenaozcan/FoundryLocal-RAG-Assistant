Title: The Trial of Joan of Arc (1962)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Robert Bresson
Main Cast: Florence Delay, Jean-Claude Fourneau, Roger Honorat, Marc Jacquier, Jean Gillibert
Keywords: heresy, torture chamber, religion, trial, interrogation, saint, martyrdom, heroine, french court, burned at the stake

Overview: A reconstruction of the trial of Joan of Arc (based entirely on the transcripts of the real-life trial), concerning Joan's imprisonment, interrogation and final execution at the hands of the English, filmed in a spare, low-key fashion.
