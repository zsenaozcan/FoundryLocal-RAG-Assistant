Title: A Day's Pleasure (1919)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Charlie Chaplin
Main Cast: Charlie Chaplin, Edna Purviance, Jackie Coogan, Babe London, Loyal Underwood
Keywords: seasickness, short, moving van

Overview: A quickly made Chaplin two-reeler to help fill a gap while working on his first feature The Kid. It's about a an outing with his wife and kids and things don't go smoothly.
