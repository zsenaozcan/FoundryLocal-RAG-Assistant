Title: Waffle Street (2015)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Eshom Nelms
Main Cast: James Lafferty, Danny Glover, Julie Gonzalo, Dale Dickey, Marshall Bell

Overview: WAFFLE STREET is the true story of Jimmy Adams, a V.P. of a $30 billion hedge fund, who loses his job and winds up working as a waiter at a waffle shop. Amidst the greasy madness of the 24-hour diner, Jimmy befriends Edward, an ex-con grill master who serves up hard lessons about life, finance, and grits.
