Title: Ararat (2002)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Atom Egoyan
Main Cast: Simon Abkarian, Charles Aznavour, Christopher Plummer, Arsinée Khanjian, David Alpay
Keywords: destruction of a civilization, turkey

Overview: A variety of characters, some close relatives, others distant strangers, are each affected by the making of a film about the Armenian Genocide of 1915.
