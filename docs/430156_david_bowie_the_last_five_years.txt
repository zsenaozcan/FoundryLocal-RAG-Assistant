Title: David Bowie: The Last Five Years (2017)
Genres: Music, Documentary
Director: Francis Whately
Main Cast: Tony Visconti, Gail Ann Dorsey, Tony Basil, Michael C. Hall, Donny McCaslin
Keywords: music, documentary, david bowie

Overview: There was nothing predictable about David Bowie - everything was designed to intrigue, to challenge, to defy all expectations. But perhaps no period in David Bowie’s extraordinary career raised more fascination, more surprise, and more questions, than the last five years.
