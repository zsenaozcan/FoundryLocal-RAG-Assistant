Title: Conquest 1453 (2012)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action, War
Director: Faruk Aksoy
Main Cast: Devrim Evin, Ibrahim Celikkol, Dilek Serbest, Recep Aktuğ, Erden Alkan
Keywords: arena, sultan, prophet, istanbul turkey, byzantium, conquest

Overview: After the death of his father Murat II, Mehmet II ascends to the Ottoman throne. After braving internal and external enemies, he decides to complete what he was destined to do: Conquer Constantinople.
