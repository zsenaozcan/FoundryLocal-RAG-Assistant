Title: Inara, the Jungle Girl (2012)
Genres: Adventure
Director: Patrick Desmarattes
Main Cast: Cali Danger, Brittany Bass, Gerald Desmarattes, Tim Ross, Brandi Alyssa Young
Keywords: exploitation, amazon women

Overview: Inara, The Jungle Girl (2012) is about a young girl in the military whose world comes crashing down when her father passes away. Offered a mission to the remote island of N'iah in order to exploit the precious oil and metals found there. Inara  discovers that the jungle and the women who inhabit it, have more in common with her than she knows.
