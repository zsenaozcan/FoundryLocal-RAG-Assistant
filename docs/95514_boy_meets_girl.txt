Title: Boy Meets Girl (1994)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: Ray Brady
Main Cast: Tim Poole, Danielle Sanderson, Margot Steinberg

Overview: A woman picks a man up in a bar and takes him back to her place. He thinks he is going to get laid, she has over plans. She proceeds to drug him and incarcerate him in a chair in her basement before torturing him, physically and phycologically. It starts as an S&amp;M excercise and goes to somewhere much much darker.
