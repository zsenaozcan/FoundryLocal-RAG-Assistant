Title: The Pill (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: J.C. Khoury
Main Cast: Noah Bean, Rachel Boston, Anna Chlumsky, Jean Brassard, S. Lue McWilliams
Keywords: pregnancy

Overview: Worried that he has gotten the free-spirited Mindy pregnant after an unprotected one-night stand, Fred feigns romantic interest and sticks by her side for twelve hours to make sure she takes both doses of the morning-after pill.
