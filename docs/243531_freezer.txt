Title: Freezer (2014)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Mikael Salomon
Main Cast: Dylan McDermott, Peter Facinelli, David McNally, Andrey Ivchenko, Pascal Petardi

Overview: Robert Saunters, a mechanic is knocked unconscious at his birthday dinner and wakes up to find himself locked up inside the restaurant's freezer.  Knowing nothing of why he's there, he realizes he has been mistaken as someone else.
