Title: The Interrupters (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Steve James
Main Cast: Tio Hardiman, Ameena Matthews, Cobe Williams, Gary Slutkin, Caprysha Anderson
Keywords: hero, social work, gang leader, honor, redemption, intervention, street crime, saving lives

Overview: The Interrupters tells the moving and surprising stories of three Violence Interrupters — former gang members who try to protect their Chicago communities from the violence they once caused.
