Title: Reindeer Games (2000)
Genres: Thriller
Director: John Frankenheimer
Main Cast: Ben Affleck, Gary Sinise, Charlize Theron, Dennis Farina, Donal Logue
Keywords: prison, robbery, role reversal, extramarital affair
