Title: Approaching the Unknown (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Mark Elijah Rosenberg
Main Cast: Mark Strong, Luke Wilson, Sanaa Lathan, Anders Danielsen Lie, Charles Baker
Keywords: mars, space travel, space mission, space

Overview: Captain William Stanaforth is on a one-way solo mission to take the first steps in colonising Mars. Like all pioneers throughout history, Stanaforth will face insurmountable odds and life and death decisions as he rockets bravely through space.
