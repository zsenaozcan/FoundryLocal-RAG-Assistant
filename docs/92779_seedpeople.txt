Title: Seedpeople (1992)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Peter Manoogian
Main Cast: Sam Hennings, Andrea Roth, Dane Witherspoon, Bernard Kates, Holly Fields
Keywords: small town, plant, transformation, forest, mind control, alien, doppelganger, seed, bed and breakfast, ultraviolet light

Overview: The citizens of Comet Valley are being taken over by seeds from an alien plant that has taken root there. A sheriff investigates the strange goings-on.
