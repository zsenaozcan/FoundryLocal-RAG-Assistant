Title: The Last Time I Saw Macao (2012)
Genres: Documentary, Thriller
Director: João Pedro Rodrigues
Main Cast: Lydie Barbara, João Rui Guerra da Mata, João Pedro Rodrigues, Cindy Scrash
Keywords: macao

Overview: Part memoir, part city symphony, part noir-ish B-movie adventure, the new feature from critically acclaimed film-making duo João Pedro Rodrigues and João Rui Guerra da Mata (To Die Like a Man) is a sensual, shape-shifting ode to one of the world's most mythic, alluring and exoticized cities.
