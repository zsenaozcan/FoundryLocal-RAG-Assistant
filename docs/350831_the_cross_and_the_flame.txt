Title: The Cross and The Flame (1957)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Armand Lohikoski
Main Cast: Tauno Palo, Tarmo Manni, Anneli Sauli, Edvin Laine

Overview: Set in early 16th century Finland, a knight Olavi Gideoninpoika meets  Mirjam Raakelintytär and falls in love. Mirjami hides in a monastery disguised as a choir boy, but is revealed by a monk Rasmus, who also desires her, and is put on trial.
