Title: Zuzu Angel (2006)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sergio Rezende
Main Cast: Patricia Pillar, Daniel de Oliveira, Leandra Leal, Alexandre Borges, Luana Piovani

Overview: "Zuzu Angel" covers the last years of the famous Brazilian fashion designer in her doomed quest for justice in the case of her activist son Stuart's arrest, torture, murder and subsequent corpse disposal by the military forces in early 1970s Rio de Janeiro, during the darkest era of Brazilian military regime and media censorship. Not even the fact that she was a widely known public figure saved her from being killed in a car crash in 1976, in one of the regime's favorite elimination techniques (many other similar "car crashes" still have to be re-investigated).
