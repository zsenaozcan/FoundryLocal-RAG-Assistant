Title: Strange Impersonation (1946)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Anthony Mann
Main Cast: Brenda Marshall, William Gargan, Hillary Brooke, George Chandler, Ruth Ford

Overview: A female research scientist conducting experiments on a new anesthetic has a very bad week. Her scheming assistant intentionally scars her face, her almost-fiancee appears to have deserted her and she finds herself being blackmailed by a women she accidentally knocked down with her car. So what is one to do?
