Title: Days of 36 (1972)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Theo Angelopoulos
Main Cast: Vangelis Kazan, Kostas Pavlou, Thanos Grammenos, Giorgos Kyritsis, Petros Zarkadis
Keywords: greece, informer, military dictatorship

Overview: The assassin of a prominent trade unionist takes a conservative MP hostage and the government prevaricates over tactics so as not to alienate potential political allies.
