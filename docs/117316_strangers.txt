Title: Strangers (2007)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Guy Nattiv
Main Cast: Liron Levo, Lubna Azabal, Mila Dekker, Patrick Albenque

Overview: Six days in the lives of an Israeli living in a kibbutz and a Palestinian living in Paris, which starts with an accidental meeting in the Berlin Subway during the World cup finals, will change them completely.
