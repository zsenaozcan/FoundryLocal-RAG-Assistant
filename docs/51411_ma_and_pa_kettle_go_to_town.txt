Title: Ma and Pa Kettle Go to Town (1950)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Charles Lamont
Main Cast: Marjorie Main, Percy Kilbride, Richard Long, Meg Randall, Gregg Martell
Keywords: ma and pa kettle

Overview: When Pa wins a jingle-writing contest, he and Ma head for New York City. They they get in trouble with gangsters when they lose some stolen money which they had already agreed to deliver to one of the thugs.
