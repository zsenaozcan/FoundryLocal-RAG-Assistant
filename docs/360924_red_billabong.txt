Title: Red Billabong (2016)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Thriller
Director: Luke Sparke
Main Cast: Dan Ewing, Tim Pocock, Jessica Green, Sophie Don, Ben Chisholm
Keywords: australian, outback

Overview: In the Australian Outback, two brothers discover old secrets and family lies. As their friends start to go missing they fear they are being stalked by someone or something from their worst nightmares - But is it just - A story? A legend? A hoax? Or is it real?
