Title: The Dam Busters (1955)
Genres: Drama, Action, History, Thriller, War
Director: Michael Anderson
Main Cast: Richard Todd, Michael Redgrave, Robert Shaw, Nigel Stock, Derek Farr
Keywords: world war ii, air force, dam, lancaster bomber, docudrama

Overview: Adapted by R.C. Sherriff from Guy Gibson's book Enemy Coast Ahead, The Dam Busters is a docudrama following the conception of a new British weapon for smashing the German dams in the Ruhr industrial complex and the execution of the raid by 617 Squadron 'The Dam Busters'
