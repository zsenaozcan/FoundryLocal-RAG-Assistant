Title: The Jazz Singer (1927)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Alan Crosland
Main Cast: Al Jolson, May McAvoy, Warner Oland, Eugenie Besserer, Otto Lederer
Keywords: new york, san francisco, dancer, parent child relationship, generations confilct, jewish life, jewish ghetto, jew

Overview: A young Jewish man is torn between tradition and individuality when his old-fashioned family objects to his career as a jazz singer. This is the first full length feature film to use synchronized sound, and is the original film musical.
