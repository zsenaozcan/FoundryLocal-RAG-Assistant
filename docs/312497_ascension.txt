Title: Ascension (2014)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Mairzee Almas
Main Cast: Tricia Helfer, Gil Bellows, Brian Van Holt, Andrea Roth, Brandon P. Bell
Keywords: miniseries

Overview: In this three-part miniseries, a young woman's murder causes the subjects of a century-long mission to populate a new world to question the true nature of the project as they approach the point of no return.
