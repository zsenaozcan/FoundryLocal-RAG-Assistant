Title: The Ordeal of Patty Hearst (1979)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Paul Wendkos
Main Cast: Dennis Weaver, Lisa Eilbacher, David Haskell, Stephen Elliott, Dolores Sutton

Overview: The story of the kidnapping of newspaper heiress Patty Hearst by members of a radical guerrilla organisation, as seen by the FBI agent in charge of her case.
