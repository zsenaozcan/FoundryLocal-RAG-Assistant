Title: Time Traveller: The Girl Who Leapt Through Time (2010)
Genres: Adventure
Director: Masaaki Taniguchi
Main Cast: Riisa Naka, Narumi Yasuda, Akiyoshi Nakao, Masanobu Katsumura, Tokio Emoto 
Keywords: time travel, erased memory, teenage romance, science labatory

Overview: Akari Yoshiyama is graduating high school soon and is expected to lead new life after she passes the university entrance exam, while her mother is working as a pharmacologist. However, her mother has a car accident and the situation is totally changed. She decides to go to 1972 to fullfil her mother's wish.
