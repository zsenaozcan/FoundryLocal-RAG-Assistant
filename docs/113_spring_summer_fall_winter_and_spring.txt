Title: Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter... and Spring (2003)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kim Ki-duk
Main Cast: Kim Ki-duk, Kim Young-min, Ha Yeo-jin, Oh Yeong-su, Seo Jae-kyeong
Keywords: buddhism, life and death, philosophy, countryside, becoming an adult, buddhist monk, temple, mountain lake, meditation, isolation

Overview: This South Korean film takes place on an isolated lake, where an old monk lives in a small floating temple. The monk has a young boy living with him, learning to become a monk. We watch as seasons and years pass by.
