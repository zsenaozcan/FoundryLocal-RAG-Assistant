Title: Sleeping Dogs (1977)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Roger Donaldson
Main Cast: Sam Neill, Nevan Rowe, Ian Mune, Warren Oates, Ian Watkin

Overview: Recluse Smith (Sam Neill) is drawn into a revolutionary struggle between guerillas and right-wingers in New Zealand. Implicated in a murder and framed as a revolutionary conspirator, Smith tries to maintain an attitude of non-violence while caught between warring factions. Written by Mike Welsch
