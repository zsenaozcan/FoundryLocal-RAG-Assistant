Title: Werner - Gekotzt wird später! (2003)
Genres: Animation, Comedy
Director: Michael Schaack
Main Cast: Klaus Büchner, Andi Feldmann, Otto Sander, Kulle Westphal, Bertram Hiese
Keywords: ford taunus, male friendship, corse, oldtimer, best friend, group of friends, tourism
