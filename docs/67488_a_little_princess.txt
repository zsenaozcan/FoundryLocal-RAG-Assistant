Title: A Little Princess (1986)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Carol Wiseman
Main Cast: Annie Lambert, Maureen Lipman, Amelia Shankley
Keywords: miniseries

Overview: Sara Crewe is the pampered darling of her father, an army colonel, and the Victorian London girls' school where he places her. But when her father dies, penniless, Sara becomes a skivvy in Miss Michin's school, befriended only by the scullery maid, Becky, her friends Ermengarde and Lottie, a little monkey, a lascar, and the mysterious man next door.
