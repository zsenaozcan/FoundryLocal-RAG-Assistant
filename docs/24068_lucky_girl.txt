Title: Lucky Girl (2001)
Genres: Drama
Director: John Fawcett
Main Cast: Elisha Cuthbert, Sherry Miller, Evan Sabba, Jonathan Whittaker, Victoria Snow
Keywords: gambling, dangerous, debt

Overview: Kaitlyn is a high school student whose obsession with gambling leads to her accumulating a mountain of debt. Her habit also causes a high degree of family tension.
