Title: Live Wire (1992)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Christian Duguay
Main Cast: Pierce Brosnan, Ron Silver, Ben Cross, Lisa Eilbacher, Michael J. Shea
Keywords: bomb, terrorist, explosive, senator, extramarital affair, bomb threat

Overview: Danny O'Neill is a bomb disposal expert assigned to a case where terrorists have developed an "invisible" liquid explosive which is activated within the human body.
