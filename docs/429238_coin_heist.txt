Title: Coin Heist (2017)
Genres: Family, Crime
Director: Emily Hagins
Main Cast: Alex Saxon, Alexis G. Zall, Sasha Pieterse, Jay Walker, Michael Cyril Creighton
Keywords: robbery, heist, based on young adult novel
