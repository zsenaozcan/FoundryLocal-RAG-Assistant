Title: The Paper Will Be Blue (2006)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, War, Foreign
Director: Radu Muntean
Main Cast: Paul Ipate, Adi Carauleanu, Dragoș Bucur, Tudor Istodor, Alexandru Potocean

Overview: Out of enthusiasm, a Militia soldier abandons his platoon and decides to fight for the cause of the Revolution. His Lieutenant and the rest of the crew look for him during the confused night of 22-23 December 1989.
