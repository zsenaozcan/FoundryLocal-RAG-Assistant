Title: Krakatoa: The Last Days (2006)
Genres: Drama, Documentary
Director: Sam Miller
Main Cast: Rupert Penry-Jones, Olivia Williams, Kevin McMonagle, Ramon Tikaram, David Dennis
Keywords: volcano, docudrama

Overview: A historical drama documentary depicting the eruption of Krakatoa volcano in 1883. The volcano was located in the Sunda strait in Indonesia and its eruption resulted in tsunami, rains of coals and ash, and ended with a very hot tsunami. The eruption killed more than 36,000 people and those survived were left with burns.
