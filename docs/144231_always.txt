Title: Always (1985)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Henry Jaglom
Main Cast: Patrice Townsend, Henry Jaglom, Joanna Frank, Alan Rachins, Melissa Leo

Overview: A middle-aged couple, on the verge of proceeding with a divorce, find themselves questioning their decision to separate when fellow friends and neighbors, oblivious to their marital troubles, assemble at their house for a 4th of July Weekend party.
