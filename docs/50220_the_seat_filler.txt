Title: The Seat Filler (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Nick Castle
Main Cast: Duane Martin, Kelly Rowland, Melanie Brown, DeRay Davis, Shemar Moore

Overview: In The Seat Filler, Derrick (Martin) is a struggling law student who takes a job as an awards show seatfiller to make ends meet. One day, he is seated next to the beautiful pop superstar Jnelle (Rowland), who mistakes him for a well-known industry executive. With an instant chemistry, the unlikely pair begin to date.
