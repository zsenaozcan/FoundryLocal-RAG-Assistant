Title: Shoot-Out At Medicine Bend (1957)
Genres: Western
Director: Richard L. Bare
Main Cast: Randolph Scott, James Craig, James Garner, Angie Dickinson, Gordon Jones
Keywords: brother, native american, faulty ammunition, saloon waiter

Overview: Director Richard L. Bare's 1957 revenge western stars Randolph Scott as an army veteran seeking the men who sold defective ammunition that caused his brother's death.
