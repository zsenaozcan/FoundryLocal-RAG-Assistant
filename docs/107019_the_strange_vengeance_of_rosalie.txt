Title: The Strange Vengeance of Rosalie (1972)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Jack Starrett
Main Cast: Bonnie Bedelia, Ken Howard, Anthony Zerbe

Overview: A traveling salesman is lured by a precocious teenage girl to her shack in the desert for some sexual escapades. However, a scuzzy biker comes along and they both find themselves dominated and tormented by him.
