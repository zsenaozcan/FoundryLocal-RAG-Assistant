Title: Set Fire to the Stars (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Andy Goddard
Main Cast: Elijah Wood, Celyn Jones, Kelly Reilly, Steven Mackintosh, Shirley Henderson
Keywords: new york, poet

Overview: An aspiring poet in 1950s New York has his ordered world shaken when he embarks on a week-long retreat to save his hell raising hero, Dylan Thomas.
