Title: Alone With People (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Drew Van Steenbergen
Main Cast: Quinn Marcus, Leigh Higginbotham, Brittany Halls, Eli Jolley, Joel Coady
Keywords: lgbt

Overview: Growing up gay in the South, a high school girl seeks the help of a therapist to come out to her family and friends in this coming-of-age, coming-of-gay story.
