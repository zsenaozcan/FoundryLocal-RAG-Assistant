Title: My Babysitter's a Vampire (2010)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Family, Fantasy, TV Movie
Director: Bruce McDonald
Main Cast: Matthew Knight, Atticus Mitchell, David Wontner, Vanessa Morgan, Ari Cohen
Keywords: babysitter, vampire, teenager

Overview: "My Babysitter's a Vampire," a comedic spin on pop culture vampires and scary movies in general, tells the story of three teens who believe their new babysitter is a real bloodsucking creature of the night -- and it's now up to them to rid their sleepy little town of the menace.
