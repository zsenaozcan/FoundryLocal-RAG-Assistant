Title: Heil (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Dietrich Brüggemann
Main Cast: Benno Fürmann, Michael Gwisdek, Michael Kind, Anna Brüggemann, Hanns Zischler
Keywords: germany, political satire, neo-nazis

Overview: An African-German Author loses his memory and is used by a modern National Socialist Party as promotion-figure for more political power over Germany.
