Title: The Italian Key (2011)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Rosa Karo
Main Cast: Gwendolyn Anslow, Joana Cartocci, Leo Vertunni, Moose Ali Khan, Joeanna Sayler
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A romantic fairy tale about a 19-year old orphan girl who, as her sole inheritance, gets an antique key that unlocks both an old Italian villa and the secrets of her family history.
