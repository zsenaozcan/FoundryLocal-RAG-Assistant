Title: All Watched Over by Machines of Loving Grace (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Adam Curtis
Main Cast: Adam Curtis, Stewart Brand, Peder Anker, Tord Björk, Daniel Botkin

Overview: A series of films about how humans have been colonized by the machines we have built. Although we don't realize it, the way we see everything in the world today is through the eyes of the computers.
