Title: Killshot (2008)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: John Madden
Main Cast: Mickey Rourke, Diane Lane, Thomas Jane, Rosario Dawson, Joseph Gordon-Levitt
Keywords: hitman, thriller, criminal

Overview: Beautiful Carmen Colson and her ironworker husband Wayne are placed in the Federal Witness Protection program after witnessing an "incident". Thinking they are at last safe, they are targeted by an experienced hit man and a psychopathic young upstart killer.
