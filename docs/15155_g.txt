Title: G (2002)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Christopher Scott Cherot
Main Cast: Richard T. Jones, Blair Underwood, Chenoa Maxwell, Andre Royo, Laz Alonso
Keywords: new york, hip-hop, long island, african american, modern day adaptation

Overview: A young Hip Hop star named Summer G falls for a middle to upper class sister while in college. After she rejects him for a fellow social climber, Summer G spends ten years building a Hip Hop empire, then moves to the Hamptons where he finds the object of his affections.
