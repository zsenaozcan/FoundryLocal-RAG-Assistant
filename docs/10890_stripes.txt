Title: Stripes (1981)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Ivan Reitman
Main Cast: Bill Murray, Harold Ramis, Warren Oates, P.J. Soles, Sean Young
Keywords: date, ex-girlfriend, u.s. army, military spoof
