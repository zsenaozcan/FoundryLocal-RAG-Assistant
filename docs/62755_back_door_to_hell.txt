Title: Back Door to Hell (1964)
Genres: Action, War, Drama
Director: Monte Hellman
Main Cast: Jimmie Rodgers, Jack Nicholson, John Hackett, Annabelle Huggins, Conrad Maga

Overview: During WWII, a three-man commando team places its trust in the hands of a band of Filipino resistors, as they try to knock out a Japenese communication center.
