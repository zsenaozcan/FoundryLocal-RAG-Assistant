Title: The Trap (1946)
Genres: Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Howard Bretherton
Main Cast: Sidney Toler, Mantan Moreland, Victor Sen Yung, Tanis Chandler, Larry J. Blake
Keywords: charlie chan

Overview: When a troupe of showgirls with their impresario and press agent vacation at a Malibu Beach resort, two of them are garroted. Charlie takes on the case assisted by Number Two Son Jimmy and faithful chauffeur Birmingham Brown.
