Title: South Central (1992)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Stephen Milburn Anderson
Main Cast: Glenn Plummer, Byron Minns, Lexie Bigham, Vincent Craig Dupree, LaRita Shelby
Keywords: gang war, los angeles

Overview: A man is put to prison for 10 years. Coming out of prison he wants to live a normal life and stop with crime but his son has yet followed the criminal path of his father.
