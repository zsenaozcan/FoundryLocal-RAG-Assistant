Title: The Mortal Storm (1940)
Genres: Drama
Director: Frank Borzage
Main Cast: Margaret Sullavan, James Stewart, Robert Young, Frank Morgan, Robert Stack
Keywords: germany, austria, professor, german, nazis, national socialism, adolf hitler, national socialist party, anti semitism, nazi party

Overview: The Roth family leads a quiet life in a small village in the German Alps during the early 1930s. When the Nazis come to power, the family is divided and Martin Brietner, a family friend is caught up in the turmoil.
