Title: Who Wants To Kill Jessie? (1966)
Genres: Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Václav Vorlíček
Main Cast: Dana Medřická, Jiří Sovák, Olga Schoberová, Juraj Višný, Karel Effa
Keywords: dream, superhero, based on comic, mad scientist, cowboy, comic strip, absurdist

Overview: In this zany Czechoslovakian comedy, a scientist invents a machine that projects a sleeping person's dream on a screen; disaster soon follows when the machine malfunctions and the cartoon-like dream characters become very real!
