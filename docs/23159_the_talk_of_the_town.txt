Title: The Talk of the Town (1942)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: George Stevens
Main Cast: Cary Grant, Jean Arthur, Ronald Colman, Edgar Buchanan, Glenda Farrell
Keywords: fire, factory, law, supreme court, murder, trial, arson, gardener, hideout

Overview: When the Holmes Woolen Mill burns down, political activist Leopold Dilg is jailed for arson (and murder; one man was lost). Escaping, Leopold hides out in the home of his childhood sweetheart Nora Shelley ...which she has just rented to unsuspecting law professor Michael Lightcap.
