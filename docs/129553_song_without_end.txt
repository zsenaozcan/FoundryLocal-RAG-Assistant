Title: Song Without End (1960)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: George Cukor
Main Cast: Dirk Bogarde, Capucine, Geneviève Page, Patricia Morison, Lyndon Brook
Keywords: composer, pianist, franz liszt

Overview: The romantic story of Hungarian pianist Franz Liszt (Dirk Bogarde), whose scandalous love affair forced him to abandon his adoring audiences.
