Title: You're Gonna Miss Me: A Film About Roky Erickson (2007)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Keven McAlester
Main Cast: Roky Erickson

Overview: Documentary about rock pioneer Roky Erickson, detailing his rise as a psychedelic hero, his lengthy institutionalization, his descent into poverty and filth, and his brother's struggle with their religious mother to improve Roky's care.
