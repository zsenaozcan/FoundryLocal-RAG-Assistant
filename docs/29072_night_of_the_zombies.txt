Title: Night of the Zombies (1981)
Genres: Horror
Director: Joel M. Reed
Main Cast: Jamie Gillis, Samantha Grey, Ryan Hilliard, Richard deFaut, Alphonso DeNoble
Keywords: nazis, zombie

Overview: Investigators search for soldiers' missing bodies, and hear unbelievable rumors about zombies. Dismissing those rumors they set out to investigate. After two men are found dead, CIA special-agent Nick Monroe is sent to flush out what are suspected to be deserters from the old U.S. Army Chemical Corps unit.
