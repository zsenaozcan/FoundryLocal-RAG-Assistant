Title: Ghost Chase (1987)
Genres: Horror, Fantasy, Science Fiction, Family
Director: Roland Emmerich
Main Cast: Jason Lively, Tim McDaniel, Leonard Lansink, Jill Whitlow, Paul Gleason

Overview: In an old Hollywood mansion, the spirit of an old family retainer inhabits an old grandfather clock. When a movie company uses the mansion for a film, the spirit inhabits the body of an alien and persuades the two filmmakers to track down an old house that will resolve a family scandal.
