Title: Don Verdean (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jared Hess
Main Cast: Sam Rockwell, Jemaine Clement, Amy Ryan, Danny McBride, Will Forte
Keywords: pastor, archaeologist, holy land

Overview: Biblical archaeologist Don Verdean is hired by a local church pastor to find faith-promoting relics in the Holy Land. But after a fruitless expedition he is forced to get creative in this comedy of faith and fraud.
