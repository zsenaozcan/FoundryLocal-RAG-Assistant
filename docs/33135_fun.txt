Title: Fun (1994)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Romance, Foreign
Director: Rafal Zielinski
Main Cast: Alicia Witt, Renée Humphrey, William R. Moses, Leslie Hope, Ania Suli
Keywords: murder, violence, rage, lost

Overview: Bonnie and Hillary are two young lost souls who meet one day and discover that they get along. They talk about their lives and run around and and keep getting really excited. Their day escalates into an eruption of violence and rage that can only be understood by the two girls.
