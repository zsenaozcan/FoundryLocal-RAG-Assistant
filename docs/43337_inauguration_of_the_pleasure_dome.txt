Title: Inauguration of the Pleasure Dome (1954)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: Kenneth Anger
Main Cast: Anaïs Nin, Marjorie Cameron, Samson De Brier, Joan Whitney, Katy Kadell
Keywords: magic, mythical creature, occult, short

Overview: Inauguration of the Pleasure Dome is a short 38 minute film by Kenneth Anger, filmed in 1954. Anger created two other versions of this film in 1966 and the late 1970s. According to Anger, the film takes the name "pleasure dome" from Samuel Taylor Coleridge's atmospheric poem Kubla Khan. Anger was inspired to make the film after attending a Halloween party called "Come as your Madness."
