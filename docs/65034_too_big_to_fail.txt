Title: Too Big to Fail (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Curtis Hanson
Main Cast: William Hurt, Paul Giamatti, James Woods, Billy Crudup, Topher Grace
Keywords: wall street, finances, financial crisis

Overview: Based on the bestselling book by Andrew Ross Sorkin, 'Too Big to Fail' offers an intimate look at the epochal financial crisis of 2008 and the powerful men and women who decided the fate of the world's economy in a matter of a few weeks.
