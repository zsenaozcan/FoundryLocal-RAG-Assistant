Title: Black Dog (1998)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Thriller
Director: Kevin Hooks
Main Cast: Patrick Swayze, Meat Loaf, Randy Travis, Gabriel Casseus, Graham Beckel
Keywords: fbi, truck

Overview: An ex-con takes a job driving a truck cross country. What he doesn't know is that the truck is filled with illegal weapons and now he must fight to survive and save his family.
