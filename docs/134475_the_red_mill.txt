Title: The Red Mill (1927)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Roscoe 'Fatty' Arbuckle
Main Cast: Marion Davies, Owen Moore, Louise Fazenda, George Siegmann, Karl Dane
Keywords: silent film, well

Overview: Marion Davies stars in this 1927 silent comedy, about a servant girl who plays matchmaker for the local burgomaster's daughter while setting her own sights on a visiting Irishman. Directed by Fatty Arbuckle (using the pseudonym "William Goodrich"), the film also features Owen Moore, Louise Fazenda and Snitz Edwards.
