Title: The Maidens' Conspiracy (2006)
Genres: Adventure
Director: Vicente Aranda
Main Cast: Casper Zafer, Esther Nubiola, Leonor Watling, Ingrid Rubio, Jane Asher

Overview: Tirante the knight is as committed to fighting the Turks in Constantinople as he is to relieving Carmesina, the heiress to the Byzantine Empire, of her virginity.
