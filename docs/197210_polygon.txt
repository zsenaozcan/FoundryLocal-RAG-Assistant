Title: Polygon (1977)
Genres: Animation
Director: Anatoliy Petrov
Main Cast: Aleksandr Belyavskiy, Anatoliy Kuznetsov, Vsevolod Yakut, Oleg Mokshantsev, Sergei Martynov

Overview: A Russian scientist creates a mind-reading weapon for his generals on a remote island. After the demonstration, the generals are pleased. Little do they realize, both the weapon, and the creator of the weapon have a mind of their own
