Title: Crows Zero (2007)
Genres: Action
Director: Takashi Miike
Main Cast: Shun Oguri, Kyôsuke Yabe, Meisa Kuroki, Takayuki Yamada, Ken'ichi Endô

Overview: The students of Suzuran High compete for the King of School title. An ex-graduate yakuza is sent to kill the son of a criminal group, but he can't make himself to do it as he reminds him of his youth.
