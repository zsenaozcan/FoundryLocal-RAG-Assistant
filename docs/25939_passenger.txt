Title: Passenger (1963)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Andrzej Munk
Main Cast: Aleksandra Śląska, Anna Ciepielewska, Janusz Bylczynski, Krzesislawa Dubielówna, Anna Golebiowska
Keywords: auschwitz

Overview: The last film of Andrzej Munk, who died in a crash during the filming. A German woman on a ship coming back to Europe notices a face of another woman which brings recollections from the past. She tells her husband that she has been an overseer in Auschwitz during the war, but she has actually saved a woman's life. Her vision is shown and then the actual events.
