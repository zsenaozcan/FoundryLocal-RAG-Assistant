Title: Click (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Fantasy, Romance
Director: Frank Coraci
Main Cast: Adam Sandler, Kate Beckinsale, Christopher Walken, David Hasselhoff, Henry Winkler
Keywords: regret, workaholic, heart attack, architect, dying and death, time travel, remote control, children, liposuction, hospital

Overview: A workaholic architect finds a universal remote that allows him to fast-forward and rewind to different parts of his life. Complications arise when the remote starts to overrule his choices.
