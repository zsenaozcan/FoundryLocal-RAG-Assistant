Title: Sweepings (1933)
Genres: Drama
Director: John Cromwell
Main Cast: Lionel Barrymore, Eric Linden, William Gargan, Gloria Stuart, Alan Dinehart
Keywords: department store

Overview: Daniel Pardway, starting with almost nothing after the great Chicago fire, builds the biggest department store in town. He wants to pass on the business to his three sons and daughter, but has to deal with their lack of interest or aptitude.
