Title: The Raven (2006)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Ulli Lommel
Main Cast: Nola Roeper, Nicole Cooke, Jack Quinn, Jillian Swanson

Overview: As a child, Lenore was tormented by nightmares and obsessed with the dark poems and stories of Edgar Allan Poe. As the lead singer in a Los Angeles band, the adult Lenore (Jillian Swanson) finds herself, friends and colleagues haunted by a murderer from beyond the grave. Only in her dreams -- in which she's visited by Poe's ghost -- will Lenore find the key to defeat her supernatural stalker and finally escape the spirits battling for her soul.
