Title: Taming of the Fire (1972)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Daniil Khrabrovitsky
Main Cast: Kirill Lavrov, Ada Rogovtseva, Igor Gorbachyov, Innokentiy Smoktunovskiy, Andrei Popov

Overview: About Russian space program and missile industry, and it's founder Sergei P. Korolev, from the 1920s to the first man in space in 1961.
