Title: The Hitcher (1986)
Genres: Action, Horror, Thriller
Director: Robert Harmon
Main Cast: C. Thomas Howell, Rutger Hauer, Jennifer Jason Leigh, Jeffrey DeMunn, John M. Jackson
Keywords: helicopter, mass murder, cop-killer, paranoia, highway, sadism, route 66, car crash, suspense, hitchhiker

Overview: A young man who escaped the clutches of a murderous hitch-hiker is subsequently stalked, framed for the hitcher's crimes, and has his life made into hell by the same man he escaped.
