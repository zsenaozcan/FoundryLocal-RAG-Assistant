Title: Nanga Parbat (2010)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Joseph Vilsmaier
Main Cast: Karl Markovics, Volker Bruch, Sebastian Bezzel, Jule Ronstedt, Florian Stetter
Keywords: himalaya, biography, pakistan, climbing, mountain climbing, mountaineering

Overview: Drama about the tragic Nanga Parbat expedition by the two Messner brothers in 1970, on which Reinhold Messners younger brother Günther died.
