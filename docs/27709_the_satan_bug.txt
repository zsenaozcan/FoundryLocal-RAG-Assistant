Title: The Satan Bug (1965)
Genres: Mystery, Science Fiction, Thriller, Crime
Director: John Sturges
Main Cast: George Maharis, Richard Basehart, Anne Francis, Dana Andrews, John Larkin
Keywords: suicide, california, robbery, based on novel, florida, biological weapon, thief, laboratory, independent film, escape

Overview: The Satan Bug (1965) is a science fiction film in which a US government germ warfare lab has had an accident. The first theory is that one of the germs has been released and killed several scientists. The big fear is that a more virulent strain, named The Satan Bug because all life can be killed off by it should it escape, may have been stolen.
