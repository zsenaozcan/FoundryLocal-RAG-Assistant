Title: Enlightened blood (2008)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Iván Ávila Dueñas
Main Cast: Joustein Roustand, Enoc Leaño, Joaquín Cosio, Gustavo Sánchez Parra

Overview: Six characters narrate their complex existence through his slides from one to another body, to form a network of shared memories and experiences. The need to explain this strange condition connects them via objects, messages and memories.
