Title: Save Me (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Robert Cary
Main Cast: Chad Allen, Robert Gant, Jeremy Glazer, David Petruzzi, Arron Shiver
Keywords: sex, independent film, drug

Overview: A sex and drug addicted young man who is forced into a Christian-run ministry in an attempt to cure him of his "gay affliction", where instead he is faced with the truth in his heart and spirit.
