Title: The Age of the Earth (1980)
Genres: Drama
Director: Glauber Rocha
Main Cast: Antônio Pitanga, Tarcísio Meira, Jece Valadão, Geraldo Del Rey, Mauricio do Valle
Keywords: mythology, third world, jesus christ, bible, experimental film, avant-garde, christ allegory

Overview: A personal vision of contemporary Brazil and its evolution, with a number of archetypes which recall some of Glauber Rocha's previous films : the Indian, the Colonizer, the Nordeste man, the Worker, the Amazon, the Bourgeois and a black Christ bringing new revolutionary hope for the Third World.
