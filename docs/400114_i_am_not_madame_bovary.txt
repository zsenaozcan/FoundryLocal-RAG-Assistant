Title: I Am Not Madame Bovary (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Feng Xiaogang
Main Cast: Fan Bingbing, Zhang Jia-Yi, Yu Hewei, Guo Tao, Dong Chengpeng

Overview: Li Xuelian, a woman from the countryside is falsely accused by her husband of having an affair. To defend herself, Li moves from her small town to the big city until she reached the capital.
