Title: Twelve Chairs (2004)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ulrike Ottinger
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The film TWELVE CHAIRS linked in a spectacular way the dramaturgy of a treasure hunt and chase with a dense imagery of people and places. It tells both of yesterday and today, the reality of the people in the CIS countries and the universal humanity of our own actions. Large social utopia thus mixes with the individual hope of personal happiness, be it through money or in love.
