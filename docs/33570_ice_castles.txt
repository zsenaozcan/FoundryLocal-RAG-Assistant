Title: Ice Castles (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Donald Wrye
Main Cast: Henry Czerny, Taylor Firth, Rob Mayes, Gillian Ferrier, Michael Higgins

Overview: Alexis Winston is a young girl who dreams of becoming a champion figure skater. While practicing, Alexis suffers a terrible accident that takes her sight and threatens to destroy her dreams. In the midst of feeling sorry for herself she falls in love with a handsome young man named Nick who (with help from her family) helps her to realize that she can still fulfill her dreams.
