Title: American Nightmare (1983)
Genres: Horror, Crime, Thriller
Director: Don McBrearty
Main Cast: Lawrence Day, Lora Staley, Neil Dainard, Lenore Zann, Claudia Udy
Keywords: murder, slasher, prostitution, canuxploitation

Overview: A man investigates the disappearance of his sister with the help of her roommate. He uncovers a trail of prostitution, incest, blackmail...and murder.
