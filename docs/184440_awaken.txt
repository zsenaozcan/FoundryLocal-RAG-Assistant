Title: Awaken (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Science Fiction
Director: Daric Loo
Main Cast: Corey Sevier, Jordan Ladd, Kim Poirier, James Gleason, Ryan Alosio

Overview: Alex's dreary life is reignited when he meets Rachel, the woman of his dreams. The only thing that stands in the way of their perfect, serendipitous relationship is that Rachel has recently passed away.
