Title: Daniel Tosh: Completely Serious (2007)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Manny Rodriguez
Main Cast: Daniel Tosh
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Fresh from his numerous appearances on late night TV and Comedy Central, cutting-edge comic Daniel Tosh brings his seriously funny brand of contemporary comedy to this riotous standup special. Like your Tosh a little raunchy? His unfiltered routine is here, along with a more family-friendly version that will have Grandma laughing, too.
