Title: Q & A (1990)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Sidney Lumet
Main Cast: Nick Nolte, Timothy Hutton, Armand Assante, Luis Guzmán, Charles S. Dutton
Keywords: police brutality, crime boss, police corruption, district attorney

Overview: A young district attorney seeking to prove a case against a corrupt police detective encounters a former lover and her new protector, a crime boss who refuse to help him.
