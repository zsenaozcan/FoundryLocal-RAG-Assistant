Title: Winky's Horse (2005)
Genres: Family
Director: Mischa Kamp
Main Cast: Ebbie Tam, Jan Decleir, Betty Schuurman, Aaron Wan, Hanyi Han
Keywords: holiday, woman director

Overview: There is only one thing Winky wants: a horse, a real horse to ride. But horses are expensive. Fortunately it is the season of Sinterklaas; Winky just asks Sinterklaas for a horse. Than everything will work out alright. Or will it?
