Title: The Hoodlum (1951)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime
Director: Max Nosseck
Main Cast: Lawrence Tierney, Allene Roberts, Marjorie Riordan, Lisa Golm, Edward Tierney
Keywords: b movie

Overview: Vincent Lubeck is a vicious ex-convict. His criminal activities are despised by his family, but he uses and abuses them in the course of his crimes. Eventually his own brother must stand up to him.
