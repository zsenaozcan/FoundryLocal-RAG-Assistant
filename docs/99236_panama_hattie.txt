Title: Panama Hattie (1942)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Norman Z. McLeod
Main Cast: Red Skelton, Ann Sothern, Rags Ragland, Ben Blue, Marsha Hunt
Keywords: fire, musical, night club, secret door, sailor

Overview: Sailors and spies mingle in between the acts at Hattie's (Ann Sothern) nightclub in the Canal Zone.
