Title: Non-Stop (2013)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Richard Gabai
Main Cast: Lacey Chabert, Amy Davidson, Will Kemp, Betsy Russell, David Lipper

Overview: Mysterious emotional thriller about a woman who, after being left at the altar, has a brief liaison with a handsome stranger on a plane which ultimately puts her – and everyone else on the flight – in terrible danger.
