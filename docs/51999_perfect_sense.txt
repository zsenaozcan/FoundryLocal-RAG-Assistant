Title: Perfect Sense (2011)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction, Romance
Director: David Mackenzie
Main Cast: Ewan McGregor, Eva Green, Ewen Bremner, Stephen Dillane, Denis Lawson
Keywords: one-night stand, insomnia, memory, scientist, epidemic, chef, anarchy

Overview: Susan is a scientist searching for answers to important questions. So important that she has given up on other things, like love - until she meets Micheal. Susan and Michael find themselves embarking on a sensual adventure while the world around them seems to be falling apart. A life-affirming look at what it means to love and be loved in turbulent times.
