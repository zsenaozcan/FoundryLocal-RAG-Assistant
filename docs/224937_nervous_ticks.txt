Title: Nervous Ticks (1992)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Rocky Lang
Main Cast: Bill Pullman, Julie Brown, Peter Boyle, Brent Jennings, James Le Gros
Keywords: infidelity, mistress, real time, jealous husband

Overview: A comedy about a man (Bill Pullman), who is on the run after his mistress (Julie Brown) confesses to her husband (Peter Boyle) that the two of them have been having an affair.
