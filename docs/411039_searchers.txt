Title: Searchers (2016)
Genres: Action, Drama, History
Director: Zacharias Kunuk
Main Cast: Benjamin Kunuk, Joey Sarpinak, Jocelyne Immaroitok, Karen Ivalu, Jonah Qunaq

Overview: Inuk filmmaker Zacharias Kunuk (Atanarjuat: The Fast Runner) returns with this Arctic epic inspired by the classic John Ford western of the same name, about a vengeful husband who sets off in pursuit of the violent men who kidnapped his wife and destroyed his home.
