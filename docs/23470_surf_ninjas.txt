Title: Surf Ninjas (1993)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Family
Director: Neal Israel
Main Cast: Rob Schneider, Ernie Reyes, Jr., Tone Loc, John Karlen, Nicolas Cowan
Keywords: surfing, surf, ninja

Overview: Two Asian-American "surfer-dude" brothers discover they are the long lost princes from a China Sea Island. Part of their inheritance are magically-induced martial arts prowess. Using their new powers they act to overthrow the island's current dictator, a despotic madman.
