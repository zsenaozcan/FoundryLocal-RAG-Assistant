Title: Must Read After My Death (2007)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Morgan Dews
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: feminism, childhood trauma, marriage crisis, patriarchy, dysfunctional marriage, psychiatric hospital, identified patient, family power struggle, nonconformism, individualism

Overview: A grandmother dies and leaves behind hours of secret film and audio recordings as well as an envelope with the words “Must read after my death,” which reveal a dark history for her family to discover.
