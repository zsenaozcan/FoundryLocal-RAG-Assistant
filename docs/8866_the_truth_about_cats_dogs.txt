Title: The Truth About Cats & Dogs (1996)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Michael Lehmann
Main Cast: Uma Thurman, Janeane Garofalo, Ben Chaplin, James McCaffrey, Richard Coca
Keywords: radio presenter, false identity, decoy, beguilement, love, art

Overview: A successful veterinarian and radio show host with low self-esteem asks her model friend to impersonate her when a handsome man wants to see her.
