Title: Boxing Helena (1993)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Jennifer Chambers Lynch
Main Cast: Julian Sands, Sherilyn Fenn, Bill Paxton, Kurtwood Smith, Art Garfunkel
Keywords: obsession, dream, kidnapping, nudity, suspense, unrequited love, doctor, car accident, obsessive love, woman director

Overview: A top surgeon is besotted with a beautiful woman who once ditched him. Unable to come to terms with life without her, he tries to convince her that they need each other. She has other ideas, but an horrific accident leaves her at his mercy. The plot is bizarre and perhaps sick at times, ending abruptly and with a twist.
