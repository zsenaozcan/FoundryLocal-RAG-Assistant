Title: The Executor (1981)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Chuan Chen
Main Cast: Chow Yun-fat, Danny Lee Sau-Yin, Ng Man-Tat, Paul Chun Pui, Deannie Yip

Overview: Chow Yun-Fat and Danny Lee star in this police action film. Two cops are trying to capture a triad boss, but are interrupted by hitman played by Chow Yun-Fat. This is one of those unique combination Hong Kong movies with action, comedy, and violence.
