Title: What Men Do! (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sarik Andreasyan
Main Cast: Tair Mamedov, Roman Yunusov, Gavril Gordeev, Konstantin Kryukov, Kristina Asmus

Overview: Four friends decide to participate in the game organized by extravagant billionaire. The winner will be the one who seduces most girls during the competition.
