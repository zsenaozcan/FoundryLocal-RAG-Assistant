Title: Mindwalk (1990)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bernt Amadeus Capra
Main Cast: Liv Ullmann, Sam Waterston, John Heard, Ione Skye
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A US politician visits his poet friend in Mont. St. Michael, France. While walking through the medieval island discussing their philosophies of life they happen upon Sonja, a scientist in recluse, who joins in their conversation. The two men listen to the ideas of this brilliant woman and discuss how her ideas can work in their own politician and poet lives.
