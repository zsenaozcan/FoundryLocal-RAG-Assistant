Title: Conflagration (1958)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kon Ichikawa
Main Cast: Raizô Ichikawa, Tatsuya Nakadai, Ganjiro Nakamura, Yoichi Funaki, Tamao Nakamura

Overview: Learning of his family's collapse, acolyte Goichi, sent to study silently at the Temple of the Golden Pavilion, must endure acute psychological distress.
