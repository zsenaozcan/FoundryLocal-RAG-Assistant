Title: 102 Minutes That Changed America (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Nicole Rittenmeyer
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: new york, mobile phone, drama, terrorism, cell phone, september 11 2001, surveillance footage, real time, cctv
