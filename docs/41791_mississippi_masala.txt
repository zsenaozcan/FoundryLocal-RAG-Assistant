Title: Mississippi Masala (1991)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Mira Nair
Main Cast: Denzel Washington, Sarita Choudhury, Roshan Seth, Sharmila Tagore, Charles S. Dutton
Keywords: indian lead, interracial relationship, independent film, woman director

Overview: An Indian family is expelled from Uganda when Idi Amin takes power. They move to Mississippi and time passes. The Indian daughter falls in love with a black man, and the respective families have to come to terms with it.
