Title: Greetings from Tim Buckley (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Daniel Algrant
Main Cast: Penn Badgley, Imogen Poots, William Sadler, Norbert Leo Butz, Frank Wood

Overview: A young Jeff Buckley (Penn Badgley) struggles to make peace with his late father Tim's legacy while preparing to perform at a tribute concert to the late singer-songwriter, in this stirring drama about the prelude to the tragically short-lived singer's creation of his monumental album Grace.
