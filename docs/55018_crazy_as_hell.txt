Title: Crazy As Hell (2002)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Eriq La Salle
Main Cast: Michael Beach, Eriq La Salle, Ronny Cox, Sinbad, Shelly Robertson
Keywords: thriller, psychiatric hospital

Overview: A famous psychiatrist (Ty Adams) takes on the job of trying to cure patients at the Sedah State Hospital, run by it's folksy doctor (Sam Delazo). All this takes a strange turn when a mysterious patient (Satan, he calls himself) enters the Hospital seeking help. Or is it just help that he wants?
