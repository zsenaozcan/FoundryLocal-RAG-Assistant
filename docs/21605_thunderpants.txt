Title: Thunderpants (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Science Fiction
Director: Peter Hewitt
Main Cast: Bruce Cook, Rupert Grint, Simon Callow, Stephen Fry, Celia Imrie
Keywords: spacecraft, comedy, astronaut, kids, farting
