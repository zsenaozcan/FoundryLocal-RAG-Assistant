Title: Youth Runs Wild (1944)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mark Robson
Main Cast: Bonita Granville, Kent Smith, Jean Brooks, Vanessa Brown, Ben Bard
Keywords: youth, war hero, truancy

Overview: During WWII, adults are either off fighting or busy in the factories, so juvenile delinquency becomes a major problem back home. Danny Hauser, a wounded soldier, finds this out as he returns and three young boys are promptly placed in the care of him and his wife by the court after some hooliganism. How to keep them straight?
