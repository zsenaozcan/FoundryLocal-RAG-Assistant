Title: The Hills Have Eyes (1977)
Genres: Horror
Director: Wes Craven
Main Cast: Martin Speer, Russ Grieve, John Steadman, James Whitworth, Virginia Vincent
Keywords: rape, mutation, government, murder, blood, cannibal, dog, desert, camper, set on fire

Overview: On the way to California, a family has the misfortune to have their car break down in an area closed to the public, and inhabited by violent savages ready to attack.
