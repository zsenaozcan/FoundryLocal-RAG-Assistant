Title: Abbott and Costello Meet the Killer, Boris Karloff (1949)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Charles Barton
Main Cast: Bud Abbott, Lou Costello, Boris Karloff, Lenore Aubert, Gar Moore
Keywords: hotel, hypnotism

Overview: Lost Caverns Hotel bellhop Freddie Phillips is suspected of murder. Swami Talpur tries to hypnotize Freddie into confessing, but Freddie is too stupid for the plot to work. Inspector Wellman uses Freddie to get the killer (and it isn't the Swami).
