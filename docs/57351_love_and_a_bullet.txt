Title: Love and a Bullet (2002)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Ben Ramsey
Main Cast: Anthony 'Treach' Criss, Kent King, Charles Guardino, Shireen Crutchfield, Parris Washington
Keywords: villain, fistfight, sniper rifle, one man army, villain turns good, taekwondo

Overview: Malik Bishop is the best killer in the business, but when his crimelord employer wants his girlfriend, who may be unfaithful, eliminated, Bishop doesn't count on falling in love with the woman. Now he must choose between his heart and, perhaps, his life.
