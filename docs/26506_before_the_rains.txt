Title: Before The Rains (2007)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Santosh Sivan
Main Cast: Linus Roache, Nandita Das, Rahul Bose, Jennifer Ehle, Indrajith Sukumaran
Keywords: india, period drama, extramarital affair, 1930s

Overview: Set in southern India in the late 1930s, this provocative tale traces the story of three people caught in an inexorable web of forbidden romance and dangerous secrets. After a British spice planter falls in love with his alluring servant, an idealistic young man finds himself torn between his ambitions and his family, his village and his past.
