Title: Fury (1936)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Fritz Lang
Main Cast: Sylvia Sidney, Spencer Tracy, Walter Abel, Bruce Cabot, Edward Ellis
Keywords: small town, kidnapping, love, revenge, murder, jail, money, mobster, presumed dead, lynch mob

Overview: When a prisoner barely survives a lynch mob attack and is presumed dead, he vindictively decides to frame the mob for his murder.
