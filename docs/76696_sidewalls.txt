Title: Sidewalls (2011)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Gustavo Taretto
Main Cast: Javier Drolas, Pilar López de Ayala, Inés Efron, Adrián Navarro, Rafael Ferro

Overview: A romantic comedy full a refreshing global perspective, SIDEWALLS is director Gustavo Taretto's clever and moving ode to the modern people and urban landscape of Buenos Aires. Martin is a neurotic web designer taking baby steps out of the isolation of his one-room apartment and his virtual reality. Mariana is an artist fresh out of a a long relationship. Her head is a mess, just like the apartment where she takes refuge. Martin and Mariana are perfect for each other-- they live in the same street, in opposite buildings, but they never meet. Can the movement of a modern city of three million people bring them together?
