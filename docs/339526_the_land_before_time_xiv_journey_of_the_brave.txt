Title: The Land Before Time XIV: Journey of the Brave (2016)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Family
Director: Davis Doi
Main Cast: Felix Avitia, Anndi McAfee, Aria Noelle Curzon, Jeff Bennett, Rob Paulsen
Keywords: sequel, prehistoric, dinosaur

Overview: On a day known to the dinosaurs as "Treasure Day", Bron becomes stuck in the Mysterious Beyond. This event urges the young Longneck to take his friends on a quest to the Fire Mountain to save his father. Along the way, they befriend a Flyer.
