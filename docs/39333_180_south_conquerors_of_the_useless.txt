Title: 180° South: Conquerors of the Useless (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary, Drama
Director: Chris Malloy
Main Cast: Yvon Chouinard, Doug Tompkins, Keith Malloy, Makohe, Timmy O'Neill

Overview: The film follows adventurer Jeff Johnson as he retraces the epic 1968 journey of his heroes Yvon Chouinard and Doug Tompkins to Patagonia.
