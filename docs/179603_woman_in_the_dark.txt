Title: Woman in the Dark (1934)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Phil Rosen
Main Cast: Fay Wray, Ralph Bellamy, Melvyn Douglas, Roscoe Ates, Ruth Gillette
Keywords: robbery, sheriff

Overview: Lightweight but entertaining crime/drama about a man (Ralph Bellamy) released from prison and deciding to stay alone in his cabin so that his bad temper won't get him back into prison.
