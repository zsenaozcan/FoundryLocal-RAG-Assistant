Title: Ishtar (1987)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Elaine May
Main Cast: Warren Beatty, Dustin Hoffman, Isabelle Adjani, Charles Grodin, Jack Weston
Keywords: wilderness, sahara, beautiful woman, singer, escape, woman director
