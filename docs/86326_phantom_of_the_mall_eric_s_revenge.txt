Title: Phantom of the Mall: Eric's Revenge (1989)
Genres: Horror
Director: Richard Friedman
Main Cast: Derek Rydall, Kari Whitman, Rob Estes, Pauly Shore, Morgan Fairchild

Overview: A guy called Eric (Hense the title) owns a huge house and some greedy people want to build a mall over it. So they get someone to burn down his house. Eric is badly burned but not dead, and a year later the mall opens. What they don't realise is that Eric is living underneath the mall and he's very angry!
