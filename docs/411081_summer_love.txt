Title: Summer Love (2016)
Genres: TV Movie, Romance
Director: Lynne Stopkewich
Main Cast: Rachael Leigh Cook, Lucas Bryant, Travis Milne, Aidan Kahn, Crystal Balint

Overview: Maya Sulliway is a struggling single mom who's studying to become an accountant. When her young daughter goes off to sleepaway camp, Maya accepts a summer internship at Kizzmit, a tech company known for developing a popular social app, in order to receive college credit.
