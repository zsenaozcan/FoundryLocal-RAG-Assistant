Title: Christmas Evil (1980)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: Lewis Jackson
Main Cast: Brandon Maggart, Jeffrey DeMunn, Dianne Hull, Andy Fenwick, Brian Neville
Keywords: santa claus, hospital, church, killer, killing spree, peeping tom, killer santa claus, christmas horror, toy factory, delusional

Overview: A toy factory worker, mentally scarred as a child upon learning Santa Claus is not real, suffers a nervous breakdown after being belittled at work, and embarks on a Yuletide killing spree.
