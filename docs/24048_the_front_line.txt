Title: The Front Line (2006)
Genres: Action, Drama, Foreign, Thriller
Director: David Gleeson
Main Cast: Eriq Ebouaney, James Frain, Gerard McSorley, Hakeem Kae-Kazim, Fatou N'Diaye
Keywords: criminal

Overview: An African immigrant bank security guard turns the tables on Dublin's nastiest criminals when they force him to be the "inside man" on a bank robbery.
