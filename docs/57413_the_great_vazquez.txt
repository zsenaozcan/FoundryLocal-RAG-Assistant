Title: The Great Vazquez (2010)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Óscar Aibar
Main Cast: Santiago Segura, Alex Angulo, Manolo Solo, Mercè Llorens, Biel Durán
Keywords: biography

Overview: Is it possible to live aside of the system, thinking only about the present and oneself? A feature film about Manuel Vázquez, the best comic book author in Barcelona during the sixties, but, mainly, a man with a very particular way of living his life.
