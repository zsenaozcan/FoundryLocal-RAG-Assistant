Title: Survive Style 5+ (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Horror, Mystery
Director: Gen Sekiguchi
Main Cast: Tadanobu Asano, Reika Hashimoto, Kyoko Koizumi, Hiroshi Abe, Ittoku Kishibe
Keywords: hitman

Overview: A man continually trying and failing to get his wife to stay dead; a self-absorbed ad agency creative director who comes up with one unworkably inane idea after another; a British hitman who only wants to know everyone's function in life; and an unfortunate office worker and father whose brain is left scrambled after a stage hypnotist is murdered in mid-performance.
