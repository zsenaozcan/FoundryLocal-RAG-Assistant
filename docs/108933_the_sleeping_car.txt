Title: The Sleeping Car (1990)
Genres: Horror
Director: Douglas Curtis
Main Cast: Judie Aronson, David Naughton, Kevin McCarthy, Jeff Conaway, Dani Minnick
Keywords: sitar music

Overview: Jason (David Naughton) moves into an abandoned train car where he resurrects the vicious ghost of his landlady's dead husband... The Mister. After some near-fatal encounters with the violent specter he seeks local exorcist Vincent Tuttle (Kevin McCarthy).
