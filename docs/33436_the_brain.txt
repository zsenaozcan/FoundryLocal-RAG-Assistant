Title: The Brain (1969)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Crime
Director: Gérard Oury
Main Cast: Jean-Paul Belmondo, Bourvil, David Niven, Eli Wallach, Silvia Monti
Keywords: french movies, train, cat burglar

Overview: Arthur and Anatole are two little robbers. They want to rob money, money that will travel in a special train from Paris to Bruxelles. They don't know that other people have planned to do the same thing.
