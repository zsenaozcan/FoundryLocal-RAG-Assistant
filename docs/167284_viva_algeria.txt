Title: Viva Algeria (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nadir Moknèche
Main Cast: Nadia Kaci, Lubna Azabal, Biyouna

Overview: This movie portrays three women living in today's Algeria between modern society and Islamic fundamentalism, self-determination and dependence. Goucem, a young woman who works for a photographer and mistress of a rich doctor, her mother Papicha, a former cabaret star, and her best friend Fifi, a prostitute, all live in a hotel in the city center of Algiers. Their difficult personal situation and the growing influence of Islam lead to dramatic consequences...
