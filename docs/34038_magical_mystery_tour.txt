Title: Magical Mystery Tour (1967)
Genres: Music
Director: Paul McCartney
Main Cast: John Lennon, Paul McCartney, George Harrison, Ringo Starr, Vivian Stanshall
Keywords: fab four, john lennon, surrealism, the beatles, paul mccartney, rock music

Overview: A typically Beatlesque film originally produced for television, this short film was intended to be an off-the-wall road movie with the Beatles and three dozen or so friends on a psychedelic bus.
