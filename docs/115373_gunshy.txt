Title: Gunshy (1998)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Jeff Celentano
Main Cast: William Petersen, Michael Wincott, Diane Lane, Kevin Gage, R. Lee Ermey
Keywords: journalist, infidelity, mobster, neo-noir

Overview: When the New York journalist Jake Bridges catches his girlfriend with another guy, he goes to Atlantic City to drink himself to oblivion. He is saved from a bar brawl by a small-time mobster Frankie, and Jake falls in love with Frankie's girlfriend Melissa. Jake soon also joins Frankie in his money-collecting duties.
