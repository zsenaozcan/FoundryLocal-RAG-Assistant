Title: The Conclave (2006)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Christoph Schrewe
Main Cast: Brian Blessed, James Faulkner, Rolf Kanies, Manu Fullola, Dominic Boeer
Keywords: renaissance, borgias, papal conclave, rodrigo borgia

Overview: In 1458, five years after the fall of Constantinople to the Moslems, eighteen cardinals meet to elect a new pope. Rodrigo Borgia, a 27 year old cardinal learns to play a very dangerous game.
