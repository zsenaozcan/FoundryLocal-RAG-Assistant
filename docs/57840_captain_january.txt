Title: Captain January (1936)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: David Butler
Main Cast: Shirley Temple, Guy Kibbee, Slim Summerville, Buddy Ebsen, Sara Haden
Keywords: boarding school, orphanage, lighthouse keeper   

Overview: Shirley lives with a lighthouse keeper (Kibbee) who rescued her when her parents drowned. A truant officer (Haden) decides she should go to boarding school, but she's rescued by relatives. Buddy Ebsen dances "At The Codfish Ball" with Shirley.
