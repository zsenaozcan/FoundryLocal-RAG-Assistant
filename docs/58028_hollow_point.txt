Title: Hollow Point (1996)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Thriller
Director: Sidney J. Furie
Main Cast: Donald Sutherland, John Lithgow, Tia Carrere, Thomas Ian Griffith, David Hemblen

Overview: Livingston is trying to merge the Russians, Chinese and Italian gangsters into one group with huge amounts of money and power. FBI agent Susan and DEA agent Max are trying to stop them.
