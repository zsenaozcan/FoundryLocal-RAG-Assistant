Title: Sunny (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kang Hyeong-Cheol
Main Cast: Ho-jeong Yu, Shim Eun-kyung, Kang So-ra, Go Soo-hee, Kim Min-young
Keywords: bullying, independent film, gang, experimental film

Overview: Na-mi, a transfer student, becomes a laughingstock on her first day of school because of her strong southern dialect. Six girls jump in to help the new kid adapt to the new environment, and the girls come to call their circle of friends "Sunny."
