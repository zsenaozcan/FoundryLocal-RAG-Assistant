Title: Cambridge Spies (2003)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Tim Fywell
Main Cast: Tom Hollander, Toby Stephens, Rupert Penry-Jones

Overview: In 1934, four brilliant Cambridge men are recruited to spy for Russia. Fueled by youthful idealism, a passion for social justice and a talent for lying, they take huge personal risks to pass Britain's biggest secrets to Moscow. Based on true events.
