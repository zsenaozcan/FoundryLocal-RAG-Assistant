Title: Wedding Doll (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nitzan Giladi
Main Cast: Moran Rosenblatt, Roy Assaf, Assi Levy, Oded Leopold
Keywords: mother daughter relationship

Overview: Fixated on romantic fantasies, a kindly and strong-willed young woman with a mild mental disability embarks on a relationship — much to the concern of her protective mother.
