Title: Appleseed: Ex Machina (2007)
Genres: Animation, Action, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Shinji Aramaki
Main Cast: Ai Kobayashi, Kouichi Yamadera, Kong Kuwata, Shinpachi Tsuji, Gara Takashima
Keywords: cyborg, terrorist, future, manga, mecha, cyberpunk

Overview: As members of ESWAT, the elite forces serving Olympus, Deunan and Briaereos they are deployed everywhere trouble strikes. Olympus finds itself under a stealth attack - cyborg terrorism, deadly nanotech zealots, and rioting citizens are just some of the threats that Deunan must contend with as she fights to save Olympus!
