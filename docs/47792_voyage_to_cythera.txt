Title: Voyage to Cythera (1984)
Genres: Drama
Director: Theo Angelopoulos
Main Cast: Manos Katrakis, Mairi Hronopoulou, Dionysis Papagiannopoulos, Dora Volanaki, Giulio Brogi

Overview: An old communist returns to Greece after 32 years in the Soviet Union. However, things aren't the way he had hoped for.
