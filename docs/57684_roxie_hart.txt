Title: Roxie Hart (1942)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Music
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: Ginger Rogers, Adolphe Menjou, George Montgomery, Lynne Overman, Nigel Bruce
Keywords: chicago, female prisoner

Overview: To try and kick-start her show-business career, our heroine admits to a Chicago murder. But although Cook County don't seem to let dames swing, and even with top slippery lawyer Billy Flynn, it's all something of a gamble. The original version of the movie "Chicago".
