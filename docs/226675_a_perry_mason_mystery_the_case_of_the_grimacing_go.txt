Title: A Perry Mason Mystery: The Case of the Grimacing Governor (1994)
Genres: Mystery, Crime
Director: Max Tash
Main Cast: Hal Holbrook, Barbara Hale, William R. Moses, Bonnie Bartlett, John Spencer
Keywords: murder, private investigator, courtroom

Overview: The alleged suicide of a gubernatorial candidate triggers a series of mysterious deaths leading to the governor's mansion, and "Wild" Bill McKenzie, a friend of Perry Mason, is on the case.
