Title: Arthur & Merlin (2015)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: Marco van Belle
Main Cast: Kirk Barker, Stefan Buttler, Adrian Bouchet, David Sterne, Nigel Cooke
Keywords: hermit, druid, celtic, king arthur, king, wizard, sword and sorcery

Overview: In dark ages Britain, a time of magic and legend, a powerful druid is bent on destroying the Celtic people. Arthur, a banished warrior, and Merlin, a hermit wizard, embark on a heroic quest to stop the druid and save their people, before the Celts are lost forever and become a myth themselves.
