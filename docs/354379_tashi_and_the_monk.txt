Title: Tashi and the Monk (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Johnny Burke
Main Cast: Tashi Drolma, Lobsang Phuntsok
Keywords: buddhist monk, biography, orphan

Overview: This moving documentary profiles a former Buddhist monk who runs a home for orphaned children in the Himalayas, and his relationship with its newest arrival, troubled five-year-old Tashi.
