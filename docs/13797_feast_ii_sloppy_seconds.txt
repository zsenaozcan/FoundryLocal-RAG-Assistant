Title: Feast II: Sloppy Seconds (2008)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Horror
Director: John Gulager
Main Cast: Jenny Wade, Clu Gulager, Diane Ayala Goldner, Martin Klebba, Cassie Shea Watson
Keywords: self-destruction, teamwork, damage, mythical creature

Overview: The monsters have made it into a small neighboring town in the middle of nowhere and the locals have to band with the survivors of the bar' slaughter to figure out how to survive.
