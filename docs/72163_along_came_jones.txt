Title: Along Came Jones (1945)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Western, Romance
Director: Stuart Heisler
Main Cast: Gary Cooper, Loretta Young, William Demarest, Dan Duryea, Frank Sully
Keywords: gunman

Overview: An easy-going cowboy is mistaken by the townsfolk for a notorious gunman. The cowboy decides it would be best to leave town, until he meets the gunman's girlfriend.
