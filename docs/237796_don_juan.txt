Title: Don Juan (1998)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Jacques Weber
Main Cast: Jacques Weber, Michel Boujenah, Emmanuelle Béart, Penélope Cruz, Ariadna Gil
Keywords: spain, countryside, horse, seduction, village, marriage, insult, war, love, honor

Overview: Spain in the mid-seventeenth century. A series of bloody wars has ravaged the nation. Don Juan the nobleman and his valet, Sganarelle, roam the countryside on horseback, on the run and lost.
