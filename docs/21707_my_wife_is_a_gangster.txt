Title: My Wife Is a Gangster (2001)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Cho Jin-Gyu
Main Cast: Shin Eun-Kyung, Ahn Jae-mo, Park Sang-myeon, Jang Se-jin, Choi Eun-ju
Keywords: yakuza, marriage

Overview: Eunjin who is a living legend among the gangsters dominates the male-centered underworld wielding only a pair of her trademark blades. One day, Eun-jin finds her sister from whom she was separated at an orphanage during childhood, and her sister tells Eun-jin that her last dying wish is to see that Eun-jin gets married.
