Title: April (1961)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Otar Iosseliani
Main Cast: Tatyana Chanturia, Gia Chiraqadze, Akakiy Chikvaidze, V. Maisuradze, A. Jorbenadze

Overview: A critique of materialism, the film is about a young couple who live in a rundown empty apartment. Their love is so strong that it makes the water flow and the electricity work, but when they start purchasing furniture and knickknacks, they fight and grow apart
