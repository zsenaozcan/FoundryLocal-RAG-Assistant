Title: On the Bowery (1957)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Lionel Rogosin
Main Cast: Gorman Hendricks, Frank Matthews, Ray Salyer
Keywords: new york, the bowery

Overview: A mix of documentary and scripted footage on the Bowery, New York City's skid row. Against a backdrop of men (and a few women) drinking in bars, talking and arguing, and sleeping on sidewalks, we have the story of Ray.
