Title: Into the Wild (2007)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Sean Penn
Main Cast: Emile Hirsch, Marcia Gay Harden, William Hurt, Jena Malone, Brian H. Dierker
Keywords: male nudity, parent child relationship, camping, cutting the cord, self-discovery, wilderness, biography, based on true story, pubic hair, alaska

Overview: The true story of top student and athlete, Christopher McCandless, who after graduating from Emory University in 1992, abandoned his possessions, gave his entire $24,000 savings account to charity and hitchhiked to Alaska to live in the wilderness.
