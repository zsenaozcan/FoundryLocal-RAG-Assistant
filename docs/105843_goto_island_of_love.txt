Title: Goto, Island of Love (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Walerian Borowczyk
Main Cast: Pierre Brasseur, Ligia Branice, Jean-Pierre Andréani, Ginette Leclerc, Fernand Bercher
Keywords: totalitarian regime

Overview: In an isolated community on a mythical island, a pompous dictator holds public executions and lets criminals fight in the streets.
