Title: 1½ Knights - In Search of the Ravishing Princess Herzelinde (2008)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Til Schweiger
Main Cast: Til Schweiger, Rick Kavanian, Julia Dietze, Thomas Gottschalk, Udo Kier
Keywords: princess, knight

Overview: The story of Lanze who is in love with the princess. One day the princess is kidnapped by the Dark Knight and Lanze starts his journey to rescue her. But he is not alone because Erdal, a turk, joins him on his journey.
