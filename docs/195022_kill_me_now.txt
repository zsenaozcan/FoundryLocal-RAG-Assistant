Title: Kill Me Now (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Travis Long
Main Cast: Michael Swaim, Jacob Reed, Beck Bennett, Brett Fancy, Kaitlin Large

Overview: When nerds Dennis and Noah crash a party thrown by the local jocks and hot girls in a remote cabin, they must combat a brainy serial murderer known as the Driller Killer, who uses power tools to rid the world of idiots, one clueless teen at a time.
