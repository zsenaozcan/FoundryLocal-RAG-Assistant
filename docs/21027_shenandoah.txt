Title: Shenandoah (1965)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Western
Director: Andrew V. McLaglen
Main Cast: James Stewart, Doug McClure, Glenn Corbett, Patrick Wayne, Rosemary Forsyth
Keywords: widower, virginia

Overview: Charlie Anderson, a farmer in Shenandoah, Virginia, finds himself and his family in the middle of the Civil War he wants nothing to do with. When his youngest boy is taken prisoner by the North the Civil War is forced upon him.
