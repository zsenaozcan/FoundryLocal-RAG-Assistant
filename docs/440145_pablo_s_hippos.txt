Title: Pablo's Hippos (2010)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Lawrence Elman
Main Cast: Antonio Von Hildebrand, José María Yazpik
Keywords: biography

Overview: Recounting the absurd and paradoxical history of Colombia's thirty-year struggle with international drug trafficking, at once a farce and a tragedy, as seen through the eyes of the extravagant pet of the most powerful drug baron in history: a hippopotamus named Pablo.
