Title: Saps at Sea (1940)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Gordon Douglas
Main Cast: Stan Laurel, Oliver Hardy, James Finlayson, Richard Cramer, Ben Turpin
Keywords: boat, horn, laurel and hardy, sea air

Overview: Stan and Ollie work in a horn factory. Ollie starts having violent fits every time he hears a horn. His doctor prescribes a restful sea voyage. Mayhem ensues.
