Title: A Town Without Christmas (2001)
Genres: Family
Director: Andy Wolk
Main Cast: Patricia Heaton, Rick Roberts, Ernie Hudson, Isabella Fink, Jeffrey R. Smith
Keywords: small town, letter to santa claus, christmas

Overview: In the small town of Seacliff, Wash., a child's letter to Santa Claus captures the nation's attention. When the little boy, Chris, writes that he wishes to leave this world so he will no longer be a burden to his divorcing parents, a race begins to find him before he harms himself.
