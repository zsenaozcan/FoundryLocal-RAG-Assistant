Title: The Serpent's Way (1986)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bo Widerberg
Main Cast: Stina Ekblad, Stellan Skarsgård, Pernilla August, Ernst Günther, Tomas von Brömssen

Overview: A salesman and his son sexually abuse the generations of women of a poor family as payment for debt. Janni must see his mother, sister, niece and wife all being exploited, and the family grow bigger with the abuser's kids.
