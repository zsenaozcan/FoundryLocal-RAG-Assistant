Title: Wild Animals (1997)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Kim Ki-duk
Main Cast: Jo Jae-hyeon, Jang Dong-jik, Ryun Jang, Sasha Rucavina, Richard Bohringer

Overview: Two Korean ex-pats in Paris are recruited by a French mobster. The duo find themselves at war with their mobster recruiters and each other.
