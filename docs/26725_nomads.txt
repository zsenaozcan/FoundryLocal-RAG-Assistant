Title: Nomads (1986)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Mystery
Director: John McTiernan
Main Cast: Pierce Brosnan, Lesley-Anne Down, Anna Maria Monticelli, Adam Ant, Mary Woronov
Keywords: male nudity, female nudity, california, photographer, nudity, graffiti, possession, murder, suspense, hospital

Overview: French anthropologist Jean-Charles Pommier and his wife have come to LA so he can take up a position as lecturer. To his surprise he encounters a group of urban nomads. As he investigates things become more unsettling as he realizes the price paid for being civilized.
