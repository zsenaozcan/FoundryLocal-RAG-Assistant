Title: Cass (2008)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Jon S. Baird
Main Cast: Nonso Anozie, Natalie Press, Leo Gregory, Gavin Brocker, Daniel Kaluuya
Keywords: biography, hooligan, independent film

Overview: The incredible true story of how an orphaned Jamaican baby, adopted by an elderly white couple and brought up in an all white area of London, became one of the most feared and respected men in Britain.
