Title: The Defender (1994)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller
Director: Corey Yuen Kwai
Main Cast: Jet Li, Christy Chung, Kent Cheng, Collin Chou, Joey Leung Wing-Chung
Keywords: assassin, bodyguard, witness, police, hong kong

Overview: A corrupt businessman commits a murder and the only witness is the girlfriend of another businessman with close connections to the Chinese government, so a bodyguard from Beijing is dispatched to help two Hong Kong cops protect the witness.
