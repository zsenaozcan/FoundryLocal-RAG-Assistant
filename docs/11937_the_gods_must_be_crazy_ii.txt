Title: The Gods Must Be Crazy II (1989)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Jamie Uys
Main Cast: Lena Farugia, Hans Strydom, Eiros, N!xau, Nadies
Keywords: civilization, safari, stowaway, slapstick, tribe, disappearance, scientist, kalahari, bushman, aeroplane

Overview: Xixo is back again. This time, his children accidentally stow away on a fast-moving poachers' truck, unable to get off, and Xixo sets out to rescue them. Along the way, he encounters a couple of soldiers trying to capture each other and a pilot and passenger of a small plane, who are each having a few problems of their own.
