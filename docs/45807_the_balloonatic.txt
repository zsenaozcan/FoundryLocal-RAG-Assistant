Title: The Balloonatic (1923)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Buster Keaton
Main Cast: Buster Keaton, Babe London, Phyllis Haver
Keywords: canoe, falling on someone, puddle

Overview: Buster and Phyllis endure a number of outdoor adventures trying to prove to each other their survival skills. The balloon which lands Buster in the wilderness proves useful later on as their canoe is about go over a waterfall.
