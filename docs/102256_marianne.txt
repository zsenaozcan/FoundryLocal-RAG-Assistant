Title: Marianne (2011)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Fantasy, Horror, Thriller
Director: Filip Tegstedt
Main Cast: Thomas Hedengran, Peter Stormare, Tintin Anderzon, Sandra Larsson, Dylan M. Johansson
Keywords: suspense

Overview: The life of Krister has become an unending nightmare, ever since his wife's death. There seems to be no respite for this man, tormented by the errors of his past and held prisoner by a grim daily existence.
