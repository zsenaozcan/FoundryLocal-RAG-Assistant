Title: Ebola Syndrome (1996)
Genres: Crime, Comedy
Director: Herman Yau
Main Cast: Anthony Wong, Lo Meng, Marianne Chan, Peter Ngor Chi-Kwan, Angel Wong Chui-Ling
Keywords: fast food restaurant, murder, violence

Overview: A restaurant employee wanted for murder contracts Ebola by raping a woman in South Africa and starts an outbreak there and in Hong Kong when he returns home.
