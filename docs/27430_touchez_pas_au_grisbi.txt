Title: Touchez Pas au Grisbi (1954)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Jacques Becker
Main Cast: Jean Gabin, René Dary, Dora Doll, Vittorio Sanipoli, Marilyn Buferd
Keywords: bar, gold, ransom, retirement, gold bar, partner, rivalry, money, gangster, french noir

Overview: An aging, world-weary gangster is double-crossed and forced out of retirement when his best friend is kidnapped and their stash of eight stolen gold bars demanded as ransom.
