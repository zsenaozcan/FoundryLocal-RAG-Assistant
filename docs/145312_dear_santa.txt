Title: Dear Santa (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Jason Priestley
Main Cast: Amy Acker, David Haydn-Jones, Gina Holden, James Dugan, Patrick Creery
Keywords: holiday, little girl, rich parents, letter to santa claus, wishes come true, rich girl, christmas story

Overview: Crystal, a rich party girl, finds a little girl's letter to Santa asking for a new mother, and she vows to win over the father and daughter before the holidays.
