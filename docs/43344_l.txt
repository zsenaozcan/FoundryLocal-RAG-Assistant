Title: Él (1953)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Romance
Director: Luis Buñuel
Main Cast: Arturo de Córdova, Delia Garcés, Aurora Walker, Carlos Martínez Baena, Manuel Dondé
Keywords: jealousy, sadness, passion, church, rage, wrath, threat, flashback, abuse, rich husband

Overview: Francisco is rich, rather strict on principles, and still a bachelor. After meeting Gloria by accident, he is suddenly intent on her becoming his wife and courts her until she agrees to marry him. Francisco is a dedicated husband, but little by little his passion starts to exhibit disturbing traits. Nevertheless, Gloria meets with scepticism as she expresses her worries to their acquaintances.
