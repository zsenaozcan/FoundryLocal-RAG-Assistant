Title: The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey (2012)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Action
Director: Peter Jackson
Main Cast: Ian McKellen, Martin Freeman, Richard Armitage, Andy Serkis, Cate Blanchett
Keywords: riddle, elves, dwarves, orcs, middle-earth (tolkien), hobbit, mountain, wizard, journey, ring

Overview: Bilbo Baggins, a hobbit enjoying his quiet life, is swept into an epic quest by Gandalf the Grey and thirteen dwarves who seek to reclaim their mountain home from Smaug, the dragon.
