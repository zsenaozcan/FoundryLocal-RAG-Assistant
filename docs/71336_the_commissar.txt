Title: The Commissar (1967)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Aleksandr Askoldov
Main Cast: Nonna Mordyukova, Rolan Bykov, Raisa Nedashkovskaya, Vasiliy Shukshin, Sergey Nikonenko
Keywords: commissar

Overview: Klavdia Vavilova, a Red Army cavalry commissar, is waylaid by an unexpected pregnancy. She stays with a Jewish family to give birth and is softened somewhat by the experience of family life.
