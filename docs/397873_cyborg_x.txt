Title: Cyborg X (2016)
Genres: Action, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Kevin King
Main Cast: Danny Trejo, Eve Mauro, Jake Stormoen, Adam Johnson, Angie Papanikolas
Keywords: cyborg, computer virus, machines, weapons manufacturer

Overview: After X-Corp, a radical weapons manufacturer, is taken over by a Cyber Virus, a group of survivors must fight to save humanity from the army of Machines the Virus now controls.
