Title: Mother Night (1996)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Keith Gordon
Main Cast: Nick Nolte, Sheryl Lee, Alan Arkin, Kirsten Dunst, Bernard Behrens
Keywords: germany, world war ii, nazis

Overview: An American spy behind the lines during WWII serves as a Nazi propagandist, a role he cannot escape in his future life as he can never reveal his real role in the war.
