Title: Vegas Vacation (1997)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Stephen Kessler
Main Cast: Chevy Chase, Beverly D'Angelo, Randy Quaid, Ethan Embry, Marisol Nichols
Keywords: gambling, chaos, roulette, family holiday

Overview: The Griswold family hits the road again for a typically ill-fated vacation, this time to the glitzy mecca of slots and showgirls—Las Vegas.
