Title: The Dead Are Alive (1972)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Armando Crispino
Main Cast: Alex Cord, Samantha Eggar, John Marley, Enzo Tarascio, Nadja Tiller
Keywords: terror, tomb, etruscans

Overview: A photographer on an archaeological expedition digging up Etruscan ruins in Italy begins to suspect that not all the Etruscans buried there are actually dead.
