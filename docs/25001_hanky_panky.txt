Title: Hanky Panky (1982)
Genres: Mystery, Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Sidney Poitier
Main Cast: Gene Wilder, Gilda Radner, Kathleen Quinlan, Richard Widmark, Robert Prosky
Keywords: taxi, hotel, government secrets

Overview: The complete innocent, Michael Jordon, is drawn into a web of secrecy and government secrets when a girl carrying a mysterious package gets into a taxi with him. When she is later murdered, Michael is the chief suspect and on the run.
