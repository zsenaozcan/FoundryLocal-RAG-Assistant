Title: Kizumonogatari Part 1: Tekketsu (2016)
Genres: Action, Animation, Mystery
Director: Akiyuki Shinbo
Main Cast: Hiroshi Kamiya, Maaya Sakamoto, Yui Horie, Takahiro Sakurai, Masashi Ebara
Keywords: vampire, supernatural, harem, orphan, demon, missing limb, blood spurting, regeneration, based on light novel, anime

Overview: One day, Koyomi Araragi encounters the horrifying vampire, Kiss-shot Acerola-orion Heart-under-blade A.K.A. the "King of Apparitions." He saves the fatally wounded Kiss-shot by offering his blood at the expense of his own life as a human. Now Koyomi has to face the vampire hunters to retrieve Kiss-shot’s limbs which were taken by these hunters...
