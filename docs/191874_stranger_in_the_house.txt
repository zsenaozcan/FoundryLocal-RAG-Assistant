Title: Stranger in the House (1997)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Rodney Gibbons
Main Cast: Michele Greene, Bruce Dinsmore, Steve Railsback, Kathleen Kinmont, Vlasta Vrána
Keywords: burglary, jewel, insurance investigator, murder plot

Overview: A botched diamond burglary and a dead publishing company executive set off a string of murders and betrayal in this suspensful, nonstop thriller!
