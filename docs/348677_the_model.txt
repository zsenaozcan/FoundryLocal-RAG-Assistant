Title: The Model (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mads Matthiesen
Main Cast: Maria Palm, Ed Skrein, Charlotte Tomaszewska, Marco Ilsø, Thierry Hancisse
Keywords: paris, fashion photographer, model, fashion model, fashion photography

Overview: When emerging fashion model Emma gets a chance to pursue her dream of becoming an international top model, she leaves her everyday life in Denmark behind, and moves to Paris. At a photo shoot in her new hometown, she meets the attractive photographer Shane White and they fall in love. But their relationship soon turns into a dangerous obsession for Emma.
