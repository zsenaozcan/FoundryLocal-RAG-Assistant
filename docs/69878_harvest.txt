Title: Harvest (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Marc Meyers
Main Cast: Robert Loggia, Jack Carpenter, Victoria Clark, Arye Gross, Barbara Barrie
Keywords: war veteran, summer

Overview: Gathered one summer in a beautiful shoreline town, three generations are drawn together by their patriarch. With endearing moments of humor and uplifting spirit, Harvest is a portrait of a family awkwardly yet delicately hanging on to what was, what now is, and to one another.
