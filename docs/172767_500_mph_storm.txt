Title: 500 MPH Storm (2013)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Daniel Lusko
Main Cast: Casper Van Dien, Sarah Lieving, Michael Beach, Bryan Head, Keith Meriweather
Keywords: hurricane

Overview: When an energy experiment goes haywire, a rash of massive hurricanes rips across North America. A high school science teacher must get his family to safety before the hurricanes merge, creating a "hypercane" with the power to wipe the US off the map.
