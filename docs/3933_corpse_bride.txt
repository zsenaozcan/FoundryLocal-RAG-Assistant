Title: Corpse Bride (2005)
Genres: Romance, Fantasy, Animation, Music
Director: Tim Burton
Main Cast: Johnny Depp, Helena Bonham Carter, Emily Watson, Tracey Ullman, Paul Whitehouse
Keywords: shyness, england, cheating, old town, grave, skeleton, musical, marriage, wedding ring, stop motion

Overview: Set in a 19th-century european village, this stop-motion animation feature follows the story of Victor, a young man whisked away to the underworld and wed to a mysterious corpse bride, while his real bride Victoria waits bereft in the land of the living.
