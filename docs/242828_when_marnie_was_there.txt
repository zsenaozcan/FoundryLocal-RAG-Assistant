Title: When Marnie Was There (2014)
Genres: Animation, Drama
Director: Hiromasa Yonebayashi
Main Cast: Sara Takatsuki, Kasumi Arimura, Nanako Matsushima, Susumu Terajima, Toshie Negishi
Keywords: friendship, imaginary friend, photograph, young girl, flashback, anime, personal diary

Overview: A young girl is sent to the country for health reasons, where she meets an unlikely friend in the form of Marnie, a young girl with flowing blonde hair. As the friendship unravels it is possible that Marnie has closer ties to the protagonist than we might expect.
