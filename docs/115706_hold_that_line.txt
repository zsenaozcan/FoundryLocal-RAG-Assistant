Title: Hold That Line (1952)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: William Beaudine
Main Cast: Leo Gorcey, Huntz Hall, John Bromfield, Veda Ann Borg, Mona Knox
Keywords: sport, bowery boys

Overview: The Bowery Boys are enrolled in a fancy college by a pair of rich snobs who think they can turn the Boys into classy guys. Sach becomes a football star, and is kidnapped by gangsters to keep him out of the big game.
