Title: The Amazing Truth About Queen Raquela (2008)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Olaf de Fleur Johannesson
Main Cast: Raquela Rios, Olivia Galudo, Stefan C. Schaefer
Keywords: biography, iceland, drama

Overview: Raquela, a transsexual from the Philippines, dreams of escaping the streets of Cebu City for a fairy tale life in Paris.
