Title: The Wicker Man (1973)
Genres: Horror, Music, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Robin Hardy
Main Cast: Edward Woodward, Christopher Lee, Britt Ekland, Diane Cilento, Ingrid Pitt
Keywords: scotland, island, virgin, ritual, cult, human sacrifice, disappearance, paganism

Overview: Police sergeant Neil Howie is called to an island village in search of a missing girl whom the locals claim never existed. Stranger still, however, are the rituals that take place there.
