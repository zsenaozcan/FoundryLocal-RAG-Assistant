Title: The Gumball Rally (1976)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Charles Bail
Main Cast: Michael Sarrazin, Norman Burton, Gary Busey, John Durren, Susan Flannery
Keywords: car race, sport

Overview: A group of people from different backgrounds have one thing in common: when they hear the world "gumball" whispered by one of the others, they know that it's time for the Gumball Rally: a no-holds barred, secret, winner-take-all rally across the USA.
