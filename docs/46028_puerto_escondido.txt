Title: Puerto Escondido (1992)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Gabriele Salvatores
Main Cast: Diego Abatantuono, Valeria Golino, Claudio Bisio, Renato Carpentieri, Antonio Catania

Overview: Mario leads a normal life, working in a Milan bank. His daily routine is shattered when he witnesses a murder and is shot himself by the killer, a deranged police commissioner.
