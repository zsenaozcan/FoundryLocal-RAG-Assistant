Title: Kabukicho Love Hotel (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Ryuichi Hiroki
Main Cast: Shota Sometani, Atsuko Maeda, Kaho Minami, Lee Eun-woo, Yutaka Matsushige
Keywords: fugitive, illegal immigrant, prostitution, love hotel

Overview: This erotically charged drama from Japanese director Ryuichi Hiroki (Vibrator) traces the intersecting stories of a group of employees and visitors at a notorious "love hotel" in Tokyo's red-light district.
