Title: Blood, Guts, Bullets and Octane (1998)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Thriller
Director: Joe Carnahan
Main Cast: Mike Maas, Nick Fenske, Mark Priolo, Andrew Fowler, Gloria Gómez

Overview: A Cherry Pontiac Lemans Convertible...Two Days...Two-Hundred &amp; Fifty Grand. When your lemon lot hits the skids you glom the gig no matter what the smell. For Bob and Sid, two slicked-back burnouts, bum luck runs in spades. With a goose-egg for cash flow and a fore-closure falling fast, they take the gig. The Upside: Fat Cash...The Flipside...Every Thug, Crook, Punk and Mercenary on the planet looking to get rich.
