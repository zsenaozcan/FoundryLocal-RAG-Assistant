Title: Summers Past (2008)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ole Giæver
Main Cast: Maria Bock, Yngvild Støen Grotmol, Fredrik Longva, Marte Magnusdotter Solem, Kim Sørensen

Overview: Renate is afraid that if she allows herself to mourn her dead mother, she will lose control over her own life. When she finally realizes that suppressing her depression is destroying the relationship with her sister, Renate understands that she is about to lose all control over herself.
