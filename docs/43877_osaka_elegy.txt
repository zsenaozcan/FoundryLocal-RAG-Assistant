Title: Osaka Elegy (1936)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kenji Mizoguchi
Main Cast: Isuzu Yamada, Chiyoko Ôkura, Shinpachirô Asaka, Benkei Shiganoya, Yôko Umemura
Keywords: mistress, embezzlement

Overview: Ayako becomes the mistress of her boss so she can pay her father's debt and prevent him from going to prison for embezzlement.
