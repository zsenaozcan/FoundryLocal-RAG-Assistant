Title: Monolith (2017)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Ivan Silvestrini
Main Cast: Katrina Bowden, Jay Hayden, Damon Dayoub, Katherine Kelly Lang, David H. Stevens
Keywords: mother, son, family relationships, relationship, parent, trapped, car, suv, suffering parent, stuck
