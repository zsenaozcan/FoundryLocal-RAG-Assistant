Title: Saving Shiloh (2006)
Genres: Family, Drama
Director: Sandy Tung
Main Cast: Jason Dolley, Jordan Garrett, Scott Wilson, Gerald McRaney, Kyle Chavarria
Keywords: small town, rescue, dog, animal actor, boy and dog, heart warming

Overview: In this third movie about Shiloh, Judd Travers is accused of killing a man he once fought in a bar. Everyone in the town consider Judd to be guilty except Marty Preston, but even him has some doubts. Trying to clear Judd's name Marty and his friend searches the woods to find the criminal.
