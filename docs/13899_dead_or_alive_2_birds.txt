Title: Dead or Alive 2: Birds (2000)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Takashi Miike
Main Cast: Sho Aikawa, Riki Takeuchi, Noriko Aota, Edison Chen, Ken'ichi Endô
Keywords: rape, yakuza, shooting, blood splatter, sequel, shootout, cigar smoking, gangster, massacre, violence

Overview: Two contract killers cross paths in the middle of the same job and realize they are childhood friends. Together they take a break from killing and visit the small island they once called home. After reflecting on their past lives they decided to team up and use their talents in killing for good... much to the upset of the crime syndicates.
