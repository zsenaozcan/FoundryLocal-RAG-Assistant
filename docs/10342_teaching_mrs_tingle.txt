Title: Teaching Mrs. Tingle (1999)
Genres: Comedy, Thriller
Director: Kevin Williamson
Main Cast: Helen Mirren, Katie Holmes, Jeffrey Tambor, Molly Ringwald, Vivica A. Fox
Keywords: pornography, exam, bed, graduation, final exam, class, scholarship, bastille, suspense, mother daughter relationship

Overview: Leanne is salutatorian when she needs to be valedictorian to get her scholarship to Harvard. The only class she is worse than the leader in is history, taught by Mrs. Tingle, and the teacher hates her. When an attempt to get ahead in Mrs. Tingle's class goes awry, mayhem ensues and friendships, loyalties and trust are tested by the teacher's intricate mind-games.
