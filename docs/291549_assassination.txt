Title: Assassination (2015)
Genres: Action, Drama, History, Thriller
Director: Dong-hun Choi
Main Cast: Jun Ji-hyun, Ha Jung-woo, Lee Jung-jae, Oh Dal-su, Cho Jin-woong
Keywords: assassination, japanese occupation

Overview: Assassination is set in Korea and Shanghai in the 1930's during the Japanese occupation. The government resistance assigns a secret commando group with killing collaborators.
