Title: Don't Look Now: We're Being Shot At (1966)
Genres: Comedy, War
Director: Gérard Oury
Main Cast: Bourvil, Louis de Funès, Terry-Thomas, Claudio Brook, Mike Marshall
Keywords: paris, world war ii, british, paratroops, french resistance, nazi occupation

Overview: During World War II, two French civilians and a downed English Bomber Crew set out from Paris to cross the demarcation line between Nazi-occupied Northern France and the South. From there they will be able to escape to England. First, they must avoid German troops - and the consequences of their own blunders.
