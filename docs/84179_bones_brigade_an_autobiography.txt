Title: Bones Brigade: An Autobiography (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Stacy Peralta
Main Cast: Tony Alva, Steve Caballero, Shepard Fairey, Tommy Guerrero, Ben Harper
Keywords: california, skateboarding, skateboarder

Overview: When six teenage boys came together as a skateboarding team in the 1980s, they reinvented not only their chosen sport but themselves too – as they evolved from insecure outsiders to the most influential athletes in the field.
