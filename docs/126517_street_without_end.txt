Title: Street Without End (1934)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mikio Naruse
Main Cast: Setsuko Shinobu, Akio Isono, Hikaru Yamanouchi, Nobuko Wakaba, Fumiko Katsuragi
Keywords: waitress, boredom, marriage, hospital, car accident, family, hometown, boyfriend

Overview: Mikio Naruse’s final silent film is a gloriously rich portrait of a waitress, Sugiko, whose life, despite a host of male admirers and even some intrigued movie talent scouts, ends up taking a suffocatingly domestic turn after a wealthy businessman accidentally hits her with his car
