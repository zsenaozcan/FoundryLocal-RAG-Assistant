Title: Confessions of an Opium Eater (1962)
Genres: Drama
Director: Albert Zugsmith
Main Cast: Vincent Price, Linda Ho, Richard Loo, June Kim, Philip Ahn
Keywords: san francisco, destiny, adventurer

Overview: Vincent Price stars in this early '60s adaptation of Thomas De Quincey's thriller about an opium addict trying to solve a mystery in San Francisco's Chinatown.
