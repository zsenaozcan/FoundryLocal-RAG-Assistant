Title: Diminished Capacity (2008)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Thriller
Director: Terry Kinney
Main Cast: Matthew Broderick, Virginia Madsen, Alan Alda, Jimmy Bennett, Louis C.K.
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A Chicago journalist suffering from memory loss takes leaves from his job and returns to his rural hometown, where he bonds with his Alzheimer's impaired uncle Rollie and his old flame.
