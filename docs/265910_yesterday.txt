Title: Yesterday (1988)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ivan Andonov
Main Cast: Hristo Shopov, Georgi Staykov, Georgi Rusev, Sofiya Kuzeva, Svetla Todorova

Overview: The late 1960s was the time of Beatles and Rolling Stones, the time of sexual revolution. These events have their echo in Bulgarian English-learning school. The school order provokes a protest of the students due to the narrow-minded teachers.
