Title: Adult World (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Scott Coffey
Main Cast: Emma Roberts, Evan Peters, John Cusack, Armando Riesco, Shannon Woodward
Keywords: sex-shop, poetry, young adult, college graduate, hero worship, college debt

Overview: Amy, a naive college graduate who believes she's destined to be a great poet, begrudgingly accepts a job at a sex shop while she pursues a mentorship with reclusive writer Rat Billings.
