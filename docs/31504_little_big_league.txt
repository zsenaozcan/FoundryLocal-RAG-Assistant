Title: Little Big League (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Andrew Scheinman
Main Cast: Luke Edwards, Timothy Busfield, John Ashton, Dennis Farina, Jonathan Silverman
Keywords: baseball, sport

Overview: The Chairman of the Minnesota Twins passes away and in his will, leaves his grandson, Billy Heywood, ownership of the team. Billy appoints himself the new manager. But will the proud, arrogant arrogant players of the team, be willing to take orders and tactics from a 12 year old boy?
