Title: At the Death House Door (2008)
Genres: Crime, Documentary
Director: Steve James
Main Cast: Carroll Pickett, Steve Mills, Maurice Possley, Anne Ellis, Charlotte Hirschfelder
Keywords: independent film

Overview: An investigation of the wrongful death of Carlos DeLuna, who was executed in Texas on December 7, 1989, after prosecutors ignored evidence inculpating a man, who bragged to friends about committing the crimes of which DeLuna was convicted.
