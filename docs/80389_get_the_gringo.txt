Title: Get the Gringo (2012)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: Adrian Grunberg
Main Cast: Mel Gibson, Peter Stormare, Dean Norris, Bob Gunton, Kevin Hernandez
Keywords: prison, mexico, vacation, prison escape

Overview: A career criminal (Gibson) nabbed by Mexican authorities is placed in a tough prison where he learns to survive with the help of a 9-year-old boy.
