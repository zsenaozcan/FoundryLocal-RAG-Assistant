Title: Enemies with Benefits (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Steven Vasquez
Main Cast: Chance Feandro, Edward Gutierrez, Desi Santana, Steven Bright, Marshall Thomas

Overview: 19 year old Jamie just wants someone to love. With no one to call his own he just decides to put an end to it all. That is until a visitor from beyond shows Jamie that his life has meaning and that he needs to stay alive and fulfill his destiny. But the visitors motives may not be all for the good in this over the top throwback to the casual sex, clothes optional adult comedy.
