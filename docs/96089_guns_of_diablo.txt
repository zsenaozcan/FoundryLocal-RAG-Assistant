Title: Guns of Diablo (1965)
Genres: Western
Director: Boris Sagal
Main Cast: Charles Bronson, Susan Oliver, Kurt Russell, Jan Merlin, John Fiedler
Keywords: showdown, shootout

Overview: Bronson and Russell are up against some bad guys who run the town to which the pair have gone for supplies. Bronson runs into an old girl friend, Oliver, who is now married to his enemy Merlin. Bronson had long ago broken Merlin's arm, and now the man wants revenge.
