Title: And the Wife Shall Revere Her Husband (1965)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Yorgos Javellas
Main Cast: Maro Kodou, Giorgos Konstadinou, Despo Diamantidou, Stavros Xenidis, Katerina Gogou

Overview: When a man decides to marry the woman he's been living with, she stops being the silent patient person she was and claims to be the boss in the house. Her behaviour changes so much after the marriage that the husband gets so irritated as to ask for a divorce even a few hours later..
