Title: How to Be a Man (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Chadd Harbold
Main Cast: Gavin McInnes, Liam Aiken, Paulo Costanzo, Brian Petsos, Megan Neuringer

Overview: When former comedian Mark McCarthy is faced with a rare form of cancer, he hires a young, impressionable cameraman to document his crude and comical lessons on what it means to be a man for his unborn son.
