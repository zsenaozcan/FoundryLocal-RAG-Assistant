Title: Experiment Perilous (1944)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Jacques Tourneur
Main Cast: Hedy Lamarr, George Brent, Paul Lukas, Albert Dekker, Carl Esmond
Keywords: train ride, insanity, step son, film noir

Overview: In 1903, Doctor Huntington Bailey (Brent) meets a friendly older lady during a train trip. She tells him that she is going to visit her brother Nick and his lovely young wife Allida. Once in New York, Bailey hears that his train companion suddenly died. Shortly afterward, he meets the strange couple and gets suspicious of Nick's treatment of his wife.
