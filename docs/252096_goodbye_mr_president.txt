Title: Goodbye, Mr. President (1987)
Genres: Action
Director: Matti Kassila
Main Cast: Hannu Lauri, Laila Räikkä, Antti Litja, Esa Saario, Aake Kalliala
Keywords: sadistic psychopath, assassination of president

Overview: Asko Mertanen is a waiter who is very interested in shooting. He gets fed up how the things are run in Finland, so he thinks that the best way to change them is to shoot the Finnish president.
