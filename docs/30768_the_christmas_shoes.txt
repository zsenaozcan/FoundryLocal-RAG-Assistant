Title: The Christmas Shoes (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: Andy Wolk
Main Cast: Rob Lowe, Max Morrow, Kimberly Williams-Paisley, Maria del Mar, Hugh Thompson
Keywords: christmas

Overview: Two separate stories mesh - in the first, a young music teacher, Maggie Andrews, begins dying of a heart condition and her son Nathan tries to get a pair of Christmas shoes for her before she dies. In the second, lawyer Robert Layton and his wife Kate are slowly drifting apart and the matter comes to a head during Christmas when Kate takes over for Maggie for the school choir.
