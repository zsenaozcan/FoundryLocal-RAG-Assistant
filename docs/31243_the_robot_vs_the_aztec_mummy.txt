Title: The Robot vs. The Aztec Mummy (1958)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror
Director: Rafael Portillo
Main Cast: Ramón Gay, Rosita Arenas, Crox Alvarado, Luis Aceves Castañeda
Keywords: mexico, killer robot, mummy

Overview: A mad doctor builds a robot in order to steal a valuable Aztec treasure from a tomb guarded by a centuries old living mummy.
