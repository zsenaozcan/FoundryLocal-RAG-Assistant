Title: Hallowed Ground (2007)
Genres: Horror
Director: David Benullo
Main Cast: Jaimie Alexander, Brian McNamara, Ethan Phillips, Chloë Grace Moretz, Ned Vaughn

Overview: After she becomes stranded in a small town, a young woman discovers her arrival there was foretold a century earlier by the town's founding preacher and that she is an integral part of his impending - and terrifying - rebirth.
