Title: Certain Prey (2011)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Action, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Chris Gerolmo
Main Cast: Mark Harmon, Lola Glaudini, Tatiana Maslany

Overview: Minneapolis Deputy Police Chief, Lucas Davenport, has to face off against a duo unlike any he's ever encountered; a lethal hit woman and a ferociously cunning killer determined to hunt him down.
