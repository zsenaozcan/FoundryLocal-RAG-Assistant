Title: Dr. Socrates (1935)
Genres: Crime
Director: William Dieterle
Main Cast: Paul Muni, Ann Dvorak, Barton MacLane, Robert Barrat, John Eldredge
Keywords: vigilante, typhoid

Overview: Dr. Socrates is a 1935 crime film starring Paul Muni as a doctor forced to treat a wounded gangster, played by Barton MacLane.
