Title: One Girl's Confession (1953)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hugo Haas
Main Cast: Cleo Moore, Hugo Haas, Glenn Langan, Ellen Stansbury, Anthony Jochim
Keywords: b movie, older man younger woman relationship

Overview: Cleo Moore stars as Mary Adams, whose first step on the road to ruin is a $25,000 robbery. Mary hides the money, then confesses to the crime, secure in the belief that she can dig up the loot upon her release from prison.
