Title: The Karen Carpenter Story (1989)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Joseph Sargent
Main Cast: Cynthia Gibb, Mitchell Anderson, Peter Michael Goetz, Michael McGuire, Lise Hilboldt
Keywords: rise and fall, positive music, musical siblings

Overview: Story of the meteoric rise and sudden fall of Karen Carpenter, who became a famous singer before battling anorexia and bulimia. This made-for-TV movie is the authorized version of the life of Karen Carpenter and was made with the approval of Richard Carpenter and the Carpenter family.
