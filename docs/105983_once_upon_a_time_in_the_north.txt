Title: Once Upon a Time in the North (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: JP Siili
Main Cast: Mikko Leppilampi, Pamela Tola, Aku Hirviniemi, Esko Salminen
Keywords: pohjanmaa

Overview: 19th century drama set on the plains of Ostrobothnia, western Finland, which are controlled by groups of knife-wielding thugs, the toughs. Traditionally, the first-born son inherits everything, so when Esko – a bum and a gang leader – learns that his father will leave the Välitalo estate to his younger and exemplary brother, all hell breaks loose.
