Title: The Spiderwick Chronicles (2008)
Genres: Adventure, Family, Fantasy
Director: Mark Waters
Main Cast: Freddie Highmore, Mary-Louise Parker, Nick Nolte, Sarah Bolger, Andrew McCarthy
Keywords: brother sister relationship, family relationships, single mother, alternate reality, mother child relationship, hidden truth, goblin, magical creature, fairies
