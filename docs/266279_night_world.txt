Title: Night World (1932)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hobart Henley
Main Cast: Lew Ayres, Mae Clarke, Boris Karloff, Dorothy Revier, Russell Hopton
Keywords: gambling, prohibition, blackmail

Overview: "Happy" MacDonald and his unfaithful wife own a Prohibition era night club. On this eventful night, he is threatened by bootleggers, and the club's star dancer falls in love with a young socialite who drinks to forget a personal tragedy, among other incidents.
