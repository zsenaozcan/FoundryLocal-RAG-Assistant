Title: Comradeship (1931)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: G.W. Pabst
Main Cast: Alexander Granach, Fritz Kampers, Ernst Busch, Elisabeth Wendt, Gustav Püttjer
Keywords: mine, german, cooperation

Overview: The picture tells of a mine disaster where German miners rescue French miners from an undergound fire and explosion. The story takes place in the Lorraine/Saar region, along the border between France and Germany.
