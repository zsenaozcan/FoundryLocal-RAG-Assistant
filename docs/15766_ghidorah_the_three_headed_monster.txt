Title: Ghidorah, the Three-Headed Monster (1964)
Genres: Action, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Ishirô Honda
Main Cast: Yōsuke Natsuki, Yuriko Hoshi, Hiroshi Koizumi, Akiko Wakabayashi, Emi Ito
Keywords: helicopter, monster, amnesia, volcano, princess, godzilla, mothra, sequel, alien, giant monster

Overview: The 5th film in the original Godzilla franchise marked a turning point in the series.  Godzilla, Rodan and Mothra must put aside their quarrelsome ways to become allies and defend earth from 3 headed Dragon King Ghidorah.
