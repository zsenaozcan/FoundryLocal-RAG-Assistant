Title: One Missed Call (2003)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Takashi Miike
Main Cast: Kou Shibasaki, Shinichi Tsutsumi, Kazue Fukiishi, Anna Nagata, Atsushi Ida
Keywords: mass murder, restaurant, victim, knife, friends, clinic, student, spirit, series of murders, death

Overview: People mysteriously start receiving voicemail messages from their future selves, in the form of the sound of them reacting to their own violent deaths, along with the exact date and time of their future death, listed on the message log. The plot thickens as the surviving characters persue the answers to this mystery which could save their live
