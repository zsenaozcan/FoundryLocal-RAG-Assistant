Title: Sometimes They Come Back (1991)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Tom McLoughlin
Main Cast: Tim Matheson, Brooke Adams, Robert Rusler, Chris Demetral, Robert Hy Gorman
Keywords: loss of brother, high school, bully, train, car, childhood, ghost, tunnel, suppressed memory

Overview: Based on a short story by Stephen King, a man and his family return to his home town, but he is then harassed by teenagers that died when he was a kid.
