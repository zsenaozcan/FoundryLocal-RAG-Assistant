Title: Me and Orson Welles (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Richard Linklater
Main Cast: Zac Efron, Claire Danes, Christian McKay, Imogen Poots, Zoe Kazan
Keywords: new york, based on novel, historical figure, theater, 1930s
