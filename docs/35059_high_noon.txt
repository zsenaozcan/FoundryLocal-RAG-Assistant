Title: High Noon (2009)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Peter Markle
Main Cast: Emilie de Ravin, Cybill Shepherd, Ivan Sergei, Brian Markinson, Ty Olsson
Keywords: tv movie

Overview: Expert hostage negotiator Lieutenant Phoebe McNamara juggles her high-pressure career with the demands of raising her young daughter and contending with her agoraphobic mother, Essie. When Phoebe meets handsome bar owner Duncan Swift, he can’t resist her take-charge attitude and attempts to earn her affections.
