Title: We Don't Care About Music Anyway (2009)
Genres: Music, Documentary
Director: Cédric Dupire
Main Cast: Hiromichi Sakamoto, Tomoko Shimazaki, Ken Takehisa, Fuyuki Yamakawa, Otomo Yoshihide
Keywords: improvisation

Overview: From radical turntablism (Otomo Yoshihide) to laptop music innovation (Numb), via classical instrument hijacking (Sakamoto Hiromichi), Tokyo's avant-garde music scene is internationally known for its boldness.  While introducing some of the greatest musicians of this scene, "We Don't Care About Music Anyway..." offers a kaleidoscopic view of Tokyo, confronting music and noise, sound and image, reality and representation, documentary and fiction.
