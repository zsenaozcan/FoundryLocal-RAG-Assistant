Title: The Lure (2015)
Genres: Horror, Music
Director: Agnieszka Smoczynska
Main Cast: Kinga Preis, Michalina Olszańska, Marta Mazurek, Katarzyna Herman, Andrzej Konopka
Keywords: musical, woman director

Overview: Two mermaid sisters, who end up performing at a nightclub, face cruel and bloody choices when one of them falls in love with a beautiful young man.
