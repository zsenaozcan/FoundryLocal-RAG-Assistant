Title: Ah Nerede (1975)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Orhan Aksoy
Main Cast: Tarık Akan, Gulsen Bubikoglu, Hulusi Kentmen, Adile Naşit, Halit Akçatepe

Overview: Three boys are having their higher education in Istanbul (!). In fact, they are messing around and dealing with radical politics, girls and gamble. However the oldest discovers love, and the three realize it is time for change.
