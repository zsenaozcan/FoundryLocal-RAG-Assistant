Title: Hitchcock (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sacha Gervasi
Main Cast: Anthony Hopkins, Helen Mirren, Scarlett Johansson, James D'Arcy, Jessica Biel
Keywords: wife husband relationship, film director, biography, movie set, movie actress, movie actor

Overview: Hitchcock follows the relationship between director Alfred Hitchcock and his wife Alma Reville during the making of his most famous film "Psycho" - and the trials and tribulations the director faced from Hollywood censors.
