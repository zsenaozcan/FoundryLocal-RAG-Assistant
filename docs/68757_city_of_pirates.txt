Title: City of Pirates (1984)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Horror, Thriller
Director: Raúl Ruiz
Main Cast: Hugues Quester, Anne Alvaro, Melvil Poupaud, André Engel, João Bénard da Costa

Overview: A surreal odyssey in which a melancholic maidservant crosses paths with a homicidal little boy, travels to a tiny island of pirates and encounters a man with multiple personalities.
