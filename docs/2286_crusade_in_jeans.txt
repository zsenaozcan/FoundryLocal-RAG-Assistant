Title: Crusade in Jeans (2006)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, History
Director: Ben Sombogaart
Main Cast: Johnny Flynn, Stephanie Leonidas, Emily Watson, Michael Culkin, Benno Fürmann
Keywords: crusade, crusader, time travel, leap in time, middle ages

Overview: Dolf a 15 year old boy is sent back in time by a timemachine. Accidentally he is sent back to the Middle Ages. He is rescued by children who are part of a childrens' crusade, on their way to rescue Jeruzalem. During the trip Dolf finds out the danger is not coming from outside the crusade, but from within.
