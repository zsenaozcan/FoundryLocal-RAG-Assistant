Title: Drunken Monkey (2002)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Liu Chia-Liang
Main Cast: Wu Jing, Wing-Kin Lau, Shannon Yao, Liu Chia-Liang, Chi Kuan-Chun
Keywords: martial arts, kung fu, opium, female martial artist, drunken master

Overview: China, the early 1930's: martial arts master Wen Biao discovers that his brother has been using their security company for illegal activities. A confrontation between the brothers leaves Web Biao missing and presumed dead.
