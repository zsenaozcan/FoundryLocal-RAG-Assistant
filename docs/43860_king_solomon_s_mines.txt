Title: King Solomon's Mines (1937)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: Robert Stevenson
Main Cast: Paul Robeson, Cedric Hardwicke, Anna Lee, Roland Young, John Loder
Keywords: allan quatermain

Overview: Adventurer Allan Quartermain leads an expedition into uncharted African territory in an attempt to locate an explorer who went missing during his search for the fabled diamond mines of King Solomon.
