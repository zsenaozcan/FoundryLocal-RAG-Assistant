Title: I'm Photogenic (1980)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Dino Risi
Main Cast: Renato Pozzetto, Edwige Fenech, Aldo Maccione, Massimo Boldi, Michel Galabru

Overview: Antonio Barozzi moves from Lago Maggiore to Rome to become an actor. He does not realize his agent and acting coach are only manipulating him to further their own careers.
