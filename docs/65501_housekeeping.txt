Title: Housekeeping (1987)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Bill Forsyth
Main Cast: Christine Lahti, Sara Walker, Andrea Burchill, Wayne Robson, Anne Pitoniak
Keywords: small town, train accident

Overview: In the Pacific Northwest during the 1950s, two young sisters whose mother has abandoned them wind up living with their Aunt Sylvie, whose views of the world and its conventions don't quite live up to most people's expectations.
