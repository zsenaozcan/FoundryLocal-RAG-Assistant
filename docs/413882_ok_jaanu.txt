Title: Ok Jaanu (2017)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Shaad Ali
Main Cast: Aditya Roy Kapoor, Shraddha Kapoor, Leela Samson, Naseeruddin Shah, Kitu Gidwani
Keywords: living together, urban life, love sickness, friends with benefit

Overview: OK Jaanu is an upcoming Indian romantic drama film directed by Shaad Ali and produced by Karan Johar under his banner Dharma Productions. It is an official remake of Mani Ratnam's Tamil film OK Kanmani.
