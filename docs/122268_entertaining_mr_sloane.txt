Title: Entertaining Mr. Sloane (1970)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Douglas Hickox
Main Cast: Beryl Reid, Harry Andrews, Peter McEnery, Alan Webb, Charles Sinnickson
Keywords: mystery

Overview: Sloane, a handsome, sexy and completely amoral young man, joins Kath's household as a lodger and proceeds to manipulate her and her brother, Ed. He is recognized by Kemp (Dadda) as the murderer of Kemp's former employer, whereupon Sloane murders Kemp. Sloane's "just desserts" are not what one would expect.
