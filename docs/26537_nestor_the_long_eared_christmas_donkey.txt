Title: Nestor, the Long-Eared Christmas Donkey (1977)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Jules Bass
Main Cast: Roger Miller, Brenda Vaccaro, Eric Stern
Keywords: holiday

Overview: Nestor the donkey is a bit of an oddity--his long ears are enough for six donkeys and stretch all the way to the ground. One night, when Nestor is locked out in the cold, he begins to wander the desert.
