Title: Stand Up Guys (2013)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy, Action
Director: Fisher Stevens
Main Cast: Alan Arkin, Julianna Margulies, Al Pacino, Christopher Walken, Katheryn Winnick
Keywords: release from prison, viagra, settle scores, last hurrah

Overview: After serving 28 years in prison for accidentally killing the son of a crime boss, newly paroled gangster Val reunites with his former partners in crime, Doc and Hirsch, for a night on the town. As the three men revisit old haunts, reflect on their glory days and try to make up for lost time, one wrestles with a terrible quandary: Doc has orders to kill Val, and time is running out for him to figure out a way out of his dilemma.
