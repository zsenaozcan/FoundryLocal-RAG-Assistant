Title: The First Nudie Musical (1976)
Genres: Music, Comedy
Director: Bruce Kimmel
Main Cast: Stephen Nathan, Cindy Williams, Bruce Kimmel, Leslie Ackerman, Alan Abelew
Keywords: musical, porn parody

Overview: The son of famous studio owner is forced to make porno films to keep the bankrupt studio from being made into a shopping center. In an attempt to get back on the high ground he makes a nudie musical. He makes a bet with the debtors who wish to take ownership of the studio, that if they finance the musical and he can't complete it within two weeks, they can foreclose.
