Title: Tanya's Island (1980)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Romance
Director: Alfred Sole
Main Cast: Vanity, Richard Sargent, Mariette Levesque, Don McLeod, Donny Burns
Keywords: beach, island, nudity, fantasy, model

Overview: Tanya is a female model who lives with her boyfriend Lobo, a surrealist painter who is extremely violent. Subjected to Lobo's constant abuse, Tanya dreams of escaping to a desert island, which comes true. The only other being on her island is an enormous blue-eyed man-ape who was born from one of Lobo's paintings.
