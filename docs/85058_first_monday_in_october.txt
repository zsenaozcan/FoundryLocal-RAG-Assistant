Title: First Monday in October (1981)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Ronald Neame
Main Cast: Walter Matthau, Jill Clayburgh, Barnard Hughes, Jan Sterling, James Stephens
Keywords: washington d.c., judge, law, supreme court, judiciary
