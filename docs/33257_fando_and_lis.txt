Title: Fando and Lis (1968)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: Alejandro Jodorowsky
Main Cast: Sergio Kleiner, Diana Mariscal, María Teresa Rivas, Tamara Garina, Valerie Jodorowsky
Keywords: post-apocalyptic, surrealism, based on play or musical

Overview: Fando and his partially paralyzed lover Lis search for the mythical city of Tar. Based on Jodorowsky's memories of a play by surrealist Fernando Arrabal.
