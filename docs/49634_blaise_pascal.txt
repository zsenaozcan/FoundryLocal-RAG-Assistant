Title: Blaise Pascal (1972)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Roberto Rossellini
Main Cast: Pierre Arditi, Rita Forzano, Giuseppe Addobbati, Christian De Sica, Livio Galassi
Keywords: independent film

Overview: In this evocative, atmospheric biography, Roberto Rossellini brings to life philosopher and mathematician Blaise Pascal, who, amid religious persecution and ignorance, believed in a harmony between God and science.
