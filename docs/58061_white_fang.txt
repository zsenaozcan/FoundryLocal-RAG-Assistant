Title: White Fang (1973)
Genres: Western, Adventure, Family
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Franco Nero, Daniel Martín, John Steiner, Virna Lisi, Fernando Rey

Overview: The wolf dog, White Fang, aids a reporter, a fur trapper, a nun, a young Eskimo boy and his father of ridding a gold mining town of a sleazy crime lord in 1896 Yukon, Canada.
