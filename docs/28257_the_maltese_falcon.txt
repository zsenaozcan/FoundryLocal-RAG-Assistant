Title: The Maltese Falcon (1931)
Genres: Crime, Mystery
Director: Roy Del Ruth
Main Cast: Bebe Daniels, Ricardo Cortez, Dudley Digges, Una Merkel, Thelma Todd
Keywords: femme fatale, mystery, black bird, sam spade

Overview: A lovely dame with dangerous lies employs the services of a private detective, who is quickly caught up in the mystery and intrigue of a statuette known as the Maltese Falcon.
