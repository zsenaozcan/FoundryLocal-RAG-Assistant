Title: The Man Who Challenged the Devil (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Moacyr Góes
Main Cast: Marcos Palmeira, Flávia Alessandra, Fernanda Paes Leme, Lívia Falcão, Antônio Pitanga
Keywords: brazil, regionalism

Overview: After being forced into a marriage and enduring a humiliating work routine in the hands of his father-in-law, Zé Araújo becomes the mythical Ojuara, an unconventional hero devoted to debauchery.
