Title: Our City Dreams (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Chiara Clemente
Main Cast: Ghada Amer, Marina Abramović, Caledonia Curry, Kiki Smith, Nancy Spero
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Filmed over the course of two years, Our City Dreams is the story of a woman's struggles and successes as an artist in New York City. Told through five women artists, from youngest to oldest, the film features Swoon, Ghada Amer, Kiki Smith, Marina Abramovic, and Nancy Spero. From the studio to the streets of New York, from the canals of Venice to the alleys of Cairo and the beaches of Phuket, Our City Dreams takes us deep into the artists' worlds.
