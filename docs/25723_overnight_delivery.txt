Title: Overnight Delivery (1998)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Jason Bloom
Main Cast: Paul Rudd, Christine Taylor, Reese Witherspoon, Sarah Silverman, Jim Belushi
Keywords: love, unfaithful girlfriend

Overview: A man suspects his girlfriend of being unfaithful. So he sends her a letter, but than finds out that he was wrong. He has 24 hours to stop the package, prevent a disaster, and fall in love. The only problem is the delivery man will not stop until the package has been delivered.
