Title: It's My Turn (1980)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Claudia Weill
Main Cast: Jill Clayburgh, Michael Douglas, Charles Grodin, Beverly Garland, Steven Hill
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A successful but stressed mathematics professor (Clayburgh) goes to her father's wedding and falls in love with her father's bride's son (Douglas), a prematurely retired pro baseball player. She must choose between him and her current boyfriend (Grodin), between Chicago and New York, and between research and administration.
