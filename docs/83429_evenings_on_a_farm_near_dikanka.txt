Title: Evenings on a Farm near Dikanka (1961)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Romance

Overview: Funny story about how on Christmas Eve in the Ukrainian village of beguiled the devil and how mighty Vakula managed to overcome evil spirits, and to take his bride to the king's slippers. A screen adaptation of Nikolai Gogol "The Night Before Christmas."
