Title: Paganini Horror (1989)
Genres: Horror, Music
Director: Luigi Cozzi
Main Cast: Daria Nicolodi, Jasmine Maimone, Pascal Persiano, Maria Cristina Mastrangeli, Michel Klippstein
Keywords: pact with the devil, supernatural, gore, mansion, series of murders

Overview: Rock and roll band purchases piece of music to record that were used in satanic rituals. Band decides to record and tape video of song in mansion where rituals took place. As one may guess, the spirits are summoned to commit mayhem and murder.
