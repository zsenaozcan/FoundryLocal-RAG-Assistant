Title: Inseparable (2011)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Thriller
Director: Dayyan Eng
Main Cast: Kevin Spacey, Daniel Wu, Gong Beibi, Yan Ni, Peter Stormare

Overview: A troubled young man, Li faces pressure at work and problems at home with his moody wife. Chuck, rescues Li from the brink of despair and becomes an unlikely mentor. But who is Chuck really?
