Title: Hear No Evil (1993)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Robert Greenwald
Main Cast: Marlee Matlin, D. B. Sweeney, Martin Sheen, John C. McGinley, Christina Carlisi
Keywords: corruption, robbery, journalist, nudity, museum, chase, cop, murder, mystery, coin

Overview: Jillian Shanahan, a deaf woman, becomes the target of a ruthless and corrupt cop. The cop is looking for a stolen coin, which he plans to keep for himself. A journalist briefly acquires the coin and hides it in Jillian's apartment, then his car explodes, now the cop is after her.
