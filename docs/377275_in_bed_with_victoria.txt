Title: In Bed with Victoria (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Justine Triet
Main Cast: Virginie Efira, Vincent Lacoste, Melvil Poupaud, Laurent Poitrenaux, Laure Calamy
Keywords: wedding, woman director, legal dispute, two daughters, bland personality

Overview: Victoria Spick, a criminal lawyer who is single, turns up at a wedding and meets three men there: David, the father of her daughters; Vincent, a friend of hers; and Sam, a former drug dealer who she bailed out. The next day, Vincent is accused of attempted murder by his partner.
