Title: Superman/Batman: Apocalypse (2010)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Animation
Director: Lauren Montgomery
Main Cast: Tim Daly, Kevin Conroy, Summer Glau, Ed Asner, Andre Braugher
Keywords: dc comics, superhero, superhuman, super powers, woman director, supergirl
