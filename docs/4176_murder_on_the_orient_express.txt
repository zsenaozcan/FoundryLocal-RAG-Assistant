Title: Murder on the Orient Express (1974)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Sidney Lumet
Main Cast: Albert Finney, Lauren Bacall, Martin Balsam, Ingrid Bergman, Jacqueline Bisset
Keywords: detective, repayment, orient express, investigation, train ride, murder mystery

Overview: In 1935, when his train is stopped by deep snow, detective Hercule Poirot is called on to solve a murder that occurred in his car the night before.
