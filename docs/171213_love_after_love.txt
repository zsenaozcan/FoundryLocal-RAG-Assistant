Title: Love After Love (1992)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Diane Kurys
Main Cast: Isabelle Huppert, Bernard Giraudeau, Hippolyte Girardot, Lio, Yvan Attal
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Lola is an independent woman, a professional writer with 2 men on a string. Both men are married with children. When the men, and Lola, face having to make choices, Lola's comfortable life becomes less appealing.
