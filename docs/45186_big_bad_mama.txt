Title: Big Bad Mama (1974)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime, Drama
Director: Steve Carver
Main Cast: Angie Dickinson, Tom Skerritt, William Shatner, Susan Sennett, Robbie Lee
Keywords: prohibition, gun battle, social satire, campy

Overview: Mama and daughters get forced by circumstances into bootlegging and bank robbing, and travel across the country trailed by the law.
