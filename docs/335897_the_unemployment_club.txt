Title: The Unemployment Club (2003)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Andy Bausch
Main Cast: Véronique Fauconnet, Fernand Fox, Christian Kmiotek, Marco Lorenzini, Myriam Muller

Overview: In Differdange, in the South of Luxembourg, six unemployed men decide to create the "Dole Club", whose rules forbid its members to work, even in the very unlikely event that they are offered a job. Geronimo, Théid, Frunnes, Sonny Boy, Abbes and Petz all agree to abide by the strict regulations of the club. In accordance with them, they manage to survive. But they can do it only on petty theft and other swindles, which is bound to end badly...
