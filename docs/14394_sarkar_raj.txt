Title: Sarkar Raj (2008)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Crime, Drama
Director: Ram Gopal Varma
Main Cast: Amitabh Bachchan, Abhishek Bachchan, Aishwarya Rai Bachchan
Keywords: corruption

Overview: The politically influential Shankar Nagre agrees to back a power plant scheme presented by corporate hotshot Anita Rajan; providing their enemies, which include upcoming political firebrands, an embezzling facilitator and shady businessmen with a unique opportunity to destroy the mass political clout enjoyed by him and his father (Subhash Nagre), the political icon known as "Sarkar".
