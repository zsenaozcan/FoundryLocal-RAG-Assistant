Title: Moontrap: Target Earth (2017)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction, Adventure
Director: Robert Dyke
Main Cast: Sarah Butler, Damon Dayoub, Charles Shaughnessy, Cara AnnMarie, Loretta Higgins
Keywords: nave espacial
