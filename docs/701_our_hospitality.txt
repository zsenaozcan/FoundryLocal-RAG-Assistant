Title: Our Hospitality (1923)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Buster Keaton
Main Cast: Buster Keaton, Joe Roberts, Natalie Talmadge, Ralph Bushman, Craig Ward
Keywords: in love with enemy, attempted murder, horse, kentucky, waterfall, rescue, family relationships, cross dressing, dog, family feud

Overview: A man returns to his Appalachian homestead. On the trip, he falls for a young woman. The only problem is her family has vowed to kill every member of his family.
