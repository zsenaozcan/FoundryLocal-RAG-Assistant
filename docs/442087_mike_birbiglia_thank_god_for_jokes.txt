Title: Mike Birbiglia: Thank God for Jokes (2017)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mike Birbiglia
Main Cast: Mike Birbiglia, Jimmy Kimmel
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Mike Birbiglia declares that a joke should never end with "I’m joking." In his all-new comedy, Birbiglia tiptoes hilariously through the minefield that is modern-day joke-telling. Join Mike as he learns that the same jokes that elicit laughter have the power to produce tears, rage, and a whole lot of getting yelled at. Ultimately it's a show that asks, “How far should we go for the laugh?”
