Title: The Gamers: Natural One (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Matt Vancil
Main Cast: Scott C. Brown, Christian Doyle, Monica Zelak, Daniel Johnston, Thomas M. Gofton
Keywords: cyberpunk, rpg

Overview: Natural One begins with a summons to Gary from his Canadian half-sister, Monica. She's getting married and family tradition calls for her future partner to be vetted in-game. Is he gamer enough to be worthy of the family name?
