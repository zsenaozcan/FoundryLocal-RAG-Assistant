Title: Gamera vs. Barugon (1966)
Genres: Fantasy, Action, Horror, Science Fiction, Adventure
Director: Shigeo Tanaka
Main Cast: Kojiro Hongo, Kyôko Enami, Yuzo Hayakawa, Takuya Fujioka, Kôji Fujiyama
Keywords: japan, helicopter, monster, turtle, sequel, dam, giant monster, kaiju, gamera, giant turtle

Overview: After a treacherous expedition to retrieve a giant opal, disaster strikes as the opal reveals itself to be an egg which spawns Barugon, demon dog from Hell! Armed with a deadly tongue and cold beams, Barugon wreaks havoc on Japan. Gamera comes to save the day.
