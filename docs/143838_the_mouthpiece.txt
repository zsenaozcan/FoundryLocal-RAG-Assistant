Title: The Mouthpiece (1932)
Genres: Drama
Director: James Flood
Main Cast: Warren William, Sidney Fox, Aline MacMahon, John Wray, Mae Madison
Keywords: underworld, false accusations

Overview: When a hot young prosecutor learns that a man he got convicted and executed was in fact innocent, he quits his DA job and becomes a defense attorney. He grows rich and powerful defending guilty racketeers, but eventually sees the errors of his ways.
