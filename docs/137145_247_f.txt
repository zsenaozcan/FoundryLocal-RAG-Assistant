Title: 247°F (2011)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Levan Bakhia
Main Cast: Scout Taylor-Compton, Travis Van Winkle, Michael Copon, Christina Ulloa, Tyler Mane
Keywords: sauna, heat, steam room, locked up

Overview: Four friends travel to a lakeside cabin for a carefree weekend, the fun turns into a nightmare when 3 of them end up locked in a hot sauna. Every minute counts and every degree matters as they fight for their lives in the heat up to 247°F.
