Title: PT 109 (1963)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Leslie H. Martinson
Main Cast: Cliff Robertson, Ty Hardin, James Gregory, Robert Culp, Grant Williams
Keywords: john f. kennedy, pt boat

Overview: Dramatization of President John F. Kennedy's war time experiences during which he captained a PT boat, took it to battle and had it sunk by a Japanese destroyer. He and the survivors had to make their way to an island, find food and shelter and signal the Navy for rescue.
