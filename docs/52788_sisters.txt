Title: Sisters (2006)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Horror
Director: Douglas Buck
Main Cast: Chloë Sevigny, Stephen Rea, Lou Doillon, Dallas Roberts, JR Bourne

Overview: A reporter witnesses a brutal murder, and becomes entangled in a mystery involving a pair of Siamese twins who were separated at birth, one of them forced to live under the eye of a watchful, controlling psychiatrist.
