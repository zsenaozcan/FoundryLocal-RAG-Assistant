Title: Caravaggio (1986)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Derek Jarman
Main Cast: Nigel Terry, Tilda Swinton, Sean Bean, Spencer Leigh, Robbie Coltrane
Keywords: italy, painter, artist, biography, homosexuality, independent film, gay interest, lgbt

Overview: As influential Italian artist Caravaggio dies in exile in 1610, he recalls his short life, from his childhood to his initial artistic failures to his later triumphs as he catches the eye of a sympathetic cardinal to his destructive relationship with a dashing gambler.
