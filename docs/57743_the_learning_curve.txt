Title: The Learning Curve (2001)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Eric Schwab
Main Cast: Carmine Giovinazzo, Monet Mazur, Vincent Ventresca, Steven Bauer, Majandra Delfino

Overview: Paul and Georgia are lovers, soul mates...and partners in crime. But when this duplicitous duo tries to dupe the wrong man, they are ensnared in a world more dangerous than they could ever have imagined. Seduced into working for him on dangerous jobs beyond their small-time capability, Paul and Georgia suddenly have everything they've ever wanted...and even more to lose.
