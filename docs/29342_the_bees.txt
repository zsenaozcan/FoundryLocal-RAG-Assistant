Title: The Bees (1978)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Alfredo Zacarías
Main Cast: John Saxon, Angel Tompkins, John Carradine, Claudio Brook, Alicia Encinas
Keywords: laboratory, killer bees

Overview: Corporate smuggling of South American killer bees into the United States results in huge swarms terrorizing the northern hemisphere. A small team of scientists work desperately to destroy the threat, but the bees soon mutate into a super-intelligent species that threatens the world.
