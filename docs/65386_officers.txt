Title: Officers (1971)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Vladimir Rogovoy
Main Cast: Georgiy Yumatov, Alina Pokrovskaya, Vasili Lanovoy, Natalya Rychagova, Aleksandr Voevodin

Overview: Two friends are army officers, and the turbulent times throw them from the Civil War in the early 20s to the Soviet conflict in Finland, from China to the Urals.
