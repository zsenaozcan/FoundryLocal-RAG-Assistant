Title: A Story Written with Water (1965)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yoshishige Yoshida
Main Cast: Mariko Okada, Ruriko Asaoka, Yasunori Irikawa, Shin Kishida, Isao Yamagata
Keywords: japanese new wave

Overview: Struggling with his true emotions, dreams, memories of the past and the reality Shizuo is about to marry, but is torn between his wife-to-be and the love to his mother.
