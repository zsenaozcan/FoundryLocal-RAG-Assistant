Title: The Lost World of Communism (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Lucy Hetherington
Main Cast: Hagen Koch, Ursula Rumin, Karel Fiala, Michael Pennington, Václav Havel
Keywords: communism

Overview: 1989 marked the collapse of communism in Eastern and Central Europe and an end to a way of life for millions of people. Having lived through extraordinary times, they tell their stories of life behind the Iron Curtain, looking beyond the headlines of spies and surveillance, secret police and political repression, to reveal a rich tapestry of experience.
