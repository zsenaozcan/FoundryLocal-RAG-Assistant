Title: All Over the Guy (2001)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Julie Davis
Main Cast: Dan Bucatinsky, Richard Ruccolo, Doris Roberts, Sasha Alexander, Adam Goldberg
Keywords: love, friends, homosexuality, independent film, woman director

Overview: A contemporary romantic comedy exploring the unlikely pairing of two 20-somethings thrown together by their respective best friends in hopes of igniting their own romance.
