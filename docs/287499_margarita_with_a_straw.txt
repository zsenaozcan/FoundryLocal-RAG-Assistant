Title: Margarita, with a Straw (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Shonali Bose
Main Cast: Kalki Koechlin, Revathi, Sayani Gupta, William Moseley

Overview: In this inspirational love story, a Delhi university student and aspiring writer afflicted with cerebral palsy leaves India for New York University, where she falls for a fiery young activist.
