Title: The Desert Trail (1935)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Lewis D. Collins
Main Cast: John Wayne, Mary Kornman, Paul Fix, Eddy Chandler, Carmen Laroux
Keywords: frontier justice, rodeo cowboy

Overview: Rodeo star John Scott and his gambler friend Kansas Charlie are wrongly accused of armed robbery. They leave town as fast as they can to go looking for their own suspects in Poker City.
