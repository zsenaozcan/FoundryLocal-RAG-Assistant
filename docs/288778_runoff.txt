Title: Runoff (2015)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Kimberly Levin
Main Cast: Joanne Kelly, Neal Huff, Alex Shaffer, Tom Bower, Joseph Melendez
Keywords: secret, farm, crop duster, tension, female protagonist, ethical dilemma, woman director

Overview: The beauty of the land cannot mask the brutality of a farm town. As harvest draws near, Betty confronts a terrifying new reality and will go to desperate lengths to save her family when they are threatened with being forced from their land.
