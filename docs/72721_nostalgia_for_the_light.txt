Title: Nostalgia for the Light (2010)
Genres: Drama, Documentary, Foreign
Director: Patricio Guzmán
Main Cast: Gaspar Galaz, Lautaro Núñez, Luís Henríquez, Miguel, Victor Gonzalez

Overview: In Chile's Atacama Desert, astronomers peer deep into the cosmos in search for answers concerning the origins of life. Nearby, a group of women sift through the sand searching for body parts of loved ones, dumped unceremoniously by Pinochet's regime.
