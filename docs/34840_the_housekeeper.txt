Title: The Housekeeper (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Claude Berri
Main Cast: Jean-Pierre Bacri, Brigitte Catillon, Jacques Frantz, Émilie Dequenne, Axelle Abbadie

Overview: After his wife leaves him for another man, Jacques hires a housekeeper, Laura, to keep his Paris apartment in order. As he starts increasing her hours and spending more time with her on her days off, Jacques is torn between the pleasure of Laura's company, and the headache that such an intrusion brings to his new domain of singlehood.
