Title: To Grandmother's House We Go (1992)
Genres: Family, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Jeff Franklin
Main Cast: Ashley Olsen, Mary-Kate Olsen, Rhea Perlman, Cynthia Geary, John Edward Peck

Overview: When the twins feel like their mom is tired of them, they take off on an exciting adventure to grandmother's house and encounter a pair of villains along the way.
