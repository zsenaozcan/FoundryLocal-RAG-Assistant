Title: The Double Man (1967)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: Franklin J. Schaffner
Main Cast: Yul Brynner, Britt Ekland, Clive Revill, Anton Diffring, Moira Lister
Keywords: spy, espionage, skiing, double

Overview: In a complex piece of espionage the Russian secret service attempts to kidnap a high ranking officer in the CIA and replace him with a double of its own.
