Title: Dreamer: Inspired By a True Story (2005)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: John Gatins
Main Cast: Kurt Russell, Dakota Fanning, Kris Kristofferson, Elisabeth Shue, David Morse
Keywords: horse race, horse, kentucky, sport, based on true story, family relationships, father daughter relationship

Overview: Ben Crane believes that a severely injured racehorse deserves another chance. He and his daughter Cale adopt the horse (in fact is a mare)and save it of being sacrificed by the owner.
