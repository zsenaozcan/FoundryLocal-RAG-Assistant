Title: One Hundred Steps (2000)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Marco Tullio Giordana
Main Cast: Luigi Lo Cascio, Paolo Briguglia, Luigi Maria Burruano, Tony Sperandeo, Lucia Sardo
Keywords: biography

Overview: The story of Peppino Impastato, a political activist who opposed the Mafia in Sicily.  One hundred steps was the number of steps it took to get from the Impastato house to the house of the Mafia boss Tano Badalamenti.
