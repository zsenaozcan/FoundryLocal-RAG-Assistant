Title: White Cannibal Queen (1980)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: Jesús Franco
Main Cast: Al Cliver, Sabrina Siani, Shirley Knight, Lina Romay, Olivier Mathot
Keywords: daughter, goddess, jungle, explorer

Overview: A man is on safari in the jungle with his wife and daughter when the wife gets eaten and daughter's captured by cannibals. Several years later he goes back to see if his daughter is still alive.
