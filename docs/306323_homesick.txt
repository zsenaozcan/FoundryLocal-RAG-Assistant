Title: Homesick (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Petri Kotwica
Main Cast: Julius Lavonen, Tarja Heinula, Janne Virtanen
Keywords: nurse, mental breakdown, mental institution, doctor

Overview: 17-year-old Sami has an accident which resulted in him reluctantly sent to closed psychiatric hospital. Sami refuses to speak or show his feelings, even when the nurse Taneli is trying everything in order to understand how the crash really happened. While Sami's and his roommate's Rude's eccentric friendships gradually increases, the truth of past events begin to unravel.
