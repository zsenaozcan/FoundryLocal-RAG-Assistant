Title: Breaking and Entering (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Benjamin Fingerhut
Main Cast: Michal Kapral, George Hood
Keywords: competition, documentary, world record

Overview: The Guinness World of Records contains the most bizarre human achievements imaginable. But who are the crazy people behind the feats? From the grape catchers, to the hula hoopers and the ‘jogglers’, this humorous, but strikingly insightful documentary introduces us to the colourful characters who devote their lives to the oddest frontiers of human excellence. A delightfully eccentric human odyssey that is not to be missed.
