Title: App (2013)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Bobby Boermans
Main Cast: Hannah Hoekstra, Isis Cabolet, Robert de Hoog, Jeroen Spitzenberger, Matthijs van de Sande Bakhuyzen

Overview: A young psychology student is drawn into the dark and fearful world of a diabolic and mysterious App that starts to terrorize her, distributing compromising photographs, videos and text messages about herself and delves deeper and deeper into her personal life, flawlessly exposing all of her deepest secrets.
