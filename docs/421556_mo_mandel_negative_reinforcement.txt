Title: Mo Mandel: Negative Reinforcement (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Steven Feinartz
Main Cast: Mo Mandel
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Mo Mandel takes the stage in his first hour-long special at the Gothic Theater in Denver. He loves Julio Iglesias, hates positive people, and aims his frenetic laser at everything from the sins of his parents, to the perils of sex, to the dark nature of his own disastrous mind.
