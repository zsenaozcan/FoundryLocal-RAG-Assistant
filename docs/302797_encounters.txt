Title: Encounters (2015)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror, Mystery
Director: Anders Johannes Bukh
Main Cast: Kristian Fjord, Jacob Wagner Guldager, Casper Sloth, Signe Mathilde Sørensen, Claus Vedel

Overview: November 18th 2011 a small Danish film crew disappeared in the woods of northern Sweden without a trace. One year later, their footage was leaked by a hacker group now known as Pandoras. This is the crew's unsettling and disturbing footage.
