Title: The Quickie (2001)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Sergei Bodrov
Main Cast: Brenda Bakke, Jennifer Jason Leigh, Sergei Bodrov Jr., Dean Stockwell, Yevgeni Lazarev
Keywords: russian mafia

Overview: A crime soap opera about a Russian mobster retiring from "the business" on New Year's Eve, only to discover he has been targeted for death by a rival mobster.
