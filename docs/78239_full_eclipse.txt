Title: Full Eclipse (1993)
Genres: Horror, Action, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Anthony Hickox
Main Cast: Mario Van Peebles, Patsy Kensit, Bruce Payne, Tony Denison, Jason Beghe
Keywords: made for cable tv, shot in the back, werewolf, drive by shooting

Overview: The LA police department have a special team of officers with a talent for reducing big-time crime. The team leader has an excellent track record for crime reduction in other big cities, but his methods are unconventional, and so is he - he's a werewolf.
