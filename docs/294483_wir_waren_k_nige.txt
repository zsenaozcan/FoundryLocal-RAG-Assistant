Title: Wir waren Könige (2014)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Thriller
Director: Philipp Leinemann
Main Cast: Ronald Zehrfeld, Frederick Lau, Hendrik Duryn, Mišel Matičević, Torben Liebrecht

Overview: Violent youth gangs and a police force way out of its depth. When a police operation goes awry and two policemen die, the powder keg threatens to ignite as the SWAT team knows only one goal: revenge - irrespective of the law.
