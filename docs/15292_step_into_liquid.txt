Title: Step Into Liquid (2003)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Dana Brown
Main Cast: Robert August, Rochelle Ballard, Shawn Barron, Layne Beachley, Bob Beaton
Keywords: surfer, surfing, sport, independent film

Overview: No special effects. No stuntmen. No stereotypes. No other feeling comes close. Surfers and secret spots from around the world are profiled in this documentary.
