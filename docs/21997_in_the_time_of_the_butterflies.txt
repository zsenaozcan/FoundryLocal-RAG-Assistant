Title: In the Time of the Butterflies (2001)
Genres: TV Movie, Thriller, Drama, History, Romance
Director: Mariano Barroso
Main Cast: Salma Hayek, Edward James Olmos, Mía Maestro, Demián Bichir, Pilar Padilla
Keywords: government, dictatorship, butterfly, dominican republic

Overview: In the Time of the Butterflies is inspired by the true story of the three Mirabal sisters who, in 1960, were murdered for their part in an underground plot to overthrow the government.
