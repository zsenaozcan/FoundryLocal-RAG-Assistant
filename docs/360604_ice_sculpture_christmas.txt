Title: Ice Sculpture Christmas (2015)
Genres: Romance, TV Movie
Director: David MacKay
Main Cast: Rachel Boston, David Alpay, Brenda Strong, Paul McGillion, Aliyah O'Brien
Keywords: competition, father daughter relationship, childhood friends, country club, ice sculpture, reunited friends, christmas, business rivalry

Overview: After starting her first job at a country club restaurant, Callie’s passions for cooking and ice sculpting are met with romance and Christmas spirit when a childhood friend enters her into the club’s annual Christmas ice sculpting competition without her knowledge.
