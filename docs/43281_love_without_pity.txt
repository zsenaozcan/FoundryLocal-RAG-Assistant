Title: Love Without Pity (1989)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Eric Rochant
Main Cast: Hippolyte Girardot, Mireille Perrier, Yvan Attal, Jean-Marie Rollin, Cécile Mazan

Overview: 25 year-old Hippo doesn't have a job, doesn't study either but lives from the money his younger brother earns with dealing and from occasional Poker winnings.
