Title: Business as Usual (1987)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lezli-An Barrett
Main Cast: Glenda Jackson, John Thaw, Cathy Tyson, Mark McGann, Eamon Boland

Overview: After seeing her husband fail in fighting a battle to keep his factory open, a manageress loses her job in a disagreement with the manager over sexual harassment of her staff. She accepts the advice of her father and joins his son, a left-wing organizer, and takes her plight to the union.
