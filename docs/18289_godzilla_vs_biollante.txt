Title: Godzilla vs. Biollante (1989)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Kazuki Omori
Main Cast: Kunihiko Mitamura, Yoshiko Tanaka, Masanobu Takashima, Kōji Takahashi, Tôru Minegishi
Keywords: biotechnology, godzilla, kaiju, biollante

Overview: Dr. Shiragami combines the DNA of roses with some extracted from Godzilla's cells. He names his creation "Biollante".
