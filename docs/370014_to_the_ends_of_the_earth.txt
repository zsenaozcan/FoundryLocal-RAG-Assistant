Title: To the Ends of the Earth (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Benedict Cumberbatch, Jared Harris, Jamie Sives, Richard McCabe, Victoria Hamilton
Keywords: miniseries

Overview: From Nobel Laureate William Golding's (Lord of the Flies) epic sea-voyage trilogy comes the story of an ambitious British aristocrat, humbled by the lives of his fellow passengers, as he embarks on an ocean voyage for Australia where he is to be an official in the colonial government.
