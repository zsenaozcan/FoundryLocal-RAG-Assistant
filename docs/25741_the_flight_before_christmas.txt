Title: The Flight Before Christmas (2008)
Genres: Animation, Family, Adventure, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Michael Henger
Main Cast: Olli Jantunen, Hannu-Pekka Björkman, Vuokko Hovatta, Vesa Vierikko, Jussi Lampi
Keywords: holiday, christmas

Overview: A young reindeer who suffers from vertigo learns to overcome his fear, takes flying lessons from a clumsy flying squirrel and heads to the North pole to save a troubled Santa and his fleet of flying reindeer.
