Title: The Girl from the Song (2017)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance
Director: Ibai Abad
Main Cast: Lewis Rainer, Joséphine Berry, Charlotte Atkinson, Charlie MacGechan, Rory Nolan
Keywords: music, love, romance, burning man

Overview: A young musician travels to Burning Man, a psychedelic festival in the middle of the Nevada desert, in an attempt to get the impetuous girl he has fallen in love with.
