Title: Lola Montès (1955)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Max Ophüls
Main Cast: Martine Carol, Peter Ustinov, Henri Guisol, Lise Delamare, Oskar Werner
Keywords: biography

Overview: The film tells the tragical story of the life of Lola Montes who was a great adventurer and stopped being the attraction of her circus after having been the lover of various important men.
