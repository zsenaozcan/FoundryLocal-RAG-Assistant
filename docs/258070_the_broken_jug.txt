Title: The Broken Jug (1937)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Gustav Ucicky
Main Cast: Emil Jannings, Friedrich Kayssler, Max Gülstorff, Lina Carstens, Angela Salloker
Keywords: judge, german, suspect, village

Overview: The man who broke the jug, the judge, is trying a case who determine who broke the jug. Long before the evidence becomes conclusive against the suspects, it becomes apparent that the blustering and bullying - and naive - village judge is the guilty one.
