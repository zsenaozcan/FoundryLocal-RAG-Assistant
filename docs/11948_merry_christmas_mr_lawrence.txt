Title: Merry Christmas, Mr. Lawrence (1983)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Nagisa Ōshima
Main Cast: David Bowie, Tom Conti, Ryuichi Sakamoto, Takeshi Kitano, Jack Thompson
Keywords: japan, samurai, world war ii, prisoners of war, soldier

Overview: In 1942, British soldier Jack Celliers comes to a japanese prison camp. The camp is run by Yonoi, who has a firm belief in discipline, honour and glory. In his view, the allied prisoners are cowards when they chose to surrender instead of commiting suicide. One of the prisoners, interpreter John Lawrence, tries to explain the japanese way of thinking, but is considered a traitor.
