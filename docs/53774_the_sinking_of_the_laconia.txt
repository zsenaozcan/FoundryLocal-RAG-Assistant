Title: The Sinking of the Laconia (2011)
Genres: Drama, History, War
Director: Uwe Janson
Main Cast: Andrew Buchan, Ken Duken, Franka Potente, Thomas Kretschmann, Brian Cox
Keywords: u boat, 1940s, weltkrieg, untergang

Overview: Two-part drama based on the true story of the Allied ship Laconia, sunk in WWII by a German U-Boat, which then surfaced against orders to rescue the civilian crew
