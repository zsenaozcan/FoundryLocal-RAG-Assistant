Title: Othello (1995)
Genres: Drama
Director: Oliver Parker
Main Cast: Laurence Fishburne, Irène Jacob, Kenneth Branagh, Nathaniel Parker, Indra Ové
Keywords: shakespeare, jealousy, friends, moor
