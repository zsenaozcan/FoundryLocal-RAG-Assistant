Title: The Italian (2005)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Andrey Kravchuk
Main Cast: Kolya Spiridonov, Mariya Kuznetsova, Nikolay Reutov, Jurij Ickov, Denis Moiseenko
Keywords: italian, russian, boy, orphanage, little boy, independent film

Overview: Set in 2002, an abandoned 5-year-old boy living in a rundown orphanage in a small Russian village is adopted by an Italian family.
