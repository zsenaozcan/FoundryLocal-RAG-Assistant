Title: Week-End at the Waldorf (1945)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Robert Z. Leonard
Main Cast: Ginger Rogers, Lana Turner, Walter Pidgeon, Van Johnson, Edward Arnold
Keywords: hotel, musical

Overview: Anything can happen during a weekend at New York's Waldorf-Astoria: a glamorous movie star meets a world-weary war correspondent and mistakes him for a jewel thief; a soldier learns that without an operation he'll die and so looks for one last romance with a beautiful but ambitious stenographer; a cub reporter tries to get the goods on a shady man's dealing with a foreign potentate. And it all happens in the opulent, grandiose New York landmark hotel as a sort of tongue-in-cheek take-off on the classic movie Grand Hotel.
