Title: Leila (1997)
Genres: Family, Drama, Romance
Director: Dariush Mehrjui
Main Cast: Leila Hatami, Ali Mosaffa, Jamileh Sheikhi, Turan Mehrzad, Amir Pievar
Keywords: infertility

Overview: This film tells the story of a semi modern Iranian couple, who are trying to fight the old beliefs & old generation. This is a failing battle because the man is not strong enough.
