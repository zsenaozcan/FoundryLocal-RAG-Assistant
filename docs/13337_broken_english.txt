Title: Broken English (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Zoe R. Cassavetes
Main Cast: Parker Posey, Drea de Matteo, Tim Guinee, Gena Rowlands, Melvil Poupaud
Keywords: career woman, single, independent film, woman director

Overview: Nora Wilder (Parker Posey), a single, career woman works at a Manhattan boutique hotel where her excellent skills in guest relations lack in the romantic department. If it is not her loving and dominant mother (Gena Rowlands) attempting to set her up that consistently fail, she has her friend’s (Drea de Matteo) disastrous blind dates to rely on as a backup for further dismay. She's surrounded by friends who are all happily engaged or romantically involved and somehow, love escapes Nora -- until she meets an unusual Frenchman (Melvil Poupaud) who helps her discover life beyond her self-imposed boundaries.
