Title: The Grump (2014)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Dome Karukoski
Main Cast: Antti Litja, Mari Perankoski, Petra Frey, Ilkka Forss, Viktor Drevitski
Keywords: aging, family

Overview: A stubbornly traditional eighty-year-old farmer — whose social attitudes verge on the prehistoric — raises hell when he is forced to move in with his sadsack, city-dwelling son and domineering daughter-in-law, in this hilarious social satire based on the wildly popular novel by Finnish author Tuomas Kyrö.
