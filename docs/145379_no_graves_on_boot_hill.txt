Title: No Graves on Boot Hill (1968)
Genres: Western
Director: Sergio Garrone
Main Cast: Craig Hill, Ida Galli, Giovanni Cianfriglia, Pietro Tordi, Mariangela Giordano
Keywords: spaghetti western

Overview: Three men, Jerry (Hill), Reno (Wood) and Paco (White), meet in the same prison and in their cell they find a Mexican boy condemned to death for murder. One night the three convicts are mysteriously released by a priest and the father of the Mexican boy who promises them a reward of $30,000 if they can find the real murderer within ten days. Certain that the Mexican boy is not guilty, Reno and the others organize a plan which leads to the unmasking of the real killers.
