Title: Stage Struck (1936)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Busby Berkeley
Main Cast: Dick Powell, Joan Blondell, Warren William, Frank McHugh, Spring Byington
Keywords: dancer, musical

Overview: Choreographer George Randall (Dick Powell) is stuck with staging a Broadway show starring Peggy Revere (Joan Blondell), a wealthy but untalented performer who is starring only because she is backing the production.
