Title: Highway Dragnet (1954)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Nathan H. Juran
Main Cast: Richard Conte, Joan Bennett, Wanda Hendrix, Reed Hadley, Mary Beth Hughes
Keywords: suspense, b movie

Overview: An ex-Marine on the lam from a murder charge. He hitches a ride from glamor-magazine photographer, who is travelling cross-country with her principal model. Tensions rise when the woman realize the man with them may be a killer.
