Title: The Drunkard (1950)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yorgos Javellas
Main Cast: Orestis Makris, Dimitris Horn, Billy Konstadopoulou, Athanasia Moustaka, Nikos Vlachopoulos
Keywords: wine, cobbler

Overview: After losing his son in WW2, a poor cobbler has become a drunkard and laughing stock of the whole neighborhood, besetting his daughter. When his daughter falls in love with a rich young man, he tries in vain to hide his passion from the young man's family...
