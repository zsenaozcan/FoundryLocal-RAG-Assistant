Title: El barrendero (1982)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Miguel M. Delgado
Main Cast: Cantinflas, María Sorté, Ursula Prats, Eduardo Alcaraz, Federico González
Keywords: robbery, police, thief, dog, street, paint, sweeper
