Title: Appointment with Danger (1951)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Lewis Allen
Main Cast: Alan Ladd, Phyllis Calvert, Paul Stewart, Jan Sterling, Jack Webb
Keywords: indiana, postal inspector, film noir

Overview: Al Goddard (Alan Ladd), a detective who works for the United States Postal Inspection Service, is assigned to arrest two criminals who've allegedly murdered a U.S. postal detective.
