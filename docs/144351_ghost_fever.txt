Title: Ghost Fever (1987)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Alan Smithee
Main Cast: Sherman Hemsley, Luis Ávalos, Jennifer Rhodes, Deborah Benson, Diana Brookes
Keywords: family business 

Overview: Two policeman are sent on a routine assignment to serve an eviction notice. It becomes anything but run-of-the-mill when they become involved in the ghostly happenings.
