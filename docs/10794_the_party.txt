Title: The Party (1968)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Blake Edwards
Main Cast: Peter Sellers, Claudine Longet, Natalia Borisova, Jean Carson, Marge Champion
Keywords: film producer, party

Overview: Hrundi V. Bakshi, an accident-prone actor from India, is accidentally put the name on the guest list for an upcoming party at the home of a Hollywood film director. Unfortunately, from the moment he arrives, one thing after another goes wrong with a compounding affect.
