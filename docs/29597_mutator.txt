Title: Mutator (1989)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: John R. Bowey
Main Cast: Brion James, Carolyn Ann Clark, Milton Raphael Murill, Brian O'Shaughnessy, Embeth Davidtz
Keywords: scientist

Overview: A beast gets loose in a laboratory, killing all in it's way. A former scientist, now working as a custodian tries to stop it.
