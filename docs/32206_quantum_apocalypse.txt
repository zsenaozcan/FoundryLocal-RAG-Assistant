Title: Quantum Apocalypse (2010)
Genres: TV Movie, Science Fiction
Director: Justin Jones
Main Cast: Rhett Giles, Stephanie Jacobsen, Gigi Edgley, Kristen Quintrall, Peter Jurasik
Keywords: doomsday

Overview: A group of talented but rebellious 'rock-star scientists' find themselves in a race against time to save Earth when a comet makes an unexpected turn towards our blue planet where all life may cease to exist within days if our small town heroes fail to find a solution.
