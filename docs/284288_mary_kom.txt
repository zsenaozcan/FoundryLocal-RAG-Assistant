Title: Mary Kom (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Omung Kumar
Main Cast: Priyanka Chopra, Darshan Kumaar, Shishir Sharma, Sunil Thapa, Robin Das
Keywords: biography, sporting event, boxing, women boxer

Overview: A chronicle of the life of Indian boxer 'Mary Kom' who went through several hardships before audaciously accomplishing her ultimate dream.
