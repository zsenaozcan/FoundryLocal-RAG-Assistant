Title: Red Scorpion (1988)
Genres: Action, History, War
Director: Joseph Zito
Main Cast: Dolph Lundgren, M. Emmet Walsh, Al White, T. P. McKenna, Carmen Argenziano
Keywords: traitor, cold war, russian, mission of murder, war, uprising, revenge, agent, spetsnaz

Overview: A Russian KGB agent is sent to Africa to kill an anti-Communist black revolutionary. However, he has a change of heart when he sees how the Russians and their Cuban allies are killing and repressing the locals, so he switches sides and helps the rebels.
