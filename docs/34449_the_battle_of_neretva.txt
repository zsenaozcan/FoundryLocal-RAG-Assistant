Title: The Battle of Neretva (1969)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Veljko Bulajić
Main Cast: Orson Welles, Yul Brynner, Curd Jürgens, Hardy Krüger, Anthony Dawson
Keywords: world war ii, river battle, 1940s

Overview: In January 1943 the German army, afraid of an Allied invasion of the Balkans, launched a great offensive against Yugoslav Partisans in Western Bosnia. The only way out for the Partisan forces and thousands of refugees was the bridge on the river Neretva.
