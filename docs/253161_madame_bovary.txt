Title: Madame Bovary (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sophie Barthes
Main Cast: Mia Wasikowska, Ezra Miller, Laura Carmichael, Henry Lloyd-Hughes, Paul Giamatti
Keywords: period drama, social status, extramarital affair, woman director

Overview: The classic story of Emma Bovary, the beautiful wife of a small-town doctor in 19th century France, who engages in extra marital affairs in an attempt to advance her social status.
