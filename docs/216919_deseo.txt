Title: Deseo (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Antonio Zavala Kugler
Main Cast: Ari Borovoy, Lila Downs, Paulina Gaitán, Paola Nuñez, Edith González
Keywords: erotic drama

Overview: Eight short stories of seduction and illicit encounters between lovers, filled with humor and eroticism, which use a circular structure located in the cosmopolitan atmosphere of a colonial city of Mexico.
