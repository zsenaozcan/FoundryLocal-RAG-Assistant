Title: The Break-up Artist (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Steve Woo
Main Cast: Amanda Crew, Ryan Kennedy, Moneca Delain, Ali Liebert, Serinda Swan

Overview: A woman who gets paid to break people up is forced to become a matchmaker when some new competition muscles her out of the break-up business. Now, in order to save her company (and keep her high-priced wardrobe) she'll have to rely on her old nemesis: love.
