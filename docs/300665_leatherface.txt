Title: Leatherface (2017)
Genres: Horror
Director: Alexandre Bustillo
Main Cast: Stephen Dorff, Lili Taylor, Sam Strike, Vanessa Grasse, Finn Jones
Keywords: chainsaw

Overview: A young nurse is kidnapped by a group of violent teens who escape from a mental hospital and take her on the road trip from hell. Pursued by an equally deranged lawman out for revenge, one of these teens is destined for tragedy and horrors that will destroy his mind, molding him into the monster we now call Leatherface.
