Title: Into the Woods (1991)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: James Lapine
Main Cast: Bernadette Peters, Chip Zien, Joanna Gleason, Tom Aldredge, Kim Crosby
Keywords: fairy tale, musical, stage show, sondheim, broadway, broadway show, stage performance

Overview: A collection of fairy tale characters head into the woods, and soon learn that fairy tales don't end at "happily ever after." This rendition of Stephen Sondheim's Tony Award-winning musical was recorded on the stage with an all-star Broadway cast.
