Title: Tickling Giants (2017)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Sara Taksler
Main Cast: Bassem Youssef, Jon Stewart
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The Arab Spring in Egypt: From a dictator to free elections, back to a dictatorship. One comedy show united the country and tested the limits of free press. This is the story of Bassem Youssef, a cardiologist turned comedian, the Jon Stewart of Egypt, and his show "The Show".
