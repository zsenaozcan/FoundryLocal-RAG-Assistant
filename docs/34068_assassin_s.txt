Title: Assassin(s) (1997)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Mathieu Kassovitz
Main Cast: Michel Serrault, Mathieu Kassovitz, Mehdi Benoufa, Robert Gendreu, Danièle Lebrun

Overview: Old professional killer Wagner seeks someone to teach what he knows as long as he is already dying, and he chooses Max, young and passionless thief to be his successor.
