Title: As I Lay Dying (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: James Franco
Main Cast: James Franco, Danny McBride, Logan Marshall-Green, Ahna O'Reilly, Jim Parrack
Keywords: based on novel, rural setting, poverty, deep south, burial

Overview: Based on the 1930 classic by Faulkner, it is the story of the death of Addie Bundren and her family's quest to honor her wish to be buried in the nearby town of Jefferson.
