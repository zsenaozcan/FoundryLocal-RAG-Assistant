Title: Steal This Film II (2007)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jamie King
Main Cast: Gottfrid Svartholm, Fredrik Neij, Peter Sunde, Aaron Swartz
Keywords: revolution, arts and culture, freedom, piracy

Overview: These are strange times indeed. While they continue to command so much attention in the mainstream media, the 'battles' between old and new modes of distribution, between the pirate and the institution of copyright, seem to many of us already lost and won. We know who the victors are. Why then say any more?
