Title: The Grass Harp (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Charles Matthau
Main Cast: Piper Laurie, Sissy Spacek, Walter Matthau, Edward Furlong, Nell Carter
Keywords: loss of mother, independent film, coming of age, maid

Overview: Based on the novel by Truman Capote, this often-witty coming-of-age drama looks at a young man growing up with an unusual family in the Deep South in the 1940s.
