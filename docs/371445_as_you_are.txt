Title: As You Are (2016)
Genres: Mystery, Drama
Director: Miles Joris-Peyrafitte
Main Cast: Owen Campbell, Charlie Heaton, Amandla Stenberg, John Scurti, Scott Cohen

Overview: Set in the early 1990s, "As You Are" is the telling and retelling of a relationship between three teenagers as it traces the course of their friendship through a construction of disparate memories prompted by a police investigation.
