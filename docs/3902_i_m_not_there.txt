Title: I'm Not There. (2007)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Todd Haynes
Main Cast: Cate Blanchett, Heath Ledger, Julianne Moore, Ben Whishaw, Charlotte Gainsbourg
Keywords: rock and roll, music style, success, john f. kennedy, advancement, bob dylan, rock, biography, music, beatnik

Overview: Six actors portray six personas of music legend Bob Dylan in scenes depicting various stages of his life, chronicling his rise from unknown folksinger to international icon and revealing how Dylan constantly reinvented himself.
