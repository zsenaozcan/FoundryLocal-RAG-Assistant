Title: Cloudy With a Chance of Love (2015)
Genres: Romance
Director: Bradford May
Main Cast: Katie Leclerc, Stacey Dash, Gregory Harrison, Michael Rady, Ben Begley

Overview: Quirky meteorologist Deb spends most of her time finishing the passionate pursuit of her meteorology Ph.D. The wind of change starts blowing, however, when a handsome news director recruits her to fill in as an on-air weather personality.
