Title: Demon Hunter (2005)
Genres: Horror
Director: Scott Ziehl
Main Cast: William Bassett, Sean Patrick Flanery, Billy Drago, Colleen Porch, Tania Deighton
Keywords: lust, violence, succubus, nosebleed, half breed

Overview: After resolving a situation of exorcism, the half-breed demon hunter Jake Greyman (Sean Patrick Flanery) reports to Cardinal White (William Bassett) and they realize that the demon Asmodeus (Billy Drago) is impregnating many mortals trying to raise offspring. Cardinal White calls Sister Sarah Ryan (Colleen Porch) to assist Jake in his hunting for Asmodeus and his women.
