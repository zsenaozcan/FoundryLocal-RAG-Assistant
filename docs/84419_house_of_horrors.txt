Title: House of Horrors (1946)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Jean Yarbrough
Main Cast: Rondo Hatton, Robert Lowery, Virginia Grey, Bill Goodwin, Martin Kosleck
Keywords: revenge, murder, serial killer, new york city, sculptor, art critic, deformity

Overview: An unsuccessful sculptor saves a madman named "The Creeper" from drowning. Seeing an opportunity for revenge, he tricks the psycho into murdering his critics.
