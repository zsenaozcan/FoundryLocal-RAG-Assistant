Title: My Führer (2007)
Genres: War, Comedy, Drama
Director: Dani Levy
Main Cast: Helge Schneider, Ulrich Mühe, Sylvester Groth, Adriana Altaras, Stefan Kurt
Keywords: nazis, nazi germany

Overview: Hitler no longer believes in himself, and can barely see himself as an equal to even his sheep dog. But to seize the helm of the war he would have to create one of his famous fiery speeches to mobilize the masses. Goebbels therefore brings a Jewish acting teacher Grünbaum and his family from the camps in order to train the leader in rhetoric. Grünbaum is torn, but starts Hitler in his therapy ...
