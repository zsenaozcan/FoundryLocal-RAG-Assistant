Title: Choose Me (1984)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Alan Rudolph
Main Cast: Geneviève Bujold, Keith Carradine, Lesley Ann Warren, Patrick Bauchau, Rae Dawn Chong
Keywords: conversation, noir

Overview: Several lost-soul night-owls, including a nightclub owner, a talkback radio relationships counseller, and an itinerant stranger have encounters that expose their contradictions and anxieties about love and acceptance.
