Title: Diary of a Madman (1963)
Genres: Horror
Director: Reginald Le Borg
Main Cast: Vincent Price, Nancy Kovack, Chris Warfield, Elaine Devry, Ian Wolfe
Keywords: psychokinesis, sculptor

Overview: Simon Cordier, a French magistrate and amateur sculptor comes into contact with a malevolent entity. The invisible - yet corporeal - being, called a "horla" is capable of limited psychokinesis and complete mind control.
