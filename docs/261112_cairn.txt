Title: Cairn (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Hanne Larsen
Main Cast: Bjorge Bondevik, Magnar Gustavsen, Henrik Carlyle, Johan Munch-Ellingsen, Brynjar Andersen Saus
Keywords: coming of age

Overview: Cairn is a dark film on adolescence in which the main character Johan (11) faces a moral dilemma after having started playing a seemingly innocent prank. The story unfolds on a l level in the turbulent transition between childhood and adulthood.
