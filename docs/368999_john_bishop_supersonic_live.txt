Title: John Bishop Supersonic Live (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Paul Wheeler
Main Cast: John Bishop
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: John Bishop’s back! After taking time out to write his autobiography, John limbered up again for his third sell-out national Arena tour, ending with a special one-off show at London’s iconic Royal Albert Hall. This latest comedy caper by the immensely talented John Bishop was described as “the funniest two hours you’ll have anywhere, anytime soon” by The Daily Mirror.
