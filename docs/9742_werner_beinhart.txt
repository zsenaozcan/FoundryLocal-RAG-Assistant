Title: Werner - Beinhart! (1990)
Genres: Animation, Comedy
Director: Niki List
Main Cast: Klaus Büchner, Andi Feldmann, Rötger Feldmann, Meret Becker, I. Stangl
Keywords: plumbing supplier, beer, rock gang, comic-strip artist, motorcycle

Overview: Comic artist Brösel trades a magic pen that helps him come up with funny stories for the promise to fulfill one of Rumpelstiltskin's wishes. The resulting animated films show episodes of the life of Werner, a plumber apprentice and motorbike enthusiast and his friends. They are interspersed with the live-action portions.
