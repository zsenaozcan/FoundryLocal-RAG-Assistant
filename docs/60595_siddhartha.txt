Title: Siddhartha (1972)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: Conrad Rooks
Main Cast: Shashi Kapoor, Simi Garewal, Romesh Sharma, Zul Vellani, Amrik Singh

Overview: Siddhartha is the story of a young Brahmin and his search for a meaningful way of life. His journey takes him through periods of harsh asceticism, sensual pleasures, material wealth, self-revulsion and eventually to the oneness and harmony that he had been seeking. The story is based on the best-selling novel by German Nobel Prize winner Hermann Hesse.
