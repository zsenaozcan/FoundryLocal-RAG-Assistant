Title: Tabloid Truth (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: Kim Kwang-sik
Main Cast: Kim Kang-woo, Jung Jin-young, Ko Chang-seok, Park Sung-woong, Park Won-sang
Keywords: 찌라시, 위험한 소문, 찌라시 : 위험한 소문

Overview: Woo-gon, a passionate manager who lost everything due to stock advertisements and a specialist gets together to find out the truth behind the rumors that have shaken up the country. From precious information to distribution, this crime chasing movie surrounds the invisible truth.
