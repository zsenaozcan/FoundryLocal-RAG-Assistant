Title: Smashing Pumpkins: If All Goes Wrong (2008)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Jack Gulick
Main Cast: Billy Corgan, Lisa Harriton, Ginger Reyes, Jeff Schroeder, Jimmy Chamberlin
Keywords: tour, smashing pumpkins, music band

Overview: A feature-length documentary about The Smashing Pumpkins, which chronicles the band's residencies at The Orange Peel in Asheville and The Fillmore in San Francisco in summer 2007. The documentary covers Pumpkins frontman Billy Corgan's attempts to write and debut new music at the residencies, as well as the revival of the band with new members.
