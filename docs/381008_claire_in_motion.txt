Title: Claire in Motion (2017)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lisa Robinson
Main Cast: Betsy Brandt, Chris Beetem, Zev Haworth, Anna Margaret Hollyman, Sakina Jaffrey
Keywords: widow, grief, woman director

Overview: Claire is sure of herself, her work and family, until — like a bad dream — her husband disappears, leaving a trail of puzzling secrets that shatter her certainty.
