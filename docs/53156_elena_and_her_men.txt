Title: Elena and Her Men (1956)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Jean Renoir
Main Cast: Ingrid Bergman, Jean Marais, Mel Ferrer, Jean Richard, Juliette Gréco
Keywords: general, arranged marriage, romance, polish, gypsy girl, 19th century

Overview: Set amid the military maneuvers and Quatorze Juillet carnivals of turn-of-the-century France, Jean Renoir’s delirious romantic comedy Elena and her Men (Elena et les hommes) stars a radiant Ingrid Bergman as a beautiful, but impoverished, Polish princess who drives men of all stations to fits of desperate love. When Elena elicits the fascination of a famous general, she finds herself at the center of romantic machinations and political scheming, with the hearts of several men—as well as the future of France—in her hands.
