Title: Spring and Chaos (1996)
Genres: Animation, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Shoji Kawamori
Main Cast: Shirô Sano, Mariko Kouda, Chikao Ohtsuka, Victor Chin

Overview: Set in the beginning of the 20th century Japan, the film follows the bright and eccentric Kenji from his late student years through his adulthood. Kenji suffers the tragedy of being an artist who's art isn't recognized during his lifetime. Based on the life of the author Kenji Miyazawa, the film depicts his brief but intense existence.
