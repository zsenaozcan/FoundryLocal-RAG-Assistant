Title: The Trouble with Harry (1955)
Genres: Comedy, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Alfred Hitchcock
Main Cast: Edmund Gwenn, John Forsythe, Shirley MacLaine, Mildred Natwick, Mildred Dunnock
Keywords: small town, chase, wife, leave, murder, suspense, female corpse

Overview: Trouble erupts in a small, quiet New England town when a man's body is found in the woods. The problem is that almost everyone in town thinks that they had something to do with his death.
