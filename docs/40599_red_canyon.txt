Title: Red Canyon (2008)
Genres: Action, Horror, Thriller
Director: Giovanni Rodríguez
Main Cast: Christine Lakin, Tim Draxl, Katie Maguire, Norman Reedus, Noah Fleiss
Keywords: sheriff, cave, drug dealer, utah, marijuana, dog, held captive, bound and gagged, meth lab, small town sheriff

Overview: In Red Canyon, Regina and Devon return to their family home in the badlands of Utah to face the memory of a brutal attack - and put it behind them. But in coming home they awaken a killing rage in a town where everyone has ties that bind.
