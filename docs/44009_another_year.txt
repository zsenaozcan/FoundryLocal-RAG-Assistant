Title: Another Year (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Mike Leigh
Main Cast: Jim Broadbent, Lesley Manville, Ruth Sheen, Oliver Maltman, Peter Wight
Keywords: jealousy, cooking, garden, funeral, season, loneliness, unrequited love, red wine

Overview: Mike Leigh’s much praised 2010 tragicomical drama. During a year, a very content couple approaching retirement are visited by friends and family less happy with their lives.
