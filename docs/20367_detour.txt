Title: Detour (1945)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Edgar G. Ulmer
Main Cast: Tom Neal, Ann Savage, Claudia Drake, Edmund MacDonald, Tim Ryan
Keywords: travel, b movie, hitchhiker, classic noir, film noir, poverty row film

Overview: Al Roberts, a New York nightclub pianist, hitch-hikes to Hollywood to meet his girlfriend Sue. The gambler he's riding with, Charles, unexpectedly dies. Afraid the police wouldn't believe the truth, Al takes the man's identity. In a gas station, he gives a lift to Vera, a woman that knew Charles and blackmails Al with tragic consequences.
