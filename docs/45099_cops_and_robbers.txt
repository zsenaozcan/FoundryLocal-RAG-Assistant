Title: Cops and Robbers (1951)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Mario Monicelli
Main Cast: Totò, Ernesto Almirante, Pietro Carloni, Mario Castellani, Carlo Delle Piane
Keywords: public works, false pretenses

Overview: Esposito is a thief who cons tourists in Rome. A lengthy persecution by police Bottoni, who manages to catch it starts. In an oversight Esposito manages to flee again. Bottoni superiors inform him that if no catches him will lose his job.
