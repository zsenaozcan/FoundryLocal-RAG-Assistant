Title: Quackser Fortune Has a Cousin in the Bronx (1970)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Waris Hussein
Main Cast: Gene Wilder, Margot Kidder, Eileen Colgan, May Ollis, Tony Doyle

Overview: In Dublin, a working class family has been unsuccessful in convincing their son to get a real job: the son prefers his job of scooping up horse's dung and selling it for flower gardens. An American exchange student almost runs him over and gets to know him. The dung man has ignored warnings from his family and suddenly the horses have been banned from Dublin. His new love is leaving for America and he must find a way to cope with the new reality.
