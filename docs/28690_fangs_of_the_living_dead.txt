Title: Fangs of the Living Dead (1969)
Genres: Horror
Director: Amando de Ossorio
Main Cast: Anita Ekberg, Gianni Medici, Diana Lorys, Rosanna Yanni, César Benet
Keywords: vampire

Overview: A beautiful virgin inherits a castle, but when she arrives at it, she finds that the inhabitants include a strange nobleman and a bevy of beautiful women she suspects may be vampires.
