Title: 11-11-11 (2011)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Darren Lynn Bousman
Main Cast: Michael Landes, Timothy Gibbs, Wendy Glenn, Lolo Herrero, Salomé Jiménez
Keywords: brother brother relationship

Overview: After the death of his wife and child, an author travels to Barcelona to see his estranged brother and dying father, where he learns that his life is plagued by events that occur on 11/11/11.
