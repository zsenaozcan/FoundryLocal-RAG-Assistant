Title: Assa (1987)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Sergey Solovyov
Main Cast: Sergei Bugayev, Tatyana Drubich, Stanislav Govorukhin, Aleksandr Bashirov, Aleksandr Domogarov

Overview: ASSA is set in Crimea during the winter in the mid eighties. A young musician (Bananan) falls for mobster's (Krymov) young mistress (Alika). The parallel story line involves an 18th century assassination plot.
