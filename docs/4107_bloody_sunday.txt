Title: Bloody Sunday (2002)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, History
Director: Paul Greengrass
Main Cast: James Nesbitt, Allan Gildea, Gerard Crossan, Mary Moulds, Carmel McCallion
Keywords: northern ireland, independent film, civil rights

Overview: The dramatised story of the Irish civil rights protest march on January 30 1972 which ended in a massacre by British troops.
