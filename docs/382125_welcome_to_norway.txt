Title: Welcome to Norway (2016)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Rune Denstad Langlo
Main Cast: Henriette Steenstrup, Anders Baasmo Christiansen, Henrik Rafaelsen, Frode Saugestad, Nini Bakke Kristiansen
Keywords: racism, interracial friendship, sham marriage, refugee crisis

Overview: A couple decide to open a home for refuges in the remote cold mountains of Norway.
