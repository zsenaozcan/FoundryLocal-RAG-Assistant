Title: Europa Europa (1990)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Agnieszka Holland
Main Cast: Salomon Perel, Marco Hofschneider, Julie Delpy, René Hofschneider, Piotr Kozlowski
Keywords: russia, german, nazis, hitler youth, jewish, woman director, jewish boy

Overview: A Jewish boy separated from his family in the early days of WWII poses as a German orphan and is taken into the heart of the Nazi world as a 'war hero' and eventually becomes a Hitler Youth. Although improbabilities and happenstance are cornerstones of the film, it is based upon a true story.
