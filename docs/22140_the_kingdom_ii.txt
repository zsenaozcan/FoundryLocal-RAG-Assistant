Title: The Kingdom II (1997)
Genres: Horror, Mystery
Director: Lars von Trier
Main Cast: Ernst-Hugo Järegård, Udo Kier, Kirsten Rolffes, Holger Juul Hansen, Laura Christensen
Keywords: pflegen

Overview: The series is set in the neurosurgical ward of Copenhagen's Rigshospitalet, the city and country's main hospital, nicknamed "Riget". "Riget" means "the realm" or "the kingdom" and leads one to think of "dødsriget", the realm of the dead.
