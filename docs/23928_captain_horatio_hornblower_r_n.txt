Title: Captain Horatio Hornblower R.N. (1951)
Genres: War, Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: Gregory Peck, Virginia Mayo, Robert Beatty, James Robertson Justice, Terence Morgan
Keywords: captain, british, battle, napoleonic wars, sea battle

Overview: A naval adventure, based on the novels by C.S. Forester, about the heroic, 19th-century British seafarer. The story sails with his ship, the Lydia, through battles with Spain and then France won with wit rather than might.
