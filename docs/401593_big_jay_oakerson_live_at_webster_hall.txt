Title: Big Jay Oakerson: Live at Webster Hall (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jeff Tomsic
Main Cast: Big Jay Oakerson

Overview: From the legendary New York City music venue, the man The New York Times called “a master of the dirty joke” dishes on the taboos of growing up with step parents, how to navigate “the friend zone,” and why nobody should feel uncomfortable about cringe-worthy material at a comedy show.
