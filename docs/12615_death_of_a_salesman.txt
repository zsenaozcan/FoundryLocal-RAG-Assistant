Title: Death of a Salesman (1985)
Genres: Drama
Director: Volker Schlöndorff
Main Cast: Dustin Hoffman, Kate Reid, John Malkovich, Stephen Lang, Charles Durning
Keywords: father son relationship, profession, failure, extramarital affair, family conflict

Overview: Salesman Willy Loman is in a crisis. He's about to lose his job, he can't pay his bills, and his sons Biff and Happy don't respect him and can't seem to live up to their potential. He wonders what went wrong and how he can make things up to his family.
