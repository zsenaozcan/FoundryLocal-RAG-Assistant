Title: The Snow Woman (1968)
Genres: Horror, Fantasy
Director: Tokuzô Tanaka
Main Cast: Shiho Fujimura, Sen Hara, Machiko Hasegawa, Akira Ishihama, Yoshiro Kitahara
Keywords: witch, snow storm, snow, sculptor, kaidan

Overview: A master sculptor and his apprentice are trapped in a bad snow storm after finding a special tree for carving a statue for the local temple. Finding refuge in an abandoned hut they celebrate their luck in finding the tree but soon they are visited by the Snow Witch who freezes the sculptor to death but takes pity on the apprentice. He must promise to never speak of this or she'll return and kill him.
