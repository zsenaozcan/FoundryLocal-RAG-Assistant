Title: Top Star (2013)
Genres: Foreign, Drama
Director: Park Joong-hoon
Main Cast: Uhm Tae-woong, So Yi-hyun, Kim Min-joon, Kim Su-ro, Lee Jae-hyeok
Keywords: manager

Overview: Actor Park Joong-hoon makes his directorial debut with this tale of a talent manager, Taeshik, who is discovered as an actor himself and sees his success quickly surpass that of his prior client. But Taeshik is compelled to hold on to his stardom through any vile means necessary. He recalls Alain Delon’s Tom Ripley in Purple Noon.
