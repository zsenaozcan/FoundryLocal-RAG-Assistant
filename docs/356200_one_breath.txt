Title: One Breath (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Christian Zübert
Main Cast: Jördis Triebel, Chara Mata Giannatou, Benjamin Sadler, Apostolis Totsikas, Nike Maria Vassil
Keywords: nanny, pregnant

Overview: A nightmarish crisis occurs after a pregnant young Greek immigrant takes a job as a nanny to a young professional couple, in this stunning drama from Germany’s Christian Zübert.
