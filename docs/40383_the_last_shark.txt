Title: The Last Shark (1981)
Genres: Horror
Director: Enzo G. Castellari
Main Cast: James Franciscus, Vic Morrow, Micaela Pignatelli, Joshua Sinclair, Giancarlo Prete
Keywords: beach, exploitation, shark attack, surf, gore, scuba diving, b movie, massacre, shark, great white shark

Overview: An enormous and angry 35 foot Great White Shark takes revenge on humans when they build a beach just for swimmers by a coastal town. After several shark attacks, and the Mayor does nothing to stop it, James Franisscus and Vic Morrow sail in pursuit to stop it.
