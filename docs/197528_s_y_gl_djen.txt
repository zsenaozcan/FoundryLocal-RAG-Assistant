Title: s/y Glädjen (1989)
Genres: Drama
Director: Göran du Rées
Main Cast: Lena Olin, Stellan Skarsgård, Viveka Seldahl, Hans Mosesson, Gunvor Pontén

Overview: A few friends builds a sailing boat together. They plan trips and during the completion of the boat marriages crumble, new relations blossom and the boat burns. They rebuild it and the families involved continue to rock on the stormy sea of emotion.
