Title: Out of It (1978)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Gérard Oury
Main Cast: Pierre Richard, Victor Lanoux, Raymond Bussières, Claude Brosset, Jacques Frantz

Overview: The lawyer is visiting a prison to meet with a violent criminal who has been condemned to death. During the visit, things turn bad, there is a riot where prisoners escape and the criminal escapes taking a lawyer hostage.
