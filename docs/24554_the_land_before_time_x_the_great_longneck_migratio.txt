Title: The Land Before Time X: The Great Longneck Migration (2003)
Genres: Adventure, Animation, Family
Director: Charles Grosvenor
Main Cast: John Ingle, Alec Medlock, Kenneth Mars, Miriam Flynn, Aria Curzon
Keywords: surrealism, family relationships, dinosaur

Overview: A bedtime story leads Littlefoot and his grandparents on a journey to a new land, where Littlefoot discovers someone who vanished before he was born: his father! Now Littlefoot must decide between two worlds. Will he leave to be with his friends in the Great Valley, or stay behind and start a new life with his father?
