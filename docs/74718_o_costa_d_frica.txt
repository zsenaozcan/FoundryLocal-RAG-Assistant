Title: O Costa d'África (1954)
Genres: Comedy
Director: João Mendes
Main Cast: Vasco Santana, Laura Alves, Erico Braga, Teresa Gomes, Ana Paula Zeiger

Overview: Costa is travelling from Africa to London, and wishes very much to his nephew Amadeu, and see by himself how well he has been invested large sums of money he is been sending him. Amadeu 'borrows' for 24 hours a wife, a villa, a car, and a servant - for he has been lies to his uncle all the time. All goes well. But, Costa decides to prolong his stay for two weeks...
