Title: Nobody's Children (1951)
Genres: Drama
Director: Raffaello Matarazzo
Main Cast: Yvonne Sanson, Amedeo Nazzari, Françoise Rosay, Folco Lulli, Enrica Dyrell
Keywords: based on novel, love, mother superior, presumed dead, nunhood, catholic priest, quarry, social differences, based on opera, rich man - poor woman

Overview: The tragic love story between Guido, the owner of a marble quarry and Luisa, the humble daughter of one of his employees, ends up in her giving birth to a baby boy. Giulio's mother is against them: at first she takes her son abroad with an excuse and then has her grandson kidnapped making Luisa think the boy died in a fire.
