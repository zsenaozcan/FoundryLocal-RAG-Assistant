Title: Last Exit to Brooklyn (1989)
Genres: Drama
Director: Uli Edel
Main Cast: Stephen Lang, Jennifer Jason Leigh, Burt Young, Peter Dobson, Jerry Orbach
Keywords: new york, prostitute, sex, sexuality, based on novel, fight, nudity, union

Overview: A gritty Brooklyn story about union corruption, violence, taboos and social norms. The stories told are of the sailors, the prostitute, the union bosses, the hooligans and the gay community set in the early 50s.
