Title: King Lear (1987)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jean-Luc Godard
Main Cast: Woody Allen, Freddy Buache, Leos Carax, Julie Delpy, Jean-Luc Godard

Overview: A descendant of Shakespeare attemps to restore his plays in a world rebuilding itself after the Chernobyl catastrophe obliterates most of human civilization.
