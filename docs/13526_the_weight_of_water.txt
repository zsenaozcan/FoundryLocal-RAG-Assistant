Title: The Weight of Water (2000)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery
Director: Kathryn Bigelow
Main Cast: Sean Penn, Catherine McCormack, Elizabeth Hurley, Sarah Polley, Josh Lucas
Keywords: suspense, woman director

Overview: A newspaper photographer, Jean, researches the lurid and sensational axe murder of two women in 1873 as an editorial tie-in with a brutal modern double murder. She discovers a cache of papers that appear to give an account of the murders by an eyewitness.
