Title: Mojin: The Lost Legend (2015)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Wuershan
Main Cast: Chen Kun, Huang Bo, Shu Qi, AngelaBaby, Xia Yu
Keywords: love triangle, past, zombie, tomb, grave robbing, flashback, treasure hunter

Overview: At the beginning of the 1990s, famous tomb explorer Hu Bayi decided to retire and move to the United States with his girlfriend Shirley. But before his wedding, Bayi discovers his first love Ding Shitian, who supposedly had died in the "One Hundred Cave" 20 years ago, is actually still alive. Together with Shirley and his old exploration partner, Bayi unravels a terrible millennium-old secret...
