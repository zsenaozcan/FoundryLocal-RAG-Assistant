Title: Cake (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Daniel Barnz
Main Cast: Jennifer Aniston, Anna Kendrick, William H. Macy, Adriana Barraza, Felicity Huffman
Keywords: suicide, support group, depression, widower, grieving, chronic pain
