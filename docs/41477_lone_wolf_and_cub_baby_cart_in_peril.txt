Title: Lone Wolf and Cub: Baby Cart in Peril (1972)
Genres: Action, Foreign
Director: Buichi Saito
Main Cast: Tomisaburō Wakayama, Yoichi Hayashi, Michie Azuma, Akihiro Tomikawa, Tatsuo Endô
Keywords: japan, assassin, samurai

Overview: In the fourth film of the Lone Wolf and Cub series, Ogami Itto is hired to kill a tattooed female assassin and battles Retsudo, head of the Yagyu clan, and his son Gunbei.
