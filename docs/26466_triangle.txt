Title: Triangle (2009)
Genres: Horror
Director: Christopher Smith
Main Cast: Melissa George, Liam Hemsworth, Emma Lung, Rachael Carpani, Michael Dorman
Keywords: ocean, florida, autism, key, yacht, ax, ship, ghost ship, murder, time loop
