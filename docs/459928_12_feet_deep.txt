Title: 12 Feet Deep (2016)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Matt Eskandari
Main Cast: Tobin Bell, Diane Farr, Nora-Jane Noone, Alexandra Park, Dogen Eyeler
Keywords: sister sister relationship, strong woman, forgiveness, family secrets, swimming pool, suspense, survival, trapped

Overview: Inspired by true events, two sisters are trapped under the fiberglass cover of an Olympic sized public pool and must brave the cold and each other to survive the harrowing night.
