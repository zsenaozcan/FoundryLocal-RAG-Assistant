Title: Scrawl (2015)
Genres: Horror, Fantasy
Director: Peter Hearn
Main Cast: Daisy Ridley, Mark Forester Evans, Joe Daly, Annabelle Le Gresley, Nathalie Pownall

Overview: A boy writes a comic book with his best friend, and finds situations depicted in the comic book coming to life. Along with the appearance of a mysterious girl, the boy is forced to face the reality of what he has written, and begins a battle to attempt to rewrite death.
