Title: The Secret (2007)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Vincent Pérez
Main Cast: David Duchovny, Olivia Thirlby, Lili Taylor, Millie Tresierra, Brandon Blue
Keywords: religion and supernatural, reincarnation, high school, car crash

Overview: Husband, wife, and daughter have moved from Boston to Williamstown. At 16, Samantha treats her mother shabbily, but when the two of them are in a horrific car crash, the mother wills Sam to live, somehow losing her own life while her spirit enters Sam.
