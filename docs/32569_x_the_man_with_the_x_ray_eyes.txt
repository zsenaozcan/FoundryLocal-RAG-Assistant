Title: X: The Man with the X-Ray Eyes (1963)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Roger Corman
Main Cast: Ray Milland, Diana Van der Vlis, Harold J. Stone, John Hoyt, Don Rickles
Keywords: casino, experiment, human experimentation, eye, belly dancing, independent film, hospital, scientist, monkey, amusement park

Overview: The story concerns Dr James Xavier, ably portrayed by Ray Milland. He has invented a serum for improving human vision. When his funding is cut he proceeds to experiment on himself -- with horrifying results. From the director who gave you Swamp Women, The Wasp Woman, and The Beast with a Million Eyes!
