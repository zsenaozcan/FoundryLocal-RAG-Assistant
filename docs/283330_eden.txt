Title: Eden (2014)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Mia Hansen-Løve
Main Cast: Félix de Givry, Brady Corbet, Greta Gerwig, Arsinée Khanjian, Pauline Étienne
Keywords: paris, france, dj, woman director

Overview: Paul, a teenager in the underground scene of early-nineties Paris, forms a DJ collective with his friends and together they plunge into the nightlife of sex, drugs, and endless music.
