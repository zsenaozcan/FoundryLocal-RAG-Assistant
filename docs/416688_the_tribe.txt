Title: The Tribe (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Roxy Shih
Main Cast: Jessica Rothe, Anne Winters, Chloe Beth Jones, Michael Nardelli, Cokey Falkow

Overview: A family of three young sisters live out their days after a pandemic has consumed most of the known world. One day a stranger suddenly shows up and their world changes in ways they never could have imagined.
