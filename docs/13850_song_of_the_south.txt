Title: Song of the South (1946)
Genres: Family, Music
Director: Harve Foster
Main Cast: Ruth Warrick, Bobby Driscoll, James Baskett, Luana Patten, Lucile Watson
Keywords: southern

Overview: Uncle Remus draws upon his tales of Brer Rabbit to help little Johnny deal with his confusion over his parents' separation as well as his new life on the plantation.
