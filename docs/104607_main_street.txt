Title: Main Street (1956)
Genres: Drama

Main Cast: Betsy Blair, José Suárez, Yves Massard, Luis Peña, Dora Doll
Keywords: regret, river, procession, bridge, coldness, pit, based on play or musical, buddy, public humiliation, dignity
