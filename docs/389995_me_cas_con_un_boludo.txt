Title: Me casé con un boludo (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Juan Taratuto
Main Cast: Adrián Suar, Valeria Bertuccelli, Gerardo Romano, Norman Briski, Alan Sabbagh
Keywords: romantic comedy, argentina

Overview: Florencia, a rising actress, ends up falling in love with her co-star, a famous film star. When they decide to marry, Florencia becomes aware that in reality she fell in love with the character and that her husband is indeed a real idiot.
