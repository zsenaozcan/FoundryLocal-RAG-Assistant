Title: Metalstorm: The Destruction of Jared-Syn (1983)
Genres: Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Charles Band
Main Cast: Jeffrey Byron, Michael Preston, Tim Thomerson, Kelly Preston, Richard Moll
Keywords: space, space opera, 3d

Overview: A seeker named Dogen rescues Dhyana after her father is murdered by the evil Jared-Syn. To avenge her father's death, Dogen must find Jared-Syn's hideout in the mysterious "Lost City", but the only person who knows where it is an aging, burned-out seeker named Rhodes. Along the way, they will need to do battle against the hunter Baal and his Cyclopean minions for engaging Jared-Syn in a final encounter.
