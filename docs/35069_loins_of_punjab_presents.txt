Title: Loins of Punjab Presents (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Shabana Azmi, Ajay Naidu, Ayesha Dharker, Michael Raimondi, Seema Rahmani
Keywords: indian lead, bollywood

Overview: A ruthless philantrophist. A bhangra rapper. An over-protected prodigy. A reckless actress. A lovelorn businessman. An entrepreneurial yogi. And a Loin King. Enter a roller-coaster world of seven strangers whose lives collide during a singing contest in a small New Jersey town.
