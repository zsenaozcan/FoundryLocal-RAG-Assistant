Title: Manos: The Hands of Fate (1966)
Genres: Horror
Director: Harold P. Warren
Main Cast: Harold P. Warren, Tom Neyman, John Reynolds, Diane Mahree, Stephanie Nielson
Keywords: fire, gun, drive, sacrifice, flashlight, cult, hands, good vs evil, fate
