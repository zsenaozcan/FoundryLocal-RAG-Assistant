Title: Castle of Sand (1974)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery, Foreign
Director: Yoshitaro Nomura
Main Cast: Tetsurō Tamba, Yoko Shimada, Gô Katô, Kensaku Morita, Chishu Ryu
Keywords: japan, class differences, police investigation

Overview: Yoshitaro Nomura’s 1975 film of Seicho Matsumoto’s immensely popular detective story tells the tale of two detectives, Imanishi (Tetsuro Tamba) and Yoshimura (Kensaku Morita), tasked with tracking down the murderer of an old man, found bludgeoned to death in a rail yard.
