Title: Swearnet: The Movie (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Warren P. Sonoda
Main Cast: Mike Smith, Robb Wells, John Paul Tremblay, Tom Green, Patrick Roach

Overview: Fed up with being censored in their post-Trailer Park Boys lives, the out of work stars/world-renowned 'swearists', Mike Smith, Robb Wells and John Paul Tremblay decide to start their own uncensored network on the internet.
