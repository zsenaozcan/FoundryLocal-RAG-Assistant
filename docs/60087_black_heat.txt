Title: Black Heat (1976)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Al Adamson
Main Cast: Timothy Brown, Russ Tamblyn, Jana Bellan, Geoffrey Land, Regina Carrol
Keywords: blaxploitation

Overview: Kicks Carter, a streetwise Las Vegs cop, is out to shut down an upscale hotel that is actually a front for a host of illegal activities. A gang headed by the scuzzy Ziggy is running everything from gun-running and loan-sharking to prostitution and drug-dealing, and a beautiful but nosy reporter keeps getting in Kicks' way.
