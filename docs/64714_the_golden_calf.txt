Title: The Golden Calf (1968)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Mikhail Shvejtser
Main Cast: Sergei Yursky, Leonid Kuravlyov, Zinoviy Gerdt, Evgeniy Evstigneev, Nikolai Boyarsky

Overview: A crook named Ostap Bender, who survived a murder attempt by Kisa Vorobyaninov in "12 Chairs," now schemes to extort 1 million from an underground millionaire.
