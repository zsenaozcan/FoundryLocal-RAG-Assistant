Title: Go Tell the Spartans (1978)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Ted Post
Main Cast: Burt Lancaster, Craig Wasson, Marc Singer, Joe Unger, David Clennon
Keywords: vietnam, major

Overview: Go Tell the Spartans is a 1978 American war film based on Daniel Ford's 1967 novel "Incident at Muc Wa." It tells the story about U.S. Army military advisers during the early part of the Vietnam War. Led my Major Asa Barker, these advisers and their South Vietnamese counterparts defend the village of Muc Wa against multiple attacks by Viet-Cong guerrillas.
