Title: Postmortem (1998)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Albert Pyun
Main Cast: Charlie Sheen, Michael Halsey, Ivana Miličević, Stephen McCole, Gary Lewis
Keywords: suspense

Overview: The only thing James wants is to remain away from Scotland. One day, however, he receives a fax, a printout of an unknown person's obituary. The next day, he is charged and arrested for the murder of this person.
