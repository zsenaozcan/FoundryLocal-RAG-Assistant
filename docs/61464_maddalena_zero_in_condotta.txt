Title: Maddalena... zero in condotta (1940)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Vittorio De Sica
Main Cast: Vittorio De Sica, Vera Bergman, Carla Del Poggio, Irasema Dilián, Pina Renzi

Overview: A young man studying in an academy secretarial practice makes writing letters addressed to an imaginary recipient who supposedly lives in Rome. One day, just for fun, it gets to write a love letter and, by chance, that letter is mailed and arrives at the hands of someone who, moved by curiosity, decided to inform the sender.
