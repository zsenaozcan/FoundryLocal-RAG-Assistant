Title: This Is Elvis (1981)
Genres: Drama, Music, Documentary
Director: Andrew Solt
Main Cast: Elvis Presley, David Scott, Paul Boensch III, Johnny Harra, Linda Thompson
Keywords: rock and roll, television, recording contract, rock, biography, music, concert, music business, live performance, rock concert

Overview: Though several actors portray Elvis Presley at different stages of his life, this documentary is comprised mostly of actual performance footage and interviews with Elvis, his fans and those close to him. This biographical docu-drama features rare footage of Elvis and dramatically recreated scenes from Elvis' life.
