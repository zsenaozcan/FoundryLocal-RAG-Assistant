Title: The Activist (2014)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Cyril Morin
Main Cast: Chadwick Brown, Tonantzin Carmelo, Michael Spears, Ron Roggé

Overview: A political thriller during the Wounded Knee insurrection in 1973. Two activist are arrested and maintain in custody in a sheriff's office. They will meet a Nixon advisor, a lawyer, a senator and a movie star who is also an activist. Feb 2013 will be the 40Th anniversary of the insurrection... Written by Anonymous
