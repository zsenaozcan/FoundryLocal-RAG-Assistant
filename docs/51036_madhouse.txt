Title: MadHouse (1990)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Tom Ropelewski
Main Cast: John Larroquette, Kirstie Alley, John Diehl, Alison La Placa, Jessica Lundy
Keywords: mental breakdown, parasite, mayhem, uninvited guest

Overview: The luxurious villa of Mark and Jessie Bannister, a yuppie couple, is overrun by loads of uninvited guests who turn the house up side down.
