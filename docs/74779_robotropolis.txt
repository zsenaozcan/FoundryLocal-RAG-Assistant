Title: Robotropolis (2011)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Christopher Hatton
Main Cast: Zoe Naylor, Graham Sibley, Edward Foy, Jourdan Lee

Overview: A group of reporters are covering the unveiling of a new facility that is completely maintained by robot prototypes. When one of the robots goes haywire, the reporters find themselves not just reporting on the malfunction, but fighting for their lives.
