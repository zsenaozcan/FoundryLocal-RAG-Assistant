Title: Life Even Looks Like a Party (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Branco Mello
Main Cast: Branco Mello, Arnaldo Antunes, Charles Gavin, Nando Reis, Paulo Miklos

Overview: Brazilian documentary that tells the story of Titas, the most famous rock band in the country. The film was directed by one of the members, Branco Mello, using images recorded from the early 80's until the present day.
