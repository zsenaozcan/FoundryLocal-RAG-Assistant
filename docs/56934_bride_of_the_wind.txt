Title: Bride of the Wind (2001)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Music
Director: Bruce Beresford
Main Cast: Sarah Wynter, Jonathan Pryce, Vincent Pérez, Simon Verhoeven, Gregor Seberg
Keywords: painter, composer, biography, independent film

Overview: A biopic of Alma Mahler, the wife of composer Gustav Mahler (as well as Walter Gropius and Franz Werfel), and the mistress of Oskar Kokoschka.
