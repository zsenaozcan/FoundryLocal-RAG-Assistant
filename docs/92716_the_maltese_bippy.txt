Title: The Maltese Bippy (1969)
Genres: Mystery, Comedy, Horror
Director: Norman Panama
Main Cast: Dan Rowan, Dick Martin, Carol Lynley, Julie Newmar, Mildred Natwick
Keywords: cemetery, partner, housekeeper

Overview: A man buys a house and comes to believe that not only is the house haunted by werewolves, but a family of vampires lives next door.
