Title: Приключения Шерлока Холмса и доктора Ватсона: Двадцатый век начинается (1986)
Genres: Mystery
Director: Igor Maslennikov
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: russia

Overview: The Twentieth Century Approaches is a 1986 Soviet film adaptation of Arthur Conan Doyle's stories about Sherlock Holmes. It is the fifth film in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson film series directed by Igor Maslennikov.
 The film is based on four stories by Conan Doyle - "The Adventure of the Engineer's Thumb", "The Adventure of the Second Stain", "The Adventure of the Bruce-Partington Plans", "His Last Bow".
