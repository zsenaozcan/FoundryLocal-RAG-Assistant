Title: The Gay Deception (1935)
Genres: Comedy
Director: William Wyler
Main Cast: Francis Lederer, Frances Dee, Benita Hume, Alan Mowbray, Lennox Pawle
Keywords: prince, jail, socialite, bellhop, false arrest

Overview: Mirabel wins a $5,000 lottery which will enable her to live like a queen in New York. There she meets Sandro, a bellboy who is really a prince, so she does get to be a queen after all.
