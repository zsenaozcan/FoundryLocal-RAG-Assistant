Title: The World Made Straight (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: David Burris
Main Cast: Haley Joel Osment, Minka Kelly, Noah Wyle, Jeremy Irvine, Adelaide Clemens
Keywords: appalachia, appalachian

Overview: In a rural Appalachian community haunted by the legacy of a Civil War massacre, a rebellious young man struggles to escape the violence that would bind him to the past.
