Title: Garfunkel and Oates: Trying to be Special (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Riki Lindhome
Main Cast: Riki Lindhome, Kate Micucci, Anthony Jeselnik
Keywords: stand-up comedy, woman director

Overview: Garkfunkel and Oates: Trying to Be Special is the first special by the musical comedy duo of Kate Micucci and Riki Lindhome. The pair filmed the special at the Neptune Theatre in Seattle, and it features songs, comedy, and a new Garfunkel and Oates music video. The special will be released as a Vimeo original, and will be available on May 5, 2016.
