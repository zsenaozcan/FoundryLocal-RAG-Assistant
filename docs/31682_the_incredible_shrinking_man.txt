Title: The Incredible Shrinking Man (1957)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Jack Arnold
Main Cast: Grant Williams, Randy Stuart, April Kent, Paul Langton, Raymond Bailey
Keywords: based on novel, cat, giant spider, shrinking, radioactivity

Overview: When exposure to a combination of radiation and insecticide causes Scott Carey to begin to shrink medical science is powerless to help him.
