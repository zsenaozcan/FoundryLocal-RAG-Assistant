Title: True Friends (1954)
Genres: Comedy, Adventure
Director: Mikhail Kalatozov
Main Cast: Vasiliy Merkurev, Boris Chirkov, Aleksandr Borisov, Aleksei Gribov, Liliya Gritsenko

Overview: Story about 3 childhood friends who found each other later in life and decided to rafting on one of the Moscovian rivers.
