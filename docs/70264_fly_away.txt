Title: Fly Away (2011)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Janet Grillo
Main Cast: Beth Broderick, Ashley Rickards, Greg Germann, Denise Dowse, JR Bourne
Keywords: autism, woman director

Overview: The poignant yet humor filled story about a single mother of a teenager severely impacted by autism, forced to reckon with her daughter's future. As her child becomes an adult, what used to work, no longer does. What will sustain her daughter, and herself? A parent-child love story, when love means letting go.
