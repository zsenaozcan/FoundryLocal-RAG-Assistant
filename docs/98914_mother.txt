Title: Mother (1926)
Genres: Drama
Director: Vsevolod Pudovkin
Main Cast: Vera Baranovskaya, Nikolai Batalov, Aleksandr Chistyakov
Keywords: black and white, silent film

Overview: A  story about a family torn apart by a worker's strike. At first, the mother wants to protect her family from the troublemakers, but eventually she realizes that her son is right and the workers should strike.
