Title: Vampire in Venice (1988)
Genres: Horror
Director: Augusto Caminito
Main Cast: Klaus Kinski, Christopher Plummer, Donald Pleasence, Barbara De Rossi, Anne Knecht
Keywords: venice, female nudity, gypsy, vampire, carnival

Overview: Professor Paris Catalano visits Venice, to investigate the last known appearance of the famous vampire Nosferatu during the carnival of 1786.
