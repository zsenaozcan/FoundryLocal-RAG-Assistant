Title: Dr. Bronner's Magic Soapbox (2007)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Sara Lamm
Main Cast: Dr. Bronner
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A human story about a socially responsible company, “Dr. Bronner’s Magic Soapbox” documents the complicated family legacy behind the counterculture’s favorite cleaning product — Bronner’s son, 68-year-old Ralph, endured over 15 orphanages and foster homes as a child, but despite difficult memories, is his father’s most ardent fan.
