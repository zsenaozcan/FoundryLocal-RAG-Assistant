Title: The Gangster (1947)
Genres: Crime
Director: Gordon Wiles
Main Cast: Barry Sullivan, Belita, Joan Lorring, Akim Tamiroff, Harry Morgan
Keywords: b movie, film noir

Overview: Based on the novel Low Company. One of the most peculiar film noirs of the 1940s stars Barry Sullivan as a small-time hood who suffers a mental breakdown as his big plans begin to crumble. Beautiful Belita is the slumming society girlfriend who only fuels his paranoia.
