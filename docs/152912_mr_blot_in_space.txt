Title: Mr. Blot in Space (1988)
Genres: Fantasy, Family
Director: Krzysztof Gradowski
Main Cast: Piotr Fronczewski, Henryk Bista, Zbigniew Buczkowski, Izabella Miko
Keywords: magic, space travel, space

Overview: It's Christmas, and children at an orphanage in Maliszewo receive a computer that was given to them by another orphan who used to live there many years ago. During the night, the computer awakes itself and tells the children a story that will happen in 25 years...
