Title: In God We Tru$t (1980)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Marty Feldman
Main Cast: Marty Feldman, Peter Boyle, Louise Lasser, Richard Pryor, Andy Kaufman
Keywords: monk, religion

Overview: A naive monk, named Brother Ambrose (Marty Feldman), is sent by the abbot on a mission to raise $5,000 in order to save their monastery from closing. He goes to Hollywood where he encounters a number of eccentric characters.
