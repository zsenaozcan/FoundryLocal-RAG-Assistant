Title: Another Harvest Moon (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Greg Swartz
Main Cast: Ernest Borgnine, Piper Laurie, Anne Meara, Doris Roberts, Richard Schiff

Overview: Another Harvest Moon is a tender drama about four elderly Americans coping with life in a nursing home. FRANK, ELLA, ALICE and JUNE gather each morning for a game of cards. They have become a family to one another, offering unyielding support, constant bickering and strong opinions about life, death and everything in between.
