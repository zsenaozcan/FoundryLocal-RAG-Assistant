Title: Shadows in an Empty Room (1976)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Alberto De Martino
Main Cast: Stuart Whitman, John Saxon, Martin Landau, Tisa Farrow, Carole Laure
Keywords: sister, magnum, revenge

Overview: An Ottawa police captain searches for the person who poisoned his sister, who was attending the university in Montreal. So desperate is he for revenge that he begin to use his own brutal methods to find the killer. Soon he discovers that not everything is what he thought it was.
