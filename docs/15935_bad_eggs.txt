Title: Bad Eggs (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Thriller
Director: Tony Martin
Main Cast: Mick Molloy, Bob Franklin, Judith Lucy, Alan Brough, Bill Hunter
Keywords: corruption, undercover agent, police

Overview: Ben Kinnear and Mike Paddock are two undercover detectives with way too much publicity, who find they can no longer turn a blind eye to the corruption in the police force.
