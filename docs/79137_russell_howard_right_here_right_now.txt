Title: Russell Howard: Right Here Right Now (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Russell Howard
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: One of Britain's most exciting and popular stand-up comedians, Russell Howard is back in 2011 with his third sell-out tour Right Here, Right Now. The star of Russell Howard's Good News and Mock The Week never fails to entertain with his trademark mix of eloquent gags and deft storytelling.
