Title: Carrie Pilby (2017)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Susan Johnson
Main Cast: Bel Powley, Nathan Lane, Gabriel Byrne, Vanessa Bayer, Colin O'Donoghue
Keywords: new york, genius, dating, teenager, woman director

Overview: Awkward, isolated and disapproving of most of the people around her, a precocious 19-year-old genius is challenged to put her convictions to the test by venturing out on to the NYC dating scene, in this adaptation of Caren Lissner’s best-selling 2003 novel.
