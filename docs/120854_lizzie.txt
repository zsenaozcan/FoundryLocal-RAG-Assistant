Title: Lizzie (2013)
Genres: Thriller
Director: David Dunn Jr.
Main Cast: Leif Holt, Amanda Baker, Corbin Bernsen, Caitlin Carmichael, Don Swayze
Keywords: nightmare, childhood trauma, axe murder

Overview: Lizzie, a young woman with amnesia, slowly comes to realize that she may be responsible for the horrific murder of her parents. After returning to the family home, Lizzie had terrifying flashbacks to her childhood, and comes face to face with the true horror of her past.
