Title: 3-Day Weekend (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rob Williams
Main Cast: Chris Carlisle, Joel Harrison, Gaetano Jones, Derek Long, Derek Meeker
Keywords: gay interest

Overview: Long-term couple Simon and Jason, along with daddy-ish Cooper and his boy du jour, escape to a mountain cabin for a three-day weekend away from their hectic city lives. Looking to spice up this long-standing tradition, they add a special twist to this years retreat - each of them must invite one attractive single friend to their mountain getaway.
