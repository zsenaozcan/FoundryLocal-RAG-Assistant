Title: And Starring Pancho Villa as Himself (2003)
Genres: Drama, History, War, Western
Director: Bruce Beresford
Main Cast: Antonio Banderas, Eion Bailey, Alan Arkin, Jim Broadbent, Matt Day
Keywords: mexico, mexican revolution, biography, pancho villa

Overview: In 1914, the Mexican revolutionary Pancho Villa (Antonio Banderas) invites studios to shoot his actual battles against Porfírio Diaz army to raise funds for financing guns and ammunition. The Mutual Film Corporation, through producer D.W. Griffith (Colm Feore), interests for the proposition and sends the filmmaker Frank Thayer (Eion Bailey) to negotiate a contract with Pancho Villa himself.
