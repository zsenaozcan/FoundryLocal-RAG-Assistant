Title: The Two Jakes (1990)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery
Director: Jack Nicholson
Main Cast: Jack Nicholson, Harvey Keitel, Meg Tilly, Madeleine Stowe, Rubén Blades
Keywords: los angeles, private detective

Overview: This sequel to the classic Chinatown finds private detective Jake Gittes still haunted from the events of the first film. Hired by a man to investigate his wife's infidelities, Jake once again finds himself involved in a complicated plot involving murder, oil, and even some ghosts from his past.
