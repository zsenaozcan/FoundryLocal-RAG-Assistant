Title: The Errand Boy (1961)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Jerry Lewis
Main Cast: Jerry Lewis, Brian Donlevy, Howard McNear, Dick Wesson, Kathleen Freeman
Keywords: mailroom

Overview: Paramutual Pictures wants to know where all the money is going so they hire Morty to be their spy. Morty works for Mr. Sneak and gets a job in the mail room so that he can have access to the lot. But all that Morty ever finds is that he can cause havoc no matter what he does.
