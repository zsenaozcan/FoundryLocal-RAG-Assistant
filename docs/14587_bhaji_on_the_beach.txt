Title: Bhaji on the Beach (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Gurinder Chadha
Main Cast: Kim Vithana, Jimmi Harkishin, Sarita Khajuria, Akbar Kurtha, Mo Sesay
Keywords: indian lead, independent film, woman director

Overview: A group of women of Indian descent take a trip together from their home in Birmingham, England to the beach resort of Blackpool.
