Title: The Violent Kind (2008)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Geoffrey Pepos
Main Cast: Kirk Harris, John Savage, Irina Björklund, Sandra Vidal, Hamish Linklater
Keywords: u.s. marine, iraq invasion
