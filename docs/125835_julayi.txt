Title: Julayi (2012)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Trivikram Srinivas
Main Cast: Allu Arjun, Ileana D'Cruz, Sonu Sood, Rajendra Prasad, Kota Srinivasa Rao

Overview: Julayi is the story of a fun loving guy who does not work and has no aim in life. He falls in love with a young girl and the story turns on how his life has to change to win her love.
