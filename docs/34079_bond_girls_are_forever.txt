Title: Bond Girls Are Forever (2002)
Genres: Documentary
Director: John Watkin
Main Cast: Maryam d'Abo, Ursula Andress, Halle Berry, Honor Blackman, Luciana Paluzzi
Keywords: spying, sensuality, actress

Overview: Through vintage film clips of past Bond movie epics, and with the participation of several former "Bond Girls" as interviewees (among them Dr. No's Ursula Andress and Diamonds Are Forever's Jill St. John), the documentary traced the evolution of the typical James Bond heroine from decorative damsel in distress to gutsy (but still decorative) participant in the action.
