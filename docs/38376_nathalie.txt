Title: Nathalie... (2003)
Genres: Drama
Director: Anne Fontaine
Main Cast: Fanny Ardant, Emmanuelle Béart, Gérard Depardieu, Wladimir Yordanoff, Judith Magre
Keywords: paris, female nudity, prostitute, nightclub, striptease, ice skating, voyeur, woman director, drunkeness

Overview: Paris doctor Catherine starts to think her husband, Bernard, is having an affair when she hears an unfamiliar woman's message on his voice mail. Hoping to learn more about his extramarital activities, Catherine heads to a strip club, where she hires call girl Nathalie to have a fling with Gerard. As the affair progresses, Nathalie gives Catherine regular status reports, and the relationship between the women evolves from business to personal.
