Title: Liza, the Fox-Fairy (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Romance
Director: Ujj Mészáros Károly
Main Cast: Balsai Mónika, David Sakurai, Bede Fazekas Szabolcs, Schmied Zoltán, Reviczky Gábor
Keywords: japan, nurse, pop singer, love, romance, curse, japanese culture, dating, hungarian, ghost

Overview: Liza's a nurse, seeking love. Her only company is a long-dead Japanese pop star, who turns her into a fox-fairy out of jealousy. Now, every men who desires Liza shall die horribly. Can she overcome the curse?
