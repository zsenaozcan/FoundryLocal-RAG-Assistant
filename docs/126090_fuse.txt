Title: Fuse (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Pjer Žalica
Main Cast: Enis Bešlagić, Bogdan Diklić, Saša Petrović, Izudin Bajrović, Jasna Žalica

Overview: Two years after the Bosnian civil war, a town that is slowly rebuilding itself must whip together a democracy when it's announced the U.S. President Bill Clinton might be paying a visit.
