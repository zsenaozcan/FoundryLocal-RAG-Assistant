Title: Who's Singin' Over There? (1980)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Adventure
Director: Slobodan Šijan
Main Cast: Pavle Vuisić, Aleksandar Berček, Dragan Nikolić, Danilo 'Bata' Stojković, Neda Arnerić
Keywords: gypsy, bus, world war ii, belgrad

Overview: On April 5, 1941, a day before Nazi attack on Yugoslavia, entourage on a country road board Krstic's bus heading for Belgrade: two Gypsies who occasionally sing about misery, an aging war veteran, a Nazi sympathizer, a dapper singer, a consumptive, and a man with a shotgun. Krstic is a world-weary cynic, out for a buck; the driver is his son, the simple, cheerful Misko. En route they pick up a more people and head towards their destiny.
