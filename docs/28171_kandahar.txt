Title: Kandahar (2001)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mojtaba Mirtahasebi
Main Cast: Ike Ogut, Nelofer Pazira, Hassan Tantai, Sadou Teymouri, Hoyatala Hakimi
Keywords: islam, middle east, refugee, afghanistan, famine, mujahid, sister, taliban, based on true story, travel

Overview: After an Afghanistan-born woman who lives in Canada receives a letter from her suicidal sister, she takes a perilous journey through Afghanistan to try to find her.
