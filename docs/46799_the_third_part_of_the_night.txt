Title: The Third Part of the Night (1971)
Genres: Drama, Horror, War
Director: Andrzej Żuławski
Main Cast: Leszek Teleszynski, Malgorzata Braunek, Jan Nowicki, Jerzy Golinski, Anna Milewska
Keywords: world war ii, typhus, german occupation, gestapo, pregnant woman, death of family, poland

Overview: Set during the Nazi occupation of Poland, in which Michal witnesses the murder of his mother, wife and child. He is hurled into a life that literally is not his own; a surreal world littered with trapdoors, doppelgängers and wormholes. It also tells the true untold story of a Nazi vaccine laboratory where Jews and members of the resistance were “employed” as feeders for parasites infected with typhus.
