Title: OSS 117: Lost in Rio (2009)
Genres: Crime, Action, Comedy, Adventure
Director: Michel Hazanavicius
Main Cast: Jean Dujardin, Louise Monot, Pierre Bellemare, Reem Kherici, Rüdiger Vogler
Keywords: hippie, secret agent

Overview: French top secret agent, Hubert Bonisseur de la Bath, is sent to Rio to buy microfilms from a running nazi. To do so, he has to team up with Mossad secret services.
