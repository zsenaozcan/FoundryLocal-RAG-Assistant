Title: Muhammad Ali's Greatest Fight (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Stephen Frears
Main Cast: Christopher Plummer, Danny Glover, Frank Langella, Ed Begley Jr., Barry Levinson
Keywords: based on novel, vietnam war, justice

Overview: Muhammad Ali’s historic Supreme Court battle from behind closed doors. When Ali was drafted into the Vietnam War at the height of his boxing career, his claim to conscientious objector status led to a controversial legal battle that rattled the U.S. judicial system right up to the highest court in the land.
