Title: A Dead Calling (2006)
Genres: Horror
Director: Michael Feifer
Main Cast: Alexandra Holden, Sid Haig, Leslie Easterbrook, John Burke, Bill Moseley
Keywords: obsession, revenge, murder, ghost, confused parentage

Overview: A young television reporter (or "investigative journalist") is attacked in her home, leading to her boyfriend being murdered. Six months later she returns to work in a smaller town, and stumbles across a house full of ghosts trying to call out to her. What is the secret of this house and how is she connected to it?
