Title: My Friends Need Killing (1976)
Genres: Horror, Drama, Crime
Director: Paul Leder
Main Cast: Greg Mullavey, Meredith MacRae, Clayton Wilcox, Elaine Partnow, Roger Cruz
Keywords: vietnam war, killing spree, proto-slasher

Overview: A traumatized Vietnam veteran goes on a killing spree, targeting the men with whom he served in the army.
