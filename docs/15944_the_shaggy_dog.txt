Title: The Shaggy Dog (1959)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Charles Barton
Main Cast: Fred MacMurray, Jean Hagen, Tommy Kirk, Annette Funicello, Tim Considine
Keywords: magic, dog, sheep dog

Overview: Through an ancient spell, a boy changes into a sheepdog and back again. It seems to happen at inopportune times and the spell can only be broken by an act of bravery....
