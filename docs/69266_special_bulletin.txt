Title: Special Bulletin (1983)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Edward Zwick
Main Cast: Ed Flanders, Kathryn Walker, Roxanne Hart, Christopher Allport, David Clennon
Keywords: terrorism, nuclear, tv movie

Overview: A TV reporter and cameraman are taken hostage on a tugboat while covering a workers strike. The demands of the hostage-takers are to collect all the nuclear detonators in the Charleston, SC area so they may be detonated at sea. They threaten to detonate a nuclear device of their own of their demand isnt met.
