Title: The Zohar Secret (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Vladek Zankovsky
Main Cast: Israel Sasha Demidov, Irina Barinova, Henry David , Roi Miron, Oleg Fendura

Overview: Max finds himself in possession of an ancient scroll that describes the entire history of humankind from beginning to end. Seeking to use the information for his own gain, Max triggers the mechanism that begins influencing his own life.
