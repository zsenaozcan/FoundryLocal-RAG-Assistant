Title: One Week (2008)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Michael McGowan
Main Cast: Joshua Jackson, Liane Balaban, Campbell Scott, Peter Spence, Marc Strange
Keywords: canada, self-discovery, travelling person, motorcycle

Overview: Ben Tyler (Joshua Jackson) has been diagnosed with cancer. With a grim chance of survival in the best case scenario even if he immediately begins treatment, he instead decides to take a motorcycle trip from Toronto through the Canadian prairies to British Columbia.
