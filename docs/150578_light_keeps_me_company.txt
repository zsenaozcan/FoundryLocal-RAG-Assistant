Title: Light Keeps Me Company (2000)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Carl-Gustav Nykvist
Main Cast: Sven Nykvist, Erland Josephson, Ingmar Bergman, Liv Ullmann, Woody Allen

Overview: Carl-Gustaf Nykvist's documentary about his father, Sven Nykvist. The film is based on Sven's memoirs with Sven himself as narrator. A journey to the place of birth, Moheda, constitutes the hub of the film and during the journey friends and memories emerge. Written by Fredrik Klasson
