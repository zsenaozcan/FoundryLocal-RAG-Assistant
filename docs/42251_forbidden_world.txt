Title: Forbidden World (1982)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Allan Holzman
Main Cast: Jesse Vint, Dawn Dunlap, June Chadwick, Linden Chiles, Fox Harris
Keywords: mutant, genetics, space, planet

Overview: In the distant future, a federation marshal arrives at a research lab on a remote planet where a genetic experiment has gotten loose and begins feeding on the dwindling scientific group.
