Title: The Scarlet Letter (1979)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Rick Hauser
Main Cast: Josef Sommer, Meg Foster, Elisa Erali, John Heard, Penelope Allen
Keywords: based on novel, infidelity, massachusetts

Overview: In the 17th century Massachusetts, a married women, whose husband is missing, has a child with the local pastor. The puritanical residents of her town condemn her to carry the Scarlet Letter of shame. Then the husband shows up.
