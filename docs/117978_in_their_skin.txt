Title: In Their Skin (2012)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Jeremy Power Regimbal
Main Cast: Selma Blair, Joshua Close, James D'Arcy, Rachel Miner, Quinn Lord
Keywords: vacation, home invasion, stranger, psycho killer

Overview: A couple and their young son retreat to their woodland vacation home, only to face sinister neighbors who intend to kill them and steal their identities.
