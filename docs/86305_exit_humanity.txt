Title: Exit Humanity (2011)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: John Geddes
Main Cast: Brian Cox, Mark Gibson, Jordan Hayes, Dee Wallace, Bill Moseley

Overview: A decade after the American Civil War, Edward Young returns home from a hunting trip to find a horrific reanimation of his wife and that their son Adam has disappeared. He must battle his way through an unexplainable outbreak of the walking dead.
