Title: The Burkittsville 7 (2000)
Genres: Horror
Director: Ben Rock
Main Cast: Monty Bane, Lucy Butler, David Grammer, Bill Dreggors, Frank Pastor
Keywords: witch, mythology, legend, serial killer, mockumentary

Overview: A film archivist revisits the story of Rustin Parr, a hermit thought to have murdered seven children while under the possession of the Blair Witch.
