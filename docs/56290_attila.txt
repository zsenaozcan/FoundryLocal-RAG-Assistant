Title: Attila (1954)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Pietro Francisci
Main Cast: Anthony Quinn, Sophia Loren, Henri Vidal, Irene Papas, Ettore Manni
Keywords: historical figure

Overview: Attila, the leader of the barbarian Huns and called by the Romans "The Scourge of God", sweeps onto the Italian peninsula, defeating all of the armies of Rome, until he and his men reach the gates of the city itself.
