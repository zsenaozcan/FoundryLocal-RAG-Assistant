Title: Fighter Pilot: Operation Red Flag (2004)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Stephen Low
Main Cast: Michael Hanrahan, John Stratton, Robert Novotny, Sam Morgan
Keywords: air force, fighter jet, imax, short

Overview: Fighter Pilot: Operation Red Flag follows American F-15 Eagle pilot John Stratton as he trains with some of the world’s best pilots. The movie depicts Stratton’s progression through the challenging and dangerous exercises of Operation Red Flag, the international training program for air forces of allied countries.
