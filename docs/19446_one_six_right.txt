Title: One Six Right (2005)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Brian Terwilliger
Main Cast: Lorenzo Lamas, Sydney Pollack
Keywords: flying, airport, maneuver, flight, aviation, general aviation, los angeles

Overview: An exhilarating documentary film that celebrates the unsung hero of aviation - the local airport - by tracing the life, history,and struggles of an airport icon: Southern California's Van Nuys Airport.
