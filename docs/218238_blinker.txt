Title: Blinker (1999)
Genres: Family, Mystery
Director: Filip Van Neyghem
Main Cast: Joren Seldeslachts, Melissa Gorduyn, Benny Claessens, Matthias Meersmans, Saskia Aelen

Overview: A series of mysterious events and the pestering motor gang 'The Red Vampires' turns the idyllic holiday of Blinker and his friends into a thrilling adventure.
