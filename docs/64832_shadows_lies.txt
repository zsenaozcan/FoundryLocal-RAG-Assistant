Title: Shadows & Lies (2010)
Genres: Drama, Action, Romance
Director: Jay Anania
Main Cast: James Franco, Julianne Nicholson, Josh Lucas, Nina Arianda, Martin Donovan

Overview: The story of William Vincent as he recounts the eccentric and curious path that has brought him, at mortal risk, to New York City, after four years in exile, to rescue a woman he scarcely knows, Ann, from the vague crime syndicate that first brought them together.
