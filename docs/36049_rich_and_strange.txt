Title: Rich and Strange (1931)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Thriller
Director: Alfred Hitchcock
Main Cast: Henry Kendall, Joan Barry, Percy Marmont, Betty Amann, Elsie Randolph
Keywords: inheritance
