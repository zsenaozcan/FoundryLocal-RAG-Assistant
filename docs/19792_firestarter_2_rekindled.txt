Title: Firestarter 2: Rekindled (2002)
Genres: TV Movie, Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Robert Iscove
Main Cast: Marguerite Moreau, Malcolm McDowell, Dennis Hopper, Danny Nucci, Skye McCole Bartusiak
Keywords: fire, supernatural

Overview: Charlie McGee is a young woman with the unwanted and often uncontrollable gift of psychokinesis, lighting fires by mere thought. Charlie has been in hiding for nearly all her life from a top-secret government fringe group headed by the maniacal John Rainbird, who wants to find and use Charlie as the ultimate weapon of war.
