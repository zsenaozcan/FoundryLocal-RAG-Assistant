Title: Sallah (1964)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ephraim Kishon
Main Cast: Geula Noni, Gila Almagor, Shaike Levi, Arik Einstein, Ya'ackov Banai
Keywords: jewish

Overview: Sallah Shabati, the patriarch of the big family recently arrived to Israel from Yemen, tries to make money and get better housing, in a country that can barely provide for its own and is in the midst of absorbing hundreds of thousands of Jewish refugees from Arab countries.
