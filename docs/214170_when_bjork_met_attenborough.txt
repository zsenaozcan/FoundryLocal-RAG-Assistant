Title: When Bjork Met Attenborough (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Louise Hooper
Main Cast: Björk, David Attenborough, Oliver Sacks, Tilda Swinton

Overview: Award-winning musician Björk and legendary broadcaster and naturalist Sir David Attenborough have admired each other's work for years but this is the first time they have discussed their mutual love of music and the natural world on screen.  In this remarkable documentary, Björk explores our unique relationship with music and discovers how technology might transform the way we engage with it in the future.
