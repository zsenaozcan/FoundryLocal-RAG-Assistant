Title: Quest for a Heart (2007)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Pekka Lehtosaari
Main Cast: Allu Tuppurainen, Saija Lentonen
Keywords: fairy-tale figure

Overview: When the most mischievous resident of Rolli Village befriends a beautiful elf girl, the hopelessly mismatched pair embarks on a magical adventure to save the Forest of Elves by traveling to the Land of Winter and recovering the mythical magical heart in this animated tale of wonder for the entire family.
