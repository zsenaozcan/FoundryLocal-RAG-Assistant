Title: Niceland (Population. 1.000.002) (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Friðrik Þór Friðriksson
Main Cast: Martin Compston, Gary Lewis, Peter Capaldi, Shauna Macdonald, Kerry Fox

Overview: Niceland is about a young man Jet, living a joyful life working at a factory with his girlfriend who he's deeply in love with. Shortly after they decide to get married and live together happily ever after her cat, who she has a weird affection for, dies. The girlfriend falls into depression which could (strangely enough) lead to her death and the only way to give her a purpose to live and save her is to tell her the meaning of life.
