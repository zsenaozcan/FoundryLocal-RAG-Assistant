Title: Catching Hell (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Alex Gibney
Main Cast: Moises Alou, Steve Bartman, Bill Buckner, Bob Costas, Alex Gibney
Keywords: sport

Overview: After the Chicago Cubs blow an opportunity to reach the World Series in 2003, Cubs fans blame the team's misfortune on fellow fan Steve Bartman, who interfered with a foul ball and prevented Moises Alou from making a catch.
