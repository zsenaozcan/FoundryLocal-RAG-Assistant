Title: The Brink's Job (1978)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Action, Drama
Director: William Friedkin
Main Cast: Peter Falk, Peter Boyle, Allen Garfield, Warren Oates, Gena Rowlands
Keywords: boston, heist

Overview: After a long time of bad luck, the little criminal Tony and his gang successfully rob one of Brink's security transport, taking $30,000. Surprisingly their coup doesn't make the press. Curious Tony checks out their headquarters and finds out that their security standard is low beyond belief. Now a really big coup is prepared... Written by Tom Zoerner
