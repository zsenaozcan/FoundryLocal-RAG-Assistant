Title: Three Violent People (1957)
Genres: Drama, Western
Director: Rudolph Maté
Main Cast: Charlton Heston, Anne Baxter, Gilbert Roland, Tom Tryon, Forrest Tucker
Keywords: brother brother relationship, carpetbagger

Overview: A rancher (Charlton Heston), his shady bride (Anne Baxter) and his one-armed brother (Tom Tryon) fight amid carpetbaggers in Texas.
