Title: Children (2006)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Foreign
Director: Ragnar Bragason
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Icelandic writer-director Ragnar Bragason's dark, brooding ensemble drama Börn (AKA Children) follows the entangled lives of a group of lower-rung citizens of Reykjavik as they struggle to just barely keep their heads above water, but all too often succeed in spiritually drowning, through a constant stream of poor decisions.
