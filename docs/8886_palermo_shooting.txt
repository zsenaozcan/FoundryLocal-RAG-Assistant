Title: Palermo Shooting (2008)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Wim Wenders
Main Cast: Dennis Hopper, Milla Jovovich, Giovanna Mezzogiorno, Campino, Lou Reed
Keywords: independent film

Overview: After the wild life-style of a famous young German photographer almost gets him killed, he goes to Palermo, Sicily to take a brake. Can the beautiful city and a beautiful local woman help him calm himself down?
