Title: Exte: Hair Extensions (2007)
Genres: Horror
Director: Sion Sono
Main Cast: Chiaki Kuriyama, Megumi Sato, Tsugumi, Eri Machimoto, Ren Osugi
Keywords: nihon

Overview: An aspiring hair dresser (Kuriyama) becomes the infatuation of a tricophilic man who sells hair extensions to nearby hair salons. The source of the hair is the corpse of a girl whose dead body continues to grow beautiful, voluminous, black hair that comes alive, driving those who use the extensions insane or killing them.
