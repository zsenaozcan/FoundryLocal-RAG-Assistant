Title: Craze (1974)
Genres: Horror, Crime
Director: Freddie Francis
Main Cast: Jack Palance, Diana Dors, Julie Ege, Edith Evans, Hugh Griffith
Keywords: voodoo, murder, series of murders, voodoo ritual

Overview: Jack Palance stars as a demented art dealer &amp; antique-shop owner who performs nightly rituals in honor of the African god Chuku, whom he believes will reward him with unimaginable wealth and power if he merely offers up human sacrifice. His methods are fairly creative, ranging from impalement, slashing and burning, to scaring people to death with an ooga-booga fright mask. But it's all about to blow up in his face...
