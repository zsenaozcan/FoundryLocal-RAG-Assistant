Title: Kamikaze 1989 (1982)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Wolf Gremm
Main Cast: Rainer Werner Fassbinder, Günther Kaufmann, Boy Gobert, Arnold Marquis, Richy Müller
Keywords: bomb, prosecution, media tycoon, dystopia, police

Overview: In a totalitarian society of the future, in which the government controls all facets of the media, a homicide detective investigates a string of bombings, and finds out more than he bargained for.
