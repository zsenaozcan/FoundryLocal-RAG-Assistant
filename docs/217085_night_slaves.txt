Title: Night Slaves (1970)
Genres: TV Movie, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Ted Post
Main Cast: James Franciscus, Lee Grant, Scott Marlowe, Andrew Prine, Tisha Sterling

Overview: A man and his wife stumble upon a town whose inhabitants turn into zombies and head for the edge of town every night... he seems to be the only one unaffected. What is happening to the townsfolk? Who is the mysterious young women he keeps seeing? Why isn't he affected?
