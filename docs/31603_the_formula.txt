Title: The Formula (1980)
Genres: Thriller, Crime
Director: John G. Avildsen
Main Cast: George C. Scott, Marlon Brando, Marthe Keller, John Gielgud, G. D. Spradlin
Keywords: big oil

Overview: A detective uncovers a formula that was devised by the Nazis in WW II to make gasoline from synthetic products, thereby eliminating the necessity for oil--and oil companies. A major oil company finds out about it and tries to destroy the formula and anyone who knows about it.
