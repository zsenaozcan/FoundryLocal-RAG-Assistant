Title: Power Trip (2003)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Paul Devlin
Main Cast: Dennis Bakke, Piers Lewis, Butch Mederos, Bill O'Reilly, Michael Scholey
Keywords: georgia europe

Overview: Corruption, assassination and street rioting surround the story of the award-winning film, Power Trip, which follows an American multi-national trying to solve the electricity crisis in Tbilisi, capital of the former Soviet Republic of Georgia. Power Trip provides insight into today's headlines, with a graphic, on-the-ground depiction of the challenges facing globalization in an environment of culture clash, electricity disconnections and blackouts.
