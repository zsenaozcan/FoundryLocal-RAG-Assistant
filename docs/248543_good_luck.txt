Title: Good Luck (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Carolina Jabor
Main Cast: João Pedro Zappa, Deborah Secco, Pablo Sanábio, Fernanda Montenegro, Gisele Fróes
Keywords: brazilian, drama

Overview: After several behavior problems, teenager John is admitted to a psychiatric clinic by his family. There he meets Judith, for who he soon falls in love. The problem is that she does not have long to live is and they know it. This shall not prevent the emergence of a great romance in the clinic.
