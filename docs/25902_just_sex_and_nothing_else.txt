Title: Just Sex and Nothing Else (2005)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Krisztina Goda
Main Cast: Judit Schell, Kata Dobó, Sándor Csányi, Károly Gesztesi
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The attractive, 32-year old and single Dora edits scripts for plays and is working on "Dangerous Liaisons." One day when her lover shoves her, half-naked, out on a balcony at the sudden appearance of the wife that Dora didn't know he had, Dora is rescued by a handsome man named Tamas.
