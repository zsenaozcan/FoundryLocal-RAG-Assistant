Title: Goya in Bordeaux (1999)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Carlos Saura
Main Cast: Francisco Rabal, José Coronado, Dafne Fernández, Maribel Verdú, Eulalia Ramón
Keywords: spain, sex, nudity, miracle, artist, passion, exile, biography, army, bordeaux

Overview: Francisco Goya (1746-1828), deaf and ill, lives the last years of his life in voluntary exile in Bordeaux, a Liberal protesting the oppressive rule of Ferdinand VII. He's living with his much younger wife Leocadia and their daughter Rosario. He continues to paint at night, and in flashbacks stirred by conversations with his daughter, by awful headaches, and by the befuddlement of age, he relives key times in his life.
