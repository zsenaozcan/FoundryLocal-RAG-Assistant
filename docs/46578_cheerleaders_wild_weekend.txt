Title: Cheerleaders' Wild Weekend (1979)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Jeff Werner
Main Cast: Kristine DeBell, Jason Williams, Anthony Lewis, Wally Ann Wharton, Leslie King
Keywords: kidnapping, cheerleader, spors

Overview: A busload containing three cheerleading teams from three different schools who are heading to compete against each other is hijacked by terrorists and the girls are kidnapped.
