Title: Lone Wolf and Cub: Baby Cart at the River Styx (1972)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Kenji Misumi
Main Cast: Tomisaburō Wakayama, Kayo Matsuo, Akiji Kobayashi, Minoru Oki, Shin Kishida
Keywords: martial arts, assassin, samurai, blood splatter, sequel, based on comic, sword fight, good vs evil, decapitation, blood

Overview: In the second film of the Lone Wolf and Cub series, Ogami Itto battles a group of female ninja in the employ of the Yagyu clan and must assassinate a traitor who plans to sell his clan's secrets to the Shogunate.
