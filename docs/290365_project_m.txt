Title: Project-M (2014)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Jean-Nicolas Verreault, Julie Perreault, Julien Deschamps Jolin, Nadia Essadiqi, Pierre Verville
Keywords: astronaut, space station

Overview: Four astronauts have to stay 1000 days in a space station around Earth in order to prove that a trip to Europa, one of Jupiter's moon, is possible. The experience goes well until something happens on earth.
