Title: Countdown to Looking Glass (1984)
Genres: Drama
Director: Fred Barzyk
Main Cast: Scott Glenn, Michael Murphy, Helen Shaver
Keywords: atomkrieg, nuclear holocaust, mockumentary

Overview: A fictional confrontation between the United States and the Soviet Union over the Strait of Hormuz, the gateway to the Persian Gulf. The narrative of the film details the events that lead up to the initial exchange of nuclear weapons from the perspective of an on-going news broadcast.
