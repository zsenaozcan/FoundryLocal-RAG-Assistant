Title: Dossier K. (2009)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Jan Verheyen
Main Cast: Koen De Bouw, Werner De Smedt, Hilde De Baerdemaeker, Blerim Destani, Greg Timmermans

Overview: Vincke and Verstuyft are detectives of the Antwerp police department. This time they have to deal with the Albanian mob and some problems in their own police division.
