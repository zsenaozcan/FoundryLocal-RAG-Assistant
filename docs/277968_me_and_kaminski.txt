Title: Me and Kaminski (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Wolfgang Becker
Main Cast: Daniel Brühl, Jesper Christensen, Amira Casar, Geraldine Chaplin, Denis Lavant

Overview: Young journalist Sebastian Zöllner is writing an article on artist Manuel Kaminski. Zöllner hopes that Kaminski dies soon, so that he can cash in on his article.
