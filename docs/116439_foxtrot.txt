Title: Foxtrot (1976)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Thriller
Director: Arturo Ripstein
Main Cast: Peter O'Toole, Charlotte Rampling, Max von Sydow

Overview: Liviu (Peter O'Toole) and Julia (Charlotte Rampling) decide it's best to hide out on an island of paradise until the war ends. However politics and jealousy ensue when Larsen (Max Von Sydow) enter the mix.
