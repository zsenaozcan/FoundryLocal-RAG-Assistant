Title: The Maze (1953)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: William Cameron Menzies
Main Cast: Richard Carlson, Veronica Hurst, Katherine Emery, Michael Pate, John Dodsworth

Overview: A Scotsman abruptly breaks off his engagement to pretty Kitty and moves to his uncle's castle in the Scottish highlands. Kitty and her aunt follow Gerald a few weeks later, and discover he has suddenly aged. Some mysterious things happen in a maze made from the hedges adjoining the castle.
