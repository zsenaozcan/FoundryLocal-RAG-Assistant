Title: The Capsule (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Athina Rachel Tsangari
Main Cast: Evangelia Randou, Ariane Labed, Sofia Dona, Clémence Poésy, Aurora Marion
Keywords: darkness, woman director

Overview: Seven young women.
 A mansion perched on a Cycladic rock.
 A series of lessons on discipline, desire, discovery, and disappearance.
 A melancholy, inescapable cycle on the brink of womanhood - infinitely.
