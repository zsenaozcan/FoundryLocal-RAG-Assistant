Title: Wild Reeds (1994)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: André Téchiné
Main Cast: Gaël Morel, Élodie Bouchez, Stéphane Rideau, Frédéric Gorny, Jacques Nolot
Keywords: love triangle, gay, province, algerienkrieg, french, homosexuality, gay relationship, coming of age, in the closet, gay sex

Overview: Set in rural France during the end of the Algerian war, and immersed in the music of the sixties, this film follows four students in friendship and love. Francois's best friend is a girl Maite, but instead he falls in love with a boy Serge, while Serge is falling for Maite. Henri is the outsider, an Algerian-born frenchman who has fled the war, and the catalyst for Francois to find his identity.
