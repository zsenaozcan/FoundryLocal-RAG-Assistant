Title: The Blacksmith (1922)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Buster Keaton
Main Cast: Buster Keaton, Virginia Fox, Joe Roberts
Keywords: rolls royce, magnet

Overview: Buster clowns around in a blacksmith's shop until he and the smithy get in a fight which sends the smithy to jail. Buster helps several customers with horses, then destroys a Rolls Royce while fixing the car parked next to it.
