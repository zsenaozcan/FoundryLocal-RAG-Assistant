Title: Panamerican Machinery (2016)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Joaquín del Paso
Main Cast: Ramiro Orozco, Delfino López, Javier Zaragoza, Cecilia García, Efrén Esquivel

Overview: It's Friday morning at Panamerican Machinery Inc., a company specialising in selling and repairing machines for construction and destruction. The employees are already winding down for the weekend, engaging in little rituals and routines when an unexpected turn of events shakes their peaceful monotony. Don Alejandro, the boss, lies dead at the back of the warehouse.
