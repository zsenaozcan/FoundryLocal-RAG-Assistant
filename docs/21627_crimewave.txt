Title: Crimewave (1985)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy, Crime
Director: Sam Raimi
Main Cast: Louise Lasser, Brion James, Paul L. Smith, Sheree J. Wilson, Edward R. Pressman

Overview: Fed up of his business partner, Ernest Trend hires the services of two exterminators. When things go drastically wrong and they murder the wrong man, the race is on to frame an innocent video surveillance man.
