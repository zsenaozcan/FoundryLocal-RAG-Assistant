Title: The Things of Life (1970)
Genres: Drama
Director: Claude Sautet
Main Cast: Michel Piccoli, Romy Schneider, Lea Massari, Jean Bouise, Gérard Lartigau
Keywords: car crash, extramarital affair

Overview: Pierre, a successful expert in building highways has a traffic accident. Being seriously wounded, he is lying waiting for death and remembering his past in flashbacks.
