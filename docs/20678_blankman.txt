Title: Blankman (1994)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Mike Binder
Main Cast: Damon Wayans, David Alan Grier, Robin Givens, Christopher Lawford, Jason Alexander
Keywords: slapstick, superhero

Overview: Darryl is a childlike man with a genius for inventing various gadgets out of junk. When he stumbles on a method to make his clothes bulletproof, he decides to use his skills to be the lowest budgeted superhero of all.
