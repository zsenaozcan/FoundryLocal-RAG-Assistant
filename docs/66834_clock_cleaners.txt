Title: Clock Cleaners (1937)
Genres: Animation
Director: Ben Sharpsteen
Main Cast: Walt Disney, Pinto Colvig, Clarence Nash
Keywords: clock tower, mickey mouse, falling from height, stork, disney short, donald duck, goofy

Overview: Mickey, Donald, and Goofy are cleaning a large clock. Among the complications: Mickey fights a sleeping stork that doesn't want to leave, Donald gets tangled up in the main-spring, and Goofy is inside the bell when the clock strikes four.
