Title: Hello Stranger (2010)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Comedy
Director: Banjong Pisantanakul
Main Cast: Chantavit Dhanasevi, หนึ่งธิดา โสภณ

Overview: Just like thousands of Thai couples, during the 9 days holiday of Songkran festival, a guy and a girl choose to visit Korea for their own specific reasons. They did not start the trip to go together, but end up returning back home together
