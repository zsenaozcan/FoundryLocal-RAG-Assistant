Title: Eye of the Needle (1981)
Genres: Action, Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Richard Marquand
Main Cast: Donald Sutherland, Kate Nelligan, Stephen MacKenna, Christopher Cazenove, Philip Martin Brown
Keywords: scotland, spy, world war ii, allied, suspense, u boat

Overview: A ruthless German spy, trying to get out of Britain with vital information about D-Day, must spend time with a young woman and her crippled husband.
