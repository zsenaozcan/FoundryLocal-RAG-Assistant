Title: The Nun (2005)
Genres: Horror, Mystery
Director: Luis de la Madrid
Main Cast: Anita Briem, Cristina Piaget,  Belen Blanco,  Alistair Freeland, Manu Fullola
Keywords: barcelona spain, water, gore, extreme violence, violence, evil nun, ritual murder, subjective camera, british horror

Overview: A group of catholic school girls are victimized by a sadistic and jealous nun, "Sister Ursula". The girls drown the nun in a pool of "holy" water. Eighteen years later circumstances arise in which the nuns spirit is released to take her revenge. She possesses the body of Eva, the daughter of one of her killers. In order for the Sister Ursula to be properly laid to rest Eva herself must be killed.
