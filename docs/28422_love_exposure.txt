Title: Love Exposure (2008)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Sion Sono
Main Cast: Takahiro Nishijima, Hikari Mitsushima, Makiko Watanabe, Sakura Ando, Atsuro Watabe
Keywords: underwear, cult, love

Overview: After Yu's mother dies his father, a priest, only seems to take solace in the confessions of his sons sins causing Yu to partake in various 'sinful' activities to appease him. This is a teen love story as only the Japanese could bring us, complete with up-skirt photography, cross dressing, and bloody gore.
