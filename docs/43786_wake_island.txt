Title: Wake Island (1942)
Genres: Action, Drama, War
Director: John Farrow
Main Cast: Brian Donlevy, Macdonald Carey, Robert Preston, William Bendix, Albert Dekker
Keywords: japan, world war ii, pearl harbor, marine corps, u.s. navy, jungle, infantry, pacific island

Overview: In November 1941, Major Caton takes command of the small Marine garrison on Wake Island. His tendency toward spit and polish upsets the men's tropical lassitude, but Pearl Harbor changes everything. Soon the island is attacked and the Marines pull together day by day; but how long can they hold out?
