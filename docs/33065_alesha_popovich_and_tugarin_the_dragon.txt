Title: Alesha Popovich and Tugarin the Dragon (2004)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Drama
Director: Konstantin Bronzit
Main Cast: Oleg Kulikovich, Lia Medvedeva, Dimitri Vysotsky, Anatoli Petrov, Natalya Danilova
Keywords: ukraine, russia, fairy tale, legend, musical, quiz, sword fight, gold coin, speaking horse

Overview: Alesha Popovich has to catch Tugarin Zmey and bring back the stolen money with the help of a talking Horse (which talks all the time and has an opinion on everything), a wise granny, a donkey and a beauty Lyubava.
