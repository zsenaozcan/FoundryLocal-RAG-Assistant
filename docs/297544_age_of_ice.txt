Title: Age of Ice (2014)
Genres: Science Fiction, Adventure
Director: Emile Edwin Smith
Main Cast: Barton Bund, Bailey Spry, Owais Ahmed, Joe Cipriano, Jules Hartley
Keywords: egypt, ice, earthquake, snow, catastrophe

Overview: Massive earthquakes open the Arabian tectonic plate, resulting in unstable weather and freezing temperatures that will be unsurvivable by nightfall. Attempting to reach safety, a vacationing family in Egypt must battle the rapidly cooling temperatures that usher in a new Ice Age, covering the Sphinx, Pyramids, and Sahara Desert with mountains of snow.
