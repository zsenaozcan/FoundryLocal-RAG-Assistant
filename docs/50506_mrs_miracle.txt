Title: Mrs. Miracle (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Romance, TV Movie
Director: Michael Scott
Main Cast: Erin Karpluk, James Van Der Beek, Doris Roberts, Michael Strusievici, Valin Shinyei

Overview: Overwhelmed widower Seth Webster is searching for a housekeeper to help him with his unruly six year old twin sons. "Mrs. Miracle" mysteriously appears and quickly becomes an irreplaceable nanny, chef, friend... and matchmaker.
