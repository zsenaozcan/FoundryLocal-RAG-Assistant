Title: A Man Called Sledge (1970)
Genres: Western
Director: Giorgio Gentili
Main Cast: James Garner, Dennis Weaver, Claude Akins, John Marley, Laura Antonelli
Keywords: spaghetti western

Overview: James Garner is Luther Sledge, the leader of a pack of rebels who are planning to steal a stash of gold. But after the thieves actually manage to get away with the bounty, they soon discover that the enemy lies within their midst. As they begin to bicker over who should get the biggest cut, the stage is set for a deadly showdown. Claude Akins and John Marley co-star in this Italian Western directed by Vic Morrow.
