Title: The Bengali Night (1988)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nicolas Klotz
Main Cast: Hugh Grant, Shabana Azmi

Overview: Allan (Hugh Grant) is an engineer working in 1930s Calcutta. He is invited to stay with the family of his boss, Narendra Sen (Soumitra Chatterjee) which includes his wife, Indira (Shabana Azmi) and daughter Gayatri (Supriya Pathak). Gayatri and Allan become romantically involved leading to tragedy.
