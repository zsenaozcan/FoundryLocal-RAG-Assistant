Title: The Go-Getter (2007)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Martin Hynes
Main Cast: Lou Taylor Pucci, Zooey Deschanel, Jena Malone, Giuseppe Andrews, Kathleen M. Darcy
Keywords: brother brother relationship, car thief, loss of mother, mobile phone, oregon, disappearance, stolen cars

Overview: When his mother dies, a teenager takes a road-trip in a stolen car to find his long-lost brother. Along the way he discovers a profound connection with the car-owner and with himself as well.
