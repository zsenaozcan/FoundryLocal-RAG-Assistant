Title: Cool Cat Saves the Kids (2015)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime, Drama, Family, Fantasy, Music, Thriller
Director: Derek Savage
Main Cast: Vivica A. Fox, Erik Estrada, Derek Savage, Jason Johnston, Connor Dean
Keywords: based on novel, graffiti, bullying, surrealism, copyright, furry, breaking the fourth wall, experimental film, mental illness, school shooting

Overview: Cool Cat is the coolest cat in town. All the kids love him. Except for the bully Butch, who hates Cool Cat for his coolness. He terrorizes Cool Cat, his friends and family with threats, taunting, and the internet. Can Cool Cat face his fears and defeat the bully Derrick.
