Title: Winter Break (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Marni Banack
Main Cast: Milo Ventimiglia, Eddie Kaye Thomas, Justin Urich, Eddie Mills, Sean Smith
Keywords: sport

Overview: Viscerally experience a "year off" in Aspen, Colorado with a group of recent college grads who have elected to defer graduate school and career opportunities to ski, snow-board, climb, party, fall in love and basically live life to its absolute fullest out in this spectacular mountain town.
