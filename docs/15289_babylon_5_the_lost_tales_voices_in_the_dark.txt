Title: Babylon 5: The Lost Tales - Voices in the Dark (2007)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Science Fiction
Director: J. Michael Straczynski
Main Cast: Bruce Boxleitner, Peter Woodward, Alan Scarfe, Teryl Rothery, Keegan MacIntosh
Keywords: babylon

Overview: This latest installment of Babylon 5 is in two parts: the first, is about an entity that has possessed a worker. It wants to be exorcised but Col. Lochley prevents the priest &amp; sends it &amp; the host both back to Earth. The second is about Galen trying to dupe President Sheridan into assassinating the future Centauri President to stop him possibly attacking Earth in 30 years time.
