Title: Future Kick (1991)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Damian Klaus
Main Cast: Don Wilson, Meg Foster, Chris Penn, Eb Lottimer, Al Ruscio

Overview: On Earth in the future Don "The Dragon" Wilson takes on a sinister corporation that trades in black market human body parts. He single handedly manages to restore law and order in a motion picture tour de force guaranteed to thrill science fiction and martial arts fans alike.
