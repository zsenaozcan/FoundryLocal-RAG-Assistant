Title: Spiral (2000)
Genres: Mystery, Fantasy, Horror
Director: Higuchinsky
Main Cast: Eriko Hatsune, Fhi Fan, Hinako Saeki, Shin Eun-Kyung, Keiko Takahashi
Keywords: helix, dying and death, business man, village, laundromat, based on manga, death, anime

Overview: In a small town in Japan, Kirie comes upon her boyfriend's father silently videotaping a snail. Later, the mans obsession with spirals becomes more and more bizarre, ending in his suicide in a washing machine which turn his body into a spiral. Soon other inhabitants become possessed with different forms of spirals.
