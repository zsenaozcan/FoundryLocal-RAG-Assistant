Title: Jonny Vang (2003)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jens Lien
Main Cast: Aksel Hennie, Laila Goody, Fridtjov Såheim, Marit Andreassen, Bjørn Sundquist
Keywords: bygda, norsk

Overview: Three friends since childhood are trying to work out their complicated relationships. Jonny tries to be best friends with Magnus, Magnus tries to be married to Tuva, and Tuva tries to have sex with Jonny.
