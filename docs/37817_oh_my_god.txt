Title: Oh My God! (2007)
Genres: Music, Comedy
Director: Monique Gardenberg
Main Cast: Lázaro Ramos, Dira Paes, Wagner Moura, Stênio Garcia, Luciana Souza

Overview: During the Carnival in the historical site of Pelourinho (Salvador, Bahia, Brazil), we follow the lives of the tenants of a falling-to-pieces tenement house who try to get by using creativity, irony, humor and music.
