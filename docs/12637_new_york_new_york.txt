Title: New York, New York (1977)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance
Director: Martin Scorsese
Main Cast: Liza Minnelli, Robert De Niro, Lionel Stander, Barry Primus, Dick Miller
Keywords: new york, jazz, saxophonist, falling in love
