Title: Right Cross (1950)
Genres: Drama
Director: John Sturges
Main Cast: June Allyson, Dick Powell, Ricardo Montalban, Lionel Barrymore, Teresa Celli
Keywords: boxer, irish american, fight manager

Overview: A sportswriter (Dick Powell) forms a ring triangle with a fight manager's daughter (June Allyson) and her Mexican-American boxer (Ricardo Montalban).
