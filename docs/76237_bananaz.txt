Title: Bananaz (2008)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Ceri Levy
Main Cast: Damon Albarn, Jamie Hewlett

Overview: A documentary on the cryptic cartoon band, Gorillaz. From 2000-2006 director Ceri Levy filmed the creators behind the scenes, revealing the first drawings, animations and music, and following the evolution of the group through to the album Demon Days.
