Title: Silent Tongue (1993)
Genres: Western, Drama, Horror
Director: Sam Shepard
Main Cast: Alan Bates, Richard Harris, River Phoenix, Dermot Mulroney, Tantoo Cardinal
Keywords: tree, wife, sister, grief, native american, corpse

Overview: Mad with grief after the death of his Kiowa wife, Roe awaits death under a tree with her body beside him. She begins to haunt him because he won't bury her. His father, who bought him the wife, thinks her sister might reason with Roe.
