Title: Live Once, Die Twice (2006)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Stefan Pleszczynski
Main Cast: Kellie Martin, Martin Cummins, Cindy Sampson, Bruce Gray, Millie Tresierra

Overview: Nicole Lauker unexpectedly loses her husband to a boating explosion accident that claims the lives of two people. An FBI investigation sparks speculation that it may not have been an accident after all.
