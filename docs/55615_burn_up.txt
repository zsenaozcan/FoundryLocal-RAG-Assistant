Title: Burn Up! (1991)
Genres: Animation
Director: Yasunori Ide
Main Cast: Kumiko Nishihara, Yumiko Shibata, Hirokazu Hiramatsu, Norio Wakamoto, Shozo Iizuka

Overview: A group of young police officers infiltrate a sleazy night club in an attempt to apprehend a man named McCoy, who is involved in kidnapping and white slavery. The plot twists when one of the officers is kidnapped!
