Title: Cold Prey (2006)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Roar Uthaug
Main Cast: Ingrid Bolsø Berdal, Viktoria Winge, Rolf Kristian Larsen, Tomas Alf Larsen, Endre Martin Midtstigen
Keywords: hotel, mountain, snowboarding, psychopathic killer

Overview: Jannicke, Morten Tobias, Eirik, Mikael and Ingunn are on a snowboarding vacation in Jotunheimen. They are forced to take shelter in an abandoned hotel when Morten Tobias breaks his leg and their car is too far away for them to reach within nightfall. They quickly discover that the hotel was closed in the seventies due to the disappearance of the managers’ son.
