Title: China Moon (1994)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller
Director: John Bailey
Main Cast: Ed Harris, Madeleine Stowe, Charles Dance, Patricia Healy, Benicio del Toro
Keywords: neo-noir

Overview: Detective Kyle Bodine falls for Rachel Munro who is trapped in a violent marriage. After shooting her husband, Kyle relucantly agrees to help hide the body, but Kyle's partner is showing an unusual flair for finding clues.
