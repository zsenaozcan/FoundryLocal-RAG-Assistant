Title: East, West, East: The Final Sprint (2009)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Gjergj Xhuvani
Main Cast: Ndriçim Xhepa, Helidon Fino, Gentiano Hazizi, Oni Pustina, Lulzim Zeqja

Overview: An amateur cycling team from Albania heads to France to take part in a tournament only to discover on the way that revolution has broken out back home.
