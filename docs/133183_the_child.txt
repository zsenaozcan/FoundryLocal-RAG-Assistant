Title: The Child (2012)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Zsolt Bács
Main Cast: Eric Roberts, Ben Becker, Christian Traeumer, Sunny Mabrey, Peter Greene

Overview: Simon Sachs, a terminally ill ten year old boy, claims to have been a serial killer in his previous life and apparently he can prove it. He leads a lawyer, Robert Stern to the locations of the bodies, who were killed long before Simon was born. Stern takes on the most compelling and mysterious case of his life.
