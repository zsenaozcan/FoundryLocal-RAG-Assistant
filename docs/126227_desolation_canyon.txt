Title: Desolation Canyon (2006)
Genres: Western
Director: David S. Cass Sr.
Main Cast: Stacy Keach, Patrick Duffy, Kelly Overton, David Rees Snell, Kenny Johnson
Keywords: bounty hunter, bank robbery, posse

Overview: Following a bank robbery, the responsible gang stops by the home of one of their members and kidnaps his son. The sheriff enlists the aid of a retired gunfighter, who is the boy's grandfather. On the gang's trail, they find there are two bounty hunters also after the gang for crimes in Mexico.
