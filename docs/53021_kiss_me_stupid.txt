Title: Kiss Me, Stupid (1964)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Billy Wilder
Main Cast: Dean Martin, Kim Novak, Ray Walston, Felicia Farr, Cliff Osmond
Keywords: piano teacher, playboy

Overview: A jealous piano teacher Orville Spooner sends his beautiful wife, Zelda, away for the night while he tries to sell a song to a famous nightclub singer Dino, who is stranded in town
