Title: The Girl Said No (1930)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Sam Wood
Main Cast: William Haines, Leila Hyams, Marie Dressler, Polly Moran, Clara Blandick
Keywords: bank, secretary, american football hero

Overview: A comedy romance in which breezy Haines, as a young lady killer, tries to capture the heart of Hyams who has turned him down for Bushman.  Haines plots dozens of extreme measures to win her over, and finally goes so far as to drag her from the altar, bound and gagged.
