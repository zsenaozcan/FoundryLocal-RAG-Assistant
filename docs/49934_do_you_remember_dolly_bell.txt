Title: Do You Remember Dolly Bell? (1981)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Emir Kusturica
Main Cast: Slavko Štimac, Slobodan Aligrudić, Ljiljana Blagojević, Pavle Vuisić, Mira Banjac
Keywords: sarajevo, coming of age, boy band, 1960s

Overview: A young man grows up in Sarajevo in the 1960s, under the shadow of his good, but ailing father, and gets attracted by the world of small-time criminals.
