Title: Angels of Revolution (2014)
Genres: Romance, Drama, History
Director: Aleksey Fedorchenko
Main Cast: Darya Ekamasova, Pavel Basov, Konstantin Balakirev, Oleg Yagodin

Overview: Five friends – a poet, an actor, a painter, an architect and a primitivist film director – are five red avant-garde artists who try to find the embodiment of their hopes and dreams in the young Soviet state. The Revolution is boiling up like a bottle with apple cider: winged service dogs and heart-shaped potatoes, dead Semashko, the People’s Commissar for Health, and cheerful angels, love for the Tsar and love for the young secretary Annushka, executions and pregnancies – everything is interlaced and inseparable!
