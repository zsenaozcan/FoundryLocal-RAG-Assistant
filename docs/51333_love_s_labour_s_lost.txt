Title: Love's Labour's Lost (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Kenneth Branagh
Main Cast: Alessandro Nivola, Alicia Silverstone, Natascha McElhone, Kenneth Branagh, Richard Briers

Overview: An update of the classic Shakespeare story, director Kenneth Branagh shot this movie like a classic 30s musical. Love's Labour's Lost tells the story of four best friends who swear off love. The King of Navarre and his three companions swear a very public oath to study together and to renounce women for three years. Their honour is immediately put to the test by the arrival of the Princess of France and her three lovely companions. It's love at first sight for all concerned followed by the men's highly entertaining but hopeless efforts to disguise their feelings.
