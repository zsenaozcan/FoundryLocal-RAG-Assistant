Title: Style (2001)
Genres: Foreign, Thriller
Director: N. Chandra
Main Cast: Sharman Joshi, Sahil Khan, Riya Sen, Shillpi Sharma, Shakti Kapoor

Overview: Style is a 2001 Bollywood low-budget film mostly regarded as a forgettable romantic-comedy caper by critics. Even though the movie was greatly criticized, it was a commercial success.
