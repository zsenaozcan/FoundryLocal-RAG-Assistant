Title: When a Man Loves (1927)
Genres: History, Romance
Director: Alan Crosland
Main Cast: John Barrymore, Dolores Costello, Warner Oland, Sam De Grasse, Holmes Herbert
Keywords: courtesan, silent film, 18th century, priesthood

Overview: A nobleman studying for the priesthood abandons his vocation in 18th Century France when he falls in love with a beautiful, but reluctant, courtesan.
