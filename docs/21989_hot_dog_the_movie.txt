Title: Hot Dog... The Movie (1984)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Peter Markle
Main Cast: David Naughton, Shannon Tweed, Patrick Houser, Tracy Smith, John Patrick Reger
Keywords: female nudity, sport, party, skiing, ski

Overview: When a hopeful young American hot-dogger goes pole-to-pole with an arrogant Austrian pro, the snow really starts to fly! But as hot as it is on the mountain, it gets even hotter off when the pro's ex-girlfriend (Tweed) sets her eyes on the new blood. Who'll win the competition and the girl? Only a race to end all races can determine which skier can really cut the mustard!
