Title: Gone to Earth (1950)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Emeric Pressburger
Main Cast: David Farrar, Cyril Cusack, Sybil Thorndike, Edward Chapman, Esmond Knight

Overview: Jennifer Jones plays Hazel Woods, a beautiful young English Gypsey girl who loves animals and in particular her pet fox. She is hotly desired by Jack Reddin (David Farrar) a fox hunting squire who vies for her affection and pursues her even after her marriage to the local pastor.
