Title: Hi, Mom! (1970)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Brian De Palma
Main Cast: Robert De Niro, Jennifer Salt, Allen Garfield, Charles Durning, Lara Parker
Keywords: male nudity, pornography, nudity, black panthers, sequel, independent film, new york city, peeping tom

Overview: Vietnam vet John Rubin returns to New York and rents a rundown flat in Greenwhich Village. It is in this flat that he begins to film, 'Peeping Tom' style, the people in the apartment across the street. His obsession with making films leads him to fall in with a radical 'Black Power' group, which in turn leads him to carry out a bizarre act of urban terrorism.
