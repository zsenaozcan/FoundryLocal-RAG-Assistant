Title: Sir Arne's Treasure (1919)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Mauritz Stiller
Main Cast: Erik Stocklassa, Bror Berger, Richard Lund, Axel Nilsson, Hjalmar Selander
Keywords: silent film

Overview: Three Scottish officers, including Sir Archi, murder Sir Arne and his household for a coffin filled with gold. The only survivor is Elsalill, who moves to relatives in Marstrand. There she meets a charming young officer - Sir Archi, and she soon understands that he was one of the murderers. She is in a classic dilemma between her love for Sir Archi and justice.
