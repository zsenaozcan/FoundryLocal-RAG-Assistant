Title: Volcano (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rúnar Rúnarsson
Main Cast: Theodór Júlíusson, Margrét Helga Jóhannsdóttir, Auður Drauma Bachmann, Þorsteinn Bachmann, Kristín Davíðsdóttir

Overview: Hannes is an old man who has grown apart from his children. Recently retired when his wife gets ill he tries to reconcile with them and to atone for his cold demeanor in the past.
