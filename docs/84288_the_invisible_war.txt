Title: The Invisible War (2012)
Genres: Crime, Drama, History, Documentary
Director: Kirby Dick
Main Cast: Kori Cioca, Jessica Hinves, Ariana Klay, Elle Helmer, Hannah Sewell
Keywords: post traumatic stress  disorder, rape, sexism, rape victim, family, misogyny, injustice, sexual assault

Overview: An investigative and powerfully emotional documentary about the epidemic of rape of soldiers within the US military, the institutions that perpetuate and cover up its existence, and its profound personal and social consequences.
