Title: The New York Ripper (1982)
Genres: Horror
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Jack Hedley, Almanta Suska, Renato Rossini, Andrea Occhipinti, Alexandra Delli Colli
Keywords: new york, female nudity, detective, razor, cut-off hand, gore, giallo, violence

Overview: A burned-out New York police detective teams up with a college psychoanalyst to track down a vicious serial killer randomly stalking and killing various young women around the city.
