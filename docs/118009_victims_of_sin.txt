Title: Victims of Sin (1951)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Emilio Fernández
Main Cast: Ninón Sevilla, Tito Junco, Rodolfo Acosta, Rita Montaner, Ismael Pérez
Keywords: prison, dancer, mexico city, pimp, cabaret, sacrifice, murder, melodrama, poverty, prostitution

Overview: In México City, a Cuban dancer from "Cabaret Changó" rescues a baby from a garbage can and decides to raise him, but her pachuco pimp gets in her way.
