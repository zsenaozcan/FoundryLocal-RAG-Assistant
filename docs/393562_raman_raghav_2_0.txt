Title: Raman Raghav 2.0 (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Crime
Director: Anurag Kashyap
Main Cast: Nawazuddin Siddiqui, Vicky Kaushal, Sobhita Dhulipala, Vipin Sharma, Amruta Subhash
Keywords: psychopathic killer, serial killer, murder investigation, inspired by a true story

Overview: Set in present day Mumbai the story follows the life of a serial killer Ramanna who is inspired by an infamous serial killer from the 1960s Raman Raghav. His strange obsession with Raghavan, a young Cop keeps growing as he closely follows him without his knowledge and often creates situations where both of them come face to face.
