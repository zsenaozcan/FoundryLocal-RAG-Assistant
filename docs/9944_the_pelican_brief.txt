Title: The Pelican Brief (1993)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller, Crime
Director: Alan J. Pakula
Main Cast: Julia Roberts, Denzel Washington, Sam Shepard, William Atherton, John Lithgow
Keywords: judge, professor, mission of murder, supreme court
