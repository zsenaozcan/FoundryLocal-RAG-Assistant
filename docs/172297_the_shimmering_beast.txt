Title: The Shimmering Beast (1982)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Pierre Perrault
Main Cast: Philippe Cross, Louis-Philippe Lécuyer, Stéphane-Albert Boulais, Maurice Chaillot, Bernard L'Heureux
Keywords: quebec, moose, hunting trip

Overview: A documentary film about a group of hunters who gather annually to hunt moose near Maniwaki, Quebec.
