Title: Bastards (2006)
Genres: Action, Drama, War
Director: Aleksandr Atanesyan
Main Cast: Andrei Panin, Andrey Krasko, Aleksandr Golovin, Vladimir Kashpur, Sergey Perelygin

Overview: A "Hitlerjugend" kind of story, set in the Soviet Union during the Second World War, based on a fictitious story from the eponymous book by Vladimir Kunin.
