Title: We'll Never Have Paris (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Simon Helberg
Main Cast: Melanie Lynskey, Maggie Grace, Simon Helberg, Zachary Quinto, Jason Ritter
Keywords: paris, new york, florist, engagement, break-up, semi autobiographical, based on true events, woman director, french family
