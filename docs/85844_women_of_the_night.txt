Title: Women of the Night (1948)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Thriller
Director: Kenji Mizoguchi
Main Cast: Kinuyo Tanaka, Sanae Takasugi, Kumeko Urabe, Tomie Tsunoda, Mitsuo Nagata
Keywords: prostitution, post war japan

Overview: Fusako, a drug dealer's young mistress in postwar Japan, loses her tenuous grasp on life upon learning about her lover's affair.
