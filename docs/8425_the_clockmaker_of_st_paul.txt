Title: The Clockmaker of St. Paul (1974)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Crime
Director: Bertrand Tavernier
Main Cast: Philippe Noiret, Jean Rochefort, Jacques Denis, Yves Afonso, Julien Bertheau
Keywords: paris, father son relationship, 1970s, kommissar geiger, murder hunt

Overview: Lyons, France. Michel Descombes is a watchmaker who lives alone with his teenage son Bernard. When the police visits and informs him that Bernard killed a man and is on the run with a girl, Michel realizes that he knew far less about his son than he thought .
