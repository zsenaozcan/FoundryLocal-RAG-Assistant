Title: Better This World (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Kelly Duane
Main Cast: Scott Crow, Brandon Darby, Matthew Vadum
Keywords: texas, political incompetence, republican party, political, woman director

Overview: Set amid arrests and subsequent trials surrounding the 2008 Republican National Convention, this portrait of two young activists caught in the web of an opportunistic mentor and a desperate justice system poignantly describes both the problems of power and the power of forgiveness and love.
