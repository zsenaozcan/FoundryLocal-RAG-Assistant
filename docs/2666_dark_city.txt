Title: Dark City (1998)
Genres: Mystery, Science Fiction
Director: Alex Proyas
Main Cast: Rufus Sewell, William Hurt, Kiefer Sutherland, Jennifer Connelly, Richard O'Brien
Keywords: soul, sleep, manipulation, future, dystopia, chaos, memory, duel, parallel world, tech noir

Overview: A man struggles with memories of his past, including a wife he cannot remember, in a nightmarish world with no sun and run by beings with telekinetic powers who seek the souls of humans.
