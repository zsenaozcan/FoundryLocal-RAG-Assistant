Title: Pork Pie (2017)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Crime
Director: Matt Murphy
Main Cast: Dean O'Gorman, James Rolleston, Ashleigh Cummings, Antonia Prebble, Matt Whelan
Keywords: new zealand, protest, media, police, remake, pursuit, lost love, road movie, mini cooper, escapade

Overview: This remake of 1981’s Goodbye Pork Pie tracks the escapades of a trio of accidental outlaws as they travel the length of New Zealand protesting conformity and chasing lost love, with a posse of cops and a media frenzy in pursuit.
