Title: Mission Bloody Mary (1965)
Genres: Adventure
Director: Sergio Grieco
Main Cast: Ken Clark, Helga Liné, Philippe Hersent, Mitsouko, Umberto Raho
Keywords: spy, secret agent, 077

Overview: Agent 077 - Mission Bloody Mary or Agente 077 missione Bloody Mary is a 1965 Italian action spy adventure film. The first of the Secret Agent 077 film series directed by Sergio Grieco.
