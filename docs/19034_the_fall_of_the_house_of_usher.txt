Title: The Fall of the House of Usher (1981)
Genres: Mystery, Animation, Horror
Director: Jan Švankmajer
Main Cast: Petr Čepek

Overview: In this animated version of Edgar Allan Poe's story, a traveller arrives at the Usher mansion to find that the sibling inhabitants are living under a mysterious family curse: The brother's senses have become painfully acute, while his sister has become nearly catatonic.
