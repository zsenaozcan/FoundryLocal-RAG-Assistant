Title: The Girl from the Naked Eye (2012)
Genres: Action, Romance, Thriller
Director: David Ren
Main Cast: Jason Yee, Samantha Streets, Gary Stretch, Dominique Swain, Ron Yuan

Overview: Jake is a driver for a seedy escort service operating out of 'The Naked Eye strip club', he's a street thug type who falls for a witty high-class escort named Sandy. Except one night Sandy is found murdered, the only clues left behind are cell phone calls made the night she died. To avenge Sandy's death Jake must risk everything and walk a bloody path to find her killer.
