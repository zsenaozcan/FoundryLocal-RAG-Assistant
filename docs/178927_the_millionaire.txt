Title: The Millionaire (1931)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: John G. Adolfi
Main Cast: George Arliss, Florence Arliss, David Manners, Evalyn Knapp, James Cagney
Keywords: garage

Overview: A millionaire automaker retires upon the advice of his doctor, but becomes so bored he buys half interest in a gas station and works it on the sly.
