Title: Valley of the Dolls (1967)
Genres: Music, Drama, Romance
Director: Mark Robson
Main Cast: Barbara Parkins, Patty Duke, Sharon Tate, Paul Burke, Tony Scotti
Keywords: based on novel, musical, drug, cult classic

Overview: Film version of Jacqueline Susann's best-selling novel chronicling the rise and fall of three young ladies in show business.
