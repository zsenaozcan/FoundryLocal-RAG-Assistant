Title: Amore (1948)
Genres: Drama
Director: Roberto Rossellini
Main Cast: Anna Magnani, Federico Fellini, Lia Corelli
Keywords: love, anthology

Overview: An Italian anthology film directed by Roberto Rossellini starring Anna Magnani and Federico Fellini. The film has two parts: "Una Voce Umana" ("The Human Voice") and "Il Miracolo" ("The Miracle").
