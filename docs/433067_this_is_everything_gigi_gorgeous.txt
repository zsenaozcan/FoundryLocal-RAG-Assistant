Title: This Is Everything: Gigi Gorgeous (2017)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Barbara Kopple
Main Cast: Gigi Lazzarato
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A groundbreaking film that portrays the journey of Gigi Lazzarato, a fearless woman who began life as Gregory, posting fashion videos to YouTube from his bedroom, only to later come out as a transgender female. With never-before-seen personal footage, the film spotlights a family’s unwavering love for a child.
