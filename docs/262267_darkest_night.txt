Title: Darkest Night (2012)
Genres: Horror, Drama
Director: Noel Tan
Main Cast: DJ Perry, Anne Gauthier, Issa Litton, Nic Campos, Jill Palencia
Keywords: asia, night, black magic, gothic horror, dark, religious cult, found footage
