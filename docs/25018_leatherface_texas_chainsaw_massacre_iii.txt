Title: Leatherface: Texas Chainsaw Massacre III (1990)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Jeff Burr
Main Cast: Kate Hodge, Ken Foree, R.A. Mihailoff, William Butler, Viggo Mortensen
Keywords: sequel, leatherface, death

Overview: A couple encounters a perverted gas station attendant who threatens them with a shotgun. They take a deserted path in Texas to seek help, but only meet up with a cannibalistic clan interested in helping themselves to fresh meat.
