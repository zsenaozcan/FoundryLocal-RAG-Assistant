Title: A Little Bit Zombie (2012)
Genres: Science Fiction, Comedy, Horror
Director: Casey Walker
Main Cast: Stephen McHattie, Kristopher Turner, Crystal Lowe, Shawn Roberts, Emilie Ullerup
Keywords: zombie

Overview: Infected by a virus, a mild mannered HR manager attempts to fulfill his overwhelming desire for brains, all while trying to keep it together so as not to incur the wrath of his bridezilla-to-be.
