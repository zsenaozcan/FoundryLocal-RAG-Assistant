Title: Not Forgotten (2009)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Dror Soref
Main Cast: Simon Baker, Paz Vega, Michael DeLorenzo, Chloë Grace Moretz, Ken Davitian
Keywords: female nudity, kidnapping, strip club, little girl, soccer, abduction

Overview: Set in a Tex-Mex border town, Not Forgotten is a classic psychological thriller about Jack and Amaya Bishop, a couple who must come to terms with their tortured pasts in order to save their kidnapped daughter. It is a tale taut with intrigue and steeped in Latino mysticism, where the line between what's real and what's imagined becomes hopelessly blurred.
