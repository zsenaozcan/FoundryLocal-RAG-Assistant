Title: Dirty Sanchez: The Movie (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jim Hickey
Main Cast: Lee Dainton, Matthew Pritchard, Mike Locke, Dan Joyce, Jaki Numazawa
Keywords: stunts

Overview: The Welsh nutters, Dirty Sanchez are making a movie: The concept of the film is that the Devil has heard about the dare devil nature of the Sanchez boys so he's set them the ultimate challenge, to complete stunts based on the 7 Deadly Sins or be damned to the gutter forever.
