Title: A Fighter's Blues (2000)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Daniel Lee
Main Cast: Andy Lau, Takako Tokiwa
Keywords: sport, muay thai

Overview: Before he went exclusively style-over-substance, Daniel Lee directed fighting drama A FIGHTER’S BLUES, about an aging boxer (Andy Lau, in a mature and underrated performance) seeking a shot at redemption. Co-starring Japanese drama queen Takako Tokiwa. Billed as Andy Lau’s 100th film. He would go on to make a whole lot more.
