Title: They Are Among Us (2004)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller, TV Movie
Director: Jeffrey Obrow
Main Cast: Alison Eastwood, Michael DiLallo, Bruce Boxleitner, Corbin Bernsen, Nana Visitor
Keywords: alien monster

Overview: The film concerns a sleepy town that is populated mostly by aliens who have been pretending to be humans for a lengthy period of time in order to eventually strip the planet of resources they need to live.
