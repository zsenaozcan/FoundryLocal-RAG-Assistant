Title: The Alchemist Cookbook (2016)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: Joel Potrykus
Main Cast: Ty Hickson, Amari Cheatom, Fiji
Keywords: hermit, chemist, ancient mystery, delusions, mania

Overview: Self-made chemist Sean, a recluse living in an old trailer in the woods, suffers from pill-popping delusions of fortune. When his manic attempts at cracking the ancient secret of alchemy go awry he unleashes something far more sinister and dangerous.
