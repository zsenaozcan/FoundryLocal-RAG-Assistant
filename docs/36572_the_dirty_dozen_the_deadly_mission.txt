Title: The Dirty Dozen: The Deadly Mission (1987)
Genres: War, Action
Director: Lee H. Katzin
Main Cast: Telly Savalas, Ernest Borgnine, Vince Edwards, Bo Svenson, Vince Van Patten
Keywords: nazis, sequel, french resistance, soldier, monastery, tv movie
