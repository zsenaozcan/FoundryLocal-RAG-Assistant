Title: The Day That Lasted 21 Years (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Camilo Tavares
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: brazilian, brazil, documentary, military dictatorship

Overview: Stunning espionage documentary on the US conspiracy that led to the 1964 Brazilian coup d'état. John F. Kennedy and Lyndon Johnson original White House tapes, and CIA Top Secret documents reveal how the US government planned to overthrow Brazilian elected president João Goulart.
