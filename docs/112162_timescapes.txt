Title: TimeScapes (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Tom Lowe
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: space, universe, astronomy

Overview: TimeScapes is the debut film from award-winning cinematographer and director Tom Lowe. The film features stunning slow-motion and timelapse cinematography of the landscapes, people, and wildlife of the American South West. Lowe spent 2 years roaming the Southwest in his Toyota pickup truck shooting the film.
