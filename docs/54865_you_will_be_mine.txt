Title: You Will Be Mine (2009)
Genres: Music, Drama
Director: Sophie Laloy
Main Cast: Judith Davis, Isild Le Besco, Johan Libéreau, Edith Scob, Fabienne Babe
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Marie leaves home to study the piano at the conservatory in Lyons. Through lack of money, she is obliged to share an apartment with Emma, a friend of the family who has lived alone since the death of her father. The two young women develop a strange fascination for one another, which soon develops into an intense mutual need...
