Title: Alamo Bay (1985)
Genres: Drama
Director: Louis Malle
Main Cast: Amy Madigan, Ed Harris, Ho Nguyen, Donald Moffat, Hoang Phuc Nguyen
Keywords: fisherman, shrimp

Overview: A despondent Vietnam veteran in danger of losing his livelihood is pushed to the edge when he sees Vietnamese immigrants moving into the fishing industry in a Texas bay town.
