Title: The Visitors (1988)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jack Ersgard
Main Cast: Kjell Bergqvist, Lena Endre, Johannes Brost, Joanna Berglund, Jonas Olsson
Keywords: isolation, haunted house, spirit

Overview: Frank, Sara and their two children have recently moved into the house of their dreams on the countryside. Frank is disturbed by mysterious sounds and somebody tears down the wallpaper from the walls. He contacts a ghost-hunter, Allan, to find out the truth.
