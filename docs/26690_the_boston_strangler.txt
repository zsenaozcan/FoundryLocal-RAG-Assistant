Title: The Boston Strangler (1968)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Richard Fleischer
Main Cast: Tony Curtis, Henry Fonda, George Kennedy, Mike Kellin, Hurd Hatfield
Keywords: detective, double life, boston, strangle, serial murder

Overview: Boston is being terrorized by a series of seemingly random murders of women. Based on the true story, the film follows the investigators path through several leads before introducing the Strangler as a character. It is seen almost exclusively from the point of view of the investigators who have very few clues to build a case upon.
