Title: Surviving the Game (1994)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Thriller, Crime
Director: Ernest R. Dickerson
Main Cast: Ice-T, Rutger Hauer, Charles S. Dutton, Gary Busey, John C. McGinley
Keywords: hunting human beings

Overview: Mason is a man down on his luck with nothing to lose when he's approached to lead a group of wealthy hunters on an expedition in the Pacific Northwest. But things get really twisted when Mason discovers the group isn't after wild animals -- they're after him.
