Title: House IV (1992)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Lewis Abernathy
Main Cast: Terri Treas, William Katt, Scott Burkholder, Denny Dillon, Melissa Clayton

Overview: Roger Cobb (William Katt) is killed in a car accident. His family must move into the house that has haunted him for several years. Soon the family begins to experience scary and unexplained phenomena.
