Title: 1990: The Bronx Warriors (1982)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Enzo G. Castellari
Main Cast: Christopher Connelly, Fred Williamson, Vic Morrow, Mark Gregory, Stefania Girolami Goodwin

Overview: In a post-apocalyptic New York City, a policeman infiltrates the Bronx, which has become a battleground for several murderous street gangs.
