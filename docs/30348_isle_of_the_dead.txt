Title: Isle of the Dead (1945)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Mark Robson
Main Cast: Boris Karloff, Ellen Drew, Marc Cramer, Katherine Emery, Helene Thimig
Keywords: island, superstition, quarantine, val lewton, plague

Overview: On a Greek island during the 1912 war, several people are trapped by quarantine for the plague. If that isn't enough worry, one of the people, a superstitious old peasant woman, suspects one young girl of being a vampiric kind of demon called a vorvolaka.
