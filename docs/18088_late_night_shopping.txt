Title: Late Night Shopping (2001)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Saul Metzstein
Main Cast: Luke de Woolfson, James Lance, Kate Ashfield, Heike Makatsch, Enzo Cilenti
Keywords: night shift, road trip, cafe

Overview: Four friends Sean, Vincent, Lenny and Jody find themselves at something of a deadend. Trapped in a twilight world of permanent night shift work, they hang out together in the local cafe, drinking coffee and entertaining themselves by observing Vincent's unwavering success in pulling women. There seems to be little prospect of change...until Vincent accidently sleeps with Sean's girlfriend.
