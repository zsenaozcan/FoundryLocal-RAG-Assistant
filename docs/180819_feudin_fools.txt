Title: Feudin' Fools (1952)
Genres: Comedy
Director: William Beaudine
Main Cast: Leo Gorcey, Huntz Hall, Dorothy Ford, Lyle Talbot, Benny Baker
Keywords: bowery boys

Overview: Sach discovers that he is heir to a farm in rural hillbilly country. He and the boys go to the farm to check it out, and find themselves mixed up with feuding hillbillies and a gang of bank robbers.
