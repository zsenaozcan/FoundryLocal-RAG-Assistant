Title: One Night at Dinner (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Giuseppe Patroni Griffi
Main Cast: Jean-Louis Trintignant, Lino Capolicchio, Tony Musante, Florinda Bolkan, Annie Girardot

Overview: A liberal-thinking author watches his wife as she attempts to seduce his best friend at a dinner party. She ends up taking on another man as well, and the writer has an affair with the another dinner guest. Soon the three men and two women are entangled in a confusing series of partner-swapping sex sprees where everyone's morals are challenged in the wake of the sexual revolution.
