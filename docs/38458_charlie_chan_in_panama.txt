Title: Charlie Chan in Panama (1940)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller, Mystery, Comedy
Director: Norman Foster
Main Cast: Sidney Toler, Jean Rogers, Lionel Atwill, Mary Nash, Victor Sen Yung
Keywords: charlie chan

Overview: Charlie impersonates an employee of the U.S. government to foil an espionage plot which would destroy part of the Panama Canal, trapping a Navy fleet on its way to the Pacific after maneuvers in the Atlantic.
