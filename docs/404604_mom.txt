Title: Mom (2017)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Ravi Udyawar
Main Cast: Sridevi Kapoor, Sajal Ali, Akshaye Khanna, Nawazuddin Siddiqui, Abhimanyu Singh
Keywords: physical abuse, sexual assault

Overview: The bliss of a biology teacher’s family life in Delhi is shattered when her daughter, Arya  is physically assaulted by Jagan and gang. Does Devki Sabarwal wait for the law to take its course? Or does Devki become Maa Durga and hunt down the perpetrators of the crime?
