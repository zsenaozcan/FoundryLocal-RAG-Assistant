Title: Xanadu (1980)
Genres: Fantasy, Music, Romance
Director: Robert Greenwald
Main Cast: Olivia Newton-John, Gene Kelly, Michael Beck, James Sloyan, Katie Hanley
Keywords: musical, roller-skating, camp, disco, greek god
