Title: Lisbon Story (1994)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Thriller
Director: Wim Wenders
Main Cast: Rüdiger Vogler, Patrick Bauchau, Vasco Sequeira, Joel Cunha Ferreira, Sofia Bénard da Costa
Keywords: upper class, city portrait, film business, film making, famous score, lisbon, life planning, camera, car journey, car breakdown

Overview: Lisbon Story is Wim Wender’s homage to Lisbon and films. A sound engineer obtains a mysterous postcard of a friend who at the moment is filming a film in Lisbon. He sets out across Europe to find him and help him.
