Title: Second String (2002)
Genres: TV Movie, Comedy, Drama
Director: Robert Lieberman
Main Cast: Jon Voight, Gil Bellows, Teri Polo, Richard T. Jones, Garcelle Beauvais
Keywords: sport

Overview: American football coach Chuck Dichter has worked wonders with the Buffalo Bills, and is even confident to crown his career with a Super Bowl victory, but before the play-offs an oyster food-poisoning wipes out his first team for a month. Dan Heller, an insurance salesman and former college quarterback, who was hired -relactantly, but his wife twisted his arm- just for practice, now has to captain and train a bunch of rookies and old-timers against the hardest adversaries. Dichter decides to sign up triple Super Bowl-winner Tommy Baker in Dan's place...
