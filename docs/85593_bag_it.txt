Title: Bag It (2011)
Genres: Documentary, Comedy
Director: Suzan Beraza
Main Cast: Jeb Berrier, Jared Blumenfeld
Keywords: woman director

Overview: An average guy makes a resolution to stop using plastic bags at the grocery store. Little does he know that this simple decision will change his life completely. He comes to the conclusion that our consumptive use of plastic has finally caught up to us, and looks at what we can do about it. Today. Right now.
