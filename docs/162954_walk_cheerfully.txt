Title: Walk Cheerfully (1930)
Genres: Romance, Crime, Drama
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Main Cast: Satoko Date, Kanji Kawahara, Hiroko Kawasaki, Kenji Kimura, Nobuko Matsuzono

Overview: Kenji is a small thief who likes drinking and fighting. When he falls in love with sweet and simple Yazue, and she finds out what kind of guy he really is, she leaves him 'until he becomes an honest person'. But it is not easy to get rid of one's past...
