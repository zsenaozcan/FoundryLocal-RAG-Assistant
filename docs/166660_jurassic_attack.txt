Title: Jurassic Attack (2012)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Anthony Fankhauser
Main Cast: Corin Nemec, Vernon Wells, Gary Stretch, Michael Worth, Natascha Berg

Overview: While returning from a military expedition a helicopter crash lands a commando unit in a dense, remote tropical jungle – a lost world populated by dinosaurs. Now they must find a way out of this isolated valley before becoming prey for prehistoric predators.
