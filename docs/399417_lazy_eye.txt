Title: Lazy Eye (2016)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Tim Kirkman
Main Cast: Lucas Near-Verbrugghe, Aaron Costa Ganis, Michaela Watkins, Harrison Givens, Debbie Jaffe

Overview: Passions re-ignite and secrets revealed when a graphic designer reconnects with the great, lost love of his life for a weekend tryst at a house in the desert near Joshua Tree.
