Title: Saai sau kwong lung (2002)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Nam Yin
Main Cast: Bessie Chan, Nadia Chan, Cheng Chu-Fung, Sammo Hung

Overview: The theme of the film is ethics. Sammo Hung is a retired cop who trains assassins to do the jobs of cops, whom Sammo sees as unable to do the right things. He sends his four students to assassinate drug dealers. They more than complete the job when they kill one of Sammo's old time buddies, played by TVB veteran Sek Sau. These young fellows have only been taught one thing: to kill. They have not been taught WHO to kill. Obviously, that creates some problems as they kill good and bad people.
