Title: Harvie Krumpet (2003)
Genres: Animation, Comedy
Director: Adam Elliot
Main Cast: Geoffrey Rush, Kamahl, John Flaus, Julie Forsyth
Keywords: tourette syndrome

Overview: Harvie Krumpet is the biography of an ordinary man seemingly cursed with perpetual bad luck. From being born with Tourette's Syndrome, to getting struck by lightning; from having his testicle removed to developing Alzheimer's disease; Harvie's troubles seem unending! Yet, Harvie learns many lessons in life and enjoys its many fruits. He finds love, freedom, nudity and ultimately the true meaning.
