Title: Ping Pong Summer (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michael Tully
Main Cast: Susan Sarandon, Lea Thompson, Amy Sedaris, John Hannah, Judah Friedlander
Keywords: ping pong, vacation, independent film

Overview: In 1985 a summer vacation in Ocean City, Md., changes the life of a shy white teen who's obsessed with table tennis and hip-hop music.
