Title: Lightning Over Water (1980)
Genres: Documentary, Foreign
Director: Nicholas Ray
Main Cast: Wim Wenders, Nicholas Ray

Overview: Director 'Nicholas Ray' is eager to complete a final film before his imminent death from cancer. Wim Wenders is working on his own film Hammett (1983) in Hollywood, but flies to New York to help Ray realize his final wish. Ray's original intent is to make a fiction film about a dying painter who sails to China to find a cure for his disease. He and Wenders discuss this idea, but it is obviously unrealistic given Ray's state of health.
