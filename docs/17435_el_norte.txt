Title: El Norte (1983)
Genres: Thriller, Adventure, Drama
Director: Gregory Nava
Main Cast: Zaide Silvia Gutiérrez, David Villalpando, Ernesto Gómez Cruz, Lupe Ontiveros, Trinidad Silva
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Brother and sister Enrique and Rosa flee persecution at home in Guatemala and journey north, through Mexico and on to the United States, with the dream of starting a new life.
