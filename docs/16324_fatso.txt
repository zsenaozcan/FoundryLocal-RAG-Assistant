Title: Fatso (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Arild Fröhlich
Main Cast: Nils Jørgen Kaalstad, Josefin Ljungman, Kyrre Hellum, Jenny Skavlan, Per Kjerstad
Keywords: shower, blind date, beer, vomit, sexual fantasy, car

Overview: Overweight loner Rino spends his days in seclusion, until his dad rents a room to an overtly sexual girl who challenges Rino to break down his walls.
