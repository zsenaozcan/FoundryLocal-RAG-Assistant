Title: Little Women (1949)
Genres: Drama, Family, Romance
Director: Mervyn LeRoy
Main Cast: June Allyson, Peter Lawford, Margaret O'Brien, Elizabeth Taylor, Janet Leigh
Keywords: based on novel, massachusetts, slice of life, tomboy, concord

Overview: Louisa May Alcott's autobiographical account of her life with her three sisters in Concord Mass in the 1860s. With their father fighting in the civil war, the sisters: Jo, Meg, Amy and Beth are at home with their mother - a very outspoken women for her time. The story is of how the sisters grow up, find love and find their place in the world.
