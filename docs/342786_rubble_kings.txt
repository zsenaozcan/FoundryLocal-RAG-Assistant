Title: Rubble Kings (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Shan Nicholson
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: 1970s, ghetto, gang war, gang, youth gang, social commentary, new york city, violence, latino, bronx

Overview: Through archival footage Nicholson tells the story of the real Warriors that walked the streets of New York City in the 1970s and the harsh reality of gang life in a city that seemed to be falling apart.
