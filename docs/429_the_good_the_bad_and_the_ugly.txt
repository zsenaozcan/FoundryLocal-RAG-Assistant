Title: The Good, the Bad and the Ugly (1966)
Genres: Western
Director: Sergio Leone
Main Cast: Eli Wallach, Clint Eastwood, Lee Van Cleef, Aldo Giuffrè, Luigi Pistilli
Keywords: bounty hunter, refugee, gold, anti hero, gallows, hitman, army, outlaw, shootout, moral ambiguity

Overview: While the Civil War rages between the Union and the Confederacy, three men – a quiet loner, a ruthless hit man and a Mexican bandit – comb the American Southwest in search of a strongbox containing $200,000 in stolen gold.
