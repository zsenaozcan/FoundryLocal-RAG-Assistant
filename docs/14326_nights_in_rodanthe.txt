Title: Nights in Rodanthe (2008)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: George C. Wolfe
Main Cast: Diane Lane, Richard Gere, Christopher Meloni, Viola Davis, Becky Ann Baker
Keywords: based on novel, beach, marriage crisis, north carolina, romance, inn, middle age

Overview: Two unhappy people's lives become entwined when they have a life changing romance. Adrienne is a woman who's trying to decide whether to stay in her unhappy marriage or not. Her life changes when Paul, a doctor who is traveling to reconcile with his estranged son, checks into an inn in a North Carolina beach town where she is staying.
