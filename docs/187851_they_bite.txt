Title: They Bite (1996)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Brett Piper
Main Cast: Donna Frotscher, Nick Baldasare, Christina Veronica, Charlie Barnett, Ron Jeremy

Overview: Aliens who've come to earth to spawn deep beneath the ocean begin terrorizing a Florida beach community. An ichthyologist and a young filmmaker team up to uncover the mystery of these monsters from the deep--before they strike again.
