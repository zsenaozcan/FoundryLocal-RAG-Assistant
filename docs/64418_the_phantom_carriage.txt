Title: The Phantom Carriage (1939)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Julien Duvivier
Main Cast: Pierre Fresnay, Louis Jouvet, Micheline Francey, Marie Bell, Ariane Borg

Overview: French version of the Selma Lagerlof story, most famously filmed in 1921 by Victor Sjostrom, about a poor sinner who only realizes what misery he's wrought when he dies on New Year's Eve and is collected by Death in his carriage.
