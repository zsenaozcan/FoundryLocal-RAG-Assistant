Title: Two of a Kind (1983)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Science Fiction, Romance
Director: John Herzfeld
Main Cast: John Travolta, Olivia Newton-John, Charles Durning, Oliver Reed, Scatman Crothers
Keywords: god, male female relationship

Overview: God throws his hands up at the foolishness of humanity and orders the race destroyed. A trio of bleeding-heart angels strike a deal with him: if they can find just two good souls, the Lord will allow Earth to survive. Unfortunately, the angels have staked the future of the species on a thieving bank teller and an inventor/con-artist
