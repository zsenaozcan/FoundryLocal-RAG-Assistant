Title: Carnal Knowledge (1971)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mike Nichols
Main Cast: Jack Nicholson, Candice Bergen, Art Garfunkel, Ann-Margret, Rita Moreno
Keywords: friends, sexual confusion

Overview: The concurrent sexual lives of best friends Jonathan and Sandy are presented, those lives which are affected by the sexual mores of the time and their own temperament, especially in relation to the respective women who end up in their lives.
