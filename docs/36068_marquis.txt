Title: Marquis (1989)
Genres: Animation, Comedy
Director: Henri Xhonneux
Main Cast: François Marthouret, Valérie Kling, Michel Robin, Isabelle Wolfe, Vicky Messica
Keywords: revolution, surreal, anthropomorphism, metaphor

Overview: In pre-French Revolution Bastille, the Marquis is held being unjustly accused of working to overthrow the king. While his talking penis, Colin, longs for action, Marquis himself only desires to write his deviant stories in peace.
