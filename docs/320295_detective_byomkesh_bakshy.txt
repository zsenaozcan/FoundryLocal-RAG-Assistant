Title: Detective Byomkesh Bakshy! (2015)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Dibakar Banerjee
Main Cast: Sushant Singh Rajput, Anand Tiwari, Swastika Mukherjee, Divya Menon, Neeraj Kabi
Keywords: detective, sherlock holmes

Overview: In war torn Calcutta during the 1940's, Byomkesh Bakshy, fresh out of college, pits himself against an evil genius who is out to destroy the world.
