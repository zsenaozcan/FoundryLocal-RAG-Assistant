Title: Driving Me Crazy (1988)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Nick Broomfield
Main Cast: André Heller, Graciela Daniele, Mercedes Ellington, George Faison, Clent Bowers

Overview: Both up-roaringly funny and unerringly cautionary, Broomfield's behind-the-scenes document of the making of a musical becomes a ceremonious unmaking-of as egos, budgets and general calamity conspire to ruin the best efforts of all involved in the New York rehearsals for an extravagant, glitzy production.
