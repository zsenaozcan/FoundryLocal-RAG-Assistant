Title: The Brother from Another Planet (1984)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: John Sayles
Main Cast: Joe Morton, Rosanna Carter, Ray Ramirez, Yves Rene, Ginny Yang
Keywords: black people, allegory, hispanoamerican, alien, independent film, science fiction

Overview: An alien slave crashes lands in New York city while being pursued by two Men in Black bounty hunters. His attempt to find a place for himself on earth parallels that of the immigrant experience.
