Title: Pippi Longstocking (1969)
Genres: Adventure, Family
Director: Olle Hellbom
Main Cast: Inger Nilsson, Pär Sundberg, Maria Persson, Hans Clarin, Paul Esser
Keywords: brother sister relationship, horse, pippi longstocking, villa kunterbunt, monkey

Overview: Pippi Longstocking, a super-strong redheaded little girl, moves into her father's cottage Villa Villekulla, and has adventures with her next-door neighbors Tommy and Annika in this compilation film of the classic Swedish TV series.
