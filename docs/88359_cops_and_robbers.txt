Title: Cops and Robbers (1973)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Aram Avakian
Main Cast: Cliff Gorman, Joseph Bologna, Charlene Dallas
Keywords: new york, cops, mafia, brooklyn new york city

Overview: Two disillusioned New York policemen plan a $10 million robbery to fuel their low pensions, only to run into one debacle after another in the process.
