Title: Heaven Adores You (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Nickolas Dylan Rossi
Main Cast: Jon Brion, Elliott Smith
Keywords: biography, music

Overview: Heaven Adores You is an intimate, meditative inquiry into the life and music of Elliott Smith. By threading the music of Elliott Smith through the dense, yet often isolating landscapes of the three major cities he lived in -- Portland, New York City, Los Angeles -- Heaven Adores You presents a visual journey and an earnest review of the singer's prolific songwriting and the impact it continues to have on fans, friends, and fellow musicians.
