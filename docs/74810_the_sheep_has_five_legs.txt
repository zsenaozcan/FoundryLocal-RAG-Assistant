Title: The Sheep Has Five Legs (1954)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Henri Verneuil
Main Cast: Fernandel, Françoise Arnoul, Louis de Funès, Paulette Dubost

Overview: The Sheep Has Five Legs (French: Le Mouton à cinq pattes) is a 1954 French film directed by Henri Verneuil. It was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Story.
