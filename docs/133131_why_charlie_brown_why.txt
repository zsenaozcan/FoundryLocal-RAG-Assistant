Title: Why, Charlie Brown, Why? (1990)
Genres: Family, Animation, Comedy, Drama
Director: Sam Jaimes
Main Cast: Kaleb Henley, Brandon Stewart, Olivia Burnette, Adrienne Stiefel, Jennifer Banko
Keywords: leukemia, cancer, school, hospital, chemotherapy

Overview: A new girl comes to the neighbourhood school and Linus is smitten with her. Unfortunately, she develops the symptoms of leukemia and must go to the hospital for cancer treatment. Linus and Charlie Brown help her through the traumas of chemotherapy as best they can while Snoopy plays at being the world's greatest doctor.
