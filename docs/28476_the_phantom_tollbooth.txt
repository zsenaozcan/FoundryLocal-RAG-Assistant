Title: The Phantom Tollbooth (1970)
Genres: Fantasy, Animation, Adventure
Director: Abe Levitow
Main Cast: Butch Patrick, Mel Blanc, Daws Butler, Candy Candido, Hans Conried
Keywords: boy, car, toy car, toll booth

Overview: The Phantom Tollbooth, based upon the children's adventure novel by Norton Juster, tells the story of a bored young boy named Milo. Unexpectedly receiving a magic tollbooth and, having nothing better to do, Milo drives through it and enters a kingdom in turmoil following the loss of it's princesses, Rhyme and Reason.
