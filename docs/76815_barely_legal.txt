Title: Barely Legal (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jose Montesinos
Main Cast: Jeneta St. Clair, Lisa Younger, Melissa Johnston, Morgan Benoit, Myko Olivier
Keywords: birthday, nudity, best friend, vibrator, virginity, college freshman

Overview: Sue, Cheryl and Lexi are three college freshmen who have been best friends since they were born on the same day. They do something special every year for their birthday, but on their eighteenth, they set out to lose their virginity.
