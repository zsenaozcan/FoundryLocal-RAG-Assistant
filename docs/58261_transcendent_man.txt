Title: Transcendent Man (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Robert Barry Ptolemy
Main Cast: Tom Abate, Hugo De Garis, Peter Diamandis, Ray Kurzweil

Overview: The compelling feature-length documentary film, by director Barry Ptolemy, chronicles the life and controversial ideas of luminary Ray Kurzweil. For more than three decades, inventor, futures, and New York Times best-selling author Ray Kurzweil has been one of the most respected and provocative advocates of the role of technology in our future.
