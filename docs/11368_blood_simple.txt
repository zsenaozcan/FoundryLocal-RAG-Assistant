Title: Blood Simple (1984)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Joel Coen
Main Cast: John Getz, Frances McDormand, Dan Hedaya, M. Emmet Walsh, Samm-Art Williams
Keywords: assassin, wife husband relationship, planned murder, lover, boss, employee, killer, dead fish, extramarital affair, shallow grave

Overview: The owner of a seedy small-town Texas bar discovers that one of his employees is having an affair with his wife. A chaotic chain of misunderstandings, lies and mischief ensues after he devises a plot to have them murdered.
