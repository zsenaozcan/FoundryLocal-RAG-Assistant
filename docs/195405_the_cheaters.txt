Title: The Cheaters (1945)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Joseph Kane
Main Cast: Joseph Schildkraut, Billie Burke, Eugene Pallette, Ona Munson, Raymond Walburn
Keywords: inheritance, christmas

Overview: The Cheaters (1945) also known as The Castaway, is a Christmas tale about a has-been actor invited to Christmas dinner by a rich family, directed by Joseph Kane.[1] Joseph Schildkraut, Billie Burke and Eugene Pallette star in the film, distributed by Republic Pictures.  CC wikipedia.org
