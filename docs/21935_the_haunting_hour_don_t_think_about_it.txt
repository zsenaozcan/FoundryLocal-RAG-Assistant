Title: The Haunting Hour: Don't Think About It (2007)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Family
Director: Alex Zamm
Main Cast: Brittany Curran, Alex Winzenread, Michelle Duffy, Emily Osment, John Hawkinson
Keywords: high school

Overview: Cassie, a 13 year old Gothic girl, is trying to fit in at her new school. To entertain herself, she plays a series of pranks on the popular kids and her younger brother, Max. When she goes to a Halloween store looking for new tricks, she finds a little book called "The Evil Thing"
