Title: The Seventh Fire (2015)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Jack Pettibone Riccobono
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: When Rob Brown, a Native American gang leader on a remote Minnesota reservation, is sentenced to prison for a fifth time, he must confront his role in bringing violent drug culture into his beloved Ojibwe community. As Rob reckons with his past, his seventeen-year-old protégé, Kevin, dreams of the future - becoming the biggest drug dealer on the reservation.
