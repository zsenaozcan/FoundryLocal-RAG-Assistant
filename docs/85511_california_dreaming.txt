Title: California Dreaming (1979)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: John D. Hancock
Main Cast: Dennis Christopher, Seymour Cassel, Glynnis O'Connor, Dorothy Tristan, John Calvin
Keywords: california

Overview: Young T.T. comes from Chicago to spend the summer in California. He slowly becomes "California-ized," while learning about love and life in the Golden State.
