Title: No Holds Barred (1989)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Thomas J. Wright
Main Cast: Hulk Hogan, Tom Lister Jr., Joan Severance, Kurt Fuller, David Paymer
Keywords: wrestling

Overview: Rip is the World Wrestling Federation champion who is faithful to his fans and the network he wrestles for, but Brell, the new head of the World Television Network, wants Rip to wrestle for his network.
