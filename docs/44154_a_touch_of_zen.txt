Title: A Touch of Zen (1971)
Genres: Action, Adventure
Director: King Hu
Main Cast: Hsu Feng, Sammo Hung, Tien Peng, Roy Chiao, Jackie Chan
Keywords: buddhism, martial arts, wuxia

Overview: Ming dynasty noblewoman Yang must escape from the evil eunuch Hsu. She seeks refuge at a decrepit town where she gets assistance from a naive scholar & a group of mysterious yet powerful monks.
