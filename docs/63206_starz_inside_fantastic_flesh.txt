Title: Starz Inside: Fantastic Flesh (2008)
Genres: Documentary, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Kevin VanHook
Main Cast: Howard Berger, Rob Bottin, Josh Brolin, John Carpenter, Wes Craven

Overview: This documentary delves into the art of make-up effects with industry legends Dick Smith, Rob Bottin, Tom Savini, John Landis, Frank Darabont, Joe Dante and many others with a strong focus on Greg Nicotero and Howard Berger of KNB Make-Up EFX. Written by Kevin VanHook
