Title: Jackboots on Whitehall (2010)
Genres: War, Animation, Comedy
Director: Edward McHenry
Main Cast: Ewan McGregor, Rosamund Pike, Richard E. Grant, Timothy Spall, Tom Wilkinson
Keywords: england, world war ii, nazis, stop motion

Overview: A satirical alternative history of World War II where the Nazis seize London and England must band together to prevent a full on invasion.
