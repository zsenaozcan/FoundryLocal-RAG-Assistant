Title: Francofonia (2015)
Genres: Documentary, Drama, History
Director: Alexander Sokurov
Main Cast: Louis-Do de Lencquesaing, Vincent Nemeth, Benjamin Utzerath, Jean-Claude Caër, Johanna Korthals Altes
Keywords: museum, louvre, painting, war, world, art, nazi occupation, nazism, art museum

Overview: Master filmmaker Alexander Sokurov (Russian Ark) transforms a portrait of the world-renowned museum into a magisterial, centuries-spanning reflection on the relation between art, culture and power.
