Title: Garden of Love (2003)
Genres: Horror, Foreign
Director: Olaf Ittenbach
Main Cast: Natacza Boon, James Matthews-Pyecka, Daryl Jackson, Bela B., Donald Stewart
Keywords: hippie, coma, nightmare, murder, slaughter

Overview: A woman whose family was brutally murdered when she was little is instructed by her family's ghosts to bring the killers to them so their souls can rest in peace.
