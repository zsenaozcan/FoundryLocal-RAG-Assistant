Title: The Wild Stallion (2009)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Family, Western
Director: Craig Clyde
Main Cast: Miranda Cosgrove, Connie Sellecca, Fred Ward, Danielle Ryan Chuchran, Corbin Allred
Keywords: ranch, stallion

Overview: A story about two girls, CJ and Hanna. CJ lives on a ranch, Hanna comes to visit and decides to photograph wild horses for a school project
