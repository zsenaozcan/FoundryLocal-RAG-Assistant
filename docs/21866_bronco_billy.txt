Title: Bronco Billy (1980)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Romance, Western
Director: Clint Eastwood
Main Cast: Clint Eastwood, Sondra Locke, Geoffrey Lewis, Scatman Crothers, Bill McKinney
Keywords: love, cowboy, wild west show

Overview: An idealistic, modern-day cowboy struggles to keep his Wild West show afloat in the face of hard luck and waning interest.
