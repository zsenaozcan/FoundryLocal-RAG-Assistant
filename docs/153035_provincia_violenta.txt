Title: Provincia violenta (1978)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Mario Bianchi
Main Cast: Calogero Caruana, Antonella Dogan, Alicia Leoni, Spartaco Battisti, Al Cliver
Keywords: poliziotteschi, eurocrime

Overview: Franco Sereni, a captain of carabiniers, is dismissed from service for being the proponent of "the law of the gun". He is asked to hold a private investigation of several cases of blackmailing wealthy women. Soon Sereni finds out that there is much more then just blackmailing.
