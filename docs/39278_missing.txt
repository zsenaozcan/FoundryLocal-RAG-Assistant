Title: Missing (2008)
Genres: Fantasy, Thriller
Director: Tsui Hark
Main Cast: Angelica Lee, Isabella Leong, Chang Chen, Guo Xiaodong, Tony Leung Ka-Fai

Overview: A man with plans to propose to his girlfriend hides an engagement ring in the ancient underwater ruins off Japan's Yonaguni Island. When he goes missing she must investigate and remember what happened.
