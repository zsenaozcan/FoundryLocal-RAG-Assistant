Title: Into the Sun (2005)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Thriller
Director: Mink 
Main Cast: Steven Seagal, Matthew Davis, Takao Osawa, William Atherton, Juliette Marquis
Keywords: cia, fbi, mobile phone, katana, sword, strip club, japanese mafia, blood splatter, see you in hell, broken neck

Overview: After the assassination of Tokyo's Governor by Yakuza members, the CIA bureau chief (William Atherton) for Tokyo puts out a call to an agent (Steven Seagal) that had been raised in Japan and trained by ex-Yakuza. Using his former ties, he quickly determines that a war is brewing between old-guard Yakuza members and a young, crazed leader (Takao Osawa) with ties to the Chinese Tong.
