Title: Frank Herbert's Children of Dune (2003)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction, Fantasy, Adventure
Director: Greg Yaitanes
Main Cast: Alec Newman, Susan Sarandon, James McAvoy, Julie Cox, Ian McNeice
Keywords: prophecy, telepathy, sandstorm, title of nobility, world religion

Overview: Under Paul ‘Muad'Dib’ Atreides' rule as Emperor, the planet Arrakis has enjoyed a renaissance. However, the revolution that Muad'dib inspired has transformed into a brutal jihad that has plunged all of humanity into a bitter, neverending cycle of interstellar war. Now a conspiracy on Arrakis threatens to rob Muad'Dib of his throne and his life.
