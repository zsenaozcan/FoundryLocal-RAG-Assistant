Title: Massaï, les guerriers de la pluie (2004)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Pascal Plisson
Main Cast: Ngotiek Ole Mako, Paul Nteri Ole Sekenan, Parkasio Ole Muntet, Musurpei Ole Toroge

Overview: After a lion kills the village leader, the expected rains fail to arrive, so a group of warriors are sent to hunt the lion down and kill it in hopes that the rains will come at last.
