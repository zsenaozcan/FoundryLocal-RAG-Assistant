Title: Bud Abbott and Lou Costello in Hollywood (1945)
Genres: Comedy
Director: S. Sylvan Simon
Main Cast: Bud Abbott, Lou Costello, Frances Rafferty, Bob Haymes, Jean Porter
Keywords: movie studio, hollywood, agent

Overview: When two bumbling barbers act as agents for a talented but unknown singer, they stage a phony murder in order to get him a plum role.
