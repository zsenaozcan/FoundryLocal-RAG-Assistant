Title: Charley and the Angel (1973)
Genres: Family, Fantasy, Comedy
Director: Vincent McEveety
Main Cast: Fred MacMurray, Harry Morgan, Kurt Russell, Cloris Leachman, Kathleen Cody
Keywords: redemption, guardian angel

Overview: Charley is a workaholic family man that finds out from an angel that his "number's up" and he will be dying soon so he tries to change his ways and be a better husband and father with the time he has left.
