Title: Boulevard (1994)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Penelope Buitenhuis
Main Cast: Kari Wuhrer, Rae Dawn Chong, Lou Diamond Phillips, Lance Henriksen, Joel Bissonnette

Overview: A street prostitute takes in an abused young woman on the run from her misogynist boyfriend, leading to both facing off against the prostitute's dreaded pimp and a relentless police detective out to arrest all of them.
