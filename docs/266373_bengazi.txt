Title: Bengazi (1955)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Adventure
Director: John Brahm
Main Cast: Richard Conte, Victor McLaglen, Mala Powers, Richard Carlson, Richard Erdman
Keywords: desert, buried treasure

Overview: An American with a shady past joins with a morally-bankrupt Irishman to find treasure buried by Arabs in a deserted mosque in the Sahara. The situation becomes complicated when they are surrounded by Bedouin bandits.
