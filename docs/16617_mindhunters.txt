Title: Mindhunters (2004)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Crime
Director: Renny Harlin
Main Cast: LL Cool J, Jonny Lee Miller, Kathryn Morris, Patricia Velásquez, Clifton Collins Jr
Keywords: fbi, island, serial killer, series of murders

Overview: Trainees in the FBI's psychological profiling program must put their training into practice when they discover a killer in their midst.
