Title: Gunman's Walk (1958)
Genres: Western
Director: Phil Karlson
Main Cast: Van Heflin, Tab Hunter, Kathryn Grant, James Darren, Mickey Shaughnessy
Keywords: son, brother, father, gunfighter

Overview: Tab Hunter plays Ed Hackett, the son of gunslinger-turned-land baron Lee Hackett (Van Heflin) in this wide-screen western.
