Title: Christmas Cupid (2010)
Genres: TV Movie, Fantasy, Comedy, Romance, Family
Director: Gil Junger
Main Cast: Chad Michael Murray, Ashley Benson, Christina Milian, Ryan Sypek, Burgess Jenkins
Keywords: christmas

Overview: Sloan is beautiful, stylish, and on the fast track to success at her public relations firm. After her client Caitlin ends up in a coma and becomes her own personal ghost of Christmas past, present, and future; she finds out first hand that her unethical ways needs to change and reuniting with a past jilted lover may be the answer.
