Title: Return to Return to Nuke 'Em High, aka Volume 2 (2017)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Lloyd Kaufman
Main Cast: Asta Paredes, Catherine Corcoran, Zac Amino, Vito Trigo, Lloyd Kaufman

Overview: Following the events of Volume 1,  the mutated glee club continue their violent rampage in Tromaville. Chrissy and Lauren, two innocent lesbian lovers, must fight not only the Cretins, mutants, and monsters but also the evil Tromorganic Foodstuffs Conglomerate. Can they, and Kevin the Wonder Duck, save Tromaville High School and the world?
