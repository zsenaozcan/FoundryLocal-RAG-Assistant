Title: Teknolust (2002)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Lynn Hershman Leeson
Main Cast: Tilda Swinton, Jeremy Davies, James Urbaniak, Karen Black, Thomas Jay Ryan
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Anxious to use artificial life to improve the world, Rosetta Stone, a bio-geneticist creates a Recipe for Cyborgs and uses her own DNA in order to breed three Self Replicating Automatons, part human, part computer named Ruby, Olive and Marine.
