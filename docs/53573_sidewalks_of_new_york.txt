Title: Sidewalks of New York (1931)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Zion Myers
Main Cast: Buster Keaton, Anita Page, Cliff Edwards, Frank Rowan, Norman Phillips Jr.
Keywords: slumlord

Overview: A dim-witted slumlord tries to reform a gang of urban boys (and impress an attractive young woman) by transforming their rough neighborhood into a more decent place.
