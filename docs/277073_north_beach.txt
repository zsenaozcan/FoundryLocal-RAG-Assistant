Title: North Beach (2000)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Richard Speight Jr.
Main Cast: Richard Speight Jr., Gabrielle Anwar, Jim Hanna, Jayden Lund, Casey Peterson
Keywords: infidelity, stripper, buddy

Overview: San Francisco comes alive in this amusing chronicle of urban life. When Tyler has a drunken one night stand with a local stripper, his secret precedes him all over town. Now amidst a haze of springtime revelry, he must make amends to his fiance before she dumps him for good.
