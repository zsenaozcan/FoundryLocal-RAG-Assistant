Title: Affinity (2008)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery, Romance
Director: Tim Fywell
Main Cast: Zoë Tapper, Domini Blythe, Amanda Plummer, Anne Reid, Anna Madeley
Keywords: prison, lesbian relationship, gay man, lesbian interest

Overview: A grieving upper class woman becomes a "Lady Visitor" at Millbank prison, hoping to escape her troubles and be a guiding figure in the lives of the female prisoners. Of all her friendships with prisoners, she is most fascinated by Selina - a medium. Set in the 1870s.
