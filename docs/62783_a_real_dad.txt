Title: A Real Dad (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sergei Bobrov
Main Cast: Михаил Пореченков, Evgeniya Dobrovolskaya, Svetlana Khodchenkova, Aleksandr Dedyushko

Overview: Roman Shilo, a known criminal, suddenly must deal with his fourteen years old daughter and not only with her but with her little stepbrother and stepsister as well.
