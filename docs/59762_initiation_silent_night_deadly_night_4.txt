Title: Initiation: Silent Night, Deadly Night 4 (1990)
Genres: Horror
Director: Brian Yuzna
Main Cast: Clint Howard, Neith Hunter, Tommy Hinkley, Hugh Fink, Maud Adams
Keywords: rape, voodoo, castration, sequel, gore, witchcraft, coven, killer santa claus, holiday horror, christmas
