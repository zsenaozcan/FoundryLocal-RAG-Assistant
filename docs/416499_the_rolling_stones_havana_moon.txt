Title: The Rolling Stones - Havana Moon (2016)
Genres: Music
Director: Paul Dugdale
Main Cast: Mick Jagger, Keith Richards, Charlie Watts, Ronnie Wood, Darryl Jones
Keywords: cuba, rolling stones, music, concert, havana cuba

Overview: The historic concert appearance of the British band in Cuba on March 25th, 2016. The "Concert for Amity" was and played at the Ciudad Deportiva Stadium - the bands first show in Cuba and the finale of their South America Tour.
