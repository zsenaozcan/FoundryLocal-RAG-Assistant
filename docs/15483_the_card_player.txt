Title: The Card Player (2004)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Dario Argento
Main Cast: Stefania Rocca, Liam Cunningham, Silvio Muccino, Cosimo Fusco, Fiore Argento
Keywords: italian, police, serial killer, giallo, slasher, card playing, video poker

Overview: Policewoman Anna Mari is forced to play a dangerous game with the title serial killer. If she loses, she witnesses the maniac's tortured victims having their throats cut in explicit close-up detail via webcam. She teams up with British cop John Brennan to find out the identity of the murderer.
