Title: The Forgotten Faces (1961)
Genres: History, War, Drama
Director: Peter Watkins
Main Cast: Frank Hickey, Michael Roy, John Newing, Stan Mercer, Irene Mallaid
Keywords: politics, docudrama, short

Overview: “The Forgotten Faces (1961), a film reconstruction of the Hungarian revolution of 1956, won Watkins another amateur Oscar, and to this day, the film is praised in England as "one of the most memorable amateur films ever made".
