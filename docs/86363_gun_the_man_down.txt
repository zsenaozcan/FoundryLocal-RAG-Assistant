Title: Gun The Man Down (1956)
Genres: Western, Action
Director: Andrew V. McLaglen
Main Cast: James Arness, Angie Dickinson, Emile Meyer, Robert J. Wilke, Harry Carey, Jr.
Keywords: bank robber

Overview: An outlaw is left for dead by his gang after being shot. A year later, he is released from jail with one thing on his mind: Revenge.
