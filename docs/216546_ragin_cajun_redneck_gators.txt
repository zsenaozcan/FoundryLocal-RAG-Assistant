Title: Ragin Cajun Redneck Gators (2013)
Genres: Horror, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Griff Furst
Main Cast: Jordan Hinson, Christopher Berry, Amy Brassette, John Chriss, Ritchie Montgomery
Keywords: louisiana, alligator, bayou, mutant animal

Overview: A group of mutant gators attack the cajun people. Pouring blue chemicals in a Louisiana swamp causes the alligator population to morph into monstrous creatures including a clan of cajuns living in the bayou.
