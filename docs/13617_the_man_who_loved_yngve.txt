Title: The Man Who Loved Yngve (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Stian Kristiansen
Main Cast: Rolf Kristian Larsen, Arthur Berning, Ida Elise Broch, Ole Christoffer Ertvåg, Jørgen Langhelle
Keywords: coming out, concert, music band, lgbt

Overview: It's November 1989 and the world is changing, so is Jarle who attends Kongsgård HS in Stavanger. He plays the guitar/lead singer in a local radical punkband, got a great girlfriend and has the coolest best friend. But its all about to change when Yngve enters the classroom.
