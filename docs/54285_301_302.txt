Title: 301, 302 (1995)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Park Chul-soo
Main Cast: Pang Eun-jin, Hwang Shin-hye, Kim Choo-ryeon, Park Cheol-Ho
Keywords: neighbor, chef, anorexic

Overview: Two obsessive-compulsives, a chef and an anorexic writer, are neighbors in an apartment building. The chef (301) tries to entice her neighbor to eat with fabulous meals. The writer (302) refuses to eat, and this refusal begins a turbulent relationship that forces both women to delve into their pasts of torment.
