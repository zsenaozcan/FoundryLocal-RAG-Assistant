Title: Manhunt (1976)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Jun'ya Satô
Main Cast: Ken Takakura, Ryoko Nakano, Yoshio Harada, Ryô Ikebe, Hideji Ôtaki

Overview: A tough police detective is falsely accused of corruption, and goes on the run to clear his name. He is assisted by the beautiful daughter of a rich man.
