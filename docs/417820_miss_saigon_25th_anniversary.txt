Title: Miss Saigon: 25th Anniversary (2016)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance, War
Director: Laurence Connor
Main Cast: Eva Noblezada, Alistair Brammer, Jon Jon Briones, Hugh Maynard, Tamsin Carroll
Keywords: vietnam, tragic love, live recording, stage musical

Overview: The 25th Anniversary performance – filmed at London's Prince Edward Theatre in 2014 – of the epic musical tale of a young Vietnamese bar girl, Kim, who falls in love with Chris, an American GI.  But their lives are torn apart by the fall of Saigon.
