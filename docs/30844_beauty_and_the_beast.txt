Title: Beauty and the Beast (2009)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: David Lister
Main Cast: Estella Warren, Rhett Giles, Victor Parascos, Vanessa Gray, Tony Bellette
Keywords: witch

Overview: A twist on the morality tale of forbidden love between the beautiful Belle and the feared Beast. As the villagers are being brutally murdered and the Beast is hunted down as the one responsible for the mayhem, Belle and Beast team up to defeat the real killer; the power-hungry witch's malevolent troll.
