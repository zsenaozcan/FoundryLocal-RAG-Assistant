Title: The Isle of Lost Ships (1987)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction, Music, Adventure, Romance
Director: Yevgeni Ginzburg
Main Cast: Larisa Belogurova, Gediminas Storpirštis, Konstantin Raykin, Natalya Lapina, Semyon Furman

Overview: A musical partially based on a sci-fi novel "The Isle of Lost Ships" by Aleksandr Belyaev.
