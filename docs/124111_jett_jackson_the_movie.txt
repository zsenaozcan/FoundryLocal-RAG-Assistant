Title: Jett Jackson: The Movie (2001)
Genres: Adventure, Family, Action, Science Fiction, Comedy
Director: Shawn Levy
Main Cast: Lee Thompson Young, Lindy Booth, Nigel Shawn Williams, Ryan Sommers Baum, Kerry Duff
Keywords: based on tv series, teenager, movie star, identity swap, teen spy

Overview: Jett Jackson, who is the star of Silverstone, is considering quitting Silverstone when it is given a three year extension. During the filming of the last episode, Jett is accidently sucked into Silverstone's world when a prop malfunctions. Silverstone is zapped into Jett's world as well. In Silverstone's world, Jett has to save the world from Dr. Kragg's evil plans.
