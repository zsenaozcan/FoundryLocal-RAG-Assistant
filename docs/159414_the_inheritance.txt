Title: The Inheritance (1962)
Genres: Drama
Director: Masaki Kobayashi
Main Cast: Keiko Kishi, Tatsuya Nakadai, Sô Yamamura, Yûsuke Kawazu, Seiji Miyaguchi

Overview: On his deathbed, a wealthy businessman announces that his fortune is to be split equally among his three illegitimate children, whose whereabouts are unknown to his family and colleagues. A bevy of lawyers and associates then begin machinations to procure the money for themselves, enlisting the aid of impostors and blackmail
