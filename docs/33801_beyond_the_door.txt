Title: Beyond the Door (1975)
Genres: Horror
Director: Ovidio G. Assonitis
Main Cast: Juliet Mills, Gabriele Lavia, Richard Johnson, Nino Segurini, Elizabeth Turner
Keywords: possession

Overview: Jessica Barrett, wife and mother of two young children, begins to show signs of demonic possession while pregnant with her third child. As she seeks help from her husband and doctor, a mysterious man approaches her and seems to have some answers.
