Title: Transsiberian (2008)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Brad Anderson
Main Cast: Woody Harrelson, Emily Mortimer, Kate Mara, Eduardo Noriega, Thomas Kretschmann
Keywords: china, married couple, backpacker, firearm, police, travel, snow, blood, cowardliness, train
