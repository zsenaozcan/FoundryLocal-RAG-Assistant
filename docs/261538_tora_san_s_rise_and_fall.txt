Title: Tora-san's Rise and Fall (1975)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Yôji Yamada
Main Cast: Kiyoshi Atsumi, Chieko Baishô, Ruriko Asaoka
Keywords: otoko wa tsurai yo

Overview: Tora-san visits Hokkaido and is reunited with Lily. Now divorced, she plans to resume her singing career and renews her unusual relationship with Tora-san.
