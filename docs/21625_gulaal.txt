Title: Gulaal (2009)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Foreign
Director: Anurag Kashyap
Main Cast: Kay Kay Menon, Piyush Mishra, Raj Singh Chaudhary, Abhimanyu Singh, Mahie Gill

Overview: Gulaal is set in Rajpur a fictional city of Rajasthan, where a member of the erstwhile royal family stakes claim to the land of Rajasthan and wants to go back to the days of royalty. He wants Rajputana back from the Government of India and challenges the very idea of democracy. His history backs him, his people too, but the power corrupts him.
