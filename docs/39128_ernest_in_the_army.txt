Title: Ernest In The Army (1998)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: John R. Cherry III
Main Cast: Jim Varney, Ivan D. Lucas

Overview: Ernest P. Worrell is now in the military as a soldier. Once he begins, him and his team investigates a dictator who was responsible for the wars in the nearly village. Suddenly, Ernest finds a lost boy and has to keep him safe until his father recovers.
