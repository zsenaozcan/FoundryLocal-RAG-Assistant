Title: Single By Contract (2010)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Marc Rothemund
Main Cast: Anna Fischer, Kostja Ullmann, Inka Friedrich, Franziska Wulf, Amber Bongard

Overview: A love connection clicks for Lila when she moves back to Berlin and meets Christopher -- never realizing that she's fallen for a rock star who's signed a contract to remain single for life.
