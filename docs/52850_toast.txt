Title: Toast (2010)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, History, Family
Director: S.J. Clarkson
Main Cast: Freddie Highmore, Ken Stott, Victoria Hamilton, Oscar Kennedy, Helena Bonham Carter
Keywords: male nudity, adolescence, nostalgia, autobiography, gay interest, cookies, woman director

Overview: An adaptation of celebrity chef Nigel Slater's bestselling memoir, 'Toast' is the ultimate nostalgic trip through everything edible in 1960's Britain. Nigel's mother was always a poor cook, but her chronic asthma and addiction to all things canned does not help.
