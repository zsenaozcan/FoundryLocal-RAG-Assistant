Title: White, White Storks (1966)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ali Khamrayev
Main Cast: Lyutfi Sarymsakova, Sairam Isayeva, Bolot Beyshenaliev, Khikmat Latypov, Mokhammed Rafikov
Keywords: uzbekistan

Overview: Set in the rural village of White Storks, the story tackles the taboo subject of an extramarital affair. Strong-willed Malika, married but childless, is openly consorting with another man with whom she shares a seemingly tender bond.
