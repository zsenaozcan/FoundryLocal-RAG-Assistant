Title: 71 Fragments of a Chronology of Chance (1994)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Haneke
Main Cast: Gabriel Cosmin Urdes, Lukas Miko, Otto Grünmandl, Anne Bennent, Udo Samel

Overview: 71 scenes revolving around multiple Viennese residents who are by chance involved with a senseless gun slaughter on Christmas Eve.
