Title: Muxmäuschenstill (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Marcus Mittermeier
Main Cast: Jan Henrik Stahlberg, Fritz Roth, Wanda Perdelwitz, Joachim Kretzer, Dieter Dost
Keywords: berlin, idealism, murder, arrogance, vigilantism

Overview: A pseudo documentary study of an archetypal German who tries to model his world according to his ideas of law and (sexual) order.
