Title: August Underground's Mordum (2003)
Genres: Horror
Director: Fred Vogel
Main Cast: Fred Vogel, Cristie Whiles, Michael Todd Schneider, Jerami Cruise, Killjoy

Overview: Two deranged friends bring along another guy to go on a random murder rampage. They kidnapped lesbian lovers, couples and they torture them in any way that the viewer can imagine. Your worst fears will come true in this shocking account of a killing spree.
