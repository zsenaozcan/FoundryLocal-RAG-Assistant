Title: Jerichow (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Christian Petzold
Main Cast: Benno Fürmann, Nina Hoss, Hilmi Sözer, André Hennicke, Claudia Geisler
Keywords: love triangle, eastern germany

Overview: The Turkish diaspora in Germany proves the catalyst for this noir-flavored drama concerning the unlikely friendship between a veteran of the Afghan-Soviet war and a middle-aged Turk in need of a helping hand.
