Title: Blood and Diamonds (1977)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Fernando Di Leo
Main Cast: Claudio Cassinelli, Martin Balsam, Olga Karlatos, Barbara Bouchet, Pier Paolo Capponi

Overview: A man is arrested and condemned to five years in jail for robbery. After serving his term, he is out for revenge on the gang members he considers were to blame for his arrest. The prize for this deadly fight is a large cache of diamonds.
