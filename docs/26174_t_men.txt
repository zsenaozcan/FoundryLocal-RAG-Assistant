Title: T-Men (1947)
Genres: Thriller, Crime
Director: Anthony Mann
Main Cast: Dennis O'Keefe, Mary Meade, Alfred Ryder, Wallace Ford, June Lockhart
Keywords: undercover, treasury department, classic noir, film noir

Overview: Two U.S. Treasury ("T-men") agents go undercover in Detroit, and then Los Angeles, in an attempt to break a U.S. currency counterfeiting ring.
