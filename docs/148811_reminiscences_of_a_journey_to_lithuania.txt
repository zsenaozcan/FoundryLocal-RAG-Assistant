Title: Reminiscences of a Journey to Lithuania (1972)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jonas Mekas
Main Cast: Pola Chapelle, Peter Kubelka, Adolfas Mekas, Jonas Mekas, Hollis Melton
Keywords: lithuania

Overview: Reminiscences of a Journey to Lithuania is a 1971–72 documentary film by Jonas Mekas. It revolves around Mekas' trip back to Semeniškiai, the village of his birth.
