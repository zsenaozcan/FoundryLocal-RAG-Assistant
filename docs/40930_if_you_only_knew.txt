Title: If You Only Knew (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: David Snedeker
Main Cast: Johnathon Schaech, Alison Eastwood, James Le Gros, Gabrielle Anwar, Paul Sampson
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A romantic comedy about a young man who fakes homosexuality to draw the interest of a woman. Instead she tries to help him find a date.
