Title: It Came from Hollywood (1982)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary
Director: Andrew Solt
Main Cast: Dan Aykroyd, John Candy, Cheech Marin, Tommy Chong, Gilda Radner
Keywords: film making, narration, b movie

Overview: Dan Ackroyd, John Candy, Gilda Radner and Cheech and Chong present this compilation of classic bad films from the 50's, 60's and 70's. Special features on gorilla pictures, anti-marijuana films and a special tribute to the worst film maker of all-time, Ed Wood.
