Title: TalhotBlond (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Courteney Cox
Main Cast: Garret Dillahunt, Brando Eaton, Laura San Giacomo, Ashley Grace, Jadin Gould
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Thomas Montgomery, a married father of two young daughters, gets seduced by the world of online gambling and chat rooms where a virtual romance and sexual obsession ultimately leads to the murder of an innocent man.
