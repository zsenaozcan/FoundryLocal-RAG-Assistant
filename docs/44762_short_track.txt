Title: Short Track (2008)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Marie Hopkins
Main Cast: Pepper Sweeney, Barbara Niven, Joshua Snyder
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Carolinian Blake Beckett (Pepper Sweeney), the son of a stock car racing legend, is a 4-time short track racing champion. Blake never made it to the speedways of the National Cup Racing circuit, but surely he could still have a shot? The daily life experiences of Blake's family and crew are exposed through unique characters that work and play through the weeks that culminate in 120 mph Saturday nights.
