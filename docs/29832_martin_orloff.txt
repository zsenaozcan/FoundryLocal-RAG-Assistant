Title: Martin & Orloff (2002)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lawrence Blume
Main Cast: Ian Roberts, Matt Walsh, Amy Poehler, H. Jon Benjamin, Matt Besser

Overview: This is the story of a marketing man and his shrink. A suicide attempt and a softball game; A PHD-toting stripper and a deranged Desert Storm vet; A giant sparerib costume and the world's largest peenis; John Woo-style violence and Steel Magnolia-esque pathos. This is the story of Martin &amp; Orloff.
