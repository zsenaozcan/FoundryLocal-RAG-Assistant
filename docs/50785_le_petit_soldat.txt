Title: Le Petit Soldat (1963)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Jean-Luc Godard
Main Cast: Michel Subor, Anna Karina, Henri-Jacques Huet, Paul Beauvais, László Szabó

Overview: During the Algerian war for independence from France, a young Frenchman living in Geneva who belongs to a right-wing terrorist group and a young woman who belongs to a left-wing terrorist group meet and fall in love. Complications ensue when the man is suspected by the members of his terrorist group of being a double agent.
