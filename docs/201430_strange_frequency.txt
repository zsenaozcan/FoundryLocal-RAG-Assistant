Title: Strange Frequency (2001)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Mary Lambert
Main Cast: Judd Nelson, Eric Roberts, Marla Sokoloff, Danny Masterson, Erik Palladino
Keywords: anthology, tv movie, woman director

Overview: A Rock 'n Roll version of the Twilight Zone, with four segments: "Disco Inferno," where metalheads find themselves in hell; "My Generation," where hitchhikers help you die before you get old; "Room Service," rock star room-trasher vs. the hotel maid; "More Than a Feeling," an A&amp;R man feels talent in his gut but can't hold on to the artists he finds.
