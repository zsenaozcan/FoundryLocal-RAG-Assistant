Title: Pavilion of Women (2001)
Genres: War, Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Yim Ho
Main Cast: Willem Dafoe, Yan Luo, Shek Sau, Yi Ding, John Cho

Overview: With World War 2 looming, a prominent family in China must confront the contrasting ideas of traditionalism, communism and Western thinking, while dealing with the most important ideal of all: love and its meaning in society.
