Title: Uncovered (1995)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Jim McBride
Main Cast: Kate Beckinsale, John Wood, Sinéad Cusack, Paudge Behan, Art Malik
Keywords: suspense

Overview: While restoring a fifteenth-century painting Julia reveals a hidden Latin phrase. A series of murders begin to rock her small world of art experts, patrons and restorers, and she finds that the mystery of the painting is interwoven with the mystery of the deaths around her.
