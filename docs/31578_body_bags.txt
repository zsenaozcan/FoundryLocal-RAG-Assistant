Title: Body Bags (1993)
Genres: TV Movie, Horror
Director: Tobe Hooper
Main Cast: John Carpenter, Robert Carradine, Alex Datcher, David Naughton, Stacy Keach
Keywords: baseball, made for cable tv, anthology, mechanic, hair loss, horror anthology

Overview: Three tales are told, each one increasingly terrifying. The first tells the story of a woman being stalked by an axe-weilding maniac. The second is the story of a man who pays the ultimate price for a beautiful head of hair. The final tale shows what it is like to see life through the eyes of a killer.
