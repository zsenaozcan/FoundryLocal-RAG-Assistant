Title: El señor doctor (1965)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Miguel M. Delgado
Main Cast: Cantinflas, Marta Romero, Miguel Ángel Álvarez, Prudencia Grifell, Wolf Ruvinskis
Keywords: nurse, small town, nightclub, hospital, doctor, waiter, old lady, doctor patient relationship, 1960s, hippies

Overview: It's about a country doctor that comes to the city while a clinic is built in his home town....he rubs off his care and compassion on others at the hospital with his humor and wit.
