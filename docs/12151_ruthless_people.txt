Title: Ruthless People (1986)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jim Abrahams
Main Cast: Danny DeVito, Bette Midler, Judge Reinhold, Helen Slater, Anita Morris
Keywords: ransom, lover (female), kidnapping, business man
