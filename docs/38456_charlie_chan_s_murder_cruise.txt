Title: Charlie Chan's Murder Cruise (1940)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Eugene Forde
Main Cast: Sidney Toler, Marjorie Weaver, Lionel Atwill, Victor Sen Yung, Don Beddoe
Keywords: cruise ship, gunfight, murder investigation, rubber mask

Overview: On a cruise ship from Honolulu to San Francisco, the famous Chinese detective encounters four more murders while trying to figure out the murder of a Scotland Yard friend.
