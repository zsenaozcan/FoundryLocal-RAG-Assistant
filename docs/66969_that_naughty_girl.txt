Title: That Naughty Girl (1956)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Michel Boisrond
Main Cast: Brigitte Bardot, Jean Bretonnière, Françoise Fabian, Mischa Auer, Michel Serrault
Keywords: nightclub, musical, counterfeiting, psychoanalyst

Overview: Nightclub entertainer Jean Clery discovers too late that the 'baby' he agreed to take care of is a wild, shapely sex kitten.
