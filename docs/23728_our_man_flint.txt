Title: Our Man Flint (1966)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Fantasy, Science Fiction
Director: Daniel Mann
Main Cast: James Coburn, Lee J. Cobb, Gila Golan, Edward Mulhare, Benson Fong
Keywords: spy, spoof, derek flint, super spy

Overview: When scientists use eco-terrorism to impose their will on the world by affecting extremes in the weather, Intelligence Chief Cramden calls in top agent Derek Flint.
