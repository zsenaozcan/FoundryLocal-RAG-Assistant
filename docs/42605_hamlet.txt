Title: Hamlet (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Tony Richardson
Main Cast: Anthony Hopkins, Nicol Williamson, Judy Parfitt, Marianne Faithfull, Mark Dignam
Keywords: shakespeare, tragedy, hamlet

Overview: Tony Richardson's Hamlet is based on his own stage production. Filmed entirely within the Roundhouse in London (a disused train shed), it is shot almost entirely in close up, focusing the attention on faces and language rather than action.
