Title: Beck 06 - Monstret (1998)
Genres: Crime
Director: Harald Hamrell
Main Cast: Peter Haber, Mikael Persbrandt, Stina Rautelin, Per Morberg

Overview: A bomb threat is called in to a police station in Stockholm. The police bomb squad blows the briefcase to pieces and it looks like false alarm, until a baby is found inside. Soon, e-mails are sent out to every newspaper about the nasty situation that the police had tried to keep secret. Will the police ever overcome the "Monster"?
