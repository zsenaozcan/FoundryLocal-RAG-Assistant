Title: Swineherd and Shepherd (1941)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Ivan Pyryev
Main Cast: Marina Ladynina, Vladimir Zeldin, Nikolay Kryuchkov, Osip Abdulov
Keywords: musical

Overview: They met in Moscow - a shy swineherd Glasha and shepherd Musaib. Long and difficult will be their way to love and a new meeting in this classic Soviet musical comedy.
