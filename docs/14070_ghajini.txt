Title: Ghajini (2008)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Drama, Mystery
Director: A.R. Murugadoss
Main Cast: Aamir Khan, Asin Thottumkal, Jiah Khan, Pradeep Ram Singh Rawat, Riyaz Khan
Keywords: tattoo, short-term memory

Overview: Sanjay a rich tycoon suffering from short term memory loss due to being hit by a metal pole when trying to intervene on his girlfriend's murder. Because of this his memory can only last for 15 minutes and he doesn't remember events or incidents that have happened before in his life.
