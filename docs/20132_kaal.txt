Title: Kaal (2005)
Genres: Action, Foreign, Thriller
Director: Soham Shah
Main Cast: Ajay Devgn, Vivek Oberoi, John Abraham, Lara Dutta, Esha Deol

Overview: Kaal is based around the story of a wildlife expert, his wife and a group of friends who battle against supernatural creatures in the Jim Corbett National Park for their lives.
