Title: Code Black (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ryan McGarry
Main Cast: Danny Cheng, Andrew Eads, Luis Enriquez, Jamie Eng, Arash Kohanteb

Overview: Code Black follows a team of young, idealistic and energetic ER doctors during the transition from the old to the new L.A. County as they try to avoid burnout and improve patient care. Why do they persist, despite being under siege by rules, regulations and paperwork?
