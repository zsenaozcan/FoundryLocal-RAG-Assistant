Title: Seoul Searching (2015)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Benson Lee
Main Cast: Cha In-pyo, Justin Chon, Jessika Van, Teo Yoo, David Lee McInnis

Overview: Seoul Searching is a comedy set in the ’80s about a group of foreign-born Korean teenagers who meet at a Seoul summer camp to learn what it means to be Korean. The three boys, from the U.S., Mexico, and Germany, then meet three girls who rock their world.
