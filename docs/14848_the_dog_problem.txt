Title: The Dog Problem (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Scott Caan
Main Cast: Giovanni Ribisi, Lynn Collins, Scott Caan, Mena Suvari, Sarah Shahi
Keywords: independent film

Overview: In the film, Solo is a down-on-his-luck writer who is encouraged by his psychiatrist to get a dog. Solo meets his love interest, who he assumes to be a dog owner when meeting her at a dog play park, but dog problems stand in their way.
