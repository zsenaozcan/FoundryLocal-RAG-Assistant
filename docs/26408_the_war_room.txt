Title: The War Room (1993)
Genres: Documentary, History
Director: D.A. Pennebaker
Main Cast: James Carville, George Stephanopoulos, Heather Beckel, Paul Begala, Bob Boorstin
Keywords: politics, election campaign, woman director, cinéma vérité

Overview: A behind-the-scenes documentary about the Clinton for President campaign, focusing on the adventures of spin doctors James Carville and George Stephanopoulos.
