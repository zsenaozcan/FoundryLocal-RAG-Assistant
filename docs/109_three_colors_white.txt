Title: Three Colors: White (1994)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Mystery
Director: Krzysztof Kieślowski
Main Cast: Zbigniew Zamachowski, Julie Delpy, Janusz Gajos, Jerzy Stuhr, Aleksander Bardini
Keywords: hairdresser, sexual frustration, funeral, business man, poland

Overview: Polish immigrant Karol Karol finds himself out of a marriage, a job and a country when his French wife, Dominique, divorces him after six months due to his impotence. Forced to leave France after losing the business they jointly owned, Karol enlists fellow Polish expatriate Mikolah to smuggle him back to their homeland.
