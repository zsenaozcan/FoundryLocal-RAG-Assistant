Title: The Belles of St. Trinian's (1954)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Frank Launder
Main Cast: Alastair Sim, Alastair Sim, George Cole
Keywords: england, girls' boarding school

Overview: Story of manic schoolgirls who are more interested in racing form than books as they try to get-rich-quick, aided by the head-mistress' brother, played by Alastair Sim, who also plays the head-mistress.
