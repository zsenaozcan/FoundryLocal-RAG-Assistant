Title: Decay (2015)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Horror
Director: Joseph Wartnerchaney
Main Cast: Rob Zabrecky, Lisa Howard, Elisha Yaffe, Jackie Hoffman, Denielle Fisher Johnson

Overview: Jonathan is a very lonely man. One day, he gets a visitor in his house: a young woman who, through a jarring turn of events, ends up dead. He does not report it because he is happy to have a friend, but now the body begins to decay.
