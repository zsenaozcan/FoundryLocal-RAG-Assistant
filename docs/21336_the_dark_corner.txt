Title: The Dark Corner (1946)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Henry Hathaway
Main Cast: Lucille Ball, Clifton Webb, William Bendix, Mark Stevens, Kurt Kreuger
Keywords: private investigator, classic noir, film noir

Overview: Ex con turned private investigator Bradford Galt suspects someone is following him and maybe even trying to kill him. With the assistance of his spunky secretary, Kathleen Stewart, he dives deep into a mystery in search of answers.
