Title: O.S.S. (1946)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Irving Pichel
Main Cast: Alan Ladd, Geraldine Fitzgerald, Patric Knowles, John Hoyt, Gloria Saunders
Keywords: spy, sabotage

Overview: John Martin is part of an American spy team dropped into France during World War II to destroy the French railway system. After successfully blowing up a tunnel he runs back to save Ellen and is told "Never come back for me again." Later he must choose whether or not to obey her wishes
