Title: Days and Nights (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Christian Camargo
Main Cast: Katie Holmes, Ben Whishaw, Allison Janney, Jean Reno, William Hurt

Overview: Reckless desire wreaks havoc over Memorial Day weekend as a family confronts the volatile and fragile nature of love. A modern retelling of Anton Chekhov's "The Seagull", set in rural New England.
