Title: DamNation (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ben Knight
Main Cast: Edward Abbey, Bruce Babbitt, Lori Bodi, Yvon Chouinard, Elmer Crow

Overview: This powerful film odyssey across America explores the sea change in national attitude from pride in big dams as engineering wonders to the growing awareness that our own future is bound to the life and health of our rivers.
