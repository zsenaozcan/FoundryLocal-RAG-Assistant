Title: Enter the Battlefield: Life on the Magic - The Gathering Pro Tour (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Nathan Holt
Main Cast: Shahar Shenhar, Patrick Chapin, Chris Pikula, Melissa DeTora, William Jensen
Keywords: esports, e-sports, gaming, strategy game, trading card game

Overview: Magic: The Gathering is the most popular collectible card game in the world. At Magic's Pro Tour, players from around the globe compete to prove who is the best. They are writers, game theorists, jewelry apprentices, female pioneers, and teenage geniuses.
