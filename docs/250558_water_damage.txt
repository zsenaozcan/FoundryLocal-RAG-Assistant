Title: Water Damage (1999)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Murray Battle
Main Cast: Daniel Baldwin, Leslie Hope, Dean Stockwell, John Neville, Roberta Maxwell

Overview: One year after the drowning death of his young son, Paul Preedy (Daniel Baldwin) receives an invitation to a reunion at his prep school. Paul decides to go, taking his remaining son with him. When they arrive, they find that only two other people had been invited and a new nightmare is about to begin.
