Title: High Lane (2009)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Horror
Director: Abel Ferry
Main Cast: Fanny Valette, Johan Libéreau, Maud Wyler, Nicolas Giraud, Justin Blanckaert
Keywords: stalking, mountain climbing, elimination derby, blockage

Overview: A group of friends on vacation decide to venture onto a trail high up in the mountains that has been closed for repairs. The climb proves more perilous than planned, especially as they soon realize that they are not alone. The adventure turns into a nightmare.
