Title: Raavan (2010)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Crime, Drama, Romance, Thriller
Director: Mani Ratnam
Main Cast: Abhishek Bachchan, Aishwarya Rai Bachchan, Govinda, Vikram, Ravi Kishan
Keywords: love triangle, dancer, gun, loss of sister, fight, kidnapping, mythology, waterfall, forest, marriage

Overview: This thrilling, allegorical drama follows the intricately interwoven lives of three people, delving into concepts of good and evil, and complex emotions. Ragini re-locates to distant Lal Matti along with her Superintendent of Police husband, Dev Pratap Sharma, in order to enable him to apprehend a bandit, Beera Munda. Shortly thereafter her life is turned upside down when she is abducted by Beera and held captive. It is during this captivity that she is forced to experience myriad complex emotions, with her life in danger of ending within 14 hours. However, she also attempts to the real reason behind her abduction. What will she discover? How will justice be served? Will Ragini's life be spared, and if so, will she be happy to return to the safety of her husband's embrace?
