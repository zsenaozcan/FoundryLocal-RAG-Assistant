Title: Gold Diggers of 1933 (1933)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Mervyn LeRoy
Main Cast: Joan Blondell, Aline MacMahon, Dick Powell, Ruby Keeler, Ginger Rogers
Keywords: musical, great depression, mistaken identity, songwriter, pre-code, hard times, broadway musical, hidden identity, chorus girls, show girls

Overview: Four aspiring musical actresses - Carol, Trixie, Polly and Fay - are struggling to make a living on Broadway in the midst of the Great Depression. When producer Barney Hopkins has the idea of creating a show about the Depression, the girls team up with  newly-discovered songwriter Brad Roberts to make it happen. But Brad is not who he seems.
