Title: The Perils of Pauline (1914)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Louis J. Gasnier
Main Cast: Milton Berle, Edward José, Pearl White, Crane Wilbur, Paul Panzer

Overview: The Perils of Pauline is a motion picture serial shown in weekly installments featuring the actress Pearl White playing the title character. Pauline has often been cited as a famous example of a damsel in distress, although some analyses hold that her character was more resourceful and less helpless than the classic damsel stereotype.
