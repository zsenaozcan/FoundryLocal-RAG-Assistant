Title: The Killing of a Chinese Bookie (1976)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: John Cassavetes
Main Cast: Ben Gazzara, Timothy Carey, Seymour Cassel, Robert Phillips, Morgan Woodward
Keywords: strip club, mobster

Overview: A proud strip club owner is forced to come to terms with himself as a man, when his gambling addiction gets him in hot water with the mob, who offer him only one alternative.
