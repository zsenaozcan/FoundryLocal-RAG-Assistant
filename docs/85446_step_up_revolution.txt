Title: Step Up Revolution (2012)
Genres: Music, Drama, Romance
Director: Scott Speer
Main Cast: Kathryn McCormick, Ryan Guzman, Stephen Boss, Megan Boone, Jessica Guadix
Keywords: flash mob, dance instructor, real estate development, nike, dance company

Overview: Emily arrives in Miami with aspirations to become a professional dancer. She sparks with Sean, the leader of a dance crew whose neighborhood is threatened by Emily's father's development plans.
