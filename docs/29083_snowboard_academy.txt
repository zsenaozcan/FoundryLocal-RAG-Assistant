Title: Snowboard Academy (1997)
Genres: Comedy
Director: John Shepphird
Main Cast: Jim Varney, Corey Haim, Brigitte Nielsen, Joe Flaherty, Andreas Apergis
Keywords: snowboarding, money, maverick, school

Overview: A wacky free-for-all comedy about the riotous rivalry between snobby skiers and knuckle-dragging snowboarders.
