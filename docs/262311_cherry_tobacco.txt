Title: Cherry Tobacco (2014)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Andres Maimik
Main Cast: Maris Nõlvak, Gert Raudsep, Getter Meresmaa, Anne Reemann, Maarja Jakobson
Keywords: woman director

Overview: An exploration of the growing friendship between small-town girl Laura, who is persuaded onto a hiking trip by her friend Merit, and Joosep, a middle-aged guide.
