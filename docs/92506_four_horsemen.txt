Title: Four Horsemen (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Ross Ashcroft
Main Cast: Noam Chomsky, Joseph Stiglitz, John Perkins, James Turk, Herman Daly
Keywords: banker, economy, global economy, banking, economics

Overview: Documentary about the modern apocalypse caused by a rapacious banking system.  23 leading thinkers – frustrated at the failure of their respective disciplines – break their silence to explain how the world really works.
