Title: Phoenix (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Christian Petzold
Main Cast: Nina Hoss, Ronald Zehrfeld, Nina Kunzendorf, Trystan Pütter, Michael Maertens
Keywords: based on novel, auschwitz, survivor, singing, neo-noir

Overview: A disfigured concentration-camp survivor, unrecognizable after facial reconstruction surgery, searches ravaged postwar Berlin for the husband who might have betrayed her to the Nazis.
