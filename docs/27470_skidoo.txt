Title: Skidoo (1968)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Otto Preminger
Main Cast: Jackie Gleason, Carol Channing, Frankie Avalon, Fred Clark, Michael Constantine

Overview: Ex-gangster Tony Banks is called out of retirement by mob kingpin God to carry out a hit on fellow mobster "Blue Chips" Packard. When Banks demurs, God kidnaps his daughter Darlene on his luxury yacht.
