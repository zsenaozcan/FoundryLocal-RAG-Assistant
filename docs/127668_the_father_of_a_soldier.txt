Title: The Father of a Soldier (1964)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Rezo Chkheidze
Main Cast: Sergo Zakariadze, Radner Muratov, Viktor Kosykh, Татьяна Решетникова

Overview: During World War II, оld Georgian peasant  wine-grower Georgy (Sergo Zakariadze) leaves his Georgian village and goes off to the front line, hoping to find his son. George should see and transfer a lot of things, he becomes a soldier and only in the last days of war finds the son...
