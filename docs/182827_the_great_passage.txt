Title: The Great Passage (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yuya Ishii
Main Cast: 松田龙平, Aoi Miyazaki, Joe Odagiri, Haru Kuroki, Misako Watanabe
Keywords: japanese, group, japanese culture

Overview: Majime, an eccentric man in publishing company, who has unique ability of words, joins the team that will compile a new dictionary, ‘The Great Passage.’ In the eclectic team, he becomes immersed in the world of dictionaries. But the team is overwhelmed with problems. Will ‘The Great Passage’ ever be completed?
