Title: Niko 2 - Little Brother, Big Trouble (2012)
Genres: Animation, Family, Adventure, Comedy
Director: Jørgen Lerdam
Main Cast: Kari Hietalahti, Juha Veijonen, Kari Ketonen, Risto Kaskilahti, Erik Carlson

Overview: Set right before Christmas, Niko the reindeer must deal with his mom getting re-married and his being tasked with looking after his little stepbrother.
