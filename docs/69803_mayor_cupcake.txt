Title: Mayor Cupcake (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Family
Director: Alex Pires
Main Cast: Lea Thompson, Judd Nelson, Zoey Deutch, Madelyn Deutch, Frankie Faison

Overview: A hard-working cupcake maker is inadvertently elected mayor of a small town burdened with debt. Uneducated, she relies on her street smarts to clean up the town.
