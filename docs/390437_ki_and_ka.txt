Title: Ki and Ka (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: R. Balki
Main Cast: Kareena Kapoor, Amitabh Bachchan, Arjun Kapoor, Rajat Kapoor, Jaya Bachchan
Keywords: married

Overview: Ki and Ka (English: Hers and His) is an Indian romantic drama film written, directed, and produced by R. Balki. The film follows a young, married couple contradicting the gender roles placed upon women and men in Indian society. It stars Kareena Kapoor and Arjun Kapoor.
