Title: How Do You Write a Joe Schermann Song (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Gary King
Main Cast: Mark DiConzo, Joe Schermann, Christina Rose, Debbie Williams, Daryl Ray Carliles

Overview: A musical dramedy about a guy named Joe and his girlfriend Evey.  They dream of hitting it big on Broadway. When Joe lands an opportunity to write for an Off-Broadway musical, he is forced to cast either the love of his life or Summer, his newly discovered muse. The realities of show business prove to Joe that writing is easy, living is hard.
