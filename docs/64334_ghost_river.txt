Title: Ghost River (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: Olivier Dahan
Main Cast: Isabelle Huppert, Pascal Greggory, Maud Forget, Fabienne Babe, André Marcon
Keywords: prostitution

Overview: Sylvie is a hooker whose illegitimate daughter commits a crime. She and her daughter flee to find Sylvie's first love in the countryside. The daughter is trying to get to know her unwilling mother. Along the way, the two meet a male fugitive and bond with each other.
