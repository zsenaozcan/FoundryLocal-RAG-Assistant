Title: Barbara the Fair with the Silken Hair (1969)
Genres: Family, Fantasy
Director: Aleksandr Rou
Main Cast: Tatyana Klyuyeva, Georgi Millyar, Mikhail Pugovkin, Anatoli Kubatsky, Vera Altayskaya

Overview: Once upon a time there lived a Tsar named Yeremey who went on a year-long journey to take inventory of all he owned. One day he is kidnapped by the underwater Tsar Chudo-Yudo. When a ransom is demanded, the Tsar's family is faced with all kinds of intrigue and treachery.
