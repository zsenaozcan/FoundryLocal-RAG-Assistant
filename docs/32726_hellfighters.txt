Title: Hellfighters (1968)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Romance
Director: Andrew V. McLaglen
Main Cast: John Wayne, Katharine Ross, Jim Hutton, Vera Miles, Jay C. Flippen
Keywords: firemen, dangerous, oil, love interest

Overview: The adventures of oil well fire specialist Chance Buckman (based on real-life Red Adair), who extinguishes massive fires in oil fields around the world.
