Title: The Siege of Firebase Gloria (1989)
Genres: Action, Drama, War
Director: Brian Trenchard-Smith
Main Cast: Wings Hauser, R. Lee Ermey, Robert Arevalo, Mark Neely, Gary Hershberger
Keywords: vietnam war, vietcong, siege, embassy, sergeant, massacre, u.s. marine corps, tet offensive

Overview: A Marine patrol stops at Firebase Gloria at the start of the Tet Offensive during the Vietnam war. With the firebase attacked, the patrol remains to help defend it. The Marines barely hold off the VC (Viet Cong), but in the end, hold the base.
