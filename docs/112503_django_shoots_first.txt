Title: Django Shoots First (1966)
Genres: Romance, Western
Director: Alberto De Martino
Main Cast: Glenn Saxson, Ida Galli, Fernando Sancho, Nando Gazzolo, Guido Lollobrigida

Overview: Django's father is framed by his business partner Clusker and shot by a bounty Killer. Django inherits his fathers part of the business and a score to settle with Clusker.
