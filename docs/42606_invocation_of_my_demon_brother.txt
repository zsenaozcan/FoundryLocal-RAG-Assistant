Title: Invocation of My Demon Brother (1969)
Genres: Horror
Director: Kenneth Anger
Main Cast: Kenneth Anger, Bobby Beausoleil, Bill Beutel, Mick Jagger, Lenore Kandel
Keywords: surreal, demon, skull, satanic ritual

Overview: Experimental short, featuring strobe-like homoerotic imagery with several shots of the Rolling Stones in performance and an original Moog score by Mick Jagger.
