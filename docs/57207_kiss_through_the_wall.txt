Title: Kiss through the Wall (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Drama
Director: Vardan Hakobyan
Main Cast: Anton Shagin, Karina Andolenko, Ivan Okhlobystin, Pavel Volya, Aleksandr Adabashyan

Overview: Kesha, a young magician assistant, is desperately in love with a young journalist Alisa. He is ready to do everything possible to conquer her.
