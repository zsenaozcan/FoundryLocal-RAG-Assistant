Title: Berserker (2015)
Genres: Mystery, Crime
Director: Pablo Hernando
Main Cast: Julián Génisson, Ingrid García Jonsson, Vicenç Miralles, Chema Adeva, Lorena Iglesias

Overview: Hugo Vartan is an author looking for a juicy story for his next book. He comes across one in the form of the sister of his flatmate's boyfriend, who has been hospitalised in a psychiatric ward for, seemingly, murdering her partner, beheading him with a knife and sticking his head on the steering wheel of her car.
