Title: Fishtales (2007)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Comedy, Science Fiction, Romance, Family, Foreign
Director: Alki David
Main Cast: Billy Zane, Amber Savva, Kelly Brook, Alki David, Olivia Grant
Keywords: mermaid, beautiful woman

Overview: A Greek History Professor &amp; his daughter travel to a Greek Island for one last chance to finish his thesis on ancient love spells before he loses his research grant. His daughter attempts to match her father with the local women without sucess until she meets a beautiful Mermaid with whose help the Professor might finally find true love for himself.
