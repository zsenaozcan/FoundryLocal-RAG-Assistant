Title: A Night to Remember (1942)
Genres: Comedy, Mystery, Romance
Director: Richard Wallace
Main Cast: Loretta Young, Brian Aherne, Jeff Donnell, William Wright, Sidney Toler
Keywords: greenwich village, mystery writer

Overview: A woman rents a gloomy basement apartment in Greenwich Village thinking it will provide the perfect atmosphere for her mystery writer husband to create his next book. They soon find themselves in the middle of a real-life mystery when a corpse turns up in their apartment.
