Title: Once Upon a Time in High School (2004)
Genres: Drama, Action, Romance, Foreign
Director: Yoo Ha
Main Cast: Lee Jung-Jin, Han Ga-in, Won-kwon Kim, Seo Dong-won, Park Hyo-joon

Overview: A model student transfers to the notorious Jungmoon High School known for its severe corporal punishment by teachers and power struggles between school gangs.
