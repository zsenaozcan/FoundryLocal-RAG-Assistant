Title: Summer Time Machine Blues (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Katsuyuki Motohiro
Main Cast: Eita, Yoshiaki Yoza, Daijiro Kawaoka, Munenori Nagano, Juri Ueno

Overview: A light hearted comedy following a group of friends in a science-fiction club, who spend their summer holidays fooling around at their club house, have a mission to go back into time, using a time machine that appeared out of nowhere, in order to save their summer days from unbearable heat by retrieving the remote control for their air conditioner. But wouldn’t changing the past have an effect on the future? Be prepared for a wild ride regarding time travel with a bunch of whacky characters and theories thrown into the mix.
