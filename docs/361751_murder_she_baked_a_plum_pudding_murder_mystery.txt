Title: Murder, She Baked: A Plum Pudding Murder Mystery (2015)
Genres: TV Movie, Mystery
Director: Kristoffer Tabori
Main Cast: Alison Sweeney, Cameron Mathison, Barbara Niven, Gabriel Hogan, Lisa Durupt

Overview: A young bakeshop owner’s holiday season takes a surprising turn when she finds a body at a local Christmas tree lot and winds up involved in a dangerous murder investigation. With colorful characters popping up as suspects, shady business practices uncovered at the tree lot and holiday romance in the air, the young baker-turned-sleuth must race against time to track down the killer and save the Christmas season.
