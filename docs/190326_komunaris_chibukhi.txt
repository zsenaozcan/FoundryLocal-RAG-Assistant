Title: Komunaris chibukhi (1929)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kote Mardjanishvili
Main Cast: Ushangi Chkheidze, Sergei Zabozlayev, Veriko Anjaparidze, T. Menzon, Aleksandre Jorjoliani
Keywords: paris commune

Overview: A movie about living and fighting of Paris Commune and fate of the little boy - Communard.  based on Ilya Ehrenburg novel.
