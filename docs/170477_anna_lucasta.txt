Title: Anna Lucasta (1958)
Genres: Drama
Director: Arnold Laven
Main Cast: Eartha Kitt, Sammy Davis, Jr., Frederick O'Neal, Henry Scott, Georgia Burke
Keywords: prostitute, based on play or musical

Overview: Anna Lucasta is a 1959 film directed by Arnold Laven. Based on the 1944 play by Philip Yordan, this drama follows the trials and tribulations of Anna Lucasta (Eartha Kitt), a young black woman who turns to prostitution after her father kicks her out of the house.
