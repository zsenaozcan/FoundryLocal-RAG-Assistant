Title: Ivanhoe (1952)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Romance
Director: Richard Thorpe
Main Cast: Robert Taylor, Elizabeth Taylor, Joan Fontaine, George Sanders, Emlyn Williams
Keywords: robin hood, ivanhoe, king richard

Overview: Sir Walter Scott's classic story of the chivalrous Ivanhoe who joins with Robin of Locksley in the fight against Prince John and for the return of King Richard the Lionheart.
