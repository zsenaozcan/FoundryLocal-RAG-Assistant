Title: Life as a Fatal Sexually Transmitted Disease (2000)
Genres: Drama
Director: Krzysztof Zanussi
Main Cast: Zbigniew Zapasiewicz, Krystyna Janda, Tadeusz Bradecki, Monika Krzywkowska, Pawel Okraska
Keywords: cancer, doctor, meaning of life

Overview: The story of a doctor named Tomasz who questions his beliefs, faith and morality as his end is near, so he makes all efforts to find dignity in his imminent death.
