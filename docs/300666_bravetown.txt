Title: Bravetown (2015)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Daniel Duran
Main Cast: Josh Duhamel, Maria Bello, Laura Dern, Katrina Norman, Lucas Till
Keywords: father son relationship, small town, dance, music, grief, teenager, u.s. military, dj, teen drama

Overview: After an accidental drug overdose, a talented teenage DJ goes to live with his estranged father in a small Army town, where he gets to the bottom of his own pain and learns empathy for others.
