Title: Abbott and Costello Meet Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde (1953)
Genres: Comedy, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Charles Lamont
Main Cast: Bud Abbott, Lou Costello, Boris Karloff, Eddie Parker, Craig Stevens
Keywords: london england, double life, horror spoof, jekyll and hyde

Overview: Abbott &amp; Costello are two bumbling (naturally) American cops, on exchange in London, hunting for Mr. Hyde, the monster that has been terrorizing the city.
