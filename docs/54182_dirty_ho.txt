Title: Dirty Ho (1979)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Liu Chia-Liang
Main Cast: Gordon Liu Chia-Hui, Wong Yu, Lo Lieh, Johnny Wang Lung-Wei, Hsiao Ho
Keywords: kung fu, prince, body guard, assassination plot

Overview: Wang Chin-chin, who is both the 11th prince and an expert fighterm has fun secretly subverting a local con-man, the inept Ho Chi (Wong Yue), but blackmails the crook into becoming his student after making it look like a B-girl named Choi Hung (Kara Hui) has poisoned Ho and beaten him up in a nightclub.
