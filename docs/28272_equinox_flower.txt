Title: Equinox Flower (1958)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Main Cast: Shin Saburi, Kinuyo Tanaka, Ineko Arima, Yoshiko Kuga, Keiji Sada
Keywords: daughter

Overview: Later in his career, Ozu started becoming increasingly sympathetic with the younger generation, a shift that was cemented in Equinox Flower, his gorgeously detailed first color film, about an old-fashioned father and his newfangled daughter.
