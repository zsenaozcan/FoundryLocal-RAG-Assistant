Title: Korso (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Akseli Tuomivaara
Main Cast: Mikko Neuvonen

Overview: Stuck in the suburbs of Helsinki, 20-year-old Markus plans on moving to New York, his sights set on pursuing streetball fame. With little money behind him, he turns to the local loan shark - and when he discovers that Markus has no plans to repay him, things take a turn for the worse.
