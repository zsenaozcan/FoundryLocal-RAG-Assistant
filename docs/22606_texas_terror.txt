Title: Texas Terror (1935)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Western
Director: Robert N. Bradbury
Main Cast: John Wayne, Lucile Browne, LeRoy Mason, Fern Emmett, George 'Gabby' Hayes
Keywords: ranch, gold prospector

Overview: Sheriff John Higgins quits and goes into prospecting after he thinks he has killed his best friend in shooting it out with robbers. He encounters his dead buddy's sister and helps her run her ranch. Then she finds out about his past.
