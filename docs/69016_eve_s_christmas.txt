Title: Eve's Christmas (2004)
Genres: Drama
Director: Timothy Bond
Main Cast: Elisa Donovan, Sebastien Spence, Cheryl Ladd, Erin Karpluk
Keywords: holiday

Overview: A wealthy and successful career woman gets a second chance in life when a magical wish transports her back in time eight years to when she walked away from her fiance to lead a business life in New York.
