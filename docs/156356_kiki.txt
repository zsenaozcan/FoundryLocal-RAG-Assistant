Title: Kiki (1931)
Genres: Music
Director: Sam Taylor
Main Cast: Mary Pickford, Reginald Denny, Joseph Cawthorn, Margaret Livingston, Phil Tead
Keywords: musical, cheesecake

Overview: A young Frenchwoman is determined to get into and stay in show business, no matter what. Then she's determined to win a recently divorced man's heart... again, no matter what.
