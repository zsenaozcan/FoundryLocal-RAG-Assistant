Title: Up at the Villa (2000)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Philip Haas
Main Cast: Kristin Scott Thomas, Sean Penn, Anne Bancroft, James Fox, Derek Jacobi
Keywords: upper class, refugee, infidelity, blackmail, seduction, widow, vacation, florence, independent film, theft

Overview: Superficial people are revealed and drastically changed by circumstance or luck in this a tale of death, seduction, blackmail and theft among British and Americans in Florence in the turbulent days just before World War II.
