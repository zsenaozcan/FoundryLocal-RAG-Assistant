Title: Minimalism: A Documentary About the Important Things (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Matt D'Avella
Main Cast: Joshua Fields Millburn, Ryan Nicodemus, Dan Harris, Joshua Becker, Shannon Whitehead
Keywords: homelessness, book, tour, promotion, tiny home, simpler life, minimalism, simplicity, declutter

Overview: How might your life be better with less? MINIMALISM: A Documentary About the Important Things, a feature-length documentary from the popular simple-living duo The Minimalists, examines the many flavors of minimalism by taking the audience inside the lives of minimalists from various walks of life.
