Title: Chopping Mall (1986)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Jim Wynorski
Main Cast: Kelli Maroney, Tony O'Dell, Barbara Crampton, Russell Todd, Karrie Emerson
Keywords: sex, nudity, laser, shopping mall, remote control, party, electrocution, robot, mall, lockdown

Overview: A group of teenagers that work at the mall all get together for a late night party in one of the stores. When the mall goes on lock down before they can get out, the robot security system activates after a malfunction and goes on a killing spree. One by one the three bots try to rid the mall of the "intruders". The only weapons the kids can use are the supplies in other stores, or if they can make it till morning when the mall opens back up.
