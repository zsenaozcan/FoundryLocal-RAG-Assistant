Title: Unconscious (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Mystery, Romance
Director: Joaquín Oristrell
Main Cast: Leonor Watling, Luis Tosar, Mercedes Sampietro, Juanjo Puigcorbé, Àlex Brendemühl

Overview: A Freudian comedy set in Barcelona, 1913, that playfully questions sexual taboos through a Sherlock Holmes-style investigation.
