Title: Susana (1951)
Genres: Drama
Director: Luis Buñuel
Main Cast: Fernando Soler, Rosita Quintana, Víctor Manuel Mendoza, Matilde Palou, María Gentil Arcos
Keywords: based on novel, jealousy, independent film, religion, sexual attraction, escaped convict, young woman, hypocrisy, temptation, farm hand
