Title: Bloody Angels (1998)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Karin Julsrud
Main Cast: Reidar Sørensen, Jon Øigarden, Gaute Skjegstad, Trond Hovik, Stig Henrik Hoff

Overview: A few months after a girl with developmental disabilities is murdered in the remote village Høtten, the two young men who most townsfolk think killed her go missing. A police detective is sent to investigate and finds this very challenging.
