Title: Wild City (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Crime
Director: Ringo Lam
Main Cast: Louis Koo, Shawn Yue, Tong Liya, Yuen Qiu, Joseph Chang Hsiao-Chuan

Overview: Former cop-turned-bar owner Kwok and his underachieving half-brother befriends a drunken woman, they soon find themselves targeted by both her former lover, a high-powered attorney, and the gangster he employs. A suitcase full of tainted cash enters the picture as Kwok finds himself torn between the gangsters and his former colleagues.
