Title: Dark Touch (2013)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Marina de Van
Main Cast: Missy Keating, Marcella Plunkett, Charlotte Flyvholm, Pádraic Delaney, Art Parkinson
Keywords: house on fire, woman director, death of parents

Overview: Niamh is the lone survivor of a bloody massacre after the furniture and objects in her family’s isolated house take on a monstrous life of their own. The police ignore her wild stories and the family friends and social worker who take her in try to introduce a new life. But in this psychological thriller, Niamh is unable to leave her violent past behind her, endangering everyone who crosses her path.
