Title: Opposing Force (1986)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Eric Karson
Main Cast: Tom Skerritt, Lisa Eichhorn, Richard Roundtree, Anthony Zerbe

Overview: A group of elite soldiers, including one woman, sign up for the ultimate training mission. The group parachutes onto a remote island, where their objective is to reach the safety zone before the "opposing force" captures them. Everything does not go as expected, and the training mission turns into the real thing.
