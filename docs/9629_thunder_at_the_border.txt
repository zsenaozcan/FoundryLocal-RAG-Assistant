Title: Thunder at the Border (1966)
Genres: Adventure, Western
Director: Alfred Vohrer
Main Cast: Rod Cameron, Pierre Brice, Marie Versini, Todd Armstrong, Harald Leipnitz
Keywords: prison, gang war, native american, american west

Overview: Firehand and his Apache friend Winnetou are determined to get justice for the murder of four young braves. They set off to track down the gang responsible for the horrendous act.
