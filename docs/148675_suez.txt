Title: Suez (1938)
Genres: Drama, History, Romance
Director: Allan Dwan
Main Cast: Tyrone Power, Loretta Young, Annabella, J. Edward Bromberg, Joseph Schildkraut
Keywords: donkey, false accusations

Overview: Ferdinand de Lesseps, disappointed in love, is sent as a junior diplomat to the Isthmus of Suez, and realizes it's just the place for a canal.
