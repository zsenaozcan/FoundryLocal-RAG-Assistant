Title: She-Wolf of London (1946)
Genres: Crime, Horror, Mystery
Director: Jean Yarbrough
Main Cast: Don Porter, June Lockhart, Sara Haden, Jan Wiley, Lloyd Corrigan
Keywords: england, scotland yard, monster, insanity, murder, curse, werewolf, child murder

Overview: A young heiress finds evidence suggesting that at night she acts under the influence of a family curse and has begun committing ghastly murders in a nearby park.
