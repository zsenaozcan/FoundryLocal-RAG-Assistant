Title: Diary of a Wimpy Kid: The Long Haul (2017)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: David Bowers
Main Cast: Jason Ian Drucker, Owen Asztalos, Charlie Wright, Alicia Silverstone, Tom Everett Scott

Overview: A Heffley family road trip to attend Meemaw's 90th birthday party goes hilariously off course thanks to Greg's newest scheme to get to a video gaming convention.
