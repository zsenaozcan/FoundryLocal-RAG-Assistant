Title: Figures in a Landscape (1970)
Genres: Thriller, Action
Director: Joseph Losey
Main Cast: Robert Shaw, Malcolm McDowell, Henry Woolf, Christopher Malcolm, Andy Bradford
Keywords: based on novel, chase, independent film, escaped prisoner

Overview: Two escaped convicts (Robert Shaw and Malcolm McDowell) are on the run in an unnamed Latin American country. But everywhere they go, they are followed and hounded by a menacing black helicopter.
