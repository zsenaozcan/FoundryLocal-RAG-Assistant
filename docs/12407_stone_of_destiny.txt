Title: Stone of Destiny (2008)
Genres: Drama, Adventure, Crime
Director: Charles Martin Smith
Main Cast: Robert Carlyle, Peter Mullan, Billy Boyd, Charlie Cox, Kate Mara
Keywords: scotland, patriotism, nationalism, students' movement, heist, independent film

Overview: Tells of the daring heist of The Stone of Destiny in the 1950s by a charming group of idealistic Scottish undergraduates, whose action rekindled Scottish nationalistic pride.
