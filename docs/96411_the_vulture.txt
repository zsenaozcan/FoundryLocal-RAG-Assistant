Title: The Vulture (1982)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: Ferenc András
Main Cast: Cserhalmi György, Temessy Hédi, Zita Perczel, Maria Gladkowska
Keywords: taxi driver, pickpocket

Overview: Two old women who happen to be pickpockets stole money from Simon, the taxi driver. The police are unable to find the thieves, so Simon decides to find them himself.
