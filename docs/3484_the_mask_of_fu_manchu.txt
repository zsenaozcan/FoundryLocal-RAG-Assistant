Title: The Mask of Fu Manchu (1932)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Horror
Director: Charles Brabin
Main Cast: Boris Karloff, Lewis Stone, Karen Morley, Charles Starrett, Myrna Loy
Keywords: treasure, sword, expedition, mask, grave, genghis khan, dr. fu manchu

Overview: Boris Karloff stars as the villainous Dr. Fu Manchu in a race with a team of Englishmen to find the tomb of Ghengis Khan. Fu Manchu wants to use the relics to cause an uprising in the East to wipe out the white race.
