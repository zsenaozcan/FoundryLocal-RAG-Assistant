Title: Rise of the Zombies (2012)
Genres: Action, Horror, Thriller
Director: Nick Lyon
Main Cast: Danny Trejo, Mariel Hemingway, LeVar Burton, Ethan Suplee, Chad Lindberg
Keywords: san francisco, alcatraz, zombie, vaccine

Overview: When zombies overrun San Francisco, a desperate group survives by locking themselves inside Alcatraz Prison. When the undead breach the island, our heros are forced to return to the mainland overrun with the undead.
