Title: Urban Menace (1999)
Genres: Horror, Action, Thriller
Director: Albert Pyun
Main Cast: Snoop Dogg, Big Pun, Ice-T, Fat Joe, T. J. Storm
Keywords: hip-hop, tragedy, serial killer, urban setting

Overview: An insane preacher seeks retribution from the local crime syndicate for the violent death of his family and the burning of his church in a horrendous ghetto crime spree.
