Title: Friday Foster (1975)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Arthur Marks
Main Cast: Pam Grier, Yaphet Kotto, Godfrey Cambridge, Thalmus Rasulala, Eartha Kitt
Keywords: airport, photographer, pimp, hitman, murder, conspiracy, blaxploitation, model

Overview: Friday Foster, an ex-model magazine photographer, goes to Los Angeles International airport to photograph the arrival of Blake Tarr, the richest black man in America. Three men attempt to assassinate Tarr. Foster photographs the melee and is plunged into a web of conspiracy involving the murder of her childhood friend, a US senator, and a shadowy plan called "Black Widow".
