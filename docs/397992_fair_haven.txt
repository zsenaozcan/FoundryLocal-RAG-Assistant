Title: Fair Haven (2017)
Genres: Family, Drama
Director: Kerstin Karlhuber
Main Cast: Tom Wopat, Michael Grant, Gregory Harrison, Jennifer Taylor, Josh Green
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A young man returns to his family farm, after a long stay in ex-gay conversion therapy, and is torn between the expectations of his emotionally distant father, and the memories of a past, loving relationship he has tried to bury.
