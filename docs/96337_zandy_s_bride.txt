Title: Zandy's Bride (1974)
Genres: Western
Director: Jan Troell
Main Cast: Gene Hackman, Liv Ullmann, Eileen Heckart, Susan Tyrrell, Harry Dean Stanton
Keywords: rape, wrestling, bear, mail order bride, 19th century

Overview: Zandy Allan purchases a mail-order bride, Hannah Lund. He treats her as a possession, without respect or humanity, until their shared ordeal as they struggle to survive develops in him a growing love.
