Title: Turk 182! (1985)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama
Director: Bob Clark
Main Cast: Timothy Hutton, Robert Urich, Kim Cattrall, Robert Culp, Darren McGavin
Keywords: new york, bar, subway, mayor, firemen, graffiti, bridge, scandal, police, new york city

Overview: After New York City firefighter Terry Lynch is unable to receive any compensation for an injury incurred during the off-duty rescue of a young girl, he grows suicidal. Furious, his brother Jimmy attempts to have Mayor Tyler intervene, but the corrupt politician instead denounces Terry as a drunk. Determined to get justice, Terry begins a graffiti campaign of embarrassing slogans mocking the mayor, which soon captivates the city.
