Title: The Mysterious Death of Pérola (2014)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Guto Parente
Main Cast: Ticiana Augusto Lima, Guto Parente

Overview: Away from home, living alone in an old and gloomy apartment, Pérola feels the effects of the time passing, being increasingly taken by nostalgia and fear to a point where dream, fantasy and reality lose its borders.
