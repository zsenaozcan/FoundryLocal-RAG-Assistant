Title: Jaws of Satan (1981)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Bob Claver
Main Cast: Fritz Weaver, Gretchen Corbett, Jon Korkes, Christina Applegate, Bob Hannah
Keywords: witch, snake, druid, priest, cobra, rattlesnake, king cobra

Overview: A preacher whose ancestors were Druids battles Satan, who has taken the form of a huge snake.
