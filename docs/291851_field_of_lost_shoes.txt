Title: Field of Lost Shoes (2015)
Genres: Drama, History, War, Action
Director: Sean McNamara
Main Cast: Lauren Holly, Jason Isaacs, Nolan Gould, Keith David, David Arquette

Overview: A group of teenage cadets sheltered from war at the Virginia Military Institute must confront the horrors of an adult world when they are called upon to defend the Shenandoah Valley.
