Title: Violent City (1970)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Sergio Sollima
Main Cast: Charles Bronson, Telly Savalas, Jill Ireland, Michel Constantin, Umberto Orsini
Keywords: revenge, double crossed, double cross

Overview: A hitman is double-crossed by his girlfriend and barely escapes a murder attempt. He then sets out to take his revenge on the woman and the gang boss who put her up to it.
