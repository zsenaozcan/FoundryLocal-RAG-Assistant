Title: For a Few Bullets (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Mystery, Action, Romance, Adventure
Director: Pan An-Zi
Main Cast: Zhang Jingchu, Lin Gengxin, Liu Xiaoqing

Overview: When a professional thief sees his lifetime goal of being the best potentially realized in stealing a rare artifact, he finds himself entangled in a worldwide plot involving the Soviets, the Japanese and a band of outsiders.  It’s up to him and a special agent to save the world!
