Title: Mandalay (1934)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: Kay Francis, Ricardo Cortez, Warner Oland, Lyle Talbot, Ruth Donnelly
Keywords: alcohol, doctor, prostitution, gunrunner

Overview: Abandoned by her lover, a woman becomes the main "hostess" in a decadent nightclub, but tries to put her past behind her on a steamer to Mandalay.
