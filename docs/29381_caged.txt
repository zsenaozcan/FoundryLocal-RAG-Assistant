Title: Caged (1950)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: John Cromwell
Main Cast: Eleanor Parker, Agnes Moorehead, Ellen Corby, Hope Emerson, Betty Garde
Keywords: prison, women's prison, prison warden, prison guard, film noir

Overview: Caged tells the story of a teenage newlywed, who is sent to prison for being an accessory to a robbery. Her experiences while incarcerated, along with the killing of her husband, change her from a very frightened young girl into a hardened convict.
