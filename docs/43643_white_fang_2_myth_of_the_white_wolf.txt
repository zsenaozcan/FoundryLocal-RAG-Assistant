Title: White Fang 2: Myth of the White Wolf (1994)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Family
Director: Ken Olin
Main Cast: Scott Bairstow, Charmaine Craig, Al Harrington, Alfred Molina, Victoria Racimo
