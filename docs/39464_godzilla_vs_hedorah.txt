Title: Godzilla vs. Hedorah (1971)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama, Horror
Director: Yoshimitsu Banno
Main Cast: Akira Yamauchi, Toshie Kimura, Hiroyuki Kawase, Keiko Mari, Toshio Shiba
Keywords: pollution, godzilla, independent film, kaiju

Overview: Psychedelic animation and a flying Godzilla highlight the 11th Godzilla film.  Spawned from earth's pollution, Hedorah destroys Japan, spews poison gas and fights Godzilla.
