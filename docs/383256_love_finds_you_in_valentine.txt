Title: Love Finds You in Valentine (2016)
Genres: Drama, TV Movie, Romance
Director: Terry Cunningham
Main Cast: Michaela McManus, Diogo Morgado, Ed Asner, Lindsay Wagner, Hunter Cross

Overview: Kennedy Blaine, a Californian girl, inherits a ranch in the small town of Valentine in Nebraska. Before she sells the property, she decides to spend the summer in her house and learn more about her family.
