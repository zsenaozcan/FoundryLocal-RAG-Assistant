Title: Nathalie Granger (1972)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Marguerite Duras
Main Cast: Lucia Bosé, Jeanne Moreau, Luce Garcia-Ville, Gérard Depardieu, Valerie Mascolo
Keywords: deputy, independent film, teacher, mother daughter relationship, woman director

Overview: With little or no embellishment, filmmaker Marguerite Duras offers a simple, often wordless chronicle of a woman's day. She and her friend are seen doing yard work, talking about their families and receiving the occasional visitor. The brightest spot in the day is when a washing machine salesman comes to call.
