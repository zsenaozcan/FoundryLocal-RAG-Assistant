Title: Embodiment of Evil (2008)
Genres: Horror
Director: José Mojica Marins
Main Cast: José Mojica Marins, Jece Valadão, Adriano Stuart, Milhem Cortaz, Rui Rezende
Keywords: grave, crucifixion, kanibalismus, sao paulo

Overview: After 30 years in a cell for the mentally ill, Coffin Joe is finally freed. Back on the streets, the sadistic gravedigger is set on fulfilling that for which he was imprisoned: find the woman who can give him the perfect child. During his wanderings through the city of Sao Paulo he leaves behind a trail of horror as he faces unnatural laws and popular beliefs.
