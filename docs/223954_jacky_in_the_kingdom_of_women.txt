Title: Jacky in the Kingdom of Women (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Riad Sattouf
Main Cast: Vincent Lacoste, Charlotte Gainsbourg, Didier Bourdon, Anémone, Valérie Bonneton

Overview: The film is set in a world, the Popular Democratic Republic of Bubunne, in which women hold the power and men are forced to wear veils and stay at home.
