Title: Latin Lover (2015)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Cristina Comencini
Main Cast: Virna Lisi, Angela Finocchiaro, Valeria Bruni Tedeschi, Marisa Paredes, Candela Peña
Keywords: woman director

Overview: The five daughters of a famous actor, all from different mothers and different nationalities, get together on the 10th anniversary of his death for a celebration of his career.
