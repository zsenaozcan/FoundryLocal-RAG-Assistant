Title: That Night's Wife (1930)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Main Cast: Mitsuko Ichimura, Tokihiko Okada, Chishu Ryu, Tatsuo Saitô, Emiko Yagumo

Overview: The film revolves around a desperate man who commits a crime in order to support his family, and the moral dilemma the policeman who tracks him down finds himself in.
