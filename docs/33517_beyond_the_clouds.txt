Title: Beyond the Clouds (1995)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Wim Wenders
Main Cast: John Malkovich, Fanny Ardant, Chiara Caselli, Irène Jacob, Sophie Marceau

Overview: Made of four short tales, linked by a story filmed by Wim Wenders. Taking place in Ferrara, Portofino, Aix en Provence and Paris, each story, which always a woman as the crux of the story, invites to an inner travel, as Antonioni says "towards the true image of that absolute and mysterious reality that nobody will ever see".
