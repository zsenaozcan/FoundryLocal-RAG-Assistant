Title: Mannequin (1937)
Genres: Drama
Director: Frank Borzage
Main Cast: Joan Crawford, Spencer Tracy, Alan Curtis, Ralph Morgan, Mary Philips
Keywords: shipping magnate, factory girl

Overview: Rags-to-riches Hennessey meets newlyweds Jessie and Eddie from his old neighborhood. Eddie plots to have Jessie divorce him, marry Hennessey, divorce Hennessey, then bring Hennessey's money into remarriage with Eddie. His plan goes awry at several points.
