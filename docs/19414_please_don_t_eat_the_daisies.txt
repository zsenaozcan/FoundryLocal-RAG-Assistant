Title: Please Don't Eat the Daisies (1960)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Romance
Director: Charles Walters
Main Cast: Doris Day, David Niven, Janis Paige, Spring Byington, Richard Haydn
Keywords: professor, romantic comedy, play, critic, theater

Overview: Drama critic Larry McKay, his wife Kay, and their four sons move from their crowded Manhattan apartment to an old house in the country. While housewife Kay settles into suburban life, Larry continues to enjoy the theater and party scene of New York. Kay soon begins to question Larry's fidelity when he mentions a flirtatious encounter with Broadway star Deborah Vaughn
