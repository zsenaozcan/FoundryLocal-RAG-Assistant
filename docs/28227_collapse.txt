Title: Collapse (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Chris Smith
Main Cast: Michael Ruppert
Keywords: capitalism, economics, oil industry, prediction, financial crisis

Overview: From the acclaimed director of American Movie, the documentary follows former Los Angeles police officer turned independent reporter Michael Ruppert. He recounts his career as a radical thinker and spells out his apocalyptic vision of the future, spanning the crises in economics, energy, environment and more.
