Title: The Fake (2013)
Genres: Animation, Drama
Director:  Yeon Sang-Ho
Main Cast: Yang Ik-june, Oh Jung-se, Kwon Hae-hyo, Park Hee-von, Hwang Seok-jeong
Keywords: suspense

Overview: A convict returns to his home town to torturing again his family and face his neighbors trying to destroy the local religious fanatics and their plans to keep money from the village people.
