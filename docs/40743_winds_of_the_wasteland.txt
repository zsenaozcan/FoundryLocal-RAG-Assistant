Title: Winds of the Wasteland (1936)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, Western
Director: Mack V. Wright
Main Cast: John Wayne, Phyllis Cerf, Lew Kelly, Douglas Cosgrove, Lane Chandler
Keywords: contract, stagecoach, pony express, telegraph

Overview: The arrival of the telegraph put Pony Express riders like John Blair and his pal Smoky out of work. A race will decide whether they or stageline owner Drake get the government mail contract.
