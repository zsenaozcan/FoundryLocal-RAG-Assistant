Title: Mercenaries (2014)
Genres: Action
Director: Christopher Ray
Main Cast: Zoë Bell, Kristanna Loken, Vivica A. Fox, Brigitte Nielsen, Cynthia Rothrock
Keywords: prison, mercenary, rescue, mockbuster

Overview: A diplomatic official is captured and imprisoned while touring a war zone, so a team of elite female commandos is assembled to infiltrate a women's prison for a daring rescue.
