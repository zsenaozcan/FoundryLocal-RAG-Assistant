Title: Kristin Lavransdatter (1995)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Liv Ullmann
Main Cast: Linus Aaberg, Gisken Armand, Berard Arnø, Bernhard Arnø, Peter Baden
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Kristin Lavransdatter is a 1995 Norwegian film directed by Liv Ullman, featuring Elisabeth Matheson, Bjørn Skagestad, Jørgen Langhelle, Lena Endre and Sverre Anker Ousdal, based on Kristin Lavransdatter by Sigrid Undset. It was the Norwegian entry to the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film in 1996.
