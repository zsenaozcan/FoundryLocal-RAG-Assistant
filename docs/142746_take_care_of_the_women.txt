Title: Take Care of the Women! (1981)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Music
Director: Viktor Makarov
Main Cast: Andrey Gradov, Marina Shimanskaya, Svetlana Penkina, Yelena Tonunts, Galina Venevitinova

Overview: Young journalist starts working on the ship in order to write a story. The only problem is - all the ships crew are young girls.
