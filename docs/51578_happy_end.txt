Title: Happy End (1967)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Oldřich Lipský
Main Cast: Vladimír Menšík, Josef Abrhám, Jaroslava Obermaierová, Bohuš Záhorský, Stella Zázvorková

Overview: A dark comedy about a murder and its consequences presented in a backwards manner, where death is actually a rebirth. The film starts with an "execution" of the main protagonist and goes back to explore his previous actions and motivations.
