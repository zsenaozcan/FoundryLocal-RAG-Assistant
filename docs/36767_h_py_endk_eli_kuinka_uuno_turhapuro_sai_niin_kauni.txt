Title: Häpy Endkö? Eli kuinka Uuno Turhapuro sai niin kauniin ja rikkaan vaimon (1977)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ere Kokkonen
Main Cast: Vesa-Matti Loiri, Marjatta Raita, Ritva Valkama, Tapio Hämäläinen, Simo Salminen

Overview: Uuno moves in flight cargo to Helsinki for the search of a rich, beautiful wife. He buys elegant clothes for eight marks from an estate auction and in two weeks becomes the most pursued bachelor in town, charming all the women. He also starts as a waiter in Vaaleanpunainen sika (Pink Pig), which starts the first quarrels between him and his father-in-law.
