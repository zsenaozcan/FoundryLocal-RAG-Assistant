Title: The Country Girl (1954)
Genres: Music, Drama
Director: George Seaton
Main Cast: Bing Crosby, Grace Kelly, William Holden, Anthony Ross, Gene Reynolds
Keywords: diva, song, stage fright, alcoholic, drunkenness, actor

Overview: The 1954 musical film based on the play by the same name. Actress Grace Kelly won an Academy Award for Best Actress. The film tells the story of an ex-theater actor who is given one more chance to star in a musical yet his alcoholism may prevent it from happening.
