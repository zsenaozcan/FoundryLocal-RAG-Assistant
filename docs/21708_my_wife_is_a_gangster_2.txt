Title: My Wife Is a Gangster 2 (2003)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Jeong Heung-sun
Main Cast: Shin Eun-Kyung, Park Jun-gyu, Jang Se-jin, Joo Hyun, Hong Soo-a

Overview: Former Scissor Gang leader Eun-jin (Shin Eun-kyung), who now suffers from amnesia, nevertheless defends Jae-cheol (Jun Gyu Park), a restaurateur who employs her, from a gangster who wants his business.
