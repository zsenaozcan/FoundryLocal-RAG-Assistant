Title: Do You See Me? (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Riccardo Milani
Main Cast: Paola Cortellesi, Raoul Bova, Ennio Fantastichini, Lunetta Savino
Keywords: crush, unrequited love, homosexuality, pioneer women, european women

Overview: Serena Bruno is an architect who studied and got several masters in different countries of the world but she decides to go back to Italy so she can live and work there. She will soon find out that her native country is not as open minded and she will struggle to find a job that she truly loves and is passionate about. Along the way she will meet Francesco a gay man who will help and support her throughout her journey.
