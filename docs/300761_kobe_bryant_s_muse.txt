Title: Kobe Bryant's Muse (2015)
Genres: Documentary, TV Movie
Director: Gotham Chopra
Main Cast: Kobe Bryant
Keywords: basketball

Overview: Documentary that goes behind the stats to reveal the story of Kobe Bryant's basketball career, exploring the mentorships, allies and rivalries that have helped shape his stellar 18-year tenure in the NBA, and offering access to his daily experiences, his lifelong inspirations and the battle with his greatest personal challenge yet
