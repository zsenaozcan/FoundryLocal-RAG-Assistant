Title: Manufactured Landscapes (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jennifer Baichwal
Main Cast: Edward Burtynsky
Keywords: china, factory, shanghai, ship, tour, industry, pollution, recycling, dam, independent film

Overview: MANUFACTURED LANDSCAPES is the striking new documentary on the world and work of renowned artist Edward Burtynsky. Internationally acclaimed for his large-scale photographs of “manufactured landscapes”—quarries, recycling yards, factories, mines and dams—Burtynsky creates stunningly beautiful art from civilization’s materials and debris.
