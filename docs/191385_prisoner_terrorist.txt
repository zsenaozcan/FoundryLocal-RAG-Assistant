Title: Prisoner/Terrorist (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Masao Adachi
Main Cast: Tomorowo Taguchi, Taka Okubo, Joji Kajiwara, Keiko Oginome, Shoichi Honda

Overview: During a suicide attack on an airport, the hand grenade of 'M', one of three terrorists, malfunctions, leaving him captured. Exposed to maltreatment in prison, he slowly loses his grip on reality as he is forced to confront his ideological convictions.
