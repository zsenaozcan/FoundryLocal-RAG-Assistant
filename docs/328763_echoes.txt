Title: Echoes (2014)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Nils Timm
Main Cast: Steven Brand, Kevin Brewerton, Ivory Dortch, Kate French
Keywords: sleep, paralysis

Overview: A young writer experiences visions during episodes of sleep paralysis, and she retreats with her boyfriend to an isolated house in the desert. As the visions worsen, she teeters on the edge of insanity as she uncovers a life-threatening secret.
