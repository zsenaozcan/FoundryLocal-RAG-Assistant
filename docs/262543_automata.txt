Title: Automata (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Gabe Ibáñez
Main Cast: Antonio Banderas, Birgitte Hjort Sørensen, Dylan McDermott, Robert Forster, Tim McInnerny
Keywords: artificial intelligence, rain, future, dystopia, robot, ecology, desert, child hitman

Overview: Jacq Vaucan, an insurance agent of ROC robotics corporation, routinely investigates the case of manipulating a robot. What he discovers will have profound consequences for the future of humanity.
