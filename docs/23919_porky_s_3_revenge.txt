Title: Porky's 3: Revenge (1985)
Genres: Comedy
Director: James Komack
Main Cast: Dan Monahan, Wyatt Knight, Tony Ganios, Mark Herrier, Kaki Hunter
Keywords: student, teenager, mischief, misadventure

Overview: As graduation nears for the class of 1955 at Angel Beach High, the gang once again faces off against their old enemy, Porky, who wants them to throw the school's championship basketball game since he has bet on the opposing team.
