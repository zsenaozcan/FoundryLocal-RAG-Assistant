Title: Fortress (2012)
Genres: Action, War
Director: Mike Phillips
Main Cast: Bug Hall, Donnie Jeffcoat, Sean McGowan, Chris Owen, Edward Finlay
Keywords: world war ii, bomber, pilot, flight

Overview: When the commander of the crew of a B-17 Flying Fortress bomber is killed in action in a raid over Sicily in 1943, his replacement, a young, naive pilot struggles to be accepted by the plane's already tight-knit Irish American crew.
