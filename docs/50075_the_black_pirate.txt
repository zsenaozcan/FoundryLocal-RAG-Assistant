Title: The Black Pirate (1926)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action
Director: Albert Parker
Main Cast: Douglas Fairbanks, Billie Dove, Anders Randolf, Donald Crisp, Tempe Pigott
Keywords: pirate

Overview: A nobleman vows to avenge the death of his father at the hands of pirates. To this end he infiltrates the pirate band. Acting in character he is instrumental in the capture of a ship, but things are complicated when he finds that there is a young woman on board whom he wishes to protect from the threat of rape.
