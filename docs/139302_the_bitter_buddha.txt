Title: The Bitter Buddha (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Steven Feinartz
Main Cast: Zach Galifianakis, Sarah Silverman, Patton Oswalt, Sean Conroy, Paul Provenza
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: The Documentary takes an unconventional journey through the life of one of America's most original comedic voices. Eddie Pepitone, "The Bitter Buddha", is looked at in this portrait of creativity, enlightenment and rage.
