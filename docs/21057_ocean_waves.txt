Title: Ocean Waves (1993)
Genres: Romance, Animation, Drama
Director: Tomomi Mochizuki
Main Cast: Toshihiko Seki, Youko Sakamoto, Yuri Amano, Kae Araki, Jun'ichi Kanemaru
Keywords: affectation, friendship, love, surrealism, tokyo japan, falling in love, high school reunion, unspoken love

Overview: At Kichijōji Station, Tokyo, Taku Morisaki glimpses a familiar woman on the platform opposite boarding a train. Later, her photo falls from a shelf as he exits his apartment before flying to Kōchi Prefecture. Picking it up, he looks at it briefly before leaving. As the aeroplane takes off he narrates the events that brought her into his life...
