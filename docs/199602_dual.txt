Title: Dual (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nejc Gazvoda
Main Cast: Nina Rakovec, Mia Nielsen-Jexen, Branko Čakarmiš, Marjan Brulc, Olga Frank
Keywords: lesbian, lgbt

Overview: The paths of two girls cross one evening in Ljubljana. Tina, a Slovene, and Iben, a Danish girl of similar age, both experiencing some kind of turning point in their lives, open up to one another and soon develop a close bond...
