Title: In God We Teach (2011)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Vic Losick
Main Cast: David Paszkiewicz, Matthew LaClair
Keywords: high school, legal battle, church and state

Overview: “In God We Teach” is the story of Matthew LaClair, a student at Kearny (NJ) public high school who secretly recorded his history teacher, David Paszkiewicz in class, and accused him of proselytizing for Jesus.
