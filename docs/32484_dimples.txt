Title: Dimples (1936)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: William A. Seiter
Main Cast: Shirley Temple, Frank Morgan, Robert Kent, Helen Westley, Stepin Fetchit

Overview: Shirley Temple lives with the pick-pocket grandfather in 19th century New York City. She entertains the crowds while he works his racket. A rich lady makes it possible for the girl to go legit. "Uncle Tom's Cabin" is performed.
