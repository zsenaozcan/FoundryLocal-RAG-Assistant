Title: Eddie Izzard: Dress to Kill (1999)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Lawrence Jordan
Main Cast: Eddie Izzard
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Executive transvestite Eddie Izzard takes his show to San Francisco to give a brief history of pagan and Christian religions, the building of Stonehenge, the birth of the Church of England and of Western empires, and the need for a European dream.
