Title: The Slanted Screen (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jeff Adachi
Main Cast: Frankie Chin, Bobby Lee, Jason Scott Lee, Will Yun Lee, Tzi Ma

Overview: From silent film star Sessue Hayakawa to Harold and Kumar Go to Whitecastle, the Slanted Screen examines the portrayal Asian men in film and television, and how new filmmakers are now re-defining age-old stereotypes.
