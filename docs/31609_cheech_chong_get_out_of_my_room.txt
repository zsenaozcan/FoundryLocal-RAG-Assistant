Title: Cheech & Chong Get Out of My Room (1985)
Genres: Comedy, Music
Director: Cheech Marin
Main Cast: Cheech Marin, Tommy Chong, John Paragon, Beverly D'Angelo, Cassandra Peterson
Keywords: marijuana, dope, mockumentary, hispanic

Overview: Get Out of My Room, was a mockumentary in the style of This Is Spinal Tap, written and directed by Cheech Marin. In the film, he and Tommy Chong are shown attempting to finish a "video album" for their novelty record Get Out of My Room.
