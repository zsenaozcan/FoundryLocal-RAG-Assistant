Title: Viva Riva! (2010)
Genres: Action, Drama, Foreign, Thriller
Director: Djo Munga
Main Cast: Patsha Bay, Manie Malone, Hoji Fortuna, Marlene Longange, Diplome Amekindra

Overview: Riva is an operator, a man with charm and ambition in equal measure. Kinshasa is an inviting place. With petrol in short supply in DRC's capital, he and his sidekick pursue a plot to get hold of a secret cache - barrels of fuel they can sell for a huge profit. Of course they're not the only ones who want the stuff.
