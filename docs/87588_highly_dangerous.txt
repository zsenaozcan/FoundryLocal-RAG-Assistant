Title: Highly Dangerous (1950)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Roy Ward Baker
Main Cast: Margaret Lockwood, Dane Clark, Marius Goring, Naunton Wayne, Wilfrid Hyde-White
Keywords: newspaper man

Overview: A U.S. newsman (Dane Clark) and a British entomologist (Margaret Lockwood) spy on germ-warfare research in a mythical country.
