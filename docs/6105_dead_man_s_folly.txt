Title: Dead Man's Folly (1986)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller, Mystery
Director: Clive Donner
Main Cast: Peter Ustinov, Jean Stapleton, Constance Cummings, Tim Pigott-Smith, Jonathan Cecil
Keywords: detective, murder mystery

Overview: During a murder hunt game at a country house, to which Hercule Poirot is invited as an "expert", a real murder occurs.
