Title: How to Make Love to a Woman (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Scott Culver
Main Cast: Josh Meyers, Krysten Ritter, Jenna Jameson, Ian Somerhalder, James Hong

Overview: When Andy hears his girlfriend Lauren sigh after sex, he panics. But even with the help of his friends, porn stars and tantric experts, he continues to stumble in the bedroom, finally realizing that in order to give Lauren the 'O' word, he first needs to say the 'L' word. Starring Josh Meyers, Krysten Ritter, Ian Somerhalder, Jenna Jameson and Ken Jeong.
