Title: I Live in Fear (1955)
Genres: Drama
Director: Akira Kurosawa
Main Cast: Toshirō Mifune, Takashi Shimura, Minoru Chiaki, Eiko Miyoshi, Kyôko Aoyama
Keywords: foundry, all knowning psychiatrist

Overview: Kiichi Nakajima, an elderly foundry owner convinced that Japan will be affected by an imminent nuclear war, resolves to move his family to safety in Brazil. His family decides to have him ruled incompetent and Dr. Harada, a Domestic Court counselor, attempts to arbitrate.
