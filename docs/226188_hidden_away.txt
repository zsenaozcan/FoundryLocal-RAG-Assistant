Title: Hidden Away (2013)
Genres: Mystery, Drama, Thriller
Director: Peter Sullivan
Main Cast: Emmanuelle Vaugier, Ivan Sergei, Sean Patrick Flanery, Elisabeth Röhm, Allie Gonino

Overview: A woman and young daughter escape her abusive husband by faking their deaths. Eight years later she is happily living in the upscale Palm Springs with her now-17-year-old daughter. When her husband discovers they are still alive, he tracks them down and spies on them to learn all about the new life they've created until he can exact his revenge.
