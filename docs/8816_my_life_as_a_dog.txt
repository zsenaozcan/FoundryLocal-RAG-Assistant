Title: My Life as a Dog (1985)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Lasse Hallström
Main Cast: Anton Glanzelius, Tomas von Brömssen, Anki Lidén, Kicki Rundgren, Melinda Kinnaman
Keywords: underdog, sweden, uncle, loneliness

Overview: A boy and his brother don't get along well. In order to let their ill mother rest, they are separated and sent to different relatives.
