Title: Love Comes Lately (2007)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Jan Schütte
Main Cast: Otto Tausig, Rhea Perlman, Barbara Hershey, Tovah Feldshuh, Elizabeth Peña

Overview: Though approaching his eighties, Max Kohn shows no signs of slowing down. He pursues his love life - both real and imagined - with youthful vigor, thereby risking his relationship to Reisel, the woman he loves but neglects. LOVE COMES LATELY is a film about real and imagined longings, the never ending dream of love and the power of fiction.
