Title: Golden Boy (1939)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Rouben Mamoulian
Main Cast: Barbara Stanwyck, Adolphe Menjou, William Holden, Lee J. Cobb, Joseph Calleia
Keywords: music lover, boxing match, sport, melodrama, boxing

Overview: Despite his talent as a musician, a city boy decides to become a boxer. He's successful as a fighter -- much to the dismay of his parents. When gangsters try to by a piece of him he begins to have second thoughts.
