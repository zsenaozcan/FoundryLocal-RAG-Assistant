Title: The Russian Woodpecker (2015)
Genres: Documentary, History
Director: Chad Gracia
Main Cast: Fedor Alexandrovich, Andrei Alexandrovich, Igor Alexandrovich, Natalia Barabovskaya, Andrei Bilyk
Keywords: russia, woodpecker, nuclear catastrophe, chernobyl

Overview: As his country is gripped by revolution and war, a Ukrainian victim of the Chernobyl nuclear disaster discovers a dark secret and must decide whether to risk his life and play his part in the revolution by revealing it.
