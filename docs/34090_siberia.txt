Title: Siberia (1998)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Romance, Foreign
Director: Robert Jan Westdijk
Main Cast: Hugo Metsers, Vlatka Simac, Nicole Eggert, Roeland Fernhout

Overview: Goof and Hugo are flatmates in Amsterdam who have made a tidy sum from having sex with tourists, then robbing them. Their goal is to spend the money together on a world trip and their scam is going well until they meet Lara, originally from Siberia, whom Goof becomes besotted with (IMDB)
