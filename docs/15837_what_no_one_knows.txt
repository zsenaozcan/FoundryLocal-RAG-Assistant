Title: What No One Knows (2008)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Søren Kragh-Jacobsen
Main Cast: Anders W. Berthelsen, Sarah Boberg, Sonja Richter, Ghita Nørby, Lars Mikkelsen

Overview: When Thomas´ sister dies in an accident, he discovers that her death may be connected to their deceased father´s work in military intelligence. As Thomas embarks on an investigation he soon finds himself and his family under surveillance by an unknown group of people.
