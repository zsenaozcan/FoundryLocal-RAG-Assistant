Title: Serial (Bad) Weddings (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Philippe de Chauveron
Main Cast: Christian Clavier, Chantal Lauby, Ary Abittan, Medi Sadoun, Frédéric Chau
Keywords: france, marriage, racism

Overview: A catholic French couple sees their life upside down when their four daughters get married to men of different religion and origins.
