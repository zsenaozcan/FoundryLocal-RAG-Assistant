Title: Fixed Bayonets! (1951)
Genres: Action, Drama, War
Director: Samuel Fuller
Main Cast: Richard Basehart, Gene Evans, Michael O'Shea, Richard Hylton, Craig Hill
Keywords: u.s. army, army, korea, infantry

Overview: The story of a platoon during the Korean War. One by one, Corporal Denno's superiors are killed until it comes to the point where he must try to take command responsibility.
