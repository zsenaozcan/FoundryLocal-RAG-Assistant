Title: The Barber of Rio (1996)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Giovanni Veronesi
Main Cast: Diego Abatantuono, Zuleica Dos Santos, Irene Grandi, Margaret Mazzantini, Giuseppe Oristanio

Overview: Matteo is a Roman barber. In Roma his activity is driving him almost to ruin, he is full of debts. He decides to escape from this depressing reality by accepting his sister Angelina's invitation to Brazil.
