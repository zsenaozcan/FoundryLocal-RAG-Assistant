Title: The Comeback (1978)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Pete Walker
Main Cast: Jack Jones, Pamela Stephenson, David Doyle, Richard Johnson, Holly Palance
Keywords: pop star, proto-slasher

Overview: A singer holes up at a sinister estate to write new songs for his act. The ghost of his murdered wife begins to haunt him, then the person who actually killed her shows up at the mansion.
