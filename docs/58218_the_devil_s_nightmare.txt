Title: The Devil's Nightmare (1971)
Genres: Horror, Fantasy
Director: Jean Brismée
Main Cast: Erika Blanc, Jean Servais, Daniel Emilfork, Jacques Monseau, Lucien Raimbourg
Keywords: castle, tourist, priest, devil, demon

Overview: Six of seven tourists in a east European castle overnight are victims of a helper of the devil. Is Satan willing to spare their souls?
