Title: Ogni maledetto Natale (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Giacomo Ciarrapico
Main Cast: Alessandro Cattelan, Alessandra Mastronardi, Francesco Pannofino, Valerio Mastandrea, Laura Morante

Overview: Massimo Marinelli Lops and Giulia Colardo meet by chance in Rome and fall in love. Giulia asks Massimo to spend Christmas Eve with her family.
