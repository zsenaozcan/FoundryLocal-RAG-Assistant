Title: Run for Cover (1955)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Nicholas Ray
Main Cast: James Cagney, Viveca Lindfors, John Derek, Ernest Borgnine, Grant Withers
Keywords: train, secret past, new sheriff

Overview: Western that starts when a man (Matt Dow) is mistaken as a train robber. After the town's sheriff shoots the kid he's riding with, Dow clears his name and ends up as the new sheriff. He romances a Swedish woman and settles in to a peaceful life only to find that the boy has a few secrets of his own.
