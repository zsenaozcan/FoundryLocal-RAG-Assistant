Title: Ax 'Em (1992)
Genres: Horror
Director: Michael Mfume
Main Cast: Michael Mfume, Sandra Pulley, Joe Clair, Racquel Price, Tracy Wiggs
Keywords: ax

Overview: A weekend retreat at a remote cabin in the woods for a group of childhood pals turns into a terrifying fight for survival, as a former friend whose family was killed years earlier comes along looking for revenge.
