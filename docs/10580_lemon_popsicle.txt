Title: Lemon Popsicle (1978)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Boaz Davidson
Main Cast: Yftach Katzur, Jonathan Sagall, Zachi Noy, Anat Atzmon, Dvora Kedar
Keywords: israel, unwillingly pregnant

Overview: Bentzi, Momo, and Yuda'leh are three teenage boys growing up in 1950's Israel, which is not much different from 1950's America if you believe what you see in the film. Of course they are obsessed with one thing - sex. Bentzi is interested in the beautiful Nili, but she prefers his friend Momo.
