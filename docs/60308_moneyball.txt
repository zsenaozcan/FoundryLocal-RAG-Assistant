Title: Moneyball (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bennett Miller
Main Cast: Brad Pitt, Jonah Hill, Philip Seymour Hoffman, Robin Wright, Chris Pratt
Keywords: underdog, based on novel, baseball, teamwork, sport, partner, meeting, oakland california, strategy, voice over
