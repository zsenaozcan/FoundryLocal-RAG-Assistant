Title: Blood Freak (1972)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror
Director: Brad F. Grinter
Main Cast: Steve Hawkes, Dana Cullivan, Heather Hughes
Keywords: lsd, biker, turkey farm

Overview: A biker comes upon a girl with a flat tire and offers her a ride home. He winds up at a drug party with the girl's sister, then follows her to a turkey farm owned by her father, a mad scientist. The father turns the biker into a giant turkey monster who goes after drug dealers.
