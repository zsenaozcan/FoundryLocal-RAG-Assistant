Title: Tsiou (2005)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Makis Papadimitratos
Main Cast: Alexandros Parisis, Makis Papadimitratos, Anastasis Kolovos, Jenny Theona, Kalliopi Tzermani

Overview: Athens, mid-August. Tsiou, addicted to substances, while trying to get his dose, mixes a number of people to his search through his sister and her mobster husband. A chain of meetings follows where the pressure passes from one to another.
