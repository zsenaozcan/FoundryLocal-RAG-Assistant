Title: The Scarlet Dove (1961)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Matti Kassila
Main Cast: Tauno Palo, Gunvor Sandkvist, Helen Elde, Matti Oravisto, Risto Mäkelä

Overview: Doctor Aitamaa is spending holiday with his family at their summer villa. He accidentally reads a letter that was meant to his wife. The letter is from another man. Aitamaa fallows his wife to the city and finds out that she has a lover. Later the wife is found killed and Aitamaa becomes the prime suspect.
