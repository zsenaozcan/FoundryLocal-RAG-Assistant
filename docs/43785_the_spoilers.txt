Title: The Spoilers (1942)
Genres: Action, Drama, Romance, Western
Director: Ray Enright
Main Cast: Marlene Dietrich, Randolph Scott, John Wayne, Margaret Lindsay, Harry Carey
Keywords: gold, miner, nome alaska

Overview: When honest ship captain Roy Glennister gets swindled out of his mine claim, he turns to saloon singer Cherry Malotte for assistance in his battle with no-good town kingpin Alexander McNamara.
