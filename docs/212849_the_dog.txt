Title: The Dog (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Allison Berg
Main Cast: John Wojtowicz, Liz Eden, George Heath, Teresa Wojtowicz, Carmen Bifulco
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In 1972, John Wojtowicz attempted to rob a Brooklyn bank to pay for his lover’s sex-change operation. The story was the basis for the film Dog Day Afternoon. The Dog captures John, who shares his story for the first time in his own unique, offensive, hilarious and heartbreaking way.
