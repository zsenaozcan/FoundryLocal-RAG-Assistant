Title: Cry of the Banshee (1970)
Genres: Horror
Director: Gordon Hessler
Main Cast: Vincent Price, Elisabeth Bergner, Essy Persson, Hugh Griffith, Sally Geeson
Keywords: female nudity, rape, witch, nudity, sorcery, tudors, revenge, murder, independent film, werewolf

Overview: In seventeenth century England Lord Whitman wages unending war on what he sees as the ever-present scourge of witchcraft, and many local villagers have suffered at his hands. But one victim uses her occult powers to curse his family, enlisting unknowing help from one of the household.
