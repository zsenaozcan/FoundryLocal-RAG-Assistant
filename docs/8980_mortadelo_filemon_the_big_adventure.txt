Title: Mortadelo & Filemon: The Big Adventure (2003)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Javier Fesser
Main Cast: Benito Pocino, Pepe Viyuela, Dominique Pinon, Paco Sagárzazu, Mariano Venancio
Keywords: inventor, laser, double agent, secret agent, faint, weapon, spoof

Overview: In the Headquarters of the T.I.A. (Terminal Intelligence Agency), someone has stolen Professor Bacterio's most dangerous invention, the D.O.T. (Demoralizer of Troops), an artifact that ends up in the hands of a very short, wacky dictator who is ready to use it for criminal purposes. The T.I.A Chief, though, is firm in his resolve: if he wants to get the D.O.T. back, he must NOT count on his agents Mortadelo &amp; Filemon. But when the crime fighting duo discover that the T.I.A. has engaged a cocky and slimy detective from outside the agency, they decide to act at their own risk, even if that risk involves all of Humanity.
