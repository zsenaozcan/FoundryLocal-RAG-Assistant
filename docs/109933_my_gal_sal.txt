Title: My Gal Sal (1942)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Irving Cummings
Main Cast: Rita Hayworth, Victor Mature, John Sutton, Carole Landis, James Gleason
Keywords: musical, biography

Overview: Biopic chronicling the early life of gay nineties-era songwriter Paul Dresser as he outgrows his job as carnival entertainer and moves up into New York society, writing one hit song after another. Despite his egotistical behavior, he manages to woo and win Sally Elliott, one of the more popular songstresses of the day
