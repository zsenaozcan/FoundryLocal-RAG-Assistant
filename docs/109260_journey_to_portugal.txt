Title: Journey to Portugal (2011)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Sérgio Tréfaut
Main Cast: Jorge Barros, Mykola Chaban, Rebeca Close, Maria de Medeiros

Overview: Maria, a Ukrainian doctor, comes to Portugal to spend a year with Greco, her husband who is also a doctor. Upon arrival at Faro airport she is the only person from Kiev approached by agents of Immigration and Customs that lead her to a room of interrogation, without any explanations. All this occurs because the authorities suspect that something illegal should be behind her trip, since she is from Eastern Europe and her husband is Senegalese.
