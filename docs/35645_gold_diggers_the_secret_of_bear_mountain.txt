Title: Gold Diggers: The Secret of Bear Mountain (1995)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kevin James Dobson
Main Cast: Christina Ricci, Anna Chlumsky, Polly Draper, Brian Kerwin, Diana Scarwid
Keywords: gold, friendship, teenage girl

Overview: Two teen girls, one from the city, one from the country, try to find the hidden gold of Bear Mountain learning about friendship, loyalty and courage on the way.
