Title: Standing Army (2010)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Thomas Fazi
Main Cast: Noam Chomsky, Gore Vidal, Chalmers Johnson, Olivier Bancoult, William Blum
Keywords: protest, military base, okinawa, anti war, u.s. military

Overview: Standing Army, directed by Enrico Parenti and Thomas Fazi, is an award-winning documentary film about the global network of U.S. military bases, the impact that these have on local populations, and the military-industrial complex that lies behind it.
