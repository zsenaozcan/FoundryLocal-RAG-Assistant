Title: Guernsey (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Nanouk Leopold
Main Cast: Maria Kraakman, Fedja van Huêt, Johanna ter Steege, Frank Vercruyssen, Hans Croiset
Keywords: suicide, dutch, sister, relationship, woman director

Overview: Guernsey is the story of a woman who suddenly looks at her own life and wonders how she became miles apart from the people she is closest to.
