Title: The Emperor Waltz (1948)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Music
Director: Billy Wilder
Main Cast: Bing Crosby, Joan Fontaine, Roland Culver, Lucile Watson, Richard Haydn

Overview: At the turn of the 20th century, travelling salesman Virgil Smith journeys to Vienna in the hope he can sell a gramophone to Emperor Franz Joseph, whose purchase of the recent American invention could spur its popularity in Austria.
