Title: Something for a Lonely Man (1968)
Genres: TV Movie, Western
Director: Don Taylor
Main Cast: Dan Blocker, Susan Clark, John Dehner, Warren Oates, Paul Petersen
Keywords: tv movie

Overview: The blacksmith of a small western town finds himself an outcast. He had led the townspeople west in hopes of starting a new life, only to find the town that they founded is to be bypassed by the railroad.
