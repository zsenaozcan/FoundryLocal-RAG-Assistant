Title: The Prisoner of Zenda (1937)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Romance
Director: John Cromwell
Main Cast: Ronald Colman, Madeleine Carroll, Raymond Massey, Mary Astor, Douglas Fairbanks Jr.
Keywords: kidnapping, coronation, villain, kingdom, heir to the throne, royalty, mistaken identity, king, identity swap, monarch

Overview: An Englishman on a Ruritarian holiday must impersonate the king when the rightful monarch, a distant cousin, is drugged and kidnapped.
