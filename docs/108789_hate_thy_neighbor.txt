Title: Hate Thy Neighbor (1968)
Genres: Western, Action, Drama
Director: Ferdinando Baldi
Main Cast: Spiros Focás, George Eastman, Horst Frank, Nicoletta Machiavelli, Ivy Holzer
Keywords: spaghetti western

Overview: Ken Dakota's search for the murderer of his brother, killed by bandit Gary Stevens, at the behest of land owner Chris Malone.  Dakota attempts to bring the men responsible for his brothers death to justice.
