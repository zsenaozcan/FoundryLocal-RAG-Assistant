Title: Don't Hang Up (2016)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Damien Macé
Main Cast: Gregg Sulkin, Sienna Guillory, Garrett Clayton, Parker Sawyers, Robert Goodman
Keywords: prank, teenager, drunk, mysterious stranger

Overview: An evening of drunken prank calls becomes a nightmare for a pair of teenagers when a mysterious stranger turns their own game against them.
