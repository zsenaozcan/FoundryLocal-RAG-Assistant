Title: Oka! (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Will Yun Lee, Haviland Morris, Peter Riegert, Kris Marshall, Isaach De Bankolé
Keywords: africa, anthropologist, woman director

Overview: The story of Louis Sarno, an American ethno-musicologist who lived among the Bayaka Pygmies in Central Africa for 25 years.
