Title: In My Dreams (2014)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Kenny Leon
Main Cast: Katharine McPhee, Mike Vogel, JoBeth Williams, Jessalyn Wanlim, Antonio Cupo

Overview: Natalie and Nick are frustrated with their luck in romance. After tossing coins into a fountain, the two then begin dreaming about each other. But, according to fountain mythology, they only have a week to turn those dreams into reality.
