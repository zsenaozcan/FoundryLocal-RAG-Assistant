Title: Bazodee (2016)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Music
Director: Todd Kessler
Main Cast: Staz Nair, Kabir Bedi, Natalie Perera, Valmike Rampersad, Cindy F. Daniel

Overview: Set in beautiful Trinidad and Tobago, BAZODEE is a Bollywood style Caribbean musical about being true to yourself and honest in love at all costs.
