Title: Love and Lemons (2013)
Genres: Romance
Director: Teresa Fabik
Main Cast: Rakel Wärmländer, Josephine Bornebusch, Sverrir Gudnason, Tomas von Brömssen, Anki Lidén
Keywords: cooking, restaurant, funeral, kitchen, food critic, recipe, woman director

Overview: Agnes has most things in life: a family who are always there for her, a good job in the restaurant industry, a boyfriend who loves her, and a best friend whom she knows inside out. Or does she? All of a sudden things begin to crumble, one by one, and soon nothing is as it was.
