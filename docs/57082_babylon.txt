Title: Babylon (1980)
Genres: Drama
Director: Franco Rosso
Main Cast: Brinsley Forde, Trevor Laird, Karl Howman, Brian Bovell, Victor Romero Evans

Overview: Drama telling the story of Blue, a young man of Jamaican descent living in Brixton in 1980, as he hangs out with his friends, fronts a dub sound system, loses his job, struggles with family problems and has his friendships tested by racism.
