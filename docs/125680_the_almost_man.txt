Title: The Almost Man (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Martin Lund
Main Cast: Henrik Rafaelsen, Janne Heltberg, Solvei Grimen Fosse, Per Kjerstad, Tore Sagen

Overview: A big new home, a lovely wife and a new job seem to steer Henrik firmly towards the middle age and a bourgeois lifestyle. There is, however, a substantial amount of boyish prankster still in him - sometimes a little bit too much. Director Martin Lund's understated, offbeat humour often evokes Bent Hamer's delightful studies of lone males (O'Horten, Kitchen Stories)
