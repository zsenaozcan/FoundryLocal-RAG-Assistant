Title: Tar Creek (2009)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Matt Myers
Main Cast: John Frazier, Earl Hatley, Bob Nairn, Mark Osborn, John Sparkman
Keywords: oklahoma, environment, joplin, environmental disaster, treece, tar creek, picher, northeast, southeast, southwest

Overview: Tar Creek is an environmentally devastated area in northeastern Oklahoma with acidic creeks, stratospheric lead poisoning and enormous sinkholes. Nearly 30 years after being designated as a Superfund cleanup program, residents are still struggling.
