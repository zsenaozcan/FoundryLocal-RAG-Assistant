Title: Magnificent Bodyguards (1978)
Genres: Action
Director: Lo Wei
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, James Tien, Bruce Leung Siu-Lung, Wang Ping, Liu Ming

Overview: Chan is asked by a young, wealthy lady to take her sick brother to a particular doctor in order to be cured. To reach this doctor, Chan and a handful of travelling companions must pass through bandit-infested wild country. They meet and kung-fu-fight several gangs of thugs along the way.
