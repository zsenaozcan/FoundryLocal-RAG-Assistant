Title: Warsaw 44 (2014)
Genres: Drama, Romance, History, War
Director: Jan Komasa
Main Cast: Józef Pawłowski, Zofia Wichłacz, Anna Próchniak, Tomasz Schuchardt, Max Riemelt
Keywords: nazism

Overview: A beautiful story about youth, love, friendship and the pursuit of adventure during the bloody and brutal reality of the Warsaw Uprising.
