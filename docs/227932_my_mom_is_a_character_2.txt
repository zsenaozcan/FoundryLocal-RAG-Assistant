Title: My Mom Is a Character 2 (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: César Rodrigues
Main Cast: Paulo Gustavo, Rodrigo Pandolfo, Herson Capri, Mariana Xavier, Suely Franco
Keywords: brazilian, comedy

Overview: Dona Hermínia is back, but now rich and famous since she is the host of her own successful TV show. However, the overprotective character will have to deal with her children's departures, as Marcelina and Juliano decide to move out. In counterpart, she will welcome, as a visitor, her sister Lúcia Helena, who's been living in New York.
