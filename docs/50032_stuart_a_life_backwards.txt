Title: Stuart: A Life Backwards (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: David Attwood
Main Cast: Tom Hardy, Benedict Cumberbatch, Nicola Duffett, Claire-Louise Cordwell, Edna Doré
Keywords: biography, alcoholism, institution

Overview: Story about the remarkable friendship between a reclusive writer and illustrator and a chaotic homeless man, whom he gets to know during a campaign to release two charity workers from prison.
