Title: Last Stop (2002)
Genres: Documentary, Foreign
Director: Ólafur Sveinsson
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: An Icelandic documentary about the people that work, and hang around Hlemmur, the iconic Reykjavik bus stop.
 The film follows homeless people, people with disabilities, and a bus driver.
 A unique view in to their lives, and the view they have on the world.  Soundtrack by Sigur Rós.
