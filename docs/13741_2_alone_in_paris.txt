Title: 2 Alone in Paris (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Eric Judor
Main Cast: Ramzy Bedia, Eric Judor, Benoît Magimel, Kristin Scott Thomas, Élodie Bouchez

Overview: A bumbling Paris policeman is doggedly determined to capture the master thief that repeatedly eludes him, even when they're the last two men on Earth.
