Title: Vampire Journals (1997)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Ted Nicolaou
Main Cast: Jonathon Morris, David Gunn, Kirsten Cerre, Starr Andreeff, Ilinca Goia
Keywords: sword, impalement, severed, severed head

Overview: A 19th century vampire stalks a more powerful vampire lord in his quest to gain revenge over the death of his mistress. In his search for the vampire lord in Eastern Europe he kills many of his servants and fellow vampires while cursing another to vampirism as well.
