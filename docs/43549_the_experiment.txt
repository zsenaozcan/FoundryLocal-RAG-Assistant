Title: The Experiment (2010)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Paul Scheuring
Main Cast: Forest Whitaker, Cam Gigandet, Adrien Brody, Maggie Grace, Clifton Collins Jr
Keywords: human experimentation, psychological thriller, experiment gone wrong

Overview: 20 men are chosen to participate in the roles of guards and prisoners in a psychological study that ultimately spirals out of control.
