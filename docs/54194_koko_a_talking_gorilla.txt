Title: Koko: A Talking Gorilla (1978)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Barbet Schroeder
Main Cast: Penny Patterson, Koko, Saul Kitchener, Carl Pribram
Keywords: gorilla, communication

Overview: Koko: A Talking Gorilla is a documentary directed by Barbet Schroeder. The film is a study of Dr. Penny Patterson and her current scientific study, Koko, a gorilla.
