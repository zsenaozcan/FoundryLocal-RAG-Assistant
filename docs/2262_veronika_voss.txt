Title: Veronika Voss (1982)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Rainer Werner Fassbinder
Main Cast: Rosel Zech, Hilmar Thate, Annemarie Düringer, Doris Schade, Erik Schumann
Keywords: alcohol, munich, doctor, drug, movie star

Overview: In Munich in 1955, sports reporter Robert Krohn gets to know attractive Veronika Voss, a former UFA-star. Robert is the lover of the enigmatic woman and tries to unravel the mystery of her life.
