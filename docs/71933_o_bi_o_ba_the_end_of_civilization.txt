Title: O-Bi, O-Ba: The End of Civilization (1985)
Genres: Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Piotr Szulkin
Main Cast: Jerzy Stuhr, Krystyna Janda, Mariusz Dmochowski, Marek Walczewski, Leon Niemczyk
Keywords: paranoia, hope, post-apocalyptic

Overview: Set in an underground dungeon inhabited by bundled, ragged human beings, after the nuclear holocaust. The story follows the wanderings of a hero through the situations of survival. People wait for the Ark to arrive and rescue them while their habitat falls apart.
