Title: Anna (1994)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Nikita Mikhalkov
Main Cast: Nikita Mikhalkov, Anna Mikhalkova

Overview: Director Nikita Mikhalkov documents the history of Russia from 1980 to 1991 by annually asking his daughter Anna such questions as "What do you love the most?", "What scares you the most?", "What do you want above anything" and "What do you hate the most?"
