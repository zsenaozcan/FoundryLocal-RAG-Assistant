Title: Zebra (2016)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Hajime Hashimoto
Main Cast: Ryo Ryusei, Kenta Suga, Kyoko Hinami, Maryjun Takahashi, Masaya Kato

Overview: Dora (Ryo Ryusei) and associates runs a badger game scheme to extort victims out of money. One day though, their target looks like an ordinary guy, but he turns out to be a yakuza. Dora and his associates fall into a far darker world. Dora steps into a business that offers to take revenge on others by any means necessary.
