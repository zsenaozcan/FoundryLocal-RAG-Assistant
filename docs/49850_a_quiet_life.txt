Title: A Quiet Life (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Claudio Cupellini
Main Cast: Toni Servillo, Marco D'Amore, Francesco Di Leva, Juliane Köhler, Leonardo Sprengler
Keywords: film noir

Overview: The story of a man who murdered thirty-two people, gained power, and then got afraid because too many people wanted to kill him. One August morning, he disappeared. For fifteen years, everyone believed him dead.
