Title: Fat Guy Goes Nutzoid (1986)
Genres: Unknown
Director: John Golden
Main Cast: Mark Alfred, Joan Allen

Overview: The plot revolves around two brothers who befriend an escaped mental patient and accompany him on his misadventures in the big city. A new low in bad comedy flicks. Most of the humor is in the title "Fat Guy Goes Nutzoid." The rest is somewhere in between the vomiting and the Auschwitz joke and the enormous amount of body hair on display.
