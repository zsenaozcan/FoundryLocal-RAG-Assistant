Title: Kull the Conqueror (1997)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Action, Science Fiction
Director: John Nicolella
Main Cast: Kevin Sorbo, Tia Carrere, Thomas Ian Griffith, Litefoot, Roy Brocksmith
Keywords: sword, planetary configuration, sword fight, fate

Overview: A barbarian named Kull becomes ruler after defeating the old king in battle, thus receiving his crown. But direct heirs of the king, trying to topple Kull and regain the throne, bring an old witch queen Akivasha back to life. Their plan backfires, however, as Akivasha plans to have her demon lords rule the kingdom alone. The only thing that can stop her is the breath of the god Volka, and Kull.
