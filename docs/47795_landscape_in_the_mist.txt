Title: Landscape in the Mist (1988)
Genres: Drama
Director: Theo Angelopoulos
Main Cast: Michalis Zeke, Tania Palaiologou, Stratos Tzortzoglou, Eva Kotamanidou, Aliki Georgouli

Overview: Road movie about two children searching for their father who is supposed to live in Germany. Their obsession for this father figure will take them to the boundaries between childhood and adolescence.
