Title: Conspiracy Theory (1997)
Genres: Action, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Richard Donner
Main Cast: Mel Gibson, Julia Roberts, Patrick Stewart, Cylk Cozart, Steve Kahan
Keywords: new york, cia, helicopter, assassin, secret, obsession, taxi driver, fbi, paranoia, wheelchair

Overview: A man obsessed with conspiracy theories becomes a target after one of his theories turns out to be true. Unfortunately, in order to save himself, he has to figure out which theory it is.
