Title: Keith Lemon: The Film (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Paul Angunawela
Main Cast: Leigh Francis, Kelly Brook, Holly Willoughby, Kevin Bishop

Overview: With dreams of becoming a successful entrepreneur just like his beloved Richard Branson, Lemon bids farewell to his hometown of Leeds and heads for the capital. When he becomes an overnight billionaire, it seems everything is going his way, but it's not long before he discovers that life can be just as cruel as it is kind.
