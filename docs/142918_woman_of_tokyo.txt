Title: Woman of Tokyo (1933)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yasujirō Ozu
Main Cast: Yoshiko Okada, Ureo Egawa, Kinuyo Tanaka, Shin'yô Nara, Chishu Ryu
Keywords: children, prostitution, parent

Overview: Ryoichi and Chikako, brother and sister, live together. Chikako toils during the day and, at night, prostitutes herself to fund his college tuition.
