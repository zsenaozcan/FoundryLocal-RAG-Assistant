Title: Disco Polo (2015)
Genres: Music, Comedy
Director: Maciej Bochniak
Main Cast: Dawid Ogrodnik, Piotr Głowacki, Joanna Kulig, Tomasz Kot, Aleksandra Hamkało

Overview: Young wannabe musicians from the province decide to write hit song and gain the top of the charts in order to become legends of disco polo
