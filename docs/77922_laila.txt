Title: Laila (1929)
Genres: Drama
Director: George Schnéevoigt
Main Cast: Mona Mårtenson, Tryggve Larssen, Harald Schwenzen, Peter Malberg, Cally Monrad
Keywords: female protagonist, silent film

Overview: Ranged across several generations, the story begins with a wealthy merchant, Lind, and his wife travelling in the coldest depths of winter to christen their baby daughter.
