Title: Bone Tomahawk (2015)
Genres: Horror, Western, Adventure, Drama
Director: S. Craig Zahler
Main Cast: Kurt Russell, Patrick Wilson, Matthew Fox, Richard Jenkins, Lili Simmons
Keywords: poison, prisoner, murder, rifle, gore, native american, decapitation, redemption, extreme violence, violence

Overview: During a shootout in a saloon, Sheriff Hunt injures a suspicious stranger. One of the villagers takes care of him in prison. One day they both disappear – only the spear of a cannibal tribe is found. Hunt and a few of his men go in search of the prisoner and his nurse.
