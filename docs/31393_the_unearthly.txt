Title: The Unearthly (1957)
Genres: Science Fiction, Horror
Director: Boris Petroff
Main Cast: John Carradine, Myron Healey, Allison Hayes, Marilyn Buferd, Tor Johnson
Keywords: monster, mad scientist

Overview: Mad doctor uses patients at his isolated psychiatric institute as subjects in his attempts to create longevity by surgically installing an artificial gland in their skulls, with disastrous results.
