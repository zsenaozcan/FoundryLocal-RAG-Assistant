Title: Hard to Hold (1984)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Larry Peerce
Main Cast: Rick Springfield, Janet Eilber, Patti Hansen, Albert Salmi, Gregory Itzin
Keywords: music

Overview: Details the efforts of a pop-rock star (James Roberts / Rick Springfield) to win the love of a woman he meets in a car accident. None of the usual gambits work on this woman, who has never heard of him. Complications arise involving the ex-girlfriend Nicky, who is still in his band.
