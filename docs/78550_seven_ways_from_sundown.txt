Title: Seven Ways from Sundown (1960)
Genres: Western
Director: Harry Keller
Main Cast: Audie Murphy, Barry Sullivan, Venetia Stevenson, John McIntire, Kenneth Tobey
Keywords: texas ranger

Overview: Audie Murphy is again the kid who puts on a badge to catch the bad guy, skillfully played by Barry Sullivan. On the way back to town the two develop a curiously close relationship - Sullivan passes up several chances to get away - but in the end Sullivan "asks for it" and Murphy obliges.
