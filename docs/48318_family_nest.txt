Title: Family Nest (1979)
Genres: Drama
Director: Béla Tarr
Main Cast: Laszlóné Horváth, László Horváth, Gábor Kun, Gábor Ifj. Kun, Gaborne Kún
Keywords: rape, hungary, black and white, violence, hungarian, apartment, extended family, mono, economic bust

Overview: Családi tüzfészek (aka Family Nest) is an intimate portrayal of a family slowly disintegrating under various pressures in late 1970s communist Hungary. The plot of the film is deceptively simple, with the occasional momentous event--including one that's relatively shocking, but plot in a conventional sense is not the focus here.
