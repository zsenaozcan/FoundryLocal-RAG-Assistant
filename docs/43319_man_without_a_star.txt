Title: Man Without a Star (1955)
Genres: Western
Director: King Vidor
Main Cast: Kirk Douglas, Jeanne Crain, Claire Trevor, William Campbell, Richard Boone
Keywords: ranch, drifter, homestead

Overview: Man Without a Star is a 1955 western film starring Kirk Douglas as a wanderer who gets dragged into a range war. It was based on the novel of the same name by Dee Linford.
