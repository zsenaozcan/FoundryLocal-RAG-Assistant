Title: Transpecos (2016)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Greg Kwedar
Main Cast: Gabriel Luna, Clifton Collins Jr, Johnny Simmons, Lora Martinez-Cunningham, Will Brittain
Keywords: border patrol, checkpoint, desert, car

Overview: For three US Border Patrol agents, the contents of one car reveal an insidious plot within their own ranks. The next 24 hours may cost them their lives.
