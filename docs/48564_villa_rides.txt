Title: Villa Rides (1968)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Buzz Kulik
Main Cast: Yul Brynner, Robert Mitchum, Charles Bronson, Maria Grazia Buccella, Herbert Lom
Keywords: mexico, resistance, mexican revolution, biography, historical figure, maverick, pancho villa, gunrunner, power struggle

Overview: Pulled into the Mexican Revolution by his own greed, Texas gunrunner &amp; pilot Lee Arnold joins bandit-turned-patriot Pancho Villa &amp; his band of dedicated men in a march across Mexico battling the Colorados &amp; stealing women's hearts as they go. But each has a nemesis among his friends: Arnold is tormented by Fierro, Villa's right-hand-man; and Villa must face possible betrayal by his own president's naiveté
