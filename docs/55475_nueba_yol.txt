Title: Nueba Yol (1995)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Ángel Muñiz
Main Cast: Luisito Marti, Alfonso Zayas, Caridad Ravelo, Raúl Carbonell hijo, Rafael Villalona
Keywords: new york, emigration, dominican republic

Overview: Balbuena is a genial illegal immigrant who makes his way to New York City to take a bite out of the Big Apple. But his hopes of finding a better life soon begin to fade as Balbuena encounters one obstacle after another in the hardscrabble, uncaring metropolis.
