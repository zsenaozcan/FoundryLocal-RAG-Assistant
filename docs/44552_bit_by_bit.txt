Title: Bit by Bit (2002)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Pontus Klänge
Main Cast: Sunil Munshi, Jan Malmsjö, Kjell Bergqvist, Bojan Westin, Eva Dahlman

Overview: The story of J whose goal is to be world master at playing Nintendo. He struggles with his traditional Jewish family and non-Jewish girlfriend who demand that he grow up and get a job. Real problems arise when he awakes to discover that his right hand is missing.
