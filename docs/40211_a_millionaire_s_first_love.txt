Title: A Millionaire's First Love (2006)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Kim Tae-gyun
Main Cast: Lee Yeon-hee, Hyun Bin, Lim Ju-hwan

Overview: "A Millionaire's First Love" focuses on Kang Jae-Kyung's struggle with a clause in his grandfather's will that says he will only get his inheritance if he graduates from a high school in the country. Relocated into the mountains, he finds puppy love with a naive country girl. Can love change the spoiled urbanite and awaken his gentler side?
