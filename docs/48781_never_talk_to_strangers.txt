Title: Never Talk to Strangers (1995)
Genres: Thriller, Romance
Director: Peter Hall
Main Cast: Rebecca De Mornay, Antonio Banderas, Dennis Miller, Len Cariou, Martha Burns
Keywords: telephone, police, love, police psychologist, erotic thriller

Overview: Sarah Taylor, a police psychologist, meets a mysterious and seductive young man, Tony Ramirez, and falls in love with him. As a cause of this relationship, she changes her personality when she begins to receive anonymous telephone calls.
