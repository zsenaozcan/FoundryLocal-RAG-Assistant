Title: Les Dames du Bois de Boulogne (1945)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Robert Bresson
Main Cast: Paul Bernard, María Casares, Elina Labourdette, Lucienne Bogaert, Jean Marchat
Keywords: prostitute, cabaret dancer

Overview: A society lady engineers a marriage between her lover and a cabaret dancer who is essentially a prostitute.
