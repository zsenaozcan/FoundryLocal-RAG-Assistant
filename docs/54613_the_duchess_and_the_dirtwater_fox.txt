Title: The Duchess and the Dirtwater Fox (1976)
Genres: Comedy, Western
Director: Melvin Frank
Main Cast: George Segal, Goldie Hawn, Conrad Janis, Thayer David, Jennifer Lee Pryor
Keywords: con man, wild west, gambler, showgirl, saloon girl

Overview: A female hustler is chasing after rich men, but becomes repeatedly mixed up with a suave con man and card shark through a series of misadventures before falling in love with him.
