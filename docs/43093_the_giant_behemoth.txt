Title: The Giant Behemoth (1959)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Science Fiction, Fantasy
Director: Eugène Lourié
Main Cast: Gene Evans, André Morell, John Turner, Leigh Madison, Alastair Hunter
Keywords: london england, monster, river thames, radiation, paleontologist, radium, cornwall, laboratory, giant monster, dinosaur

Overview: Marine atomic tests cause changes in the ocean's ecosystem resulting in dangerous blobs of radiation and the resurrection of a dormant dinosaur which threatens London.
