Title: The Sword and the Sorcerer (1982)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Albert Pyun
Main Cast: Lee Horsley, Richard Lynch, Kathleen Beller, Simon MacCorkindale, George Maharis
Keywords: mythology, prince, crucifixion, telekinesis, resurrection, princess, sword fight, torture chamber, sorcerer, king

Overview: A mercenary with a three-bladed sword rediscovers his royal heritage when he is recruited to help a princess foil a brutal tyrant and a powerful sorcerer's plans to conquer the land.
