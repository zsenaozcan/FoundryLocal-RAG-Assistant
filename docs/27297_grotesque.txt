Title: Grotesque (2009)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Kôji Shiraishi
Main Cast: Hiroaki Kawatsure, Tsugumi Nagasawa, Shigeo Osako, Kôji Shiraishi
Keywords: female nudity, sexual abuse, nudity, pain, sadism, castration, hammer, perversion, gore, mad doctor

Overview: An unnamed doctor has always had everything he's ever wanted, but that has only made him develop more extreme and depraved needs. He kidnaps a young couple in the prime of their life together and forces them into a game of torment that slowly extinguishes their hopes for survival.
