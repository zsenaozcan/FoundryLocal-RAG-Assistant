Title: Shades of Ray (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Jaffar Mahmood
Main Cast: Zachary Levi, Fran Kranz, Sarah Shahi, Bonnie Somerville, Brian George
Keywords: culture clash, pakistan, romantic comedy, los angeles, mixed heritage

Overview: American-born Ray Rehman comes home one night to find his Pakistani father on his doorstep. Ray's Caucasian mother threw him out. It's an awkward time for his father to move in as Ray just proposed to his Caucasian girlfriend - who hasn't given him an answer. While trying to get his parents back together, Ray meets a South Asian girl of mixed descent, just like him, and must decide where his identity truly lies.
