Title: The Devil to Pay! (1930)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: George Fitzmaurice
Main Cast: Ronald Colman, Frederick Kerr, Loretta Young, David Torrence, Florence Britton
Keywords: london england, broken engagement, auction, wager, heiress, betting on horse, private detective, terrier, engaged, actress

Overview: Spendthrift Willie Hale again returns penniless to the family home in London. His father is none too pleased, but Willie smooth-talks him into letting him stay. At the same time he turns the charm on Dorothy Hope, whose father is big in linoleum and who, before Willie's arrival, was about to become engaged to a Russian aristocrat.
