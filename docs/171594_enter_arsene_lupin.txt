Title: Enter Arsene Lupin (1944)
Genres: Mystery, Crime
Director: Ford Beebe
Main Cast: Charles Korvin, Ella Raines, J. Carrol Naish, George Dolenz, Gale Sondergaard
Keywords: french, jewel thief

Overview: French jewel thief Lupin (Charles Korvin) robs an heiress (Ella Raines) on a train, then follows her to England and saves her life.
