Title: Barbary Coast Gent (1944)
Genres: Western, Comedy
Director: Roy Del Ruth
Main Cast: Wallace Beery, Binnie Barnes, John Carradine, Bruce Kellogg, Frances Rafferty
Keywords: san francisco, nevada, con man, transformation, tycoon, wells fargo, assayer

Overview: Honest Plush Brannon is a con-man thrown out of the Barbary Coast in San Francisco in the 1880s and headed for the gold rush region of Nevada. He discovers a real mine which lead to several complications.
