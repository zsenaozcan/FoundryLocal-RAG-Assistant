Title: From the Land of the Moon (2016)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Nicole Garcia
Main Cast: Marion Cotillard, Louis Garrel, Àlex Brendemühl, Brigitte Roüan, Victoire Du Bois
Keywords: based on novel, female protagonist, post world war ii, woman director

Overview: An adaptation of Milena Agus' eponymous novel set after WWII, "Mal de Pierres" ("Mal di Pietre") spans 20 years, following the destiny of a passionate, free-spirited woman who is in a loveless marriage and falls for another man.
