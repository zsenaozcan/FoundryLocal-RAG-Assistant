Title: Davy Crockett, King of the Wild Frontier (1955)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Family, Western
Director: Norman Foster
Main Cast: Fess Parker, Hans Conried, Buddy Ebsen, Basil Ruysdael, William Bakewell
Keywords: frontiersman, davy crockett

Overview: Legends (and myths) from the life of famed American frontiersman Davey Crockett are depicted in this feature film edited from television episodes. Crockett and his friend George Russell fight in the Creek Indian War. Then Crockett is elected to Congress and brings his rough-hewn ways to the House of Representatives. Finally, Crockett and Russell journey to Texas and the last stand at the Alamo.
