Title: Happiness (1935)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Aleksandr Medvedkin
Main Cast: Pyotr Zinovyev, Yelena Yegorova, Nikolai Cherkasov, Mikhail Gipsi
Keywords: silent film

Overview: A hapless loser (with the surname of Loser) undergoes misadventures with avaracious clergy, a tired horse, and a walking granary (among other things) on his road to collectivized happiness.
