Title: That Guy... Who Was in That Thing (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ian Roumain
Main Cast: Zeljko Ivanek, Craig Fairbrass, Xander Berkeley, Bruce Davison, Timothy Omundson

Overview: Documentary about sixteen actors who detail their ups and downs as they struggle to forge careers in Hollywood. They've played cops, lawyers, bosses, best friends, psychopaths, politicians and everything in between. Now you'll know who they are.
