Title: The Makeover (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: John Gray
Main Cast: Julia Stiles, Camryn Manheim, Frances Fisher, David Walton, Gary Galone

Overview: This is an update of George Bernard Shaw's "Pygmalion" that changes the genders of the main characters. Hannah Higgins attempts to turn blue-collar Boston beer vendor Elliot Doolittle into a viable candidate and inadvertently learns something of Elliot's side of life.
