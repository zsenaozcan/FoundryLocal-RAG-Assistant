Title: The Adventures of Tom Sawyer (1938)
Genres: Family, Adventure
Director: Norman Taurog
Main Cast: Tommy Kelly, Jackie Moran, Ann Gillis, May Robson, Victor Jory
Keywords: tom sawyer

Overview: An orphan in 1850 Missouri gets into a variety of scrapes, including a murder mystery.  Entertaining David O. Selznick production of Mark Twain classic with more slapstick than Twain may have had in mind.  Cave sequence with Injun Joe is unforgettable.
