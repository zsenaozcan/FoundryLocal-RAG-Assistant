Title: The Seven Cervi Brothers (1968)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Gianni Puccini
Main Cast: Gian Maria Volonté, Lisa Gastoni, Carla Gravina, Riccardo Cucciolla, Gabriella Pallotta

Overview: The story of the Cervi family. Rural farmers brought up to be idealogically opposed to fascism during the era of Mussolini's rule of Italy and World War 2.
