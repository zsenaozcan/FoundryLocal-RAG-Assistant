Title: Seal Team Eight: Behind Enemy Lines (2014)
Genres: Action, War, Drama
Director: Roel Reiné
Main Cast: Tom Sizemore, Langley Kirkwood, Bonnie Lee Bouman, Leroy Gopal, Lex Shrapnel

Overview: Seal Team Eight must fight their way deep into Africa's Congo, decommission a secret uranium mine, and stop our most dangerous enemy from smuggling weapon's grade yellow-cake out of the country.
