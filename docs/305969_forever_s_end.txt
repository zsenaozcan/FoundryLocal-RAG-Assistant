Title: Forever's End (2013)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: J.C. Schroder
Main Cast: Charity Farrell, Lili Reinhart, Warren Bryson, David Wetzel
Keywords: post-apocalyptic

Overview: Six years after an apocalyptic event killed her family and seemingly everyone else on earth, a lone girl on the verge of insanity is forced to question everything she has ever known when a strange man suddenly appears at her door. The last girl on earth... is not alone.
