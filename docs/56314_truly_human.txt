Title: Truly Human (2001)
Genres: Foreign, Drama, Romance
Director: Åke Sandgren
Main Cast: Nikolaj Lie Kaas, Peter Mygind, Susan A. Olsen, Clara Nepper Winther, Troels II Munk
Keywords: dogme 95

Overview: P. is a little girl's imaginary friend who lives inside the wall of her room. But one day he magically materialises and so must learn to live in the world like everyone else - a world where he is met with suspicion and hate but also with wonder and love.
