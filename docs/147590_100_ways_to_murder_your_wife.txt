Title: 100 Ways to Murder Your Wife (1986)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Kenny Bee
Main Cast: Chow Yun-fat, Anita Mui, Joey Wong, Kenny Bee, Anthony Chan
Keywords: slapstick

Overview: Two men, Yun-Fat Chow and Kenny Bee, are both married and unhappy. Together they make diverse plans to murder their wifes.
