Title: Charm's  Incidents (1996)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michael Kreihsl
Main Cast: Johannes Silberschneider, Wolfgang Hübsch, Ela Piplits, Elżbieta Czyżewska, Ulrich Tukur

Overview: Humourous interpretation of the poems and writings of Soviet dadaist Daniil Charms. These are organized into a sequence, suggesting a storyline, about a poor Russian poet who lives in Vienna, falls in love and has several bizarre adventures.
