Title: Tag (2015)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Sion Sono
Main Cast: Reina Triendl, Mariko Shinoda, Erina Mano, Yuki Sakurai, Aki Hiraoka
Keywords: splatter

Overview: Female highs school students, including Mitsuko, Keiko and Izumi, become the targets of ghosts with various appearances including a groom with a pig's face and female teacher with a machine gun.
