Title: Carmen (1918)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ernst Lubitsch
Main Cast: Pola Negri, Harry Liedtke, Leopold von Ledebur, Grete Diercks, Heinrich Peer
Keywords: gypsy, smuggler, short

Overview: The tragic story of Don Jose, a Spanish cavalryman, who falls under the spell of a gypsy girl, Carmen, who treats him with both love and contempt and leads him into temptation and thus damnation.
