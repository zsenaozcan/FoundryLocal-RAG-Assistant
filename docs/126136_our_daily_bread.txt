Title: Our Daily Bread (1934)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: King Vidor
Main Cast: Karen Morley, Tom Keene, Barbara Pepper, Addison Richards, John Qualen
Keywords: water, drought, farming

Overview: John and Mary sims are city-dwellers hit hard by the financial fist of The Depression. Driven by bravery (and sheer desperation) they flee to the country and, with the help of other workers, set up a farming community - a socialist mini-society based upon the teachings of Edward Gallafent. The newborn community suffers many hardships - drought, vicious raccoons and the long arm of the law - but ultimately pull together to reach a bread-based Utopia.
