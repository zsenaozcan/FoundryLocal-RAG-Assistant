Title: Feast of Love (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Robert Benton
Main Cast: Morgan Freeman, Greg Kinnear, Radha Mitchell, Billy Burke, Selma Blair
Keywords: dancing, sex, adultery, fortune teller, based on novel, marriage proposal, oregon, professor, coffee, nudity

Overview: A meditation on love and its various incarnations, set within a community of friends in Oregon. It is described as an exploration of the magical, mysterious and sometimes painful incarnations of love.
