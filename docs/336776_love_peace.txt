Title: Love & Peace (2015)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Drama
Director: Sion Sono
Main Cast: Kumiko Asou, Hiroki Hasegawa, Toshiyuki Nishida, Kiyohiko Shibukawa, Makita Sports

Overview: A man, who once dreamed of becoming a punk rocker, is working as a low salaryman at a musical instrument parts company. He’s secretly in love with his colleague. One day, he finds a little turtle on the rooftop, naming it Pikadon.
