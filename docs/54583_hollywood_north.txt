Title: Hollywood North (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary
Director: Peter O'Brian
Main Cast: Matthew Modine, Deborah Kara Unger, Jennifer Tilly, Alan Thicke, Alan Bates
Keywords: canadian, satire

Overview: The making of a serious, Canadian art house film descends into Hollywood farce when its producer is forced to compromise his vision to accommodate his drug-addled star, his leading lady and his venal backers.
