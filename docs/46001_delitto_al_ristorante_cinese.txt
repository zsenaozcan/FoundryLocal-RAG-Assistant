Title: Delitto al ristorante cinese (1981)
Genres: Crime, Comedy, Thriller
Director: Bruno Corbucci
Main Cast: Tomás Milián, Bombolo, Enzo Cannavale, Olimpia Di Nardo, Giacomo Furia

Overview: The officer Nico Giraldi investigates a homicide which has taken place in a Chinese restaurant in Rome. His wife and his son are kidnappeded by the murderer in the course of the inquiry. In the end the case is worked out.
