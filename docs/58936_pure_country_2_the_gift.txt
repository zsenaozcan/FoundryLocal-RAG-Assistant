Title: Pure Country 2: The Gift (2010)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance
Director: Christopher Cain
Main Cast: Katrina Elam, George Strait, Travis Fimmel, Bronson Pinchot, Cheech Marin
Keywords: musical

Overview: Three angels bestow the gift of song to a young girl, who must follow a series of rules in order hold on to it during her rise to fame and fortune.
