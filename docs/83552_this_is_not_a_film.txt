Title: This Is Not a Film (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jafar Panahi
Main Cast: Jafar Panahi
Keywords: filmmaker

Overview: It's been months since Jafar Panahi, stuck in jail, has been awaiting a verdict by the appeals court. By depicting a day in his life, Panahi and Mojtaba Mirtahmasb try to portray the deprivations looming in contemporary Iranian cinema.
