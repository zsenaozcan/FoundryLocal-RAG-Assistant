Title: The Man Who Could Cheat Death (1959)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Terence Fisher
Main Cast: Anton Diffring, Hazel Court, Christopher Lee, Arnold Marlé, Delphi Lawrence
Keywords: remake, mad doctor, centenarian, transplant, hammer horror, adrenal gland

Overview: Dr. Bonner plans to live forever through periodic gland transplants from younger, healthier human victims. Bonner looks about 40; he's really 104 years old. But people are starting to get suspicious, and he may not make 200.
