Title: Sherlock Holmes (1922)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Albert Parker
Main Cast: John Barrymore, Roland Young, Carol Dempster, Gustav von Seyffertitz, Louis Wolheim
Keywords: silent film

Overview: Starring John Barrymore as Holmes and Roland Young as Watson the film was believed lost for decades. Much of the film finally resurfaced in the mid-1970s and was restored by the George Eastman House from rolls of negatives to the extent that it could be printed and screened. The movie features William Powell's first screen appearance.
