Title: Things We Lost in the Fire (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Susanne Bier
Main Cast: Halle Berry, Benicio del Toro, David Duchovny, Alison Lohman, Sarah Dubrovsky
Keywords: sadness, loss of father, junkie, luxury, heroin, beauty, charity, funeral, widow, loss of husband

Overview: A recent widow invites her husband's troubled best friend to live with her and her two children. As he gradually turns his life around, he helps the family cope and confront their loss.
