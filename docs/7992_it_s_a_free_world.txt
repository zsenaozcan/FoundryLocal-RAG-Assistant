Title: It's a Free World... (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ken Loach
Main Cast: Kierston Wareing, Juliet Ellis, Leslaw Zurek, Colin Caughlin, Joe Siffleet
Keywords: capitalism, great britain, illegal immigration, worker, exploitation, class society, independent film

Overview: The film focuses on Angie (Kierston Wareing), a working class woman who, after being fired by the agency that she works for, decides to set up a recruitment agency of her own, running it from her kitchen with her friend Rose (Juliet Ellis). Taking advantage of the desperation of immigrants, Angie is able to build a successful business extremely quickly.
