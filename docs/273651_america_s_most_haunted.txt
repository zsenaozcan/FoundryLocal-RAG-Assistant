Title: America's Most Haunted (2013)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Chris Randall
Main Cast: Brad Norman, James Karen, Brittany Risner, David Gries, Joe Anderson

Overview: Welcome to America's Most Haunted, the paranormal show that's not just trying to contact ghosts, they're here to kick ghosts out and send them back where they belong! But this time it's different. This time it's real. And if they can survive the night, they'll have the show of their lives.
