Title: Café au Lait (1993)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance, Foreign
Director: Mathieu Kassovitz
Main Cast: Vincent Cassel, Julie Mauduech, Hubert Koundé, Mathieu Kassovitz
Keywords: pregnancy

Overview: Lola is pregnant. But she does not know who the father is : Jamal, the black muslim, son of diplomats, or Felix, the pennyless jewish messenger. Jamal and Felix meet at Lola's, and the race begins.
