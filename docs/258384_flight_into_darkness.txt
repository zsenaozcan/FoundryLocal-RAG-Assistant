Title: Flight into Darkness (1935)
Genres: Drama
Director: Anatole Litvak
Main Cast: Annabella, Charles Vanel, Jean-Pierre Aumont, Jean Murat, Daniel Mendaille

Overview: During the First World War, before joining a squadron at the front in 1918, Herbillon (Jean-Pierre Aumont) has a liaison with Helene (Annabella), a married woman. The young man discovers that his mistress is none other than the wife of Maury (Charles Vanel), an aviator friend.
