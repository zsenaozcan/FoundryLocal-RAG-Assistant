Title: RKO 281 (1999)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Benjamin Ross
Main Cast: Liev Schreiber, James Cromwell, Melanie Griffith, John Malkovich, Brenda Blethyn
Keywords: biography, independent film, movie studio, director

Overview: A film about the making of Citizen Kane. Orson Welles produces his greatest film, Citizen Kane, despite the opposition of the film's de facto subject, William Randolph Hearst.
