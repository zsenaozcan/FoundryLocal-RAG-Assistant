Title: A High Wind in Jamaica (1965)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Alexander Mackendrick
Main Cast: Anthony Quinn, James Coburn, Deborah Baxter, Gert Fröbe, Dennis Price
Keywords: jamaica

Overview: In 1870, a Jamaican colonial family sends its children to Britain for proper schooling, but their ship is taken over by pirates, who become fond of the kids.
