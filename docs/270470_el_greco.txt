Title: El Greco (1966)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Luciano Salce
Main Cast: Mel Ferrer, Rosanna Schiaffino, Adolfo Celi, Fernando Rey, Mario Feliciani
Keywords: inquisition, painter, spain, artist, biography

Overview: Greek painter Domenikos Theotokopoulos (Mel Ferrer) woos a beauty (Rosanna Schiaffino) and faces the Inquisition in 16th-century Spain.
