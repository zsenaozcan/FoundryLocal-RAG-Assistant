Title: The Little Norse Prince (1968)
Genres: Animation, Fantasy, Adventure, Family
Director: Isao Takahata
Main Cast: Yukari Asai, Mikijiro Hira, Etsuko Ichihara, Gilbert Mack, Masao Mishima
Keywords: young

Overview: Horus, a kid living in a unnamed Scandinavian/Eastern Europe culture of the Iron Age, recovers the Sword of the Sun from the rock giant Moog and learns from his dying father that he must returns to his ancestral territory. In the process, he defends a village from the attacks of Grundewald, a warlord/ice demon and befriends the enigmatic Hilda, a lonely and beautiful girl who sings haunting songs (and who hides a terrible secret).
