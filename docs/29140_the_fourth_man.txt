Title: The Fourth Man (1983)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Romance, Thriller, Horror
Director: Paul Verhoeven
Main Cast: Jeroen Krabbé, Renée Soutendijk, Thom Hoffman, Dolf de Vries, Geert de Jong
Keywords: alcohol, nightmare, spider, vision, catholicism

Overview: An alcoholic writer begins having visions apparently warning him of impending danger. After he delivers a lecture, he begins an affair with a woman from the audience. The visions intensify, however, and begin to threaten his relationship with the woman, since it may be her the visions are warning against.
