Title: The Telephone (1988)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Rip Torn
Main Cast: Whoopi Goldberg, John Heard, Elliott Gould, Hervé Villechaize, Severn Darden

Overview: A crazy out of work actress, Vashti Blue (Whoopi Goldberg), spends all her time in her small apartment with her pet owl and her telephone, which she uses to try and solve all her problems with life.
