Title: Remote Control (1992)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Oskar Jónasson
Main Cast: Björn Jörundur Friðbjörnsson, Sigurjón Kjartansson, Þórarinn Eyfjörð, Thorarinn Oskar Thorarinsson, Óttarr Proppé

Overview: Since his mother wants to watch TV, Axel, a young auto-mechanic, must recover her remote control, accidentally taken by his punk sister Maja. During his quest, he becomes involved in the conflict between Moli, the liquor smuggler, and Aggi, a night club owner who wants to be Iceland's first mafia boss.
