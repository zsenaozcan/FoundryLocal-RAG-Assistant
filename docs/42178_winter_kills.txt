Title: Winter Kills (1979)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: William Richert
Main Cast: Jeff Bridges, John Huston, Anthony Perkins, Eli Wallach, Sterling Hayden
Keywords: suspense, assassination of president

Overview: 19 years after President Timothy Keegan was assassinated, his brother Nick discovers a dying man claiming to have been the gunman. While trying to avoid his wealthy and domineering father's attempts to control his actions, Nick follows the clues that have been handed to him. As he progresses, it becomes increasingly difficult to discern the real trails from the dead ends, and increasing dangerous as unknown parties try to stop Nick from uncovering the truth.
