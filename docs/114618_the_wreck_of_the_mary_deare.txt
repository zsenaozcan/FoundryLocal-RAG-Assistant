Title: The Wreck of the Mary Deare (1959)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller
Director: Michael Anderson
Main Cast: Gary Cooper, Charlton Heston, Michael Redgrave, Virginia McKenna, Richard Harris
Keywords: sinking ship, merchant marine

Overview: A disgraced merchant marine officer elects to stay aboard his sinking cargo ship in order to prove the vessel was deliberately scuttled and, as a result, vindicate his good name.
