Title: The Thick-Walled Room (1956)
Genres: Drama
Director: Masaki Kobayashi
Main Cast: Ko Mishima, Torahiko Hamada, Keiko Kishi, Toshiko Kobayashi, Kinzô Shin

Overview: A group of rank-and-file Japanese soldiers are jailed for crimes against humanity, themselves victims of a nation refusing to bear its burdens as a whole.
