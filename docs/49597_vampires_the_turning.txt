Title: Vampires: The Turning (2005)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Horror
Director: Marty Weiss
Main Cast: Colin Egglesfield, Stephanie Chao, Roger Yuan, Patrick Bauchau, Dom Hetrakul

Overview: An American muay-thai fighter in Thailand must join forces with a group of vampire hunters to track down and kill a vampire lord who has kidnapped his gilfriend.
