Title: What We Become (2015)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Bo Mikkelsen
Main Cast: Troels Lyby, Mille Dinesen, Benjamin Engell, Marie Hammer Boda, Ella Solgaard
Keywords: zombie, virus

Overview: A family of four is quarantined in their home as a virulent strand of the flu spreads into town and they are forced to the extreme to escape alive.
