Title: Another Gay Movie (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Todd Stephens
Main Cast: Michael Carbonaro, Jonah Blechman, Jonathan Chase, Mitch Morris, Ashlie Atkinson
Keywords: gay, gay relationship, coming of age, lesbian, loss of virginity, spoof, lgbt child, teen comedy, lgbt
