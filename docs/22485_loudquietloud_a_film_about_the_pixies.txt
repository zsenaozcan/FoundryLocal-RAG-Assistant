Title: loudQUIETloud: A Film About the Pixies (2006)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Steven Cantor
Main Cast: Frank Black, Kim Deal, David Lovering, Joey Santiago
Keywords: independent film

Overview: When college rock darlings the Pixies broke up in 1992, their fans were shocked and dismayed. When they reunited in 2004, those same fans and legions of new listeners were ecstatic and filled with high hopes. loudQUIETloud follows the rehearsals and live shows of the band as they struggle through the reunion tour "Sell Out"
