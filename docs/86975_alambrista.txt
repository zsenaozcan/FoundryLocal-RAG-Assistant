Title: Alambrista! (1977)
Genres: Drama
Director: Robert M. Young
Main Cast: Domingo Ambriz, Trinidad Silva, Linda Gillen, Ned Beatty, Jerry Hardin
Keywords: mexican

Overview: After the birth of his first child, Roberto, a young Mexican man slips across the border into the United States. Seeking work to support his family back home, he finds that working hard is not enough.
