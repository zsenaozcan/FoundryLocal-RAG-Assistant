Title: The Return (1980)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Greydon Clark
Main Cast: Jan-Michael Vincent, Cybill Shepherd, Martin Landau, Raymond Burr, Neville Brand
Keywords: sheriff, alien, independent film, alien abduction, cattle mutilation

Overview: Two young children and an adult in a small town have an encounter with an alien spaceship. 25 years later the children are reunited as adults in the same town which is now beset by strange cattle mutilations. Matters become worse when the cattle mutilations are joined by human murders and mutilations.
