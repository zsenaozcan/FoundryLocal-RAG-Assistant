Title: Black Widow (2008)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Armand Mastroianni
Main Cast: Alicia Coppola, Elizabeth Berkley, Randall Batinkoff, Adriana DeMeo, Barbara Niven

Overview: Mel, a photo journalist, gets suspicious when her best friend Danny start dating Olivia a wealthy but mysterious woman. She enlists the help of her assistant in investigating Olivia's past and present occupation putting herself in danger.
