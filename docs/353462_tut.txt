Title: Tut (2015)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: David Von Ancken
Main Cast: Ben Kingsley, Avan Jogia, Sibylla Deen, Alexander Siddig, Kylie Bunbury

Overview: Explores the drama of power, political back-stabbing, war and murder and chronicles King Tut's rise to glory, his efforts to rule a chaotic empire and the enigma surrounding his death.
