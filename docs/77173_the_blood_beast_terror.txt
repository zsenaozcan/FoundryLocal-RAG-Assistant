Title: The Blood Beast Terror (1968)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, Science Fiction
Director: Vernon Sewell
Main Cast: Peter Cushing, Robert Flemyng, Wanda Ventham, David Griffin, Glynn Edwards
Keywords: genetics, scientist, moth

Overview: A Scotland Yard Detective (Peter Cushing) must investigate a series of murders perpetrated by a giant blood-sucking moth that can take human form.
