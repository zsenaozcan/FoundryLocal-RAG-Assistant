Title: Hank and Mike (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Matthiew Klinck
Main Cast: Thomas Michael, Paolo Mancini, Chris Klein, Maggie Castle, Tony Nappo
Keywords: easter, easter bunny, independent film

Overview: Two blue-collar Easter Bunnies get fired and try their hand at an assortment of odd jobs, failing at each. Fighting depression, debt and eventually each other, their lives start to unravel until they realize that without their job they are nothing.
