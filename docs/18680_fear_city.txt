Title: Fear City (1984)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Abel Ferrara
Main Cast: Tom Berenger, Billy Dee Williams, Jack Scalia, Melanie Griffith, Rossano Brazzi
Keywords: strip club, night club, police detective

Overview: Strippers in Manhattan are being stalked and murdered by a psycho. A hard-nosed police detective and a conflicted ex-boxer-turned-private-eye, hired by the strip club owners, set out to find him before he strikes again.
