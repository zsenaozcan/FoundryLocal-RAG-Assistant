Title: Bad Moms (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jon Lucas
Main Cast: Mila Kunis, Kristen Bell, Kathryn Hahn, Christina Applegate, Jada Pinkett Smith
Keywords: alcohol, bar, party, divorce, family, hit and run, bathroom humor

Overview: When three overworked and under-appreciated moms are pushed beyond their limits, they ditch their conventional responsibilities for a jolt of long overdue freedom, fun, and comedic self-indulgence.
