Title: Camila (1984)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Romance
Director: María Luisa Bemberg
Main Cast: Imanol Arias, Susú Pecoraro, Héctor Alterio, Elena Tasisto, Carlos Muñoz
Keywords: musical, woman director

Overview: In Buenos Aires of the 1840s, a young Jesuit and a wealthy socialite fall in love and begin a torrid affair. They escape from the city, and, in disguise, set up house in a village, assuming they are safe and beyond the cares of anyone. However, both the church and Camila's family are enraged, vowing to hunt down the lovers for a capital crime. Based on a true story.
