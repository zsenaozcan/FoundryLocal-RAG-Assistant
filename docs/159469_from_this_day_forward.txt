Title: From This Day Forward (1946)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: John Berry
Main Cast: Joan Fontaine, Mark Stevens, Rosemary DeCamp, Harry Morgan, Wally Brown
Keywords: marriage, courtship

Overview: A young American soldier, with an honorable discharge, returns home from World War II to his bride, whom he married after a short courtship and has not seen for several years. The two come together with many questions, trials and tribulations in trying to preserve their marriage in the post-war years.
