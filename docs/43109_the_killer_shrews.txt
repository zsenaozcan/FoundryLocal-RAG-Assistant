Title: The Killer Shrews (1959)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Ray Kellogg
Main Cast: James Best, Ingrid Goude, Ken Curtis, Gordon McLendon, Baruch Lumet
Keywords: shrew, flesh eating, poverty row film

Overview: Trapped on a remote island by a hurricane, a group discover a doctor has been experimenting on creating half sized humans. Unfortunately, his experiments have also created giant shrews, who when they have run out of small animals to eat, turn on the humans....
