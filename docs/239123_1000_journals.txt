Title: 1000 Journals (2007)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Andrea Kreuzhage
Main Cast: Nathan Blaney
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Director Andrea Kreuzhage documents the 1000 Journals Project, an ongoing collaborative experiment, created by Nathan Blaney who attempts to follow 1000 journals throughout their travels. The goal of the project is to provide a method for interaction and shared creativity among friends and strangers.
