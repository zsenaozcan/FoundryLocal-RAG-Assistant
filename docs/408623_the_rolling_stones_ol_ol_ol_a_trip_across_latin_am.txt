Title: The Rolling Stones Olé Olé Olé! : A Trip Across Latin America (2016)
Genres: Music, Documentary
Director: Paul Dugdale
Main Cast: Mick Jagger, Keith Richards, Ronnie Wood, Charlie Watts

Overview: This exhilarating rock 'n' roll road movie follows The Rolling Stones on their 2016 tour of Latin America, climaxing with their historic concert in Havana, Cuba.
