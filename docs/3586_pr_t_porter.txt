Title: Prêt-à-Porter (1994)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Robert Altman
Main Cast: Marcello Mastroianni, Sophia Loren, Jean-Pierre Cassel, Chiara Mastroianni, Kim Basinger
Keywords: paris, hotel room, suspicion of murder, mannequin, fashion photographer, sandwich, poodle, reporter, fashion

Overview: Paris Fashion Week draws the usual bunch of people; designers, reporters, models, magazine editors, photographers. Follows the various storylines of these characters, centering around a murder investigation of a prominent fashion figure. Features an all-star cast.
