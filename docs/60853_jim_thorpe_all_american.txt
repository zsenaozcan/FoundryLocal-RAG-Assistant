Title: Jim Thorpe – All-American (1951)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: Burt Lancaster, Charles Bickford, Steve Cochran, Phyllis Thaxter, Dick Wesson
Keywords: sport

Overview: The triumph and tragedy of Native Anerican Jim Thorpe, who, after winning both the pentathlon and decathlon in the same Olympics, is stripped of his medals on a technicality.
