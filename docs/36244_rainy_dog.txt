Title: Rainy Dog (1997)
Genres: Crime, Action, Drama
Director: Takashi Miike
Main Cast: Xianmei Chen, Gao Ming-Chun, Jianqin He, Sho Aikawa, Chang Li-Wei

Overview: A Japanese assassin stranded in Taiwan must take work from a local crime boss to make ends meet when suddenly a woman from his past delivers a son to him.
