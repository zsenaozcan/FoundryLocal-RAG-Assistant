Title: Asfalto (2000)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Daniel Calparsoro
Main Cast: Najwa Nimri, Juan Diego Botto, Gustavo Salmerón, Antonia San Juan, Rubén Ochandiano

Overview: Three young and desperate inhabitants of Madrid attempt to rise above their circumstances by any means available to them in that sun-drenched city. This tight-knit trio must scramble when their hasty plans become derailed by a demanding matriarch, an abusive older brother, and the entire corrupt underclass with which they deal.
