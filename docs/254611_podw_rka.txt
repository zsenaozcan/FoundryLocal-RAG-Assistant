Title: Podwórka (2009)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Sharon Lockhart
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: ‘Podwórka’ captures six groups of neighbourhood youth as they play in seemingly deserted yards, offering an intimate portrait of daily life in Łódź, Poland. Shot with a fixed camera, this single-channel video projection highlights American artist Sharon Lockhart’s concern for the interrelationship between the still and the moving image.
