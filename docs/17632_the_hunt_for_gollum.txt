Title: The Hunt for Gollum (2009)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Fantasy
Director: Chris Bouchard
Main Cast: Adrian Webster, Patrick O'Connor, Arin Alldridge, Rita Ramnani, Gareth Brough
Keywords: middle-earth (tolkien), the lord of the rings

Overview: A British fan film based on the appendices of Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings. Set in Middle-earth, Strider must hunt down Gollum to keep the Ring secret.
