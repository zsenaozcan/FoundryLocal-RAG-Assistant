Title: I Know What I Saw (2009)
Genres: Documentary, Science Fiction
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Director James Fox assembled the most credible UFO witnesses from around the world to testify at The National Press Club in Washington D.C.: Air Force Generals, astronauts, military and commercial pilots, government and FAA officials from seven countries tell stories that, as Governor Fife Symington from Arizona stated, "will challenge your reality".
