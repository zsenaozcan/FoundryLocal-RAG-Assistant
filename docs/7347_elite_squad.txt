Title: Elite Squad (2007)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime
Director: José Padilha
Main Cast: Wagner Moura, Caio Junqueira, André Ramiro, Milhem Cortaz, Fernanda Machado
Keywords: slum, police brutality, brazilian, war on drugs, rio de janeiro, drug traffic, drug dealer, torture by the police, special forces, law enforcement

Overview: In 1997, before the visit of the pope to Rio de Janeiro, Captain Nascimento from BOPE (Special Police Operations Battalion) is assigned to eliminate the risks of the drug dealers in a dangerous slum nearby where the pope intends to be lodged.
