Title: Estômago: A Gastronomic Story (2007)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Marcos Jorge
Main Cast: João Miguel, Fabiula Nascimento, Babú Santana, Carlo Briani, Zeca Cenovicz
Keywords: brazilian, food

Overview: In a dog-eat-dog world, Raimundo Nonato has found an alternative way to move ahead: he cooks. No matter what social strata this deceptively innocent young man inhabits, he hones his skills and sharpens his knives—and then he falls in love. Jorge's nimble comic fable provides a smartly constructed gastronomic allegory for ambition and survival.
