Title: The Grandfather (1998)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: José Luis Garci
Main Cast: Fernando Fernán Gómez, Rafael Alonso, Cayetana Guillén Cuervo, Agustín González, Cristina Cruz
Keywords: spain, based on novel, infidelity, grandfather, suicidal thoughts, old age, daughter–in–law, grandchild

Overview: After his son dies, an elderly man comes back to Spain from the US and hopes to find out which of his granddaughters is true, and which one is bastard.
