Title: Across the Line: The Exodus of Charlie Wright (2010)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Crime
Director: R. Ellis Frazier
Main Cast: Aidan Quinn, Andy García, Mario Van Peebles, Danny Pino, Claudia Ferri

Overview: The story centers on Charlie, a Los Angeles billionaire financial whiz who goes into self-imposed exile in Tijuana after his empire is revealed to have been a Ponzi scheme. While looking for the woman he abandoned there 25 years before, Charlie is pursued by a Mexican gangster, a federal agent and thugs sent by a former client looking to retrieve his money.
