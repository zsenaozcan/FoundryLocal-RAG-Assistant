Title: Jigoku (1960)
Genres: Horror, Drama
Director: Nobuo Nakagawa
Main Cast: Shigeru Amachi, Utako Mitsuya, Yôichi Numata, Hiroshi Hayashi
Keywords: hell, torture, devil, demon

Overview: After a young theology student flees a hit-and-run accident, he is plagued by both his own guilt-ridden conscience and a mysterious, diabolical doppelgänger. But all possible escape routes lead straight to hell—literally!
