Title: Below Utopia (1997)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Kurt Voss
Main Cast: Alyssa Milano, Justin Theroux, Ice-T, Tom Lister Jr., Richard Danielson
Keywords: murder, mansion, theft, home invasion, sensuality

Overview: Daniel returns to his family's mansion for the holidays along with his girlfriend Susanne. His family's seemingly-utopian existence is overshadowed by not only the death of Daniel's brother, but also by Daniel's failure to live up to his brother's potential. However, this quickly becomes inconsequential, as blood-thirsty killers soon show up..
