Title: It Happened Here (1966)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Kevin Brownlow
Main Cast: Pauline Murray, Sebastian Shaw, Bart Allison, Reginald Marsh
Keywords: german occupation

Overview: It is the Second World War. The Nazis have invaded Britain. There is a split between the resistance and those who prefer to collaborate with the invaders for a quiet life. The protagonist, a nurse, is caught in the middle.
