Title: Mr. Majestyk (1974)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Crime, Thriller
Director: Richard Fleischer
Main Cast: Charles Bronson, Al Lettieri, Linda Cristal, Lee Purcell, Paul Koslo
Keywords: 1970s, immigrant, revenge, organized crime, mobster

Overview: Watermelon farmer Vince Majestyk (Charles Bronson) goes after the Mob, when they try to strong arm him to use their melon picking crew.
