Title: Alaipayuthey (2000)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Mani Ratnam
Main Cast: Madhavan, Shalini, Swarnamalya, Vivek, Jayasudha
Keywords: coma, musical, hit and run, told in flashback, social differences, family disapproval
