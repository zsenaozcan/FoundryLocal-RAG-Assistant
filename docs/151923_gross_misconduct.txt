Title: Gross Misconduct (1993)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: George T. Miller
Main Cast: Jimmy Smits, Naomi Watts, Sarah Chadwick, Adrian Wright, Ross Williams
Keywords: rape, professor, seduction, father, relationship, art, guilt

Overview: Justin Thorne is a liberal arts professor. One of his college students is Jennifer Carter. She seduces Justin, then accuses him of rape. But it turns out that she has an unusual relationship with her father.
