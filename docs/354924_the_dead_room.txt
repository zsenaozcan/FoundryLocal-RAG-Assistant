Title: The Dead Room (2015)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Jason Stutter
Main Cast: Jed Brophy, Jeffrey Thomas, Laura Petersen
Keywords: new zealand, haunting, farmhouse, spirit, psychic, scientist, family

Overview: When a terrified family flees a desolate southern New Zealand farmhouse, two cynical scientists and a young psychic are sent to investigate their claims of a haunting. There they encounter a powerful spirit that will protect the house's secrets at all costs.
