Title: Dark Night of the Scarecrow (1981)
Genres: Horror, Mystery, TV Movie
Director: Frank De Felitta
Main Cast: Larry Drake, Charles Durning, Robert F. Lyons, Claude Earl Jones, Lane Smith
Keywords: grave, scarecrow, cover-up, revenge, execution, dog, cult film, farmer, pitchfork, lynch mob

Overview: Bubba, an intellectually disabled man, is falsely accused of attacking a young girl. Disguised as a scarecrow, he hides in a cornfield, only to be hunted down and shot by four vigilante men. After they are acquitted due to lack of evidence, the men find themselves being stalked by a mysterious scarecrow.
