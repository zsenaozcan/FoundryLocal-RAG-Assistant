Title: Studentfesten (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Simon Sandquist
Main Cast: Anastasios Soulis, Henrik Lundström, Filip Berg, Anna Åström, Kjell Bergqvist

Overview: 1994. Rasmus two best friends Frans and Ian talk him into crashing graduation parties in search of fun, booze and girls to get over Klara who just broke up with him. During the day his feelings for her is tested when he learns the truth about Klara's break-up. But is their love for real? And more importantly, can it survive the night?
