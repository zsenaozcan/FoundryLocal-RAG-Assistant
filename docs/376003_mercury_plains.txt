Title: Mercury Plains (2016)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama
Director: Charles Burmeister
Main Cast: Scott Eastwood, Angela Sarafyan, Nick Chinlund, Mike Gassaway, Keith Poulson
Keywords: mexico, fbi, illegal drugs, digging

Overview: A troubled man runs away to Mexico and is recruited to join a paramilitary group of teens fighting the drug cartels. He proves himself to the group, but questions their motive.
