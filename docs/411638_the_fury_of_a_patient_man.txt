Title: The Fury of a Patient Man (2016)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Raúl Arévalo
Main Cast: Antonio de la Torre, Luis Callejo, Ruth Díaz, Raúl Jiménez, Manolo Solo
Keywords: robbery, bar, son, father, revenge, crime

Overview: The tentative romance between a gentle Madrid labourer and a working single mother becomes a struggle for survival when the woman’s violent boyfriend returns from prison, in the gritty, suspenseful debut feature from Spanish actor Raúl Arévalo.
