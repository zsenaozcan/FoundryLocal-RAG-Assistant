Title: Venus in Furs (1969)
Genres: Drama
Director: Massimo Dallamano
Main Cast: Laura Antonelli, Régis Vallée, Loren Ewing, Renate Kasché, Werner Pochath
Keywords: sex, eroticism, nudity, voyeurism

Overview: Based on the infamous novel by Leopold Sacher-Masoch this fine film follows the perverted passions of a young couple as Severin watches the beautiful Wanda writhing naked amongst furs. His disturbing peeping tomism triggers off a whirlpool of emotions due to a childhood episode which punishes voyeurism with pain.
