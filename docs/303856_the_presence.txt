Title: The Presence (2014)
Genres: Horror
Director: Daniele Grieco
Main Cast: Liv Lisa Fries, Matthias Dietrich, Henning Nöhren, Andreas Stenschke, Susanne Reuter
Keywords: haunting, ghost, found footage, haunted castle

Overview: Hoping to film paranormal activity, three friends break into an abandoned castle, where an evil presence soon hounds them into terror and madness.
