Title: First Girl I Loved (2016)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Kerem Sanga
Main Cast: Dylan Gelula, Brianna Hildebrand, Mateo Arias, Pamela Adlon, Tim Heidecker
Keywords: high school, confusion, best friend, girl, female homosexuality, softball, first love, yearbook

Overview: Seventeen-year-old Anne just fell in love with Sasha, the most popular girl at her L.A. public high school. But when Anne tells her best friend, Clifton—who has always harbored a secret crush on her—he does his best to get in the way.
