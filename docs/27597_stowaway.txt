Title: Stowaway (1936)
Genres: Music, Adventure, Family
Director: William A. Seiter
Main Cast: Shirley Temple, Robert Young, Alice Faye, Eugene Pallette, Helen Westley
Keywords: stowaway, ship, musical

Overview: Chin-Ching gets lost in Shanghai and is befriended by American playboy Tommy Randall. She falls asleep in his car which winds up on a ship headed for America. Susan Parker, also on the ship, marries Randall to give Chin-Ching a family.
