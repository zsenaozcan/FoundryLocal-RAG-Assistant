Title: Shadow of the Vampire (2000)
Genres: Drama, Horror
Director: E. Elias Merhige
Main Cast: John Malkovich, Willem Dafoe, Udo Kier, Cary Elwes, Catherine McCormack
Keywords: film director, dracula,  nosferatu

Overview: Director F.W. Murnau (John Malkovich) makes a Faustian pact with a vampire (Willem Dafoe) to get him to star in his 1922 film "Nosferatu."
