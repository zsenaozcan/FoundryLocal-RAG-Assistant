Title: Elephant Boy (1937)
Genres: Family, Adventure, Drama
Director: Robert J. Flaherty
Main Cast: Sabu, W.E. Holloway, Walter Hudd, Allan Jeayes, Bruce Gordon

Overview: This black and white movie is based on Rudyard Kipling's "Toomai, of the Elephants", in which a small native lad claims he knows the congregating place of the elephant hordes.
