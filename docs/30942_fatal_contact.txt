Title: Fatal Contact (2006)
Genres: Action
Director: Dennis Law
Main Cast: Ronald Cheng, Miki Yeung, Theresa Fu, Eddie Cheung Siu-Fai, Ken Lo
Keywords: street fighter

Overview: A young man learns the fighting techniques of Sanda from a coach. The two become best friends as the young man prepares to enter an underground tournament, competing against some of the top fighters of the world.
