Title: South of Heaven, West of Hell (2000)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Drama, Western
Director: Dwight Yoakam
Main Cast: Dwight Yoakam, Billy Bob Thornton, Bridget Fonda, Peter Fonda, Paul Reubens
Keywords: marshal, outlaw, independent film, yoakam, tucson

Overview: Valentine Casey is a Marshal in the desolate Tucson territory of the early 1900's. On Christmas Eve, his outlaw family pays him a disturbing visit. He must confront the sins of his past. He and his partner, U.S. Christmas, journey through the desert to a small town that the ruthless Henry Clan has hit in order to save Casey's love, Adelyne.
