Title: Much Ado About Nothing (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alejandro Fernández Almendras
Main Cast: Agustín Silva, Li Fridman, Paulina García, Luis Gnecco, Alejandro Goic

Overview: After a night of partying, Vicente is involved in a hit-and-run that kills a man. Vicente claims his innocence, but he was drunk and high. A tangled web of lies buries the truth, making a social scandal disappear — exonerating the real culprit, the son of a powerful politician.
