Title: For Love or Money (1963)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Michael Gordon
Main Cast: Kirk Douglas, Mitzi Gaynor, Thelma Ritter, Gig Young, Julie Newmar

Overview: Wealthy Chloe Brasher has three beautiful daughters; Bonnie, Kate, and Jan. Chloe pays attorney Deke Gentry to fix them up with three suitable husbands.
