Title: Last Minute (2016)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Tiina Lymi
Main Cast: Iina Kuustonen, Veera W. Vilo, Jussi Vatanen, Ville Tiihonen, Lotta Kaihua

Overview: This Finnish drama film, directed by Tiina Lymi, tells the story of a childless career woman and a girl on the run away from the girl's grandmother. The director, screenwriter and producer and most of the actors in this film are all women.
