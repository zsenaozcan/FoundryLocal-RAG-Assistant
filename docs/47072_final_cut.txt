Title: Final Cut (1998)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Thriller
Director: Dominic Anciano
Main Cast: Jude Law, Sadie Frost, John Beckett, Ray Winstone, William Scully
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A group of friends gather for a wake, and are shown a video shot by Jude (Jude Law) and starring them before he dies. Things go rapidly downhill.
