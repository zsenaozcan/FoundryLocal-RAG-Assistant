Title: Kaaka Muttai (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: M. Manikandan
Main Cast: Master Vignesh, Master Ramesh, Aishwarya Rajesh, Joe Malloori, RJ Ramesh Thilak
Keywords: children's story, children's adventure

Overview: Two slum kids yearn to taste a pizza after being enticed by the pizza shop that has opened near their locality. What happens when they mange to find the money to buy one?
