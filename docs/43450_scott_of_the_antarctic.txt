Title: Scott of the Antarctic (1948)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Action, History
Director: Charles Frend
Main Cast: John Mills, Derek Bond, Kenneth More, James Robertson Justice, Christopher Lee
Keywords: antarctica

Overview: The true story of the British explorer Robert Falcon Scott and his ill-fated expedition to try to be the first man to discover the South Pole - only to find that the murderously cold weather and a rival team of Norwegian explorers conspire against him
