Title: The Hypothesis of the Stolen Painting (1978)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: Raúl Ruiz
Main Cast: Jean Rougeul, Gabriel Gascon

Overview: Two narrators, one seen and one unseen, discuss possible connections between a series of paintings. The on-screen narrator walks through three-dimensional reproductions of each painting, featuring real people, sometimes moving, in an effort to explain the series' significance.
