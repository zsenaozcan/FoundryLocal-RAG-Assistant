Title: The Green Berets (1968)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: John Wayne
Main Cast: John Wayne, David Janssen, George Takei, Jack Soo, Jim Hutton
Keywords: vietnam war, green beret

Overview: Col. Mike Kirby picks two teams of crack Green Berets for a mission in South Vietnam. First off is to build and control a camp that is trying to be taken by the enemy the second mission is to kidnap a North Vietnamese General
