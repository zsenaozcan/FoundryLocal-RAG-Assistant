Title: Billy the Kid (1941)
Genres: Western
Director: Frank Borzage
Main Cast: Robert Taylor, Brian Donlevy, Gene Lockhart, Lon Chaney Jr., Ian Hunter
Keywords: billy the kid

Overview: Billy Bonney is a hot-headed gunslinger who narrowly skirts a life of crime by being befriended and hired by a peaceful rancher, Eric Keating. When Keating is killed, Billy seeks revenge on the men who killed him, even if it means opposing his friend, Marshal Jim Sherwood.
