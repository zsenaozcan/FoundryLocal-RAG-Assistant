Title: Grizzly (1976)
Genres: Horror
Director: William Girdler
Main Cast: Christopher George, Andrew Prine, Richard Jaeckel, Joan McCall, Joe Dorsey
Keywords: hunter, helicopter, camping, grizzly bear, national park, helicopter pilot, park ranger, american wilderness, pacific northwest, animal horror

Overview: A fifteen-foot grizzly bear figures out that humans make for a tasty treat. As a park ranger tries rallying his men to bring about the bear's capture or destruction, his efforts are thwarted by the introduction of dozens of drunken hunters into the area.
