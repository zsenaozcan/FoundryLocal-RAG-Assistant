Title: The House of Exorcism (1975)
Genres: Horror
Director: Mario Bava
Main Cast: Telly Savalas, Elke Sommer, Robert Alda, Sylva Koscina, Alida Valli
Keywords: exorcism, strait jacket

Overview: A total re-edit of Mario Bava's gothic classic Lisa and the Devil (1973) for US release in 1975. Cheesy exorcism scenes were shot to try to capitalize on the success of The Exorcist (1973).
