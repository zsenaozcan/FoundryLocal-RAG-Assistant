Title: Special Correspondents (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ricky Gervais
Main Cast: Ricky Gervais, Eric Bana, Vera Farmiga, Kelly Macdonald, Benjamin Bratt
Keywords: reporter, con game

Overview: A radio journalist and his technician get in over their heads when they hatch a scheme to fake their own kidnapping during a rebel uprising in South America and hide out in New York instead.
