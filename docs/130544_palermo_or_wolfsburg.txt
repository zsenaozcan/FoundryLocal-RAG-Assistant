Title: Palermo or Wolfsburg (1980)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Werner Schroeter
Main Cast: Otto Sander, Magdalena Montezuma, Isolde Barth, Gisela Hahn, Harry Baer

Overview: Palermo or Wolfsburg (German: Palermo oder Wolfsburg) is a 1980 film by Werner Schroeter. It tells the story of an Italian immigrant who comes to West Germany in search of work.
