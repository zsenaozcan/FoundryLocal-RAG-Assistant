Title: Una noche (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lucy Mulloy
Main Cast: Daniel Arrechaga, Analin de la Rua de la Torre, Javier Nunez Florian, Maria Adelaida Mendez Bonet, Greisy del Valle
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In Havana, Raul dreams of escaping to Miami. Accused of assault, he appeals to Elio to help him reach the forbidden world 90 miles across the ocean. One night, full of hope, they face the biggest challenge of their lives.
