Title: Gone Too Far! (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Destiny Ekaragha
Main Cast: Malachi Kirby, O.C. Ukeje, Shanika Warren-Markland, Adelayo Adedayo, Tosin Cole
Keywords: africa, black, urban, dog, nollywood, nigerian cinema, woman director, nigerian community, urban comedy, african community, peckham

Overview: GONE TOO FAR follows two estranged teenage brothers over the course of a single day as they meet for the first time, and struggle to accept each other for who they are. Yemi can't wait for his big brother to join him on the estate in Peckham - but when Ikudayisi arrives from Nigeria wearing socks and sandals Yemi questions both his judgement and his African heritage. A day on the estate filled with danger and excitement teaches both of them the values of family and self respect.
