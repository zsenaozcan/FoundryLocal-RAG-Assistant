Title: Dead or Alive (1999)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: Takashi Miike
Main Cast: Riki Takeuchi, Sho Aikawa, Renji Ishibashi, Hitoshi Ozawa, Shingo Tsurumi
Keywords: yakuza, undercover agent, viertel, gangster, police officer, hoodlum

Overview: Ryūichi and his small gang of Triad vie for control of the Japanese underworld in a crime-ridden Shinjuku quarter while Detective Jojima tries to bring it down. Jojima attempts to start a gang war between the Triad and yakuza. Ryūichi and Jojima meet.
