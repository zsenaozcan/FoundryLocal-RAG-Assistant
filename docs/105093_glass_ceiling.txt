Title: Glass Ceiling (1971)
Genres: Mystery, Horror
Director: Eloy de la Iglesia
Main Cast: Carmen Sevilla, Dean Selmier, Patty Shepard, Emma Cohen

Overview: A housewife is frequently left alone by her husband in their apartment, as his business requires him to travel. The woman who lives upstairs is also minus her husband, but Sevilla begins to catch occasional lies and half-truths from her upstairs neighbor, which leads Sevilla to think that Shepard has murdered her husband. Sevilla can't quite keep her mouth shut about the matter, however, and despite the fact that her friends think her imagination is running wild, she does not really begin to suspect the danger until it is too late
