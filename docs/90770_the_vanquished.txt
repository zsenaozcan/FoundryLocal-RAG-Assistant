Title: The Vanquished (1953)
Genres: Drama, Action, Crime
Director: Michelangelo Antonioni
Main Cast: Franco Interlenghi, Anna Maria Ferrero, Eduardo Ciannelli, Peter Reynolds, Evi Maltagliati

Overview: A film by Michelangelo Antonioni composed of three episodes: a French episode set in Paris, an Italian episode set in Rome and an English episode set in London. Three stories of well-off youths who commit murders.
