Title: Let the Right One In (2008)
Genres: Horror, Drama
Director: Tomas Alfredson
Main Cast: Kåre Hedebrant, Lina Leandersson, Per Ragnar, Henrik Dahl, Karin Bergquist
Keywords: female nudity, vampire, castration, bullying, child vampire, murder, bully, androgyny, new neighbor, young love

Overview: Set in 1982 in the suburb of Blackeberg, Stockholm, twelve-year-old Oskar is a lonely outsider, bullied at school by his classmates; at home, Oskar dreams of revenge against a trio of bullies. He befriends his twelve-year-old, next-door neighbor Eli, who only appears at night in the snow-covered playground outside their building.
