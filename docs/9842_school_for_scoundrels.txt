Title: School for Scoundrels (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Todd Phillips
Main Cast: Billy Bob Thornton, Jon Heder, Jacinda Barrett, Sarah Silverman, Michael Clarke Duncan
Keywords: date, competition, lovesickness, mentor, traffic policeman

Overview: A young guy short on luck, enrolls in a class to build confidence to help win over the girl of his dreams, which becomes complicated when his teacher has the same agenda.
