Title: Betty Boop's Museum (1932)
Genres: Comedy, Animation
Director: Dave Fleischer
Main Cast: Mae Questel
Keywords: museum

Overview: Koko is recruiting customers for a 50 cent sightseeing tour of the museum. Betty is Koko's only passenger. Betty gets locked inside by accident. The skeletons from the displays come to life and chase Betty, until she is finally rescued by Bimbo.
