Title: Flamenco at 5:15 (1983)
Genres: Documentary, Music
Director: Cynthia Scott
Main Cast: Susana Robledo, Antonio Robledo
Keywords: dance, flamenco, woman director

Overview: In this Oscar Winning documentary short film, students in their final year at the National Ballet School of Canada are seen learning the flamenco from Susana and Antonio Robledo, who come to the school every winter to conduct classes which are held after the day's regular schedule has ended.
