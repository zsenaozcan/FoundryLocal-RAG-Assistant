Title: Black's Game (2012)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Thriller, Action
Director: Óskar Thór Axelsson
Main Cast: Thor Kristjansson, Jóhannes Haukur Jóhannesson, Damon Younger, María Birta, Vignir Rafn Valþórsson
Keywords: orgy, iceland, drug

Overview: In the mid to late 90's, the Reykjavik crime and drug scene saw a drastic change from a relatively small and innocent world into a much more aggressive and violent one.. The film tells the story of this change through the fictional gang of pushers that took control of Iceland's underworld.
