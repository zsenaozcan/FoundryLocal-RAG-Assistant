Title: Man of the Story (1996)
Genres: Drama
Director: Adoor Gopalakrishnan
Main Cast: Vishwanathan, Mini Nair, Aranmulla Ponnamma, Urmila Unni, K P A C Lalitha

Overview: This intimately-constructed drama traces the consequences of the tumultuous political and social changes that swept through India in the years since pre-Independence 1937.
