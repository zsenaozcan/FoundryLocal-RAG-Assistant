Title: The Forest (2011)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Shan Serafin
Main Cast: Kyle Lardner, Michael Madsen, Johnny Young, Christina Myhr, Lisa Cullen

Overview: By taking her own life in the infamous Suicide Forest of Mount Fuji, a sexy American cover-girl supernaturally transforms into a demonic spirit to wreak a violent revenge, one by one, upon those who helped her ex-boyfriend leave her. .
