Title: Belles on their Toes (1952)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Henry Levin
Main Cast: Jeanne Crain, Myrna Loy, Debra Paget, Jeffrey Hunter, Edward Arnold
Keywords: college, sequel, single mother

Overview: The "Cheaper by the Dozen" crew is back, sans Clifton Webb. Lillian is struggling to make ends meet without her husband's income, while Anne, Martha, and even Ernestine find romance.
