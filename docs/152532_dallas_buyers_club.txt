Title: Dallas Buyers Club (2013)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Jean-Marc Vallée
Main Cast: Matthew McConaughey, Jennifer Garner, Jared Leto, Denis O'Hare, Steve Zahn
Keywords: women, aids, biography, based on true story, hiv, drug, 1980s

Overview: Loosely based on the true-life tale of Ron Woodroof, a drug-taking, women-loving, homophobic man who in 1986 was diagnosed with HIV/AIDS and given thirty days to live.
