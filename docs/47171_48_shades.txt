Title: 48 Shades (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Daniel Lapaine
Main Cast: Richard Wilson, Emma Lung, Robin McLeavy, Nicholas Donaldson, Victoria Thaine
Keywords: based on novel, school boy crush

Overview: This coming of age comedy is about a 16 year old, Dan, who must choose between going to Geneva with his parents for a year or move into a house with his young Aunt, Jacq, and her roommate, Naomi.
