Title: Prem Ratan Dhan Payo (2015)
Genres: Drama, Action, Romance
Director: Sooraj R. Barjatya
Main Cast: Salman Khan, Sonam Kapoor, Neil Nitin Mukesh, Anupam Kher, Swara Bhaskar

Overview: Loosely based on the novel The prince and the Pauper. Ever loving Prem is respected and loved by all and Vijay (also played by Salman Khan) is in the world of hatred and violence. They change their identities temporarily to discover the other side of the world.
