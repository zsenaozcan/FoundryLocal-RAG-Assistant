Title: One Starry Christmas (2014)
Genres: Romance
Director: John Bradshaw
Main Cast: Sarah Carter, Damon Runyan, Paul Popowich, Kathleen Laskey, Neil Crone

Overview: An aspiring astronomy professor finds unexpected Christmas romance when she meets a charming cowboy during her holiday travel. As she decides between this new cowboy and her practical boyfriend, she must decide whether it's better to play it safe in love, or let an adventurous cowboy steal her heart.
