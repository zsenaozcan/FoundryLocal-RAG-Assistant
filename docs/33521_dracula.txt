Title: Dracula (1979)
Genres: Horror, Romance
Director: John Badham
Main Cast: Frank Langella, Laurence Olivier, Donald Pleasence, Kate Nelligan, Trevor Eve
Keywords: vampire, dracula, insane asylum, remake, gothic horror

Overview: Romanticized adaptation of Bram Stoker's 1897 classic. Set in 1913 England, the bloodsucking, but handsome, charming and seductive, Count Dracula seeks an immortal bride.
