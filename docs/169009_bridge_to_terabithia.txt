Title: Bridge To Terabithia (1985)
Genres: Drama, Family, Fantasy
Director: Eric Till
Main Cast: Annette O'Toole, Julian Coutts, Julie Beaulieu
Keywords: puppy, bereavement, school, drawing, girl

Overview: Jess Aarons and new girl Leslie Burke create a world of their own and call it Terabithia and pretend to be the king and queen. They return to their magical kingdom every day after school.
