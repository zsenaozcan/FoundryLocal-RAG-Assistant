Title: The Pope Must Die (1991)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Peter Richardson
Main Cast: Robbie Coltrane, Beverly D'Angelo, Alex Rocco, Adrian Edmondson, Paul Bartel
Keywords: vatican, pope, priest, alternative comedy

Overview: A less-than-qualified and far-from-perfect priest is mistakenly named the new pope. As the pontiff, he must deal with Vatican corruption, the Mob and the reappearance of his old lover.
