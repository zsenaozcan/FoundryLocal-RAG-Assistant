Title: The Glitterball (1977)
Genres: Family, Science Fiction
Director: Harley Cokeliss
Main Cast: Ben Buckton, Ron Pember, Keith Jayne, Marjorie Yates, Barry Jackson
Keywords: extraterrestrial, spaceship, alien, children, independent film, kids

Overview: Two teenage boys try to help a tiny spherical alien get back to its mothership, while the army and a devious petty crook pursue the creature for its wonderful powers.
