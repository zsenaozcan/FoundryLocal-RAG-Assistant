Title: Dark Ride (2006)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Craig Singer
Main Cast: Patrick Renna, David Clayton Rogers, Alex Solowitz, Andrea Bogart, Jennifer Tisdale
Keywords: carnival, psychopath, road trip

Overview: Ten years after he brutally murdered two girls, a killer escapes from a mental institution and returns to his turf, the theme park attraction called Dark Ride. About to crash his path are a group of college kids on a road trip who stumble across the park.
