Title: The Wedding Chest (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Nurbek Egen
Main Cast: Natacha Régnier, Bolot Tentimyshov, Tynar Abdrazaeva, Melis Sharshenbaev, Asan Amanov
Keywords: wedding, kyrgyzstan

Overview: Aïdar, a young Kyrgyz man returns home from his studies in France, with a beautiful French fiancée in tow. His fiancée is warmly welcomed by the village and captivated by the beauty of the region, but he stubbornly refuses to tell his family that the two are engaged.
