Title: Divorce Iranian Style (1998)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Kim Longinotto
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: DIVORCE IRANIAN STYLE unfolds inside an Iranian divorce court, providing a subtle and intimate look at the lives of women in a country stereotypically associated with fanaticism and oppression. Astute and beautifully observed, the film reveals the ingenuity and humour with which Iranian women negotiate the restrictions of their society.
