Title: The Little Mermaid: Ariel's Beginning (2008)
Genres: Family, Animation
Director: Peggy Holmes
Main Cast: Jodi Benson, Samuel E. Wright, Sally Field, Jim Cummings, Parker Goris
Keywords: children, woman director

Overview: Follow Ariel's adventures before she gave up her fins for true love. When Ariel wasn't singing with her sisters, she spent time with her mother, Queen Athena. Ariel is devastated when Athena is kidnapped by pirates, and after King Triton outlaws all singing. Along with pals Flounder and Sebastian, Ariel sets off in hopes of changing her father's decision to ban music from the kingdom.
