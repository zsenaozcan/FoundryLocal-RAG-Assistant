Title: The Phantom (2009)
Genres: Science Fiction, Action
Director: Paolo Barzman
Main Cast: Ryan Carnes, Sandrine Holt, Jean Marchand, Cameron Goodman, Isabella Rossellini

Overview: Before Superman, Spider-Man, and Batman, there was The Phantom, the greatest legend in the annals of 20th century comic-book crime fighting. Now, the immortal superhero returns to strike terror in the hearts of villains around the world - and to inspire a new generation to believe in the Ghost Who Walks.
