Title: Original Gangstas (1996)
Genres: Action, Drama, Crime, Thriller
Director: Larry Cohen
Main Cast: Fred Williamson, Pam Grier, Jim Brown, Paul Winfield, Isabel Sanford
Keywords: vigilante, blaxploitation, b movie, violence, drive by shooting, gang violence, street shootout, hand to hand combat, gangsters

Overview: A violent street gang, the Rebels, rule the streets of Gary, Indiana. The Rebels shoot Marvin Bookman, a store-keeper, for giving the police information about a drive-by shooting they committed. Marvin's son, former NFL star John who created the Rebels, returns to Gary to be with his father and, with a little help from his friends, to destroy the Rebels his way.
