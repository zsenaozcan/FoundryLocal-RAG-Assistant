Title: A Study in Choreography for Camera (1945)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Maya Deren
Main Cast: Talley Beatty
Keywords: dance performance, experimental film, woman director, short

Overview: Maya Deren’s shortest, five-minute A Study in Choreography for Camera seems like an exercise piece to capture a dancer’s movement on celluloid, which later on developed into her masterpieces such as Ritual in Transfigured Time and Meditation on Violence.
