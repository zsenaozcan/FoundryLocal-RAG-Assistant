Title: Godzilla vs. Mechagodzilla (1974)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Jun Fukuda
Main Cast: Masaaki Daimon, Kazuya Aoyama, Reiko Tajima, Akihiko Hirata, Hiroshi Koizumi
Keywords: godzilla, tokusatsu, robot, kaiju

Overview: Great monster fight sequences are a highlight of the 14th Godzilla film. Aliens armed with a mechanical Godzilla intend to take over the planet.  The earthlings summon the legendary King Seesar to assist Godzilla in the battle to defend earth.
