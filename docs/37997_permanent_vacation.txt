Title: Permanent Vacation (1980)
Genres: Drama
Director: Jim Jarmusch
Main Cast: Chris Parker, Leila Gastil, Sara Driver, Charlie Spademan, Jane Fire
Keywords: independent film

Overview: In downtown Manhattan, a twenty-something boy whose Father is not around and whose Mother is institutionalized, is a big Charlie Parker fan. He almost subconsciously searches for more meaning in his life and meets a few characters along the way.
