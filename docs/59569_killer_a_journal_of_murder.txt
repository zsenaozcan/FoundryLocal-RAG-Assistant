Title: Killer: A Journal of Murder (1996)
Genres: Drama
Director: Tim Metcalfe
Main Cast: James Woods, Robert Sean Leonard, Cara Buono, Ellen Greene, Robert John Burke
Keywords: prison, serial killer, sympathy

Overview: Carl Panzram is sent to Leavenworth Prison for burglary. While there, he is brutally beaten by a guard. Neophyte guard Henry Lesser feels sympathy for Panzram, befriends him, and gets him to write his life story. Lesser learns that Panzram's past is much more violent than he thought, but also that he's capable of being a much better person than the rest of the prison staff believes - or so Lesser thinks.
