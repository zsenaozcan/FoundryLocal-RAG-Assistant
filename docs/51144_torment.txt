Title: Torment (1944)
Genres: Drama
Director: Alf Sjöberg
Main Cast: Stig Järrel, Alf Kjellin, Mai Zetterling, Olof Winnerstrand, Gösta Cederlund
Keywords: schoolmaster, schoolboy

Overview: An idealistic adolescent, suffering under the thumb of a sadistic schoolmaster, falls in love with a loose girl who is bullied and tormented by another lover.
