Title: The Bank (2001)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Robert Connolly
Main Cast: David Wenham, Anthony LaPaglia, Sibylla Budd, Steve Rodgers, Mitchell Butel
Keywords: suspense, cigarette smoking, first date, bank officer

Overview: The Bank, a world ripe with avarice and corruption, where O'Reilly and his ilk can thrive and honest Aussie battlers lose everything. Enter Jim Doyle a maverick mathematician who has devised a formula to predict the fluctuations of the stock market. When he joins O'Reilly's fold, he must first prove his loyalty to the "greed is good" ethos. Which way will he go? What does he have to hide?
