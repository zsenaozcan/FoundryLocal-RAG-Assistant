Title: I Love You Too (2010)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Daina Reid
Main Cast: Brendan Cowell, Peter Helliar, Peter Dinklage, Travis McMahon, Yvonne Strahovski
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Written by comedian Peter Helliar, I LOVE YOU TOO stars Brendan Cowell as Jim, a 30-something emotionally stunted man whose inability to declare his love to his girlfriend, Alice, threatens to cost him the best thing he ever had but leads him to befriend a talented dwarf who helps him find the words to get her back.
