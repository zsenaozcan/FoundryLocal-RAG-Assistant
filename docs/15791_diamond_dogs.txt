Title: Diamond Dogs (2007)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Thriller
Director: Samuel Dolhasca
Main Cast: Dolph Lundgren, Xue Zuren, William Shriver, Raicho Vasilev, NuoMing Huari

During the Soviet crack-down on religion in the 1930's, a priceless Buddhist artifact, a Tangka was taken across the border to China and hidden in the mountains.
