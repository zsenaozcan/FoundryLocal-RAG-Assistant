Title: This Above All (1942)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: Anatole Litvak
Main Cast: Tyrone Power, Joan Fontaine, Thomas Mitchell, Henry Stephenson, Nigel Bruce
Keywords: blind date, awol

Overview: Although she comes from an aristocratic family, beautiful Prudence Cathaway defies convention by joining the WAAFs and becoming romantically involved with an AWOL soldier.
