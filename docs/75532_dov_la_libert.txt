Title: Dov'è la libertà...? (1954)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Roberto Rossellini
Main Cast: Totò, Vera Molnar, Nyta Dover, Franca Faldini, Leopoldo Trieste

Overview: A barber, murderer because of jealousy, spends twenty years in jail. He cannot, however adjust himself to a changed world and to the hypocrisy of his own relatives and decides to return behind bars.
