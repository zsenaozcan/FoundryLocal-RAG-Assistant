Title: Enamorada (1946)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War
Director: Emilio Fernández
Main Cast: María Félix, Pedro Armendáriz, Fernando Fernández, José Morcillo, Eduardo Arozamena
Keywords: horse, mexican revolution, song, love-hate relationship, love, melodrama, singing, execution, firecracker, catholic priest

Overview: In Mexican Revolution times, a guerrilla general and his troops take the conservative town of Cholula, near by Mexico City. As the revolutionaries mistreat the town's riches, Armendáriz falls for beautiful and wild Beatriz Peñafiel, the daughter of one of the town's richest men.
