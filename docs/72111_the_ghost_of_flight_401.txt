Title: The Ghost of Flight 401 (1978)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Steven Hilliard Stern
Main Cast: Ernest Borgnine, Kim Basinger, Gary Lockwood, Tina Chen, Tom Clancy
Keywords: disaster, airplane crash

Overview: An aircraft crashes in the Florida Everglades, killing 103 passengers. After the wreckage is removed, salvageable parts from the plane are used to repair other aircraft. Soon passengers and crew on those aircraft report seeing what they believe to be the ghost of the wrecked airplane's flight engineer.
