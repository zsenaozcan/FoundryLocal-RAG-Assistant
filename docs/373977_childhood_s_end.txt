Title: Childhood's End (2015)
Genres: Thriller, TV Movie, Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Nick Hurran
Main Cast: Mike Vogel, Osy Ikhile, Daisy Betts, Georgina Haig, Charles Dance

Overview: After peaceful aliens invade earth, humanity finds itself living in a utopia under the indirect rule of the aliens, but does this utopia come at a price?
