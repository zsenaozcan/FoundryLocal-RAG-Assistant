Title: The Revenant (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: D. Kerry Prior 
Main Cast: Chris Wylde, David Anders, Louise Griffiths, Jacy King, Eric Payne
Keywords: police, vigilante, blood, gangster, revenant

Overview: A horror movie/buddy comedy about Joey and his undead friend Bart who comes back from the dead as a revenant: an articulate zombie that needs to drink blood to arrest the decomposition of his body.
