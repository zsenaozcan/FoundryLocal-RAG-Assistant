Title: Fantômas: The False Magistrate (1914)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Louis Feuillade
Main Cast: René Navarre, Georges Melchior, Edmund Breon, Mesnery, Laurent Morléas

Overview: Inspector Juve finds out that Fantômas is serving a prison sentence in Belgium. Desperately eager to get the master criminal to France, Inspecteur Juve comes up with a plot to allow Fantômas to escape.
