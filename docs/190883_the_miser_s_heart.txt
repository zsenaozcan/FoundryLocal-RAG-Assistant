Title: The Miser's Heart (1911)
Genres: Unknown
Director: D.W. Griffith
Main Cast: Linda Arvidson, Lionel Barrymore, William J. Butler, Donald Crisp, Blanche Sweet

Overview: Thieves decide to steal the money an old miser has hidden away. He refuses to open the safe for them, so they threaten to kill a girl who lives in his building
