Title: Nasty Habits (1977)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michael Lindsay-Hogg
Main Cast: Glenda Jackson, Melina Mercouri, Geraldine Page, Sandy Dennis, Anne Jackson
Keywords: nun, convent, abbess

Overview: In a Philadelphia convent, two nuns battle it out to be elected to the position of head abbess, and neither is about to let anything stand in the way of getting what she wants.
