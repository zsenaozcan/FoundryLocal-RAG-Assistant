Title: Tears of April (2008)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Aku Louhimies
Main Cast: Samuli Vauramo, Pihla Viitala, Eero Aho, Eemeli Louhimies, Miina Maasola

Overview: There is civil war in Finland. Between whites vs reds. A woman soldier of reds is captured by the whites. She is ordered to be executed.
