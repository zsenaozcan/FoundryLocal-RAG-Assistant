Title: Paul F. Tompkins: Crying and Driving (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Marcus Raboy
Main Cast: Paul F. Tompkins
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Paul F. Tompkins tells tales of haunting one’s own house, disastrous attempts at pretend fatherhood, carrying a learner’s permit to kill, and marrying a woman who used a fine-print loophole to breach a castle.
