Title: The Perfect Score (2004)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Brian Robbins
Main Cast: Chris Evans, Bryan Greenberg, Scarlett Johansson, Erika Christensen, Matthew Lillard
Keywords: secret, test, school, student, hacking, exams

Overview: Six high school seniors decide to break into the Princeton Testing Center so they can steal the answers to their upcoming SAT tests and all get perfect scores.
