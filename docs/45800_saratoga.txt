Title: Saratoga (1937)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Comedy
Director: Jack Conway
Main Cast: Jean Harlow, Clark Gable, Lionel Barrymore, Frank Morgan, Walter Pidgeon
Keywords: gambling, bookie

Overview: A horse breeder's (Lionel Barrymore) granddaughter (Jean Harlow) falls in love with a gambler (Clark Gable) in Saratoga Springs, N.Y.
