Title: The Craigslist Killer (2011)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Stephen T. Kay
Main Cast: Jake McDorman, Agnes Bruckner, Julia Campbell, Kevin Kilner, Candice Patton
Keywords: murder, student, violence, true crime

Overview: Based on a true story. Philip Markoff, a charismatic and popular med student at Boston University, leads a double life as a brutal and cruel sexual deviant who abuses prostitutes he finds via Craigslist.
