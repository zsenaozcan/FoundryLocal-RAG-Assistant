Title: The Notebook (2013)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: János Szász
Main Cast: András Gyémánt, László Gyémánt, Molnár Piroska, Ulrich Thomsen, Ulrich Matthes

Overview: In a village on the Hungarian border, two young brothers grow up during war time with their cruel grandmother and must learn every trick of evil to survive in the absurd world of adults.
