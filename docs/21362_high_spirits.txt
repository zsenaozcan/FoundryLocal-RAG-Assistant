Title: High Spirits (1988)
Genres: Horror, Fantasy, Comedy
Director: Neil Jordan
Main Cast: Steve Guttenberg, Peter O'Toole, Daryl Hannah, Beverly D'Angelo, Liam Neeson
Keywords: romantic comedy, spirit

Overview: When Peter Plunkett's Irish castle turned hotel is about to be repossesed, he decides to spice up the attraction a bit for the 'Yanks' by having his staff pretend to haunt the castle. The trouble begins when a busload of American tourists arrive - along with some real ghosts.
