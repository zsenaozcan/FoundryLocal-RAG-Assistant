Title: Anchors Aweigh (1945)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Music, Romance
Director: George Sidney
Main Cast: Frank Sinatra, Kathryn Grayson, Gene Kelly, José Iturbi, Dean Stockwell
Keywords: musical, new york city, cartoon mouse, live action and animation

Overview: Two sailors, Joe (Kelly) and Clarence (Sinatra) have four days shore leave in spend their shore leave trying to get a girl for Clarence. Clarence has his eye on a girl with musical aspirations, and before Joe can stop him, promises to get her an audition with José Iturbi. But the trouble really starts when Joe realizes he's falling for his buddy's girl.
