Title: The Christmas Toy (1986)
Genres: Animation, Family
Director: Eric Till
Main Cast: Dave Goelz, Steve Whitmire, Kathryn Mullen, Jerry Nelson, Richard Hunt
Keywords: holiday, musical, christmas eve

Overview: The Christmas Toy is a 1986 Emmy Award nominated television film produced by The Jim Henson Company, featuring Jim Henson's Muppets.  Rugby the Tiger who remembers how he was the Christmas Toy last year, and thinks he's going to be unwrapped again this year.
