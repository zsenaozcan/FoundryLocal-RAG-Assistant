Title: The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes (1939)
Genres: Crime, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Alfred L. Werker
Main Cast: Basil Rathbone, Nigel Bruce, Ida Lupino, Alan Marshal, Terry Kilburn
Keywords: jewel, tower of london, chinchilla foot, playing flute

Overview: Professor Moriarity has a scheme for stealing the crown jewels from the Tower of London. To get Holmes involved, he persuades a gaucho flute player to murder a girl.
