Title: Murder She Said (1961)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Mystery, Comedy
Director: George Pollock
Main Cast: Margaret Rutherford, Arthur Kennedy, Muriel Pavlow, James Robertson Justice, Thorley Walters
Keywords: poison, country estate, amateur detective, detective inspector, golf, false identity, employment agency, miss marple, murder mystery

Overview: Miss Marple believes she’s seen a murder in a passing by train yet when the police find no evidence she decides to investigate it on her own. Her inquisitions lead her to a mountain top. The first of four films starring Margaret Rutherford as Miss Marple that all achieved cult status.
