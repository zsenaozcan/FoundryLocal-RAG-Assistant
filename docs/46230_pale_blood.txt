Title: Pale Blood (1990)
Genres: Action, Horror, Thriller
Director: V.V. Dachin Hsu
Main Cast: George Chakiris, Wings Hauser, Pamela Ludwig, Diana Frank, Darcy DeMoss
Keywords: vampire, role reversal, serial killer, bite mark

Overview: A vampire pretender who is murdering women runs into a real vampire who is out to stop him because he is casting too much of a spotlight on the vampire community.
