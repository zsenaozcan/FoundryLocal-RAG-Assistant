Title: Sandra (1965)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, War
Director: Luchino Visconti
Main Cast: Claudia Cardinale, Michael Craig, Jean Sorel, Marie Bell, Renzo Ricci
Keywords: deportation, auschwitz, family relationships, deceit, post world war ii

Overview: Sandra comes back to Volterra, in Tuscany, the little town where she spent her childhood. She is with her American husband, Andrew. She wishes to pay homage to her father who died in Auschwitz where she was still a little child. In Volterra, Andrew meets for the first time Gianni, Sandra's brother. He soon realizes that Sandra and Gianni have a secret since their childhood. (IMDb)
