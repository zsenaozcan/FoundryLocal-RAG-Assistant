Title: Tell No One (2006)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Guillaume Canet
Main Cast: François Cluzet, Marie-Josée Croze, Kristin Scott Thomas, François Berléand, André Dussollier
Keywords: drug abuse, secret, sadness, nudity, planned murder, dying and death, e-mail, cover-up, love, grief

Overview: A man receives a mysterious e-mail appearing to be from his wife, who was murdered years earlier. As he frantically tries to find out whether she's alive, he finds himself being implicated in her death.
