Title: Wolf Girl (2001)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Horror
Director: Thom Fitzgerald
Main Cast: Shelby Fenner, Shawn Ashmore, Tony Denman, Nate Dushku, Dov Tiefenbach

Overview: Tara Talbot is afflicted with the rare genetic condition hypertrichosis: she is covered head to toe in hair. She lives her life in Harley Dune's Travelling Freak Show, on display as the 'Terrifying Wolf Girl', but underneath she is just a teenage girl who longs to be normal.
