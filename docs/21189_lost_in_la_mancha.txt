Title: Lost in La Mancha (2002)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Keith Fulton
Main Cast: Terry Gilliam, Tony Grisoni, Philip A. Patterson, René Cleitman, Nicola Pecorini
Keywords: director, don quixote, cervantes

Overview: Fulton and Pepe's 2000 documentary captures Terry Gilliam's attempt to get The Man Who Killed Don Quixote off the ground. Back injuries, freakish storms, and more zoom in to sabotage the project (which has never been resurrected).
