Title: I'm Still Here (2010)
Genres: Music, Comedy, Drama
Director: Casey Affleck
Main Cast: Joaquin Phoenix, Sean Penn, Bruce Willis, Danny DeVito, Jack Nicholson
Keywords: duringcreditsstinger

Overview: I'm Still Here is a portrayal of a tumultuous year in the life of actor Joaquin Phoenix. With remarkable access, the documentary follows the Oscar-nominee as he announces his retirement from a successful film career in the fall of 2008 and sets off to reinvent himself as a hip-hop musician. The film is a portrait of an artist at a crossroads and explores notions of courage and creative reinvention, as well as the ramifications of a life spent in the public eye.
