Title: The Life of Riley (1949)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Irving Brecher
Main Cast: William Bendix, Rosemary DeCamp, James Gleason, Beulah Bondi, Bill Goodwin

Overview: Inspired by the popular '40s radio show of the same title, director Irving Brecher's 1949 comedy stars William Bendix as a hard-working husband-and-father with no shortage of family problems.
