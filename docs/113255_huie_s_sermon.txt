Title: Huie's Sermon (1983)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Werner Herzog
Main Cast: Huie Rogers
Keywords: usa, religion, church, brooklyn new york city

Overview: Reverend Huie Rogers is a preacher at the Bible Way Church of Our Lord Jesus Christ in Brooklyn. He is the topic of this short film, during which launches into an epic call-and-response denunciation of human hubris, greed, corruption and failure. The use of lengthy shots present it less like a sermon and more a performance, and induce an almost trance-like state.
