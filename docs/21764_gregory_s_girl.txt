Title: Gregory's Girl (1981)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Bill Forsyth
Main Cast: John Gordon Sinclair, Dee Hepburn, Jake D'Arcy, Clare Grogan, Robert Buchanan
Keywords: scotland, italian, affection, advice, coming of age, school

Overview: In his Scottish New Town home, gangling Gregory and his schoolfriends are starting to find out about girls. He fancies Dorothy, not least because she has got into the football team - and is a better player than him. He finally asks her out, but it is obviously the females in control of matters here, and that very much includes Gregory's younger sister.
