Title: Miss and the Doctors (2013)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Axelle Ropert
Main Cast: Louise Bourgoin, Laurent Stocker, Cédric Kahn, Paula Denis, Serge Bozon
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Two sibling doctors take on a young diabetic patient and both fall for the girl’s lovely mother, who tends bar at a local watering hole, testing their fraternal bonds and professional relationship.
