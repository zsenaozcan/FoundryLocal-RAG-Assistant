Title: Aileen Wuornos: The Selling of a Serial Killer (1992)
Genres: Documentary, Foreign
Director: Nick Broomfield
Main Cast: Aileen Wuornos, Nick Broomfield

Overview: Aileen Wuornos: The Selling of a Serial Killer (1993) is a documentary film about Aileen Wuornos, made by Nick Broomfield. It documents Broomfield's attempts to interview Wuornos, which involves a long process of mediation through her adopted mother Arlene Pralle and lawyer, Steve Glazer.
