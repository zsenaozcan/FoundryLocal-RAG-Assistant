Title: Death Sentence (1974)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director:  E.W. Swackhamer
Main Cast: Cloris Leachman, Laurence Luckinbill, Nick Nolte
Keywords: suspense, trial

Overview: A juror on a murder case begins to believe that the man on trial is innocent of the crime - and then discovers that the real killer is her own husband.
