Title: Hijacking Agatha (1993)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Marek Piwowski
Main Cast: Karolina Rosinska, Slawomir Federowicz, Pola Raksa, Jerzy Stuhr

Overview: A parliament member's sixteen year old daughter Agatha falls in love and runs away with a convicted young tramp, while her father uses his friends in the government and police to brutally break their happiness.
