Title: Dracula's Dog (1978)
Genres: Horror
Director: Albert Band
Main Cast: Michael Pataki, Jan Shutan, José Ferrer, JoJo D'Amore, Libby Chase
Keywords: transylvania, vampire, dracula, supernatural powers, dog, german shepherd, doberman, occult, hearse

Overview: Russian soldiers accidentally unleash Dracula's servant, as well as his dog, during excavations in Romania. Together they set out for America, to find the last descendent of the great Count.
