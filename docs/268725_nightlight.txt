Title: Nightlight (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Scott Beck
Main Cast: Shelby Young, Chloe Bridges, Carter Jenkins, Mitch Hewer, Taylor Ashley Murphy
Keywords: suicide, forest, blindfold, pet dog, ghost story, shaky camera, night games

Overview: Five friends play a game in a "mysterious" forest with a long history as a beacon for troubled young people contemplating suicide.
