Title: Running on Karma (2003)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Drama
Director: Johnnie To
Main Cast: Andy Lau, Cecilia Cheung, Eddie Cheung Siu-Fai, Karen Tong, Chun Wong
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A monk turned body-builder, with the gift to see into people's lives, befriends a female cop, and uses his gift to change the force of Karma and her destiny.
