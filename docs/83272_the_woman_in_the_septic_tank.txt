Title: The Woman in the Septic Tank (2011)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Marlon Rivera
Main Cast: Eugene Domingo, JM de Guzman, Cai Cortez, Kean Cipriano, Jonathan Tadioan
Keywords: philippines, pinoy

Overview: The story of Mila, a mother from the slums, who out of desperation to survive, has sold her child to a pedophile.
