Title: The Girl King (2015)
Genres: Drama, History, Romance
Director: Mika Kaurismäki
Main Cast: Malin Buska, Sarah Gadon, Michael Nyqvist, Lucas Bryant, Laura Birn
Keywords: gay, sweden, queen, banquet, based on true story, lesbian sex, lesbian interest, 17th century

Overview: A portrait of the brilliant, extravagant Kristina of Sweden, queen from age six, who fights the conservative forces that are against her ideas to modernize Sweden and who have no tolerance for her awakening sexuality.
