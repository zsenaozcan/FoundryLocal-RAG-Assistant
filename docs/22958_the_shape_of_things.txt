Title: The Shape of Things (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Neil LaBute
Main Cast: Paul Rudd, Gretchen Mol, Rachel Weisz, Frederick Weller
Keywords: seduction, college, love, friends, betrayal, independent film, makeover
