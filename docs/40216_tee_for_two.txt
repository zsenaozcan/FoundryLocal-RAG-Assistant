Title: Tee for Two (1945)
Genres: Animation
Director: William Hanna
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: golf, cat and mouse, golf course, cartoon cat, cartoon mouse, tom and jerry

Overview: Tom is golfing, but the ball keeps jumping out of the cup, thanks to Jerry. Tom puts Jerry to work as a tee, but Jerry keeps doing things like replacing Tom's ball with a bird's egg that hatches in flight, or tying his club to his tail.
