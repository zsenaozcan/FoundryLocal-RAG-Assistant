Title: Twelfth Night (1996)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Trevor Nunn
Main Cast: Helena Bonham Carter, Richard E. Grant, Nigel Hawthorne, Ben Kingsley, Mel Smith
Keywords: love, disguise

Overview: Shakespeare's comedy of gender confusion, in which a girl disguises herself as a man to be near the count she adores, only to be pursued by the woman he loves.
