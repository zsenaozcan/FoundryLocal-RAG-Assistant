Title: Inseminoid (1981)
Genres: Foreign, Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Norman J. Warren
Main Cast: Robin Clarke, Jennifer Ashley, Stephanie Beacham, Steven Grives, Barrie Houghton
Keywords: alien

Overview: A crew of interplanetary archaeologists is threatened when an alien creature impregnates one of their members, causing her to turn homicidal and murder them one by one.
