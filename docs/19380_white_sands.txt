Title: White Sands (1992)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Roger Donaldson
Main Cast: Willem Dafoe, Mary Elizabeth Mastrantonio, Mickey Rourke, Samuel L. Jackson, M. Emmet Walsh
Keywords: suspense

Overview: A small southwestern town sheriff finds a body in the desert with a suitcase and $500,000. He impersonates the man and stumbles into an FBI investigation.
