Title: Shanghai Express (1932)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Josef von Sternberg
Main Cast: Marlene Dietrich, Clive Brook, Anna May Wong, Warner Oland, Eugene Pallette
Keywords: china, rebel, prostitute, rape, shanghai, ex-lover, insurgence, chinese civil war, war, comedy

Overview: Shanghai Express is an American love story and adventure film by Josef von Sternberg from 1932 starring Marlene Dietrich. The film takes place in a train during the Chinese civil war.
