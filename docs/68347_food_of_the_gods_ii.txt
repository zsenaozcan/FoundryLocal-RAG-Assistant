Title: Food of the Gods II (1989)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Damian Lee
Main Cast: Paul Coufos, Lisa Schrage, Réal Andrews, Stuart Hughes, Karen Hines
Keywords: sequel, giant monster, genetic engineering, rat, canuxploitation

Overview: A growth hormone experiment gets out of hand, when the the resulting giant man-eating rats escape, reaking havoc on the unsuspecting campus. Much blood-letting follows.
