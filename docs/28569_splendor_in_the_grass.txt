Title: Splendor in the Grass (1961)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Elia Kazan
Main Cast: Natalie Wood, Warren Beatty, Pat Hingle, Audrey Christie, Barbara Loden
Keywords: unrequited love, stock market crash

Overview: A fragile Kansas girl's unrequited and forbidden love for a handsome young man from the town's most powerful family drives her to heartbreak and madness.
