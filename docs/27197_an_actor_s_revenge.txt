Genres: Drama
Director: Kon Ichikawa
Main Cast: Kazuo Hasegawa, Fujiko Yamamoto, Ayako Wakao, Eiji Funakoshi, Narutoshi Hayashi
Keywords: suicide, based on novel, performance, remake, sword fight, revenge, japanese culture, katana sword, samurai era, edo period
