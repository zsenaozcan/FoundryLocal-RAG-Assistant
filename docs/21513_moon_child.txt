Title: Moon Child (2003)
Genres: Horror, Action, Thriller, Science Fiction, Foreign
Director: Takahisa Zeze
Main Cast: Gackt Camui, Hyde, Leehom Wang, Taro Yamamoto, Susumu Terajima
Keywords: japan, asia, vampire, daywalker, romantic rivalry

Overview: MOON CHILD follows a group of childhood friends as they advance in a futuristic criminal underworld. Sho feels he is doomed to walk in his idol Kei's footsteps as a vampire with the gift of eternal life and the curse of blood thirst. Over time, their tight friendship becomes corrupted because of their rivalry and love for the same woman.
