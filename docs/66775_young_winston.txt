Title: Young Winston (1972)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Drama, War
Director: Richard Attenborough
Main Cast: Robert Shaw, Anne Bancroft, Simon Ward, Jack Hawkins, Ian Holm
Keywords: parliament, winston churchill, election, turn of the century

Overview: This historical drama is an account of the early life of British politician Winston Churchill, including his childhood years, his time as a war correspondent in Africa, and culminating with his first election to Parliament.
