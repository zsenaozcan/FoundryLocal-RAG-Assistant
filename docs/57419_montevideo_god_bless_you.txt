Title: Montevideo, God Bless You! (2010)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, History, Romance
Director: Dragan Bjelogrlić
Main Cast: Miloš Biković, Petar Strugar, Viktor Savić, Нина Јанковић, Predrag Vasić
Keywords: sport

Overview: A story about one team that decides to follow a dream that takes them on a journey to the First World Football Championship in Montevideo, Uruguay in 1930. A dream that allows them to become true stars and living legends.
