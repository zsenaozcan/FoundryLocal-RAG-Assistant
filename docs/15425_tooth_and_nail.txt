Title: Tooth and Nail (2007)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Mark Young
Main Cast: Rider Strong, Michael Kelly, Robert Carradine, Nicole DuPort, Alexandra Barreto
Keywords: kanibalismus, pflegen, blood splatter

Overview: In a post-apocalyptic world, a small group of survivors, who call themselves Foragers, plan to rebuild civilization from their headquarters in an empty hospital based in what is left of Philadelphia. But they're soon forced into a face-off war with the Rovers, another gang of survivors whom are a brutal gang of cannibals.
