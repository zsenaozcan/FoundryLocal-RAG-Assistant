Title: Dr. Cyclops (1940)
Genres: Adventure, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Ernest B. Schoedsack
Main Cast: Albert Dekker, Thomas Coley, Janice Logan, Charles Halton, Victor Kilian
Keywords: experiment, expedition, paranoia, human experimentation, amazon, radium, murder, laboratory, mad scientist, miniaturization

Overview: Four explorers are summoned to Peru by the brilliant physicist Dr Thorkel. They discover a rich source of radium and a half-mad Thorkel who shrinks them down to one-fifth their normal size when they threaten to stop his unorthodox experimentation
