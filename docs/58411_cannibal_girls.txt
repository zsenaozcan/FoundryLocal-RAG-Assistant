Title: Cannibal Girls (1973)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Ivan Reitman
Main Cast: Eugene Levy, Andrea Martin, Ronald Ulrich, Randall Carpenter, Bonnie Neilson
Keywords: murder, canuxploitation, cannibalism

Overview: A young couple spend the night in a restaurant, only to find out that it is haunted by three dead women who hunger for human flesh.
