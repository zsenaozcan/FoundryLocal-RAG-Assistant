Title: Quatermass and the Pit (1967)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Roy Ward Baker
Main Cast: James Donald, Andrew Keir, Barbara Shelley, Julian Glover, Duncan Lamont
Keywords: paleontology, riot, spaceship, martian, hammer horror

Overview: An ancient Martian spaceship is unearthed in London, and proves to have powerful psychic effects on the people around.
