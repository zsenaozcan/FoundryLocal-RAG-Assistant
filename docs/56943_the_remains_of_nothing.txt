Title: The Remains of Nothing (2004)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Antonietta De Lillo
Main Cast: Maria de Medeiros, Enzo Moscato, Rosario Sparno, Maria Grazia Grassini
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Set in the 1790s, this historical drama follows the travails of an idealistic noblewoman who helps lead a daring revolution in Italy.
