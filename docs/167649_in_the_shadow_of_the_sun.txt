Title: In the Shadow of the Sun (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Harry Freeland
Main Cast: Josephat Torner, Vedastus Zangule
Keywords: africa, albino, social activism

Overview: It is not easy to be an albino in Tanzania. White skin is distinguishable and it burns quickly under the African sun. However, the constant fear is by far the worst part. According to local superstition, albino body parts bring wealth and luck; hence witch doctors pay generously for a leg or an arm.
