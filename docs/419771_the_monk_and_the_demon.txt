Title: The Monk and the Demon (2016)
Genres: Mystery, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Nikolai Dostal
Main Cast: Timofey Tribuntsev, Georgiy Fetisov, Boris Kamorzin, Roman Madyanov, Nikita Tarasov

Overview: Fantastic story of the first half of the XIX century about a monk named Ivan who must fight the dark forces in his monastery.
