Title: The Hamburg Syndrome (1979)
Genres: Comedy, Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Peter Fleischmann
Main Cast: Helmut Griem, Tilo Prückner, Ulrich Wildgruber, Evelyn Künneke, Rosel Zech

Overview: When a plague breaks out in Hamburg, several people break out of quarantine and make their way out of the city... only to find that the plague is more widespread.
