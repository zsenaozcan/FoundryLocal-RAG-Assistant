Title: Bachelor Mother (1939)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Garson Kanin
Main Cast: Ginger Rogers, David Niven, Charles Coburn, Frank Albertson, E.E. Clive
Keywords: baby, department store, misunderstanding, motherhood

Overview: Polly Parrish, a clerk at Merlin's Department Store, is mistakenly presumed to be the mother of a foundling. Outraged at Polly's unmotherly conduct, David Merlin becomes determined to keep the single woman and "her" baby together.
