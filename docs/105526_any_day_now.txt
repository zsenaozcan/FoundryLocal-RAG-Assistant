Title: Any Day Now (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Travis Fine
Main Cast: Alan Cumming, Garret Dillahunt, Isaac Leyva, Frances Fisher, Gregg Henry
Keywords: california, drag queen, homophobia, 1970s, adoption, down syndrome, gay relationship, gay interest, teenage boy, district attorney

Overview: In the late 1970s, when a mentally handicapped teenager is abandoned, a gay couple takes him in and becomes the family he's never had. But once the unconventional living arrangement is discovered by authorities, the men must fight the legal system to adopt the child.
