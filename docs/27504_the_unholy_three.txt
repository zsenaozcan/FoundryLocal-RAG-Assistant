Title: The Unholy Three (1930)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Jack Conway
Main Cast: Lon Chaney, Lila Lee, Elliott Nugent, Harry Earles, John Miljan

Overview: "Talkie" remake of Tod Browning's 1925 silent film. A trio of former sideshow performers double as the "Unholy Three" in a scam to nab some shiny rocks.
