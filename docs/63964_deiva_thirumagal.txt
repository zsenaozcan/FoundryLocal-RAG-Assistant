Title: Deiva Thirumagal (2011)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: A. L. Vijay
Main Cast: Vikram,  Sara Arjun, Anushka Shetty, Amala Paul, Nassar

Overview: A mentally retarded man fights for custody of his 7-year-old daughter, and in the process teaches his opponent lawyer the value of love and family.
