Title: Betsy's Wedding (1990)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Alan Alda
Main Cast: Alan Alda, Molly Ringwald, Joe Pesci, Ally Sheedy, Samuel L. Jackson
Keywords: debt, wedding, gangster

Overview: Offbeat fashion student Betsy Hopper and her straight-laced investment-banker fiancé, Dylan Walsh, just want an intimate little wedding reception, but Betsy's father, Eddie, a Long Island construction contractor, feels so threatened by Jake's rich WASP parents that he blows the ceremony up into a bank-breaking showpiece, sending his wife, Lola, into a financial panic.
