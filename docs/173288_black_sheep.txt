Title: Black Sheep (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Foreign
Director: Oliver Rihs
Main Cast: Robert Stadlober, Tom Schilling, Jule Böwe, Milan Peschel, Jenny Deimling

Overview: Black Sheep (original title: Schwarze Schafe) is a german/swiss black and white movie. It's separated into five episodes, which tell stories of the Berlin city life. A hopeless impostor. An East German couple who dreams of the big money. Three Turkish teenagers who do everything to have sex. Two hapless Satanists. And gay boys who dream of a better world. An anarchist, humorous black comedy about life in Berlin.
