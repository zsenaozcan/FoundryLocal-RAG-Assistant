Title: Moshe Kasher: Live in Oakland (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jay Chapman
Main Cast: Moshe Kasher
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: Oakland-bred comic and best-selling author Moshe Kasher comes back to the Bay Area in this standup special. Back on his home turf, Kasher finds comedy in these uproarious stories about the people he's met -- and how they see him. Recorded live at the New Parish nightclub in January 2012.
