Title: The Unexpected Love (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jorge Torregrossa
Main Cast: Javier Cámara, Raúl Arévalo, Tammy Blanchard, Carmen Ruiz, Sarah Sokolovic

Overview: Juanito moved to New York to succeed as an actor. Years have gone by but success has not come his way and now he takes an odd jobs to survive. One day he is visited by his apparently successful cousin. However, their life together will discover the reality behind each of them.
