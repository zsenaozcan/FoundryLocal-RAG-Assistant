Title: Patlabor: The Movie (1989)
Genres: Action, Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Mamoru Oshii
Main Cast: Miina Tominaga, Toshio Furukawa, Ryûnosuke Ohbayashi, Yoshiko Sakakibara, Yō Inoue
Keywords: police, mecha, robot

Overview: A mysterious suicide and a series of unmanned robot run-aways sparks off a Police investigation into the suspicious software on-board thousands of industrial robots around Tokyo.
