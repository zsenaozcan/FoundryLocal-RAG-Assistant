Title: Isle of Flowers (1989)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jorge Furtado
Main Cast: Paulo José, Júlia Barth, Luciane Azevedo, Ciça Reckziegel, Igor Costa
Keywords: slum, capitalism, japanese, brazilian, supermarket, hunger, pig, misery, tomato, money

Overview: A tomato is planted, harvested and sold at a supermarket, but it rots and ends up in the trash. Ends? No. Isle of Flowers follows it up until its real end, among animals, trash, women and children. And then the difference between tomatoes, pigs and human beings becomes clear.
