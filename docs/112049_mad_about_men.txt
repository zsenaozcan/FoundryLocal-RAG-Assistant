Title: Mad About Men (1954)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Comedy, Science Fiction, Romance
Director: Ralph Thomas
Main Cast: Glynis Johns, Donald Sinden, Anne Crawford, Margaret Rutherford, Dora Bryan
Keywords: mermaid

Overview: Flirtatious mermaid Miranda swaps places with a schoolteacher who has gone on holiday. All is well until she falls in love with a human.
