Title: A Sister's Revenge (2013)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Mystery
Director: Curtis Crawford
Main Cast: Brooke Burns, Tim Rozon, Ashley Jones, Rebecca Amzallag, Ken Proulx

Overview: A restaurateur has it all - a successful business, a beautiful house and a loving family - until a ravishing femme fatale with stiletto heels and an ax to grind walks into his life.
