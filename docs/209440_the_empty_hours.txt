Title: The Empty Hours (2013)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Aarón Fernández Lesur
Main Cast: Adriana Paz, Amaranta Abril, Bartolo Campos, Eliseo Lara Martínez, Fermín Martínez 

Overview: The second film from Aarón Fernández is a Spain-Mexico-France co-production already screened as part of Films in Progress at the 60th edition of the Festival. On the desolate coast of Veracruz, young Sebastián, 17, has to run his uncle's motel single-handed, renting rooms by the hour. That's how he meets Miranda, a regular customer who goes there to wait for a lover who often arrives late, sparking a fleeting game of seduction between the two.
