Title: Lili's Apron (2004)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance, Foreign
Director: Mariano Galperin
Main Cast: Luis Ziembrowski, Paula Ituriza, Cristina Banegas

Overview: After working eight years as a chef, Ramon (Luis Ziembrowski) is fired from the restaurant. To survive, Lili (Paula Ituriza) leaves the countryside with her family, and Ramon gets a job as a maid, pretending to be a woman.
