Title: Life Is a Trumpet (2015)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Antonio Nuić
Main Cast: Bojan Navojec, Goran Navojec, Iva Babić, Mirela Brekalo, Zlatko Vitez
Keywords: marriage, family

Overview: Life Is a Trumpet has a loose jazz musician as the groom, a butcher as his father, and two families of different backgrounds whose members are not as different as one might expect.
