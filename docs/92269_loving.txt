Title: Loving (1970)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Irvin Kershner
Main Cast: George Segal, Eva Marie Saint, Sterling Hayden, Keenan Wynn, Nancie Phillips
Keywords: illustrator, liquor, girlfriend, transport

Overview: Brooks Wilson is in crisis. He is torn between his wife Selma and two daughters and his mistress Grace, and also between his career as a successful illustrator and his feeling that he might still produce something worthwhile.
