Title: You May Not Kiss the Bride (2011)
Genres: Action, Crime, Comedy, Romance
Director: Rob Hedden
Main Cast: Dave Annable, Katharine McPhee, Rob Schneider, Mena Suvari, Tia Carrere

Overview: An unassuming pet photographer is thrown into serious action, adventure and romance when he's forced to marry a Croatian bride and spend his honeymoon at a remote tropical resort where she is kidnapped.
