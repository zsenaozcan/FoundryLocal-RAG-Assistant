Title: Princess (1969)
Genres: Action, Romance
Director: Herman Wuyts
Main Cast: Franz Marijnen, Herman van Veen, Virginia Mailer, Bob Van der Veken, Fanny Winkler
Keywords: exploitation

Overview: Princess is the title of a photo novel which will be written by Walter and photographed by Mark. A story of sex and violence which has great success. But Mark falls so much in love with his model that he can't tell her apart from Princess...
