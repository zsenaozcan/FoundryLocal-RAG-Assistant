Title: 88 (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Action
Director: April Mullen
Main Cast: Katharine Isabelle, Christopher Lloyd, Kyle Schmid, Jesse McCartney, Michael Ironside
Keywords: diner, woman director

Overview: A young woman comes to in a roadside diner with no idea where she is or how she got there. Split between two timelines, she gets taken on a violent journey as she seeks out the person responsible for her lover's death.
