Title: The Man Who Came Back (2008)
Genres: Action, Drama, Western
Director: Glen Pitre
Main Cast: Eric Braeden, Billy Zane, James Patrick Stuart, George Kennedy, Carol Alt
Keywords: prison escape, perjury, discrimination, post civil war

Overview: Framed for murder and left for dead, a local legend comes back to make the guilty pay as he seeks revenge on those who killed his family, in this traditional Western about one man who stood against injustice.
