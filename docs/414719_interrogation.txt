Title: Interrogation (2016)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Stephen Reynolds
Main Cast: Julia Benson, Adam Copeland, C.J. Perry, Erica Carroll, Patrick Sabongui
Keywords: fbi agent, threat, interrogator

Overview: After the FBI receives a threat that endangers the entire city, an interrogator (Copeland) and an I.T. specialist (Perry) are plunged into a series of mind games with a criminal mastermind, desperately racing against time to uncover the villain's true agenda as they fight to protect thousands of lives. Copeland and Perry deliver a knockout blow in this electrifying thriller that crackles with edge-of-your-seat suspense.
