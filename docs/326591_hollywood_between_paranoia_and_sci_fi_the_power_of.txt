Title: Hollywood between Paranoia and Sci-Fi. The Power of Myth (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Julia Kuperberg
Main Cast: James Cameron, Roland Emmerich, George Lucas, Steven Spielberg, Mark Protosevich
Keywords: science fiction, woman director, sci-fi fan

Overview: Gives insight into the creators mindset and how they culled from real life events to create some of the biggest sci fi films of all time.
