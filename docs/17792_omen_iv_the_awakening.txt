Title: Omen IV: The Awakening (1991)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror, Mystery
Director: Jorge Montesi
Main Cast: Faye Grant, Michael Woods, Michael Lerner, Madison Mason, Ann Hearn

Overview: Damien Thorn is dead, but his prophecy is reborn in a girl named Delia, who is adopted by two attorneys, Gene &amp; Karen York. When Karen realises her baby was born under suspicious circumstances, she hires a private investigator to find Delia's real parents. A series of bizarre accidents occur, and Karen begins to suspect everyone of conspiring her as she unravels the truth about her baby.
