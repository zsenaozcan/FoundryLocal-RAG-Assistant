Title: Hidden (2009)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Pål Øie
Main Cast: Arthur Berning, Knut Morten Brekke, Gabriel August Gjønnes, Agnes Karin Haaskjold, Andreas Haugsbø
Keywords: house, dead mother

Overview: Painful memories arise when Kai Koss goes back to his childhood home after 19 years and inherits his dead mother's house.
