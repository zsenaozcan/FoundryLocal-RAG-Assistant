Title: The Suburbans (1999)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Donal Lardner Ward
Main Cast: Jennifer Love Hewitt, Bridgette Wilson, Craig Bierko, Donal Lardner Ward, Tony Guma
Keywords: tattoo, music, wedding, band, scout

Overview: An 80s one-hit wonder band named The Suburbans reform for a special performance at one of the ex-member's wedding. At the wedding, a young record company talent scout happens to be in the audience and decides to give the now 40-ish performers a comeback push. The film attempts to take a satirical look at the music business of the 90s and compare it to the simpler 80s scene.
