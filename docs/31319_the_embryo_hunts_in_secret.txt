Title: The Embryo Hunts in Secret (1966)
Genres: Drama
Director: Kōji Wakamatsu
Main Cast: Hatsuo Yamaya, Miharu Shima

Overview: A man keeps his girlfriend tied up in his small apartment and tortures her. She is undressed, subjected to various types of bondage, whipped, and tortured with a razor blade. He also brushes her hair, applies make-up on her, and breaks down and cries in the fetal position. In the end the girl gets free and has her revenge
