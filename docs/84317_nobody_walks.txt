Title: Nobody Walks (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ry Russo-Young
Main Cast: Dylan McDermott, John Krasinski, Jane Levy, Olivia Thirlby, Rosemarie DeWitt
Keywords: mumblecore, woman director

Overview: A young woman's arrival sparks a surge of energy into a laid-back, artistic Los Angeles household, forcing the residents to confront their own fears and desires in an intricate dance of lust, denial and deception.
