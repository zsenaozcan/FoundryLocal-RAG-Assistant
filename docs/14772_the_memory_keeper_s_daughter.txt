Title: The Memory Keeper's Daughter (2008)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mick Jackson
Main Cast: Emily Watson, Dermot Mulroney, Gretchen Mol, Krystal Hope Nausbaum, Hugh Thompson
Keywords: down syndrome, twins

Overview: During a blizzard in 1964, Dr. David Henry delivers his son Paul with the help of nurse Caroline. But when Henry realizes his wife is also carrying a girl with Down syndrome, he hands the second child over to Caroline without his wife's knowledge. Henry's fateful decision yields grave consequences for his family over the next 20 years.
