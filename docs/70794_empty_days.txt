Title: Empty Days (2000)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Marion Vernoux
Main Cast: Valeria Bruni Tedeschi, Patrick Dell'Isola, Sergi López, Florence Thomassin
Keywords: woman director

Overview: An executive and a dowdy working-class woman, both unemployed, married and parents meet at a supermarket. They become friends but find it hard to accept that they may be in love with each other.
