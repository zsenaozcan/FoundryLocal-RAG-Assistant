Title: Farewell (1981)
Genres: Drama
Director: Elem Klimov
Main Cast: Stefaniya Stanyuta, Lev Durov, Aleksey Petrenko, Yuri Katin-Yartsev, Maya Bulgakova

Overview: Matyora is a small village on a beautiful island with the same name. The existence of the village is threatened with flooding by the construction of a dam. This is the story of the inhabitants of Matyora and their farewell to their homeland.
