Title: Gravehopping (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jan Cvitkovič
Main Cast: Gregor Bakovič, Brane Grubar, Drago Milinovič, Mojca Fatur, Primož Petkovšek
Keywords: funeral

Overview: Pero is a professional funeral speaker in a small Slovenian town. His unique gift is to make every funeral that extra bit special. Pero just can't help turning his eulogies into witty personal confessions that bring the grieving crowd to tears for all the wrong reasons.
