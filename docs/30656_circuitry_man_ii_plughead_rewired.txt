Title: Circuitry Man II: Plughead Rewired (1994)
Genres: Adventure, Science Fiction
Director: Steven Lovy
Main Cast: Vernon Wells, Deborah Shelton, Jim Metzler, Dennis Christopher, Nicholas Worth

Overview: In the backdrop of Earths polluted future a female FBI agent removes Danner, a pleasure android from an asylum in order to coerce him into helping her hunt down the criminal psychopath Plughead. But Plughead who has tangled with Danner before has his own plans as he is forcing a female scientist to help him manufacture life extending longevity chips which he intends to sell to rich and powerful clients.
