Title: Many Wars Ago (1970)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Francesco Rosi
Main Cast: Alain Cuny, Gian Maria Volonté, Mark Frechette, Giampiero Albertini, Pier Paolo Capponi
Keywords: world war i

Overview: On the Italian/Austrian front during World War I, a disastrous Italian attack upon the Austrian positions leads to a mutiny among the decimated Italian troops.
