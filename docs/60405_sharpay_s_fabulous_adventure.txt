Title: Sharpay's Fabulous Adventure (2011)
Genres: Family, Music
Director: Michael Lembeck
Main Cast: Ashley Tisdale, Austin Butler, Cameron Goodman, Bradley Steven Perry, Lucas Grabeel
Keywords: new york, musical, rivalry, spin off, duringcreditsstinger, broadway show, girl dog relationship

Overview: After a talent scout spots her performing with her dog Boi at a charity gala, Sharpay Evans sets off for the bright lights of NYC, convinced instant fame and fortune are in the bag. But theatre's a dog-eat-dog world. Fortunately, Sharpay also meets Peyton, a handsome student filmmaker who finds Sharpay nearly as fascinating as she finds herself.
