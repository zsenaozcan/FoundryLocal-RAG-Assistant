Title: Near Dark (1987)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Kathryn Bigelow
Main Cast: Adrian Pasdar, Jenny Wright, Lance Henriksen, Bill Paxton, Jenette Goldstein
Keywords: small town, motel, sunlight, vampire, victim, farm, kiss, gang leader, femme fatale, redneck

Overview: A mid-western farm boy reluctantly becomes a member of the undead when a girl he meets turns out to be part of a band of southern vampires who roam the highways in stolen cars.
