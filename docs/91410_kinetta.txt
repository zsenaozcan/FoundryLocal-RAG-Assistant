Title: Kinetta (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yorgos Lanthimos
Main Cast: Aris Servetalis, Evangelia Randou, Costas Xikominos, Youlika Skafida, Ektoras Kaloudis

Overview: At a Greek hotel in the off-season, a chambermaid, a man obsessed with BMW cars, and a photo-store clerk attempt to film and photograph various badly re-enacted struggles between a man and a woman.
