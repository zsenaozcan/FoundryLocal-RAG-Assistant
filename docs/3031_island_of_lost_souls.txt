Title: Island of Lost Souls (1932)
Genres: Adventure, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Erle C. Kenton
Main Cast: Charles Laughton, Richard Arlen, Leila Hyams, Arthur Hohl, Kathleen Burke
Keywords: exotic island, man beast, inhuman creatures

Overview: An obsessed scientist conducts profane experiments in evolution, eventually establishing himself as the self-styled demigod to a race of mutated, half-human abominations.
