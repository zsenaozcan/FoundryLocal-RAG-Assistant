Title: Goliyon Ki Raasleela Ram-Leela (2013)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Sanjay Leela Bhansali
Main Cast: Ranveer Singh, Deepika Padukone, Richa Chadda, Supriya Pathak, Homi Wadia
Keywords: love, based on play or musical, family relationships, death of lover

Overview: Goliyon Ki Raasleela Ram-Leela is an adaptation of Shakespeare's Romeo and Juliet, set in violent times.
