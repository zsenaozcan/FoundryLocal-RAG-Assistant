Title: Devasuram (1993)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: I. V. Sasi
Main Cast: Mohanlal, Revathi, Napoleon, Innocent, Nedumudi Venu
Keywords: dance, group, revenge, murder, classic, carefree, malayalam

Overview: Neelakantan, heir to his father's fortunes, is an apparent menace to society. He once belittles a dancer but later they fall in love. Shekharan, an arch enemy, kidnaps his lover due to an old enmity.
