Title: Life Is a Bed of Roses (1983)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Alain Resnais
Main Cast: Vittorio Gassman, Ruggero Raimondi, Fanny Ardant, Geraldine Chaplin, Sabine Azéma
Keywords: musical

Overview: In this whimsical fable, Resnais deftly interweaves three story lines: the creation of an early-20th-century utopia; romantic high jinks at a school conference; and a fantasy sparked by F/X pioneer Georges Méliès.
