Title: The Finger (2011)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Sergio Teubal
Main Cast: Fabián Vena, Gabriel Goity, Martin Seefeld, Mariana Briski, Mara Santucho

Overview: In this lively Spanish-Argentine comic fantasy, two luckless actors get angry and with the help of a high school drama class jolt a sleepy, rural Argentina town into taking much-needed political action.
