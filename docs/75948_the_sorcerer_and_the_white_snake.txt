Title: The Sorcerer and the White Snake (2011)
Genres: Action, Fantasy
Director: Ching Siu-Tung
Main Cast: Jet Li, Huang Sheng-Yi, Raymond Lam Fung, Wen Zhang, Charlene Choi

Overview: The Sorcerer and the White Snake is an ancient Chinese fable about a woman demon who falls in love with a mortal is brought to life through the latest advances in CGI and action techniques.
