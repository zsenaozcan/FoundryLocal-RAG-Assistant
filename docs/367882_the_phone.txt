Title: The Phone (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Mystery
Director: Kim Bong-joo
Main Cast: Son Hyun-joo, Uhm Ji-won, Bae Seong-woo, Roh Jeong-eui, Jang In-sub

Overview: A magnetic field anomaly allows a man to phone back into the past to his wife, who was murdered years ago. But to save her, he must identify the killer now and warn her until the anomaly disappears.
