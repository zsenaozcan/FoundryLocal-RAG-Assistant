Title: Blame It on Fidel! (2006)
Genres: History, Drama
Director: Julie Gavras
Main Cast: Julie Depardieu, Nina Kervel-Bey, Stefano Accorsi, Flore Vannier-Moreau
Keywords: woman director

Overview: A 9-year-old girl weathers big changes in her household as her parents become radical political activists in 1970-71 Paris.
