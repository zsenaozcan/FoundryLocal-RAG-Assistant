Title: Vic+Flo Saw a Bear (2013)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Denis Côté
Main Cast: Pierrette Robitaille, Romane Bohringer, Marc-André Grondin, Marie Brassard, Georges Molnar
Keywords: bear

Overview: This is the portrait of two recently released prisoners (Pierrette Robitaille and Romane Bohringer) who learn to live in a sugar shack deep in the forest.
