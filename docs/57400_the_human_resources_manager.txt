Title: The Human Resources Manager (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Eran Riklis
Main Cast: Mark Ivanir, Guri Alfi, Noah Silver, Rozina Cambos, Julian Negulesco

Overview: A tragic comedy centered on the HR manager of Israel's largest industrial bakery, who sets out to save the reputation of his business and prevent the publication of a defamatory article.
