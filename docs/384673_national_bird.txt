Title: National Bird (2017)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Sonia Kennebeck
Main Cast: Jesselyn Radack
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Sonia Kennebeck takes on the controversial tactic of drone warfare, and demands accountability through the personal accounts—recollections, traumas, and responses—of three American military veterans whose lives have been shaken by the roles they played in this controversial method of attack.
