Title: Seconds Apart (2011)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Antonio Negret
Main Cast: Edmund Entin, Gary Entin, Samantha Droke, Louis Herthum, Morgana Shaw
Keywords: brother brother relationship, hallucination, telekinesis, high school, twin brother, murder, russian roulette, pedophile, throat slitting, interrogation

Overview: Seth and Jonah are twins with a dangerous ability: telekinesis. Things start to spiral out of control as their classmates die in twisted and bizarre ways. Jealousy begins to divide them and soon they can no longer trust each other, leading up to a horrific battle against themselves.
