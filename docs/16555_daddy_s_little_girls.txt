Title: Daddy's Little Girls (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Tyler Perry
Main Cast: Gabrielle Union, Idris Elba, Louis Gossett, Jr., Tasha Smith, Tracee Ellis Ross
Keywords: atlanta, garage, drug dealer, lawyer, single father

Overview: Monty is a mechanic struggling to make ends meet as he raises his three young daughters. When the court awards custody of his daughters to his shady ex-wife, Monty desperately tries to win them back with the help of Julia, a beautiful, Ivy League-educated attorney. Monty and Julia couldn't be less alike, but a flame is ignited...touching off a firestorm of love and conflict.
