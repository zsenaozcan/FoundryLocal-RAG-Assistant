Title: My Way (2012)
Genres: Drama
Director: Florent-Emilio Siri
Main Cast: Jérémie Renier, Benoît Magimel, Monica Scattini, Sabrina Seyvecou, Ana Girardot
Keywords: musical, biography

Overview: A biopic of French pop star Claude Francois, most famous for co-writing the song 'My Way'. Tracing his life from his childhood in Egypt through his success in France to his untimely death in Paris in 1978.
