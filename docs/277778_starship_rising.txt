Title: Starship Rising (2014)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Neil Johnson
Main Cast: Darren Jacobs, Emii, Brooke Lewis, Claudia Wells, E.J. De la Pena
Keywords: starships

Overview: A corrupt planetary federation…  The ultimate weapon of destruction....  One starship captain stands between them….and intergalactic armageddon.  STARSHIP: RISING - RISE UP OR DIE  In the distant future, an immortal bionic leader, the product of genetic engineering, has ruled over the universe for 200 years. An uprising is being staged by those preserving natural birth.
