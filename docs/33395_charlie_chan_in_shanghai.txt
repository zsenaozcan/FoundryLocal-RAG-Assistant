Title: Charlie Chan in Shanghai (1935)
Genres: Crime, Comedy, Mystery, Thriller
Director: James Tinling
Main Cast: Warner Oland, Irene Hervey, Jon Hall, Russell Hicks, Keye Luke
Keywords: charlie chan

Overview: When a prominent official is murdered at a banquet honoring Charle Chan, the detective and son Lee team up to expose an opium-smuggling ring.
