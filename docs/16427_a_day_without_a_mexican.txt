Title: A Day Without a Mexican (2004)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Sergio Arau
Main Cast: Melinda Allen, Frankie J. Allison, Caroline Aaron, Tony Abatemarco, Yeniffer Behrens
Keywords: immigration, immigrant, racial segregation, foreign worker, racist, independent film, prejudice, racism, latino, mexican

Overview: One morning, California wakes up to find that one-third of its population -- the Hispanic third -- has disappeared. A strange pink fog envelops the state, and communication outside its boundaries is completely cut off. The economic, political and social implications of this disaster threaten California's way of life, and for a group of disparate people (all white, except for one Latina), the cracks in their private lives are forced wide open.
