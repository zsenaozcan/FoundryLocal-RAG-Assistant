Title: The Young Offenders (2016)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Peter Foott
Main Cast: Alex Murphy, Chris Walley, Hilary Rose, Dominic MacHale, Ciaran Bermingham
Keywords: hechos reales, drogas

Overview: Two teenage boys cycle 160 km on stolen bikes pursued by police to find a missing bale of cocaine worth 7 million euro. Set around the real event of Ireland's biggest cocaine seizure in 2007 of 440 million euro.
