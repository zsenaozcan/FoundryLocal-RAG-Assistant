Title: Expecting Love (2008)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Lukasz Karwowski
Main Cast: Joshua Leonard, Agnieszka Grochowska, Mikolaj Grabowski, Agnieszka Pilaszewska, Michael Dunn

Overview: A successful Los Angeles lawyer discovers that a short-term affair could have long-term consequences when a woman he had a brief affair with claims that she's currently pregnant with his unborn child.
