Title: Taste of Life (1988)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Enrico Oldoini
Main Cast: Alberto Sordi, Bernard Blier, Andréa Ferréol, Vittorio Caprioli

Overview: Elvio Battistini works as a ticket taker in the local cinema of the small town where he lives. He has a prosthesis in one leg, the result of an accident, but he tells anyone who will listen it is a war injury. Giuseppe Mondardini, is also an older man living alone in what was the splendid family home. When Elvio and Giuseppe meet, they hit it off immediately. A trip to a cooler area is in order, so Mordardini unveils his ancient automobile.
