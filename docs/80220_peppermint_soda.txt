Title: Peppermint Soda (1977)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Diane Kurys
Main Cast: Eléonore Klarwein, Odile Michel, Yves Rénier, Anouk Ferjac, Jacqueline Boyer
Keywords: paris, sister sister relationship, friendship, mother daughter relationship, school, father daughter relationship, woman director

Overview: In the fall of 1963, Anne is becoming a teenager. She lives in Paris with her mother and her older sister, Frédérique. They're just back from summer at the beach with their father. School starts. A turbulent year awaits them both.
