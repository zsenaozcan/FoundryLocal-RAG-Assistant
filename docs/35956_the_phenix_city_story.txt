Title: The Phenix City Story (1955)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Phil Karlson
Main Cast: John McIntire, Richard Kiley, Kathryn Grant, Edward Andrews, Lenka Peterson
Keywords: gambling, alabama, docudrama, film noir

Overview: Phil Karlson's docudrama based on the notorious Phenix City -- a city known for its gambling, crime and prostitution rackets.
