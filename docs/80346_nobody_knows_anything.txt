Title: Nobody Knows Anything! (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: William Tannen
Main Cast: Alanna Ubach, Michael Lerner, Mike Myers, Janeane Garofalo, Kristen Johnston
Keywords: robbery, mayor, prison guard, independent film, blue collar, lawyer, hollywood, los angeles, grocery store

Overview: An aspiring filmmaker learns that success in Hollywood doesn't come as easy as she suspected as she attempts to discover the formula to success. When the guidance of her helpful has-been uncle fails to pave the way, Sarah Wilder must seek the advice of such Hollywood heavies as Mike Meyers, Ben Stiller, and Fred Willard -- only to discover that the old adage is true and Nobody Knows Anything about how to succeed in the cutthroat world of Los Angeles.
