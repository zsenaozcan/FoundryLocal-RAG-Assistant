Title: My Wife Is an Actress (2001)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Romance
Director: Yvan Attal
Main Cast: Charlotte Gainsbourg, Yvan Attal, Terence Stamp, Noémie Lvovsky, Ludivine Sagnier

Overview: My Wife is an Actress is a French Romantic Comedy/Drama film starring real life couple Yvan Attal and Charlotte Gainsbourg. Attal plays a journalist who becomes obsessively jealous when his actress wife gets a part in a movie with an attractive co-star. Attal also wrote and directed the film.
