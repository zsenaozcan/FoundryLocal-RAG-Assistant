Title: Pitfall (1962)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Crime, Science Fiction, Foreign
Director: Hiroshi Teshigahara
Main Cast: Hisashi Igawa, Sumie Sasaki, Sen Yano, Hideo Kanze, Kunie Tanaka
Keywords: surreal, japanese new wave

Overview: A man wanders into a seemingly deserted town with his young son in search of work. But after a bit of bad luck, he joins the town's population of lost souls.
