Title: Keep Your Distance (2005)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Romance
Director: Stu Pollard
Main Cast: Gil Bellows, Jennifer Westfeldt, Christian Kane, Kim Raver, Jamie Harrold
Keywords: suspense

Overview: In the perfectly normal town of Louisville, KY, there is a perfectly normal man living the perfect life. David Dailey is a man who has it all: A great career, a community that adores him, and an enduring marriage. But beneath the surface, David's idyllic world is crumbling. He's haunted by a series of mysterious notes that warn of an imminent fall from grace...
