Title: September Vacation (1979)
Genres: Drama
Director: Vitali Melnikov
Main Cast: Oleg Dal, Yuri Bogatyryov, Irina Kupchenko, Natalya Gundareva, Nikolay Burlyaev

Overview: Engineer Zilov, having woken up, finds the funeral wreath at his place – his friends' present. This rather symbolic joke makes him recollect the previous night as well as 2 last months...
