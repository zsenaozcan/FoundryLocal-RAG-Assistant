Title: Phantom Lady (1944)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Robert Siodmak
Main Cast: Franchot Tone, Ella Raines, Alan Curtis, Aurora Miranda, Thomas Gomez
Keywords: falsely accused, classic noir, film noir

Overview: A mystery woman is a murder suspect's only alibi for the night of his wife's death. Based on a story by noir scribe Cornell Woolrich.
