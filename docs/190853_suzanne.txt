Title: Suzanne (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Katell Quillévéré
Main Cast: Sara Forestier, Adèle Haenel, François Damiens, Paul Hamy, Anne Le Ny
Keywords: family, woman director

Overview: The story of a destiny. Suzanne's and her family's. The ties that bind them, keep them together, and the love she pursues... to the point of leaving everything behind.
