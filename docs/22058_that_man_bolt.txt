Title: That Man Bolt (1973)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Henry Levin
Main Cast: Fred Williamson, Byron Webster, Miko Mayama, Teresa Graves, Masatoshi Nakamura
Keywords: martial arts, revenge, blaxploitation

Overview: Fred Williamson chop-sockeys his way through this popular blaxploitation adventure as Jefferson Bolt, a Kung Fu expert assigned to deliver a cool $1 million to Mexico City from Hong Kong with a stop in Los Angeles. When Bolt discovers the cash is dirty mob money and his gal has been killed, he heads back to the Far East to get even.
