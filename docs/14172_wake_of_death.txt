Title: Wake of Death (2004)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Thriller
Director: Philippe Martinez
Main Cast: Jean-Claude Van Damme, Simon Yam, Valerie Tian, Burt Kwouk, Philip Tan
Keywords: revenge

Overview: Action superstar, Jean-Claude Van Damme (Universal Soldier, Street Fighter), is back and at his hard-hitting best as Ben Archer, an ex-mob enforcer seeking revenge against a ruthless Chinese kingpin responsible for his wife's brutal murder. When Archer joins forces with his old underworld friends, an all-out war is waged against the Chinese Triad in this pulse-pounding action thriller.
