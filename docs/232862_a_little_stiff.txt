Title: A Little Stiff (1991)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Caveh Zahedi
Main Cast: Caveh Zahedi, Caveh Zahedi, Patrick Park
Keywords: independent film

Overview: A Little Stiff is a 1991 minimalist comedy directed by Caveh Zahedi and Greg Watkins based on true events and re-enacted by the actual participants. Caveh Zahedi plays himself as a neurotic film student who develops a crush on art student Erin McKim after a brief encounter in an elevator.
