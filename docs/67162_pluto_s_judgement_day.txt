Title: Pluto's Judgement Day (1935)
Genres: Animation
Director: David Hand
Main Cast: Walt Disney, Pinto Colvig, Billy Bletcher, Clarence Nash
Keywords: mickey mouse, disney short, pluto

Overview: Pluto chases a kitten through a window and right into Mickey's lap. Mickey scolds him, and goes off to wash the kitten. Pluto falls asleep in front of the fire, and dreams of a hell ruled by cats where he is put on trial for all his crimes against cats and, of course, found guilty.
