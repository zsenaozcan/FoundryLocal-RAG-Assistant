Title: Late Bloomers (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Bettina Oberli
Main Cast: Annemarie Düringer, Heidi Maria Glössner, Monica Gubser, Lilian Naef, Monika Niggeler
Keywords: underwear, lingerie, fashion, woman director

Overview: Centers around four older ladies from the Emmental region. When four older women decide to turn the local corner shop into a chic lingerie store, the whole community is thrown into disarray.
