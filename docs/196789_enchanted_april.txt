Title: Enchanted April (1935)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Harry Beaumont
Main Cast: Ann Harding, Frank Morgan, Katharine Alexander, Reginald Owen, Jane Baxter

Overview: Mrs. Lotty Wilkins is an unhappily wife whom's life husband and romance have departed. In order to possibly salvage some of the missing elements in her life she rents an old Italian mansion and sharing it with three women. Here the four women plan to spend the month of April away from the cares of home, husbands and the everyday monotony.
