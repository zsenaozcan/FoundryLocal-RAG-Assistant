Title: Sarah Colonna: I Can't Feel My Legs (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jay Karas
Main Cast: Sarah Colonna
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: New York Times Best Seller and "Chelsea Lately" regular, Sarah Colonna, has some stories to tell and she doesn't care what you think. From how to get out of jury duty to online dating, Sarah does not disappoint in this new special.
