Title: Crossroads (1986)
Genres: Drama, Music, Mystery, Romance
Director: Walter Hill
Main Cast: Ralph Macchio, Joe Seneca, Jami Gertz, Joe Morton, Robert Judd
Keywords: harmonica, blues, guitar duel

Overview: Cult classic starring Ralph Macchio as a want-to-be blues guitar virtuoso who seeks a long-lost song by legendary musician Robert Johnson.
