Title: Maktub (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Family
Director: Paco Arango
Main Cast: Diego Peretti, Aitana Sánchez-Gijón, Goya Toledo, Rosa Maria Sardà, Amparo Baró

Overview: The title refers to those strange coincidences that happen to us sometimes and that seem inevitable, like a product of destiny. Inspired by the experience of a Canarian child victim of cancer, the film is conceived as a Christmas story.
