Title: Murder on a Sunday Morning (2001)
Genres: Crime, Documentary
Director: Jean-Xavier de Lestrade
Main Cast: Ann Finnell, Patrick McGuinness, James Williams, Michael Glover, Dwayne Darnell
Keywords: florida, murder, trial, police corruption, courtroom, true crime, murder trial, racial injustice

Overview: 2001 French documentary about the murder trial of a 15 year old black teen accused of murder in Jacksonville, Florida. Winner of 2002 Academy Award for Best Documentary.
