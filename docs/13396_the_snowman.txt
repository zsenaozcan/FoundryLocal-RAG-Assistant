Title: The Snowman (1982)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Animation, Family, Fantasy
Director: Jimmy T. Murakami
Main Cast: David Bowie, Raymond Briggs, Mel Smith
Keywords: holiday, woman director, christmas

Overview: A young boy makes a snowman one Christmas Eve, which comes to life at midnight and takes the boy on a magical adventure to the North Pole to meet Santa Claus.
