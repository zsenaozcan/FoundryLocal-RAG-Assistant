Title: Doghouse (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Jake West
Main Cast: Stephen Graham, Danny Dyer, Noel Clarke, Emil Marwa, Lee Ingleby
Keywords: zombie, virus, drinking weekend

Overview: Six men with mid-life anxiety set out for a weekend in the country in an attempt to reconnect with their masculinity. What they find is a catastrophe so horrible and bizarre that a mid-life crisis turns out to be exactly what they need to survive it...
