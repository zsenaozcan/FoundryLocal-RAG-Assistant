Title: The Wonderful Ice Cream Suit (1998)
Genres: Comedy, Fantasy, Family
Director: Stuart Gordon
Main Cast: Edward James Olmos, Joe Mantegna, Esai Morales, Clifton Collins Jr

Overview: Gomez is a middle-aged man who dreams of buying a gorgeous white suit in a nearby store, but doesn't have enough cash. He finds 4 more people of same size, who each give $20 and get to wear the suit for an hour each in return. But the suit is not just a suit - it makes wishes of the one wearing it come true.
