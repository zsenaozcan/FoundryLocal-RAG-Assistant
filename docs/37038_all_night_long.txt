Title: All Night Long (1962)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Basil Dearden
Main Cast: Patrick McGoohan, Keith Michell, Betsy Blair, Paul Harris, Marti Stevens
Keywords: jazz

Overview: Richard Attenborough, Patrick McGoohan and a host of jazz legends including Charles Mingus and Dave Brubeck star in this 1960s melodrama, inspired by Shakespeare's 'Othello'.
