Title: The Heroic Ones (1970)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Chang Cheh
Main Cast: David Chiang, Ti Lung, Ku Feng, Chan Sing, Chin Han
Keywords: mughal empire

Overview: A Mogul king decides to take stealthy action to help overpower his greatest rivals. He chooses nine out thirteen of his loyal generals (who he treats as sons) to embark on the mission. However, jealously amongst them sparks a treacherous family feud that could lead to catastrophic consequences for all involved.
