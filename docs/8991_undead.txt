Title: Undead (2003)
Genres: Horror, Action, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Michael Spierig
Main Cast: Felicity Mason, Mungo McKay, Rob Jenkins, Lisa Cunningham, Dirk Hunter
Keywords: comet, zombie

Overview: A quaint Australian fishing village is overcome by meteorites that turn its residents into the ravenous undead, leaving a small group of those unharmed to find a way out.
