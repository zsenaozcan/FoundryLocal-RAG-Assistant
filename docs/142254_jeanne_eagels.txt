Title: Jeanne Eagels (1957)
Genres: Drama
Director: George Sidney
Main Cast: Kim Novak, Jeff Chandler, Agnes Moorehead, Charles Drake, Larry Gates

Overview: Director George Sidney's 1957 film about the life of celebrated stage actress Jeanne Eagels stars Kim Novak, Jeff Chandler, Agnes Moorehead, Gene Lockhart, Virginia Grey, Charles Drake, Larry Gates, Joe DeSantis, Murray Hamilton, Will Wright, Snub Pollard, Doris Lloyd and Michael Dante.
