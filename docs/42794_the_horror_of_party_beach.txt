Title: The Horror of Party Beach (1964)
Genres: Horror, Music
Director: Del Tenney
Main Cast: John Scott, Alice Lyon, Allan Laurel, Eulabelle Moore
Keywords: beach, monster, slumber party

Overview: Radioactive waste dumped off the coastline creates mutant monsters. The beasts attack slumber parties, beaches, tourists, and terrorize a waterfront community as a scientist, his daughter, her boyfriend and the local police try to find a way to stop them.
