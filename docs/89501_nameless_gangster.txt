Title: Nameless Gangster (2012)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Yoon Jong-bin
Main Cast: Choi Min-sik, Kwak Do-won, Ha Jung-woo, Ma Dong-seok, Kim Sung-kyun
Keywords: police, cop, gangster, crime, south korea, decomposition, 1980s, bad guy

Overview: A corrupted customs officer, Choi Ik-hyeon, faces losing his job. Then, his life turns around as he meets ganster Choi Hyeong-bae who has connection with the Yakuza. They quickly form a partnetship; Hyeong-bae helps Ik-hyeon set up a business with the money from drug trafficking while Ik-hyeon lobbies for his partner Hyeong-bae. As the government declares war on crime, however, their partnership begins to crack.
