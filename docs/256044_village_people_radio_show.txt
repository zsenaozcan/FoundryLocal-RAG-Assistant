Title: Village People Radio Show (2007)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Amir Muhammad
Main Cast: C.D. Abdullah, Suriani Abdullah, Bront Palarae
Keywords: shakespeare, based on play or musical

Overview: A portrait of village life in South Thailand, home of retired Malay-Muslim members of the communist party of neighboring Malaysia. Recollections of the decades-long guerrilla war are interspersed with a Thai radio soap opera.
