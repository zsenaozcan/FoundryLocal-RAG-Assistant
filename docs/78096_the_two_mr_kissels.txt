Title: The Two Mr. Kissels (2008)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Ed Bianchi
Main Cast: John Stamos, Anson Mount, Robin Tunney, Gretchen Egolf, Chuck Shamata

Overview: Drama based on a true story. Rich, high-flying brothers Robert and Andrew Kissel seemingly have everything: beautiful wives who love them, great jobs and huge houses. But beneath the surface lie resentments and secrets that will eventually be their horrible undoing
