Title: Felix and Meira (2015)
Genres: Drama, Family, Romance
Director: Maxime Giroux
Main Cast: Martin Dubreuil, Hadas Yaron, Luzer Twersky, Anne-Élisabeth Bossé, Benoît Girard
Keywords: propaganda, umerziehung

Overview: A young married woman from Montreal's Orthodox Jewish community finds freedom from the strictures of her faith through her relationship with a young man who is mourning the death of his estranged father.
