Title: The Long Voyage Home (1940)
Genres: Action, Drama, War
Director: John Ford
Main Cast: John Wayne, Thomas Mitchell, Ian Hunter, Barry Fitzgerald, Wilfrid Lawson
Keywords: combat, machine gun, freighter

Overview: The crew of the merchant ship Glencairn hope to survive a transatlantic crossing during World War II. Adapted from four Eugene O'Neill one-act plays.
