Title: Material Girls (2006)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Martha Coolidge
Main Cast: Hilary Duff, Haylie Duff, María Conchita Alonso, Anjelica Huston, Lukas Haas
Keywords: sister sister relationship, wealth, family, woman director, riches to rags

Overview: Two wealthy sisters, both heiresses to their family's cosmetics fortune, are given a wake-up call when a scandal and ensuing investigation strip them of their wealth.
