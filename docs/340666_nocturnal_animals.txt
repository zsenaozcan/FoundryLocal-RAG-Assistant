Title: Nocturnal Animals (2016)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Tom Ford
Main Cast: Amy Adams, Jake Gyllenhaal, Michael Shannon, Aaron Taylor-Johnson, Isla Fisher
Keywords: based on novel, hostage, cancer, mother daughter relationship, writer, overbearing mother, art, novelist, carjacking, story within the story

Overview: Susan Morrow receives a book manuscript from her ex-husband – a man she left 20 years earlier – asking for her opinion of his writing. As she reads, she is drawn into the fictional life of Tony Hastings, a mathematics professor whose family vacation turns violent.
