Title: The Bride (1985)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Romance
Director: Franc Roddam
Main Cast: Sting, Jennifer Beals, Anthony Higgins, Clancy Brown, David Rappaport
Keywords: frankenstein

Overview: Sting is Doctor Frankenstein in this remake of the old classic film Bride of Frankenstein. After years of research, the doctor finally succeeds in creating the perfect woman, who gets the name "Eva".
