Title: I'll Take Sweden (1965)
Genres: Comedy, Music, Romance
Director: Frederick de Cordova
Main Cast: Bob Hope, Tuesday Weld, Frankie Avalon, Dina Merrill, Jeremy Slate
Keywords: sweden

Overview: Bob Holcomb will do anything to stop his daughter JoJo from tying the knot with her lazy boyfriend, even move her all the way to Sweden! But once they're "safely" out of the country, JoJo falls for a sly Swedish playboy.
