Title: The Grey Zone (2001)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Tim Blake Nelson
Main Cast: David Arquette, Daniel Benzali, Steve Buscemi, Harvey Keitel, Natasha Lyonne
Keywords: holocaust, auschwitz, uprising, doctor

Overview: A Nazi doctor, along with the Sonderkomando Jews – who are forced to work in the crematoria of Auschwitz against their fellow-Jews – find themselves in a moral grey zone.
