Title: The Sensation Of Sight (2006)
Genres: Drama, Family
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Ian Somerhalder, David Strathairn, Cassidy Hinkle
Keywords: independent film

Overview: This off-beat drama about man's search for meaning amidst the ache of despair chronicles Finn, an introspective English teacher entering a mid-life crisis impelled by a recent tragedy, as he sets afoot selling encyclopedias to the town locals.
