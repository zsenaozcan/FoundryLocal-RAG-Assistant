Title: Ping Pong Playa (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jessica Yu
Main Cast: Jimmy Tsai, Andrew Vo, Khary Payton, Peter Paige, Scott Lowell
Keywords: sport, independent film, woman director

Overview: Streetwise swaggering Christopher "C-Dub" Wang is a suburban guy who waxes political on all things Asian American and clings to pro basketball pipe dreams. But when misfortune strikes his family, C-dub must overcome living at home, working a dead-end job and his worldly older brother, to run his Mom's ping pong classes and defend the family's athletic dynasty.
