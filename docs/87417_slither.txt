Title: Slither (1973)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy
Director: Howard Zieff
Main Cast: James Caan, Peter Boyle, Sally Kellerman, Louise Lasser, Allen Garfield
Keywords: heist, money

Overview: While searching for a small fortune of embezzled money, an ex-con, a small-time bandleader, his doting wife and a kooky drifter find themselves being followed. Their chase takes them to trailer camps, bingo halls, laundromats and ultimately, a showdown with a group of unconventional bad guys.
