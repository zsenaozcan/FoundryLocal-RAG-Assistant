Title: Cowboy Canteen (1944)
Genres: Music, War, Western
Director: Lew Landers
Main Cast: Charles Starrett, Jane Frazee, Barbara Jo Allen, Tex Ritter, Roy Acuff and His Smoky Mountain Boys and Girls
Keywords: world war ii, widow, whistling, white horse, wooden dummy

Overview: Song and comedy revue, featuring Western talents, along with a theatrical troupe taking their vacation on the Lazy B Ranch run by Steve Bradley. Steve is about to enter the army and he and Tex Coulter compete for the love of Connie Grey.
