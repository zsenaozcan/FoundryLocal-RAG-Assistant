Title: 150 Milligrams (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Emmanuelle Bercot
Main Cast: Sidse Babett Knudsen, Benoît Magimel, Isabelle De Hertogh, Charlotte Laemmel, Lara Neumann
Keywords: woman director

Overview: In the hospital where she works in Brest, France, a lung specialist discovers a direct link between suspicious deaths and state-approved medicine. She fights single-handedly for the truth to come out.
