Title: Ong-Bak: The Thai Warrior (2003)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Prachya Pinkaew
Main Cast: Tony Jaa, Petchtai Wongkamlao, Pumwaree Yodkamol, Suchao Pongwilai, Chumphorn Thepphithak
Keywords: martial arts, thailand, scuplture, fighter, fate

Overview: When the head of a statue sacred to a village is stolen, a young martial artist goes to the big city and finds himself taking on the underworld to retrieve it.
