Title: The Real McCoy (1999)
Genres: Music, Documentary
Director: Pekka Lehto
Main Cast: Andy McCoy, Angela Nicoletti, Michael Monroe
Keywords: biography

Overview: Rock musician Andy McCoy, formerly of Hanoi Rocks, takes us on a "trip" through his mind, memories and imagination. Documentaries, real life and Mr. McCoy's acid mind intertwine to form an interesting experience.
