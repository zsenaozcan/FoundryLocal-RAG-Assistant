Title: Traveller (1997)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Jack Green
Main Cast: Bill Paxton, Julianna Margulies, James Gammon, Nikki DeLoach, Luke Askew
Keywords: independent film, grifter, road movie, scam artist, irish traveller

Overview: A young man, Pat, visits the clan of gypsy-like grifters (Irish Travellers) in rural North Carolina from whom he is descended. He is at first rejected, but cousin Bokky takes him on as an apprentice. Pat learns the game while Bokky falls in love and desires a different life. Written by Jeff Hole
