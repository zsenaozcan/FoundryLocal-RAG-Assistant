Title: Five (1951)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Arch Oboler
Main Cast: William Phipps, Susan Douglas, James Anderson, Charles Lampkin, Earl Lee
Keywords: dystopia

Overview: The world is destroyed in a nuclear holocaust. Only five Americans survive, including a pregnant woman, a neo-Nazi, a black man and a bank clerk. The five fight each other, fall in love, and act really depressed a lot.
