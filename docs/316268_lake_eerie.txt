Title: Lake Eerie (2016)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Chris Majors
Main Cast: Lance Henriksen, Marilyn Ghigliotti, Betsy Baker, Al Snow, Meredith Majors

Overview: A young widow moves into an old house on Lake Erie to recover from the sudden loss of her husband; however, she soon discovers a dark secret and that she is not alone.
