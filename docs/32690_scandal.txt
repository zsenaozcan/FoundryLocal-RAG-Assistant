Title: Scandal (1950)
Genres: Drama
Director: Akira Kurosawa
Main Cast: Toshirō Mifune, Takashi Shimura, Shirley Yamaguchi, Yôko Katsuragi, Noriko Sengoku
Keywords: painter, inn, tabloid

Overview: Akira Kurosawa directed this drama about a paparazzi photo that a tabloid magazine spins into a scandalous story and soon sparks a court case.
