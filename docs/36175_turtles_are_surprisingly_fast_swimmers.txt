Title: Turtles Are Surprisingly Fast Swimmers (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Satoshi Miki
Main Cast: Juri Ueno, Yu Aoi, Ryo Iwamatsu, Eri Fuse, Jun Kaname

Overview: Suzume Katagura is an bored housewife, she spends her days doing chores and taking care of her husbands pet turtle. One day she sees an wanted ad for spies, hoping for some excitement she decides to give them a call.
