Title: Alphaville (1965)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction, Mystery
Director: Jean-Luc Godard
Main Cast: Eddie Constantine, Anna Karina, Akim Tamiroff, Howard Vernon, Christa Lang
Keywords: dystopia, surrealism, lemmy caution, nouvelle vague, private detective, french noir

Overview: An American private-eye, arrives in Alphaville, a futuristic city on another planet which is ruled by an evil scientist named Von Braun, who has outlawed love and self-expression.
