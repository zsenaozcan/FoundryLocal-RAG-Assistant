Title: Land of Storms (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ádám Császi
Main Cast: András Sütö, Ádám Varga, Lajos Ottó Horváth, Enikö Börcsök, Zita Téby
Keywords: homophobia, homosexuality, gay relationship, threesome

Overview: Szabolcs plays in a German football team, as does Bernard. They are roommates, best friends, inseparable. A lost match makes him reconsider his life and he goes back to Hungary in hope for more simplicity. Yet his solitude does not last long. Soon after his arrival he meets Áron and a mutual attraction between the two boys develops when suddenly Szabolcs receives an unexpected phone call from Bernard: he has arrived to Hungary...
