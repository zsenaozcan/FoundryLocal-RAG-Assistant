Title: Pagan Love Song (1950)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance
Director: Robert Alton
Main Cast: Esther Williams, Howard Keel, Minna Gombell, Charles Mauu, Rita Moreno
Keywords: musical, tahiti

Overview: Island girl Mimi (Esther Williams) plans to leave Tahiti, but maybe she'll have a reason to stay when Mr. Endicott (Howard Keel) arrives. Gorgeous, frothy fun with pristine Hawaiian locations filling in for Tahiti.
