Title: Independents' Day (2016)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction
Director: Laura Beth Love
Main Cast: Fay Gauthier, Sal Landi, William Castrogiovanni, Jacquelin Arroyo, Brian Tyler Cohen
Keywords: spaceship, aerial combat, cgi, alien invasion, woman director, mockbuster

Overview: Aliens invade, this time delivering a clear ultimatum. The fate of humanity hangs in the balance as the U.S. President and citizens decide if these aliens are to be trusted ...or feared.
