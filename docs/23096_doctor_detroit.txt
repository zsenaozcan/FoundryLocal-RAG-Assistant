Title: Doctor Detroit (1983)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michael Pressman
Main Cast: Dan Aykroyd, Howard Hesseman, Donna Dixon, Lydia Lei, T. K. Carter
Keywords: pimp, college professor

Overview: A shy but gentle man named Clifford Skridlow is a professor of comparative literature at the financially-strapped fictional Monroe College in Chicago. A chance encounter with four beautiful women at a restaurant changes his life forever.
