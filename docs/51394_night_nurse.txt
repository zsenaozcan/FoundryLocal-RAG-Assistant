Title: Night Nurse (1931)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: Barbara Stanwyck, Ben Lyon, Joan Blondell, Clark Gable, Blanche Friderici
Keywords: nurse, chauffeur, bootlegger, suspense, school, hospital, pre-code

Overview: Lora Hart manages to land a job in a hospital as a trainee nurse. Upon completion of her training she goes to work as a night nurse for two small children who seem to be very sick, but something much more sinister is going on.
