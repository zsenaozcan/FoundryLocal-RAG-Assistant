Title: American Soldiers: A Day in Iraq (2005)
Genres: Drama, Action, History, Thriller, War
Director: Sidney J. Furie
Main Cast: Curtis Morgan, Zan Calabretta, Jordan Brown, Eddie Della Siepe

Overview: Iraq, 2004: during a routine sortie a US patrol is ambushed and the young soldiers are forced to put their training and skills into action fast. A determined foe with superior local knowledge, the Fedayeen insurgents soon draw them into close quarter combat and a desperate fight for survival.
