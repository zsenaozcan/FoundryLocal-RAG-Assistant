Title: Hide Your Smiling Faces (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Daniel Patrick Carbone
Main Cast: Ryan Jones, Nathan Varnson, Colm O'Leary, Thomas Cruz, Christina Starbuck
Keywords: friendship

Overview: Tommy and his older brother Eric live in the midst of vast remote forests. The death of their friend pushes them close to the edge. Eric doesn’t know how to channel his energy. All at once, nature’s vastness feels stifling.
