Title: King of Texas (2002)
Genres: Western, Drama
Director: Uli Edel
Main Cast: Patrick Stewart, Marcia Gay Harden, Lauren Holly, Roy Scheider, David Alan Grier
Keywords: shakespeare, texas, king lear

Overview: In this re-imagining of Shakespear's King Lear, Patrick Steward stars as John Lear, a Texas cattle baron, who, after dividing his wealth among his three daughters, is rejected by them.
