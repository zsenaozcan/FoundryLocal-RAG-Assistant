Title: The Devil and Daniel Johnston (2005)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jeff Feuerzeig
Main Cast: Daniel Johnston
Keywords: west virginia, independent film, archive footage, infatuation  

Overview: This 2006 documentary film chronicles the life of Daniel Johnston, a manic-depressive genius singer/songwriter/artist, from childhood up to the present, with an emphasis on his mental illness, and how it manifested itself in demonic self-obsession.
