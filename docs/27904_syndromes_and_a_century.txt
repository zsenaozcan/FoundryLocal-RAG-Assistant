Title: Syndromes and a Century (2006)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Apichatpong Weerasethakul
Main Cast: Arkanae Cherkam, Jaruchai Iamaram, Sakda Kaewbuadee, Nantarat Sawaddikul, Sophon Pukanok
Keywords: lovers, love

Overview: A Buddhist meditation on the mysteries of love and attraction, the workings of memory, and the ways in which happiness is triggered.
