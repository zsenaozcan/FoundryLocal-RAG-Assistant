Title: Kareem: Minority of One (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Aaron Cohen
Main Cast: Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Magic Johnson, Liev Schreiber, Billy Crystal, Quincy Jones
Keywords: biography, basketball

Overview: This documentary charts the complexity and genius of the NBA's all-time leading scorer Kareem Abdul-Jabbar's legendary career, both on and off the court. Spotlighting a six-time MVP and six-time world champion, the film examines his controversial and landmark moments, his outspoken feelings about race and politics, and the evolution of the game.
