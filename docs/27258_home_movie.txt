Title: Home Movie (2008)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Christopher Denham
Main Cast: Adrian Pasdar, Cady McClain, Amber Joy Williams, Austin Williams
Keywords: new york, children, family, found footage, home movie, disturbed child, killer child

Overview: Reverend David Poe and his psychiatrist wife trade hectic New York life for an idyllic rural farmhouse; the perfect place for 10 year old twins Jack &amp; Emily to run, play and imagine. Documenting this lifestyle change, David decides to film every holiday and special family event. To the Poe's horror their home movies reveal an increasing malice and evil within their children and by Valentines Day, the children's sadism has turned to a blood lust for human prey.
