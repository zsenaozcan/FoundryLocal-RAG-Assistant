Title: First Comes Courage (1943)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Dorothy Arzner
Main Cast: Merle Oberon, Brian Aherne, Carl Esmond, Isobel Elsom, Fritz Leiber
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Merle Oberon plays a Norwegian resistance figure in a small town, married to a Nazi commandant. When his superiors begin to suspect her, the Allies land an assassin to kill him -- an assassin who happens to be her former lover.
