Title: Crazed Fruit (1956)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Kô Nakahira
Main Cast: Masahiko Tsugawa, Yûjirô Ishihara, Mie Kitahara, Harold Conway, Masumi Okada
Keywords: japanese new wave

Overview: Spending their summer on an exotic beach, two brothers fall for the same beautiful girl, whose charm and looks may hide more than they they bargained for.
