Title: Une femme d'extérieur (2000)
Genres: Drama
Director: Christophe Blanc
Main Cast: Agnès Jaoui, Serge Riaboukine

Overview: Françoise breaks up with Jacques after living with him for fifteen years. She's left alone with their three children, a lovely home, and her nursing job at a hospital. Françoise quickly refuses to assume responsibility for this new situation beyond her control. She starts going out, living by night… hanging out in new places, ready for chance encounters.
