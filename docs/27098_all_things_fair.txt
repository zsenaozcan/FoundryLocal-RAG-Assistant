Title: All Things Fair (1995)
Genres: War, Drama, Romance
Director: Bo Widerberg
Main Cast: Johan Widerberg, Marika Lagercrantz, Tomas von Brömssen, Karin Huldt, Nina Gunke
Keywords: jealousy, high school, alcoholism, older woman seduces younger guy, cheat on husband, teachers and students

Overview: This film is set in 1943 when the whole of Europe was embroiled in WWII. It deals with attraction of a 15 year old boy Stig to his teacher Viola. The whole movie revolves around the sexual encounters between Stig and Viola and how he eventually grows out of it.
