Title: Second Coming (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Debbie Tucker Green
Main Cast: Nadine Marshall, Idris Elba, Kai Francis Lewis, Sharlene Whyte, Seroca Davis
Keywords: dysfunctional marriage, pregnancy, middle age, woman director

Overview: A married, middle-class London couple are shocked when they seem to have been blessed — or cursed — with an immaculate conception.
