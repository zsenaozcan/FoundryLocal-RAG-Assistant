Title: Mysteries of Lisbon (2010)
Genres: Mystery, Drama
Director: Raúl Ruiz
Main Cast: Adriano Luz, Maria João Bastos, Clotilde Hesme, Afonso Pimentel, João Arrais
Keywords: jealousy, priest, school, young boy, battle, catholic

Overview: Follows a jealous countess, a wealthy businessman, and a young orphaned boy across Portugal, France, Italy and Brazil where they connect with a variety of mysterious individuals.
