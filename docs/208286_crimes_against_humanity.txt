Title: Crimes Against Humanity (2014)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jerzy Rose
Main Cast: Lyra Hill, Mike Lopez, Ted Tremper, Adebukola Bodunrin, Adam Paul

Overview: After cheating on her mean boyfriend, Lewis, Brownie is hospitalized by a series of severe accidents. Instead of visiting her in the hospital, Lewis (assistant to the Dean of the local university) helps investigate two professors suspected of sleeping with students.
