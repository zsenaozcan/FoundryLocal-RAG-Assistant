Title: Love Games (2013)
Genres: Animation
Director: Joung Yumi
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: I remember the games I played when I was young. We were happy playing together, but, looking back, I realize I used the games to satisfy my own desires. It was a child’s selfcentered behavior. But adult lovers, too, display immature sides in their relationships. Consumed by self-love, they act like children. I have tried to illustrate this kind of love through the metaphor of children’s games.
