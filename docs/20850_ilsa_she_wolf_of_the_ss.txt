Title: Ilsa: She Wolf of the SS (1975)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, War
Director: Don Edmonds
Main Cast: Dyanne Thorne, Gregory Knoph, Tony Mumolo, Maria Marx, Nicolle Riddell
Keywords: prisoners of war, sadism, necrophilia, medical experiment, scientific experiment, nazi exploitation film, ilse koch

Overview: Ilsa is a warden at a Nazi death camp that conducts experiments on prisoners. Ilsa's goal is to prove that woman can withstand more pain and suffering than men and should be allowed to fight on the front lines
