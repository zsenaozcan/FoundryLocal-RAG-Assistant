Title: Mad Love (1985)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Andrzej Żuławski
Main Cast: Sophie Marceau, Tchéky Karyo, Francis Huster, Yann Collette, Christiane Jean

Overview: After a successful bank robbery, Micky hopes to take back his girlfriend Mary who has been taken from him. On the way to Paris he meets Leon, a neurotic dreamer whom he considers an idiot. Leon can hardly understand what Micky is up to but he follows him everywhere and soon falls in love with Mary.
