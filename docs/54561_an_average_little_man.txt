Title: An Average Little Man (1977)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy, Drama, Crime
Director: Mario Monicelli
Main Cast: Alberto Sordi, Shelley Winters, Vincenzo Crocitti, Romolo Valli, Paolo Paoloni
Keywords: revenge, justice, accountant, unassuming, freemasonry

Overview: Giovanni Vivaldi is an average middle-aged man with a secure job as a government accountant. He wishes nothing more than to set up his only son Mario, his biggest pride in the world, with the same secure government job before he eventually retires. For this, Giovanni will stop at nothing...
