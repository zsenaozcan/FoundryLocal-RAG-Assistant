Title: The Gravedancers (2005)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Mike Mendez
Main Cast: Dominic Purcell, Josie Maran, Clare Kramer, Marcus Thomas, Tchéky Karyo
Keywords: envelope, childhood friends

Overview: After a night of drunken exploits, Allison, Harris, and Kira are chased and terrorized by the ghosts of a child pyromaniac, an ax murderer, and a rapist.
