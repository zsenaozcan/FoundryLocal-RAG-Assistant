Title: Allonsanfan (1974)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Paolo Taviani
Main Cast: Marcello Mastroianni, Lea Massari, Mimsy Farmer, Laura Betti, Claudio Cassinelli
Keywords: italy, revolution, anarchist, idealism, political

Overview: Suave Fulvio (Marcello Mastroianni) woos women as he and the Sublime Brothers, 20 of them, attempt revolution in 1816 Italy.
