Title: The Footprints of a Spirit (1998)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Carlos Rodríguez
Main Cast: Víctor Erice, Elías Querejeta, Ana Torrent

Overview: A documentary about The Spirit of the Beehive featuring director Víctor Erice, producer Elías Querejeta, coscreenwriter Ángel Fernández-Santos, and actor Ana Torrent.
