Title: The von Trapp Family: A Life of Music (2015)
Genres: Drama, Music
Director: Ben Verbong
Main Cast: Matthew Macfadyen, Eliza Bennett, Rosemary Harris, Cosima Shaw, Yvonne Catterfeld
Keywords: family

Overview: Agathe von Trapp, the eldest daughter of a well-known musical family, overcomes many obstacles to pursue her musical career and move to the United States.
