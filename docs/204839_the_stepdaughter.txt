Title: The Stepdaughter (2000)
Genres: Horror
Director: Peter Liapis
Main Cast: Andrea Roth, Lisa Dean Ryan, Jaimz Woolvett, Cindy Pickett, Gil Gerard

Overview: After a childhood spent going from one abusive foster home to the next, Susan seeks revenge on the mother who abandoned her. Susan's search leads her to a ranch where her long-lost sister begins to suspect the beautiful drifter isn't who she claims.
