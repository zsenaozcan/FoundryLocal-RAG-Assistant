Title: 72 Tenants of Prosperity (2010)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Eric Tsang
Main Cast: Jacky Cheung, Eric Tsang, Anita Yuen, Bosco Wong, Stephy Tang

Overview: Kung (Eric Tsang) and Kin's (Jacky Cheung) rivalry goes way back to the seventies when they fought over the same girl, who eventually became Kung's wife (Anita Yuen). Now they run competing phone stores right across from each other on Mongkok's busiest street, and stretch their minds trying to outdo each other with crazy promotions. There's absolutely no mixing with the enemy in these parts - until Kung and Kin's kids fall for each other! Further chaos breaks out when the landlord triples the rent of all the shop owners to force them out for redevelopment.
