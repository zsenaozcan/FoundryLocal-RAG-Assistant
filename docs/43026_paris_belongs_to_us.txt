Title: Paris Belongs to Us (1961)
Genres: Mystery
Director: Jacques Rivette
Main Cast: Giani Esposito, Françoise Prévost, Daniel Crohem, François Maistre, Jean-Luc Godard
Keywords: nouvelle vague

Overview: A young woman joins a theatrical troupe where she slowly believes that the director is involved with a secret group and that he is in grave danger.
