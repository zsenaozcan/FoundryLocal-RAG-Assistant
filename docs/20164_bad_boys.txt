Title: Bad Boys (2003)
Genres: Action, Drama, Comedy
Director: Aleksi Mäkelä
Main Cast: Peter Franzén, Niko Saarela, Lauri Nurkse, Jasper Pääkkönen, Vesa-Matti Loiri
Keywords: lottery, crime, bad father, brothers

Overview: The story bases on four Finnish brothers, nicknamed 'the Eura Daltons' who received nation-wide notoriety for tearing gas pumps apart when they needed cash. The cast is an impressive one: the brothers are portrayed by Peter Franzen, Lauri Nurkse, Niko Saarela and Jasper Pääkkönen while their really evil father is played by Vesa-Matti Loiri, one of the grand old men of Finnish cinema.
