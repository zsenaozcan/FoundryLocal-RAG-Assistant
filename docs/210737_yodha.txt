Title: Yodha (1992)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Fantasy
Director: Sangeeth Sivan
Main Cast: Mohanlal, Jagathy Sreekumar, Madhoo, Siddharth Lama, Urvashi 
Keywords: buddhism, nepal, impersonation, satanic ritual, attempted kidnapping, warrior

Overview: Ashokan (Mohanlal) goes on a trip to Nepal to meet his uncle (MS Thripunithura) who is settled there. To his surprise, his rival from his village Appukuttan (Jagathy) is already there impersonating him. He is unable to convince his uncle about the truth and gets kicked out of the house. While trying to find shelter he meets a young Nepalese boy (Sidharth Lama). Though they don't understand each others' language, they quickly form a bond. Meanwhile Ashokan is able to convince his cousin Aswathy (Madhubala) that Appukuttan is an imposter. Aswathy who is a student researching ancient Nepalese traditions realises that the young Nepalese boy is actually a Rimpoche, the spiritual leader of his tribe and is on the run from evil forces who want to banish him. Ashokan gets sucked in to the scenario unknowingly and ends up being the Rimpoche's protector.
