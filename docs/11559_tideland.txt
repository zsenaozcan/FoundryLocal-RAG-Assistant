Title: Tideland (2005)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Terry Gilliam
Main Cast: Jodelle Ferland, Janet McTeer, Jennifer Tilly, Jeff Bridges, Brendan Fletcher
Keywords: sex, isolation, magic, addicted, fear, world, explosion, drug, illness, parents

Overview: Because of the actions of her irresponsible parents, a young girl is left alone on a decrepit country estate and survives inside her fantastic imagination.
