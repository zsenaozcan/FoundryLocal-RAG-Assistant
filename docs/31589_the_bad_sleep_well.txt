Title: The Bad Sleep Well (1960)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Akira Kurosawa
Main Cast: Toshirō Mifune, Masayuki Mori, Kyôko Kagawa, Tatsuya Mihashi, Takashi Shimura
Keywords: father son relationship, corporate greed, japanese noir, film noir

Overview: A young executive hunts down his father’s killer. Continuing his legendary collaboration with actor Toshiro Mifune, Kurosawa combines elements of Hamlet and American film noir to chilling effect in exposing the corrupt boardrooms of postwar corporate Japan.
