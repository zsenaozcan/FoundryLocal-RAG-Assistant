Title: Tennessee (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Adventure
Director: Aaron Woodley
Main Cast: Adam Rothenberg, Ethan Peck, Lance Reddick, Ryan Lynn, Michele Harris

Overview: The story of three people who strive to realize their dreams. Brothers Carter and Ellis set out on a road trip to their childhood home in a desperate search for their estranged father, who may hold the key to their future.
