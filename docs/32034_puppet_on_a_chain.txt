Title: Puppet on a Chain (1971)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Geoffrey Reeve
Main Cast: Sven-Bertil Taube, Barbara Parkins, Alexander Knox, Patrick Allen, Vladek Sheybal
Keywords: murder, holland, drug syndicate

Overview: Special agent Taube is on the case of a deadly heroin smuggling operation. This one is notable for an amazing power boat chase through the canals and waterways of Amsterdam
