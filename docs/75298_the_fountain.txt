Title: The Fountain (1989)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Yuri Mamin
Main Cast: Viktor Mikhaylov, Shanna Kerimtayeva, Asankul Kuttubayev, Sergei Dreiden, Nina Usatova
Keywords: aborigine

Overview: A story of a Moscow's apartment building that is slowly falling apart, literally. First, the hot water has been cut off by an old man from Asia, who could not stand it being wasted. Then the roof is starting to collapse. Finally, the electricity has been cut off. All the tenants of the building, no matter how different they were, find themselves in the same situation.
