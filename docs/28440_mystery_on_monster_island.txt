Title: Mystery on Monster Island (1981)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy, Horror
Director: Juan Piquer Simón
Main Cast: Terence Stamp, Peter Cushing, Paul Naschy, Gérard Tichy, Ian Sera
Keywords: based on novel, treasure, island, stranded, dinosaur, creature, shipwrecked

Overview: The story concerns a shipwrecked group who discover natural wonders and a gold treasure on a dangerous island populated by dinosaurs and living seaweed. Terence Stamp is the bad guy. Supposedly the most expensive film made in Spain to that point, it has an admittedly strong genre cast including Peter Cushing, Paul Naschy, and Ian Sera.
