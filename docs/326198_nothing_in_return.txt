Title: Nothing in Return (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Daniel Guzmán
Main Cast: Miguel Herrán, Antonio Bachiller, Antonia Guzmán, Felipe Vélez, Luis Tosar
Keywords: adolescence, drama, family drama, teen drama

Overview: One summer, Dario flees the hellish environment of his home. Luismi, unconditional friend, a poor devil with pretensions of winning, and Antonia, an old woman who collects abandoned his motorcar furniture, become his new family. Three generations living together in a city too big to be alone.
