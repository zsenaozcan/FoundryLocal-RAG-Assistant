Title: God's Gift To Women (1931)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Michael Curtiz
Main Cast: Frank Fay, Laura La Plante, Joan Blondell, Charles Winninger, Alan Mowbray
Keywords: doctor, catfight, heiress, philanderer

Overview: Frank Fay stars in this 1931 Warner Bros. farce as a notorious womanizer who sets his sights on pretty American tourist Laura La Plante, only to be told by his doctor that he must give up all romance for his health. Also with Joan Blondell, Louise Brooks, Charles Winninger and Alan Mowbray.
