Title: The Hired Gun (1975)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Tonino Valerii
Main Cast: Fabio Testi, Renzo Palmer, Al Lettieri, Claudia Marsani, Luciano Catenacci

Overview: Fabio Testi is an undercover cop doubling as the bodyguard (hence, “Gorilla”) of a cantankerous middle-aged industrialist targeted for extortion.
