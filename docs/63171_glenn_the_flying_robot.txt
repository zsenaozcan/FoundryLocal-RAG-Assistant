Title: Glenn, the Flying Robot (2010)
Genres: Science Fiction, Fantasy
Director: Marc Goldstein
Main Cast: Billy Boyd, Dominic Gould, Patrick Bauchau, Gérard Depardieu, Smadi Wolfman

Overview: Henry and Jack are two famous and talented pianists: they are rivals and try to outdo each other in the various music events where they regularly compete. The former friends will clash with no mercy, from contest to contest until the day a domestic robot, GLENN, enters their lives. GLENN will jeopardize everybody and everything around them.
