Title: The Adventures of André and Wally B. (1984)
Genres: Animation
Director: Alvy Ray Smith
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: computer animation, pixar animation

Overview: There's nothing like a restful nap in a pleasant wooded valley. But when André awakens and is greeted by a pesky yellow-and-black striped insect with a nasty stinger, he ends up taking a quick (and painful) hike.
