Title: A Personal Journey with Martin Scorsese Through American Movies (1995)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Martin Scorsese
Main Cast: Martin Scorsese, Brian De Palma, Gregory Peck, Frank Capra, Francis Ford Coppola
Keywords: film making, archive footage, b movie, director, cinemascope, technicolor, shot, close-up, double exposure effects, motion pictures

Overview: Martin Scorsese celebrates American movies from the silent classics to the Hollywood of the Seventies. Films featured include: 'The Searchers', 'Citizen Kane', 'Intolerance' and 'The Crowd'.
