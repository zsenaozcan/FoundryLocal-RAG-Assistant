Title: Three Smart Girls (1936)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Music
Director: Henry Koster
Main Cast: Deanna Durbin, Barbara Read, Nan Grey, Charles Winninger, Binnie Barnes
Keywords: switzerland, musical, golddigger

Overview: The three Craig sisters, Penny, Kay, and Joan, go to New York to stop their divorced father from marrying gold digger, Donna Lyons, and re-unite him with their mother.
