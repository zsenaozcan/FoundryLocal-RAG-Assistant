Title: The Muppet Christmas Carol (1992)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Fantasy, Drama
Director: Brian Henson
Main Cast: Michael Caine, Don Austen, Meredith Braun, Don Austen, Ed Sanders
Keywords: holiday, future, musical, past, scrooge, christmas carol, charles dickens, christmas

Overview: A retelling of the classic Dickens tale of Ebenezer Scrooge, miser extraordinaire. He is held accountable for his dastardly ways during night-time visitations by the Ghosts of Christmas Past, Present, and future.
