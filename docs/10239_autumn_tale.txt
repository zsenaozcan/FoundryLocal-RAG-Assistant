Title: Autumn Tale (1998)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Éric Rohmer
Main Cast: Marie Rivière, Béatrice Romand, Alain Libolt, Didier Sandre, Alexia Portal
Keywords: women, southern france, widow, lonely hearts ad, friendship, wine, winegrower, french countryside

Overview: Magali (Béatrice Romand), forty-something, is a winemaker and a widow: she loves her work but feels lonely. Her friends Rosine (Alexia Portal) and Isabelle (Marie Rivière) both want secretly to find a husband for Magali.
