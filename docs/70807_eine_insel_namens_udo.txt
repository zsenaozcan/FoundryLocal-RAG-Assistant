Title: Eine Insel namens Udo (2011)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Markus Sehr
Main Cast: Kurt Krömer, Fritzi Haberlandt, Bernd Moss, Kari Ketonen, Maja Beckmann
Keywords: supermarket, tent, department store, love, security guard, invisibility, transvestite, rollerblading

Overview: Udo is invisible - no one ever seems to notice him. He makes the most of it, working as a department store detective and living off other people's lives as they never notice him tagging along. Until he meets the one woman who sees him.
