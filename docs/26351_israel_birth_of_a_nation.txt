Title: Israel: Birth of a Nation (1995)
Genres: History, Documentary
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: David Ben-Gurion, Martin Gilbert

Overview: Sir Martin Gilbert, author of over sixty books and the host of A&amp;E's JERUSALEM, hosts this gripping account of Israel's difficult first years. Filled with rare footage, photographs, and interviews with participants in the War of Independence, this is the definitive document of one of the turning points in modern history.
