Title: Blindsight (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Lucy Walker
Main Cast: Gavin Attwood, Sally Berg, Sonam Bhumtso, Dachung, Jeff Evans
Keywords: underdog, himalaya, mountain, blindness and impaired vision, tibet, mountaineer, himalaja, climbing, teacher, snow
