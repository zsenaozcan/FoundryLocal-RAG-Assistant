Title: Chitty Chitty Bang Bang (1968)
Genres: Music, Adventure, Fantasy, Comedy, Family
Director: Ken Hughes
Main Cast: Dick Van Dyke, Sally Ann Howes, Lionel Jeffries, Gert Fröbe, Anna Quayle
Keywords: flying car, fantasy, oldtimer

Overview: An eccentric professor invents wacky machinery, but can't seem to make ends meet. When he invents a revolutionary car, a foreign government becomes interested in it, and resorts to skulduggery to get their hands on it.
