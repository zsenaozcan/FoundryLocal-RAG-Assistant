Title: Dust (2005)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Sergei Loban
Main Cast: Алексей Подольский, Pyotr Mamonov, Gleb Mikhailov, Nina Yelisova, Mikhail Balinsky
Keywords: independent film

Overview: The genre of the film DUST could be called fantastic realism or existential drama with elements of the fantastic. Dust means the haste in which people live. Dust is a common misconception, the natural clogging up of the brain. Specks of dust how scientists perceive people, scientists who think they know more than anyone else about the universe.
