Title: The Thinning (2016)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Michael J. Gallagher
Main Cast: Peyton List, Matthew Glave, Stacey Dash, Lia Marie Johnson, Ryan Newman
Keywords: dystopia, high school, conspiracy, eugenics, aptitude test, meritocracy

Overview: In a post-apocalyptic future where population control is dictated by a high-school aptitude test, two students discover the test is smoke and mirrors hiding a larger conspiracy.
