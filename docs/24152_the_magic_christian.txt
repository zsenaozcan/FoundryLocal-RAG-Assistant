Title: The Magic Christian (1969)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Joseph McGrath
Main Cast: Peter Sellers, Ringo Starr, Isabel Jeans, Caroline Blakiston, Wilfrid Hyde-White
Keywords: based on novel, musical, black humor, music, satire, surrealism, independent film, riches, psychedelic

Overview: Sir Guy Grand, the richest man in the world, adopts a homeless boy, Youngman. Together, they set out to prove that anyone--and anything--can be bought with money.
