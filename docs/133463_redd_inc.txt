Title: Redd Inc. (2012)
Genres: Thriller, Horror, Comedy
Director: Daniel Krige
Main Cast: Nicholas Hope, Kelly Paterniti, Sam Reid, Alan Dukes, James Mackay

Overview: Six captive office workers are literally chained to their desks by a demented, escaped serial killer; former regional manager Thomas Reddmann (Redd). He assigns his 'human resources' the impossible task of proving his innocence or suffering gruesome consequences.
