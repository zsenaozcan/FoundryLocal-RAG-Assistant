Title: Junkopia (1981)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Chris Marker
Main Cast: Arielle Dombasle
Keywords: short

Overview: A short film that shows Boundless, Surreal objects that are juxtaposed with our present World. Cars, Motorways, noise of our modern society; A giant city in the distance - all that shrouds this lonely and forgotten island of Dreams. Filmed at the Emeryville Mudflats near San Francisco.
