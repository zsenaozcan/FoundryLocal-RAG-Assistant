Title: Bionicle 3: Web of Shadows (2005)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Animation, Family, Fantasy
Director: David Molina
Main Cast: Kathleen Barr, Trevor Devall, Brian Drummond, Christopher Gaze, Alessandro Juliani
Keywords: return, hero, enemy

Overview: The Web of Shadows concerns the return of the heroic Toa to Metru Nui. There, they find an old enemy has overtaken the great city with the help of Visorak--robotic spiders that can quickly encase anything or anybody in near-indestructible webs. The Toa undergo a bizarre metamorphosis while sealed within strange cocoons, and emerge as clunky-looking machines made up of mismatched parts.
