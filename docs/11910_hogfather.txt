Title: Hogfather (2006)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy
Director: Vadim Jean
Main Cast: David Jason, Marc Warren, Michelle Dockery, David Warner, Tony Robinson
Keywords: santa claus, gift, disguise, death

Overview: It's Hogswatch (equivalent to Christmas) on the Discworld and the Hogfather has gone missing, requiring Death to take his place while his granddaughter Susan endeavors to find out what has happened.
