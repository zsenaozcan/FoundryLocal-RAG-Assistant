Title: The Cruise (1998)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Bennett Miller
Main Cast: Timothy "Speed" Levitch
Keywords: tour bus, black and white

Overview: Affectionate portrait of Tim "Speed" Levitch, a tour guide for Manhattan's Gray Line double-decker buses. He talks fast, is in love with the city, and dispenses historical facts, architectural analysis, and philosophical musings in equal measures.
