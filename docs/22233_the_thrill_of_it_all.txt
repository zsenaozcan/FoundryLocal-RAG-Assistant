Title: The Thrill of It All (1963)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Norman Jewison
Main Cast: Doris Day, James Garner, Arlene Francis, Edward Andrews, Reginald Owen
Keywords: housewife, family man

Overview: The story of the film centres around urban housewife Beverly Boyer (Doris Day) and her husband, a successful gynaecologist and devoted family man, Gerald (James Garner). Beverly is offered the opportunity to star for a television commercial advertising soap. After a shaky start, she gets a contract for $80,000 per year to appear on the weekly TV commercials.
