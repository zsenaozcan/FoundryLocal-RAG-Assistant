Title: Dave Chappelle: For What it's Worth (2004)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Stan Lathan
Main Cast: Dave Chappelle
Keywords: stand-up comedy, live comedy, comedy, fillmore

Overview: 2004 television special starring stand-up comedian Dave Chappelle. The performance was recorded at The Fillmore in San Francisco, California in June 2004, and it premiered on September 4 later that year on Showtime. The special earned two Emmy nominations.
