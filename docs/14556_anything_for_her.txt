Title: Anything for Her (2008)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Fred Cavayé
Main Cast: Vincent Lindon, Diane Kruger, Lancelot Roch, Olivier Marchal, Hammou Graïa
Keywords: france, prison, airport, judge, chase, investigation, police, murder, escape, shootout

Overview: Lisa and Julien are married and lead a happy uneventful life with their son Oscar. But their life radically changes one morning, when the police comes to arrest Lisa on murder charges. She's sentenced to 20 years of prison. Convinced of his wife's innocence, Julien decides to act. How far will he be willing to go for her?
