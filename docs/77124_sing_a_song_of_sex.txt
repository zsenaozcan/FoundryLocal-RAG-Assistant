Title: Sing a Song of Sex (1967)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Nagisa Ōshima
Main Cast: Ichirô Araki, Juzo Itami, Koji Iwabuchi, Akiko Koyama, Kazuyoshi Kushida
Keywords: sexuality, vietnam war, university, rape fantasy

Overview: Four sexually hungry high school students preparing for their university entrance exams meet up with an inebriated teacher singing bawdy drinking songs. This encounter sets them on a less than academic path.
