Title: Dark Fields (2009)
Genres: Horror
Director: Douglas Schulze
Main Cast: David Carradine, Richard Lynch, Ellen Sandweiss, Dee Wallace, Derek Brandon

Overview: A farmer unearths an old top hat on his property and with it an ancient Indian curse that lays waste to all the farmers crops. All of the adults of the farming community are afflicted by a strange sickness that slowly dries them up until they are dust. It is only when the farmer communes with the hat does he find what it is that will save them all.
