Title: The Man Who Cheated Himself (1950)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime, Mystery
Director: Felix E. Feist
Main Cast: Lee J. Cobb, Jane Wyatt, John Dall, Lisa Howard, Harlan Warde
Keywords: homicide detective
