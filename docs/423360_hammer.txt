Title: Hammer (2016)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Nurbek Egen
Main Cast: Aleksey Chadov, Oksana Akinshina, Anton Shagin, Sergey Chirkov

Overview: Victor aka Hammer is an MMA champion with the world title fight ahead. After running into the mobster Shark Hammer gets injured but is still determined to win. Victor doesn't know Shark will stick at nothing to make Victor lose.
