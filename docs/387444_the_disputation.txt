Title: The Disputation (1986)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Geoffrey Sax
Main Cast: Christopher Lee, Toyah Willcox, Bob Peck, Alan Dobie, Bernard Hepton
Keywords: christianity, rabbi, debate, judaism

Overview: In 1263, King James I of Aragon organizes a debate between representatives of Judaism and Christianity regarding whether or not Jesus was the Messiah.
