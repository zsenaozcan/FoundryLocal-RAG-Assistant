Title: If These Walls Could Talk (1996)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama
Director: Cher
Main Cast: Demi Moore, Shirley Knight, Catherine Keener, Jason London, CCH Pounder
Keywords: nurse, student, pregnancy, woman director, abortion

Overview: If These Walls Could Talk follows the plights of three different women and their experiences with abortion. Each of the three stories takes place in the same house in three different years: 1952, 1974, and 1996.
