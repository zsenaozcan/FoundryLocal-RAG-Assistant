Title: Duel (1972)
Genres: TV Movie, Action, Thriller
Director: Steven Spielberg
Main Cast: Dennis Weaver, Jacqueline Scott, Eddie Firestone, Lou Frizzell, Gene Dynarski
Keywords: terror, california, falsely accused, gas station, cliff, chase, deputy, travelling person, panic, car crash

Overview: Travelling businessman, David Mann, angers the driver of a rusty tanker whilst crossing the Californian desert. A simple trip turns deadly as Mann struggles to stay on the road while the tanker plays cat and mouse with his life.
