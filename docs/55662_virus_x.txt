Title: Virus X (2010)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Ryan Stevens Harris
Main Cast: Jai Day, Domiziano Arcangeli, Joe Zaso, Dylan Vox, Sybil Danning

Overview: A thriller about a case of highly contagious swine flu virus engineered by rogue scientists. A cover up by a mysterious dark lady linked to the High Spheres adds action and mystery.
