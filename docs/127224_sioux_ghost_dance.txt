Title: Sioux Ghost Dance (1894)
Genres: Documentary
Director: William K.L. Dickson 
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: dance, silent film, sioux tribe

Overview: From Edison films catalog: One of the most peculiar customs of the Sioux Tribe is here shown, the dancers being genuine Sioux Indians, in full war paint and war costumes. 40 feet. 7.50. According to Edison film historian C. Musser, this film and others shot on the same day (see also Buffalo dance) featured Native American Indian dancers from Buffalo Bill's Wild West show, and represent the American Indian's first appearance before a motion picture camera.
