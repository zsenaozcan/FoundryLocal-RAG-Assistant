Title: True Story (2015)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery
Director: Rupert Goold
Main Cast: Jonah Hill, James Franco, Felicity Jones, Maria Dizzia, Ethan Suplee
Keywords: prison, journalist, prisoner, prison visit, based on true events, identity theft, accused of murder

Overview: A drama centered around the relationship between journalist Michael Finkel and Christian Longo, an FBI Most Wanted List murderer who for years lived outside the U.S. under Finkel's name.
