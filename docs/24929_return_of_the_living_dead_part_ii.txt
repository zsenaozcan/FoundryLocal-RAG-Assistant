Title: Return of the Living Dead Part II (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Ken Wiederhorn
Main Cast: James Karen, Thom Mathews, Suzanne Snyder, Michael Kenworthy, Marsha Dietlein
Keywords: undead, zombie

Overview: A group of kids discover one of the drums containing a rotting corpse and release the 2-4-5 Trioxin gas into the air, causing the dead to once again rise from the grave and seek out brains.
