Title: The Promise (1979)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Gilbert Cates
Main Cast: Kathleen Quinlan, Stephen Collins, Beatrice Straight, Laurence Luckinbill, William Prince
Keywords: architect, plastic surgery, car wreck, elopement

Overview: A rich student's fiancee has her face destroyed by a car accident, and refuses to return to him fearing the loss of his love.
