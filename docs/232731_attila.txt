Title: Attila (2013)
Genres: Action, Horror, Thriller
Director: Emmanuel Itier
Main Cast: Chris Conrad, Luke Barnett, Poncho Hodges, Cris Cole, Cheick Kongo
Keywords: supernatural, resurrection, attila, stolen treasure

Overview: When American soldiers inadvertently steal Attila the Hun's secret riches, the wrath of the barbarian is awakened; the mummified warrior will stop at nothing to kill the intruders.
