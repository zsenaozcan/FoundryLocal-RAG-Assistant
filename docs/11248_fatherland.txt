Title: Fatherland (1994)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Action
Director: Christopher Menaul
Main Cast: Rutger Hauer, Miranda Richardson, Peter Vaughan, Michael Kitchen, Jean Marsh
Keywords: journalist, secret, holocaust, schutzstaffel, adolf hitler, nazi germany, murder hunt

Overview: Fictional account of what might have happened if Hitler had won the war. It is now the 1960s and Germany's war crimes have so far been kept a secret. Hitler wants to talk peace with the US president. An American journalist and a German homicide cop stumble into a plot to destroy all evidence of the genocide.
