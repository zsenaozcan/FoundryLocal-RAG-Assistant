Title: Satan's Sadists (1969)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller
Director: Al Adamson
Main Cast: Russ Tamblyn, John 'Bud' Cardos, Regina Carrol, Scott Brady, Robert Dix
Keywords: suicide, nudity, outlaw, independent film, gore, motorcycle gang, switchblade

Overview: The "Satans" are a very cruel biker gang led by Anchor. The gang goes to a diner in the middle of nowhere in the California desert where they begin to terrorize Lew and his patrons and his waitress, Tracy. After a little killing, one of the patrons named Johnny manages to escape from the bikers into the desert. They need to reach a town before the Satans catch up to them and kill them.
