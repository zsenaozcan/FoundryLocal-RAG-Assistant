Title: Very Happy Alexander (1968)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Yves Robert
Main Cast: Philippe Noiret, Françoise Brion, Marlène Jobert, Jean Carmet, Paul Le Person
Keywords: french movies

Overview: Alexandre, a young and honest farmer, is oppressed by an authoritarian wife, who makes him work like a dog. When she dies in a car crash, he decides to stay in bed, absolutely free and inactive. Just a dog is occupied to carry food and newspapers to him.
