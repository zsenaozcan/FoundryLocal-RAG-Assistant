Title: Population 436 (2006)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Michelle MacLaren
Main Cast: James Blicq, Cory Cassidy, Fred Durst, Leigh Enns, David Fox
Keywords: mayor, barn, flat tire, census taker, woman director

Overview: A census-taker (Sisto) is sent to investigate why a certain small town has had the same population -- 436 residents -- for the last 100 years.
