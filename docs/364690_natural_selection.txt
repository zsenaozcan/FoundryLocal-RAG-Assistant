Title: Natural Selection (2016)
Genres: Drama
Director: Chad L. Scheifele
Main Cast: Mason Dye, Ryan Munzert, Anthony Michael Hall, Katherine McNamara, Amy Carlson
Keywords: manipulation, high school, violence

Overview: Tyler is a young man who is desperately seeking direction from a world that seems to have abandoned him. Searching for answers he looks to his new friend Indrid who appears to have it all figured out.
