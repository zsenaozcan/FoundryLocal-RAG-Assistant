Title: The Coward (1965)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Satyajit Ray
Main Cast: Soumitra Chatterjee, Madhabi Mukherjee, Santi Chatterjee, Satish Haldar, Deochand Lal

Overview: Amitabha Roy (Soumitra Chatterjee) is a Calcutta based scriptwriter who is driving around in the country to collect material for a film. His vehicle breaks down in a small town. A tea planter, Bimal Gupta, offers hospitality for the night. Amitabha is forced to accept the offer as he has no alternative.
