Title: They Died with Their Boots On (1941)
Genres: Drama, Romance, War, Western
Director: Raoul Walsh
Main Cast: Errol Flynn, Olivia de Havilland, Arthur Kennedy, Charley Grapewin, Gene Lockhart
Keywords: biography, historical figure, little big horn, custer, sitting bull, george armstrong custer

Overview: The story follows General George Armstrong Custer's adventures from his West Point days to his death. He defies orders during the Civil War, trains the 7th Cavalry, appeases Chief Crazy Horse and later engages in bloody battle with the Sioux nation.
