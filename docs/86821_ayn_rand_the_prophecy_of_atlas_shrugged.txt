Title: Ayn Rand & the Prophecy of Atlas Shrugged (2011)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Chris Mortensen
Main Cast: John Allison, Clifford Asness, Mike Berliner, Andrew Bernstein, Harry Binswanger

Overview: Ayn Rand &amp; the Prophecy of Atlas Shrugged is a feature length documentary film that examines the resurging interest in Ayn Rands epic and controversial 1957 novel and the validity of its dire prediction for America.
