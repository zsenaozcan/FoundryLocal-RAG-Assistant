Title: Deception (1946)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Irving Rapper
Main Cast: Bette Davis, Paul Henreid, Claude Rains, John Abbott, Benson Fong
Keywords: music teacher, classical music, melodrama, film noir

Overview: Davis and Rains shine in a romantic melodrama set in the world of concert music. Davis is a pianist torn between her wealthy, jealous patron and her cellist husband.
