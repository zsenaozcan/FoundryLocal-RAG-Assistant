Title: Winnebago Man (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Documentary
Director: Ben Steinbauer
Main Cast: Jack Rebney, Ben Steinbauer, Nick Prueher, Joe Pickett, Douglas Rushkoff
Keywords: underground, profanity, independent film, internet, recluse, outtakes

Overview: Jack Rebney is the most famous man you've never heard of - after cursing his way through a Winnebago sales video, Rebney's outrageously funny outtakes became an underground sensation and made him an internet superstar. Filmmaker Ben Steinbauer journeys to the top of a mountain to find the recluse who unwittingly became the "Winnebago Man".
