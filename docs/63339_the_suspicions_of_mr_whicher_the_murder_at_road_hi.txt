Title: The Suspicions of Mr. Whicher: The Murder at Road Hill House (2011)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, TV Movie
Director: James Hawes
Main Cast: Paddy Considine, Peter Capaldi, Tom Georgeson, William Beck, Emma Fielding

Overview: In 1860 Scotland Yard's 'Prince of Sleuths' Jack Whicher is sent to rural Wiltshire to investigate the murder of three-year-old Saville Kent,snatched from his bed at night and killed.
