Title: Powder Blue (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Timothy Linh Bui
Main Cast: Jessica Biel, Eddie Redmayne, Forest Whitaker, Ray Liotta, Patrick Swayze
Keywords: coffin, cocaine, nudity, rose, strip club, male with long hair, politically incorrect, hospital, lap dance, church

Overview: Several Angelenos meet on Christmas Eve through chance, tragedy and divine intervention. Swayze will play the sleazy owner of the strip club where Biel's character dances. Redmayne will portray a mortician who falls in love with her. Kristofferson will play the head of a corporate crime organization who tries to convince his former employee (Liotta) not to seek vengeance on his former co-workers.
