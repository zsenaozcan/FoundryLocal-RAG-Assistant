Title: The Raven (2012)
Genres: Crime, Thriller, Mystery
Director: James McTeigue
Main Cast: John Cusack, Luke Evans, Alice Eve, Brendan Gleeson, Kevin McNally
Keywords: poison, blackmail, masked ball, historical figure, buried alive, serial killer, deadline, edgar allan poe, newspaper review, baltimore maryland

Overview: A fictionalized account of the last days of Edgar Allan Poe's life, in which the poet is in pursuit of a serial killer whose murders mirror those in the writer's stories.
