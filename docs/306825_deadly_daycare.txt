Title: Deadly Daycare (2014)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Michael Feifer
Main Cast: Kayla Ewell, Christy Carlson Romano, Bryce Johnson, Ariella Nurkovic, Isabella Nurkovic

Overview: Rachel decides to take her three year old daughter to daycare. As the days pass, the woman feels that the teacher turns too with her daughter, reaching an almost obsessive relationship.
