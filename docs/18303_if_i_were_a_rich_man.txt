Title: If I Were a Rich Man (2002)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Gérard Bitton
Main Cast: Jean-Pierre Darroussin, Henri Guybet, Valeria Bruni Tedeschi, Richard Berry, François Morel

Overview: Between Alice, a hospital nurse and Aldo, a shampoo sales representative, things have turned sour of late. Alice blames her husband for lacking ambition and contemplates divorce.One day Aldo wins the ten million euro lotto prize. But, unwilling to share the bonanza, he keeps mum about it, all the more as he finds out Alice has an affair with Gérard, Aldo's former friend and ... new boss. However, Aldo, leading a double life, starts spending his money ...
