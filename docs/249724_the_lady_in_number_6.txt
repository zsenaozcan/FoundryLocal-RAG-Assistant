Title: The Lady in Number 6 (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Malcolm Clarke
Main Cast: Alice Herz Sommer, Zdenka Fantlova, Anita Lasker-Wallfisch, Malcolm Clarke
Keywords: short

Overview: The Lady In Number 6 is one of the most inspirational and uplifting stories of the year. 109 year old, Alice Herz Sommer, the world's oldest pianist and Holocaust survivor shares her story on how to achieve a long and happy life. She discussed the importance of music, laughter and how to have an optimistic outlook on life.
