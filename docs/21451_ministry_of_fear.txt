Title: Ministry of Fear (1944)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Fritz Lang
Main Cast: Ray Milland, Marjorie Reynolds, Carl Esmond, Hillary Brooke, Percy Waram
Keywords: spy, mental asylum, film noir

Overview: Stephen Neale is released into WWII England after two years in an asylum, but it doesn't seem so sane outside either. On his way back to London to rejoin civilization, he stumbles across a murderous spy ring and doesn't quite know  to whom to turn.
