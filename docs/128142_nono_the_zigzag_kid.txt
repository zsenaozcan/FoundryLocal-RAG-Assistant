Title: Nono, the Zigzag Kid (2012)
Genres: Family, Adventure, Drama
Director: Vincent Bal
Main Cast: Thomas Simon, Burghart Klaußner, Isabella Rossellini, Fedja van Huêt, Jessica Zeylmaker

Overview: The son of the world's greatest detective embarks on a high-stakes adventure in this action-packed adaptation of the beloved book by Israeli writer David Grossman. (TIFF)
