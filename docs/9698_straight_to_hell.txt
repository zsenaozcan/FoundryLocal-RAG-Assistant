Title: Straight to Hell (1986)
Genres: Action, Western, Comedy, Crime
Director: Alex Cox
Main Cast: Dick Rude, Sy Richardson, Courtney Love, Joe Strummer, Gloria Miralles Ruiz
Keywords: punk, independent film, anarchic comedy

Overview: A gang of bank robbers with a suitcase full of money go to the desert to hide out. After burying the loot, they find their way to a surreal town full of cowboys who drink an awful lot of coffee.
