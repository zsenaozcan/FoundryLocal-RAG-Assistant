Title: The Rainbow Thief (1990)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama
Director: Alejandro Jodorowsky
Main Cast: Peter O'Toole, Omar Sharif, Christopher Lee, Francesco Romano, Berta Domínguez D.

Overview: A petty crook, in search of the proverbial pot of gold at the end of the rainbow, hopes to cash in by befriending the heir to a huge fortune.
