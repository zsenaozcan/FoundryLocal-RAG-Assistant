Title: The Details (2012)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Jacob Aaron Estes
Main Cast: Tobey Maguire, Elizabeth Banks, Kerry Washington, Ray Liotta, Laura Linney
Keywords: worm, raccoon

Overview: When a family of raccoons discover worms living underneath the sod in Jeff and Nealy's backyard, this pest problem begins a darkly comic and wild chain reaction of domestic tension, infidelity and murder.
