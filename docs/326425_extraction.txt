Title: Extraction (2015)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Steven C. Miller
Main Cast: Bruce Willis, Gina Carano, Kellan Lutz, D. B. Sweeney, Dan Bilzerian
Keywords: terrorist, kidnapping, ex cia agent, rescue operation

Overview: A former CIA operative is kidnapped by a group of terrorists. When his son learns there is no plan for his father to be saved, he launches his own rescue operation.
