Title: Treasure Island (2012)
Genres: Adventure, TV Movie
Director: Steve Barron
Main Cast: Eddie Izzard, Toby Regbo, Donald Sutherland, Elijah Wood, Philip Glenister

Overview: Treasure Island is a two-part British television miniseries adaptation of the novel Treasure Island by Robert Louis Stevenson. It was made by BSkyB and first shown in the United Kingdom on Sky1 on 1–2 January 2012. The screenplay was by Stewart Harcourt and it was produced by Laurie Borg and directed by Steve Barron.
