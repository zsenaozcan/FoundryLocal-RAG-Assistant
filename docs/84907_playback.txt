Title: Playback (2012)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Michael A. Nickles
Main Cast: Johnny Pacar, Ambyr Childers, Christian Slater, Alessandra Torresani, Toby Hemingway
Keywords: journalism, television, police corruption, stabbing, murder investigation, old footage

Overview: While digging into their town's infamous past, a group of high school students unwittingly unlock an even darker secret. Now, an evil spirit has been awakened and will stop at nothing to find his true heir.
