Title: Keys to Tulsa (1997)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Leslie Greif
Main Cast: Eric Stoltz, James Spader, Deborah Kara Unger, Joanna Going, Michael Rooker
Keywords: blackmail

Overview: Richter Boudreau is on a bad streak: Languishing in the shadow of his celebrity mother, he loses his job as a film critic for the town paper, and now he's been approached with a dangerous proposition that ultimately leads to blackmail. Richter's friend Ronnie ropes him into a scheme to steal the inheritance of his wife, Vicky.
