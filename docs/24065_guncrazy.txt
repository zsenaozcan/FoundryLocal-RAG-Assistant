Title: Guncrazy (1992)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Thriller, Crime
Director: Tamra Davis
Main Cast: Drew Barrymore, Robert Greenberg, James Le Gros, Ione Skye, Jeremy Davies
Keywords: prison, rape, gun, murder, independent film, teenager, woman director

Overview: California teen Anita Minteer struggles in the face of an absentee mother, her mom's abusive boyfriend, Rooney, and a lack of respect from her classmates. This all changes when a pen-pal school project connects her with convict Howard. Anita secures Howard's parole and violently squares off against Rooney after he rapes her. Soon enough, the gun-crazy teen is on the run with Howard, with his parole officer in pursuit.
