Title: The Bigamist (1953)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ida Lupino
Main Cast: Edmond O'Brien, Joan Fontaine, Ida Lupino, Edmund Gwenn, Kenneth Tobey
Keywords: double life, judge, infidelity, adoption, bigamy, woman director

Overview: Mr Graham, a travelling salesman, is married to two women, Eve and Phyllis. Eventually, the truth comes out.
