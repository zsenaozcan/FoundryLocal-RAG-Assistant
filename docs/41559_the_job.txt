Title: The Job (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Thriller
Director: Shem Bitterman
Main Cast: Patrick John Flueger, Ron Perlman, Taryn Manning, Joe Pantoliano, Mark Harelik
Keywords: job, conspiracy

Overview: A guy looking to find employment and marry the love of his life, gets in over his head when a fast-talking temp agent lands him a job.
