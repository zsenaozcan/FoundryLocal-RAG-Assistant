Title: Tracks (1977)
Genres: Drama
Director: Henry Jaglom
Main Cast: Dennis Hopper, Taryn Power, Dean Stockwell, Topo Swope, Alfred Ryder
Keywords: vietnam, love, train, soldier

Overview: A soldier returns from Vietnam on special assignment, accompanying the body of his friend by train to California for burial. During the trip, he falls in love with a gentle college student. But their relationship is shattered by his flashbacks to combat.
