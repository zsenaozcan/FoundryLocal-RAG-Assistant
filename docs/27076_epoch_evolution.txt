Title: Epoch: Evolution (2003)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Ian Watson
Main Cast: David Keith, Angel Boris Reed, Billy Dee Williams, Brian Thompson

Overview: 10 years since the the first Alien object called Torus arose, two more have risen. The first torus changed the worlds view dramatically not just political but even on a religious basis. Dr. Mason Rand is on the run from religious zealots, Genesis Coalition, who fear the epidemic ramifications that will be brought on because of what DR. Rand knows.
