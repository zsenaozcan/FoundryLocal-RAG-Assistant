Title: Our Tropical Island (2001)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Marcello Cesena
Main Cast: Diego Abatantuono, Victoria Abril, Chiara Sani, Giulia Steigerwalt, Barbara Bouchet

Overview: A businessman is leaving for his family vacation in the South Pacific when he finds out that a rival has emptied his bank account. To save face he and his family lock themselves in the house, pretending to have gone on their trip, but it is not so easy to hide from the outside world.
