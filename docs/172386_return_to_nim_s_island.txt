Title: Return to Nim's Island (2013)
Genres: Family, Adventure
Director: Brendan Maher
Main Cast: Matthew Lillard, Bindi Irwin, Toby Wallace, Sebastian Gregory, Jack Pearson
Keywords: secret, island, travel

Overview: Fourteen year old Nim, more determined than ever to protect her island and all the wildlife that call it home, faces off against resort developers and animal poachers. Soon she realizes she can’t depend on her animal cohorts alone and must make her first human friend – Edmund, who’s run away to the island from the mainland – to save her home.
