Title: Under the Sun of Satan (1987)
Genres: Drama
Director: Maurice Pialat
Main Cast: Gérard Depardieu, Sandrine Bonnaire, Maurice Pialat, Brigitte Legendre, Alain Artur
Keywords: suicide, soul, based on novel, supernatural, priest, religion, dean

Overview: Satan tempts Father Dossignan, who is trying to save the soul of a young girl who killed one of her lovers.
