Title: Tea and Sympathy (1956)
Genres: Drama
Director: Vincente Minnelli
Main Cast: Deborah Kerr, Leif Erickson, Edward Andrews, Darryl Hickman, Norma Crane
Keywords: based on play or musical, school, high school student, preparatory school, sister boy

Overview: Tom Lee is a sensitive boy of 17 whose lack of interest in the "manly" pursuits of sports, mountain climbing and girls labels him "sister-boy" at the college he is attending. Head master Bill Reynolds' wife Laura sees Tom's suffering at the hands of his school mates (and her husband), and tries to help him find himself.
