Title: The Spy with a Cold Nose (1966)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Daniel Petrie
Main Cast: Laurence Harvey, Daliah Lavi, Lionel Jeffries, Eric Sykes, Eric Portman

Overview: A dog with a spying device under its skin is sent to the Russian government as a present. When the Russians send the dog to a veterinary,  British intelligence must get to the dog first and retrieve the spying device.
