Title: Nemesis 2 - Nebula (1995)
Genres: Science Fiction, Action, Thriller
Director: Albert Pyun
Main Cast: Sue Price, Chad Stahelski, Tina Cote, Earl White, Jahi J.J. Zuri
Keywords: cyborg, post-apocalyptic, sequel

Overview: It has been 73 years since Alex failed and the Humans lost the Cyborg Wars. Since then, the Humans have been enslaved. Scientists have developed a new DNA strain, which could signal the end of the Cyborgs, and they inject it into a volunteer. When the Cyborgs learn of the woman and the baby they list both for termination.
