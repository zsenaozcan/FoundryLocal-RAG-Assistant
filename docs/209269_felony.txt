Title: Felony (2014)
Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Crime
Director: Matthew Saville
Main Cast: Joel Edgerton, Jai Courtney, Melissa George, Tom Wilkinson, Sarah Roberts
Keywords: australian, australia, police, dirty cop, hit and run, accident
