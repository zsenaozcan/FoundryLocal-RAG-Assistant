Title: Carmina or Blow Up (2012)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Paco León
Main Cast: María León, Antonio Adarve, Miguel Alcíbar, Carmina Barrios, José Mari Bizcocho

Overview: Carmina, 58, runs a shop selling Iberian products in Seville. After several robberies and no help from her insurance company, she comes up with a way to recover the money she needs to keep her family. While she waits in her kitchen for her plan to kick in, she thinks back over her life, her work and her miracles.
