Title: Airport (1970)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: George Seaton
Main Cast: Burt Lancaster, Dean Martin, Jean Seberg, Jacqueline Bisset, George Kennedy
Keywords: bomb, based on novel, airport, desperation, snow storm, stowaway, airplane, panic, landing, psychopath

Overview: Melodrama about a bomber on board an airplane, an airport almost closed by snow, and various personal problems of the people involved.
