Title: A Generation (1955)
Genres: War, Drama
Director: Andrzej Wajda
Main Cast: Tadeusz Łomnicki, Urszula Modrzynska, Tadeusz Janczar, Roman Polanski, Zbigniew Cybulski
Keywords: war, polish, communism, foreign

Overview: A Generation is set in Wola, a working-class section of Warsaw, in 1942 and tells the stories of two young men at odds with the Nazi occupation of Poland.
