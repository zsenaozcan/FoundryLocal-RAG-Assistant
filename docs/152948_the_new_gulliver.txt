Title: The New Gulliver (1935)
Genres: Family, Fantasy, Animation
Director: Aleksandr Ptushko
Main Cast: Vladimir Konstantinov, Ivan Yudin, Ivan Bobrov, Mikhail Dagmarov

Overview: The story, a Communist re-telling of Gulliver's Travels, is about a young boy who dreams of himself as a version of Gulliver who has landed in Lilliput suffering under capitalist inequality and exploitation.
