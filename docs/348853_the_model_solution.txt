Title: The Model Solution (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: Edward Holzman
Main Cast: Sebastien Guy, Holly Hollywood, Jason Schnuit, Kim Yates, Regina Russell Banali

Overview: Jack's modeling agency has been losing its ground lately. His ex is running a competitive business and they both want the hot Rebecca to sign with them. He must also deal with his demanding tycoon dad and a secretary who wants him.
