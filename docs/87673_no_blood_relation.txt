Title: No Blood Relation (1932)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Mikio Naruse
Main Cast: Yoshiko Okada, Shin'yô Nara, Yukiko Tsukuba, Toshiko Kojima, Fumiko Katsuragi

Overview: In No Blood Relation, a gripping early example of Mikio Naruse’s cinematic boldness, featuring a screenplay by Ozu’s famed collaborator Kogo Noda, an actress returns to Tokyo after a successful stint in Hollywood to reclaim—with the help of her gangster brother—the daughter she abandoned years before.
