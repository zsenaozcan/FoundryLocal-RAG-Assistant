Title: The Shaman (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Marco Kalantari
Main Cast: Danny Shayler, David Sayers, Edmund Jäger, Adam Thomas Wright, Anton Noori

Overview: In the dark year 2204, the world has seen 73 years of continuous war. The face of conflict has changed. People kill people but they now rely on giant, intelligent battle machines to fight. Mankind has re-discovered Shamanism and the Generals on both sides use these well-trained spiritual warriors to face the threat of machines.
