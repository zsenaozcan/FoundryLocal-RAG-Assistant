Title: A Job to Kill For (2006)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Bill Corcoran
Main Cast: Sean Young, Ari Cohen, Lucia Walters, Jocelyne Loewen, Michael St. John Smith

Overview: The senior creative executive at a top advertising agency seems to have it all. But when he takes on a protégée to save a major account, he gets far more help than he bargained for.
