Title: The Waterboy (1998)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Frank Coraci
Main Cast: Adam Sandler, Kathy Bates, Fairuza Balk, Henry Winkler, Jerry Reed
Keywords: sport, social outcast, ridicule, lawn mower, deserted by husband, low self esteem, black and white to color

Overview: Bobby Boucher is a water boy for a struggling college football team. The coach discovers Boucher's hidden rage makes him a tackling machine whose bone-crushing power might vault his team into the playoffs.
