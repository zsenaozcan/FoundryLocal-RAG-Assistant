Title: Beyond (2014)
Genres: Romance, Science Fiction, Drama
Director: Joseph Baker
Main Cast: Richard J. Danum, Gillian McGregor, Paul Brannigan, Kristian Hart, Sid Phoenix
Keywords: post-apocalyptic, alien invasion, gun violence, troubled marriage, psychological drama

Overview: A suspenseful sci-fi journey tracking the turbulent relationship of Cole and Maya as they struggle to survive in a world where the human population has been left decimated after an extra-terrestrial attack.
