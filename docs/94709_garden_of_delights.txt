Title: Garden of Delights (1967)
Genres: Drama
Director: Silvano Agosti
Main Cast: Maurice Ronet, Ida Galli, Lea Massari, Franco Bertoni, Vanna De Rosas

Overview: Charlo and Carla are on their honeymoon. She is three months pregnant. He, furious about his marriage of convenience, recalls the past and ponders on The Garden of Earthly Delights by Hieronymus Bosch, dear to the Surrealists. While she is bleeding, he spends the night with a beautiful stranger...
