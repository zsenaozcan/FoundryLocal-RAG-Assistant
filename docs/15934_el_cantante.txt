Title: El cantante (2006)
Genres: History, Drama, Music
Director: Leon Ichaso
Main Cast: Marc Anthony, Jennifer Lopez, John Ortiz, Manny Pérez, Vincent Laresca
Keywords: dancing, 1970s, music, salsa

Overview: The rise and fall of salsa singer, Héctor Lavoe (1946-1993), as told from the perspective of his wife Puchi, who looks back from 2002.
