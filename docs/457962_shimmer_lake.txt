Title: Shimmer Lake (2017)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama, Mystery
Director: Oren Uziel
Main Cast: Rainn Wilson, Benjamin Walker, John Michael Higgins, Rob Corddry, Adam Pally

Overview: An inventive crime thriller told backwards -- reversing day by day through a week -- following a local sheriff's quest to unlock the mystery of three small town criminals and a bank heist gone wrong.
