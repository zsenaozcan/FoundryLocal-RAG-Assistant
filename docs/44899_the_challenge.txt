Title: The Challenge (2003)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Comedy, Family, Science Fiction
Director: Craig Shapiro
Main Cast: Ashley Olsen, Mary-Kate Olsen, Lukas Behnken, Brian Skala, Theo Rossi
Keywords: sister sister relationship, reality tv, twins, reality competition

Overview: The Challenge is an action adventure/adventure comedy. Mary Kate and Ashley Olsen play estranged twins with very opposite personalities. Lizzie is very focused and driven, while Shane is more relaxed, but concerned about the environment. Both girls are chosen for a Survivor-like popular game show with college scholarships as the prize.
