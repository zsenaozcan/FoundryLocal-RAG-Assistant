Title: Lady Frankenstein (1971)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Mel Welles
Main Cast: Joseph Cotten, Rosalba Neri, Paul Müller, Herbert Fux, Renate Kasché
Keywords: frankenstein

Overview: Dr. Frankensteins' daughter, who is in love with the aging lab assistant Marshall, continues with her fathers experiments and attempts to transplant Marshall's brain into a new body to prolong his life.
