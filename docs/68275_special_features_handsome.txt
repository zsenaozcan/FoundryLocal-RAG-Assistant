Title: Special Features: Handsome (1983)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Franco Castellano
Main Cast: Adriano Celentano, Federica Moro, Anna Kanakis, Tiberio Murgia

Overview: Forty-something Mattia has every woman in town lusting after him. But every time a woman wants to get hitched, he drags out his 'daughter' (actually the girl next door) to pass judgment on this woman.
