Title: Nature Calls (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Todd Rohal
Main Cast: Patton Oswalt, Johnny Knoxville, Rob Riggle, Maura Tierney, Patrice O'Neal
Keywords: brother brother relationship, camping, child kidnapping

Overview: Polar-opposite brothers Randy and Kirk never saw eye-to-eye, but their rivalry is taken to a new level when Randy hijacks Kirk's son's sleepover, taking the boys on a Scout Trip to remember.
