Title: Love's Kitchen (2011)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Comedy
Director: James Hacking
Main Cast: John Atterbury, Pamela Binns, Lee Boardman, Peter Bowles, Joshua Bowman
Keywords: helicopter, cooking, restaurant, kitchen, father daughter relationship, chef, food critic, tv show in film, english countryside

Overview: Rob Haley (Dougray Scott), an up-and-coming chef and restaurateur in London, is grief-stricken when he loses his wife. With encouragement from his infamous friend and real life TV Chef Gordon Ramsay, Rob decides to spice up his life by turning a run-down country pub into a gourmet restaurant. His food catches the eye - and taste buds - of beautiful American food critic Kate Templeton (Claire Forlani) and they soon both write a recipe for love that leaves both their hearts - and their stomachs - in full.
