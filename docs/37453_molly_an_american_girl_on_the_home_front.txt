Title: Molly: An American Girl on the Home Front (2006)
Genres: Drama, Family, History
Director: Joyce Chopra
Main Cast: Maya Ritter, David Aaron Baker, Tory Green, Sarah Manninen, Genevieve Farrell
Keywords: world war ii, war, american, woman director

Overview: Molly is a girl living in the year 1944 and WWII has brought many changes to Molly's life. An English girl comes to live with Molly's family to escape the bombings. They slowly become good friends.
