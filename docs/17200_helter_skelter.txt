Title: Helter Skelter (1976)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Tom Gries
Main Cast: George DiCenzo, Steve Railsback, Nancy Wolfe, Marilyn Burns, Christina Hart
Keywords: historical figure, charles manson, serial killer

Overview: The investigation of two horrific mass murders leads to the capture and trial of the psychotic pseudo-hippie Charles Manson and his "family".
