Title: It's Good to Be Alive (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Landon
Main Cast: Paul Winfield, Louis Gossett, Jr., Ruby Dee, Ramon Bieri, Ketty Lester

Overview: This movie details the struggles of former Brooklyn Dodger catcher Roy Campanella to adapt to life in a wheelchair following his crippling automobile accident in 1959. Cinematographer Ted Voigtlander was Emmy-nominated.
