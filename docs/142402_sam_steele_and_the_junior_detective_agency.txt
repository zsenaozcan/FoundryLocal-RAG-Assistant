Title: Sam Steele and the Junior Detective Agency (2011)
Genres: Family
Director: Tom Whitus
Main Cast: Luke Perry, M. Emmet Walsh, Jacob Hays, Darren Kennedy, Katherine McNamara

Overview: 13-year-old Sam Steele Jr. forms his own private detective agency to emulate his father, Des Moines detective Sam Steele, and helps track down a jewel thief.
