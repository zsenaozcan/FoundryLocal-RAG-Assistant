Title: Kirikou and the Sorceress (1998)
Genres: Fantasy, Adventure, Animation, Family
Director: Michel Ocelot
Main Cast: Doudou Gueye Thiaw, Maimouna N'Diaye, Awa Sene Sarr, Robert Liensol, William Nadylam

Overview: Drawn from elements of West African folk tales, it depicts how a newborn boy, Kirikou, saves his village from the evil witch Karaba.
