Title: Room 514 (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Sharon Bar-Ziv
Main Cast: Asia Naifeld, Ohad Hall, Guy Kapulnik, Udi Persi, Rafi Kalmar

Overview: A young, idealistic female Israeli military investigator confronts an elite commander with accusations of unnecessary violence in the Occupied Territories. Her integrity and determination are put to the test as the case proves less black-and-white than it originally seemed.
