Title: The Man Whose Mind Exploded (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Toby Amies
Main Cast: Tony Banwell
Keywords: gay, tattoo, autobiography, collage, love, outsider

Overview: In this "beautifully intimate and utterly unique piece of cinema", Toby Amies crosses the line between filmmaker and carer, trying to cope with the strange and hilarious world view of the fragile eccentric, Drako Zarharzar. A love story.
