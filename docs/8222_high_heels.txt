Title: High Heels (1991)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Pedro Almodóvar
Main Cast: Victoria Abril, Marisa Paredes, Miguel Bosé, Anna Lizaran, Cristina Marcos
Keywords: madrid, cheating, singer, mother daughter relationship

Overview: High Heels is a murder mystery comedy centering on flamboyant actress Becky del Paramo and her daughter Rebecca, a television anchorwoman. After being estranged for 15 years, Becky re-enters Rebecca's life when she comes to perform a concert.  Rebecca, she finds, is now married to one of Becky's ex-lovers, Manuel. The mother and daughter begin making up for lost time, when suddenly, a murder occurs...
