Title: Secret People (1952)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Thorold Dickinson
Main Cast: Valentina Cortese, Serge Reggiani, Audrey Hepburn, Megs Jenkins, Irene Worth
Keywords: europe

Overview: This tale of intrigue finds Valentina Cortese involved in an assassination plot. She helps the police apprehend the conspirators after an innocent bystander is accidentally killed.
