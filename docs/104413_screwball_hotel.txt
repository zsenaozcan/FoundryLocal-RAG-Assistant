Title: Screwball Hotel (1988)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Rafal Zielinski
Main Cast: Michael C. Bendetti, Andrew Zeller, Jeff Greenman, Lori Deann Pallett, Kelly Monteith

Overview: Three boys drop out of military school. They get jobs working at a hotel, but it's about to go under. They decide to help the owner raise enough money to stay in business. They prove that sex sells by holding "Miss Purity Pageant" starring some of the females staying at the hotel.
