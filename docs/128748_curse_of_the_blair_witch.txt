Title: Curse of the Blair Witch (1999)
Genres: Documentary, Horror, Thriller
Director: Daniel Myrick
Main Cast: Frank Pastor, Rachel Braaten, Randy Campbell, Dottie Corrigan, Bill Dreggors

Overview: Curse of the Blair Witch is a documentary about the Blair Witch legend and the three filmmakers who disappeared in October of 1999. It first aired on the Sci-Fi Channel on July 11, 1999 as a promotional prelude to the release of The Blair Witch Project on July 30, 1999.
