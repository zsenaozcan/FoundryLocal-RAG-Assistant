Title: Experience Preferred...But Not Essential (1982)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Peter Duffell
Main Cast: Elizabeth Edmonds, Sue Wallace, Geraldine Griffiths, Karen Meagher, Maggie Wilkinson
Keywords: hotel, waitress, mine, seaside town, introvert

Overview: A shy, introverted young girl takes a summer job at a seaside resort in Wales, where she finds the staff, the owners and patrons unlike anyone she has ever met before.
