Title: The Hunchback (1997)
Genres: Romance, Drama, Horror
Director: Peter Medak
Main Cast: Mandy Patinkin, Richard Harris, Salma Hayek, Edward Atterton, Benedick Blythe

Overview: Based on the novel, a young gypsy becomes a Minister's obsession in 1483. Only the bell ringer and her husband and the court of miracles can save her.
