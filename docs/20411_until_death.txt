Title: Until Death (2007)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Simon Fellows
Main Cast: Jean-Claude Van Damme, Selina Giles, Mark Dymond, William Ash, Stephen Lord

Overview: Van Damme plays a dirty cop who is hooked on heroin, and everyone dislikes him. Because of an accident he is put into a coma, and comes out of it a better person. He wakes up wanting to put things right.
