Title: Shana: The Wolf's Music (2014)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Music
Director: Nino Jacusso
Main Cast: Alana Aspinall, Marty Aspinall, Delilah Dick, Vonnet Hall, Sunshine O'Donovan

Overview: The First Nations girl Shana is a very gifted musician. But to bring her violin to sing, she must follow the wolf. Nino Jacusso has filmed this captivating coming-of-age drama with sensational beauty and spiritual tenderness.
