Title: Hitler's Madman (1943)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Douglas Sirk
Main Cast: Patricia Morison, John Carradine, Alan Curtis, Ralph Morgan, Howard Freeman
Keywords: assassination, resistance, world war ii, nazis, czechoslovakia, nazi occupation

Overview: Story of the assassination of Reinhard Heydrich, Nazi SS commander, by Czech partisans and the reprisals inflicted by the Nazis on the Czechs.
