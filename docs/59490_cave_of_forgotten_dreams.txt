Title: Cave of Forgotten Dreams (2010)
Genres: Documentary, Family
Director: Werner Herzog
Main Cast: Werner Herzog, Dominique Baffier, Jean Clottes, Jean-Michel Geneste
Keywords: secret, cave, cave painting, southern france, historian, scientist
