Title: Barry Hilton: The Live Series (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Kyle Ambrose
Main Cast: Barry Hilton, Glen Biderman-Pam
Keywords: stand-up comedy

Overview: The Live Series is an exclusive comedy special produced by the award winning production company Mannequin Pictures. In this hilarious stand-up Special, comedy household name Barry Hilton is at his best bringing you world-class humour, filmed live at The Lyric Theatre in Gold Reef City. Touching on everything from load-shedding all the way through to selfies, South Africa’s favourite cousin will have you in stitches before you can say Savannah!
