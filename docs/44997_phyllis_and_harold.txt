Title: Phyllis and Harold (2010)
Genres: Documentary, Romance
Director: Cindy Kleine
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Phyllis and Harold is a frank journey through a disastrous 59 year old marriage. Drawing on a lifetime of her family's home movies and interviews made over 12 years, filmmaker Cindy Kleine mixes reportage, cinema verite and animation to uncover family secrets and tell a story that could not be shown publicly as long as her father was alive.
