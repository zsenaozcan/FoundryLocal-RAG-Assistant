Title: Small Town of Anara (1978)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Irakli Kvirikadze
Main Cast: Sesilia Takaishvili, Rezo Esadze, Ramaz Chkhikvadze, Henrieta Lejava, Zura Qapianidze
Keywords: drinking

Overview: Tadeoz Toroshelidze is the most reknowned wine drinker in the whole area. Once he even won a drinking horn in a competition that has rested in his house ever since.
