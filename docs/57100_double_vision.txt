Title: Double Vision (2002)
Genres: Horror, Action, Thriller, Mystery, Foreign
Director: Chen Kuo-Fu
Main Cast: Tony Leung Ka-Fai, René Liu, David Morse, Leon Dai, Yang Kuei-Mei
Keywords: fbi, taiwan, serial killer

Overview: An FBI Agent pairs with a troubled Taiwan cop to hunt for a serial killer who's embedding a mysterious fungus in the brains of victims.
