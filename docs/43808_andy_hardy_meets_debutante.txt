Title: Andy Hardy Meets Debutante (1940)
Genres: Comedy, Family, Romance
Director: George B. Seitz
Main Cast: Lewis Stone, Mickey Rooney, Judy Garland, Cecilia Parker, Fay Holden
Keywords: judge, love, fish out of water, new york city, socialite, andy hardy

Overview: Judge Hardy takes his family to New York City, where Andy quickly falls in love with a socialite. He finds the high society life too expensive, and eventually decides that he liked it better back home.
