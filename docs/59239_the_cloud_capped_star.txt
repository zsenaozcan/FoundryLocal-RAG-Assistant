Title: The Cloud-Capped Star (1960)
Genres: Music, Drama
Director: Ritwik Ghatak
Main Cast: Supriya Choudhury, Anil Chatterjee, Gyanesh Mukherjee, Bijon Bhattacharya, Gita Dey
Keywords: self sacrifice

Overview: The tragic story of the beautiful daughter of a middle-class refugee family from East Pakistan. Neeta, the protagonist, sacrifices everything for her unappreciative family.
