Title: The Steel Trap (1952)
Genres: Thriller
Director: Andrew L. Stone
Main Cast: Joseph Cotten, Teresa Wright, Jonathan Hale, Walter Sande, Eddie Marr
Keywords: brazil, bank robber, b movie, bank vault

Overview: Joseph Cotten plays a assistant bank manager who steals $500,000 from the safe late on a Friday. His plans to flee to Brazil are hampered after he does get away with the money as planned.
