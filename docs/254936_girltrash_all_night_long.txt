Title: Girltrash: All Night Long (2014)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Comedy, Music
Director: Alexandra Kondracke
Main Cast: Lisa Rieffel, Michelle Lombardo, Gabrielle Christian, Kate French, Clementine Ford
Keywords: musical, lesbian, woman director

Overview: The story of five girls and one epic night. The girls will find love, lust, girl-fights, rock and roll, and a whole lot of stoned sorority girls.
