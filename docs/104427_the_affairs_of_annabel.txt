Title: The Affairs of Annabel (1938)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Benjamin Stoloff
Main Cast: Jack Oakie, Lucille Ball, Ruth Donnelly, Thurston Hall, Fritz Feld
Keywords: movie studio, publicity man

Overview: Wonder Pictures' seedy publicity man Lanny Morgan has put the studio's biggest star, Annabel Allison, in one crazy stunt after another. His latest scheme has Annabel pretending to be a maid in order to research her next screen role. Her disastrous attempts at cooking and cleaning, and her involvement with two fugitives hiding out at her employers' house provide her with more material than she really wanted.
