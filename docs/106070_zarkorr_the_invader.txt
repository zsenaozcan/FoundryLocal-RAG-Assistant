Title: Zarkorr! The Invader (1996)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Michael Deak
Main Cast: Franklin A. Vallette, Don Yanan, Peter Looney, Dyer McHenry
Keywords: hologram, chosen one, alien

Overview: An "average" postal worker is informed by a tiny alien hologram which looks like a teenage girl that he is the "choosen one" to destroy a giant reptile to save the Earth.
