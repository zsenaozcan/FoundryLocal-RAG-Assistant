Title: Last Train Home (2009)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Lixin Fan
Main Cast: Changhua Zhang, Suqin Chen, Qin Zhang, Yang Zhang, Tingsui Tang
Keywords: china, home, train

Overview: A family embarks on an annual tormenting journey along with 200 other million peasant workers to reunite with their distant family, and to revive their love and dignity as China soars as the world's next super power.
