Title: Symptoms (1974)
Genres: Horror
Director: José Ramón Larraz
Main Cast: Angela Pleasence, Peter Vaughan, Lorna Heilbron, Ronald O'Neil, Nancy Nevinson

Overview: A young woman is invited by her girlfriend, who lives in an English country mansion, to stay there with her. The estate, however, isn't quite what it seems--and neither is the friend who issued the invitation.
