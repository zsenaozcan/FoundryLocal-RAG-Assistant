Title: Babylon 5: The Gathering (1993)
Genres: Science Fiction, Action, Adventure
Director: Richard Compton
Main Cast: Michael O’Hare, Tamlyn Tomita, Jerry Doyle, Mira Furlan, Peter Jurasik
Keywords: galaxy, alien life-form, war, killer, space centre

Overview: The first installment of this Emmy award-winning series. A movie based at Babylon 5: a new space station built by Humans. The Vorlon ambassador, Kosh, has been poisoned. It is the new commanding officer's, Jeffrey Sinclair, responsibility to find the culprit. Otherwise the space station will fail in its role to bring all the races together.
