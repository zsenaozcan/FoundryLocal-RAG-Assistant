Title: Madame Tutli-Putli (2007)
Genres: Animation
Director: Chris Lavis
Main Cast: Laurie Maher
Keywords: railroad robber, train ride

Overview: Madame Tutli-Putli boards the Night Train, weighed down with all her earthly possessions and the ghosts of her past. She travels alone, facing both the kindness and menace of strangers. As day descends into dark, she finds herself caught up in a desperate metaphysical adventure.
