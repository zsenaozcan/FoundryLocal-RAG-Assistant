Title: The Magic Sword (1962)
Genres: Adventure, Fantasy, Drama, Action, Family
Director: Bert I. Gordon
Main Cast: Basil Rathbone, Estelle Winwood, Gary Lockwood, Anne Helm, Liam Sullivan
Keywords: magic, sorcery, wizardry, princess, knight

Overview: The son of a sorceress, armed with weapons, armour and six magically summoned knights, goes on a quest to save a princess from a vengeful wizard.
