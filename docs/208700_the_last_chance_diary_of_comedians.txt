Title: The Last Chance: Diary of Comedians (2013)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Teruyoshi Uchimura
Main Cast: Atsushi Ito, Keisuke Koide, Fumino Kimura, Haruna Kawaguchi, Kuranosuke Sasaki
Keywords: buddy comedy, foreign

Overview: The movie follows the life of an unpopular comedy duo. The duo, called "Boso Swimmers", have been working together for 12 years, but the duo sees little future in their comedy careers. They then begin to write their thoughts in a diary
