Title: Buck Privates (1941)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Arthur Lubin
Main Cast: Bud Abbott, Lou Costello, Lee Bowman, Alan Curtis, Jane Frazee
Keywords: musical, army

Overview: Petty con artists, Bud &amp; Lou mistakenly join the Army evading the cops. The cop chasing them winds up as their drill instructor. A rich young man and his former working class chauffeur are not only in the same unit, they're vying for a pretty girl who seems attracted to both.
