Title: Spirit of the Marathon (2008)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Jon Dunham
Main Cast: Dick Beardsley, Ryan Bradley, Leah Caille, Deena Kastor, Jerry Meyers

Overview: Four years in the making, Spirit of the Marathon is the collaborative effort of three-time Academy Award winner Mark Jonathan Harris, Telly Award winner and director/marathoner Jon Dunham and producer/marathoner Gwendolen Twist.
