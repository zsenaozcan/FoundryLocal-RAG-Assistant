Title: The Crucible (1996)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Nicholas Hytner
Main Cast: Daniel Day-Lewis, Winona Ryder, Paul Scofield, Joan Allen, Bruce Davison
Keywords: witch, massachusetts, passion, satan, curse, salem

Overview: The movie is centered around the Salem Massachusetts witch trials of 1692. The movie is based on the play "The Crucible" by Arthur Miller. He also wrote the screen play adaptation.
