Title: The Love She Sought (1990)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Joseph Sargent
Main Cast: Angela Lansbury, Denholm Elliott, Cynthia Nixon, Robert Prosky, Gary Hershberger

Overview: An aging school teacher (Lansbury) at a Catholic grammar school in Minnesota questions her life's existence when she has to start battling a new bishop (Prosky). As a result she retires and moves to Ireland where she seeks an admirer (Elliott) with whom she has been corresponding for five years.
