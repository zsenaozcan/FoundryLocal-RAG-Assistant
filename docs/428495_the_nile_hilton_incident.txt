Title: The Nile Hilton Incident (2017)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Tarik Saleh
Main Cast: Fares Fares, Mari Malek, Mohamed Yousry, Yasser Ali Maher, Slimane Dazi

Overview: In Cairo, weeks before the 2011 revolution, Police Detective Noredin is working in the infamous Kasr el-Nil Police Station when he is handed the case of a murdered singer. He soon realizes that the investigation concerns the power elite, close to the President’s inner circle.
