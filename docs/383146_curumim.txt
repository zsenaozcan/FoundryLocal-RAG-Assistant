Title: Curumim (2016)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Marcos Prado
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: brazilian

Overview: Marco ‘Curumim’ Archer’s life changed abruptly when police at Jakarta airport seize 13.5 kilogrammes of cocaine hidden in his hang-glider. At first he manages to escape, hiding out in Indonesia for sixteen days before being arrested and sentenced to death. Eleven years later, on 17 January 2015, he was executed for drug dealing.
