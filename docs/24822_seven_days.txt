Title: Seven Days (2007)
Genres: Crime
Director: Won Shin-Yeon
Main Cast: Yunjin Kim, Kim Mi-Sook, Park Hee-soon, Choi Moo-seong, Oh Gwang-rok
Keywords: suspense

Overview: A successful lawyer who, in order to save her daughter, is pressured into defending the innocence of a man slated to receive the death penalty.
