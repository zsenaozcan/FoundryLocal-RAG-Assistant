Title: The Greening of Whitney Brown (2011)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Family
Director: Peter Skillman Odiorne
Main Cast: Sammi Hanratty, Brooke Shields, Aidan Quinn, Kris Kristofferson, Natalia Dyer
Keywords: dance, horse, house, computer, city country contrast, mother daughter relationship, school, rural setting, father daughter relationship, tire swing

Overview: Whitney, a spoiled pre-teen from Philadelphia, is forced to move to the country when her parents feel the squeeze of economic hard times. A fish out of water, far from her comfort zone, she befriends an amazing horse, and undertakes a misguided journey back to her old life, only to discover that her family is her home.
