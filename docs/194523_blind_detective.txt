Title: Blind Detective (2013)
Genres: Crime, Comedy, Drama
Director: Johnnie To
Main Cast: Andy Lau, Sammi Cheng, Guo Tao, Gao Yuanyuan, Lo Hoi-Pang

Overview: A cop is forced into early retirement due to retinal damage. But after witnessing a bank robbery along with a female inspector - who believes he has acute senses - they team up in hope to solve the case.
