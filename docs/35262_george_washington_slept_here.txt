Title: George Washington Slept Here (1942)
Genres: Comedy
Director: William Keighley
Main Cast: Jack Benny, Ann Sheridan, Charles Coburn, Percy Kilbride, Hattie McDaniel
Keywords: pennsylvania, apartment, george washington

Overview: New Yorkers Bill and Connie Fuller have to move from their apartment. Without Bill's knowledge, Connie purchases a delapidated old farmhouse in Pennsylvania, where George Washington was supposed to have actually slept during the American Revolution.
