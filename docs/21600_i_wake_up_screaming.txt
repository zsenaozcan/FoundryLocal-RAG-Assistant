Title: I Wake Up Screaming (1941)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime
Director: H. Bruce Humberstone
Main Cast: Betty Grable, Victor Mature, Carole Landis, Laird Cregar, William Gargan
Keywords: classic noir, broadway

Overview: A young promoter (Mature) is accused of the murder of Vicky Lynn (Landis), a young actress he "discovered" as a waitress while out with ex-actor Robin Ray and gossip columnist Larry Evans.
