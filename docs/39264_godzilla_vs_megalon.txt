Title: Godzilla vs. Megalon (1973)
Genres: Science Fiction, Fantasy
Director: Jun Fukuda
Main Cast: Katsuhiko Sasaki, Hiroyuki Kawase, Yutaka Hayashi, Robert Dunham, Kotaro Tomita
Keywords: godzilla, kaiju

Overview: In his 13th film, Godzilla takes a supporting role to the far more prominently featured  Jet Jaguar, an Ultra-Man like robot. Aliens take control of Earth's monsters and begin using them to destroy the human race. Jet Jaguar decides to stop the attack by recruiting Godzilla and enlarging himself to Monster-size.
