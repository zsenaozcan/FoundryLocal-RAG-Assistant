Title: The Kids (1957)
Genres: Comedy
Director: François Truffaut
Main Cast: Gérard Blain, Bernadette Lafont, Michel François

Overview: A group of young boys who have a collective crush on Bernadette. As a result of this, they have an unreasoning jealousy of Gérard, and do everything they can to disrupt their relationship. When Gérard catches one of the young boys spying on them, he thrashes him severely. In retaliation, the children try to inspire Bernadette to doubt Gérard’s love.
