Title: Jonathan of the Bears (1995)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Enzo G. Castellari
Main Cast: Franco Nero, John Saxon, Floyd Red Crow Westerman, David Hess, Rodrigo Obregón
Keywords: revenge, native american, bear, spaghetti western

Overview: A young boy witnesses his parents' murder. Later, as he grows up, he befriends a bear in the wilderness and the chief of a local Indian tribe, and he stays with the Indians, but makes an enemy of the chief's son. As he enters adulthood he sets out to find the men responsible for his parents' deaths
