Title: The Garden of Sinners - Chapter 5: Paradox Spiral (2008)
Genres: Action, Animation, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Takaaki Hirao
Main Cast: Maaya Sakamoto, Takako Honda, Jouji Nakata, Kenichi Suzumura, Hiroki Touchi
Keywords: supernatural

Overview: In the middle of October 1998, Enjou Tomoe is attacked by bullies from his old school and saved by Ryougi Shiki. He asks her to hide him at her place and admits that he killed someone. Several days after the incident there are still no broadcasts about the murder as if it didn't happen.
