Title: Slovenian Girl (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Damjan Kozole
Main Cast: Nina Ivanišin, Peter Musevski, Primož Pirnat, Maruša Kink, Uroš Furst
Keywords: callgirl, sexuality, student

Overview: Aleksandra is a student from Krško, a small town in Slovenia. She has a plan to conquer the world. Working as a prostitute, her life is heading to where she wants it, but an accidental death has her wrestling with new feelings of fear, loneliness, confusion and responsibility
