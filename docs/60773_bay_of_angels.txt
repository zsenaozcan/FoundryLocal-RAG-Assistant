Title: Bay of Angels (1963)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Jacques Demy
Main Cast: Jeanne Moreau, Claude Mann, Paul Guers, Henri Nassiet, Nicole Chollet
Keywords: southern france, passion, roulette, gambler

Overview: A compulsive gambler who falls in love with a bank clerk (Claude Mann) on holiday in Nice. At first, the two lovers simply use each other as good luck charms; but what happens to love when luck runs out?
