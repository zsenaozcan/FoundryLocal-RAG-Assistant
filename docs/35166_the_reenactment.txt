Title: The Reenactment (1968)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lucian Pintilie
Main Cast: George Constantin, Emil Botta, George Mihaiță, Vladimir Găițan, Ernest Maftei
Keywords: based on novel

Overview: A prosecutor, policemen and teacher bring the students Vuica and Nicu to a restaurant to re-enact their drunken brawl there, and have it filmed to show the effects of alcoholism.
