Title: Cannibal Ferox (1981)
Genres: Adventure, Horror
Director: Umberto Lenzi
Main Cast: Giovanni Lombardo Radice, Robert Kerman, Danilo Mattei, Venantino Venantini, Fiamma Maglione
Keywords: colombia, tribe, jungle, torture, cannibal, exploitation film, anthropologist, video nasty

Overview: Three friends out to disprove cannibalism meet two men on the run who tortured and enslaved a cannibal tribe to find emeralds, and now the tribe is out for revenge.
