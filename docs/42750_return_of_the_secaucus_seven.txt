Title: Return of the Secaucus Seven (1980)
Genres: Drama
Director: John Sayles
Main Cast: Bruce MacDonald, Maggie Renzi, Adam LeFevre, Maggie Cousineau, Gordon Clapp
Keywords: protest, gathering, remembrance

Overview: Seven former college friends, along with a few new friends, gather for a weekend reunion at a summer house in New Hampshire to reminisce about the good old days, when they got arrested on the way to a protest in Washington, DC.
