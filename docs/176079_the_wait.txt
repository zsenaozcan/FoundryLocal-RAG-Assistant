Title: The Wait (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: M. Blash
Main Cast: Jena Malone, Chloë Sevigny, Luke Grimes, Devon Gearhart, Michael O'Keefe
Keywords: independent film, death, denial, fake psychic, denial of death

Overview: An enigmatic phone call from a psychic catapults a family into a state of suspended belief while waiting for their recently deceased mother to be resurrected.
