Title: Vaya con Dios (2002)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Zoltan Spirandelli
Main Cast: Michael Gwisdek, Daniel Brühl, Traugott Buhre, Matthias Brenner, Pamela Knaak
Keywords: musical, monastery

Overview: Three monks are making their way from Germany to a monastry in Italy, because they cannot afford the rent for their facility any longer.
