Title: The Bothersome Man (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Fantasy
Director: Jens Lien
Main Cast: Trond Fausa, Petronella Barker, Per Schaaning, Birgitte Larsen, Johannes Joner
Keywords: dream, revolution, modern society, surreal, dystopia, afterlife, romance, confusion

Overview: Forty-year-old Andreas arrives in a strange city with no memory of how he got there. He is presented with a job, an apartment - even a wife. But before long, Andreas notices that something is wrong. Andreas makes an attempt to escape the city, but he discovers there's no way out. Andreas meets Hugo, who has found a crack in a wall in his cellar. Beautiful music streams out from the crack. Maybe it leads to "the other side"? A new plan for escape is hatched.
