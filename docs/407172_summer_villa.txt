Title: Summer Villa (2016)
Genres: Romance, Comedy
Director: Pat Kiely
Main Cast: Hilarie Burton, Victor Webster, Jocelin Haas, Cristina Rosato, Brittany Drisdelle

Overview: Although a successful romance novelist, Terry Russell hasn't had luck in her own love life. After a disastrous first date with cocky, hot-shot New York chef Matthew Everston, she retreats to her friend's French villa for the summer to finish her latest novel, with her reluctant teenage daughter in tow.
