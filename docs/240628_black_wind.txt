Title: Black Wind (1964)
Genres: Adventure, Drama
Director: Servando González
Main Cast: David Reynoso, José Elías Moreno, Eleazar García, Fernando Luján, Jorge Martínez de Hoyos
Keywords: father son relationship, based on novel, wind, worker, sacrifice, engineer, melodrama, rural setting, desert, death

Overview: The building of a railroad under tough conditions from searing heat to freezing cold in the Sonora desert provokes clashes of passion and struggles between the engineers and the workers at the campsite. The workers also contend with sudden dust storms that are called the 'black wind'.
