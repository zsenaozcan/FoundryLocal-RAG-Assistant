Title: Tamala 2010: A Punk Cat in Space (2002)
Genres: Animation, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: Tol
Main Cast: Béatrice Dalle, Takeshi Katô, Hisayo Mochizuki, Shinje Takeda
Keywords: cat, anime

Overview: Tamala is a cat living on Planet Cat Earth in the Feline Galaxy. In attempt to leave the Feline Galaxy, which is practically owned by a mega corporation called Catty &amp; Co., she crashes on the violence-ridden Planet Q where she meets Michelangelo. Together they have fun, while Tamala seaches for her connections to Catty &amp; Co. and her mysterious homeworld Orion
