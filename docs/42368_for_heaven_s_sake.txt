Title: For Heaven's Sake (1926)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Action
Director: Sam Taylor
Main Cast: Harold Lloyd, Jobyna Ralston, Noah Young, Jim Mason, Paul Weigel
Keywords: mission, millionaire, silent film

Overview: The Uptown Boy, J. Harold Manners (Lloyd) is a millionaire playboy who falls for the Downtown Girl, Hope (Ralston) who works in Brother Paul's (Weigel) mission. In order to build up attendance, and win Hope's attention, Harold runs through town causing trouble, and winds up with a crowd chasing him right into the mission. He eventually wins the girl and they marry, but not without some interference from his high-brow friends.
