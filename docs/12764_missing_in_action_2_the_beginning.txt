Title: Missing in Action 2: The Beginning (1985)
Genres: Action, Adventure, War
Director: Lance Hool
Main Cast: Chuck Norris, Soon-Tek Oh, Steven Williams, Bennett Ohta, Cosie Costa
Keywords: prisoners of war, liberation, vietnam war, vietcong, death of a friend, revenge, escape, shootout, torture

Overview: Prequel to the first Missing In Action, set in the early 1980s it shows the capture of Colonel Braddock during the Vietnam war in the 1970s, and his captivity with other American POWs in a brutal prison camp, and his plans to escape.
