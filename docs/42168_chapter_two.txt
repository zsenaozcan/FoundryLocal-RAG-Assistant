Title: Chapter Two (1979)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Robert Moore
Main Cast: James Caan, Marsha Mason, Joseph Bologna, Valerie Harper, Alan Fudge
Keywords: friends, widower

Overview: George Schneider is an author whose wife had just died. His brother Leo gives him the number of Jennie Malone, and somehow they hit it off. And just when things are moving along, the memory of his first wife comes between them.
