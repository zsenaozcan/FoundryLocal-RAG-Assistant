Title: Party Plane (1991)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ed Hansen
Main Cast: Kent Stoddard, Karen Annarino, John F. Goff, Jill Johnson, Jacklyn Palmer

Overview: Condor Skyways has found a way to give passengers a pleasant flight. Everybody is first class and the attendants are all wearing the official uniform - a smile. Everyone has a view, but no one is fighting for a window seat. These are definitely the friendliest skies you'll ever fly.
