Title: Contagion (2011)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Science Fiction
Director: Steven Soderbergh
Main Cast: Matt Damon, Gwyneth Paltrow, Kate Winslet, Jude Law, Marion Cotillard
Keywords: saving the world, mutation, infection, terminal illness, quarantine, outbreak, medical, vaccine, lethal virus, scientist

Overview: As an epidemic of a lethal airborne virus - that kills within days - rapidly grows, the worldwide medical community races to find a cure and control the panic that spreads faster than the virus itself.
