Title: Demons 2 (1986)
Genres: Horror, Fantasy
Director: Lamberto Bava
Main Cast: David Edwin Knight, Nancy Brilli, Coralina Cataldi-Tassoni, Bobby Rhodes, Asia Argento
Keywords: television, demon, apartment building

Overview: A group of tenants and visitors are trapped in a 10-story high-rise apartment building infested with demons who proceed to hunt the dwindling humans down.
