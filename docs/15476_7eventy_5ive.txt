Title: 7eventy 5ive (2007)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Brian Hooks
Main Cast: Rutger Hauer, Jud Tylor, Wil Horneff, Germán Legarreta, Antwon Tanner

Overview: As another semester draws to a close at the University of Dreyskill, a simple game dreamt to help students avoid studying becomes a bloody battle for survival in this stalk-and-slash frightener from filmmaking duo Brian Hooks and Deon Taylor.
