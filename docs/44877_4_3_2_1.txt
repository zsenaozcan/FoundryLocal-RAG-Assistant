Title: 4.3.2.1 (2010)
Genres: Action, Thriller, Crime, Romance
Director: Noel Clarke
Main Cast: Emma Roberts, Noel Clarke, Ophelia Lovibond, Tamsin Egerton, Shanika Warren-Markland

Overview: Jo is chained down in a dead end supermarket job while all her friends are all out on their own separate adventures. But a chance encounter with some diamond thieves sends their separate worlds on a collision course with not only each other, but fate itself.
