Title: Charlie Chan at the Race Track (1936)
Genres: Mystery, Comedy, Thriller, Crime
Director: H. Bruce Humberstone
Main Cast: Warner Oland, Keye Luke, Helen Wood, Thomas Beck, Alan Dinehart
Keywords: charlie chan

Overview: When a friend of Charlie's is found kicked to death by his own race horse on board a Honolulu-bound liner, the detective discovers foul play and uncovers an international gambling ring.
