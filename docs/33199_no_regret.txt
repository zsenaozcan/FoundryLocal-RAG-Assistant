Title: No Regret (2006)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Leesong Hee-il
Main Cast: Kim Nam-gil, Lee Young-Hoon, Kim Dong-wook, Jung Seung-kil
Keywords: gay, male nudity, attempted murder, buried alive, male prostitute

Overview: Sumin is an orphan trying to balance work in a factory with study at an art college and an evening job. One night, a rich young businessman makes an advance on him during one of his driving jobs. They meet again the next day: it is during a round of redundancy cuts at the factory where Sumin refuses an attempt by a man (who is in fact the boss' son Jaemin) to save his job. Eventually, Sumin is seduced into working as a prostitute in an up-market boy-brothel as Jaemin's obsession with him grows, leaving Jaemin helpless in the face of his overwhelming desire. This is a film of bold sexuality, where unexpected passion, desire and misunderstandings wreak havoc of an operatic intensity.
