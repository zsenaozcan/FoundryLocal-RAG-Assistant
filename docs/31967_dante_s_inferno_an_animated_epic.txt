Title: Dante's Inferno: An Animated Epic (2010)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Animation, Horror
Director: Victor Cook
Main Cast: Mark Hamill, Kevin Michael Richardson, Graham McTavish, Victoria Tennant, Vanessa Branch
Keywords: crusade, adult animation

Overview: Dante journeys through the nine circles of Hell -- limbo, lust, gluttony, greed, anger, heresy, violence, fraud and treachery -- in search of his true love, Beatrice. An animated version of the video game of the same name.
