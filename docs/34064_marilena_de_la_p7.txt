Title: Marilena de la P7 (2006)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Cristian Nemescu
Main Cast: Mădălina Ghițescu, Gabriel Huian, Andi Vasluianu

Overview: Andrei, a 13 year old teenager, living on Bucharest outskirts, decides one day to steal a trolleybus in order to impress Marilena, a prostitute he fell in love with.
