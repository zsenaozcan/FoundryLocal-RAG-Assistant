Title: The Marseille Contract (1974)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Robert Parrish
Main Cast: Michael Caine, Anthony Quinn, James Mason, Maurice Ronet, Alexandra Stewart
Keywords: assassin, drug dealer

Overview: The Marseille Contract is a 1974 British thriller film directed by Robert Parrish and scored by Roy Budd. It stars Michael Caine, Anthony Quinn and James Mason. It concerns an attempt to bring down a drugs baron, by hiring an assassin.
