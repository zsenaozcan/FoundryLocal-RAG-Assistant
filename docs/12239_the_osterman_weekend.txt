Title: The Osterman Weekend (1983)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Sam Peckinpah
Main Cast: Rutger Hauer, John Hurt, Craig T. Nelson, Dennis Hopper, Chris Sarandon
Keywords: terror, cia, fight, summer residence, kgb, arrow, tv reporter, violence, family, meeting

Overview: The host of an investigative news show is convinced by the CIA that the friends he has invited to a weekend in the country are engaged in a conspiracy that threatens national security in this adaptation of the Robert Ludlum novel.
