Title: Hardware (1990)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Richard Stanley
Main Cast: Dylan McDermott, Stacey Travis, William Hootkins, Carl McCoy, Iggy Pop
Keywords: male nudity, female nudity, killer robot, nudity, artist, post-apocalyptic, dystopia, murder, robot as menace, independent film

Overview: It was the movie that stunned audiences, shocked the MPAA and marked the debut of one of the most uncompromising filmmakers in modern horror. Golden Globe winner Dylan McDermott (The Practice) stars as a post-apocalyptic scavenger who brings home a battered cyborg skull for his metal-sculptor girlfriend. But this steel scrap contains the brain of the M.A.R.K. 13, the military's most ferocious bio-mechanical combat droid. It is cunning, cruel and can reassemble itself. Tonight, it is reborn....and no flesh shall be spared. Stacey Travis (Ghost World) co-stars - along with appearances by Iggy Pop, Lemmy of Motörhead and music by Ministry and Public Image Ltd.
