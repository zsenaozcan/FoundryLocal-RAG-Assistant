Title: It Happened Tomorrow (1944)
Genres: Fantasy, Comedy, Science Fiction
Director: René Clair
Main Cast: Dick Powell, Linda Darnell, Jack Oakie, Edgar Kennedy, John Philliber
Keywords: newspaper, time warp, headline, morning

Overview: A young turn-of-the-century newspaper man finds he can get hold of the next day's paper. This brings more problems than fortune, especially as his new girlfriend is part of a phony clairvoyant act.
