Title: UltraMarathon Man: 50 Marathons, 50 States, 50 Days (2008)
Genres: Documentary

Overview: Dean Karnazes also known as the UltraMarathon Man has inspired thousands of runners. This is the epilogue to Karnazes' 50 day expedition. Dean Karnazes completed 50 Marathons in 50 States in 50 Consecutive Days. "Sure its about running, but mostly it's about life."
