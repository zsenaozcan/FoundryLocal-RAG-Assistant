Title: Down and Dangerous (2014)
Genres: Crime, Thriller
Director: Zak Forsman
Main Cast: John T. Woods, Paulie Redding, Ross Marquand, Judd Nelson, Ernest Curcio
Keywords: alias, smuggler

Overview: An inventive and resourceful cocaine smuggler defends his trade against violent rival traffickers, a monstrous and vengeful killer, and the DEA agents who want to lock him up – all while falling for a stunningly beautiful woman with a taste for danger.
