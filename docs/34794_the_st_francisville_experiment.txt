Title: The St  Francisville Experiment (2000)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Ted Nicolaou
Main Cast: Madison Charap, Troy Taylor, Ryan Larson, Paul James Palmer, Tim Baldini
Keywords: upper class, exorcism, plantation, suspense, social satire

Overview: According to local legend, the ghosts of tortured slaves still haunt New Orleans' Lalaurie House. For The St. Francisville Experiment, a paranormal researcher, a self-proclaimed psychic, a Louisiana historian, and a filmmaker were given digital video cameras for one night in the house to document whatever they saw. ~ Mark Deming, All Movie Guide
