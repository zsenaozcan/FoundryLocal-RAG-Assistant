Title: The Biggest Battle (1978)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Umberto Lenzi
Main Cast: Helmut Berger, Samantha Eggar, Giuliano Gemma, John Huston, Stacy Keach
Keywords: battle

Overview: A story of how World War II affected the lives of a German family and an American family, both of whom had sons and fathers fighting in the war.
