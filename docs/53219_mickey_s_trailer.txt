Title: Mickey's Trailer (1938)
Genres: Animation
Director: Ben Sharpsteen
Main Cast: Pinto Colvig, Walt Disney, Clarence Nash
Keywords: mickey mouse, disney short, donald duck, goofy, camping trailer, short

Overview: Goofy's in the driver's seat, Mickey's in the kitchen, and Donald's in bed in Mickey's high-tech house trailer. When Goofy comes back to eat breakfast, leaving the car on autopilot, it takes them onto a dangerous closed mountain road. When Goofy realizes this, he accidentally unhooks the trailer, sending it on a perilous route. They come very close to disaster several times, while the oblivious Goofy drives on and hooks back up to them.
