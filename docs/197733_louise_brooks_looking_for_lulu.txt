Title: Louise Brooks: Looking for Lulu (1998)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Hugh Munro Neely
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Documentary recounting the life story of Louise Brooks in 5 sections: "Lulu in Toe Shoes"; "Lulu in Hollywood"; "Lulu in Berlin"; "Lulu in Hell"; and "Resurrection". Narrated by Shirley MacLaine.
