Title: Fear of Fear (1975)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rainer Werner Fassbinder
Main Cast: Margit Carstensen, Ulrich Faulhaber, Brigitte Mira, Irm Hermann, Armin Meier
Keywords: pregnancy, mental health

Overview: After having her second child, a German housewife suffers from post-partum depression before inexplicably falling into a continually misdiagnosed mental state, befuddling her relatives.
