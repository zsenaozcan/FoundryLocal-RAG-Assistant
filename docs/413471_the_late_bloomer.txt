Title: The Late Bloomer (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Kevin Pollak
Main Cast: Johnny Simmons, Brittany Snow, J.K. Simmons, Jane Lynch, Maria Bello
Keywords: sex, based on novel, puberty, love, relationship

Overview: A sex therapist goes through puberty after the successful removal of a benign tumor resting against his pituitary gland. He experiences all the changes and effects of puberty over a three-week period.
