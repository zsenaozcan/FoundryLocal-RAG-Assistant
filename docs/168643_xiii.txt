Title: XIII (2008)
Genres: Action, Crime, Mystery
Director: Duane Clark
Main Cast: Stephen Dorff, Caterina Murino, Val Kilmer, Greg Bryk, Stephen McHattie
Keywords: amnesia, false identity, plastic surgery, based on comic, conspiracy, man hunt, miniseries, torture, based on graphic novel, multiple identities

Overview: The first female US President Sally Sheridan is shot dead by a sniper during her Veterans Day speech. Her assassin narrowly escapes the scene with his life, national security hot on his heels - or so it seems. Three months later, an elderly couple discover the body of a wounded man in a tree, wearing a parachute. The young man cannot remember the slightest thing about his own identity.
