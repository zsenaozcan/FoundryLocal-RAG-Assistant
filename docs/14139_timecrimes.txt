Title: Timecrimes (2007)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Nacho Vigalondo
Main Cast: Karra Elejalde, Candela Fernández, Bárbara Goenaga, Nacho Vigalondo, Juan Inciarte
Keywords: scissors, radio, nudity, time, woods, surrealism, travel, independent film, scientist, machine

Overview: A man accidentally gets into a time machine and travels back in time nearly an hour. Finding himself will be the first of a series of disasters of unforeseeable consequences.
