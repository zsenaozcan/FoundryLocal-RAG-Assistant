Title: Maid of Salem (1937)
Genres: Drama
Director: Frank Lloyd
Main Cast: Claudette Colbert, Fred MacMurray, Harvey Stephens, Gale Sondergaard, Louise Dresser
Keywords: puritan, tragic love, witch hunt, burned at the stake

Overview: Young lovers fall afoul of repressive society as Salem elders get caught up in the witch hunts and trials of 17th century Massachusetts.
