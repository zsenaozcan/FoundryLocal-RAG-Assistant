Title: Die Wolke (2006)
Genres: Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Gregor Schnitzler
Main Cast: Paula Kalenberg, Franz Dinda, Hans-Laurin Beyerling, Carina N. Wiese, Jennifer Ulrich
Keywords: nuclear power plant, disaster, nuclear catastrophe

Overview: DIE WOLKE (The Cloud) is about a breakdown of a nuclear power station in Germany and the story of two teenager-lovers Hannah and Elmar who take refuge. 38.000 people die and Hannah unfortunately becomes contaminated.
