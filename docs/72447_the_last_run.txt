Title: The Last Run (1971)
Genres: Action, Drama, Crime
Director: Richard Fleischer
Main Cast: George C. Scott, Tony Musante, Trish Van Devere, Colleen Dewhurst, Aldo Sambrell
Keywords: portugal, escape, automobile, pursuit, neo-noir

Overview: A former getaway driver from Chicago has retired to a peaceful life in a Portugese fishing village. He is asked to pull off one last job, involving driving a dangerous crook and his girl-friend to France.
