Title: Nola (2003)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Alan Hruska
Main Cast: Emmy Rossum, Steven Bauer, James Badge Dale, Thom Christopher, Sam Coppola

Overview: An urban fairy tale-romantic comedy, in which Nola, an aspiring songwriter, leaves an abusive Kansas home and journeys to New York to find her biological father. Once there, she finds more than she expected.
