Title: The Snow White Murder Case (2014)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Yoshihiro Nakamura
Main Cast: Mao Inoue, Go Ayano, Misako Renbutsu, Nanao, Shihori Kanjiya
Keywords: planned murder, mystery, co-worker, social media

Overview: Beautiful Noriko Miki works at a cosmetic company. One day, Noriko is murdered. People begin to suspect her co-worker Miki Shirono might have killed her. Television begins to cover her story. A TV show interviews Miki Shirono's colleagues, family, people from her hometown. Rumors turn into fear. Is she a wicked a woman?
