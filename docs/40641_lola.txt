Title: Lola (1961)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Jacques Demy
Main Cast: Anouk Aimée, Marc Michel, Jacques Harden, Alan Scott, Elina Labourdette
Keywords: nouvelle vague

Overview: Lola, is a 1961 film, the debut film directed by Jacques Demy as a tribute to director Max Ophüls and is described by Demy as a "musical without music". Anouk Aimée starred in the title role. The film was restored and re-released by Demy's widow, French filmmaker Agnès Varda.
