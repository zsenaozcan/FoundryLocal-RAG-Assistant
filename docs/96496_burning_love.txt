Title: Burning Love (1983)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Ate de Jong
Main Cast: Monique van de Ven, Peter Jan Rens, Ellen Vogel, Huub van der Lubbe

Overview: A young painter takes up French lessons with an elder lady to ensure he’ll get a grant for a French arts institute. That way he meets Anna, a beautiful married woman nursing the lady’s old father, and falls in love with her.
