Title: To the Devil a Daughter (1976)
Genres: Horror
Director: Peter Sykes
Main Cast: Richard Widmark, Christopher Lee, Honor Blackman, Denholm Elliott, Michael Goodliffe
Keywords: satanism, ritual, satanist, pact with the devil, supernatural, cult, writer, demon, hammer horror, occult

Overview: An American occult novelist battles to save the soul of a young girl from a group of Satanists, led by an excommunicated priest, who plan on using her as the representative of the Devil on Earth.
