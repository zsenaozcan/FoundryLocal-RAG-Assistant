Title: Tovarich (1937)
Genres: Romance, Comedy, Drama
Director: Anatole Litvak
Main Cast: Claudette Colbert, Charles Boyer, Basil Rathbone, Anita Louise, Melville Cooper
Keywords: butler, russian, family relationships

Overview: Ousted from their homeland by the Bolshevik revolution, a royal Russian couple find themselves impoverished and living in Paris. They take positions as butler and housemaid in a wealthy household and, owing to their impeccable breeding and manners, excel in their new jobs. But once they are recognized for the royal couple they are, they must face new -- and formidable -- responsibilities.
