Title: Reason, Debate and a Story (1974)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ritwik Ghatak
Main Cast: Tripti Mitra, Shaonli Mitra, Sugata Burman, Bijon Bhattacharya, Ranen Ray Choudhury
Keywords: society

Overview: Four men, each peculiar in his own way, embark on a quest to reason with the estranged wife of the protagonist.
