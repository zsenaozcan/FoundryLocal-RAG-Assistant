Title: Floods of Fear (1958)
Genres: Adventure, Action, Thriller
Director: Charles Crichton
Main Cast: Howard Keel, Anne Heywood, Cyril Cusack, Harry H. Corbett, John Crawford
Keywords: flooding

Overview: A man (Howard Keel) framed for murder escapes from prison during a flood and helps a young woman (Anne Heywood) in distress.
