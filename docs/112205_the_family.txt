Title: The Family (2013)
Genres: Crime, Comedy, Action
Director: Luc Besson
Main Cast: Robert De Niro, Michelle Pfeiffer, Dianna Agron, John D'Leo, Tommy Lee Jones
Keywords: france, witness protection, mafia, mafia family

Overview: The Manzoni family, a notorious mafia clan, is relocated to Normandy, France under the witness protection program, where fitting in soon becomes challenging as their old habits die hard.
