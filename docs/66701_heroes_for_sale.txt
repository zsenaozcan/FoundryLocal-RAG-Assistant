Title: Heroes for Sale (1933)
Genres: Drama
Director: William A. Wellman
Main Cast: Richard Barthelmess, Aline MacMahon, Loretta Young, Gordon Westcott, Robert Barrat
Keywords: drug addiction, invention, great depression, unrequited love, tragedy, prison sentence, war hero, bank clerk, soup kitchen, incarceration

Overview: The saga of honorable man Tom Holmes, whose Great War heroics earn fame and a medal for someone else, and his wounds result in morphine addiction and a string of challenges and tragedies.
