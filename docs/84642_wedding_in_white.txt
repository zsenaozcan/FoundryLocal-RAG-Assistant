Title: Wedding in White (1972)
Genres: Drama
Director: William Fruet
Main Cast: Carol Kane, Donald Pleasence, Doris Petrie, Leo Phillips, Christine Thomas
Keywords: rape, world war ii, dysfunctional family, independent film, pregnant, teenager, pregnancy, father daughter relationship, teenage pregnancy

Overview: A young girl's brother comes home from the army, and brings an army buddy with him. The three of them go out that night to celebrate, and after much drinking has been done, the brother's friend rapes the sister. After the two men have gone back, the girl finds that she's pregnant--and discovers that her parents don't blame the soldier, but blame her.
