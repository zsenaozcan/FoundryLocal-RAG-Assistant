Title: The Lottery (2010)
Genres: Documentary, Thriller
Director: Madeleine Sackler
Main Cast: Cory Booker, Geoffrey Canada
Keywords: father son relationship, independent film, charter schools, public school, hand clapping game, protestor, pta, woman director

Overview: Four children enter a high-stakes lottery. If they win, they can attend one of the best schools in New York. A look at the crisis in public education, The Lottery makes the case than any child can succeed.
