Title: The Blonde from Peking (1967)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Crime, Thriller
Director: Nicolas Gessner
Main Cast: Claudio Brook, Mireille Darc, Françoise Brion, Jean-Jacques Delbo, Edward G. Robinson
Keywords: based on novel, amnesia, detective agency

Overview: A CIA man poses an actor as the husband of an amnesiac, all for Chinese missile data and a jewel.
