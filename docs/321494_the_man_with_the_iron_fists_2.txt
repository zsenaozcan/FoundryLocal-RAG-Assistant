Title: The Man with the Iron Fists 2 (2015)
Genres: Action
Director: Roel Reiné
Main Cast: RZA, Dustin Nguyen, Eugenia Yuan, Pim Bubear, Cary-Hiroyuki Tagawa
Keywords: martial arts, sequel, revenge, slave

Overview: When a stranger, Thaddeus, is found badly wounded near the village, miner Li Kung and his wife Ah Ni offer him refuge. As he heals, he becomes entrenched in a conflict that pits the townsfolk against the evil Master Ho, his nefarious Beetle Clan and the terrifying Lord Pi.
