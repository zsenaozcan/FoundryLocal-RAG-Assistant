Title: Lonely Are the Brave (1962)
Genres: Drama, Western
Director: David Miller
Main Cast: Kirk Douglas, Gena Rowlands, Walter Matthau, Carroll O'Connor, Michael Kane
Keywords: loyalty, modern society, male friendship, independence, jail, escape, fugitive

Overview: A fiercely independent cowboy arranges to have himself locked up in jail in order to then escape with an old friend who has been sentenced to the penitentiary.
