Title: Mafalda - La Pelicula (1981)
Genres: Fantasy, Animation, Comedy, Family
Director: Carlos Márquez
Main Cast: Susana Klein, Cecilia Gispert, Nelly Hering, Oscar Silva

Overview: Mafalda is a comic strip written and drawn by Argentine cartoonist Joaquín Salvador Lavado, better known by his pen name Quino. The strip features a 6-year-old girl named Mafalda, who is deeply concerned about humanity and world peace and rebels against the current state of the world. The strip ran from 1964 to 1973 and was very popular in Latin America, Europe, Quebec, and in Asia, leading to two animated cartoon series and a movie.
