Title: Savage Beasts (1984)
Genres: Action, Adventure, Horror
Director: Franco Prosperi
Main Cast: Lorraine De Selle, John Aldrich, Ugo Bologna, Louisa Lloyd, Enzo Pezzu
Keywords: fire, zoo, pollution, attack, drug, animal, animal attack, pcp, animals gone wild

Overview: PCP is unexplainably released into the Frankfurt water supply and sends the zoo inhabitants crazy. One evening after a malfunction of zoo security the gonzo critters rampage through the city eating and killing whatever they fancy, wreaking a night of bloody terror. It's up to regular Italian-trash whipping girl Lorraine De Selle and obnoxious Super Mario lookalike zookeeper John Aldrich to sort it out.
