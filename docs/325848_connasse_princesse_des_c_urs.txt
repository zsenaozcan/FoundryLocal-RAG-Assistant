Title: Connasse, Princesse des cœurs (2015)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Noémie Saglio
Main Cast: Camille Cottin, Antony Hickling, Lou Cottin, Cécile Boland, Stéphane Bern
Keywords: hidden camera, social class, social climbing

Overview: Thirty-year-old Camilla, born a bitch, realizes that she doesn't have the life she deserves and decides that the only destiny worthy of her is that of a royal princess.
