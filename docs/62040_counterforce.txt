Title: CounterForce (1998)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Martin Kunert
Main Cast: Michael Rooker, Robert Patrick, Louis Mandylor, Diane DiLascio, James Kisicki

Overview: An FBI Agent (Michael Rooker) and a female cop (Diane DiLascio) join forces to stop renegade cops who are offing the bad guys. Robert Patrick is the leader of the renegades and Louis Mandylor is a reluctant young cop that is brought into the hit team.
