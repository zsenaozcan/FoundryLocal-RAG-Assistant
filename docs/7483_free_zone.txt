Title: Free Zone (2005)
Genres: Drama
Director: Amos Gitai
Main Cast: Natalie Portman, Hana Laslo, Hiam Abbass, Carmen Maura, Makram Khoury
Keywords: terror, israel, car journey, middle east conflict, war, jordan, border crossing, break-up, road movie

Overview: Rebecca, an American who has been living in Jerusalem for a few months now, has just broken off her engagement. She gets into a cab driven by Hanna, an Israeli. But Hanna is on her way to Jordan, to the Free Zone, to pick up a large of sum of money.
