Title: Ingmar Bergman Makes a Movie (1963)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Vilgot Sjöman
Main Cast: Ingmar Bergman, Vilgot Sjöman, Sven Nykvist, Gunnar Björnstrand, Ingrid Thulin
Keywords: documentary, ingmar bergman

Overview: Vilgot Sjöman (I Am Curious -- Yellow 1967) and a crew from Swedish Television followed Ingmar Bergman during the filming of Winter Light and came away with a five-part documentary, including set construction, rehearsals, editing, and behind-the-camera conversations with Bergman and the cast and crew, and audience reactions to the film.
