Title: Afflicted (2014)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Derek Lee
Main Cast: Baya Rehaz, Derek Lee, Clif Prowse, Edo van Breemen, Zachary Gray
Keywords: vampire, found footage

Overview: Two friends' tour of Europe takes a dark turn when one of them contracts a mysterious illness. They race to find out what it is and how to cure it before the sickness consumes him completely.
