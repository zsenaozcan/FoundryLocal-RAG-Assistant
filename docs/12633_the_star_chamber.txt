Title: The Star Chamber (1983)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Peter Hyams
Main Cast: Michael Douglas, Hal Holbrook, Yaphet Kotto, Sharon Gless, Michael Ensign
Keywords: judge, hitman, secret society, mission of murder, delinquency, suspense, serial killer

Overview: Disgusted with criminals escaping the judicial system via technicalities, an idealistic young judge investigates an alternative method for punishing the guilty.
