Title: Pathetic Fallacy (1958)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ritwik Ghatak
Main Cast: Kali Bannerjee, Gangapada Basu, Satindra Bhattacharya, Tulsi Chakraborty, Anil Chatterjee
Keywords: taxi, india

Overview: Bimal is a taxi-driver in a small provincial town. He lives alone, his taxi (an old 1920 Chevrolet jalopy which he named Jagaddal) is his only companion and, although very battered, it is the apple of Bimal's eye.
