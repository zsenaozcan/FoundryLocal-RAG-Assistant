Title: The Deaths of Ian Stone (2007)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Dario Piana
Main Cast: Mike Vogel, Michael Dixon, Christina Cole, Michael Feast, Jaime Murray
Keywords: ice hockey, hockey player, suspense, parallel world

Overview: Deaths tells the story of an all-American guy who is murdered each day by horrifying pursuers, only to wake up in slightly different lives to experience the terror of being murdered again.
