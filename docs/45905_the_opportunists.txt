Title: The Opportunists (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Crime, Drama, Romance
Director: Myles Connell
Main Cast: Christopher Walken, Peter McDonald, Kate Burton, Anne Pitoniak, Jim Mayzik

Overview: Vic is a struggling auto mechanic with a safe-cracking past and a lot of debt. His girlfriend runs a bar and offers to loan him the money she's saved for remodeling, but Vic is reluctant to take it. When a long-lost cousin from Ireland shows up on his doorstep, the two team up for one last heist.
