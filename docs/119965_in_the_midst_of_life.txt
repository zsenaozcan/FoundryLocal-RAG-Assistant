Title: In the Midst of Life (1963)
Genres: Drama, War
Director: Robert Enrico
Main Cast: Roger Jacquet, Anne Cornaly, Stéphane Fey, Pierre Boffety, Frédérique Ruchaud

Overview: This Civil War anthology adapts three Ambrose Bierce stories "An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge," "Chickamauga" and "The Mockingbird."
