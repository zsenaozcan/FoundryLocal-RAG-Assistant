Title: The Double (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Richard Ayoade
Main Cast: Jesse Eisenberg, Mia Wasikowska, Wallace Shawn, Yasmin Paige, Noah Taylor
Keywords: paranoia, double

Overview: An awkward office drone becomes increasingly unhinged after a charismatic and confident look-alike takes a job at his workplace and seduces the woman he desires.
