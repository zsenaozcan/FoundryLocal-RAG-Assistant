Title: 9/11: The Falling Man (2006)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Henry Singer
Main Cast: Steven Mackintosh
Keywords: september 11 2001, world trade center

Overview: An examination of an image - a falling man from the North Tower, frozen in mid air - circulated by the press immediately after the September 11 attacks, the public's reaction, and why it was later deemed un-newsworthy.
