Title: Bombay Velvet (2015)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Anurag Kashyap
Main Cast: Anushka Sharma, Ranbir Kapoor, Karan Johar, Vivaan Shah, Kay Kay Menon
Keywords: jazz, boxer, noir, organized crime, bollywood, gangster

Overview: An ordinary man, who goes against all odds to forge his own destiny and become a star in the glittering sky of 'Bombay,' thriving on jazz and violence.
