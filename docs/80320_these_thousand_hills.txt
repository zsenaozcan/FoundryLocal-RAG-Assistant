Title: These Thousand Hills (1959)
Genres: Drama, Western
Director: Richard Fleischer
Main Cast: Don Murray, Richard Egan, Lee Remick, Patricia Owens, Stuart Whitman
Keywords: fistfight, friendship, lynch mob

Overview: A cowboy (Don Murray) tries for easy money with his partner, then tries ranching with a saloon hostess's (Lee Remick) money.
