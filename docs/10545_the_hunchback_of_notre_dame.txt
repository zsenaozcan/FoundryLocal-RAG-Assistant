Title: The Hunchback of Notre Dame (1996)
Genres: Drama, Animation, Family
Director: Gary Trousdale
Main Cast: Tom Hulce, Demi Moore, Tony Jay, Kevin Kline, Paul Kandel
Keywords: paris, based on novel, judge, obsession, dance, sword, mockery, ugliness, cathedral, musical
