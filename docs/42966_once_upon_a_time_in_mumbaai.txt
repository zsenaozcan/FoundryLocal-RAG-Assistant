Title: Once Upon a Time in Mumbaai (2010)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime
Director: Milan Luthria
Main Cast: Ajay Devgn, Emraan Hashmi, Kangana Ranaut, Prachi Desai, Randeep Hooda
Keywords: bombay india, criminal past, crime boss, mafia, india

Overview: A tale of two gangsters from the eras of past and present, whose lives enter parallel paths as they struggle to survive within Bombay's criminal underworld.
