Title: A Christmas Memory (1997)
Genres: TV Movie, Drama, Family
Director: Glenn A. Jordan
Main Cast: Patty Duke, Piper Laurie, Jeffrey DeMunn, Anita Gillette

Overview: A boy, Buddy, whose parents have split and whose mother is an actress in New York, has been dumped in the south at the small-town home of some older cousins, all of whom are unmarried.
