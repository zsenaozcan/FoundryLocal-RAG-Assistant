Title: The Mad Miss Manton (1938)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Crime
Director: Leigh Jason
Main Cast: Barbara Stanwyck, Henry Fonda, Sam Levene, Frances Mercer, Hattie McDaniel
Keywords: suspect, socialite, prankster

Overview: When the murdered body discovered by beautiful, vivacious socialite Melsa Manton disappears, police and press label her a prankster until she proves them wrong.
