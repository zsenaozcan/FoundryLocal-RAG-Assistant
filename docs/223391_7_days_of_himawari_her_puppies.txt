Title: 7 Days of Himawari & Her Puppies (2013)
Genres: Drama
Director: Emiko Hiramatsu
Main Cast: Masato Sakai, Miki Nakatani, Rei Dan, Kanata Fujimoto, Denden
Keywords: animal rights

Overview: Set within an animal shelter, where if the owner of an animal doesn't claim their pet in 7 days, the animal is put to sleep. Himawari, is a female dog and a mother. She tries to desperately save her baby dogs.
