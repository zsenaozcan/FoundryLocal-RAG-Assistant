Title: Man of a Thousand Faces (1957)
Genres: Drama
Director: Joseph Pevney
Main Cast: James Cagney, Dorothy Malone, Jane Greer, Marjorie Rambeau, Jim Backus
Keywords: biography, historical figure

Overview: The life and career of vaudevillian and silent screen horror star Lon Chaney, his contentious relationship with his neurotic wife, and his premature death.
