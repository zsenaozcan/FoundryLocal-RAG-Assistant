Title: Kitchen Party (1997)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Gary Burns
Main Cast: Scott Speedman, Tygh Runyan, Laura Harris, Sarah Strange

Overview: A group of teenagers come together to indulge in that great Canadian pastime, the house-party, and find themselves literally confined to the kitchen trapped by an extraordinary well manicured carpet in the living room. Elsewhere, the parents of these kids have a dinner party of their own, where suburban pretense slowly melts away exposing childish obsessions.
