Title: The Abominable Dr. Phibes (1971)
Genres: Comedy, Horror
Director: Robert Fuest
Main Cast: Vincent Price, Joseph Cotten, Virginia North, Terry-Thomas, Sean Bury
Keywords: life and death, scotland yard, loss of lover, mask, pest, wife, organ, inspector, revenge, murder

Overview: Famous organist Anton Phibes is horribly disfigured in an automobile accident while rushing to the side of his sick wife and presumed to be dead. Once Phibes learns that his wife died on the operating table, he is convinced the doctor's are responsible and begins exacting his revenge on all those involved.
