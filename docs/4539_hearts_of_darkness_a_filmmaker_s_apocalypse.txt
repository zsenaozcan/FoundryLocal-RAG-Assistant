Title: Hearts of Darkness: A Filmmaker's Apocalypse (1991)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Fax Bahr
Main Cast: Francis Ford Coppola, Eleanor Coppola, John Milius, George Lucas, Sam Bottoms
Keywords: philippines, behind the scenes, movie studio, madness, ego, making of, life imitates art, woman director, typhoon

Overview: A chronicle of the production problems — including bad weather, actors' health, war near the filming locations, and more — which plagued the filming of Apocalypse Now, increasing costs and nearly destroying the life and career of Francis Ford Coppola.
