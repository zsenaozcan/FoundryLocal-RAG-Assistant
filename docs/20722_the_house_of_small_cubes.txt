Title: The House of Small Cubes (2008)
Genres: Animation, Drama, Family
Director: Kunio Katō
Main Cast: 长泽雅美
Keywords: oscar award, water, house, loneliness, scuba diving, memory, old man, flood, brick, short

Overview: As his town is flooded by water, an old man builds his home higher and higher to stay dry. When his pipe falls through the water, he has to revisit each layer of memories to retrieve it.
