Title: Zombie Reanimation (2009)
Genres: Horror
Director: Thomas Kercmar
Main Cast: Tasso Mintopoulos, Erich Amerkamp, Andreas Resing, Manoush, Silke Barenbrock
Keywords: zombie

Overview: It all started with the first Zombie infection in the year 1945. Two fanatic doctors accidentally discover the virus that causes the dead to walk again. However, they quickly realize the undead can not be controlled. Their appetite for human flesh is too strong to allow conscience.
 Many years later the two scientists start a new experiment, leaving behind a trail of blood...
