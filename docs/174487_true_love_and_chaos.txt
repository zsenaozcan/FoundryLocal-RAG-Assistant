Title: True Love and Chaos (1997)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Stavros Kazantzidis
Main Cast: Naveen Andrews, Kimberly Davies, Ben Mendelsohn, Miranda Otto, Noah Taylor
Keywords: drug, psychotic, perth

Overview: Hanif and Dean steal a cache of drugs from Dean's psychotic brother Jerry, and at the last minute get a lift with Mimi as she decides to drive to Perth. They pick up a drunken singer, Morris, as they cross the country, chased by Jerry. Written by Simon Bastin
