Title: Valley of Flowers (2006)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Romance
Director: Pan Nalin
Main Cast: Mylène Jampanoï, Milind Soman, Naseeruddin Shah, Jampa Kalsang Tamang, Anil Yadav
Keywords: himalaya, robber, love of one's life

Overview: A Himalayan legend of a love struggling against the inevitability of death, an astonishing tale spanning from the early 19th century mountain existence, to hectic, bustling modern day Tokyo.
