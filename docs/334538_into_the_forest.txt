Title: Into the Forest (2016)
Genres: Drama, Science Fiction
Director: Patricia Rozema
Main Cast: Ellen Page, Evan Rachel Wood, Max Minghella, Callum Keith Rennie, Michael Eklund
Keywords: female nudity, rape, fire, dancer, based on novel, baby, sister sister relationship, nudity, dystopia, forest
