Title: A Grand Night In: The Story of Aardman (2015)
Genres: Animation, Documentary
Director: Richard Mears
Main Cast: Martin Freeman, David Tennant, Julie Walters, Hugh Grant, Bill Nighy
Keywords: stop motion, animation, making of

Overview: Julie Walters tells the story of how Morph, Shaun the Sheep and that cheese-loving man Wallace and his dog Gromit first came to life.
