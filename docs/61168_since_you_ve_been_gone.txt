Title: Since You've Been Gone (1998)
Genres: TV Movie, Comedy, Romance
Director: David Schwimmer
Main Cast: Lara Flynn Boyle, Teri Hatcher, David Schwimmer, Joey Slotnick, Harry Lennix

Overview: The story of a 10th anniversary High School reunion, told through the eyes of a doctor who was humiliated on graduation day by being badly beaten up by a fellow graduate.
