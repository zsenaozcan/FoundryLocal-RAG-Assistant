Title: Pure Country (1992)
Genres: Drama, Music, Romance
Director: Christopher Cain
Main Cast: George Strait, Lesley Ann Warren, Isabel Glasser, Kyle Chandler, John Doe
Keywords: country music, rodeo

Overview: Dusty Chandler (Strait) is a super star in the country music world, but his shows have the style of a '70s rock concert. One day he takes a walk - out of his overdone concerts to find his real country roots. He's helped and hindered by friends and staff, but pushes on in his search for a real music style as well as a real romance.
