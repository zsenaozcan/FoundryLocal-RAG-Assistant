Title: Johan Falk: Barninfiltratören (2012)
Genres: Action, Crime, Thriller
Director: Richard Holm
Main Cast: Jakob Eklund, Joel Kinnaman, Jens Hultén, Mikael Tornving, Meliz Karlge

Overview: A group of young robbers, specializing in robbing shopping center security guards, are raging through out Gothenburg. A robbery goes awry when an innocent passerby is killed. Ricky, the gang's young leader, tries to quickly find an expert who can clean the money of the dye pack that exploded while Johan Falk, and his colleagues at GSI, accelerate their hunt.
