Title: Lisa Lampanelli: Take It Like a Man (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Michael Drumm
Main Cast: Lisa Lampanelli
Keywords: comedian, stand-up comedy, live comedy, dark comedy

Overview: Called "Comedy's Lovable Queen of Mean" (New York Times) and "Howard Stern on estrogen" (Toronto Star), Lisa Lampanelli is the insult comic for a new generation. Now, with a onehour special airing on Comedy Central, she makes her major-label debut with that same outrageous, in-your-face performance on CD and DVD with Take It Like A Man, recorded live at The Improv in Hollywood, FL. Shocking and hilarious, no-holds-barred and politically incorrect, Lampanelli is Don Rickles with breasts and major PMS.
