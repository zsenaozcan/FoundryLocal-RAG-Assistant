Title: Under New Management (2009)
Genres: Drama, Action, Comedy, Thriller, Crime, Romance
Director: Joe Otting
Main Cast: Chris Diamantopoulos, Kelly Overton, Richard Portnow, Christian Stolte, Tony Ray Rossi

Overview: Sparks and bullets fly when a Wall Street yuppie must steer a Brooklyn mob crew legit before a beautiful FBI agent can build RICO indictments against them.
