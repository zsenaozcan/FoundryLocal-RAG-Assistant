Title: Big Fan (2009)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Robert D. Siegel
Main Cast: Patton Oswalt, Kevin Corrigan, Michael Rapaport, Marcia Jean Kurtz, Serafina Fiore
Keywords: gun, loser, sport, beating, diner, hospital, obsessed fan, sports bar, singing in a car, new york giants football

Overview: Paul Aufiero, a 35-year-old parking-garage attendant from Staten Island, is the self-described "world's biggest New York Giants fan". One night, Paul and his best friend Sal spot Giants star linebacker Quantrell Bishop at a gas station and decide to follow him. At a strip club Paul cautiously decides to approach him but the chance encounter brings Paul's world crashing down around him.
