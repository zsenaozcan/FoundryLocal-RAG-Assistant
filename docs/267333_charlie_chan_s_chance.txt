Title: Charlie Chan's Chance (1932)
Genres: Mystery
Director: John G. Blystone
Main Cast: Warner Oland, Alexander Kirkland, H.B. Warner, Marian Nixon, Marian Nixon
Keywords: charlie chan

Overview: Charlie is the intended murder victim here, and he avoids death only by chance. To find the murderer (since, of course, murder does occur), Charlie must outguess Scotland Yard and New York City police.
