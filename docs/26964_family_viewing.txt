Title: Family Viewing (1987)
Genres: Drama
Director: Atom Egoyan
Main Cast: David Hemblen, Aidan Tierney, Gabrielle Rose, Selma Keklikian, Jeanne Sabourin
Keywords: father son relationship, hotel, grandmother grandson relationship, videoband, video surveillance, dysfunctional family

Overview: An emotionally constrained view of the displacement of human feelings in our video saturated society. Van regularly visits his grandmother in a run-down nursing home. His father depends on phone sex for guidance meanwhile erasing family homevideos of happier times with homemade pornography. Will Van rescue his grandmother and memories of his mother in time?
