Title: Arcane Sorcerer (1996)
Genres: Foreign, Horror
Director: Pupi Avati
Main Cast: Carlo Cecchi, Stefano Dionisi, Arnaldo Ninchi, Andrea Scorzoni, Consuelo Ferrara
Keywords: italian, magic, sorcery, horror, priest, sorcerer, gothic

Overview: Shunned by his church, a seminary student (Stefano Dionisi) takes refuge with an excommunicated priest (Carlo Cecchi) who teaches him wizardry and black magic.
