Title: KISS Meets the Phantom of the Park (1978)
Genres: Music, Science Fiction, TV Movie
Director: Gordon Hessler
Main Cast: Paul Stanley, Gene Simmons, Paul Daniel Frehley, Peter Criss, Anthony Zerbe
Keywords: kiss, robot, amusement park

Overview: The tale of rock band KISS and their efforts to thwart a diabolical plan by mad scientist Abner Devereaux. Devereaux has found a way to clone humans into robots in his laboratory at an amusement park. It just so happens that he plans to uses the KISS concert as a platform to unleash his plan on the world. KISS must use their special powers to stop him.
