Title: Pope Joan (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Sönke Wortmann
Main Cast: John Goodman, Johanna Wokalek, David Wenham, Iain Glen, Edward Petherbridge
Keywords: germany, vatican, pope, empowerment

Overview: A 9th century woman of English extraction born in the German city of Ingelheim disguises herself as a man and rises through the Vatican ranks.
