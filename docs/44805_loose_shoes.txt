Title: Loose Shoes (1980)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ira Miller
Main Cast: Bill Murray, Buddy Hackett, Sid Haig, Howard Hesseman, Harry Shearer

Overview: Broad satire and buffoonery presented as a series of movie trailers. Among the titles and subjects are: "The Howard Huge Story", "Skate-boarders from Hell", "The Invasion of the Penis Snatchers", Woody Allen (pre-Mia), movie trailer come-ons, Charlie Chaplin, war movies, Billy Jack. The source of the title is presented about an hour into the film.
