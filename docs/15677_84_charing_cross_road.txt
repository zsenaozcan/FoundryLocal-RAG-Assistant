Title: 84 Charing Cross Road (1987)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: David Hugh Jones
Main Cast: Anne Bancroft, Anthony Hopkins, Judi Dench, Jean De Baer, Maurice Denham

Overview: When a humorous script-reader in her New York apartment sees an ad in the Saturday Review of Literature for a bookstore in London that does mail order, she begins a very special correspondence and friendship with Frank Doel, the bookseller who works at Marks &amp; Co., 84 Charing Cross Road.
