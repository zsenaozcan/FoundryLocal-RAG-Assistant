Title: Bridegroom (2013)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Linda Bloodworth-Thomason
Main Cast: Thomas Bridegroom, Shane Bitney Crone, Cindy Bitney, David Crone, Lizzy Mohl
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Bridegroom gives an intensely personal edge to the ongoing debate over the legal rights of same-sex couples. Interviews, photos and video footage all testify to the uncommon connection that drew together Shane and Tom. For six years they remained united despite extreme challenges from both family and society, until a tragic accident tears apart their dreams. Now one must fight to be recognized as his soulmate’s legitimate counterpart.
