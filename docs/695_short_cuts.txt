Title: Short Cuts (1993)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Robert Altman
Main Cast: Julianne Moore, Andie MacDowell, Tim Robbins, Jack Lemmon, Anne Archer
Keywords: suicide, loss of son, helicopter, cheating, fishing, daily life, jazz musician, modern society, cellist, earthquake

Overview: Multi-storied, fish-eyed look at American culture with some 22 characters intersecting--profoundly or fleetingly--through each other's lives. Running the emotional gamut from disturbing to humorous, Altman's portrait of the contemporary human condition is nevertheless fascinating. Based on nine stories and a prose poem by Raymond Carver.
