Title: Skin, Skin (1966)
Genres: Drama
Director: Mikko Niskanen
Main Cast: Kristiina Halkola, Kirsti Wallasvaara, Pekka Autiovuori, Eero Melasniemi, Anneli Sauli

Overview: Mikko Niskanen's famous new wave film about the '60s urban intellectuals who get faced in the Finnish countryside with loads of beer.
