Title: All for the Winner (1990)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Corey Yuen Kwai
Main Cast: Stephen Chow, Sharla Cheung Man, Ng Man-Tat, Sandra Ng Kwun-Yu, Paul Chun Pui

Overview: Sing, a dumb, lovable mainlander with supernatural powers comes to China to visit his uncle Tat. When it's revealed that Sing can see through objects, Tat employs him as "The Saint of Gamblers," and proceeds to set him loose in the gambling world.
