Title: The Dovekeepers (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Yves Simoneau
Main Cast: Cote de Pablo, Rachel Brosnahan, Kathryn Prescott, Diego Boneta, Jonas Armstrong
Keywords: miniseries

Overview: This four-hour miniseries is based on Alice Hoffman’s acclaimed historical novel about four extraordinary women whose lives intersect in a fight for survival at the siege of Masada.
