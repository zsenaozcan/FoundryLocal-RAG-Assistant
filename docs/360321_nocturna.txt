Title: Nocturna (2015)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Buz Alexander
Main Cast: Mike Doyle, Estella Warren, Massimo Dobrovic, Johnathon Schaech, Billy Blair

Overview: Two New Orleans detectives become embroiled in a centuries-long feud between two secretive factions of vampires while investigating a runaway child's case. Torn between their everyday lives and the dangerous lure of immortality, the detectives must race to destroy the evil Moldero clan.
