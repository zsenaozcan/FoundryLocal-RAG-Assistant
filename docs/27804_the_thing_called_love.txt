Title: The Thing Called Love (1993)
Genres: Music, Drama, Romance
Director: Peter Bogdanovich
Main Cast: River Phoenix, Samantha Mathis, Dermot Mulroney, Sandra Bullock, Anthony Clark
Keywords: country music

Overview: Miranda Presley moves from New York to Nashville to become a song writer. At an unsuccessful audition she meets James Wright a promising newcomer. After only a few days they marry but start to quickly regret it. This is River Phoenix's last movie and you can see the end coming...
