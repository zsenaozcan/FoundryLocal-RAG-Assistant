Title: Chained for Life (1952)
Genres: Drama
Director: Harry L. Fraser
Main Cast: Violet Hilton, Daisy Hilton, Mario Laval, Allen Jenkins, Patricia Wright
Keywords: siamese twins

Overview: A Siamese twin kills the husband who left her. The courts have to decide if she is convicted of murder, how can they punish her sister, who had nothing to do with the crime?
