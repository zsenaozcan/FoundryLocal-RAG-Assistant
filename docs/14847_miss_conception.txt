Title: Miss Conception (2008)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Eric Styles
Main Cast: Heather Graham, Mia Kirshner, Tom Ellis, Will Mellor, Orlando Seale
Keywords: independent film

Overview: Georgina is an ambitious young London professional who learns she has only one month left in which to conceive a child. After exhausting all possibilities with her baby-phobic boyfriend, Georgina turns to her wildly optimistic friend Clem, with whom she sets out to identify and "land" the perfect father for her child.
