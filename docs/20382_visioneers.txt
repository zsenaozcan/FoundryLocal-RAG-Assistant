Title: Visioneers (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Jared Drake
Main Cast: Zach Galifianakis, Judy Greer, Mía Maestro, Missi Pyle, James Le Gros
Keywords: dystopia, independent film

Overview: Visioneer George Washington Winsterhammerman lives a comfortable but monotonous life in this slightly futuristic black comedy. When people start exploding from stress and George is showing early symptoms, he's forced to examine his life. Taking a look at his nice job, his sexless marriage and his resistance to life coaching, George reconsiders the philosophy of happiness through mindless activity.
