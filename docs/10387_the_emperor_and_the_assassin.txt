Title: The Emperor and the Assassin (1998)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Chen Kaige
Main Cast: Gong Li, Zhang Fengyi, Sun Zhou, Xiaohe Lü, Wang Zhi-Wen
Keywords: china, assassin, kingdom, occupying power, governance, concubine

Overview: In pre-unified China, the King of Qin sends his concubine to a rival kingdom to produce an assassin for a political plot, but as the king's cruelty mounts she finds her loyalty faltering.
