Title: Rabid (1977)
Genres: Horror

Main Cast: Marilyn Chambers, Frank Moore, Joe Silver, Howard Ryshpan, Patricia Gage
Keywords: nudity, vampire, montreal, motorcycle crash, murder, motorcycle, surgery, rabies, body horror, canuxploitation
