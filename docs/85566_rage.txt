Title: Rage (2010)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Science Fiction, Foreign
Director: Chris Witherspoon
Main Cast: Audrey Walker, Rick Crawford, Chris Witherspoon, Anna Lodej, Richard Topping
Keywords: biker, confrontation

Overview: A thirty something man who lives in a suburb just outside of Portland says goodbye to his beautiful and loving wife and heads into town. There he unintentionally provokes the wrath of a mysterious motorcyclist. The confrontation between the two, sets in motion a day long battle. Beginning in the form of harmless taunts then quickly escalating into something more serious and then into something unimaginable.
