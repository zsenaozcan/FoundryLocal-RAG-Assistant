Title: RISE (2014)
Genres: Crime, Thriller, Drama
Director: Mack Lindon
Main Cast: Martin Sacks, Jessica Green, Nathan Wilson, Erin Connor, Marty Rhone
Keywords: underdog, rise and fall, wrongful imprisonment

Overview: Rise follows real life events of a young nurse, Will McIntyre,  who has his freedom taken after a one night stand leads to a  charge of rape. Despite his innocence, he is found guilty upon  trial. We are taken into the maximum security prison where  Jimmy Cove, a hardened inmate, infamous for a string of armed  robberies is positively impacted by Will’s courage and  struggle to make sense of it all. Meanwhile, a Queen’s Counsel  Barrister, Julie Nile, is challenged to compromise her status  and wage to prove his innocence.
