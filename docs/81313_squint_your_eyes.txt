Title: Squint Your Eyes (2003)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Andrzej Jakimowski
Main Cast: Zbigniew Zamachowski, Ola Prószynska, Rafal Guzniczak, Małgorzata Foremniak, Andrzej Chyra

Overview: The story of a spirited ten-year-old girl who has run away from her proudly affluent parents in town and finds grudging refuge with the slightly slovenly caretaker of an abandoned farm, an ex-teacher.
