Title: Elves (1989)
Genres: Horror
Director: Jeffrey Mandel
Main Cast: Dan Haggerty, Julie Austin, Deanna Lund, Borah Silver, Mansell Rivers-Bland
Keywords: female nudity, nudity, independent film, electrocution, incest, christmas horror, christmas, elf

Overview: A young woman discovers that she is the focus of an evil nazi experiment involving selective breeding and summoned elves, an attempt to create a race of supermen. She and two of her friends are trapped in a department store with an elf, and only Dan Haggerty, as the renegade loose-cannon Santa Claus, can save them.
