Title: My Ain Folk (1973)
Genres: Drama
Director: Bill Douglas
Main Cast: Stephen Archibald, Hughie Restorick, Jean Taylor Smith, Bernard McKenna, Mr. Munro
Keywords: neglect, autobiographical, child neglect

Overview: When Jamie's maternal grandmother dies, he and his brother Tommy are separated - Tommy is taken off to a welfare home and Jamie goes to live with his other grandmother and uncle. His life is far from happy, filled with silence, rejection and bouts of violence.
