Title: Varsham (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ranjith Sankar
Main Cast: Mammootty, Asha Sarath, Prajwal Prasad, Mamta Mohandas, T. G. Ravindranathan
Keywords: father son relationship, finances, doctor, malayalam, unexpected death

Overview: The film narrates the tale of Venu, owner of a small finance company in Thrissur. His life is shaken up after some unprecedented events leaving him at a dead end. This movie is about how he finds a purpose to live.
