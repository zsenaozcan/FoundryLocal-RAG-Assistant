Title: Echo Planet (2012)
Genres: Adventure, Animation
Director: Kompim Kemgumnird
Main Cast: Nuengthida Sophon, Noppan Chantasorn, Athipich Chutiwatkajornchai, Kongdej Jaturanrasamee, Bailee Madison
Keywords: environmentalism, lost in jungle

Overview: Story of the adventures of three young men from two of the world's metropolis, New State Trinity Capital and Karen village in Northern Thailand. To help save the world from disaster recovery due to global warming.
