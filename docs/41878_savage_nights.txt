Title: Savage Nights (1992)
Genres: Drama
Director: Cyril Collard
Main Cast: Cyril Collard, Romane Bohringer, Carlos López, Corine Blue, Claude Winter

Overview: Jean is young, gay, and promiscuous. Only after he meets one or two women, including Laura does he come to realize his bisexuality. Jean has to overcome a personal crisis and a tough choice between Laura and his male lover Samy.
