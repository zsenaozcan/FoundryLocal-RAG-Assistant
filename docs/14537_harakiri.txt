Title: Harakiri (1962)
Genres: Action, Drama, History
Director: Masaki Kobayashi
Main Cast: Tatsuya Nakadai, Rentarô Mikuni, Shima Iwashita, Akira Ishihama, Yoshio Inaba
Keywords: japan, suicide, asia, samurai, revenge, poverty, seppuku, ronin, flashback, 17th century

Overview: Peace in 17th-century Japan causes the Shogunate's breakup of warrior clans, throwing thousands of samurai out of work and into poverty. An honorable end to such fate under the samurai code is ritual suicide, or hara-kiri.
