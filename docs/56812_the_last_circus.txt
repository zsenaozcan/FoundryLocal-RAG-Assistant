Title: The Last Circus (2010)
Genres: Action, Drama, Thriller
Director: Álex de la Iglesia
Main Cast: Santiago Segura, Antonio de la Torre, Raúl Arévalo, Fran Perea, Carolina Bang
Keywords: male nudity, dancer, militia, nudity, clown, revenge, dark comedy, smashed head, mad, revenge motive

Overview: The journey of Javier, the obese Sad Clown, starts during his childhood in the midst of the Spanish civil war in 1937. His father, one of Spain’s most prominent jesters, is detained and tortured by the fascist regime.
