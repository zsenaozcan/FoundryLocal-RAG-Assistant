Title: Blow Dry (2001)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Paddy Breathnach
Main Cast: Alan Rickman, Josh Hartnett, Natasha Richardson, Rachael Leigh Cook, Rachel Griffiths
Keywords: independent film

Overview: The annual British Hairdressing Championship comes to Keighley, a town where Phil and son Brian run a barbershop and Phil's ex-wife Shelly and her lover Sandra run a beauty salon.
