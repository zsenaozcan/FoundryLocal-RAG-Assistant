Title: Don't Look Back (2009)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Mystery
Director: Marina de Van
Main Cast: Sophie Marceau, Monica Bellucci, Brigitte Catillon, Andrea Di Stefano, Thierry Neuvic
Keywords: car crash, suspense, memory loss, haunted by the past, metaphor, woman director

Overview: Panic attacks and memory loss signal the plight of a writer whose body is inexplicably being taken over by another woman.
