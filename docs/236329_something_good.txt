Title: Something good (2013)
Genres: Thriller, Drama
Director: Luca Barbareschi
Main Cast: Luca Barbareschi, Zhang Jingchu, Carl Ng, Alessandro Haber, Gary Lewis

Overview: In a small village in China's Yunnan region, Xiwen mourns the sudden loss of her beloved, only child, a young boy poisoned by tainted food. Numbed by the pain, she seeks a reason to live.
