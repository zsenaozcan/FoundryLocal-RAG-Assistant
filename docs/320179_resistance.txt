Title: Resistance (2015)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Michael Graziano
Main Cast: Unknown
Keywords: medicine, biology, science, bacterial warfare, bacteria, antibiotics

Overview: Are the medicines and every day products we use putting us at risk RESISTANCE sheds light on the global crisis of antibiotic resistance and uncovers how our extensive use of bacteria-killing antibiotics has created a new kind of disease, resistant to the medicines created to destroy it.
