Title: Cinderella (1914)
Genres: Fantasy, Drama
Director: James Kirkwood
Main Cast: Mary Pickford, Owen Moore, Isabel Vernon, Georgia Wilson, Lucille Carney

Overview: Based on Charles Perrault's fairy tale: Cinderella is mistreated by her stepmother and stepsisters, but she is able to go to the Royal Ball with the help of the Fairy Godmother.
