Title: Any Number Can Win (1963)
Genres: Romance, Crime, Drama
Director: Henri Verneuil
Main Cast: Jean Gabin, Alain Delon, Maurice Biraud, Jean Carmet, Henri Virlojeux
Keywords: casino, thief

Overview: Charles, a sixty-something career criminal, fresh out of jail, rejects his wife's plan for a quiet life of bourgeois respectability. He enlists a former cell mate, Francis, to assist him in pulling off one final score, a carefully planned assault on the vault of a Cannes casino.
