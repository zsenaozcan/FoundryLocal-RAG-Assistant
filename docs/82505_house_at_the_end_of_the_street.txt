Title: House at the End of the Street (2012)
Genres: Horror, Thriller
Director: Mark Tonderai
Main Cast: Jennifer Lawrence, Max Thieriot, Elisabeth Shue, Gil Bellows, Nolan Gerard Funk
Keywords: mother daughter relationship, cross dressing, violence, mother son relationship, father son conflict

Overview: A mother and daughter move to a new town and find themselves living next door to a house where a young girl murdered her parents. When the daughter befriends the surviving son, she learns the story is far from over.
