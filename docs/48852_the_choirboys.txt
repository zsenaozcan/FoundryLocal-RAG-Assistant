Title: The Choirboys (1977)
Genres: Drama, Comedy, Crime
Director: Robert Aldrich
Main Cast: Charles Durning, Louis Gossett, Jr., Perry King, Clyde Kusatsu, Stephen Macht
Keywords: police, dominatrix, cops, los angeles

Overview: A group of Los Angeles cops decide to take off some of the pressures of their jobs by engaging in various forms of after-hours debauchery.
