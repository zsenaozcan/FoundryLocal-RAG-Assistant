Title: Laughter in Paradise (1951)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Mario Zampi
Main Cast: Alastair Sim, Fay Compton, Guy Middleton, George Cole, Hugh Griffith
Keywords: prank, millionaire

Overview: When an eccentric practical joker dies, he divides his fortune among four heirs. But before they can collect the cash they must each do something which goes completely against their nature.
