Title: Chicago (1927)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Crime
Director: Frank Urson
Main Cast: Phyllis Haver, Victor Varconi, Virginia Bradford, Robert Edeson, Eugene Pallette

Overview: Based on a true crime story, the movie is about a wild jazz-loving and boozing wife Roxie Hart who kills her boyfriend in cold blood after he leaves her, and how she finagles her way out being convicted. Remade once as a movie, and as a Broadway musical.
