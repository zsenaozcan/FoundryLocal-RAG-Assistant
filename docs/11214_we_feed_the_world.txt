Title: We Feed the World (2005)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Erwin Wagenhofer
Main Cast: Jean Ziegler, Peter Brabeck, Karl Otrok
Keywords: globalization, brazil, bread, third world, anti-globalisation activist, butcher, alimentation, tomato, industrial society , meat

Overview: A documentary that exposes the shocking truths behind industrial food production and food wastage, focusing on fishing, livestock and crop farming. A must-see for anyone interested in the true cost of the food on their plate.
