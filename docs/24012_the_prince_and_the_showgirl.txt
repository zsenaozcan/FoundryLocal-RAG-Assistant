Title: The Prince and the Showgirl (1957)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Laurence Olivier
Main Cast: Marilyn Monroe, Laurence Olivier, Sybil Thorndike, Richard Wattis, Jeremy Spenser
Keywords: london england, coronation, prince, love, king, monarch

Overview: A saucy American showgirl in London is wooed by a roving-eyed Duke, but his estranged son, the young King, interrupts their late supper with politics and angry accusations.
