Title: The Last Gladiators (2012)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Alex Gibney
Main Cast: Donald Brashear, Marty McSorley, Chris Nilan, Paul Schantz
Keywords: sport, ice hockey

Overview: Exploring the rough and tumble world of hockey, Academy Award winner Alex Gibney ("Taxi to the Dark Side") looks at the world of the NHL enforcers and specifically the career of Chris "Knuckles" Nilan who helped the Montreal Canadiens win the Stanley Cup.
