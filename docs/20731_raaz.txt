Title: Raaz (2002)
Genres: Foreign, Thriller
Director: Vikram Bhatt
Main Cast: Bipasha Basu, Dino Morea, Ashutosh Rana, Mink Brar, Vishwajeet Pradhan

Overview: Sanjana and Aditya decide to give their marriage one last chance. They plan a holiday in Ooty, but they don't know that a strange figure is waiting for them to come.
