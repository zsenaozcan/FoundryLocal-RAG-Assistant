Title: Moon Man (2012)
Genres: Family
Director: Stephan Schesch
Main Cast: Tomi Ungerer, Katharina Thalbach, Ulrich Tukur, Corinna Harfouch, Ulrich Noethen

Overview: One night, a shooting star appears, whizzing through outer space towards the moon. Moon Man seizes his chance, grabs the speeding comet by the tail and hitches a ride to earth. This 'attack from outer space' sets the alarm bells ringing in the Presidential Headquarters. While escaping the President and his soldiers, Moon Man sets off on a long journey and marvels at the many wonders the earth has to offer – and he realizes how much children love and need him.
