Title: Kitty Foyle (1940)
Genres: Romance, Drama
Director: Sam Wood
Main Cast: Eduardo Ciannelli, Ginger Rogers, James Craig, Dennis Morgan, Ernest Cossart
Keywords: philadelphia, socialite

Overview: Kitty Foyle, a hard-working white-collar girl from a Philadelphia, Pennsylvania low, middle-class family, meets and falls in love with young socialite Wyn Strafford but his family is against her.
