Title: Gypsy (1962)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Music
Director: Mervyn LeRoy
Main Cast: Natalie Wood, Rosalind Russell, Karl Malden, Parley Baer, Morgan Brittany
Keywords: burlesque

Overview: Based on the Broadway hit about the life and times of burlesque dancer Gypsy Rose Lee and her aggressive stage mother, Mama Rose.
