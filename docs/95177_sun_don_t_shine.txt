Title: Sun Don't Shine (2012)
Genres: Drama, Crime, Mystery, Romance
Director: Amy Seimetz
Main Cast: Kate Lyn Sheil, Kentucker Audley, Kit Gwin, Mark Reeb, Gregory Gordon Schmidt
Keywords: suspense, independent film, woman director

Overview: Crystal and her boyfriend Leo embark on a tense and mysterious road trip through the desolate yet hauntingly beautiful landscape of central Florida.
