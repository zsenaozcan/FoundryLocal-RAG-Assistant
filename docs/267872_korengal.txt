Title: Korengal (2014)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Sebastian Junger
Main Cast: LaMonta Caldwell, Sterling Jones, Dan Kearney, Juan "Doc" Restrepo, Aron Hijar
Keywords: war in afghanistan

Overview: Korengal picks up where Restrepo left off; the same men, the same valley, the same commanders, but a very different look at the experience of war.
