Title: Moloch (1999)
Genres: War, Drama, Romance
Director: Alexander Sokurov
Main Cast: Yelena Rufanova, Leonid Mozgovoy, Leonid Sokol, Yelena Spiridonova, Vladimir Bogdanov

Overview: In 1942, in Bavaria, Eva Braun is alone, when Adolf Hitler arrives with Dr. Josef Goebbels and his wife Magda Goebbels and Martin Bormann to spend a couple of days without talking politics.
