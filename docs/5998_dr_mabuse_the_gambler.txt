Title: Dr. Mabuse, the Gambler (1922)
Genres: Crime, Drama, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Fritz Lang
Main Cast: Rudolf Klein-Rogge, Aud Egede Nissen, Gertrude Welcker, Alfred Abel, Bernhard Goetzke
Keywords: identity, magic, mask, german mark, dollar, inflation, counterfeit, criminal, psychiatrist, disguise

Overview: Arch-criminal Dr. Mabuse, who is a master of disguise and has been hypnotising people into doing his bidding, sets out to make a fortune and run Berlin. Detective Wenk sets out to stop him.
