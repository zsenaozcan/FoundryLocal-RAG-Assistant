Title: Iron & Blood: The Legend of Taras Bulba (2009)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Vladimir Bortko
Main Cast: Magdalena Mielcarz, Bogdan Stupka, Vladimir Vdovichenkov, Igor Petrenko, Mikhail Boyarskiy
Keywords: father son relationship, based on novel, ukraine, cossack, ukrainian, sword fight

Overview: Set in the 16th century, this is a story about Ukraine's Cossack warriors and their campaign to defend their lands from the advancing Polish armies.
