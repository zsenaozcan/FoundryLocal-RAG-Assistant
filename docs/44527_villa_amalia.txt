Title: Villa Amalia (2009)
Genres: Drama, Foreign
Director: Benoît Jacquot
Main Cast: Isabelle Huppert, Jean-Hugues Anglade, Xavier Beauvois, Clara Bindi, Viviana Aliberti

Overview: Ann leaves Thomas and everything else behind when she catches him kissing another woman. With her music and help from Georges, she begins a journey to find herself.
