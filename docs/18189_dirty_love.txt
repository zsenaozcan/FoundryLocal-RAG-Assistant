Title: Dirty Love (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: John Asher
Main Cast: Jenny McCarthy, Carmen Electra, Eddie Kaye Thomas, Guillermo Díaz, Kathy Griffin
Keywords: photographer, ecstasy

Overview: A modern day Cinderella story which sees disaster prone Rebecca embark on a journey in search of true love. Betrayed by her boyfriend, Richard and following a palm reader's psychic prophecy, Rebecca goes in search of her one true soul mate.
