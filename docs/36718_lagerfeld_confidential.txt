Title: Lagerfeld Confidential (2007)
Genres: Documentary
Director: Rodolphe Marconi
Main Cast: Brad Koenig, Nicole Kidman, Karl Lagerfeld
Keywords: fashion designer

Overview: For the first time Karl Lagerfeld has agreed to let someone create an artwork on his every day life and to trust in the director. Until today there is no authorised biography existing and the memories who Karl Lagerfeld would compose stay perfectly confidential. After three years of work, and over three hundred hours of footage, Rodolphe Marconi discloses the daily life of the star through his personal lens as a filmmaker.
