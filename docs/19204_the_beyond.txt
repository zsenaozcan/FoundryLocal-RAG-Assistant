Title: The Beyond (1981)
Genres: Horror
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Catriona MacColl, David Warbeck, Cinzia Monreale, Antoine Saint-John, Veronica Lazar
Keywords: hotel, experiment, hell, gore, morgue, undead, blood, zombie, violence, gothic horror

Overview: A young woman inherits an old hotel in Louisiana where after a series of supernatural 'accidents', she learns that the building was built over one of the entrances to Hell.
