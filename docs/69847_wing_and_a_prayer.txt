Title: Wing and a Prayer (1944)
Genres: War, Action, Drama
Director: Henry Hathaway
Main Cast: Don Ameche, Dana Andrews, William Eythe, Charles Bickford, Cedric Hardwicke
Keywords: aviation, pacific theater

Overview: An aircraft carrier is sent on a decoy mission around the Pacific, with orders to avoid combat, thus lulling Japanese alertness before the battle of Midway.
