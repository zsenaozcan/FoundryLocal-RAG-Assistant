Title: The Thieves (2012)
Genres: Action, Crime
Director: Dong-hun Choi
Main Cast: Kim Yoon-seok, Kim Hye-soo, Lee Jung-jae, Jun Ji-hyun, Simon Yam
Keywords: safecracker, heist movie, diamond theft, criminal gang, macau

Overview: A gang of South Korean thieves team up with a Hong Kong crew to steal a diamond necklace from a heavily-guarded casino safe in Macau. As the cops close in, old betrayals — and misunderstandings — resurface.
