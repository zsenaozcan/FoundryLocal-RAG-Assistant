Title: Holiday (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Romance
Director: A.R. Murugadoss
Main Cast: Akshay Kumar, Sonakshi Sinha, Sumeet Raghvan, Zakir Hussain, Govinda
Keywords: war, soldier

Overview: A soldier on vacation finds himself hunting down a terrorist.  A military officer attempts to hunt down a terrorist, destroy a terrorist gang and deactivate the sleeper cells under its command.
