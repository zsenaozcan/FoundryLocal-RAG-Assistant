Title: Spirits of the Dead (1968)
Genres: Drama, Mystery, Horror
Director: Federico Fellini
Main Cast: Brigitte Bardot, Alain Delon, Jane Fonda, Terence Stamp, Peter Fonda
Keywords: obsession, army, ferrari, catholic, alcoholic, desire, edgar allan poe, movie star, doppelgänger

Overview: Three directors each adapt a Poe short story to the screen: "Toby Dammit" features a disheveled drugged and drunk English movie star who nods acceptance in the Italian press and his producers fawn over him. "Metzengerstein" features a Mediveal countess who has a love-hate relationship with a black stallion - who it turns out is really her dead lover. "William Wilson" tells the story of a sadistic Austrian student with an exact double whom he later kills.
