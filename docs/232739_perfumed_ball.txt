Title: Perfumed Ball (1997)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lírio Ferreira
Main Cast: Duda Mamberti, Luiz Carlos Vasconcelos, Aramis Trindade, Joffre Soares, Chico Diaz
Keywords: cangaço

Overview: A Lebanese photographer living in Brazil in the '30s manages to take pictures of Lampião, a legendary Brazilian outlaw, kind of a tropical Robin Hood.
