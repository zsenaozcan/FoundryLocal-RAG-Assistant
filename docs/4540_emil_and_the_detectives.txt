Title: Emil and the Detectives (1931)
Genres: Comedy, Crime
Director: Gerhard Lamprecht
Main Cast: Rolf Wenkhaus, Käthe Haack, Fritz Rasp, Rudolf Biebrach, Olga Engl
Keywords: berlin, railway car

Overview: When a suspicious man bribes Emil with chocolate in return for a bundle of cash, the young lad thinks of a plan to catch him.
