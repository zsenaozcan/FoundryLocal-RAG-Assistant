Title: We Believed (2010)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Mario Martone
Main Cast: Luigi Pisani, Luigi Lo Cascio, Valerio Binasco, Francesca Inaudi, Andrea Bosca

Overview: The stories of three young men who, in the wake of the ferocious repression by the Bourbon reign in 1828, decide to join Giuseppe Mazzini's Young Italy movement.
