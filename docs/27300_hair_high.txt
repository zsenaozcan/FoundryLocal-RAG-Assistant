Title: Hair High (2004)
Genres: Horror, Animation, Comedy
Director: Bill Plympton
Main Cast: Eric Gilliland, Sarah Silverman, Dermot Mulroney, Beverly D'Angelo, David Carradine

Overview: Bill Plympton's gothic '50s high-school comedy about a love-triangle that goes terribly wrong. Two murdered teens return from the grave, then go to their prom to get revenge.
