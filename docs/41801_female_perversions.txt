Title: Female Perversions (1996)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Susan Streitfeld
Main Cast: Tilda Swinton, Amy Madigan, Karen Sillas, Frances Fisher, Clancy Brown
Keywords: judge, daydream, sister, rope, independent film, lesbian, insecurity, incest, crucifix, lipstick
