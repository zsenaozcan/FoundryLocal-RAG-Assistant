Title: One Trick Pony (1980)
Genres: Drama
Director: Robert M. Young
Main Cast: Paul Simon, Blair Brown, Rip Torn, Joan Hackett, Allen Garfield
Keywords: music business, rock music, record, failing marriage

Overview: Jonah (Paul Simon) is an aging rock star trying to put together a new album in the face of an indifferent record label and a talentless producer. At the same time, he's struggling to save his failing marriage.
