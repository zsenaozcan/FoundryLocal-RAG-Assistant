Title: La Strada (1954)
Genres: Drama
Director: Federico Fellini
Main Cast: Anthony Quinn, Giulietta Masina, Richard Basehart, Aldo Silvani, Marcella Rovere
Keywords: circus, prison, in love with enemy, authority, harley davidson, sadness, wave, dying and death, appearance, rage and hate

Overview: A traveling strongman, Zampano, repays the death of Rosa, his assistant, by offering the same job to little sister Gelsomina,  whose mother receives compensation in return.
