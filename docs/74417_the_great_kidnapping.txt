Title: The Great Kidnapping (1973)
Genres: Crime
Director: Roberto Infascelli
Main Cast: Enrico Maria Salerno, Lee J. Cobb, Jean Sorel, Luciana Paluzzi
Keywords: kidnapping

Overview: In early seventies Italy, plagued by criminality and political terrorism, a fearless police commissioner is sent to restore law and order in a northern industrial town devastated by a recent wave of violence and kidnappings..
