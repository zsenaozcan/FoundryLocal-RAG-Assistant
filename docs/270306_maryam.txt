Title: Maryam (2002)
Genres: Drama
Director: Ramin Serry
Main Cast: Mariam Parris, David Ackert, Shaun Toub, Shohreh Aghdashloo, Maz Jobrani

Overview: An Iranian-born teenager living in suburban New Jersey thinks of herself as simply an American until anti-Iranian sentiment erupts in her community after American hostages are held in Iran.
