Title: Dead Girls (2014)
Genres: Horror
Director: Neal Fischer
Main Cast: Jessica Galang, Joseph Luis Caballero, Rebecca Mullins, Aubrey Joyce Tunnell, Nick Cardiff
Keywords: anthology, revenge

Overview: Dead Girls is a horror anthology featuring three stories of terror connected through the pages of dead girls' diaries, which chronicle each girl's act of vengeance against the people who have wronged or abused them.
