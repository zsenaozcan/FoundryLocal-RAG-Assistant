Title: Grown Up Movie Star (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Adriana Maggs
Main Cast: Shawn Doyle, Tatiana Maslany, Julia Kennedy, Jonny Harris, Mark O'Brien
Keywords: newfoundland, independent film, single father, lgbt, woman director, absent  mother

Overview: The story of Ruby, 13, determined to grow up fast after her mother runs away to become a movie star, leaving Ruby with her hopelessly rural father.
