Title: A River Called Titas (1973)
Genres: Family, Drama
Director: Ritwik Ghatak
Main Cast: Fakrul Hasan Bairagi , Narain Chakraborty , Kabari Choudhury , Roushan Jamil, Sirajul Islam
Keywords: river, fisherman

Overview: An in-depth look at the lives and struggles of the community of fishermen living by the river Titash.
