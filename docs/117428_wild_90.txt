Title: Wild 90 (1968)
Genres: Drama, Crime
Director: Norman Mailer
Main Cast: Norman Mailer, Buzz Farber, Mickey Knox, D.A. Pennebaker, Beverly Bentley

Overview: Norman Mailer’s first feature filmmaking effort stars the director and his two longtime collaborators Buzz Farbar and Mickey Knox as a trio of gangsters holed up in a ramshackle New York apartment, drinking, braying, and fighting.
