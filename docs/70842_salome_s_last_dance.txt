Title: Salome's Last Dance (1988)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Ken Russell
Main Cast: Glenda Jackson, Stratford Johns, Nickolas Grace, Douglas Hodge, Imogen Millais-Scott
Keywords: brothel

Overview: Oscar Wilde watches an outrageous staging of his banned play "Salome" at a London brothel, with parts played by prostitutes, Wilde's host, his lover Bosey, and Lady Alice.
