Title: My New Gun (1992)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Stacy Cochran
Main Cast: Diane Lane, James Le Gros, Tess Harper, Philip Seymour Hoffman, Stephen Collins
Keywords: gun, independent film, neighbor

Overview: Debbie and Gerald's lives drastically change after they get a gun. Their mysterious neighbor, Skippy, becomes an important and transforming figure in their lives.
