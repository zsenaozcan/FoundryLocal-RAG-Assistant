Title: Tainá: An Amazon Adventure (2001)
Genres: Family, Fantasy, Adventure
Director: Tania Lamarca
Main Cast: Marcos Apolo, Eunice Baía, Branca de Camargo, Beth Erthal, Jairo Mattos

Overview: A brave and bold indigenous girl, protector of her jungle and its wild life and guided by her shaman grandfather, pairs with a boy from the big city, against animal traffickers in the Amazon forest.
