Title: Water (1985)
Genres: Adventure, Comedy
Director: Dick Clement
Main Cast: Michael Caine, Brenda Vaccaro, Billy Connolly, Leonard Rossiter, Valerie Perrine
Keywords: rebel, atlantic ocean, caribbean islands, colony, comedy

Overview: Michael Caine stars as Baxter Thwaites, the laid-back Governor of the sleepy British colony of Cascara. But when American oil drillers accidentally strike a gusher of ultra-delicious mineral water, the forgotten Caribbean out-post becomes a global hotbed of political and economic chaos.
