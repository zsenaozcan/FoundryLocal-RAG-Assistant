Title: Self Made (2014)
Genres: Drama
Director: Shira Geffen
Main Cast: Samira Saraya, Doraid Liddawi, Na'ama Shoham, Ziad Bakri, Sarah Adler
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Self Made tells the story of two women - one Israeli, the other Palestinian- who are trapped within their respective worlds. After a mix-up at a checkpoint, they find themselves living the life of the other on the opposite side of the border.
