Title: Encounters of the Spooky Kind (1980)
Genres: Action, Horror, Fantasy, Comedy
Director: Sammo Hung
Main Cast: Sammo Hung, Chung Fat, Wu Ma, Lam Ching-Ying, Peter Chan Lung
Keywords: martial arts, test of courage, black magic, possession

Overview: Sammo is Courageous Cheung, a pedicab driver in a rural community who is known around town as the man who fears nothing. Well, this is proved false before the opening credits finish when he endures a particularly frightening nightmare of flesh-eating zombies. But, that's just the beginning of his run-ins with the supernatural...
