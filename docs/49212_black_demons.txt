Title: Black Demons (1991)
Genres: Horror
Director: Umberto Lenzi
Main Cast: Keith Van Hoven, Joe Balogh, Sonia Curtis, Philip Murray, Juliana Teixeira
Keywords: voodoo, jungle, student, zombie

Overview: Three college students, another couple, their housekeeper are besieged at a remote Brazilian plantation by a group of zombies raised by a voodoo curse.
