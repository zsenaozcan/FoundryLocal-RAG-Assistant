Title: Pickpocket (1959)
Genres: Crime, Drama
Director: Robert Bresson
Main Cast: Martin LaSalle, Marika Green, Jean Pélégri, Dolly Scal, Pierre Leymarie
Keywords: france, prison, modern society, pickpocket, materialism, unsociability, black and white

Overview: Michel is released from jail after serving a sentence for thievery. His mother dies and he resorts to pickpocketing as a means of survival.
