Title: Bad Turn Worse (2014)
Genres: Thriller, Crime, Drama
Director: Simon Hawkins
Main Cast: Mackenzie Davis, Logan Huffman, Jeremy Allen White, William Devane, Jon Gries
Keywords: texas, murder, heist, teenager, empty safe

Overview: Three teenage friends get in way over their head when they cross a down-home crime syndicate. They hope to make a break for it and escape their dead-end existence in a cotton-mill town but get sucked into the seedy underbelly of organized crime when one of them steals from the wrong man.
