Title: Worth Winning (1989)
Genres: Comedy, Romance
Director: Will Mackenzie
Main Cast: Mark Harmon, Madeleine Stowe, Lesley Ann Warren, Maria Holvoe
Keywords: womanizer, battle of the sexes

Overview: Taylor is a man who has no problems with women. So confident is he that he accepts a challenge from his friends: he has to secure proposals of marriage from three women of their choice.
