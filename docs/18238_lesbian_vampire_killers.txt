Title: Lesbian Vampire Killers (2009)
Genres: Horror, Comedy
Director: Phil Claydon
Main Cast: James Corden, Mathew Horne, Paul McGann, MyAnna Buring, Vera Filatova
Keywords: female nudity, horror, dark comedy, female homosexuality, female vampire

Overview: With their women having been enslaved by a pack of lesbian vampires, the remaining menfolk of a rural town send two hapless young lads out onto the moors as a sacrifice.
