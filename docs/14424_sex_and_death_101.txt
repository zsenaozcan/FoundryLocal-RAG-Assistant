Title: Sex and Death 101 (2007)
Genres: Comedy, Romance, Drama
Director: Daniel Waters
Main Cast: Simon Baker, Winona Ryder, Julie Bowen, Leslie Bibb, Skyler James Sandak

Overview: A guy's life is turned around by an email, which includes the names of everyone he's had sex with and ever will have sex with. His situation gets worse when he encounters a femme fatale (Ryder) who targets men guilty of sex crime.
