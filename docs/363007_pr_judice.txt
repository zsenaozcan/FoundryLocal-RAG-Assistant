Title: Préjudice (2015)
Genres: Drama
Director: Antoine Cuypers
Main Cast: Nathalie Baye, Arno Hintjens, Thomas Blanchard, Ariane Labed, Éric Caravaca
Keywords: family

Overview: A challenging psychological drama where the carefully nurtured harmony of a family is shattered one lovely summer evening. Cédric, who is generally shut away in a world of his own, doesn't react very well to the happy news of his sister’s pregnancy.
