Title: The Girl and Death (2012)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Jos Stelling
Main Cast: Sylvia Hoeks, Leonid Bichevin, Renata Litvinova, Sergei Makovetsky, Dieter Hallervorden

Overview: The Girl and Death tells us the impossible love story of Nicolai and the courtesan Elise. A love obstructed by materialism, wealth and death threat.
