Title: Belle épine (2010)
Genres: Drama
Director: Rebecca Zlotowski
Main Cast: Léa Seydoux, Agathe Schlenker, Anaïs Demoustier, Johan Libéreau, Nicolas Maury
Keywords: woman director

Overview: After the death of her mother, 17-year-old Prudence finds herself living alone in her Paris apartment.  Then she meets Maryline, a rebel of her own age, who introduces her to the thrills of motorcycle racing on the biker circuit at Rungis.  Prudence’s newfound lease of freedom becomes complicated when she falls for a boy Franck who wastes no time in taking advantage of her naivety...
