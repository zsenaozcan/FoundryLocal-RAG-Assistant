Title: Fools' Parade (1971)
Genres: Thriller, Drama, Comedy
Director: Andrew V. McLaglen
Main Cast: James Stewart, George Kennedy, Anne Baxter, Strother Martin, Kurt Russell
Keywords: melodrama, 1930s

Overview: Homer Grindstaff is a big named banker in Glory and Doc Council is a prison official and they team up to plot to kill Appleyard and his ex-convict friends who were attempting to open up a general store in Stone Coal, West Virginia and then to take the money.
