Title: Desert Hearts (1985)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Donna Deitch
Main Cast: Helen Shaver, Patricia Charbonneau, Audra Lindley, Andra Akers, Gwen Welles
Keywords: based on novel, nevada, coming out, farm, reno, lesbian, divorce, lgbt, woman director

Overview: The story of straight-edge literature professor Vivian who travels to Reno to get away from a relationship breakup when she falls in love with an attractive and unconventional girl named Cay.
