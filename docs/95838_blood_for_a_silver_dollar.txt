Title: Blood for a Silver Dollar (1965)
Genres: Western, Action, Romance
Director: Giorgio Ferroni
Main Cast: Giuliano Gemma, Ida Galli, Pierre Cressoy, Giuseppe Addobbati, Franco Fantasia
Keywords: spaghetti western

Overview: Two brothers part company only to be reunited when one is hired to stop a thief who turns out to be his sibling.
