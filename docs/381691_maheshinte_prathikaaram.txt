Title: Maheshinte Prathikaaram (2016)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Dileesh Pothan
Main Cast: Fahadh Faasil, Aparna Balamurali, Alencier Ley Lopez, Soubin Shahir, Sujith Sankar
Keywords: kung fu, photographer, magazine, village, photography, revenge, romance, malayalam, vow, sandel

Overview: Mahesh, a studio photographer and owner of the studio. As the story moves on, Mahesh encounters an anonymous fight ending up with taking revenge that leads to certain realizations in his life.
