Title: Parviz (2012)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Majid Barzegar
Main Cast: Levon Haftvan

Overview: Parviz has as its increasingly horrifying anti-hero the 50-year-old hulk of a passive-aggressive bachelor son (theater director/activist Haftvan), whose free ride in life screeches to a halt when his miserly widowed father forms a plan to remarry.
