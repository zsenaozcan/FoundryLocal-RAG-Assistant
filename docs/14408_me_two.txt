Title: Me Two (2008)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Nicolas Charlet
Main Cast: Daniel Auteuil, Alain Chabat, Marina Foïs, François Damiens
Keywords: double life, singer

Overview: 80's pop icon Gilles Gabriel is about to make a comeback in the French music scene, until he accidentally hits Ranu, a slightly nutty accountant, when out driving one day. Both are horrified to realise that Gilles died in the crash, and that his spirit now lives in Ranu's head! The kooky accountant's life soon becomes chaos!
