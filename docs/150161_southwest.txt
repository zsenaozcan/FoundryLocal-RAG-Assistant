Title: Southwest (2011)
Genres: Drama
Director: Eduardo Nunes
Main Cast: Júlio Adrião, Regina Bastos, Raquel Bonfante, Mariana Lima, Victor Navega Motta

Overview: In a Brazilian coastal village where everything seems motionless, Clarice grasps her life in a single day, unlike those she meets and who are living this day like any other. She tries to understand her obscure reality and the destiny of the people around her in a circular time that haunts and disorients.
