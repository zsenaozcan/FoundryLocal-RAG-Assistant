Title: I Travel Because I Have to, I Come Back Because I Love You (2009)
Genres: Drama
Director: Marcelo Gomes
Main Cast: Irandhir Santos

Overview: José Renato, a 35-year-old geologist, is sent on a fieldtrip to the scrublands of the Sertão, a semi-arid isolated region in the northeast of Brazil. The goal of his survey is to assess possible routes for a water canal from the region's only voluminous river.
