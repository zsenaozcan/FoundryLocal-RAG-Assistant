Title: The Road Builder (1971)
Genres: Drama, Horror, Thriller
Director: Alastair Reid
Main Cast: Patricia Neal, Pamela Brown, Nicholas Clay, Jean Anderson, Graham Crowden
Keywords: based on novel, melodrama, serial killer, sexual tension, gothic horror, older woman younger man relationship, blind mother

Overview: The dreary existence of middle-aged spinster Maura Prince takes an unexpected turn with the arrival of young handyman Billy Jarvis, but there is more to Billy than meets the eye.  Based on the novel *Nest in a Fallen Tree* by Joy Cowley.
