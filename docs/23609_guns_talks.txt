Title: Guns & Talks (2001)
Genres: Action, Comedy
Director: Jang Jin
Main Cast: Shin Hyun-joon, Won Bin, Shin Ha-kyun, Jung Jin-young, Oh Seung-hyeon

Overview: Guns &amp; Talks is a South Korean film released in 2001. Directed by Jang Jin, it is a comedy about two brothers and their two friends. These four form a group of assassins, who have attracted the attention of a couple of pesky detectives, Sergeant Kim and Inspector Cho.
