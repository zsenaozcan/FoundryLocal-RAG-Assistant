Title: Monument Ave. (1998)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Crime
Director: Ted Demme
Main Cast: Denis Leary, Ian Hart, Jason Barry, Lenny Clarke, Kevin Chapman

Overview: Bobby O'Grady a low level member of a Boston Irish gang run by Jackie O'Hara. Jackie demands absolute, total loyalty to him. When Jackie kills one of Bobby's buddies, Teddy, Bobby and others have to keep it an absolute secret, even from their and Teddy's relatives.
