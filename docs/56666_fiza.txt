Title: Fiza (2000)
Genres: Drama, Foreign, Romance
Director: Khalid Mohammed
Main Cast: Hrithik Roshan, Karisma Kapoor, Jaya Bachchan, Neha, Bikram Saluja
Keywords: suicide, loss of brother, laughing, jihad, love, racial tension

Overview: In 1993 Fiza's brother disappears during the riots in Mumbai. In 1999 Fiza is tired of waiting and goes looking for him.
