Title: Afro Samurai: Resurrection (2009)
Genres: Animation
Director: Fuminori Kizaki
Main Cast: Samuel L. Jackson, Lucy Liu, Mark Hamill, Grey Griffin, Zachary Gordon
Keywords: samurai

Overview: Afro Samurai avenged his father and found a life of peace. But the legendary master is forced back into the game by a beautiful and deadly woman from his past. The sparks of violence dropped along Afro’s bloody path now burn out of control – and nowhere are the flames of hatred more intense than in the eyes of Sio.
