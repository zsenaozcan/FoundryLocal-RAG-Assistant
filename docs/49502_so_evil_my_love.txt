Title: So Evil My Love (1948)
Genres: Drama
Director: Lewis Allen
Main Cast: Ray Milland, Ann Todd, Geraldine Fitzgerald, Leo G. Carroll, Raymond Huntley
Keywords: gothic, film noir

Overview: A missionary's widow meets charming Mark Bellis, artist and rogue, on the ship taking them both back to 1890s London. She will soon find out he has dark ambitions.
