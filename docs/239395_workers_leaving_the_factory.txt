Title: Workers Leaving the Factory (1995)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Harun Farocki
Main Cast: Unknown

Overview: Essayist documentary based on the film material: workers leaving the factory of the Lumière Brothers.  Based on the first film recording - workers leaving the Lumière factory - Farocki provides an insight in the changes in both the phenomenon of work and film through pictures of workers leaving factories across the years. - IFFR
