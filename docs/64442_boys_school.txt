Title: Boys' School (1938)
Genres: Adventure, Drama, Mystery
Director: Christian-Jaque
Main Cast: Erich von Stroheim, Michel Simon, Armand Bernard, Serge Grave, Jean Claudio

Overview: In a college, three friends form a secret society. There objective - going to America. A night, after one of their secret meetings, one of them see a man coming out from a wall. Then the day after he talks about it, he disappears. Then the second one vanishes. Are they gone to their dreams? That's when the art teacher is murdered. Suspicions now are too high so the third one decides to investigate.
