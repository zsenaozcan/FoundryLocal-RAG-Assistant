Title: M.D. Geist II: Death Force (1996)
Genres: Foreign, Science Fiction, Animation, Action
Director: Koichi Ohata
Main Cast: Kazuhiro Nakata, Takumi Yamazaki

Overview: Less than a year after Geist activated the Death Force, which decimated most of Jerra's population, he has kept himself busy by dismantling the Death Force machines one by one. Most of the remaining human population have fled to a remote haven run by a warlord named Krauser. Krauser is another MDS unit, MD-01, who has a technology that hides his mobile fortress from the Death Force.
