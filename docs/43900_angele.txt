Title: Angele (1934)
Genres: Drama
Director: Marcel Pagnol
Main Cast: Fernandel, Orane Demazis, Henri Poupon, Édouard Delmont, Annie Toinon

Overview: Angèle is a 1934 French drama film directed, produced and written by Marcel Pagnol. It stars Orane Demazis as a naive young woman who is seduced and abandoned. It is based on the novel Un de Baumugnes by Jean Giono.
