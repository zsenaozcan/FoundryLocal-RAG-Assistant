Title: Intimate Relations (1996)
Genres: Unknown
Director: Unknown
Main Cast: Julie Walters, Rupert Graves, Laura Sadler, Nicholas Hoult

Overview: A young merchant marine turns up in an English coastal town looking for a brother he barely knows. When his brother's wife rebukes him he takes up with the Beasley family.
