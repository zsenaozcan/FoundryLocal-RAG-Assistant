Title: Garm Wars: The Last Druid (2014)
Genres: Action, Science Fiction, Thriller
Director: Mamoru Oshii
Main Cast: Lance Henriksen, Kevin Durand, Mélanie St-Pierre, Summer H. Howell, Andrew Gillies

Overview: In a world where clone soldiers from three military tribes are locked in a perpetual battle of air, land and technology, one clone is separated from the battle and finds herself on the run with a group of unlikely companions.
