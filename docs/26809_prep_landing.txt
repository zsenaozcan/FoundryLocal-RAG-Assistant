Title: Prep & Landing (2009)
Genres: Animation, Fantasy, Family
Director: Kevin Deters
Main Cast: Dave Foley, Sarah Chalke, Derek Richardson, Mason Vale Cotton, David DeLuise
Keywords: holiday, santa claus

Overview: Wayne gets a new rookie partner, Lanny, after his previous partner got the promotion he wanted. Lanny has to remind Wayne of the Spirit of Christmas and the importance of being an elf in Santa's Prep and Landing elite unit.
