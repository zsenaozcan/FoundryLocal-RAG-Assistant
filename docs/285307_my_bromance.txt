Title: My Bromance (2014)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Nitchapoom Chaianun
Main Cast: Lohanan Teerapat, Pongsatorn Sripinta, Withawat Thaokhamlue

Overview: "Golf" a aggressive boy because he grown up with the broken family. He is only son who never be attention of the parents. "Bank" a lovely boy who came into family members. But he wasn't welcomed by his brother. They learn to live together for overcome obstacles of the surroundings. Before they know the truely of the word "Brother"
