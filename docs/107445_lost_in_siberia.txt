Title: Lost in Siberia (2012)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Ralf Huettner
Main Cast: Joachim Król, Vladimir Burlakov, Julya Men, Svetlana Tsvichenko, Katja Riemann

Overview: Matthias Bleuel, a logistician of a german mail order company is send to Kemorovo in Siberia to teach some russian subsidiary company the german work flow system. But soon this land changes him and everything for him.
