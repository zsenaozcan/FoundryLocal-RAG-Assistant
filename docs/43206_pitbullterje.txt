Title: Pitbullterje (2005)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Arild Fröhlich
Main Cast: Petrus Andreas Christensen, Jørgen Foss, Atle Antonsen, Kristin Skogheim, Robert Lindahl Haug
Keywords: christmas

Overview: Jim is a 12-year-old outsider. One day Terje (an overweight boy who claims to own a pit bull) starts in his class. He considers Jim as his best friend, but the only friend Jim can get is the only friend he really doesn't want.
