Title: L'Avventura (1960)
Genres: Drama, Mystery
Director: Michelangelo Antonioni
Main Cast: Monica Vitti, Gabriele Ferzetti, Lea Massari, Esmeralda Ruspoli, James Addams
Keywords: island, architect, mediterranean, police, boating trip

Overview: A woman disappears during a Mediterranean boating trip. During the search for the woman, her lover and her best friend become attracted to each other.
