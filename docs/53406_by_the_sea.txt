Title: By the Sea (1915)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Charlie Chaplin
Main Cast: Charlie Chaplin, Billy Armstrong, Margie Reiger, Bud Jamison, Edna Purviance
Keywords: black and white, silent film

Overview: It is windy at a bathing resort. After fighting with one of the two husbands, Charlie approaches Edna while the two husbands themselves fight over ice cream. Driven away by her husband, Charlie turns to the other's wife.
