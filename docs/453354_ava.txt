Title: Ava (2017)
Genres: Drama
Director: Léa Mysius
Main Cast: Noée Abita, Laure Calamy, Juan Cano, Tamara Cano
Keywords: beach, mother daughter relationship, teenage girl, summer, summer vacation, dog, blindness, woman director

Overview: Ava, 13, is spending the summer on the Atlantic coast when she learns that she will lose her sight
 sooner than expected. Her mother decides to act as if everything were normal so as to spend
 their best summer ever. Ava confronts the problem in her own way. She steals a big black dog that
 belongs to a young man on the run...
