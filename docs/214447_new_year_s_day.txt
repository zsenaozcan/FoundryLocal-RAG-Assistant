Title: New Year's Day (1990)
Genres: Comedy
Director: Henry Jaglom
Main Cast: Maggie Wheeler, Gwen Welles, Henry Jaglom, David Duchovny, Miloš Forman

Overview: A man returns to his sublet apartment to find the previous tenants, three offbeat young women, still in residence, under the mistaken belief that they have the apartment until the end of New Year's Day.
