Title: The Institute (2017)
Genres: Thriller
Director: James Franco
Main Cast: James Franco, Tim Blake Nelson, Lori Singer, Eric Roberts, Topher Grace
Keywords: baltimore, mental institution, 19th century, pseudo-scientific experiments

Overview: In 19th century Baltimore, Isabel Porter, a girl stricken with grief from her parents' untimely death, voluntarily checks herself into the Rosewood Institute. Subjected to bizarre and increasingly violent pseudo-scientific experiments in personality modification, brainwashing and mind control, she must escape the clutches of the Rosewood and exact her revenge, or else be forever lost.
