Title: Kung Fu Chefs (2009)
Genres: Action, Comedy, Foreign
Director: Ken Yip Wing-Kin
Main Cast: Sammo Hung, Vanness Wu, Cherrie Ying, Ai Kago, Lam Tze-Chung

Overview: Ousted chef Wong Bing-Yi is determined to help Shen Qing at her restaurant "Four Seas". He trains a young chef, Lung Kin-Yat to compete against Chef Tin, the head chef at "Imperial Palace", for the title of "Top Chef".
