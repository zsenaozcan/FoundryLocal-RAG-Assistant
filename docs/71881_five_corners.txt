Title: Five Corners (1987)
Genres: Drama
Director: Tony Bill
Main Cast: Jodie Foster, Tim Robbins, Todd Graff, John Turturro, Michael R. Howard
Keywords: new york, prison, alcohol, rape, love, theft, violence, city, hate, bronx

Overview: A psychotic young man returns to his old neighborhood after release from prison. He seeks out the woman he previously tried to rape and the man who protected her, with twisted ideas of love for her and hate for him.
