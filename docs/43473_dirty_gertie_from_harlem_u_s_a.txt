Title: Dirty Gertie from Harlem U.S.A. (1946)
Genres: Drama
Director: Spencer Williams
Main Cast: Francine Everett, Spencer Williams, Don Wilson, Katherine Moore, Alfred Hawkins
Keywords: all black cast

Overview: Dirty Gertie is a nightclub entertainer from the Harlem neighborhood of New York City. She arrives on the Caribbean island of "Rinidad" to perform as the headliner in a revue at the Paradise Hotel. On the island she attracts the attention of several men.
