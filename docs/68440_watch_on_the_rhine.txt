Title: Watch on the Rhine (1943)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Herman Shumlin
Main Cast: Bette Davis, Paul Lukas, Geraldine Fitzgerald, George Coulouris, Lucile Watson
Keywords: germany, washington d.c., anti-nazi

Overview: Kurt and Sara Müller and their three children left Germany many years ago and finally arrive to her mother's house in Washington DC. Kurt are working for the anti-nazi resistance in Germany. The Romanian count Brankovic who also lives there tries to blackmail him.
