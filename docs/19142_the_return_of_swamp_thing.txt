Title: The Return of Swamp Thing (1989)
Genres: Horror, Science Fiction
Director: Jim Wynorski
Main Cast: Louis Jourdan, Heather Locklear, Sarah Douglas, Dick Durock, Joey Sagal
Keywords: dc comics, sequel, based on comic, swamp thing

Overview: The Swamp Thing returns to battle the evil Dr. Arcane, who has a new science lab full of creatures transformed by genetic mutation, and chooses Heather Locklear as his new object of affection.
