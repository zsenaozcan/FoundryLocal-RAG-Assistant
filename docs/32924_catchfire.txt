Title: Catchfire (1990)
Genres: Drama, Action, Thriller, Romance
Director: Dennis Hopper
Main Cast: Dennis Hopper, Jodie Foster, Dean Stockwell, Vincent Price, John Turturro
Keywords: mafia

Overview: A witness to a mob assassination flees for her life from town to town, switching identities, but cannot seem to elude Milo, the chief killer out to get her.
