Title: Cruel Will (2013)
Genres: Thriller, Horror
Director: Arthur Romeo
Main Cast: Arron Kinser, Marissa Pistone, Spencer Garrett, Harry Lennix, Doug Jones
Keywords: urn, independent film, death of father

Overview: Tragedy strikes married couple Paul and Lily, when Lily's father dies. Lily has her father cremated, brings the urn home and now Paul has to come to terms with the death of his father-in-law whom he had despised. The situation gets a bit frayed when the lid to the urn mysteriously disappears, and Paul begins to experience happenings no one else is aware of.
