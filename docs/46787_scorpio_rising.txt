Title: Scorpio Rising (1964)
Genres: Fantasy
Director: Kenneth Anger
Main Cast: Ernie Allo, Bruce Byron, Bruce Byron, Steve Crandell, Johnny Dodds
Keywords: gay, nazis, biker

Overview: An army of gay/nazi bikers make their engines roar and ride the way to pain/pleasure as sexual and sadistic symbols are intercut into the dazing chaos and rhythmic experiences of this underground film by cult director Anger.
