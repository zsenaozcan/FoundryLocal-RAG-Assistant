Title: Pride and Prejudice (1980)
Genres: Drama
Director: Cyril Coke
Main Cast: Elizabeth Garvie, Sabina Franklyn, David Rintoul
Keywords: jane austen

Overview: Mrs. Bennet is determined to find husbands for her five daughters. The arrival of a new wealthy neighbor seems like the answer to her predicament. But while eldest daughter Jane catches Mr. Bingley's eye, middle child Mary has her nose stuck in a book, and youngest girls, Kitty and Lydia, chase after officers in uniform; Elizabeth, the willful, intelligent, and opinionated second daughter, is snubbed by haughty gentleman Mr. Darcy... In this class-minded society, can love triumph over pride and prejudice?
