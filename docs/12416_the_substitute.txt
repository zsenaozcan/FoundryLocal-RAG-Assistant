Title: The Substitute (2007)
Genres: Thriller, Comedy
Director: Ole Bornedal
Main Cast: Paprika Steen, Ulrich Thomsen, Jonas Wandschneider, Emma Juel Justesen, Sonja Richter
Keywords: denmark, alien, teachers and students

Overview: 6th Grade gets a new substitute teacher. She wants to train the class for an international competition in Paris. But something isn't right. How is she able read kids' minds? Why is she so mean? And how does she manage to convince everyone's parents she is so great when the whole class knows she is really an alien?
