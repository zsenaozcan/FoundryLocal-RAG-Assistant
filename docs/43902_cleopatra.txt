Title: Cleopatra (1934)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Cecil B. DeMille
Main Cast: Claudette Colbert, Warren William, Henry Wilcoxon, Joseph Schildkraut, Ian Keith
Keywords: egypt, cleopatra

Overview: The queen of Egypt (Claudette Colbert) barges the Nile and flirts with Mark Antony (Henry Wilcoxon) and Julius Caesar (Warren William).
