Title: Above Us the Waves (1955)
Genres: War
Director: Ralph Thomas
Main Cast: John Mills, John Gregson, Donald Sinden, James Robertson Justice, Michael Medwin

Overview: In World War II, the greatest threat to the British navy is the German battleship Tirpitz. While anchored in a Norwegian fjord, it is impossible to attack by conventional means, so a plan is hatched for a special commando unit to attack it, using midget submarines to plant underwater explosives.
