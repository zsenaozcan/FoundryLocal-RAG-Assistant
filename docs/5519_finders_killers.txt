Title: Finders Killers (1971)
Genres: Western
Director: Gianni Crea
Main Cast: Gordon Mitchell, Dino Strano, Pia Giancaro, Gennarino Pappagalli, Femi Benussi
Keywords: spaghetti western

Overview: A farmer returns home from the forest to find his family butchered. He decides to hunt down the murderers.
