Title: The Great Northfield Minnesota Raid (1972)
Genres: Action, Western
Director: Philip Kaufman
Main Cast: Cliff Robertson, Robert Duvall, Luke Askew, R. G. Armstrong, Dana Elcar
Keywords: jesse james, cole younger

Overview: The gangs of Jesse James and Cole Younger join forces to rob the First National Bank in Northfield, Minnesota, but things do not go as planned.
