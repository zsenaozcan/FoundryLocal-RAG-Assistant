Title: Massacre Mafia Style (1974)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Duke Mitchell
Main Cast: Duke Mitchell, Vic Caesar, Lorenzo Dodo, Louis Zito, Cara Peters

Overview: Terror reigns when Mimi, the son of a deported Don, along with his associate Jolly Rizzo wage a bloody war for control of the West Coast underworld, battling hordes of hard-boiled mobsters and deadly black pimps on their rise to the top!
