Title: Honkytonk Man (1982)
Genres: Western, Comedy, Drama, Music
Director: Clint Eastwood
Main Cast: Clint Eastwood, Kyle Eastwood, John McIntire, Alexa Kenin, Verna Bloom
Keywords: tuberculosis, whorehouse, stealing a chicken
