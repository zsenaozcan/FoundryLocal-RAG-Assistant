Title: Rajathandhiram (2015)
Genres: Romance, Crime, Drama, Thriller, Comedy
Director: A. G. Amid
Main Cast: Veera Bahu, Regina Cassandra, Pattiyal K. Shekar, Darbuka Siva, Ilavarasu

Overview: Three small-time criminals take up an offer to rob one of the biggest jewellery shops in the city. Will they be successful in their attempt, given that the store's owner, a ruthless man, is aware of the plan?
