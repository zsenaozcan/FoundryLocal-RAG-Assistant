Title: The Haunted Castle (1896)
Genres: Fantasy, Horror
Director: Georges Méliès
Main Cast: Georges Méliès, Jeanne d'Alcy
Keywords: supernatural, silent film, georges melies, early cinema

Overview: A bat flies into an ancient castle and transforms itself into Mephistopheles himself. Producing a cauldron, Mephistopheles conjures up a young girl and various supernatural creatures, one of which brandishes a crucifix in an effort to force the devil-vampire to vanish.
