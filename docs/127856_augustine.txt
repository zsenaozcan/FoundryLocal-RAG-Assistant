Title: Augustine (2012)
Genres: Drama, History
Director: Alice Winocour
Main Cast: Soko, Vincent Lindon, Chiara Mastroianni, Roxane Duran, Olivier Rabourdin
Keywords: france, epilepsy, hysteria, woman director, 19th century, inspired by true events

Overview: Set in Belle Époque France, the story follows nineteen-year-old "hysteria" patient Augustine, the star of Professor Charcot's experiments in hypnosis, as she transitions from object of study to object of desire.
