Title: Intervista (1987)
Genres: Drama, Comedy
Director: Federico Fellini
Main Cast: Marcello Mastroianni, Sergio Rubini, Antonella Ponziani, Maurizio Mein, Paola Liguori

Overview: Federico Fellini welcomes us into his world of film making with a mockumentary about his life in film, as a Japanese film crew follows him around.
