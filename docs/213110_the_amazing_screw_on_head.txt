Title: The Amazing Screw-On Head (2006)
Genres: Comedy, Action, Adventure, Animation, Science Fiction
Director: Chris Prynoski
Main Cast: Paul Giamatti, David Hyde Pierce, Patton Oswalt, Corey Burton, Mindy Sterling
Keywords: civil war, secret agent, comic book, based on comic, steampunk, animation, robot, dark horse comics

Overview: Based on the award-winning comic book by Mike Mignola (creator of Hellboy), The Amazing Screw-On Head chronicles the adventures of a Civil War-era secret agent with an extraordinary special power who serves under president Abraham Lincoln.
