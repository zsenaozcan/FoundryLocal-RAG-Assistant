Title: Sebastian (1968)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: David Greene
Main Cast: Dirk Bogarde, Susannah York, Lilli Palmer, John Gielgud, Janet Munro
Keywords: london england, spy, mathematician, british secret service, cryptologist

Overview: Sebastian is an undisciplined mathematics genius who works in the "cipher bureau" of the British Intelligence. While cracking enemy codes, Sebastian finds time to romance co-worker Rebecca Howard.
