Title: The Mummy's Tomb (1942)
Genres: Drama, Fantasy, Horror, Mystery, Thriller
Director: Harold Young
Main Cast: Lon Chaney Jr., Turhan Bey, John Hubbard, Elyse Knox, Dick Foran
Keywords: egypt, monster, mummy, princess, sequel, revenge, murder, high priest, eqgyptian

Overview: A high priest of Karnak travels to America with the living mummy Kharis (Lon Chaney Jr.) to kill all those who had desecrated the tomb of the Egyptian princess Ananka thirty years earlier.
