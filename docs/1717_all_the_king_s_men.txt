Title: All the King's Men (2006)
Genres: Drama, Thriller
Director: Steven Zaillian
Main Cast: Sean Penn, Jude Law, Kate Winslet, James Gandolfini, Patricia Clarkson
Keywords: corruption, journalist, based on novel, blackmail, manipulation, bodyguard, louisiana, scandal, power, governor

Overview: The story of an idealist's rise to power in the world of Louisiana politics and the corruption that leads to his ultimate downfall. Based on the1946 Pulitzer Prize-winning novel written by Robert Penn Warren.
