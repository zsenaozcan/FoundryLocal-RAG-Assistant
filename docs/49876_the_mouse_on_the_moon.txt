Title: The Mouse on the Moon (1963)
Genres: Comedy, Drama
Director: Richard Lester
Main Cast: Margaret Rutherford, Ron Moody, Bernard Cribbins, David Kossoff, June Ritchie
Keywords: space research

Overview: Sequel to The Mouse that Roared; The Tiny Country of Grand Fenwick has a hot water problem in the castle. To get the money necessary to put in a new set of plumbing, they request foreign aid from the U.S. for Space Research. The Russians then send aid as well to show that they too are for the internationalization of space. While the grand Duke is dreaming of hot baths, their one scientist is slapping together a rocket. The U.S. and Soviets get wind of the impending launch and try and beat them to the moon.
