Title: Erotic Games in the Third Galaxy (1981)
Genres: Science Fiction
Director: Bitto Albertini
Main Cast: Sherry Buchanan, Fausto Di Bella, Don Powell, Chris Avram, Margaret Rose Keil

Overview: The crew of a space ship confronts an evil galactic ruler out to rule the universe.  Also known as "Escape from Galaxy 3", "Starcrash II" or "Star Crash II" dubbed in English
