Title: The Delta Force (1986)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Menahem Golan
Main Cast: Chuck Norris, Lee Marvin, Shelley Winters, Martin Balsam, Joey Bishop
Keywords: terrorist, airplane hijacking, covert operation

Overview: A 707 aircraft jetliner on its way from Athens to Rome and then to New York City is hijacked by Lebanese terrorists. The terrorists demand that the pilot take them to Beirut. What the terrorists don't realize is that an elite team of commandoes led by Major McCoy (Norris) and by Colonel Alexander (Marvin) has been called into service to eliminate all terrorists on the jetliner.
