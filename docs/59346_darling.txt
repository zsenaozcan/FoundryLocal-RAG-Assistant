Title: Darling (2007)
Genres: Drama
Director: Christine Carrière
Main Cast: Marina Foïs, Guillaume Canet, Océane Decaudain, Marc Brunet, Sissi Duparc
Keywords: woman director

Overview: Darling is a woman of today. It seems to always choose the wrong path. Suffers but does not look like a victim. Do not feel sorry for herself. On the contrary, shows a passion for life against all odds. She is struggling to exist. If you fall, get up. Because Darling is naive and fearless, brave and instinctive. Has the life force of a tragic heroine.
