Title: Five Senses of Eros (2009)
Genres: Drama, Romance, Foreign
Director: Hur Jin-Ho  
Main Cast: Jang Hyuk, Cha Hyun-jung, Kim Kang-woo, Bae Jong-ok, Kim Su-ro
Keywords: eroticism, anthology, korea, romantic drama

Overview: In this anthology film centered around the theme of "Eros," five seperate stories are presented by five top Korean directors. The main characters from each segment are connected with each other in one way or another.
