Title: Me, You, Them (2000)
Genres: Comedy, Drama, Romance
Director: Andrucha Waddington
Main Cast: Regina Casé, Lima Duarte, Stênio Garcia, Luiz Carlos Vasconcelos
Keywords: love triangle

Overview: A true story that comically depicts the relationship between an ordinary woman and her three husbands, who live in the same house in a poor and arid small village of the Brazilian northeast.
