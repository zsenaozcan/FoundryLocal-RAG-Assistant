Title: New Fist of Fury (1976)
Genres: Action, Drama
Director: Lo Wei
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Nora Miao, Chan Sing, Henry Luk Yat-Lung, Yi Ming
Keywords: martial arts, kung fu, fight

Overview: A brother and sister escape from Japanese-occupied Shanghai to Japanese-occupied Taiwan, to stay with their grandfather who runs a Kung-Fu school there. However, the master of a Japanese Kung-Fu school in Taiwan has designs on bringing all other schools on the island under his domination, and part of his plan involves the murder of the siblings' grandfather.
