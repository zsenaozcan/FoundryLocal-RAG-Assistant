Title: The Possessed (1965)
Genres: Mystery, Crime, Drama
Director: Luigi Bazzoni
Main Cast: Peter Baldwin, Salvo Randone, Valentina Cortese, Pier Giovanni Anchisi, Ennio Balbo

Overview: When a visitor arrives in a small Italian village, he discovers that the residents are acting like soul-less zombies--and he begins to suspect that they may be under the influence of a mysterious local woman.
