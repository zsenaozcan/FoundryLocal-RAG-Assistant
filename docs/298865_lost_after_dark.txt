Title: Lost After Dark (2014)
Genres: Horror
Director: Ian Kessner
Main Cast: Alexander Calvert, Jesse Camacho, Elsie Gatien, Eve Harlow, Stephan James
Keywords: high school, woods, gore, slasher, retro, extreme violence, violence, cannibal, drug, killer

Overview: A group of teens sneak out of their high school dance to cruise around and have some unsupervised fun. When their car runs out of gas on a deserted road, they discover an old farmhouse and the cannibal killer living inside.
