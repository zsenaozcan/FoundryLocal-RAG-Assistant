Title: The Black Cat (1981)
Genres: Horror
Director: Lucio Fulci
Main Cast: Patrick Magee, Mimsy Farmer, David Warbeck, Al Cliver, Dagmar Lassander
Keywords: photographer, psychic, black cat

Overview: An American photographer (Mimsy Farmer) and a Scotland Yard inspector (David Warbeck) suspect a man's (Patrick Magee) cat of murder.
