Title: Butch and Sundance: The Early Days (1979)
Genres: Western
Director: Richard Lester
Main Cast: William Katt, Tom Berenger, Jeff Corey, John Schuck, Michael C. Gwynne
Keywords: outlaw, prequel

Overview: A "prequel" of sorts to "Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid," chronicling the two outlaws' lives in the years before the events portrayed in the Newman/Redford movie.
