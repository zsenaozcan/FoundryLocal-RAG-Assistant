Title: Downtown '81 (1981)
Genres: Documentary, Drama
Director: Edo Bertoglio
Main Cast: Jean Michel Basquiat, Deborah Harry, Vincent Gallo, Coati Mundi, Debi Mazar
Keywords: independent film, art, anarchic comedy

Overview: The film is a day in the life of a young artist, Jean-Michel Basquiat, who needs to raise money to reclaim the apartment from which he has been evicted. He wanders the downtown streets carrying a painting he hopes to sell, encountering friends, whose lives (and performances) we peek into.
