Title: Rapid Fire (1992)
Genres: Action, Thriller
Director: Dwight H. Little
Main Cast: Brandon Lee, Powers Boothe, Nick Mancuso, Raymond J. Barry, Kate Hodge
Keywords: undercover cop, police corruption, drug lord

Overview: College student Jake Lo is pursued by smugglers, mobsters and crooked federal agents after he witnesses a murder by a Mafia kingpin.
