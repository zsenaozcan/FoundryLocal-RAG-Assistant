Title: The Age of the Medici (1972)
Genres: History, TV Movie
Director: Roberto Rossellini
Main Cast: Marcello Di Falco, Virgilio Gazzolo, Tom Felleghy, Adriano Amidei Migliano, Michel Bardinet

Overview: This is an exposition of the social and political history of renaissance Florentine history, told through dramatised conversations between the main participants, Cosimo de Medici and Brunelleschi.
