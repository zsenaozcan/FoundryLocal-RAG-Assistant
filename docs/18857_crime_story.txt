Title: Crime Story (1993)
Genres: Thriller, Action, Crime, Drama
Director: Kirk Wong
Main Cast: Jackie Chan, Kent Cheng, Law Kar-Ying, Au-Yeung Pui-San, Christine Ng
Keywords: hostage, kidnapping, car chase

Overview: A special agent assigned to protect a wealthy business magnate. However, when the businessman is kidnapped in a daring ambush, he teams up with a seasoned detective to crack the case. But soon he discovers the case isn't that simple.
