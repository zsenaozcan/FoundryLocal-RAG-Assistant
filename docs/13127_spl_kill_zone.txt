Title: SPL: Kill Zone (2005)
Genres: Action, Crime, Drama, Thriller
Director: Wilson Yip
Main Cast: Donnie Yen, Sammo Hung, Simon Yam, Wu Jing, Ken Chang
Keywords: martial arts, neo-noir

Overview: Chan, an articulate senior detective nearing the end of his career, is taking care of the daughter of a witness killed by ruthless crime lord Po. Martial arts expert Ma is set to take over as head of the crime unit, replacing Chan who wants an early retirement.
