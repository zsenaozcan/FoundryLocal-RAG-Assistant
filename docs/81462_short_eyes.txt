Title: Short Eyes (1977)
Genres: Drama
Director: Robert M. Young
Main Cast: Bruce Davison, José Pérez, Nathan George, Don Blakely, Tony DiBenedetto
Keywords: new york, prison, rape, fear, violence, intimidation

Overview: A young man who is charged with child molestation is placed in New York City’s infamous Tombs prison. When the other inmates in his cell block find out what he is charged with, life becomes extremely difficult for him.
