Title: All Over Me (1997)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Alex Sichel
Main Cast: Alison Folland, Tara Subkoff, Cole Hauser, Wilson Cruz, Leisha Hailey
Keywords: new york, role of women, lesbian relationship, homosexuality, independent film, best friend, lgbt, woman director, white woman

Overview: Claude and Ellen are best friends who live in a not-so-nice area of New York. They're involved in the subculture of 90s youth, complete with drugs, live music, and homophobia. All is changed one night when a violent and meaningless death rocks their lives.
