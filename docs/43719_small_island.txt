Title: Small Island (2009)
Genres: Drama, Thriller, Mystery
Director: John Alexander
Main Cast: Benedict Cumberbatch, Naomie Harris, Ruth Wilson, Ashley Walters, Shaun Parkes

Overview: In 1939 Hortense, illegitimate but intelligent and ambitious, is brought up on Jamaica with the family of charismatic, free-thinking Michael, who joins the Air Force when war breaks out and goes to England. After the war, Hortense, now a qualified teacher, makes a marriage of convenience to Gilbert Joseph, paying for his passage to England on condition he sends for her.
