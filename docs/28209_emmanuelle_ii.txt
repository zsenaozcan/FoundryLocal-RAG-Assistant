Title: Emmanuelle II (1975)
Genres: Drama, Romance
Director: Francis Giacobetti
Main Cast: Sylvia Kristel, Umberto Orsini, Laura Gemser, Frédéric Lagache, Catherine Rivet
Keywords: open relationship, erotic movie, erotic 70s

Overview: Emmanuelle returns to her husband in Hong Kong and proceeds to have several extramarital affairs -- with his knowledge, of course. Her husband's lover and American guest are both very puzzled by their openness.
