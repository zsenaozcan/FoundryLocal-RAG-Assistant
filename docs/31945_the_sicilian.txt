Title: The Sicilian (1987)
Genres: Drama
Director: Michael Cimino
Main Cast: Christopher Lambert, Terence Stamp, Joss Ackland, John Turturro, Richard Bauer
Keywords: sicily, sicilian mafia, sicilian

Overview: Egocentric bandit Salvatore Guiliano fights the Church, the Mafia, and the landed gentry while leading a populist movement for Sicilian independence.
